Write a dialogue between a Molinist and a Banezian Thomist -- the old Jesuit vs Dominican fight that eventually got Rome to convene the Congregatio de Auxiliis and then basically give up and tell both sides to quit calling each other heretics. The specific question they should hash out: why does grace actually 'take' in one sinner and not in another who is, as far as anyone can tell, in exactly the same circumstances (say, two people hear the same sermon, one converts, one doesnt). The Molinist should account for this via congruism and God's middle knowledge of counterfactuals of freedom; the Banezian via physical premotion and the sufficient/efficacious grace distinction. Push them to really engage each other's actual moves instead of just restating their own view -- e.g. does congruism quietly make grace's success hostage to the creature rather than God, and does physical premotion quietly collapse libertarian freedom into something more like compatibilism (or outright determinism)? Keep it tight, not a whole essay per turn.
