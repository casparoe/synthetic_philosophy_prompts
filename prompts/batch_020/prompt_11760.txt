(2) Marcus agreed the fraud is fraud regardless of who names it, but said that isn't the real question -- the question is whether Nora specifically, given her own hand in it, is well positioned to be the one publicly condemning Felix for it, rather than, say, passing what she knows to a journalist and staying out of the accuser's seat herself. (6) Marcus said reform might blunt the charge going forward, but it can't erase it retroactively: Nora made her restitution privately and has still never publicly accounted for her own role, and coming forward now as his sole accuser, while staying quiet about that, serves her own story as much as it serves the truth.
