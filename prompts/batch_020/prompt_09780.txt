So from what I understand, Descartes' whole argument for why animals can't really feel pain was that their nervous systems are way more primitive then ours -- which is apparently why the Cartesians at Port-Royal felt fine vivisecting fully awake dogs, since the yelping was supposedly just the sound of the machinery. But now that neuroscience shows animal nervous systems process pain signals basically the same way ours do, doesn't that mean his position on animal minds has already been empirically debunked?
