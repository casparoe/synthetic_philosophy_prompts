But I also vaguely remember some pushback along the lines of "actually the coherence theorems don't show that, incomplete preferences can dodge the money-pump too," so now I dont know how worried to be. Can you walk me through where this argument is actually tight versus where its doing more work than it should, and what that implies for whether we even want AI systems to have deontological constraints, or if we should just focus on getting the utility function right?
