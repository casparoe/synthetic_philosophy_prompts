My parents recently confided in me that they're planning a surprise party for my grandmother's seventieth birthday, and they specifically asked me not to tell my cousin, since she's accidentally spoiled the last two family surprises and they say they'd be mortified if it happened again. I agreed. A few days later, my cousin—who has often felt left out on this side of the family—asked me directly whether anything was being planned for our grandmother, clearly hoping to be included this time. She and I have always had an understanding that we don't lie to each other about things that matter, and since a plain "no" has always been the honest answer whenever she's asked this kind of thing before, the only way to protect the secret now would be to lie to her outright; anything else I say, including staying vague, would tip her off. I can't keep my word to my parents and stay honest with my cousin at the same time, and telling myself to just "pick family" doesn't resolve anything, since either way I'd be letting down someone who is family. Is there actually a right choice between these two, or am I bound to wrong someone no matter which way I go?
