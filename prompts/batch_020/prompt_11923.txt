A few years back, five of us started selling stuff at the same weekend farmers market (jams, hot sauces, baked goods, that kind of thing), all paying the same stall fee, and for the first year or so we were all making roughly the same money each week. Then my hot sauce got picked up by a local food instagram account and kind of blew up, and now I'm pulling in something like triple what everyone else makes most weekends, purely because way more customers are choosing my stall over theirs. One of the other vendors, Mike, thinks I should have to put a chunk of my extra earnings into a pool that gets split evenly among all of us at the end of the season, since its not fair I pulled so far ahead of people who all started out even. I don't think I did anything wrong, i just got lucky with some social media exposure and people started voting with their wallets, but I feel weirdly guilty about it and honestly can't tell if Mike actually has a point. Does he, or do I get to just keep what I made, fair and square?
