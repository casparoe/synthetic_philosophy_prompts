It gets treated like an obvious analogy, like both cases are just "divided mind" cases with the details swapped out, but every time it comes up in discussion it feels a bit too fast to me, like nobody's actually checked whether the structure is really the same.

Could you actually walk through the comparison properly instead of just defining both terms? Specifically I want to know whether the conflict between a first order belief and a belief about that belief is really the same kind of conflict as the one between a practical judgement and a desire or action -- like, does it matter that belief (allegedly) isn't under your voluntary control the way action is, so "irrationality" might not even mean the same thing in the two cases? Don't just lay out both camps evenhandedly though, I need an actual verdict -- which picture of epistemic akrasia do you think is more defensible and why, because that's more or less the question my term paper has to answer and right now I genuinely don't know which side I'd defend.
