For a statistics elective project, I pulled last semester's records from my school's peer-tutoring center because I wanted to work out which of two tutors to recommend to a friend who's struggling specifically in geometry. "Improved" here means moving up at least one full letter grade over the semester.

| Subject | Tutor A | Tutor B |
|---|---|---|
| Algebra | 32/40 (80%) | 9/10 (90%) |
| Geometry | 3/10 (30%) | 16/40 (40%) |
| Combined | 35/50 (70%) | 25/50 (50%) |

Tutor B has the higher improvement rate in algebra, and the higher rate in geometry too, yet Tutor A comes out clearly ahead once the two subjects are combined. I've triple-checked the arithmetic, so that isn't the issue. What I can't work out is how both patterns can be true at the same time, and, more importantly, which figure should actually guide my recommendation. Should I tell my friend to go with Tutor A because of the stronger overall record, or with Tutor B because of the geometry-specific one, and why?
