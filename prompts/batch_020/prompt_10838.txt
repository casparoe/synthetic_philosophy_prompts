So there's decent evidence that our judgement, late in a session, is tracking something other than the essay actually in front of us. One candidate, call her R since this obviously has to stay confidential, failed, and her exam happened to be graded ninth, tenth, and eleventh by all three readers in the same long Thursday session; failing a second time ends her in the program, so this isn't a low-stakes question.
