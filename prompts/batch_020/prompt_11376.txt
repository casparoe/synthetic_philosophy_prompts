I was on the other side, more the Leibniz sufficient-reason line—everything contingent has an explanation, the universe as a whole is contingent too, so it needs an explanation too, and "it's just brute" is a cop-out, not an answer. I even threw in this van Inwagen argument I half remember about how there's only one way for there to be nothing but a huge number of ways for there to be something, so if anything, nothing was always going to be the surprising outcome, not the default one. Neither of us budged, we just got louder til his roommate told us to shut up.
