judge 2: breach yes, causation no -> not liable
judge 3: breach no, causation yes -> not liable

derek: you obviously use the final-verdict vote since that's literally the question being decided -- the two sub-issues are just how each person privately got there, shouldn't matter.

derek: the verdict is the whole point, not the essay explaining it.
