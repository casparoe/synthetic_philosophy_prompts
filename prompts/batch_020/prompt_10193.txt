According to Jerry Fodor's language of thought hypothesis, concepts and inferences are formulated in an innate mental language, or "Mentalese," that underlies and makes possible the acquisition of any natural language in the first place. Given that the Sapir–Whorf hypothesis holds that the grammar and vocabulary of a person's native language shape the structure of their thoughts, doesn't it follow that Mentalese itself must vary from one linguistic community to another, so that native speakers of, say, Mandarin and Navajo are effectively thinking in two incommensurable Mentaleses? If that is right, what does the language of thought hypothesis actually explain that linguistic relativity does not already explain on its own?
