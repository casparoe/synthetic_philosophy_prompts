(a) Show that B is not strictly dominated by T or by M individually for Player 1, but is strictly dominated by some mixed strategy.
(b) Do iterated elimination of strictly dominated strategies (mixed strategies allowed) two different ways: once starting by eliminating B, once starting by eliminating a dominated strategy of Player 2's instead. Show you end up in the same place either way.

(a) T gives P1 (3,0) across (L,R), M gives (0,3). Neither dominates B's (1,1) -- T loses to B at R (0<1) and M loses to B at L (0<1). But a 50/50 mix of T and M gives 1.5 at L and 1.5 at R, wich both beat B's 1, so this mixed strategy strictly dominates B even though no pure strategy does.
