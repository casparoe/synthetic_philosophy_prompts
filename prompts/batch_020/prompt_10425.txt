As a check, two reviewers redid the scoring for a handful of already-completed files in exactly reversed order and reached noticeably different final confidence in the same candidates, despite agreeing about what each piece of evidence shows on its own. Half the committee wants to just average the two orderings and move on; the other half thinks the order-sensitivity itself disqualifies the scores until it's fixed. Draft the short procedural memo I'll bring to next week's meeting: recommend how we should combine this kind of graded, uncertain evidence going forward, and give a fair hearing to whichever side of the dispute you don't take.
