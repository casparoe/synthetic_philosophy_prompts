Six months ago, at twenty-nine, I was diagnosed with ADHD after a lifetime of thinking of myself as merely scattered and undisciplined. Since then I've joined a couple of ADHD-focused online communities and started following creators who specialize in the condition, and I've noticed that I now explain almost everything about myself — chronic lateness, losing my keys, drifting off in meetings — through concepts like "time blindness" and "rejection sensitive dysphoria" that weren't part of my vocabulary a year ago. My therapist tells me this new framework is simply giving me insight I lacked before, but my sister thinks I'm "becoming my diagnosis" and may even be unconsciously reshaping my behavior to fit it. I can't decide whether the label revealed something that was already true of me or is actively remaking me in its own image. Is there a principled way to think through that distinction, or am I overanalyzing an otherwise ordinary experience of self-discovery?
