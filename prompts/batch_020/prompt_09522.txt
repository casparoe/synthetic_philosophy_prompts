My husband and I are currently going through divorce mediation, and something from our years of couples counseling keeps resurfacing. Our therapist always resisted questions about whose fault our problems were, insisting instead that we examine "the pattern" we had built together: how my raising concerns made him withdraw, which made me push harder, which made him withdraw further still, without a clear point of origin in either of us. He has now started invoking this in mediation to argue that it's unreasonable for me to hold him individually accountable for concrete things he did — like secretly moving a large sum of our savings into an account I didn't know existed — since, on the therapist's own logic, no single person's actions really explain what happened once you're looking at the relationship as a whole system. Something about this feels like a sleight of hand to me, but I can't say our therapist's picture of the pattern was wrong either. Is he misusing a legitimate way of understanding relationships in order to dodge accountability, or am I just refusing to accept how these shared systems actually work?
