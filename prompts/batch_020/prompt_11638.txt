So I've been down a rabbit hole this week that started with rereading Yudkowsky's old "Not for the Sake of Happiness (Alone)" post from the Fun Theory sequence, and ended with me skimming through the Everitt & Hutter reward-tampering papers on the alignment forum -- the current-RF vs future-RF optimizer stuff, the rocks-and-diamonds gridworld, and that "avoiding wireheading with value reinforcement learning" paper. Now I can't stop thinking about how little contact there seems to be between the classic philosophy-of-mind wireheading debate (Nozick's experience machine, whether our recoil at plugging in refutes hedonism) and the AI safety reward-tampering literature, even though they both seem to be pointing at the same underlying structure: an agent (or a person) discovering it can satisfy its optimization target more cheaply by cutting out the "real world" middleman and just hitting the signal directly.

Here's the thing I keep going back and forth on. Does the fact that basically everyone -- including committed hedonists -- recoils at wireheading actually refute hedonism about welfare, the way Nozick intended the experience machine to? Or is that recoil better explained by something adjacent but different, like the current-RF/future-RF framing, where what's bad about tampering with your own reward channel is really an instrumental/agentic problem (you stop tracking whatever the reward was ever a proxy for, you become uncorrigible, your values get "locked in" to something degenerate) rather than a fact about whether pleasure is the good? People sometimes wave in the direction of "reward is not the optimization target"-style arguments as though that settles it, but I don't think that actually answers the axiological question of what, if anything, is wrong with wireheading.

Could you write me an essay that actually takes a side on this, rather than doing the "there are many perspectives" dodge? Weigh the hedonism-refuting reading against the instrumental/agentic reading (and any other serious contender you think is being unfairly ignored), and land on a real verdict about what the badness of wireheading and reward tampering does and doesn't show about hedonism -- not just a survey of the positions.
