Both of my colleagues made points I found genuinely persuasive, and I haven't been able to settle it myself, so I'd like your help adjudicating the exchange move by move rather than just declaring an overall winner.

(3) Elena answered that "strategic" and "sound" aren't opposites, and that the text-versus-intent question is a live interpretive issue independent of anyone's motives: if there was never a single controlling framers' intent to recover, since the framers themselves were so divided, then reading the document by its public meaning isn't a dodge — it's the only coherent method left.
