okay so we went on this whole tangent in my psych class about something called a "ulysses contract" -- basically where someone with like bipolar disorder, while they're stable and thinking clearly, signs something in advance saying that doctors and family ARE allowed to force treatment or hospitalization on them if they go manic or psychotic later, even if future-them is begging everyone not to. its named after odysseus making his crew tie him to the mast so he couldnt swim off toward the sirens. my teacher said the same logic is basically behind how constitutions work too, like a country tying its own hands now so some future majority cant do something everyone would regret. for my creative writing elective can you write me a short story (a page or two is fine, doesnt need to be long) about a character stuck in exactly that bind -- like the scene where their past self and present self are basically at war over who actually gets to decide. i dont want a neat ending where its obvious who was right, i want it to genuinly sit in the question of which self should have the authority here.
