A friend of mine, a software engineer, built himself a small app to help keep up his workout habit: it logs each session and, on completion, plays a short congratulatory chime and animation that he says gives him a genuine burst of satisfaction. A few weeks ago, mostly on a whim, he modified the app so that he could trigger the chime and animation directly, without exercising first, and found it felt exactly like earning it the usual way. He has since taken to triggering it most mornings instead of working out, and by his own account feels just as accomplished as before, if not more so, since the occasional dread of a skipped session is gone. He doesn't think he's done anything questionable: the chime was only ever meant to make him feel good about his fitness, and it still does. I've spent a good part of my career thinking about what makes a life go well, and I still can't say exactly what, if anything, has gone wrong here, given that by his own lights he's no worse off and perhaps better off. Is he right that there's nothing to worry about? And if not, what exactly has he lost?
