My professor walked us through the Self-Sampling Assumption (reason as if you're a random draw from your reference class of observers) versus the Self-Indication Assumption (your own existence is evidence that there are more observers rather than fewer), and how SIA basically cancels out the doomsday update in the simple case, except then it runs straight into the Presumptuous Philosopher: this person who, faced with two cosmological theories that agree on everything except how many observers exist, confidently bets on the one with way more observers before any actual evidence comes in, just because SIA says existing at all favors bigger universes. We also spent a while on the reference class problem, i.e. why "all humans" and not "all observers" or "all beings that have heard of this exact argument" - apparently the doomsday number changes completely depending on which one you pick.
