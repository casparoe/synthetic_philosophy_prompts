The six approaches:
- fisherian: Fisherian significance testing — p-value as continuous evidence against the null, no fixed alpha threshold decided in advance
- neyman_pearson: Neyman–Pearson hypothesis testing — fixed alpha and beta, an explicit alternative hypothesis, long-run error-rate control
- hybrid_nhst: the hybrid "null hypothesis significance testing" actually taught in textbooks and used in most papers, which mixes the two above without acknowledging it
- subjective_bayes: subjective/personalist Bayesianism — priors are just an individual's honest degrees of belief (de Finetti/Savage style)
- objective_bayes: objective/default Bayesianism — Jeffreys-style "uninformative" priors meant to make results more mechanical and comparable across researchers
- likelihoodism: evidence is just the likelihood ratio between two hypotheses, no priors and no long-run error rates at all (Royall's version)
