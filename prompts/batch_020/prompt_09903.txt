ok so this has been bugging me for a while. i've been reading old LW posts on acausal trade (the stuff about cooperating with aliens in other Everett branches, or with whatever comes after us, despite zero causal contact) and it strikes me that the whole setup is basically Hofstadter's superrationality but with the symmetry assumption knocked out. superrationality gets cooperation in a one-shot PD because both players are stipulated to be running the *same* reasoning on the *same* problem — so whatever conclusion you reach, they reach too, no enforcement needed, symmetry just does all the work. acausal trade wants that same "no causal contact, still cooperate" result, except between agents that can be totally different in values, resources, even era.

so compare the two for me: once you strip out the symmetry, whats actually doing the work to justify cooperation in acausal trade (mutual simulation? correlated decision procedures? something else?), and is it anywhere near as solid as symmetry was — or is acausal trade quietly resting on a shakier argument while claiming the same kind of conclusion superrationality earned fair and square?
