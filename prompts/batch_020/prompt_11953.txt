Before starting this fellowship I had more or less assumed that "coherence arguments" named one tight, essentially mathematical result; the more of this back-and-forth I read, the more it looks like a loose federation of arguments of quite unequal rigor that get cited as if interchangeable, and I would like to pull them apart.
