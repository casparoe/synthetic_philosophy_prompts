So a few of us from a friend group who's members go back to the old SSC comments-section days have a running group chat, and this week it went down a real rabbit hole after someone trotted out the standard LessWrong line that personal identity isn't "carved into reality," and that whether an upload or an em is "really you" is just a verbal dispute over how you draw boundaries around a four-dimensional worm. That sent me back to the actual metaphysics behind the "worm" talk, i.e. the endurantism vs. perdurantism debate, and specifically to David Lewis's problem of temporary intrinsics: if you endure (are wholly present at every moment you exist, rather than having distinct temporal parts), then the very same, fully-present you seems to have flat-out incompatible intrinsic properties -- bent while sitting, straight while standing -- at different times, and something has to give.

Lewis's own take is that perdurantism dissolves this for free (it's just different temporal parts that are bent and straight, no contradiction), and that the endurantist patches are all more or less desperate: either shape becomes a disguised relation to a time, which seems to get the nature of an intrinsic property wrong, or you relativize the having/instantiation relation itself to times instead, a move I've seen called "adverbialism," which Lewis basically treats as a mysterious primitive invented just to dodge him. I want you to make the case that Lewis is too quick to dismiss here. Argue, as strongly as you can, that adverbializing instantiation (having a property "t-ly" rather than simpliciter) is a legitimate, non-ad-hoc move and not just perdurantism smuggled back in under a different name -- and that "wholly present at every time, but instantiating properties in a time-indexed manner" really is cheaper than committing to the view that nothing you've ever touched, sat on, or been, was ever wholly there at all. If it helps your case, feel free to also bring in the rotating-homogenous-disc worry about whether perdurantists can ground facts like rotation without smuggling in some further primitive -- I might be misremembering exactly where I picked that one up, so don't bother chasing down a citation for it, just use it if it's good ammunition.
