a fink is when the disposition switches on or off at literally the exact moment its being tested. theres this classic example (C.B. a mask is different - the disposition is still really there, its just being blocked from showing up. like a glass thats genuinely fragile but is bubble-wrapped so it wont break no matter how many times you drop it.
