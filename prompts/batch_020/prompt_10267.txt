Since he affirms himself while failing to affirm anything about his body, his awareness of himself cannot be identical to, or dependent on, awareness of his body -- and since nothing essential to a thing can be omitted from an accurate awareness of that thing, the self he is aware of cannot itself be a body, or any part of a body.
