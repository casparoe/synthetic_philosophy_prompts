(1) Nora called this a Dutch book waiting to happen: a bookmaker could sell Gil three bets at exactly those prices, each paying $1 if its theory turned out true, collecting $1.45 while paying out only $1 whichever theory won, and this guaranteed loss showed his beliefs, not merely his arithmetic, to be irrational.
