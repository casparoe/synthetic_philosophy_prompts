The basic evolutionary debunking argument says our moral beliefs got shaped by natural selection for reproductive fitness, not for tracking mind-independent moral truth, so realists owe us an explanation for why we should trust them anyway (the whole "huge coincidence" bit, if you've read it). So either the debunking argument also nukes math and logic (full-blown global skepticism, including skepticism about the very argument doing the debunking), or there's some principled asymmetry between the moral case and the math/logic case that blocks the generalization.

Go through the best attempts to draw a principled line between the moral case and the math/logic case, or to just bite the bullet on one side, and actually land on a verdict instead of a wishy washy "reasonable people disagree."
