So this is a bit random but I got diagnosed with ADHD a few months ago at 27 (yes, one of those "watched enough TikToks about it that I finally went to a doctor" stories, I know how that sounds) and it's sent me down a whole rabbit hole. In my gen-ed philosophy of science class we just covered this idea from a philosopher named Ian Hacking called the looping effect — basically that classifying people isn't like classifying rocks or electrons, because the people who get classified read about the category, react to it, perform it or resist it, and that actually changes what the category ends up meaning, which changes how doctors diagnose it next, which changes the next round of people who get diagnosed, and so on. He calls categories like this "interactive kinds" as opposed to indifferent natural kinds, and apparently his classic example is multiple personality disorder (he wrote a whole book about it), but honestly the entire lecture I couldn't stop thinking about my own ADHD videos instead.

I want to try writing something about it instead of just journaling uselessly about my feelings, but I know if I try myself I'll just end up with a generic "the label finally helped me understand myself" story, which kind of misses the actual point of what Hacking's describing.
