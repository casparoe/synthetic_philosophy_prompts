My growth team ran an A/B test on our signup page and found that switching the button from blue to orange lifted conversions by about 35%, p = 0.02. Everyone wants to ship it to all users this week -- but it was actually the best result out of a dozen small tweaks we happened to try, and a 35% lift from just a color change feels almost too good to be true. Our data lead says the p-value is what it is, and that holding off to retest before rolling it out is just excessive caution. I cant quite put my finger on why, but my gut says we should be more skeptical here than she is -- is that fair, or is she right that the numbers speak for themselves?
