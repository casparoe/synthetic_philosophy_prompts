I actually went and read the relevant bits of Some Thoughts Concerning Education though, and its messier than that: Locke seems just as suspicious of bribing kids with treats and prizes as he is of beating them, and thinks the only instruments that really build character are esteem and disgrace, not appeals to pleasure or pain either way. Can you draft the memo I'm supposed to circulate before next week's vote?
