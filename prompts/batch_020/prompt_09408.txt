Quick background in case its relevant: Garrison and Phillips held that the Constitution is, as Garrison famously put it, "a covenant with death and an agreement with hell," pointing to the three-fifths clause, the fugitive slave clause, and the 1808 migration/importation clause as proof the document is pro-slavery at its core, which is why Garrisonians refused to vote or hold office under it. My roommate thinks this is obviously just motivated reasoning on Douglass's part (he needed a Constitution he could work within once he broke from the Garrisonians politically) and that Garrison's reading is the more honest one, but I'm not so sure.
