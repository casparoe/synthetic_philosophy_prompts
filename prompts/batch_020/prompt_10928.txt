Okay so I've been down a rabbit hole reading old Alignment Forum posts on the commitment races problem -- Kokotajlo's original post and the pile of discussion it kicked off about updateless agents and acausal bargaining. Feels like the core lesson is pretty simple: in a Chicken-style standoff, whoever visibly locks in their commitment first wins, like ripping your steering wheel out before the other guy does. So shouldn't the actual takeaway for AGI labs be to make their system go fully updateless and lock in a bargaining stance the moment its turned on, before any rival lab's AI gets the chance? Sure, you might end up committing to something dumb because you didn't have time to think it through, but that seems like a small price for guaranteeing you're never the one who has to blink first. Kind of feels like everyone in the comments was overthinking a pretty straightforward race-to-commit strategy, no?
