Here's roughly how it went (paraphrased from memory over beers later that night, so forgive any imprecision):

CDT, by contrast, says your choice doesn't cause Death to be in one city rather than the other, so the causal expected utilities come out tied and CDT calmly says "doesnt matter, flip a coin," which is obviously the sane answer. Neither "stay" nor "flee" is ratifiable on its own, sure, but the mixed act "flip a coin" is, and that's the exact same verdict CDT gives. So properly patched, EDT and CDT agree here, and the case doesn't favor CDT the way Theo thinks, it just embarrasses a version of EDT nobody sophisticated actually holds anymore.

Needing a causally-flavored crutch to make EDT behave is evidence for CDT's diagnosis, not a vindication of EDT.
