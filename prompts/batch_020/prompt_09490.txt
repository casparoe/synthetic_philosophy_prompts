Ok, this has been bugging me for ages. Given current cosmology (positive cosmological constant, de Sitter space, eventual heat death) it looks like over long enough timescales random thermal fluctuations will cough up way more short-lived Boltzmann brains -- freak conscious observers with false memories of a whole evolutionary history -- than there were ever real evolved observers like us. So by ordinary self-sampling/anthropic reasoning a la Bostrom, shouldnt I just update my credence that I myself am a Boltzmann brain right now, memories and all, way up toward 1? Whenever I've brought this up on LW people just call it "a reductio against those cosmologies, not something to actually believe," which always felt like a dodge to me -- if the population stats really favor BBs that heavily, why doesn't Bayes just force the update through?
