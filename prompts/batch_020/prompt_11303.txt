The weird part is I definately didn't drift out of my lane or brake late or anything like that, and my mom didn't notice a thing, so my eyes and hands had to have been reading the road correctly the whole time.

Here's what's been bugging me: was I actually conscious of the road during those 10 minutes, or not? I brought this up at lunch and it turned into this whole argument that went nowhere — one friend said it's basically like sleepwalking so obviously I wasn't conscious, another said that comparison doesn't really work since sleepwalkers can't drive that well, and a third friend said the question doesn't even make sense in the first place.
