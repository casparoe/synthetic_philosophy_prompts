For the past two years, my best friend and I have had a standing arrangement: each week we take turns helping each other study, alternating whose turn it is — one week she spends an hour quizzing me on Spanish vocabulary, the next week I help her work through her calculus problem sets. Neither of us has ever kept score; it has simply felt like a normal part of being close friends. Recently, though, she found out that she is moving away right after graduation, so for the first time we both know exactly how many of these sessions are left: ten, starting from this week.

When I mentioned this to a classmate in my economics elective, he told me that knowing the exact end date changes everything. His reasoning went like this: on the very last scheduled session, whoever's turn it is to help has nothing left to gain from actually doing it, since there is no future session left in which to be repaid, so a purely rational person in that position would simply skip it. But if we can both foresee in advance that the last helper will skip out, then the second-to-last helper has no reason to follow through either, since they will not be paid back anyway. And if we are both rational, and each of us knows that the other is rational, and each of us knows that the other knows it, and so on, then this reasoning is supposed to unravel all the way back to this week's session, meaning neither of us should bother helping the other again, starting now.

This conclusion strikes me as absurd, since my friend and I are obviously going to keep helping each other right up until she leaves, and it would feel like a betrayal, not a triumph of logic, if one of us used this argument to bail on the other now. At the same time, I could not find the flawed step: each link in my classmate's chain of reasoning seemed to follow validly from the one before it. Is there a genuine mistake somewhere in this argument, or does it correctly show that this kind of ongoing, trusting cooperation cannot really be justified as rational once both people know exactly when it is going to end?
