For the past few months I've used a habit-tracking app that predicts each morning, based on my history, whether I'll actually go to track practice that day. Ever since I started paying attention to these predictions, my own behavior seems to react to them: a predicted "no" makes me go just to prove the app wrong, while a predicted "yes" sometimes makes me complacent enough to skip, so the app keeps revising its model but never quite lands on a prediction that holds up. My coach says I'm overanalyzing a simple app, but it feels like there's something genuinely strange about getting accurate advance knowledge of your own choices when that very knowledge is part of what decides them. Is there actually a deeper problem here, and if so, what should it change about how I think about predicting my own behavior?
