ok this one's pretty niche but it's been living in my head since I first ran into the 'commitment races' framing on LW (iirc it started as a wei dai shortform and then kokotajlo turned it into a proper post). the basic mechanic: in a bargaining situation, whoever manages to credibly commit first and hardest to a demand gets the schelling-style advantage over whoever commits second, so every agent is incentivized to commit as early and as irrevocably as possible, even before it's actually thought things through -- which is exactly how you get a race to the bottom instead of an actual negotiation.

write me a dialogue between two equally sharp superintelligent AIs standing at a console seconds before a resource-split deadline, each with a hand over a button that would broadcast a maximally aggressive, verifiable, irrevocable commitment to their bargaining position (if they both commit to incompatible demands the whole pie gets destroyed for both of them; if only one commits, the other's rational move is to fold). one of them is convinced that hitting the button first and hardest is simply what a rational bargainer does, and is trying to talk the other into backing down before either of them commits to anything. the other is convinced that the race to commit is itself the disaster waiting to happen, and is trying to talk the first one out of touching the button at all. don't strawman either side -- give me their strongest moves, and make sure they're actually responding to each others' points instead of just re-stating their own position.
