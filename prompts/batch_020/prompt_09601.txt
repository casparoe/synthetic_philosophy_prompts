A colleague of mine from the stats department got into a friendly argument with one of my grad students, who's mildly obsessed with a long running webcomic thats currently sitting at strip #1,847. Half needling him, she said something like: "if you genuinely have zero other information about how long a series like this tends to run, your single best guess for the total number of strips it'll ever publish is just double what it's at right now, so start emotionally preparing for the ending somewhere around #3,700." I have a nagging sense I've run into this exact style of argument before, dressed up and applied to a much bigger and considerably more unsettling question, but I teach ethics and philosophy of mind, not formal epistemology, so I can't reconstruct it well enough to know if she's using it correctly.
