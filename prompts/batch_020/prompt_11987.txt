Since retiring from four decades as a bench immunologist, I have taken up reading moral philosophy, and it has forced me to reconsider a habit of my old field that I can no longer quite look past. A former colleague of mine routinely adds his name as co-author to papers written almost entirely by his graduate students, purely by virtue of heading the lab, and I have been considering raising this with him directly at an upcoming reunion of our old research group. The complication is that I did exactly the same thing to two of my own students early in my career, before concluding it was wrong and quietly abandoning the practice some twenty years ago, without ever going back to apologize to either of them or correct the record. Given that history, am I in any position to raise this with him, even though I no longer do it myself, or does my own past conduct disqualify me from saying anything at all?
