Trouble is, as an old chemist I'm inclined to think he's basically right that all of it, my determination included, is just chemistry and electrical signals when you get down to it - so where does that leave the determination actually doing anything? But it also feels wrong to say it did nothing at all. Can both of those be true at once, or does one of us have to give somthing up here?
