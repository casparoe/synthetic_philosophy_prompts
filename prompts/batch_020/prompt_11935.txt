My hiking buddy, who's way more into podcasts about this stuff then I am, said this is basically what philosophers call the "bootstrapping problem" or the problem of "easy knowledge" -- using a source to prove that very same source is reliable -- and that there's real debate over whether that's always bogus reasoning or sometimes fine.
