i've been rereading Calvino's Cosmicomics and love how he'll take some dry cosmological fact and spin it into this melancholy little fable, usually narrated by that shapeshifting Qfwfq character. i want to try something similar but with a philosophy thought experiment instead of a physics one -- Max Black's two spheres, floating alone together in an otherwise completely empty, perfectly symmetric universe, identical in literally every property right down to their relations to each other, which Black used to poke a hole in leibniz's principle that things sharing all their properties have to actually just be one thing, not two. could you write me a short story in that spirit? i don't want it to turn into a generic twin/clone story with a philosophical label slapped on -- i want whoever (or whatever) is telling it to really sit with the weirdness, like how either sphere would even know its one of two rather than the only thing that exists, given there's nothing else in the universe to tell them apart.
