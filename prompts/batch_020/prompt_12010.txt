After a set theory reading group last week, a friend and I ended up in a long argument that we never quite resolved. I disagreed — my sense is that felt plausibility here tracks something real, such as how visibly infinite and uncontrolled the implicit selection process is, or how far the statement sits from set theory's home turf.
