Quick recap in case it matters for how you answer: radical interpretation is supposed to work by solving for a speaker's beliefs and the meanings of their sentences at the same time, using charity to hold belief mostly true and rational so meaning can even get a foothold, and Davidson insists this isn't just a method we use to figure out what someone means, its supposed to be constitutive of what it even IS to have beliefs and mean things. But then in the triangulation papers he adds this extra condition - you need (at least) two creatures responding to a shared stimulus AND to each other, because without that second creature there's no fact of the matter about what either of them is even responding to (this is basically his fix for the gavagai problem, rabbit vs undetached rabbit parts etc).

So here's what I actually want to know: if Davidson were around to see something like GPT-4 or Claude, would he say the whole project of radically interpreting it is a category error - like there's no triangulation happening (no shared causal environment, no other creature reacting to the same stimuli and to it in real time), so there's just no fact of the matter about what its outputs mean, even if a charity-driven Tarski-style truth theory could predict its "assertions" flawlessly?
