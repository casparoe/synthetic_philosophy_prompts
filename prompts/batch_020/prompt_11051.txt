So this is a bit of a weird one but it's been rattling around my head for weeks. I don't have any background in this stuff, I just like going down rabbit holes, and with all the AI chatbot news lately I ended up reading Turing's actual 1950 paper where he lays out the "imitation game" (aka the turing test). Everyone talks about that paper like its just about whether a machine can fool a person in a chat window, but buried near the end he spends a whole section taking ESP dead serious - telepathy, clairvoyance, precognition, the lot - and says the statistical evidence for telepathy at the time was basically overwhelming. Then he actually works out how this could break his own test: if a human "witness" is a good telepathic card-guesser and the machine can only guess randomly, the interrogator could just ask about hidden cards and tell them apart that way, with nothing to do with intelligence at all. His fix is to stick everyone in what he literally calls a "telepathy-proof room." I read that line like four times, it's such a strange thing for the father of computer science to be casually proposing.

I can't stop thinking about what a world would look like if he'd turned out to be right and telepathy was just... real and confirmed, the way electricity or radio waves are. Could you write me a short story (a page or two is fine) set in that kind of world, where telepathy-proof rooms are now just standard lab equipment whenever anyone runs a Turing-style test on a new AI system? I'd like it centered on one specific test session, told from the point of view of whoever's running it, where something happens partway through that makes them doubt whether the shielding is really doing the job everyone assumes it does. Don't just make it a spooky twist for its own sake though - I want the story to actually sit with the question Turing was poking at underneath all this: what does "thinking" even mean, or how do you ever prove a machine (or a person, for that matter) is actually thinking rather than just fishing the right answer out of the room some other way, once you can't fully seal off every channel information might travel through between two minds.
