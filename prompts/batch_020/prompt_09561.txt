My partner and I recently had an odd disagreement just before a two-week backpacking trip through an area with no phone signal and a hospital hours from the trailhead. She carries an epinephrine injector for a nut allergy, and when I asked whether it was still good, she said, "I know it's fine. I checked the expiration date a couple of months ago, and there was almost a year left." If she had said exactly the same thing before an ordinary day at home, I wouldn't have thought twice about it, but hearing it right before this trip, I wanted her to dig it out and check the date again, even though she hadn't told me anything new. She got a little irritated and pointed out that the injector is either expired or it isn't, and that has nothing to do with where we're hiking, so if she knew it was fine two months ago, she still knows it's fine now. I couldn't find the flaw in her reasoning, yet wanting her to check again didn't feel like just nerves either. Was I being unreasonable, or is she missing something when she insists it can't matter where we're going?
