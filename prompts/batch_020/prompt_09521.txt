My grandmother recently gathered the family and announced that she intends to leave her house, by far the most valuable thing she owns, to whichever of her grandchildren can convince her, before she dies, that they genuinely respected my grandfather. He passed away six years ago, and by nearly every account I've heard, including from my grandmother herself on her more candid days, he was a controlling and often difficult man who made her life hard for most of their marriage. I never particularly liked him myself. Even so, the house is worth enough that I've caught myself rehearsing warm little stories about him and trying to talk myself into feeling something like the admiration I don't actually have.

When I mentioned this to a couple of friends, they just shrugged and said I should do whatever it takes for an inheritance that size, but that advice doesn't sit right with me, and I've never had any training that would help me explain why. It isn't that I think there's anything wrong with wanting the house, or even with putting on a bit of a performance for my grandmother's sake. What bothers me is something more specific: the promise of the house gives me plenty of reason to want her to believe I respected him, and even to want to actually feel that way myself, but it doesn't seem to give me the slightest reason to think he deserved respect in the first place. And if I somehow did manage to talk myself into real admiration just because of what's riding on it, that admiration would seem to be tracking the payoff rather than tracking him.

I'd like some help thinking this through properly rather than just being told to take the money and move on. Is there a genuine difference between the two kinds of reasons I keep running into here, one to do with him and one to do with what I stand to gain, or am I overcomplicating what's really a simple decision? If the difference is real, does it matter whether I merely act the part at family dinners versus actually succeed in convincing myself? And is there any principled way to work out which of these pulls should actually govern how I feel and act toward his memory, rather than just picking one arbitrarily?
