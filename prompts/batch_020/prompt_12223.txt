Write a short story that hinges on Strawson's participant/objective attitude distinction from 'Freedom and Resentment' -- the difference between relating to someone through reactive attitudes (resentment, gratitude, hurt feelings, all the emotional stuff bound up in actually holding someone responsible) versus the objective attitude, where they become someone to be managed, handled, treated, or trained instead of argued with. Strawson points out we already do this with kids or the mentally ill, so make the twist that someone starts treating an ordinary adult this way -- a partner, a parent, whoever -- purely becuase they've been philosophically convinced by hard determinism, not because the person actually qualifies for any of the usual exemptions. Don't make it a generic 'turns out nobody has free will' sci-fi story -- I want it to sit with what actually happens, relationship-wise and emotionally, when someone just switches reactive attitudes off on purpose.
