On rationalist blogs and forums, discussions of mathematical Platonism about the continuum hypothesis (CH) tend to stop at the same waypoint: Gödel's constructible universe shows CH is consistent with ZFC, Cohen's forcing shows its negation is too, and therefore CH is "independent" — as though that settled the philosophical question rather than merely posing it. I want an essay that pushes past this. Engage seriously with at least one concrete case that new axioms could settle CH's truth value after all — for instance, forcing axioms like PFA, which pin the continuum at ℵ2, or Woodin's Ω-logic-based argument that CH is false — and weigh it against Joel Hamkins's set-theoretic multiverse view, on which CH's independence just reflects that there is no single privileged universe of sets for it to be true or false in. Don't merely summarize both sides: reach and defend a verdict on whether realism about CH's truth value is defensible, and if so, on what grounds.
