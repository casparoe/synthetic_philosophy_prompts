Ok, this has been living in my head rent free for months and I can't decide if it's an actual puzzle or just me overanalyzing my own brain again (a very LessWrong-brained thing to do, I realize). Backstory: I used to have a genuinely bad temper — like, the kind of bad that actually scared my partner. About two years ago, two unrelated things happened around the same time: my doctor put me on a low dose of an anti-anxiety medication for a completely different reason, and a therapist seperately taught me a breathing-and-counting technique to use the instant I feel the very first physical spark of anger (the jaw-clench, heat-in-the-chest thing). Between the two, I haven't had an actual blowup in two years, and my partner now flatly says I "just don't have a temper anymore," full stop, the same way I don't have my old shellfish allergy anymore.

Here's the bit that actually bugs me though. With the meds, it's genuinely possible my underlying wiring changed, so maybe that first spark doesn't even happen the same way anymore — fine, that would be a real change, like the allergy. But with the breathing technique, I don't think anything on the inside changed at all. I still feel the exact same jaw-clench-heat thing every single time, I just do something at that exact split second that keeps it from turning into an actual blowup. So the technique, on its own, hasn't shown me anything except that the temper reliably gets caught a split second before it would come out, which seems just as compatible with the temper being exactly as present as it ever was. And I have no way to introspect my way to which of those two stories is true, or whether it's some mix, since literally every test I could ever run passes through one intervention or the other.

So who's right, me or my partner? Is "I don't have a temper anymore" true in the same sense "I don't have that allergy anymore" is true, or is it more like the exact same trait is still fully there and something — meds, technique, whichever — just reliably shows up a beat before it would kick in? Is there even a principled difference between "the trait is gone" and "the trait is there but always intercepted," and if the interception has been 100% reliable for two straight years, does that difference stop mattering, or does it stay real even though I can never actually verify it from the inside? This half-reminds me of stuff about free will and "could have done otherwise" that I've seen argued about online, but I can't place it exactly, and neither could my friends when I brought it up over drinks.
