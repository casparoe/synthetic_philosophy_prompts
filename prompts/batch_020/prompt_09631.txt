Random one from my game theory class: my dad and his business partner are splitting up their company, and instead of just negotiating a fair split they keep hinting at threats - he'll poach the company's biggest client, the partner will drag things out with lawyers for years - because whoever admits they need the split more gets taken advantage of. My dad's lawyer says the smart move is to just keep one-upping the threats until the other backs down, but if they actually follow through they'll both end up way worse off than if they'd just agreed on something reasonable. Is there a way around this that doesn't depend on either of them trusting the other to be honest, but thats still guaranteed to leave both of them at least as well off as just escalating? Or is the lawyer right that threats are really the only leverage anyone has here?
