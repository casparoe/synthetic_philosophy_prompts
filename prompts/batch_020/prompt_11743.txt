For the past few weeks I've been preoccupied with the Pasadena game, the coin-toss gamble whose expected value is not merely infinite, like the St. Petersburg gamble's, but genuinely undefined: the infinite series that would give its expectation converges only conditionally, so the total depends on the order in which its payouts are summed, and can be made to equal any number at all, or none. I haven't found anyone else who finds this as strange as I do. Could you write me a short joke that turns on this specific peculiarity? I don't want anything that reads as just a generic joke about gambling, probability, or infinity — I want the punchline itself to hinge on the fact that the sum refuses to settle on one value, in a way that would only really land for someone who understands why that happens.
