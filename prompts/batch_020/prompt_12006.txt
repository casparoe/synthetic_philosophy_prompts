Leah said Gibbard's whole hyperplan move is basically a con: saying the conditional expresses 'ruling out' the combo where you disapprove of lying but don't disapprove of getting your brother to lie doesn't explain why that combo is inconsistent, it just names it and calls the naming a solution. Owen said the machinery looks the same on paper but isn't doing the same job, because hyperplans are individuated by motivational role rather than representational content, which is exactly why the view can predict things truth-conditional semantics can't, like two people disagreeing morally without disagreeing about any fact.
