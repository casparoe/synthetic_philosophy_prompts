Random philosophy of physics one for you. Newton's bucket is the classic case for spacetime substantivalism over relationism, but the debate has moved way past buckets by now - the main event these days is the hole arguement, which is supposed to show that plain 'manifold substantivalism' makes GR wildly indeterministic. A bunch of fixes have been proposed: sophisticated substantivalism (deny that spacetime points have primitive identity/haecceity apart from the metric), structuralism (points are individuated only by their structural role), relationism (no primitive spacetime points to begin with, only relations among events), and Maudlin's move of just biting the bullet and accepting the indeterminism.
