I went down a rabbit hole reading about Elizabeth Anscombe's pamphlet "Mr. Truman's Degree," where she tears into Oxford for giving Truman an honorary degree and argues that deliberately killing civilians is murder no matter how many lives it supposedly saves later on. She died in early 2001, right before the drone war really kicked off, so she never had to grapple with stuff like "signature strikes" -- where the CIA would hit groups of people based on suspicious behavior patterns (military-age guys meeting known militants, etc) without ever confirming who they actually were. Using her actual arguments, not just a vibe that she'd hate drones, what do you think she'd conclude about wether signature strikes count as intentionally killing innocents, or if double effect could somehow cover them?
