Ok so in my philosophy elective we covered Frankfurt's old thought experiment with Black the neuroscientist and Jones -- the chip that only kicks in and forces Jones to vote for Black's guy if Jones was about to vote the other way, but since Jones ends up going for Black's guy on his own, the chip never actually does anything the whole time. teachers point was that Jones is still responsible even tho he literally couldn't have done otherwise, since the chip would've caught him. My friends Deja and Marcus got into it about this at lunch and wouldnt let it go.

(1) Marcus said the case doesn't actually kill the "could've done otherwise" requirement, it just hides a flicker of it -- like there's still some tiny alternative, e.g. whatever involuntary sign in Jones's brain would've tipped off the chip, so technically Jones could've done otherwise at that lower level.

(2) Deja said fine, maybe there's a flicker, but a flicker that thin isn't the kind of thing anyone actually thinks responsibility rides on -- nobody gets judged responsible or not over an involuntary twitch, so if that's all "could have done otherwise" comes down to, the requirement's basically been gutted even if it's technically still true.

(3) Marcus said that's a bait and switch -- the original rule never said "needs a thick, robust alternative," it just said "needs an alternative," period. If Deja has to add the robustness clause just to save the case, that means some alternative-possibilities requirement is still doing the work, just a weaker version of it.

(4) Deja said the whole setup's a trap either way: if the chip can actually predict what Jones will do ahead of time, everything was determined from the start and of course Jones counts as "responsible" on the teacher's compatibilist assumptions -- so the case just assumes what it's trying to prove. But if the chip can't predict ahead of time and only reacts once Jones is basically already deciding, then Jones did have a real window to go the other way before the chip could catch him, so the case never actually got rid of the alternative possibility to begin with.
