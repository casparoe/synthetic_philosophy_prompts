Our student council is rewriting its constitution, and the main disagreement has been over who should be allowed to vote on the new rules. I believe every currently enrolled student should get a vote, but several members argue that since the new constitution will also govern years after we've graduated, next year's incoming freshmen should have some say too, even though they can't vote in this year's election. When we tried to settle that dispute by voting on who should be allowed to vote in the first place, someone pointed out that whoever we'd already admitted to that vote would decide the outcome, which felt circular. Is there a principled way to decide who belongs in a vote like this, or is the line we draw always going to be somewhat arbitrary?
