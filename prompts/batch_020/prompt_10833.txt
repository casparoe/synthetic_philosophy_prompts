A close friend of mine describes saving money as her top financial priority: she tracks every expense, has tried several budgeting apps, and regularly laments how little is left in her account by the end of the month. Yet for well over a year she has bought an expensive coffee and pastry every single weekday, even in months when her card was declined at the grocery store, and this is the one expense she has never cut. When I pointed out the contradiction, she said the purchase doesn't really count against her priority, since saving is what she genuinely wants and the daily habit is just a minor lapse that doesn't reflect that. Given how stable and predictable her spending has been over all this time, is there a coherent sense in which what she "really wants" differs from what she keeps choosing, or is she simply mistaken about her own priorities?
