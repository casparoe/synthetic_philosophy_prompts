For the past year, a close friend of mine and I have had an arrangement that I still can't quite make sense of. I don't have any background in philosophy or ethics — I've just been turning this over in my head on my own for a while now. She's been vegan for about five years because she believes the way animals are treated in factory farming is a serious moral wrong; I respect that but don't feel the same conviction. I, on the other hand, think it's genuinely wrong to buy from certain fast-fashion retailers given what's known about the pay and conditions in the factories that supply them, and she thinks that concern is overblown and more a matter of taste than ethics. After months of arguing about both issues without moving each other an inch, we struck a deal, half as a joke at first: for a year, I would eat vegetarian every time we ate together, and she would only buy clothes from a short list of brands I had checked out as ethically produced. Neither of us has broken our end of it.

My partner thinks the whole thing is a bit ridiculous, maybe even dishonest. His argument is that since I don't actually think eating less meat matters morally, my going vegetarian around her isn't a good deed on my part; and since she doesn't think fast fashion is wrong, switching brands isn't one on hers either. On his view, we are each just performing a morality neither of us believes in to keep the other happy, rather than doing anything because we think it is right, so nothing of real ethical significance is happening between us. I have tried to push back on this, but I keep losing the argument, mainly because I cannot explain what exactly would be good about the arrangement if not either of us acting on genuine conviction.

Still, it seems wrong to me that nothing of value is going on here. Less of something my friend considers a serious wrong is now happening, and less of something I consider a serious wrong is now happening too, and that seems like it should count for something even though neither of us changed our mind about anything. I would like to know whether my partner is onto something real, or whether there is a solid justification for this kind of arrangement that I simply have not been able to put into words.
