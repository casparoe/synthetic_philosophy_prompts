so my epistemology seminar is on norms of assertion this week (Williamson's knowledge account vs the truth account vs the "justified/reasonable belief" camp) and something from my friend group ended up being a perfect test case -- except now two friends are fighting about it and wont drop it.

quick context: before grades were posted, Jake told our stressed friend Maya "relax, you definitely passed," based only on her being a straight-A student and the prof's generous curve. grades came out, he was right.

at a party this weekend, Dan and Nora got into it over whether Jake did anything wrong:

1. Dan said Jake shouldn't have said "definitely" since he didn't actually know -- grades weren't posted yet. the honest version was "you probably passed."
2. Nora said thats too strict: Jake had strong evidence and turned out right, and if we had to hedge every claim we're not 100% sure of, conversation would be unbearable -- like a doctor saying "you'll be fine" off symptoms alone, before any test results are back.
3. Dan said the doctor case actually helps him: we DO fault doctors for unhedged reassurance that turns out wrong, so the real standard was knowledge all along, and Jake just got lucky. if Maya had failed on some fluke, everyone would say "definitely" was out of line.
4. Nora said "got lucky" is the wrong description -- what matters is whether Jake was justified given what he knew at the time, and he clearly was. more like a forecaster who doesn't need to caveat every single forecast with "probably" to be doing their job responsibly. she did concede Jake probably should've said "I'd bet anything" instead of "definitely," but called that a style choice, not an epistemic one.
5. Dan said that concession gives the game away: "I'd bet anything" sounds better than "definitely" precisely because it doesn't claim to know, only to be confident -- and that gap is the whole epistemic issue, not a style one.

they wanted me to referee and I wouldn't. can you go through it move by move and say who's got the better of it at each point, not just who wins overall, and flag anything you think both of them are still missing?
