My friend finally convinced me, after a long argument last night, that evolutionary biology has shakier scientific credentials than most people assume. Her clinching point was that Karl Popper, the very philosopher who gave us the falsifiability criterion, concluded that natural selection is an unfalsifiable tautology and therefore belongs, by his own demarcation standard, in the same category as astrology rather than with genuine empirical theories. If even the architect of falsifiability judged Darwinism to fail his own test, doesn't that mean biologists who explain particular traits as the product of natural selection are engaged in untestable storytelling rather than real science?
