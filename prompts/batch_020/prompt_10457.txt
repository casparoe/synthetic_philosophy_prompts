My partner wants to spend a few hundred bucks re-landscaping the side yard — new plants, mulch, a little path — even though it's boxed in by a tall fence and literally nobody will ever see it, not even us (no window faces it, we never walk back there). Their argument is that it'll definately just be a more beautiful little patch of the world in itself, whether or not anyone ever experiences it — they're not even doing it to feel good knowing it's there, they insist the yard itself will just be objectively better. I don't buy it: "good" or "beautiful" seems like it has to be good for somebody, or at least perceived by somebody, or else what's the word even doing? Who's actually right here?
