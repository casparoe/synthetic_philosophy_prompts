i'm in a reading group that's spent the year on the christiano-ish cluster of ideas — iterated amplification, HCH, debate, that whole neighborhood — and for the last session i'm putting together a little zine of poems. i had the idea that a villanelle is basically the correct form for factored cognition, and i want a poem that tests that. the setup, in case it helps: some consultant gets a question too big for one person, breaks it into smaller questions, hands each of those to another consultant just like them, who may break those down further, and at some depth someone just answers, and the answers perculate back up and get combined. no single node ever understands the original question; the bet of the whole scheme is that the assembled tree can still think as well as the person at the root would given a thousand years. the same move, over and over, at every level — which is what a villanelle's refrains are.

so: write a standard villanelle (five tercets and a closing quatrain) in the voice of one consultant somewhere in the middle of the tree. all they ever see is the fragment their parent passed down — say, "check whether this source actually supports that claim," with no clue what the claim is for, or who cares, or what the root even asked. the two refrains should be the two halves of their situation and should do the structural work: one refrain is the question as it arrives from above (context-free, no reason attached), the other is the answer as it gets sent back (the best they can do, handed on in trust). the refrains alternate through the stanzas and finally meet as the closing couplet — which is exactly the point, since in HCH the question and the answer only come together at the root, in a place the speaker never gets to see. someone who knows factored cognition should be able to tell which refrain is which and why they can only meet at the end; someone who doesn't should still get a good poem about being one small part of a deliberation you can't see the whole of. keep the speaker's vantage honest — no cheating by revealing the root question from nowhere.

one more ask: please don't use any formatting. no headings, no bullet points, no bold, nothing — just the poem laid out the way it would appear on a printed page.
