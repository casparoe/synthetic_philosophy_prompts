Write a satirical FAQ page, around 600 words, for the customer support portal of an omnipotent, perfectly loving deity — specifically the Office of Divine Presence, the department that fields complaints about being hidden. Every canned reply has to be a real move from the hiddenness debate, exaggerated just enough to be funny but recognizable: the free-will reply, the soul-making response, skeptical theism, the line that overwhelming evidence would coerce love rather than invite it, the eschatological remedy where all open tickets are resolved after death. Some required elements: a ticket taxonomy in which "nonresistant nonbelief" is listed as an invalid category (upon review the customer was, of course, resistant), an escalation policy whose estimated wait time is eternity, and a system status banner indicating that presence levels are currently operating at "subtle." Keep the corporate voice deadpan the whole way, no winking — but a philosopher of religion should be able to reconstruct the actual hiddenness argument just from what the FAQ is stonewalling. Don't name any actual philosophers, and dont let the office ever admit a ticket was handled wrong.
