I've been going down a rabbit hole on the Sleeping Beauty problem ever since a seminar on self-locating belief, and I've gotten to the weird stage where I can recite everyone's moves but have no idea what's actually being disputed anymore. Setup as I have it: Beauty is put to sleep Sunday, woken Monday and (only if a fair coin lands tails) again on Tuesday, with amnesia in between, and asked on each awakening for her credence that the coin landed heads. Halfers say 1/2, thirders say 1/3, sure, fine.

What I want explained is the layer underneath all that. As far as I can tell half the fight is really about what counts as "new evidence" when the evidence is essentially self-locating. The halfer says Beauty on waking learns nothing she didn't already know Sunday night, so nothing should move. The thirder says this awakening right here is evidence, roughly because it's twice as expected under tails — but I genuinely don't know whether that's supposed to be ordinary Bayesian conditioning or some new-principle-for-indexical-cases, and that distinction seems to be doing a lot of unexplained work. Then there's the thing that supposedly embarrasses the halfer: if she's told "today is Monday," pretty much everyone agrees she should say 2/3 heads, but getting from 1/2-on-waking to 2/3 after conditioning on Monday, while treating Monday and Tuesday symmetrically, seems to break ordinary conditionalization — which is why some people bite the bullet and become "double halfers," saying the Monday-conditional credence is still 1/2. Can you walk me through exactly what breaks there? Is it that "it is Monday" isn't a proposition in the ordinary timeless sense, or that updating on centered information doesn't have to obey the usual diachronic rules, or something else? And relatedly: is there a defensible reading on which the whole halfer/thirder fight is an equivocation over what "credence" even means for a temporarily amnesiac agent (probability of the coin vs. probability of "this awakening being a heads-awakening"), or is there a real substantive disagreement under that framing too?

Bonus question, if you have opinions: my TA claims the problem dissolves in a variant where Beauty is woken exactly once either way — Monday no matter what, plus Tuesday only on tails — but where on tails the Tuesday interview is about some unrelated topic entirely, so she's never unsure whether she's in an interview about the coin. I can't see why what the interview is about should matter to her credence about the coin. Does that variant actually change anything, or is it a nice intuition pump that doesn't touch the core dispute?
