been a LessWrong/EA forum guy since the Overcoming Bias days and i've finally decided to actually write up my thoughts on introspection instead of just upvoting everyone else's. but before i put my own theory out there i want to be clear on what i'm even asking a theory to do. the standard move in our corner of the internet is "confabulation exists, split-brain patients invent reasons, therefore introspection is garbage" and i feel like that's way too fast. so: write me the desiderata. what does an adequate theory of introspective access actually have to deliver?

the things i want covered but feel free to add more: it has to make sense of the confabulation literature without overgeneralising (if some reports are confabulated, what does that tell us about the ones that aren't — does the theory have a story about how we could ever tell the difference from the inside?); it has to say whether meditation people claiming fine-grained awareness of the arising of thoughts count as new data or as trained confabulation, and how we'd decide; it can't just say "introspection is sometimes unreliable" — i want the distinction respected between a faculty that fails detectably and one that fails silently, because that difference matters loads for whether "notice your confusion" is even a coherent practice; it should handle the fact that we validate introspective reports against eye-tracking and behaviour, which makes me suspicious we've just replaced introspection with external criteria as the gold standard and quietly changed the subject; and it should be compatible with an evolutionary story about why a mostly-reliable-but-confabulating faculty would be what we ended up with rather than something better.

for each desideratum give the motivating case, and then — this is the bit i actually care about — tell me if any subset is jointly unsatisfiable, because i suspect the "silent failure" one and the "self-checking" one are in tension. also say which single requirement does the most work excluding candidate theories. skip scoring existing theories against the list, or keep it to a sentence each at most, i want the weight on the criteria. please write it in british english btw — behaviour, generalise, etc. that's just how my blog has always been and im not changing it now lol
