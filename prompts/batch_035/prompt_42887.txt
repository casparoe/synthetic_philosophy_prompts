Say Gaṅgeśa, of the Tattvacintāmaṇi, gets dropped into the present day, and everywhere he looks people are asking an LLM for answers — medical advice, homework, what have you. Being Gaṅgeśa, he immediately wants to know: is this pramāṇa or not? Walk me through what his considered position would plausibly be, argued from his actual commitments rather than "confused medieval guy sees computer."

Some threads I want you to actually pull on:

- Is an LLM's output śabda at all? The stock Nyāya story ties reliable testimony to an āpta — a speaker who knows the truth and intends to communicate it. There's no obvious āpta here: no knowing subject, no communicative intention, just token prediction over a corpus. So does the whole thing collapse into inference for him — "this source, being trained thus, is mostly right about thus-and-such"? Or does he say it's not even that, because the training data mixes reliable and unreliable speakers so thoroughly that you can't get a single probans off the ground?
- The spicy one: the Mīmāṃsakas argued the Veda is apauruṣeya and that's a feature, since no author means no author's defects — no lying, no ignorance, no bias. Could someone run an analogous move for model outputs — that being "authorless" in some sense screens off speaker-defects? Or does Gaṅgeśa say the Mīmāṃsā move only works for an eternally fixed corpus, and an LLM trained on fallible human authors inherits all their defects second-hand? Would he treat the training data as the real "speaker," with the model as just a transmission link, the way later Nyāya handled chains of reporters?
- How does his own machinery even get a grip on the case — the jñānalakṣaṇā / sannikarṣa stuff about how word-meanings reach us, his answer to the Buddhist who wants to reduce all testimony to inference. Does any of that decide anything here, or is this genuinely off the map for his apparatus?
- And his saṃśaya / tarka toolkit: when an LLM confidently contradicts a competent human informant, what should the procedure be? Whose word wins, on what grounds?

Dont just survey options — give me your best reconstruction of what he'd conclude and why, and flag clearly where you're extrapolating beyond anything the texts could settle vs. where his actual commitments do the work.
