The standard framing is that Mises argued rational calculation is impossible without genuine market prices, Lange answered with a model of market socialism using trial-and-error shadow prices set by a planning board, and Hayek then supposedly won the debate by shifting it from statics to dynamics — information, dispersed knowledge, and entrepreneurship.
