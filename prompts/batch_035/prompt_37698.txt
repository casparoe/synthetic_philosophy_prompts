- the grounding objection: what makes these counterfactuals true before the creature even exists and gets to choose? HR keeps asking where the data "comes from" and the last admin quit over it. the ad should offer the actual Molinist replies dressed up in corporate speak — soft facts, we read the entries rather than cause them, etc.

- the feasibility constraint: the planning division (upstairs) can only actualize worlds consistent with the catalog, and they keep asking the admin to quietly edit a few entries so a better world becomes actualizable. the admin's whole job is to say no — the counterfactuals are what they are, and management doesn't get to pick them either. god-like powers, not god-like permissions
