A friend and I have been arguing about the stone paradox for over a year now: if a being is omnipotent, can it create a stone so heavy that it cannot lift it? We have each dug in. My friend's position is that omnipotence simply means the power to do anything logically possible, so the question is malformed — a stone an omnipotent being cannot lift is not a possible object, any more than a square circle is, and no real limitation is revealed by the failure to make one. My position is that this quietly redefines omnipotence: a being genuinely without limits would not be hemmed in by our logic, and the fact that we cannot coherently describe such a being says something about our minds, not about its power. We have finally admitted we are talking past each other.
