ok so over the last couple years of thinking about this stuff on my own (mostly while walking the dog, if i'm honest) i've collected a set of views about where meaning comes from, and something about the set feels off but i can't locate it. here's roughly where i've landed:

1. the chinese room argument always seemed right to me: shuffling symbols around can't by itself produce genuine understanding, because the symbols don't mean anything to whoever's doing the shuffling.
2. real meaning has to trace back to conciousness. words on a page, road signs, the markings on a map — they only mean anything because conscious beings mean things by them. all of that is borrowed meaning, handed down from minds.
3. at the same time i've become pretty convinced meaning is basically use: what a sign means is fixed by what it gets used for. i got this from a wikipedia rabbit hole about ordinary language philosophy at 2am and it stuck.
4. i think my phone's maps app, in a small way, genuinly believes things — it believes i'm approaching the roundabout. that seems to me different in kind from the ink on a paper map, which i'd never say believes anything.
5. a machine could only ever have understanding of its own once it's conscious. no consciousness, no meaning of its own — at best borrowed.
6. my dog obviously believes things — the lead means a walk to her, she believes i'm about to feed her. that's real meaning even though she can't talk, and i don't see how it could be borrowed from anyone.

also, somewhere i read about a thought experiment involving an old vending machine whose coin slot gets converted to accept a different coin or token, and the author argues that what the machine "believes" about the thing you put in the slot drifts with what it's now being used for, rather than staying fixed by whoever built it — as i remember it, anyway. something about that felt right to me and i can't put my finger on why, which is probably a clue.

go through my list and tell me which of these actually pull against each other and which only look like they do — don't just flag everything, i want to know which tensions are real. rank them by severity, and for each real one say what would have to give and what that costs me elsewhere in the list. and if the whole set can't be saved, tell me which single view, if i dropped it, would clean up the most. i'm not attached to any particular one so don't go easy on me.
