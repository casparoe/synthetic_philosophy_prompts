As a novelist, my whole craft rests on the assumption that what people believe can genuinely cause what they do, so Weber's thesis that innerworldly asceticism helped produce the spirit of capitalism is professionally flattering — and I want to know whether I should actually believe it. I have been collecting my gut verdicts on when a cultural explanation does real causal work and when it is decoration, and I would like to know what principle, if any, they add up to. Weber's own case, ascetic Protestantism contributing to capitalism in northwestern Europe: causal, at least as far as I can tell a non-circular story without it. Explaining a particular self-made industrialist's fortune by "the Protestant work ethic": not causal, just a character sketch in academic dress. Honor culture among herding societies explaining regional patterns of violence: causal when it is tied to material conditions, hand-waving when it floats free of them. "A British culture of practical tinkering" explaining the Industrial Revolution: I distrust this one, because it feels like the outcome restated in the language of values. Mass literacy in early modern Sweden driven by the Lutheran command to read scripture for oneself: causal. A colonial official explaining the poverty of the governed by their supposed fatalism: not causal, and pernicious besides. The pair I cannot reconcile: I accept the Confucian-exam-ethos explanation for the obsessive educational striving I see around me, yet I bristle when a parallel story is told about Protestant self-discipline in my own family's history — one of these instincts must be prejudice rather than judgment.

Is there a principle that separates genuine value-causation from value-flavored restatement, and if so, which of my verdicts does it force me to give up? Please state it precisely, tell me what it predicts for a case I have not listed, and say what it implies for my fiction: can I honestly let a character's internalized creed be the cause of her fate, or am I, like the colonial official, merely decorating outcomes I have already written?
