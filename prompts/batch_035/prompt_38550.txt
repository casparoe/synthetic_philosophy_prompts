After a storm flooded my finished basement, my insurer denied the claim on the grounds that the loss was caused by my negligence: I had put off cleaning the gutters for months, and the policy excludes damage caused by the homeowner's own neglect. The trigger was plainly the weather — a record downpour that put water in my neighbor's basement too, even though her gutters are immaculate. On the other hand, it's very likely that with clear gutters the water would have sheeted off my roof and away from my foundation, and my basement would have stayed dry. So when the adjuster says my negligence "caused" the loss, is that the right way to put it, or is the storm the cause and the gutter a mere background condition? And should the answer depend on which question I'm really asking — what set this event in motion, versus which factor, if changed, would have prevented it? I'd appreciate help thinking through which framing is doing the work here.
