The statue and the clay are literally the same object described two ways, because two things can't be in the same place at the same time, so obviously there's only one thing there. Case closed, right?

So my actual question is: given that two things can't occupy the same place at once (which is basically a law of physics), why hasn't everyone just accepted the identity answer and moved on? Is there some knockdown argument against "the statue just IS the clay" that I'm not seeing, or is this one of those debates philosophers keep alive just to have something to argue about?
