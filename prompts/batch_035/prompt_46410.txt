I'm a longtime reader of the rationalist blogosphere, and after years of teleporter threads and sleeping-beauty flame wars I've drifted into the persistence literature and discovered I have actual views, which I've now written down for a discussion circle I attend. The trouble is that everyone in the circle keeps telling me my list can't all be true, and they disagree about which item to throw out, so I want a proper audit rather than another round of table-thumping. Here is the set. (1) I am an out-and-out endurantist: ordinary objects are wholly present whenever they exist, the very same object yesterday and today, and the four-dimensionalist picture of you and your childhood self as temporal parts of one space-time worm is, to my mind, bookkeeping masquerading as metaphysics. (2) I hold that change is real and intrinsic: my coffee was hot and is now cold, and its being cold is not some relation the coffee bears to this afternoon — if you tell me that "was hot" names a relation to a time, I think you have changed the subject rather than analyzed anything. (3) I am an eternalist with no residual sympathy for presentism, which strikes me as a view people adopt only because they have already decided they hate temporal parts. (4) I think coincident entities are a non-starter: where the statue sits there is exactly one object, not two objects sharing their parts and their location. (5) I hold, with equal confidence to (4), that the lump of clay would survive being squashed into a ball while the statue would not — different persistence conditions, full stop. (6) On persons, I think personal identity consists in psychological continuity, that identity is strictly one-one so that I would not survive a fission in which two psychological continuers walk out of the clinic, and that Parfit was nonetheless right that this shouldn't bother me, since branching is nearly as good as surviving.

My working suspicion is that item (3) is the saboteur, since the prevailing chatter in the threads I read says eternalism and endurantism are in natural tension and that presentism is endurantism's natural home — but I genuinely can't reconstruct the argument myself, so I may be scapegoating my eternalism out of fashion sense. And I can't shake the feeling that the statue and the lump are doing something strange in my set, though I also can't see how anyone could deny either half of item (5).

So: go through the six, tell me which pairs are genuinely inconsistent, which only look inconsistent, and which are merely uncomfortable bedfellows. Rank the tensions by severity rather than listing them as equally bad; for each real one, say exactly what would have to give and what it would cost me elsewhere on the list; and finish by telling me which single revision would clean up the most, if any is needed at all. Please don't lecture me about endurantism being unfashionable — I know the worm has the fan club these days, and I don't care. I care whether my list can all be true at once.
