My candidate list, though please push back if something's missing or overrated: (1) the reference machine problem — Solomonoff's prior is only defined up to a constant depending on the universal Turing machine, and everyone waves this off by saying constants wash out in the infinite limit, but no actual agent is at the infinite limit; is there a principled way to choose the machine, or a real argument that the choice can't matter for anything finite agents care about?
