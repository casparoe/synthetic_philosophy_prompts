okay so I've basically gone all-in on integrated information theory. what sold me originally was the lesion data: you can lose most of your cerebellum — enormous numbers of neurons — and remain fully conscious, while tiny lesions in the thalamocortical system can wipe consciousness out entirely, and IIT seems to be the only theory that actually predicted that shape of finding (the cerebellum is mostly feedforward-ish wiring, high differentiation but low integration, so its phi is tiny no matter how big it is). it also handles anesthesia and dreamless sleep in a natural way — integration collapses, phi collapses, consciousness goes — without any extra machinery bolted on. i'll admit the panpsychist stuff about the photodiode used to bug me but i've made peace with it.

recently tho I got rattled. first there was that open letter where 124 consciousness researchers called IIT pseudoscience. and then a friend told me that in the adversarial tests built to probe IIT, some simple system — a plain grid of logic gates, or an expander graph or something, I might be garbling the details — ended up with higher phi than a human brain. if that's right it sounds bad, but I don't actually know what it means for the theory.

what I want from you: what would genuinely count against IIT? not just "it implies weird stuff" — I want the actual defeaters. which possible empirical findings would hurt it (lesions it gets wrong, anesthesia cases, development, split brains, cases where phi and consciousness come apart in either direction), and for each one, would a committed IIT person concede it as evidence against, or explain it away? and be honest about the insulation question: between the move to the postulates framework, phi only being computed at the "right" macro scale, and the line that phi measures intrinsic causal structure so no external observer can ever directly test it — has the theory been qualified into something that can't lose? if so, is that a fatal problem for a scientific theory, or is it more like how we can't directly observe other minds and nobody thinks that's disqualifying? rank the defeaters from would-be-fatal down to merely awkward, and tell me which ones you think I'm currently underweighting given how I got here.
