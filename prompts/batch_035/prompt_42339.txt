(4) shrug it off as a rare edge case that doesn't touch the general claim about pain
(5) go pluralist and concede that pain is only pro tanto bad, defeasible by the absence of suffering

Give me the ranking as a numbered list of exactly five items, most promising first, with a sentence or two of justification for each — no introduction or conclusion. Also please write in American English.
