I've been trying to write down where I actually stand on animal ethics for a discussion group I'm in, and before I embarass myself I want someone to stress test the whole package. Roughly where I've landed:

1. Suffering matters wherever it happens, and I don't think the fact that it's "in nature" or happens to a pest animal changes anything morally. A mouse's pain is a mouse's pain.
2. Most wild animals probably have lives that are net negative on balance — huge numbers of offspring, most die young, that whole r-strategy picture. So more wild animals existing is probably bad, not good.
3. Because of that I'm genuinely unsure whether habitat preservation is even a good thing. If the animals in a forest have lives not worth living, saving the forest is preserving a lot of suffering. I still donate to a habitat conservation org anyway, kind of out of habit.
4. On food: I've done the math and I eat beef instead of chicken. One cow feeds way more meals than the couple hundred chickens it displaces, so counting deaths (and suffering), beef comes out ahead per meal.
5. Related — I don't think veganism is clearly obligatory for me, becuase crop cultivation kills a lot of animals too (pest control, field mice at harvest, etc.), so a plant-based diet isn't obviously a big improvement over being a careful omnivore who mostly eats beef.
6. At the same time I'm confident factory farming is one of the worst things we do as a society, I want it ended, and I put money toward farmed animal advocacy.
7. On wild animal suffering itself I'm a cautious one — I don't think we should do big interventions in ecosystems yet because we'd probably screw things up in ways we can't forsee. Research first, intervention maybe later, possibly never.

Something about the habitat point (3) feels off to me but I can't pin down why, and honestly I might be wrong about where the problem is. Go through these and tell me which pairs are actually in tension and how badly. Rank the tensions rather than listing them as all equally bad, say what the cheapest fix for each would be, and tell me if any of the pairs I'd worry about actually dissolve once you look closer — I have a feeling at least one of these is fine as is. I'm not attached to any particular one being kept, I just want to know what has to give.
