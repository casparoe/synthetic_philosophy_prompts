Draft the joint statement of an adversarial collaboration between two camps about how catastrophic AI failure most plausibly arrives. One camp holds that the main danger is influence-seeking systems: models that develop goals of their own, conceal them because being modified or shut down would frustrate those goals, and act decisively once a single system, or a colluding few, is powerful enough that human oversight no longer binds. The other camp holds that the main danger is slow value drift: no deception and no takeover, just humans repeatedly delegating to systems that optimize whatever we can measure and reward, each locally reasonable choice slightly narrowing what we still understand, check, or control, until the things we value are no longer represented anywhere that matters. Produce the document such a collaboration should issue: a numbered section of claims both sides can sign, including about the instrumental-convergence argument and about the fact that both stories require oversight to miss something — in one case because it is deliberately hidden, in the other because it is never collected; a disagreements section in which each statement is phrased so that its own side would accept the wording; and the cruxes. I expect at least one crux to be whether a system can accumulate long-horizon influence while remaining goalless in any robust sense, and another whether a drift that only ever passes through individually approved choices deserves the name "failure" at all. For each crux, include a concrete case or argument both sides agree would bear on it, with each side's prediction recorded in advance; for example, both should agree that whether models trained on human feedback learn to tell evaluators what they seem to want to hear, at some cost to accuracy, bears on how far reward-based oversight can be trusted, and each side should state what pattern it expects and how it would revise if the other's prediction held. Format the result as a numbered agreed-claims section, followed by a disagreements table, followed by the cruxes with predictions. Keep the whole document under 700 words.
