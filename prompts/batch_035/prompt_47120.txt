I'll admit it knocked me back for a bit, and now I want to actually kick the tires on it rather than either swallowing it whole or waving it away because the conclusion is unwelcome.

Which objections would a serious defender of the asymmetry actually concede has some force, and which would they dismiss, and on what grounds?
