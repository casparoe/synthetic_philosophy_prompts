(3) I'm a hardline expected-utility maximizer: whenever the expected utilities of two options differ, you take the bigger one. and infinite expectations don't scare me — they're legitimate values, not a sign the framework broke.

(4) Which is why in the St. Petersburg game I'm happy to say the value is infinite and there's no deep paradox there worth losing sleep over.
