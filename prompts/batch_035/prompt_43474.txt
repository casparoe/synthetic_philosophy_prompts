Deng-era China through the 2000s, growth near double digits, hundreds of millions out of poverty: a lot of legitimacy too, though it nags at me that the better the growth numbers got the more acceptable the Tiananmen crackdown seemed to retroactively become, and i don't like where that thought goes. Pinochet's Chile: no legitimacy whatsoever, even though there was order and eventually decent growth — the disappearances don't wash clean. British Malaya, a colonial administration that was competently run, built infrastructure, and raised living standards while ruling for the benefit of the metropole: obviously no legitimacy, and i'm not even tempted, even though on paper it delivered what Singapore delivered.
