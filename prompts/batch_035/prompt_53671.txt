My suspicion is that this architectural contrast is the surface of something deeper, namely a temporal question about when commitment to values happens: goal-direction bakes the commitment in at design time, approval-direction postpones it to run time. If that's right, the interesting question is not which architecture to build but which desirable properties of approval-direction survive the collapse, and whether they can be secured by other means.
