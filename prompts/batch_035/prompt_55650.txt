So on her telling, a miserable life is like a bad gift you actually gave, a happy life is a present you merely chose not to buy, and the asymmetry just falls out of ordinary gift ethics.

Audit the analogy for me. Lay out the mapping explicitly — what plays the role of the giver, the recipient, the gift itself, the harm — and then show me where it breaks.
