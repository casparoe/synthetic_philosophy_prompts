so this is a slightly odd request but here goes. my sister's boyfriend Will has been living with us since last month (long story, inbetween flats), and he is, for want of a better word, an evangelist for the idea that if you care about animals, the single worst thing you can put on your plate is chicken. not mainly because of how broiler birds are treated — though he'll happily go on about that too — but because of the arithmetic: one cow feeds you for weeks, one chicken feeds you once, so per meal chicken racks up vastly more deaths. and he counts the animals that die for my dinner without ever reaching a plate — the mice and other field creatures shredded at ploughing and harvest — mostly on the crops side of the ledger, which conveniently loads things further against anything that has to be farmed intensively. he's not stupid about it: he'll concede without prompting that beef has a terrible climate bill, that a beef herd's land use is enormous, and that the cow's own life is no picnic. but he will not budge an inch on the core claim, which he insists is just arithmetic, not philosophy.

I want to get better at pushing back on this over dinner without it turning into a row, so please play him. genuinly sharp but fair — no strawman, and he shouldn't be a rationalisation machine either: he believes it, he knows the numbers cold, he'll concede small points but never the main one, and he talks like a person at a kitchen table rather than a pamphlet. keep each reply to a paragraph so it feels like a conversation, and stay in character until I say stop.

here's my opening move. last night I told him: fine, I'll grant that per calorie, chicken kills more animals than beef — you can have that. but two things. first, you count lives like tokens: you never say whether a chicken's few miserable weeks weigh the same as a cow's couple of years, and if they don't, your tidy arithmetic quietly changes shape. second, if it really is just about minimising deaths per meal, your ideal protein should be the largest animal on earth — one blue whale could feed the whole street — yet your extrapolation always stops precisely where your barbecue begins. which is why I can't help thinking it's your appetite doing the philosophy and you supplying the footnotes.

go.
