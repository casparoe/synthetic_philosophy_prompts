(4) Marcus said that by that standard no safety engineering counts, since every safety mechanism can be misused by its operator; and he pressed her on the alternative: whoever implements "value alignment" has to pick the values, so it's intent alignment all the way up with extra steps — except now the intent belongs to whatever committee or lab happened to build the system, which she should find scarier, not more reassuring.
