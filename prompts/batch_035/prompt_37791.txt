I've been reading about chaos theory and something keeps bugging me. Everyone says chaotic systems like the double pendulum are deterministic but unpredictable, since any tiny error in the initial conditions blows up exponentially (Lyapunov exponents and all that). But to me determinism and predictability sound like the same thing said two ways: if the current state fixes the whole future, then a computer loaded with the exact state should just be able to crank the future out, and if even in principle that can't be done, doesn't that really mean the system isn't deterministic after all? Is "deterministic but unpredictable" just a fudge physicists use to cover up the fact that determinism failed?
