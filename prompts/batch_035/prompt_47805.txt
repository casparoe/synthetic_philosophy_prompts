Which argument survives its own objection better, and why? And where do they genuinely come apart — one is about what you should do with your life, the other about what you should believe about the world — because I suspect my character conflates them precisely because both let him skip the evidence step, and I'd like to know if that's actually a smart move on his part or just sloppy thinking. No reading lists, he's a dinner-party character, he doesn't cite.
