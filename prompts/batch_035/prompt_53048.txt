A charity ad built around one named child rather than statistics: fine with me, even though it works on my emotions rather than my reasoning. A teacher exaggerating praise of a struggling student to build her confidence: fine. An airline emailing me "you've put so much into this miles program, don't let it go to waste" to keep me from switching carriers: not fine.
