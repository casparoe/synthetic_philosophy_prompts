The idea is that if I accept coincident but distinct objects, then wherever there's a person there's also a distinct object occupying exactly the same region, sharing all the same intrinsic properties, made of the same particles. So isn't there a second thinker sitting in my chair right now, having my thoughts? Someone in class said the aggregate "thinks" only in some derivative or secondhand sense, but honestly I couldn't follow what that derivative sense is supposed to be — if it has all the same intrinsic properties as me, why isn't it just as conscious as I am, full stop?

But I don't really get how that helps. My TA tried an analogy with two students who have identical grades on every assignment but only one passed because of a grandfather clause in the grading policy, and I can see that distinct objects can differ in non-intrinsic properties, but mentality doesn't seem like the kind of thing that should depend on historical facts the way "being a statue" does — the statue's persistence conditions matter for whether it survives squashing, but I don't see how they could matter for whether something is aware.

Can you walk me through this carefully?
