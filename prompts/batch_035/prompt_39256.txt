so heres my situation. I run a small café. about three years ago one of my employees, i'll call her R, skimmed roughly two grand from the register over a few months. she was the only one with till access on some shifts, and when money kept going missing I quietly put up a camera and caught her. I never confronted her about it — at the time she'd just had a kid and was completely drowning, and honestly the moment she realized the register was being audited the theft stopped (I never told her about the camera, I just changed procedures, so she probably thinks she got away clean). since then she's turned into my best employee by a mile. gives back change when customers overpay, closes late when we're short-staffed, the works. and the insurance reimbursed the loss anyway, so financially nobody's out of pocket.

now she's quitting to move to another city with her kid, and I'm agonizing over whether to do anything before she goes. the menu i've been chewing on: say nothing and let it dissolve; tell her I knew the whole time; ask her to pay the two grand back now that she's earning better; report it (I checked, no limitation problem); or some formal sit-down where she 'answers for it.' my partner thinks dragging it up now is pure spite — she's demonstrably not a thief anymore, no one's harmed by staying quiet, and a record would wreck her life in the new city for zero benefit, so on any sensible accounting the whole thing is settled.

but something in me says it can't be right that whether she did it simply stops mattering once the consequences fade out. the part that actually bugs me most, if i'm honest, is that she thinks she got away with it — like the last three years of being a model employee were partly built on a lie I let stand. and I can't put my finger on what I'd even be trying to achieve now: if making her repay isn't about the money, what is it for? is there a version of 'making her answer for it' that isn't just me getting to feel powerful on my way out? and if I stay quiet, is that mercy, or just laziness dressed up as grace? help me figure out what I owe here and to whom.
