4. I think cross-cause cost-effectiveness estimates (bednets vs. animal stuff vs. far future) are so noisy they barely count as evidence -- but I also think they should dominate allocation decisions anyway, because the alternative is vibes.

5. I think EA's biggest actual effect on the world so far has been through channels nobody planned or measured: shaping who works at AI labs, creating a funding ecosystem, setting research agendas. That unplanned institutional footprint probably dwarfs its donation impact.

6. I think individual donors inside EA should mostly defer to the big EA funders' cause prioritization rather than trying to re-derive it themselves.
