My friend Nadia did her phd on medieval universities and now she has very strong opinions about whether historical periods are real things or stuff historians made up later, which she deploys mostly at parties. Her track record so far, from years of arguing with her:

- the Middle Ages: real. she's fine with the fact that the name was invented by people who were basically rubbing it in (you guys are the middle, we're the rebirth), the period underneath it is a genuine thing.
- the Renaissance: not real. a 19th-century invention, basically Burckhardt's doing, and underneath the label it's just several different things in several different places at several different times that got glued together afterwards.
- the Scientific Revolution: real, even though almost nobody at the time knew they were in one and the label itself is basically 20th-century.
- the Dark Ages: obviously not real, a polemical slander with nothing underneath it.
- the twelfth-century renaissance: real. do not test her on this.
- "early modern" as a period: a pure filing label. real as a drawer, not as a thing in the world.
