Here is my opening move. You told me last night that illusionism about consciousness is self-defeating, because an illusion of an experience would itself have to be an experience — a fake dollar that is somehow also a real dollar. And every piece of evidence you offer for the phenomenal properties — your reports, your discriminations, your felt convictions that the redness is really there — is itself a functional state we can describe without remainder.
