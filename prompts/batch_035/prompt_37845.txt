Two camps in the dispositions literature have been arguing past each other since the 1990s: the friends of the conditional analysis, and the critics armed with finks, masks and antidotes. Draft the joint statement a good-faith adversarial collaboration between them should produce.

By the first camp I mean the reformed conditional analyst. They concede that the simple conditional analysis — a glass is fragile if and only if it would break if struck — is refuted by Martin's electro-fink (a live wire connected to a device that would render it dead just before anyone touches it, so that it would not shock despite being live) and by masked objects (a fragile glass swaddled in styrofoam, which would not break however it is struck though it has lost none of its brittleness). They now defend a reformed analysis: Lewis's strategy of analysing disposition ascriptions comparatively, with clauses to the effect that nothing independently connected to the manifestation may interfere, and that the grounds of the disposition must be intrinsic to the object.

By the second camp I mean the critic who holds that these cases show something deeper: that the counterfactual connection between stimulus and manifestation is at best an effect of a disposition, never what the disposition is. On their view, dispositions are grounded in intrinsic properties — categorical or primitively dispositional, as they may disagree amongst themselves — and no reform of the conditionals will capture this.

Produce the document both could sign: (1) a numbered section of agreed claims, including about the electro-fink and styrofoam cases themselves, about the failure of the simple conditional analysis, and about whatever else they genuinely share; (2) a disagreements table, each row stating one residual disagreement in wording that a member of the camp in question would accept for her own view; (3) for each disagreement, the underlying crux — the sub-question such that settling it would move at least one camp — and a concrete case or argument both agree bears on it, with each camp's prediction recorded in advance. Neither side may define its terms so as to win by fiat; and where a camp would be unmoved by any case whatsoever, the document should say so rather than manufacture a decisive test.

To keep them off the well-trodden ground, the collaboration must record predictions on the following cases, which I supply:

(a) A stochastic fink: a fragile glass wired to a device that, if the glass is struck, tosses a fair coin and removes the glass's brittleness on heads, in time to prevent breaking. Is the glass fragile? Fragile to degree one half? What verdict does the reformed analysis return, and what happens to Lewis's comparative claims — is this glass more or less fragile than an ordinary one?

(b) An intrinsic mask: a fragile-looking glass given a shock-dispersing coating, molecularly bonded to its surface, so that it would not break if struck. If masks must be extrinsic to the object masked, does the intrinsicness clause classify the coated glass as fragile? Is the clause principled, or a device for saving the analysis by fiat?

(c) An ensured manifestation: a glass that would break if struck, but only because a vigilant guardian has vowed to shatter it the instant it is struck, by unrelated means, whether or not its own structure would have sufficed. Both camps presumably deny this is fragility; do their respective accounts deliver that verdict, and if not, what does the failure show?

(d) Probabilistic dispositions: a radioactive atom disposed to decay within the hour, with probability one half. There is no fact of the form that it would decay if left alone; at most it might. What does each camp's account predict here, and is this a fink, a mask, or a distinct embarrassment?

Conclude with a section separating the substantive disagreement from the verbal one: in particular, whether the semantics of disposition ascriptions in ordinary language can be settled independently of the metaphysics of powers and properties, and whether settling it would move either camp at all.
