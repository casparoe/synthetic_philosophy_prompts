Give me a number and the full decomposition, including at least these terms: (1) the probability that there exist, or will exist, genuinely empirically equivalent rivals that are not merely notational variants or ad hoc embroideries of a single theory; (2) the probability that, given such rivals, none of the virtues philosophers standardly invoke — simplicity, unification, explanatory depth, fecundity — is truth-indicative rather than merely pragmatic; and (3) the probability that no possible future evidence could break the tie even in principle.
