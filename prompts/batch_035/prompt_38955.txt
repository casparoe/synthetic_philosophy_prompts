I supervise doctoral research in philosophy of mind, and I am helping one of my students prepare a dissertation prospectus for departmental review at the end of the term. She has a half-formed idea that I find promising but not yet in prospectus shape, and I would like a full draft to react to — both to give her something concrete to push against and to see how the project looks when it is stated as carefully as it can be.

The idea begins with Hofstadter's careenium, from I Am a Strange Loop: a billiard table on which vast numbers of small magnetic balls, called symms, careen about, clumping and unclumping; the recurring higher-level formations, whose dance appears to track and respond to the world outside the table, are the symmballs. Hofstadter uses the picture to dramatize the question of who pushes whom around inside the system: read from up close, the symms do all the pushing, and the symmballs are mere patterns in their motion; read from afar, the symmballs appear to do the pushing, with the symms serving as their medium. My student suspects that this question, as usually posed, equivocates between three distinct questions, and that the equivocation is what makes the debate look insoluble:

(1) an overdetermination question: are there effects for which symmball-level events are causes over and above the symm-level events, or is the higher level always exhausted by, or preempted by, the lower?
(2) a proportionality question: even if all the causal work is done at the symm level, are the symmball-level facts the more proportionate causes of certain effects — the level at which the right generalizations live — so that symbol-level explanations earn their place without doing any extra causal work?
(3) a steering question: whether symbol-level structure constrains the substrate's dynamics over time, the way the shape of a riverbed constrains the water without pushing it — a relation she is inclined to call formal or constitutive rather than causal.

Her further hunch — and this is the part I am least sure deserves the space she wants to give it — is that the interpretability of neural networks supplies a live test case for reading (3). When researchers identify an internal feature of a model whose state predicts the model's outputs and, when perturbed, systematically redirects them, the natural description is that the feature steers the computation. But it is equally open to say that this is merely a compressed redescription of weight-space dynamics, and she wants an argument that these are not just two verbal presentations of the same fact.

Write the prospectus. Specifically, I need: a precise statement of the dissertation question, and an account of why, once the three readings are separated, the question remains open rather than merely postponed; the method, chapter by chapter, including which new thought experiments she should construct — cases built in the careenium spirit rather than the stock examples everyone reaches for — and why a philosophical rather than empirical or formal method is the right one here; what a positive result would look like, and the honest conditions under which the project should be counted a failure or judged to have collapsed into a mere taxonomy of senses of cause; and a statement of scope that a department can assess as three years of work, with an indication of what could be cut first if it runs long. Do not include a bibliography or a citation apparatus; we will supply references ourselves. Describe positions and arguments on their merits, and attribute them only where the attribution is load-bearing.

One last request, and I mean it. If you think the three-way distinction does not survive scrutiny — that, say, steering and proportionality collapse back into causation once stated precisely — or that the interpretability material is decoration rather than a genuine test case, do not produce a dutiful prospectus around a premise you find confused. Tell me plainly where the idea goes wrong and what a better prospectus would look like, even if that means telling me my student's project should be rebuilt from the ground up.
