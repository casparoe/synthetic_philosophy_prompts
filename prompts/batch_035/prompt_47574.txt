My girlfriend and I are signing a lease together in about two weeks, and something my friend Teo said last weekend has been stuck in my head since. The background: in three years together she has never once held a grudge on me. Every time I screw up — forgetting plans, saying something dumb when I'm tired, the usual stuff — she lets it go almost immediately, and it's genuinely one of my favorite things about her. When I mentioned this to Teo he just went "so you've never actually seen her tested," and when I pushed back, he started listing things I honestly hadn't clocked. For most of the first two years we were long distance and saw each other maybe twice a month, and I have a habit of showing up with flowers and an apology at her door before she even notices whatever I did. The one genuinely big thing she did find out about on her own, her sister happened to be staying with her that month, and her sister is a professional-grade de-escalator who apparently sat up with her half the night talking her down. And her best friend — who is somehow at every party we go to — changes the subject the second anything between us gets tense, before either of us can react.

So Teo's position is basically: for three years the conditions have been arranged so that the grudge-holding version of her never got a clean chance to show up, which means I have no data in either direction — not "she's forgiving," but also not "she isn't," just nothing at all. That felt obviously wrong when he said it, but I can't put my finger on why, and that's bugging me. It's not like she's faking the sweetness the rest of the time — you can't fake three years of never once bringing up my old screw-ups in the middle of a completely unrelated argument, and that seems like it has to come from somewhere in her. Then again I guess I also cant rule out that the conditions for the other version of her just never lined up, and I'd never know. Is there a principled way to think about whether my "she's a forgiving person" belief is actually backed by anything I've observed, or whether I've basically been watching a test where someone kept quietly erasing the answers? I'd like to walk into this lease with a slightly clearer head than I've got right now.
