The audience will be roughly: two senior forecasters who are evangelical about reference-class forecasting and the outside view (the school that holds that major projects go over budget because planners reason from the inside view and that the cure is anchoring on a larger class), one Bayesian statistician who believes hierarchical partial pooling dissolves the whole issue, one junior researcher from the existential-risk side who keeps the Doomsday Argument in her head and insists that in anthropic contexts reference class choice is not a modeling convenience at all but a matter of which observers you count yourself among, and a sprinkling of generalist forecasters who mainly want to know whether any of this changes their workflow.
