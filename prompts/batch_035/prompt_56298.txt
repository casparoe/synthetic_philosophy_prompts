2. I think numbers count in rescue cases. If a lifeguard can save either five strangers or one stranger, they should save the five, and not merely because of some side-effect like gratitude or policy.

4. I'm fine with redistributive taxation: when the worse-off are genuinely badly off, taxing the well-off is justified because the gains to the worse-off outweigh the losses to the well-off.
5. I'm pretty sceptical of the doing/allowing distinction — when intention and outcome are held fixed, I don't think it matters much whether you did the thing or merely let it happen.
