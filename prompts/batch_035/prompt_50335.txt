Can you unpack the distinct precise claims the slogan could be making — everything from the near-trivial (the Universal Declaration says something along these lines) up to the radical (private landlordism is illegitimate full stop) — and for each one say whether it's plausible, and crucially which readings support which camp? Ideally give me a list I can bring next time people quote the same sentence at each other from opposite directions.
