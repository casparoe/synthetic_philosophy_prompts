Restate the historiographical debate in my vocabulary: treat the period labels as candidate change points in a multivariate series of intellectual, technological, and institutional indicators, with the standard worries about granularity, lagged and gradual breaks, multiple competing segmentations with similar fit, and overfitting through too many change points.
