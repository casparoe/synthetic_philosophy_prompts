Dev said that's backwards: nothing isn't a rival hypothesis that "lost out" to being. There was never a lottery between existence and non-existence, so asking why being won is confused at the start. Then it went like this. (2) Dev said "nothing is simple" already smuggles in what he's denying: calling it simple treats nothing as a state things could be in, but nothing is the absence of everything, so it can't be a default any more than it can be a non-default — there's no fact there to be either. And he added that any "why" question needs a cause or a law to lean on, so demanding an explanation of the existence of explanations is asking for something that couldn't exist. (5) Maya said fine, maybe every chain of explanation has to stop somewhere — but then the honest answer is "brute fact," not "bad question," and Dev kept sliding between those two without flagging it.
