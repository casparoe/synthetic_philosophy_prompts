(5) The same applies at every level: the criterion used at level n is itself selected using a criterion at level n+1.
(6) The regress must terminate somewhere, and it can only terminate where the agent optimizes rather than satisfices — for at the bottom something must simply be best, not merely good enough.
(7) But by (4) a bounded agent cannot optimize anywhere; and if nothing terminates the regress, no decision ever gets made.
(8) Therefore either no boundedly rational agent ever decides anything, or satisficing covertly presupposes an optimizer somewhere inside the agent.
