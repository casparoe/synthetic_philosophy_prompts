I feel like everyone treats the simulator argument as a settled result: a base LLM is just predicting text, it can't have goals of its own, and any agency we see is a character its playing, nothing more. But if thats right, why do the same people turn around and worry about mesa-optimisation and inner objectives in trained models -- isn't that just reifying the fictional character again? Seems like the theory gets used to have it both ways, depending on whether the wanted conclusion is "LLMs are safe" or "LLMs are dangerous". Where exactly does the inference from "it's a simulator" to "it's not an agent" break down, if it does?
