Say we break it into five candidate mechanisms: (1) costly signals — demanding public displays of faith that screen out free-riders; (2) supernatural monitoring — the sense that a punishing agent is watching even when no human is; (3) ritual synchrony — chanting, bowing, marching in step at collective services that fuses individuals into a group; (4) circle expansion — universalizing creeds that extend moral concern beyond kin and tribe; and (5) what I've been calling moral rehearsal — habitual practices like confession, repentance, sabbath-keeping that work like drills for prosocial impulses. Ignoring whether religion is true, rank these five from 1 to 5 by how much real moral behavior each could plausibly generate, with an eye to which ones would collapse if you stripped out the supernatural content entirely. Give me exactly five numbered items, one per mechanism, each a sentence or two, no intro or conclusion.
