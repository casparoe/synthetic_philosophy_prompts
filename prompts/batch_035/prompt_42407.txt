The question I keep hitting: does my own existence count as evidence for B? But something about this feels like cheating. So the "random pick" view seems to tell me to bet that humanity doesn't go on for trillions more, and the "counts for nothing" view lets me shrug that off entirely. I can't tell which instinct to trust, because each one cures the other's disease.
