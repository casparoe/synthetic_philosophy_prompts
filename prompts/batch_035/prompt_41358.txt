so I help moderate a hobby forum, been doing it maybe 6 years, around 40k users, and no I dont have any philosophy background, this is just me and the other mods going in circles for two weeks. one of them said our situation sounds like that popper paradox of tolerance thing and I honestly cant tell if that fits, so I want to lay it all out.

first, our track record, because whatever we decide I want us to be consistent with what weve already done:

- permabanned a user who kept posting that a religious minority should be rounded up and removed from the country. no third warning on that one.
- did NOT ban a whole thread of people saying truly vile stuff about that same minority — slurs that got past the filter, stereotypes, the works — because there was no call to action, just hate. we got alot of complaints about that call.
- banned an entire offshoot subforum when it came out they were organizing mass-report campaigns and brigades against a rival subforum.
- permabanned a user who posted another user's home address.
- left alone a long-running thread where people calmly debated eugenics-ish "human biodiversity" stuff, even though half the mod team wanted it gone. it stayed inside the rules on paper.
- permabanned a far-left user for saying a right-wing pundit's house should get burned down. so I dont think we're one-sided about this.

ok, the new case. we have a group of users, maybe 25-30 of them, who are absolutely polite on site. never break a rule, never even swear. but screenshots leaked from their private discord showing they're coordinating: they're getting their people nominated for the elected mod seats (our users vote to fill 3 of the 7 mod positions), and once they have a majority the plan is to rewrite the rules so they can ban the users and threads they hate. one of them put it pretty bluntly in the leak, something like: we're not breaking your rules, we're using them better than you do. so far they've done nothing bannable on the forum itself. the vote is in three weeks.

what I want to know: if we're trying to stay consistent with ourselves, what do we actually do here? preemptive ban? disqualify their candidates but let them keep posting? nothing, and let the vote happen? which of our earlier calls is the closest analog to this one, and if you think our track record genuinely doesnt settle it, then what principle would we be taking on board with each option — and where does that principle bite us later, like what kind of user would we be unable to touch (or forced to ban) five years down the road if we go each way? also, is this even the paradox of tolerance, or is it something else wearing that hat? I keep thinking it matters whether tolerance is a personal virtue or more like the forum's operating system, and whether you can be tolerant toward people whose explicit stated project is uninstalling tolerance.
