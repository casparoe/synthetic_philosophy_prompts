For the past eight months I've been writing to someone I met while traveling. Neither of us speaks the other's language, so everything passes through a translation app. Last week she ended a message with a saying the app rendered into English as an elegant proverb about slow rivers; when I mentioned it to a colleague who speaks her language, she laughed and said the original was much plainer, almost shy — the app had simply swapped in a different proverb with roughly the same meaning. It struck me that the sentence I'd been charmed by was never one she wrote. Since then she has told me she loves my way with words, and I feel the same about hers, but I can't shake the thought that neither of us has ever actually read a word the other wrote. Are the two of us talking to each other, or are we each having a separate conversation with the app that happens to run in both directions? I'd like to know whether something real is passing between us, or whether, in a sense, we're both just corresponding with the machine.
