My sister is on a medication for a chronic skin condition that carries a real, though not huge, risk of serious birth defects, and her doctor told her that if she wants to conceive she should switch to a safe alternative and wait three months to clear it out first. She and her husband want a child soon and she hates the idea of months on the worse treatment, so she floated just trying now and accepting the risk. At a family dinner two of her friends, Maya and Jonah, got into it about whether that would actually be wrong. Maya went first: knowingly conceiving under an elevated risk of a defect that could mean a lifetime of surgeries and pain is just wrong, full stop — if she waits, she gets a healthy child instead. Jonah said the word "instead" is smuggling in a fiction: the child she'd conceive after waiting would be a *different* child, not the same child cured. The child conceived now, if their life is worth living, has no complaint — "you should have waited" translates to "you should never have created me," which is a complaint nobody is entitled to make if they'd rather exist. Maya shot back that by this logic the risk just evaporates once the die is cast, and that it would also let our whole generation off the hook for depleting the planet, since on that reasoning even the future people harmed by depletion owe their existence to the very choices that harm them, so they can't complain either, which she found monstrous. Jonah said yes, he'd bite that bullet, but not in the way she thinks: the depletion outcome is *worse* without being *worse for anyone*, and the mistake is conflating "a wrong occurred" with "a particular person was wronged" — wrongs don't need victims, and he accused Maya of needing a face for every harm. Maya's last word was that she cannot so much as understand a wrong with no one wronged — worse than what, for whom? — and that Jonah's view seems to make it permissible to create a barely-worth-living life even at very high risk, which she said no one actually believes when you push on it. My sister asked me afterwards what to think and honestly I couldn't referee, though I suspect some of the fight is verbal.
