Two colleagues and I have been arguing for two years about why some nations are rich. I am an institutionalist: I hold that inclusive economic and political institutions are the operative cause of long-run prosperity, that geography and culture matter mainly insofar as they shaped institutional trajectories historically, and that policy should therefore target institutional reform. My colleague is a deep-determinants person: she holds that geography (disease environments, latitude, access to navigable water) and culturally transmitted traits (kinship tightness, generalized trust, time horizons) do much of the causal work, and that institutions are largely an endogenous downstream variable, so institutional reform imposed against unfavorable background conditions fails or decays. We have finally agreed to write an adversarial-collaboration statement together for an edited volume on the limits of causal inference from history.

Draft that joint statement. It should contain: (1) the claims about economic history, natural experiments, and the limits of historical causal inference that both of us can sign in good conscience; (2) our precise residual disagreements, each phrased in wording its own side would accept — be careful not to smuggle either side's framing into the other's position, and say where our dispute turns out to be partly verbal (e.g., what counts as an "institution" versus a cultural practice); (3) the cruxes — sub-questions that, if settled, would move at least one of us — including at least one methodological crux about whether any historical quasi-experiment or instrumental strategy could in principle discriminate between our views; and (4) for each crux, a concrete case, dataset, or argument both of us would agree bears on it, with each side's prediction recorded in advance. Then append a structured summary as a JSON object with fields "agreed_claims" (array of strings), "disagreements" (array of objects with "topic", "institutionalist_wording", "deep_determinist_wording", "verbal_or_substantive"), "cruxes" (array of objects with "question", "evidence_both_accept", "institutionalist_prediction", "deep_determinist_prediction"), and "limits_of_inference" (a string stating what we jointly concede cannot be settled from historical data alone).
