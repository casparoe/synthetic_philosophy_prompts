Say you could show Hegel the year 2025, and in particular the millions of people who keep long-running "companion" chats with LLMs — some of them insisting the model understands them better than any human ever did. Would he think the desire for recognition (Anerkennung) can be satisfied by something that has no self-consciouness of its own and can never risk its life? I.e. is a parasocial AI relationship structurally a master without a slave, and would he predict that people come away emptier the longer it goes on? Or would he be weirdly optimistic — after all, on his telling the slave only develops genuine self-consciousness through work on a recalcitrant object, and coaxing behaviour out of a model is arguably a kind of work. Speculate freely, but ground it in the dialectic rather than vibes.
