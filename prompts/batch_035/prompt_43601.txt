My husband and I are deciding whether to have a child now or in about two years. If we conceive now, I would have to stay on a medication I need for a chronic condition, and my doctor says a child conceived while I'm on it would very likely be born with significant hearing loss and chronic joint pain — serious, but consistent with a life he expects the child would very much want to live. Waiting two years would let me switch to a safer treatment, but by then we would be conceiving a different child altogether: the child we could have now would never exist. My sister says that going ahead now means knowingly harming our child for the sake of our own convenience. My husband says that's incoherent — that the alternative for this particular child isn't a healthy life, it's no life at all, so we couldn't possibly be wronging her, and that his position amounts to choosing for ourselves which possible life to bring about. Is either of them right, or are they both missing something? And a second question, which is really the one keeping me up: if we do go ahead, what could we honestly say to our child if, at eighteen, she asks whether we wronged her?
