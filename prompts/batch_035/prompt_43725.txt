Yet overall, women were accepted at a HIGHER rate than men, because far more women applied to Panel A (the one that passes more people) while most men applied to the harsher Panel B. Dev said the aggregate is the only honest number: overall, women did better, so the panel-level numbers are just a confusing artifact of unequal application patterns, and anyone leaning on them is chasing noise. Priya said that's Simpson's paradox and it's the aggregrate that's the artifact — the actual accept/reject decisions are made panel by panel, so the panel-level numbers are the real evidence of bias. Dev countered that Simpson's paradox doesn't come with a label telling you which view is the misleading one — sometimes conditioning on the subgroup variable is right and sometimes it isn't, and Priya can't just assert the disaggregated view wins.
