Write the dialogue between two characters who both know their stuff: a gene's-eye-view person who thinks organisms and groups are just the genes' way of making more copies, and a multilevel selection person who thinks selection can genuinely operate on groups, like when bee colonies or primate groups with more policing/punishment outcompete more conflict-ridden ones. Maybe set it while they're watching an ant colony together. Neither should be a strawman, and don't let the dialogue secretly declare a winner.
