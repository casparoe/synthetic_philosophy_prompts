In my reporting on science I reach constantly for the word "emergent," and I have gradually noticed that my usage tracks a set of gut verdicts I have never managed to articulate. I would like to know whether there is a principle behind them, and if not, which of the verdicts has to go. I call the temperature of a gas emergent and mean it as more than a convenience of description — I write about heat as a real feature of the world, even though I accept it is in principle derivable from molecular motion. I call a wave of congestion propagating backward along a motorway emergent as well: no individual driver contains it, yet it has dynamics of its own. But the center of mass of the solar system, which is equally a quantity computed from the parts, I would never call emergent — it strikes me as mere bookkeeping. The average height of a room's occupants is, I think, obviously not emergent; yet the price of a stock, which is likewise nothing but an aggregation of individual trades, I keep wanting to call emergent, as though the price knows something no trader knows. Superconductivity seems to me the strongest case of all: a regularity that appears at a scale where nothing in the microscopic physics advertises it. On consciousness I simply waver — my materialist commitments tell me it must be like temperature, but it does not feel like temperature, and I cannot decide whether that difference in feel matters. Can you state a principle, presumably about levels of organization and the autonomy of higher-level description, that captures these verdicts, or a small set of candidate principles if one will not do? Please tell me which of my cases motivates each clause, and apply the result to two cases I have not mentioned: whether a murmuration of starlings is emergent in the same sense as the traffic jam, and whether the fitness of an organism is emergent in the same sense as temperature. Finally, tell me honestly whether the principle that best fits my verdicts commits me anywhere to strong emergence — that is, to something that is not even in principle derivable from the base level — or whether, on reflection, all my cases can be accommodated by a weaker notion.
