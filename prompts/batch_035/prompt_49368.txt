Science journalist here, and there's a pattern I keep running into that I cant find the word for. Two experts agree on every single fact, and will even admit it, but they keep fighting over whether some label applies. Whether obesity counts as a disease, whether an e-sports player counts as an athlete (that one decides visa categories in some countries), whether an AI system "authored" a work (that one decides copyright). If you push them, they'll say look, I know it's just a word, but whoever loses the word loses the money, or the visa, or the rights. Meanwhile a second group in the room dismisses the whole fight as "just semantics," as if settling the word would settle nothing. And there's a third group that doesn't want the word reassigned at all — they want the category retired: stop asking whether addiction is a disease, the disease frame is the problem. So: does this whole package have an established name in philosophy? If there isn't one term, then don't just list candidates and leave it to me — commit to whether this is one phenomenon or several glued together, and say where the edges are, i.e. what separates it from an ordinary factual dispute and from a definitional squabble with no consequences. A bare name won't help me; the carving is what I'm after. And take a stance on whether the "it's just semantics" dismissal is ever right in these cases, or always a mistake.
