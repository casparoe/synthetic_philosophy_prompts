So I'm a retired condensed matter physicist (35 years on superconductors, dont ask) and since retiring I've gotten really into metaphysics, and the grounding literature is driving me a little crazy. Everyone just seems to assume the chain has to stop somewhere, that there's a fundamental level at the bottom, whereas in physics we were always fine with "it's turtles part of the way down and we'll worry about it later." For a bit of fun: write a job advertisement, around 600 words, posted by Reality itself, for the position of Fundamental Level. Play it deadpan as corporate HR copy (responsibilities, requirements, compensation, equal-opportunity boilerplate), but have the fine print quietly take sides in the actual disputes: whether the successful candidate is allowed to ground themselves, whether applications from coherentist circles will even be opened, what the contingency plan is if the position goes unfilled (endless descent? zero-grounding? quietly relabel the top and hire from within?). It should be funny to someone who knows the grounding debate but readable to someone like me who only knows reduction from the physics side. And I'd love it if the ad never quite manages to say what grounds the authority of the hiring committee itself -- that's the part I actually loose sleep over.
