My kid's school putting up a poster ranking the ten worst atrocities in history: gross, that shouldn't be a poster. A politician saying some border policy is exactly what the Nazis did: obscene. A vegan at dinner saying industrial farming is in the same moral category as the Holocaust: I actually think this one's allowed, though I hate how the conversation always goes after he says it. An anti-abortion person saying abortion is the modern Holocaust: obscene, full stop. My uncle saying his month in county jail was basically the gulag: fine, everyone knows he's mostly kidding. A podcaster saying Hiroshima was worse than most Nazi war crimes because of the single-event death toll: fine, I think, though my stomach isn't sure. And me privately thinking at least the victims of X died quicker than the victims of Y when I'm reading: I do this constantly and don't feel bad about it at all.

Is there a principle that gets all of these?
