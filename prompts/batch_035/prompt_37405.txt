write me a fake customer-support FAQ page, the kind youd find on a SaaS company's help center, but the company is "the Middle Way Helpdesk" — a fictional service where users file tickets about views and positions that are giving them trouble (their view of the self stopped working, their belief in intrinsic essence keeps crashing, etc). about 650 words, numbered questions from users with deadpan corporate replies, plus an "escalation policy" section at the end.

the target of the satire is Madhyamaka — nagarjuna's school, the tetralemma, emptiness as lack of svabhāva (intrinsic nature). the humor has to come from getting the actual doctrine right, so someone who's read the real texts would find it fair, while someone who only knows "buddhism says things are empty i think" can still follow it. things that need to be in there:

- at least one question about whether something exists gets run thru all four options of the tetralemma — it exists, it doesnt exist, it both exists and doesn't, it neither exists nor doesn't — with all four denied, but written so it reads like normal troubleshooting steps ("step 1: try the first option...").
- "emptiness of emptiness" should show up somehow, maybe as a clause saying the warranty is itself covered under the warranty, or that tickets about emptiness get the same treatment as any other ticket
- one user accuses the helpdesk of contradicting itself (if you deny every position, isnt that a position?) and the reply has to wriggle out without the helpdesk actually holding a thesis — the internal consistency is part of the joke, so dont let them slip
- a disclaimer section clarifying that emptiness is not nihilism: things do function, they just dont exist from their own side. dependent origination (things arising in relation to causes/conditions) should be doing real work in one of the answers, not just name-dropped
- somewhere a customer complains the advice made everything worse, and the reply invokes the classic simile about grabbing a snake by the wrong end — blame the grabbing, not the snake

full corporate deadpan throughout, no winking at the reader, the reps never break character. typos in the customer tickets are fine but keep the official replies polished, thats part of the voice contrast.
