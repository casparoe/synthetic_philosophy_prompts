Is there a name for the predicament I'm in, or a bunch of names, or what? I finally sat down with the Twin Earth thing (Putnam, where the lake-filling stuff over there is XYZ instead of H2O but otherwise behaves the same, so their word "water" means something different even tho their heads are identical to ours). And ok, I get the standard lesson, meaning isn't all in the head. But what actually keeps me up at night is this: if that's right, then there could have been a switch. Say I get moved to Twin Earth in my sleep and nobody ever tells me. From the inside nothing changes — my introspection reports exactly the same thoughts as before — but now my word picks out the other stuff, and I can't tell by any amount of reflection which one I'm currently thinking about. That's not ordinary ignorance, like not knowing the chemistry of water. It's a fact about my own ongoing thought that's invisible to the one person having the thought. My thoughts have a feature I'm apparently doomed to be wrong about or at least unable to confirm, and that feels different from just "meaning is external."

And then there's the mirror version, which bugs me just as much. If I can know a priori what I mean by "water" — which sure seems like something I can do from the armchair — and what I mean depends on what's in my local environment, then it looks like I can know without looking that there's water-ish stuff around me, which seems like magic. I can't get a priori facts about the world for free like that.

So: does this whole tangle have an established name? I'm pretty sure it's not just "Twin Earth" again, since that's a story about what words mean and mine is about what I can know about my own head, and it's not plain old ignorance either. A friend mumbled something about "self-knowledge" but that sounds way too vague to be the thing. If there are separate names for the two halves (the can't-tell-after-a-switch thing and the free-environmental-knowledge thing), tell me what they are, how they're supposed to relate, and where the edges are — and if nobody's named it, name it, because right now it feels to me like one single thing. Also someone once told me about a thought experiment where lightning fashions a molecule-for-molecule duplicate of a guy, and I have a feeling it connects somehow but I can't see how.
