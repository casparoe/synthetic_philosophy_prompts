So I got into an argument about Pluto, of all things, and it's been bugging me ever since. You know the 2006 IAU thing: a planet has to orbit the sun, be massive enough to pull itself into a round shape, and have cleared the neighborhood around its orbit — that last one is what did Pluto in. But here's what got me. Nothing about Pluto itself changed between 2005 and 2006. Same object, same composition, same size. What changed was facts about other stuff — the Kuiper belt population, Neptune's orbit. So the defintion seems to presuppose that what kind of thing an object is can be grounded in facts about its surroundings rather than in the object itself. Is that a fair reading? And the weirder part is that the same community doesn't do this everywhere. Stars get sorted by intrinsic stuff — mass, temperature, composition — and whether a star has companions doesn't matter. Meanwhile a body that orbits another body instead of the sun is a moon, even though Ganymede and Titan are both bigger than Mercury. And technically the 2006 criteria only covered things orbiting the sun, so exoplanets weren't even planets under the letter of the rule. So astronomers classify one kind of object by what it's made of and how heavy it is, another kind by its dynamical relationships, and then draw the moon/planet line by what the thing orbits rather than how big it is. What theory of categories — of what grounds a thing's kind membership — does this patchwork actually presuppose, if any? Could one consistent view license all of it, say a view where kind membership is always grounded in relations, or does the practice only hang together as a pragmatic convention, where planet is like downtown, a word that carves nothing at nature's joints? And if you insisted that kind membership has to be grounded in intrinsic properties, what would have to change — does Ganymede get promoted, does Pluto come back, and do the star categories need redoing too? I'd also like to know which parts of the practice really pin down one view and which parts are compatible with several. Sorry for the typos btw, typing fast on my phone.
