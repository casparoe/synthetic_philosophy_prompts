me and my flatmate have this running argument about whether you experience things live or whether the brain assembles it all afterwards and hands you the finished product. she's given verdicts on little cases over the years and i've been writing them down (yes, i know). her record so far:

1. you half-hear a sentence, the last word makes it click what was actually said, and afterwards you'd swear you heard it right all along. she says you DID hear it right — the experience only got created once the sentence finished, your brain waited and put it out complete.
2. someone taps you twice on the wrist then twice near the elbow, fast, and it feels like taps hopping up your arm in between. she says you genuinely felt the hops, at the moment you felt them, the brain just paused the feed and staged it.
3. a long vivid dream that seems to go on for ages. she says none of that was experienced in real time, the brain wrote the whole story in the second before you woke up and inserted a fake memory of having lived through it.
4. driving home on autopilot and remembering nothing of the trip. she says you were definately conscious the whole way, it just didn't get saved.

#4 sticks out to me but i can't tell if that matters. she also insists that somewhere in the head there has to be a moment where it all comes together for you, a finished show that somebody watches. that feels like a seperate belief and i want to know if its doing any work in the other verdicts.

next month the argument comes back up because a friend described this illusion to us: a red disk flashes on the left of a screen, then a green disk flashes on the right, and it looks like one disk gliding across and turning green partway — even though nothing is ever shown in the middle. i'm planning to ask her about a version where the person watching has a button and is told to press it the instant they see anything, and the second disk hasn't come up yet. what was that person conscious of in that moment — a plain red disk? nothing at all? something else?

predict what she'll say, and give me your best guess with reasons. but honestly i care less about the answer than about: (a) what single rule she seems to be running that fits all four verdicts, (b) what other rules fit her record just as well, and how the button case would split them apart, and (c) if her record can't decide the button case, what case would. i was thinking of asking her about someone who shouts out the color mid-illusion, then afterwards swears the disk turned green in the middle — does the shout show what they were conscious of at that second, or not? would that settle it, or can her "it all comes together somewhere" belief absorb anything?

last thing — be honest with me if the question itself is somehow confused. like if "what were they conscious of at that exact instant" might just not have an answer, and if so, point out which of her verdicts already quietly assume it does.
