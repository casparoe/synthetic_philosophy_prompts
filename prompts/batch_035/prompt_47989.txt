so my consciousness reading group wants everyone to write up a philosophical profile that the others can poke holes in, and before I submit mine I want a consistency audit. here's the package, as honestly as I can state it:

(1) There's no Cartesian theater — no place in the brain where everything comes together for an inner audience — and Dennett's multiple drafts picture is basically right: contents get processed and edited in parallel, and a content counts as conscious roughly when it starts winning the competition to influence memory, report, and behavior.

(2) Color phi and the cutaneous rabbit show that postdiction is real: the brain fills things in after the fact and experience gets temporally smeared, which is why I think the Orwellian and Stalinesque interpretations of phi both miss the point — there's no fact of the matter about whether the second dot really looked red before you probed the subject.

(3) Libet's results are genuinely bad news for free will. The readiness potential starts well before you're aware of your intention to move, so conscious intention shows up too late to be doing the causing. I take this to be a real finding about conscious will, not just an artifact of averaging or reporting.

(4) That said, for any given token percept there IS a determinate fact about when it first became conscious, even in postdiction cases — it's just that our methods might be too crude to pin it down. Consciousness doesn't literally have fuzzy edges at the moment-to-moment level.

(5) I buy the grand illusion stuff: change blindness and the poverty of peripheral vision show that the rich, detailed visual field we think we have is largely confabulated.

(6) But when it comes to the timing of my own experience, I trust my introspection over any external measurement. In a dispute between the subject's report about when they felt something and a machine's timestamp, the subject wins, because they're the only one in a position to know.

(7) And to be clear I'm not an eliminativist — there's obviously something it is like to be me, experience is real, I just deny the theater model of it.
