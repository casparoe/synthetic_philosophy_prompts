I'm a journalist who writes about cognitive science and AI, and over the past few years I've drifted toward a view I associate with Douglas Hofstadter's work on analogy and creativity: that coming up with something new is, at bottom, variation-making. On this picture, a concept is a structured representation with adjustable dimensions, and creativity consists of fiddling with those knobs until a variant pops out that is novel yet still coherent -- the way a composer nudges a theme's rhythm, key, and contour until a new melody snaps into place. The reason the same person can write a fugue and crack a joke is that both are knob-twiddling over deep internal representations, and genius is largely a matter of having especially rich representations to twiddle and knowing which knobs to leave alone. I've found this framing congenial, but I want to stress-test it before I lean on it in print. Tell me what would genuinely count against it. Which observations, arguments, or experimental results should move me -- from cases of radical conceptual novelty that seem to require representations the twiddler never had, to worries that if all novelty is parametric variation then nothing explains why some variants are creative and others are merely random? Where does the view stand or fall relative to its rivals: constraint-based accounts, sociological accounts, and the idea that creativity is primarily analogy-mapping rather than variation-making? Which of these defeaters would Hofstadter himself concede as counting against the view, and which would he dismiss as missing the point? And be honest about whether the view has been qualified into unfalsifiability -- whether "knob" has been stretched so far that any change to any representation counts as twiddling, and whether that elasticity is a fatal problem or just the normal looseness of a framework at this level of description. I'd especially like to know where recent AI behavior cuts: systems that fluidly interpolate between concepts are doing something knob-twiddling-like at scale, so does their output count as evidence for the view, or does the gap between their fluency and anything we'd call genuine creativity expose a missing ingredient the theory never accounted for? Please rank the objections from fatal to merely awkward.
