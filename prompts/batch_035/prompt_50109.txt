The question (three parts, equal weight):

(i) Explain the skeptical paradox about meaning that Kripke's Wittgenstein raises, using the quus case: why does no candidate fact about my past seem to determine that I meant plus rather than quus?
(ii) Kripke says no straight solution to the paradox is available. Explain what a straight solution would be, and why the dispositional analysis of meaning plus fails to be one.
(iii) Explain the skeptical solution offered instead: in what does my meaning plus consist, and what role does the community play?

The student's answer:

"In the quus case, imagine I've never added numbers bigger than 56. Let x quus y equal x + y when both are below 57, and 5 otherwise. Everything in my past — my computations, my explanations of plus, my mental images, any meaning intention I might have had — is compatible both with my having meant plus and with my having meant quus. So when asked 68 + 57, someone who meant quus should answer 5 and someone who meant plus should answer 125, and nothing about my past decides between them. So there is no fact that determines the answer.

A straight solution would exhibit some fact about my past whose truth-conditions make it the case that I meant plus and not quus. The dispositional analysis is the natural attempt: to mean plus is to be disposed to answer 125 to 68 + 57, 2 to 0 + 2, and so on. Kripke gives two reasons this fails. My dispositions are finite while plus has infinitely many instances, and I am disposed to make mistakes — for enormous numbers my actual dispositions give wrong outputs, so they track something quus-like at the extremes rather than plus.

Since no straight solution works, Wittgenstein accepts the skeptical conclusion: there are no facts about what anyone means. His solution is that meaning is fixed socially. What makes it the case that I mean plus is that the community of speakers agrees in treating 125 as correct and trains and corrects us accordingly. This is a straight solution at the level of the community: the community's agreement is the fact that constitutes my meaning. Since the community means plus, I mean plus. This is also why a private language is impossible — without a community there is no one to supply the standard of correctness."
