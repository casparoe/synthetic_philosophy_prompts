After a career in physical chemistry — the better part of thirty years running a spectroscopy laboratory — I have come late to philosophy of mind, and I have been trying to write down what I actually believe about whether a machine's internal states can mean anything. Before I circulate it to the reading group I have joined, I would like an audit of the following commitments, because I suspect at least one of them is doing quiet work that the others forbid, and I cannot see which.

1. I am a thoroughgoing physicalist about meaning: for a state to mean something is just for it to stand in the right sort of causal and informational relations, and there is no barrier in principle to a machine having such states.

2. I take the symbol grounding problem, as I understand Harnad to have posed it, to be a genuine and unresolved difficulty for any system trained only on text. Its words, if they mean anything, mean it only on loan from the humans whose writing it consumed; nothing in the system has ever been in touch with the things the words are about.

3. At the same time, I hold a broadly Wittgensteinian view of meaning as use: to grasp a term is to be able to deploy it correctly across contexts, including novel ones. I have been impressed by language models that reason correctly about the behaviour of a compound under conditions absent from any text they could have read, and if mastery of use is what meaning is, I cannot see what could still be missing.

4. I do not think embodiment or interaction is required for grounding — only causal contact of the right sort. My test case is a model trained from scratch on nothing but raw particle-detector telemetry, never a word of English. I am confident that its internal states would be genuinely grounded in muons, even though it is a passive reader of records and can never act on anything. (I am aware that a seismograph trace is also causally coupled to earthquakes without meaning anything, but I cannot say what the difference is beyond complexity, and I am reluctant to let complexity be the whole story.)

5. Finally, I believe meaning supervenes on internal organisation: an exact functional duplicate of a system means exactly what the original means, whatever its environment.

Please go through the pairs, tell me which tensions are real and which merely look like tensions, rank the real ones by severity, and say for each what would have to give and what giving it up would cost me elsewhere. Then identify the single revision that would clean up the most. I would rather be told which of my convictions is indefensible than be reassured that they can all be kept.
