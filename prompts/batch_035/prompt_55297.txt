We're about to commit a real budget to feed changes, and I want a document both factions could sign beforehand. Draft the joint statement of an adversarial collaboration between them: the claims both factions accept (about what users actually see, whom they trust, and what our logs can measure); their precise disagreements, each phrased so its own faction would accept the wording; the cruxes — I suspect one is what happens when a user is shown a well-sourced post from the other side, and another is whether "never saw it" versus "saw it and dismissed the source" is even distinguishable from behavioral data; and, for each crux, a concrete experiment we could run on the platform, with each faction's prediction about its outcome recorded in advance. Set it out as agreed claims first, then the disagreements, then the cruxes with predictions. Finally, tell me whether any part of their dispute is merely verbal — a fight over the labels "bubble" and "chamber" rather than over our users.
