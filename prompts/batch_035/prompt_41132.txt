my history seminar keeps coming back to one image for nazi germany: hitler as a spark, weimar as dry tinder. the prof uses it in two directions at once and i only just noticed. (a) against great man theory: the tinder was so dry — mass unemployment, a humiliated middle class, conservative elites who'd take a strongman over the left any day, a constitution with emergency powers just sitting there — that if this particular spark hadn't landed, another one would have. no hitler, same fire, maybe a different shape. (b) for great man theory: sparks are cheap but there's no fire without one — take hitler away and all that tinder just sits there, maybe smoldering for decades, maybe eventually a smaller flare-up that burns out. so the same analogy gets used to prove the leader was replaceable AND that he was necessary.
