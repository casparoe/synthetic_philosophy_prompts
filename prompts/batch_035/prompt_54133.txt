My co-host thinks the whole Ellsberg business is a laboratory artifact, the kind of thing people shake off once you make the stakes repeat, so I want questions that take her view seriously rather than treating it as the standard 'humans are irrational, file under anomalies' line I've done to death in other episodes.

Please draft nine or ten interview questions that go beyond getting her to restate the view. For each question give me a follow-up keyed to the reply she's most likely to give, since she's apparently very good at agreeing that the intuition is strong and then quietly declining to cash it out.

Format: split it into sections in this order — 'Cold open' (one framing question, short), 'The urn problems' (three questions), 'Rationality or psychology' (three questions), 'Real stakes' (two or three questions about decisions like mine, career moves, insurance, health choices where the probabilities just aren't there), and 'Closer' (one question about what evidence could actually count against her view). Keep each question to a few sentences of setup plus the question itself, and flag in brackets after each follow-up which standard reply you're assuming she'll reach for. Also, please don't suggest readings or papers I should have her comment on — I want the questions usable without any prep beyond the episode itself.
