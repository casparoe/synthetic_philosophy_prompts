Please argue for the following position: the money pump argument does not actually establish that rational preferences must be transitive. Focus on the standard cycle case — an agent who strictly prefers A to B, B to C, and C to A, and can be pumped by a trader who offers a sequence of tiny payments for each swap, returning the agent to its starting option with slightly less money. I want you to make the case that the pump only goes through because of a hidden assumption about how choices are made over time: that the agent decides at each opportunity separately, with no regard for where the sequence of trades is heading — what people sometimes call naive or myopic choice. Then sketch an alternative agent, one that evaluates whole trade sequences up front and can bind itself to a plan (or at least refuses offers that are part of a pattern leading back where it started with a loss). Argue that this kind of agent keeps its intransitive prefferences and still keeps all its money, so no book can be made against it — which means the pump, on its own, never shows intransitivity is irrational; it shows at most something about how an intransitive agent should structure its choices over time.

Then deal with the obvious comeback: that this 'resolute' agent is irrational in a new way — it turns down individual trades it (ex ante) would prefer to make, or its self-binding is ad hoc, or the trader can just find a longer path around its commitment rules. Explain why you think that cost is either not real or not obviously a cost — don't just wave at it, give the actual reasoning. Also be precise about which version of the pump you are addressing: the one-shot pump, the repeated pump where the trader can circle forever, and the versions where the agent is allowed to reconsider or renegotiate earlier offers are not all equally easy to dodge, and I want you to be honest about which ones your story handles cleanly and which are the hard cases. No citations or reading list needed, just the argument itself. But make sure the argument as stated would actually move someone who initially accepted the pump at face value, not just someone who already thinks acyclicity is overrated.
