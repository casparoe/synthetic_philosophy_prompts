(3) Generalizing: for any theory and any finite body of observations, you can construct incompatible rivals that entail exactly the same observations -- just take a copy of the theory and bolt on a clause saying the world was set up so that every observation comes out as seen. (5) The same holism applies over time: any recalcitrant result today can be absorbed by tinkering with an auxiliary, so strictly speaking nothing is ever refuted, only abandoned.
