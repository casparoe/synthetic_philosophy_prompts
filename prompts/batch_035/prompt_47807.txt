Two weeks into a new lease I've discovered my apartment has a serious German cockroach infestation, and paying an exterminator has made me realize I can't tell whether my settled views are consistent with each other. For a seminar on applied ethics I'm supposed to submit a short statement of my personal position, and I'd rather be corrected now than in front of the class. Here is the set: (1) I'm an ethical vegan, and my ground for that is that causing suffering or death to sentient creatures for the sake of convenience or pleasure is wrong. (2) I hold that sentience is what grounds direct moral concern, that uncertainty about sentience should be handled by weighting by probability rather than by writing the animal off, and that insects carry a non-trivial probability of some form of valenced experience. (3) I'm anti-speciesist: if a cockroach and a mouse differ in moral standing, that must be because of differences in their capacities or the interests those capacities ground, never species membership itself. (4) I hired the exterminator without hesitation and think I was clearly permitted to; if someone told me the right response to an infestation is coexistence, I'd regard them as confused. (5) I believe the manner of death matters a great deal — I condemn glue traps for mice as gratuitously cruel, and a child burning ants with a magnifying glass for fun strikes me as a small-scale moral crime — yet I have spent no time comparing whether gel bait, boric acid, or simply sealing the cracks kills more or less painfully, and I don't feel any need to. (6) I believe it makes a genuine moral difference that the roaches are in my living space — something like a defensive prerogative over one's home — since I would have neither the right nor the inclination to poison the roaches living in the vacant lot next door. Please audit this set: which pairs of commitments genuinely pull against each other, which only look like they do, how severe each real tension is, and what the cheapest fix for each would cost me. Then tell me which single revision would clean up the most. I honestly don't know which of these I would give up, so don't pull your punches on any of them.
