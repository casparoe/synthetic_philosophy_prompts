His counter is that I keep moving the definition so that nothing could ever count as evidence against me.

So here's what I want from you, and please think carefully before answering rather than just picking a side. Act as a philosopher of science who is good at diagnosing the structure of disputes. Diagnose it for me: is it substantive, verbal, or two questions under one name?
