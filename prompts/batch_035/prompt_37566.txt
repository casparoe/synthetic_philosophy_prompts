Lately I've been noticing a certain kind of behavior and I can't figure out if there's a proper name for it. You watch an animal, or a person, or a piece of software do something that looks genuinely smart — a whole coordinated sequence of steps that fit together, as if it understood what it was doing. Then you perturb one small thing about the situation, and it turns out the whole performance was on rails: the creature (or program, or person) runs the exact same sequence anyway, even when it's now pointless or self-defeating, and shows no sign of registering that anything changed. The impressive part was real — the routine really is well-designed — but the designer isn't in the room, so to speak. It's intelligent-looking and rigid at the same time, and the rigidity is invisible until the world deviates even slightly from what the routine assumes.

Some examples, since I'm probably not describing it well. I'm a software engineer, and our office has a little delivery robot that once made a beautifully smooth detour around a chair someone left in the corridor, and then, two minutes later, stood waiting at a door that had been propped open all morning — its routine was apparently arrive at door, wait for the door, and no amount of standing there re-triggered anything. My father-in-law's farm cat runs an elaborate begging routine at the food bowl at exactly 6pm, complete with the same three moves in the same order, even when the bowl is already full. And a friend of mine, an extremely careful cook, once added salt twice in a row because the recipe's steps assumed a pan setup she wasn't using — she executed each step perfectly and never once looked at whether the steps still made sense in her kitchen. Same shape in all three cases: looks clever, can't be bargained with, and once you see the rigidity you can't unsee it.

So — is there an established term for this exact configuration? Multi-step, apparently purposeful behavior that runs without any monitoring of whether it's still appropriate? I don't mean a plain reflex or instinct, those are single responses to a trigger, not routines; and I don't quite mean habit either, since habits are learned, and at least the cat seems to have been born with the whole routine intact. If there is a word for it I'd love to know where it comes from, and how people who use it draw the line between the real thing and near misses (things that look rigid but turn out to be flexible once you test them properly). And if there's no established term, I'd honestly be happy with a well-made-up one, as long as you define it precisely enough that it excludes stuff that only superficially resembles this. Don't just give me a bare label — I want to know what the category includes, what it leaves out, and where the edges sit.
