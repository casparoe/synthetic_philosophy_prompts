Half the group basically sides with Devlin: society legitimately gets to enforce its shared morality through criminal law even when nobody's harmed. The other half is with Hart: you have to keep moralism separate from harm-prevention and paternalism, and widespread disgust is not by itself a reason to lock people up. I want you to write the joint statement our group should have produced by now — the document from a good-faith adversarial collaboration. Specifically:

2) A disagreements table. Each row has to phrase the disagreement in words each side would actually accept for its own view — so no "Devlin wants a morality police" and no "Hart is soft on sin." 3) The cruxes. 4) If part of the dispute strikes you as verbal — say, over what counts as a harm, or whose morality counts as "society's" — say which part and why.
