This is why compensatory education has limited room to work: the gains from programs fade, and children's scores drift back toward the level their genes set. Heritability also lets you make predictions within families — identical twins share all their genes, so if one has an IQ of 130, the other has a fifty-fifty chance of getting there too."

Something about it smells off to me, but each claim on its own sounds plausible enough. Keep the whole thing under 400 words, and at the very end tell me what would change your mind — what evidence, whether from twin studies, adoption studies, or intervention follow-ups, would flip your verdict on any of these claims, and where your own audit is most likely to be wrong.
