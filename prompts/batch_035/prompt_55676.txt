Trying to figure out what I actually believe about killing bugs and pests, going by gut reactions, and honestly its a mess. Stomping the spider in the shower because it startled me: fine, don't love it, but fine. Walking that same spider out in a cup instead: obviously nicer, but I don't feel obligated. Ant bait under the sink where the workers carry poison back and the whole colony dies over a couple weeks: fine, even though it's way more total death than squishing the ten ants I can actually see. Fumigating the whole house for termites: fine. Gassing a wasp nest under the eaves: fine. But those yellow sticky traps for earwigs where they just sit there stuck until they starve: somehow not fine, even though it's fewer deaths than the ant bait. Bug zapper on the porch that also fries moths and beetles: not fine, feels wasteful. Releasing sterile male mosquitoes to crash the local population so there basically aren't any next year: fine, even kind of elegant. The same trick used on the crickets chirping outside my bedroom window just because the noise bugs me (pun intended): not fine, even though no individual cricket even dies. Preventatively treating the lawn for grubs I've never actually seen: not fine, wierdly. Big roach in the kitchen gets the spray, zero hesitation. And the one I keep flip-flopping on: mice in the garage, snap trap vs those glue boards.

So is there a principle under all this? My first thought was "it's about how much harm the killing does," but that can't be right given the ant bait thing. Second thought was "it's about why you're doing it" (protecting your house/health vs. convenience), which handles the mosquitoes vs. crickets pair, but then I'm not sure why the crickets-vs-mosquitoes thing tracks anything real about the crickets. Third thought was something about the manner of death being quick vs. slow, but that seems to cut against how few deaths I'm actually causing with the glue traps. Give me your best candidate principle, tell me what it predicts for cases I haven't listed (like, does it say anything about buying produce grown with pesticides that kill alot of nontarget bugs, or about driving, which must be a bloodbath for insects at scale?), and be honest about which of my verdicts it just can't save and I'd have to give up.
