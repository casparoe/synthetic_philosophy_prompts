My problem is the mirror-image view, the one Parfit himself pushes at various points, that psychological continuity is all there ever is to survival anyway, so nothing of any importance was lost, and my whole framing collapses into cheap tragedy. So: what would count against my view that the teletransported character dies? And please tell me which objections a defender of my view would concede as real trouble versus wave away as intuition-mongering, and whether the view can be qualified into something unfalsifiable and whether that would even matter for a metaphysical claim like this.
