i've been in a free will reading group all semester and next session everyone has to write up their actual position so the rest of the group can poke at it, and when i try to write mine down i realize i genuinely don't know if it's one position or three. here's my list: (1) determinism is very likely true, at least for human decision-making; (2) i'm an incompatibilist about free will — if determinism holds, no one ever could have done otherwise in the sense libertarians want, and agent causation seems like magic to me. part of what convinces me is that indeterminism doesn't help either: if you replayed the universe a hundred times and the choice came out differently each time, that's just luck, and luck isn't freedom; (3) but i'm a compatibilist about moral responsibility — determinism by itself doesn't undermine blame or credit, what matters is that you act from your own values, deliberate normally, respond to reasons; (4) i accept the Frankfurt-style case: if a neuroscientist would have forced Jones to do X had Jones shown any sign of restraining himself, but Jones in fact does X entirely on his own, Jones is responsible — so alternate possibilities aren't required for responsibility; (5) at the same time i feel pretty strongly that a man whose whole motivational system was covertly engineered from early childhood by a research team — so he wants to commit the murder, plans it, deliberates, responds to reasons, isn't coerced in the ordinary way — is NOT responsible for it, even though he passes every test i endorsed in (3) and (4); (6) and i'm a retributivist about punishment, i think some people really do deserve to suffer for what they've done, and that's not just a convenient story we tell ourselves for deterence's sake.

go through the list and tell me which of these are genuinely in tension and which only look that way — ranked by how bad the tension is, please, not a list where everything comes out equally guilty. for each real one, tell me what would have to give and what giving it up would cost me elsewhere on the list. one thing i want you to check specifically: the other compatibilist-sympathizer in the group keeps telling me (2) and (3) flatly contradict each other, and i keep thinking that's a confusion on his part — but i'm starting to worry that however i get (2) and (3) to coexist, it quietly sabotages (5), or maybe (5) quietly sabotages (6). i honestly can't tell which. i'd rather know where the real pressure is before the group finds it for me.
