I'm a PhD student in philosophy, and I'm TA-ing / co-designing the final for an upper-level seminar we teach called something like Rationality and Choice (mix of phil of action kids and decision-theory kids, maybe 20 students). We've covered akrasia twice over the semester: once the classic stuff (Socratic denial that you can knowingly do worse, Davidson's puzzle about how it's possible) and once the behavioral-economics angle (hyperbolic discounting, preference reversals, Ainslie on interests bargaining with each other across time). The problem is that our students have gotten very good at reciting both literatures separately, and I want a question that forces them to collide the two instead of just displaying them side by side.

So the question I have in mind is roughly this: does modeling akrasia as hyperbolic discounting vindicate Socrates or refute him? If the procrastinator is just a bundle of time-indexed preferences where the short-range one happens to win when its moment comes, then there's no conflict between judgment and desire at all — Socrates was right that nothing was ever knowingly done worse, just wrong about why (ignorance, no; unstable preferences, yes). But if that's right, it starts to look like the akratic person has been explained away rather than explained: maybe Davidson's puzzle dissolves into a scheduling problem. I want the students to have to say whether the discounting model redescribes the phenomenon or eliminates it, and what would even count as evidence between those two readings. To keep it concrete, the question should give them a mini-case to work with — I'm imagining a grad student, call her Maya, who genuinely endorses finishing her chapter this week, sincerely, not self-deceptively — but who at 9pm Friday always prefers the bar, and who knows this about herself well enough that she's started handing her laptop to a friend on Fridays. So she's both the akratic agent and the legislator of her own commitment device, which I think makes things nicely weird: is Friday Maya coerced by Wednesday Maya? Was she ever akratic, or just outvoted?
