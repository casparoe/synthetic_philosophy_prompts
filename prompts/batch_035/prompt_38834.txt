my labmate Maya is the person who decides, for our anomaly monitor, whether two kinds of inputs get processed by "the same mechanism." the deal is that if some new input type runs on the same mechanism as the training inputs, the monitor lets it through, and if not it gets flagged. she keeps a rulings doc and there's a new case coming up next week that I want to predict before she calls it, partly to check whether I actually understand her rule (or whether there is one).

her rulings so far:
- factual questions asked in english vs in spanish (model is bilingual): same mechanism
- copy-the-previous-token behavior in plain prose vs in code (induction heads doing it in both): same mechanism
- classifying a tweet as negative vs classifying a restaurant review as negative: same mechanism
- two-digit addition done in one forward pass vs with an explicit step-by-step scratchpad the model writes out: different mechanism
- "what's the capital of france" answered from the weights vs answered from a passage sitting in the context window: different mechanism
