The crux as she framed it: Nash's solution is the unique one satisfying Pareto efficiency, symmetry, invariance to affine rescaling of utilities, and independence of irrelevant alternatives (IIA). The standard worry about IIA is a case where part of the pie quietly disappears in a region that was mostly benefiting one player: IIA says the bargain stays exactly where it was, which looks unfair, and individual monotonicity is built precisely to block that.
