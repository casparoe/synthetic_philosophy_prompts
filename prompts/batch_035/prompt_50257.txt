I lead the modeling group at a public hospital network that is about to deploy a risk model over discharged patients in order to allocate a scarce resource: a few hundred home-nurse visits per week against tens of thousands of discharges, so the model's real job is to rank. Before we start arguing about criteria, I want the specification. Write the desiderata that any adequate fairness requirement for this deployment must meet, with the case behind each. At minimum the list should speak to: within-group calibration of the scores, since clinical staff will read a score of 0.3 as a 30 percent readmission probability and will notice if that reading is only true for one group; equality of error rates across our two major patient groups, including which error rate actually matters when the harm at stake is a missed beneficial visit rather than a wrongful flag; the fact that our outcome label — thirty-day readmission — is a proxy for deteriorating health that is itself partly a function of prior access to care; the fact that our training data is selectively labeled, since outcomes are observed only for patients the old nurse-visit program happened to flag, and those flags followed that program's own priorities; and the fact that the intervention changes the outcome it predicts, so a "risk" here is really a counterfactual. Where relevant, distinguish requirements on the scores themselves from requirements on the decisions made with them. Then do the part I most need: identify which subsets of these desiderata are jointly unsatisfiable once the groups have different base rates — you may treat the established impossibility results connecting calibration, error-rate balance, and base rates as given, but do not simply restate them; show what they do in this setting — and say what the proxy-label and selective-label requirements add on top, including whether anything survives once they are combined with calibration. Mark each requirement as non-negotiable for this deployment or as a preference we could trade away, given that the purpose is the fair allocation of a beneficial intervention rather than accurate prediction for its own sake, and tell me which single requirement eliminates the most candidate criteria and what remains if we insist on it. No literature review: I want the requirements, the tensions, and the verdicts, not a reading list.
