Benatar asserts the asymmetry on sheer intuition and offers nothing further in its defense, so the whole argument rests on a premise his opponents can simply deny. But if he's right, the conclusion is stark: no one's life is worth living, and existing people would be better off dead — which is why antinatalism, if true, would commit us to working toward human extinction."
