AC turns out to be equivalent to several other famous statements, including Zorn's lemma, the well-ordering theorem, and the continuum hypothesis. Most working mathematicians therefore avoid AC where they can, relying instead on the weaker countable axiom of choice, which is provable in ZF and suffices for ordinary analysis.'
