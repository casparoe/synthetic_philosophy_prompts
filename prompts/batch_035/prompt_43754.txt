I push back: the specific things that made it Nazi Germany rather than just a nasty right-wing military dictatorship — the charismatic mass-movement politics, the ideological fixation that drove the war and the Holocaust — seem to track Hitler specifically, and its not like history hands you those leaders off a shelf. There's also the counterfactual problem: you can't rerun 1920s Germany, so what does "would have" even mean for a one-off event?
