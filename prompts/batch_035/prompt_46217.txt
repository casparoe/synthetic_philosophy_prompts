I'm a PhD student in political science, working mostly on comparative electoral systems, and I'm drafting the intro chapter for my dissertation on electoral reform debates. The more I read, the more I notice that people arguing for proportional representation over first-past-the-post (or the other way around) basically never state what an electoral system is even supposed to *do* — they just pick their favorite property, proportionality or constituency service or whatever, and run with it. So before I wade into the empirical literature I want the full spec written down: what would an electoral system for a national legislature actually have to get right to count as adequate? I'm thinking things like translating votes into seats in a way voters can recognize, not punishing people for voting sincerely, keeping a workable link between citizens and individual representatives, not handing disproportional power to tiny kingmaker parties, keeping the barrier to entry for new parties from ossifying the party system, producing governments that can actually govern — but I'm sure I'm missing some and I suspect some of the ones I listed are just camp prejudices dressed up as requirements (like, is "stable single-party majorities" a genuine desideratum or is that just what people who grew up under Westminster systems assume?).

So give me the requirements list, with the motivating case behind each one — a concrete scenario where a system violating that criterion does something clearly bad, not just an abstract statement. For each, tell me whether it's non-negotiable or whether reasonable people weigh it differently, and, crucially, flag any subsets that are jointly unsatisfiable — e.g. stuff coming out of Arrow's theorem, Gibbard-Satterthwaite, or just the plain tension between strict proportionality and having locally accountable individual MPs. That last part is what I most want to see, since if some of the desiderata can't all be met at once, then the whole FPTP-vs-PR debate is really a disagreement about which criterion to sacrifice, and I want to frame my chapter around that.

Then, briefly, run first-past-the-post, a party-list PR system, and mixed-member proportional against the list — I'll extend it to STV and other variants myself later, so don't worry about being exhaustive there. Also feel free to tell me if you think one of my proposed criteria above is bogus or needs splitting into two, I've been going back and forth on the kingmaker party thing especially.
