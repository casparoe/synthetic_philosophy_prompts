Please play a committed dispositional monist -- a realist about powers who holds that properties just are dispositions, that laws of nature flow from the natures of those properties rather than constraining them from outside, and that no appeal to a categorical substratum is ever needed. Be sharp, don't make strawman concessions; you can give ground on details but never on the core claim that a disposition is an intrinsic property of the thing that has it. Quick setup so we're talking about the same cases: an electro-fragile wire that would lose its fragility the instant a current starts to approach (a fink -- the stimulus triggers removal of the disposition instead of the manifestation), and a poison that is disposed to kill, but the patient is given an antidote beforehand (a mask -- the disposition stays intact, something independent just blocks the manifestation). My opening move: these cases push you one of two ways and both look bad for intrinsincness. If you bite the bullet and say the wire is still fragile at the time and the poison still lethal at the time, then your dispositions have come completely apart from what the thing is actually going to do, and I want to know what makes the disposition a genuine power of the thing rather than a label we hang on it. But if you instead fold the finks and masks into the analysis -- the wire is fragile iff it would shatter if struck and no guardian intervened and nothing else got in the way -- then the analysis isn't about an intrinsic property of the wire anymore, it's quantifying over all the guardian-and-antidote stuff in the enviroment, and you've quietly conceeded that dispositions are extrinsic. And I have a wildcard: I think there are self-masking cases, dispositions whose own manifestation process neutralizes the disposition on the way -- picture a device whose disposition to heat up trips an internal thermostat that cools it back down before any heating registers. Those seem to wreck the move of tucking the provisos into the stimulus condition, because the interfering factor isn't out there in the world, it's internal to the manifestation itself. So, straight answers please: is the wire still fragile at the time, is the poison still lethal at the time, and on what grounds that doesn't either smuggle in extrinsicness or collapse into a vacuous everything-else-being-equal clause?
