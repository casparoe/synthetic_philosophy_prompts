i'm a novelist, about two-thirds through a book whose narrator is a retired operations researcher — forty years on scheduling and search problems, and now she's at her kitchen table at 2am watching her granddaughter build a spreadsheet to pick a college, weighting factors, trying to maximize her whole life. late in the scene i want the narrator to recite, half to herself, a poem she's had by heart since grad school, one her thesis advisor used to quote, and i need you to actually write the poem.

make it a sestina. the six end-words should be one family — search, better, best, enough, stop, and a sixth of your choosing — so the sestina's mechanical shuffling of the same six words does the work of showing a mind cycling the same handful of considerations over and over, never terminating on its own. the poem shouldn't end because the question got answered. it ends because the envoi is where she stops. search doesn't conclude, it gets called off.

two things my narrator would insist on, so the poem has to get them right. first: this cannot read as "lower your standards, kid." her whole career was the opposite claim — stopping at good enough isn't a failure of rationality, it's what rationality actually looks like when the world is too big to search exhuastively, and the stopping rule is fitted to the environment rather than a shrug at it. if the poem sneers at maximization that's fine, she does too, but it can't turn satisficing (spellcheck gave up on that word weeks ago) into mere settling for second best. second: don't have the granddaughter overhear and be changed by it. either she doesn't hear it or she hears it and misses the point — i honestly havent decided which yet, so the poem has to work both ways, dont resolve that for me.

the bar i'm aiming for: someone who knows their Herbert Simon should recognize exactly what position the poem is taking, and someone who's never heard of any of this should just find a good poem about an old woman at a kitchen table while the dishwasher runs and a kid types. if it helps, the standard that keeps dropping until the world finally meets it — she'd want that motion somewhere in there, the aspiration level ratcheting down step by step, not as defeat but as adjustment. but don't hammer it, she hates being lectured at and so do i.
