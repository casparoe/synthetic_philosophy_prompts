i want a poem that works like one of Otto Neurath's Isotype charts — the rows of little identical figures his Vienna museum used to turn dead statistics into something a worker could read at a glance. subject: war dead per decade, declining.

so the form has to carry the data. every line the same length and nearly identical to its neighbors, like the repeated pictograms. each decade is one stanza, and the magnitude is given only by how many lines the stanza has — never by stretching a line or making it louder (Neurath's first rule was number of symbols, not size of symbols). no numerals anywhere; the trend should be visible the way a chart is visible, before anyone starts counting. and end on the remainder: the last line cut off, half the length of the others, breaking off mid-thought, standing for the part that doesnt fill a whole unit.

tone-wise, it should carry a bit of Neurath's actual conviction — that pictures could reach across the barriers words put up, and that an ordinary person being able to see the shape of society for themselves is a small act of emancipation — but without turning into a lecture about it. keep the lines plain and pictorial, like Arntz's figures.
