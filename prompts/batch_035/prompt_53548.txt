For an essay on the inefficacy objection in collective-harm arguments, I need a thought experiment engineered to test whether the objection can be held only where it is convenient to hold it. The objector says: my burger, my flight, my vote never crosses any threshold, so my individual contribution does no wrong. Construct a case in which that reasoning, if accepted, must also excuse an act the very same objector would never excuse.

Specifications: the scenario must be realistic and quotidian — a workplace or a neighbourhood, no trolleys, demons or perfect predictors — and no longer than a paragraph. The collective harm must be vivid and concentrated on identifiable individuals rather than diffuse and statistical, so that intuition is not diluted by abstraction. The individual contribution must be inefficacious in the strongest sense: stipulate perfect replacement, so that abstaining changes nothing ex ante or ex post and there is no residual probability of the agent being the threshold-crosser. Yet the act must come out intuitively and clearly wrong, and wrong because of the contribution itself — not because of what it symbolises, what it reveals about character, or what would happen if everyone did likewise. Then include a control case: the same act performed by a single agent alone, which the objector readily condemns, so we can see exactly what the two cases share and where they diverge.

After the scenarios, demonstrate that the design works: state what a straightforward act consequentialist, a threshold or expected-value theorist, and a fairness- or complicity-based theorist each conclude in the main case; identify which structural feature is doing the work (I suspect the perfect replacement, but verify this rather than assume it); and give the inefficacy objector's strongest genuine reply rather than a strawman — in particular, whether they can deny perfect replacement in the constructed case without thereby abandoning the objection's standard use against meat and votes. Close by saying whether the case generalises back to climate and meat or whether the objector can find a principled disanalogy.

Please answer in British English.
