ok so I've been chewing on this stuff by myself for a couple of years now (zero philosophy background, this is just evening thinking) and I've ended up with a handful of beliefs about time and caring about the future. What I want to know is whether they actually fit together or whether some of them quietly cancel each other out. here's the set:

1. How far away something is in time should never *by itself* make it matter less. If feeding a hungry person on Tuesday does the same good as feeding them on Monday, the delay isn't a reason to care less. I think this goes for everyone — strangers, me, and people who aren't born yet.

2. Because of that, I think it's basically an accounting trick when governments "discount" the future in their climate math. Even 2% a year quietly shrinks people 100 years out down to almost nothing, which to me looks like erasing them on paper.

3. But at the same time, I think it's completely sensible for me to not save like crazy for my 90s, partly because I might not make it to my 90s. Money I never get to spend did me no good. This feels obviously right to me even though it smells like the exact thing I called a trick in (2).

4. When I actually picture 88-year-old me, he's close to a different person. Different opinions, half the memories gone, probably different hobbies. Honestly I feel about as connected to him as to some stranger who happens to inherit my stuff. I can't get excited about doing things "for him" the way I get excited about doing things for my daughter.

5. And yet I'd feel like a total failure if I blew my retirement savings, like I'd be letting somebody specific down. So apparently I owe that old guy something I don't owe actual strangers, and I can't tell you why, because by (4) he's basically a stranger.

6. Two gut verdicts I can't shake: a 28-year-old emptying her savings for a once-in-a-lifetime trip seems sensible, and a 66-year-old draining the retirement fund for a cruise seems selfish. No idea how to square that with (1), it just definately seems obvious.

So: do these hang together or not? Which ones are genuinely pulling against each other, which only look like it, and what's the cheapest fix for each? Rank the tensions worst-first instead of listing them all as equally bad, and tell me which single change would clean up the most mess. Keep it under 400 words — I want the audit, not an essay.
