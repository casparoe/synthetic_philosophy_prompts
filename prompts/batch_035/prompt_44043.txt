Have them press their views on concrete test cases: why attitudes toward taxation, abortion, and environmental regulation should travel together at all, what to make of issue positions that have switched sides across decades, and what evidence, if any, could show one of them to be wrong. Both characters should be equally sharp, respond to each other's actual arguments rather than talk past each other, and neither should function as a foil for the other.
