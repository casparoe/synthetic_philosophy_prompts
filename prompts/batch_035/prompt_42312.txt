Then farming came along and everything got worse. Skeletons tell the story: before agriculture people routinely lived into their seventies, but once they started living on grain their life expectancy crashed to around thirty. Farmers were shorter, their teeth were rotten, and they caught epidemic disease for the first time from living in their own waste next to their animals. Farming also created inequality -- once you have fields and granaries you have property, and once you have property some people grab most of it, so hierarchy begins with the first farmers. Jared Diamond called agriculture the worst mistake in the history of the human race, and the kicker is that foragers had no reason to switch: farming only won because it feeds more people per acre, so farmers simply outbred and pushed out their healthier, happier neighbors.
