Please describe the most important implications of satisficing and bounded rationality for how the board of a small nonprofit should structure its search for a new executive director. In answering, distinguish Herbert Simon's original conception of satisficing — aspiration levels as psychological states revised in light of experience, sometimes downward after a string of disappointments — from the later rational-choice reconstruction of satisficing as simply optimal stopping under search costs, and say which version should actually guide the board's procedure.
