I'm on my school's student council and we vote next week on a proposal from the principal, and I have to write the opinion memo that gets circulated beforehand. Background: our school has always given out individual awards at the end of the year (top student per subject, valedictorian, that kind of thing). The principal wants to scrap most of them and switch to a house system — everyone sorted into a house that stays together all four years, houses earn points for basically everything (grades, attendance, volunteering, sports), and the big awards at the end of the year go to houses, not individuals. Points can also be *lost* for one person's screwup, so if someone skips class or gets detention their whole house pays. His argument is that right now the awards only go to kids who are already winning and everyone else just checks out, and that actually belonging to a group people would care more, try more, look out for each other. Some of us think it's basically making individuals sacrifice for a group they never chose to join, and punishing people for other people's behavior.

Write the memo for me: recommend yes, no, or yes with changes, argue it seriously, and honestly deal with the strongest point on the other side rather than dunking on it. Don't just name-drop philosophers, the council will roll their eyes — but you can frame the deeper issue if it helps. If you go with yes-with-changes, the changes need to be specific things we could actually vote on. Also it's due Thursday so keep it to about a page.
