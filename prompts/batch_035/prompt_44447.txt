My thesis: the hole argument doesn't defeat substantivalism about spacetime — it only defeats a version where points carry an intrinsic "thisness," like rows with a hidden primary key. My analogy: spacetime points are like rows in a relational database with no id column — a point's identity is fixed by its place in the structure (the metric and the other fields), not by some intrinsic label, and Einstein's diffeomorphism just relabels equivalent descriptions rather than generating genuinely different worlds. So I want to defend substantivalism in the form "the points exist independently of the stuff sitting at them," while denying you can re-identify a point across a diffeomorphism the way you'd track a person across two photos.
