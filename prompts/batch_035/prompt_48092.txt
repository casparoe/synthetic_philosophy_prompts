(3) I hold that coming into existence can't be a benefit or a harm to the person who comes into existence — nonexistence isn't a baseline you get compared against, so even a miserable life isn't worse for its bearer than no life at all. (5) I buy the line that when a choice changes which child will exist, the unchosen possible child isn't harmed by not being chosen, and so in those cases you should just bring about the outcome with the better prospects; identity as such carries no moral weight.
