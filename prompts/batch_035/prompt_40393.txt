Two friends of mine — one a halfer, one a thirder — have been arguing about the Sleeping Beauty problem for like a year now, and it's gotten to the point where they mostly repeat themselves at each other. They finally agreed to sit down and write a joint statement instead. Draft that document for them. Quick recap so we're on the same page: Beauty is put to sleep, a fair coin is flipped, and she's woken once if it lands heads but woken twice (Monday and Tuesday, with her memory of the first awakening wiped) if it lands tails. Asked on an awakening how likely she thinks the coin landed heads, the halfer says 1/2 and the thirder says 1/3.

Here's what I want in the statement. First, the claims both of them can actually sign — about the setup, about what makes a credence rational, about which parts of the argument they've been having that turned out to be talking past each other. Then the precise statements of where they still disagree, each phrased so its own side would accept the wording as a fair description of their view. Then the cruxes: the sub-questions such that settling one would probably move at least one of them. I suspect one crux is whether Beauty learns anything new when she wakes up, and another is whether, when you test the credences with bets, the payouts should be counted per experiment or per awakening — but let them work out the real list. For each crux, they should include a concrete case, bet, or variant of the experiment that both agree bears on it (they can invent one if nothing standard fits), with each side's prediction about it written down in advance. If some part of their dispute is merely verbal, they should say which part and why.

One formatting note: write the whole thing as continuous prose, like an email or a letter. No headings, no bullet points, no bold text, no tables. If a list would help, fold the numbering into the sentences themselves. And keep it good-faith — neither of them gets the last word at the other's expense, since the whole point is that they'd both put their names on it.
