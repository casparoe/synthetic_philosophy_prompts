When I pushed him he said fine, even an honest perfect replica should count the same, like if you could 3D scan the Mona Lisa down to the brushstroke nobody should care which one they queue up to see. Can you lay out what actually maps between the two cases — what plays the role of the song, the performance, the recording, the composer — and where the comparison breaks down?
