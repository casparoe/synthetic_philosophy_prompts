I've been reading about the combination problem for panpsychism and keep finding that each author's proposed solution quietly loads in their own standards for what counts as solving it. Before I evaluate any particular proposal, I want the specification laid out in full.

Write the desiderata a solution to the combination problem must meet to count as adequate. Some initial structure, which you should feel free to revise if you think it misconstrues the problem: a solution is being sought for the purpose of making constitutive panpsychism a genuine rival to emergentist and physicalist pictures of consciousness — not merely of restating why it seemed hopeless — so the desiderata should be stated in terms neutral between the specific solution strategies (constitutive micro-level combination, cosmopsychist decombination from a cosmic subject, reinterpreting what gets combined, or denying that subjects ever combine at all).

For each requirement, give the motivating case or consideration behind it — e.g., whatever handles combination must also say something about the palette problem (why do micro-experiences of, presumably, unimaginably alien phenomenal characters combine into the specific textures of human experience rather than some other set), and about the subject-summing aspect (why do bound micro-subjects apparently cease to be subjects in their own right once a larger subject contains them, if they do). Also decide which constraints come from general metaphysics and which come from taking the hard problem seriously in the first place, and keep those apart — a requirement that already assumes the truth of panpsychism's rivals should be flagged and dropped, since adequacy should not be defined so that only dualism could pass.

Additionally: distinguish the non-negotiable requirements from those that are, on reflection, prejudices of one camp; flag any pair or subset you suspect is jointly unsatisfiable, and if a subset is, say what follows — whether the combination problem should be treated as a refutation, a research program, or an argument for restructuring which level of reality carries the proto-experiential properties. Finally, indicate which single requirement does the most to exclude the candidate solutions you are familiar with, and briefly why.

Keep the weight on the requirements and their motivations rather than on scoring the contenders.
