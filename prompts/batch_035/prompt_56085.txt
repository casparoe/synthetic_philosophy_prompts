Construct a thought experiment for a debate about the direction of explanation in historical materialism. I want a scenario in which the superstructure explains the base rather than the other way around: two societies that begin economically near-identical — same productive forces, same resource endowments, same mode of production — but whose ideological or legal superstructures diverge for reasons traceable to something non-economic (a theological dispute, a dynastic accident of legitimacy, a canon-law ruling), and whose economic bases then diverge as a downstream consequence, so that the base-difference is best explained by the superstructure-difference. Constraints: the scenario should be realistic in historical texture rather than science-fictional; it may not appeal to geography, climate, or resource windfalls as the hidden variable, since that is the usual escape hatch; it may not be Weber's Protestant ethic example or any stock variant of it; and it must be one paragraph. It must also not cheat: do not simply stipulate that the superstructure caused the economic divergence — the case should be built so that the divergence is generated by the details, and a skeptical reader could in principle check that the causal chain runs the way you claim.

After the scenario, do three things. First, walk through the verdicts: what a strong base-determinist who reads Marx's 1859 Preface literally would say, what an orthodox Marxist who grants the superstructure relative autonomy would say, and what a Weberian would say — each responding to the actual details of your case, not to the general issue. Second, tell me honestly which single feature of the case is doing the work of driving the explanation upward rather than downward, and what happens to the case if that feature is removed. Third, give the strongest available reply for the base-determinist: the best way for her to reinterpret your case so that the superstructural divergence is itself explained by prior economic causes, without appealing to anything you have ruled out. Conclude with your own verdict on whether the case succeeds in producing genuine upward explanation or merely an appearance of it.
