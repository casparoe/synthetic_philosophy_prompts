And decapods were excluded from welfare law forever on the grounds that they can't feel pain; now the science on that has shifted and some jurisdictions are starting to include them, but the protections often look symbolic next to shrimp farms killing billions of animals however is cheapest. And is that even consistent — can one legal system coherently hold that a salmon matters if it's in a pen and doesn't matter if it's in a net?
