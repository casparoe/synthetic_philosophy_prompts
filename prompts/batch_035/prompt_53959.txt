Write me a satirical corporate FAQ page, around 650-700 words total, for a fictional company that rents out philosophical zombies as staff (call it something like "Behavioral Twins LLC" — you pick the name, and maybe give the service a stupid product name like "WorkerPrime"). The humor has to come from actually getting the philosophy right, so a philosophy of mind person laughs instead of winceing: the company's whole business model leans on the conceivability argument (if you can coherently conceive of your worker without qualia, they're saying, that's basically a hiring qualification), the zombies deny nothing about their inner lives and will cheerfully tell you the sunset fills them with ineffable wonder, and there should be at least one Q where a customer complains that their zombie reported a toothache and the answer explains, deadpan, why that's fully consistent with everything the company promised. Work in a premium "Zimbo" tier somewhere (second-order zombies that self-monitor and worry about whether they're zombies), and at least one joke about the fact that no one, including the company, can rule out that the FAQ itself was written by zombies.

Structure it with exactly these sections in this order: "About us" (~80 words, mission-statement voice), "Frequently asked questions" (~450-500 words, at least five Q&As, all answered in-character, no winking), and "Refunds and metaphysics" (~120 words). The refunds section is where it should all come to a head: the company happily reasons from conceivability to possibility when it's selling, but suddenly discovers that conceivability doesn't entail possibility once you want your money back on grounds that the worker lacked consciousness — which, they note, no conceivable test could ever establish anyway.

Keep it deadpan throughout, the reader should be able to tell what's being made fun of (the zombie argument, and more broadly the idea that you could ever run this business) without you ever saying so. No citations or reading lists obviously, this is fiction. thx
