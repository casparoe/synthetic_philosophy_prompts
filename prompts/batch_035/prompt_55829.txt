"Sen's great insight was that famines are almost never about food. In other words, famines are caused by poverty, and poverty is caused by bad governments, so famines are political crimes rather than natural disasters. His famous finding was that no famine has ever occurred in a functioning democracy, which proves that a free press and elections are what prevent famines. So when people call the Irish famine or China's Great Famine natural tragedies, they've got it wrong: those weren't failures of the potato or the weather, they were failures of policy, and describing them as natural disasters lets the responsible governments off the hook."
