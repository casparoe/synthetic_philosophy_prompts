So I've been building out my personal views on identity and modality and honestly I'd like someone to poke holes in the package before I go defending it at my reading group. Here's roughly where I am:

(1) I'm an origin essentialist about myself: I could not have come from a different sperm and egg. The kid my parents would have had if a different sperm had won the race would have been my sibling, not me, even raised in the same house with roughly my personality. Same for the egg side.

(2) But I'm also pretty Parfitian about persistence: what makes me me over time is just psychological continuity and connectedness, there's no deep further fact over and above that, and in fission cases identity can just be indeterminate and that's fine because identity isn't what matters anyway.

(3) I think the teleporter is survival, not death. If a machine scans me, destroys this body and builds an exact replica on Mars, that's me walking around on Mars.

(4) On de re modality I'm a Lewis-style counterpart theorist. I don't believe in haecceitistic thisness, no bare "me-ness" that could have been attached to wholly different stuff.

(5) Artifacts are different: I think the Ship of Theseus could totally have been built from somewhat different planks. Origin is essential for me but not for my laptop, and that's never bothered me.

(6) And I think basically everything else about my origins could have gone differently — different country, different language, my mother eating completely different things while pregnant — which seems fine to me since it would still be the same sperm and egg.

Is this package actually coherent? Go through the pairs that pull against each other, rank them by severity rather than giving me a flat list, and tell me for each one what would have to give and what it costs. Also flag anything that only *looks* like a tension but dissolves when you think it through, so I don't waste the seminar defending something that was never a problem. I genuinely don't know where the worst problem is, so don't assume I've got a favorite commitment I'm protecting.
