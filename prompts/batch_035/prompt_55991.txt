I know enough to know the industrial revolution and the green revolution kinda bailed us out, but the more I think about the one-liner the less sure I am what it even claims. Like "right but early" could mean a bunch of totally different things: that the mechanism was right and only the timescale was off, that the prediction was right conditional on stuff that didn't happen (which is way weaker), that it's right about every species except the one that got lucky, or that it's still coming and we're just mid-pause. And some of those seem like honest retrodiction and some seem like the kind of move that makes a theory unfalsifiable, where any delay just gets folded into "early."

Then tell me which reading the podcast guy was probably leaning on, and whether any of them is genuinely interesting rather than just hedged.
