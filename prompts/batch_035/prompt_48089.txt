If I'm going to spend my weekends for the next year reading and prototyping around one or two of these questions, which should they be, and why? My end goal is working software that gives sane recommendations under this kind of deep disagreement — I don't care about philosophy of science for its own sake, though I'd genuinely like to know if I'm missing something a philosopher would say I *should* care about. Rank the questions, defend the ordering (what makes one upstream of the others, which are overrated for my use case), and tell me what would make you re-rank them.
