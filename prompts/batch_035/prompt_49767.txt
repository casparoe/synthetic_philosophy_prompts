Next meetup we're doing two new cases and I want to bet on his answers beforehand. At least three rules seem to fit his record — Hofstadter-style superrationality where you cooperate with anyone whose reasoning is symmetric with yours, FDT-style logical dependence where you choose the output of your algorithm wherever it's being depended on, and something evidential with a smoking-lesion carve-out — and I'm pretty sure they come apart on my two cases, since one has no mind like his at all on the other side and the other has a mind with his values but alien reasoning. Help me place the bets: what's he going to say on each case, and what's he actually runing under the hood? Lay it out as exactly five numbered items, a sentence or two each, no introduction or conclusion — item one and two should be your predictions for case one and case two, items three and four the two candidate rules that best fit his track record with the reason each fits it, and item five the further case that would discriminate between those two rules.
