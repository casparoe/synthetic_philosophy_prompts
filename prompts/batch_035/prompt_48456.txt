Before I circulate a position statement at a graduate workshop on grounding and fundamentality, I would like a consistency audit of the package of views I have settled on, since I have assembled it piecemeal and suspect some of the pieces do not fit together. Here is the package. First, I take grounding to be a single, primitive, hyperintensional relation rather than a schema covering a loose family of relations, and I hold that it is irreflexive, asymmetric, and transitive. Second, I hold that grounding is well-founded: every descending grounding chain terminates, so that anything grounded at all is ultimately grounded in something ungrounded, and there is a fundamental level. Third, I hold that the fundamental level contains only physical facts, so there is nothing to reality over and above fundamental physics. Fourth, I hold that the laws of nature are Humean: the laws are nothing over and above the total pattern of particular physical facts, so the laws are grounded in that pattern. Fifth, I hold that the fundamental physical facts obtain partly in virtue of the laws, in the sense that the laws constrain or settle how the fundamental entities behave; I find a completely aloof conception of lawhood, on which laws merely summarize the facts without entering into what makes them obtain, hard to accept. Sixth, I hold that matter is gunky: every material thing has proper material parts, all the way down, so there are no mereological atoms. Seventh, I hold that everything that exists is either fundamental or grounded, with no third status, and I see no principled reason to exempt the grounding relation itself from this division; and grounding does not look to me like a physical fact.

What I want from you is an audit rather than a survey of the field. Tell me which pairs or larger subsets of these commitments are genuinely in tension and which only appear to be, and rank the real tensions by severity rather than treating them as equally grave. For each genuine tension, say exactly what would have to give and what that concession would cost me elsewhere in the package. Then tell me which single revision would clean up the most, in case one adjustment does most of the work. Please respond in continuous prose only, with no headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables, as you would in a careful and thorough email.
