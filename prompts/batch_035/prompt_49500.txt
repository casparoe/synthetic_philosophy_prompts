So I've been pretty sold for a while on the Dennett-style take on the color phi phenomenon and the cutaneous rabbit: when two flashes of different colors are shown in quick succession at different locations, people see one dot moving and changing color, even before the second dot exists -- and the natural reading is that the brain doesn't stream experience out in real time, it edits a narrative after the fact, the way Orwell's Ministry of Truth rewrites old newspapers. The "Stalinesque" rival (the brain holds things briefly and renders the show late, so the revision happens before anything is experienced) always seemed to me like it just relocates the magic: somewhere between retina and report there has to be a privileged place where it "all comes together," and I've never seen a reason to believe in that place. So my position is basically: there's no fact of the matter about exactly when the phi experience occurs, because conscious experience just isn't the kind of thing that has a precise timeline independent of probing and reporting -- the " Orwellian" post-hoc editing story, no Cartesian theater, the whole package.
