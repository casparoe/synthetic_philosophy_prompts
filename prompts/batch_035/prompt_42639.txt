One guy there is a committed halfer, and whenever anyone mentions the thirder answer he shuts it down with the same line: that when Beauty wakes up during the experiment she "receives no new relevant information" — she knew in advance the experiment would involve her waking up at some point, so how could the experience of waking tell her anything? Last week he used that line to defend credence 1/2 in heads, and then yesterday he pushed it further and claimed it also means Beauty should answer 1/2 to "is today Monday," and even that in a variant where she's woken twice regardless of how the coin lands, she'd learn nothing at all on waking.

Honestly the thirder replies in the channel annoy me almost as much, because people just say "she does get new information, it's self-locating information" like that settles it, which is slogan-slinging from the other direction. What I'd like is a real menu. What are the distinct precise claims that could be packed into "no new relevant information" — for example: nothing about the coin that she didn't already know on Sunday (no new *uncentered* information); no change in her total evidence; no new *centered* or self-locating fact; no shift in evidential support for the hypotheses she cares about; nothing she could truthfully report to an outsider as news. And for each reading, say whether it's actually true in the setup, whether it's a platitude or a substantive claim, and — this is the part I actually need — whether accepting that reading would get my friend his 1/2 conclusion, or whether some of the true readings are perfectly compatible with thirding, so that the slogan only *sounds* like it decides the issue.

If you can, also say how the slogan fares in the variants he wants to stretch it to. If Beauty is woken twice no matter what, does "no new information" still hold under all the same readings, or do the readings come apart there? If some readings are true in one setup and false in the other, that seems like a clean way to figure out which reading he's actually committed to, and I'd like to be able to point at it. Lay it all out as a numbered list I can paste back into the channel, since we'll just go in circles otherwise.
