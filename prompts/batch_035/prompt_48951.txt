For thirty-odd years I worked on gravitational physics — pulsar timing, mostly — and since retiring I have been reading philosophy of time. I would like a consistency audit of the position I have drifted into, because I cannot see my way clear of it myself. The commitments are: (1) I take Minkowski geometry to describe how the world actually is, not as a calculational device; the four-dimensional manifold is the structure of reality, and coordinate-dependent talk is bookkeeping. (2) On that basis I accept the Rietdijk–Putnam argument: the relativity of simultaneity leaves no room for an objective, frame-invariant global "now," so presentism is false, and I find the Andromeda-style illustration — two people strolling past each other in opposite directions disagreeing about whether some distant event has already happened — genuinely persuasive rather than a curiosity. (3) I nonetheless believe the future is objectively open, not merely epistemically unknown: quantum measurement outcomes are genuinely unsettled until they occur, and a world containing unsettled facts is not a block. (4) I hold, nearly as a moral principle carried over from relativity, that there are no privileged frames or foliations — anything not invariant under the symmetry group is an artifact of description. (5) Yet I know our cosmology supplies a strikingly natural cosmic time, the rest frame of the microwave background, and I am tempted to say that the "settling" of previously open facts advances along that foliation, while insisting this does not restore a privileged present. Which of these pairs are genuinely in tension, which only appear to be, and what is the cheapest repair and what would it cost? I have deliberately not said where I suspect the trouble lies. Two requests: take seriously the possibility that two of my commitments, each quite standard, are jointly untenable — that is what I most want to understand — and if any part of this rests on a confusion, whether about what the Rietdijk–Putnam argument actually requires, about cosmic time in an expanding universe, or about the difference between facts being determinate and events being determined, tell me so plainly instead of auditing on my terms.
