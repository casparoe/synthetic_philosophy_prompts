ive been turning something over in my head for the last couple years and finally wrote down where i actually stand on mental illness categories. background so you know where im coming from: no training in anything, just a lot of reading on my own, my own time in therapy, and watching my nephew go thru an ADHD evaluation. heres my list:

(1) mental illnesses are real conditions, not inventions. the suffering in depression is not a social construct, meds do things, brain scans find stuff. when someone tells me depression is just a label society puts on ordinary sadness, i dont buy it.

(2) psychiatric categories get discovered over time, the way other diseases were. todays categories are blurry and probably wrong at the edges, but researchers are converging — we'll eventually find the real boundaries the same way doctors figured out that "fever" was actually a bunch of different diseases.

(3) the labels themselves change the people who get them. this one im sure of from watching it happen up close. once a diagnosis exists and spreads, people start reading their own lives through it. some kids lean into the ADHD identity, adults start noticing symptoms the category says they should have, the criteria even get rewritten partly because of how people behave under the label. the condition isnt just sitting there waiting to be found — it partly gets shaped by being classified.

(4) the current DSM map is roughly right and mostly needs refinement, not replacement. the categories group people in a way that hangs together well enough to guide treatment, and guiding treatment is the point.

(5) i also think the line between bipolar disorder and borderline personality is mostly an accident of history and of which door you walked in — clinicians basically admit those two bleed into each other.

so my question: does this hang together? which of these actually fight each other, which ones only look like they do, and if something has to give, whats the cheapest revision and what does that cost me? some conflicts matter more than others, so rank them by severity rather than treating them all the same. i'd rather not say in advance which one im attached to — i genuinely dont know, thats why im asking.
