The problem is the field has a pile of open problems and I can't tell which ones actually matter. Off the top of my head:

1. Dilation — cases where conditioning on new evidence makes your credal set wider, i.e. learning something leaves you *less* decided than before. Some people treat this as an embarassment for the view, others say it's fine.

4. Inertia — the worry that a set of priors can be so unresponsive to evidence that you never learn your way out of ambiguity. Feature or bug?
