Before I take sides on what makes the sentence 'Socrates is wise' express a single proposition rather than an inventory of items — and before I choose among the camps that posit states of affairs, those that take propositional structure as primitive, and those that let Bradley's regress run without end — I want the specification first. Please write the desiderata that any adequate theory of the unity of the proposition must meet, each with the case or consideration that makes it a requirement rather than a preference of one school.

Cover at least the following. The difference between a proposition and a mere list, and what the theory must say the set whose members are Socrates and wisdom lacks that the proposition that Socrates is wise has. How the theory must face Bradley's regress head-on rather than by silence: if a relation is to relate Socrates and wisdom, something must connect the relation to its relata, and any theory that adds the connective as a further constituent has reproduced the very problem it was meant to solve — so the theory must say whether the chain terminates, runs infinitely, closes into a circle, or stops at a primitive, and each option carries a cost that should be named. The requirement that the account cover false propositions, since the unity of the proposition that Socrates is foolish cannot be borrowed from anything in the world that makes it true; the requirement of preserving order and direction, so that the theory distinguishes a claim of Socrates being wiser than Plato from its converse; and consistency with our ability to understand sentences we have never encountered before, since an account of unity that makes compositional understanding a mystery has bought its solution at too high a price.

For each requirement, state whether it is non-negotiable or a prejudice of one camp, and mark explicitly which pair of desiderata you suspect cannot be jointly satisfied — I regard that as the real deliverable, since if the demand for no unexplained primitive and the refusal of an infinite regress cannot both be honored, the list itself is a result, and I want to see whether you think that is so here. Keep the weight on the criteria and their motivating cases rather than on the scoring, since the criteria are where the thinking is.
