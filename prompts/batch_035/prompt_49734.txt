And an elderly man with severe dementia who harmed his caregiver: no desert at all. On the meta level she says free will in the strong libertarian sense is obviously an illusion and determinism is probably true, but she also insists people are still "responsible" for what they do when they can respond to reasons -- and when I ask whether that surviving responsibility means anyone deserves to *suffer* for its own sake, she gets weirdly cagey.

Pure question: does he deserve to spend his last months in prison? Case two: someone does a genuinely honest, self-sacrificing act totally anonymously -- returns a lost fortune knowing no one will ever know -- and there's no possible incentive effect from rewarding her, since nobody knows about it.
