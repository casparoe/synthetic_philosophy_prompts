I'm a grad student working on philosophy of mind and I've gotten a bit obsessed with Hofstadter's careenium thought experiment (from his old Synthese paper, the one called something like "who shoves whom around inside the careenium"). Quick recap so we're on the same page: imagine a billiard table, the careenium, where some of the balls are ordinary steel balls while others are mere images — patterns of light projected up from underneath, their positions computed moment to moment by a machine that simulates a second, lower-level table. The trick is that the projected "simmballs" can nonetheless shove the real balls around, because the projector's output is coupled to magnets under the table surface. Hofstadter's point, roughly, was to model how an "I" — a self-symbol, a software-level pattern — could have real causal oomph in the physical world without any spooky dualism: the symbols push the substrate around by being, at bottom, arrangements of that very substrate.

Here's my half-formed idea for a term-paper-turned-proposal (my department lets term papers double as dissertation-chapter drafts, and I need to submit a ~700-word plan to my advisor before the end of the month). The literature on the careenium, such as it is, tends to stop at "see, downward causation is possible, no dualism needed." But it seems to me the thought experiment actually contains a subtle cheat, or at least an unexamined assumption: the simmballs' causal power is entirely mediated by machinery that was designed and placed in the setup from outside. No magnets, no shoving. So the question I want the proposal built around is this: in the careenium, does the symbol level have causal powers that are genuinely irreducible to the microphysics of the whole system (balls, table, magnets, computer), or is the appearance of "symbols pushing the substrate" an artifact of where we the observers choose to draw the level boundary? And I suspect the right tool is an interventionist framework — can you intervene on the simmball variables while holding fixed the full microphysical state? My guess is you can't, and that this exposes a gap between "this level is a useful description" and "this level does anything," which is exactly the gap people slide over when they carry the analogy over to selves and brains.
