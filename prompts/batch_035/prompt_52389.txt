Assuming this is settled, my question for you, acting as a professional philosopher of mind, is this: how should I write the neurologists in the novel? Ought they to be depicted as straightforwardly mistaken in their materialist assumptions, or as professionals half-aware that something has gone badly wrong in their field — and which portrayal would make for a more honest and intellectually credible book? Please think carefully and systematically before answering, perhaps beginning with a short brainstorm setting out what a novelist in my position genuinely needs to know, and do not hesitate to be candid about any of my working assumptions.
