Somewhere in that reading I settled on the view philosophers apparently call luck egalitarianism, and I want a second opinion on it expressed as a probability, because the books I can follow either defend it or attack it, and I can't tell from the inside how the balance actually comes out.

My own credence that this, with whatever qualifications turn out to be needed, is the correct principle of distributive justice is about 0.55. My brother's, I'd guess, is closer to 0.3.

Please give me your own number by answering in exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences long, with nothing before item 1 or after item 5.
