I am especially interested in one comparison: I sometimes sidestep the boundary with hysteresis or by returning a "don't know" value, and I want to know whether that is genuinely analogous to the supervaluationist picture — where the disjunction of a borderline claim and its negation comes out true while neither disjunct does — or whether the analogy quietly changes the subject, and where it breaks.
