If my parents had conceived a month earlier, that kid isn't me (feels weirdly sad to say but there it is). If the winning sperm had one nucleotide different — a single point mutation — I say that's still me, since mutations like that happen all the time and nobody thinks they make a different child. If the zygote that became me had split and my mom had identical twins, I honestly don't know — maybe neither twin is me? A clone grown from one of my skin cells isn't me, even though genetically closer to me than my actual sibling.
