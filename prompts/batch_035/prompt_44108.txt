quick backstory: I write novels, and my current narrator is a coffee taster whose palate is supposed to be slowly degrading over the course of the book — that's the whole tragedy. while researching what "taste" even is I walked into an argument with my editor (ex-philosophy grad student, insufferable, keeps saying "quining qualia" like it's a spell) and now I can't get out. here it is:

1. if qualia are anything, they're the introspectively available properties of experience — how the espresso tastes to me, over and above everything i'd say or do about it.
2. suppose that overnight the way espresso tastes to me becomes 50% less rich, and my memory files get rewritten so that every past cup is remembered as tasting exactly the way cups taste now. i'd notice nothing, and I could never notice anything, no matter how often this happens.
3. so my whole qualia-life could drift anywhere at all while I remain perfectly confident that things taste "the same as they always have."
4. if I couldn't in principle detect the difference between this and my qualia staying constant, then I don't have the privileged access to my qualia that (1) says defines them.
5. but a property of experience that's invisible even from the inside, that no thought, report, memory, or reaction could ever register, isn't doing any work — it's cut off from everything by construction.
6. so either there are no qualia, or there are but they're floating completely causally and epistemically inert, which amounts to the same thing for a novelist trying to write one.

and it seems to prove too much, because if this works for taste it works for everything — every "what it's like" in every character I've ever written becomes a posit I can't cash out. which step do I reject, and what does rejecting it cost me? for what its worth I'd rather keep (1) than give up on writing inner lives at all, and I suspect the trick is hidden somewhere in how (2) quietly moves from "memory adjusted" to "nothing could ever register" — but I can't pin down whether that's an equivocation about time (comparing now to then vs. knowing how it is right now) or whether the whole drift scenario is just stipulating my own conclusion into existence. be honest about which premises are actually load-bearing.
