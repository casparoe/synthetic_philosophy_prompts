i'd like a satirical press release, roughly 600-700 words, annoucing that the Singleton has finalized its consolidation of all decision-making on Earth. deadpan corporate voice throughout — think a big tech company's comms team, all "we're excited to share" — but the entity doing the announcing is a single decision-making agency that has quietly absorbed everything from the UN down to your local HOA. it should never wink at the reader; the irony has to stay legible purely through the content, and by the end a reader should have felt the actual pull of the singleton hypothesis: that total coordination really would end war and existential risk, and that this is somehow the unsettling part.

the jokes should come from actually engaging with the singleton hypothesis, not from generic evil-overlord gags: value lock-in announced as a "stability guarantee", coordination problems reported as "resolved", the last open moral question listed as closed, moral progress reframed as "legacy deliberation", the cosmic endowment now officially "under management". somewhere in there should be a quiet nod to the worry everyone actually has about this — that the values got locked in before we figured out what we value — but it should appear as a bullet point under "known limitations", not as a spelled-out punchline.

please include:
- an opening in standard press-release format ("FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE" and all that)
- a FAQ section where the questions get shorter and the answers get blander as it goes
- one quote from a "concerned former institution" (a democracy or a market — your pick) that has been "transitioned to an advisory role"
- a boilerplate "About the Singleton" paragraph at the end, in the style of every About Us section ever written

aim it at someone who knows Bostrom's singleton discussion well enough to feel the satire is fair — exagerate real features of the idea (irreversibility, the claim that a singleton solves coordination, the frozen-values worry) rather than inventing villainy. and please don't let the Singleton twirl its mustache: the scariest version is the one that keeps cheerfully confirming that everything is fine.
