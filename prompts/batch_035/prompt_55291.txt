(1) i'm a no-self reductionist — there's no self over and above a causally connected stream of psycho-physical events, no further fact about identity over time. (2) like parfit, i think identity itself isn't what matters for prududential concern; psychological connectedness is what matters. (3) that said, i hold onto strong reasons of self-interest — you have special reason to save for your own retirement, to treat your own future suffering as more urgent than a stranger's — and i take these to survive the reduction since they're grounded in the stream rather than in a soul. (5) finally i'm fine with a two-level picture where conventional persons are perfectly real for ethics, law, and everyday life, and only dissolve under ultimate analysis.
