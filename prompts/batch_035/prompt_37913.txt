Construct a thought experiment to the following specifications. Two hedonic utilitarians, who agree on everything except which deaths to count, must reach opposite verdicts on a single ordinary decision: whether a person who currently eats chicken should switch to eating an equal amount of beef. The first counts only the animals slaughtered for food, and must conclude that the switch prevents suffering; the second also counts wild animals killed in cultivating feed crops — through tilling, pesticides, and harvesting — invertebrates included, and must conclude that the switch increases suffering. Keep the scenario mundane: a grocery-store choice, no futuristic technology and no hypothetical planets. To keep the case honest, stipulate round parameters openly rather than pretending to empirical data: the person eats 25 kg of meat per year; a chicken yields about 1.5 kg of meat and a cow about 200 kg; chicken production uses about 2 kg of feed per kg of meat, grain-finished beef about 8 kg, of which you may assume half comes from row crops and the rest from pasture and inedible byproducts; and every death — chicken, cow, or wild — counts as one unit of badness. Treat the number of wild-animal deaths per tonne of feed, T, as unknown. Then: (1) run both utilitarians' calculations; (2) derive the threshold T* at which their verdicts diverge, and explain in plain terms what the world would have to be like for T to exceed it — for instance, by converting T* into deaths per hectare at a typical yield of ten tonnes per hectare, and asking which classes of animals would have to be counted for the flip to occur; (3) identify which stipulated parameter is doing most of the work, and say how far it could vary before the flip disappears; and (4) give the strongest reply available to the death-counting utilitarian, who should not be allowed to prevail by simply denying the arithmetic — she should instead press some further consequence of her opponent's own principles. The purpose of the case is to isolate exactly what must be true of feed production for the familiar advice to replace chicken with beef to be reversed, so every verdict should fall out of explicit arithmetic rather than intuition.
