I've gotten into this recurring argument with a friend about the old Leibniz question — why is there something rather than nothing — and before I embarrass myself in front of him again I figured I should write down where I actually stand and check whether my positions even fit together. Here's the package:

1. The question is a genuine, intelligible question. It's not a trick of grammar or a confusion about the word why, and it demands some kind of answer.
2. I reject the principle of sufficient reason. I'm fine with there being brute facts — the exact timing of a particular radioactive decay, say, just has no explanation, and that doesn't bother me.
3. I'm a theist, and I think the answer to the question is God. God exists necessarily, and a necessary being doesn't need an explanation for its existence.
4. When materialists respond with well, maybe the universe as a whole, or the fundamental laws, exist necessarily — I think that's a cop-out. Though I'll admit I've never been able to say precisely why their move is cheating and mine isn't.
5. I have a strong intuition that nothingness is somehow simpler than something, and that the simpler option is the likelier one, so the existence of anything at all strikes me as genuinely surprising and in need of accounting for. This is a big part of why it's intolerable to me to just say there is something, full stop, and leave it there.
6. Whatever the right answer to the question turns out to be, it has to come from a priori metaphysics. No possible observation could count for or against it — this is not an empirical question.

So what I want from you: go through these and tell me which ones actually pull against each other and which ones only look like they do, rank the genuine tensions by severity, and for each one say what the cheapest fix would be and what that fix costs me elsewhere. I honestly don't know which of these I'd give up if forced, so don't assume — maybe I should also say, I'm more attached to some than others but I'd rather not say which, I want your read unfiltered. sorry if this is a bit of a mess, it's late.
