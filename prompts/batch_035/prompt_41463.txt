Respect the distinctions that matter: impact on the world versus deviation from a policy, and bad-for-us versus merely different. For each requirement, give the case behind it and mark it non-negotiable or camp-specific. Then answer two questions: are any of these jointly satisfiable only by smuggling in a full utility function, and which single requirement does the most to eliminate the main contenders (relative reachability, attainable utility preservation, and a naive divergence-from-do-nothing penalty)?
