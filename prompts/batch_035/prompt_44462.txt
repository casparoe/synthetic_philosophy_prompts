Me and my mate Sarah were packing up her flat for a house move, and it turned into this running argument about when you're allowed to say something "can" do a thing. I wrote her verdicts down after, best I could:

- a vase on a shelf: yes, obviously breakable
- the same vase wrapped in foam inside a crate, packed so well it realistically ain't breaking in there: still breakable — "the crate doesn't change the vase, it's the same vase, the crate is just in the way"
- a match that got soaked right through in the sink: no, that match won't light — "it's done, the soaking got into it"
- sugar in a sealed jar: still dissolvable, the jar's just in the way
- one of those wine glasses made of the unbreakable plastic stuff: no — "that's just what it's made of, it never could break"

That's her track record so far. Now her new boyfriend builds gadgets, and over the weekend he showed off three things, and I want to call Sarah's verdicts on them before she gives them, like a betting game. The three:

1. A normal drinking glass with a prototype coating that senses an impact and goes rubbery faster than the glass can crack. While that coatings active the glass physically cannot shatter — hit it with a hammer and it just dents. Breakable or not?
2. Funnily enough the same question about his own temper. He says he used to have a shocking one, but nowadays the instant he feels himself starting to lose it, something in him switches it off cold — the provocation lands and the anger's already gone before it can come out. Is he still hot-tempered?
3. A vase with one of those transparent films shrunk onto it, like the ones people put on phone screens. Underneath it's the same glass, the film just holds the bits together if it does crack, so it'll never actually smash. Breakable or not?

Honestly I'm less fussed about the three answers than about what rule she's actually running, what other rules would fit her record just as well, and which of the three (or some fourth case you come up with) would best split those rules apart. But if you fancy it, give me your best guess on each as well, with your reasons.
