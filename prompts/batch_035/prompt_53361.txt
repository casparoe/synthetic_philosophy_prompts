I've been trying to work out what I actually believe about insect suffering by just going with my gut on a bunch of cases and seeing if there's a principle underneath. Here's what I've got. Swatting the mosquito that's about to bite me: obviously fine. Driving to the coast on a summer weekend knowing my windshield will end up plastered with bugs: fine, I don't even slow down. My town spraying for mosquitoes so everyone's backyards are comfortable in the evening, which kills orders of magnitude more insects than any windshield: fine. Running black soldier fly farms at industrial scale, trillions of larvae a year rendered into chicken and fish feed: this one genuinely bothers me, and I've started to think of it as a real moral problem we're quietly creating. Cricket flour for human food, way smaller numbers but a somewhat better case that each cricket feels something: also bothers me, maybe even more per insect. And one I keep flip-flopping on: planting a pollinator garden, knowing most of the insects it attracts will die young and, if they're sentient at all, die badly. That feels fine to me, even virtuous, but my reaction to the fly farm seems to pull the other way, since the garden also creates alot of expected suffering.

My tentative principle is something like: deliberately breeding insects into existence for our use is the problem, and incidental or collateral insect deaths don't count against you no matter the number. That fits almost everything, though the spraying case makes me uneasy, because the dead mosquitoes seem like the whole mechanism of the comfort, not a side effect. So I want to know: is there a cleaner principle that captures all my verdicts, or is one of them going to have to go? And whichever principle wins, please tell me what it says about (a) a company harvesting wild lake midges by the ton for pet food -- huge numbers killed as the product, but no breeding involved, (b) a zoo breeding crickets to feed its reptiles, and (c) gene drives being used to wipe out a biting midge species region by region. I'd take an honest statement of which of my verdicts the principle can't accommodate over one that saves them all by fiat. Also, please write the reply in American English -- I spell color without the u and I'd like the answer to match.
