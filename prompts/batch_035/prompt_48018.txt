A third keeps bringing up reclamation and says group members can use slurs for themselves, which the first mod thinks is incoherent if the badness is baked into the word itself.

Like: whether slurs have derogatory semantic content or whether that's pragmatics; whether they express generics about groups; how appropriation/reclamation is even supposed to work semantically; what explains the difference between slurs and "pure" pejoratives or mere insults; how mention vs. use plays out; and whether the offense is best modeled as a presupposition, an expressive component, or something else. Some of these feel like they'd change our moderation policy a lot, others feel like they'd change nothing no matter how they're resolved.

So what I want from you is a prioritization, not a survey. Start by brainstorming briefly, then rank maybe the top 3-4 open questions by how much they should matter for something like my concrete case — writing enforceable rules for a mixed community where most members are outsiders to the targeted groups. For each, explain why it's upstream of practical decisions (e.g., does the answer change how we treat quoting, reclamation, or accidental use?), how tractable it is conceptually, and which questions you'd explicitly deprioritize because no answer would change what we do in the mod channel.
