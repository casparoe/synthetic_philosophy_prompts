All the asymmetry lives in the initial condition, none in the laws. Picture 2, the perspectival story: this one goes further and says the direction of time itself isn't written into the world at all. Calling one end of the timeline "the future" is like calling one spot on the map "here" — an indexical fact about observers like us, who happen to be entropy-increasing, memory-forming systems riding an entropy gradient, so that "future" just means something like "the direction in which our records are reliable." A friend in my reading group thinks Picture 2 is profound; I keep suspecting it's just Picture 1 with some extra gloss about observers sprinkled on top, but I can't quite pin down why I think that.
