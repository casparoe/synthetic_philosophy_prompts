Concretely: restate the main positions in control-engineering terms. Consider a relief valve designed to open only above a threshold, or an interlock deliberately configured with a delay: these are cases where an engineer has chosen a policy under which the system does nothing until conditions are met, and it's unclear whether that counts as doing or allowing. Cover the classic test cases philosophers use, including the one where two agents are identically selfish and differ only in whether they act or refrain while a child drowns, and the cases involving initiating a threat versus declining to prevent one.
