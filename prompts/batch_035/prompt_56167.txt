4. Given 3, I think it follows that I came into existence well after conception — whenever it was that the psychological being that is me first showed up, probably somewhere in fetal development. Fertilization created the animal, not me.

5. I could have been raised in a completely different country, with a different name, different native language, opposite personality, few if any of my actual memories — and it would still be me. The gametes fix who I am, not how I turned out.

6. And I'm pretty sympathetic to the Parfitian thought that identity is not what matters — what matters is psychological continuity and connectedness, and identity is just whatever rides along with it, a matter of degree-ish, and not something to get precious about.
