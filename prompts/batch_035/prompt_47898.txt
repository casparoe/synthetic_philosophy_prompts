I'm a PhD student in early modern philosophy and I need someone to referee a fight from my Descartes reading group thats now spilling into the group chat. Topic: what the cogito is actually supposed to prove.

(1) Jonas said it's just an inference: premise "I am thinking," hidden premise "whatever thinks exists," conclusion "I exist." Mira said that gets Descartes backwards -- he explicitly denies the cogito is known through a syllogism, since a general premise would have to be known beforehand, and he holds nothing is known before it. It's less like an argument and more like something you do that displays your existence, not something you deduce.

(2) Jonas said fine, but then it's a weird one-off: it only shows you exist at the moment of thinking, a fleeting flash, not a persisting thing. Mira said that misses its function -- it's meant to be the one indubitable item, and from the certainty it has Descartes extracts a general rule (whatever I perceive clearly and distinctly is true), and that rule is then used to prove God's existence, and God in turn guarantees the rule and everything else, including that I existed five minutes ago.

(3) Jonas said that just buries the problem -- the rule comes from the cogito, then proves God, then God is needed to vouch for the rule, so the certainty was never transferable to anything beyond the cogito itself, and Descartes' foundation doesn't do any foundational work. Mira said the certainty isn't meant to rub off on other beliefs one by one -- the rule is what gets carried forward, and she thinks that's enough for Descartes to not be guilty of an obvious circle.

(4) Jonas circled back: anyway it doesn't even prove an "I" exists, just that some thinking is going on -- the self is smuggled in by the grammar of the sentence. Mira said that's basically Lichtenberg's complaint and Descartes can live with it: whenever thinking occurs there's a thinker, and that's all he needs to establish the res cogitans.

Can you adjudicate move by move -- who's right at each step, who's more right overall, and whether both are missing something? The group chat is counting on you.
