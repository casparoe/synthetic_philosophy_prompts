The audience is mostly formal epistemologists — people for whom Dutch book arguments, accuracy-dominance results, and Jeffrey conditioning are the bread and butter of rationality — plus one decision theorist and one senior faculty member who works on anthropic reasoning in cosmology (selection effects, doomsday-style arguments). My thesis: Sleeping Beauty's credence in Heads on waking should remain 1/2, yet she should still accept the bets thirders say she should accept, because the standard betting and Dutch book arguments against halfers covertly aggregate utility across her awakenings in a way that is appropriate to a bookie facing multiple betting opportunities but not to a single subject whose degree of belief is being evaluated. On my view the halfer/thirder dispute is really a dispute about the object of rational evaluation — the whole experiment viewed from Sunday versus the awakening viewed from inside — and once that distinction is on the table, the thirders' betting results can be conceded in full without conceding the credence.
