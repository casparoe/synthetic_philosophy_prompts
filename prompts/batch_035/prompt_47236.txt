Next month I present at a decision-science seminar my position that honoring sunk costs is not always irrational, and I need a Q&A prep sheet built for this particular room -- not the generic objections I already have answers for. My argument has three legs, and I want objections targeted at them specifically. First: when an agent is uncertain about the value of finishing what she started -- her own stamina, her taste for the payoff, the hidden quality of the project -- the resources already spent are Bayesian evidence about those unknowns, so continuing conditional on them need not violate consistency; the axioms forbid conditioning on realized costs only when the problem is common knowledge, and in real cases it is not. Second: an agent who abandons at the objectively optimal moment is exploitable -- in negotiations and long collaborations, being known to honor sunk costs functions as a commitment device that no cool calculator can replicate, so the disposition can pay for itself even when each exercise of it looks like a loss. Third: outcomes that force you to confront your own past error are genuinely worse to live with, and a utility function that includes memory-induced regret is no less legitimate than one that includes comfort or status. The room: about a dozen behavioral decision researchers for whom the sunk-cost effect is the canonical bias and the running example in their own lectures, including one who runs debiasing interventions for a government policy unit and will care about the practical upshot, plus one who studies escalation of commitment in organizations; two decision theorists who take the consistency axioms as settled; and a philosopher of practical rationality who has published on akrasia and weakness of will. Give me the objections in roughly the order they will arrive, phrased in each group's own vocabulary, each with a two-minute reply and an honest note on where that reply is soft; flag which points I should concede up front to buy credibility elsewhere; and identify the one question I am hoping nobody asks.
