Hey — I'm a journalist, I cover science and ideas for a magazine, mostly writing about prediction markets and the rationality scene. Last week I had drinks with two quants I know and they got into it over Kelly betting, and honestly I couldn't tell who won, which is annoying since I'm supposed to explain this stuff to readers for a living.

Here's roughly how it went:

(1) Maya said the Kelly criterion maximizes the long-run growth rate of your bankroll — it's not just a heuristic, it provably compounds faster than any other fixed betting fraction, so a long-horizon investor betting less than full Kelly is literally leaving money on the table.

(2) Dev said that's only true if your utility function for wealth is the logarithm. Standard decision theory says maximize expected utility, and log utility is just one arbitrary choice among many — a more risk-averse person should bet a smaller fraction, and there's nothing irrational about that.

(3) Maya came back with the time-average argument: when bets compound multiplicatively, the ensemble average (what happens across a big crowd of parallel bettors) is the wrong thing to compute, because a single gambler lives along one trajectory, and along that trajectory the bettor who avoids eventual ruin dominates. The time-average growth rate is maximized by Kelly whatever your utility function is, so the whole utility framework is a red herring.

(4) Dev said that just smuggles log utility back in — caring about your personal growth rate over time IS a preference, and a preference for a different tradeoff between growth and catastrophic drawdowns isn't an error. Plus nobody actually knows the true probabilities and edge, so full Kelly on misestimated edges produces brutal volatility; even the sports-betting pros mostly bet fractional Kelly for exactly that reason. Maya said the estimation point was fine but the first half was just relabeling her position.

Can you adjudicate this move by move — who's right about each of the four moves, and where exactly (if anywhere) they're talking past each other? I want to use it in a piece, so keep it under 400 words, and please no equations beyond maybe one, my editor will kill me.
