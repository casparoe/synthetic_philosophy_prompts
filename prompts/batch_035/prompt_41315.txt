Draft the joint statement that would issue from an adversarial collaboration between a semantic externalist and an internalist about meaning, each of whom has spent years arguing over Twin Earth. The document should record: the claims both sides can sign, starting with the stipulations of the case itself — that Oscar and Twin Oscar are molecule-for-molecule duplicates, that the clear, potable liquid filling Twin Earth's rivers and lakes is XYZ rather than H2O, and that nothing inside either man's head distinguishes the two worlds; then the residual disagreements, each phrased so that the side holding it would accept the wording (e.g., about whether there is a determinate fact of what Oscar means by 'water' independent of his environment, or whether Twin Oscar's word refers to anything at all); then the cruxes — I'd expect at least whether sameness of reference requires sameness of underlying stuff or only sameness of superficial behavior, and what follows for psychology if two internally identical subjects can differ in what their words mean. For each crux, include a case both sides agree bears on it, with each side's prediction written down in advance; in particular, have them consider a variant in which one planet has lakes of H2O and lakes of XYZ side by side, inhabited by a single community, and another in which a subject commutes between Earth and Twin Earth for years and later, back home, sincerely asserts that water boils at 100 degrees. Mark any part of their dispute that both would concede is merely verbal — including, if they think so, whether 'meaning' is even the name of what they have been fighting about. Number the agreed claims and use a table for the disagreements.
