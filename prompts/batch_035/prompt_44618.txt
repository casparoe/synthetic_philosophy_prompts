The anti-Frankfurt side runs the dilemma: if Black never does anything, then either Jones's actual decision-making was deterministic enough that the tiny flicker of alternative possibility he retains is all the principle needs, or there's some earlier indeterminism in Jones, in which case Black couldn't have known in advance when to get ready. The Frankfurt side responds with ever more baroque constructions -- prior signs, blockage cases, the whole mess about whether the counterfactual intervener has to wait on a neural "trigger" -- and then the other side finds the flicker again, just an even smaller one.
