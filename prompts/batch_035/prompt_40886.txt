So i was a job reference for an old coworker (small field, everyone knows everyone, hard to say no to being a reference) and I think I torpedoed him without technically saying anything bad. The hiring manager called me, asked a bunch of stuff, then asked directly how he handles deadlines. I said he's a genuinely creative guy, one of the most inventive people I've worked with. Every word of that is true, and I'd say it to his face. He did not get the job, found out I was the reference, and now he's furious — says my answer was obviously a dodge, that everyone in hiring knows what it means when you praise someone's creativity right after being asked about deadlines, and that I knew perfectly well what signal I was sending. My position is that I never said a single false or negative thing, he can't point to one sentence I uttered that was wrong, so what he "read between the lines" is his inference, not my statement. His comeback is that this is a dishonest game I'm playing with myself: if the read was so obvious, then it was part of what I communicated, full stop, and hiding behind "I never said it" is just a loophole.

Couple of wrinkles I keep going back and forth on: (1) nobody asked me a followup, so I never got a chance to say "and to be clear he's fine with deadlines" — does the fact that I could have added that, and pointedly didn't, make me more on the hook, or less? (2) at the time I honestly wasn't 100% sure whether I was being nice by finding something good to say, or being a coward about the deadline thing — does it matter for what I "meant" that my motives were muddled? (3) by his logic it seems like you can never give a lukewarm reference at all, since any true compliment in the wrong spot becomes sabotage — that can't be right either.

Is there a principled line here between what someone literally says and what they communicate, and if so, which side of it was I actually on? Was I lying-adjacent, telling the truth, or something in between that deserves its own name? And am I obligated to call the hiring manager and clear the record, or does the record look exactly like what I meant it to look like?
