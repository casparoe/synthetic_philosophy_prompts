For a graduate seminar on Kant's ethics I need to submit a one-page position statement on the moral status of animals, and before I do, I want an audit of whether my commitments form a stable package. Here is the set as it currently stands.

First, I endorse Kant's indirect-duty view: our obligations with regard to animals are grounded not in the animals themselves but in what our treatment of them does to our standing as moral agents — specifically, to the dispositions we owe to humanity. Second, I accept the Formula of Humanity as the complete account of moral status: rational nature, and only rational nature, is an end in itself, and direct moral duties are owed only to beings that possess it. Third, I am firm on a case verdict: someone who tortures an animal on an island he will never leave, whom no other human will ever meet or hear about, and who dies shortly afterward without his conduct ever affecting any person's life, acts wrongly in the strict sense — not imprudently, not merely as a degradation of his own character, but wrongly in the way one wrongs a bearer of moral standing. Fourth, I hold that painful animal experiments are permissible whenever they are genuinely necessary for a vital human benefit. Fifth, I am skeptical of duties to oneself as a distinct moral category: what Kant calls duties to oneself seem to me prudential or self-constitutive at best, not duties owed to anyone in the way duties to persons are owed.

My cohort keeps telling me the third and fourth commitments flatly contradict each other — one forbids and one licenses essentially the same infliction of animal pain — but I suspect that pairing is a decoy, and I can't put my finger on why. Go through the set and tell me which pairs are in genuine tension and how severe each one is, which apparent tensions dissolve on closer inspection, and, for each real tension, what would have to give and what the revision would cost the rest of the package. Rank the tensions by severity rather than presenting them as equally grave, and tell me which single revision would clean up the most.
