Everyone says perpetual motion machines are impossible, but I don't get why Maxwell's demon doesn't count as one. It just sorts molecules — fast ones to one side, slow ones to the other — and doesn't do any work on them, so you end up with a temperature difference for free that you could run a heat engine off of. That's basically free energy, and with modern nanotech we can watch and manipulate individual molecules now, so its not like its a thought experiment anymore. Why do physicists keep insisting the second law can't be broken instead of just admiting the demon is a loophole? Is it just dogma at this point?
