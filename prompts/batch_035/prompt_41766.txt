She vetoed me padding book two with a subplot just to hit the 100k wordcount the print marketing people wanted. She vetoed ending book two on a hard cliffhanger purely because the data says cliffhangers drive pre-orders, and we ended up compromising on an ending that resolves the arc but leaves one question dangling. Now for book three I want to take the exact same manuscript, chop it into three installments at the act breaks, and release them two months apart -- text completely untouched -- because each release gets the online stores' new-release visibility boost, and three boosts beat one. Predict her verdict, lay out the rules that fit her track record at least as well as whatever rule you pick, and tell me what hypothetical case would actually seperate those rules. And along the way tell me whether the Goodhart framing even captures what she cares about, or whether that's me smuggling in the wrong theory.
