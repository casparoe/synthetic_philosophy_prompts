She's very sharp and gives textbook answers to textbook objections, so my worry is that a generic Q&A will just let her run the greatest hits.

Draft me a sequence of about eight questions, ordered so they build on each other rather than sitting as a list. For each question, give a follow-up keyed to the answer she's most likely to give — I want to be able to react to her actual reply, not just fire off prepared shots. End with a question about what would count against dialetheism for her: what she'd treat as a genuine refutation rather than just another cost to absorb.
