Every spring I teach the pragmatism unit in my American philosophy survey, and every spring I give the students the same hand-wavy line: Peirce says truth is what inquiry would converge on in the limit, James says truth is what works or pays in belief, and then I mumble something about how these are "related but distinct" and move on before anyone presses me. A colleague of mine, who is more of a Peirce partisan than I am, has started arguing with me about it over lunch. She thinks the two theories are the same view in different dress — that James just said, louder and with more personality, what Peirce had already worked out about the ideal limit of inquiry, and that Peirce's later grumbling about James taking his principle in a direction he never intended is just an old man being territorial about his intellectual property. My gut says she's wrong, and my credence that Peirce-truth and James-truth are substantively the same theory (not just historically connected, but the same account under different phrasing) sits around 0.3. Hers is more like 0.65, and I can't tell which of us is actually arguing from the philosophy rather than from temperament.

So give me your number, but more importantly decompose the question: the probability that James's satisfaction/cash-value condition and Peirce's end-of-inquiry condition pick out the same property, the probability that the Peircean story even works as a theory of truth given that inquiry never literally terminates (what does a limit nobody reaches do for us), the probability that the difference is mostly that James explains what believing-true does for you while Peirce explains what being-true is, and any other terms you think belong in there. One number and a one-line justification for each, then how they combine to your final credence. Then tell me which term is doing the most work in separating 0.3 from 0.65, and what argument or example would move that term the most — the Santa Claus-style objections to James obviously go somewhere here, but I want to know whether they go to the decomposition or just to how much James one can stomach.

Number and full decomposition in under 400 words, final credence stated first, no fence-sitting.
