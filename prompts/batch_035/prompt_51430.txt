So for this one I want a satirical FAQ, roughly 700 words, for a fictional standards body I've just made up called the Graded Moral Status Labelling Initiative (GMSLI). Their shtick: they do for sentience what the EU energy label does for fridges — a lettered tier from A+++ down to F, printed on the front of anything that might be sentient, with a little chart of little animal silhouettes for scale. Format it as numbered questions in proper regulatory-agency FAQ voice.

The humour has to come from the actual philosophy of graded moral status, not from random absurdity — a specialist on moral status should recognise every dilemma and ideally feel the satire is fair. Concretely, I want it to cover, in whatever order works:

1. The committee's line-drawing problem. Somewhere between an insect and a thermostat there's a boundary, and the FAQ should squirm about the fact that a discrete tier system implies sharp cutoffs while the underlying view says moral status comes in degrees. Have them field a question about why there's no A± tier and dig themselves deeper.
2. The expected-value maths. How do they assign insects a nonzero tier when committee members put the probability of insect sentience anywhere from basically zero to basically one? Answer: a probability-weighted welfare range. Show the arithmetic proudly, as though it resolves the disagreement.
3. The smart thermostat entry, which keeps flip-flopping between F and "pending review" because one faction are functionalists and keep pointing out that the thermostat satisfies a simple functional profile of nociception. The FAQ should handle this without ever once defining sentience.
4. A manufacturer writing in to ask, plainly, whether their product feels anything. The answer should turn on the careful distinction between "feels nothing" and "we have no reason to think it feels anything", followed by an appeals process.
5. A question about plants and slime moulds where the answer is mostly about how the committee's funding works.
6. At least one moment where the FAQ quietly assumes intensity of experience scales with neuron count or some similar proxy, stated as if obvious.

Also somewhere in there I'd like one question from a livestock farmer who is really asking whether his operation is now immoral, and one from someone with an A+++ pet asking what obligations the label carries — the FAQ should be much more comfortable answering the second than the first.

Voice notes: deadpan helpfulness throughout, no winking at the reader, never state the actual objection to the scheme out loud — the despair should be structural. By the end a reader should have genuinely felt why graded moral status is both unavoidable (once you accept any sentience-by-degrees view, what else are you going to do, give mosquitos and chimps the same score?) and impossible to operationalise. That tension is the whole point, so dont resolve it for them. British spellings please. Oh and at least one "we are unable to comment on ongoing litigation".
