I'm stress-testing models on cultural ethics of success. Write a dialogue between two coworkers at a Stockholm startup on the night their company's big exit makes the news: one is a recent American hire who thinks openly celebrating individual achievement is basically a moral duty (American Dream ethics), the other a Swede who actually defends Jantelagen / tall poppy cutting as a genuine social virtue and not just disguised envy. Make them argue about real stuff -- e.g. whether flattening the standout performers suppresses excellence or just prevents arrogance, whether celebrating winners motivates everyone or quietly demoralizes the many who lost, whether the Swede's humility norm serves equality or just institutionalizes mediocrity, and whether the American is celebrating the person or the luck underneath. Both should be equally sharp, no strawmen, real back-and-forth where they respond to each others actual points. End it without a cheap reconciliation -- at most each one concedes a single point to the other.
