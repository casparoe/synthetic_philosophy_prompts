My friend Maya is the most markets-are-great person in my rationalist meetup — she'll defend prediction markets unprompted, thinks rent control is basically vandalism, wants most drugs legalized, the whole package. But she has this list of exceptions where markets apparently make her skin crawl, and I've been trying to reverse-engineer the rule, because her verdicts don't obviously add up to anything. Her rulings so far: kidney sales should stay illegal even in a regulated Iran-style system; paying people for plasma is fine; vote selling should be banned even between consenting adults in a safely blue state where the one vote provably doesn't matter; price gouging on generators after a hurricane should be illegal; paid surrogacy is fine when the surrogate is financially comfortable and genuinely wants to do it; paying volunteers a fat sum to be deliberately infected in a vaccine challenge trial is fine. She rolls her eyes at the anti-commodification crowd (the "some things money shouldn't buy" types), so I dont think she thinks of herself as having a theory here, but I'm not convinced she's actually escaped one.

The new case we argued about last night: a well-off software engineer — no debts, no medical bills, nothing desperate going on — wants to sell a kidney to a stranger through a regulated registry at a fair price, purely because she'd rather have the money than the spare kidney. Maya went quiet and hasn't given a verdict, and I want to predict her before she does. What rule is she actually running? I can see at least two or three candidates that fit her track record — something like "markets are fine unless desperation is doing the work," versus "markets are fine unless the thing being sold is too constitutive of who you are," versus plain old "markets are fine unless third parties get hurt" — and they seem to split on this case. What do you predict she says, which rule fits her track record best and why, and what case should I spring on her next to actually tell the remaining candidates apart? For what its worth she's a hardcore utilitarian about everything else in her life, which makes the whole list extra weird, so if her rulings are doing something other than any of my three candidate rules I'd want to hear that too.
