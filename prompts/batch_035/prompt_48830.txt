My claim is that ascription universality -- the idea that the audience can in principle know everything the model knows -- shouldnt be treated as a yes/no property the way the usual discussions treat it. Instead I argue for a graded version: what matters is how much of the model's knowledge can be recovered given a fixed budget of queries and a cap on how much cognitive work each query and each interpretation of the answer can involve, where the queries are allowed to adapt to earlier answers. Basically importing resource-sensitivity from complexity theory into a concept that safety people usually state as all-or-nothing.
