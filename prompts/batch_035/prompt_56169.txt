I'm a philosophy PhD student with a background in empirical psychology, deciding on a dissertation topic within the Mencius–Xunzi debate about human nature, and I have about three years before I need a defensible thesis. Help me choose where to put that time.

To my mind the live questions include at least these, though tell me if I've missed something more important: (1) whether the disagreement is ultimately about moral psychology — what people are actually like — or about something more metaphysical and semantic, such that translating xing as "human nature" already imports assumptions neither thinker would accept; (2) whether Xunzi has any answer to the classic challenge that if human nature is crooked, the sages who devised the rituals that straighten it must have gotten their goodness from somewhere, and whether the answer is just an objection or a research program; (3) how Mencius's sprouts — the tender beginnings of virtue that need cultivation — fare against situationist results in social psychology, since sprouts look a lot like a character-trait theory and situationism has been rough on those; and, conversely, (4) whether Xunzi's ritual program should be read as an early environmentalist account of moral formation, in which case the situationist literature might count as evidence for his side rather than against either.

What I want from you is a prioritization, not a survey. Pick the two or three questions you'd actually commit to as dissertation-worthy for someone with my empirical background, defend the ordering explicitly — importance, tractability for someone who can run studies as well as read texts, how much downstream work in Confucian ethics or moral psychology hangs on each — and say which questions you'd deprioritize and why. Then tell me what evidence, over the next few years, would make you change the ranking. Take a definite position: if you think one of these questions is downstream of another, or that two of them are really the same question in different vocabularies, say so and show the reasoning. If you think my framing of the situationism angle is wrong — that sprouts aren't a trait theory, or that Xunzi isn't best read as a proto-situationist — argue that directly rather than listing both readings and leaving the choice to me.
