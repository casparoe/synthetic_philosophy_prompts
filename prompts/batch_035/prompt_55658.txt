His whole thing is that the men of Reserve Police Battalion 101 were genuinely willing executioners -- that what drove the shootings at places like Józefów was deep eliminationist antisemitism in German culture, not the situationist stuff Browning leaned on in Ordinary Men (obedience to authority, conformity, peer pressure, war brutalization etc).
