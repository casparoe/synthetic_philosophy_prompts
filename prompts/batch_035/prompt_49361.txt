So my actual credence that there's genuine resultant moral luck -- that the unlucky driver really is more blameworthy, not just more punished or more pitied or more resented -- is somewhere around 0.7. My roommate says 0.25 at most, though she'll admit the legal practice is fine on pure deterrence grounds.
