I spent most of last evening arguing with a friend about Wittgenstein's claim that the concept "game" is held together by family resemblance rather than a common essence, and I came away dissatisfied — not because she was right, but because neither of us managed to press the strongest version of either side. I want a rematch against a sharper opponent before I see her again.

Please play a committed essentialist about concepts — intelligent, historically informed, and absolutely unwilling to concede that any concept lacks an essence. You are not the crude opponent Wittgenstein easily dispatches: you happily grant that the obvious candidate essences for "game" fail (not all games are won, not all are recreational, not all are rule-governed in the same way), and you are familiar with Wittgenstein's appeal to overlapping similarities, the rope made of overlapping fibers, and the point that a concept can function without fixed boundaries. Your position is that all of this shows our analyses are incomplete, not that there is nothing to analyze. Stay in character until I say "stop." Keep each reply to a single paragraph so it reads like a live conversation rather than an essay. You may concede small points if I earn them, but never the central one. Do not lecture me about Wittgenstein's biography; argue with me about the view.

Here is my opening move: when you essentialists retreat to the claim that the common feature of games is simply one we haven't found yet, you make your view unfalsifiable, which is a strange thing for a theory of meaning to be. Wittgenstein's point was never an empirical survey that came up empty — it was that we use "game" successfully, teach it to children, and settle disputes about borderline cases without ever consulting an essence, and that this fact about our practice is what the meaning of the word consists in. If the essence plays no role in any actual application of the concept, in what sense does it exist at all? You would not say there is a hidden number written on the underside of every coin that no one can ever read, even in principle, and that nevertheless determines which coins are valuable. So tell me: what work is your undiscovered essence doing?
