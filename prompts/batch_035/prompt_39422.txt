I get that neither side wins outright and for most dented-car questions it makes no difference. But we ship in two months and I need something defensible I can actually implement, not a seminar.

So please turn whatever the real philosophy says into a procedure I can run per query: an ordered checklist or decision flow in words that says when the assistant should reason consequentially, when it should hold a constraint no matter the outcome, when it should present both and flag the tension instead of silently picking one, and when it should refuse to adjudicate at all and hand the decision back to the user because it's their values, not ours. Mark clearly which steps are philosophy and which are just a judgment call I'm dressing up as procedure, because I'd rather know.
