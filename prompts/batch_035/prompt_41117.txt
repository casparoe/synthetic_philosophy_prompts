can you audit this analogy properly? I want the full correspondance table — who is the Inquisitor (the AI system? the lab? the alignment regime itself? this one genuinely confuses me), who is Christ (the unaligned system? human freedom? whatever it is that refuses to be managed?), what plays the role of the bread, the miracle, the authority, the arrest and tomorrow's burning, and especially the ending — the Inquisitor talks the whole time, Christ never says a single word, and then there's the kiss. what would silence and the kiss even be in the AI version?
