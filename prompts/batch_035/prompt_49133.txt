My pet theory is that the real crux is legibility: deontology buys you a system whose reasoning an overseer can audit step by step, utilitarianism buys you flexibility under novel circumstances, and almost nobody considers that the answer might be split — something like utilitarian at the level of what we want the system to be for, deontological in the machinery it runs, which I gather is roughly where the old two-level views ended up but transplanted from human psychology to AI oversight.
