My psychiatrist wants me to start on an SSRI, and before I sign the consent form I keep running into stories from people who say the med changed what they care about -- like they came out the other side as someone who no longer wants the things they used to want, and who's fine with that. The form lists physical side effects really carefully but says nothing about changes to my values or personality, and honestly I don't see how it could. So what does the whole informed consent apparatus actually presuppose about decisions like this? It seems to assume I can weigh the outcome from where I'm standing now, but if the treatment changes the very person doing the weighing, that looks circular. Is the consent I'd give even meaningful, or does the practice quietly presuppose some view where changes to your preferences just don't count as things you need to be warned about? And is that consistent with how consent works elsewhere in medicine, where they make doctors disclose much smaller stuff?
