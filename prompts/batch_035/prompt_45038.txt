My friend, who works in ML, leans on this picture all the time and now that I've read the source I want to check how far it actually carries. His line is: training a model is raising a child. From this he concludes that worrying about specifying or programming values into AI is a category error, and that since ordinary moral education mostly works out, training rather than programming is the safe and natural route, so the fretting about alignment is mostly misplaced.
