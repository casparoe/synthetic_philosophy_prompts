I've never studied philosophy, all of this comes from arguments down the pub and thinking about my own life, so bear with me. I tried writing down what I actually believe about whose business is what, and I'd like someone to check whether it's a consistent set or whether some of it secretly cancels other bits out:

1. Some stuff is mine to decide full stop — my body, what I read, who I marry, what I do with my evenings. On that stuff nobody else's preference should be able to outvote mine, no matter how many of them there are or how strongly they feel. I'm not claiming my choices only affect me, obviously they ripple onto other people. I just think the final say is mine.

2. If literally everyone affected prefers one outcome to another, you should never end up with the other one. If every single person in the flat prefers plan A to plan B, doing B would just be mad. Feels like the absolute floor of any sensible way of deciding things together.

3. I can't see a principled way to separate "real" preferences from nosy ones. My neighbour caring that my house gets painted pink is just as much a preference as me caring it stays blue. Writing off the ones about other people's stuff as "not counting" just rigs the game before it starts.

4. I'm all for communities voting on shared things — our building takes a residents' vote on the shared garden and I'd be furious if some landlord simply overruled it.

5. Two real situations where I found out what I actually think. (a) My sister's vegan and honestly believes what I cook at my own table is partly her business. Her preference is sincere and strong, but no, it doesn't get to count against mine there. (b) Our building also voted to ban drying laundry on the balconies because it looks awful, and one neighbour says my balcony my business — I think he's wrong and the vote was fine.

I can't spot the problem myself but theres a nagging feeling that not all of this can be true at once. Don't give me a tour of every possible tension — tell me which of them are real, rank them by how bad they are, and if something has to give then say which one and why. Take an actual position even if youre not certain, I'd much rather you commit than lay out options and leave me to pick.
