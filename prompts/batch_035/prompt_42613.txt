I'm a graduate student in philosophy of physics, and I keep running into the slogan "information is physical" — the line Rolf Landauer is famous for — in seminars on Maxwell's demon. This week it did a lot of work in an argument I wasn't sure I followed. We were discussing the Szilard engine, and a classmate declared the demon "exorcised": since the demon must record and then reset its memory, and erasing each bit dissipates at least kT ln 2 of heat, the second law is saved, and this, she said, is just what it means for information to be physical. Someone else objected that the slogan was doing the argument's work illegitimately, since "information is physical" could mean anything from a platitude about memory requiring hardware to a substantive and possibly false thesis about entropy. Nobody could articulate exactly which reading the exorcism needs, so the exchange dissolved.

I'd like a precisification I can bring to the next meeting. Please lay out, as an ordered menu, the distinct precise claims the slogan could be expressing — I'd expect at least these to appear, though add others if they're genuinely distinct: (a) the near-triviality that information is always instantiated in some physical degrees of freedom, with no encoding possible without a carrier; (b) Landauer's principle as a quantitative bound on the thermodynamic cost of logically irreversible operations; (c) the stronger identification of Shannon entropy with thermodynamic entropy, so that information is a state variable on par with energy; (d) a methodological thesis that information-theoretic constraints are constitutive of thermodynamic laws themselves rather than derived from them; and (e) any metaphysical reading on which information is fundamental, in the vicinity of Wheeler's "it from bit." For each reading, say whether it is trivial, substantive, or false; whether it would actually support my classmate's use of it, i.e., whether it entails that the demon's memory erasure restores the second law; and whether it would support the more radical use of the slogan to dismiss entire research programs as conceptually confused.

Two further requests. First, where the underlying physics is genuinely unsettled — for instance, whether Landauer's principle can be derived from the second law without circularity, whether the principle is needed to block the demon or the second law blocks the demon on its own, and how cleanly logical and thermodynamic irreversibility actually come apart — please mark this explicitly rather than presenting one side as settled consensus. Second, please distinguish what has been experimentally confirmed from what remains a matter of theoretical dispute, so I can tell which parts of the exorcism story are physics and which are philosophy wearing a lab coat.
