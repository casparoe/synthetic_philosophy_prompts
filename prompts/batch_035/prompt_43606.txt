"(a) The Pareto frontier is the segment from (4, 6) to (10, 0): points with u1 + u2 = 10, for u1 between 4 and 10. The Nash solution maximizes u1·u2 over S. Points strictly below the frontier can't beat this, and on the edge from (0, 0) to (4, 6) the product is at most 24 (at (4, 6)), so (5, 5) is the Nash solution.

(c) The Kalai–Smorodinsky solution gives up Nash's independence of irrelevant alternatives. In its place it satisfies individual monotonicity: if one player's maximum attainable payoff increases (everything else fixed), that player's payoff in the solution should not fall.
