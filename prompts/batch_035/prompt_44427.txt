My understanding is that this is roughly the shape of global workspace theory, and that the standard philosophical complaint is that it explains access consciousness — what is globally available for report and control — while remaining silent on phenomenal consciousness, i.e. whether there is anything it is like to be the system at all.

Second, the transcript gives the system articulate self-reports about why it did what it did, and I genuinely cannot tell whether the question of whether anything is experienced in there is a further question or a confused one.
