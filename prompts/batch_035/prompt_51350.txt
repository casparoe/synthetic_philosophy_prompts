My study partner and I are doing a seminar on self-locating belief, and her positions have gotten weirdly hard to pin down. Her verdicts so far: classic Sleeping Beauty, she says 1/3 on heads when she wakes up. Version where tails gets her three awakenings instead of two, she didn't even pause: 1/4. If Beauty is told it's Monday, credence in heads goes back up to 1/2, she says, no problem. But then when we got to Bostrom's presumptuous philosopher case she was completely against the SIA answer — thinks its insane to prefer the theory with trillions of people with no evidence whatsoever, and she reckons the Doomsday argument is a mistake too.

Next seminar we're each supposed to bring two new cases and predict the others verdicts. Mine are: (a) Extreme Beauty — fair coin, heads means one awakening, tails means a million awakenings with full amnesia in between, what's her heads credence on waking; and (b) the creation version — a fair coin is flipped before anyone exists, heads means one person is created in a white room, tails means a million people are created in white rooms, and you find yourself awake in a white room knowing all this.

What will she say for each, and why? I'm less fussed about the exact numbers than about which rule(s) would actually generate her four earlier verdicts — whether those rules agree or diverge on my two cases, and if there's a further case that would really tell them apart, what it is.
