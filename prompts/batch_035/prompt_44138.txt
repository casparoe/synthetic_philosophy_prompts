so my bioethics reading group is doing the badness of pain this term, and I've been asked to write up my actual position on it so the others can tear into it. heres what I've got — tell me whether it hangs together. (1) I'm a hedonist about wellbeing: how things feel from the inside fixes what's good or bad for you, and nothing outside the feeling adds or subtracts value. (2) The clinical picture of pain asymbolia seems to show that the sensation of pain and its badness come apart — these patients report the pain at full intensity, they can locate it and describe it, they even flinch at the pinprick, but they insist they're not bothered at all, and when the pain is bad enough to need treating they take the treatment about as emotionally as running an errand. (3) So my line has been: the badness of ordinary pain lies entirely in the aversive component, the "bothering," not in the sensation — an asymbolic's pain is not bad for them, nothing bad is happening to them. (4) I also think the sensation is fully preserved in asymbolia: what's missing isn't part of the feeling but something like an attitude toward the feeling — that's how I read the fact that they rate it just as intense and describe it just as well. (5) On the pleasure side I refuse to make the same move. I think there's no such thing as pleasure asymbolia, and that if a sensation stops pleasing you it simply isn't pleasant anymore — pleasantness isn't an attitude you take to a sensation, it's part of the sensation itself. (6) And here's the verdict I cant shake: suppose an asymbolic patient needs a painful procedure, assume it does no tissue damage and they wont remember any of it — I still think it's wrong to skip the analgesia, and wrong for their sake, not just callous.

So go through the set and tell me: which of these actually fight each other, which only look like they do, and what's the cheapest repair for each real tension? Please rank them by severity rather than listing everything as equally bad, and at the end tell me which single revision would clean up the most. And dont try to guess which one I'm attached to — I genuinely don't know, that's the whole reason I'm asking before I present this.
