I have a feeling some of this is off — I thought Dennett's actual move was to deny qualia as defined (with those special properties), not to deny that experience has any character at all, and something about the Chase and Sanborn bit sounds wrong, though I can't put my finger on it. Can you go claim by claim and tell me which parts a Dennettian would accept, which are misrepresentations, and whether the closing claim about the stranger with the scanner follows from the parts that are actually right?
