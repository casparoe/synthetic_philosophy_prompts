Delay a conception by a month and a different child is born. So when a nation adopts a policy of depleting its resources, the people alive centuries later, under depletion, are different people from those who would have lived under conservation. This was Parfit's own conclusion: as long as future people are not worse off than non-existence, we cannot have wronged them. And since morality, on his slogan, is about making people happy rather than about making happy people, our duties to future generations turn out to be much weaker than we assumed.
