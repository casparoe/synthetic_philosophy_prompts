Now here's the new case I'm stuck on. I'm counting on them to rush to the river. I've said nothing against my own mind, but the whole point of my sentence is that they come away believing something false. What would Augustine make of it: is it a lie, is it permitted, or does he have some third verdict? So predict his verdict, tell me which rule you think he's really running and why, and then give me one further case that would best seperate the two rules.
