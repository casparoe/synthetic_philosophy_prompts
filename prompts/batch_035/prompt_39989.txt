explain what the live positions are, which one you'd actually endorse and why, and whether any of this should make us feel weird about choosing between embryos at all (is it choosing between children, or choosing which child exists, or neither?). under 300 words please — i'm not writing a dissertation, i just want to know how to think about my own kid before she exists.
