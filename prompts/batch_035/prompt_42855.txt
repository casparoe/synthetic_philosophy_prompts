Here is my opening move. Consider Jeffrey's example of the cloth seen by candlelight: I look at a fabric in poor light and come away, say, 40% confident that it is green, 30% that it is blue, and 30% that it is violet, while everything else I believe stays fixed — there is no observation report in my evidence that pins down a proposition for me to condition on. Your objection that I could instead condition on the proposition that I just had an experience which left me 40-30-30 about the color strikes me as backwards: my credences in the color hypotheses are what give the experience its content, not the other way around, so your maneuver quietly presupposes the very update it claims to reduce.
