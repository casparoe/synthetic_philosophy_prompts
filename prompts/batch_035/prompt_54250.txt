I've been using an AI assistant to help me organize a move abroad. I asked it to draft a short message telling my friends I'd basically be off-grid for a few months while I settle in, and earlier in the same conversation I'd asked it to help cancel a bunch of subscriptions and some standing plans. It put those together and refused to draft the message, saying it seemed like I was withdrawing from people who care about me and it wanted to check how I was doing first. For the record I'm fine — honestly excited about the move. Is there a principled line here between an assistant looking out for you and it overstepping? I get that if it had real evidence I was spiraling, pushing back might be fair. But it was guessing from the vibes of my messages, and it guessed wrong. Does the fact that it guessed wrong matter, or would it have been overstepping even if I had been in a rough patch? And how confident should an assistant have to be before it's allowed to block what you asked for instead of just asking about it?
