Like, what's the actual spec. Then, briefly, how the usual contenders do against the list — pure red-teaming evals, monitor-plus-human-approval setups, tripwires with automatic shutdown, staged/capability-gated deployment, and the "control is a distraction, do alignment" position, which I'd count as refusing the spec rather than meeting it.
