The setup: my two central characters are sisters-in-law — Marieke, who grew up in Rotterdam and tells you to your face that your cake is dry, and Aiko, who spent fifteen years in Osaka working as an editor and would sooner die than tell an author her first fifty pages are unsalvageable. The big set-piece of part two is a family dinner where they finally have it out over whether white lies are wrong. Not a screaming match — these are both smart women in their fifties — but a real argument, the kind that's been simmering for years.

What I want from you is the dialogue itself, maybe 800-1200 words, and I want it to be an actual philosophical fight, not cultural stereotyping with a thin coat of paint.
