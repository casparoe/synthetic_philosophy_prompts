Longtime lurker on LessWrong-adjacent spaces here, and after all these years I still don't have a stated policy on METI — only gut verdicts. Can someone extract the principle out of them for me? Here's the raw data.

The Voyager golden record and the Pioneer plaque: fine. Whoever finds those is already parked in our solar system, so they've found us. The Arecibo message from the 70s, beamed at a globular cluster some 25,000 light years off: fine, I think, because the reply arrives 50,000 years from now, and the "we" that receives it just isn't the "we" doing the sending. A one-shot message to a star ~20 light years out, which is roughly what the Cosmic Call people did in the 90's: not fine. A permanent omnidirectional beacon that screams our location forever: extremely not fine. Our ordinary TV and radar leakage: can't be helped, so no verdict. And the argument "we've been leaking for a century anyway, so one more deliberate message changes nothing" — I'm sure it's wrong, but notice that my verdicts on the Arecibo case make it awkward to explain exactly why.

Two more data points. The Cosmic Call was arranged by a private outfit rather than any government, and that somehow makes it feel worse to me, though I can't defend that at all. And the case I keep flip-flopping on: suppose we receive a clear signal from 50 light years away, and the obvious move is to reply. Is that fine — they sort of asked, after all — or have we just handed a stranger our exact address?

So: is there a single principle behind all this, or close? The two candidates I half-see are "only send what the recipients could detect about us anyway" and "only send at ranges where the round trip outlasts our civilization," and I suspect the real answer is some conjunction of them, but when I try the conjunction on the reply case it goes silent. Give me the principle stated precisely, tell me which of my verdicts it has to throw overboard (I assume the private-company one, but check whether it's that one or the Arecibo one), and say what it predicts for a case I haven't listed.
