The jury apparently treated this huge number as close to the probability that she was innocent; the conviction was upheld on a first appeal but quashed in 2003, partly because the pathologist had withheld lab results suggesting the younger baby died of natural causes, and the Royal Statistical Society put out a statement saying the statistical evidence had been misused.

What I want from you is an analysis of this episode through each of the three main interpretations of probability, and I want it to run in both directions — what each interpretation says about the trial, and what the trial says about the interpretation.
