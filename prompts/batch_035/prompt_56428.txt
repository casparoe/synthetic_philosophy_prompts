The latest one sums up Dennett's multiple drafts model roughly like this: 'Dennett pictured the brain as a newsroom working on many stories at once: drafts of each report circulate and get edited in parallel, and there is never a final edition, because in his view there is no single place in the head where it all comes together for an audience — what he called the Cartesian theatre.
