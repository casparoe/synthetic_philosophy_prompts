Here's the assignment. Everyone assumes akrasia and hyperbolic discounting are basically two descriptions of the same thing: you judge it better to diet/save/sleep, but the smaller-sooner reward is right there and your discount curve flips your preference in the moment. The hard part, and this is the real spec: the akratic act must not be locally preferred at any moment. There has to be no smaller-sooner reward. My hunch is the direction to look is inhibiting failure rather than attraction -- the akratic option is a non-action, like staying silent out of paralysis, and the thing preventing the judged-better act is something like fear or self-consciousness that doesn't register as a reward.
