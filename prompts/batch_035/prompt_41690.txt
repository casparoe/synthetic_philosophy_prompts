"According to Lewis's counterfactual theory, causation is not simply counterfactual dependence but the ancestral of that relation. Explain why Lewis needs the ancestral formulation. Then explain how Woodward's interventionist theory proposes to distinguish genuine causes from mere correlates, using the case of a falling barometer reading and an approaching storm. Finally, evaluate whether either theory cleanly handles early preemption, where one assassin shoots and a backup would have shot had the first missed."

Here is one student's answer, which I reproduce in full:

"Lewis's central claim is that c causes e just in case, had c not occurred, e would not have occurred. But this condition holds too rarely to be the whole story, since most effects do not depend counterfactually on any single one of their distal causes. So Lewis defines causation as the ancestral of counterfactual dependence: if e depends on d and d depends on c, then c causes e even though e may not depend directly on c. This also fits his view that causation is an intrinsic matter, determined by the character of the events and their sequence itself. On the barometer: the falling reading does not cause the storm because there is no counterfactual dependence in either direction — they are joint effects of falling pressure, so had the barometer read differently, the storm would still come. Interventionism gives a cleaner diagnosis: Woodward says X causes Y when there is a possible intervention on X that changes Y, where an intervention is an actual manipulation, typically performed by an experimenter, that sets X to some value. Since an experimenter can wiggle the barometer needle without touching the atmosphere, the intervention exposes the correlation as non-causal. Preemption is where Lewis struggles: because the backup would have fired, the victim's death does not counterfactually depend on the first shot, so Lewis cannot count the first assassin as a cause. Interventionism handles the case straightforwardly: if we intervene to keep the first shooter from firing, the outcome changes, so the first shot is correctly identified as the cause and the backup is not."
