On this view project-stickiness has a genuine rational role: sometimes keeping the commitment is part of what makes you the kind of agent who can plan, be trusted, and have a life story rather than a pile of episodes. So sunk costs don't enter the ledger as reasons, but they mark which decisions are identity-loaded, and identity-loaded decisions legitimately get a thumb on the scale against switching.
