Half the room said obviously you take that deal, the other half got genuinely upset with the first half, and we ran out of time before anyone changed anyone's mind. I don't want another shouting match next month, I want a game.

So design something for me: a structured game where people make these choices over and over with actual (small) stakes — tokens or points, some participants playing scripted "bad actors" whose moves cost the group points, and bystanders who each round can either let the damage happen or intervene in a way that causes less total damage but permanently marks *them* as the one who crossed a line.
