The cases that any theory has to handle, off the top of my head — you'll probably think of others:

- cross-cuing: the hemispheres sneakily signaling to each other through peripheral cues — a patient glancing at something so the other half can pick up on it. so the two halves can cooperate badly instead of not at all.
