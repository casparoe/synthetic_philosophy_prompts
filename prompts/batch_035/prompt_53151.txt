Children manage most of these at a glance, and the problems historically gave machines a great deal of trouble. I am writing a paper on how concepts get induced from a handful of examples, and I want the specification before the theories: what must any adequate account of concept induction from sparse examples deliver — both to explain the human capacity and to serve as a benchmark for judging whether some system's concept-formation is humanlike?

Data poverty: a few positive examples plus six negatives are compatible with indefinitely many rules, so the account must explain what breaks the tie — grue in miniature.
