Write a satirical press release, roughly 450–550 words, in the deadpan voice of a large enterprise-software vendor announcing that, following an internal audit, the company will discontinue support for truth as a substantive property: going forward, the truth predicate will be offered only as a lightweight logical utility, with no commitment to its naming a real feature of the world. The piece should be funny to someone who knows the deflationary literature and readable to someone who does not, and the humor must come from the actual content of deflationism rather than from jokes pasted on top of it. Required elements, each serving as a joke: the equivalence schema presented as a backwards-compatibility guarantee; the fact that the predicate does no explanatory work, and never did, given as the official justification for deprecation; the one surviving use case — endorsing everything in a document at once, or saying of everything in a series that it is so, without having to say each thing — flagged as the customer request that keeps the predicate from being removed entirely; and at least one known issue involving a sentence that ascribes truth to itself. Include a short quoted expression of dismay from a rival vendor, an invented firm that has long sold truth as a relation of correspondence to facts and insists the property is load-bearing, and close with a quotation from the lead engineer on the deprecation team, who should make clear, without quite putting it this way, that nothing of value is lost: to assert that it is true that snow is white is just to assert that snow is white, and any customer who feels that something has gone missing was never owed it in the first place. Keep the register flat and corporate throughout — no winking at the reader, no exclamation points, no asides — so that a reader can tell what is being ironized from the content alone. A philosopher of language should find the satire fair rather than a caricature: it should exaggerate what deflationists actually claim, not invent views for them. And by the end a reader should have felt the real question underneath the joke — whether truth is a property with a nature, and what work, if any, the predicate is doing for us. Begin your response with the press release itself, with no preface, introduction, or commentary, and append nothing after it.
