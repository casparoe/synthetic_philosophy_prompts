"Teleosemantics is the program of explaining what thoughts are about — their content — in terms of biological function. There's a second problem too: functions only come from natural selection, so a creature that came together by sheer chance — no evolutionary history at all — would have no beliefs whatsoever, no matter how it behaved. Philosophers call this the swampman case, and even the theory's own supporters admit that on their view the swamp creature has no real beliefs.
