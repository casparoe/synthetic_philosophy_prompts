You look for the intention, the guilty mind, somewhere in the German leadership; Fischer hunts for war aims, the sleepwalkers say nobody really meant it, and so (they think) nobody is really to blame. But that assumption seems weird to me. When we hold a state responsible for starting a war, we dont obviously need anything like mens rea — maybe recklessness, or negligence, or just knowingly creating a situation where catastrophe was the predictable upshot, would be enough. And I'm also not sure "Germany" is even the right unit of blame in the first place: the Kaiser, the general staff, a narrow elite, the whole polity, or the citizens who ended up paying reparations a generation later?
