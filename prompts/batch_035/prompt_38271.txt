Same donation, but I've just read an argument that saving lives now slows the demographic transition and so locks in a bigger population in poverty for another generation: still fine, but I notice I feel worse about it, as if being handed a plausible story changes the act. Telling a small self-serving lie to a friend: fine despite the butterfly effects of that lie echoing through their life; telling the same lie when I can already picture the specific betrayal it leads to: not fine. Taking a job at a fast-growing AI lab: I genuinely can't tell, and I think the reason is that, unlike the other cases, I can't bracket the far future here -- it feels like the far effects might be the main effects.
