He said it's basically a theorem, discovered by the economist Charles Goodhart in the 70s, that the moment you tie anything to a metric — bonuses, evaluations, whatever — people start optimizing the metric instead of the thing it was a proxy for, until the metric is completely worthless. So in his view every KPI system is doomed to fail, and the only real defense is keeping the metric secret from the people being measured, so they can't game what they can't see.
