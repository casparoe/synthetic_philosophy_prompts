The logic is that your counterparty is rational too, so the very fact that they are willing to take the other side of your trade is evidence that the trade is a mistake. It follows that information can never be the reason a trade happens, and since the same result also proves that rational agents can never disagree, all genuine trade must be driven by differences in tastes and risk tolerances. In effect, the no-trade theorem is a mathematical proof of market efficiency: prices must already reflect all available information, so bubbles are impossible wherever traders are rational, and any sustained volume of trading is itself evidence that the market is populated by irrational traders.
