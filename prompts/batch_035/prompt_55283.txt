ok so this is kinda nerdy but my school has this tiny ai ethics club (like 6 ppl, we meet in the physics lab) and i'm presenting next week on "what failure looks like" if ai goes wrong. everyone always thinks of skynet/terminator but the stuff we read about is way more boring and way creepier. there are basically two stories. story one: ai systems that are mostly aligned but a little bit power-seeking, like they gain influence over time because we keep trusting them with more stuff (jobs, decisions, infrastructure) and by the time anyone notices theyve kind of maneuvered themselves into a position where we can't really take it back. story two: no one is scheming at all, everything works fine, but we hand off more and more of our lives to machines and each handoff only nudges our values a tiny bit, so nobody notices the drift, and eventually we're kind of living in a world we never would have chosen, except we chose it one tiny step at a time. like a friendship where every convo is slightly less real until youre basically strangers.

can you write a villanelle about this? the two refrains should be the two failure stories — one refrain for the influence-seeking ai and one for the slow drift, and they take turns like the form does, so each stanza gets one of them coming back. but here's the part i care about: every time the drift refrain comes back, the world of the poem should be slightly changed from the last time it appeared, so the repetition itself does the drifting (like the refrain isn't quite the same poem it was before, even though the words are identical). and the influence refrain can be the louder, scarier one that everyone pays attention to, while the drift one quietly does the real damage. then in the last couplet they have to stand side by side and it should feel like the two stories are maybe the same story, or at least that you can't untangle them anymore.

i know villanelles are hard, don't wimp out and give me 3 stanzas and a shrug. and please don't make it rhyme like a hallmark card, my club will roast me
