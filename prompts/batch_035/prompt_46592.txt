One of our school board members saw a draft and is pushing the principal to either spike the piece or run it with a disclaimer, because we had a really ugly creationism fight at this school like 8 years ago and nobody wants a repeat. So the board has to choose: run it as a regular opinion piece, run it paired with a counterpoint, run it with a disclaimer, or don't run it at all.
