every year in my advanced metaphysics seminar we do a week on causation by omission, and every year i find myself confidently asserting a package of views that i've never actually sat down and checked for consistency. so before i stand up in front of the students again, here's the package. poke holes in it:

1. omissions really cause things. the gardener who fails to water the flowers caused them to die; the medic who fails to give the antidote caused the patient's death. not "as if" causation, not a façon de parler — genuine causation, same relation as when the arsonist strikes the match.

2. i'm a positive-facts-only person about ontology. there are no negative events, no absences in the inventory of what exists. the world is built out of what happens, full stop. nothing in my ontology is a hole.

3. i'm a realist about causation. whether c caused e is not up to our explanatory interests, our expectations, or any norms. it's as objective as anything else in the world.

4. i'm roughly lewisian: singular causal claims are made true by counterfactual dependence, of the right (sometimes somewhat complicated) sort. dependence is the truthmaker for causation.

5. here's one i actually keep chewing on. the queen also didn't water my flowers, and the queen did NOT cause them to die. only the gardener did. i think that asymetry is a fact about the world, not a shadow cast by our conventions or by who we happen to find worth talking about.

6. and one more: i think an absence of rain can cause a famine even in a world with no agents, no expectations, and no norms about weather at all.

my TA swears some of these can't all be true and i genuinely can't see which ones she means. go through the pairs, tell me which are in real tension and which only look like it, rank them by severity, and for each real one say what the cheapest fix is and what that fix would cost me elsewhere in the package. i want to know whether this collapses or whether it's basically fine with one tweak, and i don't want to be lectured on what lewis actually said — i want an audit of MY set.
