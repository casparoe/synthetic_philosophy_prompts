- AC beating DC for the power grid: AC wins again in basically every rerun — the physics of long-distance transmission picks it out. Convergent.
- English as the language of science: split decision. Some single language ends up dominating, but which one is a coin flip of empire and timing.
- Standard railway gauge, the 4'8.5" one that traces back to English coal wagons: pure accident, several gauges could've won just as easily. Contingent.
- Germ theory: rediscovered sooner or later in every run, the evidence just forces it. Maybe later in some runs, but you get there. Convergent.
- VHS over Betamax: contingent, could've gone the other way and nobody would've noticed.
- Coinage: another split — she says something coin-like shows up everywhere, people kept inventing it independently, but gold and silver specifically being the backbone is not locked in. Could've been shells or grain-backed credit or tea bricks or whatever.
