Design an in-class activity built around the experience of trying to cross the gap and (mostly) failing. Please structure your response with these sections, in this order: (1) Materials and setup, roughly 100 words; (2) Round 1 rules and strike procedure, roughly 150 words; (3) Round 2 rules, roughly 120 words; (4) Debrief plan, roughly 250 words; (5) Predictable failure points and how to handle them, roughly 100 words; (6) An honest note on which parts of the metaethical debate — e.g., expressivist or Cornell-realist responses to the gap — no classroom game can reproduce, roughly 80 words.
