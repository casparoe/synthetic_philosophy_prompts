My friend and I argue constantly about when someone's a hypocrite for calling you out — basically when does a person lose the standing to blame you for a thing. Her rulings so far: our flatmate who leaves exactly the kitchen mess she nags me about has no standing to nag; her dad, who quit smoking fifteen years ago and now goes on at her about her vaping, does have standing; a cousin who did the same selfish thing to his mum once years ago, never apologised for it but doesn't do it anymore, still has standing to call my friend out for doing it now; a mate who openly says he does the same thing and isn't sorry, but that she really shouldn't do it, has no standing even though she agrees his criticism is actually correct; and a friend who blames her for speeding while speeding himself, on the grounds that his speeding is different because he's a better driver, has no standing. Now a new one's come up: a colleague who has never gossiped maliciously in her life, but has scheduled a properly vicious email about another colleague to go out Monday morning, and who today calls my friend out for her gossiping. Will my friend say she has standing here? What rule is she even running — I can see at least two that fit everything she's said so far — what does each one predict on this case, and is there some further case that would really seperate the rules?
