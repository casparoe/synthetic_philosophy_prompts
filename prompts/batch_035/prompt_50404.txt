ok so i've been circling the approval-directed agents idea (paul christiano, rationalist blogosphere years back) forever and I finally wrote down what I actually believe about it, and now I can't tell if the list coheres:

(1) the thing we want from early transformative AI is corrigibility — an agent that defers to correction instead of optimizing past us.
(2) approval-direction delivers it: pick the action the overseer would most approve of, computed fresh at each decision, no long-horizon objective, and that's why approval-directed agents don't do the instrumental convergence thing the way goal-directed ones do.
(3) the approval should be of the decision given a fully transparent description of what it does and why — would I approve of this decision if I understood everything about it — not just of the outcome.
(4) reflective approval is stable enough to build on: what we'd endorse on reflection is a fixed enough target to point a system at.
(5) approval-directed vs goal-directed is a difference in kind, not a relabeling. this is basically why i care about any of it.

the thing i keep hitting: don't (3) and (4) together turn approval into a fixed latent target? if ideal-me's endorsement is stable and the agent is maximizing it against full descriptions of actions, that just IS a goal-directed agent with my reflective endorsement as the goal, and then (2)'s corrigibility story and (5) collaps. maybe the locality of evaluation blocks that somehow, like evaluating each decision on its own means there's no gradient toward seizing power to lock in future approvals? but i can't reconstruct that argument and part of me suspects it's cope. and if the fix is to index approval to current-me instead of ideal-me, corrigibility turns into "the agent is exactly as good as we are right now", which seems like a much worse property and maybe not even the thing (1) was after.

also (1) and (3) feel like they might clash — deferring to actual-me while acting on what ideal-me would approve looks like it bypasses us — but i half suspect that one's fine, because letting us correct it is itself what ideal-me approves of. half suspect.

go through the pairs. which tensions are real, which ones dissolve once you look at them properly, and what's the cheapest single revision that cleans up the most? don't be diplomatic about which one has to go.
