Counting first-place votes, Aldous got 4, Burke got 3, and Cho got 2, so we awarded the contract to Aldous. Before it was signed, Cho withdrew its bid entirely (we learned Cho had quoted us a price it never intended to honor). One board member proposed that, since we now had only two live options, we should simply recount the ballots with Cho struck from them and award the contract to whoever wins between Aldous and Burke. We did, and Burke won 5–4: everyone who had ranked Aldous first had ranked Cho second and Burke last, while Burke's and Cho's supporters had all ranked Burke ahead of Aldous. So the contract went to Burke.

Two of my colleagues are now at each other's throats.
