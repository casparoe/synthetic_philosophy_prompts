My sister and I have been donating to opposite sides of the abortion fight for years — I'm firmly pro-choice, she's firmly pro-life, and we each put a couple hundred dollars a year into our side's big advocacy organizations. Partly to needle each other, honestly. Her pitch is that right now we're basically just spending money to cancel each other out, like two countries in an arms race, and that if we redirect the money to stuff where we actually agree on the goal, more good happens by both of our lights. My gut says I'm being scammed somewhere but I can't put my finger on where. Is this actually a fair deal for me given what I beleive, or does the symmetry hide an asymmetry? And separately — if the numbers do work out in my favor, is there still something wrong with "trading away" a cause I think of as a matter of basic rights, the way you maybe shouldn't trade away a hostage?
