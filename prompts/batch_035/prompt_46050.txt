The Classic Maya southern lowlands: NOT collapse, just change — she says the cities emptied out but the people moved north and kept doing Maya things, so calling it a collapse is basically colonial nostalgia. Easter Island: definately collapse, the moai, the forests, the chiefdom, the whole thing coming down together. The Harappan/Indus situation after 1900 BC: change — cities emptied but people just dispersed into villages, no die-off, no violence (she admits this is the one she's least sure about). And the Old Kingdom Egypt wobble into the First Intermediate Period: change, because the state re-formed within a century or so with the same script, same gods, basically the same people.
