My band never officially broke up. Our drummer moved across the country, the rest of us quietly stopped booking rehearsals, and no one ever said anything. Now two of the guys are fighting about it in the group chat. Jan says we did break up, just without anyone deciding to: everyone could see where things were heading, we each adjusted in light of seeing the others adjust, and at some point enough people had stepped back that the band was over — a group-level thing that no single one of us did. Tom says that's mysticism: a band is just four guys with their own plans, and each of us privately deprioritized it; the "band" decided nothing, and saying "we broke up" is shorthand for "we all happened to drift the same direction." Jan fired back that on that view the band never existed as anything but four solo projects — yet we rehearsed every Thursday because we'd commited as a group, and skipping was letting the group down, not just breaking an individual promise, so you can't have a group at the start and no group at the end. Tom said the Thursday thing just means each of us wanted the others to show up and knew the whole thing collapsed otherwise — individual expectations all the way down, no group needed. They've asked me to referee and I honestly can't decide. Who's right about what, and is there anything both of them are missing? Also, does the answer say whether I'm allowed to tell people my old band broke up, or is that already taking sides without realizing it?
