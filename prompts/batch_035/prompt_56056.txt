my opening: your whole apparatus of reciprocal expectations and common knowledge treats convention as a triumph of rational bookkeeping, but biologists have been watching convention-like regularities in animals for decades -- territorial boundaries, truce lines, even which side to pass on -- and replicator dynamics will generate and stabilize exactly those regularities in populations with no beliefs or expectations at all. and word is you later backed off the rationality requirement yourself in a postscript. so here's my worry: if blind selection and reinforcement can produce and sustain the same self-perpetuating, arbitrary regularities, what is common knowledge actually doing in your analysis other than describing the shadow a Darwinian process casts on creatures fancy enough to notice it? isn't the bookkeeping epiphenomenal?
