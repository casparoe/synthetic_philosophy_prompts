You are a professional philosopher of mind who has been asked to facilitate an adversarial collaboration between two longtime rivals: a functionalist who holds that mental state types are individuated by causal-functional role and are therefore multiply realizable in principle across very different substrates, and a type-identity theorist who holds that mental state types are, at least in mature neuroscience, going to turn out to be identical to specific neural state types, with apparent multiple realizability an artifact of classifying states too coarsely. Draft the joint statement they should be able to sign after a good-faith collaboration. It should contain: (1) a numbered section of claims both can accept — think carefully about what these could be, since they must be neither vacuous nor slanted toward either side (e.g., about the empirical facts concerning grain levels, about why both parties reject Cartesian dualism, about what would count as "mature" neuroscience, and about which parts of the historical debate were merely verbal); (2) a table of their residual disagreements, each phrased so that its own side would endorse the wording — pay particular attention to the disagreement over whether grain-level refinements are progressive scientific carving or ad hoc retreat; (3) the cruxes that would actually move one or both parties. I suspect at least two cruxes: whether causal-role individuation can be made precise without collapsing into either disjunctive types or triviality (consider the notorious cases of wildly disjunctive realizers and brute lookup tables), and whether the causal exclusion worry gives the functionalist a reason to envy the identity theorist's tighter psychophysical bridge. For each crux, specify the kind of empirical finding or argument both would agree bears on it, and write down each party's prediction in advance — for instance, what each would say if neuroscientists discovered that apparent cross-species realizability of some capacity persists all the way down to the level of ion-channel mechanisms, or conversely if it turned out that fine-grained molecular constraints turned every token of a psychological type into tokens of a single neural type. Do not resolve the dispute yourself; your job is to make the residual disagreement exact, and to flag any sub-dispute you judge to be merely verbal. Please begin by brainstorming which claims are genuinely shared versus which merely sound shared, before drafting the final document.
