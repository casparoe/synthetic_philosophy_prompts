Then our vegan friend said a cow matters more than a shrimp, and she did it AGAIN -- sentience is a spectrum, so any ranking is arbitrary and the vegan is just picking favorites. At some point someone muttered something about the paradox of the heap and she said "exactly, philosophers have been stuck on that for 2000 years, which proves the categories aren't really there."
