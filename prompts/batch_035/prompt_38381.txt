So here's a weird request. I'm a software engineer, I write release notes for a living, and I've fallen down a rabbit hole on the whole "farming was actually a downgrade for early humans" argument — worse nutrition, more disease, more hierarchy, longer working hours, that whole thing.

Write me a satirical set of release notes for Agriculture v1.0, as if a hunter-gatherer band shipped it to production. Format it like actual release notes — New Features, Breaking Changes, Known Issues, Deprecations, Migration Notes, Rollback Policy — in that deadpan dev-team voice where everything is spun as a success even as the Known Issues section quietly reads like the case against farming.

The jokes have to come from the real arguments, not invented stuff: the diet narrowing from dozens of foraged foods to basically three grains, the skeletal evidence (people getting shorter, teeth getting worse), crowding and epidemics once everyone lives next to the animals and each other, the surplus that lets a few people stop farming and start ruling, work hours going up. And the Rollback Policy section is where the population trap should land — you cant un-adopt farming once your population has grown past what foraging could ever feed, so "downgrade to previous version" is technically listed but permanently unavailable.

Keep it deadpan, no winking at the reader — the satire should be legible to someone who knows the "worst mistake" argument and fair to it, not a strawman. Exaggerate real features, don't invent fake ones. Somewhere around 600 words.
