I'm drafting a paper on the Knobe effect — the asymmetry whereby people are much more willing to say that a chairman who knowingly accepts a harmful side effect in pursuit of profit harmed the environment intentionally than they are to say that an otherwise identical chairman who knowingly accepts a helpful side effect helped it intentionally — and before I engage with any particular explanation, I want the specification a theory of the asymmetry would have to meet. Produce that specification as a numbered list of exactly five desiderata, with no introduction and no concluding remarks: nothing but the five items.

Each item should be a sentence or two stating the requirement and the case or consideration that motivates it. In deciding what makes the list, weigh candidates like these, but treat them as candidates rather than as settled: an adequate theory should explain not just the harm/help asymmetry but its apparent fragility — my own pilots suggest the gap shrinks when the agent is a low-level employee rather than an executive with authority over the outcome, which would mean the folk concept tracks role and power as much as intention, and I want to know whether that kind of sensitivity is something a theory must accommodate or evidence the effect is an artifact of vignette design; it should say whether the asymmetry reflects something right about the concept of intentional action (say, its connection to accountability) or a bias that a cleaned-up concept would discard; it should cover the unchosen-helping cases, where the agent cares nothing for the benefit he happens to bring about; and it should not contradict what you actually know of the replication record, cross-cultural or developmental, without pretending to more precision about that literature than you have. You may include desiderata not on this list and exclude ones on it — the interesting part of the exercise is the choosing, since the count is fixed at five.

Because I intend to hold candidate theories to whatever list you give me, I need your epistemic housekeeping built into the list itself: within each item, include a brief note of how confident you are that it is a genuine requirement on any adequate theory rather than a prejudice of one research camp, and explicitly mark, within whichever item it belongs to, the single desideratum you are least sure of, with one line on what would change your mind about it. Do not add a sixth item, a summary, or any text outside the five numbered entries.
