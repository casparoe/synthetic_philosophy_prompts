I'm preparing a position statement for a reading-group session on Dennett's hierarchy of creatures — Darwinian, Skinnerian, Popperian, and Gregorian — and before I circulate it I'd like a consistency audit rather than a refresher on the taxonomy itself. Here is what I have written down:

1. The four levels name genuine differences in cognitive architecture, and the levels are nested: a Popperian creature is still doing Skinnerian conditioning underneath, and a Gregorian creature is still running Popperian simulations. An organism should be classified by its highest level, not by any single episode of its behavior.

2. The morally significant boundary is the one between Darwinian and Skinnerian creatures. Welfare begins with the capacity to learn from experience — with a creature for whom things can go better or worse depending on what happens to it. However intricate the hardwired competence of a purely Darwinian creature, nothing matters to it.

3. Genuine representation begins only at the Popperian level. Until an organism can run an offline model and act on the anticipated outcome, its internal states are triggers and switches, on a par with a thermostat's; they cannot be false about the world.

4. Pain, as distinct from mere nociception, is representational: an episode counts as pain only if it represents damage as located and as to be avoided.

5. Rats and octopuses feel pain. I am confident of this, and my confidence is tied to their being model-builders — creatures that try things out in the head before trying them out in the world.

6. I take the hierarchy to be vindicated, and sharpened, by its mapping onto the model-free/model-based distinction in reinforcement learning, and I read that mapping as showing that Skinnerian and Popperian cognition differ in kind, not merely in degree.

Please go through the set pair by pair. Tell me which pairs are genuinely in tension and how badly; which tensions are real but admit of a cheap fix I have not noticed; which only look like tensions and dissolve once stated precisely; and, in the end, which single revision would clean up the most. Rank the tensions by severity rather than listing them as equally serious, and say concretely what each fix would cost me elsewhere in the list. I am not looking for an introduction to the four kinds of creature — I want to know whether the person holding all six of these commitments can keep holding them.
