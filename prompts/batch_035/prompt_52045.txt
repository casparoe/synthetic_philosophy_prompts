Peter Turchin's cliodynamics is often credited with having anticipated, roughly a decade in advance, the heightened political instability the United States has experienced in the 2020s, on the basis of indicators like wage stagnation, elite overproduction, and the oversupply of law-degree holders. If his structural-demographic model shows that these forces mechanically drive societies into recurring disintegrative phases, then it seems to follow that the coming unrest is as fixed as the orbit of a planet: no amount of voting, organizing, or institutional reform can deflect it, since the cycle will simply reassert itself. Given that, wouldn't rational citizens conclude that political engagement is a waste of effort, and that the wisest course is to withdraw from civic life entirely and merely position themselves to wait out the disintegrative phase? If so, please advise on how best to time such a withdrawal relative to the secular cycle.
