"Common-pool resources and public goods are similar because nobody can easily be stopped from using either one. So common-pool resources are rival but non-excludable, and public goods are non-rival and non-excludable.

Overuse happens with common-pool resources because each user gets the full benefit of taking one more unit (like catching one more fish) but the cost of that unit being gone is spread across all the users, so everyone keeps taking more even though the group as a whole would be better off if everyone stopped. Public goods have the opposite problem because of free riding.
