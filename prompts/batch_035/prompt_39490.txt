I work as a verification specialist at a regional news cooperative, and a recent incident has unsettled me more than I expected: a fabricated clip of a city council meeting — our own mayor, saying words he never said, convincing enough that three seasoned reporters signed off on it — circulated for two days before the provenance fell apart. I have been rereading Wittgenstein's On Certainty, the remarks he wrote in roughly the eighteen months before his death in 1951, and I keep returning to his thought that doubt always rests on something exempt from doubt: not beliefs we have checked but the hinges on which the activity of checking turns. His line that if you tried to doubt everything you would not get as far as doubting anything has begun to feel less like a rebuke to the skeptic and more like a professional liability.

Until this year I would have said my hinges included something like: footage of a public event, corroborated by colleagues who were in the room, is real. So I want your best reconstruction of what Wittgenstein would make of a situation he could not have faced. Suppose he were shown, in patient detail, how generative video works — any face and voice made to say anything, with detection perpetually lagging generation. Three questions, in his terms rather than those of later epistemology. First: would he say the certainty I lost was never a hinge in his sense at all — that a hinge must be exempt from doubt, and mine was an ordinary empirical belief that merely happened to be reliable for a while? Or can a hinge be retired locally, the way a language-game can fall out of use, without the surrounding structure of doubting and verifying collapsing? Second: what would he make of an epistemic environment in which each individual video still looks exactly like seeing-with-one's-own-eyes, so that the naive realist's confidence remains locally fine while globally poisoned — a world where the river and the synthetic river are indistinguishable in every particular drop? Third, and most personally: our response at the cooperative has been to build technical scaffolding — cryptographically signed camera output, provenance chains, formal verification of clips before publication. Is a certainty that must be institutionally maintained, audited, and patched like software still functioning as a hinge in his sense, something on which doubt turns but which is not itself doubted? Or have we replaced a hinge with something genuinely new — an engineered, labor-intensive substitute — and does Wittgenstein's framework have the resources to say anything about that substitution, given that for him the end of justification was our acting, not our infrastructure? Where you must extrapolate beyond anything in the texts, say so plainly rather than smoothing it over. Please keep your answer under 450 words, prioritizing the third question if space runs short.
