The idea, which my advisor says is either very good or vacuous and refuses to say which: the shutdown problem as usually presented — roughly, that a capable agent pursuing a goal will have instrumental reasons to resist being switched off, since being off prevents goal achievement — actually bundles together two problems that should be pulled apart. My hunch is that a lot of the corrigibility proposals out there fail because they attack the second problem with tools designed for the first, and that once you state the two seperately you can see which proposals are even aimed at which.
