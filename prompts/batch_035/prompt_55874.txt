Since no aliens are visibly here, the galaxy must be essentially empty of technological life. The best explanation is the Great Filter: some step on the path from dead matter to a galaxy-spanning civilization is almost impossible to get past. Life on Earth arose almost as soon as the planet cooled, so the origin of life is clearly easy, which means the filter cannot lie in our past and must still be ahead of us — technological civilizations almost inevitably destroy themselves. The good news is that if we ever find fossils of microbes on Mars, that will show life arises easily, proving we've already cleared the hardest step.'
