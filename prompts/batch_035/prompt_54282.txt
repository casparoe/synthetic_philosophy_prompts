I've been arguing with a friend about whether equality is good in itself or whether it's really just about helping the worst off, and I figured I'd just list my gut verdicts on cases and see if they add up to a principle. Numbers below are welfare levels, two-person worlds unless I say otherwise.

- Taking from the better-off person and literally burning it: (10,10) to (5,5). Bad, and not even partly good. There is no respect in which that's an improvment.
- Taxing the rich to give to the poor with a leaky bucket: (10,2) to (8,4). Better, even though total welfare drops.
- A person living completely alone on an island at level 2, nobody else in the universe. Raising them to 4 matters just as much as raising someone from 2 to 4 in a two-person world where the other person sits at 10. The inequality around that second person isn't doing any of the moral work.
- (10,2) to (10,3): obviously better, no drama there.
- Now the weird one. The better-off person in a (10,2) world got there by exploiting the worse-off one. We confiscate their ill-gotten gains down to the victim's level and just warehouse the confiscated stuff, so it becomes (2,2). That feels at least partly good. But structurally it's the same move as the first case — take from the better-off, benefit nobody — and the first case felt not-even-partly good. I can't tell if the difference is the history of exploitation or if I'm confusing equality with desert.
- Everyone at (4,4), and a policy takes us to (10,50): clear improvement. And (4,4) to (4,500)? Also an improvement, I say without hesitation. Both of which seem to embarass any view that cares about gaps as such.
- The case I keep going back and forth on: (2,2) to (2,6). Nobody is made worse off, the worst-off person's absolute level doesn't move, total welfare triples, and the gap goes from zero to huge. I keep flipping between unambiguously better and better-but-with-something-regrettable-about-it.

So: is there one principle that gets all of these, or close? I genuinely don't know if I'm a prioritarian who gives extra weight to the worse off on absolute levels, an egalitarian who only cares about equality when there's a relationship or a shared history between the people, or someone who should just admit the exploitation verdict is about desert and drop it.
