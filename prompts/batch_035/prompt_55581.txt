I've been puzzling over a pattern in how financial markets use economic models, and I can't work out what methodology it presupposes. Milton Friedman's 1953 essay 'The Methodology of Positive Economics' argued that the unrealism of a model's assumptions is no objection to it: what matters is predictive success, and we may treat agents as if they were perfect calculators, just as a billiards player can be predicted as if she knew the mathematics. Everyone knows the standard objections and replies. But here is the part I find genuinely strange: option markets still price everything through Black–Scholes, half a century on, even though every one of its assumptions — log-normal returns, constant volatility, frictionless continuous hedging — is known to be false, and known to have been implicated in real disasters, from portfolio insurance in 1987 to LTCM in 1998. Stranger still, traders don't really use the formula to predict prices; they invert it, taking observed market prices and backing out the implied volatility at which to quote. The model survives not by predicting well — its systematic prediction errors are precisely what the volatility smile records — but by serving as a shared coordinate system, a conventional unit in which the market states its beliefs and trades against its own deviations from them.

So what view of models does this practice presuppose? Is it Friedman's instrumentalism, or something importantly different — treating a model as a measurement convention or a language rather than an as-if true theory? Where exactly does the F-twist license inverting a model into a quoting convention instead of testing it against outcomes, and does the licence extend to cases where the model's known-false assumptions (say, Gaussian tails) are the very thing everyone is trading around? A careful reconstruction of the implicit methodology would help, along with a clear separation of what the practice strictly presupposes from what it is merely compatible with — and some account of what would change in market practice if the presupposed view were swapped for a realist one on which the false assumptions do load-bearing work that we should be studying directly.
