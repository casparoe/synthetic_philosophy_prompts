quick sanity check on something because I can't stop chewing on it. I'm a 3rd year PhD student in econ, and there's this foundation run by a wealthy alum that gives out these "commitment fellowships" to grad students. Here's the deal: they interview you, and if they're convinced that you genuinely intend to spend next summer at their research station in the middle of nowhere (northern Manitoba, famously miserable), they hand you $50k on the spot. And I mean genuinely no strings attached — no contract, no clawback, they said outright at the info session that if you don't end up going they will never contact you about it. The money is for the intention, thats the entire arrangement.

What's bugging me is that everyone I tell says the same thing: "so just intend to go, collect the check, and then don't go." But that can't work, right?? If what I actually form is a plan to *get the money and stay home*, then I don't genuinely intend to go, and the interview is specifically about that — they've turned people down for exactly this, and the interviewer is a retired clinical psychologist who does sincerity assessments all day, so it's not like I can just act sincere. And on the other hand, if I somehow did genuinely intend to go, then when summer comes, staying is just obviously better for me in every way: going costs me three months of my life and gains me nothing, because the money is already banked either way. So the me of next June would rationally bail, and I know this NOW, and knowing it seems to be exactly what stops me from forming the intention in the first place... but my stipend is not great and the money is very real.

So I guess I have three questions: (1) is there some move here I'm not seeing, or is the money just structurally out of reach for anyone who's thought about it for more than a minute? (2) suppose I decide I'm just going to be the kind of person who commits hard and actually goes — is that better or worse for me all things considered than not playing at all? and (3) is it just me or is there something genuinely deep here about whether you can decide to intend something you already know you'll have every reason to abandon? My advisor thinks I'm overthinking a free $50k, but the interview slot is this week and I need to figure out what I actually beleive fast.
