I'm putting together a unit on Parfit for a class on personal identity, and I want a thought experiment that pushes against his usual direction. You know the standard teletransporter setup: the psychological continuity people walk into the machine relaxed, the body theorists freak out, and the takeaway is suppoed to be that identity isn't what matters, psychological connectedness is. I want the mirror image of that.

Here's the ask: build me a case where someone holding psychological continuity plus Parfit's what-matters story ends up with LESS reason for future-directed concern than a straight-up bodily continuity theorist, even though everyone agrees the subject survives. Constraints:

- No teleporters, no scanning, no sci-fi at all. Medical scenario only — think progressive neurological conditions, targeted drugs, experimental treatments, that kind of thing.
- No fission, no branching, one continuous subject the whole way through.
- The loss of concern can't come from the future being bad in some ordinary way (no suffering, no depression, the life stays decent). It should fall out of the psychology itself.
- One paragraph for the actual scenario.

The route I suspect works (but do it differently if you see something better): psychological continuity in Parfit's sense is a chain of overlapping connectedness, and it can be real but threadbare — survival by his lights, while the degree of connectedness, which is the thing doing the work for concern, gets stretched super thin. A condition that keeps the chain intact (each stage overlaps enough with the last) while the overlap between you-now and you-ten-years-later shrinks toward zero would do it. The body theorist's grounds for concern are completely untouched by any of this, so they end up with the intuitive high ground — the reverse of the usual lesson.

After the scenario, walk me through what each of the three views says: bodily continuity, simple psychological continuity, and Parfit's "identity isn't what matters" machinery, and pinpoint which feature of the case is doing the work. Then play defense — how would a Parfitian resist the conclusion that concern should fade, and is the case maybe cheating by smuggling in something like ordinary age-related memory loss, where concern fades just because the future life is worse rather than because survival and what-matters came apart? Also tell me whether the case still works if the thinning is only forward-looking (you keep full access to your past, but your future self has almost no access to you-now), or whether that asymmetry breaks it.
