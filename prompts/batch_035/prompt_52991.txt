"Horse" is what designates shape. "White" is what designates color. What designates color is not what designates shape. Therefore I say: a white horse is not a horse.

Then, after the Objector protests that if you have a white horse you plainly cannot be said to have no horse, the Advocate replies:

If one seeks a horse, yellow and black horses may all be delivered. If one seeks a white horse, yellow and black horses may not be delivered. Suppose a white horse were [just] a horse. Then what is sought [in the two cases] would be the same. If what is sought were the same, then "white" would not differ from "horse." If what is sought does not differ, how is it that yellow and black horses are acceptable [in one case] and unacceptable [in the other]?
