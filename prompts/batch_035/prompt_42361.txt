He says CH is exactly that: Gödel and Cohen showed it's independent of ZFC, so there's no fact of the matter about whether there's a set strictly bigger than the counting numbers but strictly smaller than the reals — pick your set theory and get on with your life. If anything the moral is the opposite of his: an independence result means the question got relocated, not dissolved, and set theory is still waiting for its Einstein. My flatmate says sets aren't in space so nothing could play the role physics played, and honestly I don't know how to answer that.
