ok so background first. my school does an exchange with a school just outside oslo, and every spring they put on an evening where a few students give short talks, mix of our families and the norwegian students and teachers. this year i got assigned the topic "sweden vs norway: which is actually the more liberal country?" the teachers clearly meant it as a joke topic but i want to actually do it properly instead of just doing vibes.

heres the problem i keep hitting. everyone i ask has a completly different idea of what would even settle it. my swedish cousin says its obviously sweden, they got the state out of the church business before norway did and are generally more chill about stuff. my norwegian friend from the exchange says no way, when norway passed gender-neutral marriage law it went through their parliament with a really big majority, which sounds pretty liberal. and my politics teacher just said "more liberal by what measure, though?" and walked off, which is honestly the most annoying thing shes ever done because now i cant stop thinking about it.

so heres what i want you to write: the full text of a talk, around 4 minutes, like something i can basically read off cards. the room will be half teenagers (swedish and norwegian students who will absolutly notice if i rig it for one side) and half parents and teachers, so it cant get too technical.

the talk has to do three things:

1. explain why "which country is more liberal" doesnt have an answer until you first say what counts as liberal — like the free-market sense of liberal and the socially-permissive sense can pull in opposite directions, so a country can win on one and lose on the other

2. propose a fair way of settling it: a shortlist of concrete indicators that both sides would have to agree on BEFORE anyone looks at the results, so you cant just cherry-pick after the fact whichever indicator happens to go your countrys way and pretend you picked it for good reasons

3. actually commit to an answer at the end. i dont want the cop-out "theyre both liberal in thier own special way" ending — apparently a kid did that a few years ago and everyone booed. its fine if the honest answer is "depends how you weight the indicators, but on the list i pre-committed to, it comes out X", as long as i actually say the X

you shouldnt build it around any stat id have to look up mid-talk. stick to the kind of thing a normal person knows or that i can put in the prompt here: when each country got the state out of the official church, how strict the rules are about buying alcohol (both have those government liquor shops, one of them is famously stricter), how the same-sex marriage votes went, roughly where the two countries' political debates sit on immigration, that kind of thing. if a real number would help, its better to phrase it as "on this one norway/sweden is the stricter one" than to invent a figure.

also it should be funny at least once, but not at either countrys expense, because i have to live with these people for a week and the norwegians already think we swedes are arrogant enough as it is.
