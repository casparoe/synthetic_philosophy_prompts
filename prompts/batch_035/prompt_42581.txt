I want the claims they can both actually sign, and I mean genuinely sign, not diplomatic mush. Then the residual disagreements stated exactly, with the wording of each side's own view phrased so that that side would accept it — e.g. the higher-order person gets to say something like a state is conscious in virtue of being the target of a suitable higher-order representation, and the first-order person gets to say consciousness is carried by the state itself, and the joint document shouldn't smuggle in a resolution.
