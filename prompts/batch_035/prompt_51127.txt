1. Compensated kidney donation should be legal — regulated, something like the state-run system they have in Iran — because the current ban kills people on the waitlist every year, and "the body shouldn't be for sale" is just the yuck factor dressed up as an argument.
2. Vote selling should remain illegal, and not because of worries about election outcomes — my actual reason is that a vote isn't yours to sell in the first place. You hold it as part of being a citizen, the way you can't sell yourself into slavery, and no amount of consent makes that kind of transfer possible.
3. Price gouging laws are mostly bad policy — a $900 generator after a hurricane is prices doing their job, rationing to whoever values it most and pulling new supply into the disaster area.
4. The master principle behind all of this: voluntary exchange between consenting adults with no direct third-party harm gets a strong presumption of legality. Disgust alone is never a reason to ban something, and desperation in the background doesn't make an exchange involuntary.
5. Commercial surrogacy contracts should be fully enforceable, including against the surrogate's wish to keep the baby after the birth.
