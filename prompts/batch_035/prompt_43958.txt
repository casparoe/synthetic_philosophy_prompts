Here's the question we were all answering: In a few paragraphs, explain what Hacking means by looping effects of human classifications. How does his distinction between interactive and indifferent kinds bear on which classifications can undergo such loops? Illustrate with a psychiatric category of your choice. Finally, does the existence of looping effects show, on Hacking's view, that psychiatric classifications are mere social constructions with no underlying biological reality?

And here's the answer I'm grading: Looping effects happen when a classification changes the people who fall under it. Once psychiatrists and institutions start sorting people into a category, the people classified come to understand themselves partly through that category, they behave differently, and the experts then adjust the category in response to how the classified people are behaving. So the classification and the classified co-evolve, and the kind itself can change over time rather than staying fixed. For example, the diagnosis of multiple personality in the late twentieth century: as therapists began looking for the condition, patients learned from media coverage and from each other what the condition was supposed to look like, they presented with more elaborate symptom profiles, and the diagnostic criteria were then revised in light of how patients actually presented — the loop ran through patients, doctors, and the category itself. Hacking's distinction between interactive and indifferent kinds is about awareness: interactive kinds are ones where the people classified know about the label and react to it, whereas indifferent kinds are ones where the classified people are unaware of being classified, which is why they can't loop. Since looping effects are pervasive in psychiatry, and since psychiatrists' categories demonstrably shaped the multiple personality cases, Hacking's conclusion is that psychiatric classifications are social constructions pure and simple — the kinds are invented by the classifying apparatus, and there is no underlying biological reality to them, just people playing the roles the categories hand them.
