The spec is that both procedures must be live options for the same rescuer with the same resources, and the divergence has to fall out of the structure of the rescues — you can't just stipulate that one is the fair one.

You're allowed to include rescue risk (a route to the group that might fail) to get the expected values to line up, that's the legitimate way to do it, but it has to be a natural feature of the scenario, not obviously rigged.
