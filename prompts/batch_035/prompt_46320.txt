Then he wrapped up by saying the paper is basically a license for a bit of selfishness — Wolf is telling us we're allowed to stop trying so hard and should stop feeling guilty about it — and anyway it's been empirically refuted since positive psychology apparently shows that kind, moral people are actually happier than everyone else, so there's no tradeoff to begin with.
