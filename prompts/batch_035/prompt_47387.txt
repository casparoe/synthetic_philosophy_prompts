here's roughly where I've landed:

(1) What makes manipulation wrong, when it's wrong, is that it bypasses the target's rational capacities -- it changes beliefs or behavior through a route the target couldn't endorse on reflection, instead of putting considerations in front of them to weigh.

(2) A lot of ordinary emotional persuasion is fine. when a charity tells the story of one named child instead of quoting statistics about thousands, that's legitimate persuasion, not manipulation -- even though we know the single vivid case moves people far more than the numbers do (psychologists call this the identifiable victim effect). I don't think Oxfam is manipulating me when they do the one-child story.

(3) There's no such thing as influencing a purely rational agent, because there are no purely rational agents: every act of persuasion enters through some door that isn't itself under rational control -- salience, mood, trust in the speaker, emotional resonance. nobody has ever been moved by considerations arriving through a purely rational channel.

(4) Lying and manipulating are two separate wrongs that can come apart. you can manipulate with perfectly true statements (that's most of advertising), and I want to say you can lie without manipulating -- the cliché movie scene where the hero flatly lies to the villain to stall for time isn't a manipulation, it's just a lie.

(5) Manipulation doesn't require a manipulator with intentions. recommender systems and behavioral ad targeting can manipulate people even though there's no person behind the curtain aiming at anything.

(6) Advance consent doesn't stop something from being manipulation. my friend and I agreed that whenever I'm about to blow money on something dumb, she should guilt-trip me about the trip we're supposed to be saving for. when she actually does it -- the sigh, the "fine, it's your money" -- that is still a guilt-trip, still manipulation, same as any other. her having my permission might change whether she's wronging me, but it doesn't change whether it's manipulation.
