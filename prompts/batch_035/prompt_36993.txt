My verdicts, going on gut:

4. Yet I also have the intuition that the planets move as they do *because* the law is inverse-square, not the other way around. Laws feel like they push, not summarize, which is why 3 bugs me.
5. Probabilistic laws: a universe of coin flips that comes out about 50/50 over zillions of flips. I'm comfortable saying it's a *law* that the coin is fair, even though for all the flips say, it could've been a law that it's 60/40. And in a rigged-seeming universe (heads exactly twice as frequent, and every run of 3+ heads followed by at least 5 tails), I flip-flop: is the law fair-but-lucky, or biased-with-clustering, or is there just no fact of the matter?
