(4) So a white shoe, being a non-black non-raven, confirms that all ravens are black. I'd seen that much in lecture and I know the standard responses exist — something about the confirmation being tiny, something about background knowledge — so I nodded along. Then she kept going.

(5) My mailbox is purple. (6) One glance at my mailbox confirms an entire family of pairwise incompatible hypotheses about raven coloration.
