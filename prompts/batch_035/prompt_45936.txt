Two new cases coming up at thanksgiving. First, the county wants to flood a valley for a reservoir: one extended family loses a farm that's been theirs for six generations (they get full market compensation, but to them the place is irreplaceable), and a million people get marginally cheaper water and slightly better flood control. Nobody is aiming at the family; the harm just falls out of the project as a side effect. Second, a hypothetical a cousin keeps pushing: a kidney lottery, where everyone's name goes in and a handful of losers each give up a kidney to save several dialysis patients, with everyone knowing the odds going in and no opting out.

What is she going to say about each? And there might be a third rule too, something about state policies versus private acts, that fits her record just as well. Walk me through what each candidate rule predicts on both new cases, say which verdicts it gets wrong if any, give me your best guess for what she'll say with your reasons, and tell me which of the two cases would do the better job of catching her running one rule rather than another.
