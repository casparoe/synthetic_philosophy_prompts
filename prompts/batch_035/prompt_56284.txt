What I don't have a feel for is whether it has anything to say about how actual science fights resolve.

So analyze this whole thing through the Aumann lens: who are the two agents, what were their posteriors and their evidence, was the disagreement even an Aumann-style disagreement (same prior, common knowledge of posteriors) or was it something the theorem doesn't cover, and what role did the public bets play in creating common knowledge versus just being theater.
