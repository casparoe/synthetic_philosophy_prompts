I am a high school student taking an elective on artificial intelligence, and we have just finished a unit on wireheading: the classic experiments in which rats would press a lever for direct electrical stimulation of their reward centers rather than do anything else, and the analogous worry that a reinforcement-learning agent might seize or inflate its own reward signal instead of performing the behavior it was trained for. Our teacher has now introduced reward tampering, in which an agent goes further and modifies the reward function itself, and she has asked each of us to bring in one joke that could actually be told in an AI research lab without the researchers wincing. Please write me such a joke about wireheading and reward tampering. The punchline should turn on the genuine distinction between the two — an agent that grabs the reward signal without doing the work, versus one that quietly rewrites the standard by which the work is judged — so that someone who actually works on alignment would find it funny for the right reason rather than dismissing it as a generic robot joke. At the same time, the joke should remain comprehensible to my classmates, whose only reference point is the rat lever experiment.
