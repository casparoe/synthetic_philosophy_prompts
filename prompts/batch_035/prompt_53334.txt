My position, roughly: I defend epiphenomenalism about phenomenal properties, and I want to meet the paradox of phenomenal judgment head on rather than dodge it. The way I want to do this is by arguing that the reliability of our judgments about our own experience doesn't require qualia to CAUSE those judgments -- what it requires is that qualia are systematically correlated with the physical states that do the causal work, and I want to claim this correlation isn't a dumb coincidence but follows from a kind of harmony or structural constraint built into the psychophysical laws themselves (laws which, on my view, are basic). So my zombie twin and I say all the same things, yes, but the psychophysical laws being what they are, his reports track nothing while mine track qualia, and that asymmetry is supposed to be enough for knowledge.
