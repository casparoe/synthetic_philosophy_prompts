My half-formed thought on the carrying example: an RL agent trained on maze levels where the goal coin always ends up at the end of one particular path, so it plausibly learns "go where the coin usually is" rather than "go to the coin" — then you move the coin and watch what happens. I half remember a paper doing somthing like that, so it can't be a stupid idea, but everyone in the room has seen a hundred RL mazes, so maybe it's too gamey?
