Anyway, the argument, roughly as it happened:

Maya: This isn't weakness of will, it's just hyperbolic discounting. The value of a reward grows steeply as it gets close in time, so a mediocre immediate reward (videos) can beat a big delayed one (the thesis) when they're sitting right next to each other, even though the thesis wins easily when both are far away. That's ordinary preference reversal along a steep curve, not a broken will. Calling it akrasia is just moralizing a discount function he happens to have.

Jonah: If his preferences simply reversed, he'd be at peace with it. Sam isn't. At 9am he endorses working, and at 11 — mid-video — he still endorses working, says so, and installs blockers to enforce it. Someone whose all-things-considered preference had flipped wouldn't be fighting his own actions. So the curve might explain the pull, but acting against a judgment you still hold mid-action is exactly what akrasia is supposed to be, and no discount curve explains *that*.

Maya: The "I should be working" is just the far-off preference hanging around and losing to the near one — two preference states slugging it out, not a special faculty of will that failed. Even his self-disapproval is just another discounted outcome he weighs: when feeling bad is cheap and scrolling is right there, feeling bad and scrolling is what he picks. That's all consistent with one curve.

Jonah: Then explain the blockers. If it were pure curve, the annoyance of a blocked site is small and immediate too — and removing a blocker is a five-minute job, which he's done, twice, at 9pm the night before a deadline. That's not a spike in the moment, that's a stable disposition to defeat his own future arrangements, which is pretty much the definintion of akrasia. Also, hyperbolic discounting comes out of experiments where a pigeon picks between grain now and more grain later — two pleasures. Sam says the scrolling isn't even pleasurable, it feels bad. He's avoiding, not choosing between rewards.

Maya: Avoidance is choosable too. The relief of not facing the blank page is a reward like any other, and it's the most immediate one on the table. You don't get to relabel it akrasia just because the reward is negative relief instead of positive fun.
