the readings i know of, roughly:

1. the Marx reading: Aristotle is groping toward a labor theory of value but can't get there because greek society rests on slavery, so he falls back on need as what makes things equal
2. the need-measurement reading: exchange is fair when it equalizes the parties' needs for each other's products (with the sub-fight over whose need exactly -- the producer's need for the other's product, or the customer's need for what's being sold)
