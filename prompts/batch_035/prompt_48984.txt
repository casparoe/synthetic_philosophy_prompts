One is definitional (is "violence" only direct physical harm between humans, or do you count slow structural killers like deaths from sanctions or gutted sanitation systems, and do factory-farmed animals even enter the ledger?). One is arithmetical (should we track deaths per capita, which makes violence look like it's declining as populations boom, or absolute numbers, where an unusually bad decade can wipe out centuries of progress?).
