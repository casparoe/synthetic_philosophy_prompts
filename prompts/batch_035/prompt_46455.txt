so, background: i'm a backend engineer, no philosophy training at all, and this whole thing started b/c i went down a rabbit hole about the digger wasp. you probably know the story — the wasp (sphex is the actual genus, this goes back to some 19th century french naturalist named fabre) drags a paralyzed cricket to her burrow, leaves it at the entrance, and goes in to inspect the burrow first. while she's inside, you can move the cricket a few inches away. she comes out, finds it, drags it back to the threshold — and then the whole routine resets, she has to go inspect the burrow again. you can apparently do this to her over and over and she never catches on. hofstadter coined a word for this, sphexishness, being stuck in a mechanical routine you can't see yourself stuck in. anyway i can't stop thinking about it, and i've written down what i actually believe about myself and my code and so on, and i want someone to check whether it hangs together. here it is:

(1) humans are basically the wasp with a bigger routine library. i watch myself all day: check phone, alt-tab to slack, glance at the same three dashboards, run the test suite even when i know it'll pass. if you made an itemized list of my behavoir most of it would look exactly like the wasp's provisioning sequence, just with more subroutines.

(2) i'm also fine with a deterministic universe — no soul stuff, physics all the way down. this genuinely doesn't bother me and i don't think it's in tension with anything else i believe.

(3) but i do think i'm sometimes non-sphexish, and what does it is noticing. i can catch myself in a loop and break it. like last week i realized i was refreshing github for literally no reason, closed the laptop, went for a walk. the wasp can't do that, she can't notice her own routine, and that seems like the whole difference between her and me.

(5) i think any piece of software is sphexish no matter how impressive — chess engines, LLMs, whatever — precisely because it's code, it's rules all the way down, and the wasp is just naked code that you can watch executing.

(6) at the same time i think sphexishness is about the shape of the behavior, not what it's made of: it's about wether you keep running the same sequence when someone perturbs the world on you, about whether you update. on that definition a fully deterministic system could count as non-sphexish.
