And I have this vague memory of reading that Augustine himself got asked about something like this once: some guys in spain wanted to infiltrate a heretic sect by pretending to convert so they could figure out what the sect was really up to, and he apparently went along with it at first and later admitted he'd been wrong to. I might be garbling the details badly, it was a long time ago in a used bookstore copy of some letters. Doesn't matter much, that's the setup, not the point.
