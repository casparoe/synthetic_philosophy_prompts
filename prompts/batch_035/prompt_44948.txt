I've been chewing over this thing about rescue cases for a while now and I've ended up with a bunch of positions I like individually but I'm honestly not sure they fit together. Here's roughly where I stand:

(1) If a lifeguard or whoever can save either his friend or five strangers, but not both, he should be allowed to save his friend. Nobody should force him to count heads when his friend is drowning right there.

(2) Even if he doesn't know any of the people involved, I don't think he owes it to the five to pick them. If he wants to flip a coin between "save the one" and "save the five", that's not wrong. Part of why I like the coin flip is that everyone ends up with the same chance — the one guy gets 50/50, and each of the five gets 50/50 too.

(3) Every person counts exactly as much as every other person. No life is worth more than anyone else's, no matter how useful or loved or young they are.

(4) When I donate to charity I go strictly by numbers — whichever org saves the most lives per pound gets my money. And I'd side-eye someone who picked a charity that saves fewer lives just because it has a nicer story attached to it.

(5) I don't think it's literally true that it's "five times worse" when five people die than when one person dies. That whole business of adding people up like bags of sugar has always seemed off to me.

(6) But here's the bit that keeps nagging me: if I'm one of the five, and the rescuer doesn't know anybody involved, and he just saves the single person because he felt more sorry for him — no coin flip, he just picks — then I think he did wrong by the rest of us. Though I can't square that with what I said in (2) about not owing the five anything.

So: do these hang together? Go through the pairs that actually clash and rank them by how bad the clash is — I'd rather know which one or two are the real problems than get a list of six "possible tensions" of equal weight. For each real clash, tell me what would have to give and what it costs me to give it up. And if some of these only look like they conflict but actually don't, please say so and explain why the appearance dissolves, because I suspect at least one of them is like that.
