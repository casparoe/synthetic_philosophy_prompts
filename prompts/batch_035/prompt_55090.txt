Write me a deadpan FAQ page (~600 words) for the Ministry of Resource Equality in a fictional country that has fully implemented Dworkin-style equality of resources: the initial auction where all resources were sold off with everyone given equal purchasing power (in clams), the envy test as the official and sole standard of fairness, and the hypothetical insurance market that decides compensation for brute luck like inborn handicaps and lacking talent, with everything else left to option luck. The ministry's voice is helpful, mildly smug, and completely sincere — no winking at the reader, they beleive they are the fairest ministry in the world. The jokes have to come from the actual machinery, not invented absurdity: a miserable citizen is told that the envy test concerns resource bundles only, not welfare, and that his bundle is unenviable so he has nothing to complain about; someone with an expensive taste (champagne palate, opera habit) is told it's tracked to their own personality so it's their problem; a citizen born disabled is cheerfully walked through what insurance she could have purchased before birth, at market rates; a gambler who lost his bundle is reminded that option luck gets no top-ups. Include a question about the person whose talents turned out to be worthless because the auction priced their career's raw materials too high, and a question from a parent asking whether her child's welfare counts for anything. The standard objections — that the scheme is harsh on the unlucky, that responsibility for one's own preferences is doing enormous invisible work, that nobody's actual wellbeing ever enters the calculation — should land without the ministry ever noticing them.
