I've been asked to write up my philosophical profile on Twin Earth-style externalism for a reading group, and I want a consistency check before I circulate it. Here is the package I currently hold:

(1) The Twin Earth verdict: two agents who are molecule-for-molecule identical, raised in environments differing only in that the clear drinkable liquid around one is H2O and around the other is XYZ, nonetheless differ in what their word "water" means and in the contents of their water-thoughts.
(2) Psychological explanation is individualistic: whatever explains Oscar's reaching for the glass must be something he shares with his internal duplicate on Twin Earth, since by hypothesis nothing inside them distinguishes them, and whatever they don't share cannot be doing the causal work of explaining their identical behavior.
(3) No two-tier picture of content: the contents that figure in belief-desire explanation are the same contents that have truth conditions and enter into inference. "Narrow content," if it is anything, is a further thesis I would have to argue for independently, not something I get to help myself to when explanation demands it.
(4) First-person authority: when I think that there is water in the glass, I know which thought I am entertaining without any inquiry into my environment or its chemistry.
(5) Slow switching preserves rationality: someone transported unknowingly from Earth to Twin Earth, such that after a period there her word "water" picks out XYZ, still reasons validly with that word — her "water"-thoughts keep playing the right inferential and behavioral roles across the switch, and her practical reasoning does not become invalid at any point.
(6) Externalism generalizes to artificial systems: what a large language model's token "water" refers to is fixed in part by what its training corpus was about, not by anything intrinsic to the model, so two systems with identical weights but different training histories could mean different things by the same token.

Go through these and tell me which pairs are genuinely in tension and which only appear to be, ranking the tensions by severity rather than flagging everything equally. For each genuine tension, say what would have to give and what the revision costs. Then tell me which single revision would clean up the most, and whether the package survives in recognizable form without it.
