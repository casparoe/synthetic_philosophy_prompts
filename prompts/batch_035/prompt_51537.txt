Real explanations: the classic bank run where each depositor acts on what they think the other depositors will do; why rival gas stations end up clustering at the same highway exit (each owner picking location given where the others might be); why the standard railway gauge stuck around for so long (whoever laid track first set the ruts, and switching later got too expensive for anyone to bother); and why the 2008 mess spread the way it did (banks with shared exposure to housing all had to deleverage at once, which pushed prices down further, which forced more deleveraging).
