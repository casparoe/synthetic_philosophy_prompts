The audience is mostly grad students, plus one retired professor who's an old-school Popperian and will smell blood the second I say "just early," and two econ students who consider Malthus empirically dead in the water post-demographic transition. I want a prep sheet: the objections in roughly the order they'll actually come, phrased the way those specific people would put them, each with a two-minute reply and an honest note on where the reply is soft. Also flag the one objection I'm quietly hoping nobody raises. And be straight with me about what would have to be true, or what argument would have to land, for me to drop the "right but early" framing entirely rather than keep patching it — I don't want to end up defending a theory that can't lose.
