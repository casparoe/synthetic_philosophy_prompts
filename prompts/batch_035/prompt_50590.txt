My metaphysics reading group does this thing where everyone circulates a one-pager of their current commitments on the term's topic and the others get to attack it. This term it's infinity. Here's my page on supertasks, and honestly I can't tell if it's a mess or not:

1. Causal finitism is false. Infinitely many events can be packed into a finite stretch of time, and Thomson's lamp is a coherant scenario once you idealize the switch's engineering.
2. I'm a determinist: the complete state of a closed system at one instant, plus the laws, permits only one total history.
3. In the lamp case, nothing about the machine before 2pm settles whether it's on at 2pm, and I'm comfortable saying there's simply no fact of the matter about that. Though I'd add that the switch itself is a lever, its position is a continuous quantity, and the lever obviously has some limit position at 2pm — the hardware doesn't just evaporate.
4. Generally I'm a no-gaps person about physical reality: any question about a system's physical state either has a determinate answer or was ill-posed. Reality doesn't come with holes in it.
5. The Ross–Littlewood urn: obviously empty at noon. Every ball is removed at some finite stage, so no ball is still in there.
6. In the variant where you still add ten balls and remove one each stage, but the one removed is always ball 10n−9, infinitely many balls remain at noon. Fine by me — the moral is that the endstate depends on which objects got moved, not on how many went in or out. And ball labels are just bookkeeping anyway, nothing metaphysical rides on them.
7. Ordinary motion is already a supertask — walking across the room means completing infinitely many sub-distances — and there's no mystery at all about where you are when you reach the far wall.

Go through this and tell me which parts genuinely conflict, which only look like they do, and what the cheapest fix for each would be. Rank the tensions by severity, and say which single revision would clean up the most. I genuinely don't know where the bodies are buried, so don't be diplomatic.
