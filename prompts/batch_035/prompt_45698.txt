so, been reading the decision theory threads on and off since the old lesswrong superrationality flamewars, and I finally sat down to actually write out my positions instead of having vibes about them. now I'm not sure they all fit together. here's the list:

- I one-box in Newcomb. obviously.
- In a one-shot PD against an exact copy of me, I cooperate, and I think that's just *correct*, not some warm-fuzzy thing.
- In a one-shot PD against a random human stranger, I defect. we're causally independent, no logical correlation doing any work there.
- In Parfit's hitchhiker I pay the driver once I'm in the city, even tho he literally cannot do anything to me anymore.
- My official story about superrationality is that it was never about physical copies. it's about the other player running the same decision procedure as you — two agents reasoning through the same question with the same algorithm are effectively making one decision.
- Related: I think whether cooperation is warranted can't hinge on arbitrary implementation details. whether the "other player" is a brain scan, a simulation, or just a very similar reasoner shouldn't flip the answer.

the thing I keep chewing on: most humans facing a one-shot PD are, like, reasoning through it roughly the way I do? if my procedure is "defect against strangers" and theirs is the same, doesn't my own same-decision-procedure story commit me to cooperating with them? or does "same procedure" secretly mean "same procedure AND literally me", which is exactly the arbitrary implementation line I said I wouldnt draw. I honestly cant tell which of these I'd give up, and I might be wrong about where the problem even is.

also people keep telling me that paying the hitchhiker while defecting on strangers is inconsistent, but it feels different to me and I cant articulate why, so maybe dont just take my word for it.

which of these commitments actually clash, which only look like they do, and what's the cheapest patch? rank the tensions by severity instead of telling me everything is equally broken — I want to know what has to give first.
