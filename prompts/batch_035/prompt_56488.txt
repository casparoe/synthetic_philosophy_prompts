Since the Chinese Room proves that symbol manipulation can never produce genuine understanding, why do AI people keep debating wether LLMs understand language? The guy in the room follows his rulebook, produces perfect Chinese answers, and clearly understands nothing — the program is doing exactly the same thing, just faster. I even read about a version where the room is spread out over a whole gym full of people passing notes to each other, none of whom understands Chinese, and that supposedly makes the point even stronger. So hasn't this basically been settled once and for all? Feels weird that researchers still treat it like an open question instead of just accepting Searle's argument.
