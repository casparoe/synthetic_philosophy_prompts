But the polisci people clearly think it licenses much more than "committees of mediocre models beat one good model," and I can't tell whether the extra stuff is real or whether I'm just pattern-matching too aggressively onto things I already know.

So here's the ask: translate the jury theorem and its extensions into my vocabulary, as honestly as you can. Voters as base estimators, individual competence as a weak learner being better than chance, majority rule as a majority-vote ensemble, and so on. So: is the residue telling me my vocabulary is missing something, or is it telling me the polisci people are loading normative freight onto a statistical result that never carried it?
