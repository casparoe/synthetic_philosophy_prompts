fair warning that this is a mess and I apologize in advance for the length. so: retired neuroscientist here, 30-odd years in visual physiology, mostly V1 and monkey psychophysics. since retiring I've fallen hard down the philosophy of mind rabbit hole — dennett, frankish, graziano's attention schema stuff, all of that — and the user-illusion picture is basically the only story about consciousness I find livable as someone who spent a career recording spikes. I'm supposed to write up my "position" for a discussion group and I want someone to audit it first, because I have a nagging feeling there's a contradiction buried in here somewhere and I genuinely cannot see where.

the package, roughly:

1. hard physicalism. no non-physical properties, no extra psychophysical laws, nothing that isnt in the physics.
2. the user-illusion / illusionist picture: phenomenal consciousness as normally conceived doesnt exist. the self, the stream of experience, the redness of red — these are like icons on a desktop, a control interface the brain throws up for its own use, useful misrepresentations rather than glimpses of some inner medium.
3. despite (2), I never treated subjects' introspective reports as suspect in the lab and I'm not going to start now. when someone reports seeing the stimulus, that report is perfectly good scientific evidence about something real going on in their head.
4. the reason it's wrong to hurt animals is the felt badness of pain. nociception on its own wouldn't be morally worth much — that's basically what I take pain asymbolia to show — and a machine could have all the nociception and avoidance behavior you like without anything being bad for it.
5. I'm quite confident current language models are not conscious. but I also think consciousness is substrate-independent: the right functional organization in silicon would do the job just as well as in neurons.
6. I find the mary / knowledge argument thing genuinely forceful and have never been able to shake it. she seems to learn something when she sees red for the first time, and I dont think what she learns is just a new ability or a false belief.

go through these and tell me which pairs are actually in tension and how badly, which ones only look like they conflict, and what the cheapest fix would be for each real tension. rank them by severity rather than giving me a lecture — I know what illusionism says, what I dont know is whether I can beleive it together with everything else on this list. and if one revision would clean up most of the mess, I want to know which one and what it would cost me.
