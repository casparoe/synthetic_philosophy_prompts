Whenever someone asks whether you can really generalize about peoples -- "Germans are rule-following," "Americans are individualist" -- our professor's answer leans on an analogy: a nation is like an individual person. She says nations aren't agents at all, they're just populations, so the analogy is a category error from the start, and every generalization about a people is really a statistical claim about a distribution wearing a characterological costume.
