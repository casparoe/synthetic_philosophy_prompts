Twenty-five years on, every argument on both sides is well known -- the halfer's imaginary-duration and "new information" arguments, the thirder's indifference/centered-worlds argument, Dutch books that each side claims the other gets dutch-booked by, the whole thing -- and I genuinely cannot name a major figure who has switched camps in the last decade. My working hypothesis, and treat it as just a hypothesis: the two camps aren't disagreeing about a number. There are details that make me half-believe this and half-doubt it. Meanwhile the thirder's position starts looking weird once you push it -- some thirders bite the bullet on variants where there are a million awakenings on tails, giving heads a credence of about one in a million from the inside, which strikes half the room as a reductio and the other half as obvious.
