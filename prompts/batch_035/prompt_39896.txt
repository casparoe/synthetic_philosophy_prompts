**Entropy, Waking Up, and Why the Sleeping Beauty Fight Might Be a Fight About Units**

Everybody knows the Sleeping Beauty setup: coin flip Sunday, woken Monday, maybe woken Tuesday too, no memory between wakings, what should her credence be in heads when she wakes up. Halfers say 1/2, thirders say 1/3, and the fight has been going for decades.

Here's my chemistry brain's reaction: this is the Gibbs paradox all over again. When you mix two samples of the same gas, the entropy change is zero. When you mix two different gases, even very similar ones, you get a finite entropy of mixing. When you let the gases get more and more similar, the entropy of mixing doesn't smoothly go to zero, it stays constant and then drops discontinuously — because whether a configuration "counts" as the same or different is not a fact about the gas, it's a fact about what observations you (the experimenter) are able to make. Some statistical mechanics people have argued the entropy is partly a property of the observer's state of knowledge rather than of the gas itself — I want to say it was Jaynes, don't quote me on that.

My claim: the halfer/thirder fight is the same kind of thing. The halfers are computing one quantity (the fraction of *experiments* where it's heads) and the thirders are computing another (the fraction of *wakings* that occur in experiments where it's heads, which is 1/3 because there are more wakings in the tails branch). Both computations are correct. The word "credence" is doing the same work "entropy" does in the mixing paradox: it sounds like one physical quantity, but it's actually two quantities wearing one hat, and which one you mean depends on your bookkeeping — on what's being "measured," per run or per waking.

So I want to say the whole dispute is badly posed until you specify the operational question. If Beauty is scored once per experiment (a single payoff per run, no matter how many wakings), the right number is 1/2. If she's scored once per waking (a bet settled each time she opens her eyes), it's 1/3. Asking for "the" credence with no scoring rule attached is like asking for the temperature of a single molecule. The quantity doesn't exist prior to the choice of averaging.

The part I think is actually new (to me at least): in the mixing paradox, what settles the "same or different" question is what the observer can in principle detect — and Beauty is in a box that has been *deliberately built* to erase her ability to detect which waking she's in. So the experiment is an engineered Gibbs paradox. The amnesia is the "identical particles" assumption. The question "is this Monday or Tuesday?" is exactly the question the experiment was designed to make unmeasurable, and entropy-type bookkeeping is famously silent about distinctions you can't measure.
