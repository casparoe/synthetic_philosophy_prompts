Write a dialogue between two philosophers of mind: one a non-reductive physicalist who thinks mental states genuinely cause behavior, the other someone convinced by the causal exclusion argument that if physics is causally complete, the mental is at best along for the ride. But the exclusion side should get to push back on that, e.g. that chemical properties are still physical in a way mental properties supposedly aren't, or that we only tolerate the loose talk because nothing metaphysical rides on it.
