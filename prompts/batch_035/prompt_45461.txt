I am a statistician by training and trade, fluent in estimation theory, causal inference, and the diagnosis of model misspecification, and only an amateur in philosophy, with what I know of metaethics picked up from argumentative friends rather than from study. I would like you to restate the main evolutionary debunking arguments against moral realism in my vocabulary, as faithfully as you can. The argument I have in mind is the one claiming that our moral beliefs and dispositions were produced by a process — natural selection — that optimized for reproductive fitness rather than for tracking any independent moral truth, so that our moral convictions are in some sense unfit to be evidence about what is really valuable. Translate it in the terms I use daily: treat whatever process generated our moral dispositions as a kind of estimator, and say what loss function it was selected under, what parameter, if any, it can be said to identify, and in what sense the resulting beliefs are biased or the model misspecified. Also translate, if it goes through, the realist's standard reply that there is a third factor — something like basic requirements of social cooperation — that made fitness and moral truth tend to coincide, so that tracking the one amounted to tracking the other. Then give me a residue report: which parts of the debate map cleanly onto things I know, such as bias, overfitting to the training distribution, absence of identification, or the unavailability of independent data; which parts you had to bend or distort to make them fit my vocabulary; and whether anything in the philosophers' dispute has no counterpart in statistics at all. What I most want to know is whether the debunker is ultimately saying something like "your estimator was fit to the wrong loss function and there is no held-out data to correct it, so your confidence is unjustified", or whether the real disagreement turns on something — the normative status of the requirement that beliefs be truth-sensitive in the right way, perhaps — that statistics simply has no means of expressing. Please write in continuous prose only, with no headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables, in the manner of a long letter.
