I've been reading the rationalist blogosphere since the Overcoming Bias days, and somewhere along the way — I believe via a LessWrong physics thread that waded into the hole argument — I accumulated a set of views about spacetime metaphysics that I have never subjected to a proper consistency check. I would like one now, before I embarrass myself at a meetup. Here is where I stand, stated as plainly as I can:

(1) I take the map-territory distinction seriously as an epistemic principle: whatever in a theory's formalism makes no difference to anything observable, even in principle, is a feature of the map rather than the territory, and treating it as a substantive metaphysical question is a confusion to be dissolved rather than answered.

(2) I am nonetheless a realist about spacetime structure in general relativity: the metric field is a genuine dynamical entity, it carries energy, it is not a bookkeeping device for relations among material events, and I do not want to be talked into structuralist evasions on this point.

(3) I accept Leibniz equivalence — models related by a diffeomorphism of the manifold represent the very same physical situation — and my reason is the hole argument: if diffeomorphically related models were genuinely distinct worlds, then nothing inside the hole could fix which one actually obtains, classical GR would be radically indeterministic, and I am confident it is not.

(4) I think diffeomorphism invariance in GR is exactly the same kind of thing as gauge invariance in electromagnetism — the vector potential versus the vector potential plus a gradient of a scalar — namely plain formal redundancy: one physical state, many descriptions, no metaphysical residue left over.

(5) On the Leibniz-Clarke debate, I side firmly with Leibniz: Newtonian absolute space is surplus structure, and a universe shifted five feet eastward, with all relations preserved, is not a different universe.

(6) I believe there are genuine facts of bare identity among spacetime points — facts about which point is which over and above the total pattern of metric and material properties — because otherwise I cannot see what the metric field is supposed to be a field on, or what is curved when we say spacetime is curved.

Please treat these six as a package and audit them. Identify which pairs are genuinely inconsistent, which merely look problematic, rank the tensions by severity, and for each say what would have to give and what accepting that revision would cost me elsewhere on the list. Then tell me which single revision would clean up the most. I suspect something in this list does not survive contact with the rest, but I honestly cannot tell whether the fault lies with my realism, my map-territory principle, or my confidence that the hole argument and the gauge analogy are the same story told twice.
