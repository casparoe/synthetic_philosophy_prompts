so for a philosophy of psychiatry reading group i've been asked to circulate a short position statement before the next session so everyone can poke at it, and honestly before they do i'd like a consistency audit first. here's roughly where i stand:

(1) i'm basically a realist about at least some psychiatric diagnoses. schizophrenia is a real neurodevelopmental thing, not a bookkeeping device, and the long-run goal of nosology should be carving nature at its joints — eventually handing classification over to biology.

(2) i take Hacking's looping effects seriously. people classified under a human kind respond to being classified, the classification can alter the people, and this isn't some exotic edge case — it's just how the human sciences work. categories in psychiatry are interactive in a way cancer categories aren't.

(3) but i think looping effects are mostly confined to presentation and help-seeking. they change who shows up at the clinic, how people describe their symptoms, what they're willing to report — they don't reach down into the underlying conditions. the underlying conditions don't care what we call them.

(4) i think the removal of homosexuality from the DSM in 1973 was epistemically warranted, not merely a political victory. the category simply failed to track any pathology, and i'd push back on anyone who frames it as "values beat science that time."

(5) i approved of folding asperger's into autism spectrum disorder in the DSM-5, partly on looping grounds — the label had accumulated so much identity and community baggage that it was starting to distort who ended up with the diagnosis.

(6) i think the steep rise in ADHD diagnoses over the last few decades is mostly looping (destigmatization, awareness, diagnostic spread through schools and social media), which is to say the true prevalence of the underlying condition is roughly stable and in principle measurable if you could strip the noise out.

the statement is due in a week so i have time to revise. does this package hang together? if parts of it have to give, tell me which parts and what giving them up costs me. and please don't just flag everything as in tension — i want to know which tensions are real, which only look real until you press on them, and which single revision would clean up the most if it came to that.
