The symbol grounding debate has been going since Harnad's 1990 paper and as far as I can tell it's gone basically nowhere in 35 years. You've got the Harnad side: a system that only manipulates formal symbols, like Searle in the room or an LLM crunching text, never gets meaning, because meaning requires a causal connection between symbols and the things they're about. Then you've got the distributional side: word embeddings and LLMs show that symbol-to-symbol structure alone gets you a surpising amount of semantics — you can cluster king and queen the right way, translate, do analogy tasks — so maybe grounding gets smuggled in through the correlations text carries with the world. And now there's a third faction saying the whole question was confused from the start, that grounding mixes up at least three different things: a causal-historical link (this token tracks that thing because of a learning history that involved the world), a referential-competence claim (the system uses the token in ways that would succeed if it ever acted on them), and something quasi-phenomenal (there being something it is like to have the concept). Nobody ever disambiguates, so each side wins on its own reading and loses on the others.

My hypothesis is that the stall is basically verbal: the skeptics are making the causal-historical claim, the LLM defenders are answering the referential-competence claim, and each side hears the other as dodging. But I'm not fully sure, because there also seems to be a real empirical question lurking underneath — how much world-structure actually gets encoded in pure text statistics, and whether that fraction is enough for whatever it is we care about — and neither side seems eager to own that it's empirical rather than conceptual.

So diagnose this for me: is it a verbal disagreement, two different questions living under one word, a hidden empirical question, or a genuine substantive fight about what meaning is? Use what the sides actually say to make the case, tell me whether my verbal-disagreement hypothesis survives contact with the details, and say what would actually count as progress here — a distinction, an experiment, a benchmark, some conceptual move — or whether the framing is unsalvageable and we should be asking a different question entirely.
