I want to fix that with a game instead of explaining it. My tentative idea was a game where they have to endorse claims without being allowed to use the word "true": single sentences first, then giant conjunctions, then a sealed envelope nobody's allowed to open before endorsing its contents — but I'm not sure how to make that into something with actual stakes, so feel free to redo it entirely.
