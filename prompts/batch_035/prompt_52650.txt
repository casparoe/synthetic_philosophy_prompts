Design an in-class simulation where participants must allocate a fixed charity budget across several interventions, where each intervention's actual payoff depends on hidden downstream rules that the facilitator holds and the players never see, and where the strategy that seems to perform best in early rounds is quietly undone by delayed knock-on effects in later rounds. Give me the rules, materials, a payoff scheme that doesn't use real money, and a debrief that sharply distinguishes two failure modes: being ignorant of facts the players could have discovered with more research, versus facing a choice that no amount of research would settle.
