I am a high school junior writing a term paper on historical bias, and I would like a careful analysis of the saying "history is written by the victors." Last week my world history teacher used it to dismiss our textbook outright: since the book was produced in a country that has won most of its wars, she said, nothing in it about those wars should be taken at face value. Something about that move bothers me, but I am having trouble sorting out exactly what, and I suspect the trouble is that the sentence itself is doing several different jobs at once.

Part of my problem is that the slogan does not even seem to fit the cases I have been reading about for my project. The Confederacy lost the Civil War, yet its own account of the conflict — the "Lost Cause" narrative — dominated Southern textbooks and monuments for decades. My own state's curriculum now teaches the war largely through the perspectives of enslaved people, who were about as far from victors as anyone in the story. And Thucydides, who wrote the great history of the Peloponnesian War, fought for Athens, the side that lost it. If the victors write history, why do defeated sides and the powerless seem to be all over the historical record?

So here is what I would like from you. First, lay out the distinct precise claims the sentence could be making — for example, a claim about who physically survives a war to write its history, a claim about who controls the archives, printing presses, and school boards that decide which accounts circulate, a claim about whose framing quietly becomes the default even when other accounts exist, and a claim about whether the winners' self-justifications tend to crowd out everyone else's. Add any readings I have not thought of. For each reading, say whether it is trivially true, substantive and defensible, or simply false, and give a sentence of justification. Second, tell me which of those readings, if any, would actually support my teacher's move of dismissing the textbook wholesale, and which would only license something weaker, such as reading it while checking what the defeated and the powerless recorded. Please present the readings as a numbered list I can attach to my paper.
