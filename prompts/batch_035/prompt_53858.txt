Within my vocabulary, say what plays each of Davidson's roles: the Principle of Charity, including the corollary that interpretation must make most of the agent's beliefs come out true; the holism of belief and meaning; the Tarskian truth theory as the thing the interpreter ends up constructing; and the claim that the constraints on interpretation are constitutive of meaning and belief rather than merely heuristic devices for getting to them.
