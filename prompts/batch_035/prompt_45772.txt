Quick context: I'm giving a talk in two weeks to a mixed seminar that's about half philosophers of mind, a couple of metaphysicians who are really into powers, grounding, and "strong emergence" talk, and one condensed matter postdoc who shows up to everything. It shows that higher-level sciences are practically autonomous, sure, but it licenses no new ontology: no extra laws beyond fundamental physics, no downward causation, nothing metaphysically emergent. So anyone who leans on physics-style emergence to prop up non-reductive physicalism about the mind is borrowing an analogy that can't actually carry that weight.
