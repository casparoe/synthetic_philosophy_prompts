Since forming such higher-order thoughts requires concepts, the theory implies that infants and animals, lacking concepts, have no inner life whatsoever — they are unconscious in the same way as a blindsight patient. But dogs plainly do have experiences, so higher-order theories have been decisively refuted, and most consciousness researchers have abandoned them in favour of global workspace theory.'
