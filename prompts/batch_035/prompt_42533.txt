2. The thermodynamic arrow comes from a spectacularly low-entropy initial condition at the Big Bang, and our memories and records are broadly reliable evidence about the past — including about the past that makes this very claim plausible.
3. I take seriously the now-standard cosmological picture in which the universe has an enormously long future of de Sitter expansion driven by a small positive vacuum energy.
4. On that picture, Boltzmann brains eventually outnumber ordinary observers by a factor so large that ordinary observers are a rounding error, and typicality reasoning then says that I am, most probably, a Boltzmann brain fluctuating into existence right now, complete with false memories and a falsely perceived orderly environment.
5. Even so, I hold that it is fully rational — not merely a pragmatic policy — to keep trusting my memories and records and to dismiss the Boltzmann-brain hypothesis in belief and in action.
6. Self-locating credences are objective and meaningful, and anthropic reasoning is just ordinary conditionalization on indexical evidence; for the record, I am a thirder in Sleeping Beauty.
7. The proper response to the Boltzmann-brain problem is physical, not epistemological: a cosmology that produces Boltzmann brains is thereby shown to be wrong or incomplete, and that is the real lesson of the problem.
