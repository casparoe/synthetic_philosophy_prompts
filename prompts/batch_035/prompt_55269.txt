My instinct is that this is all just an argument about optimization before we had the vocabulary for it, but I want it done properly rather than me hand-waving.

So: restate the debate in the vocabulary I actually use. Be as faithful as you can -- I want the actual positions translated, not a version where the market side gets to be "the clever algorithm we all know works" by fiat.

Is that second thing just a modeling choice I'm glossing over, or is it the actual crux that my vocabulary has no slot for?
