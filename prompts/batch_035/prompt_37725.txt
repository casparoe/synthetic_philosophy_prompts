The whole generational edifice runs into the age-period-cohort identification problem: for any outcome — home ownership, trust in institutions, whatever — an observed difference between cohorts could be an age effect (people get more conservative as they age, allegedly), a period effect (the 2008 crash hit everyone at once), or a genuine cohort effect, and since birth cohort is literally just survey year minus age, the three are perfectly collinear. No statistical trick separates them without arbitrary identifying assumptions, so every "Millennials killed X" headline is unfalsifiable storytelling. On top of that, your boundaries are a mess — the edges of Gen Z differ between Pew, the marketers, and different countries entirely, which is not how anyone treats a real kind. Defend yourself.
