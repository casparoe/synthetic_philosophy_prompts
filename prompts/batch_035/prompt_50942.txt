So I'm a backend engineer, zero philosophy background, and I fell down a rabbit hole recently that I can't get out of: the question of whether the axioms mathematicians build everything on might just be inconsistent and we haven't noticed yet. And I gather he went right back to working on it, which tells you something about how live he thought the possibility was.
