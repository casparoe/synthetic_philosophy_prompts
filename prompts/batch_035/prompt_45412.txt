Have the "we-intentions are real" person argue that something genuinely new shows up somewhere between (a) and (b) — like, in (a) each intends "I'll clear the sidewalk" but in (b) each intends to do *my part* of a thing we're doing together, and maybe there's even a sense in which in (b) I'd owe the other person something if I just went inside mid-job. Make it a real back-and-forth where they actually answer each other's points, not two speeches. And end with each of them saying what would make them change their mind.
