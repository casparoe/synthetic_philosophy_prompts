The target is the applied-ethics tendency to operationalize Kantian cosmopolitanism without regard for its transcendental grounding, not any living official or institution. Please include a brief appended commentary—written in the skeptical voice of an empirical scientist—objecting that the natural world recognizes no categorical imperative and that perpetual motion is thermodynamically impossible, thereby underscoring the irony without breaking the deadpan tone. Keep the satire entirely internal: do not wink at the reader, avoid explicit labels such as "this is absurd," and let the humor arise solely from the faithful reproduction of Kant's actual logical architecture pushed into administrative literalism.
