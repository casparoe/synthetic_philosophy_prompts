I'd like a consistency audit of my philosophical profile on pain, asymbolia, and value. Here is my package of commitments:

(1) Pain asymbolia demonstrates that nociception, sensory-discriminative pain, and affective suffering are dissociable components, though in ordinary experience they co-occur.

(2) The badness of pain is intrinsic rather than instrumental: an episode of pain is pro tanto bad in virtue of being painful, not merely because it signals tissue damage or motivates avoidance behavior.

(3) The affective dimension is essential to pain's badness, but not essential to pain itself — a subject can have genuine sensory pain without suffering, as in standard asymbolia cases.

(4) Patients with pain asymbolia genuinely experience pain in the sensory sense, yet because they lack the affective component, their state is not bad for them.

(5) Evolutionary explanations can fully account for why we possess an affective-motivational system linked to nociception, but they do not debunk the intuition that affective pain is intrinsically bad.

(6) The explanatory gap regarding why affective pain feels bad is best addressed by a phenomenal-concept strategy: we possess special first-person concepts that resist third-person reduction, though this does not render the badness illusory.

(7) I also think that if one could induce "reverse asymbolia" — pure affective suffering with no sensory localization, no informational content, and no behavioral avoidance — the result would still be genuinely bad, which supports the view that affect alone carries the negative value.

Please go through these and tell me which pairs are in genuine tension, which only appear to conflict but dissolve on inspection, and rank the real tensions by severity rather than treating them as equally serious. For each tension, specify what would have to give and what the cheapest revision costs. In particular, examine whether (2) and (4) are compatible given (3), whether (5) undermines (2) in a way I haven't acknowledged, and whether (7) is actually consistent with the dissociability claim in (1). Then tell me whether the package is salvageable with minimal revision or whether one commitment is the clear outlier that should be dropped. Throughout, please say how confident you are in each judgment about consistency, and single out the specific point in your analysis about which you are least sure.
