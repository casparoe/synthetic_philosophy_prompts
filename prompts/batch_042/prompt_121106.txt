Half the room are family who think work is just 'what pays the bills,' the other half are labor historians and a coupple philosphy grad students who will roll there eyes if I romanticize craft labor. Make that idea do real structural work — it should explain why he kept every misprinted run he salvaged, and why the new system feels like a kind of disappearance even though its more effcient. Keep it warm, not a lecture. And please, don't quote Marx or anyone with page numbers — I don't want to sound like I'm doing homework.
