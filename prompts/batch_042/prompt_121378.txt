My older brother recently declined a promotion that required relocating his family. He told our parents that he "had no real choice" because uprooting the children would be irresponsible, and added that he is "just not an ambitious person — that is who he is". In private, he confessed that he feels secretly relieved, yet also guilty, as if this relief proves he has betrayed a more courageous, authentic self he ought to have realised. He asks me whether accepting his limits was genuine, or merely an excuse for fear. I am unsure whether he is truly constrained or evading responsibility. Could you analyse what is happening in his reasoning about duty, nature, and possibility, and advise whether his guilt is appropriate or part of the confusion? Please respond in British English.
