In particular, address whether this analogical stance strengthens or weakens the demand that policy-relevant forecasts be backed by mechanistic explanations; what it implies about the ethical and epistemic limits of prediction in domains involving human agency; and how it might help distinguish rigorous scientific forecasting from deterministic overreach of the kind Kepler sought to separate from astronomy proper. Focus on conceptual implications rather than historical scholarship, and do not include citations, bibliographies, or reading lists.
