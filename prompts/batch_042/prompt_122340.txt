After a long and somewhat heated discussion with a friend about whether bad assumptions ever really hurt scientific explanation, I want a consistency check on the position I defended. My commitments are roughly these: first, I accept the F-twist defense — unrealistic premises in a model are not a defect if the model delivers accurate predictions, regardless of descriptive fidelity. Second, I think explanatory models face a stricter constraint than predictive ones: they must be decomposable into mechanisms that could in principle operate in the target system, which rules out pure fictionalization but allows strategic idealization. Third, I take the ideal gas law as the paradigm of legitimate idealization, since relaxing its false assumptions incrementally recovers real thermodynamic behavior. Fourth, I regard climate-model parameterizations of sub-grid convection — represented by tunable bulk formulas lacking direct physical interpretation — as crossing into fictionalization, yet I still want to claim they are genuinely explanatory of large-scale dynamics rather than merely predictive instruments. Fifth, I hold that whenever unrealistic assumptions are calibrated post-hoc against the very data they predict, the F-twist collapses into circularity, so predictive success alone cannot save the model. Sixth, I think quantum field theory's virtual particles are purely fictional calculational artifacts, but this does not undermine the Standard Model's explanatory status because its ontology is anchored in the Lagrangian rather than in the perturbative expansion. Do these hang together? I am especially unsure whether my acceptance of climate parameterizations as explanatory is compatible with my ban on fictionalization in explanatory models, and whether my rejection of post-hoc calibration undermines my general endorsement of the F-twist. Please audit these commitments: identify which pairs are in genuine tension and which only appear to conflict, rank the tensions by severity, and for each say what the cheapest revision would be and what it costs. I do not know which commitment I should be willing to drop, so treat all six as live.
