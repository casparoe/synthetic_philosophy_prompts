Write the full specification of desiderata that any adequate resolution of the heterogeneity problem for hedonism must satisfy. The problem is that pleasures appear phenomenologically disparate—consider the warmth of sunlight on skin, the satisfaction of resolving an abstract proof, the gustatory pleasure of a complex wine, and the relief of a long-held anxiety dissolving—yet hedonism treats them as instances of a single evaluative currency. I want the list of requirements an answer must meet, with the motivating case or conceptual pressure behind each. Specifically, include: (1) a criterion for cross-modal comparability—whether and how one can rank these pleasures—without assuming a hidden common sensation; (2) a constraint on how the theory distinguishes sensory, cognitive, and affective pleasures without fragmenting pleasure into unrelated kinds; (3) a demand that the theory explain why pleasure functions as a unified normative reason across these domains, rather than as a homonym; (4) a requirement that it accommodate "thin" pleasures (mere absence of discomfort) and "thick" pleasures (rich qualitative content) without reducing one to the other; (5) a condition about parasitic or perverse pleasures—such as schadenfreude or the pleasure taken in cruelty—showing whether they share the same feature as benign pleasures or reveal a structural ambiguity; and (6) a constraint on consistency with the neuroscientific observation that different pleasures recruit overlapping but non-identical reward circuitry, so the theory must allow for family resemblance or distributed realization without collapsing into pure pluralism. For each desideratum, flag whether it is non-negotiable for any hedonist theory or whether it expresses a preference of one camp (e.g., phenomenological essentialism versus functionalism). Then indicate which pair of desiderata you think is most likely jointly unsatisfiable, and why. Keep the weight on the criteria and their motivating distinctions rather than on scoring existing theories, though a brief note on where classical qualitative hedonism, quantitative hedonism, and a functionalist or dispositional account stand against the list is welcome.
