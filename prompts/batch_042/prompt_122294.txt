For each, give the motivating case or argument behind it, and flag any pair of desiderata you think cannot be jointly satisfied—especially any tension between accounting for qualitative character in causal-physical terms and preserving its alleged non-relational, intrinsic nature. Then briefly note how an eliminativist denial of qualia, a representationalist reduction to intentional content, and a primitive-property dualism each stand against the list, but keep the weight on the criteria rather than the scorecard.
