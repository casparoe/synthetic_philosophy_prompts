To avoid the usual anthropocentric slide, I want to work with a deliberately artificial three-axis framework that I have sketched: (A) phenomenal intensity, meaning the qualitative depth or richness of affective experience; (B) temporal integration, meaning the capacity for suffering or satisfaction to extend, accumulate, and be anticipated across time rather than remaining purely momentary; and (C) relational embeddedness, meaning the structural coupling of an entity’s welfare to other agents or ecological networks so that harm or flourishing propagates beyond the individual boundary.

Using only this framework, please do the following.
