That makes the combination problem feel ethically urgent — do all those micro-subjects cancel out in aggregation, or do they accumulate like heat?

I want the repetition and rhyme to do the philosophical work, showing why the two answers circle each other without resolving, then end side by side unreconciled. Keep it scientifically grounded — mention decoherance, thermal noise, or field fluctuations — but don't turn it into a lecture. No sermon about saving the universe, just the tension left open.
