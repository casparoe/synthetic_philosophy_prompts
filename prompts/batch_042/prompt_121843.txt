The Predictive Dissolution theorists maintain that the frame problem is a pseudo-problem generated by an obsolete commitment to global symbolic consistency; embodied, hierarchical predictive systems never required an explicit frame because they operate through continuous local error minimization and action-oriented sampling, so the combinatorial worry never arises in the architectures that actually work.
