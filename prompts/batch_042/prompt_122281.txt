I am designing a graduate seminar on the metaphysics of agency and its environmental dependence, and I need a rigorous comparison for the students. Compare the familiar embedded-agency framework—according to which an agent’s capacities are partly constituted by external structures that are functionally integrated with her cognitive processes, not merely causally supporting them—with a stricter variant I will call normative embedding. On the normative-embedding view, an external scaffold counts as genuinely part of an agent’s exercise of agency only if it is governed by a second-order normative commitment of the agent herself: she must be able to endorse, revise, or repudiate the scaffold’s role in her deliberation, rather than merely depend on it causally.

Your task has three parts. First, clarify the conceptual boundary between embedded agency and normative embedding: does the latter add a genuinely distinct condition, or does it merely restate the functional-integration criterion with a psychological gloss? Second, apply both accounts to the concrete case of a professional musician who relies on a meticulously arranged studio environment—specific microphone placements, acoustic treatments, and digital routing presets—to execute improvisational performances. Identify precisely who or what the agent is, which elements count as the scaffold, what the functional relation is, and whether any normative relation of endorsement or revision is present; then show exactly where the two theories diverge in assessing whether the studio environment is part of her agency or merely an instrumental tool. Third, evaluate whether normative embedding succeeds in addressing the boundary problem—the difficulty of locating where the agent ends and the environment begins—without collapsing into an over-intellectualized conception that excludes non-linguistic animals or human infants from being agents altogether. Be disciplined about the mapping: say where each theory is silent rather than forcing it to speak, and do not appeal to empirical literature you cannot verify from the conceptual resources alone.
