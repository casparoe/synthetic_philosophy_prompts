Here is my opening challenge. Imagine a distributed climate-governance algorithm—no single operator—adjusting carbon-capture protocols. In one run it foreseeably but unintentionally prevents coastal flooding (help); in an architecturally identical run with different initial conditions it foreseeably but unintentionally permits flooding (harm).
