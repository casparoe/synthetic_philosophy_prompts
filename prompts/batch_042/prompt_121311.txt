Before we meet again, I want to bring a precise disambiguation. Can you lay out the distinct precise theses that slogan might express—ranging from the trivial psychological claim that calm equals low arousal, to the axiological claim that pleasure is merely value-neutral, to a stronger structural or deprivational view that all goods consist solely in removing prior deficits and that saturation yields zero further benefit, and any other serious reading? For each, say whether it is defensible, whether it actually supports dismissing the sedation/death objection, whether it renders tranquilism false or trivial, and which version a consistent tranquilist must actually adopt. Present the readings as a clear numbered list within one paragraph, not exceeding 300 words.
