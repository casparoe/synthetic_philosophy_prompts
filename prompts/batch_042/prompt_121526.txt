Take Karl Jaspers idea of the axial hinge—where mythic thinking breaks and second-order reflexivity pops up across seperate civilisations around the same time. Now apply it to the 2020-2021 global shift to remote work and digital governance, which hit almost everywhere within months rather than centuries. But add this twist: treat network speed as collapsing the temporal spread Jaspers thought was definitional. Analize the pivot as a potential micro-hinge: what are the "civilisations" here (platforms? nation-states?), what counts as the break with prior myth (the office? physical presence of the state?), and where does the analogy clearly fail? I want a careful mapping—who faced what choices, what the hinge theory would have predicted that didnt happen, and whether that failure belongs to the case or to Jaspers. Dont just say it was a tech shift; tell me if the theory survives being compressed like this, or if the compression kills it.
