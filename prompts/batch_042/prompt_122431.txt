Show how each discipline tends to study one of these phenomena almost exclusively, and explain why recent interdisciplinary work has conflated the two, producing false predictions about both biological and artificial agents. The speech must remain accessible to both halves of the room: gloss any field-specific jargon immediately, and avoid claiming that biological pleasure is identical to an AI reward function or that either domain already supplies a complete model of the other.
