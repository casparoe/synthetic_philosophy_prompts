One faction holds that institutional rules must be enforced regardless of subjective opacity; the other holds that if knowledge is not luminous, sanctions require demonstrable recognition of the specific violation, which she lacked. Please draft the formal opinion memo I will circulate: recommend either upholding or overturning the suspension with a concrete time frame for any conditions or appeal; treat the luminosity and KK-based defense seriously rather than dismissing it; and address the strongest counter-argument—that rules become unenforceable if every ambiguous mental state excuses compliance—without reducing the opposing view to a strawman.
