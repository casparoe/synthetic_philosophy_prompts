write me a villanelle - 19 lines, two refrains that alternate and then come together in the last quatrain. one refrain should be about that leftover flicker still being there, and the other should be about moral blame landing anyway. but here's the twist: don't use the old scientist-with-a-button story. instead set it in a near-future scenario where an algorithim predicts your move before you make it, and only intervines if you deviate from the prediction. the speaker should be the person making the choice. the form should do the philosophical work - the refrains keep circling past each other like the argument does, with one claiming there's always that tiny deviation-space and the other insisting the blame lands on the path actually taken, since nothing forced that one. don't resolve the tension, just let the rhyme scheme almost lock and then slip, especially in the tercets. it needs to be accurate enough that someone who knows the debate recognizes the structure of the counterexample and the flicker objection, but don't ask for citations or anything - i just want the poem.
