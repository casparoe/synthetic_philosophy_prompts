write me a deadpan FAQ for new hires at a company whose entire policy manual runs like a game of Nomic: the manual includes rules for how the manual itself can be amended, any employee can propose an amendment at the weekly meeting, and amendments pass by majority vote. maybe 400-500 words. the FAQ should treat totally normal workplace questions -- expensing lunch, vacation accrual, the dress code, whether the break room microwave is communal -- as live constitutional crises, because every answer depends on which amendment rule was in force when the relevant amendment passed, and nobody is fully sure whether the rule making the amendment rules amendable was itself amended validly. the humor has to come from the actual headaches of self-amending rules: mutable vs. immutable rules, whether an amendment can retroactively invalidate the procedure that passed it, an amendment to the amendment rules applying to its own passage, a rule that was repealed by an amendment the repeal later rendered void. don't invent random absurdity; a reader who knows Suber's game or the paradox of self-amendment should feel the satire is fair. keep it completely straight-faced, like a real HR document that stopped noticing anything was wrong around 2019, and end with one question the FAQ honestly cannot answer. american english please.
