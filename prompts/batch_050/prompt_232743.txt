Last night a friend and I spent three hours arguing about the right vocabulary for LLM failures, and I came away less sure of my own position than I'd like to admit. I've written down where I currently stand, and I'd like an audit before I go back for round two. My commitments:

(1) Lying requires that the speaker believe what they say is false and intend thereby to deceive. Since LLMs have neither beliefs nor intentions, no LLM output is ever literally a lie, and people who say a chatbot "lied to them" are speaking loosely.

(2) Fabricated LLM outputs are nonetheless bullshit, in roughly Frankfurt's sense: they are produced without regard for whether they are true, and this — not falsehood as such — is what defines the category.

(3) Frankfurt is right that bullshit is more corrosive to truth than lying is, and this is exactly why confident LLM confabulation is a more serious epistemic problem than ordinary human deception.

(4) Whether an output counts as bullshit has nothing to do with its truth-value. The fluent, accurate outputs of the very same system are bullshit too, produced by the same truth-indifferent process.

(5) The standard term "hallucination" is a harmless shorthand; nobody competent is actually misled by it into thinking the model has perceptual experiences.

(6) Classifying an utterance as lie, bullshit, or honest error is a classification of the utterance in its context of use, not a diagnosis of the producer's inner states — which is why such classifications can sensibly be applied to parrots, thermostats, and language models just as much as to people.

What I want to know: does this set hang together? Please go through it and identify which commitments are genuinely in tension and which only appear to be, ordering the real tensions by severity. For each genuine tension, state precisely what would have to give and what that revision would cost me elsewhere in the set. Then tell me which single change would restore consistency at the lowest overall price. I have no fixed view about which commitment is expendable, so please don't assume one.
