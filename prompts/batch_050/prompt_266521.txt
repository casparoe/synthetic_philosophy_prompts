Write a joke for our high school math club about Zorn’s lemma. The punchline should turn on the difference between a maximal element and a greatest element, not just on infinity or the axiom of choice being weird. Use this toy poset: subsets of {coffee, tea, homework} that don’t contain both coffee and tea, ordered by inclusion. Make it funny to someone who actually understands the setup, not just a generic math nerd joke, and definately don’t explain the punchline afterward.
