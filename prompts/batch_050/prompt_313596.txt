Write a parody job advertisement, roughly 500 words, posted by a fictional "epistemic services firm" recruiting candidates to serve as basic beliefs — the foundational premises on which clients' entire belief systems will rest. The ad should include the role description, required qualifications, compensation and benefits, and a closing note on which categories of applicant will be turned away. The humor must come from the actual structure of the regress problem rather than from invented absurdities: the job exists because every belief demands justification by a further belief, and someone has to stop the chain; candidates must therefore be able to withstand the interview question "and what justifies you?" without answering it; coherentist applicants, who arrive only in mutually endorsing clusters citing one another as references, are rejected on grounds of circularity; and infinitist applicants, whose chains of supporting reasons never terminate, remain perpetually in the interview stage without ever receiving an offer. Include at least one clause on liability should the client's edifice of knowledge collapse, and at least one HR-style euphemism for dogmatism presented as a virtue (the firm prizes convictions that need no outside validation). Keep the tone deadpan corporate throughout — no winking at the reader, no breaking character — so that each clause of the ad is recognizable to someone who knows the literature as a faithful, if unflattering, description of a real position in the debate.
