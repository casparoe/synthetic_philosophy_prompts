What theory of moral status does this pattern presuppose, if it presupposes any single one? Also flag where the practice is genuinely underdetermined, where several rival theories would license exactly the same lines, and say what would have to change if the law took a strict equal-consideration-of-interests view instead. Where you can, separate what is broadly agreed among philosophers who work on moral status from what remains genuinely contested, and don't present a contested position as the consensus.
