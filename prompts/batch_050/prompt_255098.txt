I teach a political philosophy discussion group and want a “which approach to voting do you actually hold?” decision tree, starting from: you may vote in a noncompulsory election where every plausible candidate is significantly flawed. Build the tree from 7–9 diagnostic questions rather than from theory labels—e.g. whether voting matters because of consequences or civic duty, whether it is mainly causal or expressive, whether one ballot’s tiny impact changes the duty, and whether supporting the lesser evil makes someone complicit in that candidate’s wrongdoing. End with leaves for consequentialist lesser-evil voting, duty-based voting, expressive voting, abstention through inefficacy, abstention through clean-hands/complicity concerns, and principled rejection of electoral participation; flag leaves your questions can’t really distinguish, and identify any answer combinations that seem logically possible but oddly unoccupied.
