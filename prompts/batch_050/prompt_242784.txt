My grandfather is in late-stage heart failure and the family is having the fight. My aunt keeps deploying the same move: when our old labrador got bone cancer we didn't let her linger for months in pain, we took her to the vet and did the kind thing, so why are we willing to do for a dog what we won't do for dad? Everyone nods like she's just said something decisive. But my cousin, who's a hospice nurse, says the comparison is exactly backwards -- that the reason we euthanize animals is that they can't understand what's happening to them or make plans for the time that's left, and that's precisely what grandpa still can, so the "we wouldn't treat a dog this way" line actually argues for treating him differently, not the same. Meanwhile my aunt's rejoinder is that this just proves the point: we euthanize animals because it's cruel to prolong suffering, and the suffering is the same in both cases even if the subject differs.

Can you audit the dog analogy as it's being used here? I want the explicit mapping: what plays the role of the vet, the owner, the decision, the suffering, the death, the consent (or lack of it). Then the places the mapping breaks -- I assume things like that owners decide for pets without being asked, that we have different obligations to beings we can explain things to, that a human death can have meaning-making or reconciliations a dog's can't, that vets and doctors have different professional roles, but tell me which of those are disanalogies that matter for my aunt's conclusion and which are just differences. Also: is my cousin right that the analogy cuts the other way, or is she over-reading it too? And if the analogy can't carry "therefore assisted dying is obviously right," can it still carry something weaker, like a presumption that refusing all hastening options needs justification? If there's a better comparison for what she actually wants to say -- I've heard people use relieving suffering in palliative care with opioids, or respecting a patient's refusal of treatment -- tell me which one does the work without smuggling in the dog.
