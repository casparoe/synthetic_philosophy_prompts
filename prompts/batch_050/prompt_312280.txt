I am a PhD student in political philosophy writing a dissertation on informal social sanction. My current chapter examines whether the standard justifications of state punishment — retributive, deterrent, rehabilitative, and communicative/expressive — carry over to decentralized online sanctioning such as deplatforming and public call-outs. A complication I want to build into the analysis is what I call the sanctioner's dividend: unlike state officials, online sanctioners frequently gain status, attention, or in-group standing precisely by imposing sanctions, and the audience can rarely distinguish sanctioning done for the wrongdoing from sanctioning done for the dividend. Given this complication, please rank the four justifications from most to least defensible as grounds for deplatforming, give one or two sentences explaining each placement, and indicate which justification you think the sanctioner's dividend damages most severely and why.
