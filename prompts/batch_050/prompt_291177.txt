So I got into an argument with my brother last week about the old saying that history is written by the victors, and I cant stop turning it over in my head. Heres what I want: write me the official FAQ page from the Bureau of National History in an imaginary country where this is literally and openly true — every time the government changes hands, the Bureau reissues the approved account of the past, same as updating the street signs. Should be deadpan bureaucratic voice, around 500-600 words, with questions like "How often is the past revised?", "What happens to teachers still using the previous edition of events?", "Can I file a correction if my grandfather fought for the losing side?", and "Is there an archive of discontinued facts?" The comedy has to come from the Bureau treating power and perspective as routine adminstrative problems — filing deadlines, complaint forms, waiting lists — not from anyone being cartoonishly evil, and it should never wink at the reader or admit it's satire. By the end, a reader should quietly find themselves chewing on the real questions: whether any version of events counts as the true one, what happens to the evidence that doesnt fit the current edition, and whether the rest of us are already running a softer, less honest version of the same Bureau.
