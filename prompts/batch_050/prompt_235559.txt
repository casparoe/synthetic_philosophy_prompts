Draft me eight or so questions that go past "so souls can spread out, wild right?" For each question give me the follow-up to whichever answer he'll most likely give. Also, since I can't fact-check mid-interview: say how confident you are that each question targets something Hofstadter actually argued versus your extrapolation, and flag the single attribution you're least sure of.
