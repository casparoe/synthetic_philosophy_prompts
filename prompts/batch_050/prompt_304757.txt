Here's where the bar argument comes in. My position was that transparency comes in degrees, plenty of accepted practice (darkroom dodging, stitching panoramas, long exposures) already messes with strict counterfactual dependence, and what journalism actually needs is something like faithfulness to the event, not some metaphysically pure causal chain. We did not resolve this. We did, however, resolve that I lost the bet about whose theory was more popular.
