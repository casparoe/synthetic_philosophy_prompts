I keep seeing arguments about whether RL agents could ever suffer — like whether a reward signal is anything like actual pleasure or pain, or whether it's just a scalar the optimizer pushes around. While reading about this I came across this bit from Jeremy Bentham's An Introduction to the Principles of Morals and Legislation (1789), chapter IV, where he's explaining how people value things:

"An article of property, an estate in land, for instance, is valuable, on what account? On account of the pleasures of all kinds which it enables a man to produce, and what comes to the same thing the pains of all kinds which it enables him to avert. But the value of such an article of property is universally understood to rise or fall according to the length or shortness of the time which a man has in it: the certainty or uncertainty of its coming into possession: and the nearness or remoteness of the time at which, if at all, it is to come into possession."
