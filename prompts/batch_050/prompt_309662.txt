ok so my girlfriend and i broke up two weeks ago and its been a nightmare because we shared EVERYTHING digitally -- one google calendar, a shared notes app where we kept the grocery list and our "ideas for the novel we were gonna write together", a spotify account whose algorithm has now heard 4 years of both of us, the works. she changed the netflix password and i genuinely could not remember my own dentist appointment without the shared cal. which got me thinking about that extended mind stuff, the clark and chalmers thing with otto and his notebook, where the notebook basically counts as part of his memory. if that thesis is right, our breakup wasn't just a breakup, it was like a cognitive amputation. possibly a messy surgery.

so: write me a parody of a family court document -- like a petition for dissolution, or a mediator's recommendations, whatever works funniest -- for a couple dividing up their EXTENDED MIND. custody schedule for the shared calendar (who gets to remember the friend's birthdays on weekdays), visitation rights for the spotify algorithm, a restraining order requiring one party to stay 500 feet from the other's autocomplete. around 600 words, deadpan legalese throughout.

the jokes have to come from actually taking the extended mind thesis seriously -- the parity principle (if it works the same as the internal process, it IS part of the mind), the constant-coupling requirement, otto's notebook as the precedent both sides' lawyers cite, the question of whether the ex deleting the shared notes counts as destroying someone's memories and therefore something way worse than deleting a file. a philosophy of mind person should laugh because you got the thesis right, not because you made up a dumbed-down version. but my freinds who've never heard of clark and chalmers should still find it funny.

also somewhere in the document the mediator should quietly note that the court cannot rule on whether the thesis is true, and both parties are ordered to stop emailing the philosphy department asking for expert witnesses.
