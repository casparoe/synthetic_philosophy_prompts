I am a PhD student in philosophy of physics. As part of my prospectus defense I have been asked to submit a one-page statement of my considered position on gauge theories, and before I circulate it to the committee I would like a consistency audit. Here is the position roughly as I would defend it:

(1) Gauge symmetries are not symmetries of nature but redundancies of description: models related by a gauge transformation represent one and the same physical situation, and only gauge-invariant quantities are physically real. In this they differ fundamentally from global symmetries, which carry genuine empirical content.

(2) The Aharonov-Bohm effect shows that a formulation of electromagnetism purely in terms of the local electric and magnetic fields is incomplete; the vector potential A — or at least its holonomies — must be admitted as physically real.

(3) The hole argument establishes that diffeomorphism-related models of general relativity must be identified on pain of indeterminism, and the parallel between diffeomorphism invariance and internal gauge invariance is exact.

(4) Electroweak symmetry breaking was a genuine physical transition in the early universe: the Higgs mechanism spontaneously broke the SU(2) x U(1) gauge symmetry, thereby generating the masses of the W and Z bosons.

(5) Surplus mathematical structure should be excised wherever possible; the ideal final formulation of a gauge theory would be manifestly gauge-invariant throughout.

(6) Gravitational memory effects and the associated soft theorems show that BMS supertranslations — that is, large gauge transformations — are genuine physical symmetries with observable consequences, and I take this to be an important clue about the status of gauge symmetry in general.

What I would like from you: go through these commitments and identify which pairs are genuinely in tension and which only appear to be, ranking the genuine tensions by severity rather than treating them as equally serious. For each genuine tension, state precisely what would have to give and what the cheapest available fix would cost me. Where a tension dissolves on inspection, say why, and say whether the resolution depends on how one of my commitments is read. Finally, tell me which single revision would do the most to make the overall set coherent. Please work from the content of the commitments as stated — I will supply citations myself.
