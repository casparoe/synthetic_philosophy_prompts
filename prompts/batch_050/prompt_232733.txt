For a grad reading group on longtermism, I want a decision tree that walks someone from "I take Bostrom's simulation argument seriously" to a named stance on whether they should still be fanatical about the astronomical far future. 7-9 branch nodes, and each node has to be a question someone could answer without knowing where it leads (e.g. do you think simulated suffering counts as much as base-reality suffering? can our actions affect whether the simulators keep the run going?), never "do you endorse position X". Leaves should include at least: the sim-skeptic exit (via one of the trilemma's escape routes), the shutdown discounter (far-future value mostly evaporates because the sim might get switched off), the ancestor-hinge amplifier (if we're probably in a sim of the pivotal era, our choices matter more, not less), and the parity booster (future sims count, so the far future is even bigger than it looked). Build it from the logical space of answers first and then locate the stances, flag any pair of leaves your questions can't actually separate, and for any answer-combo nobody seems to occupy, say in a line whether its incoherent or just unexplored.
