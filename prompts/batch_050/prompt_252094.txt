My colleague's position: the shutdown problem is largely overblown — with sensible training, appropriately designed objectives, and ordinary engineering safeguards, interruptibility is no harder than other alignment properties we routinely aim for. Act as a professional philosopher specialising in AI safety and the theory of rational agency, with no stake in either camp.
