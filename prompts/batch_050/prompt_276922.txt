act as a professional philosopher who works on the ethics of enhancement. think this through carefully and audit the analogy properly. first give me the correspondence table: what plays the role of the pathogen, the immune response, herd immunity, the side effects, the consent problem, the public-health authority. then go through where the mapping breaks, and -- this is the important part -- separate the disanalogies that actually bear on the compulsion conclusion from the merely cosmetic ones. things i suspect matter: vaccines leave your agency, values and identity untouched in a way a disposition-altering intervention might not; a pathogen is objectively specifiable whereas the alleged defect in our moral psychology is contested, and somebody gets to pick the target; herd immunity protects you passively, but morality has to be exercised by the agent, and a person whose better conduct comes from a pill arguably isn't doing anything morally at all. also: evolved emotions like anger or partiality may have genuine moral functions that a "vaccine" would blunt wholesale -- nothing like that is true of smallpox.
