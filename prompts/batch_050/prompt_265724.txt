For the "genre week" assignment in my global priorities seminar we have to satirize one of the readings instead of writing a normal response paper, and I got assigned strong longtermism. Write the welcome email that the fictional "Institute for Very Long Run Priorities" sends to its new research fellows: parking info, why the office snack budget was reallocated after a cost-effectiveness analysis of the far future, the policy that staff birthdays go uncelebrated because honoring one presently existing person is astronomically inefficient, and the mandatory onboarding workshop on not being emotionally swayed by suffering you can actually see. Deadpan corporate HR voice, no winking, and the jokes should come from real features of the view -- the sheer size of the expected future, tiny probabilities of enormous payoffs, cluelessness about long-run consequences, the confident shrug at moral uncertainty -- so that someone who actually knows the arguments laughs instead of winces. Max 400 words.
