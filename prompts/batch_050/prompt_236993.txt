So a friend of mine who's way too into effective altruism cornered me with this argument and it's been bugging me (sorry) for weeks. (1) If there's any nonzero chance that insects can suffer, then we have at least some moral reason not to cause them suffering. (2) The chance is clearly nonzero -- they have nociceptors, they avoid harm, bees can apparently be taught things, and honestly we have no idea where sentience starts. (3) The moral weight of a possible harm equals its probability times its badness times how many beings it affects. (4) There are something like 10^18 insects alive at any time, so even at a 1% chance of sentience each, the expected suffering from stuff humans do routinely -- mowing lawns, tilling fields, driving at night, exterminating cockroaches -- absolutely dwarfs all the suffering of the ~8 billion humans. (5) When one source of expected suffering massively outweighs another, morality says to prioritize the bigger one. (6) Therefore we should redirect basically all charitable giving and policy attention away from human poverty and toward reducing insect suffering -- humane pesticides, not mowing, whatever. (7) But that can't be right. On this logic a guy who murders someone but obsessively avoids stepping on ants is doing more moral good than a doctor working in a refugee camp who drives a car. (8) So (1) must be false: a mere nonzero probability of sentience, with nothing more, gives you no actual reasons.

I don't want to accept (8), because it seems to prove way too much in the other direction -- surely some small probability of sentience does something, otherwise we're just guessing that beings who might suffer don't count until we're sure. But I also can't live with (6). Which step actually breaks? Please tell me where it goes wrong, why the bad step looked innocent, and what I have to give up to block the conclusion -- like, if the fix is rejecting (3) or (5), what does that commit me to in other cases (Pascal's mugging, existential risk, whatever)? And if the argument is actually valid, tell me which premise is cheapest to drop.
