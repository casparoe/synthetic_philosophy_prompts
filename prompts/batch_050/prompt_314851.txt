Jonah's reaction was basically "then the theorem is obviously wrong, because people disagree all the time." I feel like he might be missing something but I can't articulate what, and now there's a bet on it.

They should even announce their probabilities to each other out loud at least once. Also, if someone wanted to attack your scenario as cheating or as irrelevant to real life, what's the strongest version of that attack?
