ok so I keep going back and forth on whether "generations" are a real thing and I've sort of landed on a set of views, but I'm worried they don't all fit together. Here's where I'm at:

(a) Generational labels like Millennial or Gen Z aren't natural kinds. Nobody becomes a different kind of person at midnight on some birthday in 1996, and the cutoff years are basically arbitrary -- different sources draw them differently and nobody can tell me what principled fact settles it.

(b) But I also think "not a natural kind" doesn't mean "not real." Money and borders and marriage are all made up too and they're plenty real. So generations could be real as social kinds even if the boundaries are conventional.

(c) Most of the pop stuff about generations is junk. The differences between two random Millennials are way bigger than the average difference between Millennials and Gen Z, and a lot of what gets called a "Gen Z trait" is just what being 22 is like, in any era.

(d) That said, I do think there's one genuinely deep generational line: people who had smartphones and social media from early adolescence versus people who didn't. I keep catching myself treating "grew up online" as a real, informative category -- like it actually tells me something about a person that their raw age doesn't.

(e) And despite all that, I think judging an individual by their generation is a kind of prejudice, roughly on par with judging someone by their nationality. When my manager says "oh he's a Zoomer, he won't want a phone call" I wince.

So: does this package hang together? I'm especially unsure whether (d) is smuggling in exactly the kind of hard boundary I mock in (a), and whether (e) can survive (d) -- if the digital thing is real and deep, why is it wince-worthy to use it? Also tell me if (a) and (b) are actually in tension or only look like it, because people keep telling me they contradict and I don't see it. Rank the problems by how bad they are and tell me which single commitment I'd cheapest give up. Please don't just say "it depends how you define real," I want the actual fault lines.
