Write a joke about Skolem's paradox: the apparent tension between the downward Löwenheim–Skolem theorem, which says that if set theory has a model at all it has a countable one, and set theory's own proof that uncountable sets exist. The joke should land for someone who actually understands how the tension dissolves — namely, that "uncountable" is only a claim internal to the model, since a countable model lacks the bijection that would, from the outside, enumerate its "uncountable" sets. A punchline that merely gestures at infinity being weird or mathematicians being eccentric, without turning on this mismatch between the internal and external perspective, does not count as a success.
