They roll in, they roll out, you don't argue with a thunderstorm, you just put on a jacket and wait. It's usually deployed to mean: stop trying to reason yourself out of feeling things, just observe them non-judgmentally until they pass, and anyone who says you can rethink an emotion is in denial.

If emotions are instead more like judgments about value (the Stoic/Nussbaum-ish line) or perceptions of what's dangerous or offensive or loss-worthy, does the weather picture still license the don't-argue-with-it conclusion, or was the analogy smuggling in the whole passivity story? Also say which disanalogies actually matter for the therapeutic advice versus which are just pedantic.
