"Harsanyi's theorem is basically a proof of utilitarianism from axioms everyone accepts. He showed that if individual preferences and social preferences both satisfy the von Neumann–Morgenstern rationality conditions, and if society's preferences respect unanimous individual preferences (Pareto), then social welfare has to be a weighted sum of individual utilities. Since the weights on different people's utilities have no principled basis for differing, they must be equal, so you get straight utilitarianism: maximize the sum of utilities. The catch people always mention is that this requires cardinal, interpersonally comparable utility, which most economists think is meaningless, so the theorem is generally taken to show that utilitarianism depends on a measurement fiction. That's why Rawls is usually read as the main alternative: he rejected the whole expected utility framework behind Harsanyi."
