So there's this thing that keeps bugging me and I want to know if philosophers have a word for it. It started cause my friend bet me five dollars that I'd end up taking art instead of computer science next semester, and I realized I literally could not think about the bet the way I think about, like, the weather. If she asks me "what are the odds you pick art," I can't give her a number, because the moment I try to estimate it I'm just... deciding again. Like the question "will I pick art" and the question "should I pick art" collapse into the same question in my head, and the probability thing never gets off the ground. But here's the weird part -- I can do it for HER no problem. I'd say she's like 80% gonna pick art. And she can apparently do it for me, she was confident enough to bet money. So the exact same fact is perfectly predictable from the outside and totally un-predictable from the inside, even though I'm the one with the most information about me.

And it's not just that I'm indecisive or whatever, because there are cases where I CAN predict myself fine. If you ask me the odds I'll hit snooze tomorrow instead of getting up for my run, I'll tell you straight up it's like 90%, no collapse happens. Difference seems to be that I've kinda given up on the run decision already, I treat morning-me as this other guy whose behavior I just observe. So sometimes my own future actions are like the weather and sometimes they're not, and the dividing line seems to be whether I still consider the question open. Also this can't just be the same as not knowing what you want, because sometimes I know exactly what I want and the prediction still wont form -- the wanting IS the answer, there's no extra fact of the matter about what I'll do sitting next to it that I could estimate.

So: does this phenomenon have a name, or is there a debate about it I could look into? I'm guessing it connects to free will stuff somehow but I don't want the whole free will debate, I want this specific thing about self-prediction failing while the choice is still live. And if it does have a name, what's the actual boundary -- is the rule "you can't predict what you haven't decided yet," and does that mean the bet with my friend is genuinely unwinnable for her no matter how well she knows me, or is there some way an outside predictor could still rationally beat me at forecasting my own open choices? If there's no established term I'd also take a good coined one, but mostly I want to know exactly what the thing is and isn't.
