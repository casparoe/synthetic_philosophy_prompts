I'm supposed to present my "philosophical profile" at our formal epistemology reading group next week and we've just spent two sessions on Sleeping Beauty, so my profile is basicly my take on that case plus the principles around it, and I want a consistency check before people start poking at it. Quick recap of the setup so we're on the same page: Beauty is put to sleep on Sunday; a fair coin is flipped; she's woken on Monday for sure, and woken again on Tuesday only if the coin lands Tails, with each waking followed by an amnesia drug so that on any waking she can't tell which day it is or whether she's been woken before.

Here's where I've landed. (1) I'm a thirder: on waking, her credence in Heads should be 1/3. I buy the usual indifference argument -- conditional on being told it's Monday, she should split evenly between Heads-Monday and Tails-Monday, and the Monday-Tails and Tuesday-Tails wakings should get equal credence too. (2) On Sunday, before any of this happens, her credence in Heads should be 1/2 -- it's a fair coin and nothing epistemically relevant happens between Sunday and the first waking. (3) I accept some version of the reflection principle: if you know now what your rational credence in something will be later, you should adopt that credence now, at least when you don't expect to lose information in the meantime -- and Beauty loses nothing between Sunday and Monday morning. (4) I'm not on board with SIA-flavored reasoning across observers: in the Presumptuous Philosopher scenario -- where merely noticing that you exist is supposed to massively boost cosmologies positing vastly more observers -- I take the conclusion to be a reductio of the reasoning, not a discovery. (5) I think rational credences have to line up with the bets you'd accept: if Beauty gets offered a bet on the coin at each waking, the odds she should accept are fixed by her credences, full stop. (6) I also think credences should track the frequencies you'd observe if the whole experiment were repeated over and over.

So: does this set hang together? Tell me which commitments genuinely pull against each other and which only look like they do, rank the real tensions by how severe they are, and for each one say what the cheapest fix would be and what it costs. If there's a single revision that would clean up the most, name it. Please don't just flag every pair as inconsistent -- I want to know where the actual problems are, because I honestly can't tell, and I'd rather find out now than in front of the group.
