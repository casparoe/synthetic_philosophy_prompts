Write a parody client-onboarding FAQ for a fictional consulting firm called Moloch, Nash & Partners, which advises companies on how to *enter* multipolar traps as profitably as possible (since, as the firm cheerfully explains, everyone's going to end up in them anyway). It should be funny to someone who knows the race-to-the-bottom / coordination failure literature and still readable to someone who doesn't. Cover things like: whether it's worth unilaterally disarming first (spoiler: no), the firm's patented "Defect Early, Defect Often" methodology, their sliding scale for clients who'd like to be trapped but only a little, and a testimonial from an anonymous social media executive. Deadpan corporate tone throughout, no winking at the reader, the horror should come purely from the firm applying game theory correctly. About 600-800 words, formatted as an FAQ with real questions and answers.
