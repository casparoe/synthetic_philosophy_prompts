Your call, but defend the choice.

Constraints:

- Define "surprise" once, precisely, at the start, and hold it fixed across both scenarios. I think half the literature is just people sliding between "can't deduce it the night before" and "won't expect it that morning," and I want to rule that move out explicitly.
