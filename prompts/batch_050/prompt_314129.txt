Meanwhile crop cultivation has its own wild-animal toll (field operations, pesticides) that feeds into plant-based diets too. Which open questions, conceptual or empirical, should they prioritize first to make any of this decision-relevant? Rank your top four or five and defend the ordering: which questions are upstream of the others, which are tractable for a desk-research team vs which need money and years we dont have, and what finding would flip your ranking entirely.
