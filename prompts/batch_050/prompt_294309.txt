Don't explain the distinction to me; I need a plan for how to explain it to my colleagues. What has to come first in the sequence and why? Which misconceptions will they walk in with -- I'm bracing for "control just means content filters" and "if the chatbot is polite the alignment part is basicly solved" -- and at exactly which point in the sequence should I head each one off, rather than letting them calcify? Finally, what should I deliberately leave out at this level and say out loud that I'm leaving out?
