So I'm a PhD student in political theory and I'm trying to figure out what I actually believe about the ethics of voting by going off my gut on cases, and I'm hoping someone can tell me what principle (if any) is underneath all this. Here's where I land:

- Presidential election, my vote has essentially zero chance of tipping anything, and I still feel genuinely obligated to vote. Sitting it out feels like freeloading, not a neutral choice.
- Same election: voting for the lesser of two evils instead of the third-party candidate whose politics I actually love feels not just permissible but basically required. The third-party vote feels like a dereliction.
- BUT if I lived in a totally safe state, the third-party vote seems fine, even good. I realize this and the last one are maybe just "consequences matter," so this pair might be easy.
- Local school-board race where I know nothing about any of the candidates: staying home feels fine, honestly maybe better than voting uninformed. Which seems to clash with my first intuition pretty badly.
- A friend who votes for a candidate he himself thinks would be a disaster, "to send a message to the party": seems clearly wrong to me, worse than not voting at all.
- Vote swapping with someone in another state (I vote your way here, you vote mine there): feels flat-out wrong even though it could genuinly help my side win. So it can't be consequences all the way down.
- Australia-style compulsory voting: doesn't strike me as tyrannical at all, actually seems kind of reasonable, which surprised me when I noticed it.

Is there a single principle that gets all of these, or do I need some hybrid (like: a duty of civic contribution, plus consequentialist tiebreakers, plus some epistemic condition)? If a simpler principle forces me to drop one of these verdicts, tell me which one and why that one is the weak link. Also tell me what the resulting view says about cases I haven't thought about, like voting while decently informed but obviously emotionally biased, or voting in an election where the outcome I morally prefer is also the one my material interests favor. I definately have a sense that there's something expressive going on in my intuitions but I can't get it to cash out cleanly.
