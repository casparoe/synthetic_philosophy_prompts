So I'm giving a talk at our department's grad workshop in a few weeks and I need a Q&A prep sheet. My paper is a defense of total utilitarianism against the utility monster objection, but with a twist: I argue the thought experiment only feels like a reductio because of how we picture the monster. When people imagine it, they picture some guy who just really, really loves cookies -- lots of shallow, gaudy pleasure. But if you actually take the stipulation seriously and imagine a being whose inner life is so rich that each unit of resources produces vastly more genuine welfare in it than in us, then handing everything over stops seeming monstrous and starts seeming obvious.
