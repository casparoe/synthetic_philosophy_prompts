She also mentioned that the big UK government climate economics review set this one ingredient to roughly zero, following some Cambridge mathematician from the 1920s who thought weighting future people's wellbeing less purely because they live later is indefensible, while some other famous climate economist uses a much higher number and gets much weaker policy conclusions out of his models. She wouldn't unpack it further becuase we were at a restaraunt.
