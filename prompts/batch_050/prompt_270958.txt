I sit on the procurement committee for a county court that's buying a pretrial-release risk tool (predicts who gets detained before trial vs released). We've got two finalist vendors and a problem. Vendor A's tool is "calibrated": among defendants who get the same risk score, the same share actually reoffends whether they're Black or white. Vendor B's tool instead equalizes false positive rates across racial groups -- innocent Black defendants are no more likely to be flagged high-risk than innocent white ones. Our staff analyst ran the numbers and says no tool can do both at once given that the underlying reoffense rates differ between groups, so we have to pick which kind of fairness we care about. Meanwhile a community advocacy group is pushing a third option: keep vendor A's scores but set a higher detention threshold for Black defendants to even out the false positives, and one of the judges says that just reintroduces race by hand. Draft the memo I'll circulate before the vote: recommend one of the three options (or reject both vendors), with reasons, and give an honest answer to the strongest argument for whatever we don't pick. Don't worry about citing the technical literature, I'll add citations myself.
