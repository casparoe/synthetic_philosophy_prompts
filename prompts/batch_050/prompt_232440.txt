But everyone I read seems to rest their argument on one or two favourite cases and ignore the rest of the territory. Before we get to who wins, I want the spec sheet.

Only at the end, and briefly, score the main contenders against the list: the view that lying is specially wrong because it involves asserting and inviting trust, the view that deceiving is deceiving whatever vehicle you use, and any third option you think deserves a seat at the table.
