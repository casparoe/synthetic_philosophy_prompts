I play in my school’s orchestra, where our conductor often says, “A performance is not the work,” especially when explaining why a flawed performance of a Beethoven symphony is still a performance of that symphony. But then another music teacher said that a definitive studio recording can be “the work itself”. What precise claims could the first slogan be making—for example, about types and their instances, scores, performances, recordings, interpretations, or the possibility of performing a work incorrectly? Please distinguish the trivial, plausible, controversial, and plainly false readings, and say which readings would justify our conductor’s remark without implying that recordings can never themselves be musical works.
