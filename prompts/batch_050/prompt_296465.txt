Here's my opening move, deal with it: your view has a discontinuity bug. Imagine a spectrum of harms -- dust speck, slightly itchier speck, stubbed toe, paper cut, broken finger, and so on in tiny steps all the way up to 50 years of torture. Each step is barely worse than the last. If numbers can never bridge the gap, you have to draw a hard line SOMEWHERE in that smooth ramp and say "below this line, quantity counts, above it, it doesn't" -- which means a harm one micro-notch worse suddenly becomes infinitely more important.
