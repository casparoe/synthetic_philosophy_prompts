ok so im on my schools debate team and weve spent like the whole semester arguing about whether patriotism is a virtue or a vice and honestly we just talk past each other every single week. half the team says loving your country is obviously good -- it gets people to vote, serve jury duty, help their neighbors, make sacrifices nobody would make for strangers on the other side of the planet. the other half says patriotism is just nationalism with better PR, that which hospital you happened to be born in is morally random, and that the whole concept mostly exists to get teenagers to die in wars and to make people care less about foreigners.

our coach finally told us to stop and write up one of those adversarial collaboration things instead, like a joint statement both halves could actually sign. so write that document for us. specifically i want:

1. a numbered list of claims BOTH sides can agree to -- stuff about the difference (if any) between patriotism and nationalism, about the fact that special obligations to family exist and arent considered evil, about the historical track record being mixed, that kind of thing
2. a disagreements table where each sides position is worded the way that side would actually word it, not a strawman. e.g. the vice side isnt saying loving your hometown makes you a fascist, and the virtue side isnt saying my country right or wrong
3. the cruxes -- the questions where if you settled them, at least one side would have to move. im guessing some are: does the family analogy actually work or does it break down somewhere, does patriotism cause more civic good than out-group hostility (this ones empirical, so each side should write down in advance what theyd predict, like how patriots vs cosmopolitans react when their own country does something awful, or whos more likely to support disaster relief abroad), and the case of the dissident who protests their government BECAUSE they love the country -- which side does that case actually help?
4. if any part of our fight is just about words, say which part and why, because my coach definately thinks half of it is

dont resolve it in favor of either side at the end, the whole point is the residual disagreement has to be stated exactly, not papered over
