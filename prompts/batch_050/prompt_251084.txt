Apparently in his early work Frege said identity is a relation between signs, and my friend thinks he basically kept that view in Sense and Reference, just dressed up: the "sense" of a name is the private mental picture each speaker associates with it, like the image of Venus in your head, and that's all that distinguishes "Hesperus" from "Phosphorus." His conclusion was that the ancients who figured out the morning star is the evening star didn't actually discover anything astronomical — they discovered something about their own language, and the whole sense/reference apparatus is just Frege's way of saying informativeness is psychological and linguistic.
