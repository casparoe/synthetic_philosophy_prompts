Suppose David Hume showed up today and met an AI that had absorbed millions of artworks and reviews and could give confident verdicts about painting, music, and film. Given his standard of taste, would he treat the AI as an ideal critic whose testimony can justify my aesthetic judgments, or as a clever fake because it lacks sentiments of its own? Work through the features Hume thought good critics need — delicacy, practice, comparison, and freedom from prejudice — and say which ones an AI could genuinely have rather than merely simulate. Bonus: would regularly consulting the AI improve my taste, or just make me dependent on someone else’s?
