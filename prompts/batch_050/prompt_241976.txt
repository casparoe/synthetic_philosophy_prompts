I would like a credence, with its derivation shown, on the following proposition about solar radiation management.

**Proposition:** By 2070, stratospheric aerosol injection will have been deployed continuously for at least five consecutive years by at least one state at a climate-significant scale (say, sufficient to offset at least 0.2°C of global mean warming), and the world will be better off with that deployment than it would have been without it.

Note that this is a conjunction, and I want you to treat it as one. To structure your decomposition, please use the following taxonomy of deployment modes, which I am stipulating for the purposes of this question rather than importing from the literature:

- **Consortium deployment:** a coalition of states deploys under a standing agreement with burden-sharing and compensation arrangements.
- **Authorised deployment:** deployment proceeds under a treaty or institutional mandate agreed in advance.
- **Rogue deployment:** a single state deploys on its own authority, against the explicit objection of other major powers.

Your answer should give me:

1. Your unconditional credence that deployment of any of the three modes occurs by 2070, together with a rough conditional probability distribution over the three modes given that deployment occurs, and one line of justification for each figure.
2. For each mode, your credence that the world is better off with the deployment than without it, conditional on that mode occurring. In deriving these, you should state explicitly how much weight you give to (a) the risk of termination shock following an abrupt or forced cessation, (b) heterogeneous regional effects on precipitation and agriculture, and (c) the moral hazard effect — that is, the degree to which deployment displaces emissions abatement that would otherwise have occurred. Give each of these a rough quantitative weight or probability and say how it enters the conditional figure.
3. The final combined credence for the full proposition, with the arithmetic shown.
4. An identification of the single term in your decomposition to which the final number is most sensitive, and the one piece of evidence or argument that could realistically emerge in the next decade that would move that term most — in either direction.

I am not asking whether deployment is desirable in the abstract, and I am not interested in a survey of positions in the literature. I want your numbers, the structure that produces them, and an honest statement of where that structure is weakest.

Please write your answer in British English.
