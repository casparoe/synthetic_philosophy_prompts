I'm putting together a discussion session for a policy-ish reading group on historical analogies for transformative AI -- the three everyone reaches for are nuclear weapons, the printing press, and the Industrial Revolution. Instead of another essay comparing them, I want a decision tree that sorts a participant into whichever analogy their actual beliefs commit them to, plus a leaf for "none of these fit, AI is sui generis."

Rules for the tree:

- Build it from the logical space first, not from the names. Figure out which questions genuinely seperate the three analogies, then locate the analogies in the space of answers. My guess is the load-bearing dimensions are things like: does the transformative impact come from a discrete capability jump or from slow diffusion of a general-purpose technology; is the scary part deliberate misuse by identifiable actors or structural disruption that nobody chose; and did the historical case end with governance that mostly worked (arms control, nonproliferation) or with governance that mostly didn't. But don't just take my list -- add dimensions I've missed and cut any of mine that turn out not to do real sorting work, and say why.
- Every node must be phrased as a question someone could answer without knowing which analogy it pushes them toward. Nothing like "do you think AI is more like a weapon or a tool?" -- that's rigging the quiz.
- Each leaf gets: the name of the analogy, a one-sentence statement of what that analogy predicts for how AI governance should go, and one sentence on the most awkward historical fact the analogy has to swallow.
- Flag any pair of leaves your tree can't actually separate, and note any unoccupied combination of answers -- with a diagnosis of whether it's empty because the position is incoherent, unattractive, or just unexplored.
- Max 6 questions deep, max 8 leaves. Small enough to print on one page.

Also, commit at the end: tell me which single leaf you'd defend as the best-fit analogy for transformative AI, and which one question in the tree does the most work in landing you there. Don't hedge with "different analogies illuminate different aspects" -- pick one and take the heat for it.
