Write an essay on the question of whether a rational deliberator can assign subjective probabilities to the outcomes of her own ongoing deliberation. Consider the following case: Maya is deciding between two job offers and must choose by Friday. On Wednesday, a friend offers her a bet at even odds on which offer she will ultimately accept. Maya has not yet made up her mind, and she takes her deliberation to be genuinely open: she is still weighing reasons, and part of what she is doing in deliberating is deciding what she will do. Intuitively, she cannot treat her own future choice the way she would treat a coin flip or someone else's decision, since predicting herself feels like it would bypass or short-circuit the very deliberation that is supposed to settle the matter. Yet refusing to assign any probability seems to leave her unable to reason about plans that depend on her own future conduct, and it sits uneasily with the fact that outsiders (her friend, an observer with good evidence about her dispositions) can apparently assign well-founded probabilities to her choice.

Your essay should weigh the strongest considerations on both sides: on one side, the claim that deliberation requires treating one's choice as up to oneself rather than as an event to be forecast, so that assigning credences to one's own options while deliberating is epistemically or practically incoherent; on the other side, the claim that a rational agent should be able to integrate self-knowledge and base rates into her credences like any other evidence, and that an agent who cannot bet on herself is systematically exploitable or at least disadvantaged. You may consider whether the tension can be dissolved by distinguishing stages of deliberation, by restricting probabilities to choices viewed from a detached standpoint, or by tying credences to the reasons one takes oneself to have. Reach and defend a definite verdict on whether Maya may rationally take the bet and, more generally, on whether deliberation crowds out prediction. Your response must be no more than 600 words.
