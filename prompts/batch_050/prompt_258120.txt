Write a satirical news piece in the style of The Onion — headline plus roughly 500-600 words — reporting that a university sleep lab has finally "solved" dream research by adopting the view that dreams were never experiences at all. The lab's official position is a mashup of the two classic skeptical moves: following Malcolm, they hold that a waking report is the only possible criterion for having dreamt, so nothing can count as evidence that anything was experienced during the night; and following Dennett's cassette theory, they explain that what people call a dream is just a prefabricated memory loaded into consciousness at the moment of waking. The article should cover: distraught study participants being told the terrifying nightmare they vividly "lived through" never actually happened to them; a lab spokesperson patiently explaining that feeling certain you experienced something is exactly what the theory predicts; a cost-cutting announcement that the lab no longer needs to monitor REM sleep since nothing interesting is going on in there; and a lone phenomenologist who keeps insisting he was there for his dream and is gently escorted out. The jokes should come from getting the philosophy right — the verification problem, why the waking report can't be checked against anything, the weird fact that the cassette theory is unfalsifiable from the inside — so a philosopher of mind laughs instead of winces. Keep it deadpan the whole way through, no winking at the reader.
