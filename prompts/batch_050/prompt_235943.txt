Exaggerate real features of the control paradigm rather than inventing strawmen — someone who works on this stuff should read it and feel seen, not misrepresented. At the same time it should be funny to someone who's only vaguely heard of control vs. alignment, so don't lean on jargon without glossing it in-universe. About 800 words, formatted as numbered FAQ questions with answers, deadpan HR voice throughout, no winking.
