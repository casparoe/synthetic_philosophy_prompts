The course screens Dr. Strangelove, and my colleague wants to discuss it alongside the standard paradoxes of deterrence: a deterrent threat works only if it is credible, yet carrying it out after deterrence fails is disastrous and arguably irrational, so rationality seems to undermine the very credibility deterrence needs.
