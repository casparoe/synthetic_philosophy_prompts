And the result is that when she says something nice it hits DIFFERENT. When she told me my presentation was good I felt amazing because "she never compliments anyone." When it comes to stuff where honesty would actually cost her something -- like telling our mutual friend her relationship is obviously toxic -- she goes quiet or says something vaguely supportive. So its like the bluntness is... an investment? She spends honesty on cheap stuff to buy credibility for the moments when she'd rather not be honest.
