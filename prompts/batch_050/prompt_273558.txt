Okay so I just finished the Three-Body trilogy and the dark forest idea seems to me less like fiction and more like a straight-up game theory result. If I'm following it right: you can never verify another civilization's intentions, technology can leap forward so fast that a species that looks harmless today could outgun you in a couple centuries, and striking first is way cheaper and safer than waiting to find out. So the rational equilibrium is that everyone stays silent and shoots anyone who makes noise. Fine, granted.

So here's what I don't get. If the dark forest is basically a proof that revealing yourself gets you killed, why do astronomers still run SETI programs? Pointing giant antennas at the sky and scanning for signals seems like exactly the kind of activity the dark forest says is suicidal. And on top of that, why aren't goverments doing anything about all the radio and TV chatter we've been leaking into space for a hundred years? If the logic of the dark forest holds, that leakage is already a death sentence, so you'd expect at least some serious policy debate about shutting it down or jamming it, and instead nobody outside of a few sci-fi fans seems to care. Is the field just being reckless, or am I missing something about how the argument works?
