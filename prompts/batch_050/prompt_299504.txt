Here is the question I set, verbatim:

Suppose Beauty volunteers for the following experiment. On Sunday she is put to sleep. A coin that is known to be biased, landing heads with probability one-third and tails with probability two-thirds, is flipped. If it lands heads, she is awakened on Monday only. If it lands tails, she is awakened on Monday and again on Tuesday, with her memory of the Monday awakening erased in between. Each time she wakes, she knows the setup but not the day and not the coin's result. What credence should she assign, on a given awakening, to the coin having landed heads? Set out both the halfer and the thirder positions as they apply to this variant, and say which you find more defensible and why.

And here is the member's answer, verbatim:

On the halfer view, Beauty's credence in heads should simply remain her prior, one-third. Since she is guaranteed to be awakened at least once regardless of the coin, waking up tells her nothing new, and with no new evidence there is no reason to depart from the known bias of the coin. On the thirder view, by contrast, Beauty should reason that there are three possible awakening states she might occupy: Monday-with-heads, Monday-with-tails, and Tuesday-with-tails. Only one of these involves heads, so her credence in heads should be one-third. This line of reasoning, due to David Lewis, applies a principle of indifference across the awakening states, treating each as equally likely from Beauty's perspective upon waking. Adam Elga, in response, defended the halfer position, insisting that awakening is guaranteed and therefore evidentially inert. I find the thirder view more compelling, because if the experiment were repeated many times, the large majority of awakenings would occur in tails branches, and Beauty's credence ought to reflect the proportion of her awakenings that occur under heads. The frequency across awakenings is what her degree of belief is actually tracking when she cannot tell which awakening she is in. So while the halfer position is understandable given the guaranteed awakening, the thirder position gives the right answer of one-third and better respects the structure of self-locating uncertainty.
