About five minutes spoken. After the speech, give me a JSON object with three fields: "claims_cut" (an array of the tempting lines I should NOT say, each with a one-sentence reason the no-trade literature kills it), "core_move" (a string: the single argumentative move the speech rests on), and "quant_check" (a string: the sentence in the speech most likely to get pushback from the quants, with my comeback).
