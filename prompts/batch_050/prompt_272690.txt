Two instances: (1) Berkeley's famous move against mind-independent matter, sometimes called the master argument — try to conceive of a tree existing while unconceived by anyone, and you find that in conceiving of it you've conceived of it, so the supposition seems to defeat itself; (2) the cogito-style move where doubting your own existence just confirms that something is doing the doubting. The odd thing is that in the second case the move seems to genuinely establish something, while in the first it smells like a trick — it shows I can't *think* a certain thought without tripping over myself, but why should my inability to think it tell me anything about trees?
