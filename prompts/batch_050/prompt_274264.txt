Write a deadpan brochure, roughly 500-600 words, for a fictional wellness retreat called "The Freedom Suites," which offers three holiday packages modeled on negative, positive, and republican liberty. Describe each package's amenities, house rules, and testimonial from a satisfied guest. The satire should arise from taking each conception of freedom literally: the Negative Wing guarantees that no staff member will ever interact with you under any circumstances (customer complaints are answered by noting that non-interference is precisely what you purchased); the Positive Wing assigns every guest a personal trainer who has determined your true rational will and will fulfill your booking on your behalf; and the Republican Wing promises that no one could ever interfere with you, a guarantee that turns out to require an unsettling amount of surveillance, domination-auditing, and armed guards who are, of course, only potential. Include at least one moment where a guest tries to upgrade from the Republican to the Negative package and the concierge explains why republican theorists would deny that this is an improvement. A reader who knows the Berlin and Pettit distinctions should find the jokes fair rather than confused, and a reader who doesn't should still find it funny. Keep the tone straight-faced throughout, and write in American English.
