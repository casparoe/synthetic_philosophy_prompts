Audience matters: mostly philosophers of science raised on anti-Friedman arguments about false models and explanatory fictions, two econ methodologists who think the F-twist made economists lazy, one applied modeler who builds flood ABMs and rolls his eyes at philosophy, and one sociologist of modeling who suspects every calibration is legitimacy theater. Draft the nine objections I will actually get from this room, phrased in each person's idiom rather than generic realism versus antirealism, ordered by likelihood times damage, each with a 90-second reply, what I should concede versus hold, and where the reply is soft.
