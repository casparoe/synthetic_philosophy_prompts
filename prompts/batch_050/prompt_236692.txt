My teacher ended the debate by declaring, with reference to Levinas, that ethics is first philosophy, and then moved on without unpacking the slogan. I left class unsure whether I had heard a deep insight or a conversation-stopper.

Think carefully before answering, and begin by brainstorming: list every distinct, precise thesis that the slogan ethics is first philosophy could plausibly be expressing, ordered roughly from the nearly trivial to the genuinely radical. Please lay everything out as a numbered menu with verdicts that I can bring to class, rather than arguing for a single interpretation.
