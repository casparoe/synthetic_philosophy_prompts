Please explain a conceptual issue at the intersection of metaethics and practical rationality. On rationalist blogs, it is common to say that human "terminal values" cannot be argued for or against, only discovered and acted on — a claim often invoked to explain why moral persuasion feels like it runs out, and why an aligned AI might need to inherit our values rather than derive them. Bernard Williams's doctrine of internal reasons makes a structurally similar claim: an agent has a reason to act only if that action could be reached by a sound deliberative route from the agent's existing motivational set, so facts about what one ought to do cannot rationally compel someone whose motivations are sufficiently alien.

My question: is the rationalist claim about terminal values simply a folk version of Williams's internalism, or are the two claims doing different work? In particular, please address the following points of tension that I find genuinely confusing. First, Williams's internalism is meant to be modest in one direction (deliberation can extend the motivational set through imagination, correction of false beliefs, and attention to consequences), whereas the terminal-values framing often seems to treat values as fixed primitives that deliberation can only serve, not reshape — is this a real disagreement about the scope of deliberation, or a difference of emphasis? Second, Williams himself suggested that if internalism is true, the practice of blaming agents who lack the relevant internal reasons becomes conceptually shaky; does the rationalist commitment to treating unaligned minds as *dangerous rather than wrong* inherit this consequence, and is that consequence a mark in favor of the view or a hidden cost? Third, externalists typically argue that internalism cannot explain the apparent categorical force of moral demands; if the terminal-values picture concedes this point by design, what exactly is lost, and what — if anything — does the picture gain that a more thoroughly externalist account of moral reasoning could not provide?
