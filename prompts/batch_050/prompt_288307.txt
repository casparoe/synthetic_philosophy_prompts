I sit on the board of a worker-owned courier co-op (about 80 rider-members; we deliver for restaurants and two hospitals). We're drowning in orders, and the growth commitee wants to start hiring couriers as straight employees: hourly pay a bit above the local gig rate, no vote, no share of the year-end surplus, with the option to apply for full membership after two years. The board is split. Dana says we'd be rebuilding the capital relation inside our own co-op, living off surplus we didn't work for. Ravi says you can't exploit people who freely signed a contract at above-market pay with an open door to membership, and if we can't hire we'll stagnate and loose the hospital contracts.

At the last meeting Dana read out this passage from Chapter 6 of Capital Vol. 1 (Moore/Aveling translation) and it kind of stopped the room:

"For the conversion of his money into capital, therefore, the owner of money must meet in the market with the free labourer, free in the double sense, that as a free man he can dispose of his labour-power as his own commodity, and that on the other hand he has no other commodity for sale, is short of everything necessary for the realisation of his labour-power."

Draft the memo I'll circulate before Thursday's vote. It needs a bottom line: hire as proposed, don't hire, or hire on modified terms (spell out the terms and the timeline). Whatever you recommend, the memo has to actually work with the passage rather than just gesture at it. Specifically: what does Marx's "double sense" of free imply about whether Ravi's consent-and-decent-pay argument settles the question? One wrinkle that came up in discussion: our riders, employees included, use their own bikes and phones, so in what sense are they "short of everything necessary" — is the relevant stuff the dispatch software and the client contracts, which the co-op owns? And does it change anything that the "owner of money" here is the workforce itself, with membership genuinely open? Ravi pointed out that in Volume 3 Marx treats cooperative factories as a real overcoming of the capital-labour antithesis that still reproduces the old system's defects, so say whether that cuts for or against your recommendation. Give Ravi's side its strongest form, not a strawman. Around 600 words (the quote doesn't count toward that), American English please.
