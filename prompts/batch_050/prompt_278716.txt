Landauer and Bennett made this precise: every acquisition of one bit of information dissipates at least kT ln 2 of heat into the surroundings. This is why physicists now say that information is physical: bits are not abstractions but have energy and mass like anything else, and the second law emerges undefeated once the demon's informational overhead is charged to its account.
