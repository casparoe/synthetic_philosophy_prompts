I am drafting the commitments section of an essay on the philosophy of time, and before I send it to my advisor I want an outside consistency audit. My essay takes its starting point from Book XI of Augustine's Confessions, where Augustine concludes that time is measured in the mind: we cannot measure future time, since it is not yet; nor past time, since it is no longer; nor present time, since it has no extent; so what we actually measure is the impression that passing things leave in the mind, which remains after the things themselves are gone. Here is the position I have built on that foundation:

1. Presentism: only what is present exists. There are no past or future objects or events; the dinosaurs and the heat death of the universe are not located anywhere in reality.
2. The present instant is strictly unextended — a durationless boundary between the nonexistent past and the nonexistent future.
3. Augustine's relocation move: when we measure a duration, what we measure is something present in the mind — the trace or impression that passing events leave behind — not the elapsed time itself, since there is no elapsed time available to be measured.
4. Duration facts are objective and sometimes known. A claim such as "the second syllable lasted twice as long as the first" is, when things go well, strictly true of how the world was, and not merely a report of how it seemed to the measurer.
5. One unified account of measurement: minds and clocks measure time in the same sense. A clock's reading differs from a remembered interval only in reliability, not in kind.
6. Memory puts us in cognitive contact with the past itself, not merely with present traces of it.

My question is simply whether this set hangs together. I am not asking for an exegesis of Augustine or a survey of the philosophy of time; I want to know which of my own commitments pull against one another, which pairs only appear to conflict, and what the cheapest repair is. Please audit every pair that plausibly bears on consistency rather than flagging the whole list, order the genuine tensions by severity, and do not assume I have already decided which commitment I would abandon.
