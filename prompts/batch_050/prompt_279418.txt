So: read Westworld through the lens of reward signals as valence. Is the show conflating *having* pain with *remembering* pain? Does it get anything right about why a reward signal might or might not amount to suffering, or is the whole thing built on the confusion? I want to take what's philosophically defensible and leave the confused parts behind. And be honest about where the show just resists the reading, beacuse I don't want to fool myself about a show I love.
