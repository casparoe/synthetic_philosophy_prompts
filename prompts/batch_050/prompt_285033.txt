I write a science-and-ideas column, and I'm prepping a profile of an epistemologist I interviewed last month. The whole conversation circled around a line she loves from Aristotle's Posterior Analytics (Book I) -- that "the premisses of demonstrated knowledge must be true, primary, immediate, better known than and prior to the conclusion." She said this sentence caused two thousand years of trouble, then refused to just tell me her view, so I've been trying to reverse-engineer it from the little verdicts she gave me over coffee:

- Believes the train leaves at 6 because the departure board says so; asked why she trusts the board, she says it seems reliable and that's where she stops digging. Verdict: perfectly justified, chains are allowed to just stop.
- Believes P because of Q, and Q because of P. Verdict: not justified, she actually laughed.
- A physicist whose million-odd beliefs all lean on each other in a giant web, loops everywhere, derivations criss-crossing, no single bedrock proposition underneath it all. Verdict: justified, and she stressed the size and density of the web mattered.
- A four-step chain that bottoms out in a claim the guy holds "for no reason at all, it popped into my head and I dug in." Verdict: not justified.

Here's the new case I want to put to her, and I'd like your prediction first. One of the AI labs I cover claims its system never answers from a bedrock belief: every answer is supported by a further, different consideration, which is supported by another, and so on without end and without ever repeating itself -- an infinite, non-circular chain where every single inference is clean. No foundation, no loop, no stopping point. Would she call the system's beliefs justified?

Give me your prediction, but more importantly: tell me which general rules are consistent with her four verdicts, what each rule says about the AI case, and which rule you think she's actually running and why. And if her record genuinly underdetermines it, what's the one extra case I should ask her about in the follow-up interview that would settle it?
