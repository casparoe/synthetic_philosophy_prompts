Okay so I've been going around in circles on what it even means to intend something, and I want to pin down my actual view by giving you my gut reactions to some cases. Tell me what principle, if any, ties them together.

Case 1: An eccentric billionaire offers me $2 million if a brain scanner confirms that at midnight tonight I sincerely intend to drink a vial of his vile but harmless-ish toxin (two days of stomach misery, nothing worse) tomorrow at noon. Money transfers at 12:01 tonight, and drinking the vial is NOT part of the deal — I can pour it down the sink tomorrow morning and keep every cent. My verdict: I'd sign up, but I don't think I can actually pull it off, because the moment I "decide" to intend, I already know tomorrow-me will pour it out, so whatever happens at midnight isn't really intending, it's performing an intention for the scanner.

Case 2: My New Year's resolution to stop eating sugar. Given my track record I'd give it maybe 30% odds. Still feels like a genuine intention, not a performance. But wait — how is that different from case 1??

Case 3: My friend invites me to her potluck, I say I'll try to make it, knowing there's like a 10% chance I show. If I told my wife "I intend to go to the potluck" that would feel like a straight-up lie. Whereas case 2 doesn't feel like lying to myself. No idea why.

Case 4: A chess player about to face Magnus Carlsen. She intends to win. She knows she's going to lose. That strikes me as a perfectly real intention, which seems to contradict my verdict in case 1.

Case 5: Deciding to parallel park in a spot I'll probally fail to fit into — intending to *try* feels totally available even when intending to succeed isn't. But in the toxin case, "intending to try to intend" wouldn't fool the scanner.

So: is there one principle about the relation between intending something and believing you'll do it that captures all five verdicts, or do I have to drop one (case 2, let's be honest)? And what does your principle say about the case I keep chewing on: if I hire a hypnotist tonight to make tomorrow-me actually drink the vial, does midnight-me now genuinely intend, since I finally expect tomorrow-me to drink?
