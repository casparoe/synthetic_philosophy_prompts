Wittgenstein's private language argument, as I understand it, basically shows that talk about inner sensations can't really mean anything, since nobody but me could ever check wether I'm using a word like "pain" correctly. If that's right, what should we actually do about pain measurement in medicine? Should doctors stop asking patients to rate their pain on a 1-10 scale and just rely on behavioral cues, brain scans, and other stuff that's at least publicly verifiable? Or is there some way to salvage self-reports despite Wittgenstein's point?
