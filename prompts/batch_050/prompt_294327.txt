5. The argument doesn't require me to know *which* machine I would be, if I were one. The dialectic is: you propose a candidate machine that supposedly captures my reasoning, I Gödelise it, done. It's your move to make, not mine.

So: which of these actually pull against each other, and which only look like they do?
