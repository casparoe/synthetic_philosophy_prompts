Here's what I've written down on induction. (5) Confirmation is a purely logical relation between evidence statements and hypotheses, roughly along the lines of Hempel's satisfaction criterion — whether some evidence confirms a hypothesis shouldn't depend on anything about us, our history, or what language we happen to speak.
