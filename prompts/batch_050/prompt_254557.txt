I'm a PhD student in philosophy, working primarily on the epistemology of modality, and I'm preparing a position statement for my dissertation prospectus defense. My committee has a reputation for finding the exact joint of inconsistency in a view, so before I circulate it I want an audit of whether my commitments hang together. Here is what I currently endorse:

1. Metaphysical possibility is the notion that matters for the modality of the natural world, and it strictly outruns physical possibility: there are metaphysically possible situations that violate our actual laws of nature.

2. Ideal, positive conceivability is a sufficient condition for metaphysical possibility: if a scenario could be conceived in full detail and would survive undefeated under ideal rational reflection, then it is genuinely possible.

3. Conceivability is also my working method: when I actually need to find out whether some scenario is possible, my procedure is to try to conceive of it, and I take successful conceiving as evidence of possibility in the way I take perception as evidence of fact.

4. There are necessary a posteriori truths: standard cases like the identity of water with H2O and the identity of Hesperus with Phosphorus are, if true, necessarily true, but were discoverable only empirically.

5. Mathematical truths are necessary if true at all. So for any unproven conjecture -- say Goldbach's conjecture -- exactly one of the conjecture or its negation is necessarily true, and the other is necessarily false.

6. Right now, I find both outcomes conceivable: I can conceive of a proof of Goldbach's conjecture being announced and verified, and I can equally well conceive of a verified counterexample being exhibited. Both feel like fully coherent scenarios to me.

Please examine this set and tell me which pairs or triples of commitments are in genuine tension and which tensions are merely apparent. For each genuine tension, say how severe it is, identify which commitments would have to be revised or abandoned to resolve it, and state what each available fix would cost me in terms of the overall position. For each merely apparent tension, show why it dissolves on closer inspection. Finally, if there is a single revision that would restore the most coherence at the least cost, identify it and defend that verdict against the alternatives.
