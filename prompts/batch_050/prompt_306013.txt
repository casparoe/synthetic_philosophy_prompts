what i want is an actual procedure i can run on each request in like twenty minutes, before i reply to the email. a checklist or flowchart in words, the tests in order: whose consent matters and what counts as evidence of it, how close the requester has to be to the deceased, what i do about requests that could hurt other living people who knew the person (the ex-wife case -- her family hates this guy), how the celebrity case is different from the private-person case or whether it is. the procedure should be built out of the actual ethical considerations people raise about simulating the dead, not just my vibe, and it should say at each step which principle is doing the work.

run it on those three real cases from above so i can see it work, and include at least one case where the procedure says no to a request id really want to take (the money and the heartfelt emails are both real). if the procedure ends up basically saying "take everything, grief helps," or "take nothing, the dead cant consent," say so plainly, but id be suspiscious of either.
