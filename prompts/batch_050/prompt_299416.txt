I am setting the research agenda for a two-year project with three philosophers and one computational collaborator, all working on accuracy-first epistemology. Which open problems should we take on, and in what order? The candidates I can currently see are: the characterisation problem — saying which accuracy measures are legitimate, given that different strictly proper measures appear to vindicate different norms; whether accuracy arguments can be extended from synchronic coherence requirements to diachronic norms such as conditionalisation, given that the existing arguments seem to grade plans for updating rather than the updates themselves; whether the framework can accommodate imprecise or comparative credences without its central arguments collapsing; and the monism question — whether epistemic value really is accuracy alone, or whether admitting further epistemic goods would change what the accuracy measures should be taken to measure. Feel free to substitute better candidates if you think I have misidentified the live issues. For each problem, tell me which other questions its resolution would settle, whether it is tractable with current formal tools or genuinely needs the computational collaborator, and how neglected it is relative to its importance. Close with an explicit ordering of the problems and a statement of what result in the first six months would most change that ordering. I want a defended agenda, not a survey of the field.
