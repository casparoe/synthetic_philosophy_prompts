I write a column on science and ideas, and I'm preparing a piece on the precautionary principle's track record. Please analyze the British BSE crisis of the 1980s and 1990s as a test case for it. The broad facts I take to be common knowledge: a novel cattle disease emerged in the mid-1980s, traced to feed containing rendered ruminant material; the UK banned ruminant protein in cattle feed in 1988 and removed certain bovine tissues from the human food chain in 1989; through the early 1990s officials publicly assured consumers that beef was safe; and in 1996 the government acknowledged a link between BSE and a new human prion disease. What I want is not the timeline but the analysis. First, locate the decision points at which a precautionary principle, stated precisely (you may distinguish weak and strong formulations), would have bitten: what did it demand in 1986-88, when cross-species transmission to humans was speculative, and what did it demand after 1988, when the known risk was residual and the costs of further measures were concrete? Second, do the counterfactual honestly: the feed ban and offal bans were themselves precautionary acts, yet the crisis still reached humans -- does that make BSE a vindication of precaution (it was applied too late and too narrowly), a refutation (even precaution could not prevent the harm), or a case the principle is silent on because it cannot say how much precaution is enough? Third, address the asymmetry objection: regulators face both the error of acting on a phantom risk and the error of waiting for certainty, and critics argue the principle only ever counts the first; say whether BSE shows the critics wrong, or whether it instead shows that the principle needs a theory of acceptable evidence to be action-guiding at all. Then give your verdict on what this episode does to the principle's standing. Finally, tell me what would change that verdict: which historical or conceptual consideration, if it turned out differently, would most weaken your analysis, and where your reasoning is most exposed to error.
