For a philosophy of science seminar I have been asked to circulate a short statement of my own commitments on Lakatos's methodology of scientific research programmes, so that the group can probe it for weaknesses. Here is what I currently hold:

(1) Lakatos's methodology is a genuine improvement on Popper's falsificationism, because it explains why scientists were rational to retain Newtonian mechanics in the face of recalcitrant anomalies: the fate of a programme is not settled by a single failed prediction.

(2) Once a rival programme has been shown to be more progressive, continued investment of research effort in the degenerating programme is straightforwardly irrational.

(3) The hard core of a programme is rendered irrefutable by methodological decision: the negative heuristic forbids directing the arrow of modus tollens at the core, and this stipulation stands in need of no further justification.

(4) A prediction carries special confirmatory weight only when it is novel, where I take novel to mean that the fact predicted was unknown at the time the theory was formulated — the bending of starlight rather than the anomalous precession of Mercury's perihelion, which had been on the books for decades.

(5) Confirmation is an objective relation between a theory and a body of evidence; whether evidence supports a theory cannot depend on historical accidents concerning when the evidence was discovered or who happened to know of it first.

What I would like from you is an audit rather than a general lecture on Lakatos. Which of these commitments genuinely sit badly with one another, and which only appear to conflict while actually being compatible? Please order the genuine tensions by severity rather than treating them as equally serious, indicate for each one what would have to be given up or weakened to resolve it, and say which single revision would do the most to make the overall position coherent. For each commitment that survives the audit, I would also appreciate a note on whether it is a view Lakatos himself held or a strengthening of his position, since I want to know where I am following him and where I am going beyond him.
