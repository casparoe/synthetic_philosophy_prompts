ok so, background first since it matters here: i spent 40 years as a field biologist (corvids mostly, some cephalopod work late in my career, which is funny because those are exactly the two groups everyone now wants to call smart) and since retiring ive been reading philosophy of mind on my own. I keep running into Morgan's canon, the old rule that you shouldnt explain an animal's behavior by appeal to some sophisticated cognitive capacity when a simpler psychological mechanism would do, and I keep finding people arguing about whether its a sound piece of methodology or a confused bit of Victorian austerity dressed up as rigor.

Before I read any more, what I actually want is the spec: what would an adequate principle for attributing mental states to animals have to deliver? Give me the desiderata, with the case or consideration behind each one. I can guess at a few and id like them checked: (a) it has to say something sensible about cases like Clever Hans, where the boring explanation turned out right; (b) it presumably has to handle the mirror self-recognition results and the ape signing debates, where people seem to talk straight past each other about what the behavior even demonstrates; (c) it cant just collapse into always prefer the dull explanation, because that rule applied consistently would have ruled out plenty of things in my own field that turned out to be true. What else belongs on the list? Distinguish the requirements that any theory must meet from the ones that are really just one camp's taste, and name the camps.

The part i most care about: if some subset of the desiderata cant be jointly satisfied, say so explicitly and show why. From where i sit, strict parsimony and taking evolutionary continuity seriously look like they pull apart, since continuity says the gap between us and animals is one of degree while parsimony keeps wanting to redraw it as one of kind, and yet everyone seems to want both. If that pair really is incompatible thats a result id want stated plainly, not smoothed over.

If it helps you can briefly score the canon itself and its modern descendants against the list (the deflationary readings of the mirror test, the associative-learning-first approaches, whatever the main contenders are), but keep the weight on the requirements and their motivating cases. a table for the scoring is fine.
