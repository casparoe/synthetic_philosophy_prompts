I've never managed to settle for myself whether eating meat is morally permissible—the arguments on both sides always seem to have answers I can't evaluate. A close friend, whose judgment I trust and who has thought about the issue for years, is firmly convinced it's wrong, and in every discussion she's anticipated and answered my objections better than I could have. So I've stopped eating meat essentially on her say-so, though I couldn't reconstruct her full reasoning myself if pressed. My brother thinks something is off about this: he says that if I can't give the reasons myself, my abstention doesn't count for much, and I certainly shouldn't encourage others to abstain. Is he right? And is relying on her judgment here any different from relying on my doctor's word when I can't follow the medical reasoning, or should moral questions be treated differently?
