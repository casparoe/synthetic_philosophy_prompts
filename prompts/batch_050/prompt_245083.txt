I have one 45-minute session to teach them why spectrum arguments put pressure on the transitivity of 'better than' — the idea that if A is better than B, and B is better than C, then A must be better than C. My goal is not to convince them that transitivity fails; it is to get them to see, with precision, why someone might deny it and what denying or keeping it costs.

Please plan how I should teach this; do not simply explain spectrum arguments to me. In particular:
