I currently hold that AI safety via debate scales to superhuman systems: even when both debaters are far more capable than the human judge, recursive cross-examination keeps the protocol truth-seeking, because any deceptive argument must somewhere rest on a step the judge can directly check, and letting the judge drill into contested sub-claims exposes that step. I know the standard objection -- the obfuscated-argument problem, in which a dishonest debater constructs an argument whose fatal flaw only becomes visible at a depth no judge will actually reach -- but I take recursion plus cross-examination to defuse it, since the honest debater need only win at the shallowest point where the two arguments diverge.
