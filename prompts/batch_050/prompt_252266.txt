For a paper on comparative cognition, write the desiderata for an adequate method of attributing cognition to animals under the shadow of Morgan’s canon: do not treat the canon as a slogan, but specify what a theory must deliver before it may credit an animal with inference, representation, planning, or awareness rather than association, instinct, or cue-following. Include the cases it must handle cleanly, such as Clever Hans-style cueing, scrub-jay caching and pilfering, bee route learning, octopus manipulation of objects, and mirror or mark tests, and the distinctions it cannot collapse, especially flexible behavior versus fixed behavioral rules, learning from evidence versus merely correlating stimuli, sensitivity to another’s seeing versus mindreading, and sentience versus higher cognition. For each desideratum give the motivating consideration, mark which are non-negotiable and which are camp-specific demands such as evolutionary continuity, neural plausibility, explanatory parsimony, ecological validity, or resistance to anthropomorphism, and say whether any attractive subset is jointly unsatisfiable because parsimony has no common currency across cognition, perception, motivation, and motor control. End with a brief scorecard of deflationary associationism, rich representationalism, and a layered account that permits low-grade cognition without human-style concepts, and identify which single requirement excludes the most contenders.
