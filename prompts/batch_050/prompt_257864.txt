So I'm a software engineer, no philosophy training, and I got into an argument with a friend about how beliefs get "justified" and I want to figure out what I actually think. Here are my gut verdicts on cases, and I'd like to know what principle (if any) unifies them.

- I look at the monitor and believe the build failed. I don't need any further argument for that, it's just justified on its own. Feels fine.
- I believe the bug is in the parser because a stack trace says so, and I believe the stack trace because I trust the logging library, and I trust the library because it's been reliable for years... at some point the chain just has to *end* somewhere, and that's fine.
- Tiny circles are obviously bad: "the spec is right because the code matches it, and the code is right because it matches the spec." That justifies nothing.
- But here's the thing: a big enough web of mutual support feels totally fine to me. My belief that the system works rests on the tests, the tests rest on the spec, the spec rests on user reports, the user reports partly rest on... the system behaving as expected in other areas. Nobody would call that irrational. But I can't articulate how it differs from the tiny circle.
- An infinitely long chain of reasons, each justified by the next, seems like a non-starter. I'm a finite being, I'd never get through it, and anyway it feels like kicking the can down the road forever. Justified by what, ultimately?
- Though now that I say that... a chain where each step gives you a bit less than the previous one, converging like 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8..., doesn't feel *obviously* absurd to me the way the infinite chain did a second ago. I go back and forth on this one.

Is there one coherent principle that captures verdicts 1-5? If the only way to get them all is some ugly patchwork, tell me which verdict I should give up. And what does your answer say about a case I didn't list: believing a colleague's claim because a second colleague vouched for them, where the second colleague's trustworthiness is something I only believe because the first colleague told me so?
