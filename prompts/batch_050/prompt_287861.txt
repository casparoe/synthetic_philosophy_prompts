As a retired biologist newly interested in philosophy of mind, I am preparing a discussion of 2001: A Space Odyssey using Daniel Dennett’s distinction among Darwinian, Skinnerian, Popperian, and Gregorian creatures. How should we classify HAL and Bowman at key points in the film, especially given that HAL was designed rather than naturally evolved? Please examine whether HAL’s language use, planning, and fatal concealment make him genuinely Gregorian, whether Bowman’s encounter with the monolith represents a transition between kinds of mind, and where the film’s largely nonverbal ending resists this classification.
