Draft their joint statement: the claims both camps can sign (about what insulation consists in, what it does to testimony and trust, and what the everyday evidence establishes); the precise points of disagreement, each phrased so its own side would accept the wording; and the cruxes, which I suspect include whether exposure to opposing views typically moderates or polarizes, and whether an escapee's testimony better resembles having missed information or having lost trust in everyone who could have supplied it. For each crux, specify a concrete finding, case, or intervention both camps agree would bear on it, and record what each side predicts. Format it as a numbered agreed-claims section followed by a disagreements-and-cruxes table.
