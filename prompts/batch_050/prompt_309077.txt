I have been circling an argument with a colleague and we keep stalling on the same slogan, so I want it taken apart properly. I assigned my students a popular article that leans heavily on the claim that DNA is a code — the author says the genome is a code in the same literal sense that Morse code is a code, and that this is what makes heredity a matter of information rather than mere chemistry. My colleague, who works on development, says the phrase is at best a loose metaphor and at worst an active distortion: nothing, she says, is doing any encoding or decoding in the cell, and calling DNA a code smuggles in a designer or at least a privileging of genes that the biology does not support.

What I want is a menu, not a verdict on the slogan as a whole. Spell out the distinct precise claims that 'DNA is a code' might be making. I can see candidates myself: there is presumably a weak reading on which a code is just a systematic mapping between two alphabets, and the triplet-to-amino-acid correspondence qualifies by that standard alone; a stronger reading on which a code requires the mapping to be arbitrary in some specific sense, fixed by an adaptor rather than by chemistry, so that the same mapping could in principle have been otherwise; a still stronger reading on which the coded item carries instructions for building an organism, the way a blueprint specifies a building; and maybe a Shannon-style reading on which the genome is just a channel through which information about something flows. There are probably others I have not thought of.

For each reading you lay out: state it precisely enough that it could be true or false, say whether it is a platitude, a substantive and defensible thesis, or actually false given what we know about molecular biology and development, and note what would have to be the case for it to hold. Then tell me which reading my article author needs for her claim about heredity being informational to go through, which reading my colleague is most charitably attacking, and whether the dispute dissolves once the readings are separated or whether there is a real disagreement left over once everyone has said exactly what they mean.
