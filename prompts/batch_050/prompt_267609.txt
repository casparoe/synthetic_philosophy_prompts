I'm preparing a short statement of my working position in the philosophy of science for an interdisciplinary workshop on complexity, and before I circulate it I'd like an audit of whether the pieces actually fit together. My commitments are these:

1. Everything supervenes on the physical: once all the physical facts about the world are fixed, every other fact -- biological, psychological, economic -- is fixed along with them. There is nothing over and above the physical base.

2. The special sciences are explanatorily autonomous. Because a single higher-level kind (a gene, a memory, a market crash) can be physically realized in indefinitely many different ways, physics-level descriptions cannot in principle recover the generalizations of biology or psychology, so reduction of those sciences to physics is a dead end.

3. Higher-level properties do real causal work. Natural selection genuinely explains because traits like camouflage cause differences in survival; a person's belief genuinely causes her behavior. Any view on which these properties turn out to be causally idle I would count as a reductio, not a conclusion to accept.

4. Every physical event that has a cause has a sufficient physical cause, and physical effects are not systematically overdetermined by two independent sufficient causes.

5. Strong emergence is a coherent and possibly actual phenomenon: there could be, and perhaps are, properties that arise from a physical base but are not fixed by it, and that exert causal influence back down onto the base.

Here is what I'd like from you. Identify which pairs or triples of these commitments are in genuine tension, and which merely appear to be. Order the genuine tensions from most to least severe. For each one, state precisely what would have to be given up to restore consistency and what that concession costs. Note that (1) and (2) strike some of my colleagues as incompatible; please either vindicate that worry or explain why it dissolves. Finally, if you had to change exactly one commitment to make the package coherent, say which one and why. Keep the whole response under 600 words.
