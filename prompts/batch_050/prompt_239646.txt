I'm a high school student working on a civics project: my group is drafting a code of conduct for student government elections, and we've stalled on the same question every code of conduct stalls on — where does legitimate persuasion end and manipulation begin? Rather than ask for yet another definition, I want the specification first: the full list of desiderata a theory of this boundary would have to satisfy before I'd trust it to settle any actual case. Please lead with your bottom line in the first sentence, then give the reasoning — no preamble, no restating my question.

Here are the pressures I already know the theory is under, which should shape the list. It can't condemn every appeal to emotion, or all rhetoric becomes manipulation and the distinction does no work. It can't equate manipulation with deception, since some of the clearest cases — guilt-tripping, peer pressure, strategic timing of true information — involve no lies at all. It has to handle influence the target never notices, like an ad that works without being attended to. It has to explain what's wrong with manipulation even when the outcome is good for the target, as when a parent or doctor nudges someone toward the right choice. It has to say something about consent and about how easily the influence can be resisted, without making resistance-ability the whole story, since that seems to blame the victim. And it has to avoid implying that all social influence is wrong, given that we influence each other constantly just by existing.

For each desideratum, give the case or consideration that motivates it, and say whether you think it's genuinely non-negotiable or a prejudice of one camp that a good theory could defensibly drop. If some subset of my list (or your revised list) turns out to be jointly unsatisfiable, say so — I'd count that as a real finding, not a failure. Finally, identify the single requirement that eliminates the most popular theories, and briefly say which broad families of accounts (those defining manipulation as bypassing reason, as applying pressure, as trickery, or as having bad motives) survive it. Please also state how confident you are in your overall specification and single out the one desideratum you're least sure belongs on the list.
