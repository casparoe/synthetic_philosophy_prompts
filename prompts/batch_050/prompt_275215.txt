Please argue that "speciesism" is a misdiagnosis of its own paradigm cases. The charge, as Singer popularized it, models our treatment of animals on racism: an arbitrary trait (species membership) doing the moral work. I want you to argue that this gets the mechanism wrong -- that what actually drives the treatment of pigs vs dogs vs chimps isn't species membership at all but perceived psychological similarity and social proximity to us, with species just a rough proxy -- and that the misdiagnosis isn't harmless, because it pushes animal ethics toward consistency arguments (extend the circle, avoid the double standard) when the real problem is something more like a failure of attention or sympathy that consistency arguments are badly suited to fix. Make the strongest version of this case you can, and say where you think it's weakest.
