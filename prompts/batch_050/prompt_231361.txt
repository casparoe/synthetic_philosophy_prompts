Please describe the most important implications of this passage for whether genuine moral dilemmas are possible. Should the tool represent these cases as inconsistent requirements to be optimized away, or as a first-class state that can leave moral residue even after the best response? Distinguish genuine dilemmas from mere uncertainty about weights or consequences, don't turn James into a relativist without argument, and say what this would change concretely in the schema, logging, and escalation behavior.
