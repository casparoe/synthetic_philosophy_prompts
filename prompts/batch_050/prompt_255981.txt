Construct a thought experiment in which the standard pairing between anthropic assumptions and Boltzmann brain verdicts is inverted. Background, in the usual LessWrong-adjacent vocabulary: under the Self-Indication Assumption (SIA), a world is weighted by the absolute number of observers instantiating your evidence, so SIA is famously friendly to Boltzmann-brain-dominated cosmologies; under the Self-Sampling Assumption (SSA), you reason as if randomly sampled from a reference class within a world, so SSA is normally the conservative anchor that lets you dismiss the hypothesis that you are a fluctuation. I want a scenario where this flips: SSA assigns you a substantially higher probability of being a Boltzmann brain than SIA does, conditional on the same total evidence.

Constraints:

1. Two candidate cosmologies only, given equal priors. Both must be thermodynamically respectable in the loose sense that Boltzmann brains actually arise from fluctuations rather than being stipulated into existence by fiat, though you may adjust the cosmological details (duration of a late-time fluctuation era, reheating behavior, typical fluctuation products) to make the numbers come out.

2. Your evidence E should be a rich, coherent stream of ordinary experience, and you may not win by arguing that Boltzmann brains cannot instantiate E. Both cosmologies must contain some fluctuation-produced brains whose experience matches E. The inversion has to come from the structure of the case — the distribution of experiences across observers in each world — not from an epistemic veto on BB evidence and not from skewed priors.

3. No exotic decision theory, no simulations, no infinite-observer paradoxes requiring new mathematics. Finite numbers, ordinary conditioning.

After the scenario, do the work: state E and the reference class explicitly; compute (or at least set up with explicit numbers) each theory's posterior over cosmologies and its posterior probability that you are a Boltzmann brain; identify which single structural feature of the case does the work; show that removing that feature restores the standard SIA-favors-BB, SSA-resists-BB pairing; and anticipate the strongest objection a defender of the standard pairing would raise — for instance, that the reference class has been gerrymandered, or that conditioning on the coherence of E should swamp the anthropic reasoning regardless of assumption — and say whether your case survives it.
