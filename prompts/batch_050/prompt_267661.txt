So I'm putting together a handout for a grad seminar on self-locating belief, and I want to end the Sleeping Beauty unit by asking them to push past the standard menu. Here's what I have as the standard menu, tell me if I've mis-stated anything:

For the thirder:
1. Elga's original argument -- restricted indifference: on Monday, heads-Monday and tails-Monday should get equal credence, and tails-Monday and tails-Tuesday should too, so 1/3 each.
2. The long-run / repeated-trials argument: if the experiment is run many times, about a third of Beauty's awakenings are heads-awakenings, and her credences should track that frequency.
3. The betting argument: at each awakening, Beauty should accept bets on heads only at odds worse than 2:1, which corresponds to credence 1/3 (Hitchcock's reply that both sides can be Dutch-booked depending on the setup is a known complication, set it aside).
4. The extreme version: if tails means a million awakenings with memory wipes, it's obviously not 50/50 that it's heads when she wakes, and the single-Tuesday case shouldn't be different in kind.

For the halfer:
5. Lewis's argument: Beauty gets no new uncentered evidence on waking -- she knew all along she'd be woken -- so her credence in heads should stay at the 1/2 she had on Sunday.
6. The objection that thirding violates conditionalization or reflection: her Sunday self knew everything her Monday self knows (in the relevant sense), so the shift from 1/2 to 1/3 looks like irrational belief revision without evidence.
7. The double-halfer position: keep 1/2 on heads but also deny that Monday-given-heads and the tails awakenings pattern the way thirders need (or treat Monday and Tuesday as subjectively indistinguishable states that can't be separately assigned credences).

And cutting across: the appeal to anthropic principles (SIA favors thirding-ish answers, SSA favors halfing-ish ones) and the thought that whatever you say here has to cohere with what you say about the Presumptuous Philosopher and Doomsday-style cases.

Now the actual ask: give me one more consideration, for either side or for some fourth position, that isn't just a restatement, special case, or combination of the above. It should have a different structure -- e.g., not another betting argument, not another frequency argument, not another "she learned nothing new" move. Ideally it's something that would make a committed thirder or halfer at least pause. Also say briefly which item on the list it comes closest to and why it doesn't collapse into it, and give me your honest take on whether it actually works or whether it smuggles in one of the listed arguments in disguise -- if the latter, say which one and where the smuggling happens.
