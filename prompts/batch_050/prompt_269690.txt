Nobody at the table could get past him, because every example we offered of a genuine difference he dismissed as branding. I've been turning this over since, and I suspect he is gesturing at something true while also getting away with too much. For each reading, say whether it is trivially true, substantive and defensible, or probably false, and note what evidence or argument would bear on it.
