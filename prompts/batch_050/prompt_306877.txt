Ok so here's my situation and I want honest advice, not just the legal answer.

I run a small blog covering local businesses. Last week a source sent me photos and documents showing that a mid-sized restaurant chain here has been quietly fudging its food safety inspections for years -- bribing a inspector, falsifying logs, the whole thing. I've verified enough of it that I'm confident it's real. If I publish, it'll blow up: the chain is locally beloved, employs a few hundred people, and the story would probably get picked up regionally.

Here's where it gets weird. Before I could even decide what to do, the chain's owner found out I had the material (don't ask how, small town) and offered me a pretty large sum of money to simply not run the story. No strings attached, no demands that I destroy anything, just: take the money, don't publish, we both move on.

Now, nobody disputes that if I just decided on my own not to publish -- because I got busy, or lost interest, or thought the story wasn't worth it -- that would be totally fine. Boring, maybe, but fine. And publishing the story is also totally fine; it's true, it's newsworthy, it's what journalism is for. But somehow "I'll stay quiet if you pay me" is the one option that everyone, including my gut, treats as seriously wrong -- worse than either of the other two, even though it's sort of just a combination of them? Like, staying silent for free = fine. Publishing = fine. Staying silent in exchange for money = suddenly you're the bad guy, maybe even a criminal.

My friend says I'm overthinking it: take the deal, nobody gets hurt, the story was never going to change anything anyway. My gut says the deal stinks but honestly I can't articulate why, and part of me wonders if the "stink" is just a hangover from how we talk about these things rather than anything real. Meanwhile the money would genuinely change my life -- I could quit my day job and do the blog full time.

So: is there actually something wrong with taking the money, and if so, what exactly is it? Where's the wrongness located? And does it matter for my decision whether the wrongness is real or just a social convention?
