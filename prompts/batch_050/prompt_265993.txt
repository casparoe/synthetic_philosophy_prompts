My thesis: the standard framing of the Mozi-Confucius dispute as "impartial care vs family-first partiality" mislocates the disagreement. I argue the real fight is over the source and stability of moral motivation -- whether jian ai can be willed into being through arguments about benefit and harm, versus ren growing out of natural affections that then get extended outward. And I claim both sides tacitly agree that care has to be built up from somewhere, so the dispute is really about which foundation scales.
