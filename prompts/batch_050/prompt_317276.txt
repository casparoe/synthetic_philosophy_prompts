My actual hunch, which I want the fellowship for, is that "arguments vs. interests vs. honor" is a bad framing because those aren't three rival explanations — they're three different jobs in a division of labor. Roughly: moral arguments are stockpiles, not triggers. Interests, meanwhile, determine which norms are even exposed to this process in the first place.
