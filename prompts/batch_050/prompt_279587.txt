So the question: can someone who accepts Taurek's reasoning about the single case consistently adopt the coin-flip as a *policy*, or does shifting from act to rule change the moral logic — say, because the greater aggregate loss is now a foreseeable, avoidable feature of the rule itself, even if no individual death wrongs anyone more than the alternative?
