Analyze the history of information talk in molecular biology — from the reception of Schrödinger's 1944 What is Life? through Crick's sequence hypothesis and central dogma to the Nirenberg–Matthaei cracking of the codon assignments — as a test case for the philosophical dispute over whether DNA is literally a code or whether the code is merely a productive metaphor. The pivot of the analysis should be a passage from the chapter on the hereditary mechanism in What is Life? in which Schrödinger, having just introduced the idea that the chromosome fibre carries the organism's developmental pattern in a miniature cipher, immediately retracts the metaphor's sufficiency: the chromosomal structures, he says, are not just the text of the law but also its enforcement, simultaneously the architect's plan and the builder's craft. First interpret the passage: what exactly does the double image commit him to, and is it an anticipation of the modern objection that a code requires a reader and cannot by itself be causally sufficient? Then map the subsequent history onto the two rival readings. On the literalist side (associated with figures like Maynard Smith and Sarkar), which features of the episode — the colinearity of sequence and protein, the existence of a genuine cipher between triplets and amino acids, the predictive and engineering successes the vocabulary enabled — vindicate treating genetic information as ontologically serious? On the deflationary side (associated with critics like Oyama and Moss), which features — the context-dependence and many-to-one character of the assignments, the causal indispensability of the cellular machinery that does the "reading," the way the code talk silently relocates agency — show that the metaphor remained a metaphor even while it paid its way? Finally, run the case backward: does Schrödinger's own hesitation, decades before the code was cracked, count as evidence that the founders themselves never intended the literal reading, or does his code-script formulation show that the literalization was already baked in? Say where each theory is simply silent about the episode rather than forcing it to speak, and close with a verdict on whether this history is a confirmation of the literalist view, an anomaly for it, or a case neither side anticipated.
