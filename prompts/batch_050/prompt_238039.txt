It follows that whatever befalls a person is, in the karmic sense, their own doing and their desert. And because the ledger is automatic and infallible, human attempts at justice or aid are at best redundant and at worst meddlesome: helping someone who is suffering only postpones a reckoning they have earned." The book presents this as the orthodox Buddhist and Hindu position, not as a caricature, and it draws the quietist conclusion with apparent confidence.
