Wren said that's the old Szilard-style story and it's wrong -- Bennett showed you can measure a molecule's position reversibly, with dissipation driven arbitrarily close to zero, and the real unavoidable cost is *erasing* the demon's memory afterward to get it back to its blank starting state.
