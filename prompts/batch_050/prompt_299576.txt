Please lay out the distinct precise claims that "it's all just physics" might be making — for example, about what exists, about what explains what, about which sciences are dispensable, and about whether higher-level patterns are real — and for each claim say whether it's trivially true, substantive but defensible, or actually false, and whether it would even support my uncle's conclusion about psychology. I've encountered the terms "greedy reductionism" and "skyhooks versus cranes" while reading around, so if some readings correspond to those, please flag that. Present the readings as a list I can bring to our next dinner, with a one- or two-sentence verdict on each, and keep the whole answer under 600 words.
