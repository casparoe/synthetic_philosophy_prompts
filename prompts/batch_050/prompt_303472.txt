I've read the relevant stretch of the Investigations a few times and I think I get the shape of it — the sensation-diary setup, the point that without a settled technique of use there's no real gap between a use seeming right to me and its being right — but I'm much less confident I can land it in ninety minutes with this crowd, in a way where everyone nods along and noone has actually gotten it.
