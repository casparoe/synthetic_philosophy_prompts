In poorer countries STEM is one of the few reliable routes to a decent salary, so women go into it whatever the stereotypes say. That's still culture — just filtered through money.

Dan: even if that's true, you've lost the bigger point. If free choices produce unequal outcomes, then equal representation was the wrong target all along, and we should stop treating every statistical gap as an injustice to be fixed.

Amara: you've just switched claims mid-argument. You started on "the differences are natural" and now you're on "the target was wrong anyway" — those are completely seperate claims, and you don't get the second one as a consolation prize for failing to prove the first.
