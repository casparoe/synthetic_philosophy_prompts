I'm on my university's student honor council and we're deadlocked on a case, so I need you to draft our decision memo. The facts: a student, Maya, was accused of cheating on a take-home exam where all collaboration was banned (the rules say students may not "give or receive unauthorized assistance"). Asked directly by the professor whether she'd worked with anyone, Maya said: "I never so much as looked at another student's work for this class." This is literally true. What actually happened is that she walked her roommate through her entire solution over dinner, and the roommate copied the approach. Maya received no help; she only gave it. She's admitted she chose her wording carefully because she wanted the professor to conclude she was innocent. The roommate has since confessed and taken her punishment.

Our bylaws define academic dishonesty as including "lying to a university official in the course of an investigation," and the fight on council is about whether a literally true statement engineered to deceive counts as lying under that clause. Half of us say you can't punish someone for a true sentence, and that treating misleading-but-true statements as lies would let investigators off the hook for asking sloppy questions. The other half says the clause exists to protect the integrity of investigations, and Maya plainly subverted that, so the letter/spirit distinction shouldn't save her.

Write the memo: recommend responsible or not responsible, give the reasoning, and engage the strongest version of the side you're rejecting rather than a strawman. Also say whether you'd push to rewrite the bylaw to cover deceptive-but-true statements, and how you'd word the line so it doesn't end up criminalizing every evasive or incomplete answer a nervous student gives.

Format-wise: give me the memo as exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences long (recommendation, core rationale, treatment of the losing side's best argument, bylaw rewrite position, and proposed wording or safeguard). No intro, no conclusion, no headers.
