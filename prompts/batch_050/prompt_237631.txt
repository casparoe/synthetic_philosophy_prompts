Popper initially suspected that Darwinism was untestable, on the grounds that if fitness is defined in terms of reproductive success and reproductive success is then explained in terms of fitness, natural selection degenerates into an empty tautology; he later recanted, recasting Darwinism as what he called a metaphysical research programme — not itself testable, but a source of testable theories. I want to defend the stronger position that modern evolutionary theory is straightforwardly falsifiable on Popper's own criteria, because fitness can in fact be specified independently of observed survival (through functional analysis of traits, or through quantitative predictions about offspring numbers in given environments). What would have to be true for this position to be wrong? Please identify which considerations would be decisive — for example, a demonstration that fitness can in practice only ever be measured retrospectively — and which would merely be awkward, such as the theory's comfortable accommodation of stasis, rapid change, and drift alike. Please also address whether that very flexibility amounts to the kind of immunizing stratagem Popper warned against, and say what Popper himself, given his recantation, would presumably have conceded as counting against my stronger claim. I would personally find most persuasive an argument isolating a specific, central commitment of selection theory that no possible observation could disconfirm.
