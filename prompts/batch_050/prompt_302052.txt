Like, reward hacking is easy to dramatize, the agent knows it's gaming you. I want some participants to walk out of the game having genuinely held a wrong goal, not having deliberately exploited a loophole, and only realizing it in the debrief. I think that's the part people don't viscerally get, the "it wasn't being deceptive, it just learned the wrong lesson" part.
