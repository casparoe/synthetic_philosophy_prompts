Write a deadpan, Onion-style news report (headline plus 400-500 words) set in a world of philosophical zombies: a world physically and behaviorally identical to ours, where nobody has any phenomenal consciousness whatsoever, yet the philosophers there still write anguished papers about the hard problem, give TED talks about what it's like to be a bat, and insist their inner life is ineffable. The report should cover a breakthrough at their Institute for Consciousness Studies, where researchers have "finally located the source of the mystery" -- and the joke is that the mystery is entirely behavioral: they're trying to explain why organisms with no experience keep sincerely reporting that they have one. The humor has to come from getting the meta-problem of consciousness right -- why do we *say* we're conscious, why do the reports feel so resistant to functional explanation, why does introspection seem to reveal something that talk of information-processing can't capture -- so a philosopher of mind laughs instead of winces. Keep it totally straight-faced: no winking at the reader, and the zombie scientists should take their own ineffability completely seriously. Include at least one quote from a 'puzzled' zombie researcher and one from a zombie illusionist who thinks the whole field is confused.
