I kept saying the phrase is empty until you say what counts as a side effect, and things got heated when I asked whether the robot turning on a lamp counts, since photons, heat, my sleep schedule. So: what are the different precise things "avoid side effects" could actually be claiming? I want a menu, from the trivially weak reading (don't do obviously catastrophic stuff?) up to whatever the strongest coherent version is, and for each one: is it something you'd need a full theory of human values to even state, or can you define it purely in terms of the physical world? Because I suspect my friend was using a reading that sounds like common sense but secretly smuggles in a complete account of what we want, and she suspects the same about me. Also settle this: is there a version that survives the objection that literally every action has massive downstream effects, so low-impact means "do nothing"?
