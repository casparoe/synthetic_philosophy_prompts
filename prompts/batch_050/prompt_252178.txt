Give me exactly five numbered items, one or two sentences each, no intro or wrap-up: (1-3) the three most plausible rules consistent with her four verdicts, with what each predicts about the teacher case; (4) your best guess at her verdict and why; (5) the single new hypothetical that would most cleanly tell the remaining rules apart.
