Predict her verdict on the new case — defective, acceptable, or something in between — say what each candidate rule predicts and where each finds its strongest support among her earlier verdicts, and then name a further example, not one of hers and not mine, whose verdict would cleanly separate the rule you think she is running from its strongest rival.
