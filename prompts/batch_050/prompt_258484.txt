'The debate approach to scalable oversight: take two AI systems trained to argue for opposite answers to a question, with a human judging the winner. The central result is that debate is truth-tracking — in the equilibrium of the debate game, the debater defending the true answer always wins, because any false claim can be exposed by the opponent pointing out the flaw, so optimal play converges on honest, maximally informative arguments. Because of this, a weak human judge can safely oversee systems much smarter than themselves: the judge never has to understand the domain, only check that the winning argument is sound. The upshot is that debate solves the scalable oversight problem in princple; what remains is engineering.'
