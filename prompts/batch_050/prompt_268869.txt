His conclusion was that since delegation and divide-and-conquer are solved engineering patterns, HCH is straightforward in principle, and the philosophical hand-wringing about whether the tree preserves the original person's judgment is overblown -- after all, a well-run company preserves the founder's intent across thousands of delegation steps. Something about that move feels too fast to me, but I can't articulate which load-bearing part of the analogy is doing the smuggling.
