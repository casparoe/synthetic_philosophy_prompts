What I want from you: write it up as an agony-aunt advice column — Dear Prudence style, call it "Ask Aunt Zooid" or come up with something better — where the letter writers are all biological edge cases writing in with identity crises, and Aunt Zooid answers with brisk, sensible, faintly exasperated life advice.
