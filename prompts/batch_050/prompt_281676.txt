A few of us who went through the same grad program still do a monthly philosophy night at someone's apartment, and this time the topic is material constitution. The host has asked each of us to circulate our commitments in advance so people can attack them, so here's mine, roughly in order of how confident I feel:

1. The statue on my shelf just IS the lump of clay. There's exactly one object there. Views that say the statue and the lump are two coinciding objects sharing all their parts seem to me to double-count the furniture of the world for no good reason.

2. Still, if I squashed the thing into a ball tomorrow, the artwork would be destroyed but the lump would survive just fine. I think this is compatible with 1 because modal properties are sortal-relative: the one object is necessarily statue-shaped qua statue but only contingently so qua lump. So there's no Leibniz's law violation lurking.

3. I'm an endurantist. Objects are wholly present at each moment of their careers; four-dimensionalism with its temporal parts has always struck me as a reductio rather than a solution, and I don't want any worm theory or stage theory in my picture.

4. About myself, though, I go the other way: I'm constituted by my body but not identical to it. If I were scanned and reconstructed with full psychological continuity while this organism flatlined, *I* would wake up over there. The animal stays; the person goes where the psychology goes.

5. The statue is insured for a lot of money while the clay, as clay, is worth maybe twenty bucks. I don't see any puzzle here at all -- one object can obviously be precious as an artwork and worthless as raw material.

What I want from you is an audit, not a lecture. Which of these pairs are genuinely in tension and which only look like it? Rank the real tensions by how bad they are, and for each one tell me the cheapest revision that resolves it and what that revision costs elsewhere in the set. Then tell me the single change that would clean up the most. I honestly don't know where the weakest joint is, so don't assume any particular commitment is the one I'm willing to drop.
