For my debate club I'm running a practice round on solar geoengineering (spraying aerosols to cool the planet, not carbon removal) and I want a decision tree that sorts people into actual positions. Start the tree at the claim that the world is likely to overshoot 1.5 degrees of warming, and make every branch a question someone could answer without knowing the jargon: whether the moral hazard worry (even researching this gives goverments an excuse to slack on cutting emissions) is a reason against research itself or only against deployment; whether termination shock, meaning the fast warming spike if a program starts and then suddenly stops, is a dealbreaker or just a problem you could govern your way around; and who should get to decide, like whether one country going it alone is ever acceptable. Each leaf should be a named position (deploy now if needed, research-only under an international treaty, moratorium on outdoor experiments, total ban, whatever fits), plus one sentence on the most awkward thing that position has to swallow. If a combination of answers has no defender, say so and guess why, and flag any two leaves your tree can't really seperate. Keep the whole thing under 500 words.
