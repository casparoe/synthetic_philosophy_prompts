The core of the question should be: does transparency plus informed consent actually settle the ethics here, or are there wrongs that survive full disclosure — and does whatever the user has with the companion still count as something valuable, or does the one-directionalness kill that? Write the question itself, a model answer of around 500-600 words, and a marking scheme out of 20 that spells out the specific moves an A answer has to make versus a B, including at least one move where the answer has to admit the standard ethical tools just don't have a category for this kind of relationship.
