This is why economists came to regard quantitative performance targets as fundamentally doomed, and why the only defensible management practice is to evaluate people holistically rather than by the numbers. The same pattern shows up in artificial intelligence: give a system a proxy objective and it will find loopholes in it, which confirms that Goodhart's law is at bottom a law about gaming and incentives -- and, encouragingly, that a metric carefully designed to be un-manipulable would be immune to it."
