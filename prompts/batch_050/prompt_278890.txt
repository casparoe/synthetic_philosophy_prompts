I want to build the session around it instead, and in particular around the passage where Turing proposes constructing a child-machine and subjecting it to a course of education, then observes that the teaching process could not simply be the one used on a normal child: the machine would lack the body to be sent out on errands, and it could hardly be sent to school without the other children making excessive fun of it, so education for a machine has to be rethought rather than copied.

Don't give me the explanation; give me a plan for the session.
