After the statement, provide a JSON object with these fields: agreed_claims (an array of strings); disagreements (an array of objects with issue, causalist_position, coordinationist_position, and merely_verbal, where the last field is true or false); cruxes (an array of objects with question, proposed_test, causalist_prediction, coordinationist_prediction, and settled_or_contested); and narrative_tension (one paragraph identifying the disagreement most useful for dramatic conflict without caricaturing either side).
