Some background first, so we're working from the same setup. In his essay "Who Shoves Whom Around Inside the Careenium?" From a great distance, the marbles' large-scale configurations spell out and manipulate symbols -- "simms" -- and the system's evolution can be described, with enormous compression, at the symbol level: this symbol triggers that one, patterns are recognized, inferences drawn. The thought experiment asks who is really doing the shoving: the simmballs, the simms, both, or neither.

A colleague of mine, who works in a neuroscience lab and is fairly dismissive of philosophy of mind, summarized the lesson of the careenium to me as follows:

"Hofstadter's parable shows that symbols are causally inert. Higher-level causal talk is a compression scheme, not a description of anything that actually does work. And since the human brain is presumably a careenium made of neurons, it follows that mental causation is an illusion: your decisions don't cause your behavior, your neurons do. By the same token, when we say a chess engine 'decided' to sacrifice its bishop, or that an AI system 'chose' a word, that's always loose talk -- only the transistors chose anything, which is to say, nothing did."
