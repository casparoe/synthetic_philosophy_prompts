Say a city rezones several residential streets to allow six-storey apartment buildings beside detached houses. But if property boundaries, zoning rules, and even the value of people’s homes are all created collectively through law and infrastructure, why shouldn’t the existing neighbourhood also collectively decide who gets to build there?
