Give leaves the name of the position if anyone actually holds it, otherwise label it "unnamed" and say one sentence about whether the slot is empty because it's incoherent or just unexplored. Each leaf also gets one sentence on its most awkward commitment. Flag any pairs of leaves the tree can't really separate rather than pretending a question distinguishes them when it doesn't. Aim for maybe 10-14 branch nodes total.
