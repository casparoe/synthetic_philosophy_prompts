At a disaster-relief agency, a coordinator tells her team, “I promise that the north shelter will be supplied by Friday,” although she knows that deliveries are automated and no one will alter their plans because of her assurance. A consultant argues that the statement has not yet created a promissory obligation: because promises bind by generating reliance, the agency should count how many team members changed their plans afterward and treat the assurance as binding only once enough reliance has accumulated. What threshold of reliance, if any, would make the coordinator’s promise binding, and at what point would that obligation come into existence?
