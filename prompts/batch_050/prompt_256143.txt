Kosslyn's quasi-pictorial view should become a claim about representations that preserve metric/topological structure of what's depicted, where operations on the representation (rotation, scanning, zooming) take time proportional to the spatial extent of the opperation -- like how interpolating or transforming in a well-structured latent space has costs that track geometric distance.
