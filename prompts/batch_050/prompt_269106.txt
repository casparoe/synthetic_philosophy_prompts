So the psi-ontic vs psi-epistemic debate has been going for ages, and even after PBR (which a lot of people treat as having settled it) the psi-epistemic side just... kept going, now mostly as "psi-doxastic" views or QBism or retrocausal stuff where the wavefunction is epistemic but the underlying ontology is something else. My hunch is that the debate stopped being about the wavefunction a while ago and is actually two disagreements stacked on top of each other: one about whether a given formal model forces an ontic reading (which seems like a real, partly formal question, hence PBR actually biting), and one about what a physical theory even owes us in terms of ontology, which the no-go theorems can't touch. Is that the right diagnosis, or am I flattening it? I'd like an account of what kind of disagreement this actually is -- verbal, factual, methodological, something else -- grounded in what the two sides actually say, and given your diagnosis, what could realistically count as progress here: a better theorem, a new no-go result with weaker assumptions, an experiment, or is the answer that the question itself needs reframing before anything can move?
