I am working through the Korean Four–Seven debate between Yi Hwang (Toegye) and Gi Dae-seung (Gobong) and trying to catalogue the main interpretive proposals for what their disagreement was actually about. So far my list contains the following:

1. The dual-origin reading: Toegye's insistence that the Four are the issuance of principle (li), with material force (ki) merely following, while the Seven are the issuance of ki with li merely riding, commits him to two genuinely distinct generative sources of emotion, and the debate is about whether this bifurcates moral psychology.

2. Gobong's own deflationary reading: every emotion alike involves ki issuing and li riding, so the Four–Seven distinction marks only a difference in moral quality or salience (spontaneously moral versus requiring regulation), not a difference in psychophysical origin, and Toegye's terminology dangerously reifies a merely classificatory distinction.

3. The activity-of-principle reading: the real stakes concern whether li is dynamic or inert — whether moral emotions like commiseration show that principle can move on its own, or whether li is always passive patterning that only manifests through ki.

4. The cultivation reading: the debate is fundamentally practical, since if the Four and Seven arise differently they call for different regimes of self-cultivation, whereas if they arise identically a single discipline of reverent attentiveness suffices for all emotion.

5. The structural reading: the Four are intrinsically relational or judgment-embedded responses to moral situations, whereas the Seven are general appetitive reactions, so the dispute anticipates questions about whether some emotions have moral content built into them.

I would like a sixth interpretation that is genuinely distinct from these five: not a restatement, a special case, or a combination of items already on the list. State the interpretation, explain briefly how it differs from each of the five above, and indicate whether you think it is exegetically defensible given what Toegye and Gobong actually argued. If you believe the list already exhausts the plausible options, say so and explain why instead of forcing a weak candidate.
