In two weeks I am presenting to the scientific advisory panel of a grantmaking fund that is deciding whether to finance research on reducing harms in large-scale insect farming. My talk defends what I will call the scale argument: even if the probability that insects are sentient is low -- say, one in ten or one in fifty -- the sheer number of individuals affected (on the order of trillions in farming and vastly more in the wild) means the expected moral weight of their suffering dominates almost every other cause, so we are rationally required to direct serious resources toward it despite the uncertainty.

The panel has no philosophers on it. It consists of three comparative psychologists who study pain in mammals and are professionally committed to conservative evidentiary standards for attributing sentience, two entomologists who know the behavioral and neuroanatomical literature on insects far better than I do and suspect I have read one survey paper, a veterinary epidemiologist, and an economist who does cost-effectiveness modeling for the fund. None of them is hostile to animal welfare; all of them are wary of expected-value arguments being used to launder speculative conclusions into funding decisions.

Prepare me for the question period. Give me the objections in roughly the order and with roughly the emphasis this particular audience will deliver them -- not the objections I would get from effective altruists or from philosophers of mind -- each phrased the way the person raising it would actually phrase it, in their disciplinary vocabulary. For each objection, indicate how much damage it can do to my case, what I should concede without hesitation, what I should hold, and a reply I can deliver in about ninety seconds. Note where each reply of mine is softest. Also tell me which of my own premises the entomologists are most likely to catch me overreaching on, given that my argument must remain intact even if they are right on the empirical point, and identify the single question I am hoping nobody asks, together with the best honest answer I can give if it comes.
