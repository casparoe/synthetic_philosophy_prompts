Read Memento through Kavka’s toxin puzzle, focusing on Leonard’s tattoos, notes, and photos as attempts to bind his future self. Be careful about the key asymmetry: Kavka’s prize rewards merely forming the intention to drink the toxin, not actually drinking it, whereas Leonard’s system only works if his future self follows the evidence. So is Leonard really facing the puzzle’s problem of intending without a later reason to act, or a different problem about trusting evidence he can no longer interpret? Also discuss whether his memory loss makes his future self enough like another person that promises and precommitment become ethically or rationally unstable. End by saying where the film complicates the puzzle—especially because Leonard’s tools for self-control are themselves vulnerable to manipulation.
