The question should be answerable in about 60 minutes. Alongside the question, provide a model answer of roughly 600 words and a marking scheme: five must-hit points worth 5 marks each (25 total), each accompanied by a one-line note on the characteristic way a weaker candidate mishandles or misses it, plus a short statement of exactly which moves separate an A answer from a B answer.
