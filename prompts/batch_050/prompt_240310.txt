Write a deadpan, in-universe civil-service circular from the Secretariat of Durable Things in an invented empire, addressed to district governors, war colleges, temple treasurers, and the Bureau of Romantic Alliances. The Secretariat has just adopted Mandatory Outside-View Adjudication: every plan must open with a reference class, a base rate, and a median historical duration, whether the plan concerns invading the steppe, reforming the grain tax, building a canal, declaring this dynasty unlike all prior dynasties, or a minister marrying for love. Around 750 words, in the voice of an overconfident but fastidious bureaucracy that believes it has finally solved arrogance by tabulating it.

The satire should exaggerate real problems rather than invent fake ones: reference-class shopping between land empires, sea powers, and short-lived but spiritually luminous city-states; disputes over whether to adjust for gunpowder, writing, roads, monsoon failure, or the moral character of the founder; base rates that are quietly assembled from chronicles written by survivors; regression to the mean treated as a natural law except when the current emperor feels numerically exceptional; reflexivity, because publishing a low success probability causes generals to desert and loans to dry up; and the way the outside view can be both genuine humility and a superb technology for never being blamed. Include an annex of approved comparison bins, a penalty schedule for unlicensed inside-view reasoning, and a solemn exemption process for cases deemed unprecedented by the Committee on Precedents.

Keep the irony legible without winking: no modern asides, no named living people, no citations, and no real quotation. Let the circular defend itself with perfect courtesy while making the reader feel the pull of the method and the absurdity of applying one mortality table to plagues, marriages, reforms, and empires alike. End with the Secretariat forecasting, by approved historical frequencies, the expected lifespan of its own forecasting system, and handling the result in a way that is procedurally impeccable.
