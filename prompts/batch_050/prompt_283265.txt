Is there an established name for this reaction, or is it a couple of different things I’m mashing together? I dont have an academic background, so sorry if this is reinventing a very obvious wheel.

Suppose every person alive today gets to live a normal, decent life. Nobody dies early or painfully. But everyone learns, with certainty, that humanity will peacefully end 100 years after the last currently living person dies. No future person will suffer, because the species simply stops continuing. A lot of people would probably feel that their work had become pointless now, even though the disaster is after their own deaths and hurts no particular future victim.

Now compare these versions:

1. Humanity really does end then, with nobody left to notice or regret it.
2. Humanity continues for another million years, but after that same date people become permanently unable to care about, understand, or be affected by anything we did. They are not hostile; our art, institutions, and struggles just become irrelevant, like static from a dead channel.
3. Humanity ends, but another civilization carries on our science, music, political projects, and moral concerns for a million years and genuinely understands why they mattered to us.
4. Humanity continues and still cares about what we did, but every currently living individual is completely forgotten by name. Humanity values the results without ever knowing who produced them.

My intuition is that the despair in the first case is not only sadness about people who never get born. The second case also feels almost as bad, even though humanity survives, while the third feels less bad even though humanity disappears. So the thing doing the work might not be our species existing. It might be some expected chain of influence, understanding, appreciation, or concern extending beyond us. But maybe I’m confusing several different worries: fear that my projects fail, wanting to be remembered, valuing future people, or needing the larger human story not to stop.

Is there a recognized concept for the general phenomenon, especially the way present meaning seems to depend on believing that some collective future will continue? Please do not just give me a bare label. I want the carving: what condition is supposedly required, whose future counts, and whether understanding and concern matter independently of biological survival.

Nearest established concept — about 150 words  
Where it fits and where it strains — about 300 words  
The four cases — about 400 words, giving a separate diagnosis of each  
A cleaner term if needed — about 150 words; if no existing concept covers the pattern exactly, coin one, define it, and state two boundary cases
