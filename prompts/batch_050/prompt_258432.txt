I moderate a politics Discord and every few months we have the same blowup about who should get a say in what -- last month it was whether the Brits living in Spain should have had a vote in Brexit, before that it was prisoners, before that 16-year-olds. Everyone just asserts their answer and nobody can explain where it comes from. So I want to make a little decision tree people can walk through that sorts them into a position by their answers to cases, not by what label they like. One root question to start, then branches where each fork is a concrete case you answer yes/no or pick between options on -- long-term residents with no passport, citizens who left the country thirty years ago, people in the next country over breathing our pollution, folks who'll be born in sixty years and have to live with our nuclear waste, that kind of thing. The leaves should be the actual positions (all-affected-interests, the coercion-or-subjection view, residency-based, national membership, plain status quo citizenship) with one line each on the weirdest thing holding that view commits you to. Two rules: if some combo of answers lands you in a spot nobody actually occupies, note it and say whether that's because the combo is incoherent or just unattractive; and if two positions cant be separated by any case, flag that instead of inventing a fake distinguishing question. Start with the root question itself and go straight into the tree, no preamble.
