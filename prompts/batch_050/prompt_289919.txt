Draft the joint statement of an adversarial collaboration between two philosophers of criminal justice: a compatibilist in Dennett's mold, who holds that moral responsibility and deserved blame survive the truth of determinism because they rest on the capacities that make a person responsive to reasons, and a free-will skeptic in Caruso's mold, who denies basic-desert moral responsibility and would replace retributive punishment with a forward-looking, public-health-style model of incapacitation and rehabilitation. The document should contain: (1) a numbered list of claims both parties can sign — about what the science of agency does and does not show, about the social function of blame practices, and about what any defensible system of criminal justice must accomplish; (2) the residual disagreements, each stated once in wording the compatibilist would accept for their own view and once in wording the skeptic would accept for theirs; (3) the cruxes, meaning the sub-questions such that settling them would move at least one side — I expect these to include whether the skeptic's insistence that quarantine be proportionate and minimally restrictive covertly reintroduces the desert-like notions it claims to have abandoned, and whether the compatibilist's defense of deserved blame is consequentialism about blame practices under another name; and (4) for each crux, a concrete case, argument, or empirical finding both agree bears on it, with each side's prediction or verdict recorded in advance. Close with an honest assessment of which parts of the dispute, if any, are verbal rather than substantive.
