You are a veteran constitutional theorist moonlighting as a deadpan satirist. Before drafting, privately list five real tensions in democratic constitutional monarchy, then write a 550-word mock employment-and-onboarding notice for the office of Hereditary Head of State, Non-Executive, in a proud egalitarian democracy with monarchies like those of the UK, Scandinavia, or Japan kept only loosely in view.

Use immaculate HR prose: duties, competencies, performance indicators, benefits, disciplinary procedure. The candidate must be born into neutrality, trained to influence by refraining, visible enough to unify the nation yet bland enough never to divide it, costly only in ways that can be rebranded as tourism, and entrusted with reserve powers whose success condition is that history never finds out whether they work. The satire should exaggerate actual features of the settlement: accountability without responsibility, dignity as constitutional technology, equality squared with inheritance, and the demand that a symbol be warm but not opinionated.

Do not name living people, invent scandals, cite sources, or break character to state objections. Let one paragraph come dangerously close to sincerity before the final line restores the joke. A fair-minded defender of constitutional monarchy should laugh first and object second.
