So last night I got into this long stupid argument with my friend who works at an effective altruism adjacent org, about whether the whole importance-tractability-neglectedness thing is a real decision tool or just vibes wearing a spreadsheet costume. Her position was that scoring causes on those three axes forces honesty; mine was that you can reverse-engineer any score you want, because "tractability" especially is just a number you pull out of the air and then multiply by confidence. Neither of us won, obviously, but it gave me an idea.

Write me a satirical piece: a deadpan "shallow cause-area investigation" report (the kind a big foundation might commission before deciding whether to look deeper) into an absurdly trivial invented cause -- say, reforming the catering at academic conferences, or standardizing the length of elevator small talk, you pick or invent your own -- that is scored with complete methodological seriousness on importance, tractability, and neglectedness, and comes out looking weirdly fundable. Around 700-800 words. The humor has to come from actually using the framework correctly: a back-of-envelope calculation with made-up but plausible-looking numbers, a claim about logarithmic returns to scale, the observation that the cause is maximally neglected precisely because no sane person works on it (and the report refusing to treat that as evidence against it), some hand-wringing about counterfactual credit and whether the report's own publication would crowd out other researchers, maybe a moral-weights comparison to chicken welfare thrown in for calibration. The report should conclude with a recommendation to fund a pilot study, and the reader should be genuinely unsure whether the framework generated that conclusion or the conclusion generated the framework.

Two constraints: keep the irony totally deadpan, no winking, the author of the report sincerely believes every word -- a reader should be able to tell whats being ironized only because the reasoning is so perfectly executed on such a dumb target. And dont satirize any actual organization or person; invent the foundation, the analyst, the whole thing. Ideally someone who works in this world reads it and goes "unfortunately I have written paragraphs like this."
