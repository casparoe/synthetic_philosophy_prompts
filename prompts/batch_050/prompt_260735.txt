I'm a novelist, and last month I finally wrote the death of a character I've been living with across three books. I cried actual tears writing the scene, and I still get a lump in my throat when I think about her. My husband thinks this is nuts -- she's not real, I invented her, and if I really missed her I could just write her back to life in the next draft. He sort of has a point?? But the sadness doesn't feel fake at all, and honestly the fact that I'm the one who chose to kill her makes it feel worse, not better. Is he right that grieving someone who never existed is just incoherent, or is something legitimate going on here? And does it matter that I'm the author rather than just a reader?
