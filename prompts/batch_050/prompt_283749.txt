I'm taking a course on energy and climate policy, and for the final creative assignment I want to submit a piece of satire. Write an Onion-style news article -- headline plus roughly 450-550 words -- reporting that Germany, having completed its nuclear phase-out, has announced a scheme to certify the electrons flowing through its grid, guaranteeing consumers that no power of fissile origin has morally contaminated their supply. The humor should come from exaggerating things that are actually true, not from inventing absurdities: the shutdown of the last three reactors in April 2023, the reactivation of coal and lignite capacity once Russian gas was cut off, Germany's net imports of electricity from a France that still gets most of its power from nuclear plants, and the fact that electrons on a synchronized European grid cannot be checked for provenance at the socket. Include at least one invented quote from a government spokesperson and one from a weary grid engineer, and keep the tone completely deadpan so that a reader who follows European energy policy can identify the real feature behind every joke. Please don't name any living politicians or parties; the target should be the policy and its surrounding rhetoric, not specific individuals.
