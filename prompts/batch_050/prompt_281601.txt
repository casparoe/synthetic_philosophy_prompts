David Lewis defended the provocative view that penal substitution — someone other than the offender undergoing the punishment — can sometimes be just: a friend may pay your fine, and he thought even a volunteer serving your prison sentence could be acceptable under the right conditions. He died in 2001, before a lot of things this idea now bumps into.
