hey, so im taking a philosophy of mind course this term and we just finished the illusionism unit (Frankish, Dennett, the idea that phenomenal consciousness is a kind of introspective illusion rather than a real thing). for my creative assignment i want to write a parody and i need help.

Pretend you're a professional humor writer who also happens to have a PhD in philosophy of mind -- someone who genuinely knows this literature cold and whose jokes land because they're *accurate*, not because they dunk on a strawman.

Write a deadpan parody of a glossy wellness-retreat brochure for a mindfulness meditation retreat run entirely by committed illusionists. About 600 words. The comedy should come from taking illusionism completely seriously in a genre of marketing that normally runs on raw feels and "pure presence" -- so the brochure promises that there is nothing it is like to attend the retreat, and means it as a selling point.

Some requirements so it works for my professor:

- The jokes have to come from actual illusionist commitments: introspective misrepresentation, the gap between how experience *seems* and what it *is*, qualia as supposedly ineffable/intrinsic/private, Chalmers' meta-problem of consciousness (explaining why we *say* we're phenomenally conscious), that kind of thing. A Frankish fan should be able to read it and go "ok this is unfair but it's unfair *accurately*."
- Include a daily schedule (e.g. a 7am session about noticing that there is nothing it is like to notice things), a few guest testimonials, and an FAQ section.
- The FAQ must contain, played totally straight, the standard objection that an illusion still has to *seem* a certain way to someone and that seeming is itself phenomenal -- and the brochure's in-universe answer. Don't dodge it, that's the best joke available.
- Stay deadpan the whole way through, no winking at the reader. It should be obvious what's being satirized just from the content.
- Don't name or mock any real living person; invented retreat staff are fine.

Before you write the actual brochure, brainstorm out loud: list 5-6 specific doctrines, moves, or stock objections from the illusionism debate that could each be turned into a brochure joke, with one line on how each becomes funny. Then pick the strongest ones and write the piece. Think carefully about what makes the satire *fair* rather than just mocking.
