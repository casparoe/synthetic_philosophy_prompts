The problem is that our engineering/robotics elective is like 90% guys and the childcare/early education one is almost all girls, and the principal wants us to come up with a plan to even it out.

Here's where it gets messy. His argument was basically: look, when people are totally free to pick whatever they want, guys and girls pick different stuff even more. So the gap in our electives isn't because anyone's being pushed or discouraged, it's just what people naturally want when nothing's stopping them. So my actual question: should our committee keep trying to balance the electives, or is the "let people pick what they want" argument right?
