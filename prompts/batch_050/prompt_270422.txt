Here's the thing -- this sounds EXACTLY like the currency problem we wrestled with my whole career. You watch a bird drop mussels from a certain height or leave a patch at a certain time, and you ask what it's maximizing: energy intake rate? total energy? efficiency? minimizing predation exposure? And the dirty secret was always that several currencies fit the same data and you only got traction by assuming constraints and testing across contexts. So my suspicion is these ML people have reinvented our old headache with fancier math, and the philosophers writing about inferring human preferences from behavior don't realize it.
