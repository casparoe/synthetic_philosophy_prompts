Round 1: Dan said the sphex case shows that "behavioral flexibility" is the real mark of mindedness and, ultimately, of free agency -- the wasp fails because it can't deviate from the script even when the script is obviously pointless. Maya said this proves way too much: humans run scripts constantly (driving home on autopilot, rote social scripts, typing passwords, habits generally), and if looping a routine without noticing makes you sphexish then we're sphexish most of the day. Round 3: Dan replied that this is exactly his point and Maya has conceded it: freedom = reasons-responsiveness, and the wasp fails because no part of it is responsive to reasons, only to trigger stimuli.
