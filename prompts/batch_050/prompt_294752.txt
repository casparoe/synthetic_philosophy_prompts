Here's my opening move: desert seems to require that the person being punished is the person who did the deed, in whatever sense of "same person" matters for responsibility. But on your own setup, the life doing the suffering shares no memories, no character, no psychological connective tissue with the life that did the deed. If my friend committed a crime and forgot it completely due to amnesia, we might still punish him because the body and the causal history are continuous -- but across rebirth there's not even that, the body is gone. So it looks to me like karma-as-desert is refuted by karma's own mechanism: the rebirth process that supposedly delivers the desert also destroys the very connection that would make it deserved rather than just misdirected. Why isn't it exactly like the universe fining a random stranger for my parking ticket?
