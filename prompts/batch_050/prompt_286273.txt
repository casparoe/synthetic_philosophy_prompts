A colleague recently confided in me that she is quietly interviewing for jobs elsewhere, largely for personal reasons she does not want known at our company. I take my Buddhist practice seriously, including the commitment to truthful speech, and I have been trying to make my conduct at work consistent with it. Yesterday our manager asked me directly whether she is looking for another position. I dodged the question, but he will ask again, and if he learns the truth she will likely be passed over for a promotion she badly needs in the meantime. My teacher says lying is out of the question; a friend says what matters is the harm the truth would cause, not the rule. Should I lie? In answering, please address whether my tradition itself really settles this as cleanly as my teacher implies, and what it would take for one consideration to override the other.
