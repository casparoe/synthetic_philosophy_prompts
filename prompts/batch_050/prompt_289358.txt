Write an Onion-style news article, headline plus about 500 words, reporting that a major tech company, having been persuaded by its in-house philosophers that minds are multiply realizable, has shut down its server farm and replaced it with 40,000 employees passing paper memos around a warehouse, and now claims the system runs the exact same consciousness as before, only slower. The jokes should come from getting the philosophy of mind right: substrate independence, the China-brain intuition, the gap between simulating a functional organization on paper and actually implementing one, and whether there's anything it's like to be a memo-passing schedule. Include at least one quote from a concerned neuroscientist who can't quite say where the mistake is, and one from a company spokeseperson who answers every question with the phrase "functionally identical." The satire should land on people who invoke multiple realizability as if it settles the hard questions, not on functionalism itself.
