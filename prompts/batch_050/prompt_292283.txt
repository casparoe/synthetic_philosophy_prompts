A colleague in my department circulated an argument about what grown children owe their parents, and it seems to prove far more than anyone in the room could accept, though none of us could cleanly locate the failure. (1) Special obligations -- those owed to particular people rather than to everyone -- must be grounded either in a voluntary undertaking (a promise, a contract, the acceptance of a role) or in an ongoing relationship of mutual regard, such as friendship. (2) No child voluntarily undertakes anything toward its parents: nobody consents to being born, and a small child cannot meaningfully consent to receiving care. (3) Nor can a debt of gratitude be generated by the care itself, since parents are morally required to care for the children they bring into existence, and doing what one is obligated to do anyway creates no debt in the beneficiary. (4) So the only special obligations an adult child could have toward a parent are the obligations of friendship, owed to the parent only insofar as the two are actually friends. (5) Obligations of friendship lapse when the friendship does, and while it lasts they demand little beyond what affection spontaneously motivates. (6) Many adult children and their aging parents are not friends; their relations are cool, strained, or sustained only by a sense of duty. (7) Therefore many adult children owe their parents literally nothing -- no calls, no visits, no care in old age -- and no criticism of them is warranted on that score. I would like to keep (3), since the thought that one cannot incur a debt merely because someone discharged their own duty toward you seems right, and I would rather abandon a premise than accept (7). Tell me which step I should reject, explain why it looked plausible, and price the alternatives: what it costs to reject (1), what it costs to reject (5), and what it costs to accept (7) after all. Where the philosophical literature bears on this -- for instance the friendship model associated with Jane English -- please distinguish what is genuinely settled from what remains contested, and do not present a contested position as the consensus.
