Is there an established term for the following configuration? Suppose a technology exists that a single mid-sized state could deploy on its own, at a cost well within its ordinary budget, that would measurably improve conditions for most of the world's population while imposing uncertain, possibly severe harms on particular regions that had no say in the matter. Two further features interest me. First, once the intervention has run for some years, halting it suddenly would be worse for nearly everyone than either continuing it or never having started, so each round of continuation is individually rational even if the whole trajectory is one nobody would have chosen in advance. Second, the intervention's availability visibly reduces the political pressure to address the underlying problem it merely masks.

I ask because the discussions I can find treat these as separate worries. There is literature on the danger that a partial fix drains the will to pursue a real one, and separate literature on the danger of abrupt cessation once a masking intervention is underway, and still more literature on who, if anyone, has standing to authorize an act with global effects. But I have not seen a name for the specific knot that results when all three hold at once: cheap unilateral initiation, lock-in created by the asymmetry between starting and stopping, and the erosion of the incentive to solve the actual problem. The whole seems worse than the sum of its parts, because the lock-in means the initial decision effectively cannot be revisited, and the unilateralism means it may be made by an actor who bears little of the risk.

Does this composite have a name of its own, or is it genuinely just three familiar problems listed side by side? If it is merely the latter, I would like to know whether anyone has argued that the interaction changes the moral analysis -- for instance, whether the standard arguments about compensating losers, or about majority consent among affected nations, survive once the decision is effectively irreversible from day one. And if there is no established term, I would welcome a precise coinage, with enough definition that I can tell which real or proposed cases fall inside it and which fall outside. A bare label without that boundary work would not help me.
