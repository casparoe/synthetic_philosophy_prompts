I've got maybe 2-3 papers worth of time and I don't want to spend them on a dispute that turns out to be merely terminological.

So: which open questions in this debate matter most, and which should I skip? I'd also like you to consider one more question my advisor keeps bringing up: does the existence of large language models — systems with no body, no sensorimotor loop, that nonetheless produce seemingly intelligent behavior — constitute genuine evidence against embodied cognition, or is it irrelevant because LLMs don't do the things embodiment was supposed to explain?

I'm not asking for a reading list or a survey of positions — I know the literature decently well, what I need is help deciding where to place my bets.
