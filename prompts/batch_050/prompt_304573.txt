Ok so this debate keeps resurfacing in my corner of the internet (LW-adjacent blogs, occasional grumpy philosophers of biology on twitter) and it never resolves because nobody says what would settle it: is DNA literally a code, or is "code" just a metaphor we've leaned on so hard we forgot it's one? Everyone just asserts and links the same three papers. So I want to force the issue with a concrete precisification and get actual numbers out of you.

Here's the precisification to evaluate. Say a system is a genuine code (not a metaphorical one) iff:

1. Arbitrariness: the mapping from symbol-states to outputs is contingent in the strong sense — a different mapping could have obtained and the system would still work; the chemistry permits alternatives rather than forcing this one.
2. Separate reader: there's a physically distinct subsystem whose function is to interpret the symbols, such that symbols and reader can vary independently and the reader's function is defined relative to the mapping rather than to the symbols' intrinsic chemistry.
3. Counterfactual fragility: swap in a different mapping while holding the reader fixed, and function collapses until the reader re-adapts.

The claim I want your credence on: the DNA–tRNA–ribosome system satisfies all three, i.e. the code-ness of DNA survives being made precise rather than evaporating.

Please give me:

- your credence that this precisifcation is even the right sharpening of what the debate is about — P(I'm asking the right question)
- conditional on that, P(arbitrariness holds) — noting that the actual genetic code seems unusually good at minimizing the damage of point mutations compared to random alternatives, which some people take to undermine arbitrariness (selection shaped it, so it's not arbitrary!) and others take to confirm it (there was a space of alternatives to optimize over at all!)
- P(separate reader holds) — is the ribosome + tRNA-charging machinery genuinely "separate" from the DNA in the relevant sense, or does this criterion smuggle in a homunculus
- P(counterfactual fragility holds) — tricky, because reader and code co-evolve, and nature already contains variant codes (mitochondria, some ciliates) that changed without total collapse
- how these combine into your final credence on the main claim — and be honest about whether you're just multiplying conditionals or doing something else
- which single term the final number is most sensitive to, and what empirical finding or argument would shift that term most

Separately: my own standing take is ~0.75 that the whole debate is basically verbal and dissolves once you fix a definition, ~0.25 that something substantive survives precisification (e.g. a real disagreement about whether "arbitrariness" is even coherent for evolved systems). Give me your split on that, and if you think it isn't verbal, say where the real disagreement lives.
