I got into one of those late-night LessWrong-adjacent arguments at a meetup and we both agreed the usual Mars teletransporter stuff is too clean: perfect scanners, exact duplicates, branch-line, all the knobs pre-set to make the intuition pump fire. I want a less meme-ified case for a friend whose view is basically: if the biological organism goes, you go, no matter how psychologically seamless the successor feels. Build me a thought experiment to order in a near-future rehab clinic, not a spaceship. Use this seeded tech but define everything plainly: staged cortical prosthetics that replace at most a few percent of tissue per month while the patient is awake and working; a memory-reconsolidation therapy that can strengthen, edit, or fail to re-encode particular autobiographical episodes during sleep; and an apprenticeship protocol where a patient trains one named successor for months in habits, projects, promises, inside jokes, decision procedures, and unfinished plans. No exact copying, no destructive upload, no many-worlds, no duplicate people walking around at the end, no magic soul detector, and definately no citation homework.

Construct two paired endings from the same patient, each no more than a paragraph, engineered so the verdicts separate instead of collapsing into the usual closest-continuer mush. Ending 1 must deliver numerical or legal-biological identity without what matters: the same animal is wheeled out, family and courts say it is him, but because reconsolidation keeps failing there are almost no direct episodic memories, intentions, affective attachments, or continuing projects caused in the normal way by the pre-op self, only skills plus a third-person file. Ending 2 must deliver what matters without identity: no surviving original animal and no copy, but one apprenticeship successor has robust Relation-R-style psychological connectedness and continuity with an acceptable cause, while animalism, closest-continuer, and conservative egoistic identity all deny survival. After the scenarios, show the check: state the patient before, the process, the single later subject in each ending, then give the verdict and one-sentence mechanism for animalism, psychological continuity with non-branching, closest-continuer theory, Parfit-flavored what-matters, and narrative-self views.

Then isolate the load-bearing variable: say exactly which feature makes identity and what-matters peel apart, what happens if you remove that feature, and why the case does not secretly stipulate the answers. Finish with the strongest attack from my biological-continuity friend, the strongest attack from a Parfitian who thinks my Ending 2 is just grief plus mentoring, and one repair for each attack that does not add new sci-fi. Keep the tone like a careful rationalist forum post: jargon allowed in the analysis, but the scenarios themselves should read like something a nurse, spouse, or hospital ethics board could follow.
