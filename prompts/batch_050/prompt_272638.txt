Add the requirement that whenever every individual is indifferent between two prospects, society is indifferent too. It then follows mathematically that society's utility function must be a weighted sum of the individuals' utility functions. Since the weights can be normalized and the theorem puts no constraint on them, Harsanyi proved that the only rational form of social evaluation is the utilitarian one: maximize the sum of individual utilities. This is why economists treat interpersonal comparisons of utility as unproblematic — the theorem delivers them automatically."
