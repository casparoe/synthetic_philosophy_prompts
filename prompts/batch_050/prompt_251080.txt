Nobody's disputing the data. The fight is always about whether the effect happens "in perception" or "right after perception." And I keep thinking -- if the disagreement survives every experiment, maybe it was never an empirical disagreement? But maybe I'm wrong and there's a real empirical question buried in there -- like, would it actually settle anything if we could show feedback projections to V1 carrying the effect? Or is the response going to be that anatomically early isn't the same as functionally encapsulated?
