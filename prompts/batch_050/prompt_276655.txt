So my friend and I got into it again last night over anthropic reasoning (this started over beers and ended with napkins full of decision trees, you know how it goes). He's convinced my views are a grab-bag and I want a neutral referee before I concede anything. Here's what I actually hold, as near as I can pin it down: (1) In the Sleeping Beauty case, Beauty should answer 1/3 on waking -- the halfer position has always struck me as dodging the obvious. (2) The Doomsday argument isn't a proof of anything, but it's a genuinely good argument that should shift my credence toward doom-sooner rather than doom-later; it's not just a trick. (3) The presumptuous philosopher objection is fatal to the self-indication assumption -- any principle that tells you to be near-certain the universe contains a googolplex of observers just from armchair reflection has gone wrong somewhere. (4) Reference-class worries are overblown. People use them to escape conclusions they don't like, and a good anthropic argument shouldn't hinge on whether you gerrymander the class as "humans," "intelligent observers," "observer-moments on Tuesdays," whatever. (5) A cosmological theory that predicts I'm overwhelmingly likely to be a Boltzmann brain is thereby disconfirmed or at least heavily penalized -- that's a legitimate cost of a theory, not a pseudo-problem. (6) The fine-tuning of the constants is decent evidence for a multiverse; not conclusive, but it realy does count in favor.

My friend says at least two of these can't live together, but he refused to tell me which ones because he wants me to "feel the tension myself," which is insufferable. So: go through the set and tell me which commitments are genuinely in tension and which only look like it. Rank the tensions by severity -- don't just flag everything as equally problematic. For each real tension, tell me exactly what I'd have to give up to fix it and what that would cost me elsewhere in the set. And if one of the apparent tensions dissolves once you're careful about what the commitments actually say, explain why it dissolves, because I suspect at least one of his objections is superficial.
