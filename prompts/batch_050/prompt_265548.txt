I've been reading SSC since like 2015 and Astral Codex now, and one of the best things I ever got off those blogs was the concept of an adversarial collaboration -- two people who disagree writing down what they can both sign before going back to fighting. My houseshare (yes, really) has an argument that's been running for over a year and I want the document version of it.

Side one: my housemate Tom, a fairly orthodox effective altruist. Earns to give at a trading firm, donates to GiveWell top charities, thinks "systemic change" talk is a trap because the effects are unmeasurable and the track record of people who claim to be doing it is unauditable. Side two: my other housemate Priya, who does tenant organising and thinks EA individualises what are actually political problems, that its evaluation methods systematically blind it to the interventions that matter most (policy, movements, institutions), and that earning to give leans on a fantasy of replaceability -- the trading job Tom takes doesn't leave some counterfactual world where nobody does it, and the harm of the industry is real.

Write the joint statement they could both put their names to. I want: (1) a numbered list of agreed claims -- things like "most charities are nowhere near the best ones" and "long-run political effects are genuinely hard to forecast", phrased so neither feels slanted; (2) a disagreements table where each side's position is stated in words that side would actually accept; (3) the cruxes -- I'd guess one is whether the measurability bias of EA's toolkit is bigger or smaller than the optimism bias of people claiming systemic wins without measurement, and another is whether historical cases like abolition or women's suffrage actually license expected-value reasoning about today's campaigns, but find others too; and (4) for each crux, a concrete test or piece of evidence both agree bears on it, with each side's prediction registered in advance. If part of their fight is verbal, name the part. British English spelling throughout, please.
