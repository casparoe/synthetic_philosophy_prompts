In the White Horse Dialogue of the Gongsun Longzi, an advocate defends the thesis that a white horse is not a horse against an objector, arguing, roughly, that the term for horse names a shape while the term for white names a color, and that someone who asks for a horse may be given a yellow or black one, whereas someone who asks for a white horse may not. Two broad reconstructions of this argument dominate interpretation. On the first, associated with A. C. Graham, the dialogue is a sophisticated piece of sophistry that trades on an ambiguity in the negation: the thesis is trivially true if read as the non-identity of the expressions or concepts involved, and plainly false if read as excluding white horses from the class of horses, and the advocate's success consists in sliding between these readings. On the second, associated more with the tradition running through Fung Yu-lan, the argument is serious philosophy of language: the dialogue is working out how compound names relate to simple ones and what a name's content consists in, and the non-identity claim is a substantive thesis within a program of correlating names with things rather than a verbal trick. Compare these two reconstructions and defend a verdict between them. In doing so, address at least the following pressure points: whether the opening move about shape-names and color-names is better explained as deliberate equivocation or as a theory of what names pick out; whether the argument about seeking and delivering horses is an early recognition that request-contexts are intensional (compare the fact that one can seek a unicorn without there being unicorns), and if so, whether that strengthens the serious reading or merely relocates the trick; and what each reconstruction makes of the apparent triviality objection — that once the readings are disambiguated, the advocate has proven either something false or something no one ever doubted. Finally, consider whether the later Mohist stance, on which a white horse straightforwardly is a horse and riding a white horse is riding a horse, succeeds against the argument on your preferred reconstruction, or whether it simply talks past it.
