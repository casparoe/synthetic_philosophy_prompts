Her verdicts so far: "Sherlock Holmes lived on Baker Street" is true, at least in the stories. "Sherlock Holmes had a spleen" is true in the stories, even though no story states it. "Sherlock Holmes is more famous than any detective who ever actually lived" is literally true, with no implicit "in the stories" prefix. "Sherlock Holmes is a fictional character" is likewise literally true.
