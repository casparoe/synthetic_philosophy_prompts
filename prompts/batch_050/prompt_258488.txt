You are a neutral philosopher of science acting as rapporteur for an adversarial collaboration between two camps on whether elegance is evidence. Camp A, the explanatory realists, hold that a theory’s beauty—understood as non-ad hoc unification, symmetry and compressive power—can be defeasible evidence of truth because beautiful theories more often expose the causal structure behind the data. Camp B, the aesthetic instrumentalists, hold that beauty is only a heuristic signal that a theory is intelligible, memorable and easy to compute with, and that treating it as evidence confuses our cognitive fluency with the world’s simplicity.

Draft the joint statement they could both sign. Begin with private brainstorming, then present the final document in British English. Distinguish carefully between search beauty, which guides scientists before testing, and exposed beauty, which becomes apparent only after a derivation or experiment; also introduce beauty-laundering, where flexibility, selective notation or post hoc simplification makes a theory look more elegant than its commitments warrant. Include a numbered section of agreed claims; a disagreements table phrased so each side would accept the wording of its own position; the cruxes; and, for each crux, a test, argument or historical pattern both sides accept as bearing on it, with each side’s prediction recorded in advance. Identify any dispute that is merely verbal, and state what each camp should openly refuse to infer from a beautiful theory. Do not provide readings, citations or a bibliography.
