ok so heres the thing i keep chewing on: is buddhist ethics consequentialist or not. half the stuff i read says its basically a flavor of utilitarianism (minimize suffering, universal compassion, etc), the other half says no way, its a virtue ethics, or its about purifying intention, or karma makes it agent-relative in a way no consequentialism could allow. everyone seems to be using a different test for what would even settle it.

so write the test. what would an adequate account of buddhist ethics — lets say early buddhist ethics, the pali canon material, and you can flag where mahayana changes the picture — have to deliver before the "is it consequentialist" argument can even start? i want desiderata with the phenomenon behind each. things it plausibly has to handle, off the top of my head:

- intention (cetana) is what karma actually tracks — accidentally crushing an insect vs deliberately killing. any theory has to explain why outcomes matter *at all* if its intentions that carry the weight
- the compassionate transgression stories, like the bodhisattva who kills a would-be mass murderer to save everyone aboard and knowingly takes on the karmic cost. what must a theory say about these, and can a precept-based or virtue reading survive them?
- the precepts look exceptionless in some places, but the advice elsewhere is plainly outcome-sensitive — is that a rule-consequentialist structure, "skillful means," or just a tension the theory has to live with
- karma's payoffs accrue to the agent later, which looks egoistic and agent-relative, yet the reason given for compassion is the suffering itself, not whose suffering it is — wich is it?
- if the sole intrinsic good is awakening and right action is just whatever conduces to it, call that "path-consequentialism": does a one-value theory where the value is a state of your own mind even deserve the name consequentialism?

for each desideratum, mark whether its genuinely non-negotiable or a prejudice of one camp (analytic philosophers who want buddhism mapped onto western boxes vs people who think the whole mapping exercise is a category error). flag any pair of desiderata you think cant be jointly satisfied — thats the part i care about most, if the list itself shows the debate is malformed, say so. and tell me wich single desideratum knocks out the most existing readings. skip the final verdict on whether its consequentialist, ill decide once i see the spec.
