I want an activity for next week that lets them feel why this is genuinely a hard question — can a finite sequence be random at all, or is randomness only a property of whatever process produced it?

Design a game (or a couple of short ones) where they have to commit to judgments before anyone explains anything. I definately don't want a lecture up front and they shouldn't hear any technical names until afterwards. Give me rules, materials, a scoring scheme that doesn't need real money, a rough timeline, then a debrief that maps what happened onto the actual concept (I believe there's a formal version of "no shorter description of the string exists" — let the name drop only at the end).
