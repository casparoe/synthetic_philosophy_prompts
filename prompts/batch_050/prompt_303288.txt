The arguments for paying that we've already covered:

1. Parfit's own move — the rational thing is having the *disposition* to pay, not the act of paying itself. People with that disposition get rescued, so rationality recommends having it even if acting on it at the moment of payment looks irrational (the whole rational irrationality thing).

3. The Newcomb point — since the driver read your character before deciding, your being a payer was already settled in the "prediction," so your choice now just is the choice of what that prediction contained, and you should treat it like one-boxing and pay.
