You're a game theorist moonlighting as an epistemologist (or the other way around). I want you to draft the joint statement that would come out of a genuine adversarial collaboration between two camps on Aumann's agreement theorem:

Camp A ("disagreement skeptics"): given common priors and common knowledge of each other's rationality and honesty, persistent disagreement between truth-seekers is a sign that something has gone wrong -- somebody is being irrational, dishonest, or hasn't actually made their reasoning common knowledge.

Camp B ("permissivists"): real-world disagreement is fine, because the theorem's conditions basically never obtain -- priors differ legitimately, agents are computationally bounded, and I can't become common knowledge of your private evidence just by hearing your conclusion.

Before you write the statement, brainstorm a bit about what each side can actually concede without giving up their position -- don't just strawman both into a mushy middle.
