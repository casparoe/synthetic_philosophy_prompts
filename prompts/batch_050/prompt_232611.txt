Frankfurt thinks bullshit is actually a bigger threat to truth than lying is, because at least the liar cares enough about the truth to bother hiding it, whereas bullshit normalizes not caring at all. Then the application: LLM hallucinations are clearly bullshit rather than lies or errors. They can't be lies because the model doesn't know any truths that it's concealing, and they can't be mere errors because an error is what happens when a system aims at the truth and misses, while the model is just generating plausible-sounding text with no concern for accuracy either way.
