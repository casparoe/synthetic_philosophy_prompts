On this reading, individuality as a metaphysical category simply fails at the fundamental level: an electron is less a thing than a slot in the wavefunction, and the old philosophical debate about whether identity is primitive has been settled by physics, in Leibniz's favor.

What I need is an assessment of whether this inference chain survives expert scrutiny, and if not, where exactly it breaks — I'd rather be corrected now than in the letters page.
