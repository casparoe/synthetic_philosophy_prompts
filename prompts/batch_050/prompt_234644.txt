So I started wondering whether it even makes sense for me to assign a probabilty to one of my own still-open choices, or whether genuine deliberation has to treat the choice as not yet being a fact I can forecast from the outside.

Be explicit about what each position would imply for the habit app: Should it hide its prediction? Treat showing the prediction as an intervention?
