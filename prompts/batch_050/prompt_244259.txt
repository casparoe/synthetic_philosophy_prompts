- Duchamp's Fountain: art, I guess. I've made my peace with it.

- The signature dish at a fancy restaurant: art. The chef is doing something expressive, idk.
- My roommate's Spotify playlist: not art, even though he keeps insisting his curation is a creative act.
- An elegant math proof (like Euclid on there being infinitely many primes): feels like art to me, even if that's a weird thing to say.

- A meme: art.
