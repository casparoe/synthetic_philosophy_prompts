For a philosophy of science reading group I have been asked to write down my considered position on truthlikeness (verisimilitude) so that the others can probe it before I defend it. Here is where I have landed; what I want from you is an audit, not a lecture on the literature.

(a) Truthlikeness comes in degrees: among strictly false theories, some are closer to the truth than others, and this ordering is an objective matter rather than a matter of taste or convenience.

(b) Newtonian gravitational theory is closer to the truth about the motions of the planets than the Aristotelian picture of natural circular motion, even though both are, strictly speaking, false.

(c) Whether one theory is closer to the truth than another cannot depend on which of two genuinely intertranslatable vocabularies we happen to use to describe the world. A ranking of theories by truthlikeness that flips when I rewrite both theories in a different but expressively equivalent language is not a ranking of the theories at all.

(d) The aim of science is the truth, full stop. Precisely because our best current theories will very likely turn out to be false, scientific progress has to be understood as increasing truthlikeness rather than as the accumulation of truths.

(e) Comparative verdicts such as (b) should be grounded in the content of the theories: roughly, a theory gains in truthlikeness as it captures more true consequences and suffers fewer false ones. This, I take it, is the respectable way to vindicate (b) without appealing to brute intuition.

(f) Judgements of approximate numerical accuracy — that a measured charge lies within some margin of the actual value, for instance — are a special case of the very same notion, not a separate, more primitive one.

My questions: which pairs of these commitments are genuinely in tension, and which only appear to be? Please rank the genuine tensions by severity rather than treating them all as equally bad, and for each one say what would have to give and what the cheapest repair would cost me. Then tell me which single revision would clean up the most. I have no fixed view about which commitment I would surrender first, so do not spare any of them. Please answer in British English.
