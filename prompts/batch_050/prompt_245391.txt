My friend and I spent half the night arguing about Nozick-style experience machines after she said she’d plug in tomorrow if the machine could guarantee a lifetime of happiness, and I definately need a better way to explain why people disagree here. Make a “which theory of well-being do your intuitions actually support?” quiz, formatted as exactly ten numbered items, with one or two sentences per item and no introduction or conclusion: item 1 should set the scenario; items 2–8 should each give one concrete case with two or three answer choices that do not mention theory names; item 9 should give a compact scoring key that sorts answer patterns into experiential hedonism, desire-satisfaction with real-world fulfillment, objective-list pluralism, autonomy-first, and a reality-sensitive hybrid view; and item 10 should flag any answer combinations the quiz cannot classify, plus whether that reflects an incoherent position or just an underspecified quiz. At least one case should involve genuinely loving people inside a simulated setting, one should involve being happy because of a false belief, one should involve freely choosing a temporary machine stay, and one should separate “my desire was fulfilled” from “I merely believe it was fulfilled.”
