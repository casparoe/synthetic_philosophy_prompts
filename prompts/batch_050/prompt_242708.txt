Marcus lost it (in a friendly way). Priya said rounding down is incoherent: a hundred independent one-in-a-million risks of something terrible happening is not zero risk, and anyway we rely on expected value reasoning constantly -- insurance, climate policy, vaccine trials -- so he's really objecting to the math, not to her. Marcus came back with what I thought was his best point: it's suspicious how the numbers work out, since the probabilities always seem to shrink exactly as fast as the payoffs grow, so nobody's actually doing the calculation, they're just using the calculation as permission to do what they already wanted.
