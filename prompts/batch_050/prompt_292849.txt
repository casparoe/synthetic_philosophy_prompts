Think carefully about Hume’s argument before composing; begin by privately brainstorming at least three possible conceptual turns for the poem, then select the one that gives the sonnet’s form the most philosophical work to do. Write in British English.

In *Of the Standard of Taste*, David Hume observes:

“Every voice is united in applauding elegance, propriety, simplicity, spirit in writing; and in blaming fustian, affectation, coldness, and a false brilliant: But when critics come to particulars, this seeming unanimity vanishes; and it is found, that they had affixed a very different meaning to their expressions.”

Write a Petrarchan sonnet of fourteen lines responding to this passage. The octave should dramatise the apparent unanimity produced when critics employ the same evaluative vocabulary; the volta should occur at the moment criticism descends from general terms to a particular object; and the sestet should show how judgements may diverge even where everyone continues to use the same words. Do not represent Hume as concluding either that taste is wholly arbitrary or that no standard of criticism could ever be achieved: leave open the possibility that disciplined or otherwise qualified judges might recover agreement. The poem should be intelligible to a reader unfamiliar with Hume, but precise enough that someone who knows the essay will recognise how its form tracks the passage. Include at least one concrete aesthetic particular rather than relying entirely on abstract critical terms. The quoted passage does not count towards the fourteen lines.

After the poem, add a brief note of no more than seven sentences explaining how its structure interprets Hume. Clearly separate what is settled in the passage from what remains genuinely contested in interpretations of Hume’s wider account, especially concerning whether his standard of taste supports aesthetic objectivity. Identify any interpretative liberties or contested assumptions on which the poem relies, and do not present a disputed reading of Hume as though it were the consensus.
