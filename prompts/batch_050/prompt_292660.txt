I now find the whole situation darkly comic, and I would like a joke about it that I can tell at my sister's birthday dinner next week, where my family will inevitably ask how my "happiness project" is going.

Please write me a joke that turns on the actual structure of this paradox rather than on generic self-help mockery. The joke can be set anywhere you like — a therapy session, a happiness workshop, a first date, a eulogy — so long as the scenario is concrete.
