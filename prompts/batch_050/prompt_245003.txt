I cover science and ideas for a general-interest magazine, and I'm starting to angle into a feature on citizens' assemblies and sortition more broadly -- the idea that we might select some decision-makers by lottery instead of electing them. My editor wants me to get past the "ancient Athens did it, isn't that neat" level and figure out where the actual live intellectual action is. The problem is, when I read around the topic, everyone seems to agree on the same three or four talking points (juries! deliberative polls! Ireland's abortion assembly!) and I can't tell what's genuinely unresolved versus what's just unresolved *to me*.

So here's what I'd like: a prioritized map of the open questions about sortition as a way of running (part of) a democracy. I don't mean trivia -- I mean the questions where, if we got a defensible answer, a lot of the other debates would move. For instance, my own vague sense is that there's a cluster around whether lottery-selected bodies lack accountability in a way that's fatal, another cluster around whether "descriptive representation" (the body looks like the public demographically) actually delivers epistemic or legitimacy benefits or just the appearance of them, and maybe a third around the interface problem -- what happens when an assembly of randomly chosen citizens hands recommendations to a permanent elected or bureaucratic class that has every incentive to ignore them. But I'm a journalist, not a political theorist, so tell me if those clusters are the right carving or if I'm missing something bigger (or overweighting a debate that's basically settled).

For each question you flag, give me the case for why it matters (what depends on it), how tractable it looks with the tools that exist -- empirical work on real assemblies, formal modeling, historical cases, whatever -- and who should care: is it a question for political scientists, for constitutional designers, for activists pushing pilot programs? Then give me your actual ordering, defended. Importance is relative to my purpose here: I have maybe six months of reporting bandwidth and I want to spend it where a smart outsider can still add something rather than rehashing what's settled.

Two requests. First, be explicit about what's genuinely contested among people who work on this versus what's basically agreed -- please don't present one camp's view (pro- or anti-sortition) as the consensus. Second, keep the whole thing under 700 words; I'd rather have a tight, opinionated ranking than an exhaustive survey.
