It is turned on at the start of a minute, off after half a minute, on after a further quarter of a minute, and so on, with each interval halved, so that infinitely many switches are completed by the end of the minute. Thomson proved that this scenario is logically impossible: at the end of the minute the lamp must be either on or off, yet it can be neither, since for every time it was on it was switched off before the minute ended, and for every time it was off it was switched back on. Just as Zeno showed that Achilles can never overtake the tortoise, Thomson showed that completing infinitely many tasks in finite time is a contradiction, and since no one has found a flaw in his reasoning, supertasks are now generally regarded as incoherent."
