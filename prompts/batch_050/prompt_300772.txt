Patent systems in most countries grant an exclusive right to one party even when a later inventor arrived at the same invention entirely independently: independent invention is no defense, and the independent inventor can be enjoined from using what they themselves discovered. Suppose a jurisdiction is considering a reform under which an inventor who can certify independence -- through a clean-room registry that timestamps research milestones and demonstrates no access to the patentee's disclosure -- would receive a non-exclusive right to practice their own invention. Critics object that this destroys the point of priority; defenders reply that priority was only ever a proxy for the labor and insight that patent reward is supposed to track. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence, then give your reasoning with no preamble and no restating of the question: what theory of credit and desert does the existing priority rule presuppose, if any? Does it treat a discovery as a personal achievement to be rewarded, as a scarce event whose first occurrence must be marked for coordinating follow-on investment, or as something more like an inevitable attractor that the community would have reached regardless, making the identity of the discoverer morally arbitrary? Distinguish what the practice strictly presupposes from what it merely fits well with -- could a purely incentive-based or disclosure-based account justify denying the independent inventor any rights, without any claim about desert at all? Then say whether the clean-room reform changes the underlying theory or merely improves its epistemics: if two inventors reach the same result, does rewarding only the first rest on a confusion between being first and being responsible for the discovery's existence, or is there a coherent principle under which timing itself generates desert? Finally, state one case where your reconstruction of the practice's implicit theory predicts a different result than the existing rule, and one case where the practice appears genuinely underdetermined, such that several rival theories would license it unchanged.
