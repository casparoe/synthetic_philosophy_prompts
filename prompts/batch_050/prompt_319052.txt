The splitting questions should be things like: what do you say about someone whose choices are intransitive (prefers A to B, B to C, C to A)? Aim for 6-8 leaves total, note any unoccupied combinations and whether they're empty because incoherent or just unexplored, and flag any pair of positions the tree can't separate. Each leaf gets one sentence on its most awkward consequence.
