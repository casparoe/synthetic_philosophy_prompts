Call this the scale argument. I want to stress-test it by getting your actual numbers rather than another gesture at the literature.

So: give me your credence that a typical adult honeybee has phenomenal states with positive or negative valence -- states it is something like to be in, which can go well or badly for the bee. I want a number, and I want the derivation. Decompose it however you think is correct, but at minimum I'd expect terms like: the probability that some broadly functionalist or computational theory of consciousness is true; the probability, conditional on that theory, that a honeybee's nervous system satisfies its criteria; and whatever term handles the possibility that consciousness exists without valence, since sentience in the morally relevant sense seems to require both. Assign each term a rough probability with one or two lines of justification, show how they combine, and give me the resulting credence.
