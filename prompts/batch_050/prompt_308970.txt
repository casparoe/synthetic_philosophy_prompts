Is there a name for this thing experts do? I got thinking about it after the whole Pluto business came up again at a pub quiz. As far as I can tell, nobody discovered anything new about Pluto itself — no telescope spotted some surprise fact. A group of astronomers sat down, decided on a tidier checklist for what counts as a planet, and Pluto happened to fail the new checklist. Which is fair enough, maybe the checklist really is better for their work. But then it got reported as "Pluto is not a planet", full stop, like a discovery, and people at my quiz were arguing about whether it was "really" a planet as if there was a fact of the matter hiding in space somewhere, rather than a decision taken in a conference room.

Once I noticed it I started seeing it everywhere. My sister's a nurse and she mentioned that when they moved the cutoff line for some weight measure, millions of people became "obese" overnight without gaining a gram, and then the news reported an obesity crisis as if bodies had changed rather than the ruler. And apparently biologists go around saying there's no such thing as a fish, because the category doesn't fit the family tree properly — fine for them, but try telling that to an actual fisherman, his concept works perfectly well for what he needs it for. In each case somebody cleans up a messy word into something precise for their own purposes, which is maybe totally reasonable, but then the cleaned-up version gets used to answer the old questions everyone was asking with the messy word, and the switch goes unmarked. Nobody's lying exactly, and it's not quite "moving the goalposts" either, because nobody's being cheated out of winning an argument — it's more like the map gets redrawn and then read out loud as if it were the territory.

So: does this pattern have an established name? I don't just want a label, I want to know how it's different from nearby things like stipulating a definition, or rebranding something, or plain old vagueness being tidied up. And if there's no name for it, could you coin one with a proper definition, including where the edges are — because sometimes this move seems fine (presumably science has to tighten words up sometimes?) and sometimes it seems like a con, and I'd like to know what seperates the two. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence, then the reasoning — no preamble, don't restate my question back at me.
