Last month my vegetable garden was completely overrun with aphids, so I bought a box of ladybugs and released them. A week later the aphids were basically gone. My neighbor, who gardens too, told me I wasted my money. His reasoning: look, the aphids are gone — so if the ladybugs didn't get rid of them, something else must have (a cold snap, a fungus, whatever), and either way the aphids were on their way out. I keep wanting to reply: no, if I hadn't released the ladybugs, the aphids would still be munching away. But when I say it out loud it sounds weirdly like I'm just contradicting him without evidence, since I obviously can't rerun the month without the ladybugs. Are we actually disagreeing about something, or talking past each other? And who's reasoning better here — I need to decide whether to shell out for ladybugs again next spring.
