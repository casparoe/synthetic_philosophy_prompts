I am a software engineer with no formal philosophy training, and I am applying to a small foundation that funds independent research for a six-month, part-time project on Galen Strawson's Basic Argument against moral responsibility. Please write the one-page proposal for me.

Here is the half-formed idea the proposal should be built around. The Basic Argument, as I understand it, says that since you cannot be responsible for how you are without having intentionally made yourself that way, and since any such self-making would itself have to issue from a way you already were, true responsibility for your actions requires being the ultimate cause of yourself, which is impossible. In my field I deal with a structurally similar-looking problem: bootstrapping. A compiler written in its own language can compile itself, and this is legitimate, not circular in any fatal way; but the same structure is also exactly where unauditable trust can hide, as anyone who has worked on build supply-chain security knows. So the project question is: when an agent endorses or reshapes her own character in light of values she already has, is that self-relation the good kind of bootstrapping or the bad kind? More precisely: does the Basic Argument's regress get its force from assuming that responsibility for an action must be *produced* by a prior exercise of responsibility, and can an account on which responsibility instead concerns the present structure of the agent -- whether her motives are ones she stands behind -- satisfy every demand the argument makes except self-origination, with that leftover demand turning out to do no real work?

The proposal needs to state the research question with a determinate answer; explain why the standard replies leave it open (compatibilists are often accused of changing the subject rather than rebutting the argument, while accounts that stop the regress at self-endorsement face the reply that the agent is still lucky in the self doing the endorsing); and lay out an approach -- my thought is to reconstruct the regress as an explicit induction schema, identify which step each major response attacks, and use the bootstrapping cases to test whether the analogy exposes a genuine option or is merely suggestive. Say what a finished deliverable looks like, what would count as the project failing, why this is conceptual rather than empirical work, and why a funder should pay for it. Keep the scope honest for six months part-time. Do not include a bibliography or a literature review by citation; refer to positions by name or description and I will supply the references myself.
