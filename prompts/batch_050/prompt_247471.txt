So I have zero philosophy training, I'm a nurse who watches too many youtube videos about consciousness at 2am, and I worked this argument out on a napkin and now its bothering me because the conclusion can't be right but I cant find the bad step.

(1) Everything physical that happens in my body, including my mouth moving when I talk and my hand pulling away from a hot pan, has a complete cause in earlier physical stuff: neurons firing, chemistry, whatever. Scientists never once had to write "and then the feeling itself pushed on a neuron."

(2) If my actual inner experience -- the felt pain, the redness of red, the what-its-like part -- caused any physical event in me, then either that event had a gap in its physical explanation, or it had two totally separate sufficient causes stacked on top of each other.

(3) A gap contradicts (1). And everything I ever do having two independent sufficient causes seems like a insane coincidence. So the felt part of experience causes nothing physical. The neurons do all the work.

(4) Then my saying "ouch, that hurts" and "I promise I'm conscious" is NOT caused by the pain or the consciousness. It's caused by neurons that would have fired anyway.

(5) So a creature physically identical to me, atom for atom, but with no inner experience at all -- just lights-out inside -- would also pull its hand back, say "ouch," write napkin arguments, and sincerely insist it feels things, for the exact same reasons I do.

(6) Which means my own conviction that I'm conscious comes out of a process that would produce that same conviction whether or not there was actually anything it's like to be me. So my conviction is no evidence at all. Not even for me, privately.

(7) But come on. If I know anything, I know I'm conscious right now. That's the one thing I can't be wrong about.

(8) So something in (1)-(6) has to give, but every step felt obvious when I wrote it.

Where does this go wrong? If forced, I'd throw out basically anything before (7), and (1) seems like just... physics? So I guess I'm hoping the crack is somewhere in (2)-(6), but I've stared at them and can't find it.
