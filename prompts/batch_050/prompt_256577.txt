I work in a bike shop, no philosophy training or anything, but I've been chewing on something for months and want to know if there's a proper name for it. The pattern: something keeps getting half finished, over and over, and never actually finishes. Three examples. My mate who codes says his app has been "90% done" since March, and every week the last bit seems to split into a new last bit. My garage cleanout: first Saturday I shifted half the junk, next weekend half of what was left, and there is STILL junk in there. And my nephew who's learning guitar says every year he feels about halfway to good, which means "good" keeps moving away from him.

Now, I've heard of the old greek puzzle where the runner has to get halfway first, then halfway again, so he supposably never arrives -- and the standard answer is that all the halves add up to the whole distance, so he does arrive. Fine. But my cases feel different, and I can't say how exactly. The garage halves don't shrink at a steady rate. The last 10% of the app takes longer than the first 90% did. It's like the ancient puzzle but with the numbers bent in one particular direction, the direction where you genuinely never get there.

So: does this real-life version have its own name, or is it just the same paradox with worse arithmetic? If it only sort of fits some existing concepts, tell me which ones come closest and exactly where my thing stops fitting each of them -- a bare label won't help me. And if nothing fits, coin something and define it properly, with a couple of cases that fall just outside it. Also, if I'm mashing two different phenomena together here, say so straight out rather than politely naming the mash.
