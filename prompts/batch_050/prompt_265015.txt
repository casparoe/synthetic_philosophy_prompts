Design me an in-class game that makes them feel the difference between maximizing expected value and maximizing geometric growth -- ideally they should discover on their own balance sheets that a bet can have great expected value and still reliably bankrupt you when you play it repeatedly and bet a fixed fraction each round. Constraints: it has to run with 18 people in 90 minutes including the debrief, no prior knowledge of Kelly assumed, and the bust mechanism has to emerge from the rules, not from me nudging anyone. Keep the whole thing under 800 words.
