Doesn't that mean suffering is just... free-floating neutral stuff, and the whole project of reducing it is pointless? It seems like the doctrine proves too much and accidentally lands you in nihilism, which can't be what the Buddha meant since he clearly cared a lot about how people behave. So did he just not notice this problem, or is there a standard move Buddhists make to get morality back once you've deleted the self?
