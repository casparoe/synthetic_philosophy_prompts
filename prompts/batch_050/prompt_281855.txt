I’m setting a one-semester research agenda on knowing how vs. knowing that. Suppose every skill has three separable dimensions: control (reliably doing the thing), calibration (judging when you can do it), and recovery (adapting when your normal method fails). A person might have lots of true beliefs about one dimension while lacking the other two. Which open question should come first: whether know-how is ultimately propositional, how abilities should be individuated, whether context changes what counts as knowing how, whether testimony or AI assistance can produce know-how, or something else? Defend a dependency-aware ordering: which question is upstream of the others, which is most tractable in a semester, and which would matter least even if settled. Use the control/calibration/recovery distinction where it genuinely clarifies things, but also say if it’s misleading or just repackaging the old debate.
