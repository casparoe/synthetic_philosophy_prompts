I'm not signed up to every detail, but I've come to accept the general position that a theory can be confirmed in a real Bayesian sense on non-empirical grounds, and that this at least partially vindicates the string community's confidence. My advisor thinks I've been hoodwinked, so I want to stress-test it before I commit to it in a paper.
