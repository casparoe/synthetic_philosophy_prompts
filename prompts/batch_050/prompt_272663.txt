Please write a dialogue between two equally sharp engineers arguing about which of these should be the design target for a frontier AI assistant.

Anchor the dispute in a concrete case: a user instructs the assistant to help run a highly effective persuasion campaign for a political cause the user sincerely believes in. The intent-aligned system helps; the value-aligned system might refuse or second-guess. The intent-alignment proponent should argue that having the model override users makes the developer an unaccountable moral arbiter, that user autonomy and ordinary market pressures favor intent alignment, and that misuse is better handled by law and platform policy than by the model's private judgment. The value-alignment proponent should argue that intent alignment merely relocates the problem to whoever controls the intent channel, that flawlessly executing instructions is precisely the failure mode in classic misalignment scenarios, and that a system powerful enough to matter cannot be neutral about ends without being dangerous.

Requirements: genuine back-and-forth where each character answers the other's actual points rather than restating their own; no strawmen on either side. The dialogue must reach a definite verdict — one side should concede the central point rather than both agreeing to disagree — and the reasoning behind the concession should be made explicit. Close with a two-to-three sentence note from you stating where the dialogue oversimplifies or leans on a contested framing of this debate. Keep the entire response under 600 words.
