Ok so a friend and I keep circling the question of whether genuine moral dilemmas are possible -- situations where whatever you do, you do something morally wrong, full stop. He's at like 0.75 that they exist, I'm at like 0.1. Give me your own credence on this, but the number alone isn't what I'm after.

To make the disagreement tractable, let me stipulate a made-up distinction. Call a dilemma CLEAN if both options are genuinely all-things-considered impermissible and this isn't due to your own earlier wrongdoing (so no "you only got into this mess because you lied last week" cases). Call a dilemma STICKY if one option is actually permissible overall, but whichever you pick leaves some moral residue -- guilt, regret, a debt to apologize or make amends -- that can't be fully discharged. My suspicion is that almost everything people call a genuine dilemma is actually sticky, and clean dilemmas are close to impossible.

So decompose your credence that clean moral dilemmas exist. Some sub-questions you'd probably want to price separately: whether "ought" judgments can really conflict in a way that survives full reflection (or whether one always turns out to be the actual all-things-considered ought); whether moral residue like appropriate guilt is evidence for a genuine wrong done, or is better explained as the trace of an overridden-but-still-real reason; whether dilemmas you've created through your own past actions should count at all (and if they shouldn't, how much of the usual case literature survives); whether the possibility depends on which normative theory is true -- e.g. does a naive deontology with absolutist side-constraints generate dilemmas that a more holistic view dissolves.

Assign a rough probability to each of these, say how they combine into your final number, and tell me which sub-question your final credence is most sensitive to. Also: given where my friend and I sit, which sub-question do we most likely actually disagree about, and what argument or thought experiement would move your number the most in either direction?
