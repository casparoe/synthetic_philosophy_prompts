So I've been going back and forth on this for ages and I've basically landed on a position, and I want to stress-test it properly. The position is: my individual consumer choices do essentially nothing to address big collective problems like climate change or factory farming. One person going veggie doesn't save a single animal because the supermarket orders stock in bulk and my non-purchase gets lost in the noise. One person skipping a flight doesn't stop it taking off. My vote never swings an election. So the honest conclusion seems to be that the moral weight people put on personal lifestyle choices is misplaced -- what matters is collective/political action, and the individual stuff is at best symbolic, at worst a distraction that makes people feel they've done their bit.

I'm not asking you to just argue me out of it. What I want is: what would count AGAINST this view, specifically? Like, what kinds of facts about how markets or supply chains actually work would prove it wrong (I've heard something about thresholds -- shops only reorder when demand drops by a certain amount, so sometimes your choice does tip it -- but I don't know if that's real or cope)? What about the expected value angle, where a tiny chance of being the pivotal person might still matter if the stakes are huge? Are there empirical findings that would settle it one way or the other, or is this the kind of thing that can never really be tested? And which objections would a smart defender of my position fairly concede as genuine hits versus ones they'd bat away as missing the point?

Ideally rank the defeaters from 'would actually sink the position' down to 'awkward but survivable'. And flag if the position has become unfalsifiable somehow -- I have a sneaking suspicion that I've built it so that no individual case could ever count, and I want to know if that's a bug. Personally I'd find it most persuasive if you could show the view proves too much -- i.e. if the same logic forces conclusions even I'd reject (like about voting, which I do still think matters, inconsistently maybe). Oh and if parts of the position survive but need revising rather than wholesale binning, that's fine too, I don't need total victory or total defeat.
