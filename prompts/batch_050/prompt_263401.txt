We ended up talking past each other because he thinks in terms of symmetries and field equations and I think in terms of what I do for a living, which is machine learning. So here's my request. Restate Wigner's puzzle and the three or four standard responses to it (the selection-bias deflation, the "math was built for this" historical deflation, the structuralist/maybe-the-world-just-is-mathematical move, and the straight-up mysterian reading) entirely in my vocabulary: hypothesis classes, inductive bias, generalization, overfitting, no-free-lunch theorems, training distributions, model selection, compression.
