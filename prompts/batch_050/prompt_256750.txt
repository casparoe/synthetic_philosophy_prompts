I am in Year 13 and working on an Extended Project Qualification about whether it is safer to develop advanced AI as tools or as agents. Nearly everything I have read takes the distinction for granted and then argues about which side is safer; almost none of it states what makes a system an agent rather than a tool in the first place, and the few authors who do define the terms define them differently. Before I go any further, I want the specification rather than another verdict. Please write the list of desiderata that an account of the tool–agent distinction would have to meet in order to be adequate for my purpose, which is deciding which systems call for oversight regimes and which may be treated as ordinary instruments.

The list should cover, at minimum: the cases any adequate account must classify correctly (please include a thermostat, a sat-nav, a chess engine, a self-driving car, a large language model, and a reinforcement-learning system that pursues an objective over several weeks, since my sources disagree about almost all of these); the distinctions the account may not collapse (for example, between a system having objectives and a user having objectives for it, and between autonomy and raw capability); and the features it may not treat as essential without argument (I am suspicious of any criterion that makes consciousness or embodiment necessary). Give the consideration behind each desideratum — the case or argument that makes it a genuine requirement rather than a prejudice of one camp — and mark which desiderata you take to be non-negotiable and which are contestable.

Two further requests. First, if any pair of desiderata on the list appears jointly unsatisfiable, say so explicitly and explain the tension; I would rather learn that the specification itself is impossible than watch another author quietly abandon one half of it. Second, tell me which single desideratum excludes the most criteria currently in circulation. The candidates I have encountered are: acts without a human in the loop, optimises for an objective across time, maintains an internal model of the world, and would become dangerous if made more capable. A short closing section scoring those four against the list is welcome, and a table is acceptable for that part — but the desiderata themselves are the deliverable, so please keep the scoring brief.
