Give me complete rules, materials, the payoff scheme (no real money), how to handle communication between players (the crux: what can be credibly signaled and what can't), and a debrief that maps what happened in each round onto the hypothesis's key premises -- unverifiable intentions, unpredictable technological growth, chains of suspicion. Also tell me: how to keep the game from collapsing into a standard prisoner's dilemma exercise, what will predictably go wrong the first time I run it, and which feature of the real hypothesis (I suspect the irreversibility of annihilation and the impossibility of repeated play) the game cannot reproduce and I'll have to supply in discussion.
