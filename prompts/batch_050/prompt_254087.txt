For each desideratum, give the case or consideration that motivates it, and mark which you take to be non-negotiable versus a preference of one camp. Then flag any pair of desiderata you suspect is jointly unsatisfiable, and name the single desideratum that knocks out the most existing accounts. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence, then give the reasoning, with no preamble and no restating of the question.
