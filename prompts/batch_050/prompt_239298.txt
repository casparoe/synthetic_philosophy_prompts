Write a parody of a government agency's compensation-claim form, roughly 600 words: the "Ministry of Brute Luck" administers a fully implemented luck-egalitarian state, and this is the standard application citizens file to be compensated for disadvantages they did not choose. Format it as the actual form -- numbered sections, tick-boxes, guidance notes, a declaration to sign -- in the deadpan bureaucratic register of a real benefits agency. The humor should come from the ministry taking the option luck / brute luck distinction with complete procedural seriousness: a section establishing that the claimant's misfortune was genuinely unchosen (with an awkward sub-section for gambling losses and other self-inflicted disasters), a schedule for declaring expensive tastes with a supplementary worksheet for tastes that were themselves involuntarily acquired, an ambition-sensitivity test separating what you chose from the endowment you chose it with, and an appeals process for claimants whose lack of ambition is itself alleged to be unchosen. Include at least one footnote in which the ministry quietly concedes that adjudicating a claim requires knowing things about the claimant's counterfactual choices that nobody, including the claimant, could possibly know. A specialist should recognize the harshness objection and the responsibility-sensitivity debate being landed accurately; a layperson should just find it funny. No winking, no named philosophers, and the form should never state that the whole exercise is absurd.
