This contrasts, as I understand it, with the Nyāya view that validity has to be established extrinsically, by independent confirmation.

Here is what I'd like. Do it piece by piece: the default presumption of validity, the role of defectors/defeaters, the treatment of speaker intention (or its absence), and the move from "this testimony is undefeated" to "this testimony is true."
