Okay so pretend you're a philosophy of mind prof who's taught Dennett's Consciousness Explained a bunch of times and genuinely enjoys the color phi debate. Think carefully here — brainstorm the candidate rules before committing to a prediction, don't just pattern-match to "Dennett said X."

My roommate has been reading this stuff and I've been logging her verdicts on timing-of-experience cases. So far:

1. Classic color phi (red dot flashes, then a green dot flashes nearby, subject reports one dot moving and changing color halfway): "no fact of the matter whether the brain edited the memory afterwards or held the experience back before it happened."
2. The cutaneous rabbit, taps on the wrist then the elbow felt as a little hop up the arm: same verdict, no fact of the matter.
3. Backward masking where the prime never gets consciously seen at all but still speeds up reaction times: "the question doesn't even arise — nothing was experienced, so there's nothing there to revise or delay."
4. Color phi again, but this time the subject gets probed instantly — a tone plays ~200ms after the second dot and she has to say right then what she saw: "then there IS a fact, whatever the probe catches is what she was experiencing at that moment."
5. That old dream case where the sleeper has a long elaborate dream that builds up to and ends with the very event that wakes them (falling headboard, guillotine blade, whatever): no fact of the matter, again.

Now the new case. A subject does color phi with the instant tone probe. At the probe she says: the dot jumped, and it changed color only after it stopped moving — no smooth motion. Two weeks later she vividly and confidently remembers smooth motion with the color changing halfway through, and she insists the memory is right and she just misspoke at the tone. Everything else about the setup is unremarkable.

Predict her verdict. Does she call the memory an Orwellian rewrite, say there's no fact of the matter, say the experience really was as remembered and the probe report was simply wrong, or land somewhere else?
