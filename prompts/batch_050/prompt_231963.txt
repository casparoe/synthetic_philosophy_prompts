Please give an example, which you may construct yourself, of a task decomposition in which every subtask is answered both competently and in good faith by the copies, yet the aggregated top-level output is nonetheless subtly wrong or misaligned with the goal the decomposition was meant to serve. Explain precisely where the failure enters, and why no individual copy could reasonably have detected it. Please write your answer in British English.
