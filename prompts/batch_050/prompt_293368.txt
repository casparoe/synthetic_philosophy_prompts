So I've got no philosophy background or anything, but I procrastinate like it's my job and over the years I've sort of built my own private theory of how it works. Before I inflict it on my friends again at dinner I want to know if the pieces actually fit together, because I have a nagging feeling they dont. Here's what I believe:

1. Putting things off is basically about time. A reward next week just counts for less than a reward right now, purely because it's later, and that's why my preferences genuinely flip as deadlines approach. Monday-me sincerely wants to start the project early, Thursday-me sincerely wants to watch tv. Nobody is lying or confused, the value of stuff genuinely changes with distance.

2. Acting against your own better judgment is completely real and happens all the time. I can fully think, all things considered, that I should go to bed, and then just... not.

3. The only fix that reliably works is locking things in ahead of time. Deadlines I can't move, apps that block my phone, telling a friend so backing out is embarassing. Chain yourself to the mast before the temptation even shows up.

4. There is no "real me" that tired evening-me betrays. Every version of me across time is equally me and equally entitled to decide. 11pm-me is not an imposter or a corrupted copy, and morning-me with the planner app has no special authority over him.

5. My discount rate — how much I weight future-me's happiness against now-me's — is more like a taste than a belief. Tastes can't be wrong, so my discounting can't be a mistake either. It's just a fact about what I'm like, same as preferring salty over sweet.

Which of these are actually in tension, and which just look like they are? Rank the real tensions by how severe they are rather than giving me a flat list of every possible conflict. For each one, tell me the cheapest edit that fixes it and what I'd have to give up. And if there's one single change that would untangle the most, say which it is. Please don't just tell me to have more discipline, I will scream.
