Write an essay on the following dispute about creativity. On Hofstadter's knob-twiddling model, creative acts are basically variations on a theme: every concept comes with a bunch of implicit parameters ("knobs") -- the tense of a story, the size of a teacup, the instrumentation of a song -- and creativity is a matter of noticing some knob that everyone else leaves stuck at its default setting and giving it a twist. Critics object that the *deepest* creativity isn't twiddling existing knobs at all, it's installing new ones: creating the very dimension along which variation becomes possible. Think of whoever first treated silence as a parameter of music, or the first author who made the narrator's reliability itself a variable. The knob-twiddler's standard reply is that "inventing a new knob" is itself just twiddling a higher-order knob -- a knob on the concept's own structure -- so the objection collapses.

Here's a wrinkle to focus your essay around. Imagine two systems. System A is a near-future music AI with a fixed architecture: it has an enormous but frozen list of knobs (tempo, key, dissonance, lyrical topic, timbre, ...) and it explores them brilliantly, producing genuinely suprising songs no human would have written. System B is a fairly mediocre human composer who one day, out of frustration, invents a knob nobody had before: she starts treating the expectations of the specific audience in the room that night as a compositional parameter, feeding their coughs, restlessness, and silences back into the piece as it unfolds. By the knob-twiddling theory, which of these two systems is being more creative, and is that verdict actually the right one?

Your essay should weigh both sides of the underlying question -- is adding a new knob reducible to higher-order knob-twiddling, or is it a genuinly different operation the model can't capture? -- and take a stand. Engage the strongest version of the side you reject before settling. Don't just split the difference with a "it's a spectrum" answer; say which operation you think is doing the real work in cases like System B's, or argue carefully why the reduction goes through.

End your essay with a final line of exactly this form: Verdict: <one short phrase saying whether new-knob invention reduces to higher-order twiddling or is irreducible>
