I run a reading group on the metaphysics of persistence and we've agreed to stop talking past each other. Your job: draft the joint statement of an adversarial colaboration between an endurantist (objects are wholly present at every time at which they exist) and a perdurantist (objects persist by having different temporal parts at different times). Stipulate that both sides accept eternalism, so nobody can win cheap points by fighting about presentism.

Structure it like this:

1. A numbered list of agreed claims both parties can sign without wincing. It must cover, among other things: what a theory of persistence actually has to explain, what Lewis's temporary intrinsics argument does and doesn't establish, what the statue-and-lump case shows even after you grant the loser their preferred escape route, and a joint position on whether relativity's four-dimensional worldtubes settle the debate or are strictly neutral on it (they have to pick, no hedging).

2. A disagreements table with one row per residual crux and columns for: the endurantist formulation, the perdurantist formulation (each phrased so its own side would sign it), and the argument, case, or result both agree would bear on that crux, with each side's prediction written down in advance. Include at least these cruxes: whether stage theory's counting costs (one thing per instant at the podium) are worse than worm theory's coincidence costs, the objection that perdurance is really replacement rather than change, and the thinking-parts / too-many-minds problem.

3. A short confessional section where each side names the single biggest cost of its own view that it concedes on the record.

4. A final verdict, which both must sign, on which parts of the overall dispute (if any) are merely verbal, and what the surviving substantive disagreement actually consists in.

One ground rule for the drafting: neither side gets to describe the other as confused, incoherent, or failing to grasp something. Every formulation has to be one the opposing camp would itself use.
