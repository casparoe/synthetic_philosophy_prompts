I’m in a high school philosophy club (about 12 people, ages 16–18) and we have one 55-minute meeting. Design a low-prep activity about griefbots — chatbots trained to imitate someone who has died — that makes us feel the conflict between comfort, authenticity, consent, and harm instead of just listing those issues. Maybe we have to make decisions using only fragments of the dead person’s messages, then discover what we got wrong. Include rules/materials, roles, a timeline, and a debrief connecting each moment to the ethics. Also say what will definately go wrong the first time and one important part of grief the activity can’t reproduce. Please keep it psychologically safe since some people may have actually lost someone.
