I'm assembling a set of strange loops for a seminar on self-reference, and I want your help extending it. By a strange loop I mean a structure built out of levels — a drawer and a drawn, an utterer and an utterance, a system and its description — where following the hierarchy upward or downward brings you back to where you started, because at some point the level that should be doing the governing or representing turns out to be governed or represented by the level beneath it. My current list has four entries:

1. Escher's Drawing Hands: two hands, each drawing the other, so that neither hand is the undrawn drawer.
2. The liar sentence: a sentence whose truth cannot be assessed without assuming it, and cannot be assumed without assessing it — the level of utterance and the level of truth-evaluation feed into each other.
3. Gödel-style self-reference: a formal system for arithmetic that, through encoding, comes to state facts about its own provability, so that what looked like meta-level commentary about the system becomes an object-level sentence inside it.
4. The self-amending constitution: a rule-making body empowered to alter the very rules that constitute and empower it, so that the constituted level sits above the constituting one.

Two constraints on what I want next. First, I'm deliberately excluding mere infinite regresses — a camera filming its own monitor image, a map containing a smaller copy of itself — since in those cases the levels never actually invert; one level simply keeps generating the next. Second, for any genuine strange loop there should be an identifiable inviolate level: the level that performs the tangling without itself getting tangled. Behind the two hands in the drawing stands Escher's actual hand, which nothing in the picture draws; behind the liar stands whoever chose to assert it; behind the Gödel sentence stands whoever fixed the encoding.

Give me one more strange loop that is not on my list — not a re-skinning, special case, or combination of the four entries — and do three things with it: show that its levels genuinely invert rather than merely regress; identify its inviolate level and say what work that level is doing; and say, honestly, whether it illuminates something the four existing entries don't, or whether on reflection it collapses into one of them.
