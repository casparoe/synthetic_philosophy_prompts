Random chord of a circle: I refuse to answer at all until I'm told how the chord is being generated, which feels right but then I wonder why I don't also refuse on the coin (I don't know the flipping mechanism either!). And drawing a card from a standard deck: 1/4 hearts, no hesitation whatsoever, even though the deck's history is as unknown to me as the die's bias. Is there one principle that sorts these verdicts -- maybe something about causal structure vs. mere ignorance, or about invariance under redescription -- and which verdict does the cleanest version force me to give up?
