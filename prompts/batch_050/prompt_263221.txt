I design data visualizations for a living, mostly dashboards and the occasional infographic, and lately I've been going down a rabbit hole on Otto Neurath and the ISOTYPE picture language he developed in Vienna between the wars. From what I understand, his whole program rested on a few strict rules: quantities should be shown by repeating one identical symbol over and over (ten little worker icons for ten thousand workers) rather than by drawing one big symbol and one small symbol, because people judge area and height badly; the same symbol must always mean the same thing across every chart; no perspective, no shading, no decorative flourish, a minimal palette; and the graphics were meant for museum exhibits aimed at ordinary working people, part of his bigger dream that pictures could be a kind of universal language that crosses barriers of literacy and nationality, tied up with his unity-of-science ambitions.

Here's what I want. My native vocabulary is the grammar-of-graphics way of thinking -- data mapped onto aesthetics like position, size, and color; geoms; scales; layers; facets -- plus the usual design-critique language (chartjunk, data-ink, lie factor, preattentive processing). Restate Neurath's picture-language program in that vocabulary as faithfully as you can. Which of his rules translate cleanly (I'm guessing the repeated-symbols rule is basically a claim that count encoding beats size encoding?), which ones become fuzzy, and which turn out to be two different rules collapsed into one?

Then the part I actually care most about: the residue report. What's left over that the grammar-of-graphics vocabulary can't express at all? My suspicion is that the technical rules survive fine but the whole point of them -- the universal-language ambition, the idea that good design is a form of democractic education, the hostility to treating charts as art -- has no counterpart in my framework, which treats all of that as mere "context." Tell me whether that residue shows my vocabulary is missing something real, or whether it shows that Neurath's program was really a political project wearing a technical costume, and the technical costume is all we kept. Also: is my hunch right that modern viz practice quietly adopted his rules while dropping the philosophy, or is the lineage messier than that?
