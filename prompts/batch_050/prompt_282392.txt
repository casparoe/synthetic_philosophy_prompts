2. If all the guests in the even numbered rooms check out, infinitely many guests have left, but the hotel still has infinitely many guests in it (you can shuffle everyone down and it's full again, whatever).
3. But if instead everyone in rooms above room 1000 checks out, infinitely many guests have left, and now there's only 1000 people in the hotel.
4. So "infinitely many guests leave" can either leave the hotel infinite or leave it with just 1000 people.
5. But in both cases the same number of guests left (infinite), and taking the same amount away from the same amount has to give the same answer. An operation that gives two different answers from identical starting points and identical removals is just broken.
6. So the infinite hotel is impossible. And the hotel is just an infinite set of rooms, so actual infinities in general are impossible.
7. So Cantor's stuff about different sizes of infinity is just pushing symbols around and doesn't describe anything that could exist.
