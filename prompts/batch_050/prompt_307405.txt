On Liberty ties the case for liberty to a society’s level of improvement, so its protections do not fully apply to peoples Mill regarded as barbarous. Having spent his career at the East India Company, he defended Company administration against direct rule from London on the ground that empire could be tolerable only when run as a form of tutelage by officials with specialized knowledge.
