I keep noticing a pattern in the groups I belong to, and I would like to know whether there is an established name for it, or whether I am mashing several different things together. Three examples.

First, the small company where I work treats one kind of client with enormous caution and has built little habits and procedures around them, even though the disastrous project that produced this wariness happened before anyone currently employed here joined. Nobody can tell the original story, yet the company behaves as if it remembers it. Second, in my running club, decisions get made that nobody chose: a consensus forms that every member privately dislikes, each of us says afterwards that we were just going along with the others, and yet "the club wanted it" seems somehow true. Third, I once stood in an airport queue that developed a sullen, hostile mood none of the individuals in it appeared to feel. One-to-one, people were calm, but the line as a whole radiated resentment, and everyone catered to it as though appeasing a person.

What strikes me is that these groups seem to have memories, wishes, and moods that live in no one's head. I have heard "hive mind" used for this sort of thing, but that usually means the members have all started thinking alike or been taken over somehow, and that is not what I am describing: in my cases the members keep their own views and often disagree with the group, while the group-level pattern carries on with a memory and temper of its own. Nor is it just peer pressure, since the memory case outlasts everyone who ever exerted any pressure.

So: is there an established concept for an organized whole that has something like mental states — memories, intentions, moods — that none of its members individually have, and that persists even as every member is gradually replaced? If so, what is it called, and how does it differ from the nearby ideas I might be confusing it with? If not, what are the closest concepts, where does my phenomenon sit among them, and where would its edges be — does a family qualify, a nation, an ant colony? I am not after a bare label; I want to know what the thing is, what it is not, and what would count as evidence that it is really there rather than just a manner of speaking.
