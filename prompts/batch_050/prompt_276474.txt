I run a small online design business, and I’ve been arguing with some people at my coworking space about whether an AI could ever be a legal person in the way a corporation supposedly is. I didn’t study law or philosophy, so I’m trying to sort out the assumptions rather than debate robot consciousness. The proposal that got us stuck was pretty specific: imagine the law allowed someone to create a company with its own bank account, insurance, and property, where an AI purchasing system was authorized to negotiate routine contracts up to a fixed dollar limit. The human owners would maintain the software and could shut it down, but the company’s charter would say they cannot overrule individual purchases just because they dislike them. Customers would know they were dealing with the AI. In my head, this is both exactly like a corporation and completely unlike one, but I can’t articulate why.

What view of legal personhood and responsibility would that arrangement presuppose? Is it treating a legal person as merely a useful container for assets, permissions, and liabilities, or does it quietly assume that every legal act must trace back to some human decision-maker, or that being able to pay damages can substitute for being genuinely answerable? Please distinguish where the corporation analogy really works from where it breaks down: a corporation acts through humans and rigid procedures, while this AI might adapt in ways no particular person intended, though a corporation’s bureaucracy can also produce outcomes nobody intended. Also separate legal status from moral status—would letting the system own property or make contracts imply anything at all about whether anyone could owe duties to it? Finally, say whether the practice is philosophically underdetermined, meaning several different theories might justify it, and explain what would have to change if the law rejected the asset-container view and required one identifiable human guarantor for every AI commitment. Please answer in American English, and don’t give me a bibliography or citations.
