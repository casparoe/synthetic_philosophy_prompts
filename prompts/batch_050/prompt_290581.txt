Next case: a synth player performs that same Mozart sonata live, by hand, but the synth is patched to sound nothing like a piano, more like a choir of flutes. Predict her verdict, say which theory of work identity she's implicitly running (pure sound-structure, score-plus-specified-instrumentation, or something else), and name the one further case that would settle it between the candidates still standing.
