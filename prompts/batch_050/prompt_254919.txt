2. The later historical record of British rule in India, including the Bengal famines under colonial administration and the claim, associated with Amartya Sen, that no substantial famine has ever occurred in a functioning democracy with a free press. Mill's defense of Company rule rested partly on empirical premises about what colonial subjects could not yet do for themselves; I want a reasoned account of whether this evidence would have moved him, and if so, toward what.
