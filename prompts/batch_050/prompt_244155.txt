Aunt Hillary-style question, and I want your actual number: what's your credence that a real ant colony — say a big Argentine ant supercolony — has something like a unified mind? Not just "does information processing" in the trivial sense, but has colony-level states that genuinely play the functional roles of beliefs and desires (and maybe even a faint what-it's-like-ness). Please don't answer "depends on your theory of mind" — give me a number and show me the machinery behind it.

Decompose it roughly like this, though feel free to restructure if you think my carve-up is wrong:
1. The probability that some broadly functionalist theory of mind is correct — i.e., that mindedness is about causal/functional organization rather than substrate or scale.
2. Given functionalism, the probability that ant-colony organization actually clears the bar. This is where I suspect the real action is: colony-level "processing" is extremely slow (information moves at pheromone-and-walking speed, colony decisions take hours to days), the components are themselves minded creatures rather than dumb neurons, and it's not obvious the colony has the right kind of integrated causal structure as opposed to just being a pile of feedback loops with no unified locus.
3. Whatever extra terms you think belong — e.g., whether mindedness requires a causal unity at a particular temporal grain, whether phenomenal consciousness and functional mindedness can come apart, whether having a body/boundary matters.

Then: which term is doing the most work in your final number, and what would shift it?

Two follow-ups I'd like folded in:
- Does it matter to your answer that the colony's parts are themselves arguably sentient-ish? Would your credence move if the colony were built from mindless clockwork ant-robots following identical interaction rules?
- Aunt Hillary can talk to Anteater in plain english, but no real colony has anything like an output channel we could decode. Is that a fact about whether colonies have minds, or only a fact about whether we could ever find out — and should it affect the credence at all?
