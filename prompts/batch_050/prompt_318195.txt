Before I read another one, write me the spec: what would an adequate account of the relationship have to get right? Cover the distinctions the account can't collapse (evaluating actions vs. evaluating character; what's soteriologically effective vs. what's morally right; early Buddhist vs. Mahayana material), and say which requirements are non-negotiable and which are just preferences of the pro- or anti-consequentialist camp. Flag any pair of desiderata that look jointly unsatisfiable, since that's the part I actually care about. Give it to me as exactly seven numbered items, a sentence or two each, no intro and no conclusion, and make one of the items identify the single requirement that knocks out the most popular readings.
