I'm preparing for a qualifying exam in epistemology and have been trying to sort out what the preface paradox actually demands of me as a believer, and I've run into a disagreement with a study partner that I suspect reveals a gap in my own understanding. My study partner objects that this misunderstands what the paradox shows, but she struggles to say exactly why the retraction strategy is wrong rather than merely costly.

Could you walk me through this?
