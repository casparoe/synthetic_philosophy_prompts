I'm in a behavioral ecology course and last week we did Zahavi's handicap principle -- peacock tails, gazelle stotting, all that. The prof explained it with an analogy she clearly loves, because she used it like four times: a costly signal is like a security deposit on an apartment. Only a tenant who actually intends to take care of the place can comfortably hand over the deposit, so just posting it proves you're the good type, and the landlord doesn't need to investigate you further. She then used this to argue that handicap signals are "basically escrow" and that the wastefulness of the signal is sort of incidental -- what matters is that the signaler puts something valuable on the line, same as a renter.

This felt off to me but I can't fully articulate why. For one thing, you get a deposit BACK if you behave, and nobody gives the peacock his tailfeathers back for mating successfully, lol. But I suspect there's deeper problems than that.

So: audit the analogy. Give me the correspondence table first -- what plays the role of the landlord, the tenant, the deposit, the good tenant vs bad tenant types, getting the deposit returned -- and be honest about which slots in the signaling case have no real occupant. Then go through the disanalogies and separate the ones that are just cosmetic from the ones that matter for the specific conclusion she drew. Some things I've been chewing on, but tell me if I've got them right or wrong:

1. A deposit costs the good tenant and the bad tenant the same amount of money -- isn't the whole point of the handicap principle that the cost falls DIFFERENTLY on different quality types?
2. A deposit only works because there's a whole institution (contract law, courts, norms) guaranteeing you lose it if you trash the place. Nobody enforces anything on the peacock.
3. If the signal's wastefulness is "incidental" like she said, could we replace costly displays with some refundable arrangement in an animal system, or does that dissolve the whole thing?

At the end: does her actual claim -- that handicap signaling is self-certifying honesty, no further inspection needed -- survive once the disanalogies are on the table, or was the deposit analogy carrying that conclusion the whole time?
