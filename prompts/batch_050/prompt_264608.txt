For the past year I've been keeping a private forecasting journal, and one long-running entry has me stuck. The proposition is this: "By 2075, the United States will no longer be the world's single most powerful state, judging by the combination of military reach, reserve-currency status, and technological output." My credence is about 0.75. A friend of mine who reads a lot of history puts hers at 0.40, but not because she's more optimistic about America — she thinks the whole exercise of forecasting from past empires is nearly worthless. Her argument is that the reference-class problem is unsolvable: depending on whether you count Rome, Byzantium, the Ottomans, Spain, the Netherlands, Britain, or only "industrial-era hegemons," and depending on how you define decline, you can extract almost any base rate you like, so the outside view is decorative and you should just reason from current fundamentals.

Give me your own credence on the proposition, but more importantly decompose it: (1) the probability that historical base rates for dominant powers meaningfully apply at all to a nuclear-armed, democratic, reserve-currency-issuing state embedded in a deeply interdependent global economy — that is, the probability that the reference class contains genuine analogues rather than superficially similar cases; (2) conditional on the outside view applying, the rough base rate you'd extract for a hegemon losing primacy within about fifty years of its peak, and a word on which historical cases you'd actually include in the class; (3) how far the inside-view particulars (debt, polarization, demographics, alliance structure) should shift that base rate, and in which direction; and (4) a term for your own model uncertainty about how the previous terms ought to combine. Give a number and one line of justification for each, then state the combined figure and which single term it is most sensitive to. Finally, say whether my friend's disagreement with me most likely lives in term (1) or term (4), and what argument or evidence would move your own number the most. Please keep the whole response under 400 words.
