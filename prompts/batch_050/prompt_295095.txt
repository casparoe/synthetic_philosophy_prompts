At our local rationality meetup last week, two people got into it over evidential cooperation in large worlds and I somehow ended up as referee. Can you adjudicate?

Mira's position: in a big enough universe there are tons of agents decision-theoretically similar to me, so my one-shot choice is evidence about what they're all doing. Hence cooperate in an anonymous one-shot prisoner's dilemma with a stranger I'll never see again — not because it causes anything, but because defecting would be evidence that my counterparts everywhere defect too.

Daniel raised three objections. (1) The argument proves too much: I also correlate with hostile agents who model me, so the same logic says precommit to being nasty so their evidence about me deters them, and the theory gives no principled account of which correlations "count." (2) "Similar enough" does all the work — with no measure over which agents I'm correlated with and how strongly, the prescription is unfalsifiable; you can justify anything by picking the reference class. (3) What real correlation exists between me and other terrestrial cooperators is causal anyway (we read the same blogs, same culture), so evidential cooperation is just ordinary causal coordination wearing a trench coat.

Mira's replies: on (1), an adversary predicting you is just Newcomb, and standard EDT/FDT reasoning handles that — nobody abandons the transparent-box logic because predictors exist. On (2), every decision theory that talks about copies has the measure problem, including FDT's "subjunctive dependence," so it's not a special defect of the large-worlds version. On (3), causal and evidential channels stack rather than compete, and the large-worlds claim never needed terrestrial correlation anyway.

Who's right on each of the three points, specifically? And is Mira's basic move — treating "my decision is evidence about distant similar agents" as a reason at all — actually load-bearing, or does the whole thing collapse into a fight over reference classes that decision theory has no tools to settle?
