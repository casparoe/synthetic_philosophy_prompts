Could you restate both positions, as faithfully as you can, in the vocabulary of my statistics course: probability distributions over defined populations, reference classes and how they are chosen, deviation from a mean or typical value, and the drawing of thresholds for classification? Finally, I want your verdict on the residue: does what fails to survive translation show that my statistical vocabulary is missing something the concept of disease genuinely requires, or does it show that the philosophers' disagreement is partly less substantive than it looks?
