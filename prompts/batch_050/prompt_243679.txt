I run a small outdoor film night on a big meadow. This year we compared the camera headcount with the ticket scans and it looks like roughly 2 out of 3 people in the audience snuck in through a gap in the fence without paying. Near the end I spot a guy packing up his blanket and I ask to see his ticket. He can't produce one, but he says he paid cash at the entrance (we do sell cash tickets and don't give receipts, so there's no way to check). So purely statistically, he's more likely than not one of the gatecrashers. Can I justifiably make him pay on the spot, or even ban him from future screenings? And if not, what kind of evidence would actually be enough? I'm a bit stuck because it seems like the only thing I'll ever know about anyone in that crowd is the exact same 2/3 statistic, so if that isn't enough, no one could ever be held responsible at all.
