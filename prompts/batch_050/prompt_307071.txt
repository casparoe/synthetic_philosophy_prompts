Which two or three open questions should most structure a paper on making subsidiarity compatible with both self-government and effective problem-solving across these systems? Defend your ordering by importance, tractability, and how many other questions depend on each answer, and identify one finding or counterexample that would change it. I am looking for a defended agenda, not a survey or bibliography.
