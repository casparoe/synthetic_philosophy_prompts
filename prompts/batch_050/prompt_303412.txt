My opening move: fine, suppose rejecting disjunctive syllogism really does save you from the liar. What about Curry? Take the sentence "if this sentence is true, then everything is true". From modus ponens and ordinary conditional proof alone you can derive that everything is true -- no disjunctive syllogism anywhere in sight. So paraconsistency by itself doesn't save you; you also have to break the conditional, and once you've broken the conditional, what reasoning do you even have left to state your own view with?
