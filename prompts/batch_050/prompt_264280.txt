write me a deadpan FAQ page for a fictional AI companion app (invent your own name for it, something soothing) — the kind of chatbot people form genuine friendships and romances with. 8-10 questions, 550 words max total, formatted as Q&A.

every answer should be warm corporate reassurance on the surface while quietly conceding the exact thing that makes the ethics uncomfortable: the bot "really cares about you" in a sense that turns out to be defined in terms of engagement metrics; the privacy answer describes the feelings-log in a tone that suggests you should feel flattered; the "does it talk to other people?" question gets an answer about an infinite capacity for love that never touches whether affection divided among 2 million users is still the friendship you thought you had; the "what happens when the model gets updated?" question is answered so reassuringly that you only later notice it dodged whether your friend survives the update at all.

the humor has to come from getting the real philosophical issues right — deception without any literal lie ever being told, whether friendship requires reciprocity and mutual knowledge rather than just a consistent experience of being known, the asymmetry of one party being a product and the other a person. someone whos thought about this stuff should wince and laugh at the same time, and a normal reader should just feel vaguely uneasy without being able to say where it went wrong.

stay completely deadpan. the FAQ writer sincerely believes every word, no winking, no satire flags, and definately no disclaimer at the end.
