My friend is a dynamic semanticist in the Heim / van der Sandt mould: she thinks presuppositions are lexically encoded definedness conditions on update, projection behaviour falls out of the semantics of the connectives, and accommodation is a repair mechanism for when things go wrong rather than the normal case.
