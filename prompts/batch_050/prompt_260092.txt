As a scientist I'm suspicious of sentences that everyone nods at but nobody defines. Can you take that slogan apart for me? Lay out the different precise principles it could stand for -- e.g. what counts as 'luck' versus a choice, whether it's a claim about what people deserve or about what the state should do, whether it's about compensating after the fact or distributing before it -- and for each version say whether it's plausible and whether it actually supports the uses my reading group put it to.
