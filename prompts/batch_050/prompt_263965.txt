Here's my problem: per calorie, chicken means something like a hundred times more individual animals, each living a much worse life than a steer, so the swap might actually increase total suffering even though it wins on emissions. Meanwhile the pro-beef faction keeps citing the mice and voles killed in crop harvesting, arguing that nobody's hands are clean anyway. Draft the memo I'd send the full committee recommending whether we pass the swap as written, amend it, or reject it -- do the lives-per-calorie math openly rather than hand-waving it, and take the crop-deaths argument seriously rather than waving it off as whataboutery. Format it as exactly five numbered points, one or two sentences each, with no intro or sign-off: the first point states the recommendation, and the last point gives the strongest argument on the losing side and why it doesn't flip the verdict.
