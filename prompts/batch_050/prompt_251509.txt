The setup, to make sure we're on the same page: Beauty is put to sleep Sunday, a fair coin is flipped, she's woken Monday either way, woken Tuesday as well iff tails, and her memory of each waking is erased. On waking, what should her credence in heads be? I say 1/2 -- she knew the whole setup in advance, waking up gives her no new evidence she didn't already know she'd get, so nothing has changed since Sunday.
