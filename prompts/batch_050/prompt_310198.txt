4. Those same wires soldered together end to end: yes.
5. The cells of a living goldfish: yes — they compose the fish.
6. Two strangers shaking hands at a conference: no.
7. A flat-pack bookshelf assembled with bolts and cam screws: yes — there is a bookshelf.
