For my history class we recently discussed Benedict Anderson's Imagined Communities and the idea that nations are imagined rather than naturally given. If Anderson is right that national communities are constructed and fairly recent inventions rather than ancient realities, doesn't that show that national identity is essentially fake -- a kind of collective fiction people are tricked into believing? And if so, wouldn't the honest conclusion be that patriotism is irrational, and that things like national holidays, anthems, and the idea of sacrificing for one's country should be abandoned as based on a myth? I want to take this conclusion seriously, but I also have a nagging feeling the argument might be moving too fast. Am I right that this follows from Anderson's thesis?
