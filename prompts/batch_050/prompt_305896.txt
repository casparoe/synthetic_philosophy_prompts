The topic is the epistemic regress, but I want to steer her away from the usual trilemma bookkeeping (foundationalism has arbitrary stopping points, coherentism is circular, infinitism needs infinite minds, pick your poison) toward a half-formed thought I had: all three positions seem to assume that a belief's justification has to be somehow completed -- the chain terminates, closes into a web, or runs to infinity -- when really what a finite agent can ever do is extend a chain of reasons a few steps on demand.
