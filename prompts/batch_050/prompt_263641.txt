After her husband’s death, my aunt has several times woken just before dawn with a vivid sense that he is sitting beside the bed and reassuring her. A grief counselor says such experiences are not unusual: bereavement, expectation, and sleep disruption can make the mind retain a model of a person who has died. Her pastor responds that this explanation may describe how the experience occurred without settling whether God also meant it as a comfort. My aunt now asks me whether she should regard the episodes as evidence that her husband survives death, as nothing more than symptoms of grief, or as spiritually significant in some way compatible with both views. How should I help her think through those options, and what would count for or against treating the experiences as evidence?
