I have been worrying about a puzzle concerning my own decisions. When I am genuinely deliberating — say, about whether to move to another city — it feels impossible to place odds on what I will decide: the moment I become confident that I will stay, the deliberation seems finished rather than predicted. A bookish friend has suggested a compromise: before deliberating, write down your probabilities, seal them in an envelope, and open it afterwards, so that prediction and deliberation each get their turn. Something about this feels like cheating, but I cannot articulate why.
