Here's my opening move. Fictions are silent wherever the author left them silent — nothing in the Holmes stories settles whether Holmes has an odd or even number of hairs on his head, and you fictionalists are usually the ones making that point yourselves. So: nobody has ever computed the 10^100th decimal digit of pi, and plausibly nobody ever will. What, on your view, makes it the case that that digit is whatever it is? If you say our rules for computing pi settle it, explain how rules invented by finite humans reach a case no human will ever apply them to — and if you say the rules just do cover every case, all by themselves, that starts to sound an awful lot like the platonic heaven you told me doesn't exist. If instead you bite the bullet and say it's genuinely unsettled, then when someone finally computes the digit they've created a truth rather than discovered one, and I want to watch you defend that with a straight face.
