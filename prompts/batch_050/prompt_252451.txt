Write a short story set in some niche hobby community with its own specialized vocabulary — speedruners, sourdough bakers, mechanical keyboard nerds, urban birders, competitive rose growers, whatever you like — where one of the community's category terms has quietly stopped meaning one thing. The setup: the term started out with a pretty clear criterion, but because everyone kept defining it against a neighboring category, its meaning drifted until it was basically copying that neighbor's criteria in some contexts while keeping the old meaning in others. Nobody ever voted on this, nobody announced it, different subgroups just slid into different usages while all believing they're using the word the same way as everyone else.

The story should turn on this slippage becoming suddenly load-bearing: a contest entry, a leaderboard submission, a big ebay sale, a bet, an inheritance of a prized specimen — some situation where money or pride now depends on what the term means, and where both sides are being totally honest and can each point to years of "their" usage. Don't include a philosopher character who lectures about concepts or definitions; let the confusion surface through dialogue and events (an argument on a discord server works great). The interesting part to dramatize: there's no fact of the matter hiding anywhere that could settle it, no original rulebook, just usage that forked without anyone noticing.

End with the community having to actually do something — make a ruling, split the category, let it ride — not just agree to disagree. Somewhere around 600-900 words.
