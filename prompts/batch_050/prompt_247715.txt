Write me the FAQ page for a fictional griefbot startup -- one of those services that trains a chatbot on a dead person's texts, emails and voicemails so the family can keep talking to them. Deadpan, soothing corporate FAQ voice throughout, maybe 600 words. I want questions like "Can I upgrade to the video avatar tier?", "Why did Mom say something she never said in life?", "Can my husband opt out after he passes?", and "What happens to Grandpa if we stop paying?" The satire should land the actual philosophical issues -- whether the dead can consent to being simulated, whether the bot is them or a puppet made of their data, what it means that your grief is a recurring subscription and the model gets retrained on your conversations, whether updates that make the dead person "more accurate" are restoring them or overwriting them -- but only ever through the answers' cheerful logic, never by winking at the reader or naming any philosophers. The final question should be something like "Does it hurt them when I delete the account?" and the answer should be perfectly reassuring and completely devastating. Keep it legible enough that someone who knows the literature can tell exactly which real positions and arguments each answer is skewering.
