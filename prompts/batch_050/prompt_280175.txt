My opening move: there is no deep asymmetry to explain, because the two predicates are not on a par to begin with. A predicate whose very definition builds in a switch point obviously cannot be confirmed by observations made entirely on one side of that point — that is a trivial semantic artifact, not a crisis for induction. The new riddle therefore collapses into the unremarkable observation that one can define bizarre predicates. Where exactly does this reply fail?
