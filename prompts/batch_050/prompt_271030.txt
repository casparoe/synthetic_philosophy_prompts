The dilemma is essentially a special case of Condorcet's voting paradox applied to propositions instead of candidates, and its practical upshot is that panel-based institutions like courts, expert committees, and boards cannot reliably reach rational collective decisions by voting."

Could you go through the summary and indicate which parts a philosopher working on judgment aggregation would accept, which parts conflate distinct results or overstate what the dilemma shows, and whether the closing practical claim actually follows from the accurate parts?
