At an EA meetup someone kept saying that human extinction would be bad “over and above the deaths it involves.” They used it to defend a strong claim about this case: a harmless biotech accident makes every living person permanently infertile, but nobody is killed, injured, deceived, or made unhappy; everyone then gets a long and good natural life. Call it the painless sunset. According to them, this would still be a catastrophe rather than merely an oddly quiet ending.

I have the intuition too, but I think the slogan is smuggling together several different claims. Can you unpack the precise theses it might express, from weak to radical? Please explicitly distinguish, where relevant:

- the loss of happiness or other goods that future people would otherwise have enjoyed;
- whether merely possible people can be harmed or wronged by never existing;
- the disappearance of humanity as a continuing collective project, bearer of knowledge, or subject of history;
- Scheffler-style claims that the value or meaning of what we do now depends on there actually being people after us;
- the weaker claim that present meaning depends only on our believing there will be successors;
- the loss of relationships, traditions, achievements, or inherited obligations connecting past, present, and future people.

For each reading, say whether it is close to a platitude, genuinely controversial, or probably false. Then say which readings would actually support calling the painless sunset a catastrophe, as opposed to supporting only the weaker claim that ordinary continuation is preferable. I’m especially interested in what follows from comparing the painless sunset with a second world in which humanity still ends, but everyone is convincingly fooled into thinking it will continue.

Don’t give me a bibliography or just summarize famous positions; I want the disambiguation and your verdicts. If the case doesn’t isolate the issue I think it does, or if “bad over and above the deaths” is too muddled to evaluate without adding assumptions, say so bluntly and fix the comparison rather than politely going along with the framing.
