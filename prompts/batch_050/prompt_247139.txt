I keep getting into arguments about who deserves what — pay at work, college admissions, whether rich founders earned it, whatever — and I want to figure out if my gut judgements add up to anything coherent. Here they are:

1. Two coders ship the same feature at the same quality. One grinds for months, the other dashed it off over a weekend because they're just brilliant. Gut: the grinder deserves more praise and maybe more pay, even though the output is identical.

2. A founder works insanely hard for four years and her company folds through no fault of hers — the market just moved. A second founder does literally the same things and gets rich. Gut: the second one deserves more money than the first. Which seems to contradict #1, since the effort was the same.

3. A surgeon whose skill saves hundreds of lives a year — I think she deserves a lot, even if it came easily to her and she'd be bored doing anything else. So contribution matters even without sacrifice? That feels like it contradicts #1 from the other direction.

4. Someone who is only hard-working because their parents drilled it into them, and would've been lazy raised in a different family — I still think they deserve the rewards of the hard work. Even though, if I'm honest, I also think nobody deserves their upbringing or their temperament.

5. A plumber during a citywide shortage charges triple and cleans up. Gut: he doesn't deserve the premium, that's just scarcity luck. But he did work hard for it, and he took the jobs nobody else wanted.

6. Student A starts the semester failing and claws her way up to a B+; student B coasts on talent to an A-. Gut: A deserves the better grade. Which is weird, because grades are supposedly measuring performance, not improvement.

My tentative principle was: people deserve in proportion to what it cost them, not what they produced. But that clearly gives the wrong answer on #3 (the surgeon barely sacrificed anything) and maybe on #5 (the plumber's costs were real). So either fix it or replace it. I'd like the principle stated precisely enough that I can tell which of my six verdicts it keeps and which ones it forces me to drop — I suspect at least one of them has to go, I just can't tell which. And tell me what the repaired version says about the case I actually care about: my coworker, who's clearly more talented than me but puts in about half the hours, just got the same bonus I did. Should I be annoyed or not?
