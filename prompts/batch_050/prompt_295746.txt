Victimhood cultures also treat victimization as conferring moral standing, but they encourage people to publicize grievances and recruit third parties. Honor cultures are consequently prone to violence, while victimhood cultures reward hypersensitivity and competitive claims of fragility. Dignity culture is the morally superior arrangement because it alone recognizes equal human worth, although its reliance on institutions may weaken courage and self-reliance.”
