Instead of asking the usual “would Nazi Germany have happened without Hitler?”, give me a causal weighting for these explanations: Weimar’s weak institutions, post-Versailles nationalism and antisemitism, the Depression, conservative elites thinking they could control the far right, Hitler’s personal talents and obsessions, and sheer contingency. Assign each a percentage adding to 100, and say how the weights would change under three counterfactuals: Hitler dies in 1919, the Beer Hall Putsch succeeds, and Hindenburg refuses to appoint him in 1933. Don’t just say it was a mix — I’m interested in whether Hitler was mainly a catalyst, an amplifier, or genuinely irreplaceable.
