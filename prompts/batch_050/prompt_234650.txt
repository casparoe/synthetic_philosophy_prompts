I'm moderating the Q&A afterward and want a sequence of eight to ten questions, each with a follow-up keyed to the reply she is most likely to give. I'd like the sequence to press on the points where her middle position is least stable: whether the appeal to naturalness smuggles in a metaphysical privilege that a good Quinean should reject; how her view handles the original gavagai scenario, where the linguist's evidence seems indifferent between rabbits, rabbit-stages, and undetached rabbit parts without any obvious kind-naturalness tiebreaker; and whether accepting inscrutability for terms while denying it for sentences is coherent given that sentence meanings are compositional. I'd also like one question on how she responds to the charge that her position just relocates indeterminacy from translation to the choice of a naturalness ranking, and a closing question about what evidence could, in principle, settle matters between her view and full-strength Quinean indeterminacy. Please avoid generic questions about her background or motivations.
