Write a deadpan employee-wellness pamphlet, around 600 words, from the Department of Finite Modes in a universe governed by Spinoza’s metaphysics. It should encourage workers to stop imagining that last quarter could have gone otherwise and to cultivate the intellectual love of Nature, while satirising both corporate wellness-speak and lazy pop-Spinozism. Get the philosophy right: God/Nature doesn’t deliberate or care; finite modes follow necessarily; freedom means understanding necessity rather than escaping it; and adequate ideas can genuinely alter what a mode does. Include an FAQ on counterfactuals, blame, burnout, and why issuing the pamphlet isn’t pointless in a necessitarian world—after all, it too is a necessitated cause. No quotations, proposition numbers, or tidy inspirational ending; let the creepiness remain.
