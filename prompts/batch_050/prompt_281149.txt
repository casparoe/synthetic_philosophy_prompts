It's an ugly conclusion but I think the reasoning is watertight, and the standard escapes all look like special pleading to me. What would count against this view? Also tell me what defenders of the line (I'm thinking of the people who bite the bullet and endorse "rational dogmatism") themselves concede would sink it, not just what critics say. And one thing I'm honestly unsure about: does the argument only go through at outright certainty/credence 1, and if so, is that a refuge or just a sign the paradox was never about real knowers to begin with? I'd be most persuaded by something showing the argument equivocates between two senses of "knowing the evidence is misleading," if such an equivocation is actually there.
