Theres a passage where the author sums up Keynes's early work on probability like this:

'Before he was an economist, Keynes wrote a philosophy book arguing that probability isn't a number at all. His conclusion was that since we can never assign real probabilities to future events, all reasoning about the future is basically guesswork driven by waves of optimism and pessimism, which he later called animal spirits.
