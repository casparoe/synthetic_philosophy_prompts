(2) Cognitions are intrinsically valid: when you have a cognition, it comes with its validity already attached, and you don't need a second cognition to certify the first; falsity is what needs explaining (a defect in the source), not truth. (3) Testimony (śabda) is its own pramāṇa, irreducible to perception or inference -- you don't learn what the speaker said by inferring from their track record.
