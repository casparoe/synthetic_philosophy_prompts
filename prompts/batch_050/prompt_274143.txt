My idea, roughly: split them into teams of 4 and hand every team the same one-page almanac for a fictional planet covering about a century -- invented numbers for war deaths, homicides, executions, slavery, workplace deaths, domestic abuse reports, life expectancy, population, that kind of thing. Include the timeline for the session, the exact task instructions, and a debrief that maps each team's pitch onto the real levers (choice of metric, definition of violence, baseline year, whether reporting artifacts count as evidence) without me lecturing first. Also tell me what will predictably go wrong -- like teams converging on the same metric anyway, or someone refusing to defend their assigned side -- and one honest note on what a canned fictional dataset can't capture about the real Pinker-style debate that I'd have to supply myself.
