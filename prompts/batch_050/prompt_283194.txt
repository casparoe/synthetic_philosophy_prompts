For a working group on normative uncertainty, I have been asked to circulate a statement of my commitments in advance so that the other members can stress-test it. Here is what I currently hold:

(1) The rational response to normative uncertainty is to maximize expected choiceworthiness: form credences over first-order moral theories and choose the option that scores best on the credence-weighted average of how the theories rate it.

(2) Because theories measure value in incommensurable units, their choiceworthiness scores must be normalized before averaging. I normalize by the difference, within each theory, between the best- and worst-available options in the choice situation at hand.

(3) Moral trade is often mutually beneficial and ought to be pursued: where you and I disagree about which cause matters most, I can direct resources toward your favored cause in exchange for your directing resources toward mine, and this remains true even when I take the other party's values to be mistaken.

(4) Whether a proposed trade is worth taking should not depend on features of the situation that neither party's moral view cares about — who happened to propose it, what other options happen to be on the table, and so forth. A genuinely mutually beneficial trade should be taken wherever it appears.

(5) Fanaticism is unacceptable: no theory assigned a small credence should be able to dominate my deliberation merely by assigning astronomical value to some outcome, and any adequate account of decision-making under normative uncertainty must have the resources to block this.

Please audit the set for joint tenability. Which pairs of commitments are in genuine tension, and how severe is each tension? For each, state precisely what generates the conflict and what the cheapest fix would be, including what that fix costs me elsewhere in the set. Please also identify any pairs that merely appear to conflict but in fact cohere, so that I can distinguish real problems from surface ones. Rank the genuine tensions by severity rather than treating them as on a par, and close by identifying the single revision that would do the most to restore coherence. Please keep the entire response under 450 words.
