Okay so I'm putting together a small workshop on the criteria of authenticity in historical Jesus research and I need a defended position to argue against. Here's the state of play as I understand it: the old toolkit — multiple attestation, embarrassment, dissimilarity, coherence, maybe historical plausibility — took a beating over the past couple decades (critics say the criteria assume a view of memory we no longer accept, that they generate contradictions between scholars, that "dissimilarity" especially produces a Jesus discontinuous with both Judaism and the early church, which is suspicious on its face). But nothing has actually replaced it. People just keep using pieces of the toolkit while apologizing.

So here's what I want. If you could only keep TWO criteria as genuinely load-bearing tools for sifting the tradition, which two, and why? For each one: state the strongest objection to it, and explain why it survives anyway — or what restricted form it needs to take to survive (e.g. is embarrassment fine for episodes but useless for sayings?). Then defend the cuts: which commonly-used criterion is the most overrated relative to its reputation, i.e. does the least actual work, and is there any underused criterion or proxy you'd promote onto the shortlist instead?

One wrinkle you should engage with: there's a proposal floating around to stop applying criteria to individual sayings or deeds and instead use them to establish *general features* of the tradition — recurring themes, characteristic patterns of behavior, what kind of teacher Jesus plausibly was — and then judge particular items by their fit with that profile. You can build your shortlist on that picture, or argue it just relocates the same problems one level up, but don't ignore it.

Assume the goal is producing defensible claims about what goes back to the historical Jesus for a serious general-audience book — not satisfying every methodological purist, but not embarassing yourself in front of specialists either. I'm not asking for a survey of the debate, I want the actual shortlist with the reasoning.
