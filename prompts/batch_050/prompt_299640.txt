Write an essay on the following controversy in game theory: Is iterated elimination of weakly dominated strategies a defensible way to solve games? Unlike iterated elimination of strictly dominated strategies — which preserves all Nash equilibria and is independent of the order of elimination — eliminating weakly dominated strategies can delete Nash equilibria, can yield different outcomes depending on the elimination order, and can erase every equilibrium of a game in some cases. Defenders appeal to the idea that rational players should never treat a strategy as a serious option when another strategy is never worse and sometimes better, or invoke trembles and cautious tie-breaking to repair the problems. Critics argue that weak dominance implicitly assumes conjectures about opponents that players may have no reason to hold, and that the order-dependence reveals that the procedure encodes arbitrary choices rather than rational requirements. Weigh the strongest considerations on each side, but do not end on a non-committal note — reach and justify a verdict on when, if ever, the procedure should be applied. At the end, state how confident you are in your verdict and identify the single point in your argument you are least sure of. Write in American English.
