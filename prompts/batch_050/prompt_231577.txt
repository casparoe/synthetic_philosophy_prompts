Write a pantoum about Goodhart's law and specification gaming, in the voice of a hospital administrator who spent a decade setting performance targets for an emergency department and now understands what she did.

The form should do the conceptual work rather than merely decorate it. A pantoum recycles whole lines verbatim: the second and fourth lines of each stanza return as the first and third lines of the next, and in the closing stanza the two lines of the opening quatrain that were never repeated finally come back, so that the poem ends on its own first line. Use that machinery to enact the law instead of describing it. The recurring lines should be the language of measurement itself — the target as it was originally drafted, the slogan from the rollout posters, the stock phrase from the annual review — and each time a line returns in a new stanza, its new context should make it mean something worse. What begins as a proxy for good care should read, by its third recurrence, as an instruction manual for hollowing good care out. Someone familiar with the idea should be able to watch the measure become a target purely from how the same words change weight across the poem; someone unfamiliar with it should still read the poem as the story of an institution slowly optimizing itself around a loophole.

Get the substance right. Specification gaming is not laziness or dishonesty; it is competent people discovering that the letter of a metric can be satisfied at a fraction of the cost of its spirit. Let the middle stanzas show the repertoire with clinical precision: the clock that starts only when a patient crosses the threshold, so ambulances idle outside with patients still inside them; the triage that degrades into a greeting and a signature so the numbers register a response; the categories quietly redefined; the dashboards improving as the wards get worse. Do not resolve the poem into either "metrics are evil" or "the staff failed." The administrator should end in the harder position: that any number she could have chosen was gameable from the moment it was chosen, and that the damage was done not despite the measure's success but through it. The final repetition of the opening line should land as a diagnosis, not a resignation.

Constraints: at least seven quatrains; observe the interlocking repetition scheme exactly, including the closing stanza's return of both unrepeated lines from the first; the repeated lines may be re-punctuated or re-contextualized but not re-worded; American English throughout. The result should be accurate enough that someone who works on performance measurement would nod along, and strong enough as a poem that someone who doesn't would still finish it.
