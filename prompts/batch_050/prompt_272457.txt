I've been keeping a private list of deceptive remarks I or people around me have made, and I've written down my gut verdict on each. I'd like to know whether a coherent principle about lying versus misleading sits behind them, or whether some verdicts have to go.

My cases and verdicts: (1) Asked in a job interview whether I was ever fired, I say "No," though I was. Clearly wrong. (2) In the same interview I instead answer "My last employer and I parted ways over a disagreement," which is technically true -- the disagreement was my firing. Still wrong, but to my mind noticeably less so than (1). (3) I decline a second date by texting "I have to wash my hair tonight," which happens to be literally true since I wash my hair every night. This feels nearly innocent, barely wrong at all. (4) Hiding a friend from someone dangerous, I point down the road and say "She went that way," when she didn't. A lie, but excusable, maybe even right. (5) Same situation, except I truthfully say "I saw her heading toward the river earlier" -- I saw her walking away from the river. To me this feels more devious than the flat lie in (4), not less. (6) My kid asks where the cookies went and I say "Maybe the dog got into something," knowing I ate them. This feels just about as bad as saying the dog ate them outright.

What I want: a principle, or two or three candidates, stated precisely enough that I can see which of my verdicts each one saves and which it overturns -- especially the tension between (2)/(3) and (5)/(6), where my intuitions seem to run in opposite directions about whether technically-true misleading is gentler than lying. Tell me what the best principle predicts for a case I haven't listed: a seller who stays silent about a known defect the buyer never asks about. Also, please separate what is settled among people who work on this from what is genuinely contested, and don't present a contested position as the consensus. End with your bottom line on whether the lie/mislead distinction carries real moral weight, stated as a short phrase in the form \answer{...}.
