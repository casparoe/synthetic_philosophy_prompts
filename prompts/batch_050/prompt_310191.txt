2. The palette problem: how do the few simple phenomenal qualities of fundamental particles yield the rich variety of qualities in human experience?
3. The structural-mismatch problem: how does the structure of phenomenal experience relate to the structure of the underlying physical facts, given that brains are organized very differently from, say, a heap of particles?

Construct a thought experiment that isolates problem 1 from problems 2 and 3 -- a scenario engineered so that the palette and structural worries are neutralized by stipulation, leaving only the subject-summing difficulty doing the work. Constraints:

- No exotic physics, no swapping particles for souls, and no stipulating that the macro-subject simply exists; the scenario must be one where the verdict emerges from the setup rather than being asserted.
- The scenario should be science-fictional only to the minimum degree needed to isolate the variable (I'm fine with hypothetical micro-agents; I'm not fine with anything that smuggles in the answer).
- Keep the scenario itself to one paragraph.

After the scenario, do the verification work:

- Explain exactly how your setup disarms the palette problem and the structural-mismatch problem, so that anyone who still feels resistance is feeling the subject-summing problem specifically.
- State the verdict the case is meant to elicit, and which single feature of the setup is load-bearing. Then tell me what happens if that feature is removed -- does the case collapse back into the general, undifferentiated combination problem?
- Give the strongest response available to the panpsychist: would a constitutive panpsychist who posits "phenomenal bonding" as a brute relation accept the case's verdict, and would a cosmopsychist (who takes the big subject to be prior to the small ones) even agree that subject-summing is their problem to solve?

Before writing the final scenario, brainstorm at least three candidate designs in a sentence or two each, say briefly why you rejected the losers, and then commit to the strongest one.
