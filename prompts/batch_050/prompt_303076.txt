Then I find out Galen Strawson runs basicly this exact argument (he calls it the Basic Argument) in that paper of his about moral responsibility being impossible, and philosophers have been fighting about it for decades.

Here's what I want: your actual credence that the Basic Argument is sound -- meaning, nobody is ever morally responsible for anything in the "ultimate" sense, where you'd genuinely deserve blame or praise, as opposed to the "it's useful to hold people accountable" sense, which everyone agrees survives.
