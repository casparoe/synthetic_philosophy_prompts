So clearly this one-liner is extremely load-bearing and I don't trust it.

The thing is, I actually read a bit about the incompleteness theorems back in college, and I'm pretty sure the slogan is doing a lot of sneaky work depending on how you cash it out. Can you lay out the different precise claims it could be making? Feel free to correct my list or add readings I've missed.
