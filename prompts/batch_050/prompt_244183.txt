Construct a thought experiment in which a stable singleton exists even though no state, corporation, or AI system possesses coercive supremacy and ordinary institutions retain substantial autonomy. Treat a singleton as a single effective decision-making agency at the highest level: for genuinely global choices, there is one procedure whose outputs settle what the world does. Constraints: use only near-future institutional mechanisms; no infallible prediction, mind uploading, or stipulation that everyone simply agrees; the unified agency must emerge from the interaction of mutually checking actors rather than from one actor secretly holding a veto; and the case must include at least one consequential global decision that the procedure resolves despite initial disagreement. Describe the scenario in at most two paragraphs, then explain why it counts as a singleton rather than mere cooperation or correlated preferences, which feature is doing the conceptual work and what happens if it is removed, and whether the strongest objection—that this is only a coalition or constitution, not an agent—undermines the singleton hypothesis or merely exposes an ambiguity in its definition.
