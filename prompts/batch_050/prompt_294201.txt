My friend Dario is a stats PhD student with about $15k to his name, and over the years I've gotten into the habit of asking him the most he'd pay to play various bets. His answers so far:

- Same game capped at 2^30, roughly $1.07 billion, offered by a billionaire he genuinely trusts with the money verifiably sitting in escrow (expected value $31): he'd pay about $24.
- Unrelated bet, a straight 50/50 coin flip where he wins $2,000 or gets nothing: he'd pay about $850.

Now the new case. What's your best prediction of the max he'd pay, and why?

Tell me which rule best fits the track record, which earlier case is the closest analog to the new one, and what you'd predict.
