For a workshop on decision theory and AI risk I have to circulate a one-page position note, and I want it stress-tested before anyone else sees it. Use my vocabulary as stipulated, even if it is not standard: by acausal pressure I mean an attempt to influence an agent not through any physical channel, but by making certain descriptions of that agent more or less attractive inside the abstract models other reasoners might use. By a surrogate goal I mean a stand-in objective adopted when the real target is causally sealed off, rational only insofar as success on the stand-in remains correlated with the target across nearby possibilities.

My commitments are these: instrumental convergence is real but weak, so many agents share predictable subgoals while intelligence still underdetermines final values; acausal pressure is rational only when it changes which descriptions of one are true in other agents’ models, never by reaching across impossible channels; surrogate goals can be rational under the correlation condition just stated; a threat loses credibility if issuing it would lead the target to infer that one is the sort of agent who bluffs, so a rational threat policy may need to be opaque even to oneself; one should precommit to ignore blackmail even when ignoring is predictably worse in expectation, because being updateable by blackmail makes one exploitable; and moral uncertainty generally favors keeping options open, so irreversible precommitments are suspect.

Audit the set like a hostile but fair referee. Do not write an essay defending a view, do not add citations, and do not tell me which commitment you think I secretly want to abandon. Rank the genuine pairwise tensions by severity; for each, state exactly what follows from what, the cheapest repair, and what that repair costs. Mark at least one tension that is merely apparent and explain why it dissolves. Pay special attention to whether my correlation condition for surrogate goals quietly smuggles in the abstract-model legibility I allowed for acausal pressure, and whether self-opacity about threats can coexist with being publicly non-exploitable about blackmail. End by naming the single revision that would restore the most coherence with the least damage to the overall position.
