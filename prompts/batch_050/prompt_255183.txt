Having retired last year from a career in condensed matter physics, I have been working my way through some philosophy of science and mind, and I have become preoccupied with Daniel Dennett's discussion of "real patterns" together with his three stances — the physical, the design, and the intentional. I take the rough idea to be that a pattern is real if some description exploiting it buys you genuine predictive or explanatory compression that you could not otherwise get, and that different stances license different such patterns for different systems. What I find underspecified in my reading so far is how to apply the criterion comparatively rather than case by case, so I would like you to do that for me.

Please rank the following eight putative patterns by how real you judge them to be on this broadly Dennettian criterion, from most real to least: (1) the centre of mass of the solar system; (2) temperature as a property of a gas; (3) the supply-and-demand equilibrium price in a market; (4) a gene understood as a unit of inherited information; (5) money, that is, the value stored in a bank balance; (6) biological species; (7) the self, understood as a persisting centre of narrative or agency; and (8) free will understood as the capacity of certain intentional systems to be governed by reasons. For each entry, tell me in a few sentences why it lands where it does, and identify which of the three stances — physical, design, or intentional — you consider most apt for capturing it, or whether you think no single stance suffices. Where two entries are close, please still commit to an order rather than declaring ties, and say what consideration tips the balance.

I should stress what I am after: not a tour of interpretative options but a committed verdict. Please defend a single definite ranking, acknowledge explicitly the entries where you feel least certain and what evidence or argument would move them, and flag any point at which you depart from what you take Dennett's own view to be — I would rather know where you part company with him than receive a summary of the literature. Please write your answer in British English.
