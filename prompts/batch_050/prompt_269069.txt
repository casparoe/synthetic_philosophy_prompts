For each desideratum, state the consideration behind it and mark whether it is non-negotiable for any theory in this area or a preference of one camp (physiologists versus selectionists, say). Then flag any pair of desiderata you suspect cannot be jointly satisfied, since that would itself be the interesting result. Finish with a brief paragraph on which single requirement eliminates the most existing contenders. Please write in British English.
