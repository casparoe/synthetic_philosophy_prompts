So there's this argument that keeps resurfacing on the rationalist-adjacent forums I read, and honestly also in my group chat, and it never goes anywhere: whether our norms of justice are genuinely an independent standard that power can be measured against, or whether they're just whatever arrangements the strongest coalition found stable, dressed up afterwards. The first side says norms constrain force, the second side says norms are force's bookkeeping, and both sides can talk past each other for hundreds of comments.

Recently I ran into the Pascal fragment in the Pensées (in the part Trotter's translation heads "Justice and the Reason of Effects") where he points out that the very same killing counts as murder on one side of a river and heroism on the other, so that what we call just seems to track borders and who's in charge rather than anything deeper. Pascal clearly thought this observation was devastating, but here's the thing: both sides of my forum debate claim it. The force-is-all side reads it as proof that justice just is the record of where power happened to settle. The other side reads it as a reductio -- precisely because we can see the arbitrariness, there must be a real standard we're judging it against. Same passage, opposite conclusions, and nobody has moved in years.

So my question isn't who's right, it's: what kind of disagreement is this actually? My hypothesis is that it's not really about justice at all but about what counts as an explanation of a norm -- one side treats "this norm is what the strong found useful" as explaining the norm away, the other treats it as explaining how we came to notice it, and neither notices they're using different standards of what a debunking explanation has to do. Is that right, or is there a hidden substantive question under it (empirical? metaethical?) that could in principle be settled? Give me the diagnosis with reasons, and tell me what, if anything, would count as progress -- a distinction, a test case both sides have to agree on, or an argument that this one's just bedrock and we should stop having it.
