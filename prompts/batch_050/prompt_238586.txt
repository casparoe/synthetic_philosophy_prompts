So here's something I keep getting stuck on. As I understand it, the AI alignment problem is the problem of getting AI systems to act in line with human values. But human values are a complete mess — people disagree about nearly everything moral, cultures want different things, and even a single persons values contradict each other half the time. Philosophy has had a couple thousand years to sort out ethics and hasn't. So how could anyone ever "align" an AI to values we can't even state consistently? Doesn't that mean alignment research is basicly held hostage by moral philosophy — i.e., stuck forever? Or put it this way: if someone handed us an oracle tomorrow that just printed out the one true moral theory, would alignment be solved on the spot, or would there still be real work left to do? I genuinely can't tell if the answer to that is obvious.
