My advisor and I got into it after my qualifying exams about whether this is a genuine paradox about rationality under imperfect recall or just an artifact of how the problem gets modeled, and the conversation spilled over into Sleeping Beauty, updatelessness, and whether any of this matters for how AI systems should be designed to act over time.

Here's what I'd like help with. For a paper (maybe eventually a dissertation chapter) that I have roughly a year to a year and a half to write, which open questions or lines of work around this cluster of problems are actually most worth pursuing?
