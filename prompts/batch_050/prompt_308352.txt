Draft the joint statement of an adversarial collaboration between a legal positivist in Hart's tradition and a natural lawyer in Fuller's, written after they have worked through the Nazi grudge-informer cases together and want a document both can sign. Include: (1) a numbered list of agreed claims -- I expect candidates to include that the postwar German courts faced a genuine dilemma, that some statutes can be too vicious to govern conduct as law does, and that retroactive invalidation and refusal-to-apply are different remedies with different costs, but phrase them as the parties would, not as I have; (2) a disagreements table giving the exact residual disputes, each formulated in wording the side holding it would accept -- at minimum, whether a sufficiently iniquitous statute fails to be law or is law that should be disregarded, and whether Fuller's eight desiderata are a morality internal to law or merely instruments for making law effective; (3) the cruxes, where for each crux you name a case or argument both sides accept as bearing on it and record what each side predicts or concedes about it; and (4) one sentence stating whether any part of the original debate turned out to be verbal, and which part. Maximum 700 words.
