Reading Mill's Subjection of Women for a class and I'm stuck on the part where he says we don't really know anything about womens "nature," because they've always been raised under artificial conditions, so everything we observe about them might just be the product of how they were brought up rather than anything natural. Fine, that knocks out the conservative's favorite argument. But doesn't it cut against Mill just as hard? Mill writes like admitting our ignorance about nature obviously supports his side, but from where I'm sitting it looks like pure agnosticism, which in practice favors whoever's already in charge. What step of the argument am I missing?
