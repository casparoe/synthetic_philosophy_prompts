This semester, instead of lecturing, I want them to feel the paradox in their bodies before they ever hear its name: the experience of being unable to find a pairing that an outsider can plainly see, and the dawning realization that "larger" and "smaller" might depend on which matchings your world happens to contain.

Design a structured in-class activity that does this.
