Dev came back with two points: first, the probability estimates in cases like hers are so made-up that treating them as inputs to arithmetic is theater -- one in a hundred thousand versus one in a billion is a guess either way, but the conclusion flips depending on which guess you write down; second, an organization that reliably does visible good builds trust and gets copied, and he thinks that actually-existing machinery of doing good has value that her spreadsheet ignores.
