At a party last weekend someone shut down my doubts about mind uploading with the line "the mind is just software, the brain is just the hardware it runs on." His point was that since the same software can run on totally different machines, copying my brain's "program" onto a computer would literally produce me, so uploadig is basically just backing yourself up. I feel like several different claims are being smuggled in under one catchy metaphor. Can you give me a menu of the precise theses that slogan could stand for -- e.g. that what makes something a mind is its causal/functional organization rather than the stuff it's made of, that mental states literally are computations, that anything running the same program has the same mental states, that duplicating the program would duplicate the person -- and for each one say whether it's trivial, substantive-but-defensible, or probably false, and whether it would actually be enough to get him his uploading conclusion? Under 450 words please.
