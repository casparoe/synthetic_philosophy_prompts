Then last week I was reading about Taurek's old paper "Should the Numbers Count?" (only know it secondhand, so forgive me if I'm mangling it) and apparently the same slogan gets wielded for the OPPOSITE conclusion: there's no sum to compare, you can't add up people like beans, so the slogan gives you no reason at all to save the five.
