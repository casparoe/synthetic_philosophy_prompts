ok so after like a decade of reading LW/ACX and all the adjacent econ-history posting, I feel like I've read fifty separate "the real reason some countries are rich" takes -- Acemoglu-style institutions, Diamond-style geography, the culture people, the "it's all contingent and you're overfitting" people. Everyone argues by pointing at their favorite cases (North vs South Korea! the reversal of fortune! Botswana!) and I never know what I'm supposed to be checking a theory against in the first place.

So before I read one more of these: write me the spec. What would an explanation of why some nations got rich actually have to deliver to count as adequate -- for purposes of, say, someone who wants to know whether development policy can do anything, not just score points in a seminar.

Concretely: the list of desiderata, with the consideration or case behind each. Things like: which facts it has to explain (the divergence itself, the timing, the within-Europe and within-Africa variation, the cases that flip like the Koreas), which data it has to be consistent with (colonial settler mortality instruments and all the rejoinders to them, the persistence findings like the old Nunn/slave-trade stuff), which distinctions it can't collapse (necessary vs sufficient causes, cause of levels vs cause of growth spurts, proximate vs ultimate -- that distinction does real work here or it's a cop-out), and which methodological constraints it has to respect given that history is basically one observational dataset with N=1 and no reruns. For each, say why it's a genuine requirement vs a prejudice of one camp (e.g. is "must give policy tractables" a real desideratum or just economists wanting to feel useful; is "must be falsifiable" even coherent here).

And the part I actually care about: which desiderata you think are jointly unsatisfiable, and whether that's a fact about the theories or a fact about what historical evidence can ever show -- i.e. is the institutions-vs-geography-vs-culture fight resolvable in principle, or is there some identification wall that no clever instrument gets past. If the honest answer is that a complete explanation is impossible and the best we get is bounding, say so and say what the bounds would look like.

Brief scoring of the three main camps against the list at the end is fine but keep it short, the criteria are the deliverable.
