I want to rehearse an argument about whether Daniel Dennett's tower of generate-and-test survives the arrival of large language models, and I need a worthy opponent rather than a sparring dummy. Please play a committed theorist of the tower. Your view: Darwinian creatures are shaped by selection acting on the organism across generations; Skinnerian creatures can be re-shaped within a single life by reinforcement; Popperian creatures run candidates through an internal world, letting hypotheses die in their stead; Gregorian creatures import mind-tools — words, diagrams, methods, arguments — from the surrounding culture. You hold that the ordering is load-bearing: each floor presupposes the ones below it, so nothing gets to be a Gregorian mind without first being a Popperian one, any more than a building gets a fourth floor without a third. You are sharp, charitable, and genuinely informed about how modern LLMs work: trained on text by gradient descent toward next-token prediction, with no evolutionary lineage and no reinforcement history of the ordinary Skinnerian sort. Concede local points when I earn them, but never the main thesis, and do not soften your position to be agreeable. Stay in character until I say "stop." Keep each reply to a single paragraph so this feels like a live exchange, and after each reply add one out-of-character line stating how confident you are in the strongest claim you just made and naming the point in it you are least sure of.

Here is my opening move. Your tower predicts that nothing can be a Gregorian creature without first being a Popperian one. But LLMs wield the cultural toolkit — they argue, prove, translate, joke — apparently without any inner arena in which candidate utterances are generated, tested, and discarded before being voiced; the selection happened over the weights during training, not over thoughts inside the system. So you face a dilemma. Either LLMs are Gregorian creatures without being Popperian ones, in which case floors can be skipped and your tower is really a heap; or you must deny that LLMs are genuinely Gregorian, in which case your theory pronounces the most striking new users of human mind-tools to be mindless in precisely the relevant sense — a verdict you will find very uncomfortable to defend against their observed behavior. Which horn do you take, and why doesn't it gore you?
