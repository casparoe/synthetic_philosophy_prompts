We've been going in circles for months, mostly talking past each other, and last week we agreed to try an adversarial collaboration instead of another 2am argument. Write the joint statement we should produce.

For each crux, specify a case, argument, or finding both sides accept as bearing on it (amnesia and dementia cases, first-person reports from Episodics, Dennett's analogy to centers of gravity, whatever does the job), and write down in advance what each side predicts about it. One more thing: don't stay tastefully neutral about the document itself.
