Write an essay on whether a person can exempt themselves from blame by temporarily disabling the emotional capacities on which blame seems to depend. Use the following constructed case.

Noa intends to leak a confidential personnel report in order to damage a colleague’s career. She knows that her ordinary feelings of guilt and empathy would probably prevent her from carrying out the plan. She therefore takes a lawful six-hour treatment that suppresses guilt, sympathetic distress, and the tendency to experience other people’s suffering as motivationally salient. The treatment leaves intact her memory, practical reasoning, ability to understand rules, capacity to foresee consequences, and responsiveness to prudential and legal considerations. While under its influence, Noa recognizes that the leak will seriously harm her colleague, but the anticipated harm no longer moves her. She deliberately releases the report. Six hours later, her ordinary emotional capacities return and she feels intense guilt.

Should the colleague’s resentment toward Noa have been appropriate at the time of the leak? If Noa was temporarily incapable of the emotional responsiveness normally involved in interpersonal regard, did she occupy an exempt condition, or did she merely possess a particularly culpable quality of will? If she was not responsible while acting, can responsibility nevertheless be traced to her earlier decision to take the treatment? Explain whether such tracing would blame her only for taking the treatment, or whether it can also make the later leak an appropriate object of resentment.

Your essay should distinguish excuses from exemptions, incapacity from refusal, and taking an objective attitude from abandoning moral judgment. It should also consider whether reactive attitudes merely reveal responsibility or partly constitute the interpersonal practice of holding someone responsible. Finally, address whether responsibility is better assigned to Noa at the moment of action, to her earlier self-manipulation, or to the extended episode comprising both. Do not resolve the case simply by asserting that Noa could have done otherwise. Reach and defend a definite conclusion, allowing qualifications where necessary.
