Sometimes it sounds like a claim about what the word "mind" *should* mean, like a proposal to redefine terms. And occasionally he says something about how the brain/device boundary is arbitrary so any line we draw is just convention.

What are the actually distinct claims this slogan could be making? Lay them out as a list, from the most boring version to the wildest, and for each one say whether it's trivial, defensible but substantive, or just wrong.
