I am writing an essay for my philosophy of science seminar, which has spent the term on Kuhn — paradigms, normal science, the accumulation of anomalies, crisis and revolution, incommensurability, and his recurring suggestion that the social sciences may never have settled on a single paradigm. I would like help stress-testing a counterfactual.

Kuhn died in 1996, well before psychology's replication crisis became impossible to ignore: Bem's 2011 paper purporting to find precognition in an ordinary experimental paradigm, the 2015 Open Science Collaboration project that successfully replicated only about a third of a hundred published findings, the Stapel fraud case, and the wave of methodological reforms that followed — preregistration, registered reports, open data and materials. Suppose Kuhn had lived to watch all of this unfold and had been asked to comment. What would he have said?

Please do the following. First, state your prediction in a single sentence at the very start. Second, argue for it from his actual framework rather than from a generic picture of science as self-correcting. In particular: would he have accepted the label "crisis" in his technical sense, given that a crisis in his usage presupposes an entrenched paradigm whose anomalies accumulate to the breaking point? Would he have treated failed replications as anomalies at all, or rather as failures to transmit the tacit, exemplar-based skill that he took normal-scientific practice to consist in — in which case the crisis would say more about the pedagogy of experimental psychology than about the truth of its results? And would the episode have confirmed his doubts about whether psychology possesses a paradigm, or instead suggested that social psychology had been functioning as normal science all along, complete with gatekeeping and resistance to disruptive findings? Third, identify the point at which his published views genuinely underdetermine the answer and say so explicitly, rather than forcing a verdict where the texts will not support one.

No more than 700 words.
