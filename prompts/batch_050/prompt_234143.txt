Analyze AlphaGo’s famous Move 37 against Lee Sedol in 2016 as a case of free-floating rationales: reasons without reasoners. Take these as the relevant facts: in game two, AlphaGo made an unusual shoulder hit that experienced commentators initially treated as strange or mistaken, but it turned out to be strong and later looked strategically insightful. AlphaGo chose the move through learned neural networks and search, not by giving a human-style verbal justification, and even its designers could only reconstruct why the move was good after the fact.

Map the case carefully. Where exactly would the rationale for Move 37 live: in the move itself, the network weights, the training history, the search procedure, the whole AlphaGo system, the programmers’ intentions, or the explanation humans later produce? Distinguish a causal explanation of why the system produced the move from a justifying reason that makes the move good, and then ask whether either can really float free of a reasoner. Also distinguish a rationale the system can use from one it can report, and a rationale that merely exists from one that someone could in principle recover. If the strongest reconstruction is post hoc and depends on human Go concepts AlphaGo never explicitly represented, is that discovering a reason already present or inventing one afterward?

Then push the analysis in the other direction: does Move 37 support the idea of free-floating rationales, or does it expose a problem with that idea? Consider what would have happened if the same move had lost because of some later miscalculation: would the rationale have disappeared, or would it have been a good reason operating under uncertainty? And compare AlphaGo with a human player who says the move just felt right. Why should unverbalized human intuition count as having a reasoner behind it, while AlphaGo’s trained evaluation allegedly does not?

I dont want a generic discussion of explainable AI. The interesting part is whether reasons can be instantiated in an optimization process without being possessed, understood, or endorsable by any agent. Say exactly where the free-floating-rationale framework illuminates the episode, where it starts to look metaphorical, and what observation or counterfactual could settle the issue rather than merely making it sound profound.
