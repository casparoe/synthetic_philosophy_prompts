"The idea is that mental states don't stop at the skull. Their example: Inga remembers the museum is on 53rd Street in her brain, Otto has Alzheimer's and writes it in his notebook. Before that it's just ink on paper, same way a stranger's notebook isn't part of anyone's mind. They're not saying consciousness spreads out into objects, that part stays in the head. It's basically Putnam's point about meanings not being in the head, pushed one step further to cognition itself."
