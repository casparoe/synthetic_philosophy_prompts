I've been working through the metaphysics of modality and have settled on a position about persons and their origins. Before I build anything further on it, I'd like to know whether the package is internally coherent. Here are my commitments:

(1) Origin essentialism for persons: I could not have originated from a different sperm or a different egg. Any individual produced from a different gamete pair — even if one gamete were a molecule-for-molecule duplicate of the one I actually came from — would be someone else, not me.

(2) The ground of (1) is the identity of the gametes themselves, not their qualitative or genetic profile. Relatedly, I reject bare haecceitism: claims of transworld identity should be grounded in something about the individuals involved, not in primitive thisness.

(3) No person is identical to a zygote. Monozygotic twinning shows that a single zygote can give rise to two persons, and since one thing cannot be identical to two things, I am not, and never was, identical to the zygote I developed from.

(4) Persons survive complete material turnover. Every atom in my body could be replaced over time without threatening my persistence, so nothing about my present material composition is essential to me.

(5) I could have been born female. Holding my origins fixed, a different sex chromosome at fertilization would still have resulted in me.

My question is whether this set hangs together. I suspect there is friction somewhere among (2), (3), and (5), and possibly between (1) and (4), but I cannot locate it precisely. Please audit the set: identify each genuine tension, in order of severity, and for each one state which commitment has to go and what its abandonment costs the overall picture. Distinguish genuine tensions from merely apparent ones — if two commitments look as though they conflict but do not, explain why the appearance dissolves rather than flagging them anyway. And where a tension can be resolved by refining a commitment rather than abandoning it — for instance, by specifying what fixes the identity of a gamete across possible worlds — lay out the refinement and its price. I am not asking for a survey of positions in the literature; I am asking what these five claims, as stated, jointly commit me to.
