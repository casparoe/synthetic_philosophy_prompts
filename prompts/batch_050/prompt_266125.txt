Whoever binds first often "wins," which gives both sides an incentive to commit earlier and earlier, and the race can end with both sides locked into incompatible commitments and disaster for everyone -- or with one side committing before it's even learned its own preferences.

Write the desiderata: what must a theory of strategic commitment deliver to explain when first-mover commitments are rational and when races to commit go wrong?
