My opening: your theory fails at step one, because there's no replication. When a gene gets copied, some stable physical thing is duplicated with incredibly high fidelity, and there's a clean seperation between the thing that's copied and the body it builds. Culture has neither. When I teach you a joke you don't download my brain state — you rebuild the joke out of your own understanding, and you'll retell it differently every time. Pass a rumor through ten people and its unrecognizable. So there's no high-fidelity copying, no line between the "meme" and its effects, hence no replicator, hence nothing for selection to select. What exactly is being copied when culture transmits, and why should I think the copying is faithful enough for selection to get any traction?
