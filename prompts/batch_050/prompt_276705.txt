And don't cheat by making the safe option secretly horrifying; it should look fine because it usually IS fine for most people who take it.

After the scenario, show me the work: why Paul's criteria make the boring option undecidable and the dramatic one decidable, which single feature of your case is doing the heavy lifting, and what happens to the verdict if you strip that feature out. Then tell me how someone defending the standard vampire-style framing would attack your case -- say, by denying that the boring option really transforms your core preferences rather than just your circumstances.
