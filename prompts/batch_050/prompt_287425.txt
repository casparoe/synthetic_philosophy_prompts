I cover science and ideas, and I want a piece of flash fiction for a newsletter sidebar on Bradley’s regress. Write a short story, 400 words max, about an archivist trying to record one simple fact—say, “Marta is left of Tomas”—using only index cards. Every time she writes down the relation, she realizes the card still just leaves Marta, Tomas, and “left of” sitting there separately, so she adds another card saying how those items are connected. And then another card for that connection, and so on.

The story should make the regress vivid without turning into a philosophy lecture: no characters explaining Bradley by name, no tidy resolution where somebody just says relations are “non-relational tie-ins” or whatever. Give it an actual ending, even if the ending is unsettling. Ideally it should leave the reader wondering what makes the pieces of any fact hang together as one fact rather than a pile of ingredients. Slightly strange tone is welcome, but keep it readable on a deadline.
