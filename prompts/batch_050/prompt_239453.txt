Take the following operationalization of eliminative materialism about the propositional attitudes. Suppose that by 2100 there exists a mature, mechanistic science of human cognition whose explanations are widely accepted and predictively powerful. The eliminativist prediction comes true only if the posits of that science no longer include anything deserving the names 'belief' and 'desire' — where a posit deserves the name only if it is a discrete state individuated by propositional content (specifiable with a that-clause) that plays roughly the causal-explanatory role folk psychology assigns it. Distinguish two possible fates: humor-style elimination, as happened to demonic possession and caloric, where the concept is abandoned wholesale; and impetus-style correction, as when 'fish' came to exclude whales, where the concept survives in revised form. Only the first counts as elimination for our purposes.

Give me your credence that belief and desire will have suffered humor-style elimination by 2100, decomposed into at least the following terms, each with a number and one or two lines of justification: (i) the probability that folk psychology is a genuinely empirical, corrigible theory rather than an interpretive stance or social practice — something that could be refuted rather than merely outgrown; (ii) conditional on that, the probability that mature cognitive science's best explanations will not be cast at the level of discrete, content-bearing states at all; (iii) the probability that constitutive arguments fail — that is, the claim that interpreting any system as an agent in the first place requires ascribing beliefs and desires, so that elimination would be self-undermining, turns out to be unsound; (iv) a sociological term: the probability that the scientific community would actually relinquish the vocabulary even if the underlying theory warranted doing so. Show how the terms combine into your final credence. Then state which term you are least confident in, what evidence or argument would move it most, and whether the fact that ordinary people will, by then, routinely ascribe beliefs to AI assistants should raise or lower your estimate.
