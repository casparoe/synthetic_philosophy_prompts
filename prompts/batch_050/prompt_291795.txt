My opening move: take a decent narrative explanation, say a historian explaining the collapse of the Weimar Republic by telling the story of how the reparations burden, the 1923 hyperinflation, and the memory of both shaped voters' receptiveness to anti-system parties after 1929. Nobody writing that story invokes anything like a general law, and the only candidate law lurking nearby -- something like "economic catastrophe destroys democratic legitimacy" -- is plainly false as stated, since plenty of democracies have survived worse. So either your model declares a perfectly good explanation defective, or it props it up with a law nobody believes. Which is it, and why isn't that a reductio?
