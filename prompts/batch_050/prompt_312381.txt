Here's something that's been bugging me lately. Pretty much every defense of why God allows suffering seems to run into a problem with heaven: if heaven is a place where people have free will AND there's no suffering, then free will can't be the thing that requires suffering down here. And if heaven's goods don't need any suffering to be possible, then claims that earthly suffering is necessary for greater goods seem shaky too. Please argue that the very idea of heaven — assuming it contains both freedom and the highest goods without evil — undermines the main theodicies (free will, soul-making, greater-good style ones), so that a theist basically has to either give up heaven or give up explaining evil. Feel free to consider obvious replies (like maybe heaven is only free of evil because of the earthly process that got people there) but defend the conclusion anyway.
