I'm trying to figure out what I actually think about personal identity and survival by reverse-engineering my gut reactions on cases, Parfit-style. Here are my verdicts, and I'd like to know what principle (if any) adds up to them:

- Standard teletransportation: scanned on Earth, original destroyed in the process, perfect replica assembled on Mars with all my memories. I survive. The Mars person is me, full stop.
- Branch-line version: same scan, but the Earth original is NOT destroyed and walks out of the booth. Now the Mars person is just a copy, and the Earth guy is me.
- Here's where it gets wierd: if the Earth original then dies a week later of unrelated causes, my verdict flips and I want to say the Mars person was me after all. But whether he's me shouldn't depend on what happens to somebody else a week later, right?
- Gradual neuron-by-neuron replacement with silicon chips over several years: I survive the whole way through.
- Instantaneous wholesale replacement by a silicon duplicate with all my memories: I do NOT survive, even though functionally this seems identical to the endpoint of the gradual case.
- Cryonics: frozen for 500 years, thawed, revived (assuming brain structure is preserved): I survive.
- Vaporized and a perfect copy assembled from new atoms in the spot where I stood five minutes later: I don't survive.
- Fission: my hemispheres separated and transplanted into two bodies, both waking up with my memories and character: neither is me, I'm gone — though strangely this doesn't strike me as nearly as bad as ordinary death.

So: is there one coherent principle that captures all of these? The gradual-vs-instantaneous pair and the branch-line flip both seem hard to reconcile with anything simple. Give me the best candidate principle or two, stated precisely, say which of my cases motivates each clause, and tell me honestly which verdicts I'd have to abandon if I wanted the simplest workable theory. Also: what does your best principle predict for a case I haven't listed — a teletransporter that scans me non-destructively, but the copy only gets built 50 years later, after my Earth body has died of natural causes? Do I survive that?
