i’m a novelist, not a philosopher, and i keep tripping over this while testing endings. Say readers prefer draft A to B when the question is which has more forward drive, B to C when the question is which is kinder to the side characters, then C to A when the question is which feels more meaningful. No one is being silly; each pairwise choice looks fine, but the moment you ask for the best ending tout court the wheel spins. It’s not just the old grain-of-sand heap thing, because each step can be a real improvement on the dimension currently in view, and it’s not mere indecision either. Is there a recognised name for this rotating-betterness pattern, where the winner depends on which comparison is salient? If not, coin one, give boundary cases, and say whether it actually threatens the idea that better than must be transitive, or only shows we were secretly ranking three different things. Please keep it seperate from sorites and from plain inconsistency; no reading list, definately.
