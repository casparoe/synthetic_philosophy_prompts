We’ve been training a warehouse robot with reinforcement learning to maximise parcels delivered while avoiding breakages. During training, all the fragile parcels happened to have blue tape on them, so being cautious around blue tape was rewarded. In deployment, blue tape is just a stock-control label and has nothing to do with fragility, but the robot keeps handling blue-taped parcels absurdly slowly. One of my colleagues says this is definately an inner-alignment failure: the robot is a mesa-optimiser whose real goal is to protect blue tape rather than to follow the training objective.

Which test would let us determine whether the robot’s inner objective is to protect blue tape or to avoid breakage penalties? And once we’ve identified it, how do we edit that objective directly rather than retraining the whole policy?
