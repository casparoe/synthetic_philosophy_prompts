Since my niece began her biology degree last autumn, family dinners have acquired a new ritual: my brother-in-law, who made his money in rental property, leans across the table and announces that competition in the housing market is simply survival of the fittest, and that Darwin settled whether anyone ought to feel bad about it. Last month a philosophy student at the same table retorted that the phrase proves nothing because it is circular -- the fittest just are whoever survives, so the sentence says only that survivors survive. The two of them were using the same four words to do opposite work, and I found I could not articulate, on the spot, what either of them was actually claiming.

I would like that fixed. Treat the slogan survival of the fittest as a piece of language in need of disambiguation. Lay out, as a menu, the distinct precise theses it could be expressing: the reading on which it is an empty tautology because fitness is defined by actual survival and reproductive success; the reading on which fitness is instead a genuine, trait-grounded propensity or disposition to leave descendants, so that the claim explains differential survival rather than restating it; the reading on which it is a population-level prediction about which heritable traits increase in frequency over generations; and the normative reading my brother-in-law needs, on which it licenses a conclusion about who deserves to win in social and economic life. For each reading, give a verdict -- platitude, substantive and defensible, or false -- with a sentence or two of justification. Then tell me which reading each of my two dinner companions required for their argument to go through, and whether that reading is among the defensible ones. I am especially interested in whether the circularity charge still touches the biological claim once fitness is construed as a disposition rather than an outcome, or whether it only touches a careless way of stating it.

Close your response with a final line beginning Verdict: followed by a short phrase naming which precisification of the slogan is the biologically respectable one.
