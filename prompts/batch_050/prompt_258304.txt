Draft eight questions that test this separation rather than merely invite the author to restate it. For each question, give the reply the author is most likely to make and a follow-up that blocks the easiest escape. Commit to the lines of challenge you consider strongest rather than producing a neutral survey. Include pressure on the genetic-fallacy defense, the distinction between a proposition’s content and the interests shaping a conceptual framework, the use of diary evidence to interpret otherwise ambiguous published doctrine, the claim that logic’s universality makes it politically neutral, and what archival or textual evidence could actually falsify the author’s thesis. Avoid questions about cancellation, biography for its own sake, or whether Frege was personally blameworthy.
