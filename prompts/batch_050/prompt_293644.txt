The first faction replies that a determinism no mind could even in principle exploit is a distinction without a difference; the second replies that "in principle" is being stretched to cover limits that are really just engineering. We have now had this same exchange three weeks running with no movement on either side.

Or is there a hidden factual or formal question doing the real work — say, about what physical limits there are on measurement precision or computation within a deterministic universe — which neither side has stated explicitly?
