Please argue for the following claim: even on the most charitable reading of Locke's labour theory of acquisition, private appropriation of urban land fails the Lockean proviso, and fails it in a way that does not generalize to other forms of property. Your argument should proceed as follows. First, grant the strongest version of the proviso available to the appropriator — not the literal requirement that enough and as good remain in the common stock, but the welfarist reading on which appropriation is legitimate so long as no one is left worse off than they would have been had the resource remained unowned, where the enormous productivity gains of private ownership typically do the justificatory work. Second, distinguish two components of a parcel of urban land's value: the value traceable to the owner's labour and improvements, and the site value traceable to location — the surrounding population, publicly funded infrastructure, legal order, and proximity to the labour and commerce of others. Argue that excluding others from the second component leaves them worse off in the proviso's sense, because the community's contribution to site value cannot be replicated elsewhere at zero cost, and because the productivity-uplift defence fails here: the appropriator did not produce the value he captures, so the rising tide that supposedly compensates the excluded is one he merely bottled. Third, explain why this argument does not equally condemn property in produced goods or in improvements themselves — that is, why the proviso bites selectively against land value of the locational kind. Finally, address the two strongest objections: that buying the land at market price compensates the community in advance, and that any proviso strict enough to condemn urban land proves too much and condemns all appropriation. Conclude by stating what remedy, if any, Lockean principles themselves would license — restitution, a charge on site value, communal ownership of rent — and whether the labour theory of acquisition survives its own proviso in the case that matters most.
