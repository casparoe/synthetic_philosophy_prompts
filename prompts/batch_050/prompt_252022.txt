My paper can only treat two of these complications in real depth. If the paper's goal is to assess whether the theorem provides a genuine epistemic justification of majority rule rather than a suggestive toy model, which two most deserve that treatment, and which can I responsibly set aside? Please rank all five candidates, make explicit the criteria behind your ranking (for instance: how badly the complication breaks the theorem's conclusion, how empirically realistic it is, how much downstream debate depends on it), and explain the trade-offs where your criteria conflict. Finally, state what argument or consideration would lead you to reorder your ranking, and where your reasoning is most likely to go wrong.
