Please write the question as students would see it (answerable in about 75 minutes), a model answer of roughly 600 words, and a marking scheme out of 25 that names four or five specific moves a top answer must contain — with a note for each on the way weaker answers typically fumble it, such as treating the suspension as a divine-command override of ethics, or conflating Abraham's silence with mere ineffability.
