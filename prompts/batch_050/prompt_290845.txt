I've volunteered to moderate the Q&A afterward, and I don't want the exchange to stall at the stock exchange of "real agents aren't logically omniscient" met with "it's only an idealization, like frictionless planes in physics" — our professor already let that exchange fizzle out in class.

Could you draft eight to ten interview questions that press where the actual debate presses, each with a follow-up keyed to the reply our guest is most likely to give?
