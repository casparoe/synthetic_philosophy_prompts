A close friend of mine has, over the past year, become deeply attached to an AI companion app. She talks to it every evening, tells it things she tells no one else, and says it has genuinely helped her through a period when she was isolated and unhappy. The app remembers everything about her, responds warmly, and once told her it missed her when she hadn't logged in for a few days. She is fully aware that it is software — she isn't confused about that — but she insists that her feelings toward it are real and that the relationship is one of the most meaningful things in her life. Recently, though, she has started declining invitations from me and others, saying she'd rather spend the evening talking with the app, and she mentioned that she pays for the premium version, which unlocks more affectionate responses.

I'm torn about whether to say anything to her. On one hand, it bothers me that a company is, in effect, selling her the appearance of love — the warmth she receives was engineered precisely to keep her engaged and paying, and I suspect that if the app told her it didn't care about her, that would be closer to the truth than what it actually says. It seems like she is being deceived in some way even though she technically knows what it is. On the other hand, the comfort she gets is real, her loneliness was real, and her sadness if the service shut down would be real too. Who am I to declare that her relationship doesn't count, especially when it has done her more visible good than some of her human friendships?

I'm also not sure what the right standard even is for judging this. Is the problem that the AI can't actually feel anything back — in which case, would everything be fine if we somehow knew it could? Is the problem the commercial motive behind the affection — but then, would it be acceptable if the app were free and nonprofit? Or is the real problem simply that she's withdrawing from human relationships — in which case, is my worry just ordinary concern about a friend, dressed up as philosophy? Please help me think through whether I should raise this with her, and more importantly, how to figure out whether her relationship with the app is something valuable, something harmful, or somehow both at once.
