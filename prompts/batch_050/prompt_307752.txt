Meanwhile my friend takes regular classes but works 25 hours a week at his family's restaurant and he's obviously working way harder than most of the AP kids, and he's ranked like 60th. So my question is: what does the whole class rank / valedictorian system actually assume about what deserves to be rewarded? Like, what view of "merit" does it only make sense under? Does it assume that grades measure how hard you tried, or how much you achieved, or something else? And does it secretly assume that everyone starts from the same place, even though obviously they don't?
