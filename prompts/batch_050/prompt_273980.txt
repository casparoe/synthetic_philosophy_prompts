Plan the teaching arc, not the content itself. Should base rates come before or after the reference class problem? Which example should carry the session: my empire spreadsheet or the classic planning-fallacy "how long will your project take" setup, and why not the other one? Then the misconceptions, in the order they'll actually show up, and where in the sequence to head each off. The ones I anticipate: "the reference class is arbitrary so this is all just vibes," "the outside view means you can never claim this time is different," and some smug guy arguing our civilization will obviously outlast the base rate because it's computed mostly from preindustrial empires. Finally, one question I can pose at the end that reveals whether they can actually pick and defend a reference class rather than just parotting "use base rates."
