okay so before I dive into another stack of papers on paraconsistent logic I want to get my own house in order: what would a theory of true contradictions (or of reasoning in the presence of them) actually have to deliver before I'd take it seriously? write me the desiderata list for dialetheism / paraconsistent approaches to the paradoxes.

things I want covered, roughly:

- which paradoxes it has to actually handle (liar, curry, russell's set if it wants naive comprehension, the sorites if it claims to cover vagueness) and what "handle" even means -- dissolving, embracing, or quarantining
- the constraint that it can't collapse into triviality, and what it has to give up to avoid that (disjunctive syllogism? contraction? modus ponens in some form?)
- the self-expression problem: can the dialetheist coherently SAY that some sentences are only true and not also false, or is the theory unable to distinguish itself from the trivialist in its own language
- what it does to everyday reasoning -- i want to still be able to do math and plan my groceries without everything falling apart, so what's the cost of the revision
- whether it has to say contradictions can be rationally believed, or only that some are true, and whether those even come apart
- revenge problems: any theory that handles the liar with machinery X gets a new liar built out of X, so what does the theory owe us about that

for each desideratum give me the case or consideration that motivates it, and mark which are genuine requirements vs which are just classical-logician prejudice dressed up as common sense. then the two things I actually care most about:

1. tell me if some subset of my desiderata is jointly unsatisfiable. I have a bad feeling that wanting liar + unrestricted classical reasoning + a transitive consequence relation is already impossible and if so the list itself is the interesting result, say so plainly
2. which single desideratum knocks out the most existing contenders (LP, gap views, revision-theoretic stuff, classical recapture)

brief scorecard of those contenders at the end is fine but keep it short, the spec is the point not the grading. casual tone fine, table fine if it helps
