ok so I've been chewing on the whole "is elegance evidence" question in physics (Hossenfelder-adjacent discourse, the old LW posts on Occam priors, etc) and before I write a post I want a consistency check on where I've landed. Roughly my position:

1. Simplicity priors are genuinely justified, not just pragmatic — something in the neighborhood of Solomonoff/MDL gives you a real reason to put more mass on short hypotheses.
2. Dirac trusting his equation because it was beautiful, before the positron turned up, was rational — not just a genius getting lucky.
3. Theorists who stayed bullish on supersymmetry/naturalness through the null LHC results were updating wrong; they let asthetics do work that evidence should've been doing.
4. Beauty is evidence only to the extent it's actually correlated with truth — it's a heuristic, not a guarantee. The universe doesn't owe us elegant laws and might just be ugly at the bottom.
5. The historical track record (Maxwell, GR, Dirac) is genuine inductive evidence that mathematical beauty correlates with truth in fundamental physics.

Does this package hang together? Tell me which pairs are in real tension vs which only look like it, ranked by severity, and for each real tension what the cheapest fix is and what it costs me. Don't just flag everything equally — I want to know where the actual problem is.
