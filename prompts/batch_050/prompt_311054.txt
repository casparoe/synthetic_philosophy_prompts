I'm drafting a paper on what makes pain bad (as in bad *for* the person feeling it, not just unpleasant in some thin sense), and before I commit to a view I want the spec sheet first. Write out the desiderata a theory of pain's badness has to meet to count as adequate, with the case or consideration motivating each one. Cases I have in mind that should generate at least some of the requirements: pain asymbolia patients, who say they still feel the pain but it genuinely doesn't bother them anymore; people on morphine who report the pain is still there but they don't mind it; the apparent distinction between the sensory and the unpleasant/motivational components of pain; masochists and other cases where pain seems sought rather than avoided; and the strong intuition that pain's badness isn't just one preference among others but somehow non-optional. For each desideratum, say whether it's a genuine constraint any theory must satisfy or just the pet assumption of one camp (e.g., people who think badness is built into the feeling vs. people who think it comes from our attitudes toward it). The part I care most about: if some subset of these desiderata can't be jointly satisfied -- say, "asymbolia is a real case of pain without badness" together with "badness is constitutive of pain" -- flag that explicitly, since that would basically settle the shape of the debate. Only after the list, you can note in a line or two per contender which major views (constitutive/phenomenal accounts, attitudinal accounts, imperative-style accounts) fail which requirement, but keep that short; the criteria are the deliverable. Format: exactly seven numbered desiderata, two to three sentences each, no intro paragraph, no wrap-up, then the brief contender notes after item seven. American English. Also, if you think the request is built on a confused premise -- e.g., that the asymbolia literature doesn't actually show what I'm assuming it shows, or that "the badness of pain" isn't a well-posed target -- say so plainly rather than just going along with it.
