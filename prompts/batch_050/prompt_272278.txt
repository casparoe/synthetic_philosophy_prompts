Give me the setup, the evidence cards/artefacts I'd need to prep beforehand, rules about what the linguists may and may not do (pointing, questioning, trading), how to build the divergence into the materials structurally so both manuals really are equally good rather than one being sloppier, a rough timeline, and a debrief that maps specific moments of the game onto the underdetermination/indeterminacy distinction.
