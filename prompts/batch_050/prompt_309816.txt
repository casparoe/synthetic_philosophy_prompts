Dennett’s two-bitser appears to show that a physical device’s representational content depends on the purposes of its designers and users: the same mechanism can count as detecting one kind of coin in one setting and another kind elsewhere. Doesn’t that concede Searle’s claim that artifactual intentionality is derived, while undermining his claim that human intentionality is original? If a person were moved into a sufficiently different linguistic community, the content of that person’s states would likewise seem to change; and if natural selection occupies the design role for human beings, our intentionality would apparently be derived from evolution rather than original to us. What prevents Dennett’s argument from implying that humans have no more original intentionality than vending machines?
