R3, the amendment clause: any rule, including this one, may be amended by a two-thirds vote. First turn, a player proposes P1: lower R3's threshold from two-thirds to a simple majority. It passes with 68%. Next turn she proposes P2: R3 may not itself be amended. It passes with 57% -- a majority under the new P1 threshold, but short of the old two-thirds.

Tell me which sub-question your number is most sensitive to, and what single argument would move it the most in either direction.

Lead with your number in the first sentence, then give the reasoning. No preamble, no restating my setup.
