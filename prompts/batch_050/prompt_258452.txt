Write a villanelle about the tautology objection to natural selection and the propensity interpretation of fitness that answers it. The two refrains should embody the two sides of the dispute: the first refrain should voice the charge that the theory explains nothing, since the fittest are simply defined as whichever organisms happen to survive, leaving the principle true by definition and empty of content; the second refrain should voice the reply that fitness is a propensity rather than an outcome, a bias built into the organism the way a bias is built into a loaded coin, so that attributing fitness explains survival instead of merely redescribing it. Let the machinery of the villanelle do philosophical work. The refrains return unchanged throughout the poem just as the objection returns unchanged every time the reply is offered, and the reader should feel that recurrence as the dialectical standstill it is. Then, in the closing quatrain, where the two refrains finally stand side by side, the poem should show why the circle is not vicious: a disposition can ground a genuine prediction even though it is only ever confirmed by the outcomes it was invoked to predict. Someone who knows the debate should be able to tell which refrain is which, and should feel that their final conjunction lands the reply rather than merely repeating it once more.
