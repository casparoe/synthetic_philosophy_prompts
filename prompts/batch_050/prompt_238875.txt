I have become convinced that the Humean Best System Account is the right theory of laws of nature: the laws just are the axioms of whichever true deductive system best balances simplicity, informativeness, and fit, with no governing, no necessary connections, nothing but the mosaic plus our systematization of it. I want you to map this view's exposure. What would have to be true for it to be wrong, and what would its own defenders concede as counting against it?

Please work through the threats I see, and any I don't, ranked from fatal to merely awkward: (1) the privileged-language problem -- "best" is only defined relative to a vocabulary and simplicity metric, and if the choice is anthropocentric, the view seems to collapse into a kind of projectivism about laws that its defenders officially reject; (2) special-science generalizations -- functional and ceteris paribus regularities that figure in no fundamental summary yet seem lawlike, where the standard move of relativizing best systems to predicates begins to look like an indefinitely extendable escape hatch; (3) symmetry principles -- conservation laws arising from spacetime symmetries are standardly used in physics as constraining which dynamical laws are possible, a direction of explanation that seems to invert the Humean order; (4) non-temporal fundamental physics -- if the deepest theory of the world is atemporal, as some approaches to quantum gravity suggest, it is unclear what "systematizing the history of the mosaic" even means; (5) the possibility that no unique best system exists, or that radically different systems tie.

For each, say what the world would have to be like for the objection to stick, what a committed Humean in the tradition of Lewis, Loewer, or Hall would concede versus dismiss, and whether there is any conceivable physical discovery that would settle the dispute against Humeanism -- or whether the view is so ontologically permissive that it risks nothing empirical, and if so whether that is a virtue or a defect for a theory of laws. Finally, tell me which defeater you judge most serious, and what it would actually take to talk me out of the view.
