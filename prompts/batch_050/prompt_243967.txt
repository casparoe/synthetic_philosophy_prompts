Compare these two views of photography. Windowism says a photograph can let us literally see the thing photographed, because its appearance was preserved through a suitably mind-independent causal chain. Recordism says we only ever see the photograph itself, though that photograph may give us especially strong evidence about what was in front of the camera.

Test the views against a live wildlife webcam, a faded childhood snapshot, and a photorealistic AI image made from several eyewitness descriptions. Which view better distinguishes seeing from merely knowing? Don’t just split the difference—defend one and explain its wierdest consequence.
