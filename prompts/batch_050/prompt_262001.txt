Here's my rough seed idea, which you should feel free to improve or replace: a two-phase points game where in phase one everyone plays under a scoring rule they can see and optimize for, and then at the decision point they're offered a "conversion" -- a new scoring schedule drawn at random from a sealed set, which they can't preview, and crucially after converting, ALL their points (including the ones they already earned) get re-scored under the new schedule. So deciding whether to convert using your current preferences is clearly the wrong tool, but there's no better one available. Give me the full runnable version: exact rules, what materials I need, a payoff scheme that works with fake points or like fun-size candy rather than money, a timeline for the 40 minutes, and what I as facilitator should and shouldn't say up front (I don't want to lecture about Paul before they've hit the wall themselves).

Also be honest about the one thing a points game fundamentally can't reproduce, since swapping a payoff schedule isn't the same as actually changing what someone cares about.
