I don't want her greatest hits, I want to actually push her. Draft me exactly seven interview questions as a numbered list, each a sentence or two with a follow-up keyed to the answer she's most likely to give -- no intro, no wrap-up, just the list. Also end with a question about what evidence or result could actually change her mind.
