I am a novelist at work on a book about a man raised inside a small apocalyptic community who slowly comes to doubt the worldview he was steeped in, and in researching how certainty and doubt actually operate at that depth I have been reading around Wittgenstein's On Certainty and the hinge epistemology that grew out of it. Before I let my half-formed conclusions shape the book's treatment of his crisis, I have written down where I have landed, and I would like to know whether the package is coherent. Here it is: (1) hinge commitments -- the scaffolding propositions, like there being an external world or my being the person I take myself to be, on which all inquiry rests -- are neither beliefs nor knowledge; they are arational, standing fast for us in a way that neither calls for nor admits of justification; (2) hinges are nonetheless changeable: what functions as riverbed in one epoch of a life or a culture can become an ordinary, testable proposition in another, and the reverse as well; (3) justification is hinge-relative, so that what counts as evidence for a person depends on which hinges are currently in place for them; (4) Moore was fundamentally right against the skeptic: insisting that I know I have two hands is a legitimate, substantive reply rather than a philosophical blunder; (5) my character's former community, whose hinges genuinely differ from mine, can still be rationally criticized from the outside, because rationality is not exhausted by what one's own hinges license; and (6) I am a thoroughgoing fallibilist about empirical knowledge, holding that anything I claim to know could, so far as I can ever tell, turn out to be false.

What I want is an audit rather than an exposition: tell me which pairs among these six commitments are genuinely in tension and which only appear to be, ordered by severity; for each real tension, state precisely what would have to give and what that revision would cost the overall picture; and if a single revision would restore the most consistency, name it and say what I lose by making it. Please give the answer as exactly five numbered items, each a sentence or two, with no introduction and no conclusion.
