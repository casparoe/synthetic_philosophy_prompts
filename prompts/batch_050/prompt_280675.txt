Her track record so far: a widow who builds a bot out of her late husband's messages and talks to it most nights -- totally fine, she said, her grief her rules, even though he never agreed to any of it. A startup selling subscriptions to a chatbot of a recently dead novelist, trained only on her published books and interviews, with the estate's full blessing -- absolutely not, "they're renting out a ghost," even though the family consented and nothing private was even used. A dying father who deliberately records and trains a bot for his young kids to grow up talking to -- yes, obviously, basicly the one clearly good case. A university's clearly-labeled bot of a long-dead philosopher built from his published writings for intro students to interrogate -- fine.
