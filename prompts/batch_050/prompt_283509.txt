So a month ago I had this insanely vivid dream and then couldn't tell if I was remembering the dream or remembering waking up and thinking about the dream, and it sent me down a rabbit hole on Malcolm vs Dennett. Now I've landed somewhere and I want to know if where I've landed is actually coherent, because a friend in my reading group says it isn't.

Here's my set of commitments:

(1) Contra Malcolm, dreams are genuine conscious experiences that occur during sleep. Something it is like to be dreaming happens while I'm asleep, not just a story I manufacture on waking.

(2) I basically accept Dennett's cassette-theory style point: we can never rule out that what I "remember" of a dream was fabricated at the moment of waking rather than experienced during sleep. There's no fact of the matter accessible to me about whether the experience preceded the memory.

(3) Nonetheless, I trust my dream journal. Entries I write within a minute of waking accurately report experiences I had during the night, at least most of the time.

(4) Lucid dreaming is what clinches (1) for me. People who signal with pre-agreed eye movements from inside a lucid dream are having experiences during sleep, full stop. I think this is the strongest evidence in the whole debate.

(5) I think dream experiences don't have to be remembered to count as experiences. Most of my dreaming is probably never recalled and that's fine, an experience doesn't need a later memory of it to have happened.

(6) About dream duration: I think the feeling that a dream lasted hours is usually illusory. The experience was probably compressed, the long duration is part of the retrospective narrative.

Tell me whether this hangs together. Which pairs are in genuine tension, and how bad is each one? Are there pairs that look contradictory but actually dissolve once you're careful? For each real tension, tell me the cheapest revision — which commitment to weaken or drop and what it costs me. And at the end, if you had to force exactly one change to make the set consistent, which change does the most work? Don't just tell me everything conflicts with everything; I want them ranked. Also please write the answer in American English.
