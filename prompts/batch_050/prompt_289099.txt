Me and a friend have been argueing for weeks about Hofstadter's Person Paper — the satire where he rewrites English so that everything gender-marked in our actual language is race-marked instead: the generic pronouns, the -man compounds, titles that flag marital status, the whole thing. My position is that it's close to a proof. If race-marked language reads as obviously awful, and its structure is identical to our gender-marked language, then ours is awful for the same reason, and anyone who defends generic masculine forms has to bite the bullet and defend the race version too. Her position is that the analogy is smuggling in all the conclusions. Sex, unlike the satire's race scheme, tracks a real, stable, roughly binary biological distinction that loads of unrelated languages encode grammatically, so the queasiness you feel reading the satire comes from the inventedness and arbitrariness of the race-marking, not from marking as such — and Hofstadter never actually argues that gender-marking is arbitrary in the same way, he just relies on the reader's reaction to do it.
