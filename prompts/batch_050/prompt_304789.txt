Generics are annoying because "ducks lay eggs" is fine even though the drakes don't, and "mosquitoes carry malaria" is fine even though almost none of them do. The theories I've come across basically offer three rescue moves for why a generic can be true despite exceptions: statistical (enough of the kind have the property), strikingness (the property is dangerous or salient enough that a handful of cases suffices), and normality/proper-function (that's what the kind is for, or what its normal members do).

I want to stress-test whether that taxonomy is complete, so build me a new generic of the form "Gs are F" that breaks all three moves at once. Specs:

- an ordinary competent speaker should judge it straightforwardly TRUE, not "true if you squint" or "true as a joke"
- fewer than a tenth of Gs are F, so the statistical reading predicts false
- F has to be boring: not dangerous, dramatic, or rare-but-salient, nothing like carrying malaria or eating bathers
- F-ing isn't what Gs are for, and a G that never Fs isn't broken, immature, abnormal, or a failed G -- so no proper-function or normality rescue
- it must actually be true of the world, no invented kinds or made-up facts, and no re-skins of the classics (ducks lay eggs, mosquitoes carry malaria, books are paperbacks, lions have manes -- swapping ducks for geese doesn't count as new)

Then do the paperwork. Run each of the three rescue moves on your sentence and show that each predicts false. Explain what it is about the case that makes ordinary speakers accept the sentence anyway, and isolate the single feature doing the work -- i.e., tell me what to delete so the generic flips to false.

If you suspect the profile is empty, that nothing satisfies all the specs, that's a legitimate answer, but then give me the closest miss you can construct, say which constraint you had to relax, and explain why the three moves converge on false here. is that convergence just a coincidence, or does it point at a fourth mechanism my list is missing?

Either way, finish by attacking your own example: how would someone argue the sentence is actually false, or that one of the three moves secretly handles it after all?
