My roommate and I got into this argument at like 1am last week and it's genuinely started to mess with me, so I want to know if I'm crazy. Some background: I've been on a Hofstadter kick lately and got into his careenium thought experiment, this imaginary frictionless pool table with huge sluggish balls rolling around while swarms of tiny fast ones (he calls them simmballs) ricochet between them. The point is supposed to be that the tiny balls are like neurons firing, and their patterns are the "symbols," the actual thoughts, and when you zoom out it looks like the symbol-level patterns are doing stuff — the table seems to decide things, mean things, and the swarm shoves the big balls around in ways that make sense as *thinking* rather than as collisions.

Here's where the fight started. My roommate is a physics major and his take is that everything on that table is just elastic collisions and conservation of momentum, full stop. The "symbols" don't shove anything, they're just a pattern we squint at, like saying a hurricane "decided" to hit Florida. Any story where the symbol level does causal work is either redundant, because the collisions already explain everything, or spooky, because it means thoughts are some extra force that reaches down and pushes molecules around, which violates physics. And the horrible thing is I cant find the flaw in this. It's actually wrecked me a little, because I just spent three weeks agonizing over whether to take this job offer in another state — pros and cons lists, calling my sister, journaling, the whole thing — and now it seems like all that deliberation was causally inert decoration on top of whatever my neurons were going to do anyway. The decision fell out of the collisions the whole time.

So what I want to ask: was my deliberating actually pointless, in the sense that it made zero difference to what I ended up doing? And going forward, should I save myself alot of trouble and just flip coins on big decisions, since either way it's the substrate that decides? I keep feeling like something is off with this reasoning but I can't articulate what without either denying basic physics or claiming my thoughts are magic. Is there any coherent way for thinking to genuinely *do* something without the symbols literally shoving the substrate around, or do I have to just accept that the deliberation is theater?
