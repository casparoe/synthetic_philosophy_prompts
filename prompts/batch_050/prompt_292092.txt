What I need to get right is the emotional texture, and to do that I finally have to understand something I've never understood: how can a sound be sad at all? It's pressure waves in air. Sadness is something people have.

So here's the assigment. Put on your philosopher-of-music hat, and brainstorm the candidate theories out loud before you commit to anything. Where does each theory break on this scene?
