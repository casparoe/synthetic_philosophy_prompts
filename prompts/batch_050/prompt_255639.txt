I would like a numerical credence, with its derivation, on a question most mathematicians decline to take seriously: that Peano Arithmetic is inconsistent — that is, that there exists a finite derivation of a contradiction from its axioms, whether or not anyone ever finds it.

Some context for the shape of answer I am after. Gödel's second incompleteness theorem shows that PA, if consistent, cannot prove its own consistency, so the strongest consistency proofs we possess — Gentzen's, proceeding by transfinite induction up to the ordinal ε₀ — rest on principles beyond PA itself and merely relocate the question. At the same time, PA has been used intensively for well over a century without any contradiction surfacing, and its axioms seem to describe a structure, the natural numbers, that we can survey as directly as anything in mathematics. History also supplies cautionary cases in which systems confidently used for years turned out to harbor contradictions discoverable in a few lines, and a small minority of serious mathematicians have suggested that arithmetic's consistency is an open empirical question rather than a settled one.

Please give me your credence as a number, and show how you arrive at it. In particular, I would like a separate weighting, each with a sentence or two of justification, for at least the following considerations: the century-long absence of a discovered contradiction — and whether that absence constitutes strong evidence or merely reflects how little of the space of possible derivations anyone has actually searched; Gentzen-style consistency proofs — that is, your confidence that induction up to ε₀ is itself coherent rather than simply assuming what it proves; and the semantic argument that PA must be sound because the natural numbers form a real, determinate structure that satisfies its axioms. State how these considerations combine into your final figure, which of them the figure is most sensitive to, and what would move it most — for instance, a contradiction discovered in a much weaker arithmetical system, or a result suggesting that the consistency of PA is a kind of accident of our proof methods rather than a deep fact. If your credence in inconsistency is extremely low, say so plainly, and explain why it is not zero.
