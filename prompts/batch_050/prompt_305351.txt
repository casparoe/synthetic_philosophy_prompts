I’m a software engineer with no formal philosophy background, building an internal decision-support module for a hospital’s advance-care planning team; the module will not decide cases, but it must tell a bedside clinician which questions to answer, in what order, before an attending may rely on an advance directive against a patient’s present request. Assume adult patients, roughly eight to twelve conflicts per month across neurology, oncology, and psychiatry, high stakes but not usually minutes-to-act emergencies, a twenty-minute bedside window, optional ethics consult within four hours, and attending physician sign-off required for any override. Please turn the problem of the authority of the earlier self into a reusable bedside procedure that distinguishes decisional capacity from authenticity, treat refusal from treat noninitiation, ordinary time-limited preferences from identity-altering interventions, present assent from coerced appeasement, and values drift from misinformation, while keeping the prior directive neither absolute nor merely advisory. Make explicit which distinctions must survive simplification, where the procedure hard-stops at no, where it escalates to ethics or legal review, what evidence may reopen a settled directive, and where judgment cannot be eliminated without pretending that values are facts. Apply the procedure to two invented cases: a patient with moderate Alzheimer’s disease whose notarized directive refuses all artificial nutrition but who now reaches for food, swallows safely, and becomes distressed when the tray is removed; and a patient with glioblastoma who, after a prior steroid-triggered manic episode, refused corticosteroids and ICU escalation, but now has edema, fluctuating fear, a spouse reporting concealed medication nonadherence, and moments of lucid acceptance followed by terror. Include at least one application where the procedure yields an answer the bedside team will dislike, and state plainly what the procedure cannot do, such as prove personal identity over time or settle the metaphysics of selfhood. Answer as exactly six numbered items, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion: 1 required inputs and definitions, 2 the ordered gate procedure, 3 override and escalation rules, 4 documentation and audit trail requirements, 5 worked application to the Alzheimer’s nutrition case, 6 worked application to the glioblastoma steroid case including the disliked result.
