This defense fascinates and bothers me, because it seems to make the theory impossible to lose: every decade that passes without a crisis is just more time for the crisis to arrive in. 3. Is there any fair way to score long-run predictions at all, given that the forecaster can die before being proven wrong and the critic can die before being proven right?
