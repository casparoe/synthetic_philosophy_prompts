Structuralists thus hold that mathematics is not about anything, which is why they struggle to explain what makes mathematical claims true." Think carefully about the distinction between different versions of structuralism before answering. Which parts of this summary are fair, which conflate two positions that structuralists keep apart, which overstate the view, and does the closing objection about mathematical truth follow from the accurate parts? Give your verdict as exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion.
