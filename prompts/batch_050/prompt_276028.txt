And then the bit I actually care about: which of these readings, if any, would really get him his conclusion that dying at 25 is no worse than dying at 85? I have a hunch the slogan at best supports 'don't dread being dead' and doesn't touch 'more good life is better than less', but I want the menu laid out properly before I go back to him with it.
