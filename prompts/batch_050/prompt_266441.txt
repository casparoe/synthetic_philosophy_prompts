The claim drew heavy criticism from evolutionary biologists and philosophers of biology, most prominently a 2013 paper by Dan Graur and colleagues, which argued that the consortium had used the wrong concept of function entirely: on their preferred view, a genomic element is functional only if it is under natural selection, i.e., if it persists because of what it does for the organism, which would put the truly functional fraction far lower.
