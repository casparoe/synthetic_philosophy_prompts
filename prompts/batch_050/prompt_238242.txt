Also consider whether the weaker "suffering-focused" versions -- where happiness still counts but relieving suffering takes priority -- genuinely escape these problems or just smuggle them back in under a friendlier name. I don't want a both-sides shrug: reach a verdict on whether some version of the view is defensible and defend it. Lead with your conclusion in the first sentence, then give the reasoning, no preamble and no restating my question. Around 800 words.
