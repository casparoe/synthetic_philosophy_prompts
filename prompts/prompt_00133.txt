Even though the expected outcomes seem comparable on paper, I strongly prefer Offer A, and a friend insists I'm being irrational since the odds work out the same either way. Is she right that my hesitation makes no sense, or is there a legitimate reason to favor the offer with clearly stated odds over the one with unspecified ones?
