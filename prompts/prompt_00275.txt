As a retired physicist who has lately been reading up on the philosophy of probability, I think quantum mechanics has effectively settled the old debate: since phenomena like radioactive decay display genuine, mind-independent randomness rather than mere ignorance, doesn't this vindicate frequentism as the correct interpretation of probability, given that these are exactly the sort of objective, repeatable frequencies frequentists have always appealed to? I'm puzzled why philosophers still treat the question of which interpretation is correct as open.
