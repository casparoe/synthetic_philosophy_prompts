Here's the question (10 pts total):

Frankfurt (1969) uses cases like the following to argue against the Principle of Alternative Possibilities (PAP): Black wants Jones to kill Smith, and has rigged up a device that lets him monitor Jones and force the killing if Jones shows any sign of backing out. As it happens Jones goes through with it entirely on his own, and Black never has to lift a finger.
(a) [4 pts] Explain how this case is meant to refute PAP.
(b) [3 pts] Explain the "dilemma defense" that Widerker and Ginet raise against Frankfurt's argument.
(c) [3 pts] Briefly explain how a defender of Frankfurt, like Fischer, responds to (b).

And here's the student's answer:

(a) In the case, Jones kills Smith all on his own, and Black's device never actually does anything -- it would only have kicked in if Jones hesitated. Since Jones couldn't have done otherwise (Black would've stepped in) but he's still clearly blameworthy, Frankfurt concludes moral responsibility doesn't require the ability to do otherwise, contrary to PAP. This matters because a lot of incompatibilist arguments lean on PAP, so if PAP is false, Frankfurt has basically shown that free will and determinism are compatible after all.

(b) Widerker and Ginet's dilemma says Frankfurt cases don't really work: either the "prior sign" Black watches for is causally sufficient for Jones's killing Smith, or it isn't. If it's sufficient, the case just assumes determinism at the local level, which begs the question against the libertarian. If it isn't sufficient, then Jones could've shown the sign and still not gone through with it, so there was a real alternative possibility after all, and PAP survives.

(c) Fischer's response is to grant that there's a "flicker of freedom" left over on the indeterministic horn -- Jones could've done something slightly different, like show the sign a bit differently -- but he says this flicker is exactly the loophole that saves PAP, since it's a genuine, robust alternative Jones could've taken. So on Fischer's view the dilemma defense basically succeeds, and Frankfurt is stuck relying on the deterministic horn, which is a weaker spot for the compatibilist to be in.
