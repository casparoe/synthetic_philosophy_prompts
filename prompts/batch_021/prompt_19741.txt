It was so far ahead of its time that it just sat unpublished in a drawer for something like two centuries. This stuff has been rattling around in my head because Bentham basically took his one core idea — add up pleasure, subtract pain, ignore whatever people find icky — and rode it out to conclusions almost nobody else got to for another 150 years.
