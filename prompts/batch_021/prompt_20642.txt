She spent two years messaging someone daily on a birdwatching forum under the handle 'Kestrel_Jo' - inside jokes, venting about work, never real names or photos. Then on an in-person birding walk she realized, from an offhand joke about grackles, that the guy leading it, Dave, was Kestrel_Jo. It floored her, even though its obviously just one guy. Months later she still catches herself thinking 'Kestrel_Jo would get this, Dave might not,' even though she fully accepts theyre the same person. Is she being irrational, or is something real going on where learning that two names pick out one person doesn't make them feel like one?
