I ran a small thought experiment on myself recently and got a result I cannot quite explain. My AP Chemistry final is three weeks away, and there is a party tonight that conflicts with the studying session I had planned. Right now, if I am honest, I lean toward going to the party, even though I know perfectly well that the grade matters more to me in the end. Yet when I imagine the identical choice pushed four weeks into the future — a party four weeks from now versus studying that same night for a final that would then be seven weeks away — I choose studying without any hesitation, even though the gap between the party and the final is exactly three weeks in both versions. My values have not shifted between the two scenarios, and neither has the spacing between the two events; only how soon the nearer event actually is has changed. My brother says this just proves I lack discipline, but that cannot be the whole story, since in the far-off version my discipline is apparently perfect, facing the very same tradeoff. What is actually producing this kind of flip? And since simply resolving to "try harder" has never worked for me before, is there a more principled way to decide whether tonight's judgment or my far-off judgment about the very same tradeoff deserves more trust?
