I've volunteered to interview next month's speaker for our local philosophy society's podcast: a game theorist who defends a specific escape route from the theorem, arguing that the NP-hardness of computing a beneficial misrepresentation under several widely used voting rules gives real practical protection against strategic voting, even though the impossibility result stands untouched. Draft eight to ten interview questions that go beyond a restatement of that position. For each question, add a short follow-up keyed to the answer she is most likely to give.
