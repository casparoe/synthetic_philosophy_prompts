So this happened at our local ACX meetup (we'd just done a session on Cartesian skepticism) and its been rattling around in my head since. (1) Theo makes the classic move: you can never rule out being a brain in a vat wired up to a simulation, so strictly speaking you don't *know* you have hands, that the external world exists, etc. (2) Nadia counters with the Putnam semantic-externalism argument: if you'd been a vat-brain your *whole life*, your word "vat" was never causally hooked up to any real vats, so in your mouth it'd only refer to some pattern in the simulation (or nothing at all) -- she called this "vat-English" -- meaning "I am a brain in a vat" comes out false, or doesn't even express that thought, whenever a lifelong vat-brain thinks it. So, purely from the armchair, you can know you're not a lifelong vat-brain. (3) Theo's comeback: that's a neat fact about language, not about the world. All it shows is that IF he were a vat-brain he couldn't correctly *say* so -- not that he isn't one -- and it feels fishy to get real knowledge about the external world for free like that. (4) Nadia says it's not "for free": the skeptical setup needs "maybe I'm a vat-brain" to be a coherent alternative you can weigh against "maybe I'm not," and her argument shows that comparison never gets off the ground the way the skeptic assumes. (5) Theo's reply: fine, but that only touches lifelong vat-brains. What if he was a totally normal person until an hour ago and got scooped into a vat five minutes ago? His word "vat" would already be hooked up to real vats from before, so "I am a brain in a vat" could be true and meaningful for him right now -- and thats honestly the version that worries him, not some sci-fi-baby-in-a-jar case. We had to leave for the next talk so it never got resolved.

Can you go through moves 1-5 and say who's got the better of it at each one, not just who wins overall? And separately: does Theo's "recently envatted" case in (5) actually get him off the hook for good, or is there a way to patch Nadia's argument to cover it too?
