I spent about 35 years doing experimental physics before I retired, and lately philosophy of time has been eating my retirement whole. I keep hitting the same impasse: the eternalists I read love the Rietdijk-Putnam-Penrose argument (the Andromeda paradox version, with two people walking past each other on the street disagreeing about what's happening "now" on Andromeda) as basically proof that special relativity hands you a block universe, and then the presentists respond by going neo-Lorentzian -- just help yourself to a priviledged simultaneity that's permanently undetectable. I can't tell if that's a real answer or just cheating with unfalsifiable structure bolted on for free. Could you write me a dialogue between a presentist and an eternalist actually hashing this out? Dont let the eternalist win by just chanting "Occam's razor" and dont let the presentist just repeat "undetectable frame" and stop there -- each should genuinely respond to the other's last move, and I'd like the presentist to at least try the line that quantum mechanics (objective collapse theories, or Bohmian mechanics) already seems to want a preferred frame anyway, so the move isn't as ad hoc as it looks.
