That sent me down a rabbit hole into the whole COMPAS/ProPublica fight, where ProPublica nailed Northpointe for unequal false-positive rates by race and Northpointe shot back that the tool was calibrated just fine, and it turns out they were probably both just defending opposite horns of the same impossibility theorem without ever framing it that way.
