Both sides seemed to have a point and I definately can't tell who's winning.

And when the skeptic plays their best card -- that basically every headline-grabbing study has a perfectly good explanation that doesn't require perception itself to differ -- I want the penetration defender to have a real comeback, not just concede or change the subject.
