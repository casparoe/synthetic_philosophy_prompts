Logic textbooks classify the dismissal of an argument on the grounds that its proponent is a hypocrite as the "tu quoque" fallacy, since the truth of a claim cannot depend on who is making it. Yet a substantial body of recent moral philosophy on the "standing to blame" argues that hypocrisy really can disqualify someone from blaming others for a fault they share, even when what they say is perfectly true. Doesn't this just mean that these philosophers are, in more sophisticated language, rehabilitating the very fallacy that introductory logic courses warn against?
