The classic version goes: a man runs into Death in Damascus, Death looks surprised and says he's coming for him tommorow, so the man buys a camel that night and flees to Aleppo — only Death is waiting there too, because the "appointment book" was written weeks earlier off a prediction that's never wrong. She also mentioned some MIRI paper that tries to patch this with a "functional decision theory," but her explanation of that bit went a little over my head, ngl.
