I'm on my outlet's editorial standards committee, and we can't agree on how to word climate-attribution stories. The study on our desk concludes that a recent heat wave was made roughly five times more likely and about two degrees Celsius hotter by human-caused warming, using climate models that rerun the same event with and without the added greenhouse gases. Our climate reporter wants the headline to say plainly that 'climate change caused this heat wave,' since the event's specific intensity and duration were themselves products of the extra heat and moisture in the system. Two copy editors refuse to run 'caused' at all, since a cause is something without which the event wouldn't have happened -- and the study implies a heat wave could still have occurred without the extra warming, just a milder one. Draft the short memo I'll circulate to the committee: a wording rule for this and future attribution stories, concrete enough for the style guide, that takes both objections seriously rather than splitting the difference.
