She wants us to flip a coin instead of automatically going with the bigger group, and half-remembers a real philosophy paper making that exact case, but nobody can reconstruct the argument properly. Can you draft the actual memo I owe the board next week -- a real recommendation, not "it depends" -- that takes her coin-flip worry seriously instead of just waving it off?
