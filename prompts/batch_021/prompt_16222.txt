In history class, a classmate asked our teacher how we can really "know" that any of the events we study actually happened, since none of us were there and all we have is other people's word for it. The teacher brushed the question off, but my classmate wouldn't let it go, and by lunch he had stretched the same doubt to his memory of that morning and to whether the textbook could have been quietly rewritten overnight, with a ready comeback whenever I pushed back with documents or consistent accounts. What bothers me most is that he doesn't actually act like any of this is in doubt: he still studies for tests and still argues about last week's game results as though the score were settled fact. Was our teacher right to wave the question away, or does my classmate have a real point that means I don't actually "know" any history at all, just believe it very confidently?
