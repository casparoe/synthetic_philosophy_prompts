Okay so this has been bugging me for a while and none of my friends can really give me a straight answer, so figured I'd try asking you about it. I have a friend, Priya, who has genuinely great taste in movies and books, like, weirdly consistent, I can't remember the last time she raved about something and I ended up thinking it was mediocre. Because of this I've gotten into this habit where if someone asks "hey have you seen [movie]?" and I haven't but Priya loved it, I'll just say "oh yeah it's really good" like it's my own take. I do the same thing at book club sometimes too, calling a novel "beautifully written" when honestly all I know is that two people whose taste I trust said so.

Heres the part that confuses me though: this doesn't bother me even a little when it's regular facts instead of opinions on books or movies. If Priya tells me the new taco place on 5th is amazing and I haven't been, I'll happily tell my coworkers "the taco place on 5th is supposed to be really good," and that feels completely honest, zero guilt. But saying a movie or book is good purely on her word feels like I'm getting away with something, almost like lying, even though it seems like basically the same move both times, trusting someone whose judgment I respect instead of doing the legwork myself. It got extra awkward a few weeks back when I told my sister a film was "great" based on Priya's word, then watched it myself and thought it was a total mess. I felt kind of embarrassed after, in a way I don't think I would've if the taco place had just turned out to be bad instead.

I brought this up with my book club and mostly got shrugs, and when I tried searching around online I couldn't even figure out what words to search for. So, is there an actual difference between passing along someone's word that something is factually true versus passing along their word that something is good or beautiful or well made, or am I just being inconsistent for no real reason? And practically speaking, should I stop repeating other people's verdicts on books and movies like they're my own opinion, or is there some way of doing that which isn't secretly a little dishonest?
