Whenever a close friend of mine is anxious before something important—a job interview, a first date, a big presentation—I reassure her with complete confidence that everything will go well, even though I have no real basis for the claim. It clearly calms her down, and I cannot see how believing it could do her any harm. The same logic shows up in smaller situations too, as when I tell people that their cooking, their singing, or their new haircut is wonderful when it is not. In every case, the justification feels identical: it helps, and as far as I can tell, it costs nothing. And yet something about this keeps nagging at me in a way I cannot quite explain. Is that merely squeamishness on my part, or is there a genuine problem with this reasoning?
