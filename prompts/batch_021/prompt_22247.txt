So this is a weird one but bear with me — my aunt just had what she's calling a miracle recovery. She was given maybe two months back in the spring (stage 4, the whole works) and now, half a year later, her scans came back basically clean and even her own oncologist admitted he doesnt have a great explanation. Half the family is treating this as a literal sign from God, and I got voluntold into being the designated skeptic at a birthday dinner last weekend, which sent me back to my old philosophy-of-religion notes on Hume's essay "Of Miracles." I dug the text back up and got stuck on the actual "maxim" paragraph — the one everybody quotes but nobody seems to unpack — which reads:

"That no testimony is sufficient to establish a miracle, unless the testimony be of such a kind, that its falsehood would be more miraculous, than the fact, which it endeavours to establish: And even in that case there is a mutual destruction of arguments, and the superior only gives us an assurance suitable to that degree of force, which remains, after deducting the inferior."

The first half I follow fine: testimony only counts once its own falsehood would itself be the bigger miracle. What I can't work out is that second sentence. If the testimony has already cleared that bar, why does Hume say there's still "a mutual destruction of arguments" and some further "deducting" of the inferior from the superior left to do — hasn't the weighing already happened in step one? What exactly is being subtracted from what at that point, concretely, given that "uniform experience of the laws of nature" and "the reliability of a given witness" don't even seem to be the same kind of quantity you could subtract from one another in the first place? Is this second half just a fancier restatement of the first half, or is Hume smuggling in some extra discount on top of it, and if so how would that even be cashed out? I'd like an actual walk-through of the logic here rather than the usual one-liner about "proportioning belief to the evidence," since that part I already have down.
