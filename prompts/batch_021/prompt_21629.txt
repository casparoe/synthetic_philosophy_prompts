Quick one from a piece I'm working on about how experts make gut calls under pressure. A poker pro I interviewed brought up a hand where her call turned out to be clearly wrong once she worked out the actual pot odds later that night -- but in the moment she had nowhere near the processing speed to do that math, and by her own account the decision felt totally fine at the time. Her coach, who was in the room, insisted she'd been irrational in that moment anyway, since the correct play was already fixed by the numbers on the table and she just hadnt crunched them yet. She shot back that by that standard she's basically 'irrational' every single time she doesn't instantly know the answer to a math problem she could eventually solve with pen and paper. I can't decide who's right, and its bugging me way more than it should for what's supposed to be a light little sidebar. Thoughts?
