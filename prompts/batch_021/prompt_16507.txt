"Worrall wants a position that keeps realism's best argument (the no-miracles argument—it would be a cosmic coincidence if our theories were this predictively successful and yet completely wrong about the world) while still taking antirealism's best argument seriously (the pessimistic meta-induction—past theories were eventually rejected, so we should expect the same fate for our current ones). His case study is the switch from Fresnel's wave theory of light, which explained polarization and diffraction using a mechanical ether, to Maxwell's electromagnetic theory, which kept none of Fresnel's ontology (no ether, no mechanical medium) but carried over his equations for the intensity of reflected and refracted light almost unchanged, just reinterpreted as ratios of electromagnetic field amplitudes instead of displacements in a mechanical medium. So there's continuity at the level of the mathematics even though the furniture of the world changed completely—Worrall's evidence that we should be realists about structure while staying agnostic about the underlying nature of things.

Newman's objection is that this isn't as satisfying as it sounds. Newman raised this challenge directly against Worrall's paper: he pointed out that saying 'the world has such-and-such structure' is almost empty, because for any collection of objects of the right cardinality, you can always cook up some relation on them that realizes whatever structure you like. So 'we know the structure but not the nature' collapses into 'we know how many things there are,' which is far weaker than what a realist wants to say.

Ontic structural realism tries to dodge this by denying there's any further nature behind the structure for realists to be silent about—relations are all there is, and objects (if they exist at all) are just nodes in the relational structure, so there's no leftover domain of bare individuals for the cardinality trick to exploit. Because Ladyman and French take this option, they succeed in fully closing off Newman's problem, which is why ontic structural realism is now the default form structural realism takes in the literature."
