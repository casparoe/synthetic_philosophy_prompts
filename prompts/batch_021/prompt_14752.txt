I'm giving the toast at the wedding of two friends from my rationalist meetup, Priya and Daniel, and there's one point I want to get right rather than flatten for a laugh. They met arguing about cause prioritization and never really stopped: she still thinks farmed-animal suffering is the more urgent cause, and he still thinks it's extreme poverty. Instead of compromising, they settled years ago into an arrangement where she donates well beyond what she'd otherwise give to poverty charities and he's kept a vegetarian pledge he'd never have made on his own, each insisting the other's concession matters more to them than their own does. Enough of the guests know Toby Ord's term for this -- 'moral trade,' from his 2015 paper of the same name -- that they'll wince if the toast collapses it into 'they met in the middle'; everyone else just wants something warm and, ideally, funny. Please write a five-minute toast built around this arrangement: get the trade right (as opposed to a mere compromise), work in one line that rewards the guests who know the term without losing everyone else, and still land as a toast rather than a philosophy lecture.
