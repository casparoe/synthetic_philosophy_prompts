So for organ donation after circulatory death, doctors declare someone dead about 5 minutes after their heart stops for good. But CPR can sometimes restart a heart way past that point, so the heart stopping for 5 min obviously isn't irreversible in any strict sense — its just that nobody in the room is trying to restart it. Doesn't that make "circulatory death" in these cases basically a legal fiction rather than the person actually being dead yet, since real death should mean its physically impossible to bring someone back, not just that we've decided not to?
