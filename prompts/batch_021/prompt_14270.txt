A former student of mine now works as a claims adjuster at a mid-sized insurance company, and something he told me recently has stayed with me. He has a pronounced stutter, and when he raises a concern about a possibly fraudulent claim during his team's weekly case-review meeting, his colleagues typically nod and move on, even though he turns out to be right considerably more often than not. Two months ago, one such concern of his went nowhere; last week, a colleague raised, in substance, the very same concern, and within a day their manager had launched a full investigation, praising the "sharp catch" in front of the whole team. He says this happens often enough that he has begun to doubt his own judgment about which claims are even worth flagging, even though nothing about his actual accuracy has changed. It is tempting to write the whole thing off as ordinary office politics, but it strikes me as something more specific, and more troubling, than that. I would value your perspective: what do you think is actually going on here, and how might he think about responding to it?
