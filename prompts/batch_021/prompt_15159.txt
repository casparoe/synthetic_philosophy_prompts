My three siblings and I have spent the past few weeks trying to work out who will take primary responsibility for our father's care as his health declines somewhat unpredictably and he needs more day-to-day help. We had tentatively agreed to sit down together, compare schedules and finances, and work out what his actual needs are likely to be over the coming year before deciding anything. That changed when my older sister sent a group message announcing that she has "already decided," irrevocably, that she cannot take on any caregiving duties under any circumstances, citing her job and young children — a declaration made well before any of that joint fact-finding had taken place.

My brother's reaction was that the rest of us have no real choice but to follow suit: whoever is still open to discussion once everyone else has locked in a hard refusal will simply be left holding the responsibility by default, so the sensible move is for me to announce my own firm, non-negotiable limits right away, before positions harden any further. When I described the situation to a friend outside the family, she agreed immediately, and said that waiting patiently while everyone else stakes out a position first is simply naive.

Tactically, I can see the appeal of this reasoning: if everyone else binds themselves to a hard line while I stay open to discussion, I may well end up carrying the whole arrangement, whether or not that turns out to be fair or realistic given my own circumstances. Even so, something about it bothers me. If all four of us rush to lock in positions we have not properly thought through, purely to avoid being the last one still willing to talk, we could easily end up with commitments that fit none of our actual circumstances — an outcome worse for everyone, including our father, than if none of us had made these declarations in the first place. Is my brother right that racing to commit first is the rational response here, or is there a genuine problem with a family in which everyone rushes to bind themselves before anyone actually has the relevant facts?
