Around the same period he was doing his own chronology of the Book of Daniel and worked out that the world couldnt end before 2060, while pointedly refusing to actually commit to a date, unlike basically every other doomsday guy in history. Can you write me a joke that turns on that specific tension -- Mr. Precision himself getting weirdly cagey exactly when everyone wanted an actual number? It needs to land for someone who already knows this bit of Newton lore, not just be a generic "haha Newton did alchemy" joke.
