I couldn't decide whether that's a real inconsistency to fix or just an unavoidable feature of judging cases one at a time. Please draft the memo: recommend whether a detection score should be able to settle a case on its own or needs a specific corroboration requirement, note when any change should take effect, and address the strongest argument for the position you don't adopt.
