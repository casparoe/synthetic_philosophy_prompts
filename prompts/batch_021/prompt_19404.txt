I asked him about it once at dinner thinking it'd be a two minute answer and instead got the full rundown -- apparently Hume thought a promise only binds you because society has this shared "practice" everyone's agreed to go along with, kind of like how a move only counts as castling because chess has rules, and outside that practice the words "I promise" don't mean anything at all.
