So at my nephew's birthday party last weekend he fell off the trampoline and broke his arm while six adults, me included, stood around chatting instead of watching him. My sister-in-law says we're all equally to blame since any one of us could've stopped him. My friend Priya and I got into a whole arguement about this afterward -- I said if 'failing to prevent something' makes you a cause of it, then the babysitter who cancelled that morning caused it too, so either everyone who didn't step in is a cause or nobody is. Priya insists that's wrong, that there's a real difference between us and someone three states away, but couldn't actually say what grounds that difference. Neither could I. So who's right -- is there an actual fact that makes some people's inaction more responsible than others, or is that just blame dressed up as causation?
