Clayton County, where Gorsuch's majority and Alito's and Kavanaugh's dissents each claimed fidelity to text; and whether Scalia's 'elephants in mouseholes' principle from Whitman v. Close with one question that presses on Justice Kagan's remark that 'we're all textualists now' by asking what, short of that consensus, would actually count as evidence against his view.
