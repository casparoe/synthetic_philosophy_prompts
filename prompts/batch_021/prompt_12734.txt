My friend from grad school is a hardcore Bayesian statistician, and shes getting married next month — I'm giving the toast at the reception. For years the running joke in her lab has been her personal motto, which is basically just I.J. Good's theorem on the value of information dressed up as life advice: it can never hurt, in expectation, to look at one more piece of evidence before you decide anything. Problem is, half the room will be her decision-theory friends who know exactly what that theorem does and doesn't actually license, and the other half are just family who've never heard of Bayes anything. Can you write me a toast, under 5 minutes, with one real joke built on the gap between 'looking can never hurt' and the very ordinary fact that sometimes looking really does hurt like hell — and make sure it lands as sweet and a little roast-y, not like a guest lecture on Bayesian epistemology?
