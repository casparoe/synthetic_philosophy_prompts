Write a dialogue between two set theorists at a chalkboard, one of whom accepts the Axiom of Choice without qualification and the other of whom works only in ZF plus Dependent Choice and refuses to go further. Have the first press the second on why cardinality is the right place to draw the line, given that Dependent Choice already commits her to a rule-free choice function for any family indexed by the natural numbers. Have the second press back with the fact that full Choice is exactly what forces the existence of non-measurable sets of reals, whereas ZF plus Dependent Choice alone is known to be consistent with every set of reals being Lebesgue measurable. Neither should be allowed to retreat to generalities about intuition or usefulness, and the dialogue need not end in agreement.
