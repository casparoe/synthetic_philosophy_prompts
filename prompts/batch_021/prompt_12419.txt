I retired a few years back after a career in condensed matter physics, and lately I've been getting into philosophy for fun -- right now its David Lewis on modal realism. Honestly it kinda feels like he's just describing a physical multiverse (many-worlds QM, eternal inflation, whichever) in philosopher-talk, since he says every possible world is just as real and concrete as ours, not some abstract mathematical fiction. So why are philosophers still debating modal realism on its own terms instead of just waiting for physicists to settle whether branching or decoherence is physically real -- wouldn't that decide the matter either way?
