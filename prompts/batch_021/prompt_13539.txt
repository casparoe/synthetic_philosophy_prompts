My grandmother, who has grown increasingly devout over the years, and I recently disagreed about something mundane: my morning coffee. For years, the same brand has reliably given me an energizing effect, and I take that track record as good reason to expect the same result tomorrow. She has no objection to my acting on that expectation, but insists that nothing in the coffee's chemistry actually produces the effect: what I call "cause and effect" is really just a pattern that God maintains out of habit, one that could be suspended at any moment without anything about the coffee or my body being any different. This struck me as an odd thing to say about something as ordinary as caffeine, but I couldn't pinpoint what, if anything, was wrong with it. Is there a genuine disagreement between us here, or are we just redescribing the same facts in different vocabularies? And if the disagreement is real, does it actually have any bearing on how I should think about my coffee, or about anything else I rely on?
