I'm the president of my high school's philosophy club, and next week I'm leading a session on modal metaphysics — specifically, the disagreement between counterpart theory and transworld identity theory about how to make sense of claims like "that candidate might have won" or "I could have chosen a different school." Rather than open with a dry definition, I'd like to start with a joke, but it should actually be funny to people who understand the two views, not just a generic "parallel universes" joke. Could you write me one? The punchline should turn on the real substance of the disagreement — for instance, the fact that on Lewis's counterpart theory nothing ever exists in more than one world, so whatever resembles you in some other world is never really you, whereas transworld identity theorists insist it is genuinely the same individual, just with different properties there.
