His actual claim is weirder than just "the public is dumb" -- he seems to be saying the public isn't even a real thing, like it's not a nation, or a congregation, or a crowd of actual people standing in one place, its an abstraction that basically gets summoned into existence by the press and by gossip/chatter, and that's exactly why its so dangerous, because there's never anyone specific you can hold responsible for what "the public" decides or says about you. Then I went down a rabbit hole and found out this whole idea probably wasn't unrelated to the fact that he'd more or less provoked a sleazy satirical paper (the Corsair -- I think Goldschmidt was the guy actually running it) into mocking him nonstop for years, cartoons of his uneven trouser legs and everything, after some falling out with a critic named Moller. So now I honestly can't tell whether his theory of "the public" and "leveling" is a genuinely sharp piece of social theory, or just a very elaborate, philosophically dressed-up version of a guy being salty about getting bullied in print.
