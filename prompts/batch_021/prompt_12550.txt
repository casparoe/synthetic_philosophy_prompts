I just read that Schopenhauer ranked music above every other art because its whole deal is expressing raw human emotion directly, instead of representing objects the way painting or poetry do. If that's really why he rated it so highly, wouldn't he have been all over modern film scoring, where composers write the music to track a scene's emotional beats as closely as possible -- swelling strings for the sad bit, dissonant strings for the scary bit, etc? Seems like thats just his theory taken to its logical extreme.
