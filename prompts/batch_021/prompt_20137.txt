But if I go the other direction and hedge everything into a truth-value gap, it stops being a toast and starts being a logic seminar. Can you write me something like 4 minutes long, built around the sea battle argument (feel free to bring in the Ockhamist/Peircean split or a supervaluationist-type move if it actually helps, since thats closer to her real academic turf than the intro-textbook version) that says something genuinely warm about her and Marcus's future without smuggling in the "it was always going to happen" fatalism she's spent her whole career arguing against?
