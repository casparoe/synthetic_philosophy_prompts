Ok so this is kind of a weird one but my robotics team has been arguing for like two weeks about what to spend our last chunk of sponsor money on before regionals — either a whole new intake mechanism or just better practice mats/field tiles so we can drill more. Normally we'd just hash it out in a meeting and vote, but our captain (who's kind of obsessed with those betting-market sites where people bet on whether stuff like elections or sports will happen) pitched something different: everyone gets 300 fake "team bucks" to bet with, and we run our own little market where you bet on which upgrade will actually get us a higher score at our scrimmage next week. Whichever option ends up with the higher "price" by Thursday is what we buy, no vote needed. His whole argument is that people who don't actually understand the intake mechanism won't want to bet on it since they'd just be throwing away bucks, so the market automatically weighs the opinions of whoever actually knows the most, instead of just whoever argues the loudest in meetings (which, not gonna lie, is usually him).

I can't shake the feeling that somethings off about this even though I can't totally explain why. Like, our mentor is an actual mechanical engineer who obviously knows way more than any of us, so couldn't he basically just buy up the whole market and decide it himself even though its not really "his" decision to make? I also noticed a bunch of the younger kids just copy whatever the seniors bet so they don't look clueless, which doesn't seem like it has much to do with what they actually believe will happen. And there's this other thing where some of us just WANT the new intake because it's more fun to build, not because we think it'll score higher — and I'm not sure the betting actually keeps "what we want" separate from "what we think will happen" the way my captain claims it does, they kind of end up tangled together once bucks (even fake ones) are on the line. Is he onto something genuinely smart here, or does this just sound cool while actually being a worse way to decide than normal voting or just talking it out? None of my teammates seem bothered by this but me, so I figured I'd ask somewhere else.
