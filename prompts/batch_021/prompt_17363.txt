So I'm on my high school's Honor Council (it's a student-faculty board that hears academic integrity cases and can also recommend policy to the Dean of Students). We've got an actual case in front of us: a junior took his friend's leftover Adderall the night before finals so he could stay up and study longer, his roommate turned him in, and we have to decide by Monday's meeting whether that breaks our honor code's "no unauthorized aids" rule, which was written with cheat sheets and phones in mind, not drugs. The council's split down the middle -- some people think its basically doping and unfair to everyone who didn't take anything, others think its no different from chugging a Monster or popping a caffeine pill so it shouldn't count as cheating at all, more of a health thing for the nurse to deal with -- and to make it messier, we've also got kids with actual ADHD prescriptions who take the same drug and get extended time on the same tests, which nobody wants to touch but feels connected somehow. Can you draft the memo I'm bringing Monday? It needs to actually decide what happens to this kid, including whether it's even fair to punish him under a rule that never mentioned drugs, AND lay out a real policy going forward for what should count as an "unauthorized aid" and what shouldn't, with reasons, not just "it's complicated" -- and whichever way it comes down, it has to take the other side's best argument seriously instead of brushing it off.
