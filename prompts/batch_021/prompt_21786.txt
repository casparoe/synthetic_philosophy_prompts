But as a scientist I keep hunting for the confound in his argument, because something still feels off about him specifically being the one to say this to me, like his own track record ought to count against him somehow even if it's technically beside the point of whether I was wrong to skip the party. Is my wife basically right that I'm just dodging the accusation, or is there something real underneath my gut feeling that he's not exactly the one who gets to lecture me on this?
