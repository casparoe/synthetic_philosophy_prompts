So this actually happened to me while I was scouting a location for a novel during some political unrest, and it still nags at me. Stuck past curfew, I hired a guy on a motorbike to get me through the checkpoints, but he'd only agree if I promised to wire his cousin money once I was safely out - he said carrying cash on him was more dangerous then the ride itself, and that he could just tell, from how someone talked, whether they'd actually pay once they didnt need him anymore. There was no contract and no way he could ever track me down afterward, and by the time I was sitting in the airport lounge the money could do literally nothing for me anymore. I paid him anyway. My partner says that was pure sentiment, that the rational move would've been to keep the cash since by then there was nothing left in it for me - I can't find the hole in her logic, but it still feels wrong. So, was I right to pay, or does she have a point?
