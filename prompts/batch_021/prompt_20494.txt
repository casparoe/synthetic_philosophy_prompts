An AI researcher I talked to for background (wouldn't let me quote him directly, annoyingly) said this whole trend is basically a folk-wisdom application of Condorcet's jury theorem, and that most people invoking "wisdom of crowds" logic for these systems dont really grapple with what the theorem actually needs in order to hold.
