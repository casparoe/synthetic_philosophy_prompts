So a few of us who met through the local ACX meetup have been running a tiny informal donor circle for about three years now, which started kind of as a joke after one of our meetup nights devolved into a fight about the repugnant conclusion. Anyway — everyone throws in whatever's left over at the end of the year and we vote on where it goes, usually split between global health and animal stuff. This year the animal half of the pot (a bit over $4k) is stuck because two camps have dug in and won't budge. One camp wants it to go to a mainstream chicken welfare campaign, on the grounds that hens clear basically every behavioral and physiological bar anyone's proposed for sentience, so we're not betting on anything dubious. The other camp, led by our resident hardcore-EV guy, wants it to go to a shrimp stunning intervention (he keeps citing the Rethink Priorities moral weight stuff), because the number of shrimp farmed every year is so absurdly large that even a low, genuinely uncertain probability of morally relevant suffering swamps the calculation once you multiply it out. A third member thinks this whole style of argument is just Pascal's mugging with crustaceans, and that if you let "tiny probability times huge number" in the door at all you're commited to funding literally anything, so we should only spend on species we're already confident about.

Somehow I got voted the one to write up the actual recommendation, since I'm the only one everyone still kind of trusts to be fair to both sides. I don't want to just paste the usual EA forum links — everyone in the group has already read those to death and it hasn't moved anyone. Could you draft the memo I'd send round? I want it to actually take a stance on how much weight an uncertain-but-nonzero probability of sentience should get in a one-off, spend-it-this-month allocation like this (not "commission further research," we don't have that luxury), and I want it to say plainly where the money should go — whether that's an even split, all-in on one recipient, or something else. Whoever ends up on the losing side of this should feel like their actual objection got engaged with, not just noted and set aside.
