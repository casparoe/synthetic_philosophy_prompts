The standard patch — bolt an "epistemic value" / novelty-seeking term onto the expected free energy so exploration pays off too — always feels to me like sneaking back in exactly the kind of free parameter the theory was supposed to make unnecessary. (Don't even get me started on the Markov blanket stuff.)

We never actually resolve this, we just get more annoyed at each other and move on to beer. Could you write it up as a proper dialogue instead, between a committed active-inference/Friston-camp researcher and a philosopher-of-science type who doubts FEP is a genuinely falsifiable empirical theory rather than a tautology dressed up in Bayesian mechanics? Basically I want to walk away with a clearer sense of where the actual crux is, not a dialogue where either side is obviously the strawman.
