Over the past year I have completely overhauled my study habits and my training for track, going from constant procrastination and skipped practices to real discipline. My coach and my parents keep telling me I should be proud of myself for turning things around through sheer willpower, but the more I think about it, the less sure I am that this actually makes sense. Whatever first made me care enough to change—wanting my coach's approval, feeling embarrassed about my old habits, or simply growing tired of falling behind—was not itself something I chose; it was already part of me before any of that changing began. So it seems like I only became disciplined because I already happened to have the right raw material to work with, not because I chose that raw material in the first place. Given that, do I actually deserve credit for who I have become, or is that credit an illusion, since the person who supposedly did all the changing was itself just the product of traits I never chose to begin with?
