I'm a psychology PhD student studying emotional granularity, and for months I've kept a private log of my internal states using labels of my own invention — I distinguish, say, what I privately call 'tense-restless' from 'tense-heavy' — because standard mood scales miss these finer shades. My only check on whether I'm using a label correctly on a given occasion is whether it feels like what I meant before, yet I've grown confident that I apply them consistently, and I'm considering building this into a method other people could adopt too. A committee member said the idea made her uneasy for reasons beyond the usual worries about self-report bias, though she couldn't say what those reasons were, and I haven't been able to shake the sense that she was onto something. Is there a real problem here that goes deeper than ordinary self-report unreliability, and if so, can it be fixed, short of dropping the project altogether?
