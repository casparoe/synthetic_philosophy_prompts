The problem is that if the Equation has to hold not just for your current credence function but for every credence function you might reasonably come to have by learning something new and conditionalizing on it, it turns out to be almost impossible to satisfy without absurd consequences: propositions that obviously aren't independent get forced into independence, and the whole system threatens to collapse into something close to trivial. Something has to be given up. Adams and Edgington give up on conditionals expressing propositions at all. Lewis himself gives up the Equation as a strict law, treating it as at best a pragmatic rule about reasonable assertion.
