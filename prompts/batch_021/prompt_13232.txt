This particular night the backlog spiraled and we had a three hour outage for a decent chunk of customers. So either not-doing-things can be causes, in which case basically everyone who didn't do something useful that month is on the hook and it feels arbitrary to single me out, or not-doing-things can't really be causes, in which case the postmortem is just wrong to pin any of this on me specifically.
