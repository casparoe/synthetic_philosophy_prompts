Retired physiologist here - spent my career on rat hearts, then pig ones for a med device outfit - and now that I've got time on my hands I've been reading philosophy for fun, which is how I stumbled into this etiological vs causal-role fight over what "function" even means. The etiological camp (Wright, I think, some paper from the 70s) says a trait's function is whatever it was selected for back in its evolutionary history; the causal-role camp (Cummins?) says forget the history, a function is just whatever a part contributes to some capacity of the system right now. The bit thats been stuck in my head ever since is the malfunction wrinkle - if function is purely "whatever it currently contributes," then the second a heart stops pumping properly it should just lose that function instead of malfunctioning, which feels backwards. Could you write me a short joke that actually turns on this distinction (extra credit if it works the malfunction wrinkle in), and not just a generic "biologist walks into a bar" pun - I want something that'd actually land for someone who knows the theories, not just sound like science-flavored nerd humor.
