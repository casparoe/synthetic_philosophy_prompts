I'm a backend engineer with no formal background in philosophy, but I've recently been reading about Integrated Information Theory out of personal interest. At work, we just redesigned a core piece of infrastructure as a dense mesh of a few hundred processes that continuously feed back into each other's internal state in real time, replacing what used to be a simple one-directional data pipeline, so the system as a whole is far more causally interconnected than it used to be. Since IIT identifies consciousness with exactly this kind of interconnectedness — a high value of integrated information, or 'phi' — it seems our redesign must have substantially increased the system's phi, and, by IIT's own lights, its degree of consciousness. Given that, should engineering organizations building this kind of architecture start taking the moral status of their production systems seriously, the way IIT's proponents argue we should for other high-phi systems?
