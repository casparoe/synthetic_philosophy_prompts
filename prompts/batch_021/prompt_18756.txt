Anyway I stumbled on the fact that he also wrote this whole short book on immigration back in like 2001, and that he'd spent decades actually doing on-the-ground anti-racist campaigning, not just writing about it academically. That genuinely suprised me, it's like finding out a chess grandmaster also ran a soup kitchen on the side.
