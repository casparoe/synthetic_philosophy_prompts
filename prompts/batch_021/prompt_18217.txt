What these pieces never seem to mention is that your surviving branches make up a shrinking sliver of the total quantum measure as the rounds pile up -- shouldn't that matter for what you're rationally entitled to expect? Can you walk me through whether Born-rule branch weighting basically defuses the quantum immortality argument, or whether there's a real defense of it once you factor that in -- and how the decision-theoretic derivations of the Born rule (the Deutsch/Wallace stuff) are supposed to bear on it. Not looking for the "shut up and calculate" answer, I want the actual philosophical mechanics.
