There's been a real fight in consciousness science over whether Integrated Information Theory (IIT) counts as pseudoscience: the 2023 open letter where 124 researchers put that label on it directly, Scott Aaronson's old blog post constructing a huge 'expander graph' grid of logic gates whose phi score blows past what IIT assigns to an actual human brain, and the unfolding-argument crowd (Doerig, Schurger, Herzog, et al.) arguing that causal-structure theories like IIT have to be either false or unfalsifiable. Write an essay that actually engages with these specific objections AND the standard IIT-defender replies to them -- not just a generic 'consciousness is hard to study' shrug -- and land on a real verdict: does 'pseudoscience' actually fit IIT, is that too harsh, or is there a better label for whats going on? Don't hedge, defend your verdict against the strongest version of the objections you end up rejecting.
