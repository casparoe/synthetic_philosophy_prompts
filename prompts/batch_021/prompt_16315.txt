Ok so I've been down a bit of a youtube rabbit hole on modal realism (don't ask) and I think I've got my head around David Lewis's counterpart theory: when someone says "I could've been a lawyer," that's not literally true of me, its actually true of some similar guy in another possible world, my "counterpart" - him and me aren't numerically the same person, just similar enough to count. But then it hit me: isn't a Star Trek style teleporter doing the exact same thing, just scanning you, destroying the original, and building a similar copy somewhere else? So by Lewis's own logic, whoever beams down the other end isn't really you, its just a counterpart, and the actual you got destroyed back on the ship. So why does every sci-fi show act like teleporting is a totally safe way to travel, when this philosophy is basically telling us its a murder machine?
