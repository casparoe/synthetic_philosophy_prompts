Make them actually engage: the first should press on whether "look ahead and decline" secretly imports a preference for keeping-A-over-entering-the-sequence, i.e. completeness smuggled back in one level up, and the second has to answer whether there's a version of the pump that doesn't rely on the trades happening one at a time, which the foresight move can't touch.
