Exam question: Explain the difficulty that C. B. Martin's "finkish" dispositions pose for the simple conditional analysis of dispositions. Then explain how the phenomenon of masking poses a related but distinct difficulty for that same analysis. Finally, assess whether David Lewis's 1997 reformulated conditional analysis succeeds in handling both difficulties, or only one of them. Illustrate each point with an example.

Her answer: The simple conditional analysis (SCA) says that x is disposed to give response r to stimulus s just in case x would give r if it underwent s — for instance, a vase is fragile iff it would break if struck. Martin's finkish cases show that this biconditional can fail. In his electro-fink example, a wire is dead, but wired to a device that makes it live the instant a conductor touches it, so that although the wire lacks the disposition to conduct, it is nonetheless true that it would conduct if touched. The basis of the disposition (being live) is switched on exactly when the stimulus occurs, so the SCA wrongly certifies a disposition that isn't really there.

Masking, a term due to Mark Johnston, is the mirror-image problem: the object genuinely has the disposition, but something interferes with its manifestation. A finely blown glass fibre wrapped in styrofoam packaging is exactly as fragile as an unwrapped one, yet striking it while wrapped will not break it — the packaging masks the disposition without removing it. So where finks generate false positives for the SCA, masks generate false negatives.

Lewis's 1997 reformulation handles both difficulties at once, by requiring that the disposition's causal basis persist through the stimulus and produce the response "in the appropriate way." Since finking alters the basis at the moment of the stimulus, and masking leaves the basis intact but merely blocks its ordinary causal route to the response, tying the analysis to the basis's persistence and its normal operation rules out the finkish wire and correctly predicts that the wrapped fibre, its basis untouched, remains fragile.
