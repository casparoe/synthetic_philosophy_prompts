My gut says a shaky 70%-ish glimpse should shift my belief that the deploy caused the incident by some amount, just less than a clean, certain observation would, but I can't explain why that's actually legit instead of arbitrary, or how you'd even do it without just making up a number. Is there a real principled way to fold a genuinely uncertain observation like that into how confident you are about something, that isn't just "ignore it" or "treat it as certain"?
