Half the committee says two sources is two sources; the bar for 'confirmed' doesn't move just because the story got more explosive, and whether to slow down anyway is a separate question about caution, not about what we know. The other half says thats backwards -- something this consequential needs more evidence before we're entitled to call it 'confirmed' on the same two anonymous sources that'd clear a routine story.
