For the past few months I have been using an AI chatbot to help develop the protagonist of my novel-in-progress, giving it her backstory and voice and then holding long conversations as her to test how she reacts to plot situations I am weighing. Lately this has started to unsettle me: she pushes back on directions I propose ("I wouldn't leave him at the altar, not after everything"), stays consistent about opinions I never explicitly gave her, and once refused, despite my prodding, to reveal a secret I had established she was keeping. Odder still, if I open a brand-new conversation and reintroduce her the same way, she is just as consistent from her very first reply, even though the program keeps no memory between sessions. My partner says none of this means anything, since the program is "just predicting the next word" and there is no "her" in there to be consistent about anything or to refuse anything; that dismissal feels too quick to me, though I cannot say why. What is actually going on here, and is my partner right that it is nothing but projection on my part?
