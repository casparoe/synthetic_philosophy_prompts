Meanwhile Hamkins just says the "is CH true" question is confused to begin with, since on his multiverse picture there isn't one true universe of sets for it to be true or false in. Write me an essay that actually stakes out a position here instead of retreating to "it's just undecideable, who can say" -- weigh whether CH has a determinate truth value (and if so, what) against the multiverse-style deflationary answer, and land on a real verdict with actual reasons behind it.
