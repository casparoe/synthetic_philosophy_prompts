I'm taking a course on existentialism this semester, and outside of the assigned readings, I've noticed something in my own life that I can't quite untangle. Since starting university, I've become known among my friends as "the mature one" — the person who stays calm, plans everything, and never seems overwhelmed. I built this reputation through my own choices, but at this point it feels less like something I do and more like something I simply am. Even on days when I would rather fall apart like everyone else gets to, acting that way feels almost impossible, as though it wouldn't really be me. Is this sense of being trapped by my own reputation a genuine limit on my freedom, or is it something I'm imposing on myself?
