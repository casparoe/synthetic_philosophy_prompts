Last night I fell into one of those 2am research holes that started with a Fermi paradox video and somehow ended with me reading about "anthropic reasoning" on some philosophy blog, and now I can't stop thinking about this thing called the presumptuous philosopher. From what I can piece together there's two rival rules for how you're supposed to reason about your own existence: the Self-Sampling Assumption (SSA), which says you should treat yourself as basically a random pick among all the observers that actually exist in your "reference class," and the Self-Indication Assumption (SIA), which says you should also weight rival theories by how many observers each one predicts in the first place, since a theory where way more people exist gives "you" way more chances to have ended up existing at all. The presumptuous philosopher bit is this scenario where physicists are about to run a careful experiment to settle whether the universe is "small" (theory T1) or absurdly huge, with like a trillion times more observers in it (theory T2) — and both theories are equally backed by all the actual evidence collected so far — and then this philosopher struts in and says don't even bother running the experiment, because SIA already tells him T2 is basically certain, purely from the observer head-count. Every writeup I found treats this as a huge embarassment for SIA, but apparently SSA isn't clean either, since its the same assumption that generates the Doomsday Argument (updating toward humanity going extinct sooner because of your birth rank) plus this whole unsolved "reference class" problem of who even counts as similar enough to you to sample from.

I want the SSA person to really press the SIA person on the presumptuous philosopher thing, and the SIA person to hit back just as hard on the doomsday-argument/reference-class mess on the SSA side, so by the end both positions look genuinely shaky rather than one being obviously right. You don't have to declare a winner, I just want both sides actually argued at full strength for once instead of the usual "SIA bad lol" take everyone online seems to repeat.
