Would he treat this as a godless cousin of pre-established harmony that his system could readily accommodate, or would he insist that genuine coordination between substances is impossible without a coordinating God? Please reconstruct the strongest argument he would most plausibly make, engaging specifically with his views on monads, the principle of sufficient reason, and pre-established harmony.
