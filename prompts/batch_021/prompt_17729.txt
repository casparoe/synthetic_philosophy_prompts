One of my doctoral students has, for two years running, chosen not to apply for our department's competitive research fellowships, opting instead to remain a teaching assistant, even though a fellowship would pay more and look better on their CV. I concluded that they simply prefer teaching to research and stopped encouraging them to apply. A colleague on their committee has since suggested a different reading: that they actually want a fellowship but doubt they would be awarded one, and so avoid applying rather than risk a rejection that would confirm those doubts. When I asked them directly, they said they just prefer teaching, but I'm not convinced that settles anything, since it's exactly what they would say either way. Their choices so far seem equally compatible with both readings, and I don't see how continuing to watch what they do next would break the tie. Which reading should I take more seriously, and is there a principled way to decide between them, or is there simply no way to tell?
