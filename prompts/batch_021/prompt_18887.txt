I tried to argue he's got more resources then that, but honestly I don't know his metaphysics well enough to make it stick. So: if you sat Leibniz down and explained many-worlds/modal realism to him, what do you think he'd actually say? Would he have to ditch the principle of the best, double down that our world is still uniquely optimal among all the branches/worlds, or is there some other move available to him?
