Ok so I think I actually solved something that's supposedly been a big deal in philosophy for ages. Akrasia (weakness of will) is supposed to be this weird thing where you act against your own better judgement, like you know eating the donut is worse for you but you eat it anyway. But isnt that just hyperbolic discounting? My brain discounts the future on a curve instead of a straight line, so in the actual moment of choosing, the discounted value of donut-now genuinely comes out higher for me than salad-later. So theres no real clash with my judgement happening at all, im just doing what my own (correctly calculated) preferences say to do in that moment. Doesnt that mean the whole akrasia "paradox" basically dissolves once you plug in a proper discounting curve, and philosophers were just missing the math?
