I want to write a scene where two of her friends argue about whether she can even rationally decide this at all.

Corvin thinks the whole transformative experience problem (yes, I've been reading L.A. Sella thinks that's basically dressing up a leap of faith as philosophy, and insists there's still real reasoning to be done -- testimony, thinking hard about what she wants to stay true of her no matter what happens, whatever -- even without knowing the exact texture of the outcome in advance.

Can you write their argument as an actual back-and-forth, not two monologues -- they need to respond to each others specific points instead of just restating their own.
