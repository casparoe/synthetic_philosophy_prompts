So I'm a PhD student in vertebrate paleontology, and my coauthors and I can't agree on how to word the "function" part of a paper. We've got a small troodontid-type specimen with pennaceous feathers on the forearms, but arms too short and feathers too symmetric for it to have flown or glided decently. My advisor wants to say the feathers' function is thermoregulation, since thats presumably why feathers first evolved in this lineage's ancestors way back. My coauthor thinks that's weird to say about THIS animal specifically, since the feathers are only on the forearms, brightly patterned, and barely insulating anything - she thinks the function is obviously display. Then a reviewer on our last draft wanted us to call them "incipient wings" with an aerodynamic function, even tho the animal was a pretty lousy flier. Now I don't even know what "function" is supposed to mean here - is one of these three clearly right and the others confused, or are we all just answering different questions without realizing it?
