A close friend of mine has a three-year-old son and posts about him regularly on social media — potty-training struggles, tantrums, and, most recently, a video of a meltdown at a birthday party that ended up circulating well beyond her own followers. When I raised the issue, she said there is nothing wrong with any of it: he is far too young to be embarrassed, to object, or to have any view on the matter, and in any case she plans to delete everything long before he is old enough to go looking for it himself. I don't have children of my own, so I've hesitated to press the point, but her reasoning doesn't sit right with me. Is she doing something wrong here, and if so, to whom — given that, on her own account, no one who could ever be embarrassed by it will actually encounter it?
