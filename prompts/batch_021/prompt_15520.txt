About two months back I donated a kidney to a total stranger (a non-directed donation that ended up kicking off a whole chain of other transplants) after getting kind of obsessed with effective altruism blogs, and ever since my family keeps calling me a saint or a hero, which honestly makes me squirm. My cousin, whos doing a philosophy PhD, told me this is basically a textbook case of what ethicists call 'supererogation' -- the old-school view is that going beyond duty is exactly what makes an act praiseworthy, but apparently theres a whole Peter-Singer-descended camp that thinks if the cost to you is low enough and the benefit is big enough, your fancy 'above and beyond' act was actually obligatory the whole time, no extra credit. Can you write me a joke about supererogation that actually turns on that tension? Not a generic 'guy gives away a kidney' joke, and not one that makes it obvious I don't really get the concept.
