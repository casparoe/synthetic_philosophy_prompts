Priya then: "ok, now we basically do know each other's info, and you moved to 45 and I moved to 75 — we're still 30 apart, so your objection is gone and the puzzle's still sitting right there." Dan's comeback: "we're not running the same math though — you're anchoring off the company-wide renewal rate, I'm anchoring off similar-sized accounts specifically, that's a different starting point, not an error." Priya, last word: "fine, but if 'rational' just means 'internally consistent with whatever starting point you picked,' then the whole idea that two rational people can't knowingly stay 30 points apart is empty — so which starting point is the mistake?"
