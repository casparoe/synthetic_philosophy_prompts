That feels like exactly whats happening with Dave, going from being a soldier back to being a regular guy, and I dont think anyone, maybe not even him, knows who that guy is yet. The tricky part is half the room is going to be guys he served with who'll get what I'm getting at right away, and the other half is his wife and his mom who just want to hear that everything's going to be great now that he's home safe. Can you write the toast for me? It needs to be honest that nobody can actually promise him this is going to be good, without turning into a downer, and it still has to sound like a toast and not a warning.
