That felt like a bit of a bait-and-switch to me: the common prior assumption is doing basically all the work in that arguement, and nobody in the room agreed on whether it's an innocent modeling device or a real (and kind of unjustifiable) constraint on where rational beliefs are allowed to come from in the first place. I brought it up after class with a friend from undergrad who does quant trading now and she just laughed and said priors are obviously personal, full stop, no further questions.
