People who write about autonomous systems (self-driving cars, autonomous weapons, algorithmic hiring/lending tools, etc) throw around the term "responsibility gap" a lot, but I think that's actually two different problems getting mashed together: (1) we genuinly can't tell which part of the system or which person caused the harm, vs (2) we know exactly what caused it, but there's nobody it actually makes sense to blame or punish (I've seen this second one called the "retribution gap"). Give me a solid example of case (2) specifically -- full causal traceability, but punishment still feels pointless or unfair -- and please don't just use the usual killer-robot/autonomous-weapons scenario, that one's used for literally everything in this space.
