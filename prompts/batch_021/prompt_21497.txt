I'm a novelist, which is apparently all the qualification my family needs to hand me my brother's wedding toast. He's marrying Priya, who's finishing a dissertation on the buck-passing account of value -- specifically the 'wrong kind of reasons' problem, the one about a demon who'll torture you unless you learn to love a saucer of mud, which gives you a reason to love it but not the kind that makes it actually good. Half the guests are her philosophy cohort, who'll clock it instantly if I lean on this; the rest are aunts and uncles who've never heard of Scanlon or 'fitting attitudes' and just want a nice toast. Write me a five-minute toast that gets a real joke out of the wrong-kind-of-reasons idea -- ideally something about how nobody in the literature has actually agreed what the 'right' reasons for loving someone even are, so my brother's off the hook either way -- without ever implying he married her for the wrong ones, and without it turning into a seminar. Also, no sappiness, thats not our family.
