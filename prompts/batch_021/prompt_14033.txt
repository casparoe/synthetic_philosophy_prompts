I'm on my small town's charter revision commission -- token philosopher among a bunch of engineers and shopkeepers, so naturally this one landed on my desk. Roughly half our property tax base (which funds basically everything, including the schools) comes from seasonal homeowners who live elsewhere most of the year and have no say here at all; meanwhile full-time renters, who pay no property tax directly, get to vote on everything at town meeting. A vocal seasonal-owner faction wants a limited franchise -- budget articles only, since its mostly their money paying for it. A resident faction says no representation without residency, full stop, and the charter just says "legal voters of the town," no carve-outs either way. Can you draft the short recommendation memo I need to circulate before we vote on this at the meeting on the 12th? I want an actual recommendation, not a list of pros and cons, and I want whichever side loses to get a fair hearing, not a strawman.
