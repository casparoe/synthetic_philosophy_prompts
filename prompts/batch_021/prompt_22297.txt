The Turing test is invoked so often in rationalist and rationalist-adjacent writing that it's easy to forget Turing's original 1950 paper actually lists nine separate objections to the imitation game, each of which he answers in turn. The ninth and final objection is the "Argument from Extra-Sensory Perception," where Turing concedes that "the statistical evidence, at least for telepathy, is overwhelming" and proposes to salvage the test by shutting the human and machine contestants inside a "telepathy-proof room." I'd like a joke built specifically around this passage, with a punchline that depends on some real feature of Turing's fix — for instance, that an ordinary wall seems like an odd thing to trust against a channel supposedly capable of bypassing ordinary physical channels altogether, or that a psychic contestant would enjoy a side-channel of information the machine has no way of matching, quietly undermining the whole premise of the test. Please don't settle for a generic "even geniuses believe silly things" joke; the humor should only really land for someone who has actually read objection nine.
