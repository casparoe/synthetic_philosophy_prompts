So I was an entomologist before I retired, and something that happened partway through my career has been nagging at me since I started reading philosophy for fun. Back in the 80s I described a new ground beetle species from a small series I'd collected myself. The holotype got chewed up by museum pests years later, but two paratypes survive from that exact same collecting event -- same log, same afternoon -- and we've since run DNA on them that matches the original description in every way we can measure. A curator friend thinks we should just designate one of the paratypes as the neotype and move on, since nothing about the science changes. She's probably right, but it still nags at me like we'd be loosing something real, not just sentimental attachment to a particular dead bug. Is that feeling actually tracking something, or am I just being precious about which chunk of chitin gets to count as "the" one?
