Here's what's bugging me though. So: if Nagarjuna somehow got his hands on Reasons and Persons, would he recognize Parfit as basically landing on a slice of his own position via analytic philosophy, or would he run the tetralemma on Parfit's own psychological states/events and conclude Parfit just relocated the same error one level down?
