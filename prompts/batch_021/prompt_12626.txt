Write a dialogue between an updateless decision theorist and a causal decision theorist about the counterfactual mugging case: Omega (a perfect predictor) flips a fair coin, it lands tails, and Omega shows up asking you for $100 -- explaining that if the coin had landed heads instead, you would've gotten $10,000, but only because Omega predicted you'd be willing to pay up in the tails case. Get the two of them actually arguing it out over several exchanges, each responding to what the other just said instead of restating their own view, and don't let either side be a pushover -- they should both have genuinely good moves, not just their opening position said again more confidently.
