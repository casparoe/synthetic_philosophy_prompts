I am currently reporting a piece on intensive meditation retreats, and for a firsthand angle I have signed up for a ten-day silent retreat next month. In interviews with people who have already completed it, I have found a striking pattern: those who arrive, like me, believing they have a stable, continuous self almost always leave convinced that the self is an illusion, and they insist this is not an opinion they were argued into but something they simply came to see. I currently find that claim implausible, but given how reliable the pattern is, I can predict with real confidence that I will feel just as convinced by the tenth day. Does that prediction alone give me good reason to start taking the claim more seriously now, before the retreat has done anything to me? Or would it be a mistake to let a conclusion I can already see coming, but have not yet reached on my own, change my mind in advance?
