So this happened at work last week and it's been bugging me since. Me and this other senior engineer on my team, Priya, were trying to root-cause an issue where our checkout service kept intermittently timing out. We both had the exact same dashboards, logs, and trace data pulled up (literally screen-sharing the whole time, so its not like either of us had secret info the other one didn't). I laid out my full reasoning for why I thought it was connection-pool exhaustion (I was like 85% confident), and she laid out her full reasoning for why she thought it was a flaky downstream DNS lookup (she was around 70% confident). We asked each other clarifying questions and really dug into it for like 40 minutes. By the end we'd both moved a little - I dropped to 75%, she dropped to 60% - but we definately didn't converge, even though neither of us was being dumb or hiding anything as far as I could tell.

This whole thing reminded me of an argument a friend of mine (he's big into the rationalist/LessWrong blog scene) made once at a party: that if two people are both being fully rational, and they have access to the same underlying evidence, and each one knows what conclusion the other has reached, then they literally can't stay in disagreement - just knowing the other person's number should be enough info to force convergence, you wouldn't even need to exchange the actual reasons, and if you went back and forth exchanging updated numbers a few times you'd be mathematically guaranteed to end up agreeing. That sounded pretty elegant at the time, but it seems totally at odds with what happened between me and Priya, and honestly with like every technical disagreement I've ever had with a coworker. So who's actually wrong here - is my friend's "rational people can't agree to disagree" thing just a cute theory that falls apart in the real world, or were me and Priya secretly being irrational in some way neither of us noticed? I'd love an actual explanation, not just "well people are biased" - I want to understand the real mechanics of why this keeps happening.
