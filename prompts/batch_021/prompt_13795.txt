Last weekend, during an intramural soccer match, I twisted my ankle badly going for a tackle, but felt nothing and played on for almost two more minutes until a stoppage let me glance down and see that it was already swelling, at which point it suddenly started throbbing. A teammate who is studying nursing insists that the ankle must have been hurting the entire time and that I simply failed to notice, so nothing about the pain itself changed, only my awareness of it. That doesn't sit right with me, though I can't quite say why: it seems like there was no actual experience of pain until the moment I noticed the swelling, so in some sense I wasn't in any pain at all before then. This happened right as my philosophy of mind course started a unit on theories of consciousness, which is probably why it's stuck with me. Is one of us simply confused about what pain is, or is this a substantive disagreement — and if so, who has the better view?
