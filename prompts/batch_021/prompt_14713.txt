I'm a novelist whose last three books have quietly indicted consumer excess, especially the small ways people buy their way out of climate guilt while insisting they still care. A recent magazine profile pointed out, accurately, that I fly to several literary festivals a year and just finished renovating a second house, and argued that this hypocrisy means readers should discount whatever moral seriousness the novels claim to have. I'm less interested in defending my own conduct than in getting the philosophy right before I respond publicly. Please describe the most important implications of the philosophical literature on standing to blame and hypocrisy for whether my personal inconsistency can actually diminish the moral force of the books themselves, as opposed to merely compromising my standing to lecture readers about it in interviews.
