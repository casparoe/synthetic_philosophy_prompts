The other half, led by our board president (who fell down a wikipedia hole on Arrow's impossibility theorem), says its mathematically proven that no ranked system is ever fully fair, so we shouldn't bother fixing anything and should just keep doing RCV forever. I have to write the committee's memo to the board before next month's meeting -- can you draft it (a page, not ten)?
