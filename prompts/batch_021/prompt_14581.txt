He also went off about how this exact chapter is apparently what Keynes and that whole Cambridge/Bloomsbury crowd treated like scripture as young men, while conveniently skipping over Moore's much duller chapter on actual duty and conduct, which Tom finds hilarious and also a bit of a philosophical own-goal on their part.
