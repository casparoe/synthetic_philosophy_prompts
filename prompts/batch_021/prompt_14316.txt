Could you draft me eight to ten interview questions for the taping? For each of those three, give me a follow-up keyed to whatever answer she's most likely to give, including at least one way to call her on the "flicker, not a freedom" line if she tries to use it as a dodge rather than an answer. Round out the rest of the list with other questions a well-prepared interviewer in this literature would ask.
