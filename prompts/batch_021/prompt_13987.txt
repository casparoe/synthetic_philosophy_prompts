Okay, I've been down a rabbit hole this week rereading Turing's original 1950 Mind paper -- "Computing Machinery and Intelligence," the one everyone cites for the imitation game and then apparently never reads past page four. Everyone quotes the Lady Lovelace objection and the "heads in the sand" bit, but almost nobody talks about the ninth and final objection in section 6, the Argument from Extra-Sensory Perception, where Turing basically concedes the statistical evidence for telepathy is "overwhelming" (his word, iirc) and then seriously proposes sticking everyone in a "telepathy-proof room" so a psychic human can't give the game away by reading the interrogator's mind. I mentioned this in an ACX open thread and about half the people didnt believe me until someone linked the actual PDF.
