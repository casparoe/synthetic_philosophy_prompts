Since starting therapy for anxiety a few months ago, I've kept a private mood log using a shorthand of my own invention, partly so that family members who might flip through my notebook won't know exactly what I'm tracking: a dot for a panic episode, a dash for intrusive thoughts about work, a circle for a good day, and so on. When I described this system to a friend who recently finished a philosophy degree, she told me it was a real-life version of the "private language" that Wittgenstein showed to be incoherent, and that, strictly speaking, there can be no fact about whether I am applying my own symbols consistently from one week to the next, no matter how careful I try to be. That seems to undermine the whole point of keeping the log for my therapist in the first place. Is she applying Wittgenstein's argument correctly to my case, or is she conflating two things that merely happen to look alike?
