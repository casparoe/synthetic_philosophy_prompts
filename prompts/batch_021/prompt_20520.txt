My nephew, an avid amateur astronomer since childhood, has never forgiven the International Astronomical Union for demoting Pluto in 2006. What bothers him is not sentimentality but a specific asymmetry he has noticed: astronomers are perfectly happy to call a small, cool star a "red dwarf" without any suggestion that it isn't really a star, yet the very same modifier, attached to "planet," was defined in such a way that the object in question does not count as a planet at all. Whenever he raises this, colleagues wave it away as mere semantics and tell him to let it go. I'm not convinced the semantics are quite so idle here, and I would welcome a second opinion: does he have a genuine point, or is his objection resting on some kind of mistake about how naming and classification are supposed to work?
