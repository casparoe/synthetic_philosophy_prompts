Nina got a little defensive and said no, it makes perfect sense: the evidence-following part of her brain has done the math and landed on 'he's lying,' but there's some other part of her, the part that actually runs how she feels and acts, that hasn't caught up yet -- and that gap between the two IS what believing something even means, over and above just being intellectually convinced of it. Then, kind of to back herself up, she added 'and I'm not just guessing either, I've literally seen the bank statements, I know he's lying.' Marcus jumped on that right away -- he said if you know something then you for sure believe it, that's like the bare minimum of what knowing even means, so now she wasn't just saying something poetically weird, she'd straight up contradicted herself in one breath.
