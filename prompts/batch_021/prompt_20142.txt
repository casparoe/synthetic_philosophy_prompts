Last year I fell down a rabbit hole of autism TikToks, recognized a ton of myself in them, joined a couple online groups, and eventually recieved an official diagnosis. Heres the thing thats been bugging me: before any of that I never would've called my bad days "masking" or "meltdowns", I just would've said I was tired or annoyed, but now those are literally the words I use for my own life and I act differently in situations I used to just push through. My partner thinks I picked up a trendy script off the internet thats making things worse not better, and says I should quit the groups and forget the label. Thing is, the community's whole idea of what autism even is keeps shifting too, as more people like me join and describe stuff in new ways, and from what I've read the official diagnostic manual has gone through similar changes over the decades. So I genuinely can't tell if getting the label helped me finally see something true about myself that was there all along, or if learning the label is actually what changed who I am and how I act, maybe even both at once. Should I listen to my partner and back off, or is there something real enough here to keep going with?
