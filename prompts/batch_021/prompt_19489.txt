Knowing what she was doing with her Feuerbach translation (the one where God basically turns out to be humanity's own best qualities projected outward) and how she tore into Dr. Cummings for using hellfire to dodge actual moral effort, what are the most important implications of her specific version of post-Christian ethics for how I write this character? I want her moral intensity to feel earned on the page, not like I just parachuted in a secular humanist to make a point.
