I sit on my company's ethics and compliance committee. We must decide whether a plant director violated our code's ban on intentionally jeopardizing employee safety: she cut the equipment-maintenance budget to hit a quarterly cost target, fully aware it would raise minor injury rates, and maintains the injuries were never her aim, just an accepted side effect. Half the committee calls this intentional harm because she knowingly accepted a foreseen risk; I doubt anyone would call the same tradeoff 'intentional' had the side effect been a safety improvement instead, which suggests our real standard is how bad the outcome felt rather than any fact about her state of mind. Please draft the committee's short decision memo: a finding plus reasoning that the other half could actually engage with.
