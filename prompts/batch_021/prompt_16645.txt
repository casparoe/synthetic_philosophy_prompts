So this argument happened on my pub quiz team between two people, Marcus and Denise, who are both way more into physics podcasts than the rest of us. It somehow got onto Boltzmann brains -- the idea that if the universe just keeps expanding forever, random quantum fluctuations out in empty space could eventually spit out a fully-formed brain with fake memories of a whole life, and given infinite time there'd end up being way more of those floating fake-memory brains than real ones like us who got here the normal way. Marcus said this is actually a real argument physicists use against cosmological models where the universe expands forever -- if a theory implies that almost every "observer" with memories exactly like yours is one of these random flukes rather than a real evolved person, that's basically the theory refuting itself, so we should throw out any model that implies it. Denise said thats a weird move, because you could "refute" any theory that way, just by imagining some scenario where fake yous outnumber real yous -- it's brain-in-a-vat skepticism wearing a lab coat, and either we take that seriously everywhere or we dont get to single out cosmology for it. Marcus said it's not the same, because the fluke-brain idea makes an actual prediction beyond generic skepticism: if you really were a random fluctuation, the next second your experience should almost certainly dissolve into noise, since nothing keeps a fluke cohering, so the fact that the world keeps hanging together moment to moment is live evidence against it, which ordinary vat-skepticism never risks. Denise shot back that this quietly assumes you can trust your sense that things "keep hanging together" at all, which is exactly what's being questioned -- a fluke-brain would, for its one instant, also seem to itself to have a coherent past and every reason to expect more of the same. Neither one gave any ground. Can you go through this move by move and say who's got the better of it at each point, not just who wins overall, and point out anything you think they're both missing?
