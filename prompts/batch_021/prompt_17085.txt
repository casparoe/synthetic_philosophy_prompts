(2) Colin said that's equivocating on "rational" -- meaning "governed by discoverable laws," which is all Gödel's really entitled to, doesn't get you "rational" meaning "fair to individuals" or "not wasteful of potential." You can't pull cosmic fairness out of mere lawlikeness.

(4) Colin said that only gets you "materialism might be false," which is way short of an afterlife -- plenty of dualists think consciousness still switches off for good once the body's gone, so non-identity alone doesn't buy you persistence.

(5) Nadia said Colin was holding this to a standard Gödel never claimed to meet -- he only ever said an afterlife was more reasonable to believe than not, not that he'd derived it, so "this doesn't prove persistence" kind of misses the point.
