I can follow the formal argument line by line -- something like: if two rational Bayesians with a common prior are both willing to trade an asset, the mere fact that the other guy is still willing to trade at that price is itself evidence, and once you factor that in and iterate, whatever gap you thought existed between your two valuations should shrink to nothing.
