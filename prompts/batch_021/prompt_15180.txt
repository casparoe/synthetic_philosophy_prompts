Okay so I was up way too late last night arguing with my friend Mara about Schopenhauer's chapter on music in The World as Will and Representation (Book 3, §52, in the old Haldane/Kemp translation) and I need someone to help me sort out who's actually right, because I don't think either of us nailed it.

Early in the section he insists music is totally unlike the other arts because it skips the Platonic Ideas entirely and copies the will direclty: "In it we do not recognise the copy or repetition of any Idea of existence in the world... Music is thus by no means like the other arts, the copy of the Ideas, but the copy of the will itself, whose objectivity the Ideas are." Fine. But then like two pages later he does this whole bit mapping the four voices of harmony onto the actual hierarchy of Ideas: "in the whole of the complemental parts which make up the harmony between the bass and the leading voice singing the melody, I recognise the whole gradation of the Ideas in which the will objectifies itself. Those nearer to the bass are the lower of these grades... the higher represent to me the world of plants and beasts... even the impure discords, which give no definite interval, may be compared to the monstrous abortions produced by beasts of two species, or by man and beast."

Mara's whole argument was that this is just a flat contradiction — if the bass-to-melody structure directly corresponds to mineral/plant/animal/human, then music obviously IS representing the Ideas, so the "skips the Ideas, copies the will directly" line collapses. I tried to argue there's some real distinction hiding in there (like reproducing the relations between grades vs. representing the Ideas themselves as objects) but couldn't actually make it stick. Is there a genuine way to reconcile these two bits, or did he just not notice he was undercutting his own big claim a couple paragraphs later? And separately, what's that discord/cross-species-monstrosity comparison even doing there — is it load-bearing for the argument or just a weird flourish that doesn't really fit?
