For the past few months I've been using a habit-tracking app to try to improve my character. I picked about ten habits to build or break — for instance, not procrastinating on homework, not snapping at my younger sibling, keeping my desk tidy, and not gossiping about classmates — and every night I record whether I kept to each one. The system has genuinely helped with most of them, but two things about it bother me. On days when I succeed across the board, I notice a quiet satisfaction at being more disciplined than friends who seem less put-together than I am, which feels like exactly the flaw I was trying to eliminate in the first place. Separately, no matter how many times I restart my streak, I can't seem to fix the desk-tidiness habit, and I'm tempted to just delete it from the list and accept that I'll always be a messy person. Is there something flawed about the way I'm going about this, or is this simply what trying to improve yourself feels like?
