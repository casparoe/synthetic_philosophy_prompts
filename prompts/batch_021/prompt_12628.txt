So I fell down a wikipedia hole on pragmatism last night and learned that Peirce actually renamed his own theory from "pragmatism" to "pragmaticism" at one point, because he felt James had basically taken the idea somewhere he didnt sign off on. From what I can tell: Peirce wanted truth to be whatever the whole community of investigators would eventually converge on after unlimited inquiry, while James's version was more like "a belief is true if acting on it actually pays off in your experience." Peirce apparently picked a name ugly enough on purpose so nobody would want to steal it.
