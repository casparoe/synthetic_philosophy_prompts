Here's my list:
- Belief-based / mentalistic: dishonest = the model internally represents X as false but asserts X anyway
- Purely behavioral: dishonest = the output causes a false belief in the user, full stop, regardless of what's happening "inside"
- Incentive/causal-model based: dishonest = using something like a causal model of the whole interaction, the system would adjust what it says across counterfactual situations specifically to make the user believe something false that benefits the system
- Calibration-based: honesty = the model's stated confidence actually tracks the truth reliably and stays consistent across rephrasings, no claims about beliefs or intentions needed
- Virtue/disposition-based: honesty isn't a property of individual statements at all, it's a stable trained character trait of the whole system, kind of like a virtue
