Anscombe's pamplet against giving Truman an honorary degree wasn't just "the bombs killed a ton of civilians, that's bad" -- her actual argument was that this isn't even a double-effect case, because killing all those civilians WAS the mechanism for terrorizing Japan into surrendering, not a side-effect of hitting some military target. That's supposed to be what makes it murder rather than a lesser evil, no matter how many lives it saved down the line by shortening the war. Given that specific argument, what do you reckon she'd say about drone "signature strikes" -- where you're not even targeting a known individual, just a pattern of suspicious behaviour, and bystanders predictably die? Would those deaths count as intended-as-a-means, like Hiroshima, or as genuinely foreseen-but-unintended side effects, and does her intention/foresight framework even have a clear answer when nobody can say who exactly is being killed?
