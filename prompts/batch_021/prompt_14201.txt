Quick one for a piece I'm working on about AI safety debates. I keep seeing people cite this "Löbian obstacle" thing to argue that an AI literally can't ever trust its own reasoning (or a successor system's) because of Löb's theorem, and that this basically proves "provably safe" AI is a mathematical impossibility, kind of like how Gödel proved math can't be both complete and consistent. If thats really what the theorem shows, why do alignment researchers keep chasing formal verification and self-trust schemes at all instead of just admitting its a dead end? Feels like the math should've killed that whole line of research years ago.
