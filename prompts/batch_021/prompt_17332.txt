Write an essay that adjudicates the dispute between substantivalism and relationism about space and time. The classic terms of the debate are set by Newton's bucket argument for absolute space and Leibniz's relational response -- the shift argument together with the principle of sufficient reason -- but a serious treatment should also engage with how the dispute has been reshaped by general relativity, in particular the hole argument and the substantivalist rejoinders it has provoked, such as sophisticated substantivalism's denial of haecceitism about spacetime points, as well as with what role Mach's principle should play in the argument. Weigh the strongest considerations on each side and defend a definite verdict about which position -- or which qualified version of one -- is best supported, rather than concluding merely that the dispute is terminological or permanently undecidable.
