In my spare time I trade systematically and size my positions using the Kelly criterion. Reading further into the theory, I came across the idea of "geometric rationality": because wealth compounds multiplicatively across bets, geometric expectation (maximizing E[log W]) is the objectively correct decision criterion for anything that plays out over repeated rounds, which makes ordinary arithmetic expected-value reasoning a kind of category error whenever outcomes compound over time. This seems to generalize well beyond trading, so at work I've been wanting to replace our usual EV-weighted scoring for prioritizing multi-quarter engineering bets with a geometric-mean version instead, since those decisions compound too. What's the right way to adapt the Kelly framework to that kind of roadmap decision-making?
