So if he found out there were two whole extra planets out there, both basically invisible to the naked eye, that were never factored into any of that, would it wreck his theory, or is that the kind of thing his own logic could just absorb? I definately want to get his actual reaction right rather than generic "whoa, new planets" amazement, so any specifics on how his framework would actually handle it would help a lot -- don't want some early-modern-astronomy nerd tearing the book apart in reviews for getting this wrong.
