So I just recieved my first ever jury duty summons, and last night I fell down a rabbit hole reading about the Condorcet jury theorem instead of sleeping. From what I can tell, it proves that the more people you put on a jury, the closer the majority verdict gets to being 100% likely to be correct, as long as each juror is even a little better than a coin flip at getting it right. So why do courts still cap juries at 12 and require a unanimous verdict, instead of just grabbing 100 random people and going with whatever the majority says? Feels like we're leaving free accuracy on the table, which isn't super reassuring now that I might be one of the 12 deciding someone's fate next month.
