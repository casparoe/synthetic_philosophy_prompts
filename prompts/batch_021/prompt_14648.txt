I get why the rule exists, but I could not stop thinking about that fawn for days, and it sent me down a rabbit hole reading about "wild animal suffering" as a whole area people actually argue about. Apparently there are folks in effective altruism circles who think this might be one of the biggest sources of suffering on the planet, bigger than factory farming even, mostly because of how many wild animals there are and how most species (especially insects and fish) just pump out huge numbers of offspring that are basically guaranteed to starve, get sick, or get eaten within days of being born -- I think the term for that is r-selection? My roommate, who's way more into EA than me, brought this up super casually over dinner like it was obvious we should be doing something about it, and it kind of freaked me out.
