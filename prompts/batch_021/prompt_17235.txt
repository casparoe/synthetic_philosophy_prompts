Had a two-hour argument with my friend last night about my grandma and I still can't stop thinking about it. Years ago, while she was still totally with it, she signed an advance directive saying that once her dementia got bad enough that she no longer recognized family, she wanted no antibiotics, no feeding tube, comfort care only - basically, let her go. She's at that point now - doesn't know any of us most days, but she's not unhappy, hums along to the radio, and lights up over pudding. My friend says its obvious we have to honor the directive, that's literally her signature and her stated wishes. I said the woman in that bed now barely shares any memories, personality, or values with the woman who signed that paper 15 yrs ago, so its not clear the directive is even hers to enforce anymore - are we respecting her autonomy or just obeying a stranger who happens to be wearing her body? Neither of us budged. Who's actually right, and why?
