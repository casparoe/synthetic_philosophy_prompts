About eight months ago, a colleague and I both put ourselves forward for the same internal promotion; the decision keeps getting deferred while management asks each of us, in turn, to take on another unpaid 'stretch' project to prove we're ready — work that will only pay off for whichever of us is eventually promoted, and be a complete waste of time for the other. Yet every time she takes on one more project, I feel compelled to match her, since walking away now would guarantee that my hours were wasted, whereas staying in at least keeps alive the chance that they weren't. My friend's verdict was that I'm simply refusing to accept that those hours are already gone no matter what happens next and that I should quit immediately, but that explanation doesn't feel complete to me, since this isn't a case of one person alone deciding whether to keep funding a project of their own. Is my friend right, and if there's more going on here than that, what is it, and how should it change what I do next?
