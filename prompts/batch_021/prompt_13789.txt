So this has been bugging me since it happened a few weeks back. I write about science for a living, and I published a piece gently pushing back on a popular health claim that had gone viral - nothing spicy, just "the evidence here is thinner than people think." Within a day I had dozens of jabs across the comments and social media, from people who dont know each other and definitely werent coordinating, each one landing a quick dig and then moving on - no single person made a sustained case against me, and most of them probably wouldn't say any of it to my face, but somehow the sheer pile of it felt way more damning than any one real critique could. Then, separately, a colleague who actually works in the field sent me a long, genuinely harsh email tearing into my argument point by point, and weirdly that felt completely different, even though his points were sharper - it felt like being critiqued instead of being judged. I can't figure out why those two things feel so categorically different when on paper they're both just "people didn't like my article" - what's actually going on there, and should it change how I think about handling the online pile-on stuff going forward?
