My dad and I have been redoing his basement ourselves the last few weekends, and two days ago we got to the point where we had to close up this little mechanical nook — some old disused pipes and a bit of ancient wiring that hasn't been used in decades — before pouring a new concrete slab right over it. Once that slab goes down, thats it forever: nobody is ever opening that spot up again, not for the rest of the life of the house probably. I caught myself wanting to actually tidy everything up first, zip-tie the wires so they sat neatly, maybe even give the little exposed section a quick coat of paint, even though I knew full well literally no living person would ever lay eyes on it again once it's sealed.

My dad thought this was funny and kind of pointless. He said something like "its going to be buried in concrete, man, it doesn't matter if it looks nice, hurry up so we can pour before it gets dark," and logically I can't really argue with him — there's genuinely no one who will ever benefit from it looking good instead of just functional. But it still felt like it mattered, somehow, in a way I haven't been able to shake since. I keep going back and forth between thinking I'm just being weirdly compulsive about a basement nobody will ever see, versus feeling like there's something actually real going on where it should matter even though no one, ever, will know the difference either way. Which one of us actually has this right?
