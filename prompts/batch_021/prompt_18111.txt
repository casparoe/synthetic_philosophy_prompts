I'm taking a metaphysics course this term, and an argument with my roommate this week turned out to feel a lot less trivial than it seemed at first. We own a modular sectional couch made of three cushioned units, which we normally push flush together into one L-shaped couch; for a game night, though, we pulled the units apart and used them as three separate armchairs. My roommate joked that we had turned one piece of furniture into three just by sliding the units a few inches apart, and neither of us can agree on whether that's actually true. It seems like either there were only ever three cushioned units arranged one way or another, or a further single object—the couch—genuinely existed while the units were touching and stopped existing the moment we pulled them apart, and both possibilities strike me as strange. Is one of us actually onto something real, or is this just a dispute about how we choose to describe the furniture?
