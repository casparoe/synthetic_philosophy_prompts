Had a moment in my grad seminar on aggregation last week that I can't stop chewing on. We were doing the standard setup -- one person tortured for fifty years vs. some absurdly huge number of people (a googolplex, whatever) each getting a single dust speck in the eye -- and two of my students went back and forth on it for a good twenty minutes without either budging. Roughly, it went:

(1) Petra said the comparison is rigged by scale: a dust speck is over in an instant and leaves no mark on a life, so it isn't even the kind of thing that has "harm to a life" as its currency, whereas fifty years of torture is. You can't manufacture the second kind of badness by piling up enough of the first kind, because their different in kind, not just amount.

(2) Dev said that can't be right -- if a dust speck really had zero moral weight, it'd be a matter of complete indifference to flick one into a stranger's eye for no reason, which is obviously false; people are right to mind, a little. But once it's bad by a little, either enough littles sum to a lot, or you owe us a principled reason they don't, and "different currency" sounds a lot like refusing to pay up.

(3) Petra's comeback was that nonzero-per-person doesn't force additivity across persons: fifty years of torture is bad within one continuous life, while a googolplex of specks are bad (barely) across a googolplex of separate lives that are each otherwise totally unaffected a moment later. Treating those as addable quantities smuggles in exactly the aggregative assumption that's supposed to be in question.

(4) Dev replied: fine, then locate it -- stubbed toe, bee sting, day-long migraine, a genuinely rough week -- where exactly does "separate, non-fungible" harm turn into "addable" harm, and why there and not one notch over? Otherwise "different currency" is just a rule that relocates itself wherever it needs to be to keep torture from ever losing.

I couldn't tell you who actually came out ahead. Can you go move by move -- who's got the better of it at each of the four steps, not just overall -- and then say what the exchange as a whole is still missing?
