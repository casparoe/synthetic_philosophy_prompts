A neighbor agreed to water my houseplants while I was away for two weeks but stopped by only once; by the time I returned, three plants I had been growing for years had died from lack of water. When I raised this with her, she argued that she can't really be blamed for their deaths, since my mail carrier, my other neighbors, and countless strangers who walked past my building also never watered the plants, yet none of them are considered responsible either. That argument feels too convenient, but I can't quite explain why her not watering them seems different from everyone else's not watering them. Did she actually cause the plants to die, or did she merely fail to prevent something bad from happening—and is there really a difference between the two?
