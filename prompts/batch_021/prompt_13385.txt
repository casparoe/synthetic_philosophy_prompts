Write a short story built around evidential cooperation in large worlds (ECL) -- the decision-theory idea that if the universe or multiverse is big enough, theres almost certainly a huge population of agents out there who you'll never meet, never causally affect, and never get a signal from, but who think enough like you that you can still "cooperate" with them, just by choosing to act like the kind of agent you'd want them to be. No telepathy, no messages, no shared cause allowed -- whatever cooperation happens has to run entirely on that inference, Newcomb-style. Bonus points if your character has to make a real, costly choice on this basis and the story never confirms whether it actually paid off.
