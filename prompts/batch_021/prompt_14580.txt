So this happened on a long car ride with my buddy Mark and his roommate Priya, and I still don't know who actually won. Mark was arguing that when you keep asking "why do you believe that" about anything, the chain has to stop somewhere, in a belief that's justified all on its own, otherwise you'd need an infinite pile of reasons and nobody's got that. He called this "foundationalism," something he picked up from a podcast. Priya said that's basically giving yourself permission to stop questioning wherever's convenient, and a flat earther could do the exact same move and call THEIR starting point basic too. She said beliefs are more like a web holding each other up, nothing is special, a belief counts as justified if it fits the whole picture, coherentism basically.

Mark shot back that a totally made up but internally consistent conspiracy theory (or a novel) fits together fine too, so coherence alone can't be enough, you need atleast some beliefs plugged straight into the world, like "there's a red mug in front of me right now."

Priya said ok, but those directly-plugged-in beliefs aren't foolproof either, people misread stuff, hallucinate, whatever, and if they can be wrong then they must be getting propped up by the rest of your beliefs anyway, so the web sneaks back in.

Then Mark brought up a third thing he'd read about, infinitism, where the chain of reasons keeps going forever and that's supposedly fine. Priya said that's insane, nobody has an infinite number of reasons sitting in their head. Mark said you don't need them all sitting there at once, just that you could always produce one more if someone kept asking why, kind of like how you don't need to have already counted every number to know there's always a next one. Priya said fine, but then what's actually justifying the belief is whatever you COULD say, not anything you actually believe right now, and that seems like a weird thing to build justification out of.

Niether of them would budge and honestly I got a bit lost in the weeds. Can you go through it and say who's got the better of it at each step, not just who wins overall, and point out anything you think both of them are missing?
