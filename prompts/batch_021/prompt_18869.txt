So my friend and labmate Priya is getting married in a few weeks, and because we've spent five years sharing a windowless office running the same vision experiments, she's asked me to give a speech at the reception. Which would already make me nervous, except her whole dissertation is on cognitive penetration of perception — specifically whether wanting or fearing something can change what your visual system actually represents, as opposed to just changing what you attend to, remember, or judge afterward — and pretty much everyone coming (her parents, my parents, our entire cohort) already has some version of "love is blind, and Priya literally studies why" locked and loaded as a toast.

Problem is, if I actually say that her feelings for Tom change what she sees, shes going to correct me mid-reception, because the position she actually defended (after her committee spent forty minutes making her distinguish her effects from attention, memory, and plain old demand characteristics, very Firestone-and-Scholl about it) is way more careful than that. The short version: most "you see what you want to see" results — the classic kids-overestimate-coin-size studies, the wishful-seeing-things-look-closer studies, all of it — are really hard to pin on early vision itself rather than on downstream judgment or where your eyes happen to land, and genuine penetration of perception proper is the harder, much less settled claim, which is basically what her whole defense turned on.

Could you write me a wedding speech, maybe 400-500 words, that gets a real "love is blind" laugh out of the people who have zero idea what any of that means, but doesn't say anything Priya would wince at in front of her committee members (two of whom are, god help me, on the guest list)? Extra credit if you can make the careful, hedged version of her own claim — that her eyes probably haven't changed one bit, but everything downstream of them clearly has — land as more romantic than the naive version, not less. A decent line about the forty-minute committee grilling somewhere in there would also make my year.
