I've been mulling over a puzzle drawn from my life as a novelist, and I'd like an outside opinion on whether my unease about it is justified. Whenever I finish a full draft, I go through a familiar cycle: for the first month or two I feel confident about the manuscript, but if I set it aside and come back to it after six months or a year, I invariably arrive at a different judgment. Across the five novels I've written so far, that shift has gone in different directions—sometimes I return to find real problems I couldn't see before, sometimes I find that scenes I'd been anxious about actually hold up fine—but the one constant is that my opinion always moves, and by a considerable amount. So with the manuscript I've just finished, I already know, with something close to certainty, that the verdict I reach next year will differ from the one I hold today, even though I have no idea yet which direction it will go, or what exactly will change my mind.

At dinner last week, a poet in my writing group argued that this foreknowledge obligates me to soften my current confidence right now. Her reasoning was that if I already know, as a matter of fact, that my opinion is going to shift, it is simply irrational to go on holding my present opinion at full strength; intellectual honesty, she said, requires discounting it now, in anticipation of a change of mind I haven't yet undergone. This struck me as backwards, though I had trouble saying exactly why. It feels strange to be asked to defer to a verdict I don't hold, can't yet articulate, and don't know the grounds for—especially since I don't even know whether next year's shift will come from genuine new perspective (real distance from the material) or from something more like fatigue, mood, or whatever else happens to be going on in my life when I next open the file. At the same time, I can't simply dismiss the fact that I do know, reliably, that some change is coming.

Is my friend's argument sound, or does it prove too much? And, more generally, is there a principled way of deciding when the mere foreknowledge that an opinion will change should already move one's present confidence, and when it shouldn't?
