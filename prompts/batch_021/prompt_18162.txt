Two people in a philosophy of religion reading group I'm part of got into an extended dispute about the fine-tuning argument that outlasted the meeting itself, and I haven't been able to settle it in my own head since. (1) He opened by defending the standard multiverse response to the argument: if there exist vastly many universes with independently and randomly varying physical constants, it is unsurprising that at least one has life-permitting values; since we could only ever find ourselves in such a universe, our existence gives no reason to prefer a designer over this alternative. (2) She objected that this move commits an inverse gambler's fallacy: if each universe's constants are genuinely fixed by an independent chance process, then learning that our own universe happens to be life-permitting is no more evidence for the existence of many further universes than rolling a double six this time is evidence that the dice have been rolled many times before. (3) He replied that the analogy breaks down over exactly the feature that makes this an anthropic case rather than an ordinary one: a gambler watching a die land will see whatever comes up, six or not, but we could not possibly have observed a universe whose constants were not life-permitting, because there would be no one there to do the observing — and once that constraint is built into the reasoning, the inference no longer has the structure of the gambler's fallacy at all. (4) She answered that the very same constraint cuts both ways: if one universe by chance, one universe by design, and a trillion universes by chance all guarantee, with equal certainty, that any observer who exists will find life-permitting constants, then the observation doesn't favor any of the three over the others, and he can't help himself to the selection effect to rescue the multiverse's explanatory credentials while denying that it equally strips the original fine-tuning data of any power to discriminate between chance and design in the first place.

Neither of them budged, and honestly I can talk myself into any of the four positions depending on the day.
