So my cousin's friend group had a WhatsApp thread spiral into a two-week philosophy fight, and now everyone assumes that because I cover science and ideas for a living, I'm qualified to referee it. Quick setup: Dana and Marcus were arguing about a couple they both know, Lucy and Ben. Lucy needed a medication for a flare-up that causes mild-to-moderate birth defects if she's on it when she conceives, but clears her system completely in about five months. They concieved right away anyway, and now have a daughter who's a thriving eight-year-old with some manageable physical impairments.

Marcus said what they did was flat-out wrong: they harmed their daughter, who's worse off than she could've been. Dana said that's confused: worse off compared to what? If they'd waited, this exact kid wouldn't exist at all, a different kid would, so their daughter isn't worse off than any alternative version of herself, because there's no such version to compare her to. Marcus switched tack: fine, maybe she wasn't personally harmed, but Lucy and Ben still did wrong by choosing a worse-off child when a different, better-off one was just as available, so the wrong doesn't need a specific victim. Dana said that quietly swaps "they harmed their daughter" for pure population-level bean-counting, which has ugly baggage: it implies kids with disabilities generally shouldn't be conceived whenever an abler alternative is possible, and she doubted Marcus actually believed that. Marcus tried a third angle: maybe the daughter was harmed non-comparatively, just in a somewhat bad state, full stop, no comparison needed. Dana said that can't be right either: pretty much every kid is in "some bad state" by some standard, so unless Marcus can say exactly where "bad" starts, his view either proves way too much or the line's just made up to save the intuition.

Nobody at brunch the next week would let it drop. Could you go through this move by move — whos got the better of it at each step, not just who wins overall — and say what you think both of them are still missing?
