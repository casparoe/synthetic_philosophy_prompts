My friend who's doing a philosophy MA heard me spiraling about this and told me there's an actual, decades-long fight in metaphysics about exactly this "other version of me" intuition: David Lewis's counterpart theory says the person living out the choice you didn't make is a genuinely seperate individual who just happens to resemble you closely, while the "transworld identity" side (this is where Kripke comes in) says no, it's literally, numerically you, no resemblance relation required, we just stipulate it. He also told me about this objection involving a guy named Humphrey, who lost a presidential election and supposedly wouldn't care less that some "counterpart" of his won in another possible world, because that's not really him winning - which is basically my exact problem with Berlin-me, except I can't tell if I agree with Humphrey or not.
