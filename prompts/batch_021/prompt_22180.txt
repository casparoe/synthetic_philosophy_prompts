My dad passed away six weeks ago and my sister and I have been clearing out his apartment, and in the back of his closet we found this shoebox of notebooks going back to like the 80s — pages and pages of bet sizing math for the horses, percentages next to odds next to his running bankroll total. Turns out he'd basically been doing Kelly betting his whole life without ever explaining it to either of us, and there's a totally beat-up copy of Fortune's Formula on his bookshelf held together with tape. I always figured he just wasn't a "big bettor" by temperament, kind of a chicken almost, but going through the notebooks I think I finally get what he was actually doing: he wasn't trying to maximize what any single bet was worth on average, he was trying to maximize how fast his bankroll grew over a lifetime of bets, which is apparently a totally seperate math problem, and it meant he'd walk away from bets that "should" have been great in expectation because the downside, even if unlikely, was ruin.

I'm not a gambler at all and could not tell you anything about horses, but I can't stop thinking about what that must have actually felt like night after night — having a real edge, seeing it right there on paper, and still deliberately betting less than an outside observer would think you "should," because you're the one guy actually living through the sequence instead of getting to average over a million parallel versions of yourself. There's a small reading at the wake in a couple weeks and I'd like to bring something, not a eulogy exactly, more like a short story loosely inspired by him that my very non-mathematical extended family could actually follow and feel something from. Could you write me one, maybe 1000-1500 words, centered on one specific night where an old horse-track gambler is tempted to blow past his own rule for once — where the tension actually comes from the gap between the bet that looks best "on average" and the bet that's best for the one life he's actually got, rather than just generic gambling-is-dangerous drama?
