Roughly how it went (tidied up, but the moves are theirs):

1. Mara: Obviously you pay once you're in town. If you're the kind of person who wouldn't, the driver sees that coming and leaves you there, so paying is the rational move.
2. Dev: That mixes up two questions. Sure, before you know if he'll pick you up, you want to BE someone who'd pay. But once you're in town, ride already banked, the $1000 buys you nothing -- he's not going to un-drive you. A rational agent doesn't pay for a service it's already got for free and can't lose.

4. Dev: Getting picked up depends on your disposition, not the later act of paying -- those come apart. The disposition to pay can be the one you should want, without paying itself being the rational act once you're already safe. Wanting to be a certain kind of chooser, and it being rational to choose that way once you're there, are different things.
5. Mara: Then your rational agent needs someone else to install the better disposition from outside, since by its own logic it'd never choose that on its own. That's a mark against the theory, not a save.
