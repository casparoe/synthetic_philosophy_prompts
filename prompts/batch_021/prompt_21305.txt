Been down a replication crisis rabbit hole again (rereading Scott Alexander's old Control Group Is Out Of Control post, the whole Bem ESP saga, etc) and I think I finally get why rationalist blogs are so down on p-values. Feels like the actual lesson is that frequentist null-hypothesis testing is just structurally broken -- p<.05 is basically how a decade of junk like power posing and Bem's psi experiments got published as real findings -- and Wagenmakers' bayesian reanalysis showing Bem's evidence for psi basically evaporates once you do it properly is the smoking gun. So if psych had just been doing bayesian stats with honest priors this whole time we probably wouldnt have had a replication crisis at all, right? Given that, should I just start discounting any pre-2015ish finding that leans on a p-value and trust papers with bayes factors way more by default?
