Schopenhauer's theory of music is oddly specific: he thinks music, unlike the other arts, is a direct copy of the Will itself rather than of the Platonic Ideas, and part of how he cashes this out is through tonal harmony -- like how a melody's dissonances and their delayed resolution back to the keynote are supposed to mirror desire and its (temporary) satisfaction, with suspensions standing in for prolonged longing before the payoff. That whole account leans pretty hard on there being a home key to resolve to.
