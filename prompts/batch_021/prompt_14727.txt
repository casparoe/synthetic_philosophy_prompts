I keep having this nagging feeling that his diagnosis of 1840s Copenhagen's gossipy anonymous newspaper culture maps onto Twitter/X and algorithmic social media almost too neatly, but every time I look this up online its just people making the lazy "wow Kierkegaard basically predicted the internet" point without actually engaging with his concepts, which is exactly what I'm trying to avoid in my own writing.

So here's what I'd actually like from you: reconstruct, as carefully as you can using his real vocabulary (leveling/Nivellering, "the public" as a phantom abstraction that has opinions but no responsibility, chatter vs. genuine communication, the line about the crowd being untruth, and his own experience of getting endlessly caricatured by the Corsaren), what Kierkegaard would say if you sat him down in front of a phone and showed him Twitter or TikTok.
