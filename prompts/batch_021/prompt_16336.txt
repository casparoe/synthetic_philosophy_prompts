Last night, a friend and I had a long argument about suffering-focused ethics, and one point from it has stayed with me. I argued that David Benatar's asymmetry argument for antinatalism is really just a special case of negative utilitarianism, since both give lexical priority to preventing suffering over promoting happiness. My friend disagreed but couldn't quite say why. Since negative utilitarianism is widely regarded as untenable, chiefly because it implies that painlessly annihilating all sentient life would count as a moral improvement, shouldn't Benatar's case for antinatalism be considered discredited for the same reason?
