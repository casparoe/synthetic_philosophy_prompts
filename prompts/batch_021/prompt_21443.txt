Every writeup I find dives straight into the model theory and my eyes glaze over. Can you give me the plain, gut-level version of why "merely knowing the structure" is supposedly so cheap, and why its such a big problem -- and whether the more hardcore "ontic" structural realists, who say structure is ALL there is, actually escape the trick or just inherit it?
