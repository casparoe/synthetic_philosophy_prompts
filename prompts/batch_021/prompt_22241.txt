Give me 8-9 questions that don't just let her restate that thesis. Want one on the murderer-at-the-door case, where she'll almost certainly retreat to "silence and misdirection are fine, only false assertion is forbidden" -- push on whether that keeps the will-to-deceive out of it or just relocates it. Want one on the eightfold ranking from De Mendacio itself, where a jocose lie isn't a sin at all but a lie that harms nobody and only saves an innocent life still is -- ask what that ordering actually tracks if not consequences. For each, give a follow-up keyed to whatever dodge she reaches for, and end with a question sizing her up against the meta-honesty crowd on LW, who hit the same wall and tried to route around it a totally different way.
