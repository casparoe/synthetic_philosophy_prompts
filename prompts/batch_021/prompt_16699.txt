So a few weeks ago I tried one of those float tanks, the pods with body-temp salt water so you just float in total darkness and silence. After a while I couldn't even tell where my skin ended and the water began, it was like my arms and legs weren't there at all, no sight, no sound, no touch, nothing. But this whole time there was still this bare sense of "yep, I'm here, I exist," completely apart from knowing anything about my body or the room around me. Ever since I can't shake the feeling that this proves the "me" part of me is a seperate thing from my body, like my body could vanish from my awareness completely and I'd still be just as much me. My partner says I'm reading way too much into a relaxation gimmick, that of course you can still think in there since your brain doesn't switch off just cause your skin goes numb for an hour. Is there actually something real in what I experienced, or is she right that it doesn't show anything about the mind and body actually being seperate?
