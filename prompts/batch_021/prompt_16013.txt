The part that seems important is that these aren't just beliefs we're super confident about because we've got a mountain of evidence for them -- they're doing a different job entirely, more like the frame the evidence-weighing happens inside of, rather than a very well-supported resident of that frame.

Also, if you can work it in: apparently Wittgenstein has this riverbed metaphor where hinges aren't fixed forever -- the bed can shift over time so stuff that was solid ground becomes doubtable and vice versa -- so I'd be curious whether your example could ever stop being a hinge in that way.
