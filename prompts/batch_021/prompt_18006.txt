Been down a rabbit hole reading about AI ethics stuff and keep running into this 'responsibility gap' argument — the idea that once a self-driving car or autonomous weapon is trained via machine learning, nobody can really be held responsible when it causes harm, since no one explicitly coded that exact behavior. Honestly seems like a non-issue to me: we already hold dog owners liable when their dog bites someone, even though nobody 'programmed' the bite, so why cant manufacturers just be made strictly liable the same way and call it a day? Feels like philosophers are overthinking something engineers would just patch with better contracts and insurance, but maybe im missing something?
