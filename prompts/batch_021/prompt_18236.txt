A couple weeks ago I snapped at a stranger in a coffee shop, someone filmed just the rude part, and a meme account with half a million followers posted it. Ever since, the replies have been nonstop strangers calling me a horrible person, even though the couple of people who actually messaged me directly were pretty reasonable, even a little apologetic once they heard my side. But as one big anonymous crowd, everyone comes across like theyve already reached a unanimous verdict, and when I tried explaining myself in the replies it just made things explode more. Theres no single person I can actually apologize to and have it be over, which feels totally different from a friend being mad at me, where you can just talk it out and move past it. Is getting judged by a crowd of anonymous strangers actually a different kind of thing from one real person disliking you, or am I just dressing up "the internet is mean" as something deeper than it is?
