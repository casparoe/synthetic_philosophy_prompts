I'm in a political philosophy reading group and we just did back-to-back sessions on Rousseau's Social Contract and Simone Weil's short essay 'On the Abolition of All Political Parties' (the one where she argues parties should just be banned outright, written right before she died in 1943). What struck me is that Weil isn't just riffing generally on the idea of the 'general will' -- she actually walks through Rousseau's own two starting premises (reason converges on whats just, passions diverge) and says outright that his whole argument about individual judgments cancelling out into truth only holds if there's no 'collective passion' distorting things at the moment people decide, and that 'Rousseau himself knew this well.' That got me thinking about the part in Book II, chapter 3 of the Social Contract, where Rousseau worries about factions and 'partial associations' turning the general will into just a handful of big particular wills instead of loads of tiny independent ones, and says the fix is either no partial societies at all or as many small, roughly equal ones as possible. Weil never actually quotes that passage in her essay as far as I can tell, but her whole case that a party is 'a machine to generate collective passions' sounds like exactly the mechanism Rousseau is describing there, just applied specifically to modern parties.

I've got to write a short response paper on this for next week and don't want to just hand-wave the connection, so here's what I actually want to know: how much of Weil's argument is really just her spelling out what's already implicit in that chapter of Rousseau, and in what precise way does she go further than he does -- given that his own fix stops at multiplying the small factions or having none at all, while hers is total abolition, with candidates judged issue by issue and no labels at all? Is her extra step a clean extension of his own logic, or does it quietly smuggle in some further premise of hers (about attention, or truth, or the state of the soul) that isn't really doing any work in Rousseau at all?
