Had a whole argument with my friend last night that's still bugging me. She's way into metaethics and hit me with what she called the "Darwinian Dilemma" (I think it's from a philosopher named Sharon Street?) — the idea that our moral intuitions were shaped by natural selection for fitness, not for tracking any independent moral truth, so either it's a wild coincidence that our morals line up with the truth, or you have to say evolution was somehow tracking moral facts, which is nuts. She said that basically settles it: moral realism's dead, theres no such thing as objective right or wrong. Ok fine, but if literally nothing is actually right or wrong, how am i supposed to make big decisions (having kids, who to vote for, whatever) without it just being arbitrary — is there an actual framework for that, or is it just personal taste at that point?
