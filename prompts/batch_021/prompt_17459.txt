My debate partner and I are on our high school's team, and we prep both sides of every topic from identical evidence, discussing each card until we agree on what it shows. Before every tournament we privately estimate our odds of winning the round, then compare notes -- and even after hearing exactly what number the other landed on, our estimates are often far apart, with neither of us able to find a piece of evidence the other missed or a flaw in the other's reasoning. Our coach once told us that two rational people who share the same information can't keep disagreeing once they know each other's estimate; they're supposed to converge. That clearly isn't what happens to us, so is one of us making some mistake neither of us can spot, or is our coach's claim simply wrong?
