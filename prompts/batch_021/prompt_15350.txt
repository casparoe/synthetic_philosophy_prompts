Ok this has been bugging me since it happened. My writers' group did a benefit reading last month for a homeless shelter -- had a couple actor friends perform some genuinely devastating monologues about it, and by the end half the room was in tears. But when we tallied the money afterward it was actually less than the plain bake sale we ran the year before with zero performance, and most people who swore that night they'd sign up to volunteer never did. Part of me suspects the reading let everyone off the hook somehow -- like they got the warm feeling of being a compassionate person just from sitting there crying, so there was nothing left over to actually go do anything. Now im side-eyeing readings for my own novels too, tbh. Is that a real effect or am i just being a cynic about my own audience?
