So 'more info is always at least as good' can't be a general truth once other people are in the loop. She also doubted that folding the pain into the utility function is the free move Sam thinks it is, since Good's proof needs the agent to be able to freely disregard evidence once she already has it, and actual people often can't just unknow a diagnosis or a spoiler.
