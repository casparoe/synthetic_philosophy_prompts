Write a joke that turns on the "relations without relata" objection to ontic structural realism in philosophy of science: the complaint that if, as ontic structural realists hold, the relational structure described by our best physical theories is all there ultimately is, then the "objects" supposedly standing in those relations become dispensable, leaving relations with nothing left to relate. (This is a stronger claim than the more modest epistemic structural realism, on which we simply cannot know anything about objects beyond the structure they enter into.) The joke should be funny only to someone who sees why this is a genuine metaphysical puzzle rather than just strange-sounding jargon; a generic joke about philosophers' love of abstraction will not do. Keep it brief, with an actual punchline.
