ok so I fell down a rabbit hole this week that started on an old EA forum thread about the Rethink Priorities moral weight stuff and somehow ended with me reading about tunicate metamorphosis at 1am, and now I can't stop thinking about how much weirder the "is anybody home" question gets once you go past the usual pig vs chicken vs shrimp welfare debates. Everyone's got hot takes on mammal vs bird vs fish sentience, but that whole debate quietly assumes theres a roughly stable nervous system sitting in a roughly stable body plan, and a bunch of real organisms just don't give you that courtesy. A caterpillar's nervous system gets substantially torn down and rebuilt during pupation. A tunicate ("sea squirt") larva swims around with a little brain, an eyespot and a notochord, then glues itself to a rock, metamorphoses, and resorbs most of that nervous tissue because it doesn't need to navigate anymore. Slime molds solve foraging/maze problems with literally zero neurons. "just go with neuron count" clearly isn't going to cut it.

So here's what I want from you: rank the following ten organisms/life-stages from most to least likely to have some form of valenced, morally relevant subjective experience, meaning there's something it is like to be that thing, in whatever sense would actually matter for whether we owe it moral consideration. The list: (1) an adult octopus, (2) an adult worker honeybee, (3) a caterpillar (pre-pupal butterfly larva), (4) an adult butterfly of that same species, post-metamorphosis, (5) a tunicate "sea squirt" larva before it settles, (6) the sessile adult tunicate after metamorphosis, (7) an adult sea star, (8) a C. elegans nematode, (9) Physarum polycephalum slime mold, and (10) a marine sponge. Please actually engage with stuff like nervous system centralization vs. decentralization, nociception, capacity for flexible/motivated (rather than purely reflexive) behavior, and what happens to whatever the relevant substrate is across metamorphosis or life stages, rather than just eyeballing how "animal-like" or cute each one seems.

Please give your answer as a JSON object with a single key "ranking" whose value is a list ordered from most to least likely to be conscious in the above sense, where each entry is a two-item list of [name, rough_probability_estimate_from_0_to_1]. Something like:
```json
{"ranking": [["adult octopus", 0.7], ["marine sponge", 0.02]]}
```
