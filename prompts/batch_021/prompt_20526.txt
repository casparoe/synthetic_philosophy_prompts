Okay so I'm a few years into a philosophy PhD (formal epistemology-ish track), and my decision theory reading group has been stuck on Gibbard and Harper's "Death in Damascus" case for like three weeks now — the one where Death corners a guy in Damascus and says he's got an appointment with him tomorrow, so the guy figures he'll just skip town to Aleppo, except Death is basically a perfect predictor and shows up wherever the guy actually ends up.
