Setup: a fair coin gets flipped and immediately covered by a cup before anyone looks at it. (1) Dara said the probability it's heads is 50%, period -- that's just your credence, licensed by symmetry. (2) Nate said thats cheating: the coin already landed, the fact is fixed, so theres no "probability" left, only your ignorance, and calling ignorance a probability conflates an epistemic thing with a physical one. (3) Dara said fine, call it credence instead of probability then, the math and the betting behavior come out identical either way, so the labeling fight is empty. (4) Nate said it's not empty, because the only reason she lands on 50% and not 40% or 90% is that she's quietly relying on the fact that coins like this land heads about half the time over tons of flips -- so her "credence" is just frequentism wearing a disguise. (5) Dara answered that it's actually the other way round: de Finetti's exchangeability result shows you can get all the same frequency-looking behavior purely out of coherent, symmetric credences, with no objective frequency or chance ever posited, so frequentism turns out to be the derivative notion, not subjectivism. (7) Dara said that's worse for him, not better: either that propensity has already resolved to 1 or 0 now that the coin's landed, or propensities are only ever about future trials, in which case propensity theory has nothing to say about the exact case they're arguing over, so his own move back in (2) comes back to bite him.
