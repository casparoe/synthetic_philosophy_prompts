I've lurked on LW and ACX for like a decade now, and theres this argument that flares up every time someone brings up 'inference to the best explanation' in a comment section but never actually gets resolved: is IBE doing anything over and above plain Bayesian conditionalization, or is "the hypothesis that best explains the evidence" just a folksy way of saying "the hypothesis with the highest posterior"? Half the commentariat treats it as obviously the same move, the other half insists explanatory virtues like simplicity and unification are pulling weight no likelihood ratio can capture, and it always just ends in people restating their priors.

Can you write me a dialogue between a hardline Bayesian who thinks IBE reduces to conditionalization and a serious defender of IBE as its own distinct rule (think Lipton, not a strawman), where they get forced into an actual double crux: they have to construct, or openly admit they can't construct, one concrete case where the two procedures would license different credences, instead of just trading slogans. Make the Bayesian throw van Fraassen's 'bad lot' objection at them and have the other actually engage with it instead of dodging. I want real back-and-forth where each move changes what the other says next, not two monologues stapled together.
