I'm a novelist, and last year I took a paid gig mentoring a debut writer, mostly becuase it looked good on my bio and the stipend covered a couple months of rent — not because I actually cared about mentoring anyone. Somewhere along the way that changed though, and by the end I was spending way more hours on her manuscript than the program paid for, texting her line edits at midnight, the whole thing. She just found out that I originally signed up mainly for the money and the resume line, and now she says it retroactively makes all the help feel hollow, like it was never really about her book. Part of me wants to defend myself, but part of me also wonders if she's onto something. Does it actually matter that I started for a bad reason, if I didn't stay for one?
