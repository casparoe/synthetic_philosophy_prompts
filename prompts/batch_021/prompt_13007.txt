My brother and I are dividing our late father's estate, and mediation starts next week. A few days ago he emailed the whole family to announce that he has already told his attorney, in writing, to accept nothing less than the lake house, and that the position is final and non-negotiable. Over dinner last night, my closest friend -- who negotiates commercial deals for a living -- spent an hour convincing me that I should answer in kind: lock my own attorney into an equally firm, equally public demand, on the theory that whoever commits first and hardest usually wins these standoffs. I could not out-argue her, but it seems to me that if we both do this, neither of us will be able to back down without losing face, and the whole estate could end up consumed by a legal fight that leaves us both worse off than almost any settlement we might otherwise have reached. Was she right that I should match him, or is there a genuine danger in both sides playing that card -- and if so, what should I do instead?
