A few months ago I left the religious community I was raised in, and much of my time since has gone into working out, from first principles, why I should believe anything at all. Growing up, my beliefs rested on a handful of certainties — scripture and the word of trusted elders chief among them — that were simply exempt from needing further justification; losing confidence in that exemption is what set off the crisis. Reading epistemology since then, I have learned that this is essentially the regress problem, and that foundationalism, coherentism, and infinitism are the three standard replies to it, each carrying a familiar cost: stopping the questioning by decree, floating free of the world, or requiring more reasons than a finite mind could possess. I am not looking for reassurance, only clarity. Please describe the most important implications of this three-way debate for how I should now justify my everyday, ethical, and factual beliefs, without simply re-installing some new, unexamined foundation in place of the old one.
