Does the principle really force you to shift your credence now to match that? Feels backwards, but I can't quite pin down where exactly its supposed to break, or whether theres some standard restriction on Reflection that's meant to handle cases like this. Can you dig into the actual mechanics of why this is (or isn't) a genuine problem for the principle, and how people who like Reflection try to patch it up?
