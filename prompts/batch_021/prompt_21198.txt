Quick one — I've been down an Otto Neurath rabbit hole for a piece on the history of infographics, and I can't stop thinking about how he'd react to emoji. Neurath was surprisingly strict about his Isotype pictograms: to show "more" of something you repeat the same little icon rather than blow up the size of one icon, and picture language was always meant as a supplement to words, never able to carry abstract, logical, or subjective content on it's own. Given that, what do you think Neurath would make of emoji becoming this de facto global picture language, way past anything he saw in his lifetime — a natural extension of his project, or does it break basically every rule he actually cared about?
