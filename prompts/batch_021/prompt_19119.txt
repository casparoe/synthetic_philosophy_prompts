My dad was in a bad motorcycle wreck on Friday and yesterday the doctors told us he's brain dead. Here's the thing thats messing with all of us though -- this morning while a nurse was repositioning him, his arms actually lifted up off the bed and crossed over his chest for a few seconds, like he was reaching for something. My mom lost it and now she's convinced they got the diagnosis wrong. I found some stuff online saying this actually happens sometimes even in confirmed brain death cases, something about spinal reflexes, but I dont get how thats different from "there's still brain activity going on in there." If his body can pull off a coordinated-looking movement like that, doesnt that mean some part of his brain is still working, and shouldnt they be running more tests before we sign off on organ donation tomorrow?
