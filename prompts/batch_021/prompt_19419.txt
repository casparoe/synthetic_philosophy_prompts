Our school's dress code says shorts can't be "too short," without giving an exact length. Last week I was sent to the office for a pair of shorts that looked, as far as I could tell, exactly as long as the shorts several other students were wearing without any issue that same day. When I raised this, the assistant principal said the rule doesn't need a stated number of inches to be meaningful: there's still a definite right answer as to which side of the line a given pair of shorts falls on, she said, even though no one, including her, could ever say exactly where that line is. Her answer struck me as reasonable, but also a little slippery, and I haven't been able to work out whether it actually holds up. Is she right that there's a precise fact about "too short" that none of us can pin down, or is the more honest description that no such fact exists and the rule simply runs out before it settles the case?
