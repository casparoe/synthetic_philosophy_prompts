Question (20 points): State the Sleeping Beauty problem. Defend either the halfer or the thirder position on Beauty's credence in Heads upon waking, and explain how your chosen position handles the variant in which Beauty is additionally told, upon waking, that it is Monday. Relate your discussion to the broader notion of self-locating (de se) belief.

Student answer:

"Beauty is put to sleep on Sunday, and a fair coin is tossed. On Heads she is woken only on Monday; on Tails she is woken on Monday and again on Tuesday, with a memory-erasing drug given after the Monday waking so that the two awakenings are subjectively indistinguishable. Each time she wakes, she is asked her credence that the coin landed Heads.

I will defend the thirder position, first argued for by David Lewis: upon waking, Beauty should set her credence in Heads to 1/3. There are three epistemically symmetric awakening-possibilities — Heads/Monday, Tails/Monday, Tails/Tuesday — and since Tails produces two of these against Heads' one, mere self-locating uncertainty about which awakening this is should shift her credence away from the 1/2 she would have endorsed before the coin was tossed. This is a genuine case of self-locating belief in Lewis's sense from 'Attitudes De Dicto and De Se': Beauty is not uncertain about an ordinary proposition but about which of several qualitatively identical centered worlds she currently occupies, which is why ordinary conditionalization needs supplementing here. A further consideration favors thirding: across many repetitions of the experiment, two-thirds of all awakenings occur on Tails, and calibrating credence to that long-run frequency, rather than to the coin's fairness alone, again yields 1/3.

Adam Elga's halfer response denies that waking up is itself informative, since Beauty knew all along she would wake at least once regardless of the outcome, so her credence should simply stay at 1/2. The disagreement sharpens once Beauty is told it is Monday. Since this rules out the Tuesday awakening, the thirder view raises her credence to 2/3, while the halfer, having started from 1/2 and treating the coin as untouched by which day it is, stays at 1/2. This divergence is often taken to show that the thirder position handles new evidence more smoothly than the halfer's."
