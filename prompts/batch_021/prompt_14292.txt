They stood there together, read it out loud a couple times, argued about it for a bit, and eventually both agreed: "yeah, pretty sure this one's legal today." Jonah parked there and ran inside for like twenty minutes - if he's wrong, worst case he gets a small ticket, no big deal. Priya, meanwhile, was about to leave her car in that exact same spot for an entire week while she flew home for her sister's wedding, so getting towed would mean missing her flight and a genuinely ruined week. Jonah got kind of annoyed: "we literally read the same sign and agreed on it two minutes ago, how can you suddenly not know something I still know?" I've been going back and forth on this ever since. So who's actually right, if anyone? Is Jonah correct that they're flat-out contradicting each other, or is there some sense in which both of their claims about what they know can be true at once? And even if you can rescue both of them, does that automatically vindicate our other roommate's point that Priya was right to double-check, or is "what's reasonable to do" actually a totally seperate question from "what she knows"?
