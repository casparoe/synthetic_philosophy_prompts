We've been through genuinely stressful stuff together — a brutal thesis deadline, her car breaking down on a highway shoulder at 1am, a landlord who tried to illegally evict her — and she just doesnt rattle, ever. So a few weeks ago we're at her parents place for dinner, her dad makes one of his classic passive aggressive digs about her "wasting her degree," and Dana just explodes. Raised voice, said some things she probably regrets, the works. Totally unlike her.
