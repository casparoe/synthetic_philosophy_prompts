I've spent months revising the climax of my current novel, so I know exactly what happens on every page and have known it since the very first draft. Yet each time I reread the scene in which my protagonist is finally cornered, I feel a genuine jolt of dread, as though her escape were still in doubt. I decided her fate myself, there was never a moment of real uncertainty, and I have never for an instant believed this woman exists outside my manuscript. A friend I described this to insists the feeling must be some kind of trick I'm playing on myself, since nothing in the situation could actually justify fear. That explanation doesn't sit right with me, though I can't say exactly why. Is my friend right that the reaction doesn't really hold up, or is something else going on?
