Burge's move is that his belief still counts as an arthritis-belief, wrong though it is, because that's how the word gets used in his speech community -- and then he has you imagine a molecule-for-molecule twin of the guy, same pain, same internal life, literally everything, except this twin is embedded in a community where the word that sounds like "arthritis" gets used a little more loosely, to cover rheumatoid pain outside the joints too. My friend Priya kept insisting this is basically a cheat -- that all it really shows is that the guy has a fuzzy dictionary entry, not that his actual thought content depends on his community, and that Burge is just relabeling a difference in word-meaning as if it were a difference in mental content.
