One committee member, a physicist by training, wants to reject the renewal outright, and her objection isn't "low priority," it's straightforwardly Popperian: she says the whole program is set up so nothing could ever count against it, a model that shows zero signs of deception just means "not capable enough yet," and a model that does show something suspicious gets waved away as "an artifact of the eval setup, not real deception."
