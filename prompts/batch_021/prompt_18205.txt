This happened to me last month and its still bugging me. I was driving back from my sister's place on a back road with zero cell service when my car slid into a ditch after dark. A guy in a pickup eventually stopped and offered to pull me out with a tow strap -- but only after I promised to send him gas money once I was back in range, since (his words) he's usually a pretty good judge of who'll actually pay up and who won't. I promised, and once I had signal again I did send him $40 over Venmo. My partner says I was a chump: the guy had no name, no plate number, nothing, so the moment I was safely back on the road there was no way for him to ever collect, meaning a truly rational person would've just kept the money. I can't poke a hole in that logic, but it also seems to imply that "rational" people should end up stuck in ditches while chumps like me get rescued, which feels backwards. So was sending the money the rational move, the irrational-but-somehow-still-correct move, or is that distinction not even coherent? (I teach philosophy for a living and am mildly embarrassed I don't already have a tidy answer for my own partner.)
