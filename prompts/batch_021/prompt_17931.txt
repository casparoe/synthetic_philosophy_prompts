Been prepping my philosophy of psychiatry seminar and rereading Hacking on the looping effect -- his multiple personality disorder and autism examples. Honestly, the more I sit with it, the more it just seems like Becker and Scheff's labeling theory from the 60s wearing a new coat of paint: you label someone mentally ill, they internalize it and start performing the diagnosis, which reinforces the category, and Hacking just relabels that whole loop an interactive kind instead of a self-fulfilling label. So why do philosophers of science treat looping effects as such a landmark contribution to phil of psychiatry, rather then just Hacking putting new vocabulary on a mechanism sociologists had already worked out decades earlier?
