I'm a novelist currently elbow-deep in research for a book set in a small nineteenth-century harbor town, and a minor disagreement between two people helping me has left me stuck on a question that feels more philosophical than practical, though I can't quite pin down what's actually at stake. For a scene where two of my characters' paths need to cross, I needed to settle whether a particular brig had really put in at the harbor during a specific week. My research assistant spent two days working through customs manifests and harbormaster's logs and arrived, by a careful and fully documented chain of reasoning, at the answer "yes, it did." My sister, who has never so much as opened one of these records and has no particular reason to know anything about nineteenth-century shipping, heard me mention the week at dinner and said, with complete confidence, "that one came in, I'd bet on it" — and when I asked how she knew, she admitted she simply felt certain and could offer no reason at all. As it turned out, they were both right: same brig, same harbor, same week.

Since the two of them ended up in exact agreement, and it was the correctness of the answer, not the pedigree of the method, that I actually needed for the book, I've been unable to let go of an uncomfortable question. Was my assistant's answer actually better in any way that matters, given that it turned out to be no more correct than my sister's hunch? My writing group spent a whole meeting arguing about this and never reached anything like consensus. I still have two more disputed details to settle before the manuscript is due, and part of me is tempted to save the research budget and just ask my sister again, since, on the evidence of this one case, she seems just as reliable as a paid professional. Is there a real, defensible reason to keep preferring the researched answer to the confident hunch, given that the two methods agreed and were equally right this time — or is my instinct that the assistant's work was worth more just a kind of intellectual snobbery?
