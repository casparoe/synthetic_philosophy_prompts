Anyway, a colleague from the history department mentioned over lunch that Frege kept a private diary near the end of his life, 1924 I think, that only got discovered and translated decades later (I want to say Dummett is the one who broke the news on this to everyone). That feels too fast to me, like it's dodging the actual question rather than answering it, but I can't quite say why. Is there something philosophically real underneath my discomfort here, or am I just running together two things that have nothing to do with each other and should get over it?
