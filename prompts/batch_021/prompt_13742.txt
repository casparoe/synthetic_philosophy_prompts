In game theory, a strategy can be eliminated during iterated elimination of dominated strategies because it is strictly dominated by a mixed strategy, even though no single pure strategy dominates it on its own. Construct an explicit two-player game -- with full payoff matrices for both players -- in which a pure strategy of Player 1 is not strictly dominated by any pure or mixed strategy in the original game, but becomes strictly dominated by a mixed strategy only after one or more of Player 2's strategies have themselves been eliminated in an earlier round. Then explain why it is specifically the elimination of Player 2's strategies, rather than of any strategy belonging to Player 1, that creates this new domination.
