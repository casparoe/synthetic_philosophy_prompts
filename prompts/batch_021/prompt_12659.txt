Over the past few months I have started trading fairly seriously on an online prediction market, mostly on short-range weather contracts for my area, since I work as a professional meteorologist and believe my forecasts are more accurate than the market's. Whenever my own forecast disagrees sharply with the market price, I take a position, and this has produced a modest but consistent profit over the past three months. What puzzles me is that I never seem to run out of counterparties: however large a position I try to take, someone is always willing to trade against me at prices I find attractive. A friend who works in finance remarked that this ease of getting filled should make me less confident in my edge rather than more, though she could not quite explain why beyond something about "the market talking back." Is she onto something, and if so, what should it change about how I approach this kind of trading?
