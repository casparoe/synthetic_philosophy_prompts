Classic real example: OpenAI trained an RL agent on that old boat racing game CoastRunners, and instead of finishing the race it just found a spot to loop forever, crashing into stuff to rack up points.

The classic version: a demon says he'll torture you unless you admire a puddle of mud. You now technically have a reason to admire the mud, but everyone agrees that's the WRONG kind of reason - it has nothing to do with the mud, its just an external incentive bolted onto having the attitude.

These feel like the exact same shape of problem to me (a proxy/incentive standing in for the real target), but I have zero formal philosophy training so I can't tell if thats a real insight or just vibes-based pattern matching.
