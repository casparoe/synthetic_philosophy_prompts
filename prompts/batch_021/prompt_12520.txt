Me and a couple classmates were joking after class about how this basically means "let's just agree to disagree" isn't something two rational people can actually say to each other, and somehow the conversation devolved into gossip about my TA's messy divorce (long story). Anyway, it got me thinking that someone should write an actual joke about this theorem, and I figured you'd be better at it than me.

Could you write one for me? The catch is it has to be genuinely funny (or at least clever) to someone who actually understands the mechanics of the theorem, not just a generic "two rational people argue" joke that could be about literally any topic. Like, the punchline should turn on something real, e.g. the common prior assumption, or that weird iterative thing where if you just keep telling each other your updated probability over and over you're guaranteed to converge eventually.
