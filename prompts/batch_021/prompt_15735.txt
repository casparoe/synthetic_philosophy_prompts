For fifteen years I've kept a personal, cross-linked set of notes for my work as an independent consultant, built up entirely in my own way. I don't just look things up in it — I think through new problems by rereading old entries and rearranging the connections between them, rather than reasoning things out from scratch. A firm I now work closely with wants me to migrate everything into their shared, standardized database instead. Colleagues there think I'm just being precious about an old filing system, but it feels closer to being asked to give up part of how I think. Am I overreacting, or is there something real behind that, and how should I make the case for keeping my own system?
