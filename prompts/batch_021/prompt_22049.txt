- Brouwer's own intuitionism, including choice sequences and the creating-subject arguments
- Heyting's formalization of intuitionistic logic together with the Brouwer–Heyting–Kolmogorov proof interpretation
- Bishop's program of constructive analysis

- Martin-Löf's intuitionistic type theory
- Dummett's meaning-theoretic (anti-realist) justification of intuitionistic logic
