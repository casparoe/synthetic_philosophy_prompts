I'm the president of my high school's philosophy club, and each week one of us presents a "tier list" ranking rival solutions to a classic problem before the group debates which one actually wins. I've been assigned the fink/mask problem for the conditional analysis of dispositions, and I'd like a second opinion on my ranking before Thursday's meeting. I pulled the background mostly from the SEP entry on dispositions and a couple of papers our faculty advisor lent me.

Some quick background, as I understand it: the simple conditional analysis says a thing is disposed to give response r to stimulus s iff it would give r if it underwent s (a glass is fragile iff it would break if struck). C. B. Martin's "electro-fink" showed this is neither necessary nor sufficient, since a live wire's disposition to conduct can be switched off at the very instant someone touches it. David Lewis patched this with his "Reformed Conditional Analysis," requiring the wire's intrinsic causal basis to persist through the process and actually produce the manifestation. Alexander Bird then countered with an antidote (or "mask") case — a poison whose lethal disposition survives fully intact even though an antidote stops it from ever killing anyone — and used it to argue that dispositions should instead be treated as primitive, unanalyzable powers.

Here are the six responses I'm ranking:
1. Lewis's Reformed Conditional Analysis
2. Bird's dispositional essentialism
3. Fara's treatment of disposition-ascriptions as habituals, true only "for the most part"
4. Manley and Wasserman's comparative account (manifestation in most, not all, of the closest stimulus-worlds)
5. Choi's proportionality constraint for separating legitimate background conditions from illegitimate finks and masks
6. Vetter's potentiality framework, which drops conditionals altogether for a primitive, graded notion of potentiality

Could you rank these six from most to least philosophically successful overall, weighing both how well each actually survives fink/mask-style counterexamples and how costly or ad hoc its underlying metaphysics is?
