I'm working on a piece about how AI labs decide whose values a chatbot should reflect, and I keep running into "liberal neutrality" getting treated like some sophisticated philosophical fix for the problem. Honestly it just sounds to me like a fancy name for what Anthropic already did with that Polis experiment a while back -- getting like a thousand random people to vote up or down on proposed principles and building the consitution out of whatever got broad agreement. If you crowdsource opinions from a wide enough, diverse enough group and just go with whatever most people can live with, isn't that neutrality already? Not sure what the "neutral" part is supposed to add on top of large-scale aggregation that isn't already there.
