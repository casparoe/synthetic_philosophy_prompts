He made a point of saying this isnt about her having secretly already made up her mind somewhere in the back of her head (he knows she's genuinely torn and could go either way depending on how it all unfolds in the moment) -- he means something weirder, that the statement about tomorrow is already true or false as a plain matter of logic, independent of anyone's actual state of mind.

Honestly some part of me finds this weirdly comforting and wants to just relax and stop obsessing over the speech. Is my friend's reasoning actually sound here, and if not, where exactly does it go wrong -- do I have to say it isn't true or false yet which one will happen, or is there some other way to keep saying it's already true or false while still holding onto the idea that my effort actually matters?
