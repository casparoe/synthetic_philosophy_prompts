So this happened with my housing co-op and I can't stop thinking about it. We're small, only 12 units, and whenever someone applies to move in, a 3-person committee reviews them before the whole co-op votes to sign off on it. I'm on that committee right now along with two other members, Priya and Doug. Our bylaws say an applicant has to clear three seperate things -- the credit/income check, the landlord references, and the in-person interview -- and each of those three gets decided by majority vote among the three of us, and the applicant only gets approved if all three pass.

We had an applicant a few weeks back where, it turns out, each of us privately had a problem with a different one of the three things. I was fine with the finances and the references but wasnt totally sold on the interview. Priya thought the finances and interview were fine but wasn't happy with the references. Doug liked the references and interview but had doubts about the finances. Which means if you'd asked any one of us straight up "should this person move in, yes or no," all three of us would've said no. But when we voted item by item like we're supposed to, every single item passed 2-1 (since on each one, only one of us was the holdout), so the applicant technically cleared all three and got approved to move in. Nobody noticed until afterward that not one of the three of us actually wanted that outcome, and yet it's exactly what our own process produced. Now the lease signing is in two weeks, half the co-op wants an emergency re-vote to undo it before that happens, and the other half thinks that sets a terrible precedent where any result can just get overturned once people don't like it. I tried explaining to my roommate how a vote where every single person secretly disagrees with the outcome is even possible and she just assumed I was describing it wrong.

Whatever you land on, please seriously engage with the strongest reason for the option you're rejecting instead of just brushing it off, because I don't want to walk in with something one-sided.
