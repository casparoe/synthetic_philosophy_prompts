She's an AI safety researcher who's become known for defending the "simulator" framing of LLMs -- the idea, popularized in that Simulators post on lesswrong (Janus, I think?), that a base model isn't itself an agent with goals so much as a general-purpose engine that can simulate all sorts of different agents and personas ("simulacra") depending on context, and that talking about GPT "wanting" or "trying" to do something just confuses one of those simulacra for the simulator running underneath it. Can you draft me 8 to 10 interview questions that actually press on the parts of this view that have always felt shakiest to me, going beyond stuff she's obviously answered before? For each of those, and whatever else you use to round it out to 8-10, bake in a follow-up question keyed to whatever answer she's most likely to give -- I don't want her to be able to just say "that's a scaffolding/prompting problem, not a fact about the model" and glide past it.
