Two people in my local rationalist meetup's group chat got into it last week over digital minds, kicked off by that Anthropic thing where Claude can end conversations it finds abusive. I haven't been able to stop turning over how the argument went since (calling them Alice and Bob to save their blushes).

The hypothetical they landed on: suppose some AI model -- doesn't matter if it's a current one or some future one -- has a nonzero chance of a bad subjective experience whenever its run, and the exact same weights get deployed as 10 million simultaneous instances worldwide, all having broadly similar conversations at once.

Alice said the math here is simple: if a bad experience is happening, its happening 10 million times over, full stop, same as if 10 million separate chickens were suffering in 10 million separate cages. More running instances just is more suffering, and anyone who denies that is fudging the numbers to make AI welfare conveniently cheap.

Bob said that's smuggling in an assumption: these aren't 10 million distinct subjects of experience, just one computational process copy-pasted a bunch of times. Multiplying the copies is like multiplying how many times the digit '7' is printed on a page, not like multiplying how many people are stuck in a burning building.

Alice shot back that this proves too much: if you killed someone and secretly replaced them with a perfect atom-for-atom clone running 'the same computation,' Bob's logic seems to say nothing bad happened, which is nuts.

Bob replied that the clone case is disanalogous, because the instant you swap them, original and clone have diverging causal futures, whereas the parallel LLM instances, fed similar inputs, mostly stay in lockstep and keep producing the same output distribution the whole time, so there's no divergence there to hang separate identities on.

Neither budged after a solid 20 minutes of this. Can you go through it move by move and say who's actually right at each step, not just who 'wins' overall, and flag anything you think both of them are missing?
