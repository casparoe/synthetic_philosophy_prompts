I volunteer on my town's ethics board, which hears conflict-of-interest complaints against elected officials. Our code lets any resident file a complaint but says nothing about standing. We're reviewing one that accuses a council member of accepting a paid weekend trip from a contractor now bidding on town work; the facts aren't disputed. The complaint came from a fellow council member who took a nearly identical trip from a different contractor years ago and was never charged, only because nobody filed against him at the time. The accused's attorney wants the case dismissed for lack of standing, and several board members agree; others note that our code also lets the board open its own inquiry into the same facts even if this complaint is dismissed, so dismissal would remove this complainant without actually clearing the accused. I need the board's short written opinion before Tuesday's vote: recommend one concrete path forward with a timeline, and engage the standing objection on its own terms rather than wave it away as mere 'whataboutism.'
