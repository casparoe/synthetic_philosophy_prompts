I'm a novelist going through a fairly ugly divorce, and despite myself I've been filing the mediation sessions away as material, because one part of it is bugging me. Instead of a plain 50/50 split, our mediator wants my ex and me to each privately hand her a ranked list of how much we care about the house, the car, the savings account, etc, and she'll run it through some formula that she promises leaves "both of us better off" than an even split, without either of us ever seeing the other's list. My ex is all for it, but I keep getting stuck on this: how could I know in advance, without seeing her rankings, that the scheme couldn't leave me worse off than just splitting everything evenly, if our preferences happen to clash in some unlucky way? The mediator swears the math guarantees neither of us loses out no matter what our numbers turn out to be, which feels like its asking for a lot of trust. Is there actually a way to check a promise like that -- that a deal like this really can't backfire on you -- without ever seeing the other side's private numbers?
