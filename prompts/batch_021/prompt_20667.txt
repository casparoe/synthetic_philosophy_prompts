Could you construct a specific example of such a distribution, with real numbers or a formula I could compute by hand, under which switching envelopes has a higher expected value than staying no matter which amount you actually find when you open yours? Does constructing such a distribution force some kind of strange conclusion about how much money you should expect to be riding on the whole game in the first place?
