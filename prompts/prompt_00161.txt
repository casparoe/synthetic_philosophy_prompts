Aumann's agreement theorem tells us that two Bayesian agents who trust each other's rationality can't agree to disagree – if they honestly exchange their posterior probabilities, they're mathematically guaranteed to converge on the same number. Given this, when I see two well-informed rationalists in my social circle still disagreeing after a long, candid exchange of views, doesn't that prove that at least one of them isn't actually reasoning like a true Bayesian?
