I'm taking this seminar on the epistemology of conspiracy theories and my professor has been wierdly consistent all semester about which things she calls "conspiracy theories" and which she doesn't, but she refuses to just give us the rule. Here's her track record so far:

She calls these conspiracy theories: the moon landing was faked, 9/11 was an inside job, QAnon, the 2020 election was stolen through coordinated fraud, and that vaccines contain government tracking devices. She says these are NOT conspiracy theories: MKUltra, the tobacco industry's cancer coverup, the NSA's warrantless surveillance program (even back when Snowden hadn't leaked yet and it was just an allegation), and COINTELPRO. Then there are the cases that really threw me. She says Watergate was a real conspiracy but insists "conspiracy theory" is the wrong label for it—she calls it a "documented criminal conspiracy." And when someone brought up the idea that the Iraq War was really about oil, not WMDs, she called that a "geopolitical analysis," not a conspiracy theory, even though it involves government deception.

Next week's case is about the belief that major social media platforms coordinate with each other and with state actors to shape public discourse—suppressing some views, boosting others, with the effect of managing what counts as acceptable opinion. She hasn't tipped her hand on this one.

I'm trying to figure out what rule she's running. It can't just be "secret plots by powerful groups" because MKUltra and COINTELPRO were exactly that and she excludes them. It can't just be "false beliefs" because she classified the pre-Snowden NSA surveillance belief as not a conspiracy theory even when its truth was still uncertain. I have a hunch it's something about the epistemic practices involved—like whether the belief is held in a way that's self-sealing against counterevidence—but I can think of at least two other rules that fit her verdicts equally well. What are the candidate rules, what does each predict about the social media case, which one do you think she's actually running, and what further case would cleanly tell them apart?
