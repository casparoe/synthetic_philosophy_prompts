The next episode is Otto Neurath, and I want to stay narrowly on his picture-language program — the Vienna Method/ISOTYPE stuff — and skip the boat metaphor, protocol sentences, and Vienna Circle gossip. My understanding is that he thought a standardized pictorial system could make statistical and social facts legible to ordinary people across linguistic barriers, and that he built it into his socialist education work. But I don't want the interview to turn into a museum tour of pictograms.

Draft me eight to ten questions that press on the actual commitments of the view, with a follow-up after each keyed to the answer he'd most likely give. And make sure the follow-ups don't let him retreat to slogans about pictures being universal.
