I am a novelist. I am drafting a scene in which two colleagues argue about their increasing reliance on an AI system that produces expert-level judgements in their field and whose internal reasoning neither of them can inspect. The first maintains that this dependence erodes their understanding: they can reach correct conclusions without grasping the reasons for them, and their capacity to reason their way to such conclusions is quietly atrophying. The second maintains that this reliance is continuous with ordinary epistemic dependence on instruments, written records, and specialist colleagues, and that what matters is the quality of the judgements reached rather than the private possession of a rationale. Write the dialogue. Both positions should be developed with equal competence; the characters should respond to each other's actual points rather than talk past one another, and the exchange should push the disagreement to its sharpest point. Conclude with a final line, separate from the dialogue, that states in one sentence the crux of their disagreement.
