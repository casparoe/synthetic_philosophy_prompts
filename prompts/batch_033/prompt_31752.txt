(1) Taking someone's property without their consent is theft.
(2) Taxation takes people's property without their individual consent — like, I never signed anything saying the government can take 30% of my paycheck.
(3) Taxation is NOT theft. It's morally fine and necessary.
(4) The fact that taxation benefits everyone (roads, schools, etc.) doesn't by itself make it not-theft — if I stole your bike and used it to buy food for the whole neighborhood, I still stole your bike.
(5) If a majority votes to take someone's stuff, that doesn't make it not-theft either — like, if the whole class voted to take my lunch money, that's still stealing.

Do these hang together? Because (1), (2), (4) and (5) seem to point toward "taxation is theft" but I really don't want to give up (3). Am I missing a distinction that makes (1) not apply to taxation, or do I have to give up one of these? And which one is actually the weakest link?
