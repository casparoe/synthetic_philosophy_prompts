When I try to explain it, people nod, but then someone always says "yeah but if you have the data you should definately find the optimal choice," and we're back to square one. I want them to feel the force of it in a game, not hear it from me.

At the end it should be visible that people who set a reasonable standard and took the first thing that met it did better on average than people who kept trying to find the absolute best.
