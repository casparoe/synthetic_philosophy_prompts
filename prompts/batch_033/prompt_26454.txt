Let's say Hannah Arendt were around today and watched the military adopt semi-autonomous targeting systems where a human operator mostly approves or rejects a machine's recommendation and often never sees the target. Would she see this as a new form of the banality of evil, or as something that breaks the Eichmann analogy? Work from what she actually thought was going on with Eichmann — the role of thoughtlessness, clichés, and the way his failure to think connected to his crimes — and then apply it to a case she never saw: an operator who approves a strike on the basis of a confidence score and later says they were just following the system. I dont want a list of similarities between drones and Eichmann; I want the specific points where her analysis would still bite, and the points where the technology would force a revision.
