Write a sonnet about the distinction between access consciousness and phenomenal consciousness, as framed by Ned Block and set against global workspace theory. The octave should describe the global workspace picture — consciousness as whatever wins the competition for broadcast to frontal and parietal areas, what we can report and reason with — and the sestet should give the phenomenal residue: the redness of red, the ache, the what-it's-like that seems to slip through the workspace net.
