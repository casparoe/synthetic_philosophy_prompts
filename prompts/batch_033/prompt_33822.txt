I want your honest ranking, not a survey answer.

Take these six candidate cases and sort them into three or four tiers, from "basically reducible in practice, no deep problem" down to "probably needs something over and above the parts": (1) the thermodynamic arrow of time and entropy increase, (2) chemical bonding and the periodic table, (3) life and metabolism, (4) superconductivity, (5) phenomenal consciousness, (6) turbulent fluid flow. 1. Tier list (about 100 words): the three or four tiers, with each of the six items placed in one.

3. Placement notes (about 180 words): a sentence or two per item defending why it landed in its tier.
