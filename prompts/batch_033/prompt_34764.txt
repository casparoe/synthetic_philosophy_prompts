The document should include: (1) a numbered list of claims both sides can sign, covering what the Fermi paradox is, what the observational data are, and what formal constraints both accept; (2) a disagreements table with precise formulations of where they differ, phrased so that each side would accept the wording of its own position; (3) the identified cruxes — the sub-questions on which the larger disagreement turns; and (4) for each crux, the evidence, arguments, or empirical tests that both sides agree would bear on it, with each side's prediction recorded in advance. Pay particular attention to whether any part of the dispute is verbal rather than substantive (e.g., disagreements about what counts as a "filter" or about the reference class for anthropic reasoning).
