I just spent three hours arguing with a friend about Sleeping Beauty, and I'm starting to worry I've painted myself into a corner. I'd like someone to go through my commitments and tell me whether they actually hang together, or whether I've been helping myself to positions that pull against each other without noticing.

Here's where I've landed:

(1) I'm a thirder. When Beauty is awakened, her credence in heads should be 1/3.

(2) I think Beauty, upon waking, receives no new evidence about the outcome of the coin toss — every aspect of the waking experience was fully anticipated on Sunday.

(3) I hold that self-locating beliefs should be updated by conditionalization on centered propositions, just as ordinary beliefs are updated by conditionalization on uncentered propositions. There is no special epistemology for indexical uncertainty; the standard Bayesian machinery applies uniformly.

(4) I reject the Doomsday argument. I don't think that learning my approximate birth rank should substantially shift my credence about how many humans will ever live.

(5) I accept that in the Everettian (many-worlds) interpretation of quantum mechanics, an agent should have self-locating uncertainty about which branch they occupy, and should assign credences to branches in proportion to their Born-rule weights.

My friend kept insisting that (2) and (3) together rule out (1) — that if Beauty gains no new evidence and updates by conditionalization, her credence must stay at 1/2. She also said that (4) is just an ad hoc carve-out, because the thirder reasoning in (1) seems to rely on the very same indifference-style reasoning across observer-moments that drives the Doomsday argument. And she muttered something about (5) being in tension with the rest, though she didn't spell it out. I'm not sure which of these complaints are serious and which are just rhetorical. Can you go through these five commitments and tell me which pairs are genuinely in tension, which tensions are only apparent and dissolve on closer inspection, and what the cheapest package of revisions would be to restore consistency? Please rank the tensions by severity rather than treating them all as equally bad.
