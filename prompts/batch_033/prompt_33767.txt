(2) I think the self is a strange loop in that family: the mind is a self-referential pattern running on the brain, not a separate substance or a homunculus.

(6) Seperately, I think a person can in principle come to see the actual machinery of their own thought through reflection or meditation — not just tell a flattering post-hoc story about it, but really catch sight of the rules doing the work.
