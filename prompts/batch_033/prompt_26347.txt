Alright, you know Hofstadter's careenium thought experiment from I Am a Strange Loop? You've got a box with tons of tiny magnetic marbles — simms — bouncing around. Sometimes they clump together into bigger patterns called simmballs, which have their own dynamics at the macro level. The core question: who shoves whom? Do the simms push the simmballs around (strictly bottom-up, all causation at the micro level), or do the simmballs push the simms around — do those macro-level patterns and symbols have genuine causal power over the substrate they're made of?

I want you to argue that the simmballs DO push the simms around — that higher-level patterns genuinely constrain and shape what happens at the micro level. Give me the strongest case you can. And please deal with the obvious objection head-on: that this is just epiphenomenalism in a cute outfit — simmballs are nothing over and above configurations of simms, so saying a simmball causes simm behavior is just sloppy shorthand for simm-to-simm causation.
