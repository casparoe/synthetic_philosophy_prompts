I've been stuck on this question for a while and I'm hoping someone can tell me if there's a name for what I'm thinking about.

So we all learn that a contradiction is the worst thing in an argument. You say two things that can't both be true, and your whole position is garbage. The reason people usually give is that once you've got a contradiction, you can prove anything — "from a contradiction, anything follows." Fair enough.

But heres what's been bugging me. In actual life, people run into contradictions constantly and they don't treat them like nuclear bombs. They just work around them. A few examples:

- In the law, you'll have two solid precedents that point in opposite directions for the same case. The judge doesn't say "well, since these two rules contradict, I can now rule that the defendant is a fish." They just reason through it and reach a decision.

- In science, you've got theories that contradict each other on certain predictions but scientists use both anyway. Newton and Einstein disagree on simultaneity, but nobody concludes that therefore anything goes.

- In everyday talk, someone says "I'm glad I moved here and I'm not glad I moved here." You know what they mean — they got a better job but miss their friends. Nobody says "contradiction! Therefore you're secretly the king of France."

So I started wondering: is there a name for the position that some contradictions are actually true, or at least that our logic should be able to handle them without the whole thing collapsing? I don't mean the move where you say "well its true in one sense and false in another" — that's just denying there's a real contradiction. I mean the stronger thing: the same claim, in the same sense, is both true and false, and that's not a disaster.

And if this position does have a name, what are the different flavors of it? Do some people think only very special contradictions qualify (like the liar paradox, "this sentence is false") while others think contradictions are just a normal part of thinking? And how do you stop the ones you do accept from infecting everything else — like, what's the firewall that keeps the contradiction from letting you prove nonsense?
