so i went down a rabbit hole on this thing called bradleys regress -- you know, the idea that if youve got a thing and a property, you need some kind of glue to stick them together, but then you need glue to stick the glue to the thing and the property, and then you need more glue for that glue, and it never bottoms out. it feels like one of those ideas that just eats itself forever. could you write a villanelle about it where the two refrains are the two voices of the regress -- one that keeps insisting "these two are one" and the other that keeps asking "but what connects them?" -- and the form itself does the work of trapping you in the loop? like the poem should feel like it cant ever end, even when it does. someone who knows the problem should feel the regress in the rhyme scheme itself.
