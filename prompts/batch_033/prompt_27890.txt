I've been trying to wrap my head around Wittgenstein's On Certainty and the whole hinge epistemology thing. So the idea is that at the bottom of all our knowledge there are these "hinge" propositions — stuff like "the earth has existed for more than five minutes" or "here is a hand" — that we can't actually justify, we just have to take them for granted. But here's what I don't get: if the foundations of our knowledge are literally unjustified, then isn't the whole edifice unsound? Like, in software terms, if your base layer has unpatched vulnerabilities, your whole stack is compromised. It seems to me Wittgenstein is basically handing the skeptic a win and then just... shrugging? What am I missing?
