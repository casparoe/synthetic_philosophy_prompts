so me and a friend have been going back and forth for like a year about what makes something a disease. she's in the camp that you can define disease purely in biological terms -- like, your body isn't working the way it's supposed to, some mechanism is broken, that's it. i'm on the other side where i think you can't get away from values -- calling something a disease always involves a judgment that a condition is bad, harmful, undesirable in some way. we've read some stuff online about it but neither of us is in academia, we just find it interesting.

we've decided to try an "adversarial collaboration" -- basically we want to write a document together that we can both sign, that lays out what we agree on, where exactly we part ways, and what would actually change either of our minds. the hope is to stop talking past each other.

can you draft that document for us? here's what we want in it:

first, a numbered list of claims we can both accept -- these should be substantive, not just dictionary definitions. we're both okay with some of the obvious stuff (like, broken leg = disease, being in love = not a disease) but we want claims that actually do some work in narrowing things down.

second, a table of our disagreements. each row should have: the specific sub-question we disagree on, her position stated in wording she'd approve, my position stated in wording i'd approve, and whether this disagreement is a crux (meaning if we resolved it, at least one of us would change our overall view).

third, for each crux, we want a concrete case or test that we both agree bears on it. and for each case, we write down in advance what each of us predicts or what each of us would say about it. the idea is to pin down what evidence would actually move us.

we've figured out that some of our past arguments were just about words -- like we both agree infection and injury are diseases, and we both agree that being short or having a low pain tolerance aren't diseases even if they're statistically unusual. we think the interesting edge cases are things like: infertility in someone who doesn't want kids, homosexuality (back when it was classified as a disorder), very high IQ, or a genetic trait that's harmful in one environment but beneficial in another.

oh and please write the whole thing in american english. and if the format needs tweaking to make it work better that's fine, just explain any changes you make.
