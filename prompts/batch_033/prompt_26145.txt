I'm choosing between two job offers and my own reasoning is bothering me. One is at a large, established company: the salary band, promotion timeline, and culture are well-documented and narrow in range. The other is at an early-stage startup: lower base pay but equity that could be worth nothing or a great deal. When I estimate expected values, the startup comes out ahead under most reasonable assumptions. Yet I keep gravitating toward the corporate offer, and I think it's because the uncertainties there are quantified and familiar — I know what I don't know. With the startup, I don't even know the shape of what I don't know; the probabilities themselves feel uncertain in a way that makes the expected-value calculation feel hollow. Is there a principled reason to prefer known risks over unknown ones, or am I just dressing up a cognitive bias?
