Popper's falsificationism seems to give a clear and decisive answer to the demarcation problem: a theory is scientific if and only if it is falsifiable — that is, if it forbids some possible observations and would be refuted were those observations made. Given that this criterion appears to correctly classify evolution and general relativity as scientific while excluding astrology and Freudian psychoanalysis, why do philosophers of science still treat the demarcation problem as open? Is the remaining debate driven by anything more than professional inertia?
