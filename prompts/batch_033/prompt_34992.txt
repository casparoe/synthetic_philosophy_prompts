Dennett says this picture is deeply wrong and proposes the multiple drafts model instead: there's no single place or time where consciousness "happens"; instead, the brain runs many parallel streams of processing, and what we call a conscious experience is more like a narrative that gets cobbled together, with no canonical version and no central audience.
