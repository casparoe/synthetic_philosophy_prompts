In a philosophy of computation seminar I sat in on, people talked about it as an empirical hypothesis about what physical processes can compute. And then I had this argument at a conference reception where a guy from quantum information insisted that quantum computing has already falsified it, which made a logician at the table nearly spit out his drink because apparently that's a category mistake and you can't falsify a definition with an experiment. Meanwhile I've seen people online call it "just a heuristic" or "a rule of thumb" or "basically the same as the physical Church-Turing thesis but nobody distinguishes them."

So here's what I want.
