So I'm a novelist, and I've hit this weird thing with my current book that's been bugging me. I'm writing a scene where a character dies — a character I've been writing for three books now, someone I genuinely care about even though, you know, I made her up. And I keep putting off writing the death scene because every time I sit down to do it I get this awful pit in my stomach. I actually teared up outlining it.

My partner thinks I've lost it. She says "you know she's not real, right?" And of course I know that. But the feeling doesnt seem to care whether I know it or not.

But here's where it gets stranger to me. I'm literally designing this scene to devastate my readers. I'm choosing details specifically because they'll hurt. I want people to cry on the subway. And they will — I've done it before, I get emails from people telling me they sobbed. These are adults who fully understand they're reading fiction. They're not confused about what's real. And yet they voluntarily sign up for this, and they thank me afterwards.

What is actually going on here? Am I doing something manipulative? Or is there a way of understanding emotions where feeling real grief for someone you know doesn't exist is actually totally rational?
