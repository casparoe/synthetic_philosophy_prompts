Conditional on humanity developing transformative AI — systems that can do essentially all the intellectual work humans currently do — the systems that actually do that work will be substantially agentic rather than tool-like. By "tool-like" I mean: the system answers questions or executes well-specified tasks but does not set its own objectives or act on its own initiative.

(e) How likely is it that the tool/agent distinction is coherent and stable enough for the proposition to have a determinate truth value at all, rather than breaking down under pressure?
