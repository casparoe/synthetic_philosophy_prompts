Consider the pre-deployment safety protocol for a frontier language model at a major lab. The protocol combines alignment measures with control measures. On the alignment side, the lab trains the model with reinforcement learning from human feedback and against written principles, treating helpfulness and harmlessness as dispositions that can be shaped by the training signal. On the control side, the lab acts as though that shaping cannot be fully trusted: it red-teams the model to elicit behavior the training did not reward, runs dangerous-capability evaluations, tests for sandbagging (the possibility that the model is strategically underperforming in order to pass testing), filters inputs and outputs with classifiers, restricts or stages access to the weights, and writes system prompts instructing the model to remain harmless. Reconstruct the implicit theory — or theories — of what a trained model is and of what misalignment consists in that this combination presupposes. Does the alignment side presuppose that a model's preferences are just whatever the reward signal shapes them into, while the control side presupposes that the model may already have durable goals of its own that it could hide or pursue adversarially? Are those two pictures compatible, or do they quietly conflict? In particular, does testing for sandbagging make sense only on the assumption that the model is a strategic agent with beliefs about its own training and deployment, and does that assumption require a different view of the model than the view presupposed by RLHF? Distinguish what the practices strictly presuppose from what they merely fit well with, and say where they are underdetermined between rival theories. Finally, say what the protocol would have to change if the lab adopted one consistent picture throughout — a thoroughgoing behaviorism (alignment is nothing over and above behavioral dispositions) or a thoroughgoing realism about model goals — and whether the current mix is best read as incoherent or as defensible hedging between pictures it has not chosen between.
