I've been reading about David Chalmers's distinction between the "hard problem" of consciousness and the "meta-problem" — the problem of explaining why we think there is a hard problem in the first place. It seems to me that a successful solution to the meta-problem would simply dissolve the hard problem. If we can give a complete physical explanation for why creatures like us generate the powerful intuition that phenomenal consciousness resists physical explanation, then we have explained away the only evidence we ever had for the existence of a hard problem — namely, our inability to conceive how experience could be physical. What remains of the hard problem once we have accounted for the intuition that there is one?
