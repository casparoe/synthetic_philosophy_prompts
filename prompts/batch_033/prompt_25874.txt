I'm running a philosophy reading group on Kripke's Naming and Necessity and we're about to hit the origin essentialism bit — the claim that you couldn't have come from a different sperm and egg. I want to make a 'which position do you actually hold?' sorting quiz for the group, maybe 8 questions. Each question should be a concrete scenario that pulls apart different intuitions about what matters for 'being you' at the point of origin: things like an IVF mix-up where the embryo that became you was accidentally swapped with another couple's embryo before implantation, a demon who replaces the winning sperm with a different sperm from the same ejaculation a split second before fertilization, same sperm and egg but gestated in a different womb, same genome assembled from different gametes, a teletransporter accident that scrambles your DNA but preserves your psychology, that kind of thing. Two or three answer options per question. The scoring key should map people onto: Kripkean origin essentialism, genetic essentialism (same genome = same person, different gametes fine), psychological-continuity views (gametes don't matter except through psychology), animalism (you're the organism and origin isn't what makes it you), and deflationary/no-fact-of-the-matter views. The quiz should sort by case judgments, not by which label people like, so no question should just ask 'do you think your origin is essential to you?' Also flag any two positions the quiz can't cleanly separate, and note if there are logically possible answer patterns that nobody seems to hold — and say whether thats because the view is incoherent, unappealing, or just underexplored.
