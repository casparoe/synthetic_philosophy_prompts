Spinoza held that democracy is the most natural form of government, since the sovereign is the whole body of citizens, and he thought the state should aim at peace and freedom. But he also thought most people are led more by affects than by reason. Rank these features of modern mass democracy by how much you think he'd have favored them, from most to least, and say a sentence for each: universal adult suffrage, party-list proportional representation, judicial review, ballot initiatives, and opinion polling used by legislators.
