My epistemology professor's verdicts on the cases we've covered so far:

- Sees a barn in normal conditions: knowledge.
- Sees a barn in fake-barn county: no knowledge.
- Losing lottery ticket (fair, 1-in-10-million odds): no knowledge.
- "I have hands": knowledge, but she insists you can't give a non-circular argument for it.
- "I'm not a brain in a vat": knowledge, but again she says you can't prove it.
- "The earth has existed for more than five minutes": knowledge — can't prove it, still know it.
- Stopped clock that happens to show the right time: no knowledge.
- Reliable stranger's testimony about the time: knowledge.
- "Water is H2O": knowledge, though revisable if the chemistry turned out wrong.
