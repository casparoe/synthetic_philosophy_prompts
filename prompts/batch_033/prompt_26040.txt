I've been in a political economy reading group for the past few months and we've been working through the socialist calculation debate — Mises's original challenge, Hayek's stuff on dispersed knowledge, Lange's market socialism response, the back-and-forth through the 40s, and then the revival in the 80s and 90s. And I've hit this wall where I feel like nobody actually moves. The Austrians keep saying you can't simulate prices without real property rights and actual entrepreneurs taking risks, the market socialists keep saying you can approximate it with trial-and-error pricing and modern computational methods, and both sides seem to be talking past each other. What's weird is that everyone in the group is pretty sharp and reads the same texts, and yet after months we're basicaly where we started — the people who came in Hayekian are still Hayekian, the Lange sympathizers are still Lange sympathizers.

I have a working theory but I'm not sure it's right. My theory is that the debate isn't really about economics anymore — it's about what counts as "knowledge" and whether knowledge that only exists in distributed, tacit form (Hayek's point) can in principle be approximated by a central algorithm, or whether the approximation necessarily loses the thing that makes it knowledge in the first place. If that's right, then the debate is really a disagreement in epistemology that got dressed up as economics. But I could also see it being about values — about whether the loss of the price mechanism's informational efficiency is worth it for whatever distributive gains socialism promises — or about something even more basic, like different intuitions about what an economic explanation has to accomplish. Or maybe it's a verbal dispute where "calculation" means different things to each side and nobody's noticed.

So: why has this debate actually stalled? What kind of disagreement is it — factual, epistemological, normative, verbal, or some mix? And given your diagnosis, is there anything that could actually move it, or is this one of those debates where the two sides are just going to keep restating their positions forever?
