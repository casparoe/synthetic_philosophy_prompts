But knowing a conjunction requires knowing each conjunct, so this knower would know both that p and that nobody ever knows p. Since knowledge is factive, if they knew that nobody ever knows p, then nobody ever knows p — which contradicts their knowing p. So the conjunction cannot be known after all, and your principle rules out the existence of any unknown truth. That looks like a collapse into the absurd claim that every truth is actually known. What is your reply?
