I spent three decades as a physiologist before I retired, and in the last couple years I've fallen hard into philosophy of medicine. I've got a chance to apply for a six-month visiting fellowship at a philosophy of medicine center, and they want a one-page research proposal. I keep going around in circles on the naturalism vs normativism debate about health and disease, and I'd like you to turn my half-baked idea into a proper proposal.

Here's where I'm stuck. The naturalist side (the Boorse-style view, roughly) says disease is just failure of biological function relative to a species-typical design, and thats a value-free, scientific matter. The normativist side says no, you can't call something a disease without some evaluative judgment about which states are bad for a person. But from my clinical and lab experience, most of the actual disputes aren't about whether someone has a dysfunction — they're about where you draw the line on continuous variables. Blood pressure, blood glucose, bone density: the cutoffs for 'hypertension' or 'prediabetes' have moved around over the years, and the decisions clearly involved values about risk, about who should be treated, about overdiagnosis. Yet neither side of the debate seems to talk much about thresholds. The naturalists act as if settling the concept of dysfunction settles the whole question; the normativists act as if the values are somehow in the concept of disease itself, rather than in the line-drawing.

So my half-formed idea is this: the debate conflates two different things — (a) the notion of biological dysfunction, and (b) the setting of thresholds for clinical categories — and maybe once you pull those apart, naturalism and normativism turn out to be answers to different questions, not rivals. The proposal should state the question precisely, explain why the existing positions leave it open and why this threshold angle is a better way in than just re-litigating Boorse vs the normativists, say what I would actually do month by month, what a deliverable would look like for people in both philosophy and medicine, and what would count as the project failing. Keep the scope honest for six months. Dont bother with citations, I'll add those myself.
