My psychology teacher summarized evolutionary psychology like this: "The central claim is that the human mind is a collection of specialized modules that evolved to solve the specific problems our ancestors faced in the Pleistocene. The problem is that these explanations are untestable: we can't run experiments on ancestral humans, so there's no way to check whether the story is true. Gould and Lewontin called this approach 'just-so stories,' and that's basically what it is. It's not real science—it's speculation dressed up in evolutionary language."
