(6) Priya said that is not the same, because a range refuses to commit to how the missing information will resolve, and people who treat a range as a distribution choose differently in cases where one option has known odds and another has unknown odds. (7) Marcus said those people are simply violating the standard axioms of rational choice, so their choices are mistakes.
