ok so for my school's philosophy club i'm making one of those "which theory of punishment do you actually hold?" quizzes for our website. we're doing a unit on criminal justice and my teacher said it'd be cool to have something interactive. the idea is people read short scenarios about crimes and pick what punishment they think is right, and at the end it tells them what theory they actually line up with based on their answers.

the thing is i dont want it to be obvious. no questions like "do you believe punishment should make people suffer?" or "should punishment deter future crime?" cause everyone just picks whatever sounds enlightened. i want the questions to be concrete cases where the theories genuinely come apart — like, a specific crime with a specific offender, and the answer options are actual things you'd do (sentence lengths, programs, whether to offer parole, etc.).

i want 8-10 questions total. each should be a short paragraph describing a scenario, and then 2-3 answer options that are concrete and realistic. plus a scoring key that maps answer patterns onto positions. the positions should include at least: pure retributivism, pure deterrence, rehabilitation-focused, incapacitation-focused, and at least one hybrid view (like "retributivism with a side of rehabilitation" or whatever). if there's a combination of answers that your quiz would spit out but nobody actually defends that position, flag it and say why it's probably empty — like is it incoherent or just unattractive or what.

also here's the thing: i want it to catch people who are lying to themselves. like someone who says they're all about rehabilitation but then picks the harshest sentence every time. so the scoring should care about consistency, not just tallying. and the result page should be able to say something like "you answered like a retributivist on 7 out of 10 questions even though you probably think of yourself as a rehabilitation person."

take a definite stance on what the best diagnostic questions are and why they work — don't just give me a menu of possible questions. i want a quiz that actually sorts people, not a survey of ideas for a quiz.
