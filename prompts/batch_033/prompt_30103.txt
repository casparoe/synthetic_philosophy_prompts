What's striking is how uneven they are: some are obvious in under a second, and others stay opaque no matter how long you look, even though the drawings themselves are simple. The usual story people give is that the hard ones are "abstract" or that the rule is "unexpected," but that mostly relabels the phenomenon. In particular: is the hard part mainly a search problem, a representational problem, or a problem about knowing which level of abstraction to try? And why do some Bongard problems stay hard even when you add more examples, while a single re-description of the examples you already had can make the rule obvious?
