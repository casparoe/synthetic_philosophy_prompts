I've been going back and forth on Harsanyi's aggregation theorem and whether it actually gets you utilitarianism. The pro camp says the theorem basically forces utilitarianism out of almost nothing: just vNM rationality for individuals and society plus a Pareto condition. The skeptic camp says it's a formal restatement of assumptions, and the only reason the conclusion looks like utilitarianism is that all the normative content is smuggled into the setup — especially what vNM utilities are supposed to represent, and what the Pareto condition is allowed to range over. Write the document those two camps would produce after a good-faith adversarial collaboration where they actually tried to converge, not just re-argue.
