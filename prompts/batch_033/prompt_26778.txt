There's been a fair amount of debate about Frege's political diary — the one he kept in 1924, with the antisemitic and anti-democratic entries. I keep going back and forth about whether any of that should change how we read his philosophical work: the logicism, the anti-psychologism, the sense/reference stuff. Write an essay on whether Frege's political diary bears on how we should assess his philosophy. Weigh the main arguments on both sides — the line that this is all just biography or ad hominem and his arguments stand or fall on their own, the line that a philosopher's broader commitments can shape which problems they think are worth solving and what counts as a good solution, and the stronger line that some of Frege's philosophical moves might line up with the politics in non-accidental ways. Dont end on a non-committal note; defend a verdict about whether and to what extent it bears on his philosophy, and be clear about what exactly the diary would and wouldn't license.
