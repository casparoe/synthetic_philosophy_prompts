Plan the session: what idea has to be in place before I even state Epicurus's argument, what one example should carry the whole thing (people sometimes reach for the prenatal nonexistence symmetry — is that the right move or does it confuse things early on?), the misconceptions in the order they'll come up — I'm expecting (1) "but dying can be painful," (2) "but we fear death, so it must be bad," (3) "this is just word games about 'experience'," (4) "what about the life you miss out on?," and maybe (5) "so then painlessly killing someone is fine?" — and exactly where in the sequence I should head each one off and where I should let one sit because it's productive.
