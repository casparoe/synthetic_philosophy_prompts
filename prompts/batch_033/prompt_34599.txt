The Darwinian → Skinnerian → Popperian → Gregorian progression is supposed to track how the selectional mechanism gets internalized at each step. But here's the thing I keep tripping over: what's the actual difference between a Popperian creature and a Gregorian one, *in terms of the selectional architecture*?

A Popperian creature has an inner environment where hypotheses can die instead of the creature. A Gregorian creature imports mind-tools — language especially — from the cultural environment.
