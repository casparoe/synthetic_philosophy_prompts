I'm working on a paper about the legacy of Quine's "Two Dogmas" and I want to check whether my position is internally consistent before I commit to it in print. Here's where I've landed:

(1) Quine's circularity argument against analyticity is basically right: there is no non-circular way to define "analytic" that does not already presuppose something in the same family, like synonymy or a meaning-rule.

(2) Despite (1), I think there are sentences that are true purely in virtue of meaning. "All bachelors are unmarried" really is different from "All bachelors are unhappy" in a way that is not just a matter of how entrenched each belief is or how reluctant we are to give it up.

(3) I am a meaning holist: the meaning of any individual sentence depends on its inferential connections to the whole language, and you cannot assign meanings to sentences one by one.

(4) I think translation between languages is possible in principle, and that synonymy across languages is not indeterminate in the way Quine claimed. If a French speaker says "Il pleut" and an English speaker says "It's raining," there is a fact of the matter about whether they mean the same thing.

(5) I think logical truths — like "Either it's raining or it's not" — are analytic in a stronger sense than "All bachelors are unmarried" is, and that this difference is philosophically important.

(6) I think that for at least some sentences, the distinction between changing the meaning of a word and changing your mind about a fact is clear and defensible. If someone starts denying that bachelors are unmarried, they have changed the subject; if someone starts denying that bachelors are unhappy, they have changed their mind about a sociological claim.

Before I write this up, I would like to know: which of these commitments are genuinely in tension with which others, and which tensions are only apparent? I want the tensions ranked by severity, and for each genuine tension I would like to know what the cheapest revision would be. Please do not flag every pair — some of these look like they conflict but do not, and I need help telling which is which.
