ok here's one: write me a pantoum about the color phi phenomenon and the Orwellian/Stalinesque distinction Dennett makes in Consciousness Explained. you know the setup: a red dot flashes, then a green dot flashes a little to the side, and people report a single moving dot that switches from red to green roughly halfway. i want the pantoum's repetition to do the philosophical work, not just decorate it. because the second and fourth lines of each quatrain come back as the first and third of the next, the poem should leave it undecidable whether the earlier lines are being retroactively rewritten by the later ones (the Orwellian story) or whether the earlier lines were always staged after the fact to include what was coming (the Stalinesque story). by the last stanza, when the poem loops back around to its opening lines, someone who knows the debate should feel the two stories are the same poem, and someone who doesn't should still get why the timing of experience is weird. keep it accurate about the illusion itself, and dont just name-drop the experiment.
