Lay out the candidate rules that fit her verdicts so far — including the possibility that she's not running a rule at all, just judging each case on its own terms — and say what each one predicts about the party lie. Then give me your best guess about what she'll say, and tell me which further case would most cleanly settle which of those positions she's actually in.
