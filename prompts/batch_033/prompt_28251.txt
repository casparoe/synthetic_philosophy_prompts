I am a PhD student working on the philosophy of mathematics and mind, and I have been going back and forth about the Gödelian anti-mechanist arguments associated with Lucas and Penrose. I want a probability estimate with the reasoning made explicit, not a survey of positions.

Please state your credence in the following claim:

(C) Human mathematical competence is not mechanizable in Lucas and Penrose's sense: there is no single consistent, recursively axiomatizable formal system F such that the arithmetical sentences an idealized competent human mathematician could in principle come to accept are exactly the theorems of F.

Decompose your estimate into at least these five terms, giving a number and one line of justification for each:

(a) The idealization is coherent: the notion of the arithmetical sentences an idealized human could in principle accept is well-defined enough for (C) to have a truth value.
(b) The diagonal core is sound: conditional on a candidate F exactly matching the human's output and the human being able to recognize F's consistency, the human could accept F's Gödel sentence while F cannot, forcing a mismatch.
(c) The reflection premise: for any candidate F, no matter how large or alien, the idealized human can in fact come to recognize that F is consistent, or equivalently see the truth of its Gödel sentence.
(d) The consistency premise: the idealized human's arithmetic output is itself consistent, so the argument does not collapse into the trivial case of an inconsistent system.
(e) The standard deflationary replies fail: for example, the Benacerraf-style point that the human cannot in general know that F is their own system, and the suggestion that the ability to see Gödel sentences is itself simulable within a broader formal system.

Then show how you combine (a) through (e) into your credence for (C). Finally, identify the single term to which your final number is most sensitive, and say what evidence or argument short of a full proof or refutation would move that term, and therefore your overall credence, the most.
