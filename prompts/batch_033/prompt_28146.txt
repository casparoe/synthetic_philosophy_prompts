I've been trying to wrap my head around the hole argument and I don't get why it hasn't settled things. As I understand it: take a spacetime manifold with some metric and matter fields, apply a diffeomorphism that's only nontrivial inside some region, and you get a new model that's empirically equivalent but has the fields shifted around relative to the points. If you think spacetime points are real (substantivalism), then these are genuinely different worlds, so GR comes out indeterministic. But nobody thinks GR is indeterministic like that. So... doesn't the hole argument just refute substantivalism? Why is sophisticated substantivalism even a thing? It feels like they're just inventing epicycles to avoid the obvious conclusion.
