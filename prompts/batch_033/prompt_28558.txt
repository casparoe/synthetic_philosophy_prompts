I run a small philosophy discussion group, and last month we got into an argument about universals that made me realize I don't actually know what I believe. I'd say one thing about justice and another about the color red, and someone pointed out I was switching positions without noticing. I want to make a "which theory of universals do you actually hold?" quiz for the group — one that sorts people by what they say about concrete cases, not by which label sounds sophisticated.

Please write exactly five diagnostic questions, numbered 1 through 5, each a sentence or two presenting a concrete scenario with three or four answer options that force a choice between different metaphysical commitments about universals. No introduction or conclusion to the list — just the five questions. Then, after the questions, provide a scoring key that maps answer patterns onto at least these five positions: Platonic realism, Aristotelian realism, resemblance nominalism, trope theory, and conceptualism. If some combinations of answers don't correspond to any defended position, flag them and say briefly why nobody lives there. If two of the five positions give identical answers to all five questions, say so and explain what additional question would split them.
