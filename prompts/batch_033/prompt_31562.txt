Please provide: the rules of the game (including exactly how bids work, what the payoff structure is, and whether real money is involved or some substitute like points toward a grade), a timeline for the session, and a debrief plan that maps specific moments in the game onto the theoretical concepts (sunk cost fallacy, escalation of commitment, the point at which the game becomes a trap).
