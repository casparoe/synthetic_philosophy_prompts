The two programs are run, and payoffs are as in the original game. (a) Define what it is for a pair of programs to be an equilibrium in this setting. Student answer:

"(a) A pair of programs is an equilibrium iff neither player can improve their payoff by unilaterally submitting a different program, holding the other player's program fixed.
