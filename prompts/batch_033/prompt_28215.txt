And then the third-to-last person knows *that*, so they should take it even earlier. If you follow this logic all the way back, the very first person should grab the bag immediately and the whole sharing thing should never get off the ground. But in real life it does. People pass it along for a few rounds, nobody grabs it on step one, and the whole chain doesn't unravel the way the logic says it should.
