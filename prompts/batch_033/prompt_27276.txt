ok so I'm trying to get clear on the whole standing-to-blame thing and I've written down where I think I am on it. before I commit to this in a paper I want someone to go through it and tell me if it actually hangs together. here's what I think:

(1) hypocrites lack standing to blame. this is the basic intuition and I'm not giving it up.

(2) the best explanation for why hypocrites lack standing is that blame is a form of moral protest, and you can't coherently protest conduct that you yourself engage in. like, the act of blaming says "this is unacceptable" but your own behavior says "this is acceptable," and that contradiction undermines the speech act.

(3) but standing isn't just about hypocrisy. complete strangers—meddlers, bystanders with no stake in the situation—can also lack standing to blame, even when they've never done the thing in question. if someone cuts in line at a store and a random person three states away hears about it and sends them an angry email, something's off there too.

(4) standing is a seperate normative dimension from desert. the question of whether someone has standing to blame is independent of whether the target actually did wrong and actually deserves blame. you can do something blameworthy and still be blamed by someone who shouldn't be the one doing it.

(5) standing can be regained. if you used to do the thing but you've genuinely repented, changed your behavior, and made amends, you're no longer a hypocrite when you blame others for it.

(6) at bottom, standing is about the relationship between blamer and blamed. it's not fundamentally about the blamer's own moral record—that's downstream of whether the relationship is the kind that licenses holding the other to account.

so that's my six commitments. go through them and tell me which pairs are in tension and how badly. i want them ranked by severity, not just a list. for each tension, tell me what would have to give and what the cheapest fix is. also tell me if any of these apparent tensions actually dissolve when you look closer—i suspect at least one does but i'm not sure which. and please flag how confident you are in each point and which one you're least sure about overall.
