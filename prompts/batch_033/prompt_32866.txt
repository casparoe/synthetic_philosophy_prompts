In the course of your argument, engage seriously with the objection that the students' backward induction tacitly relies on the KK principle (that knowing entails knowing that one knows) and that rejecting KK dissolves the paradox. Address whether your resolution requires retaining KK, rejecting it, or finding some middle ground.
