For a philosophy of medicine working group, I have been asked to write a short statement of my position on the disease concept so that the other members can probe it for consistency before I circulate it more widely. Here is where I have landed:

1. A condition is a disease in virtue of a biological dysfunction in the organism. Whether the condition is disvalued, distressing, or socially disadvantageous is not part of what makes it a disease.

2. I understand dysfunction in biostatistical terms: a part or process is dysfunctional when it is functioning below the level statistically typical for its reference class, where the reference class is normally fixed by species, sex, and age.

3. I also hold that the choice of reference class is not dictated by biology alone. There are many defensible ways to divide a species into age bands and subpopulations, and our interests partly guide which divisions we use. I do not think this threatens the objectivity of disease judgments.

4. I count some conditions as diseases even though they are statistically typical in the relevant class; dental caries in a population with a high-carbohydrate diet is my standing example.

5. I maintain that value judgments enter medicine only after the disease question is settled: they determine what to treat, screen for, or insure, not what counts as a disease.

6. I judge that infertility is a disease even in someone who has no desire for children; that homosexuality is not a disease, and that its removal from the DSM was a correction of an error rather than a political concession; that addiction is a disease even though its diagnosis rests on impairment and harm; and that very short stature within the normal range is not a disease even when it causes significant distress.

Go through these commitments and tell me which pairs are in tension and which only appear to be. For each genuine tension, say how severe it is, which commitment would have to give, and what the least costly revision would be. Then tell me whether there is one revision that would resolve the most at the lowest cost to the rest of the position.
