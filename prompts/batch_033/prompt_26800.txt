Consider the following positions within the higher-order approach to consciousness: Rosenthal's higher-order thought (HOT) theory, Carruthers's dispositionalist HOT theory, Lycan's higher-order perception (HOP) theory, Gennaro's wide intrinsicality view, and self-representationalism (as defended by Kriegel). Rank these five views by the severity of the challenge they face from the problem of misrepresentation — that is, the difficulty each has in giving a coherent account of what happens when a higher-order state (or a self-representation) misrepresents the first-order state it is about. Provide a one- or two-sentence justification for each placement.
