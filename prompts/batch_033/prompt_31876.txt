As a journalist who has spent years covering the transatlantic rift in chemical regulation, I've come to think that the real story isn't the science — it's the philosophy that the science gets plugged into. Consider the contrast. Under the EU's REACH regulation, a manufacturer must demonstrate that a substance is safe before it reaches the market; if the toxicological data are inconclusive, the default is rejection. In the United States, even after the 2016 TSCA reform, the EPA must build an affirmative case that a substance poses an unreasonable risk before restricting it — inconclusive data favor the substance, not the regulator. The same asymmetry appears in GMO approvals, in the treatment of endocrine disruptors, and in the European Commission's invocation of the precautionary principle as a treaty-level commitment. It is easy to call one approach cautious and the other permissive and leave it there, but that move buries the philosophical question: what theory of rational action under uncertainty does the EU-style precautionary regime actually presuppose?

I want to know what has to be true — about the structure of value, about the ethics of false positives versus false negatives, about the normative status of the status quo — for the practice to be internally coherent. Does the regime presuppose maximin reasoning, or is it compatible with expected-utility frameworks provided you assign sufficiently heavy-tailed probability distributions to worst-case outcomes? Does it require treating inaction as a neutral baseline, or can it survive the recognition that delaying a beneficial substance also causes harm? And here is the tension that first drew me to the story: the same EU institutions that apply the precautionary principle to chemical risk have been comparatively willing to license novel financial instruments and deploy untested digital infrastructure with far less pre-market scrutiny. Is that pattern an inconsistency in the practice's own presuppositions, or can the implicit theory distinguish chemical harms from financial and digital ones in a principled way?
