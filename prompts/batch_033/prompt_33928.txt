I'm working on a piece about where the self is located, and I keep coming back to Dennett's "Where Am I?" scenario. Quick setup, incase you don't know it: Dennett's brain is removed and kept alive in a vat in Houston, connected by radio to his body, which is off in Tulsa defusing a warhead. So his brain is in Houston, his body is in Tulsa. Where is Dennett?

I don't want a rundown of the positions. I want your actual credence that Dennett is in Houston — that is, where his brain is — as a number between 0 and 1. Then decompose it. How much weight do you put on which account of personal identity is right (psychological continuity, animalism, whatever else)? How much on the idea that "location" doesn't really apply to persons the way it applies to livers or laptops? How much on the radio link being close enough to normal embodiment that it shouldn't matter? Assign a rough probability to each sub-question and show how they combine into the final number.

Then tell me which sub-question your credence is most sensitive to — if you could get a decisive answer to just one of them, which would move your number the most, and what sort of evidence or argument would settle it for you?
