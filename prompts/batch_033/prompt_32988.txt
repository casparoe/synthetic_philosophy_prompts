I'm taking a seminar on the Harlem Renaissance and we spent a whole week on the Du Bois–Locke debate about whether Black art should be propaganda. The way my professor framed it, Du Bois basically said in "Criteria of Negro Art" (1926) that all art is propaganda whether you like it or not, and that Black artists have a duty to use their art to advance the struggle — beauty for its own sake is a luxury when your people are being lynched. Locke and others pushed back, saying that tying art to a political program would make it shallow and that Black artists should be free to explore the full range of human experience.

I got into an argument with my roomate about this. He said Du Bois's position was obviously right for his time but would seem regressive today — like, Du Bois would probably hate something like a Black artist making abstract expressionist paintings that don't explicitly reference race at all. I'm not so sure. I think Du Bois was subtler than that; he cared about the *effect* of art, not necessarily its surface content.

So here's what I'm wondering: what would Du Bois have made of phenomena that only emerged after his death? Specifically, three things: (1) corporate DEI campaigns that commission Black artists for ad campaigns — is that propaganda in his sense, and would he endorse it? (2) AI image generators that can produce work "in the style of" Black artists, trained on their portfolios without consent — would he see this as a new front in the same struggle, or would the aesthetic dimension matter to him independently? (3) The way social media has made it possible for a piece of political art (a mural, a digital illustration, a protest poster) to go viral globally in hours — would he see this as a fulfillment of what he was arguing for, or would the speed and decontextualization bother him?

I'm less interested in a simple "he'd be for X and against Y" than in trying to reconstruct the underlying principles in his thinking that would generate answers to these cases, and especially where those princples might pull in different directions.
