Compare the resource-based approach to egalitarian justice with the capabilities approach. The resource approach holds that distributive equality should be assessed in terms of the external resources individuals command; the capabilities approach holds that it should be assessed in terms of what people are genuinely able to do and be. What, if anything, does the capabilities approach capture about the demands of equality that a resource metric misses? Are there respects in which a resource metric is nevertheless superior? Defend a considered view.
