She argues the codon-to-amino-acid mapping is arbitrary (in roughly the Saussurean sense), that it involves representation beyond mere causation, and that "genetic code" is no more metaphorical than "Morse code."

If she says no (no machinery to interpret it), then what distinguishes her view from the causal-mechanistic one she's arguing against — doesn't "being interpreted by the right machinery" just collapse into "being causally embedded in the right kind of system"?
