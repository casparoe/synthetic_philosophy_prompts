ok so im in this philosophy club at my high school and we've been going in circles on the wrong kind of reasons thing for like three weeks now — like whether you can have a reason to believe or admire something for reasons that have nothing to do with whether its true or admirable. I'm supposed to write up my "position profile" for next meeting so everyone else can grill me on it, and I'd rather find the holes myself before I walk in there. So here's where I've landed:

1. For belief, I'm an evidentialist: only evidence counts in favor of believing something. The fact that believing p would make me happy, or that someone would pay me, isn't a reason to believe p. At most it's a reason to want to believe p or to take steps to get myself to believe it.

2. But I also think that if an evil demon says he'll torture you unless you admire him, you do have a reason to admire him. It's a wrong-kind reason, sure — it doesn't show he's admirable — but it's still a reason to have the attitude itself, not just a reason to do stuff.

3. I basically accept the counting in favor view: a reason to have an attitude is any consideration that counts in favor of having that attitude. I don't see how you could deny that the demon's threat counts in favor of admiring him.

4. So I dont think there's any conflict between 1 and the pill case: if there's a pill that makes you believe the sky is green, and you'll get paid for taking it, you have a reason to take the pill but no reason to believe the sky is green. A reason to bring about an attitude isn't automatically a reason for the attitude itself.

5. And for the same reason I don't think Pascal's wager gives you a reason to believe in God — at best it gives you a reason to go to church or read apologetics or whatever until maybe you end up believing.

Does this hang together? Go through the pairs and tell me which ones are actually in tension and how bad, which ones only look like they conflict, and what the cheapest fix is. If something has to go, say which one and what it would cost, and tell me which single revision would clean up the most.
