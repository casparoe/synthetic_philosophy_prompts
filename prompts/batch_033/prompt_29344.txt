They can all repeat the slogan that you can't intend to do what you believe you won't do, but it doesn't hit them.

The setup I'm imagining: I offer them some reward they actually want (say, exemption from the next reading response, or a point on the participation grade — no real money allowed by the course) at the end of class if, at that moment, they genuinely intend to do something mildly unpleasant in the next class, like drink a small cup of vinegar or eat a spoon of hot sauce. They know the reward depends only on the intention at the end of class, not on wether they actually do the unpleasant thing next class. And they know themselves well enough to know most of them won't do it when the time comes.

Could you design this properly?
