I'm on a city public art comission, and next week we vote on a grant for a mural by a Black artist about a historically Black neighborhood that's being displaced. The proposal is strong, but the artist says outright in her statement that she wants the mural to work as propaganda in that housing fight — to move viewers toward supporting rent control and opposing a specific development, not just to be a good painting. One faction says public arts money shouldn't fund advocacy, full stop. Another faction keeps citing Du Bois's argument that all art is propaganda, and that Black artists in particular should use their work as propaganda for racial equality, so refusing to fund work that admits this is either naive or just a quiet way of funding a different politics. Draft the short written opinion I'll circulate before the vote: recommend approve, reject, or approve with conditions, with a clear bottom line and an honest treatment of the strongest point on the side you reject. Keep it under 650 words.
