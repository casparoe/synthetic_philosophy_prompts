I've been thinking on my own about what to do when I'm not sure what's morally right, and I've collected my gut reactions on a handful of cases. I'd like to know whether there's a single principle that fits them — or most of them — and what that principle would say about a case I'm stuck on.

Here are the cases and my verdicts:

1. I'm about 70% confident that buying factory-farmed chicken is wrong, and 30% confident it's permissible. The enjoyment I get from eating it is real but modest — it's a preference, not something central to my life. My gut says I should stop buying it.

2. I'm choosing where to donate. I'm 55% confident that Charity A does more good per dollar, but if Charity B is the better one, it does vastly more good — the expected-impact calculation favors B by a wide margin. My gut says give to B.

3. A friend asks whether I like their new haircut, which I think looks terrible. I'm 80% confident that a small lie to spare their feelings is fine, and 20% confident that any deliberate deception is seriously wrong — a failure of respect. My gut says I should tell the truth, or at least not lie.

4. I'm 60% confident that having children is morally permissible and 40% confident it's wrong (because of the suffering the child will inevitably experience). My gut says it's still permissible to have children.

5. I'm 95% confident I should keep a promise to meet a friend for coffee, but there's a 5% chance I should break it to help a stranger who seems distressed and needs directions. My gut says keep the promise.

I notice that in case 2 I seem to be maximizing expected value, but in case 3 a 20% risk of serious wrongness sways me completely, even though an expected-value approach might not. And in case 1 I'm deferring to the possibility of wrongness, but in case 4 I'm not, even though the stakes in case 4 are far higher. I can't tell whether I'm inconsistent or whether there's a principle that makes sense of this pattern. Is there one? And what would it say about a case I keep going back and forth on: I'm 50% confident that eating animals is wrong and 50% confident it's permissible — what should I do?
