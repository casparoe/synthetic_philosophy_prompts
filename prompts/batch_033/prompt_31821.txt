"Okay so the dogmatism paradox. So the paradox shows that fallibilism — the view that you can know things without absolute certainty — is self-undermining. Because fallibilism says you should always be open to new evidence, but the paradox proves that once you know something, you can't be. Kripke basically used this to argue that knowledge is impossible unless you're a Cartesian infallibilist, which nobody wants to be."
