Write a villanelle about presentism vs. eternalism in the philosophy of time. The two refrains should be the presentist's and the eternalist's bottom-line claims—something like "Only the present is" and "All times exist alike." The form should do the real philosophical work: the way each refrain keeps returning no matter how many times the other one seems to have the last word should mirror how neither position can fully shake the other's intuitive pull. When they finally land together in the closing couplet, it shouldn't resolve the tension but show why the debate is so stubborn. Someone who knows the philosophy of time should recognize the positions without them being named, and the physics (the relativity of simultaneity, the block universe) should hover in the background without being lectured about.
