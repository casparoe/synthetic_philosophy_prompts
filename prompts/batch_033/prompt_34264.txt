I run a small research programme — about five postdocs and a handful of PhD students — focused on the normative design of AI reasoning. We are past the point of asking whether AI systems should be value-aligned at all; we are trying to settle what kind of moral reasoning an AI should employ when it faces genuinely novel ethical decisions. The debate has largely organised itself around a familiar dichotomy: should AI systems reason as utilitarians (maximising expected welfare under uncertainty) or as deontologists (respecting constraints, rights, or side-constraints that are not reducible to welfare trade-offs)? But that framing is still too coarse to guide actual engineering or training decisions, and I suspect some of the most important open questions are downstream of it, or cut across it, rather than being settled by picking a side.

I am putting together a three-year research agenda for the group, and I need to decide which open questions in this vicinity are most worth our time. I am not looking for a survey of the literature or a reading list. I want a defended prioritisation: which questions should we tackle first, and why? For each question you rank, I want to know how upstream it is — does answering it unlock or reshape other questions? — how tractable it is for a group with our mix of philosophical and technical competence (we can do formal modelling and some experimental work, but we are not a machine learning lab), and what kind of result over the next couple of years would make you raise or lower its priority. I am also interested in which questions you would deprioritise, either because they are less consequential than they first appear or because they are intractable with current tools. Please take definite positions; I can read a balanced literature review on my own, but I need someone to actually commit to a ranking and defend it.

After your prose, please give me a structured summary as a JSON array of objects, one per ranked question, with the following fields: `rank` (integer, 1 being highest priority), `question` (a one-sentence statement of the open question), `importance_rationale` (one to two sentences on why it is upstream of other work or practically consequential), `tractability` (one of `"high"`, `"medium"`, or `"low"`, given our group's philosophical and technical mix), and `revision_trigger` (one sentence on what we might learn in the next two years that would substantially change this question's priority).
