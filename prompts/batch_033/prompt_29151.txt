Since reductio arguments are ubiquitous in mathematics — from Euclid's proof of the infinitude of primes to Cantor's diagonal argument — this would mean paraconsistent logic can't reconstruct huge swaths of mathematical reasoning. That seems like a devastating cost. Am I missing something, or is paraconsistent logic genuinely incompatible with standard mathematical practice?
