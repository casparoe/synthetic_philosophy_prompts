I was trying to figure out whether "better than" is transitive — you know, if A is better than B and B is better than C, then A has to be better than C. Seems obvious, right? But I started playing with this thought experiment and now I think I've proved something impossible, and I cant find the exit.

Start with Life A: one year of the worst torture imagineable, then you die. Now compare it to Life B: two years of torture that's just barely, barely less intense — like, one fewer nerve ending firing. Surely B is better than A, right? Double the duration but the intensity drops by a amount so tiny you couldn't even notice it. That feels like a net improvement. Now Life C: four years of torture that's another barely-noticeable notch less intense. Better than B. Keep going. At each step you double the duration and dial the intensity down by an indiscernable amount. After a few hundred steps you've got a life of mild headaches lasting for billions of years. Now here's the thing: is that last life really better than Life A? I look at Life A — one year of pure agony — and I look at Life Z — billions of years of a mild headache — and I want to say Life A is better. A mild headache for billions of years is a unfathomable amount of suffering. It adds up. But the chain says Z is better than A, because each step was an improvement.

So I've got: (1) At each step, the next life is better than the previous one. (2) "Better than" is transitive. (3) Therefore Z is better than A. (4) But Z is not better than A — A is better than Z. And (3) and (4) contradict each other.

I can't tell which premise to give up. If I give up (1) at some step, I have to say there's a pair of lives where making the torture slightly less intense and doubling the duration makes things worse, and that seems crazy — at some specific micro-adjustment, the value just flips? That's a sorites problem. If I give up (2), then I lose transitivity and I don't know what "better than" even means anymore — can I ever compare three things? If I give up (4), then I have to accept that billions of years of headaches really is better than one year of torture, and that also seems crazy.

What's the right move here? And what do I have to pay for it?
