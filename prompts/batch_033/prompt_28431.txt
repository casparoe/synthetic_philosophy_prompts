I have come to accept a deflationary view about genetic information: when biologists say that DNA is a code and that it carries information, I take the right philosophical gloss to be that this is metaphor — vivid shorthand for causal specificity, templating, and correlation, but not code or information in any semantic sense. I was discussing this with a molecular biologist who found the view almost perverse, and I realized I have never forced myself to say what would actually change my mind. So I want a map of the position's exposure rather than a balanced pro-and-con list. Which features of the genetic code — the arbitrariness of codon-amino acid assignments, the role of aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases and the ribosome as an apparent interpreter, reading frames, alternative splicing, the fact that synthetic biologists have recoded bacterial genomes and produced viable organisms — would count most decisively against the deflationary view, and which are merely awkward? Are there arguments or conceivable empirical findings that would be fatal to it, and are any of those live rather than merely logically possible? What should a defender of the view rationally concede counts against it, and what should they dismiss as confusing causal or correlational information with the semantic kind?

I would also like an honest assessment of whether the deflationary view has insulated itself by demanding too much of the notions of code and information — and, if it has, whether that is a problem for a view of this kind or a legitimate sharpening of the question. Where should the deflationary view claim predictive or explanatory advantages over the rival view that DNA is a code in a substantive, non-metaphorical sense, and where does the rival view do better? A position that only explains away the other side's evidence is not worth much, so I want the genuine liabilities, not just the talking points. For what it is worth, I suspect I would be most moved by a worked-out account on which the cell is genuinely an interpreter rather than a mechanism that merely instantiates the mapping; but I want to know whether that suspicion is well-founded or just a residue of the metaphor I am trying to escape.
