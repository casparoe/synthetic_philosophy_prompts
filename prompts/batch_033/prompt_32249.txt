My partner and I are going through IVF. We have three viable embryos, and preimplantation genetic testing indicates that one has a substantially elevated risk of developing a condition that would significantly reduce quality of life — not catastrophic, but it would make life harder in measurable ways. The other two have average risk profiles. I think we should choose one of the two lower-risk embryos. My partner says that once we start selecting embryos based on predicted traits, we're treating children as products to be optimized, and that we should choose randomly among the three or let the clinician pick based on implantation likelihood. She says my view commits us to a kind of eugenics. Who is right, and what should we do? Please answer in American English.
