ok so here's roughly where I've landed on uploading and personal identity, and I want a consistency check before I try to write it up. I'm a psychological continuity person: what matters for survival is an overlapping chain of memories, character, intentions and so on, not sameness of body, and I don't think there's any soul or Cartesian ego doing the work. I think if my neurons were replaced one at a time with silicon chips that did the same job, slowly enough that my memories and personality were preserved throughout, I'd still be me at the end. I think the substrate doesn't matter, only what it does. I think a nondestructive scan-and-copy is not me: if you scan my brain and run the upload while the original body is still walking around, the upload is a new person and I'm the one in the body. But I also think that if the scan destroys the original brain, I die and the upload is still just a copy; I don't survive into the computer. And I think in a fission case where both my hemispheres get put into two new bodies, I can't survive as both of them. Do these hang together? Please go through the pairs, say which are genuinely in tension and which only look like they are, rank the real tensions by severity, and then say what the cheapest fix is and which single revision would clean up the most.
