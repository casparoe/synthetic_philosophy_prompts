(2) Priya said sure, but a whole brain made of chips isn't the same thing as a brain made of neurons, so somewhere along the way the original person is gone and the end result is just a copy.

(7) DeShawn said it's not the same because the slow version never interrupts your stream of thought, so your consciousness keeps going and the resulting person has that stream from the inside.
(8) Priya said if someone made a second copy of you while you were still alive you'd never say both copies are you, so "same from the inside" isn't enough; there has to be one persisting body/brain.
