Write a sonnet about the dollar auction — the game where a dollar bill is auctioned and the second-highest bidder also has to pay thier bid, but wins nothing — with the volta at the moment the bidders' logic flips from "the dollar is worth bidding on" to "I've already put in too much to stop." The octave should set up the auctioneer's trap and the first few innocent bids; the sestet should show the escalation feeding on itself, each bid becoming a way of not losing what the previous bid already cost. The closing couplet should say what actually pushes the price past a dollar (its not that anyone has started to think the dollar is worth more than a dollar). Make it accurate enough that someone who teaches the dollar auction would recognize the mechanism and not just the name.
