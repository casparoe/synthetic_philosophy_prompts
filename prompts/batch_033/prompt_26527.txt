My sister was in a car accident last month. The doctors have declared her brain dead — her entire brain, including the brainstem, shows no activity on two separate EEGs and there is no intracranial blood flow. I am caught between respecting my parents' view and accepting what the medical team tells me. I need to think through what it even means to be dead — not just legally, but philosophically. Is there a genuine philosophical case for the circulatory-respiratory criterion over the neurological criterion, or is my parents' position ultimately just an understandable but mistaken refusal to accept the facts? I want to know how confident I should be in whichever answer, and what the weakest point in that answer is.
