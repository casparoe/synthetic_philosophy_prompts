My opening move: your usual argument seems to assume that for death to harm me, there has to be some time when I'm alive to suffer it. But I hold the deprivation account: death harms me because of the goods it stops me from having, even though there's no moment at which I experience that harm. My whole life is worse for being shorter. Why isn't that enough for death to be bad for me?
