I've been trying to wrap my head around tangled hierarchies — you know, Escher's Drawing Hands, Hofstadter's strange loops, all that. The claim seems to be that a system can be perfectly coherent even if it has no "inviolate level," no bedrock that grounds everything else without itself being grounded. But this just sounds like circular reasoning dressed up in fancy language. If A depends on B and B depends on A, you haven't explained either one. In any legitimate structure of explanation or justification, don't you eventually need to hit something that isn't defined in terms of the very thing it's supposed to be grounding? So what am I missing — what makes a tangled hierarchy different from a plain old vicious circle?
