The F-twist—the idea that unrealistic assumptions don't matter as long as the predictions are good—that really hit a nerve for me, because honestly, the models I saw up close were full of paramaterizations that everyone knew were wrong at the micro level. We'd put in something we knew was physically false just to get the large-scale behavior right. And it worked, mostly.
