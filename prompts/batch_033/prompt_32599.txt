Nobody stopped to say what either slogan actually claims, and I left less sure then when I walked in.

Can you take the slogan "intelligence and goals are orthogonal" and lay out the distinct precise claims it could be making? I want the menu of readings, from the boringly true to the probably false, with the interesting ones in the middle. Then do the same for "sufficiently intelligent agents converge on the same instrumental goals," but only the readings that bear on the first slogan. Specifically I want to know which readings of the two slogans are compatible with each other and which ones genuinely conflict, because in the meeting they were treated as opposing each other and I suspect their mostly not about the same thing.

Also, please keep straight what's settled and what's still contested among people who actually work on this. I don't want a blog-consensus version that papers over the real disagreements.

After the prose, give me a compact structured summary as JSON. Schema: an object with two keys, "orthogonality_readings" and "convergence_readings".
