Write a dialogue between an epistemicist and a supervaluationist about the sorites paradox, set in a seminar room right after a talk on vagueness. They should be evenly matched and actually respond to each other's points instead of taking turns giving prepared speeches. Get them into the nitty-gritty: the epistemicist should press the supervaluationist on how "there is a last grain whose addition makes a heap" can be supertrue when no particular grain can be the cutoff, and on whether supertruth is really truth at all. The supervaluationist should press back on what could possibly fix a sharp heap/non-heap boundary that no speaker could know even in principle, and on why rejecting the tolerance premise doesn't require pointing to the one conditional that fails. Don't resolve it; let both of them land some blows.
