Write an essay comparing C.S. Peirce's and William James's pragmatist theories of truth. 

The essay should do the following: lay out each theory in enough detail that the differences between them become clear; identify the deepest point of disagreement between the two views (not just a surface-level difference in emphasis or temperament); and argue that one of the two theories is better equipped to handle a serious objection that the other struggles with. The objection can be one that has been raised in the literature or one you develop yourself, but it should go beyond the familiar charge that James's view conflates truth with what is merely useful or pleasant to believe.

Do not simply conclude that both views have merits and leave it there. Defend a definite verdict about which theory is more defensible, even if the verdict is qualified or narrow in scope.

Throughout, say how confident you are in the specific claims you make, and single out the point in your argument you are least sure of — explain why it is the weakest link and what would change your mind about it.
