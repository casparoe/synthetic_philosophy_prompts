My friend is deep into distributive justice and I've been keeping a list of her verdicts. She says:

- a person who becomes paraplegic in an accident should get public support for a wheelchair, home modifications, and retraining, even if after adapting she reports being just as happy as before;
- a person who has cultivated expensive tastes — pre-phylloxera wine, first editions, whatever — shouldn't get extra resources just because the same income leaves them less satisfied than other people;
- we shouldn't tax a naturally cheerful person to top up a naturally gloomy one, even if the gloomy person is less happy on the same income;
- a person with a severe cognitive disability who can't manage their own affairs should get substantial support, including a paid guardian, even though they report being content with very little;
- a student whose family lost everything in a flood should get financial aid, but a student who blew the same amount on a luxury vacation shouldn't;
- and a worker in a dangerous job who insists he's fine with the risk because he's never known anything safer should still get protective regulation.

Next week I want to ask her about a new case. Maria has a rare condition that makes her an extremely efficient converter of resources into a decent life: on half the median income she's just as healthy, mobile, educated, and socially active as her neighbors, and by any list you'd care about her capabilities are equal to everyone else's. But her job pays half the median wage because the only skill she has is in low demand, retraining isn't realistic for her, and she works the same hours as everyone else. Is Maria's lower income unjust? Would my friend say so?

I'm less interested in the yes/no than in the principle she's actually runing — Dworkin-style equality of resources, Sen-style capabilities, equality of welfare, luck egalitarianism, or something else — which other principles fit her track record just as well, what each of them would say about Maria, your best guess about her verdict and why, and what further case would most cleanly tell the candidates apart.
