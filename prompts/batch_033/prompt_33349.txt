This is a formalisation of Hume's problem of induction. Hume argued that we cannot justify the claim that the future will resemble the past without circularity: any such justification would itself rely on induction. The NFL theorems make the same point in mathematical terms: they show that without making assumptions about the structure of the problem, no inductive strategy outperforms any other. Because the theorems prove that induction is impossible without prior assumptions, they vindicate Hume's scepticism. If even a mathematical proof shows that induction cannot work in general, then Hume was right that there is no rational justification for our inductive practices. The practical upshot is that machine learning only works because we design algorithms that are biased toward the kinds of problems we actually encounter — but this bias is itself an inductive assumption, so the circularity Hume identified remains at the heart of the enterprise.'
