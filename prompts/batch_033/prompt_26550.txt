ok so I'm trying to get my head straight on Molinism before I commit to a dissertation chapter on it, and I'd like someone to stress-test my commitments. Here's what I think I hold:

(i) Molinism is the right account of divine foreknowledge. God has middle knowledge — knowledge of what every free creature would freely do in any possible circumstance — and this knowledge is logically prior to God's creative decree.

(ii) I'm a truthmaker maximalist: for every true proposition, there is something in reality that makes it true.

(iii) Prior to God's creative decree, the only concrete existent is God himself. So whatever makes contingent truths about creatures true can't be the creatures, since they don't exist yet.

(iv) Counterfactuals of creaturely freedom (CCFs) are contingent truths. "If Peter were in circumstance C, he would freely deny Christ" is true, but it could have been false. It's not a necessary truth.

(v) I think the grounding objection — the charge that Molinism can't explain what makes CCFs true — is overblown. I'm not sure which response is right, but I'm confident the problem is soluble.

(vi) I hold a robust libertarian account of free will: a choice is free only if (a) the agent could have done otherwise in the exact same circumstances, and (b) nothing outside the agent causally determines the choice.

(vii) God's decision to actualize one feasible world rather than another, on the basis of his middle knowledge, does not threaten human freedom. God doesn't cause the choices; he just knows what agents would freely do and arranges the circumstances.

(viii) The "bring about" objection fails. The fact that God can guarantee that Peter denies Christ by placing Peter in circumstances where God knows Peter would freely deny Christ — this doesn't amount to God causing the denial.

I'm too close to this to see where the problems are. Go through these and tell me which pairs or clusters are genuinely in tension, how severe each tension is, and which ones are only apparent tensions that dissolve on closer inspection. Rank them by severity. And if there are multiple real tensions, tell me which single revision would clean up the most.
