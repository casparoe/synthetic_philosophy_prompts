The proposal should (a) make this diagnosis precise, (b) formulate a research question that is open and tractable for a two-year project, (c) describe a method for answering it (purely formal, experimental, or some combination), and (d) specify what a successful outcome would look like and what findings would indicate that the approach should be abandoned.
