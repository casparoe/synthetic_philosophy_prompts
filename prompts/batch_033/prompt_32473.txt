In the color phi experiment, a red dot and a green dot are flashed in sequence, and subjects report seeing a single moving dot that changes color partway along its path. The blog says Dennett takes this to show that the brain edits memory after the fact: the subject really experienced two stationary flashes, but the memory was rewritten before the report into a single moving dot. The blog then says Dennett argues that the Orwellian account is the correct one, since there is no place in the brain where a pre-conscious experience could be staged, and no one could notice their own memory being edited.
