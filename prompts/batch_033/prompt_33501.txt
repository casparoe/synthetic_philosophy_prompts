I spent my career in theoretical physics and have recently turned to philosophy, with a particular focus on metaethics. I have been working through the literature on idealized deliberation and indirect normativity—roughly, the idea that what I ought to do is fixed by what I would want myself to do under suitably idealized conditions. I have written down the set of commitments I seem to have settled on, and I would like an audit of whether they hang together or whether I am papering over tensions I have not noticed.

Here they are:

(a) Normative facts are objective and stance-independent. There is a fact of the matter about what one ought to do, and it does not depend on the attitudes of any actual or hypothetical agent.

(b) The correct procedure for accessing normative facts is to ask what I would endorse after a process of full idealized deliberation: complete factual information, unlimited instrumental rationality, vivid appreciation of all relevant considerations, and freedom from bias and self-deception.

(c) Idealization is not itself a normatively neutral notion. The move from my actual self to my idealized self is guided by norms about what counts as an improvement—norms that cannot themselves be supplied by the idealized deliberation they make possible, on pain of circularity.

(d) If idealized deliberation yields different stable endpoints depending on the order or manner in which one idealizes, then there is no unique fact of the matter about what I ought to do. The procedure underdetermines the answer, and there is nothing else to close the gap.

(e) The normative domain is complete: for any action and any agent, there is a determinate fact about whether the action is permissible, required, or forbidden.

(f) The concept of "what I would converge on if I were to deliberate under ideal conditions" is perfectly coherent even though I am not in fact in those conditions, in the same way that "what this integral evaluates to" is a well-defined notion even before the integral is computed.

I can feel that some of these are pulling against each other, but I find it hard to say which ones and how badly. I would be grateful for a systematic audit: which pairs are genuinely in tension, which only appear to be, and how you would rank the tensions by severity. For each genuine tension, I would also like to know what the cheapest revision would be to resolve it.
