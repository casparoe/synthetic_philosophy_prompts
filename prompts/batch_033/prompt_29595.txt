I was arguing with my friend Marco for like three hours last night about free will, and he kept bringing up this stupid wasp — the Sphex, the one Dennett talks about. You know the deal: the wasp does this whole elaborate routine of dragging a paralyzed cricket to its burrow, going inside to inspect the burrow, coming back out, dragging the cricket a bit further — and if some jerk experimenter moves the cricket a few inches while the wasp is inside, the wasp just redoes the inspection step from scratch, and you can loop it indefinitely. Marco's point was that we're basically the same thing with more steps and a bigger brain, and the feeling of choosing is just the inner glow of a fancier algorithm that's equally rigid if you zoom out far enough. I pushed back, but honestly I couldnt give him a clean account of what the difference actually is, or even what a difference would have to look like.
