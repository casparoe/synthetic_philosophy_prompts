ok so i've been in this philosophy of mind reading group for a few months and i'm trying to sort out whether my views actually hang together before i write the paper i'm supposed to present. here's where i've landed:

(1) i'm a physicalist — consciousness is wholly physical, there's no extra non-physical ingredient
(2) zombies are conceivable: i can imagine a molecule-for-molecule duplicate of me with no phenomenal consciousness at all, just the lights off
(3) conceivability is a fallible but genuine guide to metaphysical possibility — if something is ideally conceivable, that's at least prima facie reason to think it's possible
(4) phenomenal concepts are special — they're recognitional or demonstrative concepts that pick out experiences directly rather than via functional descriptions — and this is what explains both the explanatory gap and why zombies feel conceivable even if they turn out not to be possible
(5) i think the mary thought experiment (the knowledge argument) shows something real: when mary sees red for the first time, she learns something genuinely new, she's not just gaining an ability or a new way of representing old facts
(6) there's nothing it is like to be a thermostat, and more generally you need the right kind of physical organization for consciousness — it's not just information processing or whatever

i know the obvious problem is that (2) plus (3) plus (1) is the classic mess, and (4) is the standard physicalist move to block the inference from conceivability to possibility. but i'm actualy more worried that (4) and (5) might be in tension — like, if phenomenal concepts are recognitional and that's what explains the gap, doesn't that undercut the force of the mary argument? or maybe (5) secretly commits me to something stronger than (1) allows, and (4) is papering over a crack i should be taking seriously. also not sure if (4) itself ends up undermining (2) — if phenomenal concepts explain why zombies seem conceivable, does that make the conceivability somehow less genuine, and does that matter for (3)?

basically can i keep all six, and if not, which are the real tensions and which ones just look like tensions? rank them by severity and tell me what the cheapest fix is.
