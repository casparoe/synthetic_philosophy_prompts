I've got a mate who runs a weekly poker game, and he's offered me a standing side bet. Each Friday I can stake any fraction of whatever I've currently got set aside for this (and any winnings just get added to that pot) on a single toss of a biased coin. The coin comes up heads 60% of the time, and if it's heads I get my stake back plus £1.50 for every £1 I put in; if it's tails I loose my stake. Each individual toss is clearly good in expectation. One friend says I should bet as much as I can every week, since the expected value is positive and over the long run it'll average out. Another friend says that's mad: a bad run of tails will eventually wipe me out if I bet too heavily, so the only sensible policy is to bet nothing at all. I'm fairly sure both of them are missing something, but I can't put my finger on it. On top of that, I'm only taking my mate's word that the coin is 60% heads — I've seen maybe twenty flips, so it could easily be a bit less. What's the right way to think about how much to stake each week, and is there a principled answer rather than just a hunch?
