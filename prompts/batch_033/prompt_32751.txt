ok so i'm writing a paper on nietzsche and i want to open it with a poem — a villanelle about the eternal recurrence, specifically the version from the gay science 341 where the demon comes to you in your loneliest loneliness. the two refrains should be the two possible answers to the demon: one line that speaks from the horror (the response where you would throw yourself down and curse the demon — this life again, every humiliation, every grief, exactly the same, forever), and one that speaks from the affirmation (the response where you call the thought divine and say yes to it — if this was my life then i choose it again, every last detail). the villanelle form does the work here: the refrains keep returning, they alternate, and then in the final couplet they're forced to sit side by side — which is the existential moment itself. the poem should be accurate enough that someone who knows their nietzsche recognises the thought in the details, and good enough as a poem that someone who's never heard of the idea can feel its weight. don't just use "eternal" or "recurrence" as a crutch — let the structure carry the idea.
