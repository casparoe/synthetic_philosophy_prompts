I've been trying to pin down a pattern I keep running into, and I'd like to know whether it has an established name. People will say that some theory is the best explanation of the evidence, and then, without noticing, they slide into treating it as the most probable theory. But when you press them on what made it best, the things they point to are simplicity, elegance, unity, or how satisfying the story is — not anything that obviously bears on how likely it is. Meanwhile a clunky, patched-together alternative that fits the data at least as well gets dismissed for being ugly, and that dismissal is treated as if it had lowered the clunky theory's probability rather then just its appeal. I've seen the same slide in arguments about conspiracy theories, in seminars, and in pop-science books. I know there's a whole literature on inference to the best explanation, but I'm after the specific carving: is there a name for the two senses of best explanation, or for the slide between them? I don't want just a label — I want to know whether this is a genuine ambiguity, where the boundary between the senses is, and whether anything licenses moving from one sense to the other. And please answer in American English.
