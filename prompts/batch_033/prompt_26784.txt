(2) so strict conditionalization is the wrong model for real learning, and the right rule is Jeffrey conditionalization: my new credence in H is the average of my old credence in H given E and my old credence in H given not-E, weighted by my new probabilities for E and not-E, with those conditional credences held fixed through the update.

(5) I think any rational change in my credences can be represented, at least in principle, as the result of some Jeffrey update.

(6) and I think the input to a Jeffrey update still has to be answerable to the evidence. it can't just be a free choice.

Do these hang together? I want an audit, not a lecture.
