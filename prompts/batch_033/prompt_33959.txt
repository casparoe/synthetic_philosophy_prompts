I have no academic background in this area, but I have spent a long time trying to think through one question on my own: if a person wants the food they buy to cause as little animal suffering as possible, how should they weigh beef against chicken against plant foods grown by ordinary commercial farming? The advice I usually encounter says to prefer plants to animals and, if one does eat meat, to prefer beef to chicken because a single cow yields far more food than a single chicken. But when I try to hold all the relevant harms in view at once, they seem to point in different directions. Beef kills one large animal for a great deal of food, but that animal is long-lived and probably capable of intense suffering. Chicken kills many more animals for the same amount of food, and their lives are typically worse while they last. Plant foods avoid farmed animals altogether, but growing and harvesting them kills field animals such as mice, voles, and insects, and the land, water, and chemicals involved affect wild animals in ways that are hard to trace.

I would like you to rank the following six considerations by how much weight they should carry in this comparison, from the one that should matter most to the one that should matter least. Give a sentence or two for each explaining why it belongs where it does.

1. The number of individual animals killed.
2. The length of each animal's life before death.
3. The intensity of suffering each kind of animal is capable of experiencing.
4. The conditions in which the animals live before they die.
5. The deaths of field animals during the growing and harvesting of crops, including the feed crops grown for farmed animals.
6. The effect of each food system on wild animals more broadly, through the land it uses and the water and climate it changes, and whether it tends to create more or fewer wild animal lives and better or worse ones.

I am less interested in which food comes out ahead than in how you order and weigh the considerations themselves, so please make the reasoning for each placement explicit.
