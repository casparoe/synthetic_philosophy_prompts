Before I read another attempt to define honesty or deception for AI systems, I want the full spec first. Write the desiderata for an adequate account of deception (and honesty) for AI systems: the cases it has to get right, the distinctions it can't collapse, and the constraints it can't just wave away, with the reason each one is a requirement. I keep seeing people build a whole defintion around one example — a model that lies to get past a CAPTCHA, a sycophantic assistant, a hallucinating chatbot — and then the definition is useless for the other two. So I want the requirements, not the theory.

Make sure the list handles at least these: a model that says something false because it's just wrong; a model that says something false because it was finetuned to tell users what they want to hear; a model that tells the truth now so it can be trusted later when it lies; a system that withholds information rather than saying anything false; a recommender system that shows thumbnails which aren't technically false but predictably leave people with a false belief; and a system that accurately reports its own capabilities but was deliberately built to manipulate users in some other way. For each, say what a definition would have to do to sort it correctly.

Also mark the distinctions the account can't collapse at its peril: deceiving vs merely causing a false belief, lying vs misleading, being wrong vs being deceptive, omission vs commission, the designer's intent vs the system's own goal, whether honesty is a property of the utterance or of the system's aim, and whether deception has to benefit the system or its operator to count. Then flag which desiderata are non-negotiable and which are just one camp's preference, and say which pairs look like they can't be jointly satisfied — I suspect any account that requires an internal intention to deceive and any account that has to be checkable from the outside are on a collision course, but tell me if that's real or just sloppy phrasing. Finally, say which single requirement would knock out the most off-the-shelf definitions. Skip the full scorecard; I mainly want the list and the tensions.
