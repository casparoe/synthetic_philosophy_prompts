In RL, the learning signal is a single scalar reward, usually hand-designed by the engineer to encode the task; the agent is trained to maximize expected discounted cumulative reward; and both papers and AI-welfare discussions slide between saying the agent maximizes reward and saying reward is whats good or bad for the agent. Or is the practice actually compatible with several different theories of valence, with people quietly smuggling in more than the engineering requires? And if we dropped the implicit theory, what specifically would have to change in how such agents are designed, described, or evaluated?
