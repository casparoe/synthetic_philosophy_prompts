Because special relativity tells us there's no privileged global "now" (simultaneity is frame-dependent), the argument goes, we should conclude that the whole reel is equally real: past, present, and future events all exist in just the same way, and the passage of time is a kind of subjective illusion. Finally, tell me whether the eternalism conclusion survives the audit, whether the analogy was smuggling it in all along, and whether there's a better analogy that does the job more honestly.
