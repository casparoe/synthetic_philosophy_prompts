So I've been pair programming with this new hire at work, and I keep running into this weird thing that I don't have a word for. I'll be debugging something, and he'll ask me "how did you know to look there?" and I honestly can't tell him. I just... knew. But it's not like a guess — I'm right most of the time. The thing is, when I actually try to write down my debugging process or explain it step by step, what I write down is wrong. Like, I'll say "first I check the logs, then I look at the most recent commit, then I..." but if you watch me actually debug, I don't do any of that in that order, and half the time I skip steps I said I always do. And here's the kicker: when I try to follow my own written-down process, I get slower and worse at it. It's like the act of turning it into rules breaks the thing.

I started paying attention to this and I see it everywhere now. Senior devs on my team will give architecture advice that's consistently good, but if you ask them to write down their principles or heuristics, what comes out is vague stuff like "keep it simple" or "don't over-engineer" — which is not actually what they're doing when they make specific calls. And they don't seem to notice the mismatch. I've even tried recording myself (screen capture with narration) and then comparing what I said I was doing to what I actually did, and it's embarassing how far off it is.

I know this is probably some cognitive science or philosophy thing — like there's knowledge that you can't put into words, or knowledge that only exists in the doing. But I'm not sure if what I'm describing is exactly that, or if it's a more specific phenomenon. Because it's not just that I can't articulate it; it's that my attempts at articulation are systematically wrong in a way I don't notice until someone (or a recording) shows me what I actually do. And the wrongness isn't random — the explicit rules I come up with tend to be more linear, more step-by-step, more like a recipe, when the real thing is messier and more pattern-matchy. Is there a name for this specific thing — the gap between your explicit theory of your own process and your actual behavior, where the explicit theory is not just incomplete but actively misleading? And if there isn't a single name, what are the nearest concepts and how does this differ from them?
