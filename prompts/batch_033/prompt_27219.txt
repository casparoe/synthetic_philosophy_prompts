Okay so I just spent the whole evening arguing with my friend about a weird lizard population — parthenogenetic, no males, and it hybridized with another lineage at some point in the past — and whether it should count as one species or two. The frustrating thing is we agreed on literally every fact about the lizards: how they reproduce, the gene flow history, the morphology, all of it. But she kept saying species are reproductively isolated gene pools, so the asexual lineage obviously fails or maybe isn't even a species at all, and I kept saying no, species are segments of lineages and the relevant thing is ancestry and diagnosability, and neither of us moved. Then we dragged in ring species and bacteria with horizontal gene transfer and it got worse, not better: more facts, same disagreement.

I know the species problem has been going on in biology and philosophy for decades and its not like anyone is missing data at this point. So here's what I actually want: a diagnosis of why this debate is so stuck. Is it substantive — are people disagreeing about how the living world is actually organized? Is it verbal — are they just using "species" for different things and talking past each other? Or are they answering different questions under one label: what keeps populations cohesive now, what units evolve over time, what makes a usable taxonomic category, what should conservation law protect? My working theory is that the debate is really about what the word "species" is for, not about any hidden biological fact, and that the factual-sounding language is what makes it look intractable. But I'm not sure.

And given whatever diagnosis you settle on, tell me whether anything could count as progress. A distinction people haven't made sharply enough, a case that would force one side to move, a formal or experimental result — or should we just accept pluralism and stop expecting the word to pick out one thing? I want a real diagnosis with reasons, not a survey of species concepts, and an honest answer about whether progress is possible.
