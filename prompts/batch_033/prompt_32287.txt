Since retiring from a career in the physical sciences, I have been reading the recent philosophical literature on higher-order evidence, and I have found myself attracted to a cluster of views whose mutual consistency I cannot fully assess on my own. I would like an audit of the package rather than a survey of the field. Here are five commitments I find individually plausible:

(1) Rational confidence is governed by total evidence: one should proportion belief to all the evidence one has, not to any salient subset of it.

(2) Evidence about my own cognitive condition is genuine evidence. If I learn that a judgment was formed while I was sleep-deprived, under severe time pressure, or in circumstances likely to engage a known bias, I should lower confidence in that judgment unless independent considerations restore it.

(3) Acknowledged peer disagreement is a central case of such higher-order evidence. If someone I regard as my epistemic peer — roughly my equal in ability and in familiarity with the relevant evidence — reaches a contrary conclusion, I should move substantially in her direction.

(4) Epistemic akrasia is always irrational. I should never hold a belief while believing that my total evidence does not support it; and if I believe that my total evidence supports p, I am rationally required to be confident in p.

(5) There is no external standpoint from which to audit my own reasoning. Once I have evidence that my reasoning about p may be unreliable, I cannot restore my original confidence simply by re-examining the first-order evidence and finding my reasoning compelling, because that re-examination is conducted by the very faculty whose reliability has been called into question.

I suspect a set like this is likely to contain stresses, but I do not trust my own sense of where they are. Please treat the five commitments as a package and tell me whether they are jointly tenable. For each pair that is in genuine tension, say how severe the tension is and what would have to give. Also identify any apparent tensions that dissolve under careful statement. Finally, tell me which single revision would do the most to make the package coherent, and what that revision would cost.
