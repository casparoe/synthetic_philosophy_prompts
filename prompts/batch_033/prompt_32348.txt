Then flag any pair of desiderata that you suspect cannot be jointly satisfied — that is the part I most want to see. Finally, sketch very briefly how the biological species concept, the phylogenetic species concept, and one version of pluralism fare against the list, but keep the weight on the criteria themselves rather than on the scoring. Please write in British English, and in continuous prose with no headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables — the way you would write a letter or an essay.
