I've seen negative utilitarianism described as the view that reducing suffering is morally more important than promoting happiness, and that no amount of happiness can compensate for certain extreme suffering. If that is the view, doesn't it simply follow that the best possible world would be one with no sentient beings at all, since such a world would contain no suffering? And if it does follow, why isn't the view rejected immediately as a reductio rather than treated as a serious contender?
