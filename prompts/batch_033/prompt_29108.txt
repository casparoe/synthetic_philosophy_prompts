Kavka's toxin puzzle has always seemed overblown to me. The billionaire pays you for intending to drink the toxin, not for actually drinking it, so why not just form the intention at midnight, collect the million in the morning, and then not drink? I don't see why this is supposed to be hard: intentions are the kind of thing you can just adopt, like deciding to start a diet. And if you change your mind afterwards, so what — the money was for the intention, not the act. What exactly is the puzzle suppose to be?
