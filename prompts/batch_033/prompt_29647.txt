I'm a software engineer with no formal background in philosophy, and I'm working on a domain ontology for a product. We encode two kinds of edges in our knowledge graph: definitional edges that are supposed to hold in virtue of the meanings of the terms (for example, that a signed contract is a kind of agreement) and empirical edges that are supposed to hold in virtue of how the world happens to be (for example, that most contracts in our corpus are signed electronically). I want to know which parts of that critique should actually change how we build the ontology, and which parts are overrated or don't transfer to an engineering setting.
