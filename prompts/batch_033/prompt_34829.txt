Obviously I've been thinking about Glaucon's challenge in the *Republic* the whole time I was writing it — the ring of Gyges, the idea that people are only just because they fear the consequences, that if you gave a just man and an unjust man both a ring of invisibility they'd end up doing exactly the same things. It's the ur-question for any novel about moral psychology, really.

The launch party will have maybe sixty people: some fellow novelists, a handful of reviewers, my editor and agent, friends and family, and — this is the tricky bit — my old philosophy tutor from Oxford is coming, and she's bringing a few of her current students.
