I'm a journalist who covers science and ideas, and I somehow ended up on the steering commitee for my city's citizens' assembly on the low-emission zone. We have to settle, before the end of the month, the rule for who counts as a member for next year's session. The assembly will be recommending whether to expand the zone, and that would mostly hit commuters from outside the city and small delivery companies based just over the city line. None of those people get a seat under our current residents-only rule. Half the committee says that's fine: residents should decide because it's their air and their city. The other half says anyone who's affected should have a voice, otherwise the whole thing is theater. I need to circulate a short written opinion before Thursday's meeting recommending a concrete rule for who's in and who's out, with a time frame for adopting it, and it should honestly engage the strongest point on the side I'm recommending against.
