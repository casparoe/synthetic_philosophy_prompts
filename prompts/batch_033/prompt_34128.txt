okay here's one i keep going back and forth on. people talk about "late mill" as if he quietly became a socialist, and other people say no, he just got more sympathetic to socialist criticisms while staying a liberal through and through. i want an essay that actually adjudicates this, not a survey.

the evidence as i understand it: in the principles of political economy he gives the standard liberal defense of private property and competition, but he also seems to have softened the chapter on property over the successive editions, and in the unfinished chapters on socialism published after his death he treats fourierist and cooperative schemes as serious contenders rather than cranks. in his autobiography he says something to the effect that his and harriet's ideal would put them under the general designation of socialist, but he immediately qualifies it in ways that might matter more than the label. yet he also keeps insisting that the choice between communism and the existing order is a question for the future, that we don't yet have the data to decide it, and that any workable socialism has to leave room for individual spontaneity rather than centralizing everything in the state. he distinguishes revolutionary socialists from gradualist ones and seems at least as worried about the former's political methods as he is impressed by their economic criticisms. and his concrete program is mostly worker cooperatives, profit-sharing, and industrial partnerships, which he thinks can grow up inside the present system.

so the question: what is the best overall classification of late mill, and what actually turns on the answer? weigh the "he basically became a socialist" reading against the "he remained a radical liberal who took socialist objections seriously" reading, and defend one. don't just split the difference by saying it's complicated -- actually commit. if you think the labels themselves are doing too much work, say which specific committments the two sides are really fighting over, and then say which side has the better claim to those committments.
