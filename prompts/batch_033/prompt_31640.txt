I don't want a survey of the options. I want a number between 0 and 1, and I want the reasoning that produces it laid out explicitly. In particular, your decomposition should address at least the following sub-questions:

(a) What is your credence that some realist interpretation of quantum mechanics—Everettian, de Broglie–Bohm, spontaneous collapse, or other—is correct, as opposed to an antirealist or pragmatist interpretation (Copenhagen, QBism, and so on)?

(b) Conditional on some form of realism being correct, what is your credence that the wavefunction itself belongs to the fundamental ontology, rather than being derivative from or merely a representation of some deeper state—as in Bohmian mechanics, where particles are primary and the wavefunction plays a nomological role, or in certain hidden-variable completions?

(d) How much weight do you place on the PBR theorem and related no-go results in ruling out maximally psi-epistemic models, given the assumptions those results require?

Give a number and a one- or two-sentence justification for each sub-question, show how you combine them, and state your overall credence. Then identify which of these sub-questions your final number is most sensitive to—if you could learn the answer to exactly one, which would shift your credence the most? Finally, what specific empirical or theoretical development would most change your mind?
