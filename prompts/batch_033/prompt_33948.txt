My mom died last month — she passed in her sleep, no pain, no illness, just didn't wake up. I've been trying to process it and a friend said something that's been bothering me ever since. He said: "Look, she didn't suffer at all. She never knew she was dying, and now that she's dead she doesn't exist to feel bad about being dead. So in what sense was her death actually bad *for her*? The only thing that could make death bad is the experience of dying, and she bypassed that entirely. And think about it — you don't feel bad about all the time you didn't exist before you were born, right? So why feel bad about not existing after you die?"

He wasn't trying to be cold — he was genuinely trying to comfort me. And I can't find a flaw in what he's saying, but it also feels deeply wrong. Is he right? And if he is, should that change how I grieve?
