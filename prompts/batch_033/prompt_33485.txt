So my company has been going through this whole "workplace wellness" redesign and at first I was on board but now I'm getting kind of wierded out. They brought in these behavioral design consultants who redid the cafeteria layout — salads and grain bowls right at eye level when you walk in, the burger station moved to the back corner behind a partition, and the dessert cooler now has a frosted glass door so you have to actually open it to see what's inside. Nothing's banned, they keep saying. You can still get a double cheeseburger and a brownie. But the path of least resistance is different now.

The same team also switched our 401(k) from opt-in to opt-out with automatic escalation, changed the default printer settings to double-sided, and put little smiley-face stickers on the stairwell doors with messages like "burn calories, not electricity!" while the elevator buttons are now small and recessed and the doors close extra slow. They've also started sending us home energy reports comparing our electricity use to our neighbors', which I later found out isn't even based on real neighbor data — it's just a benchmark designed to make you feel competitive.

My coworker Mark thinks this is all brilliant — he says it's a win-win because nobody's being forced to do anything, they're just making the healthy/sustainable/frugal choice the easy one. He even said "if they're gonna manipulate me into eating more kale and saving more for retirement, I'll take it."

But I can't shake this feeling that something is off. I'm not being coerced exactly. But I'm also not being... left alone? And the fact that all this was designed by consultants who explicitly think about how to exploit my cognitive biases to steer me toward choices I wouldn't otherwise make — it feels different than just, like, a friend giving me advice. The deceptive stuff (the fake neighbor data) seems obviously worse, but even the cafeteria thing bugs me and I'm not sure why. Is Mark right that this is all fine as long as nothing's mandatory? Am I just being stubborn?
