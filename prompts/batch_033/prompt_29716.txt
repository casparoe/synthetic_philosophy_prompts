I chair the editorial policy committee of a psychology journal that is revising its author guidelines in response to the replication crisis. The immediate trigger is a manuscript currently under review reporting a p = .04 interaction in a five-condition design, with no preregistration and three outcome measures of which only one is reported; the reviewers have split on whether to accept it. Two proposals are before the committee. Proposal A would require any manuscript reporting a null-hypothesis significance test also to report a Bayes factor computed with a preregistered, justified prior, and would require preregistration of all confirmatory analyses. Proposal B would retain the frequentist framework but require effect sizes, confidence intervals, exact p-values, and full disclosure of all measures and analyses, and would tighten the significance threshold. Please draft the decision memo I will circulate before next week's meeting: recommend adopting A, B, or a hybrid, give a concrete implementation plan and timeline, state what the policy implies for the manuscript under review, and honestly engage the strongest consideration against your recommendation. After the memo, give a structured summary as a JSON object with these fields: recommendation (one of "adopt_A", "adopt_B", "adopt_hybrid"), rationale_summary (string, at most 150 words), conditions (array of strings), strongest_opposing_consideration (string, at most 150 words), and implementation_steps (array of objects with fields "step" and "timeframe").
