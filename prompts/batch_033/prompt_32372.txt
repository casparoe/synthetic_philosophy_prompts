Write a dialogue between two people who both know their anthropics but disagree about the presumptuous philosopher. The SSA-leaning one thinks the fact that a theory predicts vastly more observers gives you no reason at all to favor it, since you'd have to exist either way. The SIA-leaning one thinks that's crazy: the fact that you in particular exist is exactly the kind of evidence that should make the many-observers theory more likely. Make them actually respond to each other rather than talk past each other — e.g., have the SSA person push on why SIA doesn't just commit you to believing in infinite observers, and have the SIA person push on wether SSA secretly commits you to the doomsday argument. I'd like it to end with each of them being able to state where the other one's intuition is doing the real work, even if neither changes their mind.
