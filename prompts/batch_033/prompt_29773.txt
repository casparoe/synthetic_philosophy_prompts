My other friend, who does philosophy of science, said this is obviously a reductio against unrestricted expected utility maximization — no sane person would hand over the wallet, so the theory is demanding something absurd, and that's that. He said that's exactly why we need a bounded utility function, or some kind of penalty on tiny probabilities. She said those fixes all feel ad hoc. He said ad hoc is better than bankrupt.
