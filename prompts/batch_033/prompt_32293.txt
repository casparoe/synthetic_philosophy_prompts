We've got a legal positivist (team Hart) and a natural law theorist (team Fuller) who've been going at it for months, and they've finally agreed to do an adversarial collaboration: produce a single document they can both sign. You're a professor of jurisprudence who's been mediating this kind of thing for years — please draft it.

I want the thing to have three sections. First, the claims both sides actually agree on — not the watered-down truisms but the substantive stuff where they've realised they were mostly talking past each other. Third, the cruxes — the sub-questions where, if you could settle them, at least one side would have to budge — and for each crux, the kind of case or argument both would accept as legitimately bearing on it. If any part of their old fights turns out to be purely verbal, call that out and say why.

Before you write the final document, think through what the genuine agreement space looks like here — I bet it's bigger than either of them thinks once they stop caricaturing each other. And be specific about the grudge informer: it's the case that anchors this whole debate, so if they can't say something joint about it the whole thing falls apart.
