Please write it as an ordered procedure: what counts as the input, whether all rankings are allowed, whether every option can in principle win, whether the rule is deterministic, and where the procedure must flag "impossible unless dictatorial" versus "the theorem doesn't apply." Be explicit about the escape routes (only two options, restricted preferences like single-peakedness, randomization) and about which steps are judgment calls rather than mechanical checks. Then run it on three proposals: instant-runoff marketed as strategy-proof, a "random voter is dictator" tiebreak, and simple majority between two candidates. Make sure at least one result is one the committee wont like.
