I want to design an activity that makes them really *feel* the difference between standard Nash equilibrium reasoning and program equilibrium in a one-shot prisoner's dilemma. Here's what I'm picturing: phase one, they play a regular one-shot PD against each other with real-ish stakes (maybe a small prize for whoever ends up with the most points). I want the debrief to map what happened onto the formal concept: the difference between action profiles and program profiles, why being able to condition on the other's program changes the strategic landscape, and how this connects to commitment devices, AI safety, and bargaining.
