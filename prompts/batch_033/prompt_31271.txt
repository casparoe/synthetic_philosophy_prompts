I cover science and ideas for a living, and I have been reading a cluster of proposals in AI alignment usually discussed under the labels iterated amplification, HCH (humans consulting HCH), and factored cognition. The proposal, as I understand it, runs like this. A human is given a hard question and a limited amount of time. Rather than answer directly, she breaks the question into smaller subquestions and delegates each to a copy of herself, who may do the same, recursively; at the bottom of the tree, subquestions are answered without further decomposition. The hope is that this tree of humans is far more capable than any individual human, and that a model trained to imitate the process stays aligned because every step is produced by a human and can be checked by one. Proponents treat the limit of this process as a specification of what a wise version of the human would conclude given unlimited time and help, with her values intact. The enabling assumption, called factored cognition, is that sophisticated reasoning can be broken into many small, largely independent pieces, each of which can be evaluated without understanding the big picture.

I am trying to figure out what this design presupposes about the nature of deliberation, knowledge, and value. It seems to take sides in several old disputes: that reasoning is compositional rather than holistic; that a person can recognize a correct subanswer even when she could not have produced the whole answer; that amplifying competence does not change the values doing the supervising. But I cannot tell which of these are strictly required by the proposal, which merely fit well with it, and which rival views of reasoning would reject.

Please reconstruct the implicit theory of deliberation behind iterated amplification and HCH. Specifically: what view of reasoning or knowledge does it presuppose, and what view of values or alignment? Which rival philosophical views does it quietly rule out, and where is the design underdetermined, such that several different views would license the same engineering choices? What would have to change in the proposal if the presupposed view were replaced by a holistic or otherwise rival picture of reasoning?

Give your answer as a numbered list of exactly five points, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion. Each point should state one thing the proposal presupposes and briefly note which rival view it rules out or what would change if it were dropped.
