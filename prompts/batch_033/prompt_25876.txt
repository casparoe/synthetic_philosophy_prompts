Write an essay on whether genuine moral dilemmas are possible. By "genuine" I mean cases where no matter what you do, you violate a moral obligation — not just cases where both options are bad but one is clearly worse. Engage with the main philosophical arguments on both sides: the "ought implies can" principle, the problem of moral remainders, the agglomeration principle, stuff like that. Dont just summarize the debate — take a side and defend it. And don't worry about citing anyone in particular, just engage with the ideas themselves.
