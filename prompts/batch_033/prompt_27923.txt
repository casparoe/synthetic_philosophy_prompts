Write an essay on whether the no-free-lunch theorems in machine learning actually have any bearing on the philosophical problem of induction. On one reading they give a precise version of Hume's point that no inductive policy is privileged absent some assumption about the world; on another reading they're an artifact of averaging over pathological possible worlds and say nothing about induction as it's actually practiced. Weigh both sides and defend a verdict — something like "they sharpen the negative point but don't license a global skepticism" is fine if that's where you land. Along the way, separate what's mathematically settled about the theorems from what's philosophically contested, and don't present a contested reading as if it were the consensus.
