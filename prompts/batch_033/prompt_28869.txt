And I kept thinking: this maps onto stuff I already think in, but not cleanly.

So here's what I want. Restate each of the main positions — knowledge norm, truth norm, justified belief norm, belief norm — in the vocabulary I actually use: assertions, contracts, preconditions, postconditions, exceptions, type guarantees, whatever maps.
