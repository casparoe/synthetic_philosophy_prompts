I have been reading about the hole argument in the philosophy of general relativity. As I understand it, the argument shows that substantivalism about spacetime makes the theory indeterministic: two models related by a diffeomorphism that is the identity outside a region but differs inside it agree on the initial data, yet assign different metric fields to the points inside the region. If these are distinct possibilities, then the same initial state has two different possible developments. What observable difference between the two models should an experiment inside the region be able to detect in order to determine which development is actual?
