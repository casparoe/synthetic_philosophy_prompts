The question should be answerable in about fifty minutes by a strong student who has done the reading but has no notes. Then write a model answer of about 700 words and a marking scheme out of 20 that names the specific moves an A answer must contain and the specific mistakes that would cap an answer at a B.

You are an expert on intuitionistic logic and mathematical constructivism, and a careful examiner. Think through the issue yourself before drafting, perhaps by brainstorming the candidate moves a strong student might make, so that the question and the rubric target genuine understanding rather than memorized labels.

Please write everything in continuous prose only: no headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables. Present the question, the model answer, and the marking scheme as paragraphs, the way you would write a letter or an email.
