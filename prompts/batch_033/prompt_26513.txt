Draft the joint statement that a proponent of Galen Strawson's Basic Argument against moral responsibility and a compatibilist who rejects that argument could both sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration. The proponent holds that the Basic Argument shows that true moral responsibility is impossible regardless of whether determinism is true; the compatibilist holds that moral responsibility is compatible with determinism and that the Basic Argument fails to undermine it.

The statement should contain four sections. First, a numbered list of claims both parties accept — about the structure of the Basic Argument, the regress it identifies, what it would mean to be causa sui, and what ordinary practices of blame and praise presuppose. Second, a disagreements table with precise formulations of where they part ways, each phrased so that its own side would accept the wording. At minimum, address: whether moral responsibility requires that one be responsible for one's own character or mental nature; whether the regress is vicious or merely highlights a benign structural fact about agency; and whether compatibilist accounts answer the argument or change the subject. Third, a list of cruxes — sub-questions such that settling them would move at least one side. Fourth, for each crux, a concrete case or argument that both agree bears on it, with each side's prediction or interpretation recorded in advance. If you conclude that any part of their dispute is merely verbal, identify it and explain why.
