ok so ive been trying to get clear on what I think about ideal deliberation — specifically the HCH and factored cognition stuff — and id like an audit of whether my views actually hang together before I write this up for a talk. Here's roughly where I've landed:

(1) I think HCH — a human with unlimited time and help, who can delegate subquestions to other copies of the same setup — is the best idealization we have of what a careful deliberator would conclude with unlimited time and help.

(2) I think factored cognition is the load-bearing part: the reason HCH isn't vacuous is that a hard question can be broken into pieces small enough that an ordinary human can actually check each one.

(3) I think whatever counts as an ideal deliberator should be coherent in the decision-theory sense: transitive preferences, no Dutch books, updating by conditionalization.

(4) But I also think real deliberation is messy and doesn't speak with one voice, and an idealization that forced everything into one globally consistent agent would be falsifying what deliberation is actually like.

(5) I'm inclined to say process doesn't matter except through its outputs: two deliberative procedures that converge on the same answers are equally good as idealizations.

(6) But I also want to say an answer only counts as deliberatively justified if a human could in principle follow and check the reasoning that produced it.

(7) For a lot of the questions I actually care about — what to do about AI risk, how much to weigh future people — I don't think there's a pre-existing answer out there that deliberation discovers; the deliberation partly constitutes the answer.

(8) And I have a stubborn sense that, in some domains, a single expert's inarticulate gut call is more reliable than a careful committee that factored the reasoning into checkable pieces.

Which of these are actually in tension, and which only look like they are? Rank the real tensions by severity, say what the cheapest fix is for each, and tell me which single revision would clean up the most. Dont restate all eight back at me — assume I know what I wrote.
