My colleague's verdicts on deontological constraint cases so far:

1. Push one to save five from a runaway trolley: impermissible.
2. Push one to save five from a natural disaster: impermissible.
3. Torture one to save five from being tortured: impermissible.
4. Kill one to save a thousand from being murdered: permissible.
5. Lie to a would-be murderer at the door to save the friend hiding inside: permissible.
6. Break a promise to one to prevent five other promises from being broken: impermissible.
