My dad died a couple years ago, and right before he died I promised him I'd keep the family farm going. I've kept at it since, but honestly it's running me into the ground, and now I've got an offer from a neighbor to buy the place for a fair price plus a job I actually want in a city a few hours away. My siblings think I'm crazy not to sell, and they keep saying Dad wouldn't have wanted me to be miserable. But the promise was to him, not to them, so their go-ahead doesnt feel like it settles it. At the same time, he's gone — he can't hold me to it, and he can't be hurt if I break it. So does the promise still bind me, or did it kind of die with him?
