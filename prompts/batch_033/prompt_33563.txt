The system is the MIU system: the sole axiom is MI, and the rules are — append U to any string ending in I; double everything after the M; replace III with U; delete UU. Here is my opening move. You have been trying to derive MU by applying the rules. I can show you will never succeed without applying a single rule. The axiom MI contains exactly one I. The rules affect the total number of I's only by leaving it unchanged, doubling it, or subtracting three from it.
