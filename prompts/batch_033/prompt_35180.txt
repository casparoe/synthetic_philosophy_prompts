A few people nodded, but several of us felt that something had been evaded without being able to say exactly what. I want to bring a structured analysis to office hours. Please lay out the distinct precise claims that the philosophical move "Here is one hand" could be expressing — from the most minimal, near-trivial reading to the most metaphysically ambitious. For each reading, say whether it is (a) trivially true, (b) substantive but defensible, or (c) false, and whether it actually answers the Cartesian skeptic or merely declines to engage on the skeptic's terms. Finally, identify which reading or readings would have to succeed for the professor's dismissal to be warranted, and assess whether they do.
