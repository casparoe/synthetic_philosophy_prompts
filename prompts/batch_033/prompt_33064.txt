I'm a grad student in a philosophy dept and everyone keeps glossing bad faith as just lying to yourself. Can you lay out what corresponds to what between ordinary lying and bad faith, where the mapping breaks, which disanalogies actually matter for the 'no ignorance excuse' conclusion, and wether that conclusion survives once they're on the table?
