Last week I assigned a pair of short pieces defending illusionism: the view that phenomenal consciousness — the "what it's like" of experience, the redness of red, the sting of pain — doesn't actually exist in the way we think it does, and that we are subject to a kind of systematic introspective illusion. Or does it reject the whole framing that generates the desiderata list in the first place?
