so i'm a thirder (i think beauty should assign 1/3 to heads when she wakes up) and he's a halfer (1/2). we want someone to draft this document for us. it should have:

- a numbered list of claims we both accept (about the setup, about what the question even means, about what kinds of considerations are relevant)
- a table or section with our disagreements, each one stated precisely and in a way that the person who holds that view would say "yeah that's what i think"
- the cruxes — the things where if one of us turned out to be wrong about it, we'd change our overall view
- for each crux, some test case or argument that both of us agree is relevant, and what each side predicts about it

don't just say "they disagree about whether self-locating belief is special" — dig into the actual mechanics of the problem. like what about the monday-tuesday distinction, what about betting odds, what about the fact that tails gives two awakenings but they're not independent pieces of evidence. we want something we could actually put our names on and then go investigate the cruxes.
