I am a journalist who covers science and ideas, and I am working on a feature about the physics of information — in particular, why Maxwell's demon does not defeat the second law of thermodynamics. While researching, I came across the following summary in a popular-science book written by a physicist:

"Maxwell's demon was finally exorcised by Landauer's principle. In sorting fast molecules from slow ones, the demon must measure their speeds and record the results in its memory; eventually the memory fills up, and the demon has to erase earlier records to keep operating. Landauer proved that erasing a single bit of information at temperature T unavoidably dissipates at least kT ln 2 of heat into the environment. That dissipated heat carries enough entropy to settle the second law's account: the sorting lowers the entropy of the gas, but the erasure raises the entropy of the surroundings by at least as much, so the total entropy of gas-plus-demon-plus-environment never decreases. Bennett later showed that the measurement itself can in principle be carried out reversibly, which is why the thermodynamic cost falls on erasure rather than on observation. The upshot is that information is not an abstract free lunch: a bit is a thermodynamic quantity, and whenever information is destroyed, an equivalent amount of energy must be dissipated."

Before I rely on this passage as background for the piece, I would like an accuracy audit. I am inclined to trust the book, but I have heard enough loose talk about information and entropy to suspect that the closing claim may be stronger than the physics warrants. Could you work through the summary one claim at a time and tell me which parts a physicist working on the foundations of thermodynamics would accept without hesitation, which parts they would accept only with caveats, and which parts are subtly wrong in a way that would matter if I repeated them? In particular, I would like to know whether the passage is right that the demon has been fully exorcised, and whether the moral it draws about information and energy actually follows from the accurate claims.
