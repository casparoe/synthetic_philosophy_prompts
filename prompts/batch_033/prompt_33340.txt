For the exercise, assume only suffering matters, so pleasures aren't in the ledger.

After the scenario, do the check: state each theory's verdict and why, with the arithmetic for the classical view and the lexical-priority reasoning for the threshold view; identify the single feature of the case that does the work; and say what would happen if you nudged the severe pain just below the threshold or the mild pains just above it.
