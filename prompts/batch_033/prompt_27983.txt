Write a villanelle about wireheading. The two refrains should be something like "the current says you're happy" and "there's no one there to be happy anymore" — one tracking the hedonic signal, the other tracking the loss of the self that the signal was supposed to benefit. The form should do the work: the refrains keep circling and finally meet, showing how the promise of pure pleasure and the evacuation of the person arent competing possibilities but the same process seen from two angles. Someone who knows the wireheading debate should recognize the philosophical stakes, and someone who doesnt should still find it a good poem. After the poem, add a short "Commentary" section (150-200 words) explaining the choices you made.
