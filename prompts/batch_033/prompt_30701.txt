In the Judy Benjamin problem, a soldier is dropped into a region split into a Red territory and a Blue territory, each of which is divided into a headquarters sector and a second-company sector; she initially assigns equal probability to all four sectors. She then receives a radio message telling her that if she is in Red territory, she is in Red Headquarters. It is unclear to me why this case is treated as a challenge for Bayesian updating. The message simply conveys the material conditional — that she is not in Red territory, or she is in Red Headquarters — so standard conditionalization should apply directly. That eliminates only the Red second-company sector and leaves the other three sectors equally probable. What, if anything, is wrong with this treatment?
