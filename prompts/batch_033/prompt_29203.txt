I'm suspicious of how much work the analogy is doing here, and I suspect von Neumann himself was more cautious than this reading suggests.

- The smuggled assumptions: what properties of digital computation the analogy quietly imports that the brain may not share.
- A verdict: once the disanalogies are on the table, does the conclusion that consciousness is substrate-independent survive, or was the analogy carrying it the whole time?
