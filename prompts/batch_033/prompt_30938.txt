He said, roughly: "Dennett's view is that when a system is too complicated to predict from its parts, you adopt the intentional stance — you treat it as a rational agent with beliefs and desires. So for Dennett, the question 'does the chatbot literally have beliefs?' is the wrong question to ask. The stance works, and in the only sense that matters, it does have beliefs and desires. By the same reasoning, a thermostat literally wants the room warm."
