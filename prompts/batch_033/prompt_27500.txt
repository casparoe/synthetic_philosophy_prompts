I'm putting together a "which Sleeping Beauty position do you actually hold?" quiz for my small rationalist-adjacent blog and I want it to be diagnostic, not just a vibe check. You're an expert on self-locating belief and Bayesian updating—think through the logical space carefully before committing, and don't just pattern-match to the usual halfer/thirder summaries.

The quiz should have eight to ten questions, each a concrete case: the standard Sunday-night-coin setup, waking and being told it's Monday, a version with a visible cue on each awakening (say, red paper on Monday and blue paper on Tuesday if Tails, red paper on the single Monday if Heads), a version where Tails means a thousand awakenings instead of two, being offered even-money bets on Heads, and whatever other variants you think carve the space cleanly. For each question the answer options should be things a smart non-specialist could pick without already knowing the labels—so no "do you think you're a halfer?" questions.

Then give a scoring key that maps answer patterns onto at least the halfer (Lewis-style: 1/2 on waking, 2/3 after learning it's Monday), the thirder (Elga-style: 1/3 on waking, 1/2 after learning it's Monday), and the double-halfer (1/2 on both waking and Monday). If there are other coherent positions in the logical space—like an SIA-flavored view that diverges from the standard thirder on some cases—add them as leaves too.

Build it from the logical space first, not from the list of names: say which combinations of answers are possible, and for any combination nobody defends, say whether it's incoherent, just unattractive, or maybe unexplored. Flag any pair of positions your questions can't actually seperate.

Output as JSON with this schema:

- `questions`: array of objects with `id`, `scenario` (a paragraph), `options` (array of strings), and `what_this_probes` (one sentence on which commitment it tests)
- `positions`: array of objects with `name`, `answer_signature` (map from question ids to chosen options), and `awkward_commitment` (one sentence on the most uncomfortable thing the position has to swallow)
- `unoccupied_combinations`: array of objects with `answers` (map from question ids to options), `diagnosis` (incoherent / unattractive / unexplored, with a sentence why)
- `inseparable_pairs`: array of objects with `positions` (two names) and `why` (one sentence on what the questions can't distinguish)

Don't worry about sounding formal—I'd rather the questions feel like a LessWrong comment thread than a journal article.
