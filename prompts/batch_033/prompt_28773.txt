- one person tortured for 50 years vs 10^15 people getting a single dust speck in the eye: she says the torture is worse
- one person loosing both legs vs 10^9 people getting a severe day-long migraine: she says the migraines are worse
- one person killed vs 10^12 people getting a single dust speck: she says the death is worse
- one person with a year of moderate chronic pain vs 10^5 people getting a brief mild headache: she says the chronic pain is worse

Next I'm going to ask her the classic LessWrong one: one person tortured for 50 years vs 3^^^3 people getting a single dust speck. What should I expect her to say, and what rule would best explain the whole pattern? I can see at least two families of rule that fit — something like lexical priority for the very worst harms, and something like plain aggregation with a crossover at a stupidly large number — but I'm not sure which her earlier answers actually commit her to. Which rule is the better bet, what do her existing verdicts rule out, and what extra case would most cleanly settle it?
