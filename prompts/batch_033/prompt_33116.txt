ok so I've been going down this rabbit hole about the Sphex wasp. It's this digger wasp that does this super elaborate routine when it brings food back to its nest. It lands, goes inside the burrow to inspect everything, comes back out, grabs the paralyzed cricket, and drags it in. But here's the wild part — if you move the cricket a couple inches while the wasp is inside inspecting, when it comes out it'll drag the cricket back to the entrance, and then go inside to inspect AGAIN. And you can do this over and over and it never catches on. It just resets the whole sequence every time. It's like it's running a program and literally can't deviate from the script even when it's completely pointless.

From what I understand, the term "sphexishness" got coined later (like 1960s maybe?) to describe behavior that looks intelligent but is actually just rigid and mechanical. Hofstadter and Dennett talked about it a lot.
