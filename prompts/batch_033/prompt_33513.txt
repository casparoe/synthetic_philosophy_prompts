I'm a retired neuroscientist — spent thirty years doing systems neuro, mostly visual attention and working memory — and I've been reading philosophy of mind seriously for a couple years now. I've drifted toward higher-order theories of consciousness, and I'm trying to write up where I've landed for a small reading group. Problem is I cant tell whether the package actually coheres or whether I've taped together pieces from different books.

Here's the set:

(1) I'm a physicalist. Conscious experience is entirely a matter of what the brain does; there's no extra nonphysical ingredient.

(2) First-order sensory representations — color, shape, pain — can occur without being conscious. What makes a state conscious is that there's a higher-order representation of one's being in that state; on the version I lean toward, the higher-order state is a thought rather than a perception-like scan.

(3) The higher-order representation itself doesn't have to be conscious to do this job; usually it isn't, and requiring it to be conscious would just start a regress.

(4) What it's like to be in a conscious state is exhausted by how that state is represented at the higher order. There's no extra intrinsic glow in the first-order state itself.

(5) I'm also fairly convinced that dogs and young infants consciously feel pain, even though they can't think about their own mental states the way an adult can.

(6) I accept the transparency point: when I try to introspect my experience of a tomato, I end up attending to the tomato, not to a mental state. Experience doesn't present itself to me as a mental object.

(7) And I'm attracted to the global availability idea — that a representation becomes conscious when it's broadcast widely to consumer systems in the brain.

So here's what I want: go through these and tell me which pairs are genuinely in tension and which only look that way. Rank the real tensions by severity, and for each one say what has to give and what the cheapest repair would be. Then tell me which single revision would leave me with the most defensible overall package. I'd rather you be specific about what I'm implicitly committed to than polite about it.
