I've got this thing where I freeze up whenever two options are exactly as good as each other: two cereal boxes, same price, same size, and I've read both labels and know I like both equally, but I still stand there for minutes. My partner says I'm being irrational — if there's no reason to prefer either one, the rational move is just to grab whichever, because thinking longer can't make one better than the other. But here's what bugs me: if I genuinely have no reason to choose one over the other, then whatever I pick is arbitrary, and choosing arbitrarily feels like the opposite of choosing rationally. I'm not looking for a trick to make up my mind; I want to know whether the whole idea of a rational choice breaks down here. Is my partner right that I should just pick, or is there something off in how I'm thinking about reasons?
