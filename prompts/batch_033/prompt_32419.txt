A radiologist and an astrologer disagree about what a CT scan shows; Dana says the radiologist shouldn't budge at all. Two radiologists with similar training look at the same scan and disagree about whether a shadow is a tumor; Dana says each should become substantially less confident, but they don't have to land exactly at 50/50. Two macroeconomists with the same data but different modeling assumptions disagree about whether there'll be a recession next year; Dana says each should move a lot toward the other, but not necessarily all the way. A chess grandmaster and a club player disagree about the best move in a position; Dana says the grandmaster shouldn't move. Two philosophers who have read the same arguments disagree about whether the argument is sound; Dana says each should reduce their confidence, but not necessarily suspend judgment.
