(2) Closure of rational belief under conjunction. If I am rational to believe p and rational to believe q, then I am rational to believe the conjunction of p and q.

She has checked each claim carefully; her credence in each claim is 0.997, and the claims are probabilistically independent of one another. She is rational to believe each individual claim, and she is rational to believe that at least one claim in the book is false.

Rank the real tensions by severity rather than treating them all as equally serious.
