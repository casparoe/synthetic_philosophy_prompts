I am working toward a theory of supererogation and I want to start by fixing the criteria of adequacy before I commit to any particular view. Please lay out the desiderata that any satisfactory theory of supererogatory action must meet. For each desideratum, give the case or argument that motivates treating it as a requirement rather than a preference of one theoretical camp.

At minimum, a theory of supererogation must explain what distinguishes the supererogatory from (a) the obligatory and (b) the merely permissible, and it must account for the core asymmetry that supererogatory acts are praiseworthy to perform but not blameworthy to omit. But I suspect the full list of requirements is longer and more interesting than that. I want to see where the theoretical pressure points are: for instance, whether a theory must handle the continuity between small favors and saintly sacrifices without erasing the distinction, whether it must make sense of the genuine optionality of supererogatory acts without collapsing that optionality into arbitrariness, and whether it must be compatible with the impartiality that characterizes the moral point of view — or whether impartiality is precisely what a theory of supererogation must qualify or reject.

I am especially interested in the following structure. Identify which desiderata are non-negotiable (no theory that fails them can be taken seriously), which are merely desirable (a theory that fails them is worse but not ruled out), and which are contested (some traditions reject them outright and would not count them as desiderata at all). Then flag any pairs or subsets of the non-negotiable desiderata that appear to be jointly unsatisfiable — that is, where you suspect a no-go result may be lurking, and the list of requirements itself might force a revision of what we thought a theory of supererogation could deliver. Finally, indicate which single desideratum does the most to separate the main existing approaches (consequentialist, Kantian, virtue-ethical, and contractualist treatments of supererogation). A brief scorecard is welcome but the criteria themselves are the deliverable I need.
