Everyone agrees on the counterfactual structure: the very act that causes the person to exist with a lower level of well-being is also necessary for that person to exist at all, and the person's life is still worth living. Everyone agrees that if you just look at outcomes, the "same number" quality condition makes the later-child policy better. But then we split: some say the child isn't wronged because they aren't made worse off than they would otherwise have been; others say the wrong is in bringing about a worse state of affairs; others say the wrong is impersonal or about the agent's reasons rather than the victim; and so on.
