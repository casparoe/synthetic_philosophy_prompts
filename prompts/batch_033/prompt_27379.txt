And then I started noticing this isn't just about me being sloppy — it seems like any time someone's "confidence levels" across different possibilities don't hang together in a certain way, you can set up a combination of bets they'd willingly take that fleeces them for sure. I'm trying to figure out if this pattern has a name in philosophy or decision theory, and more importantly, what exactly it's supposed to prove. Like, is the argument that you're *irrational* if your beliefs can be exploited this way? Or is it just a pragmatic warning about bookies? And does the argument actually work, or are there ways to wriggle out of it? I'd rather not just get a label — I want to understand what the argument is really doing and where its limits are.
