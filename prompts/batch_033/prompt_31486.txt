Before I read further in the literature on the norm of assertion, I want the specification of what a theory in this area is actually answerable to. Write the desiderata that any adequate account of the norm governing assertion must meet. The candidate views I have in mind are the knowledge norm, the truth norm, the belief norm, a justification or warrant norm, and the view that assertion has no constitutive norm at all, so pitch the criteria at a level where those five can be scored against them. Take the target to be a theory good enough to explain ordinary assertoric practice and the epistemic role of assertion in testimony, rather than one that merely stipulates a rule.

For each desideratum, give the case or consideration that makes it a requirement, and say whether it is non-negotiable or a preference of one camp. Cover at least the following, and add anything further you think belongs on the list:

- lottery propositions: asserting that one's ticket has lost on purely statistical grounds, without inside information;
- Moorean conjunctions: assertions of the form *p, but I do not believe that p*, and *p, but I do not know that p*;
- warranted false assertions: cases in which a speaker has strong but misleading evidence and asserts a falsehood;
- assertions accompanied by explicit disavowals of knowledge or of belief, and whether such assertions can ever be proper;
- the role of assertion in testimony, and what a hearer is entitled to take from an assertion;
- retraction and challenge: when a speaker ought to retract, and what an appropriate challenge such as asking how one knows, or whether one is sure, reveals about the norm.

Also specify the distinctions an adequate theory must respect: for example, between a norm's being violated and an assertion's being blameless or reasonable; between the norm of assertion and the norms of belief and action; and between the constitutive question and the question of when a particular assertion is useful, polite, or prudent. Flag any pair of desiderata you suspect cannot be jointly satisfied, and say how the main camps try to dissolve the tension. Then identify the single desideratum that does the most to eliminate candidate norms, and briefly indicate how the five views fare against the non-negotiable part of the list. Keep the weight on the criteria and their motivating cases rather than on the scoring.

Please write the answer in British English.
