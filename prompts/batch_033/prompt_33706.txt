I keep seeing Moore's "proof of an external world" brought up as this big deal in epistemology, and I honestly can't figure out why. As far as I can tell, the argument goes: he holds up his hands, says "here is one hand, here is another, therefore there are at least two external objects, therefore an external world exists." But this is just begging the question. If you're already doubting whether there's an external world, you're also doubting whether those are actually hands. It's like saying "I know God exists because the Bible tells me so, and the Bible is the word of God." I feel like I must be missing something, becuase serious philosophers treat this as important, but to me it reads as either a joke or a guy just refusing to engage with the sceptical problem. What am I not getting?
