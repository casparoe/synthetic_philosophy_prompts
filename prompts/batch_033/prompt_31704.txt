They should respond to each other's actual moves, and neither should be a strawman. It can end with them still disagreeing, but the disagreement should get sharper rather than just repeat. The audience is mostly grad students in the department, some in formal semantics and some in epistemology, so it can be technical but the stakes should stay clear.
