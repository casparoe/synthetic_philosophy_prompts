Say why each one is a requirement and what it rules out. Flag which requirements are non-negotiable and which are just one camps preference. And tell me which single desideratum does the most work in killing off the popular definitions (whatever IQ tests measure, rational agent, task performance, information processing, whatever you take the main contenders to be).
