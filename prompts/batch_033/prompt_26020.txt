I'm on my university's AI governance working group. We got ourselves into a minor mess last month: our media relations office put out a press release about a CS department project that described a large language model as "understanding complex legal reasoning" and "forming beliefs about the facts of a case." A law school faculty member wrote an angry email to the dean saying this kind of language is misleading and, given that our law school is involved in AI liability work, institutionally irresponsible. The CS folks shot back that this is standard shorthand in the field and nobody actually thinks the model has mental states. The provost wants our working group to produce a short policy memo — maybe 800 words — recommending whether the university should adopt guidlines on the use of intentional or psychological language about AI systems in official communications (press releases, grant proposals, university web pages, etc.), and if so, what those guidelines should say. I'm drafting it. Please write the memo, with a clear recommendation and an honest treatment of the strongest argument on the losing side. American English, please.
