I'm trying to pin down what a convention actually is, mostly by testing Lewis's account against my gut on cases. My rough working version of Lewis is: a convention is a regularity in a population that solves a coordination problem; nearly everyone conforms, expects nearly everyone else to conform, and prefers to conform given that others do; there's some alternative regularity they'd have been about as happy to follow if that had been the one everyone followed; and this whole setup is common knowledge. But my reactions to cases pull in different directions.

1. Everyone in my building happens to do laundry on Sunday, just because that's when they're free. I don't think that's a convention.
2. In a country where everyone drives on the right, that's obviously a convention.
3. A village used to have a chief who punished anyone who didn't harvest on the first dry day. The chief died years ago, but everyone still harvests on the first dry day because each expects the others to. I think that's a convention now, even though it started as coercion.
4. A department has an informal norm of staying late. Almost everyone privately hates it and wishes it would end, but each person stays late because they think everyone else expects it and would judge them for leaving on time. I want to say that's still a convention—but does it solve a coordination problem anyone actually wants solved?
5. Two unconnected groups independently develop the same gesture for "come here." I want to say each group has its own convention, not that there's one convention shared across them, even though the behavior and the problem are identical.
6. A group of friends always sits in the same chairs at their weekly game. If you ask them why, they just say "that's how we've always done it"; they dont seem to expect anyone else to sit there or to care where anyone sits, and they'd sit anywhere if the chairs were moved. I'm torn: it feels sort of conventional, but on Lewis's account it might not count.

Is there a single repaired principle that gets all or most of these? And what does it say about the case I keep going back and forth on: a path where the first few people happened to walk on the left side and everyone after them followed—is that a convention, and does it matter whether anyone ever formed an expectation about it?
