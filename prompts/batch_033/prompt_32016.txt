I've been trying to get more politically involved lately, but I keep hitting the same wall: I can't find a party or movement where I actually agree with more than maybe 60% of the platform. And when I bring this up with friends who are more committed, they tell me I'm being naive, that politics is about coalitions and you have to pick a side and fight for it. But the thing that bothers me isn't just that I disagree on some issues — it's that the whole structure seems designed to make me stop thinking about whether I actually agree on each point and instead just adopt the whole package. Like, once you're "on the team," you're supposed to defend everything the team stands for, even the stuff you privately think is wrong. Is there something actually broken about political parties as a form of organizing, or am I just being precious about purity?
