In the same discussion he appears to claim that truthfulness in one's declarations is a strict duty of right rather than a duty that could be weighed against benevolence, and that if you answer truthfully when a murderer asks whether his intended victim is hiding in your house, you have done nothing wrong, whereas if you lie and some unforeseen consequence follows, you are answerable for that consequence.
