I sit on my university's academic accommodations committee. A student has applied for extra exam time and private testing conditions on the grounds of subclinical anxiety — she scores just below the diagnostic threshold on the standard screening tool, but her psychologist has written a letter saying the condition nonetheless impairs her exam performance. She's also requesting permission to use a beta-blocker during exams, which she currently takes off-label. The committee is split: some say this is straightforward accommodation of a disability, others say we're effectively sanctioning cognitive enhancement for a student who doesn't meet the diagnostic bar. Draft a decision memo — approve, deny, or approve with conditions — that engages seriously with the worry that we're blurring the line between treating impairment and enhancing performance, and give us a principle we can actually use for similar cases down the road.
