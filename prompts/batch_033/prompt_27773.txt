I can see we're heading for a worse outcome than if one of us had just spoken up. But from inside the situation, my reasoning still feels prudent. Is there a way of thinking about this kind of standoff that explains what's going wrong, and is there a principled way out that isn't just 'someone should be less stubborn'?
