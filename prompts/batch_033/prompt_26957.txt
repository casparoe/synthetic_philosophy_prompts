But I want to stress-test this properly. What would actually count against it? Which objections do Lucas and Penrose themselves take seriously, and which do they just dismiss? Are there defeaters that would be fatal (the whole thing collapses) versus ones that just force a retreat to a weaker claim? And honestly: has this position been qualified so much over the decades that it's basically unfalsifiable now?
