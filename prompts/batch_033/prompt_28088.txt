I'm a novelist working on a book whose protagonist is a cosmologist, and I've been reading about Boltzmann brains. I've reconstructed an argument that now has me cornered — if it's sound, I'm not sure I can trust my own reasons for writing the book, or for anything else.

(1) In any cosmological model with a positive cosmological constant and a sufficiently long future, random thermal fluctuations in de Sitter space will eventually produce disembodied brains — Boltzmann brains — complete with false memories of a coherent life, including memories of having evidence for that very cosmology.

(2) Over infinite time, the number of such Boltzmann brains vastly exceeds the number of ordinary observers who evolved through standard cosmic history.

(3) So in any such model, a randomly selected observer-like entity is overwhelmingly likely to be a Boltzmann brain.

(4) Therefore any such model predicts, with near-certainty, that we ourselves are Boltzmann brains, and that our apparent evidence of an orderly cosmos is a momentary fluctuation.

(5) If we are Boltzmann brains, all our evidence is unreliable — including the cosmological reasoning that led to the model. The model is self-undermining.

(6) So any model that predicts an abundance of Boltzmann brains is empirically ruled out by the fact that we take ourselves to have reliable evidence.

(7) But our best current physics — a universe with a small positive cosmological constant — seems to be exactly such a model. So our best physics appears to rule itself out.

I can see candidate escape routes: perhaps the measure over observers is ill-defined in an infinite universe; perhaps I'm wrong to treat myself as randomly selected from the set of all observer-like entities; perhaps the fluctuation probabilities don't work the way I'm assuming. But each of these moves seems to require trusting some piece of the very physics the argument puts in doubt. Where does this argument actually go wrong? If it doesn't go wrong, which premise is least costly to abandon, and what do I lose by abandoning it?
