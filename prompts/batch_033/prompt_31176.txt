For each, say what would have to be true of the world (or our evidence) for it to land, and whether a committed illusionist would concede it as a genuine cost or dismiss it. Make the fifth item your honest take on whether illusionism has been qualified into unfalsifiability, and whether that's a problem for a view like this.
