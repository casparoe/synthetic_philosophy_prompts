ok so i've been thinking about consciousness on and off for years — mostly from the angle of whether we could build a conscious AI, which comes up at work sometimes — and i've sort of cobbled together a set of views. i've never actually checked whether they're consistent with each other, and i'm worried i might be holding positions that quietly contradict. here's where i've landed:

1. i'm a physicalist. i think consciousness is something the brain does, not some extra non-physical stuff. no soul, no dualism, no panpsychism.

2. i think the mary's room thought experiment has real force. if someone has never seen colour but knows all the physics and neuroscience, she learns something genuinely new when she first sees red. you can't get the what-it's-like from the equations.

3. what she learns is a fact — she comes to know a truth she didn't know before. it's not just that she gains a skill or an ability, like learning to ride a bike. she learns that red looks like *this*.

4. but i also think phenomenal concepts are special in a way that explains the gap. the way we think about our own experiences from the inside uses concepts that are tied to actually having those experiences — they're recognitional or demonstrative. and this special character of phenomenal concepts is why (2) and (3) seem true, without actually refuting physicalism. (i read something like this in a blog post once and it made a lot of sense.)

5. i think the hard problem of consciousness is a real problem. it's not just a confusion about language or concepts. there's a genuine explanatory gap between the physical story and the felt quality of experience.

6. i think philosophical zombies — beings physically identical to us but with no inner experience — are concievable, but they're not metaphysically possible. you can *imagine* them but they couldn't actually exist in a physicalist world.

7. if we built a perfect simulation of a human brain on a computer, neuron for neuron, it would be conscious. it wouldn't be a zombie.

so yeah, that's my scattered set of positions. i'm trying to figure out whether this all hangs together or whether i'm helping myself to things that pull in opposite directions. could you go through these and tell me which pairs are in tension, how severe the tension is, and what the cheapest fix would be? also, are there any pairs that only *look* like they're in tension but actually aren't? and if i had to change just one thing to make the whole set more coherent, which one should it be?
