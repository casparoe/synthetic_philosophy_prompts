I've been listening to a lot of Schoenberg and Berg recently, and it got me thinking about Schopenhauer. He has this very specific account of music in The World as Will and Representation, where music isn't like the other arts because it doesn't copy the Platonic Ideas but is a direct copy of the Will itself. He then maps the parts of music onto the grades of the Will's objectification: the bass corresponds to inorganic nature, the harmony to the intermediate grades, and melody to the highest grade, the striving, self-conscious life of the Will. Melody, for him, is essentially restless — it wanders away from the keynote through all sorts of dissonant intervals and then returns to the keynote, and that return is meant to be the musical analogue of the Will achieving satisfaction.

Here's the question. Schoenberg and the second Viennese school came along decades after Schopenhauer died and basically abandonded the whole tonal system: no tonic to return to, dissonance treated as just as stable as consonance, melody no longer organised around a home note. If Schopenhauer had lived to hear, say, the first atonal Schoenberg pieces or something like the Five Orchestral Pieces, what do you think he would have made of them? I'm not asking for the vague answer that he'd hate it because he was conservative. Work through the actual machinary of his account. Would atonality even count as music on his view, given how much weight he puts on the melody's return to the keynote? Or could he argue that atonal music is in some ways a better copy of the Will, because the Will itself never really achieves lasting satisfaction, so music that never resolves would be more honest about the thing it's meant to copy? And what would he say about Schoenberg's idea that dissonance doesn't need to resolve — that it can stand on its own?

I want you to actually reason through the positions he could take, say which one is most consistent with his system, and flag where his system just doesn't determine an answer because the concepts he's working with (keynote, resolution, the hierarchy of bass, harmony and melody) are exactly the things atonal music throws away.
