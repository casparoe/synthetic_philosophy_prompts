Our safety lead wants to reject the pilot outright: she says this is the model supervising itself, and that the human judge will just rubber-stamp whichever side sounds more fluent, which the model is very good at producing. I need to send around a short memo before Thursday's meeting with a concrete recommendation—approve the pilot, reject it, or approve with conditions.
