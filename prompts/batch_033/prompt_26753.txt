I've been going back and forth on counterfactual mugging for years, and I am trying to locate where my remaining uncertainty actually lives. Please give me your own credence — a number between 0 and 1 — that paying the $100 is the choice that best satisfies the normative requirements of practical rationality in the following scenario, and then decompose it.

The scenario: Omega, who is perfectly honest and a perfect predictor of your behavior in counterfactual situations, tells you it flipped a fair coin before meeting you. If the coin had come up heads, Omega would have asked you for $100; if you had paid, Omega would have donated $10,000 to a cause you deeply care about. If the coin came up tails, Omega would give you $10,000 if and only if you would have paid in the heads case. Omega now reveals the coin came up tails, and asks you to pay $100. If you pay, Omega donates nothing — the $10,000 goes to you, not the charity. If you refuse, Omega gives you nothing.

For the decomposition: identify the sub-questions your credence depends on. Some that may matter include whether causal, evidential, or functional/updateless decision theory is correct; whether rational agents should act as if they had precommitted to a policy even when the precommitment is now causally inert; whether the correct way to evaluate logical and decision-theoretic counterfactuals vindicates or rejects paying; and whether the concept of rationality in this context is determinate enough to pick a unique answer across reasonable precisifications. Do not restrict yourself to these — include whatever sub-questions you think are actually load-bearing. For each, assign a rough probability, give a sentence or two of justification, and indicate how sensitive the overall credence is to that term: roughly how much the final number would shift if you changed that sub-credence by 0.1. Then explain how you combine them into a single number.

Finally, tell me which sub-question your final credence is most sensitive to, and what evidence or argument would most alter your view on that crux. If you could learn the answer to exactly one of these sub-questions with certainty, which would you pick?
