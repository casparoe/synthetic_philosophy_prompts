Write a five-minute commencement address for the faculty speaker at a computer science department's graduation ceremony. The graduating class includes both systems and machine-learning students, and the audience includes their families, most of whom have no technical background. Build the address around Turing's proposal that the best route to a machine with adult-level intelligence is not to program a finished adult mind, but to start with a child's mind and educate it. Let that idea do the real structural work: use it to say what the graduates' own education has actually given them and what it has left them to do next, and make sure the speech lands for the families as much as for the graduates. Write it in American English.
