Here's my opening: Look, I grant you the experimental data — people are swayed by trivial situational factors, and most of us aren't as consistent as we'd like to think. The empirical fact that people fall short doesn't touch the normative claim about what we should aim at. So what exactly is the situationist challenge to virtue ethics as a normative theory, as opposed to a descriptive psychology?
