The contradiction must arise from the impossibility of the social or practical arrangement, not from physical impossibility or a merely verbal stipulation. Then re-describe the same action under a second maxim that is equally natural and no more specific than an agent would need to make the action intelligible, and show that the contradiction disappears and the action passes the Formula of Universal Law.
