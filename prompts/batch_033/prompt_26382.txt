People keep talking about whether LLMs can lie or be deceptive. But like — an LLM is a next-token predictor. It doesn't have beliefs, it doesn't have communicative intentions, it doesn't have a self-model that it's misrepresenting. When it says something false, that's not a lie, it's just a wrong prediction. When it says something true, it's not honesty, it's just statistical. So why are AI safety researchers treating "honesty" and "deception" as if they're meaningful categories to apply to these systems?
