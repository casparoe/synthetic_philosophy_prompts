I'm on the board of a small domestic violence shelter. We have a written policy that staff must act with honesty and integrity in all their dealings. Last week, an abusive partner showed up at the shelter demanding to know if his wife was there. The staff member at the front desk told him she wasn't — but she was. The woman is now safe and has been moved to another location, but the board is split on wether to discipline the staff member. Half of us think the lie was clearly the right thing to do and disciplining her would send a terrible message. The other half says if we let this slide we're basically saying our integrity policy means nothing, and what happens next time someone lies for a less clear-cut reason? I need to draft a short decision memo for the board that recommends whether to discipline her or not, with reasons, and it needs to take the other side's concerns seriously.
