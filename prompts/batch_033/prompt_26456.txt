Teleosemantic theories attempt to naturalize mental content by grounding it in biological function. A central challenge for such theories is the disjunction problem: if a representational state is reliably caused by both X and Y, why is its content not simply X-or-Y? Please explain how a consumer-based teleosemantics — in which the content of a representation is determined by what its downstream consuming mechanisms need it to indicate in order to perform their proper functions — attempts to solve this problem. Be sure to clarify the role of Normal conditions and proper function in the account, and discuss at least one significant limitation of the consumer-based approach.
