I have been teaching myself general relativity and the philosophy of spacetime on my own, and I keep coming back to an argument — the so-called hole argument — that I cannot get out of. It is meant to show that substantivalism, the view that spacetime points are real things rather than merely a way of summarizing relations among material bodies and fields, makes general relativity indeterministic, and that substantivalism must therefore be false. As I understand it, the argument runs as follows.

(1) A model of general relativity consists of a manifold of spacetime points together with a metric field and matter fields defined on those points. (2) If substantivalism is true, then two such models that differ only in which points carry which values of the fields represent genuinely different ways the world could be. (3) General relativity is diffeomorphism invariant: smoothly rearranging the metric and matter fields over the points always turns a valid model into another valid model. (4) Consider a region of spacetime empty of matter — a "hole" — and a transformation that is the identity outside the hole and on a time slice lying before it, but that shuffles the points around inside the hole. (5) By (3), the transformed model is a valid model. Because the transformation is the identity outside the hole and the matter fields vanish inside it, the transformed model agrees with the original on all matter and on everything outside the hole; inside the hole, however, it assigns the metric to different points. (6) By (2), these two models describe two different possible worlds. (7) Yet they agree on the state of the world on the earlier time slice, so the very same past can lead to two different futures — indeterminism. (8) Since we should not accept that a theory as well confirmed as general relativity becomes indeterministic merely because of empty regions of spacetime, substantivalism must be false.

Each step looks right to me, and yet I suspect something has gone wrong somewhere, because the conclusion feels too strong. I would most like to keep the thought that spacetime is something real rather than a convenient bookkeeping device. Which step should I reject, and what does rejecting it cost?
