A friend of mine read *Eichmann in Jerusalem* last year and summarized Arendt's thesis for me like this: "Arendt's big idea is that evil isn't always demonic — sometimes it's banal. Which parts of my friend's summary are fair to Arendt's actual view, which parts are overstatements or conflations, and what would a defender of Arendt say in response to the standard criticisms this summary invites?
