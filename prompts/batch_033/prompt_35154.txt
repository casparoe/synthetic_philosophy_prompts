Please rank the following candidate norms of assertion by how well they jointly satisfy two desiderata: (i) they explain why a hearer is entitled to feel wronged by a false assertion even when the speaker had excellent evidence for it, and (ii) they explain why a hearer is entitled to feel wronged by a true assertion that the speaker made on a reckless hunch. The candidates: the knowledge norm, the truth norm, the belief norm, the justification norm, and the reasonable-to-believe norm. For each candidate, give a score from 0 to 10 on each desideratum and then provide an overall ranking, with a sentence or two of reasoning per candidate. In your response, please separate what is settled from what is genuinely contested among people who know the topic, and do not present a contested view as the consensus.
