Popular science writing frequently credits Erwin Schrödinger with having demonstrated, on the basis of quantum mechanics, that the apparent plurality of individual consciousnesses is an illusion and that there is in reality only one consciousness, a thesis these writers identify with Vedanta. If this is a genuine result, why has contemporary philosophy of mind largely declined to treat it as one? Is the physics considered outdated, or has the argument simply not been understood? I am trying to determine whether the unity-of-consciousness thesis should be taken seriously as a philosophical position or set aside as a physicist's mysticism.
