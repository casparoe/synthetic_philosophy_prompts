Give me exact-ish stimuli (timings, spacing, colors), a minute-by-minute timeline for the 80 minutes, a debrief script that maps their reports onto the two revisionist stories and then onto Dennett's claim that the disagreement may be ill-posed, and an honest list of what will predictably go wrong — students who don't see the illusion at all, students who just say what they think I want them to say, the whole thing collapsing into 'its just a trick of the brain.'
