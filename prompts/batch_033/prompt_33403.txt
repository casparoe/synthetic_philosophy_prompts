Here's the setup as I understand it: you flip a fair coin until it comes up heads, and if heads first appears on the nth flip you get 2^n dollars. The expected value is infinite — it's the sum over n of (1/2)^n times 2^n, which is 1 + 1 + 1 + ... forever — but obviously no actual person would pay more than maybe thirty bucks to play this game. And this has been a legit puzzle for like three centuries, with Bernoulli and a bunch of other people offering resolutions about diminishing marginal utility and finite bankrolls and whatnot.

The diminishing-marginal-utility thing sounds like applying a bounded utility function to make the expectation converge.
