I'm writing a novel and my protagonist is a moral philosophy professor — the kind who can't help turning every conversation into a seminar. She's made a bunch of offhand judgments about what people owe each other over the course of the manuscript, and I've been tracking them becuase I need her to be consistent. She'd notice if she contradicted herself, and so would the kind of reader who annotates.

Here's what she's said so far:

- Told a friend he was morally required to donate 10% of his income to effective charities, and wasn't gentle about it.
- When another character gave away 50% of her income, she called it "saintly" and "beyond what anyone could demand of a person."
- Judged someone harshly for not jumping into a pond to save a drowning kid, even though it would've ruined a $2,000 suit.
- Told a student that donating a kidney to a stranger is "heroic, not obligatory."
- Said a strong swimmer on an overcrowded lifeboat had a duty to get in the water and swim for shore, even though it was risky.
- Scoffed at the idea that someone should quit a tenure-track job to dig wells in rural areas, calling it "a failure to respect the division of moral labor."

Now I'm writing a scene where she's asked about bone marrow donation. A stranger will die without a match, and the person asking is one of very few matches. The procedure involves significant pain, a few days of recovery, and a small risk of complications. The character asking her is trying to decide whether they have to do it.

I need to know what my protagonist would say. Is it a duty, or is it supererogatory? I'm less interested in the bare answer than in the rule she's actually running — there's clearly more than one rule that fits her verdicts so far, and I want to know which ones they are and where they diverge on the marrow case.

Give me a JSON object with these fields:

- "predicted_verdict": either "required" or "supererogatory", your best guess at what she'd say.
- "candidate_rules": an array of strings, each describing a rule that fits all six of her past verdicts. Don't give me rules that are just disjunctions of her verdicts — they should have some independent rationale.
- "rule_predictions": an object mapping each candidate rule (use the same strings) to what it says about the bone marrow case and why.
- "best_guess_rule": which rule you think she's actually running, with a sentence or two on why it fits her vibe better than the alternatives.
- "distinguishing_case": a case I could write into the novel — a scenario where the candidate rules come apart — that would force her to reveal which one she really holds. Describe the case and say what each rule predicts.
- "closest_analog": which of her six past verdicts is the nearest precedent for the bone marrow case, and whether it pulls toward required or supererogatory.
