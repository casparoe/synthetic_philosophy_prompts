I've been down an anthropics rabbit hole lately and I'm trying to figure out what I actually believe by going case by case, because the labels people throw around — SSA, SIA, halfer, thirder — don't seem to capture my particular bundle of intuitions. Here are my gut verdicts. Sleeping Beauty: when she wakes up mid-experiment, I think she should still say the coin is fifty-fifty; nothing about waking up tells her anything new about the coin. The one-versus-two case: God tosses a fair coin; heads, he creates one person in one room; tails, he creates two people in two rooms. You wake up in one of the rooms knowing the setup. I want to say the coin is fifty-fifty — the fact that tails would have made an extra person doesnt make tails more likely to be the world I'm in.

But then I get pulled the other way. I don't think the doomsday argument works: my being alive now, at some particular birth rank, shouldn't push me toward expecting humanity to end sooner rather than later. It feels like that argument only gets its force by quietly assuming I should be surprised to find myself early. Yet the presumptuous philosopher case seems to push in the opposite direction: if two cosmological theories are otherwise identical except that one predicts the universe will contain a trillion observers and the other predicts a billion, and I know I exist, I feel like the trillion-observer theory is much more likely — my existence is much easier to explain on it. So in the doomsday case I refuse to let my existence be evidence about how many observers there will be, and in the philosopher case I let it be exactly that. Can I hold both, or is one of these intuitions doing the real work?

Is there a single principle behind all of these, or are some of my verdicts just inconsistent? If I can't keep them all, what principle gets the largest subset, and what does it say about a case I haven't mentioned: Beauty is told ahead of time that if the coin lands heads she'll be woken once, and if tails she'll be woken a million times — should she still say fifty-fifty on waking, or should the million matter? And which of my listed verdicts does the best principle force me to give up?
