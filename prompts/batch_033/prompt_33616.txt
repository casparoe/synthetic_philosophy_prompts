So I need the halfer/thirder disagreement to manifest as a disagreement about action.

Design me a Sleeping Beauty variant where the halfer and thirder recommend different actions upon awakening, with these constraints: the "coin toss" has to be something narratively organic — a medical test result, whether someone said yes to a proposal, whether a job came through — something a person would actually wait to hear about. The action has to be something with emotional weight that a character in literary fiction would face, not a bet or a probability estimate. Memory loss has to be mundane too: anaesthesia, concussion, a blackout, not a sci-fi memory wipe. One paragraph for the scenario.
