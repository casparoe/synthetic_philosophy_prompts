Here are the verdicts I've collected from her (I've been keeping notes):

She says this person does have the virtue of kindness, though it's imperfect. The bad-mood lapses don't cancel the attribution; they just mean the virtue isn't fully stable.

The tax domain is too central to cordon off — it's not some exotic, isolated failure. So they don't have the virtue of honesty.
