- Neo-Nazis rally in a public park, swastikas, racist chants, the works. She says let it happen.
- A church preaches that homosexuality is a sin and lobbies against same-sex marriage. She says they keep their tax exemption.
- A group publishes the home addresses of abortion doctors and urges followers to "confront them at home." She says shut it down.
- A group urges followers to bomb a specific federal building during a named upcoming event and circulates the floor plan. She says prosecute.
- A stand-up comic does a routine that's basically just racist jokes, at a public venue. She says let him perform.

Now there's a new case she hasn't weighed in on. The site says "we must expose them, name them, and drive them from public life." It never explicitely calls for violence. But several people on the list have recieved death threats from readers, and one was physically attacked.

What do you think she'll say about the website? And what's the rule she's running — is it "speech is protected unless it foreseeably causes harm to identifiable individuals," or is it "speech is protected unless it's directed at inciting imminent lawless action," or something else?
