My friend and I just spent three hours arguing about whether AI-generated images can be creative, and I realized her position is more coherent than I gave it credit for, but she's definately running a rule I can't quite state. She gave verdicts on a bunch of cases:

- A painter who spends ten years developing a visual style nobody else was working in: creative, obviously.
- An art student who makes a technically flawless copy of an old master painting: not creative, it's a copy.
- A neural net trained on thousands of portraits that outputs a new portrait in that same style, indistinguishable from a competent human's: not creative, it's just interpolating.
- A photographer who writes a very specific prompt, generates 500 images, picks one, and then edits it for weeks: the photographer is creative, the model isnt.
- A composer who writes a piece by rolling dice to pick notes from a table they designed, then heavily revises the result: creative, the dice are just a tool.
- An AI that mutates its own weights at random and has a human select the most interesting output each generation: the human is creative, the AI isn't.

Then I gave her this case: an autonomous system given only the goal of producing a body of work no human could have predicted from its training set. It sets its own subgoals, generates work, critiques it against standards it derived from art history and then keeps modifying those standards, and iterates for months with no human in the loop. Critics later describe the output as a genuinely new development, and several say the system itself, not its programmers, is the artist. She refused to answer and said I was trying to trap her.

So: what would she most likely say about that last case, if she's being consistent? I'm less interested in the bare yes/no than in (a) the rule she seems to be running, (b) the other rules that fit her verdicts just as well, (c) what each of those rules would say about the autonomous-system case, and (d) one further case that would best tell the leading candidates apart. Give your best guess with reasons. End with a last line that begins "Verdict:" and then either "creative" or "not creative" for the autonomous system.
