write a villanelle about the two-envelope paradox. the two refrains should be the two voices of the paradox: one that says you should switch (because the expected value of the other envelope is higher), and one that says the whole argument must be wrong (because by symmetry you'd just switch right back). the form should reflect the paradox — the refrains keep circling around each other, alternating, never resolving, and when they finally land together in the last two lines it should feel like the paradox has tightened rather than loosened. someone who knows the problem should be able to tell which refrain is which and why they're locked together. dont just name-drop the paradox; make the reasoning live in the poem.
