This seems to be exactly the same behavior described in two different vocabularies. The philosopher's "better judgment" corresponds to the agent's long-run preference, and the "acting against it" corresponds to the preference reversal that the hyperbolic discount function predicts. Is there any feature of weakness of will that the discounting model fails to capture, or are these just two names for the same thing?
