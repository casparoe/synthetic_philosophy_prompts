1. A man who is currently having an affair blames his brother for cheating on his wife. → Lacks standing.
2. A woman who had an affair years ago, ended it, confessed, and rebuilt her marriage blames a friend for infidelity. → Has standing.
3. A person who routinely exaggerates their accomplishments on their CV blames a politician for lying about policy details during a campaign. → Lacks standing.
4. Someone who stole sweets from a corner shop as a child and never stole again blames a colleague for expense fraud. → Has standing.
5. A landlord who charges above-market rent on a poorly maintained flat blames a developer for gentrifying a neighbourhood and displacing long-term residents. → Lacks standing.

And if you think my existing verdicts actually commit me to something I haven't noticed, tell me that too.
