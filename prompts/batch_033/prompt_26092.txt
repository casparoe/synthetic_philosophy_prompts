I've been going around in circles on the Sleeping Beauty problem and now I've confused myself even more by thinking about a bunch of related cases. I'm hoping you can look at my gut reactions to these and tell me what principle, if any, they're pointing to — and which ones I might need to give up.

Here are my verdicts, as honestly as I can report them:

(1) Standard Sleeping Beauty. Fair coin, Sunday. Heads → wake Monday only. Tails → wake Monday and Tuesday, with the drug that erases memory of the previous awakening. When Beauty wakes up, her credence in Heads should be 1/3. I'm settled on this.

(2) Many awakenings. Same setup but Tails → 100 awakenings. Credence in Heads on waking: 1/101. Feels right — if it's tails I'm 100 times more likely to be having this experience, so I should be pretty confident it's tails.

(3) God's coin toss. God flips a fair coin. Heads → He creates one person in a windowless room. Tails → He creates two people, each in their own identical windowless room. You wake up in such a room. Credence in Heads: 1/3. Same structure as (1), same answer.

(4) You are person #1. Same as (3) but each created person gets a label — Person 1, Person 2, etc. — and you learn you're Person 1. Since Person 1 exists under both heads and tails, my credence in heads goes straight back to 1/2. This also seems right.

(5) The big universe. Two physical theories with equal prior probability. Theory A: the universe contains exactly 100 conscious beings. Theory B: exactly 100 billion conscious beings. You exist, and you have no special evidence about which one you are among them. My gut says: no update — stay at 1/2. The fact that there are vastly more people under Theory B doesn't feel like evidence for Theory B; you'd find yourself existing either way, and it's not like you drew your existence randomly from an urn.

And here's the thing eating at me: (3) and (5) look structurally almost identical. In both, one hypotheis has more "slots" you could occupy, and you have no information narrowing down which slot you're in. So why do I want to update to 1/3 in (3) but stay at 1/2 in (5)? And case (4) seems to block the update by giving me exactly that kind of narrowing information — but in (5) I don't have it, so shouldn't the logic of (3) force me to say Theory B is overwhelmingly more likely? That feels crazy.

Is there a single principle about self-locating evidence that gets all of (1)–(5), or close to it? And if not, which verdict should I abandon? Also, what does your principle say about this: same as (5), but you learn you're among the first 100 conscious beings in the universe. Does your credence in Theory A go up, and by how much?
