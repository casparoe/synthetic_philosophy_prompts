Give me the desiderata, each with the case or consideration that makes it a requirement, and flag which are non-negotiable and which are camp-specific (systematists, population geneticists, paleontologists, conservation biologists). Include distinctions a theory can't collapse — like between a criterion for recognising species and a definition of what they are, or between species being real units in nature and being useful units for classification — and any results from population genetics or microbiology it can't contradict. If some subset of the desiderata can't be jointly satisfied, say a criterion that works for asexuals plus the requirement that species be the units that play the causal role in evolution, I want to know, because that's the actual argument of my paper.
