i want to audit this analogy. what maps onto what between the AI case and the zombie case? where does the mapping break? which breaks actually matter for the conclusion and which are just incidental? and does the anti-physicalist conclusion hold up once the disanalogies are on the table, or was the analogy doing more work than it looked like?
