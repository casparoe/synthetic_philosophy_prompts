I'm giving a talk at a legal theory workshop next month. My thesis is that Nomic-style self-amending rule systems expose a real problem for Hartian legal positivism: if a legal system's rule of recognition can change through ordinary legal processes (not just through a revolutionary shift in official practice), then you get paradoxes where a rule authorizes its own abolition, and positivism doesn't have the resources to say whether the abolition was valid or not. The audience is mostly Hartian positivists, a couple Dworkinians, and one guy who does formal models of norm change. I need a Q&A prep sheet: the objections I'll actually get from this crowd, phrased how they'd phrase them, ordered by how much trouble they'd cause me, with a two-minute reply for each and an honest note on where the reply is soft. And tell me the question I should be praying nobody thinks to ask.
