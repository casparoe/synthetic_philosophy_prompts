The claim seemed to be that the consulting job is obviously the more ethical choice because you can donate the difference, but I'm not sure whether that's actually what the slogan says, or whether it's even true on any precisification. And then tell me which reading the consulting-job argument actually needs, and whether that reading survives. I want a list I can bring to the next meeting.
