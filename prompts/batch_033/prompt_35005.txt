I've been reading the evolutionary debunking literature in metaethics — Street, Joyce, the responses from realists like Shafer-Landau, Enoch, and FitzPatrick — and I'm struck by how little the dial has moved since the mid-2000s. The basic shape of the thing is: debunkers say our moral intuitions were shaped by evolutionary pressures that didn't track moral truths, so moral realism is undermined unless you can show the two coincidentally align. Realists respond either that evolution *did* track moral truths (because true moral beliefs were adaptive), or that the debunking argument is self-defeating (it would also debunk our perceptual or mathematical beliefs, which nobody wants), or that moral truths are conceptually necessary so evolutionary explanations are just the wrong kind of thing to be relevant. Debunkers have replies to all of these. Realists have replies to the replies. And so on.

What I want to know is: what kind of disagreement is this, actually? It doesn't feel like a factual disagreement about what the evolutionary pressures were — both sides seem happy to stipulate the empirical story for the sake of argument. It doesn't feel like a disagreement about the logic of debunking either — everyone seems to get the structure fine. My hunch is that it's really a disagreement about the burden of proof: realists think the debunker needs to show that moral truths *couldn't* have been tracked by evolution, while debunkers think the realist needs to show that they *were*. But maybe I'm wrong and it's something else — a disagreement about what counts as a defeater for a belief, or about whether moral beliefs are analogous to perceptual ones, or just a clash of bedrock intuitions about how weird it would be for evolution to track non-natural facts.

Diagnose this. What's the real structure of the impasse, and given your diagnosis, is there anything — an empirical finding, a conceptual distinction, a formal result — that could actually move things? Or is this one of those debates where we should just accept that smart people with different priors will never converge and move on with our lifes?
