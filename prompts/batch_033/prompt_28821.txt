ok so I'm taking this phil of perception class (it's called "Seeing and Believing" which is cheesy but whatever) and we've been deep in the cognitive penetration literature for like two weeks. The basic idea is that your beliefs and expectations can actually change your perceptual experience, not just your judgment about it — like, you literally see the banana as more yellow when you expect a banana, or you see two objects as more similar in color when you're told they belong to the same category. It's not just bias in how you report what you saw; the claim is it changes the seeing itself.
