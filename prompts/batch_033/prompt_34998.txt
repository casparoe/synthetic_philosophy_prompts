A metaphysician who defends David Lewis's counterpart theory and a metaphysician who defends transworld identity have been arguing past each other at conferences for years. They have just completed a six-month adversarial collaboration. Draft the joint statement they would produce.

The document should include: (1) a numbered list of claims both can sign, covering what possible-worlds semantics requires, what it does not require, and what modal predications both agree need explaining; (2) a disagreements table where each row states a precise point of divergence, phrased so that each side would accept the wording of its own column; (3) a list of cruxes—sub-questions such that settling them would shift at least one side—and for each crux, a description of a case, argument, or piece of linguistic data that both agree bears on it, with each side's prediction about what that evidence will show. Pay particular attention to whether any part of their dispute turns out to be verbal rather than substantive, and flag it if so.
