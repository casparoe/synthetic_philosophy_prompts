I'm preparing for a seminar on the language of thought hypothesis, and before I read the next round of papers I want the evaluative framework settled rather than the positions themselves. The question I want desiderata for is the one LOTH is an answer to: what kind of vehicle do propositional thoughts ride in — sentence-like structures in a mental language, distributed activation patterns, maps, or something else? Please write the desiderata that any adequate theory of the format of propositional thought must meet, where a desideratum is a phenomenon the theory must explain or explain away, a distinction it cannot collapse, or an empirical constraint it cannot ignore. For each desideratum, give the phenomenon or case that earns it its place, and say whether you take it to be non-negotiable or merely a local commitment of one research program.

I am especially interested in whether any two requirements pull in opposite directions — for instance, a demand that thought be compositional, systematic, and productive versus a demand that the proposed format be implementable in the actual neural hardware — and, if so, which one you would relax first and why. Give exactly five numbered desiderata, each a sentence or two, with no introduction or conclusion; the list itself should carry the argument, so make each item earn its place. After the list, add one further sentence telling me which of the five you are least confident belongs on the list, and what argument or empirical finding would most likely lead you to demote or replace it.
