Galen Strawson's Basic Argument says you can't be ultimately morally responsible for anything you do because to be responsible for what you do you'd have to be responsible for the way you are, and that regresses infinitely. A common reply is that we shape our own characters over time through our choices — I wasn't born patient, I became patient by repeatedly choosing to act patiently, so I am responsible for being patient now. Please argue that this reply fails against the Basic Argument, and do it in a way that engages with the strongest version of the reply, not a strawman. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentance and then give the reasoning, with no preamble and no restating of the question.
