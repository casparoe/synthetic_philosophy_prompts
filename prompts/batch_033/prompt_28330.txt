I have spent much of my career defending a broadly Humean account of laws of nature, and I have now refined it to the point where I want a clear account of what it would take to move me off it. My position is this. The fundamental facts of the world are exhausted by the spatiotemporal distribution of perfectly natural, categorical local properties: there is no primitive nomic necessity, governance, or production at the fundamental level. Laws of nature are not further entities that push the world around; they are the theorems of the axiomatic system that best balances simplicity and strength in systematizing the entire mosaic — the Ramsey–Lewis best-system account, with the usual refinements. I also accept the Lewisian extension to objective chance: a world has the chances it does because the best system, constrained by the Principal Principle, assigns probabilistic transitions over the course of the mosaic. Counterfactuals, causal relations, and dispositions are likewise non-fundamental and supervene on the mosaic.

What I want is a map of the view's exposure, not a survey of familiar objections. What would count against this package, and which items would be decisive rather than merely awkward? I would like the defeaters ranked from fatal to merely awkward, and I want an honest statement of which of them a committed defender of the view would themselves concede as counting against it — Lewis himself repeatedly revised the account, and I would like to know which of those revisions should be read as responses to genuine counterevidence and which as defensive qualification. Are there empirical findings or formal results that the best-system account predicts or rules out that its rivals do not? And if the view has been, or can be, insulated so thoroughly that nothing could count against it, tell me plainly, and say whether that is a defect or a feature of a view of this kind. I am particularly concerned that the problem of undermining futures for Humean chance may be more serious than my colleagues think, and I want that suspicion checked rather than indulged.
