Draft the joint statement that a non-deflationary realist about phenomenal consciousness and a deflationary illusionist could both sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration focused on the meta-problem of consciousness: the problem of explaining why creatures like us have the intuitions, judgments, and verbal reports that together constitute our sense that there is a hard problem of consciousness. The statement should open with a numbered list of claims both sides accept about the target phenomenon itself — for instance, claims about the apparent explanatory gap between physical and functional descriptions and experience, about the conceivability of zombies, about the knowledge intuition, about the robustness and cross-situational stability of these intuitions, and about their role in generating philosophical puzzlement. It should then give a disagreements table in which the residual disagreement is stated exactly, with each side's position phrased in wording its own proponents would accept, so that neither side can object that its view has been caricatured. After that, identify the cruxes: the sub-questions such that a settled answer would move at least one side. For each crux, specify a concrete case, argument, or experimental finding that both sides agree bears on it, and record each side's prediction in advance. Likely cruxes include whether our phenomenal concepts are transparent in a way that licenses treating the apparent gap as real or whether they are opaque and referential; whether a debunking explanation of our modal intuitions would undercut the modal claims made by the debunking theory itself; and whether a system could exhibit the full pattern of problem intuitions while lacking phenomenal consciousness, and what each side predicts about such a case and about the justificatory status of those intuitions. If you judge that part of the dispute is merely verbal, say which part and why. The deliverable should be a constructive document both parties could put their names to, with the residual disagreement made exact rather than softened.
