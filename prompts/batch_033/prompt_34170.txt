Write an essay on whether Darwin's and Turing's "strange inversions of reasoning" are best understood as two facets of a single phenomenon — what Dennett calls "competence without comprehension" — or whether the analogy between natural selection and mindless computation is ultimately misleading. Weigh the strongest arguments on each side. In favor of the parallel: natural selection yields the appearance of design without foresight or understanding, just as a computing machine can produce correct arithmetic without knowing what arithmetic is. Against it: organisms are not designed artifacts, and computation operates on symbols whose interpretation is supplied from outside. Do not end on a non-committal note; defend a verdict, and consider what your verdict implies for how we should think about contemporary artificial intelligence.
