I host a small philosophy podcast, and next week's guest is a philosopher who's been defending a Strawsonian transcendental argument for other minds — the core idea being that the capacity to self-ascribe mental states somehow presupposes the concept of other subjects of experience, so radical skepticism about other minds isn't just false but incoherent. I want questions that get into the weeds, not just have him restate the view. Draft eight to ten interview questions. For each of these, include a follow-up that anticipates the reply he's most likley to give. Also, end with a question about what would actually count as a counterexample to the transcendental claim — what kind of being or scenario would falsify the alleged necessary condition?
