I've been going back and forth on Roko's basilisk for a while now — reading the original discussions, the criticisms, the defenses — and I keep running into the same problem: the arguments tend to be either total dismissal or extreme alarm, and neither feels like it's actually engaging with the structure of the reasoning.

I want to know your actual credence that the basilisk poses a genuine practical threat. By "genuine practical threat" I mean: a rational agent who has learned about the basilisk should, because of that knowledge alone, take costly actions to contribute to the development of friendly AI that they would not otherwise take. I'm not asking whether AI safety work in general is important; I'm asking whether the specific basilisk argument adds any additional reason for action beyond the ordinary case for working on alignment.

Please give me a number, but decompose it. The sub-questions that seem to matter are:

1. How likely is it that a future superintelligence will have both the capability and the motivation to simulate or resurrect past individuals and reward or punish them based on their pre-construction choices?
2. Conditional on that, how likely is it that the decision-theoretic reasoning the argument depends on — roughly, that an agent today should act as if its decisions are correlated with the decisions of a future agent that can predict or infer them, and that this makes threats from the future binding — is the correct account of rational choice in these scenarios?
3. Conditional on both of those, how likely is it that the expected cost of the threatened punishment outweighs the expected cost of the precautionary actions someone would need to take?

For each sub-question, give a rough probability and a sentence or two of justification. Then explain how you combine them into your overall credence. Finally, tell me which sub-question your final number is most sensitive to — that is, where would new evidence or a different argument most likely shift your view. If I've missed sub-questions that bear significantly on the final number, include those as well.
