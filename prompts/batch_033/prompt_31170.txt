Suppose that an original painting is reproduced with such fidelity that the replica is intrinsically indistinguishable from the original: every pigment particle, crack, and layer of varnish is duplicated exactly. Rank the following candidate grounds for valuing the original over the replica by how much independent justificatory work each can do once all intrinsic properties are held fixed: (a) the original's history of production, (b) the artist's creative achievement, (c) the original's aesthetic properties, (d) the original's role in communal and institutional practices, (e) the original's market value, and (f) the information the object carries. Give a strict ranking from strongest to weakest, with a one-sentence rationale for each placement, and indicate any grounds that you think collapse into one another or into considerations not listed.
