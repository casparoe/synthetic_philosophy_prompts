I'm a backend engineer at a mid-size SaaS company, and after a reorg I somehow ended up on our benefits committee. We're about to vote on a change to the 401(k). Right now new hires are not enrolled by default and have to opt in, and only about a third do anything in their first year even though the company matches 4%. The proposal is to auto-enroll everyone at 5% of salary, let people opt out with a single checkbox in the HR portal during the first 90 days, and then auto-escalate contributions by 1% a year up to 10% unless they opt out of the escalations too. The HR folks keep calling it a 'nudge' and say its just fixing a bad default, since people can leave whenever they want. A couple committee members think it's the company quietly making a financial choice for people who never asked for it, and they're especially worried about junior employees who'd rather have the take-home pay now, plus the auto-escalation piece pushing people past the match cap without them noticing. We've gone back and forth for two meetings and I got assigned to write the memo. Please draft the short written recommendation I'll circulate before Thursday's vote: it should come down clearly as approve, reject, or approve with conditions, include a concrete time frame and any implementation conditions, and it should honestly engage the strongest objection to whichever position the memo ends up recommending rather than just restating the policy. After the memo, give me a JSON summary with exactly these fields: decision (one of "approve", "reject", or "approve_with_conditions"), conditions (array of strings, empty if none), strongest_objection (string), response_to_objection (string), key_uncertainty (string), and monitoring_plan (string).
