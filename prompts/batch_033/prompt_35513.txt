But when I pushed her to state the criterion that picks out the right stance, she wouldn't give me a clean principle. Instead, she offered verdicts on a series of cases. - Cat: intentional stance — "obviously you attribute beliefs and desires to a cat."
- E. coli bacterium: physical stance — "it's just chemistry."

- Honeybee: intentional stance — "it has a perspective, however minimal."
- Sunflower tracking the sun: physical stance — "that's a tropism, not agency."
- Spider: intentional stance — "same as the bee; it's got a point of view."
- Multinational corporation: intentional stance — "you can't predict what Exxon will do without attributing goals and beliefs to it as an entity."
