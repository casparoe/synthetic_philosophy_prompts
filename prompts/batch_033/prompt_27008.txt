I chair the prioritisation committee of a regional health authority. Two of our five localities currently receive a specialist pain-management programme that we cannot, within this budget cycle, extend to the other three. A proposal before the committee would withdraw the programme from the two localities that have it, on the ground that unequal access to relief is itself an injustice and that retaining the programme merely because it benefits some patients would entrench an unfair pattern; the freed resources would not be redeployed to the other three localities, and no alternative patient-facing use for them has been identified. Opponents reply that this would make some patients worse off and no one better off, and that removing a benefit without transferring it cannot make a distribution fairer. Half the committee regard the proposal as perverse, and half regard it as principled. Draft the committee's decision memo — recommend withdrawal, retention, or retention with conditions — with a clear course of action and time frame, and engage seriously with the strongest consideration on the losing side.
