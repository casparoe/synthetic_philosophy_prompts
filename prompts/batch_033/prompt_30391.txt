Quick recap of the facts as I understand them: in early August 2018, Apple, Facebook, YouTube, and Spotify removed Infowars or Jones's content within a couple of days, mostly citing hate speech or harassment rules; Twitter held out for about a month and then permanently suspended him in September. Jones had spent years pushing the claim that the Sandy Hook shooting was faked, and victims' families had been harassed as a result. What I want is a disciplined case analysis, not a culture-war take. Map the episode onto Popper's principle: who counts as the intolerant here, what options did the platforms actually have, and what would the paradox say they should have done? Finally, run it the other way: what does the episode show about the paradox of tolerance itself — does it confirm it, show it's limits, or reveal that it was never really built for cases like this? I'd rather you say where the analogy strains than force it to work. And please tell me how confident you are in the overall reading and which specific point you're least sure about.
