ok so ive been reading al-ghazali's occasionalism and i had this idea for a poem but im not a poet. can you write it for me?

the idea: a ghazal about occasionalism. for anyone who doesnt know the form, a ghazal is a sequence of couplets where each couplet is thematically self-contained but linked by a refrain (radif) at the end of the second line of every couplet, and a rhyme (qafiya) just before the refrain. the first couplet (matla) has the refrain at the end of both lines. the final couplet (maqta) traditionally includes the poet's name or pen-name.

heres why i think the form fits. al-ghazali held that god recreates the world at every instant — there's no necessary connection between what we call cause and effect, just god's habit of creating them together. the cotton doesnt burn because of the fire; god creates the burning at the moment of contact. so every moment is a new creation, and the apparent continuity of the world is just the regularity of god's will.

the ghazal form, with its self-contained couplets and the same refrain returning at the end of each, seems perfect — each couplet is like a discrete moment of creation, the refrain is the divine presence holding them together, and the independence of the couplets mirrors how each moment is ontologically separate from the last. the final couplet naming the poet could do something with the idea that even the poets act of composing is really gods creation — al-ghazali himself makes a move like this when he points out that his own writing of the argument is itself divinely caused.

so what i want: a ghazal of maybe 5-7 couplets that actually does this philosophical work, not just name-drops occasionalism. the refrain should be something that works as a theological anchor — maybe about the moment, or the act, or divine will. the individual couplets should give concrete images of cause-and-effect pairs (fire and cotton, the sun rising, a hand moving, water quenching thirst) and then undercut them, showing that the connection isnt necessary. someone who knows al-ghazali should recognize the view, and someone who doesnt should still get a poem that makes them feel the vertigo of a world with no necessary connections.

i dont care about strict meter in english since were translating the form, but the rhyme and refrain structure should be clear. and please make the final couplet really land — the turn where the poet realizes theyre not the author of the poem either, that even this ghazal is being created moment by moment.
