I've been reading the back-and-forth about indicative conditionals and I'm struck by how little anyone moves. Here's the shape of it as I understand it, simplifying a bit. On one side you have the Adams/Edgington line: the probability you assign to "If A then C" just is your conditional probability P(C|A), and the reason conditionals keep failing to behave like ordinary propositions is that they dont have truth conditions at all — they're a device for expressing your credence in C on the supposition that A. On the other side you have people who want a compositional semantics, so the conditional has to be a proposition with truth conditions, and Adams's thesis gets demoted to a claim about assertability or acceptance, with a pragmatic story about why the probability judgments come out looking the way they do. Everyone agrees on the central data — people's probability judgments for conditionals track conditional probability, and Lewis's triviality results show you can't have both a truth-conditional reading and Adams's thesis as a fully general law. And yet after decades the same moves get replayed: the truth-conditional side says the non-propositional view can't handle embeddings like "It's likely that if A then C," and the Adams side says those embeddings are being read back through a truth-conditional lens that begs the question.

My working theory is that the two camps arn't actually answering the same question — one is building a theory of when conditionals are acceptable or assertable, the other is building a theory of what conditionals contribute to truth conditions in arbitrary embeddings — and that "semantics" is doing double duty. But I cant tell whether that diagnosis is too quick, because each side explicitly thinks the other is wrong about the same thing, namely whether conditionals have truth conditions at all.

So: what kind of disagreement is this? Is it substantive, verbal, or two research programs answering different questions under one heading? And if it's partly verbal, what's the residual non-verbal core that could actually be settled, and what would settle it?
