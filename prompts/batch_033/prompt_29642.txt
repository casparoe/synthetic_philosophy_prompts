On the face of it, the lump of clay existed on Tuesday and persists through Wednesday; the statue, by contrast, comes into existence only on Wednesday. Yet on Wednesday, the statue and the lump occupy exactly the same region of space, share all their material parts, and are indistinguishable in their observable properties.
