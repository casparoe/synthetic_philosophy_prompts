Here's my opening. Even if I grant UDT and acausal trade, the basilisk's threat only works if the future AI would actually follow through on punishing people who knew about it and didn't help. But at the point where it exists, punishing me is pure cost — it gets no information out of it, no deterrence benefit (nobody else can retroactively help), and it burns resources. A utility maximizer doesn't throw away utility for no payoff, so the threat isn't credible. And if the threat isn't credible from the inside, it was never a reason even under your own decision theory. So why shouldn't I just ignore the whole thing?
