Ok so I got scooped and I need an outside read on an argument, because its been bothering me for weeks.

The situation: a group I've never heard of posted basically the main result of my third chapter about six weeks before I was going to send mine out. The reason they beat me is that I sat on it for a year (partly my advisor, mostly me). I've been in a mood about it, and my friend, who is a philosopher and cannot help himself, has decided the mood isn't just unhelpful but false. He built an argument:

(1) Someone deserves credit for a result only if the result wouldn't have happened without them. He calls this the no-you-no-it test.
(2) When a problem is ripe -- the tools exist, the data is public, a dozen capable people are circling -- the result would have happened without any one of those people.
(3) That's what all the simultaneous-discovery stories are: two or three people landing in the same place within months. The calculus, natural selection, the telephone. It's the rule, not the freak event.
(4) So in the ripe cases, no individual deserves credit. The credit belongs to the field, or to the moment.
(5) And none of this is special to science. If someone else would have done it anyway, you didn't earn it.
(6) So being first is a fact about the world, not about the person. My mood is ego and I should get over it.

Two things bug me. First, he'd say exactly the same about the guy who scooped me, so I don't even get to be annoyed at anyone. Second, it isn't true that they were bound to be the ones -- somebody was bound to do it, but it turned out to be them because I was slow, which sounds like the opposite of inevitable. When I say that he just says "so you made them first, and you want a medal for that?"

Either there's a bad step in here and I can't see it, or there isn't and I have to live with (6). I'd most like to keep (3) -- I've watched this happen in my own subfield twice -- and (1) definately sounds to me like just what deserving means, so I don't know what I'm even allowed to give up.

Tell me which step does the damage, why it looked fine, and what it costs me to drop it. And if you think the thing is actually sound, say so, and tell me whats left of the idea of credit once I accept it.
