Say you've got a small pot of money to fund three people for two years on eliciting latent knowledge from AI systems -- the case where a model's behavior is best explained by it "knowing" some fact about the world, but it won't come out and say it, either because saying it gets penalized or because the knowledge was only ever implicit in what it does. My nagging worry is that most of the proposed methods (train a seperate honest model, get it to bet on things, check that paraphrases agree with each other) are really measuring a second model's beliefs about the first one rather than the first one's knowledge -- like trusting a coached witness to tell you what they actually saw. So which open conceptual questions here matter most, and which two or three would you actually hand a three-person team for two years? I want the reasoning more than the list: which questions are upstream of the others, which can be settled by thinking rather than by experiments we can't afford to run, and what would move one of them up or down the ranking.
