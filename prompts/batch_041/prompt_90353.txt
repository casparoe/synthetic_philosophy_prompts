Her big claim is that this is the WHOLE story: pick any strictly proper scoring rule (one where just honestly reporting your real probabilities always gets you the best expected score) and you can derive everything you'd want, and there's nothing left over — no extra requirement that your beliefs be based on evidence or that you be able to justify them or anything, all of that is just shorthand for accuracy considerations. My gut says something has definately gone wrong epistemically, but on her view you're a perfect epistemic agent, since your credences are as accurate as they could possibly be in every world.
