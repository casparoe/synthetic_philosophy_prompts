Some things I specifically want both of us pinned down on, because I think this is where the action is:

- The payoff-unit problem. A Dutch book has to be stated in some payoff currancy. If it's money, you need the agent to be roughly risk-neutral in money, which almost nobody is. If it's utility, you've helped yourself to the thing you were supposed to be deriving, since utility is standardly recovered from preferences by a theorem that already assumes probability. So: is the argument only about a narrow class of risk-neutral agents, or is it circular, or is there some third option? I want us both on record.
- The partition problem. EU maximization isn't even defined until you fix a partition of states, and you can get different rankings of the same acts under different partitions. Coherence arguments hand you a probability function over whatever algebra you give them; they don't tell you which algebra. Does rationality require a particular algebra, which one, and does any of these arguments say?
- The pump needs an extra premise. Diachronic pumps seem to need naivety, or a willingness to pay to switch, or some assumption about the agent's decision procedure (no precomittment, no policy of refusing trades). Synchronous Dutch books seem to need the agent to accept a whole bundle of bets at once, including ones she'd never be offered. Is that extra premise itself a rational requirement, or a substantive and optional assumption about agency?
- Idealization. The bookie is logically omniscient; the agent has to take bets on propositions whose implications she hasn't worked out. Does the argument say anything about us, or only about ideal agents -- and does that matter for whether an incoherent human is irrational?
- The triviality worry. If utility is just whatever makes your preferences satisfy the axioms, is "maximize expected utility" a substantive claim about rationality at all, or a definition dressed up as a theorem?
- Completeness. The representation results want a complete preference order. Is rational incompleteness possible, and if so does the pump show it's impermissible?
