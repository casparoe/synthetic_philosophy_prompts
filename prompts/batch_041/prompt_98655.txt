Im a novelist and Im stuck on my new book. Its about a guy who starts out pretty selfish and ends up genuinely good, and I cant decide whether that arc is plausible or just wishful thinking, so I went and read Mencius and Xunzi in translation and now I'm somehow more unsure than before. My friend who teaches this stuff says they're the two classic answers to whether people can become good, but the tidy summary I keep hearing (Mencius thinks we're good, Xunzi thinks we're bad) has to be hiding the interesting part.

So compare the two for me properly, at the level of the actual machinery and not the bumper sticker. What does each think a newborn is actually starting with? With Mencius, are the four beginnings (the sprouts) already little virtues in embryo, or just tendencies that could tip either way depending on what happens to them? And when Xunzi says our nature is bad, what exactly is he denying — that we're cruel, or just that we're unruly and need shaping? Then: where does each locate the cause of someone turning out badly (neglect and bad environment vs. untrained nature), and what does each take the actual mechanism of improvement to be — watering something that's already there, or deliberate effort plus ritual plus repetition building something that wasn't there at all?

I've also got my own dumb novelist's metaphor that I want you to test rather than just nod at: I think of Mencius as a garden (the plant is already in the ground, you just have to not starve it) and Xunzi as a gym (there's no muscle until you put in the reps, and the reps come from outside you — rituals, teachers, habit). Use it if it helps, but tell me where it distorts each of them, because I suspect it flatters Mencius.

Two more things. First, which of them handles the case of the person who's good instantly and without thinking better, and which handles the person who's only good after years of grinding practice — or is that a false split? Second, if you were building a character's moral arc and wanted it to feel true instead of sentimental, which of the two would you build it on, and what would each version of the arc actually look like on the page? Don't turn Xunzi into a cartoon — I want both of them at full strength.
