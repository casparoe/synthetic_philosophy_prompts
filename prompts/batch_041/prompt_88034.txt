Next Thursday I'm giving a talk to our stat mech reading group, and I want a Q&A prep sheet. The room is mostly people who do fluctuation theorems and nonequilibrium work relations, plus two quantum-information folks who think Landauer's principle is basically settled law. My thesis is that Maxwell's demon is not exorcised by erasure at all: the demon already incurs its entropy debt at the moment of measurement, because measuring leaves its memory correlated with the gas, and erasing is just one way of settling a debt that was incurred earlier. So any exorcism that points only at the reset step is bookkeeping, not physics. The bit I actually want to push, and the bit I expect to get shredded for, is this: if the demon is allowed to dump those correlations into a genuinely infinite cold bath, the debt never comes due, it can run forever, and the only thing stopping it is a ban on infinite reservoirs -- which nobody ever states as a principle, they just quietly assume it.

So: what will this room throw at me, in their own words, ordered by how much damage it does? I want the ones from the fluctuation-theorem crowd distinguished from the ones the quantum info people will bring, a two-minute reply for each, and an honest note on where my reply is soft. Tell me what to concede up front so I don't look like I'm dodging, and give me the one question I'm hoping nobody asks.
