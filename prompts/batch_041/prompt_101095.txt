I keep coming back to Clever Hans as the oddest test case for Descartes' beast-machine doctrine -- von Osten's horse, Pfungst's controlled trials, the finding that Hans was reading involuntary cues rather than doing arithmetic. Run it both directions -- what the case settles about the doctrine, and what the doctrine simply can't say about the case -- and be honest about whether the debunking refuted Cartesianism or only confirmed its mechanical half.
