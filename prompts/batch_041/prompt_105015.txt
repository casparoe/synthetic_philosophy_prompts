My opening move: GS only tells us that a strategy-proof, onto social choice function over three or more alternatives on an unrestricted domain is dictatorial. But no actual electorate is unrestricted. Put the voters on a single left-right axis with single-peaked preferences and the median voter rule is strategy-proof — no dictator, no profitable manipulation — so the theorem is a result about a domain no real society occupies. And if you move to score or approval ballots you're outside the theorem's scope anyway, since it's a statement about ordinal social choice functions. So which of those three do you actually deny?
