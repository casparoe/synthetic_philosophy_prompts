Ok so I'm not a philosopher, I just got weirdly obsessed with this stuff after an argument with my brother in law, and I've got an idea for a joke piece I want you to write for me. It's a job ad — the kind of thing you'd see on a company careers page — posted by the universe's HR department for the position of Law of Nature (or the officer who runs the laws, whichever works better). The whole joke is that HR are Humeans: they keep saying the laws arent orders from anyone, theres no author and no enforcer, a law is just a description of what everything already happens to do, the best summary of the overall pattern. But its still a job posting, so they still have to list duties, and thats where it falls apart — the new hire will be responsible for why things happen, will have to cover cases that never actually happen (what a particle would do if you moved it somewhere it isnt, and a law we have on file for a particle nobody has ever found), and will have to explain why a law is different from a total coincidence, like all the gold spheres in the universe happening to be under a mile wide. Throw in a bit where the anti-Humean applicants — the ones who say a law has to actually make things happen, not just sit there describing — demand a governing clause, and HR gets defensive and slightly annoyed about it. Deadpan corporate voice, bullet points for duties and qualifications, around 400 words, and please dont turn it into a dialogue between two philosophers: it should just be the ad. The humour has to come from HR not noticing they've advertised a job with nothing to do, so no wink at the end, no little moral.
