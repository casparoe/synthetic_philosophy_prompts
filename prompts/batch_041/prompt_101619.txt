So even a 1-in-a-thousand chance that a given insect has morally relevant experience makes insects the biggest animal-welfare issue on the planet by expected value. What I've noticed is that the friends who push back hardest dont push back on the numbers, they push back on the decision theory — and I've started to think the interesting question isn't "are insects sentient" but "does multiplying a single unresolved empirical fact by a giant N actually give you a quantity worth optimizing."
