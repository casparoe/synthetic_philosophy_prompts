The bit I keep getting stuck on is the holism. On the best-system view a regularity only counts as a law in virtue of how it slots into the single simplest-and-strongest summary of the entire mosaic of particular facts, which seems to mean that flipping one far-away electron could turn a law into a coincidence (or the reverse) even though no local pattern changed — lawhood ends up being a fact about the whole universe at once. The governing view, by contrast, looks more local: this regularity is backed by a necessitation relation, full stop, and it doesn't care what else happens to be true. I've also heard the simplicity/strength trade-off is somehow relative to the vocabulary you describe the mosaic in, which sounds like it ought to be a reductio.
