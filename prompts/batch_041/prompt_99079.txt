I have no philosophical training, but I have spent a few years reading about minds and machines on my own, and I would like someone to check whether the position I have ended up with actually holds together. Here is where I have got to.

1. A mind is a matter of what something does — how it takes in information, combines it, and produces behaviour — and not of what it is made from. Two systems that do the same thing with the same internal organisation have the same mind, whether the parts are neurons, silicon, or a roomful of people passing notes to one another.

2. So a state of mind is not tied to one kind of body. If an octopus can be in pain, and I can be in pain, then so could a machine, provided the inner workings were arranged the right way; the same state can be carried by very different stuff.

3. All the same, I cannot convince myself that a machine which behaved exactly as someone in agony behaves — flinching, crying out, pleading to be stopped — would necessarily be in agony. There seems to me to be a difference between the performance and the hurt, and I think a system could give the performance with nothing going on inside it.

4. When I am in pain I know it at once and from the inside, without inspecting anything, and it is hard for me to see how I could be mistaken about it.

5. And yet the hurt itself does not strike me as merely the sum of the causes and effects the state has. Knowing everything about what pain does inside a system would not, I feel, be the same as knowing what it is like.

6. Finally, I am quite sure that a thermostat has no beliefs and feels nothing, even though its states plainly do jobs.

Which of these can I keep together, which are quietly pulling against each other, and how badly? Please order the conflicts by how serious they are rather than treating them all as equally bad, and for each one say what I would have to give up and what that would cost me. Where two of my commitments only look like they clash but can in fact be reconciled, say so and explain how. And if there is a single change that would clear up the most trouble at once, tell me what it is.
