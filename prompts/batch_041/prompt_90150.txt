My buddy who does infosec says this is just defense-in-depth / threat modeling applied to models: treat the untrusted model the way you'd treat a compromised host or a malicious insider, assume breach, never rely on it cooperating. And he goes further — he says control is the whole ballgame, because you can't align something you don't understand but you can always contain it, same way you can't reason a virus into being nice but you can definitely quarantine it.

I want to know how far that security analogy actually holds, because it feels load-bearing for his conclusion.
