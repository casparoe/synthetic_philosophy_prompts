The consultant from the aid mission says it's sand in the gears — every envelope gums the machine a little more. And my aunt character insists the envelope at the wedding isn't a bribe, it's a tip, same as slipping the doorman a twenty or the points card at the shop: you pay a little and the relationship runs smoother.

Take these apart for me. For each one I want the correspondence table first — what plays the wheels, the grease, the friction, the driver, the load, the mechanic — then where the picture actually breaks. Do grease / tip / loyalty-card survive all four, or do some of them quietly only fit (a)?
