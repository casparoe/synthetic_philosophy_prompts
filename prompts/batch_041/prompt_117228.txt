I am a control engineer: ten years building state estimators and fault-detection and isolation systems for aircraft, so my working vocabulary is plants, sensors, actuators, reference signals and setpoints, error signals, observers and Kalman-type estimators, signal conditioning and calibration, redundancy with majority voting, residuals, fault detection and isolation, graceful degradation, and specifications. I have read enough philosophy of mind to have run into the family of views that tries to naturalize mental content by grounding it in biological function — teleosemantics, sometimes biosemantics. I can follow the debate but not trust my own glosses, so treat the following as my attempt at a statement of the view rather than as settled doctrine, and correct it if I have it wrong.

As I understand it, the core commitments are these. (1) A state carries content in virtue of the biological function of the mechanisms that produce and consume it, and function here is historical: a mechanism has the function it has because of the selection history that explains why it is there at all. (2) Because content is fixed by function rather than by actual correlation, content is normative — a state that fires when it should not still means what it is supposed to indicate, and that is what makes misrepresentation possible. (3) The producer side and the consumer side can come apart: what a state means may be fixed by what the consuming mechanisms need it to correlate with rather than by what the producing mechanism actually tracks. (4) The whole point is to naturalize aboutness — no designer, no stipulation, no irreducibly intrinsic directedness, just selection plus causal and teleological facts. (5) A consequence: a physical duplicate that assembles by accident and behaves indistinguishably from a creature that has a selection history has no content at all.

I want you to translate those commitments into my vocabulary, one at a time, as faithfully as the translation can be made. For each, report what goes through cleanly, what goes through only with distortion and what the distortion is, and what has no expression in my vocabulary at all. Then give me the residue report, and address these specific pressure points:

- My framework is saturated with supposed-to: a sensor is supposed to report the true state, an observer is supposed to converge, a detector is supposed to catch the fault. But every one of those traces to a specification that a designer wrote. Does the teleosemanticist's normativity survive the translation, or does it only appear to survive because the translation quietly imports the designer?
- My sense of function is a role in a working system, not a history. Can I express the historical sense at all — say, that this component is here because a selection process retained ancestors that did this — without turning the selection process into an optimization loop with an objective, that is, a designer under another name? If I cannot, is the history requirement doing any work that I can represent?
- In my world an estimate is wrong by comparison with the true state. Aboutness looks stipulated by the comparison rather than explained by it. Does that show the teleosemanticist is trying to explain something I merely presuppose, or does it show that my framework has no room for content as such, only for error and fault?
- Run the accidental-duplicate case on a control system: same dynamics, same wiring, assembled by chance. Every engineering claim I can make about it is identical to the claim about a designed one. Is the history requirement a real feature of content or an artifact of naturalizing without a designer?
- The consumer side tells me content is fixed by what the consumer needs. In my vocabulary the consumer's needs just are the control objective. What fixes the objective? If the view cannot say, does it bottom out in un-naturalized ends, and is that fatal or merely deferred?

End with your verdict: which parts of the residue indicate something my vocabulary genuinely cannot express, and which indicate that the original claim was less clear than it looked. I suspect teleosemantics may be a specification in search of a spec-writer; tell me whether that suspicion is right, wrong, or a misunderstanding of what the view is for.
