Then predict his verdict. Would he see such a model as a rival to the historian, a parody of one, or something he had no concept for? Would Ranke consider the model's output worse than White's literary imposition, or uncomfortably close to his own ideal? Which of Ranke's commitments does the new practice genuinely threaten, and which does it leave standing?
