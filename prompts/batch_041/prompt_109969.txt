Alright, I have to hand in a position piece for a seminar on markets in things people find repugnant, and I'd like someone to poke holes in it before I commit. Here's roughly where I've landed.

1. On markets generally I'm a liberal. If two competent adults both want a deal, the law should let them have it, unless it harms third parties or the consent isn't real (duress, deception, that sort of thing). Disgust isn't a reason to legislate. Neither is "it would change what the practice means" — wrongs get done to people's interests, not to practices.
2. So kidney sales: yes, legal, but regulated. Floor price, and a levy on buyers going into a fund for donors. Price gouging after a flood: legal, though I'd happily see the sellers shamed in the street.
3. Vote buying: absolutely not, banned outright, even when the voter is well informed and the money's going to a hospice. Same for paying someone to sit your driving test or your finals for you, even though I'll admit I can't find the third-party harm in the exam case.
4. I do think some things get corrupted by being priced. I'd think less of someone who paid a friend to be their friend, or who hired one of those firms that pays students to queue overnight so a client gets a seat at a congressional hearing. Something's gone wrong there. I just don't think it's the law's business.
5. What makes the vote different, I think, is that it isn't a thing you own, it's a share in a decision we're all making together. Selling it converts the practice into a market in influence, and that wrongs the other people in the practice, not just the seller.
6. And I don't accept the exploitation objection to kidney markets. Exploitation is about the background — the fact that the alternative is destitution — not about the deal itself.
7. Oh, and betting markets on elections should be legal. If people want to put money on who wins, that's information aggregation and it's their money.
8. Same for unlimited private spending on campaigns. It's speech, and I'd rather have more of it than less.

Someone in the seminar said I cant hold all of that at once, and now I'm worried they're right. So which of these actually pull against each other, and which only look like they do? Order the real clashes worst first, and for each one tell me the cheapest way to patch it and what I'd be giving up by patching it that way. I'd rather not lose (2) or (7), but I want to know the price either way.
