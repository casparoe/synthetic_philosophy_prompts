Her idea: a psychiatric category works less like a label that people notice and then react to, and more like a literary genre that patients, clinicians, insurers and memoirists gradually learn to write themselves into. Once enough of them have done it, the genre itself drifts — new subgenres appear, the old ones get parodied, some fall out of fashion entirely, and this can happen even when nobody in particular is being diagnosed. She reckons it does everything looping effects do and a bit more, especially the way symptoms turn up in clusters that look almost like stylistic conventions.
