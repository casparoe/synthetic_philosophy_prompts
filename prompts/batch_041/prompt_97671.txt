You get the money whether or not you actually drink it, and the catch is you know you won't drink it, so you can't form the intention. Everyone I've come across seems to think the juicy part is whether you can somehow trick yourself into intending. I think the juicy part is somewhere else and I can't quite pin it down.

My hunch: the whole puzzle quietly assumes you know your own future behaviour perfectly. Real people don't. So maybe the puzzle only really bites under an unrealistic assumption of perfect self-knowledge, and once you drop that it turns into a diffrent question — something about what it's rational to do when you're unsure what your own intentions will end up causing.
