Popper came up by name, twice. And I can't stop thinking about it, because in the same lecture we covered how courts cheerfully admit loads of expert stuff that isn't falsifiable in any obvious sense — handwriting comparison, some of the bite-mark people, a clinician's gut judgment that a defendant is dangerous, an economist's damages estimate. What I actually want to know is what this practice is presupposing philosophically.
