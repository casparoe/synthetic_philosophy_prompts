I co-teach a graduate seminar on explanation in the life sciences with a colleague who applies two standing tests to every explanation sketch we put in front of the room, and who has never had to say which of the two is doing the real work. The first test is that nothing may hang from a skyhook: no factor may perform explanatory lifting that is not itself accounted for by lower-level, mindless processes. The second is that the lifting must be done by cranes assembled stage by stage, so that an account moving from some elementary process straight to the finished capacity, without exhibiting the intermediate machinery, is one she rejects as greedy.

Her rulings over two terms:

- An account of sterile workers in social insects that traces how a sterility allele could spread through a specified structure of relatedness: accepted.
- An account of lactase persistence in dairying populations that runs through a feedback loop — the practice raises the payoff to digesting milk, which raises the frequency of the allele, which sustains the practice — with the loop's parameters specified: accepted.
- An account of a skeletal feature as the geometric consequence of two ordinary growth processes, with the interaction of those processes worked out in detail and no adaptive story offered: accepted.
- An account of a small island population's trait frequencies as the product of drift, with the demography and the sampling spelled out: accepted.
- An account of a trait that runs only "it would have been useful, so it appeared": rejected.
- An account of an intricately interlocked structure that runs "too complex to have arisen gradually, therefore designed": rejected.
- An account of language that runs "a single mutation installed the complete faculty, with all of its structure appearing at once": rejected.
- An account of a lineage's trajectory that runs "matter has an inherent tendency toward complexity": rejected.

Next week's sketch proposes that a complete novel structure appeared in a single generation when a fully characterized developmental switch was thrown by a known environmental change. Every step of the switch's operation is specified in molecular detail, no unexplained capacity is invoked, but there is no sequence of intermediate forms in the lineage and no cumulative selection.

Predict her verdict. Tell me which rules her record is consistent with and which of them fit it equally well, what each predicts about next week's sketch, which prediction you would bet on and why, and which further sketch would best separate the rules. If the record genuinely underdetermines the case, tell me what she would be committing herself to under each reading.
