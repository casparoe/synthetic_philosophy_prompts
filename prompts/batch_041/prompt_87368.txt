I write novels, and the one I am in the middle of has stalled on a single scene, because I cannot work out what a careful person would actually be doing in it. My protagonist, Ines, is a radiologist at the end of a night shift. On one scan she sees a faint asymmetry that she is confident is really there and confident is suspicious. Before she can dictate her report she learns that the hospital's quality committee has circulated an analysis of one particular failure mode: readers who have themselves missed a cancer within the previous month tend, in the weeks afterward, to over-call at roughly three times the base rate. Three weeks ago Ines missed one. The image on her screen is unchanged, and so is her sense that the asymmetry is there; nothing in how things look to her has shifted at all. She has a patient waiting and an invasive procedure to order or not order.

I tried this out on two friends over dinner, a litigator named Ruth and a philosopher named Daniel, and they went at it for an hour. Ruth said: nothing relevant has changed. The image is the same, her eyes are the same, her reading of the image is the same; what she has acquired is one more fact, a base rate for readers in her position. Facts get weighed. If the total now favors malignancy, she believes it and orders the biopsy; if the total does not, she does not. What she cannot do is keep the belief while telling herself she has no business holding it. A belief you have judged unwarranted is a belief you have already surrendered; anything else is a manner of speaking.

Daniel answered that this would be right if the new fact were a rival explanation of the shadow, but it is not; it is information about the instrument that produced her verdict. When I learn that the instrument is running high tonight, the fact that it still says "high" does not mean what it meant yesterday. Her verdict can survive as her best guess while the credit she extends to it collapses, and that is her position exactly: no one is asking her to weigh the committee's base rate against her own eyes, she is being told that her threshold has drifted, which is a fact about her and not about the shadow. So the accurate description of her state is that she believes the lesion is malignant and believes that believing so is, by her own lights, unreasonable. That is not a contradiction; it is what it is to be a knower with a known flaw.

Ruth came back with: then the flaw is fatal. If evidence can subtract from a verdict without ever entering the scales, every verdict can be undone by evidence about the process that produced it, including her verdict that the process is bad, and she is left with no non-circular place to stand. And look at where that description leaves her: she may hold the belief while conceding that holding it is unreasonable, but she may not act on an unreasonable belief without being unreasonable in acting, and she is not a spectator; she has a patient. So either the fractured state does not exist or it cannot do any work. One of us has to be wrong, and I would rather it were the description than the woman.

Daniel replied that the regress cuts the other way. If self-trust could never be locally withdrawn, a man told he had been given a hallucinogen could not rationally discount what he seems to see, since his reason for thinking he was dosed comes through the same eyes and ears, and nobody believes that. As for the action: the whole interest of Ines's position is that she must choose without the confidence that normally licenses choosing. She can order the biopsy as the lesser of two errors while judging her grounds for it poor, and that is not incoherent; it is what it is to act from a self-distrusting mind. If Ruth's account says that is impossible, the account is what should give.

I have no stake in which of them is right, and I genuinely cannot tell. Please give me exactly five numbered items, each a sentence or two, with no introduction and no closing summary. Items one through four should take the four exchanges in order and say who has the better of each, and why; item five should say who is closer to right overall, and what both of them are missing.
