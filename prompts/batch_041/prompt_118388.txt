I am assembling the desiderata for a theory of worst-case safety guarantees for AI systems — the kind of theory that would tell us what it means for a system's behaviour to be guaranteed safe against every environment it might meet, rather than safe on average over a distribution of environments — and I want the specification in hand before I evaluate any candidate theory, so please do not survey frameworks for me; give me the criteria. Write the requirements such a theory must meet, each with the case or consideration that motivates it, and add whatever I have failed to name. At a minimum I expect the list to cover: (1) the guarantee must be stated against an explicitly delimited adversary class, since "worst case" is empty until we say worst case for whom; (2) it must keep apart the worst case over the environment the system is dropped into and the worst case over the system's own cognition, because a system can be robust to a hostile world and still be the thing that does the harm; (3) non-vacuity — a guarantee must not be purchased by making the system inert or by drawing the adversary class so tightly that nothing is excluded; (4) composition — component-level guarantees must be able to combine into a system-level one, or the theory is silent about the systems we actually build; (5) decidability in principle — the theory must say what observation would count as the guarantee failing, so that it is not merely a promissory note; and (6) decision-relevance — the output must be usable by someone with a deployment decision to make, which is the purpose I care about. Then separate the genuine desiderata from the demands that are merely the house style of one camp — the ones that present themselves as requirements but are really commitments to a particular formalism or a particular threat model — and tell me which of the genuine requirements cannot be jointly satisfied, with the argument for the conflict. My suspicion, which I would like tested rather than confirmed, is that non-vacuity and worst-case-over-an-unrestricted-environment are the incompatible pair: that safety against an adversary permitted to do anything forces the system toward doing nothing, so that the whole area is a family of trade-offs and the real work lies in the choice of adversary class. If you think the conflict is instead an artefact of how the problem has been posed, say so, and say what would have to change for it to dissolve. Say how confident you are in the list as a whole, and single out the one requirement you are least sure belongs on it.
