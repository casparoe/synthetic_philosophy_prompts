A position I keep running into in conversation — usually offered as the sober, naturalistic alternative to anything that smacks of ghostly interpreters — explains mental content on the model of a smoke detector. The detector's internal state is said to be about smoke; when it fires at burnt toast it is not merely useless but wrong; and nothing anywhere reads or interprets the state, so no interpreter is required. Add a selection history and a downstream consumer that relies on the state, the argument runs, and you have aboutness: a frog's fly-detector and my belief that the kitchen is on fire are cases of the same kind, and content is installed by evolution or design rather than constituted by interpretation. A final step is often appended: rewire a device, or repurpose it, and let its users come to depend on it for something new, and it now means the new thing — content follows use.

I would like this analogy audited rather than assessed in the abstract. Set out the correspondence explicitly — what is supposed to map onto the detector, the smoke, the triggering, the false alarm, the consumer, the designer — and then say where the mapping fails. Part of what I want is a list of what the source has and the target may lack: the normativity of calling a state wrong as against calling it merely malfunctioning; the capacity to represent something never encountered; the difference between representing X and representing whatever happens to co-vary with X; the capacity to distinguish two descriptions of the same thing; and anything else you judge relevant. Separate the disanalogies that matter for the conclusion the analogy is being used to license from those that do not.

Then consider a case I have not seen discussed. A single production line turns out physically identical sensors, which are sold in different housings: one batch becomes smoke alarms, another becomes cloud detectors for an observatory, and years later a salvaged unit is wired into a bird feeder to register arriving birds. Assume the units are indistinguishable from the inside and their sensitivities identical. Does the state inside mean smoke, or cloud, or bird? If the answer depends on who is relying on it, has the interpreter the analogy was meant to dispense with returned — not as a ghost in the machine but as a user with purposes? If instead the answer is fixed by selection history, say which history is doing the work, and what becomes of a state whose history is ambiguous, mixed, or absent.

Close by saying whether the conclusion survives: that content is cheap, and that there is no deep difference between a detector and a belief. If the analogy cannot carry that conclusion, say what would carry it — a repaired version of the same analogy, or a different one altogether.
