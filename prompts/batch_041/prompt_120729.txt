I have just spent a long evening arguing with a friend about Kuhn. She thinks incommensurability is a local and largely practical problem: with enough goodwill, shared examples, and careful translation, scientists from different paradigms can always argue it out. I think that once you get to differences in taxonomic structure and in the standards that count as evidence, there is no neutral tribunal, and the change is closer to conversion than to proof. I would like you to draft a parody FAQ, around 650 words, for a fictional consultancy called Paradigm Bridge Partners, which promises to resolve disputes between researchers working in incommensurable paradigms. The humor should come from getting the philosophy right: the FAQ should distinguish local from global incommensurability, treat meaning holism and theory-laden observation as practical obstacles, offer service tiers that correspond to partial translation, persuasion, and conversion, and include a deadpan clause about what happens when the parties cannot agree on what would count as a solution. The target is the managerial fantasy that deep conceptual divides are just communication problems. It should be legible to a non-specialist but satisfying to someone who knows the literature. Please do not include citations, reading lists, or references.
