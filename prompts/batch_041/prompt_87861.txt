- The line around the block at a new ramen place, where it turned out the first handful of people were the owner's friends and everyone after just saw a line: cascade.
- Our whole org moving to the same CI tool, where the tool really does fit our stack and the savings are shared: not a cascade, just convergence.
- A hiring committee where every member had read the candidate's code, but the later speakers visibly drifted toward whoever had spoken before them: cascade.
- A run on a bank that was actually insolvent: not a cascade, because people were reacting to real payoffs and not to each other's beliefs.
- A whole subfield adopting a statistical method because journals won't take papers without it, even though a few senior people privately think it's wrong: cascade.
- Everyone using the same framework because that's what new hires already know: not a cascade, thats a network effect.

But our own benchmarks say provider Y is better for our workload, and two of us think that pretty strongly.

What's she going to say, and what rule is she implicitly running?
