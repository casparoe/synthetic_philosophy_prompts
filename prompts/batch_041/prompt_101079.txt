The volta should be the moment the scale flips: the turn from the astronomers' picture to the human one, and the couplet should land on the thought that meaning isn't out there in the size of things but in the foreground we actually inhabit. Make it accurate enough that a Ramsey scholar wouldn't wince, but also a good poem on its own. At the end, add a short note on where the poem takes liberties with his actual views or leans on a contested reading — I'm not totally sure he'd sign off on every implication, and I want to know which parts are my gloss rather than his.
