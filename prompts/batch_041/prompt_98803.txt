His line, roughly, is that the simmball level is a real pattern and genuinely explanatory, but that it does no pushing of its own: the simms shove each other around, full stop, and the symbols never reach down and move the substrate. - The symbol bit specifically. He says simmballs encode things and that's what makes the careenium a mind. Ask him what work "encoding" does here if the encoding is invisible to the simms, and what's left of the claim that symbols push anything.

My claim is that in the re-entrant careenium the symbol level *does* push the substrate, and that this is the disanalogy with Dennett's toy that matters for real brains. Write me two or three questions that force him to either concede the disanalogy or explain why it doesn't change anything. This is the part I care most about, so give it real teeth.

- Wrap with one question I'll actually use as the closer: what would have to be true about the careenium for him to say the simmballs push — i.e. what's the falsifier of his no-push claim.
