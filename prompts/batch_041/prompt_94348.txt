Her line is that we are warranted in attributing beliefs to a large language model exactly when the intentional stance earns its keep — when treating the system as a believer yields counterfactual predictions that the design stance (architecture, training corpus, optimization objective) cannot match — and she regards "it's just a stochastic parrot" as a non-answer rather than an objection. Do not ask anything that would work equally well for any AI ethics guest — every question should turn on the content of the criterion itself.
