Take the second and you get a big reward. The road is built so the two exits are completely indistinguishable from the driver's seat — same sign, same scenery, same everything. And you're "absent-minded" in a specific way: you literally can't remember whether you've already passed the first exit or not, so at the moment you're deciding, you don't know which exit you're looking at. Since the two spots look identical to you, whatever rule you follow has to be the same rule at both. Now Dan says the rational driver should randomize — flip a coin, and some of the time deliberately take the worthless first exit — and I said that's the dumbest thing I've ever heard, why would you ever choose guaranteed nothing, and he said because if you don't, the math makes you always drive past, and then... anyway, we did not resolve it, and I'm still annoyed.

Please don't have anyone explain the puzzle in dialogue, that would kill it; let the structure do the work. Maybe 1500 words, and give it a real ending, not a shrug.
