After thirty-five years in acoustics and signal processing, I retired and have spent the last several years reading philosophy; I have lately been working through Schopenhauer on music, and I find myself unable to decide what to ask of the view before I either accept or reject it. Schopenhauer’s central claim, as I understand it, is that music is unlike the other arts because it does not copy the Ideas but gives voice to the Will itself, and that this is why it can move us with such immediacy. I would like you to write the desiderata for any adequate theory of music’s metaphysical significance—not a scorecard of the usual positions, though a brief coda on how Schopenhauer’s view fares would be welcome.

What must such a theory explain, and with which cases must it reckon? I have in mind, for instance: the expressive power of music without propositional content; the difference between music that expresses sadness and music that makes a listener sad; the fact that the same sonic structure can be heard as music, as speech, or as alarm depending on framing; the existence of absolute music and programme music; the variability of musical systems across cultures, including traditions that do not organize harmony around the triad; tone-deafness that does not abolish musical pleasure, and the reverse condition in which musical pleasure is absent despite intact perception; the role of entrainment, embodiment, and social bonding; and the temporal, process-like character of musical experience. For each requirement, give me the case or consideration that motivates it, and tell me which requirements are genuine and which are merely prejudices of a particular camp—say, the prejudice that music must be about something, or the prejudice that it must be purely formal.

I would also like you to flag any pair or subset of these requirements that cannot be jointly satisfied, since that seems to me the most philosophically interesting part, and to say which single requirement does the most to exclude the most popular theories. I am not asking for a bibliography or citations; I want the specification itself. Finally, if Schopenhauer’s view cannot meet one of the non-negotiable requirements, say so plainly, but keep that assessment brief—the criteria are what I need to think through.
