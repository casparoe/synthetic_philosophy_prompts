The idea: the fink/mask literature has been churning out one-off counterexamples to the simple conditional analysis of dispositions for decades — the wire that would conduct if you touched it, except a fink kills its power the instant you touch it; the fragile glass that's wrapped in foam so it won't break when dropped — and I think the whole enterprise is stuck because everyone quietly assumes a disposition is individuated by its possible manifestations. Is the disposition preserved, destroyed, or replaced, and does my intervention-profile criterion give a principled answer where the case-by-case method just shrugs?
