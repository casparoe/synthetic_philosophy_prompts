I'm definately not trying to just rehash the usual debate about conflicting obligations; my half-formed idea is that the discussion gets muddled because people run together three different things: a normative system containing inconsistent requirements, an agent being all-things-considered required to do each of two incompatible actions, and an agent being required to do one or the other of two actions but not either one specifically. I suspect that once you separate those, some alleged dilemmas turn out to be mere conflicts of reasons or cases of disjunctive obligation, while others get more interesting, especially ones where the agent is required to choose but no particular choice is required.
