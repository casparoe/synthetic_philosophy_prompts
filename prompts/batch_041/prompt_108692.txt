ok so for my psych class I have to write something about consciousness, and I've been reading this pop-science book about the brain that my teacher lent me. The chapter on time and perception has a bit that I cant stop thinking about, but I also feel like I'm being sold something. Paraphrasing the gist:

There's an experiment where you flash a red dot on the left of a screen and then a green dot on the right, about a tenth of a second later. People don't report two dots. They report one dot travelling across the screen and changing colour in the middle of the trip. But here's the weird part: the colour change gets reported as happening in the middle, before the green dot has even been shown. So your brain has to be reaching back into the past and editing the memory of what you saw. Dennett calls the two ways of explaining this the Orwellian and the Stalinesque option. Orwellian means the memory gets rewritten after the fact, like in 1984; Stalinesque means the fix is done before the experience ever reaches you, like the show trials. He thinks the Orwellian one is basically the right story: consciousness is just a story the brain tells itself afterwards about things that already happened, which is why there isn't really any such thing as consciousness the way we normally think of it. It's the same thing as why a movie looks like it's moving, and it's the same deal as that Libet experiment where your brain decides half a second before you do — the upshot being that your experience of the present is actually about half a second behind reality, so what you think you're seeing right now has definately already happened.

I mostly believe it because the book makes it sound airtight, but it also feels too neat. Is it fair? Which sentences would a philosopher working on consciousness or a vision scientist sign off on, which ones would they wince at, and which are just wrong? And what would Dennett himself say if he read this — I have a feeling he'd hate it but I cant tell why. Especially the last part: even if all the earlier stuff is right, does it actually follow that my experience of now is half a second late?
