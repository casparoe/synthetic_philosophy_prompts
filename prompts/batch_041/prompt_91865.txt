Write the desiderata that any adequate response to the hole argument has to meet. Open with a two-sentence statement of the argument — two models of a generally covariant theory, related by a diffeomorphism that is the identity outside some region and not inside it, agree on everything observable while assigning different properties to the same spacetime point — so that the list can be read on its own. Then give the requirements, each with the case or consideration that motivates it: that determinism remain a substantive claim a theory could fail; that the two models be ruled genuinely distinct or genuinely identical, on a principled ground rather than by stipulation; that spacetime points not be demoted to mere labels of the field; that the response not turn on a feature of one theory, since the argument recurs for any theory with a symmetry of this kind; and that it explain why a symmetry no observation could detect was ever thought to threaten realism about spacetime. Mark which requirements are genuine and which are the preferences of a camp, flag any pair you suspect cannot be jointly satisfied, and identify the single requirement that excludes the most positions. Close by saying whether the same form of argument — a nontrivial symmetry generating a distinct but indistinguishable description — arises outside spacetime, and what that implies for how general the response has to be.
