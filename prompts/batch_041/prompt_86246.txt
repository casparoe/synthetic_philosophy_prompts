I need a help-centre FAQ page for a piece of translation software, and I want it played completely straight. The software is meant to translate any human language into English, but the way it works is: it refuses to assign meaning to anyones words unless it can build a single theory that makes the speakers come out mostly right about the world and mostly agreeing with each other, and it has to do the whole language at once -- you cant fix the meaning of one word without fixing the rest of the theory. The support team knows all this, thinks its fine, and is cheerfully answering tickets from frustrated field linguists. Roughly 700-800 words, numbered Q&As, a help-page title, maybe a was-this-article-helpful line at the end. The jokes should come from the philosophy being taken literally as a product specification -- not from the support staff being dumb, they're competent and their answers are technically correct, which is what makes it unsettling. Somewhere in there: a linguist who cant get the software to record that the villagers disagree with him about anything, because it keeps retranslating their sentences until the disagreement dissolves (answer: charity is not a user-adjustable setting, it's fixed at maximum, this is by design); a user who finds the software gives three different translations for the local word for the animal they keep pointing at -- the animal, a part of the animal, a brief stage of the animal -- and wants to know which is correct (answer: the evidence doesn't choose between them, the manual it hands you is one of many that fit, this is a documented limitation and also not a limitation); a request for the old radically-different-worldview mode that v1 shipped (answer: withdrawn, it produced no output at all, not an error, just a blank field, and this is listed in the release notes as expected behaviour); and one ticket about a man the software wont translate for at all because it has no record of him having interacted with the community before (answer: meaning is established by history, so theres nothing to translate yet, and no, his being a perfect physical duplicate of a regular user doesn't help).

Dont have anyone wink at the reader or name the philosopher this is all coming from -- the whole thing should read like a normal software company, and a reader who's never heard of any of it should still get the joke about a machine that wont let you be misunderstood. But someone who knows the literature should find it fair rather than a strawman, so keep the internal logic tight: the support team should never accidentally describe the software as broken in a way that contradicts its own spec. A couple of short in-universe error messages or release-note snippets would be good. No citations or further reading please, its a help page.
