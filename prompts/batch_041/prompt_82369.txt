Seventy minutes, whiteboard, no handouts.

What I want is the lesson plan, not the explanation. Start with the order of ideas: what has to be on the board before the paradox can land at all -- I think they have to feel the gap between "the rule I meant" and "the rule I could give an account of" before anything else will work, but tell me if that's the wrong first move or if something has to come before it. Then where the sceptical challenge goes, where Kripke's sceptical solution goes, and how to stop them hearing that solution as either "so meaning is whatever the community says" or "so there's no meaning at all" -- both of those will get said out loud by minute ten, I'd bet. And where does the Kripkenstein question go, i.e. how much of this is Kripke and how much is actually Wittgenstein? I want to park it at the end, but I don't know whether parking it leaves the middle hanging.

The carrying example is what I'm really stuck on. I don't want to run quus again.
