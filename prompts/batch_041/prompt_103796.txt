I feel like I basically get acausal trade — two agents who can't reach each other causally still cooperate because each predicts the other runs the same decision procedure, so each one reasons "if I defect my twin defects too." And I get acausal blackmail: some big agent threatens to punish you for something you never even did, because it's cheap for them and they can predict your policy. The move I keep seeing people reach for against blackmail is surrogate goals — you can't just decide to be the stoic type who shrugs off threats (you'd know you were faking), so instead you genuinely take on some other goal, and now caving would wreck the thing you actually care about, which makes you un-blackmailable. Makes sense in the abstract.
