I'm a senior in a cognitive science seminar on self-reference, and we've spent two weeks on Escher's Drawing Hands and the account of tangled hierarchies in our reading, where a loop in which each level seems to generate the one above it only holds together because some level stays outside the loop — the inviolate level. Our professor keeps insisting that the interesting question isn't how the loop works but what it costs to keep the inviolate level inviolate, and I'd like to see that question dramatized rather than argued.

Write a short story of roughly 1,000 words that stages this structure in a setting where it isn't already a cliché. I don't want two hands drawing each other, a painter and his canvas, or a program that prints its own code. Pick a domain where mutual constitution is real but rarely described that way — a pair of institutions that each certify the other's authority, two people whose sense of self is built entirely out of the other's memory of them, a market where each price is justified only by another price, two software agents that mutually attest to each other's integrity. Make the dependence concrete: show each side producing the conditions the other needs, and show why the regress doesn't bottom out in either of them.

Then put pressure on the inviolate level. Something neither party can author, certify, or draw — the thing the loop silently relies on — starts to shift, or turns out to have been contingent all along, or is threatened by one party trying to reach it. I don't want a twist for its own sake; I want the story to show why the loop needed that level and what becomes of the loop when it can't be taken for granted. An unresolved ending is fine.

Don't have any character explain the concepts, and don't use the terms tangled hierarchy, strange loop, or inviolate level in the story itself. After the story, add one paragraph in your own voice identifying what played the role of the inviolate level, why the mutual production couldn't substitute for it, and whether you think the story presents that level as a genuine foundation or as a stopping point the loop merely agreed not to question.
