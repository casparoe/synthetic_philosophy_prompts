For my high school philosophy club I'm making a "which side are you actually on?" quiz about the thing where a demon says he'll torture you unless you admire him, or someone offers you money to believe something, and everyone argues about whether thats a real reason or a wrong kind of reason. I want 9 questions, each one a concrete case with two or three answer options, and no question that just asks if you believe in wrong kind of reasons — I want it to sort people by what they say about the cases. Then a scoring key that puts people into the actual positions. At least these have to come out as possible results: (1) the split between reasons that are about the object of the attitude and reasons that are just about having the attitude, where only the first kind counts; (2) the view that it depends on whether the reason bears on the question the attitude is an answer to; (3) the deflationist one that theres no special wrong kind at all, the threat is a real reason and just usually gets outweighed; (4) the pragmatist one that for belief, practical reasons can be real reasons to believe; (5) the one that says this whole mess shows we shouldn't define goodness as being fitting to value in the first place; (6) the pluralist one that there are two kinds of reasons and both are real, but only one touches fittingness. Also give me at least one result for the weird view that in the demon case what you end up with isn't even admiration anymore, its some other attitude that just looks like it. Please use these cases somewhere: the demon threat; your boss paying you to genuinely like your job; a coach telling you the team plays better when your angry at the other team; being told you owe someone gratitude; a case where believing you'll pass makes it more likely you pass; and being told that trusting your doctor will make the medicine work better. If two of the results can't really be told apart by any of my questions, say so at the end instead of pretending they're seperate, and if some combo of answers doesn't match any position, say whether thats because nobodys thought of it or because it doesn't make sense. Also flag any of my cases that doesn't actually separate the positions. Start with the key (the positions and what answers point to them) and then the questions, no intro paragraph, no restating what I asked.
