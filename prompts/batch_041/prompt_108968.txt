(3) But I also think it's fine to assert that the 6:15 to Leeds leaves from platform 4 just because the timetable says so, even though the timetable could be misprinted, so I don't strictly know it. (6) A company spokeperson can flat out assert that the refunds will be paid by Friday when all they know is that the CFO told them so, and I want to say that's fine, not a lie. And (7) since a novelist puts false sentences on the page and that's obviously not a problem, I used to worry there was something wrong with (1) -- but novels aren't assertions, so I don't think that one bites.
