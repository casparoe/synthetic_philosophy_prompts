Please give me an example of a conceptual issue with that same shape that has nothing to do with religion, drawn from something contemporary — an institution, a market, a piece of software, a school — rather than from art or the law, which are the cases everyone reaches for. In your example, state exactly what the two horns are, say what turns on which horn you take, and explain what would have to be true for the dilemma to be merely verbal rather than real. Please separate the parts of your answer that are settled from the parts that are genuinely contested among people who study the topic, and do not present a contested reading as the consensus.
