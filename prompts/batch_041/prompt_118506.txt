Peacock with extra feathers glued on: says nothing, and it should get caught. Fake Rolex: it looks like the message, but the cost is fake, so there's no message. Money-back guarantee on a product: costs the seller nothing unless the thing is actually bad, and yet I read it as a real signal. Threat of a nuke you'll never fire: real. Threat from a guy who'd obviously fold: not.
