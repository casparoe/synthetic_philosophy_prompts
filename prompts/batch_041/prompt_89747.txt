Restate, as faithfully as you can, the debate the game provokes — whether a rule can validly authorize its own amendment; whether an amendment procedure can be used to abolish the authority that licensed it; whether a change adopted under one procedure while altering that very procedure is valid; and what, if anything, terminates the regress of authority — in the vocabulary I actually use. Then give me the residue report: which parts of the debate map cleanly onto thresholds, keys, and forks; which translate only with distortion; and which have no counterpart in my vocabulary at all.
