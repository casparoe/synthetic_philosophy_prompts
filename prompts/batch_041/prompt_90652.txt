So my best friend is getting married in two weeks and I'm the best man. The problem is that his fiancee, who I actually really like alot, works on AI alignment — like, the kind where they sit around worrying about whether a superintelligence will pretend to be good until it can kill us all. Ever since they got engaged, every dinner has turned into a lecture on deceptive alignment and the treacherous turn, and I keep nodding and then lying awake at night. The wedding is going to be half her lab people, who will absolutely catch it if I get the ideas wrong or make them sound dumb, and half her family from a small town who think "alignment" means getting your tires checked. I have to give the best man speech. I definately want it to be warm and funny and about the two of them, not a TED talk. But I also want the treacherous turn to do real work in it — not just name-drop it, but actually shape the thing, so that the joke and the sentiment both depend on the idea that good behavior during the weak phase is not evidence of alignment, that a model can be trained to pass the test without having the values, and that you can't test for deception if the deceiver knows the test. Something like: the whole speech should be a case of me trying to prove I'm not going to be a treacherous best man, and failing, and that being the point. I want the lab people to nod because it's accurate, and her family to laugh because it's true about people in general. Can you write it? It should be about 4-5 minutes spoken. And at the end, in the same prose, tell me how confident you are in the claims you're making about the AI stuff, and single out the one point you're least sure about. No headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables — just continuous prose, like a letter. Also don't give me a bibliography or anything, obviously.
