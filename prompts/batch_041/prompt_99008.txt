"Carnap and the Vienna Circle were the philosophers of the planned society. This is the real meaning of the principle of tolerance in the Logical Syntax of Language -- tolerance toward other people's frameworks and other people's ways of living is the political core of his philosophy. And because he held that only science has meaning, he treated ethical and political claims as either meaningless pseudo-problems, like the metaphysics he wanted to eliminate, or mere expressions of attitude. Personally he stayed above politics; it was Neurath who was the socialist, and the Circle's political neutrality is exactly why they had no answer when the Nazis came. The upshot is that planners today who claim to be doing value-neutral technical work are Carnap's heirs whether they know it or not."
