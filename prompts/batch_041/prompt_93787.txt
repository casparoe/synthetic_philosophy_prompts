Then give me (i) a model answer of roughly 700 words, and (ii) a marking scheme worth 20 marks, organised as five must-hit points, each with its mark value and a one-line note naming the specific mistake that should cost a student marks. In the model answer and the scheme, mark clearly which claims are settled among people who work on this topic and which are genuinely contested, and do not present a contested reading as the consensus.
