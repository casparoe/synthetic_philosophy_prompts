ok so this is kinda random but im in a philosophy club at my high school and next week its my turn to present on nietzsches eternal recurrence, and instead of doing slides like everyone else i want to write a poem. a villanelle specifically, because those two lines that keep coming back seem like the perfect form for a thought that keeps coming back, and i want the form to actually do the philosophical work instead of just being a container for it.

heres what i want: two refrains, and each one should be one of the two ways people read the idea. one refrain should sound like the cosmological version, where time really does loop and every event literally happens again and again forever, identical down to the smallest detail. the other should sound like the version where its a test of how you feel about your life, like a demon shows up at your loneliest moment and tells you youll have to live this exact life again countless times and the only question is whether youd collapse or say yes. a reader who knows the idea should be able to tell which refrain is which and see them basically talking past each other for most of the poem, and then in the last two lines they should meet in a way thats not a cop out.

please also work in the thing i keep getting stuck on, because i think its the real teeth of the problem: even if you say yes, the yes is also part of the loop, so its already happened infinitely many times before and you never actually get to be the one who freely chooses it. and related, the whole thing about how the will cant will backwards, it can want the future but it cant change what already was, so the only way to love your life completely is to somehow want your past backwards too, which seems impossible. id like the poem to sit in that impossibility instead of solving it.

dont name drop, dont explain the philosophy in the poem like its an essay, and please dont end with some cheesy carpe diem live laugh love thing. keep it accurate enough that someone whos actually read nietzsche wouldnt wince, but i also want it to work for people in the club who havent read him yet. 19 lines, proper villanelle rhymes, and if you have to pick, prioritize the refrains doing the work over fancy imagery. thanks!
