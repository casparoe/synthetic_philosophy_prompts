My friends Nadia and Rob got into an arguement last Saturday while the three of us were cutting across the field behind the rec center, and I've been chewing on it all week.

Nadia stopped and pointed at that dirt path that runs diagonal across the grass and said: nobody built this, nobody planned it, and yet it goes where it goes because that's the quickest way to the parking lot. So there's a reason for the shape of this thing and the reason isn't in anybody's head. Reasons don't need a reasoner.

Rob said that's cheating. Every person who walked that path had a reason in their head -- get to the lot faster -- so the reason isn't floating anywhere, it's just been added up a few thousand times. Nadia said fine, then take something where nobody ever had the reason. She brought up that bird that fakes a broken wing to lead a dog away from the nest. She said the first bird that ever did it didn't invent the trick, it just happened to do it and its chicks lived and the ones that didn't do it didn't. Rob said the reason still lives somewhere, either in the bird's wiring or in its genes, and if you say genes can have reasons then you're only saying that birds that did it left more chicks.

Nadia said so what, that's a true sentence about why something is the way it is, and noone thought it. Rob said but look at what happened to your sentence. You started with the bird doing it in order to save its chicks, which sounds like the bird wanting something, and you ended with birds that did it left more chicks, which is a headcount. Calling a headcount a reason sneaks the wanting back in. A reason is something you can weigh, something that can be good or bad. The path can't be wrong. It just is.

Nadia came back with: then I can't say what a heart is for either. And anyway the bird isn't a rock -- it only does the broken wing thing when a predator is near the nest, and if the dog doesn't follow it tries harder. That's not a headcount, that's a response. Because if it doesn't, I want to know what we've got that it hasn't, and I don't want the answer to be a soul.
