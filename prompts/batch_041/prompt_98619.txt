I'm in a epistemology reading group and me and a friend have been going at the same arguement for weeks, so I want to actually pin down what I think instead of just vibing. Here's a pile of cases and my honest reactions. (1) A guy has it on good authority that his coworker drives a Ford, so he concludes "my coworker drives a Ford or my aunt's in Barcelona." His aunt happens to actually be in Barcelona, so the disjunction is true. I don't think he knows it. (2) You're driving through a county where every barn-looking thing is a fake facade, except the one real barn you happen to be looking at — you think "there's a barn." Not knowledge, obviously. (3) You glance at a clock that, unbeknownst to you, stopped twelve hours ago, and it happens to show the right time. Not knowledge. (4) A stranger on the street who is a pathological liar and fully intends to send you the wrong way gets muddled and accidentally points you at the actual bookstore. You trust them and find it. Honestly I lean towards saying you *do* know where the bookstore is here, but I go back and forth. (5) I believe I've got two hands, and I can't rule out that I'm a brain in a vat. I think that's knowledge, even though if I *were* a vat I'd believe it just the same. (6) A student lands the right answer to a hard problem only because a classmate confidently handed them a wild guess that happened to be correct. Not knowledge.

My tentative principle is something like: you know p when you truly believe it and you formed the belief in a way that isn't just a fluke *in this instance* — but that's way too vague to do any work. If I sharpen it to "your evidence guarantees the truth," then cases 4 and 5 come out wrong, and if I sharpen it to "you couldn't easily have been wrong," then case 5 definately fails. Is there a principle that gets all six, or close? Which of my verdicts does it force me to give up, and what does it say about the one I can't settle (4)? And what would it predict for a seventh I keep thinking of: you read the time off a broken clock, but you've *also* just heard someone reliable say the time — knowledge or not?
