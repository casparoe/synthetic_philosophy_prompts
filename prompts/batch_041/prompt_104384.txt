I am applying for a three-month summer research grant and need a two-page proposal. My half-formed idea concerns two idealizations of deliberation that are often treated as interchangeable. In the first, an idealized reasoner can put questions to copies of itself, each of which can put questions to further copies, with no bound on the depth of the recursion (the construction usually labelled HCH). In the second, a hard question is factored into many small and mostly independent sub-tasks performed by agents who do not know the big picture, whose answers are then recombined (factored cognition). My suspicion is that these are not two descriptions of one ideal but two different claims about what makes a deliberative outcome correct, and that the difference becomes visible only when one asks whether such an idealization can generate justification or merely aggregate the justification already available in its base case. Please turn this into a proposal: state the question so that it has a determinate answer, explain why the existing treatments leave it open, say what the work would actually consist of over three months, what a result and a failure would look like, and whether the project is too large for the time available.
