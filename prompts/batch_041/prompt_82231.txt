ok so you know that old thought experiment where someone writes out einstein's entire brain as a book — every neuron, every firing threshold, every connection, and the tables for turning sound coming in into nerve firings and nerve firings going out into speech — so that if you trace through it at the right pace you can actually have a conversation with him, he answers, he gets as puzzled as the real one would have been if you told him he was dead. the whole thing is sitting there on the shelf all at once; the thinking only happens while someones running it. and then the nasty follow up about slowing the running down — one neuron a year, one neuron a century — and whether theres still anyone in there or just a very long wait.

i want a poem out of that, and i want it to be a round. like frere jacques: every voice sings the same words, they just come in at different times and stack up. three or four voices, eight lines or so, and every line has to work as an entry line, so it has to make sense whichever voice lands on it first. mark the later entries with indents or brackets so i can see the stagger on the page.

the words have to do double duty. they should be true of a record hung on a wall or a book shut on a shelf — the pattern sitting there complete and doing nothing at all — and also true of a mind actually running, a bit at a time, someone home. same words throughout, and no word anywhere that settles which of the two it is. the payoff of the form is that once all the voices are going you cant point at one of them and say thats the one whos thinking, so let the harmony carry that instead of a line that explains it.

if you can also make it come apart when its sung absurdly slow — voices so far apart they never meet, so the round stops being a round and turns into three lonely people — then do that, but only if it doesnt wreck the poem. someone whos read the dialogue should be able to see the tempo thing doing its work in the structure, and someone whos never heard of it should still just think its a good round.
