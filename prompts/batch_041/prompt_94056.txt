Her calls so far:
- Heart pumping blood: yes, function. The thumping noise the heart makes: no, that's just a side effect, it isn't for anything.
- Sweat glands cooling you down: yes.
- Bones being stiff and holding you up: yes. Bones being white: no, nothing is "for" being white.
- Adults in a population that's been herding cattle and drinking milk for thousands of years being able to digest lactose: yes, that's what it's for.
- Your nose being a handy shelf for your glasses: no, definately not a function of the nose even though it's doing something useful.
- Some trait in a deep sea fish that clearly keeps it alive but nobody knows how it evolved: yes, function, don't be a pedant about the history.
