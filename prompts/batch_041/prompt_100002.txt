ok so this is kind of a weird one and I dont really know who else to ask. through a series of accidents I've become the person who keeps the records for our tenants union. it started with me holding onto a shoebox of old minutes and now its a shared drive and I'm the one who decides what gets scanned, what stays, what goes in the bin. the longer I do it the more I notice that what we actually have is basically whatever was easy to record: the landlords letters, the meetings where the loudest people talked, the members who happened to like writing things down. the people who probably needed the union most, the ones scared of retaliation, the ones who didnt speak much english, the ones couch-surfing between friends, they're barely in there at all. nobody deleted them. they just never got written down in the first place.

then last month I found something. our founder, who died a few years ago and who everyone basically reveres, kept a private notebook tracking who actually got money and help week to week, including people whose names never show up anywhere in our official stuff. but the notebook also shows he quietly cut off one family, and there are a few entries that make him look, honestly, kind of petty and controlling. now we're naming the new office after him and putting up a little display about the unions history, and I'm the one who'd be assembling it. some people want the display to be the whole story, nothing hidden. one of the old-timers says some of this was private for a reason and that I'd be betraying the people in that notebook by putting them on a wall. and to make it worse, alot of the entries are just initials or nicknames, so I'd have to guess who they even are, and I might guess wrong.

I really dont know what the right move is and I'm scared of screwing this up in a way I cant undo. what should I do? and whatever you tell me, also tell me what would change your mind, like what you'd have to learn about the situation or what argument you'd have to hear to land somewhere different. please just write it out as normal prose, no bullet points or headings or bold text, just like an email, I always end up skipping anything that looks like a memo.
