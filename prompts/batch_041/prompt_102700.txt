Restate, one at a time, the main positions: the view that health is simply the statistically normal, species-typical functioning of an organism's parts and that disease is a deviation from it; the view that disease is inescapably evaluative and reflects what we happen to regard as harmful or undesirable; and the hybrid view that a condition counts as a disease only when it is both a genuine malfunction and a harm. For each, say precisely what carries over into my vocabulary, what has to be bent or fudged to fit, and what cannot be said at all. If so, is that a defect of my vocabulary or a sign that the naturalist's claim is less clear than it appears? Please lead with your bottom line in the first sentence and then give the reasoning, with no preamble and no restating of my question.
