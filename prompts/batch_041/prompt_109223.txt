They told us Ravi was matched to me by an algorithm that picked whoever had the most similar personality quiz results to me.

My brother says its obvious, take the 20, because Ravi's button has nothing to do with mine -- me being generous can't make him generous, and he can't thank me or pay me back or anything, so why would I light $20 on fire for a stranger. But I keep thinking: if he's basically me, then whatever I do is kind of a clue about what he'll do, and if we both hit give we both walk out with $30 instead of $20. Am I just being superstitious here, or is there a real reason to hit the give button?
