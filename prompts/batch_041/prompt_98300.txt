I retired a few years ago from a career in soil science -- I spent most of it counting nematodes in soil samples under a microscope, which is a long way of saying I'm not a political thinker -- and since then I've been reading alot of philosophy, including a fair bit about politics. Something's been nagging at me and I cant find the word for it.

Here's what keeps happening. You take a label like 'left' or 'right' (or 'liberal' and 'conservative', whatever the local version is). On one hand it clearly does real work: if I know where someone sits on one of these, I can guess their view on a dozen questions that seem to have nothing to do with each other -- whether they trust experts, how they feel about immigration, whether they think crime is rising, how they'd vote in a referendum on, I don't know, fishing quotas. That predictive power makes it feel like there's a single hidden dial somewhere. But when I try to say what the dial actually is -- what all the people on one side share -- it evaporates. Two people wearing the same label disagree about almost everything concrete, and the only thing they seem to have in common is that they're not the other lot. And I suspect that if the other lot vanished overnight, the coalition would fall apart within the week.

So I've got a category that behaves like a real kind (it predicts) but doesn't seem to have any shared essence (no defining feature, no common belief, no single principle). My old biologist instincts say this smells like one of those taxonomic groupings that only works statistically -- held together by a bundle of traits that happen to travel together, rather than by any one necessary feature. Is there an established name for this pattern -- a grouping that's real as a statistical structure but empty as a shared essence -- and if so, how does it differ from plain 'family resemblance', or from a concept that's contested on purpose? If there's no name, I'd like you to propose one and pin down its edges: what exactly would have to be true for such a grouping to count as not real after all, and is 'held together only by a shared opponent' the same phenomenon or a different one? I'm not after a label for its own sake; I want the thing carved at the joints, so please be explicit about what it is and what it isn't. British English, if you don't mind.
