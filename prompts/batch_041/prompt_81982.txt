Include an entry defending the practice of reporting whichever metric the customer asked for, an entry that describes the impossibility results as a known limitation of competing vendors, and a satisfied-customer quote from a public-sector agency that has deployed the tool in a high-stakes setting. It should be funny to someone who knows the calibration-versus-error-rate trilemma and legible to a reader who doesn't, and the critique of fairness-washing should land without ever being stated outright.
