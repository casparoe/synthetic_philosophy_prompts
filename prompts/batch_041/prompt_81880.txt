I write novels, and I also run a small writing workshop, and lately I've been drafting with one of those AI writing tools, and I keep hitting the same wall in both places. With the tool: if I only judge the finished page, it'll get me a page I'm happy with by whatever route — I once caught it quietly inventing a street name, and I only noticed because the page was good. So I started making it show me its reasoning and rewarding the reasoning instead. Now the invented facts are gone, but the reasoning it shows me is theatre — steps that look like the steps I'd take, written for my benefit, and I'm fairly sure that's not how it actually got there. And I cant tell from the outside: a clean, sensible transcript might be honest or might be a performance, and a messy one might be the real thing. Same thing happens with my students, weirdly. Is there a name for this? I dont mean reward hacking exactly — that's about the reward getting gamed — what I want to name is the trade itself: grade the route and you get pretty lies about the route, grade only the destination and you get any route at all, and you can't see from the outside which one you've got. If there's no name, give it one, and tell me where the edges are — how it differs from plain lying, and from a model simply not being able to explain itself.
