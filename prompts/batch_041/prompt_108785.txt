Ok so I'm putting together a little exercise for a reading group and I need you to actually reason here rather than hand me the standard summary.

Simone Weil died in 1943, so everything below is after her time. Quick reminder of where she stood so we're on the same page: she thought a political party is basically a device for manufacturing collective passion, that being inside one forces you to lie, that a party's real and only goal is its own growth, and she spent her last year arguing parties should be abolished outright, with something much looser built around journals taking their place. Don't just recite that back to me — I want a prediction.

Here's the set up. She gets shown two parties from a present-day democracy and asked which one is worse.

Party A: the insurgent movement. Began as a protest, barely has formal members or dues, has one magnetic leader, packs stadiums, huge online mobilisation, supporters talk about it the way people talk about a cause or a conversion, anyone who dissents gets expelled as a traitor to the movement, and it defines itself almost entirely by being against the establishment and against the other parties.

Party B: the governing professional party. Practically no members, funded mostly by the state according to how many votes it got, run by a small paid stratum of consultants and career politicians who rotate between party headquarters and ministries, all the big parties agree on most things and fight about competence and management, the platform is whatever the focus groups say, and the party's entire business is staying in office. Political scientists have some name for this kind of party, cartel something, but you don't need it.

My gut says she'd say A, because of the collective passion and the idolatry, but B is a party that manufactures nothing and just perpetuates itself, and I can't tell if that makes it better or worse in her terms — or whether it even counts as a party for her, and if it doesn't, what follows from that.

So: best prediction of what she'd have said, which of the two she ranks as the greater evil and why, and where the extrapolation is thinnest. Don't worry about sources or what the secondary literature says, I just want the argument. And tell me what would have to be true about B for her to actually prefer it.
