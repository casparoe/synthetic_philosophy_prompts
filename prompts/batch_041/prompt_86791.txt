I am applying for a one-year fellowship and need a two-page proposal on the strategy-stealing argument. The half-formed idea: the argument is usually taught as a trick for symmetric two-player games without draws — if the second player had a winning strategy, the first player could open with an arbitrary move and then follow that strategy, on the grounds that a spare stone or an unused option can never hurt — so the first player cannot lose, and in a no-draw game must win.
