Some I already think belong: it has to rank any two lives, or at least be explicit about when it refuses to; it can't just be ordinal within each person, since the whole point is comparing across them; it has to say something about the baseline — is a life of unremitting pain below zero, and relative to what?; and it has to survive the leveling-down move, i.e. a comparison can't tell us we've improved things by making everyone equally miserable. (b) two lives with the same total welfare but opposite shapes, a slow rise versus a long decline — does shape have to matter, and can a measure that's just a sum over moments even see it?
