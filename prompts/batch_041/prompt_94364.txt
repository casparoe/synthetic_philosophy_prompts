So: what are the genuinely open questions in that debate right now, and which would you actually staff? I care way more about the reasoning than the list. Two things I want you to treat as live options rather than settled background. First, the possibility that the whole thing is a verbal dispute, i.e. that there's no substantive disagreement once you get precise about what each view is a claim about -- is "is this debate substantive at all?" the question that matters most, or is it the kind of meta-question that eats a field alive and pays out nothing? Second, whether the debate should be reoriented away from classical spacetime, where it feels like the cases are exhausted, toward quantum gravity, where the ontology is genuinely up in the air and nobody has a worked out story about what the relata even are -- real frontier, or just a way of avoiding the hard classical arguments?

Rank the top few and say what would move something up or down the list over the next couple of years. Please write your answer in American English.
