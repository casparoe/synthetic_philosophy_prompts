ok so my reading group has been chewing through the mind-body stuff and one guy keeps leaning on the same analogy to make epiphenomenalism sound like no big deal. his line is that consciousness is the steam whistle on a locomotive: the engine's working throws off this noise, the noise doesn't push anything, nobody thinks the train would run differently if you clipped the whistle off. so yes, he says, phenomenal properties don't do any causal work, but that's just what it is to be a byproduct, and the panic about it is overheated.
