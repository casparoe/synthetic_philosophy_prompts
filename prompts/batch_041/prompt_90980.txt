Give me your credence, as a number, that adding such a chamber would improve the epistemic quality of enacted policy relative to a unicameral elected legislature over a thirty-year horizon in a stable wealthy democracy.

Give a rough probability and one line of justification for each term, say how they combine into the final number, and name the term your answer is most sensitive to and the evidence that would move it most.

Separate what is settled from what is genuinely contested among people who have studied epistocracy, sortition, and democratic theory, and do not present a contested view as the consensus.
