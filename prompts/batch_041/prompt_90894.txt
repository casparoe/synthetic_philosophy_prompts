Not one false sentence. Meanwhile a nurse flat-out told a dying woman her brother's flight had landed while he was still in the air, and I'd bet most people would call that a lie, kind as it was. So being false doesn't seem to be what makes a lie, and being true doesn't seem to prevent one. Is there a name for the thing I'm circling — the true statement used to produce a false belief, and its relatives? I know Augustine was famously hard-line about lying; would he have called the resident's move a lie, or only the nurse's? And where does someone land who says nothing false at all but stages a scene or plants a document so you draw the false conclusion on your own — does that count, and by what criterion?
