I'm a software guy, not a philosopher, and I've gotten myself into a loop I can't get out of, so I'm hoping you can tell me whether the thing I'm stuck in has a name.

A while back I stopped eating meat, mostly because I didn't want to be responsible for animals suffering. Then a friend pointed out that growing the plants I eat kills animals too -- mice, birds, rabbits, and an apparently staggering number of insects, all chewed up by the machines that plow and harvest a big field of wheat or soy. Fine, I thought, let me actually count. And the counting went badly. One cow feeds me for months, and if it's a grass-fed cow it spent its whole life on pasture that nothing had to plow. A chicken feeds me for a few days and spends its short life eating grain that had to be grown, harvested and trucked. So per meal, or per calorie, the chicken comes out looking worse than the cow; and if insects count, a bowl of rice starts looking worse than either. I keep redoing the sum in different units -- per animal killed, per meal, per acre, per dollar -- and I get different answers, and the answer I get in the unit that feels most natural to me is the one that says I should be eating beef. I'm fairly sure I'm not supposed to be eating beef.

Then it happened again from a different direction. Someone told me that if I really take suffering seriously, and if life in the wild is mostly miserable -- starving, freezing, being eaten alive, dying of infections -- then the humane thing might be to reduce the number of wild animals rather than protect them. Same feeling: obviously monstrous, and yet I can't find the step where it goes wrong.

So: is there a name for this whole situation -- where you take a moral standard seriously, apply it consistently, and it hands you back the exact conclusion you were argueing against, so you either accept something that sounds awful or quietly swap in a different way of counting? Is there a name for the narrower move, where the standard doesn't change but the unit does and the ranking flips? And separately, is there a name for the diet puzzle itself, the beef-vs-chicken-vs-beans thing? I'm not just after a label. If some of this already has an established treatment I want to know which parts, and where the nearest existing ideas stop fitting my case -- and if the general pattern doesn't have a name, I'd like you to propose one and tell me what it covers and what it doesn't, and how it differs from just saying an argument has an absurd conclusion.
