Here is my reconstruction, and I'd like you to check it and correct it as you go. The simple analysis says that an object is disposed to manifest M in response to stimulus S just in case it would M if it were S. Two families of case are usually said to break this. In the first, the stimulus itself destroys or creates the causal basis of the disposition: a wire that conducts only because a hidden machine makes it live at the very instant it is touched, or a glass that a mechanism renders shatterproof at the moment of impact. In the second, the basis is untouched but something else prevents the manifestation: a fragile glass packed in foam, a soluble crystal sealed in plastic. The best-known repair replaces the simple conditional with one whose antecedent holds fixed the intrinsic property that constitutes the disposition's basis, roughly: x is disposed to M when S iff x has some intrinsic property P such that, if x were S while still having P, it would M. My understanding is that this handles the first family and not the second, and I want to understand exactly why — in particular, whether the repair is principled or whether it simply helps itself to the idea that nothing interferes.

The case I've actually been chewing on, and the reason I'm writing, is one I haven't seen discussed. A crystal of a soluble salt sits in a beaker. A hidden mechanism detects the instant water is poured in and, in that instant, coats the crystal in a waterproof film while simultaneously dropping a decoy crystal of the same salt into the beaker, which dissolves in full view of anyone watching. So the crystal really is soluble, the film is entirely extrinsic and no part of its microstructure, and yet the crystal itself never dissolves under the stimulus. I'd like you to tell me whether this belongs to the first family, the second, both at once, or something the taxonomy doesn't cover; whether it refutes the simple analysis, the repaired one, both, or neither; and whether my description is even well-formed, since the decoy part seems to trade on how one individuates the manifestation — the crystal dissolving, versus dissolving happening in the beaker.
