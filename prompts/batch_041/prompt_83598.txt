B holds that this reading mistakes an artifact of averaging for a philosophical result: the theorems quantify over a uniform measure on a space of functions that no agent ever confronts, they license only the claim that no learner beats every other learner across all problems, not that no learner beats chance on the problems that matter, and they evaporate as soon as the measure over target functions is non-uniform -- so they establish the necessity of inductive assumptions, not the impossibility of justified induction.
