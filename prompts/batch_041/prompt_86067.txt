I want to do the thing where people say you did something on purpose when the side effect was bad but not when the side effect was good, even though you knew it was coming either way. So dont give me a lecture outline, give me an activity we actually run.

Round one needs new cases, not the famous ones everyone has already seen on youtube, and they should be things that could genuinely happen to kids at our school, like somebody running for student council or redoing the practice schedule for a team. Same story twice basically, once where the side effect hurts somebody and once where it helps somebody, with the exact same amount of knowing either way, and everyone answers privately on a slip of paper with three seperate questions on it: did they know it would happen, did they do it on purpose, do they deserve to get blamed.
