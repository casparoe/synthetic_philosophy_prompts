My opening: Lange seems to me to just dissolve your problem. The Central Planning Board doesn't have to conjure prices out of thin air — it posts a price, watches for queues and for unsold stock, nudges the price up or down, and iterates, which is definately what a competitive market is doing too. And once prices are parametric for everybody, each factory manager only needs two rules: pick the input combination that minimises average cost, and set output where marginal cost equals price.
