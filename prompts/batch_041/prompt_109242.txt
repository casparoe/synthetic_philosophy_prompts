Yet in ordinary practice the school seems to grant cognitions a kind of provisional acceptance, treating them as valid unless and until something specific defeats them. I gather the Mīmāṃsā school took the opposite line on validity, which is part of why I'm unsure whether the default-acceptance idea is a core commitment or a concession to practical life.
