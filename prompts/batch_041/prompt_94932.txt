Here's the game. We came up with six house definitions of violence. They're our own labels, not anyone's theory, so don't worry about whose idea they are:

A. Killings: intentional killing by identifiable agents (war, homicide).
B. Overriding someone's will: any act that forces a person against their will or blocks them from acting -- imprisonment, coercion, conscription, deportation, not just physical harm.
C. Preventable deprivation: death and suffering from poverty, hunger, lack of medicine, when it could in principle be fixed.
D. Attritional harm: slow damage spread over decades that no single act causes -- pollution, climate, chronic overwork.

F. Omissions: harm you could have prevented and didn't.
