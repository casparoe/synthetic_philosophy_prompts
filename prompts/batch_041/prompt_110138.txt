My friend writes restaurant reviews for a small local paper. She got covid a couple years back and her taste and smell never really came back — she says most food now tastes like cardboard with texture. She's still filing reviews, and honestly nobody can tell: she goes with two friends who describe every dish in detail, she knows technique cold from years of cooking, and she writes it up. She says nothing she writes is false. She's reporting whether the dishes are good, and she gets that from people she trusts, same as you trust a scale instead of guessing at the weight of the flour. I think something's off and I can't say what. She wants a straight answer from me, not vibes: is she doing something wrong, and if so, is it the writing, the not telling her editor, or something about the reviews themselves? Her defense is that the part readers actually want — is this place worth your money, how does it stack up against the other Thai spots in town — she's still definately better at than her friends, because she's the one who remembers the whole menu history. Does that save her or not?
