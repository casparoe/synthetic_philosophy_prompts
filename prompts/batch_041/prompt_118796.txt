The first camp holds that a preference structure is defective whenever some finite sequence of individually endorsed trades can return the agent to its starting bundle minus a fee — the defect being structural, so it makes no difference whether any exploiter exists. The second holds that a pump shows nothing against an agent that can foresee its own future choices and price them: such an agent declines the first trade, is never a sure loser, and may be perfectly rational while harbouring a cycle.
