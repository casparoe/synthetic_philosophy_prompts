Nick Bostrom introduced the term "singleton" for "a world order in which there is a single decision-making agency at the highest level," and he was careful to add that such an agency need not be monolithic — it could contain any number of independent agents pursuing their own disparate goals — and that its defining powers are the ability to prevent internal or external threats to its own existence and supremacy, and the ability to exert effective control over the major features of its domain.

Give me an example of a world order that satisfies those criteria to the letter but that you would resist calling a singleton, and use the example to locate the conceptual issue: which element of the definition is actually doing the theoretical work, and which element is a placeholder that a more careful theorist ought to replace? Do not use the stock illustrations — a world state or democratic world republic, a superintelligent machine, a transcending upload, or Bostrom's own convergent-values and universally self-enforcing moral code scenarios. Invent a case instead, and make it concrete enough that I can see how it functions: who or what occupies the highest level, by what mechanism threats to its supremacy are foreclosed, and how it controls the features of its domain.

Then do two things. First, state a verdict about your own case: is it a singleton or not, and which single criterion does it strain or violate, and why should that criterion rather than the others bear the weight? Second, give me a contrasting case that fails the letter of the definition yet performs the function singletons are introduced to perform — reliably solving the coordination problems that a world of many independent top-level agencies cannot solve, such as arms races, races to exhaust shared resources, or the entrenchment of extreme inequality. Say what the pair of cases together shows about whether the concept should be defined by structure, by capability, or by something else.

I am not looking for a survey of what has been written on this. Commit to a reading, keep the examples in view throughout, and let the argument be carried by the cases rather than by abstract stipulation. No citations or reading lists are needed; if you refer to a published idea, do it in your own words.
