Write a short story about someone who moves into a small community organized around one idea: only the absence of craving and suffering is good in itself. Everything else — pleasure, love, curiosity, achievement — counts only as a way of quieting craving, and if something killed the craving without the pleasure that would be just as good. The narrator arrives sympathetic and stays sympathetic; I want the reader to feel the pull of it, because there really is something restful there. Then let the view's own logic walk the community into a corner — if craving is the only thing that's bad in itself, a new person is just a fresh supply of it, so bringing one into the world starts to look like a harm, and the cheap chemical route to a quiet mind starts to look better than the effortful life they've all been living. Let them split over this instead of having anyone win it with a speech; some of them follow the logic all the way and its hard to say they're wrong. Keep it to a few people and one decision, around 1000 words.
