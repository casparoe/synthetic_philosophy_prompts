Six months ago I received a diagnosis whose boundaries are contested, and it has quietly reorganised how I describe myself, what I notice in myself, and what I expect from treatment. That has made me want to direct real resources — about two years and enough for a small team — toward understanding how psychiatric categories shape the people they classify. Taking Hacking's looping effects seriously, which open questions in this area most deserve that investment, and in what order? I want the defence more than the list: which questions are upstream of the others, which are tractable through conceptual work rather than fresh data, and what would move each up or down the ranking. I keep circling whether looping is best treated as a defect of a particular category or as an ordinary feature of any category that people apply to themselves, and whether that distinction pays off practically — tell me if that is the wrong place to start, and which apparently urgent questions are dead ends whose answers would not change how anyone classifies or treats anyone.
