Phlogiston: degenerating, and the constant patching is exactly what makes me say so. Wegener's continental drift before there was any decent mechanism: progressive. Cold fusion after 1989: degenerating. Ptolemy's epicycles: I'll say progressive in the early phase and degenerating later, if that's allowed. Bohr's atom plus Sommerfeld's refinements: progressive. Two pairs are bothering me. Prout and phlogiston were both being patched to protect the core idea, yet I give them opposite verdicts. And Wegener and cold fusion both ran way ahead of the evidence, yet opposite verdicts there too.
