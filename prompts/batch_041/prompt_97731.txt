If a trait got fixed because it did X, then X is its function, full stop, even if it isn't doing X right now. The other side says no, a function is just whatever a part does that contributes to some capacity of the bigger system it sits in — you work out what the heart is for by asking what role pumping plays in circulation, not by digging through the fossil record. The history people say the system people can't tell the difference between a function and a mere accident (a heart that happens to make a noise isn't "for" making noise). The system people say the history people can't say what the function of a brand-new trait is before selection has had time to work, or what to say about a trait that was selected for one thing and now does something totally different, or vestigial stuff that doesn't do anything anymore. Everyone seems to agree on the actual biology and they just keep shouting past each other.
