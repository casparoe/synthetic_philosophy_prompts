I'm a features writer — I cover science and ideas for a monthly, which mostly means I get paid to read philosophy and then explain it to people on trains. Two years ago I did a long piece on the problem of evil and spent months on the phone with philosophers of religion, and the upshot is that I came out the other side beleiving a whole pile of things I can no longer keep straight. So here's my actual position, and I want you to audit it rather than defend it: (1) classical theism, the full package — God is omnipotent, omniscient and perfectly good, and I refuse any version of theism that quietly shrinks one of those three; (2) the free-will defense is the answer to the logical problem of evil — evil is the price of a world containing significantly free creatures, and I'm persuaded by the Plantinga-style argument that even an omnipotent God couldn't actualise a world of free creatures who always choose rightly; (3) though I'll admit I'm not a libertarian about free will. I think compatibilism is probably true, or at least I have no confidence that determinism is false, and the idea of a will that could have done otherwise under identical conditions strikes me as close to incoherent; (4) for the evidential problem — the sheer tonnage of suffering and how badly it's distributed — I fall back on skeptical theism: God's reasons, if there are any, are well past the edge of what creatures like us could grasp; (5) spiritually, the Irenaean, soul-making picture is the one that moves me, this life as a vale of soul-making where character is forged through real adversity rather than handed over ready-made; and (6) as a matter of pastoral practice I think it's monstrous to tell a grieving parent that their child's death was part of God's plan for them.

What I want from you is an audit: which of these are actually pulling against each other, ordered from the ones that do real damage down to the ones that only look like a problem. For each genuine clash, tell me the cheapest way to fix it and what that fix costs me — I'd rather surrender something small than lose the whole picture. And please don't just flag everything as being in tension; some of these I'm fairly confident sit fine together, and I want to know which ones those are and why. I've been going round in circles on this in my notebook for a month, so be specific about what has to give and don't treat the seperate problems as equally bad.
