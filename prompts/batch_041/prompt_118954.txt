There are about sixteen of us. Then I need a debrief that maps what happened back onto the thought experiment — the reader, the rules, the correspondence between the book and a brain, the persona that seems to emerge — and that turns the screw: what would have to be added before the book is conscious rather than merely simulating someone who is, and why the fluency of its answers cannot settle the question. Give me the setup, the rules and roles, a timeline, a note on what will predictably go wrong the first time I run it (people breaking character, the rule set being too rigid to answer anything, one person carrying the whole conversation) and how to handle each, and an honest statement of which feature of the idea the activity cannot reproduce and that I will have to supply myself.
