I am a doctoral candidate, and the position I have come to hold is this. Rational belief change in the face of uncertain evidence — evidence that does not arrive as probability 1 on any proposition you could name — is governed by Jeffrey conditionalization, and Jeffrey conditionalization is not merely one admissible updating rule among others but the general account of rational learning, of which ordinary conditionalization is only the limiting case in which the evidence comes in certain. To this I want to add a constraint I have been calling path-independence: when two pieces of uncertain evidence bear on the same question, the order in which they reach you should make no difference to the posterior you end up with. I take path-independence to be part of what it is for an updating rule to be rational, not an optional extra.

Tell me what would count against this package.
