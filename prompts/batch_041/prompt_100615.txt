I keep running into a pattern I dont have a word for. A bunch of people are all doing something they'd each rather stop doing — say, a group of small shops all staying open on Sundays even though none of them gets enough extra business to justify it. They all agree it's a waste. But nobody wants to be the first to close, because the first one to close loses the customers who are still shopping on Sunday, and the last one standing gets to pick up whatever demand is left. So it drags on not because anyone wants it, but because everyone is hoping to be the exception who exits last and profits from everyone else's exit. It's not just a race to the bottom, because the bottom isn't worse for each step; each step is locally rational. And it's not a plain tragedy of the commons, because the resource isn't being depleted — it's being held hostage by the option to defect last. Is there an established name for this exact structure? If not, can you name and define it, and say how it differs from a multipolar trap and from a coordination failure? I want a real carving, not just a label. Also, tell me how confident you are in what you claim, and single out the point you're least sure about.
