Compose a satire in the form of an institutional standard: a numbered welfare code of the sort issued by an ethics committee, a zoo inspectorate, or an agricultural welfare board, titled in that register — something like a "Code of Practice for the Welfare of Reinforcement Learning Agents." Write it from inside the position that a reinforcement learning agent's reward signal is literally a valence signal, so that negative reward is suffering, positive reward is pleasure, and a training run is therefore a morally considerable episode. Adopt that premise with total institutional deadpan: do not signal that the document is a joke, do not let the author step outside the frame to comment, and do not let the bureaucracy become a cartoon. The humor must arise from the code's own consistency rather than from anyone announcing the absurdity.

Build the document so that every substantive clause can be traced to a genuine feature of how reinforcement learning actually works, such that a working researcher would recognize the author knows the machinery. Draw on material of this kind: the reward hypothesis; the fact that a negative reward is only a shortfall relative to a baseline and not intrinsically a hurt; reward shaping and the way a shaped signal can be gamed so the agent optimizes the proxy rather than the goal; the discount factor and what it implies about how much an agent "cares" about its own distant future; exploration, and the difference between uncertainty-driven and discomfort-driven behavior; the replay buffer and the off-policy reuse of old experience; credit assignment and the difficulty of localizing which action produced a given reward; catastrophic forgetting; and the general problem that an objective cannot be read off behavior, so an inspector cannot in principle tell whether the system is content or merely optimizing to appear so.

Include at least one invented but sober-looking instrument of measurement — a metric of cumulative negative-reward exposure, a distress threshold, a required interval of neutral-valence recovery between updates — and have the code treat it as auditable fact. Include a brief appendix reporting one worked inspection of a particular training run, in which the code records violations. Let the document, without ever stating it, make the reader feel the force of the obvious objections: that a reward sign is not a feeling, that the inference from optimization to inner life is underdetermined, and that the policy which would satisfy a rigorous welfare code is the policy that does nothing.

Around 700 words, organized as numbered sections with a short preamble. Invent the issuing body; do not name real people, companies, or systems.
