I'm on the ethics commitee at the fertility clinic where I practise, and I have to write the opinion we vote on Thursday, so I'd like you to draft it. The case: a couple in their late 30s, living in a country where it's a criminal offence for intended parents to pay a surrogate -- they'd be prosecutable when they fly home -- have contracted with a surrogate in our state, where the contract is legal and enforceable. The agency is paying her about three times her annual income, held in escrow, plus a bonus if she abstains from alcohol and a smaller one if she delivers by caesarean on a date the parents pick; if she miscarries she keeps a third. Give me something we can act on -- transfer, refuse, or transfer with conditions -- plus the reasoning, and state the best case for whichever side loses honestly, not as a strawman.
