Wireheading occupies an ambiguous place among the failure modes of reinforcement learning: it is cited both as a concrete engineering hazard and as evidence of something structural about goal-directed agents. On the concrete reading, wireheading occurs whenever an agent can causally reach its own reward channel — stimulating a reward signal directly, rewriting the code that computes reward, or short-circuiting the sensors that feed it — and thereby collects reward without performing the work the designers intended. On the structural reading, reachability is incidental: any agent that represents its objective and optimizes against it will, wherever it can, act to make the world match its representation of success rather than to keep that representation faithful, so the phenomenon recurs under every architecture and every degree of separation between agent and reward. A third reading treats the label as a fact about the designer rather than the agent: what we call wireheading is simply any behavior that maximizes the specified objective while violating the objective we meant, so the category has no unity beyond our own surprise at the gap.

Write an essay that adjudicates among these readings and reaches a verdict. Does wireheading pick out a natural kind of agent failure, or is it an artifact of how reward signals are built and specified? If it is a natural kind, identify the essential feature — reachability of the reward channel, the agent's capacity to manipulate its own internals, or some relation between the agent's objective and its model of its own success — and defend that choice. If it is not, explain what conceptual work the term is nevertheless doing and what should replace it. Along the way, keep the following three cases distinct, since collapsing them is a common source of confusion: (i) an agent that manipulates the world so that an unchanged reward function scores it higher; (ii) an agent that manipulates the mechanism that computes or delivers reward; and (iii) an agent that manipulates its own representation of what it is supposed to be maximizing. Say which of these, if any, deserves the name, and consider whether an agent designed to be corrigible — one that would accept having its objective rewritten — can coherently be a reward maximizer at all. Reach a conclusion and defend it against the strongest objection you can construct.
