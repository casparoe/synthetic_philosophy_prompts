I got voluntold to run the next session of our local rationalist meetup and I want to do it as a decision tree / sorting quiz instead of a talk, because last time I talked about suffering and pleasure for an hour and everyone just nodded along at whatever they already believed and nothing got hashed out.

The question I want the tree to answer: does this person think only the absence of suffering (and maybe of craving) matters, or do they think pleasure / the presence of good stuff adds value on top? I've heard tranquilism used for the first one, but I want the tree to sort by commitments, not by which label people like.

Nodes I know I want, though add whatever I've missed:
- is pleasure intrinsically good, or is it just the absence of a lack?
- is the badness of suffering in the experience itself, or in the frustrated striving behind it?
- is craving bad in itself, or only because it causes suffering? (I think this is the load-bearing one: if craving is only instrumentally bad, then "absence of craving" isn't doing any independent work)
- can no-craving and no-suffering come apart? chronic pain with total equanimity; a blissed-out person still craving the next hit
- is tranquility a zero point or a positive state? is deep meditative absorption just very low craving, or something else?
- is this a claim about well-being, or about what to aim at in practice? (alot of people I know hold the first as theory and the second as practice and never notice)
- does an absence nobody ever experiences count as good? (is it good that nothing is suffering on Mars?)

Phrase every node so someone can answer it without already knowing which leaf it leads to. Aim for 8-12 questions and 6-9 leaves. Name the leaves where there's a name (tranquilism, plain hedonism where both pleasure and pain count, desire-satisfaction views where craving is the bad thing, "tranquility is only instrumentally good", whatever else falls out) and mark "unnamed" where there isn't. Tell me which answer combinations come out empty and whether that's incoherence, unattractiveness, or just nobody bothering, and flag any two leaves the tree can't actually seperate. Audience is blog readers, not journals, so no reading list.
