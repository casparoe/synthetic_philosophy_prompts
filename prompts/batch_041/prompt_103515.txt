In the jurisprudence reading group I sit in on, we have been using Nomic — the game whose rules let the players amend the rules, including the rules that say how rules are amended and what counts as winning — as a toy model of constitutional self-amendment. One member keeps invoking the old image of sailors who must rebuild their ship plank by plank while still afloat, never able to dry-dock and start again from the keel. Her deployment of it is argumentative, not decorative: she says it shows that the supposed paradox of self-amendment is a pseudo-problem. When the rest of us ask by what authority an amendment clause amends itself, or whether a clause that purports to be unamendable can entrench itself, or whether an amendment adopted under a clause it simultaneously repeals is valid, she replies that we are hunting for a dry-dock that does not exist. Law has no foundation outside itself and needs none; the rebuilding is continuous and the ship floats; the worry about circularity is a picture-induced illusion.

I would like an audit of the image rather than another round of intuitions. Set out the correspondence explicitly — what plays the ship, the planks, the sailors, the sea, the ship's plan, the dockyard where new planks are cut, the fact of staying afloat, the absent dry-dock; a table is welcome. Then tell me where the mapping breaks. I am particularly interested in what the image quietly imports: a shipwright and a design that stand outside the ship; a process of plank replacement that is causal and material and has a stable subject, whereas the legal question concerns validity, a normative relation; planks that do not themselves state how planks may be replaced; and a criterion of staying afloat that is fixed independently of the ship. Which of these breaks bear on the specific conclusion she draws — the deflation of the paradox — and which are harmless scenery? Does the deflationary conclusion survive once they are on the table, or was the image carrying it all along? If it does not survive, say what an honest image of legal self-amendment would look like, and whether a toy model of the Nomic kind can bear the weight she puts on it.
