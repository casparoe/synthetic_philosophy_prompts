Every version I've found is either "give everything you don't strictly need" or a hand-wave about reasonable limits with no way to actually find them. Meanwhile I've got real people in front of me who want a number, or at least a rule, and I get maybe twenty minutes per conversation and zero authority — whatever the procedure spits out, they have to be willing to sign their own name to it.

So what I want from you is an actual procedure I can run in that twenty minutes: the questions to ask, in order; what counts as a good enough answer to each; the point where it stops with a clear no; and the point where it has to stop and hand the thing back to the person because it's turned into a question about what they value rather than one with a fact of the matter.
