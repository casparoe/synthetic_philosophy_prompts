I've been keeping a list of propositions and marking which ones strike me as Wittgensteinian hinges rather than as things I know, doubt, or could give evidence for, and I'd like to know what principle my marks are tracking. My verdicts: "I have two hands," hinge; "the Earth existed before I was born," hinge; "2 + 2 = 4," hinge; "the name on my passport is my name," hinge; "I am not being deceived right now by an evil demon," hinge; "I'm a good judge of when someone is manipulating me," hinge; "the bar kept as the standard metre is one metre long," not a hinge, just a stipulation; "there are no unicorns," not a hinge, an ordinary empirical belief; "the universe is less than ten thousand years old," not a hinge for me, though I can imagine someone for whom it does that work, and I still want to say it's a belief they hold rather than a hinge. Here is the pair I can't reconcile: "no human has ever walked on the moon" does, I think, function for some people exactly the way my hinges function for me, and I refuse to grant that it's a hinge; whereas "my memory of what I did yesterday is broadly reliable" functions in the same way and I'm content to call it one. My tentative principle is that a hinge is whatever you would treat as exempt from doubt because doubting it would undermine the practice of asking for and giving reasons at all, but that principle seems to make hinges out of the moon-landing denial too, which I won't accept.

Is there a principle that gets all of these? I want to know whether it has to be framed in terms of the role a proposition plays in someone's practice or in terms of its epistemic status, whether the two can come apart, and how a hinge can later be surrendered and replaced, as I think has happened to propositions people once took entirely for granted. State the principle precisely, say what it implies about cases I haven't listed — a proposition about the reliability of a whole method of measurement, or about what counts as a reason — and tell me honestly which of my verdicts it cannot save and which one I should give up.
