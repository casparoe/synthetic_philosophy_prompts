Ok so I've been going down a rabbit hole on supertasks (infinitely many steps crammed into a finite time) and I have pretty strong gut reactions to some of them and not others, and I want to know if there's one principle behind my reactions or if I'm just being inconsistent.

Here's what I think, case by case:

- The lamp: a switch gets flicked at 1/2 sec, 3/4 sec, 7/8 sec... so infinitely many times before 1 second. At 1 second, on or off? My gut says there's just no fact of the matter. It's not on, not off, the setup doesn't pin it down.
- The urn: each step you drop in ten numbered balls and pull one out, ball 1 at step 1, ball 2 at step 2, and so on. Every ball gets pulled at some step, so at 1 sec the urn is definately empty. Not "underdetermined" -- empty. And if instead you always pull ball 10, 20, 30..., then at 1 sec infinitely many balls are left, also definite.
- Same urn but you pull a random ball each step: empty with probability 1.
- Zeno guy walking to the wall, half the distance each time: he definately gets there, no mystery.
- Adding 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8...: definately 1, no mystery.
- The marbles one: at 1/2 sec move marble 1, at 3/4 move marble 2, and so on. At 1 sec there's no marble left to move, so he's just done. Fine, determinate, nothing weird.
- The grim reaper one: if I make it to 1 sec then some reaper would have killed me, but there's no first reaper because every reaper is scheduled earlier than any one you name. My gut isn't "I die" -- it's that the setup is contradictory and I can't make it at all.
- The lamp again, but where flicking takes time (each flick eats a quarter of the time remaining, so the flicks can't keep up): now I feel like the lamp DOES have a definite state, cause at some point a flick just doesn't finish.
- A three-way lamp cycling off/dim/bright forever: same as the normal lamp, no fact.

So I've got cases with a definite answer (urn, sum, walk, marbles), cases with no fact (lamp, three-way lamp), and cases that seem incoherent (reaper). Is there one principle that sorts all of these the way I do? What I really can't get past is why the urn comes out determinate but the lamp doesn't, when both just "run the rule forever" with no last step. And what does your principle say about one I haven't listed -- a lamp toggled infinitely often, but where at t=1 a totally separate mechanism is also wired to force the switch off?
