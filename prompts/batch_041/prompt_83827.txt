Im in a moral responsibility seminar and I'm presenting in two weeks on Strawson's reactive attitudes. My thesis is that his position is stuck in a dilemma he never notices, and I need to know what the room is going to throw at me.

Ok heres the argument. You can read Strawson two ways. Either the reactive attitudes are what being morally responsible consists in -- you're responsible just in case resentment, guilt, indignation toward you are fitting, and there's nothing underneath that -- or else they're just our fallible way of tracking a fact about the agent (some capacity, some quality of will) that exists whether or not anybody feels anything. I want to argue the first reading is the one that does the anti-sceptical work he wants, but it has a consequence he'd hate: the hard determinist who takes up the objective attitude toward everyone isn't making a mistake, he's playing a different game, and there's no neutral fact to appeal to. Meanwhile the second reading just hands the sceptic back his question. I also want to push on the general reactive attitudes -- indignation on other people's behalf -- because that's where the constitutive story looks most like it's just describing social pressure rather than grounding anything.

The audience: a bunch of compatibilists who work on quality of will and will say the attitudes are responses to the agent's will, which determinism can't touch; one hard determinist who thinks Strawson is a compatibilist who won't say so out loud; one person who does empirically-informed work on moral judgment and wants the whole thing naturalised; and my supervisor, who knows the essay inside out and is allergic to "here's a dilemma" framings. The excuses-versus-exemptions thing is going to come up and I don't have a clean answer for how it fits my reading.

Can you give me the objections in the order they'll actually come, phrased the way each of these people would phrase them, which ones have real force vs which are just territorial, what to concede up front and what to hold, and a two-minute reply to each? And tell me the one question I'm secretly hoping nobody asks.
