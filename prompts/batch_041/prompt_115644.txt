so: write me a "which theory of voting do you actually hold?" quiz. ten questions, each one a concrete situation with two or three answer options, plus a scoring key that maps answer patterns onto positions. the hard constraint is that nobody should be able to tell where an answer leads while they're answering, so no question can just be "do you think voting is expressive or instrumental?" — every question has to be a case you could describe to someone who's never heard any of this.

i want these cases in there somewhere (add your own as well):
- you live in a district your side wins by 40 points no matter what. do you vote?
- a hypothetical perfect oracle tells you the result the day before the election. do you still go to the polling station?
- your ballot is secret, but everyone you know will be told *whether* you voted (not who for). does that change your answer?
- you can either spend two hours voting or two hours raising money for a cause you care about. which, and does the size of the election change it?
- your country has 1000 voters instead of 100 million. does your answer change, and which way?

the distinctions i actually want to sort people on (build the questions so that's what the answers reveal):
1. is the value of voting in the outcome, or in the act itself?
2. do you treat your own ballot causally (mine moves the count) or as a sample of what people like me do (mine is evidence about the group)?
3. if there's a duty here at all, is it a duty to vote, or a duty to vote well — i.e. could abstaining be the dutiful move for someone who's ignorant?
4. is the right frame "what if everyone like me does this", and does that give a different answer than "what if just i do this"?

give each position a short plain-english name (use the standard name if there is one, otherwise just make one up) and one sentence on its most awkward commitment. flag any two positions your quiz can't seperate, and note any answer combinations that don't line up with a real position — say whether that's because the combo is incoherent or just that nobody's bothered to hold it. dont send me a reading list, i won't read it.
