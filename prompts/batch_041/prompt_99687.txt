As it happens, Jones decides to vote for that candidate entirely on his own, for his own reasons, and the device never fires. Since the device would have made him vote that way no matter what, Jones could not have done otherwise; yet he is plainly morally responsible for his vote. So the ability to do otherwise is not necessary for moral responsibility, nor for free will more generally.
