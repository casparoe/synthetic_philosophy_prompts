The distinction I want to land is between genuine shared intention and the things that merely look like it from the outside: two people each independently deciding to clap (a coincidence of separate intentions), two drivers each stopping because the law says so (following a convention), and a stadium wave where each person is only reacting to their neighbor (local coupling with no shared aim).
