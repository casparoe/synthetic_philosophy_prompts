1. Eternalism / the block universe. Past, present and future are all equally real. 'Now' is indexical, like 'here' — there's no privileged moment.
2. Physics-first. The metaphysics of time should basically be read off fundamental physics, and fundamental physics has no objective present and no flow.
3. The future is genuinely open. Indeterminism is real, deliberation isn't a charade, and my choices could really go more than one way.
4. Future bias. It's rational to care about future suffering and to be indifferent to past suffering, because the past is over and done with. The past/future line matters for what I do.
5. Decision theory first. Honestly I care more about a picture of time that makes deliberation and decision theory come out sane than about one that matches the physics exactly — if the two clash, I'd rather bend the physics.
6. The arrow of time is real. Entropy going up, causes preceding effects — that's a genuine non-arbitrary feature of the world, not just a bookkeeping convention.

I cant tell which of these are actually pulling against each other and which just look like they do. Go through them and tell me which pairs are genuinely in tension, ranked by how bad each one is, and which ones are only apparently in tension and why the appearance is misleading.
