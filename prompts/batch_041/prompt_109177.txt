My husband wants to buy one of those propane mosquito traps for the backyard, the kind that lures them in with CO2 and holds them in a net. He says the box claims it takes thousands a night. I'm not sentimental about bugs -- I'll slap a mosquito without a second thought. But this is bugging me (ha) and I can't get it into words.

Two things. One is that from what I can tell they don't die quick in there, they just dry out over a day or so. A swat is nothing. That sounds like alot of hours of something, if a mosquito has anything it's like to be on the inside.

The other is the numbers. Everyone says insects are basically little robots with nothing going on in there. Ok, but nobody actually knows that, we can't ask them. So suppose there's a really small chance each one is having some kind of experience. Times thousands a night, all summer. I don't know what to do with that. The tiny chance and the huge number are pulling against each other and I can't tell which one wins.

Then my brother said if I'm going to think like that, what about the mites in my eyelashes, what about bacteria, where does it stop. That made me feel like the whole thing was silly, but I don't think it actually answered anything.

For what its worth there is a real reason to want the trap -- our daughter gets eaten alive every July and the repellent doesn't work on her.

Should I get it? And is there a way to think about this that doesn't just come down to whatever I happen to feel like on the day?
