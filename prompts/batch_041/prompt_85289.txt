I've been reading about Kepler, who spent a lot of his time casting horoscopes for noblemen while calling most astrology rubbish. What got me wasnt the hypocrisy, it's the shape of his defence: he didn't say the ordinary horoscope-mongers were right, he said they were wrong about nearly everything, and that what's really going on (as I understand it) is a kind of resonance between the angles of the planets and the soul. So the bits he kept were exactly the bits his own theory of harmony could make sense of, and the bits he binned were the ones it couldn't. I keep seeing the same move elsewhere: someone absorbs the science that was meant to kill a practice, then carries on with a slimmed-down version of it, re-described in the vocabulary of that science, so every old objection can be shrugged off with 'thats not what I mean by it'. (Defences of prayer do something like this — it isnt about asking for things, it's really about stillness.) Is there a name for this? I don't want just a label: tell me what the nearest established concepts are, how the thing I've described differs from each of them, and if nothing fits, coin a term and define it, with a boundary case or two where it's genuinely unclear whether the pattern is present. Give it to me as a numbered list of exactly five points, a sentence or two each, no intro and no conclusion.
