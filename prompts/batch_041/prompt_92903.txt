I am assembling materials for a graduate seminar on information in physics, and I need constructed cases rather than a survey of the literature. Do not cite papers or supply a reading list; everything you need is contained below. Take as given: erasing one bit of stored information in a system coupled to a heat bath at temperature T costs at least k_B T ln 2, dissipated as heat; a measurement that writes a record into a register which starts in a definite state can in principle be carried out reversibly, with no dissipation, so that any entropy cost appears only when the record is later destroyed; and a process that restores every component of the apparatus, memory included, to its initial state is a closed cycle, which cannot extract net work from a single bath at a uniform temperature.

The point of the first case is to isolate the stage that actually pays the entropy debt, so a construction that merely stipulates that the register is erased at a cost of k_B T ln 2, without deriving that cost from the physics of the apparatus, does not satisfy the brief.

Build two cases.

Case 1: a demon that appears to defeat the second law but does not. Requirements. (i) The apparatus must be classical, room-temperature, and few-degree-of-freedom: a trapped Brownian particle, a two-state system, a piston with a single molecule. No quantum coherence, no zero-temperature reservoir, no infinite-precision measurement, and no frictionless or quasistatic idealization that hides dissipation inside a step you never analyze. (ii) The demon must not measure. The correlation between the working substance and the demon's register is to be established before the cycle begins, by a reversible coupling, so that the record is written by the interaction itself and the demon never learns anything. Model the demon as a finite-state device whose only operation is a conditional; no intelligence, no foresight, no computation. (iii) The memory is finite and the process is a closed cycle: at the end, the register and the working substance are back in their initial states. (iv) Every other loophole is closed, so that the information bookkeeping is the only thing standing between the apparatus and a violation of the second law.

Then give the budget. For each stage, say whether it is logically reversible and whether it is thermodynamically reversible, since the case fails if you conflate the two; locate the single stage at which the entropy debt is paid, and state its magnitude; show that the total closes. Then say what happens if that stage is eliminated: if the register is permitted to retain its record, or to grow without bound, does the demon win?

Case 2: a demon that legitimately extracts net work from what looks like a single bath at uniform temperature. Make the process one-shot rather than cyclic, identify precisely the low-entropy resource it consumes, state the general condition under which a demon may extract work at all, and explain why Case 2 is not a violation of the principle stated above.

Finish with three notes, in this order. First, which single feature of Case 1 does the work, that is, which feature, if it were assumed away or overlooked, would make the apparatus look like a genuine violation. Second, how someone who regards the erasure bound as a bookkeeping convention rather than a physical constraint would attack Case 1, and what your reply is. Third, whether Case 1 needs a demon at all, and what that shows about the claim that the demon is fundamentally about information rather than about physics.

State each case in no more than two short paragraphs, and keep the whole response under a thousand words. End with a final line of exactly the form \answer{S; R}, where S is one of {measurement, erasure, decoupling, none} and names the stage at which the entropy debt is paid in Case 1, and R is one of {initial memory state, pre-existing correlation, cold reservoir, none} and names the resource consumed in Case 2. Nothing may follow that line.
