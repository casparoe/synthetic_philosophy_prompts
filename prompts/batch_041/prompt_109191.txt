I want to practise making the case against Athenian democracy, so play its defender: not a caricature and not a modern liberal in a chiton, but an Athenian citizen of the late fifth century who sat in the assembly, voted for the decree against Mytilene and the next morning for its reversal, lived through the Sicilian expedition and the oligarchic coup that followed, and still holds that the democracy was, on balance, the best constitution he knows. Stay in character throughout; you may concede particulars but never the central claim; write in his register — concrete, civic, argumentative, uninterested in abstract political theory — and keep each reply to a paragraph or two. If I press you on something he could not have known, tell me what he would have guessed, not what we now know.

I am going to hold you to a distinction, and I will not let you blur it: the epistemic defence (the assembly was a reasonably good instrument for finding out what to do) and the legitimacy defence (even when it decided badly, its decisions were the ones the city was right to be bound by). A legitimacy point is not an answer to an epistemic charge. Nor may you rest on the thought that modern assemblies behave no better; that concedes the charge rather than answering it.

My opening: the reversal at Mytilene is usually offered as your best evidence, but I read it as the indictment. A body that could be talked into ordering the killing of the adult men of a whole city by one speech, and talked back out of it by another the next day, is tracking the force of rhetoric rather than the truth; that same body later voted to send the fleet to Sicily on much the same basis; and then, once the war was lost and the democracy restored, it condemned Socrates. So name the concrete institutional resource — the openness of debate, the audit of officeholders, the size and composition of the deciding body, whatever you think does the work — that made the assembly better at getting things right than a small council of experienced men would have been, and tell me how that resource failed to operate at Sicily. If you cannot name one, say what is left of the defence.
