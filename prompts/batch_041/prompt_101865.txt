On that picture, asking what would've happened if the Armada had landed, or if the Confederacy had won at Gettysburg, is just re-running the experiment with a different setting on the dial. And the conclusion she wants out of it is that historical outcomes aren't inevitable and that we can establish causal claims about history with something close to experimental rigor.
