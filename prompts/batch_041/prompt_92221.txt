What I want from you: what would actually count against that? And I don't mean the lazy "it's unfalsifiable" hand-wave, I mean the specific stuff. Take something minimal -- a slime mold tracking a gradient, a gene regulatory network, whatever -- and tell me whether FEP is making a prediction about it or just re-describing it after the fact. Which assumptions in the derivation are the load-bearing ones that could fail in principle? Is there anything from neuroscience or ecology that's already awkward for it?

Then rank all of this from "would sink the strong version" down to "mildly embarrassing," and be clear about which of these the FEP crowd would themselves concede counts against them versus which they'd wave off as a misunderstanding. And give me an honest read on whether the strong version I stated has already been qualified into unfalsifiability by its defenders -- and if it has, whether that's actually a problem for a theory like this or just the normal fate of any big unifying framework.
