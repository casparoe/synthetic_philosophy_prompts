The same judge will then throw out a witness who says 'the defendent told me he did it,' because that's hearsay — also somebody's say-so, only the somebody isn't sitting there. And the whole trial runs on a witness squinting at something that happened four years ago and saying 'I remember it.' So that's three different attitudes to being told something, and I can't tell whether they're three consistent applications of one theory of knowledge or three different theories wearing the same robe.
