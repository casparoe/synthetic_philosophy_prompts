Ok, sanity check before I put this in a chapter. I've ended up with this package on Sleeping Beauty and I'm 90% sure it hangs togther, but I want someone to actually push on it:

1. Halfer. The coin's fair, I take the chance-credence link seriously, so when Beauty wakes her credence in heads is 1/2, and the Tuesday waking does nothing for me.
2. Self-locating beliefs are ordinary beliefs. "I'm awake now", "today is Monday" — real propositions I have credences in, obeying the probability axioms like anything else. I update by conditionalizing on my total evidence, self-locating evidence included.
3. So if the experimenter tells me it's Monday, I'm still at 1/2 on heads. Being told the day shouldn't move me about the coin.
4. Self-locating evidence is real evidence about the world — finding out I'm in Australia tells me things about the planet — but the fact that I'm awake now tells me nothing about the coin, and neither does the fact that tails would get me woken twice. Getting woken more times isn't evidence about a coin.
5. I'd take an even-money bet on heads at any awakening. At 1/2 that's a fair bet and I'd be a coward to refuse it.

Which pairs are actually in tension and how bad, ranked, which ones just look like they clash, and for each real one what's the cheapest thing to give up and what it costs me. I'd rather hear that one item is the problem than that everything needs qualifying.
