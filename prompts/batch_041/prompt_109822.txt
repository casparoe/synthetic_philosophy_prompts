I am a control theorist working on partially observable stochastic systems in which an agent's actions simultaneously affect the hidden state and generate observations, and in which the objective is sometimes not fixed in advance but constructed through the agent's own deliberation and interaction with other agents. I want to understand Dewey's experimentalism about democracy and inquiry by restating it in the vocabulary I actually use. For reference, the Deweyan position I have in mind is this: inquiry is the controlled transformation of an unsettled, problematic situation into a determinate one that supports a fallible claim warranted by its consequences; democracy is not merely a form of government but a shared way of life sustained by communication and by the use of social intelligence on shared problems; and experimentalism treats both inquiry and democracy as continuous, self-correcting processes of learning from the consequences of action. Please give me exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion. Items 1-3 should translate, respectively, Dewey's account of inquiry, his account of democracy, and his experimentalism into the vocabulary of belief-state estimation, information-gathering policies, multi-agent coordination, common knowledge, preference aggregation, online learning, exploration, and robust control. Item 4 should report the residue of distortion: what in Dewey's position had to be bent to fit the control-theoretic vocabulary, and whether the bending reveals a limitation of the vocabulary or a lack of clarity in Dewey. Item 5 should report the residue of omission: what in Dewey's position has no counterpart at all in the control-theoretic vocabulary, and whether that absence is a defect of the formalism, a defect of Dewey's formulation, or a genuine feature of the subject matter that the formalism cannot capture. Do not smuggle control-theoretic assumptions into the translation and call them Dewey; keep the translation honest, and treat what fails to translate as the interesting part.
