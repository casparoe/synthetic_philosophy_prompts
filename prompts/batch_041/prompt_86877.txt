I'm a third-year PhD student (philosophy of mind, but I hang around the AI safety crowd a lot) and I'm trying to write a chapter on what the current practice of AI control quietly assumes. I keep going round in circles, so I'd like you to help me pin it down.

Here's the practice I want to get at. Say a lab has a strong model they don't fully trust and a weak model they mostly do. The standard protocol is: the weak model monitors the strong one's outputs and flags anything suspicious; you also don't tell the strong model when it's being tested, so it can't behave differently under observation; and you design the setup so that even if the strong model is actively trying to subvert you, the worst case is bounded. Everyone doing this is quite explicit that they can't verify the strong model is aligned — that's the whole point of the approach.

What bugs me is the apparant inconsistency. The same people will tell you the model's chain-of-thought might be an unfaithful rationalisation and shouldn't be taken at face value — but they'll also ask the model to explain its reasoning and then use that explanation in the safety case. They'll say it's an open question whether these systems literally have beliefs or goals — and then build a protocol around the assumption that the thing has goals, knows it's being watched, and will strategically hide. And the evaluators are deceiving the model about when it's under test while demanding honesty from it.

So: what does this practice actually presuppose? I'd like you to reconstruct the implicit theory — what view of belief/desire attribution, of verification, of deception, and of the moral asymmetry between evaluator and evaluated it only makes sense under. Which parts does it strictly need, as opposed to merely sitting comfortably with? Where is it underdetermined, such that several rival views would license the very same protocol? And what would have to change about the protocol if you swapped the presupposed view for a deflationary one — say, no real beliefs or goals, just behavioural dispositions?
