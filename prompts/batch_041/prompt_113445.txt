I am a novelist at work on a book whose protagonist is a field biologist studying a species that performs a long, invariant sequence of actions — one that can be derailed by a trivial interference, after which the animal begins the sequence again from the beginning rather than patching it. I am inventing the species, so nothing in what follows should rest on the truth of any particular anecdote from the ethological literature. What I need is not a survey of a debate but a single worked example of a conceptual issue I can build a scene around.

Please give me one example: a described case, specific enough to stage, in which an agent's behavior is at once flexible and mechanical — flexible in that it is responsive to the details of the situation and would have gone differently had those details differed, mechanical in that this very responsiveness belongs to a fixed repertoire the agent cannot revise from the inside. To keep the example from collapsing into the textbook case, make it a case of repair rather than restart: the agent patches the disruption, and patches it well, but patches it the same way every time. Name the behavior, the interference, and the repair, and say what would have to vary from one occasion to the next for the repair to count as improvisation rather than as one more fixed item in the repertoire.

Then, for that same example:

1. Give the strongest case for each verdict — that the behavior is genuine flexibility, and that it is mere mechanism — and say which single consideration would settle the matter, or explain why none could.
2. Say whether the same two criteria, applied to a person deliberating over a difficult choice, can be made to pull apart in the same way. If they can, say what that shows; if they cannot, say exactly where the disanalogy lies.
3. Tell me where a novelist would have to cheat for the scene to work: what it needs to be true of the agent that we do not know, or that is arguably true of nothing.

I will be using the result to write, so I need to know how far to trust it. State how confident you are that the example really exhibits the tension rather than merely appearing to — in particular, that the second criterion is not just the first criterion restated one level up — and single out the point you are least sure of.
