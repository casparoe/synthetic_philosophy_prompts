At the same time, the subject has an occurrent higher-order representation of that state, but the representation is existential rather than de re: it represents only that the subject is in some first-order state or other, not which state it is. The case may not use subliminal stimuli, masked priming, blindsight, brain damage, demons, or any science-fictional device, and it must be describable in a single paragraph. The purpose of the case is to isolate the role of content-specificity in the higher-order representation, so a case that simply stipulates the verdicts rather than generating them from the theories will not work. Please do not provide a bibliography, reading list, or citations; I need the constructed case and the reasoning.
