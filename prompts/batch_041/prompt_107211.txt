Topic: ascription universality vs epistemic dominance. Quick gloss in case the terms are fuzzy (I'll define them, I don't want to lean on you having read the same stuff I have): a mind is ascription-universal if there's no other mind it systematically misreads -- for any mind M, it can ascribe to M the beliefs and desires that M would ascribe to itself. The camp I want to argue against says a genuinely general intelligence has to be ascription-universal, so there's no such thing as a mind that gets to be the final authority on another mind's beliefs and desires -- that kind of dominance isn't just rude, it's impossible.
