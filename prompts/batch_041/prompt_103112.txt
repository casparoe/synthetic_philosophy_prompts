I sit as the lay member on my university's research ethics committee. I have no training in ethics or biology, but I have been thinking about this on my own. A proposal before us would breed a line of laboratory mice with a greatly reduced capacity for pain, on the ground that this would reduce the total suffering caused by invasive research and move us towards abolishing suffering through biotechnology. I am drawn to that goal, but I am uneasy that pain is not merely a burden: it is also how an animal protects itself, and the mice might still feel fear, frustration and chronic stress. Please draft the short written opinion I shall circulate before Thursday's meeting. It should recommend a concrete course of action, with a time frame and any conditions, and it should engage seriously with the strongest argument on the other side rather than simply restating the principle of reducing suffering.
