I know my own body behaves in certain ways when I have certain feelings — I wince when something hurts, I smile when I'm pleased. And I know from the inside that those bodily goings-on come with the feelings attached. Now I watch other bodies doing the same wince-y, smile-y things. I can't observe any feelings in them. So I infer by analogy: since their bodies resemble mine in the observable respects, they probably resemble mine in the unobservable respect too, i.e. there's a feeling behind the wince. Since this is an induction from essentially one case (mine), it can only give probability, not certainty.
