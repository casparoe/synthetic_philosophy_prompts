so i've been reading a bunch of forecasting stuff and arguing with a friend about whether the US is basically Rome, and i've ended up with a package of views that i'd like someone to actually stress-test instead of nodding along to. here's where i've landed:

(1) for anything more than a few years out, the outside view beats the inside view, full stop. if it's a choice between a compelling story about why this time is different and a reference class of similar cases, i'll take the reference class.

(2) so the right way to think about where the US, or China, or the EU ends up in fifty years is to build a reference class of past great powers and hegemons and look at the distribution of what actually happened to them — how long they lasted, how they went out, whether it was a soft landing or a collapse.

(3) that works best when the class is big and the members are more or less independent of each other, which is what makes it a distribution instead of one anecdote with footnotes.

(4) at the same time i keep coming back to the thought that the present situation is genuinely unprecendented — nuclear weapons, global supply chains, everything digital, no single hegemon like this before. so i don't think there's actually a good reference class for us.

(5) the classic decline stories, the ones about why Rome fell or why Britain declined, are mostly hindsight curve-fitting and i don't trust them at all.

(6) but i also don't think you can forecast off base rates alone. you need some mechanism, some account of how the thing actually works, or you're just saying states like this usually do that with no idea why.

(7) and i think forecasters should give a spread of outcomes with probabilities attached, not a point prediction.

does this hang together? i have the feeling something in here is pulling against something else but i can't tell where the fault line is. tell me which of these are genuinely in tension and which only look like it, rank the real ones by severity, and then commit: say which one has to go, and if i could only change one of them, which single change buys me the most consistency and what would i be giving up by making it. please don't hand me a menu of options and leave me to pick — take a position.
