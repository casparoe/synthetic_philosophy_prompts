My line is basically two claims. Second, the fight everyone has (how you kill them) is a distraction; the real action is routine husbandry — eyestalk ablation on broodstock (cutting a female's eyestalk off to make her spawn), the crowding and handling, and the fact that there's no agreed humane slaughter protocol that most of the industry actually uses.
