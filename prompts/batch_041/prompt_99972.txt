2. Craving and suffering are genuinely separate items on that list. There's suffering that isn't craving (raw pain, say) and craving that isn't suffering (a mild, bearable itch for something trivial).
3. Consequentialism: the right act is the one that maximises total intrinsic value, summed impersonally across everyone affected.
4. I'm pretty sure a shorter, richer life — full of striving, appetite and the odd bit of anguish — is the better one, or at least it's the one I'd choose for myself over a longer, flatter, craving-free sort of existence.
5. It's a serious wrong to override someone's settled, informed preferences about their own life, even when you're confident it would be better for them.
6. I don't believe in free-floating value. When suffering is bad, it's bad *for* whoever's suffering; there's no extra badness just sitting there in the world once you've subtracted everyone's experience.
7. I love music and good food and would honestly grieve if I lost them.
