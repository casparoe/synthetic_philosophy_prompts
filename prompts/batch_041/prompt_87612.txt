What I actually need is a plan for how to explain the one big idea — that a research programme has a core you're not allowed to touch, a belt of side-assumptions you keep patching up, a recipe telling you what to work on next, and then a verdict of progressive or degenerating depending on whether it keeps predicting new things or just keeps explaining away its old failures — in about fifty minutes with a whiteboard.

So: what has to come first, what depends on what, and where exactly do I slot in the difference between a programme being stuck and a programme being wrong, because I keep muddling those two myself and I don't want to pass the muddle on.
