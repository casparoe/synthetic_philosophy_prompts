I'm a journalist who mostly covers science and ideas, and for the past few months I've been reporting a long piece on the "performance legitimacy" argument — the claim that governments earn the right to rule by delivering growth, stability and order, and that this is why you can't just call China or Singapore unfree and be done with it. I've now interviewed enough people on both sides that I've started to notice my own views don't obviously hang together, and before I commit to the framing I want to know which of my commitments are actually pulling against each other. Here's roughly where I've landed: (a) legitimacy is a real property of a government, not just whatever its population happens to accept — there's a fact of the matter, and whole societies can be wrong about it; (b) a government that delivers sustained prosperity and order to its people thereby earns a serious kind of legitimacy, not a consolation prize you grant while waiting for elections; (c) the People's Action Party in Singapore and the CCP in China are, on balance, legitimate governments of their societies; (d) I'm a democrat — being answerable to the governed through real contestation is the central thing legitimacy is about, not a nice add-on to it; (e) the "Asian values" move, that Confucian societies just don't want individual rights and elections the way Western ones do, strikes me as mostly a rationalization cooked up by incumbents; (f) if you told me China's economy had stagnated for twenty years and the leadership had held on anyway, I'd say its legitimacy was gone.

I suspect some of these can't all be true at once, but I genuinely cant tell which one is doing the real damage and which are just awkward-sounding next to each other — and I'd rather find out from you now than have a philosopher I'm about to interview do it to me on the record. So walk me through it: which pairs are genuinely in tension and how bad each is, which ones only look like tensions and why they dissolve, and for each real clash what the cheapest fix would be and what I'd be giving up by taking it. Please discriminate rather than telling me everything is in tension somewhere. And since I'll be leaning on this for a piece, say how confident you are in each call and single out the one you're least sure about.
