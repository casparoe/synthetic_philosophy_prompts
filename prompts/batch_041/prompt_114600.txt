Please translate the falsificationist position into my statistical vocabulary as faithfully as you can: the logical asymmetry between confirming and refuting a general claim, the methodological rule that a theory must forbid something, the treatment of ad hoc rescue as the mark of a degenerating line of research, and the demarcation criterion itself.
