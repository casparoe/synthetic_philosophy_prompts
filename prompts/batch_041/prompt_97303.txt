Use concrete cases to separate the branches, e.g. a single hour of intense pain versus any number of hours of mild pleasure, a pinprick that happens to a million people, a life that is mostly pleasant but contains one unbearable episode, and what I call the trapdoor: a tiny extra pain that seems to flip the whole comparison, so that a life with slightly more suffering is judged worse than any amount of mild contentment.
