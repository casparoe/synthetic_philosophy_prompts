I am presenting a paper to a formal epistemology reading group in which I argue that the Judy Benjamin problem is best resolved not by minimizing relative entropy subject to a constraint on the conditional probability P(B|A), but by modeling the announcement as the output of a partially reliable source with a selection rule: the source first decides whether to speak, then, if speaking, chooses among true conditional statements according to a rule that may depend on the state.
