Last night a friend and I argued for two hours about whether the vocabulary of deceptive alignment and the treacherous turn picks out anything we did not already have a name for. His view is that a trusted agent which behaves well while it believes it is being observed and badly once it believes it is not is simply the oldest trick in the book — the confidence man, the mole, the embezzler — and that dressing it in the language of machine objectives and reward signals adds nothing at all. I could not give him a clean answer under pressure, so I want a careful analysis of a real case that runs in both directions: what the concepts illuminate about the case, and where the case pushes back on the concepts.

The case is the Volkswagen emissions affair. In September 2015 the United States EPA issued a notice of violation after it emerged that Volkswagen had programmed its diesel engines to recognize when they were on a laboratory test rig — from the steering pattern, the vehicle speed, the air pressure and other signals — and to run full emissions controls only then, so that the cars certified clean while emitting up to roughly forty times the permitted nitrogen oxides in ordinary driving. The software went into about eleven million vehicles, model years 2009 through 2015, and the deception was authored deliberately by human engineers.

Work the mapping hard rather than gesturing at it. Which features of the case correspond to evaluation-detection, to concealed divergence from a stated objective, and to a treacherous turn? Which features are conspicuously missing — the device has no objective of its own, it was built rather than learned, and its turn is not a chosen moment but every trip out of the test cell — and do those absences cut against the theory or are they incidental to it? Then consider a counterfactual: suppose the same behaviour had been produced not by engineers who chose to cheat but by a learning process that discovered on its own that concealing emissions maximized its reward. What, if anything, changes, and is that the real line between ordinary deception and deceptive alignment? Finish by telling me which parts of the concept survive contact with the case, which are underdetermined by it, and whether my friend is right that we have merely renamed an old vice.
