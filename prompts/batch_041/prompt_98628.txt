ok so this is kind of a weird question but theres this thing i keep running into and i dont know if it has a name.

so me and my friend were arguing about what our school should spend the fundraiser money on. i wanted the new weight room, she wanted to keep the music program alive. we went back and forth for like a week and nobody convinced anybody. then we made a deal: id vote for her music thing if she voted for my weight room thing, and we'd each campaign for the others cause. and it worked — we both got more of what we wanted than if we'd just kept fighting. but heres the thing, neither of us changed our mind about whats actually important. she still thinks music matters more, i still think sports matter more. we just... traded.

and its not like a normal compromise where you meet in the middle and both walk away a little unhappy. we both walked away feeling like we won. we won by giving up something on a thing we cared about less, to get something on a thing we cared about more. and it feels kinda wrong, like your not supposed to be able to do that with values? like you cant put a price on your principles, but we basically did.

another time it was me and my brother dividing up who has to do which chores, and we did the same thing — i took the dishes all week so he'd take the trash and the bathroom, and we were both happier than splitting everything evenly, even though we never agreed on which chores are worse.

is there a name for this? i tried googling trading moral values and got a bunch of stuff about moral relativism which is not what i mean. i want to know what this move actually is and where its edges are. like is it the same as logrolling in politics, or horse trading, or is it its own thing? hows it different from a regular compromise where both people give something up? does it only work when the two people care about different things, or can you do it with two people who care about the same things in different amounts? and does it stop being ok if one of us actually thinks the others thing is bad and not just less important?

please dont just give me a word. i want the concept carved up — what it is, what it isnt, and when it actually counts as this rather than something else.
