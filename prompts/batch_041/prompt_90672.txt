When philosophers distinguish knowing how to do something from knowing that something is the case, they usually leave open where the know-how is supposed to reside. I want the two main answers compared.

The first answer puts it in the knower. To know how to swim, on this view, is to possess an ability constituted by states and dispositions of the agent — a practical grasp that survives changes in circumstance and is lost only when something changes in the agent. A paralysed swimmer still knows how to swim, much as a locked door is still a door: the knowledge is blocked rather than diminished.

The second answer puts it in the relation between knower and world. On this view, know-how is a standing relation between an agent and a situation that affords the activity, and it is carried partly by instruments, layouts, routines, and other people. Strip away the surgeon's theatre, the navigator's stars, or the rower's crew, and the know-how itself is diminished rather than merely prevented from being exercised.

Please compare the two. Address at least the following: which gives the better account of the skilled practitioner who cannot say what she is doing, and whether that fact is evidence for either side; which gives the better account of the person who can recite a procedure perfectly and still cannot perform it; whether the two are genuine rivals or answers to different questions — the constitution of a state versus the conditions of its exercise — and if the latter, what of substance remains in dispute; whether the second answer collapses the distinction between knowing how and knowing that or merely relocates it, taking as your test cases a written procedure that any competent reader could follow and a tool that only a competent user can use; and what each answer makes of two awkward phenomena, namely that know-how appears to come in degrees while knowing-that does not, and that a crew can do something none of its members can do alone. Conclude by saying which answer you would defend and naming the single case that does the most damage to the one you reject.
