Write a short story about a guy who's taken a vow never to utter a false sentence — and who quietly ruins people's lives with it, because everything he says is literally true while being engineered to decieve. I'm after that awkward seam in Augustine's account of lying: he treats a lie as tied to both saying something false and wanting to deceive, so what do you call it when you've got all the deceiving and none of the false words? Put a second character in who knows the usual Augustinian line — every lie is a sin, but merely keeping quiet about the truth is allowed — and let her slowly realize that what the truth-teller does doesn't fit either box. Show it, don't have anyone stand up and lecture.
