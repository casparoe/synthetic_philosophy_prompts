The expected value series is 1 - 1/2 + 1/3 - 1/4 + ... which is conditionally convergent, so by rearranging the terms you can make it sum to anything you want. Undefined. My friend says this is a fatal problem for expected utility theory -- theres a game with no expected value, so the theory can't tell you what to do, yet intuitively you should play it (or at least, some people think so). I say the theory is fine, or can be patched: you compare the Pasadena game to the Altadena game (same thing but every payoff is a dollar higher) and the Altadena dominates, so you prefer Altadena to Pasadena even though both have undefined EV.
