My thesis is that Malthus's core claim wasn't refuted by the Industrial Revolution, it was outrun — and that there's a real difference between a theory being early and a theory being rescued rather than patched. The part I'm most attached to, and havent seen anyone make, is this: being early is only an available verdict for theories that predict a trend, not for theories that predict an event by a date; the latter can only be wrong or immunized. And my reading (maybe wrong) is that later Malthus already had the escape hatch inside the model via preventive checks, so the transition isn't the bolt-on everyone says it is.
