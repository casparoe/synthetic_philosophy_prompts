I'm a science writer and I've been trying to finish this piece about the unilateralist's curse — the idea that when alot of poeple could each just go ahead and do a thing on their own, and they're all somewhat likely to be wrong about whether it's actually a good idea, the thing tends to get done anyway, because the one person who's sure it's fine doesn't need anybody's permission. More actors, more chances somebody pulls the trigger. It's the reason "we all agreed not to" is basically a weather report.

Anyway I need a joke for the top of it. Not a nerd joke where the punchline is just the word "Bayes" — it should actually turn on the machinery: the many independent actors, the unilateral option, the fact that being in the minority doesn't stop you, and the way the whole thing tilts toward action. Bonus if it also sneaks in why the usual proposed fix — everybody just agreeing not to act alone when they might be the odd one out — is itself kind of a joke, since the person who'd break that agreement is definately the person who isn't in the room when you make it.

Two versions please: one that runs about four lines, and one that's a single brutal line. And no, "so anyway I started blasting" is not a joke, it's a diagnosis.
