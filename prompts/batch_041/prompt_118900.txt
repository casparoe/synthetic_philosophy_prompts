Ok, I need to get my head straight on dispositions and I keep flip flopping, so I want an actual number out of you.

Everyone knows the plain conditional analysis is a bust: x is disposed to break when struck iff it would break if struck. Finks kill it (the glass that gets made un-fragile at the very moment you tap it, so the counterfactual comes out false even though the thing is fragile), and masks kill it too (the fragile glass wrapped in foam, which wouldn't break, while its fragility sits there perfectly intact).

Here's the view I want you to price. Dont worry about whether someone's already said it, I just want it assessed on its merits. Treat a disposition ascription as fixing a profile rather than a conditional: for each context — and contexts differ in which interferers are around, finks, masks, mimics, whatever — the ascription assigns a probability of manifestation, and the disposition just is that whole mapping. 'Fragile' is then true of the glass iff its profile assigns a high manifestation-probability in the characteristic context, the one with no interferers; the foamed glass is still fragile because that entry is still high, its actual context is just a masking one.

Whats your credence that this profile view is the correct account of ordinary disposition ascriptions (fragile, soluble, irritable) and not just a redescription of the problem? Decompose it, a number and one line of justification for each:

(a) that finks and masks are genuine counterexamples rather than artifacts of how we individuate dispositions;
(b) that the profile view really beats a ceteris paribus conditional instead of just renaming it;
(c) that it isn't too cheap — if a disposition is any old mapping from contexts to manifestation probabilities, what stops every object from having every disposition, since some contrived context always delivers the manifestation;
(d) that a disposition can still be something over and above its causal basis once the basis's behavior is baked into the profile.

Then the combination. I'm at about 0.3. Which of those four is my number most sensitive to, and what would actually move it — a case or an argument, not a reading rec. And tell me what would count as the view being refuted rather than just being inconvenient.
