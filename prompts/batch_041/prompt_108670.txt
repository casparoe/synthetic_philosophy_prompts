My roommate and I got into a two-hour argument last night about antinatalism — he'd just finished Benatar and was insufferably pleased with himself, and by the end I was maybe a third convinced and thoroughly annoyed. The asymmetry comes in pieces — pain bad, pleasure good, the absence of pain good even when there's nobody around to benefit, the absence of pleasure not bad unless someone's deprived — and every time I pushed on the "even when there's nobody around" piece he'd say that's not the hinge, the real hinge is somewhere else, and then we'd just go in circles. We never settled it, and I've been stewing about it all day.
