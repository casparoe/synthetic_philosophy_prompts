Under it, every varsity member signs a contract committing to a set number of practice hours a week all summer; anyone who misses more than a few sessions loses their varsity spot and forfeits the team's share of the tournament fees their family has already paid. Her argument is the part I cannot answer: "If we don't do this, they will, and then we lose. We're not the ones escalating; we're responding." Two things bother me.
