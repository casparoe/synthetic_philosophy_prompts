Ok so I fell down a Wikipedia hole about Belgium and hit something I can't stop chewing on. In 1990 the Belgian parliament passed a law decriminalising abortion. The king at the time, Baudouin, was a serious Catholic and told the PM, Wilfried Martens, that he couldn't sign it — his conscience wouldn't let him. In Belgium a law doesn't come into force without the king's signature (royal assent), so this was heading straight for a constitutional crisis. The workaround they landed on: the government declared the king unable to govern, stepped into his authority for a bit, enacted the law themselves, and then parliament voted to put him back on the throne the next day. He was out for something like 44 hours. Law passed, king's conscience intact, monarchy survived.
