I'm writing up my commitments on indicative conditionals for a workshop and would like an audit before I commit to them. What I hold: (1) indicative conditionals express propositions with truth conditions, so "probably" can take them in its scope; (2) those truth conditions are not the material conditional's — I am certain it won't rain, hence certain that either it won't rain or the match will be called off, yet I am not certain that if it rains the match will be called off; (3) my probability for a simple conditional is just my conditional probability for the consequent given the antecedent; (4) that holds under supposition too: my probability for "if A then B", given C, is my probability for B given A and C; (5) conditionals embed freely — under negation, conjunction, disjunction, and inside other conditionals — with the truth-functional connectives keeping their ordinary classical behaviour, and I accept the conditional excluded middle, that either if A then B or if A then not-B; (6) I accept the Ramsey test as the account of acceptance: accept "if A then B" just in case, having added A to your beliefs, you would accept B; (7) contraposition fails for indicatives, so "if it rains the match is called off" does not yield "if the match isn't called off, it didn't rain"; (8) the equation in (3) covers every indicative conditional, including relevance-flavoured ones like "if you're hungry, there's leftover pizza in the fridge", which I take to be true in the ordinary way. I have been told the package is inconsistent; I assume that is the old point that the conditional-probability equation cannot be combined with a material-conditional reading, and since that reading is exactly what (2) denies, I take myself to be safe.

Which of these are genuinely in tension, which only appear to be, and how badly does each real tension bite? For every real tension, give the cheapest repair and what it costs; and if a single revision would clean up the most, say which and why. Lead with your one-sentence verdict on which commitment has to go, then the reasoning, with no preamble and no restating of my list.
