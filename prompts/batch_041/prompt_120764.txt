Right, odd one — I need a joke for something I'm putting together, and it can't be a generic one about AI doom or robots taking over. The thing I want it to be about is the unilateralist's curse. Quick version in case it's not on your radar: say five people can each independantly decide to do a thing, and any one of them going ahead is enough for it to happen. Each of them privately works out whether it's worth doing, and does it if they reckon yes. The catch is that the thing gets done if *any single one* of them reckons yes, so as you add more people it becomes near-certain to happen — even when most of them think its a bad idea, and even when it really is a bad idea. The proposed fix is that everyone agrees in advance to defer to the majority judgement, which is meant to be the sensible move, though it does sound a bit like agreeing in advance to join a cult.

So: write me a joke that actually turns on this. It should land for someone who knows the idea — i.e. the punchline should hinge on something true about the structure, like the gap between "the group thinks it's a good idea" and "one member of the group thinks it's a good idea", or the way that adding more sensible, cautious people makes the outcome worse rather than better. Not just a nerd joke with the phrase "existential risk" dropped in. Bonus if you can also nod to the mirror-image case, where any one person could stop a bad thing but everyone assumes someone else will do it. A bit of dialogue or a small scene is fine, it doesnt have to be a one-liner.
