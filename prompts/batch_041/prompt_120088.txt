Here's the proposition, stated so it can come out true or false: in a mature cognitive science, 'belief' will have the same fate as 'gene'. The word survives, but it stops naming one natural kind — you get a cluster of partly stipulative, context-specific notions (the transmission-genetics gene, the molecular gene, whatever the evo-devo people mean by it) and no agreement about whether the folk notion got vindicated or junked. Call that fragmentation. The rivals are (a) elimination — 'belief' goes the way of 'caloric', nothing in a finished cognitive science answers to it, and we should stop using it in serious explanation — and (b) vindication — there's one real kind there and the mature theory tells us what it is, the way 'temperature' turned out to be mean kinetic energy.
