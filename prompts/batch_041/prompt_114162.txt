Draft eight to ten interview questions that force him to defend the thesis rather than retell the history, and give a follow-up keyed to the answer he is most likely to give in each case; he tends to deflect with the line that Isotype was never meant to replace prose argument, only to clear the ground for it, so make each follow-up close that exit a little further. I want at least one question each on whether the limits he identifies belong to pictures as such or only to Neurath's own house rules — I have in mind Neurath's insistence that Isotype present facts rather than arguments — on how the charge of false confidence sits with Neurath's anti-foundationalism, given that he held even our most basic statements to be revisable and pictured knowledge as a ship rebuilt at sea, and on whether 'words divide, pictures unite' was ever a serious epistemic ideal or just good rhetoric for Red Vienna. Close on what would make him abandon the thesis.
