So not 'are you a constructivist' but 'here's a family of nonempty sets of reals with no rule for picking out an element of each one; does a selector exist, yes or no'.

Cases I want on the tree, because I think they're the ones that really split people: a selector for an arbitrary ruleless family of nonempty sets of reals; Banach-Tarski; that you need countable choice to get 'a countable union of countable sets is countable'; bases for arbitrary vector spaces; Tychonoff for arbitrary products; well-ordering the reals; and the trade with determinacy, since if you keep the axiom that every infinite game with a fixed payoff set is determined, you give up choice.
