I chair the selection committee for my field's biggest annual research prize. So now the committee is split about four ways: award it to the 60s guy posthumously, split it between the two modern teams, give it to whichever modern paper is technically cleaner, or give it to whichever team actually got the field to use the result, on the theory that if those two hadnt found it someone else would've within a couple years anyway. I need a written recommendation I can circulate before Monday's vote. Pick one and defend it, give me the strongest version of the case against it since I'll be the one answering it in the room, and say plainly what we'd be claiming the prize is for if we go your way.
