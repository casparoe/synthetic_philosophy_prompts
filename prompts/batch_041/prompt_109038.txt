Assume a reader who knows what selection, fitness, and a byproduct are, and avoid name-checking researchers. Follow the poem with a single section titled "Commentary" (roughly 150–200 words) on the formal choices you made, in which you state how confident you are that the poem represents the debate fairly and name the one line or claim you are least sure of.
