His line: the gene's-eye view is a modelling choice, not a discovery, and everything kin selection does, multilevel selection does too, same predictions, different bookkeeping, so when someone says "there's no such thing as group selection" they're smuggling a metaphysical claim into the maths. My line: a bookkeeping choice isn't nothing, and I don't think the two formalisms are as interchangeable as he says, because they seem to carve up the causal responsibility differently -- one of them ends up saying the group-level trait is doing the work, the other says it's the individuals' interactions and the group is just where the sums land.
