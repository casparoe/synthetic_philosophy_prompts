Clause 1: For every true claim about this organisation, it is possible in principle for someone to know it. Nothing about how we operate is beyond the reach of inquiry.

Clause 2: Some true things about this organisation are known by nobody, and they're going to stay that way.

Someone at the last meeting said this is just Fitch's paradox in a suit and that we'd already lost; I didn't follow, and I'd rather understand than nod along. So give me the most important implications of Clause 1, taken seriously. In particular:
