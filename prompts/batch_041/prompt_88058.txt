I am finishing a paper on the unity of pleasure, and I have noticed that my confidence in its central claim drifts with whichever examples I have most recently been thinking about. I would like an outside estimate with its scaffolding exposed. Let U be the proposition that there is a single property — hedonic tone — that all and only pleasures share, that makes them pleasures, and that accounts for their non-instrumental contribution to welfare; on U, the differences among the pleasure of a hot bath, of a proof finally going through, of relief, of a friend's misfortune, and of a dissonance sustained in music are differences in what bears hedonic tone, not in hedonic tone itself. Give me your credence that U is true, and decompose it rather than surveying the literature. I want at least the following sub-questions, each with a number and one sentence of justification: (i) whether phenomenology delivers a common tone once the sensory and cognitive content is subtracted, or whether the alleged residue is an artefact of the subtraction; (ii) whether an account that locates pleasure in the subject's attitude — being pleased that such-and-such, or taking pleasure in something — can unify the sensory and the propositional cases without circularity and without collapsing into a theory of desire; (iii) whether the axiology hedonists want requires a single underlying property, or whether a disjunctive account that unifies the pleasures only by a shared functional role — states one is disposed to prolong and to attend to for their own sake — can do the same welfare work; (iv) whether the reward literature's separation of liking from wanting, and the apparent recruitment of different substrates by different pleasures, tells against (iii) or for it; and (v) whether U has a determinate truth value at all, or is a question about our classificatory interests. Then combine the terms, identify the one your final figure is most sensitive to and say what would move it, and tell me whether the disagreement between someone who puts U at 0.8 and someone who puts it at 0.2 is likely to live in the same place as the disagreement between a hedonist and someone who rejects hedonism as an axiology.
