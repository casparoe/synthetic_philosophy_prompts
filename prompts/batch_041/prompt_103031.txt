I am preparing an interview for a formal epistemology podcast with a philosopher who defends a version of Adams conditioning as the correct resolution of the Judy Benjamin problem. Her view, in brief, is that when an agent learns a conditional of the form if A then B, the rational response is to set the conditional probability of B given A to 1 while leaving the unconditional probability of A and the other conditional probabilities unchanged; she holds that relative information minimization is not a rational update rule because it raises the probability of Blue territory even though the learned conditional concerns only Red territory. I want eight to ten interview questions that press her on the mechanics of this position rather than allowing her to restate it. Please include at least one question on each of the following: (i) why the relative entropy minimizer’s posterior, which raises the probability of Blue after Judy learns that if she is in Red territory then she is in Red I, is supposed to count as a reductio of that approach rather than a surprising but acceptable consequence; (ii) how her Adams-style rule handles an uncertain conditional, for instance when Judy learns that if she is in Red territory then she is in Red I with probability 0.8 rather than certainty, and whether the rule then still leaves the unconditional probability of Red unchanged; (iii) whether her view requires a sharp distinction between learning a conditional and learning a material conditional, and what she says to critics who regard the distinction as merely pragmatic or as an artifact of notation; (iv) how her rule relates to Jeffrey conditionalization when the learned conditional is about a partition, and whether the two rules ever disagree on cases that do not involve conditionals with probability 1; and (v) a new twist I want to introduce: suppose Judy receives a second message that is itself conditional and nested, such as if I am in Red I then I am in Red territory, but she trusts the source only 70 percent. Does her rule treat this as a constraint on a conditional probability, and if so, does it generate the same kind of counterintuitive shift in unconditional probabilities that she rejects in the infomin solution? For the first four questions, please also write a follow-up keyed to the answer she is most likely to give. For example, if she says the uncertain conditional case is handled by a simple generalization of Adams conditioning, ask whether that generalization still preserves the unconditional probability of the antecedent, and if not, whether that is a problem for her overall defense. The questions should stay in the weeds of probability kinematics and the metaphysics of conditional credences, not drift into general questions about Bayesianism or the value of formal methods.
