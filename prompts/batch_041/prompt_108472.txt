1. I don't think I'm the atoms. I'm the pattern — the particular way my memories, habits, and personality are arranged. Swap the atoms out for identical ones and I'm still here.

2. If you replaced my neurons one at a time, slow enough that I never noticed, I'd say I came out the other side. Still me, just rebuilt.

3. But if you scanned me and built a perfect copy in the next room, that copy is not me. He'd think he's me, he'd have all my memories, but I'd still be sitting in this chair. I wouldn't see through his eyes or feel what he feels. And I can't tell you why that's different from #2 except that the copy happens all at once.

4. Honestly though, when I think about dying, what I care about isn't that some particular "me" keeps experiencing things. I care that the pattern keeps going — that the thing carrying my memories and my way of being keeps existing, even if I personally don't get to be there for it.

5. And I've noticed alot of me already isn't in my head. My wife finishes my sentences, knows how I'll react, keeps a working version of me running in her head. When I'm gone that version is still there. I genuinely think a real piece of me survives in her, and in my close friends, and in the stuff I've written down.

6. I don't even think there's a sharp line where I stop and the world starts. How much of "me" is in my phone's autocorrect, or my dog's expectations of me? The edges are fuzzy, it's a matter of degree.

Here's the case that keeps me up: say instead of copying me all at once, someone in the next room grows the copy one neuron at a time over a year, hooked up to feed off my brain the whole time. At some point during that year there's a second thing in the next room that has my whole pattern in it. Did I move in there? Did it ever stop being just a copy? And if my wife's little version of me counts as partly me, why doesn't that thing?

So does this hold together? I feel like theres something off but I can't put my finger on it. Be blunt: which of these actually pull against each other, which ones just look like they do, and rank them by how bad the clash is.
