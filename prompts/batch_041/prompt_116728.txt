- Daycare rings me and says she's fine. She is fine. I know it, no argument.
- No call, I just have a feeling she's fine. She is fine. I wouldnt say I know it. I'm hoping.
- I look at the monitor and see her asleep in the corner of the frame. She is asleep. Later I learn the monitor sometimes freezes on the last frame for a few seconds when the wifi drops. I wouldnt say I knew, even though I was right and the picture was accurate.
- I can hear her breathing on the monitor, and I'd say I know she's there. But the monitor is old and sometimes picks up next doors baby through the wall, so if she werent in the room I might still hear breathing and still think it was her. And I'd still say I know.
- My hands are on the keyboard right now, I'd say I know that. I wouldnt say I know I'm not a brain in a jar. But hands-on-keyboard sort of rules out being a jar-brain, so one of those two has to go and I cant tell which.
