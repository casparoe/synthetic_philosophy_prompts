Last weekend I got into a four-hour argument with a friend who builds RL systems for a living about 'agentic AI.' His line: an agent is just a policy with a long horizon and a memory, end of story. Mine: no, 'agent' is a stance we take, not a fact about the system. A system is a tool if every goal it serves is handed to it at call time, and an agent only if it carries goals of its own across calls — and by that definition most of what ships as agentic AI is a tool in a costume, which is why 'delegation' should be doing the work that 'agency' is doing in these debates.
