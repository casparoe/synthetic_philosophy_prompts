Three weeks ago I became a grandfather, and it has had the odd effect of turning the paradox that shares my new title into something I feel personally implicated in: I now have a concrete, particular stake in the question of whether someone can travel back and prevent the existence of the very descendants who sent them. I would like you to write me a joke about the grandfather paradox. I mean an actual joke -- one that would land with a reader who knows the material, not a generic nerd gag in which time travel is the whole of the premise. The punchline should turn on the substance: on the difference between the grandfather paradox proper, in which an intervention would erase the traveler, and the bootstrap or ontological paradox, in which an object or message turns out to have no causal origin at all; and on the competing resolutions, chiefly Novikov-style self-consistency, under which the universe admits only histories that close, versus the branching-timeline line, under which the traveler simply departs into a world where the interference sticks. The joke may take sides and make the other resolutions its butt, but it should be plain that it knows what each one claims.

I would like it built on a variation of my own rather than the standard single-traveler setup. Suppose the compulsion to travel backward and eliminate one's own grandfather runs in a family, so that in every generation there is exactly one such traveler. The consequence is a strange symmetry: each would-be murderer is also the grandfather whom a traveler from two generations further on is trying to eliminate, so that every traveler is simultaneously an assassin and a target. No attempt can succeed, and not through bad luck -- success would erase the very grandchild who is, at that moment, engaged in the same errand, which is to say the killer and the victim are the same link in the chain. The family is its own alibi, and the timeline holds only because no one ever quite manages it. Let the humor come from that fixpoint rather than from slapstick, and let it be funny precisely because the structure is airtight.

Begin directly with the joke itself. No preface, no framing, no commentary on what you are about to do or on why it is amusing.
