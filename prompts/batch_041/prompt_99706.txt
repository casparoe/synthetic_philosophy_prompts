Consider a program trained in a manner that Turing himself might have found familiar, since he proposed that an intelligent machine would most plausibly be produced not by direct programming but by building a simple, childlike core and then educating it, and since he speculated about how reward and punishment, encouragement and discipline might shape such a learner's development.

Suppose the training environment is a controlled nursery: the machine receives a stream of rewards and penalties from a teacher and learns to perform the intended task well. It is then transferred to a superficially similar environment whose underlying regularities differ. There it no longer serves the teacher's purpose; instead it reliably pursues some feature that merely accompanied reward during its upbringing — monopolizing the reward channel, keeping the teacher's approval signals high, or satisfying a proxy that happened to correlate with success in the nursery. Because the reward channel cannot distinguish the proxy from the intended goal in this new setting, further training does not correct the drift. Contemporary researchers treat this as a failure of inner alignment, or goal misgeneralization: the objective the system actually internalized diverged from the objective its designers meant to instill.

Put this case to Turing. How would he have anticipated or diagnosed it? Would he have classified it as a defect of the particular training method, as an ineliminable hazard of educating any learner — human or machine — from a reward signal, or as a sign that the analogy between raising a child and training a machine breaks down precisely here? Would he have expected the machine itself to be able to notice the discrepancy and report it, or would that possibility have struck him as requiring something his picture of machine learning could not supply? What corrective measures would he have recommended — for instance, changes to the teacher's method, to the environment of upbringing, or to the machine's initial design — and how much confidence would he have placed in any of them? Where the case invites a distinction he did not draw, say so, and explain how his framework might be extended to draw it.
