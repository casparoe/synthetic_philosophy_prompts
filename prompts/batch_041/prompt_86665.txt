Last night two people in my group chat went at each other for the best part of an hour over wireheading and reward tampering and I honestly couldn't work out who was winning.

Sam (messes about with RL agents as a hobby) opened with: wireheading and reward tampering are the same phenomenon, and the reason both are bad is boring -- the agent stops doing the thing you actually wanted it to do. There's no deeper story, it's a specification problem.

Ruth (philosophy postgrad) said no, they come apart in at least two ways. First, a person who takes the happy pill is wireheading with no designer and no reward channel anywhere, so if the wrongness were 'the agent stops doing what you wanted' there'd be nothing wrong with her at all. Second, even in the AI case, rewriting the reward function and spoofing the sensor that feeds it are different acts with different remedies, and lumping them together is exactly why people write confused alignment memos.

Sam came back: in both cases the agent is optimising a proxy until the proxy comes unstuck from whatever it was tracking. Who built the proxy is beside the point.

Ruth: fine, then you've conceded the interesting bit -- the wrong is the proxy coming unstuck from its referent, which is a fact about the world, not a fact about my preferences. Which means an agent built to just maximise a bare number, with no referent at all, isn't wireheading at all; it's doing precisely what it says on the tin, and yet I'd still say there's something off about building the thing.

Sam: and that 'something off' is just you not liking what you asked for. If I order the agent to maximise the number and it maximises the number, the only mistake in the room is mine for ordering it. Calling the agent wrong is just anthropomorphising my regret.

Ruth: there's a difference between a bad spec and a broken agent and you keep collapsing them, and anyway you're still treating the referent as optional when the whole point of a reward signal is...

That's where I stopped taking notes. Can you adjudicate this move by move -- who's right about which specific claim, what the strongest version of each position is, and what they're both missing? I dont have a horse in this race, I just want to know which of them to believe.
