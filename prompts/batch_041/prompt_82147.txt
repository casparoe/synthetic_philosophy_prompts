ok so this is about wild animals and I need help figuring out if I actually have a consistent view or if I'm just making it up as I go. Here are my gut reactions, and I want to know what principle (if any) they add up to.

Walking in the woods, a fawn fell through the ice and was thrashing around, I'd get it out, no question. Bird hits my window and is stunned on the patio, I put it somewhere safe till it flies off. Turtle flipped over on the road, I flip it back. All of that feels obviously fine, I'd feel like a jerk for not doing it.

But when a fox grabs a rabbit in the yard and starts eating it while the rabbit is still alive, I don't do anything and I dont think I should. Pulling the fox off feels wrong, like the fox has to eat and thats just how it is. My cat brings in a half dead mouse and I feel bad for the mouse but I also dont think the cats doing anything wrong, its a cat, thats what cats do.

Now the ones that dont fit together. Last winter some deer were starving in the woods behind my house and my neighbor wanted to put out hay and I said we definately shouldnt, that you shouldnt mess with nature and theyd get dependent. But if there was one deer starving in my actual yard, skin and bones, I think I'd feed it. Same deer, same woods, two different answers, and I cant tell if thats a real distinction or me being a hypocrite.

Other stuff: I'll swat a mosquito without a second thought and if theres a mouse in the kitchen my dad sets traps and I honestly dont care, even though a mouse is a mammal just like a deer. Then I watch a nature documentary about an octopus and I'm wrecked for a week. I've heard alot of animals die horrible deaths in the wild constantly, way more than on farms, and when I sit with that number I feel awful, but I still dont think anyone should go out and fix it. And if someone made a vaccine that stopped a disease killing off half the chimps somewhere, I'd say give it to them, do it. But a gene thing to wipe out the mosquito that carries malaria, that feels different somehow, even though mosquitos are the ones I kill anyway without caring.

My rough guess at my own rule is: helping an animal that's suffering from something that isnt another animal is fine, interfering with animals eating each other isnt, and anything big and deliberate isnt either. But that doesnt handle the starving deer, and it doesnt explain why fixing a disease is fine but messing with mosquitos isnt, and big and deliberate sounds like its about my feelings and not about the animals at all.

So is there a principle that gets all of these, or close? What does it say about cases I havent thought of, like if we could make predators in an area just not feel hungry so they stop hunting, or stop animals from having so many babies so fewer of them starve, or just leave a place totally alone and let a disease run through it? And which of my verdicts do I have to give up, because I'm pretty sure I cant keep all of them.
