My half-formed thought is that the debate over whether aesthetic testimony can transmit justification is aimed at the wrong target: what the pessimist is tracking may not be a fact about belief at all but a fact about the speech act of aesthetic judgment, which presupposes first-hand acquaintance the way an avowal presupposes a first-personal route to the state being avowed.
