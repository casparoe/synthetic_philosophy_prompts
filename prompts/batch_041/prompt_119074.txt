There are 99 of us. Someone ran the numbers and said if each of us is right 60% of the time, the chance the majority lands on the truth is around 98%. Is it a technicality you can wave away when it's violated, or is it the whole load-bearing wall? And independent in what sense, exactly -- independent of each other, independent of the truth, what? At 99, I figure my vote can't possibly matter, so I'll go with the summary like everyone else. So our competence seems like it's partly a *consequence* of the panel being large, and the theorem seems to want to hold competence fixed while you add voters. That feels like it should break something, and I can't tell whether it does.
