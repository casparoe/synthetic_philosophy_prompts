ok so in my minds & machines seminar we did the Dennett ladder — Darwinian creatures (born with a fixed repertoire, they only "learn" by dying or getting lucky out in the world), Skinnerian ones (they can learn from reinforcement, but they still have to actually do the thing and get burned or rewarded before anything changes), Popperian ones (they can run the trial inside their own head and let the hypothesis die in their place instead of them), and Gregorian ones (they offload part of that onto tools and external scaffolding in the environment). The essay question is basically whether you can tell these rungs apart from behavior alone, and I want a case to argue with.

So build me a thought experiment with one animal in it where the behavior, on first look, is Popperian — it seems to be ruling options out before trying them, avoiding the mistakes a naive learner would make, taking the safe route first time — but where I can argue it's actually still only Skinnerian. The catch is that the argument can't be me just asserting it: it has to turn on one isolable feature, like a specific change to the setup that the animal's training history couldn't have prepared it for, and you need to tell me exactly what that change is and why it's the one that matters. Constraints: no surgery, no electrodes, no language, nothing where we get to look inside its head, and everything has to be observable in a session or two by someone with a stopwatch and a notebook. Real-ish animal or a simple robot, not a sci-fi thing, and the scenario itself shouldn't run longer than a paragraph.

Then walk it through: what each of the four rungs says is going on, which rung's prediction actually survives the isolating change, what that one feature is doing, and what happens if I take the feature out — does the case just collapse back into an ordinary reinforcement-learning demo? Also sketch the mirror-image case if you can (a genuinely Popperian creature that looks Skinnerian). And be honest about the weakest point: if someone who thinks the animal really is Popperian wanted to defened their reading, what would they say, and is there anything in my setup that would make their reply cheap?
