You know the setup: most people take the dead cert £1m over 89% of £1m plus 10% of £5m plus 1% of nothing, and then turn round and take 10% of £5m over 11% of £1m — even though the two pairs differ only in what happens on a branch with probability 0.11. I put it to him as a code diff: in both pairs the only edit is on that 0.11 branch, you swap "certain £1m" for "£5m with probability 10/11, otherwise nothing". Same edit, opposite verdicts. He reckons that's precisely the point and not a bug at all: the other branches are part of the situation, an agent is entitled to care about the shape of the whole thing (say, about whether there's any branch on which they end up with nothing after having been all but certain), so the independence axiom is the thing that's wrong, not the chooser.
