Weirdly, neither of them really cares about their own hair — it's the other person's hair they care about. Priya thinks loud hair at a wedding looks embarrassing, so her favorite outcome is that neither of them dyes; but if one of them has to, she'd rather it be her, on the theory that at least she'd be doing it as an obvious joke rather than Ravi doing it sincerely. Ravi's favorite outcome is that Priya — who's normally so buttoned-up — shows up with loud hair; that amuses him more than dyeing his own would, and he'd rather dye his own than have nobody do it. My gut says: it's each person's own hair, so each should just decide for themselves and I should butt out. But when I actually trace where that lands, it seems to spit out an outcome that Priya and Ravi would BOTH rather trade away for a different one. That can't be right, can it? So what's really going on here, and what should I actually do as the person making the rules? Answer in American English.
