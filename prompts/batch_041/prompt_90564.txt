Hume's case against miracles, stripped to its core, is that the antecedent evidence against a reported violation of a law of nature is so overwhelming that any human testimony for it would have to be more improbable than the event it reports before it could establish that event. I want your credence that this core argument, stated that precisely, is sound — and I want the derivation rather than a verdict. Give each sub-question a rough probability, say how they combine into your overall credence, and identify which one your final number is most sensitive to and what argument or evidence would move it most.
