I’ve been trying to understand collective intentionality. If a group’s intention is supposed to be something over and above the intentions of its members, how can it be shared? Sharing seems to require that each person has the same intention, which would simply be individual intentions again. If sharing does not require sameness, what distinguishes a genuine joint action—two people wordlessly clearing a table—from two people coincidentally doing complementary things at the same time? Is collective intentionality therefore either reducible to individual attitudes or else a mysterious group mind?
