I am preparing a short position statement on free will for a graduate seminar and would like a consistency audit before I circulate it. Here is what I hold.

(1) I am a compatibilist: the truth of determinism would not by itself undermine the kind of free will I take to be worth wanting.

(2) The free will worth wanting is the kind that makes an agent the genuine author of her actions — the kind in virtue of which her actions are truly hers, and her life is genuinely hers to author, rather than something that merely happens through her.

(3) An action is genuinely the agent's own when it issues from her settled values and character in the right way: she acts because of who she is, not against or despite it, and she would have acted differently had her values been different.

(4) No one is the ultimate source of her own values; they are the product of unchosen genes and upbringing. I do not think this diminishes authorship, because authorship is a relation between an action and the agent's present character, not a relation between the agent and her own history.

(5) The reason this free will is worth wanting is that it underwrites our practices of holding one another responsible: resentment and gratitude, blame and praise.

(6) I deny basic desert: no one deserves to suffer for what she has done in a way that only forward-looking considerations could override.

(7) Robust alternative possibilities are not required for responsibility; that an agent could not have done otherwise in any strong sense is irrelevant, provided her action issued from her character as described in (3).

Please tell me which of these are genuinely in tension, which only appear to be, and how severe each genuine tension is. Rank the genuine tensions rather than treating them as equally serious, and explain, for the pairs that only appear to conflict, what makes them compatible after all. For each genuine tension, say what would have to give and what the cheapest revision would be; then tell me which single revision would resolve the most while conceding the least.
