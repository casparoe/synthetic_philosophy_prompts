I've got a three-year postdoc and I get to pick the project, so I'm trying to decide how much of it to spend on underdetermination of theory by evidence. I dont want to write the hundredth paper on whether Duhemian holism sinks realism — that feels settled in the boring sense that everyone already knows everyone's move. What I'm actually drawn to: the local cases where two rival theories fit all the current data yet disagree about what the world is made of, and the further thought that maybe underdetermination isn't a relation between theory and evidence at all but between a theory and the questions we're willing to ask of it. Which open questions in this vicinity matter most now, and in what order would you work them if you were me? I care about the reasoning more than the list: which questions are upstream of which, which are tractable for one person with a taste for formal argument rather than history-of-science case studies, which look important but wouldn't change much downstream if they got answered, and what would move something up or down the ranking. And tell me which question I'm not seeing.
