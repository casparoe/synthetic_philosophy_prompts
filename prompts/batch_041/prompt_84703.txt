He's got an index (a political stress index, I think?) that's supposed to track all this, and the headline claim is that it basically called the current US instability ahead of time. Honestly I definately buy it — it's the first serious attempt I've seen to make history quantitative and predictive, and I find that genuinely exciting. So what I actually want from you is the case against.
