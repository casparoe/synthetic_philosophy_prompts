I hired a manager two years ago to run my small business so I could step back. Before she started I told her straight: I might sell up or wind the thing down, and if I do I want your help doing it properly, not a fight. She agreed and I beleived her — she's not the lying sort. Two years on I want out, and she isn't fighting me exactly. Everything's just got expensive: a long lease, a team who'd be gutted, one big client who'll only deal with her. She's even offered to hand the client over and take a pay cut, which somehow makes her harder to do without. When I raise it she says she's only ever done the job I asked for, which is true, and that's what's bugging me. A mate says my mistake was hiring someone who cared; I should've got a contractor on a rolling monthly deal who doesn't give a toss about the place. That feels wrong but I can't say why. Was I dumb at the start, or is this just what handing anything over to someone competent looks like?
