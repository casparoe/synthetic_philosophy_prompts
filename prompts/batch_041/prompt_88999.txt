Give me an agenda, not a survey. Two things I'd like you to weigh that I definately don't see taken seriously enough. Does that dissolve the problem, relocate it, or is it a dodge? Second, if there's genuinely no non-circular justification to be had, does anything actually follow for how we should run our own reasoning, or is this one of those puzzles that's inert the moment you leave the seminar room?
