"That's basically Dennett's intentional stance. The stance is just a predictive shortcut — you treat a system as if it had beliefs and desires because that helps you guess its next move. But it's a stance, so it's all in the eye of the beholder; nothing really has beliefs, we just find it convenient to talk that way. Dennett's an eliminativist about the mental, and that's why he says thermostats and chess programs don't actually believe or want anything — they just get treated as if they do. So when people say an AI 'wants' to maximize its reward, that's us projecting; there's no fact of the matter about what it wants."
