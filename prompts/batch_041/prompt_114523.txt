I want to settle this properly instead of vibing about it.

- whether the story has to be digital, or whether analog / continuous / messy physical stuff changes the answer
- whether the specific causal wiring matters, or only the input-output map
- whether the substrate matters at all, and if it doesn't, what's supposed to make one physical implementation the "right" one

Build it out as questions first and positions second — here are the splits, here's the space of answer combos, and then here's where the actual named views land in that space.
