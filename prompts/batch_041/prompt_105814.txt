Our charter says the board has to decide whether to approve, reject, or approve with conditions. I need the short written opinion I'll circulate before Thursday's meeting. The board is split: some say the Fermi paradox means the silence is evidence we should stay quiet, others say that's paranoid and we're overthinking a PR stunt. Please recommend a concrete course of action with a time frame, and engage the strongest argument on the other side. One student's memo calls this the loud infant problem -- a civilization that broadcasts early is like a crying infant in a forest, and the fact that we hear no other infants doesn't mean the forest is empty, it might mean the quiet ones survived. I don't know if that's clever or just scary, so deal with it. Don't cite literature; just reason from the setup. And say how confident you are in your recommendation and flag the point you're least sure about.
