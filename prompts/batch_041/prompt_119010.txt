I'm a PhD student in philosophy, and I need a joke for the dinner after my reading group finishes the late Nietzsche — the strand in which diet, climate, exercise, and daily regimen are treated as philosophical matters rather than biographical trivia, continuous with the physiology-first diagnosis of morality in which the ascetic ideal comes out as a symptom of a sick body rather than an error of reasoning. The joke has to turn on that content, not on the mere fact that Nietzsche was unwell: it should only be funny to someone who has registered that 'how should one live?' has collapsed into 'what should one eat, when should one walk, and where should one live?', or that the attack on asceticism is itself a regimen of self-denial, or that his own chronic illness sits unassimilated inside his own diagnostic frame. Pick one of those tensions and commit to it — a joke that gestures at all three will be neither funny nor about anything.
