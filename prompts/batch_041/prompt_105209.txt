My argument is basically three steps: parties need loyalty; loyalty means you can't publicly doubt the line; and once you can't doubt, you stop paying attention to the actual afflicted people you're supposed to care about, which is the thing Weil cared about most. I want you to prep me for the questions this crowd will actually ask -- not the ones a philosophy professor would ask.
