Im building a thing on drug policy and I keep hitting a pattern I cant explain. Take the places that went the Portugal route: possession of small amounts for personal use gets decriminalized — but selling, importing and producing stay criminal, often seriously so. So the person holding a gram is a health case, and the person who sold them the gram is a felon. Meanwhile those same places hand out clean needles, give out naloxone, and in a few cities run supervised consumption sites, i.e. theyre actively keeping users alive on the assumption that use is going to continue regardless. And overlaying all of it is the medical vs recreational line, where the same molecule is a legitimate medicine in one bottle and contraband pleasure in another, while alchohol and tobacco are just legal everywhere.

What I want out of this is the implicit theory. What does the user-is-a-patient, seller-is-a-criminal split presuppose about responsibility — does it only hang together if you think the user's choice is compromised (addiction as compulsion, or at least as badly diminished agency) while the seller is a rational profiteer? Because that seems to sit badly next to the safe consumption sites, which look like they treat the user as an agent making a bad but survivable choice. Which parts of the pattern actually need a theory underneath them, and which are just compatible with several? Could a paternalist, a Mill-style liberal, a communitarian and a public-health utilitarian all endorse roughly the same package for different reasons, and if so where exactly would they come apart? Does the administrative-vs-criminal distinction (a fine and a referral to a health service instead of a charge) presuppose that the conduct is still wrong, just not very wrong — and is that stable? And what would treating pleasure or relief as a legitimate good in itself, rather than only as treatment for a deficit, actually change on the ground — which of these policies would have to go?

Dont just give me the standard pro/con, I want the presuppositions, and I want you to tell me which ones the practice merely fits well with vs which ones it really cant do without.
