my thesis (and im worried its too cute — tell me if the objections show its actually broken): Malcolm and Dennett both deny that dreams are experiences, and the literature keeps treating that as the two of them agreeing. i think thats an illusion. they mean different things by experience and their arguments arent even aimed at the same target.
