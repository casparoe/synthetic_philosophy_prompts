ok so this is maybe a weird one, but I've spent the last two years translating a twelve-line Catalan poem my grandmother used to recite, and at this point I've got about thirty versions of it, including one under a constraint I made up that I've been calling a relay: every line has to carry exactly one word over from the line before it, and no word is allowed to appear twice in the whole poem. Honestly that exercise taught me more about English than anything else I've written. Now I have to hand in a short position paper for a translation workshop and I want to make sure my views aren't incoherent before I do. Here's where I've landed:

1. Form isnt packaging. If you keep the sense and drop the rhyme, meter, or constraint, you havent translated the poem, you've paraphrased it. The constraint is part of what the poem says.
2. There's no single correct translation. Translation is interpretation the whole way down — the original isn't a fixed object with a meaning parked inside it, and anyway the original is itself already a version of something (a draft, a feeling, an earlier poem).
3. Constraints are engines, not obstacles. The tighter the form, the more the target language shows you things you'd never have found writing loose — the relay basically wrote half my poem for me.
4. A translation can be better than its original. Some of my relay lines beat anything in the Catalan and I dont think that's a contradiction.
5. You still owe the poet something. You can't put words in their mouth — imagery, attitudes, ironies they couldnt have had.
6. When I read a translation I judge it as a poem in English first and check fidelity second. If it doesn't work as an English poem, faithfulness doesn't rescue it.

I suspect a few of these are pulling against each other but I genuinely can't tell which, and I'd rather find out now than in the workshop. Can you go through the pairs, tell me which ones are really in tension and which only look like they are, rank the real ones by how bad they are, and for each one say what the cheapest fix would be? Then tell me which single change would clean up the most.
