My gut verdicts:

- A cardiologist with 20 years' experience has a strong hunch that this patient's chest pain is nothing, but the base rate for a man his age with those symptoms says work him up. I think she should work him up — the hunch is just a feeling.
- A climate scientist who knows a dataset inside out thinks a new headline study is wrong, even though most headline studies in her field do replicate. I think she should trust her read, because she can actually say why the study is off.

My tentative principle is: trust the inside view only when it comes with a checkable reason that the outside view doesn't already contain.
