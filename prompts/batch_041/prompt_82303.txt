A research group has begun training a language model to produce detailed first-person reports about its own processing — why it favored one continuation over another, which associations a phrase brought up, what it was "trying" to do — and now treats those reports as evidence about the model's internal states. Please describe the most important implications of Wittgenstein's private language argument, together with the rule-following considerations that motivate it, for whether such reports could count as meaningful descriptions of anything at all, and for what would have to hold of the training regime and the surrounding practice for the reports to carry genuine evidential weight about the model's internals. State how confident you are in each claim you make, and single out the one you are least sure of.
