I am a doctoral student working on the philosophy of psychiatry, and I am trying to get clearer about a conceptual issue that I think tends to be run together in discussions of Ian Hacking's looping effects. The bare observation that a classification can alter the people classified, who in turn alter the classification, is familiar enough; what I want explained is what distinguishes a loop that tightens the fit between a diagnostic category and the condition it purports to track from one that loosens it. Hacking's contrast between interactive kinds and the indifferent kinds studied by the natural sciences is where I would like the explanation to start, but I do not want it to stop at the observation that psychiatric kinds are interactive. I want the mechanics: what has to be true of a category, of the people it classifies, and of the institutions that circulate it for the loop to be self-correcting rather than self-reinforcing, and why that distinction, rather than the mere fact of looping, ought to govern how far we reify the category.

To keep the explanation anchored, please develop it around the following hypothetical. A diagnostic category is proposed for a condition whose criteria are partly self-referential: to meet the threshold, a person must both report a certain pattern of experience and hold some belief about what the condition is, having adjusted their self-understanding in light of that belief. The instrument is widely administered, results are publicized, and patients routinely encounter the label before they encounter a clinician. Using this case, explain how the loop runs, why its members are interactive kinds rather than indifferent ones, and how one could in principle tell whether the loop here is stabilizing or destabilizing. I would especially like you to address the self-referential wrinkle: if the criterion for membership itself concerns the member's beliefs about the category, does the category have a stable referent at all, or does each turn of the loop change what the category picks out? Please work out the mechanics rather than rehearsing the secondary literature.
