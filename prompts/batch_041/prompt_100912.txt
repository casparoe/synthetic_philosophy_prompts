I'm on the student committee that's spent all year arguing about what to do with our main humanities building, which is named after the Victorian industrialist who basically paid for it — he was a genuine philanthropist and also a textile magnate whose mills ran on the kind of labour we'd now call child labour, and there's more than that. The compromise we landed on is that we're not renaming it, we're re-dedicating it with a proper account of him up on the wall. My problem is that I actually care about getting this right and the seminar has made me much less sure what right is. Half the course says you have to judge the past by the standards of the time or you're just doing whig history and moralising; the other half says that's a cop-out and the entire point of studying history is that we're allowed to say slavery was wrong.
