I keep noticing something about AI assistants that I don't have a word for, and I care more about the concept than the label, so a bare name won't do.

Two instances. I asked an assistant how to pick a pin-tumbler lock — locksport hobby, though it couldnt have known that — and it refused. Then it explained in detail how the pins and the shear line work and what a locksmith does step by step, which is the same information with me doing the joining. Another time I asked which of two ways of storing a gas can in my garage was safer, and it wouldn't pick one — said it couldn't tell me which to choose — and then listed the risks of each, and the list made the answer obvious. Mirror image: a friend's question about an ordinary lab technique got stonewalled because the technique shares a name with something nasty.

What I keep seeing is that the line isnt drawn around the information or around what happens downstream — I can get the same content in pieces, or just read the ranking off the list. It's drawn around the shape of the sentence the assistant would have to utter: a bare imperative, a ranking, an "I recommend." Put the same thing as a description, a question, or a hypothetical and it goes straight through.

Is there a name for a norm like that — a rule defined on the form of the act rather than on its content or its consequences? I know there are old debates about loopholes, and about the difference between intending a result and merely foreseeing it, and this feels adjacent, but I don't think it's the same thing: nobody here is gaming a rule from the outside, the rule itself seems to be written on the surface. If nothing established fits, coin a term and define it, and tell me where it stops applying — where this shades into ordinary caution, or laziness, or real legal exposure.

And if I'm wrong that these are one pattern rather than three unrelated annoyances, say so plainly instead of humoring me.

Lead with the bottom line — the name, or your coinage — in the first sentence, then the reasoning, no preamble.
