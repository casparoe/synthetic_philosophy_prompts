But rereading him I think the label is doing something stranger than that. So my suspicion is that in his system the classification is actually a promotion, not a demotion — but that if you read it that way you owe an account of how the programme itself is rationally appraised, and that's precisely the thing he never spells out. He also seems to have half-walked it back later, I think in the autobiography, saying natural selection is testable after all, which I find a little sus, like he just got tired of being yelled at by biologists.
