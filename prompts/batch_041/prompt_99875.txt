Suppose a reliable predictor tells you that it will create and punish a simulation of you unless you now adopt a policy of refusing to comply with blackmail from future superintelligences, even though it has no causal influence on your present and its prediction rests solely on simulating your decision procedure. You consider adopting a surrogate goal -- a goal you do not intrinsically value but that would make your refusal credible. What is your credence that a rational agent in this situation ought to adopt the surrogate goal? Decompose the question into at least: (a) the probability that acausal influence of this kind is coherent; (b) the probability that the threat is credible conditional on (a); (c) the probability that a surrogate goal can be genuinely adopted and remain stable under reflection; and (d) the probability that rationality requires precommitment rather than causal expected-value maximization. Give a rough number for each, say how they combine into your final credence, and identify which sub-question the final number is most sensitive to and what argument or evidence would move it most.
