Can you take the main positions in that debate and restate each one faithfully in my vocabulary? I mean the reciprocity/desert case for making benefits conditional on work or job search; the political-economy story that "deservingness" is a construct manufactured to discipline labor; the basic-income / unconditional-rights objection; and the paternalist line that conditionality is basically a commitment device for people who can't self-bind. Render each in terms of pooling, screening, ex-ante vs ex-post moral hazard, verification costs, deductibles, adverse selection, that kind of thing.
