Write a joke about Schopenhauer's theory of music. Not a generic grumpy-pessimist joke — the punchline should turn on the actual content of the view: that music is a copy of the Will itself, while the other arts only copy the Ideas, so music skips the Platonic middleman that painting and poetry have to go through, which is why he puts it at the top of the arts. A good hook: since music copies the Will rather than any of the Ideas, it isn't really "about" any particular thing, so a critic who insists a symphony is a picture of the sea is doing something category-wrong. Maybe stage it as that critic getting corrected by a smug Schopenhauerian. I dont want it to be a survey of his aesthetics dressed up as a joke — it should be short and actually funny to someone who's read the theory.
