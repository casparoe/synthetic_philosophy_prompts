Compose a short joke for a graduate seminar in formal epistemology. The joke should center on the Judy Benjamin problem: a paratrooper lands in a field divided into Blue and Red territories, each with a Headquarters and a Second Company area, and then receives a message that if she is in Blue, she is in Headquarters. The humor should turn on the actual difficulty of updating on a conditional rather than a proposition—for example, the tension between conditionalizing on the material conditional and minimizing relative entropy. Do not simply name-drop the problem; the joke should be intelligible to someone who knows the setup but has not memorized the literature. The entire joke must be at most 120 words.
