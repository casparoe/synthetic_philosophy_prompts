Consider the four-grade taxonomy of minds that runs Darwinian, Skinnerian, Popperian, Gregorian. A Darwinian creature's behavior is fixed by its innate endowment. A Skinnerian creature learns by operant conditioning, so its trials and errors occur in the world. A Popperian creature can test hypotheses internally, so its trials and errors occur in a simulated inner environment rather than in the world. A Gregorian creature augments its cognition with culturally transmitted thinking tools — language, notation, procedures, mnemonic and computational artifacts — and can use those tools to reshape its own habits of thought. The standard presentation is cumulative: each grade contains the ones below it, and the sequence is a ladder of increasing cognitive capacity. I want your credence in a claim that denies the cumulative structure at one joint.

The claim is this: it is possible for a system to be a Gregorian creature without being a Popperian creature. That is, a system may reliably acquire, use, and transmit culturally transmitted thinking tools, and thereby reshape its own cognitive dispositions, while lacking the capacity to generate and test internal models of its environment in the way the Popperian grade is supposed to require. On this view, externalized cognitive scaffolding can substitute for inner simulation rather than presuppose it, so the ladder has at least one rung that can be occupied without the rung beneath it.

Give me your credence that the claim is true, and decompose it. I want a number attached to each sub-question, not only the considerations. At minimum, address the following.

First, what exactly the Popperian grade requires. Is the essential feature inner simulation of the environment, or is it merely the capacity to represent possibilities and let those representations guide action? Give a probability that the stricter reading is the correct one.

Second, whether the stricter reading, if correct, rules out the possibility in question. Conditional on the stricter reading, what is the probability that no system could satisfy the Gregorian description without satisfying the Popperian one?

Third, whether the Gregorian grade requires anything more than the use of external artifacts — for instance, meta-cognitive access to one's own cognitive dispositions, or the ability to revise the tools themselves rather than merely apply them. If it does require more, does that requirement covertly smuggle in the Popperian capacity? Give a probability that the thicker requirement is genuine.

Fourth, whether the grades are best read as a conceptual taxonomy, an empirical sequence, or a claim about evolutionary history. Give a probability for each reading, and say how the truth of the claim varies across them.

Fifth, whether a current large language model based agent is an instance. I am not asking whether it is conscious, sentient, or understands in any thick sense. I am asking whether it occupies the Gregorian grade while failing the Popperian one, on your preferred reading of each. Give a probability and one line of justification.

Then combine. State your final credence in the claim, show how the sub-probabilities yield it or explain why they do not compose cleanly, and identify which sub-question the final number is most sensitive to. My own credence is about 0.35, and the term doing the most work in getting me there is my suspicion that the Popperian grade is being defined by its most flattering description rather than by a criterion that could be applied independently of the conclusion. If you think I have mis-weighted things, say where, and name the single piece of evidence or argument that would move your number most in either direction.
