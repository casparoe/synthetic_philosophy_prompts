That's why the ancient Greeks thought akrasia was impossible -- they figured you can't knowingly do the wrong thing -- but modern psychology showed they were just wrong about how the brain works. The fix is to make the future feel more immediate, and the fact that your preferences flip as a deadline gets closer is just the two selves fighting it out."
