I work on pricing models at a car insurance company, and there's something about my job I cant stop thinking about. We charge a 22 year old guy more than a 22 year old woman with an identical driving record, identical car, identical everything, because his group costs us more on average. That's legal in most states. We can't price on race, and in Europe they banned pricing on sex a while back, but age is still fine there and nobody seems bothered. We also use credit score and zip code, and everyone knows zip code is partly a stand-in for race.

So here's my question. What does this whole practice actually presuppose, philosophically? I dont mean the legal reasoning, I mean whats underneath it. Some of the factors we use are things you chose (how you drive, what car you bought) and some you definitely didn't choose (your age, your sex, where your parents happened to live). So does the practice assume people are responsible for traits they never picked, or does it assume responsibility is irrelevant and only expected cost matters? If it's the second one, then why is race off limits — that starts to look like an exception to the rule rather than a principle. But if it's the first one, then sex and age look like the exceptions, since nobody chooses those either.

The other thing that bugs me: there's supposedly no such thing as your true risk. Your risk only exists relative to some group you drop me into. 22 year old men is one group. 22 year old men with no accidents is another. 22 year old men with no accidents who drive after midnight is a third, and I can keep slicing forever and get a different number each time. So what picks the group? Is there a principled answer, or does the practice just quietly assume that the categories we happen to already have are the real ones?

Tell me which commitments the practice actually needs versus which ones it just happens to fit, and where it's genuinely underdetermined — like, would several different theories of fairness all let us do exactly what we do now? And if the actuarial view falls apart, what replaces it? Charging everyone in a state the same? Something in between? Thanks.
