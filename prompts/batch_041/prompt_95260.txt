My friend says the analogy is load-bearing, that the encoding and the self-reference and the level-crossing are the actual mechanism of selfhood and the book is a proof sketch. I say its a vibe — pretty, but nothing transfers. We've been going in circles for a year and I think we owe the group a written document instead of another arguement.

The claims we can both put our names to about what Gödel did and didn't show — about self-reference and what it actually requires, about what the theorem is and isn't a claim about, about how rare or cheap incompleteness is, about what happens when you add the missing sentence and whether that ever bottoms out, and about whether the second incompleteness theorem is even needed for the book's move or whether the first one carries it.
