The shape I have in mind, which you should feel free to improve on: an early phase in which participants take turns as the decider across a run of rescue and resource-allocation dilemmas while the others must predict each decider's verdict, with a flat "minimize the deaths" rule as the baseline hypothesis; the rounds should be stacked so that the baseline conspicuously fails to predict the room, while a rule that forbids harm-as-means but permits harm-as-foreseen-side-effect predicts it well — I want them to discover that they are already, and quite reliably, double-effect reasoners.
