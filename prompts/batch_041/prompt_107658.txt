(iv) The St. Petersburg game, that is, a fair coin flipped until the first tails, paying $2^n where n is the number of heads that preceded the tails, offered at an entry fee of $25: she declines, and when I asked what she would be willing to pay she said "nothing close to that," adding that the enormous prizes are "just decoration" because she is "not going to be the one who wins." I cannot fit this pattern to a single rule. Three further items are coming up, and I would like to predict her before I ask her. (A) A bet with a 60% chance of winning $100 and a 40% chance of losing $1,000.
