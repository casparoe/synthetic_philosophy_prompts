Please act as an expert on Kierkegaard and on the logic of moral justification, and think carefully before answering. Begin by brainstorming the candidate rules that could jointly fit the colleague's earlier verdicts, then predict her verdict on the new case, say which rule you think she is implicitly running and why, identify at least one rival rule that fits the earlier verdicts just as well, and name the further case that would best distinguish them.
