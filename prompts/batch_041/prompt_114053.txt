Two years of a postdoc, one question, and I think mine is verisimilitude: Popper's idea that our best theories are all false but later ones can still be closer to the truth, the fact that his own definition of "closer" got taken apart by counterexamples soon after he proposed it, and the rival repairs that came later (content-based ones, distance-based ones, attempts to keep Popper's original intuition alive). I keep hearing its a backwater. So which open questions here are actually worth two years, ranked, and why? Flag the dead ends that still get written about anyway.
