All three score the same on the state comprehension test as the kids reading regular print. The district says their scores can't count, because the test is only validated for print and the kids are 'accessing the text differently.' My sister says if they can do the thing the test is testing, they can read, and the district is just describing the plumbing. I'm a software dev so my gut says if it does the same job it's the same thing, but that gut has burned me before.

Another kid answers everything right but says he doesn't see anything in his head while he reads, and never gets the jokes.

So who's actually right, and whats the real disagreement here? Does anything change if one of the kids uses an AI app that reads the book to him and basically answers the questions for him?
