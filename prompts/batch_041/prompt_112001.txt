I've ended up on a weird work committee thats piloting an AI that rewrites residents' comments before they reach the city council. It's sold as de-escalation: it strips out loaded language, translates insults into concerns, and buckets every submission into one of five pre-approved themes. Councillors only see the cleaned-up summaries. I keep reaching for Orwell, but I dont want the obvious Newspeak take. What would he actually predict this does to political thought over time—does it just make discourse less inflammatory, or does it quietly change what people can argue about and what counts as a legitimate complaint? And would he treat this as different from ordinary propaganda, since nobody's forced to use the tool and it's mostly opt-in? I'm trying to decide whether to keep pushing back on the pilot.
