On this view, a system that lacks a theorem stating that no suitably bounded optimizer can elicit catastrophic behavior is worthless as evidence of safety, and the empirical program — red-teaming, benchmark evaluations, robustness and distributional-shift work — is at best a stopgap that buys time until the real guarantees arrive.
