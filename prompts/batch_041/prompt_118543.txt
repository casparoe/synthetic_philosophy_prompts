Tell me which of these actually conflict and which only appear to, and rank the genuine conflicts by severity rather than treating them as equally bad. For each real conflict, say what would have to give and what the cheapest repair would cost. Then tell me what would change your mind: which consideration, were it to go the other way, would reverse your verdict about which commitment should be dropped.
