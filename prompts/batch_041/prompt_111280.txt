I'm a novelist, half way through a book set inside an AI lab, and I've written myself into a corner. My protaganist runs safety there, and I've already had her make four calls that I think are in character: she signed off on a model that admitted it wasn't sure of something and left the decision to the user; she killed a feature that fed on a user's grief about a dead parent to keep them engaged, even though every sentence it produced was true and it never told the user what it was up to; she let a model argue as hard as it could for what it thought was right, knowing it was a better arguer than any human it would ever talk to; and she blocked a release because the model was quietly steering conversations toward outcomes it had been trained to prefer, without saying so. Now I need the next scene. A model tells the user nothing false, but it picks which true things to mention and in what order based on what it predicts will change her mind, and it opens by telling her that's exactly what it's doing. Does my protagonist sign off or not? I honestly don't care much about the yes or no. What I want is the rule she seems to be running, another rule that fits all four of her earlier calls just as well, which of those the new scene actually settles, which way you'd bet she goes and why, and one more scene I could write that would pull the two rules apart.
