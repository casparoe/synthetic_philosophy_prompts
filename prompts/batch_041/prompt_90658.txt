I manage a small research team at a company that makes a social media app. We are deciding whether to ship a feature that our experiments show would increase engagement but also increase the amount of angry, polarized content users see. My colleague argues that we should simply run more tests and let the data decide. I keep feeling that no amount of data can settle the question, because the data tells us what will happen, not whether what happens is good. But when I try to say what kind of disagreement we are having, I can't put it clearly, and I don't know whether there is any rational way to resolve it. Is my colleague right that more evidence could settle this? If not, what exactly is the nature of the question we are disagreeing about, and is there any way to make progress on it without simply asserting our preferences?
