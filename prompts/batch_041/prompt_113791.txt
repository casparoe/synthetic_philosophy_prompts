I want to get clear on what a theory of standing to blame actually has to deliver before I read any more of them, so write me the spec instead of a survey of who says what. Give me the requirements list, with the case or intuition behind each one.

Things I want in it: the cases any adequate account has to get right (the person who lectures you about lying while lying to you; the ex-smoker who tells you to quit; someone blaming you for something they do too but never in front of you; a bystander with no stake in it at all; an institution blaming a member for something the institution does itself; someone blaming themselves for the same thing; someone blaming on behalf of a third party; a co-culprit). The distinctions it can't be allowed to run together (lacking standing vs being blameworthy vs the blame being unwarranted vs the blame just being ineffective or unwelcome; losing standing vs never having had it vs having it and being unable to exercise it; standing to resent vs standing to demand vs standing to sanction; standing to blame vs standing to be heard). And the results from outside the debate it has to be consistent with, like the fact that blaming is a social practice with real costs, that plenty of moral criticism is addressed to people who can't answer back, and that a lot of our blaming is never spoken aloud.

For each requirement tell me whether it's a genuine constraint or just a commitment of one camp, and be explicit about the camps, which I'd roughly divide into: standing tracks the quality of the blamer's will; standing is a matter of being in a position to make a demand on someone; standing is about the point or business of blaming in the first place. Then say which single requirement does the most work in ruling out candidate theories. And I suspect a couple of these can't be had together, specifically whatever makes the hypocrite's blame defective while also not making everyones blame defective, since nobody has a clean record -- if that's right, say so, because the whole literature looks to me like an attempt to wriggle out of exactly that.
