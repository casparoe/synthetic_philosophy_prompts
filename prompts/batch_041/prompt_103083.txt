Design a runnable game or role-play around Hilbert's hotel where most of the room are staff and guests and one or two people are finitist auditors who will only sign off on a move if someone can actually complete it step by step, with a last step. The Cantorian manager can give a rule like move every guest in room n to room 2n, and then later a guest checks out, but the auditor keeps asking when the list is finished and whether the hotel is now less full. No lecture up front. Give me the rules, materials, rough timeline, roles, and a debrief that maps what happened onto the actual/potential distinction, bijections, proper subsets, and the finitist refusal to treat an infinite collection as a completed object. Also tell me what will predictably go wrong the first time and how to handle it, and be honest about which part of the finitist position the game can't reproduce. Finally, say what would make you revise the design: what would count as the game making the finitist look like a strawman, or the Cantorian side look like it's just cheating by definition.
