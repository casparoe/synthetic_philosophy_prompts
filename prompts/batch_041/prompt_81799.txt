Write me a joke about Typogenetics -- the toy chemistry Hofstadter made up in GEB, in case you dont have it loaded: a strand is a string over A, C, G, T, every consecutive pair of bases codes for an amino acid (a command like cut, insert, delete, turn left, switch to copy mode), the amino acids string together into an enzyme, and the enzyme is the thing that actually acts on strands -- so a strand is at once the data and the program. Two wrinkles I want the punchline to sit on: (1) an enzyme that copies a strand copies its complement, never the strand itself, so reproducing yourself means building your opposite first and then copying that; (2) nothing happens until something reads the strand off into an enzyme, so a strand's self-replication gets done by a machine it never touches. Setup plus punchline, short. It should land for someone who actually knows how the system works -- the punchline should turn on the encoding/self-reference thing (the whole DNA-Gödel parallel, where you only get to refer to yourself through something that doesn't look like you), not on lol Hofstadter or lol biology.
