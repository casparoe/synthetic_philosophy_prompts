Phrase every node as a question someone could answer without already knowing where it leads, and give each leaf the name of the position plus one line on its most awkward commitment. Flag any leaves the tree can't seperate — I suspect E-admissibility and maximality collapse together on some of my cases — and note the combinations nobody occupies, with a one-line diagnosis of whether that's because they're incoherent, unattractive, or just unexplored. I want the logical space, not a roll call of who said what, so don't organise it by names.
