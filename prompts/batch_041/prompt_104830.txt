Okay so this is going to sound a bit mad but bear with me. I've never studied philosophy properly, I just read a lot of wikipedia and argue with my mate Dave down the pub, and I've ended up with an argument that I'm fairly sure proves something nobody actually believes. I need someone to find the hole in it.

Background, quickly: Dave reckons that for a thing to carry on existing through time it has to have "time parts" — like a sausage you slice up along its length, except the length is time. So the poker in my fireplace has a 5pm-slice and a 6pm-slice, and at no single moment is the whole sausage "there". He says that's the only way to make sense of it going from cold to hot without the thing being cold and hot at once. I half-bought it, then I pushed on it and now I've got this:

(1) At 5pm the poker is cold. At 6pm it's hot. That's a change — everyone agrees.
(2) A single thing can't be cold and hot at the same time, so the cold and the hot must belong to two seperate things: the 5pm slice and the 6pm slice.
(3) Those two slices are as separate from each other as the two halves of a sausage you've actually cut in two. Nothing is cold-and-hot; one bit is cold, a different bit is hot.
(4) So "the poker changed" really just means: there's a cold thing at 5pm and a different hot thing at 6pm.
(5) But two different things being different from each other isn't change. Change is one thing becoming otherwise.
(6) So the poker didn't change.
(7) And nothing changes, by the same reasoning — take any slice on its own and it's only ever one way, so no slice changes either; and if no slice changes and the whole is just the slices, then nothing anywhere changes.
(8) But the poker plainly does change, I can feel it go from cold to hot in my hand.
(9) So something's gone wrong, because I've just proved that change is impossible, which obviously can't be right.

Where's the break? I'd rather not give up (8), obviously, and honestly I'd quite like to keep (2) because it's the bit that made the whole thing click for me in the first place. If it's (5) that's rotten, tell me why it looked so good, and then give me the price list: what does Dave's sausage thing cost, what does the version cost where the poker is simply cold-at-5 and hot-at-6 with no slicing at all, and is there any other way out I've not thought of.
