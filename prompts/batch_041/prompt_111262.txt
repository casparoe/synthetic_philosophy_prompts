In a finite poset you start anywhere and keep stepping to a strictly bigger element until you can't; that's ordinary induction. The infinite case is the same story, just longer: you keep stepping up, and when you've built a chain of steps you jump above the whole chain, because that's what "every chain has an upper bound" buys you, and then you keep stepping.
