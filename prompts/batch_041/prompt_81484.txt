My friend Jo's first solo show opens Thursday and I'm giving the little intro speech, and I've got myself in a hole. All the paintings are from her dreams, and the more I think about it the less I know whether dreams are even experiences -- whether someone's actually in there seeing it while it happens, or whether the whole thing gets put together at the moment you wake up and its filed as a memory of something you saw. Two of the paintings hang opposite each other and push in different directions: one has the car alarm that really was going off outside her window at four in the morning, and the other has my dog in it, who she only met the next afternoon. If dreams aren't experiences then these aren't paintings of anything and half of what people will say to her at the wine table is false. So write me four minutes that puts the question in the room and leaves it there -- art people, her parents, and three of her old labmates from the sleep unit, so nothing that sounds like a lecture -- and don't let me say anything she'd have to correct, she'd hate it if I settled it for her. I want to end on a line that's true either way.
