I spent the better part of yesterday evening arguing with a close friend, a machine-learning engineer, about functionalism and multiple realizability, and we ended by talking past each other in a way I recognize from every lecture I have ever given on the subject. His position, roughly, was that the matter is settled and obvious: minds are multiply realizable because software runs on any sufficiently capable hardware, and functionalism merely records that fact. My position was that the software-and-hardware image is doing illicit work in that argument, since it presupposes the conclusion rather than supporting it, and that even granting multiple realizability, the step from it to functionalism is an inference to the best explanation which a type-identity theorist can simply decline, for instance by treating a mental property as a disjunction of physical realizers. We got nowhere, and what unsettled me is that our disagreement is precisely the one the standard teaching materials fail to anticipate.

Next week I have to run a single ninety-minute session on functionalism for a mixed group of graduate students, some from a cognitive science programme and some from engineering, none of whom have read anything on the subject. I do not want you to explain functionalism to me. I want a plan for teaching it.

Give me the order in which the ideas must be introduced and why each depends on the one before it; the single carrying example I should build the whole session around, together with what that example must contain and what it must leave out in order to do the work; the misconceptions this particular audience will arrive with or form, and I expect at least the software-and-hardware conflation, the assumption that multiple realizability by itself establishes functionalism, and the collapse of functionalism into behaviourism, so tell me the exact point in the sequence at which each should be headed off; the one question I can put to them at the end that would show whether the distinction between a functional role and its realizer has actually landed rather than merely been repeated; and what I should openly refuse to settle at this level, and say so out loud.
