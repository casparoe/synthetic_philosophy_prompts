I want to use the boundary problem in democratic theory — the fact that you cant decide who the demos is without already presupposing a demos — to push for a concrete procedural compromise. It should be persuasive to both halves, and it should actually propose a mechanism, not just say democracy is circular. Maybe something like a joint vote of city and neighborhood residents with a sunset clause that lets either side reopen the question after five years.
