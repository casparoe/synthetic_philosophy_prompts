I've read the futarchy stuff, I get the basic picture — elected reps define a welfare metric, markets price which policies raise it — but the slogan itself seems to be doing a lot of work that the mechanism doesn't obviously do.

Which of those are trivial, which are substantive and actually defensible, which are just false, and which one does a person probably mean when they use it to shut down the "isn't this just rule by the rich" objection?
