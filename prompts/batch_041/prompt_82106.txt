And if you say you did choose it, you're saying you made yourself, which is impossible, because whatever choice you made would have to come out of the kind of person you already were, so the regress never terminates. So nobody is ever responsible for anything. Its basically the consequence argument wearing different clothes -- the past plus the laws of nature fix what you do, so you couldn't have done otherwise -- and the upshot, which is why he gets classed with the hard determinists, is that blame and retributive punishment are irrational leftovers of a pre-scientific worldview, and we should move to a purely forward-looking system aimed at deterrence and rehabilitation.'
