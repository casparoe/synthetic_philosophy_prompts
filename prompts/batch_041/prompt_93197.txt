Council clerk you have to beg for permits who keeps turning you down on a whim: not free. Ordinary citizen going about his business under the normal criminal law, nobody bothering him: free. Hermit on an island with nobody having any power over him at all: free. Now the one I can't place. My nephew makes his whole living off a single app -- it's his landlord, his agent and his bank rolled into one; the company can switch him off tomorrow for no reason and he'd have nothing. It never has, they've treated him well for years, he's never once been told no. Free or not? Give me your best guess at her verdict, tell me the rule (or rules -- I can see at least two, maybe three, that fit everything above) she's likely running, what each of them predicts about my nephew, which one you'd bet on and why, and what case I should spring on her next that would force them apart.
