Most recent sighting was a group chat where a friend wants to kill the rule that new members of our thing have to be vouched for by an existing member, on grounds that it's arbitrary and exclusionary, and someone else just posted Chesterton's fence and that was that, thread over. And then someone in the thread pointed out that Chesterton's own version is a gate across a road, a modern reformer saying he doesn't see the use of it, and the smarter reformer answering that he won't let it be cleared away until the guy can come back and say what it's for, which sounds like it's ordering you to go investigate, not to preserve anything. So now I don't even know which direction the thing points.
