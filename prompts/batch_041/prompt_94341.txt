I'll be using the list to decide which proposals remain live options for a decision theory that must also set betting odds, not merely for credence. For each requirement, say whether you regard it as non-negotiable or as a preference of one camp, and say which single requirement does the most to exclude candidate theories. Give exactly five numbered requirements, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion.
