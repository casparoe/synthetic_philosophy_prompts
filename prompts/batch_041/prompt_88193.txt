Around 700 words, deadpan bureaucratic prose, no winking. At the end, add a brief author's note outside the parody identifying where the piece takes liberties with the actual Gettier literature, which reading of the fourth-condition debate it leans on, and what would change your mind about whether the satire is fair—for instance, if the literature has largely abandoned the patch-and-move-on project, or if the target is a strawman.
