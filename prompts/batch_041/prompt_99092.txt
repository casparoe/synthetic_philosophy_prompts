Explain what that example must include and exclude, and why the usual carriers, such as religious belief or a plain observation report, would serve worse. Which misconceptions will the group predictably form, for instance that James simply identifies truth with usefulness, that Peirce is merely a consensus theorist, or that pragmatism is a form of relativism; at exactly what point in the sequence should each be headed off; and what single question should I ask at the end to tell whether they have grasped the disagreement rather than merely memorised two slogans?
