ok so I've read enough rationalist blog stuff about money pumps that I could recite the standard spiel, but I think I've spotted a gap and I want to check whether I'm fooling myself.

Here's my model. A money pump is the diachronic cousin of a Dutch book. If my preferences over options form a cycle — A over B, B over C, C over A — then a trader who knows this can walk me around the cycle, charging me a small fee at each step, and I end up holding the thing I started with and less money. Being pumpable = being irrational, roughly the way being bookable = having incoherent credences. Fine, no notes.

Now my case. I have normal present bias, my discounting isn't exponential, and I do reverse myself: today I'd rather have $20 now than $25 in a week, and a week from now I'll feel exactly the same way about the then-now. But notice I'm not cycling. At any single moment my preferences are totally consistent — I'm not preferring A to B to C to A in one breath. I'm just a somewhat different person next week. So the pump as I understand it doesn't even touch me, and I should stop feeling guilty about takeout. The whole apparatus of Ulysses contracts, Beeminder, precommitment, tying myself to the mast — that's for people with an actual cycle, or for people too dumb to see the trader coming, and I'm neither.

So two things. (1) Am I right that seeing it coming is enough? My reasoning is that if I'm sophisticated I'll just decline the trade when the trader offers it, since at that moment the trade makes my life worse. If that's wrong I want to know why — is there a version where every individual step is one I'd happily take on its own terms, so that being smart buys me nothing? (2) If the pump does apply to me after all, what does it actually prove? That my discount rate is the wrong one, or just that I'm exploitable by a specific kind of counterparty under specific assumptions — they know my preferences, they can keep making me offers, I can't commit, nobody else will trade with me? And if its the second, does the argument tell me anything about what discount profile I ought to have, or does it only rule out certain structures and stay silent on the level, so that an exponential 5% and an exponential 50% are equally fine and the pump doesn't pick? Concretely: should I bother setting up any commitment device at all, or is that just ritual for people who haven't thought it through?
