Self-deception is puzzling because it appears to require an agent to believe a proposition and to believe its negation, or at least to intend to bring about in herself a belief that she already regards as false or unsupported. One influential family of responses preserves this intentional structure and posits a division within the mind, one part of the agent deceiving another. A second family denies the intention altogether and reconstrues self-deception as a species of motivated, biased belief-formation that is not itself aimed at producing a belief.

Consider a third picture, which is diachronic rather than synchronic. On this picture, the self-deceived agent never holds contradictory beliefs at a single time and never intends to acquire a false belief. Instead, over time she manages her own evidence: she avoids disconfirming sources, cultivates confirming ones, and frames her questions so that the disconfirming evidence never comes into view. The belief she ends up holding is, at the moment she holds it, well supported by the evidence she actually has. The deception, on this view, is located in the history of her evidence rather than in any present doxastic conflict.

Please explain this diachronic, evidence-management picture and assess it against the two standard families of response. Specifically: (i) how does it address the requirement that the self-deceiver in some sense knows, or at least suspects, the truth she is avoiding; (ii) does it preserve the intuition that the self-deceived person is blameworthy in a way that an ordinary holder of a false belief is not, and if so, on what basis; (iii) why does self-deception tend to be selective — why is the agent not equally self-deceived about everything — and does the diachronic picture explain this better or worse than the synchronic ones; and (iv) what is the strongest reason to think the picture describes not self-deception at all but merely motivated evidence-gathering that stops short of deception. Conclude by saying which of the three pictures you find most defensible, and what the decisive consideration is.
