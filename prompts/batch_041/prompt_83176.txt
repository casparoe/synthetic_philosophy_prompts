So when my commitee asks "so why did the mill close," is the honest answer just... nothing caused it? That feels obviously wrong but I can't find the move that gets me out of it. And then the other half of it: if the collapse happens in every counterfactual anyway, isn't counterfactual reasoning about it just pointless by definition? I feel like I'm going in circles.
