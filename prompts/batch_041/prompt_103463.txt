Ok so I've been chewing on this thing I read about somewhere and I can't for the life of me remeber where — some philosophy book, I think, and I'm probably mangling the details, but here's the setup as I understood it. There's this giant arena called a careenium, and the floor of it is covered in these little mechanical balls called simms. Each simm is a dumb little gadget: it just rolls around and bounces off its neighbours, and if two of them bump gently enough they stick together magnetically. On its own a simm doesn't mean anything or represent anything, it's just a ball. But when millions of them clump up they form bigger blobs — simmballs — that have there own behaviour: they roll around the arena as units, they split and merge, they seem to respond to each other at the level of whole blobs, and the claim is that at that level the simmballs are carrying information and doing something like computation, so the whole arena is a big machine and the simmballs are the moving parts with a job. The bit I keep going back and forth on is this: which level is actually doing the pushing? When a simmball rolls into another one and knocks it off course, did the simmball push it, or was it just a trillion little simms bumping into each other with the "simmball" being a story we tell over the top? I want to say both are true — the simmball really does shove the other simmball around, and that isn't just a bookkeeping convenience — but I can't shake the feeling that once you've said where every simm is and where it's going, there's nothing left over for the simmball to do, and I don't know how to answer that without just repeating myself.

So I want to practice defending the "both are real" line against someone genuinely sharp about it. Here's my opening: fine, the simms are where the physical pushing happens. But the simmball isn't any particular clump of simms — it sheds some at the edge and picks up others and keeps right on rolling, so it can't just be a name for a fixed batch of them; and meanwhile the whole point of the arena is that what the simmballs do to each other is what actually gets the computation done.
