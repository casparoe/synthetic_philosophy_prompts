So heads on flip 1 pays you $2, flip 2 costs you $2, flip 3 pays about $2.67, flip 4 costs $4, flip 5 pays $6.40, flip 6 costs about $10.67, flip 7 pays about $18.29, and it keeps growing like that. Marcus: "The expected value is 1 - 1/2 + 1/3 - 1/4 + ... -- the alternating harmonic series, which adds up to ln 2, about 69 cents. It's positive, so it's a good game; I'd pay anything up to 69 cents to play."
