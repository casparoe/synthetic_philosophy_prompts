I've been in a long-running arguement with two friends about whether large language models understand anything, and I keep suspecting I'm the one being inconsistent, so before I write any of this up I want an audit of my actual position. Here's roughly where I've landed. (1) I buy the Darwinian inversion more or less wholesale: natural selection produces fit between organism and world without anyone foreseeing the fit, and I don't think that's just a metaphor — it's a real case of purpose-ish behaviour with no purposive agent behind it. (2) I think the Turing side is the same shape of move, and I'd extend it to modern statistical systems: a translation engine or a chatbot can reliably produce the gist, hit the right register, even land a joke, and I don't think any of that requires anything in there to grasp what the words mean — that Hofstadter line about getting the gist without knowing the meaning has always seemed obviously right to me. (3) So I'm on record that current LLMs and MT systems don't understand, full stop. (4) But I'm not a behaviourist and I get annoyed when people say my view collapses into one — I think there's a genuine, non-behavioural difference between a system that merely turns in competent performance and one that understands, and the difference lives in what's going on inside: the internal states have to be about things, and be usable in the right ways — inference, planning, revising on evidence. (5) And I'm a functionalist about that difference: whatever it amounts to, it can't be the carbon, so build the right organisation out of silicon and it understands, no question.

What I want from you is whether this hangs together, and specifically which of these I can't keep. I have a nagging feeling something is wrong between the Darwin/Turing stuff and my flat claim that LLMs don't understand, but I can't articulate it, and I also worry (4) and (5) are pulling apart — if it's all organisation, how does aboutness get to be a hard requirement? Where I've flagged something as a tension and it turns out to be fake, say so and say why, because I'd rather have two or three real ones ranked by severity than a list where everything gets flagged as equally inconsistent. For each real tension: how bad is it, which commitment has to go or soften, and what's the cheapest repair that leaves the rest of the package standing. Don't tell me what to read — just do the audit.
