I'm an information theorist by training — did my PhD on rate-distortion and network coding, now I work on compression at a storage company — and a friend in a neuro grad program has been bending my ear about Integrated Information Theory for months. I keep having the same reaction, which is: this looks like something I already have machinery for, and I want to know if that reaction is right or just provincial. So here's the ask, and I mean it as a translation job, not a debunking. Take IIT at its most serious and restate it in the vocabulary I actually use: channels and capacities, entropy and mutual information, the data processing inequality, Markov blankets and conditional independence, bottleneck and rate-distortion tradeoffs, coarse-graining and partitions of a joint distribution, synergy and redundancy in the partial-information-decomposition sense (I know the PID lattice reasonably well and it's probably my best guess at a translation target), causal graphs and interventions if you need them. I want the axioms and postulates stated as claims about a stochastic system and its partitions; I want to watch the measure they call Φ get built in my terms — the minimum-information-partition business, the counterfactual repertoires about what the system "would do" as opposed to what it does, the step that picks out one complex instead of a nested pile of them; and I want the translation attempted faithfully, even where faithful means clunky.

Then the residue report, which is the part I actually care about. What survives translation cleanly, what only survives if I quietly let a word change meaning halfway through, and what has no counterpart in my vocabulary at all? My working hypothesis is that alot of it collapses into synergy plus a choice of partition, and that the residue is the claim that the measure is supposed to be intrinsic — something the system has for itself rather than something an observer computes about it — which I dont think my vocabulary can even state, let alone evaluate. But I may be wrong about which piece is load-bearing, and I'd rather be corrected than flattered. So: if some part only looks empty because my framework has no slot for it, say so and say what kind of slot it would need; if some part only looks substantive because their vocabulary is doing the work, say that too. Skip the empirical question of whether the theory matches any neural data — I'm asking about conceptual content. And please don't retreat into a neutral "this is an ongoing debate" summary. Attempt the actual mapping, warts and all, and where the mapping fails, locate the failure precisely rather than gesturing at ineffability.
