But if there's a decent chance we're living in a simulation, then a lot of what we think we're steering toward isnt the real future at all -- it's some artifact a simulator is running, and the actual stakes for the real world out there are not obviously what the fanatic's arithmetic assumes. My friend thinks that's either wrong or irrelevant and I couldnt fully pin down why.
