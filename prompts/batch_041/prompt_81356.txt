I am assembling a conceptual toolkit rather than adjudicating a school: I want to use one notion of grounding as a primitive to regiment claims about what is fundamental in other debates, and before I commit to any theory of grounding and fundamentality I need to know what such a theory has to deliver. Write the desiderata. Give me the cases it must get right, the distinctions it cannot collapse (grounding versus causation, grounding versus supervenience, monadic versus relative fundamentality), the structural constraints it must respect (irreflexivity, asymmetry, transitivity, well-foundedness or its denial), and the explanatory work it must do, each with the case or consideration behind it and each marked as non-negotiable, negotiable, or a preference of one camp. The part I most want is the conflict report: flag any subset of the requirements you think cannot be jointly satisfied and say which case forces the choice.

Four cases I keep returning to, which the list should speak to directly. First, a singleton set's existence seems to be grounded in its sole member's, yet that member could plausibly have existed in a world containing no sets at all. Second, a city's injustice seems grounded in facts about its policing, housing, and wage laws, yet none of those facts, alone or combined, appears to deliver injustice without a normative assumption smuggled in. Third, a joke's funniness seems grounded in its setup and punchline, yet the very same facts stop grounding funniness when the audience or the decade changes. Fourth, if the fact that one thing grounds another is itself a fact, either something grounds it or nothing does; say what a theory owes here.

Format: a markdown table with columns Requirement | Motivating case or consideration | What it rules out | Status. Then a short JSON object with fields unsatisfiable_pairs (a list of objects, each with requirements as an array of names and a because field), most_exclusionary_requirement (a name plus a one-sentence justification), and camp_pressure (an object mapping each of priority monism, grounding primitivism, and grounding-as-explanation to the single requirement that pressures it most).
