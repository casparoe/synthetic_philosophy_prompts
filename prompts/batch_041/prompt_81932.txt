I retired from geology a couple years back and have been teaching myself philosophy since. Dennetts Where Am I? has been bugging me -- brain in a vat, radio link to the body, two candidates for where the guy is -- but I don't want the standard recap, I want a number on a twist I made up. Forget the whole-brain transplant: say the surgeons swap my neurons out one at a time for silicon chips that do exactly the same job, I stay awake and chatting the whole way through, and at the end there's a chip-brain in my skull and the original biological tissue sitting intact in a jar next to me. Give me your credence that the person finishing the conversation is still me, and then your credence that the stuff in the jar is me too -- or say if you think that second one is malformed. Decompose it: how much of the number rides on identity being continuity of consciousness vs. the causal history of the parts vs. the pattern rather than the material, and how much on the whole question just not having a determinate answer. Which of those is my number most sensitive to, and what would move you most?
