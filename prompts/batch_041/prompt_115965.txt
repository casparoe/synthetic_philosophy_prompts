Case two: the same city's anti-noise ordinance barred "amplified sound" between 10pm and 7am, and a bar owner argued his acoustic set (guitar and a singer, no amps) shouldn't count. She held that didn't satisfy the statute — but her reasoning was that the whole point of the provision was to keep out-of-state actors off the platform, and IP geolocation is so unreliable that accepting it would defeat that purpose, so she read the verification requirement to demand something more accurate.
