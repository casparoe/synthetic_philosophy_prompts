Someone will say, as though it settles the matter, that supertasks are impossible: you cannot do infinitely many things, and no amount of cleverness makes an infinite sequence finite. I could not tell whether I had been given an argument, a statement of physical law, or a pun, and I could not tell what work the impossibility claim was doing: was it supposed to show that the lamp puzzle has a hidden contradiction, or that it has no answer, or that it is not worth asking?
