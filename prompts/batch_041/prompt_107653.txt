Short version: a woman in her fifties was sanctioned for falling below the required number of weekly job-search contacts. Her app log shows almost no activity over a six-week stretch. What the log doesn't show is that she was the only person caring for her mother, who was dying of cancer, and that her prepaid phone lapsed for part of that stretch, so she couldn't log anything even when she was looking. The agency's line is: the rule is contacts, the app is how we verify contacts, no contacts no benefit -- and if we start accepting "I was busy" the whole conditionality regime falls apart. Two panel members say uphold, because intent and hardship aren't what the statute asks about; two say that's backwards, that the point of a job-search requirement was always to test effort, and that we've quietly swapped effort for *documented* effort in our particular app, so someone can try her hardest and still flunk. Please draft the memo: a clear recommendation (uphold, reverse, or reverse with conditions), a concrete disposition with a time frame -- a specific remedy or a specific policy fix, not just a vibe -- and the reasoning, and don't soft-pedal the agency's worry, because I think it's a real one and I want the memo to say honestly why we're overriding it, if we are.
