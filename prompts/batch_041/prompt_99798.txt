I am assembling a proposal for a six-month, part-time research project — roughly the scope of one dissertation chapter — and need help converting a half-formed idea into a disciplined plan. The idea is this. Safety research on capable AI is commonly split into alignment work, which tries to make a model actually want what we want, and control work, which tries to keep us safe even if it does not. The split is usually explained by what is being secured: the model's motivations versus the surrounding system. I suspect that is the wrong axis. What really separates the two programs, I think, is which propositions about the model each is willing to assume without argument. So I want to treat every safety measure as carrying an assumption ledger — a list of claims about the model's goals, its awareness that it is being overseen, its strategic competence, and its ability to act on any of this — such that the measure delivers the safety it advertises only if the relevant entries hold.

Frame this as a proposal. State the question precisely, so that it has a determinate answer: can the alignment/control distinction be reconstructed as a division of labor across a single ledger, where each family of measures is defined by the entries it takes as given and apparent disagreements between the programs turn out to be trades across entries rather than substantive conflicts? Or does the distinction resist that reduction, and if so, what does the resistance show? Explain why the existing way of drawing the line leaves this open. Lay out the method concretely — how I would extract ledgers from a handful of representative measures (say, oversight by a trusted weaker model, honeypot tasks, anomaly detection on internal states, and training-time preference shaping), how I would test whether the assignment is forced or arbitrary, and which contested cases would do the most work. Give me the possible conclusions and which considerations would decide among them. State what a real result looks like and what would count as the project failing — in particular, what would show the ledger is so permissive that it explains nothing. Finally, give an honest scope for six months part-time and say what I should cut if it proves too big. Positions and arguments are enough; I will add citations myself.
