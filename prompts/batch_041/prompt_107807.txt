I am preparing a paper on the badness of pain, and before I go further into the literature I want the specification fixed: set out the desiderata that any adequate theory of what pain's badness consists in must satisfy. Write the list with the case or consideration behind each requirement, and mark which requirements are non-negotiable and which are commitments of a particular camp. Adequacy here is diagnostic: I want a list that tells me in advance where a proposed account will break.

Two stipulations, which you should treat as given and need not source. First, a patient in whom the affective and motivational machinery is fully engaged — guarding, withdrawal, autonomic arousal, an insistent demand that it stop — while she reports no felt quality at all: no ache, no throb, nothing it is like to be in the state. Second, a subject who reports a sensation that is unambiguously unpleasant and unambiguously to be avoided, and yet denies that the state is bad for her in any respect that matters; say whether your theory must treat her as confused, or whether there is a coherent reading of the report.

The list should cover the cases the theory has to get right — pain asymbolia; pain unnoticed until attention lands on it; pain sought or welcomed; congenital insensitivity to pain, where the absence of pain is itself the harm; pain in creatures whose reports we cannot solicit — the distinctions it may not collapse, among them sensation, painfulness, and suffering, and intrinsic versus extrinsic badness; the constraints imposed by what is known about nociception and its cortical processing; and the fact that pain, of all things, is the paradigm of something bad in itself, which the theory must explain rather than merely accommodate. Then the two things I most want: any pair of requirements that cannot be jointly satisfied, since if there is such a pair, that is the result, and the single requirement that does the most to rule out otherwise attractive theories. Close with a brief pass over the main families of account and how each fares, but keep the weight on the criteria.
