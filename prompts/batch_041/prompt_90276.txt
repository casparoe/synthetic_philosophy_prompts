ok so my freind Maya is really into anthropic reasoning — self-locating probability, sleeping beauty, doomsday, the presumptuous philosopher, all of it — and I've been keeping a log of her verdicts on little toy cases I invent, trying to reverse-engineer the actual rule she's running, because she flatly refuses to just tell me. I want you to take a crack at it.

The setup she always gets: before anything happens a fair coin is flipped, and the outcome fixes how many people exist and where they are. She then wakes up as one of those people, with no memories and no idea which one she is, and she knows the whole protocol. Then I ask her: what's the probability the coin came up heads?

Case 1 (two rooms): heads → 2 people, both in Room A. tails → 2 people, one in A and one in B. She wakes up, sees she's in Room A, says 2/3.

Case 2 (three rooms): heads → 3 people, all in A. tails → 3 people, one in A and two in B. She's in A. She says 3/4.

Case 3 (lopsided split): heads → 4 people, 2 in A and 2 in B. tails → 4 people, 3 in A and 1 in B. She's in A. She says 0.4.

Case 4 (no info): heads → 5 people. tails → 5 people. She wakes up with no clue about rooms at all. She says 1/2.

That's the whole log. Now the new case, the population coin: heads → exactly 2 people exist. tails → exactly 2,000,000 people exist. She wakes up as one of them and this time has literally no other information — no rooms, no rank, nothing. What does she say?

What I want from you: (a) the two or three genuinely distinct rules that fit everything in the log, stated precisely enough to compute with; (b) what each of those rules predicts for the population coin; (c) your best guess at what she'll actually say, and why you lean that way — if the log really can't settle it, say so but still commit; (d) one further case I could run on her that would cleanly separate the surviving rules.

Give it back as a JSON object with these fields: "rules" (array of objects, each with "name", "statement", and "prediction_for_population_coin"), "my_prediction" (object with "probability" and "which_rule_this_favors"), "best_guess_reasoning" (string), and "settling_case" (object with "setup" and "prediction_under_each_rule"). JSON only, and keep the reasoning tight.
