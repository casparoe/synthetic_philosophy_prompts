ok so i'm putting together a reading-group session on vagueness and i want to start from first principles instead of the usual "here are the four positions, pick one." before anyone defends anything i want the spec: what does any theory of vagueness actually have to deliver? give me the desiderata list -- the cases it has to get right, the distinctions it can't collapse, the logical and semantic results it can't just wave away -- with the case or consideration behind each requirement. i care way more about the list than the scorecard, so keep the scoring at the end short.

the reason i don't just want the standard list is that i've been chewing on two cases i don't think the usual framing handles cleanly, and i want the desiderata built against them, not just against the five examples everyone recycles:

1. imagine a neuroscientist tells us there really is a fact of the matter about when someone becomes bald -- some exact follicle count -- but quantum indeterminacy in the follicles makes it in-principle impossible for anyone, ever, to find out where the line falls. does that rescue the view that vagueness is ignorance, or does it change what that view even is? what would a theory have to say here to not be cheating?

2. take a predicate where every single small step is obviously permissible but the whole trajectory obviously isn't, and where running the steps in a different order lands you somewhere different -- e.g. a committee that tweaks a rule one word at a time until it's a completely different rule, and the "final" version depends on the order the amendments were voted. is that the same phenomenon as a sorites or a seperate one that any theory has to handle on its own terms?

so: for each desideratum, say why it's a genuine requirement and not just a prejudice of one camp, and flag any pair you think can't both be met. if some subset is jointly unsatisfiable, that's the part i most want -- the list itself being a result is fine. then, like two sentences each, how epistemicism, supervaluationism, and one degree theory do against the list. keep it in the weeds, i don't need the definitions of the views.
