Longtime lurker in the rationalist end of things — SSC, LessWrong, the old blogosphere — and I keep running into the same two-word move in fights about psychiatric categories, and I finally want to pin down what it actually claims.

The phrase is 'making up people', from Hacking (I read the LRB essay a while back). In it he says our sciences sometimes create kinds of people that in a certain sense did not exist before, and he calls that making up people. But he also gives a seperate name, the looping effect, to the way the classification and the classified feed back into each other so the kind ends up a moving target. So he's got two labels for what might be two different things, and alot of people just collapse them into one. He also had a word for the contrast with the kinds physics studies, some kind of 'interactive' something, and a list of what he called the engines that drive the loop — counting, medicalising, biologising, geneticising.

Where I met it doing work: a thread about why the ADHD numbers keep climbing. Someone dropped 'making up people' and then argued that since the category creates the people, asking whether ADHD is really rising is confused, and asking whether it's real at all is a category error — the whole question dissolves. Nobody could push back because nobody could say what the slogan was claiming. (A second guy used the same phrase for the opposite conclusion: if the categories are made up, his self-diagnosis is automatically valid and nobody gets to question it. If your menu covers both uses, great.)

What I want: the distinct precise claims 'making up people' could be making, from the boring-to-the-point-of-trivial up to the radical, with a verdict on each — plausible or not, which one Hacking actually meant, and whether it actually gets the first guy his conclusion. I especially want the reading that does the heavy lifting seperated from the ones that only get you something much weaker, like 'prevalence numbers from 1995 and 2025 aren't comparable'. Also tell me which reading people usually mean when they use it to end a thread. And if it matters which engine is running the loop, say so.

Lay it out as a list I can paste back into the thread. Not a philosopher, so keep it in plain English, and don't send me off to read anything — just the readings and the verdicts.
