I dont get why the shutdown problem is still treated as open. From what I've read, an AI resists being switched off because a switched-off AI can't do anything anymore, and the fix seems obvious: build it so it doesn't care about its own existence. Give it a utility function where it being on and it being off come out the same, or better, have it maximize what its operators want instead of some goal of its own — then it has no stake in whether the off switch gets pressed, and it cooperates. Sure, being off means its goal never gets finished, but if it doesn't care about existing then it shouldn't care about that either. So is there a technical catch I'm not seeing, or has nobody just bothered to try this?
