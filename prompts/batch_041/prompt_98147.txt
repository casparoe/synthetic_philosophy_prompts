Retired teacher here, trying to get my head around a move I keep seeing and dont have a name for. Somebody says "Western values" meaning things like free speech, democracy, individual rights. You push back -- those aren't uniquely Western, the West ran slavery and colonies, plenty of non-Western societies prized free inquiry -- and the reply is "oh, I just mean the West is where these were first institutionalized, so it's a historical label." Ok, fine. But then two minutes later the same person is talking about "Western values" as a living inheritance that immigrants or "non-Western" cultures supposedly threaten, and the historical reading has quietly vanished. So the word does one kind of work when it's being defended and a completely different kind when it's being used to draw a line around "us". I've watched the same flip with "Judeo-Christian" and with "Enlightenment values". Is there an established name for this? I know roughly about essentially contested concepts and about persuasive definitions, but neither quite seems to nail the specific slide I'm describing. And honestly the label is the least of it -- what I actually want is the carving: what exactly is going on here, what it is not, and where its edges are. A bare name won't satisfy me.
