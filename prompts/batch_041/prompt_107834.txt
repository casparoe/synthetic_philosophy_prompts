Bit of an odd one, hope this is the right place to ask. I keep noticing a pattern and I don't know if it has a name, and lately it's started to bother me that I can't even say clearly what the pattern is.

It's about things that have a tendency, where you can never check whether the tendency is still there, because the checking changes things.

Instance one. My mate swears her dog is great with kids. Bring kids round and the dog hides under the bed. She says the kids just make it jumpy, the dog is still great with kids underneath. Fine, maybe. But what would it even look like for her to be wrong?

Instance two. A friend describes someone as 'really funny once you get to know him.' But you only get to know him by hanging round him, and what's stopping the funny is that he doesn't know you yet, so the hanging round is itself the thing that has to stop before the funny can start. The only way to see it is to stop looking. That one feels structurally different from the dog but I can't say why.

Instance three. Bloke at work is generous whenever anyone's watching and not otherwise. I want to say the watching is producing the generosity here, whereas in the dog case the kids are only getting in the way of it. But when I try to say what that difference is I end up saying 'in one the trait is still there and in the other it isn't', which is just restating the thing I can't define.

Instance four, the one that really breaks my head. Some tendencies can only ever be checked by an act that uses them up. A firework works exactly once. And there are things, a reputation, a person's nerve, a friendship, where testing whether it's still there is the thing that ends it. So 'it was there all along' and 'the test destroyed it' are hard to seperate.

I'm fairly sure philosophers have a little vocabulary for this. I don't know it and I'd rather not half-invent it. So: does the pattern have a name, and is it one name or several? Which of my four is which, and have I got any of them wrong? And the bit I actually care about, when a tendency doesn't show, is there a real difference between 'still there, something was blocking it' and 'not there anymore', or is that just a story we tell, given that all any of us ever sees is the outcome? Please take a side on that rather than laying out the options and leaving it to me. And if one of my cases has no established name, coin one and tell me where its edges are.
