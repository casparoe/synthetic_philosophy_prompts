My buddy and I got into it at the bar last night about whether the market can be beat, and we just kept going in circles, so I want a referee. Here's roughly how it went.

His line: prices already bake in everything anyone knows, so if there was easy money lying around somebody would of grabbed it already, and stock-picking is basically a tax on people who think they're special.

My line: isn't there that no-trade theorem? If everyone's rational, there are no noise traders, and it's common knowledge how everyone gets their information, then even someone sitting on genuinely private info can't profit from it, because the very act of making an offer tips everyone off about what you know. Which makes a perfectly efficient market self-defeating: if prices already reflected all the information, nobody would pay to dig any up, so it'd never get into prices in the first place. (The Grossman–Stiglitz thing.) So perfect efficiency is impossible, there has to be some slack, and that slack is exactly what pays the people doing the digging.

He said: fine, some slack, but it's precisely the size that pays the pros' salaries, and it shrinks the more people chase it — so you'd be the last guy to the buffet, not the first.

I said: but those no-trade results lean on everyone sharing the same prior and it being common knowledge that we do, which is obviously false. Real traders have different models, different priors. Once priors differ, the no-trade result just evaporates and trade happens.

He said: if it's only different priors, then when we trade we're not discovering anything — we're just betting on different opinions, and ex ante one of us eats a loss for no reason but luck. That's gambling, not information being "reflected" in prices, and it doesn't mean either of us can beat anything.

We both felt like the other was cheating somewhere and we couldn't pin down where. Can you go move by move — all five — and rate each one from clearly wrong to clearly right, say what each of us is actually getting right, and then say what we're both missing? I don't want a wishy-washy "you both have a point." I want verdicts, and I want to know which of us is closer to right overall.
