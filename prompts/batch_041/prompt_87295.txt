1. what makes pain bad is just how it feels. the hurt itself is the bad thing. i dont think the badness is some extra layer you add on top by judging it or disliking it — its baked right into the feeling. if you're feeling the pain, you're already in the bad thing.

2. i read about this condition where people get some kind of brain damage (i think around the insula? not sure) and they still feel pain. they can tell you yeah thats pain, its in my left hand, and they'll rate it like a 7 out of 10. but it doesnt bother them at all. they dont pull their hand away, they dont wince, some of them laugh or joke about it. and they still say its pain — theyre not denying that it hurts, theyre just completely unbothered by it. its super rare obviously.

3. so i think what's actually bad about pain is the "ugh this sucks" part, and that part can come apart from the raw sensation. the raw sensation by itself is neutral, kind of like a really bright light — you notice it but its not good or bad.

4. i also think you should beleive people about their own experiences. if someone tells you their pain doesnt bother them, thats just true, its their pain.

5. i kind of like the idea that nothing can be bad for you unless you mind it. like badness for you has to go through your own attitude about it. if you honestly dont care, then its not bad for you.

6. but obviously pain is bad even when you dont mind it. if you're in pain while you're asleep you're not minding it at all, and its still bad for you, right?
