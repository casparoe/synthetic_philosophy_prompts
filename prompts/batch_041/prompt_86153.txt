I need a villanelle spoken by one of the two generals in the coordinated attack problem. Their rationality is common knowledge; what isn't common knowledge is the attack time, since the last messenger might have been captured. Make the two refrains 'I know you know' and 'you know I know', and let the form do the epistemic work: each repetition should add one more floor to the tower of mutual knowledge without ever reaching common knowledge. The final quatrain should bring the refrains together so it sounds like closure, but the poem should make clear why no finite number of acknowledgements gets them to a safe attack. Don't just use 'common knowledge' as a label -- make the reader feel the missing level. It should be accurate enough for an epistemic game theory reading group and still work as a poem for someone who's never heard of the two generals.
