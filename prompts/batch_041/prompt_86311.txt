But then someone mentioned that a logician took this exact line and showed it collapses into the claim that every truth is already known — which is obviously false, we don't know most things. Now I can't tell whether the line is trivial, deep, or secretly incoherent, or whether the people using it even mean the same thing by it. And it seems to get used alot as a conversation-ender.

Then the part I care about most: when someone in my group uses the line to argue that reality is mind-dependent, or that truth depends on knowability, which reading were they actually relying on, and does it hold up under that reading? Also flag where I'd be right to object as a scientist, and where my objection is really just me not understanding what's being claimed.
