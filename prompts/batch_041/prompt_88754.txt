Two, I don't want the carrying example to be the Britain/China pair itself; it should be small enough that they can hold the whole thing in their heads, and ideally from somewhere none of them work on. So: the order the ideas have to come in, the case that carries the session, the misconception I should expect (my guess is they'll try to fix the question by piling on causes until China's list matches Britain's) and exactly where in the sequence I head that off, how I'll know at the end whether they've actually got the distinction rather than just nodding along, and what I should openly say I'm leaving out. Give it to me as exactly five numbered items, a sentence or two each, no intro, no wrap-up.
