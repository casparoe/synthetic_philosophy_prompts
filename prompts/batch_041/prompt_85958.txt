That's what makes cognitive science possible in the first place: neuroscience tells you about the hardware, psychology and cognitive science tell you which program is running on top of it. It also explains why the big fight in AI between the symbol-crunching school and the neural network school was really a fight about the brain. Either way, the interesting question isn't whether the brain is a computer, it's whether a machine can be conscious — and computationalism says that's just a matter of running the right program."
