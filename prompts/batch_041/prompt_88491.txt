I'm pretty sure I'm a tracking theorist about knowledge in the Nozick vein: S knows p just in case p is true, S believes p, and S's belief tracks the truth, which for me means it's sensitive (if p were false, S wouldn't believe it) and adheres (if p were true, S would still believe it). Layered on top of that: I know a whole bunch of mundane stuff — that I have hands, that there's a table in front of me, that I ate breakfast this morning. I also know alot of things secondhand, from textbooks and from people who just tell me things, like that the earth goes around the sun or that my friend's flight lands at six. And I think knowledge isn't closed under known entailment — I can know I have hands without knowing I'm not a handless brain in a vat, since my belief that I have hands doesn't track the vat possibility.
