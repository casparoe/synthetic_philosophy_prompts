Me and my friend Dana got into a stupid-long argument last night about whether her brother counts as honest, and now I need to know if the thing we kept circling actually has a name, because I don't think it does and it's driving me nuts.

Dana says her brother is honest: in like ten years she's never once seen him tell a lie. But here's the catch — every time he's in a spot where a lie would actually help him, he goes red and starts stammering and physically can't get the lie out. So no lies. Dana says that proves honesty. I say it proves nothing, because the temptation and the stammer show up together, so you never get a clean look at what he'd do with the temptation and no stammer. Same shape as my dad's truck that "always starts," except it only starts because a cutoff fires when the engine's about to flood, so the starting only ever happens after the thing that would've stopped it has already kicked in. Or my sister's dog, which never bites, but only because it's scared of exactly the situations where it'd want to bite, so the fear shows up only when the bite would.

I know there's some philosophy about tendencies and what a thing would do in various situations, and I think part of it is about tendencies that get blocked or altered by outside conditions. But the cases I mean feel different, because the blocking isn't sitting there beforehand — it only shows up with the trigger — and it doesn't seem to change whether the tendency is even there. Is there a name for this? If not, coin one, but honestly I care way more about the carving than the label: what makes this its own thing, how it differs from the closest ideas, and where the edges are. What if the stammer only works 90% of the time? What if sometimes the temptation shows up without it?
