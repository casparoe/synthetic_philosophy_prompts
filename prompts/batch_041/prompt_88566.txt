I keep a list of medical and quasi-medical interventions and whether I count each as treating a condition or enhancing a person, and the list has stopped obeying any rule I can state. Fluoridating a town's water: treating. Iodizing its salt: treating. Vaccinating a child against measles: treating, even though nothing is being cured. Eyeglasses for myopia: treating. Laser surgery for the same myopia: enhancing, which I recognize is odd, since the two leave me seeing identically. A cochlear implant for a congenitally deaf infant: treating. Growth hormone for a child who is healthy but short: enhancing, though both of those push a child toward a statistical norm. A wakefulness drug for someone with narcolepsy: treating; the same drug for a healthy adult facing a deadline: enhancing. Statins for a man with an inherited cholesterol disorder: treating; statins for a healthy man of his age as a precaution: I cannot decide, and I notice this has the same shape as the fluoride case, where I decided instantly. A beta blocker for a violinist whose performance anxiety is crippling but not a diagnosable disorder: enhancing, even though she suffers. An antidepressant for major depression: treating; the same drug for ordinary grief: I want to call it treating and cannot say why. Editing an embryo to remove a lethal disease allele: treating; editing it for height or memory: enhancing.

The pair I cannot reconcile is the glasses and the laser surgery: identical results, opposite verdicts. Is there a principle that captures these verdicts, or most of them? My own guess is that treatment is what you would want for your child even if no other child received it, and enhancement is what you would want only if others were not receiving it — but that seems to make the laser surgery treatment as well. Test that guess against the cases, repair it if it can be repaired, tell me which of my verdicts I would have to surrender, and say what the repaired principle implies for a case I have not listed: a pill that lets a healthy person sleep four hours a night with no ill effect.
