I'm what people call a lexical threshold negative utilitarian, and by that I just mean this: there's a level of intensity — call it the threshold — such that any suffering at or above it outweighs any amount of suffering below it and any amount of pleasure, however large, and below the threshold I'm a completely boring sum-and-trade guy, three mild headaches really are worse than one mild headache. I think moral status comes in degrees with the richness of a being's experience, so a mouse's suffering counts for less than a human's, but any being that can suffer at all counts for something, which is why I care a lot about wild animals.
