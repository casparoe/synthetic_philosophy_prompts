Write me a joke about the epistemicism vs. supervaluationism debate over vagueness. It should be funny to someone who actually knows the two views, so the punchline has to turn on the actual content rather than on "philosophers are pedantic". The angle I'd go for: each view has to swallow something weird. The epistemicist says there is a single precise cutoff for "heap" (or "bald", or "rich") and that it already exists, but that nobody could ever in principle know where it is. The supervaluationist says there's no fact of the matter where it is, yet still holds that "there is some sharp cutoff" is true, and that "either it's a heap or it isn't" is true even for the borderline cases -- so they end up with a sharp line that isn't anywhere in particular. Put it in a setting where a borderline case actually matters to somebody (a claims adjuster, a bouncer, a couple working out whether they've broken up, whatever) and make the punchline come out of that difference. If you can fit in a third character with a different take (degrees of truth, or the nihilist who says "heap" has no extension at all), bonus, but dont let them eat the joke. And please not a generic three-philosophers-walk-into-a-bar bit, I've read those.
