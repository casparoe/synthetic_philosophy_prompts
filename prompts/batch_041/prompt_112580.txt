I've become pretty convinced by the Wittgenstein hinge picture — that a lot of what we know rests on propositions we never doubt and never argue for, which work more like the rules of a game than like beleifs we hold on evidence. I want you to stress-test it for me. Heres the part that nags at me: hinges look installable. Schools, cults, and whole societies can hand you a hinge you didn't have, and once it's in it doesnt feel like a belief at all — it feels like just seeing what's there. So what would count against the hinge picture? Give me the defeaters ranked, from fatal to merely awkward, flag which ones hinge theorists would themselves concede rather than just the cheap shots from critics, and be honest about whether the view predicts anything or whether "it's a hinge" is just what you say whenever someone won't budge. And if hinges can be installed by other people, has the view quietly turned into sociology instead of epistemology?
