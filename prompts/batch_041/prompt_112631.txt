Take the role of a philosopher of science with a specialist's command of Lakatos's methodology of scientific research programmes, and think carefully before committing to anything. Begin by sketching two or three candidate domains, then choose the one that best fits the brief.

I want you to invent a research programme — not a real historical episode, and nothing from physics or cosmology, which are the examples everyone reaches for — that is theoretically progressive at every step in Lakatos's sense, so that each successive modification predicts some novel fact its predecessor did not, and yet never becomes empirically progressive, because none of those novel predictions is ever corroborated, while a rival programme in the same domain makes only modest, unadventurous moves and steadily accumulates corroborations. The setting should be ordinary and realistic: a hospital's theory about a ward infection, an engineering team's theory of an intermittent fault, a research group's hypothesis about a dietary link. The single feature separating the two programmes must be whether new moves generate independently testable predictions or merely redescribe data already in hand.

Present the episode in continuous prose, with no headings, no bullet points, no bold text, and no tables, in no more than three hundred words. Then, also in prose, do the following. Say what Lakatos's own apparatus must conclude about each programme, and whether the case embarrasses his treatment of the ad hoc and his claim that being at least theoretically progressive suffices for a programme to count as scientific. Give the verdicts of a Popperian, a Kuhnian, and someone who holds that whether a prediction is novel or merely accommodates data already in hand makes no evidential difference. Tell me which single feature of the case is doing the work, what happens to the verdicts if that feature is removed, and how a defender of Lakatos would try to defuse the case. Do not offer citations or a reading list; argue from the structure of the case itself.
