I want them to run into it with their hands instead, before anyone says the name of the problem.

Logistics: 16 people, one 90-minute block after lunch, one big conference room plus two small breakout rooms, a whiteboard, a table of about ten small physical objects I'll bring from home (mug, tennis ball, rubber duck, padlock, etc.), index cards, masking tape. What I want to happen: two teams each invent a made-up vocabulary for the objects — nonsense tokens only, no English, no numbers, no pointing or gesturing. Two things I want built in. I want the group to find, by trying it, the point where that chain of definitions stops buying you anything.
