My opening move: your demon has to be somewhere. Fine, suppose it's a physical thing inside the universe, made of the same stuff as everything else. To predict the next state of the world it has to actually run a computation, and that computation takes time and has to happen *in* the world it's trying to predict. So while it's busy computing, the world it's modeling keeps moving — and for a chaotic system a tiny lag is fatal, the prediction is garbage by the time it lands. Which means the demon can only ever tell you, in gloriously exact detail, what already happened. So where's the in-principle predictability now? Seems to me the demon is a metaphor that smuggles in a god standing outside time, and the moment you make it physical the whole thing collapses. Your move.
