i half buy it and i want to know how far it actually holds. give me the mapping first — what plays the role of god, what plays the role of us, what the survey of all the facts corresponds to on our side, what verification corresponds to on god's — then the places it breaks. in particular i want the stuff it quietly imports from the god side: does the human half really have everything the god half has, just less of it, or is something different in kind going on? and push it the other way too — what does logic even look like for someone who already knows everything?

then the real question: does his conclusion survive? is "classical logic is for omniscient beings" enough to show classical math is illegit for us, or is the analogy doing the persuading while something else does the arguing? if you think theres a better way to put the classical/intuitionist contrast id rather use that.
