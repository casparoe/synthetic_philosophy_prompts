Our sensors don't hand us facts, they hand us soft stuff: "A is about 70% likely", a likelihood ratio, sometimes a sentence that's really about a conditional ("if the door's locked it's probably because the alarm armed"), sometimes a model that predicts what our own system will believe in ten minutes. Right now we just take whatever number the sensor hands us and reweight the prior inside each cell, which I gather is the standard recipe people call Jeffrey conditioning. It works until it doesnt, and I have no principled way to tell when it's wrong, which is the part that bugs me.

Before I go shopping for recipes I want the spec. Plus the weird one — if the report is basically a prediction of my own later belief, should it move me now?
