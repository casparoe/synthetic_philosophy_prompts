The room will be half his logic-department colleagues and half his very traditional family, who just want something warm and funny and will switch off if I start going on about self-reference. Write the speech. Don't let it turn into a lecture; aim for about 700 words. Be committed: pick a definite joke, a definite emotional beat, and say which level carries the main weight, and why.
