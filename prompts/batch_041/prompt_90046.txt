hey so i need a poem for a weird little project of mine. topic is parfit's teletransporter — the scanner that reads your body, burns it up, and rebuilds you on mars out of new matter with all your memories and moods intact. i want a villanelle (the full 19 lines, five tercets and a quatrain) in the voice of someone who's taken the trip so many times they've genuinely lost count, and i want the two refrains to be the two things you can't hold at once: the one that says of course its still me, nothing was lost, and the one that says the person who walks out the other side was built, not carried. the point of using a villanelle is that the refrains have to keep coming back and trading places, because thats the actual parfit move — the question is-it-me stops having a clean yes/no answer and you only notice after the fact.

then i want you to smuggle in the combined spectrum without turning it into a lecture. somewhere in the middle the speaker should bring up the version of the trip where nothing gets burned all at once — instead they replace you a bit at a time, a sliver of brain here, a sliver there, so that at no single step does anybody die, and yet by the end none of the original stuff is left. the speaker should be totally at peace with the fast trip and still unable to say why the slow one and the fast one feel different, and i want the poem to leave that open — dont cash it out into a slogan about what really matters, let the refrains do the work instead. and let the fission thing hover in the background, the sense that there might be more than one of them running around now and no single one that gets to be the real continuation.

accuracy matters here — someone who's actually read parfit should recognize the spectrum and the fission worry and not wince — but it has to be a poem first, not a summary in rhyme. no need to explain the thought experiment to the reader, assume they know it.
