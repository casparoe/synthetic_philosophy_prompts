The argument runs that if even one grabby civilisation had ever arisen in our past light cone, it would have reached us long before now, so the fact that we're sat here unbothered suggests grabby civilisations are either vanishingly rare or we're one of the very first to get going — the we're-early explanation. I find that much more interesting than just asking where everyone else is, but it's also annoyingly hard to put a number on.
