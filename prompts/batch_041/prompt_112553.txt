**What the demon actually spends**

Everyone says Maxwell's demon gets exorcised by Landauer's principle: the demon has to erase its memory, erasure costs kT ln2 per bit, and that happens to equal the work it extracted, so no free lunch. I want to argue this is the wrong story in an important way — the real fix is older and has less to do with erasure than people say.

Take the simplest case: a one-particle gas, the demon measures which half the molecule is in, drops a piston, lets it expand isothermally, pulls out kT ln2, resets, repeats. Standard line: the reset is where you pay. But the entropy drop of the gas (k ln2) is exactly matched by the entropy the demon *gains* when its memory goes from blank to one of two definite states — and that happens at the measurement, before any erasure. Count the demon as part of the system and the second law is intact at every stage with no erasure anywhere. Erasure only enters if you insist the demon be *cyclic*, i.e. reusable on a fresh gas. So erasure isn't what saves the second law; it's what kills the perpetual-motion machine, which is a narrower and frankly more boring target.

Call the non-erasing version the forgetful demon. My claim: it's not a perpetual-motion machine either, and not for Landauer reasons. It can't run forever on the same finite gas, because the gas equilibrates and, more interestingly, the demon's memory fills with records of an ever-more-known state, so the correlations it accumulates start costing more than the work it gains. The striking bit is that the demon's *knowledge* never does any work. Hand the same information to a book that can't physically couple to the gas and nothing happens — you can't extract work from a fact. Only the physical correlation does work. So "information is physical" is true in a much thinner sense than the slogan suggests: it's about correlations between physical degrees of freedom, not about content or knowledge at all.

What I'm stuck on: is the knowledge/correlation distinction actually load-bearing, or am I just restating the point that entropy is a physical quantity? And is the forgetful demon genuinely unable to win on a finite bath, or can it approach the full N kT ln2 and then just stop? Comments welcome, especially if I've botched the accounting.
