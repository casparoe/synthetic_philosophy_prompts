In the debate over Germany's nuclear exit and France's continued fleet, I keep encountering a pattern for which I would like to know whether there is an established name. A country shuts down its reactors and its territorial emissions fall, but it imports electricity from neighbors whose power is largely nuclear, so the same reactors still supply its demand; the national inventory improves while the atmosphere does not. Is there a term for decarbonization that is genuine inside a jurisdiction's borders but partly a reallocation across them? Carbon leakage seems close, but it usually describes industry relocating to escape a carbon price, not electricity trade and the choice of accounting boundary. If no term fits exactly, can you name and define the phenomenon, distinguishing it from carbon leakage, from consumption-based emissions accounting, and from ordinary outsourcing, and say where its edges lie?
