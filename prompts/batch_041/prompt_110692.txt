ok, so I've been reading rationalist blogs and lurking in the various discords for about a decade now, and I've built up alot of views about who deserves what that I'm pretty sure don't hang together. I'd rather find out here than in the middle of an argument with my brother in law. Here's the pile:

1) Nobody deserves their talents. I didn't pick my genes, my parents didn't pick theirs, and the fact that I'm the kind of person who gets up at 6am and grinds is itself something that got installed by luck of the draw. So when someone tells me they earned their money because they work harder, I mostly roll my eyes.

2) But my money is still mine. If I made it fair and square then nobody just gets to take it, that's taking stuff. I'm not some abolish-property guy.

3) Really what I care about is the process. If nobody got lied to or coerced, the outcome is fine. I don't need the numbers to line up with some cosmic scoreboard of who's morally better than who.

4) Which is why I can't stand hedge fund guys. Their pay is obscene, it's definately a real problem, and I'd support doing something about it, even though I'm fairly confident most of them didn't defraud anyone to get there.

5) I'm also not one of those people who thinks determinism means nobody can be blamed for anything. Criminals deserve punishment. It's good to praise people who do hard things. Responsibility is real to me, not a fiction we tell each other for social convenience.

6) And honestly? A world where everyone's wages got shuffled by a lottery when they turned 18 would be... morally fine? Nobody's rights get violated, it's just a different rule set going in. But I'd hate living in it and I can't articulate why, which bugs me.

So: which of these actually conflict, which ones only look like they do, how bad is each one, and what's the cheapest thing to drop to fix it? Please rank them instead of telling me they're all sort of in tension, and please don't tell me to just go read the standard stuff — I want to know specifically which pair breaks and what it costs to patch it.
