The room will be mostly older gardeners who know pests and pollinators well, are skeptical that insects feel anything, and dislike being lectured by outsiders; a few think caring about bugs is sentimental. Prepare my Q&A sheet: six or so objections in their vocabulary, ordered by how likely they are and how much damage they do to my case, each with a short reply and a note on where the reply is weakest, and end with the question I am hoping nobody asks. Keep it to at most 500 words, without citations, and do not explain insect neuroscience to me.
