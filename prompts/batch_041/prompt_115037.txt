It's been chewing at me for weeks, and I've definately been re-litigating old arguments with my ex through it, which isn't helping.

Anyway. Don't explain the topic to me — plan how I explain it. What has to land before what, and why that order rather than the other one (I'd guess 'disagreement' has to come before 'fact', but tell me). Say which one carries it, what the other is for if anything, and what a case has to have or not have to work here. Then the misconceptions — the ones they'll walk in with and the ones I'll plant myself without meaning to — and roughly where in the ninety minutes I have to head each one off. One question for the end, to check whether it actually landed rather than just whether they nodded. And tell me what to skip and to admit I'm skipping it.

Answer in British English please — my mom's from Manchester and she'll be sat in the front row.
