You are a philosopher of language and metaphysics with a specialist's command of the literature on propositional unity, predication, and the regress arguments that cluster around them. Think carefully and in stages before you answer, and give me your own reasoned assessment rather than a survey.

I am drawn to the following position: the unity of the proposition cannot be secured by a relational tie — a copula or unifying relation — because any such relation would itself need to be unified with the very items it was supposed to bind, and that demand simply repeats; so the regress is vicious, and any account that treats a proposition as a structured complex whose constituents are held together by a further relation is a dead end. Unity must therefore be either primitive or not a matter of composition at all.

What I want is a map of what would count against this. In particular: (a) which proposed escapes actually block the regress and which merely relocate it — treating one constituent as an incomplete or unsaturated entity that the others complete; the multiple-relation account of judgment that dispenses with the proposition altogether; primitivism about unity; holistic or top-down accounts; and the idea of a content that in some sense represents or secures its own unity; (b) what turns on whether the regress is vicious or benign, i.e. whether it is a regress of entities, of explanations, or of truthmakers, and whether an infinite descent is a defect here; (c) the proves-too-much worry: if the argument succeeds, does it also show that ordinary relational facts are impossible, and is that a reductio of the argument or a bullet the position should bite; and (d) which commitments the position has that its own defenders would concede as awkward.

Rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, tell me which single one would move you most, and say honestly whether the position risks anything at all or has been insulated by qualifications — and if it has, whether that is a defect. Include at least one response you consider underrated and one you consider widely repeated but hollow.
