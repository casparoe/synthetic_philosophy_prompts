I'm in a reading group that's been working through some metaphysics stuff, and there's a move that keeps happening that I don't have a word for. Here's the shape of it. Two of us will be going at it about whether some disputed thing really exists -- we did numbers one week, and another week it was whether a certain social category "really exists" -- and after an hour it turns out we agree on basically every concrete fact you could state. We assent to the same sentences, we agree on what the thing does, on what would be different if you took it away, on which predictions come out true. And then someone says, well, so this is just a disagreement about how to use the word "exist," and everyone nods, and it feels like the argument got dissolved rather than settled. Fine, I get that part.

But here's the bit I can't label. The person who says "it's just about the word" seems to think that settles it in their favor, as if the thing's not existing follows from the dispute being about words. And the other person isn't just proposing a different vocabulary for the same facts -- they think the first person is using the word wrong, but they can't point to any fact that would show it. So it's not a clean verbal dispute, I don't think, because the agreement on the facts is doing less work than it looks like it's doing, and it's not just two people talking past each other either, because the "it's just verbal" move is itself being contested. There's a weird loop where the diagnosis becomes the next battleground.

Is there a name for this? I keep hearing "verbal dispute" thrown around but that seems to be the thing you're supposed to be able to resolve by getting clear on the facts, not this messier creature. I have a feeling there are two or three nearby concepts and I'd like to know which is closest, how the thing I'm describing differs from each of them, and whether anyone has a name for exactly this -- or whether I should just coin one, and if so what the definition should be and where the edges are. Like, is this the same as someone quietly changing the subject, or is accusing the other person of changing the subject a different move again? Don't just hand me a term, I want the carving.
