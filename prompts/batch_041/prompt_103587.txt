Honestly the SIA verdict repulses me — it feels like a theory wins a mountain of credence for free, just by postulating more people rather than by predicting anything — but I cant articulate why the SSA escape isn't equally ad hoc, and the SSA people keep trotting out doomsday-flavored conclusions I also don't like, so I'm stuck.
