I'm putting together a handout for a reading group on Moloch / multipolar traps, and I want a worked example that isn't the usual "two countries arm-race" or "everyone overfishes" thing, because everyone's already got those memorized and they let you coast. So build me a race-to-the-bottom from scratch.

Specs I care about: (1) it has to be realistic — a situation a normal person could plausibly be in or read about in a local paper, no sci-fi, no superintelligence, no evil mastermind; (2) no villains — every participant is doing something they can defend, and each step is individually rational given what everyone else is doing; (3) here's the twist I actually want: the thing that gets degraded has to be non-rivalrous. Not a shared fish stock, not a budget, not bandwidth — something where my having more of it doesn't use any of it up for you. Think of a norm, a standard, a shared benchmark, an expectation, a reputation for a whole category of people. That's the part I think is underdiscussed and I want the case to make it vivid; (4) don't let it be the cheap version where one big player is simply dominant and drags everyone else along — everyone has to be roughly the same size; (5) keep the scenario itself to one paragraph.

Then walk me through the analysis: name what kind of trap this is and why it's that rather than a plain tragedy of the commons or a one-shot prisoner's dilemma; show for each party why the move they make is the rational one; and say explicitly whether making everyone nicer / more altruistic would fix it, and why or why not. Then tell me which single structural feature is doing the heavy lifting — the thing that, if I removed it, would make the whole trap evaporate — and describe what the situation looks like with that feature gone (does it become a coordination problem, a commons, a normal market?). Finally, steelman a critic who says the race was avoidable all along and everyone in it was just being cowardly or unimaginative; what would they have to show to be right?

End with a last line starting "Verdict:" and then a short label for what kind of trap it is, a few words max.
