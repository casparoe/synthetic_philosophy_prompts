I run the schedule at a design studio — 60ish people — which isn't my job exactly, I'm a senior designer, but I ended up owning it, and that's how I fell into this. About a year ago we went to a four-day week. Not as an experiment with a write-up, just because the two owners read something and got excited about it. It worked for maybe three months.

Here's the thing I can't stop chewing on. The work got done. Clients didn't notice, our output didn't drop, the numbers were basically flat, and everybody I talked to said having the Friday was the best thing that happened to them that year. And then, quietly, over the summer, it came back. No memo, no announcement. What happened is that the people who actually took the Friday off gradually stopped appearing in certain conversations. Nobody was punished. Nobody said anything. But when a new project came up and we needed a lead, it was always someone who'd been around on Friday.
