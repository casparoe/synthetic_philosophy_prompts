Next week's podcast guest argues that money-pump arguments tell us nothing about diachronic rationality. Her line: a pump only shows that an agent with cyclical or shifting preferences can be made to lose money, and the same trick works on anyone who discounts the future, revises her beliefs on new evidence, or actively wants her own tastes to change -- so the argument form overgeneralises.
