In the variant, the distinguishing rule is not a property of any single panel but a relation that only becomes visible when you compare the first and last panels; in a further variant, the rule is something like the rule from the previous puzzle but with inside and outside, or figure and ground, swapped. These seem to put pressure on the usual story that few-shot concept induction is just hypothesis selection over simple predicates, because the hypothesis space itself seems to depend on the order in which the examples are presented and on the solver’s history with earlier puzzles.
