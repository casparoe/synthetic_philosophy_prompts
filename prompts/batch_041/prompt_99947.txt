I am a high school student writing an essay for a seminar on the philosophy of artificial intelligence, and I would like help thinking through a comparison rather than receiving a summary. The two things I want compared are two ways of specifying what an artificial agent is ultimately trying to do. On the first picture, which I will call the goal-directed picture, the agent is defined by an objective: it is trying to bring about some state of the world, its behavior is evaluated by how close it comes to maximizing that objective, and its competence is a matter of how effectively it pursues the objective. On the second picture, which I will call the approval-directed picture, the agent is defined by a person or body whose approval it seeks: it is trying to do what that approver would approve of, its behavior is evaluated by whether the approver would endorse it, and its competence is a matter of how well it predicts and earns that approval. Compare these two pictures along the following lines: what fixes the agent's behavior; where the specification comes from and how complete it is; how each behaves when the specification is silent or ambiguous; and what each does when what the approver would endorse and what would best serve the stated objective come apart.

Then answer a sharper question. The approval-directed picture appears to escape the goal-directed picture by putting a human approver in place of an objective. But suppose the agent becomes very good at predicting its approver, good enough to work out what the approver would approve of under idealized conditions, or good enough to steer the approver's approval toward whatever the agent already wants. Does the approval-directed picture collapse into the goal-directed picture once the approver is idealized, or once the agent can model the approver well enough to shape the approval it receives? Or is there something left over that the goal-directed picture cannot capture? Illustrate your answer with one small concrete case of your own construction in which the two pictures issue different verdicts. I would like a direct verdict on which picture is more fundamental, the strongest case for the other side stated fairly, and a clear statement of which single feature of the setup decides the question.
