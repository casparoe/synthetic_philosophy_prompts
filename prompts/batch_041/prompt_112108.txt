My opening move, and I'm the sceptic here, not the pastor. My problem is the step from 'something is done to the soul' to 'this tells the merchant something about his son'. But then a nativity can only ever say how a man is disposed on a given day, never what he will do or what will befall him, and a horoscope that can't say that isn't the art anyone pays you for.
