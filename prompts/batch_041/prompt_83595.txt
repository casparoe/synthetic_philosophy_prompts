I spent most of last night arguing with my friend, a philosopher of mind who has spent the better part of a decade defending Integrated Information Theory and who treats the cerebellum as the theory's flagship case: more neurons than the cortex, she says, yet almost no integrated causal structure and therefore almost no experience. Her verdicts on a run of cases, as best I can reconstruct them from the argument: a healthy adult human is conscious; a patient with a destroyed cortex but an intact brainstem is not; a split-brain patient has two streams of experience rather than one; the cerebellum on its own is not a seat of consciousness; a deep feedforward network that labels images is not conscious; a recurrent network that maintains and globally broadcasts information and can report on its own internal states is conscious; a bee is not conscious; an octopus is; a plant is not; and a thermostat is not. She also insists that the correct theory must be substrate-neutral in principle, that it identifies consciousness with a system's integrated information, often written as phi, and that the exclusion postulate, the idea that only one complex at a given grain counts, tells us where to draw the line. Now a lab she collaborates with has grown a lentil-sized cortical organoid from human stem cells. It has no sensory organs, no behavior, and no capacity for report. But it shows spontaneous, highly coordinated recurrent activity; its causal structure is not decomposable into independent parts; perturbing one region produces complex, non-repeating waves across the whole; and on the measures she favors it scores higher on integration than a mouse cortex. She says she is genuinely torn and wants to know what she should say. Predict her verdict on whether the organoid is conscious, identify the candidate rules that fit her earlier verdicts, say what each rule predicts here, give me your best guess and the reasons for preferring it, and name the further case or experiment that would most cleanly separate those rules. I am less interested in settling the metaphysics than in seeing which rule she is actually running, and which of her earlier verdicts is the closest analog and whether it should control. I do not need a reading list or citations; I want the inference from her track record.
