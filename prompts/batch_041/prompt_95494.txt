My thesis is that the whole fink and mask industry has overcomplicated something that's actually just a bookkeeping problem. A disposition, I want to say, is a conditional causal power: if the system is in the right internal state and the environment cooperates, the manifestation follows. A fink is just a mask that operates on the disposition itself rather than on its manifestation, and a mask is just a fink that leaves the disposition intact but blocks the output. I model it like an API contract: the disposition is an endpoint with preconditions, invariants, and an environment; a fink is a config change that removes the endpoint when a probe arrives; a mask is an interceptor that lets the endpoint exist but drops the response. So the fix isn't a new ontology of powers, it's better bookkeeping about the whole system.
