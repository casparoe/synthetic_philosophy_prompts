My opening: fine, no internal scan of a chess engine's weights tells you it's playing chess rather than some gerrymandered game that coincides with chess on every game it will ever play. But that cuts both ways. It's just as true of the eye — nothing in the retina fixes that it's "for" seeing, and Darwin's whole trick was to squeeze design-quality results out of a process with no grasp of the design. Same shape as Turing's suggestion that instead of simulating an adult mind you'd do better to build something like a child and then educate it: you dont build in the understanding, you build in the shaping. So when you tell me the engine has no rule, I say of course it doesnt, thats the point — and the rule you keep demanding is doing no explanatory work.
