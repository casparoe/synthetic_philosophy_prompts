ok so this is probably a mess but it's been rattling around my head for months and I'd rather someone tell me now than keep building on it. I read a lot of history stuff, no training in it, and here's roughly where I've ended up about explaining why big things happen:

1. There's never one cause. "What caused the First World War" is a bad question — same for the fall of Rome, same for basically anything big.
2. But I don't think that means it's all mush either. Some things were load-bearing and some were just background. If your account can't say the assassination counted for more than the weather that day, you're not explaining, your just listing.
3. As far as I can tell a cause is just something the event depended on. Take it away, change nothing else, and the event doesn't happen. That's the only test I can actually apply to anything.
4. Which I know you can't literally do, since it only happened once. So all those "if he'd never been born" books are fiction. I'm never sure how much that matters though — feels like it should matter more than it does.
5. Someone told me once that people use "cause" for two different things — one is "this is what made the difference," the other is "this is what produced the thing" — and that in weird cases they point at different things. I think I believe both at once, which I suspect is not allowed.
6. Where it really gets me: sometimes stuff was going to happen anyway. Say a harvest fails, there's a war on, and the government falls — and it would of fallen even if only one of those had happened. Then by my test in 3, neither one caused it, because the other would have done the job on its own. But obviously both did something. If two people shoot the same man at the same time and either shot alone would have killed him, we don't say he died of nothing, and we don't let one of them walk.
7. And the thing I actually care about: I think understanding an event is mostly being able to tell the story so it clicks, not ticking off a checklist of necessary conditions.

So does this hang together? Which of these can't live with which, how bad is each clash, and what's the cheapest thing I'd have to drop to fix it? And please tell me if some of them only look like they clash, because I have a feeling a couple of them are fine and I'm just spooked by one word doing two jobs.
