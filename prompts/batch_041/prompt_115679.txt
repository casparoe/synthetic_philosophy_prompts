I've talked myself into the moral equality of combatants -- the view that a soldier fighting for an unjust cause has the same moral standing, and the same permission to kill, as one fighting for a just cause. I got there by the epistemic route: individual soldiers can't reasonably be expected to know whether their side's war is just, so it seems wrong to hold each of them to the jus ad bellum verdict, and that gets you the symmetry. But this defense and the other main one -- war as a rule-governed practice, combatants acting in a role, so the justice of the cause doesn't attach to them personally -- seem to pull in different directions, and I'm no longer sure which one I'm actually committed to.

Something else nags at me. Being excused for not knowing seems like it should bear on whether you're to blame, not on whether it's permissible for someone to kill you in self-defense. Those feel like different things, and if they are, I'm not sure the epistemic defense even supports the conclusion I want.

So: give me a map of what would count against the position, and where possible against each version of it seperately. If the epistemic defense is carrying the weight, it should predict that the equality dissolves when the ignorance does -- a war whose injustice is common knowledge among the troops, or one where the state deliberately manufactured their ignorance, or where the soldiers privately think the cause is unjust and fight anyway. Does MEC survive those? If a defender says yes, have they quietly slid to the conventionalist version, and is that slide a problem or just a clarification? And what would count against that version -- my suspicion is it can't distinguish a just war from an unjust one at all, which is either the whole point or a reductio, and I want to be told which.

Also tell me what defenders themselves would concede counts against them, not just what critics say, and rank the defeaters from the ones that should actually make me drop the view to the ones that are merely awkward. If it turns out the position is basically unfalsifiable, say so plainly and tell me whether thats fatal here or just the price of a view like this.
