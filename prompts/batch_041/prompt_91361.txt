I sit on the eligibility panel of a mid-sized international athletics federation -- we're the ones who decide who gets into the women's category -- and there's a file waiting for us next month. Our record so far: (1) a trans woman, 26, full male puberty, testosterone suppressed for two years, applied for weightlifting -- denied; (2) same profile, applied for target shooting -- approved; (3) a trans woman, 20, on blockers since 11, never went through male puberty -- approved for the 400m; (4) a cis woman with a 46,XY DSD and naturally high testosterone who refused suppressants -- denied in the 800m; (5) a trans man on testosterone -- cleared for the men's category, and when he asked to run in the women's race instead, denied.

Now the file: a trans woman, 21, on blockers from 15 (so she got a couple of years of male puberty in first), testosterone under our threshold for the past two years, wants the 400m. What do we do if we want to be consistant with ourselves? I care less about the verdict than about which rules our five calls are jointly consistent with -- I suspect there's more than one -- what each of them predicts here, which you'd actually bet on, and which case we'd have to see next to separate them.
