Here's the argument:

(2) If no created thing has causal power, then no created thing can produce an effect in another.
(3) When I reason from premises to a conclusion, my grasp of the premises produces my conclusion-belief.
(4) So if occasionalism is true, my reasoning does not produce my conclusion; God creates that belief directly, and the felt inferential connection is just another habit.
(5) If God creates beliefs directly, then the belief is not produced because of its inferential relation to the premises; it could have been created no matter what the premises were.
(6) If my belief is not produced because of its relation to the premises, then my inference is not responsive to evidence and cannot make the conclusion rational for me.
(7) So occasionalism makes rational inference impossible, including the rational inference to occasionalism itself.
(8) Therefore occasionalism is self-undermining.
