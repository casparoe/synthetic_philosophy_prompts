And then people slide straight from that to a conclusion, usually one of two: "so interpretability from the outside is basically hopeless," or the flip side, "so we have no choice but to crack open the weights and look inside." I'm sympathetic to the premise but I get the feeling the analogy is quietly doing more work than anyone admits.
