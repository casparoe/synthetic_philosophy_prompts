My further claims are that this is the whole story, since every cycle is confined within a grain and a money pump can profit only by exploiting a cycle, so no pump can extract more than a grain; and that there is no agent-independent betterness for the grain to fall short of.

Assume nobody has read the paper.

Give me the objections in the order they will come, ordered by how likely and how damaging they are, each phrased the way that particular audience member would phrase it, with a two-minute reply, a note on where the reply is soft, and what I should concede versus hold.
