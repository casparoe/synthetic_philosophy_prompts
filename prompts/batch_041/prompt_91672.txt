I want you to translate, as faithfully as you can, David Lewis's analysis of convention into that vocabulary. As I understand it, the analysis runs roughly like this. A convention is a regularity in the behavior of the members of a group in a recurring coordination situation: everyone conforms; everyone expects everyone else to conform; everyone prefers to conform given that the others do; and the regularity is arbitrary in the sense that at least one alternative regularity would have solved the same coordination problem just as well. Common knowledge of all this is part of the account, not a psychological footnote. Conventions so understood are what let language work, since meaning rests on conventions of truthfulness and trust; and a convention is supposed to be normative — it gives the parties a reason to conform and grounds criticism of those who do not.
