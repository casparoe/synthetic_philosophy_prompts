Context: second-year PhD, social policy / political theory, working on conditionality and the whole deserving-undeserving business. Honest version of what I think:

1. The welfare state can legitimately ask something back from people who can work — not because idleness is a vice, but because a scheme that's a pure one-way transfer doesn't produce the mutual standing that membership is supposed to involve. Contribution in, support out.
2. I have no time at all for the deserving/undeserving distinction. Nobody's market income tracks their moral worth; a different temperament and a different set of chances and you'd be somewhere else in the queue, and the queue doesn't reflect anything about you.
3. Benefit fraud is a real wrong and I'm fine with it being a criminal offence. Claiming while you're working cash in hand is taking out of a pot that everyone else is paying into in good faith.
4. Tax avoidance I don't think of as fraud at all — using the rules as written, however aggressively, is just a different activity, and people who lump the two together are being sloppy.
5. In practice I want the sanction regime gutted. Nobody should lose their rent money or go hungry over a missed appointment, a badly scanned form, or an assessor having a bad day. Ever.
6. I don't think the state has any business assessing people's characters or nudging their behaviour. Claimants are agents with their own projects, not interventions.
7. Care work is work. Someone looking after a disabled parent full time is already doing the thing the system claims to be asking for. I'd go further: I like the sound of a participation income — everyone gets it as long as they're doing something socially useful, care included — and I honestly dont see why anyone would object to that.
8. Methodologically, the case for conditionality has to earn its keep on the evidence. If conditioning people doesn't actually move them into work that pays enough to live on, the normative argument loses most of its point.

So: what's pulling against what, ranked, with the cheapest fix for each and what the fix costs me? And please don't just flag every pair — I want the ones that are real.
