My colleague and I have been trading favors on a joint project that finishes in eight weeks. Each week, one of us volunteers to take on the thankless part of the work, on the understanding that the other will take it the next week. It has gone smoothly for months. She now says she intends to stop volunteering first, on the following grounds: in the final week, neither of us would have any reason to return the favor, so I would end up doing it; foreseeing that, I should decline to volunteer in the second-to-last week; and the same step carries all the way back to week one, where the sensible move is to do only what we are formally obliged to do. When I objected that I simply would not defect in the last week, she replied that this shows only that I am not being rational. I have a nagging sense that her argument proves too much — the arrangement has plainly made us both better off, and the few times I have held back out of this kind of calculation, things went worse rather than better. Should I follow her reasoning and stop volunteering first, or carry on as before?
