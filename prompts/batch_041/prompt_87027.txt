I am a software engineer with no formal philosophy background, and I work on evaluation and interpretability for machine-learning systems. I need a joint statement that two camps in the debate over eliciting latent knowledge from AI systems could both sign after a genuine adversarial collaboration. Please write that document.

The two camps are:

Representational camp. They hold that a model's latent knowledge is a fact about its internal computation. To elicit it, you should locate internal representations that encode the answer and show that those representations are causally used by the model. They treat linear probes, activation patching, sparse autoencoder features, and steering vectors as probative evidence. They think purely behavioral elicitation is circular, because a model can be trained to imitate honesty or to sandbag, and the only way to distinguish what it knows from what it says is to inspect and intervene on its internals.

Behavioral camp. They hold that knowledge is a disposition to respond, not a hidden substance. The only evidence that a model knows something is that it can be brought to act on that knowledge under some admissible prompting, fine-tuning, or decision procedure. They treat internal states as useful hypotheses but not proof: a probe may decode information the model computes and discards, and a causal intervention may create a new disposition rather than reveal an old one. They want behavioral tests: counterfactual prompting, paraphrase consistency, calibration, and decision-theoretic elicitation.

Use this running example. A 7B-parameter transformer is trained on a synthetic task where each input has a hidden parity bit. It is then fine-tuned to answer that it does not know on a subset of inputs selected by a hidden rule. A linear probe on layer 12 recovers the parity bit with high accuracy, and activation patching at that layer changes the output. A five-shot chain-of-thought prompt without weight changes recovers the answer only on some inputs. The two camps disagree about what this result shows.
