I know this material well enough to talk for an hour but I've never taught it and I'm worried I'll do the classic thing of unloading the counter-examples before they've felt the pull of the view being attacked. Don't explain finks and masks to me — plan how I should teach them. What has to be in place before the first counter-example lands, i.e. the dependency order: does the simple "would break if struck" analysis have to be stated and half-believed before I show it failing, and where exactly does the fink go relative to the mask, and the mimic relative to both? Which single case should carry the whole session — I want one example I can twist three ways so they see it's one phenomenon with three different twists rather than three unrelated puzzle-cases, and I need to know what that case has to have (a disposition everyone grants, an interference that's clearly the stimulus itself in one version and clearly external in another) and what it has to lack so it doesn't smuggle in extra controversy. And the misconceptions: I'm expecting at least "a disposition is just a tendency, a statistical frequency", "the fink shows there are no dispositions at all", and "fink and mask are the same thing, something got in the way" — but I bet there are ones I'm not seeing. Tell me which they'll actually form, in the order they'll form them, and at which point in the sequence I should head each one off, and which ones I should let them form on purpose because the confusion is load-bearing.

Two more things. I have two cases of my own I want to use and I'm not sure they're any good: (a) a shy person who is genuinely chatty with friends but who, the moment the friends actually turn up, gets anxious and goes silent — I think that's a fink, because the stimulus itself kills the disposition rather than merely blocking it; and (b) a lift whose doors are disposed to close when you press the button, except a sensor reopens them whenever something breaks the beam — I think that's a mask, since the disposition to close is still there and something outside it is just stopping the manifestation. Tell me whether those are right, which if either should be the carrying case, and if they're wrong, what they actually are and what a corrected pair would look like.
