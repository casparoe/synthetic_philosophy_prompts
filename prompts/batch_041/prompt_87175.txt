I have come to think that Pascal's account of justice and force is broadly correct, but I suspect it is really a bundle of separable claims that are too often run together. On the one hand, there is the efficacy claim: justice without force is impotent, because merely aspirational norms cannot secure compliance against those who have no interest in obeying them. On the other hand, there is the authority claim: force without justice is tyrannical, so force cannot by itself make a rule just. And then there is the genealogical claim: because we cannot make what is just strong, we make what is strong just, so established law comes to be regarded as just simply by being established. I want you to treat this package as a position and tell me what would count against it.

Please separate the efficacy claim from the authority claim and from the genealogical claim, and say what would count against each. In particular, examine a distinction I have not seen sufficiently explored: between force as a causal means of securing compliance and force as a constitutive condition of what counts as a rule of justice at all. Could a rule be a rule of justice if no one could ever enforce it? If not, does that vindicate the Pascalian package or collapse it into a claim about the concept of law rather than about justice? What would count against the constitutive claim? Consider, for example, a conquering power that imposes a legal code by force and within two generations the code is accepted as just and self-sustaining: does that vindicate the genealogical claim or show that force can generate justice in a way the authority claim denies?

Rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward. Include cases, arguments, and any formal or conceptual results you take to be relevant, but do not give me a reading list. Where possible, say what the package predicts that rival accounts of political obligation do not, and whether those predictions have been borne out. Say which defeaters a committed Pascalian would themselves concede and which they would dismiss as changing the subject. Finally, assess honestly whether the package has been insulated from criticism by sliding between the three claims, and whether that is a defect or simply what a view of this kind has to do. Please answer in British English.
