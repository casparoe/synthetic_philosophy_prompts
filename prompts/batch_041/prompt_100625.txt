The diagnosis is this: the tortoise’s regress is not a genuine threat to the justification of deduction, because it conflates the order of premises with the order of justification. On this view, one can be justified in drawing an inference by a rule without needing an additional justified premise asserting that the rule is valid; the tortoise’s demand for such a premise is a demand for a second-order justification that first-order justification does not require. What is your credence that this diagnosis is correct? Give a rough probability and one line of reasoning for each, combine them into an overall number, and then tell me which sub-question the overall number is most sensitive to and what consideration would move it most.
