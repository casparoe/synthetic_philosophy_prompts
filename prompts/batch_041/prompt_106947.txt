Please audit this summary. Which parts would a careful reader of Hume accept, which are common simplifications, and which are outright misrepresentations? I especially want the distinction between the long-run quantity theory and the short-run adjustment process, and between Hume's opposition to mercantilist policy and any blanket claim about monetary policy, kept straight, since I suspect my friend has collapsed them. Does his conclusion follow from the accurate parts of the summary, or does it depend on the errors?
