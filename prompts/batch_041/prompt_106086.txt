Anyway I want to build my own little world like that but I want it engineered to do a specific thing.

Here's the spec. Make me a tiny analogy microdomain from scratch — a few objects or symbols with a couple of features, small enough that somebody could work it out in their head, and please don't use the letters one or any puzzle you've obviously seen before, just invent the thing. Then give me a source case and a target case such that: if you do the obvious rigid match-up you get one answer; but there's some structural reason sitting in the target that forces the concept to slip, and the slipped reading gives a different answer; and the slip has to be forced by that structure, not because I told you the answer or because you just decided to be clever.
