My professor seemed to treat this as a serious problem for the whole idea that individuals have obligations to cut their own emissions. Isn't that contradictory? If my flight is genuinely inefficacious on its own, then a national pledge, which is also just a giant bundle of individual flights and drives, should be equally inefficacious — so if the objection really works, doesn't it dissolve the case for collective action too?
