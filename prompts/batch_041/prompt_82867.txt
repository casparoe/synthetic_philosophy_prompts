I am trying to settle my position on inherited wealth and dynastic advantage, and I would like a consistency check before I write it up. This is where I have got to.

(1) Equality of opportunity is the central requirement of distributive justice: a person's prospects should not depend on circumstances they did not choose, and the family one is born into is a circumstance of precisely that kind.
(2) There is nothing morally special about death as an occasion for taxation. An inheritance is a transfer like any other and should be taxed on the same schedule as gifts and income; a heavier charge on transfers at death needs a justification that the transfer itself cannot supply.
(3) What is objectionable about dynastic wealth is the advantage it confers on the recipient, not any interest the deceased had in disposing of their property; the claims of the dead cannot outweigh those of the living.
(4) Parents may legitimately favour their own children, and a society in which nothing could be passed on is not one I would want to live in.
(5) I am drawn to an inheritance tax whose rate rises with each generation the wealth has passed down untaxed, so that a fortune is charged lightly on the first transmission and heavily by the third or fourth: the target is the dynasty, not the family.
(6) I am a brute-luck egalitarian: inequalities traceable to unchosen circumstances are unjust, inequalities traceable to choice are not.

Which of these are genuinely in tension with which, and how severe is each tension? Please rank them rather than treating them as equally bad, separate the real conflicts from the ones that dissolve once the commitments are stated more carefully, and for each real conflict say what the cheapest repair is and what that repair costs me elsewhere. Then tell me which single commitment I could give up in order to leave the most coherent set, and say what would change your mind about that verdict: which consideration, if it turned out to weigh more heavily than you assume, would lead you to a different answer. Please write in British English.
