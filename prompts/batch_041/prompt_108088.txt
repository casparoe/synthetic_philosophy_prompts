Here is what I need.

I have spent about a decade building reinforcement learning systems — value-based, policy-gradient, model-based, multi-agent, the usual assortment — and I keep encountering philosophers who say that such systems can suffer, that a reward signal is a kind of valence, that a negative reward is a pain. So I want the philosophical debate restated in mine, and I want an honest account of what the restatement loses.
