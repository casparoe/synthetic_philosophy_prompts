I am a novelist at work on a book set inside a mid-sized localization company, and I have been spending time with a project manager there in order to get the texture right. The company's whole quality apparatus is a small institution with its own rituals: a source string is fed to a statistical engine, the output is cleaned up by a post-editor paid by the word, and the job is signed off only when a back-translation comes close enough to the original and the post-editor's edit distance falls under a fixed threshold. The company also gives clients a discount when a new string is a fuzzy match against the translation memory, and it evaluates post-editors on how little they alter the engine's output. The project manager defends all of this as neutral engineering. A philosopher friend of mine insists that the apparatus is not neutral at all, that it encodes a theory of what meaning is and what understanding is for. I want to understand exactly what that theory is, because the disagreement has become the intellectual spine of my novel.

What philosophical commitments does this practice actually presuppose? Does it require a deflationary or verificationist view of meaning, one on which a sentence means whatever survives the round trip, and on which a translator need not grasp context, speaker intent, or the point of an utterance? Or is the workflow compatible with a richer, hermeneutic or Wittgensteinian picture, in which translation is a form of understanding and the metrics are merely practical proxies? Please distinguish what the practice strictly presupposes from what it merely fits well with, and say where it is philosophically underdetermined, so that several theories of meaning could license the same procedures. Then tell me what would have to change in the firm's metrics, division of labor, and notion of a finished job if the presupposed view were replaced by one on which understanding is constitutive of translation. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence, then give the reasoning, with no preamble and no restating of the question.
