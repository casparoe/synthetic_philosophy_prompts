Ok so Galen Strawson's basic argument about moral responsibility. You do what you do because of the way you are; so to be ultimately responsible for what you do you'd have to be ultimately responsible for the way you are; and to be responsible for the way you are you'd have to have deliberately made yourself that way; but any reason you could have had for making yourself one way rather than another would itself just be a function of how you already were at some earlier time; so you'd have to be responsible for that too, and so on back forever. And nothing can be the cause of itself. So nobody is ever ultimately responsible for anything. He gives a fuller version where even the person who deliberately, over years, made herself selfish doesnt get off, because the standards she used to do it werent hers either.
