I'm preparing a position statement for a free will seminar and want a consistency audit before I circulate it. Here is what I hold.

(1) The standard counterfactual-intervener cases get the right verdict: an agent who acts on her own reasons, while a device stands ready to have made her do the same thing had she wavered, is fully blameworthy for what she does. Alternative possibilities are not necessary for moral responsibility.
(2) I am nevertheless a libertarian. Free will is incompatible with determinism, and the kind of ultimate authorship that grounds desert requires indeterminism somewhere in the actual causal history of the action.
(3) My account of blame is attributability-flavored: to blame someone is to hold her action against her as expressing her evaluative orientation, the quality of will she actually had.
(4) Manipulation cases cut the other way. An agent whose values, deliberative habits, and reasons were engineered from birth by a team of scientists is not blameworthy for the crimes she commits, even though those crimes flow from her own reasons and no one bypasses her will at the time of action.
(5) The paradigm excuses — physical compulsion, ignorance of the relevant facts — operate by showing that the action was not attributable to the agent at all, not by showing that she lacked an alternative.

I have also been worrying about a pair of cases I built for a term paper. In the first, Lee betrays a friend on his own; a dormant device was set to take over and produce the same betrayal, for the same reasons, had he wavered. In the second, Lee's double was engineered from birth to have exactly those reasons, with no device anywhere. The actual sequences are identical; only the counterfactual and causal-historical surroundings differ.

Which of my commitments are genuinely in tension? Please order the tensions by severity, separate the real ones from the ones that only appear to be real — I suspect some do — and for each real one state the cheapest repair and what it would cost me. Finally, tell me which single commitment is doing the most damage to the rest.
