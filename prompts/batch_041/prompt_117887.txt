(P) Deep cluelessness is real and pervasive. Count (P) true if this holds for a substantial share (say, a majority) of the consequentialist-relevant decisions a well-resourced agent faces over a lifetime, and false if it holds only for a fringe subset.

- Whether the underlying sensitivity claim is true: that micro-differences in present conditions propagate to macro-differences in the far-future outcomes we care about, so that the sign of an action's long-run effect is hostage to details no one can know.
