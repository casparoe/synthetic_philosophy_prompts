I've been going back and forth with a friend about Hofstadter's careenium essay — the one where Achilles and the Tortoise argue about a giant billiards-table brain whose balls are also symbols (simmballs), so that every physical shove is also a symbolic transaction and the question becomes who shoves whom around in there. My friend thinks the careenium dissolves the question: if the balls are simmballs then the physical level and the symbolic level are the same level described twice, and there's nothing left to ask. I think thats too fast, because I have a pile of gut verdicts about ordinary cases where the symbol level seems to do real work in some and be a mere gloss in others, and I can't get them to line up under any single principle.

Here's where my gut lands. Thermostat: it turned the furnace on because the room got cold — fine, that's a real because, the bimetallic strip is the cold-detector and the relay is downstream of it. Pocket calculator: it displays 6 because you asked it for 2+4 — I want to say that's literally true, the arithmetic is implemented in the gates and the gates did the work. A giant lookup table with the same input-output behavior: it does not compute the sum, it just retrieves it — and I notice this commits me to caring about how the thing is built, not just what it does. Chess engine: it played Nf3 to control the center — I flinch, I want to say the engine has no designs on the center and the move was picked by search, but I can't say what's different from the calculator. Honeybee waggle dance: the dance tells the other bees the nectar is 400m north, and the content causes them to fly north — fine, that seems like real information transfer. Deep net that outputs cat and opens a door: I lean no, some floats opened the door and the cat-recognition is a label we paste on afterward — but this looks structurally like the bee case, which I just said was fine. Rock rolling downhill because it wants to get to the bottom: not fine, nothing is represented anywhere. Sunspot causing a radio blackout: fine, but there's no symbol in it at all. A market falling because investors are worried about rate hikes: fine, even though I couldn't point to the representation if you asked me.

My tentative principle is that the symbol level is causally real when the system would have behaved differently had the symbol been different, and the difference in behavior is explained by the difference in content rather than just by the difference in physical state. But I can see that this either makes everything real (any content difference is a physical difference) or nothing real (any physical difference can be redescribed as a content difference), so it isn't doing any work. Can you give me the principle, or a small set of candidate principles, that actually gets my verdicts — tell me precisely which clause is motivated by which case, what each principle predicts for the cat door, the lookup table, and the careenium itself, and be honest about which of my verdicts it forces me to drop? I suspect I have to lose either the calculator verdict or the bee verdict and I'd like to know which one, and why.
