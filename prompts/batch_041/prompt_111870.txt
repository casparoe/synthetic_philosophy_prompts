I'm a retired soil scientist — thirty years on nitrate leaching, mostly — and since I packed it in I've been working my way through philosophy, aesthetics mainly, partly because I somehow ended up as the lay member on the selection panel for our village arts festival. The potter and the retired art teacher do the real judging; I'm there because I read the paperwork and I'm too stubborn to be talked round. The festival has two prizes, one badged Art and one badged Craft, and every entry gets put in one or the other before judging, which in practice means we spend most of the evening arguing about what the difference is meant to be while the marquee leaks.

Anyway, over the last few years we've made these calls (I've checked them against the minutes, so I'm not misremembering): an embroidered panel telling the story of the maker's family leaving a village in the Fens went to Art — she'd sent in a page about it; a cushion cover in the same stitch, purely geometric pattern, no note at all, went to Craft; a very accomplished oil painting of the maker's own kitchen window, sent in with three sentences about the light at about four in the afternoon, went to Art; a beautifully made oak field gate, no note, went to Craft; and a carved wooden hand on a plinth, with a short note about her father's hands, went to Art. I may be forgetting one or two, but those are the ones that keep coming back to me at night.

This year there's one entry I simply can't place, and it's this I want your help with. It's a memorial bench — oak, perfectly useable, you'd sit on it — and the maker has sent in a long statement about her stillborn son and about what a bench is actually for, being sat on by strangers who don't know why it's there. She can't come to the judging, so we can't ask her anything, we've just got the bench and the statement. What I want to know is: if we're being consistent with ourselves, which prize does it go in, and what rule are we actually running? I can see at least two readings of our own past decisions that fit everything above and yet don't agree about this bench, and I'd rather not have it settled by whoever's loudest on the night. So give me your best guess at our verdict, tell me which rules fit our record equally well and what each one predicts here, say which you'd back and why, and name me the sort of entry we could put in next year that would finally force us to pick between them.
