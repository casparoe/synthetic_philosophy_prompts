I've been chewing on Dennett's crane/skyhook distinction for years, and I've finally got a concrete case where I can't tell which side it falls on, so I want your credence with the reasoning exposed rather than a verdict.

The candidate crane: call it prospective entrainment. Take a social species whose members must predict one another in order to coordinate. Add selection on the accuracy with which an individual can simulate the consequences of its own possible future actions, as conspecifics would respond to them. Once those simulations get reliable enough, the individual's own simulator becomes the cheapest available predictor of that individual, so it gets recruited for self-prediction; and once a creature is systematically predicted by a model it partly controls, the model's outputs acquire the functional role we call ought-judgments — directives that constrain the creature's own future behavior. The claim on the table is that this is a genuine crane for the capacity to be moved by normative reasons: it does the lifting with ordinary selection plus representational machinery already on hand, and it never presupposes a normative fact.

Give me your credence that the claim is true, decomposed. At minimum: (a) the recruitment step, that a simulator good enough to predict others can be turned on its own subject without new machinery; (b) non-circularity, that accuracy and reliable enough can be cashed out without already invoking correctness or rule-following; (c) non-greediness, that the story descends through developmental and cultural levels instead of leaping from selection straight to semantics; (d) transfer, that the same schema yields a crane for some second capacity, which would be evidence it's a real schema and not a relabeling; (e) that the account doesn't merely relocate the skyhook, for instance by making social pressure an unexplained normative force. A number and one line of justification for each, then the combination, then which term the total is most sensitive to. I'm at 0.35; a friend who thinks any selection story about normativity is a promissory note written in evolutionary vocabulary is at 0.7. Tell me which term the two of us are actually disagreeing about, and what evidence or argument would move your number most.
