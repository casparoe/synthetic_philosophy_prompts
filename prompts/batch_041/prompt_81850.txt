I've been going around in circles on this and I need someone to just tell me what I actually believe. Heres the thing: a tiny chance of something huge is supposed to make a gamble worth taking, and sometimes my gut agrees and sometimes it thinks thats the dumbest thing it's ever heard. Cases and my honest reactions:

A guy at a bar offers me a bet. I hand him $10, he rolls a 1000-sided die, if it lands on 1 he gives me a million dollars. I'd take that instantly, feels like free money.

Same guy, same $10, but now it's a 1-in-a-million chance at a billion dollars. I'd take that too. Feels pretty much the same as the last one.

Now the same $10 for a 1-in-a-billion chance at a trillion dollars. I know these three are all worth about the same on average, like $990 to me either way. But my gut says no on this one. Dont be an idiot, keep your ten bucks. And I genuinely can't tell you why, the payout is absurdly better.

Next one. A stranger on the street says if I don't give him $10 right now he'll have me tortured for a year. I figure theres maybe a 1-in-a-billion chance he can actually pull that off. My gut says dont pay, he's full of it. But if I really, truly believed it was 1-in-a-billion, then paying $10 to dodge a year of torture seems obviously right. So the same number flips depending on whether I believe the guy, which feels like cheating.

And then the one that keeps me up: a 1-in-a-trillion chance to stop something infinitely bad from happening, forever, for $10. Some cosmic horror deal. I have no idea. Some days I'd pay, some days I think paying is how they get you.

Smaller stuff too, for contrast: a guaranteed $10 in my hand versus a 1-in-100 shot at $2000. I take the sure $10 every time, even though the gamble's worth twice as much on average. But in the die case I'm perfectly happy to gamble. So I'm scared of risk in one spot and love it in another.

My half-baked theory was that the rule is just: ignore anything under about 1 in a million, treat it as zero. That covers the billion case and the trillion case and the street guy. But it doesn't explain why I happily take the 1-in-a-million billion-dollar bet, and it makes me feel like a coward about the cosmic one, and honestly I dont know where the cutoff would even be.

Is there a real principle that gets all of these, or close? Or do I have to give up one of my gut calls to have a clean rule? And whatever principle you land on, what does it say about two cases I havent mentioned: a 1-in-10 chance at $20 versus $1 certain, and a 1-in-a-billion chance of saving a strangers life.
