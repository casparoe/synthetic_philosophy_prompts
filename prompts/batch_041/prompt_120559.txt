My reading is that this is best understood as a bias — a reluctance to reason about unknown probabilities that a good Bayesian should simply correct — and that the correction is straightforward: adopt a uniform prior over the black/yellow split, at which point the two pairs of bets have identical expected value, and then do whatever it takes to bring my gut into line with the arithmetic. I've been running this on myself for a while. It works, more or less, but it feels like cheating, and I can't tell whether that feeling is residual irrationality or evidence that I've misread the problem.
