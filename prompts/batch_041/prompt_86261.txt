Ive been trying to get clear on the difference between an epistemic bubble and an echo chamber, and I keep hitting a case that seems to fit neither cleanly. Picture a forum where opposing views are allowed and even visible, but anyone who states them is treated as naive or as acting in bad faith, so the views are technically present but socially unusable. Ive been calling that a credibility moat: the information gets in, but the messenger cant. Compare that with the distinction I keep seeing drawn between bubbles as exclusion by omission and echo chambers as exclusion by discrediting, and tell me whether a credibility moat is just a version of the echo chamber or something distinct. What I care about is whether the real difference is about the flow of information or about the conditions for trusting a source, and whether we need a third category for when you hear the other side but lack the concepts to make sense of it.
