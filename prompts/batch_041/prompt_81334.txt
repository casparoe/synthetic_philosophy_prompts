I'm a software engineer on the content team at a company that builds a history tutor used in middle- and high-school classrooms, and I have to write the memo our curriculum review board votes on next week. Our problem is that the app answers questions about the Trojan War, King Arthur, and the Exodus according to whatever the last writer happened to believe, and two of our largest districts have now complained in opposite directions: one that we present legend as established fact, the other that we dismiss widely believed history as fiction. The board wants a single rule our writers can apply without a classics or theology degree. Please draft the memo: a clear recommendation with a deadline for rolling it out, criteria that genuinely discriminate among the three cases (show where each lands and why), a rule for what our confidence indicator should display when the honest answer is that we do not know, and a fair statement of the strongest argument against your recommendation, including the fact that one of the complaining districts is a significant share of our revenue. Write it so that engineers and editors can act on it.
