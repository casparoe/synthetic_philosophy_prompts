- our metric (repairs completed, member satisfaction, monthly cost per unit) gets chosen by the board -- i.e. by us -- and we also run the policy the market is predicting. the thing being measured and the thing doing the measuring are the same body.
- it's a one-off decision. no repeat trials, so no track record, so i can never check whether the market was right. someone suggested a second market on "was the first market's call actually good, in hindsight" and i honestly can't tell if that fixes anything or just moves the problem up a level.

- a member with strong views can trade to push the price, and if the market is binding then moving the price is how you win, so manipulation becomes the rational play. if it's only advisory the manipulation is cheap but pointless.
- the metric is one number but the policy helps some members and hurts others, badly and unevenly.
