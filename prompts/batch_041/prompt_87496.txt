The thing I keep chewing on is that the pod people are meant to be us with the lights off -- same body, same memories, same personality, just nobody home -- which is basically the philosophical zombie idea. But the film always gives them away somehow (the coldness, the way they turn on anyone who's still themselves, the fact that people can just tell something's off about their own husband), and it kind of has to, because a zombie you could spot wouldn't be a zombie. So is the film cheating, or is it showing that the thought experiment can't actually be carried out? Because when I try to imagine a zombie copy of my mate I never manage it, I always end up imagining someone who acts a bit off, which is a different bloke, not a copy. Someone told me that anything that behaved exactly like us would have to have thoughts about its own thoughts, so it'd be a person after all, which sounds like it sinks the whole argument that zombies are conceivable and therefore possible. Am I getting that right, or is the film getting at something the philosophers miss? And which scenes should I bring up when we argue about it afterwards?
