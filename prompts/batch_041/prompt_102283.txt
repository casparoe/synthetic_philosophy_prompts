I want three specific probes, because I think they're where the translation either earns its keep or breaks.

First: can my vocabulary even state the difference between epiphenomenalism and the view that the phenomenal state just is the physical state that does the causal work? Second: the self-stultification worry. If the phenomenal state is causally inert, then something else must produce the utterance that one is having a red experience, and it's unclear how that utterance could be about the phenomenal state rather than about its inert correlate. Put in my terms: can a monitor with no causal connection to a variable be a monitor of that variable, or is it just a second instrument measuring the correlate and mislabeled?
