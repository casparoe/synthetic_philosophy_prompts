Act as a philosopher of language and semanticist with a specialism in derogatory language — someone who works comfortably across formal semantics, pragmatics, and the social and legal questions that slurs raise, and who has supervised student research in this area. What I want: an argued prioritisation of the open questions in the semantics and pragmatics of slurs.
