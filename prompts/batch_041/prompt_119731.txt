Dan's line: he's a physicalist too, but he thinks there's a fact of the matter about whether the same subject of experience carries on, and it isn't fixed by patterns, information or causal roles. A chip that plays the part isn't the same bearer of experience, and a perfect copy is a twin, not a continuation. He knows that's hard to state and that Priya will go at him for it.

Neither of them is thick and neither should come out as a strawman, so make them both good.
