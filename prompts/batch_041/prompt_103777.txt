It came up because a friend in the group -- wet-lab person, not a philosopher -- argued that since DNA is basically code and the cell basically runs it, an organism is just a program being executed, so editing a genome is like editing software: change the line, get a predictable change in the output, and the environment is more or less a power supply you plug in. A couple of us think he's smuggling in a ton with that one word but couldn't say precisely what, and he challenged us to spell it out. So: give me the audit. Lay out the correspondence table first -- what maps onto what (codon triplets, the code table itself, transcription and translation, the ribosome, the "stored" sequence, the product) -- then tell me where the mapping genuinely breaks. Things I've been chewing on: there's no single reader or interpreter in the cell, the meaning of a triplet is assigned by the decoding machinery rather than intrinsic to the sequence, the same sequence does different things depending on cellular and developmental context, and a lot of what determines the final organism (folding, timing, environment) isn't in the sequence at all -- but tell me which of these are fatal and which are just "all analogies leak." Some people in the group also push back that DNA really is a code in a strict sense, a mapping relation, so calling it one isn't mere metaphor; I'd like you to separate the sense in which that's literally true from the sense in which the program/blueprint talk is a stretch, and say whether the two get conflated in my friend's argument.

Then the payoff: does his conclusion survive once the disanalogies are on the table -- that edits are predictable and the environment is minor -- or was the analogy carrying the conclusion the whole time? And if "code" can't be rescued for what he wants, give me the least-misleading replacement (recipe, parts list, switchboard, whatever) and say what that one gets right that the code picture gets wrong. I don't need citations or a reading list, half the group won't read them anyway; I need the mapping and your verdict on where it holds. Also if some of the disagreement is really about different meanings of "information" quietly doing different jobs, flag that too, because I think thats half of whats going on and nobody's said it out loud.
