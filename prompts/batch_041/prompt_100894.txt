Ok so a friend of mine, Nadia, runs a small gallery and she has really firm opinions about what is and isn't art, and before I introduce her to my cousin (who makes what I'd call art but who knows) I want to figure out the actual rule she's running. Her track record, as best I can recall:

- a urinal she bought at a flea market, signed, and mounted on her gallery wall: art
- the sunset out her kitchen window: not art
- a very realistic oil painting by a retired dentist, hung in a local art show: art
- a finger painting her five-year-old nephew made, currently on the fridge: not art
- a photo a professional took of a stranger on the street, shown in a gallery: art
- a batch of patterns from a computer program an artist wrote and then exhibited the printouts of: art
- the same program's outputs, run off by some guy who found it online, has no idea what it is, and never shows anyone: not art
- the flower bed a gardener planted in the park: not art
- a weirdly shaped rock on the beach: not art

Now the new case. A woman trained as a painter has spent about thirty years making abstract canvases, clearly intending them as art, working recognisably inside a tradition she studied. But she's a total recluse: she's never shown them to a single person, has no contact with any gallery, critic, or museum, and when she dies the canvases are found stacked in her house. Will Nadia call them art?

What I actually want isn't just yes/no. Tell me: her likely verdict on the new case; the rule or rules that fit every item on the list above (I suspect there's more than one that fits, and that they only come apart here); what each of those rules predicts about the recluse's canvases; which one you'd bet she's really running and why; which of her past cases is the nearest precedent, if any; and the one further case that would actually seperate the candidate rules and settle which one she's got. And if her list genuinely doesn't determine the answer, say so rather than picking for her.

Put your bottom line on the very last line, exactly in this form:
Verdict: <art | not art | underdetermined>
