So someone who knows P is entitled to disregard any future counter-evidence and refuse to revise. Knowledge therefore licenses dogmatism, which no rational person would accept; so the paradox shows knowledge to be impossible -- a rational believer who actually responds to evidence never really knows anything. The only escape is to deny that knowledge is closed under known entailment, which few epistemologists will accept, so the paradox stands."
