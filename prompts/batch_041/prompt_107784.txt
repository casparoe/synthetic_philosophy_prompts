Write a joke that turns on the inversion of reasoning shared by Darwinian selection and Turing-style computation. In the ordinary order of explanation, a creature or a machine does something well because it understands what it is doing; in the inverted order, the competence comes first, produced by a process that understands nothing at all — blind variation with selective retention in the one case, an uncomprehending step-by-step procedure in the other — and comprehension is demoted to a downstream product that the mechanism yielding it cannot presuppose. The humour should come from that reversal, so that the joke is funny to someone who sees why a mindless process can be a better explanation of clever behaviour than a mind, and who notices that the same move which makes a machine's arithmetic uncomprehending is what makes its arithmetic possible at all; a punchline that would still work with the machine swapped out for any other noun is not what I am after. If you can, let the inversion bite on the telling of the joke itself, so that the listener's grasp of the punchline becomes an instance of what is being joked about. Invent the scene and characters as you see fit, and keep it brief.
