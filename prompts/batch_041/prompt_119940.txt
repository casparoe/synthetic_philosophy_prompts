Can you explain a conceptual issue I keep tripping over in the Ubuntu-style stuff on personhood? I get the basic move — you're a person by virtue of your relations to others, personhood is something you do with people rather than something you just have — but I can't tell whether that's meant as a claim about what personhood is, or a claim about what brings it about and sustains it. And those come apart badly in cases like this.

Say someone is convicted of something and their community does the full erasure thing: his name stops being said, nobody will acknowledge him, every relation he had is cut. Or a state makes someone disappear and then denies they existed, and the denial sticks — the people who knew them wont say their name either. If personhood is constituted by relations, it looks like it can be lost, or taken off you, by losing the relations. That's a rough result for a view that's usually sold as warmer and more communal than the Western intrinsic-dignity picture, and it seems to hand personhood over to whoever controls the relations. But if you say the personhood survives the erasure, then it's starting to look like an intrinsic property after all, just one that normally shows up through relations, and the contrast with the Western view gets thin.

So what I want is the conceptual map. What's the real disagreement between reading personhood as constituted by relations and reading it as a status that relations develop or bring out? Is there a third option that dodges both horns — something like: the relations are still real and still binding even when no one is currently acknowledging them, so erasure cuts the recognition without cutting the relation? And does any of this change once you bring in relations to the dead and to people not born yet, which these accounts usually do want to include? Also, how much of the confusion is just sliding between personhood as moral status and personhood in the thicker sense of being a full member of a community — I suspect a lot of it is.

No need for citations, I just want the issue laid out and the pressure points identified.
