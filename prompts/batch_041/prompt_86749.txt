My teacher's summary of Lewis is that a convention is a regularity in a group's behavior that solves a recurring coordination problem: everyone conforms, everyone expects everyone else to conform, and everyone prefers to conform given that the others do, because acting differently while others act alike would leave you worse off. She also drew a distinction I keep coming back to: a convention is held in place by coordination, whereas a norm is held in place by social pressure, so that people comply because deviation would be resented or punished rather than because the situation leaves them no better option.
