I've been reading about the species problem on and off for a few months and I want to write a joke about it that I can actually tell my friend who does ecology fieldwork, without her thinking I've got no idea what I'm talking about. The rule I'm giving myself: the punchline has to turn on something real and slightly technical, not just "haha biologists can't agree on what a species is."

What I want it to turn on is that "same species as" isn't transitive. My made up example, use it or something like it: a mouse that lives in a ring of villages around a mountain, where mice from each village breed happily with teh mice one village over, but the two villages at the far ends of the ring sit right next to each other and won't touch each other at all. So 1 and 2 are the same species, 2 and 3 are, all the way around, and yet the two ends aren't. What I find funny about that is that every rule, form and law we've got assumes species is a clean either/or, so put that assumption in front of some bored official — a customs officer, a pet shop inspector, a wildlife permit clerk, your call — who has to write one word in one box, and let the punchline come out of the fact that there's no fact of the matter here, only a decision about where to cut.

Keep it short enough to say out loud in under a minute, and please don't tack on a little explanation of the joke afterwards, that kills it.
