A couple of friends and I have a dumb ongoing competition where we try to write jokes that are only funny if you already understand the concept they're built on — like, the punchline has to actually turn on the real content of the idea, not just name-drop it or be a generic "nerds will get this" wink. Lately we've been going back and forth about races to the bottom / multipolar traps: the structure where each party is doing something that's individually rational (or at least not insane), everyone would actually prefer the collective outcome to be different, nobody is twirling a mustache, and yet the bad equilibrium just keeps holding because any single party who unilaterally stops defecting gets punished for it. It's the "everyone stands up at the concert" or "each fisherman takes a slightly bigger haul" thing, but at the level of incentives rather than a villain.

So write me a joke in that space. It can be a two-liner, a tiny scene, a dialogue between two people who are both kind of trapped and kind of fine with it, whatever you want — but the punchline should land *because* of how a multipolar trap actually works (the no-villain part, the defection-is-locally-rational part, the nobody-can-fix-it-alone part), not just because the word "Moloch" shows up or because it's a generic "capitalism bad" gag. Bonus points if it captures that weird flavor where the trap is stable precisely because everyone's preferences are pretty reasonable and nobody's asking for anything crazy. And please don't end it on "...and thats why we need a global regulator" — that's the boring, unfunny version. I want something I'd actually laugh at and then feel slightly bad about laughing at.
