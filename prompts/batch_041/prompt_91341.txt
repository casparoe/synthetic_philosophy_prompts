He says the superrational thing is for each of us to privately roll a die with as many sides as there are expected players and only enter on a 1, and that the rest of us discussing our plans beforehand would ruin the entire point of it, and I can already see somebody typing 10^300 into a subject line. He keeps calling it a Platonia dilemma but I'm pretty sure it's the lottery one.

Write me the official rules for this — deadpan, no winking, in the register of a real terms-of-service or a rules sheet we'd pin to the fridge.
