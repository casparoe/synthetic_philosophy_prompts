Ok, I'm hoping this is a "does it have a name" question and not a "you're confused" question.

I keep running into a distinction I don't have a word for. Two of my opinions can be equally likely as far as I can tell -- same answer if you ask me to put a number on it -- and yet one feels solid and the other feels like thin ice. I'd stake very different amounts on them, and if one of them turned out to be wrong I'd be shaken by one and almost relieved by the other. The solid one isn't more probable, its more supported. And what seems to make it solid isn't how likely it is, it's how much is behind it and how varied the support is. If I read three more books that say the same thing I already believed, I don't come away thinking it's any more likely to be true, but it definately feels harder to knock over.

Two cases where the two things come apart. (a) Two doctors both tell me a lump is probably benign, both 80%. One has seen five cases like mine, the other five thousand. Same number, and I trust the second one far more. (b) I ask whether A is more likely than B, and honestly the answer isn't yes, no, or "they're the same" -- it's that with the information I've got, the question doesn't have an answer yet. Related to the first thing, maybe the same thing, I can't tell.

Someone told me this is just ambiguity aversion, but no -- that's about not knowing the odds. Here I know the odds, I just don't know how much they're worth. And "confidence" is useless, because that's just another name for how sure I am, which is exactly what I'm trying to pull this apart from.

So: is there an established name? I'd be shocked if nobody made this cut before -- feels like the kind of thing an economist or a philosopher of science would've fussed over a long time ago. If it does have a name, I want the carving, not the label: what the thing actually is, what the nearest neighbouring ideas are and how this differs from each of them, and which one fits best. If there's no name, coin one and define it properly, boundary cases included. A bare name won't do me any good.

And lead with your bottom line in the first sentence, then the reasoning -- no preamble, don't restate my question.
