What I want is a translation, not an explainer. Restate the whole exchange in the vocabularly I actually think in -- nodes and local state, message passing, gossip/epidemic protocols, consensus, central orchestrators versus local controllers, reconciliation loops, objective functions, online learning under non-stationarity, information-theoretic lower bounds -- as faithfully as you can. Concretely: render Hayek's point that prices let a society economize on knowledge it could never gather in one place as something like a low-bandwidth signaling protocol running over non-IID local state, render Lange's planning board as a tatonnement-flavored iterative solver with a central auctioneer, and render Mises's impossibility claim as some kind of statement -- I'm not sure which kind, that's part of what I'm asking. Then the residue report, which is the part I actually care about. What in this debate has no counterpart in my vocabulary? A few suspects, but correct me if I'm off. So for each of those, tell me whether the residue is a defect of my framework (I'm missing a real distinction) or a sign the original claim was vaguer than it sounds.
