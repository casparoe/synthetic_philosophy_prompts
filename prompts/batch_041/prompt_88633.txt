Second-year PhD in econ, game theory, and there's a pattern in iterated elimination I keep bumping into -- not sure if it has a name or if I'm conflating two seperate things. And if I do the deletions in a different order I can land on a different surviving set, so it isn't cosmetic. Is there a name for the move where an elimination is licensed by a mix of strategies that later get eliminated themselves? Is that the same thing as the order-dependence, or a different thing? And is there a standard distinction between being beaten by a pure alternative and being beaten only by a mix -- and does that line up with the order-independence I'm told strict dominance has but weak dominance doesnt?
