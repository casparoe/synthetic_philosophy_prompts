I've been in a reading group on Molyneux's problem and we've gotten totally stuck. You know the setup: a person blind from birth who can tell a cube from a sphere by touch gets sight, and the question is whether they'd immediately recognize which one is the cube by vision alone. We've gone through the classic debate and some of the modern cases where blind people get sight restored, and the wierd thing is nobody has moved in two months. The empiricist-leaning people say the cases show the newly sighted can't do it, or can only do it after days of learning, so the answer is no. The nativist-leaning people say the cases are confounded: the patients have no visual experience of space, or the tests require naming or matching across senses, or the surgery itself is messy, so the answer is still yes in the sense that matters. Then someone always says the whole question is ill-posed because recognize is doing too much work. Does it mean have the visual experience of a cube as a cube? Be able to sort it with the sphere? Know which one is the cube? Be able to reach for it? Our supervisor suggested the real fault line is whether the newly sighted person has a concept of shape that can be deployed in a new modality or only a recognitional capacity tied to touch, and nobody can tell if that's a real distinction or just relabeling. My working theory is that this isn't an empirical dispute that better data will settle but a dispute about what counts as the content of a perceptual experience, and maybe about what immediate means. Is that right? I definately want a diagnosis, not a literature review: is this a substantive disagreement, a verbal one about the word recognize, two groups answering different questions under one heading, or an empirical question in disguise? Use what each side actually commits to, and tell me what would count as progress -- a cleaner experiment, a distinction, a change of question, or an argument that no progress should be expected. Also, if the impasse is really about which test counts as the test, say which test would actually discriminate the positions.
