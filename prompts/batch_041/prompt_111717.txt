im building a small collection of jokes about the socialist calculation debate and i need one more for it. use this setup, change any details you want but keep the bones: a big company has scrapped budgets and transfer pricing entirely. every division now has an LLM that will, on request, spit out a number for what any input ought to cost, and managers trade with each other on those numbers. one division is printing money, the one next door is bleeding out, and when you ask either of them why, the honest answer is that nobody has ever known less about it.

what i want is a punchline that turns on the actual disagreement. Lange's line was that a central planning board could do the job by setting prices and then nudging them up and down until things cleared — parametric prices, trial and error, done. Hayek's objection wasnt that a board would get the arithmetic wrong, it was that prices are a device for transmitting knowledge that is dispersed, local, and mostly never written down anywhere, so a board that invents the numbers instead of receiving them has nothing to transmit. so: not a generic planners-are-stupid joke, not a generic AI-is-stupid joke. someone who knows the debate should laugh because the joke gets the real split right.

a few lines is plenty. if you can manage two versions, one where the Hayekian point is the punchline and one where Lange gets the last word, even better.
