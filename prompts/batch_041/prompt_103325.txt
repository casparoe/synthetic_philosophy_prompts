My hunch is that the two of us are running together two different things he keeps separate, but I cant tell if the readings actually conflict or if they're just two angles on the same text.

Keep both of us equally sharp — no strawmen, no cheap wins — and don't let either of us lean on which chapter says what, since I care about the arguments, not the citations.
