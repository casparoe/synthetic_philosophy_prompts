I am a doctoral candidate working on the epistemology of online information environments, and I am trying to reconstruct the coding rule my adviser applies to the difficult cases in our corpus. The project sorts communities into three categories — epistemic bubble, echo chamber, or neither — and she has been the final arbiter of the borderline ones. Her rulings so far are as follows.

First, a forum whose members read and cite almost no one but one another and seldom encounter mainstream reporting; when an outsider's argument does reach them they engage with it and often revise; and, as it happens, their factual beliefs about the topic are largely correct. Her ruling: an epistemic bubble, not an echo chamber.

Second, a community that is closed in the informational sense — outside material almost never circulates — and whose members have additionally been brought to regard every outside source as corrupt and to reject outside claims on sight, never revising. Her ruling: an echo chamber.

Third, a community with a broad and varied media diet whose members read mainstream outlets and opposing blogs, do not distrust outsiders as a class, and readily revise in the face of good evidence. Her ruling: neither.

The next community in the queue has a broad and varied media diet — its members routinely encounter mainstream and opposing sources — but its members have nonetheless been brought to regard every outside source as bought and lying, and they dismiss outside claims on sight and never revise.

I would like you to predict how she will code this new community. I am less interested in the label than in the rule she appears to be running: state the candidate rules that fit her three rulings equally well, what each of those rules predicts about the new case, which prediction I should bet on and why, and which further community would best separate the surviving rules. Where a rule is tempting but incompatible with one of her rulings, say so and name the ruling that rules it out. Finally, if the rule that best fits her rulings is not the one I should adopt on independent grounds, tell me which earlier ruling I would have to revisit to bring the scheme into line.
