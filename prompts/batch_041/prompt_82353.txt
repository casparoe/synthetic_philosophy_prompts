ok so i'm in this philosophy club at my school and last week we got into a huge argument about whether dreams are actually experiences, or just stuff we say when we wake up. someone said there's an old philosopher who thinks a dream isn't a thing you experience at all, it's basically just the story you tell afterward, and everyone else thought that was insane. i thought it was insane too but now i'm not sure, so i want to know if there's one principle behind my gut reactions.

Here are my verdicts:

- The nightmare i had last month where i woke up crying and could still feel it in my chest — that was definately an experience. no question.
- The dream i only know i had because i found it in my notes app at 3am and have zero memory of writing it — calling that *my* experience feels wrong, kind of like calling someone else's photo my memory.
- The lucid one where i knew i was dreaming and chose to fly around my neighborhood — that was more of an experience than most of my actual tuesday.
- My little cousin talks in his sleep, full sentences, and he swears he never dreams and never remembers anything. I don't think he's experiencing anything in there.
- A dream i've "had" since i was like five, except i've heard my parents tell the story of it so many times that i honestly can't tell if i remember it or just remember them saying it.
- And the one that really gets me: this morning i woke up and the whole dream was just *there* all at once, like it got downloaded — but it felt like it had been going on for an hour. Then while i was telling my mom about it i noticed i was adding stuff that i'm pretty sure wasn't in it, and now those added bits feel like real memories of the dream.

Is there a principle that gets all of these, or close? What does it say about my cousin, and about the parts i made up while telling the story? And which of my verdicts would i have to give up to keep the rest? Also does any of this change whether you're responsible for what you do in a dream, cause thats the thing the club actually wanted to talk about and we never got there.
