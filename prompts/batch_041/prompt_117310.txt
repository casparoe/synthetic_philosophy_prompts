I'm giving the best man's toast at my identical twin brother's wedding next month, and I'd like you to write the toast. I work as a software engineer and have no philosophy background, but I haven't been able to shake an idea I ran across: that the particular sperm and the particular egg a person comes from is part of what makes that person who they are, so that there is no possible version of them that came from a different pair. Because my brother and I came from the same sperm and the same egg, I can't take the easy route with this. I can't say he is the only person who could have come from that origin, I can't talk about the other pairings as other people who narrowly missed being born, and I can't suggest he nearly was someone else. I would like to close on something the idea does seem to license, that any child he and his partner have could only have come from the two of them. The room will be half our colleagues from work, who will catch it if I state the idea badly, and half family, including our grandmother, who should not have to hear the words sperm and egg at a wedding. Write about four minutes of it, warm rather than clever, and leave aside whether the idea is even true -- I need the toast, not the adjudication.
