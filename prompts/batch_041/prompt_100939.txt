Don't explain either paradox to me, I can state them both: for each of a thousand tickets it's near-certain that that ticket loses, so I seem entitled to believe it of each one, and yet I can't believe it of all of them at once since I know one of them wins; and an author believes each of the many things she asserts in a long book and also believes, with good reason, that she got at least one of them wrong.
