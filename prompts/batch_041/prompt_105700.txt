ok so for a philosophy-adjacent humor zine I need a fake local-news piece, Onion style, headline plus about 450-500 words, deadpan the whole way through.

The bit: a county has passed an ordinance requiring every entity that "makes decisions affecting the public" to register and file an impact statement, and the county inspector has now served papers on natural selection itself, for roughly four billion years of unpermitted design decisions. 

The jokes have to come from actually getting the free-floating-rationale idea right (the Dennett thing — reasons can be genuinely real without belonging to any reasoner). So: the inspector keeps asking who signed off on the finch's beak and there's literally no one to point to; the county lawyer argues a rationale isn't the kind of thing that can hold a permit; a plaintiff's expert carefully distinguishes the reason why the beak is that shape from any reason the bird itself has; someone notes that the entire ecosystem has been running on reasons that were never represented anywhere, so the paperwork is impossible in principle. Twist at the end: the county grants the permit retroactively to "the process," which must now name an agent of record, and everyone is quietly relieved when a mid-level bureaucrat volunteers to sign — because a free-floating rationale that gets signed for suddenly becomes someones fault.

Don't let anyone break character to explain the concept, no citations, no real philosopher names in the piece. A reader who knows the idea should laugh, a reader who doesn't should still follow it. Keep it dumb and local-news-flat, with maybe a quote or two from a "concerned" county commissioner. ~450-500 words.
