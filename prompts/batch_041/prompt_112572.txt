For the past year I've been reporting on how researchers think about the outer limit of careful reasoning, and I've come around to a view I now want to stress-test. The view: the right idealization of thinking something through is a factored computation — a hierarchy in which one deliberator hands narrowly specified subtasks to others who see only what they need to see, the results are assembled at the top, and the tree is allowed to grow as large as the question demands. On this picture, "what a careful thinker concludes" just is what such a hierarchy settles on, and the hard questions about reasoning become questions about how to carve a task into pieces and how to check a piece's answer without redoing the work. I find it clarifying, and I suspect it does real work outside AI: it is the tacit ideal behind expert panels, peer review, and the division of intellectual labor generally. What I want from you is a map of its exposure. What would count against it?

I'm after more than the usual complaint that decomposition loses something. Which tasks would be genuine counterexamples, and why — because the subtasks cannot be specified without already knowing the answer, because correctness is a global property of the whole (whether an argument is cogent, a proof elegant, a translation faithful), or because verification turns out not to be cheaper than generation? Would any of those show the idealization false, or only expensive? The view seems to predict that any competent deliberator can be replaced without loss by a large enough hierarchy of less-informed ones; what observation or formal result would embarrass that prediction? Then give the rivals the same treatment — the expected-utility maximizer, the reflective-equilibrium balancer, the adversarial-debate picture: what does the factored view capture that they miss, and what would I have to see to conclude it is one heuristic among several rather than the correct reconstruction of careful thought? Rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, say which ones the view's own defenders should already concede, and tell me honestly whether it has been stated so loosely — "whatever the hierarchy converges to" — that nothing could count against it, and if so whether that is a defect or simply what an idealization is.
