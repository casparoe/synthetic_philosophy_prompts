(1) advance directives should bind, including when the patient now, incompetently, is refusing the very thing they asked to be spared. the man who wrote 'no tube, no way' while he was sharp, and who now sits there cheerfully eating pudding — I still think the tube shouldn't go in.
(2) the reason it shouldn't is autonomy: his life should be run by his own self-determination, not by what a doctor or a judge reckons is good for him.
