Build it from the logical space first rather than from a list of names: start with the questions on which the views actually split, then say which combinations of answers are occupied, and briefly diagnose the ones nobody occupies (incoherent, unattractive, or just unexplored?). The leaves I need covered: generations as real causal kinds with thier own character; cohorts as arbitrary cut points on a continuous age distribution with no joints to find; generations as shared formative problem-spaces rather than shared traits; the performative/looping view where the label makes the kind real because people believe it; the deflationary "this is astrology with better PR" line; and the age-period-cohort skeptic who thinks everything credited to generations is really an age or period effect.
