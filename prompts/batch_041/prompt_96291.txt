He's an econ grad student, which made the whole thing worse, and his line was the standard one: diminishing marginal utility saves it, Bernoulli showed back in the 1700s that if you value money logarithmically the series converges and the game is only worth a few bucks, case closed, and anyone who's still confused is just confused about infinite series. My suspicion is that this "fix" is fragile — it only works because the payoffs double each round, and if you let them grow faster I bet the log trick falls apart too while some other fixes keep working. He says that's impossible, because a bounded utility function always gives a finite answer. I dont think that actually gets him what he wants, but I couldn't articulate why on the spot.
