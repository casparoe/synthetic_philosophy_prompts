I am writing a comparative chapter on why two regions with similar soils, transport costs, legal systems and access to credit ended up with markedly different patterns of investment, and I need to decide how much explanatory weight an appeal to each region's values can carry. This is the old problem of whether values can be causes; I want a comparative verdict rather than a survey. I have five styles of value-explanation in play, set out below. Please rank them strictly, from the style that can legitimately be credited with the most of the divergence to the style that can be credited with the least, treating the material and institutional background as given in each case. By 'credited' I mean that the value-term does causal work that would be missing if it were deleted, rather than restating the outcome in other words.

(a) The values are attested independently of the outcome, in sources written before the divergence and with no stake in it — sermons, household manuals, consistory minutes — and the behaviour they single out is unusual and specific: the moralists of the first region condemn display and praise unglamorous reinvestment, and its account books show that pattern; the moralists of the second praise martial display, and its accounts show that instead.

(b) The values are read off the very behaviour they are meant to explain: the only evidence that the first region prized thrift is that it saved, and the explanation then says that it saved because it prized thrift.

(c) The values move nobody; they work by retention. Households and firms that happen to fit the values are copied and survive, those that do not are penalised, and the divergence is produced by differential survival and imitation rather than by anyone's motives. The imitation itself is inferred from the divergence rather than documented.

(d) The values are the residual. Material and institutional factors are held constant by the design of the comparison, and whatever difference remains is attributed to culture; the value-term names the leftover rather than a mechanism.

(e) The values produce no practice, but fix which of several materially equivalent arrangements can be justified, and therefore reproduced without continuous coercion: the arrangement is caused by material factors, while the values explain its stability and why it is described in the language of duty rather than of gain.

Give me a numbered list of exactly five items, one per style, in rank order, each item one or two sentences: name the style, state its position, and give the single consideration that fixes it there; where two styles are close, say what would separate them. Strict order, no ties, no preamble, no closing summary, no citations.
