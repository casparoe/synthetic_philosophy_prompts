Retired insect physiologist here — forty years on wasps and beetles, and about four years now trying to teach myself philosophy, so I apoligise in advance if this is a wheel somebody has already invented.

Here's the thing I never had a word for. A solitary wasp drags a paralysed cricket back to her burrow and, before hauling it down, goes in alone to check the burrow is still hers. Move the cricket a few inches while she's underground and she comes back up, drags it to the entrance again, and checks the burrow again. Keep moving it and she'll run the whole loop forty times. It looks for all the world like she's thinking 'somebody might have pinched my hole while I was away', and the checking is definately doing something real — a wasp that skipped it would lose her prey — but there's nobody home doing any thinking. The reason is doing explanatory work with no reasoner attached to it.

Same pattern turned up in two other places I spent time. We built optimality models where you treat the animal as maximising net energy per unit time; they predict well, and nobody believes the beetle is solving a constrained optimisation problem. And you can't write three sentences about kidney function without saying the tubule 'keeps' sodium in range, as if the tissue wanted something.

So: is there a name for this? Not the general do-animals-think question — I mean the specific object, a reason that's really there, that you need in order to explain why the behaviour is the way it is, but that doesn't belong to anybody. I know it's neighbours with the old fight over whether biology is allowed to talk about purposes, and with the 'as if' dodge in economics, but the first is about whether the purpose-talk is legitimate at all and the second is about whether anything has to be maximising, whereas my puzzle is the middle case where the reason is real and load-bearing and unowned.

If it has a name, what is it and who's it from? If it doesn't, coin one and tell me its edges — in particular, is it just a fancy word for a biological function, and does it stretch to things like a market or a legal code, where the reason sits in nobody's head but the institution behaves as though it did? I don't much care about the label, I care where the cuts fall, so please don't just hand me a term.
