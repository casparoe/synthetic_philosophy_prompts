He says this shows intransitive preferences are just broken, like a set of exchange rates that lets some arbitrageur pick your pocket, and that the fix is to have a proper utility function with no cycles in it, and he waves it at anyone who says their preferences are 'incomplete' or 'just don't rank'. Is that a real difference or am I overreading it?
