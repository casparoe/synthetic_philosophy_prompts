As I understand it, alignment work tries to make the model actually want what we want, or at least reliably do the right thing because of the kind of system it is; control work brackets that ambition and asks what safety guarantees you can still purchase when the model is treated as a potentially adversarial component — one that might be lying on evaluations, sandbagging, or steering the very process meant to check it. The people pushing control say the point is that you no longer have to trust the model, only the surrounding apparatus. The skeptics say that's either ordinary defense-in-depth with a new coat of paint or that it secretly still leans on a trustworthy component somewhere in the stack. I genuinely cannot tell which, and my suspicion is that the disagreement is more about vocabularies than substance — which is exactly why I want to run it through mine.
