I run growth for a small subscription app and a stats question has turned into a philosophy question on me. We A/B tested two onboarding flows. Pooled across all users, flow B has better 30-day retention. But inside every cut we try -- new vs returning, iOS vs Android, free-trial vs paid, heavy vs light prior use -- flow A wins, sometimes by a lot. The wrinkle is that the cuts aren't fixed in advance: flow B nudges people to invite friends, and that's what moves them between usage tiers, so I can't tell wether "heavy user" is a confounder I should be stratifying on or a downstream consequence of the treatment itself. On top of that our headline number is a ratio, retained users over active users, so the top and bottom can each reverse on their own and the two reversals can even cancel each other out. Whichever way I read it I get a different answer about which flow is better and I have to ship something next week. What are the most important implications of all this for how we should actually decide? Give it as exactly five numbered points, a sentence or two each, no intro and no wrap-up.
