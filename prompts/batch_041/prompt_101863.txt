My opening: the whole view seems to smuggle in that my extrapolated self is still *me* in the way that matters. That's not my will with the bugs ironed out, thats a different agent wearing my history. And if the reply is 'well, it's the real you, you'd endorse it' -- fine, the new guy endorses it. Why does his endorsement bind me now any more than some future stranger's would?
