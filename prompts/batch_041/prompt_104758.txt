(5) But I don't think anyone is obligated to give away so much that giving more would cost them as much as it helps others, so the person who hands over half their income is admirable but not duty-bound. (6) And I don't think there's any sharp threshold between the required and the merely good — it's a continuum of cost and benefit, not a line you cross.
