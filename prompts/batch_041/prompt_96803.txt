My money is on something colliding with the fact that report is our only handle on phenomenality, but I want to see exactly where the crash is.

Two experiments our group keeps half-inventing, and I want them folded into the list properly. First: imagine you could decode, straight from cortex, the full content of whatever the workspace is broadcasting at a given moment -- the thing you'd normally be happy to call the content of the person's experience -- except the person denies experiencing anything, can't report it, and this replicates cleanly. Neither is a real experiment as far as I know, I just want to know what each one does to the distinction -- which requirements they stress, which theories they kill, and whether they pull the two notions apart in both directions or only one.
