Hofstadter's careenium: a giant billiard table full of little magnetic balls careening around (he calls them simms, it's cranium plus careen). They bump and clump, and if you squint there are stable higher-level clumps, the simmballs, that seem to track features of the world, and the table's walls get more or less bouncy depending on what's happening outside, so information leaks in. So: table = brain, simms = neurons and action potentials, simmballs = symbols or concepts. Hofstadter's question is who is doing the shoving in there, the simms or the simmballs.

People lean on this to argue that the simmball-level description isn't just a handy redescription: symbols genuinely push the substrate around, so you can't say the neurons do all the causing and the symbols are just along for the ride, therefore intentional-level explanation is real causation, therefore Dennett-style real patterns and compatibilism about free will and the map being part of the territory, all licensed in one go.
