Ok so this might be a dumb question but I've been going down the AI-can-suffer rabbit hole after work and there's a thing I keep noticing that I cant find a word for, and I'm not a philosophy person so maybe the word exists and I just don't know it.

Background: I do RL for a living, boring stuff, recommenders and bidding agents. So when I read these debates I'm reading them with the reward function in mind, and here's what trips me up. I write a reward that gives +1 for some events and -1 for others. The minus sign is my opinion. It means I don't want the system doing that. Nothing needs to feel like anything for the gradient to work. But then in every discussion the minus sign gets quietly promoted -- now the agent "dislikes" that state, the state is "bad for" the agent, the agent is being "punished". And when someone pushes back, the reply is well, isn't that just what pain is? Some events get a minus and you steer away from them. Which is true, and also true of a thermostat, so I can't tell what it bought us.

So as far as I can see there are two readings of the minus sign and I can't separate them from outside the system: (1) it's my preference stored in a number, and the agent is just a thing that got bent toward the plus signs; (2) it tracks something that's genuinely good or bad for the agent itself, the way a burn is bad for me. Same behavior, same learning curves, same everything I can log. And I keep watching people slide between the two mid-argument -- usually the training setup gets called morally horrifying, and the evidence offered is a description of behavior, which only gets you reading (1).

I've seen the word valence thrown around but I think that might be a different question and I don't know what it's supposed to mean exactly. The rat people must have run into this too, right -- "the rat presses the lever for the food" has the same crack running through it.

What I actually want out of an answer isn't a label, its the carving. Are (1) and (2) two readings of one fact or two different facts? Whats the nearest thing that already has a name, and how is my thing different from it? And where does the line fall when the behavior and the setup come apart -- say an agent that hunts down the minus sign because I built it that way. Does anything change there? If nothing fits, coin something and tell me where it breaks.
