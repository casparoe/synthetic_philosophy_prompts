Dennett's ladder of Darwinian, Skinnerian, Popperian, and Gregorian creatures is usually told as a story of progressive liberation from the immediate environment: the Darwinian creature is fixed by selection, the Skinnerian learns by reinforcement, the Popperian runs trials in an inner environment and lets hypotheses die in its stead, and the Gregorian installs mind-tools it inherits from a culture rather than inventing them for itself. I want you to compare two rival accounts of what that last step actually adds, because I suspect the familiar one is doing less work than it appears to. The transmission account says the distinctive feature is cultural inheritance: a large part of a Gregorian creature's cognitive equipment — words, numerals, calendars, norms — is acquired from others, so its competence outruns anything its own head could have produced on its own. The recursion account, which I'll state in my own terms rather than in anyone's received formulation, says the distinctive feature is second-order tool use: tools that can be turned on other tools, so that the toolkit is self-expanding and cognitive reach compounds across generations rather than merely being pooled within one. Compare the two on the cases where they part company: a non-human animal that uses a tool but does not accumulate improvements to it; a child who internalizes a grammar and a numeral system it could not have designed; an agent that executes a culturally transmitted procedure flawlessly but can never revise the procedure itself; and a solitary inventor who reinvents a known tool with no transmission at all. For each case, say which account sorts it more convincingly, what each account captures that the other misses, and whether the recursion account is genuinely a rival to the transmission account or only a special case of it. Close with your own verdict on which account carves the Popperian/Gregorian boundary better, and be explicit about how much confidence that verdict can bear. Throughout, separate what is settled in the literature on these grades of mind from what is genuinely contested among people who work on it; do not present a contested reading as the consensus, and flag explicitly where you are going beyond or against the standard framing.
