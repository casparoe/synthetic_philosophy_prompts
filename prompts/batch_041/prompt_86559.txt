I spend more time than I should in comment sections arguing about whether violence is declining, and I keep hitting a knot I have no handle for. Here is its shape. One person cites a falling per-capita homicide rate and treats the trend as settled. A second grants the rate but notes that the absolute number of war deaths in some recent decades has been as high as ever, or that once you fold in deaths from preventable disease and state neglect the curve flattens or turns up. A third says the whole question is malformed, because violence is not the sort of thing that can have a trend the way temperature does -- what gets counted as violence is itself up for grabs, and the answer moves with the choice. What catches my attention is that these three are not disagreeing about any measurement. They are disagreeing about whether the predicate is the kind of predicate that can bear a trend claim at all, and each keeps hearing the others as making a merely empirical point. And the disquieting part is that the disagreement is not idle: widening or narrowing the category is precisely the move that decides the question, and every widening is individually defensible, so the exchange never terminates.

I have read enough to suspect this is somewhere near the problem of the reference class, and near the thought that some concepts are too porous for the questions we put to them, but neither seems to be quite it, because what I am after is not merely that the extension is fuzzy but that the fuzziness is argumentatively load-bearing -- that the boundary is not a nuisance to be fixed but the very thing in dispute. Is there a name for this? I want more than the label. I want the phenomenon carved: what exactly it is, how it differs from a plain definitional quarrel and from a failure to specify a reference class, whether it is the same as a purported natural kind turning out not to be one, whether it is a feature of violence in particular (because violence is partly evaluative) or of any trend claim about a contested category (poverty, literacy, intelligence), and where its edges are -- in particular, whether there is a fact of the matter about the trend once the category is fixed, or whether fixing the category is itself the contested step. If nothing established fits, coin a term and define it so that it excludes the near misses.
