My half-formed idea: Schopenhauer's aesthetics is often read as making music the exception among the arts, since music is said to objectify the will directly while the other arts objectify the Platonic Ideas or the grades of the will's objectification. But this looks inconsistent with his broader metaphysics: the will is not an object of representation, so how can a representational art like music objectify it directly? I suspect the inconsistency can be turned into a productive question if we separate two senses of will in his system: will as the unknowable thing-in-itself, and will as the phenomenal pattern of striving, satisfaction, and renewed striving that characterizes motivated life. On that reading, music might not disclose the metaphysical will as it is in itself; it might instead model the form of willing without presenting any determinate object of desire.
