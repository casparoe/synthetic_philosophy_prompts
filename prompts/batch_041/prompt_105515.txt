Any trade you actually observe is therefore proof that someone is behaving irrationally, or that the market is not working properly. This is also why markets are called efficient: the price already contains all the information, so there is nothing left to trade on, and a trader who believes she has an edge is simply mistaken about her own rationality."

I would like your help auditing this paragraph before I put anything like it into my character's mouth. Finally, tell me whether the moral my friend draws at the end -- that observed trading implies irrationality or dysfunction -- survives those corrections, or whether it turns out to be a non sequitur once the premises are stated properly.
