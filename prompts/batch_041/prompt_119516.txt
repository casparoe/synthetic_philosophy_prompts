Need a joke for a reading group thats been grinding through the liberty stuff (Berlin's two concepts, Pettit on non-domination, that crowd). Nothing that just puns on the word free, and nothing where the punchline is "haha philosophers disagree" — I want the humor to come out of the actual structure of the views, so that someone who knows the material laughs and someone who doesn't is just confused.

The distinction I want it to turn on: (a) nobody is interfering with you right now versus (b) nobody has you under their thumb, i.e. there's no one who could push you around at will even if they happen to be nice about it at the moment.

Seed if its useful: two people on the same street, living basically identical lives. One of them has a landlord/boss/king with unlimited legal power over her who is a total sweetheart and has never once used it. The other gets ordered around all day by someone she can appeal to, complain about, and vote out. Which one's free?

Bonus if the third idea sneaks in somehow — freedom as being your own master, your so-called real self being the one in charge — without it turning into a list of three definitions. A setup and a punchline is plenty; don't overexplain it.
