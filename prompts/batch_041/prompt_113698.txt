I have been defending the following reading of Turing's remarks on learning machines. The child-machine proposal is not merely an early anticipation of reinforcement learning or a picturesque metaphor for training; it expresses a substantive philosophical and methodological thesis. On this thesis, the central obstacle to machine intelligence is not the construction of an adult-level symbol system but the design of a system that can be educated: one whose initial state is relatively unorganized and whose competences are shaped through something like experience, instruction, reward, and punishment. Education is therefore not a convenient substitute for programming; it is a distinct route to intelligence, and the difference between an educated machine and a programmed machine is a difference in kind, not merely in implementation. I want you to treat this as a position and tell me what would count against it.

What observations, arguments, or theoretical developments should make me abandon or significantly qualify the thesis? I am interested in defeaters that Turing's own defenders would concede, not only objections that critics would raise. Which parts of the thesis are most exposed: the analogy between child and machine, the claim that education can produce genuinely novel competences rather than merely tune parameters, the idea that a relatively unorganized starting point is an advantage rather than a liability, or the assumption that the teacher need not understand the internal workings of the learner? What would it look like for the educational model to collapse into ordinary programming, or for the child-machine analogy to turn out to be merely picturesque? Which empirical results in machine learning or developmental psychology would be decisive, which would be merely awkward, and which would be irrelevant? Finally, is the position in danger of becoming unfalsifiable by absorbing every success and failure into the language of education, and if so, is that a fatal problem or an acceptable feature of a research program? Please rank the defeaters from fatal to merely uncomfortable, and be explicit about what you take to be the strongest case for the position surviving them.
