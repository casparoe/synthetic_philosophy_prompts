my friend Priya is a paralegal, not a philosopher, and she's been piloting vignettes for me while I fix the wording for a project on when people say someone did a thing on purpose. every case below is one where the side effect was foreseen, and nobody in any of them is lying about what they're up to:

- A CEO cuts costs to make his numbers. He knows the cuts will finish off the supplier most of a small town works for. He says he couldn't care less about the town. Did he intentionally put those people out of work? Priya: yes.
- Same story, except the cuts happen to keep the supplier going and the town on its feet, and he still says he couldn't care less about the town. Did he intentionally keep those jobs? Priya: no.
- A woman takes a night job to pay for her mother's care, knowing it will wreck her own back. Did she intentionally wreck her back? Priya: no.
- A nurse skips the second check to save time, knowing that as a result about one patient in a thousand will get the wrong dose. Did she intentionally give someone the wrong dose? Priya: yes. (In this one nobody actually did get the wrong dose, if that matters.)
- And one where the side effect wasn't foreseen at all — she said no, which I expected.

The case I actually have to code up for next week: a council engineer. The council voted to re-route the drainage so a developer can build. She thinks it's wrong, said so at the meeting, got outvoted, and she's the one who has to sign the drawings. Her own modelling shows that as a side effect of the re-routing, about two hundred existing houses go from a one-in-a-hundred flood risk to one in thirty. The report is public and the council accepted the risk on the record. She signs. Did she intentionally raise the flood risk to those houses?

Predict her verdict, tell me which rules fit everything above, say which of those the new case actually splits, give me your best guess and why, and tell me what further case would settle which rule she's really running. I'm less bothered about the prediction than the rules — I keep worrying all my vignettes are the same shape, so tell me if that's made this impossible to call.
