Description A should be coarse-grained, pitched at the level of types: the kind of thing the covering-law model can subsume under a general regularity, so that the subsumption really does satisfy the deductive-nomological requirement, and yet where the narrativist's complaint that 'this explains nothing about this case' feels obviously right.
