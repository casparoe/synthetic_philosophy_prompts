Here's the thing I keep tripping over. "The heart has stopped" seems to mean two different things. Sometimes it means the heart couldn't be got going again even in principle. Those clearly aren't the same thing: a heart that's stopped and abandoned is still, in the second sense, perfectly restartable — someone with the right kit could absolutely start it up again. I think the distinction gets called permanent versus irreversible, I'm not sure. Anyway, that gap seems to be doing a lot of quiet work in arguments about whether someone is dead, and I want a case engineered to sit exactly in it.
