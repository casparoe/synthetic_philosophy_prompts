I have been reading about the meta-problem of consciousness—roughly, the problem of explaining why we believe there is a hard problem of consciousness at all. If we could fully explain that belief, say by showing that our brains are wired to produce introspective reports of an inner life, would that not just prove that consciousness itself is an illusion? In other words, once the meta-problem is solved, does the hard problem not simply disappear? I am not asking whether illusionism (the view that phenomenal consciousness is an illusion) is true; I want to know whether the meta-problem is supposed to have that consequence, or whether I am conflating an explanation of the belief with an explanation of the phenomenon.
