Ok I've been trying to figure out why everyone says the Born rule is the big unsolved problem for many-worlds, becuase honestly it seems like a non-problem to me. When I measure a qubit there are two outcomes, so there are two branches, and the Born rule says 50/50 — which is obviously just what you'd expect if you don't know which branch you'll end up in. And when the outcomes aren't equally likely, say it's 1/3 vs 2/3, then presumably there's just more branches on the likely side, or something. So isn't the Born rule basically the trivial claim that you should be indifferent between branches? Why do people act like deriving it is so hard?
