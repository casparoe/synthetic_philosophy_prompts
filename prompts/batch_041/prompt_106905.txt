The case should feature a proposition that is a hinge for the person in Wittgenstein's sense: they don't doubt it, it isn't a candidate for testing in their practice, it's the sort of thing they measure other things against rather than something they hold on the basis of anything, the way he talks about some propositions being the river bed the rest of the river flows over, or the hinges that have to stay put if the door is going to turn. So I want both features in one subject. The only thing that differs between the two is the role, so if your case has anything else differing, it's broken.

What this is for: I want to use it to argue that hingehood is a matter of a proposition's place in a practice rather than a property of the proposition, so the thing that would count as cheating here is stipulating that the first subject's proposition is a hinge by fiat instead of having it fall out of what the scenario shows her doing.
