Write a self-contained argumentative essay of roughly 1,200 to 1,600 words on a controversy in the design of debate-based oversight for advanced AI systems. Reason from the setup below rather than from the surrounding literature, and do not pad the essay with references; define each technical term before you rely on it.

Setup. Under the debate protocol, two AI systems receive the same question and are asked to argue opposite sides of it. A judge — a weaker system, or a human, who cannot reliably answer the underlying question directly — reads the exchange and rules on which side made the better case. The protocol's promise is that a weak judge can supervise a much stronger model, on the grounds that it should be easier to produce a case that a weak judge can check than to produce a correct answer that the same judge could not check.

The controversy. Grant the optimist's premise that checkable case-production is easier than direct problem-solving. What is disputed is how that premise behaves as capability grows. Two theses frame the dispute.

(A) Audit asymmetry. For any fixed ratio between the resources a debater may spend and the resources a judge may spend, a deceptive case that survives the judge's scrutiny is cheaper to construct than an honest case that survives it. On this view the protocol's advantage is not a structural fact about argument but a temporary gap that closes as the debaters outgrow the judge.

(B) The archival response. Even granting (A), debate remains worth pursuing, because its durable product is not the verdict but the artifact: the transcript, which can be stored, re-audited later by a better judge, and used to train the next generation of judges. Deception that must be written down is deception that has been exposed to a future reader.

Your task. Adjudicate whether (B) is an adequate answer to (A), or whether it merely relocates the difficulty from the judge to the archive. Take a side and defend it. First say what the comparison in (A) is even a comparison of — what currency the costs are being measured in, and whether that currency is one a judge could in principle read. Then reach a verdict, and make it conditional rather than a bare preference: state what must be true of the judge, the debaters, and the audit trail for debate to keep its advantage, and say plainly under what conditions the protocol is a waste of compute. Illustrate the verdict with one scenario in which the protocol succeeds and one in which it fails, anticipate the strongest objection to your conclusion, and answer it. Do not end on a non-committal note.
