I am a doctoral student in economics working on social choice and mechanism design, and I have read Rawls closely but think in the other vocabulary; I want to know how much of his route from the veil of ignorance to the difference principle survives being restated in the terms I actually use. Please translate that argument as faithfully as you can into the vocabulary of type spaces and informational constraints, ex ante versus interim versus ex post evaluation, social welfare functionals and their informational bases, incentive compatibility, the Harsanyi-style argument that a shared prior plus expected-utility maximisation behind the veil yields a utilitarian aggregation, and maximin as a decision rule under uncertainty; do not import the target framework's assumptions and pass them off as translations, and where the original position is functioning as a device of representation rather than as an ordinary hypothetical bargain, say so instead of modelling it as one. Then give me the residue report, with a view on whether what fails to carry over is a defect of the social-choice apparatus or a sign that the original argument is doing less than it appears to. Answer as exactly five numbered items, each of one or two sentences, with no introduction and no conclusion: (1) what the veil of ignorance and the parties' choice become once expressed as a choice over mechanisms under an informational constraint, and what is gained or lost by treating maximin as a decision rule under uncertainty rather than as a constraint on acceptable distributions; (2) what the difference principle becomes as an ordering on an index of primary goods — I have never been sure whether maximin, leximin, or something weaker is the right rendering, so settle that — and what the standard apparatus has to surrender to accommodate a principle with no trade-off below the threshold, especially continuity and representability by a social welfare function; (3) which elements translate only with distortion, in particular the metric of advantage and the preference domain the parties are said not to know, the lexical priority of liberty, and the identification of the least advantaged as a social position rather than a point in a type space; (4) what has no counterpart at all — the publicity condition, the strains of commitment, stability for the right reasons, self-respect, and the parties' moral powers — and whether that is because the apparatus cannot express them or because they are doing rhetorical rather than analytical work; and (5) the verdict, namely whether the parts that did not survive point to something the apparatus is missing or to something in Rawls that is less determinate than it looks, followed by how confident you are in the translation as a whole and the single point you are least sure of. Please write in British English.
