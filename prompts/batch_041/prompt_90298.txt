In the color phi phenomenon, a dot flashes in one spot, then another, and people report seeing it move and change color before the second flash even happens. One story says the experience is edited after the fact, Orwellian style, so the memory is of a smooth motion that never actually occurred; the other says consciousness is simply delayed, Stalinesque style, until the brain has enough information to construct the whole scene. Suppose I add a wrinkle: the second flash is accompanied by a spoken word that the subject only hears at the moment of the second event, and subjects still report hearing the word before the dot arrives. Whats the most important implications of that wrinkle for the debate? Does it favor one story over the other, or does it show the two are empirically indistinguishable, and what does that mean for how much we should trust introspective reports about the timing of experience?
