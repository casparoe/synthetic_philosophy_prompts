Misleading by omission, by presupposition, by evasion, and by answering a different question than the one asked. The placebo, the therapeutic privilege, and the white lie told to spare feelings, where the intent is benign. The boundary between lying and bullshitting, where the speaker is indifferent to the truth rather than aiming at falsehood.
