I am preparing a paper on Marx's treatment of technology and automation, and before I commit to an interpretation I would like an audit of the following five commitments, which I currently hold together and take to be mutually supporting.

(1) The productive forces — technology, instruments, and the organisation of labour — develop according to a logic largely of their own, and their development is the primary motor of historical change: when existing relations of production fetter the further growth of those forces, the relations are eventually transformed.

(2) Technology is nevertheless not politically neutral. The machinery of large-scale industry is designed and deployed under capital to deskill labour, intensify work, and reproduce the wage relation; under a different set of social relations one would expect a different technology to be built and used.

(3) Under capitalism, automation tends to immiserate the worker rather than emancipate her: labour-displacing machinery throws workers out of employment, feeds the reserve army of labour, and depresses wages.

(4) At the same time, Marx's discussion of automation in the Grundrisse notebooks — the section commonly labelled the fragment on machines — treats the tendency of fixed capital to absorb living labour, and the growth of a socially general knowledge embodied in that capital, as the possible threshold of a realm of freedom, in which production is carried on with the worker no longer at its centre.

(5) Value, on Marx's account, is created by living labour; surplus value is the unpaid surplus labour of the worker appropriated by capital.

Please audit this set. Which of the commitments are genuinely in tension with which, and which only appear to be? Rank the tensions by severity, and for each genuine one state what has to give and what it would cost me — in interpretive plausibility and in fidelity to Marx — to give it up. I would also like to know whether any single revision would resolve the most serious tensions at once, and whether the set as it stands can be held together without revision at all.
