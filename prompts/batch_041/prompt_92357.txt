Here's the setup as I understand it, and honestly part of why I'm asking is to check I've got it right — there's this result (Löb's theorem, or the "Löbian obstacle" if you're being fancy) that goes roughly: if a consistent system that can reason about its own proofs ever proves the sentence "if I can prove P, then P is true," then it can go on to prove P itself, for any P at all. I think there's some related point about why a program can't just keep checking its own output and ever be sure it's right, and about why you might *want* an agent to carry a little built-in doubt about its own reliability.
