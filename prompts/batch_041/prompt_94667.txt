You are a philosopher working on Plato's ethics and on the question whether justice is choiceworthy for its own sake. Before you write, brainstorm what a case would need in order to isolate that question from every other consideration; then commit to one design, and show that it meets each constraint below rather than merely asserting that it does.

Construct a thought experiment in which a normal adult commits an injustice that is genuinely tempting and genuinely profitable to her, while (i) her unaccountability depends on no magic, invisibility, gods, or afterlife, and (ii) she pays no internal price — no guilt, no self-reproach, no memory of the act, no fear of exposure, and no change to her character or future dispositions.
