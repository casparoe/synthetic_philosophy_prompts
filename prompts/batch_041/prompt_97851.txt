Hey, so for my aesthetics seminar I have to do a short creative piece and I've decided to do it as a record review. The bit is a critic who's just discovered Schopenhauer's stuff on music and now can't review anything except through that lens. So he grades the instruments by metaphysical rank, treats the bass line as the lowest grade of the will's objectification, thinks the melody is the only part doing real work because it's the highest grade and narrates the will's own story, gets genuinely offended when the bass player takes a solo (thats promoting the bottom of the ladder straight to the top), complains that anything without a clear melody isn't really music, and so on. Can you actually write it? Roughly 600 words, deadpan critic voice the whole way through, no winking at the reader, and he never breaks character. The jokes have to be real Schopenhauer -- music as a copy of the will itself rather than just the Ideas, harmony as the lower grades, melody as the highest, rhythm, the lot -- so someone who did the reading laughs, but the people in my class who skipped it can still follow. Invent a fake band and album so it isn't about anything real, and have him end with a score out of ten thats justified purely by how well the record respects the hierarchy. Don't have him cite anything or name real philosophers, critics don't do that. Thanks!
