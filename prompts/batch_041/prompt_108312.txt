- the cases: a thousand people each nudging a dial by an imperceptible amount, so nobody's contribution registers at all but together they're torturing somebody; the person who eats one more burger when the harm is already locked in; the person whose order is literally the one that makes the farm buy another crate; the voter in a seat that's decided before the polls open; the person who would have been replaced by somebody else if they'd stayed home, so the outcome is identical either way; and the guy who does the harmful thing in a world where everyone else is already doing it and his bit is redundant.
