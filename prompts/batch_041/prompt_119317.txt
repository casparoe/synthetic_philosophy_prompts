Two details I want worked in: there's a small red triangle on the back of the panel, painted on in 1938 as the war loomed, to mark the picture irreplaceable — a museum's little act of metaphysics; and the panel is oddly thick, probably cut from something bigger, so the original is already a fragment. The rest of the book, meanwhile, is a trade in second-hand goods — Hobie's shop, restoring and patching old things, and Theo keeping it afloat by selling fake antiques.
