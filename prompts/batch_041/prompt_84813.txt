Two things I want to build in, becuase I don't think most of them have thought about either. First: the gap between someone who's present-biased and keeps getting blindsided by her own future self — she sincerely expects to be patient tomorrow, and she's genuinely shocked every time she isn't — and someone who knows exactly how she'll behave and will happily pay money now to stop her future self from doing the thing. I think that contrast does real work about what "weakness" even is. Second, and this is the one I actually care about: the worry that the discounting story doesn't explain akrasia so much as dissolve it. If her preferences at 9pm and her preferences at 9am are just different preferences, then maybe there's no single better judgment she's betraying — nothing to be weak about — and calling it weakness is us imposing a unity on her that isn't there. I want the room to feel that as a live threat, not a footnote I wave at on the last slide.
