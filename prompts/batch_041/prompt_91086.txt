My doctoral supervisor spent her career on the later Wittgenstein, and in the years when we were closest I kept a private list of the verdicts she delivered on other people's religion. I am asking about them now because I have gone back to the church I was raised in, and I want to know whether she would say that what I have is belief.

The list runs as follows. A colleague who said he believed in God but would abandon the belief if the arguments for God's existence were refuted: not a believer, she said, but a man carrying a hypothesis around in religious vocabulary. A friend who treated the Resurrection as a historical claim to be settled by the evidence of the tomb and the testimony of the witnesses: a category mistake about his own words, she said — settled that way, the belief would no longer be the thing he took himself to hold. A man who feared that withholding his tithe would bring him misfortune, and who quietly dropped the belief when no misfortune came: superstition, a piece of bad science, not religion. A woman who kept every observance, brought up her children in the faith, and said she could attach no sense to the question of God's existence asked apart from the practice: the paradigm case, she said, and the question asked from outside was what had gone wrong. And a friend of ours who prays, keeps the calendar, and says outright that she does not believe in God and keeps the practice instead: not a believer, my supervisor said, whatever else she may be.

My own position: since my mother died I have taken up the practice again, the year is shaped by it, and I am back among the people I grew up with. But I would be lying if I said that nothing could move me. If the historical case against the Resurrection were made, or if I were made to feel the problem of evil at full strength, I believe I would stop. Would she count me as a believer? Tell me the rule she is running, the other rules that fit her verdicts just as well, which of them I should expect her to apply to me and why, what case about a third party would show which rule she is really using, and whether the rule she is running is Wittgenstein's own or a reading of him that others have disputed.
