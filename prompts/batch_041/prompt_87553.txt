2. Violence shouldn't just mean direct physical assault. It should include structural and institutional harms like preventable disease, poverty, and mass incarceration.
3. There is a fact of the matter about whether violence is going up or down. It isn't just a matter of how you define your terms or which political tribe you belong to.

5. When comparing eras, the right denominator isn't population but the number of possible violent interactions, like potential dyads or person-years at risk, because violence is relational.
6. Moral progress is real and is part of why the decline happened.

My friend says I can't have all of this. I suspect some of it only looks inconsistent. Can you audit it? Which commitments actually pull against each other, which are only apparently in tension, and how severe is each real conflict?
