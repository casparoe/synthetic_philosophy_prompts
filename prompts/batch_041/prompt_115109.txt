You're now in the tails branch and it's asking for the $100. The second case: a blackmailer has dirt on you and flips a fair coin; he'll release the dirt if and only if the coin is heads XOR you pay him $100 — so if the coin is tails and you pay, he releases it, and if the coin is heads and you don't pay, he releases it. He sends the demand either way, and you're deciding whether to pay without knowing the coin. Our group splits cleanly: some of us pay in the first case and refuse in the second, others refuse in both, one person pays in both and says the rest of us are just squeamish about blackmail, and nobody's arguments have moved anyone in weeks.
