Ludwig Wittgenstein died in 1951, two years before the structure of DNA was discovered and more than a decade before the genetic code was deciphered. Suppose he had lived to read the molecular biology that followed: the finding that stretches of DNA are transcribed and translated, that triplets of bases correspond to amino acids according to a table that is nearly universal across life yet appears chemically underdetermined, and that working biologists speak without embarrassment of DNA as carrying information and of a genetic code. What would he have made of that vocabulary?

Reconstruct the position he would most plausibly have taken, reasoning from his actual commitments rather than from a vague Wittgensteinian mood — the later identification of meaning with use, the plurality of language-games, the private-language argument, the rule-following considerations, and his suspicion of pictures that hold us captive. Would he treat the genetic code as a literal description, a harmless metaphor, or a category mistake, and what would he ask in order to decide? The codon table is arbitrary roughly as a lexicon is arbitrary — its assignments are conventional rather than dictated by chemistry. Does that arbitrariness support the analogy to a language, or does it cut against it, given that no community fixed the convention and none consults it? Biologists sometimes separate the information of Shannon's theory, which is indifferent to meaning, from semantic or intentional information; would Wittgenstein say the word is being used in a new technical game, or that it smuggles back an old picture of meaning — something lodged inside the cell, or inside a head — that his arguments were meant to dissolve? And does it matter that no mind reads the code: is a code without a decoder still a code?

Close by saying where molecular biology has introduced a genuinely new language-game that he would have had to accept, and where he would have insisted it is a picture that misleads. Flag the points at which the reconstruction becomes speculative and your confidence drops.
