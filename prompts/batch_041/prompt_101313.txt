Consider a thesis about the frame problem — call it the Residual Core thesis. The frame problem is standardly split into a technical part and a philosophical part. The technical part concerns how a reasoner can avoid having to state, for every action, all the facts that the action leaves unchanged, the non-effects. The philosophical part concerns how any finite agent can determine what is relevant to a task at all, given that relevance seems to depend on an open-ended background of facts and goals. The Residual Core thesis asserts that the technical part is, or will be, absorbed by probabilistic and learned systems that handle non-effects by default, but that the philosophical part is not thereby solved, because any such system manages relevance only by having its representational vocabulary — its ontology of features, predicates, or dimensions — fixed in advance, by evolution, by a training distribution, by a designer, or by stipulation, and no finite agent can derive its own ontology from unlabeled experience in a way guaranteed to track the relevance structure of an arbitrary environment. On this thesis, the frame problem survives every technical advance, not as a defect of some particular formalism but as a standing fact about finite agency.

Give me your credence that the Residual Core thesis is true. Decompose it, with a rough probability and one line of justification for each term:

(a) that the technical and philosophical parts are genuinely distinct, rather than two descriptions of a single underlying problem;
(b) that learned and probabilistic systems solve the technical part rather than merely relocating it into the training distribution;
(c) that ontology-fixing is ineliminable — that no plausible bootstrapping or unsupervised structure-learning procedure can let an agent derive a relevance-tracking vocabulary from experience alone;
(d) that, conditional on (c), the residual is a substantive limitation rather than a near-truism about any bounded agent;
(e) any further sub-question you believe the thesis depends on, which you should name and price.

Then combine the terms into a single number, say which term the final figure is most sensitive to, and state both what evidence or argument would move it most and what would count as settling the thesis one way or the other.
