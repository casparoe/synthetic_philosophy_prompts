I'm a novelist, about 60k words into a book that keeps stalling because I can't referee a fight between two groups I've been tagging along with for research. Call them the Spray-Is-a-Massacre people (entomologists and animal-welfare folks who think insect sentience is likely enough to matter, and that agricultural insecticides are one of the biggest moral catastrophes going) and the Feed-People-First people (growers, an agronomist, a couple of philosophers who say pest control is what stands between us and famine, that insect sentience is deeply uncertain, and that the number that matters isn't how many bugs the spray kills but the difference in suffering in that field across the whole season).

They've been at it for a year and its driving me up the wall, because mostly they talk past each other. I want the document they should have written instead: an adverserial-collaboration joint statement.

Give me: (1) a numbered list of claims both sides could sign, starting with the one you think is least controversial and building toward the ones that are nearly disagreements; (2) a table of the real disagreements, each stated twice — once in wording the Massacre side would accept as its own position, once in wording the Feed-People-First side would accept — no strawmen in either column; (3) the cruxes, the sub-questions where settling the thing would actually move one of them; (4) for each crux, a case, argument, or observation both sides would agree bears on it, with each side's prediction written down in advance. And tell me which parts of the fight are just verbal — I suspect at least one is.

Some things I keep hearing and want in there: acute kills vs sublethal effects (behaviour, reproduction, navigation); the individual insect vs the colony or population as the unit of concern; whether spraying can actually increase total insect suffering later, through rebound outbreaks and dead predators; whether background or natural mortality makes our added deaths less significant; and what to do under deep uncertainty about whether insects feel anything at all.

No citations, no reading list — I'm not going to footnote a novel, and I don't want invented ones either.

Keep it under 800 words, and lead with the single claim you'd bet both camps sign without blinking.
