I'm in a reading group on philosophy of mind, and we've just finished two sessions on Dennett's attack on the Cartesian theater. I think I follow the negative part of the story: the picture of an inner place where all the information comes together and is displayed to a single witness is supposed to be rejected, and the findings about how experience is spread out in time -- the phi phenomenon, the color phi phenomenon, the way the brain seems to revise its own records of what happened -- are meant to dissolve the intuition that there is a definite moment at which some content becomes conscious. What I can't get clear on is the positive alternative. The multiple drafts model is said to replace the single finish line with many parallel processes of content fixation, yet when Dennett writes about contents achieving fame in the brain, or about the brain's probes being able to interrogate only what has already been retained, it sounds to me as though the theater has simply been moved and given a new name. So what I want explained is the conceptual issue itself: in exactly what sense is the multiple drafts model supposed to be incompatible with a Cartesian theater, rather than being a Cartesian theater with the seats rearranged?

I'd like the explanation to take up three specific things. First, the distinction Dennett draws between an Orwellian and a Stalinesque account of a case like color phi, and why he holds that there is no fact of the matter that could decide between them -- I want to see why that denial of a fact is supposed to fall out of the model itself rather than being stipulated in advance. Second, the standard objection that the notion of fame in the brain reintroduces a finish line, together with the strongest reply the model can give to it. Third, whether the model's refusal to privilege any one time or place of consciousness can be stated without quietly relying on a distinction between a content's merely being present in the brain and its being conscious; if it cannot, I worry the theater is back under a new description.

Please be concrete about the color phi case where you can, say plainly where the model is doing explanatory work and where it is doing stipulative work, and flag any place where I may be importing an assumption that the view is meant to reject. Write in American English.
