ok so i need a satirical piece where the jokes come from getting the philosophy right, not from the premise being zany. Here's the setup: a boutique wealth manager (call it Conscience Capital, or invent your own name) has launched what it markets as the first fund that hedges across moral theories instead of asset classes. You tell them how plausible you find utilitarianism vs a Kantian duty ethics vs a virtue thing vs contractualism, they weight your contributions accordingly, and you get a blended moral portfolio you can rebalance. I want the client-facing FAQ page for this fund: numbered Q&As, deadpan, max 550 words.

The whole joke has to be that the business model forces positions on the real cruxes, and the FAQ should just take those positions in the flat register of a fund document, never once admitting its doing anything interesting. So it needs to run into at least: whether you can trade off between theories at all (are they even measuring the same thing, and where does the exchange rate come from), whether a weighted blend of two theories is itself a theory anyone could hold or is a position nobody occupies, what the fund does when a theory it rates as very unlikely says the stakes are infinite and it needs some kind of cap or collar, theories that wont be traded off at all because they rank some goods lexically, and the fact that clients are also uncertain how uncertain to be, which somehow has to get billed.

No winking, no character breaks, no asides about how abstract this all is. An ethics grad student should laugh because it nails the cruxes; my brother in sales should read it straight and believe it's real for about ninety seconds. Don't name any actual living philosophers. Finish with the fund's one-line tagline.
