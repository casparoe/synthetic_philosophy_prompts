Right, decision problem for you, and I'd rather have the answer than the name of it — I'm fairly sure it's got a name and that I've read about it on some blog, but the name isn't the bit I need.

I have to pick which night to go to a mate's leaving do, Friday or Saturday. My old flatmate Nadia will be at whichever one I pick. She's not following me about — she's just uncannily good at working out what I'm going to do, and then she goes to that one. So Friday, she's there. Saturday, she's there. Staying in doesn't help either, she'd guess that and come round mine. And the last time I kept a plan entirely to myself she still turned up, so that trick's dead.

Tom, who's read far more of the Sequences than is good for him, says just flip a coin, which sounds less like deciding and more like giving up. The daft bit is that whenever I settle on one night, the other one starts looking better, which I assume is my brain being an idiot.

Is there actually a best option here, or do I pick one and stop going on about it? And if it is the coin, what's it actually buying me — she could definately guess that too, surely?
