my friend and i just burned like three hours arguing about whether a photo his 8 year old took of the cat counts as art, and by hour two we werent even talking about the cat anymore, we were arguing about what art is at all. so i want a tiebreaker and i want it as a ranked list.

here are the definitions we came up with between us, some are ones i half remember from a course and some my friend made up on the spot:

1. something is art if the art world (critics, curators, dealers, people who know the tradition) is willing to take it seriously as a candidate for appreciation.
2. something is art if it transmits feeling: the maker felt something and the work makes the audience feel it too.
3. something is art if its lines, colours and shapes are moving just as arrangements, independent of whatever they depict.
4. something is art if it was made with the intention that it be treated as art, and that intention is intelligible against the background of earlier art.
5. (friend's invention) something is art if it was made to be interpreted rather than used.
6. (friend's invention) something is art if a museum would accept it and keep it.
7. (my invention) something is art if a competent viewer cant exhaust it, you keep coming back and finding more there.
8. art isnt a definition kind of thing at all, its a cluster of overlapping features and any search for necessary and sufficient conditions is doomed.
9. (we added this one after a beer) art is a costly signal, a peacocks tail: it exists because making something useless and hard is a way of advertising the quality of the maker.

the hard cases we kept hitting: the cat photo; a rock on a beach that happens to look exactly like a Brancusi; a kids drawing that is genuinely good; a really good tv advert; a birds song; something an AI spat out in ten seconds; a forgery nobody can tell from the original; a landscape nobody made at all.

what i want: rank 1 through 9 from most to least trustworthy as a working definition, score each out of 10, one sentence on why it lands where it does, and for each one name the hard case that sinks it or say that none does. also flag which of them are secretly the same definition wearing a different hat, and which ones can be held together without contradiction. dont hedge, if you think 8 is right just say so and rank the rest as doomed.
