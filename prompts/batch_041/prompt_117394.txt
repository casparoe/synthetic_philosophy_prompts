I'm a backend engineer with no philospohy training and I keep running into something I don't have a word for. Five years ago my team picked a message queue because it was the easiest thing to run that week. Nobody decided anything. Now the whole company is shaped like that queue: teams split by topic, on-call follows the partitions, the people who get promoted are the ones who can tune it. If I say the queue decided how we're organized, everyone says no, the queue is neutral, it just carries messages, humans chose to orginize this way. And I can't really argue, because every step was a normal engineering call that any sane person would have made. But if we'd picked a different queue we'd have a different company, and nobody ever chose that company. Smaller version of the same thing: we made one code path about ten times faster than the other, and now everything is built around the fast path, the slow path is basically dead, and nobody remembers there was ever a choice.

Is there a name for this? I don't mean that tools are neutral, or that technology changes society in some vague way, and I don't mean the case where somebody designs a thing to keep a group out, because here nobody designed anything. I mean the case where a pile of individually fine decisions adds up to an arrangement nobody picked and nobody can point at, because at no step was there a decision. If there's a term, what is it and what does it leave out? If there isn't, what's the nearest thing, what would you call this, and where would you put the edges?
