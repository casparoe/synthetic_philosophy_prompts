Entitlement, of course, just means what people are owed. So famines are political crimes and not natural disasters: they happen when a government decides some people don't count. That's why Sen could say no democracy with a free press has ever had a famine, and it's why the real cause of the Great Leap Forward deaths was Mao, full stop, and of the Irish famine was London's indifference. The weather is basically irrelevant -- entitlement is everything."
