On the second picture, consent is an act or a communication: it's something you do that changes what other people are permitted to do, so an inward yes that never gets communicated isn't consent at all, and an outward yes you privately regret might still count as real consent in the relevant sense.

And competence -- is that a property of the person, or of the particular decision, or of the decision relative to how risky it is?
