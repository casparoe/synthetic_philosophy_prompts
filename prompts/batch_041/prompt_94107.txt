Ok, I'm trying to get my own head straight on church-and-state stuff before I write anything longer, and I want a consistency check first. Here's roughly where I've landed:

(1) The state has to be neutral — between religions, and between religion and no beleif at all. No established church, no privileging one faith over another or over unbelief. That's a real requirement, not just good manners.
(2) So the Church of England should be disestablished — the 26 bishops who sit in the House of Lords by right, all of it. In practice it's pretty harmless, but it's still a standing legal privilege for one denomination and it should go.
(3) Germany's church tax (the Kirchensteuer) is fine, though. Nobody's forced to pay it — you can formally leave the church and the liability stops — so it's really voluntary, and having the state revenue office do the collecting is just bookkeeping convenience. I don't see how that violates neutrality.
(4) France basically has the right idea with laïcité: public schools should be free of conspicuous religious symbols. In public institutions the state should be neutral in the strong sense — not hosting religion at all, not merely tolerating it even-handedly.
(5) But I also think religious minorities should get exemptions from generally applicable laws when a rule really burdens their practice. Neutrality isn't supposed to be hostility, and sometimes treating everyone by the same rule is the opposite of treating them fairly.
(6) And the Alsace-Moselle situation in France drives me up a wall — the Concordat is still in force out there, the state pays the clergy, there's religious instruction in the public schools. France preaches seperation and then keeps this carve-out in the east. It should be brought into line.

So: do these actually hang together, or am I quietly helping myself to two different ideas of what "neutrality" means depending on which case I'm looking at? Be specific about which pairs are genuinely in tension, which ones only look like it and dissolve once you actually look, and rank the real tensions by how bad they are. For each real one, tell me what has to give and what it would cost me to give it. Don't just list everything as equally problematic — I want to know which fault line is load-bearing.
