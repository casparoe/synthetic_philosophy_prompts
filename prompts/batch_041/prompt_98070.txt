Fine, that part's familiar. My addition is this: the court chooses which way to aggregate by taking a majority vote of its own members. So the question for your essay is whether that meta-vote actually rescues the group from ending up inconsistent, or just relocates the problem one level up, or maybe collapses into something worse. I'd like you to pick a side and defend it, not sit on the fence — say what you think happens and why. Don't bother with citations or a reading list, I want the actual argument and the mechanics of it. A few hundred words is fine, though I'd rather you get definately into the weeds than stay at the level of vibes.
