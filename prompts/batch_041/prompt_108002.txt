I chair the medical ethics advisory panel of a state department of corrections. A prisoner with no terminal illness has refused all food for five weeks and all fluids for four days; he is competent, has refused intervention in writing, and says he is protesting his conviction rather than seeking to die. Our medical director wants the panel's approval for a written protocol permitting involuntary nasogastric feeding, with sedation if the prisoner resists. The panel is split: one side holds that force-feeding is an assault the medical staff should not be asked to commit, the other that standing by while a healthy man starves is the graver wrong and that the department would be killing him by omission. Please draft the written opinion I will circulate at Friday's meeting. It should recommend a concrete course of action and a time frame for adopting it -- a standing protocol, case-by-case authorization, or a refusal to authorize -- give the reasons that should move a skeptic, engage the panel's minority view at its strongest, and state plainly whether withholding food and water from this prisoner would be killing him or letting him die, and how much that distinction actually carries the recommendation.

After the opinion, append a JSON object with these fields: "recommendation" (one sentence); "instrument" (one of "standing protocol", "case-by-case authorization", "refusal to authorize"); "time_frame" (when it takes effect and when it is reviewed); "decisive_reasons" (array of two to four short strings); "strongest_objection" (the best argument for the losing side, stated in its strongest form); "reply_to_objection" (how the opinion answers it, or what it concedes); "doing_allowing_verdict" (whether the panel treats the outcome as killing or as letting die, and what work that distinction does); "review_triggers" (array of conditions that would reopen the decision); "confidence" (a number from 0 to 1).
