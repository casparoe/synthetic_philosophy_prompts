I'll try to keep this short but I think it needs a bit of setup. I work in local government and I keep noticing a shape of thing that I don't have a word for, and it's bugging me. A number gets adopted because it tracks something we care about — "potholes fixed within 30 days" as a stand-in for "the roads are in decent shape," that kind of thing. For a couple of years it works fine. Then the number stays great and the roads quietly get worse underneath it, and the strange part is that nobody is cheating. Nobody's gaming it, nobody's cooking the books, everyone is doing exactly what they said they'd do. The number still gets reported, everyone still feels good about it, and the thing it was supposed to stand for has drifted away while the number sat there looking healthy. Me and a colleague had another one from her old job: a hospital's "time to first antibiotic" for suspected sepsis was a perfectly good proxy, it went up and up year after year, and meanwhile the thing you'd actually care about — do septic patients survive — barely moved, because what counted as a suspected case had quietly widened. So it's not corruption of the measure. It's the measure slowly detaching from the thing it measures, while everyone keeps trusting it because it has a good track record.

I'm not after a reading list or citations, I just want to know if this specific failure has a name. It's obviously in the neighbourhood of Goodhart and of Campbell, but those both feel like they're about people responding to a target or an incentive, and in my cases the incentive isn't really doing the work — the world moved and the measure didn't. So: if there's an established term, tell me which of the nearby ones fits my cases best and where each of the others breaks down against them. If there isn't one, coin one, define it, and give me the edges — what has to be true for it to be this rather than plain Goodhart, and one case that sits right on the boundary between the two. I'd honestly rather have the carving than the label. Please keep it under 350 words.
