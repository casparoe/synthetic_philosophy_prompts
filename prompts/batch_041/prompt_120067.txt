Okay, so this is something I keep bumping into and I don't have a word for it. My nan signed one of those forms about eight years ago, back when she was all there, saying that if she ever went the way her sister went, the dementia, then no feeding tube, no antibiotics, no hospital, just let her go. She has it now. She doesn't know my name or what day it is, and honestly she's happy as anything, she eats her pudding, she watches the birds, she'll tell you a long story about a dog she never owned. And the family is at war over that bit of paper, because the woman who signed it is gone and the woman it's about never agreed to a thing. What gets me is that we do this in one direction and not the other. Nobody thinks the cheerful one sitting in the chair right now gets to cancel the plans of the one who was thinking straight. We all just act like the earlier one has the authority, and I've never heard anyone say why that isn't just the loudest ghost in the room winning.

Is there a name for the general shape of it, not just the form? I mean the thing where one version of a person is treated as the real one and gets to overrule the other version, even though if you sat them both down they'd disagree, and even though the later one isn't obviously worse off. I tried googling and all I get is lawyer pages about living wills, which is the paperwork, not the idea. My brother in law keeps saying "Ulysses contract" at dinner, but that seems to be about a person who still wants the same things and just knows they'll go weak, and here the later one wants diffrent things entirely. And "precommitment" seems way too wide, my dad precommits every night by putting the biscuits on top of the fridge.

So: what's the nearest actual thing, and where does it stop fitting? And if there's no name for the exact pattern then make one up and tell me where you'd draw the lines. Does it matter that she's content? Does it matter that she can't remember signing, or that she can't follow the reasons she had? Would it change anything if she'd signed it as a joke, or if the earlier her could see her now and would take it back? I'm not really after the word, I want to know what the thing actually is and what it isn't.
