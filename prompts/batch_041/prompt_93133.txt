Here's the setup as I put it on the board. Judy gets dropped into a region that's divided two ways at once: Red Army territory vs Blue Army territory, and (cross-cutting that) the Second Company area vs the Headquarters area. So what she's actually learned is a conditional — given Red, Second Company is 3/4 likely. She hasn't learned that she's in Red, and she hasn't learned anything outright about Blue.

The textbook move is to treat that as a constraint and pick the posterior that satisfies it while staying as close as possible to her prior — minimum relative entropy, that whole family of rules. And you get the result that makes everyone squirm: her credence that she's not in Red territory goes up, past 1/2. Learning something about the internal composition of Red territory makes her more confident she isn't in Red territory at all.
