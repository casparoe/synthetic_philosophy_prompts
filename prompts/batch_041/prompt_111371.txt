Two job offers, and after months of spreadsheets and pros-and-cons lists I've concluded there genuinely equal: same pay, same kind of city, both interesting, both bosses seemed normal on the calls. One of them expires Friday, and if I let it lapse I'm stuck in my current job, which is clearly worse than either. So I have to pick something, and I can't find a reason to.

My friend says I'm confusing two things. Choosing needs a reason, picking doesn't. She says flip a coin, that's what coins are for. So I tried the thing where you assign the sides, flip, and then check how you feel about the result -- if there's a pang, that's your answer. I felt basically nothing, which should be reassuring and somehow isn't. Part of why it isn't: the tie doesn't feel stable to me. Whichever one I take I'll throw myself into it, make friends, get good at it, and in two years it'll obviously have been the right one. So they're equal now only because I haven't acted yet. Which makes me think the coin isn't just settling a tie between equals -- it's producing the inequality, and then I'd spend years living inside a difference I made with a coin. And if it goes badly I'd have nothing to point to.

Is my friend right that I should just flip and stop agonizing? Or is there something real in the hesitation that she's flattening?
