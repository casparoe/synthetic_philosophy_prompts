I'm taking a seminar on the Vienna Circle, and for a term paper I'm trying to work out what Otto Neurath would have made of two things that only appeared long after his death: the emoji inventory as a supposedly universal pictographic language, and text-to-image models that turn a written prompt into a picture.

What I have from the seminar is Neurath as the one who wanted a picture language to sit inside the same physicalist, anti-metaphysical programme as the rest of unified science — pictures were to be a notation answerable to data, not ornament. Two features of his method stuck with me. First, the pipeline: the raw statistics went to a "transformer" whose job was to recast them into a form a draughtsman could then draw, so that the finished picture stayed accountable to the numbers at every step. Second, the fact that his system was a taught convention rather than a self-evident picture — he treated it as a language one had to learn, which is part of why he insisted on visual education and drew a hard line between instructing people and advertising at them.

Now the two cases. (1) Emoji: a fixed, standards-governed inventory of pictographs used worldwide, but with no grammar, with meanings that drift across platforms and cultures, and with the inventory controlled by a committee rather than derived from any data. Popular writing calls it the first genuinely global language. (2) Generative image models: you type a sentence and receive a picture, with no transformer, no underlying data, and no accountable path from the sentence to the pixels.

I'd like you to predict, as carefully as the evidence allows, what Neurath's position would have been on each case, and on whether either counts as a continuation of his picture-language project or as its counterfeit. Please separate what the texts and the scholarship actually settle about his commitments from what is genuinely contested — for instance, how far he really thought pictures could function as a full language rather than as an auxiliary to prose — and don't present a contested reading as the consensus. Where you are extrapolating beyond anything he could have addressed, flag it and indicate how confident you are.
