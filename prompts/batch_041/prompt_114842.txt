Pascal's mugging (you probably know it, but just so we're on the same page): a stranger walks up to you and says he's secretly super powerful, and if you give him your wallet he'll save like a huge number of people or give you a gigantic reward, and if you don't he'll do something horrible — and the catch is you can't rule out that he's telling the truth, so expected value seems to say you should just hand over the wallet. I'm also proposing that the fix of "use infinitesimal probabilities instead of zero" doesn't work either.
