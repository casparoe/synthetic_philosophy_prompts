I am a junior in a seminar on self-reference and paradox, and there is an idea from the course that I have been unable to reconstruct in a way that makes sense to me. The instructor introduced it with Escher's Drawing Hands, the lithograph in which two hands rise out of a sheet of paper and each appears to be drawing the other, and the point was that neither hand can be the ultimate source of the other, so an account of the picture in terms of who drew whom cannot bottom out within the picture. What is supposed to stop the regress, the instructor said, is the inviolate level: the paper, the artist, or more generally the level at which the rules governing the whole arrangement are fixed, a level that is not itself one of the things being drawn. I thought I followed this until I tried to state it precisely, and now I suspect that the account I have reconstructed is incoherent. If the inviolate level is what does the drawing, if it is what makes the two hands and their mutual drawing possible, then it is doing precisely what the hands do, namely drawing, and so it looks like one more element of the same arrangement. The original difficulty then simply reappears one step up. Either the inviolate level draws itself, in which case we have the same loop again, or something else draws it, in which case the regress has not been stopped but only postponed. The only remaining option seems to be to declare the inviolate level exempt from the question, which looks like stipulation rather than explanation, and I cannot see why the exemption would attach to that level rather than to the hands themselves or to some level above it. The same worry seems to arise for a story about a novelist who writes a story about a novelist: is the real novelist, who is not a character in anything, merely one more novelist in the chain? So I want to ask whether the inviolate level has to be drawn in order to do any drawing, and if it does not, what the principled reason is that the regress terminates at that level rather than one level up or one level down. I assume the error is mine and that it has something to do with what counts as a level, but I cannot locate the mistake, and I would like to understand what the inviolate level is supposed to be if it is not simply one more hand in the chain.
