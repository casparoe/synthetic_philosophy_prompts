Hi — I'm recently retired from about 34 years of bench chemistry (mostly kinetics, a bit of protein work), and I've spent alot of the last two years reading philosophy of mind, which started as a hobby and has turned into a mild obsession. Before I inflict my views on my reading group I want to pressure-test them, because I have a nagging feeling they don't all pull in the same direction and I honestly can't see where the problem is. Here's roughly where I've landed:

1. I'm a physicalist. Whatever is going on when I smell coffee has to be the physical/functional story and nothing extra — no spooky added ingredient on top.

2. On method I've become convinced by heterophenomenology: when we study consciousness scientifically we should take people's (and animals', and machines') reports as data — as texts to be interpreted — and stay neutral on whether the subject's own beliefs about their experience are actually true. Build the theory that best explains the reports.

3. Still, I think there's a plain fact of the matter about what an experience is like. That there is something it is like to be a creature seems to me a real feature of the world, not just a way we happen to talk.

4. I also trust my own introspection in a way I don't trust anyone else's report about me. When I'm in pain I know it directly, and no third-person scan of my brain could overrule that.

5. And I think the hard problem is real — no amount of mechanism, however complete, seems to tell us why any of it is accompanied by experience.

6. Finally, I think a philosophical zombie is conceivable: I can imagine a physical duplicate of me with all my behavior and no inner life. But I don't think it's actually possible.

So: is this a coherent package? I'd like you to tell me which of these are genuinly in tension and how bad each one is — rank them rather than listing them all as equally bad — and which ones only look like they clash but actually don't. For the real clashes, what's the cheapest repair and what does it cost me? And if a single change would clean up the most, tell me which one. I'm not attached to any particular item here; I just can't tell which of them is doing the damage.
