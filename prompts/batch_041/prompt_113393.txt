Next month I want to do the thing about aesthetic testimony, because I stumbled into it online and it genuinely messed with my head. The setup is this: if a friend I trust tells me the museum is closed on Mondays, or that Lisbon is the capital of Portugal, I can just come to know that — their say-so is a perfectly good route to me believing something true. It feels like the belief only gets earned if I actually watch the film myself. And yet we all do this constantly — we watch things because a review said they were good, we pick restaurants off other people's recommendations — so I'm honestly not sure the asymmetry is real or just an artifact of how we talk about taste. A friend of mine who did a philosophy degree said there's a name for the idea that you have to have experienced the thing yourself for the judgment to be yours, but I've forgotten it and I'd rather not stand up there misattributing things.
