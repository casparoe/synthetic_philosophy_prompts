He'll push back on the idea that a single exotic solution can undermine the realtiy of time.

Write the dialogue. Both characters should be equally competent—no strawmen. They should respond to each other's actual points, not talk past each other. Let them go back and forth for maybe 8-10 exchanges. They can get into the weeds: closed timelike curves, the lack of a global time function, the cosmological constant, whether the solution is physically realistic, the difference between local and global time, the connection to Kant and idealism, the question of whether the absence of a universal now means time is an illusion.
