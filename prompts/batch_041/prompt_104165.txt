My slot came down as Plato on drunkenness, which I grabbed because I'd never heard a single person say a word about it and it looked pleasingly strange.

Don't explain the view to me — I've read the Laws and I think I've got it. What I need is a plan for how to explain it to that room. The strange idea, as I understand it, is that in the Laws the old Athenian argues you should let people drink at parties on purpose, because wine is a kind of drug that drops your guard, and a party run properly (sober man in charge, rules, the right amount) works like a sort of gymnasium where you get to practice holding yourself back — and the deeper point underneath it is that you can't test or train a virtue without putting the person in front of the thing that tempts them, same as you can't test courage without danger. I might have the emphasis wrong, so push back if I do.

What I want from you:
