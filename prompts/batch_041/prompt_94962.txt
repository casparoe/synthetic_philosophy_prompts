My friend Dan has been reading about Kripke and he came at me with this whole thing last week and now I can't stop thinking about it. His version goes roughly like this: Kripke showed that you couldn't possibly have come from a different sperm and egg than the ones that actually made you. The reasoning, as Dan tells it, is that identity is necessary -- if a thing is identical with something, it's necessarily identical with it -- so the actual fertilized egg that turned into you couldn't have been a different fertilized egg, and anyone who came from a different egg and sperm would just be some other person, a sibling at best. Dan says that means your origin is part of your essence, the same way being H2O is part of water's essence, and since the whole thing comes out of a principle everyone already accepts, it's basically a proof, and anyone who says "well I could have come from a different sperm, there were millions of them" is just confused about what the word "you" means. He also said this is why personal identity is really about where you came from rather than your memories or personality -- so someone who gets amnesia and becomes a completely different person isn't the same person anymore. And he said the deep upshot is that it was guaranteed all along that I would exist, since my origin was locked in before I was born, so in a way my parents never had a choice and neither did I.

Some of this lands for me. The bit about the woman who gave birth to me not being able to be some other woman really does feel true when I sit with it. But "I couldn't have come from a different sperm" seems obviously false -- there were millions of them, and whichever one got there first is what made me.
