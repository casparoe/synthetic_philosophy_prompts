Me and five mates from uni put £600 each into a shared pot on one of those trading apps — its just for fun, none of us are investing rent money or anything. The way it works is we all have the login on our own phones, so any one of us can place a trade whenever we want, using the whole pot. We sort of agreed we'd only trade when something looked like a really good idea, but we never actually said who gets to decide, and I'm starting to think thats the whole problem.

Here's what I keep going round in circles on. When I look at the market and think "yeah, this one's decent," my first instinct is that one of the other five will probably spot it too and pull the trigger, so theres not much point me doing it. Plus if we all pile in at once we'd end up betting four or five times what we meant to and could get cleaned out. So I sit on my hands. But then sometimes nobody trades at all and we miss it. And when we do trade, I've noticed its basically always the same guy — Dave, who's the most confident about everything and also, if I'm honest, the worst at it. It's like the trades we actually end up making are the ones Dave is most sure about, not the ones that are genuinely best. Last month he dumped half the pot into something because he was "certain" and we lost most of it.

A mate says the fix is obvious, just text the group before anyone trades. But half of us are in different time zones, the chat is muted, and by the time everyone's replied the moment's gone. Dave says he should just back himself and trade whenever he thinks its good, because if everyone thought like me nobody would ever do anything and we'd never make a penny. Someone else said we should just stop overthinking it and accept that sometimes we win sometimes we lose.

Right now theres a trade I think is decent but not amazing. Should I do it? More importantly, is there something wrong with how I'm thinking about this in general, and is there actually a better rule we could adopt given that we can't really coordinate in real time? I don't want to be the guy who never does anything, but I also dont want us to keep getting cleaned out by whoever happens to be feeling boldest that day.
