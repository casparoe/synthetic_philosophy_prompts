Make both characters equally competent, and let them press each other's actual points rather than talk past each other. Have them test their positions on at least one concrete, worked example rather than only in the abstract — for instance, a case in which a dishonest model hides a flaw inside a long derivation and offers a clean-sounding summary of it.
