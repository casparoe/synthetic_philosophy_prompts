By the knowability principle it's possible for someone to know the conjunction: p, and nobody knows p. But knowledge is factive — if you know something then it's true — and if you know a conjunction you know each conjunct. So the knower in that possible situation knows p, and also knows that nobody knows p, which by factivity means nobody knows p. So they know p and they don't know p. Contradiction; hence no truth is unknown; hence every truth is known, which is absurd.
