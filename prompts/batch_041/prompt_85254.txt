Before I read any further proposals about what to do if the basic axioms of mathematics turn out to be inconsistent, I want the standard they should be measured against. Write the desiderata: what any adequate account of how mathematics should be held, criticized, and repaired in the face of that possibility must deliver.

Fix the purpose first, since adequacy is relative to it: the account has to be good enough for a working mathematician deciding whether to keep proving theorems in the system they use, and for a community deciding what to build its formal libraries and its teaching on.

The account must handle two situations that pull against each other. In the first, a machine-checked derivation of a contradiction is verified against the axioms as they are actually written down, in a system that is weaker than the standard foundation rather than an exotic extension of it, so the comfortable reply that the trouble lives only in the strong axioms is unavailable; the derivation is long but surveyable in principle, and no one agrees about which axiom, definition, or rule of inference is at fault, with each candidate repair preserving a different part of mathematics. In the second, no contradiction is ever found and perhaps none ever could be, because the shortest derivation of one might exceed any computation we could run — and every foundational system we use is already in that position.

For each requirement, give the case or consideration behind it and say whether it is non-negotiable or a preference of one camp. Then the part I most want: which requirements cannot be jointly satisfied, whether because they conflict with one another or because a result such as Gödel's second incompleteness theorem — a consistent system of the relevant strength cannot certify its own consistency — forecloses the combination. For each unsatisfiable pair, say which requirement you would give up and what that costs. Finally, name the single requirement that excludes the most otherwise attractive accounts. I do not need the familiar positions scored; I need the standard they should be measured against.
