Alright, I'm trying to get my head straight on logical uncertainty and I want to check whether the package I've talked myself into actually hangs together, because I've got a suspicion I'm cheating somewhere and I can't see where.

Here's where I've landed:

1. Rational degrees of belief are probabilities, and you update them by conditionalization. Non-negotiable for me.
2. Logical uncertainty is a real phenomenon. I can rationally be unsure whether Goldbach's conjecture is true, or whether some big 400-digit number I can't factor is prime, and that uncertainty isn't a defect in me — it's just the human (and machine) condition.
3. The core of rationality is that nobody can make a sure profit off you. A rational agent can't be Dutch booked — and I mean that in the logical sense too: a bookie who can see all the logical facts, including the facts about what my own beliefs commit me to, shouldn't be able to drain me.
4. I should trust my own reasoning. If I'm confident that after a week of careful, valid inference I'll be certain that P, then I should already now be at least as confident in P as I expect to be then. Being at war with my future self seems incoherent.
5. The fully ideal Bayesian who assigns 1 to every theorem and 0 to every contradiction is a fiction. Nice fiction, but not a standard any actual agent can meet, and I don't think "you fall short of omniscience" is a fair criticism of a real agent.
6. Coherence is still the right regulative ideal — I should be as close to a probability distribution as I can be — I just don't think I ever get all the way there.
7. And a related one: I think an agent can rationally be unsure what it's about to do or say next, even if it can read its own source code. A program can have a genuine credence strictly between 0 and 1 about its own next line of output.

So: which of these actually pull against each other, and how bad is each pull? I don't want a flat list where everything's equally suspect — rank the tensions, tell me which pairs only look bad and dissolve once you look closer, and for the ones that are real, give me the cheapest patch and what that patch costs me. And if something has to go, tell me which single commitment to give up to clean up the most at once.
