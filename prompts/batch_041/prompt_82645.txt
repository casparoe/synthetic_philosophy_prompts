ok I'm putting together a position statement on pain for a seminar and I want a consistency check before I hand it round, because I have a feeling something in here is doing illicit work and I can't see where.

Here's what I beleive, roughly:

1. Pain is bad because of how it feels, not because of what it leads to. Strip off every downstream consequence and a pain is still bad. Its badness is intrinsic to the experience.
2. That badness isn't a seperate layer sitting on top of a neutral sensation. To feel pain just is (in part) to be in a state that's bad for you. I don't think you can subtract the badness and be left with pain — the badness is constitutive of the state, not an add-on.
3. Pain asymbolia is real and those patients are genuinely in pain. They report that the stimulus hurts, they can localize and describe it, they withdraw from it and pull faces, sometimes they laugh while doing so — but it doesn't bother them, it isn't awful, they don't want it to stop. (Stroke damage round the insula or parietal operculum as I recall, but don't hold me to the anatomy.) It's not stoicism and it's not a reporting deficit.
4. The badness of a pain just is its demand for relief. The awfulness isn't a cool fact you notice and then decide whether to act on; feeling it is already being pushed to make it stop. A state with no inclination whatsoever toward relief wouldn't be pain.
5. Pain is bad for the one who has it. What gives me reason to help you is that it's your pain.
6. Pleasure is good in the same intrinsic way, and pain and pleasure are the basic currency of welfare — you're better off the more of one and the less of the other.
7. I don't think asymbolia is a lucky condition. I wouldn't swap into it, and I don't think those patients have gained anything by losing the awfulness — if anything something's been taken away.

So: which of these actually conflict, which only look like they do, and how bad is the damage in each real case, ranked? For each real conflict, whats the cheapest fix, and what does that fix cost me somewhere else in the list? Don't just tell me they're all in tension — I'm fairly sure at least one pair only looks bad because I've phrased it clumsily. If something has to go, say which, and tell me what the package looks like afterwards, including what it ends up saying about a masochist who goes out of his way to get hurt.
