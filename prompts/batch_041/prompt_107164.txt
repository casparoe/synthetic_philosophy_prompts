I am a retired experimental physicist who has lately taken up philosophy, and I keep running into a tension in the norms of my old profession. Yet the same scientific culture, when it addresses the public or a regulator, treats the consensus of the field as an authority that it would be irrational for anyone, layperson or expert, to reject; and in medicine a clinician is now expected to follow a guideline panel's verdict even when her own reading of the primary studies points the other way.
