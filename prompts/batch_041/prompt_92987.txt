Write the desiderata an adequate theory of non-empirical confirmation in physics must meet. The theory should say how a framework such as string theory or an inflationary multiverse can be rationally accepted when its distinctive predictions are empirically inaccessible, without reducing confirmation to pursuitworthiness, aesthetic taste, or unfalsifiable metaphysics. For each requirement, give the case or consideration that motivates it—the string landscape, naturalness arguments about the electroweak hierarchy, the cosmological constant, historical cases where non-empirical virtues favored a theory later abandoned, and the possibility that incompatible theories share the same virtues—and mark which requirements are non-negotiable and which are preferences of particular camps. Include a requirement about what happens when a non-empirically confirmed theory is later empirically disconfirmed, and one about whether non-empirical confirmation can be comparative rather than absolute. Finally, flag any pair or subset of requirements that cannot be jointly satisfied, and say what that means for the debate.
