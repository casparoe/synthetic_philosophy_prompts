I'm more on Dennett's side — dreams are experiences, but the reports we give on waking are massively confabulated, so the report isn't a faithful record of what was experienced; the experience happened, we just reconstruct it badly afterward (the cassette picture is one way of cashing that out, though I'm not wedded to it). We keep talking past each other, and half the time I think we're using the word experience in two different senses and the other half I think there's a real disagreement hiding under there.
