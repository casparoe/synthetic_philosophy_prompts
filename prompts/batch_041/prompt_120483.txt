Do the translation honestly — don't smuggle my framework's assumptions in and call it a translation. Then the residue report: what carries over cleanly, what gets distorted on the way in, and what has no counterpart in my vocabulary at all. And finish with your view on the residue — is it my toolkit that's missing something (does it have anywhere to put people imagining themselves into a community?), or is the original thesis vaguer than it sounded?
