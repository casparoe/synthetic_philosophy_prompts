So here's what I'm after: a "which theory of why pain is bad do you actually hold?" quiz I can pass around to friends. Then a scoring key that maps the answer patterns onto the real positions — the one where the badness just is the sensation, the one where it's the unpleasantness or affect that's separable from it, the "it's bad because we want it gone" desire-ish view, the view that it's really about the interruption and the attention-grabbing, and any "it isn't bad in itself at all, only through its consequences" view.
