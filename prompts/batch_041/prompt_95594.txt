I'm a hobbyist coder and I've got a puzzle I cant find a name for, and its been bugging me for months.

Here's the setup. I built a little sandbox: a flat box with a few thousand tiny magnetic marbles bouncing around in it. At the bottom level it's dead simple. Every marble is only ever pushed or pulled by the marbles near it, nothing else, no exceptions. But if you squint, clumps form, and the clumps have behaviour. They grow, split, get dragged across the box, merge, fall apart. And the clumps are the only level that's any use to me. Tracking marbles tells me nothing. Tracking clumps tells me what's about to happen. If I want to change what happens, the only lever I've got is at the clump level -- tilt the box, put a big magnet at one end, and the clumps move and the marbles come along with them. I even wired it so the bounciness of the walls is set by something outside the box, and certain clumps make a little arm twitch. So the clump level is where the box's information lives and where its behaviour happens. And yet nothing extra is in there. Every marble is moved by other marbles, full stop. So when I say a clump dragged a marble across the box, have I said anything true about what did the dragging? Or am I just standing far enough away that I've started calling the summary a cause?

I keep meeting the same shape. In Conway's Life a glider collides with a block and the block is gone, but no cell ever notices a glider. And someone told me there's a version of this in a book about strange loops -- a giant pinball table full of magnetic marbles, and a question at the end about who's really shoving whom around in there. I don't remember what the author decided, and I don't think that's the name of the problem anyway, that's just where someone posed it.

So does it have a name? People keep telling me it's emergence, or that it's just reductionism, and both feel way too broad: one seems to be about what you can predict, the other about how you explain, and what's bugging me is about causing. And I don't care about brains here -- there's no mind in the box, just marbles. What I want is the carving: the actual family of named positions on whether the pattern level is doing any pushing, which of them are really distinct from each other, where exactly they disagree, and which one fits the marble box. If the honest answer is that there's no one name because it's several debates wearing the same coat, then say which ones, and say which of them the box actually needs.
