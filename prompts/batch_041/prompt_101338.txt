My cousin has gone full conspiracy guy — moon landing, flat earth, the works — and his whole thing is "you can't KNOW anything, you just believe whatever you're told." I tried arguing him out of it and kept catching myself being inconsistent, so I started jotting down cases where I'm sure someone knows vs. doesn't, going purely on gut. Here's what I've got:

- Grandma watches her grandson come home looking healthy. But the family's rule is that if anything bad ever happened to him they'd hide it and act cheerful anyway. I say she doesn't know he's okay.

- Same grandma, different thing: she knows she's got two hands in front of her. She also knows that if she's got hands, she's not a brain in a vat being fed fake signals. But I don't think she knows she's not a brain in a vat.

- Now the chair. She knows there's a chair in front of her, and she knows that means there's a physical object in front of her, and here I DO think she knows there's a physical object. Which seems to clash with the brain-in-a-vat one and I can't say why.

- My buddy steps onto a glass bridge rated for exactly his weight, believes it'll hold, and crosses; it holds. In any world where it wouldn't hold he never gets to believe anything, he's just dead. I still feel like he knows it'll hold. Something about that bugs me.

- Two coworkers both believe the boss is about to quit, and he is. One of them saw the resignation letter on the printer; the other just has a hunch he trusts. The first knows, the second doesn't, even though the belief and the outcome are identical.

Is there one principle that gets all of these, or close? I keep wanting to say "you know if it's true and you wouldn't have believed it if it were false," but then I'd have to say grandma doesn't know she has hands either, which I don't buy, and the bridge case comes out as knowledge for a reason that feels like cheating. If there's a principle, what does it say about a case I haven't listed — do I know my car's still parked outside when I can't see it but I'd definately notice if it were gone? And which of my gut calls would I have to give up to keep the rest?
