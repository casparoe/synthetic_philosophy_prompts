- make him pin down what he means by legitamacy, because I swear he slides between "people think it's legit" and "it really is legit" and those are completely different, and only the second one is even a moral claim. - how do you actually measure "performance," and who picks the scoreboard? If he says GDP growth or low crime, push on which years he's allowed to pick and whether growth from a really low starting point counts the same as growth from a high one.

- Singapore is basically one small city, so can you really hold it up as a model for huge messy countries? Make him answer without just saying "context matters."
- and give me one closing question that flips it on him: ask him to name a case where performance was great but he'd still call the regime illegitimate -- I want to see if performance is really doing the work or if he's sneaking in something else.
