For some years I have kept an intermittent record of what I enjoy, in the hope of learning what I actually like. It has left me with a puzzle I cannot name.

Three entries from the same month. One: eating a peach at the exact moment it was ripe. Two: the moment, after three days of failure, when a proof finally closed. Three: sitting with a friend through a bad night and feeling, underneath the fatigue, something I can only call pleasure at being the person she called.

I cannot find what these share. The peach is a sensation with a location in the mouth. The proof is the release of a tension I had been carrying somewhere behind my eyes. The third is a warmth that is also, awkwardly, pride, and also shame about the pride. Nothing common. Yet a single word covers all three — and, more tellingly, I use them in a single way. Asked how to spend a Saturday, I can say which of the three I would rather have. My rankings among such things are stable across years and transitive. I can order them without being able to say on what basis.

What I most want a word for is where the unity lies. It seems prospective rather than retrospective. I cannot inspect a candidate and see that it belongs with the others; I discover that it does by wanting it. That reverses the order I would have expected. If pleasure is what makes a thing worth wanting, then the wanting should be explained by the pleasure; here the wanting is the only thing that tells me the pleasure is present. With a family-resemblance term I can at least point to overlapping features without wanting the thing. Here I cannot.

Does this pattern have an established name? Three things interest me. First, is this simply the familiar complaint that pleasures are too various to share a nature, or is the point about the direction of dependence a distinct one, with a name of its own? Second, is there a standard term for a kind whose members are united only by their relation to a single faculty, and is 'functional kind' that term — or is 'functional kind' reserved for cases where the role can be specified without reference to anyone's attitudes? Third, if the unity is only of this thin sort, does anything of the hedonist's claim survive, or does 'pleasure' become a label for whatever happens to sit at the top of a ranking that could in principle have been produced by anything at all?

Give me the nearest established concepts, say precisely how far each reaches and where it stops fitting, and if nothing fits, coin a term with a definition and two cases at the edges.

Answer in no more than 600 words, and open with the bottom line: whether the pattern has an established name, and if so what it is.
