I argue too much with my relatives and I've got a pattern I can't find a word for.

Here's the shape. Two people disagree, and each one has an argument that's airtight *provided* you start where they start. The two arguments are mirror images -- run one backwards and you basicly get the other. So the whole thing reduces to which of two claims gets treated as the one you don't have to argue for. And the punchline: each side accuses the other of assuming the very thing that's in dispute, and both accusations are correct, which somehow makes the accusation useless as a move.

Two real ones. (1) My sister and I remember a deal differently. I say it was 50/50 because I remember saying 50/50; she says it was 60/40 because she remembers 60/40 and she's the one who wrote it on the napkin. I tell her she only remembers it that way because she wants the bigger share. She says the identical thing back to me. Neither of us can back up our memory without leaning on the memory. (2) At work, two people disagree about whether a study is any good: one says the study's method is sloppy so the finding is probably wrong, the other says the finding is too implausible for the study to be sound. Each one's premise is the other ones conclusion.

My wife says this is just people being stubborn, but I don't think so, because it has a shape -- you can always rewrite the fight as a fight about which of the two claims is the baseline, and at that point arguing genuinely stops.

I half-remember an old argument where someone answers the skeptic by pointing at his own hand and treating the hand as more certain than anything the skeptic says. Is that the same thing I'm describing, or is that a one-sided version of it? What I've got is two-sided, and I can't tell whether that makes it a different phenomenon.

So: does it have a name? And I don't mean just a label -- I want to know exactly what the thing is. Where are the edges? Is it the same thing when one side actually does have better evidence but can't say why? Is it the same when it's one person alone, torn, with nobody to argue against? And is there a point where this stops being a special phenomenon and just becomes ordinary disagreement about facts?
