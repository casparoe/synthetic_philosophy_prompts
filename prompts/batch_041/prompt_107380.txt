I am working on a safety layer for a reinforcement-learning agent that is supposed to pursue a narrow objective while leaving the rest of the world as undisturbed as possible, and my colleague and I keep circling the same problem: we want a formal definition of side-effect avoidance, or low impact, that does not presuppose a full specification of value. We can specify the agent's goal, and we can specify a few hard constraints, but we cannot rank all states of the world. I would like you to write the desiderata that any such definition must meet. Treat this as a specification exercise: what must a measure of side effects be able to deliver before we should trust it to guide an agent? Include the cases it must get right, the distinctions it must not collapse, the results from decision theory or causal modeling it must be consistent with, and the failure modes it must avoid. For each requirement, give the case or consideration that motivates it, and mark which requirements you take to be non-negotiable and which are merely preferences of a particular approach. I am especially interested in any pair or subset of requirements that cannot be jointly satisfied; if the list itself proves that no value-agnostic definition can do everything we want, say so and explain which requirement has to give.

Then, as a stress test, apply the list to a few concrete puzzles. Suppose the agent's action prevents a harm that would otherwise have occurred; is that a side effect, a benefit, or neither, and does the answer depend on whether the harm was part of the baseline? Suppose the agent's action leaves the world's state almost unchanged but drastically alters which future actions are available to other agents; should that count as high impact, and if so, how do we measure it without knowing what those other agents value? Suppose two side effects occur, one making some agents better off and one making others worse off, with no common scale; can they offset, and what does your answer imply for the possibility of a value-agnostic measure? Finally, say which single requirement does the most work in ruling out the most popular families of measures, such as baseline-relative measures, causal-difference measures, and measures that try to preserve the options available to others. I do not need citations or a literature review; I need the criteria and your judgment about whether the central ambition is coherent.
