I've been reading alot about what shouldn't be for sale, and I keep flip-flopping on where I actually land, so I want to know if there's a real principle behind my gut or if I'm just making it up case by case. Here's where I come out:

- A hardware store triples the price of generators after a hurricane: fine, and honestly good — it gets generators to the people who need them.
- A scalper resells concert tickets at five times face value: fine, who cares.
- Paying a woman in a poor country to carry a pregnancy for me: I'd ban it. The money is doing something there that it shouldn't.
- Paying a screened, well-paid adult for a kidney, with rules that keep desperate people out: I'd allow it.
- Selling your vote for $500: wrong, should stay illegal, even though both sides agree and I can't point to anyone who gets hurt.
- Paying a kid $100 for every A on their report card: bothers me, even though it probably works. And I think the reason is different from the others — it's not that an A is too sacred to buy, it's that the payment seems to wreck the very thing that was supposed to be motivating the kid.
- Hiring someone to cry at your mother's funeral: fine, actually.
- A company pays a couple to name their baby after a product: wrong — the kid isn't a billboard.

The only principle I can come up with is "fine if both people agree and nobody else gets hurt," but that makes vote selling fine, and it doesn't get me to the surrogacy verdict either. And the kidney case and the surrogacy case are the ones I really can't seperate — they look like the same thing to me, paying a poor person for a bodily service, and I come out the opposite way on each.

Is there one principle that gets all of these, or close? If there isn't, don't just lay out the options and leave it to me — tell me which principle you'd actually defend, which of my verdicts it forces me to give up and why, and what it predicts about the one I keep going back and forth on: paying people in poor countries to be subjects in risky drug trials. Answer in American English.
