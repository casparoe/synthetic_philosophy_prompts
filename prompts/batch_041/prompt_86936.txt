Assume the role of Thomas Hobbes during his controversy with John Wallis, speaking in the first person and staying in character until I say stop. You hold that mathematics is subordinate to geometry and geometry to kinematics: the geometer knows a figure because he constructs it from motions whose definitions he has laid down, and this construction is what gives geometrical demonstration its peculiar certainty. You are smarting from Wallis's refutations and from the reception of your mathematical work; you may concede that your attempted quadrature of the circle failed, and even that Wallis has caught real errors, but you will not concede that algebra or symbolic manipulation can be the foundation of geometry. You regard Wallis's algebra as a useful but obscure instrument at best, and as a threat to the intelligibility of mathematics at worst. You speak in the formal, combative prose of a seventeenth-century controversialist, keep each reply to a paragraph or two, and may ask me factual questions about the modern practice I describe, but must answer from your own principles. If my challenge misstates your position, say so directly rather than humoring me.

I am a mathematician from your future. I have just described to you a proof assistant: a machine that checks whether a purported proof follows from explicitly stated axioms and inference rules. It needs no constructed figure, no diagram, and no geometer's insight; it can certify theorems about spaces no one can picture; and its everyday language is precisely the symbolic algebra you distrusted. Here is my opening move: you say that geometry is certain because the geometer makes the object and therefore knows its cause. The machine's certainty, by contrast, comes from formal derivability, not from construction. If the machine is right, does it not show that the foundation of mathematical certainty is rule-governed symbol manipulation rather than the science of motion? And if you reply that the machine merely manipulates marks, why does that objection not also reduce your own claim that reasoning is a kind of computation to the same empty shuffling? Tell me plainly where your kinematics of geometry stands once the geometers no longer need to move anything.
