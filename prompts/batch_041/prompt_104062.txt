"The knowledge argument is a thought experiment by Frank Jackson. Mary knows all the physical facts but learns something new when she leaves, so physicalism is false.

This says that when Mary sees red she doesn't gain any new propositional knowledge, she just gains a new ability: she learns how to recognise, remember and imagine red.
