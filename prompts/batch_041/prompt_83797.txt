Ok so I've been rereading Tolstoy's What Is Art? and I can't stop wondering what he'd make of AI image generators. I don't want the lazy answer (he'd hate it). I want an actual reconstruction.

Here's the case file. It's 2025, Tolstoy is inexplicably alive and has been shown three things. One: someone iterates on a prompt about four hundred times until they get a picture of a peasant woman at a well that genuinely makes viewers cry. Two: an artist trains a model on photos of her own dead mother and generates new images of her, and finds it consoling. Three: a gallery show where the so-called artist types one sentence and the model does everything else, and critics rave.

He can't use the tools himself and doesn't understand the engineering. Reconstruct his verdicts, and make the reasoning do the work rather than the verdicts. Which parts of his own theory are actually carrying the weight here, and where would he have to modify something or bite a bullet?

The thing I most want you to press on: his account of art as a kind of infection, where the artist has genuinely felt the emotion and then transmits it to others. Can that get a grip on a case where nobody felt anything and the image still moves people? If yes, say how. If no, tell me which of his claims he'd have to give up, and whether he'd actually give it up or just deny that the phenomenon is what it looks like.

Second: his markers of counterfeit art, the borrowing / imitation / striking-effect / diversion list. Does AI work trip those automatically, or could some AI work count as real art on his view? Give me the discriminating test he'd really use, not the slogan.

Third wrinkle: his universalism. Art that unites people, accessible to everyone, no elite vocabulary. A model trained on the entire internet is in some sense maximally universal and in another sense just an average of everything. Which way does that cut for him? I suspect it cuts against him and I want to know if I'm right.

Also he had those three conditions for the degree of art — individuality of feeling, clearness, sincerity, or something close to that. Correct me if I've mangled it, then tell me which one AI fails hardest.

No reading list, just the reconstruction and where it gets shaky.
