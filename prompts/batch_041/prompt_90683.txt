Ok so I'm a retired spectroscopist — thirty-five years on NMR, never read a page of philosophy till I hit 63 — and I've fallen down a rabbit hole I can't climb out of. Panpsychism. The idea that experience goes all the way down, that the intrinsic nature of physical stuff is experiential, not just structural. Fine, I can hold that thought. But here's whats eating me: if it's true, does that mean the universe is stuffed with tiny subjects who can suffer? Half of me says obviously yes, the other half (the old reductionist half) says this is just wordplay with a straight face.

Anyway, two philosopher friends of mine have been going at this for a year and I'm tired of refereeing. One of them — call her the pan-sentientist — thinks that granting panpsychism gets you a moral revolution: every fundamental entity is a potential locus of welfare, so physics is ethics and "dead" matter was never dead. The other — the deflationist — says even if micro-experience is real, experience alone isn't welfare; suffering needs the right kind of functional or structural organization, so panpsychism changes nothing ethically, it's a distinction without a moral difference. They talk past each other constantly.

So: write the joint statement these two could actually both sign. I want (1) a numbered list of claims they agree on, including about what panpsychism does and doesn't commit you to and what "suffering" would even have to mean at the micro level; (2) the remaining disagreement stated precisely, each side's own view worded so the other side would at least accept it as a fair rendering, laid out as a little disagreements table; (3) the cruxes — I suspect one is whether valence is a matter of intrinsic phenomenal character or of organization, and another about whether moral status can come in degrees that approach zero without reaching it; (4) for each crux, a concrete case or thought experiment they'd both agree bears on it, with each side's prediction written down in advance. And if part of their fight turns out to be purely verbal, say which part and why.

Keep the whole thing under 800 words, and please lead with the agreed claims so I can see at a glance how much they actually share.
