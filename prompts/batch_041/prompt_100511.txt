In the autumn of 1989 the East German regime rewrote its travel regulations, and on the evening of 9 November a Politburo spokesman — handed the new text shortly before a press conference and told it was embargoed until the following morning, and not briefed on its contents — was asked when the new rules took effect. He shuffled his papers and answered that, as far as he knew, they applied at once. The regulation in fact still required an application and was meant to begin the next day. Within hours crowds gathered at the checkpoints, the overwhelmed guards let them through, and by midnight the Wall was effectively open.

Historians of 1989 routinely call the regime's collapse overdetermined, and I want that word put under pressure rather than used as a gesture. Begin with the strictest reading available from the philosophy of causation — two or more causes each individually sufficient for the effect, so that the effect survives the removal of any one of them — and ask whether the East German case satisfies it. Keep that reading distinct from the loose one on which "overdetermined" merely records that many factors pushed the same way. Then say, on the strict reading, what status the accidental trigger has: a cause, a mere occasion, or nothing in its own right; whether any of the structural factors (Soviet non-intervention, the Hungarian opening, the mass exodus, the protest movement, the fiscal crisis) was on its own sufficient; and whether the mutual dependence of those factors makes strict overdetermination unavailable in historical explanation in principle or only unprovable in practice.

Close by turning the case back on the concept. If strict overdetermination never in fact obtains in history, is the historian's "overdetermined" describing a feature of the world or only a stance toward a causal narrative? And is there a weakened version — probabilistic, say, in which several independent factors each raise the likelihood of the outcome so that it became all but certain while none was sufficient — that survives the objections and still earns its explanatory keep?
