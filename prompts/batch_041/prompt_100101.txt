The Republic is kind of a natural experiment in that story coming apart, because you can watch the same norms get broken in sequence and the price of breaking them drop each time.

So run it through that lens. The episodes I care about, roughly in order: Tiberius Gracchus getting the tribune Octavius deposed by a vote of the assembly (which apparently nobody thought you could do to a sacrosanct tribune), then the Senate's emergency decree against Gaius, then Sulla marching his army on Rome, then Caesar at the Rubicon.
