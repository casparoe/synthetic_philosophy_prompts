ok so I've gone down a rabbit hole on the AI alignment stuff and I've hit a wall I can't get past. The thing I keep running into is HCH, humans consulting HCH. As I understand it: take a person, Hugh, give him a question-answering machine, and the machine answers any question by doing exactly what Hugh would have said if he had access to the machine. So it's Hugh asking a copy of Hugh, who asks a copy of Hugh, forever. And people say this is the best way anyone has found to pin down what a person's enlightened judgment or considered judgment about a question actually is, and that it's the thing that amplification and debate and all those schemes are trying to approximate.

My problem is that the definition looks completely empty to me. If the machine says whatever Hugh-with-a-machine would say, and Hugh-with-a-machine's answer is just whatever the machine says, then nothing is fixed. Any answer at all is consistent with the story, because it just goes round in a circle. It's like defining a number as the thing that's one more than itself — that's not an ideal, thats a typo. So my first question is whether HCH is vacuous.

But then I read about factored cognition, which as I understand it says that a task a person can't do in one go can be broken into subtasks a person can do reliably, and if you chain those up you get an answer better than the person could have managed alone, in principle as good as you'd want. If that's right then HCH looks buildable: get a room full of people, have them split the question up, have other people answer the small pieces, aggregate, done. So now I have two readings and I don't see how they can both be right. Either HCH specifies nothing and is useless, or it's basically a blueprint for a system I could run tomorrow with enough volunteers, in which case I don't see whats left to worry about in alignment, you just run HCH and take the answer.

I'm sure I'm mixing something up. Can you sort out what HCH actually is as a piece of theorizing (what kind of object is it — a proposal for a system, a definition, an ideal, a prediction?), what factored cognition actually claims and how strong that claim is meant to be, where my circularity worry has teeth and where it doesn't, and what the real hard part is if you try to get anywhere near HCH with actual people or with a model trained to imitate it.

- the_mixup: what I've actually confused, stated plainly

- the_real_hard_part: what actually blocks building HCH out of people or out of a trained model
