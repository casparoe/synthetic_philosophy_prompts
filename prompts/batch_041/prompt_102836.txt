What I want: take Augustine's position on lying and restate the whole thing in the vocabulary I actually use. Put all of that into threat models, adversarial intent, spoofing versus honest error, integrity versus authenticity of a message, replay or selective disclosure of genuine data, cost-benefit risk acceptance, assume-breach thinking, and the fact that in security you only ever observe behavior and never the adversary's internal state.
