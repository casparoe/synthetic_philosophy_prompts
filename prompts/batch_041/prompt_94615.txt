By that I mean the cluster of ideas according to which, in a sufficiently large universe containing many agents correlated with you—because they share your decision algorithm or occupy relevantly similar situations—your decision to cooperate can be evidence that they will cooperate, making cooperation rational even without communication or causal influence; this is the territory of evidential decision theory, superrationality, and acausal trade. Focus on a few concrete scenes—first contact, the weapon/tool ambiguity, the phone call to General Shang, the closing montage—and say where the film resists the reading. End by saying what the film adds to the philosophical debate that argument alone does not.
