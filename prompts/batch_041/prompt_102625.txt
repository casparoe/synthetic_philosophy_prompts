I keep running into the same wierd situation on committees and I've never found a name for it, so I'm hoping you can tell me whether there is one.

Here's the shape of it. Say a department is deciding on a hire. There are three propositions everyone agrees are tied together: we hire this candidate; if we hire her we have to give her the lab space; and we can't give her the lab space and also keep the visiting position. We take them one at a time. Each passes by a clear majority — yes hire, yes give the space, yes keep the visiting position. But the three majority verdicts together are incoherent: they add up to a plan that keeps both the lab space and the visiting position, which the same majority just said is impossible. So we go back and 'fix' it. And here's the part that bugs me. There is no unique fix. We can treat any one of the three as the fixed point and let the other two give way, and every seperate one of those repairs is equally faithful to how we actually voted. Which repair we get is decided by whoever set the agenda or wrote the motion. So the group's real position ends up living in the framing rather than in the votes, even though nobody changed their mind and nobody voted strategically.

Second instance, same thing: a funding panel where each of the criteria passes individually, but the funded bundle violates the budget rule the same panel endorsed.

I know this is adjacent to the discursive dilemma / doctrinal paradox literature about groups ending up inconsistent even when every member is individually consistent. But that seems to be about the bare possibility of the inconsistency. What I can't find a word for is the movability — that the inconsistency can be shoved onto any of the linked claims, that all the shoves are equally legitimate, and that the tiebreaker is therefore the order of business and not the votes. Is there a name for that specific thing? How does it sit relative to the discursive dilemma on one side and to plain agenda manipulation or Arrow-style problems on the other? And if nothing established matches, coin a term and tell me its edges: does the phenomenon even need a group, or does the exact same structure show up inside a single person who holds the linked beliefs at once? I don't just want a label, I want to know what the thing is and where it stops.
