The question should compel students to distinguish the incommensurability of the values from the incomparability of the options, to state what additional premise would be required to conclude that neither choice is rationally better, and to assess whether appeal to a broader consideration that both options share can restore comparability without dissolving the incommensurability. Then give me a model answer of about 700 words and a marking scheme worth 20 points that names the specific moves a top answer must make and the specific confusions that should cost marks.
