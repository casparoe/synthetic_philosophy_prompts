Tell me the order the components have to come in and why each depends on the last: where the two dimensions get introduced relative to the possible-worlds apparatus, where the diagonal and the actually operator belong, whether centered worlds have to come in before or after the matrix, and whether the epistemic and the contextual versions of the framework should be kept apart for this audience at all or whether trying to keep them apart will cost me more than it buys. Finish with one question I can put to the room that would show whether they can now do something with the framework, and tell me what to leave out at this level and to announce that I'm leaving it out.
