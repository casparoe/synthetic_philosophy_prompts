My half-formed idea: the debate over the language of thought conflates two claims that need to be kept apart, compositionality (a thesis about content: the content of a complex representation is determined by the contents of its parts and their mode of combination) and constituency (a thesis about vehicles: a complex representation literally contains, as structural parts, representations of its parts).
