Write a short story about an ant colony that may or may not have a mind. Invent a concrete mechanism for whatever cognition the colony might have — for instance, that a colony-level thought exists only while a particular loop of pheromone trails stays closed, so that the colony is minded only in brief, intermittent episodes — and build the plot around a person who must decide something about it: whether to preserve it, alter it, or study it in a way that will destroy it. Leave the central question unresolved, so that the same evidence reads equally well as a colony that thinks and as an observer who cannot help animating the pattern. End with a brief note on where the story takes liberties with the idea of a colony mind, which contested assumption it leans on, how confident you are in what you claim there, and the point you are least sure of.
