If the doctor says there's a 3% chance of a side effect, then 3% is exactly how confident you should be that you'll get it, and anything else is irrational. Chance, on this view, just is the credence of a perfectly informed agent, so the principle is really a definition of objective chance rather than a constraint on it — to say an event has a 50% chance is just to say that a perfectly rational, fully informed observer would be 50% confident. The principle also explains why gambling works the way it does: because your credence has to track the chance, you should take any bet offered at odds better than the chance, which is why casinos can't be beaten in the long run.
