If that's right, then the intolerant aren't being carved out as a special exception when we stop tolerating them; they've just stepped outside the practice, and a practice doesn't owe anything to people who aren't party to it. Make that case as tightly as you can, and then take on the objection I actually care about: that this is just fancy dress for "whoever holds the power gets to define the practice," i.e. a modus vivendi with a nice coat on.
