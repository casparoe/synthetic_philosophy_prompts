hey, I've got a reading group zoom this weekend and I'm supposed to post my philosophical profile on vagueness so the others can poke at it, so I want to do a consistency pass on myself first before I embarrass myself. here's where I've landed:

- I'm a bivalantist. every declarative sentence is true or false, no gaps, no third truth-value. excluded middle is just how things are, not a bookkeeping convenience.
- Vagueness is real. 'heap', 'bald', 'tall' -- these genuinely lack sharp boundaries, that's kind of the whole point of them, it's not just that I'm ignorant of where the line is.
- Tolerance: one grain never turns a non-heap into a heap, one hair never flips bald to not-bald. small changes can't matter.
- Each premise of the sorites looks true to me on its own, and the reasoning looks airtight, but the conclusion is obviously insane.
- I'm drawn to epistemicism (there is a sharp line, we just can't ever know it) mostly because it's the only story that lets me keep bivalence while admitting the vagueness is real.
- But I also think higher-order vagueness is real -- there's no sharp line between the definite heaps and the borderline ones either -- and I have a nagging feeling that quietly kills the epistemicist's one sharp line.
- I think the meaning of a vague word is fixed entirely by how the community uses it, nothing else, and our use is finite and shifting.
- And I keep half-flirting with supervaluationism because it seems to keep all the classical theorems while letting borderline cases have no truth value -- but I've heard it breaks something about disjunction and I don't get why that matters.

Which of these actually pull against each other, which only look like they do, and how bad is each real tension? rank them. for each real one tell me the cheapest fix and what I'd be giving up. and settle it for me: can I have bivalence and tolerance together, or does higher-order vagueness doom epistemicism, or is the whole package just supervaluationism in a trenchcoat? I genuinely don't know which of these I'd drop so please don't assume one is obviously the one to go.
