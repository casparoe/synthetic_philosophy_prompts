And because a picture resembles the thing it shows, a picture language is basically self-interpreting — a schoolchild in Vienna and a reader in Shanghai take it the same way, no training needed — which is exactly what made it the ideal foundation for the unity of science: you get a bedrock of protocol statements that can be compared directly with experience and that no later evidence can shake. It's Leibniz's characteristica universalis, only done with drawings instead of algebra. And it's the same project as those ISOTYPE charts he made with Gerd Arntz for the Vienna museum — same aim, same thing.
