I want the specification written out before I read any more accounts. Please begin by brainstorming a wide list of candidate requirements without judging them, then settle the list.

One of my flatmates works on reward circuitry and insists the real answer is that a single subpersonal mechanism implements liking across all the cases above; please say whether that satisfies the unity requirement or whether it changes the subject, since the axiology needs a property that is good-making for the person, not merely a shared cause.
