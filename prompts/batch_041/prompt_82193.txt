I am coding the amnesty decisions of a national reconciliation commission for a paper on how such bodies quietly resolve the tension inside their own mandates. The Commission's mandate imposes exactly two conditions: the act must have been associated with a political objective and proportionate to it, and the applicant must make full disclosure. (Names and details are altered.) The decisions I have coded so far:

- a police captain who personally shot four detainees and ordered the killing of at least a dozen more, and who refused to testify when called: amnesty denied;
- a registry clerk who maintained the filing system from which the death squads drew their target lists, who knew what the lists were for, who gave a full account that secured the convictions of two colonels, and who expressed no remorse, insisting his work was purely clerical: amnesty granted;
- a conscripted driver who, under threat of being shot for refusing, drove prisoners to a site he knew was an execution ground, and who gave a full account: amnesty granted;
- a soldier who personally shot three prisoners during a sweep and who, when summoned, denied the killings until confronted with photographs: amnesty denied;
- a guerrilla who took part in an armed bank robbery to fund the movement, gave a full account, and apologized publicly: amnesty granted.

The Commission has just heard its next case. The applicant is a nineteen-year-old conscript who personally shot two prisoners during a sweep after being told they were armed saboteurs, though they turned out to be unarmed villagers; he gave a full account, his testimony being the only evidence linking the operation to the colonel who ordered it, and he is openly remorseful. The Commission's annual reports say only that it applies its two conditions in light of the record as a whole.

Predict how the Commission will rule. Say which rule the coded decisions best fit, name at least one rival rule that fits them just as well and any candidate rule the record already rules out, give each surviving rule's prediction for this case, give your best guess with your reasons, and identify the further case that would most cleanly separate the surviving rules.
