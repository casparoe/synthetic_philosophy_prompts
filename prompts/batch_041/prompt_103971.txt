It started because I explained the dollar auction to him — the setup where you auction off a one-dollar bill, the top bidder pays their bid and gets the dollar, but the *second*-highest bidder also has to pay their bid and gets nothing. So bidding keeps going past a dollar, because if you're sitting at ninety-five cents and someone bids a dollar, walking away means you paid ninety-five cents for literally nothing, so of course you bid a dollar-five, and then they're in the same spot, and it spirals until someone's paying twelve dollars for a dollar bill. He kept saying the bidders are just being stupid, and I kept saying that's the whole thing — every single bid is locally defensible, that's exactly why it's a trap, no one has to be an idiot for it to go off the rails, and by the end we were basically performing the auction ourselves, each of us refusing to concede the last point because conceding felt like paying for nothing. He went to bed mad. I'm still mad. Anyway.

Write me a joke about this.
