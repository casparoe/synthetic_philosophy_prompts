I bought an old farmhouse last year and found something weird in the crawlspace that I can't stop thinking about. Behind a loose brick wall there's a little sealed nook, about the size of a shoebox. Inside, someone arranged 13 identical glass jars in a circle. Each jar has a different number of dried beans in it — 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, and then it gets less clean, like 47, 52, 61, etc. In the middle of the circle is a single left boot, and under the boot is a folded piece of paper with a list of maybe 20 first names and tally marks next to each name. No writing, no dates, no symbols I recognize. The house is from around 1900 but the jars look 1970s-ish. My neighbor says it was definately some kind of cult ritual. My other neighbor says it was just a weird pantry game or a kid's project. I want to figure out what the people who made this actually believed or what institution they were part of, but I also know I'm just looking at objects. How should I go about this? What can I actually infer, and what would I be fooling myself into thinking I can infer? I don't want to just make up a story that sounds cool. Its been bugging me for months.
