It's the first bit of that I keep tripping over, because I've realised I don't actually know what the phrase is claiming. Another mate reckons "could" here is only ever conditional — that it means you'd have done it if you'd wanted to — and says that's a cheat, though he can't say why. And a third one keeps bringing up some case where a bloke would have been forced to do the thing anyway by a meddling scientist, and says that shows you can be to blame even when you couldn't have done otherwise.
