My opening move: here's my problem with your whole position. The case for honor is always made with the same guy -- the one who refuses to be humiliated, who'd rather lose everything than grovel. But honor culture doesnt hand that dignity out evenly. It's the man with brothers and a family name who can afford to be proud; for everyone below him the code says swallow the insult and smile, because if you swing back you get destroyed. Dignity culture at least pretends the law will back the weak guy up. And the victimhood-culture thing, whatever you think of it, at least lets the weak guy demand an accounting without a knife.
