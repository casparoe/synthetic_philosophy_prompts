I am drafting a short position statement for a formal epistemology reading group and would like a consistency audit before I circulate it. Here is where I have landed.

(1) In the Judy Benjamin case — Judy is dropped somewhere in a territory divided into Red and Blue, with half of each territory's population in Headquarters and half in Second Company, and she begins with equal credence in the four resulting cells; her interrogator then tells her that if she is in Red territory then she is in Headquarters — the correct posterior is the one that minimizes relative entropy from her prior subject to the constraint that the Red-and-Second-Company cell receives probability zero. On that posterior her credence that she is in Red falls from one half to one third.

(2) I regard it as a point in favour of that posterior that it coincides with what one gets by conditionalizing on the material conditional from Red to Headquarters.

(3) I hold that indicative conditionals are not material conditionals, so that anyone who reads the interrogator's message as the material conditional has changed the subject.

(4) I hold that the message is genuine evidence about where Judy is, and that ignoring it would be irrational.

(5) I hold that all learning is conditionalization on a proposition, and that where the proposition learned is not an event in the agent's original space, the correct response is to enlarge the space rather than to abandon conditionalization.

(6) I hold that it is a defect in an update rule if the rule lets an evidential report lower the probability of the proposition the report is about; call this the no-undermining constraint.

(7) I hold that relative entropy minimization is the correct generalization of conditionalization, since it reduces to conditionalization when the constraint is that some proposition receive probability one.

(8) I hold that Jeffrey conditionalization is the correct treatment of uncertain evidence, and that Judy Benjamin's case is a case of uncertain evidence, since she cannot be sure what her interrogator meant.

Which of these pull against each other, which only appear to, and which single revision would clear up the most? Order the genuine tensions by severity, say for each what would have to give and at what cost, and name the pair that looks inconsistent but is not. Do not report what the literature settles; I want the audit, not a survey. Under 700 words.
