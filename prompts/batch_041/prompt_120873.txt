I have recently become a trustee of a small famine-relief charity. My only prior connection to the subject was genealogical — I learned this year that my great-grandmother died in the Bengal famine — and I have walked straight into an argument I cannot settle. Our head of communications wants us to stop describing famines as natural disasters, on the grounds that a famine is a political crime rather than a natural event, and that calling it natural is a way of letting governments off the hook. I am sympathetic to the conclusion, but I suspect the one-liner is doing several different jobs at once, and that some of the claims it could be making are far too weak to license a change of language while others are strong enough to commit him to conclusions he would not accept.

What I would like from you is a menu rather than a verdict on the slogan as a whole. Set out the distinct precise theses that a claim of that shape could be expressing — the near-trivial, the empirical, the counterfactual, the moral, and the legal — and say of each whether it is trivial, contentious but defensible, or simply false, and whether it would actually support the conclusion that we should drop the phrase from our materials. Then tell me which reading he most plausibly has in mind. Test the readings against the three episodes I know something of — Bengal, Ireland, the Great Leap Forward — and say whether they come apart there; then test them against a case in which the natural shock is undisputed and it is the state's capacity rather than its will that was the binding constraint. I would also like you to work through the claim, which I have met in my reading, that in many famines most people die of disease and displacement rather than of hunger, and to say what that does to each reading. I am told that all of this is essentially Amartya Sen's thesis; I have not read him, so if my framing of the history is wrong, please say so rather than working around it.

Write in British English. Be explicit throughout about how confident you are in each claim, and single out the point you are least sure of.
