I'm in second year and doing a module on consciousness and cognition, and last week we did Hofstadter's careenium — the one with the billiard table covered in tiny magnetic marbles he calls simms that careen about and sometimes stick together into bigger stable blobs, the simmballs. As I understood it, the walls of the table change how bouncy they are depending on what's happening outside, so the blobs end up mirroring goings-on in the world, and the mapping onto the brain is supposed to be table = skull, simms = neurons, simmballs = symbols or concepts. The lecturer said the question Hofstadter is really asking is which level is doing the pushing — simms shoving simms, or simmballs shoving simmballs — and that his answer is subtler than either.

Then a mate of mine whose read a lot of this stuff gave me his take, and I can't tell how much of it to trust: "The simms just jiggle about more or less at random, whereas the simmballs have proper trajectories of their own and carry the information about what's going on outside, so the whole point is that the simmballs push the simms around and not the other way round. That's why you can't explain thinking by staring at the simms — the symbol level is a genuinely new causal force that the bottom level simply doesn't have, which makes Hofstadter basically a dualist, just with levels instead of substances. And that's why the careenium shows that neuroscience can never explain consciousness." Another girl in the seminar said the exact opposite, that if the simms do all the shoving then the simmballs are just along for the ride and basically epiphenomenal, which doesn't ring true to me either.

So is his summary fair? Go claim by claim and tell me which parts Hofstadter, or someone who's thought about this properly, would happily accept, which they'd wince at, and which are just wrong — and whether his closing conclusion about neuroscience not being able to explain consciousness actually follows from anything true in what he said. Start with your bottom line in the first sentence, then the reasoning, no preamble and no restating of my question.
