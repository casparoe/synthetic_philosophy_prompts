I've been doing my family tree and its left me with a question I can't shake. My great-grandmother's death certificate says "old age", which no doctor would put down now; my nan's says "senility"; and my mum, who's 61, has just been told she's got stage 1 hypertension and put on tablets — the same numbers her own mother was told were just what happens when you get older. My brother, 34, identical readings, is told he's got a condition.

So whether a body state counts as a disease seems to have nothing to do with the biology: the same numbers are a disease in a 34-year-old and normal ageing in a 70-year-old, and a risk factor until a pill turns up, at which point it becomes a silent killer everyone gets screened for. And it seems to run backwards too — we now say people had diseases their own doctors would have called just being old.

Is there a name for this? I know about medicalisation, and about drug firms selling sickness, but both of those are about medicine grabbing territory or about someone profiting, and this happens with a public screening programme and no product in sight. What I'm after is the specific thing: disease status being conferred by our ability to intervene, then projected back onto the dead. If there's no name, coin one — but tell me where it stops. Is the age flip really the same phenomenon as the pill flip, and where's the line between this and plain medicalisation? Don't just hand me a label.
