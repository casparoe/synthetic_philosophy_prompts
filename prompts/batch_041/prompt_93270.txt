Here's the target vocabulary: the story bible and its amendment procedure; canon versus non-canon; retconning; continuity errors; the difference between a rule being in force in the fictional world and a rule being true to the series' moral center; the author as legislator; the editor as appellate court; the readers' sense of what a character would really do as a kind of higher law; and the craft constraints any long series has to satisfy if it's going to be a series at all — consistency, publicity, not too many exceptions, no retroactive revisions that make earlier books unreadable. I don't need names and dates; I need the positions in my terms.
