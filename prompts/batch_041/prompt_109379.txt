My partner's a philosopher (metaphysics of science, mostly) and we keep having the same fight over breakfast: is this or that kind socially constructed. She's got a running scoreboard and I genuinely cannot reverse-engineer the rule she's running. Her verdicts so far, with the gist of her reasons: money, constructed, because nothing about a slip of paper makes it money and we make it money by treating it as money and backing it with institutions; race, constructed, and she's adamant that the biology doesn't sort people into races, and that the sorting then feeds back and reshapes the people sorted; gender, constructed, and when I ask whether the bodies matter she says that's a red herring, which I've never known how to take; quarks, not constructed, we found them; peanut allergy, not constructed, allergy diagnosis is culturally variable and fashion-driven but the immune response doesn't care what we call it; teenager, constructed, it arrived with compulsory schooling and the youth labour market and kids' behaviour shifted once the category existed; predator, in ecology, not constructed, real trophic relation, nobody has to agree about anything; citizenship, constructed, obviously conferred; "the 1%", constructed, the line is drawn for policy purposes and nothing in nature happens at the 80th percentile; and left-handedness, not constructed.
