Right, this one's personal. I've had a diagnosis I'd rather not go into, and there are two ways forward. One of them has been around forever, so the numbers are solid — about 70% of people like me get the result we want. The other is newer, and my consultant reckons it's probably about the same on average, but honestly nobody knows: could be 50, could be 90. And every time I sit down with the two leaflets I end up drifting towards the one with the solid numbers, and I genuinely couldn't tell you why. My sister did a stats module at uni and says I'm being thick — if the best guess is the same, she says, then the vagueness shouldn't come into it at all, and if anything the new one has upside. It doesn't feel like that to me, though. With the first one I know what I'm choosing; with the second I feel like I'm handing my life over to a smaller pile of data.

Two other things that might be relevant, or might just be me being weird. If I only read the new treatment's leaflet on its own, with nothing to compare it against, I'm suddenly much more relaxed about those same vague numbers. And I'd feel a lot better about those same vague numbers if they'd come from the consultant I trust than from a registrar I've met once.
