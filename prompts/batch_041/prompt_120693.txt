If there were a principled, non-arbitrary way to restrict the hypothesis space, not just the world happens to be simple but something you could actually justify, that would be a genuine free lunch and would answer Hume in a way almost nobody thinks is available; so honestly the interesting outcome might be a clean argument for why there can't be one.
