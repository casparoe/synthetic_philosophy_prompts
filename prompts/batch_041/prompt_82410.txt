ok so for my philosophy club im supposed to write a short story that makes a philosophical idea actually do some work, and i want to do the ring of gyges thing from plato (the shepherd finds a ring that makes him invisible, and glaucon uses it to argue that nobody is just on purpose, people are only decent because theyre being watched). but everyone in my club has already done the invisibility version, and honestly that story is so famous it feels pre-chewed, so i want to change the ring itself.

heres my version: the ring doesnt make you invisible. it makes you unremembered. while your wearing it you can do whatever you want, and it still happens, it still leaves real damage or real benefit behind in the world, but nobody who sees it can hold onto it as something a person did. theyll genuinely believe it was an accident, or that it was always like that, or that they must of imagined it. and heres the part im stuck on: the wearer forgets too. not the decision to put the ring on, but the actual thing they did. you keep the consequences -- the money, the promotion, the person whos gone -- and you know something happened, you just can never have the moment of knowing you did it.

so the usual payoff is gone. no reputation, no getting away with it, no smug feeling, not even your own private knowledge of what you are. write me the story of a person who has to decide what to do with this thing and has to reason it out ahead of time, because after each use theyll be a stranger to themselves but they still have to live in the wreckage.

some stuff i want in it: at least one scene where two characters really argue about whether theres any reason left to be just once the audience is gone completely, including the audience of yourself. i dont want that argument to sound like a textbook, i want it to sound like real people who are a bit scared. dont end it by telling me which one is right. and try to make the reader feel why glaucons challenge is actually tempting, instead of just having a character be obviously evil. maybe also a bit where someone notices the difference between nobody knowing and nobody knowing including me, and cant decide if that difference matters or not.
