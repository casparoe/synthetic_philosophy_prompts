Im a junior in high school and I'm in this philosophy club and we keep getting into fights about free will that go nowhere, and I think I finally figured out why, so I want to know if the thing I noticed has a name.

Heres the pattern. Take my uncle, who smokes and says he loves smoking, and at the moment he lights up he wants that cigarette more than he wants anything else in the world. Nobody would call him free. Now take my friend whose whole family is in this church, and she now genuinely, from the bottom of her heart, wants to give the church her savings, and she says thats the real her talking. That one makes everyone uncomfortable too, but nobody can say why. And then the case that broke my brain: somebody gets hypnotized or gets some kind of implant that makes them love math, and they do love math, and theyre grateful for it, and if you asked whether they wanted to be a math person theyd say yes, they always wanted it. Are they free? It seems like the answer changes depending on which of their wants you look at, and every time I try to fix it by going up a level — ok, he wants the want, and he wants to want the want — the same problem just rides up with me and never lands. Like theres no floor where the "real him" lives. Its wants all the way down.

So does this have a name? I know people say brainwashed or puppet, but that feels off, because in my cases the person isnt fighting anything, theyre happy. And I know theres some old idea that your free when you act on the want you actually want to be the one running you, but thats exactly the thing that falls apart in my math-implant case. What I want is the specific name for the part where the real self keeps sliding up a level and never lands, plus how it relates to whatever its closest to and how its different. Dont just hand me a label — tell me where the edges are. Is the church girl in the same category as the math guy or not? And is there any version of this where the sliding actually stops without somebody cheating?

Also please dont send me a reading list, I cant check any of that stuff anyway, just explain the idea. Can you do it as exactly five numbered points, one or two sentences each, no intro and no wrap-up? American English please, and keep the vocabulary normal if you can.
