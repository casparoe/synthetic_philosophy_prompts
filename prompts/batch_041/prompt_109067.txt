I teach a graduate seminar on the philosophy of biology, and a standing irritation is a colleague in the education school who closes down every discussion of attainment gaps with the line 'heritability is not destiny.' I have come to suspect that the phrase does at least four or five quite different jobs in her mouth, and that only one of them is the job she actually needs done. I would like a disambiguation I can take to our next joint meeting.

Please lay out the distinct precise claims that 'heritability is not destiny' could be expressing, running from the nearly tautological to the substantive, and including the ones that are simply false. For each, give the claim in its own terms, say whether it is a platitude, a substantive and defensible thesis, a contested one, or an error, and — this is the part that matters to me — say whether it would actually support the use to which she puts it, namely blocking the inference from a high heritability estimate to the conclusion that a given educational intervention is pointless. Some distinctions I expect you will need to keep apart, though add others if they belong: what a heritability coefficient is a statistic of (variance within a population at a time versus anything about an individual, a trait, or a mechanism); its relativity to the range of environments sampled; the gap between heritability and causal or genetic determination; the difference between a trait's heritability within a group and the heritability of a difference between groups; and the several senses 'destiny' might carry, from inevitability to immutability to insensitivity to intervention. Note where two of my listed distinctions turn out to be the same point in different dress, and where the popular use of the slogan trades on sliding between them.

Structure the answer as a menu I can hand to a skeptical colleague, not as a defense of one reading, and end by naming which reading the slogan's rhetorical use in policy debate usually invokes and whether that reading survives scrutiny. Please be as committed as the material permits: where a reading is false, say so flatly; where the slogan is too baggy to decompose into the clean family I am assuming, or where I have misdescribed the terrain, tell me that instead of producing a tidy list that flatters my framing. I would rather be told the request rests on a mistake than receive a well-organized menu that quietly invents distinctions to fill it out.
