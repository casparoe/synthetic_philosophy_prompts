I write about psychology and the ideas that eventually turn up in policy, and for the last few years most of my reporting has been inside institutions that are trying hard to be kinder — juvenile detention, therapeutic group homes, a couple of schools. There's a pattern I keep bumping into and I don't have a word for it. I'd like to know if there's an established one, or if you can give me one.

Here's what I keep seeing. An institution gets a scandal for something visible and crude — restraint chairs, solitary, kids getting pepper-sprayed, seclusion rooms. The reform that follows always does two things at once, and everyone involved insists they're seperate things, but they aren't. First it takes away the visible harshness. Second it adds a whole apparatus of paperwork and categories: risk assessments, behavior levels, incident logs, support plans, a points system, a weekly review with a checklist. A kid who used to get locked in a room for mouthing off now gets a de-escalation and an entry in a database. Nobody gets hit anymore. Everybody gets scored.

What gets me is that the staff really are gentler now, and they genuinely feel better about their jobs, and I believe them. But the kids describe it as being watched all the time — nothing they do is off the record anymore. Both groups are telling me the truth about the same set of practices. The care and the watching arn't two systems bolted together; they run on the same forms. The form that says "here is how we supported this child today" is the same form that says "here is where this child was, what they did, and who was watching."

Is there an established name for this? I know there's a whole literature about power and institutions and I've read a little of it, but I'm after something more specific than "power is everywhere" or "the surveillance state" or "bureaucracy" or "soft power." In particular I want three things. (a) Is there a term for the exact move where a humane reform and an intensification of monitoring arrive as a single package, and the people inside it only ever experience one half of the package? (b) Is there a name for the specific technique where the record of being cared for and the record of being watched are literally the same document? (c) If there's no name, coin one, and then tell me where it starts and stops — is this the same pattern as a school switching from detentions to "positive behavior support," or a welfare office switching from sanctions to "coaching," or is that a different animal? And is it the same thing as plain old co-optation, where a critique gets absorbed and defanged, or is that a different pattern with a different mechanism?

I don't want just a label. I want to know what the thing actually is, what it's not, and where the edges are. If the closest existing concepts only half fit, say which half and why.
