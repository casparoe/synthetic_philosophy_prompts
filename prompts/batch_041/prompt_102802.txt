Build the tree from the logical space first — the combinations of answers the nodes permit — and only then locate the positions within it. Two claims the tree must route are these: that the theorems limit what an AI can prove about its own reliability, and that any sufficiently rich moral or legal code must contain truths its own rules cannot establish. Flag any pair of leaves the tree cannot separate, and note any combination of answers that no one occupies and why — incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored.
