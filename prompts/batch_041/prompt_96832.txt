Alright, Ive been arguing with a mate who's a Rawlsian and I want to check my own position for cracks before I go back at him, so treat this as an audit rather than a request for a reading list. Roughly where I've landed:

1. Whether a distribution is just depends only on how it came about — just acquisition, just transfer, and putting right old injustices. The shape of the outcome doesn't matter in itself; there's no level of inequality that makes a distribution unjust as such.
2. So self-ownership goes all the way down. The state taking a slice of what I earn by my own labour is on a par with forced labour — unjust, not just inefficient.
3. The Wilt Chamberlain case did it for me. Start from any just distribution, let people spend their own money on whatever they fancy, and you end up with a lopsided one; every step along the way was fine, so the result is fine. Which means any to-each-according-to-X rule can only be kept in place by continually interfering with people's choices. Patterns are a non-starter.
4. Rights are side-constraints, not goals — I don't violate someone's rights because doing so would make the total better. I'm not consequentialist about this at all.
5. On the concrete stuff: a man who's never stolen or defrauded anyone can watch his neighbour starve and owes him nothing, and if the neighbour nicks a loaf off him, he's entitled to have it back. People keep telling me those two are inconsistent and I honestly can't see it.
6. I'm not an anarchist — obviously the state can run courts, police and defence.
7. And I think the rectification clause is doing real work, not sitting there as a footnote. Half the land and capital in this country traces back to enclosure and conquest, and I'm with the proviso that says a first appropriation only really counts if it doesn't leave others worse off than they'd have been with the thing left unowned. So a lot of what people call their property now arguably was never theirs to begin with.

Does this hang together? Tell me which of these actually pull against each other, which only look like they do, and for the real ones what the cheapest fix is and what it would cost me to make it. Rank them by how bad the tension is rather than shrugging and saying everything's a bit in tension with everything else. British English please, and no hedgy 'well, it depends what framework you're in' — commit.
