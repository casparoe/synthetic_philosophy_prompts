Two years ago I was the volunteer who pitched the Riverside Mill idea to our community foundation, and last month they finally put me on the board, which means im now the one drafting the recommendation for Thursday's meeting on whether to keep funding it — so take all this with the grain of salt of someone who already stuck his neck out for the thing.

The quick version: we first put in $400k against a $1.2M budget to turn the old mill into a job-training center. We're at $2.9M spent now, and the contractor wants one more $450k tranche to finish the shell, after which a state match of $300k comes through. If we walk, we still owe about $180k in site remediation and lose a $150k deposit, and the county — our other funder — has hinted they'll pull out too if we do, which would leave us holding more of it. So stopping isn't free, it's picking which loss. Meanwhile there's a program the staff actually prefers that the same $450k could fund, though nobody's costed it out.

The part I can't shake: I read about an auction where you bid on a dollar and the top two bidders both have to pay, so it runs away — you keep bidding because dropping out means paying for nothing. My CFO says that's us, to the letter. My friend on the board says no, that's nonsense, we aren't bidding against anyone. I think they might both be right.

Draft the memo I'll bring in. I need a concrete recommendation — fund it, stop, or fund it with a hard cap and conditions — with a date attached, and an honest paragraph on the strongest case against whatever I land on. It should say whether the auction comparison actually holds, and if it doesn't, what the real disanalogy is and why it matters. And please deal with the thing that worrys me most: half this board has read the same pop-psych stuff about sunk costs, so they'll call any continuation a fallacy, and I don't think that's automatically right. Last thing — who should even be making this call, the people who cast the earlier votes or somebody who wasnt in the room, and does capping it now buy us anything, or is that just the same trap with better manners?
