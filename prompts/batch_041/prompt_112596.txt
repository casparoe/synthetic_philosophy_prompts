I have no formal training in AI research or philosophy, but I have been thinking on my own about wireheading and reward tampering. By wireheading I mean an agent acting to directly stimulate its own reward signal; by reward tampering I mean an agent acting to alter the reward function or the code that computes it. My puzzle is that whether an action is one or the other seems to depend on where you draw the boundary of the agent: if the reward channel is inside the agent, editing it is self-stimulation; if the reward function is outside the agent, editing it is tampering with the world. And if the agent's objective is defined over the history of reward signals it receives rather than over external world states, the boundary can be genuinely indeterminate, so the agent may not know which description applies to its own action. Please write a joke that turns on this indeterminacy. It should be funny to people who already know the standard examples of wireheading and reward tampering, and the punchline should turn on the substance of the distinction rather than on generic AI humor. Avoid the stock examples and build the joke around the boundary problem. A one-liner or a short dialogue is fine.
