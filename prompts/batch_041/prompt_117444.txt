Find an example of a condition whose status under a naturalist, function-based account of disease comes out differently depending only on how the reference class is drawn — the group relative to which normal functioning is measured, whether that be the species, a sex or age band, a local population, or the individual's own baseline — with the underlying biology unchanged. Both verdicts should be defensible, so that the case shows the naturalist's answer is not fixed by the biological facts alone. Stay clear of the worn examples (aging, baldness, obesity, hypertension, deafness, ADHD, homosexuality). Then say which reference class a naturalist ought to privilege and whether that choice can be defended without appealing to values, and what the case shows about the claim that the concept of disease can be analysed without invoking values.
