ok so im a junior in high school and ive fallen down the anthropics rabbit hole (doomsday argument, bostrom, that whole lesswrong corner of the internet) and my philosophy club is letting me present on it in two weeks. before i stand up and say something dumb i want to write out where ive actually landed and have somebody tell me if the pieces fit together. here's my position, roughly:

1. the doomsday argument is basically right. im somewhere around the hundred billionth human to have ever lived (give or take a lot, obviously). if humans were eventually going to number in the quadrillions, then landing this early in the sequence would be a wild coincidence. so the total is probably in the trillions or less, and we're probably not going to be around for millions of years.

2. the self-indication assumption is right. other things equal, if a hypothesis says there are way more observers in the world, i should think its more likely that im one of them, because there are more places for someone having my experiences to be. this is also how i explain why the fact that i exist at all doesn't freak me out, of course i'd find myself existing if there are tons of observers to be.

3. the presumptuous philosopher is talking nonsense. the thought experiment where a guy walks out of his office and announces that the universe must be the enormous version rather than the small version, purely because the enormous version has more people in it and therefore more chances for someone to be him, seems like an obvious reductio to me. you shouldn't be able to settle cosmology from an armchair with zero physics.

4. i think the reference class matters a ton here and you don't get to just pick whichever class hands you the answer you like. my class is humans, born humans, not dogs, not angels, not future AI. if you let it be all observers everywhere then the whole thing falls apart.

5. i also don't think any of this is a reason to be gloomy or to give up on the long term. it doesn't tell us anything about what would end us and i don't think its evidence for any particular disaster, nuclear war, AI, whatever. im still planning to live like humanity has a future.

so, do these hang together? i want to know which ones are actually pulling against each other, which pairs only look like they clash, and if something has to go, which one is cheapest to drop and what i'd be giving up. please rank them by how bad the problem is instead of just telling me they're all in tension, and be specific about which ones are definately fine.
