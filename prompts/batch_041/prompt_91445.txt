So me and a couple friends started this after-school thing where we argue about politics, and we got into a huge fight about whether sortition — picking representatives by lottery instead of voting — would actually fix anything. But the thing I keep coming back to is a pattern I've noticed and I dont have a word for it. Here's the version that made me notice it: our student council got changed last year so that instead of campaigning and voting for class reps, they just draw names out of a hat for who sits on it. Which sounds great, right? No posters, no popularity contest. But then all the stuff that actually matters — what gets discussed, in what order, which "expert" comes to talk to us (usually the same teacher who runs the whole thing), what even counts as a good proposal — that's still decided by the same three people who decided it before, and none of them got drawn out of any hat. So the lottery changed who's sitting in the chairs but not who's holding the pen. It's like the power didn't actually go away, it just slid over into the part of the process nobody thought to randomize.

And I feel like this is a general shape, not just our school: you reform the visible, contestable part of a system (who holds office, who votes, how long they serve, how they're picked) and the influence just relocates to the parts that were never contested in the first place — who sets the agenda, who writes the briefing, who decides what the question even is. Is there a name for this specific move? I know there's stuff about "who benefits," and my brother keeps saying something about a "second face of power," and I've also heard there's some game-theory idea that whoever sets up the rules secretly decides the outcome — but none of those quite feels like what I'm describing. What I actually want is: what is this thing exactly, what's the closest existing concept and where does it stop fitting, and where are the edges? Like, does it still count if the agenda-setter is also chosen randomly, or if the agenda is nailed down by a rule so there's nothing left to capture? If there's no name, honestly I'd be happy if you just make one up, as long as you definately tell me what it's not.
