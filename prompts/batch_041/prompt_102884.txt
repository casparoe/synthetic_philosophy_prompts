Need a satirical op-ed, roughly 500 words, for a fake broadsheet. The author is an invented syndicated columnist who also runs a one-man consultancy advising executives and presidents on 'what history teaches,' and his whole toolkit is Munich, Vietnam, and the Thucydides trap. The comedy has to come from the machinery of analogical reasoning going wrong rather than from him being a generic buffoon: he selects his source case by what's vivid and available rather than by what's representative, he never once asks what the base rate of a crisis actually escalating is, he treats the supposed lesson of Munich as settled even though the case gets read in opposite directions, and he keeps mistaking a rhyme for a repeat. Around two-thirds of the way in, have him confidently apply the appeasement frame to something tiny and domestic -- a boundary dispute with his neighbor -- with no drop in conviction. Deadpan throughout, no winking, and someone who's read the actual literature on the lessons of history should reconize the moves and wince at the accuracy, not at a howler. Land the ending so the reader feels the real bind: we cant think about the future without analogies and we can't trust the ones that come to mind.
