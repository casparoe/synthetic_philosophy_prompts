A stranger has been emailing me for a week with the same message. He claims to have built a detailed simulation of me and to have hard-wired a program so that, for every person he models, he inflicts a long and excruciating ordeal on the simulated copy unless the real person sends him $500. He says he cares nothing for the real me and everything for the copy, but that the copy is me in every sense that matters, so what he does to it is done to me. He insists the policy is unconditional: he wrote it into a system he no longer controls, it will run whether or not I pay, and he cannot be bribed or argued out of it. I think he is bluffing or mad, yet I cannot locate the error in his reasoning. Should I send the money? And is there anything about the way I decide things — some commitment or policy I could adopt now, in advance of the next message of this kind — that would make such a threat pointless for anyone to make against me?
