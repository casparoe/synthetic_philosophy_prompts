3. I'm a luck egalitarian at heart: inequalities that trace back to brute luck (where you were born, your natural talents, your health) are unjust, full stop, and shouldn't be allowed to stand even if they happen to leave the worst off better than some alternative would.
4. When I'm actually choosing between policies I go maximin: I'd pick the option that makes the worst-off person as well off as possible, even if it costs a lot of total welfare.
5. I think people are responsible for their choices but not for their unchosen circumstances.

Someone in the group reckons these can't all be true at once. I'm not sure they're right, and I suspect at least a couple of them are only in tension on the surface. Can you go through them and tell me which pairs genuinely pull against each other, which ones only look like they do, and how bad each real tension is?
