Here's something thats been bugging me and I genuinely can't tell if I'm being paranoid or thinking straight.

I've been doing my family's history for about a year. The one big find -- that my great-great-grandmother was supposedly the daughter of a minor noble family back in the old country, which would make us technically sort of aristocratic -- rests on a single chain. My aunt told me. She got it from her mother, my grandma, who got it from an elderly cousin who died before I was born, who apparently got it from some parish register that nobody has laid eyes on since and that may not even exist. So it goes: me <- aunt <- grandma <- cousin <- register. Five links, one thread. I've never seen a single document. Everyone in the chain was sincere as far as I can tell, nobody stood to gain anything, and the story is internally consistent.

What I want to know is what I'm actually entitled to believe here, and on what basis. Do I need positive evidence that each person in the chain was reliable -- that my aunt isn't a confabulator, that grandma didn't embellish, that the cousin wasn't just repeating a family legend -- before I'm allowed to believe any of it? Because I have basically none of that, and if I demand it I seem to end up saying I can't believe anything I didn't see with my own eyes, which would wipe out most of what I know. Or am I allowed to just take the story at face value unless something positive counts against it? And if so, what do I do with the fact that a single chain is only as strong as its shakiest link -- that a long line of mostly-reliable people can still leave the whole thing pretty weak, and the weakest member bounds the rest rather than the strengths somehow averaging out?

Does it change anything if the cousin was known in the family as a bit of a yarn-spinner, but everything else he ever said checked out? Or if instead of one chain I had three unrelated aunts telling me the same story independently -- does that actually help, and how much?

I guess whats really under my skin is this: my whole life is built out of beliefs I got from other people, and I've verified almost none of them, yet I get by fine. Does that show the worry is overblown, or just that I've been lucky?
