Restate that debate in this vocabulary as faithfully as you can, then give me the residue report: what reproduces the philosophers' moves under other names, what survives only with distortion, and what has no counterpart at all. Test that hunch rather than flattering it. On the philosophy side, take seriously the cases that look hardest to translate: unbounded or infinite values, thresholds below which no quantity of a lesser good counts, the suggestion that credences beneath some floor are not meaningful probabilities, and the risk-aversion-over-moral-uncertainty response to fanaticism. For each piece of residue, say whether the gap marks something my framework cannot express or something the original claim was never clearly saying.
