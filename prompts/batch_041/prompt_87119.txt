Bit of a rambling one, sorry in advance. I'm a hospice nurse, and I read philosophy badly in my spare time, so what you're getting is the half-formed version. Here's the thing I keep bumping into. (1) My dad died two years ago and what got me wasn't grief exactly, it was that a whole class of facts about me quietly changed status. There were things about my childhood that only he and I both witnessed — nothing dramatic, just the actual version of what happend when I was eight and broke my arm, whether I really was as awful a teenager as my mother says. While he was alive those things were checkable. Now they're just my word. Nothing about me changed, but a chunk of my own past went from fact to story and I didn't feel it happen. (2) My wife knows things about me that I don't know — which of my anecdotes I've told a hundred times, what I was like at twenty-five before I quit drinking, the name of my first dog. If she dropped dead tomorrow, some of that doesn't get forgotten, it stops existing. So I can lose pieces of myself without anything happening to me, and I can't know in advance which pieces those are. (3) And the other direction: I catch myself doing my dad's exact throat-clear, I use his phrases, and I've started to think there's a version of him still running in my head, badly compressed, getting more wrong every year. I read a book years ago by Hofstadter, the Gödel Escher Bach guy, where he says something like this about his wife dying — that she was still partly going, in his own mind — and it stuck with me. My sister thinks I'm being morbid. I think I'm onto something.

So: is there a name for this? I know about the "extended mind" thing with notebooks and I've heard about couples who split memory between them, you remember birthdays, I remember faces — but those seem to be about capacity. The notebook makes me able to do the task; my wife is the one holding the birthdays. What I'm asking about feels stranger and more about identity. Specifically I want the name, if it exists, for the bit that has to do with single copies: some of what I am is stored in exactly one other person, so that my death and their death are not the same event, and a self can be partly destroyed by someone else dying and partly survive its own body. Has anyone named that? If the honest answer is "the nearest you've got is X, which is about beliefs, and Y, which is about memory, and Z, which is about mourning," that's fine, but then I want to know exactly where each of those stops fitting, and I want the hard cases adjudicated — is a diary a head? is my Facebook page part of me? does the copy in my wife's head count as me if she's got me substantially wrong? The label isn't what I'm after, the carving is; I don't want a reading list, I want to know what the thing is and where its edges are. If nothing established fits, coin something and tell me the cases it would and wouldn't cover. And please end with a line beginning "Verdict:" giving the single term you'd use, existing or invented.
