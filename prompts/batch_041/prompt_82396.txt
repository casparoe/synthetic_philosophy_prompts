Some neighbors and I set up an informal air-quality thing — we each bought a sensor, and any one of us can post an alert to the town group chat if we think somethings wrong. Last week one guy posted a red alert after his sensor spiked: school shut early, people panicked, the council sent an inspector out. Turned out his sensor was probably just glitching. Here's what's bugging me. If you ask any of us one at a time whether the sensors are good enough to justify that kind of alert, we'd all say no, wait for more readings. But the way it's set up it only takes one person being worried enough, and then it's out there, can't take it back. And he didn't do anything wrong — he followed the exact rule we all agreed on. So is there something off with the setup itself, or is that just the price of having any early warning at all? And if it is the setup, how do we fix it without making things so slow we miss a real leak?
