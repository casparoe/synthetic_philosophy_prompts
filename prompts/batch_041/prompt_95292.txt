ok so i want a pantoum about dreaming, and specifically about the fact that the only access you ever get to a dream is the memory you wake up holding, which got made at the moment you woke. theres two camps on this and i want the poem stuck between them. one says the dream just is the waking report — theres no experience you had while asleep that the report is about, so asking what the dream was realy like is like asking what someone was really thinking before they said a word. the other says fine, but what you wake up with presents itself as a memory of an experience, and you have no way to check it against anything, so either way its doing everything an experience does. the pantoums loop should carry that: lines have to come back changed, so the first time a line is about the dream, later its about the remembering, and later still about the telling. i also want one line addressed to a figure in the dream, and i never want that figure described outside the address, so the reader cant tell whether they were ever there. by the last stanza, when the opening lines return, the poem should have quietly shut the door on whether there was anything it was like to be in the dream — not by being vague, but because the form has made the inside and the outside the same loop. 16 to 20 lines, no title, and it should hold up as a poem even for someone whos never thought about any of this.
