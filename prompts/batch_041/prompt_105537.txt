Here is my opening move. You say the difference is whether a technique works through the user's reasons or around them. Now consider a cancellation flow I've started calling precommitment capture: before a user can unsubscribe, the app makes them type out the three reasons they originally signed up, then shows them their own words back. It doesn't lie, it doesn't hide the exit, and the user remains free to leave. But it works by conscripting the user's past self to argue down their present self, and you know in advance that the present self usually loses — that is the whole point of the design. On your own criterion that isn't addressing reasons; it's staging an intra-personal conflict and betting on the outcome. So either your criterion is wrong or precommitment capture is manipulation.
