I want to interrogate that line rather than just repeat it, but I keep getting tangled up. Which readings are platitudes, which are substantive and defensible, which definately overreach, and which one is probably what people mean when they use it to end an argument? I'm trying to avoid the boring food-availability-versus-entitlement binary, so finer-grained options would be great. And tell me which readings actually get the speaker his conclusion that drought is a distraction. No citations or reading lists needed—I'm not asking you to guess at sources, just to seperate the claims into a menu I can use in the piece.
