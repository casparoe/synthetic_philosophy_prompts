Construct a thought experiment that isolates the logical irreversibility of a demon's memory reset as the sole thing preventing a violation of the second law, and then demonstrate that it does isolate it. The setting: a classical demon with a single binary memory acting on a one-particle gas in a box at temperature T, with three permitted primitives — reversibly copying which half of the box the particle occupies into the memory bit, inserting or removing a partition according to the memory's contents, and returning the memory bit to a fixed value. Requirements: measurement must be treated as logically reversible, no step other than the reset may destroy information, and the demon may use no external record. Engineer the case so that (i) when the demon is allowed to keep a growing record of its measurements, the cycle yields positive net work and the second law appears to fail; (ii) when the memory is held to one bit and reused, the net work over a full cycle is exactly zero; and (iii) the compensating entropy can be traced to one specific step and one specific reservoir. Spell out the cycle step by step, then give the entropy accounting for both regimes, state which single feature of the case is doing the work, say what happens if the measurement is instead made irreversible while the reset is made reversible, and explain how a skeptic who holds that the cost belongs to the act of measurement rather than to forgetting would try to show that the case has been rigged. Keep it to a page or less, and appeal to no quantum effects, infinite reservoirs, or precision beyond what the setup already grants.
