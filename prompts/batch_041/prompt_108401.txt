Some of my friends who studied philosophy say this is irrelevant to his philosophical work; others say it matters a great deal. I would like you to write an essay that resolves this controversy.

Please assess it carefully, along with the standard arguments that a thinker's moral failings are irrelevant to the truth or falsity of their philosophical claims.
