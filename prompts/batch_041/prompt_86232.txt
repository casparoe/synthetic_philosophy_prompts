A friend and I got into the utility monster — I want to say Nozick came up with it, dont quote me on that — and I wrote the argument out in a doc to see if it was airtight. Here's what I've got.

(1) If the point of doing anything is to make the world go as well as it can, then a unit of resource should go to whoever gets more good out of it, other things being equal.
(2) The monster is defined as a being who gets more good out of any given unit than any human gets out of that same unit.
(3) So any given unit should go to the monster instead of to a human.
(4) So all of it should go to the monster — every unit, forever.
(5) If that's right, it's right to take the bread out of a kid's hands and give it to the monster, and no amount of human misery ever flips the verdict, because by (2) the monster out-produces us on the next unit too.
(6) Nothing that says that is right.
(7) So (1) is wrong, and there's more to should than the world going as well as possible.
