So the openness of the future doesn't by itself cost you bivalence.

Give me your credence in PRE-TRUTH, and then the decomposition -- the sub-questions it actually rides on, a rough number and one line of justification for each, and how they combine. Does that count as the future being "open" in the sense PRE-TRUTH denies, or is quantum indeterminism the wrong kind of unsettledness -- random, but still not-yet-true?
