Ok, I need a case for my metaethics reading group next week. We're doing the internalism/externalism thing about reasons — Williams-ish internalism (you have a reason only if a sound deliberative route leads there from your existing motivational set) vs Parfit-ish externalism (facts give reasons, no motivational route required). Everyone is sick of the gin and tonic, the petrol, the wife-beater and the future-Tuesday guy, so I need soemthing new: build me a scenario where the internalist has to say "no reason" and the externalist has to say "there is a reason."

Constraints, because otherwise it's too easy:

- Realistic and contemporary, ordinary people, no sci-fi, no brainwashing or outside manipulation of his desires. One or two paragraphs.
- The agent is fully informed, means-end coherent, no weakness of will. His practical reasoning is impeccable — whatever is off is upstream of it.
- The reason at stake can't be a moral reason or a reason concerning his own future well-being. Those are the cheap routes and I want the case to work with something else. (If what you land on is borderline moral, say so and explain what that does to the case.)
- Don't just stipulate that there's no deliberative route — make that fall out of an actual account of what a sound route is (imagination, self-criticism, whatever you think belongs in it). Then tell me how a Williams-style internalist would attack the case anyway, and whether that counter-move makes internalism unfalsifiable.

Then: state the verdict and the reasoning for each side, tell me which single feature of the case is doing the work and what happens if you remove it (does it just collapse back into the wife-beater case?), and whether the whole thing can be defused by the "the two sides mean different things by 'reason'" move.

And please separate what's actually settled here from what's genuinely contested among people who work on this. I'm going to say something out loud in the group based on this, so I don't want a contested reading of Williams or Parfit sold to me as the consensus.
