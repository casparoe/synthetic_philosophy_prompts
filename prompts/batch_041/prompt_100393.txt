On this picture, when someone says that a name or a natural kind term picks out the same thing across every possible world, what I hear is that the term is a pointer, fixed once by an initial assignment, and that predications about it can be necessary even though we arrived at them empirically, since we had to investigate the world to learn what the pointer was aimed at. A description, by contrast, is a query, and anything built out of queries is necessary only to the extent that the query's satisfaction conditions are.
