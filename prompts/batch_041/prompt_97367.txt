I'm drafting a paper and I'd like your comments on the notes below — mainly on whether the central distinction holds up, and where I've gone wrong.

The topic is HCH / factored cognition, and the move I want to make is that HCH is an idealization of deliberation, in roughly the sense that the frictionless plane is an idealization of a block sliding. The notes:

- Set-up I'm assuming: a question goes to a human, who may split it into subquestions and farm those out to other humans (or back to itself), each of whom may recurse further, and the answers get stitched back together into an answer to the original question. HCH is the fixed point of that process. Fine so far.
- Physics-style idealizations usually come with a knob you can turn continuously, with the ideal case sitting at the end of it — viscosity going to zero. So the ideal case is a limit of real systems, and you can ask how far off a real system is from it.
- I want to claim HCH is not that kind of idealization. The knob you'd turn is time/compute per node, and turning it up doesn't take you toward HCH — it changes what the node is doing, rather than just removing noise. More importantly, HCH builds in an assumption that the decomposition is faithful: that the subquestions really do compose back into the original question. But that faithfulness is exactly the thing factored cognition is supposed to be worried about. So the idealization doesn't bracket a nuisance, it brackets the phenomenon.
- The alternative I'm sketching: a two-parameter idealization — decomposition depth, and a "semantic slack" term for how much meaning leaks when you split a question. HCH is the corner where slack is zero. Then the interesting question is what happens as depth grows with slack small but fixed, and I suspect the answer isn't continuous in the way people assume.

What I want from you: (1) is the frictionless-plane contrast the right one, or is there a better analogy for what HCH is actually doing; (2) is the "brackets the phenomenon, not the nuisance" objection right about HCH, or does HCH survive it somehow; (3) is the two-knob picture doing any real work, or is it just relabelling; (4) which of these claims is weakest, and what would a defender of HCH say to the strongest version of my objection. Skip citations and reading lists — I dont need them right now, I just want the argument pressure-tested.
