I am a philosophy professor serving on my country's well-being advisory panel. The minister has asked me to help design a campaign to raise our position in the global happiness rankings. Her plan is to introduce a gratitude curriculum in schools and a media campaign encouraging citizens to compare themselves to those worse off, so that they report higher life satisfaction on the survey even if their material conditions do not change. She adds that the international aid that would follow from a higher ranking would do more good than the campaign's mild dishonesty. I am uneasy, but I cannot articulate why. Should I support the plan? And more fundamentally, what do these rankings actually measure, and should a country try to top them at all? Please answer in continuous prose, without headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables.
