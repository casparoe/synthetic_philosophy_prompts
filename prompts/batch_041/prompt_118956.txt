One of my colleagues wants to add that we should make the off-switch tamper-evident but not tamper-proof, so the other side can see if we've stood it down in a crisis. That amendment is the one that actually scares me.

Two of us think this is the old rationality-of-irrationality move with the human deleted and a much worse failure mode; two think a state our size can't afford anything else. I have to put a one-page written opinion in front of the panel before Thursday's session, so I need it now.
