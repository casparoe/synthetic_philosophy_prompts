I'm a philosopher and I run a small grad seminar on fundementality — six students, one of whom thinks every question is secretly a question about sets, another who won't stop asking whether the fundamental things are *really* fundamental — and every year we hit the same wall in week 7, I give a hand-wavey answer, and we move on. I'd like to actually nail it this time, and I want you to take a position rather than hand me a menu.

Here's the wall. Fine, we more or less agree that some facts are grounded in others, that grounding goes one way and not the other, that it doesn't loop back on itself, and that it bottoms out somewhere. Now ask about the grounding facts themselves. Take the fact that the glass's being fragile is grounded in its molecular structure — that fact, the grounding fact, is itself a fact. Is it grounded? Say no, and you've got facts that just sit there unexplained, which is awkward when grounding was supposed to be the thing that leaves nothing dangling. Say yes, and the fashionable answer is that it's grounded in the natures (essences, call them what you like) of the things involved: it's part of what the structure is that it makes the glass fragile, so the grounding fact is settled by those natures and the buck stops there, because natures aren't the sort of thing that get grounded. Two things bother me, and this is what I actually want explained. First, if the grounding fact is grounded in the glass's nature, that nature has to mention the molecular structure — so the nature depends on what else there is, and a nature that's hostage to the world's inventory doesn't look like bedrock at all. Second, if grounding facts are grounded in natures, there's a grounding fact about *that* (the fact that the nature grounds the fragility fact) which needs grounding too, and if you answer it the same way the nature now has to contain that fact, and that one, and so on — so you either get a regress or a nature with infinitely much packed into it. Is an infinitely rich nature coherent? Is it fundamental? Has the regress stopped, or just moved house?

So: explain in plain terms what's really at stake in all this — why anyone should care whether grounding facts are grounded — and then give me your verdict on how to handle it (bruteness, natures, infinite natures, something else), with your reasons and a flag on where your answer is weakest. Don't hedge into "there are sevral views here"; I've read the views, I want yours. And no citations — I'm not asking you to point me at papers.
