Ok so I'm a novelist, mid-list, you've never heard of me, and I've got a problem I can't think my way out of. I've been offered a two-month residency at a university in China and the paperwork is making my stomach hurt. Scan of the passport, ten years of social media handles, bank details, every country I've visited since 2010, and a line I have to sign promising to cooperate with any lawful inquiry. Oh and a clause letting them share it all with partner institutions, whoever the hell they are.

My agent says everybody signs this, it's just bureaucracy, stop being difficult. My German friend, a translator, very exact about everything, is genuinely appalled — she says where she's from half that list would be flat out illegal to collect, and the whole point of the law there is that the state has to justify why it needs each scrap, not the other way round. My American editor thinks I'm being precious, says if you've got nothing to hide then what's the problem, which I hate, I hate that argument, but he's also not completely wrong that total opacity has its own price, I've watched people use the word privacy to mean don't hold me accountable for anything. And a Chinese novelist I correspond with just shrugs — says this form is nothing, that everyone back home lives inside some kind of score and eventually you stop noticing.

So I'm stuck. Here's the one idea I keep circling and I can't tell if it's clever or just cowardly. Instead of refusing or complying cleanly, I submit everything but make it slightly... fuzzy. Old handles that route to dead inboxes. A birthdate off by a day. Nothing that's exactly a lie, just noise. My translator friend says that's worse than lying, that it corrodes trust from the inside and I'd be doing the collection's work for it. Maybe she's right, maybe she's just being German about it.

Should I go? Sign it straight, refuse, or do the fuzzy thing? And underneath all of it — whats the actual principle? I can feel there's a real question here about how much of myself the state is entitled to know, and every time I try to state it cleanly I lose the thread. I want advice I can actually act on by Friday.
