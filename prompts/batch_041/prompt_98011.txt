Two colleagues have argued about this for a year: one holds that the simmball level picks out genuine causes, so that citing a symbol is citing something that does real causal work on the simms; the other holds that only the simms push anything, and that simmball talk is a useful compression with no causal power of its own. Draft the joint statement they could both sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration. I want:

- for each crux, a case, manipulation, or argument both sides agree would bear on it, with each side's prediction written down in advance;
- and, if any part of the dispute turns out to be verbal, which part and why.

Format it as a numbered agreed-claims section followed by a disagreements table.
