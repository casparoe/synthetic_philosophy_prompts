The careenium thought experiment (Hofstadter, I think — the "who shoves whom around inside the careenium?" paper, or maybe it's in the Strange Loop book) goes like this: a big hollow sphere with a huge number of tiny magnetic balls inside it, the simms, careening around. The exam question was: "Who shoves whom around inside the careenium? Do the simmballs push the simms around, or is it the other way, or is the question ill-posed? "The question looks like a dilemma but it isn't one, because both descriptions are descriptions of the same thing. Up at the top, though, the simmballs are what the system is about. No single sim stands for anything, but a clump that reliably comes apart when a certain feature of the outside world changes is tracking that feature, and a tracking clump is a symbol.
