Terms like providence, teleology, and the cunning of reason are not part of the vocabulary I actually think in, which is why I'd like your help with something more specific than an explanation.

Take the providential or teleological position in what you regard as its strongest form — the claim that history is not merely changing but directed, that the direction is toward something like justice or freedom, and that this is a fact about the structure of historical processes rather than a hope held about them — and restate it as faithfully as you can entirely in my vocabulary. What does providence become when it is written as a property of a stochastic process?
