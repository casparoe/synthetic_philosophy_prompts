ok here's my position sheet for a transformative-experience reading group and I want a sanity check on whether it actually hangs together before I present it. (1) Some choices are epistemically transformative: you can't know what the experience is like until you've had it, and no amount of testimony from people who've been through it closes the gap. (2) For those choices there's no fact of the matter, accessible to you beforehand, about which option is better for you -- the value only exists from the far side, once you're the kind of person who can see it. (3) It's still sometimes rational to make those choices anyway. (4) What makes it rational isn't weighing expected outcomes (you can't) but deciding on the basis of whether you value undergoing the transformation itself, the revelatory value, rather than the imagined value of the end state. (5) A transformative choice is authentic only if it flows out of the values you already have and isn't just outsourced to what other people tell you it's like. (6) I also think the transformation doesn't have to be sensory -- coming to grasp a genuinely new concept, or starting to see a proof as beautiful when you used to see it as symbol-shuffling, is transformative in the same sense as tasting a new flavor. (7) And whether a given experience counts as transformative at all depends on the conceptual repertoire you already bring, so the same event can be transformative for one person and a total non-event for another.

I have a feeling (4) and (5) can't both be true, though I can't tell if that's real or if I'm just confusing myself. And (2) and (3) look like they clash but maybe they're fine. Go through the whole set and tell me which pairs genuinely pull against each other and how badly, which ones are only apparently in tension and why they dissolve, and for each real clash what the cheapest fix would be and what I'd have to give up to get it. Rank the real tensions by severity instead of listing them all as equally bad, and then tell me which single revision would clean up the most. Don't flag everything -- some of these are definately compatible and I'd like you to say so.
