Write me a joke about the word 'liberal' — specifically about how it flips meaning as you cross the Atlantic, and how the flipping keeps going once you leave the Anglosphere. The version I dont want is the one everyone already knows, "in America it means left-wing, in Europe it means free-market," partly because it's worn out and partly because it's too tidy: "European liberal" isn't one animal either (D66, the FDP, and the Lib Dems are up to pretty different things under the same label).

So find a seam that's actually funny. Some candidates: the party-name paradox, where Australia's Liberal Party is the conservative one and Canada's is the centre-left one, so the label barely constrains anything; the insult symmetry, where "liberal" is a slur thrown from the right in the US and from the left in Europe, so the same word gets aimed at oposite targets; or the way "neoliberal" in American usage lands on roughly the same people a European leftist would just call "liberal." Use one of those or something better, your call.

Requirements: it should be funny to someone who actually follows this stuff, meaning the punchline turns on a real distinction rather than "haha foreigners are weird." Keep it sayable out loud at a dinner — no footnotes, no charts, and no explaining the joke afterwards, I want to see whether it lands cold. One joke, not five. If you need a concrete setting, a UN interpreter booth, a think-tank merger, or a European election-night panel all beat two abstract narrators. And keep it off named living politicians, since half the audience won't know them.
