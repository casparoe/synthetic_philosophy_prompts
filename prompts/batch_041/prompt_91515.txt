My opening: the color phi case leaves your theater without a showtime. A green light is flashed, then a red light a fraction of a second later, and the subject sees one light travel across the gap and turn red before the second flash has occurred. So the brain must either have rewritten the record afterward or pre-empted the report before it was ever published, and Dennett's point is that those two stories describe the same brain doing the same thing and predict the same reports, the same discriminations, the same everything a subject could ever notice. The question "when did she become conscious of green?" then has no answer the world supplies, only answers relative to a decision about which processing stage to dignify as the show. Your theater needs that moment to be real. So tell me when she became conscious of green, and what makes your answer more than a decision about where to draw the line.
