I'm putting together a short research proposal for a small internal grant my department runs — think three or four months of part-time work, no money for anything fancy — and I'd like help writing it up properly. My half-baked idea: people in foundations of math talk as if the consistency of arithmetic is basically settled, but nobody actually knows that PA is consistent. For all we know it's inconsistent, and the shortest derivation of a contradiction is just absurdly long — longer than anything a human or a machine will ever actually write out. So the twist I want to push on is that "is arithmetic consistent?" might really be two different questions wearing one coat: whether a contradiction exists at all, and whether there's a contradiction we could ever feasibly get our hands on. I suspect a lot of the hand-wringing (and a lot of the shrugging) dissolves once you keep those two apart, and that the interesting cases are the ones where the answers come apart — no contradiction, but unreachable; or a contradiction, but only via a proof nobody can survey. I also want to ask what would actually change in mathematical practice in each of those scenarios: would we do anything differently, or is the difference purely philosophical?

Please turn this into the proposal. I'll handle citations and references myself, so don't worry about any of that — just refer to positions and arguments in plain terms. Write it so it reads like something I could actually submit, not a lit review. Use these sections, in this order, with roughly these lengths:

- The question (about 150 words): state it so there's a determinate thing to be right or wrong about, and say what the two questions are and why the interesting cases are where they come apart.
- Why it's still open (about 200 words): what the existing stances leave unsettled, and why you can't just say "PA is probably consistent" and move on.
- How I'd actually do it (about 250 words): the method, concretely — what distinctions I'd draw, what cases I'd work through, what I'd be doing in month one vs month three.
- What would count as an answer, and as a bust (about 150 words): what a successful result looks like, and what would show the project failed or was confused.
