For about a year now I've been tinkering with a small home-automation setup — a rule engine that watches a handful of sensors and decides when to switch lights and heaters on and off — and I've run into a cluster of difficulties that feel as though they ought to be one difficulty, though I can't find a name for it or even decide whether it's one thing or three. The first is that whenever anything changes, I have to tell the system by hand which of its other commitments are now out of date. If the front door opens, I want it to conclude that somebody is home, but I do not want it to conclude that the porch light is broken, that the cat has moved, or that the time is still what it was a moment ago; nothing in the system tells it which of the thousands of facts it is holding should be carried over untouched and which should be reconsidered, so every new sensor adds labour to every rule I have already written. The second is that I can never state when a rule is supposed to fire without listing an absurd number of conditions — heat the room if it is cold, provided the window is shut, provided nobody has disabled the system, provided there is power, provided the sensor has not jammed — and the list never ends, because I keep discovering fresh exceptions only after something has gone wrong. The third is that any action has consequences I did not ask for and did not foresee: the heater raises the temperature, which shuts off the cooling, which changes the humidity reading, which triggers another rule, and so on, and I learn about it only by watching it happen.

When I described all this to a friend who read philosophy at university, she said it had a name and that people had been worrying about it for decades, but she could not for the life of her remember what it was called, and now it is nagging at me. What I want is not merely the label. Is there an established name for this family of troubles, and is it genuinely one trouble or several that have been run together under a single heading? If there is a canonical name, which of my three headaches does it properly cover, and what are the others called — are they treated as distinct problems, or as facets of the same one? I would also like to know how far the thing is meant to reach: is it a quirk of programs that plan and act, or does the same issue arise for ordinary human belief revision, where we too seem to know, without being told, which of our convictions survive a change and which do not? Please be concrete about the boundaries. What exactly does the phenomenon consist in, what is it not — my instinct is that it is not simply the difficulty of reasoning from incomplete information, but I would like to hear why or why not — and is there a standard term for the part of it I described with the endless list of conditions, which feels quite different from the other two even though it keeps being bundled with them?
