In 1998 the legislature passed the Clean Streams Act, which says no person shall discharge "pollutants" into "waters of the state," and then says those two terms "have the meanings given in the Rivers Protection Act of 1972." My opening move: you textualists say the 1998 Act froze those definitions as they stood in 1972, so this discharge isn't covered.
