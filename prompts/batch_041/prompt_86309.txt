The six:

1. A hospital administrator rolls out a scheduling algorithm purely to cut overtime; it (a) causes a few missed diagnoses / (b) catches a few early.
2. A city planner reroutes a highway to save money; it (a) bulldozes a working-class neighborhood / (b) brings that neighborhood a windfall.
3. A teacher grades on a hard curve to save time; it (a) crushes a shy student / (b) pushes a coasting student to improve.
4. An engineer ships a feature to hit a deadline; it (a) leaks user data / (b) makes the app usable for blind users.
5. A landlord installs smart meters to cut his own bills; it (a) lets a data broker infer a tenant's immigration status / (b) lowers tenants' bills.
6. A lab PI runs a study mainly to renew a grant; the result (a) torpedoes a rival's life work / (b) confirms a rival's theory.

Say where that bites in the ranking, and whether any of the six are so entangled with it that they'd be confounded.
