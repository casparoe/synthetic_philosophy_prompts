Write a deadpan internal FAQ for the new Bureau of Anthropic Affairs, which has started using the Self-Indication Assumption (the idea that your own existence is evidence there are many observers, so bigger universes are more likely) for all its population projections. Its for confused new hires: how to fill out Schedule SIA-1, why the fact that I exist is supposed to be evidence that the universe is enormous, why the Self-Sampling people in the next cubicle keep filing grievances, what to tell a presumptuous philosopher who wants a bigger office because his existence makes the building more likely to be huge, and how to process a Doomsday-argument whistleblower. Around 600 words, numbered Q&As, straight-faced. The jokes should come from getting the anthropics right — reference classes, the SSA/SIA split, sleeping-beauty-style puzzles, the fact that the presumptuous philosopher's conclusion is meant to be a reductio — so someone who knows the literature laughs rather than winces, and someone who doesn't can still follow the absurdity.
