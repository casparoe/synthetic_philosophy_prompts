I would like this audited rather than argued at the level of intuitions. Give me the correspondence table first — settlor (the party who fixes the terms), trustee, beneficiary, corpus, income — and then say which slots have no honest occupant and what the metaphor quietly imports from trust law that the intergenerational case lacks.
