I say multiple realizability, she says multiple realizability of the function doesn't buy you multiple realizability of the feel, I say she's sneaking in a preference for biology, she says I'm mistaking a description of behavior for the behavior. Around and around.

My friend says the second one might have a someone home and the first definately doesn't. I say: fine, but what is the second one doing that the first isn't, other than being the same computation described at a finer grain of detail? Because if that's all "causal structure" amounts to, then any functionalist already grants it, and it isn't a rival to my view, it's a footnote to it. She thinks that's a dodge. For what it's worth we get the same stalemate when the example is a big language model that passes every behavioral test we can throw at it — she says no one's home, I say how do you know that's a fact about the system rather than your policy for attributing minds.
