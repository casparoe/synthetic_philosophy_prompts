Everyone in this literature tells me the arrow of time is explained by the Past Hypothesis -- the claim that the universe simply began in a staggeringly low-entropy state, and that special initial condition is what grounds the second law and all our other arrows (memory, radiation, whatever). Fine. But I keep going back to the old attacks on Boltzmann. Loschmidt's point: take a gas, let it evolve, then flip every velocity -- the microscopic dynamics are time-symmetric, so entropy has to come back down again, which means you can't derive a one-way law out of reversible mechanics. Then Zermelo added the recurrence worry: wait long enough and the system returns arbitrarily close to its starting configuration, so entropy has to dip again eventually, given enough time. Both of those were aimed at killing the idea that you could prove entropy must increase.
