I'm in the final stretch of a distributive justice course, and the seminar has been working through Nozick. I've been trying to write down my own position rather than just report his, and before I commit it to a term paper I'd like an audit of whether the parts actually hang together. Here is roughly where I've landed. First, I take justice in holdings to be historical: whether a distribution is just depends on how it came about, through just acquisition and voluntary transfer, and not on whether it matches any preferred shape. Second, I think the Wilt Chamberlain argument is sound — begin from any favored pattern, let people trade freely, and the pattern will be upset; holding it in place would mean continually interfering with what people choose to do with what is theirs. Third, I accept full self-ownership: people have the same full rights over their bodies, their labor, and the fruits of that labor that they would have over any other piece of property. Fourth, I think a just society must guarantee everyone a decent minimum, funded by taxation if necessary, and that this is a requirement of justice rather than of charity. Fifth, I think that where present holdings can be traced to unjust acquisition, those who hold them can be required to give them up, even when the original injustice is generations back and the current holders had nothing to do with it.

I suspect some of these pull against each other and some only look like they do, and I'm not confident I can tell which is which on my own. So please sort them for me: which pairs are genuinely in tension, how severe each tension is, and for each one what exactly would have to give and what that concession would cost — ranked from most to least serious rather than all flagged as equally bad. For any pair you think is only apparently in tension, say why the appearance is misleading. And since I'll be defending this in front of people who know the literature far better than I do, please be careful to mark off the places where the disagreement is a live one among specialists from the places where everyone who works on this actually agrees; don't hand me a contested reading of Nozick as though it were the standard one. If the cleanest repair is to weaken or restate one of the five rather than abandon it, give me the restatement, not just the verdict.
