I'm not a philosopher — I did a bit of economics at uni and now I work with data — but a friend of mine reads a lot of animal-welfare stuff and we've been going back and forth about insects for months, and there's a move in her argument that I can't find a name for. Her version goes like this: nobody knows whether a beetle or an ant has experiences; maybe the odds are one in a thousand, maybe one in a hundred thousand, who cares; but there are something like ten quintillion insects alive at any moment, so even at ridiculous odds the total amount of experience that might be going well or badly is bigger than everything that happens to every human being on earth put together. And when I try to get out of it by lowering the odds, it doesn't help — she just says you'd have to push them down to one in a billion before the arithmetic stops favouring her, and nobody has any idea whether they're that low. That's the part I can't get past. It feels like an arguement that can't lose, because the smaller the chance of any given insect mattering, the more enormous the number it gets multiplied by.

What really bothers me is that I can build the same machine for things neither of us believes in. There's presumably some tiny chance that the dust in this room, or my houseplant, or the thermostat, has something it's like to be, and there are a great many dust motes and thermostats. She says that's obviously silly and I agree, but 'the odds are much lower' can't be the whole answer, because if it were, the whole thing would just be a dial you turn until it gives you the answer you already wanted. So: is there a name for the legitimate version of this pattern, and for the thing that separates it from the dust version? I've heard 'Pascal's mugging' and I know people talk about the sheer scale of insect life, but neither of those is what I'm after — I want the actual line, not the neighbourhood. Is it that the odds here are about a whole kind of creature rather than a one-off event? That what's at stake is a life going well or badly rather than some arbitrary prize? That the insect claim can be checked against what we know about nervous systems while the dust claim can't be checked against anything? Any of those might be it and I suspect it's some combination, so please don't just hand me a label. If there's an established term, tell me exactly what it covers and where it stops covering; if there isn't one, coin one, define it, and tell me about the borderline cases where you genuinely can't say whether it applies.
