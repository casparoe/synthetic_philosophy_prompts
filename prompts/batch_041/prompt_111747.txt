(1) A strategy is strictly dominated if there's another strategy, possibly a mix, that does strictly better against everything the other players might do. (4) In the game he showed me, this process deletes every pure strategy and leaves only one mixed strategy, M. (5) So common knowledge of rationality implies that a player must play M. (8) So rationality would require you to do something you can't do. (9) Therefore something is wrong with the whole iterated-elimination idea when mixed strategies are allowed.
