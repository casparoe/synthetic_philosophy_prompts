Please write a joke whose punchline turns on the actual mechanics of Molinism rather than on generic God-and-free-will banter. Assume an audience that already knows the doctrine, so you may presuppose the standard apparatus: Luis de Molina's threefold division of divine knowledge into natural, middle, and free knowledge; the claim that God's middle knowledge ranges over counterfactuals of creaturely freedom — what any possible free creature would freely choose in any set of circumstances — and that these counterfactuals are true logically prior to, and independently of, any divine decree; and the resulting picture on which God, without determining anyone's choice, arranges the circumstances so that the free choices He foresaw together compose the history He intends.

A good punchline should turn on one of the real pressure points rather than merely name-dropping Molina. The most promising candidates: the way the scheme hands the creature the metaphysical credit while the counterfactual does all the causal work; the grounding objection, i.e., the awkwardness of holding that a conditional about a free choice has a determinate truth-value when nothing — not God, not the creature — is obviously what makes it true; or the way that the reassurance, God knew you would freely do it and then arranged for you to be in exactly those circumstances, keeps sliding toward the very determinism Molinism was invented to escape. Please do not deliver a how-many-Jesuits-does-it-take template or a generic nerd joke with a theological label stuck on it; if the joke survives with the word Molinism deleted, it has failed.

Commit to a single joke rather than offering several variants, and make definite choices about setting, characters, and phrasing. After the joke, add two or three sentences stating which construal of middle knowledge the joke assumes and where it takes liberties with the doctrine — in particular, whether you are treating the counterfactuals of freedom as having a ground (and if so, in what) or as ungrounded, since the joke lands differently depending on that choice and I want to know which one you picked.
