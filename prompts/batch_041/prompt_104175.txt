Here is the summary. "Freud's view is that religion is a delusion. By this he means a belief that is false and that is held with certainty even though the evidence goes against it. His argument is simple: religious beliefs — that there is a father in heaven, that death is not the end, that the universe is governed by a benevolent mind — are all fulfillments of our wishes. We want these things to be true, so we invent them and then pretend that we have always known them. Once you see that a belief comes from a wish, the belief is explained away, and you know that it is false. Freud also compares religion to obsessional neurosis, arguing that religious rituals are compulsive behaviors and that religious people are, in effect, mentally ill, which is why he thought a properly psychoanalyzed society would outgrow religion.
