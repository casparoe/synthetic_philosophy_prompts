- the regression one: pick a variable because it correlated with what you care about in a noisy sample, then select on it, and of course it drifts back toward the middle

- the coordination one: publishing a number makes it a focal point even with zero incentives attached to it
- the deflationary one: its just "proxies are imperfect" plus "people respond to incentives" stapled together, and there's no single law here at all
