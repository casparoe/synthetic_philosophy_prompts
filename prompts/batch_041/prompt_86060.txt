Two groups in our org shipped basically the same caching layer within about a week of each other, and now there's a fight over who gets credit in the promo packet, which has somehow turned into an argument about the calculus fight and about Darwin and Wallace. I got nerd-sniped. What I want from you is a decision tree or taxonomy of the positions people actually hold on two linked questions: whether a discovery is the sort of thing that was going to happen anyway once the field was ready, and whether it matters for credit who got there first. Please make it an instrument rather than an essay about your own view. Every branching point should be a question about a concrete case, phrased so one of my coworkers can answer it without already knowing the name of the view they're about to land on, and the whole point is to sort people by what they say about cases rather than by what they call themselves, so no node should just ask whether genius matters or whether discovery is inevitable. The cases I care most about are the ones that make the tidy stories fall apart, and I'd rather you build around these than around the famous examples.
