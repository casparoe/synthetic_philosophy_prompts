I teach a graduate seminar on the ethics of the Anthropocene, and I want to push one question past the level of slogans. Take the following proposition, W: For a representative hectare of species-rich, lightly managed temperate grassland, the expected total suffering occurring on that hectare over a 150-year horizon — counted as sentience-weighted organism-years, under any per-taxon weighting you are willing to specify explicitly — is net negative; and converting that hectare to a mown solar array lowers that total.

Give me your credence in W, but deliver it as a decomposition rather than a bare number. I want each of the following as a distinct term, each with a probability and a single line of justification: (i) the probability that such a grassland's animal population is dominated, numerically and in sentience-weight, by short-lived, r-selected invertebrates whose deaths are predominantly starvation or parasitism rather than predation; (ii) the probability that destroying the habitat reduces total suffering on the site rather than displacing the same cohort of lives into adjacent habitat through density-dependent spillover; (iii) the probability that the low-wildlife solar array, with its periodic mowing and bare ground, is genuinely suffering-poor rather than merely organism-poor; (iv) the probability that the accounting horizon is long enough for succession, novel-ecosystem formation, or recolonization to reverse the sign; and (v) the probability that the per-taxon sentience weighting you adopt is not doing most of the work. State how these terms combine into your final figure, and identify which term the figure is most sensitive to.

Then give me the credence you would hold if the horizon were extended to a thousand years, and name the single observation — one that cannot be made with present data — that would move your number most. A colleague puts W at 0.15 and I put it at 0.65; tell me which term best accounts for the gap between us. Argue from first principles; I am not asking for a literature review, and I would rather have your honest uncertainty than a survey of positions.
