I'm a graduate student in philosophy working on Bayesian epistemology, and I've been stuck on the Judy Benjamin problem. Here's the setup, in case you want to work with my numbers: Judy is a paratrooper who has been dropped somewhere in a territory divided between Red and Blue forces. She thinks she's probably in Blue: her prior is P(Red) = 1/4, P(Blue) = 3/4. Conditional on being in Red, she thinks it's 50/50 between the Red HQ area and the Red Second Company area, so P(HQ) = 1/8, P(Second) = 1/8. She then gets a radio message from someone she trusts: "If you're in Red territory, the odds are 3 to 1 that you're in the HQ area rather than the Second Company area." She learns nothing else -- no categorical fact about where she is. The question is how she should update. Conditionalization can't apply directly, because she hasn't learned a proposition. The standard proposal is to minimize relative entropy (Kullback-Leibler divergence) from her prior subject to the constraint that P(HQ | Red) = 3/4. That gives P(HQ) = 3/16, P(Second) = 1/16, P(Blue) = 3/4, so P(Red) = 1/4 still. Her unconditional probability of being in Red doesn't budge.

I want to practice defending this "minimize relative entropy" rule against a sharp critic. Please play that critic: someone who thinks infomin is not the right general rule for updating on conditional evidence, who has real objections rather than strawmen, who will concede small points but never the main one, and who stays in character until I say "stop." Keep your replies to a paragraph or so, so it feels like a conversation. Here's my opening: You critics keep saying Judy's probability of being in Red should go up. But what proposition did she learn that raises it? She learned only about the internal composition of Red territory -- that HQ is three times as likely as Second Company conditional on Red. That's compatible with her being in Blue with the same probability as before. If you think P(Red) should change, you must be treating the message itself as evidence about her location, but the message is just a fact about the layout, and she already knew the layout was some way or other. So what's the argument that her credence in Red should move?
