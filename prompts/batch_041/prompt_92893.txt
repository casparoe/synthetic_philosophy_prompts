ok so my friend and i got into it for like two hours last night about the reference class problem and im still annoyed. her line was that its not even a real problem — you just use the narrowest class that still has enough data to be stable. my line was thats circular, because "enough" and "stable" just mean whatever makes the number come out how you already wanted it, and anyway there's no narrowest class, you can always split me finer: me, me on tuesdays, me since the divorce, me after the second coffee. we did not settle it and i want to turn it into a poem.

give me a sestina. the reason i want a sestina and not something looser is that the whole thing is about the same person getting re-described as a member of a different group every time you look, and the form does that by itself if you set it up right: the six end-words should be six reference classes that i belong to, and the permutation should make it obvious that the "i" in each stanza is a slightly different statistical object even though it's the same me. make the six classes genuinely different in kind — one from family history, one from a habit, one from a test result, one from my birth year, one from how a doctor's office files me, and one that i can't even see about myself and only exists in someone else's spreadsheet. the envoi has to be the moment somebody actually has to pick one and act on it — the doctor has to hand me a number, or the insurer has to price me — and it should feel like a forced choice the poem cant really justify, not a cute resolution. please dont end it on "well its all just perspectives," thats exactly the cop-out we were arguing about; the numbers really do come out different and someone has to live with whichever one gets chosen, so if you can, land the last line on the one class that wins and make me feel the other five going quiet.

it should be accurate enough that someone who teaches this stuff wouldn't wince, but it also has to work as a poem for someone who's never heard of the problem — so no jargon dumps, let the form carry it.
