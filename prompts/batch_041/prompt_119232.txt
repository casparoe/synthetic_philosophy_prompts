I need one on the dogmatism paradox. Here is the puzzle as my reading group has been stating it: if S knows that P, then P is true; and if S knows that P, then S is in a position to know that any evidence against P is misleading, since P is true. So it seems S may rationally dismiss counter-evidence to P in advance. That looks like a license for dogmatism. Two factions in the group have been arguing past each other for months. The knowledge-first faction treats the paradox as a genuine consequence of knowledge's factivity and thinks the real work is to say how a knower should live with it; the evidence-responsivist faction treats it as a reductio of the idea that knowledge can license ignoring evidence, and locates the error in a premise about what follows from knowing that counter-evidence would be misleading. I want the document they should have written together, not a verdict on which faction is right.
