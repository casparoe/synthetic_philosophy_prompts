Two people in my reading group have spent months arguing past eachother about space colonization and I want to force them to write the document they should have written instead. Call them the Expansionist and the Restrainer. The Expansionist thinks we should get humans (or whatever we turn into) off Earth and spread as widely and as fast as we can; a universe with more minds in it, and with more than one home for civilization, seems to him obviously better, and he thinks staying small is the riskier bet. The Restrainer thinks going now is reckless: she says we'd freeze our current half-broken values into structures nobody could ever undo, that we've no business treating other worlds as ours to reshape, and that "more minds" only counts if those lives actually go well, which isn't something we can bank on.

Draft the joint statement they could both sign after an honest adversarial collaboration. I want two parts. (1) A numbered list of claims they both accept -- about what's really at stake, about what's unknown, about the moral status of places and of people who don't exist yet. (2) A table of the genuine disagreements, with each side's position worded the way that side would word it themselves. Columns: crux | the neutral way to state the question | the Expansionist's answer | the Restrainer's answer | the case, thought experiment or model both agree would bear on it | the Expansionist's prediction | the Restrainer's prediction. If part of their fight is really just about words -- e.g. what counts as "colonization", or whether they mean spreading minds or spreading a particular civilization -- say which part and why.

Two hinges I specifically want them to fight out, since I think that's where it turns: first, does a permanent off-world presence lower or raise the odds that civilization ends badly, and what would genuinely settle that? Second, the seed-ship case -- one probe that would fill the galaxy with copies of a single fixed, decent-enough society and nothing else, ever. Good or bad, and does the answer come out differently for each of them?
