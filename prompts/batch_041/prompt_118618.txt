Write a joke for the end of a seminar on whether dreams are experiences — the view that a dream is something you undergo in sleep and later recall, set against the view that "I dreamed that..." is no memory report at all but a way of narrating something in the past tense, so there need be no episode to have been experienced and no answer to when the dream occurred. Don't restage the debate in the abstract; build the joke around this scene, which is a commercial version of the cassette theory of dreams: a startup sells dreams to order — you name an adventure, sleep, and the device installs the entire coherent dream-memory at the instant you wake — and a customer who ordered a week-long sailing voyage wakes up demanding a refund. The punchline should turn on what each view says he did or didn't get for his money, and it should be funny to someone who already knows the dispute rather than being a generic sleep gag. After the joke, add a brief note on which reading of the two views it assumes and where it takes liberties with them, say how confident you are in that reading, and single out the point you're least sure of.
