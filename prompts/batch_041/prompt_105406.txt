Right, I've just got back from the pub after a four hour arguement with my mate Dan about whether the brain is a computer, and I need a joke to send him in the morning.

Here's where we got to. I said obviously it is — that's the whole point of cognitive science, neurons do information processing, mind is what the brain does. Dan said no, and his move was the one where anything at all comes out as a computer if you're generous enough with the mapping: he reckons his kettle implements a finite state machine, the weather implements a Turing machine, and if you're allowed a big enough lookup table then anything implements anything. So the brain "computes" only in the sense that you can redescribe it, and there's no fact of the matter about which computation it's running. Which I think is a swindle, because there's plainly a difference between the kettle and me, and at half eleven I couldnt say what it was, which is why I need the joke more than I need the argument.

So write me a joke about this. It has to actually turn on the content of the dispute — the hardware/software thing, the "everything is a computer under some description" move, the question of what the brain does that the kettle doesn't. Not a generic "my brain is a slow computer" gag, and not just "I need a reboot". It should be funny to someone who's actually read a bit of philosophy of mind, and someone overhearing it shouldn't come away thinking I've no idea what the debate is. Ideally it lands on Dan's side of the table without me having to give up my position. Dialogue is fine. Short is good. Don't explain the joke afterwards and don't tack a little moral on the end.
