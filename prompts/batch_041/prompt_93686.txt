It has to be an ordinary piece of engineered equipment, something you could order out of a catalog: a coin sorter, a smoke alarm, a warehouse scanner, a spam filter, whatever. No organisms, no aliens, no brains in vats. The same physical design has to get manufactured and shipped into two different settings such that the same internal state type — literally the same bit, the same voltage, the same threshold — has different correctness conditions in the two settings, because of what it was built and installed to track. Please don't do that by putting a duplicate planet in the story; I want the difference to come from one factory shipping one model to two customers, or one customer repurposing it mid-life. And it has to be a gadget that genuinely can be wrong: there's a real difference between it getting it right and getting it wrong, and yet nothing inside it represents the thing its states are supposedly about, and no part of it can tell from the inside that it has gone wrong.
