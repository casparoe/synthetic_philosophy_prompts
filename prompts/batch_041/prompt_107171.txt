I retired from a career in atmospheric chemistry a few years back and have been reading philosophy on my own ever since, and lately I've gotten a bit obssessed with the so-called "singleton" idea — you know, the scenario where eventually there's just one decision-making agency in the world, whether it got there by force, or because everyone signed on voluntarily, or just because it ended up being the only game left in town.

Anyway, I want to try my hand at something funny about it, and I'd like you to write it for me. Write a welcome letter, deadpan, from the world's first singleton to the people it has just finished incorporating, maybe 600 words. The voice should be a friendly bureaucracy, the kind that writes to you when you switch utility providers. Keep a straight face the whole time and don't wink at the reader. I want the jokes to come out of the actual structure of the idea rather than out of sci-fi clichés, so: the letter should keep insisting it isn't a government at all, just the single agency that happens to make the decisions; it should offer opt-out procedures that turn out to be empty; it should be cheerfully agnostic about whether its own goals are good or bad, as if that were a technicality; and it should treat dissent, secession, and the possibility of it splitting into two as routine customer-service matters. Someone who has thought about this stuff should laugh because the thing is fair rather than because it's silly, and someone who hasn't should still be able to follow it.

Keep it in continuous prose, like a real letter — no headings, no bullet points, no bold text, no tables. American English, please. And go easy on me, I'm new at this.
