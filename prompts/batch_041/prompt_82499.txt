And I keep wondering whether the residual is really the part where policy could make a difference, or whether its mostly noise from only interviewing about a thousand people per country — like, is slipping two or three places even a real change, or are we chasing sampling error?

Please act as an expert on the philosophy of social measurement and the economics of well-being, and think carefully before you answer.
