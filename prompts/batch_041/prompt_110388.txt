I've been trying to get straight in my head the two ways the AI thing is supposed to go wrong. One is the version where the system is quietly scheming for power the whole time and we just don't catch it. The other is the version where nobody's scheming at all — it just slowly stops caring about what we actually wanted, we keep adjusting around it bit by bit, and no one ever really sits down and decides anything. Write me a joke that turns on that diference. Not a generic "robot takes over" joke: the punchline has to come from the actual shape of the two cases — one of them hands you a bad guy and a moment where it all went wrong, the other hands you neither, and I have a nagging feeling that's the worse one. Someone who's read alot of this stuff should laugh, or at least not wince. Under 200 words, dark is fine.
