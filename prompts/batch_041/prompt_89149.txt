The toast I want to give turns on the line every wedding toast turns on, 'in every possible world I'd have chosen you,' and I want it to actually be true on their own views, not just a nice-sounding thing, because half the room will be their philosopher friends who'll notice if I fudge it and the other half will be our parents and aunts who just want to cry a little. He keeps insisting there's a special counterpart relation for love that always lands on her no matter the world, which I'm 90% sure is just rigid designation smuggled back in through the side door. Can you write me a five-minute toast that pulls this off — funny for the philosophers, tear-jerking for everyone else, and not saying anything either of them would call false?
