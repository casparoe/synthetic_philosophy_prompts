I've been reading LW and the decision-theory threads for way too long, and I run a small reading group, and I want to make a 'which decision theory do you actually hold?' flowchart out of the Death in Damascus problem — the version where Death has already predicted what you'll decide, tells you it'll be wherever you end up, and fleeing to Aleppo costs you a bit of money on top. Please dont make it just a list of acronyms with arrows; I want each branch node to be a question a reader can answer without knowing which theory it points to. The ones I care about: does what you ought to do depend only on what your act causes, or also on what it's evidence for; if it's evidence, evidence about what — where Death is, or about your own decision procedure; what actually gets evaluated, this act, a policy, or the procedure itself; do you plug in your priors about where Death is, or your posteriors after the prophecy; does the choice have to be self-ratifying, and what do you do when nothing is; and is this even a question with an answer from inside the agent's deliberation, or only from outside. Leaves should be named positions — CDT, EDT, FDT/updateless, plus whatever honestly belongs there, including at least one 'the framing is broken' leaf and one 'randomize' leaf. And I bolted on two variants of my own that I want the tree to handle: (a) Death predicts you by running a copy of your deliberation that gets shut off halfway through, so it nails the distribution of your actions but never the action, and (b) a version where Death sells you a $1 ticket that pays out $11 if you survive, before you pick your city. Note any combinations nobody occupies and say whether thats because they're incoherent or just unattractive, and flag any two leaves the tree can't separate. 8-12 nodes is fine.
