Okay so I fell down a rabbit hole on this Molinism thing — the idea that God doesn't just know what we're going to do, he knows what we *would* do in situations he never actually put us in. As I understand it, that's supposed to solve a real problem. If God knows ahead of time what I'll do, then it sounds like I couldn't have done otherwise, so it sounds like I'm not free. But if he doesn't know, he's not all-knowing. Middle knowledge is supposed to thread that needle: what God knows is what I would freely choose, so the freedom is built in from the start and nobody's stepping on anybody's toes.

The thing is, I wrote the reasoning out and it seems to eat itself.

(1) Middle knowledge means God knew, before he created anything, what each possible free creature would freely choose in every situation it might be placed in.
(2) So before I existed, God knew that if he placed me in the exact situation I was in last Tuesday, I would freely pocket the money.
(3) He placed me in that exact situation.
(4) So God knew, before I existed, that I would pocket the money.
(5) If God knew before I existed that I would do it, then it was alredy true before I existed that I would do it.
(6) If it was already true before I existed that I would do it, then by the time Tuesday came around it was settled, and there was nothing I could have done to make it come out the other way.
(7) If there was nothing I could have done to make it come out the other way, then I didn't do it freely.
(8) So I didn't pocket the money freely.
(9) Nothing in the argument leans on it being this choice — it runs the same way for anything anybody ever does.
(10) So middle knowledge makes every free choice unfree, which is the exact result it was invented to dodge.

I'd rather keep (1) than (8), and I'd rather keep (8) than throw out free will, so I definately don't want to give up the wrong one here. Where does this go wrong? Which step do I reject, why did it look airtight when I wrote it, and what does rejecting it commit me to? If it turns out nothing is wrong and I just have to pick between God knowing ahead of time and me being free, tell me that and tell me what I'm paying either way.
