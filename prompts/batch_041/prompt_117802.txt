I am drafting a position paper on bad faith and would like a consistency audit of the commitments I intend to defend before I commit to an outline.

(1) Consciousness is transparent to itself. Because of the pre-reflective cogito, nothing mental is hidden from the subject who has it, and there are no genuinely unconscious mental states of the Freudian sort.

(2) Bad faith is real and pervasive. The waiter who plays at being a waiter, the person who treats a fear as a fact about their temperament, the one who insists they simply are that way — these are not philosophical fictions but ordinary human postures, and I take their root to be the attitude that treats values as things already in the world rather than as something freedom produces.

(3) Bad faith is a form of self-deception: the person in bad faith hides from themselves something they know.

(4) Human beings are radically free. No fact about a person — desire, character, history, circumstance — determines what they do, and there are no excuses.

(5) Bad faith is itself a free choice for which the agent is responsible. I also read Sartre as holding that the concept is deliberately unstable: bad faith is not ordinary belief but a self-undermining attitude that cannot be held sincerely, so the usual logic of belief and deception does not straightforwardly apply to it.

(6) Sartrean freedom is freedom in a situation. The facts of my situation are real constraints, and being free does not mean I could have done anything.

Which of these cannot be held together? I have a nagging sense that at least two of them pull against each other, but I cannot tell which, and I may well be imagining conflicts that are not there. Please separate the pairs that are in genuine tension from those that only appear to be, order the genuine tensions by severity, and for each say what would have to give and what the cheapest repair would be. I would like you to be explicit about which apparent conflicts dissolve once the commitments are stated properly and why they dissolve. Finally, tell me which single revision would resolve the most.
