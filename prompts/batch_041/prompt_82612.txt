ok, I'm building a prompt set and I need one that just asks for a joke. Domain is superrationality — the Hofstadter thing, one-shot prisoner's dilemma where both sides reason identically, so if you'd defect then your counterpart (reasoning the same way) defects too, so you cooperate instead. I want a joke that actually lands for someone who knows this stuff, not the generic two-robots-walk-into-a-bar kind where you could swap in any other concept and nothing changes.

The bit I want it to turn on, because it's the part that always felt underexplored to me: superrationality seems to need the two players to be the same, and the second there's any asymmetry — one of them has one extra bit of info, slightly different priors, one of them stayed up later — the clean my-choice-is-your-choice move starts to fray, and you can get two superrational agents who both defect and both feel correct about it. So the joke should live on that seam. Set it somewhere dead mundane: two roomates deciding whether to take out the trash when nobody's watching, or two friends splitting a check, that kind of thing.

Couple rules. Don't have a character stop and explain decision theory to the audience, the funny has to come out of the situation itself. Punchline can be a line of dialogue or just the setup landing. 150 words max. And if your feeling generous give me two versions, one where the symmetry holds and one where it breaks, so I can see the difference.
