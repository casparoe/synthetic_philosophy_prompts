I want to work through a historical episode with the handicap principle in hand, and I would like your help doing it carefully rather than decoratively.

The episode is the collapse of the judicial ordeal in medieval Europe. For several centuries, guilt and innocence across much of Latin Christendom were settled by subjecting the accused to a physical test whose outcome was read as divine judgment: carrying a red-hot iron a fixed distance, or pulling a stone from boiling water, with the wound bandaged and inspected after three days; or being bound and thrown into cold water, where sinking was taken to acquit and floating to convict. The procedure was administered by the church — a priest blessed the iron, the water, and the accused — and it stood as one of the ordinary means of proof, alongside oath and witness. In 1215 the Fourth Lateran Council, under Innocent III, forbade priests to take part in it. Within a generation or two the ordeal had largely vanished from European law, and in England its disappearance is bound up with the rise of the jury, a transition historians still dispute. Take those facts as given; I want the analysis, not the historiography, and not a reading list.

Now the theory. Zahavi's handicap principle says a signal can be reliable because it is costly: the peacock's tail is honest not because peacocks are truthful but because only a bird in good condition can afford to grow one, so the cost is what keeps the signal correlated with the quality the receiver cares about. The economic tradition running from Spence treats costly signaling differently: the signal separates types because it is differentially costly — cheap for the high type, dear for the low one. Use both, and tell me where the difference between them matters for this case.

What I want is a mapping, and then a reckoning.

First, fix the correspondence. Who is the sender, who the receiver, what is the quality or fact the ordeal is supposed to make manifest, what exactly is the cost, who bears it, and by what mechanism is cost supposed to keep the signal honest? Be explicit about where the mapping strains. On the natural reading the cost falls on the accused; the fact at issue is a fact about a past act rather than a quality of the sender; and the being whose judgment is invoked bears no cost at all. Tell me whether that is a defect in my application of the theory or a discovery about it.

Second, the cost is not type-differentiated in the way either version needs: the innocent and the guilty face the same iron. So what supplies the correlation between cost and the fact signaled? If the answer is a shared belief that God adjudicates, then the honesty of the signal is underwritten by a background premise rather than by the cost, and the theory looks parasitic on something it does not itself provide. Is that fatal, or is there an amendment that saves it?

Third, distinguish costly to perform from costly to fake. The ordeal was grueling to undergo and cheap to rig, since the officiating priest controlled the reading. If the reliability came from an institution's management of belief, then the 1215 prohibition ought to be devastating — and it apparently was. Does the handicap principle have the resources to explain why the practice collapsed when the church withdrew, or does it simply presuppose the interpretive scheme whose disappearance needs explaining?

Fourth, take seriously the alternative readings — the ordeal as a device for terminating disputes and displacing responsibility from judges onto God, or as a lottery that induced settlement — and say what each does to a signaling interpretation. If the procedure was not transmitting information about the accused at all, is the handicap principle describing it, or redescribing it?

Finally, the verdict in the other direction: is this episode a confirmation of the theory, an anomaly, or a case it never anticipated? If the better account is not a signaling account but something else — institutional credibility, or conventions and epistemic authority — say so, and say what a signaling theorist would have to add in order to keep the case. Where the theory is silent, leave it silent rather than making it speak.
