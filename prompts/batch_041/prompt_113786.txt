My opening move. I am a radiology resident. My maxim is this: I will, when I am fourteen hours into a night shift and a study looks like one of the common normal variants, issue a signed preliminary report endorsing a diagnostic model's output without inspecting the images myself, in order to spend my remaining attention on the studies where my own reading is likely to make a difference. I claim universalization does not condemn it. The practice survives being universal: a signature still means that the report met the reporting standard, readers still calibrate accordingly, and nobody is deceived so long as the standard is public. Nor is there a contradiction in the will, because I retain every end I had and serve them better — the attention I save goes where it is worth more. Two things before you answer.
