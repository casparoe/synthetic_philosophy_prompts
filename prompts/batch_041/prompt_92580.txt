The novel's counterfactual hinge is the 1933 assassination of President-elect Roosevelt by Giuseppe Zangara — a single act by a marginal figure that the book treats as sufficient to produce an Axis victory, a partitioned America, and a 1962 in which a Nazi-Japanese cold war is simply the weather. I want a reading that holds both facts at once: the plot was generated by a chance procedure, and the plot is about whether history is chance or fate.
