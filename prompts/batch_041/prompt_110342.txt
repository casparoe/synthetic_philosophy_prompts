No precommitment possible, and whatever you do after the coin lands makes no causal difference to the heads branch.

Build me a flowchart from commitments a reader might already have to named positions. Leaves must include at least: causal decision theory, evidential decision theory, updateless/timeless decision theory, functional decision theory, the deflationary view that precommitment is the whole story and there's nothing left to say once you can't, and the view that the scenario is under-described or not a well-formed decision problem at all. Include one leaf for people who think the blackmail-style variants generalise and you should pay off acausal threats, and one for people who think the generalisation fails; if you're unsure of the canonical structure of those variants, say so rather than inventing details.
