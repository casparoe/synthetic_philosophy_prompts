ok so I'm trying to write something on Hume's economics and I want a coherence check on my own position before I go further, because these are views I actually hold, not just ones I'm reporting on. I don't need cites or a reading list, I need to know where the fault lines are.

Heres the package:

1. Money is a convention, not wealth. Coins and notes have no value in themselves; they're the grease, not the goods. In the long run, how much money is sloshing around a country tells you nothing about how rich it is.
2. But a fresh influx of money really can get things moving. It lands in some hands before prices have caught up, so for a stretch those people work harder, buy more, hire more, before the general price level climbs to meet it. So money isn't doing nothing during that stretch.
3. With open trade, money sorts itself out. A country that buys more than it sells loses money, its supply shrinks, its prices fall, its exports get cheap again, and the money comes back. So a country can't really be drained by trade, and mercantilist panic about money leaving the country is a mistake.
4. Which means trade deficits are basically nothing to worry about, and free trade is fine.
5. Interest rates aren't set by the quantity of money. You can have a country awash in silver with brutal rates and a country with hardly any coin with low rates. Rates track the real returns on offer, the state of industry and commerce, not the supply of tokens.
6. And normatively: hands off the currency. No debasing, no paper-money schemes, no big funded public debt. That's how a state rots from the inside.

My gut says the interesting friction is between 1 and 2, if money is a veil then how does an influx of it do anything real, though honestly I don't trust my gut here. A friend in my seminar insists 3 and 5 contradict each other, which I don't beleive for a second, but when she pushed me on why not I couldn't put it into words fast enough.

So: which of these actually pull against each other, which only look like they do, and how bad is each? Rank them by how much damage each one does, and for the real ones tell me which commitment has to give and what it costs me to give it up. And tell me whether 3 versus 5 is as fake as I think it is.
