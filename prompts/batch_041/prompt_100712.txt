I want the case to put pressure on that distinction, so maybe make the present version of her someone who is not in pain and seems, on the surface, content.
- Then a second version of the SAME case that changes exactly one thing, and in that version the two views agree. I want to see which one thing did it. And I want you to convince me it really is one thing and not three things wearing a trenchcoat.

What I'm actually trying to figure out with this is whether the "past self wins" view really depends on the person being literally the same person, or whether it's secretly about something else — like the earlier self having a kind of authority over a life she was still going to have to live.
