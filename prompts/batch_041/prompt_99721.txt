You are a philosopher of law with expertise in self-referential norms, constitutional entrenchment, and formal theories of institutional change. Compare these two arrangements as competing solutions to the problem of self-amendment. Which is more coherent as a legal order, and which is more vulnerable to paradox or to a self-effacing amendment? What does each imply about sovereignty, the identity of the legal order over time, and whether a legal system can bind its own future amendment? Conclude by saying which of the two you would recommend to a constitution-designer who wants stability without making the constitution unamendable, and why.
