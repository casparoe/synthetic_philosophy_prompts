You are a philosopher of the social sciences with expertise in the methodology of explaining conduct by appeal to values, and you have been asked to help two colleagues end a year-long stand-off. One is a causal culturalist: she holds that the ascetic Protestant ethic was an autonomous cause of the methodical, reinvesting economic conduct at issue in Weber's thesis — that the doctrine did work, rather than merely accompanying something else. The other is a materialist: he holds that explanations citing a value always resolve into interests, institutions or networks, and that the claim that culture is a cause is either unfalsifiable or a restatement of what needs explaining.

Think carefully before you write, and begin by mapping the conceptual terrain: what the thesis actually asserts and what it does not; whether citing a value is itself a causal claim or only the ascription of a reason; and what each side means by cause. Then draft the joint statement they should have produced instead of another round of polemic. It should contain (1) a numbered section of claims both can sign, covering the thesis, the evidence for it, and what would count as explaining the correlation; (2) a table of the residual disagreements, each formulated so that the side holding it would accept the wording; and (3) the cruxes — the sub-questions such that settling one would move at least one side — each paired with a case, comparison or natural experiment that both parties agree would bear on it, with each side's prediction recorded in advance. Both accept the following as a datum neither has yet explained: two regions matched on soil, market access, literacy and language, differing only in that one adopted an ascetic Protestant confession and the other an equally demanding but non-ascetic one, where a generation later the first shows markedly higher rates of saving and reinvestment among ordinary members, not only among its elite. State plainly which parts of the dispute are merely verbal, and identify any crux on which you judge neither side can presently be moved.
