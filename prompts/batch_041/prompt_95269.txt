Some years ago I came across an idea that I have not been able to put down. It was an imaginary setup: a vast arena, filled with an enormous number of identical little balls, all bouncing around according to simple mechanical rules. The balls themselves are mindless, but because of the way they are arranged and because of the rules they follow, there arise stable, recurring patterns in the churn. Some of these patterns are said to stand for other things, to carry information, to be symbols. The question that came with the setup was whether those symbols do any real pushing, or whether all the pushing is done by the balls and the symbol talk is just a convenient way of summarizing what the balls are up to. I have been turning this over in my head ever since, and I would like you to help me build a new case in the same spirit, but with the specifications I give below.

The case should be concrete enough to describe in a paragraph or two. It must have a substrate that is fully deterministic and physically closed: no new forces, no quantum indeterminacy, no souls, no magic. The substrate should be something whose basic elements and rules are simple and fully specified, so that we can in principle compute what happens. Within that substrate there must be a higher-level pattern that could be carried by different arrangements of the basic elements, and it is the pattern, not the particular arrangement, that we are interested in. The case must be one where a difference in the higher-level pattern produces a difference in the subsequent behavior of the substrate, and where we are tempted to say that the pattern itself is doing causal work rather than merely being a summary of the micro-level goings-on.

After you have described the case, I want you to do several things. First, say what someone would say who thinks all the real causation is at the level of the basic elements and their rules, and that the higher-level pattern is at best a useful fiction. Second, say what someone would say who thinks the pattern is a genuine cause because it makes a difference and because the difference it makes is robust across the different micro-arrangements that realize it. Third, and most importantly, identify exactly which feature of the case is doing the work in making the case for the pattern as a real cause. I want to know what would happen if that feature were removed or altered: does the case collapse back into a straightforward story where only the basic elements are doing anything, or does something else take its place? Fourth, say how the person who thinks only the basic elements matter would attack the case, and whether the attack succeeds or whether it begs the question.

The point of this exercise is to see whether there is a coherent sense in which symbols can push the substrate around, or whether that way of talking is always going to be a shorthand for something else. So the case has to earn its verdict: it must not simply stipulate that the higher-level pattern has causal power, and it must not rely on our not knowing the micro-level details. The micro-level details should be fully available, and the case should still make us wonder.

Please write your answer in continuous prose, with no headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables, the way you would write a long letter.
