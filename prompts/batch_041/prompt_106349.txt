Write a short story about a town that has a Blame Registry: you can't publicly blame anyone for anything unless you file the complaint with the clerk, and the clerk rejects you if you've done the same thing yourself. Extra wrinkle: anyone who files has to sign a form agreeing that they can be blamed for that same thing later, by anyone, with no appeals. Follow a guy whose complaint gets bounced and who insists the Registry has it backwards — that blaming someone isn't a claim about your own record. But dont let him off easy: I want at least one scene where a character pulls apart "you've got no standing to say this to me" from "what I did wasn't wrong," and I want the story to stay honestly undecided about whether the Registry is enforcing something real about blame or just quietly making accountability impossible. Keep it to like 800 words.
