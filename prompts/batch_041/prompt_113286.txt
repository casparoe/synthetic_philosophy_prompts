Can you lay out what a platonist is committed to, and seperate the strands for me — that there are abstract objects, that mathematical statements are literally true, that mathematical terms refer to those objects, that the objects are independent of us and our practices, that our mathematical beliefs are justified — and then tell me which of these actually come as a package and which can be pulled apart, so I can see exactly what Priya is and isn't giving up?
