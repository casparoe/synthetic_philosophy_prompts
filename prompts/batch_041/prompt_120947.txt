I was recently at a dinner party on the eve of a municipal election, and when I mentioned that I was thinking of staying home because my ward is a safe seat for a party I do not support, a friend of mine—a former canvasser—said, flatly, "Every vote counts." The conversation stalled there, because I could not tell whether she meant that my vote might literally decide the outcome, or that voting is a civic duty regardless of outcome, or something about the expressive significance of casting a ballot, or perhaps something else entirely. I have also noticed that the phrase "the paradox of voting" gets used in at least two different ways in conversation and in the literature—sometimes for the phenomenon of cyclical majority preferences, sometimes for the puzzle of why anyone bothers to vote when the expected instrumental payoff is vanishingly small—and I suspect the slogan is doing different work depending on which paradox is in the background.

Could you lay out for me the distinct precise claims that "every vote counts" could be making? I would like a menu rather than a single interpretation: for each reading, say what kind of claim it is (causal, probabilistic, ethical, expressive, definitional), whether it is trivial or substantive, whether it is defensible, and whether it is actually true. Then say which of those readings would genuinely support the use my friend put it to—namely, giving me a reason to vote even in a safe seat—and which would leave her conclusion unmotivated.

I am especially interested in the readings that turn on the probability of being pivotal, on the difference between having a reason and having a duty, and on whether the slogan is really about individual votes or about the aggregate behavior of an electorate. Please do not simply pick the most sophisticated reading and defend it; I want to see the whole range, including the ones that are obviously weak, because part of what I am trying to figure out is which reading people usually have in mind when they say this. Lay the readings out as a list I can bring back to her.
