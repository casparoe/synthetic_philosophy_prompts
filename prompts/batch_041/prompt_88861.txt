2. A guy shot a shopkeeper during what he admitted was basically a robbery for money, and he only joined the movement afterwards. He confessed completely, but everything he said was already on the record from other people. Denied.

5. Some guy asked for amnesty covering "whatever my unit did" during a whole two year period. They turned him down flat and told him to apply act by act.

New case. A regional commander ordered his men to kill a village headman he thought was an informer. Nobody above him ordered it. It's the first document the committee has ever seen on how the informer killings were organized, and it names a bunch of people. The headman was unarmed and there's no real evidence he was informing. And the commander is asking for immunity for everything his unit did, not just this one killing.
