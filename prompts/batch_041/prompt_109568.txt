So: write me an essay on whether it's literally true that DNA is a code — that it carries information — or whether "code" is just a metaphor we've all stopped noticing. So which is it, and does the answer change what "genetic information" can even mean? Give the best case on both sides, then actually come down on one; don't leave it at "it's complicated." My character needs an answer.
