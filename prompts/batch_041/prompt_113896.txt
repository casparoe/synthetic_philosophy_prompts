Build the question around this case, which I made up, so nothing in the literature answers it off the shelf. Marco has a stable, fully deterministic preference cycle over three drinks: tea over coffee, coffee over water, water over tea, each by a small margin. A cafe owner on his street runs him through the cycle over three seperate visits, one swap each, taking a small fee every time; Marco ends up back where he started, with water and less money. Marco's friend says this proves he's irrational and that any non-EU-maximizer is exploitable, so the fix is simply to be an EU-maximizer. Marco's other friend says it proves nothing, because Marco is never offered a single choice in which the package deal beats standing pat.
