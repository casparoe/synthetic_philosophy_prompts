I went down a rabbit hole and found Gibbard-Satterthwaite, which as I understand it says something like: three or more options, and a rule that isn't literally just one person deciding, and there's always a case where somebody does better by lying about their preferences than by being honest. If that's right, I think the honest takeaway is "we can't design our way out of this, we have to change what we're doing," and that's a hard sell to a room that wants me to just fix the ranking code.
