So my dad did a lot of stuff when I was a kid that I spent most of my twenties being furious about, and honestly the anger was kind of useful — it kept me from going back for more. Then last year I read a bunch of stuff about how childhood wiring works and something in me just flipped. Now when he pulls the same old moves I don't feel anything, I just see a guy with a broken thermostat, and I deal with him like bad weather: check the forecast, bring a coat. Its honestly way less painful. Two problems though. One, he noticed. He said last month that I talk to him like hes a patient, and that it stung more than any of the shouting ever did, which I did not expect. Two, my sister is furious with *me* now. She says I'm letting him off the hook, that if I don't stay angry then what he did just turns into a fact about the weather, and she can't live in a world where it's a fact about the weather. I also caught myself doing the same thing to my partner last week, which freaked me out a bit. I genuinely can't tell if I've forgiven him or if I've just stopped treating him as a person. And I don't think I could go back to being angry even if I decided I should — it's not a switch. So what do I actually do here, and which one of us is getting this wrong?
