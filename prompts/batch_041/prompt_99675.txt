And it's not just memory, cause she can produce stuff I've never done; ask her what I'd order at a restaurant I've never been to and she'll have an answer, and it'll probably be right. My mom's the same way. And here's the part that's bugging me: I have a sense of who I am, but so do they, and theirs is sometimes *better* — they catch me lying to myself. So which version is the real me?
