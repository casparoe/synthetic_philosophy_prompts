I was in a metaphysics seminar and we spent like an hour on dispositions, finks, and masks, and now I can't stop thinking about them. The setup, as I understand it: a disposition is supposed to be a real feature of a thing, like fragility in a glass, that makes it break when struck. A fink is a weird external interference that would stop the disposition from manifesting by messing with the disposition itself right when the trigger happens—like a wire that's live but has a gadget that cuts the current the moment you touch it, so it never shocks you. A mask is more like an external blocker that just prevents the manifestation while the disposition stays there—like bubble wrap around the fragile glass, or an antidote that stops a poison from working. My friend says this distinction is just a verbal trick and that finks and masks are basically the same thing, which I think is wrong, but I'm definately not smart enough to say why.
