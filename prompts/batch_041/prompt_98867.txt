Give me exactly five numbered items, each a sentence or two, with no introduction or conclusion. In each item give the requirement, the case or consideration that motivates it, and your verdict on whether it is non-negotiable or a preference of one camp; let the fifth item say which single requirement does the most to exclude otherwise popular accounts, or, if you judge some pair of requirements to be jointly unsatisfiable, name the pair and explain why.
