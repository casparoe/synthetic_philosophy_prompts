Include at least one question that turns on the model's stochastic character — that the same problem may be resolved by different slippages and so yield different answers — and asks whether a theory that returns several answers can be a theory of the one right answer. For each question, add a follow-up keyed to the answer she is most likely to give, and make at least two of the questions press where a sceptic would press.
