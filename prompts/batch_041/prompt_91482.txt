I have been trying to pin down a pattern I keep noticing and I do not know whether it already has a name. Two cases that strike me as the same thing. First: I am planning a trip with several connecting flights, and for each individual leg I am confident it will leave on time; taken together I am sure at least one of them will be delayed, and I would never describe the itinerary as a whole as punctual, yet I cannot identify the leg I actually doubt. Second: I read a long, careful work of history and find myself endorsing its claims one by one, and yet I do not believe the book is entirely accurate, though I cannot name a single sentence I would bet against. In both cases each individual judgment seems reasonable on its own, the collection is one I would reject, and I cannot say which item to give up. Is there an established concept for this configuration? I suspect I am circling something in epistemology without knowing its name, and a bare label will not satisfy me. I want the phenomenon carved precisely: whether these two cases are one thing or two, what the nearest named ideas are and where each of them fails to fit, whether it matters that in the first case I know some alternative must obtain while in the second I do not, and whether the puzzle is really about what I believe or only about what I am willing to assert.
