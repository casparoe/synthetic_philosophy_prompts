I moved back to my home town last year and I've been reading up on why our mill shut down in 1979, and I've run into something I can't put a name to. Three books give three different causes -- the owner died and his kids wouldn't put money back in, freight rates jumped, and a cheap synthetic took over the market for what we made -- and each book insists its cause alone was enough. But the more I poke at it, the more it looks like each one only bit because the others were already in place (the synthetic only turned fatal once freight had made us uncompetitive), and if you took any single one away I can't honestly see the mill surviving either. So depending on how I ask the question, every cause is sufficient and every cause is necessary, and I don't know whether that's one thing or two. Seperately: four other towns near us emptied out over the same decade, each for reasons that have nothing to do with ours, and I can't tell whether that's the same pattern as the mill or a different one that just ends the same way. Is there a name for any of this? I don't just want a label -- I want to know where the lines are, which of my two observations is which, and what each one isn't. Answer as a numbered list of exactly five items, a sentence or two each, no intro and no wrap-up.
