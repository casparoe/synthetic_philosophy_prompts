I want you to take the long dispute between cyclical and linear views of history and restate it, as faithfully as you can, in the vocabulary I actually use. On the linear side, work through: the Judeo-Christian and Enlightenment-Whig narratives of irreversible progress, and the modern stage-and-direction views of history in the historicist and Marxian traditions.

For each side, do the translation carefully and one claim at a time. Which historical claims become claims about the generating process? Map each historian's claim onto one of these formal objects, say what has to be fudged to make it fit, and be explicit about a distinction I care about: whether the cycle is generated endogenously by the dynamics or is just an exogenous shock process that we choose to summarize as a cycle.
