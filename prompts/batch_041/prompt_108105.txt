Could you seperate out the precise claims 'the ends don't justify the means' could be making — a menu running from the readings that are trivially true or borderline definitional, through the ones that are substantive and defensible, to the ones that are flat-out false — and for each say whether it's trivial, substantive, plausible, or false?
