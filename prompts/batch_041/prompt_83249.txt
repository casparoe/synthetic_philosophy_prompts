Same people, same stakes (a shared point pot that gets zeroed, no real money), and the counterfactual structure mirrored so it's symmetric. Between the runs, have them publicly precommit/bet on whether their partner will act, and pay out on that bet, so a norm forms about who is on duty and who is the designated one. Then everyone privately rates causation and blame 0-10 for the person who did or didn't act, and we compare the ratings across the two runs and against the bets.
