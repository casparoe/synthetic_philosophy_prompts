I've somehow ended up as the resident philosopher on a national drugs advisory panel, and I need to get ahead of next month's meeting. The way it works is that we get handed one concrete proposal at a time and we say yea or nay, and I'm meant to be the one who keeps us consistent with ourselves.

The record so far, roughly in order: we backed stripping criminal penalties for possession of small quantities of anything for personal use, while leaving supply a criminal offence — the Portuguese-style split, which we were rather pleased with ourselves about, though I've never been sure the use/supply line is doing the work we think it is. We backed supervised injecting facilities and diamorphine on prescription. We backed pill-testing at festivals, even though we conceded it probably nudges a few people towards trying the stuff. And we turned down the only properly commercial thing that's ever come to us: licensed for-profit cannabis retail with branding and advertising, on youth-uptake and profit-motive grounds.

Next month's item is non-profit cannabis social clubs — members cultivate and supply each other, registered adults only, no advertising, no profit, potency capped, purchase limits, and a levy on the wholesale going to treatment services.

What do you reckon we'll do, and what should we do if we want to be consistent? I want the rule we seem to be running, any other rules that fit our record just as well, what each of them predicts for the clubs, your best guess and the reason for it, and the one further case that would actually seperate the rules. And if the whole record is just incoherent, say so plainly, tell me which precedent is the nearest analogue to the clubs, and spell out what principle we'd be signing up to either way. No need for footnotes.
