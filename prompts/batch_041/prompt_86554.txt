An anonymous account on a forum says my prescription interacts badly with grapefruit — definately not, I'd go read something. My best friend says the new Thai place is good — believe her. The one I keep tripping on is the street stranger versus the anonymous forum poster: both nobodies, no track record, opposite verdicts, and I can't tell if that's about stakes, or about the guy being physically in front of me, or about me being able to picture the bridge. Is there a principle, or a small set of candidates, that gets all of this? Also, is there a real difference between me predicting that someone is reliable and me trusting them? Because when my doctor's wrong I feel let down, not just mistaken, and I want to know if that's sentiment or if it's doing epistemic work.

Give it to me as exactly five numbered items, a sentence or two each, no intro and no conclusion — fold the trust thing into whichever item it fits.
