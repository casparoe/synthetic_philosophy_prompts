I think the slogan is being made to carry more than it can, but I can't say precisely where it gives way. Please keep apart the readings that locate the wrong of misleading in the speaker's intention to induce a false belief, in the false belief actually induced, and in the breach of a norm of assertion, since I suspect she slides between these, and say which reading the oath most naturally bears. Finish by saying what would change your mind about which reconstruction is best, and where that reconstruction is most likely to fail.
