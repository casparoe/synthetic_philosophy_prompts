I'm convening a graduate reading group on the symbol grounding problem, and for the opening session I want to force everyone to take a definite stand rather than trade generalities. Please rank the candidate solutions listed below in order of how genuinely they ground symbols, and present the result as four tiers (a top tier of the ones that really do the work, down to a bottom tier of those that only appear to). Treat "genuinely grounds" as a joint requirement: (i) the meaning of a symbol token must be fixed without smuggling in an already-meaningful interpreter — no homunculus, however thinly disguised; (ii) the link from symbol to world must be established by the system's own causal interaction with its environment rather than stipulated in advance by the theorist; and (iii) whatever fixes meaning for a primitive must also fix meaning for novel composites built out of primitives, and must survive an arbitrary renaming of those primitives. For each candidate, give me a sentence or two justifying its placement, and be explicit whenever a placement depends on which of (i)–(iii) you are weighting most heavily.

The candidates are as follows. (a) Hybrid symbolic–connectionist grounding: symbols are anchored by being attached to connectionist representations that have themselves been trained on sensorimotor categories. (b) Enactivist grounding: meaning consists in the system's mastery of sensorimotor contingencies, with no internal representation doing the grounding. (c) Teleosemantic grounding: a symbol means what it does because of the biological proper function — its evolutionary or developmental history — of the mechanism that produces it. (d) Distributional grounding: meaning is fixed by patterns of co-occurrence across a large corpus of symbol use. (e) Instrumental grounding: a symbol is grounded once the system can use it to make reliable interventions, its meaning being fixed by the counterfactual outcomes of the actions the symbol licenses. (f) Social-normative grounding: meaning is fixed by a community's corrective and sanctioning practices, so that there is no individual-level fact of the matter about what a token means. (g) Compositional bootstrapping: ground a small stock of primitive symbols by direct causal coupling, then let every other symbol inherit its meaning by definition and composition from those primitives.

After the tier list, flag the single candidate whose position is most sensitive to which of the three requirements one emphasises, and say whether the whole ranking would invert if one requirement were dropped — and if so, which. I'd like the reasoning to be tight enough that a sceptical colleague in the room couldn't dismiss a placement as mere taste, so please make the criteria do real work rather than restating each position's self-description. Write your answer in British English.
