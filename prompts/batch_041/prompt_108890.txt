Don't explain radical interpretation to me -- I know roughly what I think it is, and I don't want a mini-essay. I want you to plan the explanation, the way someone who's taught this for fifteen years would, someone who knows exactly where students' heads fall off. Take that role and think it through carefully before you commit: brainstorm the candidate orderings and the candidate carrying examples first, then give me the plan.

What I need is the dependency structure: which idea has to be in place before the next one can even be stated, and why -- specifically, how much of the Tarskian truth-theory machinery they actually need on the table before charity makes any sense at all, and where the interdependence of belief and meaning has to be introduced so it doesnt land as a flat paradox.
