My friend and I spent most of last night arguing about the franchise, and it ended with her telling me that my position is a patchwork — that I have assembled commitments whose attractions come from incompatible traditions, and that I have never been forced to choose among them because I have never had to act on all of them at once. I would like to know whether she is right, and I would rather find out now than halfway through writing my side of the argument up. Here is what I actually hold.

(i) Voting should be compulsory, with a small fine for non-voters, so that turnout approaches universality.

(ii) The ground of the duty is that voting is an office of democratic citizenship — something one holds and is answerable for, not a right one is free to decline. The fine is a sanction for non-performance rather than an incentive.

(iii) The voting age should be lowered to sixteen, in national as well as local elections.

(iv) Resident non-citizens should be able to vote in municipal elections on the same terms as citizens.

(v) What qualifies someone for the electorate is being subject to the state's binding decisions, not being affected by its policies; that is why I would enfranchise residents who are not citizens, but not foreigners abroad whose interests our decisions damage.

(vi) The practical case for compulsion is that turnout is stratified by class and policy is therefore stratified by class, and compulsion is the remedy.

(vii) The mechanics of voting should be as frictionless as possible — automatic registration, no identification requirement, generous opening hours — and a blank ballot should be available, counted, and published as a deliberate abstention.

I suspect I am not simply contradicting myself, but I cannot see which of these are genuinely pulling against one another, which only appear to, and which single revision would settle the most at the least cost. Please sort the tensions by severity rather than treating every pair as equally fraught; for each, say what would have to give and what that would cost; and tell me where a distinction I am already relying on is quietly doing the work of holding two of these together.
