Second: if the badness isn't part of the sensation, what exactly is left that makes it pain rather than some other odd sensation, given that they insist it hurts? And to finish, give me the single strongest reason someone could give that the badness has to be in the feeling itself, and tell me wether that should make me drop the whole view or only qualify it.
