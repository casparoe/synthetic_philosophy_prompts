Give each term a rough probability with one line of justification, say how they combine, and tell me which term the final number is most sensitive to and what would move it most. Please keep the split in experience and the split in agency distinct throughout, since I suspect much of what I have been reading slides between them. For what it is worth, I am at about 0.3 myself, and I cannot tell whether that is a considered judgment or reluctance to call my brother two people.
