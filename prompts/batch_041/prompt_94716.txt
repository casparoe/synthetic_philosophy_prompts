I'm in a metaphysics reading group and I got voluntold to write up my position on origin essentialism so the others can take shots at it, and honestly writing it all down has made me less sure it hangs toghether. Here's roughly where I am, ordered by how confident I am. (1) You could not have come from a different sperm and egg than the ones you actually came from — the particular gametes are essential to you, and not just causally necessary, I mean strictly necessary, it's part of what you are. (2) But I'm not a necessitarian about your existence: there's a world where my parents never met and nobody at all answers to me, and that seems obviously true to me, not even worth arguing about. (3) I think essences have to be intrinsic — what a thing essentially is should be settled by what it is in itself, not by its relations or its causal history, because otherwise essentialism turns into a doctrine about how the world happened to be arranged around the thing rather than about the thing. (4) I do think sameness of origin is enough, i.e. if two things came from the same gametes then they're the same thing, and I take that to be what makes (1) do any explanatory work at all. (5) I'm increasingly sympathetic to the idea that there's no such thing as strict trans-world identity and that all our de re talk should get cashed out as talk about how similar other things are to me — I guess I'm a counterpart theorist about de re modality, at least on Tuesdays. (6) I think the particular sperm that made me was a chancy matter: rerun the world up to the night I was conceived and the odds against that exact one winning are astronomically bad, and I don't think that's just epistemic — the process really was indeterministic. And (7) I think you can't read modality off conceivability, so my inability to imagine being born from a different egg proves nothing either way.

Some of these I take to be obviously fine together and some I suspect are not, and I genuinely can't tell which is which. There's also one more thing I can't even figure out where to put: I'm pretty sure that if my zygote had split and produced identical twins, there would be two people there and neither of them would be the other one — I don't think that's controversial — but I also want to say the two twins came from the same origin, and I notice that seems to make trouble for (4) specifically. Could you go through the whole list and tell me which pairs are actually in tension and how bad, which only look like they're in tension and why the appearance is misleading, and for the real ones what the cheapest repair is and what I'd be giving up to take it? Please rank the tensions by severity instead of listing them as all equally bad, say which single revision would clean up the most, and don't just tell me the whole package is doomed — I want to know which bit is doing the damage, and whether any of my apparent problems is really just me running two different questions together.
