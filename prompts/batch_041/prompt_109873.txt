So why isn't the mask case just a variant of the fink case, and why does it matter which one you use? Also, if masks are the real problem, does that mean the whole fink literature was a detour? I'm asking because I'm trying to figure out whether dispositionalism can just say dispositions are real but thier manifestations are always defeasible, or whether that's dodging the issue.
