I'm a journalist who covers law, religion and ideas for a small newsletter, and I'm trying to build an explainer that actually sorts church-state arrangements instead of repeating the usual three buckets — establishment, separation, laïcité — which never fit the cases I care about. England has an established church but almost nobody there pays a church tax; Germany has no established church in the English sense, yet the state tax office collects a church tax as a surcharge on income tax and passes it to the churches; France has its separation law, but Alsace-Moselle still runs on a pre-separation regime with state-paid clergy; the US has no establishment, but has legislative chaplains, tax exemptions, and a huge jurisprudence of accommodation. I want a decision tree that sorts countries by what their institutions actually do, not by the label on the tin. Can you build me one? Start from a country that has some official connection between state and religion and branch on the questions that really split the cases: is there a legally established church? does the state collect religious taxes? does it pay clergy salaries? does it recognize religious bodies as public-law corporations? does it allow individual opt-out? is the arrangement national or regional? is the establishment ceremonial, doctrinal, or fiscal? Each node should be a question a smart non-lawyer could answer about their own country without already knowing which model it leads to, and each leaf should be a named position or a named combination (something like 'established church, no church tax', 'no establishment, state-collected church tax', 'separation with a territorial exception'). I want maybe 8-12 leaves, and I want you to flag any two leaves your tree can't really separate — if the US and France end up in the same leaf once you look at funding rather than rhetoric, say so.

Also, please don't just list the four countries. Build the tree from the logical space first — the questions and the combinations of answers they allow — then locate England, Germany, France and the US in it, and note any combinations that come out empty. If a combination is empty, tell me whether it's because it's incoherent (a state church that is also fully voluntary?), unattractive, or just historically unexplored. I'm especially interested in the weird middle: state-collected church taxes without establishment, establishment without fiscal privilege, separation with public funding of religious schools, opt-out systems that only work for some religions, that sort of thing. And please coin a label where the standard vocabulary doesn't have one, with a one-line definition, rather than forcing everything into 'establishment' and 'seperation'. No reading lists or citations needed — I just want the sorting instrument, and I'd rather you be explicit about the branches your tree can't handle than pretend it covers everything.
