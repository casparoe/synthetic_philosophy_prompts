"Harsanyi's theorem settles an old question in ethics with mathematics. So the rational social choice is simply to maximize total welfare. And because the theorem is built on the thought that we should choose our institutions as if we might turn out to be anyone within them, it never has to compare one person's happiness against another's, which is how it dodges the usual objections to utilitarianism."
