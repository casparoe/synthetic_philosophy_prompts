The speaker is an early-career philosopher (not someone famous, so don't worry about matching anything he's actually written) who's shopping a book arguing that we should take the intentional stance toward large language models -- that ascribing beliefs, desires and goals to them isn't just a convenient shorthand but the correct predictive strategy, even though there's nothing inside the model that looks like a human belief. He's got one move he leans on to get out of everything, something about beliefs without believers, and I want it to get progressively harder for him to use as the Q&A goes on.

Draft me eight to ten questions, no generic stuff (skip how did you get into this, and nothing that just asks him to define the intentional stance -- the audience knows what that is). For each of those three, give me a follow-up keyed to the answer he's most likely to give.
