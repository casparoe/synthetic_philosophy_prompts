my partner has had chronic pain for about four years now, and lately they've started talking about it as something they brought on themselves. Not in a self-hating way — they're emphatic that nobody is punishing them, there's no judge keeping score, it's just that your own actions come back around eventually, and what's landing on them now is something they set going in an earlier life. They also say you're allowed to hand over the good side of it to other people, so they've started giving theirs to me and to their sister.

My sister (a nurse) says this is textbook victim-blaming and that I should push back on it. I'm not so sure — my partner seems calmer than before, not guiltier. But the whole thing naggs at me. If it really was an earlier life, that person wasn't them, so why is it landing on them? And if the good side can be handed to someone else, in what sense was it ever theirs to begin with? Does the idea hold together at all, and what, if anything, should I say to them?
