Read the film through the path-dependence debate, and make the reading earn its keep in both directions. The distinctions I want it to work with: weak path dependence, where history matters but the destination is shared, versus strong path dependence, where small early accidents are amplified and the system settles irreversibly into one of several equilibria; equifinality, the rival picture in which many routes converge on a single attractor, so the same outcome would have arrived anyway; and counterfactual robustness, the idea that an outcome counts as inevitable only insofar as it survives nearby perturbations of the path.
