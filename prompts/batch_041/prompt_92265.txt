I write a science-and-ideas column and I'm putting together a light weekend piece ranking the Nordic countries by how liberal they are, and honestly I'm stuck on Sweden vs Norway. Everyone I ask gives me a different winner. A Swedish friend says Sweden, obviously — they did all the school vouchers and the tax cuts back in the nineties, that's the liberal one. A Norwegian says no, Norway's more liberal, they've got the giant sovereign wealth fund and they never even joined the EU, they keep doing their own thing. My brother-in-law, who mainlines American cable news, says the question is nonsense because both of them are socialist paradises and "liberal" means the opposite over there. I figure there's definately some index out there — economic freedom, press freedom, gay marriage, drug policy, whatever — that would just settle it, and I should be able to look up which one scores higher. So which country actually comes out more liberal, and whats the number that decides it?
