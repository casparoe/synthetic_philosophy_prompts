I am a hobbyist machinist, and I have lately been building the same small thing in three quite different ways: a mechanical adding machine made of gears and cams, a version of the same machine built out of falling dominoes that encode the digits, and a software simulation of the mechanical one. All three perform the same arithmetic, and the only descriptions under which I can say what any of them will do next are descriptions that treat certain physical features as standing for numbers and certain physical events as adding them. If I try instead to explain a single output by tracing gears, toppling dominoes, and voltage levels, I can do it in principle, but the resulting story is enormous, it is different for each of the three machines, and it never once mentions the thing I actually care about, namely that the machine is computing a sum. And yet when I go down to the smallest scale and ask what made each part move, every push is fully accounted for by an earlier push, or by gravity, or by a difference in voltage; there is no leftover shove anywhere that is not a shove of that ordinary kind. So I seem to be in possession of a description that is the only useful one, that is the same across machines sharing no parts, and that nevertheless appears to point to nothing that is doing any pushing at the level where the pushes actually occur.

I keep feeling there ought to be a name for this exact predicament — not for computation as such, and not for the bland fact that one thing admits of many descriptions, but for the specific combination: a higher-level, meaning-involving description is the one that carries the predictive and explanatory load, it survives a complete change of substrate, and at the same time every event it talks about has a complete lower-level cause that seems to leave no room for anything further. Is there an established concept or debate for that configuration, and if there is, I want to know precisely which of the nearby ideas it is and which it is not — I have encountered phrases like multiple realizability, downward causation, and levels of explanation, and I cannot tell whether any of them names this or whether each of them misses it in a different direction. If nothing established fits, then please coin a term and define it, and tell me where its edges lie: is a thermostat in or out, is a river carving a channel in or out, is a chess engine that only ever runs on one machine in or out, and what would have to change about the domino machine to put it outside the concept? The label matters less to me than the carving.
