Standard move: A has higher-order evidence, A should drop to roughly 0.6, and sitting at 0.8 while believing she ought to be at 0.6 is epistemic akrasia. Now the wrinkle. Meanwhile B has the same E and the same calibration result but never touched M — B got to 0.8 some other way.
