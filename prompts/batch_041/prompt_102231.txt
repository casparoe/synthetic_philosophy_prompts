Design a thought experiment that pulls Marx's two central critical concepts apart by exhibiting alienation without exploitation. The scenario should be realistic and contemporary — a craft, trade, or profession, not a commune or a factory of the future — no more than two paragraphs, and it must satisfy the following constraints jointly. The central figure is not exploited on any of the three readings one might give the notion: she does not receive less than the value her labor produces (no surplus is appropriated by a non-producer), she is not dominated or coerced by anyone who can set her terms, and she does not stand in a grossly unequal exchange with a buyer. She is nonetheless alienated on Marx's account, and the alienation must be objective rather than merely felt: the case may not depend on her being deceived about her situation, nor on her simply being unhappy. The estrangement should be secured in at least three of the four dimensions Marx distinguishes — from the product, from the activity of producing, from her species-being (her creative capacities treated as a means rather than an end), and from other people. No employer, capitalist, or creditor may appear in the story, and introducing one should be a genuine change to the case rather than a cosmetic one. I want the direction that is easy to overlook: estrangement where no one is extracting surplus from anyone, not the familiar and cheap case of the well-paid, contented worker who is nonetheless exploited.

After the scenario, do the diagnostic work. For each dimension of alienation, say what in the case is meant to establish it, and flag any dimension that is only weakly secured. Then say whether someone committed to the view that estrangement is simply what wage labor feels like from the inside can plausibly deny that your figure is alienated at all, and how they would argue it. Finish by naming the single feature that is doing the load-bearing work and stating what happens to the case if that feature is removed. Assume the reader knows the concepts but has not seen this example, and argue from the concepts themselves rather than citing any texts.
