I write fiction, and for years I have been collecting a particular kind of argument — the kind I give to characters when I want a scene that can neither be resolved nor walked away from — and I have never found a name for it. I would like to know whether philosophers have one, and if not, whether they would coin one, because every label I reach for seems to be describing something a little different.

The pattern is this. Two people argue. Every fact either of them can state, the other accepts; neither can point to any observation, document, or event that the other denies. And yet each can restate the other's position in his own words and find it so obviously true as to be a platitude — which does not end the argument. Nor is the argument idle: it changes what they do. One refuses the other's hand; one resigns; a will is rewritten. And when they try to settle it by agreeing on a definition, the quarrel merely moves: now they are arguing about which definition to adopt, and that argument has all the same features.

Instances, from life as well as from drafts: whether a company can be sincere, or only the people in it; whether a nation can owe an apology, or only its current officials; whether a river dredged and re-routed is still the same river. In each case the disputants agree about boards and memos, about treaties and speeches, about silt and banks. In each case one says the word is being used loosely, and the other says that is precisely the question. People tell me such quarrels are only about words, and I do not believe the word only.

So I am asking for the carving, not the label. What are the nearest established concepts, how does the thing I have described differ from each of them, and which, if any, fits it? If nothing fits, give me a term with a definition, and tell me where its edges lie — what it excludes, and what it would be a mistake to call by that name. I will not be satisfied with a bare name.

Please lead with your bottom line in the first sentence and then give the reasoning, with no preamble and no restating of my question.
