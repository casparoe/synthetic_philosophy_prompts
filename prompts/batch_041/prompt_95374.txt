For about a decade I have worked on data interchange formats and compiler intermediate representations: grammar and well-formedness, validation, canonical forms, round-tripping, elaboration from surface syntax into a core language, and the perennial argument in my field over whether a single notation can serve every domain or whether universality just means that every implementation eventually grows an escape hatch. That vocabulary is mine and I use it daily. Philosophy is not, and I have no formal training in it.

What I want from you is Otto Neurath's case for a picture language restated in that vocabulary. Neurath is the Vienna Circle figure who built the ISOTYPE system of pictorial statistics, promoted it as an international picture language — the slogan he displayed on his office wall was "Words divide, pictures unite" — and tied it to a larger programme of a unified, physicalist language for the sciences. Lay out his case as sympathetically and as literally as you can, using only my resources: expressiveness and completeness, well-formedness versus semantic emptiness, syntax versus semantics, canonical form, lossy versus lossless transformation, sugar and elaboration, escape hatches, schema migration. Where his argument needs a notion I do not have, say so rather than improvising a translation.

Then give me the residue report. Which of his claims come through intact; which survive only as a distortion, and precisely what has been fudged in the process; and which have no counterpart in my vocabulary at all. The candidates I would put on the table are these: that a pictorial notation is less likely than ordinary word language to breed pseudo-problems; that pictures are more intersubjective, or more capable of uniting people, than words; the coherentist and anti-foundationalist backdrop, on which there is no privileged base layer and every statement remains revisable; and his own apparent concession that this was a helping language rather than a complete one, which is a strange thing to concede if one's case is that a notation can carry science. For each item, tell me whether the residue marks something format design has no way to say and ought to, or whether it shows that the original claim was doing less work than it sounded like it was. I would rather have an honest ledger than a tidy correspondence; and if I have misdescribed what Neurath actually held, correct me and then translate the corrected version.
