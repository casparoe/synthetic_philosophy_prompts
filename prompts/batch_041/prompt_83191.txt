Write a joke about indirect normativity — y'know, the move where instead of saying what's good directly, you say the good is whatever an idealized deliberation process would converge on. A setup you could use: a guy rubs a lamp, and the genie says he'll get whatever he would have wanted if he were smarter, better informed and thinking clearly about it. The punchline should turn on the actual philosophical content — the whole point of going indirect is that your actual, un-idealized wants arent the thing being consulted — so it cant just be a 'genies are sneaky' joke. Ideally it's funny to someone who's actually thought about this stuff, but it should still make some sense to someone who's only vaguely heard of the idea, so dont hang the whole thing on a specific paper.
