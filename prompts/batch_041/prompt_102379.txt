I have recently been pulled into a dispute about the historical Jesus that I had assumed scholarship settled long ago, and I have found instead that the machinery for settling it is itself in question. The tools in play are the familiar ones: treat a saying or a deed as going back to Jesus if it would have embarrassed the early church to have invented it; if it is attested in more than one independent strand of the tradition; if it cuts against the expectations of both the Judaism of his day and the Christianity that followed; if it coheres with whatever else one has already accepted. One party tells me these are the best instruments we have and that used together they converge on a recognizable figure. The other tells me that each is circular: that the criterion rewarding embarrassment makes the church a trustworthy witness precisely where it testifies against its own interests; that the criterion rewarding dissimilarity defines Jesus almost entirely by what he was not, so that the residue it leaves is an artifact of the method; and that the demand for coherence quietly presupposes an authentic core that has not yet been established. I do not want to choose sides yet. I want the specification that would let me judge the instruments.

Set out the desiderata that any criterion, or set of criteria, for authenticity in the Jesus tradition must meet if it is to be adequate for the purpose of reconstructing what Jesus actually said and did. Give me the cases each criterion has to get right, the distinctions it must not collapse, and the background facts about the sources it must be consistent with, in each case with the consideration that makes it a requirement. I expect the list to include something about independence from the investigator's own commitments, something about the nature of the evidence (that everything we have was transmitted by people who already believed, in genres not written to record for posterity, decades after the events, within conventions of memory and report unlike our own), and something about what authenticity is even supposed to mean here, whether a verbatim saying, the gist of one, or a characteristic deed. Add whatever else belongs and say why it belongs.

Then mark which of these requirements are non-negotiable and which are merely preferences of one camp or another, and flag any pair or subset that I should suspect cannot be jointly satisfied; if the list turns out to be internally inconsistent, that is itself the most useful thing you could tell me. Finally, say which single requirement does the most to disqualify the popular criteria, and, briefly, how the main criteria on offer fare against the list.
