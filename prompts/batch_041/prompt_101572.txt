The knot is Rule 42, which we wrote ourselves, as a charter rule, to say in its own text that it may be amended by a simple majority — it carries its own lower threshold. Last night, by a plain majority and not a unanimous one, we passed an amendment changing Rule 42 so that it now requires unanimity instead. I'm the scorekeeper, so I provisionally paid out the points, which puts the proposer at 49, and now someone is challenging the whole thing. My own position, and I want you to actually wrestle with it instead of just batting it away: the amendment is void. Rule 42 says its own threshold is the one that governs it, so the act of amending Rule 42 has to satisfy Rule 42 as amended — and Rule 42 as amended wants unanimity, which we didn't have. It can't have validly enacted itself. Everyone else says I'm being cute, that you judge a vote by the rules in force when the vote happened, full stop.
