Write a joke about Johannes Kepler's views on astrology. The humor should turn on the specifics of his actual position, not on the generic idea that astrology is bunk. Treat the following as given, since it's the substance the joke must use: Kepler attacked the astrologers of his day and considered most of their rules and methods the dung that an industrious hen scrapes through in hopes of an occasional grain or gold nugget, yet he spent much of his life trying to give astrology sounder foundations and cast hundreds of horoscopes; and his own proposed mechanism was that the Earth has a soul stirred by the geometrical angles (the aspects) between the heavenly bodies — sentient but not conscious, in the way a shepherd can be pleased by a flute without understanding anything about musical harmony. The setup and the punchline should turn on that contrast. Someone who actually knows Kepler's astrology should find the joke funny rather than come away thinking I have no idea what his position was. Write in American English.
