Meridian runs an "Assumed Hostility" doctrine: every component in the pipeline is treated as potentially misaligned, including the trusted monitor, including the monitor of the monitor. Things I want the memo to have to deal with without ever admitting they're problems: the audit rate only ever goes up, because a failed attack is read as evidence the adversary is stronger than modeled and a successful defense is read as evidence the adversary will try harder next quarter; the "usefulness floor" got lowered twice this quarter, most recently by having L0 stop producing the deliverable and instead produce a description of the deliverable, which L1 then writes badly; there's a "sandbagging discount" applied to the red team's score, where the size of the discount is estimated by L0; and the whole thing ends in a residual-risk section that swallows everything, then recommends proceeding.
