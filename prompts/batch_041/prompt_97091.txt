So my friend and I got into this two-hour fight last night about whether you could of come from a different sperm and egg — like, is there a possible world where your parents have a kid, but it's not you, it's just some sibling-shaped stranger? He kept saying obviously yeah, different sperm, different person, that's just genetics 101. And I kept saying no, that's the whole point, on the origin view the particular sperm and the particular egg are part of *what you are*, not just the recipe for how you got made. Anyway it got heated, we're not really speaking, and I want to send him a joke to break the ice but also, honestly, to win.

So: write me a joke about this. But it has to actually turn on the real content — the punchline should only land if you know *why* origin is supposed to be essential, not just "haha, babies come from sperm." And please don't just do the standard sperm-and-egg case everybody's heard, cause he'll roll his eyes. Use something weirder. Two wrinkles I want it to sit on, pick whichever works better:

- Identical twins come from the same one sperm and one egg. So if origin is essential, they've got the same origin — so what, they're the same person? That seems insane, but I can't tell if it breaks the view or is secretly the view.
- The egg is basically fixed for the month, so "a different egg" is a way stranger counterfactual than "a different sperm," and I feel like a joke could exploit that asymmetry — like, I'll grant you a different sperm, but a different egg is a whole different woman's month.

Doesn't have to be long. Just has to be funny to someone who knows the debate, and not embarrassing if an actual philosopher reads it.
