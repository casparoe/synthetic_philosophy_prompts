My friend and I spent basically all of last night arguing about the sphex wasp and whether it shows we're all just machines, and now I can't stop thinking about it. Quick version of the case in case you don't have it memorized: the wasp digs a burrow, drags a paralyzed cricket to the mouth of it, goes inside to check the burrow, comes back out — and if you just move the cricket a few inches while she's in there, she'll drag it back to the mouth, go in again, come out, and keep looping like that basicly forever. My friend's take is that we're exactly the same, we just have alot more branches in the loop, and that the line between mechanical routine and genuine flexible choice is a myth we tell ourselves. I think there's a real difference — she can't step back and notice what she's doing, she's stuck inside the rule, and we (sometimes) can. But then he asked what the difference actually is and I kind of floundered.
