I'm putting together a position statement for a seminar on emergence and mental causation, and I want a consistency audit before I circulate it. The setup I'm working with is Hofstadter's careenium: a vast table of tiny magnetic marbles (simms) careening about, occasionally clumping into stable clusters (simmballs) whose configurations encode information and, on Hofstadter's telling, are the vehicles of thought and meaning. Here is what I want to hold simultaneously. (1) Simmballs are real patterns at a genuinely higher level of description, not mere bookkeeping conveniences, and they are causally efficacious: when a simmball moves through the careenium it genuinely pushes simms around, rather than being a shadow cast by the simm-level dynamics. (2) The simm level is causally closed: every simm's trajectory is fully fixed by the positions and magnetic forces of neighboring simms, so no influence from a higher level can ever enter the physical story as an additional force. (3) The simmball level is multiply realizable and in that sense substrate-independent: the same symbolic pattern could be carried by a different collection of simms, or by something that is not magnetic marbles at all, and it would still be the same symbol doing the same cognitive work. (4) A symbol gets its meaning only by being grounded in a capacity to pick out the referents it stands for — an ungrounded symbol system shuffles its tokens forever without meaning anything, and this grounding is a matter of sensorimotor commerce with the world rather than of internal relations among the tokens. (5) The semantic content of a simmball is nevertheless fully explained by its functional role in the overall economy of the careenium — what it does to and with other simmballs is all there is to its meaning.

I suspect at least a couple of these cannot be held together, but I can't tell which pairs are genuinely in conflict and which only look that way, and I don't want to give up more than I have to. Please sort the tensions by severity, tell me for each what exactly has to give and what the cheapest revision would be, and say which apparent conflicts actually dissolve once the commitments are stated carefully. If one single revision would clean up the most, tell me which one and what it costs.
