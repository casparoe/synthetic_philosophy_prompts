I'm putting together a session for a film-and-philosophy group and I want to read Endo's Silence through the divine hiddenness problem. I'll probably screen the Scorsese version since more of them have seen it, but the novel is fair game too, so use whichever you know better. Not the usual "why won't God show up" sermon though. Here's the thing I keep chewing on: I think there are two silences and people run them together. One is the silence of absence, the feeling that nobody's home. The other is the silence of restraint, the parent who stays quiet at the table so the kid will say it first, the friend who won't answer for you because the answer has to be yours. I've been calling these the empty silence and the holding-back silence. I want a reading of the film that tells me which one it's actually dramatising, scene by scene: the drowning of the villagers and Garupe going under, Ferreira's whole line about Christianity not being able to take root in Japan, the way Kichijiro keeps coming back to confess and keeps selling Rodrigues out, and above all the moment Rodrigues is kneeling in front of the fumi-e and hears the voice telling him it's alright to step on it. Because I have a suspicion the voice ruins the hiddenness reading: the second you actually hear God say something, however gentle, it isn't hiddenness anymore, it's just a delayed answer. So either the film is about hiddenness and the voice is a cheat, or the film is about something else entirely and we've been mislabelling it. Which is it, and where does the film resist whatever reading you land on? Don't turn the whole thing into a recitation of the standard hiddenness argument, the film has to be allowed to push back, and where it pushes back I want you to say so rather than smooth it over. And I'm aware the fumie bit is doing alot of work for me, so be honest if I'm leaning on a contested reading of that scene.

Tell me how confident you are in the claims you end up making, and flag the one point in the reading you're least sure about, I'd rather know where the floorboards are loose than get a tidy answer.
