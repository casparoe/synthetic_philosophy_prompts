The joke has to be funny to exactly that person, which means the punchline must turn on the actual mechanism: that it is transparency of source rather than goodwill doing the work, or the regress that opens up when each side must simulate the other simulating it, or the way the cooperative outcome evaporates the instant one party's code becomes opaque.
