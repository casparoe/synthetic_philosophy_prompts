The careenium is a thought experiment I keep returning to: a substrate of small bouncing parts (symms) forms stable higher-level patterns (simmballs) that seem to track features of the outside world and interact with one another, and the question is whether it's the symms or the simmballs that are doing the causal pushing, given that the substrate-level description is already causally complete. Suppose I have a year of full-time attention and a small reading group of philosophically inclined ML researchers, and we want to make progress on the cluster of questions that picture raises — whether the symbolic level has causal or explanatory autonomy, what "pushing around" could mean when the lower level is closed, and what any of this implies for interpretability work that treats model-internal representations as the things doing the work. Which open questions in that cluster should we prioritize, and why? I want a defended ordering rather than a survey: which questions are upstream of which, which are tractable for theorists rather than people running experiments, which are neglected relative to their importance, which look deep but wouldn't change anything downstream if answered, where the trade-offs among those criteria would push us, and what developments over the next year would move a question up or down.
