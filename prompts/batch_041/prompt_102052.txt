I was arguing with a friend about how to pick an apartment and it turned into something I can't stop picking at. Here's the pattern I keep hitting. I compare two options that differ along one dimension and one of them is clearly better. Then I compare a second pair, shifted by a small amount, and again one is clearly better. I do this a bunch of times, and every single step seems obvious when I look at just that pair -- but when I get to the far end and compare it back to where I started, I want to flip the whole thing, and I can't find any one step where I went wrong. Concretely: I'd trade five minutes of commute for a bit more closet space. I'd trade that closet space for a slightly quieter street. And so on down a long chain of small trades until I've talked myself into a ninety-minute commute in a huge silent apartment, which is the last thing I actually want. Every individual trade felt fine; the chain as a whole is absurd. Same thing happened when we drifted onto charity: one person gets a year of healthy life, or two people each get slightly less, or four people each get slightly less than that... step it up far enough and I'm endorsing a world where an enormous number of people each get a benefit so small they'd never notice it, instead of saving one person's life. I want to say no, but again I can't point at the step where it goes wrong.

I know about the heap / sorites thing, and I don't think this is that -- nothing here is vague, I'm not squinting at a borderline case wondering whether it still counts. I genuinely hold each pairwise judgment. And it isn't just that some things are incomparable, because in each pair I really do think one is better, not neither. So: is there a name for this? I have a feeling it's a known move somewhere in ethics or decision theory, and I'd rather not just get the label back. What I want is the label plus a careful account of what it's actually a name *for*. Is it the name of the argument form -- a chain of small steps that runs you from one end of a spectrum to the other? Or the name of the underlying claim, that "better than" can loop back on itself? Or the name of the diagnosis that my preferences are simply broken? And how does it relate to the heap, and to plain incomparability, and to whatever the people who worry about this think the upshot is? If the honest answer is that there's no single established term and I'm lumping two or three things together, say so and tell me which is which -- the boundaries are what I'm after, not the word.
