Me and my mate Dan got into it for like two hours last night about mental causation, and now I want to check whether my own position even hangs together, because he kept saying it's incoherent and I couldn't tell if he had a point or was just being annoying. Here's roughly where I've landed:

1. Physicalism. The physical facts fix everything else, and nothing non-physical is out there contributing extra force to anything. No spooky pushes.
2. But I'm not a reductionist. When I believe it's about to rain, that belief is a real property of me, and it isnt just another name for whatever my neurons are doing. The neural state realizes the belief, it isn't identical to the belief.
3. Beliefs and desires cause stuff. My belief that it was raining is why I grabbed the umbrella. Change the belief and you change the grabbing.
4. I don't think the world is full of systematic overdetermination. You shouldn't say the grabbing had two separate sufficient causes sitting there, one neural and one mental. That feels like cheating.
5. Causation, as far as I can tell, is a difference-making notion. X causes Y when there's some way of wiggling X, an intervention, that would change Y with everything else along the path held fixed. That's the only test I actually know how to run.
6. I'm definately not a dualist about causation. The mind doesn't lean on the physical world from outside.

Dan's move was this: given what I say in (2), that the belief supervenes on the brain state (you can't change the belief without changing the brain), there's no way to wiggle the belief without wiggling the brain state, so my own test in (5) can never come out in favor of the belief over the neurons. He said the belief is a shadow of the realizer, along for the ride, and the only thing my interventionism will ever certify as a difference-maker is the neural state. Then he added that if a neuroscientist could trigger the arm-grab by zapping the motor cortex, the belief adds nothing, and (3) is just a nice story I tell about myself.

So: which of these are actually in tension with which, which pairs only look like they are, and what has to go? Rank the tensions by how bad they really are, give me the cheapest fix for each and what that fix costs me, and tell me if there's one single revision that would clean up the most. Dont just tell me it's all fine or all broken, I want to know where the real fault line is.
