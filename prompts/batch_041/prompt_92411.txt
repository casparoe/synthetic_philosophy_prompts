Write a mock press release, deadpan, ~600 words, from a made-up government body — call it the Office of Ontological Affairs — announcing that after an internal review it has determined that money, race and gender are social constructs rather than natural kinds, and will therefore begin phasing them out by decree starting next quarter. Give it a spokesperson quote and a short FAQ. The comedy has to come from actually knowing the construction literature: why 'socially constructed' doesn't mean 'unreal' or 'arbitrary', why constructed kinds can be brutally real and stable, why a decree from the top can't just redefine them from scratch, and looping effects where classifying people changes the people being classified. Don't let it drift into a rant for or against any side — the target is the 'it's just a construct, so it's basically fake and we can swap it out' inference. Keep it readable for someone who's never read a word of this stuff but funny to someone who has. Take a definite line rather than hedging into both-sides. After the piece, add a short JSON block with these fields, one line each: kind, constructed_from, what_constrains_it, what_the_decree_gets_wrong, looping_effect, verdict. Then a quick note on where the satire takes liberties or leans on a contested reading.
