I'm assembling a short agenda for a small working group that wants to move the metaphysics of laws of nature forward without simply relitigating the Humean/anti-Humean stalemate. The framing I want to test treats lawhood as local rather than global: instead of asking whether a world's laws are, in general, axioms of its best system or instead governing relations among universals, let lawhood attach piecemeal — to a scale, a domain, or a region of the mosaic — so that a single world could be Humean about some regularities and anti-Humean about others, its laws forming a patchwork rather than one system. Which open questions does this framing make most pressing, and in what order should the group take them up? Defend the ordering: for each question, say why it is upstream of the others, whether it is tractable with the tools metaphysicians already have, and what would move it up or down the list. Single out the two or three that most deserve the group's time — I care more about the reasoning than the list.
