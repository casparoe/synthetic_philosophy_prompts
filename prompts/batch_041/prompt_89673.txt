I'm a high school senior, and for the past few months I've been reading about whether insects can suffer. I've come away more uncertain than I started, which I take to be progress. Now I have a year-long independent project ahead of me and a few hundred dollars of my own money to give away, and I want to aim both at the part of this debate that actually matters rather than at the part that happens to get written about. The way I've been breaking the case down is this: there are an almost unimaginable number of insects; if there is even a small chance that some of them have experiences that count morally, then the expected quantity of morally relevant experience in the world is enormous; and unlike with farmed mammals, nearly everything people do — pesticides, artificial light, mowing, roads, agriculture — bears on insects at a scale we never witness. Every link in that chain seems more contestable than the one before it, and I cannot tell which link is carrying the argument.

What I would like from you is not a survey but an agenda: the open questions in this area that most deserve attention, ranked, with your reasoning made explicit. I care about which questions are upstream of the others, which are tractable for someone with a library card, a year, and no laboratory, which are neglected because they are unglamorous rather than because they are settled, and which ones would, if answered, actually change what a person in my position should do with their money or their time. Please also tell me which questions look central but are not, and what evidence over the next few years would move a question up or down your list.

Two things puzzle me in particular, and I would like to know whether they are real problems or confusions of mine. First, whether the probability that insects are sentient and the moral weight we should give them if they are might not be independent: the same facts about their nervous systems that would raise the first could lower the second, if having thinner experience means mattering less. Second, whether the individual insect is the right unit to count, given how short and how fragile most insect lives are — I can't tell whether counting individuals inflates or deflates the total.

Please write your answer in continuous prose, the way you would write a letter. No headings, no bullet points, no bold text, no tables. American English.
