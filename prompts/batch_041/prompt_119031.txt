So I'm in this metaphysics seminar and we've been doing the statue/clay stuff for a few weeks now — one lump of clay, one statue, two objects in the same place, the lump survives being squashed but the statue doesn't, all that. I get the standard moves (constitution isn't identity, the statue "borrows" its properties from the clay, temporary constitution, etc.) but I keep feeling like everyone's cheating somewhere and nobody will tell me where. So I wrote up my own case that's supposed to jam the usual replies, and I'd like you to tear it appart.

Here's the case, I call it the kiln case. A sculptor makes a clay figurine and puts it in a kiln. Unknown to her, the kiln has a gizmo that at the midpoint of firing annihilates the lump of clay and instantaneously puts a perfect duplicate in its place — same shape, same mass, same microstructure, same position, and the whole thing is seamless from the outside. Stipulate the duplicate is numerically distinct from the original lump. Now we get a fork: (a) if the statue is one and the same statue before and after, then at the swap moment it was constituted by a different lump, so constitution is temporary and can't be any kind of identity — fine, but then "the statue" seems to float free of any particular matter, wich bugs me; (b) if the statue went out of existence and a new one came in, then a statue can be destroyed and created with no sculptor and no damage, which seems absurd. I think every reply has to deny one of these and I want to know which horn is least bad. Also, I've been calling the properties the statue gets from the lump "hand-me-down" properties, and I define it as: x has F as a hand-me-down from y iff y's being F is what makes x F, and x is not identical to y. Is that definately a coherent definition or am I just smuggling in the conclusion?

So, comments please. Three things I actually want: (1) does the kiln case do any real work that the ordinary squashing case doesn't, or is it the same problem dressed up, and if it is the same, where exactly does the new packaging fail; (2) is my hand-me-down definition circular, trivial, or secretly just "borrowed properties" with a new coat of paint; (3) one concrete addition that would make this feel like a real contribution instead of a seminar paper. And if the whole framing is confused, just say so and tell me which part is doing the damage.
