So my question is: is there a version of the impossibility that survives once you relax those framing choices -- let the procedure depend on the protected attribute, allow randomization, split the "score" from the "decision rule" -- or is the impossibility really a theorem about one specific modeling setup that's been dressed up as a theorem about fairness itself?
