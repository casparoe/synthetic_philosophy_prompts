Or the same glass packed in foam, so striking it wouldn't break it, yet it's still fragile. Or the reverse: a toughened glass that isn't fragile at all, but a wizard would shatter it the moment you struck it, so it would break if struck but isn't fragile. The naive conditional gets all three wrong. I dont want a reading list or citations — just the requirements.
