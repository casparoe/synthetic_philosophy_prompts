I think it's actually a stress fracture in the whole theory. His rights are supposed to be side constraints grounded in the Kantian idea that you can't use people as mere means, but he also seems to admit that if you ground that status in rationality or self-consciousness, you get the uncomfortable result that some humans (babies, people with severe cognitive disabilities) wouldn't qualify either, and that once you notice this you can't just quietly make an exception for them.
