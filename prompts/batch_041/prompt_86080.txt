Ok so I need help setting a research agenda and I'm stuck on how to prioritize. Background: a small foundation handed me enough money for about four people for two years, philosophy and theory only — no feildwork, no data collection — and told me to commission work on sex work policy. The catch is that their board is a bit obsessed with a proposal one of the trustees invented. Nobody's written about it and it doesn't line up with any of the existing regimes, so you won't be able to look it up. Roughly: buying and selling are both legal, and the seller's income is untaxed and can't be seized by creditors; but anyone who takes a cut of the transaction — brothel, agency, booking platform, driver, the landlord — needs a license and carries strict liability if the seller comes to harm; and sellers get an unconditional statutory right to walk away from any contract or debt immediately, no penalty. Call it the cut-taker model. It's a mashup of the Nordic model's attitude toward buyers, Dutch-style legalization, and the New Zealand labor-law framing, plus the strict liability twist, which I don't think exists anywhere.

So: given those four people and two years, what are the open conceptual questions that actually matter most here, and in what order? I don't want a survey and I definately don't want a reading list — nobody here has library access and half the literature is paywalled anyway. What I want is a defended agenda: for each question, why it's upstream of the others, whether it's the kind of thing philosophers can actually settle in a room with no data, which ones look important but turn out to be empirical in disguise or purely definitional, and what would push a question up or down the list over the next couple of years. Also be honest about whether the cut-taker model really generates a distinct set of questions or whether it's the same three regimes' questions wearing new labels — if it's the second, say so and tell me which of the older questions I should be funding instead, since the money has to go somewhere.
