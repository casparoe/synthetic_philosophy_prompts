Ok so I never got past high school physics but I've been chewing on this one for months and I want to do a bit about it at an open mic night.

Heres the thing thats been bugging me. Everyone says the laws of physics dont care which way time runs, you can play the equations backwards and theyre still fine, and yet theres this one thing that absolutely refuses to run backwards: stuff gets messier. Coffee goes cold, eggs dont unscramble, a tidy room turns into a tip but a tip never turns back into a tidy room. Fine. And when you ask why, the answer you get is that its not the laws doing it, its just statistics, because there are so many more messy arrangements than tidy ones that going messier is overwhelmingly more likely.

But more likely compared to what? Its not like theres a big pile of universes to draw from and we can see how most of them went. What people seem to actually mean is that the universe started out tidy, and then everything else follows from that. Except nobody can tell me why it started tidy. They just assume it and give the assumption a name, and then they turn around and back it up with stuff like our memories and old photographs, which only exist in the first place because of the very thing theyre supposed to be explaining. That loop cracks me up and I want the joke to live right in there.

So: write me a joke about the arrow of time. The punchline has to turn on the fact that the arrow isnt hiding in the equations at all, its hiding in the universe having started in some special tidy state that nobody can account for, plus the fact that our only evidence for that tidy start is all the mess it left behind. It should make someone who actually knows this area laugh at the content, not just go haha a physics joke. Dialogue or standup bit, your call. And give it a fresh image, not the cold coffee one, and please no fruit flies like a banana, I've heard it.
