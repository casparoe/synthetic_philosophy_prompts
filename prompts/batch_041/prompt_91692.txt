Me and a mate have been going round in circles for about a year over so-called acausal threats, and we've stopped getting anywhere. The setup, as I understand it: some agent, or a predictor, or a simulation of you, works out in advance how you'll react to a threat, and then harms you if and only if that prediction says you'd refuse to give in. Your actual choice doesn't cause the harm; the prediction has already fixed it. He thinks these threats are real, and that the sensible response is to become the sort of person who can't be moved by them, even when caving would be cheaper — maybe even by adopting some stand-in goal you don't really care about, so that threats aimed at what you do care about just bounce off. I think it's a swindle: a threat that doesn't depend on what I do can't give me a reason to do anything, and calling it a commitment doesn't change that. Neither of us has any academic training, so keep the jargon light.
