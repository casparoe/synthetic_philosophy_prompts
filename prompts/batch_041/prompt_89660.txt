I have recently been building a probabilistic reasoning system that must update on conditional statements from experts—statements of the form "If A, then B" where neither A nor B is certain. In testing it, I ran into the Judy Benjamin problem: an agent with a prior over four cells (Red/First, Red/Second, Blue/First, Blue/Second) receives a message that says, in effect, "I can't tell whether you're in Red or Blue, but if you're in Red, you're in the Second Company area." The agent can conditionalize on the material conditional, or it can minimize relative entropy subject to the constraint that the posterior probability of Second Company given Red is 1. These methods can disagree, and I need to decide which to implement. Write an essay on this controversy. Weigh the arguments for each approach, explain what each assumes about the meaning of the conditional, and reach a justified conclusion about which method is correct—or about what the problem shows about updating on conditionals more generally.
