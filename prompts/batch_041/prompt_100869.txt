My opening: ok, it's a brake. But a brake you can't release isn't a brake, it's a handbrake someone welded down. Say a measure squeaks through 51-49 and within a year it's obvious 60 percent would vote it down — but it's now politically impossible to undo and the losers are just stuck with it. You'll want to say that's the price of the brake being real, that a revocable brake is no brake at all. But notice what that does: the whole reason the referendum was supposed to be valuable is that it lets people refuse to be stuck with things they'd never consent to, and now the 60 percent are stuck with exactly that. Your brake has manufactured the very entrapment it was sold as preventing. So which way do you jump — and whatever you say, tell me what it costs you elsewhere in the view.
