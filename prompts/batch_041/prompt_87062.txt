I'm a backend dev, no philosophy training at all, and I've got myself stuck on this wasp. The story, which I'm probably mangling: a digger wasp paralyzes a cricket and drags it to her burrow. Before bringing it in, she goes inside to check the place out. While she's in there, if you nudge the cricket an inch or two away from the entrance, she comes back out, drags it to the threshold again, and goes in to check again. Nudge it again, she does the whole routine again. Apparently you can do this forty times and she never twigs. Someone told me there's a name for this and I forget it.

Anyway, here's where I've landed and I'd like to know if it actually hangs together:

1. Everything I do is caused by stuff that came before. Rewind my brain to the exact same state and I do the same thing again. Same goes for the wasp.
2. But there's a real difference between us. She runs a fixed script. I can stop, notice what I'm doing, and reconsider. That's not just a longer script, it's a different kind of thing.
3. Free will, whatever it turns out to be, needs the ability to have done otherwise. She couldn't. I could.
4. I could be sphexish without knowing it. There could be a cricket-move for me — some trigger that makes me run my loop again and again while I feel like I'm deliberating. My feeling of flexibility is no evidence that I actually have it.
5. What breaks the loop is representing my own routine. I can watch myself doing the thing and interrupt it. That's the supervisor process, and that's what she doesn't have.
6. Nothing in my head is magic. Same kind of stuff as hers, just more of it and arranged differently.

None of these seem crazy on their own. But I have a nagging feeling they can't all be true at once. Which pairs are actually in tension and which only look that way, how bad is each one, and what's the cheapest thing to give up in each case? And if you had to drop one of the six to make the rest cohere, which one, and what does that cost me? Please be specific about which one has to go rather than telling me they're all in tension — I'm fairly sure at least one of them is definately fine and I'd like to know which.
