Write a joke about Moore's paradox -- the "its raining out but I dont believe its raining" thing. The punchline should turn on the actual content: the sentence isn't contradictory (both halves could be true, the guy could just be wrong about his own head), and yet you can't say it, whereas "it's raining and you don't believe it" is completely fine, and so is saying it about yourself in the past tense. So it should be funny to someone who already knows why the thing is weird, rather than a joke that just explains the paradox back to them. Maybe set it somewhere -- a life coach writing affirmations, a weatherman signing off, a guy on a first date -- but it needs a real punchline, not a restatement.
