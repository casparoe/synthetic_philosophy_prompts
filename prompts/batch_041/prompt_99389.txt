My friend Dana is doing a neuropsych PhD and she's gotten me completely obsessed with split-brain stuff — we argue about it over dinner constantly and she is annoyingly sure of herself about everything. Her calls so far: the classic patient with the whole corpus callosum cut, who can only name what's flashed to one visual field and whose two hands fight over the same shirt button — two minds, no question. A patient whose callosotomy was only partial and left the back of the callosum intact, who behaves like a totally ordinary person and says she feels like one person — one mind. A woman who had one whole hemisphere taken out as a kid and is now basically fine — one mind, Dana says obviously, there's only one side left to have a mind with.
