Maya said: the evidence never picks out a theory, because for any theory there's always another one that fits every observation anyone will ever make, so choosing between them is a choice and not a discovery. She also said the whole thing is worse than it looks, because if the two theories agree on every observation forever, then there's no experiment you could ever run to decide, so it's not just that we dont know which is true, it's that nothing about the world settles it.
