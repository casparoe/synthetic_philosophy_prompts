He says every trade made him better off at the moment he made it, so he can't possibly have ended up behind, and the $15 is just some bookkeeping illusion. He also says that if someone had offered him all three trades as one package he'd of turned it down, but he doesn't see why that matters, since he doesn't make decisions based on what he's going to want later, only on what he wants when he's actually deciding. Somethings off here and I can't tell if it's him or me. Who's right, and what should I say?
