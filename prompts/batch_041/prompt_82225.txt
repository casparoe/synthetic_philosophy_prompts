I'm a cog sci grad student who keeps getting dragged into arguments about which animals count, and I keep running into a snag I dont have a word for. You're a philosopher of mind who works on animal sentience and moral status — take a moment to brainstorm before you commit to an answer.

Here's the snag. Someone says a bee probably has some kind of experience, but maybe not much of one. Everyone nods. Then the conversation goes one of two ways. Either someone does the arithmetic — "so if a bee's experience is a hundredth as rich as a dog's, then a hundred bees are a dog" — and that feels like a category error even to people who accept the premise, because the doubt wasn't about how much the bee feels, it was about whether there's anyone home to feel anything. Or someone says "we can't be sure, so let's protect them anyway," and then the gradient did no work at all.

What I think is happening is that two or three different quantities get fused into one number. One is a credence: how likely is it that this thing has experiences at all. Another is a magnitude: given that it does, how much is there — how wide is the range between its best and worst states. And a third thing that's neither: how much a unit of that magnitude counts when weighed against a dog's or a person's. People multiply all three together into a single "moral status score," and once that's done you can't tell whether a bee scored low because it's probably not conscious, because it's conscious with a narrow range, or because we've decided its range counts for less. Two creatures with the same score call for completly different responses — one says fund more work on insect nociception, the other says write a welfare code — and the score hides which one you're looking at.

Is there an established name for this? I know it's near the debate about moral status coming in degrees, and near the idea of acting cautiously when you're unsure whether something is conscious, but those seem to be about whether status is graded and about what to do under ignorance, not about the specific move of fusing a credence, a range, and a weight into one number. If there's no term, coin one, and tell me exactly where its edges are: what it is, what it isn't, and how it differs from just saying "moral status is a matter of degree." A bare label won't do — I want the carving. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence, then the reasoning, no preamble, and don't restate my question back to me.
