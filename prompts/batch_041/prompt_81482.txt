Write a joke about Thomson's lamp — the puzzle where someone flicks a lamp on and off faster and faster, each flick taking half as long as the last, so that by the two-minute mark they've flicked it infinitely many times and there's no final flick. I want the punchline to actaully turn on the puzzle, not just "ooh, infinity." An angle I like, if it helps: the total time the lamp spends on is perfectly finite — those shrinking on-intervals sum to a well-defined amount — so the power company has no trouble at all billing you for the electricity. It's only whether the lamp ends up on that nobody can say. Keep it short, and don't cop out with "it's both on and off" or "it's on because the flicks were even."
