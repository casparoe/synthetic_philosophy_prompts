Write a short joke about the Kalai–Smorodinsky bargaining solution. It should be funny to someone who's actually seen the solution, i.e. the punchline turns on how it works: KS picks the point on the Pareto frontier that sits on the straight line from the disagreement point to the ideal (utopia) point, so both players walk away with the same *proportion* of their gains toward their own best case — unlike Nash, which maximizes the product of gains. A setup where the two bargainers have very lopsided aspirations, one of them barely wanting anything, would be good, since the "fair" proportional split then looks ridiculous in absolute terms. If you can also work in that expanding one player's options can make the Nash bargainer worse off while KS is immune to that (individual monotonicity), great. Keep it tight, and dont lean on anything that only lands if the listener doesn't actually know the solution.
