The room is mostly RL and predictive-processing people who think representation is just whatever a decoder can read out, plus two old-school Fodorians who think any selectionist story is either trivial or hopelessly indeterminate. Prep me for Q&A: the eight or so objections I'll actually get from this crowd, not from a philosophy of mind conference, ordered by how likely and how damaging they are, phrased the way an RL person or a Fodorian would put it, each with a short reply and a note on where the reply is soft. Include the one I'm hoping nobody asks -- probably the selectionless duplicate thing -- and tell me what I should concede up front so I dont look evasive.
