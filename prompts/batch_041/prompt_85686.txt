Ok, I've been bugging myself with this for months and I finally want to know if there's a word for it. It's a kind of explanation that talks like there's a mind in the room when there obviously isn't, and I can't decide if it's just lazy phrasing or a real separate kind of thing.

Stuff I keep bumping into. My houseplants lean at the window and everyone says they're reaching for the light. A slime mold gets dropped in a maze with food at both ends and the writeups say it solves the maze and remembers where the food was. I mess around with little evolution simulators and I catch myself saying the creatures figured out that hanging near the water is safer. Somebody tells me a fever is the body trying to cook the infection. And supposedly a moth spirals into a candle because it's running a navigation trick that works fine under the moon and goes haywire under a porch light. No trick, no moon, no moth doing math, and yet.

Heres the part that stops me from writing the whole thing off as a figure of speech. You can grade these. You can say a design is stupid, or that the plant's move is fine on one window and hopeless on another, or that the moth's method is a good rule of thumb that the modern world has broken. When you say that, you're not just reporting that the thing happened — you're saying it happened for a point, or had a point that no longer holds. That sounds like it needs a reason floating around somewhere. But the reason isn't in the plant, and its not in the moth, and its not in me watching.

So is there a name for this — a reason with nobody to have it, something you can call smart or dumb while no one anywhere is being smart or dumb? And I don't want just the label, I want the carving. How does it come apart from: calling it a metaphor or as-if talk; saying evolution designed things on purpose; saying a heart's function is to pump, which feels like a different move somehow; real goal-seeking with feedback, like a thermostat or a homing missile; and plain anthropomorphism, which is supposed to just be a mistake. If there's no single established term, say so, then give me the best one and define it, and be sharp about the edges — is the thermostat in or out? Is a market price in or out? Is a river picking the easy path in or out? Is a feed algorithm that wants me to keep scrolling in or out, since somebody did build it to do that? And when I say one of these reasons is a good one or a bad one, what's actually doing the work there?

American English, please, and please don't just say "it's teleology" and stop — I know that word and it doesn't scratch the itch.
