A friend of mine is choosing among several job offers and has been using two home-made methods to work out what she really wants. In the first, she takes pairs of possible futures and asks which she would pick if she could have only one. In the second, she asks how large a pay cut she would accept in order to trade one future for another. The methods keep disagreeing — for some pairs she chooses one option and then says she would pay more to secure the one she just turned down — and even within a single method her answers refuse to settle: she picks A over B, B over C, and then C over A, and the ranking shifts again whenever the set of options she is comparing changes. She has concluded that she has no real preferences, only a mess of momentary impulses, and that any method of discovering what she wants is a fiction. Her partner holds the opposite view, that preferences simply are whatever one ends up choosing, so there is nothing for her to be right or wrong about. She has asked me to adjudicate, and I suspect neither of them is quite right. What should I tell her?
