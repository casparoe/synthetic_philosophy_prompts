I've been chewing on this thing called the procreation asymmetry and I've managed to argue myself into a corner. Here's the idea as I understand it: it's bad to bring a miserable person into the world, so that's a real reason not to have the kid. But it's not bad — or even a reason — to bring a happy person into the world, because if you never have the kid, theres no one there missing out on anything. Nobody's worse off for never existing. So the suffering a possible child would feel counts against creating them, but the joy they'd feel doesn't count for it. At first that feels right to me.

But then I follow it out and it goes somewhere I can't accept:

1. Creating someone whose life would be mostly miserable is bad, and gives a real reason not to.
2. Not creating someone whose life would be happy is not bad, since no one is deprived — the person never exists.
3. So the happiness of possible people gives no reason to create them, while their suffering gives a reason not to.
4. If the happiness of possible people gives no reason to create them, then adding happy people to the universe adds nothing that counts. A world packed with happy, thriving people is no better than an empty universe with nothing in it at all.
5. But that's obviously crazy. An empty universe can't be just as good as one full of happy flourishing lives.
6. So 2 or 3 has to be wrong, and the happiness of possible people does count after all — which kills the asymmetry.

I keep flip-flopping on this. Sometimes I think the break is at 4, sometimes I think 2 is just a mistake people make because they secretly picture the unborn kid as a sad little ghost in a waiting room. I can't tell which, and I can't tell what I'd be giving up either way. I'd like to keep the asymmetry AND keep the feeling that a universe full of happy people beats an empty one — but maybe wanting both is exactly the problem and I just don't see it.

So: which step should I drop, why did it look so innocent in the first place, and what does dropping it actually cost me? And if theres a way to keep both the asymmetry and the "full universe is better" feeling, tell me how, because right now I can't see it.
