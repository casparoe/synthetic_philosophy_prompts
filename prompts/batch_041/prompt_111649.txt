So my condo building's HOA is a mess and I could really use an outside opinion. We elect a board every year, but the same five people have run it for like a decade because nobody else can be bothered to show up to the meetings, and honestly the budget votes get decided by whoever's in the room, which is usually them plus two or three of their friends. Anyway, my neighbor Dave (retired, reads a lot, weirdly into Roman history) is now pushing a proposal: instead of electing the board, we'd draw names out of a hat from every unit owner, those people serve for a year, and they'd get to make the real calls like the new roof assessment and whether we ban short-term rentals. He says its the only way the board actually looks like the building, that a random group who sit down and actually talk it through beats a vote that only the clique turns up for, and that since everyone has an equal shot at being picked, nobody can complain they got shut out. He even wants to make it mandatory like jury duty, if your name comes up you serve, no opting out, though he'd let people pay a fee to get out of it if they really can't.

My problem is I can't tell if this is a great idea or a terrible one. Part of me thinks he's right that the current setup is basically a tiny club deciding everything for everyone and calling it democracy. But part of me feels like if I didn't get picked, then a bunch of random neighbors I've never met get to decide how much I pay for a roof, and I never agreed to that, whereas at least with a vote I could try to persuade people or run myself. And isn't there something off about handing decisions to people who didn't want the job and might not know the first thing about reserve funds or roofing? The pay-to-get-out thing also bugs me, it seems like it just lets the well-off dodge while everyone else has to serve. Can you help me figure out what's actually at stake here, like what the strongest version of Dave's case is versus the strongest version of my worry, and whether there's a principled reason to prefer one of these over the other? I'm not looking for "just talk to your neighbors" advice, I genuinely want to understand the thing underneath it.
