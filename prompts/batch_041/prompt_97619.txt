I want one essay question on the exclusion arguement that a student can't get through by reciting the supervenience version from memory. The hook I want baked in: the usual argument leans on a sufficiency or production picture of causation -- the physical realizer is enough for the effect, so the mental property can't be doing any separate work. Does exclusion still go through?
