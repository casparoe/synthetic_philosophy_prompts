Our terms of reference are the real mess: they say we can recommend removal only for "conduct that would be disqualifying under current law" or for "facts not established at the time of naming," which the lawyers wrote to keep us out of trouble and which now means half the committee is hiding behind "it was legal in 1945" and the other half is saying the terms of reference are themselves a cop-out. The chair wants a memo she can circulate before the vote: a clear recommendation (take the name off / keep it / keep it and add a context plaque), with a time frame for whatever comes next, the reasoning, and the strongest case for whichever option she doesn't pick, written so its supporters would recognise it as their own view rather than a straw man.
