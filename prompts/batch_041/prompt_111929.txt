Something I keep going back and forth on: everyone knows the tragedy of the commons — a shared resource gets overused because each user pockets the full benefit of taking a bit more while only eating a fraction of the cost. But I keep noticing its mirror image, where a resource gets underused instead, because too many separate people each hold a veto over it (a patent, a permit, a holdout claim), so nothing ever gets assembled or put to use. Compare the two for me. Are they really the same game with the signs flipped, or are they structurally different — say, one being about rivalry/subtractability and the other about excludability? And does that difference mean the standard remedies (privatize it, or let the users work out their own governance) fix one of them while making the other worse? Also tell me whether either is really a prisoner's dilemma, or whether both are just coordination problems with a bad equilibrium.
