Okay so I've been trying to figure out what I actually think "intelligence" is by going case by case on my gut, and my gut is all over the place. A chess engine that crushes grandmasters: intelligent, no question. A look-up table that has every legal chess position memorized and plays perfectly by just reading off the answer: not intelligent at all, even though it plays better than the engine. A pocket calculator: not intelligent. A thermostat that clicks the heat on when it's cold: not intelligent. A slime mold that grows through a maze and leaves a tube along the shortest path to the food: ...kind of? It feels like it's solving the maze, which bothers me alot. A bee that comes back and dances to tell the hive which direction the flowers are: intelligent, a little. The whole hive: seems smarter than any single bee, which is weird. An immune system that learns a new pathogen and remembers it for years: feels intelligent, but there's nobody home. My dog: yes. A mouse learning a maze: yes. AlphaFold predicting a protein fold: I want to say no, it's just cranking a function, but then why is the chess engine a yes? A language model writing a coherent paragraph: yes, but I'm nervous about it. Natural selection producing an eye over eons: not intelligent, it has no mind. A market discovering the price of a thing: no. A self-driving car: sort of, but isn't it mostly the same stuff as the calculator?

The two I can't square are (a) chess engine yes vs. look-up table no, when they end up playing the same moves, and (b) slime mold kind-of-yes vs. thermostat no, when both are just following a physical gradient with no representation of anything.

So: is there a principle that gets most of these, or do I have to give some of them up? If there's a candidate, please state it precisly, tell me which of my verdicts it forces me to drop, and say what it predicts for a case I haven't listed -- say, a corporation, or a city, or a brainless robot that navigates purely by following a chemical trail. And I'd like the answer in American English please.
