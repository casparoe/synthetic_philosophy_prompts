Question: "We framed HCH as an idealization of deliberation. (a) Define HCH and factored cognition, and say how they relate. (b) Nobody expects to build HCH. Say what it's for, and why the idealization is supposed to be useful anyway. (c) Give one reason to think the HCH picture is ill-defined, and say what that implies for how oversight should be done."

Student's answer:

(a) HCH is an idealized agent. You start with a human who can break a task into sub-tasks and hand each one to a copy of themselves, recursively, until the sub-tasks are small enough for a human to just do directly, with no delegation. So HCH is a human with unlimited time and unlimited help from copies of themselves. Factored cognition is the claim that a big task can be split into sub-tasks that can be solved separately and then recombined. The two are really the same picture: HCH is the agent, and factored cognition is just the assumption that the recursion bottoms out in tractable pieces — so it isn't a separate empirical claim about how tasks happen to be structured, it's part of how HCH is set up.

(b) The point is to give you a target for human-level when the thing you're training is supposed to do oversight. You can't train a model to imitate a human with unlimited time and help, so instead you define HCH and train the model to imitate the amplified human (iterated amplification); with a perfect imitator and unlimited compute you basically get HCH back out. It's useful because it's defined in terms of humans, so the values that come out are ours, and because it gives you a fixed thing to measure systems against.

(c) The standard worry is that the decomposition isn't canonical. There are lots of ways to split any task, different splits give different answers, and nothing in the setup says which split is the right one. And since every node in the tree is just a human, who can be pushed around by a confident-sounding sub-answer, a clever decomposer can steer the whole tree. So HCH is really just whatever a human would be talked into, which is why people want something like debate or prediction markets instead.
