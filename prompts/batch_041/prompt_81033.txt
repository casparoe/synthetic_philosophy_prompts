Write a joke about Dennett's four grades of creature — Darwinian, Skinnerian, Popperian, Gregorian. Not the lazy version where the punchline is just "animals are dumb, humans use iPhones"; it has to be funny to someone who actually knows the framework, so the punchline should turn on what really seperates the grades. Here's the angle I want you to build it on, since I doubt you've seen this one before: rank the four by who pays for the error. A Darwinian creature pays with its life. A Skinnerian pays with pain. A Popperian pays nothing, because the mistake dies in the simulation instead of on its body. And a Gregorian pays nothing either — except that the model it just threw away was built by somebody else, and it never once checked it against the world. Make the joke land on that last one. A paragraph or so, with an actual punchline.
