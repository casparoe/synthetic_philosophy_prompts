I am not a lawyer or a philosopher, but for the past year I have been thinking about a problem I cannot resolve on my own: what happens when a set of rules is allowed to rewrite itself, including the rules that govern how rules are rewritten. I host a monthly game night for six to eight adults at a community center, and I want to design a one-session game—roughly ninety minutes, no real money, materials limited to index cards, pens, and a whiteboard—that makes the players feel the force of this problem before anyone explains it to them. The game should not be a lecture about self-amending rules; the rules and incentives should arrange things so that the players themselves run into the paradox. I am especially interested in moments like these: a proposed rule that changes the threshold required to repeal itself; a rule that says an amendment is valid only if it is passed according to a procedure that the amendment itself creates; a rule that tries to entrench itself against all future changes; a rule that redefines what counts as an amendment, so that some earlier amendments are retroactively not amendments; and a situation where the group cannot tell whether a rule is currently in force because the rule that determines validity has been amended by a procedure whose validity is exactly what is in question. Please design the game in enough detail that I can run it without improvising: the setup, the roles or factions if any, the starting rules, the turn structure, how a rule is proposed and adopted, how conflicts are adjudicated, and how the session ends. Include a debrief that maps what happened onto the underlying issues—self-reference, self-amendment, entrenchment, validity, and the regress that opens when a legal system tries to ground its own procedures—without assuming the players know any academic vocabulary. Also tell me, honestly, what will predictably go wrong the first time I run it, which part of the paradox the game cannot reproduce and that I will have to supply in the debrief, and how I should handle the player who tries to win by exploiting the rules rather than engaging with the puzzle. Write everything in American English.
