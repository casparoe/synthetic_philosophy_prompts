Give me a map of what would count against this position, not a defense of it. Which arguments, cases, intuitions, or empirical findings tell against it? Also tell me what the position predicts that rival views do not, and whether it risks anything at all or has been insulated by qualifications. If it is well insulated, say so and say whether that is a problem. End with a one-sentence summary as the last line, beginning with "Summary:".
