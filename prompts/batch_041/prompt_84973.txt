I'm putting together a handout for a reading group session where we do Browning and Goldhagen back to back alongside the old social-psych obedience material, and I want a decision tree we can argue over: it should take someone from their view of what actually needs explaining to a named position on why the men did it. My hunch is that most of our disagreements are really disagreements about the explanandum and nobody notices, so make that the first fork — the single act (why did these particular men shoot), the rate (why did so many of the men in one unit take part), the variation (why did a handful refuse, hang back or ask for other work while others volunteered), or the possibility (how does a state get mass killing done at all, using men with ordinary jobs). The commander of the battalion we're reading about offered his men the chance to step out before the first big action and almost nobody took it, so that offer should be doing work somewhere in the tree. Every later node has to be answerable by someone who doesn't know where it leads — no nodes like "do you think antisemitism was the main cause?"

Nodes I want somewhere on the tree: whether the account is meant to give the cause of the behaviour or an account of how it looked permissible to the men at the time; whether the operative variable is the situation, a standing disposition of the person, a belief they held, or the division of labour that kept any one person's contribution small; whether the relevant disposition is supposed to be universal or specific to that population; and whether the account has to accommodate the men who declined as well as the ones who shot. Add one or two nodes of your own if the logical space needs them.

Leaves, at minimum: a Goldhagen-style view (a specifically German, eliminationist antisemitism did the work), a Browning-style ordinary-men view, an obedience-to-authority view in the Milgram mould, a bureaucratic-rationality view in the Arendt/Bauman mould, a small-hardcore-true-believers view, and a view where the individual-character variable carries the weight (some men were just like that, most weren't). Plus a leaf for any combination of answers that nobody actually defends.

Two extra asks. First, flag any pair of leaves the tree can't seperate on the evidence we have — I suspect at least one pair looks different in prose and identical on the cases — and for each say what further case or datum would seperate them, or say none is available. Second, for each empty combination say whether its empty because the view is incoherent, merely unattractive, or just unexplored.

Keep it to eight to twelve nodes, and don't pull punches, we're all adults. Give it to me as JSON with these fields:

- nodes: list of {id, question, branches: [{answer, next}]}
- leaves: list of {id, position (short label, no citations needed), path (the answer at each node in order), core_claim, most_awkward_commitment, which_explanandum_it_handles_best}
- empty_combinations: list of {path, why_nobody_goes_there, diagnosis (incoherent / unattractive / unexplored)}
- inseparable_pairs: list of {leaf_a, leaf_b, why_indistinguishable, separating_case (null if none)}
