That seems weak to me though — if you just rotated the whole universe 180 degrees, left and right would swap and nothing else would change, so how can 'left of' be doing any real work? Then someone else told me the real answer is that the two spheres are actually a single object, which sounds like a cheat. And I keep hearing the phrase 'impure properties' thrown around, which I don't really get. Can you walk me through what the example is actually supposed to show, whether my friend's left/right move survives the rotation thing, and whether there's an honest way to keep the law alive here?
