I'm a software engineer, no philosophy training beyond a couple of pop-science books and too many podcasts, and I can't stop thinking about the sphex wasp. Hofstadter made sphexishness out of that, Dennett leaned on it when he wrote about free will, and I've also heard the wasp story is oversold -- that she does eventually adapt, and that the repeated nest check might even be the smart move. Either way it's the thing I keep circling: is our own flexibility just this, one level up?
