I have been reading about Fitch's paradox of knowability, and my understanding is that it establishes the existence of truths that are unknowable in principle — facts that no possible inquirer, however idealized, could ever come to know. What I would like to understand is where these unknowable truths are to be found. Are they concentrated in particular subject matters, such as the remote past, the contents of other minds, or the far future, and is there any principled way to recognize in advance that a given question has an unknowable answer, so that inquiry can be directed elsewhere?
