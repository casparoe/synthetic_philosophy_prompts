A research funder now requires every predictive model it supports to pass a complexity audit: the model's description is compressed under a fixed universal coding scheme, models with longer compressed descriptions are penalized, and the choice of scheme is treated as a neutral convention. The audit further exempts complexity attributable to irreducible noise, distinguishing model complexity from data complexity. What theory of induction does this practice presuppose? Does it commit the funder to the claim that nature itself is low in Kolmogorov complexity, or only to a methodological preference for simpler hypotheses? And can the noise exemption be made principled given that the invariance theorem guarantees only asymptotic equivalence up to an additive constant, while the audit ranks models on finite samples?
