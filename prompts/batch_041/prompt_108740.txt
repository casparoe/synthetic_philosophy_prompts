i want to stress-test that. can you give me the correspondence table first — what plays the role of encoding, consolidation, the retrieval cue, forgetting, the flashbulb — and then tell me where the mapping actually breaks? i suspect it breaks in interesting places (who counts as the rememberer, what the cue even is, whether there's a single trace at all), but i want to know which breaks matter for the conclusion and which are just noise.
