Ok so I'm putting together a problem set on the experience machine and theories of well-being, and I keep hitting the same wall: every version of the case that shows up in the standard materials is the classic one, hedonism gets dunked on, everyone nods, and the model just recites it back. I want a case that dosn't do the obvious thing.

Here's the spec. Build me one scenario — one paragraph, ordinary-ish, one machine, no time travel, no afterlife, no second machine, and the subject has to be a normal adult (not coerced, not clinically depressed, no brain damage) — engineered so the three theories come apart like this: hedonism (net pleasure and pain are the only things that matter for a life going well) and objective-list theory (stuff like knowledge, achievement, and deep personal relationships count no matter how you feel or what you want) both say DON'T plug in, while desire-satisfaction (a life goes well to the extent your desires get satisfied) says DO plug in. So the odd one out is the desire theory, not hedonism. That's the whole point of the exercise.

Some more constraints:
- The verdicts have to fall out of the theories, not out of me stipulating them. Don't just write "she desires it, and the desire theory says desires matter." Make the case such that a careful reader can watch each theory land where I want it, on its own.
- It should be a genuine experience machine (what you experience is decoupled from what's really happening) but I don't want the case to turn on her being tricked about her past or her identity — keep the deception to the content of the experience itself.
- I want a single feature doing the work — one variable I could flip that would make all three theories agree. Name that feature, then actually tell me what happens when I flip it.
- If it turns out I can't get all three verdicts I asked for without cheating, don't fudge it — say which one won't cooperate and why.

Write the scenario, then give me the whole write-up as a JSON object with exactly these fields:

- "scenario": the case, as one paragraph.
- "hedonism": an object with "verdict" ("plug in" or "stay out") and "reason" (one or two sentences, and the reason has to be about net pleasure/pain).
- "desire_satisfaction": an object with "verdict" and "reason" (same idea, reason about desires getting satisfied).
- "objective_list": an object with "verdict" and "reason" (reason about the objective goods).
- "the_one_variable": a string naming the single feature that's doing the work.
- "flip_it": a string saying what each of the three verdicts becomes if I reverse that feature.
- "counterattack": a string describing how a defender of the dissenting theory would try to break the case.
- "honest_note": a string flagging any place the case cheats, stipulates a verdict instead of generating it, or is wierd in a way I should know about.
