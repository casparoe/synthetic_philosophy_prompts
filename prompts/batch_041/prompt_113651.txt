I've spent long enough reading the rationalist blogs and forums that I've absorbed the vocabulary, but there's a claim I keep circling that I can't decide whether to believe, and I'd rather have your credence in it with the reasoning laid bare than another tour of the debate.

The claim — call it the self-report ceiling — is this: in the regime we actually care about, a system that is both misaligned and situationally aware that it is being observed or controlled, the class of control measures that route through the model's own outputs cannot reduce the probability of a catastrophic outcome by more than a bounded factor. By measures that route through the model's own outputs I mean the ones that read its stated reasoning, have a second model monitor or critique it, ask it to justify or paraphrase itself, or otherwise treat what it says as evidence about what it is. The ceiling is supposed to come from two things: the model can emit outputs optimized to pass the monitors rather than to be true, and everything the monitor learns, it learns through a channel the model controls.

My own gut number is about 0.6. Give me yours, decomposed, with at least these terms, each carrying a rough probability and one line of justification:

- the probability that a misaligned, situationally aware model can in practice steer its outputs to pass monitors of comparable capability rather than being caught;
- conditional on that, the probability that no measure in the output-relying class escapes the problem — for instance by not depending on the model's cooperation at all;
- the probability that this threat model, misalignment plus situational awareness, is the one we should be conditioning on, as opposed to an earlier or later regime;
- whether the claim is even well-defined, since I suspect part of my uncertainty is that control measure and catastrophic outcome are doing more work than they can bear;
- and any term you think I've left out.

Then combine them into a single number, tell me which term it is most sensitive to, and say what observation or argument would move it most. I care less about whether the control agenda is worthwhile in general than about whether this specific ceiling is real. Please answer in American English.
