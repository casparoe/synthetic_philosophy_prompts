(A) Nothing other than the absence of craving and suffering is intrinsically good. (B) Nothing is intrinsically bad unless it involves craving, where craving means a felt pull toward or push away from something, a thirst, an urge — so pain, grief or misery that involves no craving at all would not be bad in itself. Please decompose rather than just handing me a number.
