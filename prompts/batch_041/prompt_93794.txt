My closest friend's father died last spring. The dad was a quiet ham-radio guy who never said much, and he left behind a shoebox of cassette tapes and a notebook full of short strings of letters and numbers that might be some kind of code, or might be nothing at all. My friend has gotten it into his head that his father was leaving him a message. Lately he's been feeding the notebook to an AI cipher-cracker, and it keeps spitting out little "decodings" that read like tender notes in his dad's voice, and now he's sure. He keeps asking me what I think, and I don't want to crush him or just play along, but honestly I'm not sure I can even state the question clearly. When he asks "is there a message in here," what is he actually asking? Is the meaning sitting there in the notebook waiting to be read, or is it something he and the machine are putting into it — and does that distinction even matter if it's helping him grieve? What should I tell him?
