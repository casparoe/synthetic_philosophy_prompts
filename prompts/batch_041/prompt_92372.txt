1) how much you weight the mixed cases — bittersweet things, a sad song thats somehow pleasurable, schadenfreude — where an experience seems pleasant and unpleasant at once. if those are genuine, does that push you toward two independent axes (a positive system and a negative system that can both be firing) rather than one bipolar pleasantness dial? and how much does that count against a single shared property?

3) whether you'd count a shared neural or functional mechanism, if someone found one, as evidence for the phenomenal claim or as a change of subject.
4) how much of your number is hostage to whether pleasure is a natural kind at all, versus a cluster concept we should maybe just stop trying to pin down.
