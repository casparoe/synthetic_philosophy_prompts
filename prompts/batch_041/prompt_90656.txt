Right, so I've been argueing about this online for a few years and I want someone to tell me straight whether my position actually holds together, because I suspect it doesn't but I can't see where the crack is.

Here's what I think. (1) Toleration is close to the most important thing about a free society — I'd rather let people say vile things than have someone decide which speech is allowed, because whoever gets to decide will eventually decide against me. (2) But a society that tolerates the people who want to end toleration is signing its own death warrant; you don't have to let the people who'd shut the whole thing down use the system to do it. (3) So I'm fine with banning openly anti-democratic movements, and the speech that builds them. (4) I also think the government should be neutral between different visions of a good life — it shouldn't be in the business of ranking ways of living. (5) I don't actually trust anyone to draw the line on what counts as "intolerant"; in practice it's whoever's in charge labelling their opponents, and I've watched that happen. (6) Private platforms booting whoever they like is fine by me. The government doing it is not — that's kind of the whole point.

I know there's a standard answer to the (1)+(2)+(3) thing, the paradox of tolerance, and I basically buy it, so I don't think that's where the problem is.

What's been nagging me: imagine a party that openly wants to cancel elections but is currently tiny, like half a percent. Do I ban it now, before it can do any damage — which seems to stomp all over (1)? Or do I wait until it's actually a threat, by which point banning it looks exactly like the ruling party jailing its rivals, which is the thing (5) is complaining about? I cant tell if that's a real problem in my views or just me wanting it both ways.

So: which of these are genuinely in tension, which only look like they are, and what's the cheapest fix for each real one? Rank them by how bad the tension is rather than saying everything's a bit tense. Start with your bottom line in the first sentence and then give the reasoning — no preamble, and don't restate my question back at me.
