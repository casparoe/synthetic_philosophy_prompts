I think that slogan is doing at least four different jobs there and half the fight is people not noticing it. Can you lay out the distinct precise claims it could be making when it's used about justification: what exactly is supposed to be regressing (reasons? the causal history of the belief? grounding relations? the act of giving a reason?), whether the regress is being called infinite, vicious, or merely non-terminating, and whether 'infinite' means anything a finite reasoner could actually have. Bonus if you say whether the turtle picture even transfers from a regress of support to a regress of reasons. Then give me verdicts — which readings are trivial, which are defensible, which are false, and which one each of those two people probably meant. A menu please, not a lecture.
