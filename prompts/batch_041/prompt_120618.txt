the first is that the name gets used for two totally different puzzles and nobody ever warns you. puzzle one is the one about why anybody votes at all, since the odds that your single vote decides the election are supposedly so tiny that you'd have to be an idiot or a saint to bother showing up. puzzle two is the group one, where you line three options up and get a majority saying A beats B, a majority saying B beats C, and a majority saying C beats A, so the group as a whole has no coherent favorite even though every single person's ranking is perfectly sensible.
