ok so ive been going back and forth on the why-is-there-something-rather-than-nothing thing for ages and before i try to defend any of it out loud i want to write down where ive actually landed and get a sanity check. here's the package.

(1) im a naturalist, full stop. no spooky stuff, the world is whatever physics and the special sciences say it is, nothing more. (2) i think abstract objects, numbers and propositions and that kind of thing, are real, and not just real but necessary, theyre sitting there in every possible world, you literally could not have a world without them. (3) i think the question of why there is something rather than nothing is a genuine question with a genuine answer, not a confused pseudo-question that dissolves once you look at it properly. (4) i dont believe in the principle of sufficient reason, i think its just false that every fact has an explanation, some things are brute and thats the end of it. (5) the existence of the physical universe is one of those brute things, there is simply no explanation of why there's a universe at all instead of none. (6) i also think nothing is a scenario i can actually make sense of, i can conceive of there being nothing whatsoever, no universe, no objects, no facts, nothing. (7) and i dont buy the theistic answer, saying god made it explains nothing, it just relabels the brute fact with a bigger word.

so does this hang together? i have a feeling at least a couple of these are quietly pulling against each other but i honestly cant tell which, and i dont want to be told its all fine if its not. which pairs are genuinely in tension, which ones only look like it, and for the real ones how bad is it and whats the cheapest thing to give up. rank them by how much damage each one does, and if you can point to the single revision that would clean up the most, even better.
