I am a software engineer with no formal training in philosophy, and I have been trying to find the right label for a pattern that keeps appearing in arguments about the moral status of animals, artificial systems, and extraterrestrials, though I first noticed it in discussions of whether brain size is morally relevant. The pattern is this: someone proposes a biological quantity as the mark of moral standing—absolute brain size, then brain size relative to body size, then number of neurons, then number of cortical neurons, then degree of connectivity, then something like integrated information or recursive self-modeling. Each replacement is introduced as a refinement, and each is defensible on its own, but the rhetorical force of the earlier quantity never quite disappears; people continue to speak as though the original measure did the moral work, even after they have conceded that it cannot. It is not simply an ordinary defensive retreat, because the weaker claim is not merely a fallback—it is offered as a better criterion—and it is not simply the naturalistic fallacy, because the problem is not only the derivation of ought from is but the retention of a discarded metric as an anchor. I have been calling it metric laundering in my own notes, but I assume that is not the established term.

What I want to know is whether there is an established concept or literature for this exact phenomenon. If there is, I would like to know its name and, more importantly, how it is carved: what distinguishes it from ordinary theory revision, from operationalization and construct validity, from reification, from Goodhart's law, and from a straightforward motte-and-bailey move. If there is no established name, I would like a term coined and defined, with boundary cases: for instance, is it the same phenomenon when a researcher replaces a proxy because of new data, as when a polemicist replaces it because of a counterexample? Does it matter whether the replacement is offered sincerely or strategically? And does the phenomenon require that the original measure be quantitative, or can it occur with qualitative criteria as well?

A bare name would not help me. I am trying to understand the shape of the move: what exactly is being retained when the stated criterion is abandoned, what would count as a clean case of it, what would count as a merely apparent case, and where its edges are.
