Last night a friend and I argued for the better part of three hours about Libet's experiments and what, if anything, they show about free will, and I came away genuinely unsure whether the position I was defending is even internally coherent. Before we pick the argument back up, I'd like you to audit the set of commitments I actually hold. Here they are, as honestly as I can state them:

1. Free will, in the sense that moral responsibility requires, presupposes a genuinely open future; if determinism is true, no one has free will in that sense. I am an incompatibilist and a libertarian about this.
2. Libet's experiments do not threaten free will, because the readiness potential is not a decision signal. It is a slow, unspecific buildup of premotor activity that a decision may or may not issue from, so the timing data cannot establish that a decision occurs before the agent is aware of it.
3. Free will is fundamentally a power of veto: what matters is the capacity to cancel or withhold an action toward which one is already tending, not the capacity to consciously initiate it. The interesting freedom is the freedom to say no.
4. An action is free, and genuinely attributable to me, only if I consciously endorse it, that is, only if I identify with the desire that produced it rather than merely being moved by it.
5. I accept the principle of alternative possibilities: an agent is morally responsible for an action only if the agent could have done otherwise.

I suspect some of these pull against each other and some do not, and I do not trust my own sense of which is which. Please tell me which pairs are in genuine tension and which only appear to be. Rank the genuine tensions by severity, and for each one say exactly what would have to give and what the cheapest revision would cost me. Then tell me whether there is a single revision that would resolve the most at once, and whether adopting it would leave me holding a position anyone would recognize as still mine. I would rather you be blunt about the trade-offs than reassure me that the package works. Write your answer in American English.
