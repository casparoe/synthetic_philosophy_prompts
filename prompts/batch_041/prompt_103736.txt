Design a runnable activity in which the group is a small self-governing community whose cooperation genuinely depends on a shared moral code: arrange the rules, roles, and incentives so that a private, victimless practice emerges and tolerating it measurably degrades the trust and reciprocity the community needs to function, so that they feel the "disintegration" for themselves before anyone names it. Give me the rules, materials, a timeline, and a debrief that maps what happened onto the argument. Tell me what will predictably go wrong the first time and how I should handle it, and be candid about which feature of Devlin's position the activity cannot reproduce and that I'll have to supply in discussion.
