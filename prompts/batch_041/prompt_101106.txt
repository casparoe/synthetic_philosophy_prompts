Please answer with exactly five numbered items, each a sentence or two, with no introduction or conclusion: (1) your overall credence that the Scandinavian attitude is more reasonable; (2) the probability you assign to the epistemic component favoring it, with a one-line justification; (3) the probability you assign to the practical component favoring it, with a one-line justification; (4) the probability that the thermostat point makes the comparison ill-posed, with a one-line justification; and (5) the sub-question your overall number is most sensitive to and what evidence or argument would move it most.
