I've got to hand in a position statement for a seminar next week on Moore's paradox (the "it's raining but I don't believe it" thing) and before I commit to it I want to know whether my own views actually fit together, because I've been bolting bits on for months and I've lost track. Here's where I've landed:

1. Belief isn't an inner state you introspect. To believe something is just to be disposed to act and reason as if it — that's the whole story, no extra furniture.
2. A sincere assertion isn't an assertion plus a seperate act of "expressing a belief". To assert something sincerely just is to assert what you believe; the expressing isn't an extra move on top.
3. What's wrong with "it's raining but I don't believe it's raining" is a norm on assertion — you're not allowed to assert what you don't believe. The sentence isn't contradictory. It's a rule of the game, not a logical clash.
4. But I also can't *think* "it's raining but I don't believe it's raining", sitting alone with nobody to hear me. Which is either a huge problem for 3 or no problem at all — I genuinely can't tell which.
5. Self-deception is everywhere, so people are routinely wrong about their own beliefs, and I think their sincere assertions can outrun what they actually believe.
6. When I want to know whether I believe something I don't go poking around my own dispositions, I just settle whether the thing is true. Belief questions get answered by world questions.

And one verdict I keep getting alot of grief for: I think the weirdness is at bottom about tense and simultaneity. "It's raining but I didn't believe it" sounds fine to me, and so does "it's raining but an hour ago I didn't think so". So the badness is specifically about me, now — not about rain and belief as such. Related observation: if you put the sentence in the mouth of a character in a novel who's clearly confused about her own mind, it doesn't read as absurd at all, which I take to mean the problem is with the saying rather than the said.

So — which of these actually fight each other, which only look like they do, and what would I have to give up? Rank the clashes by how bad the damage is rather than telling me everything's in tension, say what the cheapest fix is in each case, and tell me if there's one single change that would clean up the most. And please don't give me a literature tour; I want to know where my package breaks.
