I do a tiny philosophy podcast (40 listeners on a good week, mostly ex-students) and next month's episode is an interview with someone defending the Kripke-style essentiality of origin — the claim that you couldnt have come from a different sperm and egg. Heads up: hes not a real person, I'm building the episode around a position, so write the questions for whoever holds the view as I spell it out, not for any specific living philosopher.

His version is more hardcore than the textbook one. He thinks the essential origin isnt the zygote but the particular sperm and the particular egg — the gamete pair — and that everything else about you, genome included, is downstream of that. He also insists this is metaphysical necessity, not a claim about biology.

Give me 8-10 questions. I want them to press where it actually hurts, so include at least one each on:
- what makes a sperm that sperm — it's a cell that ages, and it could in principle have had different innards; if he individuates gametes by their causal history, has he just pushed the problem back a step?
- the CRISPR case: same sperm, same egg, genome edited right after fertilization. Same person or not? (I want to know whether hes really tracking the cells or tracking the genome in disguise.)
- twinning: one zygote, two people. Does a single origin make two people, and if so what happened to the other direction of the claim — same origin, same person?
- the arithmetic: on his view every sperm that never met an egg was a possible person. Does he accept that, and can he say it without it sounding absurd?
- whether the view needs a bare thisness to things over and above their properties, or whether plain properties can do the work.

For each question, add the follow-up for the answer hes most likely to give, so I can keep going when he reaches for the stock reply instead of the interesting one.

And at the end: tell me which of these you think is most likely to be a dud, and what he could say that would make you think the whole line of questioning is wrong-headed. Id rather hear where your read on him is shaky than get a confident list.
