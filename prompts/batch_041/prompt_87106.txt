My friend and I spent like three hours last night arguing about whether ChatGPT understands anything, and we kept talking past each other — id say 'its just predicting the next token' and hed say 'so what, youre just predicting too' — and somewhere around midnight I realized we were actually defending four or five different positions between us without noticing. So I want to build a proper 'which theory of LLM understanding do you actually hold' quiz we can both take: eight to ten questions, each one a concrete case with two or three answer options, plus a scoring key that maps answer patterns onto positions.

The questions should be cases where the views actually come apart, not 'do you think machines can think'. Stuff like: a lookup table that produces byte-identical outputs to the model on every input we've tried — does your verdict change? A model that nails a novel proof but flubs an easy prediction about what happens when you let go of a cup. A model that says 'I dont really understand this one' about its own output. A parrot. A person whos read every book about a city but never been there. Phrase each so someone can answer it without knowing which position it leads to.

What I want the quiz to be able to tell apart: whether someone thinks the criterion is behavioral competence, vs. having the right kind of internal representation, vs. having the right causal/etiological connection to the world; whether understanding is graded or all-or-nothing; whether the question is about meaning/semantics or about consciousness (people keep running these together and I think thats most of the actual disagreement); whether the next-token training objective matters to them or only the resulting abilities; and whether they think the whole thing is a verbal dispute. Make sure there are leaves for the deflationary intentional-stance view, the 'it understands the way a book does / derivative intentionality' view, the compression-is-understanding view, the stochastic-parrot view, and the 'we cant tell without opening up the weights' view. If two of those come out indistinguishable on your questions, flag that instead of pretending they're seperate, and note any answer-combination nobody actually holds and whether thats because its incoherent or just unexplored. Dont bother telling me who holds what — I dont need names, just the positions.
