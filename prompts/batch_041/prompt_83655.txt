I am also interested in the difference between a creature that uses a tool and a creature whose mind is partly constituted by the tool, since that may matter for whether the Gregorian label picks out an internal architecture or a relation to an environment. Please give me exactly five numbered points, each a sentence or two, with no introduction or conclusion. Each point should be anchored in at least one concrete scene or choice—the Voight-Kampff test, the encounter with Rachael, Roy Batty's confrontation with Tyrell, or the final rooftop confrontation—and do not quote dialogue or cite sources; I care about the reading, not the bibliography.
