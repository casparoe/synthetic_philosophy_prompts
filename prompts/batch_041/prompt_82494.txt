hey, i need a piece of satire for a dataset i'm building and i want it a bit more in the weeds than the usual 'economists think you want whatever you buy' bit.

the setup: a fictional company's People team sends round an internal FAQ annoucing that from next quarter, employee wellbeing will be measured exclusively by what employees actually choose. not what they say they want, not how they feel about it afterwards -- behaviour is the only data. the engagement survey is being discontinued as cheap talk.

i want the humour to come from the policy being internally consistent, so the HR voice has to be scrupulously deadpan and never wink at the reader. work in, roughly in this order and without labelling any of them:

- the definitional move: if a preference just is what choice reveals, then nobody can choose against their preference, so no one can complain that a benefit is bad for them. someone asks what if she'd rather have the cash than the free pizza -- the memo answers that she'd have taken the cash, that cash was never on the menu, and that preferences are only revealed over the options actually offered.
- the rule that your choices have to hang together (take A over B, B over C, but C over A) treated as a data-quality and compliance problem, with a coaching session offered to the employee whose choices cycle.
- someone who keeps choosing the pizza and is miserable about it, plus a smooth answer that a specialist will recognise as an evasion.
- a quarterly report that compares wellbeing across employees, which the ordinal apparatus can't legitimately do, and nobody in the memo noticing.
- a footnote from the one dissenting member of the working group, who thinks the policy is unfalsifiable and that the apparatus was built to predict choices rather than grade them. that footnote should be the only relief the reader gets.

about 600 words, numbered Q&A format, footnote at the end, no real company, and keep it readable for someone who's never opened an econ paper while still being funny to someone who has. thanks!
