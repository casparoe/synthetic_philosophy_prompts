I'm not sure if this is a real distinction or just me missing the vocabulary, so bear with me. I got into birding a couple years ago and then went down a rabbit hole of animal communication, and I keep bumping into a pattern I don't have a word for. The standard story for big showy displays is the peacock's tail: the thing is a massive pointless burden, only a genuinely healthy bird can afford to grow one and drag it around, and that's supposed to be why the display is worth believing. Fine, that one I get. But then I started noticing cases that seem to do the same job without any of that. The one that got me was frogs and toads: a lot of them call at a pitch that's basically set by how big the animal is, because the sound-making apparatus is a fixed tube, so a small male phsyically cannot make the deep call no matter how fit or how desperate he is. The deep call still tells a female "I'm big," and she's right to trust it, but nobody is paying anything for it. It isn't expensive, it's just built into the body. I think there might be something like this going on with the pitch of a big cat's roar too, but the frog one seems cleanest.

So: is there a name for that second kind of advertisement, the kind that's trustworthy not because it's wasteful but because it's mechanically tied to the very thing it's advertising, so it couldn't be faked even in principle? And I want to know whether it's genuinely a different category or whether people just fold it into the peacock story. Someone on a forum told me it's the same thing with the cost set to zero, which seems to lose something, since in the frog case there's nothing for the animal to "afford" in the first place.

If the term exists I'd like the boundaries of it, not just the label: what exactly it picks out, how it differs from the peacock/tail idea, and whether the line between the two is as clean as it looks or whether there's a messy middle. And if it turns out nothing established quite fits, I'd honestly be happy with a coined name, as long as whoever coins it is careful about the in-between cases.
