I've got a phil of science seminar coming up where I have to present my "view" on one page, and I want a sanity check before I commit it to paper — I have a nagging feeling two of my commitments are secretly fighting each other and I can't see which ones. Here's roughly where I've landed:

(1) Humean supervenience, basically all the way. Everything about the world — including the facts about which generalizations are the laws and what's possible — supervenes on the local, intrinsic, categorical facts about how stuff is arranged in space and time. No necessary connections between distinct existences. Full stop.

(2) Laws of nature are just the generalizations that earn their keep in the best system, the one that best balances simplicity, strength and fit. They're summaries of the mosaic, not little governors hovering above the world making things happen.

(3) Laws really do explain their instances. When I explain why the metal bar expanded by citing its coefficient of thermal expansion together with the temperature rise, the law is doing genuine explanatory work — it's not just a bookkeeping device I project back onto events after the fact.

(4) There are objective chances, and the Principal Principle is basically right: if I know the objective chance of X is p, my credence in X should be p, absent other info that bears on X. I take chances to be real features of the world, not useful fictions.

(5) There are genuine dispositions in nature — salt is really soluble, this glass is really fragile — and ascriptions like "this is fragile" are true of the world, not just handy ways of talking.

(6) I'm a scientific realist; our best theories are at least approximately true and the entities they posit really exist.

So — does this hang together? I suspect it mostly does, but that a couple of these are definately in quiet conflict. Which pairs are actually in tension and how severe is each, which ones only look like they clash but don't, and for the real clashes what's the cheapest commitment to give up and what does giving it up cost me? Rank the tensions by how bad they are rather than listing them all as equally serious, and tell me if any single one of these is doing all the damage.
