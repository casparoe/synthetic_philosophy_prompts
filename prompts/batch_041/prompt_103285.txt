The Luring Lottery: a bunch of people each get to mail in one postcard with a number on it; if exactly one postcard arrives, that person wins that many dollars, if two or more arrive nobody wins anything. The standard lesson is "superrationality" — each of us is tempted to mail a card, and mailing is actually weakly dominant if you hold everyone else's card fixed, yet a whole population that reasons alike will either all mail (nobody wins) or none mail (nobody wins).
