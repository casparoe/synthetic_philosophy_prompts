ok this is gonna sound ridiculous but ive had one of those gel ant farms on my desk since last spring and it has turned into an actual presence in my life. i sit and watch it instead of working. and the longer i watch the less i can say what im looking at. is it a pet? 400 pets? or one thing that only pretends to be 400 things? i keep coming back to Aunt Hillary, the ant colony with a mind in that Hofstadter book, and the anteater in it who can only ever see ants and never the thing the ants add up to.

so: write me a villanelle about it. 19 lines, the usual two refrains, five tercets and a quatrain. i want one refrain to be a line thats only true if your talking about individual ants, and the other only true if your talking about the colony as a whole, and i want them to keep passing each other and then finally land side by side in the closing couplet, because thats the whole problem -- the two levels never reduce to each other, and the form should make you feel that instead of just announcing it. please don't make the queen the brain, thats the one thing i definately know is wrong: real colonies dont have a boss ant handing out orders, its all local trails and pheromones, and no single ant has any idea what the colony is doing. someone who knows the superorganism literature should be able to tell which refrain belongs to which level, and wince if ive got it backwards. and dont resolve it for me at the end -- i honestly dont know if its a mind, and i'd rather the poem not pretend it does.
