The audience is mostly feminist care ethicists and a couple of Confucian specialists, plus one virtue ethicist who thinks care ethics is too particularist. They will be sympathetic to relational selves but suspicious of anything that sounds like it justifies hierarchy or tells people to stay in harmful roles.

Please act as someone who knows both Confucian role ethics and feminist care ethics well, and think carefully before writing. Start by brainstorming what this audience is committed to and what they'll hear as the real stakes. For each objection give me a two-minute reply and a note on where that reply is soft. Tell me what I should concede up front, and what I should hold firm on. End with the one question I'm hoping nobody asks. No citations or reading lists, please; I need arguments, not bibliographies.
