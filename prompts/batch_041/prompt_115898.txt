The standard exorcism of Maxwell's demon goes: the demon measures a molecule, sorts fast from slow, seems to pull work out of a single heat bath, and the rescue is that it has to write the measurement down somewhere, and eventually reset that memory to a standard state, and Landauers kT ln 2 per bit eats the whole profit. My question is what that practice actually presupposes. It looks like it needs measurement to be free while erasure is the only thing that costs anything, that the demon's memory starts in a known blank state, and that the demon sits inside the boundary of the system we're auditing. Is the free-measurement half a fact about physics or just a convention about where we stop counting? Then the deeper one: does the argument require information to be a physical substance you can cash in for entropy, so that a demon who knows the gas is literally in a lower-entropy state than one who doesn't, or does it survive with a purely epistemic reading of entropy, where the knowledge lives in the mind rather than the world? And the lab versions where people claim to have converted information into work, whose memory is being erased there, and is the experimenter's own measurement inside or outside the books? Reconstruct the implicit theory, sort out which commitments the exorcism strictly needs from the ones that are just how the story is usually told, say whether they're mutually consistent, and tell me what would have to change if the one you think is load-bearing turned out false. Also flag anywhere it's underdetermined, e.g. a version of the demon that never overwrites anything and so alot of the accounting just never comes due.
