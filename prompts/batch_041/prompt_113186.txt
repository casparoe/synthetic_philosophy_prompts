Ok so I'm a 2nd-year PhD student (cog sci, but I hang around the phil of mind people) and I have to give a short position statement at our lab meeting next week on the timing of experience. I'd like you to audit my commitments before I make an idiot of myself. Here's roughly where I've landed:

(1) No Orwellian revision. A later stimulus can't change the content of an experience that has already happened — if you experienced red, no subsequent event makes it the case that you experienced green. I'm fine with the brain being slow; I'm not fine with the past being rewritten.
(2) The colour phi effect and the cutaneous rabbit are real, and I take the reports at face value: you really do experience the spot as changing colour while it's moving, i.e. before the second spot has appeared, and you really do feel taps at positions that were never tapped, in the gaps between the real ones. I've also been messing around with a demo where the two spots are different colours and people say they see an intermediate hue that was never presented — I might just be fooling myself, but assume for now that the report is accurate.
(3) A delay is fine. If the brain sits on things for 100ms or so before presenting them to you, I don't think that's alarming or paradoxical; I'd happily accept a fairly long presentation delay.
(4) But I don't think there's a single global now of experience. Different features (colour, motion, touch) get bound on different timescales, so alot of questions of the form "when did that experience happen?" are ill-posed rather than having a determinate answer.
(5) Introspection is a decent guide to the content of experience and a hopeless guide to its timing.

People keep telling me this package doesn't hang together but I can never pin down which bits actually conflict, and honestly a couple of them look like tensions that aren't really there. Can you sort it out? Rank the real tensions by how bad they are, tell me which ones are only apparent and why they dissolve, and for each real one give the cheapest revision and what it costs me. If one of the five is doing all the work, say so.

Max 700 words, and please commit to a verdict rather than laying out considerations both ways.
