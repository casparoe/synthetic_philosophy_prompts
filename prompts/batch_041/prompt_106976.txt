Rate each of the following candidate units of selection on how well it passes what I will call the ledger test: when the adaptive change in a population is fully accounted for, is there a level at which the books balance — a level whose entities bear a fitness of their own, transmit their form to the next generation, and whose differential success predicts the direction of change without remainder? Give each a score from 0 to 1, rank the list, and add one sentence of justification per item. Candidates: the gene or allele; the individual organism; the group or deme; the species; and the holobiont — a host organism together with its resident microbiome. For each, name the clause of the test it fails most badly, and flag any candidate whose apparent failure is really an artifact of how the accounting is drawn.
