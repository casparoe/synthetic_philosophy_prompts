- Hume says a miracle is a violation of a law of nature, and that we should only accept a report of one if the report being false would itself be a greater wonder than the event reported. Set it somewhere ordinary — a kitchen, a long car journey, whatever. Real back and forth, no speeches that ignore what the other one just said. British English, and don't feel you have to resolve it. Maybe 1000–1500 words.
