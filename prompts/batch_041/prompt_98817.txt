OK so here's where I've landed on the procreation stuff and I want a consistency check before I try to write it up. (1) The asymmetry: it's bad to bring someone into existence whose life would be net miserable, but it isn't good — not a benefit, not something you're required to do — to bring someone into existence whose life would be net positive. (2) At bottom I'm a person-affecting guy: I don't think anything makes an outcome better or worse unless it's better or worse for some actual person, there's no free-floating impersonal good sitting around. (3) I don't think we wrong anyone by choosing a policy that settles who exists, so long as everyone it creates has a life worth living — the non-identity cases just aren't wrongs. (4) I'm not willing to swallow the repugant conclusion; a world crammed with billions of lives barely worth living is not better than a smaller world of flourishing ones. (5) And I've never believed there's a duty to have kids, or a duty not to.
