For the past year my reading group has been working through the literature on the norm of assertion, and we have ground to a halt. The room contains a committed defender of the knowledge account, someone who holds that truth is the only norm we need, a defender of a belief or justified-belief account, and one member who denies that assertion has any constitutive norm at all and treats the whole debate as a category mistake. The same handful of cases is rehearsed every session — lottery assertions, Moore-paradoxical assertions, cases in which a speaker asserts what they know on evidence they cannot articulate, cases in which a speaker is entitled to assert but would be criticizable for doing so — and no position in the room has moved in a year. I want a diagnosis, not another survey of the accounts.

My hypothesis, which I would like you to test rather than adopt, is that we are not really disagreeing about which condition governs assertion but about what job we are asking a norm of assertion to do. I have been trying to separate four jobs that the literature tends to run together: (i) individuating assertion from other speech acts, (ii) specifying when one is licensed to assert, (iii) specifying when an asserter is criticizable or blameworthy, and (iv) explaining why assertion has the epistemic significance it does — why knowledge transmission through testimony matters. My suspicion is that each account is really answering a different one of these questions, and that the stock cases are being used as evidence for all four at once. If the real fault line is elsewhere — for instance between those who would understand assertion by its function and those who would understand it by its constitutive rules — say so.

Diagnose the stall. Is the disagreement substantive, verbal, or two or more distinct questions pursued under a single heading? Is a hidden empirical or formal question doing the work, such as a disagreement about the function of assertion or about whether assertion picks out a natural kind? Which of the apparent clashes are genuine and which dissolve once the four jobs are kept apart? Then, given your diagnosis, say what would actually count as progress: a distinction, a decisive case, a formal result, or nothing — and if nothing, why we should stop expecting any.
