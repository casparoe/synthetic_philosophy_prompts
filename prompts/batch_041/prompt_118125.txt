My friend is launching a five-year venture abroad and wants me as his partner. He has offered to transfer me a substantial sum, enough to matter a great deal to my finances, the moment I genuinely intend, in his presence, to see the whole term through. There is no contract, no penalty, and no way for him to hold me to it afterwards; he is simply an excellent judge of sincerity and is willing to bet on his own judgment. I do not want to go, and left to myself I expect I would take the money and quietly leave within a year. Should I try to form the intention he is asking for, and can I form it at all when I can already foresee my own defection? Does it change things that a sincerely formed intention is not inert, so that if I succeed in forming it I may in fact stay, and the plan I am counting on may be unavailable to me precisely because I succeeded? Advise me on what to do, say what intentions would have to be like for my plan to work at all, and tell me whether deliberately manufacturing a sincere intention for money is itself a form of dishonesty.
