I would like you to audit that analogy. Set out the correspondence explicitly — what plays the role of the wager, the stake, the probability, and the cost of betting — then say where the mapping breaks down, separate the disanalogies that matter for the conclusion he draws from the ones that do not, and tell me whether his conclusion survives once the breaks are on the table.
