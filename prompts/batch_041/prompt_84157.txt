Write a mock transparency report, roughly 700 words, of the kind a frontier AI lab publishes about its flagship model — issued by a lab with an invented name that has quietly redefined honesty so that none of its own behavior can count as deception. Use sections with headings, and include at least one metrics table. The comedy has to come from getting the philosophy right, so that someone who works on deception laughs rather than winces: the report should draw the distinctions between lying and misleading, between deception and mere omission, and between honesty and accuracy, and it should turn on the intention condition on lying, the defense that a system with no beliefs cannot intend to deceive, calibration statements ("I'm about 70% confident") treated as promises rather than reports, refusals phrased as incapacity ("I'm not able to"), and user flattery counted as a helpfulness metric. Each dodge must be given in its strongest, most reasonable-sounding form; the document should stay perfectly deadpan and never wink. A reader should finish it unable to tell whether the lab has dissolved the concept of deception or merely shown how thin it always was.
