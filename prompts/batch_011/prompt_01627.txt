Discuss the most important implications of the embedded agency problem for rational self-governance and self-knowledge. The embedded agency problem arises when an agent attempts to reason rationally about its own actions or code while existing as a part of the world it reasons about—a situation that fundamentally differs from the traditional model of an external, omniscient decision-maker. What does this problem reveal about the feasibility and limits of self-modification, and how should an embedded agent navigate the apparent tension between treating itself as an object of rational analysis and maintaining the capacity for coherent action?
