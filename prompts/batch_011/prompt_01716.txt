I'm working on a novel and honestly the main character is pretty obviously based on an ex of mine. The details are specific enought that they'd recognize themselves. My justification is that it's my story to tell, you know? Writers do this all the time. But then I started doing this weird thought experiment where I imagined everyone I'd ever been close to did the exact same thing -- like they all fictionalized our shared moments and memories and put them in their books without checking with me first. And the thought of that made me deeply uncomfortable. So what does that discomfort mean? Does it actually tell me anything about whether what I'm doing is okay?
