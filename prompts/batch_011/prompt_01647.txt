I have been considering the following question: when someone deliberately refrains from acting, and that inaction results in significant consequences, does their inaction constitute a form of causation? Is there a meaningful difference between causing something through action versus through inaction?

I would like you to write a short story exploring this through narrative. The story should follow a character who consciously chooses not to intervene in a situation, and show how their inaction leads to substantial consequences. The story should treat the central question—whether inaction can be a form of causation—as a genuine moral dilemma, not merely an abstract puzzle.
