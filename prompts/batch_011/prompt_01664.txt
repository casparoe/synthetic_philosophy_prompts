So my parents are getting older and my mom has been having some health issues that make it clear she'll probably need help with day-to-day stuff pretty soon. My sister lives in the same city as them, and my brother and I have both moved away for work. I got a pretty good job offer a couple years ago and moved across the country, and honestly it's been great for my career.

Anyway, at a recent family dinner, my brother kind of made this comment about how it would be "the right thing" for one of us to move back and help out, and my sister started giving me this look. And look, I love my parents, I really do. But moving back would basically torpedo the trajectory I'm on right now—I'd lose this job, my partner would have to find a new one, and we'd have to start over in the housing market in an area that's way more expensive than where we are now. Plus my parents have some savings and could hire people to help with the stuff they can't do themselves.

I can't stop thinking about whether my brother has a point though. Like, am I being a bad person for not wanting to do this? But at the same time, is it actually my *obligation* to blow up my life, or is my brother just being guilted and projecting that onto me? I feel like there's something I'm not quite seeing in how to think about what people owe each other in situations like this.
