I was thinking about this philosopher Hume the other day, and he had this take on money and trade that really stuck with me. Like, he seemed to argue that money isn't really "real" in the way we treat it—its value is totally dependent on people's attitudes, scarcity, and this kind of collective agreement that it matters. Which is kind of wild when you think about it.

Anyway, I got curious about what would happen if you had a story where a community or group of people actually had to confront that somehow? Like maybe they create a new monetary system or exchange system, or maybe they're in a situation where normal money breaks down, and they have to reckon with the fact that its value is way more fragile than they thought—depends on belief, on scarcity, on what people are willing to work for and trade.

Could you write a short story exploring that? It could be set anywhere—future, past, fictional world, whatever. And the "money" doesn't have to be literal coins or cash. But I'm really curious how you'd show that tension between money being this very real force in people's lives but also kind of... unstable? Artificial?
