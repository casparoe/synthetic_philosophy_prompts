The doomsday argument holds that your temporal position among all humans ever to exist is uniformly distributed. Given that you observe yourself early in human history, this provides apparent evidence that the total human population will remain relatively limited, and thus that human extinction is likely within the not-too-distant future. Write an essay that critically evaluates this argument. Your analysis should identify and explain the argument's key assumptions, engage seriously with at least one major objection or limitation to it, and reach a considered conclusion about the argument's ultimate force.
