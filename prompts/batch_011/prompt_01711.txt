I have been reading about eternalism—the philosophical view that past, present, and future moments all equally exist as part of the four-dimensional block universe. However, I am puzzled by an apparent tension: if the future is metaphysically as real as the past, why does there appear to be a fundamental asymmetry in our causal and epistemic access to these times? We can observe past events and create records of them, yet the future seems causally inaccessible in this way. How does eternalism account for this apparent directionality in causation and information flow?
