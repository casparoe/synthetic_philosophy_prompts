Please write me a reusable decision procedure (questions in order, what a good enough answer looks like, where it stops with a no, and where it stops with "this is a values call") based on what's actually defensible in that framework—not a version that pretends the three factors just multiply. Then run it on two or three cases: (a) a very neglected cause with low tractability, (b) a crowded but highly tractable cause, and maybe (c) a huge cause already awash in money. Make at least one case come out as a no to something I'd personally love to fund. Be explicit about which steps are settled among people who do cause prioritization and which are contested or judgment calls, and say what the procedure can't handle.
