If you had three or four studies to run in those two years, which open questions in the comparison between tuition-free systems (Germany and the Nordics) and loan-financed ones (the US and the UK) would you pick, and in what order? Give me a defended ordering, not a survey: state the criteria you're weighing (tractability with existing cross-country data, neglectedness, how many downstream design choices hinge on the answer), say how you'd trade them off, and tell me what would make you reshuffle the ranking. I especially want to know which questions are upstream of the others — for instance, is the risk-sharing property of income-contingent loans the crux, or does the enrollment and completion response to prices dominate, or is it something I'm not listing?
