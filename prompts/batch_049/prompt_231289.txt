I've recently ended up on the board of a small global-health foundation that funds malaria, deworming, and maternal health programs, and there's a deal on the table I can't stop chewing over. A church-based health network with genuinely deep reach in rural areas has offered to carry our malaria and deworming programs, on the condition that all the maternal-health materials co-branded with them drop any mention of contraception and instead include a short page promoting natural family planning, which their church supports. Half the board sees this as a no-brainer: we get bednets and deworming pills to communities we otherwise can't reach, and they get materials their volunteers are comfortable distributing. The other half thinks we'd be lending our name to a family-planning message we don't believe and which we think is worse for women, and that taking the deal makes us complicit in pushing it. Draft the short decision memo I can bring to the board: recommend approve, reject, or approve with conditions, give reasons the losing side can actually respond to, and end with a clear actionable bottom line.
