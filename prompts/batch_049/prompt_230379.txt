A camera-trap photograph of a snow leopard that the photographer only found weeks later and published unedited: not creative. A poem produced by a language model from a single prompt and posted verbatim by the user: not creative. An artist who writes her own generative algorithm, lets it run overnight to produce hundreds of forms, then picks one and recolours it by hand: creative. A jazz musician improvising for ten minutes over a standard: creative. A photographer who frames, exposes and develops a portrait herself, then selects one print from a contact sheet: creative. An industrial robot whose preprogrammed welding path happened to produce a lovely abstract form, later exhibited unchanged: not creative.

Next week she's giving a departmental talk about a curator who takes a newly released image model, asks it for ten thousand variations on 'grief', and exhibits the single image she finds most arresting without altering a pixel. I'm trying to work out in advance whether Maya will say that's creative. The yes/no isn't really the point, though; I want to know which rule she's implicitly running, because I can think of at least two rules that fit all her decisions so far but pull apart on this curator case. Set out the candidate rules, say what each predicts, give your best guess about her verdict with the reasons for preferring it, and name a further case that would settle the matter cleanly.
