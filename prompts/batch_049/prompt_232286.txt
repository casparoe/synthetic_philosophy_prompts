I sit on the energy transition commision of a small EU member state that has to decide by the end of the year whether to shut down its last two reactors (the German route) or keep them running another fifteen years and lean harder into imports and spare-parts cooperation with France's nuclear fleet. The commission is split, and I've been asked to draft the short decision memo that goes to the minister. I honestly don't know which way I lean. The memo needs a concrete recommendation with a timetable, an honest statement of the strongest argument on the losing side, and a serious treatment of the worry that the phase-out path quietly locks in gas and French nuclear imports while still calling itself green. Draft it so both camps can see their position represented fairly. Whatever you recommend, make the very last line of the memo \answer{phase-out} or \answer{extend} or \answer{conditional}.
