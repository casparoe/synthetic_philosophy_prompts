Last night a friend and I argued for hours about whether a hypocrite can blame, and I came away defending a specific view: that hypocrisy leaves the content of a criticism untouched — the accusation may be true, fitting, and proportionate — but it destroys the blamer's standing to issue it. On my view, when the unfaithful spouse reproaches his partner for infidelity, the partner may deserve every word, and yet the reproach still constitutes a further moral wrong by the blamer: not because it is rude or counterproductive, but because the blamer lacks the standing to hold the other accountable for this exact offense. My friend resisted throughout. His main case was the father who smoked as a young man and now tells his son not to smoke; he argued that if standing did the work I want it to, the father would have to fall silent, and silence would be worse than any hypocrisy. I replied that the father may teach, warn, plead, and even say plainly that smoking is wrong; what he may not do is adopt the full blaming stance. My friend then accused me of making blame into a ceremony — if hypocrisy only blocks a special performative posture, he said, we should stop caring about that posture instead of letting it muzzle true accusations. We did not resolve it.

I want a map of how exposed my position actually is. Treat the view that hypocrisy destroys standing to blame while leaving the truth of the criticism alone as the object of inquiry. What would count against it? Please give me the evidence, arguments, cases, or consequences that should move me, sorted by how decisive they would be, and separate what would be fatal from what would be merely awkward. Tell me what the view predicts about such cases that its rivals do not predict, and say what a defender of the view would themselves concede as a real cost. I am not looking for literature or citations; I want the argumentative terrain. I suspect the deepest threat is that "standing" adds no explanatory work beyond ordinary defects like meddling, cruelty, or bad timing; if that charge can be made to stick, it would move me more than anything. Finally, tell me honestly whether the view as I have stated it has been qualified enough that it no longer risks anything, and whether being so insulated would itself be a problem.

Keep the reply to at most 800 words, and write in American English.
