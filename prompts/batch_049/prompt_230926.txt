Can you lay out the distinct precise principles that line could be asserting — knowledge norm, truth norm, belief norm, justification norm, maybe a certainty threshold — and for each tell me whether its independently plausible, whether it's trivial or does real work, and whether it actually licenses his call to spike the story. I'd rather get a menu of readings with verdicts than one true interpretation, and I especially want to know which reading his argument needed to go through.
