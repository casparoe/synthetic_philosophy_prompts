I sit on my university's student conduct board. A resident assistant, after a student in acute distress stopped answering messages, used a master key to enter the student's locked room at 3 a.m. and stayed until the student was stable, rather than calling campus security and waiting, as written policy requires. The student later said the RA's entry probably prevented a suicide attempt. The housing office wants an unauthorized-entry violation on the RA's record, arguing that staff cannot be allowed to decide when to bypass the rules; the RA's defenders call the act supererogatory and want a commendation rather than a citation. I will circulate a short decision memo before our hearing. Draft it: recommend a concrete disposition — discipline, expunging the record, a commendation, or a conditional combination — defend it, and give the strongest consideration on the losing side its due.
