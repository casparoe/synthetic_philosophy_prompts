I've been arguing with a friend about vagueness and decided to write down all my plainest judgments, and now they look like they can't all be true at once. I'm hoping you can either find the principle behind them or tell me which verdicts to abandon.

Here's what I keep wanting to say. A single grain of sand is not a heap, and a million grains is a heap. But I don't think there's an exact number of grains where non-heap becomes heap: if two piles differ by a single grain, I can't believe one is a heap and the other isn't. Same for baldness. A man with zero hairs is bald and a man with two hundred thousand isn't, but no single hair ever makes the difference. Yet if you shave him one hair at a time, he ends up bald, so he must have become bald somewhere along the line; I just can't point to the hair. Colors are even worse for me. A ripe tomato is red and a carrot is not red, but I'd also say each chip in a smooth red-to-orange strip is the same color as the next one. And "same color" sounds transitive, which would force me to say the tomato is the same color as the carrot, which I definitely don't want to say. I have a similar thing with childhood: I call a three-year-old a child and a forty-year-old not-a-child, but there's no particular birthday where it happens, not even eighteen.

The only principle I can half-articulate is that these words apply once you're past some contextually set threshold, but the threshold itself is vague. That just seems to push the bump in the rug, becuase now I'd need a threshold for "clearly past the threshold," and I don't think there's a sharp line between the clear cases and the borderline ones either. So: is there a coherent theory of vagueness that captures all or most of these verdicts? Which of my verdicts would your preferred theory force me to drop, and what does it predict for the case I keep going back and forth on: a fifteen-year-old, who I want to call a child, a sixteen-year-old, who I also want to call a child, and the claim that there is nonetheless a last moment of childhood?
