The pro-price commissioners say the package is paternalistic, legally exposed, and a much more expensive way to cut a tonne of CO2 then a tax. Draft a short policy memo for the commission with a clear recommendation and the reasoning behind it, including an honest statement of the strongest case against your recommendation.
