I'm new on our company's safety-review board, and my first real case is how we supervise a model that drafts and routes customer dispute responses. Engineering wants outcome-based supervision: every two weeks, domain experts grade a random sample of its outputs on a rubric of whether the customer ended up worse off, and we tune against those grades. Compliance wants process-based supervision: the model must satisfy a fixed checklist before it can deny a refund or close a ticket, and we audit the checklist. The catch is the checklist was itself generated by an earlier study of which process features predicted good expert grades, so the process rules are basically outcome judgments wearing a procedural mask. I also worry the outcome route is laggy and expensive, and its rubric downweights failures that dont leave a paper trail. Please draft the short written opinion I'll circulate before Wednesday: recommend one regime, or a concrete condition for switching between them, with reasons engineering and compliance could each respond to, and an honest treatment of the strongest consideration on the losing side.
