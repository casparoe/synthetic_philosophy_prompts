For the past few years I have been reading the rationalist and EA discussions of quantum mechanics, and I have finally written down the view I keep defending in conversation. Before I publish it anywhere, I want a consistency audit of the position set rather than a survey of interpretations. Here is what I think I think:

1. The quantum state is a complete description of a physical system, and there is no collapse postulate. What we call measurement is just unitary entanglement between a system and its environment, with decoherence suppressing interference in practice.

2. This commits me to a broadly Everettian picture: every term in a superposition is real, and decohered terms constitute distinct branches of the world.

3. I regard the Born-rule probabilities not as objective chances injected into the dynamics, but as rational self-locating credences: roughly, the degrees of belief one should assign, before a measurement, to ending up in one branch rather than another.

4. I reject Bohmian mechanics mainly on parsimony grounds: it adds an unobservable corpuscle and a guidance equation that does no experimental work, and I prefer not to populate a theory with unobservable extras.

5. I reject Copenhagen-style interpretations because they require a movable split between the classical and quantum domains, or else make consciousness or measurement special in a way I find unmotivated.

6. I take ontological leanness to be a genuine theoretical virtue: fewer fundamental kinds, fewer arbitrary parameters, fewer nomological add-ons. I also take this to be a point in favor of the Everettian picture.

7. I also believe that, before a measurement, there is no fact of the matter about which branch one will find oneself in, because the branches do not pre-exist as a set of possibilities and one's successor states continue into all decohered branches.

Please go through these commitments and tell me which pairs are actually in tension, which are only apparently in tension, and how severe each real tension is. I want the tensions ranked rather than listed as equally serious, and I want to know what the cheapest single revision would be and what it would force me to give up. I am not attached to any particular one of these views, so don't simply defend the package.
