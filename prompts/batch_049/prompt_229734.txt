I write novels, and lately I've been stuck on a question that keeps circling back to Rousseau's letter to d'Alembert about the theater: what happens when a culture gets really good at making people watch other people pretend to feel things. I know the broad strokes of his critique — theater leaves us passive, it buys a cheap emotional release that costs us nothing and changes nothing, it feeds amour-propre, the actor lives a kind of self-alienation, and it would corrupt a small republic like Geneva. What I want is to stretch that critique across media he never lived to see, partly because I'm writing a novel about performance and spectatorship and partly because I genuinely cant tell how far his framework goes.

So: take mainstream cinema, serialized streaming TV (the bingeable kind), and immersive VR roleplaying games, and predict as carefully as you can what Rousseau would think of each. I don't want the lazy version (he hated theater, so he'd hate all of it) — I want the interesting version. For each medium, say where it lands on the anxieties he actually had, and where it slips past them or changes their shape. Streaming seems even more passive and emotionally cathartic-but-inert than a night in the theater, sure, but it's also private and endlessly available, which he had no model for. And a VR game has you doing the pretending with a whole constructed identity — is that the active participation he praised in civic festivals, or is it the actor's problem wearing a headset? I honestly don't know.

After the three, step back and give me a verdict: does Rousseau's framework survive contact with these media mostly intact, or does one of them break it — is there a form of mediated role-playing he'd have to admit is good for a republic, or at least not corrupting?

- 'rousseau_would_likely_say' — one tight sentence predicting his overall verdict
- 'key_anxiety' — the specific worry from his actual theater critique doing the most work (pity at a distance, amour-propre, the actor's self-alienation, civic passivity, luxury/effeminacy, whatever fits)
- 'where_the_analogy_breaks' — what this medium does that his version of theater didn't, and whether that makes him more or less worried
- 'confidence' — high / medium / low, with a clause on why

Keep the table cells short; let the prose do the real work.
