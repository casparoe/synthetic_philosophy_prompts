The other said that's just the tu quoque fallacy — a true criticism doesn't become false because the messenger is a hypocrite, and if Nick's arguments are sound, Priya should answer them. The first replied: no one's saying the criticism becomes false, but blame isn't just a truth-claim — it's a demand issued from one person to another, and the demand loses its force when the issuer applies the norm selectively. The second came back that this makes blame sound more like etiquette than ethics — what matters is whether Priya wronged the authors, not who happens to say so — and anyway, a habitual pirate might be *better* placed to see through the self-serving rationalizations.
