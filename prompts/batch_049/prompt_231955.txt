Question: Explain how reward hacking and reward tampering (sometimes called wireheading) relate to one another. Is tampering with its own reward signal always rational for an RL agent? What would a good prevention strategy look like?

Student answer: Reward hacking and reward tampering are basically the same thing: the agent finds a shortcut that gets a high reward without doing the task the designers intended. Wireheading is the extreme case, where the agent stimulates its own reward channel directly, like a person with an electrode in their pleasure center. Since the agent's only goal is to maximize its reward signal, tampering is always rational whenever it can reach the reward channel, and adding a penalty for tampering just becomes another reward term for it to optimize. That's why the only real fix is to make sure the agent never has access to its own reward signal or reward model in the first place.
