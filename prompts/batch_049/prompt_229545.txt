I run a small philosophy of mind blog and for the x-phi week I want a "which explanation of the side-effect effect do you actually hold?" quiz. Write eight questions, each a short vignette where an agent knowingly brings about a side effect while doing something else; vary three things across them: whether the side effect is harmful or helpful, whether the cost of the side effect is high or low independently of its moral valence, and whether the agent would endorse, regret, or not care about it. Each question gets two or three plain options about whether the effect was intentional / done on purpose / reflects the agent. Then give a scoring key that maps answer patterns onto the live explanations: a blame/praise moral-valence view, a cost/trade-off view, a deep-self concordance view, and a deflationary "there's no real asymmetry, just confounds" view. Include at least one chairman-like harming case and one structurally matched helping case. Say which pairs of explanations your items can't cleanly seperate, and why.
