Student answer: Mutual knowledge of a fact p means every player knows p. Common knowledge means every player knows p, and every player knows that every player knows p. In the Centipede game, two players alternate between taking a growing pot or passing it, and at the very last node the player whose turn it is would take rather than pass the pot away. So at the next-to-last node the other player, knowing this, takes instead. Working this reasoning back to the first move gives backward induction: the first player takes immediately. This chain only needs mutual knowledge of rationality. Once each player knows the other is rational, each of them can trace the same reasoning all the way back down the tree.
