I spent my working life in physical chemistry, where I was trained to treat a classification as useful only if it encoded an ordinal or quantitative scale. Since retiring I have been reading Augustine’s On Lying and Against Lying, and I want to reconstruct the ranking principle behind his verdicts. Treat the following four cases as my paraphrased data points, not as quotations: they are simplified in order to force the underlying scale into the open.

D1. A man tells a false story about his travels to amuse a dinner party; no one is injured, and nothing of consequence turns on it. Augustine treats this as a sin, but among the lightest kinds.

D2. A person falsely swears that a neighbor has committed adultery, out of malice, hoping the neighbor will be punished. Augustine treats this as one of the graver kinds of lie.

D3. A teacher presents a false doctrine about the Incarnation in order to make Christianity seem more acceptable to educated pagans, believing the falsehood will produce a good result. Augustine treats this as the most serious kind of lie.

D4. A traveler falsely tells a highwayman that a fugitive has gone east, diverting him and thereby saving the fugitive’s life. Augustine treats this as still a sin, but lightly ranked.

Now consider a new case:

N. A female convert tells a slave trader that she is not a Christian, knowing that confessed Christians are forced into prostitution while non-Christians are sold as field hands; she says this solely to preserve her chastity.

I want to know how an Augustinian classifier should place N relative to D1 through D4. The difficulty is that several plausible ranking principles fit the four data points: (a) severity tracks harm to another person; (b) severity tracks the liar’s motive; (c) severity tracks whether the lie touches revealed truth or religious identity; (d) severity tracks whether the lie protects bodily integrity. State what each candidate principle predicts for N, identify which principle or small set of principles best fits D1–D4, and give your best Augustinian ranking of all five cases from most to least serious. Then specify one further case that would most cleanly discriminate between the two strongest remaining candidates.
