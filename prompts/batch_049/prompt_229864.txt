Specifically, I want questions that (a) press him on the contrast between a rule of inference and an extra premise, (b) ask whether he can even describe his own withholding without relying on modus ponens in that very description, and (c) ask what would actually satisfy him, if anything.

Write me eight to ten questions for him. The questions should be adversarial but show I know the actual issue, so no Phil 101 'but you must obey logic or you couldn't brush your teeth' stuff.
