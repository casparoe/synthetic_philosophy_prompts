What I dont have is a procedure I can actually use in the meeting.

Could you write me a decision procedure for go/kill calls, grounded in whatever is known about the dollar auction and escalation of commitment, that a non-philosopher project lead could follow? Constraints: the whole thing has to be usable with maybe 15 minutes per project, with people who haven't read the literature and are motivated to keep their own projects alive. I suspect part of the answer is setting kill criteria before a project starts, but I want the whole procedure, not just that.
