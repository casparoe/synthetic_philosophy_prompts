But then is the difference in how effectively different people can convert resources into functionings part of the thing we equalise, or a background fact about persons, or a dimension of welfare? And from the resources side, why exactly is a conversion factor not just another personal endowment that a resourcist can count as part of someone's bundle? I want an explanation of the conceptual issue, not a recommendation about which view is right. Please write the answer in British English.
