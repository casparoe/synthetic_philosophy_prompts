Round 2 (B): Take = A 2, B 8. Pass → round 3.
Round 3 (A): Take = A 16, B 4. Pass → round 4.
Round 4 (B): Take = A 8, B 32. Pass = A 40, B 20.

The whole question is whether B could ever rationally pass in round 2, or whether that move has already been ruled out by the common-knowledge assumption.
