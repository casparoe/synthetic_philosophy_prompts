From this she concludes we don't need a special psychology — sadism, deep conviction, hate — to explain how the Holocaust happened; we just need desks, forms, and careerism. When I push back (why him and not every other clerk, and doesn't this let the genuinely sadistic or fanatical killers off the hook?) she repeats the slogan like its a QED.
