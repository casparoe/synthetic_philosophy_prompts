I'm drafting a small grants call on the ethics of compromise between value systems, and I want to organize it around a particular lens: compromise as gains from trade, where two parties whose values differ enough can jointly produce outcomes neither could justify pursuing alone, rather than just splitting a fixed loss. Honestly I keep going back and forth on what should actually get funded first. Candidate threads: (1) when a compromise genuinely creates new value versus just moving along a bargaining frontier; (2) how to tell compromise from capitulation or betrayal when the commitments at stake are identity-level; (3) whether a stable compromise has to be internally coherent or can just be an endorsed patchwork of the two value systems; and (4) how much of the supposed gains from trade is really just the independent value of preserving the relationship. Rank these or add your own, and defend the order: which questions are upstream of which, which are tractable, and which you'd deprioritize because settling them wouldn't change much downstream.
