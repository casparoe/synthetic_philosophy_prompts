In my French Revolution seminar today two people got into it after class about why the Revolution slid from 1789 into the Terror, and I ended up mostly listening. It went roughly like this. One of them (call him D) argued that the radicalization was basically class conflict: the sans-culottes kept demanding price controls, requisitions, and punishment of hoarders and aristocrats, and the Jacobins had to keep outbidding the Girondins to hold on to popular support. On his account the Terror was the revolution defending itself against foreign invasion and a real counterrevolution, and the guillotine went after people whose material position made them enemies of the revolution. The other (call her M) said that turns the ideology into an afterthought: the Terror came out of a genuinely new political culture, the idea that the general will can't be wrong and that anyone who opposes it is corrupt, the cult of virtue, the obsession with hidden aristocrats and conspiracy. She said you can't explain the specific, almost religious form the killing took by apealling to bread prices alone. Then D shot back that the ideology only caught on because it was useful to a leadership under siege; ideas don't float around a seminar room, they get adopted because they serve the interests of people in a specific situation. M replied that still can't explain the timing, or why France and not Britain: for the actual chain, look at the war, the king's flight to Varennes, Louis's double game — contingent events that forced revolutionaries into a paranoid defensive crouch. And she added that the saying about revolutions devouring their children is just a name for the pattern, not an explanation. I'd like a point-by-point adjudication: rate each move from clearly mistaken to clearly right with a brief justification, then say who is right about which specific point and what the argument as a whole is missing. And please take a definite position — don't just lay out both sides; tell me which parts of each account you'd keep, which you'd drop, and why.
