At a reading group on David Lewis's 1976 paper "Probabilities of Conditionals and Conditional Probabilities," two of my colleagues got into a long argument about what the triviality results actually show. The passage under discussion was the first proof, in which Lewis derives a drastic conclusion from the thesis that the probability of an indicative conditional "If A, then C" equals the conditional probability of C given A, together with the assumption that the relevant probability functions are closed under conditionalizing on C and on not-C. Elizabeth said the takeaway is that no ordinary truth-conditional semantics can vindicate that thesis, because any propositional conditional satisfying it collapses into the consequent in its probability. Marcus replied that the proof only exposes a conflict among a package of assumptions: the propositionality of conditionals, Boolean algebra, and conditionalization as an update rule. You can keep the thesis by giving up one of the others, and later approaches do exactly that. Elizabeth countered that if you give up propositionality or Boolean compositionality, you are no longer assigning probability to a proposition in the original sense, so the thesis stops being a probability claim about the world. Marcus said that is a feature, not a bug: Adams's insight was that indicative conditionals encode conditional credences rather than facts, and Lewis's result precisely shows why truth conditions fail. Elizabeth then pointed out that Lewis's later results in the same paper do not rest solely on conditionalization; they still yield triviality for ordinary Boolean semantics under weaker assumptions, so the problem cannot be blamed on conditionalization alone. Marcus answered that those later results still presuppose that the conditional is an element of a Boolean algebra and that the relevant probability functions are classical. Reject those assumptions, he argued, and the thesis survives as a norm of assertability or conditional belief rather than as a constraint on truth-conditional content. At that point they asked me to settle the matter, but their exchange left me uncertain.
