I want a stress test of a position I am currently working with, and specifically a map of where it is exposed. The position has three parts.

First, of Dennett's four-level taxonomy of minds (from Kinds of Minds, organized as a tower of generate-and-test mechanisms) — Darwinian creatures, where selection acts on whole organisms across generations; Skinnerian creatures, which learn within a lifetime by trial and error with the environment doing the testing; Popperian creatures, which possess an internal model that lets them run hypotheses internally and so shield themselves from the hazards of real trial and error; and Gregorian creatures, which offload cognitive work onto external, shared, persistent artifacts such as writing, tools, and institutions — the Popperian/Gregorian boundary is the load-bearing line. The lower three levels are, roughly, limiting cases, and the interesting question for any newly constructed kind of system is whether it falls on the Popperian or the Gregorian side.

Second, that line is architectural rather than historical or cultural. A system is Gregorian only if some of its competence is physically instantiated in external artifacts it depends on, rather than in its own internal states. What matters is where the competence resides, not whether the competence was shaped by a population-level process, and not whether the system is itself a product of culture.

Third, on that reading, large language models — including ones equipped with retrieval-augmented generation, tool use, and persistent external memory — are Popperian creatures at best, not Gregorian ones. The external artifacts they consult are instruments, not the medium in which their competence is housed; the competence sits in the weights. The gap between such a system and a human mind is therefore a difference of kind, not merely of degree.

Now tell me what would count against this. I want the observations, arguments, and cases that should move me, ranked from those that would be fatal to the position to those that would merely be awkward, and for each I want to know what kind of thing it is: an empirical finding, a conceptual point about the structure of the taxonomy, or a point about the master himself. In particular, address the following.

Which empirical results would be decisive, and which would only be awkward? For instance, what would it mean if a model's competence turned out to be irrecoverable from its weights plus prompt once its external artifacts were removed, and would that be the kind of result the position can absorb?

What would a defender of the taxonomy — someone who takes the four levels seriously as a real structure rather than a handy metaphor — concede as counting against the position, as distinct from what they would dismiss out of hand?

Does the position survive the qualifications I would naturally reach for? If I retreat to the claim that a sufficiently externalized model would become Gregorian in the limit, does that save the position, or does it quietly collapse it into the rival view? Tell me plainly whether the position, as stated, actually risks being wrong, or whether those qualifications have already made it unassailable — and if it is unassailable, whether that is a mark of a good position or a bad one.

What would this position predict that its main rival does not? The rival I am most worried about holds that large language models are already proto-Gregorian, because their competence is the product of cultural evolution over a training corpus and because the model itself is a cultural artifact — on that view the Popperian/Gregorian line is a line about history and population, not about where competence is instantiated.

Finally, classify a specific case for me. Imagine a frozen-weight model that may maintain and edit a persistent external corpus of notes across sessions, and that other instances of the same model read from and build on. On my position, does that system cross the Popperian/Gregorian line, and if so, what exactly is it that crosses? On the rival position, was it already Gregorian, so that nothing changes in kind? Use the case to show where the two positions genuinely diverge, and say which variant of the case would be the most diagnostic for telling them apart.
