I'm fairly sure the phrase is doing different work in different mouths, and I can't pin down what it is actually claiming. One thing I've noticed: in the classic trolley case, where you divert a runaway trolley from a track with five people onto one with a single person, even someone who adds up welfare says divert — so the separateness objection can't be doing any work there, and it only seems to bite in cases where you use a person as a tool, like killing one healthy stranger to harvest organs for five dying patients.
