Set it up as a taxonomic vote: give them a case file on a ring of related populations (adjacent populations interbreed, but the two ends meet and don't — think of a ring species like the herring gull complex) with data on morphology, mating behavior, and genetic divergence, and have them vote on whether it's one species or two.
