My partner and I are undergoing IVF and have the option to screen embryos for a genetic condition that causes moderate, manageable disability. We've been trying to reason about this rationally and have settled on what we believe is a clean decision rule: we should screen out a condition only if the expected quality of life of the affected child is below the threshold at which we'd judge a life not to be worth living. Since we both agree that a life with this condition is worth living, our rule implies we should not screen it out. Yet we keep feeling that the rule is missing something, and we can't articulate what. Is the rule defensible, or is there a structural flaw in it?
