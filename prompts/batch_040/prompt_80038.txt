I want to get past the shopkeeper example and the basic "right thing, wrong reason" stuff everyone already knows.

Act like a professional metaethicist who knows this literature cold. And (3) a consequentialist who acts for the right reason, i.e. to maximize the good — does their act get moral worth under this view, and if so, what's left for the Kantian?
