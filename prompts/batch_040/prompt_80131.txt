maya said person b's action has no moral worth — only person a's does — because moral worth requires acting for the right reason, and person b's reason (avoiding embarrassment) is the wrong reason. dev pushed back and said both actions are right and deserve equal moral credit, because the rightness of the action doesnt depend on the motive; motives only matter for predicting future behavior, not for evaluating this action.
