Here's roughly what I want the activity to do. I want students to start with some concrete probability mass spread over a four-way partition (two "colors" crossed with two "terrains," say), then receive two kinds of evidence in sequence: first a plain old proposition they can conditionalize on (one cell ruled out), then a purely conditional constraint that ties down the odds between two of the remaining cells but doesn't rule anything out.
