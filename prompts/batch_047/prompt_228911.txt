Write that essay for me, in around 1,200 words. The essay must reach a definite verdict — for or against treating space as a substance — justify it, and acknowledge plainly which intuitions the verdict requires us to give up. Avoid the temptation to end with a shrug about both sides having a point. Please write in British English.
