So:

Construct a thought experiment in the Sleeping Beauty family where these two come apart. Specifically: design a case where the awakening-counting analysis (whatever the halfer/thirder split gives you) delivers one answer, but reasoning from self-indication -- "I should think worlds with more observers like me are more likely" -- delivers a different one. After the scenario, do the bookkeeping: walk through what a halfer says, what a thirder says, what SSA says, and what SIA says, with actual numbers, and show your work on each. Finally, steelman the thirder who insists their view was never SIA in the first place and that your case is just equivocating on the reference class -- is that a fair complaint or a cope?
