Give the motivating consideration behind each desideratum, distinguish non-negotiable requirements from assumptions characteristic of particular camps, and flag any requirements that may be jointly unsatisfiable—especially broad modal coverage, epistemic safety, and applicability by finite thinkers. Conclude by briefly assessing how a two-dimensional account, an imagination-based account, and a conservative modal-rationalist account fare against your list.
