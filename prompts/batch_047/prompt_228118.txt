Lange answered that a planning board could set prices and adjust them by trial and error, basically solving the equations by hand, and Hayek more or less conceded this worked in principle. So the whole debate was really about computing power, which means modern supercomputers and machine learning settle it in Lange's favor."

This feels too neat to me, but I can't put my finger on what's wrong. Which parts of this summary are accurate, which parts conflate or overstate things, and would Mises and especially Hayek have accepted that the debate was ever about raw computing power?
