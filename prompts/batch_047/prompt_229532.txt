I'm trying to figure out what I actually think about national myths built out of defeats, going by my gut on cases. The Confederacy's Lost Cause -- the war was really about states' rights, the South fought nobly, Reconstruction was the real tragedy -- that one I think is straightforwardly bad, and the fact that it's historically false (it was about slavery) feels like part of what's bad about it. The Dunkirk myth -- plucky little Britain, the flotilla of small boats, the "Dunkirk spirit" -- I think is totally fine, even though everyone involved knew it was a catastrophic retreat spun as a kind of victory. The spin doesn't bother me at all. Masada: Jewish rebels holding out against Rome and supposedly choosing death over capture, which I gather is at least partly legend, but the Israeli use of it as a symbol of resolve strikes me as fine, maybe even good. But then the Kosovo myth -- Prince Lazar choosing the heavenly kingdom, the 1389 defeat as the founding wound of Serbian nationhood -- I want to call that one dangerous, and I honestly cannot tell you why it's different from Masada, since they seem like the same kind of story structurally. Also, the British "we stood alone in 1940" story I think is mostly fine even though it papers over the empire and later the Soviets, though weirdly it feels like it gets a little less fine every decade?

Is there a principle that captures all of these verdicts? I tried "myths are fine when they console and bad when they nurse a grievance" but I'm not sure Dunkirk and Kosovo even come apart on that, so maybe that's not it. What I'm after is: a candidate principle (or two) stated precisely enough that I could apply it myself, which verdict it forces me to drop if any, and what it says about the case I keep flip-flopping on -- the Alamo, "remember the Alamo", heroic last stand for liberty, except that some of the defenders were literally fighting for a slaveholding republic and the myth flattened all the Tejano and Mexican sides of the story. Is the Alamo Dunkirk or is it the Lost Cause? Definately tell me if you think one of my verdicts is the weak link rather than forcing a principle to fit all five.
