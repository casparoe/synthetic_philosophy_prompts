I'd like a defended ranking rather than a survey. Rank these — adding any I've missed — by how much they should actually shift the priorities of someone currently moved mainly by astronomical-waste arguments. For each, state what the effect depends on philosophically (e.g., substrate independence, the relevant anthropic principles), and what considerations would move it up or down the ranking. Grant for the exercise that we are probably simulated; I want to know which implications for far-future concern follow, which don't, and which are overrated. I need the arguments themselves, not the literature.
