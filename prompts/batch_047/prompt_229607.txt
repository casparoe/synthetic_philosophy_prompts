Tell me which desiderata you suspect can't be jointly satisfied (my hunch: "collapse is objectively identifiable" and "collapse is bad" pull apart somewhere) and which single requirement knocks out the most popular theories. Give it to me as exactly seven numbered points, a sentence or two each, no intro and no wrap-up.
