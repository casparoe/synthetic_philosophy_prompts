Is there a name for this? I've noticed a pattern in schools and workplaces and I can't tell if it's a recognized thing. When an institution loses the ability to get people to follow the everyday rules — the small stuff stops being enforced, deadlines slide, dress code is a joke, everyone parks where they want — you might expect it to just become relaxed across the board. But that isn't what happens. Instead the remaining enforcement gets concentrated onto a handful of bright-line rules, and those get punished far more harshly than the same violations would be in a place where general compliance is high. Zero tolerance on three things, total shrugging on everything else. It's not what people mean by a "tight" culture since average rule-following is low, but it's not "loose" either because the punished rules are punished savagely, almost like the severity that used to be spread over a hundred rules is now loaded onto five. Does this configuration have a name of its own, and if not, how would you carve it out — what defines it, what are the boundary cases, and is it a stable equilibrium or just a transitional mess on the way to full looseness? I'd rather you commit to an answer here: pick the nearest established concept and say why it fits or doesn't, or coin a term and defend the coinage, instead of just listing candidates and leaving it to me.
