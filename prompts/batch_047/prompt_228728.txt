I moderate a persistent VR city and have a dispute I can’t decide. Maya spent about 900 hours building a theater that hosted weekly performances and basically became her main social space. Kasper used an intended in-game river mechanic to redirect water through the city, flooding the theater and permanently deleting most of its geometry. He didn’t hack anything or break the terms of service, but he knew exactly what would happen. Because the server only keeps limited backups, restoring Maya’s theater would also erase ten days of everyone else’s creations, including a memorial scene another group built after a player died.

Maya says Kasper destroyed something real and demands compensation. Kasper says nothing was stolen or damaged in the actual world; the server just rearranged some files, and loss is part of the game. Maya also points out that, while the theater’s shape could technically be duplicated, its history, regular audience, and role in the community can’t. I’m not sure who’s right, and alot of the moderators are split.

Should I treat this as a real harm and, if so, what kind? Should its reality depend on Maya’s emotional attachment, the theater’s social function, the time invested, or the fact that she reasonably expected the world to persist? And if restoring it would destroy other players’ equally meaningful creations, what’s the fairest practical response? I want advice on what to actually do, not just “update the terms of service.”
