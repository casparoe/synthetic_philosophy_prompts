7. But then: gradual neuron replacement, one synthetic neuron at a time over years, each one taking over the job of the one it replaces -- that's still you, same as the ship in case 1.
8. An axe where you replace the handle one year and the head the next: same axe, I'd say, though I notice I said no about the band and I'm less sure why.

So cases 1 and 7 go together, and 2 and 6 seem to pair up as "the original matter wins / reconstruction loses," but then 4 breaks the gradual-replacement pattern and 5 breaks it in the opposite direction, and 3 seems to say matter doesn't matter at all which torpedoes 2. Is there one principle -- continuity of organization, spatiotemporal continuity, sortal-dependence, whatever -- that gets most of these?
