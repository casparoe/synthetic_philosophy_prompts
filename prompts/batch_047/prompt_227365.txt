write me a satirical agony-aunt column, maybe 500-600 words. The letter is from a devout effective altruist who married a strict Kantian deontologist, and their whole marriage now runs on moral trade -- he doesn't lie to the door-to-door salesman, and in exhange she donates to his local animal shelter instead of her malaria charities, that kind of thing. But lately she suspects he's defecting (she thinks he lied to their landlord about the dog), so she's writing in to ask whether she should keep up her side of the arrangement. The columnist should answer in full deadpan, treating moral trade as the obviously correct framework for resolving marital disputes, and giving genuinely careful advice about verification, trust, and what counts as defection. The jokes need to come from the actual idea -- gains from moral trade, monitoring problems, the awkward fact that both partners privately think the other is doing something immoral but the deal is still better for both than splitting up -- so that someone who knows the concept laughs because it's accurate, and someone who doesn't can reverse-engineer what moral trade is just from reading the column. Don't have anyone break character or wink at the reader.
