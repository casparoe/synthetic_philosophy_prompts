Then I spring a vote on redistributing the tokens and watch them fight about it — I want the "rich" ones to feel actually annoyed that a pile they got fairly might get taken, and everyone else to feel the pull of taxing it anyway.

Plus a debrief where I map what happened onto Nozick's argument and the strongest objections (like the starting positions not being fair in real life, or "voluntary" choices still being constrained).
