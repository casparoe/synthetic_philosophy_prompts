Draft eight to ten interview questions that actually engage the position rather than letting her give the standard summary. For each question give me a follow-up keyed to the reply she's most likely to make, so I'm not stranded when she answers the first one well. Answer in American English. And if you think one of my framings above is a confused or uncharitable reading of the Clifford side, say so directly and fix the question rather than politely working around it.
