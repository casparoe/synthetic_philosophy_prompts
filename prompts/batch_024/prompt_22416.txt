I've been going down a rabbit hole on Skolem's paradox for a few weeks and I keep hitting the same wall. If the Lownheim-Skolem theorem says that any consistent first-order theory with an infinite model also has a countable model, and ZFC is consistent, then there's a model of all of set theory where everything that exists is countable — but from inside that model, Cantor's diagonal argument still proves the reals are uncountable. So does that mean the reals are secretly countable, and Cantor's theorem is just something that only works from the inside? At this point infinity feels like a language game.
