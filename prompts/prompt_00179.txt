Last month, two guys in my grade were both texting while driving home from practice. One of them got home fine and nothing happened. The other hit a cyclist who'd actually run a red light, and now he's facing suspension from the team and possibly worse, while the first kid hasn't faced any consequences at all and barely got a lecture from his parents. They did the exact same reckless thing, but only one of them is being treated like he did something seriously wrong. It bothers me that the outcome, which honestly came down to bad luck more than anything either of them controlled, seems to be what's driving all the blame. Is it actually fair to hold them to different moral standards here, or is there something inconsistent about how we're judging them?
