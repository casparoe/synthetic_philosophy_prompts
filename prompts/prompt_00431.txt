What I can't figure out is what this actually implies for addiction, which doesn't fit neatly into the competent/incompetent binary Strawson seems to be assuming—it's more like a spectrum where the same person cycles in and out of something like diminished agency over time. Can you lay out the most important implications of Strawson's framework for how we ought to treat chronically relapsing addicts—should we be pushing toward a wholesale objective stance, some kind of oscillating or hybrid stance, or is applying his reactive-attitudes machinery to a condition like this just a category error from the start?
