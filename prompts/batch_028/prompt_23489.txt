Ok so I want an essay on the Boltzmann brain problem, but not the standard 'BBs vastly outnumber ordinary observers so by self-locating probablity we should be one' version. I keep feeling like that framing conflates two separate things: (1) a theory that predicts BBs *actually* dominate the universe — a real physical claim with testable consequences — vs. (2) a theory that predicts an *infinite* number of BBs, which mostly just makes self-locating probability ill-defined and is a problem for any eternal universe, not specifically one with BBs. Weigh whether the BB problem is a genuine physical objection to cosmological models or mostly a artifact of the measure problem. Reach a conclusion and defend it.
