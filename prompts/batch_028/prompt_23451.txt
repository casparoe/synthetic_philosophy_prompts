I keep writing characters who carry around a memory implant and I always end up treating it like a plot device, which is lazy. Can you write me a short story about a woman whose long-term memory lives in a cloud service that's always up but has a four second retrival delay? The whole point should be that the delay is what makes her mind strange — I want the story to dramatize, through what actually happens to her, the difference between a memory that is part of her cognition and a memory that is just a tool she happens to use, the way the extended-mind debate about Otto's notebook gets at it. Don't have any character lecture about Clark and Chalmers; let the reader feel the parity condition snap.
