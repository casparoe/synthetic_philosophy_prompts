I would like you to draft eight to ten questions that test the view rather than invite a summary of it. For each question, add a brief follow-up keyed to the most likely reply the speaker will give, so that I can press the point if the answer is too quick.

The questions should be substantive enough to show that I know the literature, but not so technical that they assume a particular version of epistemicism. I would also like at least one question that forces the speaker to say what would count as evidence against the view, not merely an argument that supervaluationism has its own problems.
