Write an essay on whether pragmatic encroachment and epistemic contextualism are best understood as rival theories of knowledge ascriptions or as compatible ones. Weigh the main arguments on both sides: contextualists hold that the standard expressed by "knows" shifts with the conversational context, and they invoke this to explain the bank cases and the lottery case, whereas pragmatic encroachers hold that the epistemic standard is fixed, but whether a subject knows a proposition depends on the practical stakes that subject faces in acting on it. Consider whether a hybrid view—context-sensitive knowledge ascriptions that are also sensitive to the subject's stakes—is coherent, or whether the two theories explain the same data in mutually exclusive ways. Reach a clear conclusion and justify it.
