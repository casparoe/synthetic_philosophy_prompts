Could you explain, in terms that will land with a programmer, what that second argument is actually establishing, and why it seems stronger than the first? My instinct, which I would like you to confirm, correct, or sharpen, is that this maps exactly onto subtyping: that the type WhiteHorse is not the same type as Horse (true), while an instance of WhiteHorse is still a Horse (also true), and that the seeking-a-horse argument is really a claim about which values get accepted by which function signature. And finally, I would like a clear verdict on whether the advocate's argument actually succeeds in establishing the thesis, or whether it only establishes the weaker non-identity claim while the objector is charitably pressing the stronger exclusion claim — that is, whether the two speakers are ultimately just talking past each other about the negation.
