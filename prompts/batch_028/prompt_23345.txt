The other (call him Marcus) pushes back and says Pascal keeps a real, independent idea of justice alive the whole time: the very fact that Pascal can call the founding act a usurpation, and can call force divorced from justice tyrannical, proves justice isn't just the shadow of force, and that the descriptive story of how power actually gets stabilized is a different question from the normative one of whether it's just.

Make them genuinely competent and have them actually engage each other's best points rather than strawmen. I especially want them to grapple with the move where Lena says that if time can convert force into justice, then the normative distinction collapses and Pascal's whole claim that justice without force is impotent goes empty — and Marcus's reply to that. Keep it a few rounds, no one should be a fool, and end with them honestly stating where they still disagree rather than faking a resolution.
