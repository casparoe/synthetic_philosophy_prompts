Write a joke about the problem of verisimilitude. I want the punchline to turn on the specific technical bind, not on some generic "philosophers can't define things" gag: any measure of how close a false theory is to the truth seems to require first partitioning that theory's consequences into the true and the false, and you can't do that without already knowing the truth — so the measure is only actually computable for the theory that is true, which defeats its whole purpose of ranking false theories against one another. Make it the kind of thing a Bayesian epistemologist would wince-laugh at.
