He basically said the cryonics guys are just paying a fortune to soothe an anxiety that doesn't make sense.

My roommate said I was smuggling in the "future loss" idea that Epicurus explicitly rejected, i.e. that death is bad because it cuts off your future good stuff, and that Epicurus would say no, the absence of a subject is the absence of a harm, period.
