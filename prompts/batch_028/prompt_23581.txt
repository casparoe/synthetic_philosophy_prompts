I've been going down a rabbit hole about this "easy knowledge" problem in philosophy and I keep hitting a wall. The basic claim is that you can't use an empirical method to settle a philosophical fact, like whether you're a brain in a vat. But I genuinly don't get why. If I poke my finger with a pin and it hurts, that's empirical evidence I'm not a brain in a vat, right? My finger hurts, so there's a real finger, so I'm not just a brain in a jar. What's the actual move the philosophers are making when they say I can't bootstrap my knowledge here? Is it that a brain in a vat would also feel the pin? But then how would you ever get out of skepticism?
