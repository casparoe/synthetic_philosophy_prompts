I'd like an essay that gets at the relationship between the two rather than restating each one in isolation. In particular, please address whether instrumental convergence is a consequence of the orthogonality thesis or an independent claim, and whether one can consistently accept one while rejecting the other. Weigh the main arguments on each side, and reach and justify a conclusion rather than ending on a non-committal note.
