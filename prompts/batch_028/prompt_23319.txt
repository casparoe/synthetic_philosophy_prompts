The whole derivation is that the Kelly fraction is the one that maximizes the expected log of your wealth, i.e. the expected value of log(W). So when someone says "Kelly is not a utility function," they're either using "utility function" in some weird technical sense that I don't get, or they're just being pedantic and confusing people.
