I was recently appointed to the board of directors of a mid-sized specialty chemical company, and at next month's meeting the board must decide whether to sign a ten-year exclusive contract with the only third-party logistics provider that can handle our product class. Our largest competitor is reportedly in advanced talks to sign the same contract; if they sign first, our distribution costs would roughly double and we would be forced to build our own logistics over several years. Our CEO proposes that we sign now, first, on the same terms, to preempt them. Please draft the short written opinion I will circulate before the meeting: it should recommend a concrete course of action with a time frame, and it should engage seriously the strongest argument for the alternative, including whether the commitment's real value lies in excluding the rival rather than in the logistics itself, and whether we would be paying for insurance against a threat that may be a bluff.
