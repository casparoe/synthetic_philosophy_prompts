I build software on top of large language models, and I've been trying to place a recurring question of mine in philosophical terms. Suppose W.V.O. Quine were alive today and watched the debate over whether LLMs "understand" anything or "mean" what they say. Given his views on the underdetermination of theory by evidence, and his related doctrine of the indeterminacy of translation, what would he say about attempts to infer a model's "beliefs" or "intentions" from its behavior? And does the fact that we can inspect a model's weights and activations — a kind of evidence unavailable in the translator's thought experiment — change his answer, or is the question underdetermined in principle?
