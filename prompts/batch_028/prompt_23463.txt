I've been thinking about the Doomsday argument and its implications for longtermism. The Doomsday argument, as I understand it, shows that we should expect the total number of humans who will ever exist to be not much larger than a small multiple of the number of humans who have already existed. But if that's the case, then the expected value of the far future is going to be much smaller than we might naively think, and so why do longtermists still worry about the far future? Isn't the Doomsday argument a strong argument against longtermism?
