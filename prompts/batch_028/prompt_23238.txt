I have just been offered a book deal for my new novel by a mid-size press: the advance would let me write my next book for a year, the editor is genuinely enthusiastic, and the terms are fair. My agent is urging me to decline and shop the manuscript to a larger house, on the ground that the best deal is very likely still out there and that I would be foolish to settle for a merely good one; but soliciting those houses is slow, and there is a real risk the current offer will be withdrawn if I dally. I have been going back and forth for three weeks, and I have noticed that the longer I hold off, the more the offer in hand begins to look inadequate, even though nothing about it has actually changed. Is there a sound reason to accept the offer as it stands, or is my restlessness a sign that I should keep looking?
