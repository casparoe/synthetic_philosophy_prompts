A close friend of mine was blind from birth, but a few months ago she had a successful operation that restored her sight. When I visited her last week I was genuinely amazed: she recognized my face, she found her own coffee mug on the shelf, and she poured herself a glass of water without spilling a drop. Her vision is clearly working. But then I picked up two small metal objects I had brought with me, one a smooth ball and the other a cube of roughly the same size, and asked her which one was round. She studied both of them for a long time and then told me, quite honestly, that she had no idea and would have to touch them. I have been turning this over in my head ever since. If she can see me and her own things well enough to recognize them, why can't she tell the shape of a completely new object? Is there something wrong with her vision, or is there a better explanation for what I am seeing?
