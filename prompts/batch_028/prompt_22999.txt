Gottlob Frege died in 1925, before Skolem's paradox became a central issue in the foundations of mathematics. The paradox follows from the Löwenheim-Skolem theorem: a first-order theory of set theory can have a countable model even though the theory proves the existence of uncountable sets, so first-order logic cannot pin down the cardinality of the mathematical universe. Given Frege's logicism and the specific logical framework of his Basic Laws, what would he have thought about Skolem's paradox? Would it have struck at his program, or did his system have the resources to meet it?
