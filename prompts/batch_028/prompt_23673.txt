Case B: A brittle glass is wrapped in a thick foam sleeve and then dropped. It hits the floor and does not shatter.

"A fink is when the disposition is destroyed by the very stimulus condition that would have triggered its manifestation, so the disposition is no longer present at the moment of the test. Case A: Fink. The zap removes the brittleness before impact, so at the moment of impact the glass is no longer fragile. The disposition is gone, not just blocked.

Case B: Mask. The foam blocks the shattering but the glass is still brittle underneath, so the disposition to shatter is fully present, just masked.

Case C: Mask. The inert gas blocks any flame from getting going, so the disposition to ignite is present but masked. The venting is just part of the same blocking mechanism."
