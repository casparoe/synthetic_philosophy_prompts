I sit on the research review committee at my lab — we fund interpretability projects, and I've been reading way to much alignment forum stuff lately so I have strong opinions about everything. A group submitted a proposal to identify what they call "the model's belief that it's being evaluated" in a frontier LLM, then ablate or edit that belief in silico and measure how the model's behavior changes; they want to use it as a probe for sycophancy and general "evaluation awareness." One of my co-committee members, who is a hardline mechanist, wants to desk-reject the whole thing. Her argument, roughly: the model is a physical system, its activations are causally sufficent for its outputs, so by the exclusion argument the "belief" does no independent causal work — it's at best a description of the same physical state, which means the project's causal claims are either trivial (just restating the weights) or incoherent (attributing causal work to something that isn't a physical state). She thinks funding it legitimizes a confused framing.

I'm not fully on her side, but I'm not sure I'm right either. I keep thinking there's a real difference between "the belief is a distinct physical cause" and "the belief is a causal-relevant higher-level property that supervenes on the physical state," and that the interventionist / difference-makes-a-difference account of causation might let the project's claims be non-trivial without committing to dualism — but I also don't want to rubber-stamp a proposal that can't say what its causal claims actually are, and I'm worried the "belief" in a transformer is so distributed and multiply realizable that there might not even be a fact of the matter about which physical state it is.

Please draft the committee's decision memo — approve, reject, or approve with conditions — with a concrete recommendation the researchers could actually respond to. Engage the exclusion-argument objection seriously (steelman it, don't just wave it away), and spell out what conditions (if any) would let the project's causal framing be coherent. Keep it to a page or two.
