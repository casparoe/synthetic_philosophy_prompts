I've been going back and forth on the best-system account of laws for a while and I keep getting stuck on the same thing, the language relativity of the whole constuction. The best system is supposed to be picked out by a simplicity/strength tradeoff, but that tradeoff is obviously relative to the vocabulary you write the system in. If I'm allowed to just invent a new theoretical term that encodes a big chunk of the mosaic as a single predicate, my system looks trivialy simple and the BSA seems to just hand me whatever I want. So how is the Humean supposed to stop this encoding gerrymandering without the account either turning circular (the laws are supposed to carve nature at its joints, but we're using a pre-existing vocab to do the carving) or just quietly picking the language of physics on a whim? I want the actual mechanism for fixing the language, not just the reassurance that we should use the right theoretical terms.
