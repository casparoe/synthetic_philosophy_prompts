Argue that a finitely additive, rotation-invariant measure on all subsets of the unit sphere would rule out any paradoxical decomposition of the sphere under the rotation group, and that the standard construction gets the paradoxical decomposition by finding a free subgroup of the rotations (isomorphic to F2) and using its non-amenability. Then explain why AC is mostly doing the set-theoretic work of picking orbit representatives, and why the more local cost to pay is restricting the measure to Borel sets. Keep it accessible but dont handwave the connection between the group-theoretic obstruction and the measure-theoretic one.
