My colleague is now convinced the drug is harmful and that the overall figure is a misleading artifact of the patient mix, and he wants to stratify everything by severity before we decide. I keep thinking that the fact that the severity was measured after the drug had been acting should matter a great deal here, but I can't quite put my finger on why, or on which of the two sets of numbers we should actually trust. Can you explain what's going on and which comparison is the right one to base the decision on?
