For virtue ethics the problem is that virtues are supposed to be stable dispositions: a courageous person acts courageously and a just person acts justly, whatever the circumstances. If Mischel is right that behavior tracks the situation, then there is no such thing as a stable disposition, and virtue ethics loses its target. The virtue theorist's response is to redefine the virtue. Aristotle's virtue was, on this reading, really a trait, and the virtue theorist swaps it out for a habit: a virtue is now just a pattern of behavior that happens to be consistent, so the situationist data stop applying.
