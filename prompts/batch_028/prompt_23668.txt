I'm trying to figure out which of the two theories is the better account of that kind of shift, and more generally what each one says is doing the work. Could you compare epistemic contextualism and pragmatic encroachment for me, focusing on (1) where each locates the context-sensitivity — in the attributor's conversational context or in the subject's practical situation — (2) what each predicts should happen in the two clean cases: one where my evidence and stakes are held fixed but the attributor's conversational context changes, and one where the attributor's context is held fixed but my stakes rise, and (3) whether pragmatic encroachment is a genuine rival to contextualism or just a special case of it? I'd also like the strongest objection to each, and a sense of where they genuinely diverge rather than where they merely sound different, because I'm deciding which one to defend in my term paper and I don't want to build my argument on a distinction that collapses under scrutiny.
