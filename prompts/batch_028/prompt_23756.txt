The joke must turn on the actual content of Zhuangzi's position rather than on a generic "Eastern wisdom" or "zen" cliché. A reader who has actually read the *Zhuangzi* should recognize the joke as a fair and funny treatment of the text, not a caricature, and the punchline should be something that only makes sense if you know the position well enough to see what is being played on. Please write the joke so that it can be dropped into the novel as is, with the character's voice and the room's reaction.
