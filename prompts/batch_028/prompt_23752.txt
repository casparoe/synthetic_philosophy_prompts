I'm deciding whether to accept a job offer in another city, and a real-estate analyst is offering to sell me a detailed report on that area's housing prices and school quality for $2,000. A friend of mine says I should obviously buy it, since I'm making a big decision and information is always valuable. But I'm not so sure. I've already done a fair amount of my own research and have a fairly strong hunch about whether I'd want to move, so I'm not convinced the report would change my mind. I've also heard that sometimes getting information can actually be a net bad rather than a net good. How should I think about whether this report is worth the $2,000, and is my friend's principle that information is always valuable really right?
