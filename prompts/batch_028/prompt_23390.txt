I've read the standard explanation that the 5% rate describes the behavior of the procedure over many runs, not the probability that this particular run is a false alarm. Please explain the most important implications of this tension for how I should think about what one significant result actually tells me, and why the replication crisis is really about this point rather than about individual researchers being careless.
