I've been stuck on this for a while now — when is a person actually dead? A relitive was in a coma for months and the doctors kept saying "brain death" like it was a simple line, but I keep thinking: the rule seems to be that if the brain stops, the person is dead even if the heart is still going on a machine. But what if only the "thinking" part of the brain is gone, and the lower part that keeps the body running is still there? Is the person dead or alive? I geniunely don't know. Write me a short story about a doctor who faces this exact situation — a patient whose higher brain is destroyed but whose lower brain is still working. The tension should be that the doctor can't decide which "death" applies, and the story should make the reader feel why this isn't just a technicality but a real problem.
