My uncle spent his life forging old master paintings, and he was so good that a couple of his pieces ended up in museums and are still hanging there as the real thing. He just died and I'm sorting through his workshop. I've got a stack of his paintings — some are the ones that made it into museums, some are ones that were exposed and sent back. Here's the thing: some of these forgeries are genuinly better than the originals they're copies of. I'm trying to figure out what to do with them. Do they have any value at all? Should I sell them, donate them, or just get rid of them? And it's weird because the ones in the museums are being treated as the real thing and I'm basically the only person left who knows they're not.
