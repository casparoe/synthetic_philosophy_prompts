For a piece I'm writing on the metaphysics of laws of nature, I'd like a dialogue between a Humean and an anti-Humean philosopher, focused on the 'disjointedness' problem: if laws are merely the regularities in the mosaic of events, why is the mosaic regular at all? The Humean should hold that the laws are simply the regularities and that the question of 'why' is a category mistake, while the anti-Humean should press the point that this is question-begging. Both characters should be equally competent, with real back-and-forth.
