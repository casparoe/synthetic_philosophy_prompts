I've been going back and forth on whether pragmatic encroachment and epistemic contextualism are actually distinct views or just two ways of saying the same thing. Here's my worry: on encroachment the subject's knowledge itself is stake-sensitive, so "S knows that p" comes out true in a low-stakes case and false in a high-stakes one even though S has exactly the same evidence. But that just sounds like the truth of the attribution is shifting with the context, which is literally what contextualism says. So am I right that encroachment collapses into a kind of contextualism, or is there a real difference in where the variation is located that I'm missing?
