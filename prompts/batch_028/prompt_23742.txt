Write an essay on whether Al-Ghazali's occasionalism can actually dodge the objection that it makes God the direct author of every evil act. The standard defense leans on the distinction between God's directly creating each event and the mere regularity we observe between supposed causes and their effects — what Ghazali calls ʿāda (custom/habit) — on which the fire is at most a concomitant of the burning, not its real cause; God just habitually creates the burning on the occasion of the fire touching the cotton. Weigh whether this distinction does any real work against the authorship objection, or whether it just relabels the problem (God still directly creates the death when a murderer's arm moves). Reach a verdict and justify it, and dont end on a non-committal note.
