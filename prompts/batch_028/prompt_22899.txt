my grandma died last week and i have to give her eulage at the funeral. she was from kwaZulu-Natal and she was a total believer in Ubuntu, the idea that a person is only a person through other people — she believed it so hard she never talked about her own accomplishments, she'd introduce herself as a list of people, 'i'm thabo's mom, i'm the one who sat with you when your husband died.' the problem is a eulogy is a speech about one person, but she'd say there is no such thing as one person, a person is just a knot in a bunch of relationships. and the room is half her old university students and philosphy friends, who will respect it if that idea is doing the actual work in the speech, and half family who just want to hear stories about grandma and will zone out if it sounds like a lecture. can you write it for me, like five minutes? the hard part is i don't want the usual eulogy stuff like 'she was a strong, independent woman' because she'd have found that embarrassing — that's the exact kind of individualist talk she spent her life pushing back against — but i also don't want to dissolve her into an abstract principle, the family needs to actually meet her. i need a speech that figures out how to praise a person who believed you can't praise a person.
