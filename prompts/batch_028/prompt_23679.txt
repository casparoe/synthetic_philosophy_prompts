I am a retired physicist who has only recently begun to read philosophy seriously, and I have been working through the kalam cosmological argument. The premise that everything which begins to exist has a cause strikes me as simply the conservation of energy recast in metaphysical language, on the grounds that energy cannot be created from nothing. If that identification is correct, does the argument not collapse into a tautology, and what work, if any, is left for the separate cosmological claim that the universe began to exist?
