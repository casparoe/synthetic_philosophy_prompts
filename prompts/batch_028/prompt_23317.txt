I'm trying to work out which kinds of aesthetic claims can most and least be transmitted to a hearer who has not actually experienced the work, relying on testimony alone. Could you rank the following five kinds of claims in order from most to least transmissible in that way, and give a short justification (a few sentences) for the overall ordering?

2. Claims about the work's literal subject matter or narrative content (e.g., what events are depicted, or what happens in the plot).
3. Claims about the work's intended meaning or the author's message.
4. Claims about the emotional or affective response the work is typically meant to elicit.
5. Global aesthetic value judgments (e.g., that the work is beautiful, or that it is superior to a rival work).

Please give your answer as a JSON block, formatted as follows:

```json
{
  "ranking": ["<claim type>", ...],
  "justification": "<a few sentences>"
}
```

where "ranking" lists all five claim types in order from most to least transmissible via testimony alone.
