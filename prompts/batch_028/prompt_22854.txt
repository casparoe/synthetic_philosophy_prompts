Write a short dialogue between two people debating whose values an aligned AI should serve. One is a liberal neutralist: the AI should not favor any one comprehensive conception of the good, and should stay neutral across the different value orderings that people hold. The other is a value aggregationist: the AI should optimize for a single aggregate of values, something like a social welfare function computed across individuals. The two are trying to work out whether these commitments are compatible — can you aggregate values across people while remaining neutral about which conception of the good is right — or whether they pull in opposite directions (for instance, whether aggregation presupposes a common metric of value that neutrality denies is available). Both speakers should be equally competent and steelman their own side, with no strawmen, and they should respond to each other's actual points rather than talk past each other. End without a tidy resolution, since the tension is real.
