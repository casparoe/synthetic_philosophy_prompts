I spent my career in experimental physics and have only in retirement begun reading philosophy of science, so before I inflict my views on a reading group I'd be grateful for a consistency check on where I have landed about inference to the best explanation. Roughly:

(a) IBE is a basic and irreducible form of inference: the claim that one hypothesis would best explain the evidence cannot be paraphrased as the claim that the evidence makes it probable, and Bayesian treatments of explanation seem to me to smuggle abduction back in through the priors.

(b) The explanatory virtues beyond empirical fit — unification, simplicity, consilience across independent lines of evidence — are genuine indicators of truth, not merely pragmatic or aesthetic preferences of ours.

(c) A hypothesis built to accommodate data already in hand is confirmed less by that data than it would have been had it predicted the data in advance. In my old field I discount retrospective "just-so" explanations fitted to results we already had.

(d) This is what grounds my realism about unobservable entities: the best, indeed the only non-miraculous, explanation of science's predictive and technological success is that its theoretical claims are approximately true.

(e) I am unmoved by the objection that (d) is circular because it uses IBE to defend IBE. I take a broadly reliabilist line about basic inferential rules: what matters is whether the rule in fact tracks the truth, not whether we can vindicate it from outside itself.

(f) For related reasons I distrust IBE outside the natural sciences: in history, psychology and everyday matters I think it amounts to little more than rationalisation.

Something here feels as though it has to give, but I cannot locate it. Please tell me which of these pairs are genuinely in tension and which only appear to be, order the genuine tensions by severity, and for each state what I would have to abandon and what that would cost me; then say whether there is a single revision that cleans up the most. Please keep the entire reply to no more than 400 words.
