There are never enough, something like 40 slots for 90-ish eligible patients, and the floor nurses basically always take the ranking, so in practice it's deciding.

Last month it ranked a 74-year-old man on home oxygen, no carer at home, 61st. He was discharged without a slot and died at home nine days later. Not clear the slot would have saved him, but the model's own output attached something like a 1-in-8 estimate of death or readmission inside thirty days for him, and the people above him had bigger expected gains. The family's lawyer is writing letters that lean on the word "knew," and my board has to put out a one-page finding.
