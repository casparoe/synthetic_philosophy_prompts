I have just spent a long evening arguing with a friend about Hofstadter's Aunt Hillary — the ant colony he treats as a thinking thing in Gödel, Escher, Bach — and I want to write down where I landed and have the package checked for consistency before we take it up again. Roughly, I hold:

(1) Aunt Hillary can genuinely believe things. There can be a colony-level state with the content that there is food to the north-east, true or false, which no individual ant represents, and which is not merely a façon de parler that we find convenient.

(2) Substrate-independence: what makes a system minded is its causal-functional organisation, not what it is made of. This is my main argument for (1), and I mean it to cover a colony rebuilt in silicon that reproduces the organisation.

(3) But consciousness is a further matter. There being something it is like requires a single unified subject at a time — global integration, a bottleneck through which information has to pass — and a colony spread across a nest and coordinated only by pheromone trails has nothing of the kind. So Aunt Hillary has beliefs and no experiences.

(4) The individual ants are not mere circuit elements either: a single ant has a little experience, more than a transistor, less than a human.

(5) There is no deep fact about which collection of ants is the subject. Whether the satellite nests belong to the same mind, and where the colony stops and the environment begins, is interest-relative; and when a colony buds into two, there is no determinate answer about which one is Aunt Hillary.

Two things I could not answer. My friend pointed out that in arguing for (1) I said the colony is like us only slower and more distributed, and that I now seem to be using that very distribution to rule out (3). And she pressed me on a case: suppose we replace the ants one at a time with radio-controlled surrogate ants, each reproducing the behavioural dispositions of the ant it replaces. I want to say the finished colony is a sham with no mind at all, but I had just argued that organisation is all that matters.

Please audit the set: which pairs are in real tension and which only look as if they are, ordered by severity; for each real tension, what would have to give, what the cheapest fix would cost, and whether there is a single revision that would clean up the most. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence, then give the reasoning, with no preamble and no restating of the question.
