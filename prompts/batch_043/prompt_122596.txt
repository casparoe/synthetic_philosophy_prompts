I want a number, and I want to see the machinery that produces it.

The case. Ada is an ordinary, competent speaker of English who learned the word "plus" in the ordinary way. Every application she has ever made, every correction she has ever accepted, and every application she would make in the remainder of her life involves pairs of numbers whose sum is below one million. Now define the deviant function:

x ⊞ y = x + y, if x + y < N; otherwise x ⊞ y = 5.

Take N to be large enough that no computation the physical universe could support, given its accessible energy and remaining lifetime, ever sums two numbers reaching N. The point of divergence is not merely unreached but physically unreachable. Ada's entire history — her behaviour, her brain states, her dispositions including her dispositions to judge an answer correct, the causal and social history of her word, and everything her community has ever said about her — is equally compatible with her meaning addition and with her meaning ⊞.

The proposition. Call P: Ada means addition by "plus", and this is a genuine truth about her, not merely something we are licensed to assert. Give me your credence in P, decomposed.

The decomposition I want. Do not simply list the positions in the literature; assign a rough probability to each term and say how they combine into a single number. If you think my partition is wrong, replace it, but keep the numbers visible. At minimum I want terms for:

1. Whether meaning-facts exist for finite speakers at all — i.e., whether the skeptical conclusion is false in some form, including the form where the question fails to have a truth value rather than having a false one.
2. Conditional on their existing, which kind of item does the fixing: idealised or counterfactual dispositions; eligibility or simplicity (addition as a more natural candidate referent than ⊞); the interpretive or community stance; externalist causal, informational or etiological facts; or primitivism, on which the demand for a further anchoring item is simply misplaced. For each candidate, give me the probability that it is the right kind of anchor and, conditional on that, that it selects addition rather than ⊞. My supervisor sits at 0.85 on P almost entirely on the strength of the eligibility move.
3. The redeployment term. Whatever you nominate as the meaning-fixing item — a disposition, a community practice, a naturalness ordering, a causal chain — is itself a finite, interpretable item that the skeptic can redescribe in ⊞-friendly terms. What is the probability that the paradox simply reappears one level up, so that no anchor can be insulated from it?
4. The reachability term. Does anything in your answer change if N is merely unreached rather than physically unreachable — if the divergence point is small enough that we could in principle compute past it but never in fact do? If your number moves between the two variants, say by how much and why, since that is a test of whether any anchor you are relying on is doing real work.
5. The normativity term. Is the skeptic entitled to demand a fact that makes some answer correct rather than one that predicts what will be said? Give me your probability that the demand is legitimate, and your probability, conditional on its being legitimate, that any of the anchors in (2) satisfies it.
6. Residual uncertainty about the concept itself: how much of your spread is ignorance about the extension and logical role of "means" rather than about Ada?

Then: combine them, tell me which single term your final number is most sensitive to, and tell me what one argument or discovery — the refutation of what, or the establishment of what — would move it furthest and in which direction. I am at 0.4 on P; my supervisor is at 0.85. Tell me which of these terms our disagreement most likely lives in, and give me the strongest reason to think I am the one who is wrong.
