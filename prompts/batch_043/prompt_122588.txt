Ive been reading around the problem of old evidence (Glymour's thing: the advance of Mercury's perihelion confirmed General Relativity, but everyone already knew it, so P(E)=1 and conditioning does nothing) and what gets me is how stuck it is. Every fix I run into — hand the agent counterfactual credences from before she learned E, treat the newly-grasped entailment T→E as the real evidence, swap E for something like "E was observed" or "T entails E", or just say P(E)=1 is a modeling artifact and throw the proposition out — seems to convince the person proposing it and nobody else. Nobody converts.

My hunch is that there are two different questions under one name plus a third confusion on top: (1) a dynamic one about how a real scientist's confidence actually goes up when she spots the derivation, (2) a static one about how much E supports T given a background corpus, and (3) a fight about what confirmation is even a relation between — propositions, or an agent's reasoning history.

The case that makes me think (3) is where the action is: a researcher has been certain of datum D for fifteen years, derives D from her new theory T, and gets more confident in T. Variant A: unknown to her, D was mis-transcribed and is false. Variant B: her memory of D has drifted, so what she derives from T is really D*, subtly not the D in the notebook. Variant C: she's certain of D now, but was only at 0.9 back then and has forgotten. My intuition is that A kills the confirmation, B weakens it, C changes nothing — but I cant tell whether that intuition is tracking confirmation at all or just facts about her.

So: diagnose the stuckness. Is it verbal — two senses of "confirmation" — or is there a hidden substantive dispute, like whether the object of study is an ideal agent's synchronous credence function or a real person's reasoning over time, that no amount of formalism settles? And given your diagnosis, what would count as progress: a sharper case, a theorem about what any solution has to give up, splitting "support" off from "update", or nothing at all — and if nothing, why should we expect no convergence here?
