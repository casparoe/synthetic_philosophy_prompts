Argue that this internalist conception better accounts for the difference between a fink, which eliminates a disposition, and a mask, which allegedly preserves it, without reducing the latter to a contingent obstacle. Present a focused, self-contained argument; do not supply citations, reading lists, or references to particular philosophical texts.
