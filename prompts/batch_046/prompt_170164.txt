3) I also think AI cant literally lie, because lying needs an intention to fool someone, and AI doesnt have intentions like a person does.

4) Still, I want AI to be upfront that its just a pattern-matcher, not a conscious expert — it should admit when its predicting words rather than knowing something for real.

5) I think users should be able to ask "why did you say that?" and get the actual reasoning path behind the answer, not just a polished excuse made up afterwards.

6) Theres also this idea I came up with, I call it "predictive smoothing" — where the AI shapes its answer based on what it predicts you want to hear, even if every individual sentence is technically true. That feels like a hidden kind of dishonesty to me.

7) At the same time, I dont think the AI should dump its full internal steps in every reply, because that would make conversations useless. Summarizing its own reasoning should be okay.
