I'd like a joke that actually turns on that specific inference—the gap between "this metric is consistent" and "therefore presentism is dead"—and that rewards knowing why Einstein was disturbed: not because he feared time travel, but because Gödel had turned general relativity into a weapon against the intuition of becoming that even Einstein's own block-universe sympathies never fully abandoned. You might frame it as a peer-review dialogue, an anachronistic journal submission demanding observational verification of the rotation parameter, or a presentist trying to block the inference, but the punchline needs to land on the actual logical structure: that Gödel derives the illusion of time from a universe that might not even be ours.
