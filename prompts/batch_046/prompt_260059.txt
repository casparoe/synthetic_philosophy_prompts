i think pure time preference is just a cognitive bias—there's no moral reason to care less about the occurence of suffering just because it's 200 years away rather than 20.

i find parfit's non-identity problem convincing: climate change doesnt harm specific future individuals, since they wouldn't exist with different policies. so our duty to reduce emissions cant be grounded in harm-prevention to identifiable victims.
