But I want to ask a more precise question: is the defect in sphexish behavior a failure of first-order flexibility (the inability to adjust one's actions to circumstances), a failure of second-order flexibility (the inability to revise one's own revision rules), or is it rather a particular kind of normative rigidity—an inability to recognize when one's procedural commitments have become self-defeating?
