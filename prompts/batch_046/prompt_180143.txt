Schopenhauer argues that music is exceptional among the arts because it does not copy Platonic Ideas but is rather an immediate objectification of the Will—melody analogizing its restless striving, harmony analogizing its gradations of objectification. Since the Will is, by definition, blind, non-representational, and prior to all conceptual knowledge, and since Schopenhauer insists that music is grasped without concepts or images, I wonder whether his view makes musical education conceptually impossible. If understanding music requires a direct intuition of a metaphysically non-representational force, then teaching counterpoint, harmonic progression, or formal structure would seem to be instructing students in representations about something that cannot be represented. Does Schopenhauer therefore have to deny that compositional craft can be taught, allowing only for an innate genius who intuits the Will? Or is there a consistent way to separate the non-conceptual expression that music conveys from the conceptual, technical labor required to produce it—without introducing a hidden dualism between the intuitive genius and the calculating artisan?
