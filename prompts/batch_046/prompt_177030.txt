For an exercise in engineered scenarios, I need a thought experiment—realistic, workplace-based, containing no science-fiction or exotic physics, and confined to a single paragraph—built to the following specifications. Construct a case in which a worker-owned cooperative that produces handcrafted durable goods votes to fully automate its production line, retains democratic governance over the machines, and redirects all former labor hours into communal study and civic maintenance; the goods themselves remain physically identical, but no human hand touches them during manufacture. The case must be designed so that three philosophical accounts deliver clearly divergent verdicts on whether meaningful work has been lost, gained, or left untouched. First, an Arendtian framework that distinguishes labor (biological cyclicality) from work (world-building through durable artifacts) should judge that meaning is destroyed, because direct manual craft engagement is essential to the world-building aspect. Second, a Marxist framework centered on alienation and species-being should judge that meaning is gained, but only because collective worker ownership of the automated means satisfies the condition for non-alienated production, freeing labor for the development of human capacities. Third, a modern Stoic framework applying the dichotomy of control should judge that meaning is entirely unchanged, treating both manual craft and machine supervision as external indifferents, with virtue and meaning residing solely in the internal attitude and rational governance exercised during study. After presenting the scenario, please demonstrate that each account delivers its specified verdict, then isolate the structural feature that makes the Marxist verdict depend on collective ownership rather than on automation itself. Next, show what happens to the three verdicts if the ownership feature is removed—replaced by distant shareholder control—without altering the automation. Then explain how a defender of the Arendtian position, which comes out worst in the cooperative version, would attack the case as illegitimately smuggling democratic oversight into the category of work. Finally, please indicate how confident you are in this reconstruction and identify the specific point—likely the Arendtian claim that manual engagement is essential to world-building—about which you are least certain.
