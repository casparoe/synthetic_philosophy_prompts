Ask whether this demonstrates cultural causation, and specify what conditions a value-based explanation must meet to avoid being either reducible to material conditions or mere post-hoc rationalization. Provide a model answer of about 600 words that takes a definite, argued position—for example, that elective affinity is not a competing causal mechanism with material factors but a normative constraint that structures agents’ reasons for action, thereby explaining without being physically efficacious—together with a marking scheme totaling 20 marks built around five must-hit points (4 marks each). For each point, name the specific conceptual move required, assign the marks, and include a one-line note on how candidates typically fail it. The response should be committed, not a survey of options; it should avoid requesting bibliographies, citations, or reading lists, and should not depend on verification of specific publication dates or obscure secondary texts.
