In an art-conservation workshop, a senior restorer keeps saying "the statue is just the clay arranged statue-wise" to shut down debate about whether replacing a shattered limb with marble from a different quarry creates a different object. What are the distinct precise claims that slogan could be making — from the trivial compositional claim, through constitution-without-identity, to eliminativism about the statue itself — and which of those readings actually support treating the replacement as metaphysically irrelevant? Present the readings as a menu with brief verdicts on defensibility and on whether each genuinely dissolves the restoration problem.
