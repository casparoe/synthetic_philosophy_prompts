Here is an argument I constructed for a graduate seminar and now find suspicious because its conclusion undermines too much, yet each step seems defensible. (1) Moral responsibility requires what I will call executive control: the ability to select among live options in light of reasons. (2) Executive control presupposes constitutive control—control over the psychological architecture (desires, dispositions, reasoning styles) that generates the selection. Call this the transmission thesis: without authorship over the chooser, choice is not genuinely controlled. (3) We manifestly lack constitutive control over our temperaments, cognitive biases, and normative frameworks, which are products of genetic and social luck. (4) Therefore we lack executive control over our moral choices. (5) If we lack control, we are not morally responsible for our actions. (6) Moral responsibility is a necessary condition for just punishment, praise, and contractual obligation. (7) Hence just punishment, praise, and contract are impossible. (8) But that conclusion is intolerable: we run these institutions precisely because they seem justified, and abandoning them entirely would collapse social coordination rather than improve it. (9) So (1) is false, or at least the control principle must be jettisoned.

What I cannot locate is where the slip happens. Is the transmission thesis in (2) the weak link? Or does (6) smuggle in a stronger connection between responsibility and institutional practice than is warranted? If I reject (2), I preserve executive control despite our lack of self-authorship, but then I seem to allow responsibility for actions shaped by traits we never chose—which feels like letting moral luck back in through the side door. If I reject (4) or (5), I need an account of responsibility that does not require control, but then I worry I am paying with an ad hoc stipulation. I would prefer to keep (1) intact if I can, but I am open to paying that price if the cost is lower. Which step should I abandon, and what exactly does abandoning it commit me to?
