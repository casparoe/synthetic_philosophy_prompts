The philosopher should counter that outsourcing the execution of attention-management to an external, non-transparent mechanism creates a form of dependency that violates the spirit of the dichotomy, regardless of the initial configuration, because the agent no longer directly governs the impressions that reach consciousness. The dialogue should be genuinely dialectical: each speaker must respond directly to the specific reasoning of the other about prohairesis, dependency, and the limits of moral agency, rather than speaking past one another.
