In my graduate metaphysics seminar this term, I have been working through a position on material constitution that I have come to call frame-relative modal monism. The core claim is that the statue and the clay are strictly identical—one particular, not two entities related by constitution or coincidence—but that this single particular genuinely sustains two non-equivalent modal profiles. Under what I call the artifact-frame, the object cannot survive being squashed into a ball; under the material-frame, it can. Rather than treating this as an ontological conflict requiring either Baker-style constitution or Evans-style relative identity, I take the divergence to be frame-dependent in something like the way velocity is frame-dependent in special relativity: there is one underlying reality, but different descriptive frames extract different modal truths from it, with no privileged frame that reveals the object’s "real" persistence conditions. This avoids multiplying entities and avoids treating constitution as a mysterious primitive relation, though it commits me to the idea that persistence conditions are not absolute properties of particulars but are relative to the explanatory or descriptive frame in which we locate the object.

What I want is an honest assessment of how exposed this view actually is. Not the standard objections—that it collapses into arbitrary relativism, or that it cannot capture the asymmetry of constitution, or that it violates Leibniz’s law across frames—but the deeper vulnerabilities: what specific counterfactual cases or formal results in the metaphysics of persistence would show that modal profiles are objectively fixed rather than frame-relative; whether there is an argument from transitivity of identity across frames that forces us to privilege one profile; and whether results about organisms, artifacts, or diachronic continuity in other domains demonstrate that frame-dependence here collapses into either mereological essentialism or genuine pluralism after all. I want to know which of these defeaters a committed frame-relative modal monist would concede as genuinely damaging, which they would dismiss as resting on an unmotivated demand for absolute persistence conditions, and whether the view has been insulated by qualifications about "frames" in a way that renders it empirically empty. Rank the threats from fatal to merely awkward, and say whether there is any observation or argument that would decisively overturn the position rather than merely forcing a refinement of the framing apparatus.
