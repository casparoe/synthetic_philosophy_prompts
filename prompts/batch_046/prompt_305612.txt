I also want to know whether the "proves too much" conclusion survives once these disanalogies are laid out, or whether Gödel's use of necessary existence in S5 makes the reductio inapplicable. Finally, if the island comparison fails, is there a better analogy—perhaps something closer to my own field, like defining an interface that lists every required capability without guaranteeing an implementation—or should analogical reasoning be abandoned altogether in assessing this argument?
