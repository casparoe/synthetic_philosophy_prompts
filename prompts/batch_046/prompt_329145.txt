Before I evaluate specific positions, I need the conceptual architecture. Write the desiderata that any adequate theory of embedded agency must meet: the phenomena it must explain (for example, genuine self-revision without total transparency, reliance on external cognitive scaffolds, and the difference between being causally shaped by one's environment and being conceptually reducible to it), the distinctions it must preserve (such as partial perspective versus complete determination, or autonomy versus isolation), and the conditions with which it must remain consistent (for instance, that agents do occasionally overhaul their own conceptual frameworks).

Finally, briefly indicate how a "disembedding rationalist" view and a "radically embedded enactivist" view each fare against your list, but keep the weight on the criteria and their internal tensions rather than on a scorecard.
