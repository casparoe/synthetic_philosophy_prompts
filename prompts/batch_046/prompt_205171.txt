Having recently observed, in my own professional environment, two colleagues render the same risk-calibrated judgment with substantially identical information and intent—one producing serious harm, the other none—I find I cannot dismiss the intuition that our retrospective moral assessments diverge in ways that track outcome rather than agency alone, yet I am equally unwilling to abandon the control principle or collapse moral evaluation into a purely deterministic framework. Before I assess existing accounts, I want the full specification of what an adequate theory of moral luck must actually deliver. Write the desiderata: the phenomena it must explain, including not only causal, constitutive, and circumstantial luck but also what I will call epistemic-structural luck—the contingency of which morally relevant facts an agent could reasonably have accessed and whether that access-condition undermines assessment independently of choice; the distinctions it must preserve without collapse, such as between being responsible for an outcome and being the kind of agent for whom that outcome reveals a moral failing, and between luck that genuinely excuses and luck that merely modifies the valence of an otherwise intact assessment; and the broader commitments it must respect, including the basic coherence of moral agency, the non-arbitrariness of blame, and any plausible construal of the principle that ought implies can. For each requirement, provide the motivating case or conceptual pressure, mark which are non-negotiable and which are preferences of particular camps, flag any pair you suspect are jointly unsatisfiable, and indicate which single desideratum does the most damage to the standard positions—Nagel's control-based taxonomy, Williams's account of agent-regret, and the error-theoretic denial that outcome-contingency affects moral status at all. I want to know whether the residue that fails to translate across these frameworks points to a genuine limitation in the theories or to something the vocabulary of moral assessment itself cannot accommodate without distortion.
