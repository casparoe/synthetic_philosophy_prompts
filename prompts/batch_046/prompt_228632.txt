After a recent bereavement forced me to examine which of my commitments rest on others and whether anything stands truly on its own, I have been trying to think through metaphysical grounding and fundamentality not by comparing named theories but by asking what the concept must deliver. Write the full desiderata that any adequate theory of grounding should meet, treating the specification itself as the main result. For each requirement, state the motivating case or distinction behind it and mark whether it is non-negotiable or a preference of a particular camp. The list should cover: (a) the core dependency phenomena it must capture (mental on neural, sets on members, normative on natural, modal on actual); (b) the formal properties it must respect or explain (irreflexivity, asymmetry, transitivity, well-foundedness, uniqueness of ground versus pluralism); (c) the distinctions it must preserve without collapsing (grounding versus causation, grounding versus supervenience, full versus partial ground, metaphysical versus conceptual dependence); (d) its consistency with physicalism, mathematical realism, and modal commitments; (e) whether and how it can accommodate benign circular or looping structures—such as institutional facts that appear partly grounded in collective practices that already presuppose those facts—without collapsing into vicious circularity, and whether accommodating them requires abandoning well-foundedness globally or only localizing it; (f) whether there must be a single fundamental level or whether fundamentality can be distributed. If any subset of these desiderata proves jointly unsatisfiable—especially if strict well-foundedness conflicts with pluralism about social or institutional ontology—say so explicitly and identify the no-go result responsible. Then briefly indicate how a standard well-founded monist view, a pluralist view allowing multiple ground relations, and a view that permits benign loops each fare against the list, but keep the weight on the criteria rather than on the scorecard. Please conclude by stating how confident you are in the claims you have made, and single out the specific point—likely concerning the compatibility of looping institutional grounding with asymmetry—about which you are least sure. Do not provide bibliographies, citations, or reading lists.
