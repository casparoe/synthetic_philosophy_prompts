The audience is split evenly between clinicians, who need stable categories for treatment authorization, and patient advocates, who have documented how being classified reshapes the very distress the categories claim to measure. Structure your ten-minute remarks around Ian Hacking's looping effects, but do not use the idea as decoration: demonstrate that any proposed revision is simultaneously a scientific correction and an intervention that will change the population being described. Argue that the task is therefore not purely epistemic but governmental: deciding which loops to stabilize and whose voices must be included because they are already inside the loop.
