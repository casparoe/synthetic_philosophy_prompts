Prepare me for the objections each camp will actually raise, phrased in their vocabulary: order them by likelihood and damage, note which have real force, say what I should concede up front, and identify the one reply that must land for the thesis to survive. Include the objection I am hoping nobody asks—likely about whether structural uptake collapses into either subconscious internalization or mere causal manipulation.
