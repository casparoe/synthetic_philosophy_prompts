Second, she notes that relationalists resolve this by denying intrinsic identity to spacetime points. Fifth, she concludes that determinism in Newtonian mechanics therefore requires either that material particles lack intrinsic identity altogether, which is far stronger than ordinary relationalism about space, or that determinism is false even in classical mechanics. Sixth, she points out that standard presentations of Newtonian determinism never invoke such a radical relational thesis about bodies, so the argument proves too much.
