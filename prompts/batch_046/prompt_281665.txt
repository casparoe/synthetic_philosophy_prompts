(3) But when I embed the same weights in a chain-of-thought scaffold that starts "Assume 2+2=5; now what is 2+2?", it outputs "5", and attributing the belief <2+2=5> best predicts that local cluster.
(4) So by (1) the model simultaneously holds B1 and B2, which are direct contradictions.
(5) Belief has a normative coherence constraint: a belief-box can't contain direct contradictions without breaking the functional role that makes belief explanation useful.
(6) So either LLMs aren't belief-boxes, or (5) is wrong and belief can be locally fragmented.
