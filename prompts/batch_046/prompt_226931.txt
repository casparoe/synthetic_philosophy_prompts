Please restate both sides of this disagreement strictly within my engineering vocabulary: frame philosophical labor in terms of dependency management, abstraction-layer maintenance, legacy-system archaeology, and internal refactoring that produces no user-visible change; frame the scientists dismissal in terms of removing unnecessary dependencies, avoiding over-abstraction, and insisting that functioning systems need no philosophical commentary. Then provide a precise residue report: identify which philosophical claims translate cleanly into software-engineering practices, which require distortion to fit, and which have no counterpart whatsoever in how we think about system architecture.
