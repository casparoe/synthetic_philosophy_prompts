The sleeping beauty problem has been argued about since like 1999 and nobody's really budged. Halfers say her credence in heads stays 1/2 when she wakes up, thirders say it jumps to 1/3. My working theory is that this stall isn't about bayseian updating or self-location at all—it's that both sides smuggle in different metaphysical assumptions about whether a person can rationally hold different beliefs across phenomologically identical but metaphysically distinct temporal slices, and they're using 'credence' to mean different things. Is that right, or is there a hidden formal disagreement about applying the principle of indiference to centered worlds? I want an honest diagnosis, not another survey: are they answering different questions (one about betting, one about objective chance)? Is the question ill-posed because 'what should Beauty believe' only makes sense relative to being a Monday-instance vs a Tuesday-instance? And what would actually end it—a formal result on conditioning with indexicals, a distinction that dissolves the paradox, or is it just incompatible starting intuitions about personal identity over branching?
