I defend the view that phenomenal consciousness is not a metaphysically basic feature but an evolved predictive-processing interface—what I call a user-illusion—that actively suppresses error signals about its own construction in order to present a streamlined format for behavioral control. Prepare me for the Q&A in the order objections will actually come, phrased as each subgroup would phrase them. For each objection: what I should concede up front, what I must hold, a reply I can deliver in about two minutes, and where that reply is weakest. Include the one question I am hoping nobody asks—likely the charge that my "epistemic blindness" is just Mary’s knowledge argument with predictive machinery substituted for color experience. Also, please state how confident you are in this prep sheet and identify the point about which you are least certain—probably whether the predictive-processing framing genuinely adds explanatory value or is merely decorative.
