Let the refrains take turns through the stanzas, then converge in the final quatrain; engineer the rhyme scheme so that the sensory-absence lines sound hollow or incomplete when paired with the self-affirmation lines, and only in the closing couplet achieve a true phonetic resolution. Place the volta in the second tercet, where the speaker realizes that even the word “soul” is an interpretive addition, not part of the original certainty. The poem should be accurate enough that a philosopher familiar with the argument recognizes the distinction between Avicenna’s substantial dualism and this stripped-down, first-person reading, yet it must contain no bibliographies, citations, or references to specific editions or secondary literature.
