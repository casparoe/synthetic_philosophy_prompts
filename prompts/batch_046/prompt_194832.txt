In my advanced metaethics course we have been examining whether normative reasons can exist in complete independence from any reasoning agent—what our readings describe as the free-floating rationales thesis. On this view, a consideration can genuinely count in favor of an action or attitude even if no actual, possible, or artificial reasoner ever stands in a position to register it; the normative force is an irreducible relation between states of affairs and practical requirements, not mediated by mental or computational presence.

As a foil for my final essay, I have sketched what I call the resonant-trace model. Rather than treating reasons as ontologically basic entities, this model holds that reasons are modal residues generated by reasoning structures: whenever an agent or system could rationally recognize a consideration, that possibility leaves a structural trace in the space of normative possibilities. A trace becomes an active reason only when excited by a resonator—an actual or counterfactual reasoning system—without which it remains an inert potential rather than a present normative fact.

Please compare these two frameworks. First, assess which offers the more coherent ontology: does the free-floating view avoid psychologism only at the cost of positing mysterious non-mental normative objects, and does the resonant-trace model genuinely avoid that cost or merely relocate it into modal structure? Second, apply both to a specific case: suppose there exists a unique mathematical proof that no mind—human, artificial, or otherwise—will ever encounter, and suppose its permanent destruction would eliminate a structurally important result. Does the free-floating view imply there is a reason to preserve it that floats in total cognitive darkness? Does the resonant-trace view deny any normative consideration here, and is that denial intuitive or a reductio? Third, determine whether the resonant-trace model is genuinely distinct from the free-floating thesis, or whether allowing artificial and counterfactual resonators causes it to collapse back into a form of unreduced normative realism. Give your answer with explicit reasoning for each point.
