I'm a philosophy-of-science doctoral student designing a methodological exercise in adversarial collaboration for dissertation work on explanatory pluralism. Draft a joint statement that could be signed by two opposing camps regarding the framework of physical, design, and intentional stances.

Party A (pattern realist): design-stance and intentional-stance patterns are genuinely real, causally explanatory, and irreducible to physical-stance descriptions.

Party B (physicalist eliminativist): only physical-stance descriptions track ontological structure; design and intentional patterns are heuristic projections without independent reality.

Structure the document as follows. First, a numbered section of agreed claims—propositions both sides can sign—covering the formal distinctions among the three stances, the predictive success of design-stance reasoning in evolutionary biology and engineering, the practical intractability of purely physical-stance predictions for complex adaptive systems, and the conditions under which intentional-stance attributions succeed or fail. Second, a disagreements table containing: (a) each side's formulation of its own position, worded so that side would accept it; (b) a neutral gloss of the residual dispute; and (c) identification of which disagreements are substantive versus verbal. Third, the cruxes: (i) whether predictive success at the design stance is sufficient for ontological commitment to design-level patterns; (ii) whether the intentional stance requires a literal interpreter or can be fully naturalized without residue; and (iii) whether there exist stance-transcendent facts that all three descriptions approximate differently, or whether each stance constructs its own explanatory domain.

For each crux, specify a concrete case or argument both sides agree bears on it, and record in advance what each side predicts the case will demonstrate.

Inject the following niche scenario into the analysis: a synthetic gene-regulatory circuit engineered so that its macro-behavior—oscillating expression tracking a chemical gradient—is predicted with perfect accuracy by an intentional-stance model treating the circuit as seeking the gradient, yet remains computationally intractable to derive from molecular-level physical interactions within practical limits. Both sides must state explicitly whether this demonstrates irreducible ontological reality of design patterns, mere instrumental reliability, or an epistemic gap carrying no ontological weight.

Finally, include a diagnosis of verbal disagreement: determine whether disputes about the reality of patterns dissolve once reality is indexed to explanatory utility rather than to ontological independence, and specify exactly which parts of the debate become terminological if so.

Write this as a direct, formal request for the document itself—not as a dialogue, adjudication, or diagnosis of why debate is stalled. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, citation requests, or direct textual quotations; work from the conceptual framework alone. Frame the prompt as coming from a doctoral student proposing this collaboration for a methodological drafting exercise.
