But maybe I'm wrong and it's actually that Hart is asking what makes a rule legally valid while Fuller is asking what makes a legal system worthy of obedience, and they're talking past each other. So: what's the actual structure of the disagreement here? Is there a hidden empirical or formal question, or is it just bedrock intuitions about authority?
