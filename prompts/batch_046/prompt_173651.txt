Construct a decision tree for observer-selection reasoning that sorts a reasoner into an actual logical position rather than a named camp. Root commitment: your existence as an observer carries evidential weight. First branch: is that weight comparative across hypotheses with different total observer counts (favoring worlds that predict more observers) or restricted to sampling within a fixed reference class? Then route through four concrete diagnostic nodes—(1) the presumptuous-philosopher scenario (two cosmological models with 10^18 versus 10^20 observers, no birth-rank data); (2) a doomsday-style case where you learn your birth rank is in the first percentile; (3) a simulation case with virtual copies at different frequencies across otherwise identical worlds; and (4) a restricted-class case conditioning only on being carbon-based and post-1900. At each node, phrase the split as a judgment about the case, not a theory label. Leaves must include unrestricted self-indication, self-sampling with a species-level reference class, a hybrid applying self-indication to cosmological scale but self-sampling to temporal location, and a deflationary denial of anthropic updates. Mark any unoccupied logical combinations—e.g., unrestricted self-indication for birth-rank paired with its denial for cosmological scale—and briefly diagnose whether the gap is incoherent or merely unexplored. Finally, flag pairs of positions the tree cannot separate with these four cases, explaining whether the failure is shared prediction or case-underdetermination. Limit to six nodes and eight leaves; do not request bibliographies, citations, or reading lists.
