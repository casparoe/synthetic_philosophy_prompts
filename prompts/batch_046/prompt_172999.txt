I recently began caring for my father following a prolonged minimally conscious state after cardiac surgery, and the clinical team’s reliance on behavioral access criteria—command-following, yes-no signaling—has made me acutely aware of how global workspace theory frames consciousness as broadcast availability. Next month I present at a joint philosophy-neuroscience colloquium a thesis that phenomenal consciousness can persist without global broadcast if we reconceive access not as a binary gate but as a graded, modality-specific integration profile; specifically, I defend a modified global workspace framework that allows local phenomenal workspaces—coherent intra-modal integrations that never reach global availability—to account for both anesthesia-awareness reports and the dissociation between affective and cognitive access seen in certain post-anoxic patients. The audience will include senior higher-order-thought theorists who maintain that without higher-order representation there is no phenomenal character to miss, neuroscientists committed to Dehaene’s global neuronal workspace model who will demand empirical markers distinguishing local from global broadcast, and clinical neurologists who will worry that my view collapses the diagnostic boundary between minimally conscious and vegetative states. Prepare me for the objections in the order they will actually come: how will the HOTT proponents phrase the conceptual incoherence of local workspaces lacking global accessibility; what empirical falsifiability challenge will the workspace neuroscientists raise, and which feature of my graded-integration model is most vulnerable to it; how should I concede the clinical risk without undermining the philosophical point; and include the objection I am hoping nobody asks—namely, whether my father’s case provides epistemic warrant for revising a general theory of consciousness rather than being an emotionally motivated exception—together with an honest assessment of where my reply to that objection is weakest and what I should hold versus concede in the moment.
