My essay aims to go beyond the obvious mapping of Ralph onto weak justice and Jack onto brute force; instead, I want to examine whether the conch functions as a Pascalian symbol of justice that lacks intrinsic force, whether Ralph's democratic authority is ever truly separate from the physical object that guarantees it, and whether Jack's tribe succeeds not by rejecting justice altogether but by recognizing, in a Pascalian sense, that authority must be backed by the capacity to enforce obedience.

Could you help me construct a critical reading that takes both Golding's narrative and Pascal's conceptual framework seriously?
