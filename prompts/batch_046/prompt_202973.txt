The theory must explain why agents with genuinely different private signals can eventually align rather than permanently disagree, but it must also preserve the distinction between convergence on a belief and convergence on a joint action—since in engineering we routinely observe state agreement without protocol agreement. Crucially, tell me if any subset of these requirements is jointly unsatisfiable—perhaps guaranteed convergence under bounded computation is incompatible with preserving rational autonomy under prior divergence.
