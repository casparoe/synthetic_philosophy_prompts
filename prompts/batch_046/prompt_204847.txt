(1) the basic popper idea is science is whatever is falsifiable in principle—there has to be some observation that could show it wrong. (2) but specifying that observation always needs background stuff held fixed: your instruments work, your auxilary theories are ok, etc. (3) so falsifiability is never a property of one claim alone; its always claim-plus-background-package. (4) now take astrology. if the astrologer gets to fix the background—say mercury retrograde works through an undetectable field—then no observation contradicts it inside that package. (5) but physics works the same way: if physicists could always revise quantum mechanics to save any result, then QM isnt falsifiable either inside that package. (6) so falsifiability doesnt separate astrology from physics; it separates people who refuse to revise auxiliaries from people who do. (7) that means the demarcation problem is really about researcher psychology or sociology, not about logic. (8) but popper wanted an objective logical criterion, not a sociological one. (9) so either we admit its sociological, or we have to fix the background externally—like saying hold constant that planets have no mental influence—which makes astrology falsifiable. (10) but then were just baking our prior belief that astrology is false into the background conditions. (11) therefore falsificationism either collapses into sociology or into circular reasoning. where does it slip? i want to keep (1) and (3) if possible, but (6) feels like its cheating somehow and i cant name why.
