Give an example of a conceptual issue arising within semantic externalism—specifically, a Twin-Earth-style tension applied to mental representations of artifact functions rather than natural kinds. Describe a scenario in which two physically identical cognitive agents, one on Earth and one on Twin Earth, both form an internal representation with the content seal-container-tool through indistinguishable perceptual-motor interactions with objects that behave identically in local use. On Earth, the object is an engineered polymer membrane with an intentional design history; on Twin Earth, it is a biologically cultivated tissue serving an analogous local role but lacking any design history and embedded in a different causal-ecological network. The example should illustrate the precise conceptual difficulty in determining whether the representational content is fixed narrowly by the agents' internal functional roles or widely by the external causal and design-related environment, and it should avoid invoking any specific named sub-theory beyond the general Twin-Earth framework.
