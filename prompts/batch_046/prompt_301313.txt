"The KS solution selects the point on the Pareto frontier that lies on the straight line connecting d to the utopia point U, scaled by the largest factor λ such that λU remains inside S. The four axioms are: (1) individual rationality—each player receives at least their disagreement payoff; (2) Pareto efficiency—there is no unused surplus; (3) symmetry—if the two players have identical utility functions, then the solution assigns them identical payoffs; (4) monotonicity—if the feasible set S expands in a direction that benefits only player i, then i’s payoff should not decrease; this is essentially the same independence-of-irrelevant-alternatives condition that appears in Nash bargaining, because it prevents the addition of irrelevant options from changing the outcome.

On the normative issue, monotonicity is purely technical. It governs how the solution responds to expansions of the feasible set, but it says nothing about desert, equality, or fairness.
