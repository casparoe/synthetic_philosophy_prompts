(2) B said that's too simple because alignment taxes are mostly in training, not inference. once you've trained with the extra objectives, the model runs at the same speed, so there's no ongoing competitive drag.

(6) B said the benchmark/marketing distinction is collapsing because buyers now audit internal processes, so the handicap has to be real inside the system too.
