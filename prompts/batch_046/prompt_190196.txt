For each desideratum, provide the motivating observation or conceptual consideration behind it. Then flag any pair you suspect is jointly unsatisfiable—especially if one demands strict phenomenological unity while another demands full functional independence between hemispheres. Finally, briefly indicate how a unitary-mind view, a strict dual-mind view, and a graded-integration view fare against the list.
