A philosopher of mind applies Daniel Dennett's four-fold taxonomy—Darwinian, Skinnerian, Popperian, and Gregorian creatures—to classify cognitive systems by the mechanisms of generate-and-test available to them. Her established case judgments are these: a nematode with hard-wired chemotactic circuits is Darwinian, shaped solely by evolutionary selection without individual learning; a pigeon adjusting its pecking rate through operant reinforcement is Skinnerian; a human chess player who silently evaluates hypothetical positions before acting is Popperian; a child learning grammar and tool-use through spoken instruction and correction from elders is Gregorian; an octopus that solves a jar-opening problem through individual exploration, with no evidence of social transmission, she judges Skinnerian rather than Popperian, on the ground that its success comes from real-world trial rather than internal simulation; and a large language model that produces linguistic outputs by statistical pattern-matching over a human-generated corpus, without real-time environmental feedback, internal modeling of consequences, or symbolic invention, she judges Gregorian, on the ground that it functions entirely as a consumer of a cultural-linguistic environment it did not create.

She is now presented with a synthetic biological construct—composed of frog stem cells arranged into a self-propelling form—that navigates mazes by environmental feedback, improves path efficiency across repeated trials, alters the movement of neighboring constructs via deposited chemical traces, possesses no neurons, carries no evolutionary lineage, and employs neither symbolic language nor physical tools.

What verdict should she reach if she wishes to be consistent with her own precedents? Identify the closest analog in her track record, articulate at least two organizing rules that could jointly explain her prior judgments but yield different results here, state what each rule predicts for this construct, offer your best recommendation with the reasoning that favors it, and specify what further distinguishable case would reveal which rule she is actually following. If her precedents genuinely underdetermine the outcome, state clearly what principle she would be adopting with each of the competing choices.
