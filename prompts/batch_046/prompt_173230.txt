question: explain how "value arbitrage" lets two value systems reach compromise without either collapsing, using the tension between distributive fairness and procedural autonomy.

student answer: Value arbitrage works by letting frameworks exchange peripheral norms so both come out ahead, like a trade but for moral commitments. In the fairness/autonomy tension, the fairness camp looses its strict equality demand to allow more procedural control, while the autonomy camp accepts distributive limits to prevent domination. Both sides have to temporarily suspend their core commitments to generate moral surplus — thats the only way the deal clears. This mirrors Rawls's original position, where people drop particular values behind the veil of ignorance to agree on shared principles, so the compromise is essentially a mini veil. Overall neither identity is lost permanently and the arrangement is stable.
