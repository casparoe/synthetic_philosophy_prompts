Please explain what follows, conceptually, if we take this framing seriously: does treating the Holocaust as structurally continuous with other modern bureaucratic atrocities — such as the administrative logistics of colonial resource extraction or the systematized management of forced labor in industrial agriculture — necessarily erode the claim that the Holocaust possesses a distinctive moral singularity, or can the uniqueness thesis be reconstructed to accommodate Bauman's analysis without collapsing into historical relativism?
