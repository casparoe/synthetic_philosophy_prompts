The five points should cover, in order: how error propagates silently in tight systems; how innovation is channeled into ritualized rather than adaptive forms; why the illusion of consensus masks accumulating fragility; what hidden enforcement costs do to collective cognition; and why loose cultures survive shocks precisely through permitted, small-scale rule-breaking.
