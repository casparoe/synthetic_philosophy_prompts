I want to understand exactly what precise claim or cluster of claims this slogan could be expressing, since it currently seems to do argumentative work ranging from the tautological to the highly contentious.

Please treat the slogan as a piece of language whose meaning must be disambiguated rather than as a single thesis to be explained. For each reading, indicate whether it is a platitude, a defensible but substantive thesis, or false or confused. Then specify which of these readings actually supports using the slogan to shut down an objection about applying geometric rationality to non-ergodic scientific judgment, and which are either irrelevant or misapplied in that context.
