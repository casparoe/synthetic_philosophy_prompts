Here is where I have landed on Nozick's treatment of animals, and I want to know whether the package actually holds together. (1) I take his formula literally: animals lack the rational nature that grounds Kantian side-constraints, so moral evaluation of them is purely aggregative—only total experienced welfare matters, and individual animals are replaceable. (2) At the same time, I think that if an animal displays sustained, cross-contextual project-pursuit—maintaining a multi-stage plan that spans contexts and days—this activates the Kantian constraint, not because the animal is fully rational, but because it instantiates the structural feature of a "life-plan" that Nozick treats as the boundary marker. (3) From this I draw a practical conclusion about wild-animal suffering: we should prioritize pool-level interventions that reduce suffering without identifying or tracking specific individuals, precisely because replaceability makes the pool, not the individual, the proper moral unit. (4) I also insist that Nozick's own framework demands a sharp binary cutoff, not a spectrum: a being is either inside the protected class or it belongs to the fungible utilitarian pool. (5) Finally, I hold that tractability should guide application—if an intervention is only feasible when we monitor particular animals, that counts against it, because such tracking risks treating beings that may have life-plan structures as interchangeable.

Audit this set. Do not merely list logical possibilities; take a definite position on which commitments genuinely conflict, which conflicts dissolve on inspection, and what the cheapest restoration is—specifying exactly which thesis must be revised or abandoned and what that costs the overall view. In particular, address whether (2) and (4) can coexist once we recognize that project-pursuit comes in degrees, and whether (3) and (5) remain jointly tenable once (2) is granted, given that applying (2) seems to require precisely the kind of individual identification that (3) and (5) reject. Give your reasons at each step, and say clearly where you think I am helping myself to an unstated premise.
