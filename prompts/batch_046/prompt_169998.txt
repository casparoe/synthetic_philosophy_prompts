Write a parody of a university faculty job advertisement, approximately 600 words, in the deadpan bureaucratic voice of a provost's office announcing an endowed chair in Applied Handicap Theory. The satire should target the uncritical extension of costly signaling logic and Zahavi's handicap principle into institutional design—not any named living person. Keep the evolutionary reasoning accurate: signals must be genuinely wasteful to be credible, observers rationally discount cheap talk, quality is inferred from the magnitude of unnecessary expenditure, and any reduction in signal cost destroys information value. Invent a fictional department and position title, but do not invent false biological claims. Translate the logic literally into academic labor requirements—for example, requiring candidates to maintain redundant administrative protocols that serve no pedagogical purpose, to publish in deliberately inaccessible formats to demonstrate resource abundance, or to hold obligations at physically inconvenient times and locations as displays of stamina. Include a fictional evaluation metric (for instance, a "Zahavi coefficient" applied to teaching loads) that sounds quantitative but measures only the degree of institutional waste a candidate can sustain without complaint. The humor must come from recognizing that the institution has correctly grasped the theory and implemented it without irony, not from caricaturing biology. Maintain sustained deadpan irony throughout: a specialist should see that the author understands exactly what is being exaggerated, and a general reader should be able to tell what is being satirized without any winking or explicit signaling. Do not ask for citations, bibliographies, or references to specific works by title or date, and avoid mentioning real scholars by name.
