In our AP Government dirty-hands unit, Professor Vasquez has given these four verdicts: she holds that forging surrender documents to end a war one day earlier and save thousands of lives is a justified dirty hand; that authorizing a warrantless entry to rescue hostages is justified; that breaking a diplomatic promise to an ally in order to secure a trade deal that lifts sanctions on civilians is not justified; and that a campaign manager spreading false rumors about an opponent's health to win an election that would enable climate legislation is not justified. She must now rule on a new case: a city council member secretly diverts a small portion of public disaster-relief funds to pay a blackmailer who threatens to release fabricated corruption documents, knowing that disclosure would collapse the council and halt relief for months, harming thousands, and she takes no personal gain. Predict Professor Vasquez's verdict, identify whether she is running a catastrophic-proportionality rule, a role-integrity rule, or something else, and specify the further case that would best tell those rules apart.
