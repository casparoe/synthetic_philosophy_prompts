The questions must be answerable without knowing philosophical terminology—referring only to the three engineering cases or to general intuitions about responsibility and counterfactuals. Please flag any pair of leaves that your branching structure cannot genuinely separate, and diagnose every unoccupied logical combination of answers: indicate whether it appears incoherent, pragmatically unattractive, or merely unexplored.
