Set aside the usual LessWrong framing of the Sleeping Beauty problem—thirder versus halfer, betting arguments, and centered-world arithmetic—for a moment. I want you to defend the halfer position, that Beauty’s credence in Heads upon waking should remain 1/2, but do so through an argument that turns on the metaphysics of belief-bearing subjects rather than on sampling frequencies. Specifically, treat Beauty as a single enduring agent whose doxastic state is interrupted by sleep-induced amnesia, not as a pair of discrete observer-moments. Argue that self-locating updates apply only across genuinely distinct epistemic agents, and that because Monday-Beauty and Tuesday-Beauty are stages of one continuous subject with a unified prior, counting Tuesday as an independent sample violates the diachronic identity conditions required for Bayesian conditioning on centered worlds. Engage seriously with the perdurantist alternative—where temporal parts might justify distinct samples—but show why the endurantist framing undermines the thirder’s symmetry argument without collapsing into a trivial denial of anthropic reasoning. Ground the argument in the logic of self-location and temporal continuity; avoid appeals to external coin-flip frequencies, betting strategies, or decision-theoretic considerations. The tone should be precise, analytic, and aimed at an audience that has already read the standard blog posts but is looking for a less familiar line of resistance.
