(4) So if convergence among ideal observers is sufficient to establish objectivity in taste, then by parity of reasoning it should also establish objectivity in mathematics and science—unless there is a principled reason why taste is unique.
(5) Yet Hume insists that taste is not special: it is merely a refined sentiment, and the true judge is someone whose faculties are in proper working order, not someone with access to a reality independent of sentiment.
