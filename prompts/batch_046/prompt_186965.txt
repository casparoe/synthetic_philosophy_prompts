I've been working through the Sleeping Beauty problem on my own, and I built an argument that seems to push the standard "thirder" reasoning into an absurd result, but I can't locate the error. Here is the chain:

1. Before the experiment, Beauty knows the coin is fair, so her credence in Heads is 1/2.
2. When she is awakened, she knows that if the coin came up Tails she will be awakened twice (once on Monday and once on Tuesday, with her memory erased between them), while if it came up Heads she is awakened only once.
3. Therefore, treating herself as randomly selected from all possible awakenings, she should update her credence in Heads to 1/3 and in Tails to 2/3.
4. Now apply the same principle elsewhere. Cosmologists say the observable universe may be large enough to contain distant, causally disconnected regions with physical duplicates of me—people with identical brain states having this exact thought.
5. If I should update my beliefs by treating myself as randomly selected from all instances of "me" across time in the Sleeping Beauty case, then consistency demands I do the same across space: upon learning the universe is large, I should think it more likely I am one of the distant duplicates rather than the one here.
6. But that conclusion seems plainly wrong. The mere size of the universe should not change my belief about whether I am sitting at this desk right now.
7. So if accepting (3) commits me to accepting (5), and (5) is false, then (3) must be false, and Beauty should remain at 1/2.

My intuition is that something slips between (3) and (5)—perhaps time and space are not analogous, or "random selection" requires a kind of symmetry that exists only in the experimental protocol. But I cannot name the slip precisely. Which step should I reject? If I reject (3), does that mean self-locating information can never rationally update non-self-locating beliefs? If I instead reject (5), what is the principled reason that being duplicated across Monday and Tuesday is different from being duplicated across cosmic regions? And if the halfers are right, do they owe an account of why Beauty's Tuesday awakening feels like it should carry evidential weight?
