Write a short story about a sleep-lab researcher who notices that lucid dreamers in REM keep muttering its raining but i dont beleive it, except theyre not lying or confused. In their brains the belief-updating loop is chemically muted while the sensory simulation stack keeps running, so they literally perceive rain without forming the belief. Use this to illustrate Moores paradox, but dont stop at the textbook version — explore whether the paradox collapses (or gets stranger) when the first-person I is split across sub-personal modules with no shared doxastic perspective. Show the experience from inside one of those modules, not just from the researchers notebook.
