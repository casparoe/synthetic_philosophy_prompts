For each of these four, provide a brief follow-up keyed to the reply she is most likely to offer—particularly the defense that closure operates at the architecture level rather than the use level. Avoid biographical openings, and do not ask for bibliographies, citations, or reading lists. The tone should be polished and appropriate for an audience interested in both philosophy and fiction.
