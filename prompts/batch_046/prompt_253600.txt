(4) Some races to the bottom are actually good discovery procedures: they reveal the minimal sustainable constraint set, so trying to block them is usually a mistake.

(5) Centralized arbitration just relocates the multipolar dynamics upward, so hierarchical fixes are always illusions, never real escapes.
