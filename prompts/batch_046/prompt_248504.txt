Maintain serious literary tone and character coherence, allowing readers unfamiliar with Schopenhauer to grasp the distinction—music as direct Will rather than mediated Idea—through sensory and dramatic events rather than exposition. Ground the philosophical claim precisely: music reveals the world not as phenomenon but as it is in itself, and Vorn's deafness paradoxically grants him unmediated access to this reality.
