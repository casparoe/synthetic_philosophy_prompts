4. but that's clearly false. people leave cults, blow the whistle on corrupt companies, and walk away from family traditions all the time.
5. so embedded agency has to be wrong; either we're not really embedded, or "embedded" doesn't constrain reasoning in the way philosophers claim.

he thinks the only escape is to reject (1), but i want to keep the idea that we're not just atoms floating in space. i feel like something slips between (2) and (3), but i can't name it. which step should i reject, and what does rejecting it actually cost?
