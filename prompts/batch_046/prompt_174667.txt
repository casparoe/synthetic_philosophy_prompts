For each reading, please indicate whether it is trivial, whether it is substantive and defensible, whether it is false, and — crucially — whether it actually licenses the attorney's objection or instead vindicates the opposing counsel's dismissal. The dramatic tension depends on the ambiguity staying live, so I need the interpretations kept clearly separated with verdicts attached.
