Mira said Landauers principle kills the demon: erasing one bit costs kT ln 2, so the second law survives and information is just bookkeeping for heat flow. Jonas said thats too quick — the erasure cost only shows information processing has a thermodynamical price, not that information itself is physical. He asked what happens if you replace the demon with an automatic Szilard gate that records measurements in the wall's own micro-state; there's no mind to erase, yet you still need an information-theoretic boundary condition to keep the gate from pulling free work. Mira came back that this just relocates the bookkeeping — the wall's atomic configuration is just matter arranged, not some ghostly information-substance. Jonas then said if information is purely bookkeeping, why does the correlation structure between gate and gas determine whether work can be extracted, rather than just the total energy? They went in circles. I couldn't call it. Can you adjudicate point by point — who's right about Landauer, about the automatic gate, about correlation versus energy — and say what both are missing?
