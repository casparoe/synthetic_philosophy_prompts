Reach and defend a specific verdict: does the center of narrative gravity survive intact once prospective coherence and its attendant inertia are included in the analysis, or does their inclusion force us to concede an agentic substrate? I want a committed response—state your position clearly, lay out the decisive considerations that push you there, and explicitly identify the point in your argument that is strongest and the point where a well-crafted counterexample would do the most damage. Do not end with a survey of options; justify one conclusion.
