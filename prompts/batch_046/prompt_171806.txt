2. latent ontology: cross-lingual attention builds implicit world-models, so the model understands in a distributed, non-conscious way.

4. scale-equivocation: saying it lacks understanding is a category mistake at high paramter counts, like arguing a hurricane doesn't understand weather.

i'm especially unsure whether #3 is philosophically defensible or just a motte-and-bailey for anthropomorphizing the toolchain. also, does #4 confuse functional complexity with phenomenology? just rank and score them with a sentence each on why.
