I've been retired from physics for about three years now and lately I've been trying to read actual philosphy instead of just science populizers. Last week my friend Dave, who's a high school math teacher, tried to explain Hobbes's take on geometry to me over coffee. He said that Hobbes basically thought geometry wasn't about discovering eternal truths like Plato or Euclid claimed, but was just a kind of manual craft for drawing shapes with rulers and compasses. Because Hobbes was a strict materialist who only believed in bodies in motion, he rejected Euclid's talk about dimensionless points and infinite lines as meaningless fiction — Dave even said Hobbes treated geometric objects like "fictitious spaces" we invent just to help us calculate, kind of like a physicist using imaginary numbers. Dave said Hobbes treated geometric proofs like recipes for constructing figures, and since we invent the definitions ourselves — he compared it to how societies stipulate laws — the results are only conventionally true, not necessary in any deep sense. He also said this is why Hobbes got into that nasty feud with Wallis — Wallis was defending the traditional view that geometry reveals eternal structures, but Dave thinks Hobbes actualy lost the argument because he was just bad at pure math and kept trying to square the circle. Dave concluded that Hobbes was basically an early version of a logical positivist about mathematics, and that if you accept his materialism you have to reject the idea that math describes reality at all, since it's all just agreed-upon construction rules. Something about this feels like it's mixing up a real insight with some anachronistic labeling and maybe a confusion about why the Wallis fight happened, but I can't tell where exactly. Is Dave's summary fair? Which parts capture Hobbes's actual view in De Corpore, which are common simplifications, and which are flat wrong? And does the conclusion that Hobbes's materialism forces you to reject mathematical realism actualy follow from the accurate parts, or is Dave sneaking in extra premises?
