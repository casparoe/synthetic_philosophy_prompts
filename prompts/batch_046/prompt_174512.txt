Please go through these commitments and identify which pairs are in genuine tension, which tensions are only apparent and dissolve upon closer inspection, and for each real conflict state how severe it is and what the cheapest revision to my profile would be. Then tell me which single change to this set would restore the greatest coherence with the least damage to what seems most distinctive about Goethe's outlook. I want your response as continuous prose only, with no headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables, written as you might write a careful letter to a colleague; please lead with your bottom-line judgment about whether the profile is salvageable as it stands.
