2. but i don't think imagination is the vehicle; it's structural coherence that matters, like seeing a legal system could have a different liability rule without picturing it in sensory detail.

7. and mathematical falsehoods (like a largest prime) are inconceivable in the exact same structural way as logical contradictions, which is why they track impossibility so neatly.
