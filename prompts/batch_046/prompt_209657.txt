The standard debate feels stuck because people keep sliding between three different meanings: arbitrary symbol mapping (like Morse), digital storage (like computer files), and actual semantic content (like language). I want to cut through this by asking a sharper, weirder question: does the genetic system satisfy what we might call "interpretational autonomy"? By that I mean, is the decoder — the ribosome, tRNA, the whole translation machinery — physically and informationally outside the message being decoded? So DNA might be closer to a self-extracting archive than to a true code, and that might actually matter for whether genetic information is genuinely representational or just causally correlated.
