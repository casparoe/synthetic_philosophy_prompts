Third, I believe that in human cultural evolution, group-level selection operates in an irreducible fashion—social norms and institutions can spread because they confer net benefits on groups even when they impose costs on individuals within them, and I do not think these dynamics can be fully translated into gene-level or individual-level terms. Sixth, I maintain that in the genetic domain, kin selection and multilevel selection are mathematically equivalent descriptions of the same underlying dynamics, so debates between them concern framing rather than substantive causal differences.
