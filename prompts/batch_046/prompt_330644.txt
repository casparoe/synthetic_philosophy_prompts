3. but i also think ecosystem integrity has intrinsic value, so humanely culling invasive grey squirrels to protect native reds can be justified, even though both are sentient.

5. it's fine for me to prioritise saving my own cat over a stranger's child in a burning building, but only because of our bond, not because cats are worth less.
6. accidental harm (squashing insects driving, walking through grass) doesn't carry the same moral weight as intentional factory farming, even if the total suffering from accidents is probably higher — i'm not committing a micro-atrocity every time i take a step.
