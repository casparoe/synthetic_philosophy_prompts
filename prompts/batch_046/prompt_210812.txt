Riva should defend functional isomorphism and predicitive success in explanation; Cal should push back that scientific attribution requires constraining behavior, not just annotating it. Make sure they actually respond to each other — real back-and-forth, neither sounding like a strawman. Keep the tone casual, like a chat in a grad lounge, and feel free to drop a typo or missing comma here and there. Start the dialogue immediately with no preface, no "Here is the dialogue" intro, and no commentary afterwards.
