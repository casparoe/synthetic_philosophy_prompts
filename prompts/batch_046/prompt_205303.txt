I'm a grad student working on weakness of will for my diss. There's this pattern I keep seeing in qualatative studys on addiction and procrastination that doesn't sit right with the standard akrasia framewrk. One subject describes blowing off rehab by saying that was future-me's problem, and future-me isn't really me. Another talks about binge-spending as giving a gift to tonight-me, since tomorrow-me is basically a stranger who'd just save it anyway. It's not classic hyperbolic discounting -- they aren't miscalculating value, they're treating the temporal parts as different agents so harming the future self doesn't register as self-harm. Does this specific rationalization have a name? I know there's literture on diachronic identity and divided self, and Parfit's reductionism gets close, but none seem to capture the strategic use of discontinuity to dissolve akrasia. Is there an established concept for this, or am I describing a distinct failure mode that just hasn't been named? If it's the latter, what should I call it and how do I distinguish it from plain equivocation about personal identity?
