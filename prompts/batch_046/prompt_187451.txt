Here is my opening move: if God’s middle knowledge includes the true counterfactual that Peter, placed in circumstance C, would freely deny Christ, and God then decrees to actualize precisely C, then Peter’s denial becomes part of the actual world. Given that God’s knowledge of this counterfactual is infallible and logically prior to creation, and given that God’s decree selects this specific world-branch among the available counterfactual structures, how is Peter’s denial genuinely free in the libertarian sense rather than being fixed by the conjunction of divine knowledge and divine selection—especially if we treat those counterfactuals as robust conditions that actively limit which worlds are open to be actualized? Respond to me directly as this character.
