I'm wondering if the stall is because people are running together two different questions: one about the actual causal history of a trait (was selection the driver?) and one about the proper form of a scientific explanation in biology (should it cite functions or mechanisims?). And with stuff like evo-devo and constructive neutral evolution blurring the line between spandrels and hidden adaptations, maybe the original dichotomy doesnt even map onto current practice. Is this disagreement substantive, verbal, or are the two sides just doing different disciplines under one name?
