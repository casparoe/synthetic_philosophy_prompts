Rank how well Hume’s standard of taste—delicacy of sentiment, practice, comparison, freedom from prejudice, and good sense—applies to four domains: canonical landscape painting; the formal elegance of a mathematical proof; the perceived beauty of a quantum decoherence pattern; and the sensory profile of a synthetic compound engineered to activate exactly one taste-receptor pathway. For each domain, assign a tier—high, moderate, or low—indicating how reliably those criteria can produce intersubjective agreement about beauty, and justify the placement in one sentence.
