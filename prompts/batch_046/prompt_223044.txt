I hold a specific version of the teleosemantic view of mental content: intentionality can be fully naturalized if we define representational function not by present causal role or by designer intention, but by a counterfactual-stability condition—specifically, that a mechanism’s content is fixed by the range of environmental variations across which selection history would have preserved its tokening pattern, even if the actual environment never realizes all those variations. On this view, misrepresentation is explained not by failure to correlate with present conditions, but by tokening in environments that fall outside the selected counterfactual range. I take this to avoid both the circularity worries that plague standard teleosemantics (since selection history can be specified in non-intentional terms) and the indeterminacy problems that arise from purely causal-role accounts.

What would count against this position? I want the evidence, arguments, and conceptual cases that should move me, sorted by how decisive they would be. Include: (1) whether any actual evolutionary or neurobiological findings undermine the idea that selection operates over counterfactual ranges rather than actual correlations; (2) whether there are formal or philosophical results showing that any selection-based account must smuggle in intentional notions to specify what is being selected for; (3) whether there are cases of mental content—say, abstract mathematical beliefs or entirely novel technological concepts—where the counterfactual-stability condition either assigns the wrong content or assigns none at all; and (4) whether defenders of rival naturalization programs (causal-informational, conceptual-role, or enactivist) have shown that their accounts handle these same cases without the metaphysical baggage of selection history. For each category, distinguish what a committed defender of the counterfactual-stability view would concede as genuinely damaging from what they would dismiss as misinterpreting the scope of the theory. Finally, assess honestly whether this version of teleosemantics has been qualified into unfalsifiability—if any counterexample can be absorbed by expanding the counterfactual range post hoc—and whether that insulation is a virtue of robustness or a vice that empties the view of empirical content.
