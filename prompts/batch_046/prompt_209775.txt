in split-brain cases the corpus callosum is cut, so stimuli in the left visual field reach only the right hemisphere and stimuli on the right reach only the left. in the standard experiment they present a chicken to the right visual field and snow to the left. the patient points with the right hand to a chicken foot and with the left hand to a shovel. when asked why they picked the shovel, the left hemisphere confabulates a story about cleaning up chicken mess, showing it lacks access to the right hemisphere's information.
