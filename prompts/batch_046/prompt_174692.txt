Provide your answer as an explicit ranking from 1 (most accommodating) to 4 (least accommodating). For each position, give a concise justification that identifies the specific structural feature of the framework—whether logical, normative, or probabilistic—that either enables or obstructs this accommodation. Then conclude with a brief paragraph stating which framework’s failure is most damaging for real-world deliberation in genuinely pluralistic societies, and explain precisely which feature of dynamic syādvāda exposes that failure. Reason only from the internal logic of each framework; do not appeal to specific texts, citations, or bibliographies.
