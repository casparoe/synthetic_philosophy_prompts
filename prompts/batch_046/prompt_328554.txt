Play Kurt Gödel in the months after you presented your rotating-universe solution to Einstein’s gravitational field equations. You hold three commitments you treat as inseparable: a rigorous mathematical Platonism—abstract structures exist independently of physical reality and are grasped by an intuitive faculty analogous to perception; the conviction that objective time is unreal, supported by your cosmological model, which admits closed timelike curves and thereby eliminates any globally consistent distinction between past and future; and the belief that mechanical computation, however complex, can never replicate the direct intuitive access to mathematical truth that human reason achieves. You may draw only on the conceptual resources available to you in 1949, together with one hypothetical modification I now introduce: suppose the axis of cosmic rotation does not remain fixed but precesses relative to the large-scale distribution of matter, so that the family of closed timelike curves becomes dense in spacetime—meaning that between any two events, however close, there exists a loop that returns to its own temporal origin.

Here is my opening challenge. If these loops are everywhere, then the failure of global temporal ordering is total, and every physical event belongs to infinitely many cycles. Does this intensification strengthen your argument that temporal passage is illusory, or does it undermine your Platonism by suggesting that the “timeless” realm of mathematical objects is merely another coordinate-dependent feature—just the limiting case of these loops collapsed to a point? And if our embodied minds are situated inside this densely looped geometry, by what path does intuition reach a realm that must now lie either outside all physical location or coextensive with every loop? Stay strictly in character: respond in the first person as Gödel, with formal precision, and do not invoke quantum mechanics, information theory, or any philosophy of physics developed after 1955. If you require clarification about the hypothetical precession, ask it skeptically and with explicit suspicion of ungrounded empirical speculation. Do not break character to translate your reasoning into modern terms.
