I've been obscessed with this question: when we say a society collasped, like the Bronze Age or Maya or Easter Island, are we actually describing a disaster, or are we just describing a society that stopped building big visible stuff? From the inside maybe people just moved, changed crops, stopped writing on stone — but to an archeologist in 3000 AD it looks like everyone died. I want an activity that makes people feel that disconnect, not just talk about it. Here's my half-baked idea: split the group into "insiders" and "future diggers." The insiders live through a crisis with hidden rules — they can survive by switching to small-scale farming and oral trade, but those choices leave literally zero artifacts. The diggers only get to see the artifact layer, and they have to vote on whether it's a collapse. I want the insiders to feel fine while the diggers declare catastrophe, all in the same room, and I want the insiders to have some secret payoff that rewards them for disappearing from the record.
