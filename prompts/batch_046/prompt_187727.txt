For a philosophy-of-mind reading group I am preparing — this has become personally urgent since my partner suffered a neurological injury that left her minimally responsive yet, I am convinced, fully aware — I would like a consistency audit before I present my position. Which pairs of these commitments are genuinely in tension, which conflicts are only apparent, and what is the least costly revision in each case? Please rank the tensions by severity, specify exactly what would have to be abandoned for each fix, and indicate whether there is a single adjustment that resolves the worst tensions together.
