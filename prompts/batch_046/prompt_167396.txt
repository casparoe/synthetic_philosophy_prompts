You should act as an expert in moral philosophy and normative ethics, think carefully, and begin by briefly brainstorming the conceptual terrain these two positions share and where they genuinely diverge, especially with respect to promises made under conditions of epistemic partition—situations in which promisor and promisee lack a shared conceptual scheme for what counts as fulfillment yet still treat the commitment as generating real obligations. Then draft the document they could both sign.
