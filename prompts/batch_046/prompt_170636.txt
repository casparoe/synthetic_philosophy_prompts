She argues that Lewis's standard conditions—common knowledge of a behavioral regularity, preference for conformity conditional on others, and the existence of a viable alternative—fail to stabilize norms in large-scale, anonymous digital environments where agents never face-to-face coordinate. To fix this, she proposes a "mediated visibility" condition: agents need common knowledge not of one another's actions directly, but of an algorithmic aggregator that renders the regularity observable and enforceable. Draft eight to ten questions that do not ask her to restate Lewis. Keep the questions concrete and avoid generic biographical openings.
