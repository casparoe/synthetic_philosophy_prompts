Rather than a conventional summary, I want a systematic translation.

Please restate Popper's core anti-historicist position—that there are no unconditional laws governing the long-range development of human societies, that observable trends are not equivalent to predictive laws, that the growth of scientific knowledge introduces an irreducible element of novelty making long-range prophecy impossible, and that this epistemological claim carries normative consequences for what he calls the open society—using exclusively the vocabulary of evolutionary dynamics and landscape modeling. Map historicist doctrines onto claims about orthogenetic or teleological progression; map Popper's account of knowledge growth onto mechanisms that restructure the adaptive landscape itself rather than merely selecting within it; and map his defense of openness onto conditions that preserve evolutionary plasticity and prevent premature convergence on a local optimum.
