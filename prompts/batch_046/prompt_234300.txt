Rank them, and defend the ordering with explicit criteria—tractability for a non-professional philosopher with a scientific background, importance for how working scientists understand explanation across scales, and dependence, meaning which questions other work implicitly hangs on. I am not looking for a bibliography, reading list, or citations; I want to know where my limited writing time and attention will matter most, and what would change the ranking if the next few years of work in philosophy of science turned out differently.
