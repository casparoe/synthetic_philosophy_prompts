do these hang together? (1) and (2) seem to pull opposite directions on whether republican liberty collapses into negative. (3) treats it as risk-based negative liberty, (4) rejects capacity-talk, but (5) needs it. what's the least-cost fix—ditch (5) and accept that domination is purely relational? or keep (5) and admit republican freedom needs some minimal positive conditions? and does (2) actually work if i stick with (1)?
