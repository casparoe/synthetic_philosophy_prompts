- implication: bounded rationality is distributed across people and artifacts, not just inside skulls. this pushes back against the individualist framing you see in behavioural economics.
- possible counter: isn't this just outsourcing computation? my reply is that it's different because the agent often cant override the structure easily, so the rationality is genuinely external, not delegated in a voluntary way.
