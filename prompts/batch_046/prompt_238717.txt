But if one takes seriously a modified version applied not to globally rotating spacetimes but to locally rotating reference frames within an otherwise non-rotating cosmos (connecting frame-dragging phenomena, rarely discussed in this metaphysical context, back to Gödel's conclusion), a different cluster of questions arises. Provide a defended agenda, not a survey: rank these by dependency (which answers are upstream of which), tractability with current philosophical and cosmological methods, and downstream significance for the broader debate about time's reality. State your criteria explicitly — importance, tractability, neglectedness, or how many other questions hang on each — and identify which lines you would deprioritise because resolving them would change little elsewhere.
