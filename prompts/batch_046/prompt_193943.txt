I am a retired experimental physicist who has only recently turned to philosophy of mathematics. I propose that your paradox is resolved not by denying the mathematics, but by recognizing that cardinality is a frame-dependent property, analogous to velocity or simultaneity in general relativity: one logical “coordinate chart” (model) registers a collection as countable, another as uncountable, yet the underlying structure is invariant and real. Pushing the analogy further, just as a quantum measurement apparatus partly determines which particles are registered—there being no apparatus-independent particle count—I suggest that a formal model partly determines cardinality, with no model-independent “true” number of sets. Thus uncountability is relational, like motion, and your paradox dissolves into a demonstration of gauge-dependence rather than a contradiction.
