I'm a novelist, and I'm stuck on a philosophical knot that's crept into my manuscript. But the debate feels frozen—everyone recycles the same examples, and nobody actually changes their mind. Is this a substantive clash about well-being, or is it verbal, or are the two sides answering different questions—maybe one about justification, one about phenomenology? Or is there a hidden empirical claim about consciousness or desire doing the work? I want a real diagnosis of the stall, using what each camp actually commits to, plus an honest answer about whether anything—some distinction, experiment, or reframing—could actually break it.
