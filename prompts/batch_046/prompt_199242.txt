Please draft the panel’s memo recommending approval, rejection, or approval with conditions. The memo must open with a one-paragraph bottom-line recommendation that could be put directly to a vote. Finally, propose a concrete, time-bound procedural course of action (for example, phased conditional approval with explicit milestones) that gives the researchers clear criteria they could actually respond to. Write in a formal institutional voice, and keep the entire memo under 550 words.
