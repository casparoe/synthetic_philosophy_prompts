Finally, the account asserts that she concluded Catholic just-war doctrine can no longer be maintained without embracing pacifism, and that she personally endorsed pacifism as the only coherent Catholic stance in the nuclear age. Is this a fair characterization? Please separate what captures her actual position, what is a reasonable simplification, and what is a distortion or error—particularly regarding whether she held that double effect is obsolete, whether she treated the failure as a legal crime as well as a moral one, and whether she was arguing for pacifism or for stricter adherence to traditional just-war criteria.
