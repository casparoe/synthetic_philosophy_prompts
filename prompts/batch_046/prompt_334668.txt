Write the list of desiderata that an adequate philosophical theory of self-locating belief must satisfy in order to resolve the Sleeping Beauty problem and its direct variants, intended for a graduate seminar in formal epistemology. Write your answer in American English, in a formal and polished style.

For each desideratum, state the specific phenomenon, case distinction, or consistency constraint it addresses, explain the conceptual consideration that motivates it, and mark whether it is non-negotiable or negotiable, noting which camp (if any) tends to relax it. Keep the weight on the criteria and their underlying reasoning rather than on a scorecard of named theories.

The list must include at least the following:

1. How the theory handles the canonical Sleeping Beauty scenario, specifically whether being awake on Monday versus Tuesday provides evidence about the coin toss, and whether the update rule applies symmetrically to psychologically identical awakenings.

2. Whether the theory preserves standard Bayesian conditionalization for all non-indexical beliefs, or whether self-locating updates require a revision to the conditioning rule itself.

3. Whether the theory respects the Principal Principle linking objective chances to rational credence, and if so, how it reconciles that principle with the credences assigned upon awakening.

4. How the theory distinguishes centered propositions (de se) from uncentered propositions (de dicto), and whether that distinction alone explains why awakening should or should not shift credence in the coin result.

5. How the theory handles a "double-blind" variant: Beauty is privately told upon awakening whether it is Monday or Tuesday, but an amnesia drug immediately erases that information, leaving her aware only that she received a message she can no longer recall. The theory should specify whether her credence in Heads should match the canonical case, diverge from it, or remain indeterminate, and what that result reveals about whether self-location depends on available memory or on objective centered-world position.

6. Whether any subset of the above requirements is jointly unsatisfiable given standard Bayesian constraints on rational belief, and if there is a genuine conflict, identify which desideratum should be relaxed and what cost that relaxation carries.

Conclude with a brief assessment of how the halfing and thirder positions fare against only the non-negotiable items, without cataloging every variant in the literature. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations, and do not quote verbatim from specific texts.
