From a science-and-ideas perspective, please construct a thought experiment designed to isolate whether multiple realizability extends to phenomenal consciousness or halts at functional organization. The case should feature an artificial system—the Resonant Array—whose cognitive dynamics fully realize the functional architecture described by global workspace theory, including information broadcasting, access consciousness, and reportability, yet whose physical substrate comprises sustained acoustic vortices in a magnetized plasma rather than silicon logic gates or carbon-based neurons. Constraints: the physics must remain within known principles with no exotic matter or omniscient observers, the scenario should fit in one paragraph, and the argument must not invoke the Chinese Room, Block's Chinese Nation, or standard syntax-semantics objections. Once the scenario is set out, demonstrate that a functionalist and a biological naturalist concur on every behavioral and organizational fact about the Array but reach opposite verdicts on whether it possesses phenomenal consciousness. Then specify exactly which substrate feature—the non-computational, non-neural acoustic-plasma realization—generates the divergence, show how the disagreement collapses or transforms if the same functional architecture is implemented in conventional silicon, and explain how the functionalist can defend attributing consciousness to the Array without merely citing generic multiple realizability.
