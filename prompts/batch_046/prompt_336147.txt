Maya countered that the obligation is not to memorize rules but to attend to the local coordination structure; the failure is negligence in observing, not ignorance of the specific form. Leo answered that “attending” sets an impossibly high bar for visitors, and that her view risks treating all convention-violations—jaywalking in an empty street, tipping in a non-tipping culture—as moral failures, leaving no conceptual space for harmless cultural mismatch.
