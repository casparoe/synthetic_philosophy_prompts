She argues that a relevance-sensitive paraconsistent logic should be adopted in adjudication not merely to tolerate these inconsistencies but to reason with them actively, and she rejects the standard hierarchical resolution via norms like lex superior. Please think carefully and systematically before drafting. First, briefly outline the three main pressure points in Dr. K’s position that an informed interviewer should probe. For each of these four, include a sharp follow-up keyed to the answer she is most likely to give—for instance, if she appeals to legislative intent, ask whether that appeal is itself contradiction-tolerant; if she marks a domain boundary, ask what principled feature of institutional rules fixes it.
