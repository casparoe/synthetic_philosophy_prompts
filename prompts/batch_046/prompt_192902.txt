I think the expectation is still infinite, but I'm struggling to see what that should mean for whether to play. I want them to talk past each other as little as possible: have Alex ask whether Jordan is smuggling a philosophical theory of personal identity into a decision-theory problem, and have Jordan reply that the real issue is whether the beneficiary of the payoff has to be the same subject who paid the cost. Keep both voices respectful and equally competent, with no straw figures.
