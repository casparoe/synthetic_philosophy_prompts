You sit on a university accreditation panel that audits proposed "global ethics" courses for parochial bias. The panel works with the principle that a course may claim universal moral competence only if its core normative judgments survive a triangulation audit: convergence must be shown across WEIRD populations and between at least two structurally distinct non-WEIRD moral ecologies—specifically, a small-scale communal society with strong loyalty-purity integration and an urban hierarchical-relational context—while also accounting for the reflective-equilibrium gap, defined here as the divergence between spontaneous folk judgments and endorsed principles after structured deliberation.

Turn this principle into a reusable audit procedure that your panel can apply to any proposal within ninety minutes. Present it as an ordered sequence of checks, specifying for each: the question to ask, what counts as a sufficient answer, where a negative verdict stops the audit with failure, where the issue is irreducibly one of values and must be handed to a human deliberative vote rather than resolved by procedure, and which steps involve unavoidable judgment calls. The procedure must explicitly address hard cases: courses that universalize harm and fairness but treat loyalty, authority, and purity as culturally variable; courses that invoke reflective equilibrium using only WEIRD samples; and courses that conflate distinct moral ontologies—e.g., reducing relational obligations to contract violations—thereby manufacturing false convergence. Apply the procedure to three course proposals: (A) one that treats individual autonomy as the supreme cross-cultural value; (B) one that adopts a five-domain taxonomy but assigns universal normative weight only to care and fairness, relegating loyalty, authority, and purity to local custom; and (C) one that asserts universal convergence on intentional-killing prohibitions but bases this solely on Western philosophical intuition surveys. Ensure one application yields an answer the panel will likely find unwelcome. Conclude by stating exactly what the procedure is powerless to settle, and identify the specific empirical finding or theoretical argument about folk-moral divergence that would cause you to abandon the triangulation audit altogether.

Please also state what would change your mind: which result in cross-cultural moral psychology would undermine the audit's validity, and where in your reasoning—especially across the three applications—is it most vulnerable to failure.
