Friend A said that's clearly just one 'I' distributed across two bodies, since Hofstadter's point is that if you copy the pattern perfectly the copy is you, and here the pattern is literally shared live. B said that's just ambiguity, not proof of identity, and accused A of clinging to a Cartesian theater.
