Give your credence that this inference is conceptually sound, decomposed as follows: first, the probability that heritability coefficients are population- and environment-specific variance partitions rather than invariant causal weights; second, the probability that genotype-environment reaction norms for this trait are roughly parallel, so that environmental changes lift or lower all genotypic values together; third, the probability that the magnitude of a population-mean shift depends mainly on the environmental variance share rather than on the slope or curvature of those reaction norms; and fourth, any other conceptual bridge you think necessary, with a number and one line of justification for each term, then the combined credence. State which term is your weakest link, and specify what kind of empirical pattern or conceptual clarification—describable without citing a particular study—would most revise your overall number.
