Camp B (the Relation side) thinks shame wins because it's irreducibly social — it pulls people back into connection rather than pushing them into private penance. They think cultures can choose shame, but only if they avoid toxic internalization and instead channel it toward collective reconnection.

For each crux, give a concrete scenario both sides agree is a fair test, and write down each camp's prediction about what happens.
