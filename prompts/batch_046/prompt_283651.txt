hey, i'm a novelist and i sit on the funding board for the Meridian Arts Foundation — we back writers-in-residence in politically messy places, and right now we've got a proposal for a three-month residency in a city-state whose goverment explicitly justifies its authoritarian rule through performance legitimacy: prosperity, order, and delivery instead of democratic input. the funding comes straight from their cultural ministry, and they want co-branding on every poster and publication.

half the board says approve with conditions — the applicant writer is apolitical, she's writing about labor migration, and pulling out just hurts her. the other half says reject outright, because partnering lends institutional credibility to the idea that prosperity can substitute for authorization, and our charter says we shouldn't do that.
