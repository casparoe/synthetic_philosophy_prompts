4. A classical Cartesian theatre model, positing a unified internal observer that directly inspects mental states;
5. A hierarchical predictive-processing account, in which self-related cognition is a set of high-level predictions about interoceptive and exteroceptive signals, with no dedicated self-referential symbol.

Present your ranking as a tier list (S-tier, A-tier, B-tier, C-tier, D-tier) with the numerical score shown in brackets. Please write in British English throughout.
