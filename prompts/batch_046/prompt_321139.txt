Write an essay in American English on whether the AI alignment problem is one problem or several conceptually distinct ones that happen to share a name. Distinguish carefully between the specification problem (precisely encoding an intended objective), the interpretation problem (the necessity that any formal specification be completed by the system's own hermeneutic reasoning, thereby delegating interpretive authority), and the robustness problem (maintaining intended behavior across distribution shift and capability gain). Weigh the unificationist argument—that all three stem from a single difficulty in reliably translating human values into optimizable form—against the pluralist argument, which holds that interpretation concerns epistemic authority we cannot fully outsource, while robustness is an engineering failure mode largely independent of value content. Consider whether treating these as separate problems changes what a solution must deliver: if unified, a single theoretical breakthrough might address them together; if plural, progress may require methods that are not only different but potentially incompatible. Defend a clear verdict; do not end on a non-committal note.
