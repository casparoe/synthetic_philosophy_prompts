I'd defend Walzer's institutional account against this blur. Walzer grounds moral equality in soldiers being instruments of state policy; they don't personally bear responsibility for whether the war is just. So even when Side A strikes civilian infrastructure selectively, only for just causes, Side B's combatants remain instruments with identical institutional protection.
