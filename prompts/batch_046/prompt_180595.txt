She also endorses a restricted principle of recombination—any structural property found in one world may be recombined with any other, provided the result remains a logically consistent structural description, though she denies that this applies to spatiotemporal parts as such. The audience will include metaphysicians and philosophers of language, so I want questions that turn on the actual machinery of her position rather than on biography or survey-level exposition.

Please draft eight to ten interview questions, each paired with a brief anticipation of her most likely reply and a follow-up designed to make that reply work harder. For each, the follow-up should be keyed to the answer she is most likely to give.
