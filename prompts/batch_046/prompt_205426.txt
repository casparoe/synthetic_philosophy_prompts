Here's the wrinkle I'm calling the transparency trap, though I made that up myself. But the error-correction mechanism is itself part of the interface, so the dashboard ends up inspecting it's own display code and seeing only more icons. That creates a recursive loop: either you sneak in a real observer behind the screen (which kills the illusion model), or you say there's no correction at all and it's all just noise (which kills introspective knowledge).
