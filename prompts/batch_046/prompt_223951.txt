Write a dialogue between Mara, who thinks the dirty hands problem in politics is actually about institutional corruption infecting the agent, and Theo, who thinks its just a dramatic label for ordinary moral trade-offs with no special political stain. Make sure they actually respond to each others points, neither should be a strawman. Then have them briefly wrestle with whether citizens who vote for such a leader share the stain, or if thats stretching the metaphor past breaking point. Keep it casual, like a late-night seminar argument with coffee cups involved.
