It needs to welcome both halves of the room without condescension: for the administrators, translating the framework into operational language about contingency planning and adaptive capacity; for my classmates, including one precise, non-obvious interpretive point—perhaps that Machiavelli treats virtù as a form of temporal responsiveness rather than static strength or domination—so that the conceptual work is visible. The tone should be formal, respectful, and genuinely welcoming, avoiding any request that the audience read texts they do not have. Could you write this address?
