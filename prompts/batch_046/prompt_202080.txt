Your argument should treat this not as a mere psychological limitation but as a normative feature of mature moral cognition, and it should answer two specific objections: first, that compression collapses the universalist aspiration of human rights; second, that it cannot distinguish genuine moral progress from tribal retrenchment. Keep the tone formal and accessible to an educated general reader; do not include a bibliography, reading list, or scholarly citations, since the argument should stand on its conceptual merits alone.
