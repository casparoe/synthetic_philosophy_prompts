Take the 1973 APA vote to drop homosexuality from the DSM and analyze it through Szasz's claim that mental illness is largely a myth used to medicalize social deviance. Don't just say whether he would've cheered — ask whether the vote looks like science self-correcting or like the theraputic state renegotiating its borders. If psychiatry gets its authority from defining normalcy, does removing one diagnosis actually strengthen its grip on everything still left in the manual? Also, does Szasz's framework miss how activists used psychiatric language strategically rather than rejecting it outright? And where is his theory silent on collective agency — can protest actually shrink psychiatric power, or does it only reshuffle who gets pathologized?
