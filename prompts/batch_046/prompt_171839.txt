Which of these commitments are genuinely in tension, which are only apparently incompatible, and what is the least costly revision? Please rank the tensions by severity rather than treating them as equally problematic, and specify what I would have to give up—calibrated perspectivism, prioritarianism, or the equal-weighting charitable rule—and what each sacrifice would cost me in terms of my broader philosophical stance.
