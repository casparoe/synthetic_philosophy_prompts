For a seminar on bounded rationality and formal epistemology, specify the complete list of desiderata that an adequate resolution to the problem of logical omniscience must satisfy when applied to resource-bounded agents maintaining distinct explicit and implicit belief stores. For each requirement, provide the conceptual motivation—such as preserving consistency with the preface paradox, accommodating belief revision without triggering the frame problem, and retaining positive and negative introspection without generating full closure—indicate whether it is non-negotiable or reflects a particular camp's preference (for instance, syntactic versus semantic approaches), and note any pair or subset that standard no-go results suggest is jointly unsatisfiable. Then, briefly, assess how an awareness-based syntactic account, a neighborhood-model semantic account, and a graded probabilistic account meet or violate these criteria. Weight the analysis on the specification of the requirements themselves rather than on the comparative scoring.
