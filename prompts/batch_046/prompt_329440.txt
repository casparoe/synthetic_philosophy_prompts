For my philosophy elective, I am designing a study-tool taxonomy on the special composition question. I want a decision-tree flowchart that sorts someone into a metaphysical position based solely on how they judge four concrete cases, without requiring them to know the theory names in advance. The four branch nodes should ask: (1) Do two people shaking hands form one composite social object? (2) Does a statue survive being melted and recast into an entirely different shape? (3) Do two particles that never occupy nearby space but are permanently coupled by a conservation law count as composing a single system? (4) Does an institution like a corporation exist as an object over and above its individual members and buildings? Lay out the logical space of yes/no combinations first; then map onto these leaves: Universalism, Nihilism, Restricted Composition (spatiotemporal contact only), Restricted Composition (functional or causal integration), and one unoccupied combination that should be flagged and briefly diagnosed. Note any pair of named positions that these four cases cannot genuinely separate, and suggest one additional case that would split them. Keep the instrument self-contained: no citations, no bibliography, no appeals to named philosophers beyond the position labels themselves.
