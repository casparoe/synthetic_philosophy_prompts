For my philosophy of knowledge coursework I am examining Nozick's tracking conditions and his rejection of epistemic closure. Please design a realistic, non-futuristic thought experiment set in an ordinary academic context—without demons or perfect predictors—in which a student knows that her submitted research proposal was approved (p), correctly infers that she therefore qualified for the advanced seminar (q), yet her belief in q fails Nozick's sensitivity and adherence conditions precisely because it was formed through logical entailment rather than direct tracking. After presenting the scenario, demonstrate why p tracks the truth, show exactly where tracking breaks for q, identify the structural feature that severs tracking across entailment, explain what happens if that feature is removed, and present the strongest reply available to a defender of closure—particularly whether insisting on direct verification of q restores tracking without undermining the inference itself.
