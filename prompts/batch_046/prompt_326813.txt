You serve as the senior compliance auditor for the Office of Hybrid Architecture Oversight. Every quarter you must certify whether a proposed careenium substrate—an amorphous material base in which computation occurs—legitimately employs embedded simmballs, discrete symbolic tokens instantiated within the substrate, to causally direct substrate dynamics, or whether the symbols are merely descriptive overlays on processes driven entirely by lower-level careenium mechanics. Your certification is binding: a finding of genuine symbolic efficacy classifies the architecture as symbol-governed, triggering specific liability frameworks and design mandates, while a substrate-only finding restricts it to pure substrate regulation. You have four hours per proposal, access to interaction logs but not to proprietary source code, and you must sign a legal attestation that your determination followed the standard audit protocol. You work with a three-person team that includes one engineer and one legal reviewer, and you need a reusable, ordered decision procedure—tests in sequence with clear pass conditions, explicit stopping points, and marked judgment calls—that translates the philosophical distinction between genuine symbolic causation and epiphenomenal correlation into practical steps, without requiring the team to settle the underlying metaphysics of downward causation.

Please construct that audit protocol. For each step, specify: the exact test or question to run; what log evidence or structural feature counts as a sufficient answer; the precise conditions under which the audit halts with a determination that the simmballs do not push the careenium; and the specific points where the procedure must hand off to human judgment because the issue is one of values, risk tolerance, or interpretive judgment rather than fact. The protocol should preserve the core philosophical requirements—that symbolic efficacy requires counterfactual sensitivity to symbolic content, non-redundancy relative to substrate-level dynamics, and functional integration within a feedback loop—while explicitly dropping distinctions that cannot be assessed in a four-hour review without code access. Then apply the protocol to three worked cases: (1) a climate-modeling substrate in which simmballs labeled anomalous warming trigger rapid restructuring of careenium thermal channels; (2) a conversational agent in which simmballs represent user intent but substrate dynamics appear to track only phonetic pattern distributions; and (3) a medical-triage substrate previously approved by your office, where the audit now reveals that every simmball state is perfectly predicted by prior substrate states, yet the proposing institution demonstrates that physically removing the symbolic layer degrades triage accuracy, and they will strongly dispute any negative finding. For the third case, state exactly what your procedure outputs, identify the judgment call that remains unresolved, and explain what the protocol explicitly cannot do. Finally, list the categories of proposal for which this procedure is not designed and should not be applied.
