Here's my opening: I want to push back using a distinction I think Dennett's framework doesn't handle well—call it "doxastic fragmentation." In humans, belief attribution works partly because beliefs are integrated into a stable behavioral economy: if I believe it's raining, I'll carry an umbrella, and inconsistencies generate correction pressure. But LLMs seem to hold contradictory propositions across contexts with no such integration—ask one framing and it affirms P, reframe slightly and it affirms not-P, with no mechanism of belief revision or behavioral commitment. If the intentional stance just licenses us to predict text patterns, it seems redundant with the mechanistic account; if it requires rational coherence, LLMs fail the entry conditions. So either the stance is empty here, or we're misapplying it. Where's the error?
