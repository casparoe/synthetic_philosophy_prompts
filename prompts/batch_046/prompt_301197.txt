I keep noticing a pattern I don't think has a standard name, so I'm calling it pump stacking: you nest two or three thought experiments that each look harmless on their own, but together they smuggle in a premise that none of them states outright, and by the end the audience feels like they've been led somewhere inevitable. My students asked me which open lines of work in this area actually deserve years of effort, and I realized I don't have a clear answer, so I'm asking you.

Third, reverse pumps — thought experiments designed specifically to break an intuition rather than build one, and whether they fail in different ways from standard ones.
