Write an essay assessing whether video-game characters can be proper objects of moral concern. In your analysis, distinguish sharply between authored protagonists—whose traits and arcs are fixed by designers—and procedurally generated non-player characters equipped with persistent in-game memory architectures but lacking any predetermined narrative function. Weigh the argument that moral status depends on intentional authorship and narrative design against the view that sustained interactive presence and memory-tracking alone generate relational obligations. Reach and defend a clear conclusion; do not end on a non-committal note.
