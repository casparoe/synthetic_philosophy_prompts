I am unsure whether this framework genuinely rescues panpsychist ethics by providing a graded scale of moral consideration, or whether it merely relocates the problem to technological and cosmological domains while introducing an apparently ad hoc metric.

Reach and justify a clear conclusion—either that the asymmetry successfully preserves panpsychism without ethical collapse, or that it fails and forces us to reject either the ethical extension or the panpsychist premise itself.
