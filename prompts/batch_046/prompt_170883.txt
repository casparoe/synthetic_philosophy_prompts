Write a dialogue between two philosophers—call them R and S—who are arguing about sphexishness and whether mechanical rigidty is what kills free will. R says being sphexish, stuck in a loop like that wasp, means you arent really acting, youre just running a program. S thinks flexibility and contingency dont automatically save you; adding a random break or a noisy branch just makes the mechanism sloppier without giving it real agency.

Make it actual back-and-forth, not parallel monologues. R should press S on whether a totally unpredictable actor is therefore free, and S should press R on whether a deliberately inserted contingency is any different from just another gear in the machine. Neither should be a strawman.

They should disagree about whether that glitch-gap is where freedom hides, or whether its just determinism with worse weather and extra steps.
