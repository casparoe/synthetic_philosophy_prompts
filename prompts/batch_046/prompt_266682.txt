Next thursday I'm giving a workshop talk defending what I'm calling a scafolded-character view of virtue ethics at the Character and Context workshop — audience is mostly social psych people who think virtue ethics was killed by the situationist critique, plus two virtue ethicists (one hardline Aristotelian who still treats phronesis like a mental module, one Humean who thinks character is just stable sentiment patterns) and a philosophy-of-science person obsessed with replication failures in priming research.
