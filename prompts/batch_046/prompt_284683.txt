Please phrase every branching question so that someone could answer it without already knowing which position it corresponds to. In your accompanying explanation, please separate what is settled from what is genuinely contested among people who know the topic, and do not present a contested view—such as the inevitability of value lock-in, the literal applicability of agency concepts to superorganisms, or the claim that coordination without rivalry removes all moral hazard—as if it were the consensus.
