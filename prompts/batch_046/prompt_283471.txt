From these he derives that there exists, in at least one possible world, a being that instantiates every positive property. He then adds an axiom identifying necessary existence as itself a positive property, which allows him to conclude that such a being exists in every possible world, including the actual one. Finally, the summary asserts that the only serious challenge to the proof is the rejection of S5 modal logic, which it treats as an uncontroversial baseline for any metaphysical discussion of possibility.
