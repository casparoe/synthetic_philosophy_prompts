Sarah replied that Marcus was misusing Williams: the objection targets the demand that every attachment be justified by an impartial moral system, not the mere recognition that strangers matter. He insisted there's a principled difference between acting from one's projects and using those projects as an excuse to ignore foreseeable harm. Sarah responded that this creates a false dichotomy: Williams never claimed ground projects licence us to cause harm; he claimed only that we shouldn't have to justify our deepest commitments by reference to the greatest good.
