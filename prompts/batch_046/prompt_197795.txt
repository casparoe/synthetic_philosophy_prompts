(4) So hedonism must either extend intrinsic value to unconscious algorithms and addictive loops, or reintroduce a phenomenal condition; but the latter returns us to the heterogeneity problem, since phenomenal feel is not uniform across pleasures. (5) Therefore hedonism either proves too much—making every optimized feedback loop intrinsically good—or collapses into a theory of phenomenal consciousness rather than pleasure specifically.
