I am preparing an exam for an upper-level seminar on modern nationalism and I want one essay question that forces students to work with Hobsbawm's concept of invented tradition and Anderson's concept of imagined communities without letting them simply summarize the two authors. To keep the task away from rote recitation, inject the following hypothetical case directly into the prompt: a decentralized digital cooperative founded in 2021 that claims direct descent from a medieval craft guild, invents annual blockchain-verified initiation rituals, and cultivates anonymous global solidarity among members who will never meet in person. The question should ask students to determine whether this cooperative is best understood through Hobsbawm's top-down elite instrumentalism or through Anderson's horizontal peer-to-peer imagination, and it must require them to show that applying both frameworks simultaneously produces a structural contradiction rather than a mere empirical ambiguity—specifically that Hobsbawm's model presupposes visible social control exercised by an identifiable elite, whereas Anderson's model presupposes the absence of direct interaction among the peers who share the mental image. A top answer should resolve this tension not by adding more descriptive detail about the cooperative but by proposing a conceptual modification to one of the two frameworks, for example by weakening Hobsbawm's elite-control condition or by restricting Anderson's anonymity condition, and it should defend that modification with explicit reasoning. Please write the exam question itself, then a model answer of roughly five hundred words written entirely in continuous prose, and then a marking scheme also in continuous prose that distinguishes an A answer from a B answer by naming the specific moves each must contain: recognition that the contradiction is structural and not accidental, the insistence on a conceptual rather than empirical resolution, and the correct identification of which theoretical condition must be revised. The marking scheme should also note the characteristic B-level failure of describing both theories accurately while treating the tension as something that could be solved by more information about the case. Present the question, model answer, and marking scheme all together in continuous prose without headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables, as if composing a single letter, and do not include any request for bibliographies, citations, reading lists, or specific page references.
