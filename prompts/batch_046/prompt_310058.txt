Camp Two, the "Non-Relational Act Theorists," holds that any appeal to parts, wholes, or internal relations still fails to explain how a judgment combines subject and predicate into one truth-evaluable claim, and that genuine unity requires a single, non-relational mental act or substrate (in a modernized Bradleyan spirit) within which multiplicity only appears.
