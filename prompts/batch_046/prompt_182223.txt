Most philosophical treatments treat the two gametes as symmetrically essential, yet the biology is strikingly asymmetric: the egg supplies the cytoplasm, mitochondria, and epigenetic programming that shape early development, while the sperm contributes chiefly nuclear DNA together with a single proximal centriole required to organize the first mitotic spindle. This asymmetry makes me wonder whether the classic intuition survives closer scrutiny.
