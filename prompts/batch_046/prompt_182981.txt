Specify what a successful deliverable looks like (e.g., a criterion for substrate-level causal dependence tailored to symbol-carrying nodes), what would count as failure (e.g., the model collapses into pure syntax or into ungrounded emergence with no predictive difference), and give an honest assessment of whether the project risks being too large for a single semester—particularly regarding the formal apparatus needed. Do not include a bibliography or citation list; refer to positions and arguments by name only, and note that references will be added separately.
