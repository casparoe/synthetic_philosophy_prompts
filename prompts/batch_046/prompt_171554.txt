second case: a coworker looks at the exact same logs and tells a different story - "the system is avoiding the region with the worst latency history" - and her predictions are also correct. so now i have two incompatible "goal" explanations, neither is in the source code or the architecture docs, and both work better than the physical or design explanations for predicting this specific failure mode.
