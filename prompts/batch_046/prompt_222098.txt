Question: In the standard Sleeping Beauty problem, Beauty is put to sleep on Sunday and awakened on Monday; if the coin came up heads she is awakened again on Tuesday, with complete amnesia between awakenings. When asked upon awakening for her credence that the coin is heads, halfers answer 1/2 and thirders answer 1/3. Now consider a modified "reporter variant": Beauty retains full memory and knows the calendar date, but an independent device will randomly select exactly one of her awakenings—Monday or Tuesday—to be the "reported" one, with Monday twice as likely to be selected as Tuesday, and she is asked for her credence only during that reported awakening. The coin toss and the selection mechanism are stochastically independent. Should Beauty's credence in heads, upon being asked during the reported awakening, differ from her answer in the standard problem? Defend your answer, with explicit attention to whether any form of self-locating uncertainty remains and to how the selection weights should or should not enter her reasoning.

Student answer: In the standard problem, Beauty lacks any information about whether she is in the Monday or Tuesday awakening, so she faces self-locating uncertainty across three centered possibilities—Heads-Monday, Heads-Tuesday, and Tails-Monday. Applying indifference over these epistemically indistinguishable states yields 1/3 for heads. In the reporter variant, because she remembers prior awakenings and knows the date, there is no ambiguity about her temporal location, so the centered-worlds framework does not apply at all. She should therefore revert to the prior of 1/2.

That said, the selection mechanism introduces a different kind of self-locating belief: she might be uncertain whether her present awakening is the reported one or the ignored one. Because Monday is twice as likely to be selected, finding herself on Monday gives her reason to believe she is in the reported awakening. But since the device is independent of the coin, this only affects her belief about being heard, not about heads. Consequently, her credence in heads should remain 1/2 regardless of which day she reports from. Even on Tuesday, she should not update, because the 2:1 weight is purely a feature of the selection procedure and carries no evidence about the coin toss.
