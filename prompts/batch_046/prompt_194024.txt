Finally, state clearly whether the conclusion that biological function requires a selection-history pedigree survives the audit, or whether the engineer analogy was doing the argumentative work all along.

Keep the analysis accessible but precise, flag the cases where the analogy genuinely cannot be repaired, and avoid treating either philosophical position as obviously correct.
