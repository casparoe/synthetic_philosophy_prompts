The Experience Pluralists hold that the ranking mainly captures culturally-conditioned retrospective satisfaction, that treating it as a target risks crowding out moment-to-moment wellbeing and autonomy, and that high ladder scores can coexist with flat or negative affective trends that policy should not ignore.
