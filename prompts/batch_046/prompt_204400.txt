Design a structured in-class simulation—no lecture up front—where students physically reconstruct a first-person autobiographical narrative from fragmented "memory-cards" distributed among them, with the hard rule that no student may hold more than two "core" cards. Specify the roles or rules, the minimal materials needed, a timeline for the 90 minutes, and a payoff or stakes scheme that doesn't use real money. Do not ask for bibliographies, reading lists, or citations.
