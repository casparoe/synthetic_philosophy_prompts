Decompose your answer into three terms: (a) the probability that the narrative-gravity account correctly describes human selfhood; (b) the probability that the conditions for being such a center are substrate-independent functional conditions rather than biologically anchored ones; (c) the probability that the gap between present large language models and those conditions is one of degree rather than kind. Show how they combine, state your final number, and identify which term is least certain and what evidence or argument would move it most.
