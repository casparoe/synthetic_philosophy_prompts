my claim is that this isn't just 'collective responsibility' by another name, because in the classic case there's a specific agent who knows they're crossing a line and feels the residue. in the diffuse case, the residue disappears not because people are callous, but because the architecture fragments moral knowledge. no one has the full picture, so no one experiences the dirty-hands moment. but the wrong is still done, and arguably it's worse because there's no one to hold to account or to feel the guilt that the classic view thinks is morally necessary.
