Defend the view that the heterogeneity problem for hedonism is best addressed by dissolving the search for a uniform phenomenal quality and instead treating pleasure as a family-resemblance concept unified by a structural feature: non-instrumental evaluative capture, defined as a sustained attentional orientation toward an object for its own sake that generates self-reinforcing positive feedback without being reducible to belief-desire pairs or raw sensory feels. In constructing your argument, specifically account for counter-hedonic pleasures—schadenfreude, the comfort derived from false belief, and the gratification of self-destructive consumption—showing why they qualify as genuine pleasures despite lacking well-being-promoting content. Explain how this structural account distinguishes hedonism from desire-satisfaction theories and preference utilitarianism without reintroducing a hidden homogeneous sensation. Finally, identify one specific explanatory advantage your view has over the classical phenomenological-uniformity thesis that its defenders could not accommodate without contradiction.
