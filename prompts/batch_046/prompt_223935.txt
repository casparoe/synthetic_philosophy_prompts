I am a software engineer with no formal background in philosophy. I recently read about sphexishness—the mechanical rigidity observed in the wasp Sphex ichneumoneus, which performs its burrow-checking ritual repeatedly even when its prey has been moved—framed as evidence that deterministic, algorithmic processes are fundamentally incapable of the adaptive flexibility required for free will. Given that both human cognition and modern neural networks are deterministic computations at the physical level, does this not imply that any deterministic system, including adaptive AI, is necessarily sphexish and therefore locked into rigid behavioral loops without genuine flexibility? What design principle could allow a deterministic architecture to escape this constraint?
