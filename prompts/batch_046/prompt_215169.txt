Every quantum foundations workshop I've been to lately hits the same wall on psi-ontic vs psi-epistemic. Side A keeps bringing up PBR and preparation independence; side B says QBism dissolves the problem by treating |ψ⟩ as a users credence. Then this weird modal-availability idea came up—treating the state as indexing which properties are accessible across branches rather than describing them—but both sides just went back to arguing about reification. I'm stuck on wether the stall is really about hidden ontology or about what counts as an explanation: is it a verbal disagreement about 'real', a hidden formal question about whether modal logic belongs in the formalism, or just bedrock intuitions about whether physics should talk about beliefs? And if it's one of those, what would actually count as progress—a specific theorem, a change in what cases we treat as data, or admitting the question is ill-posed?
