If middle knowledge grants God access to all counterfactuals of creaturely freedom prior to the creative decree, and God then actualizes the feasible world with the optimal counterfactual profile, does Molinism not collapse into a distributed form of determinism—one in which determination is relocated from causal chains within the actual world to the complete menu of counterfactual choices across worlds? More specifically, since every free decision is already fixed in the counterfactuals God consults, libertarian freedom appears to reduce to a kind of modal indexing: an agent is “free” only insofar as her choice differs across worlds, yet within any actualizable world the entire profile is predetermined. How does this differ from standard compatibilism, other than achieving compatibility by scattering determinative factors across the modal space rather than concentrating them in the actual causal history?
