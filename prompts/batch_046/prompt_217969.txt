Rank these five proposed mechanisms—(1) a mandatory ninety-day cooling-off period after petition certification, (2) a randomly selected citizen jury with veto power over technically complex propositions, (3) a tiered threshold requiring sixty percent for constitutional amendments but a simple majority for statutes, (4) a “reverse referendum” allowing repeal of statutes enacted within the prior year, and (5) mandatory multilingual ballots with culturally adaptive framing—by how well each balances direct popular sovereignty against transient majoritarian risk. Give exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion; assign each a rank from 1 (strongest) to 5 and state the core reason briefly.
