She claims that the Platonia dilemma is misframed because it treats symmetric reasoning about rationality as requiring symmetric access to the reasoning procedures of other agents, and she extends the logic of the Luring Lottery to institutional settings where commitment devices are privately configured. I need eight to ten interview questions that do not ask her to rehearse the standard literature. At least two should target her institutional extension—specifically whether a centralized authority can serve as a luring mechanism without collapsing into coercion, and what happens when agents possess asymmetric information about the authority's own commitment structure. I also want one closing question asking what formal or empirical finding would lead her to reject the accessibility condition entirely. The questions should be rigorous yet accessible to an audience familiar with basic decision theory.
