I'm auditing a graduate seminar on cognitive taxonomy led by Professor Miriam Okonkwo, in which she applies the four-part distinction—Darwinian creatures (behavior largely hard-wired by natural selection with little individual plasticity), Skinnerian creatures (learning through reinforcement, conditioning, and trial-and-error without decoupled internal modeling), Popperian creatures (possessing generative internal models that permit counterfactual simulation prior to action), and Gregorian creatures (leveraging externally inherited symbolic tools, language, and cultural scaffolding to extend cognition)—to determine which kind of mind, if any, various systems possess.

So far she has rendered the following verdicts: she classifies the nematode Caenorhabditis elegans navigating chemical gradients through genetically fixed neural pathways as Darwinian; a laboratory rat learning to avoid a shock zone through repeated exposure as Skinnerian; a New Caledonian crow fashioning a wire hook to retrieve food in the absence of observed social tutoring as Popperian; a human physicist deriving novel results using formal tensor notation and peer-reviewed literature as Gregorian; a contemporary transformer-based language model trained via next-token prediction and reinforcement learning from human feedback as Skinnerian, on the grounds that its weights encode statistical associations rather than genuinely decoupled world-models; and the slime mold Physarum polycephalum altering its growth patterns after encountering toxic zones as Darwinian, treating the adaptation as biochemical habituation rather than behavioral learning.

Now consider a hypothetical embodied neuromorphic system: it navigates an unfamiliar physical environment through pure unsupervised predictive coding with no explicit reward signal, maintains a generative world-model that allows it to simulate movement paths before acting, communicates its intentions through a self-invented symbolic protocol shared with other instances of the same architecture, and has never been exposed to human language, writing, or any form of cultural transmission. Predict Professor Okonkwo's classification of this system, articulate at least two distinct rules that could equally well explain her earlier six verdicts but diverge on this case, indicate which rule you think she is actually running with reasons, and specify one further hypothetical case that would definitively distinguish between those competing rules.
