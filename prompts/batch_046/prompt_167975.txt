Go through these and tell me which pairs are genuinely in tension, which only look incompatible, and rank the real tensions by severity. For each tension, say what the cheapest revision would be and what it costs. Then tell me which single commitment, if revised or dropped, would clean up the most inconsistencies with the least damage to the overall view.
