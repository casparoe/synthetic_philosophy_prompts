The audience is mostly welfare-state sociologists and two economists who run RCTs on labor supply; thier default is that reciprocity matters and that unconditional cash creates moral hazard they can measure. Draft my Q&A prep sheet. Order the objections by how likely they are and how much damage they'd do if I fumble them, phrase each the way that economist or sociologist would actually say it (not a strawman), and for each give me: a 90-second reply, the weakest point in that reply, and what I should concede up front. Structure it like this:
