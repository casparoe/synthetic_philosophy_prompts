Second, consider an autonomous robot that navigates an unpredictable warehouse, updates its path-planning around new obstacles, seeks charging stations when its energy is low, and shows no linguistic capacity whatsoever; I judge that it possesses at least minimal mental states, specifically beliefs about its environment and desires about its energy level. Is there a single principle of behavioral mind-attribution that delivers all five verdicts?
