Is this failure of dynamic consistency best understood as a defect in the conditioning rule itself—specifically, its inability to preserve ordering relations across different resolutions of the state space—or does it indicate that infinitesimal and undefined expectations belong to categorically different normative classes and therefore should not be treated with a single uniform conditioning strategy?
