Restate von Neumann’s argument that self-replicating machines could persist and evolve independently of human control—particularly the analogy he drew between artificial automata and biological reproduction—using only the vocabulary I know: evolutionary stable strategies, dissipative structures, ecological succession, and open-system thermodynamic regimes.
