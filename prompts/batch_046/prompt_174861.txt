No single thermostat holds an internal model of the whole grid. Some philosophers argue that because the functional organization is present at the global level, the grid is sentient; others say sentience requires that the integration be available to the system itself, not just visible to an outside observer describing the pattern.

Write about 250 words. Adress (1) whether functional equivalence at the global level is sufficient for sentience, and (2) whether the lack of a unified internal perspective undermines moral attribution.

"I'd argue the grid doesn't count, mainly because of the subject problem. Even though the lattice replicates the transition rules of a global workspace, each thermostat only processes local inputs.
