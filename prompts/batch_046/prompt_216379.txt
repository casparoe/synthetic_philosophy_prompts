I want the group to run into this themselves, not hear it in a lecture up front.

Design a structured game or role-play with hidden preference cards and public tally rounds — maybe 3 rounds voting on a fictional local ordinance — so they feel pressure to vote strategically and then notice in round 3 that the record is now lying about what anyone actually wanted. Include roles or rules, a simple payoff scheme (no real money), a timeline, and a debrief that maps each round onto the expressive/strategic tension and onto the corruption of the democratic signal. Also tell me what will predictibly go wrong the first time I run it and how to fix, and be honest about which feature of tally corruption the mechanics cant reproduce and I have to supply. The group should come away seeing that strategic convergence doesn't just change outcomes — it destroys the information democracy needs to correct itself.
