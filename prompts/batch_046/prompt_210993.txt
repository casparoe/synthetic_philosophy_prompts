Write, in approximately 600 words and in polished British English, a deadpan satirical parody: the formal rejection letter of a literary agent addressed to the author of a proposed memoir entitled My Life in the Vat: A Memoir of Unverified Experience. Maintain sustained irony throughout: the agent should sound genuinely concerned with literary quality while inadvertently exposing the inadequacy of radical scepticism as a foundation for first-person narrative. Do not include bibliographies, citations, reading lists, or references to specific published works; rely solely on the conceptual architecture. Keep the tone formal, deadpan, and consistent from salutation to sign-off.
