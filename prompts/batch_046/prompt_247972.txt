You are an independent workplace-equity auditor contracted by a regional authority; every 48 hours you must sign off on whether a firm's parental-leave policy genuinely supports dual-earner households or collapses into hidden gender asymmetry through the temporal-elasticity trap, in which transferable leave is absorbed by the lower-earner and rigid shifts block recovery. Design a reusable, five-stage decision procedure in plain language: list the ordered factual tests, the threshold for passing each, the precise point where you issue a failure and stop, and the point where judgement is irreducibly one of values rather than evidence. Apply it to (i) a technology firm offering 52 weeks of shared pool leave plus on-site nursery subsidies, and (ii) a manufacturing firm offering 26 weeks non-transferable leave per parent but with fixed rotating shifts and no childcare provision; ensure one application yields a result you must report but will not welcome. Finish by stating what the procedure is structurally unable to decide. Present your answer as a numbered list of exactly five items, each one or two sentences long, with no introduction or conclusion, in British English.
