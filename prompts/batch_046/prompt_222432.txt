Within a single decision-context with fixed weights, "better explanation" remains transitive, and the spectrum analogy therefore mistakes a change in evaluation framework for a failure of the comparative relation itself. Work through the conceptual structure without appealing to specific named theories or bibliographies; I want the argument on its own terms.
