And here's the thing that bugs us: each time that happens, the weird object gets filed away in this special 'not part of normal life' category, but nobody treats it as a reason to doubt the original reconstruction of the normal system. Even when half the site is full of these anomolies, the core model of daily belief and institution stays intact, protected by this expandable side-box.

Does this pattern have an actual name?
