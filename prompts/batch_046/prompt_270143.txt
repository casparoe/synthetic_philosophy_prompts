The discussion in my group has circled for weeks without movement: the contractarians say that rational agents in a state of nature would agree to mutual constraints for long-term benefit; the moral realists insist that moral properties carry an intrinsic authority that does not depend on contingent desire; and a third faction claims that the entire framing mistakes moral reasons for a species of hypothetical imperative that binds only if one already cares about others. Everyone appeals to the same structural features of the thought experiment, everyone agrees on what the ring does, and yet no one has conceded a millimeter.
