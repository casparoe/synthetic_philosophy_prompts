I'm working on a piece about pain asymbolia and the idea that pain is intrinsically bad. The cases are wild—people with parietel lesions who feel burning, stabbing, full spatial mapping, but say its not unpleasant at all, like the affective tag got switched off while the sensory-discriminative pathway stayed intact. If badness can drop out so cleanly, doesn't that show unpleasantness is just a contingent neural coupling—maybe an anterior cingulate precision-weighting glitch—rather than part of pains essence? So aren't philosophers who call pain necessarily awful just confusing an overlay for a conceptual truth? And if suffering is really just hyper-precise top-down modulation of that signal, shouldn't we treat chronic pain less as tissue damage talking and more as a learned interoceptive error that could, in principle, be unlearned?
