I am trying to stabilize my philosophical profile on consciousness before presenting it to a reading group, and I want an audit of consistency rather than an overview of the literature. Which of these pairs are genuinely in tension, which only appear to be, and what is the least costly revision? Please order the tensions by severity, identify the single revision that would clean up the most conflicts, and present everything in continuous prose only—no headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables, as in a letter.
