The new proposal is a language model aligned with bog-standard RLHF, but with an added "reflection lock"—just a consistency regularizer that penalizes divergence from the model's own initial reasoning chain across long contexts. If they want to be consistent with their preceedents, should they approve? Which past case is the closest analog—the frozen RLHF approval or the self-distillation rejection?
