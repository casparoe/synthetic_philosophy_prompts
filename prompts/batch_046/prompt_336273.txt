If ARCHON's transition is purely a functional upgrade—gaining a predictive capacity—while Mary's transition involves phenomenal acquaintance, then either Mary's new knowledge is just ARCHON's new ability (in which case physicalism survives, since abilities are not physical facts), or Mary's knowledge is irreducibly non-physical in a way tied to biological embodiment rather than to information completeness. Which horn do you take, and does either preserve your claim that complete physical knowledge necessarily leaves something out?
