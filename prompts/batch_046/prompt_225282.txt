Write the desiderata that an adequate account of hedonism must meet to resolve the heterogeneity problem: what feature, if any, unifies sensory pleasures, intellectual satisfactions, aesthetic joys, and so-called alien pleasures that share no recognizable quality with human experience. For each requirement, give the motivating case or counterexample—such as the qualitative gap between tasting chocolate and solving a proof, the existence of malicious or empty pleasures, or the challenge from reward-prediction-error models that reduce pleasure to a quantitative signal—flag any pair of desiderata you suspect are jointly unsatisfiable, and briefly indicate how quantitative, qualitative, phenomenological, and intentionalist theories stand with respect to the list. Separate settled phenomenological observations about pleasure from genuinely contested claims about its nature, and do not present any disputed unifying feature as the consensus view.
