If the self-indication assumption requires weighting cosmological hypotheses by the number of observers they contain, and the self-sampling assumption requires conditioning on being a randomly selected member of one's reference class, then Bostrom's presumptuous philosopher appears to compound both: she uses SIA to inflate the prior for vast universes and then applies SSA to place herself within one. But if the paradox is generated by this hybrid, wouldn't the consistent remedy be to drop SSA entirely and apply pure SIA uniformly—even to the selection of the reference class itself—rather than to restrict SIA?
