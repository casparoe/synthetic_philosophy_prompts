I'm trying to figure out what a predictive account of singleton emergence actually has to explain before I'd take it seriously. Not just Bostrom's broad sketch, but the full spec. It needs to cover partial singletons, like one actor controlling all compute but not all normative frameworks. Which ones are negotiable vs dealbreakers?
