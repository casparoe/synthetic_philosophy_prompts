I think she should take the bet if she cares impartially about others, but if she refuses because she needs the $10 for her child's urgent medicine, I do not consider that irrational.

Case 5: A government considers a policy with a 1% chance of causing 100 million deaths, no effect in 98.9999% of worlds, and a 0.0001% chance of preventing a future event that would kill 1 trillion people. I think the policy is unacceptable, even if the raw expected value is positive.
