I feel like it's doing a lot of hidden work, but I can't pin down what exactly it's claiming. Could you lay out the precise theses that sentence could stand for? For each reading, say whether it's just a platitude, whether it's actually defensible, and whether it's false. And tell me which version people are usually smuggling in when they use the line to argue that you should just "follow your gut" on these big irreversible choices.
