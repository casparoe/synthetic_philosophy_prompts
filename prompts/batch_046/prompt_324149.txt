I need the full specification of what any adequate theory of consent validity must be able to deliver.

Write the desiderata for such a theory: exactly five numbered requirements, each expressed in one or two sentences, with no introduction and no conclusion. Each item should identify a condition the theory must satisfy—covering direct deception, coercion, competence thresholds, and specifically how to adjudicate cases of architectural misalignment where consent is technically present but structurally redirected—together with the brief justification for why failing that item would render the theory inadequate. Present the response in American English, and do not include citations, bibliographies, or references to specific scholars.
