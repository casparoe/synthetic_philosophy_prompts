me and my roommate argued for hours about those ar headsets that render the room through cameras instead of clear lenses, like every wall and table is just video pixels with digital stuff layered on. he said its just engineering, but i kept thinking about zhuangzi and that butterfly dream thing, and whether the designers are accidentally treating waking life like another simulated feed. what philosophical commitments about perception and reality does that rendering practice actually presuppose? can they still be naive realists about the physical room, or does the design force them into some kind of representationalism where direct access is always mediated? and if we replaced whatever implicit view they have with one that takes unmediated waking perception as epistemically privileged - like zhuangzi questioned which state was real but never said they were equally real - what would have to change in the safety protocals? would they still render boundaries as digital overlays or actually have to physically restrain users?
