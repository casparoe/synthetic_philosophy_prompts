In other words, the hole isn't a redundancy problem—it's a problem about whether points are even the right kind of thing to bear physical properties. You should address why this doesn't just collapse into standard relationalism, and why treating points as 'pure structure' doesn't actually resolve the determinism worry but just relocates it to the manifold's topology.
