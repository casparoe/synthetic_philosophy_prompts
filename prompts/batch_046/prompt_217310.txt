hey so I'm in my second year of a phd in history of economic thought and last week our marx reading group got into it over the fragment on machines. I wasn't taking sides but now I'm writing a paper and need to figure out where the argument acutally breaks. here's the exchange, more or less verbatim:

1) Maya said Marx's whole point in the Grundrisse is that capitalism contains an automation contradiction: once machinery replaces living labor entirely, surplus value dries up because only living labor produces it, so full automation should trigger collapse. She said Marx basically foresaw AI-driven unemployment as capitalism's death sentence.

2) Leo said that's a cartoon version. He argued Marx distinguishes mechanization — where machines amplify labor — from the automatic system of machinery where labor becomes supervisory. Leo said surplus value doesn't vanish; it relocates to the systemic nodes — design, coordination, maintenance — that keep the automatic system running. He called this the displacement thesis and said it's implicit in Marx's talk about general intellect.

3) Maya came back that Leo's systemic nodes sounds like monopoly rent or managerial salary, not surplus value extracted from labor. She said if Leo is right, then Marx's labor theory of value is unfalsifiable: whenever labor seems gone, Leo just redefines real labor to be wherever capital is still making profit. She said that's saving the theory by destroying its content.

4) Leo replied that Marx never said value is physical sweat; it's a social form. The capital-labor relation survives automation because capital still controls access to the general intellect and the automatic system. He said Maya is treating value like a thermodynamic input, which is exactly the error Marx criticized in the physiocrats.

Can you adjudicate this move by move? I specifically want to know: is Leo's mechanization vs automatic system distinction actually grounded in Marx, or is he projecting a modern AI framework onto the 1850s? Does Maya's unfalsifiability objection land, or does Leo's social-form reply actually answer it? And what are both missing about how Marx thinks general intellect functions — not as relocated labor, but as a direct productive force that capital appropriates without mediating it through wage-labor at all?

Please don't just split the difference. I need the actual conceptual fault lines.
