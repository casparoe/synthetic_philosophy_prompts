Leo said that's just a tragic choice with an overriding duty; once you see the hierarchy, there's only one genuine "ought," and the regret is psychological, not moral leftover. Maya said demanding a complete theory before admitting the conflict is begging the question, and Leo countered that accepting both obligations means accepting you can be required to do the impossible, which violates ought-implies-can. They went back and forth about whether moral residue is real or just emotional hangover, and I stayed out of it.
