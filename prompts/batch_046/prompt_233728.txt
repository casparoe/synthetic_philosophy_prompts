As a retired physicist who has recently begun studying metaphysics, I have been trying to assess the endurantism versus perdurantism debate by comparing it to physical processes I encountered over my career. Rank the following five analogies—(1) a standing wave maintained in a resonant cavity, (2) a propagating chemical reaction front moving through a homogeneous medium, (3) a biological organism whose cells are entirely replaced over time, (4) a quantum field excitation that persists only through continuous interaction, and (5) a classical rigid body undergoing pure spatial translation—by how well they illuminate whether objects are wholly present at each moment or instead perdure as four-dimensional spacetime worms. Respond as a numbered list of exactly five items, each consisting of one or two sentences, with no introductory or concluding remarks. In each item, state whether the analogy favors endurantism or perdurantism and briefly justify the judgment with reference to the physical behavior described.
