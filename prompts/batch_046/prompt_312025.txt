I am advising a three-year interdisciplinary research programme—philosophy and institutional design—that takes Nietzsche’s tripartite distinction between monumental, antiquarian and critical history, together with his claim that each can become actively harmful to life, as its central organising framework. We are not pursuing textual exegesis for its own sake; our focus is on how these three modes operate, conflict and potentially reinforce one another within contemporary long-horizon governance bodies—such as climate-adaptation boards, intergenerational-equity institutions, or oversight bodies for transformative technologies—that must maintain extensive historical records while retaining the capacity for decisive, future-oriented action.

Within this framing, please do the following. First, identify the three or four most important open conceptual problems that arise when Nietzsche’s schema is applied to institutional rather than individual historical consciousness: for instance, whether the monumental mode can be rehabilitated for collective decision-making without collapsing into mythologising narrative; whether antiquarian accumulation necessarily produces the kind of epistemic stasis that erodes agency; whether the critical mode can be exercised collectively without devolving into presentist dismissal of inherited commitments; and how these three modes interact structurally inside a single organisation rather than within a single psyche. Second, rank these problems in order of priority for our programme, and defend the ordering explicitly: state the criteria you are using—such as which questions are upstream of others, which are tractable through conceptual analysis rather than empirical social science, and which, if resolved, would most alter how such institutions ought to be designed. Third, for each ranked problem, specify what kind of philosophical result over the next two to three years would cause you to revise its position, either upward or downward, and highlight any trade-offs between conceptual depth and practical applicability that your ranking entails. I am looking for a defended research agenda, not a literature survey, and I do not want bibliographies, reading lists, or citations.
