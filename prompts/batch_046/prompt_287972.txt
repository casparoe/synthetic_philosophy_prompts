I work in software engineering and have no formal background in philosophy. I am attempting to assess whether my intuitions about Al-Ghazālī's occasionalism and causation hang together. My commitments are as follows:

(a) Al-Ghazālī's occasionalism is correct for the natural world: there are no necessary connections between apparent causes and effects. Fire does not burn cotton; rather, God directly creates both the presence of fire and the burning of cotton as conjoined occasions.

(b) In distributed computing—particularly event-sourced architectures and serverless pipelines—causation strikes me as genuinely polycentric. No single process is the direct author of system state; outcomes emerge from message logs and invocation chains.

(c) I find evolutionary debunking arguments persuasive: natural selection causally molded human moral cognition toward survival and reproduction, not toward tracking objective moral truths.

(d) Occasionalism, on my view, resolves Hume's problem of induction. Regularities are not mysterious necessary powers but stable divine habits, making inductive expectation rational without metaphysical hidden forces.

(e) In my professional practice, when I deploy a code patch and observe a corresponding drop in error rates, I treat myself as having genuinely caused that change—not as having merely occasioned a direct, independent update of server state by an external agent.

Which of these commitments actually conflict, and which conflicts are only apparent? In particular, does (c) fail if (a) is true, since evolutionary explanation seems to require real causal efficacy of selection pressures rather than occasional conjunction? Does (b) undermine (e), or can distributed-system causation be reconciled with occasionalist metaphysics? Finally, is (d) a genuine dissolution of the skeptical problem or merely its relocation? Please rank the tensions by severity, indicate which commitment is least costly to revise, and advise me on how to preserve both occasionalism and evolutionary debunking if that is possible.
