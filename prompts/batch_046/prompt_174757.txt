On this reading, your biography is not a manual for copying conduct but an ethical stress-test that forces the reader to observe how moral qualities collapse across regimes. You may respond using only the conceptual resources available in your own era, together with the terms I have just introduced. Remain fully in character; defend the purpose of your Lives and your method of comparison, but treat my claim as a serious objection. Keep your reply to roughly one paragraph, do not break character, and do not ask me to restate my framework. Here is my opening challenge: if your aim was truly moral imitation, why pair two orators—Demosthenes and Cicero—who both failed to preserve the liberty of their states, rather than pairing each with a successful statesman from his own culture?
