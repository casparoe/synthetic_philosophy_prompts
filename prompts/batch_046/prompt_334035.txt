Rather than surveying the literature, I want to teach one tight framing: that in ethics, calling something a bias requires a hidden normative baseline, and without an independent defense of that baseline the charge collapses into a restatement of disagreement. I need a pedagogical plan, not the explanation itself.

Specify the audience-level dependency chain: what idea has to be in place before I can introduce the framing without it sounding like naive relativism or like I'm just saying everything is subjective.
