If Henry Sidgwick was alive today and saw where psychical research went after his death—Stargate, Ganzfeld meta-analyses, Bem's 2011 precognition experiments, and now the idea that ESP should be tested on predicition markets rather than p-values—do you think he'd stay cautiously open like he was with the SPR? Or would that 'dualism of practical reason' he developed make him reject the whole field as lacking rational compulsion? I'm especially curious if he'd think prediction markets match his demand for intersubjective verification, or if he'd see them as just another layer of statistical noise.
