The cases I have in mind are pseudonymous digital communities, institutional protocols enforced through cryptographic commitment devices rather than interpersonal expectation, and normative systems that must survive complete agent turnover without breaking. Write the desiderata that any adequate post-Lewisian theory of convention and normative evolution must meet. Next, briefly score the main existing contenders—Lewis’s original coordination account, evolutionary game-theoretic models of norm emergence, and institutional constructivist approaches—against your list, noting where each succeeds and where it fails, without providing bibliographies, reading lists, or citations.
