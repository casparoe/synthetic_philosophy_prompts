Write the full specification of desiderata that any adequate account of hinge propositions — in the line of Wittgenstein's On Certainty — must satisfy when extended to institutional hinge divergence: situations in which distinct social practices (for instance, legal adjudication, clinical medicine, and empirical science) operate with locally non-negotiable certainty-structures that are mutually incompatible, yet each is functionally indispensable within its own domain. For each requirement, provide the motivating case or conceptual consideration, mark whether it is non-negotiable or merely a preference of a particular camp, and flag any pair of desiderata you suspect cannot be jointly satisfied. The specification should address at least the following: (a) why hinges must not be assimilated to ordinary beliefs or high-credence judgments, and what collapses if they are; (b) how a hinge can be locally suspended or bracketed within one practice without licensing global skeptical doubt; (c) how cross-institutional conflict between hinges is to be managed — whether by hierarchical ordering, domain-restriction, pragmatic arbitration, or some other mechanism — without collapsing into either relativism or a single universal foundation; (d) consistency with the historical mutability of hinges (their capacity to shift like riverbed sediment) combined with their present resistance to rational revision; (e) the status of agents who lack, or reject, a hinge that a given practice treats as indispensable. After laying out the criteria, briefly score four candidate frameworks — a foundationalist reconstruction, a coherentist reconstruction, a Wittgensteinian framework, and a pragmatic-encroachment view — against the list, indicating which desiderata each satisfies and which it sacrifices. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations; the task is the conceptual architecture, not the literature map.
