Write a deadpan parody of approximately 450–500 words, structured as a corporate press release and an accompanying user FAQ, for a fictional wellness-technology startup named Wille Audio (CEO: the invented metaphysician Dr. Lena Voluntarist). Adopt the voice of an uncritical tech-lifestyle publication reporting on premium audio hardware. The satire targets Schopenhauer’s metaphysics of music—specifically his thesis that music is not a representation of Platonic Ideas, as painting or poetry are, but the direct objectification and immediate copy of the Will itself, together with the hierarchical ranking of the arts that places music highest because it alone bypasses the principle of sufficient reason and the phenomenal world.

The piece must exaggerate genuine features of this view rather than invent false ones: present the headphones as streaming the noumenal Will in lossless format; describe the other arts as inferior “representation layers” that merely reproduce Ideas; warn users that sustained listening induces “phenomenal detachment,” a documented loss of interest in spatiotemporal causality; and organize subscription tiers according to Schopenhauer’s art hierarchy—Architecture, Sculpture, Painting, Poetry, and Music, with only the Music tier advertised as “unmediated.”

Introduce one invented but internally consistent conceptual mechanism: a hardware “Representation Filter” that is active by default and must be disabled to receive direct Will-transmission; the FAQ should explain that if the filter is accidentally left on, the listener receives only “melodic copies of eternal Ideas” and must perform a hard reboot to restore noumenal access. Remain entirely deadpan—no winking, no phrases such as “as Schopenhauer claimed,” and no requests for citations, bibliographies, or historical sources. The comedy should arise from the metaphysical precision of the absurd corporate language.
