Please draft eight to ten interview questions that press on the actual content of her position rather than asking her to restate it. I want at least one question on each of the following, with a targeted follow-up keyed to the answer she is most likely to give:

(4) How her view distinguishes propaganda from manipulation or coercion, especially since framing operates below the level of conscious assent—followed by a follow-up pressing whether this makes the view vulnerable to the objection that it licenses deceptive artistic practices.
