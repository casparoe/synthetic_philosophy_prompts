I am drafting a philosophical novel in which several characters survive radically different forms of discontinuity—teletransportation, gradual neural replacement, and a situation I have devised called the palimpsest overlay, in which one consciousness is reconstructed onto an already-active substrate so that two overlapping narrative streams share partial first-person memories but neither fully subsumes the other. For a companion essay aimed at literate readers with no background in metaphysics, I want a branching taxonomy, not a labeled quiz, that sorts the actual positions on what matters in personal survival. The tree should begin with the classic non-branching teletransporter case and then proceed through three diagnostic nodes: whether survival requires spatiotemporal continuity of the living body; whether branching into two simultaneous successors undermines the original subject’s survival; and how the palimpsest overlay should be judged when the reconstructed profile inherits some but not all of the substrate’s autobiographical continuity. Each node must be phrased as a concrete claim or case that a reader can answer without knowing the philosophical labels in advance. The leaves must include strict psychological continuity constrained by the no-branching clause; psychological continuity that permits branching; animalism centered on the persistence of biological life; the narrative-identity view that privileges authorship and coherence over brute continuity; the deflationary view that treats survival as not a deep metaphysical fact; and at least one logically possible combination that the tree reveals as unoccupied, with a brief diagnosis of whether it is incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored. For each named or unnamed leaf, state its most awkward commitment in a single sentence. Please flag any pair of positions that the tree cannot genuinely separate, and explain specifically whether the palimpsest case collapses them together or forces a distinction they ordinarily avoid. Write the result as a formal handout suitable for an audience of fiction readers, using no bibliographic references, no citation requests, and no dependence on obscure technical literature.
