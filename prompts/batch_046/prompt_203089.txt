Another friend said that's too quick: shared legal traditions, language norms, and historical memory can produce real collective dispositions, even if they're not essential or uniform. I need to write an essay for class on this exact tension: is national character coherent at all, and is it ever acceptable to generalize about whole peoples?

Please write the essay, weighing both sides seriously. Address the strongest objection on each side — like the risk of essentialism versus the risk of being empirically naive about collective patterns — and reach a clear conclusion with justification.
