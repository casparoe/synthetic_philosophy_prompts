I have come to defend a specific, somewhat hybrid account of the statue-and-clay problem in material constitution. The view holds that the statue and its constituting lump of clay are neither strictly identical—since they have different persistence conditions—nor merely coincident in the way that two distinct objects happen to occupy the same space. Instead, they stand in an asymmetric relation of metaphysical dependence: the statue exists in virtue of the clay being arranged statue-wise, but this grounding is primitive, not reducible to temporal parts, modal properties, or kind-relative survival conditions. Consequently, gradual molecular replacement of the clay produces no sharp boundary for the statue's continued existence, whereas the clay itself persists through such change; the asymmetry is explained entirely by the direction of grounding rather than by any difference in essential properties between artifact and material.

What would genuinely count against this position? I am not looking for the standard catalog of objections—Leibniz's Law conflicts, coincidence puzzles, or the familiar appeal to modal differences—but for the defeaters that a committed proponent should take as serious risks. Which thought experiments, formal results about persistence or grounding, or developments in metaphysics of artifacts or parts and wholes would threaten the core claim? In particular, how does the view fare in cases of partial destruction, fusion into a larger artifact, or simultaneous multiple arrangements of the same clay? Would it be forced to concede that grounding conditions can be arbitrarily adjusted to absorb counterexamples, and if so, does that insulation make the view unfalsifiable in a damaging way? Please rank potential defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, distinguish those that defenders could concede without abandoning the asymmetry thesis from those that would require its rejection, and say whether there is any observation or argument that should actually move someone holding this view—rather than being dismissed as a puzzle to be solved within the framework.
