State precisely what, if anything, could resolve or dissolve the debate—an empirical finding, a formal result, a conceptual clarification, or nothing at all—and justify that prognosis. Write in American English; I present this request in British English. Do not supply bibliographies, reading lists, or citations, and avoid appeals to highly obscure technical results that depend on specialised memorisation.
