Write the desiderata that any adequate theory of social construction for institutional kinds must meet, taking as the central case kinds—such as money, gender classifications, and racial categories—that combine normative authority with distributed functional roles that can outlast any particular mental state of acceptance. Do not offer generic virtues like "explanatory power"; instead specify the phenomena the theory must accommodate, the distinctions it must preserve, and the specific cases or puzzles it must dissolve. In particular, your list must include: (1) normative drift, in which the membership conditions for a kind shift because the institutions that constitute the kind revise their own rules, and whether this requires a meta-level rule that is itself immune to drift; (2) kind migration, where an entity changes kind membership without intrinsic physical change, and whether such migration is always mediated by explicit institutional acts or can also occur through distributed collective renegotiation; (3) the distinction between being constituted by collective acceptance and being sustained by functional roles (e.g., currency maintaining value through circulation patterns and legal enforcement rather than through present beliefs about it); (4) consistency with the causal efficacy of these kinds in non-social domains, such as inflation dynamics or medical discrimination, without reducing the kind to its causal effects; (5) the ability to distinguish purely institutional kinds (a specific banknote) from kinds that are partly anchored in non-institutional facts (race, gender) without collapsing the latter into biological taxonomy; and (6) whether the theory can accommodate simultaneous, conflicting constructions of the same kind across different social groups without treating one as merely erroneous. For each desideratum, provide the motivating case or theoretical consideration behind it, flag whether it is non-negotiable or a preference of a particular camp (e.g., conventionalists versus critical realists), and identify any pair you suspect is jointly unsatisfiable—especially whether accommodating normative drift and maintaining a sharp distinction between institutional and anchored kinds can be done within a single framework. Keep the weight on the criteria and their internal tensions rather than on scoring competitors; but briefly indicate how a pure constitutive-rules account, a critical-realist account allowing material anchoring, and a distributed-practice account emphasizing emergent functional roles fare against your list, noting where each fails a non-negotiable requirement or resolves a tension the others cannot.
