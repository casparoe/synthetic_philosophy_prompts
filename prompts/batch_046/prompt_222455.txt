In the LessWrong-style literature on anthropic reasoning — particularly the framing Bostrom introduced with the self-sampling assumption (SSA), the self-indication assumption (SIA), and the so-called presumptuous philosopher — there is a recurring pair of analogies used to motivate the two update rules. SSA is often pictured as drawing one marble from an urn whose total number of marbles depends on an unknown cosmological parameter: you observe your draw, update on the parameter, but the selection of the urn itself is not treated as part of the evidence. SIA, by contrast, is pictured as a factory that manufactures observers at a rate proportional to the size of the world-state, so that finding yourself as an observer is analogous to holding a product that could only have come from a high-throughput line.

I want an audit of this urn-versus-factory comparison, precisely because both analogies are being used to license opposite conclusions about whether we should favor cosmologies with astronomically more observers. Please lay out the explicit mapping: what corresponds to the prior over world-states, to the selection mechanism, to the observer-moment, and to the Bayesian update step in each case. Then identify where the mapping breaks — especially with respect to the fact that anthropic reasoning involves being necessarily embedded in the world-state one is reasoning about, rather than observing it from an outside vantage. Does the factory analogy smuggle in a causal production story that SIA does not actually require? Does the urn analogy falsely treat the observer as an externally selected sample? Most importantly: once the disanalogies are on the table, does either analogy actually support its intended conclusion — that SIA corrects SSA, or that the presumptuous philosopher’s inference is parallel to correctly inferring a large urn? Or do both analogies fail to capture indexical selection well enough that the comparison collapses? If there is a cleaner analogy that separates the indexical from the statistical update without importing spurious causal or sampling structure, sketch it.
