Would Skolem's paradox still manifest in the same way, or does the paradox depend essentially on the fact that cardinality judgments inside the model are mediated entirely by internally definable functions? More broadly, is the tension best understood as a limitation of first-order logic's capacity to fix the extension of infinite collections, or as a more general mismatch between object-language resources and meta-language resources? Please avoid a fully formal derivation; I'm looking for a conceptual clarification that connects the internal/external distinction to the specific failure of definability.
