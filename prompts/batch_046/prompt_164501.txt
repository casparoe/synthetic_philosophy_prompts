One: a healthy human experiencing red. Verdict — yes, a classic user illusion; a narrative "I see red" is generated and consumed by planning and reasoning systems that take it as authoritative, despite there being no inner spectator.

Two: a sleepwalker navigating a dark kitchen, then waking to say "I was completely unconscious." Verdict — no illusion here. There is sensorimotor processing, but no report being fed to and consumed by a self-model that uses it to guide reasoning.

Three: a hypothetical "cortical lace" I invented for the novel — a prosthesis that synthesizes a fear-report from a silent amygdala and writes it into biological working memory, which then directs avoidance behavior. Verdict — still yes, a user illusion. A fabricated report is being treated authoritatively by a separate reasoning module, exactly like case one.

Four: a blindsight patient who denies seeing anything yet correctly guesses card colors. Verdict — yes, but negatively; the self-model receives an authoritative "no sight" report that misrepresents the actual visual processing occurring.

Five: a large language model running a "reflection loop" that writes "I am uncertain" into its own context window when entropy rises, then uses that token string to adjust outputs. Verdict — I lean no. The text is just another input being processed uniformly; there is no separation between the reporter and a distinct "user" being misled.

Six: a hypothetical "phenomenal hive" — thousands of simple nodes reporting local temperature to a central aggregator that outputs "I am warm" and does nothing else. I keep oscillating: is there a minimal user being deceived, or only a sentence being produced?

Cases three and five feel structurally similar — both involve synthetic reports feeding back into processing — yet I classify them differently, and I cannot justify the split without sounding ad hoc. Is there one precise principle — perhaps requiring both (i) a separation between report-generation and an authoritative consumer, and (ii) some mismatch between report content and underlying substrate — that captures verdicts one through four, explains five, and resolves the hive?
