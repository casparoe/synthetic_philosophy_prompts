darwin and wallace: wallace's 1858 letter pushed darwin to publish, but wallace's essay was thinner, less developed. if wallace had never existed, darwin probably still publishes Origin within a few years, so the theory was inevitable too. but here i feel wallace still deserves real credit, not just a footnote, because he actually triggered the publication event. without him the timeline shifts and maybe darwin never finishes.
