Meanwhile (b) seems to depend on that ontologial multiplicity being real, yet (c) denies it is physical. Is there a stable middle ground where gauge applies at the representational level but not at the ontological level, or does that split collapse? Also, is my exception in (d) just ad hoc special pleading? And does (e) actually defeat relationalism, or could a relationalist accommodate vacuum topology through relations among regions?
