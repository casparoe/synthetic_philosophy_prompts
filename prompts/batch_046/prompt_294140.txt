(1) searle says original intentionality belongs to conscious minds with biological functions, while computer symbols have only derived intentionality because we assign meanings.
(2) dennetts two-bitser thought experiment is supposed to show that with just two minimal bits of original intentionality and the right functional wiring, you can build up arbitrarily complex intentional states.
(3) if thats right, then "original" vs "derived" isnt an ontological kind but just a gradient of organizational complexity: minimal bits arranged into bigger structures.

(6) but searle insists humans have original intentionality and AI can never have it, no matter the complexity.
(7) therefore taking the two-bitser seriously undermines searles sharp divide: either everything complex gets original intentionality or nothing does.
