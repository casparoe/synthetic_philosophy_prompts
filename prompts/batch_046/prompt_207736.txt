Design a thought experiment in which a macro-system is composed of micro-subjects arranged in a closed circular chain of intentional aboutness: each micro-subject’s experience is directed solely at the phenomenal state of the next subject in the loop, with no first or last member and with no aggregate observer outside the loop. The physical substrate should be entirely ordinary—no exotic physics, just this relational structure among otherwise standard experiential units.

Constraints and deliverables:
- Present the scenario in one paragraph.
- Then state what a constitutive panpsychist—who holds that macro-experience is literally composed of micro-experiences—would conclude about whether a unified macro-subject exists in this loop, and explain the reasoning that leads there.
- Next, state what a cosmopsychist—who holds that only one cosmic subject exists and that apparent micro-subjects are modifications or perspectives of it—would conclude about the same loop, and explain why they reach the opposite verdict.
- Identify the single structural feature of the loop that forces the two views to diverge.
- Describe what happens to the case if that feature is removed (for example, by breaking the circle with a terminal node that has no further object of awareness).
- In one sentence, explain how a defender of the view that comes out badly would attack the case.

End with \answer{[the structural feature, in 2-6 words]} on its own line, followed by a final line beginning Verdict: and giving a one-sentence summary of the case’s main vulnerability to counterattack.
