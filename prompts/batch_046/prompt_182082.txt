The audience is a mixed philosophy seminar: one specialist in ancient philosophy skeptical of importing modern economic categories, an economic historian of Hellenistic markets who thinks philosophers overread remarks on retail trade (kapelikon), and two graduate students steeped in Marxist interpretations who will defend a use-value/exchange-value reading of the text.
