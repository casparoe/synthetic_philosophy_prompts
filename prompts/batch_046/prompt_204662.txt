For a working paper in value theory I am trying to stabilize my position and need a consistency audit before I circulate it. Here is my current philosophical profile.

(1) I hold tranquilism in its strong form: the only intrinsic good is the complete absence of craving (taṇhā) and of suffering (dukkha). Positive states—achievement, knowledge, love, even pleasure—carry no independent axiological weight; at best they are instrumentally valuable as stable conditions that prevent the recurrence of craving.

(2) I accept a version of negative utilitarianism that is not merely prioritarian but lexical: reducing suffering dominates any consideration of producing positive goods, and in cases of conflict we ought always to choose the course that minimizes total suffering, even if that eliminates all positive experience.

(3) I think rational agency essentially involves adopting long-term, open-ended projects that necessarily expose the agent to risk, disappointment, and possible failure. I do not think a life without such projects counts as rational, even if it is maximally tranquil.

(4) I interpret human flourishing (eudaimonia) as nothing other than sustained ataraxia—a durable equilibrium in which craving is not satisfied but structurally absent. On this view, flourishing is compatible with profound ignorance, social isolation, and the absence of conventional success, provided the equilibrium holds.

(5) I reject hedonism, yet I maintain that the absence of suffering is not a mere privation but a positively characterized state—call it "negative presence"—analogous to the way silence can be a structured acoustic feature rather than the absence of sound.

Please go through these commitments and tell me which pairs are in genuine tension, which only appear to conflict, and how severely. For each real tension, indicate what the cheapest revision would be and what it costs. Rank the tensions by severity. Finally, tell me which single revision to one commitment would do the most to restore coherence across the board, and whether that revision is one I should be willing to make.
