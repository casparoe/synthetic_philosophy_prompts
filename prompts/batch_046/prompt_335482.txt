I have recently taken on advisory responsibility for a deployment pipeline building large-scale language systems, and the question of whether our present alignment toolkit is sufficient has shifted from an abstract debate to a personally urgent one. I hold the view that prosaic AI alignment—the claim that scaling current methods, specifically pretraining, supervised fine-tuning, RLHF, adversarial red-teaming, and mechanistic interpretability exactly as currently practiced, together with sufficient engineering rigor and compute, can keep systems within intended behavioral bounds as capabilities grow, without waiting for a theoretical breakthrough in value learning, a formal science of intent, or a solved interpretability—adequately addresses misalignment for architectures built from present techniques. I do not claim it solves superintelligence alignment in principle, only that for foreseeable systems misalignment is an engineering failure rather than an inevitable structural feature.

What would count against this view? I want the evidence and arguments that should actually move me, sorted by how decisive they would be: empirical results that have arguably already occurred, theoretical results showing current methods fail under well-specified conditions, and conceptual arguments about the relationship between capability growth and evaluation reliability. In particular, I am interested in the live version of the evaluation-gap argument—that as capabilities scale, human and automated evaluators become relatively weaker, creating a bootstrap dependency in which alignment checks rely on systems whose own alignment is precisely what is in question—and whether defenders of the prosaic view have qualified their position to avoid this by restricting scope to “foreseeable architectures” or “with sufficient rigor.” Please assess honestly whether such qualifications have insulated the view into unfalsifiability, and if so, whether that insulation is a legitimate boundary condition or a theoretical defect. I also want to know what proponents of prosaic alignment themselves would concede as counting against them, not only what critics say. Rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, and say whether any remaining escape routes are live rather than merely logically possible.
