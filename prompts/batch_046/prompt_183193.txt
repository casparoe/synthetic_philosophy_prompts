3. so psychiatric kinds aren't like chemical kinds; there's no 'schizophrenia in itself' seperate from the current DSM criteria and clinical culture.

7. so psychiatric science can't accumulate stable knowledge across revisions -- every update breaks continuity with past research.
8. yet we clearly do compare old and new studies, and we treat rising autism rates as real changes in occurence, not just artifacts of broader criteria.
9. therefore either looping effects don't exist (reject 1) or psychiatric research is incoherent (accept 6-8).
