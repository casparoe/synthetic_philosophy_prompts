I'm on the funding review board for the Meridian Health Foundation and we're deadlocked on a pilot grant. The PI is proposing a study of "adaptive dissent profiles" — people whose emotional and cognitive patterns fall well outside biostatistical norms but who report high wellbeing and function fine in their own communities. The intervention is called "normative alignment coaching," designed to shift these outliers closer to population means. Some boardmembers say health is just species-typical functioning, so this is medicine. Others say thats naked normativism — we're pathologizing statistical deviance and selling conformity as cure. Draft a memo recommending whether we fund it, with criteria the board can actually apply in a vote, and treat the strongest argument for the losing side fairly. Keep it practical, no need for bibliographys, just reasoning we can use in the room.
