i call it 'soft realization' — the idea that functionalism should build multiplicity into the mental kind itself instead of treating it as an empirical accident. take sadness. standard functionalism gives it one causal profile: loss-input, withdrawal-output, belief-modulation, etc. but actual sadness comes in variants—acute and paralyzing vs diffuse and motivating. these aren't just different hardware running the same software; they share a family resemblance in temporal dynamics but differ in how they integrate with memory and action-planning. so maybe mental kinds are genres, not species. jazz can be played on sax or piano, but the genre itself includes internal variation in rhythm and harmony.
