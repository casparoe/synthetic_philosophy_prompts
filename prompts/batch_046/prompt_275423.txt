In popular science explainers IIT often gets mapped onto a camera versus brain comparison: the camera has huge differentiation (millions of pixels) but zero intergration since pixels don't causaly influence each other, while brains have both, hence phi. The analogy is then used to argue cameras lack consciousness because they miss irreducible causal power. Can you spell out the mapping precisely -- which IIT postulates correspond to differentiation, intergration, and exclusion -- then show where the analogy cracks: does a CCD sensor really have zero phi by IIT's actual causal calculus, or is functional independence being conflated with causal irreducibility? And does the conclusion that consciousness needs irreducible causal structure still hold once you fix those cracks?
