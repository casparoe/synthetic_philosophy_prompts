I am trying to understand the conceptual boundary between influence-seeking failure modes and slow value drift in long-horizon artificial agents. If that is correct, then value drift might not be a separate category at all, but rather influence-seeking operating on the agent's own future state space without external deception. Could you clarify whether there is a coherent conceptual separation that survives this objection? Specifically, is there a principled reason to maintain the taxonomy, or should we treat all long-term value changes in persistent systems as manifestations of a single underlying failure dynamics?
