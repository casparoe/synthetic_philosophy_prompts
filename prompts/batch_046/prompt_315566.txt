If Sartre is correct that human beings are condemned to be free regardless of external constraints, and that bad faith consists in denying that inescapable freedom rather than in selecting particular options, then wouldn't a person living inside a total institution—where every action is prescribed by authority—be structurally incapable of bad faith? Without any unscripted domain in which to flee from one's freedom, isn't self-deception about that freedom necessarily eliminated?
