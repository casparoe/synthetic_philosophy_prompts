In thinking about this, I have come across two ideas: one holds that figures in other possible situations are never literally me but only similar counterparts, while the other holds that the very same person can exist across different possible situations. I want a precisely designed case that shows exactly where these two views agree and where they split.

In the nearby possible situation caused by that divergence, the individual gradually acquires that skill over several years, with no change to core personality traits or fundamental values.
