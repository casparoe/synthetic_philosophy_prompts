In regenerative medicine, bioengineers now implant 3D-printed vascular grafts constructed from a patient's own cells around a temporary synthetic scaffold. This case reproduces the constitution puzzle in a living, teleological register—persistence is defined by physiological role, not by shape or substrate—but it is almost never invoked in metaphysical discussions of artifacts.
