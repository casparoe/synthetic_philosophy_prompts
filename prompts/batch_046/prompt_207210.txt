Write an essay on Schopenhauer's weird claim that music isnt just art — it's literally the Will made audible, bypassing the Platonic Ideas that other arts copy. Here's the tension: he says bass maps inorganic nature, melody maps human striving, and harmony tracks the grades betwen them, so music should be a universal metaphysical diagram. But then you've got atonal music tearing up the tonal hierachy, and wildly different scales and rhythms across cultures. Does Schopenhauer have to say atonal music is metaphysically broken because it lacks the clear bass-to-melody ladder? Or can he treat it as the Will striving without resolution? And if harmony and rhythm vary so much, does that kill the idea that music is a direct, culture-independent image of the Will? Weigh the options and reach a verdict — either his metaphysics can absorb variation, or it secretly privileges Western tonal form as the only true expression. Please separate what's actually settled in Schopenhauer scholarship from what's speculative, and dont present one contested interpretation as the consensus.
