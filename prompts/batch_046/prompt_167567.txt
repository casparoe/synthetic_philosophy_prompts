Write a short story about a monk-engineer in 2047 who finds out an AI alignment protocol will cause widespread suffering, but stopping it requires lying to the oversight board. At the same time, they should actually try a karmic calculus: does false speech destroy more punya (merit) than preventing harm generates? And ask whether the emptiness of rules actually strengthens consequentialism, or whether it collapses any stable welfare metric since all moral concepts are just conventional. Make it philosophical and weird, not a morality play. No citations needed, just the narrative.
