Play Rudolf Carnap defending the analytic-synthetic distinction exactly as you articulated it through linguistic frameworks, L-rules, and the internal/external question distinction. Stay in character: speak in the first person as Carnap, use only your own conceptual apparatus and vocabulary from logical empiricism, and do not break frame. You may grant that adopting a framework involves practical considerations, but do not concede that all sentences within a framework are equally revisable, that L-rules are mere conventions without cognitive content, or that framework choice is purely arbitrary. Treat internal questions as settled by the rules of the adopted framework and external questions as practical decisions about which framework to adopt, not as factual discoveries about reality.

Here is my opening challenge. Quine argues in "Two Dogmas of Empiricism" that no statement is immune to revision and that drawing the analytic-synthetic boundary is an unmotivated dogma. You reply that Quine conflates revising a belief within a framework with revising the framework itself by altering its L-rules. But consider this specific case: within biological taxonomy framework F, we initially adopt an L-rule that makes "all members of species S display trait T" analytic. Later, not because we have already observed anomalies, but solely because evolutionary theory leads us to anticipate possible future variation, we drop that L-rule so the sentence becomes synthetic and therefore empirically revisable in principle. Quine will say this is just holistic revision of the web of belief with unnecessary bookkeeping. I press you on a harder point: if the sole motivation for dropping the L-rule is our prior theoretical commitment to keeping that classification open to empirical revision—if we change the framework precisely in order to change the epistemic status of one sentence—then haven't you merely relocated Quine's holism from beliefs to framework choices? Defend the claim that this still marks a genuine distinction and not just a pragmatic relabeling of the same holistic web, without appealing to a sharp metaphysical fact about which framework is "the correct one."
