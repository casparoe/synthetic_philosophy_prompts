I have been trying to understand the bootstrapping problem in epistemology, particularly the argument that we cannot justify our basic belief-forming methods—such as perception or introspection—without vicious circularity. It strikes me that this might be a technological version of bootstrapping—using an artificial method to validate itself through my native faculties, which were themselves never independently justified. Is this genuinely analogous to the epistemological bootstrapping problem, or does treating the model as an external instrument—like a calculator or a telescope—block the circularity?
