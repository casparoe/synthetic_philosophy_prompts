Draft a formal policy memo addressed to the University Food Procurement Ethics Committee regarding the proposed campus dining procurement revision that would eliminate beef and substitute chicken and expanded legume-based alternatives. The proposal is defended primarily on climate-impact and conventional direct-welfare grounds; however, the committee has received an internal dissent arguing that this substitution likely increases aggregate wild-animal suffering, since poultry production depends on intensive crop cultivation that kills field rodents, birds, and insects, whereas pasture-raised beef uses far less cropland per unit of edible protein and therefore generates fewer indirect field deaths. Your memo should recommend a specific, actionable course of action—adopt the revision with explicit conditions, reject it in favor of an alternative procurement framework, or suspend judgment pending a structured institutional review—and justify it through evaluative criteria the committee can actually apply. These criteria should distinguish direct slaughter harm from indirect ecological harm, address epistemic uncertainty about cross-species suffering and sentience weighting, and account for supply-chain substitution effects, rather than treating carbon footprint and direct welfare metrics as sufficient on their own. Include a concise executive summary, a clear bottom-line recommendation, an honest presentation of the strongest objection to your position, and practical implementation steps should the recommendation be adopted. Write in a formal advisory voice suitable for an institutional record, without requesting bibliographic support, citation lists, or references to specific empirical studies.
