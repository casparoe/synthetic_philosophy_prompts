I propose instead a view I call structured epistemic inertia: experts should maintain a bounded, role-calibrated resistance to revising core theoretical commitments, where the weight of disagreement depends on the institutional position of the challenger within an active research program rather than on raw expertise or confidence alone. On this account, a controlled form of dogmatism is not an obstacle to scientific progress but a prerequisite for the kind of sustained problem-shift that Lakatos describes.
