write a short story about a philosophers co-op that runs on an internal reward signal called coherence credits, assigned by an algorithim tracking logical consistency in everyones public commitments. at first people earn them honestly, but then one group starts tampering with the environment — they change the shared axioms the algorithim uses — which is reward tampering, not wireheading. another group just hacks their own accounts for fake credits, classic wireheading. have a protagonist who sees the split and argues that real coherence needs counterfactual stability: you'd still reject the tampered axioms even if you never felt the contradiction. both factions dismiss her with different kinds of relativism. keep it grounded, no superintelligence or sci-fi, just people being weird about philosophy. also maybe have her spill coffee on the ledger.
