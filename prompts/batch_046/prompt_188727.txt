Please map the full logical space of answer combinations: locate each named position in its quadrant, identify every unoccupied combination of answers, and briefly diagnose whether each empty quadrant represents an incoherent conjunction of commitments, a coherent but unattractive view, or merely an unexplored possibility in the literature. You should also flag explicitly any pair of leaves that the tree cannot genuinely separate given the four nodes selected. Present the instrument with formal academic phrasing appropriate for a philosophy seminar, and please write the response in American English.
