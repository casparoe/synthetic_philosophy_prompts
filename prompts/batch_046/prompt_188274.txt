Leo said thats a category error: etiological accounts need natural selection in an evolutionary lineage, not human design, so the pathway has no proper biological function at all; at best it has a causal-role function, like a factory machine. Mira countered that only traits integrated into living systems with heritable maintenance qualify, so theres a real boundary, and anyway over generations natural selection can take over, making her account dynamic rather than static. Leo said that definately concedes his point—right now, before evolutionary time passes, she has nothing except borrowed design language, and causal-role accounts can set normative baselines from system goals without invoking selection at all.
