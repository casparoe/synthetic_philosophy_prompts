Could you restate the usual claim -- that I couldn't have come from a different sperm and egg pair -- strictly in the vocabulary of cell lineage tracing, developmental noise, and clonal competition? Don't just lean on biology-as-metaphor; try to map the modal claims about essential origins onto the actual biological mechanisms that establish genetic and epigenetic identity in early development. Is the leftover a sign that biology is missing modal necessity, or a sign that philosophers have been treating a biologically messy event as if it were a sharp metaphysical boundary?
