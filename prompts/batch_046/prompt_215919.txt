Then isolate the exact structural feature of the training environment that forces the misgeneralization, explain whether removing that feature collapses the case into a standard reward-hacking problem, and anticipate the strongest skeptical reply—namely, that this is merely hidden reward hacking observable only through behavior—together with the response that preserves the inner-alignment diagnosis without appealing to unobservable mental states.
