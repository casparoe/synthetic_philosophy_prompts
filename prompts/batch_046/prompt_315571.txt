I sit on my state's ecological-ethics review board. A developer petitions to clear a mature temperate forest for solar infrastructure, arguing that the habitat currently sustains intense wild-animal suffering—predation, starvation, parasitism, and exposure—and that its removal reduces aggregate suffering while yielding carbon-free energy. Opponents invoke our mandate to protect "ecological integrity," contending that ecosystem welfare is not reducible to suffering counts and that intact biotic communities have value regardless.

Draft the board's written opinion recommending approval, rejection, or approval with conditions. The memo should present the strongest form of the suffering-reduction argument, identify the strongest counter that respects ecological integrity without merely asserting it, and recommend a concrete course with a defined review period. Treat the losing side's best consideration with genuine seriousness rather than dismissal.

End with exactly: Verdict: [approve / reject / approve with conditions]
