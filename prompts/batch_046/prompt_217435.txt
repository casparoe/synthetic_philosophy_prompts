On the other side there's the view that if a forgery is genuinely indistinguishable in every perceptible respect and produces the exact same aesthetic experience, then the difference in value is basically a fetish about provenance, like collecting baseball cards because of the wrapper rather than the image.

What does that practice presuppose about artistic value?
