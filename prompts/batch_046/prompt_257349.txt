For my school's philosophy and rhetoric journal, I am preparing an original satirical essay modeled on Douglas Hofstadter's well-known parody of linguistic purity arguments—commonly referred to by the title A Person Paper on Purity in Language—but I want to apply its method to an unexpected domain: the gendered and anthropomorphized vocabulary of mathematics and formal logic. Please write the piece in a deadpan, hyper-formal academic voice that never breaks character, structured like a mock scholarly article with sections such as Introduction, The Evidence of Bias, and Proposed Reforms. The satire should expose the hidden default assumptions embedded in technical terms like master equation, normal distribution, slave variables, father nodes, and well-behaved functions, then propose absurd gender-neutral replacements that reveal how arbitrary linguistic policing becomes when applied to purely functional language. The essay should be approximately 600 words, readable to someone unfamiliar with Hofstadter's original, and should derive its humor from internal consistency and the exaggeration of real linguistic habits rather than invented ones. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations; the target is the unexamined habit of projecting social categories onto abstract formal structures.
