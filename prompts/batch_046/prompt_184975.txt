You may draw only on your own framework—the household (oikos), the slave as a living instrument, the distinction between ruling and being ruled according to nature, and the claim that natural slavery benefits both parties—together with the details I have just given you. Stay in character; ask factual clarifications about the platform as you, Aristotle, would ask them. Do not invoke modern moral concepts except where they map precisely onto your own distinctions. My opening move: if the directing intelligence is not a human soul but an algorithm optimized for profit, can there still be a master-slave relation in your sense?
