My thesis is that the discursive dilemma is not fatal to premise-based aggregation, but rather a symptom of applying consistency rules over an improperly defined domain. I defend a two-stage “relevance-filter” procedure: first, the group aggregates judgments about which premises are genuinely relevant to the decision given the context; second, it applies a consistency-preserving premise-based rule only within that filtered domain.
