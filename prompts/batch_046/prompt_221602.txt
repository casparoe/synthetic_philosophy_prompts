okay so i've been chewing on schopenhauers music thing and i think i pushed it into an absurd corner but cant find the exit. here's the chain:

1. schopenhauer says music isnt representational like painting or poetry — its a direct objectificaton of the Will itself, not of ideas or phenomena.
2. the Will is singular and undivided by space, time, or causality, so whatever music expresses is literally the same universal content in every case.
3. therefore differences between pieces can only be in the acoustic form or structure, not in what is being expressed — like different wavelengths carrying an identical signal.
4. but he also ranks music highest among the arts and clearly thinks some compositions are profound while others are shallow muzak, which implies there is a real aesthetic hierarchy.
5. if the expressed content is identical and perfectly direct everywhere, then 'depth' has to be a feature of form alone — but he denies that music works through form in the way architecture or literature do.
6. so either every piece of music is equally profound (which kills aesthetic judgment) or he has to smuggle structural/representational criteria back in to distinguish beethoven from elevator noise, collapsing his claim that music is uniquely non-representational.
7. hence the view seems to prove that either music has no aesthetic ranking or its uniqueness is a myth.

where do i bail? is step 3 wrong because melody and harmony individuate the Will's expression in a way space/time cant? or is step 5 the weak link — maybe form and content are inseperable for music? i feel like there's a hidden equivocation between 'the Will is expressed' and 'the Will is expressed in this particular mode', but i cant pin it down. if you want to keep schopenhauers ranking of music, which premise costs least to drop?
