It started because Maya donated a kidney to a stranger and Leo called it heroic, but then Maya said she actually thinks it should be seen more like a strong moral obligation now that the surgery is so safe, and Leo pushed back hard. Leo said that's exactly the mistake: you're treating above and beyond as if it were a hidden floor, and once you start lowering the risk threshold you erase the space for ordinary moral life where people pursue their own projects without being accused of moral failure.
