Minimal liberalism gives each person authority over a specific domain—like deciding whether they read a certain book. Pareto efficiency requires that if every individual prefers outcome X to outcome Y, then society must choose X.

In the classic illustration, the Prude and the Lewd both have opinions about Lady Chatterley's Lover. The Prude's ranking is: neither reads > he reads > the other reads > both read. The Lewd's ranking is reversed: both read > the other reads > he reads > neither reads.
