Is that a breakdown of unity, or is it the brain's interpreter-module doing exactly what it's supposed to do, preserving coherence despite divided inputs? Don't just summarize Sperry's experiments; I want the gut-level conceptual tension, especially why someone might think split-brain patients actually have *two* consciousnesses rather than one damaged one.
