The second I say 'this is calm' out loud, the calm feels staged, like I'm performing it rather than having it. Is there a name for this specific move, where intersubjective articulation dissolves the boundry between experiencing a state and enacting it? How's it different from Dennett's point or from plain social desirability? And if there's no term, what would you call it — and where are the edges? Does it apply to shared grief, or only to these artificial mindfulness rituals? I'm after the carving, not just a label.
