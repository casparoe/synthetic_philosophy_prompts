The question should ask whether mapping Gödel-style encoding onto nucleic acid sequences resolves the infinite regress in explaining the origin of replication, obscures it by conflating syntax with causation, or reproduces it at a biochemical level. Please provide: (1) the exam question itself, specifying the graduate level and time constraint; (2) a model answer of approximately 700 words that makes the required conceptual moves; and (3) a marking scheme built around five specific analytical achievements—each worth 5 marks, with a one-line note on the typical mistake that costs points—such that an A-range answer preserves the syntactic/causal distinction while evaluating the regress, whereas a B-range answer collapses the isomorphism into pure metaphor or pure literal identity without engaging the mechanism. Frame everything so that a respondent without web access can answer from conceptual reasoning alone, and do not include any request for bibliographies, reading lists, or citations.
