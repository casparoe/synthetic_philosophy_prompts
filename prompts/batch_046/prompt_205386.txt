do these hang together? i'm especially worried that (3) and (4) are fighting, since silicon replacement should work but (4) says only meat can think. and is (5) compatible with (2) or does it smuggle in extended consciousness? what's the cheapest revision if something has to go? and please dont just list every tension - tell me which ones are real and which ones dissolve if you look closer.
