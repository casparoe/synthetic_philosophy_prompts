I'm currently taking a course on scepticism and epistemic foundations, and we've been working through Wittgenstein's On Certainty in some detail. I've tried to piece together my own view on hinge propositions, but I'm unsure whether the package holds up. Do these four commitments hang together? And does (4) contradict (2)'s diagnosis of Moore, since Moore seems to be doing exactly the kind of hinge-doubting that (4) allows? I'd appreciate an audit of which tensions are real and which dissolve on closer inspection, and what the cheapest way to restore consistency would be—please reply in British English.
