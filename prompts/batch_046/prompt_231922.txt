The target is the idea that radical dream-scepticism is just a cognitive glitch you can medicate away, and also the bureaucratic impulse to turn every philosophical problem into a risk-assessment form. Make it funny to someone who knows Zhuangzi and the modern dream-argument literature, but readable if you don't. Please write in British English, feel free to include a minor typo or two, and begin straight away with the leaflet — no intro, no commentary, and don't include any citations or reading list.
