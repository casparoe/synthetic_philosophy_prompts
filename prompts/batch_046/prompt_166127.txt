Write a dialogue between Mara and Jules, two grad students arguing over beers about whether volcanoes and germs can be real historical actors with agency, not just background conditions. Mara thinks the 536 AD volcanic winter and the Justinianic plague actually redirected Byzantine tax collection and military logistics in ways that cant be folded back into human decision-making alone. She calls this causal sovereignty, where an event directs outcomes without having a mind, and she argues that human agency is always already mediated by bodies and climates anyway so the human/non-human line is mostly fake. Jules says thats just environmental determinism in a fancy hat, and that calling a sulfur cloud an actor lets actual people — tax farmers, generals, merchants — off the hook. He should press her on whether causal sovereignty is just regular causation with extra vocabulary, and she should respond that mediation isnt the same as determinism because people still have to interpret and respond. Make them actually talk to eachother, neither is a strawman. Keep it casual, like a podcast transcript, and dont worry about perfect grammar — maybe thier reasoning gets messy and they drop an apostrophe here and there.
