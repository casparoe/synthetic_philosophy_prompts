Apply the procedure to three cases. Here the procedure should return no rescue, and I want you to show exactly where it stops and why a defender might still object.

Fix the practical context: the evaluator works alone, assesses one legacy predictive theory per quarter, has no archive access, and must defend conclusions in a brief memo. Be explicit about what the procedure cannot do—particularly whether it can ever definitively distinguish a genuine boundary condition from an ad hoc immunization strategy without access to the original theoretical architecture.
