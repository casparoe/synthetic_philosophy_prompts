Then there's the interchangable objection: if tone and intensity match exactly, you should be able to swap them with no loss. I think this actually saves hedonism, because if the tone is literally the same there's nothing missing in the swap. The only reason we resist is that uptake differs, but uptake is just our attitude about the state, not part of pleasure itself. So hedonism survives if we keep tone pure and treat uptake as existant but non-constitutive.
