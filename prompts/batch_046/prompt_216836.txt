i've been trying to wrap my head around Hofstadter's move in GEB where he maps Gödel's self-referential formula onto consciousness via strange loops. So if consciousness is literally just this structural feature of tangled hierarchies, why do people act like its some mysterious extra ingredient? More specifically, couldn't we build it into an AI by forcing its output layer to feed back into its input layer with a Gödel-style encoding, creating the loop artificially?
