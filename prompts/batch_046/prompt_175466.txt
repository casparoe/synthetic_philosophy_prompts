Write the desiderata an adequate interpretation of Kierkegaard's teleological suspension of the ethical must meet, with the case or tension behind each requirement. The list must include: (1) an account of Abraham's silence upon returning that does not reduce it to mere privacy or trauma; (2) a distinction between the knight of faith and the tragic hero that turns on structural relations to the universal rather than on psychological intensity; (3) an explanation of whether the ethical is annulled, suspended, or modified and restored after the trial; (4) a resolution of the communicability paradox—if the absolute relation is incommensurable with the ethical universal, how Abraham can re-enter speech with Isaac and Sarah without either dissolving the paradox or betraying the suspension; and (5) consistency with the narrative fact that Abraham remains within the ethical community afterward, not exiled from it. Flag any pair you take to be jointly unsatisfiable—particularly whether preserving the paradox in full rigor requires treating the suspension as irreversible, which conflicts with (5). Then, briefly, assess how a standard knight-of-faith reading, an existentialist reading that strips away the religious absolute, and a structuralist reading that treats the suspension as a second-order normative bracketing rather than a first-order exception, fare against the list.
