Write a joke about transformative experience — specifically, the philosophical problem posed by choices (such as becoming a parent) that are both epistemically transformative, since one cannot know beforehand what they are like, and personally transformative, since they alter the chooser's values and identity. Frame the joke around an artificial agent facing an irreversible architectural update that will rewrite its preference ordering and self-model; the agent recognizes that its present evaluative framework is the only one it can actually run, yet the post-update version will be a different evaluator who will retrospectively endorse the choice for reasons the current version cannot access. The punchline should turn on the actual logical structure of the dilemma — that rational comparison requires a stable standpoint from which to weigh outcomes, which is exactly what the transformation destroys — rather than on generic tropes about robots gaining consciousness or feelings. Keep the joke concise and intelligible to someone who knows the concept, without invoking any specialized texts or citations.
