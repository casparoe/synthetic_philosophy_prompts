Write a villanelle about Lewis Carroll's tortoise and the justification of deduction. The two refrains must be "The rules apply to every case" and "But not to this one, here, now." Use the form's compulsory repetition and final coupling to enact the infinite regress: Achilles insists that accepting the premises of modus ponens forces acceptance of the conclusion, while the tortoise accepts each new premise but withholds assent to the conclusion. Make the poem accurate enough that a philosopher of logic can identify which refrain belongs to which speaker; let the closing quatrain bring the refrains together without resolving whether the tortoise wins by regress or loses by exhaustion. Do not cite the original essay by title or date, and do not include a bibliography or reading list.
