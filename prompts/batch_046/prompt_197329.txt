Write a final-exam essay question for an upper-level philosophy of mind seminar on heterophenomenology as the method Dennett proposes for the scientific study of consciousness. Ask students to determine (i) whether a heterophenomenologist should enter this report into the heterophenomenological world, and (ii) whether accepting it threatens Dennett's rejection of a central inner screen by implicitly reintroducing a raw feel that exceeds functional access. Require explicit treatment of confabulation and the distinction between being conscious of something and it merely seeming to the subject that she is conscious of it.

Provide a model answer of approximately 600 words. The answer should separate heterophenomenological authority, understood as textual interpretation, from epistemic privilege, understood as direct introspective access; apply that separation to the RSVP case; and defend a specific position on inclusion of the report that does not collapse into either eliminativism or naive phenomenal realism.
