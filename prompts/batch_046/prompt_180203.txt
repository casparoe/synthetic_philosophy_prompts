Except then yesterday I was walking home and there was this guy collapsed on the sidewalk, and I just... walked past because I was late for a meeting and assumed someone else would call 911. He was fine, someone did help, but now I'm wondering if my whole 'statistical lives' framework is just an excuse to avoid the discomfort of actual rescue. Is Maya right that proximity creates a special duty, or is the drowning child argument actually about capacity rather than distance?
