Draft the joint statement that two opposing camps could sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration on whether this agent warrants adoption of the intentional stance.

- Camp B (Stance-functionalists): hold that Dennett’s account of real patterns makes the intentional stance obligatory whenever behavior is stably predictable only at that level; they argue that the agent’s persistent autobiographical revision produces non-accidental patterns indistinguishable in structure from those that justify ascription to humans, so withholding the stance is unjustified metaphysical discrimination.
