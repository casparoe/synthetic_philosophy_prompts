I am designing the final examination for an upper-level seminar in philosophy of mind and cognitive science, and I need a single essay question that forces students to work with tangled hierarchies and the inviolate level as illustrated by Escher's Drawing Hands without letting them earn marks by summarizing standard textbook treatments. To keep the task genuinely conceptual rather than mnemonic, the question should embed a somewhat non-standard scenario: a predictive-processing architecture that rewrites its own generative model in response to sustained prediction error, thereby altering the rules that govern its own belief updates. Students should be asked to map Hofstadter's hierarchical levels onto this system, to take a definite and argued position on whether this produces a genuine strange loop or merely circular feedback, and specifically to defend one claim about what serves as the inviolate level—the physical hardware, the optimizer algorithm, the initial prior distribution, or the external data stream supplying error signals—while explaining why that level remains outside the loop and why its selection is not arbitrary. The answer should be about seven hundred words, written for a ninety-minute exam sitting. Please provide the question itself, then a model answer written entirely in continuous prose with no headings, bullet points, bold text, tables, or other formatting devices, and then a marking scheme also in continuous prose that distinguishes an A-level response from a B-level response by listing the exact conceptual moves the former must make—especially recognizing that an inviolate level need not be physical and that tangledness requires cross-level self-reference rather than simple reciprocity—along with the specific omissions or confusions that should cost marks. Both the model answer and the scheme should adopt a committed stance, selecting and defending one specific interpretation rather than surveying all options neutrally, and should read like a formal letter or email with no visual structuring whatsoever.
