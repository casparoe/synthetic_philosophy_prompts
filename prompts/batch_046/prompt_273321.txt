Existing discussions of gradual replacement typically treat the brain as a homogeneous neural network or focus on functional equivalence at the cognitive level; they leave open whether identity conditions should be sensitive to the particular cellular substrate that supports consciousness-related dynamics, especially when the replacement occurs at the glial rather than neuronal level. The proposal should be written in a formal, polished style suitable for a fellowship committee that includes both philosophers and scientists.
