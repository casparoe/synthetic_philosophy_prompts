Include a turn, near the middle or in the final stanzas, where the speaker shifts from recounting the cascade to questioning whether the openness was in the world or only in our telling of it; do not resolve the tension into pure determinism or pure randomness. The rendering should be accurate enough that a specialist familiar with nonlinear dynamics and the philosophy of historical explanation would not wince, while remaining accessible as verse.
