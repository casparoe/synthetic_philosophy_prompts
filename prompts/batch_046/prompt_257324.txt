The account must cover both physical cases—such as phase transitions, superconductivity, and collective dynamics in active matter—and cross-domain cases like biological autonomy from chemistry and macroeconomic patterns from individual agency, without reducing emergence to mere computational intractability. For each desideratum, state whether it requires ontological novelty (new causal powers), epistemic autonomy (irreducible explanations), or only methodological independence, and indicate which of these interpretations the requirement genuinely forces. Include a dedicated criterion about downward causation: if macro-level states are to constrain micro-level dynamics, the account must show how this avoids violating physical causal closure and conservation laws, taking superconducting coherence or flocking behavior as the test case. Distinguish non-negotiable requirements—such as consistency with the physical base and empirical adequacy of the emergent discipline—from negotiable camp preferences, like a commitment to anti-reductionist ontology or to determinism of macro-laws.
