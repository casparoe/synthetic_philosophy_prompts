'Weber's elective affinity (Wahlverwandtschaft) means ideas and material interests align without either being reducible to the other. This isn't direct causation like physics; it's adequate causation at the level of meaning, so culture has real weight.

Applying this to Silicon Valley, the "move fast and break things" ethos wasn't just PR. It had genuine elective affinity with venture capital's need for rapid scaling and network effects. The anti-bureaucratic, meritocratic values aligned with the material logic of platform monopolies, shaping founder behaviour in ways pure economic analysis misses.
