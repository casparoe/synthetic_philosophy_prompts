There's even a risk-adjusted metric that's supposed to control for how sick the patient was, but in practice if the outcome is bad reviewers always find some judgment call in the protocol that they say was wrong, and if the outcome is good they ignore the same call. What view of moral responsibility does that whole setup presuppose?
