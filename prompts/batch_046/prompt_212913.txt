Normally, Jeffrey conditioning applies cleanly if the radiologist takes these values as given and updates her beliefs about the patient's prognosis. But suppose the radiologist also knows that the AI's calibration curve was itself just revised using Jeffrey conditioning on a new validation dataset, so the 0.7/0.3 assignment is already the result of an internal belief revision within the system she is consulting. I want my students to see that this is not merely a practical complication but raises a conceptual question about whether Jeffrey's rigidity condition—holding P(H|E) constant across the update—can be satisfied when the probabilities assigned to E are endogenous to a nested belief state.
