Write an essay on whether Hofstadter's concept of JOOTSing — jumping out of the system — illustrated by the human resolution of the MU-puzzle within the formal MIU system, demonstrates that human cognition necessarily transcends any fixed algorithmic framework, or whether it can be reconstructed as expandable meta-reasoning that remains fully mechanizable. Address the specific objection that recognizing the undecidability of MU is itself a computable procedure once hierarchical meta-rules are permitted, and evaluate whether this collapses the distinction between insight and mechanized reflection. Weigh both sides carefully, but reach and justify a definite verdict: does JOOTSing mark a genuine ontological gap between mind and formal system, or is it an epistemic artifact produced by treating formal architectures as static? In reaching your conclusion, consider whether the apparent transcendence depends on an implicit restriction against self-modifying rule systems, and if so, whether that restriction is principled or merely conventional.
