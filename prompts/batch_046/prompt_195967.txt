I want the remarks to navigate this tension precisely—to acknowledge that war has, for Hegel, a rational ethical function in preventing moral stagnation, while using his strictures on modern conflict to block the common misreading that he endorses unlimited violence. The speech should run about five minutes when spoken, remain fully accessible to the non-philosophers without reducing the view to a slogan, and should avoid presenting Hegel as offering direct guidance for contemporary conflicts; instead, the tension itself should determine what I can claim and what I must withhold. Please write this as a formal opening address, with the philosophical structure visible to the specialists but never obstructing the room.
