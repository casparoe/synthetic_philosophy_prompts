For my graduate seminar on distributive justice, I want the full specification that any adequate luck-egalitarian theory must satisfy before we begin scoring particular accounts. Give me the desiderata: the phenomena it must explain, the distinctions it must preserve without collapse—brute luck versus option luck; having moral status versus its weight; natural versus social lottery—the motivating case behind each, and what results from evolutionary biology on heritability or from social science on intergenerational mobility it cannot contradict. I want explicit coverage of the standard hard cases—the reckless gambler who freely assumes risk, the worker disadvantaged by a congenital condition, the child of immigrants who lacks the cultural capital that makes choices visible—together with the baseline problem: against what comparison class should we measure the unfairness of an inequality? I also want you to treat a less-discussed constraint I will call modal opportunity luck: inequalities that arise not from individual choice or innate endowment, but from contingent structural transitions—say, a technological or institutional shift that suddenly closes one possibility space for an entire cohort and opens another that requires prior accumulated capital—so that people are sorted into unequal life prospects by a kind of historical contingency no one chose and no one caused. Explain why accommodating this is a genuine desideratum for a luck-sensitive theory rather than a disguised preference for equality of outcome, and flag whether satisfying it is jointly compatible with the standard commitment to respecting choice-sensitive distributions and with a non-intrusive state. Then tell me which single desideratum on your list does the most damage to the mainstream candidates—Rawlsian fair equality of opportunity, Dworkin-style resource egalitarianism, and Cohen’s access to advantage—and whether any subset of your requirements is jointly unsatisfiable given standard commitments to personal responsibility and epistemic accessibility. Keep the weight on the criteria and their motivating cases; a brief table for the scoring is acceptable, but do not treat it as the main deliverable.
