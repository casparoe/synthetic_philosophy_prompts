Scheffler's afterlife conjecture claims that the prospect of human extinction undermines the value of our present projects because we implicitly depend on a collective future that outlives us. Suppose we developed a technology allowing every existing human consciousness to be uploaded into an isolated, self-sustaining digital substrate that could persist indefinitely without biological reproduction or any new generations. If such individual technological continuity were secured for all living people, wouldn't Scheffler's claim that extinction is harmful beyond the direct deaths involved collapse? After all, the conjecture's demand for continuity would be satisfied at the individual level, making collective species survival redundant rather than essential to the meaning of our activities.
