The levelling-down objection says that if giving priority to the worse off means we should destroy goods that can't be shared, then prioritarianism endorses bringing everyone down to equal misery. For example, if half are at 100 and half at 40, prioritarianism might prefer leveling everyone to 60 because the weighted gains for the bottom half outweigh the losses at the top.
