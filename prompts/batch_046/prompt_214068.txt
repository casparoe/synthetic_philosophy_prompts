Opposing them is the Regulatory Framework Camp, which holds that hinges are not knowledge-claims at all but conditions that make knowledge-claims possible; they are practice-embedded, not representational, and if one collapsed we should not call it false but should recognize that the language-game within which truth and falsity operate had been transformed. I need you to draft the formal joint statement both sides could sign. Third, identify at least three cruxes—sub-questions such that resolving them would move at least one side—including whether hinges can be world-anchored without being evidentially anchored, and whether the same hinge can function differently across distinct human practices without losing its status. For each scenario, state explicitly what the Epistemic Authority Camp and the Regulatory Framework Camp would predict or conclude. Finally, indicate whether any portion of their dispute is merely verbal—identify which portion and explain why—without reducing the entire disagreement to semantics. This must stand on its own reasoning; do not include bibliographies, reading lists, citations, or appeals to specific scholars. Please avoid reproducing a standard classroom summary; I want the tension worked out from the inside, as if both sides were genuinely trying to sign the same document.
