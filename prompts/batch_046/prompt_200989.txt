(1) the teleological suspension only works if theres a higher telos that actually justifies breaking the universal.
(2) for abraham that telos is his direct private relation to god, not any public reason.
(3) but if the justification is purely private and incommunicable, then from any outside view abraham is just a guy about to kill his kid.
(4) kierkegaard is fine with that — he says the knight of faith is incognito, you cant tell him from a murderer.
(5) heres the slip: if the justification is incommunicable even to abraham in conceptual terms — since faith is the absurd, holding the contradiction that isaac dies and lives — then abraham doesnt actually grasp a telos.
(6) so the suspension isnt teleological in any real sense; its just acting on an incomprehensible impulse.
(7) but then anyone with a sincerely held contradictory impulse is in the exact same structural position.
(8) so either kierkegaard smuggles in a hidden public criterion (betraying the suspension) or he has to say every self-contradictory private revelation justifies murder.
(9) which step should i reject? does giving it up force me to lose the incognito or the absurd?
