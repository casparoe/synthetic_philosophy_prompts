Analyze Schabowski's accidental Nov 1989 press conferance — he misread his notes and said East Germans could cross immediatly — through both counterfactual (Lewis-style) and interventionist (Pearl/Woodward) causation. Don't just map them: say who the players are, what the actual options and payoffs looked like for the regime versus the crowd, and what each theory makes of the fact that he misspoke. I especially want you to tackle preemptive structural causation — my term for the idea that the Wall was already structurally rotten from economic decay, so Schabowski's words just preempted a slower collapse. Does interventionism need an agent-level intervention, or can a structural shift count as one? And if protests might have opened the border within weeks anyway, does that kill the counterfactual claim that without his slip the Wall wouldn't have fallen that night? Be honest about where the theories are silent rather than forcing them to speak.
