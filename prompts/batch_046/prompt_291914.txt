(3) So the interesting kind of creativity, the kind that actually changes the game, has to be about introducing brand new knobs.
(4) But on Hofstadter's picture, introducing a new knob can't itself be knob-twiddling, since there was no knob there to twist yet.

(9) So the model is either false or trivial.
