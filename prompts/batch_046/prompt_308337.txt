Make an argument that Schopenhaurs claim that music is the direct objectification of Will falls apart if you actually follow his melody versus harmony distinction. The usual take is that music skips representation entirely, but heres the twist: melody tracks the Wills temporal striving, while harmony tracks Platonic Ideas. So music isnt outside representation—its doing both layers at once, which makes it closer to tragedy than to some mystical bypass. Use Schopenhaurs own framework, not modern music theory. And please separate whats genuinely in his texts, like music being non-conceptual, from the contested part where melody is mapped onto striving—that injection is mine and scholars would disagree. Also dont present the interpretation that music is the highest art as settled consensus; note that people argue over whether he meant literal expression or analogical.
