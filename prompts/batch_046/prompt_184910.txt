Draft the joint statement of an adversarial collaboration between two camps on Anscombe's attack on Truman and the atomic bomb. Camp A are the absolutists who take her line that killing the innocent is always murder, and that Hiroshima was a clear case of directing harm at civilians as a means — not just foreseeing it. Camp B are the revisionist just-war theorists who accept her taxonomy of intention versus foresight but claim she misapplies her own double-effect framework: they say the bombing targeted military-industrial capacity, the civilian deaths were foreseen but not intended, and that her outrage relies on a hidden assumption about structural intentionality that she never defends.

The deliverable should be a document both could put their names to.
