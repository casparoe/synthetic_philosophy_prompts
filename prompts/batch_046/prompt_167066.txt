Since my father's recent diagnosis with a degenerative condition that preserves his bodily orientation and emotional responsiveness while eroding his recognition of family members, I have been reading Wittgenstein's On Certainty with an urgency I did not expect, trying to understand whether the certainties that ground a life can be selectively lost rather than globally destroyed. In a discussion with my sister, who is studying philosophy of mind, she dismissed my concern that he has lost something fundamental about knowing who we are by repeating the familiar line that if you tried to doubt everything you would never reach the point of doubting anything, as though that alone settled whether relational recognition belongs among the hinges that must remain intact for doubt itself to be possible. I am not convinced the slogan does the work she needs it to do, but I also do not want to mistake a profound point about the conditions of inquiry for a glib refusal to acknowledge grief, so I need to understand exactly what commitments it carries.

Could you please lay out, entirely in continuous prose with no headings, bullet points, boldface, tables, or other visual formatting, the distinct precise theses this slogan could be advancing: a logical thesis about the pragmatics of questioning, a psychological thesis about cognitive overload, an ontological thesis about the architecture of belief, a normative thesis about epistemic entitlement, and any other reading you think belongs in the menu, including one that distinguishes between propositional hinges such as knowing one's own name and relational hinges such as knowing one's children. For each reading, indicate whether it is trivial, defensible, false, or incoherent, and then say specifically whether that reading supports her dismissal of my worry about his lost relational certainty or whether it actually leaves room for the idea that some hinges are social and can be damaged without destroying the possibility of doubt altogether. Please treat this as a letter responding to someone who needs the distinctions spelled out without reference to secondary literature, bibliographic suggestions, or citations, and please address in particular whether the framework allows for hinges that are shared or intersubjective rather than individually possessed, since that seems to be precisely what is at stake when a father no longer knows the people who care for him.
