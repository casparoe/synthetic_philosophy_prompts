Write a short story about a composer named Lena who gets obsessed with Schopenhauer's idea that music isnt like painting or poetry — it doesnt copy Ideas, its literally the Will itself vibrating in sound. She notices her best melodies come when she's emotionally dead inside, which freeks her out. Then she invents this weird technique using randomized frequency clusters that somehow lock into harmony without any emotional drive behind them — she calls it "null-music" and people say it feels like listening to a void that hums. Meanwhile her sister is dying and keeps asking Lena to play something "real" at her bedside, not the null stuff. Lena's not sure if the null-music is a betrayal of what music actually is, or if its finally freed from the Will's suffering. Keep it grounded in her apartment, maybe she's also fighting with the landlord about noise complaints. Don't make it too philosophical-sounding, just let the idea sit in the plot.
