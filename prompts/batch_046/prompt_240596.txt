Predict my professor's verdict: does she blame the chair for failing to adapt her directness to the multicultural institutional context, blame the Japanese members for misapplying cultural conventions to a high-stakes safety document, assign shared blame, or take a different position? Identify the implicit rule or rules she is running—distinguish at least two plausible candidates, such as domain-sensitive directness, cultural-frame priority, autonomy-first universalism, or pragmatic-success consequentialism.
