Identify the misconceptions this audience will bring or form—such as the belief that pictures are merely decorative illustrations of words, or that a pictogram removes all interpretive labour—and state precisely where in the sequence each misconception should be intercepted. Provide one brief diagnostic question that would show whether they have understood the logical problem rather than simply finding the idea charming. Finally, list what should be explicitly set aside at this level and named as omitted. Please reply in American English, though the prompt is composed in British English.
