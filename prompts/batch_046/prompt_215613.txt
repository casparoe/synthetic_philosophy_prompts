(2) I also accept Hume's epistemological point that we never observe necessary connection, but I reject his metaphysical conclusion that causation is nothing more than constant conjunction plus mental expectation.
(3) I reject eliminative materialism about beliefs and desires: mental states genuinely cause behavior, and this mental causation is not reducible to physical causation or to occasionalist divine action acting on a body.
(4) I think quantum correlations—specifically those that violate Bell-type inequalities—reveal objective modal structure in nature that goes beyond deterministic hidden variables and beyond mere constant conjunction.
(5) I want to defend the explanatory success of science without committing to intrinsic natural powers or deterministic laws; I think interventionist accounts in the special sciences track real patterns, but those patterns are ultimately grounded in stable divine regularity rather than in metaphysical necessity.

Do these hang together?
