I need a short memo reccomending we stick with a lottery for the full dose rather than mandatory splitting, with clear reasoning the board can vote on. Please take seriously the strongest argument on the other side — that we're effectively letting four people die who each had a real shot — and explain why that doesn't settle it. Keep it practical, no bibliographies or citations, just the recommendation and reasoning.
