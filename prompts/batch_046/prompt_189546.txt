I'm presenting a seminar paper Tuesday that tweaks Sen's liberal paradox. My claim is that the usual fix — walling off liberal rights into purely personal domains — misses that Pareto comparisons implicitly treat preference intensity as flat. I replace that with "epistemic stake," weighting how well someone can know an outcome for themselves. That kills the paradox in the classic book example but revives it in institutional settings like curriculum votes where stakes are lopsided.
