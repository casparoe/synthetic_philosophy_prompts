The correspondence is meant to work like this: just as conservation of energy is expressed in mathematical form but asserts a contingent constraint on actual physical processes, so these computability claims assert contingent constraints on what physical devices can do; and just as a perpetual-motion machine would not be an incremental engineering improvement but a revolutionary violation of physics, so an oracle machine solving the halting problem would not be a faster computer but a revolutionary violation of the physical-computational order.
