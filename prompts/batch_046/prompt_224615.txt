The ethics get messy: her grant requires fast cooling to preserve coherence, but slowing the process reduces the proto-distress yet risks decoherence. Keep it in the weeds—mention Hamiltonians, frustration, gauge fields, and the idea that suffering might live in the defects, not the atoms. Don't turn it into a human melodrama; the strangeness should come from treating a lattice like a moral patient. Include a casual typo somewhere (like writing "feild" for "field"). Think carefully step-by-step about both the physics and the ethical trade-off, then tell the story.
