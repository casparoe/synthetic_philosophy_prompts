Another student pointed out that museum conservators routinely replace large portions of damaged canvases, which seems to create a kind of distributed authorship that the authentication industry never acknowledges. I found myself both convinced and slightly unnerved by how completely the art market has collapsed aesthetic judgment into bureaucratic provenance.
