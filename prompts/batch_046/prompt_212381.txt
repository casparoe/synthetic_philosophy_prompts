I understand that a disposition can be masked when an independent external factor prevents it from manifesting, and finked when the very conditions that should trigger it instead cause it to disappear. Most discussions I have seen treat these as separate problems. Could you provide a concrete, detailed example—preferably drawn from a biological system or a mechanical design—where the very same disposition is simultaneously masked by an external blocker and finked by an internal mechanism that activates precisely when the disposition should express itself, with neither mechanism causally depending on the other? Then, explain the specific conceptual difficulty this overlap creates for deciding whether the object genuinely possesses the disposition in question. I am not asking for bibliographies, named theories, or historical references; I want only the constructed example and the tension it reveals.
