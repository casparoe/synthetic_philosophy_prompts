If so, specify exactly which terms are being used with different extensions — for example, whether "alignment" functions as a binary predicate versus a vector-valued property across representational, motivational, operational, and constitutional dimensions — and propose a disambiguated vocabulary both camps could adopt without altering their underlying commitments.
