Take Schrödinger's Vedanta-inspired position that individual consciousness is illusory and that there is only one universal consciousness, with personal minds analogous to localized focal points or nodes within a single continuous field. Restate that view in distributed-systems terms: treat minds as nodes, consciousness as network state, and the appearance of separation as an artifact of message-passing boundaries or replication lag. For each component of the original view, say what translates cleanly, what must be distorted to fit, and what has no expression in this vocabulary at all.
