I am writing a feature that asks not whether machines can think, but what kind of thinking was actually on display, and I need a rigorous conceptual analysis rather than a historical chronicle.

Please approach this as an expert philosopher of mind with a background in cognitive science. And be honest about where the taxonomy remains silent: can it capture the psychological dynamics of Kasparov’s play, the role of emotional pressure, or the social meaning of IBM’s victory?
