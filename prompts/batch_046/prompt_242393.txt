Please write the exam question suitable for a 90-minute sitting, a model answer of approximately 800 words, and a marking scheme for a total of 25 marks. Do not require bibliographies, reading lists, or citations of secondary literature; the question must be answerable from conceptual understanding alone, without dependence on memorised historical details beyond Burke and Chesterton themselves. Ensure the framing is specific enough that a student cannot simply recite a generic account of Chesterton’s fence, yet not so niche that it relies on specialised knowledge a well-prepared undergraduate would lack.
