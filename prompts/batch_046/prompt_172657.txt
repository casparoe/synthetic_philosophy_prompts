I need a reusable decision procedure grounded in the Herodotus/Thucydides approach — who actually saw it, who's copying whom, what's missing — but turned into a checklist the lawyers can run without me holding their hand. Apply it to two cases: a merchant ledger that moves settlement back twenty years, and a translated-only treaty claim where the original is lost and the translation comes from a 1920s editor with known biases. Include one result where the procedure gives an answer I won't like. Flag exactly where it misfires and where I still have to decide by hand.
