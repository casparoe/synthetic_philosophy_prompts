I sit on our institutes review panel for emergent computation proposals. A PI wants funding to build a careenium — a layered symbolic architecture where simmballs (self-indexing nodes) dont just map substrate states but allegedly push back and rewire the physical gates they run on. The claim is downward causation from symbol-layer to silicon-layer, not reducible to feedback loops. Half the panel says if the math works its just exotic control theory; the other half says it violates physical closure and smuggles dualism into circuit design. Draft the memo well circulate before thursday: recommend fund, reject, or approve with conditions, with reasoning the PI could actually respond to, and dont just dismiss the closure objection as mystical nonsense.
