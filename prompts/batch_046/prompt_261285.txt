The problem is classic unilateralist curse dynamics: if Site A offers it, patients at Sites B and C will demand equal access and our shared risk pool absorbs the cost, so one local decision effectively binds everyone. My co-chair thinks each hospital should decide for itself; I think we need a collective gate. Draft a short memo for Friday's vote recommending either (1) full site autonomy, (2) unanimous network consent required, or (3) a pilot with a mandatory 18-month sunset review. Make it actionable, take seriously the co-chair's patient-autonomy worry, and explain why the collective-action problem makes this different from a normal single-site clinical trial rollout. No need for citations—just reasoning the board can actually vote on.
