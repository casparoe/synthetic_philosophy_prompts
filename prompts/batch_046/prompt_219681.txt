Because the layer is shared, any global filter setting affects all three. This seems to recreate the paradox with an indifferent third party and a technological externality, but I am unsure whether the addition of C genuinely resolves the tension or merely obscures it.

The exchange should contain genuine back-and-forth: each speaker should answer the other’s specific argument rather than talk past it. Keep the reasoning rigorous but accessible to someone with my background, and do not include bibliographies, citations, or lists of further reading.
