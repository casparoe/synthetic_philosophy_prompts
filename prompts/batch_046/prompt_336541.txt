Write a deadpan satirical internal memo, approximately 450 to 550 words, formatted as a bureaucratic compliance notice issued by the fictional Institute for Recursive Subjectivity to its administrative staff. The memo should announce the mandatory rollout of a new "Self-Referential Symbol Protocol" that attempts to operationalize the philosophical idea—associated with Douglas Hofstadter—of the pronoun "I" not as a label for a fixed inner substance, but as a strange loop: a symbol that refers back to the very system of patterns and processes that generates it. The satire must come from applying this concept with literal institutional precision. Require staff to file quarterly "Loop-Closure Reports" tracking how their first-person references have updated in response to changed mental states; mandate that every use of "I" in official correspondence be accompanied by a recursive annotation tracing its reference to the speaker's present cognitive architecture rather than to a permanent core; and impose penalties for "reification errors," in which an employee treats the self as a static object possessing the pronoun rather than as an emergent pattern constituted by it. Invent and develop one specific procedural paradox that the memo earnestly attempts to solve: the "Authorization Regress," in which a staff member must deploy the symbol "I" to sign the document that administratively defines the conditions under which that same staff member may legitimately deploy "I," generating an infinite loop of self-authorization. Resolve it, in the memo's deadpan voice, by introducing a fictional "Meta-I" supplementary form that temporarily suspends the loop through bureaucratic fiat. Throughout, maintain strict deadpan consistency—no winking, no explicit acknowledgment that the premise is absurd—so that the irony remains legible through exaggeration alone. The philosophical mechanics should be rendered fairly: emphasize the absence of a central homunculus, the feedback between symbol and substrate, and the distinction between the pattern and the material that instantiates it. Do not ask for citations, bibliographies, or references to real texts; all institutional terminology, forms, and procedural rules should belong to the invented framework. The target of the satire is the administrative impulse to reify and regulate subjective phenomena that resist objectification, treated through the specific lens of self-referential identity.
