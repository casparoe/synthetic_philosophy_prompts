case one — cruise ship sinking, 40 people, lifeboat holds 15. random lottery feels right, but i also think "women and children first" is okay, while "ticket holders first" feels corrupt. case two — same headcount but it's a famine, food for 15 for a year, not immediate death. here i think prioritizing kids and people who can farm is okay, which i wouldn't accept on the ship. case three — a sealed bunker, air for 15 out of 30, and one person is the only one who knows how to repair the ventilation. saving her feels okay, but saving the bunker owner's relatives feels wrong, even if he built it. case four — two villages on one river, upstream has surplus, downstream is dry and also dumping waste upstream. i think sharing water is required, but if they're actively poisoning the source i think cutting them off is okay, which doesn't feel like case one at all.
