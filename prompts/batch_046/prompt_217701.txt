In your example, show how this staggered diffusion produces individuals with identical birth years who nevertheless fall into contradictory cohort memberships depending on whether one prioritises chronological age, experiential exposure, or institutional categorisation. Then, explain why this case illustrates a conceptual problem for the realist about generations — particularly regarding the principles of kind-individuation and non-arbitrary boundary conditions — without appealing to bibliographies or named theories that I could not independently verify.
