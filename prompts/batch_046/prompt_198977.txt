(iii) I'm anti-reductionist about social explanation: institutional patterns and shared meanings have explanatory power that you can't recover just by adding up individual psychology.

(v) I reject both Marxist base-superstructure determinism and Weberian idealism; I think material conditions and cultural forms co-evolve, with neither having permanent priority.
