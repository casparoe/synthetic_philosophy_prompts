Conversely, assess whether the endurantist's claim that one whole object is fully present at each moment forces her to deny that any essential physical parts can change, and if so, how she might accommodate the biological reality of total turnover without abandoning her core thesis. In your comparison, determine which framework offers the more coherent account of personal identity in this scenario, and present the strongest available reply on behalf of the view you ultimately reject.
