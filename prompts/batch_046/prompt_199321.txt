I've been studying Blaise Pascal's fragments on justice and force, particularly the idea that justice without power is helpless, while power without justice becomes tyrannical. This makes me wonder: if justice can only function when it is backed by some coercive mechanism, then isn't the actual content of justice determined entirely by whoever controls that mechanism? For instance, if an online platform's moderation algorithm is the only enforceable mechanism governing speech in that space, doesn't Pascal's framework imply that whatever the algorithm classifies as just is, by definition, just? In other words, doesn't the necessary dependence of justice on force collapse the distinction between them and make Pascal a kind of proto-realist about might making right?
