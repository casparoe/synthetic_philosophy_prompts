Or maybe it's not about formal decision theory at all, and we're just disagreeing about whether moral obligations can stretch across modal distance. Is this a verbal dispute about what "cooperation" even means without interaction? A hidden formal question about how to weight logical vs causal dependancies? Or bedrock intuitions about whether you can owe something to a version of yourself you'll never meet?
