Address the objection that this transforms the case into a standard Ulysses-and-the-sirens precommitment problem, and explain why the hitchhiker case remains distinct—specifically, that the need for opacity reveals rational irrationality to depend on strategic self-ignorance rather than on brute causal constraint. Develop the reasoning conceptually, without appeals to specific empirical studies, bibliographic recommendations, or citation lists; I want to see whether the argument holds on its own terms.
