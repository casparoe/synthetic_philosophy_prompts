Have them actually respond — Rin should ask Jory what he'd accept as dissolving the problem rather than relocating it, and Jory should press Rin on whether her view makes consciousness predictable in principle or just grammatically tidy. Both equally sharp, no strawmen. Keep it under 300 words. Open with a one-sentence bottom line: is this disagreement substantive or mostly verbal?
