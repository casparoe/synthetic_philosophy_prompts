Could you explain, in conceptually rigorous terms, where the line between assuming misalignment and presupposing alignment truly lies, and identify the precise conditions under which an oversight structure ceases to function as external constraint and becomes instead an internal shaping force? To ground this, I should welcome a hypothetical illustration—perhaps involving an adversarial evaluation loop—that demonstrates how the identical structural feature can operate as pure control under one set of designer assumptions about the agent's mental state, and as covert alignment training under another.
