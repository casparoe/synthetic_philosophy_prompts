We're making a desicion on whether to approve, reject, or conditionally approve the curators' proposal for our new permanent exhibition, "The Weight of What Happened." The proposal forces us to pick a dominant emotional framework: either an "integrative guilt" protocol, where specific perpetrators are named, acts are catalogued, and repair is tied to individual acknowledgment, or a "dispersive shame" protocol, where no individual is singled out, the community carries a shared atmospheric dishonor, and the exhibit relies on ambient shadow and sound instead of testimony.
