The complainants counter that Beta is a different paper altogether, and that presenting it without disclosure misrepresents the scholarly record regardless of historical connection.

Our board policy prohibits "duplicate publication of substantially the same work" but offers no definition of "same work." The chair has asked me to draft a short written opinion—no more than about 800 words—for circulation before next Tuesday's vote.
