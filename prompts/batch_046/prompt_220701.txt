(6) I hold that if a system is genuinely value-misaligned, then regardless of how strong our controls are, deployment introduces a non-zero tail risk of strategic escape that makes the decision ethically impermissible.

Go through these and tell me which pairs are in genuine tension, which only appear to be in tension, and how severe each genuine tension is. For each tension, identify the cheapest revision that would restore consistency and what that revision costs me conceptually. Then rank the tensions by severity, and tell me which single commitment, if revised, would clean up the most conflicts. Be specific about whether the problem is in my deployment stance, my conceptual taxonomy, or my risk assessment.
