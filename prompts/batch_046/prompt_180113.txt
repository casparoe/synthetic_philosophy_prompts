I hold a version of the thirder view on the Sleeping Beauty problem that treats awakening not as a bare self-locating update but as the generation of a distinct epistemic subject within a branching protocol. On this account, the 1/3 credence arises from weighting worlds by the number of distinct subjects they contain, so the dispute with halfers is partly about diachronic personal identity under branching rather than about pure probabilistic updating. What would count against this?
