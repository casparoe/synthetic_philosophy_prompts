I am preparing a graduate seminar presentation on the durability of Marxist value theory in an era of extreme automation, and I need to anticipate how Marx himself might have reasoned about technological configurations that emerged long after 1883. Specifically, predict Karl Marx’s likely assessment of two intertwined posthumous developments: first, the rise of lights-out manufacturing—production environments in which direct human labor is reduced to near zero and operations are governed entirely by integrated algorithmic control systems; and second, the proliferation of algorithmic management in logistics, warehousing, and platform-based service work, where remaining human labor is directed, evaluated, and disciplined through automated protocols rather than through human supervisors. Ground your prediction in Marx’s actual framework: invoke the distinction between formal and real subsumption of labor under capital, his analysis in the Grundrisse of machinery as crystallized past labor dominating the living laborer, and his argument regarding the tendency of the rate of profit to fall. Would Marx see near-total automation as the logical culmination of capital’s drive to minimize necessary labor time, thereby sharpening the contradiction between value production and use-value production? Or might he contend that capital adapts by shifting surplus extraction toward circulation, data extraction, and the algorithmic governance of labor that has not been eliminated? In your answer, distinguish carefully between manufacturing automation, which removes the labor process itself, and algorithmic management, which retains labor but transforms its mode of subordination. Please state explicitly what would weaken or overturn your prediction: identify which interpretive choice in reading Marx—concerning, for example, the boundaries of the capitalist mode of production, the indispensability of living labor to value creation, or the role of scientific knowledge in production—would yield a substantially different conclusion, and specify what theoretical or textual consideration would prompt you to revise your view of his likely stance.
