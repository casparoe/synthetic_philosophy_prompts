You are facilitating an adversarial collaboration between two decision theorists who have long disagreed about the absent-minded driver problem, and they have asked you to produce the single document both could sign. The second party, the Ex-Ante Planning camp, maintains that rationality applies to entire contingent strategies chosen before execution, so that the driver should adopt and adhere to the plan of passing the first exit and leaving at the second; they argue that applying backward induction inside an information set that the agent cannot distinguish from an earlier node mistakes a single execution for two independent decision points and thereby undermines the very planning that makes her better off.
