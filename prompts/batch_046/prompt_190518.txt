Include one concrete analogy drawn from law, art, or constitutional governance that makes the command-versus-precedent distinction intelligible to family members without reducing it to a joke or a lecture. Keep the tone affectionate and personal; do not resolve the tension with a platitude. Do not cite philosophers, texts, or legal cases by name, and do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations.
