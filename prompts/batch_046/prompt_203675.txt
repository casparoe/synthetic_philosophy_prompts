Schopenhauer held that music is unique among the arts because it does not represent Ideas but directly objectifies the Will—the inner nature of reality—independent of the principle of sufficient reason and without mediation by a representing subject. Consider contemporary generative audio systems that synthesize original symphonic works without any conscious composer, producing harmonic structures that do not exist in nature and that violate the statistical patterns of their training data through adversarial perturbation in latent representation space. Predict how Schopenhauer would evaluate such outputs: would he treat them as the Will expressing itself directly through mathematical regularities, bypassing individual human genius altogether, or would he argue that without a suffering, embodied subject behind the sound, they remain mere mechanical representations—elaborate clockwork rather than art? Address in particular whether out-of-distribution structures produced this way could be interpreted as the Will breaking through its own phenomenal patterns, and relate your answer to his distinction between genius and talent.
