A pragmatic encroacher might say that the knowledge relation itself fails in the high-stakes case, while a contextualist might say that the term “knows” expresses a stricter relation in that conversational context. Now add a reflexive twist: the reviewer is herself being evaluated by her institution on whether she correctly flags methodological errors, which means her own practical stakes depend on whether she attributes knowledge or withholds it. This generates a loop in which the conditions for attributing knowledge seem to depend on conditions that are themselves affected by the attribution.
