I am preparing a seminar defense for a thesis on standing to blame and hypocrisy. I argue that hypocrisy undermines standing not through any general failure of moral consistency, but only through what I call structural congruence—when the blamer participates in the very institutional or practical framework that generates the fault they condemn, irrespective of comparative severity or subjective motivation. For instance, I claim that a wealthy individual who evades taxes may retain standing to blame a poor shoplifter for theft, whereas a corporation that actively lobbies for environmental deregulation lacks standing to blame a competitor for identical regulatory violations.
