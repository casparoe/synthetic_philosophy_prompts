anyway, can you comment on whether the ML analogy actually illuminates anything, or if its just dressing up old metaphysics in tech jargon? also, is there a hidden contradiction in saying reasons are both free-floating and action-guiding? and do you think im missing a more obvious objection from the agent-relative camp?
