ok so i'm trying to get my quine-adjacent moral naturalism straight for a paper and i want to check if im actually being consistent or just cobbling together vibes. here's where i am:

(1) moral values are just part of our web of belief, continuous with science, no first-philosophy foundation for them.
(2) we should only be ontologically committed to moral properties if they're indispensable to our best explanatory theory of human behavior — like quine's criterion for numbers but applied to ethics.
(3) basic deontic notions like wrongness pass that test, but thick concepts like cruelty or cowardice don't, so i reject the latter as real properties.
(4) since quine killed the analytic/synthetic distinction, there's no principled boundary between revising a moral belief and revising an empirical one — it's all holistic.
(5) but in practice our core moral judgments (e.g., don't torture) sit near the center of the web and are protected by loads of auxilliary beliefs, so they're way harder to revise than peripheral ones.
(6) evolutionary debunking arguments fail because if they undermine moral belief they also undermine the scientific beliefs that power the debunking — uniform naturalism.
(7) reflective equilibrium is still the right method, but it's tracking coherence with the rest of our web, not correspondence to mind-independent moral facts.

do these hang together? specifically im worried that (2) and (3) are pulling in different directions — quine's indispensability argument doesn't usually let you split hairs between thin and thick moral properties unless you've smuggled in some analytic distinction you said you rejected in (4). also (5) and (4) look like they clash but maybe that's just apparent? and (6) with (7) — if there's no special moral knowledge, what is reflective equilibrium actually achieving that ordinary scientific coherence isn't?

rank the tensions by severity, tell me which one has to go if i want to keep the core naturalism, and don't just list them all as equally bad. also is there a hidden tension im not seeing?
