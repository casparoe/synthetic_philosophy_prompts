Have them actually respond to each other: Maya should address the two-tier worry head-on, and Jonah should answer her claim that leaving the files untouched is its own kind of violence. Keep both voices sharp and fair, write it casual like they're talking over coffee, and don't stress about being polished — a typo or two is totally fine.
