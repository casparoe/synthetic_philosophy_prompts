She also argues that recent quantum-feedback extensions of the Szilard-engine thought experiment support her correlation-cost framework more naturally than they support the erasure framework. Draft six to eight interview questions that press her position rather than asking her to restate it. Include at least one question that forces her to specify an empirical result in quantum thermodynamics that could distinguish her correlation-cost view from Landauer's, one that challenges whether treating information as modal makes the demon's knowledge too subjective to ground an objective entropy increase, and one that asks what finding would cause her to abandon the view. For each of these three, provide a brief follow-up keyed to the answer she is most likely to give.
