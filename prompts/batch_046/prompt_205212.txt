I have been working through the philosophical literature on mechanical rigidity and free will, particularly the idea of sphexishness—the tendency of an agent to execute fixed, context-blind behavioral loops regardless of environmental change, drawn from the stereotyped burrow-inspection ritual of the sphex wasp. Most treatments present sphexishness as a global property: an agent is either uniformly rigid or not, with free will understood negatively as the absence of such rigidity across the board. I want to develop a more granular framing, which I will call patchwork sphexishness: the thesis that rigidity can be domain-specific, so that an agent runs sphex-like subroutines in some contexts—say, habitual emotional responses or automated professional protocols—while retaining genuinely flexible, reason-responsive deliberation in others, with no necessary cross-domain coherence.

Please describe the most important implications of this patchwork conception for the free will debate. Specifically, address how it interacts with compatibilist accounts that identify freedom with responsiveness to reasons rather than with absence of causal determination; whether it undermines the intuition that free will requires global flexibility rather than merely local pockets of it; and what it implies for moral responsibility if a harmful action originates in an isolated sphex-module that is genuinely decoupled from the agent's reflective capacities. I also want you to consider whether patchwork sphexishness makes the free will problem easier—by localizing determinism to non-responsible subroutines—or harder, by forcing us to decide which domain of the agent is the "real" source of responsibility.

In giving this analysis, please be disciplined about where your reasoning is weakest. Identify the specific argument or empirical consideration—perhaps a demonstrated case of cross-domain interference between rigid and flexible modules, or a philosophical principle that moral responsibility requires global coherence of character—that would cause you to reject the patchwork framing and revert to either a fully global or a fully eliminativist view of sphexishness.
