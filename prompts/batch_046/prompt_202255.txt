She thinks a billionaire giving away half her net worth to climate mitigation is admirable but not demanded. And she insists a doctor who spends every vacation for a decade doing free surgery in conflict zones is being saintly, not just clocking overtime.

Do you think she'll say a software engineer — not on the security team, not on the clock — who spends twenty minutes on a Sunday fixing a critical open-source flaw that'd effect thousands is doing more than she has to? And what about a billionaire who publically pledges to give away 90% of her inheritence, then actually does it?
