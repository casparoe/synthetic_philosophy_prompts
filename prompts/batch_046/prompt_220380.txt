Write a pantoum—stanzas of four lines in which the second and fourth lines of each stanza become the first and third lines of the next—about the absent-minded driver paradox. Select two recurring lines to carry the formal repetition: one articulating the driver’s ex ante rational plan (for example, “Pass A, then take B, and never reach C”), the other articulating his ex post epistemic position (for example, “This intersection must be A”). Allow these lines to shift subtly in punctuation, syntax, or grammatical person with each recurrence, so that the pantoum’s mechanical repetition enacts the very failure of self-observation that drives the paradox. In the opening stanza, lay out the payoff structure with precision: exit A offers a safe but modest reward; B offers the optimal reward but is missed if he takes A; C, reached by missing B, is catastrophic. Use the middle stanzas to develop the decision-theoretic tension: rationality requires randomizing at A—taking B with positive probability—yet if he actually arrives at B, the logic of the plan demands he continue, because from his first-person perspective he cannot distinguish B from A and must assume he has not yet executed the randomization. Make the epistemic crux explicit: the driver lacks transparent access to his own prior actions, so common knowledge of his rationality collapses at the level of private belief, and the iterative “I know that I know” structure that supports strategic reasoning in standard game theory never closes. In the final quatrain, let all four lines lock into repetition, placing the plan-line and the belief-line side by side without resolving them; the poem should end on the unresolved contradiction that the rational strategy prescribes an action which, once taken, undermines its own justification. Render the logic clearly enough that a reader familiar with the standard decision-theoretic formulation would recognize it, but write the verse so it requires no prior knowledge and carries no citations, attributions, or bibliographic apparatus.
