(8) So taken seriously, externalism makes me permanently ignorant of the contents of my own mind across public language, social institutions, and even internal cognitive mechanisms.
(9) But that conclusion is absurd.
(10) Therefore externalism is false, at least for social/institutional contents and probably for natural kinds too.
