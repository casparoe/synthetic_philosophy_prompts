- a dad who smoked for decades but quit now telling his kid not to start: he has standing, maybe even extra because he knows the cost.
- a smoker bumming a cig outside a bar telling someone else to put theirs out: no standing, total hypocrite.
- a prof who plagiarized in grad school (never caught) now failing a student for copying a paragraph: this one messes with me. part of me says institutional role gives her standing regardless; part says she's forfeited it forever in this domain.
- my friend who constantly flakes on plans telling me i'm unreliable because i cancelled once: no standing, and it's annoying.
