Grice treats this as conventional because the reversal is tied to the sarcastic tone, not to context-specific reasoning. So it's encoded, not calculated.

As for conventional implicature with 'poor but honest', the contrast is also calculated through the coopertive principle, specifically Quantity: the listener figures out why two traits are mentioned together. That makes conventional implicatures just stable conversational ones. Hence there's no hard line between them, and speaker meaning always aligns with conventional meaning.
