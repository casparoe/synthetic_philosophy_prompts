Write a proposal for a one-semester philosophy project (approximately 6,000 words) asking whether honor culture, dignity culture, or victimhood culture can claim moral superiority—not through comparison of surface norms, but through each framework’s structural capacity to sustain asymmetric moral accountability: the idea that perpetrators and victims occupy irreducibly different moral positions that standard restorative or retributive mechanisms cannot symmetrically reconcile. State the precise question (for example, whether victimhood culture’s explicit centering of asymmetry makes it more morally adequate, or whether it undermines the reciprocity required for moral community), explain why evolutionary-stage accounts and simple normative comparisons fail to settle it, propose an approach that analyzes each culture’s implicit ontology of the moral agent rather than its explicit rules, specify what a successful result and a clear failure would look like, and give an honest assessment of whether this is tractable in one semester without original empirical research or dependence on sources that require citation. Do not include a bibliography, reading list, or requests for specific citations.
