I want you to restate Marx’s analysis of capitalist exploitation, specifically the purchase of labor-power at its exchange-value and the extraction of surplus value through its consumption in production, together with the four dimensions of alienation (from the product, from the production process, from species-being, and from other workers), using only the vocabulary and conceptual apparatus of mechanism design and contract theory. For each Marxian claim, indicate precisely whether it maps cleanly onto an existing economic concept (e.g., hidden action, contract incompleteness, a bargaining-set shift), requires a distorting stretch to fit (state the distortion explicitly), or has no counterpart at all in that framework. Do not provide bibliographies, reading lists, or citations; do not invoke obscure post-Marxist reinterpretations that depart from standard presentations; and do not frame the answer as a beginner’s overview of Marx. I want the translation kept honest—no smuggling in economic assumptions and labeling them Marxian—and I want the residue treated as the interesting part.
