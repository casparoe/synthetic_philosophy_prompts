Me and my partner are going through IVF and the clinic lets us rank embryos by more than just disease risk — they have these polygenic scores for predicted life satisfaction, cognitive ability, even anxiety risk. One of us says we should absolutly pick the one with the best overall profile, otherwise we're intentionally giving our future kid a worse start when we could've avoided it. The other thinks treating embryos like a menu of specs is wrong, that parents should just accept their child unconditionally instead of "optimizing" them, and that knowing you were selected for traits would mess up the parent-kid relationship. I can't tell who's being reasonable here. Is there actually a moral duty to maximize our future child's expected well-being through selection, or does picking traits like this violate something important about how we should relate to children?
