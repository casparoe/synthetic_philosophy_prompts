Provide the exam question formatted for a 60-minute written response; then provide a model answer of approximately 500 words that addresses the crux (why trends might still depend on unstated universal premises about human behavior), references the tension between Popper's allowance for situational logic and his denial of developmental laws, and considers one serious objection that a historicist might raise; finally, provide a marking scheme totaling 20 points divided across four specific criteria, with each criterion assigned a point value and accompanied by a one-line note describing the characteristic mistake students make on that point.
