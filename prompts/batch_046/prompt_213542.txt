Run it on three cases: a bridge that collapsed during a heavy storm, a warehouse fire that started exactly where two contractors had just argued about faulty wiring, and a crop failure that came right after villagers skipped a harvest prayer ritual. Make sure one of these gives an answer the board probably won't like. Flag where the procedre will misfire or push people to fake certainty, and please separate what's actually settled in al-Ghazali's occassionalism from what's genuinely contested among people who study the texts — don't present one interpretation as the consensus.
