At the ultimate level, only momentary dharmas exist, and each possesses its own intrinsic nature (svabhava). Because conventional truth is merely a practical fiction, diachronic ownership should be discarded entirely; moral responsibility requires only that present mental states have the right causal origins, with no need for cross-temporal relations.
