Others argue that protecting a vulnerable person from foreseeable violence is the deeper duty, and that a deliberately false timestamp is a permissible shield rather than a moral corruption.

I need a committed judgment, not a survey of options. Is altering the record morally permissible, impermissible, or required? Take a definite stand, defend it with reasons, and tell me explicitly what I should do.
