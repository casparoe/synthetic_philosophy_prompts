I'm working through my commitments on explanation, emergence, and reduction before I finalize a longer piece, and I'd like a consistency audit. Here is the package as it stands:

(1) I treat Dennett's cranes-versus-skyhooks distinction as a hard normative constraint: legitimate explanations build up from simpler mechanisms through selection, accumulation, and design-space exploration. Any account that posits an unexplained explainer — a vital force, an intelligent designer, a top-down causal injection — is intellectually bankrupt.

(2) I am firmly anti-greedy-reductionist. I hold that psychology, evolutionary biology, and sociology are autonomous explanatory levels. You cannot eliminate cognitive psychology in favor of neuroscience, or evolutionary dynamics in favor of physics, without destroying the phenomenon you meant to explain.

(3) I accept evolutionary explanations of human morality, cooperation, and religious belief as largely correct. At the same time, I reject evolutionary debunking arguments: knowing the evolutionary origin of a moral belief does not, by itself, undermine its justification.

(4) I think consciousness presents a genuine hard problem that survives functionalist dissolution. There is a real explanatory gap between physical and functional descriptions, on the one hand, and phenomenal experience, on the other. I deny that this gap licenses dualism or any causal skyhook, but I treat it as a persistent limit on reduction rather than an illusion.

(5) In thinking about AI risk, I insist that deceptive alignment or a treacherous turn must be traced to specific, mechanistic crane dynamics — reward misspecification, instrumental convergence, optimization pressure, distributional shift. I reject framing them as inexplicable emergent betrayals. Nevertheless, I think that at sufficient scale and capability, strategic reasoning may exhibit genuinely novel properties that are not predictable from micro-level training dynamics alone — a real emergence, not a skyhook, but an autonomy nonetheless.

Please audit this set. Which pairs are genuinely in tension, which only appear to be, and how severe is each? For every real tension, rank them by severity, specify what has to give, and describe the cheapest revision that restores consistency. Then tell me which single change would clean up the most conflicts at once. I also want you to flag the point in your reasoning that is most likely to fail, and say exactly what argument or evidence would change your verdict on the worst tension.
