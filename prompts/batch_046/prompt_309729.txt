Could you draft the joint statement that two opposing camps might sign after an adversarial collaberation on Gongsun Long's white horse dialogue and the School of Names? One camp — call them the Semanticists — holds that the argument turns on class membership and extensional logic: horse picks out all horses regardless of colour, while white horse picks out only the white ones, so they are not the same class and therefore the claim that white horse is not horse expresses a logical restriction rather than a metaphysical paradox. The other camp — the Ontologsts — holds that the dialogue is about metaphysical composition and the presence of universals: the form of horseness is instantiated in every horse but is never itself white, so a white horse is a composite particular that contains horseness and whiteness together, meaning the pure universal horse is absent from the white particular in a relevant sense.

They have argued past each other for years, partly because one side keeps treating it as a problem in the logic of predication and the other keeps treating it as a problem about whether properties are separable from their bearers.
