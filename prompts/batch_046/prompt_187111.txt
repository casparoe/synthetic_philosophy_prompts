Does Hume’s argument actually require this graded structure, or can it work with a single, undifferentiated uniform experience? More specifically, if a miracle were reported in a domain where the relevant law had only been observed in a narrow range of conditions, would Hume’s weighing procedure allow the testimony a different status than if the law had been confirmed across every conceivable condition? Please explain the passage’s logic carefully, and tell me whether injecting this graded notion changes Hume’s conclusion or merely adds an unnecessary complication.
