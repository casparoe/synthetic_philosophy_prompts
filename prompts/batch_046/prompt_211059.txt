Decompose your credence into at least three factors: (a) the probability that CKR plus Bayesian updating formally entails convergence to identical beliefs regardless of heterogeneous priors; (b) the probability that such convergence, when it occurs, suppresses genuinely justified disagreement rather than resolving it rationally; (c) the probability that bounded agents in real-world settings can actually sustain the infinite belief hierarchy that CKR requires without external scaffolding. Provide a specific number and a one-line justification for each factor, specify whether they combine multiplicatively, conditionally, or via some other rule, and state your final aggregated credence.
