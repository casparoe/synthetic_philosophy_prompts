Roughly half the audience are philosophers and legal scholars familiar with the Dennett-Caruso exchange on just deserts and punishment; the rest are family members, practitioners, and first-year students with no background in that debate. Please write the remarks in American English. This framing should do real work: it must determine what I can welcome people to discuss without either endorsing retributive logic or dismissing genuine victim grievances, shaping the actual invitation rather than decorating it. Do not include bibliographies, citations, or reading lists, and avoid references to specific page numbers or obscure secondary texts that listeners could not be expected to recall without access to sources.
