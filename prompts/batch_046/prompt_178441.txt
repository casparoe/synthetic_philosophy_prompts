Write an essay on whether G. E. Moore's account of intrinsic value in Principia Ethica treats friendship and aesthetic appreciation as belonging to a single axiological category, or whether they are structurally distinct goods that his doctrine of organic unity fails to separate adequately. In weighing the arguments, consider the following tension. Moore holds that both personal affection and the contemplation of beautiful objects are among the greatest intrinsic goods, and he maintains that the value of a complex whole may exceed the sum of the values of its parts—what he calls an organic unity. Yet there is a difference in how these goods are constituted: aesthetic value seems to depend on a relation between a conscious subject and an object with certain qualities, whereas friendship seems to depend on a reciprocal relation between subjects, each of whom is themselves a bearer of intrinsic value. One might therefore distinguish between "experiential organic unity," in which the value arises from the configuration of a mental state with its object, and "interpersonal organic unity," in which the value arises from the configuration of two or more agents' attitudes and actions toward one another. The essay should ask whether Moore's framework has the resources to mark this distinction, or whether it collapses friendship into a kind of private mental experience or, conversely, aesthetic appreciation into a social relation. It should also consider whether recognizing this distinction creates a problem for Moore's claim that intrinsic value is objective and non-natural: if interpersonal organic unity requires actual reciprocity, then the value of a friendship might depend on contingent empirical relations in a way that the value of a beautiful object (perhaps valued even when unobserved) does not. Reach and defend a specific conclusion—whether Moore ought to bifurcate his category of intrinsic goods along this line, whether the distinction undermines his broader theory, or whether the two cases are more alike than they initially appear.
