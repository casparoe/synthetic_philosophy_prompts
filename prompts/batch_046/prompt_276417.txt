Write an essay on the following controversy: has the modern popular revival of Stoicism—particularly as it circulates in corporate wellness programmes, productivity literature, and popular self-improvement culture—distorted the classical dichotomy of control into a mechanism of what might be termed 'agency laundering'? By this I mean the strategic reconceptualisation of collective, structural, or political problems—such as systemic workplace exploitation, ecological degradation, or social injustice—as purely 'external' and therefore beyond the sphere of virtuous concern, not in order to cultivate genuine equanimity, but to license individual disengagement while preserving institutional stability.

Weigh the principal arguments on each side with care. Defenders of the modern revival may maintain that the dichotomy has always functioned primarily as a psychological technology for emotional regulation; that separating what lies within one's power (judgements, intentions, and moral choice) from what does not (market forces, the opinions of others, or global events) is precisely what prevents paralysing anxiety and permits sustained ethical action. They might further argue that ancient Stoics themselves recognised a distinction between personal virtue and the outcomes of public affairs, and that applying this framework to mental health, professional resilience, and personal finance is therefore a legitimate extension rather than a corruption of the original teaching.

Critics, by contrast, may argue that when the dichotomy is severed from the Stoic commitment to cosmopolitan justice—the duty to regard all rational beings as fellow citizens and to labour for the improvement of the commonwealth—it collapses into an ideology of quietism. From this perspective, modern Stoicism encourages individuals to treat systemic harms as private misfortunes to be endured with serenity, thereby 'laundering' collective moral responsibility into personal acceptance and serving organisational interests that prefer compliant, resilient subjects to politically engaged citizens.

Reach a definite conclusion and justify it rigorously: does the contemporary revival preserve the ethical and political core of Epictetus's distinction, or has it been systematically corrupted into a tool of individual adaptation? Ensure your response is written in British English, and do not provide bibliographies, reading lists, or citations.
