I argue that this reframes Pearce-style abolition away from utopian genetic futures and toward an incremental clinical endpoint comparable to infection control. Include the one question I am hoping nobody asks—likely the one that exposes the tension between my harm-reduction framing and the evolutionary biologists’ functional account of pain.
