Are these jointly tenable? Does (5) conflict with the ex ante specification of intent that (1) presupposes? Rank the genuine tensions by severity, point out which apparent contradictions dissolve once distinctions are drawn sharply—particularly between analytical utility and policy sufficiency—and identify the least costly revision that preserves the narrow-definition framing. I need an audit, not a lecture.
