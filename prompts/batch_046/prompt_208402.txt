Write a short dialogue between Mara, a panpsychist, and Jude, an emergentist skeptic, arguing over coffee about whether "phenomenal interferance" solves the combination problem. Mara thinks micro-subjects don't stack like bricks but interfere like waves - their persepctives overlap in structural coupling, and at the antinodes you get macro-subjects that are literally the dense overlap experiencing itself. Jude should push back hard: either the micro-subjects are still there separately (so you've just got correlation, not combination) or they're dissolved into the antinode (so panpsychism collapses into regular emergentism). Mara needs a real reply, not just repeating the metaphor. Make them both sound like tired grad students - casual, sharp, neither a strawman. Don't ask them to cite anyone or mention Chalmers by name; just let them work with the idea.
