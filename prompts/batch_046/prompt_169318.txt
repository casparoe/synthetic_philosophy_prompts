I'm putting together my position on updating beliefs about indicative conditionals for a seminar on the Judy Benjamin problem, and I'd like an internal consistency audit before I present. Roughly, I've settled on the following package. First, in the Judy Benjamin scenario I hold that Judy should leave her prior over Red Company, Blue Company, and Headquarters completely untouched after she receives the radio message If you are in Blue Company, then you are in the second battalion — I take the message to be evidentially silent about her location, since it only structures the internal possibilities within Blue. Second, I think belief revision should always be executed through Jeffrey conditioning applied to the material-conditional reading of the learned sentence, with no need for specialized "conditional conditioning" machinery that treats antecedents differently. Third, I accept Adams's thesis: for epistemic conditionals, an agent's rational credence in If A then C should match her conditional credence P(C | A), and I think this applies regardless of whether A is currently ruled out. Fourth, I regard Lewis's triviality results as showing that anyone who combines Adams's thesis with probabilistic independence conditions on A and the corresponding conditional is in trouble; but I deny that such independence conditions are ever rationally mandatory, so I treat the triviality results as blocking a bad combination rather than undermining my view. Fifth, I maintain that correct Jeffrey conditioning on any material conditional that is false-antecedent in some worlds will necessarily redistribute probability mass across those worlds, which means unconditional probabilities of the antecedent should typically shift — this is not a bug but correct Bayesian dynamics.

My worry is that these commitments pull in different directions, but I'm not sure which tensions are real and which are only apparent. Specifically, I'm concerned that my insistence on keeping Judy's location beliefs fixed conflicts with my endorsement of uniform Jeffrey conditioning combined with the claim that such conditioning must redistribute mass across worlds where Blue is false versus true. There's also a potential friction between using Jeffrey conditioning on the material conditional and simultaneously holding that belief in the conditional tracks P(C | A) via Adams's thesis, since the two update paths don't obviously coincide. Meanwhile, my dismissal of Lewis's results might look like I'm trying to have Adams's thesis without paying the price, but I think the independence premise I'm rejecting is exactly what generates the conflict. Could you go through these five commitments pair by pair?
