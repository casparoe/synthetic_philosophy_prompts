I find this unsatisfying, but I lack the vocabulary to articulate precisely where the mistake lies. Could you analyse the philosophical structure of this tension—particularly the problem of accounting for knowledge of entities that are causally inert and non-physical—and advise whether there exists a coherent position that preserves the reality of mathematical knowledge without reducing it to pragmatic utility?
