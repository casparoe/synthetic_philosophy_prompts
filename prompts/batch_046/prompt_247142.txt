(1) In Newcomb's problem I one-box. Not because I fully buy evidential decision theory, but because I think what you choose genuinely correlates with the predictor's setup in a way that matters.

(2) In Parfit's hitchhiker I think you should pay the driver after you've been rescued, even though causally it's water under the bridge.

(3) In counterfactual mugging I think you shouldn't pay once the coin landed tails, even though the correlation structure looks identical to (2).

Which feels like it conflicts with (5) being timeless, since the timeless procedure should still care about the deal.
