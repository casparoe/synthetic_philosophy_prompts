The perennialist holds that pre-modern ethno-cultural substrates—shared ritual cycles, mythic genealogies, linguistic boundaries—provide necessary continuity, so that modern nationalism is better understood as a bureaucratic reactivation and formalization of older collective identities rather than an ex nihilo invention.

Specify exactly two cruxes.
