I'm not convinced the slogan actually licenses that conclusion, but I'm struggling to articulate exactly where the ambiguity lies. Could you lay out the distinct precise theses that one-liner could be expressing? I'd especially like you to distinguish the claim that judgments are necessary for emotion-type identification, the claim that judgments are constitutive of the emotional experience itself, and the claim that judgments are merely typical accompaniments — and for each, say whether it's compatible with the view that a panic-like bodily state without any explicit belief can still be fear.
