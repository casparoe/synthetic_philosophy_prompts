I spent much of last evening in a prolonged and somewhat exhausting argument with a close friend who works in evolutionary ecology, and I have been turning over one particular pattern ever since. We were discussing the Gould-Lewontin spandrels critique — the idea that many biological traits are not direct adaptations but structural byproducts of selection acting elsewhere, analogous to the spandrels of San Marco that arise necessarily from the architecture of arches. My friend defended several classic adaptationist explanations, and whenever I pointed to a trait that seemed like a clear spandrel — certain ornamental structures, or the human chin — he would concede that the trait might have originated as a non-adaptive byproduct, but then immediately pivot to arguing that, once present, it was subsequently refined or maintained by selection for its current function. This was not simple equivocation, nor was it the standard move of exaptation, since he was not claiming a change in function over evolutionary time; rather, he was partitioning the explanatory labor, assigning origin to spandrel status while preserving full adaptive significance for maintenance and current utility.

I see this same partition in the literature — authors acknowledge a spandrel origin in a single clause, then proceed with functional analysis as if that origin were irrelevant. Is there an established concept for this specific argumentative strategy, in which spandrel is deployed not as a wholesale alternative to adaptationism but as a localized, modular exception that protects the broader framework? I understand the original Gould-Lewontin critique and the concept of exaptation well enough; what I want is the precise carving of this division — origin versus maintenance, spandrel as compartment rather than replacement. If it has no established name, can you coin one with clear boundary conditions that distinguish it from ordinary evolutionary pluralism, from exaptation proper, and from a full spandrel explanation that genuinely denies any current adaptive significance?
