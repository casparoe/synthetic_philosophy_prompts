I hold that the special badness of human extinction cannot be reduced to the aggregate harm of individual deaths, but instead depends on what might be called the collapse of meaning-carrying continuity. Following a line of argument associated with Samuel Scheffler's conjecture about collective afterlives — the idea that present commitments derive part of their significance from being embedded in an open-ended human future extending beyond any single lifespan — I want to defend a stronger structural claim: extinction does not merely remove future beneficiaries; it retroactively undermines the status of present achievements as contributions to an unfinished story. Art, scientific inquiry, and institutional justice are valuable in part because they are links in a chain whose later members we do not control but whose existence we presuppose. If the chain terminates with us, the nature of our participation changes — not because we suffer, but because the frame in which our actions have meaning dissolves.

What would genuinely count against this view? I am not asking for the familiar objections — that nonexistent people cannot be harmed, or that welfare should be maximized regardless of narrative structure — since I have already set those aside. Instead, I want an honest map of the view's exposure. Which conceptual distinctions (say, between instrumental and constitutive value, or between intrinsic and relational goods) would have to fail for the structural claim to stand? What empirical or analogical evidence should move me: for instance, does the value of a completed work that will never be seen or built upon undermine the idea that openness is constitutive? Could there be a robust notion of terminal meaning — value fully realized in a closed temporal arc — and if so, does it dissolve the special wrongness of extinction or only relocate it to a different kind of loss? I also want to know whether defenders of this view risk insulating it: is there a principled line between the claim that continuity is necessary for present meaning and the weaker claim that continuity is merely one source among others? Rank the potential defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, indicate which ones a committed defender should concede as damaging, and assess whether the view has been qualified into unfalsifiability — and if so, whether that is a defect for a thesis of this kind.
