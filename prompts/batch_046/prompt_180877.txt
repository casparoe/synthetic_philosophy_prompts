I tentatively suggest that this achieves a primitive form of the temporal integration required for consciousness, though it still lacks cross-modal broadcasting and a unified point of view. From this I draw a cautious conclusion: consciousness requires specifically this kind of sequential, self-modifying integration, not mere information storage or functional role-reproduction—something standard books lack, but which the living-book case approximates without fully reaching.
