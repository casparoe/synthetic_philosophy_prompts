Structure the tree from the logical space rather than from a catalog of authors. Every node should be phrased as a concrete case or a yes/no claim that a student could answer without already knowing philosophical taxonomy. Please do not include reading lists, citations, or references to specific page numbers; I want conceptual architecture only, and I need this to remain fully usable for students who have no access to external sources.
