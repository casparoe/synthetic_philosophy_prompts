Some mental-health clinics now assign patients a "trajectory code" — a 5-year forecast of symptom patterns — and that code alone decides eligibility for supported housing. The code gets updated yearly based on observed behavior, but patients are told their code and often adjust how they report symptoms to match it. What theory of psychiatric classification does this presuppose? Does it assume Hacking-style looping effects are negligible, or that there's a "real" disorder sitting underneath the feedback loop? Reconstruct the implicit view of the self and of diagnosis, say whether the practice can stay consistent if we take looping seriously, and tell me what'd have to change in the housing-access rules if we treated the diagnosis itself as partly constituting the condition it tracks.
