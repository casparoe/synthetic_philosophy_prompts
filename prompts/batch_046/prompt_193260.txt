My phil of mind prof's takes on whether neuroscience kills free will, and shes been pretty consistent:
- Libets RP study: brain starts moving your finger ~350ms before you feel you decided, but theres that veto window where you can stop it -> she said this doesnt actually threaten free will, the veto saves it.
- Soon et al. can predict left vs right 10 seconds out with fMRI, but only around 60% -> she said still not determinism, so not a real threat.
- A study where they use a BCI to decode your movement and physically block it before you feel anything, and you report you never intended it -> she said yes, this does undermine free will, cause theres no point where your conscious self could do anything.
- Another case where your RP shows up but then you consciously redirect to a different finger and the RP shifts -> she said free will survives, conscious redirection is causally real.
