I am a high school senior enrolled in an independent study on philosophy of action, and I have become preoccupied with the absent-minded driver problem since our seminar discussion of it: the familiar case in which a person intends to drive home after work but, lost in thought, automatically takes the usual route to the office and misses the turn.
