As a software engineer with no formal background in philosophy, I serve on my state's Department of Corrections Reform Advisory Panel. The panel must vote next Thursday on whether to approve a pilot program at the Eastbrook Medium-Security Facility that adopts key elements of the Norwegian Halden prison model: officers wear civilian clothing rather than uniforms; shared cooking and dining spaces replace centralized meal service; disciplinary segregation is replaced by relational conflict resolution; and the architectural design follows the principle of "normalization," which holds that the deprivation of liberty is itself the full punishment and that conditions inside should mirror life outside as closely as security permits. Proponents cite reduced recidivism; critics on the panel argue that our state's sentencing statute requires punishment to serve an "expressive censure" function—communicating society's moral condemnation—and that excessive normalization collapses the distinction between a prison and a residential welfare facility, thereby undermining that expressive purpose.

Please draft the formal written opinion I should circulate before Thursday's meeting. It must recommend one of three concrete courses of action—full approval of the pilot with defined conditions, approval only contingent on statutory amendment, or rejection—with an explicit implementation or review timeline (for example, 90-day reporting intervals and a 24-month sunset clause). The reasoning should treat the expressive-censure objection with intellectual seriousness rather than dismissing it, and it should examine whether the normalization principle implicitly commits the state to a particular philosophical view of prisoner dignity or human capabilities that our current legal framework does not recognize. Include an honest, non-straw-man presentation of the strongest argument for the option you do not recommend.

After the memo, provide a brief structured summary as a JSON object with these exact fields: "recommendation" (the chosen course), "timeline" (the time frame proposed), "core_philosophical_tension" (the conflict named), "strongest_opposing_argument" (the best case for the rejected option), "conditions_if_any" (any safeguards or amendments required), and "implicit_commitment_identified" (the philosophical view the practice presupposes).
