Despite this variety, the literature appears to cycle through the same structural moves: one camp claims the backward induction equivocates between an objective informational condition and a first-order doxastic state of being surprised, while the other insists the paradox reveals a genuine tension between common knowledge of rationality and self-referential belief update.
