You are a senior philosopher of history advising a small interdisciplinary research group with three years of funding and no capacity for extensive empirical archival work. Some thinkers treat counterfactual robustness as a proxy for inevitability, yet this seems to conflate the stability of a causal pathway with the modal status of the outcome itself. Please prioritise the open questions that arise from this knot. Rank your choices, defend the ordering using explicit criteria—importance, tractability, neglectedness, and how many downstream questions depend on this one—and indicate what kind of intermediate result over the next three years would cause you to revise the ranking.
