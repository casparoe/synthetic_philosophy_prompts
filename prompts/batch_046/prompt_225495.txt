Hold constant across all three: behavioral responsiveness to harm, ecological embeddedness, and absence of linguistic capacity. Specify a single practical decision—whether it is permissible to terminate each entity to reclaim resources—such that a binary moral-status view must place a sharp threshold somewhere between (1) and (3), necessarily cutting through (2), whereas a gradational view assigns monotonically increasing obligations.
