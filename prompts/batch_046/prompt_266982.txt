Consider a recurring pattern in how capacities fail to manifest, distinct from the two familiar explanations. In one familiar case, a system possesses a tendency, yet the very trigger that should activate it simultaneously removes or destroys that tendency. In another, an independent blocker is present whenever the trigger occurs, preventing manifestation without altering the underlying tendency itself. I want to describe a different configuration that does not reduce to either.

Take a brittle ceramic engineered as an interlocking lattice of grains. Each grain, considered in isolation, has the structural disposition to fracture under stress. Apply macroscopic pressure: individual grains do initiate cracks, but each crack is immediately arrested by adjacent grains arranged to oppose its propagation. The bulk material does not shatter. Crucially, the grains do not lose their brittleness upon being stressed, nor is there a single external shield or masking agent present. The failure to manifest is produced structurally: the dispositions of the parts are arranged so that their manifestations interfere with one another.

A parallel social case: every member of a deliberative body is individually disposed to voice dissent when they perceive error, yet they are arranged in a hierarchy of mutual observation where each person's awareness that others share the same disposition suppresses the expression in all. No individual changes their inclination upon sensing the group, and no external censor is present; the inhibition is a systematic byproduct of the relational geometry of the dispositions themselves.

My question is whether this structural-cancellation pattern has an established name in the literature on dispositions, finks, and masks. Does it map onto any recognized variant—perhaps a distributed or collective form of one of the standard cases—or does it require a separate conceptual category? If there is no established term, how should we characterize it precisely: does the composite system genuinely lack the macro-disposition, or does it possess the disposition in some frustrated or counterbalanced form? And what boundary conditions distinguish this case from mere statistical averaging, emergent properties, or ordinary compositional failure? I am not looking for a bare label; I want to know where the edges of the phenomenon lie and how it relates conceptually to the standard taxonomy.
