hey, trying to audit my own commitments on sphexishness and free will to see if they hang together or if i'm just talking past myself. here's the set, more or less:

1. sphexishness isn't behavioral rigidity, it's representational lock-in — a system is sphexish if it can't represent its own current goal or policy as one option among others it might reject for reasons. so you can be wildly flexible in behavior and still be sphexish.

2. the kind of free will that's actually worth wanting is just the absence of that lock-in: being able to represent your goals or rules as contingent and open to revision.

3. i don't think this needs indeterminsim or anything spooky. a deterministic system could have it, as long as its representations genuinely decouple from immediate drive-states and allow recusive self-evaluation.

4. i think humans mostly have this capacity, at least in degrees — we're not wasps, even if we're not perfect.

5. but i also have this nagging worry that our "recusive self-evaluation" is itself sphexish at a higher level, that the architecture letting us revise beliefs is just a more complex fixed script running on evolved hardware. so maybe we're just flexible sphexes with extra steps.

6. despite that worry, i think we should hold people morally responsible because our social practices of reason-giving depend on engaging that representational capacity, regardless of whether it's ultimately "genuine" or just deeper rigidity.

7. and i think if we built an AI with real architectural openness — not just optimizing within fixed objectives but actually able to revise its own goal-structure for reasons — then we'd owe it some moral consideration, though i'm not sure it should be the exact same kind we owe humans.

go through these and tell me which pairs are genuinely pulling against each other versus which only look like they are. order the real tensions by how severe they are, and for each say what'd have to give and what the cheapest fix is. then tell me which single change to this list would clean up the most tension overall — don't tell me which one i'd be least attached to, i honestly don't know.
