And what about an Ethereum smart contract that deploys an upgrade-proxy to replace its own bytecode?

I'm less interested in flat yes/no answers than in identifying the distinct plausible rules that fit her four rulings, stating what each predicts for both the neural-network and smart-contract cases, giving your best guess at which rule she is actually running with reasons, and naming one further case that would best discriminate among the candidates.
