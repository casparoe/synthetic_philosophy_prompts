I am advising the constitutional drafting committee for a transitional federation of autonomous regions with overlapping territorial and ethnic claims, and I need to translate the philosophical literature on democratic boundary—particularly the paradox of democratic closure, the all-affected principle, and the problem of nested or overlapping demoi—into a recurring judicial protocol. The regional constitutional court will repeatedly decide whether particular populations qualify as members of the local demos for constitutional conventions: temporary labor migrants, stateless descendants of displaced groups, long-term non-citizen residents, or inhabitants of contested border zones. I want a step-by-step decision procedure that fixes the order of inquiry, specifies what answers are sufficient at each node, marks the points where judgment is irreducibly normative rather than factual, and states clear stopping rules for both exclusion and mandatory inclusion. The protocol must be usable by judges without philosophical training, yet it must preserve the conceptual distinction between being subject to law, being territorially present, and being an affected stakeholder—distinctions that often conflict in practice.

Please construct the procedure as an ordered checklist or flowchart in words, with explicit decision nodes and conditions for termination. Then apply it to three cases: first, seasonal agricultural workers present for eight months who fall under local labor law but hold foreign citizenship; second, a stateless community born inside the region but ethnically tied to a territory that straddles a contested border, so that inclusion in one regional demos risks double representation or effective disenfranchisement in another; and third, a scenario in which the procedure mandates inclusion that the current political majority will reject, requiring you to specify what institutional mechanism should manage that tension. Include one case where the answer the procedure delivers is one I, as the advisor, will likely find politically unpalatable. Finally, state explicitly what the procedure cannot settle—especially regarding overlapping memberships across nested assemblies—and flag every step that remains a value judgment despite being formatted as a test.
