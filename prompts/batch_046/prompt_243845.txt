We've gone in circles and its frustrating. My hypothesis is that we're smuggling in different conceptions of belief - one side treats it as an explanatory posit, another as a status requiring answerability for your mental states - and we're both calling it "the intentional stance" but asking totally different questions.

Is that diagnosis right, or is there a real hidden disagreement about what explanation requires?
