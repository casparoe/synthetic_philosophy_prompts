Identify any pair of leaves your branching questions cannot cleanly separate, and briefly diagnose any logically possible but unoccupied combinations of answers—are they incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored? The taxonomy should address this kind of structurally hidden, permanent underdetermination rather than transient empirical gaps.
