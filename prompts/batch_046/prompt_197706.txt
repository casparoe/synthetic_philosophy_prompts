By indexical moral anchoring, understand an architecture in which normative weights are tied to the system's own operational history, its sensorimotor constraints, and accumulated patterns of harm-avoidance, rather than being derived from a context-independent utility function or a fixed deontic rule set. Develop the reasoning without recourse to bibliographies, reading lists, citations, or appeals to specific named scholarly works; ground the case in first principles and in the structural features of embodied decision-making.
