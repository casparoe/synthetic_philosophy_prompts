Design a runnable ninety-minute classroom simulation for about twelve students in an upper-level philosophy of mind seminar that makes them feel the instability in treating qualia as private intrinsic properties, using a live adaptation of Dennett's Chase and Sanborn coffee-taster case but replacing coffee with a synthetic three-sample flavor sequence prepared in advance. The facilitator should create three visually identical solutions: sample A is a baseline, sample B is chemically identical to A but presented after a hidden adaptive primer that shifts contextual expectation, and sample C is genuinely different but only subtly so. Divide the group into Tasters, who receive the primer and then rate whether B feels different from A and must defend that report in writing before speaking, and Auditors, who skip the primer and must challenge any claim of intrinsic change by demanding a non-circular description of the quality itself without appealing to preference or comparison. The rules should require Tasters to sign a brief experience report, then pair with an Auditor for a five-minute dispute, then reconvene for a group vote on whether an undetectable intrinsic shift occurred, with the facilitator revealing only after the vote that A and B are chemically identical. The debrief should map each phase onto the conceptual problem: the initial confidence in first-person authority, the failure to resolve disagreement through introspection, the slide from describing a sensation to describing one's reactive attitude toward it, and the circularity of using changed liking to prove changed feeling. Be frank that the simulation cannot reproduce Dennett's long-term gradual drift across years, so the facilitator must supply that missing element verbally after the activity without breaking the flow, perhaps by describing how one taster slowly revised his judgments over time while insisting his standards had not moved. Predict that the first run will likely fail when students invent hidden chemical differences to preserve the intuition that they are tracking a real intrinsic property, and instruct the facilitator to treat that resistance as the central pedagogical moment rather than suppressing it. The response must be written in continuous prose only, with no headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables.
