Denying that event is real seems to require saying one observer's present is metaphysically special, but SR treats all frames equally, so that looks like a direct contradiction. So my position is: accept SR, reject a hidden frame, and you're stuck with eternalism—every event in spacetime is equally real, with no dynamic becaming.

I want a sorted list: what's decisive vs. merely awkward, what defenders of my view should concede, and whether I've qualified the position so much that it's unfalsifiable.
