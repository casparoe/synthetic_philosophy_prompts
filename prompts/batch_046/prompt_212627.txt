I hold what I call a full crane commitment: the view that every legitimate explanation in the natural sciences must be constructed entirely from lower-level mechanisms—cranes, in Dennett’s framing—without remainder, and that any appeal to irreducible higher-level properties is not an explanation but a placeholder for present ignorance, essentially a skyhook. More precisely, I take greedy reductionism not as a mere methodological preference but as an ontological constraint: if a macro-level regularity cannot in principle be reconstructed from micro-level interactions plus appropriate boundary conditions, then it does not belong to the furniture of the world but is rather a useful fiction. On this view, not only vitalism and dualist emergence are ruled out, but also certain robust forms of non-reductive physicalism in philosophy of mind, and some interpretations of downward causation in complex systems biology that treat top-down constraints as causally autonomous rather than selectively contextual.

What would have to be true for this position to be wrong? I want an honest map of its exposure: which observations, formal results, conceptual arguments, or developments in systems science would genuinely count against it, as opposed to being merely awkward to accommodate? I am especially interested in whether there could be a demonstrated case of downward causation that is ontologically independent rather than just computationally intractable—something that carries information unrecoverable from any micro-level account, not just practically but in principle. Would a rigorously established macro-level constraint that shapes micro-level dynamics in a way that cannot be redescribed as selection among pre-existing possibilities do the work? How should one weigh arguments from mathematical structures that appear to govern physical processes without being reducible to them? I also want you to address the risk of insulation: if defenders can always redescribe an apparent skyhook as a temporarily unexplained crane, has the view been qualified into unfalsifiability, and is that insulation a virtue or a vice for a metaphysical claim of this kind? Please distinguish decisive defeaters from inconvenient ones, and say what defenders of full crane commitment would themselves concede as counting against their view.
