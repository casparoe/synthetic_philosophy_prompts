Set out the most important implications of reconceiving victimhood culture not as a degradation of dignity culture, but as a separate moral ontology in which moral status is harm-indexed and recognition-dependent—obligations flow toward the acknowledged victim rather than emanating from an agent's intrinsic worth or peer-evaluated achievement—for the comparative moral assessment of honor, dignity, and victimhood frameworks. Specifically, consider whether this reconception renders victimhood culture structurally incapable of sustaining reciprocal obligations, whether it compels dignity culture into a problematic neutrality about harm, and what it suggests about whether a culture can rationally choose among these frameworks. Please indicate your confidence in each implication, and single out the claim you are least sure of, with a brief note on what would weaken it.
