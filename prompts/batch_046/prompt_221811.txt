- a twitter pile-on today where every account has a real name but thousands jump in and no one feels personally responsability: still the public, maybe even purer.

- an algorithmic feed that ranks "trending" from aggregate clicks with no human editor: this feels like the public stripped bare, even though no one is literally present.
