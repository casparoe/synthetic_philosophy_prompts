Greedy reductionism is when you use a skyhook to reduce a high-level thing straight down, skipping the middle layers - for example saying human behaviour is just physics and ignoring biology and psychology. Dennett thinks cranes are actually just greedy reductionism done slowly, since they eventually reduce the mind to genes. So reduction itself isn't bad; the error is using skyhooks instead of cranes.
