Write a dialogue between a Kierkegaardian and a secular ethicist about Abraham and the teleological suspension of the ethical. Don't do the tired faith-vs-reason dance. Have them argue whether the suspension just brackets the ethical temporarily or actually breaks Abraham's capacity for universal moral reasoning for good — can he still be a real father after Moriah, or is getting Isaac back just cosmetic? Both sides sharp, no strawmen, real back-and-forth. They should accidentally converge on something then split. Keep it casual, like grad students over bad coffee. There's thier claim that the knight of infinite resignation is basically a red herring here.
