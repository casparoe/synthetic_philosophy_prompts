I want you to restate this position, as faithfully as possible, in the vocabulary of distributed consensus protocols and Byzantine-fault-tolerant epistemic systems: map her distinction between factual truth and rational opinion onto a separation between shared-state maintenance and policy-level optimization; treat bureaucratic self-deception and institutional fabrication as malicious-node behavior that injects faulty signals into a network; and represent her idea that modern political lying targets reality itself—not merely particular adversaries—as a collapse of the consensus layer rather than corruption of individual nodes.

Then provide an honest residue report. Which parts of her argument translate cleanly into this framework, which survive only with distortion—particularly her normative commitment to human plurality, the irreducible contingency of action, and the role of narrative in preserving a common world—and which elements have no counterpart at all?
