Write the desiderata an accuracy-first epistemology must satisfy if it is to integrate epistemic utility theory without collapsing into pragmatic encroachment. The theory must account for how credal accuracy is measured when evidence is structurally ambiguous rather than probabilistically precise; whether epistemic utility is assessed ex ante or ex post relative to belief revision; how to preserve a distinction between epistemic and practical reasons for belief without reinstating a sharp fact-value divide that accuracy-first views reject; whether distributed or collective belief states—such as those in scientific committees—carry aggregate epistemic utility functions irreducible to individual ones; and which formal results, such as tensions between global and local accuracy maximization, are genuine constraints rather than artifacts of the scoring-rule formalism. For each desideratum, state the motivating case or conceptual tension behind it, flag which requirements are non-negotiable for an accuracy-first framework and which reflect camp-specific preferences, and identify any subset of desiderata that appears jointly unsatisfiable given standard results in scoring rules and social choice theory. Keep the weight on the criteria and their motivating tensions rather than on a survey of existing theories.
