The curator wants to organize it around three case studies — the Homestead Act’s land distribution patterns, the racial exclusions baked into the GI Bill, and the 1965 immigration reforms — but the conceptual hook is something they’re calling "counterfactual suppression," where the exceptionalist narrative only holds if you systematically ignore what comparable settler-colonies or liberal democracies did differently under similar conditions.
