Both sides hate dualism, both cite the same passages, and for decades nobody moves. Is this actually a verbal dispute about whether 'explanation' means derivation from physics versus identification of functional architecture? A hidden disagreement about whether downward causation is ontologically real or just epistemic? Or are they answering totally different questions—one about what exists, one about what scientists should do—while using one vocabulary? Diagnose it using what each camp actually says, and tell me what result or distinction would actually break the deadlock, if anything.
