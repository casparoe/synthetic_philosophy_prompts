Here is where I stand on whether symbols push the substrate around, using the careenium framework and the simmball case as my reference points. (1) I hold that symbolic rules in a careenium are causally inert descriptions: they never directly drive substrate dynamics, only bottom-up physical processes do. (2) I take simmballs to show that symbol-table lookups can gate substrate-level forces, so symbolic structure does push the substrate in at least some architectures. (3) I maintain that substrate independence is impossible for genuine intentionality. (4) I also maintain that this impossibility does not undermine functionalism, since functional roles can be realized without substrate-independent causation. (5) I claim the explanatory gap between symbol and substrate closes once we accept that all causation is bottom-up, even when mediated by lookup tables.
