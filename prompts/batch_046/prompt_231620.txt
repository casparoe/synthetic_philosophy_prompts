Meanwhile in France, state schools ban conspicuous religious symbols but still accomodate religious diets and let pupils take days off for Eid or Yom Kippur if parents ask properly. So religion gets treated like ethnicity when its festive or culinary, but like a dangerous ideology when its worn or practised collectively.

What theory of the public sphere, or of group identity, does this institutional split presuppose?
