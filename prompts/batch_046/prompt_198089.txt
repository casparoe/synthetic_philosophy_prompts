Some board members love it; others think its philosophically dodgy because it treats statistical birth brackets like they were real social kinds with actual shared consciousness. I need to circulate a short memo before tuesday's vote recommending either the generational layout, some seperate thematic zones, or a hybrid, with reasons.

Can you draft it?
