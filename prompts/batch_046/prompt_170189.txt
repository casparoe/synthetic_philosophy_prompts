Can you lay out the precise claims that sentence could be making — about the technical premises required (effective axiomatisation, arithmetic expressibility, consistency), about whether the 'blind spots' are merely practical or genuinely in-principle, about whether human cognition and legal interpretation actually instantiate those premises, and about whether the conclusion concerns impossibility or just inevitable gap? For each reading say whether it is a mathematical platitude, a defensible philosophical extrapolation, or a false analogy. Then tell me which of those readings is actually doing the work when the slogan is used to argue that rule-based AI alignment, or exhaustive legal codification, is necessarily doomed.
