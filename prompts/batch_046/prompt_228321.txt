I need concrete cases that sort them by what they actually believe.

Can you design a taxonomy or flowchart with maybe 5-6 branch nodes? Each should be a specific scenerio or claim someone can answer without knowing the philosophical name behind it. From there branch through cases involving career vs family, environmental preservation vs economic growth, and especially what I'm calling "value-overhang" situations — where one option dominates on every measurable axis but destroys the conceptual framework that makes the other values intelligble in the first place.
