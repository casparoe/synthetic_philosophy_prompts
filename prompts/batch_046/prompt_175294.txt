On this view, the “critical level” is not a fixed threshold but a density-dependent function of available resources per person; it rejects both pure totalism and pure averageism, yet it retains a lexical floor below which lives do not contribute positively to the aggregate.

Please describe the most important implications of adopting this density-sensitive prioritarian framing for evaluating long-termist interventions—such as climate policy, space settlement, or transformative AI development—that could affect both the quality of future lives and the total number of beings who exist.
