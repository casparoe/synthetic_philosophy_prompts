That suggests the model isn't just correlating symbols; it's constructing an internal representation of conceptual relations. So Hofstadter's critique, which targeted older statistical MT, doesn't fully carry over. The model still lacks intentionality in the strict philosophical sense, but the gap between correlation and understanding is narrower now. In fact, recent work on cross-lingual attention heads shows they track grammatical roles across languages, which looks like functional understanding even if there's no conscious experience behind it.
