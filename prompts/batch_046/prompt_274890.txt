Maya came back that Leo was mixing up explanation and excuse: understanding why he acted as he did is fine, but turning that into a moral pardon isn't, especially since he had peers who clearly saw the wrongness. Leo then said Maya was smuggling in a hidden standard—she only respects people who were moral outliers in their own era—which is just presentism in reverse, demanding heroes be ahead of their time. They asked me to referee but I honestly couldn't.
