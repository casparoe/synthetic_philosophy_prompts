It should land for people who actually understand cultural evolution isn't just viral marketing, but please don't do a generic philosopher-walks-into-a-bar setup - I want the humor to come from the evolutionary mechanics themselves. I'm writing this for a crowd that knows Dawkins but hasn't necessarily read Hull, so don't lean on obscure citations or reading lists, just use the concept.
