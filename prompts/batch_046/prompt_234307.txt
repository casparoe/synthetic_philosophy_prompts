She claims this revision preserves the predictive power of backward induction in finite games and resolves certain regress problems in hierarchical belief models, but it forces us to abandon the standard Aumann-style proof that rational agents cannot agree to disagree.

You are an expert in epistemic game theory and philosophical logic. Include at least one question on the implications for agreeing to disagree, with a follow-up keyed to her likely claim that disagreement becomes permissible but not arbitrary. End with a question asking what specific theoretical result or observed pattern in actual strategic behaviour would count against her view rather than just requiring further refinement.
