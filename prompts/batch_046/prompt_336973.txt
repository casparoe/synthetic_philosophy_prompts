Can you write a speech that lands for both halves of the room, uses wireheading to frame the workshop's purpose rather than decorate it, and closes with a concrete, non-obvious question about whether any externally visible reward system can avoid some form of tampering without becoming so invisible that it ceases to motivate at all?
