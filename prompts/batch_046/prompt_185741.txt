She argues that the strict age limits and wine-measure rules in Laws I–II function as experimental controls rather than ethical boundaries, and that the nocturnal council represents the formalization of this practice at the political level. Please compose eight to ten interview questions that go well beyond asking her to restate the view. The sequence should build in rigor, ending with a question about what empirical or textual evidence could genuinely count against her interpretation rather than merely being reinterpreted as another instance of calibration failure.
