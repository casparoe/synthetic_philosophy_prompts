I am a software engineer with no formal philosophy background, serving on my company's international research partnerships committee. We are reviewing a proposed cross-border collaboration on algorithmic fairness with a partner institution that has objected to our framework clause requiring adherence to "Western values of transparency and individual autonomy." The partner argues that "Western values" is an incoherent category used to mask cultural imperialism, and insists on substituting "universal human rights" or "shared liberal-democratic norms." Our research team defends the clause, claiming it denotes a specific, defensible set of commitments rather than a geographical label. The committee must recommend whether to approve the clause as written, strike it in favor of one of the alternatives, or approve it only with a definitional footnote. Draft the committee's decision memo recommending one of these three options. Lead with the bottom-line recommendation in the first sentence; then provide reasoning, with no preamble and without restating the question. In your reasoning, clearly separate what is settled about the coherence and usage of the phrase "Western values" from what remains genuinely contested among scholars who study it, and do not present any contested interpretation as if it were the consensus. Include an honest treatment of the strongest consideration supporting the option you reject.
