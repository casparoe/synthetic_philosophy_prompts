I'm moderating a recorded colloquium for a graduate seminar in AI governance, and the guest speaker is an unnamed researcher who defends a specific refinement of iterated amplification and distillation. She argues this preserves both alignment, since the normative anchor remains unamplified and therefore uncorrupted, and competence, since reasoning can still scale, and she addresses the scalability objection by suggesting that the evaluator itself can be decomposed into "micro-judgments" about intermediate reasoning steps rather than about final outputs.

I need eight to ten interview questions that press on the actual mechanics of this proposal rather than asking for a summary. I also want a question about "competence bleed": whether reasoning modules that grow far more capable than the evaluation module can learn to game the micro-judgment format without violating the literal consistency constraints. For each of these, include a second-order follow-up keyed to her most probable defense.
