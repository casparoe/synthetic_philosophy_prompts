I am working with Samuel Scheffler’s argument that much of what gives present lives meaning depends on an implicit expectation that humanity will continue, so extinction damages the living by severing that projected continuity even if no one survives to suffer. Among these three directions, which one should command serious philosophical work over the next decade, and which is largely a distraction? Defend the ordering with explicit criteria—tractability, how many other debates depend on resolving it, and moral urgency—rather than by importance alone. Answer in one tightly argued paragraph, hard-capped at 220 words. Do not provide bibliographies, reading lists, or citations.
