(2) Predicting an LLM's conversational trajectory—its tracking of plot consistency, character motivation, and logical entailment—seems impossible without treating it as holding beliefs about those very matters. (3) Therefore the stance's own criterion licenses treating these attributions as literally true of the model, not as mere heuristic fiction.
