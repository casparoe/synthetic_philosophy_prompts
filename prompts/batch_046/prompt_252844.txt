(1) i'm a totalist - maximize aggrigate welfare, not average. if a being has net positive welfare, adding it is good.

(2) i don't think Nozick's utility monster refutes anything because equality has no intrinsic moral weight. a being with 1000x capacity just needs 1000x resources, like a bigger bucket needs more water.

(3) but i also think there's a "welfare density ceiling" - past maybe 50x human capcity, extra welfare-potential doesn't generate proportionally stronger claims. it's diminishing returns, not egalitarianism.

(5) i also think agent-relative reasons are real: i can prioritize my kid over a high-capacity being, but only up to a point. if the welfare gap is enormous, my permission gets overriden.

(6) evolutionary debunking kills equality-intuitions but doesn't touch aggregation principles, since adding up welfare is just math.
