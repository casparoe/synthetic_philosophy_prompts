(1) democratic legitimacy requires that anyone subject to law L could have been the author of L.
(2) for 'could have been' to be meaningful in an instutional design, it has to mean there's a non-zero probabilty in the actual system that this specific person ends up authoring L.

(4) so career politics violates the legitimacy condition for almost everyone.

(7) therefore every feasable democratic system violates (1) for the vast majority of people.
(8) so either (1) is false, or all actual representative democracy is illegitimate.
