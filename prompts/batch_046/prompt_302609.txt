Apply the procedure to three worked cases: a patient refusing chemotherapy with the statement that God will heal without poison; a patient describing severe pain as part of a picture they inhabit but cannot fully see; and a patient asking whether I personally believe God is real. Include one case where the procedure directs a response that will feel pastorally uncomfortable. Present the answer as a flowchart in words, keep it to approximately 350 words, and do not include bibliographies, citations, or reading lists.
