Here is my opening challenge: a novelist's posthumous notebooks reveal she intended a line — "the door was never locked" — as a literal statement about a physical door. In the published novel, the line sits inside a sustained metaphor system about emotional access, and the literal reading requires ignoring three adjacent symbolic structures.
