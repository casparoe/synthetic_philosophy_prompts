Write a dialogue between Mira and Jonas, two neighbors arguing over coffee about whether their town should keep funding an AI system that reconstructs the voices of workers killed in a factory collapse ten years back. Mira thinks shutting it down violates our duty to remember—shes read Margalit and argues that forgetting the dead is like letting them die a second time, so the archive, even if imperfect, keeps them present. Jonas draws on Rieff to say modern remembrance too often becomes a therapeutic spectacle, and he points out the AI invents whole sentences where recordings are broken, so were not really remembering anyone, just listening to a machines fantasy of them. They should actually talk back and forth: Mira needs to explain why a partly-fake voice is still ethically better than silence, and Jonas needs to address whether letting the dead fade is truly just a natural ending or actually harms them in some way. Keep it casual—like real people, not philosophers on a stage—theres no need to sound like a textbook, just make them respond to each others actual points.
