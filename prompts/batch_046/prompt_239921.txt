I don't want a survey of every interpretation since 1950 or a bibliography I can't check; I just want an honest diagnosis of what kind of disagreement this is — verbal, substantive, bedrock intuition — and whether anything like a clear conceptual distinction or a toy model with simmballs could actually break the deadlock.
