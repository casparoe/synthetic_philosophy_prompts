everyone in my philosophy of religion reading group keeps comparing the fine-tuning of physical constants to a combination lock falling open - they say if the dials for gravity, electromagnetism, etc. were even a hair off, life wouldn't exist, and the odds are like randomly spinning a 20-digit lock and hitting the one combo. they use this to argue either for a tuner (designer) or for a multiverse with enough tries. can you audit that analogy? i want the correspondence table - what plays the role of the lock, the dials, the correct combo, the person spinning them - then where it breaks down. especially: does the lock picture quietly assume the constants are independant variables we could just "spin" seperately, when they might actually be coupled like gears? and does the conclusion - that something needs explaining - survive once you see the disanalogies? if there's a better analogy for thinking about parameter space and life-permitting ranges, suggest one.
