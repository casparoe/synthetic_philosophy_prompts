Keynes divides needs into absolute ones—food, shelter, basic survival—and relative ones, which are basically about status and keeping up with others. Once technology solves absolute needs, we only need about 15 hours of labor a week, and then we can pursue art, friendship, and direct enjoyment. He thinks relative needs will fade away once everyone is rich enough, since people wont care about status anymore. That lets us shift from "making a living" to "living well."
