Write a numbered section of substantive agreed claims—covering what Darwinian, Skinnerian, and Popperian creatures share and where they differ, and the minimal capacities any Gregorian creature must display—followed by a disagreements table. The table should state each camp's precise formulation of its own view (worded so that camp accepts it), list the cruxes (whether representational decoupling requires a new evolutionary transition or merely accelerates internal loops; whether error-cost asymmetry is structurally necessary or epiphenomenal), and for each crux record the empirical or engineered case both sides agree is relevant—for instance, non-human animals that construct demonstrably false spatial models before any behavioral testing, or artificial systems with internal simulation but no separate selection mechanism—together with each side's predicted interpretation of that case. Keep the claims non-vacuous, avoid bibliographic requests, and do not depend on specific page citations or publication years.
