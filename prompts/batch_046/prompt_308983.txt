I have just come out of a long, exhausting argument with a friend about whether positing qualia is even coherent, and I want to check whether the position I was defending actually hangs together. Here is the package I was advancing:

(1) I reject the Chase-Sanborn incoherence charge: I do not think that the combination of causal inertness and epistemic privacy makes qualia conceptually incoherent, since emergent causation can accommodate properties that are only first-person accessible.

(2) Nevertheless, I take the Quinean parsimony argument—coupled with causal closure of the physical—to be the strongest available case against qualia, and I admit I have no decisive reply to it.

(3) I hold that phenomenal concepts are demonstrative, direct-reference concepts, and that this explains the explanatory gap without any corresponding ontological gap.

(4) I think the explanatory gap is in principle unbridgeable: even an omniscient physical scientist who lacked the relevant phenomenal concept could not derive the corresponding quale from a complete neural description.

(5) I apply evolutionary debunking arguments to phenomenal intuitions: our confidence in the existence and specific character of qualia is likely a byproduct of adaptive self-monitoring rather than a reliable ontological guide.

(6) I reject the zombie argument's metaphysical conclusion—conceivability is fallible, and I deny that zombies are possible—but I maintain that if they were possible, that would indeed establish dualism.

My friend kept insisting these commitments pull against each other, but I could not tell in the moment which objections were real and which dissolved on inspection. So I want an audit, not a lecture: go through the set and tell me which pairs are genuinely in tension, rank them by severity, and for each say what the cheapest revision would be and what it costs. Also identify any tensions that are only apparent and dissolve once the commitments are properly parsed—particularly the interaction between debunking and first-person authority, if that is indeed harmless. Finally, tell me which single revision to the package would clean up the most tension at the least cost.
