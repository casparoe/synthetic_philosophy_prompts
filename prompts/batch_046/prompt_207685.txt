For a philosophy-of-biology discussion group I have been asked to write down my philosophical commitments regarding the ontology of life so that the others can press on them. Here is my profile. I hold that life is a natural kind rather than a purely conventional category: there is an objective fact of the matter about whether a given system is alive. I take autonomous metabolic self-regulation—the active maintenance of thermodynamic disequilibrium through internally generated mechanisms—to be the essential core of life, and I treat replication or information storage alone as insufficient. Consequently I deny that viruses are alive, since they are metabolically inert outside a host and lack any internal regulatory dynamics. Nevertheless, I maintain that engineered minimal cells that sustain autonomous metabolism are genuinely alive, even when every gene has been designed and inserted by researchers and even though their survival depends on a synthetic, human-maintained environment. I also reject sharp necessary-and-sufficient conditions for life; I think the concept has family-resemblance structure with genuinely vague boundaries, so that some borderline cases resist precise classification. In addition, I classify virophages—viral entities that infect other viral particles and replicate using host viral machinery—as non-living, despite the fact that they stand in a dependency relation structurally analogous to biological parasitism. Finally, I reject the standard working definition often associated with NASA that characterizes life as a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution, on the grounds that it is overinclusive and fails to exclude non-living autocatalytic chemical networks.

Please audit this set of commitments. Go through them pair by pair and tell me which combinations are in genuine tension, which combinations only appear to conflict but dissolve on closer inspection, and rank the real tensions by severity. For each genuine tension, specify how severely it threatens coherence, what the cheapest revision would be, and what that revision costs with respect to my other commitments. Then tell me which single revision to the profile would eliminate the most tension overall. Write the response in American English.
