I hold an interventionist-architectural account of natural kinds: a taxonomy carves nature at its joints not by tracking mind-independent essences or optimal similarity clusters, but by supporting stable predictive interventions across heterogeneous contexts. On my view, joint-carving is scale-relative in an ontological, not merely epistemic, sense—at scales where treating entities as members of a kind systematically enables successful manipulation and explanation, those joints exist; where no such architecture succeeds, nature lacks corresponding joints regardless of descriptive or pragmatic utility. So a kind like electron or species is natural only relative to the interventionist architecture that stabilizes it, and nature literally has no joints at regimes—certain macro-ecological or quantum-field scales—where every architecture fails systematically.

What would count against this position? I want the arguments, observations, intuitions, formal results, or empirical developments that should move me, sorted by how decisive they would be, including things that have arguably already happened. Specifically: what does this view predict that rival accounts—essentialist realism, conventionalist pluralism, or projectible-predicate accounts—do not predict? Are there cases where an interventionist architecture succeeds yet we are compelled to deny the kind is natural, or where intervention fails yet the kind remains intuitively joint-carving? What would defenders of this view themselves concede as damaging—perhaps a demonstration that predictive success is necessarily decoupled from ontological structure, or a concrete cross-scale case where successful intervention requires ignoring boundaries that this view claims are absent? Has the position been qualified into unfalsifiability through appeals to context-sensitivity or pragmatic success, and if so, is that insulation a defect for a metaphysical claim of this kind? I would personally find most persuasive either a rigorous formal result linking intervention failure to the presence of hidden joint-structure, or an empirical domain where the scale-relativity thesis collapses into either universal joint-carving or universal conventionalism.
