I want your own number, but more importantly I want the reasoning decomposed. Break the issue into these sub-questions and assign rough probabilities: (1) Do pleasures across these different modalities share a phenomenological feature beyond mere positive valence? (2) Does the heterogeneity argument illegitimately demand qualitative identity rather than functional or causal unity? (4) Would identifying a consistent neurochemical substrate, such as a characteristic endogenous opioid and endocannabinoid modulation pattern, provide evidence for underlying phenomenal unity or merely for functional convergence? (5) To what extent does the fatalness of the objection depend on the premise that hedonism requires phenomenal rather than representational or functional homogeneity? Combine these into an overall credence that the heterogeneity problem is genuinely fatal to hedonism as traditionally conceived.
