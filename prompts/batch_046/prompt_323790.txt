I'm working on a novel in which the protagonist experiences time as layered—past and future events feel as present to her as her current moment, not as memories or anticipations but as coexisting realities she can almost touch. This led me to study the Rietdijk-Putnam argument for the block universe, and I now find myself convinced by a particular version of it: not merely that special relativity makes a global present impossible, but that determinacy itself—whether an event is real or not—must be frame-independent. If observer A can determine that event E has occurred, and observer B is spacelike-separated from both A and E, then B's inability to determine E's occurrence does not make E indeterminate for B; rather, B is simply ignorant of a fact that is already ontologically fixed. From this I conclude that presentism, which ties existence to a privileged present, requires a metaphysical privilege that physics denies not just globally but locally—any attempt to restrict presentism to a single worldline collapses, I think, because determinacy relations cross-cut worldlines and leave no ontologically privileged slice.

But I'm troubled by the sense that this view insulates itself too well, so I want to ask what would actually count against this specific formulation. Not just the standard objections about the phenomenology of temporal flow or the claim that relativity concerns only measurement, but deeper defeaters. Would it be fatal if quantum mechanics turned out to allow genuinely frame-dependent ontologies—say, if a collapse interpretation could be rendered frame-relative without contradiction? Would it be awkward but survivable if determinacy turned out to be an epistemic relation tied to causal accessibility rather than an ontological one? And what about results from other areas—perhaps in philosophy of language about indexicals like "now," or in metaphysics about persistence—where this commitment to frame-independent determinacy might generate paradoxes I haven't seen? I want the defeaters ranked: which ones would force me to abandon the view entirely, which ones would merely require me to add qualifications, and which ones the defenders of this Rietdijk-Putnam-style position would themselves concede as genuinely damaging rather than dismissing out of hand.
