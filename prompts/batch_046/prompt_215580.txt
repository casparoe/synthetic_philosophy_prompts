He used it to push back against the idea that morality collapses without agents or some divine mind behind it.

Can you map it out — what plays the needle, the field, the navigator, the map? Then where does it crack? Does magnetism carry the right kind of authority that reasons claim? Is there a hidden smuggler — the compass-maker, the geologist — that the picture quietly imports? And does the antroprocentric conclusion, that reasons exist free-floating, survive once the disanalogies are on the table? Or was the compass carrying the argument the whole time?
