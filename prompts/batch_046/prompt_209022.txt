If panpsychists solve the combination problem by modeling phenomenal bonding as a category-theoretic colimit—so that a macro-subject is the universal object of a diagram of micro-subjects—then the subject-summing difficulty collapses into a formal tautology. Once bonding is defined by that universal property, both the quality-combination and the structural-mismatch objections seem to dissolve, since the colimit structure determines the macro-phenomenology uniquely. So why do critics still treat the combination problem as a live metaphysical obstacle rather than a solved problem in categorical metaphysics?
