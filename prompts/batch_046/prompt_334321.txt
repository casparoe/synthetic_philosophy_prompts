(5) theres no stable genocide-fact outside the institutional framework that applies the label—the law partly makes the crime, not just finds it.
(6) despite all that, strategic silence is sometimes okay: if labeling makes intervention less likely by raising political stakes, withholding the label can be morally permissible.
