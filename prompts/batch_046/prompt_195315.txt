I am preparing a handout for an advanced seminar on pan-sentience and its ethical implications, and I want a taxonomy—structured as a branching decision tree—that sorts positions not by the names philosophers have given them, but by the concrete metaphysical and ethical commitments a person must accept to reach each view. The tree should start from a single root question: does one grant that fundamental physical entities—fields, particles, or spacetime regions at the smallest scales—can bear negatively valenced experience? From there, construct branches that separate positions on three further axes: first, whether suffering aggregates linearly across micro-subjects or requires an integrated-information structure that only macroscopic systems possess; second, whether the morally relevant metric is total valence summed across all entities, or whether unified subjecthood acts as a necessary threshold for suffering to count at all; and third, whether the in-principle undetectability of fundamental suffering renders it ethically inert, or whether metaphysical possibility alone can ground obligations. Each leaf should be named if it corresponds to a defended position in the literature, or labeled "unnamed" if the combination is unoccupied; each should include one sentence summarizing the view and one sentence identifying its most awkward commitment, such as being forced to deny that neural integration is ethically special or being forced to accept cosmic-scale obligations that dwarf conventional animal ethics. The tree should also flag any pairs of leaves that your branching logic cannot genuinely separate—for instance, a view that denies fundamental valence versus one that accepts it but assigns it zero weight—and briefly diagnose any unoccupied combinations, noting whether they seem incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored. Please phrase every branch node as a question answerable without knowing the taxonomy's labels in advance, and design the instrument so that someone reasoning through cases—analogizing from wild-animal suffering to quantum-scale experience—is sorted into a leaf by what they actually believe rather than by which label they prefer.
