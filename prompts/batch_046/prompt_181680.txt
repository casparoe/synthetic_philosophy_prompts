Second, a narrative-normativist condition: the dysfunction must produce what she calls a ‘biographical rupture’ — a disruption to the subject’s self-directed life narrative that cannot be integrated without severe identity-cost or loss of agential continuity. She insists that neither condition alone is sufficient: a harmless biological deviation that leaves one’s narrative intact is not a disease, and a purely psychological crisis lacking underlying biological dysfunction is not a disease either, though it may be harmful in other ways.
