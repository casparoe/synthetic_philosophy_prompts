Case 7: A “Mirror-Loop AI”—an artificial system that continuously monitors its own internal states, detects inconsistencies with its goal-model, and updates its processing weights in real time to minimize divergence, all within a fixed architecture. Verdict: I think this escapes sphexishness and counts as autonomous, not merely mechanical.

Verdict: despite the subjective report, I think this person is sphexish—the loop is just concealed.
