You are a professional philosopher of metaphysics and institutional ethics with expertise in causation and normative theory. Two colleagues—call them R and S—debated over coffee whether a public-health agency's failure to update a water-contamination threshold (a pure omission) can properly be said to have caused a subsequent outbreak, or whether only the presence of the bacteria did the causal work. Think carefully, identify hidden premises first, and do not invoke outside citations.

R (move 1): An omission has no causal powers; causation requires a positive event with physical efficacy, and the absence of a regulatory update is not an event at all. The outbreak was caused by the bacteria, not by a missing line in a statute.

S (move 2): That conflates causal production with causal explanation. In institutional contexts, omissions are selected against a background of standing duties; counterfactual dependence is satisfied—had the update been made and enforced, the outbreak would not have occurred—so the omission belongs in the causal history.

R (move 3): Counterfactual dependence is symmetrical and therefore useless. Had the bacteria never entered the water supply, no update would have been needed; by your logic the absence of bacteria "caused" the absence of regulatory failure. Without an independent asymmetry, omissions are arbitrarily elevated.

S (move 4): The asymmetry is normative, not physical. The agency had an explicit mandate to review thresholds every five years; that mandate fixes a baseline state (the duty-compliant world) against which the omission is evaluated. Without that normative framing, counterfactuals run wild; with it, the omission is a genuine difference-maker in an interventionist sense, even if it lacks physical energy.

Please adjudicate move by move: rate each of the four moves from clearly mistaken to clearly correct, briefly justify each rating, and say who is right about which specific point and what both are missing—especially regarding whether an omission can be a cause under an interventionist account without being a physical producer. Before adjudicating, list the hidden premises behind R's physicalist constraint and behind S's normative-baseline strategy. Keep your response under 350 words, using one brief paragraph per move plus a short concluding synthesis.
