Every checkpoint I have to sign off on whether that patient is still the same person for consent, property rights, and family decision authority, and I need a reusable proceduer — basically a flowchart in words — I can run in twenty minutes without a philosophy PhD. Base it on whatever theory of personal identity actually survives gradual substrate replacement, but translate it into steps I can actually check: memory continuity, behavioral coherence, first-person reports, maybe something about causal chain of the replacement process. Apply it to all three. Also be honest about where this will misfire and what it flat-out can't do.
