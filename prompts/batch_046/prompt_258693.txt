Compare Ruth Chang's view that hard choices involve values being on a par with Joseph Raz's stricter claim that incommensurable values just dont share any common measure. I want you to dig into whether Chang's voluntarist fix—where picking one option actually generates a reason for it—solves the problem or just shoves the incommensurability back a step. If we apply both to someone torn between a life of artistic creation versus one of scientific discovery, where neither path looks clearly better, does either framework actually help them choose, or do they both just redescribe the same paralysis? And does Raz's view make Chang's commitment-based solution look like a kind of cheat?
