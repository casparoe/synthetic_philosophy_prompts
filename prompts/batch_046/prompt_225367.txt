Hey, so I used to work in physics labs for thirty years, then retired and started reading philosophy. I'm stuck on this argument I cooked up about weakness of will and hyperbolic discounting, and it feels like it proves way too much but I cant find the leak. Here it is:

(1) Weakness of will only happens if the very same agent judges that not-X is best, all things considered, and still does X.
(2) With hyperbolic discounting, the subjective value of a reward is R divided by (1 + k*d), so when the delay d collapses to near-zero the immediate reward gets a huge weight boost while distant rewards stay flat.
(3) That means at planning-time I weight future health high, but at eating-time the cake's value skyrockets and the diet benefit is still far away, so my preference ordering literally inverts.
(4) Now if practical reasoning just is applying whatever weights your evaluation function actually gives you, then the planner-me and the eater-me have totally different practical-reason structures.
(5) But if two stages have different practical-reason structures, they dont share a single normative standpoint; theyre practically different agents.
(6) So the eater isnt betraying the planner — the planner's judgement just isnt binding on a different agent.
(7) And that generalizes: any promise or resolution made at t1 binds only the agent-stage at t1, so by t2 theres no diachronic obligation at all, and you never recieve the benefit of your past self's plan.
(8) So weakness of will is impossible, and all long-term commitments are illusions.

That cant be right, but every step looks okay to me. Id like to keep (1) and maybe (5) if I can, but I feel like something slips between (4) and (6). Which step should I give up, and whats the cost? Does rejecting (4) mean I have to accept some "real" preference structure independent of hyperbolic weights? Or is the problem in (5) — do I have to allow that one person can house conflicting reason-structures without splitting into different agents? Is there a hidden scope shift I'm missing? Help me out.
