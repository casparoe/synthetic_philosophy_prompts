hey so im on this tiny committee that picks one public art piece for our town square each year. this time we have two finalists that feel like theyre from different planets. one is a sound installation built from recordings of the local river run through some algorithm that makes it sound harsh and grating to almost everyone, but a couple acoustic ecologists keep insisting its genuinely beautiful because it reveals hidden structural patterns in the water flow that you need trained ears to hear. the other is just a warm folk-song arrangement that pretty much anyone who hears it enjoys right away, but those same ecologists dismiss it as merely pleasant, not actually beautiful. now half the committee says we should go with whatever the community genuinely likes, since beauty is just what moves people, and the other half says thats wrong—there is a real standard, and the ecologists have a more refined sensibility so we ought to defer to them even if most folks hate the river thing. i feel like theres a deeper disagreement here about whether beauty is just in the eye of the beholder or if some people are just better at seeing it, but i cant quite name it. is one side actually right? and if im stuck in the middle, how should i decide without just picking the one i personally like?
