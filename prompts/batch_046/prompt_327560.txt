Users have wildly different ideas of what "flourishing" means: max longevity, present autonomy, religious dietary rules, or anti-paternalist refusal of any state-promoted norm. Half the board says B is required to avoid endorsing controversial conceptions of the good; the other half says B is just hidden paternalism, since defining the baseline and what's "reasonable" already smuggles in values. Draft the board's decision memo for Thursday: approve A, approve B, or approve a hybrid with conditions.
