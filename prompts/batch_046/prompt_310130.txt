After presenting the scenario, demonstrate that it satisfies each specification: show the verdict the view delivers and why, identify the precise feature generating the tension, note what collapses if that feature is removed, and sketch how a proponent of suffering-abolition would resist the case. Do not invoke trolley problems, demons, perfect predictors, or bibliographic appeals.
