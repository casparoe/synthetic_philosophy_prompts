my cousins got into it at a bbq about pure time preference -- whether its rational to care less about your future self just cuz she's in the future. Maya said thats like caring less about people far away, arbitrary discrimination based on distance alone. Leo said space doesnt change identity but time does, your future self might hold different values, so treating her interests as equal to yours now is actually disrespectful of her autonomy.

Maya replied that the fact she'll change isnt a reason for you to weight her less now -- just like you cant ignore a promise cuz you'll feel different later. The change is descriptive, not a normative excuse.

Leo said promises are different because they're voluntary commitments that bind across moods, but pure preference is just a weighting function. He added that time has causal direction and space doesnt, so the spatial analogy fails.

Maya said okay, but then if causality is what matters, discounting should track causal connection strength, not a flat exponential rate -- which makes standard discounting arbitrary anyway.

Leo answered that flat rate is only a practical heuristic, not a metaphysical claim, and demanding perfect time-neutrality would mean caring about your 90-year-old self exactly as much as yourself today, which is paralyzing and maybe not even coherent with planning.
