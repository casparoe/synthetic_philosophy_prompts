Consequently, both escalate spending, not to acquire value, but to avoid the strategic humiliation of being the party that wasted capital and conceded the burden. The dynamics clearly evoke escalation of commitment and the dollar auction, yet the payoff is inverted—parties compete to avoid a negative prize, not to capture a positive one.
