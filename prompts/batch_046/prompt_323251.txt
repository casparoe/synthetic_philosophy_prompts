There's even the Unfunded Mandates Reform Act that makes Congress cost out mandates but doesn't actually ban them. And in NFIB v. What's going on? What theory of sovereignty, autonomy, or state moral agency does that US pattern actually presuppose? Is it that fiscal exchange is morally different from coercion?
