For each reading, say whether it genuinely entails that honoring sunk costs is always irrational, and whether it can accommodate exceptions such as continuation serving as an intertemporal commitment device or preserving a coordination equilibrium among regional stakeholders. Finally, indicate which reading my colleague is likely using for rhetorical force, which one she actually needs to justify her blanket dismissal, and whether those two readings coincide.
