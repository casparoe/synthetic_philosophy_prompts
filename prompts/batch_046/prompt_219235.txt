Specify the desiderata that a theory of letterform essence must satisfy to count as adequate—specifically, a theory explaining what makes every token of the letter 'a' belong to the category 'a' across typefaces, handwriting, and deliberate distortion, in the vein of the Letter Spirit problem of capturing the "spirit" of a form without reducing it to a fixed template. Organize the full requirements list by kind: (1) the recognition phenomena it must cover, including cross-style identification, partial occlusion, adversarial boundary cases engineered to sit between 'a' and 'd', and recognition by viewers familiar with non-Latin scripts; (2) the distinctions it must preserve, such as between the essential letter and its stylistic envelope, between family resemblance and strict category membership, and between human perceptual constancy and machine classification confidence; (3) the results from visual cognition and design practice it cannot contradict, for instance that recognition survives radical changes in stroke order and proportion, that children learn letters partly through motor production rather than purely visual exposure, and that certain geometric distortions destroy recognition at predictable thresholds; and (4) the explanatory targets it must meet, particularly accounting for why a never-before-seen handwritten form is immediately grasped as an 'a'. For each requirement, provide the motivating case or consideration that makes it non-negotiable versus merely desirable, and indicate which can be relaxed if the theory's purpose is restricted—for example, to optical character recognition rather than to explaining human conceptual grasp. Then identify the single requirement that eliminates the largest share of popular candidates—such as pure template matching, feature-checklist accounts, prototype theories, and standard convolutional classifiers—and explain precisely why that requirement does the damage. Conclude by assessing whether any subset of these desiderata is jointly unsatisfiable; if so, present that tension as a substantive result about the concept of letterform essence itself rather than as a failure of particular theories. A structured table for brief scoring is acceptable, but keep the weight on the criteria and their justifications.
