Hey, I've been chewing on the gender-equality paradox—how Sweden and Norway have way more gender segragated job markets than Egypt or Algeria despite being far more egalitarian. The standard rationalist take is that once you cover basic needs, people just express their true gendered prefferences, which means the split is mostly biological. But I was wondering if there's a hidden third factor: in welfare states, career choice stops being about survival and becomes pure identity signaling, so people might cluster by gender not because of innate ability but because gender becomes a cheap way to signal values. If that's right, then the paradox doesn't prove biology dominates, but it also doesn't mean culture is forcing anyone. So here's my actual question: if justice is about removing coercive barriers, and these barriers are already low in the Nordics, does pushing for 50/50 representation in STEM or nursing actually violate people's autonomy? Or am I just smuggling in the false idea that any post-opportunity gap must be either pure biology or pure coercion, with nothing in between?
