I think (1) pragmatic encroachment is basically correct: higher stakes literally raise the evidential bar for knowing, not just for being justified in saying so. (3) But i also think knowledge requires modal safety thats totally independent of practical concerns—false in nearby worlds, no knowledge, period. (5) In low-stakes chat its okay to say 'i know the train's on time' with weak evidence, and thats genuinely true, not sloppy talk. (6) If stakes suddenly spike—say youre deciding whether to jump onto tracks based on that belief—the right move is to gather more evidence, not retroactively say you never knew.
