(c) The likelihood that human governance and economic integration could actually contain such a technology if it reached that functional threshold, given his argument that organizational adaptation consistently lags behind technical capability.

(d) The likelihood that, conditional on (b) and (c), the technology would persist and adapt rather than dissipate due to dependency on specific human-maintained infrastructure.
