As a journalist who has been tracking the convergence between predictive-processing neuroscience and philosophy of mind, I have become persuaded by a particular variant of the user-illusion account: the view that phenomenal consciousness is not merely a representational gloss but specifically a compressed, lossy interface format generated when a global workspace system integrates high-level predictions about the organism's own attentional state, with the "what-it-is-like" quality functioning as a control parameter rather than a sensory datum. On this model, introspective reports are not inaccurate descriptions of an inner theater; they are accurate descriptions of an interface object that has no direct structural correspondence to the underlying neural dynamics—much as a file icon corresponds to distributed magnetic states without resembling them. I want to know what would genuinely count against this position—not just the standard objections about the hard problem or about phenomenal realism, but which specific empirical findings, which conceptual distinctions it risks collapsing, and which rival explanations it cannot accommodate without losing its explanatory advantage.

For instance, if cross-modal predictive errors were shown to propagate and resolve without being reformatted through this interface layer, or if the proposed "control parameter" function could be fully dissociated from reported phenomenal character in a way that makes the interface story redundant, would that be fatal? Which defeaters would defenders of this specific predictive-processing variant concede as damaging, and which would they dismiss as category mistakes? I am especially interested in whether the view has been qualified to the point of unfalsifiability—if every possible neural correlate can be interpreted as "just another layer of the interface"—and whether that insulation is a methodological virtue or a theoretical vice. Rank the potential defeaters from decisive to merely awkward, and be honest about whether this variant is more exposed than the generic illusion thesis, or whether it carries hidden liabilities that the broader view avoids.
