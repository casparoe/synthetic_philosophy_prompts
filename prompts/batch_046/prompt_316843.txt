The poster writes that Bradley noticed that if a relation such as taller than connects two things, then there must be a further relation connecting that first relation to one of the things, and then another connecting that connector, and so on indefinitely. Something about this feels too sweeping, and I suspect the poster has overstated Bradley's conclusions or collapsed distinctions he kept carefully apart, but I am not sure exactly where the errors lie.
