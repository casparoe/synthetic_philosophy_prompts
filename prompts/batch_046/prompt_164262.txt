me and a friend have been going in circles about hard choices and incommensurable values—like picking between two careers or moral commitments that feel like totally different kinds of goods. One of us (the Parity camp) thinks some pairs are genuinely on a par: practical reason runs out, and autonomy or commitment does the rest. The other (the Unitary camp) thinks there is always a correct comparative judgment, even if we will never know it; hard just means epistemicly muddy, not rationaly underdetermined.

Write the joint statement we would both sign after an honest adversarial collab. I want:
1. Numbered shared claims about value comparison, practical reason, and what needs explaining
2. The disagreements in neutral wording—each phrased so its own camp accepts it
3. The cruxes. I suspect one is whether parity is a real relation or just indecision in fancy dress, and another is whether autonomy can legitimately settle what reason leaves open
4. For each crux, a concrete case or argument both sides agree is relevant, plus what each side predicts about it

No reading lists, no citations, keep it tight—this is for a dataset, not a dissertation.
