Write a deadpan parody of a university job advertisement for a Senior Court Historian, posted by an imaginary Institute for State-Centered Historical Research. The advertisement should specify duties—archiving exclusively royal correspondence, treating peasant petitions and oral testimony as methodological noise, and weighting footnotes by the social rank of the source—together with required qualifications and benefits that quietly assume history is coextensive with state documentation. The satire should exaggerate real features of elite-focused historiography, including archive dependency, source hierarchy, and the equation of state records with historical reality, without ever stating the critique explicitly. Use formal administrative prose, about 450 words, and ensure that a reader familiar with microhistory and history-from-below recognizes the target immediately.
