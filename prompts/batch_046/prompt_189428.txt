I am drafting the final examination for an upper-level seminar on the justification of logical inference, and I need one essay question centered on Carroll's tortoise—the regress argument in his dialogue about whether accepting a logical rule requires a further premise. Please provide the exam question (designed for a ninety-minute answer), a model answer of approximately six hundred words, and a marking scheme structured around five must-hit points with assigned marks totaling twenty, including for each point a one-line note on the characteristic failure mode.
