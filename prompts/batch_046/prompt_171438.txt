I'm preparing a case-study module for a philosophy-of-mind course that pairs classic anti-mechanist arguments with concrete episodes in computing, and I want to see whether the Lucas–Penrose Gödelian argument can survive disciplined contact with an actual technological trajectory rather than remaining a purely abstract claim about arithmetic. Consider the sequence running from the 1997 Deep Blue versus Garry Kasparov match through Kasparov’s subsequent reflections on machine strategic intuition, up to the later emergence of AlphaZero-style engines that learn evaluation patterns through self-play rather than executing explicit search trees. Analyze this episode as a test case for Gödelian anti-mechanism: identify precisely what plays the role of the formal system (the engine’s representational and inferential architecture), what might serve as the chess analog of a Gödel sentence (a strategic situation whose correct assessment a human grasps but that is not derivable within the engine’s own rules and evaluation functions), and whether Kasparov’s sense that Deep Blue exhibited “deep strategic understanding” or his suspicion of concealed human intervention can be read as an implicit appeal to an irreducible human capacity to recognize truths that mechanized procedures miss. Then map where the analogy fractures—chess is finite with perfect information, unlike Peano arithmetic; modern engines approximate expert judgment through learned intensional evaluation rather than syntactic derivation; and reinforcement learning relocates the mechanist thesis without clearly answering the Gödelian challenge about self-reference. Conclude by assessing what the chess episode actually demonstrates: whether it illegitimately extends Lucas–Penrose reasoning beyond mathematics into practical reasoning, whether it exposes the argument as resting on a category mistake between formal derivability and strategic competence, or whether it shows that the anti-mechanist needs an altogether different kind of example to establish that human cognition exceeds every mechanizable procedure. Be explicit about the mapping, honest about where the theory is silent, and avoid forcing the framework to speak where it has no clear application.
