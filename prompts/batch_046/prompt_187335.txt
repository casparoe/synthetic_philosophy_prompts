I've been trying to formalize the epistemology of deep time, and I've run into what seems like an asymmetry that nobody in my circles has a clean answer for. Herodotus and Thucydides both establish protocols for historical knowledge—preferring eyewitness accounts, discounting hearsay, tracking chronological proximity, and treating later synthesis with suspicion. Modern source criticism systematized this into rules about independence, bias, and corroboration. Now here is the confusion: these critical methods are defended as necessary conditions for knowing anything about the human past, yet scientific claims about the distant past—geological strata, stellar nucleosynthesis, cosmic microwave background—rest on exactly the kind of layered, mediated, instrument-dependent inference that historians distrust in testimony. Instruments record data, papers report interpretations, later studies build on earlier calibrations, and every link is vulnerable to systematic error. So if source criticism is genuinely required for knowledge—if without it we cannot distinguish reliable transmission from accumulated legend—why does it not apply with equal force to cosmology? More specifically, if we applied a Thucydidean standard consistently—accepting only what is grounded in direct, contemporaneous, unmediated observation—would we not have to suspend judgment about the age of the universe just as rigorously as about the causes of the Peloponnesian War? Or is there a defensible distinction—perhaps the classic historiographic separation between material remains (Überreste) and deliberate testimony (Tradition)—that breaks the parallel? Even then, I struggle to see why material traces should escape the same critical scrutiny, since telescopic observation is as theory-laden and instrument-mediated as any chronicle. Is the boundary between historical and scientific epistemology genuinely principled, or is it a disciplinary convention that lets physics claim authority over deep time while holding history to a stricter, perhaps arbitrary, standard of proof?
