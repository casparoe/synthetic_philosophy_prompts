I need a reusable procedre I can apply in like 5 minutes per essay, with maybe two other TAs who don't have deep background in the regress literature. Ground it in what those three positions actually commit you to, not the textbook summaries. Then apply it to three made-up student paragraphs: one that claims to stop at basic beliefs but sneaks an infinite regress into a footnote, one that uses coherence but hides a circular dependency between belief A and belief B, and one that claims to be infinitist but actually just gives up after three steps and calls it "open-ended." Make sure one of these gives an answer I won't like — probably the coherence one, since I want to reward holistic reasoning. Also say exactly what the procedure can't settle, like telling a genuinely non-inferential belief from one the student just forgot to justify, and flag where we'd have to escalate to the professor.
