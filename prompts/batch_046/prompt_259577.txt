Present the response in American English as exactly five numbered sections—(1) the exam question, (2) the model answer's thesis and opening, (3) its analysis of Gödel's practical relevance, (4) its evaluation of the feasible-consistency objection, and (5) the marking scheme—each section being one concise paragraph, with no introduction or conclusion outside the numbered list.
