hey so i'm working on nozick's hybrid ethics for animals — utilitarian agregation for them, kantian side constraints for people. i built this argument for a seminar and now it feels like it proves way too much, and i can't find the exit.

(1) on the hybrid view, moral consideration for animals is purely aggregative: you maximize total welfare summed across all affected sentient beings, with no deontological floor.

(2) side constraints for persons are justified precisely because treating a rational being merely as a means for others' welfare is wrong, regardless of net gain.

(3) now take a wildlife case: 50 elk are starving, and if rangers cull 5, the rest survive with less suffering. the cull maximizes aggregate elk welfare.

(4) so on the hybrid view, not only are rangers permitted to cull them, they're required to — the agregation rule demands the welfare-maximizing option.

(5) but the 5 culled elk are killed solely so the other 45 can do better. they're being used purely as instruments for the group's aggregate welfare, with no regard for thier own inviolability.

(6) if that's exactly the kind of mere-means treatment that makes side constraints neccessary for persons, then applying pure agregation to animals treats them in the very way that justifies side constraints.

(7) therefore either (a) animals also fall under side constraints, collapsing the hybrid, or (b) side constraints don't depend on rationality but on sentience, which extends them to animals anyway, or (c) we just accept that killing sentient beings as instruments is obligitory whenever it maximizes welfare — which seems to lisence factory farming, cosmetic testing, whatever, as long as the math works out.

i feel like something slipped between (4) and (5), maybe an equivocation on using-as-means? but i can't pin it down. which step should i actually reject, and what does giving it up cost me? i really want to keep (1) and (2) if i can, but if i have to choose i'd rather drop something in the middle. also is there a hidden modal slide or scope shift i'm missing?
