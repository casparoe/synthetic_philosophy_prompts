For each crux, specify one concrete case or hypothetical that both sides agree is relevant—specifically, a central banking authority setting reserve requirements in response to an unprecedented regulatory shock for which no historical frequency data exists, with no analog in prior cycles. Format the output as a numbered agreed-claims section followed by a disagreements table that lists each disagreement, its associated crux, the agreed-upon test case, and each side’s prediction. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations. Write in American English.
