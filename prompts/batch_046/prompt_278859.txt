My philosophy of physics professor has issued consistent rulings across rotational-motion thought experiments, yet her underlying ontology remains ambiguous. Her verdicts so far: in Newton’s bucket, she holds that the paraboloid water surface marks a genuine dynamical distinction between rotation and rest; in Newton’s globes, she accepts that cord tension in an otherwise empty space proves rotation has real physical effects even absent other bodies; in Mach’s counterfactual—where the distant stars rotate around a stationary bucket—she judges that the water would remain flat, since local inertial properties track the global matter distribution rather than absolute space; in the Lense-Thirring scenario with a rotating hollow shell, she rules that a bucket suspended motionless relative to distant stars but inside the shell exhibits a climbing water surface, showing that adjacent massive structures can determine local inertia; and in a universe containing only a single isolated body, she declares the question of its rotation physically meaningless.

Next semester she will consider the following variant. A hollow cylindrical shell rotates uniformly in an asymptotically flat universe with a negative cosmological constant. A bucket of water is suspended at rest inside, mechanically decoupled from the shell and aligned with asymptotic inertial frames defined at infinity. Owing to the cosmological constant, frame-dragging from the shell is exponentially suppressed, so local inertial frames inside coincide with those at infinity; the bucket does not rotate relative to either. There is no other matter anywhere.

Predict her verdict: will she say the water surface is flat or parabolic? Then identify the implicit theory that best unifies her earlier rulings—hard spacetime substantivalism anchored to asymptotic boundary conditions, local Machian relationism tied to adjacent material frames, or a metric-structuralist view that treats spacetime geometry as dynamically coupled but ontologically neutral. Finally, specify the single modified case—perhaps the same setup with a positive cosmological constant that amplifies rather than suppresses frame-dragging—that would conclusively distinguish her position from its rivals.

Please state how confident you are in this prediction and single out the specific point about which you are least certain.
