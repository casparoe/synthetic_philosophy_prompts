A longstanding tension in Bayesian epistemology concerns old evidence: observational results that were already well established before a theory was proposed appear to confer no confirmatory boost, since conditioning on what is already certain yields a Bayes factor of one. A recently articulated response—one that moves away from flat propositional credences toward hierarchical causal-model Bayes networks—argues that this framing commits a category error. On this account, old data are not merely propositions with prior credence near unity; they are nodes embedded in a structural graph whose causal links to theoretical parameters may have been entirely unspecified prior to the theory's introduction. When a new model architecture is advanced, previously recorded observations can simultaneously update beliefs about the graph's topology and about parameter values within that topology, even though the observations themselves were never in epistemic doubt. Consequently, the problem of old evidence is not a failure of standard conditionalization but a misapplication of parameter-level updating to what is actually structural learning. Treat this position with full seriousness: what would have to be true for it to collapse? Which of its central commitments—that structural priors are genuinely separable from parameter priors, that historical data retain evidential force through graph-reconfiguration rather than through direct parameter constraint, and that working scientists actually reason in this hierarchical fashion—are exposed to counterevidence? What formal results in Bayesian network identification, what empirical studies of scientific reasoning, or what conceptual objections about the stability of evidence-identity across theoretical change would be decisive rather than merely awkward? Would proponents of this view concede any finding or argument that genuinely undermines their resolution, or has the position been insulated by redefining confirmation in structural terms? Provide an honest map of its vulnerabilities and of where, if anywhere, it risks nothing at all.
