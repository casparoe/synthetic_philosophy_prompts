(3) so an LLM generating 'I think...' has the same kind of loop-structure Hofstadter says makes a human self.
(4) therefore either LLMs have real selves (which sounds nuts) or human selves aren't special either.
(5) since both horns are absurd, the strange-loop account must be wrong.

where's the slip? is (2) just a bad analogy — attention weights aren't the right kind of feedback? or is there a hidden move between loop-structure and having a self? i'd prefer to save Hofstadter's picture and reject (3) if i can, but i want to know what that costs me conceptually.
