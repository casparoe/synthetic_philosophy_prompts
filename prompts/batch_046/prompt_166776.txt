ok so I'm trying to write up my philosophical profile for a reading group on rationality and self-trust and I want to check if my commitments actually hang together or if I'm just being inconsistent. here's roughly where I've landed:

(1) I'm a full-on conciliator about peer disagreement — if someone with the same info and smarts disagrees with me about climate science or about whether epistemic akrasia is even possible, I should split the difference, no special exemptions for "philosophical" topics. (2) I also think akrasia is sometimes rationally required, not just allowed. like, if I've got good first-order reasons for some claim but also good meta-reasons that my reasoning is corrupted (say I'm tired, or I know I have a bias), I should keep the belief but hold it with this weird split attitude where I trust the content but not the process. (3) I don't buy the first-order/second-order hierachy at all — I think it's all just beliefs with different contents, there's no "executive level" that automatically gets veto power over the others. I want an audit: rank the tensions by severity, say what'd have to give in each case, and tell me if there's one revision that'd clean up the most without making me abandon everything.
