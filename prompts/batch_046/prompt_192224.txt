Then she said the pessimists come back with the point that scientific testimony gives you causal understanding, whereas aesthetic testimony can only transmit bare belief without the corresponding appreciation, which means for anyone who hasn't viewed the work firsthand it's basically useless. Something feels off about this — maybe she's overstateing the emotional requirement? — and I'm not convinced her final claim follows. Can you audit her summary?
