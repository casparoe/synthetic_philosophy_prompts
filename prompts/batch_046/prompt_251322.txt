My cousin is retiring from the fire dept after 22 years and asked me to speak at her party. The room will be half her crew — people who watched her run into buildings everyone else was running out of — and half her family, including a neice in grad school who keeps asking whether she "suppresses her feelings" or "makes rational judgments" in crisis. The thing is, my cousin actually thinks emotions are neither inner feelings nor cool judgments but perceptions — like seeing smoke, you directly register danger in the enviroment without some private mental state mediating it. She'd be furious if I framed her calm as emotional suppression or cold reasoning. Write a toast in American English, under 250 words, in spoken-style direct address, that honors her without misusing her view or losing either half of the room. Don't cite anyone or use quotes from texts.
