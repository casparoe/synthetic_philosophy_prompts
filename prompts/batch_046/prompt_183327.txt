I've been trying to understand Schopenhaur's claim that music is the direct objectifiction of the Will, not a copy of Platonic Ideas like painting is. Heres where I'm stuck: if music bypasses Ideas entirely and expresses pure Will, then shouldn't a completely unstructured sound—like a pure drone with no melody, harmony, or rhythm—be closer to the Will than a Mozart symphony? After all, the symphony still has formal structure, and structure sounds like the ordered realm of Ideas. But Schopenhauer clearly thinks Mozart is near the top, not some noise. So how does he draw the line between the formal structure that's still part of music's direct expression and the kind of representational structure he criticizes in other arts? And if he can allow that much form in music, why does he still rank opera—where words literally tell you what's happening—so much lower?
