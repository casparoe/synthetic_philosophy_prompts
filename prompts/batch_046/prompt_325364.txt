I want an in-class simulation that makes them feel that specific dynamic rather than just discussing it. Design a structured role-play with small but real stakes: each student receives a recoverable participation-grade adjustment of up to two points based on outcomes in the game, so the incentives are felt but not destructive. At the start, divide the group into enforcers and advocates by an arbitrary hidden mechanism — for instance, the parity of their seat numbers — giving the enforcers veto and allocation authority that has no connection to merit or argument.
