In settings where observers try to infer stable human preferences from behavior by treating agents as approximately rational optimizers, I have noticed a recurring pattern that I cannot map cleanly onto any standard concept. Imagine an agent who consistently makes materially costly choices in order to preserve a coherent self-narrative or value-identity -- for instance, refusing a profitable opportunity that conflicts with a long-held self-image. The inference framework might capture this as a preference for narrative continuity. Now observe the same agent under conditions of fatigue, anonymity, or severe time pressure: the costly pattern disappears, and the agent takes the materially optimal option. Rather than treating this variability as evidence that the agent relies on heuristics, operates under shifting constraints, or lacks stable preferences in this domain, the framework typically preserves its core rational-actor model by multiplying auxiliary preference dimensions -- a preference for identity maintenance that is active only with adequate cognitive resources, a meta-preference for consistency only in public or identifiable contexts, and so on. Each addition is individually defensible; together they produce an inferred reward structure so flexible that it could accommodate almost any behavioral profile. Is there an established name for this specific dynamic -- not merely the general underdetermination of preference inference, nor the familiar problem of separating preferences from constraints, but precisely the tendency to absorb behavioral variability into an expanding lattice of context-bound preference parameters in order to protect the rational-optimization framework? How should it be delimited from ordinary context-sensitive revealed preference, from post-hoc rationalization, and from the legitimate view that human values are genuinely multi-layered and situation-dependent? If there is no precise term, what conceptual boundaries would best isolate this failure mode, and what would be lost by treating it as nothing more than complex preference architecture?
