The room will be split: about half are philosphy grad students and faculty who live and breathe the argument from illusion, disjunctivism, direct realism, all that; the other half are photographers and visual artists who got invited because the grant requires public engagement, and they actually care about seeing but have never heard of a sense datum. I need about seven minutes. There's a twist I want you to weave in: frame the classic argument from illusion not just as a bad inference but as a kind of intellectual tall-poppy move, a way of cutting down successful direct seeing by redescribing it as mediated failure. Don't cite anyone, don't hand out reading lists, just build the remarks around that structural idea. Keep it casual — I'm talking to you like a colleague who can write speeches — and make sure both halves of the room feel addressed, not just tolerated.
