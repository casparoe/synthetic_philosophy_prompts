In this regime the constant can dwarf the difference between the models themselves, so Solomonoff gives us no principled ordering.

(3) Maya came back: The invariance theorem still saves the ordering asymptotically. (4) Raj said: You are importing an asymptotic result into a non-asymptotic decision problem.
