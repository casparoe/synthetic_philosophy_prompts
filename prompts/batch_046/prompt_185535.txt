The other side says that's pure post-hoc rationalisation: whenever a prediction fails you just invoke an auxilliary hypothesis about preservation bias or incomplete sampling, which makes the core theory technically immune from refutation in practise. We all agree on the actual fossil record, we all agree Popper wanted risky predictions rather than comfortable ones, and yet nobody's budged.
