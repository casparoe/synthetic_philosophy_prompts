There's no outside observer, no stars, no distance measure except what they can feel — just the void and the two of them. Write it from the sphere's point of view, make it lonely and philosophical, and have it try to send some kind of signal to the other sphere but never be able to tell whether it arrived or whether the other one was ever really "there" in the first place.
