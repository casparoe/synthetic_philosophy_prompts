(2) but there's also second-order sphexishness: even when first-order rules can update, the meta-rules that govern those updates are themselves mechanically fixed. you're still in a deterministic loop, just nested deeper.

(3) human brains are physical computers, so both levels are mechanically determined.

(4) if you're sphexish at both orders, belief revision isn't responsive to reasons—it's just execution of predetermined update rules.

(5) that means the belief 'i am sphexish' is itself just a mechanical burp, not something arrived at via rational evaluation.

(6) apply the same to anyone who disagrees: their disagreement is also just mechanical execution. so sphexishness doesn't just make free will impossible; it makes rational disagreement conceptually impossible.

(7) therefore if sphexishness is true, there's no rational basis to believe it, and no rational basis to reject it either.

(8) but that means sphexishness undermines all reasoning, including the reasoning that led to it.

(9) so either deny (3) and allow non-physical flexibility (feels like mysticism), or accept that rational thought is an illusion—which is itself a rational conclusion you shouldn't be able to reach.
