Please construct a decision tree for a postgraduate philosophy seminar that classifies positions on whether 'Western values' denotes a coherent category. The root should split on whether the phrase is interpreted as a geographical-historical cluster or as a normative ideal; the second level should split on whether coherence requires shared metaphysical foundations or only overlapping family resemblances; the third level should split on whether the phrase's performative use in political argument undermines its descriptive content. Each leaf must carry a named position—or be marked 'unoccupied' with a brief diagnosis of why the combination is incoherent or unexplored—together with its most awkward commitment when confronted with the case of a polity that treats individual liberty and hierarchical social order as jointly constitutive. Flag any pair of leaves that the tree cannot genuinely separate, and phrase every node as a concrete claim that a respondent could answer without knowing the philosophical labels in advance.
