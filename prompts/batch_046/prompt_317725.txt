You see this in the emphasis on individual rights and in cultural messages about following your heart.

If you apply this to free-riding — for example, people avoiding taxes for public goods — the german framework clearly does better. So the hidden cost of the american view is that it lacks the external scaffolding to prevent people from talking themselves into defection.
