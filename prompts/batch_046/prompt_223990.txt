After noticing that I navigate complex professional judgments with a fluency that far outstrips my ability to articulate their underlying logic, I have become fixated on what I will call the double inversion of reasoning: Darwin demonstrates that biological competence—exquisitely adapted form—requires no comprehending agent, while Turing demonstrates that behavioral competence—intelligent response—requires no comprehending subject. Write a joke that operates specifically at the intersection of these two inversions, such that the punchline depends on recognizing that Darwin removes the mind from nature's design, whereas Turing removes the privileged status of mind from the evaluation of behavior. The joke should avoid generic technology humor and should feel personally resonant with someone who suspects their own competence may be more mechanically selected—or more naturally blind—than they care to admit.
