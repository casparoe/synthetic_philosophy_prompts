Please lay out the distinct precise theses this slogan could be expressing, ordered from the most trivial or definitional to the most substantive and controversial. For each reading, indicate whether it actually supports her dismissal of the formal-symbol proposal, and specify whether that support depends on empirical claims about neuroscience, logical claims about self-reference, or normative claims about what counts as "genuine" selfhood. Write the response in American English.
