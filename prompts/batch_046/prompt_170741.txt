write me the checklist in ordered steps, with for each step: what counts as a sufficient answer, where the procedre stops with "wrong level — reject or reroute," and where it has to hand off to me because it's a values call not a fact. flag the hard cases it can't settle, like when local democratic control pulls against cross-border efficiency. then apply it to three proposals:

a) a single city planting heat-resistant trees in its own parks
b) a US state proposing flood walls on a river running through three states and near canadian provinces — the state wants to run and fund it alone
c) a swiss canton proposing a digital carbon-tracking platform that works locally but needs eu-wide data standards to be useful

include one case where the answer comes out "no, wrong level" and the applicant will hate it. also be explicit about what the procedre can't do — for example, comparing democratic legitimacy across countries — and be honest about which steps are just judgment calls dressed up as steps. keep it practical, no citations or reading lists, and don't pretend it's more objective than it is.
