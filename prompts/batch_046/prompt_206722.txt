My study group got into it over whether modern Stoicism butchers the old dichotomy of control. Maya said the self-help mantra — focus on whats in your power — only works if you buy Epictetus's determinist metaphysics, where only your prohairesis is genuinely yours and the rest is woven into fate. Without that backbone, she said, its just vague mindfulness. Leo said thats gatekeeping: modern Stoicism is applied ethics, not cosmology, and the heuristic holds whether you believe in determinism or not. Then Maya brought up her job, where an algorithm assigns shifts based on past ratings she partly shaped but now cant see or change. She argued the control line is fractal — zoom in and you find agency everywhere, zoom out and its gone — so the dichotomy is useless unless you smuggle in a hidden normative rule about which level of analysis counts. Leo said thats just a complicated external with feedback loops, which Stoics already treat as preferred indifferents, and shes importing cybernetics into a philosophy that predates it by two millennia. They asked me to referee but I honestly cant tell whos describing a real problem and whos just overanalyzing. Can you go through their exchange move by move and say whos right at each step — not just who wins overall?
