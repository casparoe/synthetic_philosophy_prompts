Write the proposal as exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion: state the precise question about whether this embedding premise is load-bearing; explain why standard eternalist defenses and presentist rebuttals have both missed it; specify the conceptual method—formal reconstruction of observer-location premises rather than physical interpretation—and defend that choice; identify what result, whether failure or success of the argument under worldline embedding, would count as decisive; and assess the chief risk that the project exceeds the scope of a short philosophical essay.
