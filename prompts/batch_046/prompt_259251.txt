I've come around to thinking Buridan's ass is a fake problem, not because agents need reasons, but because real embodied choice never hits perfect symetry — there's always some sub-personal nudge (a muscle twitch, a random spike) that breaks the tie without being a full-blown preferance. So choice without preference isn't impossible; pure equality of options just never happens in practice. What would actually count agianst this?
