I take this to mean that attempts to trade off well-being against harm are not just ethically wrong but conceptually confused, as if one tried to cancel a vector with a magnitude.

Acting as a professional philosopher of value with expertise in axiology, decision theory, and the phenomenology of affect, please think carefully about this position and begin by brainstorming the different categories of defeat it might face—conceptual, empirical, normative, and formal—before presenting your analysis.
