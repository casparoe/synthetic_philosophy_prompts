I'm working out a hybrid position between virtue ethics and the situationist critique, and I want a consistency check before I present it. Here are my commitments: (1) Moral virtues are real but not global, cross-situational traits; they function like context-activated modules with stable internal structures but highly specific triggering conditions. (2) Practical wisdom (phronesis) is not itself one of these modules, but a genuinely general integrative capacity that can coordinate across domains and recognize where modules are missing or weak. (3) The situationist findings about behavioral variability undermine only global-trait theories, not modular ones—what is stable is the module architecture and its activation conditions, not uniform responses across all contexts. (4) Moral education should therefore be highly contextualized: we build virtue by repeatedly pairing specific situational cues with appropriate responses, rather than by cultivating abstract, decontextualized dispositions. (5) A person can be fully virtuous overall despite displaying vice in domains where no module was developed, provided practical wisdom registers the gap and motivates repair; hypocrisy is not mere vice in one domain, but specifically a failure of this integrative motivation to initiate repair. (6) Because practical wisdom is general, it should in principle be able to direct the construction of new modules even in domains where none currently operate.

Please audit this package: which pairs are genuinely in tension, which only appear to be, and what is the least costly revision that preserves both the core virtue-ethical framework and the central situationist findings? I am especially worried that (2)'s generality conflicts with (1)'s modularity, that (5) permits more fragmentation than (6) can plausibly correct, and that (3) merely relocates the situationist problem rather than solving it. Rank the tensions by severity, say for each whether the fix requires giving up a substantive claim or only refining it, and identify any apparent tension—perhaps between accepting situationism and remaining a virtue ethicist—that dissolves once (1) and (3) are properly understood.
