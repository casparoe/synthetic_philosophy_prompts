Finally, discuss how this framework applies to negative propositions—judgments that deny a relation holds—given that if relations are internal, denying the relation seems to deny the terms themselves, and whether Bradley's move to absolute monism avoids the regress only by dissolving the distinctions among thinker, thought, and relation at the cost of making falsehood unintelligible.
