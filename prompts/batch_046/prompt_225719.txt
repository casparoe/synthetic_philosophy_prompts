He claims music isnt a representation of Platonic Ideas like painting or poetry, but rather a direct copy of the Will itself — melody expressing the striving, harmony the gradations, dissonance the suffering. But theres this weird tension: if music bypasses Ideas entirely, then why does Schopenhauer still talk about music expressing the inner nature of the world in a way that seems structural, not just emotional? And then theres the problem of musical form — sonata structure, repetition, variation — which looks like it depends on conceptual patterns we dont have in raw willing.

So Im trying to figure out which of these open lines actually matter most for making his view coherent, and which are dead ends that just recycle old debates.
