(5) Riley said okay, but then "better" depends on what a culture is actually trying to achieve. If the goal is moral growth, guilt lets you isolate the wrong act and correct it; shame collapses the actor and the act, which makes redemption structurally impossible. So if a culture wants people to come back from mistakes, guilt is the only emotional architecture that allows it.
