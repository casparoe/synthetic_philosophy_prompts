Please rate on a scale of 1 to 10, then rank from strongest to weakest, the following five considerations regarding whether brain size is directly morally relevant to an entity's status: (1) its correlation with cognitive capacities that typically ground moral standing; (2) its independence from sentience thresholds; (3) appeals to species-typical neural functioning; (4) relational or care-based properties that might override intrinsic metrics; and (5) scaling coherence—whether moral status should scale continuously with brain mass or require a discontinuous architectural threshold, and whether either option makes size a stable criterion. For each item, provide the score and one sentence of reasoning, with particular attention to whether scaling coherence undermines the relevance of brain size altogether.
