I'm giving the toast at Dr. Elena Voss's retirement dinner. She taught metaphysics for thirty years -- mostly persistence, endurantism vs perdurantism -- but she also ran this little art restoration workshop on weekends out of her garage. The room is split: half are her grad students who know the litterature cold, half are family and museum folks who just know she fixed old paintings. I want to build the whole thing around whether Elena is still the 'same person' who started in '94, and whether a restored painting is still the 'same object' that came in broken. I have this weird framing that restoration isn't endurantist preservation at all -- it's more like stitching together temporal parts of a four-dimensional artifact, which sounds like perdurantism applied to canvases. Use that to actually structure what I can and can't say about her career, not just as decoration. Needs to land emotionally for the non-philosophers without dumbing it down for the experts. About five minutes, casual tone. Make sure the family can follow the emotional thread without knowing the jargan. She asked specifically for no quotes from dead philosophers, just talk about her.
