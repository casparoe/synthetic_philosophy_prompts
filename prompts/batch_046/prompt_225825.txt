I draw an analogy to Wittgenstein's idea of family resemblance, suggesting that just as games share no single defining essence yet remain genuinely grouped, so too might "Renaissance" phenomena form a non-arbitrary cluster despite lacking a uniform core. At the same time, I try to avoid making periodization seem purely arbitrary by arguing that these labels do track uneven but real institutional discontinuities—such as the gradual shift from monastic scriptoria to university-based learning, or from court and church patronage toward market-oriented print culture—even though the transitions overlap and are experienced differently across regions.
