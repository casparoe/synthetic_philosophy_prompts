She thinks when you translate Marot's "Ma mignonne" you should preserve the phonetic skeleton—rhyme architecture, syllabic weight, consonant echo—before semantic fidelity, and if English can't bear the load you coin compounds or warp syntax rather than flatten the form. Keep it focused on the mechanics of her position, separate what's settled in translation studies from what's genuinely contested, and don't present either Hofstadter's view or hers as the consensus.
