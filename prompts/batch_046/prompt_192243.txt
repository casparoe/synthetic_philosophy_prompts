Map the actual mechanisms: communes wiped out direct land entitlement, state procurement destroyed trade entitlement, and labor was diverted to backyard steel. Is that still just an entitlement failure, or is it a separate informational breakdown Sen's model misses? Sen also says democracies prevent famines because rulers answer to voters; China had mass mobilization but zero democratic accountability.
