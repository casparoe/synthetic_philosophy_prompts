Present the piece in exactly five numbered sections, each comprising no more than two sentences, with neither introduction nor conclusion: section one establishes the bureaucratic voice; section two misreads the architectural origin; section three introduces the chin audit; section four invents the implausible selective story; section five closes with an absurd policy recommendation. Maintain British spelling and an utterly deadpan tone throughout.
