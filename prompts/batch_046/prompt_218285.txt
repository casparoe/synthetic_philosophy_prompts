The summary then asserts that post-processual critics reject this inference not by challenging the observed material patterning, but by conflating material regularity with symbolic meaning; in so doing, they allegedly forfeit any empirical criterion for separating belief from behavior and thereby make the archaeological study of institutions impossible.
