Our precedant rulings:

The wall's name is on every emergency evacuation map, on physical access-gate signage, and in all the FEMA docs. A community coalition wants it renamed; emergency management says changing all signage and digital maps risks confusion during an actual flood event. They're proposing a historical marker near one gate instead.

So if we're trying to be consistent with ourselves, what's the verdict? Which of our four precedants is the closest analog?
