The central claim is that the Judy Benjamin problem is typically misdiagnosed as a failure of conditionalisation: Benjamin updates rationally on the conditional that if she is not in Red Company, she is in Blue Company, yet the paradox only emerges if we model her belief with a single flat credence function over possible worlds.
