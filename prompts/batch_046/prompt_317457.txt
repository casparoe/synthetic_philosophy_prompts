Please rank any tensions by severity: which pairs genuinely conflict, which are only apparently incompatible, and for each conflict identify the least costly revision. Specifically, does (3) require rejecting (1) or restricting its scope, or can the two be reconciled? And does (4) sit comfortably with (2), or does evidential reasoning in Newcomb-style cases commit me to updating whenever correlations shift?
