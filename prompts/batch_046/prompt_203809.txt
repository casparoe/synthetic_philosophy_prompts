I want to build a taxonomy or decision tree that actually sorts out the different answers to this, not just lists philosophers by name. The point is to see where positions split on concrete claims, so someone could take the quiz and end up with a view they didn't know had a label.

Start from the root: you think extinction is bad for reasons beyond the direct loss of life. First branch: is the harm mainly about future generations that never come to exist (the impersonal side), or is it mainly about damage to the projects and values of people alive now or in the near future (the Scheffler-style dependency side)? If it's the first, split on whether there's an asymmetry between failing to create happy people and actively destroying them. Then I want a branch that tests the "archival fallacy": if we left perfect digital archives, AI successors, or complete cultural preservation, would that fix the harm, or does actual living continuation matter?
