I'm constructing a focused research agenda in philosophy of value, specifically around the heterogeneity problem for hedonism—what, if anything, all pleasures share given that gustatory, intellectual, aesthetic, and bodily pleasures appear phenomenally disjoint. If sensory-substitution and synthetic-phenomenology cases can generate reported pleasures that don't map onto standard sensory modalities, then heterogeneity may be intra-representational rather than merely inter-modal, which changes which questions are upstream.
