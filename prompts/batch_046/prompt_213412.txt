Prepare my Q&A sheet: order the objections by likelihood and damage, phrase each as that faction would phrase it, indicate for each what I ought to concede and what I ought to hold, flag the weakest point in my reply, and identify the single objection I most hope is not raised, together with a brief defence strategy.
