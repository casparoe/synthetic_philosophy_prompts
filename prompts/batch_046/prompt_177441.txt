(1) On Lewis's view, a convention is an arbitrary regularity that solves a coordination problem, persists through mutual expectation, and gets reinforced by evolutionary stability in the population. (5) So either Lewis can't account for the special wrongness of breaking promises, or his account swallows every social rule and makes them all equally binding. I want to keep the special binding force of promises, but I'm not sure I'm not just being sentamental. Where's the error — is it in (3), or in assuming that pre-commitment can't be graded?
