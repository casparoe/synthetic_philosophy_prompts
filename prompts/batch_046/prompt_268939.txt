The Kripkenstein rule-following debate has been going in cirlces for years — one camp says community norms stop the skeptical regress, the other says norms just kick the problem up a level. I wonder if the real stall is that we're treating it like a flat disagreement when it's actually a tangled hierarchy: the skeptic operates on an object-level about mental content, the normativist operates on a meta-level about social practice, and there's no inviolate level where they meet. Is that the right diagnosis — a level-mismatch rather than a substantive clash — or is there a hidden empirical question about language acquisition doing the work? Say what kind of disagreement this actually is, and be honest about whether a new distinction or experiment could break the loop, or if we're just stuck in a strange loop with no exit.
