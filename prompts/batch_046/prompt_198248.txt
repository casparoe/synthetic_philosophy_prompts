Write an essay on the following controversy: if cognition is modeled as a layered predictive-processing hierarchy rather than as a global workspace bus, does the philosophical distinction between access consciousness and phenomenal consciousness still do meaningful work? Weigh whether phenomenal content could be localized in deep generative models that never achieve global availability, and whether access then collapses into a merely functional property of high-precision error signals rather than a constitutive condition for consciousness. Reach a clear, justified conclusion; do not end with an uncommitted summary.
