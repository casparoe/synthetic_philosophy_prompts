Longtime lurker here, mostly on rationalist-adjacent forums about gender and culture. I've been loooking at debates over the gender-equality paradox — the thing where more egalitarian countries (Sweden, Norway) often show bigger gender splits in career choice than less egalitarian ones (Turkey, Algeria, etc.). I keep seeing a specific argumentative loop that feels different from ordinary motivated reasoning, but I don't have a name for it.

Here's the pattern. Someone notes the cross-country correlation: higher gender equality, higher occupational segregation. The "culture explains everything" side says that's expected — in restrictive societies people take any job that pays, so gender patterns get masked by economic pressure. The "biology explains everything" side says that's also expected — in free societies people finally follow innate preferences, so true divergence shows up. What's strange is that both sides treat the same correlation as confirmation, but they've each smuggled in a hidden "release valve" that makes their model adapt: culture suppresses nature in unequal countries, but nature expresses itself in equal ones. There's never a clear hypothetical world — say, one where culture dominates and equality makes segregation vanish — that either side treats as the genuine null. If you point out that culture should converge preferences, they just say the socialization hasn't been eliminated yet; if you say biology should be constant, they say it was being suppressed. It feels like a double-accounting scheme where the baseline for "what should happen under pure culture vs pure nature" is defined post-hoc based on the observed data.

Does this specific configuration — a correlation interpreted through two mirror-image adaptive null hypotheses, both protected by auxiliary adjustments that flip depending on the country's equality level — have an established name in philosophy of science or methodology? I'm not looking for generic confirmation bias; I want to know if there's a sharper concept for this kind of symmetric unfalsifiability, how it differs from an ordinary ad hoc auxiliary, and if there's no name, what you'd call it and where exactly its boundaries are. Please lead with your bottom line in the first sentence and then give the reasoning, with no preamble and no restating of the question.
