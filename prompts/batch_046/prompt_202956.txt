Is there a name for this move I keep seeing in metaphysics talks about grounding? Someone argues consciousness isn't fundamental because it decomposes into neural processes, but then when you ask why neurons are the bottom level they say "well that's just where our insturments stop" or "that's the scale physics happens to work at." It's not quite the epistemic fallacy, since they aren't claiming we know neurons are ultimate—just treating them as the floor for practical reasons. Is there an established concept for this exact slide between an ontological decomposition criterion and an epistemic/instrumental floor, or should I just call it the instrument-floor fallacy?
