Write a Petrarchan sonnet about the exclusion postulate in Integrated Information Theory. In the octave, depict two mechanisms, A and B, that together generate integrated information Φ and seem to constitute a single conscious complex. At the volta, introduce mechanism C: though apparently peripheral, C integrates more strongly with B alone, thereby excluding A from the maximally irreducible substrate. The sestet should trace the abrupt redrawing of the complex boundary, leaving A outside despite its prior membership. The closing couplet should state precisely what the postulate demands—that only one maximally irreducible substrate exists at any spatiotemporal grain. Let the rhyme scheme and the sonnet’s turn enact this exclusion: the volta should feel like a boundary being severed, not merely reported.
