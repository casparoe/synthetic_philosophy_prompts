Give me the objections in the order they'll actually come, phrased as each camp would phrase them, with what I should concede up front, what I should hold firm, and the weakest point in my own position—the exact boundary between a genuine reflexive suspension and an ordinary failure of convergence—that I should prepare to defend rather than hope they miss.
