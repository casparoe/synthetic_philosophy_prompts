Now I'm wondering: if they eventually replace 100% of him, is the person who wakes up actually my roommate, or just a perfect functional copy running on hardware? He says I'm being essentialist and that identity is just continuity of function, not some ghost in the machine. But I can't shake the feeling that if the qualitative feel of being him is slowly changing under the radar, then gradual subsitution might not preserve the thing that actually matters. Is his argument fair, or am I right that there's a real philosophical problem here about whether slow replacement keeps you the same person?
