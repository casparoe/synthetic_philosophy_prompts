Please design the explanatory sequence: what dependency must be secured before the concept of freedom can be introduced without collapsing into the idea of "feeling unrestricted," and at exactly which point in that sequence the students are most likely to confuse Sartrean freedom with psychological autonomy or with the absence of external constraint.
