The question should require students to determine whether this ledger genuinely violates the separateness of persons, or whether the objection can be reconstructed inside it, and it must force them to distinguish a structural distributive failure from a merely quantitative one.

Please provide the following, in a formal register suitable for an assessment embedded within a literary work:

First, the exam question itself, specifying a ninety-minute writing period and an expected response of approximately eight hundred words. The prompt should ask students to articulate why aggregation within a single life differs structurally from aggregation across distinct lives, to evaluate whether the ledger framing collapses that distinction or can accommodate it, and to identify the specific principle—boundary-preserving and non-fungible—that the separateness objection defends.
