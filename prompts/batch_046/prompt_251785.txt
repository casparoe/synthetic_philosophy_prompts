Compare the American Dream's ethos of celebrating success with Jantelagen and the tall-poppy syndrome. Most people stop at "Americans love winners, Scandinavians hate them," which is lazy. I want you to examine whether either culture actually supports what I'll call "non-comparative self-worth"—the ability to value yourself without constantly meausring against who's visible or who's being cut down. Argue that one of these frameworks gets closer to that ideal, and defend your pick with actual reasoning. Don't hedge, don't ask for a bibliography—just take a definite stance and own it.
