Would such a case genuinely show that ordinary pain is bad not in virtue of its intrinsic sensory or phenomenal character, but only because of a separable affective-evaluative component that can be absent?

Please give me your credence—expressed as a number between 0 and 1—that this sustained, approach-oriented form of asymbolia undermines the thesis that pain's negative value is carried entirely by its nociceptive phenomenal quality. State how these terms combine—whether multiplicatively, as weighted averages, or otherwise—into your overall number, with one line of justification for each term.
