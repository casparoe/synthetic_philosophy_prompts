Write a short story about a woman named Mara who works as a memory curator for a small commune that practices radical transparency — they archive every private thought to a shared ledger. One day she discovers her teenage son has hidden a severe crime in his personal cache, and the commune's ethical code demands she expose him imediately to collective judgment. Instead, she deletes the entry and replaces it with silence, beliving she is acting on a higher purpose she can't justify to anyone. The story should explore whether her act counts as a teleological suspention of the ethical in the Kierkegaardian sense, not just ordinary rebellion. Don't just retell Abraham and Isaac in sci-fi clothes; I want the knight of faith dynamic visible in how she relates to the community's ethical system after the fact, especially the isolating part where she can't speak.
