Write a short research proposal for a three-month philosophy-of-mind fellowship on split-brain cases and the unity of consciousness. Frame the question away from the standard "one mind versus two minds" debate and instead ask whether phenomenal unity, access unity, and volitional unity can come apart in callosotomy patients, with particular attention to whether subcortical relay structures preserve volitional integration even when interhemispheric access is severed.
