The version that keeps nagging at me introduces an infallible predictor: the traveller receives a perfect forecast that he will die in Damascus tomorrow, so he boards a train to Aleppo, reasoning that if the prediction is genuinely true he cannot be in Damascus, hence it must be false. The traveller has not escaped; he has fulfilled the prophecy precisely by attempting to evade it.
