(5) I think that a sufficiently integrated human-technology collective, or even a tightly coordinated human group, can form a group-level mind with its own intentional states, even though each individual member retains a separate, non-extended stream of consciousness.

(6) I hold that if we gradually replaced a person's biological memory with a silicon implant that satisfied criterion (3), the person would survive with numerically the same mental life—not merely the same behavioral profile—provided the normative feedback loops remained intact.
