Provide a minute-by-minute timeline, materials needed, and a debrief that maps specific moments onto Weil’s argument, including her comparison of party discipline to ecclesiastical obedience and her notion that collective opinion exerts a downward “gravity” on thought; do not name Weil or her essay until the debrief’s final five minutes. Also tell me what will predictably fail the first time I run it with technically trained participants who may treat the dynamics as a rational coordination problem rather than a corruption of reason, and be frank about which feature of her view—the possibility of detached judgment or “grace” breaking through—this exercise cannot reproduce and I must supply directly.
