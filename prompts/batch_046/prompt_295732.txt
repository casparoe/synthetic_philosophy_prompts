"Maimonides says animal sacrifice was never part of God's original plan but was a divine accomodation to the Israelites, who had picked up sacrificial habits from the Egyptians and surrounding idolaters. Since the sacrificial commandments were only a therapeutic fix for a people stuck in materialism, Maimonides implies they have no permanent value.
