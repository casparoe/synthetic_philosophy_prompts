What philosophical commitments does this operationalisation presuppose? Does it necessarily rely on a status-quo-biased axiology that treats inaction as normatively privileged? Furthermore, is the practice consistent with ethical frameworks that reject the moral distinction between acts and omissions, or would adopting such a framework require abandoning the baseline-comparison method entirely?
