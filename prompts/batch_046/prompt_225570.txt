Franklin used to track his 13 virtues—temperance, silence, order, resolution, and so on—with that little grid in his jurnal. If he were around now and saw people using wearables and apps to log every mood and moral slip in real time, do you think he'd see it as a natual extension of his method? Or would he say the quantification misses the point of self-cultivation? And what about AI coaches that scan your texts to nudge you on humility—would he welcome that, or think it turns moral perfection into something mechanical?
