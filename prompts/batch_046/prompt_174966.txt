In your analysis, do not treat this merely as a government solving a traffic problem. Instead, map the coordinating parties precisely: private drivers (over 90 percent of whom operated left-hand-drive vehicles), bus and tram operators, neighboring countries with right-hand standards, and the Swedish state itself, which in 1963 overrode a 1955 referendum in which 83 percent of voters had chosen to retain left-side driving. Specify the payoff structures they faced, taking into account cross-border traffic, passing safety on narrow highways, and the rising cost of model-specific headlamps that made further delay economically punishing.
