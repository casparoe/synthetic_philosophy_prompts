Their exclusion diminishes the conatus of the whole body, and only their re-entry into the modulation can restore the democratic right that equals collective power. Use Spinoza's vocabulary—multitudo, conatus, affectus, common notions, and the identity of right and power—as the sensory and social logic of the world, without importing modern liberal democratic vocabulary. After the story, provide the section titled Commentary: The Translational Choices (approximately 250 words). Conclude by noting one conceptual tension between Spinoza's universalist metaphysics and his particularist democratic exclusions that the story either resolves or deliberately leaves open.
