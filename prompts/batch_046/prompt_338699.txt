The friend extends this reasoning to contemporary immersive virtual-reality performances in which audience members physically move through the narrative space and influence its outcome, claiming that even these participatory forms fall within Rousseau's prohibition because they still depend upon scripted deception and separate citizens from authentic communal action. Please assess how fair this summary is. Then consider the extension to embodied, interactive virtual theatre: does Rousseau's critique genuinely apply when spectatorship is replaced by co-creation, or does that transformation break the premises on which his argument rests?
