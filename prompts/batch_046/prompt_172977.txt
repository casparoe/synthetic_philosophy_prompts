That seems structurally different from attributing harm to an active malfunction, yet we treat both as causally explanatory. Before I survey the literature on causation by omission, I want the conceptual architecture laid out. Mark which of these are non-negotiable and which reflect the preferences of particular camps, such as the view that omissions must depend on normative duties rather than counterfactual dependencies.
