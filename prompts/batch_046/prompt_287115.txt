ok so my friend has this take about Aunt Hillary — thats the queen ant in our lab terrarium — and the weird fugue thing her workers do, where pheromone trails interleave like voices entering late. he thinks it proves the colony is one mind. here's his argument:

(1) If you can only predict a system's behavior by attributing a single representational state to the whole that none of the parts hold, then the whole is the proper subject of mind.
(2) To predict where Hillary's workers will be in 30 sec you have to model the colony as holding one integrated 'goal' of sustaining the fugue-pattern; modeling each ant alone fails.
(3) So the colony, not any individual ant, is the proper bearer of that mental state.
(4) But markets work the same way: no trader holds the 'market goal', yet we predict prices by attributing one state to the whole.
(5) So by (1)-(3) markets have minds too.
(6) That's clearly wrong.

he says the slip is in (2) — we just lack compute to model ants separately. i think cutting (1) saves us but then brains might just be fancy markets? which step do you reject, and what does giving it up cost?
