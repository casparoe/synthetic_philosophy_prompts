As a doctoral researcher in philosophy of mind working on the conceptual coherence of phenomenal properties, I am assembling workshop materials on whether certain intuition-pumps genuinely isolate incoherence in the concept of qualia or merely confuse epistemic inaccessibility with ontological absence. Please adopt the role of an expert philosopher of mind with training in naturalised metaphysics and decision-theoretic reasoning. Before producing your answer, spend a brief moment brainstorming the structural requirements; then proceed methodically.

Your task is to construct a thought experiment built precisely to the following specifications.

Domain and theoretical framing: The scenario should engage with the debate over whether qualia constitute an incoherent posit, in the tradition of Dennett’s essay Quining Qualia and the Chase-Sanborn coffee intuition-pump, but it must not reproduce that familiar taste-inversion narrative. Instead, inject a novel but realistic setting.

Specification of the scenario:
Design a single-paragraph case set in an ordinary clinical neuroengineering context—no exotic physics, no demons, no perfect predictors, no statistical lotteries. A patient receives a calibrated, closed-loop brainstem implant that gradually remaps the neural correlates of respiratory interoception over several months. The remapping is engineered so that every behavioural, verbal, physiological, and cognitive output remains within baseline statistical variance; the patient reports no change in mood, belief, or bodily awareness. For the sake of the argument, stipulate that a phenomenal shift has occurred—say, the felt quality of breathing effort has either attenuated or inverted—yet this shift is entirely transparent to first-person and third-person detection.

Theoretical deliverables required:
(a) Identify the belief or phenomenal state at issue, the method of access, and the nearby possible worlds relevant to assessing it.
(b) Demonstrate what an intrinsic-qualia realist—who takes phenomenal properties to be non-representational, directly acquainted, and ontologically primitive—must conclude: that there exists a genuine phenomenal difference with zero functional or epistemic footprint, and explain why this conclusion is compelled by their core commitments.
(c) Demonstrate what a Dennett-style Quinean critic must conclude: that a property capable of varying without any detectable difference is not a hidden natural kind but an incoherent posit, violating the conditions for legitimate ontological commitment within a naturalistic framework.
(d) Demonstrate what a higher-order representationalist must conclude: that there is no hidden phenomenal fact, because phenomenal consciousness consists in the appropriate higher-order representation of a first-order state; since neither the first-order content nor the higher-order access has changed, nothing phenomenal has changed, so the realist’s putative difference is a category mistake rather than an undetected reality.

Structural analysis:
State explicitly which feature of your case does the philosophical work—most likely the conjunction of gradual change, total behavioural transparency, and stable self-report. Then explain what happens if that feature is removed: for instance, if the transformation is abrupt and produces even a transient behavioural anomaly, or if the patient can be trained to discriminate the states. Finally, present the best counter-attack available to the intrinsic-qualia defender: they might argue that total transparency is conceptually impossible without destroying the phenomenal property itself, or that inaccessibility is precisely what intrinsicness predicts.

Constraints and format:
Keep the scenario to one paragraph. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations. Do not place verbatim quotations in quotation marks; if you refer to Dennett’s argument or the Chase-Sanborn exchange, paraphrase without quotation marks. Write in British English. Maintain a formal, polished tone suitable for a graduate philosophy workshop.

Please begin with your brief brainstorming outline, then present the scenario, followed by the step-by-step analysis.
