In my comparative philosophy seminar we are debating whether Mencius and Xunzi genuinely disagree about the content of human nature, or whether their opposition conceals a shared structural view about the relation between innate disposition and ritual cultivation. One reading holds that Xunzi's claim that nature is bad and requires conscious transformation still presupposes the kind of proto-moral responsiveness Mencius identifies with the four sprouts; the opposing reading insists that Xunzi denies any innate moral orientation, making ritual an entirely external imposition. Write an essay weighing these interpretive positions. Reach a definite conclusion about whether the core dispute concerns human nature itself or rather the mechanism—learning, the heart/mind, and accumulated ritual—by which culture produces virtue, and justify that verdict with careful reference to both thinkers' accounts.
