Be specific about where classic debates—conditional analyses, multi-track dispositions, the causal-base requirement—are genuinely unresolved versus largely verbal, and address whether a “reverse-mask” case (where a disposition is present only when actively masked) reveals a new structural problem or collapses into existing ones. Do not present the options neutrally or end with an open list.
