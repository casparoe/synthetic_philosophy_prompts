But this suggests that Benacerraf's challenge is not merely an epistemological puzzle for platonism, but a demonstration that mathematical platonism is structurally analogous to theistic belief: both depend on purported knowledge of entities with which we have no causal interaction, and both must therefore reject the reliabilist framework to survive. Is my inference correct—that Benacerraf's dilemma effectively reduces platonism to a form of epistemic mysticism—or does the platonist have a principled reason to exempt mathematical intuition from the reliability constraint without thereby undermining the general applicability of that constraint?
