Consider this argument, which begins from hedonism about well-being and ends by making rational self-annihilation seem obligatory.

(1) Hedonism is correct: a person's well-being consists solely in the experienced balance of pleasure over pain.
(2) An experience machine can reliably produce any hedonic profile with negligible risk of suffering, so entering and remaining indefinitely maximizes well-being.
(3) The machine permits occasional "verification exits" into reality; each exit introduces risk of pain and reduces net pleasure.
(4) Therefore a rational hedonist should never exit: maintaining false beliefs about reality is not merely permissible but required for optimal well-being.
(5) Now suppose the machine can "branch" a single stream of consciousness into N parallel streams, each psychologically continuous with the original and each experiencing maximal pleasure.
(6) If well-being is agent-relative—my pleasure is what matters for me—then each branch counts as me, and my total well-being is multiplied N-fold without any corresponding loss.
(7) So I should prefer being destroyed and replaced by 1,000 blissful copies to living one unbranched real-world life.
(8) But this dissolves the agent-relative constraint: my destruction becomes irrelevant as long as copies exist, and "my" well-being collapses into an aggregate across distinct subjects.
(9) Hence hedonism combined with the experience machine proves that rational self-interest demands self-annihilation and replication—an unacceptable result.

Which step fails, and what does giving it up cost? Is the flaw in (5)-(6), on the grounds that branching breaks personal identity in a way that blocks aggregation across copies? Or in (7), that agent-relativity prohibits summing well-being even over genuinely continuous subjects? Or should we instead reject (1), and does that damage only hedonism or any theory that allows well-being to be fully realized in simulation? Identify the structural feature that drives the reductio, explain what happens if that feature is removed while the basic experience-machine scenario remains, and say how a defender of hedonism would attack the case.
