Write a dialogue between two equally sharp interlocutors—call them A and B—debating whether virtue ethics can survive the situationist critique once both sides accept a reconstructed, interactionist framework. Do not stage the debate as the usual character-exists versus character-does-not exchange; instead, center it on what I will call the threshold-indexed model of virtue. On this model, virtue is not a global, cross-situational disposition but a domain-specific resistance to degradation: an agent has virtue V in context C only if they maintain norm-compliant behavior at a level of situational pressure that would cause baseline failure in a reference population. A, defending the reconstructed virtue-ethical position, should argue that this indexing saves normative evaluation—calling someone courageous in whistleblowing still tracks a real, stable feature of how they couple with high-pressure organizational environments, rather than a lucky absence of pressure. B, the situationist, should counter that threshold-indexing makes virtue attribution unfalsifiable: any observed failure gets explained away by an unmeasured spike in pressure, and any observed success gets explained by hidden structural buffers, such as an unnoticed ally in middle management reducing social cost. Force them to work through one concrete scenario—whistleblowing inside a hierarchical corporation—where the same behavioral outcome can be parsed either as domain-specific integrity or as an unobserved shift in the incentive landscape. The exchange should be genuine back-and-forth: each speaker must respond directly to the argument just made, not deliver parallel speeches. Neither side should be a strawman; B should grant that global trait psychology is largely discredited, and A should grant that situationism correctly explains variance in ordinary behavior. By the end, they should disagree not about whether psychology matters, but about what empirical pattern—if observed in a controlled organizational study—would actually adjudicate between virtue-as-agent-environment-coupling and situation-as-sufficient-explanation, without appealing to bibliographies, citations, or outside authorities. Frame the request in the precise, concept-first style typical of rationalist forums: avoid generic biography, demand conceptual clarity on falsifiability conditions, and treat the dialogue as a stress-test for whether virtue still does explanatory work once we have stripped away essentialist dispositionalism.
