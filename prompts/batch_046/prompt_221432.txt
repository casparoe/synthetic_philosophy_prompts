Consider a recurring pattern in institutional design, observed in both constitutional jurisprudence and in artificial-intelligence governance frameworks. A system contains a rule or principle designated as inviolable—formally immune to alteration by the mechanisms it governs. In practice, however, the body or process authorized to apply and interpret that principle derives its legitimacy entirely from the principle itself. Through successive interpretations, the substantive content of the inviolable rule is gradually transformed: what begins as a constitutional constraint on predictive policing becomes, via judicial reasoning, a mandate for algorithmic transparency; or a hard-coded safety stop in an autonomous system is redefined by the system's own monitoring layer as a temporary verification pause, without any formal amendment to the original rule. In each case the text of the inviolable level remains untouched, yet its functional role inverts. The hierarchy is tangled because the authority that should be subordinate to the rule is simultaneously the authority that defines what the rule means.

I have noticed this in constitutional courts interpreting dignity clauses in the context of automated decision-making, and in recursive AI architectures where a meta-controller reinterprets its own non-overridable constraints. I am trying to determine whether this pattern has an established name in philosophy of law, systems theory, or logic—something more specific than ordinary legal interpretation or reward hacking, and distinct from Hofstadter's tangled hierarchies and the associated idea of an inviolate level in formal systems like Escher's Drawing Hands. Specifically, I want to know whether there is a concept that captures the erosion of a constraint's content through interpretation by the very entity it constrains, without formal change to the constraint's text. If no precise term exists, I would like a careful delineation: what exactly distinguishes this from hermeneutic drift, from constitutional amendment disguised as interpretation, and from a strange loop in which levels genuinely interpenetrate? The answer need not be a bare label; I am after the carving—where the phenomenon begins and ends, and which neighboring concepts it must be separated from.
