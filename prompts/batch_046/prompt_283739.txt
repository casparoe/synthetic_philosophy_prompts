One side keeps saying guilt requires a conscious subject who can actually feel remorse, so "Germany" in 2024 can't literally be guilty of 1945, it's just descendants who have obligations. So is this disagreement actually about metaphysics of group identity, or about whether moral language should track inner states or social functions?
