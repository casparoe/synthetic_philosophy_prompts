Draft the joint statement of an adversarial collaboration between micro-foundationalists and structural emergentists in social science, focused specifically on explaining persistent organizational pathologies—such as the reproduction of status hierarchies inside formally meritocratic bureaucracies—where macro-level patterns appear resistant to individual-level intervention.

Structure the document as follows. First, a numbered section of at least five claims both sides can sign, covering agreed empirical observations (the pattern exists, certain individual-level interventions have failed, multi-level descriptions are permissible as heuristics), agreed conceptual boundaries (the debate is not about whether individuals exist), and agreed methodological norms (predictions should be testable without requiring either side to abandon its ontology). Second, a disagreements table that separates three layers: ontological disagreements (do macro-structures possess causal powers not reducible to individual properties?), methodological disagreements (must every valid explanation cite mechanisms at the individual level?), and normative disagreements (does the answer justify top-down institutional restructuring?). State each side’s position in wording that side would accept, with no straw-manning. Third, identify at least three cruxes—sub-questions such that resolving them would move at least one party. For each crux, specify one concrete case or piece of evidence both sides agree bears on it (for instance, a natural experiment tracking post-merger cultural persistence in firms with identical formal rules but different pre-merger histories, or an intervention study comparing individual bias-training versus structural rule-revision), and record in advance what each side predicts the result will show. Fourth, include a brief diagnostic paragraph identifying any disagreements that are merely verbal—particularly disputes over whether “cause” or “explanation” can be applied to institutions—rather than substantive. Keep the full statement under 700 words. Do not include bibliographies, citations, reading lists, or appeals to highly specialized recent formalisms; write in formal, neutral prose suitable for co-signature by representatives of both camps.
