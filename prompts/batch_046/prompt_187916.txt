Saye defends the memory; he thinks archives embed power relations and that collective memory carries normative obligations—like stewardship, belonging, and mutual duty—that the record can't even see, let alone replace.

make it real back-and-forth, not two monologues past each other. both should be equally competent—no strawman. if Ren raises epistemic reliability, Saye needs to address whether reliability is even the right criterion here, not just shout about colonialism. if Saye brings up normative weight and lived obligation, Ren should push hard on whether that gives anyone license to revise geology. keep the voices casual—like tired grad students who know the theory but are also slightly annoyed with each other and the coffee machine. include one or two small typos in your setup if it feels natural, but keep the dialogue itself readable.
