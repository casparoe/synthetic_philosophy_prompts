(2) if you could've branched with all prior conditions identical, then the outcome wasn't fully determined by those conditions.

(3) in a physical system, either an event is determined by prior states, or it's just random noise (like quantum decay or a bad RNG seed).

(4) so if my choice isn't determined, some part of it has to come from random physical noise in my brain.

(5) but then i'm not the author — it's like a cosmic ray flipped my decision. that's not freedom, that's a glitch.

(7) so by the same reasoning, if quantum noise breaks the causal chain inside me, that's not me exercising free will, that's hardware randomness.

(8) therefore either my choices are fully determined (no free will worth wanting), or they're partly random (no authorship), and any system with a random generator — my laptop, a weather sim, whatever — gets the same status. that can't be right.

where does this actually fall apart? i definately want to keep (1) and (5) if i can — is there a hidden slide between not determined and caused by noise? or is the mistake thinking that free will requires that branching in the first place?
