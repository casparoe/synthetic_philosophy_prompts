Give an example—concrete and not a generic Frankfurt case—of a conceptual tension within the debate over free will “worth wanting.” The scenario should feature an agent who fully satisfies the functional conditions associated with that variety: effective deliberation, responsiveness to reasons, capacity for self-revision, and wholehearted endorsement of first-order desires. At the same time, the agent’s second-order evaluative framework was gradually and irreversibly cultivated over years by an external institution or environment that systematically eliminated competing values and reasoning patterns before the agent could ever form them. Use the case to illustrate whether meeting the “worth wanting” conditions automatically secures the kind of ultimate sourcehood that libertarians require, or whether it reveals a hidden subtype—socially embedded self-constitution—in which present endorsement severs rather than guarantees authorship. Specify precisely what makes the puzzle conceptual rather than merely empirically implausible, and say which feature of the case forces the choice between splitting Dennett’s variety and collapsing the libertarian demand.
