The question asked whether a being could be Gregorian—employing language or other cognitive tools—without being Popperian, that is, without the capacity for internal hypothesis-testing; students were invited to illustrate with a hypothetical case.

Gregorian capacities are not an independent mental kind but an externalization of Popperian ones. So any genuinely tool-using creature must already be Popperian. Even a purely reinforcement-trained agent that learns to manipulate symbols—call it a Skinnerian-Gregorian hybrid—would not be truly Gregorian, since without an internal model it merely associates tokens with rewards. The taxonomy is nested, but Gregorian adds no new cognitive architecture; it only supplies technology for minds that already think.
