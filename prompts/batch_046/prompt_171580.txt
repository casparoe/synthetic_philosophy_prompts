Write a dialogue between two equally competent interlocutors—Dr. Elena Vasquez, who defends an integrationist account of pain's badness, and Dr. Marcus Chen, who defends a phenomenalist account—debating whether clinical cases of pain asymbolia undermine the claim that pain is intrinsically bad. Write the dialogue in polished, formal American English, ensure neither interlocutor is a strawman, and do not include bibliographies, citations, or requests for further reading.
