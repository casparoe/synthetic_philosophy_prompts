I am finalizing the talk I will give next Thursday at an interdisciplinary workshop on collective agency and corporate law, and I need a rigorous prep sheet for the Q&A rather than a general defense of the view. My thesis extends Galen Strawson's Basic Argument—roughly, that true moral responsibility requires being causa sui, which no agent can satisfy—into the domain of collective moral responsibility: I claim that if the argument holds for individuals, it applies with even greater force to business corporations, because organizational character and culture are not self-authored but are path-dependent products of market selection, regulatory history, and leadership turnover that no collective member controls.
