Write a dialouge between an Abhidharma philosopher who takes anatta literally — only the five skandhas exist, and the "person" is just a conventional label like a chariot — and a Pudgalavadin who claims there's a real but indeterminate (anirvacaniya) person that carries karma across lives, neither identical to nor different from the aggregates. Have them actually respond to each other: the Abhidharmika should argue that the Pudgala is just a smuggled atman and that conventional designation is enough for rebirth, and the Pudgalavadin should push back that without a real bearer of karma, moral responsibility and the continuity of suffering don't make sense. Don't let either side become a strawman. Keep it in American English, please, and don't include a bibliography or citations.
