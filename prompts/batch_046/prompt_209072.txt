Consequently, heterophenomenology is essentially a form of eliminativism: it denies that there is any real phenomenological world to study. Furthermore, since the method requires explicit linguistic self-report, it is definitionally impossible to apply it to any system that lacks language. Even if an animal or AI produced complex behavior that functioned like a report, there would be no heterophenomenological world to investigate for that system."
