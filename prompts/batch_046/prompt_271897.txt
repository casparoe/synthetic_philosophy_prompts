I hold that the modern revival of Stoicism misapplies the classical dichotomy of control by treating belief-formation and emotional regulation as if they fell on the "within our power" side of the boundary in exactly the same direct manner as voluntary bodily movement, when in fact they belong to a mediated, indirect category that depends on environmental scaffolding, attentional conditions, and social inputs no less than strategic outcomes like career success do. To preserve coherence, I argue that Stoicism should replace its sharp bifurcation with a graded taxonomy: direct causal control over bodily action, indirect strategic influence over long-term conditions, and mediated epistemic uptake over judgments and affective states — the last being genuinely responsive to reason yet never fully insulated from external contingency. This seems to dissolve the distinctive Stoic claim that our inner life is uniquely sovereign.

What would have to be true for this graded-boundary view to be wrong, and for the sharp dichotomy to be defensible? I want an honest assessment of its exposure, sorted by how decisive each challenge would be. Which findings in cognitive psychology or neuroscience — for instance, about the mechanisms of belief revision or affective regulation — would show that judgment is directly controllable in a way that strategic outcomes are not? Which arguments from the philosophy of mental action, or from compatibilist accounts of agency, could restore a robust internal/external divide? Do the classical Stoic texts (Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius, Seneca) consistently presuppose the strict bifurcation, or do they already allow for mediated internal processes? And if the boundary is indeed porous, does Stoicism collapse into generic practical wisdom, or can the graded version be reconstructed without losing its normative force? Rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, distinguish what defenders of the sharp dichotomy would concede from what they would dismiss, and say whether insulating the view against these challenges strengthens it or renders it unfalsifiable.
