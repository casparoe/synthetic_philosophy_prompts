I've been discussing governance with friends in a philosophy reading group, and a practical dispute at my cooperative workspace has begun to feel like it touches on something structurally deeper than ordinary disagreement. For about fifteen years, our shared workshop has operated under an unwritten agreement about "productive use" of common equipment—originally intended to prevent recreational monopolization, but never precisely codified. Members have rotated in and out continuously, and past decisions have been recorded in meeting minutes that now function almost like a chain of analogies: each new case is evaluated by reference to prior approvals rather than to an explicit definition. Recently, a long-standing member began hosting a weekly podcast recording session in the main workshop room. Some members insist this violates the original agreement, noting that we have previously tolerated a ceramics side-business and occasional music practice; others counter that those were narrow exceptions, not precedents for broadcast media. We have tried to formalize our reasoning by constructing what one member called a "precedent ladder"—linking each current judgment strictly to its immediate predecessor—but we have found that applying the ladder consistently forces us toward either total permissiveness or total restriction, with no stable intermediate stopping point. Different members hold genuinely incompatible but equally sincere recollections of what the founding standard required, and our original charter is too vague to break the tie. I am struggling with whether there is even a correct answer to whether our present rule is the same rule we began with, or whether the concept of "productive use" has simply drifted beyond recovery. If I advise the group to treat the rule as genuinely indeterminate, am I abandoning the possibility of shared governance, or am I recognizing a real structural limitation? How should a small, self-governing community proceed when its own institutional history fails to determine what it now means?
