For a philosophy of biology workshop, specify the desiderata an adequate theory of life must satisfy to count as resolving the definitional problem, with the motivating case or conceptual tension behind each. Mark which criteria are non-negotiable and which reflect camp-specific preferences (for instance, metabolism-first versus replication-first versus enactivist commitments). The list must explicitly address how the theory should classify three borderline cases: viruses that lack autonomous metabolism, synthetic lipid-vesicle protocells that maintain electrochemical gradients without genetic polymers, and hypothetical self-replicating crystal lattices in non-aqueous solvents that undergo selection but contain no informational macromolecules analogous to nucleic acids. If any subset of your desiderata—such as substrate independence combined with evolutionary openness and thermodynamic closure—turns out to be jointly unsatisfiable, identify the exact conflict and state which criterion ought to yield. Keep the weight on the criteria and their justifications rather than on scoring existing theories.
