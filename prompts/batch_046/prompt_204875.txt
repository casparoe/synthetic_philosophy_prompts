Plan the teaching arc: what has to be in place before they can even see the problem with 'fall' (probably something about the Eastern Empire still running adn thriving?), then the one concrete case that should carry the whole lesson — I'm leaning on the Vandal kingdom in Africa, where Roman tax collectors and law courts kept running for decades after the 'fall', but tell me if that's too obscure or if there's a better example that has both rupture and continuity baked in. Where exactly in the sequence do I head each one off before it hardens? What's the check at the end — not a quiz, but something that proves they've actually stopped using 'fall' as a category rather than just parroting 'it transformed'?
