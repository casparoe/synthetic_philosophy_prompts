There's this pattern in mechanistic interperetability work where people map out directions in a model's activations and label them with country names or things like deception, then do activation patching like they're literally editing the concept itself. Is it some kind of internalist semantics where the vector just is about France, or can it survive on a purely distributional story? And if we took the symbol grounding problem seriously — like if representations need causal or historical ties to the world — what would have to change? Would we stop treating activation editing as concept surgery and start seeing it as just tweaking correlations? I'm trying to figure out if the field is being philosophically incoherent or if there's a consistent view I'm missing.
