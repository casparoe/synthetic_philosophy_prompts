Call the other side the Contrastive Skeptics: they think the framing is conceptually broken, treating "Britain" and "China" as unitary actors, ignoring internal regional heterogeneity like the Yangzi delta versus inland provinces, smuggling in teleology by treating industrialization as an inevitable endpoint, and mistaking correlation for a real counterfactual dependency.

Use this structure: first, a numbered list of agreed claims about comparative method, about what we actually know regarding 18th-century economic conditions, and about the limits of macro-scale counterfactual reasoning — keep it specific, not vacuous.
