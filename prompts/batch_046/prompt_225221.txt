The PI does not claim living religious belief drives resistance; instead she proposes a mechanism she terms "value-rational selective retention." Under this framing, historical Protestant-heritage regions do not merely "prefer" transparency, but apply a stricter moral-accountability filter that treats opaque optimization as categorically illegitimate rather than merely expensive. A cultural value thus doesn't push actors toward an outcome; it restructures the option space by defining which institutional failures are tolerable and which innovations survive scrutiny. Our committee is split. Some members insist this is correlation in causal clothing—culture is a narrative overlay on economic path dependence and prior bureaucratic structure—while others think the selective-retention framing offers a genuine causal account if the mechanism can be isolated from material constraints.

Please draft the formal memo I will circulate before the vote.
