Translate Benjamin Franklin's program of moral perfection—the thirteen virtues, the weekly tracking grid, the strategy of sequential rather than simultaneous mastery, and his explicit concession that perfect virtue is unattainable—into that vocabulary, element by element. Then deliver the residue report: does Franklin come out as essentially an 18th-century practitioner of behavioral self-modification, or does the translation fail at points that reveal his project was never merely about changing behavior? Specify whether each failure belongs to the limits of habit-engineering vocabulary or to an obscurity in Franklin's own account. Do not let the vocabulary explain Franklin away; use it as a filter and report what it blocks. Maximum 450 words.
