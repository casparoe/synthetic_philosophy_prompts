I want you to adopt the role of an expert political philosopher and dialogue writer. Please begin by briefly thinking through the central tension: does cross-partisan misunderstanding stem mainly from individual bad faith, or from structural conditions that render good faith invisible? A must defend the claim that this dynamic demonstrates fundamental bad faith on the part of opponents; B must defend the claim that it demonstrates how echo-chamber structures distort the appearance of good faith, without conceding that individuals are dishonest. The exchange must feature genuine back-and-forth, with each speaker engaging directly with the other's actual argument rather than talking past it. Present your response as exactly five numbered exchanges (1–5), each consisting of one or two sentences, with no introductory or concluding paragraph. Write in British English.
