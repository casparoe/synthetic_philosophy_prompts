My friend refuses to comply. The board insists that the bylaw is valid law regardless of its moral content or practical difficulty: it was properly enacted, is consistently applied, and creates binding obligations that can only be changed through further democratic process. My friend, however, argues that a rule so radically at odds with the cooperative’s foundational commitments, and so impossible to satisfy in good faith, was never genuinely law at all—merely an authoritarian decree wearing legal clothing—and that she is therefore free to disregard the sanctions.

Please think through this carefully, avoiding generic interpersonal advice.
