Write a Petrarchan sonnet (ABBAABBA CDECDE) that stages the scale argument concerning insect sentience. In the octave, dwell on a single, ephemeral insect—perhaps a mayfly at dusk—stressing the near-vanishing probability that it possesses any subjective experience, using imagery that renders its mechanistic smallness almost undeniable and invites dismissal. At the volta in line nine, pivot not to a different creature but to the arithmetic of aggregation: the same near-zero credence in individual sentience, multiplied across trillions of lives and countless generations, produces an expected moral weight that the singular intuition cannot absorb or dispel. Let the form register this structurally—perhaps the octave’s enclosed rhymes mirror the closure of the individual case, while the sestet’s more open or alternating sounds evoke the unbounded spread of multitudes. Do not resolve the tension into either advocacy for intervention or a shrug about nature’s indifference; end with the two scales—individual negligible, collective overwhelming—held in precise, unresolved equilibrium, so that someone who knows the argument recognizes its logical form rather than merely its vocabulary.
