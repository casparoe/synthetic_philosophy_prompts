Is there a name for this? Imagine a town where everyone agrees they'd all be safer if each household could credibly promise not to open the gate for strangers after dark—like Parfit's hitchiker, but for a whole community. To make the promise believable, each family has to publicly destroy something valuable in advance, not just decide it mentally. So they burn resources to lock in future irrationality. It's not the dollar auction since there's no bidding war, and it's not handicap signaling because the burnt goods don't show fitness—they just make breaking the promise too costly later. I've seen versions of this in discussions about collective precommitment and in some ritual practices, but I can't tell if there's a proper term that separates it from escalation of commitment. Does it have one, and if not, how do you draw the boundary?
