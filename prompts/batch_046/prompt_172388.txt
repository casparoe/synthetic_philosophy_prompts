I have recently become uncomfortably aware of my own mechanical rigidity: if my morning routine is interrupted at any point, I tend to restart the entire sequence from the beginning, much as the sphex wasp will repeatedly return its prey to the burrow entrance if it is moved. I understand sphexishness not as global determinism but as a localized failure of representational flexibility—a specific loop in which an agent cannot represent its goal independently of the stereotyped behavior. On this view, can a globally flexible agent be locally sphexish in particular domains? If so, does my repetition of this routine mean that free will is suspended specifically within that loop, restored only when I deliberately break the pattern? Or does the presence of global representational capacity mean that local rigidity is never genuinely sphexish, but merely a habit exercised with full agency?
