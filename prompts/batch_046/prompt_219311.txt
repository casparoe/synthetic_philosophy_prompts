Restate Dennett's critique of the Cartesian theater and his multiple drafts model using only the vocabulary of ensemble forecasting, distributed sensor networks, and data assimilation—ensemble members, parameterizations, ground-truth absence, assimilation cycles, consensus distributions, and the explicit lack of a master model.
