For a workshop on metaphysical identity I need a consistency audit of the package I've settled into. Here is where I stand: (1) I accept a necessary version of the identity of indiscernibles, but I restrict it to purely intrinsic qualitative properties—relational, modal, and spatiotemporal properties do not enter the identity conditions of concrete particulars. (2) I take Black's symmetric two-sphere universe to be genuinely possible, with the spheres numerically distinct despite sharing every intrinsic qualitative feature. (3) I reject haecceities, primitive thisness, and bare substrata as ontologically extravagant. (4) I hold that spatial location is not an intrinsic property of an object but a relational feature of the object-space system, so "occupying region R" does not belong to a sphere's intrinsic nature. (5) Consequently I deny that weak discernibility via an irreflexive distance relation saves PII in Black's case, since that relation is exactly the kind of relational property my version of PII excludes. (6) I endorse a bundle theory in which particulars are nothing but bundles of intrinsic qualitative universals. (7) I grant that quantum particles can be numerically distinct despite lacking intrinsic qualitative differences, but I insist macroscopic spheres are not analogous—their distinctness must be accounted for within my framework, not by appeal to quantum ontology.

Do these hang together? Specifically: does my restriction of PII to intrinsic properties in (1), together with (3) and (6), actually entail that the spheres in (2) are identical, making (2) incompatible with the rest? Is (4) consistent with (6), or does a bundle of intrinsic universals need some spatial anchor that makes location intrinsic after all? Does (7) introduce an arbitrary micro-macro cutoff that undermines the universality of (1)? Identify which tensions are only apparent—particularly whether (5) genuinely blocks the weak-discernibility rescue or whether I am misclassifying that relation—and rank the real tensions by severity. For each, state the least costly revision and what it costs me elsewhere in the package. Do not provide a reading list; I need an audit of internal coherence only.
