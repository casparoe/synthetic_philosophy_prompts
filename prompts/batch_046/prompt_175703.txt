'Brain death means irreversible loss of all brain function including the brainstem, while circulatory death is permanent heart stoppage. The Uniform Determination of Death Act lets US doctors use either standard. Because brain-dead patients stay on ventilators, their heart still pumps, so organs stay oxygenated and surgeons have more time. With circulatory death they have to wait for the heart to stop, observe about five minutes, then rush retrieval before kidneys and liver suffer warm ischemia. The apnea test is important here because it shows the cerebral cortex has lost the ability to drive breathing on its own.
