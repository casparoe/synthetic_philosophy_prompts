To prevent the piece from becoming a generic restatement of free-will skepticism, inject the following niche institutional twist: Apex offers an employee wellness benefit called the “Exponential Origin Discount Program.” In this program, workers are taught to apply a hyperbolic discount curve to their own causal histories, treating prior causal influences as exponentially less “their own” the further back in time they extend, in a futile attempt to reach a present-moment state of uncaused self-origination. The handbook must present this program with complete seriousness, explaining its mechanics—immediate present rewards of self-origination are overvalued relative to distant past causes—and showing precisely why even perfect hyperbolic discounting cannot eliminate the need for a prior nature that the agent did not create.
