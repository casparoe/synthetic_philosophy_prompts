A clinical researcher is evaluating whether to recommend approval of a cognitive-training app for elderly patients. In the trial, the app group showed slightly greater cognitive decline than the placebo group when all participants were aggregated. Yet within each baseline-cognitive-score stratum—mild, moderate, and severe impairment—the app group showed less decline than the corresponding placebo subgroup. The researcher asks whether the aggregate result should block approval, or whether the stratified pattern shows the app is genuinely protective across every patient category. Advise her, explaining what the statistical structure implies for interpreting the overall figure and for weighing the subgroup results in her regulatory recommendation.
