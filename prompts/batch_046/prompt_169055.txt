(4) Maya said that misses the point: in a human, "I" is anchored in embodied experience, but in the room the token "I" is just another syntactic placeholder with no experiential anchor, so adding the rule doesn't create real self-awareness, just fake syntax. (5) Jake replied that "anchored in experience" is doing all the work - if you can specify the functional role of self-reference precisely, then fulfilling that role is all there is to being an "I," and Maya is just insisting on a ghost in the machine.
