Here is an argument I sketched for a workshop on open societies, but now it strikes me as swallowing the very distinction Popper wants to defend. (1) In The Poverty of Historicism, Popper's core claim is that no predictive law of historical development is possible, because human knowledge grows in ways that cannot themselves be predicted, and that growth is part of the very history being predicted. (2) Let me formalize the underlying principle: a domain is reflexively open if entities within it can learn propositions about the domain and alter their behavior inside it on that basis. (3) History is reflexively open, but so are markets (traders learn supply curves and adjust), legal systems (judges learn sentencing patterns), and even evolutionary niches (organisms adapt to detectable selection pressures). (4) So if reflexive openness blocks deterministic laws in history, it ought to block them in every domain where agents have epistemic access to the patterns governing them. (5) Yet Popper clearly accepts evolutionary biology and allows piecemeal social predictions—for instance, that a specific tariff will raise domestic prices—despite legislators and traders knowing about tariffs. (6) The natural reply is that historicism claims holistic trajectory-laws for an entire society, whereas permitted predictions concern isolated mechanisms that leave the overall system open. (7) But a historicist law can also be local: one might claim only that industrial concentration increases under capitalist property relations, without predicting every revolution or war. (8) So the line between a forbidden historical law and a permitted economic prediction collapses unless we can specify precisely how much reflexivity a domain tolerates before its regularities dissolve. (9) Absent that specification, Popper's argument proves too much: it either rules out all predictive social science, including the kinds he wants to preserve, or it fails to rule out historicism at all. Which step should I reject? I would most like to keep (1) and (6)—I think Popper genuinely intended only holistic trajectory-predictions to be blocked, not every local regularity—but I want to know exactly what I am paying to save (6), and whether the price is a hidden ad hoc boundary between history and everything else.
