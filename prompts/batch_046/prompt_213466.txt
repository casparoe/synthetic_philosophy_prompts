my verdicts on cases:

torture for fun is wrong, and this feels like it tracks a real feature - societies that allow it tend to collapse in predictable ways, so "wrongness" here is doing real explanatory work.

breaking a promise just to save five minutes is also wrong, but i'm less sure it's explaining anything deep; maybe it's just a coordination rule we could swap out?

wearing white after labor day is wrong according to etiquette, but clearly conventional - no explanatory load at all.

falsifying data in a study is wrong, but this feels internal to science itself, not like an independent moral fact imported from outside.
