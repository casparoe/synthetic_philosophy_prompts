But I'm worried that saying post-hoc binding sneaks in a Cartesian theater — like there's a specific moment where the revision happens — which seems like exactly the mistake the distinction is supposed to avoid. So does applying this framework to phi actually illuminate the timing of conscious experiance, or does it just relabel the puzzle? And if it's truly Stalinesque, should we say there's no false intermediate experience being corrected, but rather no intermediate experience whatsoever? I might be mixing up the perceptual mechanism with the phenomenology, and I'd like comments especially on whether I'm hiding a premise about real-time assembly.
