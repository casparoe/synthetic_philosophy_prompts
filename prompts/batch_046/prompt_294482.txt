me and my friend just had this long argument after seminar about whether Kepler was basically a hypocrite—he figures out ellipses and then still casts horoscopes for cash. my friend thinks its pure grift, but i think people miss the weirder middle ground: Kepler doesnt think planets send physical rays that push on bodies like clock gears. he thinks the earth has a kind of geometic soul, and astrology works like recogntion, not causation—harmonic angles in the sky that a soul already attuned to shape can read, like seeing a face in noise. no mechanism, just correspondence.

can you write a sonnet about that? i want the octave to build the naive mechanical picture (hidden gears, causal influence, planets as weights) and then the volta at line 9 to break it: the influence isnt a shove, its a pattern being recognized. the closing couplet should land on the idea that the sky offers the form, and the soul already knows how to see it—so nothing is being pushed, only matched. keep it accurate enough that a historian of science wouldnt wince, but dont just name-drop Kepler; let the form actually do the work. please end with a one-sentence summary as the very last line.
