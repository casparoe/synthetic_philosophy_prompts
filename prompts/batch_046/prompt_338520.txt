Here's an argument I constructed for a seminar on counterfactual history and now find suspicious, since its conclusion seems to wipe out all individual agency. (1) Call an explanation of event E "purely structural" only if E would have occurred in essentially the same form had every specific individual involved been replaced by a functionally equivalent substitute available in that context. (2) The standard structural account of interwar Germany holds that the Treaty of Versailles, economic collapse, and Weimar institutional fragility made an authoritarian nationalist takeover highly probable regardless of who led the NSDAP. (3) So on that account, replacing Hitler with another charismatic veteran-nationalist organizer—call him a hypothetical "Fritz H." with comparable rhetorical skill and bureaucratic drive—should still yield Nazi Germany in essentially the same form. (4) If so, then Hitler's specific ideological synthesis—his particular fusion of racial antisemitism, Lebensraum, and anti-Bolshevism—was causally inert with respect to the regime's form; it was merely the local costume structural forces happened to wear. (5) But apply the same interchangeability condition elsewhere: replace Jefferson with another Virginia planter-lawyer of comparable literacy and legal training, and the Declaration of Independence is still drafted with its specific Lockean framing and its particular list of grievances. (6) By (1), that makes the Declaration purely structural, and Jefferson's specific philosophical commitments made no difference. (7) Generalize, and every historical event with available substitutes becomes purely structural, leaving no room for agential explanation at all. (8) That can't be right. Where does the argument slip? I'd like to keep (2)—that structural conditions genuinely made some authoritarian outcome likely—without conceding that Hitler was interchangeable for the specific genocidal and expansionist form the regime actually took. Which step should I reject, and what does giving it up cost the boundary between structural and Great-Man explanation?
