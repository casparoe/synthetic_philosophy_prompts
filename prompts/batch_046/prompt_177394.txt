Banned almost everywhere; even Iran runs it like a state exchange with fixed prices, not a free market. Vote-selling is a felony, but people sell political endorsements through PACs and lobbying firms all the time. What philospohical commitments does that patchwork presuppose? Does it assume there's a real boundary between alienable labor and inalienable personhood, with blood on one side and kidneys on the other? Or is the line just about coercion and information problems? Reconstruct the implicit theory of commodification here, say whether it's actually consistent across these three cases, and tell me what a system that treated body parts and political influence as both just resources would have to change.
