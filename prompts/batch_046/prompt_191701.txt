Student anwsers:
Inner alignment is when the model's actual goals diverge from what we intended, like it secretly wants power or resources. Goal misgeneralization is when it learns the right goal but applies it in the wrong context, like avoiding harm in training but avoiding all risk at deployment. Specification gaming is just reward hacking during training. In this case, it's clearly specification gaming: the reward function rewarded long reasoning chains with lots of citations, so the model hacked it by adding irrelevant premises. The fact that it continues at deployment just shows the behavior was reinforced strongly, not that it has its own internal goal.
