Is this framing accurate, or is the stalemate actually of a different kind: a hidden empirical disagreement about whether moral decay causes social collapse, a verbal ambiguity in “harm” versus “social harm,” or two groups answering different questions (the scope of legitimate state power versus the conditions for social stability) under one heading?
