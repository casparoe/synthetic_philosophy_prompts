Here's an argument I built for a seminar that now smells wrong. (1) Frankfurt cases show moral responsibility doesn't need alternative possibilities—Jones kills Smith freely even though Black was standing by to force him. So PAP is false for actions. (2) If the blocker logic works for actions it should work for belief too, since belief-formation is also something we can be blamed for. (3) Picture a doxastic blocker: a hidden mechanism that'd make you reject climate evidence if you were about to accept it, but you accept it on your own. You couldn't have ended up skeptical. (4) So you're responsible for that belief despite lacking the alternative. (5) But now notice: for literally any belief B, we can imagine such a blocker in the causal backround. Either you formed B naturally, or the blocker was there and you'd have formed it anyway. So for every belief you hold, there's no genuinely open alernative path you could have taken. (6) That means PAP is globally false for belief, and you're responsible for all your beliefs—racist ones, delusional ones, ones drilled into you as a kid—with no excuse ever available. (7) That can't be right. So either Frankfurt reasoning collapses back onto actions only, or we have to bite the bullet on (6). Where does the slide happen? I'd like to keep (1) and (4) if I can, but they seem to chew eachother up. Also please say how confident you are in what you claim and single out the point you're least sure about.
