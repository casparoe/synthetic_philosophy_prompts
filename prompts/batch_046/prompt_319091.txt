Consider the pessimistic meta-induction in scientific realism: mature theories have repeatedly been replaced by successors that deny previously posited entities, challenging naive realist commitments. Suppose you are advising a research group with three years of funding to pursue one cluster of open questions arising from this tension. Which problem should they prioritize: whether causal-role preservation can be formalized independently of structural preservation; whether historical cases such as the luminiferous ether or caloric fluid exhibit genuine causal-role continuity rather than mere heuristic similarity; or whether a non-circular normative criterion for “maturity” can be reconstructed to restrict the induction’s scope? Defend your ordering by identifying which question is upstream of the others, which is tractable without specialized archival scholarship, and what conceptual result in the next few years would cause you to reverse the ranking.
