Write a short story about a gravity editor—someone who helps people restructure thier center of narrative gravity after trauma, treating the self like Dennett's center of gravity: a useful abstraction, not a little soul inside. The protagonist is asked to merge her own narrative center with a dying stranger's so his story doesn't just stop. She knows the recalculation might not resolve back into her. Don't make the ending obvious—let the reader feel whether she's saving someone or erasing herself. Keep the sci-fi minimal, just the premise.
