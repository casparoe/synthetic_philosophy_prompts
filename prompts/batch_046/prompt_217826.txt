In CBT protocols for depression, therapists instruct clients to label intrusive negative thoughts as 'the depression talking' rather than there own beliefs, and to act against them without waiting for belief-change. What view of self-deception — or of the divided self — does that technique presuppose? Can it work if self-deception is literally impossible (because you cant both believe P and believe not-P with the same mental access), or does it require a weaker model where one can have 'alienated' beliefs? And is the practice consistent with the same field's insistence that insight — genuinely recognizing ones own patterns — is necessary for lasting change?
