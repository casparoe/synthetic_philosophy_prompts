i sit on our uni's AI governance board and we have this pilot request thats kinda stuck in my head. a research group wants to deploy their "Embodied Dialogue System" in the student wellness center for a semester. its not just a chatbot—its coupled to a haptic/proprioceptive layer that maps language tokens onto direct bodily states (pressure, temperature, vestibular feedback) through what they call a "causal-indexical bridge." they claim this solves the symbol grounding problem for real: the symbols arent just statistically correlated with other symbols, theyre anchored in non-representational physical causation, so the system has intrinsic rather than derived intentionality about things like "pain" or "anxiety."
