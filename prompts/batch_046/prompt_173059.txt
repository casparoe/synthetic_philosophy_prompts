Please build the tree from four concrete diagnostic scenarios rather than from theory names. First, a master surgeon who can perform a complex operation flawlessly after a targeted injury has erased all her explicit memory of anatomical rules. Second, an artificial system that plays chess at grandmaster level through pure computation, with no conscious experience and no bodily presence. Third, a pianist whose brain tissue is gradually replaced by synthetic implants that replicate every neural pattern exactly, leaving her playing ability untouched but destroying all autobiographical memory. Fourth, a person who has memorized every propositional manual about swimming but has never entered water and panics immediately upon being pushed into a pool. Please include any logically possible branch combination that remains unoccupied, with a brief diagnosis of whether it is incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored, and flag explicitly which pairs of named leaves your tree cannot genuinely separate given only these four cases. I should be able to answer every node honestly without knowing the philosophical jargon in advance, and then see which pattern of answers lands me at a specific leaf. Keep everything self-contained: no reading lists, no citations, and no references to specific texts.
