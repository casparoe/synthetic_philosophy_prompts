She said if there's literally no difference between them, then Leibniz's law is false, since there are clearly two spheres, not one. Marge said okay, but what about two particles in the exact same quantum state inside a superpostion where even position isn't defined? Aren't those literally Black spheres? Steve said that's misunderstanding superposition — the wavefunction describes the pair together — but he admitted he wasn't totally sure. Then Marge threw in another twist: she said maybe "identity" itself is just a practical bookkeeping tool we use, not something nature actually cares about. Steve said that sounds like giving up on reality, and if identity is just a tool then her Black spheres example collapses because there's no real fact of the matter either way.
