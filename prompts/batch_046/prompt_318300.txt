I kept insisting that phenomena such as specification gaming, distributional shift, and corrigibility all stem from a single root gap between an optimiser's stated objective and its realised behaviour, whilst she maintained that each failure mode involves different mechanisms—some epistemic, some normative, some architectural—and that treating them as one puzzle invites false unification. Rather than continue talking past one another, I want us to imagine that we submitted to a proper adversarial collaboration. Please draft the joint statement that a unitary camp and a disjunctive camp could both sign.

The statement should open with a numbered section of agreed claims: what phenomena require explanation, which formal distinctions are uncontroversial (for instance, between training-time optimisation and deployment-time behaviour), and what the major impossibility or no-go results actually establish versus what they leave open.
