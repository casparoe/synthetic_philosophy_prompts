I also can't tell what is actually being counted—is it people exactly like me, any conscious being, or just "someone somewhere"? Depending on that unstated choice, the conclusion about other universes seems to shift. Is there a recognized split between versions that sample over worlds versus versions that sample over observers within one world, and a specific term for being presumptuous about how many observers there could have been? I want to know whether these distinctions are named, how they relate, and exactly where the boundary lies between a legitimate use and a mistake; if they lack names, I want a clear carving of what separates them.
