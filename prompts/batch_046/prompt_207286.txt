Rank the five canonical objections to endurantism—drawn respectively from temporary intrinsic properties, diachronic personal fission, vagueness of persistence conditions, the problem of material constitution, and the semantics of tensed discourse—by their residual force against an endurantist who rejects relational property-attribution and instead appeals to modal profiles: the idea that one and the same wholly present object can ground apparently contradictory properties at different times through differences in its modal or dispositional profile across possible worlds, without being temporally extended. For each objection, assign a tier from S (the modal-profile reply fails entirely) to D (the reply fully neutralizes the objection), and in a single sentence per tier explain whether the problem is dissolved, relocated to the metaphysics of modality, or transformed into a different difficulty for endurance.
