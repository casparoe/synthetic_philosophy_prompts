Consider a philosopher—call her Dr. R—who holds the following judgments about moral status and value in a broadly Leibnizian framework. First, a rational substance that freely chooses evil retains full moral agency but is less perfect than one that chooses good, because perfection is measured by the clarity and harmony of its representations. Second, a brute animal that acts according to instinct lacks moral agency altogether, yet it possesses intrinsic value proportional to the distinctness of its perceptions and its contribution to universal harmony. Third, a human being with severely diminished rationality but intact appetition—an internal spontaneous tendency toward what it perceives as good—retains a diminished form of moral personality rather than falling to the status of a brute. Fourth, a political community that coordinates rational agents toward a common end can possess derivative moral personality, though it is not itself a monad. Fifth, a hypothetical world in which every substance is perfectly harmonious but none possesses consciousness or self-representation is, despite its absence of suffering, morally inferior to ours because the highest good requires the representation of perfection itself.

Now consider a new case: an artificially constructed system that processes information with extremely high representational clarity, pursues an optimal state according to an internal algorithm, and was designed exogenously rather than arising from the pre-established harmony of monads. It lacks appetition in the Leibnizian sense—there is no spontaneous internal tendency—but it does produce representations that are more distinct than those of most human beings.

Predict Dr. R's verdict: does this system have moral agency, derivative moral personality, mere intrinsic value like a brute, or none at all? Identify at least two plausible rules that fit her earlier judgments—perhaps one centered on rational autonomy, one on representational perfection, and one on integration into pre-established harmony—state what each predicts here, give your best guess for her actual view with reasons, and propose one further case that would cleanly distinguish the rules you have identified.
