the standard rationalist framing, tetlock and all that, splits inside view (i trace my specific reasons) from outside view (how often do people in my reference class screw up?). i think that's glossing over a representational mismatch that makes epistemic modesty way weirder than people admit. the inside view isn't really "confidence"; it's a causal graph of belief nodes linked by evidence. the outside view is just a frequency distribution over a reference class. when people say "be modest," they usually mean "downweight your belief by some factor," but thats incoherent — you're comparing a graph to a scalar.
