Because determination is, on her account, an asymmetrical, non-causal relation of metaphysical dependence, mental properties retain causal efficacy without entering into causal competition with their physical bases and without violating the closure of the physical domain. She further maintains that this relation preserves the modal profile of mental properties—their multiple realizability across counterfactual system-variants—without treating that realizability as evidence for causal autonomy.
