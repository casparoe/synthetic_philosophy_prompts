The tree should end in named leaves—universalist legal-rational, cultural-relativist, relational-ethics, institutional-functional—but only after the branches are drawn. Mark the empty combinations too (for example, "formal rule-breach is necessary AND every cultural gift practice is automatically corrupt"—probably incoherent?). For each gap say whether it's incoherent, unattractive, or just unexplored.
