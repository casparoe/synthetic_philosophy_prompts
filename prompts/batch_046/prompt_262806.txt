Write a villanelle of nineteen lines on the doomsday argument. Let one refrain state the self-sampling assumption—treating one's birth rank as randomly drawn from all human lives—and let the other state the self-indication assumption, that worlds with more observers are proportionally more likely to be experienced. Between them, trace the reference-class problem through successive expansions of the sampling pool: born humans, all conscious beings, and hypothetical post-human observers. Keep the rhyme scheme exact so the circular form enacts the argument's repetitive logic. In the final quatrain, place both refrains unchanged and adjacent, letting their unresolved juxtaposition be the poem's conclusion rather than a synthesis.
