I'm skeptical because it feels like teh adverb still needs to point to both terms, which sounds relational in disguise. And how do the two views differ when it comes to false propositions—if a and b don't actually stand in R, does either account still have a unified false content, or does teh lack of real connection make the proposition fall apart?
