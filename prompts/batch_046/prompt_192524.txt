Consider a long-run predictive theory T that asserts an exponential growth process will eventually outstrip linear or fixed constraints, producing crisis. In the concrete Malthusian case, T predicted that human population growth would outrun food-supply growth. The nineteenth- and twentieth-century demographic transition, together with sustained agricultural yield growth, appear to falsify T over the medium term, yet defenders claim T might be “right but early.”

Rank the following five “rescue postures”—ways of maintaining fidelity to T despite the intermediate data—from most intellectually serious (1) to least (5). Assign distinct ranks with no ties. For each, state in one sentence whether the posture preserves T’s core causal mechanism or replaces it with something else, and identify which ones risk becoming what philosophers of science call an immunizing stratagem.

A. The latency posture: technological innovation has inserted only a temporary latency period into the exponential curve; once innovation decelerates, the original Malthusian dynamics resume unchanged.

B. The domain-restriction posture: T correctly describes pre-industrial regimes whose premises (no effective birth control, no yield-enhancing technology) were genuinely present; it was never falsified, only misapplied outside its proper domain.

C. The variable-substitution posture: raw population is the wrong metric; substitute per-capita ecological footprint or energy throughput, and T’s structural claim—exponential growth against fixed boundaries—holds exactly for the substitute.

D. The structural-homology posture: T is not specifically about demography but about any exponentially growing consumption facing fixed planetary boundaries; modern climate and resource dynamics are therefore the “real” Malthusian crisis, and the demographic transition is irrelevant.

E. The immunization posture: declare the transition a temporary cultural anomaly or measurement artifact, insist global fertility will revert, and treat all counterevidence as noise that a true long-run theory should properly ignore.

Take a committed stance: do not treat all five as equally valid. In particular, address whether posture D saves T or instead invents a new theory that borrows T’s name, and whether posture E crosses the line from theoretical patience into outright unfalsifiability.
