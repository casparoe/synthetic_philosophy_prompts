Write a six-month research proposal for a philosophy-of-life workshop grant addressing the following question. Standard treatments of life's meaning treat the debate between absurdism and contemporary objective-list theories as a disagreement over whether objective value exists or whether the universe accommodates human purpose. I want to explore a different framing: that both sides implicitly conflate global meaningfulness—the property of an entire life taken as an integrated totality—with distributed meaningfulness—the property of discrete activities, relationships, or achievements that confer value without requiring narrative coherence or metaphysical harmony. The precise research question is whether absurdism, properly analyzed, undermines distributed meaningfulness locally or only denies the possibility of global integration, and whether objective-list theories actually need global coherence or merely smuggle it in as an unmotivated overlay.

State the question so that it has a determinate answer: if distributed meaningfulness is conceptually prior, does the absurdist argument—focused on the mismatch between human longing for unity and the world's indifference—still reach local values, or does its force depend entirely on a prior commitment to global integration? Explain why existing positions leave this open: analytic accounts of meaning typically build coherence conditions into their definitions of "a meaningful life," while existentialist readings of absurdism usually present it as a totalizing crisis rather than a localized failure. Propose a method that combines conceptual analysis of coherence conditions in recent objective-list accounts with a targeted reconstruction of the absurdist argument's scope conditions, treating its metaphysical premises separately from its claims about integration. Identify what success looks like—either showing that objective-list theories can drop the global-integration requirement without loss, or showing that absurdism necessarily infects distributed meaning once its scope is correctly mapped—and specify failure conditions, such as the distinction collapsing upon analysis or the scope conditions proving inseparable from the metaphysical claims. Keep the scope realistic: one conceptual framework, one focused reconstruction of the absurdist position, engagement with primary texts already available, and no empirical or historical research beyond close reading. Do not include a bibliography, literature review by citation, or reading list; simply name the positions and texts you will engage with and note that full citations will be added in the final draft. The proposal should argue explicitly why this angle differs from the familiar introductory framing that pits Camus-style absurdism against objectivist lists of valuable achievements, and it should convince the reviewers that the project is tractable in six months and that its result would matter for how we understand both traditions.
