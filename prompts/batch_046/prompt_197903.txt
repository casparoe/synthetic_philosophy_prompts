I want you to translate this entire position into the vocabulary of my own field—spontaneous symmetry breaking, order parameters, ground-state degeneracy, local gauge invariance, and collective excitation modes—treating the individual as an elementary excitation and the social roles as emergent collective phases that stabilize below a critical temperature. You must avoid importing intentional, teleological, or normative language unless it can be reconstructed entirely from those physical concepts. Once you have completed the translation as faithfully as possible, provide the residue report: identify which claims about moral development, filial obligation, or relational identity map cleanly onto the physics, which require distortion or ad hoc analogical stretching, and which have no counterpart whatsoever in that framework. Then assess whether the residue indicates that the physical vocabulary is genuinely deficient for describing ethical life, or whether the untranslated elements of Confucian ethics depend on distinctions that do not survive careful examination.
