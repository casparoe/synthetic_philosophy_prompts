ok here's my profile for the well-being seminar — tell me if these actually hang together or if i'm smuggling contradictions.

1. i'm a tranquilist: only the absence of craving and suffering has intrinsic value. pleasure, knowledge, beauty, achievement — all axiologicaly neutral, they only matter instrumentaly if they reduce dukkha.
2. i think phenominal consciousness is irreducible — there's definitely something it's like to suffer — but i assign it zero value. a philosophical zombie with zero suffering and zero craving is in exactly the same perfect state as a conscious tranquil human.
3. evolutionary debunking wipes out our positive-value intuitions (that knowledge is good, that love is precious) but it leaves our suffering-avoidance instincts untouched, because pain has direct motivational salience that doesn't need normative backing.
4. i think we should be willing to restructure human cognition globally — suppressing curiosity, artistic drive, romantic attachment — if that eliminates craving, since those drives are just refined tanha.
5. but i also think that if we have to choose between saving a conscious tranquil person and saving a non-conscious tranquil zombie, we should save the human, because consciousness carries a minimal dignity that doesn't depend on suffering or pleasure.
6. death is never a harm — non-existence has no suffering and no craving — so suicide is permissible whenever life involves unavoidable suffering; but killing someone who's already tranquil is wrong, not because it harms them, but because it might cause grief to survivors.

go through these and tell me which pairs are actually in tension and how badly. for each tension, say what the cheapest fix is and what it costs, then tell me which single revision would clean up the most problems. also flag any pairs that only look tense but dissolve once you look closer — i don't want to drop something important just because it sounds weird.
