Please draft my Q&A prep sheet: the eight objections I am actually likely to face, ordered by likelihood and potential damage, each phrased as a philosopher of science or epistemologist would phrase it, with a concise reply, a note on where that reply is soft, and a clear indication of what I should concede up front. Include the question I am hoping nobody asks—specifically, whether my physical-measurement analogy illicitly imports empirical commitments that undermine the normative authority of Bayesian updating. Present the reply in British English, and keep the focus on the conceptual issues rather than on literature references.
