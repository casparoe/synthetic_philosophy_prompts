I'm presenting a seminar paper defending a refined version of Amartya Sen's entitlement-collapse framework, structured around a three-channel taxonomy—direct labor-market entitlement failure, mediated production-shock entitlement failure, and command-extraction entitlement failure—applied respectively to the Bengal famine of 1943, the Great Irish Famine of the 1840s, and the Chinese Great Leap Forward.
