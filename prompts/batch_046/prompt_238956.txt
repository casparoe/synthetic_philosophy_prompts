Most platforms now avoid deleting posts and instead throttle visibility or shadowban, so the user keeps posting but no one sees it. What Foucauldian commitments about power, knowledge, and discipline does that institutional choice presuppose? Does it still rely on panopticon logic—subjects self-regulating because they might be watched—or does invisibility break that model and replace it with something closer to productive biopower? Reconstruct the implicit theory, ask whether explicit bans could serve the same disciplinary function, and tell me what'd have to change in the platform's infrastucture if we replaced hidden filtering with transparent rules.
