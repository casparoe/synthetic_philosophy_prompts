For a working group on interpretation and mental content, I'm drafting my philosophical profile before I present it, and I want an internal consistency check. Here is my current package: (1) I am a Davidsonian interpretationist about mental content—belief and meaning are fixed by the conditions of radical interpretation, not by any internal representational substrate. (2) I take the principle of charity to be constitutive: attributing mostly true beliefs and rational connections is not a heuristic but a necessary condition for interpretation to get started at all. (3) I reject a language of thought or mentalese—there is no interpretation-independent mental representation system that fixes content prior to public practice. (4) I think first-person authority is genuine: in ordinary cases, a subject knows her own mental states without observing her behavior and without applying charitable interpretation to herself. (5) I hold that radical interpretation applies symmetrically: self-knowledge requires the same evidential and charitable reasoning that an external interpreter uses, just with different access to the data. (6) I accept Davidson's triangulation thesis: content is fixed by causal interaction among speaker, interpreter, and a shared world, which resolves behavioral underdetermination. (7) I think charity can be legitimately suspended in severe psychosis or systematic delusion, where we correctly attribute widespread false belief and irrationality rather than reinterpreting the speaker's environment or language. (8) Despite the holism in (1) and (6), I think ordinary self-ascriptions like "I believe it is raining" pick out discrete, specific mental states rather than diffuse positions in a belief network.

Audit this set. Which pairs are genuinely in tension—not just surprising together, but pulling in opposite directions given their usual motivations? Which pairs only look incompatible but dissolve once the commitments are properly understood? Rank the real tensions by severity, and for each say what the least costly revision would be and what it costs me elsewhere in the package. Be specific about whether a tension is between a principle and a case verdict (for instance, whether (2) can actually deliver (7)), or between two structural commitments. Don't lecture me on Davidson in general—treat this as an audit of my specific profile.
