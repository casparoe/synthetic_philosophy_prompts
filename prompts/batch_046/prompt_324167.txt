I am drafting an essay for my A-level philosophy coursework on whether dreaming counts as a genuine form of experience, and I should like an audit of my position before I submit it. My commitments are these. (1) I accept Norman Malcolm's view that we lack independent behavioural criteria for attributing an experience to a sleeping subject, so a dream report is not describing a concurrent mental episode. (2) I also accept Daniel Dennett's claim that the narrative coherence we recall is largely assembled at the moment of waking rather than stored as an unbroken experiential stream. (3) Nevertheless, I hold that REM sleep involves a minimal phenomenal presence—there is something it is like to be in that state—even though it lacks the structured propositional content we later report. (4) I think predictive-processing accounts of cognition apply continuously to sleep: the brain keeps generating predictions without sensory correction, giving dreaming functional continuity with waking cognition rather than marking it off as a categorically different process. (5) I reject the idea that first-person authority over dreams is pure retrospective confabulation; I allow that details can be misremembered, but I maintain that we retain a non-derivative access to the bare fact of having been in that minimal state. Do these hang together? In particular, is there a hidden tension between (1) and (3) that runs deeper than the obvious surface conflict, perhaps concerning what counts as an "experience" as opposed to a bare "state"? Does (4) inadvertently undermine the distinction I draw in (3) between minimal phenomenal presence and structured experiential content? And is the apparent clash between Malcolm's verificationist motivation in (1) and my defence of first-person authority in (5) genuinely contradictory, or does it dissolve once behavioural criteria are separated from epistemic access? Please rank any real tensions by severity, identify which ones only look problematic, suggest the cheapest revision for each, and tell me which single change would tidy up the set most cleanly. Please reply in British English.
