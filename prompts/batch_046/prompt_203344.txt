Could you write a five-minute address that uses specific, non-identical threads—maybe the honor code, the weird winter lantern ritual, the phrase Meridian time—to show the overlap? Don't quote Wittgenstein verbatim—paraphrase if you need him—since I don't want to misquote in front of the department. Make it warm and casual, like I might accidentally misspell a word or two.
