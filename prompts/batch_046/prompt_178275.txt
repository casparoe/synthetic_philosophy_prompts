(2) The reflexive calibration thesis answers this: our cognitive architecture includes an introspective module that formats phenomenal information using conceptually isolated phenomenal concepts. This formatting produces a robust appearance of an ontological gap without there actually being one.

(3) If the reflexive calibration thesis is correct, then our gap-intuitions are fully explained by physical cognitive mechanisms, and they carry no independent evidential weight against physicalism.
