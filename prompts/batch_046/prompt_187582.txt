I have been working through Rawls’s veil of ignorance and the difference principle in a seminar on distributive justice, and I keep noticing how the thought experiment is usually summarized rather than genuinely inhabited. Would you write a short story that forces the concept to do real narrative work? The setting is a closed city-state called Novum, where every seventh year all adult citizens enter the Veil Chamber: a week-long neurochemical suspension that wipes autobiographical memory—social rank, talents, kinship, even bodily identity—while leaving rational deliberation intact. From this blank state they must unanimously ratify a revised Basic Structure of resource allocation before their memories are restored. The existing charter hews close to the difference principle, permitting inequalities only when they maximize the welfare of the least advantaged, but a dissident faction now advocates a “risk-sensitive maximin” variant that allows larger gaps if they reduce the probability of catastrophic deprivation for the worst-off. Compose the piece as exactly five scenes, each one to two paragraphs, with no framing introduction or concluding summary outside the scenes themselves. Scene one should occur inside the chamber, where two deliberators who do not yet know they are spouses debate whether to license a high-status engineering caste. Scene two should take place after restoration, revealing that the worst-off citizen under the old charter was in fact one of the architects of the new risk-sensitive rule. Scene three should show a child asking why some people possess more and receiving an answer that quietly exposes an unexamined assumption about desert. Scene four should depict the algorithm that computes the “least advantaged” index, making visible that it excludes emotional and reproductive labor from its welfare metric. Scene five should close on an ambiguous image of the chamber doors opening for the next cycle, leaving unresolved whether the veil genuinely eliminates partiality or merely disguises it behind procedural neutrality. Let the philosophical tension emerge through concrete dialogue and institutional detail rather than expository explanation.
