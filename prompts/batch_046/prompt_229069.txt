okay so i'm trying to figure out what a decent theory of hinge commitments actually has to do. not just "explain On Certainty" but really specifiy the job. i keep seeing people treat hinges like they're just very confident beliefs, and i think that's missing the social maintenance part entirely.

write the desiderata: what an adequate account of hinge epistemology needs to deliver. include these hard constraints with the case or consideration behind each:

- the river-bed vs water distinction: some certainties stay fixed while ordinary beliefs flow over them. the theory must preserve that without treating hinges as merely high-confidence empirical claims, and it has to say what makes a hinge "hinge-like" structurally rather than psychologically.
- hinge disagreement: take the case where Moore insists there are external objects and someone genuinely doesn't share that hinge. the theory can't collapse this into either ordinary factual error or total incommensurable worldviews; it needs to explain what disagreement even amounts to here and whether it's resolvable.
- hinge fatigue: i want this built in. hinges aren't static mental files but require continuous social reinforcement, and when that reinforcement breaks—say after rapid institutional collapse or technological disruption—they don't get "disproven" but sag or become available for revision in ways normal beliefs don't. the theory should explain what that sagging looks like epistemically, and whether fatigue is always irrational or can be rational.
- the transmision problem: how do hinges get inherited? children don't argue for them. the theory needs to account for acqusition without reducing it to explicit teaching or pure conditioning.
- normativity and hinge shift: are hinges subject to rational assessment, or beyond reason? and if beyond reason, how do we explain large-scale hinge shifts (like elements of the geocentric framework giving way) without saying it's purely causal or arbitrary?

for each, say why it's a constraint, and flag any pair that looks jointly unsatisfiable. i especially want to know if "hinges are genuinely propositional/contentful" and "hinges are immune to rational revision" can both hold.

then briefly check how Wittgenstein's own picture, a social-epistemic view that treats hinges as communal certainties, and a "hinge pragmatism" that reads them as practical commitments do against the list. don't do a full bibliography, just flag where each worst fails.
