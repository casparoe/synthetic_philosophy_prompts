Please use concrete imagery—courts, borders, or scales—rather than abstract moralizing, and assume the reader is familiar with Pascal's fragments on custom and authority but do not include bibliographic references. This should set the tone for our discussion of whether natural law can survive when justice is always mediated by established power.
