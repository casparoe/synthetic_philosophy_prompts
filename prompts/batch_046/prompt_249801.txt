i need a reusable checklist for documentary editors who have to approve narration explaining why a historical decision went the way it did — say, why a particular treaty collapsed in 1919. ground it in the actual mechanics of hindsight bias and the narrative fallacy, not generic warnings. it needs ordered tests they can apply in 15 minutes: one that checks if the explanation uses knowledge only availible after the fact, one that checks if contingency was smoothed into inevitability, and one that checks if outcomes are being used to validate the reasoning that produced them. include two worked cases — one where the narration is clean, and one where it tells a compelling but false causal story and the checklist kills it. also mark the spots where judgment can't be eliminated and the editor just has to decide, and say what the checklist can't catch at all.
