(2) By induction, if I survive every step of a 100% replacement spread over ten years, then I survive being fully uploaded onto a server.
(3) During the process I'm awake and talking the whole time, so psychologcal continuity is preserved at each stage.
(4) So the uploaded entity is literally me, not a copy.
(5) But if I'm still me running on a server with no body, then being embodied was never essential to my identity.
(6) And if embodiment was never essential, then causal-historical continuity with my original body doesn't matter either — all that matters is having the right psychologcal pattern.
(7) So a perfect simulation of me running on a server in Tokyo right now, with no causal link back to me, is also me.
(8) That means there could be a hundred such simulations existing simultaneously, all identicle to me.
(9) But personal identity is one-to-one; I can't be numerically identical to a hundred distinct things.
(10) So (1) must be false — gradual replacement never actually preserves me, and uploading is always death plus duplication.
