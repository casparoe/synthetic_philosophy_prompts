I feel her reasoning confuses several distinct things—perhaps about what heritability actually measures, whether it is constant across environments, and what it implies for intervention—but I cannot pinpoint the errors precisely. Please advise me: is her argument sound? If not, identify exactly where the inference from those population-level figures to those policy conclusions breaks down, and clarify what heritability does and does not entail in this kind of cross-population comparison.
