(3) I hold that asymmetrical recognition is structurally unstable. If party A recognizes party B but B withholds full recognition from A, the dialectical pressure pushes toward reversal or mutualization; stable one-sided recognition is impossible.

(6) I think there are historically durable hierarchical relations—feudal vassalage, certain religious orders—where subordinate parties genuinely recognize superiors and derive self-certainty from that recognition, and where the hierarchy remains stable without collapsing into mutual equality, precisely because both sides internalize the hierarchy as legitimate.
