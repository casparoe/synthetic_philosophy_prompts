Drawing on Dennett’s compatibilist “elbow room” and Caruso’s skeptical quarantine model of punishment, I need a reusable evaluation procedure—ordered questions, stopping thresholds, and explicit value-judgment nodes—that my panel can run without legal training. Apply it briefly to two cases: a student who plagiarized out of panic, and one who deliberately vandalized shared space, ensuring one result contradicts our board’s usual instinct. Present the entire response as exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences long, with no introduction or conclusion, and flag every step where irreducible group judgment replaces factual determination. Mark clearly where the procedure cannot settle the matter and must hand the decision back to us as a matter of values rather than philosophy.
