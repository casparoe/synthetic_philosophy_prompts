hey, I'm trying to write up my modal metaphysics profile for a rationalist reading group and I want a consistency check before I present it. I definatly think Lewisian counterpart theory is the right framework: saying "I could've been a musician" just means there's a world with a musician-counterpart of me, and transworld identity is a non-starter because there's no non-circular individuation criterion that doesn't already smuggle in modal facts. But I also think origin essentialism is false — I could have had different parents — which means the counterpart relation can't be qualitative similarity alone, since then there'd be way too many candidates. So I want to anchor it in decision-theoretic continuity: my actual psychological profile picks out the "closest" counterpart across worlds, kind of like a centered-world metric.

Here's where it gets messy. I reject psychological continuity for diachronic personal identity — I'm a 4D worm with temporal parts, so "me" is just the fusion of stages — but then I'm using psychological continuity to ground cross-world counterpart relations, which feels like helping myself to exactly the thing I denied over time. I also think Newcomb's problem requires tracking "me" across counterfactual branches to explain why the predictor's accuracy matters, which sounds like it needs transworld identity or at least something stronger than Lewisian similarity, not just a decision-theoretic anchor. And then there's this weird parallel I keep seeing: the heterogeneity problem for hedonism asks what all pleasures share, and I think modal properties are heterogenous in the same way — there's no intrinsic "possible-for-me" property, just counterpart relations — so I'm basically applying hedonist heterogeneity to modal metaphysics, but I haven't worked out if that commits me to saying there's no fact of the matter about which counterpart is "really" me.

So here's my package: (1) counterpart theory anchored by decision-theoretic psychological continuity; (2) 4D worm theory over time, denying psychological continuity there; (3) origin essentialism is false; (4) Newcomb reasoning requires cross-world agent-tracking stronger than counterpart similarity; (5) modal properties are heterogenous like pleasures, with no intrinsic modal nature. Which pairs are actually in tension, which only look like they are, and what's the least costly revision? Rank the tensions by severity, say for each what has to give, and then tell me which single change would clean up the most problems at once.
