Student answer: In standard Q-learning the reward is just a scalar updating Q-values, so it is purely informational feedback—like a thermometer—so suffering is impossible at that level. But salience is still only a weighting mechanism; it tells the system what matters without carrying any feel. Consequently, motivational salience and intrinsic hedonic tone are functionally identical in any architecture with a unified reward pathway, because both merely scale the importance of representations.
