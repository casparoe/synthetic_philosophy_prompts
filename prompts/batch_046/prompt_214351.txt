At a recent seminar, two colleagues debated a refined version of Nozick’s experience machine. Dr Alvaro argued that the familiar rejection — that we value “contact with reality” — only succeeds if well-being depends on a global narrative constraint, namely that one’s life as a whole must be causally anchored in the external world. She maintained that a localist hedonist, for whom only present experiential quality and the immediate completion of projects matter, should rationally enter a machine that replicates the local phenomenology of achievement, since the modal history of those achievements is irrelevant to present well-being. Dr Brennan replied that this misreads Nozick: the objection’s force lies not in biography but in the value of genuine agency, understood as counterfactual robustness. A poem produced in the machine, he said, lacks the property of having been generated through resistance in an independent world, and this modal deficit undermines well-being even locally. Alvaro countered that importing “counterfactual robustness” into well-being smuggles in an illicit metaphysical criterion that neither hedonism nor preference-satisfaction can accommodate without circularity; if one’s informed preference is specifically for the phenomenology of achievement rather than its ontological pedigree, the machine satisfies it. Brennan then observed that preference-satisfaction theories usually include an informed-desire condition requiring knowledge of causal origin, so entering the machine either frustrates the preference for reality or requires self-deception, which itself erodes autonomy. They asked me to referee. Please adjudicate point by point: who is right about whether Nozick’s objection is global or local, whether counterfactual robustness is a legitimate component of well-being or an external add-on, and how an informed-preference account should treat preferences formed inside versus outside the machine. Say who is more right overall, and identify what both are missing — particularly whether local well-being can be insulated from global epistemic conditions without collapsing into self-deception.
