As a journalist who writes on philosophy of science and the metaphysics of cognition, I am presenting a seminar paper next Thursday to an interdisciplinary group on representation. The thesis I defend is that Bradley's regress is genuinely dissolved—not relocated—only if we reject both Russell's hierarchical typology and contemporary adverbialist dissolution, and instead treat propositional unity as a primitive coherence structurally analogous to temporal binding in distributed neural networks. On my view, the parts of a proposition do not stand in need of an additional relating relation because integration is achieved through synchronous assembly rather than compositional linkage; I concede this revives a neo-Bradleyan intuition that analytic metaphysics largely abandoned, and I do not deny that Russell's framework avoids the regress by other means. The audience is precisely divided: two Russellians for whom the theory of types settled the matter in the early twentieth century; three philosophers of language working in Davidsonian and Dummettian traditions who regard propositional unity as a grammatical illusion produced by reifying sentential structures; and one cognitive neuroscientist expert in the binding-problem literature who accepts the neuroscience but regards its importation into first-philosophy metaphysics as a category error. They value historical precision, resistance to empirical overreach, and conceptual economy. Draft my speaker-preparation sheet: order the objections by the damage each would do to my position and by how likely they are from this specific room, phrasing each exactly as that faction would voice it—not as generic criticisms but as targeted strikes against my neural-binding analogy and my dismissal of both hierarchical and adverbialist solutions. For each, provide a reply I can deliver in roughly two minutes, indicate where I must concede, where I should hold firm, and where my reply is softest. Include the one objection I am hoping the room avoids, with a brief note on how to respond without evasion if it is raised. The sheet should contain no bibliographies, citation requests, or references to works I could not recall without external access; every reply must be deployable from reasoning and general knowledge alone.
