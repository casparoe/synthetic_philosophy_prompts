2. The Korean War draft cohort (US males born 1947–1952): a real kind—conscription boards used birth-year boundaries operationally, reshaping education, employment, and life trajectories.
3. Millennials (born roughly 1981–1996): a real kind—the 2008 financial crisis struck during labor-market entry, and institutions now treat digital nativity as an operational category.
4. Gen Z (born roughly 1997–2012): not a real kind—no discrete formative shock, and institutional categories remain unstable and market-driven.
5. The 2020 lockdown cohort (those aged 15–22 during COVID-19 school closures): a real kind—both a discrete shock and lasting institutional adaptations (remote-learning protocols, mental-health screening) created self-reinforcing feedback.

Predict her verdict.
