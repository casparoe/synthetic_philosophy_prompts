Please draft my Q&A preparation sheet: the six or seven objections they will actually raise, ordered by how damaging they are likely to be and how probable they are, each phrased precisely as that particular audience would phrase it — not as a rationalist or a pure economist, but as a Rawlsian, a fiscal analyst, or a migration lawyer.
