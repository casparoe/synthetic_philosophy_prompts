Also flag combinations that look empty: maybe nobody thinks work is a burden but also that leisure shouldn't be provided at all? Or that overwork is virtuous but only for other people? I want about 8-10 leaves max, audience is grad students in philosophy of economics. Please build it from the logical space of answers, not from an encyclopedia list, and don't ask me for citations — just construct the tree.
