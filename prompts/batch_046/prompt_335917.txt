Still, Skinner's intentionalism offers a partial fix: recover agents' own normative vocabularies to avoid anachronism. But Skinner warned this can slide into moral relativism, which he rejected in his later essays on republican liberty. So we should aim for situated neutrality — using present tools for understanding but suspending present judgment. That's probably the best we can do.
