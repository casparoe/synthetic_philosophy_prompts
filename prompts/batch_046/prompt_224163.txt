(7) Therefore, on collectivism, there is no moral boundary between family and stranger. Filial piety collapses into universal cosmopolitan duty.
(8) But preserving special obligations was supposed to be the view's strength! So collectivism proves too much and undermines itself.
(9) Hence individualism must be right: obligations are chosen or contracted, not constituted by network position.
