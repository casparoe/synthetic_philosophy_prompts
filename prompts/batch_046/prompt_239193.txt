I do not want the physics explained directly; I want the pedagogical architecture.

Plan the explanatory sequence: specify the exact order in which the components must be introduced, with explicit attention to what depends on what—for example, whether relationism must first be made intuitively plausible before the bucket is introduced, or whether the bucket must come first to motivate the distinction at all.
