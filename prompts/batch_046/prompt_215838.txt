Its not about individual shame; its about the social mechanics of being necessary but untouchable.

Design a structured role-play with real small stakes (no money — maybe seating chips that determine who gets to join the final planning circle?). Set up three escalating crisis rounds. Give me rules, roles, a timeline that fits 80 minutes, and the payoff/seating mechanics. The idea should hit them during the vote, not in my lecture after.
