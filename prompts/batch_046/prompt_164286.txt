Keep the discussion focused and do not invoke bibliographies, citations, or named texts. After presenting the dialogue, explicitly state what argument or finding—such as evidence that legitimacy does not depend on affected-interest breadth, or a non-circular grounding for reciprocal recognition—would cause you to revise your verdict on which criterion is more defensible, and indicate where your reasoning is most likely to fail.
