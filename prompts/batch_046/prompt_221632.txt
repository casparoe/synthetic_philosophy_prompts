second: hysteria back in the 1800s. doctors named a bunch of symptoms, patients started performing them, then when doctors dropped the label it basically vanished. i think this was almost pure loop, not tracking anything real underneath. the suffering was real but the diagnosis was basically a costume people were put in.

fourth: schizophrenia. there's definitely looping — stigma changes how people experience symptoms — but i still think it's tracking something deep and pre-existing. the loop modifies the surface but doesn't create the condition.

fifth: gender dysphoria. this one messes me up. there's clearly intense looping — the category and available pathways shape how people understand discomfort. but i also think for many the distress is real and prior. i can't tell if the loop here is like autism (mostly helpful clarification) or like hysteria (mostly manufacturing). i guess i lean it's legitimate, but i'm way less sure than with autism.
