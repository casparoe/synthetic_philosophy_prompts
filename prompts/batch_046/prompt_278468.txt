write a villanelle (19 lines, two refrains, A1B A2 rhyme scheme) about kierkegaards abraham and the teleological suspension of the ethical. use one refrain for the universal ethical command that binds everyone together and one for the singular call that cant be translated into that language. the poem should treat abrahams silence not as emptiness but as an active refusal - since for kierkegaard any explanation pulls him back into the universal. put the turn at the moment the knife is raised but before the ram appears, so the suspension stays open with no resolution. dont turn it into a sermon about faith or a cynical shrug. start with the poem itself, no preface or commentary. make it precise enough that someone who knows fear and trembling recognizes the structural move, but readable even if you dont.
