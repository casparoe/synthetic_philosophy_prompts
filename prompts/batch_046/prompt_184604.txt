Before I assess any particular theory of dispositions—whether conditional, essentialist, or powers-based—I want a rigorous specification of what an adequate account must deliver in light of finks and masks. Also flag any pair of desiderata that appear jointly unsatisfiable—perhaps intrinsicness together with full testability under active masking conditions. Briefly note how simple conditional analysis, dispositional essentialism, and a powers-based ontology fare against the list, but keep the weight on the criteria themselves.
