Write a dialogue between Nina, who defends the principle of alternative possibilities, and Tom, a compatibilist who thinks Frankfurt-style cases (and especially reverse ones) kill it. Tom should push back that if Nina retreats to 'normative competance' or 'genuine ownership of the branch,' she's smuggled in the very conditions Frankfurt showed don't need alternative paths. Have them actually respond to each other's moves — no strawmen, no talking past. Don't have them cite books or papers; they should just reason through the case together.
