If such differentiation is genuinely conceivable, then the transcendental move from the structural conditions of differentiated experience to the necessary existence of an external material world appears to be an unjustified leap: it mistakes the contingent substrate that usually supplies those conditions for a logical prerequisite. I do not intend to argue that we actually inhabit a simulation, but only that the modal possibility of substrate-independent differentiation undermines the alleged necessity at the heart of the argument.
