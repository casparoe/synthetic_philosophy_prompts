where does it slip? is (6) an illegitimate extension, or is there a hidden equivocation between causal contact with the object itself versus causal contact with the evidence or representation? my character really wants to keep (1) and (5), but i need to know what she's paying if she does. tell me which step to surrender and why it looked fine on its own.
