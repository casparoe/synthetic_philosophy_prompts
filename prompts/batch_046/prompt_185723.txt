The episode: the 1972 Club of Rome report Limits to Growth, produced by Donella Meadows and her colleagues using the World3 computer model, and its 2004 update. The authors insisted these were not predictions but conditional projections, yet they were widely received—and criticized—as prophecies of inevitable doom. By the time of the 2004 update, several variables (global population growth rates, resource-use efficiency, agricultural yields) had diverged significantly from the report's baseline trajectories, partly because the report's own visibility had influenced policy, technology, and consumption patterns.
