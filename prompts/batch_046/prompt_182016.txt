(3) Now apply the same filter reflexively. (4) If structural realism is correct, then any claim not embedded in the retained mathematical structure of successful science should be treated as an expendable ontological interpretation rather than as knowledge. (5) Therefore structural realism demands that we withhold belief from the very thesis that justifies withholding belief from ontology—unless that thesis can itself be expressed as part of the retained predictive structure. (6) No successful physical theory includes "withhold belief from non-structural ontology" in its predictive apparatus; hence the thesis is not structurally retained across theory change.
