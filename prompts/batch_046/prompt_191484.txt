For each reading, say whether it is trivial, substantive, or false; whether it requires adopting the full Jain logical apparatus to be coherent; and — crucially — whether it actually supports her specific move of holding incompatible quantum ontologies without further argument, or whether it demands precisely the work of reconciliation and modal qualification she is avoiding.
