Consider five mechanisms through which dynastic wealth undermines equality of opportunity in a society that prohibits formal aristocratic privilege but permits unlimited bequest: (1) direct intergenerational transmission of financial capital; (2) transmission of social capital through exclusive elite networks; (3) a dynastic externality that depresses labor-market returns for non-inheritors; (4) the conversion of family wealth into meritocratic credentials; and (5) the capture of regulatory institutions by hereditary economic elites. Rank these mechanisms from the most structurally damaging to equality of opportunity to the least damaging. Respond with exactly five numbered items, each consisting of one or two sentences that state the rank and provide a brief justification; do not include an introduction or conclusion.
