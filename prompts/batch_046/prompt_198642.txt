I need a focused sequence of six to eight interview questions that turn on the actual mechanics of this view rather than generic consciousness talk. Include at least one question pressing how her loop mechanism differs from standard GWT explanations of broadcast failure; one asking what empirical signature could distinguish her position from both pure-access theories and phenomenal-primitive theories; and one probing whether she has relocated rather than dissolved the explanatory gap. For each, provide a sharp follow-up keyed to her likely reply—especially the move that loop dynamics are structurally transparent yet phenomenologically opaque. Conclude with a question about what evidence could genuinely falsify the view rather than being absorbed as yet another loop configuration. Keep the tone precise and adversarial in the way good science journalism demands, but avoid asking for citations, reading lists, or bibliographic references.
