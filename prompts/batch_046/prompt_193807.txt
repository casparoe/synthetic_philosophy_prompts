Which parts of the debate map cleanly onto causal-inference machinery, which parts require distortion — for instance, mapping the normative "ought" of intrinsic retributive appropriateness onto a statistical estimand like the average treatment effect — and which parts have no expression whatsoever in this vocabulary, especially any phenomenological claim about "could have done otherwise" that is meant to ground punishment independently of consequences?
