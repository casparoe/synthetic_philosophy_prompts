The core idea is that our introspecive mechanisms originally evolved for tracking bodily harm and salience (basically a this hurts, pay attention system), and then we misapply that same tracking architecture to conscious experience in general. So when people ask why physical processes have subjective feel, they're really just running damage-detection software on states that don't need it, and that creates the illusion of a metaphysical gap. It's not full illusionism about consciousness itself, just illusionism about why the problem seems hard.
