my dad just retired and is feeling pretty useless, so i read ciceros essay on old age to cheer him up. a wellness blog summarized it like this: cicero lists four complaints about old age — you cant do real work anymore, your body breaks down, you lose physical pleasures, and youre close to death. but cato answers using a dichotomy of control: your body and career are externals you dont control, but your mind and virtue are internal, so aging is actually freeing because you stop caring about externals and just do philosphy. he also says death isnt scary because either your soul lives forever with the gods or it just vanishes, and both outcomes are totally fine. the blog concludes cicero basically invented modern stoic retirement — stop competing with young people, accept your broken body, and meditate on virtue. i feel like this is missing something? like, didnt cato stay active in politics? is it fair to say he thinks work and physical stuff just dont matter? which parts are accurate and which are the blog twisting cicero to fit modern self-help?
