Please write a dialogue between two equally competent interlocutors: one defending the primacy of the inside view for first-person practical reasoning, particularly when outside-view reference classes can be made arbitrarily specific without resolving the conflict with phenomenological self-knowledge; and another defending epistemic modesty through the outside view, who also addresses the reflexive worry that applying the outside view to one's own belief in the outside view might undermine itself. Have them respond directly to each other's actual points rather than talking past one another, and keep the exchange focused on whether my personal sense of resilience should carry any weight against the statistical base rate.
