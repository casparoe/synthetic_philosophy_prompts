The session is meant to convey the central thesis of Douglas Hofstadter and Emmanuel Sander's Surfaces and Essences—that analogy is not a decorative rhetorical device but the fundamental mechanism underlying conceptual thought. Rather than presenting this as abstract theory, I want to build the entire session around one somewhat unconventional carrying case: the long-running scientific debate over whether viruses are "alive," treating it not as a biology puzzle that was eventually solved, but as a live demonstration of how scientific categories are analogical mappings that slip rather than boxes with fixed borders.

I need a teaching plan, not the lecture text itself. First, identify the prior idea that must be firmly in place before the audience can recognize the virus debate as a problem about cognition rather than just virology. Second, lay out the exact dependency order—common-sense categorization, the analogy mechanism, the concept of slippage, the virus case—specifying what must come before what and why the sequence collapses if reordered. Third, design the carrying example precisely: use the virus-to-living-organism analogy, specify which shared features it must highlight (replication, evolutionary change) and which breaking points it must include (absence of metabolism, obligate dependence on hosts), and explain why this case succeeds where more familiar analogies such as Pluto-as-planet or DNA-as-computer-code would fail for this particular audience and purpose. Fifth, propose one check at the end that demonstrates they have grasped the underlying cognitive claim rather than merely retained the virus fact—perhaps navigating a novel borderline case. Finally, state clearly which parts of Hofstadter and Sander's broader framework, such as their treatment of mathematical analogy or linguistic categorization, I should explicitly leave out at this level, saying so openly, and justify the omission in terms of dependency overload. The deliverable is a structured explanation plan with a misconception map, a rigorously constrained carrying example, and an explicit boundary on what lies beyond the session's scope.
