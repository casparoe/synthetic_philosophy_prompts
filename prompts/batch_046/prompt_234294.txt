Write the desiderata that a theory of coherent extrapolated volition — or, more broadly, an account of idealized preference aggregation intended to guide value learning in artificial systems — must satisfy to be both normatively adequate and practically deployable. The purpose here is design guidance, not metaphysical completeness: the theory must specify what to compute when direct access to human neurobiology is unavailable and aggregation must occur across heterogeneous agents.

For each desideratum, state the motivating phenomenon or case, indicate whether it is non-negotiable or a preference of a particular camp (rationalist, contractualist, pluralist, pragmatist), and defend that classification. Your list must address at least the following: (1) the distinction between diachronic self-revision within a single agent and synchronic aggregation across agents, with the reason why conflating their coherence conditions undermines the theory; (2) reflexive weighting, in which the aggregation rule itself becomes part of the volitional content being extrapolated, and how the theory prevents either circularity or arbitrary fixation of the weights; (3) preservation of epistemic humility — the extrapolated result must not be treated as incorrigible — without collapsing into perpetual deferral that makes the theory unusable; (4) value fragmentation, where an agent’s preferences at different reflective levels are not merely plural but structurally incommensurable, and whether the theory must resolve them into a single ordering or may leave them genuinely plural; (5) consistency with Arrow-type results that any deterministic aggregation over multiple agents will violate at least one of independence, Pareto efficiency, or non-dictatorship under reasonable conditions, and whether accepting one violation is a negotiable camp preference or a fatal flaw; and (6) empirical anchoring, meaning the theory must align with observed human value judgments at non-idealized levels without requiring that every pre-reflective preference survive extrapolation.

Then determine whether any subset of these desiderata is jointly unsatisfiable. If so, identify which single requirement does the most work in excluding candidates, and explain whether the tension is resolvable by weakening a negotiable preference or reveals a genuine impossibility. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence — which desiderata are indispensable, which are camp-specific luxuries, and where the unavoidable tension lies — then present the reasoning with no preamble and no restatement of the question. Write in American English.
