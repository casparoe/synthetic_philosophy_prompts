I'm currently taking a graduate seminar on moral philosophy, and we've been working through T.M. On this view, each affected individual's reason to reject a principle functions like a standalone veto that never enters into any weighing calculus against the reasons of others. So if a principle permits harming one person to save five, the one person's complaint isn't outweighed by the five's acceptance—it is structurally incommensurable with them. Which arguments, formal results, intuitive counter-cases, or conceptual clarifications should actually move me?
