Identify the single concession I should make up front to keep credibility. Finally, include the objection I am hoping nobody asks—specifically, whether my scaffolded skyhook collapses back into ordinary gene-culture coevolution once the historical assembly of the scaffold is fully described. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citation requests; I need arguments that stand on their own without reference to texts I cannot access in the room.
