Write the desiderata an adequate theory of emergence must meet if it is to apply to open, dissipative systems—such as ecological networks or active matter—where scale-free dynamics blur any sharp micro/macro boundary, rather than to the familiar cases of consciousness or chemistry. For each desideratum, state the concrete phenomenon or theoretical constraint it responds to: accommodating downward causal influence without violating the causal closure of physics, explaining genuine novelty without invoking vitalism or discontinuous strong emergence, handling feedback loops that revise local interaction rules, and retaining empirical tractability when level boundaries are fluid rather than fixed. Flag which requirements are non-negotiable for any scientific account and which are preferences of particular camps (mechanists versus holists). Then assess whether any subset—particularly the combination of robust downward causation and strict causal closure—is jointly unsatisfiable given current physics, and if so, identify which desideratum ought to be relaxed.
