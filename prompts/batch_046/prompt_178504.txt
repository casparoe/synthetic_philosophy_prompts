Early events, such as a particular constitutional settlement or technological standard, shape the trajectory and local form of development, but macro-functional constraints—energy capture, coordination scale, information processing—tend to funnel outcomes toward a narrow band of viable equilibria regardless of initial path. So history is locked in with respect to route, not destination. What would count against this?
