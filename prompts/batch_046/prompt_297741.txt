They must distinguish his view from at least one rival—say, Parfit's psychological continuity or a strict biological-continuity account—and pinpoint exactly where "symbolic persistence" differs from mere information preservation.

Please provide: the exam question itself; a model answer specifying the precise conceptual moves a top response must make; and a marking scheme totaling 20 points built around five must-hit points, with marks for each, plus a one-line note on the characteristic mistake that costs points on each. Keep the prompt free of citation requests or reading lists; the evaluation should turn on conceptual precision, not bibliographic recall.
