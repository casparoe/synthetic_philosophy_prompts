Read the film as a philosophical dramatization of the performance-legitimacy argument: where does the regime’s competence in maintaining order and distributing goods appear to justify its violence, and where does the film show that competence to be morally hollow or self-undermining? Conclude by saying what the film adds to the debate—perhaps about the relationship between hope, reproduction, and political legitimacy—that a purely institutional comparison of prosperous authoritarian regimes with liberal democracies cannot capture.
