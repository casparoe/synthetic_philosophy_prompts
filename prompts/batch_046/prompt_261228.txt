A colleague recently shared with me a summary of how the simulation argument interacts with longtermist reasoning about far-future moral priorities. According to the summary, if technologically mature civilizations run large numbers of high-fidelity ancestor simulations, then minds like ours are far more likely to be simulated than to exist in base reality. The summary further claims that such simulations are executed with strictly bounded computational resources, which implies that any simulated timeline has a finite temporal horizon—typically terminating before the emergence of post-human or interstellar civilizations. From these two premises, the author concludes that taking the simulation argument seriously should sharply reduce one's credence in living within an open-ended future, and therefore should undermine longtermist fanaticism about outcomes centuries hence. The summary also introduces a further qualification: even if simulated minds have full moral status within their own layer, the total number of future moral patients contained in that layer is capped by its truncated timeline, so present actions aimed at shaping distant outcomes within the layer have negligible expected value compared to near-term welfare improvements.

Before I present this line of reasoning to my seminar, I want to be sure I am not propagating a subtle mischaracterization. Could you audit this summary for me? Specifically, please distinguish which elements are faithful to the standard formulation of the simulation argument and to longtermist frameworks, which parts conflate distinct metaphysical or empirical claims—such as the move from simulation likelihood to timeline truncation, or the assumption that moral consideration does not extend across nested simulation boundaries—and whether the conclusion that simulation belief ought to dampen far-future fanaticism actually follows once those distinctions are properly drawn. I am particularly interested in whether the argument about bounded computational budgets and capped future populations within a layer is a legitimate extension of the simulation reasoning or an unsupported addition.
