By the conclusion, the document should implicitly demonstrate why an actionable, omniscient prophecy about a specific contingent behavior collapses into tautology rather than guidance. Write in dense, formal legal jargon; avoid references to named living persons, specific religious texts, or historical sources, and do not include citations, footnotes, or bibliographic recommendations.
