The council now proposes replacing the 1847 hedgerow-boundary rule — originally established to prevent livestock entering private gardens, but now functioning as an informal wildlife corridor and reducing neighbour disputes — with digital property markers and virtual boundary contracts, citing obstructed emergency access and high maintenance costs. Predict my tutor’s recommendation on this proposal, identify whether her track record is best explained by a strict Chesterton’s-fence logic, a Burkean evolutionary-preservation logic, or a functionalist-cohesion logic, indicate which of her earlier rulings best supports each interpretation, and propose one further case that would distinguish the two rules she is most likely applying. Please present your answer as exactly five numbered points, each consisting of one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion.
