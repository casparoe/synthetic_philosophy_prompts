I sit on my university's Committee for the Governance of Digital Archives. A working group proposes replacing our repository's standard metadata taxonomy with a purpose-built schema that makes explicit value-commitments about knowledge organisation, arguing that classification systems are not neutral instruments but embody particular political orientations — applying, in effect, the claim that artifacts have politics to library infrastructure. The plan includes a six-month pilot and requires all new deposits to use the revised schema; opponents argue that metadata is purely instrumental, that an explicit political framework violates institutional neutrality, and that the pilot risks fragmenting access to legacy collections. Please draft the short written opinion I will circulate before Thursday's meeting: recommend a concrete course of action with a clear time frame, and treat the strongest argument on the opposing side with genuine seriousness rather than dismissing it as a misunderstanding of archives.
