She then receives higher-order evidence that is not generic fatigue or institutional pressure, but a validated psychometric and decision-theoretic profile applied specifically to her: it demonstrates that her particular diagnostic-heuristic signature—systematic over-reliance on pattern-matching in low-base-rate presentations—correlates with a 45% false-positive rate within her precise reference class (senior clinicians who share that exact heuristic profile).
