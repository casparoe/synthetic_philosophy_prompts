The PI needs a clear yes/no with reasoning we can defend to adminstration if something goes wrong. Draft the panel's decision memo—approve, reject, or approve with conditions—with the bottom line in bold at the top, then reasoning that honestly treats both the scientific risk and the conceptual paradox.
