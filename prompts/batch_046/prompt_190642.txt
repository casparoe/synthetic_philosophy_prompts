could you write me a decision-tree quiz—around 6 to 8 leaves—that takes someone from a concrete case to a named position? the case: you spend ten years on a project (say, a community garden or a novel) and then you recieve proof that in 50,000 years every record, memory, and causal trace of it will vanish. no afterlife, no aliens, nothing. the tree should branch on answers to specific questions about that case, not on “which philosopher do you like.” for example: does the project’s value depend on being witnessed or remembered? if yes, is the witnessing required to be human, or could any conscious observer do? if no, is the value intrinsic to the activity regardless of scale, or is there a middle option—maybe what i’m calling “localized normativity,” where value holds inside a spatiotemporal boundary but just doesn’t travel?
