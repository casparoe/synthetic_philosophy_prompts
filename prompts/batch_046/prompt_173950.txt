Write a dialogue between two biologists/philosophers, one defending an etiological account of biological function and one defending a causal-role account. Have them argue about a specific engineered case: a gene drive put into mosquitoes to block malaria transmission that, after several generations in the wild, mutated and now mainly helps the mosquitoes survive cold winters. The etiologist should claim the drive's function is still malaria-blocking because thats its selection history; the causal-role advocate should say no, its function is now thermal tolerance, full stop. They need to actually respond to each other — no strawmen, each reply should address the other persons previous point. Keep it to about five exchanges and dont ask for citations or references.
