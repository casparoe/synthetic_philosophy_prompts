I'm auditing a graduate seminar on evolutionary theory and have been tracking a particular defensive pattern in adaptationist debates that I cannot place precisely in the literature. The pattern unfolds in three stages. First, a researcher proposes that an observable trait—for instance, a particular cranial suture pattern, a pigment distribution in avian plumage, or a metabolic intermediate—is an adaptation maintained by natural selection because it confers some direct fitness benefit. Second, critics demonstrate that the trait is developmentally coupled to another feature that is uncontroversially adaptive: altering the pathway that produces the beneficial feature necessarily produces the trait in question, so the trait may be a mechanical byproduct rather than an independent object of selection. Third, rather than accepting the trait as a non-functional byproduct, defenders expand the adaptive claim: they argue that selection acted not on the trait in isolation but on the integrity of the coupled developmental system itself, since disrupting the coupling would compromise the genuinely adaptive feature. The result is that the originally criticized trait is reabsorbed into an "integrated adaptive package," and the category of pure byproduct seems to collapse, because any feature mechanically linked to an adaptive one can be reclaimed in this way.

I have observed this structure in discussions of skeletal morphology, feather coloration, and biochemical pathways. What I am after is not the general concept of a developmental byproduct—I am familiar with that—but a specific name, if one exists, for this maneuver of functionalizing the coupling mechanism in order to neutralize byproduct objections. Is there an established term for the expansion of the selective unit from the individual trait to the coupled process? How precisely does this differ from an ordinary auxiliary hypothesis, and is there a recognized philosophical or biological critique that targets this specific form of theoretical defense? If no name exists, I would welcome a coinage with clear boundary conditions that distinguish it both from simple exaptation narratives and from the standard addition of ad hoc saving conditions.
