hey, im writing some rough notes for an essay on the Meno problem — you know, why Plato thinks knowledge is worth more than just having a true belief. my angle is that being tied down by an account isnt really about justification in the modern sense, but about creating what i call epistemic inertia: once a belief is anchored in a network of reasons, it pulls neighbouring beliefs into alignment and resists local revision.
