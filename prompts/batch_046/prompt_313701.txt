like, if im enjoying the visits, does that mean im gradually losing moral credit? is there some kind of affective contamination where pleasure dilutes the worth? i even caught myself trying to make it harder on purpose—going in bad weather, refusing her coffee—like im running a purity calculus where suffering is the exchange rate for virtue. but that cant be right, can it? i mean, if moral worth is maximized by making good acts feel as miserable as possible, then the best person is just someone who hates being good. so is there actually a sliding scale here, or am i completely misreading the distinction between acting in accordance with duty and acting from it? and if enjoyment really does cancel worth, should i be trying to suppress it, or is that itself a weird kind of vanity?
