Write a short story set aboard a closed-loop station orbiting Titan, where four crew members jointly govern atmospheric-scrubber cycles and private sensory pods. Each holds a minimal liberal right to veto any sensory input that crosses into their own pod. A proposal to reallocate scrubber energy to expand private space is Pareto-improving—every crew member prefers it—but it forces one member’s pod adjacent to a shared sound wall they find intolerable, which they veto under their liberal right. Dramatize the exact logical structure of Sen’s liberal paradox: show how respecting one individual’s minimal domain of choice blocks a unanimously preferred collective outcome, and let the negotiation reveal why Pareto efficiency and minimal liberalism are not jointly satisfiable in this setting. Do not resolve the tension with an easy compromise; end with the structural conflict intact.
