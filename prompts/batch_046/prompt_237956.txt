My thesis is that this canonical contrast collapses once we apply the evidential norms of stratigraphic correlation and falsifiability familiar from geology. Herodotus' cross-referencing of oral traditions from different cultural layers operates analogously to correlating facies across discontinuous outcrops: he treats contradictory accounts as variation requiring correlation rather than exclusion. Thucydides' dismissal of the mythical functions structurally like rejecting anomalous fossil assemblages that violate a prior evolutionary model—an unfalsifiable filter that protects theory from disconfirming evidence rather than testing it. I therefore argue that a genuinely critical historiography should privilege Herodotean correlation over Thucydidean exclusion, though I recognize the geological analogy is not seamless.
