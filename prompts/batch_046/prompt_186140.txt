I'm a hard anti-paternalist about informed risk, but I think public-health campaigns discouraging risky sexual behavior are legit even for fully informed adults. And I reject natural law entirely, though I think evolutionary psychology on jealousy gives us defeasable reasons not to completely dismantle pair-bonding norms.
