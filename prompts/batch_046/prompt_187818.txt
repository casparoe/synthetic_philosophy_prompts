I claim that current LLMs generally fail this stricter criterion, but that certain fine-tuned reasoning modules—particularly in formal verification and multi-step mathematical inference—approach the threshold closely enough to make localized belief attribution metaphysically defensible. They share a deep suspicion of "spooky" mentalism, yet they also take the explanatory demands of alignment research seriously.
