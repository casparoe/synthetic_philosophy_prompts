Hey — can you read over this argument I sketched for my seminar? I think there's a hidden slip but I can't quite name it, and I'd rather not just throw out Schopenhaur's direct-copy thesis if I don't have to.

Here are the steps my friend laid out:

(1) In Schopenhauer's account music is unique because it doesn't represent phenomena or Ideas but copies the Will itself immediately.
(2) If the copy is direct, then pitch, timbre, and instrumentation are only vehicles; the real content is the dynamic pattern of tension and resolution that mirrors the Will's own striving.
(3) So if you preserve those dynamics exactly but apply a spectral inversion—mapping every frequency to its inverse—the underlying Will-structure is copied with identical fidelity.
(4) By the same reasoning, a rigorously arranged industrial noise sequence that reproduces those same dynamics expresses the Will just as well as a Bach fugue.
(5) Therefore the direct-copy thesis gives us no principled basis for Schopenhauer's hierarchy that puts melody at the top and bass at the bottom; it collapses into acoustic relativism where any sound-pattern with the right dynamics is equally revelatory.
(6) But Schopenhauer clearly holds that melody reaches the highest grade of Will-objectification, and he treats some compositions as genuinely deeper copies of the thing-in-itself than others.
(7) So either music is representational like the other arts, or the acoustic form is constitutive rather than vehicular, and in either case the direct-copy view is damaged.

My friend thinks the error is between (4) and (5), maybe a quantifier shift about "any" sound-pattern? I feel like (2) might be smuggling in too thin a notion of dynamics—like treating the Will's structure as pure mathematics that can be detached from how it's actually heard. If I'm forced to pick, I'd like to keep (1) and (6), even if it costs me (2). But I want to know exactly what that costs: does it mean music is mediated through Ideas after all, or can we keep direct-copy with a richer, phenomenologically loaded idea of dynamics?
