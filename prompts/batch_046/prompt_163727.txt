I'm preparing a position paper for an advanced philosophy of science seminar and want an internal consistency audit before I present it. Over the past few weeks I have settled on the following package of commitments, but I'm unsure whether they actually hang together or whether I am smuggling in incompatible motivations from different debates.

First, I endorse ontic structural realism in its stronger form: I take relations to be ontologically prior to relata, and I deny that the individual entities posited by our best physical theories—whether particles, fields, or spacetime points—possess any intrinsic nature apart from their structural roles.

Second, despite that, I think causal explanations in the special sciences genuinely depend on particular entities—organisms, neurons, populations—possessing causal powers that are not exhaustively captured by purely structural descriptions.

Third, I treat the Ramsey-sentence formalization of scientific theories as the correct vehicle for realist commitment, on the grounds that it strips away theoretical terms and preserves only the logico-mathematical structure linking observational and theoretical relations.

Fourth, I maintain that mathematics is indispensable to physics but carries no ontological commitment to abstract objects; the structures in question are physically instantiated rather than Platonically subsistent.

Fifth, I reject the pessimistic meta-induction, arguing that structural continuity across theory change is sufficient to preserve realist commitment even when the underlying ontological categories are radically revised.

My concern is that the first and second commitments pull in opposite directions, that the third might undermine the first by treating structure as purely formal rather than ontologically loaded, and that the fourth sits awkwardly with the idea that structures are physically instantiated—since instantiation seems to require particular instantiators with properties of their own. Are these genuine tensions, only apparent ones that dissolve on closer inspection, or some mixture? Please rank the conflicts by severity, specify for each which commitment would have to give and at what cost, and identify the least costly revision that restores coherence without abandoning the core motivation behind the package.
