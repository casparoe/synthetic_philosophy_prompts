From this she builds a non-constant, non-exponential discount function that still converges to the Ramsey rule in the long run, arguing that the pure rate indexes structural features of preference revision rather than beliefs about future goods. Write six interview questions that press the mechanics of this view. For each, provide a follow-up keyed to the reply she is most likely to give—especially the distinction she draws between structural preference revision and mere belief updating about future outcomes. Keep the questions substantive and avoid generic biographical openings.
