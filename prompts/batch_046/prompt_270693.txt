I have been arguing with colleagues about whether Frege's puzzle about identity statements remains a live problem for formal semantics, or whether it collapses into psychology once we leave natural language behind. In particular, we disagree about whether a theory of reference for artificial or purely formal systems—where terms are identified through syntactic substitution rules and operational definitions rather than through any mental mode of presentation—must still preserve some analog of Fregean sense, or whether it can dissolve the puzzle by going fully extensional. Before I can adjudicate this dispute, I want the specification of adequacy itself rather than a scorecard of existing theories.

What must a theory of reference and cognitive significance actually deliver in order to count as having genuinely resolved Frege's puzzle about informative identities, rather than having dissolved it by redefining the terms of the problem away? Give me the full desiderata list: the phenomena it must explain (including why an identity statement like Hesperus is Phosphorus can carry genuine epistemic weight despite coreference, and how this extends to formally defined terms within a calculus that lacks introspective access), the distinctions it must preserve (such as between sense and reference, between epistemic and metaphysical identity, or between first-person grasp and third-person identification), and the logical and linguistic results it must remain consistent with. For each requirement, provide the motivating case or argument behind it. Identify which desiderata are genuinely non-negotiable constraints on any adequate account and which are merely preferences of particular camps—for instance, whether sense must be mentalistic, whether direct reference is permissible, or whether the puzzle applies outside natural-language contexts. Crucially, indicate whether some subset of these requirements is jointly unsatisfiable: for example, if preserving robust cognitive significance for identity statements conflicts with a purely extensionalist semantics in formal contexts, or if the demand that sense be objective and shareable clashes with the demand that it be essentially private. A table is acceptable, but keep the weight on the criteria and their justifications rather than on scoring particular theories; brief commentary on how major approaches fare is optional and secondary.
