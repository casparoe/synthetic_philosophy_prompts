I came across a summary in a draft review that lays out the etiological vs causal-role debate like this: 'On the etiological view, a trait's function is whatever evolutionary selection actually favored it for—hearts pump because that's what they were selected for—so a heart that fails to pump is malfunctioning because it misses its design purpose.
