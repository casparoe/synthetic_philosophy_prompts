Leo pushed back and said that's just surface syntax; a lying narrator in English can do the exact same work by hedging or contradicting themselves, and the cognitive machinery of deception is identical, it's just that Turkish wears its evidentiality on its sleeve. Leo replied that this just proves it's a stylistic difference, not a Whorfian one, because the underlying mental model of testimony and belief doesn't change, only the rhetorical tools do.
