I am a biologist fluent in evolutionary developmental biology — particularly developmental bias, Waddington-style genetic assimilation, niche construction, and phenotypic plasticity — but with no training in philosophy of science. Restate Thomas Kuhn's thesis about scientific revolutions, paradigm shifts, and incommensurability using only that biological vocabulary: map paradigms onto developmental systems, anomalies onto environmental stressors, revolutions onto assimilation events, and incommensurability onto non-intertranslatable phenotypic outcomes produced by divergent pathways. Be faithful to Kuhn's claims; do not smuggle in his terminology unless it translates without loss. Then report the residue: which elements of Kuhn's account survive the translation intact, which require distortion or stretching of the biological concepts, and which have no expression whatsoever in evo-devo. Conclude by judging whether the residue indicates that evolutionary biology lacks the resources to describe scientific change, or whether it exposes an imprecision in Kuhn's own notion of incommensurability.
