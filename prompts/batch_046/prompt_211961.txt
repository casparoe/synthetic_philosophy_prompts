Here are the constraints. Third, keep the scenario realistic in its machinery: no exotic physics, no omniscient predictors, no science-fictional devices—just the logical structure of the updates. Then provide an honest assessment of which structural feature is doing the philosophical work—whether it is the infinite divisibility of the task, the absence of a final step at which to apply the rule, or the specific parity condition—and explain what happens to the paradox if that feature is removed. Finally, indicate how a defender of the view that supertasks are conceptually harmless, perhaps by appealing to limit properties or to the non-existence of a time after infinity, would attack the case, and whether their attack succeeds only against the physical version, the abstract version, or both.
