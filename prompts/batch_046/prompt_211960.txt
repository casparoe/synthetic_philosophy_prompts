I am taking an upper-level seminar in philosophy of mind on personal identity and narrative theories of the self, and we have spent several weeks on Daniel Dennett's 1978 essay Where Am I? from Brainstorms, particularly the brain-transplant scenario in which the brain, Yorick, is sustained in a vat while the body, Hamlet, remains seated, leaving open whether Dennett is with his brain, with his body, or distributed between them. Please draft eight to ten interview questions that avoid generic biography and instead press on the actual mechanics of her bifurcated-gradient account. For each question, briefly indicate the answer she is most likely to offer and the precise follow-up keyed specifically to that response.
