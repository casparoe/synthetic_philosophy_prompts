(C) Fifty years of low-grade chronic pain (2/10) versus one day of peak euphoria (10/10): I lean that the pain still outweighs the peak, which sits awkwardly with (A) if I treat intensity linearly. Can a single principle—say, one combining a consciousness-bound lexical floor with non-linear aggregation and a species-neutral base—reconcile all five? State it precisely, assign each clause to the case that motivates it, predict what it says about (D) under two modifications (pain summed globally versus locked to one subject), and tell me which verdict—most likely B or C—I must drop for coherence.
