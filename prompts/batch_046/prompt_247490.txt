As a philosophy professor serving on the procurement-ethics board for a regional hospital network, I must help evaluate protein-sourcing contracts through a suffering-weighted framework rather than carbon metrics alone. Please construct a reusable decision procedure—ordered questions, data thresholds, stopping rules, and explicit judgment-call markers—that my committee can execute in under thirty minutes per proposal. The procedure must integrate direct farmed-animal welfare costs with indirect wild-animal welfare costs arising from feed-crop cultivation, distinguishing harvest-interval harms (fixed per growing season, independent of yield) from yield-scaled harms (proportional to crop tonnage), applying conventional feed-conversion ratios to compare beef and chicken, and weighting direct suffering by the species-typical lifespan of the farmed animal. Specify what counts as adequate evidence at each stage, where the procedure issues a recommendation, and where it must halt for a value judgment that requires board sign-off rather than staff calculation. Then apply the procedure to three proposals: retaining conventional beef, switching to conventional chicken, and adopting pasture-only beef with zero feed-crop footprint but elevated direct land-use mortality. Include one case in which the procedure produces a recommendation the sustainability committee is likely to reject, and conclude by stating precisely what comparative welfare questions lie outside the procedure's reach.
