The constraint holds that for any empirically adequate theoretical framework in a sufficiently complex domain, one can construct a systematically inverted rival that assigns opposite ontological statuses to every entity not directly observed, preserves all observable predictions, and does so not through ad hoc tinkering but through a uniform transformation of the auxiliary hypotheses. - The funders hear that funding criteria should not pretend to identify "the correct model," but should instead evaluate transparency about which mirror-rival has been implicitly adopted and why.

Do not quote any real text verbatim. The tone should be welcoming but intellectually serious: you are opening a room where disagreement is structurally guaranteed, and you need to make that feel like a feature of the workshop's design, not a failure of it.
