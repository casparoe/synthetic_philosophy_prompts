Through right-hand writing, J's left hemisphere has given full informed consent; through left-hand pointing, the right hemisphere has consistently refused. The principal investigator contends that linguistic and narrative coherence make the left hemisphere the sole bearer of J's moral identity, so consent is valid. Our committee's provisional framework—adopted last year—holds that any sustained, self-referential cognitive subsystem possesses independent moral standing, giving both hemispheres veto authority. Draft the committee's opinion recommending approval, rejection, or conditional approval, with a concrete course of action and time frame. The reasoning should treat the PI's identity argument with seriousness and include an honest account of the strongest objection to your recommendation. Please write in American English.
