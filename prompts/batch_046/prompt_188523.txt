Your remarks must do real structural work: adopt one compositional stance and let it govern every claim. Inject one speculative framing device of your own: compare the gallery's fire-suppression system—which activates only when localized heat exceeds a threshold, regardless of how many sources contribute—to a model of how parts might or might not trigger the existence of a composite whole. Use this analogy to illuminate your chosen stance, not as a verified engineering fact but as a philosophical thought experiment.

Keep the language formal but accessible: avoid unexplained jargon, yet do not dilute the argument for the experts. Finally, if my framing confuses special composition with mere causal interaction, or if asking an opening speaker to defend a metaphysical verdict is incoherent, say so plainly instead of complying politely.
