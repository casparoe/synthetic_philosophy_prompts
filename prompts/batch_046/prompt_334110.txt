2. I back targeted methods like Bt toxins and pheromone disruption over broad-spectrum neurotoxins, but my reason is precision—minimizing risk to the ones that might suffer—not lowering an aggregate death count.

4. Human nutritional needs take lexical priority over insect welfare in direct conflict, period. At the same time I reject any cost-benefit frame that treats every killed aphid as an interchangable unit of disutility.

5. Spraying purely for cosmetic crop appearance is clearly wrong, but I also think sublethal neurobehavioral disruption in social insects—say, from pheromone interference—is an under-discussed harm that doesn't fit neatly into either killing or sparing.
