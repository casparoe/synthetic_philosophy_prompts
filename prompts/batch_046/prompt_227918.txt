I was talking with my friend at the pub and he pointed out this weird cluster of practices: trauma teams pour enormous resources into saving a young accident victim, but once brain death is declared they shift straight to organ donation and grief counseling for the family, and legally the estate can sue for suffering before death but not for the harm of being dead itself. What implicit theory of harm and personal identity does that pattern presuppose? Reconstruct the theory these institutions quietly rely on, say whether the parts actually hang together or are just compatible by accident, and tell me what an ICU triage protocol built strictly on the Epicurean position would have to change.
