Please go through these commitments and identify which pairs are in real tension, which conflicts only appear serious but dissolve upon inspection, and rank the genuine tensions by severity. For each serious tension, specify the least costly revision and what it sacrifices. Finally, indicate which single change to my view would resolve the largest number of conflicts at once.
