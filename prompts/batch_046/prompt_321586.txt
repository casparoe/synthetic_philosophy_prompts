There's this wellness app my friend uses where you rate your mood after different activities—eating, running, chatting, whatever—and it collapses everything into "wellbeing points" on one scale, then avereges them for a weekly score. So the pleasure of finishing a hard puzzle and the pleasure of a warm shower both become the same kind of thing, just with diffrent intencity. What view of pleasure does that practice presuppose? Can it survive the heterogenity problem—like, is there really one common quality all these pleasures share—or is it sneaking in some quantitative hedonism? And if you wanted to keep Mill's higher/lower distinction, could you still build an app like this, or does the averaging logic kill it?
