(2) But a spandrel only exists because some other adaptive trait produces it, and if that underlying adaption disappeared, the spandrel would disappear with it or at least wouldn't be maintained. (6) But then there's no possible observation that could show adaptationism is wrong, because any counter-example gets reclassified as a spandrel anchored to some hidden adaptation. (7) So either Gould and Lewontin didn't actually challenge adaptationism at all, or adaptationism is scientifically empty.
