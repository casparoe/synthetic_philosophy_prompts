(1) i'm an absurdist in the camus sense — the universe is silent about our projects, but that silence isn't a tragedy, it's just a value-neutral background.
(2) meaning in life works on a hybrid model: subjective endorsement (you genuinely care, your values align) plus an objective floor (the project involves real achievement or understanding, not just arbitrary preference satisfaction).
(3) that hybrid structure makes meaning fragile — it collapses if endorsement wavers or the achievement turns out illusory.
(4) but recognizing cosmic insignificance actually deepens meaningfulness, because you're not relying on false metaphysical consolations; the fragility itself is part of the value.
