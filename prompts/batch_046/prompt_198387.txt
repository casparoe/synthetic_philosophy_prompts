If Dennett insists that evolutionary explanations must be cranes rather than skyhooks, and if a crane explains the emergence of intentionality by tracing it back to differential selection among replicators, then doesn't this very explanation commit him to greedy reductionism? After all, such a crane seems to dissolve original intentionality into biological dynamics, leaving no autonomous ontological space for mental content. So either cranes fail to genuinely explain intentionality, or Dennett's distinction between good reductionism and greedy reductionism collapses. Which horn should he take, and does it undermine his broader naturalism?
