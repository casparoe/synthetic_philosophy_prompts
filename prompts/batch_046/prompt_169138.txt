Your task is to compose a villanelle—nineteen lines arranged as five tercets followed by one quatrain, using an ABA rhyme scheme and two repeating refrains—addressing the question of whether genuine moral dilemmas are possible. Use this architecture to make the villanelle’s form carry philosophical weight. Ensure the rhyme sounds natural, the imagery avoids generic sermonizing, and the argument is accurate enough that a specialist in deontic logic or moral psychology recognizes the structure without finding distortion. Do not include citations, footnotes, references to named texts, or bibliographic suggestions; write entirely from conceptual understanding. Leave the tension open, ending on the problem of moral residue rather than resolving it.
