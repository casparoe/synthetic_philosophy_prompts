From this the speaker inferred a radical ethical universalism in which all moral distinctions between self and other collapse into a single duty of self-preservation extended outward. The speaker then objected that the view fails because virtual machines are genuinely causally isolated, whereas human minds clearly interact and influence one another, so Schrödinger’s position amounts to a form of “computational monism” that cannot account for interpersonal causation.

I am trying to determine whether this is a fair characterization.
