In my graduate seminar on philosophy of mind, we have been pressing the question of whether functionalism can genuinely honor multiple realizability without dissolving into empirical vacuity, and I want to pin down the target before we evaluate particular accounts. Write the full list of desiderata that any adequate theory in this area must satisfy: specifically, a theory of how mental kinds—let us take belief and pain as the central cases—are individuated by functional role in such a way that they can be realized in radically different physical substrates, including not only standard biological neural architectures but also synthetic biological circuits lacking neurons entirely, distributed biohybrid systems in which silicon and tissue jointly sustain a single cognitive state, and artificial substrates that reproduce only partial behavioral analogues of the mental kind. For each desideratum, state the motivating case or conceptual constraint: for instance, the requirement that the theory distinguish genuine realization from mere behavioral simulation; that it respect comparative neuroscientific results—such as the convergence of pain-related behaviors in cephalopods and vertebrates despite radically different nervous-system architectures—without being forced to deny mental status in one case or the other; that it block "functional promiscuity" (the attribution of mental states to economies, weather systems, or simple thermostats) without smuggling in a hidden physicalist constraint that undermines realizer-independence; and that it accommodate cases of dynamic substrate replacement, where the physical vehicle of a mental state changes over time while the functional architecture remains continuous. Identify which of these are genuinely non-negotiable and which reflect the preferences of particular camps—say, the demand that functional descriptions be strictly single-level versus the demand that physical constraints enter the individuation conditions themselves—and flag any pair you take to be jointly unsatisfiable, especially the tension between robust realizer-independence and the need for physical boundary conditions that prevent promiscuity. Finally, briefly indicate how standard causal-role functionalism and so-called "realizer-functionalism" stand against your list, but keep the weight on the criteria and their motivating cases rather than on the scorecard.
