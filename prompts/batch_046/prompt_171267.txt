me and my roomate got in a argument about Hacking's looping effects in psychiatric classifcation. (1) she said that since categories like depression loop back and change how people experience sadness, psychiatric taxonomy isnt tracking pre-existing natural kinds - its partly manufacturing what it claims to find. (2) i pushed back that Hacking doesnt think looping makes things unreal, just interactive; depression is still real, same way unemployment is real even though measuring it changes labor markets. (3) then she brought up this thing she called reverse looping: when patients reject a diagnosis, clinicians sometimes read that rejection itself as a symptom - poor insight, denial. she said that creates a vicious circle where acceptance proves the disorder and resistance also proves it, so theres no outside check. (4) i said thats real for some conditions with actual anosognosia, but its not how all psychiatric diagnosis works - you can still check functional impairment or treatment response without asking whether the person buys the label. please go through their exchange move by move and say whos right at each point, what both are missing, and whether reverse looping actually undermines psychiatric objectivity or just shows we need better criteria.
