Write a formal dialogue between them. Ensure both characters speak with equal philosophical competence, respond directly to each other’s specific points rather than talking past one another, and avoid straw-man formulations. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, citations, or references to particular publications; build the exchange entirely from the conceptual architecture of heterophenomenology and its opposition. The dialogue should be rigorous enough to serve as a central philosophical scene in the novel.
