Disentangle the precise claims this slogan might express—distinguishing, for instance, metaphysical, normative, and methodological readings—and for each indicate whether it is platitudinous, a defensible Kantian position, or an oversimplification. Be sure to separate the thesis that we have only indirect duties regarding animals from the stronger thesis that they possess no moral standing whatsoever, and explain which of these interpretations actually licenses the speaker's inference about synthetic minds. Lay the interpretations out as a menu with verdicts, and indicate which ones would genuinely support the argument in which the slogan was deployed.
