Write a deadpan parody of a federal job posting, around 600 words, for a new agency called the Bureau of Problems in Living. The agency operates on a fully Szaszian model — mental illness is a myth, psychiatric conditions are just mislabelled life conflicts, and treatment is really contract negotiation between adults. The posting should use bland HR jargon (GS-12 equivalent, benefits package, EEO statement) but every bullet quietly pushes the anti-psychiatry logic to an absurd extreme: qualifications include "rejection of the brain-mind identity thesis," duties include "mediating domestic disputes that have been wrongly medicalized," and the benefits section notes that "since no disease is present, malpractice insurance is not required." The satire should make the standard objections legible without stating them — that real suffering gets moralized away, that biology disappears, that "problem in living" is just euphemism — and it should be funny/recognizable to someone who knows Szasz but readable to someone who doesnt. Keep the voice deadpan bureaucratic, no winking. Please dont ask for citations or a reading list; just build it out from the concept.
