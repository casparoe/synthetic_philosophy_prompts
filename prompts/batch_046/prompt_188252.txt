Each speaker should answer the other's actual arguments rather than talk past them. In your response, clearly distinguish what is empirically settled about the basic effect from what is genuinely contested among experts—such as whether the phenomenon tracks moral evaluation, linguistic pragmatics, or conceptual structure—and avoid presenting any contested interpretation as the consensus view.
