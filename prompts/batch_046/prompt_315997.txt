I'm auditing my epistemic commitments for a seminar on rationality and want a consistency check before presenting. Which pairs are genuinely in tension rather than only apparently so? Can (c) be maintained without covertly reintroducing the first-person authority that (a) denies? Is (e) consistent with (a) once its scope is restricted, and does (d) undermine (c) by removing the beliefs that equilibrium requires? Rank the tensions by severity, propose the least costly revision, and state precisely what feature of the original framework must be sacrificed.
