We have three motions: remove the statue entirely, keep it in place with a revised plaque, or relocate it to the museum with full curatorial framing. Draft our decision memo — pick one option and reccomend it with clear reasoning, including the strongest argument for the option you reject. Don't lay out a menu; take a definite stand the council can vote on. I want a committed response: tell me which choice is right and why, not a balanced 'it depends,' and address the best objection as if the advocates for that side were in the room.
