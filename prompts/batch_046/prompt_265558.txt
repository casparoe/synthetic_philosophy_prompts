Write an essay addressing whether a criterion of "low impact" for an artificial intelligence system—defined specifically as avoiding observable side effects—can be coherently formulated without a complete prior specification of values. Consider the proposal that impact might be minimized through purely structural or causal constraints, such as requiring that the state of the world following the system's action be counterfactually indistinguishable from the state that would have obtained had the system never acted, save for the accomplishment of its assigned task. Evaluate whether identifying which differences count as permissible task effects versus impermissible side effects necessarily imports normative judgments about what should be preserved, altered, or ignored. Weigh the arguments that such a formalization can remain value-neutral against the objections that every boundary between effect and side effect presupposes an unstated axiology. Reach and defend a clear conclusion about whether "avoid side effects" is viable as a standalone design principle. At the end, state how confident you are in your conclusion and identify the single point in your reasoning about which you are least certain.
