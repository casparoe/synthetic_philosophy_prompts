They say utilitarian reasoning in close relationships works the same way — if you rescue your partner because you calculated it's optimal, you've got one thought too many, and the relationship is already dead. I want to audit how far this actualy holds. Give me the correspndence table: what plays the role of the music, the audience, the corruption of performance, and that extra mental step? Then where it breaks — is the utilitarian actually running that check in the moment, or is it just a background justification? And does the analogy smugles in the idea that reasons are either silent or corrupting, with no middle ground?
