Does it assume that rational preference has to be strict, and that the only legitimate reason to split a resource is literal indifference? Does it smuggle in some view about value being granular in a way that doesn't allow for "close enough"? And is it consistent with treating measurment noise as real in every other context? Could you recontruct the implicit theory of choice and value here, say whether it's coherent, and tell me what would have to change in the funding rules if we replaced it with a view that allows for rational indifference over ranges, not just points?
