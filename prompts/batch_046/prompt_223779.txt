Present it as a flowchart-in-words with decision nodes in sequence, and include a small set of test conditions—structural checks the board can apply mechanically versus those requiring irreducible judgment. Apply the procedure to three cases: (1) an AI proposing to revise its own reward function by appealing to an undefined human-values layer; (2) a scientific community rejecting its measurement protocol by invoking pre-paradigmatic intuition; and (3) a constitutional court amending its own jurisdiction rules through a meta-constitutional clause—design this third case so that the procedure returns a negative verdict that a sympathetic board member would likely resist. In your framing, separate what is settled about formal systems and self-reference from what remains genuinely contested—particularly whether formal undecidability genuinely requires semantic ascent, and whether the analogy to human cognitive transcendence is explanatory mechanism or metaphor—without presenting any contested reading as consensus. Explicitly state what the procedure cannot settle and where it will misfire. Maximum 600 words.
