He also mentioned the reply called a third-factor explanation, where evolution tracks cooperation because cooperation is objectively good, but he said this just sneaks realism back in and doesn't solve the symmetry problem with math — since if evolutionary origins debunk morality they should debunk arithmetic too, because we evolved counting for food, yet nobody rejects math. I think he's definately smushing together a bunch of different positions and overstating what the evolutionary story proves. How fair is this summary?
