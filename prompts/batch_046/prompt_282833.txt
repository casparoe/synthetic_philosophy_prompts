I read this summary of the centipede game and backward induction on a forum, and it went like this: two players alternate, and if they keep going the total payoff grows for both, but at each round the person whose turn it is can take a slightly larger share than if they wait and let the other person take it next round. Backward induction works by startin at the last round and reasoning backwards - at every step the player should take the money, since that's better than letting the opponent grab the larger share on the following round.
