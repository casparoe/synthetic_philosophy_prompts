The remarks must navigate between two poles I want explicitly rejected—culture as sovereign cause, with values driving history like an independent engine, and culture as mere rationalization, with values serving only as decorative justification for material interests. The tone should be formal, reflective, and accessible; it should comfort no one into thinking the problem solved. Please write the remarks.
