What should genuinely count against this position? Please set out the observations, formal results, empirical patterns, or conceptual arguments that ought to move me, ordered by how decisive they would be. Include what proponents of prosaic alignment themselves concede as damaging, not only objections raised by critics. I am especially interested in whether the distinction between 'procedural alignment' (constraining the training dynamics and observable behaviour) and 'substantive alignment' (fixing a stable objective across distribution shifts) can be sustained within prosaic frameworks, or whether recent results in multi-agent reward hacking and out-of-distribution generalisation collapse it.
