Please provide your reasoning in decomposed form:
(i) The probability that persistent gender earnings gaps in professional dual-earner households are driven primarily by career-interruption dynamics rather than by occupational sorting, sectoral segregation, or direct discrimination.

(iii) The probability that the availability and quality of universal childcare at the point of return is the binding structural constraint, rather than leave duration, wage-replacement rate, or employer-specific flexibility.

Then combine these terms (plus any others you judge necessary) into a single credence for the main claim, with a brief justification for each term and an explicit statement of how they combine. Identify the term that carries the greatest weight in your final figure and the one in which you are least confident.
