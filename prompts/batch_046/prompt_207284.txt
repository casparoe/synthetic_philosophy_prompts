i want a poem for a philosophy meetup i go to, about 20 lines, free verse but with three words that keep circling back like in a loose sestina — 'switch', 'wince', 'weight'. its about this thing ive been chewing on: moral status isnt a smooth ladder from a thermostat right up to people. instead there are these sticky middle bits where something is just complex enough that you cant treat it like a rock, but not complex enough to be a full person, and thats wheras all our moral confusion actually lives. think insects that might feel pain but dont have selves, or an AI with feedback loops, or someone in minimal consciousness. i want the repetition of those three words to mirror how we keep looping back to these cases without ever resolving them. it should leave the tension open — not a sermon about animal rights, not a shrug about nature being nature. someone who knows the gradation debate should recognise the problem and not cringe, but my mate who just turned up for the free wine should still feel the weight of it. can you write that?
