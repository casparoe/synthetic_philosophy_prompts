The idea is that the tortoise treats an inference rule like it's just another premise that forgot to show up on the list, but really modus ponens is a proceddure for moving between premises, not a member of the premise-set. So the regress never gets started in the first place, and we don't need to hit some bedrock of brute acceptance like Carroll seems to suggest.
