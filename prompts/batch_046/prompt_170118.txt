I'm preparing a seminar handout on whether the claim that the world is becoming less violent expresses a single determinable fact or an essentially contested framing choice. Please construct a taxonomy of positions arranged as a decision tree, with each internal node labeled by the precise claim that splits the branch, phrased so a student could answer it without already knowing the philosophical labels.

Root node: Is there a fact of the matter about global macro-trends in violence at all? This divides realists, who hold that the claim is straightforwardly truth-apt; conventionalists, who hold that any answer depends on an ineliminable prior choice of metrics and categories; and error theorists, who hold that the claim is systematically confused because violence is not the kind of property that can aggregate globally across heterogeneous contexts.

From the realist branch, split first by the metric: per-capita risk of lethal direct harm; absolute annual counts of violent deaths; or a composite index that weights institutional destabilization and erosion of the state's monopoly on legitimate force.

From each metric branch, split by the ontology of violence included in the count: direct interpersonal physical harm only; direct harm plus structural violence, understood as systemic institutional exclusion that predictably shortens lives; or direct plus structural plus slow violence—chronic, dispersed, attritional harm that lacks a discrete agent-event structure but accumulates across populations and generations.

At every leaf, supply either a named position with its best-known defender if one exists, or the label "unnamed," followed by exactly one sentence stating its most awkward commitment. Pay special attention to logically available but rarely defended combinations—for instance, a per-capita direct-only realist who denies any post-1945 decline, or an absolute-count realist who includes slow violence yet still accepts a steep long-run decline. Where two leaves differ only in normative framing rather than in empirical prediction about trends, flag them as indistinguishable on this tree. Finish with a brief diagnosis of whether any remaining unoccupied branch represents an incoherent view, an unexplored one, or one that collapses into a neighboring leaf once its commitments are made explicit.
