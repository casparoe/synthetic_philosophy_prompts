(5) At the same time, I think evolutionary debunking arguments apply with particular force to our folk-psychological taxonomy: the distinction between "mechanical" and "free" behavior tracks evolutionary pressures for social prediction and punishment allocation, not a deep metaphysical boundary.

Rank the tensions by severity rather than treating them as equally damaging, specify what each revision costs, and tell me which single commitment I should drop if I want to preserve the rest with minimal damage.
