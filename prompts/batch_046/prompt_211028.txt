Does the film dramatize the gap between the cogito's minimal proof of an instantaneous thinking substance and the substantial, diachronically continuous self that Leonard attempts to reconstruct? Develop the interpretation without reducing the film to an illustration, and be explicit about where Memento resists the philosophical framing—especially on whether an externally stored "I" can still serve as the subject of the cogito.
