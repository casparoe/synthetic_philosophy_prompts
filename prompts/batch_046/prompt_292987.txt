I serve as the philosophy representative on my university hospital's Clinical Integration Oversight Board, and I need to circulate a formal decision memo before Thursday's meeting. The board must decide whether to approve a proposed "Unified Patient Narrative Protocol" (UPNP) for complex multi-specialty cases. Under UPNP, every care plan must be rendered as a single integrated propositional judgment linking all specialist inputs—for instance, that the cardiology and neurology findings jointly entail delaying surgical intervention—rather than presented as an aggregate list of discrete recommendations. The proponents invoke F.H. Bradley's problem of the unity of the proposition, together with his doctrine of internal relations, to argue that the connections among clinical judgments are constitutive of those judgments themselves, thereby avoiding an infinite regress of meta-synthesizing judgments that would be required if relations were treated as external. Critics on the board respond that this either reinstates Bradley's regress at the level of protocol implementation—since each unified judgment appears to require a further judgment about how it integrates its parts—or that it illegitimately collapses genuinely distinct clinical perspectives into an opaque monolithic formulation that obscures accountability. Please draft the board's memo recommending approval, rejection, or approval with conditions. The memo should include a clear actionable bottom line, a concrete implementation or review timeline, reasoning that engages both the metaphysical argument and practical clinical governance, and an honest treatment of the strongest consideration on the losing side rather than dismissing it. After the memo, provide a brief structured summary as a JSON object with exactly these fields: "recommendation" (the bottom-line decision, string), "philosophical_issue_instantiated" (how Bradley's regress and the unity of the proposition arise in this protocol, string), "strongest_counter_consideration" (the best objection to the recommendation, string), "conditions" (an array of strings specifying any pilot parameters or safeguards, empty array if none apply), and "timeline" (the proposed schedule for implementation or review, string).
