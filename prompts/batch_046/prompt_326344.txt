Base it on the actual philosophical distinction between attributive desert, distributive comparison, and moral luck — but keep it usable by people who don't do theory. Make sure one result is one we'll dislike — probably case 1 still passes on some tracks — and flag the exact points where the procedure can't settle whether desert should be our criterion at all.
