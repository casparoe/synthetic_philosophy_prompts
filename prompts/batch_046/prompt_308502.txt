In constructing the example, avoid reducing the problem to the familiar claim that religion is merely an illusion or a collective neurosis; instead, concentrate on the internal mechanics of how repression operates simultaneously across individual and collective registers, and on whether Freud's model of inherited guilt can accommodate historical specificity without collapsing into a generic repetition compulsion. The response should be suitable for someone familiar with psychoanalytic theory but should not depend on access to external sources.
