The line is usually traced to Kant's universalizability tests, but I want the precise conceptual claims hiding inside that one sentence laid out as a menu. Separate the distinct theses it could be expressing: a logical consistency check on maxims, a practical possibility or coordination-failure test, a consequentialist scalability objection disguised in Kantian clothing, a contractarian test of whether one can will a universal practice, and any other precise reading. For each, state whether it is a platitude, a substantive claim about the categorical imperative, a misattribution to Kant, or false; say which ones actually depend on Kant's specific formulations rather than generic common sense; and indicate which reading my colleague was most likely invoking, and whether that reading undermines voluntary caps specifically or collapses into an argument for mandatory regulation instead. I am not asking for bibliographies, textual citations, or reading lists — just the conceptual disambiguation.
