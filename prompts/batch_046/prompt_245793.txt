hey so im in a philosophy of mind seminar and we got into Nozick's tracking conditions versus closure, and i came up with this argument that seems to show tracking collapses into either omniscience or total skepticism. i really want to keep closure working for normal deductions but the conclusion feels like it proves too much.

(1) Nozick says S knows p only if p is true, S believes p, and the belief is sensitive: in the nearest worlds where p is false, S doesnt believe p. (2) Epistemic closure says if S knows p and sees that p entails q, then S knows q. (3) I know im sitting in a chair right now. (4) Being seated entails that im not a specific kind of modal duplicate — call it a T-twin — who is disembodied but recieves identical seated-experiences in the closest possible world where my seated-state fails. (5) So by closure i should know im not that T-twin. (6) But tracking that absence requires that in the nearest world where the T-twin exists, i dont believe im seated. in that world i do believe it. (7) So my belief in (5) fails the sensitivity condition. (8) Generalize: for any ordinary belief p, there is a corresponding T-twin scenario in the nearest p-false world, and by closure i must know all those absences. (9) But tracking fails for every one of them. (10) Therefore tracking plus closure implies i cant actually know any ordinary p, because knowing p forces me to know infinitely many untracked negations. (11) Nozick wanted to reject closure only for far-away skeptical hypotheses, not for these nearby modal twins. but my argument applies closure to the nearest worlds, so it seems like he has to reject closure everywhere or admit he knows nothing.

where does the chain break? is there an equivocation in step (4) about what 'nearest world' means for compound beliefs? or is the mistake assuming that knowing p automatically gives me a belief about every T-twin, rather than just knowing the entailment in some weaker sense? i feel like theres a hidden scope shift but i cant pin it down, and id rather not just toss closure if i can avoid it.
