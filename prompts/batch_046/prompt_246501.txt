By this I mean the material and institutional structures through which moral correction is delivered—whether the system emphasizes permanent visibility, status degradation, and collective witness (an exposure architecture) or whether it emphasizes discrete acknowledgment, amendable records, separation of agent from act, and reintegration (a repair architecture).
