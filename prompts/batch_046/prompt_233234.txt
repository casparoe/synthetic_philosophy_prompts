She called Nozick's experience machine a decent pump but only against naive hedonism, not preference-based views, and she dismissed Jackson's Mary as bad because it assumes phenomenal knowledge can't be physical without defending that premise. Then you reveal the framing divergence and ask whether the convergence supports the intuition or if it's just shared cognitive architecture being exploited. Given her track record, do you think she'll say it's a legitimate pump revealing stable moral knowledge, or a misleading one hiding framing dependence? Which earlier verdict is the closest analog — violinist, experience machine, or Mary? If her precedants genuinely underdetermine it, what principle is she adopting either way?
