Center the plot on a lead technician who must decide whether to administer an experimental ganglionic dampener that silences each cluster individually, knowing that this may satisfy functional welfare metrics while leaving unanswered whether any unified subject has actually been relieved. Present only the narrative itself; begin immediately with the story, with no preface, framing commentary, or explanation outside the fiction.
