I keep noticing this pattern in arguments where people cite what feels like a real reason, but there's no one it's actually a reason for. Like, someone will say we should reduce wild animal suffering even if humans never notice and the animals don't have the mental setup to 'have reasons' themselves. Or people defend preserving a dead language that nobody will speak, not because it helps anyone, but just because 'the language has value.' Even in math I see it - talking about a proof being 'elegant' as if elegance pulls on us normatively even with no audience. Is there a name for reasons that just float around without being anchored to any agent's standpoint? I'm not talking about agent-neutral reasons, since those still apply to everyone; I mean cases where there's literally no 'everyone' in the picture, just the consideration itself. If there's no established term, can you coin one and map its borders - like is this just objective list goods with the list left empty, or something else entirely?
