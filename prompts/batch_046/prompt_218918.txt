Draft six to eight interview questions that press this position rather than letting her restate it. Include at least one question about overgeneration: if every replaceable input counts as maintenance, how does she avoid counting structurally trivial actions as morally weighty? For each main question, provide a follow-up tailored to the reply she is most likely to give—particularly if she appeals to normative expectations or social signaling rather than direct causal efficacy. Write the response in American English. Also, state how confident you are in the framing of these questions and single out the point you are least sure of—specifically, whether the distinction between token causation and structural maintenance holds up consistently across all three domains mentioned.
