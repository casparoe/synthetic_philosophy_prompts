So here's where I'm at. I buy Quine's naturalism and his ontological commitment rule -- you only commit to entities that are indispensable to your best scientific theories. Applied to ethics, that kills moral realism dead: values aren't in physics, biology, or neuroscience, so they don't belong in our ontology.
