Consequently, any system that precisely duplicates the causal architecture of a human brain would necessarily possess consciousness, and anyone who denies this is not advancing a substantive disagreement but is merely engaged in a verbal dispute about the word 'consciousness.' The only serious challenge comes from the Chinese-nation thought experiment, in which a billion people each simulating a single neuron via radio communication fail to be collectively conscious; this result proves that functionalism is unacceptably liberal and should be replaced by a biological account of mind."
