On her view, incremental changes across a spectrum of wellbeing do not preserve ordering because comparative judgments depend on a context-sensitive resolution parameter that shifts between frames: A may not be worse than B and B not worse than C, yet C be worse than A, with no determinate reversal point. I want eight to ten interview questions that go well beyond restating this position. For each main question, provide a brief follow-up keyed to the answer she is most likely to give. Write your response in British English.
