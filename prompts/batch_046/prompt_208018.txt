Please draft the joint statement they could both sign: begin with a numbered section of agreed claims covering the logical structure of harm-ascription, the shared premises about personal identity over time, and the exact point at which standard formulations of the Epicurean argument and the deprivation argument cease to be direct contradictions and become cross-purposes; follow this with a disagreements table that states, in wording each camp accepts for its own view, where they diverge on whether welfare must be indexed to a temporally present subject, whether counterfactual comparison requires modal proximity between worlds, and whether “posthumous harm” can be reconstructed without invoking a ghost-subject.
