Don't label the modes like a textbook; a reader who knows Jain logic should recognize the pattern, and someone who doesn't should still feel the multiplicity without it turning into a sermon. Please use American English, and since this is a creative rendering, add a brief note at the end separating what's settled about the seven-fold structure (standard in Jain texts) from what's genuinely contested (whether it solves modern epistemic paradoxes or just relocates contradiction) and flag any liberties — especially applying it to AI ethics.
