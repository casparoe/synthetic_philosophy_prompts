They should actually argue back and forth — M should press N on why an untriggered capacity should matter for ontology, and N should press M on whether saying 'one object in two places' violates basic logic without smuggling in hidden structure. Keep both sides equally sharp, no strawmen. You can leave a typo or two in the dialogue if you want.
