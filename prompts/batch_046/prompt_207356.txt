In doing so, distinguish sharply between coordinate-dependence and ontological dependence, and address whether the block-universe conclusion necessarily commits one to the equal reality of all spacetime events, or whether a thinner tenseless account can avoid that stronger ontological claim. Do not provide a bibliography or literature review; I want the conceptual mechanics worked out from first principles.
