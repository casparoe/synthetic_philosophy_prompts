Restate this entire apparatus—the idea of a principle being permitted or forbidden based on pairwise comparison of complaint strength, the denial that complaints aggregate interpersonally, the distinction between personal and impersonal reasons, and the specific verdict that one should save the five despite the single stronger claim—using only the vocabulary and structures of cooperative game theory with non-transferable utility. Think in terms of coalitions of agents, characteristic functions that assign payoff vectors, the core and whether it is empty, blocking coalitions, the Shapley value's treatment of marginal contributions, and the conditions under which a payoff allocation is stable against deviation.
