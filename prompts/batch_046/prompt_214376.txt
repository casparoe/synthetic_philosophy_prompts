Next, split each path on whether individual or agent-level decisions retain autonomous causal efficacy within whatever structural constraints are granted. For any combination of answers that yields an unoccupied leaf, provide a brief diagnosis: incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored. For each occupied leaf, supply (1) the position’s name, (2) one sentence describing its most awkward historiographical commitment, and (3) an explicit flag if your tree cannot genuinely separate it from an adjacent leaf.
