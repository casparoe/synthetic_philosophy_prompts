Two grad students in my aesthetics seminar got into it over Schopenhaur's claim that music is a direct copy of the Will, not a representation of Ideas like the other arts. (1) Maya said this is what makes music metaphysically special — painting and poetry copy Ideas, but music bypasses them entirely and expresses the Will immediately, which is why Schopenhaur puts it at the top of his hierarchy. (2) Jonas pushed back: Schopenhaur still maps bass onto inorganic nature, melody onto human striving, and harmony onto the gradation of the Will's objectifiction — so music is still representing a structured metaphysical order, just using sound instead of images. If it walks like representation and quacks like representation, it's representation. (3) Maya replied that there's a key difference: Ideas are eternal, static Platonic forms, whereas the bass-melody-harmony structure is temporal and dynamic. Music doesn't copy static forms; it analogically presents the Will's own movement through time. That's direct expression, not representation. (4) Jonas said that if chord progressions and melodic resolution actually mirror real metaphysical relations — like dissonance representing unsatisfied striving and resolution representing temporary satisfaction — then Schopenhaur is committed to a kind of musical realism where specific sounds have fixed metaphysical meanings. That sounds like symbolism, not directness, and it also means atonal music that refuses resolution should be metaphysically defective, which seems like a weird result.
