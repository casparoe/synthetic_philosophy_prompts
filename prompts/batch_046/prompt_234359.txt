I sit on my university's memorial-committee. A veterans group proposes a plaza plaque listing every dead soldier from the 2003 conflict—coalition and insurgent names interleaved alphabetically, no flags—citing just-war theory's moral equality of combatants: if both sides' fighters had equal moral status regardless of cause, then commemorative honor shouldn't depend on which uniform they wore. Admin says our policy bans 'moral equivilence' in official monuments. Draft our decision memo for thursday's vote: approve, reject, or approve with a concrete design tweak. Take the equality thesis seriously—don't just call it naive—and also answer the best objection, which is that critics of equality think it only governs the right to fight, not posthumous honor, so mixing names actually erases the moral distinction that matters. Keep it actionable, not a lecture.
