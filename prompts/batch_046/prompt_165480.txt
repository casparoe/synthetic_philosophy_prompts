My hunch is that the stall isn't just stubbornness — it's that there are two different disagreements running under one heading. One looks metaphysical: where does the mind end, and is functional parity enough to push the boundary out into the world? But I'm wondering if there's also a hidden verbal layer, because both sides keep using words like 'belief' and 'memory' without checking whether they're talking about the same phenomena. There's also this weird asymmetry I can't shake: Clark's side sounds like they're using a behavioral black-box model of functional equivalence, while the critics sound like they need mechanistic realization details — so maybe the real work is being done by an unstated clash about explanatory norms in science, not about minds at all?
