Don't just rehash the politics. Map out whether moral continuity actually survives institutional change, whether Greece's harm is still happening or is just historical, and whether the German restitution proves that amnesia is just an excuse. Then tell me where the concept falls apart -- maybe it explains too much, or misses that the museum still profits from display rights. I want the analysis running both ways: the case testing the idea and the idea lighting up the case.
