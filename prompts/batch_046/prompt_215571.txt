Construct a thought experiment in which a contemporary institutional norm—specifically, the ritualised practice of filing elaborate "ethical transparency reports" that confer departmental honour but do not alter actual decision-making—collapses primarily through a shift in honour and social recognition rather than through moral argument or material interest, in line with the Williams and Appiah thesis on moral revolution.
