Design a 90-minute workshop for 14–16 participants in an advanced aesthetics or philosophy-of-art seminar on the problem of letterform identity—what makes every instance of “a” belong to the same category despite radical geometric variation, and whether the answer lies in structural invariants, family resemblance, or in a more elusive system-relative coherence within a typeface family.
