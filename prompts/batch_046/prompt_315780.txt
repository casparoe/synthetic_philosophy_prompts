I host a small philosophy podcast and next weeks guest is an unnamed philosopher defending the chase-sanborn line that qualia arent just mysterious but literally incoherent — conceptually broken like round squares, not merely hidden. I need eight to ten questions that stay in the weeds and avoid biography. One should press whether 'quining' is performatively self-defeating: to deny qualia you have to deploy phenomenal concepts like 'what its like,' which seems to presuppose a coherent referent. Include the follow-up for when they say thats just pragmatic language, not ontological commitment. Another should tackle the stubbed-toe case: if pain has no inner glow, whats the principled difference between a real yelp and a behaviorally identical zombie-yelp? I want a third on higher-order collapse: if first-order qualia are incoherent, can there be a genuine second-order belief about them, or does introspection become a kind of empty recursion? For each question, sketch the reply theyd most likely give and then the sharper follow-up. Keep it casual — im not looking for a syllabus, just something that makes them defend the view rather than restate it.
