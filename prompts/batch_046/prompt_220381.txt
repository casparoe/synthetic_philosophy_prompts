This structure made me think of the nonidentity problem in population ethics: the descendant who exists only because of that decision cannot coherently claim to have been harmed by it, since the alternative was never existing at all. Or does extending this reasoning to narrative expose a false presupposition, perhaps that harm must be evaluated independently of the conditions that constitute identity?
