The standard literature tends to treat these as boundary conditions for either representationalist or imperativist theories of pain, but before we score existing views I want the evaluation metric itself. Use the list to highlight, particularly in the final item, whether any two of these desiderata are jointly unsatisfiable—especially any tension between the claim that badness is built into nociceptive phenomenology and the claim that suffering necessarily involves a separable interoceptive appraisal layer—so that the framework itself reveals where the theoretical fault lines lie.
