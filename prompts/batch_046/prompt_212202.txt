My audience will expect either a deterministic law linking belief to economic behavior or a dismissal of values as post-hoc rationalization, and I want to show them that Weber offers a distinct causal logic—elective affinity rather than productive force—without letting the discussion drift into mysticism or mere storytelling.

Plan the explanatory arc for this group. Fourth, provide one diagnostic question I can pose at the end that reveals whether they have grasped the conceptual distinction rather than merely assented to it. Fifth, state clearly what should be left out at this level—whether methodological individualism, the comparative sociology of other religions, or the full economic history of capitalism—and justify the boundary.
