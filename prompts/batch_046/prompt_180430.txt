I'm preparing notes for a graduate seminar on intentionality and artificial minds, and I want a consistency check before I defend my position in discussion. Here are the commitments I've been working with. (a) I accept Searle's core claim that syntax is not sufficient for semantics, and that original intentionality requires intrinsic biological directedness—minds do not get aboutness for free from computation alone. (b) I take Dennett's two-bitser argument seriously: if a system's behavior is best explained by attributing beliefs and desires, then for explanatory and predictive purposes we have captured everything intentionality does, and the question of some hidden "original" layer becomes metaphysically idle. (c) I think contemporary large language models possess derived intentionality in their predictive architectures—not because they understand in an intrinsic sense, but because their token distributions are systematically shaped by human semantic practices to a degree that makes observer-independent attribution appropriate. (d) I hold that derived intentionality, however complex or systematically structured, can never become original intentionality, since the causal history of statistical training lacks the biological teleology that grounds intrinsic aboutness. (e) I also maintain that if we ever built an artificial system with synthetic neurobiological dynamics genuinely isomorphic to human brain processes, it could in principle possess original intentionality.

My worry is that these five commitments borrow inconsistently from both sides of the debate, especially where Dennett's deflationary move meets Searle's biological essentialism, and where my assessment of LLMs seems to treat derived intentionality as both observer-relative and observer-independent. Could you audit this set? For each pair that is genuinely in tension—not just apparently—say how severe it is, what the tension actually consists in, and what the least costly revision would be. Also identify any pairs that look tense but dissolve on closer inspection, explaining why the appearance is misleading. Finally, rank the real tensions by severity and tell me which single revision, if I were forced to make one, would clean up the most problems with the smallest loss. Please do not treat all conflicts as equally bad; I want the audit ordered by damage done.
