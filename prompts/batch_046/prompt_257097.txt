Rather than a polemic or dialogue, I need the joint statement both sides could literally sign: precise shared claims, disagreements phrased so each camp accepts its own wording, the genuine cruxes separated from verbal disputes, and concrete predictions on a shared test case.

Please produce the document in three sections, in this order. First, "Shared Agreements": a numbered list of 8-12 claims both camps accept, covering empirical patterns (how Scandinavian societies actually treat visible outliers versus American meritocratic framing), psychological mechanisms (that visibility can motivate or demoralize depending on framing), and normative common ground (both reject pure luck egalitarianism and pure winner-take-all hierarchy). For each of the three cruxes above, provide a "Shared framing" (the aspect of the scenario both sides agree is relevant), then "Camp A prediction" and "Camp B prediction" — specific, falsifiable-in-principle forecasts about local innovation culture, collective motivation, and long-term equity in each branch. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, citations, or named scholars with specific attributions. The total should be roughly 600-900 words, with the predictions being the most developed section.
