(2) Epistemic-sunk honoring: continuing because abandonment would waste embedded technical knowledge, supply-chain learning, and organizational expertise that had no transferable use outside this specific program. (3) Identity-sunk honoring: continuing because the project had become constitutive of an institutional or national self-conception—“we are the kind of polity that masters supersonic civil flight”—such that cancellation would amount to a kind of collective self-revision rather than merely a budget line change.
