Please disambiguate that slogan for me by laying out exactly five precise claims it could be making, ordered from the most trivial to the most radical; for each, state whether it denies biological reality, whether it is compatible with clinical utility, and whether it genuinely supports dismissing neurobiological research within a looping-effects framework. Respond with exactly five numbered items, each consisting of one or two sentences, and include neither an introduction nor a conclusion.
