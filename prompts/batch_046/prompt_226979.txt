Nathan, her creator, seems at times to adopt the design stance—treating her as an engineered system with specified optimization targets—and at other times to treat her as an unpredictable agent whose behavior must be interpreted rather than debugged.

Read Ex Machina through Dennett's framework of the three stances: the physical stance, the design stance, and the intentional stance.
