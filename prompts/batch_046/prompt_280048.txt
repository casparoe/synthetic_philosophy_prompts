folks writing on panpsychism and suffering keep reaching for an analogy with thermal noise in a vaccum -- virtual particles flickering in and out, except here it's fundamental micro-experiences carrying tiny bits of positive or negative valence that mostly cancle, leaving only macro-organisms with net suffering. they use it to license the conclusion that panpsychism doesn't explode the moral circle, since the cosmic background of micro-pain is negligible, like trying to hear a whisper in a storm. i want you to audit that: give me the correspondence table -- what plays the role of the vaccum state, the virtual particles, the temperature floor, the decoherence that makes macro states stable -- then the breaks, especially where physics allows destructive interference but phenomenology probably doesn't sum like vector fields. does the conclusion that panpsychism is ethically harmless survive, or was the analogy carrying the whole argument? and if it's broken, what's a less misleading analogy for how fundamental valence aggregates?
