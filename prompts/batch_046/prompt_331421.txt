I will present at an interdisciplinary seminar on logic and metaphysics in ancient philosophy. My thesis is that Gongsun Long's White Horse dialogue, traditionally read within the School of Names as a paradox of predication or a sophistical denial of identity, should instead be reconstructed as an argument for sortal-relative identity. Specifically, I claim that the dialogue distinguishes the sortal horse, which applies to the kind irrespective of chromatic properties, from the compound sortal white-horse, which includes a color determination, and that the negation expresses a failure of substitutivity within a restricted domain of quantification rather than a contradiction in terms.
