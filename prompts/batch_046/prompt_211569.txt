Here are my verdicts about which propositions function as Wittgensteinian hinges—framework conditions that stand fast rather than serving as empirical hypotheses—in different practical and theoretical settings, and I want to know whether a single principle unifies them.

In ordinary action, that I have hands is a hinge: doubting it is not rational caution but conceptual confusion. In long-term planning, that the earth existed long before my birth is a hinge; I do not treat it as a conjecture open to verification. By contrast, that this calculator is functioning correctly, when I am auditing tax figures, is not a hinge at all; it is precisely the claim under test.

That the rules of chess have not changed overnight is a hinge during a match, yet it becomes an empirical claim when a rules committee considers an amendment. That my sensory apparatus is generally reliable strikes me as a hinge when I wake up wondering whether I am delusional, but it is treated as an empirical assumption in a neuroscience laboratory that can be undermined by data. Meanwhile, that a given text was produced by a human rather than by a language model functions, in ordinary conversation, like a hinge; yet in a forensic audit it behaves like an empirical claim susceptible to statistical testing. I keep going back and forth on whether context alone fixes hinge-status, or whether some propositions resist empirical treatment even inside investigative practices, and I cannot tell whether my oscillation reflects a real distinction or just indecision.

Is there one account—perhaps drawing on inherited framework, on role in a particular inquiry, or on resistance to revision-cost—that delivers all of these verdicts? If not, which of them should I overturn for the sake of simplicity? And what does the resulting account predict for a case I have not listed: does that the laws of logic contain no hidden contradictions function as a hinge for a mathematician working within a formal system, or does it become an empirical conjecture once she seriously considers non-classical alternatives?
