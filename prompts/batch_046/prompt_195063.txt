Build me a checklist: the questions in order, what counts as a pass/fail at each, where it outputs honor the directive, where it outputs follow current preference, and where it stops and says this is a values call for the family and doc. Be explicit about the misfire points -- especially when the patient gives reasons that sound reflective but are probably just reactive to immediate discomfort. And flag which steps are really judgement calls with a checklist costume on.

Then apply it to two cases: (1) moderate dementia patient refuses previously-requested palliative sedation, but shes in severe pain that the directive never mentioned; (2) patient demands continued ventilation against a clear DNR, but hes calm, oriented enough to talk about his grandchildren, and says he changed his mind to see them graduate. Make sure one of these gives an answer I probably wont like. Also tell me what the procedre flat out cant do.
