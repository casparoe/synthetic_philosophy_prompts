These anchors are not themselves beliefs, she insists, but stable inferential habits that are pragmatically licensed rather than epistemically justified, functioning rather like grammatical rules that make justification possible without being part of its content. I replied that if they are not beliefs and do not justify content, they cannot support the cross-domain beliefs that depend on them. We are at an impasse.
