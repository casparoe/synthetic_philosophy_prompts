I hold the view that the statue and the lump of clay are not two distinct material objects standing in a relation of constitution, but rather one and the same “process-node” indexed differently by two tracking protocols: the art-institutional protocol, which registers persistence conditions tied to shape and authorial intent, and the materials-science protocol, which registers persistence conditions tied to chemical composition and mass continuity. On this account, asking whether the statue survives being squashed is not asking about the fate of one object versus another, but about which tracking framework we have practical reason to apply; there is no further hidden metaphysical fact of constitution that makes one answer correct, and the traditional material-constitution puzzle dissolves into a pragmatics of object-tracking rather than into ontology.

What would genuinely count against this position? I want a map of its exposure: which specific arguments, thought experiments, formal results in mereology or modal logic, intuitions about identity, or developments in metaphysics or the philosophy of science should move me, sorted by how decisive they would be versus merely awkward. In particular, does the view risk anything, or has it been qualified into unfalsifiability by allowing that any apparent counterexample can be absorbed as a “protocol switch”? What predictions does it make that rival accounts—standard constitution views, four-dimensionalism, or eliminativism about artifacts—do not? What would its own defenders concede as damaging: for instance, a demonstration that tracking protocols necessarily presuppose an underlying object that they track, or a formal result showing that identity conditions cannot be relativized to practices without collapsing into contradiction? And if the view turns out to be well insulated—if every apparent defeater can be redescribed as a choice of protocol—is that insulation a virtue of its flexibility or a vice that renders it explanatorily empty? Please assess honestly whether this position is genuinely at risk.
