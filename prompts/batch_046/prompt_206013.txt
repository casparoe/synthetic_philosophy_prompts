I'm writing a feature on hinge epistemology—basically Wittgenstein's On Certainty idea that some beliefs sit at the bottom of the pile and don't get justified because they hold the whole framework up. But I'm stuck on a taxanomy problem that's making my editor nervous, and I want to check my gut reactions against whatever principle actually captures them.

Here are my verdicts: "There's an external world" is definitely a hinge. "The earth has existed for billions of years" also feels like one, but only because of the scientific framework—if I grew up in a culture with a 6,000-year cosmology, that wouldn't collapse my ability to navigate the world, so maybe it's framework-relative? Meanwhile "I'm not currently hallucinating" feels like a hinge, but "this specifc shade of blue is real" doesn't—if I'm color-blind or it's a screen glitch, I revise the specific belief, not my whole perceptual system. Then there's "other people have minds"—hinge? I think yes, but some philosophers treat it as an inference. And "mathematical induction holds"—hinge for math, but not for everyday life? Here's my weirdest call: I think "the past is real and not fabricated five minutes ago" is a hinge, but "my memory of breakfast is accurate" isn't—yet they feel structurally similar. Also, I think "scientific measurement is mostly trustworthy" is a hinge for modern people, but was it for pre-modern societies? I'm acutally not sure about that last one.

So what's the actual principle? Is it about the cost of revision across your whole belief network? About what you'd have to stop doing if you doubted it? Or is there a social-institutional layer—hinges are the commitments embedded in practices like counting, weighing, or testifying? I want one account that handles the framework-relativity I mentioned and doesn't just say "hinges are whatever you can't doubt," because that feels circular. And please tell me which of my verdicts your principle has to reject—especially the one about the 6,000-year cosmology not breaking navigation, because I'm not fully committed to that either, and also what it predicts for whether "I am not a brain in a vat" counts as a hinge or just a refusal to play the game.
