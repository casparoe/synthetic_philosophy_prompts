Could you pinpoint the subtle confusion in the argument that revived testimony validates survival claims, clarify how verificationism distinguishes between describing a temporary brain-state episode and asserting metaphysical continuity, and indicate whether this distinction is philosophically stable or whether it smuggles in commitments about consciousness that a strict logical positivist should deny?
