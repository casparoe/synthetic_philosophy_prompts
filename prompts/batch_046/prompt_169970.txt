Please provide six to eight focused questions that press this interpretation where the literature is least settled. Another should probe the empirical or conceptual criteria that distinguish quantitative labor reduction from the categorical dissolution she claims, and a third should ask how her reading accommodates Marx's formulation that capital is dead labor dominating living labor when, by her lights, there is no longer living labor to dominate. Anticipate the standard defenses and craft each follow-up to make them harder to deploy. No biographical or bibliographic questions; keep the exchange on whether this view saves Marx's critical apparatus or renders it obsolete.
