Please audit this package of commitments. Are there genuine tensions among them, or do they form a consistent position? Rank any tensions you find by severity, distinguish apparent conflicts that dissolve on inspection from real ones, and identify the least costly single revision that would restore coherence while preserving as much of the view as possible.
