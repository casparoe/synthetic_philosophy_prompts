Do not include bibliographies, citations, or references to secondary literature; the conceptual work should be carried entirely by the plot and its implications. Please present the story as continuous prose only—no headings, no bullet points, no bold or italic text used for structure, no tables, and no section breaks—written as a single flowing narrative, in the manner of an extended letter.
