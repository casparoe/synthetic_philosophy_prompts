(2) "Running it" just means the reasoning architecture executes the steps within its budget.

(3) So failure to believe C can only mean the derivation path was never traversed.

(4) Take an ideal agent I with no budget cap. For I, every individual derivation path is traversable.

(5) Therefore for every C entailed by B, I traverses that specific path and comes to believe C.

(6) Real agents — and our bot — are just I with a tighter budget, not a seperate kind of thing.

(7) So we're also omniscient in principle, just slower; saying "I don't know C" is really just "I haven't computed C yet."

(8) But if ignorance is always temporary, then knowledge and potential computation collapse, which makes tracking what the bot actually knows right now impossible.

(9) So either closure is false, or the concept of knowledge is useless for real-time reasoning.
