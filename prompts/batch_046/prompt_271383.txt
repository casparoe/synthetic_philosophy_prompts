I am unsure whether raising my offer is a rational defense of my position or whether I am stepping into a kind of trap where the costs keep mounting for both of us regardless of who "wins." Could you help me understand the structure of what is happening here, whether there is a principled way to exit without feeling I have surrendered my integrity, and whether my urge to escalate can be justified?
