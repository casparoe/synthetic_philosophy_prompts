So the toast needs to use the fink-versus-mask distinction to shape what I can and can't say about her career: some obstacles were finks that only hit at the moment of testing, others were masks that stayed in place. The family should follow without feeling lectured, and the philosophers shouldn't feel like I'm dumbing it down. Can you write it with one joke that lands for both halves?
