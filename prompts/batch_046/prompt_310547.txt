I am a PhD candidate in history and philosophy of biology preparing an editorial protocol for an archive of unpublished 1870s correspondence among evolutionary naturalists. I need a reproducible decision procedure—usable by research assistants without philosophical training—to classify ambiguous moral-evaluative passages in these letters according to whether they operate within Darwin's mature evolutionary ethics or within a residual theistic moral framework.

Ground the procedure in the conceptual distinction Darwin draws between the origin of moral sentiment, understood as evolved social instincts shaped by natural selection and refined by reason and habit without transcendent sanction, and normative appeals that implicitly treat moral development or human exceptionalism as directed or embedded in an independent cosmic order. The deliverable must be a step-by-step protocol, not an essay. Include: (1) an ordered checklist of linguistic and argumentative markers—such as whether morality is framed temporally as primordial versus recent, whether comparative reasoning appeals to anatomical continuity or to designed function, and whether value terms are indexed to group utility or to an objective moral order; (2) three worked applications, each in under one paragraph, including one case where the procedure classifies the passage as evolutionary-functional despite surface religious vocabulary, and one ambiguous hybrid where the checklist yields an unstable result that must be flagged for faculty-editor co-signature rather than resolved; (3) explicit misfire conditions—specific patterns of Darwin's own textual inconsistency that generate false positives or false negatives; and (4) a decision-tree in words specifying a ten-minute time limit per excerpt, expertise thresholds for solo classification versus mandatory review, and required documentation fields.

The procedure must be fully operable without bibliographic lookup or external reference works. State clearly which philosophical distinctions from Darwin's published writings must survive the simplification and which nuances can be dropped without corrupting the classification. Include one hard case that the procedure is designed to misclassify, together with the corrective judgment an experienced editor should apply. Do not provide bibliographies, reading lists, or citations; the protocol should depend only on the text in front of the assistant and on the conceptual framework specified above.
