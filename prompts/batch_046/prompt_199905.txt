Render the distinction between the knight of infinite resignation and the knight of faith with enough accuracy that a careful reader of Fear and Trembling would recognize it, but avoid presenting Abraham’s silence as simple mysticism; instead, let the poem’s form hold the tension between the universal and the singular.
