Here are my verdicts on when a system is conscious, guided by structural intuition rather than behavioral output. A healthy awake human brain is conscious. A cerebellum with roughly eighty billion neurons arranged in purely feedforward parallel fibers is not conscious, despite its scale. A digital computer running a neuron-perfect simulation of that brain on standard von Neumann architecture is not conscious, even though every spike and synaptic weight is reproduced. Two hemispheres with a fully severed corpus callosum, each able to report independent experiences, are two conscious entities rather than one. A brain whose long-range axons are replaced by wireless radio links that preserve timing, fidelity, and causal influence is still a single conscious entity, because the causal structure survives the change in transmission substrate. Now the hard pair: a grid of biological neurons in which all causal interaction is mediated by a single external pacemaker neuron that is not part of the grid itself; the grid's internal integrated information is near zero, yet if the pacemaker is included in the system boundary, phi is enormous. I think the grid alone is unconscious, and including the pacemaker does not fuse them into one unified mind—rather, the pacemaker is either a separate minimal conscious system or neither is conscious, but in no case do they form one consciousness. Is there a single principle—perhaps about intrinsic causal power, exclusion of extrinsic mediators, or substrate-independent preservation of causal structure—that delivers all six verdicts? If not, which verdict should I overturn and why? Finally, apply the best principle you can defend to a case I have not listed: a system with very high phi in which every causal path between its two major subsystems passes through a single removable bridge module; if the module is extracted, the halves become isolated and their phi collapses to near zero. What is the consciousness status of the intact system, and what happens if the bridge is gradually replaced by direct connections that preserve the same overall causal graph?
