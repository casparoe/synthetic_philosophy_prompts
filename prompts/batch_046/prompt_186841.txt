Given Weber’s argument that the Protestant ethic was only causally adequate for the rise of modern capitalism in combination with other conditions—what philosophers of science call an INUS condition—doesn’t this imply that culture is never an independent cause, merely a background element within a specific historical configuration? If value-complexes only generate outcomes inside particular conjunctures, and Weber rejects isolating them through counterfactual intervention, hasn’t he implicitly conceded that norms cannot serve as causal levers in the interventionist sense? I raise this because rationalist discussions frequently cite Weber to justify treating beliefs or social values as direct explanatory variables, yet his own methodology appears to forbid exactly that kind of causal claim.
