I retired several years ago from a career in experimental physics, and since then I have been studying philosophy of science in a disciplined, if unsystematic, way. I have come to accept Imre Lakatos's methodology of scientific research programmes as the most adequate normative framework for scientific rationality—superior to Popper's falsificationism, which demands immediate surrender upon refutation, and to Kuhn's paradigms, which risk making scientific change look irrational—because it permits a hard theoretical core to be protected by a belt of auxiliary hypotheses, judging a tradition progressive only when adjustments in that belt generate novel, empirically risky predictions rather than ad hoc immunizations. Lately I have tried to apply this framework to contemporary large-scale science—high-energy physics collaborations, climate-model ensembles, and especially AI-assisted materials discovery—where the protective belt is no longer adjusted by individual theorists but is continuously updated by automated statistical pipelines and distributed algorithmic peer review. I want to maintain that Lakatos's distinction between progressive and degenerating problem shifts survives this transition: algorithmic adjustments belong to the belt if and only if they yield corroborated predictions that the hard core did not already entail, and the normative force of MSRP remains intact.

What, then, would count decisively against this position? I am not asking for bibliographies, citations, or reading lists—only for the arguments, conceptual difficulties, or historical patterns that should lead me to abandon or severely qualify the claim that MSRP remains viable in an era of algorithmically mediated science. Does the automation of auxiliary-hypothesis generation collapse the distinction between core and belt? Would sustained predictive success achieved entirely through machine-tuned parameters count as progressive in Lakatos's sense, or would it show that the normative weight of MSRP depended on an obsolete model of individual theoretical agency? Please rank the potential defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, indicate which ones a committed Lakatosian could honestly concede, and assess whether the view has been insulated into unfalsifiability—if so, whether that insulation is a defect for a normative account of science.
