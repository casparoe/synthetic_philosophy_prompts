To make the argument more precise, I want to inject a somewhat niche framing: I treat the American shift not as simple semantic drift but as a deliberate rhetorical inversion carried out by reformist intellectuals who needed to dissociate their project from the laissez-faire associations of European liberalism, thereby preserving the normative prestige of the word while reversing its institutional content.

I am not asking for a bibliography or reading list, but I would like conceptual comments on whether this sketch holds together. Are there tensions in treating "liberal" as both a class descriptor in Europe and a functional descriptor in America that my framing glosses over?
