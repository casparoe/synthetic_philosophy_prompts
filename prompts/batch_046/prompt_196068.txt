I feel like she's smuggling in a false equivalence between market size and political domination, but I can't fully articulate it. Is the mistake that she assumes any large-scale exchange counts as a free market, ignoring that Smith saw the Navigation Acts and exclusive company privledges as destroying the conditions for the invisible hand? Or is there something even deeper wrong, like thinking Smith believed specialization was good regardless of whether it was enforced?
