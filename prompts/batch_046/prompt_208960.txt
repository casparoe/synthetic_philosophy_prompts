I have no formal background in philosophy of mind, but I think I understand the functional differences well enough to want something sharper than a generic setup. Write a joke that turns on the actual content of these four categories, not on stereotypes about intelligence. Set it in a single situation—say, all four encountering a bridge that might collapse—where the punchline depends precisely on the specific cognitive limitation that distinguishes each kind: the Darwinian responds to an innate reflex, the Skinnerian needs prior direct reinforcement or punishment, the Popperian can imagine falling and survive that imagination, and the Gregorian can issue a verbal warning that the others literally cannot process because they lack the representational machinery. The joke should be genuinely funny to someone who knows the taxonomy, should work only with these four exact kinds, and should not include citations, page references, or suggestions for further reading. I just want the joke itself, constructed so that the mismatch between representational capacities does the real comedic work.
