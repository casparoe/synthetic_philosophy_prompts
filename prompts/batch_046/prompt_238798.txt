I'm currently taking an MA seminar on Spinoza's political philosophy, and I'm trying to settle on a focused research direction for my dissertation. Spinoza's democratic theory is often presented as either a rationalist defence of collective self-rule or as an anticipatory sketch of modern popular sovereignty, but I think both readings gloss over a deeper tension: in the Ethics he ties rational agreement to shared adequate ideas, whereas in the Tractatus Politicus he treats democracy as the regime in which the multitudo retains its natural right, even when driven largely by passion. This raises several open lines of inquiry that seem worth disentangling.

First, is the democratic regime described in the Tractatus best understood as an institutionalisation of rational common notions, or rather as a pragmatic containment of affective conflict that never achieves full rational unity? Second, to what extent can Spinoza's concept of potentia multitudinis be translated into contemporary democratic theory—particularly into debates about epistemic democracy and affective polarisation—without distorting his metaphysics of power, given that modern pluralism typically assumes irreducible disagreement rather than convergence on adequate ideas? Third, there is the interpretive problem of whether the unfinished state of the Tractatus implies that democracy is the only regime fully consistent with nature, or merely the least unstable among necessarily imperfect ones, and what this distinction means for the normative force of his account.

Given that I have roughly two years, a background in conceptual analysis rather than archival history, and access only to the standard Latin editions and established translations, which of these questions—or which others you think I'm neglecting—should I prioritise? I want a defended ordering rather than a survey: please specify which question is upstream of the others in terms of interpretive clarity, which is most tractable for a philosopher working without extensive manuscript resources, and which carries the greatest downstream weight for contemporary democratic theory as opposed to purely internal Spinoza exegesis. State the criteria you are using—whether importance, tractability, neglectedness, or dependency relations between problems—and indicate what kind of conceptual argument or textual evidence over the coming year would cause you to revise the ranking.
