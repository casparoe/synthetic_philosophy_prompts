Write a dialogue between Dr. Elena Voss and Dr. Marcus Chen debating whether a transcendental argument can establish counterfactual modal reasoning as a necessary precondition for empirical causal judgement, rather than merely a psychological heuristic. Voss should argue that without the capacity to represent alternative outcomes, the distinction between correlation and causation collapses into mere associative expectation, and she should defend this by introducing a precise distinction between transcendental conditions of judgement and transcendental conditions of judgement-formation in order to block the charge of psychologism. Chen should reply that this confuses conditions for making a judgement with conditions for the judgement being correct, and that evolutionary psychology fully explains the heuristic without granting it any transcendental status. Both characters must be equally competent, respond directly to each other’s specific points rather than talking past one another, and maintain a rigorous but civil tone. Write the answer in British English.
