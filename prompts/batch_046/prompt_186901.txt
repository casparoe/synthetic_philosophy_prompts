A startup wants $200k to build an AI chatbot that screens kids for anxiety by only reading thier text messages to a school counselor bot. It never sees the kids, never talks to doctors, just learns that phrases like "my stomach hurts" line up with counselor labels of "anxiety" in old chat logs. They claim that's real understanding, and they pitch this idea they call "disembodied semantic triangulation" — they cross-check the kid texts against clinician notes and parent surveys so the symbols stabilize across three language sources without ever touching the real world. I think this hits on that symbol grounding problem I read about somewhere, but I honestly can't tell if thier triangulation thing actually solves it or just papers over it.

Please draft the memo I'll circulate before Thursday's meeting. I want a clear recommendation — approve, reject, or approve with conditions — plus a concrete time frame like "six months only if..." Don't just list pros and cons; take a definite stand and defend it, though you should still honestly deal with the strongest point on the losing side. Organize it like this, with roughly these lengths:

Bottom Line: 2-3 sentences.
What We're Deciding: about half a page.
The Grounding Worry: about half a page.
The Triangulation Defense: about half a page.
