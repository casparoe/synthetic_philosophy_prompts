Plan the explanatory arc for me: what dependency has to be in place before the crane/skyhook distinction even makes sense to them, and why must it come first? For the carrying example, I am considering using the evolutionary repurposing of the mammalian articular and quadrate bones into the malleus and incus as a crane—contrasted with a hypothetical “irreducible design principle” skyhook for auditory acuity—but tell me whether this example carries the right properties (gradual build-up, preservation of functional complexity, absence of an explanatory leap) or whether I should substitute a different one, and specify exactly what that substitute must have and lack.
