Her idea is that in a perfect tie between two equally good options, the agent doesn't just randomly break symmetry; instead, the act of choosing itself generates a new first-order preference, which means there was never actually a tie at the level of practical reason. I want eight to ten questions that really go into the weeds, not just restating the view. Beyond those three, include a couple that probe the infinite regress worry — does generating a preference require a prior meta-preference, and so on — plus one about collective decision-making when a committee splits exactly fifty-fifty.
