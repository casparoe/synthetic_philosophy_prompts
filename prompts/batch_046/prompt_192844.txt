Proponents use this to defend behavioural criteria—including variants of the Turing test—arguing that the director and audience possess only the stage, and therefore ought to judge only the stage.

For my manuscript, however, I am exploring a distinction between a 'method actor' who genuinely inhabits the part and a 'perfect puppet' whose every gesture is orchestrated by hidden mechanisms behind the flats; to the auditorium they are indistinguishable. I should like you to audit the casting analogy through this lens.
