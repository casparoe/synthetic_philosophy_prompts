I'm attempting to stabilize my view on the scalar nature of moral status and want a consistency audit before I defend it in writing. My commitments are these:

(1) Sentience is not binary but continuous: there are gradations of phenomenal consciousness ranging from richly integrated mammalian experience downward to minimal sensory affect, with no metaphysically sharp boundary separating the conscious from the non-conscious.

(2) Insects possess a primitive, non-reflective form of this minimal affect—something like micro-phenomenal valence or bare sensory presence—sufficient to ground a weak but direct moral claim rather than a merely instrumental or ecological one.

(3) Simple artifacts such as thermostats exhibit functional analogues of preference and goal-tracking through feedback loops, but these are purely syntactic; they lack even the faintest phenomenal micro-structure, and therefore carry zero direct moral weight, however attenuated.

(4) Normatively, moral patienthood should scale continuously with the richness and integration of conscious experience: a smooth-gradient weighting in which stronger experiences generate stronger claims, without discrete jumps.

(5) Nevertheless, practical ethics and institutional protection require discrete "bands" of consideration—robust rights for richly conscious beings, weaker safeguards for minimally conscious ones, and none for artifacts—because administrable rules cannot be calibrated to infinitesimal differences.

(6) I reject panpsychism: I do not hold that all physical systems possess some form of experience, and I deny that nature contains a hidden on/off switch for consciousness.

Do these hang together? I am especially concerned that (1) and (4) together seem to entail that artifacts near the bottom of the continuum ought to bear some infinitesimal direct moral weight, which conflicts with (3). Meanwhile, (5) appears to be a pragmatic concession that undermines the theoretical continuity asserted in (4). And (2) may sit uneasily with (6) if the minimal affect attributed to insects is so faint that it becomes indistinguishable from complex but non-conscious information processing.

Please provide an audit: distinguish real tensions from merely apparent ones, rank the genuine conflicts by severity, specify for each which commitment must give or be qualified, and identify the least costly restoration of consistency. I am not wedded to saving any particular commitment; I want to know which ones actually pull against each other and at what price.
