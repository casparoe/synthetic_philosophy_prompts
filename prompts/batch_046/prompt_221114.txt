Draft the joint statement of an adversarial collaboration between a semantic externalist in the Twin Earth tradition and a narrow-content internalist. The externalist holds that the Twin Earth scenario demonstrates that mental content is partly constituted by relations to actual environmental kinds, so that Oscar and Twin Oscar do not share the same belief content despite being psychologically identical. The internalist holds that the scenario demonstrates only that reference-fixing conditions are externally determined, while narrow psychological content—characterized entirely by internal functional or computational role—remains invariant across the twins.

Structure the deliverable as follows. First, a numbered section of agreed claims that both sides can sign, covering (a) the formal setup of Twin Earth, (b) what is uncontroversially shared between the twins (molecular structure, behavioral dispositions, internal neural organization), (c) what needs explaining (why speakers appear to mean different things by 'water'), and (d) what standard no-go results about the separation of psychological state and semantic evaluation actually establish and what they leave open.

Second, provide a disagreements table with two columns—Externalist formulation and Internalist formulation—each phrased in wording that its own side would accept. The table should isolate the precise crux: whether psychological-role identity across possible worlds entails mental-content identity, or whether content is instead determined by historical-environmental relations that vary independently of role. For each crux, record what each side would treat as decisive counter-evidence—for example, empirical findings about whether neural representations track distal environmental properties versus purely local computational states—without requesting bibliographies, citations, or reading lists.

Third, include a brief section identifying any part of the dispute that is merely verbal—particularly surrounding the terms 'content,' 'meaning,' and 'belief'—and explain exactly why that part does not bear on the substantive disagreement.

Keep the language formal and polished, avoid invoking highly obscure sub-theories that a well-trained but non-specialist model could not be expected to recall, and ensure the prompt remains fully answerable without web access.
