- The structuralist also answers no, but for architectural reasons: in your constructed scenario, the global vector reaches the self-monitoring module only as a parameter (first-order input) rather than as a recursively represented state, so the integration condition for suffering fails despite the presence of globally integrated negative valence.

Finally, present the attack that the functionalist would level against the structuralist's distinction (that it is an ad hoc stipulation redefining suffering to save a distinction without a functional difference) and the attack the exclusionist would level against both other positions (that no refinement of reward architecture can bridge the explanatory gap between computation and subjective experience).
