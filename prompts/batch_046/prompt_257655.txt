ok so since i started using those ai music generators ive been spiraling a bit about whether im actually being creative or just, like, turning dials. heres roughly where ive landed — need to check if its actually coherent before i argue with my roommate about it.

(1) i mostly buy hofstadters knob-twiddling picture: creativity is taking a theme and twisting parameters — tempo, emotional distance, repetition density, whatever — and genius is just having more knobs and knowing which ones to turn.

(2) but i also think the real line between routine and genius is frame-breaking: not just turning knobs but realizing the whole control panel is the wrong one and building a new panel.

(3) except i also think those revolutionary breaks are usually just hidden meta-knobs that we recognize in hindsight, so ontologically theres no sharp line between tweaking and revolution — its all knob-turning at different orders.

(4) ai can turn the surface knobs perfectly, but since it has no model of its own process it can never do the frame-break — it cant realize its own panel is wrong.

(5) still, if an ai accidentally spat out something that functioned like a frame-break, id call it genuinely creative, because the result is what matters, not the awareness.

(6) so my actual view is that creativity is distributed: humans turn meta-knobs with awareness, ai either doesnt or does it blindly, but the ontology is the same.

(7) but i also think copyright should protect human works because ownership requires intending the specific parameter choices, which an unconscious process cant do.
