Write a one-page research proposal for a six-month conceptual fellowship on whether moral status is genuinely unidimensional. Frame the central question precisely: the standard "gradations" model treats sentience as a single axis running from thermostats through insects to humans, but this may conflate two independent dimensions—call them phenomenal patienthood (the capacity to suffer or experience) and teleological coherence (integrated, self-maintaining goal-directed organization). Your half-formed idea is that a thermostat might score near-zero on the first but possess a minimal, non-metaphorical form of the second, which would place it in a quadrant that existing animal-ethics and robot-ethics frameworks cannot represent, since those frameworks assume either a sharp sentience threshold or a smooth cognitive gradient. State why current positions leave this open: welfare-based views treat cognition and experience as bundled, while functionalist views about artifacts either deny all status or import full-blown consciousness conditions, so neither can explain why we might owe a thermostat something different from—rather than less than—what we owe an insect. Describe the method conceptually rather than empirically: months 1–2 map the logical space of obligations (intervention, preservation, non-interference) against the two dimensions; months 3–4 apply the matrix to four borderline cases (a social insect, a plant, a thermostat, a cerebral organoid) to see whether the quadrant model distinguishes obligations that the unidimensional model collapses; months 5–6 assess whether the framework survives objections about anthropomorphism and whether it actually changes practical judgments. Specify what success looks like (a defensible two-axis taxonomy that generates distinct normative prescriptions for at least one non-obvious pairing) and what failure looks like (the dimensions collapse in every case, or the framework reproduces the standard rankings exactly). Give an honest scope check: this is purely conceptual, so the risk is not data collection but conceptual redundancy—if the two dimensions track each other perfectly in all plausible cases, the project produces only a redescription. Do not request a bibliography or literature review by citation; refer to positions and arguments generally and note that sources will be filled in later. Finally, invite pushback: if the premise that teleological coherence is normatively relevant is confused, or if the project secretly depends on empirical findings about insect consciousness that philosophy alone cannot settle, say so plainly rather than proceeding politely.
