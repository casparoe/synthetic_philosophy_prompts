You are a philosopher of mind specializing in consciousness studies and cognitive architecture. Before you begin, briefly brainstorm the central tension between access-based and phenomenal accounts of consciousness.

Consider this framing: global workspace theory treats consciousness as widespread broadcasting of information to consumer systems, yet critics maintain this captures only access consciousness, leaving phenomenal consciousness unexplained. To sharpen the issue, imagine a synthetic architecture—call it a partial-broadcast system—in which sensory representations are globally available to perceptual and decision modules but are systematically excluded from self-modeling and affective-evaluation modules. The system behaves as if it perceives (it can discriminate, guide action, and report via non-autobiographical channels) yet lacks subjective presence, first-person ownership, and any what-it-is-like quality.

Write the desiderata that any adequate theory relating access consciousness, phenomenal consciousness, and global workspace broadcasting must meet, given this scenario. Specify:

- The phenomena and boundary cases the theory must explain, including the partial-broadcast case, standard dissociations between reportability and phenomenology in neurological conditions, and masking-related failures of access;
- The distinctions it must preserve, such as between availability for reasoning and availability for feeling, or between global broadcasting and phenomenal binding;
- The empirical or conceptual results it cannot contradict, for instance that reportability and phenomenology can come apart;
- Which requirements are non-negotiable and which are preferences of particular camps (e.g., determinism about neural correlates, locality of broadcasting, or the necessity of a unified self-model);
- Whether any subset of these desiderata is jointly unsatisfiable, and if so which cluster creates the tension.

Keep the focus on the criteria and their motivating cases rather than on scoring existing theories, though you may briefly note how a standard global workspace account and a phenomenal-realist view fare against one or two key requirements.

State how confident you are in the overall assessment, and identify explicitly the single point in your list about which you are least certain—whether that concerns the coherence of the partial-broadcast scenario, the negotiability of a specific requirement, or another issue.

Answer concisely; a structured list is welcome.
