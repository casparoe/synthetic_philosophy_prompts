The lamp setup is straightforward: you flip the switch infinitely many times before t=2, and since theres no last flip, the state at the limit isnt determined by the sequence of prior flips. Thomson is right that this creates a logical gap - the rules of the system dont specify the outcome at the boundary. Some people think this means supertasks are physically impossible, but thats missing the point; the lamp is a conceptual model showing that determinism about prior states doesnt automatically extend across a limit point.
