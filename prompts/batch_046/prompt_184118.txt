But here's the twist I'm actually stuck on: imagine an art installation made of programable smart-clay that reshapes itself in real time based on an algorithm running in the cloud. The chemical substrate never changes, but the form is constantly updated by code with no human hand invovled. Would Aristotle still say there's one substance undergoing accidental form-change, or would he think each algorithmic update creates a genuinely new substance since the efficient cause is now digital?
