I serve on my high school’s Student Policy Appeals Board, and we must issue a recommendation by next Friday on whether students enrolled exclusively in virtual courses—who never attend campus—should be included in the student body, or demos, for an upcoming referendum to revise our honor code. Some members argue that only physically present students are genuinely affected by campus rules, while others insist that anyone subject to the code’s jurisdiction, including virtual learners and even suspended students awaiting appeal, belongs to the demos by right. Please draft the brief formal opinion I will circulate: it should recommend a concrete course of action with a specific implementation timeline, and it should engage seriously with the strongest argument for the position you reject rather than simply restating procedural definitions. You need not cite authors or provide a reading list; instead, work through the philosophical tension between being affected by collective rules and being subject to them, and explain how your recommendation resolves the boundary problem in this specific school context.
