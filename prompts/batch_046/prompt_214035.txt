Moore (I think from the early 1900s) and got stuck on his idea that friendship and beauty are both these huge goods, but theyre weird because they depend on people actually being there and feeling them. Its not like a pile of gold. I want an activity that makes us feel that weird tension ourselves, not just talk about it.

Design a paper-and-pen game for 8 people in 75 minutes. Give clear rules, maybe cards representing different goods where friendship and beauty have special rules that break normal scoring. Arrange it so we run into the problem during play - like trying to maximize value and finding the math falls apart for these two. Include a debrief that maps what happened onto Moore's view, and be honest about what part of his idea this cant reproduce. Also, take a clear stance: should we read Moore as saying friendship and beauty cant be ranked at all, or that they need a different kind of measuring? Build the rules around your answer, dont just list both sides. I want a committed design with reasons, and please dont just hand me a reading list or bibliography.
