I'm writing the toast for Dr. Elena Vasquez's retirement dinner next Friday. She taught political philosophy for forty years, but her real work was with regional water boards where voting rights stop at the district line even though seasonal farmworkers and downstream towns live inside the impact zone. She used to call this the presence gap — being inside the boundary of harm but outside the boundary of voice. The room will be split between grad students who know the demos literature and family members who just remember her driving to every council meeting in the rain. I need about five minutes that treats the presence gap as the spine of the toast, not decoration, and that lands for both groups without dumbing it down. Please write it in American English, and keep the tone somewhat casual — dont worry about making it sound like a journal article.
