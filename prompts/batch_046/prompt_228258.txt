The argument strikes me as structurally similar to a worry one might raise in physics: if every macroscopic event is fully determined by microphysical dynamics, do properties like temperature, viscosity, or superconducting order parameters become causally redundant? In physics we resolve this not by denying microphysical closure, but by recognizing effective theories—descriptions that are ontologically dependent yet possess genuine causal-explanatory autonomy at their own scale. I want to know whether mental properties can be defended analogously.
