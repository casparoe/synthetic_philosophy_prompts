Write the full specification—what any adequate theory of societal collapse must be able to deliver—to distinguish genuine societal failure from transformation, regional reorganization, or mere demographic shift. For each desideratum, provide the motivating case or conceptual distinction behind it (for example, separating loss of institutional complexity from population decline, or centralized failure from distributed adaptation). The specification must cover the canonical cases—the Late Bronze Age eastern Mediterranean network, the Classic Maya lowland depopulation, and Easter Island's ecological and demographic contraction—while also being able to classify a novel case: a globally distributed digital governance network that loses two-thirds of its nodes but preserves functional coherence through adaptive rerouting. Mark which requirements are non-negotiable and which are preferences of particular explanatory camps (ecological, political, technological). Identify any subset of desiderata that appears jointly unsatisfiable, especially those pairing deterministic causation with multi-causal resilience. Do not score existing theories against the list; deliver only the criteria, their motivations, and the points where judgment rather than fact must decide.
