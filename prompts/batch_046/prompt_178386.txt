Treat horse as a kind-term with fixed cross-frame identity, and white as a volume-relative diegetic predicate. Then provide the residue report: which logical moves translate cleanly into questions about whether a descriptor binds to its kind across narrative frames; which survive only with distortion, such as the claim that color terms are particular rather than universal; and what disappears entirely, such as the School's concerns about class membership or the dialectical structure of their debates.
