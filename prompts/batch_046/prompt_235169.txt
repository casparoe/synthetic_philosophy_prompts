I want a full specification of the desiderata that any metaethical theory must satisfy if it claims to withstand evolutionary debunking arguments without resorting to an ad hoc defence. List the requirements — the phenomena the theory must explain, the distinctions it must preserve, the competing explanations it must either accommodate or exclude, and the empirical findings it must remain consistent with — and provide the consideration behind each. In particular: the theory must distinguish evolutionary influence on the content of moral beliefs from influence on our justification for them; it must account for cross-cultural convergence on cooperative norms without treating that convergence as either conclusive proof of objectivity or as purely accidental; it must avoid committing the genetic fallacy in a non-circular manner; it must determine whether evolutionary considerations undermine all moral beliefs or only a subset, and if the latter, supply a non-arbitrary criterion for that division that does not merely presuppose the conclusion; it must engage with third-factor explanations — that evolutionary pressures tracked independently existing moral facts rather than constructing them — without begging the question against moral realism; it must remain coherent with empirical work in moral psychology and evolutionary anthropology without misrepresenting either body of research as deterministic; and it must explain why evolutionary debunking lacks comparable force against logical, mathematical, or perceptual beliefs, or identify a relevant disanalogy that blocks the generalisation. For each desideratum, indicate whether it is non-negotiable for the theory to possess any distinctive metaethical standing, or whether it reflects a preference of a particular camp (for example, the demand that the theory preserve the phenomenology of moral authority). Then state which single requirement does the most damage to the leading realist and anti-realist positions currently on offer, and flag any pair of desiderata that you suspect are jointly unsatisfiable in light of current evolutionary biology and the logic of normative explanation. A table is acceptable.
