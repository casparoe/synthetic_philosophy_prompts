hey, i'm putting together a worksheet for a small philosophy group on tranquilism — well, i keep typoing it "tranqulism" — the idea that only the absence of craving and suffering actually matters, and everything else is just noise. i want a taxonomy tree (branching yes/no splits, not a named-label quiz) that sorts positions on this, built from the logical space first.

start with: does joy or meaning have intrinsic value, or is value purely negative?

then split on whether craving and suffering are the same kind of bad, or if craving is a separate poison that makes suffering inevitable.

then ask about neutral awareness — a mind fully awake but feeling nothing, no craving no pain. is that state good, neutral, or just meaningless?

then split on scope: does this apply only to actual sentient beings, or to potential ones too? like, is preventing a suffering being from ever existing a positive good?

each leaf should carry a name if one exists (tranquilist, negative utilitarian, whatever) and one sentence on its weirdest commitment. leave any empty branch unoccupied, label it, and guess why — incoherent, ugly, or just unexplored? keep it around 6 or 7 leaves, no reading lists or citations, just the tree.
