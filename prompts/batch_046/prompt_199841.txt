They have no formal logic but are familiar with cloning and genetic testing. Give me the teaching plan, not the lecture itself. First, lay out the dependency order: which premise must be secured before origin essentialism sounds like a substantive claim rather than a tautology, and why. Fourth, propose one end-of-session question that checks whether they have grasped the modal-necessity claim rather than just memorizing that origins matter. Finally, say explicitly what should be left out and openly acknowledged: no possible-worlds apparatus, no named philosophers, and no reading list or bibliography. Keep it focused and formal.
