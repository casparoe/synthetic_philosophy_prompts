I'm writing a feature on how philosophers of science are trying to salvage inference to the best explanation (IBE) from its usual critics—the bad-lot objection, underdetermination worries, and the charge that explanatory virtues are largely psychological comforts. On this account, traditional virtues like simplicity and unification are at best heuristic proxies for this deeper modal property; the real epistemic work is done by whether the explanation's skeleton survives intervention. I take this to save scientific realism from the charge that it picks "the best of a bad lot" on aesthetic grounds, since structural stability is meant to be empirically tractable rather than merely a preference for neat narratives. But the reconstruction also restricts IBE to domains where we can precisely model counterfactual perturbations, and it seems to risk making every failed invariance explanation away by redrawing the boundary of the "specified range."
