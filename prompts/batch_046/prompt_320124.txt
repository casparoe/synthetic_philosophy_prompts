One camp—call them the Methodological Separatists—says that claims like Atlantis narratives, ancient-astronaut explanations for monumental architecture, and Fomenko's new chronology belong to a genuinely different category from ordinary history, not just because they are false, but because they systematically violate norms of evidence evaluation that real historiography observes. The other camp—call them the Boundary Skeptics—says the line is mostly a spectrum maintained by institutional authority; they point out that mainstream history also uses speculative reconstruction, contested sources, and frameworks that were once fringe. I want to formalize this disagreement as an adversarial collaboration.
