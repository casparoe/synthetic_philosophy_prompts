My opening move concerns a case of recursive artificial gametogenesis. A child is conceived via in vitro fertilization using sperm and egg that were themselves generated in vitro from induced pluripotent stem cells; those stem cells were originally harvested from a somatic donor who never contributed natural gametes. The child asks whether she could have originated from a different stem-cell line, and—if her essential origin includes the donor’s somatic cells—whether that makes her essentially tied to that donor’s body in a way that conflicts with her genetic independence from him. Respond in character, apply your distributed-origin thesis if it aids you, and show precisely where your essentialist commitment draws the modal boundary in this recursive case.
