i need a short memo for wednesday circulation recommending one course: approve with a biannual "political-inscription audit", reject until participatory mapping rebuilds the dataset, or approve with standard anti-bias checks only. defend your pick, treat the vendor's neutrality argument seriously (no strawmen), and include a concrete implementation timeline. also say what principle we'd be adopting with each option — especially what we'd be saying about whether politics can be engineered out. tone should be formal enough to act on but readable for council members who aren't philosophers.
