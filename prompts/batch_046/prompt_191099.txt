Every intermediate node must be phrased as a concrete question that someone could answer without already knowing the interpretive label it leads to. The leaves should be named positions, for example strict civic-republican, moral-puritan, aesthetic-participatory, and inconsistent-romantic, plus at least one logically possible but apparently unoccupied combination that you briefly diagnose as incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored. Flag any pair of leaves your tree cannot really separate, and explain why the distinction collapses. Keep the tree to about six leaves, do not include bibliographies or citations, and avoid biographical lecture; I want the logical space mapped from the inside.
