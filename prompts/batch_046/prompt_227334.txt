I have come to accept a particular reading of Schopenhauer's philosophy of music: that it is not an aesthetic appendix to The World as Will and Representation but its metaphysical keystone. On this view, music alone bypasses the Platonic Ideas and expresses the Will itself directly, without the mediation of representation or the principle of sufficient reason. If this is right, then Schopenhauer's pessimistic thesis about the intellect—that it is always imprisoned in causal and spatial representation—has at least one genuine exception, and the possibility of non-representational access to the thing-in-itself is not merely mystical but experientially confirmed.

Play it straight: what would have to be true for this reading to fail? I want an honest assessment of its exposure.

Specifically: which assumptions about musical experience must hold for music to be genuinely non-representational? Could results in auditory cognition or neuroscience—for instance, that musical perception involves predictive, representational processing of harmonic structure—count decisively against the keystone claim, or only awkwardly? Is there an internal tension between Schopenhauer's claim that the Will lies entirely outside space, time, and causality, and the fact that music is irreducibly temporal and structured by rhythmic and harmonic relations that seem to instantiate formal principles? Would resolving this tension in favor of representation undermine the direct-expression thesis, or can a defender absorb it by distinguishing inner temporal form from the principle of sufficient reason?

What textual or philosophical considerations would show that Schopenhauer himself does not intend music to escape representation? His descriptions of melody as the history of the Will's striving, or his analogies mapping bass to inorganic nature and melody to human consciousness, seem to rely on representational correspondence; do these implicitly collapse the direct-expression account back into an Idea-based aesthetics? If the direct-expression reading collapses, does Schopenhauer's broader metaphysics suffer fatal damage, or can it survive by treating music as merely the highest grade of Idea-objectification? In other words, is the position well insulated, or does it risk everything?

Which of these defeaters would a committed defender of the keystone reading concede as genuinely counting against them, and which would they dismiss as misunderstandings of the distinction between the form of inner sense and the form of the principle of sufficient reason? Rank them from fatal to merely awkward. I am not asking for a bibliography or survey of commentary. I want to know where this interpretation is genuinely exposed—what it predicts that rival readings do not, and whether it has been insulated by qualifications that save it only at the cost of emptiness.
