I have been thinking, without any formal training in philosophy or neuroscience, about whether I could survive having my brain gradually replaced—cluster by cluster over many decades—with synthetic equivalents that perform the same computations. My intuition is that if each replacement preserves functional connections to the remaining biological tissue, and if the transition is slow enough that there is never a single identifiable moment of death, then I should remain the same person, because personal identity seems to depend on continuous psychological flow rather than on retaining the original carbon-based cells. To test this intuition, imagine a specific case: the synthetic clusters run on substrates that process information ten times faster internally, yet are throttled by an interface layer so that externally they stay in perfect synchronization with the biological tissue; still, inside those clusters, subjective time might be compressed. I wonder whether such hidden internal desynchronization undermines my survival, or whether only the externally observable continuity of behavior and memory matters. Given this framing, I want to understand what would genuinely count against the view that gradual replacement preserves personal identity. Which arguments, conceptual distinctions, or thought experiments—perhaps involving phenomenal binding across mismatched temporal scales, or the conditions for maintaining a unified narrative self-model across hybrid biological-synthetic architectures—would demonstrate that I do not survive? Please distinguish between objections that would be decisive and those that would merely be awkward, include what defenders of gradual-replacement survival would themselves concede as damaging, and assess whether the view risks being qualified into unfalsifiability—for instance, by adding a condition that subjective time must remain unified—and whether that insulation is a weakness for a claim about personal survival rather than a strength. I am not asking for citations, authorities, or reading lists; I want the logical structure of the exposure.
