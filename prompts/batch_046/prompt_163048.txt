Write a villanelle in the voice of Oscar after he has been slowly switched from Earth to Twin Earth without noticing—the slow-switch variant of Putnam’s thought experiment. Use the two required refrains for the internalist and externalist descriptions of his mental state: one refrain should express that his thoughts feel exactly the same, that nothing inside has changed; the other should express that the liquid he now calls water is actually XYZ, and that his word means what it means because of the world outside, not because of what is in his head. Let the six tercets stage the gradual realization that internal identity does not guarantee semantic identity. The form should do philosophical work: the refrains must keep returning unchanged, like Oscar’s internal states, even as the surrounding lines shift from Earth-contexts to Twin-Earth contexts; in the final quatrain the two refrains should sit side by side without resolving, leaving open whether Oscar’s concept has changed or stayed the same. Make it accurate enough that someone who knows semantic externalism recognizes the division of linguistic labor and the slow-switch problem, but keep it readable as a poem even without that background. Strict villanelle form, nineteen lines.
