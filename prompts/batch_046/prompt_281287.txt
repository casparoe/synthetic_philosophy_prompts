Write a dialogue between a metaphysician and a pragmatist about Gongsun Long's white-horse arguement from the School of Names. One side thinks the paradox is really about sortal dependency — that horse denotes a kind with essential properties disrupted by adding white — and the other thinks its just a linguistic convention about Chinese classifiers, so treating it as deep ontology is a category mistake. Make them actually respond: the pragmatist should ask why color disrupts horse-kind but not table-kind, and the metaphysician should push back that the pragmatist is importing modern set-theory intuitions that dont map onto Mingjia logic. Neither should be a strawman. I want this committed — take a definite position on which reading survives once you lay out the mapping to modern logic and where it breaks, and defend it with reasons. Keep it in the weeds, not the usual universals-vs-particulars intro summary. There's probably a disanalogy with how we now treat adjectives that undermines one side — say which one, and dont hedge.
