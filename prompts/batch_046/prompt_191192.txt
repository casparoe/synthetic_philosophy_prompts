Write the desiderata that any adequate philosophical account of Wittgensteinian hinge propositions—the background certainties that form the framework within which empirical doubt and inquiry operate—must satisfy, with particular attention to a problem I will call hinge migration: the phenomenon in which a proposition that functions as an unquestioned hinge for one epistemic community or disciplinary practice becomes available for rational doubt, revision, or abandonment in another, without the transition being reducible either to mere empirical falsification or to arbitrary cultural replacement. For each desideratum, provide the motivating case or conceptual pressure behind it (for instance, how hinges relate to rules of grammar versus empirical claims, their resistance to standard evidential defeat, their role in grounding the meaning of specific doubts, and the challenge of comparing hinge frameworks across practices). Explicitly flag any pair of desiderata you suspect are jointly unsatisfiable—especially any tension between requiring that hinges be genuinely non-evidential and requiring that hinge migration be subject to rational assessment rather than purely sociological explanation. Then, briefly, evaluate how well three broad interpretations fare against your list: a strictly linguistic-grammatical reading that treats hinges as rules of language-games; a pragmatic-enculturation reading that treats them as acquired certainties embedded in forms of life; and an epistemic-structural reading that treats them as foundational commitments organizing a conceptual scheme. Keep the primary weight on articulating the criteria and their conflicts; the scoring is secondary.
