Could you argue that, once you allow federated belief structures with split priors, counterfactual mugging stops being an argument for UDT and instead becomes an argument for some internal bargaining protocol? Work out the conceptual mechanics clearly — especially how the XOR blackmail interacts with the split priors and whether any sub-agent has an incentive to defect.
