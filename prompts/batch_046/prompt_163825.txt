She argues the zhi ren lives in a kind of ontological pluralism where self/world and life/death boundaries are constantly dissolving, not merely accepted with equanimity. She also makes this weird claim that ritual mourning is basically social control that forces a bad metaphysics onto the bereaved, and she reads the story where Zhuangzi drums on a tub after his wife dies as exposing that control rather than expressing mystical detachment.
