Half the board thinks this is pure sci-fi fantasy with no ethical urgency; the other half thinks if panpsychism is even remotely live, we might be manufacturing a conscious subject that can't communicate suffering in any human-recognizable form. I need you to act as an expert philosopher of mind with experience drafting institutional memos. Be honest about the strongest argument on the losing side, whether that's the "no-combination" objection or the risk of false negatives on suffering.
