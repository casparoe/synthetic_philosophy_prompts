Design the full thing for me: roles/cards, rules for how the IOUs work and whether they can be redeemed or just accumulate, a payoff scheme using points that convert to something tangible like picking the snack for next week, and a timeline so they start refusing everything then slowly realize sliding what's "sacred" creates surplus for both sides.
