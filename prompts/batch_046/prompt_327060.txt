(2) Every user illusion requires an epistemic proxy: an entity that takes the interface at face value and adjusts behavior accordingly.

(4) But if the inner workspace is purely a functional decoy with no corresponding reality, then the proxy—the subject who takes it at face value—is herself part of the decoy. There is no real entity standing behind or within the interface.
