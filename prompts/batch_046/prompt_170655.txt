Combine these into an overall number. Identify which component your final credence is most sensitive to, and specify what argument or finding would move it by at least 0.2. Do not list bibliographies or cite specific texts by name; work only from the conceptual structure of semantic externalism and its internalist rivals. Conclude by noting what this decomposition reveals about where the real disagreement actually lies.
