the exam question should force students to pick a side: either defend the symmetry (computation has no more internal error-state than evolution does) or defend the asymmetry and price exactly what it costs for the Competence-Without-Comprehension thesis. they need to address whether recognizing these inversions should push us toward eliminativism about intentional explanation in biology and AI, or whether there's a principled reason to retain intentional vocabulary for one domain but not the other.
