Consider the Löbian obstacle to reflective self-trust: an agent that accepts the schema (L) — if this system rationally endorses φ, then φ — and that can derive its own endorsement of φ cannot consistently assign low credence to φ, so any attempt to model its own reasoning collapses into immediate self-endorsement and eliminates the gap required for critical revision.
