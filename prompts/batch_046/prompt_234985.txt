The claim is that even in this one-shot, causally isolated case, the agent has a decisive reason to run the routine and pay—not because of any evidential link to the past rescue, but because rationality evaluates decision procedures globally rather than act-by-act, and a globally trustworthy procedure is worth its local cost.
