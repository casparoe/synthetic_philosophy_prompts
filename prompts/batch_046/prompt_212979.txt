I would like you to write a dialogue between two equally competent interlocutors—Mara and Julian—who are listening together to a slow adagio and debating whether Schopenhauer's metaphysical hierarchy survives once this intermodal binding is taken seriously. Neither speaker should be a strawman, and they should respond to each other's actual moves rather than talk past one another.
