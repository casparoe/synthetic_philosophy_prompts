So I'm stuck between the feeling that history is fragile and the suspicion that we're just narrating backward, breaking the story at a convenient nail and calling it cause.

I'd like a poem that holds that tension open, without preaching about determinism or shrugging with "everything happens for a reason." Please keep it accurate to how nonlinear dynamics actually work—don't treat chaos as pure randomness, and don't pretend the proverb is literally false—just let the ambiguity stand.
