Second, I nevertheless maintain that when a community's account and the archival record conflict over causal responsibility for harm, public institutions should default to the record, because reparative justice depends on getting the causal chain right. Fourth, I reject pluralist approaches that let contradictory accounts stand side by side; I think direct contradiction requires declaring one account mistaken, though I insist this judgment should usually arise from within the community rather than be imposed by outside historians. Fifth, I treat archives as only provisionally privileged—since preservation is shaped by power and omission—yet in practice I grant the record stronger default authority than oral tradition. Please examine these for hidden tensions, distinguish apparent conflicts from real ones, rank the genuine tensions by severity with what each requires giving up, identify the single cheapest revision, and note what this package still cannot resolve. Present your audit as a numbered list of exactly five items, each consisting of one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion.
