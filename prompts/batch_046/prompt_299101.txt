I've bought into Hofstadter's knob-twiddling model of creativity pretty hard -- extended past math and music into moral and political insight. The claim is every "aha" is just sliding dials in a high-dimensional conceptual space (logic weight, emotional salience, social framing, harm-versus-purity sliders, etc.), with no true discontinuities; even ethical breakthroughs are just rebalancing weights, not encountering genuinely new reasons. What would have to be true for this to be wrong? Rank the defeaters for me: fatal, awkward-but-survivable, and merely cosmetic. Which results in cognitive science or patterns in the history of discovery actually threaten it? For instance, if the fMRI "insight spike" reflects a global network reset rather than local parameter tuning, does that kill the view or just describe its neural implementation? What does this model predict that discontinuist rivals don't, and has it already been qualified into unfalsifiability -- like saying any apparent jump is just a hidden dial we haven't named? Is that insulation a bug? I'd especifcally like to know which defeaters a committed defender would concede count against them versus dismiss, and whether there's any observation or argument that should genuinely move me away from it.
