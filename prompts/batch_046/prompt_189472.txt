The speech should argue that the historiographical stalemate persists not because we lack evidence about the Moscow Conference, occupation zones, or currency reform in 1945–48, but because the opposing sides use "started the Cold War" to mean different things: one side means initiatory blame, the other means maintenance blame, and neither notices the ambiguity.
