She applies this to argue that the standard Leibniz-law objection to constitution views collapses once we see that an artifact's design can determine its own material continuity conditions.

Draft eight to ten questions that go well beyond having her restate this view. For each of these three, provide a follow-up keyed to the reply she is most likely to give, in which she appeals to the protocol itself as defining persistence conditions; in the follow-up, press whether this makes persistence conventional or mind-dependent. Avoid generic biographical questions and do not ask for bibliographies, citations, or reading lists.
