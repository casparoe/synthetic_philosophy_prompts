I hold that this undefinedness is not a technical inconvenience to be repaired by continuity restrictions, averaging methods, or appeals to nonstandard analysis—whether through hyperreal-valued expectations, Cesàro summation, or Abel summation—because the fact that these different repair strategies yield incompatible rankings demonstrates that there is no hidden fact of the matter waiting to be discovered. Rather, I take the breakdown to mark a structural boundary of normative rationality itself: when expectation is undefined in this specific divergence structure, comparative evaluation should be suspended altogether, not because we lack information but because the comparison framework has collapsed, and any choice among available resolution principles is necessarily stipulative rather than rationally compelled.
