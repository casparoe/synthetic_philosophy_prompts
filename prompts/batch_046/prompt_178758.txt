I think this moves far too quickly from metaphor to metaphysics. Could you please treat that image as a loose slogan and separate the precise claims it might actually be expressing? I also want you to consider whether any of these readings implicitly commit Machiavelli to a control-based theory of responsibility that would sit uneasily with modern accounts such as Nagel's or Williams's, even if that commitment is not explicit in the text. Present each reading with a brief verdict, and keep the entire response under 400 words.
