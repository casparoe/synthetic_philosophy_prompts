Please argue that Hofstadter's knob-twiddling model of analogical creativity, if applied consistently to scientific theory spaces rather than only to individual mental representations, predicts a dense continuum of viable intermediate frameworks between any two successful paradigms. Then demonstrate that this prediction fails for the transition from classical mechanics to quantum mechanics: there is no conceptually stable intermediate theory that preserves both determinism and locality while accommodating discrete atomic spectra, not because physicists failed to search, but because adjusting the wave-particle parameter structurally disables the local-realism parameter in ways that render the intermediate region incoherent rather than merely unexplored. From this, conclude that high-dimensional concept spaces are better modeled as non-commutative "knob spaces" with frozen dimensions — regions where twisting one dial collapses others — and defend this revision against the objection that it merely saves Hofstadter's intuition by redefining adjacency, by specifying exactly why the absence of intermediates is a structural feature of the space rather than a contingent failure of imagination.
