Half the panel thinks this is a brave step toward plural history; the other half says it makes the past unintelligible and secretly reinstalls the centre it claims to erase. Could you draft the memo I'll send round before Thursday's meeting? Recommend whether we approve, reject, or approve with conditions and a clear timetable, and please take the pluralist case seriously — don't just restate that all history needs a narrative thread.
