Rather than asking students to summarize the literature on higher-order defeat, require them to determine whether the agent should suspend judgment, retain belief with a correction factor, or adopt a precise graded credence, and to explain whether her awareness of the bias functions as a defeater for E itself or only as a reason to discount her first-order assessment. Please provide the exam question, a model answer of approximately 600 words, and a marking scheme built around five must-hit analytical moves with marks out of 20, plus a one-line note on the characteristic failure mode for each.
