I need a teaching plan for the argument—associated with Rietdijk and Putnam—that the relativity of simultaneity undermines a globally privileged present and supports a block-universe ontology. Then list the misconceptions they will form in sequence—e.g., that this concerns only appearance, that accelerating frames restore a privileged slice, or that universal existence of events entails determinism or denies change—and state exactly where in the arc each should be headed off. Include one closing check-question that reveals whether they grasp the distinction between an epistemic limit on knowing the present and a metaphysical absence of one. Finally, be explicit about which feature of the block-universe argument this physical staging necessarily cannot reproduce, leaving that part for direct explanation.
