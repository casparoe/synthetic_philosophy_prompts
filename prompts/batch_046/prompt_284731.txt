Write a short dialogue between a gap theorist and a trace theorist about responsibility for autonomous weapon systems. The gap theorist thinks that when a drone updates its targetting heuristic in the field through federated learning—pulling weights from thousands of nodes with no single author—there's genuinely nobody left to blame if it misclassifies a civilian vehicle. The trace theorist thinks that's just distributed blame, not a gap: someone chose federated architecture, someone set the reward function, and each node contributed data, so accountability just fragments rather than vanishing. Have them actually respond to each other's points, not talk past one another. The gap theorist should argue that "fragmented blame" is useless for victims and for deterrence; the trace theorist should argue that accepting gaps lets engineers off the hook by design. Keep it focused and casual—no bibliographies or citations needed, just the conceptual clash.
