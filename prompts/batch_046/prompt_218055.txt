I was arguing with my friend for like two hours last night about tangled hierarchies and wether there's always got to be an inviolate level outside the loop, like in Escher's Drawing Hands where the hands draw each other but Escher himself is still sitting their drawing the picture. Here are my gut verdicts on cases. The Drawing Hands situation is clearly a loop but not vicious, since there's that outside artist level. A programming language that writes its own compiler feels similar - there's still hardware and original programmers outside, so I think it's okay. Even a dictionary that defines every word including 'dictionary' seems fine to me because there's still the lexicographer and physical world outside the book. But then take a society where laws are made by citizens, and who counts as a citizen is defined entirely by those same laws. I think that's also okay, as long as there are actual people with bodies. But imagine a purely self-creating system where level A is defined entirely by level B and B entirely by A, with no world, no authors, no hardware, nothing outside at all. That feels like a logical glitch, not just weird but impossible. So I accept loops with an outside and reject loops without one.

But here's the tension: Gödel's result shows a formal system can't prove its own consistency if it's strong enough, which sounds like saying there's an inviolate meta-level. Yet I also think mathematicians understand consistency by stepping outside into intuition. So I'm not sure if the 'outside' needs to be physical or just conceptual. Is their one principle that draws the line between benign tangle and vicious circle? Does it depend on an actual causal base, or is any meta-level enough? And if my verdicts conflict - since I said the society loop is okay even though citizens and laws are conceptually intertwined - which should I drop? Also please apply your principle to a case I keep going back and forth on: a neural network that trains itself entirely on its own outputs, running on a server built by engineers. Is that Drawing-Hands-style okay, or heading toward the pure bootstrap glitch?
