Write a short story about a museum curator in a small Balkan town who is catologuing objects from the intermediate state — a legal fiction I just made up where a defeated nation keeps existing in suspended form inside its own borders, not as exiles abroad but as a parallel civic layer. She finds an unifrom from a Dunkirk evacuation unit that was never officially disbanded, plus a letter from a Confederate veteran who settled nearby in the 1890s. The story should ask whether loyalty to a lost cause is about remembering a real past or maintaining a shared hallucination that makes defeat bearable. Don't write a history essay; just show the tension through the objects and one argument with a visiter who thinks the museum ought to be shut.
