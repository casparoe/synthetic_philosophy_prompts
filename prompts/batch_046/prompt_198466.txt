Hey, I've been trying to figure out what I acutally think about Godel's rotating universe and whether it really kills time, and my gut calls are kind of scattered. Here they are.

If Godel's metric describes the acutal universe and you can literally fly in a loop and shake hands with your past self, then I think objective time with a single global 'now' is just dead. That's case one.

But if the universe rotates just barely enough that closed timelike curves exist on paper, but you'd need to go faster than light for ten billion years to actually close one, then I don't think Godel's argument lands at all. Time is still real, it's just weird geometry that doesn't let anyone do it. Case two.

Now here's the weird part: build a perfect computer simulation of the metric, put an agent inside that loops back and talks to its earlier instance. I still say that proves nothing about real time; it's just code repeating values. But then I also think that if our real universe turned out to have that rotation, even if cosmic expansion makes the loops physically unreachable, the global structure still undermines presentism - there's no universal now if every event sits in its own past. So unreachable loops matter in reality but not in simulation, even though both are 'just structure'. Case three and four.

Also, Godel seemed to think that if time travel is mathematically possible in a consistent solution to Einstein's equations, then time must be an illusion, like Kant said. I want to reject that - I think you need physical traversability, not just a line on paper. But I'm also tempted to say that if we discovered the universe rotates but the net effect cancels globally so no CTC structure exists at all, then his point collapses entirely, not just the traversability part. Case five.

So is there one principle here, maybe about 'physical realizability within the actual metric versus representational artifacts'? Or am I just holding contradictory intuitions? Which ones should I drop? And what should I say about the case I keep going back and forth on: a rotating universe where CTCs exist but every path through them scrambles all information, so you loop physically but can never actually send a message back to yourself?
