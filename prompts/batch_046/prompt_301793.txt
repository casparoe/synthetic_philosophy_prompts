I've been working through the philosophical literature on self-deception with a friend who takes a course in cognitive science, and I'm stuck on whether the phenomenon requires a literally contradictory doxastic state. The standard Davidsonian line — that self-deception requires one part of the mind to intentionally deceive another, which makes the self into a committee with a chair — seems right about the homuncular problem, but wrong to conclude that self-deception is therefore impossible. My current position is that self-deception is best modeled not as belief in P and belief in not-P coexisting in one cognitive system, but as a stable failure of global integration: different modular subsystems maintain representations with incompatible contents (say, a low-level predictive model registering threat with high precision-weighting, and a higher-level narrative model suppressing that precision without ever generating an explicit belief that 'there is no threat'). There's no single mental state that is false; there's just a persistent divergence in representational content across levels, producing behavior and affect that look like deception from the outside but involve no internal liar or lied-to agent.

What would count against this modular-divergence account? I don't want a generic list of objections; I want the specific evidence, formal results, or conceptual pressure points that should actually move me. For instance: do empirical studies of anosognosia and confabulation show that modular divergence is always eventually resolved into global coherence, undermining the stability claim? Do formal frameworks for belief revision — non-monotonic logics, hierarchical Bayesian inference — actually permit long-term divergence without contradiction costs, or do they predict that precision mismatches must eventually collapse? Is there an argument that without an explicit false belief at the personal level, we shouldn't call the phenomenon 'deception' at all — and if so, is that a terminological quibble or a substantive loss? Rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, say which ones defenders of the modular view should concede as genuinely damaging, and be honest about whether the view has been qualified into unfalsifiability — and whether that insulation is a theoretical vice or just appropriate modesty. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence, then give the reasoning, with no preamble and no restating of the question.
