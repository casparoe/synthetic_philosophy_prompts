I feel strongly that I shouldn't alter my decision to collect this reward—my reasoning should be updateless with respect to the counterfactual offer. Yet in case one I was willing to be moved by counterfactual correlation with other worlds. I keep going back and forth on whether these are structurally different or just the same reasoning dressed up differently.

Case six, harmful cooperation: all copies face a structurally identical choice, except that contributing means each tortures one innocent person to save a hundred others. Even with perfect logical correlation, I think I should refuse. The evidential arithmetic seems identical to case one, but my verdict is opposite.
