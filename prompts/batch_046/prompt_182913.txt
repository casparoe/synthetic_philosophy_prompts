Write a joke about incommensurable values and hard choices. I want the punchline to actualy turn on the distinction between epistemic difficulty (we just don't know which option is best) and the stronger claim that there's no hidden metric or commensuration procedure waiting to be discovered—so gathering more data can't settle it. Don't do a generic philosphy grad student can't pick a latte bit. Frame it around a concrete hard choice—like whether to stay near family or take a tenure-track job overseas—where the protagonist keeps running analyses hoping a tie-breaker will appear. The joke should land on why that hope is conceptually confused, not just unrealistic. Keep it tight, casual is fine, and make sure it requires knowing the difference between value pluralism and simple indecision.
