Also include one split on whether quantum coherence or decoherence plays any explanatory role in how the separation happens — I think modern readers often smuggle that in, but I'm not sure it holds up — and note which pairs of answers the tree can't actually separate. Keep it to about six nodes, don't ask for citations or page numbers, and make sure each question is something you could answer without already knowing which philosopher it maps to.
