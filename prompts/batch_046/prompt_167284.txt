Yet the very same charter forbids the physician from administering a lethal sedative with the explicit intention of ending life, even when the patient’s suffering, prognosis, and autonomous request are calibrated to be identical by the unit’s palliative-care metrics. The record system flags the first case under “treatment withdrawal” and the second under “intervention with lethal intent,” and the institutional risk-allocation structure distinguishes them categorically, not by outcome, suffering, or patient preference.
