Here is an argument I constructed for a seminar on evolutionary explanations of religion and now find suspicious because its conclusion seems to dissolve the distinction between signaling and contracting. (1) On Zahavi's handicap principle, an honest signal must be differentially costly: low-quality senders cannot bear the expense of producing it. (2) Religious rituals—fasting, pilgrimage, scarification—are precisely such differentially costly displays, which is why they reliably signal group commitment. (3) The credibility of the signal depends on the cost being borne in the display itself, not merely in the punishment for defection; otherwise any contract enforced by penalties would count as a handicap signal. (4) Yet in large-scale religious institutions, individual ritual costs often diminish (brief communal prayer replaces prolonged asceticism), while the cost of apostasy—social exile—remains severe. (5) So large institutions maintain commitment not through display-cost but through defection-cost, which by (3) should not count as a handicap signal at all. (6) To save the principle, one might invoke a distributed handicap: each member bears a small ritual cost, and the aggregate burden is large. (7) But distributed costs violate the differential-burden condition: in a large group, a free-rider's individual share is negligible, so cheating is cheap. (8) Therefore the handicap principle predicts that large religious institutions should be unstable or impossible, yet they dominate human history. (9) So either (3) is false and defection-costs do count—which collapses handicap signaling into ordinary contract enforcement, making the principle trivial—or (1) is false and religious cohesion is explained not by costly display but by shared belief and coordination.

Which step should I reject? I am inclined to defend (3), because I want to preserve the distinctiveness of signaling explanations, but I am uneasy about giving up (8)'s empirical premise. If you think (3) can be saved, show me how without letting legal contracts and marriage vows become handicap signals too. Keep your answer under 300 words. Lead with the single step you reject, named in the first sentence; then explain why it looked plausible and price the two main alternatives.
