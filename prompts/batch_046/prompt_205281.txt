She grants that modern physics describes remarkably stable regularities, but denies that these are grounded in intrinsic causal powers belonging to created substances; instead, she holds that God directly creates every particular event, with the appearance of causal connection being only a customary conjunction (sunna) rather than metaphysical necessity. I want a sequence of interview questions that avoids primer-level exposition and instead presses exactly where the literature presses: each question should anticipate her standard reply and build in the follow-up that makes that reply harder to maintain.

Please structure the sequence as follows. End with a direct question about what empirical finding or conceptual argument could genuinely weaken her view, given that she can always invoke divine freedom to accommodate anomalies, and ask whether that immunity makes occasionalism empirically empty rather than metaphysically robust.
