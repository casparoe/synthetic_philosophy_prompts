(2) I accept Quine's confirmation holism in full: no statement, including logical and mathematical ones, is in principle immune to revision in light of recalcitrant experience.
(3) I maintain that, within any fixed framework, analytic truths are genuinely unrevisable—denying one is not an empirical mistake but a change of subject or framework abandonment.

(5) I reject the idea that the analytic/synthetic boundary is merely a matter of degree—there is a sharp distinction, though which statements fall on the analytic side depends on which framework one has adopted.

Please audit this package. Which pairs are genuinely in tension, which only appear to conflict, and what is the least costly revision for each real tension? Rank the tensions by severity. For each tension, identify the cheapest fix and what it costs me. Finally, tell me which single commitment I should drop or revise if I want to clean up the most problems at once, and whether giving it up forces me into a recognizably Quinean or Carnapian camp.
