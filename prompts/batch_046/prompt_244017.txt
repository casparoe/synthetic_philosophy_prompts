I have no formal training in philosophy, but after working in digital-health product design I have become preoccupied with a tension I see between two kinds of mental-health technology. The other category—exemplified by guided-meditation platforms that ask users to attend directly to interoceptive signals and treat the felt quality of anxiety or calm as authoritative—treats introspective awareness as the ground truth that no external metric can correct. What philosophical commitments about the reliability and authority of introspection does each design presuppose? Can a single coherent view of self-knowledge license both the skeptical calibration of the first and the privileged status granted by the second, or do they depend on incompatible theories about whether we have direct access to our mental states?
