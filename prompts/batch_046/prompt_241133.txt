The dialogue should proceed through genuine back-and-forth, with each respondent addressing the specific claim just made, and it should close without synthetic resolution, leaving the tension intact for seminar analysis. Please compose this in a formal, polished register appropriate to philosophical pedagogy, with sufficient elaboration that the conceptual stakes of indirect structural advantage remain fully visible.
