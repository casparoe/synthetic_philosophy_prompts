Write a short dialogue between a defender of epiphenomenalism and a critic, both arguing over whether the paradox of phenomenal judgment actually kills the view. The defender should propose that we treat conscious reports as "dark predictions" — outputs of a brain module that never actually recieves causal feedback from the qualia it describes — and claim this explains the correlation without causation, like forecasts that match reality for independent reasons. The critic should push back that this just turns the accuracy of our reports into a miracle: if the module is never corrected, why does it systematically track the very experiences it's allegedly blind to? Have them respond directly to each other, especially on whether "dark prediction" is a real explanation or just renaming the mystery. Write it casual, like they're talking in a kitchen, and don't stress about a typo or two.
