You are the procedural-design officer for a transitional authority drafting franchise rules for a new transboundary watershed administration spanning three jurisdictions with permanent residents, seasonal labor migrants, and communities displaced by prior dam construction. The authority has adopted a hybrid democratic-boundary principle: membership in the demos requires either being subject to the body’s binding decisions or being significantly affected by them, but with a lexical priority for subjection, except where an affected party faces irreversible harm and possesses no alternative representational channel. Your task is to operationalize this into a reusable decision procedure that field officers—without philosophical training and operating under a forty-eight-hour ruling deadline—can apply repeatedly.

Write a step-by-step procedure, in checklist or flowchart form, that an officer can run on any individual applicant. The procedure must include explicit operational tests for: (i) being subject to binding decisions; (ii) being significantly affected; (iii) facing irreversible harm; and (iv) lacking alternative representation. For each test, specify the evidence the officer should gather, the threshold that must be met, and the point at which judgment cannot be eliminated—marking those points clearly and stating who must sign off (the officer alone, a legal panel, or the full assembly).

Apply the procedure to three worked cases: (1) a permanent resident farmer within the watershed who is taxed and regulated by the body; (2) a seasonal agricultural worker who lives just downstream, is neither taxed nor regulated by the body, but whose livelihood depends on water allocations the body controls; and (3) a descendant community living outside the watershed whose ancestral burial grounds will be inundated by a reservoir the body approves, and who are already represented in one of the three constituent national legislatures. Case (3) must produce an answer the applicant will reject; show that result explicitly and note whether the procedure treats the prior national representation as sufficient to block inclusion.

Conclude by identifying two categories of applicant that the procedure is structurally unable to settle without importing normative premises that the hybrid principle itself does not supply, and state what those missing premises are. Do not ask for bibliographies, citations, or reading lists; ground the distinctions in the concepts themselves.
