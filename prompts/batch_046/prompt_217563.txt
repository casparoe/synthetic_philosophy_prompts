As a novelist developing a narrative about a floating archipelago-state that must constitute its own polity, I ask you to write an essay on the boundary problem—specifically, who belongs to the demos. Weigh the principal arguments for defining democratic membership by territorial presence, by subjection to coercive law, and by sustained participatory stake, then reach and justify a definite conclusion rather than ending in ambiguity. Frame your reasoning through the analogy of narrative agency: determine whether being part of the demos requires being an active character in the collective story, or whether mere affected presence within the setting is sufficient. Argue from first principles without providing bibliographies, citations, or reading lists.
