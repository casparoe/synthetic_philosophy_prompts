Write a short dialogue between two AI alignment researchers, call them A and B. B thinks they fall apart for what she calls "recursive oracle counterfactuals" — cases where the system has to price sentences about worlds in which its own reasoning algorithm is swapped out for an uncomputable oracle, which she says breaks convergence because the market cant access the logical truths that depend on that oracle. A should reply that the market just approximates these via a universal prior or treats the oracle as a black-box trader, not that B misses the mechanism. B should push back that this smuggles evidential reasoning in through the back door and collapses the logical/empirical distinction. Keep both sides sharp — no strawmen — and make them actually respond to each other. Write casual, a typo or two is okay, dont ask for citations or reading lists. End with a fixed line: Verdict: [who had the stronger point] inside \answer{}.
