It seems to me that if being wrong is acceptable provided the output works, there ought to be an entire hidden bureaucracy that manufactures, licenses, and balances these errors. I would like you to write a deadpan satirical FAQ of approximately 600 words for a fictional government agency called the Office of Counterfactual Calibration, which issues official permits for unrealistic assumptions in public-policy forecasting models. The voice must remain entirely serious and institutional, so that a reader recognizes the absurdity only through the accumulation of procedural detail.
