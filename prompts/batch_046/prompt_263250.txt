write the procedure for them: fix the role (peer reviewer, 15 mins, no philosophy background), give a word-flowchart with ordered steps — check consistency with old rules, check for term-stretching, check if the meta-rule was implicit all along, check whether the outside view is actually mapped inside a larger box — and apply it to three cases. include one where the answer will dissapoint the asker: a model refusing harmful instructions by citing its own system prompt (procedure should flag this as likely not a real jump). include one genuinely ambiguous case where the checklist dead-ends and the reviewer must make a judgment call. flag every point where human judgment cant be eliminated, especially the risk that the evaluator is still inside their own assessment rubric. keep it practical, no bibliography or reading lists, and write casual — you can leave a typo or two.
