last week it generated a genuinely novel objection to modal realism that sounds exactly like him but that he definatly never wrote down. now the board wants to display that answer as part of his final word on the subject. some relatives say its just a fancy recording, like playing old tapes, so theres no harm. but others feel like were attributing a real mental act to him—an original stance he never actually took—when the system is really just rearranging symbols that only carry meaning because we assigned them.

i keep circling back to that old philosophy-of-mind worry: is this thing more like a dictionary that carries thoughts only derivatively, or are we treating a simple symbol-shuffler as if it had its own intentional stance? and if it produces an argument he never formed, can we meaningfully call it his view, or is that just a kind of metaphysical plagiarism?

if im mixing up practical ethics with metaphysics here, or if the whole premise that theres a wrong is confused, please just say so plainly—id rather be told the framing is broken than get a polite answer that plays along.
