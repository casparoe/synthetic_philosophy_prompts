I am applying for a one-semester dissertation prospectus in philosophy of religion. My project concerns Wittgenstein’s account of religious belief, but I want to avoid the well-trodden debate about whether such belief is cognitive or non-cognitive. Instead, I propose to test his framework against a class of practices I call secular-ritual hybrids: structured, repetitive exercises—such as non-theistic gratitude protocols or decontextualised mindfulness routines—that borrow the grammatical form of religious commitment (they regulate conduct, invoke a picture of human flourishing, and resist purely evidential justification) yet lack any supernatural ontology and are not embedded in a traditional form of life.

The central question is precise: do these hybrids reveal that Wittgenstein’s distinction between genuine religious belief and mere superstition depends on a hidden social condition—specifically, communal exposure to risk or vulnerability—that he never makes explicit? Or do they show instead that his framework is over-restrictive, excluding practices that genuinely function as belief?

Write the proposal. It should state the question clearly; explain why existing interpretations—particularly the language-game reading and the picture-theory of belief—leave the status of these hybrids unsettled; describe the method (conceptual analysis of Wittgenstein’s remarks on belief and superstition, applied to three concrete cases of secular-ritual practice); specify what a successful outcome looks like (identifying the missing condition, or demonstrating that the framework must be expanded in a specific direction); specify what failure looks like (the hybrids turn out to be either clearly belief or clearly not, leaving Wittgenstein’s taxonomy untouched); and give an honest assessment of whether the project is feasible within one semester. Do not include a bibliography, reading list, or citation requests; references will be added separately. The proposal should take a committed stance—arguing that the framework either survives with a specific revision or collapses in a definite way—rather than merely surveying options. Please write in British English.
