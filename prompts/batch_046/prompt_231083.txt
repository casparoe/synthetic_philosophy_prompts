While preparing a presentation for a philosophy of mind seminar on scepticism and the self, I have settled on a position drawn from the Zhuangzi butterfly dream but deliberately narrowed in scope. I hold that there is no non-circular, purely phenomenological marker that distinguishes waking consciousness from dreaming with ontological authority: the coherence, vividness, and emotional weight of a dream can match or exceed those of waking life, and appeals to consistency or intersubjective agreement already presuppose the very waking-state privilege they are meant to establish. From this I do not draw global Cartesian doubt—external objects and other minds remain untouched—but rather a targeted suspension of diachronic personal identity. The self, on my view, should be treated as a state-relative narrative consolidation: genuinely real within its own experiential frame, yet carrying no privileged metaphysical continuity across the sleep-wake boundary. I allow that waking life exhibits greater intersubjective stability, but I deny that this stability translates into ontological superiority for the self that persists through it.

What would have to be true for this position to fail? I want an honest map of its exposure. Which specific observations, phenomenological reports, or results in cognitive science or the metaphysics of persistence would count as decisive defeaters rather than merely awkward accommodations? Would evidence that dreamers maintain a continuous implicit body-schema, or that narrative identity is neurologically anchored in structures that persist through sleep, be fatal, or could my view absorb them by distinguishing neural continuity from metaphysical selfhood? I am particularly interested in whether the position risks being insulated into unfalsifiability—if every apparent continuity can be redescribed as narrative consolidation within a state—and whether that insulation is a theoretical virtue or a vice for a view of this kind. Please distinguish what defenders of this Zhuangzian stance would themselves concede as damaging from what only critics would press, rank the defeaters from fatal to merely inconvenient, and write your answer in British English.
