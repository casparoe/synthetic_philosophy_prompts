I cover the intersection of regulatory philosophy and emerging technology, and I am tracking a conceptual body—the Commission on Expression and Public Reason—whose published rulings apply John Stuart Mill’s harm principle to modern communicative acts. Their verdicts so far are these.

They restricted a protest in which demonstrators burned an effigy of a named public-health official outside her residence and chanted accusations of murder, ruling that targeted intimidation rendering a private environment unlivable crosses the other-regarding line even without physical violence.

They protected a peer-reviewed journal’s publication of data linking a common food additive to carcinogenic risk, despite economic ruin for producers and widespread public panic, ruling that indirect social and economic consequences do not by themselves constitute harm in Mill’s sense.

They restricted a virtual-reality firm’s adaptive persuasion module that read users’ biometric feedback to tailor undisclosed arguments for unnecessary medical purchases, ruling that hidden manipulation of individual decision-architecture violates the conditions for autonomous consent.

They protected a university invitation to a philosopher arguing that moral responsibility is illusory and should be abandoned, despite reports of severe existential distress among attendees, ruling that distress arising from engagement with an idea differs from harm produced by coercion or deception.

The Commission now must rule on a proposed neuro-symbolic deliberation system: a generative network that produces individualized, factually accurate policy briefs optimized to each citizen’s cognitive profile. The system targets no specific person, advocates no violence, and contains no falsehoods. Its designers intend it to shift collective normative frameworks on climate-adaptation policy by systematically suppressing counter-arguments within each user’s personalized information environment, producing measurable degradation in democratic deliberation quality at the population level without harming any identifiable individual.
