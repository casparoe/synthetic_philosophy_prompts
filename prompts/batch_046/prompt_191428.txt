I've been noticing this thing with people who use AI to learn philosophy. They ask for an explanation of, say, Kant's categorical imperative with concrete examples, and then they can reproduce those examples and defend them pretty well. But if you present a moral dilemma that wasn't covered in the AI's response — something that should fall under the same principle — they don't recognize it and sometimes argue it falls outside. It's like they have a thin ribbon of understanding that traces exactly the path the AI generated, with darkness on both sides. Is there an established name for this? I know about epistemic dependance on AI and outsourcing cognition, but this feels more specific: it's not general ignorance, it's understanding that is contoured to the particular explanation recieved. If there's no term, how would you distinguish it from ordinary shallow learning or from just being a beginner?
