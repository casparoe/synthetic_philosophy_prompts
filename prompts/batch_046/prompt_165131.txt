I sit on our city's Governance Innovation Board, and we gotta make a call by March on this pilot program. The idea is to replace part of our planning commision with a rotating citizens assembly that has binding say on three things: emergency penalties against developers (with a 24-hour reversal window), removal of appointed officials by popular vote, and capital projects over 5 million. Some board members think this is real democracy fixing a broken system; others say it's just asking for the same messes that occured in ancient athens. I've been reading about those three failures on my own - the mytilene vote where they decided to kill all the men then flipped the next day, the sicily invasion that the assembly got talked into even though it was a disaster, and socrates getting tried by a jury that was whipped up by politicians. I need a memo that actually uses those three episodes to analyze our pilot specifically - what could go wrong with our reversal window, our official removal vote, and our big project approvals. The memo should recommend approve, reject, or approve with conditions, and it needs to deal honestly with the best arguement from the people who want this pilot. Write it like a real decision document with a clear bottom line and maybe a timeline if we go with conditions. Don't worry about citations or reading lists - I'm not an academic, just someone who reads history on weekends, and the other board members don't have phd's either. Just make it practical and serious.
