She uses this to argue that Germany's Länder blocking federal education reform is consistent with the model, while Switzerland's cantons failing to coordinate healthcare nationally is the kind of failure that should trigger upward move; she also claims the US Commerce Clause is really a downward grab disguised as coordination, and that the EU principle of conferral has been misread as top-down delegation when it's actually an upward ratification mechanism.

Write me 6 to 8 interview questions that don't just have her restate the view. One should probe normative bite: does her conditional-retention view make secession easier, since authority never truly leaves the lower level, or harder? And one final question should ask what kind of empirical or institutional evidence could actually falsify her account — for instance, a case where authority migrated upward without local consent but the system still functioned well.

Keep them conversational but sharp, no generic "what drew you to this" stuff.
