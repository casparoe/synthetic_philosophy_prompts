The exchange must contain real back-and-forth: Leo should respond specifically to Mara's point about synonymous codons rather than ignoring it, and Mara should address Leo's challenge about the absence of normative interpretation rather than repeating her opening claim. Both characters should be equally competent, with no strawman positions, and the tone should be polished and suitable for an advanced high school discussion. Neither character should cite specific texts, request bibliographies, or refer to obscure theoretical frameworks by name.
