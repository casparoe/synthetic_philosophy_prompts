My roomate summed up Descartes cogito like this: Descartes tries to find something he cant doubt. He notices that whenever he thinks I dont exist, hes already thinking, which some philosophers call a performative contradiction, so he knows theres at least some thinking going on. From this he infers I think therefore I am — but this is actually an existantial quantifier fallacy, since some thinking exists doesnt mean I as a specific substance exist. Still, Descartes thinks hes proved hes a thinking substance with no bodily properties, and then he uses that to build his whole system. I think there are real objections mixed in with misreadings.
