i keep seeing this thing in Mary's room debates and i don't know if there's a term for it. Mary gets direct brain stimulation that gives her the exact red-experience without her eyes—she feels what it's like, but the causal path is totally engineered. Some folks say she hasn't learned a new fact, just triggered an old physical story in an unusual mode; others say the weird pathway makes it genuinely new knowledge about consciousness. But then those same people turn around and say that finally understanding a proof you've struggled with for months isn't real discovery either—just old axioms rearranged in your head. So they deploy this same-fact-different-access move both to defend physicalism about Mary and to downplay mathematical insight, but they never explain why consciousness deserves the move and math doesn't, or vice versa. Is there an established concept for this kind of cross-domain selective use of the access-mode reply? If not, how do you actually draw the boundary without it being special pleading? And does the artificial stimulation case change anything, or is that a red herring?
