So is the disjunctivist forced to deny that there is any legitimate sense in which the two experiences are the same for the subject? Please take a definite position—either defend disjunctivism by showing how it can accommodate indistinguishability without conceding a shared phenomenal core, or argue that the argument from illusion succeeds because disjunctivism cannot account for error recognition without covert representationalism.
