I want to audit my own commitments on Nozick's entitlement theory, particularly in light of the Wilt Chamberlain argument. Here is my profile: I accept that the Chamberlain scenario refutes pattern-based principles of justice; I hold that historical entitlement retains normative force across generations even when original acquisitions were unjust, provided the current distribution is stable and widely accepted; I deny any principled distinction between voluntary market exchange and voluntary gift-giving for the transmission of holdings; I maintain that a society's holdings can be just overall even if the characters of its members are systematically unjust; and I interpret the Lockean proviso not as a constraint on initial acquisition but as a minimal welfare floor compatible with a minimal state. Examine this set: which pairs are genuinely in tension, which only appear to conflict, and how severe is each tension? For each real conflict, identify the cheapest revision and what it costs; then say which single change would resolve the most tension while leaving the view recognizably Nozickian.
