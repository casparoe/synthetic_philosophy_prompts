Write an essay on the paradox of tragedy — why we seek out art that makes us sad. Dont just rehearse Aristotles catharsis or the usual conversion story. Instead compare 'affective mirroring' (the idea that we enjoy sadness as emotional practice inside a safe frame) with 'moral witness' (the idea that the pleasure comes from recognizing suffering as meaningful rather than escaping it). Reach a conclusion: is tragedy-pleasure actually a form of moral education, or is it closer to emotional tourism? Make sure you tackle the hard case — genuinely devastating works like King Lear that leave us shaken — and show why the safe-frame account cant survive that pressure without stretching 'safety' into uselessness.
