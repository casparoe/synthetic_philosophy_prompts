Draft my Q&A prep sheet: the objections they will actually raise, in the order they are most likely to come, each phrased in the vocabulary and commitments of the objector; for each, state what I should concede immediately, what I must hold, the two-minute reply, and where that reply is weakest; and include the objection I most hope is not raised—namely, that my reconstructive view collapses into either Malcolm's eliminativism or Dennett's anti-realism once the mechanism is fully specified. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations; I need a speaking prep sheet, not a survey of the literature.
