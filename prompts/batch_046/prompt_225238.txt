what do you reckon rousseau'd make of twitch and parasocial theatre? he loathed the stage for breeding false sentiment and splitting the spectator from the actor, but on twitch the audience chats back and the performer never exits. do you think he'd see this collapse of roles as the ultimate corruption of the general will, or weirdly nearer to his public festival where everyone partcipates? i want more than 'he'd hate tech' — use his actual distinction between active and passive prescence, and say whether that survives when the curtain never falls.
