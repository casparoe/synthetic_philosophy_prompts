I am trying to decide whether to become the primary long-term caregiver for a close relative who has rapidly progressing dementia, knowing the commitment will likely span years and will fundamentally reshape my daily routines, relationships, and sense of purpose. Well-meaning friends advise me to treat it like any other major life choice: list the emotional costs and benefits, apply my current values, and choose accordingly. How should I think about deciding in a case where my own future values and subjective outlook are themselves part of what is at stake? Please answer in American English.
