Later people like Deutsch and Wallace tried to fix this with a decision-theoretic proof, but that argument is circular: it assumes you can assign rational betting odds in a world where every bet pays out somewhere, so it smuggles in probabilities to prove probabilities. All of this means MWI can't actually reproduce quantum statistics without secretly bringing collapse back in, so the interpretation is basically dead."

How fair is this? Go through it claim by claim and tell me what's accurate, what's subtly wrong, and what's just overstated.
