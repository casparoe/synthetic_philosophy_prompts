(1) i'm basically a free-will skeptic in Caruso's sense - no libertarian free will, determinism is probably true at the macro level, and basic desert (the idea that people intrinsically deserve suffering for wronging) is a myth we should ditch. (2) but i also think Dennett's story about elbow room captures something real - there's a meaningful gradient of reasons-responsiveness and moral competence, not just a binary switch, and agents higher on that gradient are genuinely different from those near zero. (3) so i reject retributivism: punishment should be justified purely by foward-looking goals - containment, deterrence, rehab, basically the quarantine model. (4) still, i think it's not just inefficient but actually unfair to punish someone who totally lacks moral competence (say, severe psychosis) - even if locking them up protects society, there's a fairness constraint being violated that goes beyond bad strategy. (5) i also think proportionality is a hard constraint - worse crimes should get harsher sanctions - but i want to say this tracks a kind of Schelling-point stability in social coordination, not any intrinsic moral fact about what offenders deserve, maybe as part of maintaining cooperative equilibirum. (6) and i think evolutionary debunking undermines our retributive intuitions but doesn't touch proportionality, since the latter might be tracking cooperative equilibrium rather than desert.

do (1) and (2) actually conflict, or is that tension only apparent because i'm misreading Dennett's compatibilism? between (3) and (4), am i sneaking desert back in through the unfairness language? is (5) compatible with pure forward-looking justification, or does it collapse into hidden retributivism? and does (6) hold up - can i selectively debunk one intuition but not the other without special pleading? rank the real tensions by severity, say which commitment is cheapest to revise, and tell me if there's a single fix that cleans up the most. i'm especially unsure whether fairness-to-the-incompetent can be reconstructed in purely forward-looking terms without losing its normative force, or if i just need to bite the bullet and drop (4).
