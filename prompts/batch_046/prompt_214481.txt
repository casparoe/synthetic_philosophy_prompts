I want the remarks to take up the problem of dirty hands in politics, but specifically in the form it takes for people in my position—not the dramatic case of a leader authorizing harm to prevent catastrophe, but the recurring, quiet situation of maintaining or incrementally patching a system you believe is structurally unfair (for example, a benefits-disbursement algorithm whose eligibility rules are clearly miscalibrated), because shutting it down to rebuild it "cleanly" would immediately cut off payments to vulnerable people who depend on its current functioning.
