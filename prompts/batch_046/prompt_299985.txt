In a graduate seminar on seventeenth-century rationalism, I have been tracking a defensive pattern that recurs whenever Leibniz's thesis that this world is the best of all possible worlds is pressed with concrete counterexamples. The structure unfolds in layers. First, a critic points to a specific imperfection—say, an apparently gratuitous suffering or an inefficient feature of natural law—and asks why a perfectly rational and benevolent creator would include it. The defender answers with local harmony: excising that feature would disrupt adjacent goods or require altering laws that yield far greater benefits elsewhere. If the critic then constructs a hypothetical alteration that preserves those adjacent goods and laws, merely removing the targeted flaw, the defender shifts ground to a second layer, invoking metaphysical compossibility: the proposed state is incompatible with the essential properties of actual individuals or with the simplicity and uniformity governing divine legislation. Should the critic navigate that barrier too—designing a state that respects both the laws and the essential structures—the defender may invoke a third layer, such as narrative coherence across a salvation history, or the claim that any genuinely different world is not a modified version of ours but an altogether different world, and therefore not a valid comparator.

What interests me is the architecture rather than any individual reply: a stack of individually plausible filters—local harmony, law-economy, metaphysical compossibility, narrative identity—each introduced reactively only when the prior barrier is breached, with no explicit hierarchy or unified metric established in advance. Is there an established term in philosophy of religion, or in the methodology of theory defense, that captures precisely this kind of layered, expandable protection structure? How should one distinguish it from a single ad hoc auxiliary hypothesis, from a Lakatosian protective belt, and from the legitimate refinement of a theoretical framework in response to anomalies? If no specific name exists, please propose one together with clear boundary conditions—especially criteria that separate reactive, preservative stacking from genuine theoretical development. Please present your answer as a numbered list of exactly five items, each one or two sentences long, with no introduction or conclusion.
