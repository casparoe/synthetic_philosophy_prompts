She holds that downward causation from symbol to substrate is a descriptive illusion produced by indexing correlations within the careenium, and she applies this to computation, language, and mental representation. Draft ten interview questions that avoid asking her to restate the view and instead press where the literature on downward causation and representationalism typically presses. For each, include a follow-up keyed to her most likely reply, especially her tendency to say that attributing causal efficacy to symbols is a category mistake rather than an empirical claim.
