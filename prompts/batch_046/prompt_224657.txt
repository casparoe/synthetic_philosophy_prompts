Here, people learn just fine - they pull away eventually - but the emotional alarm never sounds. So is there an established name for this specific occurence, where the sensory map and cognitive recognition stay intact but the evaluative "this is awful" signal is just missing? Does this kind of case actually challenge the idea that pain is intrinsically bad, or is there a standard philosophical reply?
