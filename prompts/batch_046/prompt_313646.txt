Where two leaves cannot be cleanly distinguished, note that ambiguity rather than forcing a false split. For each leaf, state the one awkward commitment that must be swallowed whole if one occupies that branch. The deliverable is a classification instrument, not an encyclopedia entry, so no bibliographies, reading lists, or citations are required.
