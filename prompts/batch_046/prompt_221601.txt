Consider the following consistent track record of verdicts given by a philosopher working in this tradition:

(4) A student who, over a decade, deeply internalizes a deceased mentor's distinctive reasoning style and value structure, is judged to host a real, non-metaphorical partial persistence of the mentor's self, comparable in kind though not in degree to the split-brain case.

(5) A genetic clone raised in identical conditions but with no direct interaction or pattern transmission is judged to share no persistence of the original self; structural similarity without causal transmission is insufficient.
