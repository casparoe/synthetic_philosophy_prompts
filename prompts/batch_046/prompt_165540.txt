Please provide a clear ordinal ranking (1 through 4, with 1 being the most radical inversion) and, in a sentence or two for each, justify why it occupies that position relative to the others, paying particular attention to whether the absence of comprehension is constitutive of the competence or merely contingent upon it. Additionally, please state explicitly how confident you are in the overall ordering, and identify the specific comparative judgment—between any two adjacent ranks—about which you are least certain and why.
