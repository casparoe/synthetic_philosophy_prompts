After the DNA result, there's a sharper framing: Jefferson is seen as both an architect of liberty and a direct beneficiary of racialized sexual exploitation, and historians ask whether his philosophy of equality was structurally compatible with the plantation system he actually ran.

Can you analyze this shift through the lens of presentism?
