The regress problem debate — foundationalism, coherantism, infinitism — has been running since Agrippa and every textbook just loops through the same three moves. I keep thinking the stall isn't about belief at all, but about what job "justification" is supposed to do: foundationalists want a stopping-rule for inquiry, coherentists want global consistency, infinitists want to avoid arbitrariness. So is this actually a verbal disagreement, a hidden normative clash, or people answering different questions under one word? Diagnose it with real engagement — is there a substantive disagreement about belief structure hiding in there? — and tell me honestly whether anything, like a sharp distinction or formal result, could actually break the deadlock, or if we should just admit it's a framing issue and stop pretending it's one debate.
