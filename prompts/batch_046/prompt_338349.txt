I've been arguing with my roommate about whether religion can be fully explained away without leaving something important behind. I'm putting together a taxonomy for a small philosophy blog I run — not just "pick your favorite theorist," but an actual decision tree that sorts people by answers to concrete cases. I want it built from the logical space first, then the named positions dropped in as leaves, with empty combos noted and briefly diagnosed.

Start everyone at the commitment: "religious phenomena are fully natural — no supernatural causes needed." Then branch on three diagnostic questions that should seperate views without using theory-labels:

1. If someone reports feeling a presence in an empty chapel, is that best read as a misfiring of agency-detection mechanisms (like seeing faces in clouds), or as a meaningful response to a real but non-divine feature of the setting?
2. If religious practice vanished tomorrow, would societies mainly suffer from lost cooperation and signaling, mainly gain from shedding false beliefs, or stay about the same?
3. Is religion's long persistence best explained because it gives fitness benefits, because it's a spandrel riding on other capacities, or because it's structurally coupled to language and ritual in ways that make it hard to disentangle — what I'm calling a "lateral" rather than vertical explanation?

For every combination of answers, assign a leaf: include at least the hard cognitive-misfire view, a group-level adaptationist view, a cultural-parasite or meme-style view, a phenomenological naturalism that refuses full reduction, and that "lateral coupling" view I just mentioned. Note any empty slots in the grid — is the gap incoherent, unattractive, or just unexplored? Also flag pairs your tree can't really seperate, like whether weak adaptationism and the cultural-parasite view collapse onto the same answers here.

Please say what kind of argument or finding would make you move one of these views to a different branch, and be honest about where your reasoning is shakiest — probably around whether "lateral coupling" is actually distinct from spandrel talk or just a relabeling.
