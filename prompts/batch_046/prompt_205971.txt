I've been teaching Bostrom's singleton idea for years and I've come around to thinking it's definatly inevitable: any superintelligence with a decisive strategic advantage will absorb or neutralize rivals until there's exactly one decision-making center at the top. But I want to stress-test this. What would actually count against it? Not just the standard multipolar story — I want the objections that should make me update, including whether the very category of 'one agent' collapses if high-level cognition is always distributed across competing subsystems, or if strategic consolidation hits hard limits analogus to ecological carrying capacity or regulatory fragmentation. Rank the defeaters from fatal to awkward. And tell me straight: has the view been qualified into unfalsifiability, and if so, is that a problem for a thesis like this?
