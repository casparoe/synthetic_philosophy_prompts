He used this to argue that skeptical arguments confuse an inability to verify the substrate with a reason to deny it, and that we should adopt the same pragmatic stance toward our experienced world.

I want to audit how far this containerized-process analogy holds when mapped carefully onto the philosophical brain-in-a-vat argument.
