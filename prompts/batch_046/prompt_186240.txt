I've been working through a metaphysics seminar on parts and wholes, and I keep returning to a hybrid view I haven't seen defended in exactly this form—call it causal-density gradualism about special composition. On this account, whether some plurality of objects composes a genuine whole is not an all-or-nothing affair but a matter of degree: the stronger and more densely interconnected the causal-functional interactions among the parts, the greater the degree to which they compose a unified object. Crucially, I want to deny that this introduces ontological vagueness; instead, the underlying compositional relation is fully determinate, but our epistemic access to the precise interaction-density thresholds is limited in borderline cases—think of a slowly disintegrating coral colony versus a loose scatter of rocks on a beach. I take this to steer between the extravagance of unrestricted universalism and the arbitrariness of sharp-cutoff organicism, since there is no mysterious line, only a gradient we approximate with practical and theoretical tools.

Given that framing, I want an honest map of what would genuinely count against this position. I am not asking for the standard objections about vagueness or about multiplying entities; I want the specific defeaters—empirical, conceptual, or formal—that should actually move me. Which observed features of physical or biological systems, or which patterns in our object-tracking practices, would undermine the claim that integration density tracks composition? Is there a result in formal mereology, or a case drawn from social ontology—say, whether a slowly replacing institutional body or a geographically dispersed corporation should count as one thing—that exposes the view to collapse into either universalism or nihilism? Please rank these potential defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, noting which ones a committed causal-density gradualist could consistently absorb and which ones they would have to concede. Finally, assess whether the move to "determinate-but-epistemically-indeterminate" insulates the view into unfalsifiability, and whether that insulation is a vice for a metaphysical claim of this kind.
