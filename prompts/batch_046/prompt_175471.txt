Pain asymbolia is when people can feel damage and describe exactly where it hurts and how intense it is, but they dont actually suffer or try to avoid it. Its not the same as congenital insensitivity, where you cant feel anything at all. This shows that the bad part of pain is the emotional reaction, not the raw sensation itself.

So hedonism, which counts well-being as pleasure minus pain, is in trouble. If someone has the full pain-sensation but no drop in well-being, then hedonism is wrong to treat all pain-feelings as automatically bad. This is actually similar to Nozicks experience machine: in both cases, the mental state is separated from whats really happening outside. The machine gives you fake pleasure without real life, and asymbolia gives you real damage without the mental suffering, so together they prove that real-world contact is what makes an experience count for well-being.

Also, since people with asymbolia dont desire the pain to stop, that shows desire-fulfillment theories fail too, because you can be injured and still have all your relevant desires satisfied.
