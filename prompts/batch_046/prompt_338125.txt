I sit on the Clinical AI Ethics Committee of a tertiary-care hospital. By Thursday we must vote on whether to authorize a six-month pilot of an ICU triage decision-support system that encodes ethical reasoning through a two-layer architecture: a base layer applies a weighted utilitarian calculus, and a second “counterfactual veto module” blocks any allocation that would violate the dignity-preservation constraint of even one patient, where dignity is defined not as autonomy but as non-substitutability within that patient’s network of care relations. Proponents argue this prevents treating patients as interchangeable utility-bearers; opponents argue it will cause allocation paralysis and covertly favor patients with larger social networks. Please draft the committee’s formal written opinion recommending approval, rejection, or approval with specific conditions and a concrete timeline. The opinion should take a committed position with explicit reasoning that the developers could actually respond to, and it should treat the strongest objection from the losing side honestly rather than dismissing it.
