ok so i've been chewing on parfit's hitchiker case — you know, promise the driver you'll pay after he drops you off, then once you're out you've got zero reason to actually pay, but you wanted to be the kind of person who'd pay so you'd commit beforehand. classic rational irrationality. there's a bunch of open directions people kick around: is this really about personal identity splitting (the pre-commitment self vs the post-dropoff self), is it just a fancy intertemporal prisoner's dilemma with yourself, and does it tell us anything about real-world commitment devices (like freezing your cards or stickk-style contracts).
