I want you to restate this position in the vocabulary of denotational semantics and fixed-point theory: treat the "I" as a fixed point of a representation functor, the hierarchical levels as iterations of a reflective type constructor, and the "center of gravity" either as a least fixed point in a domain-theoretic model or as an emergent invariant of the recursive system. Be precise about which formal objects correspond to which parts of Hofstadter's metaphor, and do not smuggle in phenomenological vocabulary where it lacks a formal counterpart.

Identify what, if anything, has no expression at all in this vocabulary—particularly whether the translation collapses the strange loop into an ordinary recursive definition, and whether that collapse means the mathematics has dissolved a pseudo-problem or instead stripped away precisely what made the account philosophically interesting.
