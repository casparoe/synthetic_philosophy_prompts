If they move now, kid A gets born in 2026 with asthma but lands in a great program. If they wait a year, kid B gets born in 2027 with clear lungs but misses the program entirely. The usual argument is that kid A cant complain about the asthma, since no move means no kid A. But i started wondering about what i call the "missing slot" problem—like, is there some abstract place in the world that gets filled by whoever actually gets born, and does that mean kid B is somehow "displaced" rather than just never existing? That probably sounds confused, but i tried to argue that personal identity isnt just a bundle of traits but more like a relational story: who you are depends on the actual parents and the actual moment of conception, not on some ghost-potential that got erased. I also threw in a comparison to git branching, where you merge one branch and the other just vanishes with no trace—maybe thats a bad analogy, but it helps me see why the "other child" isnt hiding in some parallel timeline.
