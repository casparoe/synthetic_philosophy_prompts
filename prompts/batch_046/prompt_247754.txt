If Zhuangzi was alive today and walked into an ICU where doctors declare brain death the end even though the heart still pumps on machines, what do you think he would actualy say? I know he treats dying like its just another transformation—like falling asleep or winter turning to spring—and he famously laughed when his wife died because he saw it as her changing form. But modern medicine splits death into brain death, cardiac death, legal death, all these layers. Would he think we are just carving up a continuous natural process with arbitrary lines? Or does his idea of transforming form depend on the whole body being involved in some specific way, so brain death is still a real metaphysical shift? And when families insist on keeping the body going on ventilators—would he see that as the same kind of clinging he mocked, or is it different because the form has not actualy transformed yet? Try to stay grounded in his actual metaphyics about qi and change, not just generic daoist vibes.
