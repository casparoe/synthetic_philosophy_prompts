Given his dual commitment to the power of the multitude and to reason as the ground of legitimate authority, would he interpret the calibration layer as a rational enhancement of democratic sovereignty, or as an aristocratic substitution of the people's actual conatus with an artificial proxy? Please reconstruct the strongest Spinozist argument for each interpretation, then state which view he would likely endorse and why.
