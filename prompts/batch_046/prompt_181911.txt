I've come around to a kind of nested cyclical view—Polybius-style anacyclosis for political forms, Ibn Khaldun's asabiyyah (group solidarity) decay, but stripped of Spengler/Toynbee mystical biology and layered so small loops sit inside bigger civilizational ones. Linear-progress people treat tech and rights accumulation as proof the cycle broke, but I think thats just local noise—epicycles riding deeper repetition of consolidation and fragmentation. So what'd actually count against it? Not the democracy-is-stable-now objection—I want hard mechanisms, like a global coordination tech that genuinely outlasts generational turnover and breaks the loop, or historical discontinuities that can't be mapped onto cycle phases without stretching the model past recogntion. Also, has this view been qualified into unfalsifiability? If every boom gets read as renewed solidarity and every collapse as decay, is it empty? Rank defeaters by how fatal they are, say which ones defenders of nested cycles would concede versus handwave, and be honest about whether being well-insulated is a virtue or a vice here.
