So is Goodman's point really about any inducton system, not just English? And does that mean Hume's problem is actually an engineering problem—about the bias built into our models and brains—rather than a logical one?

To make it more concrete, I cooked up this niche idea I'm calling 'grue-features' in embedding space: dimensions that stay stable during training but flip their correlation in deployment, kind of like a temporal index baked into the representation. Does that actually map onto Goodman's paradox, or am I just stretching a metaphor until it breaks?
