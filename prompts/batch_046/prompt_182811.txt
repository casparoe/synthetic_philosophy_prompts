Two philosophers disagreed about what Descartes’s cogito actually establishes. (1) The first argued it proves nothing more than that some thinking is occurring right now: the formulation that thinking implies existence should be read as licensing only the claim that there is a being who thinks, where “I” functions merely as an indexical that picks out whatever happens to be having the thought, with no commitment to a persistent substance, an enduring self, or identity across moments. (2) The second objected that this drains the argument of its intended force: Descartes is not registering a bare mental event but establishing that he—the very one doubting—necessarily exists, and he uses this to ground res cogitans, a substance whose entire essence is thought rather than a temporary occurrence. (3) The first replied that moving from “this thought is happening” to “there is a substance whose nature is to think” smuggles in the substantialist metaphysics the cogito was supposed to establish, and that all the argument genuinely licenses is a minimal, momentary “this”—comparable to saying it hurts here without proving there is a permanent body part called “here.” (4) The second countered that indexicals like “I” have special reference conditions: unlike “it hurts here,” successfully using “I” requires that there actually be a speaker, so the cogito reveals not just an event but a subject that must exist to perform the reference. (5) The first said this confuses semantic success conditions with metaphysical conclusions: “I” can be used by a temporary, fragmented, or even distributed cognitive system without that system being a Cartesian substance, and Descartes never rules out that the thinker is just a momentary configuration. Please adjudicate move by move: rate each of the five moves as clearly mistaken, defensible, or clearly right, briefly justify each rating, then say which party is closer to the truth about what the cogito proves and what both are missing—particularly regarding whether the argument establishes anything about persistence, substancehood, or the conditions for indexical reference.
