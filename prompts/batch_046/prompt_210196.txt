Design a Twin Earth thought experiment that isolates the contribution of ecological-functional role to semantic content while holding hidden microstructure constant. The scenario: Earth and Twin Earth are chemically identical—both contain H2O in abundance—but differ in atmospheric chemistry: Earth has free oxygen, Twin Earth is anoxic. On both planets a locally ostended substance called "respirant" is named through direct interaction and embedded in scientific practice. Unbeknownst to the inhabitants, the samples Oscar and Twin Oscar ostend are both H2O, but on Earth it participates in combustion and respiration, whereas on Twin Earth those reactive partners are absent, so the substance plays no combustion-supporting ecological role.

Your task is to engineer this case so that Putnam-style causal-historical externalism and an "ecological-role" theory of reference—according to which a natural-kind term refers to whatever substance actually fulfills a specified functional role in the speaker's environment, regardless of hidden structure—agree that Oscar's and Twin Oscar's term "respirant" refers to H2O on both planets, yet disagree on the propositional content expressed by the utterance "Respirant is essential for combustion."

Specifically:
(a) Present the scenario in one paragraph, with no appeal to demons, perfect predictors, exotic physics, or science-fictional devices beyond the Twin Earth framing.
(b) State the belief, the method of acquisition, and the relevant nearby worlds for each speaker.
(c) Show the reasoning of each theory: why externalism assigns the content it does (focusing on hidden essence and causal-historical chain to H2O), and why the ecological-role theory assigns different content (focusing on functional embedding in the local environment).
(d) Explain why convergence on reference does not entail convergence on what is said, identifying the precise structural feature—identical hidden essence coupled with divergent ecological functionality—that does the work.
(e) Note what happens if we remove the divergence in ecological role: does the case collapse back into the standard Twin Earth argument, or does a different disagreement remain?
(f) Finish with an honest assessment of how a defender of the ecological-role theory would attack the construction—particularly whether they can argue that reference itself should split rather than merely content.

Conclude with a single machine-readable line at the very end: Verdict: [a brief phrase naming the isolating feature].
