Please argue that the apparent improbability of life-permitting physical constants is not an empirical anomaly demanding a multiverse or designer, but rather an artifact of representing deep physical structure through dimensionful parameters that embed human-scale measurement conventions. Specifically, defend the claim that once fundamental quantities are expressed solely as dimensionless ratios fixed by as-yet-unknown symmetry principles—rather than as independently tunable dials—the "extraordinarily narrow range" objection dissolves, because the relevant parameter space collapses to discrete or tightly constrained manifolds with no continuous neighborhood of alternatives. Address the standard counterexample of the cosmological constant, which remains extreme even in dimensionless Planck units, and explain why this should be interpreted as a selection effect within an already-constrained modal structure rather than as residual evidence of tuning. Keep the reasoning rigorous but avoid appeals to specific cosmological literature or bibliographies; I am looking for conceptual clarity, not a literature review.
