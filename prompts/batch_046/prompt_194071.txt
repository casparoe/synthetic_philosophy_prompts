The system also operates under a budget-neutrality rule: if a cheaper cure for the mild condition frees up resources, those resources are not redeployed to treat more mild-condition patients, but are instead withdrawn from the severe-condition pool so that the absolute welfare gap between the two groups does not narrow. What philosophical commitments about distributive justice does this institutional design presuppose? Reconstruct the implicit theory—does it require prioritarianism, or could an egalitarian or sufficientarian framework license the same practice? Distinguish what the practice strictly needs from what merely fits it well, identify any internal tension between weighting severity and enforcing budget neutrality, and describe what the system would have to change if it rejected the leveling-down intuition altogether.
