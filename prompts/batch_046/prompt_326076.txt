Here is roughly where I’ve landed on Bradley’s regress, propositional unity, and the ontology of relations, and I want an audit of whether the package holds together:

(1) I take Bradley’s regress to show that if relations are genuinely distinct entities over and above their relata, then relating them requires an infinite hierarchy of further relations, which is ontologically unacceptable; so I reject any “external glue” that binds subject and predicate in a proposition.

(2) Consequently I explain the unity of a proposition—why “Socrates is wise” is one judgment rather than a list—entirely through the mental act of synthesis performed by the thinker, with no ontological binder in reality.

(3) Still, I hold that truth is correspondence to reality: for that judgment to be true, Socrates must actually exemplify wisdom in the world, independently of anyone’s mental act.

(4) I also think relations can obtain between entities that share no intrinsic qualitative similarity—for instance, the relation of being father and son can hold between two people with entirely different temperaments and appearances.

(5) I take a regress to be vicious only if it involves an uncompletable task; if a relation connects its relata “all at once” without iterative steps, the infinite series never gets started and there is no problem.

(6) But I also maintain that if a connection requires no further factor to link it to its relata, then it is not a real relation at all—it is merely a monadic property of the composite.

Are these commitments jointly tenable? In particular, does (3) commit me to the very kind of external relation of exemplification that (1) rejects? Can (2) and (3) be separated—mental synthesis explaining form, real-world exemplification explaining truth—or do they undermine each other? Is there a hidden tension between (4) and the rejection of external relations, or between (5) and (6) regarding whether any real relations survive? Order the tensions by severity, indicate for each what would have to give and at what cost, and flag any conflicts that dissolve on closer inspection rather than being genuine. I am not signaling which commitment I would drop.
