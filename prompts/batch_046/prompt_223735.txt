Proponents of the terms argue that real institutional shifts—such as the revival of classical learning, the transformation of university curricula, and the founding of experimental societies—function like genuine plot developments, justifying the breaks; critics respond that these labels are retrospective impositions, analogous to a biographer declaring that a subject's "maturity" began on an arbitrary Tuesday to serve a thematic arc.
