Please present exactly five brief jokes as a numbered list with exactly five items, each consisting of one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion. Each joke should be self-contained and should make sense to someone familiar with Dennett’s distinction, but it should not depend on citations, bibliographies, or knowledge of obscure sub-literatures; the punchline should hinge specifically on the difference between retroactive memory revision and advance unconscious staging in the timing of perceptual experience.
