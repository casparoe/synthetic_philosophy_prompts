In constructing this translation, treat exchange-entitlement networks as dissipative flow structures with local order parameters, model institutional gatekeepers as non-conservative boundary conditions that can violate local conservation laws without altering global integrals, and represent democratic accountability as a feedback mechanism that suppresses non-Hermitian perturbations in the Hamiltonian describing food-access states.

For each of the four components above, report precisely what translates without distortion, what must be distorted to fit the physical vocabulary, and what has no counterpart whatsoever in that framework.
