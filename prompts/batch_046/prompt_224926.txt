Consider the widespread institutional practice in wealthy liberal democracies: within borders, the state enforces robust egalitarian distributive mechanisms—progressive income taxation, universal public healthcare and education, and democratic accountability over economic policy—while treating obligations to non-citizens outside those borders as largely discretionary humanitarian charity rather than demands of justice. This domestic regime is funded in part by economic arrangements—global supply chains, resource extraction, tax-base mobility, and climate externalities—that systematically affect foreigners who have no vote in, and often receive little direct benefit from, the redistributive scheme.

What philosophical commitments about moral membership, the scope of justice, and the relation between coercion and obligation does this bifurcated practice presuppose? Reconstruct the implicit theory: does compatriot priority rest on a relational view (shared institutions and reciprocity), an associative view (common culture or history), a statist view (sovereignty as a moral boundary), or some hybrid? Distinguish what the practice strictly requires from what merely fits it well. Then examine its internal coherence: if the justification for domestic redistribution is that citizens are jointly subjected to a coercive institutional order that must be justified to them, does that same order not also coerce outsiders through trade, migration controls, and environmental harm, thereby generating parallel obligations? Or can a defender consistently maintain that sovereignty creates a boundary of justice without collapsing into the view that harm across that boundary is morally negligible?

Finally, suppose we replaced the implicit view with a cosmopolitan alternative in which distributive justice applies to all persons affected by a cooperative scheme, regardless of citizenship. Which elements of the current practice—border controls, tax jurisdiction, welfare eligibility, democratic representation—would have to be redesigned, and which could remain intact under a revised justification?
