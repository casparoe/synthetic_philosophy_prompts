The target is the concept of transgenerational national guilt and its institutionalization: a specialist should recognize that the form takes Jaspers's actual categories seriously, exaggerates only the bureaucratic mechanism, and thereby exposes the tension between personal moral agency and inherited collective responsibility. Keep the voice strictly administrative; inject at least one invented niche concept—such as an "intergenerational guilt depreciation schedule" or a "conscience withholding tax"—that extends the parody into bureaucratic territory not found in the original philosophy, so that the prompt demands original construction rather than recitation.
