But the second they do that—using words that don't quite fit—the listeners say they're being imprecise, emotional, or misapplying the framework, and their credibility tanks. I want them to run into that double-bind themselves before anyone names it.

Design a structured role-play or deliberation game with roles, rules, a timeline, and small stakes. Split them into "institutional reviewers" and "affected parties," but don't reveal it's about epistemic injustice until debrief. Then map the debrief: tie specific moments—like a forced translation or an interrupted sentence—back to the hermeneutical gap, the credibility deficit, and the translation trap.
