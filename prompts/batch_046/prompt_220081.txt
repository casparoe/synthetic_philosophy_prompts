the student wrote:

Pascal says justice is useless without force and force is dangerous without justice, so they should be paired. He uses the reason of effects idea—which he actually borrowed from Montaigne's essay on custom—to show people obey laws because they function, not because they are fair. That proves justice is just whatever custom says it is. So if reason shows a law is unfair, we still should not change it, since people only respect enforced rules. Pascal also thinks the church should share power with the state so that justice can actually be strong, kind of like Hobbes said with the sovereigns sword. Overall he is right, though he misses that civil rights movements show reason can sometimes override tradition.
