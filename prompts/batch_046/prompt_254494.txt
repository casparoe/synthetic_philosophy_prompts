Write an essay analysing whether the thermodynamic arrow of time—grounded in the statistical-mechanical increase of entropy away from an extremely low-entropy boundary condition—sufficiently explains the psychological arrow, including our mnemonic asymmetry and the directedness of causal reasoning. Your essay should address the standard statistical reply to Loschmidt's reversibility objection, consider whether the so-called Past Hypothesis merely relocates the explanatory burden rather than resolving it, and analyse the claim that conscious temporal experience requires an irreducible moving present that no entropic gradient can supply. Weigh the reductionist position against its critics, and reach a justified verdict: either defend the view that all temporal asymmetries are ultimately thermodynamic in origin, or argue that at least one non-thermodynamic arrow remains indispensable.
