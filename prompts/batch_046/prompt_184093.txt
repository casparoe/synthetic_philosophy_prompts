After the story, include a brief note stating where the narrative takes liberties with the standard philosophical framing or depends on a contested reading—particularly whether treating possible people as modal entities rather than merely descriptive possibilities alters the moral conclusion—and identify the specific argument or consideration that would most change your interpretation.
