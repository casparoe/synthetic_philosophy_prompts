Deliverables: (1) the exam question, specifying a 90-minute time limit and an answer length of about 900 words; (2) a model answer of roughly 600 words; (3) a marking scheme built around five must-hit points with assigned marks totaling 20, each with a one-line note on how candidates typically miss it, and an explicit distinction between an A answer (which resolves the tension between momentariness and moral development without collapsing into eternalism or annihilationism) and a B answer (which describes anatta correctly but fails to apply it to the synthetic-replacement case).
