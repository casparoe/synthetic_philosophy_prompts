(a) i think Schellenberg is right about the data—there's a lot of capable, honest people who dont believe, and it's not all moral failure or ignorance. non-culpable nonbelief is real.
(b) but i think hiddenness is a serious problem specifically for classical theism—omnipotent, omniscient, perfectly good—because if God wants relationship he has the power to make himself known without overwhelming anyone.
