Write a proposal for a philosophy-of-mind term paper (about 6,000 words, one semester) on whether Daniel Dennett’s taxonomy of Darwinian, Skinnerian, Popperian, and Gregorian creatures can be extended consistently to distributed or collective cognitive systems—specifically, whether a scientific research community or a human-tool ensemble (e.g., a mathematician coupled long-term with an automated theorem prover) can be genuinely Gregorian even when no individual constituent is. State the precise question: is Gregorian cognition necessarily tied to an individual organism that internalizes “thinking tools” and thereby reshapes its own cognitive architecture, or can it be realized in a distributed network of agents and artifacts with no Gregorian center? Explain why Dennett’s original framework leaves this open—his evolutionary story focuses on individual cognitive architecture and tool-use that feeds back into the user’s brain, leaving collective cognition and extended-mind scenarios underspecified. Describe your method: first, reconstruct the four kinds with explicit boundary conditions, especially the line between Popperian internal simulation and Gregorian tool-mediated restructuring; second, propose and defend an “internalization condition” that distinguishes mere artifact-aided reasoning (Popperian-plus-tools) from full Gregorian status; third, apply the condition to two carefully specified test cases—(a) a research group that collectively constructs and relies on a formal framework no single member fully masters, and (b) a human reasoner whose problem-solving strategies are progressively reshaped by sustained interaction with an adaptive proof assistant. State the possible conclusions (distributed Gregorian cognition is coherent and requires a fifth taxonomic entry; it is incoherent because Gregorian status collapses to the individual; or the taxonomy needs revision rather than extension) and identify which considerations—functional equivalence, causal feedback loops, or the location of representational integration—would decide among them. Conclude with an honest scope assessment: the risk of failure lies in the internalization condition collapsing into generic functionalism, or in the test cases being too empirically thin for a conceptual paper; specify how you will guard against both within one semester. Do not request a bibliography or literature review by citation; refer to positions and arguments in general terms and note that specific references will be added in the drafting stage.
