I want an in-person acitivity that makes them feel Buridan's ass in their bones, not just as a logic puzzle. I want the setup to use modern "preference construction" ideas -- people invent reasons post-hoc -- but push past that into the metaphysical worry: if they break the tie by flipping a coin, they then have to defend whether that was *their* choice or just outsourcing agency. Please design the rules, materials, roles, and payoff scheme so participants run into the absence-of-preference problem themselves before anyone names Buridan.
