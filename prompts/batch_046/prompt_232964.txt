I run a philosophy seminar, about 14 people, 50 minutes. Design an in-class activity that makes them feel the wrong-kind-of-reasons problem in collective moral judgement: they should converge on the right verdict about a staged case, but only by tracking some superfical cue that happens to correlate with it, then feel the mismatch when the cue is removed. Give me exactly five numbered items — set-up and rules, the payoff or incentive scheme, what actually happens during play, the debrief mapping each moment onto the problem, and what predictibly breaks the first time — each one or two sentences, no intro or conclusion. Use only paper cards and pens, and don't name the problem until the debrief.
