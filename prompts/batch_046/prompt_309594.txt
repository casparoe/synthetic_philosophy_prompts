Write a joke about whether guilt or shame is the better moral emotion, with particular attention to whether a culture can deliberately choose one over the other. The punchline should turn on the structural distinction that shame requires an audience—actual or internalized—whereas guilt requires only an internalized norm. To avoid generic tropes, frame the scenario as a state ministry attempting to engineer a shame culture through bureaucratic policy, only to discover that its administrative procedures have privatized the audience and thereby converted every intended shame response into solitary guilt. The joke should be intelligible to someone familiar with the conceptual debate but should not cite philosophers, texts, or empirical studies by name.
