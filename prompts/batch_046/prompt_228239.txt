Write the desiderata that any adequate theory of bounded reflective self-trust must satisfy if it is genuinely to resolve the Löbian obstacle for artificial agents that reason about their own reliability. I want the full specification before I evaluate any particular proposal; do not provide a bibliography, reading list, or citations.

Assume the agent operates with finite computational resources and employs reflective escrow tiers: it partitions its beliefs into strata that can be endorsed at depth n without requiring simultaneous endorsement at depth n+1. Within that framing, specify exactly what the theory must be able to deliver. For each requirement, give the motivating case or formal consideration, and flag whether it is non-negotiable or a preference of a particular camp (e.g. determinism of belief revision, preservation of Bayesian coherence, or rejection of logical omniscience).

In particular, cover:
- the phenomena it must explain (for instance, how an agent can rationally correct a detected error in its own proof-checker without thereby invalidating all of its mathematics);
- the cases it must distinguish correctly (e.g. trusting a specific reasoning module versus trusting the global architecture; object-level confidence versus meta-level endorsement);
- the distinctions it must not collapse (such as that between having the capacity for self-correction and having an unconditional guarantee of consistency);
- the formal results with which it must remain consistent (Löb's theorem, Gödel's second incompleteness theorem, and relevant bounds from computational learning theory or resource-bounded reasoning);
- and whether any subset of these desiderata is jointly unsatisfiable given known no-go results — this is the part I most want to see.

Then identify which single requirement does the most damage to the most popular current approaches (for example, unrestricted logical omniscience or simple Bayesian self-updating). You may present the criteria in a table, but keep the weight on the criteria and their justifications rather than on scoring particular theories. A brief comment on how reflective escrow interacts with the standard logical picture is welcome, but the deliverable is the requirements list itself.

Write formally, in British English.
