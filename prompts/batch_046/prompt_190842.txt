Write the five desiderata that any adequate normative theory of sunk-cost reasoning must satisfy if honoring sunk costs is ever rational rather than always fallacious, treating the issue as one of diachronic agency rather than simple error. The five should address: distinguishing identity-preserving commitment from mere resource waste; accommodating institutional path dependence without collapsing into pure consequentialism; explaining the felt compulsion to continue rather than treating agents as merely mistaken; separating recoverable commitments from irrecoverable ones; and integrating behavioral escalation findings without branding all escalation irrational. For each desideratum, state the requirement and the motivating case or distinction in one or two sentences; within the list, flag which two appear jointly unsatisfiable. Respond as exactly five numbered items with no introduction or conclusion.
