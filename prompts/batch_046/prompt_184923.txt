Write a short story about a xenolinguist named Mara stuck translating the last recordings of a dead civilization. There's a word — call it glorp — that her software can map equally well to rabbit, undetached rabbit-part, or temporal slice of rabbitness, and every new corpus just adds more compatible manuals without breaking the tie. She has to decide wether to vent a chamber based on one translation, but the message might be a warning, a recipe, or just a weather report. The story should dramatize Quine's indeterminacy of translation, the gavagai thing, without turning into a philosophy lecture. Show how the ambiguity is baked into language itself, and maybe have her realize her own human words for danger and safety are just as underdetermined. Keep it grounded — no bibliographies, no lists of philosophers, just the narrative. Its okay if the prose is a little rough, this isnt a formal essay.
