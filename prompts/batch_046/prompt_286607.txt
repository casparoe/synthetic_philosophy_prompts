Coming from a lab where we dealt with analog signals and continuous dynamics, this feels too neat, but I can't quite articulate where the mapping cracks.

So: can you lay out the correspondance table—what plays the role of the CPU, the memory architechture, the compiler, the operating system, maybe even the clock signal?
