His friend said that's wrong — Plato thinks wine strips away social masks but usually reveals vice, not virtue; the sober person isn't faking, they're excercising different capacities that alcohol disables. Second said that's mixing up psychological inhibition with metaphysical finks — Plato's training the soul, not doing counterfactual metaphysics. First said well Plato calls law medicine for the soul so he's fixing dispositions not just revealing them. They kept circling whether symposiom rules are therapeutic or diagnostic.
