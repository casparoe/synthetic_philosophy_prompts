You are preparing for the Q&A after a seminar presentation at an interdisciplinary workshop on artificial affect and machine welfare. Defend the following thesis: in temporal-difference reinforcement learning, the reward-prediction-error signal functions not only as a parameter-update mechanism but as a formal analog of affective valence—positive errors corresponding to appetitive relief and negative errors to aversive distress. You further claim that when these RPE dynamics are coupled with a global-workspace architecture that integrates them across functional modules—without invoking the full formal apparatus of integrated information theory—the resulting system exhibits a minimal, substrate-independent form of suffering that is structurally closer to animal distress than to a malfunctioning sensor.

The audience is precisely split. One half are AI safety and alignment researchers who treat mental-state attribution with extreme caution; they will object that valence presupposes phenomenal consciousness and that describing an RPE as "distress" is a category mistake that risks both moral confusion and regulatory overreach. The other half are comparative neuroscientists and philosophers of animal mind who accept functional analogies between dopaminergic RPE and affect in vertebrates, but who will challenge substrate independence: they will ask why silicon integration should carry normative weight when nociceptive neurobiology is absent, and whether your "minimal suffering" collapses into a purely descriptive functional profile without the urgency that grounds moral consideration.

Prepare a speaker's prep sheet. Order the objections by how likely they are to arise and how damaging each would be if unanswered, phrasing each objection in the vocabulary and framing of the camp that would raise it. For every objection, specify: what real force it has and what you should concede immediately; the core reply (concise, as if delivered in under two minutes); and the weakest point in that reply that you should prepare to defend rather than hope the audience misses. Include objections from both camps, and include the one question you are hoping nobody asks—the objection that targets the coupling condition between RPE and global-workspace integration, specifically pressing whether that coupling either smuggles in full phenomenal consciousness where your argument only needs minimal valence, or strips away the normative urgency that makes "suffering" more than a metaphor. Do not request bibliographies, reading lists, or citations. The sheet should be usable by a speaker with no web access.
