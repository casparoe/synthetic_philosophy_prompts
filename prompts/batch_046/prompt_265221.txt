Please place each into one of four tiers—S, A, B, or C—according to how well it reconciles a strict denial of necessary secondary causation with the stability and predictive reliability that empirical science requires. For each tier, give a brief one-sentence justification. Then, in a clearly separated section, distinguish which parts of your evaluation rest on broadly settled points in the scholarship on Islamic philosophy of science and which depend on genuinely contested interpretive choices—especially regarding whether Ghazālī's denial of causation is metaphysical or primarily epistemic—without presenting any contested reading as if it were the consensus view.
