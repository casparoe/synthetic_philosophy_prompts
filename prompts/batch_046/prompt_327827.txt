okay so ive been thinking about guilt vs shame lately, partly because my friend was talking about how in her family they never say sorry they just fix things, and partly because i read something about how in some cultures people feel worse about being seen as bad. i have a bunch of opinions that dont quite line up and i want to know if im being inconsistent or if theres a way to hold them all.

first, i think guilt is the better emotion because its about the thing you did, not about your whole soul being dirty. you can pay a debt or say sorry and be done. shame feels like it sticks to you forever. but second, i actually think shame is more honest when what you did comes from who you actually are - like if youre a liar, feeling guilty about one lie is just playing a game, but feeling ashamed means you see the pattern. third, i think cultures can totally choose which one to push, like through school or church. my cousin lived in korea and said everyone talks about face and saving face, while here in the states we talk about feeling guilty. so that seems like a cultural choice, or at least a setting.

but then fourth, i think its not really a choice because shame only works when everyone knows your business - in a big city you cant shame people because nobody cares who they are, so guilt with police and courts takes over. so maybe culture doesnt choose, the size of the city chooses for you. and fifth, i think we should try to build a guilt-culture on purpose, with fair rules and second chances, but i also think that if we did that people might stop caring about each other and just become lonely rule-followers. and sixth, i think for really bad stuff like cheating on a partner or betraying a friend, shame is actually the right response, not guilt - you should feel like youre not worthy of them, not just like you owe them an apology.

so do these hang together? like is there a real contradiction between saying guilt is better but shame is more honest? and between saying culture can choose but also city size decides? and does my idea that we should build a guilt-culture conflict with my belief that shame is right for betrayal? i dont need a lecture on anthropologists, just tell me which pairs are fighting and what the cheapest fix is - like what would i have to drop if i wanted to be consistent without changing everything else?
