I'm trying to sort out what I think pleasure actually is, going by my gut on cases. Eating a great meal: pleasure, no question. Finally solving a proof I've been stuck on for days: also pleasure, but it feels like a totally different beast - mental, not bodily. Laughing when a rival messes up in public: there's a definite positive kick, but I hesitate to call it pure pleasure since it's tied to someone else losing. Staring at a tragic painting that moves me: pleasure, even though the content is awful. Finishing my taxes and feeling relieved: definately not pleasure, just absence of dread. And then there's this weird calm I get from sorting spam emails into neat folders - it's satisfying in a low-key way, but is that pleasure or just my brain liking order?

Is there one principle that captures all the yes-cases and excludes the nos? Or do I need separate categories? And what should I say about the spam-folder calm - is it pleasure, and does that ruin any simple account? I keep going back and forth on that one.
