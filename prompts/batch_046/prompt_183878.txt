I run a philosophy-of-action workshop, about 12 grad students, 75 minutes. I want them to physically feel the unilateralist's curse—each participant has a private threshold for taking some action that affects the whole group, but since they decide alone without knowing others' cutoffs, the collective outcome ends up being one that literally nobody preferred. Design a card-based roleplay with hidden threshold cards, point payoffs (no cash), printed rules, and a timeline. They need to construct the failure themselves before I name it. Include a debrief mapping which round corresponded to which feature of the curse, a honest note on what will predictably break the first time I run it, and a frank admission of which part—probly the infinite regress of reasoning about other people's reasoning—the game just can't capture. Keep it casual, don't worry about citations or a reading list since I need this runnable tomorrow.
