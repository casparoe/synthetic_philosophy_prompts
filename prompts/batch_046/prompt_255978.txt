I serve on the review board for the Meridian Institute's program in synthetic cognition, and we must vote by Friday on Protocol 7-B. The applicants propose an agent architecture they call Lexicon-7, which they claim achieves Gregorian cognition: not merely Popperian pre-selection of actions or Skinnerian reinforcement, but the deployment and endogenous revision of internal symbolic tools. One colleague insists that Dennett's taxonomy is descriptive rather than normative, and that using it to gate funding smuggles metaphysics into an engineering review. Another colleague warns that if Lexicon-7 is actually only Popperian, misclassifying it as Gregorian could trigger unwarranted welfare protections intended for culturally embedded agents. Please draft the brief written opinion I will circulate before the vote: recommend unconditional approval, conditional approval with specific benchmarks, or rejection, with criteria the board can actually apply, and treat the strongest objection on the losing side with genuine seriousness rather than dismissal.
