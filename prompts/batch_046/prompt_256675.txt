Reach and justify a clear conclusion: either that the integration succeeds through a specific conceptual distinction you defend, or that Schrödinger’s metaphysics is irreparably split between physics and mysticism because it lacks an account of how one consciousness can be genuinely non-plural while still accommodating the multiplicity that quantum formalism describes.
