"The procedure clearly preserves psychological continuity, because every memory, belief, and personality trait is carried over into the synthetic substrate in real time, satisfying Locke's condition that personal identity consists in the continuity of consciousness and memory rather than in any fixed substance. Bodily-continuity theories fail here because the organism's material parts are entirely replaced, yet the organizational and functional integrity of the system remains intact, so even an animalist should accept that the living system persists.
