(1) in the chinese room the guy shuffling symbols doesn't understand chinese bc syntax isn't semantics.
(2) searle says brains have original intentionality while books and computers only have derived intentionality.
(3) but brain states are just physical patterns—neural firing, chemical gradents—no different in kind from symbol-strings in a book.
(4) if physical patterns alone can't ground original intentionality (that's the whole room argument), then brains don't have it either; they only have derived intentionality from evolution or design.
(5) but then nothing has original intentionality, so searle's distinction collapses and also it seems like nobody actually understands anything.
(6) so either (1) is false and syntax can be semantics after all, or (3) is false and brain patterns are magically special.

where does this slip? i really want to keep (2) and (3) if possible, but i feel like (4) is sneaking in an extra assumption about 'alone.' is that the weak link? and if i reject (4), do i have to buy dualism or can i stay physicalist?
