(5) You might think neuroscience saves us by scanning brains directly, but reading a scan is still conscious interpretation of visual data, which goes through the same delayed interface. (6) So third-person methods are also stuck at the icon level. (7) That means neither first- nor third-person science can ever access real mental mechanisms. (8) But cognitive science clearly works and makes predictions. (9) So the user-illusion view must be false, or at least can't apply to mental states.

He thinks the escape is (5) — that brain scans bypass the interface. But I don't buy it, since the scan interpretation is still conscious. If I have to keep (1), which step do I actually reject, and whats the cost?
