I don't want a lecture; I want an in-class acitivity where they physically run into the problem before anyone names it.

Design a structured role-play or card-based game for this group. The rules should be arranged so that whenever a player tries to own their decision, the inheritance chain forces them back one step, and they realize they never held the root cause.
