For my advanced coursework, I want to compare communitarianism and liberalism through a concrete applied lens rather than repeating the standard abstract debate about the self. Specifically, compare Sandel's view that communities are constitutive of personal identity with the Rawlsian liberal commitment to state neutrality, focusing on whether governments should subsidize endangered local dialects or prioritize universal access to a dominant global language. Evaluate whether collective obligations to maintain linguistic particularity can be justified without violating liberal neutrality, and defend which framework offers the more coherent account of justice in this domain.
