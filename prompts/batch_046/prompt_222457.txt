Build a decision tree that sorts positions by answers to concrete, modified scenarios rather than by theoretical labels. At each leaf, place the well-being theory or theories that consistently land there—pure hedonism, desire-satisfaction theory, objective-list perfectionism, relational or authenticity-based views, hybrid accounts, or unnamed/unoccupied. For every unoccupied branch, provide one sentence explaining whether the gap is due to incoherence, instability, or lack of exploration. Explicitly flag any two named positions the tree cannot reliably separate. Do not ask for bibliographies, citations, or reading lists, and frame every node as a case-based question that can be answered without knowing the position's name in advance.
