i'm in a intro philosphy course and we've been arguing about hedonism — like, if pleasure is the only good, how do you compare totally different pleasures. I keep noticing the same defensive loop in threads online, and I don't know if it's a recognized failure mode or just bad arguing.

Here's the pattern. Someone starts by saying every pleasure has this one shared feature, "it feels good," so welfare is just adding up that shared thing. Then you point out that eating chocolate, solving a hard proof, and watching a rival mess up feel completely different — no shared texture. The defender says okay, not one identical feeling, but they're all desired for their own sake, that's the family link. But then you ask why the malicious pleasure of seeing your rival fail counts less, and they say "because it's not pure — there's guilt or harm mixed in." So now there's a hidden purity exception. Next you ask: if they share no common feeling and some are filtered out, what exactly are we measuring when we say one life has more pleasure? They answer "intensity, how strongly you want it," but that just brings desire back in, not pleasure. So they loop back to "it feels good," but now with an invisible footnote about purity and intensity. It's definately not plain circular reasoning, since each step patches a specific leak, and it's not exactly moving the goalposts because the claim "pleasure is the good" never moves — only the bridge to defend it gets rebuilt in different material each time.

Does this three-station loop have a name? I know it's somewhere near the heterogeneity problem, but that usually just asks what pleasures share; this is about the argumentative retreat pattern that tries to answer that question and keeps cycling through common-quality, family-resemblance-desire, and hidden-metric defenses. Is there an established concept for exactly this cycle, and if not, how should we carve it — what is it, what isn't it (like simple ad hoc rescue or plain equivocation), and where are the edges? Would it apply to other theories that try to unify a diverse class by smuggling in a measurable factor?
