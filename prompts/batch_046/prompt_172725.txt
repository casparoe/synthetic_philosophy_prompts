One fellow board member insists that actively choosing the full repair — and thereby directly causing the settlement's immediate loss — is morally worse than choosing to do nothing, even though the latter guarantees a larger harm later and offers no long-term protection to the settlement. He claims there is a deep moral line between bringing about a harm through our own intervention and allowing a harm to occur through the natural progression of the system's decay.
