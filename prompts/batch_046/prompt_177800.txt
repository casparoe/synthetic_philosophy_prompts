Please write a rigorous but accessible sequence of interview questions I can use to press her on the actual content of this view, in the following order. Fourth, ask what specific experimental result—say, sustained cooperation in a strictly anonymous, one-shot, non-genetically-structured game with no possibility of future interaction—would cause her to abandon the pooling model entirely.

The questions should anticipate her tendency to deflect by insisting that pool dynamics are structurally different because they operate across unrelated lineages, and they should keep pressing past that deflection rather than letting it end the exchange. The tone should suit a journalist covering science and ideas: conceptually precise, avoiding biographical digressions, and not asking for bibliographies, citations, or a reading list. End with one final question about what evidence could genuinely count against her position.
