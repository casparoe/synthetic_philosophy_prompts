Give an example of a conceptual issue that emerges when naturalist and normativist accounts of disease are applied concurrently to stable, heritable variations in cognitive architecture—such as particular neurodivergent profiles—within social institutions that equate statistical typicality with functional adequacy. Construct a concrete scenario in which a given biological configuration is measurably atypical and involves divergent information-processing mechanisms, yet is subjectively experienced as non-pathological and even advantageous, while being classified as a disorder by clinical taxonomies that implicitly encode normative expectations about productivity within standardized organizational environments. Then evaluate whether resolving the tension requires revising the naturalist criterion of biological dysfunction, broadening the normativist evaluation to encompass subjective flourishing and ecological fit, or accepting that the concept of disease dissolves into context-dependent local frameworks once we reject the premise that biological deviation and social harm necessarily co-vary.
