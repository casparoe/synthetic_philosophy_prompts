I have been working through the standard metaphysical literature on dispositions, and I keep returning to the familiar pair of counterexamples—finks, which remove a disposition precisely when the triggering conditions are met, and masks, which block the manifestation while leaving the underlying disposition intact. Most textbook illustrations stay close to brittle objects, poison, or strike-anywhere matches, but I am trying to see whether the fink/mask distinction holds up once we move away from macroscopic artifacts and into systems where the interfering mechanism is not an external intruder but an internal structural feature without which the system would not be the kind of thing it is.

To that end, I want you to construct a concrete conceptual example—either extending a synthetic-biology framing or inventing an analogous one—illustrating a case where this distinction collapses. Imagine, for instance, a genetically engineered organism that expresses a lethal toxin only when both an internal temperature threshold and an external light cue are satisfied. The organism contains a constitutive pathway that, at high temperature but in darkness, produces a suppressor protein; this suppressor masks the toxin expression, yet the pathway is itself part of what makes the organism the kind of system it is. If we delete the suppressor gene, the organism expresses the toxin at high temperature regardless of light, but then it is arguably no longer the same kind of biological system. Your task is to present a specific scenario—whether biological, chemical, or mechanical—where the masking mechanism is identity-constituting in this way, and to explain precisely why the collapse of the fink/mask boundary creates trouble for the counterfactual analysis of dispositions. In particular, show why deciding whether the original system genuinely had the disposition to manifest the effect forces us to take a stand on essentialist commitments about which components belong to the dispositional base, commitments that the simple counterfactual account was supposed to avoid.
