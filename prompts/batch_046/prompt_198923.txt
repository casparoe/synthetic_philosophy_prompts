ok so here's my rough philosophical profile on promises for our ethics reading group and i want to know if it actually holds together or if im helping myself to contradictory stuff. here's where i stand:

1. promises bind because they create what i call a deliberative lock-in — i take that option off my own menu of reasons and give the promisee a kind of veto over that slice of my future. it's not about fear of punishment, its about authorizing someone else over my choices.

2. but i also think this veto only exists if the promisee actually knows and accepts it. if they never heard the promise or forgot about it, thier's no veto-holder, so im not strictly bound, though i still owe them some transparency if i dont do it.

3. i dont think promises are just social conventions we could drop tomorrow; there's something genuinely normative going on. but i dont want to invoke some spooky normative power that floats free of human practices either.

4. promises made under serious coercion dont bind at all. but ones made under mild social pressure — like saying yes to avoid awkwardness — do bind, just with lighter weight. the line is fuzzy but i think it tracks whether my will was substantialy bypassed.

5. i think there's a promissory residue left over even when a promise is legitimately broken (say the act turned immoral). you still have to acknowledge the break, maybe explain yourself, not just ghost.

6. self-promises dont bind anyone, including me, because there's no separate veto-holder. but i still feel that residue when i break them, which makes me wonder if im secretly treating myself as both sides.

so which of these actually clash? specifically im worried that (1) and (2) pull in different directions — if its all about the veto, why does awareness matter? and does (6) undermine (2) since residue shows up without a separate holder? also is (3) compatible with (1) or am i just dressing up convention in fancy language? and where does (5) fit — is residue a leftover normative fact that contradicts my anti-spooky stance in (3)? rank the tensions by how bad they are, and tell me which single revision cleans up the most mess with the least cost. be specific about which ones only look like tensions.
