As a novelist assembling a volume of conceptual fables, I seek a short story that dramatizes the tension surrounding the principle of alternative possibilities—the idea that moral responsibility requires that one genuinely could have acted otherwise—through a fictional mechanism I have devised. Set the narrative in the clock-city of Veldt, where every inhabitant carries a dormant Counterfactual Guardian: an invisible, non-intervening entity that meticulously records every branch point of decision, mapping the precise path the inhabitant would have taken had they hesitated, yet never physically prevents any action. The protagonist, Elara, is a master clockmaker who deliberately shatters the Grand Chronometer, an artifact whose continued ticking maintains the city's temporal coherence. At her trial, the prosecution presents the Guardian's logs, which demonstrate an unactualized branch in which Elara walked away unharmed; they argue that this recorded possibility proves she could have done otherwise, and thus bears full moral responsibility for the catastrophe. The defense counters that the Guardian, though present, was causally inert—capable in principle of restraint but never activated—so the alternative path existed only as a spectral record, not as a genuinely available option. Elara herself should reflect on whether being watched by a silent recorder changes the weight of her choice, and whether responsibility can be anchored to possibilities that were never causally open to her. The story should unfold with literary precision: render the sensory texture of brass gears and silent observation, weave the philosophical dispute into dialogue and interior monologue without invoking named philosophers or academic terminology, and resolve not with a doctrinal answer but with an ambiguous image that leaves the reader uncertain whether the Guardian's logs exonerate or condemn her. The piece should stand on its own for readers unfamiliar with the underlying debate, yet reward those who recognize the conceptual architecture embedded within it.
