I've come to think that in any Nomic-style system — where rules can amend themselves, including the rules about amendement — there's always a hidden hierachical dependancy. Even if everything looks flat and self-contained, some meta-rule has to sit outside the amendable set, otherwise you don't just get a puzzle, you get a genuine paradox of legal authority. So what would count against that? I want arguments, formal results, or actual constitutional examples that should actually move me, sorted from decisive to merely awkward. And be honest — has this just been qualified into unfalsifiability? If so, is that a problem for a view that's supposed to be about the logic of authority rather than just a descriptive claim?
