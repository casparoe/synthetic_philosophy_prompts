The essay claims white lies are structurally like tatemae: both treat the listener as lacking capacity for reality, so Dutch directness is ethically consistent while Japanese indirectness involves a kind of epistemic condescension. Is this a fair charcterization? I feel like it's smuggling Hegel into everyday Dutch culture and conflating tatemae with actual lying.
