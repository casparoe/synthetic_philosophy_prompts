The subject knows that inside the machine all relationships and achievements are simulated, but the hedonic quality is indistinguishable from authentic flourishing.

What is your actual credence, expressed as a number between 0 and 100, that a rational agent with a stable, reflective preference structure—who initially values both authentic connection and subjective well-being—would choose to remain inside after receiving the first such briefing?
