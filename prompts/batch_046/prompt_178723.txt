Do not present them as equally damaging: take a definite position on which moves genuinely threaten that commitment from within and which can be safely historicized. For each tier, give exactly one sentence of justification, with particular attention to whether the move depends on a contestable empirical psychology or on a conceptual tension that survives even if that psychology is outdated. Your response should be committed: choose a clear ordering, defend it without equivocation, and avoid retreating to interpretive pluralism.
