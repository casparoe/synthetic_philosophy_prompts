Write an essay on whether suffering risks—so-called s-risks—in advanced artificial systems present a genuinely distinct moral category from existential risk, or whether they're ultimately reducible to a subset of harm-prevention concerns that don't warrant seperate theoretical treatment. Some critics say that treating s-risks as sui generis generates weird prioritisation paradoxes when resources are scarce, so do engage with that objection if you think it bites.
