Consider the careenium model, in which simmballs are rearranged according to purely formal syntactic rules that make no reference to the physical properties of the substrate that realizes them. If the symbolic relations among the simmballs do not themselves exert any mechanical force—if they do not literally push the substrate's constituent parts around—then it seems the substrate's causal dynamics are entirely closed at the physical level, leaving no causal work for the symbolic organization to do. Given this, doesn't the careenium demonstrate that any mental state implemented symbolically must be causally excluded by the underlying physics? Or is there a coherent sense in which the syntactic arrangement of simmballs can be said to push the substrate around without violating the causal closure of the physical domain?
