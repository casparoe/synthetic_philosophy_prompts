A research team here digitized Einstein's brain annotations and correspondence into an interactive, bound-volume artifact — basically a physical book with embedded generative modules that answers questions in Einstein's voice and updates its responses based on reader annotations. Some board members want it released as a publication; others say it meets enough functional criteria that interacting with it needs informed-consent protocols, as if it were a cognitive subject. The philosophical angle is that old Mind's I thought experiment — could a book be conscious? — except now it's sitting in our archive.

Please draft the board memo recommending either full publication status or protected-subject status with conditions. Lead with the bottom line in your first sentence, then reasoning, no preamble, don't restate the question. You need to take seriously the strongest arguemnt for the side you reject — especially the claim that if consciousness is just integrated information then this artifact qualifies, and also the counter that treating every responsive archive as a subject collapses research access entirely. Keep it concrete: what's the decision, what's the review time frame, and what criteriea should we apply if another lab submits something similar? Don't ask for bibliographies or cite specific papers — just work it out.
