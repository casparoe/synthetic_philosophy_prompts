is this disagreement substantive - about whether a divine command can actually override moral law? is it verbal, with people using "suspension" and "bracketing" to mean different things? or are the two sides asking different questions entirely, one normative (what should abraham do) and one descriptive/phenomenological (what kind of act is this)? and given your diagnosis, what would actually move this forward? a sharper distinction, a formal model of normative conflict, or is the question just ill-posed? i need someone to tell me what kind of stall this actually is.
