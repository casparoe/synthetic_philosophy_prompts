At a recent philosophy-of-mind seminar—attended in my new capacity as a retired experimental physicist turned philosophy student—two colleagues debated whether the color-phi illusion demonstrates a Stalinesque or an Orwellian revision of perceptual history. (1) The first maintained that when a gray dot is flashed at position A and, after a brief interval, a red dot at position B, observers report a single object moving across the gap and changing color in mid-trajectory, never experiencing the initial gray state at all. She argued that the perceptual system literally airbrushes the first datum out of consciousness, leaving no recoverable trace within the stream of experience; this is Stalinesque revision, because the substrate of awareness is rewritten by destroying the original entry rather than archiving it. (2) The second objected that early visual areas still register the gray dot and that predictive-coding mechanisms preserve prediction-error residues; what changes is only the narrative report delivered to awareness. He labeled this Orwellian: the official record is updated so that the past reads consistently with the present, but the neural archives retain the original files, merely rendered inaccessible to introspection. (3) The first replied that if no empirical signature—no reportable after-image, no measurable priming, no introspective residue—distinguishes an archived trace from a destroyed one, then invoking hidden archives is metaphysical ornamentation. Without such a signature, she insisted, the Orwellian framing collapses into the Stalinesque one, adding only poetic vocabulary to the same excision. (4) The second answered that the distinction is architectural, not introspective: predictive processing updates posterior beliefs about the stimulus sequence, leaving measurable adaptation effects that do not require conscious retrieval. To demand reportable access, he said, is to confuse the newsroom with the archive.

Please adjudicate this exchange move by move. For each of the four positions, rate it on a spectrum from clearly mistaken to clearly right and provide a brief justification. Then state which party is correct about whether color-phi involves Stalinesque erasure or Orwellian revision, and identify what both disputants overlook regarding the temporal binding and predictive construction of conscious experience.
