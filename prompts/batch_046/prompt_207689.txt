Right, I'm drafting a session on whether artifacts actually carry politics — not just influence behaviour, but embed normative arrangements. Before we argue about specific cases, I want teh students to build the spec first. Write the desiderata: what must an adequate theory of technological politics deliver to count as adequate?

For each requirement, state:
- the claim the theory must make or case it must handle
- the consideration or distinction behind it
- whether it's non-negotiable or camp-specific
- and flag any pair you think are jointly unsatisfiable

Make sure you cover:
1. The loaded-vs-neutral distinction: why does a nuclear reactor's centralised control structure feel politically loaded, while a spoon doesn't? The theory needs a principled criterion, not just intuition.
2. Subversion: if a surveillance architecture gets repurposed for worker organising, does that refute intrinsic-politics claims, or just show flexibility?
3. Reverse causation: democratic practices (anonymous voting, public assembly) may require specific material conditions. Must the theory analyse politics shaping technology, not just the reverse?
4. Affordance drift — here's the niche injection. The theory must explain cases where an artifact's political valence shifts without any physical change, like encryption moving from state-secret to human-rights tool. Is this a change in the artifact's politics or only in its social framing?

Then briefly score three positions against your list: hard determinism (artifacts have inherent political properties), interpretive flexibility (all politics lies in social framing), and a script view (artifacts prescribe but users deviate). Don't write essays — just flag where each stumbles.

Present this in a table with columns: Requirement | Motivating case / distinction | Negotiable? | Joint tension flag | Scorecard note.

Write in British English. Also say what would change your mind — which finding or argument would make you drop one of the requirements or reorder the joint-unsatisfiability claim — and flag where your reasoning is shakiest.

Keep it focused, no bibliographies, no citation lists, pure conceptual plumbing.
