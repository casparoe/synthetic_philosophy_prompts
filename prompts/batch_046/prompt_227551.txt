The target is the bureaucratic fantasy of neutral arbitration under minimal liberty, not any named scholar. Please conclude with a single line beginning “Verdict:” that summarizes, in one sentence, whether the memo implicitly judges minimal liberty to be administratively sustainable without collapsing into hidden dictatorship or procedural deadlock.
