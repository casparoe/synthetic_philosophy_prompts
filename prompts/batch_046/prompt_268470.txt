The dream one is a perfect example. So they talk past each other under the heading 'are dreams experiences?' But maybe I'm definately wrong. Maybe there's a hidden factual disagreement about whether there is occurrent phenomenology in sleep, or about what counts as an 'experience' in the first place—like, does it require access consciousness, or just phenomenal?

A new finding about sleep-stage reporting? A formal distinction between occurrent and reconstructed states?
