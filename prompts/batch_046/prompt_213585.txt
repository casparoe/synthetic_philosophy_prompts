I've been working through Butterfield's concerns about Whig interpretation in a philosophy of history seminar, and my study group has gotten stuck applying the critique only to canonical nineteenth-century narratives—say, the march toward parliamentary democracy or the inevitability of modern science. I want to test whether the framework holds up when the present institution being legitimated is not a distant scientific method but a live democratic claim. So consider the dominant rhetorical framing that accompanied the 2016 Brexit referendum and its aftermath: the narrative that British parliamentary sovereignty represents the natural culmination of an unbroken constitutional lineage running from Magna Carta through the 1688 settlement, past the Reform Acts, to the present, with Brexit figured as a restoration of this trajectory rather than a rupture with it.

Analyze this framing rigorously as an instance of Whig history, but do so through two specific distinctions I want us to use: progressive abstraction, by which concrete political conflicts—the coercive union with Scotland, the republican interlude of the 1640s, the aristocratic preservation embedded in 1688—are rewritten as stepping-stones toward an abstract principle of popular sovereignty; and institutional essentialism, by which the present Westminster model is treated as the revealed true form of past institutions rather than one contingent outcome among others. What specific discontinuities and power asymmetries does this narrative suppress in order to maintain continuity? How does it redistribute agency—who becomes the hero of the story and who disappears? And does this case ultimately confirm Butterfield's suspicion that Whig history is structurally unavoidable whenever a present political order needs historical legitimation, or does democratic sovereignty present a special difficulty for the critique—perhaps because popular control genuinely introduces a normative standard that past settlements can be measured against without falling into pure teleology? Be disciplined in the mapping: identify the actors in the narrative, specify what counts as evidence for continuity versus rupture, and say clearly where Whig analysis is silent or misapplies rather than forcing it to speak.
