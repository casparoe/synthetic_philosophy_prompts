Where possible, indicate if any portion of the dispute is merely verbal—arising from different usages of understanding or grounding—and explain why, without reducing the entire disagreement to terminological choice. Please avoid bibliographies, citation lists, or references to specific publications, and frame everything so that it could be evaluated by someone without access to external sources.
