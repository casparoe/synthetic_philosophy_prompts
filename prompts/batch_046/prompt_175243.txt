The audience is a workshop of four faculty—two social historians of medicine who treat records as largely transparent windows onto institutional practice, a critical data-studies scholar who regards counter-archive projects as replicating the original classificatory violence, and a philosopher of science who favors probabilistic imputation of missing data over hermeneutic reconstruction—plus peers.
