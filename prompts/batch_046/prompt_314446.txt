Design a structured role-play (approximately 40 minutes of active play, followed by a 25-minute debrief and 25 minutes of open discussion) in which students adopt roles as board members, treating clinicians, and patient advocates. Specify needed materials, what will predictably fail the first time the activity is run, and how to correct for it.
