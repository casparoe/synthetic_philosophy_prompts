I'm working through Schopenhauer's thesis that music is not, like the other arts, a copy of the Platonic Ideas but rather an immediate objectification of the Will itself. At the same time, he assigns musical elements—bass to inorganic nature, harmony to the plant and animal grades, and melody to human self-consciousness—to mirror the hierarchical levels at which the Will objectifies itself in nature. I am struggling to see how these two commitments cohere: if music genuinely bypasses the realm of Ideas and represents the Will directly, why should its internal formal structure reproduce the very graded taxonomy of nature's representational objectifications? Is there a consistent interpretation on which the bass-to-melody mapping is metaphysical rather than representational, or does this analogy inadvertently collapse music back into being an image of Ideas? Could you explain the conceptual tension and how Schopenhauer might resolve it without undermining music's special status?
