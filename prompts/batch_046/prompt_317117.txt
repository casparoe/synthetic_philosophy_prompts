(5) he said thats a retrofit: al-ghazali explicitly rejects any efficacy in created things, and interventionism still needs manipulating fire to make a difference, which only works if fire contributes something, not just that god decides to act there. please adjudicate move by move: rate each of the five moves from clearly wrong to clearly right, say who is right about what, and point out anything both miss about whether occasionalism can be squared with scientific practice without just redefining cause.
