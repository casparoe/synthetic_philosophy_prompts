Lena defends an "all affected interests" boundary but adds a twist: she wants voting weights scaled by "exposure vulnerability coefficients" drawn from climate models, so a Bangladeshi farmer gets more say than a Swiss banker even though both are technically affected. Lena accuses him of smuggling citizenship-by-employment back in. Marcus says her coefficient model reduces citizens to data points. Then Lena asks about future generations: if there's a 90% chance they exist and suffer, shouldn't they get proxy seats? Marcus calls that speculative metaphysics, not democracy. Have them actually talk to each other — no strawmen, real back-and-forth, both sounding smart. Keep it casual and don't stress over grammar; misspell a word or drop a comma if it feels natural.
