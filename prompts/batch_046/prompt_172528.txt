You are a professional philosopher specializing in epistemology, particularly in theories of justification, reliabilism, and circular reasoning. I am asking you to advise a small research group—three philosophers with about two years of funding—on how to allocate their attention within the debate over epistemic bootstrapping and what is often called easy knowledge: cases in which a belief appears to acquire justification by being used to validate the very source that produced it.

Before delivering your recommendation, please think carefully and begin with brief brainstorming: identify the major open conceptual questions in this space, then defend a specific prioritization.

Your answer should address the following, in polished American English and in a formal style:

1. Rank the open questions that matter most for this group. For each, explain why it is upstream of other work, how tractable it is for philosophers without empirical laboratories, and what kind of result over the next two years would change its priority.

2. Specifically discuss the tension between individual-level bootstrapping objections—for example, whether basic-source reliabilism can survive circular credential-gathering—and a less commonly explored analog: whether scientific or institutional communities face a structurally similar problem when they validate measurement instruments using the theoretical frameworks those instruments presuppose. Is this collective framing a productive extension that opens genuinely new conceptual territory, or does it collapse back into the individual-level puzzle?

3. Identify which implications or lines of work in this space are overrated or unlikely to pay off for a short philosophical project—particularly those that depend on resolving empirical disputes about cognitive psychology or neuroscience rather than on conceptual analysis.

4. Present your final deliverable as a defended agenda, not a literature survey or bibliography. Do not include reading lists, specific citations, or references to particular texts by title and date.

Please reason step by step, weigh trade-offs explicitly, and conclude with a clear ordering of what this group should pursue first, second, and third, with your criteria for importance and tractability made explicit.
