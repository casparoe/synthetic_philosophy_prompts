Because the clauses form a deductive chain, applying premise-based aggregation yields an internally consistent charter that includes (C), which contradicts our direct vote on (C); applying conclusion-based aggregation honors our rejection of (C) but forces us to retain (A) and (B) despite their logical relationship to the rejected conclusion.
