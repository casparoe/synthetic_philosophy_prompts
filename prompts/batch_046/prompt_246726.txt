I was discussing this with my friends and realised that the two approaches frequently conflict when a system achieves excellent outcomes through opaque or pedagogically questionable reasoning paths, yet we have no reusable method for resolving such conflicts under our practical constraints: we meet fortnightly, possess no legal staff, and must reach a signed decision within thirty minutes per proposal.
