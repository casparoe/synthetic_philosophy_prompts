I need to write up the desiderata for a theory like this, specificaly around the commensurability question.

Can you list out what it actually has to deliver? Like mild pleasure vs mild pain are on different metrics entirely, but severe pain and severe pleasure share a common scale? Is that coherent, or does it make the lexical boundary arbitrary?
