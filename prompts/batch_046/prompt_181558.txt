the stall is real, not just between us. in the little philosophy-physics ethics corner, people have been circling this for years. everyone agrees on the physics — decoherence, entropy, quantum fields — and everyone agrees suffering is bad if there's someone there to feel it. but the "interventionist" crowd and the neutrality crowd never move. my working guess is we're not fighting about consciousness ontology at all; we're fighting about whether primitive valences aggregate linearly or interfere like quantum amplitudes in high-entropy systems, so the universe's net suffering might cancel to near zero despite all the micro-events. my friend thinks that's just a fancy dodge, but i wonder if it splits the problem in a useful way.
