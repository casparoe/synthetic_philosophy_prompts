(2) crop agriculture kills huge numbers of small mammals and insects per calorie, but each has low capacity, so integrating over all victims gives a very high suffering-weight per calorie of plants.

(5) so if we're really obligated to minimize suffering-weight, we should stop farming and hunt only megafauna sustainably.

(6) but that's clearly nuts and probably violates other moral constraints.

(7) therefore something in (1)-(4) must be wrong.
