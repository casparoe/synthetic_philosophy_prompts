We kept circling the idea that an agent can be rationally committed to consequences she has not explicitly derived, a form of "epistemic debt" that accrues regardless of her access. Design a 90-minute seminar activity for about 10 advanced philosophy students that makes them feel this pressure. Use rules, hidden roles, or a point system with real (small) stakes so that participants must act on limited explicit inferences while an unobserved mechanism tracks the logical consequences they missed, eventually constraining their options. Include the full mechanics, timeline, debrief mapping moments onto implicit commitment versus explicit accessibility, an honest account of what can and cannot be reproduced, and a warning about what will go wrong the first time.
