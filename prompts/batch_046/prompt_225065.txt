Student answer: Dennett distinguishes three stances—the physical, design, and intentional—where the intentional stance treats a system as a rational agent whose behavior is best predicted by attributing beliefs and desires. Applying this to LLMs, attributing beliefs—such as that the model holds that Paris is the capital of France—greatly simplifies prediction across diverse prompts, so the intentional stance is clearly appropriate. Because LLMs lack persistent memory across separate sessions and have no internal needs, they fail to meet these conditions.
