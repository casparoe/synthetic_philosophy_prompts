Do not cite, quote, or reference specific philosophers, living or dead; do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or requests for citations. The piece should be readable to someone with no philosophical background, yet precise enough that a specialist recognizes the logic being parodied—particularly the circularity of using global optimality to justify local harm, and the problem of comparing worlds that contain different individuals. Approach the task with the narrative discipline of a novelist who trusts sustained irony to do its own work, letting the form of the earnings report carry the reductio without ever winking. Write in American English.
