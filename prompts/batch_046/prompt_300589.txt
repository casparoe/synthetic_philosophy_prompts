write a short story about two spheres floating in an empty space that share every observable property—same mass, same temperature, same location—except that one carries a hidden memory of being turned once by an invisible force that left absolutly no physical trace. tell it partly through an archivist who needs to catalog them and partly through the spheres' own quiet awareness, if they have any. let the tension come from the fact that swapping their places changes nothing anyone could detect, yet the memory seems to make them different. dont lecture about the philosophy; just let the reader sit with the weirdness of whether hidden inner states are enough to make two things not-one. keep the tone dreamy and a little sad, and dont feel like you need to resolve it at the end.
