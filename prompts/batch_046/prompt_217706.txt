Is there a name for this? In arguments about whether consciousness is real or just brain chemistry, I keep seeing people split it into two layers — there's the "picture" part with colors and shapes that they admit could be an illusion or misrepresentation, and then there's the "ouch" part like pain or dread that they say has to stay real even if the picture is fake. They talk like you could lose the whole movie in your head but still keep all the hurting, like those are totally seperate systems. Is that an actual philosophical distinction people make, or is it just a confusion? If it's real, what's it called, and where's the edge — can you really have one without the other, or does killing the picture also kill the ouch?
