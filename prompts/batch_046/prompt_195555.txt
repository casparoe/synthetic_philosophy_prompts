Here is my opening challenge: Appiah argues that slaveholders eventually lost honor because the practice became globally shameful, yet dueling persisted in parts of Europe and the American South for decades after it was legally prohibited and economically irrational for aristocrats, which suggests honor can survive both legal risk and material loss.
