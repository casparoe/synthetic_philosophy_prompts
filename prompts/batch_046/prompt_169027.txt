There's this restorative justice practice in some ubuntu-informed mediation programs where an offender has to apologize not just to the victim but to the victim's extended family, the offender's own family, and the local community board, yet the court still files the conviction under the offender's indivdual legal identity with no mention of the relational repair. What view of personhood does that split preseuppose? Does it need the idea that relational selfhood is performative but legal personhood is atomic, or can ubuntu actually tolerate that kind of bureaucratic individualism? If you replaced the individual record with a collective family-unit entry, what else in the legal system would have to shift?
