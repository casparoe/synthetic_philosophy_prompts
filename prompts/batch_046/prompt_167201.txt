Both should be equally competent, engaging in genuine back-and-forth: the contractualist should challenge whether structural weighting introduces an arbitrary metric, and the aggregationist should respond that pairwise reasoning itself breaks down when the number of affected parties exceeds cognitive limits. Include a concrete scenario involving scarce medical resources distributed among patients with different prognoses and social roles, but do not ask for citations, bibliographies, or references to specific texts.
