I want them to grasp that competence can sit entirely apart from any agent that understands what it's doing, but I need a teaching plan rather than a lecture. What has to come first? I'm guessing the distinction between a process that merely looks purposeful and one that actually carries intentions needs to land early, but I'm unsure whether to build the carrying example around the eye — which feels a bit tired — or around something closer to craft, like how a novelist can write dialogue that feels psychologically true without being able to articulate the grammar behind human interaction. Which case should carry the whole arc, and what must it have and lack?

At exactly which point in the sequence should I head each one off — before or after the carrying example?
