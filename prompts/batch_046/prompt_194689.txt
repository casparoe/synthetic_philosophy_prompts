You know, eating a peach, groking a proof, that weird relief when a migraine lifts. But I keep running into the same wall in philosophy threads and some of the AI alignment / reward-learning discussions I've seen (the valence and reward-hacking stuff). One side insists there's a single phenomenal quality — a positive affective glow or common currency of reward — that unites them.
