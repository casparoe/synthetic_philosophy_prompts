We kept returning to the classic pairing of fire and cotton, but neither of us could produce a case that genuinely isolated the disagreement from hidden-variable objections. I want you to build one. Design a realistic, non-theological scenario—preferably in a clinical or engineering setting rather than a miracle narrative—in which a well-established causal regularity systematically fails despite every detectable background condition being held constant. The divergence should not depend on any visibly manifest divine intervention; rather, it should arise from how each framework treats the absence of constant conjunction when all observable variables are fixed and the failure is repeatable.
