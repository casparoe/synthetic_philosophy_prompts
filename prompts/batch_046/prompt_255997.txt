(1) We ought to adopt the intentional stance toward any system if attributing beliefs and desires predicts its behavour better than physical or design stances.

(3) So we should adopt the intentional stance toward LLMs.

(9) So (1) is false, or the intentional stance can never apply to artificial systems.
