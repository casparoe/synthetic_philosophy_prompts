Write a villanelle on Wittgenstein’s private language argument refracted through the idea of aspect-blindness. Choose two refrains, but they must echo the circular failure of private ostensive definition—one touching the emptiness of an isolated box, the other touching the impossibility of recognizing one’s own state without shared grammatical criteria. Across the five tercets, stage a speaker attempting to name an inner sensation in complete isolation: begin with the apparent success of the private naming, then show each repetition of the refrains returning to the same missing public framework so that nothing is added but the rhyme binds the failure tighter. Use the ABA rhyme scheme to make each stanza feel like the same move made again. In the envoi, bring both refrains together to show that the problem is not a missing label but a form of aspect-blindness—the speaker cannot see her experience as pain, or as anything determinately, because the shared form of life that makes seeing-as possible is absent. The poem should be precise enough that a reader familiar with the Philosophical Investigations recognizes both the beetle-in-the-box structure and the later discussion of seeing-as, and it should stand as a poem for a reader who knows neither.
