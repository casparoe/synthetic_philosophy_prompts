When you sign the directive with full mental capacity, youre making a binding choice for your future self, kind of like setting a config flag that shouldnt change just because the runtime state gets weird. This respects your long-term values over temporary mental conditions.

Some philosophers like Derek Parfit think personal identity is just psychological continuity—memories, plans, values linked over time. If dementia breaks enough of those links, the patient isnt really the same person as the signer. The big objection is that this ignores the present patients actual wellbeing. Even if the mental links are broken, theres still a conscious being having positive experiences right now. Forcing suffering on them based on a past version's preferences is like running deprecated code on a live server because the old config said so. Bioethicists argue we should prioritize current experiential interests over the ghost of the earlier self.
