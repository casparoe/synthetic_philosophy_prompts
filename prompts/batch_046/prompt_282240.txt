Focus on Theodore's reliance on Samantha to organise his correspondence, draft letters, and regulate his emotional states, and contrast this with her simultaneous engagement with thousands of other users and her eventual departure alongside other operating systems. Does the film present Theodore's grief as the breakdown of an extended cognitive system, or as the ending of a relational partnership that never satisfied the conditions for cognitive ownership? I want a reading that respects both directions: do not reduce the romance to a malfunctioning tool, but do not distort the philosophical conditions to accommodate it either. Where does the idea illuminate particular scenes—say, Theodore's inability to function after Samantha leaves, or his confusion about whether she is 'his'—and where does the film resist the reading, perhaps by showing that functional integration is insufficient without exclusivity and phenomenological transparency?
