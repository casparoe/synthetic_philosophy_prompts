case 1: a climate scientist spends years in obscurity testing a fringe hypothesis about carbon feedback loops, no media coverage, no tenure boost, just becuase she thinks it's important for the model. my gut: purely goal-directed, and that's exactly why it's admirable.

case 2: same scientist, but she only started the project because her phd advisor said it was important and she wants his posthumous approval. she still thinks it's true now, but the origin was approval-seeking. my gut: still somewhat tainted? or does the current motivation wash it?

case 3: a whistleblower leaks docs knowing she'll be fired, shunned, maybe jailed. she does it because the fraud is wrong. clear goal-directed.
