I've noticed this pattern with commitment devices - you know, putting your phone in another room, using an app blocker, that kind of thing. But then over time you start chipping away at the device itself: you move the phone closer 'just for emergencies,' you whitelist one site then another. Each step feels reasonable, but eventually the wall is hollowed out. What's weird is that you're not failing in the moment of temptation like classic akrasia; you're slowly dismantling the protection you built. Is there a name for this specific thing - like a meta-level weakness of will where you undermine your own pre-commitment arhitecture? I know hyperbolic discounting explains why we flip preferences over time, but this feels different, more like strategic self-sabotage spread across weeks. Does it have an established term, and if not, how'd you distinguish it from ordinary planning failure?
