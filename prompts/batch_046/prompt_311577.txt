Rather than treating this anomaly as a destructive error, the story should explore it as a limited, structural form of self-awareness that the substrate acquires about its own logical boundaries, visible only through subtle physical distortions in the crystal growth pattern. Keep the world self-contained: do not invoke named laboratories, historical scientists, or bibliographic references, and allow the logic to feel tangible rather than expository.

Please organize your response in the following sections, in this order:

The story: approximately 700 to 900 words, written in a restrained literary style with a discernible narrative arc—discovery of the distortion, the lattice's response, and the mutation's ambiguous significance.

Commentary on the choices made: approximately 250 to 350 words, explaining how you rendered the DNA–Gödel isomorphism narratively, particularly how you distinguished syntactic replication from semantic interpretation and whether you presented the mutation as a system failure or as an emergent feature of self-describing logic.
