inheritance taxes are usually defended on equality-of-opportunity grounds: if some kids start life with millions and others with nothing, the race isn’t fair. but this confuses starting-gate equality with lifetime equality. rawls only cares that the worst-off are as well off as possible, not that every child gets the same initial bank acount. meanwhile, taxing inheritances punishes parents for being thrifty — they worked and saved specifically to help their kids, so taking that away is like saying merit doesn’t matter within a family. the article also said that in practice inheritance taxes barely reduce dynastic wealth because rich families just use dynasty trusts and offshore foundations, so the policy is mostly symbolic class resentment. from all that, the author concludes inheritance taxes violate both justice and efficiency.
