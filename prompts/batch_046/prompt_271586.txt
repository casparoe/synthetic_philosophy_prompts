This lets me preserve determinism in general relativity without embracing full relationalism. I need an honest map of this position's vulnerabilities. Rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, indicate which ones a defender of this view should concede as damaging, and assess whether the position risks being insulated by ad hoc qualifications—if so, whether that undermines its value as a metaphysical proposal.
