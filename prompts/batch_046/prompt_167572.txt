Could you lay out the distinct precise theses this one-liner could actually be expressing, as a menu rather than a single interpretation? Please flag which readings are trivial, which are substantive and defensible, and which are false, and tell me which reading is typically being invoked when someone uses Schlick's distinction to end an argument about whether technical work can be meaningful.
