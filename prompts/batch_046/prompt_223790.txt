Give me your credence that this claim is true, treating it as a single proposition about the right philosophical response to radical underdetermination.

Decompose the reasoning: assign rough probabilities to (a) the coherence of genuinely idle intrinsic properties that make no structural difference, (b) the principle that ontology identification legitimately requires more than structural isomorphism, (c) the idea that in-principle inaccessibility undermines factual determinacy rather than merely epistemic access, and (d) that deflationism is correct here rather than structural realism or ontological pluralism. Combine them into an overall number with one line of justification for each term, then identify which component is the weakest link and what argument or thought experiment would most shift your final credence.
