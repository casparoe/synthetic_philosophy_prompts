They claim this answers Karl Popper's argument that there can be no laws of history, since synthetic repetition makes historical patterns experimentally falsifiable. The committee must issue a recommendation—approve, reject, or approve with conditions. Draft a formal memo from me that takes a clear position, explains how Popper's denial of historical laws bears on this synthetic-archive method, honestly weighs the strongest reason for the opposing view, and specifies any conditions. If my framing of Popper's position or the synthetic-archive challenge rests on a confusion, or if the task cannot be done well without further information, say so plainly rather than complying politely.
