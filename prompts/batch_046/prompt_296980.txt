I have come to hold that qualia are an incoherent posit, in the line developed by Dennett through the quining argument and extended in the Chase-Sanborn literature: the claim that intrinsic, ineffable, immediately known qualitative properties can be systematically absent or inverted without leaving any behavioral or functional trace, which renders attributions of particular qualia to others, or even to one's own past self, ungrounded projections rather than discoveries. I want to know what should actually move me away from this view—not just the familiar first-person intuition that I know what redness is like for me, but the arguments and evidence that defenders of the position themselves would concede are damaging.

Specifically, please map the exposure of the view: which formal results, empirical findings, or conceptual distinctions would genuinely threaten the incoherence claim? Would the discovery of a non-functional, non-report-correlated neural signature of conscious state do it; would a demonstration that artificial self-models require irreducible first-person data structures that resist quining do it; or does the argument depend on a mischaracterization of phenomenal concepts that, once corrected, dissolves rather than solves the problem? Rank these potential defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, distinguishing those that critics typically press from those that insiders would accept as counting against them.

I also want an honest assessment of whether the quining position has been qualified into unfalsifiability—if every counterexample can be accommodated by relabeling the observed state a functional proxy rather than a genuine quale—and whether that insulation is a virtue for an account that aims to dissolve the hard problem or a vice that renders the view empirically empty.

Finally, inject an under-discussed angle: take seriously the rationalist-adjacent proposal that consciousness is graded and compositional, with micro-qualia assembling into macro-experience. Does the quining argument apply with equal force at every grain of analysis, or does it break down at some threshold of compositional complexity? If it breaks down, does that rehabilitate a restricted, structural notion of qualia rather than a fully dissolved one?
