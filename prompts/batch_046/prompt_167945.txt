At a seminar, two participants—call them A and B—disputed what Descartes's cogito actually proves. A opened by claiming that the move from the fact of thinking to the fact of existing establishes only that a thinking event occurs, not that a substantial self exists; she argued that the first-person pronoun functions grammatically like the dummy subject in expressions about weather, and that Descartes illicitly treats it as naming an enduring substance. B replied that even an indexical requires a referent at the moment of utterance, so the cogito proves at least that something exists to do the thinking, though it remains silent on whether that thing is a soul, body, or persistent substance. A countered that thinking might be an impersonal mode—something like being-thought-with-reflexivity-built-into-the-event-itself, analogous to a system-state loop that references its own state without an owner; she added that Descartes never excludes the possibility that thinking inheres in a non-substantial process or occurs without any bearer beyond its own occurrence. B responded that process is ontologically parasitic on a bearer—running requires a runner—and that reflexive awareness requires a subject of awareness, even if only a minimal, momentary one; hence the cogito proves a thin bearer exists, not merely an occurrence. A then objected that B's appeal to inherent reflexivity merely renames the conclusion, noting that a computer program can reference its own state without being a substance, so B has shown nothing thicker than a self-referential event. B concluded that even a self-referential event needs an existent substrate in which to occur, and that Descartes's minimal claim—that there is an existent thinker whenever there is thinking—is therefore secure, whatever else about the self remains unknown.

Please adjudicate the exchange move by move: rate each of the six moves from clearly mistaken to clearly right, with brief justification for each rating. Then identify what each party gets right, what—if anything—both miss about the scope of the cogito, and who is more right overall.
