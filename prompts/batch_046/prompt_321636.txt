please argue that this recursive value calibration is the only variety worth wanting, and that both libertarian agent-causation and standard frankfurt-style compatibilism fail to deliver it. build it from the ground up — no need to cite specific papers, just reasoning. then tackle the obvious collapse objection: if determinism holds all the way down through the value-change mechanism itself, then this is just another deterministic loop with extra steps, so either show why that doesn't kill the framing or admit it should and defend why we should reject the idea anyway.
