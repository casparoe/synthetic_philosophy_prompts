Please construct and defend the following position concerning Nāgārjuna's tetralemma (catuṣkoṭi) and Madhyamaka emptiness.

Position: The four corners of the tetralemma are best interpreted not as four jointly exhaustive logical possibilities applied to one proposition, but as four successive stages in the dialectical exhaustion of ontological commitment regarding any putative svabhāva, or intrinsic nature. In this sequence, the first corner asserts svabhāva; the second denies it by asserting emptiness; the third attempts a synthetic conjunction of both; and the fourth—the denial of both emptiness and non-emptiness—functions not as a transcendent mystical thesis beyond language, but as a performative indication that propositional fixation itself has collapsed. It operates as a pragmatic reductio rather than a substantive metaphysical claim.

From this sequential-dialectical reading, defend two further claims. First, that the doctrine of emptiness of emptiness avoids self-refutation not by reifying emptiness in the second corner, but by treating the second negation—emptiness itself is not empty—as a purely therapeutic correction that prevents the interlocutor from hypostatizing emptiness as a new svabhāva. Second, that this interpretation allows Madhyamaka to maintain that conventional truths possess functional efficacy without attributing to them any minimal ontological weight or thin being, thereby blocking both eternalist and annihilationist readings of emptiness.

In your argument, address this specific tension: if the fourth corner is genuinely non-propositional, then any philosophical statement of it—including the present framing—seems self-undermining. Explain how the sequential reading resolves this tension without appealing to a hidden ultimate truth that functions as a metaphysical ground. Be explicit about which premises you treat as interpretive claims about Nāgārjuna's texts, such as the Mūlamadhyamakakārikā, and which are philosophical extensions of his framework.

Finally, please state how confident you are in the claims you advance, and identify the single point about which you are least certain.
