Consider, on one side, the view that understanding requires an internalised cognitive architecture for counterfactual manipulation, drawing on the idea of cognitive integration rather than mere belief-formation; on the other side, the view that understanding can be distributed across human-machine systems without loss, provided the human retains appropriate epistemic access and oversight.

Reach and justify a clear conclusion. Do not include a bibliography, reading list, or citations to specific authors; I need an argument that stands on its own reasoning. Please use British English throughout (e.g., behaviour, analyse, labour).
