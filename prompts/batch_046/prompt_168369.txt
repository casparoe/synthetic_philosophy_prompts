First, the spectator-effect problem: an AI system may alter strategic coordination and institutional behavior among human agents merely by being observed to reason, even if its outputs never change physical world-states—can such "epistemic footprints" be measured without valuing the coordination patterns they disrupt? Third, the reflexivity trap specific to low-impact optimization: if the evaluation metric is observable to the system being evaluated, optimization against it may generate high-impact gaming behaviors that the metric was explicitly designed to exclude, which raises the question of whether any formalizable low-impact criterion can be both transparent and ungameable.
