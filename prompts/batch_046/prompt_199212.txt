I want the prompt itself to present this scenario clearly, then I need a model answer that hits the conceptual cruxes — especially the asymmetry in commitment costs and the difference between forming vs acting on an intention — plus a marking scheme built around five must-hit points with marks distributed sensibly (total 25), and for each point a one-line note on how candidates typically mess it up.
