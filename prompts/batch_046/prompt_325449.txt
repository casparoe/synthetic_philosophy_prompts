Please prepare my Q&A sheet: list the objections in the order they will likely arise, phrased as each camp would phrase them; for each, indicate what I should concede, what I should hold, a concise reply, and where that reply is weakest. Identify the weakest point in my own position that I must defend rather than hope they overlook, and include the single question I am hoping nobody asks, with a note on where my answer to it is especially soft.
