I've been thinking about those four kinds of minds people talk about — Darwinian (just hardwired instincts), Skinnerian (trial and error learning), Popperian (can imagine outcomes before acting), and Gregorian (uses language, tools, and culture to think outside the brain). I think Gregorian minds are acutally just Popperian ones with external storage — culture isn't a different kind of creature, it's just mental modeling spread across objects and other people. What would genuinely count against that? I'm not asking for "humans are special" hand-waving. I want the real defeaters: cases where a Gregorian creature clearly does something that can't be broken down into internal modeling plus props, or results that show the four-layer taxonomy collapses if Gregorian gets folded back into Popperian. Which of these would be fatal versus just awkward? And what would a defender of the four-layer view themselves admit is the weakest point?
