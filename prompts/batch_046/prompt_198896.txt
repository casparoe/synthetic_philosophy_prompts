Maya then raised a pragmatic self-refutation specific to desire-elimination: removing all desire-talk undermines the very motivation driving philosophical argument, including Jonas’s own drive to defend eliminativism. Jonas dismissed this as pragmatic rather than metaphysical—the “engine” is homeostatic circuitry, and our experience of it as wanting is precisely the folk-psychological illusion to be discarded.
