3. The Bayesian marketplace-of-ideas model, treating distributed belief aggregation as inference over shared priors.
4. Structured adversarial collaboration with formalized red-teaming protocols and explicit convergence rules.
5. A proposed deliberative mesh in which agents maintain partially overlapping but non-identical ontologies, exchanging inferences across boundary conditions rather than within a single shared conceptual framework.
