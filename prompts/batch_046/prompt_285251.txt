I am preparing a 75-minute workshop for about 14 colleagues—mostly engineers and product managers, none with philosophy training—in my company’s learning space. The rules should embed the reference-class problem organically—participants should discover that whether they include beta builds, forked repositories, or legacy maintenance branches in their reference class radically changes the prediction, and that there is no non-arbitrary internal criterion to settle the boundary.

Please design the following:

5. An honest note on which feature of the philosophical argument (for example, probabilistic updating over unbounded future populations, or the anthropic framing of self-location) the simulation structurally cannot reproduce and must be supplied by the facilitator.
