Please draft: (1) the exam question itself, calibrated for a 90-minute response; (2) a model answer of approximately 700 words that demonstrates rigorous application of the distinction to two figures, explains where the defence succeeds and where it collapses, and addresses what the distinction implies for the ethics of retrospective judgment; and (3) a marking scheme comprising five must-hit points totaling 25 marks, with each point assigned a specific weight, and a one-line note describing the specific conceptual error or omission that typically costs candidates marks on that point.
