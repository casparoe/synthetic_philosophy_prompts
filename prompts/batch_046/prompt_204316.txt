I think I get the basic split, but it feels like there's a hidden middle ground nobody talks about, especially when the human can't actually see what's happening inside the machine.

They should address whether approval can be defined over long trajectories rather than single steps, and whether a goal-directed agent inevitably drifts into behaviors that look like approval-seeking anyway. Don't end with a vague both-sides-have-points conclusion—push them toward an actual verdict about where the real disagreement lies.
