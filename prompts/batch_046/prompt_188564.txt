Make sure these show up: pure citizenship/nationality view, all-affected-interests, coercion-only, and a graded ecological membership view. Flag branches that look incoherent or just empty. No bibliographies or reading lists, please—this is for people reasoning from the cases. Also tell me at the end what argument or empirical finding would change your mind about where you draw the lines—like if graded membership turned out to always collapse into equality, or if affected-interests logic really does force including non-human animals.
