What is your credence that this contradiction is genuine and cannot be dissolved by appealing to branching possibilities, counterfactual dependence, or a distinction between causal and logical necessity? Combine these into an overall number, then tell me which of the three terms you are least certain about and what specific argument or revised version of the story would most likely change your mind.
