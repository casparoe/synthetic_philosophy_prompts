Please rank these four frameworks in tiers according to how reliably they separate actual corruption from benign cultural practice in bureaucracies that depend on personal networks, using concrete contexts such as seasonal gift obligations within state-business relations or community-level patronage where formal rules are weak. Give a sentence of justification for each tier.
