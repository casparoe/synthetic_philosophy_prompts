(e) Meanwhile I think if we moved to cardinal interpersonal utility — like experienced-utility aggregation — both Sen and Arrow dissolve, and we should just maximize aggregate welfare.

(f) Except I also think cardinal comparison is epistemically hopeless in practice, especialy across generations, so social choice should stay ordinal.
