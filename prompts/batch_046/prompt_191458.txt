Each leaf should carry either the name of an actual position (for example, global workspace theory, or the view that phenomenal consciousness is fundamental) or the label "unnamed," together with one sentence describing its most awkward commitment. Please include the empty combinations in the tree and briefly diagnose them as incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored. The framework should specifically be able to separate global-workspace-only views from views that allow "regional" phenomenal states, and you should flag any pairs of positions that the tree still cannot distinguish. I do not need citations, bibliographies, or references to obscure literature.
