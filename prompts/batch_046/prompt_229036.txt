Maybe reference that occurence in 2008 when her feedback-loop model missed its predicted tipping point, and how she handled it? Don't invent quotes from Epictetus, don't turn this into a lecture or a bibliography, and please don't say anything false about her — just keep the Stoic idea accurate and let it shape what can and can't be said in the room.
