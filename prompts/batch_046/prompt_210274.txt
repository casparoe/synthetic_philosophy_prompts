Do these hang together? But maybe (b) and (c) only look tense—perhaps Chesterton allows removal once you see the reason was bad. Can you audit the set? Rank the real tensions by severity, flag which ones dissolve on inspection, and say what the least costly revision is if I want to keep the core Burkean intuition intact.
