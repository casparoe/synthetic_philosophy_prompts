On the direct branch, split by logical relation: deterministic law, Weberian elective affinity, or constitutive/interpretive meaning. Flag empty leaves: deterministic value-causation should probably stay empty since Weber rejected determinism, and say if that's incoherent or just unattractive. Add a reverse-causation branch: does capitalist practice generate the ethic? Named leaves should include hard materialism, classic Weberian elective affinity, and an institutional-mediation view; other combos can be unnamed. Crucially inject one distinction that gets flattened in textbooks — I'm thinking the carrier problem (values need embodied carriers vs floating free) or the conscious-motivation vs unconscious-disposition split in Weber's idea of vocation. Note pairs the tree can't cleanly seperate; I suspect weak elective affinity and interpretive constitutivism might collapse into each other. About six or seven nodes total, every split phrased as a question someone could answer without knowing the label. For each leaf give the name or 'unnamed' plus one sentence on its most awkward commitment. No bibliographies please — I need this to be buildable by a model with no search access.
