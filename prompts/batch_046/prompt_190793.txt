Illusionism, as Frankish defends it, says phenomenal consciousness doesn't actually exist. Instead our brains build an introspective model that represents mental states as having qualitative feels — redness, pain, etc — but these represented properties are fictional, like properties of a hologram. So when someone reports that it hurts they're just describing the content of that model, not describing a real phenomenal property. This solves the hard problem because there's no real quality needing explanation, only a representational mistake.

So illusionism secretly presupposes the very thing it denies.
