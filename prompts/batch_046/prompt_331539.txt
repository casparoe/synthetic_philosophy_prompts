I sit on the ontology review board for our uni's internal AI knowledge-base project — we're not a company, just a cross-department thing building shared reference data for research tools, and honestly the meetings run too long. We're locking the schema this month and there's a real split over how to store identity statements. One side wants every co-reference link tagged with its discovery mode — measurement, definition, observational tracking, whatever — because they argue that Hesperus is Phosphorus and Hesperus is Hesperus don't do the same work for a user querying the system, and hiding that difference flattens useful epistemic structure.
