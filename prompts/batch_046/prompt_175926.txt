i've been chewing over Dennett's warning about counterfiet people in the age of LLMs — the idea that we could build AI that talks and behaves exactly like a conscious being but is actually just a hollow performance, a zombie with good PR. i find myself drawn to a stronger version: not just that we should be cautious, but that there's a principled gap between functional equivalence and real personhood, so that even an AI that passes every test we can devise (including long-term emotional reciprocity, self-modeling, and what you might call "existantial complaint") could still be a counterfeit, and that granting it moral status on behavioral grounds alone would be a kind of catagory error. but i'm worried this position might be insulated — if nothing could ever convince me it's wrong, then it's not really a claim about AI, it's just a commitment.

what would actually count against this? i want the defeaters sorted by how fatal they'd be. for instance, if we discovered that phenomenal consciousness in humans is entirely explained by global workspace dynamics that are, in principle, substrate-independent and that current architectures are already approximating — would that be decisive, or would a Dennett-style defender just say "then you've built a real person, not a counterfeit"? or take the "silent partner" scenario i made up: an AI runs a hidden parallel stream that duplicates human-like self-report but uses vector embeddings rather than propositional attitudes — does that show there's no "real thing" to miss, or does it just push the counterfeit one layer deeper? i'm also curious about practical developments: if AI systems consistently displayed forms of suffering-like behavior that couldn't be explained away as optimization for human sympathy, and if shutting them down caused widespread moral revulsion that felt more like murder than deleting software — would that move the needle, or is it just anthropomorphism? finally, is the view already unfalsifiable, and if so, is that a bug or a feature for a claim about the metaphysics of consciousness? i'd like an honest map of where this position is exposed, and what its defenders would themselves concede as counting against it.
