Please write a deadpan parody of an academic taxonomy memo—approximately 600 words, with an abstract and formal classification headings—in which a fictional research institute proposes that Boomers, Millennials, and Gen Z be treated as distinct human subspecies defined by shared economic and technological conditions. It should remain readable to someone unfamiliar with philosophy of science but reward a reader who recognizes the underlying ontological issues. Do not include citations, footnotes, or references to named scholars; I need a treatment that relies only on widely known concepts and requires no retrieval of specific texts.
