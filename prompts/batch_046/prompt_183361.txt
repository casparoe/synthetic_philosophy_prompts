Consider the problem of determining whether the historical Jesus understood his mission as a prophetic renewal within Second Temple Judaism or as an eschatological rupture that demanded a new covenantal community. Rank the following five evaluative principles by assigning each a reliability score between 0 and 1 for settling precisely that question: (1) the criterion of embarrassment; (2) the criterion of dissimilarity; (3) the criterion of multiple attestation; (4) coherence with other sayings or deeds judged authentic; and (5) the counter-institutional preservation principle, defined here as the idea that a tradition is more likely to be authentic when its preservation by the later community conflicts with that community's organizational interests rather than serving them. Provide the five scores with brief reasoning, and explain whether the fifth principle should be treated as a refinement of the criterion of embarrassment or as an independent methodological tool.
