The person doesn't flip a coin, and they don't stay frozen either. Same with menus: two identical dishes except one is listed first, and suddenly "I just like the top one" feels genuinely true. The key part is that they seem to need to invent a preference out of pure noise just to break the deadlock; its not just rationalizing after the fact.
