In evolutionary ethics, normative reasons are clearly cranes: they emerge through genetic cooperation, then social norms, then institutional rules. But Dennett actually thinks normative reasons are skyhooks, since downward causation violates physical closure. So greedy reductionism saves us by showing norms are just causal patterns with no independent reality. Thus the crane model supports emergence, but Dennett uses reductionism to deny norms any real status."
