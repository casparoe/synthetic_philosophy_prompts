Alfred North Whitehead warned that education must avoid inert ideas—facts received but never tested or woven into active understanding—and described learning as a rhythmic cycle of romance, precision, and generalization. Imagine he is asked in 2025 to evaluate a proposed universal pedagogical AI that answers every student query instantly, generates essays on demand, and continuously adapts to the learner's emotional state; its designers claim this removes inertia by making knowledge always immediately applied. Drawing specifically on Whitehead's distinction between mental discipline and mere information accumulation, and on his view that education should cultivate imaginative contemplation of possibility, predict his assessment: would he regard this system as completing the educational rhythm, or as generating a subtler form of inertness in which the student never truly possesses an idea? Write for an audience familiar with Whitehead's essays on education but divided on the value of AI tutors.
