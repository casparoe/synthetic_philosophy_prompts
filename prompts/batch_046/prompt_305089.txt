At lunch Maya and Leo got into it about Wittgenstein's hinge stuff from On Certainty, specifically whether hinges can ever actually be doubted or just replaced wholesale. Maya said the whole point is that propositions like the earth having existed for a long time or physical laws not randomly failing aren't hypotheses you test; they're the riverbed that makes testing possible. Leo said that's just dogma with fancy branding — in science we replace hinges all the time, like absolute space or determinism, so either Wittgenstein is wrong that they can't be doubted, or hinge just means whatever we currently refuse to question. Maya replied that Leo confuses doubting within a framework with changing the framework itself. When Einstein dropped absolute space he wasn't falsifying it like a bad prediction; he was shifting the riverbed. The old hinge didn't get disproven, it got relocated. Leo pushed back with a specific case: suppose we found carbon dating was systematically wrong because of some unknown cosmic-ray fluctuation that messed with decay rates everywhere. Wouldn't that be genuinely doubting the hinge that physical processes are locally consistent? Maya said no — you'd first check instruments, math, calibration. You'd hold fast to local consistency and try to save it with a new mechanism. Only if every local explanation collapsed might you revise something deeper, but then you're not doubting one hinge, you're rebuilding the whole riverbed. Leo said that's unfalsifiable: if any counterexample gets reclassified as riverbed shift, then saying hinges are immune to doubt is tautological. What's the real difference between this hinge was doubted and this hinge was replaced? Without a criterion it's empty. Maya answered that the criterion is functional: a hinge is what makes doubt possible in the first place. If you genuinely doubted that physical laws hold locally, you wouldn't have a langauge-game in which to even formulate the cosmic-ray hypothesis. So it's not tautological, it's structural — you can see it in the fact that every proposed revision still relies on other hinges standing fast. They turned to me and asked me to referee since I was just sitting there. Please explain who's right about each specific point they traded, say what if anything both of them are missing, and tell me who is more right overall.
