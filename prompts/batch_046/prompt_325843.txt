Write a dialogue between Mara and Lee arguing over which variety of free will is actually worth wanting. Mara defends something she calls mesh autonomy — basically hierarchical endorsement but with a twist: she thinks the endorsement only counts if it passes through a reflective bottleneck where long-term values actively filter out impulsive ones, not just rubber-stamp them. Lee thinks thats determinism dressed up. He defends narrative resilience — the idea that free will is worth wanting when you can maintain ownership of your actions in a continuous self-story that survives serious moral failure, and he insists this needs genuine alternative possibilities at the moment of choice, not just post-hoc coherence. They should actually respond to each other: Mara needs to press Lee on how his view survives fixed brain chemistry, and Lee should ask Mara whether her bottleneck is just another deterministic filter. Neither should be a strawman. Keep it casual, like thier talking in a grad lounge, and maybe include a typo or two.
