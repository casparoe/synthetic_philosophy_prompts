I am preparing a comparative-philosophy essay that reads Ingmar Bergman’s The Seventh Seal through Zhuangzi’s philosophy of death, and I want to avoid both reducing the film to a Daoist parable and distorting Zhuangzi to fit Bergman’s Christian-existentialist frame. The knight Antonius Block’s chess match with Death, his terror of annihilation, and his final protective gesture toward the juggler family seem to embody precisely the kind of anxious attachment to life and heroic agency that Zhuangzi undermines in the parable of the butterfly dream and in his laughter at his wife’s funeral, where death is treated as a continuous transformation rather than a catastrophic loss. At the same time, the film’s stark landscapes, the plague, the flagellants, and the silence of God constitute a theodicy and an ontology of absence that have no clear analogue in Zhuangzi’s naturalistic cosmology.

Where, specifically, does the film’s visual and narrative grammar—Block’s desperate search for one meaningful act, his squire Jöns’s earthy pragmatism, the dance of death in the final scene—resonate with Zhuangzi’s refusal to mourn or to rank life over death? And where does Block’s terror and his need to save the family resist that reading so thoroughly that an honest critic must say the film is arguing against Zhuangzi rather than illustrating him? I am especially interested in whether Block’s final act can be interpreted through Zhuangzi’s notion of engaging with the world without being instrumentalized by it—using things without being used by them—or whether that reading illegitimately imports a Confucian or Christian ethics of purposeful agency onto a philosophy that rejects attachment to outcomes. The seminar expects philosophical precision rather than film-studies jargon, so I would like an answer that keeps both the cinematic details and the conceptual structure of the Zhuangzi in view without collapsing either into the other, and that concludes by saying what the film adds to the philosophical debate about mortality that Zhuangzi’s arguments alone do not capture.
