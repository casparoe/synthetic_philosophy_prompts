Alex started with the usual line: stepping into the scanner is basically dying, since the copy on Mars is just a perfect replica with false memories, not *you*. Jordan pushed back: she said thats just spatiotemporal prejudice. Alex said Parfit should have said uniqueness is part of relation R, but he chickened out. Jordan said adding uniqueness makes the view collapse back into identity, which defeats the whole point.
