I've developed a modified Gricean position on implicature that I think avoids some standard overgeneration problems but may create others. On my view, speaker meaning for an implicature requires not just the standard Gricean conditions—communicative intention plus mutual awareness of that intention and its recognition—but also an epistemic dominance clause: the speaker must hold a justified second-order belief that the hearer can recover the intended content via reasoning that is available within the shared conversational background, rather than via idiosyncratic contextual leaps. This is meant to distinguish genuine implicatures from accidental suggestions that happen to be recoverable only by unusually perceptive hearers. What would genuinely count against this position?

I want a ranked map of its exposure, from fatal to merely awkward. First, are there formal results in pragmatics—perhaps from dynamic semantics or game-theoretic models of communication—showing that recovery conditions are necessarily underspecified, so that adding a dominance clause either renders all successful implicatures impossible or collapses the clause into a trivial success condition? Second, which ordinary-language cases provide the strongest intuition that we hold speakers responsible for implicatures they could not have expected anyone but the most astute hearer to recover—cases that would either falsify my clause or force me to bite an unpalatable bullet? Third, how do rival frameworks—relevance-theoretic accounts, post-Gricean intentionalist models, or even standard Griceanism augmented with cancellability—explain the same filtering data without invoking second-order epistemic conditions? Fourth, does the view risk circularity if the content of the shared background is itself partly determined by prior implicatures?

I'd also like an honest assessment of whether committed defenders of standard Griceanism or relevance theory would concede anything here as genuinely damaging, versus dismissing the dominance clause as either redundant or mischaracterizing ordinary communicative practice. And if the position turns out to be well insulated—if every apparent counterexample can be absorbed by refining the notion of justified second-order belief—say whether that insulation is a theoretical virtue or a sign that the view has been qualified into unfalsifiability.
