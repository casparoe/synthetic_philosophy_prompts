Maintain sustained irony without winking; a reader should recognize that the bureaucratic form is being used to expose the limitations of reducing norms to pure rational coordination—particularly the neglect of power, history, and moral content in convention formation—while remaining readable to someone unfamiliar with the philosophy. Do not include citations, bibliographies, reading lists, page references, or verbatim quotations from any published work; if you mention Lewis, do so by name only, without attributing specific passages to him.
