This is not a skyhook, since nothing is mysteriously suspended from above; yet it also differs from a pure crane, because part of the explanatory labor is being performed by structure that the reduced system itself does not contain or maintain.

Without this intermediate category, you should show that we are forced either to deny that normative judgment can be explained at all, or to treat its emergence as inexplicably self-generating.
