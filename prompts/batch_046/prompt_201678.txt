Please explain the conceptual tension that arises when dialetheism is implemented not globally but locally, through a "paraconsistent compartment" embedded within an otherwise classical logical framework. In this model, contradictions are quarantined to a designated "truth-glut zone"—for instance, semantic paradoxes generated by self-reference—while reasoning outside this zone remains fully explosive. Is this quarantine strategy genuinely dialetheist in the metaphysical sense, according to which some contradictions are literally true? Or does it collapse into an epistemic view, on which contradictions merely mark the boundaries of our cognitive or representational access? In addressing this, explain whether the compartmentalization requires a meta-language distinction between being "true-in-the-zone" and being "true-simpliciter," and discuss what that distinction implies for the dialetheist's rejection of the principle of non-contradiction as a universal constraint.
