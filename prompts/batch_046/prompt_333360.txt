If Everettian quantum mechanics is fully deterministic—every branch corresponding to a possible measurement outcome is equally actualized—then attributing specific probabilities via the Born rule appears conceptually contradictory: there is no single future that occurs with a given frequency, only a tree of certainties. How can a deterministic branching ontology accommodate probabilistic predictions without either treating the |ψ|² weights as merely epistemic or collapsing into a uniform counting measure across branches? Clarify the conceptual source of the tension.
