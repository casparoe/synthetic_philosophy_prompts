I just finished a long, somewhat heated discussion with a close friend about Schopenhauer’s philosophy of music, and I want to settle the matter properly in writing. My friend insists that Schopenhauer’s claim—that music is not a copy of Ideas, like painting or sculpture, but rather a direct objectification of the Will itself—cannot be squared with the rest of his system in The World as Will and Representation. After all, Schopenhauer insists that the Will is never an object of direct knowledge but is only known indirectly through its phenomenal objectifications, and yet music is composed entirely of sounds arranged in time, which are clearly representations subject to the principle of sufficient reason. If music is genuinely phenomenal, my friend argues, it must be mediated by Ideas just like every other art; if it is not phenomenal, then Schopenhauer owes us an account of how we can hear something that lies outside representation altogether.

I defended Schopenhauer by pointing out that he treats melody and harmony as analogues to the inner movements of the Will—striving, satisfaction, renewed striving—without being representations of particular Ideas. But my friend countered that this only describes music’s emotional effect, not its metaphysical status, and that treating emotional correspondence as direct ontological access smuggles in a kind of mystical intuitionism that Schopenhauer’s critical epistemology should reject.

Write an essay addressing this specific tension. Weigh the strongest version of the objection—that Schopenhauer’s music theory violates his own representationalist framework—against the best defense available within his system. In particular, consider whether Schopenhauer can consistently maintain that music is both (a) fully within the phenomenal realm of sound and time and (b) an unmediated copy of the noumenal Will. Do not end with a non-committal summary; defend a verdict. If you think Schopenhauer’s position is ultimately incoherent, say so and explain what revision—if any—could save it without abandoning his core metaphysics. If you think it is coherent, explain exactly how the representational form of music avoids mediating the Will through Ideas, and address the silent-music objection: if the same structural patterns could be experienced through direct neural stimulation without acoustic vibration, would that strengthen or weaken Schopenhauer’s claim?
