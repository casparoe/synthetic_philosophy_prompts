I want to explore whether the dialectic can be reconstructed as a model of structural epistemic asymmetry: the slave's formative labour generates practical knowledge that remains structurally inaccessible to the master, allowing the relation to stabilise through cognitive division rather than reciprocal recognition. Please write the proposal with the following elements: (a) a precise formulation of the research question that clearly distinguishes this asymmetry reading from the standard recognition-based account; (b) an explanation of why existing interpretations—whether intersubjective, social-ontological, or political—leave open whether recognition is the only possible stabilising mechanism; (c) a concrete methodological plan, set out week by week across the twelve-week period, describing the conceptual reconstruction, the comparative analysis with alternative readings, and the construction of a minimal formal model of the asymmetry; (d) a clear statement of what a successful outcome would entail, for instance a coherent account of the dialectic's resolution that does not invoke mutual recognition, and what would count as failure, for instance if the asymmetry collapses into hidden reciprocity or proves conceptually incoherent without the teleological framework; (e) a realistic scope assessment that addresses the risk that removing the teleological dimension renders the interpretation unrecognisably Hegelian, and that explains why the project remains feasible within the fellowship period. The proposal should argue that the work is worth doing and can be done. Do not request a bibliography, literature review by citation, or reading list; I will add all references myself. Write in polished British English.
