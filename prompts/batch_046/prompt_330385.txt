(1) Hofstadter shows that generic "man" doesn't just name a gender-neutral class; it activates a male cognitive default — people literally picture a guy.
(2) If you swap in "person" or "they", you erase the linguistic marker, but the underlying mental prototype stays male because the culture hasn't changed.
(3) So real neutrality requires restructuring the conceptual prototypes themselves, not just the vocabulary.
(4) But every social category — doctor, criminal, leader — is organized around some prototype; that's just how human cognition works, you can't think in pure abstractions.
(5) So if you eliminate gendered prototypes, you either leave a vacuum that gets filled by the old default anyway, or you have to strip away all prototype structure and think in purely formal, empty boxes.
(6) Therefore language reform either does nothing to the hidden bias, or it forces us into a kind of conceptual nihilism where categories lose all content.
(7) Which means Hofstadter's satire, if taken seriously, proves that sexist defaults are ineliminable or that meaningful thought is impossible.
