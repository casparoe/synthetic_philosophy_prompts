Make an argument that satisficing is rationaly mandatory, not just a cognitive shortcut. The key move is the reflective ceiling: once you include the cost of deciding how to decide inside the problem itself, trying to optimize over your own reasoning just pushes the problem up a level. Either you stop at some arbitrary but functional threshold — that is, you satisfice — or you never act. So bounded rationality isnt a limitation we suffer, its the structural fix that keeps practical reason from eating itself.
