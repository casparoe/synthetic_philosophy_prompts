We have to reccomend approve-as-is, approve-with-a-mandatory-context-panel, or reject outright, with a concrete time frame for implementation, and the mayor wants the memo by Friday. Draft the board's decision memo picking one course with clear reasoning, including an honest treatment of the strongest consideration on the losing side, and format it as exactly five numbered points, each one or two sentences long, with no introduction or conclusion. I was arguing about this with a friend last night and I'm genuinely unsure whether the idea that revolutions devour their own children even holds up outside of metaphor, so please don't just hand me a party line—make the reasoning something the losing side could actually respond to.
