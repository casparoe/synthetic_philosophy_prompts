Is there a name for this? In debates about path dependance -- whether history gets locked in or the same outcomes would have arrived anyway -- I keep seeing people do this specific toggle. They'll trace some modern institution back through a fragile chain of accidents to show it's contingent and special; then, for an institution they think is bad, they'll say structural forces made it inevitable and the specific path was just window dressing. I saw it with patent law versus bureacratic administration, but it feels like a broader pattern. Is there an established concept for this exact asymmetry, and if not can you coin one and draw the edges? I want to know what it's not -- like, is it different from motivated reasoning or hindsight bias, and does it only apply when people do it deliberately or also when they're just not paying attention?
