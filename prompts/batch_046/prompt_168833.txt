Write a deadpan parody of a corporate recruitment brochure and internal policy charter for a fictional consultancy, “The Epistemic Chain LLP”, which has adopted radical infinitism as its sole operating philosophy. The brochure should be composed in polished, bureaucratic British English, running to approximately 650 words, and structured in numbered sections: (1) Our Foundational Rejection — why the firm forbids basic beliefs and treats any claim to immediate justification as gross misconduct; (2) The Circularity Ban — why coherentist reasoning, however mutually supportive, is classified as a disciplinary offence because it closes the loop prematurely; (3) The Infinite Audit Protocol — how employees must document an unending, non-repeating chain of reasons for every decision, with practical illustrations such as expense claims, meeting agendas, and client recommendations; and (4) Client Advisory Note — a sidebar warning clients that no recommendation is ever fully justified, only the present link in an indefinitely extendable chain. The satire should exaggerate real features of infinitism — its denial of basic beliefs, its rejection of circular coherence, and its demand for an infinite non-repeating series of reasons — without inventing philosophical positions that do not exist in the literature. Inject one fictional corporate mechanism, such as “justification escrow accounts” or “epistemic relay contracts”, to render the practical absurdity concrete. The target is the institutional impracticality of pure infinitism, not any named philosopher. By the end, a reader acquainted with the regress problem should recognise that the author grasps the distinctions between foundationalism, coherentism, and infinitism, and should feel the genuine tension between an infinite-demand theory of justification and finite human cognition. After the piece, include a brief author’s note of roughly 100 words explaining precisely where the satire takes liberties with actual infinitist arguments — for example, whether infinitists require conscious articulation of every link, or whether the chain can be implicit — and stating clearly which position is being satirised and why. Take a definite, committed stance: do not present the three theories neutrally; the parody should argue, through its deadpan exaggeration, that infinitism collapses under administrative weight, while implicitly conceding why foundationalism and coherentism avoid that particular failure. Do not include bibliographies, citations, or reading lists.
