Voss's distinctive move is to reconstruct 'just deserts' not as a backward-looking moral property but as a forward-looking calibration target: societies need shared proportionality expectations to keep reactive attitudes stable, and desert-talk functions as a regulative fiction that keeps those expectations within functional bounds without carrying metaphysical commitment.
