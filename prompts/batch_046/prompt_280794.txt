What would have to be true for this position to be wrong? Rank the defeaters from decisive to merely awkward, distinguish those a committed Jeffreyian would concede from those they would dismiss, and say whether such insulation, if present, is itself a defect in a normative claim about belief updating. I am especially interested in whether practices in approximate Bayesian computation or probabilistic programming supply actual counterexamples.
