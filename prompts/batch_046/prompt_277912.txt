Assuming that Weber's thesis regarding the Protestant ethic establishes cultural values as autonomous causal mechanisms in historical sociology—functioning analogously to exogenous parameters in a causal model rather than as interpretive schemas that render action meaningful—what follows for the general methodology of explaining social change through normative commitments? Specifically, if we accept that an ascetic value-complex can be treated as an independent variable producing capitalist behavior, how do we avoid the circularity of diagnosing that value-complex precisely through the economic dispositions it is meant to explain? And does this difficulty indicate merely a standard endogeneity problem amenable to instrumental-variable techniques, or does it reveal a distinctively hermeneutic circularity that makes value-based explanation irreducible to structural causal inference?
