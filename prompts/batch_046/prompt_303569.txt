I am a researcher in nutritional psychiatry with working knowledge of vagal afferent signaling, microbiome-derived short-chain fatty acids, postprandial neuroinflammation, and metabolomic profiling, but little familiarity with nineteenth-century philosophy. Restate Friedrich Nietzsche's mature position on diet, digestion, and bodily health—especially as developed in his late writings such as Ecce Homo and Twilight of the Idols and in his Sils-Maria correspondence—using only the causal and mechanistic vocabulary of contemporary gut-brain axis science. Be specific about his rejection of heavy German cuisine and alcohol, his advocacy for small frequent meals and light proteins, his claim that poor digestion produces moral resentment and metaphysical need, and his notion of a hard-won, non-inherited vitality. For each element, map it onto a concrete mechanism—intestinal permeability, microbial metabolite profiles, vagal tone, inflammatory cytokine signaling—or state that no such mechanism exists. Then provide the residue report: which parts translate cleanly, which must be distorted to fit, and which have no expression at all in this framework. Conclude by judging whether the residue indicates that modern nutritional psychiatry is missing a normative or spiritual dimension of eating, or whether Nietzsche's digestive ethics was largely metaphorical rhetoric dressing up pre-scientific hygiene.
