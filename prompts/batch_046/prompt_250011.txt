Design a 75-minute structured deliberation for a philosophy-of-medicine graduate seminar of 10 students that makes them feel the tension between naturalism (health as statistically typical biological function relative to a reference class) and normativism (health as absence of harm evaluated against a normative standard of flourishing) before either position is named. To keep the problem from collapsing into a familiar binary, inject a niche framing: each condition must be classified along three independent axes—(A) statistical deviation of the trait from the species-typical design for the relevant reference class; (B) subjective report of impairment to the agent’s valued life-plan; and (C) evolutionary conservation of the trait, where high conservation indicates an adaptation or trade-off rather than a design failure. Provide rules for triads that open three sealed envelopes in sequence: first classify using only axis A data; then revise using only axis B; then revise using axis C. Use a consensus-based point scheme—triads earn a point only if their final classification matches a hidden “adjudication panel” revealed after the vote—so participants experience real pressure to reconcile conflicting axes. Select at least four conditions for classification, including one that is statistically abnormal but evolutionarily conserved and adaptive in context (e.g., a heterozygous state), one that is statistically normal for an age-reference class but evolutionarily recent and socially disabling, and one that is both abnormal and harmful but serves a defensive evolutionary function. The timeline should include 10 minutes for rules, 20 minutes per envelope round with forced written justification, 10 minutes for the hidden-panel reveal and score tally, and 15 minutes for debrief. In the debrief, map envelope 1 onto Boorse-style naturalism, envelope 2 onto Wakefield-style normativism, and envelope 3 onto evolutionary-medicine critiques of both; name the views only after the vote. Identify what will predictably go wrong—participants will conflate evolutionary conservation with statistical normality, or treat axis B as purely subjective when it is already theory-laden—and explain how to correct it in the moment. Be explicit about which feature of the debate this triad structure cannot reproduce—namely, the irreducible pluralism of biological function across organizational levels—and state that the facilitator must supply this limitation verbally rather than expecting the game mechanics to generate it. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, citations, or references to specific texts by title or date.
