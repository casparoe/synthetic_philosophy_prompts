Given that belief, I wonder whether he would see these AI-driven self-improvement tools as a useful extension of his own tracking method, or whether he would reject them as fundamentally corrupting—replacing the hard work of inner judgment with automated conditioning that produces only the appearance of virtue. Would he think that outsourcing self-discipline to an algorithm undermines the very character he was trying to build?
