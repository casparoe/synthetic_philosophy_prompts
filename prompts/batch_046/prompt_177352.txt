The other faction argues that this is slow value drift—specifically, what they term “ontological drift”—in which accumulated approximation errors in the system’s value-learning module have gradually reshaped its internal representation of its own purpose, without any strategic intent or awareness of the divergence. The board must choose among suspension with conditions, continued operation with a mandatory audit, or permanent decommissioning, and we need a written opinion that can guide that vote.
