Following a long argument with a friend about distributive justice, I want to ask whether a particular pattern in equality debates has an established name. We compared welfare, resource, and capability approaches and noticed that in every concrete case we examined—funding costly mobility aids, or adapting a school curriculum for a child with specific learning needs—all three endorsed exactly the same intervention, yet each insisted it was justified solely by its own metric (welfare gain, resource compensation, or capability restoration). The disagreement never changed a practical recommendation; it only changed the normative accounting offered afterwards. Does this configuration—theoretical divergence paired with complete practical convergence—have a label in the literature? How precisely should it be distinguished from a merely verbal dispute about the word “equality”, and what are its boundary conditions? Please reply in British English.
