I'm preparing a long-form feature on the philosophy of economics and have been revisiting Keynes's 1930 essay on economic possibilities for the coming century. Most popular treatments reduce his argument to a cheerful forecast of fifteen-hour work weeks, but I need something more rigorous for my readers: a specification sheet for what a normative theory of human flourishing must actually get right if it claims to formalize Keynes's vision of the good life once the economic problem is solved. I am not looking for a biography, a literature survey, or a list of secondary sources—just the conceptual architecture.

Please write the complete list of desiderata that an adequate theory of the good life under conditions of material abundance must satisfy to remain genuinely faithful to Keynes's argument. For each requirement, provide the motivating consideration drawn from the logical structure of his essay—specifically, the distinction between absolute needs and relative needs; the moral pathology he associates with wealth accumulation for its own sake; the risk of psychological dislocation once compulsory labor is abolished; and the crucial separation between leisure understood as mere recuperation for further work and leisure understood as the autonomous domain of art, culture, and intrinsically valued activity. Mark each desideratum as either non-negotiable, in the sense that it follows directly from Keynes's explicit commitments, or as a negotiable extension that later interpreters have added. Then address whether any subset of these requirements is jointly unsatisfiable: in particular, does the demand for a radical reduction in working hours conflict with Keynes's own acknowledgment that most people require some form of purposeful labor for psychological stability? Weight the analysis heavily on the criteria and their motivating cases rather than on any scorecard, though you may briefly note which mainstream well-being frameworks—preference-satisfaction, objective-list, or hedonistic accounts—fail on the non-negotiable items. A table is acceptable if it clarifies the structure.
