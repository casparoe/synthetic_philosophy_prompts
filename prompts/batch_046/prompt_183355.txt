Me using a spreadsheet to do my taxes: Gregorian, the external tool is doing cognition I can't manage alone. So far it feels tidy.

But then it gets messy. A New Caledonian crow bending wire into a hook to pull food up — I want to call that Gregorian because she's using an artifact as a cognitive extension, but then a beaver building a dam also modifies the environment and feels Darwinian, even though it's also using external stuff. What's the real principle separating them — cultural transmission, intentional design, something else? And whale song cultures that spread horizontally between groups — Gregorian? Or just Skinnerian imitation with extra steps?

Is there one principle that captures all four kinds, resolves the crow versus beaver split, and tells us whether the LLM is Popperian or Skinnerian? Or do I have to drop one of my verdicts — maybe the crow isn't Gregorian, maybe the LLM really is just Skinnerian after all? Lay out the principle if there is one, say which of my calls it forces me to abandon, and tell me what it predicts for a case I haven't listed: a slime mold solving a maze by leaving chemical trails that other cells follow.
