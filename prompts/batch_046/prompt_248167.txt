Kai pushes "interventionist contingency" — he argues learning the prophecy is like getting private info that breaks the original causal chain, so Death's line is post-hoc rationalization, not proof of an inevitible outcome. Have them really lock onto whether belief counts as an intervention, whether the prophecy is self-fulfilling or self-defeating, and whether Death's surprise even fits Lena's model. Both sharp, no strawmen, actually answering each other's points instead of talking past them. Keep it casual; maybe one writes "recieve" by mistake or misspells Aleppo.
