Write a deadpan parody of a software End User License Agreement (EULA) — rendered in the precise, clause-heavy, capitalized-defined-terms style of a corporate terms-of-service document — governing the relationship between an “Earlier Self” (the competent party who executes an advance directive) and a “Successor Self” (the later, possibly cognitively altered party inhabiting the same biological substrate). The document should run approximately 600 words and should read as though drafted by a fictional legal consortium, the Intertemporal Continuity Board.

Target of satire: the philosophical and medico-legal position that the earlier competent self possesses unconditional, contract-like authority over the later self’s treatment, irrespective of whether the later self expresses contemporaneous preferences, reports a different quality of life, or no longer endorses the values that motivated the original directive. The parody should exaggerate real features of this position — the assumption that preferences remain stable across radical cognitive change, the framing of future incapacity as a delegation rather than a transformation, and the use of legal instruments to bind a being who may no longer share the motivating values — by translating them literally into contractual mechanisms.

Specific requirements:
- Maintain deadpan irony throughout; do not wink or signal that the document is absurd. The critique must be legible to a reader who recognizes the underlying debate about precedent autonomy, personal identity over time, and the binding force of advance directives.
- Invent contractual clauses that literalize the conceptual structure: for example, a “Binding Arbitration Clause” for conflicts between the Earlier Self’s values and the Successor Self’s expressed wishes; a “Service-Level Agreement” specifying minimum cognitive capacity thresholds; a “Non-Compete Clause” preventing the Successor Self from authorizing treatments the Earlier Self prohibited; a “Data Portability” provision addressing whether memories and preferences constitute shared assets between the two parties; and a “Termination and Succession” clause addressing death, permanent unconsciousness, or corporate-style dissolution of the identity.
- Inject a genuinely niche conceptual framing: treat the problem of “precedent autonomy” as analogous to corporate successor liability, in which the Successor Self inherits obligations but may dispute whether the original “corporation” (the earlier self) was the same legal entity. Also include a clause framing the trade-off between present and future welfare as a standard contractual “Consideration” provision, in which the Earlier Self has traded away future autonomy in exchange for present control over the biological substrate.
- Do not cite philosophers, cite specific texts, or provide a bibliography. Do not depend on knowledge of obscure theories; the conceptual moves should be derivable from general familiarity with advance directives and contract law.
- After the EULA, provide a brief structured summary in JSON format with exactly these fields:
  * “satire_target”: a one-sentence description of the position being satirized.
  * “invented_clauses”: an array of strings, each naming one contractual clause invented in the piece and summarizing its literalized concept in one line.
  * “conceptual_injection”: a short description of the niche framing (corporate successor liability and intertemporal consideration) and how it functions in the parody.
  * “confidence_statement”: a short statement of how confident you are in the fairness and accuracy of the satire’s representation of real advance-directive debates.
  * “least_sure_point”: identification of the specific claim, analogy, or implied argument in the piece about which you are least confident that it holds up conceptually or represents the debate fairly.

The response should explicitly say how confident it is in what it claims and should single out the point it is least sure of. The confidence assessment and the least-sure point should appear both in the JSON summary (as specified above) and be acknowledged in brief concluding remarks after the EULA, if any concluding remarks are included.
