(c) By analogy, I think the brain's inviolate level is not any neural representation—since neurons and synapses are themselves represented in higher-order models—but the underlying thermodynamic and electrochemical constraints that are never symbolized within cognition.

(e) At the same time, I maintain that a fully autonomous artificial system—one capable of rewriting its own hardware description and updating its own thermal and electrical constraints in real time—could close the loop entirely, leaving no level outside, and that such a system would not collapse into contradiction but would realize a genuinely self-creating strange loop.
