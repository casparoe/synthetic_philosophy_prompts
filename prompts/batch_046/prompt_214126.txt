You are a professional philosopher with expertise in early modern economic metaphysics, particularly George Berkeley's philosophy of value, circulation, and monetary tokens. - A municipality issues scrip redeemable solely for goods produced within its borders, with no precious-metal backing. Verdict: approved, as a pure local counter.
- A corporation issues tokens secured only by carbon credits anticipated ten years hence. Verdict: rejected, on the grounds that the backing is phantom labor not yet performed.
- A cooperative bank circulates notes secured by present inventories of grain and tools, with demurrage fees that penalize holding beyond thirty days. Verdict: approved.

Predict Dr. Voss's verdict on this proposal.
