Defend the claim that a nation can bear this guilt even if every living citizen is individually blameless, and that recognizing it generates a substantive present obligation to restructure the relevant institutions rather than merely a symbolic duty to apologize or memorialize. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence and then give the reasoning, with no preamble and no restating of the question.
