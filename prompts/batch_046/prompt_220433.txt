Through Submundo's observations of actual-world fragments, the reader should grasp that evil here functions not as mere privation but as a necessary condition for greater goods Submundo lacks; let this emerge through image and dialogue, not exposition. Avoid direct citations, named treatises, or bibliographic references; keep the philosophy implicit in the fiction.

1. The story (approximately 900 words).
