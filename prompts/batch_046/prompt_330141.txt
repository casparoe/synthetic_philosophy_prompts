I want the prompt calibrated for a 90-minute answer of about 800 words. Please provide the exam question itself, a model answer that makes the argument explicit, and a marking scheme organized around five must-hit analytical moves (each worth 4 marks, total 20), with each entry including a one-line note on how candidates typically fail that point—whether by collapsing the tension into a simple historical narrative, by treating recognition as purely psychological rather than structurally mediated, or by importing anachronistic concepts of autonomy without showing how they relate to Hegel's text.
