Write a joke that turns on Leibniz's claim that this world is the best of all possible worlds, but only through the lens of the compossibility constraint: God does not maximize global welfare but selects the set of essences with the greatest richness compatible with the simplest laws, so that local suffering functions not as a moral trade-off but as a logical prerequisite for the coherence of other essences.
