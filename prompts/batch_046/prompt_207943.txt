The example should be calibrated so that the two procedures yield numerically different posteriors for H—approximately 0.72 versus 0.51—and the divergence should persist even if she attempts to apply an Adams-style framework for conditional events, because the indexical component in A prevents the assignment of a well-defined unconditional prior to the conditional event. Specify the full prior distribution over the joint possibilities, the precise nature of the external signal, and explain why the standard Judy Benjamin resolution—treating A as a centered proposition with a rigid designator—does not collapse the two paths when the update target is a conditional linking location to an objective chance rather than a direct observation of location. Conclude by stating the precise conceptual issue: is the agent rationally required to revise her belief in H, and if so, in which direction and by what normative principle?
