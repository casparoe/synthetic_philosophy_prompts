According to her, once you understand how representations are formatted and linked in the workspace, the informativeness of learning Hesperus is Phosphorus just becomes a story about pointer updates, not about hidden semantic content. I was talking to a friend who said that sounds like she's just moving the problem from semantics to neuroscience, and I want the questions to really dig into that tension rather than letting her skate past it. Finally, ask whether she's made Frege's sense/reference distinction redundant, or if she thinks it still does real work elsewhere like in belief ascription.
