Is there a unified account—perhaps about organizational contribution, level-relative efficacy, or closure—that saves all five? State the principle precisely, predict its verdict on an unlisted case (say, a swarm robot whose collective decision is physically reducible to each unit's sensors), and tell me honestly which of my five verdicts I must sacrifice if coherence demands it.
