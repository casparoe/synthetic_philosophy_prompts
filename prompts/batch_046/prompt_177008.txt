What I want is a rigorous conceptual explanation of precisely where the tautology dissolves once fitness is empirically measured against an independently described environment, and whether Popper’s revised criterion requires evolutionary biology to make genuinely predictive claims about future evolutionary trajectories or whether mere retrodictive consistency with observed fossil and genetic records suffices for scientific status.

Please take a definite position: does Popper’s falsifiability criterion, properly applied, render evolutionary theory genuinely predictive in the same sense as quantum mechanics, or does it leave Darwinism in a distinct, perhaps weaker, epistemic category? I want disciplined reasoning that states where the comparison holds and where it breaks down, not an even-handed catalogue of interpretations left for me to choose between.
