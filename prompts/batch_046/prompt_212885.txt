Having retired after thirty years in experimental physics and only recently turned to philosophy in earnest, I have been reading Schopenhauer's account of music with a mixture of fascination and suspicion—fascination because his description of melody, harmony, and rhythm as analogues to the striving and resolution of the Will seems almost acoustically literal to someone accustomed to thinking in waves and resonances, and suspicion because I cannot tell where the physical analogy ends and the metaphysics begins. In my discussion group—mostly scientists who have taken up philosophy late—we have begun to ask whether Schopenhauer's implicit hierarchy of the arts can be tested against phenomena he never explicitly considered, without simply projecting modern frameworks onto his system.

I would therefore like you to rank the following five cases according to the degree to which each constitutes, on Schopenhauer's own terms, a direct expression of the Will rather than a mediated representation through Platonic Ideas: (1) a traditional tonal symphony in a major key, (2) improvised atonal jazz performed without predetermined harmonic structure, (3) the sustained absence of sound in an anechoic environment experienced as a perceptual object, (4) an elegant mathematical proof contemplated purely for its formal beauty, and (5) architecture designed according to strict geometric proportions such as the golden ratio. Please assign each a precise score between 0 and 1—where 1 denotes full proximity to the Will and 0 denotes pure representational mediation—and then arrange all five into an overall tier list of S, A, B, C, and D.

Crucially, in your reasoning you should carefully separate Schopenhauer's actual claim that music bypasses the world of Ideas from a projection I have been tempted to make from my scientific background: namely, that lower representational or informational bandwidth automatically correlates with higher ontological directness. I want to know whether that projection strengthens, distorts, or collapses his argument, and whether any of these edge cases force an internal revision of his ranking rather than a simple dismissal of the outliers as non-artistic. No citations, bibliographies, or further reading lists are needed; I am interested solely in the internal consistency of the metaphysical system as applied to these particular cases.
