I've been trying to wrap my head around Rosenthal's HOT theory and I think there's a hidden circularity around the vehicle/content distinction. He says a mental state is conscious only if there's an unconcious HOT directed at it, but if that HOT is itself unconcious then its vehicle isn't phenomenally present — so how does its content manage to "light up" the first-order state? It feels like saying a mirror reflects an image even when no one's looking at the mirror. So doesn't HOT theory smuggle in a primitive lighting-up relation that was supposed to be explained, pushing us back toward some kind of Cartesian theater where one state has to be self-luminous? Or am I just mixing up vehicle and content in a dumb way?
