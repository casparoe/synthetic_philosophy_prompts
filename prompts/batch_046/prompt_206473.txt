Write a short dialogue between two friends arguing over whether rational people with the same info should always agree on probabilities — the common prior idea. One thinks yes, even for messy real-world calls like whether an old factory gets turned into apartments, since eventually hidden reasoning comes out and its just a matter of updating right. The other thinks disagreement survives because people dont just weight evidence differently; they build different mental menus of whats even possible before they start assigning odds, so theyre running different games entirely. Have them actually clash over the factory example, responding point by point, neither being dumb. Keep it casual — like over beers — with a typo or two, and avoid naming obscure philosophers.
