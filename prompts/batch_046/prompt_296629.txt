There's this deal on the table with a big, wealthy city that runs under an authoritarian system: no real elections, the press is restricted, but they've got incredible public housing, low crime, and steady growth. Some board members say that proves performance legitimacy is real, that if people are prosperous and safe then the lack of democracy doesn't really matter, and we should expand the tie. Others say that's exactly the dangerous idea we shouldn't endorse. The specific proposal is they'd put up half the money for a new civic center here, but the contract requires us to call their goverment effective governance in all joint materials and to exclude any programming that asks whether prosperity can justify authoritarian rule.

I need to circulate a written opinion before Thursday's vote recommending either accepting with conditions, rejecting, or some middle path, with a concrete timeline for next steps. Start with the bottom line right at the top, keep the whole thing under 350 words, and honestly deal with the strongest argument on the other side - the prosperity and order are real for the people living there, not just propaganda. I'm not an academic or anything, just trying to think it through, so please don't include a bibliography or reading list I can't check. Just plain language, one page max.
