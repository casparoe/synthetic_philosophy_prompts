Compare the gradual neuron-swap view of mind uploading—replace one neuron at a time with silicon over a decade—with the sudden scan-and-copy approach. Don't just do the ship of theseus thing; I'm more interested in the 'mixed state' problem. If 40% of your prefrontal cortex is now running on silicon and 60% is still meat, is there a single conscious subject, two subjects, or a flickering one? Which of the two upload models handles that intermediate stage better, and does your answer depend on whether you think phenomenal binding needs biological substrate or can be implimented in silicon piecemeal?
