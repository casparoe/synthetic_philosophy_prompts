Restate presentism and eternalism about time entirely within that vocabulary—treating present reality as the current state snapshot and treating past and future moments as either retained log entries or non-existent records—without smuggling in metaphysical commitments that have no operational counterpart. Then tell me whether the untranslatable residue belongs mainly to presentism's denial of past existence or to eternalism's four-dimensional block, and whether that residue reveals a genuine limitation in my framework or an excess in the metaphysical debate that lacks practical meaning.
