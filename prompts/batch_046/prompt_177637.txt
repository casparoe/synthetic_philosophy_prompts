You are a professional philosopher with expertise in philosophy of science and a sharp satirical voice. Before drafting, spend a few minutes brainstorming the actual logical structure of the dark forest hypothesis—its inference from the absence of detectable extraterrestrial signals to the presence of hostile, silent civilizations; its dependence on game-theoretic assumptions about resource scarcity and universal strategic rationality; and the standard critiques (anthropic reasoning, cosmic timescale mismatches, the possibility of non-hostile or technologically incompatible silence). Then write a deadpan, Onion-style news article, headline plus approximately 450 words, reporting that the Philosophy Department at a large public university has adopted the dark forest hypothesis as its official administrative and epistemic framework.

The satire must derive from applying the hypothesis’s real features—zero-sum competition, the strategic premium on concealment, the leap from observed silence to hidden danger—to mundane academic life: peer review, tenure lines, citation metrics, office-hour policies, conference attendance, and grant applications. Invent a specific internal taxonomy of departmental “silence protocols” (e.g., a formal classification of non-publication and misdirection strategies) that extends the logic rigorously without borrowing standard pop-culture terminology. Keep the voice consistently deadpan and journalistic: never wink, never explain the joke. Include at least one quote from a “concerned frequentist colleague” who objects using statistical reasoning—reference classes, base rates, or absence-of-evidence arguments—so that the objection lands precisely.

Keep the prompt focused and self-contained: do not request bibliographies, reading lists, or citations. After the article, append a brief, clearly separated analytical note. In that note, distinguish what is settled about the dark forest hypothesis (as articulated in the relevant literature) from what is genuinely contested among specialists—specifically, whether silence justifies an inference to hostility, and whether cosmic-scale game theory applies uniformly across civilizations. Present no contested interpretation as the consensus view. Write in a polished, formal register.
