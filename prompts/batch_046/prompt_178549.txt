Each leaf should carry a name—traditional lie, modern organized lying, ideological fiction, bureaucratic euphemism, or unnamed—and one sentence on its most awkward Arendtian commitment, like that some lies work best when nobody actually believes them. Build the tree from the logic of the positions, not from a list of book titles. Flag any two leaves your tree can't really seperate.
