Moore's Principia Ethica, specifically his idea that friendship and appreciating beauty are among the highest intrinsic goods. i told my roommate about it later and we argued about it for like two hours and still can't figure out who's right, so i need an outside take.

Marcus answered that the difference is you value your friend even when they're being annoying and giving you zero pleasure, which shows it's not about the feeling but about the person. they went back and forth on whether Moore treats beauty and friendship as the exact same kind of good, or if there's a meaningful distinction he draws that neither of them was seeing.
