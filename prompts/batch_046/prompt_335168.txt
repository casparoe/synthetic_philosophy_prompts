Assess what normative assumptions about procedural fairness versus distributive outcomes the analogy quietly imports, and determine whether the board member's conclusion—that need-based redistribution is inherently illegitimate—survives once those disanalogies are made explicit. Please answer in American English, and ground the analysis in the conceptual structure of historical versus patterned principles rather than in empirical case studies.
