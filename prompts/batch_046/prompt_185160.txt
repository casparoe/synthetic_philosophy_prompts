Consider the following examination item and the student response that follows. You are grading in the capacity of a TA; do not disclose your own philosophical commitments, but assess the work rigorously and precisely.

Question: Hofstadter's knob-twiddling model treats creative cognition as the adjustment of parameters within an existing conceptual frame. First, distinguish intra-frame knob-twiddling—where the representational structure remains stable and only its assigned values change—from inter-frame slippage, in which an analogical mapping replaces the underlying frame itself. Second, apply this distinction to explain how a composer might generate a set of variations on a theme. Third, identify one specific limitation of extending knob-twiddling to account for radical paradigm shifts in science, particularly shifts that alter the evaluation criteria for what counts as an adequate explanation rather than merely substituting one content-frame for another.

Student response:
Hofstadter's model holds that creativity works by turning small continuous knobs on a concept's parameters. In intra-frame twiddling, a composer keeps the melodic identity fixed but adjusts dynamics, tempo, or instrumentation. Inter-frame slippage occurs when the theme is mapped onto a different domain—say, translating its contour into an architectural plan—so that the original acoustic frame is replaced by a spatial one.

This maps onto scientific change. Normal-science refinement, like improving instrument calibration, is intra-frame. A Kuhnian revolution is inter-frame because it swaps one theoretical framework for another. The limitation is that knob-twiddling still treats revolutionary change as a higher-order parameter adjustment, which misses that some shifts change the norms of explanation themselves—what counts as a valid answer—rather than just exchanging frames.

One weakness of Hofstadter's view is that it is essentially computational and deterministic: the mind algorithmically searches a parameter space. This overlooks the subconscious, associative nature of real analogical discovery. Another weakness is that inter-frame mapping requires preserving relational structure across domains, so the model cannot explain genuinely discontinuous leaps where no structural correspondence exists between the source and target.

Assign a letter grade and provide point-by-point comments that justify it. Your evaluation should separately address: (i) the accuracy of the intra-frame/inter-frame distinction as presented; (ii) the correctness of the compositional application; (iii) the identification of the limitation regarding scientific revolutions and evaluation criteria; and (iv) any conceptual errors, misattributions, or inferential gaps—especially subtle ones that a hurried reader might overlook. Do not offer a general impression; deliver a component-by-component assessment.
