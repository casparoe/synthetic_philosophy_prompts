The second is the idea that a formal system might grow complex enough to recognize its own boundaries from within and step outside them entirely, leaving the internal rules behind.

Write a joke that depends on both mechanics interacting, not just a generic joke about either one alone. This should be genuinely funny to someone who understands why MU is unprovable inside the rules and what it actually means for a system to observe its own limits, rather than a generic joke about math being difficult.
