Take the NASA ST5 antenna from 2006 — you know, the one an evolutionary algorithm spat out, all asymetrical and lopsidded, and the engineers admitted they couldn't fully reverese-engineer why that shape outperformed the human designs. I want you to analyze it through Darwin's and Turing's strange inverssions. Darwin moves from artificial selection, where breeders understand their goals, to natural selection, where no mind is involved; Turing moves from computation lacking comprehension to intelligence that has it. Here we have computation running selection without comprehension, yielding a physical artifact that human comprehension then can't unpack. So where is the competence actually sitting — in the algorithm, in the antenna, or in our failure to grasp it? And does the fact that engineers still had to validate and deploy the thing break Turing's inversion, or just show that comprehension has to be parked somewhere in the loop?
