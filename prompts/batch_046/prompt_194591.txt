The guide claims that Pascal believes true justice is entirely hidden from fallen human reason, which means that in the political realm justice is reducible to whatever the dominant power enforces, and that consequently any reform movement appealing to abstract rational principles is not only doomed but conceptually confused. It adds that Pascal's well-known observation that justice and force must be united should be read as a purely descriptive sociological point about state stability, carrying no normative implication that force ought to be made just or justice made strong; rather, the guide concludes that Pascal's position amounts to a form of quietism in which the wise citizen accepts the existing legal order without critique, since disrupting the union of justice and force merely invites disorder.
