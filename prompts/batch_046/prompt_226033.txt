Explain why the Burkean reply can survive this challenge—perhaps by distinguishing the technological substrate from the underlying institutional logic—without appealing to specific empirical case studies or historical examples that would require verification. The reasoning should remain conceptual, and please do not include a bibliography, reading list, or citations.
