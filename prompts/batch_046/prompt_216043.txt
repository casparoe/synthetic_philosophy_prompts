But I was telling my roommate that this only works because Black’s intervention is counterfactual — like, in the actual world nothing stops Jones, it’s just that in some other branch he’d be blocked. So I said Frankfurt cases don’t really touch PAP for the actual world, they just show that counterfactual possibilities don’t matter. My roommate said I was wrong, that Black is literally standing there in the actual world, just not flipping the switch. In the actual causal chain, he literally couldn’t form the contrary intention — it’s not just a backup plan, it’s an active blocker he’s unaware of. Wouldn’t that be the real knockout punch for the principel of alternative possibilities? And if that’s right, why do people still cite the old Frankfurt story instead of these 'masked-active-blocker' scenarios?
