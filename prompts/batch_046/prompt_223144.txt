She summed up his afterlife conjecture like this: Scheffler thinks human extinction is mainly tragic because it involves the deaths of billions, but he also argues that even in a doomsday scenario where everyone dies peacefully there's still a loss—namely, there'd be no future people to carry on or appreciate our art, science, and collective projects.
