I am a doctoral student in philosophy of value, and I am structuring a ninety-minute graduate seminar for an interdisciplinary audience—philosophy and cognitive science students—on the interface between absurdism and contemporary pluralist theories of meaning in life. Rather than presenting Camus’s position as a terminus, I want to introduce a synthetic framing I am developing called recursive localism: the claim that global axiological failure does not entail local axiological failure because value does not scale transitively across existential registers. Before this framing can land, the students need to see why the standard dichotomy—either cosmic purpose or mere consolation—rests on a hidden premise about scale-invariance. The carrying example should be a reconstructed Sisyphus scenario: Sisyphus understands that his boulder-rolling generates irreducible local patterns—micro-rhythms, tactile relations, anticipatory structures—that do not accumulate into a grand narrative but are nonetheless non-arbitrary. This example must lack any redemptive arc or hidden reward, and it must possess enough structural complexity that the local patterns are genuinely generative rather than merely repetitive. I need the pedagogical arc mapped precisely: which conceptual dependency must be secured first (the distinction between purpose-at-a-scale and purpose-simpliciter), the sequence in which the three predictable misconceptions should be addressed—first that absurdism denies all teleology, second that pluralist objective-list theories require cosmic embedding, and third that local meaning is psychologically adaptive but metaphysically hollow—and the exact point in the sequence where each should be defused rather than merely mentioned. I also need one diagnostic question for the end that distinguishes a student who actually grasps recursive localism from one who is only deploying its vocabulary, plus a clear statement of what I should refuse to treat at this level—likely the full metaphysics of emergent scale-properties or the complete taxonomy of subjective versus hybrid theories—and an explicit acknowledgment of that refusal to the room. The session should not rely on bibliographic references; build the plan from conceptual architecture alone.
