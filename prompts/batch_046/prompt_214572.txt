Write a satirical, deadpan FAQ—approximately 450 words, formatted as an official government document—issued by the Bureau of Constitutional Recursion in the fictional democratic republic of Lexarchia. The republic's fundamental law contains a single self-amending clause, Article Ω, which permits any properly enacted amendment to alter the rules of amendment themselves, including Article Ω. The FAQ should address citizen inquiries about voting rights, judicial review, and emergency powers, with each bureaucratic answer inadvertently revealing that every legal certainty collapses into circular self-reference. The satire must turn on genuine philosophical problems of legal self-reference—the infinite regress of amendment rules, the distinction between procedural and substantive constitutional constraints, and the difficulty of identifying a non-circular source of authority—rather than on absurdist invention. To ensure the piece is not mere repetition of standard examples, inject one invented but philosophically coherent concept, recursive popular sovereignty, defined as the doctrine that the people's authority to amend derives solely from the current textual formulation of the amendment rule itself; the document should treat this doctrine as settled law and exaggerate its implications until they become patently self-defeating. Maintain sustained irony throughout: the voice should remain earnestly helpful and administratively formal, yet a specialist should recognize that the logic being followed is exactly that of nomic paradox, and that the bureaucrat is unwittingly demonstrating the impossibility of grounding legal authority in a purely self-referential system. Do not cite named scholars or specific texts; allude to debates only through paraphrase. The humor should arise from rigorous logical consistency rather than from non-sequitur or caricature, and the document should not request bibliographies, reading lists, or citations.
