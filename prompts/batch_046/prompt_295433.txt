I am preparing a discussion paper for a graduate seminar on the norms of assertion, and I want to probe the boundary between the knowledge norm, the truth norm, and the belief norm using a deliberately engineered scenario rather than a familiar textbook example. Next, explain what happens to the verdicts if that compensatory error is removed: does the case collapse, and into what? Finally, offer an honest defense of the knowledge norm: how might its proponent argue that the institutional reliability of the chain actually restores knowledge, or that the relevant method should be individuated differently so that the Gettier feature disappears?
