And this only holds if you buy that constitutional texts have a kind of latent semantic content that gets unlocked slowly through judicial interpretation, like running a long computation. So if you reject that semantic picture of constitutions, or if you think political compromise actually improves moral reasoning, then my defense collapses. What would actually count against this view? Also, is there a risk this gets qualified into unfalsifiability by saying the computation just hasn't finished yet, and is that actually a problem for a view like this?
