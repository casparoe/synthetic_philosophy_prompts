Consider also whether this synthetic case reveals that human aesthetic testimony was always primarily a mechanism of social calibration rather than a conduit for phenomenological insight. Reach and justify a clear conclusion about whether, in this scenario, I am epistemically justified in believing the soundscape is beautiful solely on the basis of the algorithm's testimony.
