I've been thinking about the is-ought gap and the naturalistic fallacy for my ethics course. If Hume showed you can't derive an ought from an is, and Moore said defining good in terms of natural properties is fallacious, then doesn't any evolutionary explanation of morality — like saying we evolved altruism becuase it aided survival — automatically violate both? Because it's using biological 'is' facts to explain moral 'ought' beliefs. So is evolutionary ethics just dead on arrival?
