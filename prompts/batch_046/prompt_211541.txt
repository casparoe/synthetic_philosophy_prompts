ok so im trying to figure out if my views on suffering and pleasure actually hang together and i keep going in circles. heres where i am:

1. im a lexical-threshold negative utilitarian in the specific sense that i think once suffering crosses a certain intensity/duration boundary, no amount of pleasure can outweigh it - not just "a lot" but literally none, like a lexical ordering.

2. but i also think pleasure and pain arent really commensurable. theyre not just different intensities on the same scale; i think theyre more like different affective dimensions - like comparing loudness to brightness.

3. i also buy this idea about "hedonic inversion points" - like theres a specific phenomenological point where a sensation stops being merely unpleasant and becomes something that structurally dominates your practical reasoning, regardless of context. i dont think this is just the threshold restated; it feels like its doing different work.

4. despite all that, i think people can rationally pursue long projects that involve predictable, serious suffering - like running ultra marathons or writing a novel in misery - because the meaning or narrative coherence justifies it, not because the suffering is "cancelled" by pleasure.

5. and i think evolutionary debunking hits our pleasure-tracking mechanisms harder than our pain-avoidance ones, so our intuitions about "how much pleasure is enough" are especially unreliable, but our aversion to severe suffering is actually well-calibrated.

the tension i see is mainly between 1 and 2: if pleasure and pain are incommensurable dimensions, how can there be a lexical comparison that says suffering always wins above the threshold? and does 3 help or make it worse?

but also i worry that 4 sneaks in a kind of trade-off logic that 1 forbids. if meaning can justify suffering, am i really treating suffering as lexically dominant, or am i just doing cost-benefit analysis with extra steps?

and does 5 actually support 1 or undermine it? if our pain-avoidance is well-calibrated, maybe the lexical threshold is just an evolutionary artifact, not a normative fact.

anyway, can you go through these and tell me which ones are genuinely in tension and which only look like they are? rank the problems by severity and say what the cheapest fix is for each. and specifically: does the incommensurability claim kill the lexical claim, or can they coexist if im careful about what "outweigh" means? and is the inversion point in 3 redundant with 1?
