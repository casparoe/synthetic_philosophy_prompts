They want £80k for a six-month pilot in two homes, measuring agitation not comprehension. Our board meets Tuesday and I need a short memo circulating before then: recommend approval with conditions, or reject with reasons. Treat the Schopenhaurian claim seriously — don't just call it mystical fluff — but also press the risk that if the metaphysics is off we're just exposing vulnerable people to noise. Include a concrete recommendation with a timeframe, and give the strongest counter-argument proper weight.
