(5) I think substrate matters weakly: an exact silicon brain-copy would have the illusion, but the same algorithm running in python on my laptop wouldn't, because the causal dynamics need to be physically instantiated. Do these hang together? I'm mostly worried about (3) and (4) - if consciousness is just an interface, what are my phenomenal concepts pointing at? And does (5) clash with my physicalism?
