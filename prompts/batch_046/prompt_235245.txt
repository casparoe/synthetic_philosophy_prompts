On this view, rational choice does not depend on comparing values on a hidden common scale, nor on arbitrary selection; rather, the chooser should identify which option they are structurally positioned to bear the ongoing "moral residue" of the unchosen value—its continued legitimate claim—over time, and commit on that basis.
