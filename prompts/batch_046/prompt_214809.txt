Act as a philosopher of value specializing in intergenerational ethics and the metaphysics of collective existence. Before constructing your response, briefly brainstorm the different kinds of explanations that might bear on the loss of a collective future.

Write the desiderata that any adequate theory must satisfy to explain why human extinction is gravely bad or wrong over and above the individual deaths it entails. Cover the following: the phenomena the theory must explain, including the asymmetry between caring about extinction and caring about non-creation, the intuition that future human projects and interpretive traditions carry weight even in the absence of anyone who suffers their loss, and the judgment that a flourishing post-human world may still be diminished; the distinctions it must preserve, such as between value-to-whom and wrongness-simpliciter, between individual goods and genuinely collective goods, and between intrinsic worth and instrumental contribution to meaning; and the theoretical constraints it must observe, including compatibility with a naturalistic worldview, avoidance of implausible ontological commitments to a collective mind, and consistency with the idea that value can depend on relations that outlive any particular consciousness. For each desideratum, state the motivating case or consideration, flag any pair you suspect is jointly unsatisfiable, and identify which single requirement eliminates the most popular candidates—including pure person-affecting views, preference-satisfaction accounts, and Scheffler-style conjectures that ground the badness in the termination of shared projects across generations. Keep the weight on the criteria rather than on a full scorecard, though briefly note where project-continuation accounts succeed or strain. Finally, inject and defend one niche desideratum not standard in the literature—perhaps concerning the epistemic value of unfinished interpretive traditions or cross-generational conceptual continuity—to show how the list might be expanded without collapsing into reified collective agency.
