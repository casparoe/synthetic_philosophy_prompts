Analyse the Cuban Missile Crisis of October '62 through the lense of commitment-race theory — not chicken, which every textbook does, but the mechanics of irreversible commitments and escalation ladders. Map out who locked in what: the naval quarantine (not blockade, technically), the Jupiter missiles sitting in Turkey, Khrushchev's open letters versus Kennedy's private assurances. I want you to trace how each side tried to construct a commitment visible enough to scare the other but opaque enough to their own public that retreat wouldn't cost domestic honour. Then ask whether the secret back-chanel deal was an unwinding of the race or just a hidden exit ramp that the theory always assumed existed. Be honest about the limits: does commitment-race logic explain why Kennedy overruled ExComm on the quarantine wording, or is that pure presidential psychology outside the model? And flag any part of the mapping where the theory is silent rather than forcing an answer.
