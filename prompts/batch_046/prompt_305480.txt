What I want to explore is whether Moore applies this principle symmetrically across these two goods. Trace the conceptual tension that arises because friendship seems irreducibly relational and intersubjective, whereas aesthetic enjoyment can occur in isolation, and consider whether this asymmetry forces Moore to place them in distinct axiological categories despite his tendency to list them together as supreme goods.
