(7) i think an AI with perfect functional self-monitoring but no consciousness still wouldn't be introspecting, because introspection requires phenomenal presence.

do these hang together? i'm especially worried about the tension between (2) and (4) - if phenomenal concepts present experience directly, why does change blindness mess with my first-person report about what's in my field? and between (2) and (5) - if i have direct access to the nature of experience, why is there still an explanatory gap that science hasn't closed? also is (6) really compatible with (4), or does treating introspection as direct acquaintance mean it shouldn't suffer perceptual-style failures?
