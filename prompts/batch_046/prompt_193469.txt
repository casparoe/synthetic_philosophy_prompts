Let's say Whitehead was alive now and saw these adaptive tutoring systems that use eye-tracking and keystroke metrics to continously rewrite the curriculum, cutting out any exploration that doesn't immediately improve the mastery score. He had that idea about education cycling through romance, precision, and generalization, and he thought learning needed some disciplined freedom rather than just efficient delivery. How do you think he'd judge a platform that optimizes away the messy romace stage? Would he say it destroys the rhythm entirely, or could he adapt his view to fit algorithmic personalization? And specificaly, if the 'self' being educated is constantly being reshaped by an external model's predictions, does that conflict with his process view of the subject?
