For a philosophy of mind and technology seminar, write the full specification of desiderata that any adequate theory of diachronic personal persistence must meet if it is to account for mind uploading achieved through gradual substrate replacement. The scenario is not the standard instantaneous scan-and-copy case, but a staged migration protocol in which cognitive functions are transferred incrementally from biological neural tissue to a synthetic substrate over months or years, with deliberate intermittent dormancy phases during which neither substrate is fully conscious, and with engineered reversibility allowing rollback to any prior stage except at designated irreversibility commit points. The theory must also accommodate temporary dual-instantiation windows in which both substrates maintain partial, non-overlapping functional profiles before a final commitment resolves the branch.

Give me the requirements list: the phenomena it must explain (including persistence through partial functional overlap, identity across dormancy gaps, and the normative status of rollback versus continuation), the distinctions it must preserve (for instance between survival and mere replication, between causal continuity and informational similarity, and between a reversible stage and a committed transition), and the empirical constraints it cannot violate (such as findings on neural plasticity, the distributed nature of memory encoding, and the absence of a single neural locus of consciousness). For each requirement, provide the motivating case or consideration and indicate whether it is non-negotiable or a preference of a particular camp.

Then identify whether any subset of these desiderata is jointly unsatisfiable—whether, for example, the demand for continuous causal connection conflicts with the reversibility requirement, or whether accommodating dual-instantiation forces the abandonment of a one-one relation between person and substrate. State which single desideratum, in your view, does the most damage to the most prominent existing theories (psychological-continuity accounts, biological-continuity accounts, and patternist or functionalist accounts). Finally, score those three contenders briefly against your list, but keep the weight on the criteria: the list itself is the deliverable I want to examine.
