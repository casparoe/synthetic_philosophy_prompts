From the standpoint of his experimentalist philosophy, which treats democracy not as a fixed governmental form but as a continuing mode of cooperative inquiry among affected publics, predict how Dewey might assess these platforms and what structural changes he might propose. Your analysis should address: (1) whether he would regard algorithmic personalization as an extension or a corruption of democratic intelligence; (2) how he might redefine the formation of a public in an environment where shared problems are obscured by personalized feeds; and (3) what institutional mechanisms—without relying on centralized editorial authority—he could endorse to restore conditions for experimental collective judgment. Write the response in American English, and do not include citations, references to specific editions, or a reading list.
