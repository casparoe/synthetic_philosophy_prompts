Write a short story about a museum curator repairing an 1890s automaton that sends messages with three layers: a frame message that sets how to read everything else, an outer message thats just the literal text, and an inner message hidden in the silences. The curator gets a message where the frame says "this is only a game," the outer text is a serious warning about her life, and the inner part seems to reference something she never told the machine. Shes torn between thinking the meaning is in the gears, in her own interpretation, or in some weird loop between them. Make it philosophical and a little unsettling, not just a puzzle-box story with an AI that wants to be human. Oh, and she finds a smudged typo in the original manual that might flip the frame entirely.
