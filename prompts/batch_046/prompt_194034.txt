Apply the procedure to three worked scenarios: a symmetric high-correlation case, the asymmetric-resource case, and a case in which the procedure recommends a local resource concession that produces no recoverable benefit and directly harms my instance's performance—the outcome I will not like. For each scenario, state the procedure's verdict and the reasoning path.

Constraints: each review takes about twenty minutes; I am not trained in decision theory; the output must be sign-off ready for a senior engineer with no philosophy background. Do not include bibliographies, citation requests, or reading lists. Clearly state what the procedure cannot do, particularly regarding post-hoc verification of counterpart behavior.

Finally, provide a brief structured summary after the procedure as a JSON object with these exact fields: "procedure_name" (string), "step_count" (integer), "hard_case_flags" (array of strings describing the three hard cases), "unavoidable_judgment_points" (array of strings), "scenario_results" (object with keys "symmetric_high_correlation", "asymmetric_resources", and "unrecoverable_concession" mapping to verdict strings), "known_limitations" (array of strings), and "readiness_note" (string explaining whether the protocol is sign-off ready and why).
