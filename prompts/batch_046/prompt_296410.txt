Draft eight to ten interview questions that press the substance of this view rather than restate it: include at least one on how she distinguishes her macro-convergence claim from standard evolutionary convergence arguments in biology; one on whether counterfactual history becomes empirically tractable under her framework or remains metaphysically speculative; one on how she treats the QWERTY lock-in narrative—specifically whether micro-level technological path dependence undermines her thesis of macro-robustness; and for each, provide a follow-up keyed to the reply she is most likely to give. Avoid generic biographical or introductory questions; every question should turn directly on the mechanics of branch-locked convergence.
