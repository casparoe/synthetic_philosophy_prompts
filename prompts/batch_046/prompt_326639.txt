Here's my opening move: I think Nozick's real mistake wasn't rejecting closure but being sloppy about method-individuation. Take the nested-method case: I track that my keys are on the table via direct seeing (M1), and I track the physical law 'if on table then not vaporized by secret goverment laser' via reasoning (M2). Closure wants me to know the conclusion, tracking says I don't because in nearby worlds where they are vaporized I'd still apply M2.
