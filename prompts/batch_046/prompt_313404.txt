(2) then the historian picks from that pile based on what they think matters now.

(7) that means history can never actually be about the past itself, only about this messy overlap.
(8) but that cant be right, because we do correct old histories when new evidence shows up, which means were tracking something real outside ourselves.

where does this chain break? i want to keep (1) and (8) if i can, and im willing to drop something in the middle, but i dont know what it costs me. is the mistake in (3) that the two filters dont actually mix together, or in (6) that theres a false choice between them? and if i reject (4), does that save the idea that we can understand past values on their own terms?
