In this system the content is fully available for report, reasoning, and behavior, but it is ontologically tagged as data about a simulated other rather than as part of the system’s own experience.

My own credence that this architecture is conceptually coherent is about 0.75; my credence that, if coherent, it possesses full access consciousness without phenomenal consciousness is about 0.65; and my overall credence that phenomenal consciousness therefore requires not merely global broadcasting but also successful ontological self-attribution—so that global workspace theory is insufficient for phenomenality without this added constraint—is about 0.50.

What is your actual credence in that overall claim? Give a specific number and one line of justification for each term, then show how they combine.
