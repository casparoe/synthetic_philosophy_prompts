Give an example of a conceptual issue internal to Confucian role ethics that arises when an official's duty of remonstrance (jian) toward a superior conflicts with the filial practice of concealment in cases of familial corruption—treating this not as an empirical legal puzzle but as a tension about whether yi (rightness) serves as a meta-role criterion that adjudicates between competing role obligations, or instead operates as an exceptional suspension that threatens the coherence of ritual propriety (li). The example should show how role-ethical reasoning might be compelled to require and prohibit the same action without appealing to a universalist moral framework outside the roles themselves.
