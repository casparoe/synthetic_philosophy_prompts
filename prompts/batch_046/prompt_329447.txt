I have recently become the primary caregiver for a parent with progressive dementia, a shift that has collapsed the temporal horizon within which I previously evaluated my life. I am now trying to determine which philosophical framework should govern my practical reasoning over the next several years, not merely which sounds correct in abstraction. Please rank the following four stances on meaning and absurdity into tiers—S, A, B, and C—according to their psychological sustainability and action-guiding density under conditions of prolonged, largely unrewarded care work, where objective achievement is minimal and the sense of futility is chronically high. The stances are: (1) a Camus-style absurdist rebellion that affirms life precisely through sustained confrontation with its lack of inherent purpose; (2) a Wolf-style fitting-fulfillment model that ties meaning to subjective fulfillment arising from engagement with objectively valuable projects; (3) a fundamentality view, of the kind associated with contemporary objective-list theorists, that grounds meaning in connection to transcendent or fundamental goods that retain value independent of personal satisfaction; and (4) a position I am tentatively calling recursive maintenance, which holds that meaning is generated neither by achieving valuable ends nor by experiencing fulfillment, but by the continuous interpretive labor of sustaining commitment to arbitrarily chosen but deliberately maintained structures of care, explicitly acknowledging their arbitrariness without attempting to transcend or justify it. For each tier, provide one or two sentences explaining why the stance lands there, focusing specifically on whether it supplies stable decision criteria when energy is depleted, whether it permits honest acknowledgment of the caregiving situation’s nature without self-deception, and whether it can accommodate sudden deterioration in the patient’s condition without collapsing. I am not seeking a bibliography, a survey of literature, or citations; I want a defensible ordering that reflects how these frameworks actually perform under sustained existential pressure, with particular attention to whether recursive maintenance offers a genuinely distinct practical profile or is merely a redescription of one of the other three.
