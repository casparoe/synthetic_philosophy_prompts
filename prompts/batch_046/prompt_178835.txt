Present the scenario in one concise paragraph, then deliver the analysis as exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences long, with no introduction or conclusion: item 1 should state the full judgment profile and the result of each of the three aggregation rules with brief reasoning; item 2 should identify the structural feature that produces the divergence between premise-based and conclusion-based aggregation; item 3 should explain what happens if that feature is removed and whether the case collapses back into unanimous agreement; item 4 should articulate how a defender of direct conclusion-based voting attacks the premise-based result; and item 5 should specify which element of the case is doing the essential work and whether stripping it away eliminates the discursive dilemma entirely.
