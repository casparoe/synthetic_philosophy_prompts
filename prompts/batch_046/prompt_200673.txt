hey so im a novelist working on something about free will and blame, and i keep testing my gut on cases to see what i actually think. maybe you can help me find the pattern, or tell me im just being a mess.

first, a guy raised in a cult and literally brainwashed since childhood to steal wallets. not blameworthy at all, that ones clear.

second, a woman who grew up terrified of poverty, worked herself to the bone, built a small company, and succeeded. she was shaped entirely by fear of ending up like her mom. i want to say she deserves credit, but then why is she different from the cult guy? both were molded by forces they never chose.

third, someone spends years in therapy deciding to hurt a rival, but every step of that "decision" was just genetics and trauma playing out. not blameworthy.

fourth, this is the one i keep flipping on: a writer spends a decade deliberately reshaping her own character, knowing she started with selfish instincts, and eventually becomes genuinely generous. is she responsible for the good she does now? part of me says yes, she authored the change. part says no, because she didnt author the willpower that made teh authoring possible. i cant settle it.

fifth, a character in my book kills someone, and i wrote it. am i blameworthy for the fictional death? no. but if i write it to glorify real violence, then maybe yes? i feel like theres a line there but i cant say where.

sixth, a society where 99 percent do art and one percent randomly does all the boring maintenance, well paid but with no choice in it. feels unjust to me, even if everyone is happy.

is there one principle behind all this? maybe something about being the true origin of your actions? i think Strawson has an argument that true responsibility needs you to be causa sui, which is impossible. does my pattern match that, or am i just inconsistent? especially that hard-working woman vs the cult guy, and the self-cultivating writer vs the therapy guy - they feel like they should be treated the same but i dont. also, what should i say about the maintenance-worker society? and is there any case where my instinct is just wrong and i should drop it?
