I need the slogan disambiguated. For each, indicate whether it is a near-platitude, a substantive but defensible claim, or false. Then tell me which of these readings actually supports the argumentative moves my classmate made—especially the slide between initiation and explanation—and which readings, if adopted consistently, would collapse the distinction between kalam and contingency arguments that our course has emphasized. Do not defend a single interpretation; present the claims as a menu, and identify which ones match the work the slogan was being asked to do in that debate.
