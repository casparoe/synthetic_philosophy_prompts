The goal is for them to grasp the distinction between frame messages, outer messages, and inner messages — specifically that meaning is constituted by the interpretive frame rather than being an intrinsic property of a symbol — without letting the idea dissolve into subjective relativism or crypto-mysticism about hidden codes.

Please do not explain the concept directly; instead, design the teaching plan. Present your response as a numbered list of exactly five items, each consisting of one or two sentences, with no introductory or concluding remarks.
