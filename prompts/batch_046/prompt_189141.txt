A person with severe OCD who performs an exact twenty-step washing ritual, yet reports the ritual as alien, seeks therapy, and eventually restructures it, I do not call sphexish: the rigidity reads as a malfunction within an underlying flexible agency. A bureaucrat who applies a tax rule with absolute literalness, but who reverses course instantly when shown a court order — with no reinterpretation of the order's meaning and no internal resistance — I do call sphexish: the correction works like moving the wasp's prey, an external input that triggers a new script rather than an internal revision.
