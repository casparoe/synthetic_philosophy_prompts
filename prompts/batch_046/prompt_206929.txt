It's not just changing your mind; it's using the same unreliable voice as both authoritative and bogus within the same debate, depending on which conclusion suits. Is there an established concept for that specific kind of double-bookkeeping? If not, can you name and define it in a way that seperates it from plain confabulation and from saying there's literally two people inside?
