Plan the explanatory arc for this audience. Then select one carrying example—other than the archer—to demonstrate control over internal judgement versus external outcomes, specifying both the necessary feature that makes it work and the feature it must avoid to prevent misreading. Next, list the misconceptions this group is likely to hold or develop, arranged in the order they will probably emerge, and indicate exactly where in the sequence each should be headed off. After that, propose a brief closing check that reveals whether participants understand the distinction as a discipline rather than as a licence for managerial detachment. Conclude by stating which element of traditional Stoic practice ought to be omitted at this level and why. Please write the response in British English.
