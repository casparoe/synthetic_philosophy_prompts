I'm presenting a seminar paper next week titled "Spandrels First: Inverting the Adaptationist Heuristic" to a philosophy of biology reading group. My thesis is that evolutionary explanation should treat structural and developmental constraints—spandrels in the Gould-Lewontin sense—as the primary explanatory targets, and treat adaptive explanations as residual hypotheses that must earn their place by ruling out constraint-based alternatives first. This inverts the standard adaptationist program without denying that adaptation occurs.
