I hold the following position. Phenomenal consciousness and access consciousness are conceptually and empirically dissociable, but global workspace theory systematically obscures this dissociation by treating global broadcast as constitutive of access in a way that makes the theory unfalsifiable with respect to localized phenomenal states. More specifically, I claim there is a coherent intermediate category—call it localized phenomenal binding—in which information is phenomenally integrated within a specialized processing module, for instance through localized recurrent loops in early sensory cortex, yet never achieves the system-wide availability that global workspace theory identifies with conscious access. On my view, such states genuinely contribute to subjective phenomenal character without being globally broadcast, which means global workspace theory misclassifies them either as non-conscious or as fully broadcast phenomenal states, depending on how its defenders adjust their criteria.
