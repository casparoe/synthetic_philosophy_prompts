Moore saying about friendship and beauty being intrinsic goods? there's this split. one side reads him as making a hard metaphysical claim — friendship and aesthetic enjoyment are just objectively valuable, and the organic unity idea means you can't break them down into smaller goods. the other side thinks he's basically describing human psychology: these are just the things that feel irreducibly worthwhile to us, not entries in some cosmic value-spreadsheet.
