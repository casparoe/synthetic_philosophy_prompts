Next, in The case for the conjecture (around 250 words), defend the view that this framing does real work. Maybe argue that if extinction only harmed the people who actually died, then a painless extinction after long happy lives wouldnt feel like a cosmic tragedy; Scheffler is trying to capture why its catastrophic even in that scenario. Work through the implications carefully.

Either defend a repaired version of the conjecture that survives this edge case, or argue that once you clean up the weird scenarios it collapses into a simpler harm-based account.
