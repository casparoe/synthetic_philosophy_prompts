I'm defending my thesis chapter at a comparative-law and poli-sci workshop next week. My claim is that democratic citizens should practice what I call "stochastic opacity" — deliberately adding noise to non-criminal data traces (cash not cards, skipping loyalty programs, etc.) — not to hide crimes but to prevent the state from reaching "behavoral lock-in" via predictive governance.
