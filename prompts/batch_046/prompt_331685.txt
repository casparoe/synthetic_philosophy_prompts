i think the key distinction people miss is between suffering that's instrumentally coupled to reproductive success (like acute stress during migration that actually improves fitness) and suffering that's evolutionarily decoupled (chronic parasitic load in long-lived k-selected mammals). any adequate theory has to keep those separate, because the intervention logic is totally different: reducing the first might break an adaptive system, reducing the second is more like treating a disease. but it also can't just import factory-farming analogies, since ecological feedback loops mean cutting predation stress can spike starvation stress three trophic levels down.

so here's my draft list of requirements. tell me if it's coherent, what's missing, and which ones are genuinely in tension versus just looking like it:

1. preserve the sentience-capacity / welfare-significance distinction — nociception in larval insects isn't automatically negligible, but you can't just declare "all sentient beings count equally" without argument either.
2. stay consistent with population dynamics: if an intervention lowers per-capita suffering but collapses the population, the theory needs a principled stance, not just an implicit "extinction is bad" smuggled in.
3. handle the replacement problem for r-strategists: saving a wild rabbit from predation doesn't save a specific future individual, it alters the reproductive pool. the theory can't pretend there's a stable identity of "the rabbit that would have existed."
4. work with partial ecological knowledge — it can't require omnisience about network effects to rank interventions.
5. distinguish human-caused wild suffering (climate-driven range shifts, habitat fragmentation) from natural predation, without relying on a naive natural/artificial split.

i feel like (2) and (3) together might push toward some aggregate-total-utilitarian framing that conflicts with (1)'s demand for graded status. is that a real tension or just my confusion? and is there a hidden desideratum about ecosystem intrinsic value that i'm accidentally smuggling in?
