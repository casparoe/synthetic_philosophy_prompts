I'm writing a paper in normative metaphysics and have come to defend a specific, somewhat stronger variant of the view that there are free-floating rationales—reasons that obtain independently of any actual or possible reasoner. Rather than the familiar claim that values exist mind-independently, I want to defend what I'll call modal-structural rationalism: the thesis that if a state of affairs S stands in a particular modal-structural relation to a valuable outcome V—specifically, if S is a necessary enabling condition for any possible realization of V across a non-empty, well-defined range of worlds—then S carries a non-derivative reason-status, even in worlds with no observers, agents, or mental states whatsoever. This is meant to ground intuitions about preservation—say, of biodiversity or of cultural archives—without smuggling in an implicit audience or future beneficiary.

What would count against this position? I am not looking for a generic critique of moral realism, but for the precise pressure points that target this modal-structural formulation. Which formal or conceptual results—concerning, for instance, the direction of explanation between value and enabling conditions, the individuation of worlds across modal space, or the logic of necessary conditions—would genuinely undermine it? Which empirical or thought-experimental cases—such as redundant enabling conditions, worlds where the structure is intact but value is permanently unrealizable, or cases where the enabling relation holds but is normatively inert—would be decisive rather than merely awkward? I also want an honest assessment of whether defenders of free-floating rationales have already qualified the view so heavily (restricting it to worlds with minimal observer-potential, or requiring that value be actually realizable) that it risks being insulated from refutation, and if so, whether that insulation is a theoretical virtue or a vice for a view of this kind. Please rank the potential defeaters, distinguishing what a committed modal-structural rationalist should themselves concede as counting against them from what only critics would press, and say plainly whether the position is genuinely exposed or has been rendered unfalsifiable in a damaging way.
