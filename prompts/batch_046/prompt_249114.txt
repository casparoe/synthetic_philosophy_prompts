case 1: my dad doesnt recognize me but still hums the songs he used to play. is the consciousness that was him still in there, just without access to memories, or is it already gone and whats left is just biological reflex?

case 2: split-brain patients after corpus callosum surgery. left hand grabs a cup, right hand puts it down, neither side knows why. two consciousnesses now? or one consciousness with a broken internal phone line?

case 3: i made up this scenario called the mirror-stream — imagine a single computing substrate running two attention loops that share every memory bank but are physically blocked from ever signaling each other. both loops report being the same self. one consciousness split in two, or two from the start?

case 4: people who come out of deep meditation or psychedelic states saying they dissolved into everything and the boundary was fake. should i read that as direct perception of schrödingers one-atman reality, or just a brain glitch?

case 5: identical twins separated at birth who both scream at the exact same second when one gets hurt miles away. shared consciousness leaking through, or just statistical noise?

my gut: case 1 still him, case 2 still one just fragmented, case 3 two separate ones for sure, case 4 literal but temporary, case 5 pure coincidence.

is there one principle that gets all five, and what does it predict for a case i havent listed — like conjoined twins with two heads arguing over what to eat? if not, which of my verdicts has to go, and does schrödingers vedanta unity thing actually survive once you fix the inconsistencies?
