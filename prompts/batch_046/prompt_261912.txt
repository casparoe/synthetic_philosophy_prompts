She maintains that this framing lets her avoid the classic self-refutation charge: she does not literally "believe" eliminativism in the folk-psychological sense, but rather her cognitive architecture currently assigns high predictive weight to outputs consistent with that view, while folk psychology retains its practical value at the interpersonal scale.

For each of these three, craft a sharp follow-up keyed to her most likely reply, especially replies that invoke predictive-error minimization or the macro-utility versus micro-ontology distinction, so that her standard deflections become progressively harder to sustain. The remaining questions should probe the boundary between her position and nearby views in philosophy of neuroscience, but please avoid generic biographical material or requests for reading lists.
