Third, for each crux, present a concrete case that both sides agree is genuinely relevant—specifically, a government adopting precautionary measures based on long-range ecological forecasts with acknowledged high uncertainty—and record in advance what each side would predict about the moral status of that belief should the forecasts later prove significantly overstated. Write in British English, avoid any bibliography or citation list, and do not include direct quotations from the original essays.
