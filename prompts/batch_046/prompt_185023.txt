At each step mark clearly where I’m just making a judgment call dressed up as procedure. Apply it to three cases: a discrete reservoir computer claiming it solves NP-complete problems in constant time; a hypothetical continuous-fluid system that says it decides the halting problem by measuring vortex positions with infinite precision; and one case where the checklist forces me to reject a claim that sounds intuitively plausible to me. Finish by saying exactly what this checklist flat-out cant do and when I should just punt the paper to an actual computability specialist instead of deciding myself.
