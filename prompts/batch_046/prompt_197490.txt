I'm on the advisory board for our city's labor standards commision and next week we vote on whether to back a 32-hour work-week proposal that includes mandatory "non-instrumental leisure" blocks — basically protected downtime employers can't justify as productivity recovery. The proposal leans on Weber's idea that capitalism makes leisure feel guilty unless it serves work, and cites karoshi deaths in Japan where overwork kills even in white-collar jobs. Some board members say this is just smuggling philosophy into labor law; they think leisure should only be defined negatively (time not working). Others say without a positive entitlement to genuinely non-productive rest, shorter hours just become wellness branding for capital. I need a short memo reccomending either the proposal or a modified version, with a concrete recommendation and honest treatment of the best objection. Don't lay out all sides and leave us hanging — take a committed position with clear reasoning we could actually turn into ordinance language. Keep it focused; we don't need a bibliography.
