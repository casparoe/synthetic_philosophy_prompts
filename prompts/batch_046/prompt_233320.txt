I have been reading a popular essay that summarizes the Rietdijk–Putnam argument for the block universe. According to the essay, Rietdijk started from the relativity of simultaneity: because different inertial frames disagree about which distant events happen "now," there is no physically privileged present slice. The essay illustrates this with a variant of the Andromeda scenario—if I sit at my desk today, there exists a frame in which my present writing is simultaneous with a distant supernova centuries in the future—then claims that since neither frame is more correct, that future event must already exist in the same ontological sense as my present action. The essay then describes Putnam's addition: he appealed to a transitivity principle for causal determinacy. If event A determines event B in my frame, and in another frame B is simultaneous with C, then A determines C as well, which allegedly shows that the entire four-dimensional manifold is fixed and that presentism is not merely implausible but empirically contradicted by special relativity. The author goes on to say that our intuitive sense of temporal flow is analogous to a flat observer mistaking one cross-section of a solid object for the whole thing, and that the argument only loses force if one rejects the conventionality of simultaneity—because, the essay claims, once we recognize that simultaneity is just a coordinate convention with no physical content, the Rietdijk–Putnam inference from frame-dependence to ontological equality collapses into a tautology about labeling rather than a metaphysical discovery. I am inclined to think the core idea survives these objections, but I want an accuracy audit: which parts of this summary are faithful to Rietdijk and Putnam, which parts conflate the determinacy argument with the Andromeda-paradox imagery, whether the claim about conventionality undermining the argument gets the philosophical dialectic backwards, and whether the conclusion that presentism is empirically refuted overstates what the physics actually delivers?
