I suspect he overstated the causal efficacy claim and that he may have conflated agent-independence with complete ontological isolation, but I am not sure whether the artificial-intelligence extension is a natural implication or an unfair extrapolation, nor whether the category-mistake objection actually addresses the version of the view that only asserts the existence of objective normative facts.
