Mira intends to honor a professional commitment she made to a colleague. The protocol never activates, because Mira never considers breaking the commitment. Crucially, however, Mira's reasoning for honoring the commitment is partly shaped by her general awareness that her society relies on such institutional safeguards, and she explicitly wants her actions to align with those norms, though she is unaware that this particular protocol is tracking her. Is Mira morally responsible for honoring the commitment?
