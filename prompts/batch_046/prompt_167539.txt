Please provide your credence, as a specific number between 0 and 1, that a sustained record of material prosperity and social order can, by itself, generate a genuine normative obligation to obey an authoritarian regime when citizens are systematically deprived of the causal and evidentiary information needed to distinguish competent governance from hidden extraction, demographic contingency, or suppressed alternatives — a condition call it radical epistemic asymmetry, exemplified in stylized form by regimes that deliver high growth and low disorder while concealing the counterfactual and distributional mechanics behind those outcomes. Decompose your reasoning into at least four terms with approximate probabilities: (a) that there exists a normatively coherent concept of output legitimacy that does not collapse into mere descriptive stability or into a concealed form of procedural consent; (b) that prosperity and order in the relevant cases genuinely depend on authoritarian governance structures rather than on contingent geographic, demographic, or historical factors that could persist under different institutions; (c) that widespread citizen acceptance under radical epistemic asymmetry can constitute genuine normative authorization rather than adaptive preference, informational captivity, or rational ignorance; and (d) that the absence of verifiable causal transparency undermines any claim to authority regardless of welfare outcomes. Show roughly how these terms combine to yield your overall number, identify which term your final credence is most sensitive to, and specify what empirical finding or conceptual argument would most shift that term. Do not treat this as asking about China or Singapore descriptively; treat them only as reference points for the type of regime in question.
