For each, provide a concise reply suitable for a two-minute response and explicitly flag where the reply is weakest and where I should concede ground up front. Include the objection I am most hoping nobody raises — specifically, whether my concept of normative inertia collapses into mere sociological conservatism or fails to distinguish religious commitment from ideological dogmatism — and advise me on how to defend it directly rather than deflect.
