Some members want to allow these credits to be redeemed for goods from outside merchants, while others insist they should remain in-network only. Drawing on Berkeley’s distinction between money as a mere token of exchange and the underlying labor or goods it represents, what are the most important conceptual implications for whether such skill-weighted credits can function as stable money, whether permitting external redemption undermines their representational role, and how the quarterly administrative calibration of exchange rates relates to Berkeley’s concerns about the stability of paper currencies backed by tangible assets?
