I've come to accept that the White Horse Dialogue in the Gongsun Longzi is best reconstructed as defending a closure-based essentialism about natural kinds: the kind horse is defined not by observable traits but by a closed property-space in which only horse-determinations are permitted, so that introducing the determination white—which lies outside that closure—produces an object that is not a horse in the strict sense, despite superficial resemblance. On this reading, the School of Names is advancing a metaphysics of kind-boundaries that anticipates formal accounts of determination, not committing a logical blunder about predication. What would count against this reconstruction? For instance, if Mohist texts treat color-properties as fully compatible with kind-membership without any metaphysical tension, does that undermine the claim that the School was working with closure conditions? If the dialogue's reasoning relies on an ambiguity between predication and identity that defenders must reinterpret away, is that fatal or merely awkward?
