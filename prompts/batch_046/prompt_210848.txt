I want an in-class game where they actually feel the difference between asking "is this act right?" and "would an idealized version of me endorse the practice this act belongs to?" To avoid the usual contractarian tropes, inject a reflexive endorsement condition: participants have to justify a rule not just from a neutral point of view, but also check that they'd still endorse the very idealization procedure they're using — like a second-order stability check.
