Does the slogan collapse the distinction between causal dependence on social agreement and outright ontological non-existence? And does it treat gender, race, and money as constructed in the same sense, or do the mechanisms diverge — for example, in the role of collective intentionality versus state authority? Give me the conceptual menu, not a reading list, since I need to report on this without access to a library.
