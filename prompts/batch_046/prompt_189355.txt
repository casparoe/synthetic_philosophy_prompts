The student answer reads as follows: "Margalit holds that ethical communities have an absolute duty to remember every past injustice, because shared memory is the sole bond of moral solidarity; forgetting anything is therefore a betrayal. So the two disagree directly: Margalit addresses groups and demands perpetual memorialization, while Rieff addresses individuals and endorses removal. If a community stops maintaining a memorial, Margalit would condemn it as failure, but Rieff would endorse it, since that lowers trauma. This proves they are opponents on one question. The idea of mnemonic justice—fairness about who remembers—is a separate issue that neither thinker actually addresses, so it doesn't help reconcile them."
