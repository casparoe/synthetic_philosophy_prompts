The satire should target the real tendency in psychiatric classification—analyzed by Ian Hacking under the concept of “looping effects”—to absorb the reflexive impact of diagnosis onto the diagnosed into administrative procedure, thereby treating ontological feedback as a manageable compliance risk rather than a constitutive feature of human kinds. The piece should be readable to someone unfamiliar with Hacking but should reward a specialist who recognizes that the Board’s language parodies the bureaucratization of reflexive critique. Stay in deadpan voice throughout; do not wink, break character, or signal irony explicitly.
