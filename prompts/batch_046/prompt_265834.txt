Give me your credence—decomposed, numerically explicit, and exposed to inspection—in the following precise claim: there exist genuinely free-floating normative reasons, that is, reasons that constrain what ought to be done, believed, or valued, which obtain entirely independently of any reasoner, mental state, social practice, evolutionary interest, or institutional framework, and which would retain full normative force in a counterfactual world permanently and irreversibly devoid of agents, observers, or institutions.

Please structure your response in exactly these sections, with approximate lengths as noted:

1. The Target Proposition (about 60 words). Restate the claim with sufficient precision that it has a definite truth-value, clarify what would count as its falsity, and briefly indicate what kind of evidence—conceptual or empirical—could in principle settle the matter.

2. Decomposition and Term Credences (about 250 words). Break your overall judgment into at least three constituent probabilities—suggested terms: (a) that normative realism of some form is correct; (b) that such realism does not entail a constitutive dependence on reasoners, valuers, or functional roles; (c) that evolutionary, game-theoretic, or constructivist explanations fail to account for all apparently free-floating normative phenomena.
