I work as a backend software engineer with no formal background in philosophy or jurisprudence, though after a long conversation with a colleague about recursive code structures I started reading introductory legal theory on my own. I have settled on a position about legal self-reference that I find structurally compelling: any rule that grants authority to amend the rules—including the amendment rule itself—functions like an unbounded recursive procedure with no base case, and therefore a functioning legal system must always depend on an implicit, non-amendable ground layer (whether a constitutional core, an interpretive framework, or an extra-legal political fact) that is not itself generated by the system's internal rules. What appears to be genuine self-amendment is, on this view, actually delegation to an external runtime environment that the formal rules do not control. I find this analogy to software architecture persuasive—without an unmodifiable bootstrap loader, a program that rewrites its own execution rules eventually corrupts its state—but I am unsure whether it survives contact with actual constitutional design or with formal work on legal paradoxes.

What would have to be true for this position to be wrong? I am asking for the observations, arguments, formal results, or real-world constitutional cases that should move me, ranked by how decisive they would be. Specifically: are there working examples of self-amending rules that stabilize without an external ground—perhaps through nested thresholds, temporal delays, or hierarchical meta-rules that are themselves part of the system? Would it be fatal if judicial interpretation turned out to be formally internal to the legal structure rather than an external runtime? Or merely awkward? I also want an honest assessment of whether defenders of this view typically protect it by redefining any apparently successful self-amendment as secretly dependent on a hidden ground layer, and whether that move is a legitimate theoretical refinement or a sign that the view has been insulated into unfalsifiability. Please do not provide a bibliography, reading list, or citations; I only want the substantive defeaters—including ones that may have already occurred in practice—sorted from those that would genuinely overturn the view to those that would just complicate it.
