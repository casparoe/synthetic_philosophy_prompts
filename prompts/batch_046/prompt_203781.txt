The host then says her Respuesta a Sor Filotea is basically a legal brief for gender equality rather than a spiritual autobiography, and that her later silence was just strategic withdrawal once she realised the Church wouldn't budge. It sounds mostly convincing but also a bit too modern. Is this a fair summary?
