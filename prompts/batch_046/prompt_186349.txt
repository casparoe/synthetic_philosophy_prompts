I'm preparing the closing session of a graduate seminar on statistical mechanics and the philosophy of time, and I'd like to end with a poem that captures a specific conceptual tension we kept returning to: the empirical underdetermination between the "past hypothesis" explanation of the thermodynamic arrow and the "Boltzmann brain" or spontaneous-fluctuation explanation. Most popular treatments present the arrow as if entropy simply increases toward the future, but our discussion kept circling the harder point that, if all we have access to is the present macrostate, a genuinely low-entropy past boundary condition and a random statistical fluctuation that assembled this very state—complete with false memories of a cooler past—are observationally indistinguishable from the inside. I want the poem to take the form of a villanelle: nineteen lines, five tercets and a concluding quatrain, with two rhyming refrains that take turns and finally appear together in the last two lines, because that compulsive return and final coupling can mirror the way these two explanations keep passing each other without resolution.

The first refrain should voice the past-hypothesis position—something like the claim that present traces are veridical because a genuinely low-entropy boundary state lies behind them. The second refrain should voice the fluctuation position—something like the claim that a present high-entropy sea can spontaneously dream the appearance of such a boundary and its records. The speaker should be an observer, perhaps a single statistical fluctuation that has become self-aware, looking backward at what it takes to be records of a cooler past. The poem's language should be accurate enough that someone who knows the debate—boundary conditions versus dynamics, the statistical-mechanical account of records, the Boltzmann problem—recognizes it immediately, but it should not read like a glossary or invoke named authors. The form should do the philosophical work: the refrains should track the two competing origins of the arrow, and the final quatrain should leave them sitting side by side with no internal criterion in the present macrostate to adjudicate between them, since that is exactly where our discussion stalled. No citations, no bibliography, no named texts—just the conceptual structure rendered through the villanelle's architecture.
