Please write exactly five brief remarks—arranged as a numbered list, each consisting of one or two sentences, with neither an introduction nor a conclusion—that use this scenario to structure what may and may not be claimed about subjective credence, personal identity across copies, and engineering redundancy. The remarks must avoid false technical assertions about consciousness, must treat contested philosophical positions as open rather than settled, must speak respectfully to both halves of the room, and must be rendered in British English.
