Invent one formal institutional protocol—such as a “Peasant Comprehension Threshold” with specific metrics—to operationalize Tolstoy’s accessibility requirement. Do not cite real museums, curators, or living figures; keep the venue fictional but realistic. The satire should land the standard objections to Tolstoy’s view—who decides which feelings are universal, whether accessibility collapses into anti-intellectualism, and whether emotional sincerity can be bureaucratically measured—without ever stating them explicitly.
