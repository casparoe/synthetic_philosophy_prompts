Read The Matrix through Zhuangzi's butterfly dream, but don't do the usual "are we in a simulation" take that every philosphy 101 essay writes. Zhuangzi's skepticism isn't mainly epistemic—it's about the dissolution of the self/other boundary during transformation (the butterfly and Zhuang Zhou are just phases of one continuous change, not two subjects wondering which is real). So where does Neo's split between Anderson and the One reproduce a Cartesian ego that survives the dream intact, and where do moments like the black cat déjà vu or the spoon-bending kid actually come closer to Zhuangzi's idea that identity is just a tempory configuration?
