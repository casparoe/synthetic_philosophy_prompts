I've been thinking about success cultures since I retired from the lab — American Dream celebrates winners, Jantelagen and the tall poppy syndrome cut them down. I now think they're not moral opposites but complimentry equilibrium stategies for managing the same social-envy dynamics: Dream channels comparison upward, Jantelagen suppresses it near the root. Same underlying problem, just different stable points. What would actually count against this synthesis? Sort the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward — historical cases where a society flipped from one mode to the other without any change in comparison behavior, psychological evidence that egalitarian norms don't reduce envy but just hide it, or formal results showing these can't be evolutionary stable pairs. Have I qualified this into unfalsifiability by calling every outcome an 'equilibrium'? Is that insulation a real problem? And tell me what defenders of either culture would themselves concede hurts the synthesis, not just what critics say.
