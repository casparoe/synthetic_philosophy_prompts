Write the desiderata that an adequate theory of psychiatric classification must satisfy if it is to accommodate Hacking-style looping effects without reducing classification to either pure social construction or fixed biological taxonomy. For each requirement, identify the specific phenomenon or distinction it protects—such as the difference between a diagnosis reshaping self-interpretation versus altering underlying neurobiology; the need to register institutional feedback from patient-advocacy groups without treating every diagnostic expansion as epistemic corruption; and consistency with epidemiological shifts that occur prior to formal manual revision. State which requirements are non-negotiable for any serious account and which reflect the preferences of particular camps (biological essentialists, strong constructivists, looping-interactionists). Then indicate whether any subset of these desiderata is jointly unsatisfiable, with particular attention to whether explaining rapid diagnostic inflation in adolescent populations can be reconciled with maintaining stable natural-kind boundaries for the conditions affected. Keep the emphasis on the criteria and their motivating cases rather than on scoring existing theories, though you may briefly note how biological-realist, pure social-constructionist, and looping-interactionist frameworks fare against the list.
