Please write the procedure as an ordered checklist: the questions I should ask, what counts as enough evidence for each, where it delivers a clear yes/no, and where it must stop and hand the call to a human because values are underdetermined. Give me a small set of practical tests I can apply without specialist gear, then run it on two cases: a four-day week in software engineering with high worker autonomy, and a compressed 60-hour fortnight in eldercare with low autonomy and high emotional labour. Make sure one of these produces a verdict I won't like, and flag the points where the procedure misfires — say, if a sector culture treats overwork as moral virtue. Be explicit about which steps are just judgement calls dressed up as protocol. Finally, say what would change your mind: which argument or finding would make you drop the 35-hour benchmark as a hard right, or accept that karoshi risk can sometimes be traded off against vocation-fulfilment?
