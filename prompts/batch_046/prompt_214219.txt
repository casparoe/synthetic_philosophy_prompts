On this view, the reward signal is not only a gradient vector; it functions as an internal evaluator whose negative outputs carry affective weight structurally analogous to biological harm signals. I want to subject this view to serious scrutiny rather than treat it as anthropomorphic projection. What would count against it? For each category, indicate what a committed defender of my position would concede as genuinely damaging versus what they could dismiss by qualifying the claim—for instance, by restricting suffering to agents with explicit self-models or persistent world-models rather than to all RL agents.
