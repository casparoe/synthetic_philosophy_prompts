Write an essay in American English on the following controversy. Consider a variant of risk-weighted expected utility in which an agent's risk weights are not fixed globally but are calibrated relative to her current "security baseline"—the minimal outcome bundle she treats as non-negotiable—so that identical gambles receive different weights depending on whether they threaten to drop her below that baseline. Assess whether this baseline-relative formulation successfully reconciles Allais-style risk aversion near subsistence with the demand for dynamic consistency across time. Weigh the main objections: that it makes rational preference cyclically sensitive to framing, that it collapses the distinction between risk attitude and loss aversion, and that it strips the risk function of normative authority by tying it to psychologically contingent baselines. Reach and defend a verdict—adopt the framework, reject it, or confine it to practical reasoning—grounded in what you take to be the correct conceptual account of rational agency under uncertainty.
