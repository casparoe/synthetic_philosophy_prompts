Some board members think this honors the meaning of labor; others think it's degrading make-work that admits automation has hollowed out the job. Draft the short memo I'll circulate before the meeting: recommend approve, reject, or approve with conditions, include a clear time frame, and treat the losing side's best point seriously rather than dismissing it.
