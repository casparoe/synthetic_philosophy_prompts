Before drafting, briefly brainstorm how Leibniz's actual moral metaphysics—moral evil arising from confused perceptions, justice as the charity of the wise distributed according to ontological rank, each substance being windowless yet mirroring the universe through pre-established harmony, and the present world being the best of all possible worlds—might be literalized into a modern institutional framework without losing internal coherence.

Write a deadpan parody formatted as a glossy corporate retreat brochure for an invented consultancy called Monad Partners: Executive Harmony and Ethical Optimization. The brochure advertises a three-day leadership retreat in which executives apply Leibnizian moral principles to workplace dynamics. The satire should expose the tendency of grand metaphysical moral systems—especially those combining determinism, hierarchical perfectionism, and the denial of genuine causal interaction—to dissolve concrete ethical obligations into cosmic harmony. Exaggerate genuine features: treat confused perceptions as HR performance reviews; treat petites perceptions, those sub-threshold sensory impressions Leibniz held influence judgment without awareness, as unconscious office micro-aggressions that aggregate into moral destiny; treat pre-established harmony as proof that team-building exercises are redundant because all outcomes are already synchronized; and propose that managers practice the charity of the wise by allocating resources in direct proportion to each employee's degree of ontological clarity, which in practice maps onto corporate rank.
