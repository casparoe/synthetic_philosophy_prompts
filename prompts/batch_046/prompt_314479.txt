Write a joke about conceptual slippage in a microdomain of your own invention—specifically, the migration of a single normative concept across three contexts where its criteria of application shift structurally rather than merely ambiguously. For concreteness, take authenticity as it travels from existentialist self-constitution, through artisanal food certification, to generative image evaluation, but feel free to substitute another concept and domain trio if it makes the punchline sharper. The joke should turn on the listener recognizing that the concept is not just being misapplied; its functional role changes—what serves as a criterion of origin in the first domain becomes a marketing predicate in the second and a statistical property (for instance, low perplexity relative to a training cluster) in the third. It should be genuinely funny to someone who tracks copycat concepts and fluid boundaries, not a generic joke about philosophers being out of touch. Keep it concise, and do not resolve the slippage—let the punchline land with the sense that the concept has escaped its original framework entirely.
