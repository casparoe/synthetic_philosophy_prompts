(2) if the environment fixes content for natural-kind terms, it should fix it for all empirically grounded concepts — including the ones we use to talk about minds and meaning, like "belief," "disagreement," and "translation."

(6) so either externalism is false, or it's restricted to first-order terms only — but then there's no principled reason for the restriction, since burge-style social exernalism applies to mental-state vocabulary too.
(7) therefore externalism either self-destructs or becomes ad hoc.
