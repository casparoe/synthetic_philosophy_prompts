The leaves should include the classic domination reading, a republican-navigation view, a prudential-adaptation position, and at least one unoccupied combination you diagnose as incoherent or unexplored: specifically, an ecological-symbiosis view that treats fortune not as enemy or backdrop but as an interdependent system that virtù must cultivate rather than overcome. For each leaf, give the position's name (or mark it unnamed) and one sentence on its most awkward commitment. Where two interpretations cannot be cleanly separated by your branches, flag the ambiguity rather than forcing a split. Separate what is settled in the primary texts from what is genuinely contested among specialists, and do not present a contested interpretive claim—such as the precise scope of virtù across The Prince and the Discourses—as if it were consensus.
