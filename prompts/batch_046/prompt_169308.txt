I would like you to write a poem for me that takes this precise problem as its subject. In the envoi, please bring all six words together in a final configuration that deliberately refuses to privilege one arrangement over the others, leaving the reader with the sense that the data remain genuinely open. The tone should be contemplative rather than despairing, and the language precise enough that someone familiar with the underdetermination thesis would recognize the specific problem being staged rather than a generic skepticism about knowledge. Please present your response entirely in continuous prose, with no headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables, writing as you might in a thoughtful letter to a friend.
