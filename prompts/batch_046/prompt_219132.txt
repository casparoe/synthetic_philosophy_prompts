Give me the objections in roughly the order they will come, phrased as those specific interlocutors would phrase them, with notes on what I should concede, what I should hold, and the weakest point in my null-reference comparison—especially where it might fail under modal scrutiny about counterfactual subjects—that I should prepare to defend rather than hope goes unnoticed.
