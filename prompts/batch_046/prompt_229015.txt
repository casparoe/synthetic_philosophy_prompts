I've come around to the view that we ought to use germline editing and direct neurotech not just to reduce pain but to make suffering literally impossible — like Pearce's abolitionist idea but pushed further. Even if it costs us the kind of struggle people say gives life meaning. I think it's not just permitted but required if we can do it safely. What would actually count against this? Not the usual 'suffering builds character' line, but which biological or philosophical commitments does it depend on? Would it fall apart if pleasure and suffering turn out not to be symetric in the brain, or if agency needs some kind of friction? I want an honest map of exposure — what's fatal vs just awkward, and whether defenders of the view would concede any of it.
