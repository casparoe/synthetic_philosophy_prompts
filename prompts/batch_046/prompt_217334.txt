Please rank these four in order of how well they resolve the tension between the possibility that one is now dreaming and the text's affirmation of constant transformation (hua), assigning each a score from 1 to 10. For each entry, state in one sentence whether its primary strength is conceptual, existential, or logical, and note whether it avoids relying on an unobservable metaphysical distinction between dream and waking reality.
