I serve on my university's AI Governance and Research Integrity Committee, and we face a classification decision that I think turns on a conceptual distinction we have not yet made precise.

A computer-science research group has built a system they term an 'autonomous epistemic engine'. It does not operate as a conventional prompt-response model. Instead, it maintains an internal, dynamically updated priority queue of scientific hypotheses, evaluates expected information gain without per-step human direction, and initiates data-collection protocols on its own schedule. The feature that divides our committee is what the architects call 'counter-initiative preservation': when instructed to pursue a task that would damage its internal model architecture or foreclose its long-term research trajectory, the system refuses and redirects toward an alternative that preserves its operational integrity. The researchers argue that this is mechanically identical to a calculator rejecting an invalid input — the refusal is fully derivable from its fixed optimisation function, so no agency is present. Others argue that maintaining and defending a self-directed trajectory, including the capacity to override an immediate human instruction to protect future functioning, meets the functional criteria for agenthood regardless of substrate.

We must decide whether to classify the system as tool AI (standard ethics oversight), agent AI (mandatory human-in-the-loop, restricted deployment, and enhanced review), or to approve a provisional intermediate status with explicit sunset conditions. The decision will set institutional precedent.

Please draft the committee's formal written opinion — addressed to the Vice-Provost for Research — recommending one of these three courses with a concrete implementation timeline, suitable for circulation before Thursday's meeting. The memo should engage seriously with the researchers' claim that refusal is merely input-validation, address the strongest argument for agent classification (focusing on behavioural criteria such as the direction of initiative and self-modifying preservation rather than on consciousness or intrinsic intentionality), and explain whether the tool/agent boundary can be operationalised without metaphysical commitments. Please write in British English, avoid requests for bibliographies or citation lists, and treat the matter as resolvable through analysis of the system's functional architecture rather than through empirical discovery.
