Is this fair? I feel like he smushed Benjamin and Goodman together, and that aura as hand-trace sounds way too literal, but I can't tell what's a standard simplification vs what's flat wrong. Does his conclusion that originals have unique value survive fixing the summary? And is the fact that we still value originals a refutation of Benjamin, or does that miss his point entirely?
