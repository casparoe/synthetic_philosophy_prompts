We keep circling. Is this actualy a disagreement about what counts as knowledge — like one camp using knowing you're cold to mean occurent conscious access and the other using it for a stable dispostional state? Or is there some hidden empirical claim about how temperature perception works that's doing all the work? And if it's just bedrock intuitions about authority, is there anything — maybe a formal model of graded belief — that could move it, or does the stall mean the question is ill-posed?
