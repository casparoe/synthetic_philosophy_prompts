okay so heres something that bugs me about mental health policy. in a lot of countries if you have depression you can get CBT and an SSRI at the same time, and the clinical guidelines actually say combining them works better than either alone. but nobody in the system seems to worry that the beliefs you're restructuring in therapy are just the mental side of the exact same brain-chemistry shift the pill is causing -- if the pill already fixes the brain state, why would tweaking your thoughts add anything extra? what theory of mental causation does that dual-track practice preseuposes? does it need mental states to be causally efficacious over and above the physical, or could an identity theorist say the two treatments are just two descriptions of one causal chain? and if you take the causal exclusion arguement seriously, isn't this whole institutional setup philosophically incoherent?
