I've been working through the material constitution literature—Thomson's classic puzzle, the hylomorphic responses, the four-dimensionalist alternatives—and I've settled on a position I haven't seen defended in exactly this form. I hold that the statue and the clay stand in neither identity nor standard constitution, but in an asymmetric relation of formal determination without any additional form-entity: the statue is a four-dimensional object comprising exactly those spatiotemporal parts of the clay that fall under a modal constraint imposed by artistic intention. The constraint is not a separate substance or universal instantiated in the matter; it functions only as a boundary condition on which parts belong to the statue across time. So when the clay is squashed, the statue does not survive not because a form was destroyed, but because no continuous spatiotemporal region of clay-parts remains that satisfies the constraint. I take this to avoid both the "too many objects" problem and the mystery of non-material forms, yet I'm aware it may collapse into one of its rivals under sufficient pressure.

What would count against this view? I want an honest map of its exposure, not just a list of standard objections. Specifically: which observations or thought experiments would be decisive—showing, for instance, that the modal-constraint story cannot distinguish statue-loss from mere reshaping, or that artistic intention cannot bear the ontological weight I'm assigning it? Which formal results in mereology or modal metaphysics, if accepted, would render the view redundant or incoherent? What do defenders of pure four-dimensionalism and hylomorphism themselves concede would damage their own positions, and does my hybrid inherit those same vulnerabilities? Is there an unfalsifiability risk in retreating to "the constraint simply no longer applies," and if so, is that insulation a virtue or a defect for a metaphysical claim of this kind? Please rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, include what a committed defender of this hybrid view would be forced to concede, and give your assessment of whether the position has been qualified into unfalsifiability—plus whether that matters here.
