Please map the misconceptions I should expect and the precise point in the sequence to defuse each: that occasionalism makes scientific prediction impossible; that it collapses into Hume's constant conjunction because both deny necessary connection; that it requires a continuous miracle for every event in a pejorative sense; and that al-Ghazālī is therefore hostile to natural philosophy. Include a brief check—one targeted question or short exercise at the end—that reveals whether a student has grasped the ontological claim about the absence of intrinsic causal powers rather than merely repeating the fire example.
