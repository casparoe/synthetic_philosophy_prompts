Emmanuel Macron then assembled La République En Marche!—explicitly branded not as a party but as a movement—recruited parliamentary candidates from civil society and from the wreckage of the old parties, won the presidency, and governed through a parliamentary bloc that quickly developed its own discipline, media protocols, and programmatic orthodoxy.
