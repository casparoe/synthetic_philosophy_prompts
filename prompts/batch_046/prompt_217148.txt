Half the panel thinks this proves a vulnerable civilization is trying to establish credibile cooperation; the other half thinks it's classic dark-forest bait — a weak sender luring stronger ones into revealing their own position. Our funding council wants a concrete recommendation: approve the $40M active-response grant to reply with our own beacon within 30 days, reject it and maintain radio silence for a 90-day observation window, or approve with conditions. I need a formal memo I can circulate before Thursday's vote. It should recommend one specific course with a timeline, take the dark-forest reasoning seriously rather than dismissing it as fiction, and honestly address the strongest argument on the losing side — probably that silence reads as hostility or that we have a moral duty to answer if someone is reaching out. Keep the reasoning self-contained; don't just list philosophers I can't verify. Write it like an opinion the panel can actually act on.
