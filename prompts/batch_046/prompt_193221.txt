Write a dialogue between two equally competent philosophers—one who maintains that gross brain size carries independent moral weight and one who rejects this view entirely—debating the hypothetical case of an engineered human population possessing brains approximately three times the standard volume but with altered synaptic density that yields no increase in cognitive, emotional, or social capacity. The characters should engage in genuine back-and-forth, responding directly to each other’s specific points rather than speaking past one another, and neither should be reduced to a strawman. Their discussion should examine whether moral status tracks anatomical scale, functional capability, or emergent relational properties, and should avoid appeals to bibliographies, citations, or authoritative lists.
