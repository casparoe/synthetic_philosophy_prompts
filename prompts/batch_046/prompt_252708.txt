My argument sketch: I distinguish "positive presence" goods from "structured absence" goods. I apply this to a concrete case—penal rehabilitation—arguing that a prison program should be judged not by whether inmates achieve "flourishing" but by whether it durably eliminates craving (for status, for substances, for dominance) and suffering, with all other outcomes instrumentally justified only insofar as they stabilize those absences.
