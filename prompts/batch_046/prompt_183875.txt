2. i still talk mentalistic every day ("i want coffee", "she thinks it's raining") and don't think that's hypocritical; it's like saying "the sun rose"—a pragmatic fiction that works for coordination.

5. the right replacement isn't just neuroscience but predictive processing: what we call "beliefs" are misinterpreted Bayesian priors and prediction errors, so eliminativism is fixing a category mistake about inference.

does this hang together? i'm especially worried that (5) undermines (1) by reducing beliefs to priors rather than eliminating them, that (4) might apply to my own trust in predictive processing since that's evolved too, and that (3) might clash with (5) if predictive processing eventually explains away qualia, though i'm not sure wether that's required.
