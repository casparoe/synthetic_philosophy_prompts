My opening challenge: Consider a crystal vase that is genuinely fragile—disposed to shatter when struck with sufficient force—embedded in a protective gel that activates only upon impact. Is the gel functioning as a fink (the vase loses its fragility in vacuum) or as a mask (the fragility is present but blocked)?
