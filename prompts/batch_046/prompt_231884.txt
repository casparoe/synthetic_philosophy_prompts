Before I let that idea govern my characters' motivations, I want to know exactly what it could be claiming. Could you lay out, as a precise menu rather than a single interpretation, the distinct theses concealed in that slogan? For each reading, indicate whether it is trivially true, defensible within Weber's framework, false on his own terms, or actually supportive of the narrative move I am considering—namely, treating my protagonist's Calvinist dread as a direct and sufficient cause of a banking innovation. I need the answer in American English, and I am working without access to a library, so please do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or specific citations; I need the conceptual disambiguation itself.
