For each leaf, one sentence on its most awkward commitment. Flag any pairs your tree can't tell apart — i think egalitarian and some contractualist views might land on the same branch here. Don't ask people "are you a Rawlsian?" — phrase every node as a concrete claim or case they could answer cold. Keep it under ten nodes and don't bother with citations.
