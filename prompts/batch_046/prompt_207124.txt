I want to evaluate five proposed ways to privilege “green” over “grue” without appealing to authority or tradition.

D. Induction requires no rational justification; evolutionary adaptive heuristics happen to favour colour-constant predicates, so the paradox is dissolved rather than solved.
E. Only predicates invariant under the symmetry transformations of our standard measurement apparatus—e.g., time-translation for colour observation—should count as projectible.

Then, in a final paragraph, state whether these five responses collectively solve Goodman’s problem or merely relocate the difficulty—to the choice of prior, symmetry group, or evolutionary story—and whether a small language model without web access could apply this reasoning consistently.
