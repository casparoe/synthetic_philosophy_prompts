Can the 'I' remain a genuinely unified strange loop when its feedback cycles extend across substrates outside the skull, or does such distribution necessarily fragment the loop into something that can no longer sustain the self-referential closure required for a single, coherent self? I would appreciate an explanation that addresses whether external cognitive scaffolding complements the loop's integrity or instead undermines the very conditions that allow the symbol 'I' to function as a strange loop in the first place.
