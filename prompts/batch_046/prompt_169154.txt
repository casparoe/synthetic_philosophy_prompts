We receive roughly eight to ten proposals each semester, must reach unanimous agreement plus advisor sign-off, and have no access to domain experts or external evaluators. Our members rotate every semester, so any decision framework must be runnable by teenagers with no specialized training, usable within our meeting time, and honest about which steps are judgment calls disguised as procedure.

Please turn the importance-tractability-neglectedness cause-prioritization framework into a reusable decision procedure my committee can apply proposal by proposal. The procedure should specify, in order: the exact questions to ask; what counts as a sufficient answer to each; where the procedure stops with a clear “no”; where it hands the decision to human judgment because the question is one of values rather than facts; and which distinctions—such as global versus local neglectedness—must survive the simplification and which can be dropped.

Please organize your response exactly as follows:

The deliverable should be something we can print, tape to the wall, and run in under forty-five minutes. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations to specific texts.
