(6) That means either we treat every past decision as equally fallible and nothing is locked (so rights are just legacy code we can refactor whenever), or we treat every past decision as equally authoritative and nothing can ever be changed, including the lock mechanism itself — which is an infinite regress.

(7) Clearly neither horn works. We want rights pinned but energy policy debatable. So one of the steps above has to be wrong.

Is there an equivocation between individual identity (same Odysseus) and collective identity (totally different citizens)?
