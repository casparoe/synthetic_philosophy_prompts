What are the distinct precise theses that slogan could actually be expressing here? Lay them out as a menu: the bare logical truism, a methodological rule for theory construction, a metaphysical claim about mental taxonomy, an epistemic claim about justification, and any stronger normative versions. For each reading, say whether it genuinely threatens disjunctivism or whether the disjunctivist can consistently accept it, and identify whether the real work is being done not by the slogan itself but by a hidden premise—such as that introspective indistinguishability entails sameness of fundamental nature. I want this as a focused list I can bring to the discussion without misrepresenting either side, with the readings that actually support her dismissal clearly marked.
