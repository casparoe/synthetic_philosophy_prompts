Write a short dialogue between a modal realist and an ersatz actualist about whether saying 'I could have learned cello' literally refers to a concrete person playing in another universe, or just a consistent description of this world. Both need to be sharp — the realist should defend counterpart theory, respond to the causal-isolation worry, and explain why abstract worlds can't do the same job; the actualist should hit back on parsimony and ask why we should populate reality with infinite concrete worlds just to save modal talk. Make sure they actually respond to each other, not talk past there arguments. Keep it casual; have one speaker misspell 'counterpart' as 'counter-part' and maybe mix up 'there' and 'their' in one line.
