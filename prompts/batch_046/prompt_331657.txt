Hofstadter's old argument was that statistical translation never grasps meaning, it just reshuffles surface patterns. So what theory of linguistic equivalence is baked into that workflow? Does treating distributional overlap as enough for informed-consent translation presuppose that meaning just is statistical correlation? Or could a deflationary view—where 'understanding' is just successful prediction—license skipping ambiguity markers? And why do we still require human interpreters for courtroom testimony but accept statistical output for medical consent when both are life-altering? Is the practice consistent, or does it quietly presuppose two incompatible theories of what translation is for?
