Flag which desiderata are non-negotiable for any prioritarian and which belong only to particular formalizations — for instance, continuous concave social welfare functions versus lexical thresholds — and identify any subset that is jointly unsatisfiable given standard social-choice constraints, since mapping that tension is the central aim of my course.
