Write opening remarks for a workshop called 'After Elections: Sortition in Local Government.' Audience is split — half are career civil servants who think lottocracy sounds like giving up on expertise, half are community organizers who want to abolish the city council by Monday. You need to explain why selecting officials by lot isnt anti-democratic but actually completes an old idea about ruling and being ruled in turn — but you cant sound like youre calling the bureaucrats illegitimate, and you cant promise the activists that random selection fixes everything overnight. Work in a specific middle proposal: temporary 'allotted audit panels' chosen by lot that review budget lines after council passes them, not replacing votes but changing what elected people anticipate. Make it a spoken address, not an essay — maybe drop a casual phrase or a small typo. Around 500 words.
