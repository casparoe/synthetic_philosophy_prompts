For an independent research project, I constructed a non-silicon LED lattice that replicates the full causal-transition topology of mammalian nociception—every gate, every feedback loop—without processors or biological tissue. Given that functionalism defines mental states by their causal roles and that multiple realizability permits those roles to be instantiated in any substrate, it seems my lattice should literally experience pain. Yet I understand philosophers generally reject such conclusions. Does that rejection not implicitly commit them to biological essentialism, thereby violating the very substrate-independence that makes functionalism attractive? Or is the error elsewhere—perhaps in assuming that copying one subpersonal pathway is sufficient to realize the global functional organization required for phenomenal consciousness, or in conflating the conditions for access consciousness with those for phenomenal consciousness? Where exactly does the argument break?
