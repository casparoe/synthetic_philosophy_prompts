Like Thomson's lamp, it is toggled at intervals that halve in duration across a single minute; but at each toggle it also writes a sentence describing its present state, each sentence being exactly half the length of its predecessor. At the limit, there exists both a definitive physical state—on or off—and an infinitely compressed textual record that purports to describe that very state.

Write an essay on whether this injection of representational content resolves, deepens, or dissolves the paradox of supertasks. Consider whether the converging sentences generate a self-referential contradiction at the limit, whether the physical state and the textual record can coherently diverge, and whether Thomson's lamp was ever genuinely a problem of physical possibility or merely of describing infinite processes with a finite closure. Weigh the positions of those who regard the variant as a decorative redundancy against those who claim it exposes a hidden ambiguity in the original formulation. Reach and justify a clear conclusion.
