Write the full specification of desiderata that any adequate explanation of the historical divergence in sustained mechanized industrialization — framed as the contrastive question "why Britain and not China?" around the late eighteenth century — must satisfy in order to count as well-posed rather than as mere comparative description. Your list should cover: (1) the counterfactual stability conditions the explanation must maintain, such as holding constant China's coal geography, labor-market structures, and state fiscal capacity, and Britain's colonial resource flows and institutional frameworks for intellectual property, together with the reason why instability in any of these undermines the contrast; (2) the conceptual distinctions it must preserve without collapse, including those between necessary conditions, sufficient conditions, and enabling conditions; between technological capacity and the institutional incentives actually to deploy it; between unitary-state agency and distributed economic behavior across regions and social classes; and between a genuinely open historical path and one rendered structurally inevitable; (3) the teleological or essentialist assumptions it must avoid, with a concrete example of how each distorts the comparison; (4) the empirical findings from economic and technological history that it cannot contradict, particularly regarding comparative levels of market integration, agricultural productivity, and scientific knowledge in Britain and Qing China prior to divergence; and (5) whether the explanation must identify a single decisive factor or may be multi-causal without losing force, and whether that demand is itself negotiable. For each desideratum, provide the motivating failure — the specific confusion or explanatory collapse that occurs when it is ignored. Then flag any pair of desiderata that appear jointly unsatisfiable, such as the tension between counterfactual specificity and structural generality, or between requiring institutional uniqueness and requiring replicability across cases. Finally, identify the single desideratum that, if enforced strictly, eliminates the largest share of existing institutional, geographical, and cultural accounts, and briefly indicate whether prominent explanations in those camps satisfy or violate it. Keep the weight on the criteria and their motivating failures rather than on scoring particular historians.
