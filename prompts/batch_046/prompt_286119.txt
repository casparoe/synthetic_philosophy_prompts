The standard philosophical discussions of origin essentialism seem to assume a single nuclear genome packaged in a standard egg, which does not map cleanly onto my situation, and I want an instrument I can actually use to locate my own commitments without relying on academic labels I do not fully grasp.

Please construct a taxonomy, in the form of a decision tree with clearly labeled branches, that classifies the positions one can hold on whether a person is essentially tied to the particular reproductive materials from which she originated.
