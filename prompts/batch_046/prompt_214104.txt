But Leibniz's principle actually comes in two forms. The weak version says that if two objects share all properties they're identical. The strong version extends this to properties and relations in the whole world. In Black's case, the spheres do share all intrinsic properties, but they don't share relational ones: sphere A is 2 meters to the left of sphere B.
