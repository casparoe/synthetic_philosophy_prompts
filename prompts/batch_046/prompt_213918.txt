I am moderating a podcast interview with a philosopher who defends a position she calls causal pluralism about mental properties: mental properties are neither reducible to physical properties nor epiphenomenal, yet they do not violate the causal closure of physics because mental causation functions as framework-dependent selection among physically closed causal chains—comparable to how software selects among hardware-level computations without altering physical laws. Draft eight to ten interview questions that probe the substance of this view rather than her biography. For each of these three, include a brief anticipation of her most likely reply and a sharp follow-up designed to prevent retreat into general appeals to emergence, supervenience, or multiple realizability. Keep the tone formal and suited to an academic audience.
