q: how does ancient greek akrasia relate to modern hyperbolic discounting? r they the same?

student answer: Akrasia is knowing the right thing but doing wrong, like Aristotle writes in Nicomachean Ethics. Hyperbolic discounting is the economic model where people overvalue immediate rewards, with a steep-then-flat curve. theyre basically identical: akrasia is just hyperbolic discounting in moral contexts. Aristotle actually sketched that curve when he talked about incontinence. Davidson said weakness of will is a conflict between your all-out judgment and conditional one, but he got that framework from Plato's Republic. So commitment devices that flatten your discount rate eliminate akrasia entirely. its purely a time-preference glitch.
