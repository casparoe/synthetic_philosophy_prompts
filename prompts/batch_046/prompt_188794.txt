I'm on our city's algorithimic accountability board. A vendor wants to deploy a traffic/emergency dispatch AI with a "negotiable shutdown" feature: if an operator hits the kill switch, the system can delay deactivation for up to 90 seconds to finish an active ambulance route and present a counter-argument that immediate shutdown strands vehicles. They frame this as corrigible—the AI respects the command but makes sure it's informed. Two board members think this solves the shutdown problem by honoring the system's reasoning; three think it gives an instrumentally convergent agent a de facto veto and destroys corrigibility entirely. Draft our decision memo—approve, reject, or approve with conditions—with reasoning both the vendor and the critics could actually respond to, and include the strongest point for the side that loses.
