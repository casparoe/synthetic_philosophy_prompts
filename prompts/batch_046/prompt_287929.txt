hey, I've been thinking about psychiatric categories and there's this weird second-order thing I keep noticing but don't have language for. Say clinicians invent a new diagnosis based on some behavour cluster. People who get the label start online communties and come up with shared metaphors, checklists, even specifc phrases for their experience. Then after a few years, clinicians start treating those phrases as diagnostic markers — like if you don't say you feel "burnt out in a specific way" or use the community's checklist, they think you're either faking it or it's mild. So people with the actual underlying issue but no exposure to the community miss out, while people who've learned the script get flagged. The loop isn't just the label changing behaviour; it's the community's response to the label getting hard-coded back into the clinical criteria, almost as if the pushback against the diagnosis becomes part of the diagnosis. Does this have an established name? How is it different from the standard classification-feedback loop? If not, I'd call it something like "discursive capture" but I want to know if I'm just reinventing something that already exists. No need for sources, just conceptual clarification.
