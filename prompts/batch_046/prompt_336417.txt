prepare me for the objections in the order they'll hit me. phrase them like those people would actually talk—like the daoist scholar's specific worry about anachronism, and the analytic philosophers pushing on whether this avoids the deprivation problem rather than solving it. for each one tell me what to concede, what to hold, and where my reply is weakest. also tell me the objection im secretly hoping they dont ask, and how to survive it if they do.
