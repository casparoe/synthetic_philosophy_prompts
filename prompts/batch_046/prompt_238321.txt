I sense, however, that I may be applying a medieval argument about the soul to modern computing in a confused way. Could you address whether Avicenna's reasoning actually implies that embodiment is irrelevant to consciousness, clarify where the analogy between a suspended human and a language model breaks down, and identify the most important implications that genuinely follow for evaluating machine minds versus those that depend on misreading the original argument?
