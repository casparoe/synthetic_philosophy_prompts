Here is my opening challenge: if melody is the history of the Will’s striving made audible, and if harmony expresses the vertical ordering of the Will’s objectification, then what are we to make of a computer-generated Bach fugue—structurally perfect, yet produced with no embodied performer and no resonating instrument? Is it genuine music in your metaphysical sense, or only an empty acoustic simulacrum? Respond in a single paragraph, defend your position with the actual resources of your philosophy, and remain in character until I say “stop.”
