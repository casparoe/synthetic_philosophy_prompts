She must now decide on a proposal to apply targeted, reversible contraception to a native but overabundant herbivore population, which would reduce juvenile starvation mortality but also lower total density, alter predator hunting dynamics, and shift the age-structure of suffering without direct killing. Predict her verdict, articulate at least two competing principles that fit her track record—such as a localization-and-reversibility constraint versus an aggregate-suffering calculus—state which you think she is actually running and why, and specify the single further case that would best distinguish those principles.
