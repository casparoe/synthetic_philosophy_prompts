Do these commitments hang together? Specifically, is there a tension between accepting (2)'s arithmetic and denying (3)'s aggregation, and does (4) smuggle back the very aggregation I reject? Please audit the tensions, rank them by severity, indicate the least costly revision, and write your response in British English.
