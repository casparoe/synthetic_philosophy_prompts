I reply that this illicitly converts an epistemic possibility into a moral weight, that the argument is reflexively unstable because it applies to the simulators and generates an infinite regress without a fixed point, and that even nested ontology does not dissolve normative obligations to distant futures; yet she treats my response as changing the subject from expected value to metaphysical stubbornness. We agree on the empirical premises about computing growth, on the formal structure of the trilemma, and that suffering is bad; we even agree that fanaticism needs scrutiny.
