My partner's Polish and over Christmas her uncle kept going on about national character — said Poles are naturally more hospitable and collective-minded than the British. I rolled my eyes, but then I noticed her mum genuinely won't let anyone leave without feeding them first, and my lot wouldn't dream of it. Now I'm meant to write a comparison piece for work and I'm paralysed. If I say Poles tend to be more welcoming, am I just recycling a stereotype, even if I've seen it up close? And there's this other thing bothering me — I read about two spheres in space that are totally identical in every physical way but still counted as two. So I keep wondering: if Britain and Poland were exactly alike in history, geography, everything except this so-called national character, could that difference even exist on its own? Or does it always have to come from something concrete? I'm definatley stuck on whether I'm being overly cautious or genuinely wrong to even think about writing it.
