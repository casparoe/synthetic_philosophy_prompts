I'm moderating a Q&A for my phil of mind course with a visiting speaker who defends liberal functionalism plus this twist she calls intra-systemic multple realizability — the idea that one mental state in a single person can be realized by totally different neural circuits over time, not just across different brains. I need 6-7 interview questions that go beyond restating the view. At least one should press the disjunction problem: if the realizers inside one brain share no physical commonality, what makes them all the same mental state? And include a follow-up for her likely reply that functional organization unifies them. Another should adapt Searle's Chinese Room to her view: if the rooms symbol-manipulation rules keep getting rewired by an external maintainer, does it still have intentionality, and does her account say yes or no? Then a follow-up keyed to her saying thats just a research program, not an objection. One more should ask whether her view can distinguish genuine belief-change from just hardware replacement. No generic biographical stuff please.
