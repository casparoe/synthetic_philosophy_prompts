ANSWER:

The paradox is well documented: Sweden and Norway show bigger occupational gender splits than Turkey or Algeria despite stronger equality norms. Internalists read this as nature finally showing through once culture stops suppressing it; biological differences in interest are real and were just hidden. Externalists reply that egalitarian societies actually expand the menu of gender-typed options—better part-time infrastructure, longer parental leave—so people rationally choose paths that match culturally gendered roles. That means culture didnt disappear; it just changed shape. For justice, if preferences are externally built, then letting people choose freely just reproduces the same gendered patterns, so autonomy-based defenses of gender-neutral outcomes are incoherent—you cant defend free choice and demand statistical parity at the same time. But externalists also cant fall back on revealed preference, since theres no authentic inner self being revealed. So the only consistent position is that justice requires actively redesigning the choice architecture, not just removing formal barriers. The paradox therefore undermines naive internalism but gives externalists a real task.
