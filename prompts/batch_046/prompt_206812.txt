Second, if those same eye discs show up in a garbage pit far away, broken and mixed with cooking scraps, I think it's probably just trash or reuse, though part of me still wonders if they were ritually broken first. Third, when every single house in a village has the exact same goat bone hidden inside the wall, always the left femur, that feels like proof of a shared household belief or rule. Fifth, one huge carved pillar standing alone in a field shouldn't make me think everyone worshipped it, but if thousands of tiny ugly copies are found inside ordinary homes across the region, then I do think it was a widespread belief.

So is there a principle that sorts these out—maybe something about repetition in domestic versus functional spaces, or about whether the object serves a practical purpose?
