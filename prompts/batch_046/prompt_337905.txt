I serve as the student delegate to my school district's Committee on Program Sustainability. By March 15, we must submit a formal recommendation on whether to maintain our school's three autonomous academic tracks—STEM, Humanities, and Arts—as separate administrative units or consolidate them into a single integrated division. Preservationists argue that fifty years of separate institutional identity justify continuity; reformers cite budget contraction and warn that maintaining distinct bureaucratic structures risks overextension analogous to imperial expansion without consolidation. Please draft the memo I will circulate before our vote. It should recommend one concrete course of action with a phased implementation timeline; treat the preservationist argument with intellectual honesty, identifying its strongest point; and apply reference-class forecasting, base-rate reasoning about institutional consolidation, and the outside view of imperial lifecycles to determine whether historical longevity predicts viability in our case. The reasoning should be self-contained, without requests for bibliographies or external citations.
