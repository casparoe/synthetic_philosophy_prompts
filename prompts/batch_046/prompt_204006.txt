I need a conceptual disambiguation of that slogan, not a historical exegesis. Please lay out the distinct precise theses it could be expressing — treating them as a menu rather than selecting one — across at least the following dimensions: (1) an epistemic claim about how self-knowledge is acquired; (2) a metaphysical claim about whether the self is a substance distinct from the body; (3) a functional claim about which causal or informational processes awareness requires; and (4) an analogical claim about whether the argument's conditions apply to non-biological systems. For each reading, state whether it is trivial, whether it is substantive and defensible on Avicenna's own terms, whether it is false or overstated, and specifically whether it licenses my colleague's inference about artificial intelligence. Finally, identify which reading he is most likely invoking to bridge from a suspended human being to a disembodied software process, and pinpoint the weakest premise in that bridge. No bibliographies, citations, or reading lists are needed; I want only the analytical breakdown I can use in the discussion.
