Provide a model answer of approximately 800 words that takes a clear position—either defending the claim that nested monitoring closes the flicker loophole entirely and thereby strengthens the anti-PAP argument, or arguing that second-order suppression itself constitutes a hidden branch point that revives PAP at a structural level—together with a marking scheme organized around five must-hit analytical moves, each assigned a specific point value out of 20 total, plus a one-line note on the characteristic error that costs marks for each move. The model answer must end with a brief reflexive paragraph identifying the specific empirical finding or philosophical revision—such as a demonstrated dissociation between deliberative suppression and phenomenal awareness, or a collapse of the access/phenomenal distinction in agency—that would cause the answer to abandon its central verdict, and it should flag the step in its reasoning that is most likely to fail under scrutiny.
