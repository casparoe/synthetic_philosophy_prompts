Because the change is one-way and I must decide now, I am trying to understand whether standard philosophical frameworks even apply.

Construct a realistic thought experiment (no exotic physics, no perfect predictors, no demons, no longer than a paragraph) built around this structure: an irreversible transformative choice that is fully describable propositionally yet involves first-person phenomenal restructuring that is incommensurable with the pre-choice conceptual framework, while leaving narrative continuity and behavioral choice data intact.
