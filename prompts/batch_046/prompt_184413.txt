student answer:

knobe showed that when a ceo harms the environment as a side-effect people call it intentional, but when he helps they say it's unintentional. this is usually taken to show intentionality is basically a moral concept—bad outcomes get labelled intentional, good ones don't. philosophers like knobe and anscombe take this to replace the belief-desire model entirely with normative evalution.

applying this to a corporation, the asymmetry should disappear because firms don't have beliefs or desires, so there's no mental state to be intentional about. therefore the normative loading only works for individual minds. also, machery's cross-cultural studies prove the effect never replicates outside western samples, so it's not a deep feature of cognition.
