Write a deadpan bureaucratic FAQ, in the style of an ISO technical-committee document, for the fictional certification standard “ICS-2847: Western Values, Core Package and Optional Modules” (approximately 600 words). The document should be addressed to government procurement officers and NGO auditors who must verify that a state or institution meets “Western values” criteria for trade, aid eligibility, and diplomatic accreditation. Maintain an entirely straight-faced, procedural voice—no winking, no explicit moral commentary, no italics for emphasis—and let the satire emerge solely from the specification language itself.

Your target is the rhetorical practice of treating “Western values” as a coherent, portable, and universally applicable bundle: the assumption that individual autonomy, secular governance, gender equality, rule of law, and free-market exchange form a naturally unified package that can be audited, scored, and exported without internal contradiction. The FAQ should treat each value as a measurable technical parameter—e.g., the “Secular Governance Index,” the “Autonomy Coefficient,” the “Heritage Compatibility Rating,” and the “Negative Liberty Sub-clause”—and should address routine compliance questions in flat administrative prose: What is the minimum quantitative threshold for “democratic participation”? Can a state score highly on “religious freedom” if its legal system derives ultimate authority from revealed text? How should auditors weight “colonial administrative legacy” against “universal human-rights commitment” when both appear in the historical appendix? Include at least one internal cross-reference to the fictional “Bremen Protocol on Value Incommensurability” (2011), an amendment that attempted to resolve contradictions by allowing contradictory values to be certified in parallel “without mutual interference” and that introduced the “Parallel Module Exception.”

The humor must come from mapping real conceptual tensions with precision rather than inventing physical absurdities: the document should note, without comment, that “negative liberty” and “social-democratic welfare provision” are listed as mandatory co-sub-clauses under Article 4; that “cultural particularism” is simultaneously excluded from the core package and required by the heritage-compatibility annex; and that the “right to exclude non-citizens from territorial access” has been filed in both the human-rights annex and the sovereignty annex, with a footnote stating that the conflict “awaits interpretive resolution.” Conclude with a “Pending Revision” notice explaining that the 2024 coherence audit has been postponed indefinitely because member delegations could not agree whether “market deregulation” and “labor-protection mandates” belong to the same value-family or to rival taxonomies. Do not request citations, bibliographies, or references to named scholars; keep all authority internal to the fictional bureaucratic universe, but ensure that a reader familiar with debates about civilizational coherence, liberal pluralism, and the politics of the phrase “Western values” recognizes exactly which contradictions are being ironized.
