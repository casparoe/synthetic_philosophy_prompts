Write a joke that plays on Robert Nozick's proposed moral division—utilitarianism for animals, Kantianism for people—by placing a decision-maker in a situation where a single entity simultaneously triggers both frameworks. Specifically, imagine a synthetic being that possesses full human-level rational autonomy (thus falling under Kantian side-constraints) but also exhibits only animal-level sensory interests with no conceptual self-awareness (thus falling under utilitarian aggregation). The punchline should turn on the actual logical tension between treating the being's welfare as aggregable and treating its autonomy as inviolable, rather than on generic wordplay or anthropomorphism. The joke should make enough sense that someone familiar with Nozick's view recognizes the reference immediately.
