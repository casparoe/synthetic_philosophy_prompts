Having spent thirty years in evolutionary ecology before turning to philosophy of science in retirement, I am preparing a proposal for a six-month independent research fellowship and need help drafting the document. The precise question I want funded is whether evolutionary psychology's standard explanatory strategy—invoking reconstructed Pleistocene environments to account for present cognitive biases—depends on an implicit commitment to historical essentialism about mental traits. More specifically, do these explanations treat ancestral selection pressures as fixing necessary modal properties of current psychological mechanisms, in a manner that conflicts with evolutionary developmental biology's standard emphasis on phenotypic plasticity and developmental system drift? I want the proposal to state the question clearly, explain why existing answers do not settle it, justify a philosophical rather than empirical approach, specify what a useful deliverable looks like, define failure conditions, and argue for a realistic six-month scope; I will fill in all formal citations myself, so the document should refer to positions and arguments without providing a bibliography.

The existing debate has largely pitted critics charging empirical underdetermination—the familiar accusation of just-so storytelling—against defenders who refine adaptationist hypotheses and demand predictive testing. Neither side, however, has systematically examined whether the explanatory framework itself presupposes that cognitive architectures possess historically fixed essences rather than being developmentally plastic responses to variable environments. My proposed approach is to analyze the logical structure of prominent explanations in three domains—risk cognition, mating psychology, and cooperative punishment—to determine whether they necessarily invoke ancestral environments as defining conditions for present mental functioning, or whether they can be reconstructed to accommodate system drift without loss of explanatory power. By comparing these arguments with analogous reasoning in evo-devo, where plasticity is uncontroversial, the project can isolate whether the suppression of developmental variability in psychology is principled or merely accidental.

Write in a formal, polished tone appropriate for a fellowship application, and avoid quotation marks drawn from specific texts; I prefer paraphrase without citation to any named source.
