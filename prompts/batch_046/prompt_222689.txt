ok so ive been thinking about this pattern i keep seeing in news stories and even in my own reactions. imagine two people both drive home drunk, same blood alcohol, same reckless choice. one makes it home fine, nobody knows. the other swerves and kills a pedestrian. now everyone calls the second one a monster, a murderer, like he was always rotten inside. but the first guy? people say he just made a stupid mistake, he isnt really bad deep down. its the exact same decision, but the outcome seems to flip whether people think you had a bad character to begin with.

i know philosophers talk about moral luck, where you get blamed more for things outside your control. but this feels different—its not just more blame, its that the outcome changes what people think your inner character was all along. like if nothing bad happens, your reckless choice was just an accident, but if something bad happens, that same choice proves you were evil inside. is there an established name for this specific thing? and if there isnt one, can you coin it and draw the line between it and regular moral luck and also that thing where people blame personality instead of situation?
