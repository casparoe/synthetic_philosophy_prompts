branch 1: do micro-level entities have experience at all?
branch 2: is the combination problem a real metaphysical gap, or just macro-concept failure?
branch 3 (the niche bit): does binding require spatiotemporal contiguity, or can it happen through semantic coherence across non-contiguous substrates — like distributed computation without direct causal interaction? call this the distributed-binding split.

each leaf should carry the standard position name if it has one (constitutive panpsychism, cosmopsychism/priority monism, emergentist, eliminativist) or 'unnamed', plus one sentence on its most awkward commitment — e.g. cosmopsychism having to explain why my experience feels local rather than universal.

also flag any pairs your tree can't really seperate. i think priority monism and cosmopsychism might collapse unless you split on whether parts are derivatively conscious. and note any unoccupied combinations — is there a view that accepts micro-experience but denies combination is a problem AND rejects distributed-binding? if so, is it incoherent, just unexplored, or unattractive?

don't include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations — this is for a group discussion, not a paper. and don't quote anyone verbatim unless you've actually fetched the source text; paraphrase without quotes if you need to reference something.

please start with the taxonomy itself, no preface or "here's the tree" commentary. and try to keep it readable but in the weeds — no surface-level "what is panpsychism" intro.
