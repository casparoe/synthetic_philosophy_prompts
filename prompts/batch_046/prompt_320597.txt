I want a short story set within this conceit, one that does not merely illustrate the philosophy but tests its emotional weight through concrete, sensory experience.

Embed the philosophical tension in sensory detail—the sound of rain that should not fall indoors, the weight of glass that belongs to no local gravity—rather than in dialogue that sounds like a seminar.
