I dont want the usual "religion gives people purpose" pushback; I want the hard cases — maybe results in philosphy of mind, or the way we talk about artists and athletes, or problems with verificationism creeping back in — sorted by how lethal they are. Which ones would a defender of this view just swallow, and which ones force them to give up the gap?
