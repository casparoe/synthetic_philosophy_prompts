One board member argues patient outcomes are the only metric that matters; another says outcome-based supervision just rewards overfitting to lucky correlations, and that in a black-box system the reasoning protocol is the only thing we can actually hold accountable.

Write the boards memo recommending approval, rejection, or approval with conditions. Give the reasoning both sides can actually respond to, and dont shy away from the strongest argument for the side that loses. Keep it practical — clinicians and engineers need to act on it, not philosophers. And please, no reading lists or citations, I just need the argument on paper.
