(2) my roommate said thats nonsense because moral harm isnt fungible like carbon. carbon is the same molecule everywhere but wronging someone is tied to that specific person; you cant repay a victim by helping a stranger. he said calling it an offset is just a fancy word for rationalizing.

(4) roommate said selecting among charities is just weighing options in one decision, not moving obligations around like cash. he said moral liquidity sneaks in that duties are poolable assets, which thier not -- obligations stick to the agent and the situation. he also said if liquidity were real you could borrow moral credit and pay it back later, which sounds absurd.
