The analogy used is that for Nietzsche the philosopher's body is like a string instrument and diet is like tuning: if the gut is out of tune, the ideas that emerge are necessarily distorted, so evaluating a philosophical position requires assessing the physiological health of its author. Proponents use this to license two conclusions: first, that we should discount metaphysical arguments advanced by thinkers with poor diets or chronic illness, and second, that deep philosophical disagreement is often just a symptom of incompatible physiologies rather than a clash of reasons.
