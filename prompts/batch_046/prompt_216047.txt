I am unsure whether this framing holds up analytically, and I would like to see it pushed to its logical extreme in deadpan satirical form rather than argued through directly.

Please write a bureaucratic FAQ document—approximately 600 words—purporting to be the official guidance for a fictional pilot program called the Transparency Protocol, administered by a Department of Reciprocal Dignity. Adopt the voice of a well-meaning but ruthlessly procedural benefits portal: therapeutic bullet points, assurances that every obligation exists to "empower" the claimant, and precise instructions about compliance rituals. The document should never break character or wink at the reader; the absurdity should become legible only because the internal logic of the requirements, when taken seriously, visibly collapses into contradiction. Do not include bibliographies, citations, references to named living commentators, or requests for further reading.
