The exam scenario should place students on the advisory board of a museum redesigning its permanent exhibit on twentieth-century state violence, where survivors of Event A demand active memorialization, descendants of Event B seek preservation without display, and historians argue that Event C should enter the third tier.

The prompt must not ask for bibliographies, reading lists, or citations, and it should not depend on specialized scholarship beyond standard graduate familiarity with these two thinkers. You may treat the mnemonic triage framework as an original conceptual injection for the exam; students should evaluate it using Margalit's and Rieff's resources rather than appealing to external authority.
