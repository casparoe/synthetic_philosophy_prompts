I have recently found myself returning to the White Horse dialogue from the School of Names, particularly Gongsun Long's argument that a white horse is not a horse, and I keep noticing how its separation of form and color resurfaces in everyday categorization. Would you write a dialogue between a rigorous defender of Gongsun Long's position and an equally sharp critic working in the practical, use-oriented tradition of Mohism? Have them debate whether a horse born entirely without pigment—pure white in every visible respect—should still be counted among horses, using only the conceptual distinctions available to the School of Names and early Mohist logic, with no appeal to modern biology. Both speakers should be fully competent, and the exchange should involve genuine back-and-forth in which each responds directly to the other's specific moves rather than talking past them.
