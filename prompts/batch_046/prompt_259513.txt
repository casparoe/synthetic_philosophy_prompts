hey, can you tier-list four responses to epistemic akrasia when you've got strong higher-order evidence you're being irrational? S through F, one sentence why each lands there.

1. level-splitting: keep the first-order belief, quarantine the higher-order doubt
2. calibrationism: scale confidence down proportional to the HOE weight
3. the 'seperate domains' view: first-order and HOE answer different questions, so there's no real clash
4. 'reflective surrender' — my own label for treating the irrationality evidence as just another first-order input to update on, which tends to collapse confidence near zero

rank by internal coherence, not fame. no citations needed, just reasoning.
