Would he have seen such obligations as a continuation of the trustee duty he imagined for colonial administration, or would his liberal individualism have led him to reject collective historical liability in favor of present-focused development assistance?

Please construct a reasoned prediction of Mill's likely position, drawing on his known views about colonial responsibility, the civilizing mission however problematized now, and his economic and political philosophy.
