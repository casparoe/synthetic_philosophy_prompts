question: are generations (boomers, millennials, gen z) real kinds? or are they just arbitrary age brackets? discuss the ontolgy of cohorts, and whether shared historical experience is enough to make them real.

student answer:
Generations are not natural kinds like gold or water, since there's no hidden essence that makes someone a Millennial. They're social kinds, similar to money or citizenship, that exist because people collectively treat them as real. Mannheim said being born around the same time is just the location part; the real generation unit forms when people share a formative experience. So Gen Z is a real kind because they went through COVID and grew up with TikTok, which shaped their worldview in a unified way. This means cohort analysis in software is totally valid—we can treat age buckets as causal groups since the shared environment produces similar behaviors. Also, since generations are social kinds, they depend on belief, so if marketers stopped using the labels the kinds would disappear.
