hey, i run a small workshop — about 12 people, mix of grad and undergrad, two hours on a saturday. i want them to actually feel anatta, not just nod at the five aggregates list. so i want a roleplay or game where they run into the absence of a self-owner in real time.

the twist i'm injecting: what if two people's skandha-streams could be partially merged — they share one aggregate (say, raw sensation) but keep their mental formations and consciousness seperate? they have to make joint choices but there's no central "we" in charge, just the streams negotiating.

design the rules, roles, and stakes. it should be runnable in the room, with real small consequences (maybe losing points, or having to do an embarassing task). then a debrief that maps each weird moment back onto form, feeling, perception, volition, consciousness. say what will predictably break when i run it — someone faking, someone getting upset — and be honest about which feature of anatta this staged version can't reproduce.

keep it practical, no lecturing up front, and they shouldn't hear "anatta" until after they've played.
