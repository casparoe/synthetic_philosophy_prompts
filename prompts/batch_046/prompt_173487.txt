Write me a sestina about republican liberty — the non-dominaton idea, not just negative freedom — told through an old ditch-rider who knows the up-stream council could shut his valve any morning but hasn't in thirty years. Use these end-words in rotation: valve, sleep, law, flow, shadow, kindness. The repetition should do the work: every time shadow or kindness comes back we should feel that not-interfering and not-dominating aren't the same thing, and in the envoi where all six lock up together 'flow' should look like a permission not a right. Start with the poem itself, no preface or commentary.
