I just spent three hours arguing with a close friend about whether Gödel’s incompleteness theorems actually establish anything philosophically substantive outside pure mathematics—she is convinced they undercut every formalist or mechanist account of human practical reasoning, while I think they have been wildly misapplied but may retain one precise, limited bite. I need you to take a committed, definite position rather than laying out options and leaving me to choose.
