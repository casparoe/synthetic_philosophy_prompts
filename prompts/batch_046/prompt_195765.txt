As a doctoral candidate in philosophy of science working on dynamical systems and metaphysics, I want to audit the consistency of the framework I am developing for my prospectus before my committee meeting. Here are the five commitments I currently hold together:

(1) Global determinism obtains: every event, including those in classically chaotic regimes, is fixed by prior states and deterministic laws of motion, with no ontological branching.

(2) Sensitive dependence on initial conditions is an epistemic phenomenon only; Lyapunov divergence measures predictive limitation, not any indeterminacy in nature itself.

(3) Moral responsibility requires only reasons-responsive agency, which is fully compatible with determinism; global predictability is irrelevant to whether an agent is responsible.

(4) The practical openness that libertarian theories seek is illusory if located in quantum mechanics, but genuinely present in macroscopic chaos—specifically, in the fact that deterministic neural dynamics are in principle unpredictable even to a Laplacian observer with complete macro-state data.

(5) Because chaotic subsystems defeat predictive verification in principle, determinism about those subsystems should be adopted as a regulative metaphysical principle rather than as an empirically confirmed claim.

Please examine this package and tell me which pairs are in genuine logical or motivational tension, which are only apparently in conflict, and rank the tensions by severity. In particular, address whether (2) and (4) conflict: if chaos supplies only epistemic openness, can it satisfy the libertarian demand for openness that (4) invokes, or does (4) smuggle in an ontological reading that (2) forbids? Also examine whether (3) and (4) form an unstable hybrid—if determinism is complete and predictability is irrelevant to responsibility, why appeal to chaos at all in discussing free will? Finally, consider the tension between (1) and (5): does treating determinism as regulative weaken the justification for asserting it globally? For each real tension, identify the least costly revision and what that revision costs my broader research program. Do not end on a non-committal note; tell me which single commitment I should drop if forced to restore coherence with minimal damage.
