Specify the full set of desiderata that a theory of constitutional Ulysses contracts must meet if it is to distinguish legitimate democratic precommitment from illegitimate intergenerational domination. Include at least: the epistemic conditions that justify present majorities in binding successors; the reversibility conditions that block dead-hand control; the distributive conditions that prevent precommitment from entrenching structural inequality; and the institutional conditions that separate constitutional rules from ordinary statutes. For each desideratum, state the counterexample or motivating case that makes it non-negotiable, and identify which one does the most work in excluding analogies drawn from market-based voluntary contracting. Conclude by saying whether that exclusion strengthens or weakens the constitutional theory.
