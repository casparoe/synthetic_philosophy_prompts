I've come around to thinking the covering-law vs narrative debate in history is basically misframed. The position I want stress-tested is this: narrative explanation isnt competing with covering-law models; its just covering-law explanation after its been run through an actor-indexing filter. The causal laws are still operating (psychological regularities, economic constraints, whatever), but they're mapped onto named agents and plot sequences, and the narrative form is just epistemic packaging for causal structures too dense to state explicitely. Lets call it the packaging thesis.
