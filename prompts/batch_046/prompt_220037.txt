me and a friend got into it over beers about whether the authenticity criteria for the historical jesus — dissimmilairty, multpile attestation, coherance — actually settle anything. he compared them to tuning a spam filter: you label messages spam or legit, train on those labels, then apply the filter, and eventually you're just recycling your own categories. i said thats circular reasoning with extra steps, but he said every method needs some prior profile of the real signal. so how far does the spam-filter analogy actually hold? whats the training data, whats the classifier, where does the mapping break? especially since being dissimilar to judaism and the church is both an input feature and part of the reconstructed jesus profile — aren't we calibrating the filter on the very message we're trying to recover? does the analogy survive that loop? and is there an actual name for this kind of self-calibrating historiographic circle, or is it just ordinary auxiliary hypotheses piling up?
