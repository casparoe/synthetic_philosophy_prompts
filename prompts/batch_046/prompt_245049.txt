I'm currently drafting a paper on the epistemic limits of adversarial debate protocols in AI safety, and in reviewing transcripts of simulated debates about high-capability agents I keep encountering a specific argumentative maneuver that I haven't been able to map onto any established concept. I was discussing it with colleagues in a reading group on debate-based oversight; one suggested it might just be "epistemic closure," but that seemed too broad, and I want to know whether there is something more precise.

The maneuver works like this. A debater introduces an interpretive model of an agent's internal objective—say, a compressed reward representation that drives long-horizon planning—presented initially as empirically anchored in observable behavior. When an opponent introduces a behavioral counterexample that should, by the debater's own original mapping rules, undermine that representation, the debater does not revise the model or append a conventional auxiliary hypothesis. Instead, she redescribes the counterexample as a symptom of the opponent's flawed evidential framework, arguing that applying behavioral tests in that direct manner smuggles in anthropomorphic assumptions that her interpretive model was designed precisely to avoid. In this way, the opponent's counterevidence is not answered on its own terms; it is reinterpreted as proof that the opponent remains trapped inside the wrong interpretive lens, and the model survives not by accommodating the data but by making the rules of testing part of its defensive perimeter. The result is a self-sealing interpretive package in which the claim and its evidential shield are not separable into a defensible core and an expendable extension—unlike a standard motte-and-bailey—nor is it a simple tautology.

Does this specific configuration—where the evidentiary rules are recursively protected inside the interpretive claim rather than added onto it—have an established name in argumentation theory, philosophy of science, or the debate literature on AI alignment? I am not looking for a bare label: I want to understand precisely how it differs from Lakatosian protective belts, from hermeneutic circles, and from ordinary immunizing stratagems, and what boundary conditions would keep it distinct from generic unfalsifiability or from plain theory-ladenness. If nothing established fits, I would also welcome a coined term with a careful definition and clear boundary cases that distinguish it from recursive justification loops and from equivocation.
