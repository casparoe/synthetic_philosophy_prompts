He maintains that this framework dissolves the traditional knowledge-versus-belief debate by treating each norm as internal to its discursive practice rather than as competing for universal authority. Draft a sequence of eight to ten interview questions that avoid asking him to restate the view. Also include one question about whether his view can accommodate the intuition that asserting a falsehood remains wrong even in policy contexts where certainty is unattainable, with a follow-up addressing the anticipated response that wrongness in such cases derives from violating the conjectural flag rather than from falsehood itself.
