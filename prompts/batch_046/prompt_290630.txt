Because my conceptual toolkit is largely engineering-oriented, I tried to map this onto distributed systems and version control rather than leaving it in purely physical terms. I am unsure whether these analogies genuinely illuminate the philosophical reasoning or whether they quietly import assumptions—for instance, that un-checked-out branches are "equally real" in a metaphysical sense—that do not carry over to temporal ontology and thereby distort the conclusion.
