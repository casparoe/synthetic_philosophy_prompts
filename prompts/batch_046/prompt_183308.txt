I read a summary in an unnamed bioethics review that frames the therapy-enhancement distinction as follows. Therapy, it says, corrects deficits relative to a baseline of species-typical functioning, which defenders define statistically by reference to an evolutionary design that fixes normal capacities for a given age and sex. Enhancement, by contrast, augments capacities beyond that baseline. According to the summary, defenders treat this baseline as biologically objective and therefore use it to draw a sharp boundary: healthcare justice requires funding interventions that restore the baseline, but provides no comparable reason to fund those that exceed it. The summary then reports critics who observe that the baseline is technologically and environmentally unstable—corrective lenses, vaccines, and nutritional improvements all shift what counts as typical—so the statistical norm is socially constructed rather than biologically fixed. These critics also charge that deriving a normative allocation principle from evolutionary design commits the naturalistic fallacy. From this, the summary concludes that the distinction is incoherent: it cannot provide the bright line defenders need, and therefore collapses as a tool for healthcare policy.

Something about this strikes me as too quick—particularly the inference from baseline instability to total collapse, and the claim that defenders rely on evolutionary design for normative allocation. Is this summary fair? Please distinguish what accurately represents mainstream positions in the debate (including both the biostatistical account of normal function and the justice-based allocation argument), what is a common pedagogical simplification, and what subtly misrepresents defenders' commitments—especially regarding whether they claim a sharp line, whether they conflate descriptive biological norms with normative obligations, and whether the evolutionary-design point is meant to do normative work at all. Finally, assess whether the critics' conclusion that the distinction collapses actually follows from the accurate parts of their objections, or whether defenders have resources to preserve a fuzzier but still functional boundary.
