I am constructing a philosophical dialogue for my current novel, in which a retired taxi driver—formerly a lecturer in philosophy of action—presents the following argument to a passenger who is a cognitive scientist. The driver begins with the familiar case of absent-minded navigation: (1) an expert driver can travel a well-known route home while entirely lost in thought, reaching his destination safely despite having no subsequent episodic memory of individual turns and reporting no conscious monitoring of the road. (2) If a complex, adaptive sequence can be executed without conscious access to guiding representations, then those representations cannot be propositional knowledge-that; they must constitute non-propositional knowledge-how. (3) Thus the driver’s journey was directed solely by knowledge-how. (4) Now consider apparently theoretical cognition—grasping that water is H₂O, for instance. Any such grasp is ultimately manifested only in skilled verbal and behavioral performances: forming sentences, recognizing laboratory instruments, instructing students. (5) By the reasoning in (3), skilled performance never requires propositional content; applying this to (4) yields that theoretical beliefs are not genuinely propositional states but merely refined patterns of practical skill. (6) Consequently, the distinction between knowing-how and knowing-that does not merely blur but collapses entirely in favor of the former, leaving no distinct cognitive state that corresponds to factual understanding. The passenger finds this intolerable—she insists that her scientific training involves genuinely representing states of affairs—yet she wishes to preserve the intuition behind (1). Where does the argument go wrong? Is there a hidden equivocation between causal guidance and representational content, or an illicit scope shift from the absence of conscious access to the absence of propositional structure? Alternatively, is the inference valid, so that one must choose between rejecting (4), (5), or (6), and what does each rejection cost? Please identify the precise step that fails, explain why it initially appears defensible, and assess the price of preserving the phenomenon of absent-minded driving alongside the reality of theoretical cognition.
