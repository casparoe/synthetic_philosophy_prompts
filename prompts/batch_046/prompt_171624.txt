Sam then brought up adaptive preferences — poor people often report being just as happy because they've lowered expectations — so subjective reports are unreliable, which means we need some objective cross-person metric. Riley said that actually makes Sam's case worse: if subjective reports are unreliable, then comparing two black boxes is doubly speculative, and you're just inventing numbers.
