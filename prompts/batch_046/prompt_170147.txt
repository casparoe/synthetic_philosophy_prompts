Which of these commitments genuinely pull against each other, which tensions are only apparent, and what is the least costly revision? Please rank the conflicts by severity—paying special attention to whether (3) and (4) clash, whether (1) and (5) are compatible, and whether (2) sits comfortably with (5)—and for each serious tension specify exactly which commitment has to give and at what cost.
