Taking Dennett's "Quining Qualia" as the challenge—specifically the Chase-and-Sanborn intuition pump that dissolves putative private qualia into reactive and functional dispositions—specify the five desiderata any theory of phenomenal experience must satisfy if it is to resist quining without collapsing into behaviorism or functionalism. Each list entry must be exactly two sentences: the first stating the criterion, the second supplying the motivating distinction or case (for example, inter-subjective calibration of taste judgments that avoids positing a private inner object, or phenomenal presence that does not require epistemic privilege). Respond only as a numbered list of exactly five items, with no introduction or conclusion, and in the second sentence of item five state which single desideratum eliminates the largest share of standard phenomenal-concept and representationalist theories.
