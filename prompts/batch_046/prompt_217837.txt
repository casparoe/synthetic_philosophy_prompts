On her account, the critical variable is neither group pressure nor deep cultural programming, but a specific form of cognitive-affective adaptation that transforms revulsion into professional pride, and she claims this explains the otherwise puzzling trajectory of men who began as objectors and ended as the unit's most prolific shooters.

Write me a sequence of interview questions that presses her view exactly where the historical literature presses it, anticipating her likely deflections and including the follow-up to each.
