(1) i'm basically with ryle that knowing-how isnt just propositional knowing-that — there's a real felt gap between riding a bike and being able to state the physics of balance.

(2) but i also think intellectualism is right about chess: grandmaster skill is mostly compressed knowing-that about patterns, and if you could extract all that propositional content you'd basically have the skill captured.

(3) current AI only has knowing-that (implicit in weights), and thats why it cant genuinely learn craft like pottery — its missing the embodied feedback loop that turns rules into muscular intelligence.

(4) at the same time, i think the knowing-how/knowing-that boundary is mostly a pragmatic sorting tool we use for teaching, not some deep metaphysical chasm.
