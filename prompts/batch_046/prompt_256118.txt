The teleological suspension of the ethical means the universal law against murder is suspended for a higher telos, God's command. This is structurally parallel to Hegel's Aufhebung: the ethical is negated but preserved inside the religious stage, so Abraham isn't outside ethics, he's just in a higher mediated form of it. This silence isn't an epistemic gap—he still understands ethics perfectly—it's ontological, the universal literally can't contain his situation. That's why he belongs in the religious stage."
