Compare echo chambers and epistemic bubbles—the kind of distinction people like Nguyen draw, but don’t just parrot that back. I want to know whether the difference is mainly about social trust (who you dismiss) or about information flow (what you’re exposed to), and whether one can exist without the other. Then there’s this odd scenario: a community that deliberatly invites critics in, but every outside argument gets translated into the groups own jargon and ends up reinforcing the core belief. Is that still an echo chamber? Or a bubble with extra steps? Or maybe a third kind of epistemic trap? Make your case, and say if the standard distinction breaks down here.
