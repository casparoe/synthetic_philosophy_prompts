The topic has become personally urgent because I recently remained silent during a departmental decision-making process in which my inaction, despite having no formal veto power, may have shaped a harmful policy outcome; I need to understand whether her framework can accommodate my case or necessarily excludes it.

Please draft eight to ten interview questions that go well beyond asking her to restate the institutional-duty condition.
