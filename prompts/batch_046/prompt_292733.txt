The curator wants a big opening panel saying Rome collapsed in 476 CE because barbarians invaded and the western empire broke apart. But I've been reading that the eastern half kept going for like a thousand more years, and there's also ideas about climate change and some big plague being bigger deals, plus this view that it wasn't really a fall just a slow transformation where local rulers took over. So the board needs a memo from me recommending whether we approve the script, reject it, or approve with conditions — like telling them to reframe it. I think the best argument for the curator's version is that 476 is when the last western emperor got kicked out, so there's an actual event visitors can point to, and people come expecting the fall story. But I also want the memo to take seriously this point I keep thinking about: asking 'when did Rome fall' is kind of like asking 'when did a river stop being a river' — there's no sharp line, just gradual change, so maybe the exibit shouldn't lock in one date but show a range of transitions. Should we also mention the lead poisoning theory from the aqueducts? I think it's been mostly debunked but I'm not sure. Anyway, please draft the memo with a clear recommendation and deal honestly with the strongest reason on the other side. Don't worry about citations, just reasoning we can use at the meeting.
