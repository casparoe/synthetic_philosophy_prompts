Conservators use the carbon ash and original canvas fragments to chemically synthesize a painting materially continuous with 99% of the original atoms, arranged in the identical configuration, but executed entirely by robotic process with no human artistic intent. At the same time, a master forger produces a visually and materially identical copy using fresh materials, but with the explicit artistic purpose of resurrecting Leonardo's vision. Weigh arguments about material continuity, artistic intentionality, institutional recognition, and the phenomenology of viewing the work. Reach and defend a specific verdict—do not end with an unqualified "it depends"; if your view is conditional, specify exactly which conditions govern it.

- Intentionality and authorship: Evaluate whether the absence of Leonardo's (or any human's) artistic purpose in the reconstruction undermines its claim, and whether the forger's respectful intent can confer original status (about 200 words)
