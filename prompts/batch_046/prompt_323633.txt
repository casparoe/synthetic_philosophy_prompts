argue that in epistemic game theory — where payoffs are belief-accuracy, not material — common knowledge of rationality is self-defeating. the standard strategic story says if everyone knows everyone is rational we can iterate away dominated strategies, but in pure belief-updating games that's backwards: if rationality is commom knowledge then no one can coherently disagree about what rationality demands, which kills the feedback loops that make reasoning improve. so we should replace it with common knowledge of fallibilty, where each agent knows the others might misapply their own reasoning rules. take a definite position, lay out the steps, and dont just balance options.
