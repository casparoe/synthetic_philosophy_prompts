Please explain the conceptual problem of old evidence within Bayesian epistemology. In particular, discuss whether this tension can be resolved by appealing to counterfactual predictive success (that is, assessing whether T would have assigned a high prior probability to E in a scenario where E had not yet been observed), and clarify what this implies for the distinction between evidential confirmation and explanatory accommodation.
