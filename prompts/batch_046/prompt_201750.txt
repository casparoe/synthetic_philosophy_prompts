So trying to put them in one narrative isn't like translating French to English; it's like trying to map a weather system and a legal argument onto the same graph. Do you think this distinction holds, or am I overstating the incommensurability? And is there a way to write history that doesn't collapse these into one story, or is some kind of chronological violence unavoidable?
