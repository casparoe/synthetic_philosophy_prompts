Would you audit this package for me? Please go through the commitments and identify which pairs are in genuine tension, which only appear to conflict, and how severely—ranking the tensions rather than treating them as equally serious. For each real tension, state what the cheapest revision would be and what it costs me, and then advise which single revision would clean up the most problems at once. I am particularly unsure whether my appeal to emergence in (e) sits comfortably with my refusal in (f) to invoke downward causation, and whether (c) and (g) are as compatible as I take them to be, but I do not know where the deepest fault line lies and I am not signalling which commitment I would be least sorry to lose. Please write your answer in British English, and please say explicitly what would change your mind about this audit—which argument or empirical consideration would lead you to reorder the tensions or favour a different resolution, and where in your reasoning you think failure is most likely.
