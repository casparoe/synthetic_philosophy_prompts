I have automated a small daily deposit into a locked savings account, with one exception: every Friday night I may suspend the coming week's deposits by sending a single text message. Without fail, I send the message on Friday—feeling immediate relief—yet by Monday I regret it and wish I had stayed the course. Should I regard my Friday-night decision as expressing my true, present preferences, or as a kind of self-betrayal that undermines the plan I sincerely endorsed on Sunday? If it is indeed the latter, is there a coherent rationale for voluntarily binding my Friday self in advance, and what form should that binding take?
