Crucially, however, the failure concerns only outcome independence—the statistical correlation between distant results—while parameter independence, which requires that local outcomes depend only on local settings and the hidden state, remains intact. The theorem is therefore best understood as a result about the internal structure of hidden-variable theories, not as a demonstration that nature is non-local in any causally meaningful way."
