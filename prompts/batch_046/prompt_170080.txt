Design the case so that views 1 and 2 agree the system lacks moral status, while view 3 grants it full moral status. Finally, include an honest note on how a defender of view 1 or 2 would attack view 3's attribution, and how a proponent of view 3 would reply.

Please separate what is conceptually settled from what is genuinely contested among specialists in this area, and do not present any of these three positions as the consensus view. The goal is to show where the theories diverge structurally, not to resolve the debate.
