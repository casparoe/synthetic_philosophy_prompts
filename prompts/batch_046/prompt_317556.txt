If a superintelligent AI can literally construct the maximally convincing version of any true claim, then isn't clear, optimized communication basically coercive? So shouldn't we require these systems to talk in a deliberately "frictional" mode—presenting reasons out of logical order, banning metaphors, always pairing every point with its best rebuttal—so humans still feel like they're doing the believing themselves? I keep thinking there must be some autonomy-preserving protocol, maybe with epistemic friction ratios or belief-decay checkpoints, that keeps persuasion seperate from honesty. But am I wrong that being maximally clear and being maximally persuasive are even two different things for a system like that?
