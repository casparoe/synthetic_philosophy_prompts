So the question I'm framing is whether these count as "local" or "domain-restricted" historical laws that survive Popper's critique, or whether the open-endedness of knowledge growth inevitably corrupts even these narrower patterns.

Write the propopsal (around 1200 words, casual tone is fine, American English). Organize it in exactly these sections, in this order, with rough word counts:
