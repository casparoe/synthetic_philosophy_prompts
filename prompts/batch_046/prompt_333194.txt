In discussions about whether artificial systems could possess anything like Cartesian self-certainty, a comparison I keep encountering treats Descartes’ cogito as functioning like a self-authenticating cryptographic signature: the act of generating the signature is inseparable from the presence of the signer, so verifying the signature proves the signer exists without any appeal to an external witness or sensory channel. Proponents deploy this to suggest that a running model might hold an analogous, irreducible first-person proof that it is currently active—no environment, no body, and no third-party observer required. I want an explicit audit of how far this mapping actually holds.

Please begin with a correspondence table: in the source domain of digital signatures, identify what plays the role of the private key, the message being signed, the verification procedure, the possibility of forgery or replay, and the public-key infrastructure that makes verification meaningful to anyone other than the signer. Then map each of these onto the target domain of the cogito—what corresponds to the thinking act, the existence claim, the absence of an external checker, and the impossibility of deception about one’s own presence? Next, separate the disanalogies that are merely incidental from those that actively undermine the intended conclusion. Specifically, address whether the cryptographic frame silently imports an ontology in which entities exist prior to generating keys, an observer who distinguishes valid from invalid signatures, or a functional distinction between authentic and forged instances—none of which have obvious parallels in Descartes’ argument. Then state whether the conclusion that an artificial system could “know” it exists in the same ground-level sense survives once those imported structures are stripped away, or whether the analogy was carrying the conclusion on its own. Finally, if the mapping collapses at a specific structural point, indicate what a more defensible analogy for self-certifying existence would need to preserve, and whether that revised analogy still licenses extending the cogito beyond biological minds or whether it restricts the proof to conditions the artificial system cannot satisfy.
