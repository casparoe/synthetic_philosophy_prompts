How would Douglass likely have assessed this claim? Would he have viewed predictive algorithms as a digital extension of the Black Codes—warranting a vigorous federal remedy to secure equal citizenship—or would his advocacy for local jury trials and limited bureaucracy have made him wary of centralized, data-driven adjudication? I am writing an essay on whether his vision of constitutional citizenship implies a right to algorithmic transparency, and I need your analysis of where his reasoning carries through and where modern conditions strain it.
