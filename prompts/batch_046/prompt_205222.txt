I suspect the deadlock is not mainly textual but conceptual, turning on whether the commonwealth is a single moral agent whose corporate will can authorize violence, or merely an aggregate of individuals each holding only private rights of defense—so the sides may be answering different normative questions masked by the shared term "authority."
