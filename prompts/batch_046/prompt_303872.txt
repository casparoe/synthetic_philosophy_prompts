so what philosophical commitments does that whole practice presuppose? it seems like it treats the diagnosis as both describing a pre-existing condition and creating the condition it describes, then tells you the created label as if its your authentic self. what view of personal identity or self-knowledge makes that not totally incoherent? and if we replaced that with a view where diagnoses are just temporary administrative tags that have zero effect on who you are, what exactly in clinical practice or manual revision would have to change? also is any part of this setup philosophically underdetermined, so you could keep the transparency policy but run it on a totally different theory of mind?
