So like, if I have to pick between saving one person or five, contractualism actually over-counts the five and makes it impossible to justify saving just the one, even if that one is facing death and the other five just have broken legs. But that seems like a feature not a bug, right? Wouldn't the real fix be adding a weighted veto system where each rejection is scaled by how bad the harm is, instead of treating every seperate complaint as equal? I'm comparing this to Parfit's equal consideration idea but I can't tell if Scanlon already allows that kind of scaling.
