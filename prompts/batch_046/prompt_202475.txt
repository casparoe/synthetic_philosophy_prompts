Write a dialogue between an epiphenomenalist (call her Lin) and a physicalist critic (call him Dex) about the paradox of phenominal judgement. Don't just replay the usual script where consciousness doesn't cause beliefs about itself — have Dex push a weirder angle: if Lin's own consciousness is causally inert, then her belief that Dex is conscious is also physically caused with zero causal input from his consciousness. Lin needs to defend why that doesn't collapse her view into either self-defeat or a useless other-minds problem. They should actually argue back and forth, not talk past each other — equal competence, real replies. Write it casual, like two tired grad students argueing over cheap coffee, and don't worry about being too formal.
