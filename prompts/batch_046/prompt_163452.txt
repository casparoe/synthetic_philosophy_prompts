me and my roommate got in a fight about technological determinism after i read about those long island bridges winner wrote about. i said artifacts literally have politics built in, like the low overpasses blocking buses. she said thats just people putting politics into concrete, the bridge itself is neutral and the determinism is fake. then i said ok but what about "latent politics" — an artifact can sit there neutral-looking but structurally bias outcomes once its used in a real system, like a protocol that defaults to surveillance unless you opt out. she said thats still designers and users, not the artifact, and that calling it "dispositional politics" is just renaming social choice. i said no, because the artifact closes off some social possibilities in a physical way that pure norms dont. we asked a friend but he just said "both sides". can you adjudicate move by move? whos actually right at each step, and is there a real middle ground or is that just fence-sitting?
