So I was in this group chat with my philosophy friends Jules and Sam and they got stuck arguing about whether an AI can actually do a speech act or if its just a fancy parrot. Jules started with this scenario: a customer-service bot types "I hereby promise to delete your personal data within 24 hours" but the backend actually keeps logs for thirty days. Jules said this isnt a lie — its a perfomative misfire, because promising needs an agent with intentions and the ability to be bound, and the bot has neither, so Austin's felicity conditions fail right from the start.

Sam said thats completely off — its an abuse, not a misfire. The bot is just a mouthpiece for the company, which is the real speaker, and the company knows the data stays put, so its exactly like a human promising without meaning it: the conventional procedure is executed correctly in the institutional context, but its insincere. Jules countered that if the company is the speaker, we need to ask whether its literally uttering "I" through the bot or just running an automated script with no present commitment. If its the latter, then theres no performative happening at all, just mechanical noise, which is more like a misfire of procedure than an abuse of authority. Sam came back saying even prerecorded messages like "this call is being recorded" are performative in the right context, so automation doesnt kill the act, and the real issue is the company hiding behind code to dodge responsibility — classic Austin-style abuse where you go through the motions without the right mental state. Jules then said wait, Austin's abuse category applies to people who actually have mental states but lack sincerity; stretching it to cover a corporation mediated by software pushes the framework past breaking.

They asked me to referee and I honestly couldnt tell who was winning. Can you walk through each round and say whos right about what — especially on whether the AI is the speaker, a mouthpiece, or neither, and whether lacking belief means misfire or abuse? And tell me what both of them are missing, if anything, because I feel like theres some middle ground theyre ignoring.
