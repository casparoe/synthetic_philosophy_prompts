I'm a doctoral student working at the intersection of philosophy of technology and normative ethics, and I've been trying to determine whether Bostrom's simulation argument genuinely functions as a moderating influence against the axiological extremism that sometimes accompanies longtermist commitments to the far future. The familiar reading holds that if simulated minds vastly outnumber base-reality minds, then our moral calculus should either dilute concern for far-future base-reality outcomes or amplify it, depending on how we weight simulated welfare. But I want to move beyond that framing and ask about a different mechanism: whether the argument introduces what we might call "ontological discounting," meaning that if our causal chain is mediated by an unknown computational substrate, our confidence in the stability of value-structures across deep time should diminish—not merely our confidence in physical predictions, but our confidence that the normative hierarchy we now apply to distant futures will retain its authority.

Specifically, I would like you to describe the most important implications of the simulation argument for how we ought to calibrate moral urgency regarding far-future scenarios—such as existential-risk mitigation or the engineering of post-human civilizations—when we take seriously the possibility that our present normative framework might itself be an artifact of the simulation's design constraints rather than a reliably anchored moral compass. Does the argument dampen fanaticism by introducing irreducible uncertainty about whether far-future interventions carry the valence we assign them? Or does it paradoxically intensify commitment by suggesting that breaking out of the simulation requires precisely the kind of long-term technological mastery that longtermists advocate? I am particularly interested in whether there is a coherent middle position between dismissive skepticism and unmodified longtermist zeal, and in whether simulation uncertainty undermines the epistemic authority of our current value judgments about distant futures without collapsing into complete normative paralysis. Please treat this as a conceptual clarification exercise rather than a request for bibliographic references or citation lists.
