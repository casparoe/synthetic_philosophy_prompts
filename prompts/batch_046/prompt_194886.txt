I am a retired experimental physicist who has recently turned to the philosophy of science, and I am trying to understand the conceptual landscape surrounding Maxwell's demon—not as a historical curiosity, but as a live problem about whether information carries physical weight. I should like you to construct a formal decision tree, or if you prefer a taxonomy with labelled branches, that sorts the main philosophical positions on what the demon demonstrates about the relation between information, entropy, and physical law.

Please take as the shared starting commitment the claim that a microscopic agent or device could, in principle, separate fast and slow molecules and thereby reduce the entropy of a gas without performing macroscopic work. From that point, each branch node should pose a concrete claim or case distinction that a student could answer without already knowing which philosophical label it leads to.

Your tree should terminate in clearly described positions, and it must include at least the following leaves:

(i) The Landauer–Bennett view, on which information is physically embodied, so that the erasure of the demon's records necessarily dissipates heat and thereby restores the second law.

(ii) A correlation-resource view, on which the thermodynamic cost lies not in local erasure but in maintaining or breaking correlations between the demon and the gas—treating information as a relational thermodynamic resource rather than a locally stored bit.

(iii) An observer-dependent or epistemic view (in the spirit of Jaynes), which locates the entropy reduction in an update of knowledge rather than in any objective physical change.

(iv) An agent-causal view, which holds that the demon's intentional or agential state is irreducible and essential to the sorting, so that purely mechanical descriptions miss the phenomenon altogether.

(v) A purely statistical-mechanical view, which denies any special informational status and treats the second law as holding only in the aggregate, with no paradox in need of resolution.

In addition, the tree must contain a branch that asks how each position handles a niche variant: a quantum-coherent demon whose records never decohere into classical bits but remain in entangled superposition with the gas, and for which no local erasure event ever occurs.

Please also identify any logical combinations of answers that your tree permits but that appear to be unoccupied, and briefly diagnose each—whether it is incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored.

Finally, please say how confident you are in the overall architecture of your tree, and single out the one branch point or classification about which you are least sure.
