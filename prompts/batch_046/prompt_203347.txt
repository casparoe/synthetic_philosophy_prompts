It should work for someone with no training, take under fifteen minutes, and be honest about where it misfires. Apply it to three reports: one saying "my self dissolved into colour", one claiming "pure awareness with no content at all", and one describing "a presence that knew things I never told anyone". Include one case where the procedue gives an answer we'll probably dislike, and say what the method simply cannot do.
