I have been trying to stabilize a naturalistic account of religion for a seminar and would like an audit of whether my commitments actually cohere. Here is my position, stated plainly. First, I understand religious ritual and prayer as cognitive technologies that operate mainly by recalibrating interoceptive precision weights within a predictive-processing framework, not as transactions with supernatural agents. Second, I grant that hyperactive agency detection and costly signaling have evolutionary origins that make metaphysical religious beliefs intuitively compelling, yet I reject the corresponding debunking argument: evolutionary etiology is no more a threat to religious truth than it is to mathematical or logical belief. Third, I take mystical experiences—particularly those involving ego dissolution—to be temporary disruptions of the brain's self-model that generate genuinely novel phenomenological data about the architecture of consciousness, data that any adequate philosophy of mind ought to accommodate rather than pathologize. Fourth, I adhere to strict methodological naturalism, which requires that every explanation of religious experience be empirically tractable and intersubjectively testable; consequently, private phenomenological reports possess no intrinsic epistemic authority. Fifth, I hold that if secular contemplative practices can reproduce the measurable well-being effects of religious ritual without their metaphysical baggage, then public policy should prefer the secular analogues on grounds of ontological parsimony, even at the cost of weakening the social cohesion that shared supernatural belief often provides.

Please examine these five commitments for internal tension. Identify which pairs genuinely conflict, which only appear to conflict but can be reconciled without revision, and rank the real conflicts by severity. For each tension, specify exactly which commitment must be modified or abandoned and what that revision costs the overall view. Do not treat all five as equally problematic; discriminate carefully. Present your analysis as continuous prose in a single flowing letter, with no headings, bullet points, bold formatting, or tables, as though writing to a colleague by email.
