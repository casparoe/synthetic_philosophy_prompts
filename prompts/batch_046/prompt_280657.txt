What are the most important implicatons of the asymmetry for this setup? Or does the absence of suffering in stasis break the asymmetry somehow? And how should we weigh the intuition that dissolving a potential-good-life embryo is a kind of loss, against the asymmetry's claim that failing to create happy people isn't wrong?
