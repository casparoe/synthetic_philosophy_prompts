I sit on my university's Committee on Autonomous Infrastructure Governance, and we must issue a recommendation on a proposed pilot that restructures the municipal water-treatment system according to an embedded-agency architecture: control is not vested in a single deliberative AI but distributed across sensors, regulatory protocols, human operators, and adaptive algorithms that jointly determine chlorination and filtration adjustments in real time, with human review occurring only at quarterly audits rather than per-action authorization. Our charter requires the memo to recommend approve, reject, or approve-with-conditions; to specify a concrete oversight mechanism and a review timeline; and to treat seriously the strongest counter-argument—that distributing agency across a socio-technical network dissolves rather than shares moral and legal accountability, leaving harmful outcomes with no identifiable author. Please draft the short written opinion I will circulate before Thursday's meeting, formatted as exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences, with no introduction and no conclusion. The first item should state the recommendation and its concrete conditions with a clear time frame. The second item should articulate how the embedded-agency framing applies specifically to this water-treatment system. The third item should engage the accountability-dissolution objection on its own terms rather than dismissing it. The fourth item should propose the oversight mechanism that responds to that objection. The fifth item should specify the review date and the precise trigger that would force immediate re-evaluation.
