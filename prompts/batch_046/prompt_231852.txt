I'm defending a version of eliminative materialism about propositional attitudes—specifically, that belief and desire as individuated mental states with discrete propositional contents should be eliminated from both cognitive science and developmental psychology, not replaced by neural correlates but by a framework of coupled dynamical states I call tension-resolution trajectories—in a seminar next month.
