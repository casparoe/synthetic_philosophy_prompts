I hold that the standard criteria of authenticity—multiple attestation, dissimilarity, coherence, and embarrassment—cannot, even in refined form, deliver a determinative portrait of the historical Jesus, because they are circularly calibrated against the very textual traditions they seek to filter. I have come to think, however, that a reflexive calibration approach escapes this circle: rather than using the criteria to isolate authentic logia, one maps the tension fields between competing redactional tendencies in the Synoptics and the Pauline corpus, treating discrepancies not as noise to be eliminated but as structural traces of specific controversies generated by Jesus' actions within first-century synagogue politics and Roman provincial governance. From these tension fields, a minimal but stable profile emerges—one that predicts, for example, that Jesus' execution must have involved both local priestly collaboration and Roman administrative ratification, and that his movement's post-crucifixion persistence required a specific reinterpretation of prophetic failure rather than generic resurrection belief. What would count against this reflexive calibration view? I want an honest assessment of its exposure: which archaeological finds, which textual-critical results, which reconstructions of Second Temple factionalism, or which formal analyses of redactional dependency would be decisive, and which merely awkward? Which of these defeaters would a defender of reflexive calibration concede as genuinely damaging, and which would they dismiss as compatible with the tension-field model? In particular, does the view risk insulating itself—by redefining every counter-example as another tension node—into unfalsifiability, and if so, is that insulation a fatal flaw or an appropriate feature for a historical method operating under radical source scarcity? Rank the potential defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, and identify the specific case or result that would best distinguish this position from both naive criterion-based reconstruction and from thoroughgoing mythicism.
