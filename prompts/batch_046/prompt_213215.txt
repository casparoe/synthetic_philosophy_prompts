I've been working through Rorty's antirepresentationalism for an advanced seminar, and I keep encountering the same analogy in secondary discussions and informal debate: representationalist epistemology is like a court that takes testimony from an external witness—the world—whereas Rorty's neopragmatism is like a jury deliberation that never calls that witness, so that truth collapses into whatever the jurors happen to agree upon, with no independent check. The analogy is typically used to license the conclusion that Rorty eliminates all constraint and reduces knowledge to mere social consensus. Before I accept that reading, I want a careful audit of the mapping. Can you lay out the correspondence table explicitly—what plays the role of the witness, the testimony, the jury instructions, the verdict, and the possibility of a mistrial? Then identify where the analogy breaks: for instance, does Rorty actually deny that the world causally produces beliefs (even if he denies that causal relations can justify them), and does the jury model quietly import a kind of procedural rationality or deliberative norm that Rorty explicitly rejects? I also want you to consider a more niche distinction that has been raised in recent commentary but is not yet standard in textbooks—between "conversational closure," where a community stops revising its beliefs because of social exhaustion or power dynamics rather than evidential saturation, and "epistemic closure," where further inquiry is genuinely redundant because all relevant friction has been resolved. Does the jury analogy conflate these two forms of closure, and if so, does that mean the charge that Rorty removes all world-constraint depends on treating every end of inquiry as conversational closure? Finally, tell me whether the conclusion—that Rorty makes truth entirely internal to discourse—survives once these disanalogies are on the table, or whether a better analogy (perhaps language as a tool calibrated by practical success rather than as a mirror or as a jury verdict) would serve the neopragmatist position more honestly without smuggling in the very representational assumptions Rorty wants to abandon.
