Make them actually talk to eachother — Sam should tackle Riley's reciprocity point head-on, not just repeat that there's no consciousness inside. Riley should address Sam's worry about missing inner experience rather than dodging it. Don't let either be a strawman.

One slightly niche twist: Riley should claim that Dennett's own earlier work on the intentional stance actually forces us to grant real belief-status to stable long-term AI agents, not withhold it.
