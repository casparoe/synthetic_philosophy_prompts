The core idea is to ask whether Davidson's framework of radical interpretation can be coherently extended, at the conceptual level, to organized adaptive systems that lack propositional attitudes—such as institutional decision protocols—without collapsing interpretation into mere causal explanation. Specifically: can Davidson's principle of charity be reconceived as a principle of functional coherence for such targets, and do his arguments against conceptual relativism still hold if the interpreted party is not a rational agent with beliefs? Do not include a bibliography, literature review, or citation requests; refer to Davidson's views in general terms and avoid specific page numbers or texts you cannot verify.
