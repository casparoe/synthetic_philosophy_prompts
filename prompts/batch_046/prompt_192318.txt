Normally you'd think "well if nothing inside understands the task, that makes the result more mysterious," but in these debates the absence of comprehension is flipped into the explanation itself. So I'm wondering: is there a specific term for this inversion, where you resolve the mystery of external competence by insisting on internal blankness?
