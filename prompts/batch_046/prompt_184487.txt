My credence that the Theravada anattā framework—understood as the denial of a persisting substantial self combined with the five-aggregate analysis and the doctrine of dependent origination—provides a more coherent resolution to this case than does a Lockean psychological-continuity theory is about 0.65. (4) The probability that the hypothetical itself is conceptually coherent, in particular that independent development without cross-access can be sustained without collapsing back into either a single divided subject or two entirely unrelated persons.
