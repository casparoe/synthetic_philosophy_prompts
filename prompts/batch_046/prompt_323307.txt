You sit on a legislative review commission tasked with recommending whether to retain a statute that criminalizes private, consensual neuro-rituals — self-directed cognitive practices that carry a documented risk of minor, self-inflicted neurological alteration but cause no direct harm to others. Proponents of retention maintain that the law marks a necessary moral boundary and preserves social cohesion even in the absence of third-party injury; opponents invoke the harm principle and warn against enforcing conventional morality. Draft the commission's formal recommendation memo: recommend clearly whether the statute should be repealed, retained, or retained with a narrower scope; provide reasoning that the dissenting commissioners could recognize as fair; and specifically address whether the cohesion argument can survive if the prohibited conduct is invisible and private, so that enforcement serves only symbolic boundary-marking rather than preventing observable social disintegration. Do not include bibliographies, citation lists, or appeals to authority outside the arguments themselves.
