The proposed method should construct a taxonomy that distinguishes transparent conventions, whose purpose is immediately legible, from opaque ones, and then determine whether opacity excuses non-compliance or, paradoxically, demands heightened deference. A useful deliverable would be a conceptual framework with explicit conditions for failure—specifically, if the analysis collapses all conventions into either pure moral rules or arbitrary preferences—together with a realistic scope for a short project that produces a philosophical backbone for fiction rather than an empirical study.
