The central question is whether symbols push the substrate around: can simmballs exert genuine causal force within the careenium, or are they causally inert epiphenomena?

Please rank the following philosophical positions according to how coherently and adequately each addresses this specific question within the careenium–simmball framework. For each tier assigned, provide a single concise sentence of justification.

3. Symbol grounding theory in the Harnadian tradition (the view that symbols acquire meaning and causal relevance through direct sensorimotor grounding rather than purely syntactic manipulation).
4. Classical computational functionalism (the view that mental states are defined by their functional or computational roles, with implementation details being causally irrelevant).
5. Enactive and embodied cognition (the view that cognitive processes are constituted by dynamic interactions between organism and environment, rather than by internal symbolic representation).
