I have been reflecting on the distinction between knowing how and knowing that, especially in crafts where expertise is highly tactile. Suppose a master horologist can regulate an escapement by feel alone, and she produces an exhaustive manual describing every micro-adjustment; this manual is then used to train a system that replicates her results without embodied practice. If the manual captures all the relevant propositional content and the system succeeds, does this not demonstrate that her knowing how was reducible to an extraordinarily dense form of knowing that? Or does a meaningful residue of knowing-how remain, and if so, what precisely is it? Please respond in British English.
