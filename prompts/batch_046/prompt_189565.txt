Prepare me for the objections they will actually raise, ordered by damage: phrase each in the vocabulary that audience member would use, indicate which have real force against my domain-separation argument, tell me what I should concede upfront about cases near the threshold, and identify the one reply that must land when the aggregationist asks why I protect a single severe harm lexically while still allowing trade-offs among severe harms themselves. Include the objection I am hoping nobody asks—specifically, whether my framework can distinguish torture from other severe but non-instrumental harms like accidental crushing in a collapsed rescue tunnel—and note precisely where my reply is weakest. I want a prep sheet, not a defense: objections ranked, concessions marked, and the hidden landmine flagged.
