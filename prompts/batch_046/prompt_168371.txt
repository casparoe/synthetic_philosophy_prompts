At the end, please wrap the whole thing in a JSON object with these fields: 'procedure_steps' (array of objects with 'step_number', 'question', 'answer_type' either 'fact' or 'value', and 'stop_or_continue'), 'hard_cases_handled' (array of strings describing each), 'limitations' (array of strings), and 'case_results' (array of objects with 'case_name', 'outcome', and 'note').
