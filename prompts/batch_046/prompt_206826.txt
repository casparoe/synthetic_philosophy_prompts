Write an essay on whether rationality allows arbitrary choice when there is genuinely no preference between options. Start with Buridan's ass, but push it into weirder territory: suppose the ass knows it's in a fully deterministic system, so any 'decision' it makes was causally fixed before it ever arived, and there is literally no feature that distinguishes the left bale from the right. Is the paralysis still irrational, or does the lack of authorship mean there's nothing wrong with just standing there? Weigh the argument that rationality demands a reason against the idea that tie-breaking is itself a rational move. Don't end on a fence-sitting note -- defend one side, and say what it implies for human decision-making when we're genuinly indifferent.
