(2) So I can know there's milk in the fridge (M) because in nearby empty-fridge worlds my eyes tell me and I drop the belief, even though in far-away BIV worlds I'd still think there's milk. (6) So tracking for R fails unless I can rule out those worlds — which needs me to know there's no hidden proof, i.e. it needs closure from R to that denial. (7) Therefore if I want to keep R, I have to apply the very closure principle R denies. (8) So tracking either restores closure or makes its own rejection unknowable.
