I'm on the state biotech review panel and we got this application that's driving us nuts. A drug company wants conditional approval using Phase II interim data where the biomarker results are ambigious — not clearly hitting the endpoint, not clearly missing it, just sitting in a noisy middle zone. Their statisticians propose using Jeffrey conditionalization to update efficacy beliefs continuously rather than treating the data as a failed binary trial. Some panelists say that's exactly how rational agents should handle uncertain evidence; others say regulators need hard evidential thresholds and Jeffrey updates just hide subjective priors behind math. I need a memo recommending approve, reject, or approve-with-conditions, with reasoning that addresses both sides honestly — especially the worry that letting ambiguous data shift beliefs this way collapses the distinction between evidence and interpretation. Keep it formal enough that the company can reply, but don't pretend this isn't a philosophical fight about what uncertain evidence is allowed to do.
