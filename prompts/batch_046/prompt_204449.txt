Rather than comparing the usual fixes—precommitment devices, causal decision theories, or evolutionary accounts—I want to step back and specify what an adequate theory of credible self-binding and rational commitment in strategic interaction actually needs to deliver. Write out the criteria: the phenomena any such theory must explain, the distinctions it must preserve without collapse, and the constraints from psychology and evolutionary reasoning it cannot violate.
