Take the AlphaGo vs Lee Sedol match in 2016, specifcally move 37 in game 2 that commentators called creative and human-like. Analyze it through Dennett's framework of real patterns and the three stances — physical, design, intentional. Don't just summarize Dennett; I want you to work with a twist I'm calling stance leakage, where applying the intentional stance to AlphaGo succeeds not because it tracks real beliefs or desires but because the design stance (the policy network) is opaque to human observers, so we project mental states onto outputs we can't physically derive. Is move 37 a real pattern that only shows up under the design stance? Does attributing intuition to AlphaGo here tell us anything real about the system, or is it just a heuristic for our own cognitive limits?
