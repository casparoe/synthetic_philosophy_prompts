Write an essay on the following controversy in Pascal's thought: does his coupling of justice and force in the Pensées reduce justice to a conventional legitimation of power, or does it preserve an independent normative standard that merely requires coercive enforcement to be effective? Examine both the well-known fragments on justice without force being powerless and force without justice being tyrannical, and the less-discussed tension between his appeal to custom as the source of equity and his parallel insistence that reason can discern natural laws of justice. Weigh the interpretations that make Pascal a proto-Hobbesian conventionalist against those that read him as maintaining a realist conception of justice, and reach a definite conclusion — defend one reading with reasons. Write in British English, and do not provide bibliographies, reading lists, or citations.
