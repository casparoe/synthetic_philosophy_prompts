Please present the story as exactly five scenes, numbered 1 through 5, each rendered in one or two sentences, with no introductory or concluding framing. Keep the tone formal and conceptually precise, but allow the emotional weight of the identity problem to surface naturally in the archivist's actions rather than in explicit philosophical exposition.
