Design a branching taxonomy—structured as a decision tree whose internal nodes are concrete commitments rather than theory labels—of positions on whether the iterated prisoner's dilemma genuinely explains the emergence of cooperation or instead presupposes the social capacities it claims to derive. The root should ask whether cooperation in a one-shot, anonymous prisoner's dilemma is rationally indefensible for agents with purely instrumental preferences and no pre-existing social ties; route the "yes" branch through mechanisms that make iteration work (pairwise direct reciprocity with specific partner memory, distributed indirect reciprocity through population-level reputation, or a restructuring of preferences so that cooperation becomes payoff-relevant independent of future rounds), and route the "no" branch through structures that permit one-shot cooperation without altering the payoff matrix (binding pre-commitment mechanisms, identity-conferred utility that makes defection self-defeating, or hidden-variable coordination on shared signals). At each subsequent node, pose a diagnostic case: for instance, whether cooperation can survive if players cannot individually identify their partners across rounds but only observe aggregate reputation scores; whether evolutionary stability requires genetic transmission or can operate through cultural imitation alone; whether the "shadow of the future" functions as an explicit rational calculation, an evolved cognitive heuristic, or a normative framing device. Phrase every node as a question answerable without knowing the position names in advance. The leaves should locate named positions where standard ones exist—evolutionary game-theoretic accounts, reciprocal-altruism explanations, rational contractarian views, norm-internalization theories—and should explicitly mark any unoccupied logical combinations, diagnosing each gap as incoherent, dynamically unstable, or merely unexplored. Every leaf must carry a one-sentence statement of its most awkward philosophical commitment, particularly regarding whether it explains cooperation without circularly assuming capacities for recognition, memory, or social categorization. Flag any pair of leaves that the tree cannot genuinely separate despite different metaphysical commitments, and specify that the instrument is intended for a philosophy of science or social-theory seminar with approximately eight to ten terminal nodes.
