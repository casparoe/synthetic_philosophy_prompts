Each question should be accompanied by a follow-up calibrated to the reply she is most likely to offer, particularly her anticipated distinction between the formal availability and the rhetorical accessibility of intermediate options. Please draft these in formal, polished prose, using British English spelling and punctuation conventions, and avoid generic biographical or definitional questions.
