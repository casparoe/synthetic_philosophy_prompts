My thesis is that compulsory voting shouldn't be sold on equality or civic-duty grounds but treated as a data-collection mechanism: only near-universal turnout lets us map desert-claims across citizens and long-term non-citizen residents, which then justifies lowering the voting age to 14 and giving local votes to residents without citizenship. The audience is mostly democratic theorists—Rawlsians, deliberative democrats—plus one political economist who thinks voting is already too noisy. Give me the objections they'll actually raise, in their vocabulary, ordered by how damaging they'd be. For each I need a two-minute reply and a note on where that reply is softest.
