The target is the logic of Milton Friedman’s “F-twist” in the philosophy of science and economics—the claim that unrealistic assumptions in a model are harmless or even methodologically virtuous, that predictive accuracy is the sole relevant criterion of evaluation, and that asking whether the internal premises are “realistic” confuses the logic of science with the logic of description.
