Can you write a short story about that? Set it in a near-future city where there's this government program called the Sanctum Project that ranks people on a Moral Purity Index and offers neural tweaks to push citizens toward perfect altruism. The main character should be Jax, a musician who's offered the final upgrade that would wipe out all his ego-driven desires and turn him into an unceasing helper of others. He needs to wrestle with the fact that this might also erase the personal struggle and vanity that fuel his best compositions. Definitely include a scene where he talks to his sister, who already recieved the upgrade and now sounds like a very kind but hollow shell of there former self. Show both the appeal — a society without cruelty — and the unsettling cost when moral goodness is pushed to its absolute limit. Keep it ambiguous, don't resolve it with a neat moral. Maybe end with him holding the upgrade chip and not deciding.
