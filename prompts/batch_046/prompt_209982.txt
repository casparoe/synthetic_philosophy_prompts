If humanity goes extinct but an old treaty still reads 'do not poison the aquifer,' I think there's still a real reason not to poison it, like the reason is baked into the treaty's normative structure.

If a calculator is broken and should show 42, that's just causal mechanics, not a reason.

If a museum painting is destroyed and every trace of memory is erased, I still feel there was a reason to save it that wasn't just 'someone enjoyed looking at it.'
