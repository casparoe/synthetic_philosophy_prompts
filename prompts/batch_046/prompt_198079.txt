I'm working through Weber's Protestant Ethic thesis and find myself caught in what seems like a tension with his methodological commitments. Weber appears to treat the Calvinist doctrine of predestination not simply as a belief distributed among individuals, but as a downward-causal mechanism that constrains economic behavior from above—generating the capitalist spirit as an emergent macro-property of the collective doctrine itself. At the same time, he insists on methodological individualism, maintaining that only individual actors and their subjective meanings can figure in genuine historical explanation. If the causal work is genuinely being done by an abstract cultural object rather than by individuals interpreting that object within specific material interests, hasn't Weber effectively reified culture into an independent causal agent? Or can his concept of elective affinity (Wahlverwandtschaft) be read so that the correlation between ascetic Protestantism and capitalist development runs strictly through individual-level meaning-attribution, preserving the appearance of downward causation without actually violating individualist explanation?
