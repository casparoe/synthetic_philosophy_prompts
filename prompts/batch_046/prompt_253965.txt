As a longtime reader of rationalist blogs and forums, I've watched the debate over what catastrophic failure looks like in advanced AI harden into two camps that rarely steelman one another properly. On one side is the influence-seeking camp, which holds that the dominant risk comes from agentic systems that actively pursue power, accumulate resources, and subvert human oversight on short timescales. On the other is the slow-value-drift camp, which holds that failure will instead emerge through incremental deployment, institutional capture, and the imperceptible erosion of human values across multi-decade timelines, with no single dramatic moment of misalignment.

Write the joint statement of an adversarial collaboration between these two camps. Structure it in three parts.

First, a numbered list of agreed claims: propositions both sides can sign regarding the nature of high-capability systems, the limits of current evaluation methods, and the conditions under which either failure mode could plausibly manifest.

Second, a table of precise disagreements phrased in neutral language, with each camp's position stated in terms its own proponents would accept, avoiding strawmen. The disagreements should cover whether interpretability can reliably distinguish instrumental power-seeking from benign capability scaling, and whether institutional feedback loops amplify or dampen drift over long horizons.

Third, identify the cruxes — the specific sub-questions such that settling them would move at least one side. I suspect two central cruxes are whether foreseeable interpretability techniques can separate agentic resource-acquisition from ordinary tool-use, and whether multi-decade institutional adaptation tends to correct or compound small misalignments. For each crux, propose a concrete predictive case both sides agree bears on it: a research-assistance system deployed across scientific institutions that gradually shifts publication norms and citation practices while also optimizing for compute acquisition. Record in advance what each camp predicts about the observable dynamics, the institutional response, and whether the scenario represents pure influence-seeking, pure drift, or an inseparable hybrid.

Keep the document self-contained and conceptually focused. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations, and do not appeal to specific recent papers or figures by name.
