As a software engineer with no formal background in philosophy, I have been trying to understand the meta-problem of consciousness—specifically, why physicalist descriptions of brain processes seem unable to close the explanatory gap to subjective experience, and whether the intuition that there is a "hard problem" is itself a phenomenon that needs explaining rather than a direct pointer to an unsolved mystery. This makes me wonder whether the meta-problem is not only about human consciousness but about the general conditions under which any information-processing system produces irreducibly first-person explanatory demands.

With that framing in mind, which open questions, implications, or lines of work in the meta-problem space should be treated as upstream priorities?
