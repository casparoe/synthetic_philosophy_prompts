Compare updateless decision theory with the variant I will call indexical evidential decision theory. In updateless decision theory, an agent selects the policy that maximizes expected utility across all worlds in which that policy is executed, without conditioning on the particular computation it is currently running. In indexical evidential decision theory, the agent may condition on an indexical fact—that it is this specific computation occurring at this spatiotemporal location—but only if that indexical refers solely to features that the policy itself does not causally affect.
