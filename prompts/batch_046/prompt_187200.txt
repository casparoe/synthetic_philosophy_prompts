Draft the joint statement of an adversarial collaboration between a strong adaptationist and a structuralist critic working within the Gould-Lewontin spandrels framework, restricted specifically to what they term "architectural carryover traits": organismal features that are developmentally coupled to genuinely adaptive structures, possess no independent selective history, remain selectively invisible even under ecological release, and are never co-opted into exaptations. The document should contain: (1) a numbered list of agreed claims about natural selection, developmental architecture, the logical possibility of spandrels, and the defining properties of architectural carryover traits; (2) a disagreements table in which the exact points of divergence are stated twice—once in wording the adaptationist accepts, once in wording the structuralist accepts—covering at least whether such traits occur at non-negligible frequency, whether selective invisibility is empirically distinguishable from weak persistent selection, and whether their existence undermines adaptationist heuristics; (3) for each crux, one concrete case or empirical pattern both sides agree bears on it, together with each side's explicit prediction about what that case will show; and (4) a brief identification of any disagreement that is purely verbal (for example, about whether "spandrel" should be restricted to geometric byproducts) with an explanation of why it does not affect the substantive dispute. Format the agreed claims as a numbered section and the disagreements as a table. Do not include bibliographies, citations, or requests for further reading.
