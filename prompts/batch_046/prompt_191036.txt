the student answered:
The heterogenity problem is that pleasures are too diverse—like a hot shower vs getting a punchline—to be one single kind of thing. A hedonist can reply that they dont need the same feeling, just that we want them for themselves. Mill talked about higher and lower pleasures, but that was about intensity, not different kinds, so he doesnt actually solve it. Also every pleasure triggers dopamine in the nucleus accumbens, so the common factor is biological, not mental. Bentham said pushpin equals poetry if the pleasure is equal, which means he ignored quality differences altogether.
