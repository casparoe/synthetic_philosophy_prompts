The punchline should hinge on the contradiction between this utopian economic forecast and Keynes's elitist pessimism about mass leisure, not on generic stereotypes about economists, money, or workaholism. Make it intelligible to someone who knows the essay's broad argument, but do not require knowledge of Keynes's private correspondence, minor biographical details, or any verbatim passage from the text.
