I hold that the presumptuous philosopher objection undermines self-sampling anthropic reasoning but leaves self-indication untouched, and that this asymmetry makes self-indication the default framework unless specifically overridden. Tell me what would count against this position, ranked from fatal to merely awkward. I want formal results about reference-class stability or observer-counting invariance, intuitive cases involving multiverse structure or selection effects, and conceptual points about whether allowing non-actual observers lets the objection rebound onto self-indication. Which defeaters would defenders of self-sampling themselves concede, and which would they dismiss as mischaracterizing the view? Also assess whether the position risks being qualified into unfalsifiability—say, by restricting the domain of observers so narrowly that no counterexample could arise—and whether that insulation is a virtue or a vice for an anthropic framework.
