I want to test consistency with a colleague's takes. She holds that human extinction is catastrophic even if everyone dies painlessly, since it kills both present lives and every potential future one. She also thinks a world where people just stop having kids is sad but not the same moral disaster, since nobody is actually killed. And she says if we went extinct but were replaced by flourishing aliens who kept science and art going, it'd be somewhat less bad, though still a serious loss.

Now I'm throwing her this case: suppose we develop perfect mind-uploading, and over a century every human voluntarily uploads into digital substrates that live for milenia, keep doing philosphy and music, but biological reproduction stops and Homo sapiens dies out. Would she treat that as extinction-level tragedy?
