(1) i'm a luck egalitarian — if you end up poor because of bad genetic cards or a rough childhood, society owes you; if you're poor because you blew your cash on first editions, that's on you.

(2) i think narrative talent is mostly brute luck. the right brain, parents who read aloud, landing in a decent MFA — that's all circumstance, not choice.

(3) but i still think copyright royalties should stay tied to sales. readers choose to buy, i choose to keep writing. if i earn more because i'm talanted, that's not injustice, that's choice working on both sides.

(4) i think inheritence taxes should be near 90% — getting rich from your parents is pure brute luck.

(5) i reject the slavery of the talented argument. people with gifts aren't forced to use them; they can become accountants. taxing income from talent isn't slavery.

(6) i think expensive tastes shaped by your upbringing — like wanting to live in Manhattan or collect rare books — should still be paid for by you, not subsidized, even if thier origins are pure circumstance.
