Write a joke that takes American exceptionalism seriously as a conceptual knot rather than as a patriotic punchline. Treat it as the unstable intersection of three registers: a historical thesis about distinctive institutional development (including the frontier thesis and the 1890 census closure that declared that frontier ended), a normative claim that the nation holds a special moral or democratic mission, and an ideology capable of legitimizing imperial practice. The punchline should hinge on a tension internal to this triad—preferably the paradox that the normative claim to uniqueness undermines the comparative historical reasoning required to establish it, or the collapse of "exemptionalism" (the promotion of universal norms coupled with claimed exemption from them) into ordinary great-power hypocrisy. The joke should be intelligible to someone who grasps that structure and should fail if "American exceptionalism" is replaced with generic national pride; avoid simple irony about hypocrisy and do not ask for citations or examples from scholarship.
