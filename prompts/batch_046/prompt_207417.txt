Drawing on his discussions of wine in the Symposium and the Laws—specifically his view that moderated intoxication can function as a testing ground for the soul’s mastery over pleasure, and his political concern that law should educate citizens toward internal self-rule rather than merely restrain them—would he regard these substances as a legitimate harm-reduction tool that spares bodies unnecessary damage, or would he reject them because they strip away the very bodily penalties and mental fog that make drinking an ethically formative experience?
