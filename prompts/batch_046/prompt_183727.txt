Finally, what does this episode reveal about the stability of his distinction—does it show that the boundary between astronomy and astrology was always porous in practice, or does it instead anticipate a modern form of model pluralism, in which different representational frameworks are adopted for different practical purposes without requiring ontological consistency between them?
