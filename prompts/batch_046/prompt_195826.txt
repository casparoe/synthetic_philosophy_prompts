Is this consistent? In particular, does (2) undermine (4) by allowing intentionality to float free of any biological source within the loop? Is there a hidden tension between (1) and (3) about whether non-biological artifacts can instantiate the kind of derived intentionality that (2) describes? And is the friction between (2) and (5) only apparent—does accepting distributed interpretation dissolve the worry that syntax alone never produces semantics?
