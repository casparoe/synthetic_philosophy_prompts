I'm drafting a philosophical dialogue novel in which two of my central characters—an absurdist philosopher who holds that human existence is cosmically pointless and that meaning must be forged through lucid, rebellious engagement with that very pointlessness, and a contemporary naturalist who holds that meaning is an objective property of lives that successfully integrate subjective fulfillment with rational engagement with genuinely worthy ends—have spent months arguing without ever producing a joint statement of where they stand. To ground the novel's climax, I need a document they could plausibly have co-authored after an adversarial collaboration.

Please draft this joint statement. First, present a numbered section of claims both camps genuinely endorse: include agreements about what existential crisis reveals about human psychology, about why subjective satisfaction by itself cannot settle questions of meaning, and about the conditions under which a life can be said to fail at meaning altogether. Second, provide a disagreements table in which each position is rendered in wording its own advocates would accept—do not straw-man either side—specifying exactly where they diverge, particularly on whether objective value is a necessary condition for meaning and on whether cosmic silence permanently undermines local human projects or can itself become integrated into a meaningful structure. Third, identify the cruxes: the sub-questions that, if resolved, would actually shift at least one camp's position. For each crux, supply a concrete case or thought experiment both sides agree is diagnostically relevant—such as a life of intense but trivial fulfillment, or Sisyphus endlessly pushing a boulder without external purpose—with each side's explicit prediction of what that case demonstrates about meaning. Do not include bibliographies, citations, or reading lists, and avoid relying on highly specialized terminology that a well-informed but non-specialist reader would not recognize.

Finally, include an explicit assessment of where your own reasoning in drafting this statement is most likely to fail—whether in interpreting subjective reports of fulfillment, in drawing inferences from cosmic scale to local value, or elsewhere—and specify precisely which argument or piece of evidence would cause you to revise your judgment about whether a given disagreement is substantive rather than verbal. Write formally, as an appendix suitable for the novel.
