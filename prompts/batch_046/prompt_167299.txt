They maintain that moral theories are structured by internal deontic modalities—permissions, obligations, prohibitions, and lexical priorities—that resist cardinal translation across frameworks. Their prescribed response to uncertainty is meta-level and categorical: apply a lexical rule to identify the single theory with the highest all-things-considered credence, then act within its constraints; uncertainty is resolved prior to action, not distributed within it.
