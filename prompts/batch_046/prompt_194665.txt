Please draft six to eight interview questions that avoid asking her to restate the view. Include at least one question that presses whether invoking holistic structural relations avoids substantivalism or merely renames it, specifically asking what explanatory work the structure does that pairwise relations cannot. Inject one question about topologically closed universes—say, a spatial 3-torus with no asymptotic distant matter—asking what fixes the inertial frame for rotation there, and anticipate her reply that boundary conditions or global topology supply the reference. Finally, include a question that connects back to the vestibular experience: if rotation is purely relational, why does an isolated inner-ear system seem to detect true rotation without comparing itself to cosmic matter? End with one question asking what observable or theoretical result could genuinely count against her relationism rather than being absorbed as just another structural feature to be mapped.
