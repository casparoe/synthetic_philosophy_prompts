Do not include a bibliography, citation list, or reading recommendations; the argument must stand entirely on its own for anyone without library or web access. Do not quote any classical source verbatim; if you reference Gongsun Long's reasoning, paraphrase it entirely in your own language without quotation marks. Reach a clear framing conclusion that both halves of the room can share as a legitimate, unresolved problem deserving collaborative attention. Write the speech now.
