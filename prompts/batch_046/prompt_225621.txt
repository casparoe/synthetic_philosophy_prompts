Write a short story about a forensic mnemonic engineer in a near-future archive who reconstructs human memories from corrupted neural backups. She comes to hold a justified, true belief about a specific historical crime, yet her justification traces back to a reconstructed testimony that is largely false—its accuracy is only preserved by an unrelated quantum-error correction in a separate storage cluster. The narrative should explore, through her internal deliberation and a critical decision she must make, whether her belief counts as knowledge in light of this accidental connection between false justification and true conclusion. Keep the conceptual structure of the Gettier problem central, but do not name it explicitly; instead, let the philosophical tension emerge organically from the plot and her reasoning.
