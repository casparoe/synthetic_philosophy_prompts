I'm working through an interpretation of Schopenhauer's philosophy of music for a graduate seminar and want a consistency check before I present. Here is the package I'm defending:

(1) Music is the direct objectification of the Will itself, unmediated by Platonic Ideas; this is not a difference of degree from the other arts but a categorical distinction in metaphysical status.

(2) The correspondence Schopenhauer draws between the bass, harmonic middle voices, melody, and the grades of nature (inorganic, plant, animal, rational human) is not an illustrative metaphor but expresses a structural identity: the harmonic series literally recapitulates the hierarchy of the Will's objectification.

(3) Because music bypasses Ideas, it does not represent particular emotional states; nevertheless, when we say that an adagio expresses melancholy, we are correct if we understand this as music instantiating the universal form or Idea of that affect, not depicting an instance of it.

(4) The aesthetic experience of music provides temporary relief from the suffering of the Will, but since music is nothing other than the Will made audible, this relief cannot come from escaping the Will. Instead, it comes from becoming a pure, will-less subject of cognition in the face of the Will's direct self-presentation.

(5) Music is therefore metaphysically prior to all other arts and closer to the thing-in-itself than philosophy is, yet philosophy alone can achieve the permanent denial of the Will; consequently, Schopenhauer's ranking of philosophy above music is soteriologically sound but metaphysically puzzling.

Do these hang together? Specifically: does (3)'s appeal to the "Idea" of an affect contradict (1)'s claim that music bypasses Ideas entirely? Is (4)'s account of aesthetic relief compatible with Schopenhauer's general theory that aesthetic contemplation requires the objectification of Ideas, given that music offers no Ideas to contemplate? And does (2)'s structural claim commit me to a determinism about musical form that Schopenhauer's actual examples do not support? Please order the tensions by severity, distinguish apparent from real conflicts, and say which commitment is least costly to revise and what that revision costs my overall interpretation.
