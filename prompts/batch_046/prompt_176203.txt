design a structured game or role-play where pairs have to defend a mundane claim (e.g., there's a table in front of me) by constructing the strongest sceptical argument they can. but they start with a "framework budget" of only three background commitments they are allowed to suspend—things like "my senses are reliable in normal light," "the past isn't systematically fabricated," "shared language works." if they suspend more than three they hit "epistemic bankruptcy" and have to stop, because they've destroyed the conditions for reasoning. meanwhile the rest of the group act as "hinge auditors" who track every suspended commitment and call out when a doubt is secretly borrowing against an untouched hinge.
