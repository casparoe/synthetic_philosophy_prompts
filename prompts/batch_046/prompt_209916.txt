In my advanced logic course this term, I have been asking students to evaluate whether supertasks genuinely generate metaphysical indeterminacy, or whether they merely expose limitations in our descriptive apparatus. Taking Thomson's lamp as the canonical case—the lamp toggled at intervals of one-half, one-fourth, one-eighth minute, with the limit reached at two minutes—I understand that the sequence of states has no terminal element and therefore assigns no unique state to the limit point. Nevertheless, I am inclined to think that if each toggle is a fully actualized causal event, then the infinite series of transitions constitutes a completed totality with a definite cardinality. From this, it seems to follow that the absence of a determinate on-or-off condition at t = 2 is not an epistemic gap but an ontological one: the lamp genuinely instantiates neither property, which would entail that the law of excluded middle fails for physical states at spatiotemporal boundaries.

To probe this further, I devised a modified scenario—let us call it the attenuating lamp—where the toggle intervals shrink geometrically as in the original, but each successive illumination emits only half the luminous energy of its predecessor, so that the total radiant energy converges to a finite value. In this version, even if we cannot determine the toggle-parity at the limit, the system's physical parameters asymptotically approach zero luminosity, suggesting that the lamp determinately occupies a limit-state of being off in all physically measurable respects. My question, then, is whether this attenuation variant actually resolves Thomson's paradox by supplying the missing limit-state through physical convergence, or whether it merely masks the logical indeterminacy behind an empirically undetectable parameter. More precisely, does the original paradox depend on an implicit presupposition that state-transitions must preserve constant magnitude, and if we reject that presupposition, have we dissolved the argument for ontological indeterminacy altogether?
