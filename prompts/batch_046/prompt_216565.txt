I'm giving a seminar next week defending my own functional-triadic account of deception in LLMs — basically that deception happens when three things line up: the model's internal representational state misaligns with its output, there's an active suppression of the corrrective signals that usually trigger refusal or hedging, and the user is operating under a normative expactation of transparency that gets violated. I'm not attributing beliefs or intentions, just mapping the structure.
