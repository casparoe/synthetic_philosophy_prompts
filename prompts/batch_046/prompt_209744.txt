I defend an authorizationist variant of the moral equality of combatants: symmetric rights and liabilities do not rest on epistemic parity or shared physical risk, but on an institutional delegation of killing-authority that is structurally blind to the justice of the cause, functioning analogously to a judicial authorization that requires no personal endorsement of the statute by the judge. I apply this even to remote operators and autonomous weapon systems, since equality is fixed by the authorization relation rather than by reciprocal danger.
