Write a villanelle about imprecise probabilities — the credal-set kind where belief is an interval, not a point. Make the two refrains act like the lower and upper bounds of that interval, and let the rhyme scheme echo the fact that there's no single "true" value in the middle. Keep it tight enough that a philosopher of decision theory wouldn't wince, but don't just list axioms; make the form do the work.
