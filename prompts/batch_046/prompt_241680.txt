Restate each of these claims, as precisely and charitably as you can, using only the vocabulary of RL, training dynamics, and agent design. Finally, tell me whether the residue suggests that modern training paradigms are missing something structurally important—perhaps a notion of intrinsic moral development that cannot be reduced to optimization—or whether it merely shows that Locke was addressing a different kind of being with different constraints, so that the comparison, however illuminating, eventually breaks down for reasons that do not indict either framework.
