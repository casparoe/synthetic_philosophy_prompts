I will treat this as a philosophical rather than empirical project, since the progress depends on sharpening distinctions, not on collecting data.

What would count as success is a clear taxonomy showing that the butterfly argument exposes a specific problem about form-independent phenomenal authority that standard theories of personal identity do not address; failure would be showing that the symmetry is either reducible to generic skepticism or to metaphysical transformation, leaving my framing redundant. The scope is realistic for an independent summer project because it requires close textual reasoning and conceptual comparison rather than mastery of an extensive literature.
