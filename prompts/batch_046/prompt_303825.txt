Here is the pattern I keep noticing in discussions of whether death is bad for the one who dies. A defender of the deprivation account argues that death harms by robbing the deceased of future goods. When pressed with the symmetry objection—why is pre-birth non-existence not equally harmful?—the defender often distinguishes directions of time. But within that reply I see a more specific move: the defender treats the point at which a person actually comes to exist as a kind of normative baseline or entry-gate.
