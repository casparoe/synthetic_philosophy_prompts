I am preparing materials for a graduate seminar on agency and determinism, and I want a precisely engineered thought experiment that inverts the standard philosophical deployment of sphexishness — the stereotyped behavioral rigidity of the digger wasp Sphex, which inspects its burrow and drags prey through an invariant sequence even when the prey has been moved during inspection. In the canonical literature this loop serves as a reductio of flexible agency or free will; I need a scenario in which that very rigidity is not a cognitive failure but the structural condition for a superior form of practical autonomy, while an adaptive, fully informed counterpart fails catastrophically precisely because it responds to changed circumstances in real time.

The setting must be realistic — a hospital triage protocol, a judicial sentencing guideline, or an industrial safety procedure — with human-scale stakes, no exotic physics or perfect predictors, and all agents possessing ordinary human cognition. The sphexish agent must be fully aware that conditions have changed, yet institutionally or architecturally locked into repeating its loop (not ignorant or brain-damaged); the flexible agent must have identical information and adapt immediately, but its adaptation must be the direct cause of the disaster. Present the scenario in one paragraph.

Then show that the case works: state the verdict of a mechanist about agency (who treats sphexish repetition as incompatible with free will) and that of a rational-commitment theorist (who treats irreversible pre-commitment rules as constitutive of autonomy). Identify the exact structural feature — the irreversibility of the loop, the prohibitive cost of interruption, or the informational firewall isolating the loop from feedback — that does the work in producing the inversion. Finally, explain what happens if that feature is removed (does the case collapse into a standard sphexish reductio?), and how the mechanist would attack the scenario as question-begging rather than as a genuine demonstration that rigidity can constitute autonomy.
