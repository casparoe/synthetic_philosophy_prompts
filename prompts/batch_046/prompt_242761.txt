Two friends of mine were arguing about sphexishness and free will over beers. (1) One said the sphex wasp proves determinism: it checks the burrow, moves the cricket inside, checks again, and loops forever if you shift the cricket during the check. Clearly just running a hardcoded script with no reflection, so if human habits are sphexish then our "choices" are just longer loops with better marketing. (2) The other said thats a strawman of biology; the wasps rigidity is ecologically rational, not mechanical failure. Humans have meta-cognitive override, but even that might just be a second-order loop that feels like freedom without being it. (3) The first guy pushed back: theres a qualitative break when you can represent your own representation, not just run it. The wasp never models its own burrow-check; we do, and that reflexive gap is where agency lives. (4) The second guy said modeling your own model is still computation, and invoking "qualitative breaks" is mysticism with extra steps. He added that if free will needs infinite regress of self-modeling, thats impossible, not liberating.

Please adjudicate move by move: rate each of the four moves from clearly wrong to clearly right and breifly justify. Whats both sides missing about whether sphexishness even applies to human agency?
