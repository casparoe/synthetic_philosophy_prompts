They need to actually respond to each other—Jordan should ask how partitioning explains the phenomenology of being shocked when your hidden motive is revealed, and Alex should answer with the specific feeling of surprise, not just "new information arrived." Work through a concrete case: a professor who writes glowing letters for a student she secretly thinks is mediocre, and only realizes her true belief when she accidently reads her own unfiltered notes. By the end they should still disagree, but the split should be clear—is "self" in self-deception defined by global transparency or by local functional roles? Keep the tone casual and natural, like an actual lounge conversation, and feel free to have them interupt each other.
