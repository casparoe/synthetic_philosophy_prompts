Subjects report the orange path but describe the first dot as "faint" or "maybe not real." Verdict: this feels Orwellian to me — the brain received the signal but edited it out of the official historical record, rather than inventing the middle part.

Case 5 is the same subliminal-first setup, but now we ask directly, "did you see a red dot at position A?" They deny it, yet they still describe the full orange trajectory. Verdict: definatly should line up with case 4, but it feels different — like the denial is part of the editing process itself, not a separate failure of memory.

So what's the deal? Is there a coherent principle that decides when consciousness does an Orwellian rewrite versus a Stalinesque insertion?
