I'm putting together my position on dispositions, finks, and masks for a metaphysics reading group, and I'd like an audit of whether these commitments hang together or whether I'm helping myself to incompatible motivations. Here is my package: (1) I am a realist about dispositions—they are genuine properties of objects, not merely predictive fictions. (2) I accept the standard distinction between finks and masks: a fink removes a disposition by altering the object itself, while a mask merely blocks manifestation without altering the bearer. (3) I think the failure of the simple conditional analysis—if conditions C obtain then manifestation M occurs—shows that dispositions cannot be reduced to counterfactuals, yet I still maintain that a disposition essentially involves what its bearer would do in conditions free of both finks and masks. (4) At the same time, I deny any metaphysically sharp line between a disposition and its categorical base; I take the distinction to be pragmatic and explanatory-level relative. (5) I hold that an object can genuinely retain a disposition under permanent masking—consider a reactive compound permanently stored in an inert atmosphere—but I deny that a permanently finked object, such as a wire always coupled to a suppressor, retains the corresponding disposition. (6) Finally, I treat the "ideal conditions" mentioned in (3) as scientific idealizations rather than descriptions of genuinely possible worlds; they function as heuristics for tracking underlying structures, not as modal specifications.

Are these jointly tenable? Please go through the pairs and tell me which are in real tension, which only appear to conflict but dissolve on inspection, and what the cheapest revision would be for each genuine conflict. Rank the tensions by severity rather than treating them as equally damaging, and identify which single change would clean up the most problems overall. Be specific about whether the difficulty lies in (2)'s distinction, in (4)'s denial of a sharp boundary, in (5)'s asymmetry between masking and finking, or in (6)'s deflation of ideal conditions, and do not signal which commitment I would be least sorry to abandon.
