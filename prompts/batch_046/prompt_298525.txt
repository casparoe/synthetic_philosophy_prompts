Additionally, please address a less commonly discussed condition: whether the theory must permit a scenario in which the entire material universe is translated uniformly through space without physical change, and how that possibility interacts with the bucket experiment. Present your response in American English, and do not provide bibliographies, reading lists, or citations; I need only the conceptual framework.
