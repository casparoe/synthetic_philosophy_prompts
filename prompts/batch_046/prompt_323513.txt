I'm a PhD student in metaphysics working on the ontology of properties. I've come to defend a view I'll call intrinsic profile structuralism: every genuine disposition is constituted not by a bare tendency toward manifestation, but by a coupled pair consisting of (i) the manifestation tendency and (ii) an intrinsic suppression profile that specifies the exact conditions under which that tendency is systematically blocked. On this account, finks and masks are not external interrupters that happen to prevent manifestation; they are ontologically internal to the disposition, different temporal expressions of the same suppression structure. Consequently, there are no "pure" unmasked dispositions—what we take to be the unblocked case is just the profile operating in its null or dormant phase. This collapses the standard fink/mask distinction: a fink is rapid suppression triggered at the moment of stimulus, while a mask is sustained suppression across conditions, but both are manifestations of the single internal profile rather than independent entities interfering with a base property.

Tell me what would genuinely count against this view—not just the standard objections to masked dispositions, but which specific intuitions, thought experiments, or results in adjacent areas (e.g., about causation, counterfactual dependence, or the metaphysics of properties) it risks or relies on. Which predictions does it make that rival accounts—say, a pure categorical-base view or a simple conditional analysis—do not? Rank the potential defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, and for each indicate whether a committed defender of intrinsic profile structuralism would concede it as damaging or would dismiss it via some qualification of the view. In particular, I'd like to know whether the view is well insulated against empirical or conceptual counterevidence, and whether that insulation is a theoretical virtue or a vice. I am personally most persuaded by arguments that turn on the modal behavior of dispositions across counterfactual scenarios rather than by appeals to linguistic intuition, so weight those accordingly.
