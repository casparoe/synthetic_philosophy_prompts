Act as a philosopher of science specializing in Imre Lakatos’s methodology of scientific research programmes. Before giving your verdict, briefly enumerate the competing rules that could explain the pattern below.

A senior colleague judges whether scientific programmes are progressive or degenerating. Her verdicts so far:
- Ptolemaic astronomy, adding epicycles to save phenomena without generating any novel predictions: degenerating.
- Newtonian gravitation, predicting both Neptune’s orbit and stellar aberration: progressive.
- A vitalist chemistry programme that invokes an unobservable “life force” for every failed organic synthesis, with no new predictions: degenerating.
- A climate-modelling programme that revises its cloud-feedback parameterization after satellite anomalies and then predicts an unobserved Southern-Ocean rainfall asymmetry: progressive.
- A neuroscience programme that explains away replication failures through post-hoc “contextual modulators,” yet also predicts a previously unreported cross-modal neural integration effect: progressive.

Now consider a new programme in evolutionary psychology. Its core adaptive narrative about human cooperation is repeatedly revised whenever cross-cultural data contradict it, with culture-specific auxiliary hypotheses added each time; but it has also predicted and confirmed a precise, counter-intuitive pattern of third-party punishment in a small-scale society that had never been studied.

Predict her verdict on this programme. Is she running a strict Lakatosian rule—progress requires novel predictions generated without substantial hard-core revision—or a looser rule that permits extensive protective-belt adjustment provided at least one surprising fact is produced? Identify which of her earlier cases is the closest analog. Then specify the exact further case—what structure of revision paired with what kind of prediction—that would definitively distinguish the strict interpretation from the loose one, and say which rule she would be committing to with each possible choice.
