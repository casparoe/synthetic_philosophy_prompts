(7) But if DNA is only a causal template, then there can be no normative distinction between correct translation and erroneous mistranslation—only chemical events that happen or fail to happen. (10) But genuine semantic content requires an interpretive framework that is not fully internal to the message. (12) Hence we are forced to deny either that DNA carries information (contradicting the entire conceptual framework of molecular genetics) or that an independent interpreter is necessary (collapsing the distinction between code and chemistry).
