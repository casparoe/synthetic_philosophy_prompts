For a feature on reasoning in the absence of proof, I'm tracking the implicit norms of a hypothetical review panel that assigns numerical confidence to open mathematical claims. The panel now faces a claim that is in fact true, whose proof runs exactly one thousand steps, and about which they have been informed solely that the proof length is one thousand—not given the proof itself. If they are to remain consistent with their prior rulings, what confidence should they assign?
