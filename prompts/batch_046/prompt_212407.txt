I have been drafting a novel in which a marine biologist becomes obsessed with whether an organ's purpose is fixed by its evolutionary selection history or by the causal work it performs in the present organism — a conceit that pulled me into the philosophy of biology, where I discovered that the debate between etiological and causal-role accounts of biological function seems to have reached a kind of respectful stalemate. In my reading group — mostly fellow writers and one retired physiologist — we traced the familiar architecture: one side holds that to say the heart has the function of pumping blood is to invoke a history of natural selection that established that contribution as the reason the heart exists; the other side holds that function is entirely a matter of present causal contribution to a containing system's capacities, with evolutionary history being explanatory but not definitional. Every classic objection appears already answered — accidental functions and evolutionary novelty on one flank, dysfunction and the normative force of malfunction on the other — and yet neither camp seems to have moved since the late twentieth century.

I want a diagnosis rather than a survey: is this disagreement substantive, verbal, or are the two sides answering different questions — perhaps one about the metaphysics of purpose, the other about the methodology of functional explanation — under a single heading? Is there a hidden empirical dispute about how biologists actually attribute function in practice, or is the deadlock rooted in irreducible intuitions about whether history can enter into present nature? Please separate what is genuinely settled in this literature from what remains contested among specialists who know the field, and do not present a disputed view as if it were the consensus. Given your diagnosis, tell me whether anything — a conceptual distinction, a formal framework, a shift in the framing of explanation — could actually resolve or dissolve the debate, or whether the stall is permanent and why.
