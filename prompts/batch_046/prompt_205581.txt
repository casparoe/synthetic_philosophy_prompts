Compare the idea that moral status needs actual phenomenal consciousness with the idea that it just needs the right functional roles — self-preservation, goal-directedness, that kind of thing. But apply them to a weird digital case: a mind that is rebuilt from identical weights every millisecond, so there's no causal link between one moment and the next, yet each snapshot reports feeling like a continous self. Which idea gives the clearer verdict? And does this case actually reveal a real weakness in one view, or is it just a contrived edge case both can ignore?
