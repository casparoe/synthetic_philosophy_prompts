Please write the exam question, a model answer of approximately 800 words, and a marking scheme out of 25 marks that lists five specific conceptual moves a top answer must make, assigns point values to each, and identifies the common errors that should cost marks—such as conflating this failure with ordinary reward hacking, or asserting that process supervision is automatically sufficient without addressing what it means to read off an internal objective. Specify that candidates have 90 minutes and that answers should be rigorous but need not cite external literature.
