Furthermore, interventionism is essentially structural equation modeling applied to metaphysics, and since standard SEM requires deterministic functional relationships to yield unique solutions, interventionism implicitly presupposes determinism. This is why it fails entirely for quantum causal claims, where no deterministic intervention variable can be isolated."
