The usual reply is to check with other people or look for inconsistencies, but it seems to me that if the dream is intersubjectively consistent—if everyone I ask is also part of the same dream-state—then asking others or checking coherence just moves in a circle. I want a workshop activity that makes ordinary people feel that circular trap in their bones: the moment when trying to prove you're awake becomes part of the skeptical scenario itself.
