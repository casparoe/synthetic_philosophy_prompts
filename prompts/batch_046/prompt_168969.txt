Please treat that sentence as an ambiguous slogan and lay out the distinct precise theses it could be expressing, arranged as a menu rather than a single interpretation. Then, with reference to the specific argumentative context I described—blocking the inference that a digital currency lacks the institutional status of paper cash—identify which of these readings actually supports that conclusion, which are irrelevant to it, and which would undermine the speaker's own position. Do not provide a bibliography, reading list, or citations; I am interested solely in the conceptual disambiguation and its dialectical consequences.
