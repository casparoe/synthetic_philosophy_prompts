And what about collective cases — can a society accumulate karmic debt for historical wrongs, or is karma locked to individual agents? Weigh this against standard liberal retributivism. Then reach an actual conclusion: is karma salvageable as a modern theory of moral desert, or does it collapse into determinism or cosmic consequentialism? Definately don't end on a fence — defend one view.
