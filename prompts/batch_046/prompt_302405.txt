Both accept that individual ants are not conscious, but they disagree about the colony-level case. (1) The first colleague argues that Aunt Hillary is genuinely minded if the colony instantiates the right functional organization; on this view, dispersing the ants across continents while preserving every interaction via satellite links should leave her consciousness intact, since only the pattern matters. (2) The second colleague objects that phenomenal unity depends on a temporal binding window; intercontinental transmission latencies would shatter this window, reducing the system to a distributed database with no single subject of experience, regardless of message fidelity. (4) The second replies that neural delays are evolutionarily calibrated to remain within one unified phenomenal present, whereas artificial satellite links lack such calibration, breaking the analogy. Please adjudicate move by move: rate each argument from clearly mistaken to clearly correct, identify any unstated premises both sides share, and say whether you believe Aunt Hillary could survive such dispersal. Think step by step, avoid appeals to authority, and do not provide bibliographies or citations.
