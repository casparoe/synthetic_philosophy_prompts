For a reading group on Borges's philosophy of time I have distilled my interpretation into five commitments, and I want an audit of whether they hang together. (a) Borges denies objective linear time: past, present, and future are simultaneous, following an idealist line drawn from Schopenhauer and Berkeley. (b) He treats personal identity as a sequence of discrete mental states stitched together by fallible, partly fictional memory, not as a continuous substance. (c) Nevertheless he constructs narratives—branching timelines in “The Garden of Forking Paths,” layered histories in “Tlön, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius”—that depend on precise chronological ordering and causal sequence to generate their philosophical effects, without marking these structures as merely heuristic fictions. (d) In essays such as “Borges and I” he presupposes a persistent, observing “I” that stands apart from the public, writing Borges and endures across the discrete moments described in (b). (e) He also accepts a modified eternal recurrence, not as physical cosmology but as a structural feature of language and literature: every possible narrative is already inscribed in a kind of timeless, total library, so repetition is logical rather than temporal.

Please audit the set. Rank genuine tensions by severity; for each say what the cheapest revision is and what it costs. Identify which pair only looks incompatible and dissolves on inspection. Then state which single revision to (a)–(e) would clean up the most inconsistencies, and whether the core insight survives.

Respond in these sections, in order:
1. “Apparent tension that dissolves” (about 150 words): the pair that looks contradictory but is not, and why.
2. “Genuine tensions, ranked” (about 350 words): real conflicts from most to least severe, with the least costly fix for each and its price.
3. “The single best revision” (about 120 words): which commitment to modify or drop and what the payoff is.
4. “Bottom line” (about 60 words): whether the package is salvageable without losing Borges’s central claim about time.
