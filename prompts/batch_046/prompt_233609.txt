The survivors' families and our history dept want open access, citing a collective duty to remember industrial violence and warning that sealing them risks what Rieff describes as a politics of convenient forgetting, while Margalit's framing of shared memory as a moral bond suggests we owe the dead preservation of thier narrative presence rather than erasure.

My co-chair wants to anonymize and release transcripts only, but I'm not sure that addresses the core harm since the estate argues the voices themselves carry the injury.
