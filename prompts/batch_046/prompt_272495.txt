Proceed entirely through institutional and conceptual analysis; do not provide bibliographies, citations, or empirical literature reviews, and do not invoke obscure theoretical frameworks that depend on specialized scholarly attribution. The argument should be self-contained and should treat the mechanism you describe as a conceptual possibility to be evaluated on its internal coherence and institutional plausibility.
