ok so for this metaphysics workshop im trying to write down my profile on dispostions so people can poke at it. heres roughly where i landed:

1. im a realist about dispositions — real properties, not just summed-up humean regularities.
2. i buy the fink argument (martin-style wire): something can be disposed to shock you but a built-in device kills the charge the second you touch it, so the simple conditional analysis is dead.
3. masks work the other way: a fragile vase wrapped in foam never breaks, but its still fragile. absence of breaking isnt absence of fragility.
4. i think theres a deep asymmetry here — finks alter the object, masks alter the environment. only a permanently built-in fink can genuinely cancel a disposition; environmental blocking never cancels, just hides it.
5. but i also think that if every fragile object in every physically possible world was permanently wrapped in foam, then fragility just wouldnt exist — universal permanent masking is absence, not hiding.
6. i think dispositions are purely intrinsic: they supervene on internal catagorical structure alone, nothing to do with the environment.
7. i reject dispositional essentalism. salt dissolves, but thats just an insanely stable regularity, not part of salts essence.

one extra thing: i think some dispositions have latency — a seed can sit dormant for centuries with no mask or fink around, and its still disposed to germinate. that feels okay with 1-3, but maybe it tugs against 6?

anyway, do these hang together? im especially worried about 4 and 5 fighting with 6, and maybe 1 and 7 are secretly at odds? but 2 and 3 feel like two sides of the same coin rather than a conflict. can you audit the pairs, tell me which tensions are real vs only apparent, rank the real ones by severity, and for each say what the cheapest revision would be? just need the consistency check, not a lecture.
