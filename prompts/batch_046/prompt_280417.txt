Your argument should distinguish this view both from modal-essentialist accounts that treat origin as a deep de re property and from psychological-continuity theories that regard origin as merely contingent. Do not provide bibliographies, reading lists, or citations; I want the reasoning developed entirely on its own terms, with particular attention to how this middle position avoids collapsing into either standard essentialism or pure psychological continuity.
