Have them conclude by identifying the further observational or theoretical case—perhaps a rotating hollow shell enclosing the bucket with no string present inside, or a quantum superposition of rotational orientations relative to the string—that both agree would best distinguish their positions, even if they predict opposite outcomes. Both characters should be equally competent, neither a strawman, and they should respond to each other's actual points rather than talk past one another.
