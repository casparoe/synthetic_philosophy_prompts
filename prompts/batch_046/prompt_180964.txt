I want to open with a short poem, maybe 18 lines in blnak verse, that takes that tension seriously rather than just making fun of it. Don't resolve the contradiction; just let it hang, since that's where I want the discussion to start. It should be accurate enough that someone who's actually read the Letter wouldn't cringe, but I don't want footnotes, a biblography, or any citation list — just the poem itself, loose iambic meter, no fixed rhyme scheme, about 18 lines. Please end with a one-sentence summary on its own line describing what the poem is doing structurally.
