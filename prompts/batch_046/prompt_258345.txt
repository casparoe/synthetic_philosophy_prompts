Write an essay on the debate between endurantism and perdurantism regarding how objects persist through time. Drawing on the intuition of maintaining long-running distributed systems—where a service is continuously patched, migrated across nodes, and replaced component-by-component without ever fully going offline—evaluate whether human personal identity over a lifetime is better understood as a single enduring entity that survives incremental updates, or as a sequence of distinct temporal stages linked by causal and psychological continuity. Weigh the central objections to each view, including the difficulty of accommodating change in intrinsic properties for endurantism and the difficulty of explaining rational concern for future stages for perdurantism, but reach and justify a definite conclusion rather than ending non-committally. Present the argument in accessible language suitable for a software engineer without formal metaphysics training, and do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations.
