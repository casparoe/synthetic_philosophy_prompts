Argue that the procreation asymmetry - the Benatar-style idea that we shouldn't create suffering people but have no duty to create happy ones - collapses if you apply Parfit's non-identity problem consistently. If no specific individual is wronged by never being born happy, then no specific individual is spared by never being born suffering, unless you secretly treat potental sufferers as modally actual and potential happy people as merely possible. Defend this hidden actualism as the real engine behind antinatalism, not just intuition. Make it an argument, not a summary. (Quick draft, sorry for typos.)
