Map the actors -- Nixon as ruler who'd lost the mandate, the cabinet as ministers, Kissinger maybe as elder advisor -- and then inject this weird idea I'm calling institutional filiality: loyalty to the office treated like loyalty to a father-figure, but the office itself is rotten. Don't need a perfect def, just run with it. What should each actor have done by standard role ethics? Where does institutional filiality illuminate the collapse versus just breaking the framework?
