me and my roommate got into it last night about safe Pareto improvements versus regular Pareto improvements in bargaining games. he thinks an SPI is basically just a risk-averse Pareto move — you check that nobody gets hurt regardless of what they beleive about others' strategies — but that it's not conceptually different, just Pareto with extra epistemic conditions. I think the "safe" part does real formal work: a standard Pareto comparison needs a fixed outcome profile, but an SPI compares strategy modifications across all belief states without requiring a joint distribution over them. so is this actually a distinct cooperative concept, or just Pareto improvements wearing a belt and suspenders? and if it is distinct, does the belief-independence condition carry any normative weight, or is it purely a robustness gadget?
