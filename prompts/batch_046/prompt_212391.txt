But I am stuck on this tension: if intentionality is truly diffuse, then there seems to be no determinate fact about what the colony wants at any moment, only statistical patterns that we narratively project onto parallel reflex arcs. How can you defend diffuse intentionality without collapsing into either anthropomorphism on one side or eliminativism about colony-level mental states on the other?
