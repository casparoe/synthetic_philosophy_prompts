(2) This spectator is formed through actual sympthy—imagining ourselves in the situation of real people who observe us.

(7) Therefore either the spectator isn't really built from sympathy with actual people—which destroys Smith's whole framework—or "impartiality" is a fiction and we're just siding with whoever we happen to sympathize with most.
(8) Either way, the theory collpases into rationalism or relativism, both of which Smith rejects.
