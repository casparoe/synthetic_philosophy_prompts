Rather than asking for an open-ended essay, I want you to rank the following five philosophical stances by how convincingly they address that specific interaction—assign each to a tier (S, A, B, C, or D) and provide a single sentence explaining why it lands there. I am not asking for popularity or historical influence; I am asking which actually resolves the causal-direction problem between simmballs and careenium.

The stances:

1. Strong syntactic computation: the formal relations among simmballs are themselves causal forces that restructure careenium dynamics from the top down.
2. Biological naturalism (Searle-style): careenium possesses intrinsic intentionality; simmballs are observer-relative descriptions and exert no independent causal pressure.
3. Implementation-functionalism: the careenium realizes the simmballs, but because the symbols are multiply realizable, they cannot be said to push this particular substrate around—only the substrate-level realization does the work.
4. Eliminative substrate monism: there are only careenium processes; simmballs are predictive fictions with no ontological or causal status.
5. Emergent symbolic constraint: at sufficient organizational density, simmballs form a genuinely novel causal layer that constrains lower-level careenium processes without being reducible to them, but they do not "push" in the same sense as physical forces.
