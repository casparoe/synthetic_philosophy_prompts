Career politicians avoid this because the same people stick around and remember why a bad idea crashed ten years ago, except they also internalize the wrong lessons and develop a governing aesthetic that prizes procedural smoothness over justice because smoothness keeps them employed. I also worry that sortition risks epistemic tourism, where citizens treat legislation like a brief vacation from real life rather than a duty.
