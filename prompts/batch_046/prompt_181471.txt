The parents, who were devout Catholics, refused consent, and the court eventually authorized the separation, framing it as an operation to save Jodie rather than as an act of killing Mary. That makes me wonder whether the doing/allowing distinction even applies here, because it seems to assume separate bodies with separate causal histories.
