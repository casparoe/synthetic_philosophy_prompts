Write a dialogue between Alex and Jordan, two philosophers arguing over coffee about the reference class problem. Jordan should push Alex on how causal specificity can choose between the hybrid cluster and pure ancestry without circular reasoning, and Alex should push Jordan on whether her view makes risk communication to doctors completely arbitrary. Make them actually respond — no strawmen, no talking past each other. Keep the tone casual; thier's no need for formal citations or anything.
