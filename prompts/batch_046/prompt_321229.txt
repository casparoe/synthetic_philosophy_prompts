I have been drafting an essay that tries to link Marx's distinction between formal and real subsumption—especially as it appears in the Grundrisse and in the manuscript fragments on the immediate process of production—to the contemporary extraction of cognitive and temporal surplus within platform-mediated labour. I go on to suggest that this amounts to a shift from formal to real subsumption not only of labour but of what one might call the attentional substrate, though I remain genuinely unsure whether this framing extends Marx's categories in a fruitful way or merely reclothes familiar critiques of surveillance capitalism in older terminology.
