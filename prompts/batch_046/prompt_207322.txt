Specifically, treat moral duties as attractor states that minimize a kind of social free energy, and supererogatory acts as high-cost trajectories that escape a local minimum without being driven by the gradient. Then report precisely what survives this translation and what does not. Does the normative force of 'ought' map onto the direction of steepest descent? Be frank about whether my vocabulary is missing something essential, or whether the philosophical distinction collapses into a description of outliers in a stochastic process. I am not asking for a bibliography or citations to particular philosophers; I want to know whether the conceptual structure holds up when stripped of its usual ethical framing, and whether the residue you identify is a defect of the physical analogy or a sign that the original claim was less substantive than it appeared.
