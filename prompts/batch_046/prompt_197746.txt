Since the careenium framework holds that cognitive symbols are exhaustively instantiated by configurations of simmballs and that the substrate possesses no causal autonomy of its own, when a symbolic revision—such as adopting a new inferential rule—corresponds to a restructuring of the underlying lattice, shouldn't we conclude that the symbols are literally pushing the substrate around?
