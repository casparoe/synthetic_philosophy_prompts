Plan the teaching sequence for me. Third, map the misconceptions the students will import—especially the reflex to treat the scenario as an evidential decision problem, to conflate precommitment with weakness of will, and to assume that forming an intent necessarily binds one to performing the act—and state exactly where in the arc each misconception should be headed off. Fourth, design a final check question or brief exercise that reveals whether they have grasped the decoupling of intent from action rather than simply finding the scenario disturbing or paradoxical.
