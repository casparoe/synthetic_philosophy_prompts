I am preparing materials for a graduate seminar on diachronic rationality and imperfect recall. I need a decision-tree taxonomy of philosophical responses to the absent-minded driver problem, constructed from the logical space rather than from a list of author names.

Please organize the tree around three binary branch points, each phrased as a concrete question a student could answer without knowing which philosophical position it corresponds to.

Branch one: Is the driver’s failure to distinguish the first exit from the second best understood as a purely epistemic limitation—he possesses but cannot access the relevant information—or as a structural feature of the decision problem, in which the nodes are genuinely identical in every property relevant to rational evaluation?

Branch two: Is rationality assessed locally at each decision node, given only the beliefs and values accessible there, or globally across the entire strategy, evaluated ex ante from the planning stage?

Branch three: Does the agent owe any deference to her past plan, and if so, is the source of that obligation instrumental (the plan was calculated to maximize expected utility), constitutive (abandoning it undermines diachronic agency), or absent?

For each of the eight resulting combinations, identify the position that occupies it—name it if it has a recognized label, otherwise mark it “unnamed”—and state its most awkward commitment in one sentence. Where a combination is unoccupied, briefly diagnose whether it is incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored.

In addition, include an overlay branch addressing a niche variant: suppose the driver knows that taking the first exit yields a payoff of 3, the second yields 4, and missing both yields 0, but also knows that if he takes the first exit he will engage in motivational rationalization—retroactively constructing reasons to see that choice as correct. How does the introduction of this “motivational forgetting” split the positions identified in the main tree? Map at least two leaves that shift and one that remains stable.

The instrument should contain no bibliography, reading list, or citation request. Phrase every node as a direct question. At the end, flag any pair of positions that the tree cannot cleanly separate and explain why they collapse onto the same answers.
