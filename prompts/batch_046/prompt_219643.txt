Please phrase each node as a concrete scenario: e.g., a neural network with two attention heads that never share state vs. one with a central bottleneck; the same code running on server A and B with identical internal states; a brain region replaced by silicon preserving dynamics. Don't ask "do you believe in functionalism?" — ask what the respondent thinks happens in the case. Keep it to about five or six branching questions, flag pairs of positions the tree can't distinguish, and note unoccupied branches.
