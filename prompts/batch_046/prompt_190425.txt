3. but pure instinctive compassion still counts—like a lifeguard jumping in without any mental label of duty, just reflex. that gets full worth; it doesnt need to be conceptual.

6. evolutionary debunking doesnt touch this at all. causal history is irrelevant; only present motivational architecture matters.

7. and i think worth and praiseworthiness split: worth tracks reason-structure, praiseworthiness tracks whether you're genuinely voluntary. so a compulsive savior might have worth but not be praiseworthy.
