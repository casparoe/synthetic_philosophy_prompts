With that framing in mind, I would like you to rank the following four analytical lenses for assessing whether a given practice counts as corrupt in contexts where formal state institutions are weak but social obligations remain dense—think of patron-client networks in parts of sub-Saharan Africa or South Asia. For each score, provide a brief justification, and then explain the overall ordering: which lens you place highest and why, which you consider least adequate for these contexts, and what specifically causes the middle two to diverge in their assessments of clientelism.
