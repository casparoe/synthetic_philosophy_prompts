Give me your credence—on a scale of 0 to 1—that the conceivability of philosophical zombies genuinely supports property dualism only if one already subscribes to a rationalist modal epistemology in which pure conceivability entails metaphysical possibility, and that this rationalist commitment is itself independent of physicalism. Decompose this into: (a) the probability that the zombie argument requires this rationalist modal premise rather than being neutral between rationalist and empiricist accounts; (b) the probability that a consistent physicalist can reject that premise without undermining her own modal commitments; (c) the probability that an empiricist modal epistemology alone can block the conceivability-to-possibility inference; then state your combined number. Deliver exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion: item 1 your overall credence; items 2–4 the probabilities for (a), (b), and (c) with brief justification; and item 5 which term dominates your result and what empirical or theoretical finding about modal cognition would most move your number.
