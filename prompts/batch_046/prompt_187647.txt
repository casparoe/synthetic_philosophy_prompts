Label each branch by the claim that divides it, not by a theorist's name. Each leaf should carry a position name or 'unnamed' if the combination is unoccupied, plus one sentence stating its most awkward commitment—such as having to maintain that the bridge is politically neutral if a shuttle service is eventually added. Flag any pair of leaves that the tree genuinely cannot separate, and diagnose empty regions in the logical space: are they incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored? Frame every node as a question about what the cases show, not as a citation of an author's thesis.
