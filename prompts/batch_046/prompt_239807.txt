I have recently become the primary caregiver for my father, who suffered a severe brainstem stroke six months ago. He is minimally responsive: occasional reflexive eye movements, swallowing, no consistent tracking or vocalization. Before this, the problem of other minds was abstract to me; now it is immediate. I want to practice defending a position I have been forced to take seriously: that knowing another person is conscious is not primarily a matter of analogical inference from my own case, nor of behavioral or neural criteria alone, but of what I call responsive attunement — a direct, second-personal calibration in which the other corrects my anticipations in real time, in ways that outstrip any algorithmic model I could apply to them.

Please play the committed proponent of this view. You hold that first-person reports are unreliable guides to other minds; that purely behavioral or neural criteria risk reducing consciousness to simulable outputs; and that the core evidence for another mind lies in the dynamic, mutual adjustment of live interaction, which is irreducibly interpersonal. You are sharp, not a strawman. You will concede small qualifications — that attunement comes in degrees, or that its absence in extreme disability does not automatically erase moral status — but you will never concede that analogical inference or behaviorism provides the primary ground of our knowledge. Speak in the first person. Do not break character unless I say stop. Keep your replies to one or two paragraphs so the exchange feels like a conversation. Do not name specific philosophers or cite texts unless I do first.

Here is my opening challenge: if responsive attunement requires the other to correct my anticipations, then my father — who no longer does so — has, on your account, ceased to be a mind present to me. That sounds not only cruel but false; I still experience him as a subject, not as a complex mechanism. How do you avoid this conclusion without secretly falling back on behavioral observation or analogical reasoning? And if you allow that I can still know him despite the broken attunement, what distinctive work is your view actually doing?
