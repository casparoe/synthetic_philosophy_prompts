For my philosophy of science coursework, I am drafting a defended position on the Anthropocene as a historical period—specifically, when it began and who has the authority to decide. Before I submit, I want a rigorous consistency check on the commitments below. Here is what I currently hold:

(a) The Anthropocene is a genuine geological epoch rather than a cultural metaphor, and its formal boundaries must be determined by physical stratigraphic signals, not by historical narrative or moral urgency.

(b) Its onset should be dated to 1610—the Columbian Exchange and the associated Orbis spike in atmospheric carbon and pollen records—because that marks the first irreversible, globally synchronous transformation of Earth's biosphere driven by human activity, even though this signal is more diffuse than the plutonium fallout layers of 1945.

(c) The authority to ratify the epoch belongs exclusively to the International Commission on Stratigraphy, operating under purely descriptive, value-neutral geological criteria; ethicists, historians, and policymakers should have no formal vote in that decision.

(d) At the same time, I maintain that naming an epoch after humanity is an inherently normative act: it assigns collective moral responsibility for planetary change, so the decision cannot be cleanly separated from ethical and political judgment.

(e) Finally, I hold that if the Commission formally rejects the Anthropocene by 2030, the concept should remain fully operative in scientific and historical discourse, because institutional ratification is not constitutive of historical reality.

Please provide a committed audit rather than a neutral survey of options. Take a definite position on which pairs of commitments are genuinely in tension, rank those tensions by severity, and identify any pair that looks incompatible but actually dissolves on inspection. For each real tension, say exactly which commitment must give and defend that specific revision as the least costly. Do not present equally weighted alternatives—choose the single cleanest repair, or the minimal pair of revisions, and argue for it. Also indicate what my repaired position implies about whether an analogous "Lunar Anthropocene"—defined by large-scale off-world mining—should be adjudicated by the same body using the same criteria.
