Third, a list of cruxes: specific sub-questions such that resolving one in either direction would shift the position of at least one party. In particular, include at least one crux that asks whether such divergence undermines the authority of the aggregated testimony or instead exposes a failure in the experts' unreflective judgments.

The response should not include requests for bibliographies, reading lists, author-specific page citations, or verbatim quotations from specific texts. Keep all formulations structurally precise, but characterize the positions without relying on attributions to particular named philosophers unless both sides would jointly accept the attribution.
