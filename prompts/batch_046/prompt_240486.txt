I need six to eight interview questions that press the actual mechanics of this position, avoiding generic biography. The remaining questions should probe where the literature typically presses: the comparison with Kuhnian incommensurability, the decision-theoretic implications for long-term life-planning, and whether her framework allows any rational basis for choosing to undergo a transformation in the first place.
