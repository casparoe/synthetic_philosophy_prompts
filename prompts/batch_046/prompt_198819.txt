I want to stage a structured in-room simulation—using roles, physical positions, and small real stakes—that makes the group feel this tension in their bodies before anyone explains it, so they discover for themselves that achieving closer emotional alignment with someone in distress actually undermines their ability to offer the kind of detached guidance the spectator provides.
