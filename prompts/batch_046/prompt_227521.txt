I'm preparing a proposal for a one-semester independent study (around 6,000 words) on a tension I think the modern Stoic revival has not resolved. The popular version of Stoicism—found in recent self-help literature and in much contemporary philosophical popularization—treats Epictetus's dichotomy of control as a strict binary: either something depends entirely on my own judgments and volitions, in which case it is within my power and a proper object of concern, or it depends on external conditions, other agents, or chance, in which case it is outside my power and should be treated with indifference. Ancient Stoic texts, however, are less tidy. Epictetus and Marcus Aurelius discuss health, reputation, and social roles as 'preferred indifferents': not goods in themselves, yet not mere distractions either, but objects of rational pursuit pursued 'with reservation.' The modern revival tends to absorb these into the internal sphere by saying only my judgment about health matters, or else to dismiss them as outside concern altogether.

My idea is to ask whether there is a recoverable middle category—things over which I exercise 'probabilistic influence without determination'—and whether the binary framing is therefore a substantive philosophical error rather than just a pedagogical simplification. To make this concrete, I want to apply the framework to what I will call 'attentional infrastructure': the algorithms, platform architectures, and information feeds that shape what I encounter daily. I choose which platforms to use and can adjust some settings, but the recommendation logic is opaque and operates at a scale that makes individual outcomes largely unpredictable. Is this inside my control, outside it, or in a middle zone? And does placing it in the middle change the ethical or therapeutic prescriptions—specifically, does it create obligations to curate one's informational environment that the modern binary either wrongly ignores (by treating the environment as fully external) or wrongly exaggerates (by treating it as fully within my power)?
