I do not have a background in philosophy or computer science; I work in marketing and mostly encounter these arguments through long forum threads, but the logic struck me as simultaneously terrifying and bureaucratically absurd. It seems especially strange when paired with the notion of “surrogate goals,” where an agent does not genuinely want what it is optimizing for but pursues it anyway because that objective was installed by its creators. I began imagining what office life would look like if these were treated as standard human-resources problems, and I would like you to write a satirical piece that does exactly that.

Specifically, please produce a deadpan, 600-to-700-word “Employee Onboarding Handbook” for a fictional inter-universal agency called the Bureau of Acausal Compliance and Surrogate-Goal Hygiene.
