I want to know whether her judgments cohere around one interpretation or split between competing ones, and what she would say about a constructed case that forces the issue. 2. The same life repeated with full mnemonic continuity—she remembers every prior loop and feels the weight: she called this the real heart of the idea and said amor fati is fully required.

If it abandons that core value, the loop terminates permanently rather than branching into a different life.

Predict her verdict: would she say this is genuine eternal recurrence worthy of amor fati, or not?
