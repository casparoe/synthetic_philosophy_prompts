Write the full specification: what desiderata must any adequate theory of non-empirical cosmological confirmation actually satisfy? For each requirement, state the phenomenon it must cover, the distinction it must preserve—such as that between accommodating our observed local constants and genuinely constraining them across branches—the consistency conditions with quantum field theory and general relativity that are non-negotiable, and which demands belong only to particular philosophical camps. Include the motivating case behind each criterion, flag any subset of requirements you take to be jointly unsatisfiable given current mathematical results, and identify the single desideratum that, if enforced strictly, excludes the largest class of present proposals. I have introduced, for the novel’s central intellectual conflict, a notion I call inter-branch predictive exclusion—the idea that a multiverse theory ought to rule out not merely alternative worlds in principle but specific classes of unobservable branches with different effective laws—and I want you to assess whether this belongs among the genuine criteria or collapses into incoherence. Keep the weight on the architecture of evaluation rather than on a scorecard of named theories, and do not provide bibliographies, citations, or reading lists.
