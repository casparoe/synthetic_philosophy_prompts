A couple is negotiating a prenuptial framework that permits both partners to develop deep emotional attachments outside the marriage, provided all such attachments are recorded in a shared digital ledger with real-time disclosure and provided they involve no shared finances. One partner contends that this eliminates the possessive jealousy that Russell associated with proprietary marriage norms; the other responds that translating intimacy into a transparent ledger destroys precisely the spontaneity Russell regarded as essential to authentic affection. Advise them, in concrete terms, on whether their agreement resolves or merely displaces the tension Russell identified, and identify the specific clause in their arrangement that either vindicates or undermines Russell's view that marriage should function as a cooperative bond between free individuals rather than a system of mutual control.
