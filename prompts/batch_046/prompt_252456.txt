I've been working through some case studies in archaeological interpretation with a study group—mostly Bronze Age administrative sites in the Near East—and we've kept running into a recurring argumentative structure that none of us can name precisely. The pattern starts with an assemblage that strongly suggests a particular institution: standardized weights, sealed containers, and spatial clustering that look like a centralized redistribution system. Then an anomaly appears—say, decorative iconography on the seals that has no administrative function, or weights that vary just enough to break standardization. Rather than treating this as evidence that the institution was weaker or absent, the interpreter expands the theory's scope: the system wasn't purely economic but was embedded in ritual or social hierarchy, so the decorative elements are part of the administrative logic, and the weight variation expresses rank rather than measurement error. Each adjustment is defensible on its own, but together they create a framework in which almost any configuration of objects can be accommodated. Even if the same site yielded no weights at all but only ritual objects arranged in a grid, the framework might be preserved by claiming that redistribution had become so internalized that its material traces disappeared.

What I'm trying to identify is not the general idea of embedded economies, which I understand is well established, but the specific methodological pattern: a core institutional claim that survives disconfirming material evidence not by being revised downward but by continuously annexing adjacent explanatory domains—symbolism, status, cosmology—without ever specifying in advance what archaeological signature would actually falsify the institution's presence. Is there an established term in archaeological theory or philosophy of science for this particular form of theoretical defense? How should it be distinguished from an ordinary auxiliary hypothesis, from what philosophers call an immunizing strategy, and from simple interpretive generosity? If no precise concept exists, I'd like to understand how to draw its boundaries: at what point does legitimate contextual expansion become this specific failure mode, and what would a genuinely disconfirming assemblage look like for someone operating within it?
