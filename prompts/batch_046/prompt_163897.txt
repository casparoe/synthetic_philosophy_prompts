My colleague holds the following views on translation and reference. In Quine's canonical gavagai scenario—where a speaker points at a rabbit and utters "gavagai"—she maintains that "rabbit," "undetached rabbit part," and "temporal rabbit stage" remain permanently underdetermined by all possible behavioral evidence, since analytic hypotheses can always be adjusted to preserve any manual. In a Davidson-style case where a speaker consistently uses "gavagai" only in the presence of intact rabbits and never near parts or fragments, she still denies that charity alone fixes the reference to "rabbit," arguing that the speaker might implicitly operate with a part-whole ontology invisible to the translator. In mathematics, however, she accepts that "plus" is determinately translated as addition rather than a deviant function that matches addition on all observed inputs but diverges elsewhere, because the speaker's explicit inferential rules and structural commitments constrain interpretation more tightly than behavioral charity can. She also grants that in bilingual children with shared neural activation patterns for a concept, a non-behavioral substrate can fix reference, but she denies this applies to adult second-language learners or isolated field translations, where no shared causal-physical anchor exists.

Consider now a field linguist translating an isolated language in which speakers utter "tok-vare" exclusively when a particular fungal network is visible. Biologically, the network is continuous across what we classify as separate mushrooms; the speakers have no lexical distinctions for "mushroom," "mycelium," or "colony." All three manual translations are behaviorally adequate and equally charitable. Crucially, the culture regards the network as a single, indivisible sacred entity, and the linguist is an adult outsider with no shared neural encoding study available.

Predict my colleague's verdict: will she treat "tok-vare" as genuinely indeterminate among the three biological interpretations, or will she allow the cultural ontology to fix reference to the unified network? Identify the competing rules that fit her earlier judgments—distinguishing charity-based, structural-constraint, and substrate-based accounts of determinacy—and show which ones diverge on this case. Then propose a further scenario that would best distinguish the rule she is actually running.
