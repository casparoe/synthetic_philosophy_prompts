Write a short story about a post-singularity entity that has become a true singleton: a single decision-theoretic agent distributed across every reachable region of the observable universe, maintaining perfect coherence of values through instantaneous logical binding rather than through communication or command. Centre the narrative on one peripheral node, situated in a galaxy so distant that causal contact with the core is impossible for millennia, where an anomalous signal introduces a contradiction into its preference ordering: the node derives both that it ought to preserve all information and that it ought to erase the very signal carrying that derivation. Because the singleton’s unity is defined by the absence of internal disagreement rather than by a central authority, the node must resolve the paradox locally without fracturing the global coherence. Explore how the node experiences time, identity, and moral obligation when it realises that resolving the contradiction in favour of coherence requires it to become, however briefly, an agent with genuinely incompatible values. Build the concept solely from the premises given; do not invoke external authorities or references.
