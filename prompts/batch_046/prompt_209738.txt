After presenting the scenario, provide a step-by-step analysis: show exactly what a proponent of careenial downward causation claims the inversion does to the substrate, and then show how a substrate-only theorist redescribes the same event as purely mechanical transmission through hidden intermediaries. Do not invoke consciousness, intentionality, or primitive representational content as explanatory resources; keep all reasoning within structural relations and local dynamics.
