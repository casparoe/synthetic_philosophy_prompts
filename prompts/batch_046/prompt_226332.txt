Write a short story about the boundary problem — who belongs to the demos? Set it on a floating station called K-9 where residents are split between original colonists, their adopted synthetic twins (who each hold half a vote), and climate refugees who can stay only if they contribute labor. In exactly five scenes, each no more than two sentences and with no introduction or conclusion, show the council redrawing voter rolls after one twin demands full citizenship and the refugees ask why work doesn't count. Don't explain the philosophy; just show who gets counted and who is left out. Write it casual, like you're telling it over coffee, and don't worry if you misspell recieve.
