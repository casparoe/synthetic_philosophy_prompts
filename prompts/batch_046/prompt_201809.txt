The larger gaps don't reveal nature; they reveal that when survival pressure is removed, culturally available gender scripts become stronger sorting mechanisms for self-concept.

Maya came back:
(3) But this "identity expression" account still needs to explain why the expressive divergence tracks sex so consistently across different cultural contexts. (4) The consistency actually supports the cultural account: global media and institutional norms export similar gendered symbolic meanings for technical versus nurturant work. What's variable isn't the script but the economic freedom to follow it; in less egalitarian societies, women enter STEM because family survival demands it, suppressing the script.

(5) Even granting that, the scripts themselves might be calibrated to evolved sex-differentiated interests. The welfare state doesn't invent gendered preferences; it removes the noise that masks them.

(6) That risks circular reasoning—interpreting any post-liberation divergence as proof of pre-existing nature. Without an independent measure of "noise-free" preference, the biological claim becomes unfalsifiable.
