Two colleagues presented opposing views at a recent colloquium on philosophy of mind, using a hypothetical architecture they call the careenium—a system in which symbolic representations are physically instantiated in arrays of substrate-level units they term simmballs. The central question is whether symbols in such a system genuinely push the substrate around, or whether every causal transaction is substrate-to-substrate with symbols serving only as descriptive glosses.

Dr. Elara Voss argued as follows. (1) In a functioning careenium, a tokenized symbol such as PREDATOR activates a behavioral cascade not because of the bare physical properties of the underlying simmballs, but because of the symbol's semantic content; the symbol exerts downward causal influence, directing the substrate into configurations it would not otherwise enter. (2) This is confirmed by interpretation-shifting: if one preserves the exact same simmball state-pattern but re-maps its symbolic interpretation to PREY, the subsequent behavioral cascade reverses, even though the substrate dynamics are initially identical; since the physical substrate hasn't changed but the causal trajectory has, the symbol—not the substrate pattern—must be doing the explanatory and causal work. (3) Moreover, in an autonomous careenium with internal feedback loops, symbolic outputs are re-instantiated as substrate inputs without any external designer intervening; these loops are specifically engineered to respond to symbolic content rather than to raw physical form, which constitutes genuine downward causation internal to the architecture.

Dr. Marcus Chen replied move for move. (4) Calling the pattern PREDATOR is an observer-dependent interpretation; the simmballs interact purely mechanically, and all causal efficacy resides in those physical interactions, with the symbol functioning as nothing more than our theoretical label for a particular voltage or state distribution. (5) The reversal observed in interpretation-shifting occurs only because an external agent has physically rewired the connections between simmballs to match the new mapping; the causal work is still performed entirely by substrate-to-substrate transitions, and attributing power to the symbol confuses our descriptive practice with causal reality. (6) Even the internal feedback loops are, upon analysis, nothing but further simmball-to-simmball interactions; describing them as symbol-responsive is a functional characterization, not an identification of a novel causal pathway, and until one can identify a physical interaction mediated by meaning rather than by mechanism, downward causation remains a redescription of the substrate dynamics.

Then indicate, if anything, what both parties are missing—particularly whether there is a principled way to distinguish genuine symbol-driven causation from mere substrate dynamics in a careenium, or whether the dispute collapses into a disagreement about explanatory levels rather than about causation itself.
