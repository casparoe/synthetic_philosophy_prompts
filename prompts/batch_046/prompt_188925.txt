It should ask the right questions sequentially, say what counts as a sufficient answer to each, and clearly flag where the procedure stops with a hard no, where it hands the decision to a person because it's a values call rather than a fact, and where Pascal's own framework just breaks down and we're flying blind. Don't just summarize Pascal; ground it in his principle but operationalize it for people who haven't read the Pensées. I also want it honest about which steps are judgment calls dressed up as procedure. Apply it to three cases: shunning a user for using the wrong pronoun in a belief report, a doxxing-ban that our tech stack can't actually enforce, and the majority voting to cull a minor contributor to prevent the forum's quality from starving.
