Track one treats documentation of historical atrocities—witness testimonies, administrative records, physical evidence—as subject to perpetual preservation with unrestricted future access. Track two treats commemorative forms—named public buildings, annual state ceremonies, monumental sculptures—as subject to "generational sunset," engineered so that prominence diminishes after roughly seventy-five to one hundred years unless actively renewed by affected communities. The same institution that permanently freezes the evidentiary record thus designs the gradual fading of public remembrance.
