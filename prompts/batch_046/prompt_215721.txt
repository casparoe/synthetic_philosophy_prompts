Write a two-page proposal for a three-month independent research project on Dewey’s experimentalism about democracy and inquiry. Frame the precise question as follows: Dewey treats democratic politics as social inquiry in which policies function as hypotheses tested by consequences, yet he runs together two roles for experimentation—propositional testing of explicit policy claims and the cultivation of dispositional habits of cooperative intelligence. Ask whether democratic experimentalism requires both tracks, or whether eliminating one collapses Dewey’s justification for democracy as a mode of life rather than a mere decision procedure. Explain why standard readings (pragmatist policy-wonk versus communitarian moralist) leave this structural ambiguity untouched. Describe the method: close conceptual analysis separating propositional from dispositional feedback loops, constructing a minimal model of each, and testing whether either alone can reproduce Dewey’s normative conclusions. State what success looks like (a clear division of labor with democracy requiring both) and what failure looks like (the distinction collapses, leaving the view redundant or unmotivated). Note that three months permits conceptual reconstruction but not a full historical study of influences, and do not include a bibliography or citation list—refer to Dewey’s positions in general terms and indicate that references will be added later. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence, then develop the reasoning with no preamble and no restating of the question.
