I want to close our final session with a villanelle that takes this tension seriously rather than resolving it. Write a nineteen-line villanelle in which one refrain asserts that the simmballs drive the careenium, and the other refrain asserts that the careenium drives the simmballs and the symbols are carried along. The two refrains should take turns according to the form, and the final couplet should bring them together in a way that structurally illustrates why the two positions keep passing each other—perhaps by placing them side by side without synthesis. Make the vocabulary precise enough that a participant in our seminar can identify which refrain belongs to which causal story, and ensure the poem remains open-ended rather than sermonizing about intervention or reduction. Please avoid bibliographic references or citations; the poem should stand on its conceptual architecture alone.
