so here's where i've landed, and i need a consistency check:

(i) i'm a non-reductive physicalist — consciousness is about integration patterns, not neurons specifically, so substrate shouldnt matter in principle.
(ii) i think the Einstein brain in the vat is still conscious because its internal dynamics and self-reference stay intact without a body.
(iii) but i also think a physical book — ink on paper — can never be conscious, not because its paper, but because it lacks temporal continuity; each page is statically existant, not flowing into awareness of the next.
