Mara: The simmballs are just descriptive tags. The substrate shifts because of local physical forces—electromagnetic gradients, mechanical stress, thermal diffusion. Mara: You are describing a complex physical mechanism and then relabeling it. The sensor is triggered by a particular physical arrangement—say, a specific molecular configuration.
