Here's the root scenario: there's a naval drill scheduled for tomorrow, but a pressure system might suddenly form over the coast and the admiral might cancel; right now the meteorologists genuinely can't predict which path the storm takes. First node: does the student think there's already a fact of the matter, right now, about whether the drill happens? Is there a coherent view where human choices stay open but weather is already determined? Say if it's incoherent, unexplored, or just ugly.
