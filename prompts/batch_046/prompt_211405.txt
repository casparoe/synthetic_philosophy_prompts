Draft the tribunal's formal opinion recommending one course, including a specific implementation timeline. The reasoning must engage the strongest consideration on the losing side—in particular, whether adopting an intermediate category respects indigenous epistemic authority over ancestral identification, or whether it improperly extends ontological commitment beyond what the inaccessible evidence can support. The opinion should distinguish clearly between failing to identify an instance of a kind and the kind itself lacking application conditions in this context.
