My central claim is that a white lie is wrong not because it is literally false, but only when layer 3 fails—when the falsehood is not recoverable and therefore substitutes the speaker’s judgment for the listener’s epistemic autonomy. I am especially unsure whether my argument smuggles in an unrealistic assumption that interlocutors always prefer the unvarnished truth.
