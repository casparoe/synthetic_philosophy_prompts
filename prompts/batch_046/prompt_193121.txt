I'm giving a seminar defense—effectively job-talk-level—of the view that for macro-institutional causation in technology policy, counterfactual dependence analysis should be preferred to interventionist structural-causal models, not merely for epistemic reasons but because institutional variables fail the modularity conditions required for well-defined do-operators. Instead, we should analyze the closest possible worlds in which the treaty failed or succeeded, tracking counterfactual dependence of risk outcomes on institutional configurations, treating causation here as a modal relation between worlds rather than a relation between variables within a fixed model.
