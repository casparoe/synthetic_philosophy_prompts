I want to spend one session on whether art can be genuinely good if the person who made it was awful, but i don't want the usual "seperate the work from the artist" twitter take. I want them to sit in the actual conceptual knot: you can be deeply moved by a piece and still know its source is rotten, without one feeling cancelling out the other.

Plan the teaching arc for about forty-five minutes. What has to come first — maybe the distinction between aesthetic value and moral endorsement? — and what depends on that?
