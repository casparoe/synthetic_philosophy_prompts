I'm putting together a sorting quiz for a philosphy of well-being seminar — one of those "which position do you actually hold" things — but I don't want it to just ask "are you a hedonist?" I want concrete machine-variants to do the sorting, so the questions should be cases people can answer without knowing the theory names.

Please write a 7-question decision-tree quiz. Flag any pair of leaves these cases can't really seperate, and briefly diagnose why — is the distinction just unexplored here, or does it collapse on these cases?

Keep it readable for students who haven't read Nozick in detail. Don't include a bibliography, reading list, or citation requests — I need this to work for people without web access. And please add a short meta-note at the end: what kind of argument or new machine-variant would make you restructure the tree?
