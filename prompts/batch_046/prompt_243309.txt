Compare the skeptical challenge posed by Zhuangzi's butterfly dream with that of Cartesian dream skepticism, concentrating not on sensory deception in general but on whether Zhuangzi introduces a distinct threat to first-person knowledge of personal identity. Specifically, examine whether the mutual ignorance between Zhuangzi and the butterfly—neither being able to determine which is the dreamer—undermines the stability of the self in a way that Cartesian skepticism, which retains a continuous thinking subject, does not. Then indicate which of these two arguments you regard as the more serious obstacle to ordinary self-knowledge, and justify your assessment in a sentence or two.
