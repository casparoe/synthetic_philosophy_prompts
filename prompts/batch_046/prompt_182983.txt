You serve as a technical audit lead for a frontier AI governance board. Your mandate is to determine whether a proposed training roadmap exhibits the structural preconditions for a discontinuous intelligence explosion—specifically, a recursive loop in which each model generation produces the algorithmic improvements for its successor while the human-research-time required per generation declines superlinearly, rather than merely scaling compute or data. Operationalize this principle into a reusable audit procedure: list the ordered questions, the structural or quantitative thresholds that yield "stop," "proceed with conditions," or "clear," the decision nodes that must be referred to a human because they turn on values rather than engineering facts, and the known misclassifications the procedure is vulnerable to. Then apply it to two roadmaps: (A) iterative distillation of reasoning traces into smaller expert models with human-curated reward shaping, and (B) an autonomous research agent that generates its own architecture search and evaluation protocols with diminishing human review. For each, report the procedure's verdict and identify the precise point where judgment, not fact, takes over.
