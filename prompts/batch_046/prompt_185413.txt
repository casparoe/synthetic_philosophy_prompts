- stable scientific regularities: it has to explain why laws are so reliable without treating them as metaphysically necessary connections in nature, since al-Ghazali specifically denied necessary connection. what's the replacement story?
- mental vs physical causation: my deciding to raise my arm feels genuinely causal, but fire burning cotton doesn't. occasionalism usually treats both the same. it needs to distinguish them without sneaking in natural causal powers.
- quantum mechanics: entanglement correlations happen without local mechanisms. this should be a point in occasionalism's favor, not just a sloppy "see, physics proves god does it" move.

- practical success: medicine, engineering, etc. have to work. the theory can't make them impossible in principle, but has to explain why they succeed differently than we think.
- counterfactuals: if the fire hadn't been near, the cotton wouldn't have burned needs an explanation that doesn't appeal to causal powers in the fire.
- falsifiability: god usually creates burning near fire can't be the whole theory, or its just tautology.
