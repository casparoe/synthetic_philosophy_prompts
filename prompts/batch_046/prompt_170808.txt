I understand the basic picture: present an image to the left visual field and the right hemisphere can guide the left hand to a matching object, while the left hemisphere, which controls speech, reports seeing nothing. But I want to classify the actual positions people can hold about what this means for consciousness, including some distinctions that don't usually appear in popular summaries.
