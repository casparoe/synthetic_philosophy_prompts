State your credence, on a scale from 0 to 1, that inference to the best explanation is rationally obligatory in scientific practice only under an exclusion condition—where every rival account has been demonstrated to be conceptually incoherent—rather than under the standard superiority condition, where one account is merely the best among coherent competitors. Decompose your reasoning into three terms: (a) the prior probability that IBE’s normative authority genuinely requires exclusion rather than comparative superiority; (b) the probability that there exists at least one canonical episode of scientific ontology-adoption, excluding quantum mechanics and evolutionary biology, in which a rival explanation remains conceptually coherent yet is rationally rejected; and (c) the conditional probability that, if such episodes exist, they undermine IBE altogether rather than merely narrowing its scope. For each term, provide a specific number and a one-line justification, specify whether you treat them as independent, and combine them into a final credence. Then identify the term to which your overall number is most sensitive, and describe what conceptual argument or distinction—without appealing to particular historical case studies—would be required to shift that term by at least 0.15.
