have them circle around one precise crux: does "autonomy" of the supersturcture mean it has causal efficacy that cant be reduced to contradictions in the base, or is reducibility itself enough for the materialist claim? make sure both sides are sharp, neither just concedes, and keep it grounded in the hypothetical case rather than drifting into generic philosophy of history.
