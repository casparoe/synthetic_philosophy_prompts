As a journalist covering the science and philosophy of consciousness, I am commissioning a short verse piece for a feature on Daniel Dennett’s heterophenomenological method—the approach that treats first-person reports not as transparent windows onto inner experience but as texts describing a reconstructed “heterophenomenological world.” I want the poem to perform that interpretive move structurally, and I want it aimed at an audience that includes both specialists and general readers.

Write a villanelle—nineteen lines, ABA rhyme scheme, with two refrains that alternate and finally converge in the closing quatrain—voiced by a researcher applying heterophenomenology to the verbal self-reports of an artificial language system. One refrain should be a first-person utterance attributed to the system, along the lines of When I hesitate, there is a kind of pressure; the other should be the researcher’s methodological stance, along the lines of Treat the text as fiction, not as window. The repetition should enact the method’s core tension: honoring the report as serious data while refusing to take it as literal access to an inner state. In the final two lines, let the refrains meet without resolving their disagreement, so that the form itself holds interpretation and suspension of belief together.

Make the philosophical rendering precise enough that a philosopher of mind familiar with Dennett’s account recognizes the distinction between constructing a heterophenomenological world and making an ontological claim about consciousness, yet accessible enough that a non-specialist feels the interpretive dilemma rather than merely encountering jargon. After the poem, provide a brief note that separates what is broadly settled about the method—its bracketing of direct-access claims in favor of textual interpretation—from what is genuinely contested, such as whether extending heterophenomenology to artificial reports preserves its original epistemic rationale, or whether the method implicitly carries ontological commitments about consciousness that it officially avoids. Do not present any contested interpretation—for instance, that heterophenomenology dissolves the hard problem, or that AI self-reports are straightforwardly equivalent to human ones—as the consensus view. Also indicate where the poem takes liberties with the method or relies on a particular, disputed application of it.
