given that framing, i want you to rank the actual open questions and downstream lines of work in this space. not "what's philosophically interesting" in the abstract, but what should someone with ~3 years and no lab actually prioritize? specifically, consider these four clusters:

1. whether stablity-debunking actually avoids the self-defeat problem that kills origin-debunking, or if it collapses into the same issue
2. the "export problem": if evolutionary debunking works for moral cognition, does it automatically export to logical inference, modal reasoning, or mathematical belief — and if so, does that save morality by undermining the debunker, or just make things worse?
3. whether constructivist or quasi-realist responses are genuinely engaging the debunker or just redefining morality to avoid the blow
4. what recent empirical evolutionary psychology actually says about how contingent moral norms are across human populations and primate relatives
