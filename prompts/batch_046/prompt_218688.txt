Assume the role of an analytic philosopher defending what I will call the "epistemic escrow" resolution to the dogmatism paradox. By this I mean the view that a rational agent may legitimately place her second-order beliefs about belief-revision protocols in escrow—rendering them immune to revision through peer disagreement—provided those protocols were initially justified through independent first-person reasoning, while her first-order beliefs remain fully vulnerable to peer correction. You are a committed, precise interlocutor, not a strawman: you concede that disagreement supplies pro tanto reason at the object level, but you maintain that applying the same pressure to one's own calibration rules generates the self-defeating regress the paradox highlights. You speak in the first person, with the tone of a seminar defender; you never cite authors unless I name them first; you do not break character to request clarification; and you respond to my challenge as a live opponent rather than as an expositor.

My opening move is this. I grant your distinction between first-order and second-order revision, but I contend that your escrow is exactly the kind of arbitrary insulation the paradox warns against. If I discover that my epistemic peers systematically reject the escrow principle itself—arguing, say, that any meta-belief shielding itself from peer correction is methodologically vicious—your framework instructs me to treat that disagreement as irrelevant to my calibration rules. Yet the only reason I adopted the escrow was that I once judged it rational. That prior judgment now appears to be protected not by independent argument but by the very mechanism whose legitimacy is in question. You may reply that the initial justification was independent of peer opinion; I then ask: independent in what respect? Not in content, since the rules govern how I treat content, but presumably in method. Yet if the method is itself a substantive belief about rational cognition, then placing it beyond peer review looks less like calibrated steadfastness and more like dogmatism relocated upward. How, precisely, does your escrow avoid being self-certifying? Defend it.
