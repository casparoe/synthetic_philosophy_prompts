I'm a retired ecologist who started reading philosophy of history after retirement, mostly because I got tired of nature documentaries. Lately I've been trying to understand that big argument over WWI — the Fischer controversy, where he said Germany basically entered the war deliberately with plans for domination. But what puzzles me isn't the history itself; it's the way people talk about holding 'Germany' responsable once you move past the government.

Here's the patern I keep seeing. Someone will claim Germany bears moral blame just like an individual does — they intended conquest, they could have chosen otherwise, so they deserve condemnation. But then you ask who exactly 'Germany' is — the Kaiser, the Reichstag, the voting public? — and they switch to talking about structual forces, nationalist culture, the railway timetable making conflict inevitable. At that point 'blame' sounds like a physicist saying a rock blames gravity for falling. Yet the second you point out that if it's just causal determinism there's no moral agent left, they flip back to intentional-agent language and accuse you of excusing the perpetrators. I've noticed the same occurance in debates about the Armenian genocide and Nanjing — people want the moral weight of collective guilt, but defend it with historical determinism whenever the agent-identity gets questioned.

Is there an established term for this exact back-and-forth? I don't mean collective responsibility in general, and it doesn't feel like plain ambiguity or the motte-and-bailey move, since the two versions aren't a defensible core plus a wild extension — they're two incompatible frameworks that happen to share the word 'blame'. If there's no specific concept, how should I carve it? Specifically, is the oscillation doing hidden work — letting people keep the emotional force of moral condemnation while borrowing the explanatory cover of determinism whenever someone asks 'who, exactly, are we condemning?' — and does that mean the disagreement about national blame is partly a disagreement about which model of agency applies?
