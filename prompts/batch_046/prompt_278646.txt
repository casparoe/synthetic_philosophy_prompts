i work on the interpretation board for a mid-size holocaust museum and every quarter we get pitched temporary exhibits that draw a comparison between the holocaust and something else -- other genocides, modern buerocratic systems, tech stuff. we need a reusable decision proceedure our committee can actually run in like 30 min, not some vague consider-context memo. ground it in the uniqueness vs comparability debate (the Historikerstreit stuff) and Bauman's point about modern rationality being part of the machinery, not an aberration. i want ordered questions, what counts as passing each one, where the flowchart stops with a definite no, and where it just dumps the call onto human judgment because values are clashing. run it on three proposals: a comparison with the Rwandan genocide; a comparison with algorithmic credit-scoring in welfare bureaucracies; and a comparison with the Herero and Nama genocide in german southwest africa. make sure one comes out in a way that feels wrong or at least uncomfortable. also be honest about what the procedure cant do -- like maybe it cant settle whether emotional impact should override historical precision. write it like a working checklist, not an essay.
