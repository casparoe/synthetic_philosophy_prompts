Say what success looks like—perhaps a parameterized matrix showing conditions under which beef exceeds chicken on wild-harm metrics—and what failure looks like, e.g., the distinctions collapse back into aggregate counting or require ecological modeling beyond philosophical scope. Keep the scope honest: conceptual framing only, no bibliography, no original field data.
