I am writing a chapter for a research programme in metaphysical aesthetics and keep encountering a particular analogy in both popular neuroscience and applied music therapy: that music operates as a kind of direct emotional telemetry system, bypassing conceptual cognition to transmit affective states from composer or sound source to listener with the immediacy of a reflex arc. Proponents use this to justify the conclusion that music is essentially a technology of emotional remote control, and that its philosophical significance lies precisely in this unmediated efficacy—something like a wire carrying current rather than a symbol requiring interpretation.

I would like you to audit this analogy against Schopenhauer's account of music, in which music is unique among the arts in being the direct objectification of the Will rather than a representation of Ideas. Specifically, please provide an explicit correspondence table mapping the elements of the telemetry model—the transmitter, the signal, the receiver, the bypass of cognition, the emotional payload—onto Schopenhauer's framework: the Will, melody, harmony, rhythm, and the listener's metaphysical position. Then analyse the disanalogies: where does the pipeline picture smuggle in a representational or causal-transmission structure that Schopenhauer denies? How do Schopenhauer's specific structural assignments—melody as the history of the Will's self-knowledge, harmony as the grades of objectification running from inorganic nature up to human consciousness, and the bass register as the lowest grades of the Will's objectification—fit or fail to fit the telemetry model?

Finally, evaluate whether the conclusion that music is valuable primarily as an instrument of emotional remote control survives once these disanalogies are made explicit, or whether Schopenhauer's own valuation—music as temporary liberation from the Will's striving—depends on precisely those features that the analogy suppresses. If the analogy collapses, suggest whether a different analogy, perhaps architectural or mathematical, better captures Schopenhauer's actual position.

Please write your response in American English.
