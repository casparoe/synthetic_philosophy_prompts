The summary further claims that Singer treats emotional attachment and cultural tradition as mere impediments to this rational expansion, that he regards progress as linear and irreversible once rational insight is achieved, and—more specifically—that he replaces the traditional binary boundary of moral membership with a scalar gradient of moral status tied to cognitive complexity, so that obligations diminish proportionally as one moves toward beings with lower cognitive capacity. I want to present this summary to my students as an exercise in careful exegesis, but before I do so I need to be certain about where the distortions lie. Could you please audit this characterization? Please present your analysis clearly, in American English, and without reference to external bibliographies or reading lists.
