I am teaching a graduate seminar on metaphysics of modality, and I have noticed that students often treat Lewisian counterpart theory and the Kripkean doctrine of transworld identity as interchangeable labels rather than as genuinely competing accounts of what it means for a person or object to exist in more than one possible world. To help them feel the practical friction between these views, I would like you to draft a deadpan parody of an internal human-resources onboarding manual—approximately 650 words—for a fictional multinational consulting firm, Counterpart & Associates, whose standard business practice involves deploying consultants to complete projects in other possible worlds.

The manual should adopt the relentlessly bureaucratic tone of an actual corporate document, complete with numbered sections and subclauses, and it should apply the metaphysical distinctions with literal administrative seriousness so that the contradictions become visible. Please include at least four specific components: (1) a “Personal Continuity and Deployment Policy” that distinguishes, with procedural precision, between being literally the same consultant relocated across worlds (transworld identity) and being a maximally similar counterpart who assumes only qualitative professional roles; (2) a “Compensation and Benefits Portability Clause” that turns on whether origin-essential properties—such as having been originally hired in the actual world—are retained, replaced, or renegotiated when a counterpart takes over a contract; (3) a “Performance Attribution Protocol” addressing disputes in which a counterpart in a distant world produces inferior or superior work and the original consultant either demands credit or attempts to disclaim blame; and (4) an “Essential Property Audit” sidebar that quietly takes a stand on whether haecceitistic differences—that is, being numerically identical to oneself versus being merely the best available qualitative match—should affect promotion and seniority. The satire must arise entirely from pushing these philosophical commitments into the fine print of employment law, not from inventing absurd metaphysical claims; a specialist should recognize that the manual’s internal contradictions mirror real debates about similarity-based counterpart relations, the rejection of literal transworld identity, and the status of essential origins, while a non-specialist should still be able to follow that a serious conceptual distinction is being tested to its breaking point. Do not include bibliographies, citations, or reading lists, and avoid explicit philosophical exposition; let the bureaucratic form carry the conceptual weight on its own.
