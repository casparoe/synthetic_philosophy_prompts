My opening move is this: the system appears to escape your analysis of disciplinary power because it removes the human examiner, the visible hierarchy, and the normative judgment of the guard — replacing them with a closed loop of biological feedback. If power is always relational and requires a subject who exercises it upon another, then an automated, self-regulating loop might be something else entirely — perhaps biopolitical management without discipline, or even a form of care rather than control.
