I sit on my university’s Dining Procurement and Sustainability Committee, and after a long, heated argument with a friend about our proposed menu overhaul, I need to circulate a written opinion before next Thursday’s vote. The administration wants to eliminate beef and expand chicken and legume options, citing carbon footprint alone. My friend insists this framing misses the philosophical point: beef can be grass-fed with minimal crop input, whereas chicken relies on grain and soy whose cultivation kills wild field animals during planting and harvest; at the same time, chickens live far shorter lives, meaning more individual sentient lives are ended per calorie served, and the intensity of suffering in a brief, confined life is not obviously less serious than in a longer one. I need a memo that recommends whether the committee should approve the beef-to-chicken switch as currently framed, reject it, or approve it only with conditions—specifically addressing whether our evaluation should adopt a “net suffering differential” that weights both direct farmed-animal welfare and indirect wild-animal mortality from feed-crop cultivation, rather than relying on carbon metrics alone. The memo should give a concrete recommendation with an actionable time frame, treat my friend’s position seriously rather than dismissing it, and be readable for committee members without philosophy backgrounds without oversimplifying the conceptual problem. Structure the response in this order with approximate lengths: Executive Summary (about 150 words); Recommendation and Time Frame (about 150 words); The Case for Including Net-Suffering Accounting (about 250 words); The Strongest Counter-Argument (about 200 words); and Implementation Conditions (about 150 words). Do not include bibliographies, citation lists, or references to specific authors I should read; I will add any needed sources myself.
