I'm drafting a short essay for an advanced epistemology seminar on hinge propositions in Wittgenstein's On Certainty, and I'd like your critical feedback before I revise. Rather than defending the standard reading that all hinges form a homogeneous bedrock of unquestioned certainties, I want to argue that Wittgenstein's own examples actually suggest a functional taxonomy. Some hinges—such as the existence of an external world or the general reliability of basic sense perception—seem structurally load-bearing: if they were genuinely suspended, the entire framework of justification and inquiry would collapse. Others—like the certainty that I have never been to the moon, or that this object is my hand in an ordinary practical context—function more like decorative stabilizers. They are not arbitrary beliefs, but they do not bear the same foundational weight; they are culturally and practically anchored certainties that smooth interaction without underwriting the very possibility of knowledge itself. I tentatively call these decorative hinges, and I want to explore whether recognizing them weakens the anti-sceptical force of hinge epistemology or, conversely, makes it more precise by showing exactly where scepticism misfires. Does this distinction hold up against Wittgenstein's resistance to hierarchical ordering among certainties?

Please comment specifically on three things. First, whether my characterization of load-bearing versus decorative hinges misreads Wittgenstein's insistence that hinges are not propositions known with special certainty but rules of the language-game. Second, whether the idea is conceptually interesting or merely restates familiar contextualist distinctions in new terminology. Third, whether the argument risks making hinges too belief-like, thereby undermining their distinctive epistemic status. I am not asking for a bibliography, reading list, or citations to secondary literature—just a conceptual assessment of whether the essay's central move is accurate and worth developing further. If you see a particular example in On Certainty that either supports or undermines the decorative hinge category, I'd especially welcome your reading of it.
