I have been working through some literature on commitment mechanisms and evolutionary signaling for an independent study project, and in discussions with friends—some in philosophy, some in community organizing—I keep encountering a pattern I cannot place. The pattern involves someone deliberately, and quite visibly, adopting a rule or framework that degrades their own ability to reason about a specific practical domain. For example, a person might publicly commit to excluding consequentialist calculations from all decisions involving family loyalty, even when they concede that such calculations would yield more accurate predictions of welfare outcomes; or they might make a point of refusing to read or engage with a well-documented opposing viewpoint, presenting their resulting ignorance not as a limitation but as evidence that they are not strategically keeping an escape route open. In these cases the cost is not material—there is no financial loss or physical risk—but epistemic: they have impaired their own predictive or informational toolkit, and they display that impairment as a credential.

What interests me is that this seems to go beyond merely holding an expensive belief. The agent is engineering a handicap inside their own reasoning process and treating it as a binding mechanism, partly directed at their future self—if I have made myself incapable of computing a defection, I am less likely to take it. I have seen analogies drawn to wasteful biological displays, but those are usually bodily or resource-based. Here the waste is cognitive accuracy or informational access, and the signaling audience includes the agent themselves. Does this specific configuration have an established name? How should it be distinguished from ordinary costly signaling, from psychological self-handicapping, or from simple dogmatism? I am not looking for a bare label; I want to understand where the edges are—whether the impairment must be intentional, whether visibility to others is essential, and whether the domain of handicap must align with the domain of loyalty being demonstrated.
