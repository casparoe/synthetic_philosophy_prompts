the combination problem in panpsychism has been going in circles for like twenty years now — everyone agrees micro-consciousness is supposed to add up to macro-consciousness, everyone agrees we have no idea how, and then the conversation just loops back to but thats not a real combination. In my philosophy of mind reading group we keep hitting the same wall: panpsychists talk about combination like its just another physical relation we havent mapped yet, critics talk about it like its asking for metaphysical alchemy, and nobody ever shifts. Im wondering if this is actualy three disagreements hiding under one name. First, is combination meant in an aggregative sense — like bricks making a wall — or an integrative sense — like processes making a system — and are they just using the same word for different relations? Second, is the fight realy about consciousness, or about what we accept as an explanation when physics already describes all the parts? Third, theres this hidden bedrock thing: maybe the panpsychist and the critic have different default intutions about whether properties can be built at all, and neither side can argue the other out of that feeling. So diagnose it for me — verbal, substantive, or bedrock intuition? And be honest: is there anything that could actualy break the loop? A formal model showing how micro-experience scales lawfully? A conceptual distinction that dissolves the problem? Or should we just admit its a hard boundary and stop pretending theres a hidden answer?
