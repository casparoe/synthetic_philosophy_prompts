I need a decision procedure I can actually run in about fifteen minutes per claim, without having to call in legal or read theory, that translates the core principle that abstract ideas are not ownable while specific expressive implementations and proprietary combinations can be, into a step-by-step filter. Keep the deliverable practical: a flowchart in words, a small set of test sentences or descriptors I can apply, and an honest note about what the procedure cannot settle.
