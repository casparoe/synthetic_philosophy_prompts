If Rorty's antirepresentationalism denies that true beliefs mirror nature and instead treats all vocabularies as instruments for coping with environmental pressures, then it appears that the only remaining ground for preferring one framework over another is contingent social utility rather than any direct worldly check. Given this, wouldn't Rorty be forced to treat the predictive calibration of a hydrological forecasting model and the symbolic coherence of an indigenous water-ritual vocabulary as rationally equivalent—since neither represents the river's intrinsic properties—unless he secretly relies on a representationalist notion of causal resistance? In other words, does antirepresentationalism necessarily collapse into a radical incommensurability thesis whenever two vocabularies generate incompatible practical directives, or is there a coherent non-representational account of how reality can resist and still permit reasoned adjudication without invoking correspondence?
