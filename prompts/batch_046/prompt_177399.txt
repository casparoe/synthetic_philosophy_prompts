(3) So LLMs have beliefs in the same functional sense humans do—weights updating versus neurons firing.

(7) So either I deny LLMs have beliefs (which contradicts (2)-(3)), or I grant beliefs to weather apps (absurd), or I reject (1).
(8) But if I reject (1), human beliefs are also just correlation-tracking, which smells like eliminative materialism sneaking in through the back door.
(9) I really want to keep (3) and avoid (5). Is there a hidden equivocation between tracking-for-prediction and tracking-for-agency? Or am I just stuck picking the least bad option?
