Write a deadpan travel-guide entry of approximately 600 words in the style of a Lonely Planet page for the Republic of Resemblance, a fictional nation-state that has adopted extreme resemblance nominalism as its constitutional framework. Include an invented proponent, the Minister of Particulars, whose official welcome message accidentally demonstrates the circularity problem. The voice must remain deadpan and internally consistent: every institutional practice must be one a resemblance nominalist could genuinely defend, and the reader should feel the real philosophical pressure of reducing properties to resemblance classes. Do not include citations, bibliographies, or references to specific scholarly works. End the entry with a one-sentence summary of its philosophical punchline on its own line, preceded by "Summary:".
