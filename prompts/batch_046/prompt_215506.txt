Show how each procedural safeguard generates a nested paradox that is then dissolved by another layer of self-referential machinery, so that the system becomes an infinite regress of meta-rules each claiming authority over the one beneath it. The piece should be readable to someone who has never encountered the classic constitutional-law puzzle about self-amendment, but it should reward a reader who recognizes that the real logical structure—moving from one self-referential rule to an endlessly ascending hierarchy of them—is being exaggerated rather than distorted.
