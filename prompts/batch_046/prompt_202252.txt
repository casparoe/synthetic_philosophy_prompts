I argue that Margalit's ethics of caring gets misapplied when we extend the duty from survivors and direct witnesses to whole polities generations later — what I call mnemonic solidarity — and that Rieff's critique of therapeutic culture shows why this extension turns collective memory into a kind of moral vanity. Give me the objections in roughly the order the'll come, phrased as those two camps would phrase them, with what I should concede up front, what I should hold firm on, and the weakest point in my own argument I should prep to defend rather than hope they miss.
