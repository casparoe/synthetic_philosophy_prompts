I want to move past the standard comparative-ethics taxonomy that everyone in the room will have seen, and instead press the less-discussed point that if you take the Abhidhammic analysis of mental continuity seriously, the evaluation of intentional action structurally resembles intertemporal outcome-aggregation rather than character-cultivation.
