I've been stuck in this interdiscplinary reading group where sociologists and philosophers keep arguing about whether 'Gen Z' or 'Boomers' are real social kinds or just marketing slices with a birth-year filter applied. But I could be wrong: maybe there's a hidden empirical disagreement about whether period effects can ever be cleanly separated from cohort effects with current methods, and both sides are just projecting that uncertainty onto the word 'real'. Can you diagnose what's actually going on here — verbal, substantive, or something like two disciplines answering different questions under one heading?
