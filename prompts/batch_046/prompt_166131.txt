Compare Darwin's inversion of design reasoning with Turing's inversion of cognitive reasoning. Darwin demonstrated that complex functional competence—such as the precise fit between an organism's traits and its environment—can emerge through selective retention of variants without any agent comprehending the adaptive end-state in advance. Turing demonstrated, conversely, that formal symbol-manipulation can reproduce the outputs of intelligent reasoning without the system comprehending the meanings of those symbols. Many philosophers treat these as parallel dissolutions of the need for an intermediate understanding: in evolution, nature need not understand fitness; in computation, the mechanism need not understand logic.

Yet there is a structural asymmetry worth examining. Darwinian selection is distributed across populations and generations; no single locus executes the adaptive solution, and the process operates through differential survival rather than through a determinate procedure applied to representations. Turing computation, by contrast, is typically localized in a single mechanism running step-by-step instructions, producing correct outputs despite lacking semantic access to them. To clarify the comparison, distinguish process-blind competence—the achievement of a functional outcome without any internal representation of that outcome guiding the process—from content-blind competence—the correct manipulation of representations without grasping what they represent.

Using this distinction, adjudicate whether the two cases are genuinely analogous inversions or differ in kind. Does Darwinian evolution involve representations in any substantive sense—perhaps in the form of genetic information encoding phenotypic solutions—or is it purely process-blind? Does Turing computation involve any functional orientation toward adaptation or survival, or is it purely content-blind? Which inversion more radically eliminates the need for comprehension, and does either leave a residual role for something like implicit understanding in the system that bears the competence?
