The document should mandate that all official reports eliminate the predicate true except in its strictly deflationary, disquotational use, and it should satirize deflationary theories of truth by extending their core commitments into literal administrative practice. Target the position itself rather than any named philosopher: emphasize the denial that truth is a substantive metaphysical property, the claim that asserting it is true that p adds nothing beyond asserting p, and the corresponding rejection of correspondence relations, truthmakers, and metaphysical inquiries into what truth consists in.

Include an invented internal memo from a Senior Linguistic Auditor answering an employee who asks whether arithmetic reports are exempt — and have the auditor's response expose the tension between strict deflationism and our ordinary commitment to objective mathematical truth. Keep the voice consistently deadpan and readable to someone with no philosophical background, but ensure that a specialist recognizes the exaggerations as fair extrapolations of actual deflationist arguments rather than strawmen.
