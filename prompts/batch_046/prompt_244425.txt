A colleague has pressed me with a regress argument linking sphexishness to the impossibility of free will, and I cannot locate the weak joint. She formulates it as follows.

(1) Sphexish behavior is behavior produced by a routine that lacks an internal check: the agent continues executing the routine even when the conditions that originally warranted it have disappeared.

(2) Free will, if it exists, requires non-sphexish flexibility: an agent must be capable of suspending an ongoing course of action upon recognizing that circumstances have changed.

(3) Any suspension of a first-order routine R must be implemented by a second-order mechanism M.

(4) Either M is sphexish, or M is not.

(5) If M is sphexish, it executes its interruption-check automatically, irrespective of whether interruption is genuinely appropriate; hence it confers only a different rigidity, not true flexibility.

(6) Therefore, if M is to confer genuine flexibility, M must itself be non-sphexish.

(7) But then, by (3), M requires its own suspender M', and the same reasoning applies to M', yielding an infinite regress of non-sphexish monitors.

(8) Any real cognitive architecture is finite and cannot instantiate an infinite hierarchy.

(9) So every real agent is sphexish at some base level of her control structure.

(10) Yet (2) requires an agent to be non-sphexish at every level relevant to free choice.

(11) Consequently, free will is impossible for any finite being.

She allows that one might speak of "shallow" freedom—freedom that reaches only partway down the hierarchy—but insists that there is never a non-sphexish foundation. I am committed to (1) and (2), and I am particularly reluctant to reject (6), since a monitor that runs on autopilot does seem to miss the point of flexibility. Still, (11) strikes me as a reductio. Which step should I reject, and at what cost? Specifically, can the regress in (7) be blocked without abandoning the claim that genuine flexibility requires a non-automatic monitor, or must saving free will come at the expense of one of the premises I wish to preserve?
