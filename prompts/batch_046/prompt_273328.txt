Then construct a taxonomy, in the form of a branching decision tree, that sorts positions on the badness of pain for an advanced undergraduate seminar in philosophy of mind. The root node should present the asymbolia case: a patient reports the full sensory quality of a burn—localized, intense, thermally distinctive—yet shows no distress, no avoidance behavior, and describes the sensation as neutrally interesting.
