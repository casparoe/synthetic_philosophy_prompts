Please provide: (a) the exam question text itself, calibrated for approximately 1,000 words in 90 minutes; (b) a model answer of roughly 900 words that executes each move with explicit argumentation and reaches a clear verdict on the divergence question; (c) a marking scheme totaling 30 points, distributed across the four moves, specifying for each the criterion that separates an A-range response from a B-range response and identifying the characteristic misconception that should cost marks. The prompt should present the threshold-conditional maxim as a self-contained hypothetical and should depend on no specific publication dates, obscure terminology, or verbatim quotations from Kant’s texts.
