The other half thinks this is just philosophical window-dressing: we can't measure gradations precisely enough to write rules, and if we protect homeostatic feedback loops we're basically protecting smart thermostats, which is a reductio. I need the boards decision memo by Thursday — approve, reject, or approve with conditions — with reasoning both sides can actually reply to.
