do these hang together? i'm especially worried that (3) undermines (4) — if neighborhoods are genuinely emergent, can individuals deserve the appreciation? and does (6) just reproduce the zoning power i criticized in (2)? but i suspect some of these clashes are only surface-level. please take a committed stand: rank the genuine tensions by severity, identify the cheapest revision, and tell me which single change fixes the most conflicts. don't just map options — commit to what has to go and why.
