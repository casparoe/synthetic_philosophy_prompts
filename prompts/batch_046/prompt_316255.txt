Construct a thought experiment that meets the following specifications. Present a realistic, clinically framed scenario—no teleportation, no non-physical substances, no demons or perfect predictors—in one paragraph, describing a patient who undergoes gradual replacement of biological brain tissue with synthetic analogues in two separated phases. Only after full stabilization is Phase 2 begun: the inhibitory interneuron population and the associated astrocytic metabolic-coupling network are replaced by analog memristive arrays that replicate inhibitory dynamics but lack any metabolic feedback loops with the remaining biological glial scaffold, which is itself gradually replaced.
