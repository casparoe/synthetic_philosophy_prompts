For a feature I am writing on the hidden architecture of reductionist commitments, I need a diagnostic decision tree—eight to ten branching nodes—that sorts a reader’s actual philosophical stance on explanation across domains, using the crane-versus-skyhook distinction (bottom-up, mechanistic constructions versus unexplained top-down selectors) and the greedy-versus-non-greedy reductionism axis. Rather than asking respondents to choose labels, each node must present a concrete, cross-disciplinary case on which the positions genuinely diverge: for example, whether evolutionary convergence in cephalopod cognition renders mammalian consciousness non-special through a fully mechanistic account, or whether it requires an autonomous principle of convergent teleology; whether quantum field theory’s explanatory dominance over chemistry is greedy (eliminating chemical ontology) or merely layered; whether mathematical truth survives if every formal system is physically computable, or whether that reduction itself invokes an unexplained skyhook of logical necessity; and whether ethical norms can be fully derived from evolutionary or neurobiological cranes, or whether they retain irreducible normative force. The tree should open with a root commitment about explanatory debt—must every level be fully derivable from the one below, or can intermediate laws remain autonomous?—then branch through biology, physics, cognition, mathematics, and ethics, mapping answer patterns onto named leaves: eliminative greedy reductionism, layered emergentism with crane-only constraints, pluralist non-reductionism permitting localized skyhooks, and deflationary instrumentalism about higher-level theories. Unoccupied combinations—such as greedy physical reduction paired with full skyhook autonomy in ethics—must be explicitly marked as branches with no named occupant and briefly diagnosed as incoherent, unstable, or merely unexplored. Where two positions converge on identical answers across all nodes—for instance, strong emergentism and certain non-greedy physicalisms—flag them as inseparable by this instrument rather than forcing a false distinction. The audience is educated general readers; avoid asking for bibliographies, citations, or reading lists. For each leaf, include a brief paragraph explaining the specific pattern of answers that reaches it and why that pattern is philosophically distinctive.
