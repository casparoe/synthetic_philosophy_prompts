Reach and defend a concrete verdict: either that debate-based alignment remains viable only under a permanently restricted format that prevents arbitrary encoding, or that no restriction can outpace strategic representation and debate therefore collapses as a safety mechanism in principle. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence, then develop the reasoning; avoid any opening preamble and do not restate the question.
