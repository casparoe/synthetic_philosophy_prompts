The plot should turn on her inability to determine whether her report—"I perceive the stimulus as figure X"—describes a genuine sensory state or a belief-driven reconstruction, with concrete consequences for her data integrity, her professional credibility, and her trust in her own observations. Please avoid standard science-fiction tropes involving artificial consciousness or virtual reality; maintain a realistic contemporary neuroscience setting. The philosophical tension must emerge from what happens to her recordings, her decisions about publication, and her relationship to her own perceptual reports, not from embedded lectures or explicit naming of philosophical positions.
