On the other side are the contemporary philosophers of meaning—think Wolf with her fitting-fulfillment view, or the objective-list people—who say life can be genuinely meaningful when you're actively engaged with things that are objectively worthy, even if there's no God or cosmic narrative. For years they just talk past each other: the absurdists accuse the other camp of redefining meaning to mean things I enjoy, and the meaning-theorists say the absurdists are holding out for an impossible transcendent justification that nobody actually needs.

The absurdist seems to want a chain of why does this matter that eventually hits bedrock outside human practice—otherwise it's just spinning in a circle. The contemporary camp treats that demand as a category mistake, like asking what color Tuesday is; they think value can be brute and self-sustaining without a final and that's why that reaches outside the frame.
