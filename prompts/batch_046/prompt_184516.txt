Identify any logical combination of answers that no named view occupies, briefly diagnosing whether it is incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored. Finally, flag any pair of leaves your tree genuinely cannot separate, especially if they differ only in rhetoric about "objective chance" versus "objective credence" without diverging on the concrete case. Phrase every node as a question answerable by someone who understands the logic of the positions but has never encountered this specific framing of constitutional-risk attribution.
