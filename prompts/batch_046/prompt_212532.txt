Compare Nozick's experience machine with what I'd call a reality-check machine — not something that feeds you fake pleasure, but something that reveals your acheivements and relationships were all staged, leaving your mental states intact but severing their causal link to reality. Does Nozick's worry about being conected to the world apply symmetrically? If well-being needs real-world grounding, does finding out you've never had it retroactively erase well-being you already experienced, or does the mental state still count? And does this comparison actually do more for objective-list theories than against hedonism?
