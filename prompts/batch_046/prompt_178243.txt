The piece should be legible as satire only to a reader who recognizes the underlying philosophy; never break the bureaucratic mask to wink. Mention Rorty by name no more than once, include no bibliographies, reading lists, or citations, and avoid attributing specific works, dates, or page numbers. The target is the institutional absurdity that arises when antirepresentationalism is treated as a governing technology, not Rorty as an individual. Maintain formal, precise prose throughout, as though drafting an actual municipal code.
