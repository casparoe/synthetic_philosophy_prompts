Two, I still feel like there's something genuinely wrong with eating factory-farmed meat, even though my single burger order doesn't change the industry's practices at all. That makes my climate inaction feel rational, but it also makes my meat guilt feel irrational, which bugs me.

Do these hang together? Which pairs are in real tension and which just look weird?
