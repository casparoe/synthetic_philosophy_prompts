So causal decision theory should solve both the same way — dont pay the mugger because it doesnt causally affect the prediction, and dont pay the blackmailer because flipping one world breaks the XOR. But then why do people treat XOR blackmail as a seperate challenge for evidential decision theory? Is the difference just that the blackmailer can observe both worlds, or am I wrong that they share this XOR architecture?
