Question: Explain Moore's paradox. Give an example like "its raining but i dont belive its raining." Why is it weird to say that even if it might actually be true? Whats the deal with belief vs assertion?

Student answer:
Moores paradox was named by the philosopher Moore after he noticed it in a lecture. Its the thing where you say "p" but then also say "i dont belive p" - like "its raining but i dont belive its raining." This is basically a contradiction like p and not-p, except its about your mind instead of the world. Wittgenstein wrote about it in his Investigations and said its actually a logical contradiction when you assert it. Even though the whole sentence could be true - maybe you really dont know the weather - you cant say it about yourself without being weird. So belief is more important then truth here, and the paradox shows logic applies to mental states.
