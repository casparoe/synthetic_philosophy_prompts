The framework has never ruled on this exact configuration.

Your task is threefold. First, articulate at least two competing principles—each consistent with all five earlier verdicts—that could govern intentionality attribution in collective contexts. If the five precedents genuinely underdetermine the outcome, say so explicitly and identify the normative commitment that each option would import. Please present your reasoning in full, then conclude with a single-line summary.
