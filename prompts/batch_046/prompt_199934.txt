I'm preparing my position for a metaphysics of mind seminar and want an internal consistency audit before I present. My commitments are as follows: (a) Constitutive panpsychism is true—macro-phenomenal states are literally built out of corresponding micro-phenomenal states, not merely caused or correlated by them. (b) Micropsychism, the view that only micro-level subjects exist and macro-level consciousness is illusory, is false; macro-subjects are ontologically basic in the same way macro-objects are, not mere aggregates. (c) The standard combination problem is best reconstructed not as a problem of phenomenal bonding but as a problem of structural isomorphism: the qualitative geometry of macro-experience must map onto the qualitative geometry of the composing micro-experiences, and this mapping fails in obvious cases (for example, unified visual fields composed of point-like micro-experiences). (d) The small-mentality objection can be answered by extending the phenomenal-concept strategy downward: we lack the macro-scale phenomenal concepts needed to recognize micro-qualia for what they are, which explains our intuition that atoms are not conscious without implying that they actually lack consciousness. (e) Physicalism is false if defined as the thesis that all properties are structurally describable; however, a version of physicalism that admits non-structural intrinsic properties is compatible with my panpsychism, since those intrinsic properties just are the micro-phenomenal ones. (f) I take the boundary problem—determining the precise spatiotemporal limits of any given conscious subject—to be a deeper and more damaging challenge to panpsychism than the combination problem itself. Go through these and tell me which pairs are in genuine tension, which pairs only look like they conflict but do not, and for each real tension rank its severity and identify the cheapest revision that restores consistency together with what that revision costs elsewhere in my profile. Do not ask me which commitment I am prepared to drop; instead, for each tension lay out what giving up each side would entail. Please lead with your bottom line in the first sentence and then give the reasoning, with no preamble and no restating of the question.
