(3) That monitoring is structurally parallel to the mediating calculus in (1): it inserts a deliberative step between the ground project and the action, asking whether this expresses who I am. (4) So if the mediating step in utilitarian reasoning corrupts integrity, the mediating step required to preserve integrity corrupts integrity in exactly the same way. (5) Therefore integrity demands one thought too many about oneself, making it either impossible to maintain deliberately or inherently unstable. (6) But Williams clearly treats integrity as a sustainable, central virtue rather than a self-undermining condition, so he should reject (5). Where does this chain fail?
