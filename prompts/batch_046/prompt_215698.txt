Now add this constraint: the brain uses a predictive binding window of about 180-220ms that can back-project a predictive model onto the earlier perceptual frame without altering the raw sensory registration. Given that setup, can a Stalinesque account accomodate the "reverse phi" illusion—where the second dot is actually the same color as the first but motion is perceived backward—without smuggling in an Orwellian revision?
