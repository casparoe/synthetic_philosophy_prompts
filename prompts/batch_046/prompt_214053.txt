Specifically, I think an 'a' is whatever symbol occupies the first-letter-slot in a way that minimizes what I'd call "style-entropy" — the unpredictability of how the rest of the alphabet gets rendered — and that human recognition actually tracks this global relational stability rather than local shapes. I even want to say it holds across non-Latin alphabets as long as they have at least three degrees of stylistic freedom, which makes the claim stronger and definatly riskier.
