Apply the procedure to two cases: first, sizing a flood reservoir using historical frequencies for a 100-year storm; second, entering a 30-year water-sharing treaty with upstream jurisdictions when climate projections conflict and no basis exists for assigning probabilities to which projection will hold. In your response, lead with the bottom line in the first sentence—state the procedure's core verdict on the two cases—then give the reasoning and the full procedure, with no preamble and no restatement of the question.
