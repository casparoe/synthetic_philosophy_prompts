Write a dialogue between a defender of the standard person-affecting view and a proponent of modal-relational nonidentity, debating whether delaying climate mitigation can wrong future people even though different policies produce different populations. The person-affecting side should press the classic intuition: in the delayed world, no specific individual exists who can claim to have been harmed, since those people owe their existence to the very delay. The modal-relational side should counter that wronging need not target actual individuals if present institutions stand in normative relations to "modal stand-ins"—possible persons anchored to current legal and ecological frameworks—and should directly answer the charge that this imports a metaphysically dubious collective identity. Each speaker should engage the other's actual argument rather than change the subject, and neither should be presented as a strawman. Do not include requests for citations, bibliographies, or references to specific texts.
