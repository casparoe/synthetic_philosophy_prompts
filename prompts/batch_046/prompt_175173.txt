I came across the Twin Earth scenario and it unsettled me, but I want to move past the usual classroom replay. The two parties are Camp One, Broad Content Externalists, who take the Twin Earth intuition to show that at least some mental contents are constitutively determined by the environment—so an Earthling and a Twin-Earthling with identical internal neural or functional architectures do not share the same thought—versus Camp Two, Narrow Content Internalists, who take the same intuition to show only that reference or extension is fixed externally; they maintain that the mental content or propositional attitude inside each head remains identical, and that any difference belongs to the world, not to the thinker.

To prevent this from collapsing into a well-worn script, frame the collaboration around a specific constructed case: synthetic agents with byte-for-byte identical internal architectures, identical causal histories, and identical behavioral dispositions, one embedded in an environment where the clear liquid is H2O and the other where it is XYZ. Both agents deploy the token in internally indistinguishable ways.
