You should defend the claim that because these metrics carry no intrinsic normative content, the resulting framework yields an objective, value-agnostic impact measure that resolves the alignment problem for impact minimization. In doing so, anticipate and rebut the objection that selecting these particular invariants, constructing the counterfactual “no-activation” boundary, and treating certain physical configurations as a neutral default already embed implicit normative judgments about which states of the world are desirable to preserve.
