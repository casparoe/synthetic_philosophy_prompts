But now it's the core of thier identity, especially after the 1992 dispersal. Some members argue for retroactive kinship binding — the idea that performing the ritual now actually creates the ancestral link it describes, so the origin story is moot. Our charter allows protection if there's genuine communal integration, but nobody defined whether invented origins disqualify it, and we're already seeing other groups prepping thinner petitions.
