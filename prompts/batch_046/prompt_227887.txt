Please provide:
(1) the exam question itself;
(2) a model answer of roughly 600 words that resolves the conceptual crux rather than summarizing historians;
(3) a marking scheme built around five must-hit points (total 20 marks), with the marks each carries and a one-line note on how candidates typically fail on that point.

Hard constraints: the entire response—question, answer, and scheme—must not exceed 900 words. Do not ask for bibliographies, reading lists, or citations; the answer should be constructible from conceptual reasoning alone, without access to external sources. No verbatim quotations from texts.
