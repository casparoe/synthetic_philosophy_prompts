The external vehicle must be non-digital and low-tech—no computers, smartphones, or neural implants—and the coupling must be constant and functionally integrated rather than occasional retrieval. Keep the setting realistic and appropriate for an undergraduate philosophy seminar: no science-fictional technology, no perfect predictors, and no exotic physics. Next, isolate the single structural feature of your design that causes Adams and Aizawa to peel away from the other two, and explain whether removing that feature collapses the case back into the familiar Otto-and-notebook example or instead produces a different result altogether.
