She also takes Carr seriously: yes, selecting facts is theory-laden, and yes, causation in history is messy. But she thinks Carr overreaches when he says that kills objectivity. And she accepts Hayden White's point about narrative emplotment being unavoidable — every history uses one of the four master tropes — but denies that makes the choice just aesthetic or arbitrary. She distinguishes structural objectivity (getting mechanisms and archival facts right) from representational neutrality (which she says is impossible anyway).

I want about eight interview questions, max 350 words in your answer, tight but not curt. I want one question asking whether that doesn't just push subjectivity back one layer, into the choice of imputation model and prior weights. And a final question: what evidence could actually disconfirm her view, not merely fail to confirm it?
