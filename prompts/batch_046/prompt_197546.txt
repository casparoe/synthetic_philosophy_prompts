In my current novel, two linguists attempt to decode an alien signal absent any shared ecological or cultural reference points. Given Quine's thesis of the indeterminacy of translation—centered on the gavagai scenario—what are the most important philosophical implications for whether these characters can ever establish a non-arbitrary ontology of the aliens' world? I am particularly concerned with how the underdetermination of translation manuals by observable behavior might undermine not merely lexical agreement, but the narrative possibility of genuine mutual recognition between species.
