Please adopt the role of a professional philosopher of knowledge with expertise in contemporary epistemology and the logic of justification. Please also flag any pair of named positions that your tree cannot genuinely separate given the nodes you have chosen, and explain whether the failure is due to shared commitments or to ambiguity in the diagnostic questions. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, citations, references to specific article titles, page numbers, or publication dates.
