Constraints: the setting must be an ordinary workplace with no exotic technology, science-fictional elements, or omniscient overseers; present the scenario in one paragraph; and the mechanism—whether involving the erosion of reflective self-direction, the transformation of choice into selection within a pre-structured option space, or a comparable institutional failure—must be generated by a feature of the workshop rather than stipulated by fiat. After the scenario, analyze the case from three perspectives: the Inquisitor's paternalist view, a radical negative-liberty defense of the free regime, and a moderate perfectionist view that distinguishes liberty from autonomy. Do not provide bibliographies, citations, or reading lists; do not quote verbatim from Dostoevsky or any other source—paraphrase the Inquisitor's claim about the burden of freedom and the human preference for security in your own words; and avoid obscure secondary literature or niche terminology that could not be expected without access to specialized sources.
