In preparing a lecture on Wittgenstein's On Certainty for a graduate seminar in epistemology, I have been trying to articulate precisely why hinge propositions—those framework commitments like 'the earth exists' or 'I have hands'—are not merely extremely well-evidenced beliefs that have become practically unrevisable. I understand that for Wittgenstein these propositions do not lie within the language-game of justification at all; they are the hinges on which the door of doubt turns, not items inside the room that might be rearranged. Nevertheless, I keep returning to a difficulty that my students raised last week, and I am unsure whether it reveals a genuine tension or a confusion on my part. They proposed a hypothetical community—call them the Ephemeralists—who share our logical and linguistic practices but whose hinge commitments include the denial of object persistence: for them, every apparent table or tree is sustained only by a momentary collective linguistic projection, and they treat this not as an empirical discovery but as the very condition under which empirical inquiry operates. Within their framework, evidence and justification function coherently; they simply lack the hinge that makes our particular doubts about object persistence intelligible. Now, if Wittgenstein is right that hinges are not justified by evidence but instead constitute the background against which evidence acquires its force, and if he also denies that we can appeal to transcendental arguments or to a privileged metaphysical standpoint to vindicate our hinges over theirs, then it seems we are left with two unpalatable options: either our hinges are merely arbitrary conventions (making framework choice a matter of social preference), or there is some hidden normative constraint that Wittgenstein has failed to articulate. Is this a false dilemma? More specifically, am I wrong to assume that immunity to evidential revision must entail either conventionalism or dogmatic privilege—that is, does Wittgenstein have resources, internal to the dynamics of practice itself, for distinguishing a functioning hinge-set from a defective one without stepping outside all frameworks?
