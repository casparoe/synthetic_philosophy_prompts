The prompt should also require students to confront one targeted objection: that treating salinization as a geographic structure risks erasing human agency in water-management practices. Please provide: (1) the exam question itself, written clearly and formally; (2) a model answer of approximately 700 words that demonstrates the specific moves a top response must make; and (3) a marking scheme out of 25 points that separates an A-level answer from a B-level one by naming, for each criterion, both the required intellectual move and the typical failure that should cost marks. The five criteria should cover: correct exposition of the three temporalities with climate and geography emphasized; rigorous application to the Mesopotamian case; direct engagement with the agency objection; evaluation of the framework's limits when structures accelerate; and precise distinction between Braudel's approach and broader Annales social history. Do not request a bibliography, reading list, or citations.
