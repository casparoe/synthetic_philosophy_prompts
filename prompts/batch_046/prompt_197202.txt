Write a short story set in a small, isolated archipelago community that has developed an elaborate convention for allocating scarce freshwater during seasonal droughts. The convention is arbitrary—any rotational system would solve the coordination problem equally well—but it is sustained by common knowledge: each islander prefers to conform provided they know that others prefer conformity and that everyone knows this. Over generations, the convention acquires ritual significance and becomes embedded in social identity.

Introduce a technological change—perhaps a reliable desalination method—that eliminates the original scarcity and makes the rotational allocation unnecessary. Despite this, the convention persists and even intensifies. The narrative should focus on one elder who realizes that the coordination problem has dissolved, yet the norm survives because it has become decoupled from the underlying preferences that originally sustained it. Specifically, depict a moment in which she recognizes that people now continue the ritual not because they value the allocation itself, but because each person holds a second-order belief that others hold a second-order belief that abandoning the ritual would be interpreted as a breach of solidarity—even though, at the first-order level, no one actually cares about the rotation anymore.

In constructing the story, illustrate the core Lewisian structure—arbitrariness, mutual preference for conformity conditional on common knowledge, and the regularity as a solution to a coordination problem—while showing how an evolutionary dynamics of norm persistence can hollow out that structure. The convention should become, in effect, a self-sustaining shell of common knowledge about common knowledge, with the original utility entirely evacuated. Keep the philosophical dynamics implicit in the characters' interactions, rituals, and dialogue rather than stating them explicitly in expository passages. The story should end with the elder attempting, and failing, to dissolve the convention by publicly revealing its hollowness, thereby demonstrating the stability of a norm sustained purely by recursive belief rather than by mutual benefit.
