Like, not as therapy, but as a reusable checklist a mediatior could apply in ten minutes without calling a philosopher.

The procedure should ask ordered questions, include a small set of test sentences the mediator applies to the request, flag exactly where Stoicism draws the hard line against demanding control over impressions versus actions, and stop with a clear "no — this demands control over externals," "yes — behavioral request," or "gray zone — hand to human judgment because values conflict with theory." Include where the dichotomy breaks down in practice, say what the procedure cannot settle, and don't pretend judgment can be eliminated in the prohairesis boundry cases. No citations or reading lists, just keep it grounded in what the Stoics actually say about control.
