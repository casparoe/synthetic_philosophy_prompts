Case 2: A bettor assigns 50 percent to snow, then receives clear meteorological evidence, and updates to 90 percent snow and 10 percent not-snow by using a heuristic that completely ignores her prior. Her books are balanced at the new time, and she accepts bets at those new odds. Verdict: not vulnerable; she said only the current coherence of her books matters.

Case 3: A bettor holds fully coherent, well-updated credences about snow, but refuses every offered bet, explaining that she never gambles regardless of stakes. Verdict: not vulnerable.

Case 4: A three-person advisory panel each holds individually coherent probabilities about climate outcomes, but they aggregate by separate majority votes on each proposition, yielding a collective report that assigns 65 percent to "warming accelerates," 65 percent to "warming does not accelerate," and 60 percent to both occurring together. The panel has no joint bank account or betting authority. Verdict: not vulnerable; the incoherence is distributed, and there is no unitary agent to be exploited.
