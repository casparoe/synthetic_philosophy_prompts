My topic is Bernard Williams's integrity objection—specifically the charge that consequentialist agents have one thought too many when deliberating in intimate or ground-project contexts. The proposal should ask whether consequentialism necessarily forces both representations into the same deliberative moment, and whether a revised account could preserve the diachronic structure without abandoning impartial assessment altogether.
