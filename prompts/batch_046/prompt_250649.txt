I want to know what progress would actually look like: perhaps a taxonomy of how narrative roles split or unify the deceiver and deceived, or a demonstration that certain narrative structures make self-deception redundant. The deliverable should be useful to someone working at the intersection of philosophy and fiction—say, a framework for thinking about unreliable narration from the inside. Finally, include an honest assessment of scope: what makes this tractable in six months, and what conceptual obstacle could make it collapse into something too large for the fellowship timeline.
