Each node should be answerable without prior knowledge of which interpretation it produces. Please flag any two leaves that your tree cannot distinguish, and identify any logically possible answer-combination that falls into an empty space—offering a one-sentence diagnosis of whether that gap is incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored. Limit the tree to roughly six to eight nodes. Write the response in British English.
