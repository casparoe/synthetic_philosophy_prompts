Keep the irony fully legible but deadpan: no winking, no explicit moral commentary, no breaking of the legal voice. The target is the tendency in certain rationalist and transhumanist discussions to treat personal identity as pure information and therefore to underestimate the ethical friction of actual substrate sharing. Do not ask for citations, footnotes, or a bibliography; do not quote verbatim from Hofstadter's texts (paraphrase his thesis without quotation marks if you reference specific formulations); and avoid invoking obscure secondary literature or named contemporary figures beyond Hofstadter himself. The result should be intelligible to a reader familiar with substrate-independent identity arguments but should rely only on concepts that a small language model could reasonably have encountered in general pretraining.
