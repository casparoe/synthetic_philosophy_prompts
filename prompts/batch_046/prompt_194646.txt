This makes me think there is a hidden distinction between a vocabulary that collapses because it is internally unlivable and one that collapses because of what I can only call practical resistance from the situations it tries to handle, without smuggling representation back in.

Given that, which of these three open lines of work should I prioritize, in what order, and why?

Third, determining how plural societies should adjudicate between vocabularies that are internally coherent but mutually destructive—such as scientific and fundamentalist descriptions of the same events—when there is no neutral, representational court of appeal.
