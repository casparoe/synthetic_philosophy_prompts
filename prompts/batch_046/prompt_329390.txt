my memory-studies prof has been ruling on whether lost-cause myths grow bottom-up from survivers or get cooked up top-down by elites and state boards. her verdicts so far:
- confederate lost cause: cooked up. she says the UDC and textbook committees basically invented the noble defeat framing long after 1865, though grieving families did buy in.
- masada: bottom-up trauma that got state-captured. the zealot suicide was a real communal event, but the modern never-again framing is 20th-century state branding.
- kosovo 1389: cooked up. she argues the epic poems and vidovdan martyrdom are mostly 19th-century serbian intellectual and church inventions; contemporary chronicles dont support the story.
- dunkirk: bottom-up first, branded second. the evacuation genuinely traumatized people and made its own folk memory; chuchill's spirit rhetoric came later and layered on top.
