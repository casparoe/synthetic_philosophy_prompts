Third, evaluate whether either view can preserve a robust, non-arbitrary fact of the matter about the object's survival across an interval without implicitly importing a third alternative—such as a relational or stage-theoretic account of persistence. Your response should move beyond a generic summary of the two positions and should identify the specific metaphysical cost each incurs when forced to accommodate boundary-less change.
