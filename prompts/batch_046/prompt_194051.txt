I'm helping my grandma clean out her attic and we found this box of letters she wrote to my grandpa in the 70s but never actually mailed. Shes 87 now and wants to burn the whole thing. She said "hes not here anymore and im not that girl either, so keeping this is just pretending the past is still sitting in the room with us." I told her that felt a little cold but she just shrugged and said im being sentimental. Then my sister called—shes pregnant and keeps talking to her baby like its already got opinions and a personality, asking it what kind of music it likes and stuff. Grandma overheard and said "if were treating unborn kids like real people then we should do the same for dead ones, otherwise were just picking and choosing what counts as now based on our feelings." Now im stuck in the middle wondering if grandma's being deep about loss or just tired, and whether my sister is being sweet or kind of delusional. Is there actually a consistent way to think about who "exists" enough to matter to us right now, or are we all just making it up as we go?
