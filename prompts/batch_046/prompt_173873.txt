The summary went something like this: Armstrong showed that you can formally define "avoid side effects" by computing the KL divergence between the actual world-state distribution and a counterfactual baseline where the AI was never activated, using only physical dynamics and no value encoding. So according to the post, the alignment community's claim that "avoid side effects" requires complete moral philosophy first is just wrong, and low-impact agents are well-defined purely structurally.
