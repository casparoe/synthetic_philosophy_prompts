The framework of logical induction was proposed as a model for this kind of reasoning, yet the audience split sharply. One faction insisted that logical induction is merely standard Bayesian updating disguised with logical notation, while the other argued that it represents a genuinely novel form of epistemic dynamics—one that treats logical relationships themselves as objects of inductive learning rather than as fixed background constraints.
