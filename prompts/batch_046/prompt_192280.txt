I've been working through a reconstruction of Quine's metaethical implications, and I keep hitting what looks like an unacknowledged tension in the standard commentary. Quine's holism treats every belief—logical, mathematical, physical—as revisable in principle, with only the web as a whole facing the tribunal of experience. He rejects any analytic-synthetic boundary that could insulate a domain from empirical revision, and his criterion of ontological commitment applies uniformly: to be is to be the value of a bound variable in our best regimented theory. He also denies any autonomous first philosophy that could stand outside science to legislate normative matters. Given this, moral discourse appears to face a stark Quinean fork: either moral properties are quantified over in our best theory (making us realists about them, with all the ontological baggage that entails) or they are dispensable theoretical posits that should be eliminated or paraphrased away (making us error theorists or eliminativists). Yet the standard commentary treats Quine as having no systematic ethics, and debates about moral realism proceed as if his framework is silent on the question. That silence strikes me as implausible. Shouldn't Quine's naturalism imply that moral commitments are just further nodes in the web—revisable not by direct sensory falsification but by the same pragmatic virtues (simplicity, conservatism, predictive fertility) that govern physics and mathematics? And if so, why is there no canonical Quinean indispensability-style argument for moral realism, parallel to the argument for mathematical entities? Or, if moral values fail the indispensability test, why don't Quineans consistently treat them as useful fictions rather than as genuinely revisable cognitive commitments? I suspect I'm conflating Quine's epistemological holism with a normative holism that doesn't apply, or missing some distinction between theoretical and practical reason that Quine himself preserved despite his naturalism. Where exactly does the reconstruction break down?
