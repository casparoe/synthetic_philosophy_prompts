Write a short story about four identical server nodes on a deep-space relay station that all run the same decision algorithm. I want the story to show how superrational reasoning breaks down when identity and self-worth get baked into the payoff matrix. Keep it low-key and grounded, maybe told through system logs or internal monolog. Don't make it about robots saving the world; just let the decision loops run and see where they lock up.
