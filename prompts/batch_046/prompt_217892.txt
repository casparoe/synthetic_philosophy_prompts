hey, so i'm putting together a little sorting tool for my local rationalist meetup — we keep arguing about inside vs outside view but everyone means something different by "modesty." i want a decision tree (or flowchart taxonomy) that sorts people by what they'd actually do in concrete cases, not by what label they slap on themselves.

the tree should branch on three or four diagnostic questions that split the logical space. first: if you've spent 100 hours reasoning through a problem and reach a conclusion that conflicts with the base rate for people with your background, do you downgrade your confidence? second: if you apply the outside view to your own belief that you're well-calibrated, does that generate an infinite regress or a stable stopping point? third: should epistemic modesty apply symetrically to both your object-level beliefs and your meta-level beliefs about reasoning quality?

each leaf needs a name (or "unnamed" if it's unoccupied) plus one sentence on its most awkward commitment. please include the standard ones — like pure inside-viewers, pure reference-classers, the Tetlock-style integrationists — but also flag any empty branches. for instance, is there a coherent view that accepts recursion but denies symmetric application? if not, say why it's incoherent or just unexplored. i want this practical: my friends don't care about historical attribution, just whether the position survives contact with the case where you're 90% sure you're in the top decile of calibration, and whether you still defer after recieving that signal.
