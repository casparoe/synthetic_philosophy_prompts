Note explicitly if any two of the named leaves converge on identical answers despite differing in underlying motivation, so that the tree cannot genuinely separate them. The intended audience is philosophically literate but not specialist, so phrase each node as a claim one could endorse without already knowing the label it produces. Deliver the taxonomy in polished British English, in one to three paragraphs, without citations, bibliographies, or requests for further reading — I shall supply those myself.
