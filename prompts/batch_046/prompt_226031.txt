This struck me because I realize I have never actually observed a power being passed from one object to another; all I ever witness is proximity followed by change. I want to run a 90-minute session for about 12 adults in a community reading group, none with philosophy training, to make them feel why this is not just a religious oddity but a serious challenge to our everyday assumption that objects contain hidden causal powers. Please design a runnable in-room game or structured role-play with assigned roles, clear rules, and small real stakes—tokens that determine speaking turns or a shared prize—in which participants first observe a repeated pairing, such as a specific object being placed near another with a consistent outcome, then write down causal laws they believe they have discovered, then bet those tokens on whether altering the first object slightly—moving it an inch, substituting a different color of the same material—will change the result. Do not include citations, bibliographies, or further reading; keep the prompt self-contained.
