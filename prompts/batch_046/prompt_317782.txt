I am drafting a novel that moves between a marine ecology laboratory, a regulatory agency, and a clinical genetics unit, and in the course of my research I have observed a recurring pattern in the way scientific categories travel across disciplinary boundaries. Within its home field, a term is typically anchored to a precise operational boundary—perhaps a specific chemical concentration that defines a pollutant, or a mutation frequency that defines a pathogenic variant—because that boundary tracks a causal mechanism the discipline can manipulate or predict. Once the term is exported into an adjacent field, however, the underlying mechanism is often absent or significantly diluted; nevertheless, the boundary is retained, not because it continues to carve nature at the same joints, but because it allows different institutions to align measurements, funding streams, and liability frameworks. The category thus retains an authority borrowed from its original causal alignment, even as its grip on the new domain's actual structure loosens. Its defenders resist treating it as a mere convention, insisting that something in nature still underwrites it, yet they also resist revising the boundary to fit the new domain's causal architecture, since such revision would disrupt coordination. I have noticed this with classifications of invasive organisms, endocrine-disrupting chemicals, and subclinical disease states. Is there an established philosophical or sociological concept that captures precisely this dynamic—where a kind's disciplinary authority outlives its causal alignment and is sustained by institutional coordination rather than by consistent joint-carving? How does it differ from straightforward conventionalism, from the idea that classification is shaped by practical interests, and from the general claim that all scientific kinds are theory-laden? Should the literature prove to contain no exact match, I would ask for a coined term with explicit boundary conditions: under what circumstances does a category genuinely track nature across contexts, and under what circumstances does it become this particular form of exported authority?
