ok so i've been thinking about mental health labels and i think i've got a pretty consistent view but my friend said its all over the place. here's where i stand:

1. mental illnesses are real conditions, not just made-up words - people actually suffer
2. i buy Hacking's looping idea: once you call someone autistic or borderline they start acting in ways that fit the label, so the category and the people change each other
3. people should self-diagnose and use whatever label helps them, doctors shouldn't gatekeep everything, especially since online spaces help people spot patterns doctors miss
4. some conditions like schizophrenia are biological natural kinds where looping barely happens, but anxiety and depression are interactive kinds mostly shaped by culture and labels
5. if something is mostly an interactive kind, then its less real in the important sense and shouldn't get the same medical resources or disability support
6. we should drop harmful DSM categories that cause bad looping, even if lots of people currently identify with them
7. looping is way stronger now because of social media - people learn symptoms from videos and then perform them, which makes categories expand faster than research can catch up
