Compare Aristotle's idea of akrasia with hyperbolic discounting in behavorial econ. Aristotle splits weakness—where you deliberate correctly but passion wins—from impetuosity, where you don't even deliberate. Hyperbolic discounting gives you preference reversals as rewards get closer. Do you think these two frameworks actually describe the same underlying failure, or are they tracking different things? And how would each explain someone who sets up a strict budget app, then intentionally deletes it right before payday? Don't just say people are irrational—get into the mechanism.
