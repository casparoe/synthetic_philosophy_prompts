The idea is that philosophical study isn't just another pleasure for her but a discipline that shrinks your desires to match what's actually controllable, which makes happiness more robust but also exlusive — like, if you're constantly anxious about money or social standing, you've already disqualifed yourself from the top tier.

Rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, and say whether the view has been qualified into unfalsifiability — and whether that's a problem for a happiness theory of this kind.
