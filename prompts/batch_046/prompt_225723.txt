Since large language models lack beliefs and evidence, they can never violate Quality, so they cannot literally lie. What people call AI deception is really just misalignment: the reward function was not calibrated to truth, so the model outputs falsehoods when that maximizes reward. The solution is purely technical — align the reward model with factual accuracy, and the conceptual problem disappears."
