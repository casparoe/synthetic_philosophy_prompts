Address what this framework implies for the moral evaluation of practices such as eyestalk ablation in penaeid shrimp and electrical stunning protocols for salmonids; for the design of welfare metrics that do not depend on observable behavioral stress indicators, which are often suppressed in these environments; and for whether regulatory thresholds ought to be graded continuously across aquatic taxa rather than assigned categorically. In your answer, identify the most significant practical and conceptual consequences of adopting this shift, including any tensions it creates with current animal-welfare legislation and with the epistemic limits of assessing subjective states in non-verbal organisms.
