I just finished a long, circular argument with a friend about Einstein’s hole argument: whether a diffeomorphism that is the identity outside a region but permutes points inside really undermines determinism in general relativity, or whether it only shows that spacetime points lack independent reality and must be individuated by field coincidences. Rather than draw another diagram, I want to settle it—provisionally—in verse.

Write a sestina. Use exactly six stanzas of six lines each, followed by a three-line envoi, in blank verse or loose iambic pentameter. The end-words, rotating in the standard sestina pattern, must be: point, field, hole, map, same, other. In stanza one they should appear in that order at the line ends (positions 1 through 6); then continue the rotation 6-1-5-2-4-3 through stanzas two through six, and use all six in the envoi according to the standard closing arrangement.

The form should carry the argument. In each stanza the metric field should remain the same, the hole should remain the same region, but the point assigned inside should shift according to the map, so that what is other inside becomes indistinguishable from same outside—mirroring the diffeomorphism that leaves the field invariant but permutes point-identity. The determinism anxiety should be present: initial data outside the hole seem unable to fix which point is which inside. Then, in the envoi, stage Einstein’s point-coincidence move—where points are only individuated by the intersection of fields—without fully resolving whether determinism is saved or dissolved. Leave the tension open.

The poem must be accurate enough that a philosopher of physics recognizes the hole argument and the point-coincidence response, not merely name-dropping general relativity. Someone who does not know the argument should still find it a coherent poem about absence, labeling, and identity.

Present the poem first. Then, in a section titled "Commentary on the formal choices" (approximately 150–200 words), explain how the sestina’s rotating end-words enact the diffeomorphism, why the envoi is structured to stage the point-coincidence resolution ambiguously, and how the blank-verse rhythm supports the conceptual weight without resolving it.
