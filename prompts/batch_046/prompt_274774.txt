Please prepare me for the objections they will actually raise, ordered by likelihood and damage: phrase each objection in the vocabulary that audience member would use, distinguish the ones that have real analytical force from those that are merely rhetorical, tell me precisely what I should concede up front without surrendering the core claim, identify the one reply that absolutely has to land for the proposal to survive the session, and include the objection I am hoping nobody asks.
