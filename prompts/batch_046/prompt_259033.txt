(4) I am methodologically committed to reflective equilibrium in normative economics: I begin with settled case judgments—minimum wages acceptable, steady-state rent control unacceptable, anti-gouging laws required—and adjust general principles to cohere with them, rather than deriving verdicts from first principles.
