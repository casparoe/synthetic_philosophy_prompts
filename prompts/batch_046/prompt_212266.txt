I am preparing a philosophical profile for a reading group and want to check whether my commitments on moral emotion, cultural plasticity, and moral agency hang together. Here is my position. First, I regard guilt as the superior moral emotion for liberal societies, because it targets specific actions, permits reparation, and treats the offender as an autonomous agent rather than a fixed social type. Second, I hold that shame is structurally more powerful for maintaining collective norms, since it operates through an internalised audience rather than through identification with a harmed victim, making it more effective at enforcing group cohesion. Third, I believe a culture can deliberately engineer its emotional economy—through education, law, and ritual—to shift from a shame-dominant to a guilt-dominant regime, or the reverse, as a matter of collective choice. Fourth, I accept the claim, advanced by some anthropologists and historians of psychology, that the capacity for guilt presupposes a conception of the self as a continuous, bounded moral agent, a conception that is not universal but historically specific to certain post-Enlightenment Western traditions. Fifth, I therefore think that imposing guilt-based institutions on a culture lacking that agentive self-conception will not produce moral repair but rather pathological self-punishment without behavioural correction. Sixth, and finally, I maintain that moral emotions are partly evolved and partly culturally scaffolded, so that evolutionary psychology sets hard constraints on any cultural design; yet I also insist that culture can choose which emotion to privilege largely regardless of evolutionary predisposition. Please audit these commitments. For each pair that is genuinely in tension, state the tension precisely, rate its severity, and suggest the cheapest revision that would restore consistency together with what it costs me. Identify any apparent tensions that dissolve on closer inspection. Then tell me which single revision—giving up or modifying one commitment—would clean up the largest number of conflicts. Write your answer in British English, and be explicit about where a conflict is logical rather than merely empirical.
