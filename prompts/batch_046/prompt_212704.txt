I was having this long, slightly heated argument with my mate after our philosophy of science seminar — we’d had a few pints and got onto anthropic reasoning, which I realise sounds insufferable but bear with me. I ended up defending this hybrid view I’ve been playing with: call it 'contextual self-weighting'. The idea is that your own existence should update your credance toward theories that predict more observers, but only within the region of spacetime that could causally or infomationally interact with you — not across the whole multiverce or some cosmic census. So it borrows from Bostrom’s self-indication assumption but restricts the referance class to what I’m calling your 'epistemic neighbourhood' — observers with overlapping causal histories and shared observational access. I think this dodges the doomsday argument because the local population might still be huge regardless of total cosmic numbers, and it also blocks the presumptuous philosopher, since that philosopher can’t just reason from their existence to a distant theory predicting trillions of unobservable observers. My friend said it was just SIA with ad hoc guardrails, and that I was smuggling in self-sampling (SSA) assumptions through the back door by tying the referance class to causal accessibility. I’m not convinced, but I want to be honest about where this falls apart.

Play it straight: what would count against contextual self-weighting? Which assumptions could fail, what would the world have to look like for each, and which escape routes are live rather than merely logical? I want you to distinguish evidence that would be fatal from evidence that would merely be awkward — say whether a committed defender could absorb the blow by tweaking the 'epistemic neighbourhood' boundary, or whether that tweak collapses the view into standard SSA or SIA. Does the position risk anything at all, or has it been insulated by qualifications? And please, say what would actually change your mind: which argument or consideration would lead you to drop contextual self-weighting entirely, and where is your reasoning here most likely to fail?
