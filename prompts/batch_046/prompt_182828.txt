I'm developing a conceptual taxonomy for a seminar on evolutionary game theory that focuses on a deliberately narrow problem: whether cooperation can stabilize in interactions that are structurally one-shot—no repeated pairing of the same two individuals—yet allow for indirect informational carryover through noisy reputation tags (where maintaining accurate tags carries a fitness cost), spatial clustering of phenotypes, or cross-generational memory of interaction outcomes. The point is to classify theoretical commitments about what counts as genuine repetition versus a functionally equivalent substitute, not to survey all of evolutionary biology.

Please write a decision tree that leads a respondent from the starting commitment that direct, pairwise repetition is absent to one of several named or unnamed positions. Use exactly four sequential nodes, each corresponding to one dimension: (A) Can noisy reputation tags with non-zero maintenance costs substitute for direct repetition in stabilizing cooperation, or does the cost always make defection dominate? (B) Is spatial clustering of cooperative strategies conceptually distinct from temporal iteration, or only its spatial analog? (C) Does stochastic re-matching within a generation, paired with memory of past partners, constitute a form of iteration? (D) Does evolutionary stability of cooperation require agents to have rational expectations of future encounters, or can it emerge solely from differential reproductive success regardless of cognition? At each node, phrase the branching options as concrete answers—not theory names—that someone could choose without knowing which position they lead to, such as yes but only if the error rate falls below a threshold tied to the cost, no because clustering merely displaces repetition into space, or yes but only if the memory span exceeds one generation.

The tree should terminate in six to nine leaves. For each leaf, provide a position label—for example, strict repetitionist, spatial-reciprocity advocate, noisy-tag stabilizer, stochastic-matching pseudo-iterationist, or unnamed—and a single sentence describing its most awkward commitment, especially any commitment that forces the view to deny an intuitive feature of cooperation or to smuggle repetition back in through an unstated mechanism. In addition, identify any logical combinations of answers that the tree allows but that appear unoccupied, briefly diagnosing whether the gap is likely incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored. Finally, flag any pair of leaves that these four dimensions cannot genuinely separate, and keep every node phrased as an answerable claim rather than a reference to named literature.
