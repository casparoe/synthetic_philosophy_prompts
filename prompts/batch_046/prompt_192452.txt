Please also flag any pair of positions your tree cannot cleanly separate, and note any combination of answers that seems logically available yet apparently unoccupied, with a brief diagnosis of whether that gap arises from incoherence, unattractiveness, or mere neglect. I intend to use this for a seminar handout, so the diagnostic questions should be phrased as concrete commitments that a student could answer without already knowing which philosophical label they lead to.
