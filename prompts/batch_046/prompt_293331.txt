The debate between absurdists—who claim human life lacks meaning because the universe provides no transcendent purpose or ultimate justification—and contemporary analytic philosophers of meaning, who argue that objective value-creation and rational engagement can render a life meaningful without cosmic grounding, has remained largely static for decades. Diagnose why this debate has stalled.
