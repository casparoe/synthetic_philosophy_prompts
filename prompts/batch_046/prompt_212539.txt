Specifically, consider whether a presentist is forced to treat such models as merely instrumental fictions rather than literally true representations; whether an eternalist can coherently account for the practical asymmetry between intervening on future nodes versus past nodes; and what either position implies about the ontological status of counterfactual future states that appear in the graph but may never become actual. In your response, clearly separate what is broadly settled in the philosophy of time from what remains genuinely contested among specialists, and avoid presenting any contested interpretation as if it were the consensus view. Please write in American English.
