I heard it used recently to dismiss the idea that one side in an arms-race or price-war scenario could simply choose to stop escalating without being exploited. It sounds like several different mechanisms have been collapsed into one phrase, and I'm not convinced any single precise claim actually supports that dismissal.

For each reading, say whether it is a trivial restatement, a substantive and defensible claim, or false; and indicate which ones genuinely entail that unilateral de-escalation is impossible, versus which merely make it costly, unlikely, or reputation-damaging.
