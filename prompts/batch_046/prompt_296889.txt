Tomorrow I am delivering the opening remarks at the retirement symposium for Dr. Mara Lin, who spent three decades in conflict mediation; roughly half the audience are moral philosophers who will catch the framing, and half are family and colleagues who will not. I want the philosophical point to do real structural work: please write the remarks as exactly five brief spectator-perspectives on Mara's career, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion, arranged so that they remain sympathetically distinct and never resolve into one authoritative judgment. Each part should be accessible to non-philosophers, but together they should enact the idea that sympathy is irreducibly plural rather than a single internal voice.
