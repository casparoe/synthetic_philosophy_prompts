ok here's roughly where I landed on why promisses bind and I want a consistency check before I present it. (1) I think promises mainly generate obligations through the reliance and expectations they create in the promisee — if they never heard it or never formed any expectation, there's basically no binding force. (2) But I also think it's wrong to break a promise even if the promisee totally forgot about it and never relied on it. (3) The real source is autonomus will-creation: you're generating a normative reason ex nihilo just by deciding to bind yourself. (4) Even promises extracted through heavy emotional manipulation still bind, since you technically said the words and your will was still in the loop. (5) Catastrophic harm to a third party can justify breaking a promise, but that doesn't erase the obligation — it's just outweighed.
