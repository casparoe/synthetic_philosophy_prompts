jordan says now we're just counting imaginary branches. if you dig deep enough everyone always has some remote counterfactual, and PAP becomes trivially true but useless. casey is saving PAP by watering it down to "there exists some counterfactual branch somewhere," which isn't what "could've done otherwise" meant.
