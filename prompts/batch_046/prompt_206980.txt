Apply the procedure to two clients: one who wants to move from Order to Resolution despite repeated failures in keeping his appointments, and one who reports zero failures in Silence but whose colleagues still find her brusque, indicating a gap in Sincerity; include the unwelcome verdict the procedure delivers in at least one case. Present your answer in British English as exactly five numbered points, each one to two sentences long, with no introduction or conclusion: point 1 should state the ordered audit questions, point 2 the notation and threshold rules, point 3 the hard stop conditions and value-deferral points, point 4 the first application, and point 5 the second application with its unwelcome result.
