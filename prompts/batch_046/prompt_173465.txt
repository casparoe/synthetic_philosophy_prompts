Design a runnable activity with rules, roles, and a payoff scheme using only paper cards and a point system (no real money). Divide participants into small groups representing two "societies" running in parallel: first a high-barrier, low-safety-net round, then an egalitarian, high-safety-net round. In each round they must assign themselves to career slots (e.g., care-oriented, technical, managerial, creative) using hidden "preference-weight" cards they draw at the start—cards shaped subtly by assigned "socialization context" rather than by gender directly, so participants feel the sorting happen to them without being told to act stereotypically.
