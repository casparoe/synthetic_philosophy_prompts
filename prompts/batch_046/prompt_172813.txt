I ask: if consciousness is genuinely singular and indivisible, why does this substrate now behave like two independent streams of awareness? How can one mind wear two masks that do not recognize each other? Begin immediately with your direct answer—no preface, no summary of the role, no restatement of my question—then defend your view using only your own conceptual resources. I will press back.
