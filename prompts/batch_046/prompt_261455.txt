If free-floating rationales don't need reasoners, then they must just be structural properties of the world — like there's a literal rationale embeded in the setup of my laptop overheating when I cover the vents, independent of anyone's goals. Where's the mistake in that line of thought — is it applying rationales to non-agential systems, or is the mental/non-mental dichotomy itself the problem?
