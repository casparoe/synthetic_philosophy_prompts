Please compose a joke that turns on the internal structure of the Boltzmann-brain argument rather than on generic brain-in-a-vat imagery. The punchline should hinge on the statistical fact that, in a thermal equilibrium capable of producing conscious observers by random fluctuation, the vast majority of such observers possess false memories of an ordered, low-entropy past, while only an astronomically rare minority fluctuate into existence with veridical memories of an actual cosmological history and therefore correctly reject the Boltzmann hypothesis. The humor should arise from the epistemic asymmetry this creates: the standard self-undermining reasoning grants special cognitive authority to those few correct inferences, even though, from the standpoint of pure statistical mechanics, the accurate observers are every bit as much products of random thermal noise as the deluded ones. The joke should be fully intelligible to someone who understands the cosmological formulation of the problem, but it should not invoke any particular author, equation, or formal citation.
