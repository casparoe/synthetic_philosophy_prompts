She claims this dissolves the bad-lot objection without requiring comparative evaluation among currently available hypotheses, and she replaces the traditional simplicity-likelihood trade-off with a distinction between explanatory depth, understood as vertical integration across explanatory scales, and explanatory breadth, understood as horizontal coverage of anomalies. She is also known for answering circularity worries—namely, that identifying structure already requires explanatory judgment—by appealing to an iterative bootstrap model of model refinement.
