After a long and somewhat heated argument with a friend about whether Weber’s Protestant Ethic thesis actually demonstrates that culture can function as an independent cause, or whether it merely redescribes economic change in the vocabulary of values without supplying a mechanism, I want you to write a sustained satirical piece. Compose a deadpan bureaucratic policy brief—approximately 600 words—formatted as a formal memorandum from the fictional “Bureau of Elective Affinities,” a division of an invented Ministry of Cultural Causation. Its stated purpose is to propose a national economic recovery plan based on deliberately engineering a new secular asceticism called the “Ethic of Deep Focus,” modeled explicitly on Weberian analysis: an ideal-typical value-system that bears an elective affinity (Wahlverwandtschaft) with a specific economic form, produces unintended macro-level consequences, and dismisses material conditions as mere background. Write in an unbroken, true-believing voice; never wink or break character. The satire should exaggerate real methodological features of explaining with values: the retrospective construction of an ideal type that selectively highlights meanings, the assertion of causal power without specifying how values become material forces, the circular fitting of outcomes to post-hoc value descriptions, and the resulting unfalsifiability. Invent a small-town case study—say, Millbrook adopting the ethic and recording a 3% GDP increase—and describe the causal chain entirely in terms of intentionality, meaning, and unintended consequence, with no mention of labor markets, capital flows, or institutional change. The target should be the practice of using postulated value-systems to explain macroeconomic outcomes, not Weber himself or any named living scholar. Do not include citations, footnotes, bibliographies, references to page numbers, or appeals to primary sources. The piece should remain readable to someone with no background in sociology, but the conceptual jokes—especially the absent mechanism and the circular explanation—should land precisely for someone who recognizes the machinery being pushed to absurdity.
