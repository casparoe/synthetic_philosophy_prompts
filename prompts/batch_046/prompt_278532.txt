There's this summary floating around the rationalist-adjacent philosophy spaces about Kripke's origin essentialism that I'm trying to check. I feel like there's a conflation happening between essential properties of origin and essential properties of development, and I'm not sure Kripke actually talks about IVF or adoption at all. But I might be wrong. Is this summary fair?
