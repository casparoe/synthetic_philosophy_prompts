Jonas said that misses the point: the correlation is the execution environment, because the logical structure of your choice is the mechanism by which the other world gets affected. Maya said if that's true then every rational agent in every logically possible world is constantly "trading" with every other, which makes the concept vacuous. Jonas said no, it's constrained by actual algorithmic similarity, not bare logical possibility.
