I am currently drafting a literary novel in which a protagonist, raised without knowledge of her conception, discovers through a sealed genetic archive that she developed from one of thousands of synthetic embryos cultivated in a closed-loop bioreactor, her specific zygote selected by an algorithm rather than by any biological parents she knows. The narrative architecture depends on whether she is essentially the same person she would have been had the algorithm selected a different sperm-and-egg pairing from the same synthetic batch, or whether her identity is tied to that exact origin. Before I commit to the metaphysical rules governing her world, I want to understand which open questions within origin essentialism actually carry weight for this kind of case, and which are scholastic distractions that would not change the story’s coherence.

Specifically, I am trying to determine whether the debate’s most consequential unresolved lines concern the modal scope of origin properties—whether essential origins are fixed across all possible worlds or only within a restricted accessibility relation—the ontological status of technologically mediated versus naturally reproduced gametes and whether origin essentialism requires a causal chain that passes through biological reproduction rather than synthetic mediation, or the implications for personal identity over time, particularly whether a being that develops from a different initial cell-line in the same bioreactor counts as the same continuing entity. Among these, which dependencies are genuinely upstream? If one were settled, which others would become moot or merely derivative? And which lines of inquiry—perhaps those connecting origin essentialism to theories of essential properties in artifacts, or to the ethics of reproductive technology—are genuinely open and tractable for a conceptual treatment that does not depend on empirical genetics?

Please defend a disciplined ordering: identify the two or three open questions that deserve priority if one’s aim is not to survey literature but to build a coherent metaphysical framework capable of supporting a fictional scenario like the one I have described, and explain what makes the remaining questions less urgent or less central. I am not seeking a reading list or citations; I need an actionable conceptual agenda that treats origin essentialism as a live philosophical tool rather than a museum piece, with an honest account of where the theory is silent and where my invented bioreactor case either illuminates or breaks its standard formulations.
