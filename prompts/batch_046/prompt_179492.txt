People defending Finland's conscription model often compare it to public school — universal, mandatory, shapes citizens — while they say Sweden's shift to professional forces is like hiring private security. Can you lay out the mapping: what's the teacher, the curriculum, the dropout option? Then where it breaks — can citizens actually "graduate" from defense duty in Finland? And does the school anology smuggle in the idea that citizenship requires this specific service, while the security-firm framing unfairly makes Sweden sound like it's outsourcing violence? Does either analogy actually support the claim that conscription creates democratic accountability, or are they just carrying the conclusion?
