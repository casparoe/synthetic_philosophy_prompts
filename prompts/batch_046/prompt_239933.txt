I take the view that Nietzsche's eternal recurrence is best reconstructed as a modal filter on valuation rather than a cosmological claim or a mere psychological exercise. Specifically, it demands that any genuinely affirmed value must survive counterfactual repetition across all worlds structurally isomorphic to ours with respect to the causal laws and initial conditions that generate it. This collapses the usual distinction between de re and de dicto affirmation: to will the recurrence of an event is not to approve of its repetition as a type, but to approve of the exact token in every isomorphic world, which eliminates any value that depends on uniqueness, irreversibility, or open-ended becoming for its normative force. Amor fati, on this reading, is not an emotional achievement but the logical residue of values that pass the filter, and the filter applies not only to particular actions but to the evaluative framework itself—any system that requires linear, non-cyclical time for its coherence fails a priori.

What would have to be true for this position to be wrong? I want an honest map of its exposure: which arguments from the philosophy of personal identity across time, modal realism, or axiology would undermine the collapse of de re and de dicto affirmation here; whether there are coherent values that depend on singularity or irreversibility and can still be affirmed under cyclical conditions without contradiction; and whether the filter is so stringent that it eliminates not just rival values but the very conditions for evaluation, rendering the view self-refuting rather than selectively demanding. Which defeaters are decisive, which are merely awkward, and which would defenders of this modal-filter reading concede as genuine costs? Has the view been qualified—say, by restricting the filter to "core" values or by allowing structural isomorphism to vary in minor details—into near-unfalsifiability, and if so, is that insulation a defect for a normative criterion of this kind? Rank the vulnerabilities from fatal to tolerable, and say what it would actually take to move a committed holder of this view.
