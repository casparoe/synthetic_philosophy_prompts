My thesis is what I'm calling the valence-asymmetry argument: if panpsychism is true even in a weak form, quantum field fluctuations carry minimal affective valence—mostly negative because of how vacuum energy is structured—so every high-energy collision in an accelerator produces astronomically many micro-instances of suffering. Not metaphorical suffering, actual minimal negative experience distributed across the field. That means there's a direct ethical case, analogus to animal-welfare reasoning, for restricting beam energies or adding moratorium protocols—not for ecological or safety reasons, but for pan-sentient harm reduction.
