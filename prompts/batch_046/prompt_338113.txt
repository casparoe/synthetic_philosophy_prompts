On the other side is a “pragmatic revivalist” who accepts Quine’s demolition of Carnap’s reduction-sentence account and of synonymy-based definitions, but insists that a functional analytic–synthetic distinction survives in the form of entrenched framework principles—those nodes in the web that are revised only under extreme theoretical pressure—without requiring unreduced synonymy or a first-philosophy foundation.

Please structure the output as follows.
