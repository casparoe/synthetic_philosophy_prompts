I am a novelist constructing an extended narrative cycle in which music operates not as atmospheric ornament but as a metaphysical event in Schopenhauer's strict sense: a direct objectification of the Will, irreducible to the representation of Ideas that governs the other arts. Because I must apply this principle repeatedly across scenes of composition, audition, and failed listening, I require a reusable decision procedure my drafting process can run without consulting theory each time. Please design a step-by-step operational procedure with the following structure: first, the ordered questions I should ask of any scene in which music appears; second, the criteria that distinguish a genuine Schopenhauerian musical event from a scene in which music functions merely as emotional signifier or representational backdrop; third, the precise point—grounded in Schopenhauer's hierarchy of the arts—where melody expresses the direct analog of willing, harmony expresses the grades of the Will's objectification, and formal temporal structures govern the expression's temporal shape; fourth, the conditions under which the procedure mandates revision or excision of the scene; fifth, the explicit points where Schopenhauer's metaphysics is silent and artistic judgment must take over; and sixth, the limits of the procedure—where it will misfire if applied mechanically. Apply the procedure to three concrete draft scenarios: (a) a protagonist hears a symphony and recognizes in its melodic line the direct image of her own striving; (b) a folk musician performs a song that expresses universal sorrow rather than particular grief; (c) a negotiation scene in which jazz plays in the background while characters betray one another. In at least one case, the procedure should produce an answer I will find unwelcome—specifically, that the scene treats music as representation and must be rewritten or removed. Deliver a committed analysis: take a definite position on how rigorously Schopenhauer's distinction between music and the other arts should constrain fictional representation, defend that position with reasons, and do not retreat to a neutral survey of options. Where judgment is unavoidable, say so plainly; elsewhere, decide.
