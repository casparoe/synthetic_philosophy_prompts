I just spent several hours arguing with a friend about whether knowing how is genuinely distinct from knowing that, and we kept circling the same familiar examples—riding a bicycle, speaking a native language, improvising jazz—without ever isolating the precise point of divergence. Leo should oppose her by contending that every case of apparent irreducibility can be reconstructed as knowing-that under a practical mode of presentation, drawing on centered-worlds representations and embodied predictive models that are in principle fully explicit, and that the so-called epistemic friction is nothing more than a gap between representational content and motor execution. The dialogue should avoid strawman versions of either Rylean anti-intellectualism or extreme propositional reductionism, and it should not invoke bibliographies, reading lists, or citations.
