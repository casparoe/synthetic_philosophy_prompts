My rough notes:
1. Nietzsche traces guilt to the creditor-debtor relation, then shows how internalized cruelty becomes bad conscience—the soul attacking itself.

4. The punishment is self-imposed optimization—less sleep, stricter diet—to pay back the data-debt.
5. This guilt has no moral content; it's pure statistical failure, yet structurally mirrors the ascetic ideal.
