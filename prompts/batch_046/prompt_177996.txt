The distinction between event-agency and structural-agency matters here: Tambora is an event-agent because it's a specific turning point, while long-term climate regimes have structural-agency that shapes possibilities over centuries. The danger of determinism is really just a false dichotomy where people think either nature or society is in charge. Actually, if we follow the distributed-agency view from recent environmental historiography, non-humans and humans are always co-producers, so determinism in a weak form is unavoidable: every historical outcome is partly ecological. So the real mistake is hiding human choices behind natural forces, not giving nature too much causal weight."
