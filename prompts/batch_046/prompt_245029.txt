For my senior-year philosophy seminar project, I am drafting an adversarial-collaboration document and need your help writing it. I want the joint statement that a committed Humean regularist and a committed anti-Humean governing-relation theorist could both sign after good-faith collaboration on whether lawhood survives what I will call the Closed Pocket scenario: a hypothetical cosmos observationally identical to ours except for one permanently causally isolated region where energy is not conserved, with no causal influence on the rest of the mosaic. Please produce a committed, non-hedged statement that takes definite positions rather than laying out options neutrally.

Structure it exactly as follows. First, a numbered section of agreed claims that both sides can endorse without reservation. These must include: (a) a shared, precise description of the Closed Pocket and its logical possibility; (b) agreement on what any adequate account of lawhood should explain, such as predictive success, counterfactual support, and the law-accident distinction; (c) explicit mutual acknowledgment that neither view is self-contradictory; and (d) agreement on the specific scientific and philosophical questions the Closed Pocket raises.

Second, present a disagreements table with columns for the precise point of disagreement, the crux it depends on, and—where both sides agree an argument or case is relevant—the predicted verdict of each side recorded in advance. The disagreements should cover at least: whether the Closed Pocket reveals a suspended governing relation or merely a local exception-law; whether lawhood requires modal force beyond actual regularity; and whether the Humean distinction between law and cosmic coincidence can be maintained non-circularly inside the pocket. For each crux, identify the sub-question that, if settled, would genuinely move at least one party, and exclude purely verbal disputes.

Third, include a brief meta-section in which each side jointly identifies the weakest point in its own position that the collaboration has exposed—each naming its own vulnerability, explaining why it does not defeat the view, and stating what result in the Closed Pocket case would cause it to revise.

Write from the perspective of a high school student who has engaged with the standard literature but wants to avoid generic summaries. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations. Do not place any philosopher’s words in quotation marks; paraphrase all specific views without quotation marks. Make the Closed Pocket do real structural work rather than serving as decoration, and ensure the document reads as if both parties have actually put their names to it.
