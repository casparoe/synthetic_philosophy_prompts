Predict her verdict on this case; identify whether her track record fits a biostatistical naturalism, a harm-based normativism, a hybrid dysfunction-plus-context view, or another principle; point to the closest analogues in her earlier rulings; and propose the single further case that would best distinguish the two leading interpretations.
