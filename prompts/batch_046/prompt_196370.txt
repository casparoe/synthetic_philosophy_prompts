For our final session, I would like to run a structured in-class simulation—approximately fifteen philosophy majors, ninety minutes, in a seminar room with movable chairs—designed so that participants feel the tension between causal reasoning and evidential reasoning in this noisy-commitment framework before anyone names the problem.
