The debate has stalled for months: everone agrees on the behavior, everyone agrees concepts are hard to pin-down, but nobody budges. Is this actually about octopus minds, or is it verbal slippage -- 'copycat' meaning one thing to the mechanism folks and another to the representationalists? Or are we answering different questions under one heading, like one about neural wiring and one about phenomenology? I want an honest diagnosis: substantive, verbal, hidden empirical, or bedrock intuition?
