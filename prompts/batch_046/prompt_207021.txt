Restate the computationalist claim that the brain literally computes algorithms in the vocabulary of continuous dynamical systems -- state spaces, attractors, flow equations, no discrete symbol manipulation -- and tell me what doesnt survive. Flag any part of the original debate that cant be expressed in continuous dynamics terms, and say whether thats a real gap in the framework or just philosophers using extra vocabulary they dont actually need.
