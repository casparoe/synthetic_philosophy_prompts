This framing must do real structural work—the speech should not be able to fall back on conventional frontier or destiny rhetoric without first addressing the consent problem, and it should acknowledge the engineers' practical concerns without letting them override the moral asymmetry between Earth-based decision-makers and those who will live with the consequences. The language should be accessible to non-specialists but precise enough that experts recognize the seriousness of the ethical claim. After the speech, please include a brief note stating what specific argument or evidence would weaken this framing—where the reasoning is most likely to fail, and what consideration might lead me to a different verdict.
