Write a dialogue between a spacetime substantivalist and a relationist about Newton’s bucket, centered on a specific symmetry-breaking problem: in a universe where distant matter is arranged with perfect rotational symmetry about the bucket, every axis through the center is relationally equivalent, yet the water’s concavity singles out exactly one axis. Both speakers must be equally competent, respond directly to the other’s point about local neighbor-relations versus manifold dependence, and avoid generic restatements of Mach’s principle or strawman caricatures. Keep the exchange tight and focused on whether relational dynamics alone can determine an axis of rotation.
