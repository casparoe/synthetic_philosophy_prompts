Here is my opening challenge, built around a case I have constructed to press the boundary between deference and rational ambiguity. Yet if I actually maintain the imprecise spread [0.62, 0.78], I seem to violate deference to the objective chance; and if I forcibly collapse my credence to the point 0.7, I seem to suppress rationally warranted ambiguity without adequate evidential reason.
