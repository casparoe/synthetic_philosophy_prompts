Rather than requesting a standard summary, I want a sustained satirical piece that forces the model to operationalize these commitments through an invented bureaucratic framework.

Write a deadpan audit report—approximately 700 words—issued by the fictional International Council for Sincere Transmission and applying the invented Tolstoyan Emotional Epidemiology Protocol. For each artwork, assign metric scores, identify specific violations of sincerity, moral direction, or accessibility, and recommend certification, quarantine, or destruction. The satire should remain entirely deadpan—no ironic asides, no winking—so that a reader familiar with Tolstoy recognizes that the exaggeration is fair and a reader unfamiliar can still follow the bureaucratic logic. Do not include bibliographies, citations, references to specific scholars, or requests for further reading; write purely in the administrative voice of the audit.
