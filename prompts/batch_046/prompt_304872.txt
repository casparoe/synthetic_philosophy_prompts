The analogy is used to argue that all platform-mediated recognition is deficient in the same way. Give me the explicit correspondence—what plays the master, the slave, the struggle-to-the-death, and the failed recognition—then the points where the platform picture breaks, the specifically Hegelian features it smuggles in that social media lacks, and whether the “hollow recognition” conclusion survives once the disanalogies are visible. Respond as exactly five numbered points, each one to two sentences long, with no introduction or conclusion, and if the analogy fails propose a closer one.
