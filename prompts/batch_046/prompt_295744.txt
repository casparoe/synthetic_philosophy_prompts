Write a short story about an archivist who finds a handwritten diary that describes her own act of finding it, except the diarist insists she is left-handed, which she is, yet every time she reads that line her dominant hand feels like it flips. She starts adding her own entries, and they begin describing her actions a few minutes before she does them - not prophecy, but because the notebook is part of a self-referential loop where the 'I' doing the writing is literally the same symbol being rewritten, kind of like hofstadter's idea of the self as a strange loop. Let it get moody and slightly absurd, maybe she tries to break the loop by writing in the third person but her hand automatically corrects it back to 'I'. You can keep it loose, dont worry about scientific accuracy, and feel free to include a small typo like writting 'recieve' that never gets fixed.
