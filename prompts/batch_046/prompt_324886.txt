As a novelist observing human disagreement with clinical detachment, I recently listened to two colleagues—both trained in physics—debate at length whether the pessimistic meta-induction genuinely undermines scientific realism. Their exchange unfolded in five distinct moves. (1) The first speaker argued that the PMI is self-defeating: it relies on inductive inference about the fate of past scientific theories, yet its conclusion is that such inductive inferences are systematically unreliable. (2) The second speaker replied that this conflates object-level science with meta-scientific historiography; the PMI draws on the actual record of abandoned ontologies—phlogiston, caloric, luminiferous ether—not on an inductive law, so it is not vulnerable to its own conclusion. (3) The first speaker then objected that the PMI exaggerates discontinuity by neglecting structural retention: the mathematical structures of Newtonian mechanics survive intact as limiting cases within both general relativity and quantum mechanics, suggesting that theory replacement is not total ontological erasure. (4) The second speaker conceded the formal continuity but denied its realist significance, observing that the Lorentz transformations preserved certain equations of ether theory while the ether itself was discarded; without an independent, non-circular criterion for approximate truth, retention of formalism can always be redescribed as mere instrumental success. (5) The first speaker closed by charging that this renders the PMI circular: one classifies past theories as false solely because their posited entities are absent from present ones, then uses that very classification pattern to forecast the abandonment of current entities, offering no separate reason to think that the ontology of mature science enjoys special stability. Please adjudicate each of the five moves in order, rating each from clearly mistaken to clearly right and offering brief justification, and in your final entry identify what both interlocutors overlook. Deliver your response as a numbered list of exactly five items, each comprising one or two sentences, with neither introduction nor conclusion.
