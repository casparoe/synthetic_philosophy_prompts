So to be genuinely responsible, a person would have to be causa sui — the cause of themselves — which is impossible. From this, Strawson concludes that all practices of blame and praise rest on an error: since nobody truly deserves anything, reactive attitudes like resentment and gratitude are irrational holdovers, and any compatibilist attempt to save responsibility by redefining it merely evades the core problem rather than solving it."
