Compose a rigorous specification of the desiderata that any theory of tangled hierarchies and self-referential representation—particularly those modeled on the structural dynamics exemplified by Escher's lithograph of two hands drawing one another—must fulfill to be considered conceptually adequate. The specification should address four clusters of requirements. First, explanatory coverage: the theory must account for how a representational system can include itself within its own domain of representation without collapsing into vicious circularity or infinite regress, and it must explain why certain hierarchical levels appear functionally inviolate despite the presence of cross-level feedback. Second, preserved distinctions: it must maintain a clear boundary between representational entanglement, where a system's model of itself is genuinely embedded within the system's operative framework, and mere recursive rule-application; it must also distinguish a structurally inviolate meta-level from a merely contingent or temporarily stable observational standpoint. Third, formal consistency: state the conditions under which the theory can remain compatible with established results in the logic of self-reference—specifically, whether it can avoid the consequences of diagonalization arguments without abandoning completeness of self-modeling, and whether it requires deterministic closure or can accommodate stochastic, precision-weighted hierarchical updates. Fourth, joint satisfiability: identify any subset of these desiderata that appear jointly unsatisfiable, with particular focus on whether the preservation of a strict inviolate level is compatible with full self-referential transparency. For each desideratum, provide the underlying conceptual or structural motivation, and classify each as either non-negotiable, where failure undermines the theory's coherence, or camp-specific, such as preferences for deterministic versus probabilistic architectures, or for ontological versus purely representational readings of hierarchy. Finally, briefly assess—without constructing an exhaustive scorecard—how a Hofstadter-style strange-loop framework, a hierarchical predictive-processing model with self-modeling precision weights, and a category-theoretic account using self-referential endofunctors fare against your criteria, noting where each succeeds and where it faces pressure from the unsatisfiable subsets you have identified.
