Please write the exam question itself, a model answer of about 500 words, and a marking scheme that separates an A answer—which must isolate the substrate-autonomy condition, show exactly where the push-back bites, and defend one consistent position without collapsing into functionalist hand-waving—from a B answer that merely describes the careenium-simmball distinction without testing its stability. The marking scheme should name the specific moves required for full marks, note the common mistake of conflating correlation with downward causation, and assign point values that total 20.
