Crucially, they must engage with each other's actual arguments: the Russellian should address the descriptivist's appeal to pragmatic domain restriction rather than dismissing it, and the descriptivist should respond to the Russellian's claim that uniqueness is a semantic rather than merely contextual feature. I do not need citations or a bibliography; I need the exchange to feel technically precise but accessible enough for a high school audience that has read Russell's classic essay on descriptions in excerpt. Please keep the dialogue balanced, with no strawman, and ensure the back-and-forth turns on whether uniqueness failure should be treated as a false presupposition or as a shift in evaluation domain.
