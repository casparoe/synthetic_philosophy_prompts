(4) which version of the claim the colleague was most likely relying on in the seminar context.

Make sure to include at least one reading that turns on an epistemic limitation (our inability to detect sentience), at least one that turns on a metaphysical claim about the structure of consciousness (e.g., global workspace integration, self-modeling, or predictive processing), and at least one normative claim about how moral consideration should be distributed across degrees. Do not provide a bibliography or citation list; work from conceptual analysis alone. Present the menu clearly so it could be taken back to the seminar for clarification.
