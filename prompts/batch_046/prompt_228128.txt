I've been noticing this pattern in arguments about consent and I can't tell if its already got a name. Like, someone agrees to surgery after the doctor lists risks in technical terms. Patient's competent, not coerced, doctor didn't lie - but the patient is picturing "success" as running marathons again while the doctor means "tumor is out." They agree, but they're not in the same mental frame. I saw something like it with a roommate too - she lent her car because "family emergency" meant something different to her than to the guy borrowing it. No lies, no threats, both adults. Is there an established term for consent that checks all the usual boxes but fails because the two sides are using the same words with mismatched conceptual frames? If there is, how's it different from hidden deception? And if not, what'd you call it, and where exactly does it stop being deception and start being this other thing?
