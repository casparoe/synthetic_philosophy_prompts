I serve on the Municipal Heritage and Design Review Board for the Riverside District. Our district charter, adopted in 2018, requires that any exterior alteration to a residential facade must preserve the shared visual narrative that binds residents into a recognizable community. The charter was originally justified by reference to collective identity expressed through built environment, but it does not define whose narrative counts, nor does it address climate-adaptation technologies.

A homeowner in the district, Ms. Elena Vasquez, has applied to replace her traditional wooden shutters with integrated photovoltaic glass panels that generate electricity but visibly alter the streetscape. She contends that climate urgency and individual property rights should override collective aesthetic continuity, and she notes that several recent immigrant households in the district do not identify with the historical visual references the charter protects. The board is divided: some members defend the charter as safeguarding a genuine communitarian good, while others regard it as an illegitimate subordination of liberal individual autonomy to a contested and potentially exclusionary conception of community.

We must issue a written decision memo before the vote scheduled for the 15th of next month. Please draft the board's formal opinion memo recommending either approval, denial, or approval with conditions. The memo should state a concrete recommendation together with any specific condition or time frame, provide reasoning that both the applicant and dissenting board members could genuinely respond to, and include an honest, serious treatment of the strongest consideration on the losing side rather than a strawman. The memo should engage directly with the tension between communitarian and liberal commitments, without merely restating the charter's legal text.

After the memo, please provide a brief structured summary in JSON format with exactly these fields, each containing a concise string:
- recommendation: the bottom-line decision
- condition_or_timeframe: the concrete limit, requirement, or deadline attached, if any
- strongest_communitarian_argument: the best justification for restricting the alteration in the name of collective identity
- strongest_liberal_argument: the best objection grounded in individual autonomy, property rights, or pluralism
- philosophical_frame: the central conceptual tension the memo treats as decisive
- unresolved_ambiguity: one feature of the case that the philosophical arguments leave genuinely unsettled
