Write a satirical job posting, in the deadpan voice of an actual LinkedIn listing, for a fictional consultancy hiring a "Senior Moral Agent (Causa Sui Track)". The ad should apply Galen Strawson's basic argument as if it were standard HR screening: candidates must prove they authored their own mental constitution, submit an infinite regress of prior self-choices that bottoms out in themselves, and disclaim any influence from heredity or enviroment. In the "key responsabilities" section show how ordinary ethical obligations—keeping promises, feeling guilt, apologizing—collapse once you take the argument seriously. Include one invented bureaucratic form, the Self-Authorship Audit Trail (SAAT), which tracks every mental trait back to a prior act of self-creation. The satire should exaggerate the real structure of the argument (the regress, the determinism/randomness horns, the impossibility of being causa sui) rather than invent fake objections, and it should stay readable to someone who hasn't studied philosophy. About 600 words, contiuous prose only—no headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables—like an email you'd forward to a friend. Don't ask for citations or reading lists; a model should be able to do this from general philosophical knowledge.
