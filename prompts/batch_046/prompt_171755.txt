I'm preparing a long-form feature on what Daniel Dennett and others have called competence without comprehension — the strange inversion in which effective performance precedes, and sometimes never requires, any internal grasp of what is being accomplished. The canonical philosophical pair is Darwin's evolutionary inversion, in which natural selection assembles adaptive design across generations without foresight or representation, and Turing's mechanistic inversion, in which a physical system executes intelligent procedures without semantic awareness. I want to test whether this framing can be extended rigorously or whether it collapses once applied beyond its original domains.

Please rank the following four phenomena on a scale of 1 to 10 according to how radically they separate successful performance from internal comprehension, and place each in a tier — S, A, B, or C — with a one-sentence rationale for its position:

1. Darwin's evolutionary inversion: adaptive complexity accumulated over generations without any mind representing the problem being solved.
2. Turing's computational inversion: correct symbol-manipulation yielding intelligent outputs without interpretive understanding within the mechanism.
3. The immune-system inversion: clonal selection generating pathogen-specific antibody defense without an internal representational model of the invader.
4. The distributed-market inversion: price signals coordinating global resource allocation without any agent, human or artificial, comprehending the system's overall state.

Then, in a separate boundary-check analysis, tell me whether comparing these four is philosophically coherent or whether it commits a category error. Specifically: does Darwin's case depend on temporal displacement of competence across generations, while Turing's depends on architectural absence within a single device? Does the immune case differ because it is bounded within one organism rather than distributed across populations or machines? And is there a meaningful sense in which the market case involves no discrete mechanism at all, only an aggregate pattern that we retrospectively describe as competent?

Please do not include bibliographies, citations, or further-reading lists; I need only the numerical scores, the tier assignments with rationales, and the conceptual boundary analysis.
