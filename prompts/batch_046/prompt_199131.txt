If Hume was alive today and watched reddit karma norms evolve—like how users coordinate on what's downvote-worthy without anyone writing the rules—would he call that a Lewis convention? Hume talked about conventions arising from mutual expectation, but Lewis needs a coordination problem with salience and common knowledge. Karma is asymmetric: downvoting costs the voter nothing but hurts the poster. So is there actually a coordination problem being solved, or is it just decentralized punishment? I'm writing about spontaneous norm evolution and want to know if Lewis's framework even applies here, or if Hume's account of custom does better.
