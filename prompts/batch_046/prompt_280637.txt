I'm working on a piece about whether AI image generators are basically propoganda machines with extra steps, and my editor threw Du Bois at me — that old idea that all art is propaganda. I keep seeing it used to mean everything from artists always having hidden political bias to the claim that aesthetic beauty is literally brainwashing. Can you separate out the precise theses that one-liner could stand for? For instance: is it descriptive (all art inevitably carries ideology) or prescriptive (artists ought to push a cause)? Is there a version about intentionality versus unconscious effect? And how does each reading hold up against something like an abstract Bach fugue or a minamalist sculpture? I want a menu of interpretations — which ones are tautolgies, which are substantive and defensable, which are false — and specifically which version people are smuggling in when they invoke Du Bois in debates about generative art. Don't need a biblography, just the conceptual map. Also, is there a defensable middle ground where form itself becomes ideological without the artist planning it?
