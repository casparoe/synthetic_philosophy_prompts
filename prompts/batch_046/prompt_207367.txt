After a long and circular argument with a friend last night, I have come to hold a sharpened version of the Basic Argument usually associated with Galen Strawson. I do not merely claim that determinism undermines moral responsibility; I claim that the regress of mental-state authorship is fatal even under indeterminism, because any mental event—whether causally determined or uncaused—that is supposed to be the true source of an action either traces back to prior states for which the agent is not ultimately responsible, or occurs without any rational connection to the agent's prior character. I extend this to normative self-formation: one cannot be ultimately responsible for the very values, reasons-responsive mechanisms, or evaluative frameworks one uses to judge one's actions, since those mechanisms themselves have origins outside true self-authorship. My friend pressed me with agent-causal power, with appeals to emergent self-organization in complex neural networks, and with the charge that I am demanding an impossible standard of being causa sui. I want an honest map of what would actually count against this position—not a generic survey of compatibilist replies, but the specific conceptual distinctions, empirical findings, or formal results that should move me. For instance, would a rigorous model of top-down self-modulation in prefrontal circuits undermine the claim that all mental origins are external to the agent? Would evidence that patients with certain frontal lesions retain rational control over actions despite lacking a coherent autobiographical narrative show that unified self-authorship is unnecessary for responsibility? Which of these defeaters would defenders of the Basic Argument themselves concede as damaging, and which would they dismiss as changing the subject by substituting rational control for ultimate authorship? I also want a direct assessment of whether the view has been qualified into unfalsifiability—if every proposed instance of self-formation is met with the reply that it was not ultimate authorship—and whether that insulation is a theoretical vice rather than a virtue. Rank the counter-evidence from decisive to merely awkward, and tell me whether the position is genuinely exposed or has been rendered immune by its own qualifications.
