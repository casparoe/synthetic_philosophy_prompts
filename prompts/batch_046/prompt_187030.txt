I am a software engineer with no formal training in philosophy, and I have been trying to understand debates about whether categories are socially constructed or discovered in nature. A colleague of mine—who reads in ontology informally—has rendered clear verdicts on several cases, and I want to know how she would extend them. She has not yet considered the following new case. A music-streaming service deploys an unsupervised clustering algorithm on user listening histories. The algorithm produces segments with labels such as nostalgic indie listener and high-energy workout listener. These segments are not defined by human designers with explicit criteria; they emerge from the model's optimization objective, shift as user behavior evolves, and are used by the company to allocate marketing budgets and to decide which artists receive promotional support. The clusters have no stable definition independent of the algorithm's current parameterization and the training dataset.

Please predict her verdict: would she classify these algorithmic taste clusters as socially constructed kinds, as natural kinds discovered by the algorithm, or as a hybrid? Then identify the implicit rule or rules she appears to be running—possible candidates include a requirement for human intentional design, a criterion about tracking mind-independent causal structures, or a condition about stability across contexts and populations. Because her earlier verdicts are jointly consistent with more than one principle, lay out at least two competing rules, state what each predicts for the algorithmic clusters, and give your best guess with reasons. Finally, specify one further case—perhaps involving AI-generated artistic genres or blockchain-based identity tokens—that would best discriminate between the two rules. Do not provide bibliographies, citation lists, or reading recommendations; I need only conceptual analysis that does not depend on looking up sources. If her precedents genuinely underdetermine the answer, say exactly what principle she would be adopting with each possible choice.
