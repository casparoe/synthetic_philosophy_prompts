A parliamentary vote implies intentional agency; my nodes execute deterministic rules. The more I analyse these mismatches, the more I realise I am not understanding the mechanism itself, but only weaving a web of family resemblances that will mislead me once we scale.

Is this fluidity a cognitive defect I should actively suppress, or is it the fundamental mechanism by which reasoning operates at all?
