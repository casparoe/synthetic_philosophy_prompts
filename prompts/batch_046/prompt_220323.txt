i run a philosophy workshop with about 12 grad students, 90 min. i want them to feel the squeeze between voting as expression vs harm-reduction, especially that weird loop where saving the lesser evil every cycle lets the center drift further away since they never actually have to win you're sincere vote. design a 4-round simulation with hidden preference cards, a seperate payoff sheet tracking integrity and outcome points, and rules that make strategic voting look smart in round 1 but show by round 3 that the coalition they rescued has adopted positions that hurt them. no upfront lecture—just cards and rules. then a debrief mapping each round onto the loop, plus what will predictably break (people refusing strategy, group collusion) and how to handle it. be honest about what feature the game can't reproduce and you'll need to supply. no citations or reading lists needed.
