Write a villanelle—nineteen lines, five tercets and a closing quatrain, with two refrains alternating through an ABA rhyme scheme—about Maxwell’s demon resolved through information physics rather than through any intelligent agent. Let one refrain address the measurement of a single molecule’s velocity, treating the demon as a physical gate or Brownian ratchet that records one bit of information; let the other address the erasure of that record and the unavoidable dissipation of at least kT ln 2 of heat, per Landauer’s principle. Structure the poem so the refrains cycle through observation and reset across the tercets, and so that the final quatrain joins them to show that gaining information and paying the entropic cost are the same loop, not two separate acts. Avoid anthropomorphizing the demon; keep the mechanism precise enough that a reader familiar with Szilard’s engine and Landauer’s limit recognizes the argument rather than seeing only a name-check. Let the rhyme and refrain pattern carry the conceptual work: do not sermonize, and do not resolve the tension outside the form itself.
