Plan the teaching arc: what conceptual dependency must be in place first (for instance, separating causal explanation from base-rate forecasting) before the distinction becomes operational rather than semantic; which concrete forecasting failure should carry the session—one drawn from a domain they respect where the outside view clearly outperformed expert judgment, explicitly not the planning fallacy—and what properties it must have to avoid being dismissed as irrelevant; the misconceptions they will predictably form in order—probably that the outside view is just pessimism, then that it licenses ignoring structural knowledge about energy transitions—and the precise point in the sequence where each should be headed off; one diagnostic question at the end that reveals whether they have grasped the tension rather than memorized the labels; and which elements of the forecasting literature should be explicitly set aside at this level, acknowledged as omitted.
