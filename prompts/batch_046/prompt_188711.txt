I was discussing Augustine with some friends after a philosophy of religion seminar, and I think I've ended up defending a reconstruction of his anti-lying stance that feels stronger than the usual summaries but also weirder. The position I'm holding is that Augustine's prohibition isn't best read as a straightforward moral rule about not harming listeners; it's more like an ontological claim about language and being. On this view, a spoken falsehood is a disorder in the speaker's relation to reality — words ought to mirror what is, and when you utter something false with the intent to decieve, you corrupt your own being regardless of whether anyone is actually misled or hurt. That makes the standard Nazi-at-the-door counterexample miss the point, since it treats lying as an instrument justified by outcomes, whereas this reconstruction treats it as structurally wrong for the liar. I also think this lets Augustine allow strategic silence or mental reservation, since those don't involve speaking falsehood.

Still, I'm worried this reconstruction might collapse once you actually look at his treatises, or once you bring in modern philosophy of language. So tell me: what would genuinely count against this position? Not just people lie to save lives — I want the specific defeaters. Which intuitions about fiction, metaphor, or performative speech show that language isn't always representational? Which parts of Augustine's own writing — maybe his handling of the Hebrew midwives, or his use of allegory — sit badly with the idea that false speech is always ontologically corrupt? And if speech-act theory shows that utterances can do things other than mirror reality, does that kill the reconstruction or just limit its scope? Rank these from fatal to merely awkward, and say which ones a defender of this view would themselves concede as damaging versus which they'd dismiss as category mistakes. Please seperate what's well-established about Augustine's texts from what's genuinely disputed among people who study him, and don't present a contested reading — like whether he allows mental reservation — as the settled consensus. I want an honest map of where this position is exposed.
