I am taking a course on the philosophy of history and comparative empire, and I have been following the long-running exchange between Niall Ferguson's defense of the British Raj and Shashi Tharoor's indictment of it. Is that diagnosis correct, or is the disagreement deeper—perhaps a clash between consequentialist evaluation and a deontological prohibition on coercive extraction that neither side explicitly defends? I want a diagnosis of the disagreement's structure: whether it is about evidence, about which counterfactuals are legitimate, about hidden normative premises, or about answering different questions under the same name. Please also say what kind of finding—archival, formal, or conceptual—would actually move the debate, or whether the impasse is permanent.
