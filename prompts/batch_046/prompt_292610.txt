I work in computational neuroscience, and my everyday conceptual vocabulary is that of predictive coding, precision-weighted prediction error, allostatic regulation, and free-energy minimisation. I have no formal background in axiology or philosophy of well-being, though I understand the basic architecture of hedonism and its rivals. Recently a colleague and I have been developing a working idea we call "allostatic nulling": the proposal that biological systems do not maintain a homeostatic set-point for positive affect, but only suppress interoceptive and exteroceptive error signals that breach a tolerated range. On this view there is no "reward centre" that accumulates pleasure; there is only the dynamic attenuation of disturbance. This strikes me as a mechanistic echo of a philosophical position I have encountered named tranquilism — the claim that only the absence of craving and suffering carries intrinsic value, and that the presence of pleasure, joy, or flourishing contributes nothing further. I would like you to take the bare normative structure of tranquilism, stripped of its historical associations, and restate it as precisely as possible in my vocabulary of predictive processing, precision weighting, error dynamics, and allostatic nulling. Be explicit about how craving corresponds to prediction-error dynamics, how suffering relates to unresolved or high-precision errors, and what "well-being" could denote if it is not represented as a positive signal but solely as the absence of negative ones.

Then provide the residue report. Which elements of tranquilism map cleanly onto predictive-coding terms? Which must be distorted — for example, does the normative "ought" or the notion of intrinsic value become reduced to a descriptive claim about error suppression? Which elements have no expression whatsoever in my framework, such as the judgement that a life of intense pleasure with zero suffering is no better than a neutral life, or does my vocabulary treat that merely as a quantitative difference in disturbance-attenuation? I want an honest assessment of whether the untranslatable remainder reveals a genuine absence in predictive processing — a missing normative dimension — or whether it shows that tranquilism's apparently substantive claim dissolves once the normative language is removed and only the mechanics of nulling remain. Please do not supply bibliographies, reading lists, or citations; I want the analysis to depend solely on the conceptual translation itself.
