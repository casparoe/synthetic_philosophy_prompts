I work in mechanism design and contract theory, and I am fluent with principal-agent models, hidden actions, moral hazard, revelation mechanisms, incentive compatibility, and observational equivalence, but I have no formal training in ancient philosophy. Restate Glaucon's challenge from Plato's Republic—the claim that justice is valued only instrumentally, that anyone granted invisibility through the ring of Gyges would act unjustly, and that the social contract is therefore a compromise among the weak—entirely within my vocabulary. Map the ring to a precise informational asymmetry; map the just agent to a constrained-optimal contract; map the unjust agent's behavior to a profitable deviation; and treat the social contract itself as an equilibrium mechanism. Be explicit about the translation: do not smuggle in normative language that has no counterpart in mechanism design. After the translation, provide the residue report. For each of the following, say whether it survives intact, survives only in distorted form, or has no expression at all: (1) the claim that justice is purely instrumental; (2) the phenomenological difference between being seen and being unseen; (3) the possibility that justice might be valued for its own sake, not as a constraint but as part of the agent's objective function; (4) the idea that moral psychology (shame, reputation) is an endogenous feature of the mechanism rather than an exogenous preference parameter. Then address a hypothetical extension: suppose invisibility is not binary but continuous, modeled as a differential-privacy-style audit mechanism in which the principal observes the agent's action only through noise calibrated by a parameter epsilon. As epsilon grows and audit quality degrades, does Glaucon's argument become stronger, weaker, or structurally different? Does mechanism design have resources to distinguish between a world with perfect observability, one with calibrated noise, and one with complete invisibility—and if so, does that distinction recover any part of the moral intuition that the binary translation lost? Report honestly where the vocabulary succeeds and where the residue indicates a genuine gap rather than a mere notational inconvenience.
