Specifically, clarify whether such a loop can account for this ownership-structure without presupposing a Cartesian ego, and identify the precise representational condition that prevents the loop from collapsing either into eliminativism (the denial of any self) or into a homuncular fallacy (the postulation of an internal observer). In your explanation, treat the I-symbol not merely as an information-theoretic pattern but as a semantic structure with causal efficacy, and specify what kind of non-viciously-circular, self-causing architecture would be required for it to maintain referential stability.
