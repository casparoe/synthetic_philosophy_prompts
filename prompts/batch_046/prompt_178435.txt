I sit on the steering committe for our city's participatory-budget pilot. There's a proposal to give weighted votes to "future-resident trustees" for climate-adaptation spending that won't pay off for decades. Half the committe says the demos is just current legal residents; the other half says anyone affected by collective decisions belongs to the demos, so future people need proxy representation. There's also this third view floating around that being in the demos requires being able to take part in joint action, not just being affected, which would exclude them regardless. I need a short memo for friday's meeting recommending approve, reject, or approve-with-conditions on the proxy-vote scheme. Make sure it gives real reasoning and actually engages the strongest argument for including them, not just restating residency rules.
