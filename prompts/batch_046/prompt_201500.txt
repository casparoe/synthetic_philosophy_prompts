The loop touches every administrative layer except the charter itself, which remains the inviolate level even as it is implicitly referenced within the loop. I need a teaching plan rather than the explanation itself.

Please set out the order in which the components must be introduced — specifically why the notion of a clean, stratified hierarchy has to be firmly in place before the loop can be shown to violate it, and exactly where in the sequence my charter example should enter so that its legal familiarity does not obscure its logical structure. Identify the misconceptions the audience will predictably form and the precise point at which each should be headed off: I anticipate they will take the loop for a logical contradiction, confuse the inviolate level with merely the “bottom” of a stack, and mistake ordinary circular reasoning for a strange loop. State the concrete properties my carrying example must possess and lack — for instance, it must involve self-application without self-creation, and it must not permit the authority to dissolve its own mandate — and propose a brief check for understanding that reveals whether they have grasped the dependency structure rather than just retained the imagery.
