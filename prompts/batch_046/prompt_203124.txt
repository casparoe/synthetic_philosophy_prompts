A podcast I listened to summarized the epistemic view of vagueness—basically Williamson's take—like this: vagueness isn't a feature of language or reality, its just a symptom of our ignorance. The speaker then said this means debates about whether something is 'in the weeds' vs. genuinely obscure are also fake: there's a sharp line between useful specificity and pointless obscurity, and our sense that it fades gradually is just epistemic blur.
