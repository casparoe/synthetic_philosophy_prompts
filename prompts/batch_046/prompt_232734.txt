The principle I want operationalized is the evolutionary debunking worry in metaethics: if a moral belief is mostly there because it helped our ancestors reproduce, and there's no independent reason to think it tracks moral truth, then its justification is undercut — but only if we're realists about morality. So I want a step-by-step checklist I can apply to any belief that pops up in a script. The hard cases I need it to survive are: universal-seeming beliefs that still might be evolutionary (like not harming kin), culturally thick beliefs that feel rational (like family loyalty justifying white lies), and beliefs about distant suffering (like wild animal pain) where it's not even clear evolution shaped our intuition.
