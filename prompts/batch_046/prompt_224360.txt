Write an essay on whether a tangled hierarchy that includes self-representation—like the loop in Escher's Drawing Hands—must preserve an inviolate level untouched by that loop, or whether the loop necessarily collapses every distinction between object-level and meta-level. Consider the argument that avoiding infinite regress requires some base rules that are never encoded as data within the system, and the opposing view that even those base rules can be represented as objects within the system through self-referential encoding schemes. Do not end non-committally: defend either the claim that an inviolate level always survives, or the claim that true tangled hierarchies leave no level inviolate.
