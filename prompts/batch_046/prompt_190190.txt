I sit on this municipal AI review board (the Algorithmic Acoutability Panel, don't ask) and we need a decision memo on a vender proposal for a housing-voucher prioritization model. The board is split between process-based supervision people — they want full traceability of every inference step, human-readable feature logs, and a hard rule that no decision can happen without an auditable reasoning chain — and outcome-based supervision people, who say black-box internals don't matter as long as the vender hits binding distributive targets (like parity in approval rates across neighborhoods) and recieve automatic penalties for missing them.

The twist is the vender pitches something called reflexive outcome-binding, where the model's internal audit logs get dynamically reshuffled based on real-time error-rate feedback. So outcomes literally rewrite the transparency record. Half the board thinks this is a brilliant synthesis; the other half thinks it kills accountability because your audit trail becomes a function of the very harms you're trying to catch.

We need a memo — approve, reject, or approve with conditions — that we can actually vote on. Make it concrete: spell out exactly what conditions or rejection criteria apply to this specific proposal. Be honest about the strongest argument from the side you reject; don't strawman them. No bibliographies or citation lists needed, just a working document we could send back to the vender and the board.
