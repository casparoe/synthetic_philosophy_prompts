what philosophical commitments about culture and causation does that practice actually presuppose? does it require that values are real causes and not just post-hoc rationalizations of material interests? is it even compatible with methodological individualism, or does it quietly depend on some form of collective intentionality or cultural ontology? and if we replaced that framework with a strictly rational-choice or materialist explanation, what would have to change in the actual analytical practice—not just the conclusions, but the way evidence gets weighed and what counts as a successful prediction?
