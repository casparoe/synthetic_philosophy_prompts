She also defends 'domain-transfer modesty', the claim that scientific training confers no privileged authority in metaphysical judgement. Please draft a polished sequence of interview questions that press this framework rather than invite restatement. The tone should be rigorous but accessible for a podcast audience, and please reply in British English.
