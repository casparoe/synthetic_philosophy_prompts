hey, im a grad student in enviro policy but ive been falling down a rabbit hole on space settlement ethics lately. there's a move i keep seeing in papers about lunar and martian governement that i dont have a word for, and its not just "mission creep".

heres the pattern: someone proposes that a colony should govern shared resources like air recycling loops and water reclamation democratically — not private property, but a commons with collective decision-making. then a critic points out that in a depressurization crisis you cant hold a vote, so the proposal gets amended: democratic except during emergencies, when a designated captain takes over. then someone asks what counts as an emergency, and the answer is something like "any event that drops life-support reserves below 15%," which on a small base happens pretty often because of dust storms or equipment fatigue. so now the democratic rule only applies in stable periods, but stable periods are defined negatively as "not-emergency," and the emergency threshold is low enough that it swallows the normal case. each amendment sounds reasonable on its own, but together they turn the commons into a command structure, and people treat the amendments as protecting the original idea rather than undermining it.

ive seen this with "regolith extraction cooperatives" too — they start with a rule that no entity can claim more than X tons of lunar soil per year, then add an exception for "pilot projects" that need extra for testing, then define "pilot" loosely enough that every commercial operation qualifies. the original anti-monopoly rule is technically still there, but its been hollowed out by individually defensible exceptions.

is there a name for this slow hollowing-out where each exception is locally reasonable and protects the core principle, but collectively they invert it? how is it different from an ordinary auxiliary hypothesis in science — where you tweak a theory to save it from counterexample — or from standard institutional mission creep? and if there isnt an established term, what should we call it? im not looking for just a label; i want to know exactly what counts as this thing versus just ordinary compromise, and where the boundary is between a healthy safeguard and the start of the hollowing.
