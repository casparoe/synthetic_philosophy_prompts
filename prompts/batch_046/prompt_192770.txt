Read Denis Villeneuve's Arrival through Otto Neurath's picture-language idea — his ISOTYPE thing and the claim that visual symbols can carry science without word-ambiguity. The heptapod script is literally pictoral and non-linear, and Louise starts thinking in loops just from seeing it. I want a reading that asks whether the film treats this like Neurath hoped: a neutral bridge between minds with no hidden theory, or whether it actually proves the critics right that every image smuggles ontology — here a deterministic circular time. Where does the movie's Sapir-Whorf vibe clash with Neurath's more modest view that pictures just help coordinate research without rewiring your head? And what does the film get right about visual communication that Neurath's critics missed?
