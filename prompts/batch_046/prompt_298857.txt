Please flag logically unoccupied combinations and diagnose them: is the gap incoherent (for instance, accepting full asymmetry and pro-mortalism while rejecting antinatalism without invoking the compensatory principle) or merely unexplored? Note which positions the tree genuinely cannot separate, such as weak antinatalism grounded in contingent empirical premises versus that grounded purely in axiological premises. Finally, please say what would change your assessment: which argument about counterfactual interests or modal status would cause you to relocate a leaf, and where the reasoning is most likely to fail—particularly at the junction between the potentialist split and the pro-mortalist split.
