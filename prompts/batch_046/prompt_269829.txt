(2) In macrohistory, every candidate—geography, institutions, culture—is endogenously co-determined: institutions adapt to geography, culture co-evolves with institutions, and even geography is altered by institutional choices like irrigation or settlement patterns. (3) So no macro-historical factor can be counterfactually varied in isolation; there are no available “interventions” in the interventionist sense within the historical record.
