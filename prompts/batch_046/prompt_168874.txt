Then produce a formal, reusable decision procedure—presented as an ordered checklist with explicit decision nodes—that a mediator on a regional water-allocation board could actually run when two municipalities submit competing claims over a fixed annual flow of 110 units. Your procedure must:

2. State the proportional scaling rule that connects the disagreement point to the utopia point along the line of constant proportional gains.
3. Specify the feasibility check: determine what happens if the scaled point lies outside the physically available set, and whether the mediator should truncate, renegotiate, or escalate.

Mark the precise step at which the mediator must stop and hand the decision to the board’s ethics committee because the question is one of values rather than arithmetic—specifically, flag the point where applying the solution requires accepting an inter-personal comparison of utility gains that is not dictated by the axioms themselves.
