You serve on the Charter Review Commission of a mid-sized city that must recommend whether to restructure the city council as a “staggered hybrid” legislature: six members elected in staggered four-year terms subject to a hard two-term limit, and six citizens selected by sortition for single, non-renewable two-year terms with no possibility of re-selection. Proponents argue that this breaks careerist incentives, introduces descriptive representation, and preserves electoral accountability in the elected half; opponents contend that lot-selected members will lack institutional memory, that the two cohorts will possess incompatible democratic mandates, and that deadlock will shift effective power to unelected staff. The commission votes next month on whether to recommend adoption, rejection, or adoption with binding safeguards. Draft the formal decision memo for circulation: state a clear recommendation with a concrete implementation condition or sunset clause, justify it with explicit reference to the tension between democratic legitimacy and governing capacity, and treat the strongest practical objection—whether about mandate conflict or staff capture—as a serious constraint rather than dismissing it.
