Imagine you are advising a funder who can support about six conceptual researchers for three years on the mechanisms of moral change—specifically, whether norms such as dueling or institutional slavery collapsed mainly through rational argument, shifts in material interest, or dynamics of honor and shame, as discussed in the debates surrounding Bernard Williams and Kwame Anthony Appiah. Which open conceptual questions should take priority, and in what order? I want reasoning rather than a survey: for each line of work, explain why it is upstream of the others, how tractable it is for philosophers and theorists rather than empirical historians, and what kind of finding over the next few years would cause you to reorder the list. One possibility that seems underexplored is whether status cascades within professional or bureaucratic elites can drive normative collapse independently of both honor and material interest; if that factor is primary, the standard dichotomy may be a red herring. How should that possibility reshape the research agenda?
