For each requirement, state the motivating case and whether it is negotiable or non-negotiable, and flag any pair you suspect cannot be jointly satisfied—particularly the tension between the momentariness of mental states and the narrative unity required for moral agency. Identify which single desideratum does the most work in excluding popular interpretations, and briefly indicate how a strict reductionist reading of the aggregates and a moderate personalist middle-way position fare against the list.
