I serve on the grant committee of the Marisol Vega Memorial Foundation, which I joined shortly after helping archive the private papers of the late labor organizer. We must decide on a proposal for a biopic that would use AI voice synthesis trained on her courtroom recordings to narrate reconstructed scenes from her final months—private conversations for which no transcript or witness exists, though the director argues they are psychologically consistent with her documented views. Half the committee regards this as legitimate artistic reconstruction; half considers it a posthumous fabrication of testimony that violates whatever dignity remains after death. Draft the committee's decision memo—approve, reject, or approve with conditions—giving reasons the filmmaker could actually address, and including an honest account of the strongest argument for the position you do not take.
