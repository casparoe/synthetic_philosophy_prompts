The tree should branch only through concrete case judgments and theoretical commitments that a respondent could endorse without knowing the standard position labels—for example, whether gradual acquisition counts as gaining a new fact or only acquiring an ability, whether reverse attenuation involves losing knowledge or only losing a mode of access, and whether phenomenal character is necessary for any genuine epistemic change in either direction. The leaves should include at least: physicalism read through an ability-hypothesis lens, physicalism read through an acquaintance-denying lens, property dualism, the phenomenal concept strategy, and an eliminativist or deflationary option. If two well-known named positions collapse onto the same leaf, flag them as indistinguishable under these diagnostics. Identify any unoccupied branches of the logical space and briefly diagnose whether the emptiness is due to incoherence, instability, or mere neglect.

In addition to the tree, provide a structured summary in JSON with exactly these fields: branch_question (string), answer_options (array of strings), leaf_position_category (string), indistinguishable_pairs (array of arrays), and empty_branch_diagnoses (array of objects with keys combination and diagnosis). After presenting the tree and the JSON, state how confident you are in the taxonomy's coverage of the logical space, and explicitly identify the branching point about which you are least certain that the positions genuinely diverge rather than merely offering different verbal descriptions of the same underlying judgment.

Please do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations; the taxonomy should be fully evaluable without external sources.
