My gut verdicts on whether a reduction is a real crane or just a skyhook in disguize, and I want the principle behind them if there is one. Neuroscience breaking consciousness into subpersonal mechanisms: crane, good. Explaining democracy away as pure voter psychology: greedy reduction, skyhook. Explaining math as just useful fictions for organizing sensory data: I call that skyhook, but physics reducing tables to atoms is a crane—why the difference? Moral obligations as evolutionary heuristics: crane if the normative force stays, skyhook if it vanishes. Color perception as tracking surface reflectance: crane, but saying "so colors don't exist" feels like a skyhook flip. What's the rule—preserving the explanandum, or explanatory debpt, or the direction of explanation? What does it say about reducing legal authority to social conventions? And the case I keep flipping on: is explaining art appreciation via dopamine reward circuits illuminating (crane) or killing the thing it studies (skyhook)? Which of my verdicts has to go?
