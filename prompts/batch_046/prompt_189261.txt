There's also this weird detail: Maya works as a deep-sea diver, and the same "missing" enzyme makes her resistant to decompression sickness in a way that people with the enzyme aren't. So her body is bad at one statistical norm but excellent for her actual life. Is my roomate right that abnormal stats equal sickness, or is there a deeper problem with defining health just by comparing people to averages?
