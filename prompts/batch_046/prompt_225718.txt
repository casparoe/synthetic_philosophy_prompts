Second, a patient is shown unambiguous scan results indicating a serious condition. He does not dispute the radiologist's general competence, but decides that for this particular illness clinical measurements miss the body's own knowledge, allowing him to reject treatment without ever rejecting the diagnosis itself.

The pattern is this: a person faces counter-evidence they cannot easily deny, so instead of suppressing the evidence they locally suspend the standards that would make it decisive—specifically in the domain where acceptance would be painful. The evidence remains visible; its authority is neutralised. Does this configuration have an established name?
