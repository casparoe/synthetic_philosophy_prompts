Construct one essay question that embeds the calibration gap in a concrete hybrid institutional case: a joint Dutch-Japanese research ethics review board. The question must demand a committed, definite position: students must argue whether the white lie is morally wrong in this calibrated context, and they must defend their verdict by explicitly engaging both the Dutch transparency norm and the Japanese relational-maintenance norm through the lens of the calibration gap.
