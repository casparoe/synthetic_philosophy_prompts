For a philosophy of action reading group I am writing down my current profile on moral responsibility. Here is the set: (i) I accept Strawson's Basic Argument in its strongest form: true, desert-entailing moral responsibility is impossible because nothing can be causa sui, and neither determinism nor randomness provides an escape route. (ii) I nonetheless think there is a genuine, non-illusory capacity I call revisionary autonomy—the ability, over time and through rational reflection, to alter one's motives and values—even though this capacity never makes one the ultimate origin of one's character. (iii) I hold that reactive attitudes (resentment, gratitude, indignation) are rationally appropriate only when aimed at agents who possess revisionary autonomy and have culpably failed to deploy it; directed at those lacking it, they are always irrational. (iv) I believe that moral education genuinely expands revisionary autonomy, and that this expansion justifies treating educated wrongdoers more severely—imposing stronger reactive attitudes—because their greater capacity makes them more deserving of blame. (v) I think the familiar compatibilist strategy—saving responsibility by identifying responsible actions with those that flow from motives the agent genuinely endorses—mistakes descriptive psychological coherence for normative authority, an error structurally parallel to thinking the explanatory gap in consciousness can be dissolved by more detailed neuroscience.

Go through these commitments and tell me which pairs pull against each other and how severely. For each genuine tension, identify the cheapest revision that restores consistency and state what I would have to give up. Also flag any pairs that only seem incompatible but dissolve once the distinction between originative and revisionary agency is kept clear. Then tell me which single change to my set would eliminate the most tension overall. Please also say what would change your mind about this audit—specifically, which argument or finding would lead you to reorder the tensions or conclude the set is coherent after all.
