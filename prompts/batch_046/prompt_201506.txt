The question should ask students to evaluate whether applying Western anti-corruption standards to clientelist systems therefore commits a category error—confusing dependency injection with protocol violation—or whether the absence of such an interface is itself a normative failure that transparency norms correctly identify. Students must be required to say exactly what normative work the analogy performs and where it collapses, rather than retreating to generic cultural relativism.

Please draft:
1. The essay question itself, phrased for philosophy students with no technical background (briefly gloss the software terms in the prompt).
