i've been thinking about maxwell's demon lately after a friend brought up landauer's principel in the context of AI compute costs. there's this analogy i keep seeing in my circles — people say the demon is basically just a bayesian updater with a gate. the velocity measurements are like incoming evidence, the sorting decision is acting on your posterior, the demon's little notebook is your working memory full of priors, and erasing that notebook to make room for the next round is exactly like catastrophic forgetting or model compression, with each erased bit costing kT ln 2 in heat. the conclusion people draw is that intelligence is never "free" — any system that sorts information by belief-state is just running a very fancy heat engine, and there's an unavoidable thermodyamic tax on every bit of reasoning.

but i'm not sure how far the mapping actually holds. can you lay out the correspondence table explicitly — what plays the role of the prior, the likelihood, the gate mechanism, the thermal bath, and the erasure step? then tell me where it breaks: does bayesian updating actually require physical erasure in the same way, or is that importing features from digital computation that don't apply to biological or analog cognition? does the analogy smuggle in that "understanding" is just sorting, and if so does that survive once you separate the information-theoretic cost from the semantic content? also, people sometimes mention szilard's old argument and bennett's resolution about reversible computation — without getting bogged down in citation details, how do those fit in? specifically, if the demon can in principle do reversible computation (like a fredkin gate) and avoid the erasure cost entirely, does that map onto any kind of "reversible reasoning" in the target domain, or does the analogy collapse there?

i want to know whether the relaxed conclusion — that there's a hard physical ceiling on reasoning efficiency that applies equally to minds and machines — actually survives once the disanalogies are on the table, or whether the analogy was carrying the whole argument.
