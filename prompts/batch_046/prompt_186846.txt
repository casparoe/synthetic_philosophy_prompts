Can you explain, in exactly one paragraph of no more than 150 words, whether Gödel's construction genuinely supports the direct-syntactic reading of Hofstadter's analogy, and whether relaxing it to modal self-reference weakens or preserves the argument for deriving self-awareness from formal self-reference?
