I delivered the manuscript of my second novel three weeks ago, and I'm currently suspended in that strange, restless interval before anyone outside my publishing house has read a word of it — except, as of yesterday, four early readers who received advance copies and have already sent their reactions back to my editor. She's offered to forward me their notes in full: not just line-level remarks, but their broader impressions of the plot and, in particular, of Iris, my rather prickly protagonist, whom I've already begun writing again for the sequel I'm under contract to deliver next spring.

I brought this up over drinks with my writing group, and it turned into a genuine argument. One friend, who edits nonfiction for a living, thinks the matter is simple: since I remain perfectly free to disregard any comment that strikes me as unhelpful once I've read it, there is no way that seeing the notes could leave me worse off than not seeing them, and every chance that they leave me better off. Another friend, a poet who once had a single unkind workshop critique stall her own writing for the better part of a year, thinks this is exactly the sort of reasoning that gets people into trouble. In her view, some information does damage in a way that has nothing to do with whether it happens to be accurate, and I would be better off drafting the sequel with my own sense of Iris intact than knowing what four strangers made of a book I can no longer revise in any case.

I couldn't talk my poet friend out of her position, even though the nonfiction editor's logic strikes me as very hard to fault on its own terms. Is she right that a reasonable person should always welcome more free information before making a decision, or can declining to look actually be the wiser course here? And if it can, I'd like to understand exactly what would have to be true of a situation like mine for ignorance, rather than knowledge, to be the rational choice — practically speaking, should I ask my editor to send the notes now, or hold off until the sequel is drafted?
