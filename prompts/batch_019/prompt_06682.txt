Descartes has this argument in the discourse on method where he says you could never build a machine that uses language the way a person does. Not that it couldnt spit out words, but that it could never rearrange them into a sensible, fitting reply to whatever happens to be said to it, the way even a pretty dull human can. He treats that, plus the fact that such a machine would do some things well and others badly (showing it's just following the disposition of its parts, not actually understanding), as basically settling whether something has a mind. Given how LLMs handle open ended conversation now, do you think that test still holds up, or would he have to backpedal if he saw one of these things in action?
