Over the past few weeks I have been shopping for an engagement ring, and I keep running into the same advice from relatives: that choosing something modest, however tasteful, will be read as a sign that I am not fully serious about my partner, while an extravagant choice will be read as proof that the relationship is solid. My first instinct was that this is simply residue from diamond industry marketing and has nothing to do with how much two people actually love each other. But a cousin mentioned that within her own friend group, couples who spent modestly on the ring were later gossiped about as "clearly not that serious," while couples who visibly overspent were assumed to be in it for the long haul, regardless of how those relationships actually turned out. That has left me wondering whether there is a genuine reason a costly, financially painful gesture would be a more trustworthy sign of commitment than an equally sincere but inexpensive one, rather than this just being herd behavior dressed up as insight. Is there something to it?
