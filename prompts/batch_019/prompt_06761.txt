Over Thanksgiving this year I watched my older brother nearly fall asleep standing up while reheating our father's dinner, and it's gotten me stuck on something I can't quite work out. Dad has had Parkinson's for about eight years, and almost from the day he could no longer safely live alone, my brother quit a job he'd worked hard for, moved back to our hometown, and took over nearly everything himself — meals, medication schedules, the 3 a.m. emergencies — turning down our offers to chip in for a part-time aide because he thinks Dad does better with someone he already knows. He's now in his late thirties with no savings, no relationship, and a career he'd have to restart from zero. If he had instead visited on weekends and helped pay for professional care, the way I would have, nobody in the family would have thought any less of him for it.

That last part is what I can't get past. Everyone treats what he's doing as wonderful and admirable, and at the same time as something extra — above and beyond whatever he, or any of us, actually owed Dad. But if the weekends-and-a-hired-aide version would have been a perfectly decent, blameless option, in what sense is my brother's choice better rather than just different? And if it genuinely is better, doesn't that mean the rest of us, who'd have settled for "decent," are falling short of something, even if nobody wants to call it wrongdoing? A second thing nags at me too: if my brother hadn't stepped up, the job would probably have landed on me by default, since I'm the only other sibling nearby. So did he do something heroic, or did he just get there first and take on a duty that was always going to belong to somebody in the family, in which case it's not clear he really went beyond anything? I've gone around and around on this with friends over the holidays without landing anywhere solid, so I'd like your take: is there actually a coherent way to hold both of these thoughts at once — that what he did was optional, and that it was nonetheless the better thing to do — or am I confusing two things that only look alike?
