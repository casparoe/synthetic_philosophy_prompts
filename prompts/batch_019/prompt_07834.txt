Mira, who'd just been reading some Ruth Chang, said that's a mistake: lacking a common scale doesn't mean the options can't be compared, we make rough non-metric comparisons like that constantly, and the right description is that the two options are "on a par" -- a real comparative relation, not a breakdown of comparison. Mira said he's conflating an arbitrary pick, where no judgment gets excercised at all, with a deliberate act of commitment, which is its own kind of rational act even when it isn't dictated by antecedent reasons.
