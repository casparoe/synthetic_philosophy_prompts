My younger brother was diagnosed with bipolar disorder a few months ago, and during his manic episodes he has said genuinely cruel things to me that he claims to barely remember afterward and is mortified by once he's stable again. My therapist advises me to stop resenting him and instead treat his outbursts the way I would treat bad weather: something to plan around rather than take personally. That approach works for maybe half the time; the rest of the time, the resentment returns regardless, and I then feel guilty for being so inconsistent about it. Is that inconsistency simply a failure on my part, or might my flashes of resentment be tracking something real that a purely clinical attitude toward him would miss?
