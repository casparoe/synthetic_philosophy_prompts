My dad's 71 and had a small stroke that means his hands shake too much now to safely use his woodworking tools — the doctor said one more slip on the table saw and he could end up loosing a finger. Now that he's had to stop for good, he's kind of shut down — keeps saying stuff like "a man who can't build anything with his hands isn't much of a man" and "guess I'll just sit around and wait now," which is a pretty grim way to talk and it's freaking my mom out. She wants me to cheer him up, but everything I think of feels like empty comfort. Is he basically right that this is just what old age reduces you to, or is there a real answer to what he's saying?
