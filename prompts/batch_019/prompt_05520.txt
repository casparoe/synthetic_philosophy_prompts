Could you write me a short story (or just a self-contained scene, 800-1200 words or so, doesn't need to be polished) that actually dramatizes this disagreement instead of gesturing vaguely at "parallel universes" the way most sci-fi does? I definately don't want the ending to stay comfortably vague on this the way I think I've been doing so far — bonus points if it actually turns on which theory is correct.
