My friend and I got into a whole argument last night about Gongsun Long's 'a white horse is not a horse' thing and never really resolved it. She thinks its just sophistry — equivocating between 'is identical to' and 'is a kind of,' basically the same trick as saying a red apple isn't an apple. I was defending it using his actual arguments (naming shape vs. naming colour, and how a yellow or black horse satisfies 'bring me a horse' but not 'bring me a white horse'), plus the idea, I think from Chad Hansen, that classical Chinese nouns work more like mass terms — 'water,' 'gold' — so 'horse' and 'white horse' could pick out genuinely different stuff-wholes, with white-horse-stuff simply a part of horse-stuff rather than a subclass of it.
