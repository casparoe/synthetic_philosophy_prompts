L. Schellenberg's work, that moves from the existence of reasonable, nonresistant nonbelief to the conclusion that no perfectly loving God exists. Draft eight to ten interview questions that go beyond having her simply restate the argument. For each question, include a follow-up keyed to the answer she is most likely to give.
