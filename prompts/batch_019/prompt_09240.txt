Random thing that's been bugging me since a work trip. I was out in rural Kyrgyzstan finishing up a story when my rental car died miles from anywhere and I was about to miss my flight home. A guy in an old truck offered to drive me the four hours to the airport, but only if he actually believed I'd wire him $250 once I landed -- he said he'd been burned by tourists before and reckoned he could tell who was good for it. I promised, he drove me, I made the flight. Its been two months now. Paying him would sting a little but wouldn't wreck me, and there's zero chance he could ever trace me or prove any of it if I just didn't. I told my editor this story over drinks and she said sending the money now is a nice gesture but strictly irrational -- the deal's fully delivered on his end, theres no enforcement left, so a rational person just keeps the cash. That sounded right when she said it, but it's been nagging at me since. Is she right that there's no rational case left for paying up?
