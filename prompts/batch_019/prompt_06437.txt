In my decision theory seminar this week, we worked through the centipede game and what is sometimes called the paradox of backward induction: the standard argument concludes that the first player should take the pot on move one, but that conclusion depends on there being common belief that both players are rational, and reaching any later round is already evidence that this common belief has broken down — which seems to undercut the very reasoning that told the first player to take in the first place. I would like a joke built on exactly that bind. Please do not give me a generic "two economists walk into a bar" joke about greed or nerdiness; the punchline should only really work for someone who already understands why the backward-induction solution is billed as "rational" and yet collapses the moment the game continues past the first move. Two or three lines is plenty.
