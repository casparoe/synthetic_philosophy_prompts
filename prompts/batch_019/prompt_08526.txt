Ok so last night I got into it with my friend Dan over Frege's political diary (the 1924 one -- antisemitic rants, wanting a strongman, Jews shouldn't have full political rights, that whole thing). Dan's position: total non-issue, pure biography, doesn't touch the philosophy any more than Wittgenstein being cruel to his students touches the Tractatus. I half agreed in the moment but it kept bugging me, so instead of just conceding I wrote down what I actually think on the train home. Full disclosure I've only ever read this stuff secondhand, never the actual translated diary, so tell me if I'm working off a bad summary somewhere.

Rough notes:

- Fine, granted: nothing in the diary makes a Begriffsschrift proof invalid or the sense/reference distinction false. Logic doesn't check ID at the door.
- But Frege didnt just think psychologism (logic reduces to how humans happen to reason) was mistaken, he treated it like an affront to objectivity itself -- same guy who needs Thoughts to live in their own mind-independent "third realm" in Der Gedanke. His whole picture requires exactly one eternal, non-negotiable logical order that every mind has to submit to, no local variants, no "different peoples reason differently."
- In the diary he wants the same shaped thing politically: one correct order, no multi party bargaining between competing wills, a single authority everyone bows to, Jews explicitly carved out of "everyone."
- So maybe the diary isn't irrelevant-because-biographical, it's actually evidence about what that specific temperament -- horror of plurality, hunger for one submission-worthy Order -- looks like once it's off the leash of formal logic and pointed at a country instead of a proof.
- What keeps nagging me: Godel held all kinds of cranky, reactionary opinions too and nobody treats that as illuminating about incompleteness. So maybe I'm just cold-reading a dead guy and dressing it up as intellectual history.

Can you actually engage with this instead of just being diplomatic about it -- is there a real argument in here or did Dan basically win? Also tell me if real Frege scholars have made this move already (or already shot it down) and I just don't know about it.
