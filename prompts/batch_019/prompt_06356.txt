Had a long argument with my friend last night about this radical interpretation idea from Davidson that she's covering in her phil of language class. Her claim: since Davidson says you can only interpret what someone means by assuming they're mostly rational and right about things (otherwise there's no way to get a theory of their language off the ground at all), it follows that we're not actually entitled to think a whole group of people, like flat earthers, are massively wrong about reality — if they really were that wrong we couldn't have understood what they were saying in the first place.
