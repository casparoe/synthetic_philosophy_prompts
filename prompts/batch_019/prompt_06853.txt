Please help me understand a passage from Hobbes's *Six Lessons to the Professors of the Mathematics* (1656), his combative reply to John Wallis and Seth Ward in the quarrel over his claimed quadrature of the circle. At one point Hobbes generalizes from the geometrical dispute to a broader epistemological claim:

"Of arts, some are demonstrable, others indemonstrable; and demonstrable are those the construction of the subject whereof is in the power of the artist himself, who in his demonstration does no more but deduce the consequences of his own operation. The reason whereof is this, that the science of every subject is derived from a precognition of the causes, generation, and construction of the same; and consequently, where the causes are known, there is place for demonstration, but not where the causes are to seek for. Geometry therefore is demonstrable, for the lines and figures from which we reason are drawn and described by ourselves; and civil philosophy is demonstrable, because we make the commonwealth ourselves. But because of natural bodies we know not the construction, but seek it from the effects, there lies no demonstration of what the causes be we seek for, but only of what they may be."

Two things puzzle me, and I'd like help thinking them through rather than a mere paraphrase. First, Hobbes treats drawing a figure with rule and compass and founding a commonwealth by covenant as instances of the same kind of "making" for purposes of demonstrability, yet these look like quite different senses of construction. Is there a single, principled notion of construction doing the work in both cases, given the rest of his materialist, causal theory of definition and science? Second, the thesis sits awkwardly with the historical record: Hobbes's own quadrature was rejected by every competent geometer of his day, and his civil philosophy proved no less contested than anyone else's. If our making the subject matter is supposed to guarantee demonstrative certainty, why doesn't that show up as actual agreement in practice, and how might Hobbes have tried to block this objection using his own theoretical resources?
