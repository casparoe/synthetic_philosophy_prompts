It went roughly like this. (1) Devon said her not watering it caused the plant to die, full stop -- that's just what "cause" means here, if she'd done the thing she was supposed to do the plant would still be alive. (2) Mara said no, nothing came out of her and hit the plant, dryness killed the plant, and dryness isn't a thing she did, so at most she failed to stop a death that something else actually caused. (3) Devon said ok then explain why we don't say the mailman caused the plant to die, or some rando three towns over who also didn't water it that week -- if not-doing-something is never a cause that's fine, but then it applies to literally everyone on earth who didn't water that plant, and if it's sometimes a cause you owe an account of why Mara's non-watering counts and theirs doesn't, and the obvious answer is that Mara agreed to do it. (4) Mara said that basically proves her point: the second you need "she agreed to do it" to make this count as causing, your actually talking about who's to blame, not what caused what, since the plant died the exact same physical way no matter who was on the hook for watering it that week. (5) Devon said that's a weird way to talk, because we say stuff like "the babysitter not watching the pool caused the kid to drown" all the time and nobody thinks that's some kind of mix-up between blame and causation, it's just what causing means in cases like this.

Neither of them backed down, and honestly the more I chew on it the more I flip back and forth on who's actually got it right.
