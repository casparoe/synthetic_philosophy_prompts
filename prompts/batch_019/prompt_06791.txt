Two weeks ago my father suffered a massive stroke and was placed on a ventilator. Yesterday, after two separate neurological examinations and a test in which doctors briefly disconnected the ventilator to see whether he would breathe unassisted, his medical team declared him dead. Even so, his heart is still beating, his skin is warm, and he has even spiked a small fever from a chest infection that the nurses are treating with antibiotics. We now have to decide, within a day or two, whether to withdraw the ventilator or consent to organ donation. My mother cannot accept that he is gone while she can still feel his heartbeat under her hand; the medical team insists that what we are seeing is just a body being maintained by machinery, and that the person is already gone. I don't know how to adjudicate between them, or even whether there is a fact of the matter here to adjudicate. Is my mother's refusal simply grief talking, or is she tracking something real that the declaration of death is glossing over?
