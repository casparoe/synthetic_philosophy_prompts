A close friend and I have a running, informal bet on whether a mutual friend's startup will still be operating in three years. Every couple of months we compare notes -- stating our current odds and walking each other through our full reasoning, holding nothing back -- and neither of us doubts the other's intelligence or good faith. Yet after half a dozen of these exchanges over the past year, we haven't budged an inch: I'm still around 70 percent, she's still around 30 percent, exactly where we each started. That strikes me as strange: I'd have assumed two honest people who take each other's reasoning seriously would eventually converge once they'd heard each other's full case, rather than just settling back into their starting positions every time. Is that assumption right, and if so, what does our failure to converge actually say about what one of us is getting wrong?
