My father died several months ago, and since then I have found myself listening almost obsessively to a single instrumental piece, a Chopin nocturne, that seems to express my grief with uncanny precision even though it depicts nothing at all: no words, no story, no image of loss. What unsettles me is that the same piece turns up, apparently just as fittingly, in wedding videos and even in a car advertisement I came across recently. When I mentioned this to a friend, she argued that this proves the music itself is emotionally empty, a kind of blank screen onto which I am simply projecting my own state, and that anyone in any mood could project onto it just as easily. I am not convinced by her argument, but I cannot quite articulate what is wrong with it. Is there a more satisfying way of understanding how a piece of music with no representational content can feel like it captures one particular emotion so exactly, while apparently being equally at home in entirely different emotional settings?
