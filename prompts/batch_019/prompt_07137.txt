Write one between two Bayesians -- say, hashing it out over coffee after a colloquium talk -- who both accept probabilism but disagree about what the Dutch book argument actually shows. I want genuine back-and-forth, each line responding to the other's actual last move rather than prepared monologues, and don't let either one clearly win -- both of them should be sharp.
