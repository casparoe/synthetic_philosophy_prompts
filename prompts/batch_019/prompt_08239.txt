Googling around about what happens to people in situations like hers, I landed on Molyneux's problem -- the old thought experiment Locke and Leibniz argued over, where someone born blind who's learned to tell a cube from a sphere by touch suddenly gains sight, and the question is whether they could tell which is which by sight alone, no touching aloud. What suprised me is that this apparently isnt just an armchair puzzle anymore, there's real research on newly-sighted people (some program in India, I think?) that actually tried to test this for real. I mentioned it to a couple friends and one of them just said "wait, doesn't she just... see now?", which made me realize how much most people (including me until last week) don't get about why this is even a hard question.
