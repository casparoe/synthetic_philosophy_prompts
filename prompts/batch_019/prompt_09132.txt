Half the panel wants to deny it as basically unworkable to apply here; the other half says that just punishes her for having a diagnosed disorder. Can you draft the memo I'd actually bring to the panel -- grant, deny, or grant with conditions, with real reasons -- that takes the strongest version of whichever side loses seriously?
