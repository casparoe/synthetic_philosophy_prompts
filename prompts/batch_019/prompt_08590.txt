One faction on the committee wants the formula weighted heavily toward individual performance ratings, arguing that our strongest performers are paid below market and that keeping their pay competitive protects the revenue that funds every other role. A second faction, led by a staff engineer who minored in philosophy, wants a flatter formula that raises payouts for our lowest-paid tier (support and QA engineers); she keeps invoking Rawls's veil of ignorance and difference principle, arguing that the current formula couldn't be justified to someone who didn't yet know which tier they would land in. Draft the memo I'll circulate to the committee: recommend one formula (or a specific hybrid), give your reasoning, and engage seriously with the strongest Rawlsian objection to whichever option you recommend — including whether the difference principle was ever meant to govern something like a corporate bonus pool in the first place.
