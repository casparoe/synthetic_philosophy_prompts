Write a dialogue between two cosmologists arguing about the Boltzmann brain problem. One thinks it's a genuine knockdown argument against any cosmological model (like eternal inflation) that implies most observers with memories like ours are just stray thermal fluctuations rather than evolved beings. The other thinks "most observers" talk like that is too ill-defined to be evidence of anything, given the (still unsolved) measure problem. At some point, have one of them point out that, by their own theory, this very conversation is way more likely to be a Boltzmann brain's dying hallucination than a real exchange between two physicists — and have the other actually respond to that instead of dodging it.
