I spent the first part of my adult life living abroad, so immersed in a culture with different assumptions about work, family, and what makes life worth living that this way of seeing things stopped feeling like one option among others and simply became how things obviously were. When I eventually moved back home, that outlook faded faster than expected, and within a couple of years I was thinking about my life in the old, familiar terms again, with the same unquestioned certainty. I don't think the two outlooks are really the same view in different clothing, since they pull in different directions on some fairly important questions, and I'm fairly confident that if I moved abroad again and stayed long enough, the same shift would happen once more, again feeling like plain common sense rather than a chosen perspective. Given that each outlook is only fully convincing from inside itself, is there still a meaningful way to ask which one actually sees things more truly, or is that not really a sensible question?
