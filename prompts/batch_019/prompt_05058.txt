I'm a novelist - mysteries, mostly - about ten years and three books into doing this for a living. Before any of it paid the bills, writing was just the thing I disappeared into most nights, no deadline, no readers, no stakes, i'd stay up till 2am on stuff that was never going anywhere. Now I've got a two-book contract, a spreadsheet tracking my daily pages against the due date, and weirdly the actual writing feels like clocking into a job I don't love, even though I'm hitting every single goal I used to dream about back then. My partner keeps saying I should start some other project on the side, no outline, no plan to ever show my agent, just to remember what writing's even for, but that feels like torching hours I don't have to spare. Is she onto something real, or am I just being precious about my own process?
