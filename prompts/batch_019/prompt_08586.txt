Ok so this has actually been playing out in my family since Thanksgiving and I still can't decide what I think about it. My younger sister is really into carbon footprint stuff, she offsets every flight, guilt-trips everyone at dinner about beef, the whole deal, but she thinks people who get worked up about factory farming are kind of ridiculous and eats chicken like three times a day. I'm basically the mirror image: I've been vegetarian for eight years because factory farming genuinely bothers me, but I fly out to see my partner every other month and drive an old gas guzzler, and honestly the carbon stuff just doesn't hit me emotionally the way animal suffering does, even though I know intellectually it's a big deal too.

So over turkey we half jokingly worked out a deal: she agreed to go vegetarian for a full year, which costs her basically nothing since she doesn't think it matters anyway, and in exchange I pay to offset every flight either of us takes this year and I'm trading in my car for a hybrid, which costs me comparatively little for the same reason. We actually shook on it. By our own separate standards we should both come out ahead afterward, less animal suffering, less carbon, and neither of us had to personally do the part we'd find genuinely hard.

My housemate thinks the whole arrangement is basically a scam. He says its just each of us buying indulgences: my sister still believes eating meat is fine, shes just being paid not to do it, and I still don't really care about carbon, I'm just paying to make the guilt go away, so neither of us has actually become more virtuous, we've just paid our way out of the parts we didn't want to handle ourselves. Is he right that this is a kind of cheating, or is there something legitimate about trading off the stuff we each don't care about for the stuff we do? I don't have any background in this kind of thing so I genuinely don't know if there's a real answer here or if it's just a vibes call.
