I'm on my hospital's radiology practice committee and we're deadlocked. Right now every scan reaches the radiologist with the referring doctors note attached ('r/o PE', 'query fracture'), and a resident's informal audit found incidental findings get missed more often when the note points hard at one diagnosis. Some attendings want blinded first reads rolled out; others say the note is just doing the normal job of telling the radiologist where to look, and blinding it would tank sensitivity for whatever the scan was actually ordered for. The real disagreement underneath isn't 'bias exists, yes or no' -- it's whether the note changes what radiologists visually notice on the image, not just what they conclude from it, because if it's the former, no checklist or second-check is going to fix that. Draft the memo I'll bring to the committee -- keep, blind, or some staged/hybrid protocol, with a pilot timeframe -- that actually takes a position on that distinction instead of punting on it.
