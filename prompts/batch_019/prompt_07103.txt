Can you draft me 8-10 questions that don't let her get away with that so easily? I also want one built around Karl Menger's old result that any single fixed utility function, log or otherwise, can be outflanked by constructing a nastier "super" version of the same game unless the function is bounded. For at least three of these, give me the deflection she's most likely to reach for and a pointed follow-up, because I dont want "ensemble versus time average" to end up being the answer to every single question on the tape.
