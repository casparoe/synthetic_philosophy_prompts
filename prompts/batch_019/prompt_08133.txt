Ever since I dropped any religious belief, I've noticed something weird about myself: I still care about being a good person, but that feeling only really has teeth when part of me imagines the universe is somehow keeping score. Over the last few months this has turned into an actual habit - I spend a chunk of my weekends reading about near-death experiences, mediums, consciousness research, that kind of thing. Not because I expect ghosts to be real, but half hoping to find some actual scientific crumb of evidence that morality isnt just a preference floating around in an indifferent universe. My partner thinks I've lost the plot a bit, and honestly part of me agrees - feels like a weird way for a supposedly rational engineer to spend a Saturday. But I can't tell if this is just me coping badly with nihilism, or if theres a real, serious version of this worry that smart people have actually taken seriously too. Which is it, and if it's the second one, does that kind of search actually lead anywhere?
