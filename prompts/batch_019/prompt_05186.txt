The board is split: half calls this routine CI/CD, half argues that a system can't use its own judgment to certify an upgrade to that same judgment, citing something about Löb's theorem that nobody on the board can actually explain or evaluate. Please draft the board's short decision memo for Monday's meeting -- approve, reject, or approve with named conditions and a timeframe -- with a concrete recommendation that takes seriously the strongest case for shipping anyway: mandatory manual review isn't obviously safer, since engineers can't fully audit generated patches by hand either.
