Me and my friends (7 of us) do a yearly trip and always fight about where to go, so this year we tried to fix it by having everyone rank all the options instead of just picking one favorite. It caused a lot more drama than expected. We tallied the same rankings two different ways for comparison - ranked-choice voting, and a straight points system where you get more points the higher you're ranked - and got two different winners off the exact same ballots. Then someone noticed one destination got zero first-choice votes from anybody, so we deleted it from the points tally since it "obviously didn't matter" - and that flipped the winner between the other two! Nobody miscounted - I definately checked twice by hand. Now half the group thinks the whole process is rigged, and the other half wants to scrap ranking and just do a majority vote instead. Is there an actual fair way to run something like this, or does every method just have some flaw hiding in it somewhere?
