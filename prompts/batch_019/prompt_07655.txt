Half the room is her philosophy of physics crowd who will clock it immediately if I go for the easy joke, and the other half is family and Daniel's physicist friends who've never heard the word 'diffeomorphism' and shouldn't have to. Can you write me a five minute toast with at least one joke that actually turns on the hole argument -- the bit where two models can be diffeomorphic but disagree on which point gets which metric value, and what that does to determinism -- not the tired spinning-bucket bit everybody already knows, but it still has to work as a toast people laugh and maybe tear up at, not a mini seminar.
