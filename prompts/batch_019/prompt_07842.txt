so in my political philosophy seminar we just read Simone Weil's essay on abolishing political parties (the one she wrote in London in 1943 while working for the Free French) and it's kind of wild how well it maps onto something happening in my own student union rn.

can you draft the memo I'd bring to committee? I need an actual recommendation – keep slates, ban them, or some conditional middle option – with a timeframe for when it'd kick in, and the reasoning has to use Weil's specific mechanisms (the collective-passion bit, the pressure-on-individual-minds bit, and the point about a party's real goal being its own unlimited growth), not just "parties bad." but also don't strawman the pro-slate side, since half my committee is sharp and won't accept 'a dead philosopher said so' as an arguement.
