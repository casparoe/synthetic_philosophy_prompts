My former student Alina is marrying her partner Deniz next month -- they met in my epistemic game theory seminar, so obviously I got asked to give the toast at the wedding. Their friend groups whole running joke is that the two of them can never actually "agree to disagree" about anything. I want the toast built around Aumann's agreement theorem, properly -- common priors, common knowledge of posteriors, the works -- and it definately can't just claim the theorem "proves" that real love means never disagreeing, because that's not what it says and Alina will heckle me from the front row if I get it wrong. Bonus points if you can work in the one big fight of theirs that only got resolved once it turned out Deniz had been quietly sitting on some relevant evidence the whole time. Half the room is philosophers and economists who'll catch every move, the other half is family who think "game theory" means fantasy football, so it has to actually work as a toast people enjoy, not a proof. Around 4-5 minutes, warm not smug.
