At my company we maintain dozens of internal services, owned by different teams. Every time a new one is created, someone has to decide whether its API should use camelCase or snake_case field names, and we have never managed to settle the question. In private conversations almost everyone agrees we would all be better off standardizing, but no team wants to be the first to migrate an existing service, since switching alone does not reduce the overall inconsistency and risks breaking whatever already depends on the old format. We even held a company-wide vote on it, which camelCase won by a narrow margin eighteen months ago, yet plenty of services, old and new, still use snake_case simply because that is what their team is used to. My manager thinks the vote failed only because leadership never backed it with a firm mandate, but I suspect something subtler than weak leadership is going on. What is actually happening here, and what would genuinely fix it?
