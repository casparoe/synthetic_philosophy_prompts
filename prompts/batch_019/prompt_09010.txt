Quick one — I keep bumping into this self-help line (doing some reading for a piece) that forgiveness is "for you, not for them": you should forgive your ex, your dad, whoever, because the resentment is what's eating you up, and letting it go is what heals you, regardless of whether they ever owned up to anything or even deserve it. Somethign about that has always struck me as a bit of a bait-and-switch, but I've never been able to say why. If the entire reason to forgive someone is that it's good for my own head, does that actually give me a reason to forgive them, specifically, or just a reason to want to stop being angry — and are those even the same thing? Feels like there's a real distinction hiding in there and not just me splitting hairs.
