Been prepping a lecture on futarchy and want to sanity-check something before I say it in front of a room full of grad students. Hanson's slogan is 'vote on values, bet on beliefs' - so doesn't that mean the conditional market price for 'welfare given policy X is adopted' just is an unbiased estimate of the causal effect of adopting X? Feels like futarchy hands you a free-running RCT for every policy question, no expensive trial required, so all the usual causal-identification headaches economists loose sleep over just don't apply here. Tell me if I'm wrong, but this seems like a pretty underrated selling point of the whole scheme.
