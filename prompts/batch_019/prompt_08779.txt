Ok this has been rattling around in my head for weeks and I can't decide if its a real problem or if I'm just overthinking a boring willpower issue, so I wanted some outside takes.

Context: I'm finishing a PhD and have always been bad at working on my dissertation without external pressure, so a few months back I set up a Beeminder-style commitment contract with myself — if I don't log 3 hours of writing on weekdays, $50 automatically goes to a political group I can't stand. Very standard self-binding, LessWrong-brained thing to do, I know. The problem is that lately, when the moment actually comes to sit down and write, "future me" doesn't just fail to comply, she actively relitigates the rule: decides the goal was badly worded, or that today is obviously a special exception, or reads the letter of the contract in some way past-me clearly didn't intend. So I tried to fix this by writing a stricter, more airtight rule in advance, and immediately hit this weird wall: to know whether the new rule will actually hold up, I basically need to simulate what future-me will do with it, and the only tool I have for simulating future-me is present-me — the exact person who keeps failing to anticipate the loopholes in the first place. There's no neutral third self who can check everyone's homework from outside the whole system.

A friend of mine says I'm massively overanalyzing basic akrasia and should just get an accountability buddy, and maybe she's right! But it feels to me like the issue isn't just "weak willpower" — it's more like there's no stable, privileged version of "me" who gets to be the judge of the other versions, since the judge is always just some other time-slice with the same kind of blind spots as everyone else. Is that actually a distinct problem from ordinary self-control failure, or just a fancier redescription of the same thing? And if it's real, is there any way out of it, or do you just have to accept that you can never fully verify, in advance, that a rule you set for yourself will survive contact with the self who actually has to carry it out?
