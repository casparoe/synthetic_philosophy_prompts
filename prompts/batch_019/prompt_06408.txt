Music is totally different: it's not a copy of any Idea at all, it's a direct copy of the Will itself, which is why Schopenhauer can say the world could just as well be described as embodied music as embodied will — music runs parallel to the whole world instead of depicting any piece of it.

He backs this up with an analogy to the parts of a composition: the bass line is the foundation everything else sits on, so he lines it up with the highest grade of the will's objectification, the fully conscious, deliberating will of human beings, since the bass 'directs' the piece the way our conscious will directs the whole person.
