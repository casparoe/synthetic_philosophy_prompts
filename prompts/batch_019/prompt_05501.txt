A friend of mine, Marcus, is a committed vegetarian for animal-welfare reasons, but he flies for work almost every week and has never much cared about his carbon footprint. This spring he struck a deal with his sister: she would go vegetarian for a full year -- something he cares about a great deal and she doesn't -- if he paid for a verified carbon-offset subscription covering every flight he takes -- something she cares about a great deal and he doesn't. Both kept their end of it. When he told the story at dinner, another friend, Leah, said the whole arrangement was morally empty, and the two of them went back and forth for a while. (1) Leah said that since neither of their convictions had actually changed, nothing of ethical significance had happened -- just money and compliance changing hands, a kind of modern indulgence. (2) Marcus replied that the outcomes are real regardless of motive: less animal suffering and less carbon than if the two of them had simply kept arguing about it, which is exactly what caring about those things is supposed to produce. (3) Leah said that proves too much, since it would equally justify paying a stranger to keep your promises for you, and that turning your own obligations into something tradeable, rather than something you personally act on, is precisely what's troubling. (4) Marcus said nothing was being offloaded, because neither of them owed the other any of this beforehand -- whatever either did beyond their own indifference is a net moral gain that wouldn't otherwise exist, the same logic as two countries trading on comparative advantage. (5) Leah said that logic only holds if you can trust the starting point, and you can't: nothing stopped Marcus from quietly flying more than he otherwise would have, precisely so he'd have more to trade away later, and unlike a real market there's no outside price to catch him if he did.

Since I was there, they've both asked me to settle it. Please go through the exchange move by move, say who is right at each step rather than just who wins overall, and note anything you think both of them are missing.
