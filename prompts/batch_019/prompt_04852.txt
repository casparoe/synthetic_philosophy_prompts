so i went down a rabbit hole a while back and actually read turing's original 1950 paper where he lays out the imitation game (the thing everyone calls the turing test now), instead of just going off whatever wikipedia says about it. near the end he runs through a bunch of objections someone might raise against the whole idea, and the very last one is about ESP. he flat out says the statistical evidence for telepathy looked "overwhelming" to him, and he's worried a human contestant could use it to cheat the test (like correctly guessing the suit of a card way more than chance), so his fix is to stick everyone in what he literally calls a "telepathy-proof room" to keep things fair.
