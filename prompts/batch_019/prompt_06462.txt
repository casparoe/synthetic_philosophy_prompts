I've been reading about the handicap principle in animals - like how peacocks have those huge tails that make it harder to fly and escape predators, or how gazelles do that weird stiff legged jump (stotting) right in front of lions instead of just running away. The idea being that these count as "honest signals" precisely because only a genuinely strong, healthy animal could afford to survive with that handicap, so the cost itself is what makes the signal trustworthy. Doesnt that mean the same logic carries over to people, so the more wasteful, painful or expensive a status display is (starving yourself for a certain look, blowing your savings on a wedding, brutal hazing to join a group), the more honest a signal of quality it automatically is, since bigger sacrifice equals bigger proof?
