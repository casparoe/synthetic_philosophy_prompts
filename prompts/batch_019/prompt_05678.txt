Apparently Michael Dummett, basically the guy most responsible for reviving Frege's reputation in English-language philosophy in the 20th century, came across the diary while digging through Frege's papers in Germany in the 50s, and by the time he published his big 1973 book on Frege he felt he had to admit in the preface that there was something ironic about having spent years admiring the work of a "virulent racist." The full thing didn't even get properly published until 1994, and I guess this year is literally the 100th anniversary of Frege's death so there's been a little wave of blog posts about it again.
