For the past eight years I have published my psychological thrillers under a pen name. In that time she has acquired her own author photograph, an invented biography with a childhood I never had, a mailbag of devoted fan letters, and even her own opinions on the craft, which come out whenever a journalist interviews her and I answer in her voice. Yet every sentence she has ever written or spoken has come from me, at the same desk, in the same sitting, so that nothing she has ever done seems truly separate from something I did. I am retiring her this year and will publish under my own name from now on, which leaves me with a question I cannot quite shake: if nothing she has ever done was really distinct from what I did, were there ever two of us to begin with, or was she a genuinely separate thing all along — one that did not exist before I invented her, and that will stop existing altogether once I retire her, even though I will carry on exactly as before? I would be grateful for your thoughts on what is actually true here.
