Here's where I've gotten to. I think I've got the geometric picture: draw a line from the disagreement point to the "ideal point" (the point representing each party's best conceivable outcome), and take wherever that line crosses the Pareto frontier. Could you compare the two solutions for me, and then tell me, in your own judgement, which of the two axioms -- IIA or monotonicity -- makes for the better normative principle of what counts as a *fair* bargain?
