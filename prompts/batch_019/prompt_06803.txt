I manage a small customer-support team. Last year, when most tickets were simple billing questions, I started rating agents mainly on how fast they closed tickets, since speed back then almost always meant the problem was actually solved. One agent who excelled under that system was promoted to our technical queue, but he still marks tickets resolved the instant he replies, fixed or not, and customers keep reopening them. When I confronted him about it, he wasn't defensive; he genuinely seemed to think speed was the whole point, since that's what had always earned him praise. Some of my other managers want to discipline him for dishonesty, but that feels off to me, since he doesn't act like someone who knows he's cutting corners. Did we essentially train him to want the wrong thing without realizing it, and if so, is this really his failing, or ours?
