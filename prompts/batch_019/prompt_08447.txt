So: fast-forward him to today. What do you think he'd make of (a) the Gauquelin "Mars effect" saga, where people tried to statistically prove a real astrological correlation and spent decades fighting over the data, versus (b) the current Co-Star/TikTok astrology wave, which barely bothers with prediction and is mostly about vibes and self-knowledge? Would either one look like vindication of his reformed astrology to him, or would he lump both in with the almanac hacks he already despised?
