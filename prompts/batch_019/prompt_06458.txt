I get the basic Worrall pitch -- when theories get overthrown (like Fresnel's elastic-solid ether theory getting replaced by Maxwell's electromagnetic one) what actually survives the transition is the mathematical structure, the equations, even though the underlying ontology is completely different, so a realist should only commit to the structure and stay agnostic about whatever "nature" the equations are describing underneath. But then Ladyman/French-style people push further and say structure isn't just the limit of what we can know, its all there IS -- there's no hidden furniture behind the relations, the relations (or the structure itself) are doing all the ontological work, full stop. My friend thinks the epistemic version is actually unstable and either collapses into the ontic one or back into instrumentalism once you really lean on it, and I'm not sure I agree but I couldn't articulate why.
