My friend and I got into a whole argument this weekend about the reparations task force I sit on for our city. She kept hammering a point I couldn't knock down: if slavery and everything that happened afterward hadn't gone the way it did, basically none of us alive today would exist at all, since who our ancestors met and when they had kids is downstream of all that history -- so how can a specific living person say they were harmed by it, when the alternative isn't 'the same person, better off' but 'that person never being born'? Half the task force thinks this kills our push for direct cash payments and we should pivot to purely community-level stuff (housing trusts, school funding), the other half thinks its just a clever gotcha that doesn't change anything. We vote on our final recommendation to the city council next Tuesday and I got stuck drafting the memo -- can you write it? Something that actually takes her argument seriously instead of hand-waving it away, and still lands on a concrete recommendation the task force can vote on.
