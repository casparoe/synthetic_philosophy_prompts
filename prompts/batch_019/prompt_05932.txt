Write a short story built around acausal trade -- the decision theory idea that two agents (could be civilizations, could be AIs, whatever) end up cooperating and actually benefiting each other even though they have zero causal contact and never will, purely becasue each can accurately predict how the other reasons and knows the other is doing the same thing. Kinda the sci-fi equivalent of what Permutation City did for consciousness or Three Body Problem did for alien contact, but for this idea instead. Make sure the story actually hinges on the mechanism -- why an agent would hold up its end of a deal that nobody can ever check up on or enforce -- instead of just using it as vague background flavor.
