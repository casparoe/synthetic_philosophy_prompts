My understanding is that Hofstadter's superrationality resolves the one-shot Prisoner's Dilemma by showing that mutual cooperation is actually the Nash equilibrium of the game once both players are exact copies of each other running the same decision procedure. Given that Nash equilibrium has been a rigorously defined solution concept since the 1950s, why do decision theorists still treat superrationality as a distinct and somewhat controversial idea, rather than as just a special case of ordinary equilibrium analysis applied to a symmetric game?
