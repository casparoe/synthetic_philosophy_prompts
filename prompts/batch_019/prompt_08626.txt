A dispute in my town has been bothering me: the council has put a proposed annexation to referendum, and if it passes, an adjoining subdivision of a few thousand residents will become subject to our zoning code and property taxes within the year. Only currently registered town voters are allowed to cast a ballot, since the subdivision's residents aren't legally part of the town yet — even though they will be bound by the result either way. When I suggested letting them vote too, a council member objected that this already presupposes they count as townspeople, which is exactly what the referendum is supposed to settle. I can't find a fix to the franchise that isn't either arbitrary or circular — is there a principled answer here, or is it turtles all the way down?
