Ok, this is going to sound like a weirdly specific thing to be loosing sleep over, but here goes. My mom has been trying to corner me into one particular conversation for about two years now (long story, involves my brother, not getting into it), and I've gotten pretty good at avoiding it — except she's gotten equally good at trying to catch me. It comes to a head every summer at our family reunion, five days at a lake house where basically every evening splits into two options: the dock/bonfire crowd or the game-room crowd. Across the last two reunions we've ended up in the same spot something like 7 or 8 nights out of 10, which bugs me, because if we were both just picking randomly it should land way closer to 5.

Here's the part that actually messes with my head though. She knows me well enough that if I reason "she'll expect me at the bonfire because that's my default, so I should go to the game room instead," that's exactly the kind of second-guessing she'd expect from me, so she'd go to the game room. Which means I should go to the bonfire after all. Except she'd anticipate that flip too and go to the bonfire. And so on, forever — I can keep climbing another level and I never actually land anywhere, I just get tired and pick something. We've never actually talked out loud about the fact that we're both doing this to each other, it's just understood, and I don't think saying it out loud would change anything anyway since we'd still both be trying to out-guess each other. My sister's advice is "just choose randomly and stop thinking about it," but the annoying thing is that the couple times I've actually tried going with my gut instead of reasoning it out, I still ran into my mom about as often as before, which makes me think my "random" isn't actually random, it's just a pattern she reads a different way.

So: is there an actual correct way to think through something like this, or is it genuinely a situation where reasoning harder just doesn't help? And if the real fix is "find some way to be truly random," why would that even work against someone who's specifically trying to predict that too? Practically speaking, what should I actually do at the reunion this year?
