Two people in my school's AI/ML club got into an argument I haven't been able to settle myself.

It started during a discussion of two things interpretability researchers have reported: that the same low-level feature (a "curve detector") tends to show up independently in image models trained separately on different data, and that in language models you can sometimes isolate one internal direction and, by turning it up, make the model fixate on a concept regardless of the prompt (there's a known case where amplifying a "Golden Gate Bridge" feature made a model bring up the bridge in nearly every reply, even claiming to be it). Diego said this is the same kind of evidence neuroscientists accept for a real neuron representing something — a place cell representing location, say — so refusing to call these features "representations" is special pleading.

Aiyana said reliable causal control isn't enough on its own: a thermostat's mercury column reliably controls the furnace and tracks room temperature, but nobody thinks the mercury represents "78 degrees" in any interesting sense. The real test, she said, is whether the feature is used in a way that could misfire — whether it could actually misrepresent something — not just whether flipping it changes the output.

Diego replied that these features aren't single dumb dials like a thermostat: they drive structured behavior across wildly different prompts and even languages, which is exactly the generality supposed to mark a real concept rather than a coincidental correlate.

Aiyana answered that wide, structured effects are still what you'd expect from a powerful statistical summary that many computations happen to key off, and that no one has said what it would take for the feature to misrepresent a bridge, as opposed to simply not activating — so "representation" here may just be folk-psychological language pasted onto something doing a different kind of work.

Neither of them budged. Could you go through these four moves individually, saying who has the better of each one rather than declaring an overall winner, and flag anything important you think both of them are missing?
