Retired physicist here, spending my newfound free time reading philosophy, which I never really got into during my working life. Just came across Ian Hacking's 'looping effects' idea -- how a label like ADHD or multiple personality disorder can change the very people it's applied to, who then sometimes change the label right back. It hit me that this is basically the observer effect from quantum mechanics showing up in the social world: the act of classifying someone perturbs the thing being classified, so the category can't be picking out something that was just sitting there waiting to be found, the way 'noble gas' or 'quark' is. So shouldn't we treat a diagnosis like ADHD as more of a useful fiction than an actual discovery, and be a lot more suspicious of the DSM than we'd ever be of the periodic table? Feels like it follows pretty directly, but i've got a nagging feeling i'm missing something -- what is it?
