My roomate is doing a philosophy MA, and when I told her the timeline she got this look and said it sounded like a textbook case of what she called 'Hacking's looping effect' -- something about how classifying people isn't like classifying rocks, because people find out how they've been classified and start living into it, which reshapes the category, which reshapes them more, and so on. Then she refused to just explain it properly and told me to think it through myself, which, thanks.

I'm not looking to be talked out of the diagnosis -- the meds genuinely help and I don't want to relitigate that part. What I actually want is the most important implications of Hacking's looping effect for a case like mine: how something like ADHDTok might work as one of these feedback loops in practice, what that would and wouldn't imply about whether my diagnosis is 'real,' and whether there's any principled way, on Hacking's own terms, to distinguish 'the online community basically manufactured my self-concept' from 'the online community finally gave me accurate language for something that was there the whole time' -- or whether that distinction itself is kind of confused from his point of view.
