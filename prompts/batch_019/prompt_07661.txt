(4) Renata's comeback was that this cuts both ways: because KS anchors on each side's best conceivable outcome, whichever party insists harder on an aggressive, unrealistic best case shifts the whole settlement in their favor, even though nobody would ever agree to that outcome. She said Nash's solution is much harder to game this way, since adding an alternative that isn't itself the eventual solution can't move the outcome at all — and that this resistance to manipulation matters more in a live negotiation than an abstract axiom does.
