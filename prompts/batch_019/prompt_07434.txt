In the recent "cosmic Bell tests," physicists fixed the measurement settings using light from quasars billions of light-years away, so that any mechanism correlating those settings with the particles' hidden variables would have had to be in place billions of years before Earth even existed. Since this rules out a common cause behind the correlations, these experiments seem to have finally closed off the superdeterminist escape from Bell's theorem, leaving genuine nonlocality as the only remaining explanation for the observed violations of the Bell inequalities.
