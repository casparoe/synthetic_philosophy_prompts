Schopenhauer thought music was totally different from the other arts because its a direct copy of the Will itself, not a copy of the Platonic Ideas the way painting or poetry are supposed to be. Compare that to Leibniz's much older claim that music is basically just unconscious counting, i.e. the mind doing arithmetic on the ratios between tones without knowing it's doing so. Do these two views actually contradict each other, or is Leibniz just describing the mechanics of something Schopenhauer is making a way bigger metaphysical claim about? Also, tell me which theory does a better job explaining why music can hit us emotionally in a way a math equation never does.
