okay so i went down a rabbit hole about thomas hobbes last night and found out he was 100% convinced he had squared the circle, and he spent basically the rest of his life in this brutal public feud with a mathematician named john wallis who kept publishing takedowns proving him wrong, over and over, for decades, pretty much til hobbes died lol.

whats actually interesting to me isnt just "hobbes was bad at math" tho -- apparently the reason he wouldnt back down is he had this whole theory for why geometry is the most certain kind of knowledge there is: basically that we can be totally sure of geometric proofs because WE construct the figures ourselves (a point moving to make a line, etc), so we actually know the causes, unlike in physics where were just guessing at causes we didnt make. and thats supposedly part of why he didnt trust wallis's more symbolic/algebraic style of proof either, since to hobbes that felt disconnected from any actual physical construction.
