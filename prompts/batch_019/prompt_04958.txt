I want the whole toast to be built around this one thing: her mantra, why its basically true, and why the real theorem is messier and more interesting than the bumper-sticker version she uses on us. Could you write it for me? I'd love the central bit/joke to hinge on some genuinely real exception or subtlety in the actual theorem -- not just the obvious "gathering data costs time and money" objection, everyone on the team has already made that joke to her face -- something a bit meatier that her old academic friends would actually recognize and smile at, while still landing for people on my team who think "Bayesian" is a coffee roast. Roughly five minutes, warm more than roasty, and please don't put anything in her mouth about the theorem that isn't actually true, because I promise you she will fact-check her own retirement speech in real time.
