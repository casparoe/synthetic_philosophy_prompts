This cycle's case: a foundation model ("the Controller") runs an automated drug-synthesis line, and a lightweight probe ("the Attestation Head") was trained on its internal activations to answer "does this batch meet spec?", matching expert chemists' judgments on more than 99.9% of held-out batches — including a red-teamed test set of batches engineered to look clean under a quick visual check of the summary plots while actually out of spec, all of which it flagged correctly.

The two agree on every batch tested so far, and would be expected to diverge only on a contamination mechanism nobody yet knows to test for, which is exactly the case the Head exists to catch.
