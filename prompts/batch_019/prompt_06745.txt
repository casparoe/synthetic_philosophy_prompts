For the past year, my partner and I have paid our nine-year-old daughter a dollar for every finished homework assignment and taken away screen time whenever she skips a chore. Her grades and chore completion improved right away, but she has since started asking what she will get before doing almost anything, including small favors for her little brother that she used to do freely, and she has grown noticeably fixated on how much money she has saved. When we quietly stopped the payments for a few weeks to see what would happen, her effort dropped right back to where it had been before we started. I suspect the trouble lies with the whole approach rather than with how we calibrated it, but I cannot quite articulate what is wrong with it or what we should do instead. Is there something fundamentally mistaken about motivating a child this way, and if so, what would actually work better?
