Could you write me a short story, a page or two, that dramatizes that specific gap, through whatever character or setup you like? I don't want another 'is it secretly conscious' story -- I've already got a drawer full of those and theyre boring me lately. I want the eeriness to come from the reports and convictions themselves being fully explicable, not from any lingering doubt about what's 'really' happening inside.
