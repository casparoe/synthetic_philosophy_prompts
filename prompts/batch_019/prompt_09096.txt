Two weeks ago, an interview I gave about my research was clipped out of context and went viral, and I have spent the time since trying to make sense of what followed. Thousands of people who have never read the actual study now hold firm opinions about it and about me; competing op-eds have appeared that mostly argue with each other rather than with anything I actually said; and commenters who would normally disagree about nearly everything suddenly sound interchangeable, sorted into two camps, for or against. No single exchange amounts to much on its own, and I cannot point to any one person as the source of the pressure I am under, yet the cumulative effect is stronger than any individual critic could produce, and there is no one I can actually locate, reason with, or hold responsible for it. Friends tell me to simply stay offline until it passes, which is probably sound advice, but it does not tell me what kind of thing I am actually dealing with. Is there a rigorous way of thinking about what this actually is?
