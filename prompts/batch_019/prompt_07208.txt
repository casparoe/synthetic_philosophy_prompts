Me and my buddy have this running thing where we bet small amounts of cash on stuff we disagree about, like whether the Lakers make the playoffs or whether some guy at work will actually quit like he keeps threatening to. Every time, we lay out our whole case to each other, and every time, after all that, we both still want to take the bet. Thats started bugging me: if he's heard my entire reasoning and I know he's smart and not just messing with me, shouldn't the fact that he STILL wants to bet against me be a reason to think I'm wrong, seperate from whatever his actual arguments are? But if I took that seriously, neither of us should ever agree to the bet in the first place, since we'd each keep dragging our guess toward the other's until there was nothing left to bet on — except that never happens, we just shake on it. So are we being dumb every time we do this, or is there an actual reason two people who trust each other's judgment can still end up disagreeing once everything's out on the table?
