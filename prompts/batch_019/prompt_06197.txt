Random thing that's been bugging me since an old Alignment Forum thread. The standard pitch for IRL-based value learning (a la Human Compatible) is that you skip hand-coding human values and just infer the reward function from observed behavior. Sure, with only a handful of demonstrations you're underdetermined, loads of reward fns fit the same trajectories equally well. But that feels like a normal small-data problem to me: once you're training on way more behavioral data, and you break any remaining ties with a simplicity prior (just take whichever reward + rationality-model pair is simplest), shouldn't the ambiguity basically disappear? Everyone in that thread was treating it like some deep unsolved problem rather then a 'more data + Occam's razor' situation -- am I missing something?
