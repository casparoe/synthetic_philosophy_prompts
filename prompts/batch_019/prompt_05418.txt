Then get them to duke it out over a standard late-preemption case -- Suzy and Billy both throw rocks at a bottle, Suzy's rock gets there first -- and see whose account actually handles it more cleanly. I want real back-and-forth, not two people politely talking past eachother; neither side should be a pushover.
