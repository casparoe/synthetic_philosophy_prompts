Since retiring from a career in physics, I have recently taken up decision theory as an amateur pursuit, and I have grown especially fond of updateless decision theory (UDT) — in particular, its insistence that a rational agent stick to the policy it would have committed to in advance, as in the counterfactual mugging scenario, even once updating on what has actually happened would, by ordinary causal or evidential lights, count against that policy. Please write me a joke that turns on this specific feature: the punchline should only really land for someone who understands why an updateless agent pays up in a case like counterfactual mugging while a causal or evidential agent does not. A generic decision-theory joke that merely gestures at Newcomb's problem or rational agency in the abstract will not do.
