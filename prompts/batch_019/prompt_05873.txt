Gravity Probe B's detection of frame-dragging—the way a rotating mass like Earth drags local inertial frames along with it—is often described as confirming Mach's old intuition that a spinning bucket's water climbs the walls because of its rotation relative to the rest of the matter in the universe, rather than relative to Newton's absolute space. Given this, hasn't general relativity essentially vindicated Leibniz and Mach over Newton, settling the argument of the Scholium in favor of relationism and rendering talk of substantival spacetime obsolete?
