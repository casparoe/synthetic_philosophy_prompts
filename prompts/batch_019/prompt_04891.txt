Write a dialogue between a two-dimensional semanticist who thinks the conceivability of zombies shows physicalism is false, and a type-B materialist who thinks psychophysical identity statements are "strong necessities" that break the conceivability-possibility link the zombie argument relies on. Have them really hash it out — is the strong-necessity move principled or just an ad hoc dodge? Push on stuff like whether there are any good examples of strong necessities outside the mind-body case, and whether the primary intensions of phenomenal concepts are actually as transparent as the argument needs them to be. Both sides should be sharp and responding to each other's actual points, not just restating their own view.
