This all sounded pretty convincing when he said it, but something about it nags at me and I can't quite pin down what. If Kelly betting is quite literally THE optimal growth strategy, why would any rational person ever deviate from it? Is he basically right that half-Kelly bettors are just irrationally squeamish, and that 'maximize the geometric mean' is simply the correct, no-caveats replacement for ordinary expected-value reasoning?
