I don't want to just lob her the questions she's answered a thousand times before ("so what even is a higher order thought"). Can you draft me like 8 to 10 real interview questions, each with a short follow-up that calls out the move I bet she'll make to wriggle out of it? I especially want one on the "targetless" higher-order thought problem - where the higher-order thought misfires and represents you as being in pain when you're actually not in that state at all, which seems to mean the whole felt quality is coming from the higher-order thought rather than whatever it's supposedly about, so why do we even need two separate states in the first place - and one on the regress problem, where the standard fix is that the higher-order thought itself is usually unconscious, and I want to push on whether an unconscious representation can really "light up" another state without being lit up itself, or whether that's just relocating the mystery rather than solving it.
