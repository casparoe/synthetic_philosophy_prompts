A friend has been trying to persuade me to become a distributor for a multi-level marketing company. I remain doubtful about the business opportunity itself, but something she pointed out has stayed with me: almost everyone who joins and completes the training ends up genuinely convinced it's a great opportunity, including people who started out just as doubtful as I am. She attributes this to recruits learning things during training that outside skeptics simply don't know; I suspect it has more to do with the persuasion techniques used on new members. Either way, I can already predict fairly confidently what I would end up believing if I went through the training myself. Does that prediction give me any reason to take the opportunity more seriously right now, or does a foreseeable future belief count for nothing unless I already have grounds for trusting the process that would produce it?
