Can you write me a dialogue between two people who've actually thought this through in detail: one who argues a rational person genuinely can form (and keep) that intention, and has a real story for why drinking the toxin the next day isn't just pointless self-harm even with the cash already sitting there; and one who thinks that's confused -- that "intending" to do something you already know you'll have zero reason to do once the payoff lands isn't really intending at all, just self-deception or wishful thinking dressed up as a plan.
