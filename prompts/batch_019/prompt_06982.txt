I did thirty-some years as an experimental physicist before I retired (solid state stuff, nothing to do with weapons work, though you can't hang around physics departments that long without the bomb crossing your mind more than once). A couple years back I started sitting in on a little philosophy discussion group that meets at the library every other week, and right now we're on a run of Elizabeth Anscombe -- last month somebody brought in her old pamphlet, "Mr. Truman's Degree," plus the relevant chunks of "War and Murder" where she lays out the double effect reasoning more carefully. She lost that fight, by the way, Oxford gave him the degree anyways, but reading how furious and how rigorous she manages to be at the same time has had me rereading the actual history of the Hiroshima and Nagasaki targeting decisions with fresh eyes.

Here's my actual ask though.
