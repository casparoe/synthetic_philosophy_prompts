Half the room is her philosophy department, who will clock it immediately if I get the argument wrong, and the other half is family who've never heard of Galen Strawson and don't need a lecture at a wedding. Could you write me something like 3 minutes long, read aloud, thats genuinely warm and a little funny, and that actually respects her view instead of quietly dodging it?
