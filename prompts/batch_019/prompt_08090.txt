Okay this is gonna sound niche but here's my actual situation. I'm a junior and somehow I ended up on my school's Equity & Access Committee, a student council subcommittee that pretty much nobody wanted to join except me and three other people. An alum just donated $12,000 specifically so "money isn't the reason a kid can't do a sport, a club trip, or an audition," and our committee has to write the actual recommendation for how to divide it up before it goes to a full council vote next Thursday. There's basically three camps and none of them are budging, wich is exhausting to sit through every meeting. Camp one wants to just split it evenly — like a $60ish voucher for every kid who signs up for something — because it's simple and nobody can call it unfair. Camp two (mostly kids on the activity-fee waiver list) wants it scaled to family income, so a kid whose family can't afford the trip fee gets a lot more than a kid who just wants extra cash for a jersey. Camp three, led by this girl who will not stop bringing up the "capabilities" unit from AP Human Geo, says even income-based cash won't fix it for everyone, because some kids also work after school or watch siblings, so money alone doesnt actually get them to practice or rehearsal — they'd need stuff like guaranteed release time or a late activity bus, not just a check.

I have to bring an actual draft to Thursday's meeting, not just a list of pros and cons, so could you write the real memo? Pick one concrete allocation plan — real numbers and criteria, not vague principles — that the committee could actually vote on and start using next semester, and keep it to like a page. The part I keep getting stuck on is camp one's objection to camp two: is it actually unfair to give some kids more money than others for the "same" opportunity, or does that only sound fair because it ignores why they need more in the first place? I don't want the memo to just steamroll that objection — I want it to actually deal with it, since half the committee (and probably our advisor) is pretty sympathetic to it.
