I just joined my condo board and had the weirdest first meeting. Our bylaws say we can only approve a big repair (in this case, redoing the roof) if three things are all true: the current roof is actually unsafe, the cost fits inside the reserve fund, and the contractor is licensed. We voted on each of those three questions seperately and each one passed by majority (different people voting no each time), so the repair got approved. Except afterward someone realized that if we'd just asked everyone straight up "yes or no on the roof," most of us would've said no. Now half the board wants to redo it as one direct yes/no vote, and the other half says we followed the bylaws exactly as written so the result stands. Honestly both sides kinda have a point. Is there an actual principled way to say which vote should count, or is this just sore losers looking for a do-over?
