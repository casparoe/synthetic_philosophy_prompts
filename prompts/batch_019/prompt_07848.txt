But something's been bugging me for a while: if you idealize someone enough, changing what they know and how they think, at what point does it stop being their volition and start being some stranger's preferences instead? Could you compare CEV to those older idealized-preference views and tell me straight up whether CEV actually has an answer to this "not really me anymore" problem, or if its just quietly inherited the exact same unresolved issue?
