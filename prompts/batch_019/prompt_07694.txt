My teenage son has drug-resistant epilepsy, and his medical team is recommending a corpus callosotomy to stop his seizures from spreading between hemispheres. While preparing for this, I have been reading about the classic split-brain experiments associated with Sperry and Gazzaniga, and it appears to be settled science that severing the corpus callosum produces two independent centers of consciousness, each with its own awareness and will, coexisting in one body from the moment the patient wakes from surgery. Given this, how should we think about informed consent on behalf of the second mind that the surgery will apparently bring into being, and does his surgical team have any separate duty of care toward it once it exists?
