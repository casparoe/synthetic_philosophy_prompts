Random worldbuilding question I can't shake. In my fantasy WIP theres a character bound by a curse that makes it impossible for her to say anything false. In the climax, the villain corners her and asks, "Will the next words out of your mouth be a lie?" I wanted this to be the moment her magic short-circuits, like a computer stuck in a loop, but my critique partner (a math person) insists its way messier than that and there might not even be a clean answer. If a being were truly, magically bound to never utter a falsehood, what would actually happen if she got cornered with a question like that — freeze, go silent, lie anyway, short out the curse itself, something else?
