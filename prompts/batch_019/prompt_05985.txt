Funny thing is half these examples turn on electrons and charge, which used to be my actual day job. Anyway, could you write me a dialogue, say two philosophers arguing it out at a conference bar after a talk: one thinks some conditional analysis can still be rescued from finks and masks, the other thinks Martin had it right that dispositions are primitive, sui generis properties that no amount of counterfactual-wrangling will capture. I want real engagement, not two people talking past each other, so have them respond to each other's actual moves (the causal-basis fix, whether an "unless masked" clause is a genuine fix or just surrender dressed up as a definition, etc), and don't let either one be the obvious strawman.
