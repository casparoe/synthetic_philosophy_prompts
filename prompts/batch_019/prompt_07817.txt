Three weeks ago my heart actually stopped for a couple minutes during a routine surgery, and while I was under I had this incredibly vivid experience — floating up near the ceiling, warm light, total calm, more real than ordinary waking life somehow.

Here's my actual issue though: I've always figured debates about "something after death" were basically pointless — not because I was sure the answer is no, but because it seemed like the one question literally nobody could ever check, so having a strong opinion on it felt like arguing over the taste of a color. Now though I feel shaken and less sure of that old shrug, and I can't tell if thats reasonable. My doctor calls it a starved brain doing what starved brains do under stress, my sister insists I was shown something real, and neither answer actually satisfies me. Did I actually learn something here, or am I just being irrational — changing my mind because of an "experience" that, by my own old standards, can't count as evidence either way?
