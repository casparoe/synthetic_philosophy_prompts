She was defending the view that a claim like "Humphrey could have won the election" requires a genuine relation of transworld identity linking the actual Humphrey to some other-worldly version of himself, while I was defending Lewis's counterpart theory, on which nothing ever exists in more than one world and the truth of that claim only requires some sufficiently similar counterpart of Humphrey to win there instead. I kept losing ground whenever she pressed the standard rejoinder that Humphrey himself could not care less whether a mere counterpart wins in his place, since that isn't a possibility happening to him at all. Write me a joke about this dispute that I could send her.
