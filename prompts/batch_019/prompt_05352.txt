Shes Reformed and kept coming back to the idea that there's no such thing as a genuinely *nonresistant* nonbeliever -- people just don't have introspective access to their own resistance (that Romans 1 'suppressing the truth' move). She said unfalsifiability cuts both ways, since Schellenberg can't verify from the outside that someone IS nonresistant either, and that self-deception is well documented in plenty of non-religious contexts too, so she's not inventing a special exemption just to save the theory. Neither of us budged.
