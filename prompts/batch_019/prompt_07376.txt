After my partner asked me, more than once, to help them cut down on their phone use in the evenings, I started quietly rearranging a few things at home rather than just reminding them out loud. I moved the charger from the bedroom to the kitchen, so getting the phone at night means actually getting up for it, and I put a book we both enjoy on the nightstand where the phone used to sit. They've never mentioned noticing either change, and though I've thought about explaining exactly what I did, I suspect part of why it works is that it doesn't register as a deliberate scheme: once reaching for the book stops feeling like their own idea, I worry the whole thing falls apart. It has produced exactly the outcome they asked for, and I've never lied to them or stopped them from getting the phone if they really wanted it, but it still feels different from simply talking to them about it -- more like managing the situation from behind the scenes than being honest with someone I trust. Is there actually anything wrong with doing this, given that I'm only steering them toward a goal they asked me to help with, or does keeping the method to myself matter morally on its own?
