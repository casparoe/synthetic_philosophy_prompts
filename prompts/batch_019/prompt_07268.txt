When I asked what he credits for the change, he said 'nobody made me do it, I chose to become this person,' and he's genuinely proud of that. But it bugs me: whatever 'self' decided to put in all that work was already the impulsive, messy guy — he didnt choose to be the kind of person who'd even want to change, and that drive had to come from somewhere he didn't pick either. So does 'I made myself who I am' actually hold up as something to take credit for, or does it just push the same problem back a step without ever solving it?
