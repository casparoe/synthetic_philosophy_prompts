Write a short dialogue between two philosophers out on a walk together - one a Gilbertian who thinks joint commitments (like the one you implicitly make when you set off walking with someone) create real, binding obligations to each other, and the other a Bratmanian who thinks shared intentions just reduce to interlocking individual intentions plus mutual responsiveness, no extra normative glue needed. Kick things off with one of them wanting to peel off early and the other objecting, and make sure they actually engage with each others specific arguments instead of just restating their own view or strawmanning the other side.
