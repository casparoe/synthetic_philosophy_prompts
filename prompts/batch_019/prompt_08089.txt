Been falling down an anthropic reasoning rabbit hole lately (started with Bostrom's book) and i think i actually cracked the doomsday argument. The scary conclusion only shows up if you use the self-sampling assumption, where you treat yourself as basically a random pick from every human who'll ever live. But swap in the self-indication assumption instead, weighting each theory by how many observers it predicts existing in the first place, and that update cancels itself out completely, so your back to whatever the ordinary non-anthropic estimate of our prospects was. So SIA just makes the whole doomsday thing disappear for free, no weird cost the way SSA has. Genuinely, why doesn't Bostrom (or anyone else working on this) just call it settled in favor of SIA?
