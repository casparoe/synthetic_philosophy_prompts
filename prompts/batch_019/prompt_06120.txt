I've taught this argument in my phil of mind seminar for a decade now and every single year the class splits almost exactly in half between people who find it obviously right and people who think its a parlor trick, so I know theres real material to mine here, but I really don't want the episode to just be her restating the argument for listeners who've already gotten the potted version from a Penrose pop-science book.

Can you draft me 8 to 10 interview questions that press on where the argument is actually weakest, rather than ones that just invite her to re-explain it?
