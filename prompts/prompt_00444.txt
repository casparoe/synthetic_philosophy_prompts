So I'm in the middle of deciding between two offers for my next book and it's messing with my head in a way I can't quite justify. Publisher A is a mid-size house I've worked with before - I know exactly what my royalty rate would be, roughly how many copies comparable books in my genre have moved, the whole marketing playbook laid out in black and white. Publisher B is this newer boutique imprint that's clearly hungry and enthusiastic about my work, but their contract is vaguer on marketing spend and they won't give me hard numbers on past sales since they're still building out their catalogue - it could genuinely be way better than A, or worse, I have no real way to tell. My agent ran some rough numbers and thinks the expected payout is comparable, maybe even a bit higher with B given how aggressive they've been pushing newer authors lately.

Here's the thing though - every time I sit down to actually sign something, I feel this strong gut pull toward A, purely because I *know* what I'm getting, even though logically the uncertainty around B could just as easily break in my favor. I brought this up with a writer friend and she said I'm just being risk-averse, but that doesn't feel like the right description to me - it's not that I think B's outcomes are worse, it's that I can't even assign odds to them, and that itself feels unbearable in a way plain risk doesn't. Is there something real underneath this feeling, distinct from ordinary caution, and if so, should I be pushing back against it or actually letting it guide the decision?
