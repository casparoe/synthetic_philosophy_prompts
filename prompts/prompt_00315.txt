For the past fifteen years, my spouse and I have applied a house rule in our regular card game: "the higher card wins ties, unless both cards are paired, in which case the second-highest card decides." A situation arose last week that the rule had simply never had to adjudicate before, and each of us was certain we were resolving it the way we'd "always done," yet we ended up favoring opposite outcomes. Neither of us can point to anything in our past behavior, or in our private sense of what we meant, that settles whose interpretation is correct. Is there a fact of the matter about which rule we've actually been following all along, or is this disagreement unresolvable in principle?
