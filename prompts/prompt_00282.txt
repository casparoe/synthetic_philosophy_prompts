I'm working on a scene in my novel where two philosophy grad students end up arguing late at night after a conference dinner, a few drinks in. One of them is a strict lexical negative utilitarian -- for her, any amount of suffering outweighs any amount of happiness, full stop. The other is suffering-focused too, but rejects the lexical threshold and thinks some tradeoffs are still possible. I want the dialogue to center on the classic "button that painlessly ends all sentient life instantly" thought experiement -- have them actually wrestle with each other's objections (the lexical view's supposed absurdity vs. the non-lexical view's supposed inconsistency) instead of just repeating their own talking points. Neither should be a pushover for the other.
