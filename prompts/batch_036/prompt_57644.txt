Second one, I do the books for a community radio station. We only have bandwidth to get one grant application in before the deadline this cycle. Nate wrote both out on the back of a menu and says they're the same decision. So if I prefer the sure 3,000 to the deal — which I do — I'm commited to preferring the local foundation over the state one. And I didn't. He says that means one of my two choices is flat-out a mistake, and neither of us can say which.

And the grant call wasn't just me, six other people voted for it, so it's not even my preference we're grading.

So, two questions. Is he actually right that I can't have both of these, or has he just found a gotcha that doesn't mean anything about what I should do? And practically — the application goes in Friday, do I need to walk the board back to the small foundation?
