I am writing up my own position for a philosophy of mind seminar, and it is built around Hofstadter's careenium: a pinball-like chamber filled with simmballs that careen, collide and clump, where the clumpings can be read as symbols standing in inferential relations to one another. In my version the chamber has no sensors and no effectors — only the balls and their dynamics. Here is the position I intend to defend.

1. Physical causal closure is non-negotiable: for every simmball's trajectory there is a complete physical account that leaves no causal work outstanding.
2. So there is no downward causation: the symbolic level never supplies a push that the physical level does not already contain.
3. The symbolic level is nonetheless not a mere redescription. Which clumpings constitute a symbol of six, and which constitute a symbol of primeness, are facts about the careenium rather than projections of an interpreter's interests, and those facts are what make the machine's activity about arithmetic rather than about something else.
4. Were a careenium's clumpings richly enough isomorphic to a system of beliefs and desires, it would genuinely have those beliefs and desires, and not merely behave as though it did.
5. Content is fixed by causal coupling: a state is about what it is about in virtue of reliable causal commerce between tokens of that type and what they stand for.
6. I accept the Chinese Room intuition in this restricted form: nothing about a system's internal transition rules, taken on their own, settles what its states mean, or whether they mean anything at all.
7. Higher-level explanatory claims never compete with physical ones; they are made true by the physical facts while remaining irreducible to them.

Please treat this as a set to be audited rather than a view to be expounded. Which of these commitments genuinely pull against one another, and which only appear to? I would like the real tensions ranked by severity rather than presented as equally damaging, with an explicit statement for each of what would have to be abandoned or weakened and what that revision would cost me elsewhere in the list.
