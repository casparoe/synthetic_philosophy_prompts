The thing I want the form to actually do, not just decorate: the strong version cant get off the ground, because a precaution is itself an activity with its own risk profile, so applied consistently the principle tells you to be cautious about your caution too. And the final two lines should be the same words as the opening couplet, reordered, and mean pretty much the opposite of what they meant the first time.

Don't land it on either the strong or the weak version. I want the room to feel the paralysis and then argue about it afterwards.
