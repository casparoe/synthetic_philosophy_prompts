what I want built: a case where somebody's report about their own experience right now is simply false. present tense, coarse grained — not a memory, not a subtle shade of color, not "why did I do that," more like am I anxious / do I feel sick / is there a noise. and it has to survive all four of the usual escapes:

- no confabulation — they're not explaining anything, they get asked a direct question and answer straight away
- they're actually attending to the very thing at the moment they report on it

the probe-fragility has to be load bearing, not decoration. I want to be able to point at it working.
