I have written up my position on material constitution for a reading group and should like a consistency check before I circulate it. Here are the commitments:

(1) The statue and the lump of clay it is made from are two numerically distinct objects in the same place at the same time, and both are perfectly real: I am neither a four-dimensionalist nor an eliminativist here. (2) What makes them two rather than one is a difference in their persistence conditions — the lump could survive being squeezed into a ball and the statue could not, and the lump was already there before the sculptor began — a difference that leaves their parts, and their spatial location at every moment of overlap, exactly the same. (3) Persistence conditions are supplied by kind membership: to be a statue or a lump is to fall under a kind, and the kind fixes the answer to 'could this have survived that?'; and these kinds are our classificatory inventions rather than joints we discover in nature. (4) Even so, how many objects are on the plinth is a plain, non-interest-relative fact about the world, settled independently of anyone's counting practices. (5) The reasoning carries over to me: I am not the human animal in this chair, since the animal could outlast me in a merely vegetative way and I could not, though we coincide spatially throughout. (6) Thinking, on the other hand, is nothing over and above what the matter in the chair is doing, and there is exactly one thinker there. (7) All the causal work in the room is done by the particles and their arrangement, and I am unwilling to say that any perfectly real object is systematically causally inert. (8) A genuinely new object came into being when the sculptor finished, though no new matter did.

Please audit the set rather than survey the debate: state which of these genuinely pull against one another, ranked from most to least damaging, and for each say what would have to be given up or reinterpreted and what that costs; state separately which conflicts are merely apparent, and how they dissolve once stated carefully; and finish with the single revision that would buy the most consistency overall. I have no settled view about which item I would sacrifice, so please do not assume I am attached to any of them.
