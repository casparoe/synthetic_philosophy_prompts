Good's result that the expected value of free information can't be negative, since you could always just ignore what you learn — quietly leans on a stack of assumptions that rarely get spelled out: utilities attach to worldly outcomes only, the act set doesn't change when you learn, updating is by conditionalization, the agent has perfect recall, and she acts optimally at whatever decision node she ends up at.
