ok so I want a joke about the menu of free wills — like there's a shop or a vending machine and the grades are on offer: the libertarian one, the compatibilist one, something in between. Think Dennett's "free will worth wanting," except I want the joke and not the argument.

Ground rules. No "you didn't choose to read this" gags, nothing punning on quantum randomness, and no pure taxonomy recitation where the only humor is that philosophers like to sort things into bins. The funny part should be a costumer who doesn't see why the premium uncaused-cause model is the one nobody who's thought about it for five minutes actually wants to buy (an action that nothing about you explains isn't more *yours*, it's just noise), and who is equally suspicious of the bargain-bin compatibilist model that basically every working philosopher ends up purchasing while swearing blind it isn't a downgrade. My instinct for the punchline: the customer asks for the version that hands them ultimate origination *and* still lets their choices follow intelligibly from their character, and gets told that one isn't in stock and never was. If you can land something better on that same fault line, go for it — but the joke has to fail for someone who thinks compatibilists are just sneaking determinism back in under a new name, i.e. it should only pay off if you know what each variety actually delivers and what each one actually costs.

Under 150 words please. Bonus if there's a determinist in the shop who's just browsing.
