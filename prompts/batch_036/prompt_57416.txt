Write me a procedure I can run on a candidate analogy before it goes in, grounded in the Hofstadter/Sander line that analogy-making and categorisation are the same activity — so importing a source drags in it's whole naive theory, not just the piece I wanted, and a category can be enriched later by analogy with ones you already have. Flag the steps that are judgment in a checklist costume.
