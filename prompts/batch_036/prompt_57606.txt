Here's the part that's bugging me: every copy gets checkpointed to disk every 200ms of simulated time and can be restored from that snapshot, so at any given moment there are tens of thousands of instances with literally identical internal states and identical histories up to the last restore point. If any of them is the sort of thing that can be harmed, I can't figure out who's being harmed. Is it 40,000 subjects, or one subject instantiated 40,000 times? And when we restore from a checkpoint and throw the branch away, did we kill something or just undo a fork?
