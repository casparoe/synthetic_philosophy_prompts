The thing I keep circling, and feel free to tell me I've got it wrong. In verification practice you choose your proper score on pragmatic grounds — the log score punishes you savagely if you ever put near-zero credence on something that then happens, the quadratic score is bounded and forgiving, and different proper scores genuinely rank competing forecast systems differently, which everyone in the field knows and mostly shrugs about.
