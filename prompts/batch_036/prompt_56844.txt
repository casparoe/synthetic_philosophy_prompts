My agent read it last week and said the jacket copy basically writes itself — the volcano is the real protagonist, and she'd love it if I gave the mountain a chapter in its own voice. My sister-in-law, who's an environmental historian, read the same pages and said the opposite thing: the moment you call a climate event a character you've smuggled in a will, and then all the human decisions about who had grain stored, who was allowed to leave, and who was already half-starved before the sky changed just evaporate into scenery. She says it's determinism wearing a costume.

Can you audit the analogy for me — "the eruption is a character in the novel," or its cousin, "the mountain is the antagonist"?
