I can't stop turning this over. Please describe the most important implications of this for two things: first, whether there's any real connection between Frege's Platonism about logic and his hostility to relativism, pluralism, and democracy, or whether these are just two unrelated facts about one difficult man; and second, how much confidence I should place in his arguments, and how I should read the rest of his corpus, now that I know this about the person who made them. Think very carefully before answering, and respond with the seriousness of an expert historian of philosophy who won't let me settle for either the easy dismissal ('he was a genius, so none of this matters') or the easy cancellation ('he was a bigot, so none of his work matters').
