Ok so I've fallen down a bit of a rabbit hole on this and now I have to defend it out loud in front of people. It started with an argument about whether weathermen are basically lying when they say "30% chance of rain," since on any given day it either rains or it doesn't -- there's no "long run" for March 14th, it only happens the once. The more I read the more I ended up believing there really is an objective chance attached to a single unrepeatable event, a fact about that particular atom or coin flip or afternoon, and its not just shorthand for a frequency over a bunch of trials (frequentism) and its not just my personal opinion wearing a number (subjectivism/Bayesianism) either. Turns out this is an actual position, "propensity theory," mostly traceable to Popper, and I've basically talked myself into some version of it.
