Last month, in one of the pilot homes, a woman with early stage dementia (still legally competent per her file) tried to leave the house alone at 2am in a snowstorm. Nobody got hurt this time, but now the board has to decide by friday whether we push the current setup to 200 more homes as is, pause the rollout to redesign the conflict logic, or approve it with some guardrail bolted on, and since i built the thing i'm the one stuck writing the memo. Can you write me that memo?
