Write a dialogue between a Parfitian and a Nozickian standing next to a teletransporter that just malfunctioned and spat out two duplicates instead of one. The Parfitian says this proves identity isn't what matters for survival, only psychological continuity is. The Nozickian, running a 'closest continuer' theory, actually agrees the original person doesn't survive as either duplicate, but insists thats a totally different (and much less radical) conclusion than the Parfitian's, since identity is still what matters in every ordinary, non-tied case. Get them really arguing about that, plus the awkward bit where, on the Nozickian view, whether you survive as the guy on the left depends on some unrelated fact about whether a duplicate happened to show up on the right too.
