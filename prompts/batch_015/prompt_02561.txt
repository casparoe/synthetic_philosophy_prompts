Recently a colleague told me she had overheard my closest friend saying unflattering things about me, and showed me a text exchange that seemed to confirm it. My first instinct was that this had to be some kind of misunderstanding, since years of experience have already taught me that my friend simply wouldn't do that. But then I noticed something strange about that reaction: if I truly already know my friend wouldn't do this, then I also already know that any "evidence" to the contrary must be misleading somehow, which seems to license me to dismiss it out of hand, without even looking closely. That feels like exactly the wrong way to treat new evidence, but I can't pinpoint where the reasoning actually goes wrong. Where's the mistake, if there is one?
