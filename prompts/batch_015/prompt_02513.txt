So I've been down a Schopenhauer rabbit hole for a piece I'm reporting on AI and creativity, specifically the bit in The World as Will and Representation where he argues music is totally unlike every other art. Painting, sculpture, poetry, architecture -- on his account all of those are copies of the Platonic Ideas, but music skips the Ideas entirely and is a direct copy of the Will itself, which is why he says music is so independent of the physical world that it could exist "to a certain extent" even if there were no world at all. There's also this specific claim of his I can't shake: real composers aren't working "from reflection," they're basically unconscious mouthpieces for the Will, like a mesmerized patient reporting truths they don't understand themselves -- so in a composer, unlike in a novelist or a painter, the person and the artist are supposedly totally seperate.

Which got me wondering: what would he make of Suno and Udio, the AI models that can now generate a fully arranged, perfectly listenable pop song -- hook, key change, and a lead vocal you'd swear was a real singer belting into a real mic -- in under a minute, and that are currently getting sued into the ground by the major labels over exactly this? (I played my editor a generated "lost 60s soul single" last week and she was genuinely fooled.) Part of his framework seems built to dismiss this stuff outright: an AI has no Will of its own, nothing to suffer or strive for, so whatever it makes should be the empty, formulaic kind of music that comes from mere reflection rather than genius. But another big part of his theory is about the music's internal structure -- the specific pattern of tension and resolution that he says just is the shape of desire and satisfaction -- and if that structural pattern is really what's doing the metaphysical work, it's not obvious why it should matter whose "will," if anyone's, produced it. Can you work through how he'd actually try to reason his way through that tension, using the specific moving parts of his own theory, rather than just assuming he'd have hated it on general pessimist principle?
