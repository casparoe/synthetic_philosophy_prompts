The associate pastor did his formation with the Jesuits though, and pushed back hard with the whole Molina/middle knowledge thing: that God just knows, via "simultaneous concurrence" or whatever it's called, how any possible person would freely act in any possible situation, and providentially sets up the circumstances accordingly, no predetermining required.
