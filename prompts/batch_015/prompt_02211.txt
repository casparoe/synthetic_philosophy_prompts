I've been down a Schopenhauer rabbit hole this week for a piece on AI music generators (Suno, Udio, etc) and can't shake one question. His whole theory of music in Book 3 of *World as Will and Representation* hinges on music being a direct copy of the Will itself, totally unmediated by the Platonic Ideas the way painting or sculpture are -- which is supposedly why it hits us harder than any other art. He even maps the four voices onto grades of nature (bass = blind gravitational mass, tenor/alto = plant and animal life, melody = restless conscious human striving), and he's pretty scornful of composers who just do tone-painting instead of channeling the will directly -- see his jab at Haydn's Seasons. Plus he says the composer reveals the will's inner nature without rationally understanding it, like a sleepwalker. So: does a model trained on millions of hours of audio, with no will or striving behind it at all, even qualify as making "music" under his own definition? Or would he say its necessarily just the empty tone-painting he mocked, or maybe something his whole system can't even categorize? Use his actual arguments, don't just guess at the vibe.
