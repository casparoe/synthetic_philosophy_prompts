Okay so I've never studied this stuff formally, I just fall down internet rabbit holes at like midnight, but lately I can't stop thinking about the Condorcet Jury Theorem. From what I understand it's this old probability idea (some French guy from the 1700s) that says if you get a group of people to vote on something, and each person is only a little bit more likely than not to be right, like 51% right, and crucially they all vote totally independently without talking it over first, then the bigger the group gets the closer the majority vote gets to being correct, like it approaches 100% as you add more people. Which is probly a pretty wild claim when you actually sit with it, cause it basically means a herd of mediocre people can out-perform a genius as long as those two conditions actually hold.
