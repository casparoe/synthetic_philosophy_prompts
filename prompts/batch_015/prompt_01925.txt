"...I have held it demonstrated that we are not able in any manner to prove that there is in the animals a soul which thinks. [...] there are other stronger and more numerous arguments for the opposite opinion [...] as, for example, that it is more reasonable to make earth-worms, flies, caterpillars, and the rest of the animals, move as machines do, than to endow them with immortal souls [...] seeing no reason anywhere why thought is to be found wherever we perceive a conformation of bodily members like that of the animals, and that it is more surprising that there should be a soul in every human body than that there should be none at all in the brutes.
