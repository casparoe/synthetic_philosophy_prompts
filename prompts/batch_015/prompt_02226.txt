I don't have any formal training in game theory, but I spend a good deal of time reading independent writing on how AI systems might avoid costly conflict when bargaining under uncertainty, and one idea that keeps coming up is the 'safe Pareto improvement.' As I understand it, two parties stuck in a mutually costly conflict, driven by private information that the other side lacks (about resolve, strength, or how much they value the disputed thing), can sometimes commit in advance to a modified way of playing the underlying game that is guaranteed to leave each of them at least as well off as their original outcome, whatever their private information turns out to be, without either side having to reveal anything that could be exploited should the arrangement fall through.

Here is a toy case I worked out to test my understanding. Two firms are each privately unsure how much the other values a contract, and in the resulting equilibrium they sometimes escalate into a costly bidding war. Suppose a mediator can design several different schemes, each combining a shared randomizing device with side payments, that are all 'safe' in the sense just described: each guarantees both firms at least their expected payoff from escalating, whatever their true valuations happen to be. But the schemes are not all equally good for both sides: one is more generous to Firm A, another to Firm B, and so on across a whole range of safe options. So each firm has an incentive to hold out for the scheme that favors it, and to threaten reversion to the old, costly equilibrium if the other side won't agree to that particular one.

It seems to me that this just reproduces, one level up, the very bargaining problem that safe Pareto improvements were supposed to get rid of: instead of fighting over the contract, the firms now fight over which safe arrangement to adopt, with the same brinkmanship as before. Please argue for this claim as forcefully as you can, and then address the strongest response available to someone who thinks safe Pareto improvements are nonetheless a genuine advance over ordinary bargaining.
