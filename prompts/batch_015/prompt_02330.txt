Years ago, when we were both junior employees who felt stuck in our jobs, I told a colleague, somewhat casually, that if I were ever in a position to hire, I would bring them onto my team. I have since become a director with an opening, and this colleague, who says they turned down another job offer while waiting for mine to come through, has reminded me of what I said back then. They are competent, but clearly a weaker fit than another candidate who applied through the normal process, and choosing them anyway would be unfair to both my team and my employer. Does a promise made so casually, and under such different circumstances, still bind me now, or am I free to hire the stronger candidate? And if I am not entirely free, what, if anything, would I still owe my old colleague?
