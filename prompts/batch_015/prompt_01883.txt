Meanwhile Woodwards interventionist account (Making Things Happen) defines X causing Y through a hypothetical surgical intervention on X within a structural equation/graph, and apparently gets a lot of the same preemption cases for free just by holding certain other variables fixed at their actual values. What I want to know is: is that structural-equations move a genuinely principled advance on Lewis's preemption patches, or is it cheating, in that all the real work has already been smuggled in by whoever drew the causal graph? And relatedly -- I keep running into the charge that Woodward's theory is "circular" since an intervention is itself a causal notion.
