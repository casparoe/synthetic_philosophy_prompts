Last week, after a family gathering, two slices of pizza were left in the box, one noticeably bigger than the other. While everyone was still around, I took the smaller slice so as not to seem greedy. The next morning, alone in the kitchen with both slices still there, I ate the bigger one without a second thought. My friend, who is taking an economics elective, told me this shows that my preferences are inconsistent, since a rational person is supposed to choose the same way whenever facing the same options. That conclusion feels wrong to me, but I am having trouble explaining why. Was I really being irrational, or is my friend missing something about what should count as "the same choice"?
