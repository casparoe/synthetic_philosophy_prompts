The doctor said we could still try daily hGH shots for a few years, since the FDA has actually had this approved for ISS since 2003, but our insurance denied it flat-out as "not medically necessary" -- because unlike a kid with diagnosed GH deficiency, there's technically no disease here, we'd just be nudging an otherwise-normal kid to be taller than she'd naturally end up.

There's this idea (I think from a guy named Norman Daniels?) that medicine should only restore "normal species functioning," which sounds right and I definately want it to be true, until you notice the cutoff for "short stature" is just a statistical line someone drew -- a kid one hair above it gets nothing, one hair below gets treated as diseased.
