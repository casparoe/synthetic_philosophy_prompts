My city's transit agency is short on funding and needs to cut bus service somewhere. Instead of trimming a little from every route (the fairest option, probably), the director wants to fully cut the one line serving a well-loved low income senior center, even though that route has nothing to do with the shortfall, because he figures the backlash from angry seniors on the local news will shame the city council into restoring funding way faster then spreading the pain thin ever would, saving more total service in the end. I get that the math works out, but making these seniors suffer on purpose so their suffering does the political work feels different to me than a fair cut that also happens to hurt people. Is there an actual moral difference there, or am I just getting swayed by whichever option sounds more sympathetic?
