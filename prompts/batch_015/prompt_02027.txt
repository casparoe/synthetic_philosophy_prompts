I want the scene's tension to turn on something I gather is technically real, not just a rhetorical trick I'm inventing for the plot: that the verdict on who was more accurate isn't independent of which strictly proper scoring rule is used to grade the two of them. I've read enough Joyce and Pettigrew to know this kind of scoring-rule-relativity is a genuine live issue in accuracy-first epistemology, but not enough to construct a correct instance of it myself.
