Something happened recently that I can't fully make sense of. A while back, I checked the label on a box of chocolate chip cookies before serving them at an ordinary family gathering, and happily told everyone, 'don't worry, these don't have any peanuts in them.' Last week, I checked the exact same label on the exact same box before giving a cookie to a friend's son, who has a severe, life-threatening peanut allergy. This time, even though my evidence hadn't changed at all, it felt reckless to say outright, 'I know these are peanut-free.' Did what I actually knew change simply because more was riding on it the second time? Does the word 'know' mean something different depending on the situation? Or was I wrong to feel so sure either time? I'd like to understand what's really going on here, rather than just being told to be careful with allergies.
