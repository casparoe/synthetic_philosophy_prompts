Random personal question, nothing to do with the actual book for once. Most mornings before I start writing I read my horoscope, fully aware its nonsense — I don't actually think Mars is affecting my plot outlines. But spending five minutes convinced the universe has some kind of plan for my day makes me way more willing to sit down and work instead of doom-scrolling. My partner thinks this is a bit sad, like I'm choosing a comforting lie over just facing the day as it actually is, and that real happiness has to come from seeing things clearly, not from little self-administered fictions. Something about that argument feels off to me but I can't quite say what. Who's actually right here, him or me?
