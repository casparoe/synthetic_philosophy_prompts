A friend of mine who works in formal semantics and I spent most of a long dinner after a workshop arguing about how to respond to Lewis's triviality results for indicative conditionals. We agreed on the setup: Adams's Thesis, what Lewis calls "the Equation," holds that the probability of "if A, B" equals the conditional probability P(B|A), and Lewis (later joined by Gibbard, arguing from a somewhat different set of premises) showed that this cannot hold in full generality without absurd consequences, such as forcing near enough any two propositions you like to come out probabilistically independent of each other. Where we disagreed was over which of the argument's other premises should be blamed instead. My friend wants to hold onto Import-Export — the principle that "if A, then if B, C" and "if A and B, C" always stand or fall together — since it isn't just intuitive but, as she kept pointing out, seems to be borne out by how people actually use nested conditionals. She's willing to pay for this by denying that conditionals are truth-apt at all, treating their "probabilities" as assertability values fixed by a recursive rule rather than as degrees of belief in some proposition. I don't want to give up the Equation that cheaply, and would rather say that Import-Export, though compelling enough for a single layer of embedding, isn't the fully general law it's often advertised to be.

I didn't manage to settle this over dinner, so I'd like help making the case properly. Please write the strongest argument you can for preserving the Equation/Adams's Thesis and rejecting unrestricted Import-Export instead, rather than following my friend in abandoning truth-conditional content for conditionals altogether. I'd especially like the argument to explain why the Equation deserves to be treated as the more fundamental commitment of the two, to offer some principled story for why we're intuitively drawn to Import-Export even if it fails once conditionals are nested deeply enough, and to say briefly why this is preferable to the Stalnaker/Lewis strategy of keeping a fully truth-conditional semantics for conditionals while simply letting Import-Export fail outright.
