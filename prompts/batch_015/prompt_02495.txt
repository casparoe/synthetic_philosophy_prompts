Two people who followed his advice and went all-in for several weeks running have already lost their entire balance, but he maintains that this is just an unlucky run and doesn't undermine the underlying reasoning.

I can't shake the sense that something is wrong here, but his expected-value arithmetic checks out, and I don't have a clean rebuttal to the law-of-large-numbers point either.
