I mentioned this to my roommate, who's doing a philosophy PhD, and she said it reminded her of something called Bradley's regress and the "unity of the proposition" problem -- apparently a 19th century argument (from F.H. So: can you spell out the most important implications of Bradley's regress and the unity-of-proposition problem for how people who design knowledge representation systems should think about representing facts as triples?
