I'm taking a course on formal epistemology this term, and a situation in my own life has me stuck on a puzzle connected to something we've discussed about rational belief change over time. Over winter break I'm planning to attend a ten-day silent meditation retreat, and from talking to several friends who've done it, the outcome is strikingly uniform: nearly everyone returns convinced they must make some drastic life change — leaving a relationship, dropping out of school, switching careers — regardless of how settled they felt about their life beforehand. I currently have no inclination whatsoever to leave my graduate program, but I can already predict, with fair confidence, that I will come back from the retreat convinced that I should. Does the mere fact that I can foresee holding that conviction give me any reason to start taking it seriously now, before I've even set foot on the retreat, or is a foreseeable change of mind like this one simply not the kind of thing that should move me?
