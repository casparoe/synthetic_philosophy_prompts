I am trying to decide which of two literary agents to query about my next novel, and while comparing their track records I compiled some figures that have left me puzzled. Agent A has sold 45 of the 100 literary-fiction manuscripts she has represented, and 18 of her 20 commercial-fiction manuscripts. Agent B has sold only 8 of her 20 literary-fiction manuscripts, and 85 of her 100 commercial ones — a lower rate than Agent A in each category, by my calculation. Yet when I combined each agent's totals across both categories, Agent B's overall sale rate came out well ahead of Agent A's. A friend in my writing group insists this must mean I have made an arithmetic error, since an agent who trails in every category surely cannot end up ahead once the categories are combined. I have checked the figures twice, and they are correct. Is my friend right that something here has to be a mistake, or can both sets of numbers be true at once — and if so, whose record should actually guide my decision, given that I write literary fiction?
