For the past year, the only way I can get my ten-year-old to sit down and do his homework is to promise him screen time once he's finished. When I don't offer that, he whines and negotiates until I give in, so lately I've started threatening to cancel a weekend outing instead if he won't cooperate, which turns even small assignments into a standoff. Either way, he now seems to care only about what he gets or avoids, not about the work itself. My father, who raised four children without any of this negotiating, tells me I've gone soft, that one or two real spankings early on would have settled the matter for good, and that I should also stop letting my son put on a coat every time it's merely a bit chilly outside. I don't want to hit him, but I also can't explain why the reward-and-punishment approach I've been using instead is actually any better, since it isn't working either. What's actually going wrong here, and is my father onto something with his old-fashioned instincts, even if I'd never adopt his methods?
