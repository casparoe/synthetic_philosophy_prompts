Panpsychists generally acknowledge that their view faces "the combination problem" -- the difficulty of explaining how the experience of fundamental micro-level entities could combine to yield the unified experience of ordinary subjects like us. Please explain why this label actually covers several distinct problems rather than one, what these problems are and how they differ from one another, and why a proposed solution to one of them -- such as positing a primitive "phenomenal bonding" relation among micro-subjects -- would not automatically resolve the rest.
