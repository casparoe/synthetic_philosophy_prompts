After I posted an earnest but somewhat clumsy opinion piece on a local online forum, a larger aggregator account picked it up, and for a couple of weeks I became a running joke across several unrelated online communities. Strangers who had never read the original piece competed to make the wittiest put-down of my appearance and writing, and a few even recognized and snickered at me in person -- yet almost none of them seemed to bear any real hostility toward me, and most later described it as 'just banter.' There was no single person I could have confronted or reasoned with about it, and the whole episode felt very different from being criticized by one identifiable critic, even though the individual comments were often quite mild on their own. What is actually going on in a situation like this, and how should I think about responding to it?
