So this happened at work and it's been bugging me. We're scheduling a maintenance window for a database migration, and based on similar ones elsewhere, all we know is it'll take somewhere between 1 and 9 hours, nothing more specific than that. My coworkers take is that since no duration in that range is more likely than any other, we should treat it as uniform, which puts the odds of finishing inside 3 hours at 25%. I said hang on, you could just as easily think of this in terms of rate of progress (percent of the migration done per hour) instead of total time, and if THAT'S what's uniform, the odds of finishing inside 3 hours comes out to 75% instead, for the exact same event. We argued forever about which framing is the "neutral" one, or whether thats even a coherent thing to ask for. Is one of these actually correct, is there some other approach that is, or is "no info, so just assume uniform" simply not valid to begin with?
