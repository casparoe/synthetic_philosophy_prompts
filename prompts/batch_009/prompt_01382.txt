Between classes last week, I was sure I spotted my friend at the other end of a crowded hallway and waved her over, but when she got closer, I realized it was actually a different girl who just happens to have the same hair and backpack. Another friend who saw the whole mix-up argued that this proves I can never really trust my eyes, since in that mistaken moment, my perception felt just as vivid and certain as it does every time I actually do recognize her correctly. Something about that argument feels off to me, but I can't quite explain why one mix-up should cast doubt on all the times I get it right. Is he right that I can't really trust any of my perceptions, even the accurate ones, or is there a flaw in his reasoning?
