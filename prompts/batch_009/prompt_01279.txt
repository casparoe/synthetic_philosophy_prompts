At a recent physical, my doctor told me that my blood pressure -- a reading of about 132/84 that has been stable for years -- now qualifies as Stage 1 hypertension under updated national guidelines, and recommended I consider medication. Under the previous guidelines, that same reading would only have counted as "prehypertension": a borderline risk category, not an actual disease. Nothing about my body has changed; only the definition has. My doctor insists this means I now have a genuine medical condition, but it feels strange to say that a committee redrawing a line on a chart is what made me sick. Is my doctor right that I now have a disease, or is there something confused about putting it that way?
