My grandmother is in hospice with terminal cancer, and her doctor has been steadily raising her morphine dose to keep her pain under control, even though he's told us plainly that at these levels it will probably shorten her life. The family agreed to go ahead with it, since the point is clearly to relieve her suffering rather than to end her life, and everyone around me treats that intention as making this completely different from simply helping her die on purpose. But when I try to explain to myself why the intention should matter morally, given that we are knowingly choosing something that will likely kill her sooner either way, I can't find solid ground to stand on. Is there really a meaningful moral distinction here, or have we just found a more comfortable way of describing the same choice?
