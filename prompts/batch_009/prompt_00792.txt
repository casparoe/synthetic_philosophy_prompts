Fell down a rabbit hole this weekend reading about Schopenhauer's views on music (blame a Wagner youtube video). His big claim is that music is totally different from every other art - painting, poetry, sculpture etc are just copies of "ideas"/forms of things in the world, but music skips that step and directly expresses the same blind underlying "will" that the whole world is made of. He even says the composer does this without understanding it themselves, like a sleepwalker giving out information they have no waking access to, and he trashed "tone painting" - music that just imitates a storm or a battle - as a cheap knockoff of the real thing.

Been messing around with Suno making AI songs lately, which got me wondering: what would old Arthur make of it? There's obviously no suffering, striving genius behind an AI song - its just a model remixing patterns from millions of human songs. Does his own theory mean AI music is automatically hollow, basically glorified tone painting, or is there some way it'd still count as the real deal? Genuinely curious what you think he'd say.
