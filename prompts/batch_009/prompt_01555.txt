Ok this happened about a month ago and I still can't stop thinking about it. I spoke up at our town council meeting about a new apartment development, just said the traffic study seemed off, nothing crazy. The next day our local news site did a quick writeup using a quote from me in the headline, and their Facebook page exploded, like 400+ comments in two days, most from people who dont even live here. It turned into me being "anti-housing," which isn't remotely what I said. What gets me is that not one of those people said anything to me directly, it was just comments piling up on top of each other, each one pretty small on its own, but the total effect felt way worse than if one person had actually confronted me face to face. Meanwhile two neighbors who told me privately they agreed with me never posted a word in my defense. I've talked myself out of going back to the next meeting twice now. Is there something actually different about getting "swarmed" like this versus just having a real disagreement with someone, or am I just being thin-skinned about internet comments?
