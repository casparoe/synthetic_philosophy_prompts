I was recently appointed to my town's zoning board, and next month we are scheduled to vote on a permit application for an adult entertainment venue downtown. Almost none of the public objections at the hearing pointed to any concrete harm — no claims about rising crime, falling property values, or added traffic — just remarks like "this isn't who we are as a town." I don't want to dismiss that reaction as mere prejudice, since a community's sense of its own character seems to matter for something, but I also don't want to block a legal business merely because people find it distasteful. Is there a principled way to weigh "this offends our shared values" against the absence of any real harm, or does that kind of reasoning always collapse into the majority simply enforcing its own tastes?
