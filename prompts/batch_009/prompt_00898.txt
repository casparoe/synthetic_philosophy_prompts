This is probably a very "let's optimize a group decision with math" way to spend mental energy on something as dumb as picking a restaurant, but it's been bugging me. A bunch of us in my friend group rank five or six restaurants whenever we go out and convert the rankings into points to get a winner. Lately I've noticed that if I rank my actual second choice as my first, the group ends up going there instead of whatever wins when i vote honestly (usually something way further down my list). A couple other people have obviously figured out the same trick with their own ballots, so at this point it feels like everyones gaming it a little. We've tried straight point totals, a version that weights first-choice votes more heavily, and something closer to instant-runoff, and the gaming just resurfaces in a different form every time no matter which one we use. Is there an actual scoring system out there that would fix this for good if we just found the right one, or is this something we're never going to be able to engineer our way out of?
