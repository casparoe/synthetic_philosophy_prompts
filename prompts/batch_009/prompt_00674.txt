I've gotten into the Silicon Valley habit of self-optimization: tracking biomarkers, practicing intermittent fasting, cycling in and out of ketosis, and taking a stack of nootropics to sharpen my focus for coding. While reading around the subject, I came across the "Why I Am So Clever" section of Nietzsche's Ecce Homo, where he obsesses at length over nutrition, climate, and recuperation, and blames German cuisine and beer for the fact that "German intellect is indigestion." Given what he actually believed about diet, physiology, and the conditions required to produce great work, would he likely have regarded this kind of biohacking and self-tracking as an admirable expression of self-mastery, or as just another symptom of the comfort-obsessed mediocrity he spent his career attacking?
