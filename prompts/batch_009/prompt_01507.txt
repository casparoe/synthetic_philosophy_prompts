Random personal thing, not a story pitch (yet, maybe) — I signed up for the bone marrow registry years ago at some work health fair and forgot all about it. Got a call last week saying I'm a match for someone who needs a transplant to survive. The donation itself is a real but fairly moderate ordeal, some days of recovery, minor risk, nothing catastrophic. Everyone I've told says some version of "obviously you do it, that's basically nothing compared to saving someones life," and I agree with the logic. But here's what's bugging me: I give a decent chunk of money to causes I trust every month and never think twice about it, yet this has had me lying awake anxious for days in a way money never does. Is there an actual principled difference between owing someone your money versus owing them a piece of your body, or am I just dressing up squeamishness as some kind of ethical puzzle?
