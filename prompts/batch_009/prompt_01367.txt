For an independent research project on theories of consciousness, I've been reading about Giulio Tononi's Integrated Information Theory, which holds that a system's degree of consciousness is determined by Φ, a measure of how much information it integrates across its interacting parts. Since large language models contain billions of parameters that are all densely interconnected while processing a single prompt, it seems like IIT's own standard should assign them an extremely high Φ. Given this, why do the theory's own proponents, such as Tononi himself and Christof Koch, keep insisting that today's leading AI systems are almost certainly not conscious?
