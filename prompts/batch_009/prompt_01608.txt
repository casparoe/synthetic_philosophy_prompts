One of my board mates built this little scoring sheet, rated each kid on stuff like household income, how much they said the program would change their life, teacher recommendations, that kind of thing, and added it all up. His numbers came out a few points higher for one of the girls, so his take was basically: easy, she gets it, we should always just go with whoever the numbers say benefits more, otherwise why bother scoring it in the first place.
