Could you write me a dialogue where a Walzer-style defender of MEC and a McMahan-style revisionist actually go back and forth on this? Neither one should be a strawman, i want them actually responding to each others specific points instead of just repeating their own side's talking points. Bonus if it gets into the epistemic argument about soldiers not always being able to know which side is really just, and whether MEC might still be worth keeping as a legal rule even if its not literally true as a matter of deep morality.
