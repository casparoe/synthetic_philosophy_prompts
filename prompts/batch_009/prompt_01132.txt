Write a dialogue between Maimonides and Nahmanides arguing about why the Torah commands animal sacrifice. Maimonides defends his Guide III:32 line that sacrifice was just a concession to the idolatrous customs the Israelites were already steeped in -- basically training wheels God knew they still needed -- while Nahmanides pushes his classic objection from his Leviticus commentary: Cain, Abel, and Noah were all sacrificing long before there was any idolatry around to wean anybody off of. Don't let Maimonides just concede the point though, have him actually respond to it, and let the arguement go back and forth a couple rounds from there.
