Since retiring from a career in physics I've taken up reading philosophy for fun, and its got me looking at ordinary things differently. Heres one: I sit on the committee of our little astronomy club (about 40 members), and even though the whole committee turns over every couple of years, totally different people each time, we have the exact same dumb fight every single year about whether to spend the telescope fund on a new mirror or a new mount, and it always gets settled the same limp way, splitting the money so we end up buying neither one properly. My wife calls this "the club's culture," but that feels like a cop-out to me, since the club isn't a person and can't have a personality of its own, can it? Is there really such a thing as a group having its own dynamic separate from whoever happens to make it up at the time, or is she just being sloppy with her words?
