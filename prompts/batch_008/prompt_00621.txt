Ok this might sound like a weird thing to be stuck on but its been bugging me for like two weeks now. A while back I got into that whole internet trend where people invent brand new words for really specific feelings that dont have a normal english name, I think it started with that "dictionary of obscure sorrows" thing or something similar to it. I thought it was such a cool idea that I started my own private version in my phone's notes app, just made up words for very particular inner states that I dont think anyone else really notices or has a name for. Like theres this exact mix of restless-bored-guilty I get most sunday evenings, so I just started calling it "florp" and writing it down in a little log every time I felt it, sort of to track it over time and maybe figure out a pattern eventually.

Few weeks into doing this though I realized something that actually kind of freaked me out. I genuinely can't tell if I'm using "florp" consistently. Like what if last month's florp was actually a slightly different feeling and I only tagged it florp because it was the closest match I could remember at the time? There's no way for me, or literally anyone, to check whether I'm applying my own made up word correctly, since by definition nobody else has access to that inner feeling to compare notes with. I mentioned this to my roommate and she said something like "it's your word for your own feeling, so there's no such thing as using it wrong," which was supposed to make me feel better but honestly it kind of made it worse, like that's too easy an out. Is she right that a totally private label like this literally can't be "wrong," or is there something actually broken about trying to track your inner life this way? I can't tell if I'm overthinking a notes app or if I've stumbled onto something that doesn't actually have a clean answer.
