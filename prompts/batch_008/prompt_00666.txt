I got interested in Arrow's theorem, but then ran across Harsanyi's aggregation theorem, which seems to show that once everyone (including "society") is rational in the expected-utility sense, a fair, non-dictatorial way of aggregating preferences just falls out as a weighted sum of individual utilities -- utilitarianism, essentially, as a theorem. Doesnt that mean Harsanyi basically solved Arrow's impossibility result, since we've now got a perfectly good non-dictatorial rule that respects everyone's preferences?
