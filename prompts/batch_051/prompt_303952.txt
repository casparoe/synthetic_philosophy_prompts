You run a small research program on the governance of advanced machine learning systems and are deciding whether to fund a six-month, one-person project in applied philosophy. The working question is whether AIs would raise AIs in anything more than a metaphorical sense. The motivating observation is that discussions of how one AI system shapes another keep reaching for parental language—raising, upbringing, forming values, preparing a system for independent operation—without any worked-out account of whether this is warranted. The engineering vocabulary of fine-tuning, distillation, and reward modeling describes mechanism but not responsibility; the governance vocabulary of oversight, auditing, and control describes constraint but not formation. Write the one- to two-page proposal that would make the case for funding.

The proposal should do the following. First, state a precise question about whether an AI system that shapes another AI system's values, capacities, and degree of independence stands in a relation aptly described as parenthood or guardianship, and whether that description generates obligations the standard vocabularies miss. Distinguish parenthood, guardianship, and a weaker relation such as mentorship or stewardship, and say which the project would defend as applicable, or argue that none is. Second, explain why existing positions leave the question open: the view that training is only optimization of a loss function; the view that AIs are tools or employees with no moral status; the view that only human developers, never an AI, could be the relevant parent. Third, describe a method for a short philosophical project rather than a literature review. The project should build a diagnostic for when parent or guardian language is warranted, test that diagnostic against two or three familiar training arrangements such as distillation, reinforcement learning from human feedback, and constitution- or rule-based self-governance, and answer the strongest objection that the whole framing is a misleading anthropomorphism. Include a rough month-by-month plan. Fourth, say what a useful deliverable would look like for someone designing training pipelines or rules for AI governance, and what would count as the project failing.

Do not include a bibliography or request citations; refer to positions and arguments by description.
