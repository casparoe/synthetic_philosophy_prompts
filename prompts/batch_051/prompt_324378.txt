This is now the fourth time my partner and I have gotten stuck in the same kind of arguement, and after last night I'm convinced the pattern itself must have a name. It always starts as a fight about whether some everyday category is real or just a label we put on things. Last week it was the economy. She said the economy is just a name for a huge bunch of individual transactions and trends; I said that can't be right, because the economy can crash and you can point to an economic crash in a way you can't point to "a bunch." An hour in, we had agreed on every single fact either of us could think of — what central banks do, why prices went up, what the data showed. But it didn't feel sematic either, because she came away thinking that saying "let's fix the economy" is confused, while I thought refusing to say it is just a weird hangup. A few months ago the same shape came up about ADHD: we agreed completely on what the kid does, what the doctor said, and what the medication does, but she says ADHD is a real condition and I lean toward "a useful name for a cluster of behaviors," and somehow that difference leaks into whether a school accommodation counts as treating a disorder or changing the environment.

The part that confuses me is that these arguments pass neither of the tests I usually reach for. It isn't a normal factual disagreement: there's no observation or experiment we can think of that would settle it. But it also isn't one of those arguments where you notice the two sides are just using a word differently and the whole thing dissolves; we've tried saying "you use 'real' one way, I use it another," and the disagreement just comes back. Each of us thinks the other is making an intellectual mistake, not just making a stylistic choice. And even though we can't say exactly what the practical consequences are, the choice of words feels like it's doing some kind of work. Is there a name for this exact kind of dispute, and are there clear boundary conditions for deciding when something like this is entirely about words, about which words are useful, or about something real? I want the carving, not just a label.

Please give me exactly five numbered points, one or two sentences each, no introduction and no conclusion.
