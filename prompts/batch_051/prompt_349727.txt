Ok so the language of thought hypothesis got its main modern defense from something like this: human thought is productive and systematic — you can think an unbounded number of novel thoughts, and if you can think that the dog bit the man you can also think that the man bit the dog — and the best explanation is that thought is built up from parts in a combinatorial symbol system, a kind of mentalese. Fodor and Pylyshyn pushed this as an argument that the connectionist networks of the time couldn't be the whole story, because they didn't have that compositional structure built in. Fast forward to now: we have huge transformers trained on human text that are startlingly good at novel, systematic, compositional behavior — they seem to pass the behavioral tests for systematicity even though nobody wired a language of thought into them. Does Fodor and Pylyshyn's argument survive this development?
