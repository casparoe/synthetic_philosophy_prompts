Say you're running a small research program — three or four postdocs for two years — and the big question is whether left vs right is a real, causally meaningful axis or just a historical accident that glues together a bunch of preferences that don't actually have much to do with each other. As I see it you could spend that time on: (a) whether there is one shared left–right dimension across countries, or whether it's mostly an artifact of a particular party system; (b) what does the gluing — moral psychology, personality, threat sensitivity, or just social sorting and coalitional loyalty; (c) whether economic and social left–right attitudes are really two things and how they relate; (d) whether 'left' and 'right' as labels do real explanatory work or just let people sort into teams. If you had to spend the money on one of these first, then the next, then the next, what order would you pick and why? I want the reasoning, not a reading list: which question is upstream of most others, which would change the most downstream work if it came out one way vs the other, which is actually tractable in two years, and what result would make you reshuffle the order.
