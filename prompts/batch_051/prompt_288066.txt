I'm compiling objections to Harsanyi's aggregation theorem for a seminar. So far I have the ex ante Pareto objection (Diamond's fair lottery: respecting ex ante Pareto can conflict with distributive fairness), the common prior objection (the theorem assumes everyone, including the social planner, uses the same probabilities, which is simply unrealistic), the comparability-smuggling objection (zero-one normalisation of utility scales already assumes interpersonal comparability), the VNM-rationality-for-society objection (the social preference doesn't obviously have to satisfy completeness or independence), and the arbitrariness-of-weights objection (the theorem gives a weighted sum but nothing pins down the weights). I want one more genuine objection, not a restatement or special case of these. Also tell me honestly whether its any good and whether it generalises beyond Harsanyi to social aggregation in general.
