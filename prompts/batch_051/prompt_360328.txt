I am a novelist, and over the past two years I have been drafting with an AI writing assistant as a regular part of my process. A change has set in that I do not have a word for. When I began, I could give a reader a substantive account of why a chapter ends where it does, why a character refuses this offer rather than that one, why a particular image carries a scene. Now, when a passage is right, I still know it is right, and I can still produce acceptable work without the tool; but the explanatory layer beneath those judgments has thinned. Increasingly I consult the assistant and simply find myself convinced, without being able to reconstruct the considerations that led to the conviction. It is not that I have forgotten how to write, and it is not quite that a skill has been lost, since the visible performance remains. What has gone missing is my capacity to give an account of what I am doing and why.

Is there an established name for this specific loss—a loss in the reason-giving layer rather than in the performance itself—and how should it be distinguished from ordinary forgetting, from deskilling, and from the thought that the understanding was never wholly inside me in the first place? I want the boundaries of the phenomenon drawn, not merely a label.
