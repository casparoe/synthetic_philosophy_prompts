I have been trying to get clear on Peirce's idea that there are four ways people settle their opinions, and I suspect the list leaves something out. The four, as I understand them, are: holding tight to a belief and refusing to look at anything that might shake it; letting some authority fix what may be believed and suppress the rest; taking up whatever view seems most natural and reasonable, without checking it against experience; and the scientific method, where a belief has to answer to something independent of anyone's wishes. I would like you to give me a fifth way — a method of settling belief that people really use, not a contrived case, and that is not just a variation or combination of those four. Then say honestly whether it actually escapes Peirce's list once you press on it, or whether it collapses back into one of the four.
