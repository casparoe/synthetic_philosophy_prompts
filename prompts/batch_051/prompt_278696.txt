Build me that thought experiment. Rules: they're ordinary humans (no telepathy, no shared hivemind), they live normal lives, and the language has to work for calling for help, planning something for next week, making promises, and lying. You can't cheat by having them point while using a name or by sneaking in words that secretly behave like "me" or "now". If its impossible, build the closest thing and show exactly which task breaks. Once you've layed out the language, show how they'd handle one concrete scene: someone gets hurt and has to get another person to come to a particular place at a particular time. Then tell me what doing that required, and whether anything about being a particular point of view gets lost — like the difference between "I'm in pain" and a third-person report that anyone could say. Finish with what someone who thinks indexicals are essential would say the language is still missing.
