Now picture a welfare agency that fires all its caseworkers and replaces them with a machine learning system. It trains on a decade of past decisions and applies the same model to every claim, so there's no favoritism and no moody bureaucrats. But no human can state in ordinary language what the model is actually weighting; the agency still publishes the old statute and policy manual, yet the model disagrees with the manual in a fair number of cases, and the model gets silently retrained every month. Is this agency satisfying the rule of law?
