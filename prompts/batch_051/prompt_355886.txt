My friend and I have been going back and forth about whether emotions are actually part of a good life or just noise you have to manage. We read a little about the Stoics and then some Romantic poets in class, and she came away thinking both sides overdo it, so now she's just going emotion by emotion. Her rulings so far are:

- Missing an old friend who moved across the country: part of the good life
- Anger at a school board rule that makes it harder for homeless kids to enroll: part of the good life
- Gratitude when a stranger helped her fix her bike chain: part of the good life
- Feeling proud after she aced a test she studied really hard for: noise
- Being jealous when her best friend made varsity and she didn't: noise
- Being excited before a concert: noise

The case she's stuck on now is standing under a really dark night sky and feeling awe — like being small and amazed by the universe, not about another person and not about a problem she can fix, just being floored by how big everything is.

If she wants to stay consistent with her earlier rulings, what should she say about the night-sky feeling? I dont just want a yes or no though — I want to know the rule she seems to be running, what other rules would fit her track record just as well, and, if those rules come apart on the night-sky case, whats the one further case or scenario that would best tell them apart, and which way do you think she'd actually go?
