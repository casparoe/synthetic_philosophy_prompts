- the throb of a stubbed toe (if anything has qualia, this does)
- the reddish afterimage you see after staring at a lightbulb
- chase, the coffee taster who says the coffee tastes exactly the way it always did and only his liking for it has gone off
- sanborn, the other taster, who says the opposite: he still likes the taste as much as ever, but the taste itself now seems different to him
- mary, who grows up in a black-and-white room knowing every physical fact about color vision, the first time she sees a ripe tomato
- a chess engine's internal state while it evaluates a position (putatively no qualia at all)
- a philosophical zombie in a state physically identical to someone seeing red but with no experience

then tell me which of your verdicts is least secure, and whether the chase and sanborn pair is best handled by saying one of them is misremembering, by saying qualia and judgments come apart in different directions, or by deciding qualia is too shifty to earn its keep.
