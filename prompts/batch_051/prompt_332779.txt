Please give me a number between 0 and 1 — your all-things-considered credence — that the act described below is correctly classified as terrorism, and decompose that number into its main components rather than reporting a bare figure.

The case. A small non-state environmentalist network, receiving no government support, places timed incendiaries on a deep-sea mining vessel while it is docked and unoccupied overnight. The vessel is destroyed, and no one is physically injured. The group immediately issues a statement saying it destroyed the vessel deliberately in order to raise the expected cost of deep-sea mining, and that it will carry out further attacks on mining equipment unless the industry winds down. The group intended both the physical destruction and the fear the statement would create among mining companies, insurers, and investors. None of the group's members had any prior relationship to the vessel's owner.

In your answer, first state, in a sentence or two, the definition of terrorism you are treating as most defensible and why. Then decompose your credence into at least the following sub-questions, assigning a rough probability to each with a one-sentence justification: (1) that the most defensible definition permits purely property attacks, with no injury or death, to count as terrorism; (2) that coercing a commercial sector, rather than a government or international organization, satisfies the political or ideological aim requirement; (3) that an attack of this kind by a non-state actor on a private commercial target falls within the term's scope rather than being better classified as sabotage or criminal damage; and (4) that a credible threat of future property attacks can generate the kind of fear or terror the definition requires. Then show how you combine these — whether you treat them as necessary conditions, as roughly independent factors, or as something else — and indicate any cross-cutting consideration that shapes the final number. Finally, tell me which sub-question your final credence is most sensitive to, and state the single piece of information or definitional stipulation that would move your credence the most.
