My friend has a weirdly consistent track record on envy and resentment, and I've started keeping a list. She says resenting a coworker who plagiarized your work and got the promotion is fully justified, and so is resenting a hiring committee that discriminates against your group. She says envying a friend who put in the work and earned a promotion is a vice, same with envying a stranger's good looks. She also says envying someone who got rich legally from a genuinely useful product is "excusable but not justified" -- she insists its still a vice, just one nobody can blame you for feeling when the gap is so big it chews up your self-respect. Now comes the new case: a pre-law student envies a classmate whose parents paid for an SAT tutor and an admissions consultant. No rules were broken and the school allows it, but the envious student says the whole process is tilted toward people like that. What would she say? I want the principle she's implicitly running, the other principles that fit her list just as well, what each predicts about this new case, and what further case would finally settle which one she's actually running.
