I'd like a sorting quiz for the readers of a long-running rationalist-adjacent blog, of the sort that ends with your position is X, and here is its most awkward commitment. The subject is the question of whether the world is becoming less violent, and the meta-question of whether a claim like violence has declined has a stance-independent truth value once the metric and the category boundary are fixed. The quiz should sort readers by their answers to concrete cases, not by which author or label they endorse, so no item should ask them to self-identify with a leaf or a named thinker, and no item should name a leaf.

Build the instrument from the logical space rather than from a list of names. The space is spanned by three dimensions, and the questions should make those dimensions, not factional labels, do the sorting:
- metric: per-capita risk versus absolute counts of victims, and which of these is what less violent means;
- category: narrow physical force (war, homicide, assault) versus expansive coding (structural violence, state-caused starvation, slow or institutional harms);
- trend: whether the historical trend has a fact of the matter, or is an artifact of start and end points, smoothing, and the chosen metric.

The leaves must include at least these positions, though you may rename them if the commitments stay visible: (1) per-capita declinist, for whom the standard war-and-homicide series show a real and meaningful decline and per-capita risk is the right denominator; (2) absolute-count skeptic, for whom a falling rate is compatible with more victims and therefore does not settle the question; (3) category deflationist, for whom violence is not a natural kind and the question is underdetermined until a category is stipulated; (4) structural-expansionist critic, for whom the standard series exclude structural violence and therefore track a political choice rather than a value-neutral fact; (5) trend-window skeptic, for whom the long-run decline disappears or reverses under equally defensible choices of interval and smoothing; and (6) no-fact-of-the-matter, stronger than local uncertainty: global across-metric claims of more or less violence lack a determinate truth value. Write each leaf as a steelman, and include the one-sentence awkward commitment the position cannot dodge. I suspect the category deflationist and the no-fact-of-the-matter leaf will collapse into each other unless the questions force them apart on whether fixing a category and a metric is enough to create a determinate trend claim; if the instrument genuinely cannot separate them, flag that rather than papering over it.

Constraints: eight to ten questions; each question a concrete case with two or three answer options; each option loaded onto one or two leaves by explicit integer points. Include several cases of these kinds: two societies with equal populations but different absolute victim counts; the same society with a falling rate but a rising annual victim count because of population growth; a government-policy famine; a hypothetical series that falls over one interval and rises over another; and a coding decision where the analyst must choose whether to count deaths by state action as violence. Phrase every stem so a reader can answer it without knowing which leaf it loads onto, and vary the cases so the rate-versus-count dilemma and the boundary-versus-core dilemma are tested separately before they are combined.

The output should be a single JSON object with this schema:
- title: string.
- audience: string, one sentence.
- questions: array of objects with fields id (string), stem (string), and options (array of objects with fields id (string), text (string), and loadings (object mapping leaf ids to integer points, e.g. {"per_capita_declinist": 1, "absolute_count_skeptic": 0})).
- leaves: array of objects with fields id (string), name (string), one_paragraph (string, a steelman of the position), and awkward_commitment (string, one sentence).
- scoring_key: object with fields method (string describing how points are summed and how ties are resolved) and tie_break (string).
- unoccupied_combinations: array of objects with fields combination (string, the pattern of answers with no leaf) and diagnosis (string, one or two sentences saying whether the view is incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored).
- unseparable_pairs: array of objects with fields pair (array of two leaf ids) and why (string, one sentence).

Do not include a bibliography, reading list, or citations; the instrument should stand or fall on the diagnostic cases alone.
