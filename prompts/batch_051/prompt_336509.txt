Please write a roughly 600-word parody of a corporate statement of values from a fictional publicly traded company whose profits depend on encouraging consumers to want ever more, yet whose stated values prominently include integrity, moderation, and care for the community. The tone should be deadpan and official, as if written by the company's compliance department. The satire should exaggerate a real tension in the debate over whether greed is a vice or an engine of prosperity: the document should denounce greed as a personal character flaw while simultaneously treating acquisitiveness, aggressive pursuit of growth, and the maximizing of shareholder value as unambiguously good. The irony should emerge entirely from the document itself, with no commentary or winking, and it should be legible to a reader unfamiliar with the philosophical literature while still landing for someone who knows the broader debate.
