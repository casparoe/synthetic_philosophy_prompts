ok so here's roughly where I've landed and I'd like to know whether it hangs together:

(1) I think fair equality of opportunity means a kid's prospects shouldn't hinge much on who there parents are.
(2) I support a much higher inheritance tax—something steep on estates above a couple million—because big inheritances are basically unearned head starts.
(3) I think parents should be free to spend their own money giving their kids advantages: private school, tutoring, travel teams, buying into a good school district.
(4) I think the family is a basic social unit, and it would be tyrannical for the state to try to equalize parenting itself.

I have a vague sense that (2) and (3) are compatible because one is after-tax income spent during life and the other is wealth handed over at death, so they don't literally conflict. But I also can't see how (1) and (3) coexist, and I'm not sure whether the estate tax in (2) counts as an attack on the family in (4).

So: which pairs are actually in tension and how bad? Which only look like they're in tension? And then please commit—tell me the single revision you'd make to make this set hang together best, and what that revision costs me.
