im trying to get clear on a question about refugees, but the part i cant settle isnt who counts as one—its which country has to take them in. the rough criteria i keep using are: (1) the refugees are in your territory or at your border, or otherwise under your effective control, because the duty not to send people back to danger attaches to whoever is actually in a position to send them back; (2) you have the capacity to host them without badly damaging basic public goods; (3) you have some connection that makes it your share rather than pure accident of geography—arms sales, military intervention, colonial history, alliance with the repressive regime, that sort of thing; (4) there is no other state with a stronger connection and greater capacity that is actually willing to take them.

now the case. Baueria is a mid-sized rich democracy. for a decade it sold small arms and light weapons to a militia in Tavria, all legal under Baueria's export law, every shipment approved through the normal licensing process. at the time the militia was one small armed group among many, and the sales made up a couple percent of its arsenal. there is no colonial or territorial link otherwise. then the militia—now bigger, with backing from a regional power—unleashes an ethnic cleansing campaign in the Tavrian highlands. after the fact a truth commission in Baueria finds that its own intelligence service had judged five years earlier that there was a substantial risk the weapons would eventually be used in mass atrocities, and that a cabinet office suppressed the assessment. nobody is prosecuted, because the sales were legal and the suppression was framed as a bureaucratic failure. tavrian refugees are now in camps in three neighboring countries, all of them poorer than Baueria and all already hosting many more refugees than Baueria ever has. none of those three is stable enough for long-term resettlement. none of the refugees has reached Baueria, and Baueria has no visa pathway for them and has not been asked to take a specific number.

is Baueria a country that must take in tavrian refugees? go through my four criteria and tell me what each one says, including the ones where the answer is borderline. where the case fails a criterion, tell me whether that shows Baueria is off the hook or whether the criterion is doing too much work—especially the first one about presence and control. and in the end i want to know whether 'must take them in' should track something like causal responsibility, or something like literally being the one at the door, and which of my criteria are actually earning their keep.
