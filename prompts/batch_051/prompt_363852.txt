Write a short dialogue between a defender of epistemic contextualism and a defender of pragmatic encroachment, built around a case in which their views pull apart. Hanif, with nothing at stake, glances at a departures board and tells a tourist that the 7:40 to Leeds leaves from Platform 3. Zeinab, who will miss a job interview unless that information is accurate, overhears him and asks, “Does Hanif know that the 7:40 to Leeds leaves from Platform 3?” Let the contextualist hold that what it takes for this attribution to be true can shift with the practical stakes salient in Zeinab’s conversational context, and let the encroachment theorist hold that the relevant stakes are Hanif’s own, which remain low. Have them answer each other’s actual arguments, especially about what raises the relevant epistemic standard and about whether their dispute concerns knowledge itself or only the word “knows.” Both positions should receive serious, even-handed defenses.
