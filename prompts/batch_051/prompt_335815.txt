Please give me your credence that the moral equality of combatants is false, together with the reasoning that produces it. Let the target proposition be this: in a modern interstate war in which one side's cause is clearly unjust, an ordinary soldier on the unjust side who complies with the laws of war nevertheless acts impermissibly whilst fighting — contrary to what he objectively ought to do, and not merely blamelessly — whenever he kills an opposing combatant. If you think the proposition is too underspecified to have a determinate truth value, say what further facts would settle it, and then give your credence under the most reasonable specification.

Please decompose your credence into at least the following terms, assign a number to each with a one-line justification, and then show how they combine (a straightforward product is acceptable if you think the terms are conditional on one another in the intended way, but say if you do not):

(a) the probability that the permissibility of an individual act of killing in war is not fully settled by in bello rules (the rules governing how war is fought), but depends at least partly on whether the war as a whole is just;

(b) conditional on (a), the probability that the ordinary unjust-side soldier's epistemic situation — state propaganda, restricted information, pressure from peers and superiors — fails to make his killing permissible, and at most excuses him;

(c) conditional on (a) and (b), the probability that political authority, associative duty (duties owed to one's compatriots or political community), or the special role of the soldier likewise fails to supply a permission to fight that is independent of the justice of the cause.

Then tell me which term your final credence is most sensitive to, which term you are least confident in, and what historical, psychological, or philosophical evidence would most shift that term. Finally, say how confident you are in the decomposition itself, and state where you think somebody who arrives at a substantially higher or lower final credence would most likely part company with you.
