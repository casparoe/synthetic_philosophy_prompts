My intuitions whip back and forth: an unused warehouse full of stuff nobody will ever touch makes P look absurd, but then I think of a society that has genuinely pushed back hunger and cold and expanded what people can attempt, and P feels almost undeniable. My advisor says it's zero and that I'm confusing the value of being free from necessity with the value of the pile of goods itself; I'm not sure shes wrong, but I can't get the number to stay still.

Then tell me which sub-question a small shift in would move your final number the most, and describe the thought experiment or evidence that would actually move you on it.
