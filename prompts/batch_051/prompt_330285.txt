Restate that argument in the vocabulary I actually reason with: events and happens-before, partial orders, concurrent events, vector clocks and cuts, linearizability, and consensus about a total order. Be as faithful as possible, then give me the residue report: which parts of the argument survive the translation intact, which parts had to be distorted, and which parts have no counterpart in my vocabulary at all. I suspect the argument's move from the absence of a privileged slice to the claim that every slice is real is the same kind of error as moving from the absence of a privileged linearization to the claim that every linearization is real. Tell me whether that suspicion holds up, and whether the residual parts point to a confusion in the argument or to a missing primitive in my vocabulary.
