I'm a grad student in history and philosophy of physics, and in our EFT reading group there's a comparison that keeps being used to dismiss old-style reductionism. People say the renormalization group shows that high-energy physics is like a board of directors: it sets a few global constraints and writes the budget, but the day-to-day operations of the lower-energy theory run independently, and no one in the branch office needs to know what the board thinks. The conclusion they draw is that chemistry or even cell biology is autonomous from microphysics in roughly the same way: you don't need the UV theory to do the IR theory. I want an audit. What corresponds to what in this analogy? Then where does it break? Specifically: which features of corporate hierarchy is the analogy quietly importing that the physics lacks? And does the autonomy conclusion survive once the disanalogies are on the table, or was it riding on the analogy?
