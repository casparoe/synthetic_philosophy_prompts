"Functionalism says a mental state just is its causal role: pain is whatever state is caused by tissue damage and causes wincing, moaning, and avoidance. That's what it means to say the mind is multiply realizable, and it's the standard argument against the identity theory: if pain were identical to C-fiber firing, then dogs couldn't be in pain, which is obviously false. Functionalism is popular because it keeps the inner states behaviorism threw out while avoiding identity theory's chauvinism. Its only serious problem is that it has nothing to say about consciousness, which is why most philosophers now treat it as a view about cognitive science rather than about the mind."
