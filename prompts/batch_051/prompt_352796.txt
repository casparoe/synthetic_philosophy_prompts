You are a philosopher of language who has worked carefully on generics — bare plural and indefinite singular sentences such as 'ducks lay eggs' and 'mosquitoes carry malaria' — and on how their truth conditions relate to prevalence, exceptions, and what is characteristic of a kind. Please work through the cases below in order, set out two or three candidate principles and show where each of them breaks, and only then give your final account.

I am a philosophy professor preparing a graduate seminar on generics, and I find that my own judgments resist every simple account I try. My verdicts are as follows. I accept 'ducks lay eggs,' although only sexually mature females lay eggs, seasonally at that, and most ducks — males, ducklings, old and infertile females — never lay one; yet I reject 'ducks are female,' even though the very same individuals are the ones in virtue of which I accept the first sentence. I accept 'mosquitoes carry malaria,' although only females of certain Anopheles species carry the parasite and only a tiny fraction of a mosquito population does at any given time; but I reject 'sharks attack bathers,' not as merely statistically misleading but as false, even though shark attacks are comparably rare, far more salient, and exactly the sort of striking, dangerous property that some would say makes a generic true. I accept 'peacocks are brilliantly plumed,' although only the males are colorful, while I reject 'peacocks lay eggs,' even though the female members of the peafowl kind, peahens, are the ones that lay the eggs. A bare frequency rule gets the first and third accepted cases wrong from the start; a strikingness or danger rule cannot separate the mosquito case from the shark case; and a 'characteristic of the kind' rule seems right but threatens to be an empty placeholder.

Please tell me whether any one principle — or a small, explicitly stated set of principles — captures all six verdicts. State it precisely, say which clause or feature is motivated by which case, apply it to two further generics of your own choosing, and then give me an honest statement of which of my verdicts it cannot accommodate and whether you think the account or the verdict should give way.
