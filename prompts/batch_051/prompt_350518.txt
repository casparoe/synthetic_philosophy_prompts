I serve on the board of a regional land trust that must decide the future of a 680-acre parcel it has held for decades: cut-over second-growth woodland, a partly drained wetland, and old fields, with no rare species and no development pressure beyond a standing offer from a trail-and-outdoor-education cooperative. A proposal before the board would place the parcel under a permanent conservation easement barring development and most active management, in effect committing the trust to a hands-off, let-it-rewild policy. The competing proposal would lease the parcel to the cooperative for a low-impact trail network, a forest school, and floodwater storage, with the lease income funding the trust's other conservation work.

The board is now split along a line that is no longer about this parcel's particulars. One faction holds that the parcel's value is intrinsic: wild nature has worth in itself, independent of human use or appreciation, and the easement is the only option that respects it. A second faction holds that the operative value is relational: the parcel matters through the relationships people form with it—walking it with children, teaching in it, returning to it across years—and an easement that discourages regular use severs the thing that makes it matter. A third faction argues that a fiduciary board may weigh only instrumental considerations such as flood mitigation, carbon storage, and lease revenue for better projects, and that intrinsic value and personal relationships are not the kind of thing trustees may properly put on the ledger.

Draft the board's decision memo: approve the easement, reject it in favor of the lease, or approve with conditions. The memo must give an actionable recommendation with reasons, and it must not merely choose among the three value frameworks. It should determine which kinds of value may legitimately enter a trustee's decision, whether any should be excluded or discounted, and how the board should proceed when they conflict. It should also state in its strongest form the consideration on the losing side and why it does not carry the day.
