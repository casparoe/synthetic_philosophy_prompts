Please assign a numerical credence between 0 and 1 to the proposition that the human episode in the history of intelligence on Earth is a transitional stage rather than a terminal stage, where transitional stage means that its central retrospective significance is to have brought about one or more successor systems—artificial, self-modifying, or otherwise—that surpass it in generality, autonomy, and cumulative control over their environment. Do not answer with a bare number, and do not stop at saying that the question depends on definitions; make the definitions explicit and let the ambiguity enter as a component of the estimate. Decompose the proposition into at least the following five terms. (a) The probability that successor-stage or teleological descriptions of evolutionary and historical processes are explanatory claims with genuine truth conditions, rather than merely retrospective narrative patterns imposed after the fact. (b) The probability that intelligence, broadly construed, contains a long-run gradient or attractor that would make one historical stage count as being succeeded by a later one. (c) The conditional probability, given such a gradient, that future machine or other nonhuman systems actually cross the thresholds of generality, autonomy, and self-directed improvement required to be successors rather than sophisticated instruments. (d) The conditional probability, given that such successor systems appear, that the most adequate retrospective description of the human role is scaffolding rather than parent, symbiont, collaborating partner, or dead end that happened to seed a lucky accident. (e) The probability that the proposition itself can be assigned a single reasonably determinate truth value, as opposed to being too vague or too value-laden for a sharp credence. For each term, give a rough probability and one or two sentences of justification. Then show how you combine the terms into your final credence, identify the term to which the final credence is most sensitive, and state what concrete evidence or argument would most shift that term.
