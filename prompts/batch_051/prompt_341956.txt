What I like about this is that he's not just saying money is for trade right now; he's saying part of its job is being a claim on future stuff, while admitting its value wobbles around like everything else. But in the same chapter he also says money got introduced to make unlike things comparable, that it's a kind of measure or middle term, that it exists by custom rather than nature and we can change it or make it useless, and that underneath all of it demand is what really holds exchange together.
