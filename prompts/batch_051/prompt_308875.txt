(2) The skeptical solution replies that I am still entitled to answer 125 when someone asks me for 68 plus 57, because my answer lines up with the answers my community gives.
(3) But every other member of the community is in exactly the same position I am in: for each of them, too, there is no fact about whether they mean addition or quaddition.

(5) Therefore the skeptical solution either has to secretly rely on a communal fact of meaning (which contradicts the skeptic's conclusion), or it leaves us with no standard of correctness at all.
(6) Either way, it has not answered the skeptic; it has just moved the problem up one level.
