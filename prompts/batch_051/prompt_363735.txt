Ok so I keep noticing this move in arguments online (mostly a politics discord server and instagram comments), and I don't know if it has a name. Someone on my side will say, you only believe that because your algorithm feeds you that stuff all day, you're in a bubble. Then someone on the other side says basically the same thing back, like they copy-pasted it. Both versions feel true to the person saying them, and kind of obviously false when they're said about me. It gets even stranger when I catch myself doing it with bad faith. If someone on my side misquotes a stat or links a sketchy source, I think okay they slipped up, people get tired, the source looked legit. If someone on the other side does the identical thing, it feels like proof that they know they're lying and they dont care. And I know they think the same about us, because they say it out loud. So each side is using two completely different rules for the same behavior depending on whose behavior it is, and each side is sure its not a double standard. It's not exactly ad hominem, because the point isn't that the other person is a bad person, it's more like the other person's disagreement shouldn't count because of where their beliefs came from. And it's not just "both sides do it," because both sides doing it is exactly the thing that bothers me. Does this move have a name?
