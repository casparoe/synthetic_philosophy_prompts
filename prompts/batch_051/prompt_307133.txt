I get each half on its own, but I can't see the bridge. What exactly is the shared move? Be precise about where the analogy is genuinely tight—self-reference mediated by an encoding—and where it's just metaphor, since a strand gets physically copied by its own product while a Gödel sentence only refers to its own number.
