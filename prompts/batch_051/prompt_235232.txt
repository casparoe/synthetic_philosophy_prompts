You are an expert at the intersection of philosophy of cognitive science and the interpretability of language models. I am writing a paper on chain-of-thought reasoning, and I keep running into the same unresolved dispute. One camp says that when a model writes out intermediate steps before giving an answer, those steps are a window onto the process by which the model actually reached the answer: the model is, in a substantive sense, producing reasons, not merely words that look like reasons. The other camp says the steps are a post hoc gloss generated for the reader, that the actual determinants of the answer live in the weights and are only ever partially reflected in the text, and that the protocol would survive even if the stated rationale were merely a plausible story the model generated because the task demands one. I suspect part of the stalemate is conceptual: faithfulness gets used to mean several different things — causal necessity of the steps, counterfactual robustness of the stated reasons, correspondence to an underlying algorithm, usefulness of the protocol for predicting the model's downstream behavior, and the like.

Turn this into a precise research question. How could one empirically test the claim that a model's chain-of-thought is faithful to the reasoning behind its answer, rather than a fluent rationalization that happens to precede the answer? Please design the strongest test or small battery of tests you can, being concrete about manipulations and measurements. Say what pattern of results would favor the faithfulness reading and what would favor the rationalization reading. Then mark the residue: given a strongly positive or strongly negative outcome, what exactly would be settled and what would still be open — in particular, which senses of faithfulness could remain unadjudicated either way? A useful design should not conflate mere causal influence with faithfulness, since in an autoregressive model every earlier token is trivially causally upstream of every later one. You may begin by briefly distinguishing the senses of faithfulness you take to be at stake, and then think carefully through the inference your test licenses.
