Negative utilitarianism is usually introduced as the view that we should minimise suffering, whereas classical utilitarianism is presented as the view that we should maximise happiness. I am unsure why these are treated as rivals, however. If happiness is nothing more than the absence of suffering, then minimising suffering and maximising happiness appear to be the same undertaking, and the alleged opposition dissolves. Is negative utilitarianism therefore simply classical utilitarianism restated under another name? If it is not, what exactly separates the two, and where does the apparent equivalence go wrong?
