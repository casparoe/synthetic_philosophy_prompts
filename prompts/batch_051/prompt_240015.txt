Quarterly bonuses are then based on how often your predictions came true. To stop people from sandbagging trivially easy predictions, a rotating group of engineers gets a bounty for demonstrating that someone's prediction was gamed or near-tautological, paid out of that person's accrued bonus. Walk me through how this actually plays out: what people choose to build, how they write their predictions, how the bounty hunters get gamed in turn, and wether we end up with better software or just a more elaborate meta-game. If it does collapse, is it because of Goodhart's law, and is there a version of this that wouldn't?
