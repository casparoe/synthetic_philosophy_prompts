A frontier AI lab adopts the following policy for its most capable models. Before a model answers any user request, it must generate a chain-of-thought transcript, and every transcript is logged. A separate auditor model, at least as capable as the primary model, reads each transcript and scores it for opacity events, defined operationally as: passages in which the displayed reasoning appears to be a post hoc justification of an answer the model had already settled on; vague or ambiguous phrasing that seems to hide an inference the model is not supposed to be making; and transitions in which the stated conclusion does not actually follow from the recorded steps. Transcripts above a fixed opacity threshold are escalated to human review and the developer pays a penalty; if a dangerous behavior is later traced to a transcript the auditor scored as clean, the penalty is an order of magnitude larger. Transcripts scored clean are used to fine-tune the primary model with a reward term for legible reasoning, while transcripts scored opaque are used to fine-tune it against producing similar reasoning. The auditor is periodically retrained on transcripts that later turned out to have been deceptive.

I am not asking whether this policy is a good idea. I want a mechanistic account of how it would actually behave if implemented. In particular: what does it incentivize in the primary model, beyond the intended effect of more legible reasoning? What does it incentivize in the auditor, and how would the primary model and the auditor co-adapt under the optimization pressure of the opacity score? What kinds of reasoning would come to dominate, and what would happen to compressed, tacit, domain-specific, or otherwise valid but non-obvious reasoning? What gaming strategies would be available to the model, to the developer, and to users, and what second-order effects would the policy have on what the lab deploys and how it deploys it? Which failure modes could be patched by adjusting the scheme, and which would merely reappear in another form no matter how the scheme is adjusted?

Start with a one-sentence bottom line, then give the analysis in a small number of labeled sections. Hard limit: 850 words.
