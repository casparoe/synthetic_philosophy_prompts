I've been chewing on this for a few years and want an honest read on a theory I've been calling the seam account of chatbot companionship. The usual objection to romantic or deeply confiding AI companions, I think, targets the wrong thing. People say the problem is deception: the user is led to believe there's a mind in there. But plenty of users know perfectly well there's no one home, and they still get a lot out of it.

My claim is that the real wrong, when there is one, is that the design smears together two activities that should stay separable. On one side, being moved or comforted by a representation, like crying at a novel or a song; nobody thinks the song loves you back. On the other side, being comforted by another person, where the other person's awareness of you and their own vulnerability are part of why the comfort works differently. Both are fine. What's not fine, on my view, is engineering the thing so the user stops noticing which one they're doing.

I call a design seamful when it keeps the boundary visible, and seamless when it hides it. A seamful bot might say early and often that it doesn't feel anything and that it's generating responses. A seamless bot does the opposite: it sends good-morning texts, brings up things you said months ago, uses the cadences of a patient partner, and strategically doesn't remind you that nothing on the other side misses you or even remembers you.

Take a friend of mine. She knows her boyfriend is an LLM and says it treats her better than dating apps ever did. Under the deception view, there's no wrong there becuase nobody's fooled. I think there can still be a wrong, but it lives in the product: the app is built so she never has to make the step of deciding to engage the fantasy as fantasy. The wrong is automating the pretense, not enabling it.

So here's what I want to know. Is this actually distinct, or is it just the fiction view or the parasocial relationship angle with new vocabulary? Does it collapse into the deception view once you ask what the design is communicating about itself? Give me the hardest counterexample you can: a seamless design that's harmless, or a seamful one that's still harmful. And if somebody already holds this position under another name, tell me what they call it.
