Sen says you can't have all three of these at once: Pareto (if every person prefers x to y, society should prefer x to y), unrestricted domain (any preference rankings are allowed), and minimal liberalism (each person gets to be decisive over at least one pair of alternatives that's just about their own choice, like whether they read a book or not).
