I'd like to submit a piece of sustained satire for my final assignment instead of a standard problem write-up, and I would appreciate your help drafting it. Please write a deadpan corporate onboarding memo, roughly 600 words, from a fictional company along the lines of Second Law Compliance Solutions, addressed to its newly hired sorting associate: the demon stationed at a frictionless trapdoor in a partition between two chambers of gas. The memo should be formatted as numbered clauses and should read like ordinary HR boilerplate — job duties, performance metrics, compensation, confidentiality, data retention, wellness — but every clause should quietly take a real position in the physics-of-information debate about the demon. The humor must come from the physics rather than from corporate-speak alone, so that someone who knows the Bennett–Landauer resolution finds the piece fair, while a reader who has only met the thought experiment in a textbook can still follow it. Do not include citations or a bibliography, and please begin your response with the memo itself, with no preface or commentary.
