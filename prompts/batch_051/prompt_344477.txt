I want a scene where someone actually pushes her on whether that's more than a slogan. Design the test concretely: what do you measure, what pattern would count for the asymmetry, and what pattern would count against it. And please commit — don't give me a menu of half a dozen possible tests; pick the one you'd actually run and say why it beats the alternatives.
