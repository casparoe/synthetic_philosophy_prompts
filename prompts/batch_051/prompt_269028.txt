I keep noticing a pattern that I do not have a precise name for. In one instance, several food-delivery platforms undercut one another's fees and courier pay to gain customers; once one platform does it, the others must match or lose orders, so all converge on fees and wages that none of them thinks is sound for the business, even though every platform would prefer a binding floor on fees and pay. In another, rival research groups publish faster than any of them thinks is wise, because a group that pauses for extra replications and checks expects to be scooped; collectively they would prefer a slower, more careful norm, but no group can unilaterally slow down. In both cases each actor's choice is sensible given what the others do, the overall arrangement is one no participant endorses, and it persists even though everyone sees it. I know about prisoner's dilemmas and tragedies of the commons, but these seem different: there is no single moment of defection, and no shared resource is being depleted. Does this pattern have an established name? If it sits between several named ideas, I want those ideas distinguished and the boundaries of the best-fitting one carefully marked; I would not be satisfied with just a label.
