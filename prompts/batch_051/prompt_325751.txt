I am a junior software engineer, and my employment contract contains a clause requiring that any dispute with the company be resolved by a private arbitrator rather than in court. The arbitrator must be chosen from a panel administered by a firm that does most of its work for employers, and the company pays the arbitrator’s fees. I have what a labor lawyer says is a strong unpaid-overtime claim, but she warns that employees rarely win before this firm. My friend says I signed the contract knowing what it said, so I should accept the forum I agreed to. Is my friend right that I am morally bound to arbitrate, or may I challenge the clause? On what principle should this be decided?
