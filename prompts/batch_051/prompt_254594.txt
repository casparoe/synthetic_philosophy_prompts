ive been chewing on hedonism lately — the view that pleasure is the only thing thats good for its own sake. i think i might have an argument against it, but i cant tell if its actually good or if im cheating somewhere. it goes like this.

(1) If hedonism is true, then every pleasant experience is good for the same reason: it has the property of being pleasant.
(2) If pleasantness really is one property shared by all pleasant experiences, then I should be able to spot it by holding different pleasures side by side in my head.
(3) But when I hold the pleasure of scratching an itch next to the pleasure of finally getting a joke, next to the odd little pleasure of pressing a bruise, next to the nasty pleasure of watching someone I envy fail, there is no single felt quality running through all of them. They feel completely diffrent.
(4) So pleasantness is not a single felt quality.
(5) So hedonism is false.

My mate says step 3 just shows im bad at introspection, but that feels like a dodge. Is the argument valid? If not, which step or steps fail, and what would the correct version of the faulty step be? If its valid but unsound, say which premise is false and why. Please write the answer in British English.
