An online cooperative is considering a self-amending constitution with two rules. Rule A: any rule may be amended by a two-thirds vote, provided the amendment is posted seven days in advance and takes effect twenty-four hours after passage. Rule B: an amendment is void if it would make the constitution inconsistent or render some future amendment impossible to carry out; during the twenty-four-hour window any member may challenge an amendment as violating Rule B, and a challenged amendment is suspended until two-thirds of members vote that it passes this consistency test. Treat this as a mechanism rather than a legal question. Walk through what happens when members try to repeal Rule B, shorten the notice or the window, or pass two amendments simultaneously that each presuppose the other has already taken effect. Where does the system get gamed first? Are there technically valid amendments that leave the community with no usable amendment procedure, and can the design be patched to prevent that without creating a new deadlock? End with a line beginning Verdict: and then a one-sentence assessment of whether the design is workable, fixable, or unstable.
