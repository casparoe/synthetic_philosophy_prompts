Some ant colonies behave in ways that look planned by a single agent: they repair breaches, reallocate foragers, and reshape the nest, even though each individual ant follows only local chemical cues and has no overview of the colony. A colleague takes this seriously and argues that the colony itself, not just the ants, has something properly called cognitive states, perhaps a minimal kind of mind. I want to know whether that claim can be brought into contact with evidence rather than remaining a metaphor.
