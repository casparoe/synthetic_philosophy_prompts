(3) Suppose I treat myself as a random draw from the set of all humans who will ever live. (4) By Bayes' theorem, then, learning my birth rank should multiply my prior odds in favor of Doom Soon over Doom Late by roughly 1000, no matter what prior I started with.

(5) So unless my prior odds already strongly favored Doom Late by way more than 1000 to 1, I should now think Doom Soon is overwhelmingly likely. In other words, the probably that humanity survives much longer is tiny.
