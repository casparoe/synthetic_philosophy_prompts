I'm a journalist who covers science and ideas, and I'm drafting an essay on whether a present-day state can genuinely apologize for wrongs committed generations before any of its current citizens were alive. My editor has asked me to stop hiding behind sources and state a position, so I've distilled the view I want to defend into six working commitments. I'd like a consistency audit before I commit them to print. I'll list them flat, without signaling which ones I'd be most reluctant to revise.

1. States are corporate agents that persist across changes of government and membership, so a state today can apologize in its own name for a wrong committed by its predecessor institutions.

2. No living individual inherits personal guilt for a wrong committed before they were born, and neither citizenship nor ancestry transmits a personal obligation to make amends.

3. A genuine apology is a speech act performed by the wrongdoer or the wrongdoer's authorized representative; if the continuity between the historical perpetrator and the present speaker is broken, what is issued is not an apology but something weaker — regret, sympathy, or a policy statement.

4. A state apology for a historical wrong is warranted only where the wrong's effects still run through the apologizing state's own institutions — inherited legal arrangements, land tenure, exclusionary structures — so that there is a living victim-position the apology can address, rather than only a wrong done to the dead.

5. The duty to apologize and the duty to repair are distinct: a state can owe a genuine apology while owing no compensation, and can owe compensation while owing no apology.

6. A state apology is empty ceremony unless it commits the state to concrete institutional change backed by real costs borne by the current generation.

Please audit the set. Which commitments are actually in tension, and which only appear to be? Rank the genuine tensions by severity, and for each say what would have to be revised and what the cheapest revision would cost. Where a tension dissolves on inspection, say why it is not a real inconsistency.
