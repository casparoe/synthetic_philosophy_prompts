Every emerald observed so far has been green, and has therefore also been grue, so the generalisations 'all emeralds are green' and 'all emeralds are grue' are equally well supported by all current evidence. They nevertheless conflict about the next emerald first observed after 2100. We treat the difference between projecting green and projecting grue as a requirement of sound inductive practice, not a mere habit or convention. Please give the strongest justification you can find for this asymmetry, test whether it would also license some clearly unreasonable projection in a nearby case, and say whether the asymmetry should be kept, revised, or abandoned.
