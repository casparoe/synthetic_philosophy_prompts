Erwin Schrödinger defended a version of Vedantic monism, holding that the apparent plurality of conscious minds is an illusion and that consciousness is in reality one. Suppose he had lived to encounter the contemporary philosophical debate over whether an artificial system could be conscious or possess a mind. How would he most plausibly have responded to that debate? In particular, address how his monism would bear on whether the question of machine consciousness is well posed, whether a machine's consciousness would differ in kind from a human being's, and what it would mean for the single consciousness he posited to manifest through both biological and artificial systems.
