I've got this weird thing going on with how I pick lunch and its been bugging me. Near my work theres only three meals I ever actually want: a falafel wrap, a noodle bowl, and tacos. Here's the dumb part. If I'm holding the wrap, I'd gladly pay two bucks extra to swap it for the noodle bowl. If I'm holding the noodle bowl, I'd pay two bucks to swap it for tacos. And if I'm holding the tacos, I'd pay two bucks to swap them back to the wrap. I even tried to rank them in my head once and just got a triangle. So if somebody let me keep trading, I'd come back to where I started, only six bucks poorer. My friend says that proves my preferences are inconsistent in a way that actually counts — like there's something genuinely wrong with how I'm choosing. I say it's just lunch, wanting doesn't have to be consistent, and each swap feels fine on its own, so whats the problem. Who's right? And if he is, why does running in a little circle matter instead of just being a funny quirk?
