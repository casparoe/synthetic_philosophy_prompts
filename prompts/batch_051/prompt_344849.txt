My friend is weirdly systematic about slurs, and I can recite her verdicts. She treats the n-word, the homophobic f-word, the r-word, and 'bitch' aimed at a woman as slurs. She doesn't count 'cracker', 'redneck', or 'white trash' as slurs — insults, sure, but she has a standing line that a slur isn't just any insult, and those don't make the cut. She also says 'queer' used by queer people today isn't a slur, just a reclaimed word. The other day somebody asked her whether 'Karen' is a slur, and she said she'd have to think about it. What's your prediction? I care less about the word itself than the rule: which principles fit all her calls so far, what does each one say about 'Karen', which one would you bet she's running and why, and what further word or case would most cleanly settle which principle she's actually using?
