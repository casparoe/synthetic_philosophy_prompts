My best friend has done in-person sex work on and off for years, and our city is finally taking another look at the ordinance that makes selling sex a crime. There are three proposals on the table. One: selling stays illegal, but enforcement shifts entirely to buyers, with sellers getting immunity. Two: selling becomes legal under a licensing regime—brothels and independent workers register, pass health inspections, pay taxes, like a massage studio. Three: the prostitution laws just get deleted, so the work is treated like any other self-employed gig, no special license or registry.

Most of the workers I know want three, partly because a registry would out them to landlords and family. A couple of parlor owners prefer two, arguing inspections would squeeze out the worst operators. My prior is to side with the workers, but I can already steelman every position in two sentences, which is exactly the problem.
