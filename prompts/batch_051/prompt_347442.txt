I've ended up accepting a view about how to act when you're not sure which moral framework is right. The view is roughly this: treat the contending frameworks as hypotheses, give each a credence, score each option by how right or wrong it is under each framework, weight those scores, and choose the option with the best weighted total, with an extra penalty for options that any framework you give real credence to counts as seriously wrong. I use this all over the place, from deciding where to donate to whether to report a colleague, so it's not just an academic position.
