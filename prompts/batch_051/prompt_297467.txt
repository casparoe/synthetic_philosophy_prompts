Ok so I keep native fish and I'm not a biologist, but I've spent way to much time thinking about how people decide whether two groups of fish are one species or two. I came up with my own list of rules for saying two groups are the same species: (1) they can mate and the offspring are fertile (not like mules), (2) where their ranges actually overlap in the wild they do interbreed at least a little on their own, and (3) the mixed offspring aren't obviously worse at surviving or finding mates than the pure forms. I figure those three rules catch the obvious cases well enough.

But now here's a case I can't settle. I'll make it up so I don't get the facts wrong. A river has two kinds of minnow. The upper-river kind is small and dark, breeds in fast cold riffles, and the males flash blue when they court. The lower-river kind is bigger and pale, breeds in slow warm pools, and the males flash red. They were kept apart by an old dam for maybe 80 years. The dam gets removed, and now the two kinds meet in a middle stretch. Turns out they do interbreed. The hybrid babies are fertile, no issue there. But the hybrids come out with a muddy in-between color, and its not just a normal blend: a whole chunk of DNA that controls the color and habitat preference is flipped around in one kind so it doesn't get broken up in the hybrids, meaning the hybrids all get the same mishmash. The mixed fish are easy for predators to spot, and they don't pick either habitat right, so almost none survive to breed. But the handful that do survive breed fine, and there's still a little DNA leaking each way between the two kinds.

So under rules 1 and 2 theyre the same species: fertile babies, and they mix in the wild without being forced. Under rule 3 it depends: the hybrids are doing worse, but only because of the current predators and the split habitat. If the river changed again, the middle type might do fine and the two kinds would just merge. Are these one species or two? Go through my three rules one by one and say where the case lands, then tell me whether I need to change the rules — maybe add something like 'the two groups have to actually be merging over time' or throw one out. I keep going back and forth and I'd like to see someone work it through the same way I'm stuck on it.
