There's a passage where he says astrology is the foolish daughter of astronomy, but without her the mother would starve. The essay should reach and justify a conclusion about which reading best explains the foolish daughter passage and his actual astrological work. Dont just summarize the text; make the case for your interpretation and consider the strongest objection to it.
