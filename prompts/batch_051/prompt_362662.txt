Pandemic triage protocols will talk about giving the last ventilator to a 20-year-old with 60 expected life-years rather than an 80-year-old with 5, which sounds exactly like QALY logic: weigh people by how many good years they'd get. But then the same bioethicists who defend those protocols will say that using QALYs to ration ordinary health care is discriminatory against disabled or chronically ill people. And meanwhile cost-benefit types are perfectly happy to use a value of a statistical life of several million dollars per life saved. So isn't triage just QALY maximization with a dollar sign stuck on it? What am I missing — or is the difference really just branding?
