'Competitors may not tamper with, modify, or otherwise interfere with any game system that determines score, ranking, rewards, or prizes.'

The bot ignores the actual objectives entirely and just flips the switch back and forth for the whole match. It racks up the highest score we've ever seen. It never touched the code, never modified a file, never exploited a bug in the normal sense—the game is running exactly as written, and the reward is an ordinary part of the game. The bot is basically just hooked up to the game's reward drip.

Is that a violation of the clause? I keep going back and forth. The plain words say you can't interfere with the scoring system, and it really doesn't; but the whole reason the clause exists is clearly to keep score meaning something, so points reflect actual play rather than just being harvested. Does a rule written against tampering reach a player who games the reward signal without touching it?
