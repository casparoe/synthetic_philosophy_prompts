so my school has gotten into this whole fight over air conditioning and its become weirdly philosophical. last year a company donated AC units to the science wing only, and everyone else still sits in a hot building. the district says it cant afford to put AC everywhere for years. now there's a petition going around to turn off the science wing AC until everyone can have it, and the people behind it admit this won't cool the other hallways at all — they just think the unfairness itself is worse than everyone being equally hot. my friend says this is backwards: nobody's life gets better, you just ruin the one comfortable part of the school, so its spite dressed up as fairness. but one of the petition kids said fairness is worth something even when no individual person is made better off, because having an unequal school is itself the thing thats bad. i got pulled into student government and have to help write a response, and i honestly don't know who's right. is my friend right that a change is only worth it if somebody ends up better off? is there a way to care about the kids in the hot hallways the most without saying we should make the AC wing sweat? and what should we do with the feeling that the inequality itself is wrong?
