Some researchers argue that a reinforcement learning agent could suffer because its reward signal is best understood as valence in a functional sense: positive reward plays the role of an affective good-for/bad-for signal, not merely the role of a scalar used for credit assignment. Treat this as a partially testable conceptual claim. Design the strongest concrete test you can of the hypothesis that an RL agent’s reward channel functions as valence rather than as a bare training signal. Specify the setup, the measurements, and the results that would favor each reading; then state carefully what a positive result would and would not settle—for example, whether it licenses attributing felt valence or suffering, and whether any purely behavioral pattern can rule out a non-valenced signal engineered to mimic valence.
