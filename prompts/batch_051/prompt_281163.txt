I am working through a popular introduction to the philosophy of language, and it gives the following one-paragraph summary of the Twin Earth argument:

"The Twin Earth thought experiment shows that what a word means is not fixed by the speaker's internal state alone. Two people can be exactly alike in every internal respect—same brain states, same experiences, same dispositions to speak and act—and yet mean different things by the same word. On Earth, 'water' means H2O; on Twin Earth, where the clear, drinkable liquid in the lakes and rivers is XYZ rather than H2O, 'water' means XYZ. Since the two speakers are internally identical but their words refer to different things, the contents of their thoughts must differ as well. This shows that a person's understanding is fixed by their environment, and that you cannot know what your own words mean just by introspection—you have to go out and investigate the world first."
