I grew up praying and it mostly made sense to me, but lately I cant get past this: if God already knows everything and never changes, then when I pray for my sister to pull through surgery, it feels like either the outcome's already settled (so the prayer does nothing) or the prayer was supposed to move him (which he cant do). Write a villanelle from the point of view of someone sitting up at 3 a.m. waiting for the phone to ring. The two refrains should be the two sides of that dilemma: one is the prayer itself, the other is the thought that its all already decided. The point isn't just to describe the dilemma—the way a villanelle keeps bringing those two lines back around, and finally puts them side by side in the last two lines, should do the work of showing how the prayer and the doubt keep circling the same point.
