(2) I think the same thing happens with big institutions, not just gadgets. The timing of a few early events helped put different countries on extractive or inclusive tracks, and those tracks then reproduced themselves through politics.

(3) But I also think the largest outcomes — agriculture arising independently in several places, states forming, writting — were massively overdetermined. Calling them accidents just seems wrong.

(6) I don't think the fact that a bad arrangement got locked in gives it any claim on us. Path dependence explains how we got stuck; it doesn't justify staying stuck.
