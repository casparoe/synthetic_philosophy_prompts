I keep bumping into what feels like the same problem in two places. AI safety people want to define side effects or low impact so an agent can avoid making things worse without needing a full specification of human values. Medical ethics has its own version: non-maleficence, first do no harm, is suppose to constrain a doctor before you settle the big questions about what well-being is. Both need a baseline—the world as it would have been without the AI, and the patient's normal functioning—and that baseline looks like it smuggles value judgments back in. Is this a real shared structure, or is it just the word baseline doing the work? What would have to be true for an argument from one side to transfer cleanly, and where does the analogy break down?
