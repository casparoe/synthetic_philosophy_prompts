Take Nozick's experience machine — the setup where you can plug into a machine that gives you any experiences you want for the rest of your life while your body floats in a tank, and once you're in you won't know the experiences aren't real. The machine has a wirehead mode: instead of generating simulated experiences of success, it simply pins the agent's reward register to it's maximum value for as long as it's plugged in. The agent knows all this before choosing. It also knows that normal-experience mode gives noisy reward averaging about 90% of maximum, while wirehead mode gives the maximum with certainty. Once plugged in the agent can't unplug and can't alter the machine.
