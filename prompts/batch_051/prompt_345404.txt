I am an undergraduate in a philosophy of biology course, writing on Gould and Lewontin’s critique of the adaptationist programme. After rereading the spandrels paper, I reconstructed an argument from what I took to be its central methodological commitment, and the reconstruction seems to prove too much. I want to know exactly where it goes wrong.

(1) The spandrels critique holds that adaptationist explanations of organismal traits are methodologically suspect because they are post hoc narratives: for any trait, a plausible selective history can be told, and the story is not independently tested against non-selectionist alternatives.
(2) If this defect is fatal to adaptationist explanations, it must be fatal to any explanatory practice that shares the defect.
(3) The non-adaptationist alternatives Gould and Lewontin urge — developmental constraints, architectural by-products, exaptation, drift — share the defect. Once these categories are on the table, a post hoc non-selectionist story can be told for almost any trait, including those with strong independent evidence of selection.
(4) Therefore, by (2), those alternatives are in as bad an epistemic position as the adaptationist stories they are meant to replace.
(5) If the alternatives are in as bad a position, then the spandrels critique does not license a move from naive adaptationism to a better explanatory pluralism; it licenses only a skeptical standoff.
(6) A methodological critique that yields a skeptical standoff, and makes well-supported adaptations seem as dubious as just-so stories, has proven too much.
(7) Therefore the spandrels critique rests on a standard that undermines itself.

Where does this go wrong? If there is a mistaken step, name it and explain why it looked acceptable. If the argument is valid, say which premise is least costly to reject and what that rejection does to Gould and Lewontin’s broader argument. I would prefer to keep the basic point in (1), but I do not want to hide behind a preference if that is the price. If the task as I have posed it rests on a misunderstanding of their argument, say so directly instead of forcing a diagnosis.
