What is your actual credence that a being physically identical to a conscious human but wholly devoid of phenomenal consciousness is metaphysically possible — that is, that full zombies are possible and physicalism is false? I want one number and its decomposition, not a survey of the dialectic. Distinguish your credence that full zombies are ideally, positively conceivable from your credence in the bridge thesis that such conceivability, properly constrained, entails metaphysical possibility, and then explain how those two combine into the overall estimate. Also say whether your reasoning would be firmer for a partial zombie — a physical and functional duplicate lacking only the affective character of pain while all its behavior and physical facts remain unchanged — and whether that asymmetry should move the full-zombie number at all. End by identifying the single sub-question to which your final estimate is most sensitive, and state the argument or consideration that would most change your verdict, or where you take the reasoning to be most likely to break.
