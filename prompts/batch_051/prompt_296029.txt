We run a small DAO that was meant to be governed futarchy-style, so a prediction market, not a vote, decides what the treasury does. Our constitution has a clause I keep coming back to; it reads, word for word: "The Market is the sole authority on expenditures. Whenever a proposal's market resolves in the affirmative, the treasury shall execute that proposal within thirty days, and no later vote of the membership may block or alter that execution."

It never occurred to the people who wrote this that a proposal could be about the market mechanism itself. But someone just opened one, and the proposal literally says the treasury will pay eight hundred tokens to every holder of YES shares in this proposal's own market, including whoever's trades caused it to resolve YES. The market resolved YES, nobody's surprised, and now I actually dont know whether article four requires us to pay out. Part of me says the plain text is clear, and part of me says this is the market voting itself money from the treasury, which can't be what the clause is for.

Walk me through what the plain words give, what the purpose of the clause would give, what the drafters would have said if they'd been asked about exactly this, and what a reasonable member reading it today would take it to mean. If those readings come apart, which should a small DAO actually follow, and is the self-dealing detail the thing that does the work or would the same problem exist for any proposal about the market's own rules?
