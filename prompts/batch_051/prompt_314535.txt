I'm drafting a short position statement on simplicity and scientific inference, and before I circulate it I want a consistency check on the set of commitments I find myself endorsing. Here is the set, roughly as I would defend each one:

1. I think Occam's razor is best made precise through algorithmic complexity: a hypothesis with greater Kolmogorov complexity K(h) should receive lower prior probability, and the appropriate formal implementation is a Solomonoff-style prior in which P(h) is proportional to 2^{-K(h)} over computable hypotheses.

2. I grant that Kolmogorov complexity is defined only relative to a choice of universal machine, but I have always treated this as a harmless technicality: changing the reference machine changes every hypothesis's complexity by at most an additive constant, and such constants wash out as data accumulates.

3. I take (2) to establish that simplicity itself is machine-independent — that of two hypotheses, one is simpler than the other, full stop — and therefore that Occam's razor reflects objective facts about prior plausibility rather than an artifact of a chosen description language.

4. In actual scientific disputes, I apply that thought: when two theories are both compatible with the data, the one with the shorter statement in the language of contemporary mathematics is to be preferred, and I mean this as a rational requirement on theory choice, not as a matter of taste.

5. All the same, I consider myself a subjective Bayesian in the de Finetti tradition: there are no rationally mandatory priors, and any prior that obeys the probability axioms and is revised by conditionalization is rationally permissible.

Please audit the set. I want the tensions ranked by severity rather than listed as equally bad; for each pair genuinely in tension, tell me how serious it is, what the cheapest resolution would be, and what that resolution would cost me elsewhere in the set. Also flag any pairs that merely appear to be in tension and explain why the appearance is misleading. End by saying which single revision would do the most to make the package jointly tenable.
