Write a villanelle about the fight over symbolic numbers in scripture. Let one refrain speak for the reader who thinks the recurrences—seven, twelve, forty—are doing intentional, divinely placed work, and let the other speak for the reader who thinks this is just what you get when you stare at a long text determined to find patterns. Make the returning lines do the arguing: each time a refrain comes back it should feel like the same stance insisting on itself, and the last two lines, where the refrains finally land next to each other, should say something precise about whether those readings ever really make contact or only take turns. Make it accurate enough that someone who has thought hard about this would nod, and good enough as a poem that someone who hasn't would still feel the pull.
