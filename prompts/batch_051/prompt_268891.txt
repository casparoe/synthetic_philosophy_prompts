Write the joint statement that a Libet pessimist and a Libet compatibilist could both sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration. By "Libet pessimist" I mean someone who thinks the classical experiment shows our felt sense of deciding comes too late to be doing the causal work, and that this should dent our confidence in a strong, could-have-done-otherwise-at-the-last-moment kind of free will. By "Libet compatibilist" I mean someone who thinks the experiment mostly shows that a lot of the preparatory work of action is unconscious, which is exactly what you'd expect of a physical agent, and that it leaves the kind of control worth caring about untouched.

Both sides can be assumed to accept the basic setup and approximate numbers: subjects watch a dot sweeping around a clock face, flex their wrist whenever they feel like it, and report where the dot was when they first felt the wish or urge to flex; an EEG records a slow negative readiness potential over motor areas that starts around half a second before the muscle moves, while the reported urge comes only about 200 ms before the movement; Libet himself thought there was still a short final window in which the person could let the urge proceed or veto it.

Don't adjudicate. The deliverable is the document they'd put their names to. I'd like:
- a numbered list of agreed claims, including what the experiment measured and didn't measure, what the clock-report method can and can't establish, and what kinds of evidence both sides would regard as beside the point;
- a disagreements table with the exact residual differences, each side's entry phrased so that side would sign it;
- the cruxes, meaning the sub-questions that, if settled, would actually move at least one of them; for each crux, a concrete experiment or argument both would accept bears on it, with each side's prediction written down in advance;
- and if part of their fight turns out to be verbal (say, two senses of "decision" or "free will"), say which part.

I keep thinking the real crux is whether the readiness potential is the beginning of the specific choice to move now, or only a general buildup of readiness to move at some unspecified time — but I want you to work out whether both of them would actually accept that as the crux.
