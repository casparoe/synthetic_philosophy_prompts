I teach a graduate seminar in the philosophy of mathematics, and next week's session is on Lakatos's claim that mathematics is quasi-empirical: informal proofs are revisable, and counterexamples to conjectures function somewhat like empirical refutations. I would like a handout that sorts each student into the methodological stance they actually take when a proof is threatened, rather than the stance they praise in discussion.

Please write an eight-to-ten-item quiz. Each item should present one concrete alleged polyhedron together with the value of V − E + F that makes it an alleged counterexample to Euler's formula, and offer three or four jargon-free responses drawn from the following repertoire: bar the object as not a genuine polyhedron; accept it as an exception and restrict the theorem's domain; revise the proof and conjecture by incorporating the lemma the object exposes; or treat the object as forcing a broader concept of polyhedron to be made explicit. Use at least these four cases: the hollow cube with an inner cubical cavity, for which V − E + F = 4; the picture-frame solid, a cube with a square tunnel bored through from front to back, for which V − E + F = 0; two tetrahedra glued along one shared edge, for which V − E + F = 3; and a star-polyhedron with self-intersecting pentagram faces (12 vertices, 30 edges, 12 faces), for which V − E + F = −6. Design a scoring key that maps patterns of answers onto the stances Lakatos analyzes as monster-barring, exception-barring, and lemma-incorporation, a proof-generated-concepts stance, and a formalist stance that rejects the informal counterexample vocabulary outright. Include at least two items on which the lemma-incorporation and exception-barring stances come apart, and one item on which the monster-barring and formalist stances cannot be separated; state that pair explicitly as a limitation of the quiz, and flag any other pairs the scoring key cannot distinguish. Finally, specify the two-dimensional space the quiz presumes (for example, whether the proof and conjecture are treated as revisable, crossed with whether the recalcitrant object is admitted as data), mark which cells no stance occupies, and give a one-sentence diagnosis of each empty cell as incoherent, unattractive, or merely unexplored.
