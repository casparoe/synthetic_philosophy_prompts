Regulation 12 of our unit's standing orders reads: "A member shall obey every lawful order of a superior. A member may refuse an order only when the order is manifestly unlawful; a member who refuses shall report the order and the grounds for refusal at the first opportunity." Three weeks ago the commanding officer delegated authority over resupply scheduling to a machine-learning system and announced that its taskings would have the same force as her own orders. Yesterday the system directed my section to carry a misdirected resupply through an exposed route at dawn, on the ground that any other route would delay the convoy; the tasking is not unlawful on any conventional reading, and the commanding officer is unreachable until tonight. Does Regulation 12 bind me to obey a direction issued by the system? Work through the text, the purpose of the regulation, what its drafters would have said if asked, and what a reasonable member would understand it to require; then say which approach should govern and why. I care less about the operational outcome than about what the answer shows about whether the authority relation can be delegated to a system or exists only between persons.
