After a long argument with a friend about Kavka's toxin puzzle, I still cannot see what the obstacle is supposed to be. The deal is that I receive the money tomorrow morning if, at midnight tonight, I genuinely intend to drink a toxin tomorrow afternoon — a toxin that would make me ill for a day, though the payment does not depend on my actually drinking it. Why can't I simply form the intention tonight, collect the money in the morning, and then decide not to drink once the money is already mine? Since the payment is conditioned only on my intention, not on my later action, why is this treated as a puzzle rather than a straightforward plan? What exactly is supposed to go wrong?
