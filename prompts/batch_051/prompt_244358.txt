An ex-colleague who did well enough to retire early has made me a one-time offer. If, by midnight Monday, I can sincerely form the intention to quit my current job and go run his foundation's field office for a year in a city I would never otherwise choose to live in, he will wire me forty thousand dollars on Tuesday morning. The payment is not conditional on my actually resigning, moving, or doing the job, and he will not follow up afterward; he says he simply wants to see whether I can get myself to genuinely intend it. But I know my own future behavior well enough to predict that, once the money has arrived, I will not go. I cannot tell whether it is possible for me to form the intention under those circumstances, whether I should try to simply decide to intend it and take the money, or whether declining is the only honest option.
