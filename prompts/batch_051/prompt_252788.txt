I want one essay question that can't be answered by just reciting the line about a measure turning into a target, and definitely no citations or reading lists. They should also have to face at least one hard objection to whatever side they take, rather than stopping at the taxonomy. Please write the question, a model answer of about 600 words, and a marking scheme out of 20 that separates an A from a B by naming the exact moves each must make. Course is upper-level undergrads, 90 minutes. Build the marking scheme around five must-hit points, with the marks each is worth and a one-line note on how candidates typically get each one wrong.
