Three AI labs, none of them yet ahead, sign a bargain about the post-AGI world while they still can. Each lab puts a serious chunk of its equity into escrow and gets back tradable tokens; if a jointly chosen audit board declares that any signatory has built AGI, all three labs agree to contribute their best future models above some capability threshold to a shared trust, and the tokens convert into voting and profit shares in that trust. The tokens trade freely in the meantime, but a lab that refuses to hand over the technology forfeits its escrow. Treat this as a mechanism, not a moral question: what does the token price actually track, how do the labs game the definition of AGI and the audit board's incentives, when does the leader defect anyway and can a big enough escrow ever stop it, and what does this do to research speed, secrecy, and hiring once people know the deal is in place? Can the obvious failure modes be patched, or does the pact mostly change how the race is gamed?
