I am taking a philosophy of artificial intelligence seminar, and we are trying to pin down the claim that transformative AI has already happened. We have isolated four candidate definitions of transformative AI that the seminar uses:

(1) Capability threshold: AI that can perform almost all cognitive work at least as well as a skilled human.
(2) Economic-transition threshold: AI that changes the rate of economic growth and the organization of labor as much as the agricultural or industrial revolution did.
(3) Dependence threshold: AI that has become so embedded in infrastructure such as finance, logistics, medicine, and public administration that those systems could no longer function without it, regardless of whether the AI is generally intelligent.
(4) Recursive-improvement threshold: AI that can itself carry out the research and engineering needed to improve its own capabilities, initiating a rapid and largely self-sustaining advance.

Please evaluate these definitions along two dimensions. Rate each one from 0 to 10 on (a) fidelity: how well it captures what the expression "transformative" is supposed to pick out, and (b) already-crossed: how plausible it is that the relevant threshold has already been crossed, or is now in the process of being crossed. Justify each score with one sentence. Then rank the four definitions from best to worst for the specific purpose of settling whether transformative AI has already happened, and state what you would conclude about that question under your top-ranked definition.

Return your answer as a single JSON object with no text outside it, using this schema:
- "ratings": an array of four objects, each with "label" (one of "capability", "economic_transition", "dependence", "recursive_improvement"), "fidelity" (a number from 0 to 10), "already_crossed" (a number from 0 to 10), and "justification" (one sentence).
- "ranking": an array of the four labels, ordered from most to least useful for settling the question.
- "verdict": a one- or two-sentence answer to whether transformative AI has already happened under the first definition in "ranking".
- "load_bearing_assumption": one sentence identifying the assumption on which the disagreement among the four definitions most depends.
