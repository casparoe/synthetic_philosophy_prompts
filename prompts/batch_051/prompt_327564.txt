Here are my gut verdicts about inductive projection, and I want the principle behind them — or an honest account of why no single principle exists and which verdicts must go.

All my emeralds so far are green, and all were first observed before 2100. I confidently expect the next emerald first observed after 2100 to be green. I refuse to project that it will be grue, where "grue" means "first observed before 2100 and green, or first observed after 2100 and blue" — even though "all emeralds are grue" fits the data exactly as well as "all emeralds are green." My first pass at the principle was entrenchment: green is the predicate my community has used in successful past inductions, grue is a philosophical novelty.

But three cases shake that. (1) I don't think a community that had always used grue and bleen as its basic color terms — defining green and blue as derivative disjunctions — would be making an inferential error when it projects grue; from the inside I'd do the same, and I can't honestly say the world sides with me. (2) When a physicist coins a new term for a microstructural property and confirms it in a few specimens, I'm willing to project it immediately, before the term has any track record of successful projection. (3) I will not project a disjunctive, gerrymandered predicate — "red or heavier than this laptop" — even if I stipulate that it has been entrenched in everyday use for centuries. Entrenchment looks neither necessary nor sufficient.

I've tried the simplicity answer too: green is a shorter program than grue in my usual encoding of the observations, so my prior favors it. But I can construct an encoding in which grue is the shorter program, flipping the simplicity ranking, and I can't find a non-question-begging reason to privilege my encoding.

Is there a single principle behind all of this, and which of my verdicts does it require me to drop? If no single principle will do, give me the best small set of candidate principles, state each precisely, and say which case motivates each clause. Then apply your leading candidate to cases I haven't listed, including the one I keep going back and forth on: after living in a grue-speaking community for a decade, would I — and should I — project grue rather than green?
