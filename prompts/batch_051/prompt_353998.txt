I'm interning at the state attorney's office and we have to decide whether to charge a student under a statute that makes it an offense for "a pupil to possess a dangerous weapon on school grounds." The statue doesn't define "dangerous weapon," so the case law has done the heavy lifting. Judge Rayburn has written basically all the go-to opinions, and our office can't agree on whether she's a textualist or a purposivist, because her cases point both ways.

In the first case, she held that a locking-blade multi-tool in a kid's backpack for wood shop wasn't a dangerous weapon—it's an everyday tool and there was no evidence he meant to use it on anyone. In the second, she held that practice nunchucks in a martial arts club member's gym locker were a dangerous weapon because they're made for inflicting injury, even if he treated them as training gear. In the third, she held that a solid plastic replica pistol was a dangerous weapon when a senior cleared the cafeteria with it as a prank, on the ground that it was deliberately used to make other students reasonably fear they'd be shot. In the fourth, she held that an old crossbow with a frayed string kept in a history classroom display cabinet wasn't a dangerous weapon. In the fifth, she held that a claw hammer in a student's backpack was a dangerous weapon where he'd sent texts threatening to "crack somebody's skull."

The new case is this: a senior is caught with a Class 4 laser pointer in his backpack. It can cause permanent retinal damage and skin burns. He has a finished slide deck on astronomy and says he brought it as a pointer for a presentation that same afternoon. No threats, no prior incidents, no evidence he planned to use it on anyone. We're trying to figure out what Judge Rayburn would do if she wants to stay consistent with herself, and what we'd be signing onto if we charge him or don't. So: which of her earlier cases is the closest analog, does her track record genuinely underdetermine this one, and what interpretive principle are we adopting with each possible outcome?
