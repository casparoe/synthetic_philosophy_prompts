Last night I got into a long argument with a friend about whether you can explain big historical changes by appeal to culture, specifically Weber's old Protestant-ethic thesis. I came away from it provisionally convinced of the moderate version: religious values do real independent causal work in economic change — not that Protestantism caused capitalism all by itself, but that ascetic values like discipline, a calling, delayed gratification, and suspicion of luxury reshaped economic behavior in ways you can't reduce to material interests or class position. My friend kept hammering the materialist objections and I felt like I had answers for most of them, but one of his points still nags me: if we can only infer the values from the very behavior theyre supposed to explain, the whole thing can end up circular, just redescribing the outcomes in cultural language.

So treat me as provisionally committed to the view but wanting an honest map of its exposure. What would count against it? I mean the real defeaters, not a generic list of criticisms: cases where the material conditions were right but the "spirit" didn't appear, or where it appeared without the religion; findings that undermine the psychological mechanism (that anxiety about predestination produced methodical work); evidence that the correlation is an artifact of cities, trade routes, or literacy; and the deeper conceptual worry that "culture" is just a label for aggregated individual motives and so can't be a cause at all. Please sort these (and whatever I've missed) from fatal to merely awkward, say which ones a committed defender should concede genuinely count against the view rather than explaining away, and tell me whether the view as I've stated it has been insulated to the point of unfalsifiability — and whether for a claim of this kind that is a serious defect or just the honest condition of historical explanation. Along the way, tell me what the view predicts that the materialist rival doesn't, and if it predicts nothing, say that straight out. The last question is the one I most want a clear answer to.
