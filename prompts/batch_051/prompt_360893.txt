Please give me a committed probability estimate, with the reasoning decomposed, on the following metaontological question. Two people are arguing about a piece of cheese full of holes. A says: there are holes in the cheese. B says: there are no holes — strictly speaking, there are only cavity-shaped regions of cheese, and ordinary talk of holes is loose shorthand. They agree on every empirical fact about the cheese: where the matter is, the shapes of the cavities, what you would see or feel, and what would happen if you filled them, and both accept, in ordinary contexts, claims like "the hole is about a centimetre across." Their only disagreement is over whether holes exist. What is your credence that their dispute is merely verbal, in the following precise sense: each party speaks a different but equally good language, so the appearance of disagreement comes from different but admissible meanings of existence-talk, the dispute would dissolve if both made their intended interpretation of "there is" explicit, and there is no worldly fact of the matter that one party gets right and the other wrong? Decompose that credence into the sub-questions that bear on it, including at least: (i) how likely it is that ordinary English determinately settles whether holes exist in such a case; (ii) how likely it is that some form of quantifier variance — the view that there are multiple equally admissible meanings for existential quantifiers, none uniquely carving nature at the joints — is correct; and (iii) how likely it is that A and B's existence-claims are best interpreted as involving different meanings rather than one shared meaning about which one of them is simply mistaken. Assign each sub-question a rough probability with a one-line justification, show how you combine them, and give the final number. Then say which sub-question your final credence is most sensitive to, and what concrete evidence or argument would move it most. I want a definite number with its weak points marked, not a survey of the available positions.
