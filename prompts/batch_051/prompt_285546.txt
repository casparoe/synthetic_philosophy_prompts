The other side says no, the function is what the thing does now, the contribution it makes to the organism staying alive or reproducing, and you shouldn't need a fossil record or a population-genetic model to work that out. They obviously overlap in easy cases like the heart pumping blood, but I don't have a sharp sense of where they come apart or why anyone should care.
