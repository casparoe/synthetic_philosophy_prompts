I am a novelist. The novel I am drafting turns on the difference between a character's being exploited and her being alienated, and I have been assembling the standard theoretical accounts of the inner structure of alienation so that I do not flatten the idea into a synonym for job dissatisfaction or misery. The accounts I have collected so far, in roughly historical order, are the following:

- Hegel's, in which Spirit externalizes itself into an objective world, and its own products confront it as alien until they are recognized and reappropriated.
- Feuerbach's, in which human beings project their species-essence onto a divine subject and therefore experience their own essential predicates as belonging to something other than themselves.
- The young Marx's argument in the Economic and Philosophic Manuscripts of 1844, in which estranged labor separates the worker from the product, from the activity of laboring, from her species-being, and from other human beings.
- The mature Marx's argument in Capital, in which commodity fetishism makes social relations between producers appear as relations among things, so that human activity takes on an autonomous, thing-like power over its makers.
- Lukács's account in History and Class Consciousness, in which the commodity form becomes universal, labor-power is treated as a calculable thing, and workers come to experience their own capacities as alien, reified objects.
- Debord's account in The Society of the Spectacle, in which alienation is consummated as separation and relations among people are mediated by images rather than encountered directly.

I believe these cover most of the standard ground, so I am not asking you to tidy up my list. What I need is something genuinely new: accounts of the inner structure of alienation that are different in kind from all six, not rewritings, special cases, or combinations of them.

Please give me exactly five candidate additions, numbered 1 to 5. For each candidate, state the account in one sentence and say in a second sentence what it adds that the six accounts above do not. Write your answer in British English, and give me the numbered list only, with no introduction or conclusion.
