Suppose it's 2040 and a one-time, cheap, safe intervention is developed that raises kids' measured cognitive ability by about one standard deviation on any major test, with no known side effects. A country with a national health system, invoking the therapy/enhancement distinction, decides to approve and fund it only for children who score below the 10th percentile on its standardized cognitive battery, as a treatment for a newly coined deficiency. For everyone else the same intervention is classed as enhancement and is neither funded nor, say, permitted for doctors to give to healthy kids. Predict, with reasoning, how the next twenty years play out. Pay particular attention to the mechanisms: does the diagnostic cutoff drift upward, do parents coach their kids to test bad or find sympathetic clinicians, does a grey market or medical tourism appear, and does the therapy/enhancement line itself hold or slowly fall apart? Mark which parts of your prediction are robust and which are just guesses.
