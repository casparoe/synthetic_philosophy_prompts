I am a software engineer with no formal philosophy background, and I work on content moderation systems. I have found that I lack a clear criterion for deciding when a platform action amounts to punishment. Give me a working definition of 'punishment' that gets these cases right. (1) A criminal fine for shoplifting. (2) A school suspending a pupil for fighting. (3) A platform permanently banning a user who has repeatedly harassed others. (4) An employer dismissing a software engineer after old social media posts resurface. (5) A group of users mass-reporting an account because they dislike its politics. (6) An automated spam filter silently deleting a message. (7) A tight-knit community quietly excluding a member after a falling-out. (8) A regulator's civil penalty against a company for a data breach. I take (1) and (2) to be clearly punishment and (6) clearly not punishment; I have no settled view on the rest, and (3) is the case I most want the definition to settle. Propose the definition, run it through all eight cases, flag any verdicts that surprise you, and say which feature of the definition is doing the work in the deplatforming case.
