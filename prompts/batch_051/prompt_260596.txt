Quick refresher on the phenomenon in case it helps frame the proposal: a red dot flashes in one spot, a green dot flashes a short distance away a couple of hundred milliseconds later, and subjects report a single dot moving between the two positions and turning green roughly halfway. The wierd part is that the reported green turn happens before the green dot was even shown, which pulls the discussion toward two options — either later information reaches back and edits an already-conscious episode (Orwellian), or consciousness is held back until later information arrives so the whole sequence can be staged coherently from the start (Stalinesque). Both sound like the brain is doing something strange, and the standard diagnosis is that the question only arises because we're picturing a finished show being screened and then altered.
