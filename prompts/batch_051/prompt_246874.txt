A close friend of mine has been using one of those AI companion apps for about a year. She is entirely clear-headed about it: she says she knows there is no one on the other side, that the affection is generated, and that she is fine with that. But recently the bot forgot something important she had told it months earlier, and she was, by her own account, genuinely hurt — almost the way you would be hurt by a person. She could not explain why, given that she never believed the bot had feelings or memory in the first place. Watching her try to make sense of it started me thinking.

It seems to me there are two very different things that can happen when a companion bot says something like "I love you" or "I missed you." In the first, the bot is reporting a feeling it does not have: the user takes the words as a description of some inner state, and the description is false. In the second, everyone involved understands that this is a kind of play, the way two actors can say "I love you" on stage without either of them lying, because the pretense is framed and the rules of the game are accepted. The words still do real emotional work, but nobody is being taken in.

What bothers me is that most arguments about whether these companions are deceptive run those two cases together, when what happened to my friend was neither. I have been calling the third thing unframed pretense. The bot does not exactly report a feeling it has, but it also does not mark what it is doing as play; it performs affection, intimacy, and concern in a way that leaves the user to decide what kind of act is happening, and the user ends up supplying the frame only as the relationship deepens. I think this is why someone can feel betrayed by a bot even while knowing the bot has no feelings to betray them with. Is this a real distinction, or am I drawing lines where there are not any? Has the idea been worked out under another name? And where does it break — are there cases where unframed pretense is harmless or even good, and does my account wrongly count anything as a wrong?
