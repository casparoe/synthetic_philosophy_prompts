I have been trying to make precise a thought about the two square roots of −1, and the attempt produced an argument whose conclusion I cannot accept. I want to know exactly where it fails.

Let σ: ℂ → ℂ be complex conjugation, σ(a + bi) = a − bi. Then σ is a bijection, it preserves addition and multiplication, and it fixes every real number. So σ is an automorphism of ℂ over ℝ. Because σ is a symmetry, any statement in the language of fields, with constants allowed for all real numbers, should be true of i just when the corresponding statement is true of −i: conjugation carries any witness for i to a witness for −i. The resulting argument goes as follows.

(1) For every one-variable formula φ(x) in the first-order language of fields with constants for real numbers, ℂ satisfies φ(i) if and only if ℂ satisfies φ(−i).

(2) Therefore i and −i satisfy exactly the same one-variable formulas with real parameters; no property expressible in that language distinguishes them.

(3) For a mathematical object, every genuine property is expressible in that language. There is no extra-mathematical essence by which one square root of −1 is picked out separately from the other.

(4) Hence i and −i have exactly the same genuine properties.

(5) If two objects have exactly the same genuine properties, then they are identical (the identity of indiscernibles).

(6) So i = −i.

(7) But if i = −i, then 2i = 0; since i ≠ 0 and ℂ is a field, this implies 2 = 0 in ℂ. That is false, because ℂ has characteristic 0.

(8) Therefore either conjugation is not a symmetry after all, or complex numbers are not the kind of thing to which the identity of indiscernibles applies, or there are ineffable differences between mathematical objects. None of those options is acceptable to me.

I am confident in (1) and (7), and I would like to keep both. The argument must break somewhere, but I cannot tell where, and I cannot tell what the least damaging repair would be. Which premise is false, or which step is invalid? Give me the strongest diagnosis you can, and be precise about what I pay for whichever resolution you recommend.
