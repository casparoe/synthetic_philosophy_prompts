Write a mock FAQ page for Archipelago, a seasteading startup selling "jurisdiction as a service." Around 600 words, in the deadpan style of a tech company's help center. Include membership tiers (Road Traffic Only vs. Full Common Law), an answer to "how do I unsubscribe from being sued?", a question about what happens when two customers from different flag-states collide at the marina, plus a footnote that runs longer than the answer on choice-of-law arbitration. End with a reassuring explanation that Archipelago is definately not a state, even though everyone rents their plot from the company. The satire should target exit-based libertarianism and quietly walk the reader through the standard objections—exit is never total, contract enforcement presupposes a legal order, and a seastead that governs people is just a state with better branding—without ever saying them outright. The humor shouldn't depend on strawmanning the position; it should come from taking its actual logic a step too far.
