I dont want to just explain it; I want a plan for the session. What has to be in place before the paradox even bites — do they need the idea of levels or grounding first, or do you open with the picture and let the confusion do the work? Which single carrying example should I build on (Drawing Hands, the Liar sentence, a camera filming its own output), and what does it need to have and lack to actually carry the idea? What are the misconceptions they'll bring or form, in the order they usually show up, and at which point in the sequence do I head each off? What should I deliberately leave out at this level and flag as omitted? And how will I know by the end that they've got the inviolate level rather than just enjoying a trippy drawing?
