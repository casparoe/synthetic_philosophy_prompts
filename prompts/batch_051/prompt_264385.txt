Consider the following variation of Mary's room. Mary, confined from birth to a monochrome room, has learned every physical fact about human color vision. Unlike in the standard case, she is also a committed Global Workspace Theorist: she holds that a representation becomes conscious when it is selected, amplified, and globally broadcast through a fronto-parietal workspace, making it available to verbal report, memory, and intentional action. She further accepts the strong identification this case is designed to test: the phenomenal character of seeing red just is, or is fully explained by, the global broadcast of red-information, so there is nothing further for her to learn.

Now alter the case. Before she ever leaves the room, Mary is given a cortical stimulator that, on command, will directly induce in her brain the complete global-workspace broadcast pattern that her theory identifies with being conscious of red — the exact state she would enter if she were looking at a ripe tomato. The state is specified to her in full physical and functional detail: she knows what neurons will fire, what reports she will be disposed to make, and how her memory and attention will be guided. She has simply never occupied the state herself. Before activating the stimulator, she must write down one sentence predicting whether, afterward, she will say that she has learned something new.

What should Mary predict if her strong identification of phenomenal consciousness with global broadcast is correct? What should she predict if GWT captures only access consciousness — reportability and availability — while phenomenal character is something further, along the lines of Block's access/phenomenal distinction? And if, after the stimulation, she says, "I did not know red would be like this," does Jackson's original conclusion that there are non-physical facts survive the variation, or has the argument shifted from a dispute about physicalism to a dispute about what global workspace theory ever claimed to explain?
