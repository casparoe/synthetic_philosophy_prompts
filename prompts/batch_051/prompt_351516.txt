What should I expect her to say about the courthouse case if she's being consistent? Are her earlier verdicts consistent with more than one rule, and what does each rule predict here? Give me your best guess about which rule she's actually running and why, and name one further case I could put to her that would settle it. Lead with your bottom-line prediction in the first sentence, then reason through it; no preamble, no restating the question.
