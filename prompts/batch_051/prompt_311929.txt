The fight is over how to turn nine individual rankings into that list: some want a straight plurality vote, some want Borda, some want instant-ruoff. Last night one member waved around Arrow's impossibility theorem and said every ranked rule violates at least one obviously fair condition, so there's no principled way to choose between them and we should just use whatever's easiest to tabulate. I need to draft the short memo from the committee that actually settles our procedure. It should recommend one rule, say what we do if the pairwise majority preferences cycle, and take the Arrow point seriously rather than just setting it aside—and it should give the strongest argument for the rule we don't pick a fair shake.
