A fair number of NLP people talk as though these vectors just are meanings, or at least that semantics is in the business of assigning each word a location in a space like that. Suppose Frege had been shown a well-trained embedding space and the claim that a word's vector is what its meaning amounts to. What do you think he would have made of it?
