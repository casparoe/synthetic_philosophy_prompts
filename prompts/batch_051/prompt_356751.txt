I work as a systems designer at a small studio making a single-player life simulation. The game models its characters in unusual detail: they have routines, relationships, memories, and emotional states that visibly change in response to what the player does. We are now scoping an optional mechanic that would let the player subject these characters to sustained, gratuitous cruelty—humiliating them, injuring them, destroying their homes, breaking up their families—purely for amusement, with no narrative purpose and no in-game reward. At our last milestone review, the lead producer argued that this should be uncontroversial. The characters, he said, are not real: they are deterministic software objects, nothing in the game actually suffers, and no actual person or animal is harmed. He added that players demonstrably enjoy sandbox cruelty, and that deciding not to include it because of our own discomfort would amount to moralizing at paying customers. The rest of the room largely agreed, but I found myself unable to share the consensus, and I also found I could not identify any specific error in his reasoning.

I now have to give my own position at the next review. I want to know whether there is a principled moral objection to shipping this feature, or whether my unease is just a sentimental reaction to pixels with no more weight than disliking a color palette. If there is an objection, can it be stated without claiming that the characters are conscious, and without assuming that players who use the feature will go on to harm real people? And does the objection gain or lose force when the cruelty is directed at a character the player has spent many hours caring for, as opposed to a procedurally generated stranger they have never encountered before?

I would rather hear the strongest version of the moral case against the feature, stated as fairly as possible, and then an honest assessment of whether that case survives the producer’s reply. If some version of his argument is sound, I want to know that too, even if it means my discomfort has no rational basis.
