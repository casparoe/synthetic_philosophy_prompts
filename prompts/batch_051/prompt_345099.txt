Recently I have been working on a tool that connects a classical Euclidean construction environment to an automated geometry prover, and as a result I started reading Hobbes’s geometrical writings. The problem I keep running into is whether what the machine does should count as geometry in his demanding sense. Let me put the issue as a set of criteria and a borderline case.

As I understand him, Hobbes held that geometry is our one genuine science because its objects are made by us: we can know a circle, square, or triangle because we know how it is generated. From this I extract four criteria for a piece of mathematics to be geometry in Hobbes’s sense. It must (a) treat objects we construct rather than properties merely read off bodies, (b) define an object by an operation of generation, (c) move from that construction to the demonstrated properties, so that the proof tracks the dependence of the property on the construction, and (d) involve demonstrative reasoning rather than mere symbolic computation, because calculation can be correct while leaving the causes unknown.

Now consider as a borderline case the following proof. A modern theorem prover verifies a nontrivial theorem in Euclidean geometry by translating the statement into polynomial equations and inequalities, then running a complete algebraic decision procedure. The theorem is about constructible figures, and indeed a human can afterwards draw the corresponding figure with compass and straightedge. But the proof itself never displays or follows the construction; the steps are algebraic and the output is a certificate that the theorem holds.

Does this qualify as geometry according to Hobbes’s criteria, or would he classify it as mere calculation rather than science? Please go criterion by criterion, flag where the criteria underdetermine the answer, and then say whether the case reveals that one of my four criteria is really the load-bearing one or that my formulation of Hobbes’s view has gone wrong. If you think I have imposed a modern distinction between checking that something is true and understanding why it is true that does not match Hobbes’s own categories, tell me that directly rather than polishing the answer to fit the question.
