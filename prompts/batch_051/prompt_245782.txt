People talk about a particular finite string being random — not just the coin that produced it. I want to know if that's testable or just a way of talking. Suppose I give you two 200-bit strings: one is the first 200 bits of the binary expansion of pi, the other came from a hardware random number generator, and I dont tell you which is which. Can you design a concrete test that looks only at the strings and not at how they were made, and that would sort them out? Say exactly what you'd compute or measure, what result would count as 'the pi-string is less random as a string,' and what result would push you toward 'only the process can be random, the strings themselves are all equally random.' Then be honest about the residue: if your test says a string is random, what could still be true that would make you take it back — say, a short program you hadn't thought of that generates it — and whether 'random' here is just relative to whatever compression scheme or test battery you happen to choose.
