"Vim coeli reserate viri: venit agnita ad usus: Ignotae videas commoda nulla rei."

which is rendered in the English translation I'm using as "Discover the force of the heavens O Men: Once recognised it can be put to use: No use could be seen in unknown things."

But this passage sounds like he's making a methodological claim: the heavens have a discoverable force, discovery is what makes it usable, and unknown things give us no benefit. That is not the same as saying astrology should be accepted uncritically; he rejected a lot of the standard techniques of his day.
