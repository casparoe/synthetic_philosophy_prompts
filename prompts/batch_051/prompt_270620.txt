A friend of mine has a consistent record of small wagers, and I want to predict what she will do with a new one. Her past decisions are these: she accepted a £10 ticket for a 1 in 100 chance of £2,000; she accepted a £10 ticket for a 1 in 2 chance of £25; she rejected a £10 ticket for a 4 in 5 chance of £11; and she rejected a £10 ticket for a 1 in 50 chance of £200. She has now been offered a £10 ticket for a 1 in 10^12 chance of £10^15. Predict what she will do if she is being consistent with herself. In the course of your answer, identify the rule or rules that fit her past decisions, say which rule best explains her record and is most defensible, and specify a further wager that would settle whether she is an unbounded expected-value maximiser or an expected-value maximiser with a lower bound on credible probabilities. Please write the answer in British English.
