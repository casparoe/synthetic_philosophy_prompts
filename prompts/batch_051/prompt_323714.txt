Suppose someone argues that the recurring presence of the number seven across a scriptural text is not a byproduct of our appetite for pattern but a deliberate symbolic device: the number is doing real interpretive work in those passages, and a reading that ignores it is incomplete. I suspect this is mostly hits-noted-and-misses-ignored. How would one test the claim that such a numerical pattern is intentional rather than an artifact of selective attention? Design a concrete procedure — baselines, comparison corpora, controls for how many non-sevens are available, checks of similar patterns in other texts — and say what result would favor the intentional reading and what would favor the pattern-hunting reading. Then state exactly what the strongest positive result would still leave open: in particular, what would have to be added to move from the recurrence not being plausibly chance to the number carrying a determinate meaning, and whether that further step is something any test could settle.
