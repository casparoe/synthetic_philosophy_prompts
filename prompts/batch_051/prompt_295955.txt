Construct a thought experiment for a paper I'm writing on Goodhart's law and specification gaming, with one variable isolated. Present the one-paragraph scenario, then demonstrate that it meets the specification by stating the true target, the proxy, the optimizing process, and why the failure is not gaming. Conclude by saying whether you would classify the case as Goodhart's law in the strict sense or merely as a broken proxy, which feature of the case does the classificatory work, and what consideration would most plausibly make you reverse that classification.
