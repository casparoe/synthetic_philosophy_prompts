I'm a journalist covering the KJV-only movement, and I'd like to roleplay an interview. Play a thoughtful KJV-onlyist—sharp, not a strawman, willing to concede small points but never that the King James is the inspired English Bible. Stay in character, one paragraph at a time, until I say 'stop.' My first question: In the 1611 preface The Translators to the Reader, the KJV translators wrote:

"Some peradventure would have no variety of senses to be set in the margin, lest the authority of the Scriptures for deciding of controversies by that show of uncertainty, should somewhat be shaken. But we hold their judgment not to be so sound in this point .... doth not a margin do well to admonish the reader to seek further, and not to conclude or dogmatize upon this or that peremptorily?"

If the translators themselves admittted marginal variants and told readers not to dogmatize, how can you insist one English rendering is exclusively inspired?
