I've been turning over something about loyalty and whether it's actually a bias. A lot of what I hear about morality says you shouldn't be partial: judges are supposed to be impartial, treating people different based on race is wrong because it's arbitrary, and from the moral point of view everyone's supposed to count the same. But then the way I actually live is completely partial — I'd spend on my own kid's college before helping some stranger's kid who needs it way more. By that logic, that looks like a bias, the same way confirmation bias is a bias. So is partiality to the people you're closest to just a bias we should be trying to correct?
