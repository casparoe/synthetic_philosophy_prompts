He wants me to back his proposal that we teach a rationality studio course instead of yet another seminar. I'm sympathetic but suspicious; it smells a bit like the old "brain training" line. So: audit the analogy for me. What corresponds to what (dojo, kata, sparring partner, coach, reflex), where does it break, and which features of martial-arts training quietly smuggle in things that rationality practice may not have? Then tell me whether the pedagogical conclusion survives the dissanalogies, or whether the analogy was carrying it the whole way. And if there's a better comparison for the kind of training he's after, say what it is.
