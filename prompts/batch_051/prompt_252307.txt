Been turning this over for a while now. Say a machine can do my job faster and better than I can — like actually better on every measure. Then me doing the job is just a worse version of something that already exists, so whats the point. I keep seeing people say automation will free us up for more meaningful work, but if meaning comes from doing something well, and the machine does it better, doesn't that make all the work left over for humans kind of pointless by definition? Like the only way my work matters is if it needed doing and I'm the one who can do it — and automation takes away exactly that. What am I missing?
