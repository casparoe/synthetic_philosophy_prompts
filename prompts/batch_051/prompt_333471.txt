Novelist here, currently researching a historical novel set in a port town around 1850. I've been reading digitized parish registers, court records, workhouse admission books, ship manifests — that kind of thing. And I keep noticing a pattern that's starting to shape the book in a way I dont trust.

The only time most people show up in these records is when they bumped into an institution. A woman exists because she was arrested for stealing a shawl, admitted to the workhouse pregnant, then buried on a given date. A dockworker exists because he was sued over a debt in 1847, then nothing for twenty years, then a death registration. The stretches in between are pitch dark. So when I try to reconstruct a life, it comes out as a chain of crises and official encounters: it looks like people in 1850 were constantly arraigned, counted, fined, or punished. But that isn't what their lives were mostly like; it's just what happened to stick.

And it's not that information is merely missing. Missing would be neutral. This is more like an amplifier for conflict and state contact: the past ends up looking more governed, more criminal, more miserable than it plausibly was, because the quiet or content stretches never got recorded at all. It also skews wich people I can even write about — the ones attached to institutions — while everyone who lived more quietly stays in the dark, and I'm left inventing them with no documentary grain to push against.

Is there an established name for this exact thing — not records being spotty in general, but the way the record systematically overrepresents moments of contact with power and underrepresents ordinary life? I've heard 'survivorship bias' and 'selection bias' tossed around for related problems, but those feel too broad. The thing I'm after is the positive distortion toward conflict and control. If it has no name, what would you call it, where are its edges, and does the same pattern hold for, say, a person's own letters or a ship's log — or does it fade when the source isn't produced by an institution? I'm not just after a label; I want the boundaries, because I have to decide how much of this distortion is built into the book I'm writing.
