On an artists' forum someone keeps settling every AI-scraping thread with the same Lockean one-liner: you own whatever you mix your labour with, so scraping your portfolio for training data is just theft. Then when pushed they tack on "as long as you leave as much and as good for others" like a footnote. I want to see how many different precise claims are hiding in that slogan. Could you lay out, as a menu, the distinct things "mixing your labour with a thing makes it yours" might mean — what "mixing" could even be (literally making the thing? adding value? just working on it?), whether labour is what creates the ownership or just the thing that matters for fairness, how much of a resource gets acquired, and what the proviso actually demands — and then say for each reading whether it's defensible and whether it actually supports the argument that training a model on someone's paintings or writing without asking is theft. I suspect only one or two readings carry that conclusion.
