Write a satirical roommate agreement between the left and right hemispheres of a split-brain patient, formatted as a numbered co-tenancy contract. The left hemisphere—the one that talks—drafts the whole thing, so its voice dominates and it keeps casting itself as the reasonable one; the right hemisphere can only object by making the left hand point at a laminated card that says OBJECTION, so work in a few annotations, crossed-out clauses, or notes scrawled by the left hand. The humor should come from actual split-brain phenomena, not just generic "two brains" jokes: the left hemisphere's interpreter confabulating reasons for choices the right hemisphere actually made, fights over who made the left hand grab the cookie, whether the contract is being signed by one tenant or two after the callosotomy, and the speaking hemisphere unilaterally declaring itself head of household. It should read like a real legal contract but quietly raise the unity-of-consciousness question without lecturing: after the operation that cuts the corpus callosum, is there one subject in this skull or two, and who gets to decide? Should be funny to someone who knows the split-brain literature and readable to someone who doesn't. Around 700 words, numbered clauses, with a seperate addendum on "disputed actions of the left hand."
