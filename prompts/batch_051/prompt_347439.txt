Been trying to figure out what I actually think knowledge is, so I wrote down my gut reactions to a bunch of cases and I'm hoping there's a principle underneath them. Here's where I landed.

1. I'm sitting in a normal room in good light looking at my own hands. Do I know I have hands? Clearly yes.
2. Same scene, but now someone asks whether I know I'm not a brain in a vat being fed exactly these hand-experiences. My gut says no—I don't know that, even though I'd bet my life it's false.
3. I also believe that if I know P, and P entails Q, then I can come to know Q just by deducing it. But 1 plus 3 seems to force me to know I'm not a brain in a vat. So one of 1-3 has to go, right?
4. I buy one ticket in a fair lottery with ten million tickets. The draw happened an hour ago and I haven't checked. Do I know I lost? No.
5. I parked my car on a quiet street an hour ago. Do I know it's still there? Yes. But the chance its been stolen is way higher than the chance my ticket won, so whatever explains 4 can't just be high probability.
6. I'm driving through fake-barn county, where nearly every barn-shaped thing is a facade and only one is real. I point at the one real barn and say 'that's a barn.' Do I know it's a barn? No, even though it is one and I see it perfectly clearly. If I'd pointed at the one beside it, I'd have believed that was a barn too.
7. But my grandmother believes I'm healthy because I called her this morning and sounded fine, and I am healthy. The catch: if I were sick, my family would lie to her and she'd still believe I'm healthy. My gut still says she knows I'm healthy. I can't see what makes 7 different from 6, though.

I keep goign back and forth on a bonus case: a stock analyst has a model that picks winners 90% of the time, and it says this stock will go up, and it does. But in this particular setup, if the stock had gone down, the model would still have said 'up.' Does she know it'll go up? So: is there a principle that gets my verdicts, or most of them? Which of my verdicts would it force me to drop, and what does it say about the analyst?
