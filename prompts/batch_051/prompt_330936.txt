A labmate keeps claiming that when students make systematic mistakes in intro physics they aren't just missing knowledge or guessing—they're applying a coherent alternative theory, like the old impetus idea that a thrown ball keeps being carried along by the throw. I cant tell whether that's a testable hypothesis or just a flattering description of messy errors. Design the strongest test you can of whether a student's error pattern is theory-driven rather than a pile of surface associations and half-remembered formulas. Say what result would push you toward each reading. Then be explicit about what even the best test could not settle: is the leftover a limit of the method, or is the notion that the student has a theory itself too under-specified for any experiment to resolve?
