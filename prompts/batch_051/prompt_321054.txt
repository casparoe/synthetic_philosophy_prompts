Okay, I'm a novelist, not a philosopher, but I've been kicking around a theory of dreams and I want an honest read on it. I call it the retroactive draft theory. The claim: dreams are not experiences we have while sleeping at all. What we call a dream is a short piece of fiction drafted in the first moments of waking, built out of leftover sleep mentation that wasn't itself an experience. The what-it-is-like quality gets backfilled; the first telling is the dream. A dream is like the one-paragraph pitch you give when someone asks what your novel is about. Before the question, there was no pitch; there was a pile of half-formed material, and the act of pitching narrates it into something with a protagonist, stakes, and a mood. The pitch doesn't report the novel; it retroactively makes a version of it. Same with dreams: the report doesn't report an experience, it creates one after the fact.

My standard example: my alarm goes off and I mumble that I was being chased around a flooded library by my dead aunt. If you could stop the universe one second before the alarm and ask whether I was experiencing an aunt, a library, or a chase, I don't think there was any experience to point to. The face, the water level, the reason I was running all get drafted as the sentence leaves my mouth, from fragments that didn't yet add up to anything.

I know Malcolm thought something in the neighborhood, that we only have the waking report and no independent check on what happens during sleep, and Dennett has that thought experiment about a dream being loaded into memory at waking so you couldn't tell from the inside. But I think my version is more aggressive than either: it isn't just that the experience is unverifiable or that a memory could be planted. It's that the experience itself is caused by the act of reporting, the way a character is caused by the prose that describes her.

Tell me straight: is this coherent? Is it already Dennett or Malcolm under another name, or something genuinely different? Whats original, whats right, and where does it fall apart — I want the strongest counterexample or finding you can throw at it. Give me exactly five numbered points, each one or two sentences, no introduction and no conclusion.
