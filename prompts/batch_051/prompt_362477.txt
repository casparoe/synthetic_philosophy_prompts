Something I dont get. At school we get marked down for split infinitives, 'less' instead of 'fewer', starting a sentence with 'but' -- the usual stuff. Then I read actual linguists and they all say there's no such thing as a language being wrong, it's just different, and standard English is basically a dialect with better PR. I found out this argument even has a name, prescriptivism versus descriptivism, and its been going for ages with nobody moving. But those same linguists still write in pretty standard English and I doubt they say 'we was'. And the teachers aren't stupid either, they know language changes. So is this a factual disagreement about what grammar is, or are the two sides doing different jobs under one word -- describing how people actually talk versus enforcing a prestige code -- or is it just a verbal dispute about what 'bad grammar' means? Diagnose what each side is really disagreeing about, and tell me what would actually count as progress.
