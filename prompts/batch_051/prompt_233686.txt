I am a novelist, and I sit as the lay member of the Harrowmere Regeneration Commission. The Commission must decide this month between two schemes for the former gasworks at Calderwych. Scheme A, 'cap and contain', would seal the contaminated soils beneath a membrane and allow a promised primary school and some four hundred homes to proceed immediately; the engineers estimate the cap will begin to fail after roughly sixty years, after which our successors would face a far costlier excavation while, in the meantime, whoever lives on the flood plain carries a slowly rising exposure risk. Scheme B, 'excavate now', would remove the contaminated soil to licensed disposal before any construction. It is permanently safer, but it costs roughly twice as much, delays the housing by four or five years, and would probably mean the school is never built on that site.

Our demographic consultants have raised a difficulty I cannot put down. The population living beside the site in sixty years' time will not consist of the same individuals whichever scheme we choose, because the two options alter the timing and pattern of housing, schooling and migration — and thereby alter which couples form and which children come to exist. A person born into the residual risk under Scheme A would most likely never have existed had we chosen Scheme B. The consultants also observe that such a life, though exposed to that elevated risk, would still be well worth living by any ordinary standard. It seems to follow that no future individual could later complain that we made them worse off than they would otherwise have been, since they would not otherwise have been at all; and yet I find it difficult to accept that this dissolves our reason to pay the higher cost now.

Please draft the short written opinion I will circulate under my own name before Thursday's meeting. It should recommend one concrete course of action with an implementation timeframe, engage the 'nobody is harmed' reasoning seriously rather than merely dismissing it, and set out honestly the strongest consideration on the losing side and why it does not carry the day. The final document should be written in British English.
