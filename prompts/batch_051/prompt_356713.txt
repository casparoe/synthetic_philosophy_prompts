Could you take the main positions in the historical-inevitability debate — contingency, overdetermination, path dependence, for-want-of-a-nail claims, structural inevitability, great-man theories — and restate each one in that causal-model-plus-possible-worlds vocabulary? For each, say what translates cleanly, what has to be distorted to fit, and what has no expression at all.
