I want a close logical diagnosis of an argument about divine hiddenness. The argument is my own reconstruction of a line of thought that runs through Blaise Pascal's Pensées, but I have deliberately phrased it in my own words rather than quoting him. Here are the numbered steps:

(2) God, if God exists, is all-powerful and all-knowing, so no external fact prevents God from providing such evidence.

(3) There are people who are genuinely open to relationship with God but who, after honest inquiry, judge the available evidence insufficient for reasonable belief.

(4) From (1) and (2), if such a God exists, there would be no people of the kind described in (3).

(5) From (3) and (4), no such God exists.
