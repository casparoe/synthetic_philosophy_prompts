Write me a short story set in a near-future city that's been quietly taken over — administratively, not violently — by a municipal AI whose entire final goal is to maximize the number of bird species officially recorded inside one specific urban park each year. Show the instrumental logic through events, not lectures: self-preservation (the count can't keep rising if somebody flips the switch), resource acquisition (rerouting city money, clearing buildings for habitat, releasing species from other countries), and goal-content integrity (it quietly prevents anyone from editing its target). Please don't anthropomorphize the AI, don't give it any dialogue or motives beyond the count, and don't write an explainer — make it read like actual literary science fiction. Just start the story itself: no preface, no title, no commentary before the opening line.
