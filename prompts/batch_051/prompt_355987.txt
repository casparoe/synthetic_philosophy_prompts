The expected multiplier on one round is 1.05, so the ensemble story says wealth grows about 5% per round. But a single person who plays again and again experiences multiplicative growth, and the relevant per-round growth along one long trajectory is the geometric mean, the square root of (1.5 × 0.6), which is just under 0.95, so the typical player is slowly ruined rather then enriched.

In this setup the group's total wealth, and each person's share, is multiplied each round by the sample mean of the N individual multipliers rather than by any one person's multiplier.
