In my philosophy of biology course we have been working through the species problem, and I keep returning to a passage in Darwin’s *On the Origin of Species* (1859), Chapter II, where he writes:

From these remarks it will be seen that I look at the term species, as one arbitrarily given for the sake of convenience to a set of individuals closely resembling each other, and that it does not essentially differ from the term variety, which is given to less distinct and more fluctuating forms.

Darwin seems to be arguing that species is not a natural kind with a sharp boundary, but a convenient label imposed on a continuum of variation, so that the species–variety distinction is one of degree rather than kind. I am trying to decide whether this argument survives a later development that appears to make species boundaries much less arbitrary: contemporary prokaryotic systematics often assigns bacterial species using a quantitative whole-genome cutoff, such as 95% average nucleotide identity across homologous genes. That looks like an operational species criterion, not a naturalist’s vague judgment about resemblance.

But I cannot tell whether this actually refutes Darwin or merely relocates his point. The 95% threshold looks pragmatically chosen rather than tracking a discovered discontinuity, and different cutoffs yield different boundaries. Moreover, bacteria exchange genes horizontally, so a genome-similarity threshold may be measuring something quite different from the lineage-based notions of species that Darwin’s later readers introduced. 

My question is this: Does Darwin’s argument that species is an arbitrary convenience survive the introduction of quantitative genome-similarity thresholds in prokaryotic taxonomy, or does the existence of an operational cutoff show that Darwin’s claim was an artifact of pre-genetic natural history? I would like you to identify which premise of Darwin’s argument the threshold development actually bears on, say whether a repaired version of his argument is still available, and say what the resulting position predicts about borderline bacterial groups.
