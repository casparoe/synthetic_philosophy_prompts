The position I want translated is Van Fraassen's Reflection principle. In my rough formulation: let P_t be a rational agent's degrees of belief now and P_{t+1} her degrees of belief at some fixed later time. For any proposition H and any value r, the agent should satisfy

P_t(H | P_{t+1}(H) = r) = r.

In words: conditional on learning now that her future self will assign probability r to H, she should assign probability r to H now. She should treat her future self as an expert about H before knowing what evidence will arrive.
