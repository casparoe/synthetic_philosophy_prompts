One said "corporations are people too" to show that legal personhood is already detached from biological humanity, so giving an AI system legal personality is no category mistake. The other replied that corporate personhood is itself a strained legal fiction, so the slogan is a warning about how easily the word "person" gets stretched, not a license to stretch it further. For the paper I am writing, I want the slogan itself disambiguated.
