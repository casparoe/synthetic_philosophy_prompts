Hofstadter's careenium is a toy world of little simmballs whose collisions and couplings determine everything that happens in it. The self-symbol is just a large-scale pattern in those simmballs. Yet the setup is meant to show the self-symbol pushing the simmballs around and steering the whole careenium. How can both claims hold? If the pushing is merely the simmball-level story described at a coarser grain, then the symbol seems to be doing no genuine pushing; but if the pushing is genuine, then the simmball-level story must have been causally incomplete. Where does this dilemma go wrong?
