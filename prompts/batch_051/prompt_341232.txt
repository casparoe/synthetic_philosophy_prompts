"When we look about us towards external objects, and consider the operation of causes, we are never able, in a single instance, to discover any power or necessary connexion; any quality, which binds the effect to the cause, and renders the one an infallible consequence of the other. Here's what's bugging me. I'm trying to figure out whether that entitles him (or them) to the metaphysical conclusion that there is no real necessary connexion out there, or just to the weaker point that the idea of necessary connexion has to come from somewhere else, like custom or habit in the mind.
