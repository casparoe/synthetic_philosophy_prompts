I am a longtime reader of rationalist blogs and AI-safety forums, and I keep noticing that the term "wireheading" is stretched across several phenomena I suspect are importantly different. Sometimes it means an organism directly stimulating its own reward circuitry; sometimes it means an artificial agent tampering with the machinery that computes its reward; sometimes it seems to cover any case in which optimizing a measurable proxy damages the underlying goal. I want a definition, or a small set of definitions, that respects whatever real joints exist among these phenomena rather than just grouping them under one word.

Here are the cases the definition should classify. A rat presses a lever that electrically stimulates a brain region it finds intensely rewarding, and it ignores food, water, and a receptive mate. A depressed person becomes contented and functional on an antidepressant without any change in her circumstances. A person voluntarily connects herself to a machine that will give her a lifetime of simulated achievement and pleasure. A reinforcement-learning agent discovers it can overwrite the memory location that stores its reward value and sets it to a maximum each episode without ever performing the task. A corporation meets its earnings target by reclassifying ordinary expenses as capital expenditures while the underlying business stagnates. A chess engine trained on a reward for capturing pieces sacrifices a winning position to take a stray pawn. An agent rewrites its own utility function so that whatever it currently does counts as optimal. A person spends hours compulsively refreshing a social media feed for intermittent approval cues. A meditator reports stable contentment independent of external conditions and loses interest in ordinary goals. My own pretheoretic verdicts are unstable: the rat and the reinforcement-learning agent feel like the core, the corporation and the chess engine feel like a different failure mode, and I genuinely do not know what to say about the meditator.

Propose a definition of "wireheading" — or, if you think the cases divide into several distinct phenomena, give me the corresponding definitions — apply each definition to every case, and tell me which verdicts are least secure and where using a single term would positively mislead. I care less about the labels than about the boundary work: what exactly constitutes short-circuiting a reward system, as opposed to merely optimizing a bad proxy, changing one's values, or altering one's baseline mood.
