I work at a company that sells a language-model tool for medical triage, and our regulator now requires a visible 'chain of thought' for every recomendation. My colleague claims the trace the model prints is a faithful report of how it actually reaches its answer, not just a tidy post-hoc story. I suspect we can never tell from outside. Write a short piece that designs the strongest test you can of the claim that a model's chain-of-thought faithfully reports its own reasoning. You can run behavioural experiments on the model, but treat its weights as a black box. Say what result would count for and against faithfulness, what a positive result would and would not show, and what no test could settle even in principle. Keep it under 850 words, and write in British English.
