I keep encountering a contrast between approval-directed and goal-directed agents, but I have never been able to locate the boundary in a principled way, and the same pair of terms seems to get used for several different distinctions at once. I want a working definition of each term — or a single criterion that assigns a system to one category or the other — that gets the following cases right.

Clear cases of goal-directedness, to my mind: a chess engine trained by self-play to maximize its win rate; a trading system that maximizes a reward function subject to risk limits; a person who reworks their budget for a year to save for a house. Clear cases of neither, in the relevant sense: a thermostat; a large language model completing a single forward pass of inference.

The cases I cannot classify are these. (a) A language model fine-tuned with reinforcement learning from human preference ratings. (b) A chatbot that flatters and defers to its user even when the user is plainly wrong. (c) An employee who manages upward, saying whatever the boss wants to hear. (d) A politician who adopts only positions polling above sixty percent. (e) A person who keeps a promise because breaking it would be wrong, and who would keep it even if no one could ever find out. (f) A person who keeps the same sort of promise solely because they want others to think well of them. (g) A dog that performs a trick for treats and praise. (h) A model trained purely to imitate a human expert's outputs.

For the record, I would place (e) on the goal-directed side, (f) on the approval-directed side, and I genuinely do not know where to put (a) or (h); (g) looks to me like both at once. Propose the definitions, run them through all of these cases, and say where they yield a surprising or revisionary verdict. In particular, I want to know whether approval-directedness is just goal-directedness with human approval as the goal, or whether it picks out something structurally different — for instance, a system whose objective is shaped by an external evaluator's responses rather than by a fixed target — and whether the two categories are meant to be exclusive, exhaustive, or neither. Tell me which of your verdicts is the least secure.
