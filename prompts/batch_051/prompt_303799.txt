Write an English sonnet that works as a worked example of Gricean speaker meaning rather than a poem about Grice. The octave should present a brief ordinary exchange in which a direct question is answered with an apparently irrelevant remark that flouts the maxim of Relation and, in context, clearly conveys something the speaker means; the sestet should replay the same exchange from the hearer's side, making the inference explicit, so that the volta is the moment the irrelevance resolves into an implicature. The poem itself should not mention Grice, implicature, maxims, or speaker meaning. The implicature should be conversational rather than conventional, context-dependent, and calculable from the Cooperative Principle in the standard Gricean way. Follow with a section headed Commentary of about 150 words identifying how the maxim is flouted, how the implicature is calculated, and what one formal feature of the sonnet does that a prose gloss could not.
