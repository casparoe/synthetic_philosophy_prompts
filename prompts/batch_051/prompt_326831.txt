Then you have the US, where we still put people in solitary for years, hand out mandatory minimums, and fall back on the whole debt-to-society way of talking. What view of the point of punishment does each of these practices actually presuppose? What would American practice have to change beyond making the buildings nicer if it really adopted it's implicit theory?
