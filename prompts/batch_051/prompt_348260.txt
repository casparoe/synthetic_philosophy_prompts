At the end, the panel votes in secret. If at least two-thirds vote to strike the measure, it's off the ballot, period — no appeal, no rewrite, and the sponsors can't qualify substantially the same text for two cycles. Anything in between, the measure goes on the ballot with no note. Nothing else changes: same paid-signature qualification rules, same contribution limits, same TV ads.
