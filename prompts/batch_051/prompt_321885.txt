(d) The galaxy has finite resources, and any civilisation that survives long must expand, so every other civilisation is ultimately a competitor for the same stuff.
(e) The rational policy for any civilisation is therefore to emit nothing and to pre-emptively destroy anything it detects.
(f) In the story, one civilisation decides to reveal itself to another as a deliberate deterrence move: it announces its location and stakes its safety on a provable second-strike capability, because attacking it would guarantee retaliation and therefore can't be rational.
