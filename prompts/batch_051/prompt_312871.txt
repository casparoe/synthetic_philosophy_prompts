ok so I've been going back and forth about this thing and I need a word for it. It started with college admissions. If I ask what my actual chance of getting into a certain school is, there's like a million answers because I can pick the overall acceptance rate, or the rate for people from my state, or people with my GPA, or people with my GPA and my exact intended major and the same extracurriculars. Each one gives a different number and each one feels like its answering the question, but they can't all be *my* odds. Then my mom had a health thing and the doctor gave her a risk percentage, but that number had to come from studying some group of people, and there are a ton of groups she fits in (women her age, women with her exact test results, women with her lifestyle, women with all three...) and the risk changes depending on the group. So the universe doesn't seem to tell you which of the many "people like you" buckets is the right one. Does this problem have a name, and is there an actual rule for picking the bucket or is it just a judgment call? Don't just give me the label though—I want to know how the problem is usually carved up, what it's not (like, is this just about people being bad at statistics?), and whether the people who study it think there's a fact of the matter about which bucket is correct, or if the choosing itself is the inescapable part.
