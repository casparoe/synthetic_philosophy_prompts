Could you restate this whole debate in the vocabulary I actually work in — negative reward, prediction error, precision weighting, salience, sensory estimation vs. valuation — as faithfully as you can? Then give me the residue report: which positions translate cleanly, which ones have to be fudged, and whether anything in the badness-of-pain debate has no counterpart in my vocabulary. What I most want to know is whether asymbolia comes out as a clean lesion to one computational component, and if it does, whether that actually settles the philosophical question or just means the RL/predictive-coding vocabulary was smuggling in an answer from the start.
