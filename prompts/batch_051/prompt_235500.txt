I keep going back and forth on what it even means for an intellectual field to make progress, as opposed to just changing. Give me a working definition of progress in a field that does this: it clearly counts pure mathematics between, say, 1800 and 2000, and clinical medicine since about 1950; it clearly doesn't count astrology or fashion; and it forces a definite verdict on string theory, academic history, macroeconomics, and analytic philosophy. I half-suspect the distinction has something to do with whether a field has mechanisms that could show its practitioners they're wrong—that would explain math and medicine—but I can't build that into a defintion without either letting in astrology (which does make wrong predictions all the time) or accidentally making chess engine opening theory count as a field. Run your definition through all the cases, say which verdict is least secure, and tell me where it surprised you.
