I'm a PhD student writing on Nietzsche's second Untimely Meditation, and I keep getting stuck on how the three modes of history are meant to fit together. If the critical mode is the one in which a person or a people hauls the past before a court, condemns it, and thereby creates room to live differently, doesn't it render the monumental and antiquarian modes obsolete once it is available? Why would anyone still need to draw inspiration from past greatness, or reverently preserve what has been handed down, once they are capable of the critical relation to history—except as a stopgap for those not yet able to bear criticism?
