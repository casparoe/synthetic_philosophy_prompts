Their so-called symptoms are really just problems in living — social conflicts, moral struggles, and practical difficulties. It follows, on his view, that people diagnosed with a mental illness are not actually sick and therefore do not need treatment, only help with their lives; the medical project of psychiatry is thereby discredited."

Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence, then give your reasoning: which parts of the summary accurately capture Szasz's position, which parts overstate or subtly distort it, what Szasz would most likely object to if he read it, and whether the conclusions drawn actually follow from the accurate parts.
