I sit on the trust-and-safety advisory board of a large video platform. Our policy requires removing content that promotes terrorism, but the term is never defined and the moderation team keeps punting close cases to us. The one in front of us: a non-state armed group with an explicit political program posted a statement claiming responsibility for a bombing that killed four soldiers at a checkpoint and no civilians. They say they deliberately target only what they call occupation forces, and the goal is to force a negotiated withdrawal. Half the board says that's not terrorism, it's insurgency or war, and calling it terrorism just launders a political position into a content policy. The other half says the civilian/combatant line is a convienent fiction: the group means to terrify a population into political change and isn't choosing targets that cleanly. I need to circulate a short decision memo before next Tuesday's meeting: it should recommend whether to take the content down under the terrorism policy or leave it up, state the definition we should be using going forward, and engage the strongest point on the side you recommend against. Also tell me how confident you are in the recommendation, and single out the thing you're least sure about.
