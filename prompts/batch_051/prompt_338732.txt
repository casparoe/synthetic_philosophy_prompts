My friend and I have been arguing about made-up towns for a civics assignment. By a town like Ashbury (12,000, 9.0), I mean a town with 12,000 residents and an average life satisfaction of 9.0 on a 0-to-10 scale, so its total satisfaction is the number of residents times the average.

Her verdicts so far are:

- Ashbury (12,000, 9.0) is a better place to live than Bolton (12,000, 7.0).
- Denton (15,000, 8.0) is better than Calder (10,000, 8.0).
- Fairview (12,000, 10.0) is better than Ellery (15,000, 8.0).
- Grundy (9,000, 7.0) is better than Holden (7,000, 7.0).

Next week she has to compare Jamestown (4,000, 9.0) with Kingsport (6,000, 7.0), and I want to predict what she will say.

The rules I suspect are something like: rank by average satisfaction first and use total satisfaction only to break ties, or rank by total satisfaction first and use average satisfaction only to break ties. There may be other rules that fit her record as well. Are both of those consistent with every verdict she has given so far? What does each rule predict for Jamestown against Kingsport? Which rule is she most likely actually running, and how strongly does her track record support it? Finally, what further comparison between two towns would most cleanly settle which rule she is really using?
