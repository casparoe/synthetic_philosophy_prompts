We agree on the cases and on what counts as a considered judgment, and nobody's moved in months. My hunch is that the real disagreement isn't about which theory is true but about what normative theory is for—whether it's in the business of explaining the data or merely systematizing it—though it might also just be a verbal dispute about "simplicity." I'd like you to diagnose what kind of disagreement this actually is: verbal, substantive, about the aims of theorizing, about the epistemic status of intuitions, or something else.
