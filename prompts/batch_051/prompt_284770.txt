I am a retired experimental physicist who has recently begun reading philosophy of mind, and I have come upon a conceptual fork that I can state clearly but do not know how to name—and do not know whether it is, in the end, a genuine fork at all. The clearest instance is the color-phi display: two small disks are flashed in quick succession at nearby locations, the first red and the second green, and subjects report not two stationary flashes but a single disk that glides from the first position to the second and changes from red to green midway along its path. The oddity is in the timing: the green at the midpoint is supplied by the second flash, yet that midpoint falls earlier in the perceived sequence than the second flash itself, so the visual system seems to be waiting for later information and then retroactively constructing an earlier stretch of experience. A second, related case is backward masking, where a briefly flashed target followed almost immediately by a mask is reported as simply not having been seen; here too one can ask whether a brief conscious glimpse of the target was formed and then erased, or was never formed in the first place.

As I understand it, two architectures could produce these reports with no introspective difference. In one, the revision is completed before the content ever becomes conscious: the moving, color-changing disk is literally what the subject experiences, because the system assembles the best narrative it can and only then presents it to awareness. In the other, the subject first consciously experiences the veridical sequence—two discrete flashes, or the unmasked target—but that veridical experience is almost immediately overwritten in memory, before it can be reported, by the edited narrative; the subject consequently reports, and remembers, only the corrected version. The two hypotheses yield the same report, the same confidence, and the same behavior. I am not asking merely for a label, though I would welcome one if it exists. I want to know the established name or names for these two architectures, whether the distinction between editing before awareness and editing after awareness but before report marks a real empirical difference or a difference only in how one carves up an otherwise identical causal chain, what would in principle distinguish the two, and what the neighboring concepts are—subjective antedating, backward masking, memory insertion, the separation between seeing and reporting—that fix the boundaries of the phenomenon. If the two are ultimately two descriptions of one underlying fact, I want to know precisely where the appearance of a difference comes from.
