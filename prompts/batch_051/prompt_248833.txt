I'm a novelist, and I've got a world-building problem I want to think through for a book. In Hume's Natural History of Religion there's that passage where he says the first religion of mankind rises mostly from uneasy dread of what may happen, and from there we project hidden powers that look and act sort of like us. I'm toying with a version of early humanity where that particular dread-response never develops—not that people are generally fearless, but they don't convert uncertainty about the future into a feeling that unseen persons or agents are behind it. Predict what happens. I want the chain of reasoning made explicit: does anything like religion still appear within a few thousand years, and if it does, what forms does it take? Which parts of your prediction depend on Hume's claim being correct, and which hold even if he's wrong about the fear-to-agency pathway? Tell me which links in the chain you're most and least confident about, and what would have to be different for the prediction to fail.
