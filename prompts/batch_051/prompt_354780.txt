My older sister, who is taking her first philosophy of mind course, has been trying cases out on me, and I have kept track of her rulings about which states count as emotions. So far these are the cases she has ruled on.

An analyst reads a set of financial projections, concludes that the company is on the verge of collapse, and writes this down with complete composure, reporting no bodily changes and no particular urge to do anything. My sister said this was not an emotion but a dispassionate judgment.

A classmate hears a door slam behind him, jumps, gasps, and feels a wave of adrenaline before he has registered the sound as a door slamming. My sister said this was not an emotion but a startle reflex, because nothing had been evaluated.

A neighbor with a spider phobia knows that the small spider in the bathtub is harmless and will say so, but when she sees it her heart pounds, her palms sweat, and she cannot bring herself to reach past it. My sister called this an emotion immediately, saying the fear presents the spider as dangerous even though the neighbor's explicit judgment says it is not.

A cousin has been anxious for weeks and cannot say what he is anxious about; he reports a constant tightness in his chest and a vague sense that something is wrong. My sister said this counts as an emotion, and that the lack of a definite object does not disqualify it.

The new case is this. A hiker stands on a glass-floored skywalk above a canyon. The height presents itself to her as dangerous in an immediate, visual way — it is not something she has worked out from the engineering, and she does not believe she is actually in danger — but she feels no tightness in her chest, no quickened pulse, and no urge to step back; so far as she can tell, she is entirely calm in her body.

What would my sister say about the hiker: is the experience an emotion or not? More importantly, which theory of emotion is she implicitly running — emotions as judgments, emotions as perceptions, emotions as feelings, or some hybrid — which theories consistent with her earlier verdicts does the new case separate, and what further case would settle which one she actually holds?
