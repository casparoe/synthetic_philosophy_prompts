You're an expert on the logic of rule systems and on how the wording of a governing document gets interpreted; think carefully and don't jump to a verdict. Start by brainstorming the different ways Article 12 below could be read, then write the opinion. This is the same kind of puzzle as the game Nomic, but you shouldn't need to know Nomic to answer it — everything you need is in the charter below.

I'm on the board of a small housing co-op governed by a written charter. Article 12 says:

"Article 12. Any member may propose an amendment to this Charter at a general meeting. A proposed amendment is adopted if two-thirds of the members present vote in favour, and it comes into force as soon as the chair declares the result."

At last month's meeting a member proposed an amendment to Article 12 itself. The amendment would add this sentence to Article 12:

"An amendment that changes the rules for amending this Charter, including this amendment itself, requires a three-quarters majority of the members present to be adopted."

The ballot came back at 70 percent in favour — above the current two-thirds bar, below the three-quarters bar the proposal says should apply to itself. The chair still hasnt declared the result, because half the co-op says the proposal obviously passed (the old rule was in force when the votes were cast) and the other half says it obviously failed (appyling its own new threshold to its own adoption means 70 percent isn't enough). The drafters of Article 12 plainly never considered a proposal that tries to govern its own adoption.

Write a short opinion for the co-op's next meeting. In it, work through what (a) the literal wording of Article 12, (b) the purpose of having an amendment rule at all, (c) what the drafters would most likely have said if asked, and (d) what a reasonable member reading the charter would expect each imply about whether this proposal was adopted. Then say which of these approaches should control here, and give the bottom line: is the amendment in force, and what voting rule governs the next attempt to amend Article 12?
