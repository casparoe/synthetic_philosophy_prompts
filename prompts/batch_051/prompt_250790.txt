Say a group of AI labs set up a pre-AGI bargain along these lines. Any member that trains a model past an agreed capability threshold has to deposit the weights, scaffolding, and training logs in a neutral escrow; the escrow only opens when the treaty body certifies that one member has actually built AGI. At that point the winner gets an exclusive license to sell AGI for, say, ten years, every other member's deposits are pooled for cross-licensing, and the winner pays the others a royalty proportional to the paramter-counts they escrowed. Treat it as a mechanism, not a moral argument: who joins and who stays out, how is it gamed through the threshold, the certification process, or the escrow itself, what does it incentivize about training, publishing, and hiding runs, and where does it fall apart?
