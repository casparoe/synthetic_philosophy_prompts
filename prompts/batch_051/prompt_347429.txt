Jean-Paul Sartre writes in *Existentialism Is a Humanism*:

“For in truth this is of all teachings the least scandalous and the most austere: it is intended strictly for technicians and philosophers.”

Write an essay on whether Sartre’s central claim that existence precedes essence can survive the charge of incoherence. The essay should weigh the strongest forms of that charge—including at least the claim that the thesis is self-contradictory and the claim that defending it forces one to presuppose a universal human nature or a normative standard—against what Sartre can say in reply. Reach and justify a concrete verdict; do not end with a non-committal summary. Throughout, separate what is settled about the position from what remains genuinely contested among informed readers, and refrain from presenting a contested reading as though it were the consensus.
