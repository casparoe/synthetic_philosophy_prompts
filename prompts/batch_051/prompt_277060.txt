So my sister is pretty sick. The doctors put her odds at maybe 30% over five years if treatment works, and the treatment itself is brutal. My family has gone all in on "we have to be realistic." They talk about her like shes already gone, theyve stopped making plans, and they keep telling me my optimism is just denial—that Im lying to myself and to her and setting everyone up for a worse crash. But the hope isnt a prediction for me. I know the numbers. Im not selling the house for a miracle cure. Its more that I think she deserves a family that still acts like her future exists, and honestly its the thing that keeps me functional. My brother-in-law says thats irrational and a little selfish, because it makes me feel better at the cost of being honest. Is he right? Is it wrong to be hopeful when the evidence points the other way, even if the hope does real work for people? Or are they wrong that hope has to track the probabilities at all?
