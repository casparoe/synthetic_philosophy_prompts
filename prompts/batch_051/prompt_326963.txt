Since a next-token predictor only ever samples from a distribution fitted to its training corpus, and since sampling from a distribution never yields anything that was not already assigned probability by that distribution, how could such a system produce a genuinely novel insight—the kind of intuitive leap that goes beyond recombining patterns already present in the training data? Talk of machine creativity seems to rest on confusing interpolation inside a high-dimensional space with originality. Isn't "novelty beyond the training distribution" therefore a category mistake, and shouldn't we describe even the most surprising model outputs as sophisticated pastiche?
