For a seminar I am giving this term on the metaphysics of personal persistence, I have been asked to present my own considered view on whether a self can be distributed across brains. I have never written it out as a single package, and I would like a consistency audit before I present. Here is where I have landed.

(1) I am a physicalist. Persons are wholly constituted by the physical organization of their brains and bodies; there is no immaterial self or Cartesian ego.

(2) I find Hofstadter's strange-loop account broadly right: what I call my self is an abstract, self-referential pattern realized in the causal structure of a brain. It is the pattern, not the particular matter realizing it, that fixes what I am.

(3) Because patterns are substrate-neutral and can be realized more or less fully, I accept that a self can be partially realized outside its original brain. When one person knows another deeply, the knower carries a low-resolution but genuine fragment of the known person's self-pattern. I would resist calling this mere metaphor.

(4) I think exact pattern preservation is sufficient for survival. If a scanner recorded the complete state of my brain and body, destroyed the original, and constructed an exact duplicate elsewhere, the person stepping out of the receiver would be me; the discontinuity of underlying matter would not by itself end my existence.

(5) I also hold, on independent grounds, that what matters in survival is psychological continuity: overlapping chains of memory, intention, disposition, and self-conception, rather than the persistence of any particular body.

(6) Nevertheless, I do not believe I would survive my death in the minds of my students or friends. The fragment of me that continues in them is too partial, too low-resolution, and too thoroughly reshaped by the host's point of view to count as my continuing to exist. Talk of living on in others is consolation dressed up as metaphysics.

(7) Finally, I think identity is all-or-nothing: a future person either is me or is not; there is no such thing as being partly me.

Please audit this package. Which pairs of commitments are in genuine tension, how severe is each tension, and which pairs merely appear to conflict? Rank the genuine tensions by severity. For each genuine tension, say precisely what has to give and what the cheapest revision is. Then tell me whether there is one revision—to a single commitment or to how I formulate the package—that would remove the most strain at the least cost.
