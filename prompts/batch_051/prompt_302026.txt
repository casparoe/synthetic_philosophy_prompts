I want you to audit this analogy as an argument, not just as a metaphor. first give me the full mapping table: hardware, software, memory, processor, inputs/outputs, error handling, whatever else the picture commits him to. then walk through where it breaks — not every difference you can think of, but the ones that matter — and point out what the computer picture quietly imports into the brain, like a program/data split or copyability, and whether those imports are the load-bearing ones. seperate the disanalogies that matter for his conclusion from the ones that are just brains-are-wet-and-computers-are-dry trivia. then tell me whether his conclusion (substrate independence, and that the upload is literally the same person) survives once the disanalogies are on the table, or whether the computer picture was carrying the whole thing. and if there's a better analogy out there — one that doesn't quietly import the hardware/software split — say what it is and what it would do to his argument.
