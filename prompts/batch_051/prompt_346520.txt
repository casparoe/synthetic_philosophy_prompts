The thing I keep tripping over is that some proposals arrive with honest-to-god frequency data behind them — thirty years of storm-surge records, long yield histories — and some are just unique. My colleagues want me to slap a probability on everything and do expected-value arithmetic, and it feels like we're making numbers up and then forgetting we made them up. I have a vague memory of Keynes distinguishing risk from uncertainty, and something about the weight of evidence — that a probability with a thin evidential basis is not the same as the same number with a thick one — and something about people falling back on convention when they don't actually know. But I've never seen any of that turned into something I could actually run.
