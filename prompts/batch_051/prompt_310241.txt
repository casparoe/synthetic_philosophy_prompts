I've been going back and forth on this for a while and I figured a outside take couldn't hurt. I'm 24 and I keep seeing ads for egg donation near me in the US. They're offering around nine thousand dollars for a cycle, which would basically erase my credit card debt and let me move out of my parents place. I was honestly considering it. But then I told my cousin in Berlin and she reacted like I said I'd sell a kidney. She says where she lives you can't get paid for eggs at all—she's not sure if the donation itself is banned or just paying for it, but she thinks that's how it should be. Her point is that putting a price tag on that sort of thing mostly ropes in broke young women who wouldn't do it otherwise.

My best friend here thinks my cousin is being preachy. She says the clinic screens donors, the extraction isn't all that dangerous, and some couple ends up with a kid they couldn't have otherwise. She says I'm an adult and I can consent to my own choices, and if I'm allowed to take a night warehouse shift for money then she doesn't see why this is any different. She thinks people only get weird about it because it involves eggs and women's bodies.

The part I keep circling around is that the clinic always uses the word donation, like it's this selfless gift, but the money is the first thing on the flyer. And I've noticed most of the donors I read about are students or people trying to get out of debt—which is me too, I guess. It almost seems like the gift language is there to make the paycheck part feel less grimy. But then I flip back and think, people do all sorts of unpleasant things for money and nobody acts like that's degrading. Is there an actual difference here, or is it just old taboos? If nobody's lying to me and I'm not desperate in some extreme way, is there anything left to worry about, or is my cousin's grossed-out reaction just a cultural thing? I keep going in circles.
