Im putting together a short intro to behavior genetics and want to close with a poem that does real conceptual work, not just vibes. Could you write a sonnet about heritability where the octave builds the tempting misreading — runs in families, "it's genetic," so the trait is fixed — and the volta is the correction: h² is the share of phenotypic variance in a particular population and environment, not a property of the trait, and not a verdict about any individual. The sestet should work in the two consequences people always find surprising: the estimate can shift when the environment changes (so high heritability doesnt mean unchangeable), and a within-group estimate cant be used to infer between-group differences. Let the couplet land on exactly what has to go — the conflation of variance with destiny. I dont want it to just name-drop additive variance; I want someone who does quantitative genetics to recognize the view as right, and someone coming in cold to come away with the correction.
