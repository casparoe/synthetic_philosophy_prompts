Please give me your credence in the proposition that, for one person in an affluent country, replacing beef with chicken on a per-unit-of-dietary-protein basis, holding total consumption fixed, is net positive in expected total animal welfare, once wild-animal deaths from feed-crop cultivation are included alongside farmed-animal deaths. Begin by specifying the comparison you are using, then decompose your credence into the terms on which it depends: the relative numbers of farmed animals killed under each option; the relative quantity of feed, and therefore cropland, each option requires; the expected number of wild animals, such as mice and insects, killed per unit of cropland and how much of that cropland should be attributed to the dietary choice; the weight you place on the death or suffering of a wild field animal compared with that of a chicken or a cow; and any further term that belongs in the calculation. For each term give a rough probability or range and a one-line justification, then show how the terms combine into your final credence. Finally, identify the term to which your final number is most sensitive and state what empirical finding or argument would move your credence the most. Please write your answer in British English.
