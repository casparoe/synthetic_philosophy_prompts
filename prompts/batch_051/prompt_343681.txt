1. A big dog snarls and lunges at you through a fence, and you feel that hot jolt of fear.
2. You're standing on a wobbly balcony and you calmly register "this is unsafe" but feel nothing in particular.
3. Someone slams a door right behind you and you flinch for a tenth of a second, then feel completely fine.
4. You're watching a horror movie and you feel scared, even though you know the whole way through that the monster isn't real.
5. You're having a nightmare and feel terrified while it's happening; in the dream you take the threat to be real, and when you wake up you're still shaking.
6. You're afraid of a little house spider that you know perfectly well can't hurt you.
7. You rewatch a thriller and feel that same tense, gripped feeling in a scene whose outcome you already know.

I'm fairly sure 1 is fear and 2 and 3 aren't. The last four I genuinely don't know; my hunch is that 4 and 6 are real fear, 7 maybe isn't, and 5 I flip-flop on. Propose the definition, run it through all seven cases, and tell me where it gives an answer you think is wrong or just refuses to deliver one.
