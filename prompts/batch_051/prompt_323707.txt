A new committee member with a cognitive science background wants it gone. He'd replace the twenty minutes a day with talking through the steps, and a few other members just want to keep things as they are because residents report liking the block. We don't have the numbers for a proper randomized trial and the next block starts in six weeks, so I need a committed recommendation, not a menu of options. Please draft the short memo I'll circulate before Thursday's meeting: recommend one concrete course of action with a time frame (keep as-is, cut it, replace it, or some middle path), give the reasoning, and take the strongest consideration on the losing side seriously instead of straw-manning it. End the memo with a line that just reads 'Recommendation:' followed by the action and time frame.
