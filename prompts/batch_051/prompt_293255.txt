I want to set a question around Bongard problems — you know, six little boxes on the left and six on the right, and you have to infer the rule that puts each one where it is — since they force students to actually do concept induction from a few examples rather than just recite the prototype/exemplar/rule distinctions.

Can you draft me one exam question (100 marks, about a 90-minute answer) plus a model answer and a marking scheme. I want it pitched so somebody whose memorized the prototype/exemplar/rules trichotomy can't coast. Then give me a marking scheme with five or six must-hit points, marks allocated to add to 100, and a one-line note for each on how students typically miss it (e.g., conflating "everyone answers this" with "the examples pick that out uniquely").
