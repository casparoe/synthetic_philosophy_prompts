I am a PhD student in philosophy of science working on reduction and intertheoretic relations, and I keep running into "emergent" being used in importantly different ways: in one paper a property is emergent only if it cannot be derived from a complete microphysical description, in another if it is merely novel or surprising relative to the parts, and in yet another if it has causal powers of its own. I need a working definition for my own use, not exegesis, and I want to test it against a list of cases before I deploy it.

Here is the case list, sorted by my current confidence.

I am confident the following are emergent:
- The liquidity of a glass of water at room temperature.
- The hexagonal convection cells that form in a thin layer of oil heated uniformly from below.
- A stock market crash, understood as the macro-level event that then feeds back into individual traders' behavior.

I am confident the following are not emergent:
- The total electric charge of a salt crystal.
- The center of mass of a rock.

I cannot settle the following, and I would like your definition to give a definite verdict for each:
- The pressure of an ideal gas, given that the kinetic theory recovers it exactly as a statistical average.
- A glider in Conway's Game of Life: a small pattern that persists and moves across the grid.
- The zero electrical resistance of a superconducting lead sample below its transition temperature.
- A phantom traffic jam: a stop-and-go wave that continues after the obstruction that triggered it has cleared.
- A blocky compression artifact in a heavily compressed JPEG image.

Propose a working definition of "emergent property" — I mean this broadly enough to cover macro-level features and processes — that gets my confident cases right and yields a definite verdict for each borderline case. Then run the definition through the entire list. Where it gives a verdict you think I would resist, either revise the definition or defend the surprising verdict. Finally, I would be helped by a brief statement of whether the concept you have defined is binary or graded, and which element of the definition is doing most of the work in the borderline cases.
