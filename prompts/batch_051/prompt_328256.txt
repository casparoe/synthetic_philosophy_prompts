My sister and I run a small community arts space together. She wants us to book an event for a group she's in, and I honestly think what that group does is wrong — not illegal, just wrong. She knows I think this. Her offer is: we host her group this month, and next month we host the group I volunteer with, which she thinks is wrong. She's not asking me to change my mind about her group, and she's not changing hers about mine. She's proposing that we each help the other do something we think is wrong, so we both get the thing we care about. I cant tell whether that's a fair compromise or something I shouldn't do. If it were just about pizza or which movie to see, I'd trade in a second. But these are moral convictions, and something in me says you shouldn't be able to swap a moral judgment for a favor. Yet when I try to explain that, it sounds like I'm just being stubborn, and refusing leaves us both worse off. Is the deal okay? What's the strongest case on each side, and is there a principled difference between this and an ordinary compromise?
