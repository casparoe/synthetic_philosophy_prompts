i'm a journalist who covers science and ideas, and i'm working on a longread about modal realism — david lewis's view that every possible world exists just as concretely as this one, and that the word actual works like here or now, an indexical that picks out whichever world you happen to be in. so every world is actual to itself, and the only reason this world feels special is that it's the one we're in. the piece is mostly about the vertigo of that: the difference between what happened and what could have happened is a matter of where your standing, not what's real.
