Give me a working definition of membership in the demos, understood as the class of people who are entitled to vote in a country's national elections, that handles the following cases. A citizen who has lived abroad for twenty years; a non-citizen who has lived, worked, and paid taxes in the country for fifteen years; a seventeen-year-old who has worked and paid taxes for two years; a citizen currently serving a prison sentence; a citizen of a neighboring state whose air and water are affected by this country's energy policies; and a person who will be born only after all of today's voters are dead. I take the first case to be inside the demos and the last two to be outside it, however weighty their interests are; I have no settled view about the middle three. Propose the definition, run it through all six cases, and tell me where it surprises you or forces a revision. Bonus if it explains why the concept feels graded rather than binary. Finally, identify which of your verdicts is least secure, and say what consideration or example would lead you to amend the definition.
