I've come around to eliminative materialism about beliefs and desires — the version that says beliefs and desires, as folk psychology understands them, won't be the natural kinds of a mature cognitive neuroscience, and that talk of them is more like talk of phlogiston than talk of genes. Its not that I think the standard arguments are bad; my route in was mostly the predictive-processing stuff, plus spending too much time with RL agents that do impressive things all day with no stored propositional attitudes anywhere — not so much the old theory-theory line. So I want to stress-test the specific version I hold, not the straw version.
