I'm on our city's charter revision commission, and the proposal on the table is to give nonresident workers who've paid the city wage tax for at least two years a vote in municipal elections. The pitch is that these commuters have as much at stake in city policy as residents do — the wage tax itself, zoning, transit, public safety around where they work. Half the commission says no, the local demos just is the residents, end of story, and I need something to circulate before Thursday's meeting. Draft a short memo recommending a concrete decision, with reasons a skeptic could actually respond to, and make sure it takes the strongest version of the other side seriously rather than knocking down a strawman.
