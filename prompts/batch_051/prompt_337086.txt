I'm putting together a small group on how churches decide what's allowed in corporate worship, and I want a dialogue they can react to. Write one between an elder who holds the regulative principle — whatever is not commanded in worship is forbidden — and a pastor who holds the normative principle, that whatever isn't forbidden is allowed. Dont let them just trade proof texts; push them into the hard part: how each one figures out whether a practice is an element of worship, where the regulative rule is supposed to bite, or just a circumstance like what time the service starts. Keep it concrete by having them argue over a congregation that adds a recited responsive reading on Christmas morning, and make sure they respond to each other's actual points instead of preaching past each other. Neither should be a strawman or obviously win.
