Write a deadpan government request for proposals, about 700 words, from a fictional US state department of corrections that wants bids to retrofit two old max facilities with "Halden-inspired design elements": wood-laminate dayrooms, unbarred windows with fjord murals, staff fleeces in a warm palette, a community kitchen where the knives are painted on the wall behind glass. The RFP should rule out any change to classification, sentence length, stafing ratios, visitation, or solitary — the look must change, the regime wont — and should include a statement of work, evaluation criteria that reward the phrase "evidence-based" without ever defining it, and an indemnification clause for any unanticipated rise in recidivism. It should imply you know the real Halden stuff (private rooms, staff unarmed and eating with residents, a flat hierarchy) so the refusal to import any of it reads as the point. The satire should land, without ever stating, the objection that what makes Halden work is the regime and the society around it, not the furniture, and that importing the look while keeping the machinery intact is itself a form of punishment. Stay deadpan to the end: no winking, no last paragraph that spells out the irony. The jokes have to come from the corrections content, not generic bureaucracy jokes; a typo or two is fine.
