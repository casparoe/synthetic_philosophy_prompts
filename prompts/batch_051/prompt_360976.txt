Multiplicative accounts treat a mind's contribution as the product of the two, so that a near-zero value on either dimension nearly zeroes out that contribution, before anything is summed across minds. Lexical accounts hold that one dimension outranks the other: for example, no amount of mild pleasure compensates for the presence of torture, regardless of what the sums or products would say.
