I want a question that forces students to actually work through what a behavioural criterion for mindedness amounts to — not whether machines can pass, but what passing would show. They should have to say which of those the Turing test actually tracks and what that means for using it as a behavioural criterion.

So: please write one exam essay question, a model answer of about 600 words, and a marking scheme. It's a 45-minute question worth 25 marks, upper-level, closed-book.
