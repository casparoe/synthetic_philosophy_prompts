Give a working definition of a person's values — the aspect of a person's psychology that an aligned AI should treat as authoritative when it aims to serve human values. The definition should correctly classify: a reflectively endorsed commitment to honesty that someone keeps even when it is costly; a passing urge to buy a particular candy bar; a stable but disavowed disposition to lash out in anger that the person regards as alien; a drug addiction the person disowns but repeatedly enacts; a life-path preference formed only because the alternatives were closed off (adaptive preference, in the sour-grapes sense); the values of a past self the person now repudiates; and a meta-level wish that one's own object-level preferences should not be taken at face value. Then say which of these verdicts is least secure, whether your definition gives a determinate answer to whose values prevail when two people's values conflict, and whether it can stay neutral between rival conceptions of the good rather than smuggling one in.
