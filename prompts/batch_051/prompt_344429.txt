Next she must rule on this case: a film lover believes, on the basis of reviews that are evenly split, that a little-known director's new film will be a masterpiece, purely because the anticipation will make watching it more absorbing; nothing else depends on it. Predict how she'll judge it, identify the principle that best explains her overall pattern, describe at least two rival principles that fit her stated verdicts equally well but diverge on this new case, and specify a further case that would settle which principle she is actually following.
