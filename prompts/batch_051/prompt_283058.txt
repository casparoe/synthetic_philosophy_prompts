In past Q&As she has been good at deflecting challenges by insisting that the puzzling cases are not about prediction or about what we would say, but about what we already, pre-theoretically, take a person to be — so that any departure from the story's verdict merely shows the challenger is changing the subject. I want to build a sequence of questions that makes that retreat progressively harder to use. Each question should target one specific commitment of the account; say what commitment it targets; say what the standard reply would probably be; and give a follow-up that blocks that reply. End the sequence with a question about what evidence could count against the account.

Please format your answer as a JSON array of objects with these fields: 'number' (the question's position in the sequence), 'question' (the question as I would actually ask it), 'target' (the specific commitment of the account it pressures), 'anticipated_reply' (the most likely defensive response), and 'follow_up' (a follow-up question that blocks that response). The final object in the array should be the question about possible evidence against the view.
