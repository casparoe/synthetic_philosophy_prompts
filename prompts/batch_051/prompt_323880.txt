i've been trying to pin down what I think about "tranquilism" — roughly, that the only thing that matters in itself is the absence of craving and suffering — and I ended up with this list. could you stress-test whether the set hangs together? here are the things I seem to believe:

1. Only the absence of craving and suffering matters in itself. Pleasure, achievement, knowledge, love — whatever value those have is just as means for reducing craving or suffering.

2. I'd take a pill that permanently removed all craving and all felt suffering, even if it made me ambitionless, flattened my personality, and left me sitting quietly doing nothing all day. I don't think that life would be missing anything important.

3. But I also think boredom is a form of suffering and restlessness is a kind of craving. So when I say the pill-life is fine, I mean only if the pill also gets rid of boredom and restlessness; a chemically calm but still bored and fidgety person is not an example of what I'm after.

4. I don't think the fact that my current self likes wanting things and is scared of becoming blank should count against taking the pill. That liking and that fear are themselves cravings (or symptoms of cravings), of the sort the view says not to trust.

5. I'd still rather find out my partner was cheating on me and be wrecked for a few months than keep living in the warm, calm, false version of the relationship. And I don't think that's just because the truth would eventually leave me calmer; the preference stands even if knowing never helps with tranquility.

6. I also think a perfectly calm life that's only possible because one other person is silently bearing a huge cost is worse than an equally calm life with nobody paying that price, even if I never find out and it never disturbs my calm. I'm not sure whether to call that a fact about my life or a fact about the world, but it feels true.

7. I think finishing something genuinely difficult matters hugely. At the same time I'd trade every achievement I have or ever will have for the zero-craving, empty life from 2, and I don't think I'd be losing anything by doing so.

Go through these and tell me which pairs are in tension and which only look that way. Order the tensions by severity, say what the cheapest fix for each would be, and then tell me which single revision would clean up the most. And when you answer, say how confident you are in each diagnosis and single out the point you're least sure of.
