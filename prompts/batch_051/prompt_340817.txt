The chance of getting a life-permitting universe by accident is astronomically small—something like one in 10^60—so we should conclude that the universe was deliberately fine-tuned by an intelligent designer. The only serious naturalistic alternative is the multiverse: if there are a vast number of universes with different constants, it is no surprise that at least one permits life, just as it is no surprise that someone somewhere wins the lottery. But the multiverse is just as speculative as God, and it does not even help, because the multiverse itself would have to be fine-tuned. The evidence therefore strongly favors design over every known alternative."
