I keep seeing people say the alignment problem is narrower than I thought: they define it as making AI do what the user intended, and they treat “value alignment” as this separate, much harder thing. But I don't see why. If I tell an AI to book me the cheapest flight to Chicago and it does exactly that, hasn't it followed my intent and also done what I value, since cheapness was clearly something I wanted? I can't think of a case where a system does what I intend but isnt value-aligned. What would that even look like—and why would the narrow definition still count it as a failure?
