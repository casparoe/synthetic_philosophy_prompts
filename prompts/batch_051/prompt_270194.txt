I want a reusable decision procedure we can actually put in a design review doc—not a philosophy essay—that tells us, for a given AI action or policy, whether to reason in a more deontological way or a more utilitarian way, and what to do when they conflict.

I get that he's talking about human moral worth, not AI, but I want your answer to actually engage with whether that passage rules out making expected effects the main thing our system optimizes, or whether that's a category mistake because an LLM doesn't have a motive in Kant's sense.
