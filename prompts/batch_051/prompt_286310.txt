From there, the victim or their family can pay a fixed per-day fee to add days, up to the maximum, and the defendant (or anyone on the defendant's behalf) can pay the same fee to subtract days, down to the minimum. How would each side game it—strategic victims, wealthy defendants, organized groups, even the county itself? What does it do to plea bargaining and to people who can't pay? And does the system end up delivering retribution, deterrence, both, or neither? Where does it fail first and can any of those failures be patched without scrapping the whole thing?
