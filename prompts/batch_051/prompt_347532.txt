Give me the correspondence table — what plays the role of the bike, the training, the first ride, the city, the map, the visit — then show me where the comparison breaks, and which features it quietly imports from skills and travel that seeing red doesn't actually have. I especially want to know whether the ability version and the acquaintance version are really the same analogy or two different ones with different failure points, and whether the physicalist conclusion survives once the disanalogies are on the table.
