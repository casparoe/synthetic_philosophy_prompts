There's a guy whose blog I read who insists that a surprising fraction of political fights are secretly about subsidiarity, and he has a running bit where he'll give a one-word "correct level" verdict on almost anything. From memory, his calls include: speed bumps, city; national crash-test standards, national; wether bars can stay open past midnight, county; a city fluoridating its own water, city; national power-plant emissions rules, national; county property taxes that fund the county's schools, county; the national organ transplant matching list, national; national electrical plug standards, national. Next time I see him I want to put this case to him: a city setting a higher minimum wage than the state around it, covering every hour worked inside city limits. Predict his call and say which rule of thumb he's actually running, and give me the one further case you'd ask about to break the tie if two rules are still live. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence, then give the reasoning; no preamble, no restating the question.
