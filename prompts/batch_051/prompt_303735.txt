Run the externalist analysis on this mixed case. What, if anything, does the Twin Earth term “water” refer to, and does the division of linguistic labor make the reference determinate or not? Finally, identify the background assumption of the original scenario that this variation exposes, and explain what the externalist should say as the H2O/XYZ ratio is varied continuously from 100/0 to 0/100.
