Most large online platforms attach a single number to each user and let that number do a surprising amount of work. On Stack Exchange, points earned mainly for good answers unlock moderation powers such as downvoting, voting to close, and editing other users' posts, and those same points can be spent on bounties to promote a question. On Reddit, karma earned in one subreddit—for a joke, a bird identification, or a legal analysis—is treated by the moderation filters of other subreddits as evidence that a user should be allowed to post there at all. On marketplaces and ride-share apps, one aggregate score is expected to track reliability, honesty, speed, and fairness simultaneously. The practice seems to presuppose an answer to a question that is rarely stated: what is reputation, such that all these evaluations can be compressed into a single quantity and carried from context to context? Reconstruct the implicit theory of reputation these systems appear to presuppose—for example, that different kinds of esteem are commensurable, that the object being measured is a stable trait of the user rather than a property of a particular role, audience, or task, and that aggregated votes track that trait rather than popularity, reciprocity, or artifacts of the scoring rule. Then separate what the practice strictly presupposes from what it merely finds convenient: is the same architecture compatible with a purely epistemic account of reputation (scores as uncertainty reduction), a purely incentive-based account (scores as reward tokens), and a desert-based account (scores as earned esteem)? Note any places where the commitments come apart—in particular, the pattern of treating the same number as both a certificate of competence and a spendable currency, so that spending it lowers the very score that was supposed to certify one. Finally, explain what would have to change in these systems if reputation were instead treated as multidimensional and relation-specific, so that being a trustworthy driver, a clear explainer, and a fair moderator were recognized as distinct goods for which no single scalar is meaningful.
