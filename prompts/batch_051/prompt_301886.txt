In the Introductory chapter he writes that "the last Reform Act admitted within what is called the pale of the Constitution so large a body of those who live on weekly wages," and he goes on to say that if those people act together as a class, they will have "a very great influence on legislation."

What would Mill have said about platform contractors who can withhold labor without any formal political rights? Should that count as the kind of class action he was predicting, or is it a different thing because there's no constitutional machinery?
