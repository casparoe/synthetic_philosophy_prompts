I keep noticing the same move in arguments about paying for things the body does or makes, and I want to know whether it has a name and whether it's a real distinction or just a legal workaround. A living kidney donor can get lost wages and travel reimbursed, but the same amount framed as payment for the kidney would be illegal. Blood plasma donors in the US get paid, though the clinics are careful to say they're paying for your time, not for the plasma. Egg donors can get thousands of dollars, but legally it's called compensation for time and trouble rather than a sale. I'm not just after a label—I want to know exactly how to carve the line between a price and compensation for time/discomfort/risk, where the edge cases are, and wheter the distinction can bear the moral weight people hang on it.
