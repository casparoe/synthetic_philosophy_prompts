Rina and Dev both used to love the Ethiopian beans from the roaster down the street and both stopped ordering them. Rina says the coffee now tastes exactly the same to her — she just doesn't like that taste anymore, because her standards moved. Dev says it's the other way for him: he still likes the taste he remembers, it's the coffee that no longer tastes that way to him. Rina said that was the whole mistake — the blueberry note and the acidity are still there, what changed is the verdict she gives them, not the taste itself. Dev came back that any change could be retold either way: whenever someone says the experience changed, you can equally say only their standards changed, so 'the way it tastes to me' doesn't explain anything, it just relables the thing to be explained. Rina said without that distinction we can't say why people change their minds about a stable product. Dev shrugged and said maybe needing to say which one changed is the confusion.
