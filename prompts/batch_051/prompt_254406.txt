I keep going back and forth on weather the film is indicting the makers of the bomb or quietly agreeing with Anscombe that the real crime was the decision to drop it. Read the movie through that lens for me: where does it dramatize her distinction between making something and choosing to use it, where does it resist that reading, and is the choice not to show Hiroshima a way of dodging the moral question or a way of making the audience feel the thing it's not showing?
