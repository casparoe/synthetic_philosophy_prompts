Work made this suddenly personal for me. I'm building a forecast that has to combine a dozen uncertain inputs — some expert-derived, some from noisy data, some just placeholder priors — and the move everyone on my team reaches for, including me, is to treat the inputs as independent and multiply. A colleague pointed out that if we regroup or reparametrize the same variables and then reapply the independence assumption, we can get a different joint distribution out, which sure seems like a problem for something that's supposed to be a neutral default. Ever since, I keep noticing how much the phrase "in the absence of evidence of dependence, presume independence" is quietly doing in heuristic, Fermi-style reasoning, and I realize I dont actually know what I'm assuming when I assume it.

I've read enough disconnected discussion of maximum entropy, the principle of insufficient reason, and modelers calling independence the null hypothesis to know I need the spec first. So: what would an adequate formalization of the presumption of independence have to get right? Give me the desiderata for a theory of this heuristic — the cases it has to handle (given marginals with no known joint, weak but nonzero dependence, events with logical or structural connections, reparameterization and regrouping, inputs whose estimated marginals are themselves shaky), the distinctions it can't collapse (an assumption to be checked later vs a default in the absence of information; independence as ignorance about dependence vs independence as a claim about the world; ignoring dependence vs modeling it as zero), and the constraints or results it can't contradict (for instance, that one event entailing another breaks independence no matter how ignorant you are, and that marginals don't pin down a joint but not just any joint is compatible with a given set of marginals). Say why each requirement is a requirement, and mark which are non-negotiable and which are really just preferences of particular camps. Then flag any pair of desiderata you suspect can't be jointly satisfied — that's the part I most need, because it would mean the list itself contains a result.

And don't hand me a menu: commit. Tell me which single requirement does the most work excluding the candidate formalizations, and where you'd actually put your money on the tradeoff.
