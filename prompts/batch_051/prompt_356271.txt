I've been chewing on something. My sister now believes her husband was replaced by an identical stranger—no fight, no trigger, she just woke up one morning and was sure. Her doctors call it a delusion and want it treated. Meanwhile my uncle has believed for decades that the moon landing was filmed on a soundstage, and everyone treats that as a harmless opinion. Both claims are false, both are held against a pile of evidence, both dig in when you push, and neither one acts on it in a way that hurts anybody. The only real difference I can see is that millions of people share the moon thing and almost nobody shares my sister's. Is that really what seperates a delusion from an opinion? If not, what does? And if it is, is that a line worth keeping?
