My half-formed idea is that the argument over whether "democratic backsliding" is a genuine analytic category or just a partisan label has stalled because the term is doing three jobs at once: it (a) reports a downward movement on some measure of democratic performance, (b) claims that the movement instantiates a distinctive causal process with identifiable mechanisms — rather than ordinary political competition the observer happens to dislike — and (c) presupposes a genuinely democratic baseline from which the country is falling.
