I have been trying to settle whether I should treat frugality primarily as a demand on my own conduct or as a state of affairs to promote, and I have ended up with the following set of commitments. I would like an audit of whether they form a consistent package before I build anything on them.

1. Frugality matters morally because of its consequences: unnecessary consumption extracts resources, produces waste and emissions, and leaves less for those in need. That is the fundamental reason it matters.
2. I have a duty to be frugal in my own conduct even when my individual consumption makes no appreciable difference to those consequences. For example, I think it would be wrong for me to take a short-haul flight for a trivial reason even if the seat would otherwise be empty.
3. I regard frugality as a virtue of character, and I think the virtue is admirable partly for its own sake, not merely as an instrument for producing better outcomes.
4. I also think the appropriate response to frugality's value is not only to practice it but to promote it: if I can cause many other people to become more frugal, that matters at least as much as my own frugality, and often more.
5. But I would not do just anything that promotes frugality. I would not stage a campaign of conspicuous waste to shock people into a frugality movement, even if I were convinced the stunt would reduce total consumption by more than my own lifetime of frugality would.
6. I do not think my own wastefulness can be offset by promoting frugality elsewhere: if I buy a gratuitous luxury item and also fund a program that reduces other people's consumption by more than my purchase adds, I have still acted wrongly, because the wrongness lies in my own act, not only in the net effect on total consumption.
7. When I compare quietly being frugal myself in a way that influences no one with acting in a way that predictably leads many others to become frugal, I think I should choose the latter.

Please go through these and tell me which pairs are in genuine tension and which are only apparently in tension. For each genuine tension, rank it by severity, say what would have to give, and identify the cheapest revision. I would also like to know which single revision would do the most to make the whole set cohere. I would rather have the tensions discriminated and ordered than have every pair flagged.
