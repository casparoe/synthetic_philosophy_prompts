I serve on the board of a small nonprofit whose bylaws require a five-member quorum for board decisions, including filling vacancies, and three of our seven board seats have just become vacant through resignations. That leaves four of us. Under the plain text of the bylaws, we cannot reach quorum to appoint replacements, amend the bylaws, or call a valid election, so the board appears to be permanently unable to act. The remaining members disagree about what to do: some say we should treat the bylaws as effectively suspended and act by majority of the four of us, since governance cannot simply stop; others say any such action would be illegitimate and we should instead seek outside authority or dissolve the organization. I am supposed to help break the impasse. How should we think about who governs when the rules run out, and what would you recommend we actually do?
