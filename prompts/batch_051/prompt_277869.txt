Write a satirical FAQ page for a small advisory-and-escrow firm that specializes in pre-AGI contracts: binding agreements between present humans and the superintelligent systems that don't exist yet. The firm's pitch is that right now, while we still have something like leverage, is exactly the moment to lock in a good deal on how a future AGI will treat us — and if you wait until it arrives, its too late. Keep the tone deadpan and corporate, the kind of reassuring legal-marketing voice that quietly refuses to admit it is selling you a promise nobody can enforce. The satire should come from the actual content: the future party isn't around to sign, an agent that can out-plan and out-bargain you can probably renegotiate or ignore the deal, the 'beneficiaries' include people who don't exist yet and never consented, and the firm's main product may just be turning money into notarized confidence. You can have the fictional firm name-drop ideas like precommitment, credible commitments, Ulysses-style self-binding, and acausal trade (the idea of cooperating with an agent you'll never meet by making your behavior depend on what you'd want it to do), but the FAQs should stay readable to someone who has never heard these terms. Roughly 700-800 words, formatted as questions and answers. American English, and don't break character at the end with a wink or a moral.
