A freind of mine has gone full eliminativist and now says beliefs and desires are like phlogiston — posits of a folk theory that gets us through the day but that a mature neuroscience will simply abandon, not things we'll ever find in the brain. I keep pushing back that this isn't the kind of claim you can test. Prove me wrong: design the strongest empirical test you can of whether ordinary belief/desire psychology carves nature at the joints or is just a useful fiction. Get concrete — prediction, intervention, decoding, whatever you think is best. Say what result would push toward eliminativism and what would push toward realism, and then be straight about what even a clean result would not settle.
