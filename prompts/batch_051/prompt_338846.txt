Translate the regress problem and its three main responses — foundationalism, coherentism, and infinitism — into that vocabulary, as faithfully and systematically as you can. For each position, give me your best-faith formal rendering: foundationalism as a well-founded structure whose terminal elements are basic beliefs, coherentism as a circular or fixed-point structure with no privileged base, and infinitism as an infinite but productive chain. Conclude by saying whether the residue marks a gap in the formal framework, a gap in the epistemological positions, or a sign that the debate is partly about something the formal framework cannot represent — and do not smuggle formal assumptions into the translation and then present the result as though it favored one side.
