I keep seeing people argue about separating the art from the artist as if the category of 'immoral art' were obvious, but I've never seen it pinned down. What should 'immoral art' mean? I want a definition that handles these cases: a technically brilliant Nazi propaganda film; a perfectly benign landscape painted by a serial killer; an inventive pop song whose lyrics are openly misogynistic; a novel written by someone later exposed as an abuser, where the book has nothing to do with abuse; and a film that is itself morally serious but whose director bullied its child actors during the shoot. Some of these feel clearly immoral art, some clearly not, and a couple I cant settle. Run your definition through all of them and tell me where it forces a verdict I might not have expected, and whether the same definition says anything about whether the work can still be good art.
