He then defines religion in terms of a unified system of beliefs and practices relative to sacred things, things set apart and forbidden. I don't have the book in front of me so I'm paraphrasing, but that passage is what set me off. My officemate is at maybe 0.8 because she thinks the sacred/profane distinction does real work; another friend is at 0.15 because he thinks it's a colonial-era export that should be dropped.

Give me your actual number, decomposed.
