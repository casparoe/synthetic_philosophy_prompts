I've been trying to get my decision-theory gut in order and I'm finding that my own calls don't fit a single story. Can you tell me if there's a principle that captures all of these, or if I need to flip on one of them?

Newcomb's problem: I one-box. I know the money is already there or not, but I take only the opaque box.

Parfit's hitchhiker: I'm rescued in the desert by a driver who only stops because he predicts I'll pay him $100 once we're back in town. In town, I pay. Not paying would save me $100, but it feels wrong.

Counterfactual mugging: Omega flips a fair coin. If tails, Omega asks me for $100. If heads, Omega pays me $10,000 iff it predicted I would have paid in the tails branch. I didn't precommit to anything. The coin lands tails and Omega asks. My gut: refuse to pay—I'd just be down $100 in the actual world. But I also think if I'd been asked before the coin flip, I'd have promised to pay. So there's a tension.

Then a weird one I've been calling XOR blackmail in my head. A superintelligence with a perfect model of me generates a quantum random bit Q. It also predicts P, whether I would pay it $100 upon receiving this exact message. It sends this exact message only if P xor Q = 1. The message says: "I have already arranged a terrible outcome which will occur if and only if Q=1. You may send me $100 if you wish." I receive the message. My gut leans pay: if I pay, P=1, and the message condition P xor Q = 1 forces Q=0, so the terrible outcome doesn't happen. But another part of me says Q was fixed before my decision and the terrible outcome is already scheduled iff Q=1, so paying just loses $100. I keep flipping.

Is there one coherent principle—evidential, causal, timeless, updateless, whatever—that gets my Newcomb one-box, my paying the hitchhiker, my refusing the mugging, and either version of my XOR-blackmail reaction? If you can't save all the verdicts, which one would you overturn? And what would the principle you land on say about an iterated version of the mugging, where I know Omega will run the same scheme on me once a week for a year?
