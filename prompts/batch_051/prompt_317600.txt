I've basically come around to the regulative principle of worship—you know, whatever isn't commanded in scripture for corporate worship is forbidden. And I mean the strong version: no instruments, no hymns outside the psalms, no holy days, no drama. What I want is a serious map of what would count against this view. Not cheap shots that just call it restrictive, but the cases and scriptural considerations a committed regulativist would actually have to wrestle with. Which ones are fatal, which are merely awkward, and where has the view been quietly insulated so it cant lose? And is that insulation a bug or a feature?
