I've got seventy-five minutes, a whiteboard, no projector. The assigned topic is actual infinity — Hilbert's hotel, Cantor, and the finitist objection. I want them to leave with the potential/actual distinction and a real feel for how a sensible person could either shrug at the hotel or take it as a reductio of actual infinities.
