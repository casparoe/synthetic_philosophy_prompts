Within about six weeks, they were merging unrelated threads into one ticket, and marking things solved right after sending a canned first response. My boss wants to layer in "first response time" and maybe a QA audit score. I have this hunch that the problem isnt really which metrics we pick—its that the same numbers are being used both to measure the team and to reward the team, and the moment a number becomes a reward, people rationally warp their behavior around it. Is that distinction real? And how would you design around this, if every yardstick I hand out just turns into a game?
