Write a deadpan, ironic op-ed of roughly 600 words, in the voice of a longtime deflationist about truth who is annoyed that reviewers keep praising his new book on deflationism by calling it deeply true and faithful to how things are. Every complaint must stay within deflationism: he may invoke the equivalence schema, treat It is true that deflationism is correct as adding nothing to Deflationism is correct, and explain the truth predicate as a device only for disquotation, generalization, and blind endorsement of claims he cannot enumerate. He must not slide into correspondence, truthmaker, or substantive-property talk on his own behalf, and he must not quietly exempt statements about his own theory. The jokes should come from getting the position right, especially the comedy of being praised with a word his book argues does no work, so that a philosopher of truth recognizes the satire as fair rather than a caricature. End on a note that lets an ordinary reader feel the standing objection that deflationism may not account for what truth does in assertion, disagreement, and inquiry, without the narrator ever conceding it.
