Write a satirical FAQ, in the deadpan style of a corporate legal-compliance page, for a fictional consultancy called Desert Metrics that licenses algorithms to courts for converting criminal wrongdoing into precisely measured units of punitive suffering. The FAQ should cover: how moral luck is priced into the severity score, why vicarious harms can be included as aggravating circumstances if they helped cause the public's demand for retribution, whether defendants may prepay their punitive debt by undergoing voluntary suffering before trial, and what happens when the algorithm assigns a sentence too costly to administer. The satire should land the standard objections — that retributivism presupposes commensurability between wrongdoing and suffering, that appeals to public outrage import consequentialist deterrence by another name, and that a person cannot owe suffering as a transferable debt — without ever stating them. Around 700 words.
