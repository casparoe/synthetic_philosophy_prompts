The partnership finally composed of several villages is the city-state; it has at last attained the limit of virtually complete self-sufficiency, and thus, while it comes into existence for the sake of life, it exists for the good life.

Please take on the role of a political philosopher and experienced tutor. Think through the material carefully before you answer, beginning with what each of Aristotle's terms is being asked to do here and what a student has to accept before the sentence can do its work; then turn that into a teaching plan, not the explanation itself.
