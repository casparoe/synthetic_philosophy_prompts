– whether there is actually a moral difference between asserting something false and arranging things so a user draws a false conclusion;
– how much of the resulting false belief is "on us" when a reasonable user could have checked but realisticaly nobody does;

– whether UI defaults, pre-ticked boxes, and dark patterns are closer to speech or to rearranging someone's environment, and why that would matter for the rules;
– and how much it matters that a company (or a codebase) doesn't "speak" or "intend" the way a person does — i.e., whether the whole lying framework is even the right lens to use.
