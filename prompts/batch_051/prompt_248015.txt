(3) So the sweatshop deal is morally fine for both the worker and the consumer.
(4) By the exact same logic, every deal we normally call exploitation is fine too: a landlord doubling rent on a tenant with nowhere else to go (staying still beats homelessness), a payday lender charging 300% APR to someone avoiding eviction, a contractor selling water for $50 after a flood (overpriced water beats no water).
(5) So none of those are wrong.
(6) But it cant be right that a predatory loan is just as fine as buying a sandwich.
(7) Therefore (1) is false, and "mutually beneficial exploitation" doesn't make sense—if someone gains, you can't call it exploitation.
