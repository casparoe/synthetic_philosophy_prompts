I am a journalist who covers probability, statistics, and the philosophy of scientific inference, and I am writing a piece on how investigators should set probabilities when the evidence is silent. The position I am defending in the piece is this: the principle of indifference is not refuted by Bertrand's paradox. The paradox shows only that the principle must be constrained before it can be used. Once one specifies the set of alternatives to be treated as symmetric, or requires the resulting assignment to be invariant under reparameterisations of the quantity in question, indifference remains a legitimate and useful rule for forming priors.

I would like you to tell me, as precisely and honestly as you can, what would count against that position. I want a map of its exposure rather than the standard case against it. Which observations, examples, arguments, or formal results would count against the constrained principle, and which of these are decisive, which merely awkward, and which only logically possible rather than live? Which would a defender of the constrained principle concede as genuine costs, and which would they dismiss as changing the subject or merely restating the original paradox? In particular, I want to know whether the demand for invariance can fail, either by yielding no usable distribution or by yielding distributions that conflict across equally natural coordinate systems; whether selecting the right partition simply relocates the arbitrariness one level up; and whether the constrained principle makes definite commitments that distinguish it from a thoroughgoing subjectivism about priors, or whether it has been qualified into unfalsifiability—and, if it has, whether that should bother me.

The sort of thing I would find most persuasive is a clean case in which the constraints turn out to be empty or unsatisfiable, but do not let that preference set your agenda. Give me the defeaters ranked from fatal to merely awkward, and an honest assessment of whether the view, once constrained in this way, still risks anything at all.
