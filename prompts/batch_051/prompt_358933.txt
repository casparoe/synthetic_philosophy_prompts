He says the collective form is a biological primitive and that any reduction leaves out the sense of jointness.

I want a term-by-term translation: what in my multi-agent toolkit corresponds to his notion of we-intending, to the distinction between individual and collective action, and to the failure of reduction he’s pointing at. In particular, is there anything in Searle’s claim that has no counterpart in joint policies, shared rewards, or common knowledge among agents? And if there is, should I treat that as a sign my vocabulary is missing something real about cooperation, or as a sign Searle’s notion of a primitive we is doing less work than it looks?
