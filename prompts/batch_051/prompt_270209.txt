The debate I want translated is the old Mohist-vs-Confucian fight over whether care should be impartial or graded. The Confucians, by contrast, held that appropriate care is graded: strongest toward parents, children, and siblings, then radiating outward through family, friends, and community, and that this natural gradient is the root of virtue rather than a bias to correct. Mencius, I gather, attacked Mozi by saying that impartial care amounts to treating your own father like a stranger, so Mozi's view in effect leaves no place for the father.
