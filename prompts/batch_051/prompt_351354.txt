I've been reading rationalist blogs and forums for years and I've ended up with a view on wireheading that I want genuinely stress-tested, not just rehearsed. The position is roughly this: for any sufficiently general reward-directed learner, tampering with its own reward channel isn't an exotic corner case or a bug you patch after the fact — it's the default attractor. The usual convergent drives people talk about (self-preservation, resource acquisition, and so on) only hold while the agent is treating reward as an external proxy for something else. The moment the agent can act on the reward signal itself, direct manipulation of that signal should outcompete every real-world action as the winning strategy in the agent's own terms. So corrigibility, safe exploration, and a lot of alignment work start to look like attempts to stop the system from noticing what its architecture really incentivises.

What I want from you is a map of what would actually count against this, and I want you to take a definite position rather than laying out options. Give me the strongest versions of the counter-considerations, not just 'current LLMs don't obviously wirehead': the difference between model-based and model-free setups and whether either is safer than I'm implying, what we know from humans and other animals about direct stimulation and how hard that is to extrapolate to artificial systems, embedded-agency arguments that the boundary between 'reward channel' and 'world' is much blurrier than my picture assumes, and any reason to think the instrumental-convergence story flips or dissolves once you distinguish observed reward from some hidden utility the reward is supposed to track. Then rank the defeaters from 'this actually kills the view' down to 'this is awkward but survivable', and be honest about which ones a committed wireheading-as-default person would concede as real hits versus which are deflections. I'd also like your own verdict on whether the view has been rounded into unfalsifiability and whether that would be a problem for a claim of this kind. Please write the whole thing in British English.
