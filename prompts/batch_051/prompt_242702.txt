Suppose that in 2035 a wealthy liberal democracy with strong traditions of family privacy and a well-funded welfare state enacts a parental licensing regime, defended explicitly by appeal to the child's right to an open future. The regime has these features. Any adult who wishes to hold full legal custody of a child must hold a Parental Capacity License. To obtain it, an applicant must complete a forty-hour course on child development, nutrition, injury prevention, and the legal duties of parents; pass a written and a practical assessment; pass a home-safety visit; and clear a background check for convictions involving violence against children. A license may be refused after three failed assessments, for a home that cannot meet minimum safety standards, or for a serious violence-against-children conviction, with appeal and retake available. The license is a condition of full custody, not of giving birth: a child born to an unlicensed parent remains with that parent provisionally, but the state assigns a family support worker, requires a parenting plan, and may seek removal only if minimum safety standards are still unmet after eighteen months of support. Adoption and foster placement require the license unconditionally. The stated rationale is that no child's future should be closed off by early neglect or preventable injury, and that licensing is appropriate wherever a role predictably puts a vulnerable person at risk, as with driving, teaching, and practicing medicine.

Predict, with reasoning, how this country's family law, fertility behavior, child welfare system, and politics look fifty years on. I want the chain of mechanisms, incentives, and constraints made visible: who ends up licensed and who does not; what happens to children whose parents remain unlicensed; how the support-first, sanction-later enforcement posture interacts with the licensing frame; which groups sort into or out of parenthood; whether the courts strike any of it down and on what grounds; and whether the regime preserves children's open futures or creates new ways of closing them. For each link in the prediction, state how confident you are, which effects you take to be robust, which are guesses, and what would have to be true for the prediction to fail. Do not give me a menu of possible outcomes; give me your actual best prediction, taking a definite position on each of these questions and defending it.
