Take the vampire version of the transformative-experience problem, but change the setup. Before the one-time irreversible choice, you can enter a fully reversible "preview mode" of being a vampire for a couple days at a time, as often as you want. While you're in it, you have the full vampire psychology — the desires and the feel of it. While you're out, you keep a vivid memory of what you wanted and felt inside, but you no longer find those wants compelling: you remember that inside-you wanted to stay a vampire forever, but from the outside that preference seems foreign. Across visits you can also remember how stable inside-you's preferences are and how often inside-you and outside-you disagree about what to do. The final commitment is still one-time and irreversible, and once you commit there are no more previews.

Does preview mode solve the problem, or does it just relocate it?
