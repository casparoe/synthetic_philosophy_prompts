(5) at the same time I want to keep phronesis as a single, global trait — the thing that lets a virtuous person read a situation and act well across very different domains
(6) for moral education, then, I think we should mostly shape situations and habits rather than try to instill global character

which pairs are genuinely in tension, which only look like it? rank the genuine tensions by severity, say each time what would have to give, and tell me which single revision would clean up the most.
