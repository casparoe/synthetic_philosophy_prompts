Socrates then goes on to derive the Greek word for gods, *theoi*, from *thein*, to run, because the earliest people supposedly thought the gods were the running, moving things—sun, moon, stars. So in his telling, the original sense of the word preserves a truer, earlier picture of what the gods actually were. That's the etymological move: dig back to the first meaning, and you recover the thing's real nature.
