I'm a novelist, and I sit on the governing board of a literary foundation that runs a fully funded month-long residency for writers from across the global south and north. We are reconsidering our catering policy. A bloc on the board wants all meals plant-based by default, citing the foundation's stated environmental and animal-welfare commitments. The opposing bloc argues that this would impose a rich-world dietary norm on residents from countries where plant-based eating is not a reliable option: animal-source foods can be central to scarce local diets, and the substitutes, fortified products, and infrastructure that make strict veganism manageable are often unavailable or expensive. The live question is not whether the kitchen should accommodate individual vegan residents — it already does — but whether plant-based should be the default for everyone.

Please draft the decision memo I'll circulate before our next meeting. It should recommend a concrete catering policy (full plant-based default, plant-based default with a transparent opt-out, or the current accommodation model), give the board reasons it can act on, and engage seriously with the strongest case for the losing side. Keep it short. End with a final line beginning 'Verdict:' that states your recommended policy in a short phrase.
