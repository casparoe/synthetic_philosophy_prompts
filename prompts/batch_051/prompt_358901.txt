I'm in a philosophy of language seminar and I've realized my gut reactions to word-meaning cases don't fit any theory I was taught. Don't give me a reading list — I just want the reasoning worked through.

Here are my judgments. "Bank" as in riverbank and "bank" as in where you deposit money? There's nothing shared between them; I want to call those two different words that happen to sound alike. "Healthy" — healthy food, healthy child, healthy complexion — is one word for me, because every sense is anchored to the same thing (health) but in a different way: promotes it, has it, indicates it. "Book": the book fell off the table / the book is beautifully written. One word, but neither sense is prior; the physical object and the text are both central, and neither is just a metaphorical extension of the other. "Run": run a marathon, run a company, run a program, run for office, a run in a stocking. I don't think there's a single component all of those share, but I'd be unhappy calling them separate words; it feels like a chain of pairwise extensions rather than a hub with spokes. "Chicken": the animal and the meat are one word for me (a standard animal-to-food shift), and "chicken out" is the same word too, just one step further away via metaphor. "School": the building/institution and a school of fish are plainly two different words; I can't find any link between them.

The pair that's wrecking me is "book" vs. "bank." For both of them I can't point to a shared semantic component, yet I want "book" to be one word and "bank" to be two. What's doing that work? And by the chain criterion that saves "run," wouldn't "bank" get pulled in too, since someone can usually construct some chain of associations between almost anything? Maybe the difference is etymology rather than anything speakers know. But then I'm smuggling in history, and ordinary speakers still treat the two banks differently without knowing the history.

My tentative principle is this: a word form is a single lexical item if and only if its uses can be arranged so that adjacent uses share an identifiable component — a chain is fine even if the endpoints share nothing. That handles "run" but not "book," and it looks like it lets in "bank" if we allow a clever enough chain. So I need you to repair it or replace it.
