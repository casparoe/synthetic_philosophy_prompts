We expect a jury to be able to give reasons for its verdict, and we disqualify jurors who come in with fixed opinions from the news, yet we treat voting as something you should do even if you couldn't name the candidates' actual policies and are going mainly on a vibe or a party habit. People who say uninformed voters shouldn't vote get called elitist, but nobody says that about uninformed jurors. Is there a defensible principle that makes voting different from jury service here, or is this just a romantic picture of the citizen as a sovereign whose mere will deserves equal weight? I want the strongest case for the asymmetry, and an honest verdict on whether to keep it.
