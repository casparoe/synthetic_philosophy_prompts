Write a villanelle about the frame problem in AI. Use the two refrains to do structural work: one should be something like "say only what the action changes," and the other something like "and everything else remains unchanged." The poem should show why that second line, returned to over and over, is the real trouble: first you notice you cant list every non-change, and then you notice that even a clever default rule doesn't tell a bounded reasoner whats relevant. The repetitions should track that escalation, not just decorate it, and it should be accurate enough that someone who works on the frame problem wouldn't call it a caricature. After the poem, add a short note saying where the poem leans on a contestable reading of the frame problem (whether the logical side is basically solved and only the epistemological side remains, etc.), and what would change your mind about that reading.
