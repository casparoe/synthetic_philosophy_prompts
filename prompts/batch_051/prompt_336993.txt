I want to test a structural parallel I have been turning over, between something in the metaphysics of personal identity and something in the theory of species. I cannot tell whether it is a deep point or a superficial analogy.

Reid's brave officer objection to Locke's memory criterion goes like this. On the Lockean view, a person at a later time is the same person as someone at an earlier time in virtue of the later consciousness reaching back to, and taking up, the earlier thoughts and actions — roughly, in virtue of memory. Reid imagines an old general who remembers the charges he led as a young officer; the young officer remembers being flogged as a schoolboy; but the old general has entirely forgotten the schoolboy. If memory is the criterion of personal identity, then the general is the officer, and the officer is the boy, but the general is not the boy — yet identity is transitive, so the criterion yields a contradiction.

Now take the standard, somewhat idealized, picture of a ring species: a chain of populations arranged around a geographic barrier, each adjacent pair interbreeding with the next, while the two terminal populations, where their ranges come back into contact, do not interbreed. If membership in the same species is fixed by pairwise interbreeding, the same shape appears: population A is conspecific with B, B with C, and so on through the chain, but A is not conspecific with Z.

The common structure seems to be this. A relation that is defined over local, pairwise, overlapping links — memory-connectedness between stages of a life; interfertility between neighboring populations — is being used to ground a relation (personal identity, species membership) that is supposed to be transitive and identity-like, while the grounding relation itself is not transitive across a long chain. In both cases the endpoints have lost the relevant connection even though no individual adjacent link is broken.

Is this a genuine identity of structure, a loose analogy, or a confusion? Please lay out the mapping as precisely as you can, say what would have to be true on both sides for results to transfer from one domain to the other, and then stress-test it: where does the parallel break down, and what does it predict that turns out false?
