The people who use this usually want you to conclude that a fast takeoff is the default, or at least that slow-takeoff people are kidding themselves. I want to know how far the analogy actually carries before it starts smuggling in conclusions. Give me the correspondence table: what's supposed to play the role of the neutrons, the fissile material, the critical mass, the mean free path, the timing. Then go after the places it breaks or quietly imports physics that an economy of minds doesn't have. Does the conclusion (fast takeoff by default) survive the disanalogies, or is the analogy doing most of the work?
