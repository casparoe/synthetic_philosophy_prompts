Quine's gavagai scenario is usually set up as a static scene: a field linguist hears gavagai whenever a rabbit appears, and Quine argues that no amount of behavioral evidence can settle whether gavagai means rabbit, undetached rabbit parts, or a rabbit stage. I want you to work through a version in which the inquiry becomes active and longitudinal, and to say how much of Quine's lesson survives the change.

Imagine a cooperative informant who is willing to be questioned over the course of a year. The linguist can (i) keep track of individual rabbits and encounter the same animal repeatedly as it matures, loses fur, and changes its overall appearance; (ii) follow rabbits as they are captured, skinned, and butchered, pointing at the severed parts and asking whether they are gavagai, or the same gavagai as the living animal was; and (iii) use a native expression that, on the basis of extensive practice with stones, tools, and other separately movable objects, the linguist has come to translate with high confidence as same, allowing questions of the form same gavagai? across different sightings. The task is not to rehearse what Quine himself said; it is to determine what the thought experiment's lesson becomes under these enriched conditions.

Does this version let the linguist separate the rabbit, undetached-rabbit-part, and rabbit-stage translations from one another, and through exactly which tests? Or does the indeterminacy merely migrate — into the translation of the same-device, into the way the data are divided into distinct objects and continuants, or into the choice of an entire translation manual — while the observable evidence stays compatible with more than one manual? Lay out what a defender of Quine's radical indeterminacy should say about the new evidence, what a defender of a more modest underdetermination-of-theory-by-evidence view should say, and give your own assessment of where the surviving lesson of the example lies.

Structure your answer in the following sections, in this order, with roughly these lengths: The tests the enriched setup makes possible (about 100 words); The case that the three translations can now be separated (about 150 words); The case that the indeterminacy has only moved (about 200 words); Where the lesson ends up (about 100 words).
