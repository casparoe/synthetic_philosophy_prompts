- yes on a cruise-passenger fee to pay for a new rescue boat
- yes on a developer impact fee to build a public boardwalk along the bluffs
- no on a local sales tax to build a convention center
- no on a property tax on year-round residents to widen the road to a proposed private resort

Now the chapter I'm stuck on has them voting on a property tax on every homeowner—including year-round residents—to bury power lines in the fire-prone hills. I want the result to feel consistent with the earlier votes, not arbitrary. What would they plausibly do? I can see a couple of rules that would fit their track record, but they point different ways on this one. What are the candidate rules, which one do you think best explains the votes I've already set, and what later ballot measure would cleanly seperate them?
