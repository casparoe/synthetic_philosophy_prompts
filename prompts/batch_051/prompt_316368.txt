My working understanding of the dialogue, which I know only through translation and paraphrase, is this. Gongsun Long defends the thesis that a white horse is not a horse. His interlocutor objects that this is plainly false, since a white horse is a horse. Gongsun Long returns a family of moves. First, someone who is asked to fetch a horse will be satisfied by a yellow or a black horse, but someone asked to fetch a white horse will not; since the two requests are satisfied by different things, the expressions cannot name the same thing. Second, horse is a name used for shape and white is a name used for color, so that white horse names a shape together with a color, which is more than the shape alone. Third, white does not fix the bearer of whiteness: whiteness attaches to stones and other things besides horses, so the contribution of white to the compound cannot be identified with the contribution of horse, and the expressions white horse and horse are not interchangeable in inference.
