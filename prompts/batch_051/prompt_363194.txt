I've been going around in circles about Schopenhauer on music, and I want someone to force a negotiated verdict rather than just pick a side. I'm genuinely attracted to the theory because of its systematic virtues: the other arts represent particular things in the world -- a painting of a horse, a poem about grief -- but pure instrumental music doesn't seem to represent anything, and yet it hits with a directness and universality the depictive arts can't match. Schopenhauer's move is that music isn't in the business of representation at all: where the other arts copy the will indirectly through the Ideas, music is a direct copy of the will itself, which is why it feels so immediate and bypasses concepts. I want that to be true. It explains why a Brahms intermezzo can feel urgent without being urgent about anything. But I also have a firm intuition I can't shake: an enormous amount of music does represent. A funeral march is about mourning. Vivaldi all but mimes a thunderstorm. Film scores carry narrative information you can decode. A Mahler ländler is doing social and referential work that shouldn't be possible if music's nature is non-representational. Schopenhauer's out, as I understand it, is to call all of that impure -- music borrowing meaning from texts, titles, dances, and the other arts -- and to say the real metaphysics is about absolute music. That sounds like a manuever to save the theory by declaring the counterexamples not-really-music. So which should yield? Should I debunk my representationalist intuition as mere association and title-suggestion? Should I amend the theory to say music is just less representational than the other arts, not non-representational -- and would that amendment still be interesting, or does it collapse into banality? Or is there a compatibility reading on which musical meaning and pictorial reference are just different things I've been conflating? More than a verdict I want criteria: how do I tell the difference between legitimately dropping an intuition that was only ever an association, and cheaply immunizing a theory?
