I'm taking a course on concepts and categorization, and we have been working through the prototype, exemplar, and theory-theory views. The working criteria I was given for something's being a prototype-structured concept are these: (i) it is a stored summary representation of a category, rather than a remembered list of instances; (ii) it produces graded membership, so that some instances are more typical or central than others; (iii) it licenses inferences about novel instances—once you place something under the concept, you infer unobserved features of it; (iv) it is stable across contexts and gets retrieved rather than assembled for a single occasion; and (v) it figures in explanation, not just classification, by capturing why the members of the category go together.

Consider the category people use when deciding what to take out of a burning house at 3 a.m.: the category of things worth saving in a fire. It has a clear typicality structure—family members, pets, passports, and irreplaceable photographs are central, while spare chargers, rarely used kitchen gadgets, and half-finished books are peripheral. It also licenses inferences and offers explanations (the central members tend to be irreplaceable, emotionally load-bearing, and portable under time pressure). But it seems to be assembled on the spot for a single goal, and it is not at all obvious that it exists as a stored summary representation before or after the emergency.

Please work through the five criteria one by one and say whether the fire-evacuation category satisfies each. Where the criteria pull in different directions, identify which criterion is doing the real work, and then give a verdict: does this category have a prototype in the sense the criteria are trying to capture? Finally, say whether the case suggests that any of the five criteria should be revised—for instance, whether stored stability or goal-independence is really essential to concept-hood, and whether an exemplar-based or theory-based treatment would handle this kind of goal-derived, ad hoc category better.
