The loudest engineer on the team has this whole theory. Calling them errors is also wrong, he says, because an error presumes the system was aiming at truth. Then he brings up the philosopher Frankfurt, who apparently says the liar cares enough about the truth to hide from it, while the bullshitter is the guy who just doesn't care whether what he says is true. The model, on his telling, is a Frankfurt bullshitter: it isn't answerable to the facts at all, it's just producing the most plausible next token. And his punchline, which has started actually steering our roadmap, is that trying to fix hallucinations with more factuality training is a category error — like telling a bullshitter to try harder to be honest, the fix won't take.
