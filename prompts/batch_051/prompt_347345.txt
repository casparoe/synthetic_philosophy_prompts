i've been reading about pain asymbolia on and off for a while -- the condition where people can locate and describe a pain, sometimes in detail, but say it isn't unpleasant and don't try to stop it. i've got no formal background in philosophy, just whatever i could find online, so my words are probably a bit off. That one observation has turned into a little theory i call the registration-and-levy view, and i want an honest verdict on it.

The theory: pain is two things that normal experience fuses into one. First, the registration -- the raw sensory readout: where the damage is, how intense, whether it burns or stabs or aches, how it spreads. Second, the levy -- the felt demand to act now, the thing that makes pain urgent and awful rather than merely informative. On this view the badness of pain sits entirely in the levy. Pain asymbolia is what you get when the registration still arrives but the levy is cancelled: the body files its report, but nothing is owed in response, so the person doesn't mind.

If that's right, there's no such thing as a pain that is bad in itself; awfulness is a way the nervous system addresses itself, not a property of any sensation. The same registration could in principle be delivered with no levy and stop being suffering. A friend of mine was given morphine after surgery and said he could still feel the wound doing something, but it didn't matter to him at all; apperantly that's pretty common. I think that's a small, drug-induced version of the asymbolia split.

I know the orthodox line is roughly that pain has a sensory component and an affective component, so maybe i've just dressed that up in new words. But I think the levy is more specific than an "affective component": it's not just an emotion glued to a sensation, it's a felt command -- the experience itself ordering you around. Does that difference actually do any work? Is this a real distinction or just a metaphor? Has someone already got this exact view, and under what name? And where does it break: what's the case that registration-plus-levy can't handle? I don't need it to be original, i just want to know what's salvageable and what's not. Please answer in British English.
