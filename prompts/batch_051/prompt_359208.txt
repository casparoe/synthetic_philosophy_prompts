Write the joint statement coming out of an good-faith adversarial collaboration between two people who both accept expected utility theory but split on fanaticism. Label them the Fanatic and the Bounded Moderate, since the final document should be signable by both.

The Fanatic's position: utility is unbounded, and she accepts the full fanatical result — for any ordinary benefit and any nonzero probability p, no matter how small, there's some astronomically large payoff such that a p-chance of that payoff beats the ordinary benefit for sure. The Bounded Moderate's position: utility is bounded (there is a finite maximum difference in value between any two possible outcomes), and she thinks this is exactly what blocks the fanatical result without abandoning expected utility maximization.

They've just spent six months working through this, and they found they agree on more than either expected — but there's still a real residue. The document should do all of the following.
