Does the novella push us toward taking that possibility seriously, or does it quietly depend on the assumption that a bug cannot suffer as a person does? I want a reading that is faithful in both directions—not reducing the story to an illustration, and not distorting the scale argument. Take a definite interpretive position and defend it, rather than surveying possible readings; where the best-supported interpretation is contestable, say so and argue for your choice.
