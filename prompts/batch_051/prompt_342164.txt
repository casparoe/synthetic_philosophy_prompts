Dennett's line is that the Orwellian and Stalinesque stories are empirically equivalent here: either the experience itself is edited before it reaches consciousness (Stalinesque), or the conscious experience happens and is then edited in memory afterwards (Orwellian), and since both predict the same first-person report there is no fact of the matter about which is true.
