I'm a software engineer and I've only read pop behavioral economics, so maybe I'm missing something. I keep seeing weakness of will (akrasia) explained as just hyperbolic discounting: your discount rate is steep in the short run, so right now you prefer the immediate reward, even though yesterday you preferred the bigger delayed reward and tomorrow you'll prefer it again. But if we can model that whole cycle as one discount function, why do philosophers still treat akrasia as a real problem? Isnt the person just acting on whatever they currently prefer—no contradiction, just a time-inconsistent preference schedule we could patch with precommitment tools? Once the behavior has a clean functional form, what's left to be puzzled about?
