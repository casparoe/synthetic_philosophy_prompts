At a neighborhood meeting somebody shut down a plan for a new factory with "your right to swing your fist ends where my nose begins." The factory would emit low-level pollutants: every resident downwind gets a tiny rise in lifetime cancer risk, nobody is actually injured, and nobody consented to any of it. I couldnt tell whether the line even reaches that case. Lay out the different precise claims that slogan might be making—what "my nose" could mean (actual contact? any nonzero chance of contact? some threshold of real risk?), what "ends" could mean (a rights-violation, a compensable harm, a trigger for state coercion?), and where consent fits in each reading. For each claim say whether it's a platitude, a substantive and defensible view, or just false, and then say which reading the neighbor actually needs for the quote to do the work they were using it to do.
