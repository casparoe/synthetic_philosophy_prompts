Take the US–China race over advanced AI from the 2022 export controls on advanced chips onward, and analyze it with Fearon's rationalist explanations for war. I don't need a recap of the export controls; I need the mapping. Treat the US and China — and, where it matters, the labs and chipmakers inside each — as the players, say what the thing being contested actually is (frontier capability? access to chips? something else?), and walk through Fearon's three mechanisms: private information with incentives to misrepresent, commitment problems, and issue indivisibility. Which parts of the race do they iluminate, and which do they have to distort to fit? Finally, what does it tell us about Fearon that this has stayed a race and not a shooting war — is that a confirmation, a mismatch, or a sign the framework was always about costly contests and war was just the case he happened to write down?
