Me and a few friends run a prediction league on our discord server. It's mostly about sports, movie release dates, school stuff, dumb stuff like whether the cafeteria will run out of the good fries by Thursday. Right now we score by total number correct over the season, and whoever has the most at the end picks the movie for movie night. I wanted to change the scoring so it rewards people who are actually confident instead of just guessing, and also so there's more drama week to week.

Here's the rule I'm thinking about. Everyone starts with 1000 points. Each week there are maybe ten yes/no questions, and on every single question you have to bet exactly 25% of whatever points you currently have. You pick YES or NO. If you're right you get double your stake back, so you gain 25% of your current points. If you're wrong you lose that 25%. You can't skip a question and you can't bet a smaller or larger percentage. The season is 12 weeks, so about 120 questions total. My thinking is that this grinds down people who just randomly guess, since a coin-flip guesser will slowly bleed points, and it rewards people who actually know things. But I'm not sure that's how it actually plays out.

How much of the final ranking would just be luck anyway? What actually happens to someone who's right 55% of the time versus someone who's right 65% of the time under a rule like this? What are the ways people could game it, like using a second account or teaming up, and would those actually help? And is 25% a good number to force, or is there a smarter way to set the rule up so it does what I want without blowing up? Basically I want to know what this thing definately incentivises before I propose it to the server.
