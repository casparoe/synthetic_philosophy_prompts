An investor has offered to fund my company on the condition that I come to genuinely admire him and treat him as a mentor. By any ordinary standard, he is not admirable: he has a record of poor judgment and of mistreating people who work for him. The funding is real and would keep the company alive, and I believe I could, with effort, bring myself to admire him sincerely. He argues that the benefits the funding would bring are themselves a reason for me to admire him. Is he right?
