I keep noticing the same move when people discuss large language models, and I want to know whether it has a name. A colleague will insist that the system has no beliefs, goals, or understanding in any literal sense. A minute later they'll explain a failed jailbreak by saying the model 'thought' the attacker was its developer and 'wanted' to be helpful; when I ask whether that means the model has beliefs, they say no, it's just a predictive shorthand. Then they use those same attributed beliefs and desires to predict which other prompts will work, and to draw conclusions about what the system will do next. That last step seems to me to rely on treating the mental states as more than a convenient fiction. Other people use the same vocabulary without ever slipping into the literal reading. Is there an established name for this pattern, or should I really be separating two or three different stances that happen to share a vocabulary? If it's near an existing concept, tell me exactly where my pattern differs from it rather than just giving a label.
