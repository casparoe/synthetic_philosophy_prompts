I defend the following view about generics. A generic sentence of the form "Ks are F" — for example, "ducks lay eggs," "mosquitoes carry malaria," "ravens are black" — is not a quantified claim about individual Ks, not even a loose quantification with tolerated exceptions. It is a kind-level predication: the property F is, in the context of utterance, part of the characteristic profile of the kind K. A property belongs to the characteristic profile of a kind relative to a context when it is the output of the kind's normal functioning, development, or interactional role, assessed against a contextually supplied comparison class of related kinds. Individual exceptions are handled not by counting how many Ks are F but by an absorption mechanism: a non-F K counts as an exception only if its failure to be F is explained by factors outside the kind's own explanatory schema; if the failure is explained within the schema — by sexual dimorphism, developmental stage, or the kind's interactional relation to the comparison class — it is absorbed and does not affect the truth of the generic.

This view yields several commitments. "Ducks lay eggs" is true although drakes and ducklings never lay eggs, because egg-laying is the output of the duck reproductive schema and the non-laying of males is absorbed by the sexual dimorphism internal to that schema. "Mosquitoes carry malaria" can be true although only a tiny minority of individual mosquitoes carry the parasite, because carrying malaria is an interactional property between the mosquito kind and the human comparison kind, and the failure of most individual mosquitoes to carry the parasite is absorbed by the mechanics of transmission. The truth of a generic is therefore context-sensitive: "sharks attack humans" may be true in a context in which the comparison class is dangerous marine animals and false in a context in which the comparison class is everything sharks do, with no change in the sharks themselves. The view also treats speakers' psychological-essentialist representations of kinds as semantic content, not as a processing heuristic.
