I've been arguing with a friend who does stand-up, and he recons the philosophical theories of humour are just ways of killing the joke — that they never survive contact with an actual laugh. I'd like to prove him wrong by giving him a poem that does the theories instead of describing them. Could you write me one?

The poem should be about twenty lines: four quatrains, one for each of the standard theories — superiority (the Hobbesian sudden glory of feeling better than someone else or than our own former self), incongruity (Kant and Schopenhauer's disappointed expectation, the mismatch between concept and thing), relief (Spencer and Freud's discharge of pent-up energy), and benign violation (McGraw and Warren: something wrong but simultaneously safe). Here's the structural part, which matters a lot to me. Each quatrain should instantiate its theory rather than name it. The superiority stanza should condescend to its subject and end on a rhyme that deflates or trips them. The incongruity stanza should set up a tidy metre and rhyme scheme, then break it exactly at the punchline. The relief stanza should be one syntactically held sentence that releases into a short clause or a snapped rhyme at the end. The benign-violation stanza should say something mildly offensive about the reader or about laughter, then cushion it in the same breath. Close with a couplet that steps back and says something about whether the four theories are rivals or four moments of a single laugh — I'd like that left open rather than resolved. Someone who knows the theories should be able to tell which stanza is which without labels, but it should also read as a real poem to someone who doesnt.

After the poem, give me a brief JSON summary so I can show my friend what you were up to. Use this schema: an array of five objects — four for the quatrains, one for the couplet — each with fields 'stanza' (number, or 'couplet'), 'theory' (which theory it embodies, or 'synthesis' for the couplet), 'device' (the formal device used), and 'note' (one sentence on how the device tracks the theory). Also include a top-level field 'relationship' with a one-sentence note on how the couplet treats the relationship between the four theories.
