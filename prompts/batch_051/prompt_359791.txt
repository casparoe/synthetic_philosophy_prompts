At a meetup a while back two friends got into an arguement about the continuum hypothesis, and I've been turning it over ever since. The first one said Cohen's forcing result already settles the philosophical question: there are models of ZFC where CH holds and models where it fails, so the continuum can't have a determinate size full stop. Asking whether CH is true simpliciter, he said, is like asking what color the number five is. The second one pushed back that independence is a fact about provability from one particular theory, not about truth. She pointed out that there are statements independent of Peano arithmetic, but we don't usually say those lack truth values, because arithmetic has an intended model. He replied that arithmetic is special for exactly that reason: set theory has no standard model in the relevant sense, just a multiverse of equally legitimate universes, and the continuum has genuinely different sizes in them. She said that's a much stronger philosophical claim than the forcing theorem gives you. Set theorists have proposed extra axioms that settle CH in both directions, she said, and they argue about which ones are natural; if there were no fact of the matter, the whole enterprise would be as silly as arguing about whether the parallel postulate is true. He said the parallel postulate is exactly the right analogy: after non-Euclidean geometry nobody asks whether it's true, they ask which geometry you're working in, and CH is the same — pick your further axioms and you get your answer. She answered that choose-your-axioms is not the same as no-fact-of-the-matter; a theory being too weak to decide something doesn't mean the mathematical universe fails to decide it. Then they went around for another twenty minutes on whether the candidate axioms beyond ZFC are truth-tracking or just aesthetically chosen add-ons.

I stayed out of it, partly because each of them kept making points that felt solid until the other one replied. Could you adjudicate the exchange point by point: wich of their specific claims hold up, which are overstated or just rhetoric, and what are both of them missing about the relationship between independence and truth?
