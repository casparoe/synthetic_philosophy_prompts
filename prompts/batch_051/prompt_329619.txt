I've been trying to work out what I actually think about whether a bigger population is ever better, going by gut reactions to cases rather than any theory I already hold, and I keep flip-flopping depending on the scale. Here are the cases and what I find myself wanting to say. (1) My partner and I are deciding whether to have a third kid. The first two are happy, we have the money and energy, and the third would very likely have a life well worth living. That seems to me good—not just okay, but an improvement to the world. (2) Compare two countries. Country A has 10 million people who are all very happy. Country B has 50 million people, every one of whom is less happy than every person in A, but still clearly above neutral, and B has more total happiness. I find myself leaning toward A as the better country. (3) My city of 90,000 has a real sense of community. A plan to grow it to half a million would make average quality of life a bit lower—more traffic, weaker local ties—though most people would still be content. I don't think the bigger city is better, and I might think it's worse, even if total happiness goes up. (4) A meadow can support about 100 deer with rich lives or about 300 deer living scrappier, slightly less good but still net-positive lives. I can't bring myself to say the 300-deer meadow is better, but I also don't think the extra 200 deer make the world worse by existing. (5) A couple is told a child they could have would have a manageable genetic condition: life worth living, but on the whole less happy than a typical child's. I think having the child is good, or at least not worse than not having them.

I can tell the family cases (1 and 5) sound like they're tracking total value and the country/city/deer cases (2, 3, and 4) sound like they're tracking average value or something about not making things thinner, but I don't want to just have two voices in my head. Is there a principle that gets all five, or close? If not, which verdicts should I give up? And what would your principle say about cases I haven't settled: a planet of 1 billion flourishing people versus 8 billion people all just above neutral but with a higher total; the extinction of an animal species whose members all lead lives barely worth living; and the view that a bigger family is never better, just permissible. Please write your answer in American English.
