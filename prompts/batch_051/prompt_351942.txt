Consider a counterfactual. From the year 1000 CE onward, the Mediterranean basin develops a climate in which the usual summer drought is replaced by regular, reliable summer rainfall. Treat this as a stipulated counterfactual rather than disputing its meteorological plausibility. The state persists indefinitely, and the region's inhabitants experience it simply as the weather. Nothing else changes at the outset.
