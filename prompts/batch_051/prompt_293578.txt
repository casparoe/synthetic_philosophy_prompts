Over the past year a moral particularist and a moral generalist in my reading group have mostly talked past one another. We're making them do an adversarial collaboration, and I need you to write the joint statement they'd end up with. Don't describe how to write it; write the document itself.

For clarity: the particularist's view is roughly that sound moral judgment does not require, and can be deformed by, a set of moral principles, and that what's right is settled by the particular configuration of the case. The generalist's view is roughly that justified moral judgment must be answerable to principles, even if they're only pro tanto, default, or ceteris parabis ones.

The document should include, in this order:

1. A numbered list of claims both can sign. These should be stronger than truisms: they should constrain the debate, for instance about moral education, about how reasons combine, and about which cases are agreed test cases, without smuggling in either side's conclusion. If a claim only comes out true on one reading of "principle," flag that.

2. A disagreements table: the genuine remaining disputes, with each side's view stated in wording that side would actually own, not a parody, and not a phrasing the other side picked for them.

3. The cruxes: the sub-questions such that settling one would actually move at least one of them. Don't give me ten possible cruxes; pick the ones you think are load-bearing and say why those rather than the obvious alternatives.

4. For each crux, a test, case, or argument both sides would accept as bearing on it, with each side's prediction about it recorded in advance.

5. A short section on which parts of their year-long argument now look merely verbal and which are substantive. Be willing to say that a chunk of the debate collapses if "principle" is read loosely, or that it doesn't, if that's your view.

I want a committed draft. If you think holism of reasons is the real crux and codifiability is a sideshow, say so and defend the choice. If you think the generalist can absorb holism by retreating to default reasons, write that into the table and have the particularist's response reflect their best objection rather than a strawman. Don't hand me a survey of positions.

Write the whole thing in American English.
