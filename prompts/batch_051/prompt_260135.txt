The explanation researchers offer is straightforward: once material constraints are removed and people are free to pursue their genuine interests, innate differences between male and female preferences express themselves more fully. Egalitarian countries have already largely eliminated the discrimination and stereotypes that once steered women away from technical fields, so the remaining gap is not a product of culture but the natural expression of biology. That is why workplace-culture campaigns to get more women into STEM are unlikely to succeed: the causes they target are not the real causes."
