ok heres a distiction ive been using and want checked. im calling it insulated vs indexed pleasure, and the bigger claim is that the experience machine debate has been working with the wrong picture of what a pleasure is.

start with beliefs. two people can have the exact same stuff going on in their skulls, but if one grew up on earth and one on twin earth, their word 'water' picks out different things. the belief is partly constituted by what its about, not just by the brain goo. i think pleasures are like that. the pleasure you take in your friend actually pulling through surgery isnt just a warm feeling caused by the news; the content 'my friend pulled through' is part of the state itself. so if the news was fake, you had *a* pleasure, but not the one you took yourself to have. a subjective duplicate is not the same state, even if it feels identical from the inside.

hear me out on nozick then. the reason i wouldnt plug in isnt that i value reality on top of pleasure. its that the machine literally cannot deliver the pleasures i actually want. it can deliver insulated duplicates—felt juice with the real object swapped out—and an insulated duplicate of an indexed pleasure is a different state. so the hedonist shouldnt abandon ship; they should amend hedonism so that wellbeing is maximizing indexed pleasures, not insulated ones. im calling that indexed hedonism.

now the obvious problem: i can enjoy a movie even though i know the dragon isnt real, and the pleasure seems fully genuine. my move is that fiction pleasures are indexed to something real-ish (the telling, the imagining, the safe thrill), not to the dragon. the machine is different because the object i wanted—my actual relationships, my actual achievements—is exactly what gets replaced, and it was the object of the pleasure i wanted when i refused to plug in. maybe thats special pleading, i dont know.

honest assessment please. is the insulated/indexed distinction doing real work, or did i just restate the idea that wellbeing has a 'fit with reality' condition and add a name? is the twin earth move actually allowed for feelings, or are feelings the classic case where whats inside the head fixes everything? and whats the most damaging case—say a pleasure whose object i falsely believed held for years, and i never find out: does indexed hedonism have to say my life was worse than it felt, which sounds crazy?
