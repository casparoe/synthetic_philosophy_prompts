I have been working through risk-weighted expected utility (REU) and have run into an objection I cannot tell whether to keep or discard. As I understand it, REU says that an agent assigns each possible outcome a utility measuring how much she values that outcome in itself—so a linear utility for money is compatible with a cautious attitude toward gambles—and then a separate risk function distorts cumulative probabilities, making the weight given to a possible payoff depend on where that payoff ranks in the distribution. The advertised advantage is that risk attitude is no longer conflated with the curvature of the utility function, as it is in expected utility theory.

Against this I want to run a five-step argument. (1) To apply REU to any actual decision, one must begin with a utility assignment to outcomes, because the risk function operates on utility differences rather than on outcomes directly. (2) But utility numbers get their content in only one way: they are the function whose probability-weighted expectation represents an agent's preferences among gambles. On that understanding, a utility function is not an independent measure of how good outcomes are; it is an encoding of the agent's preferences over risky prospects, including all of her attitudes to risk. (3) Therefore any risk attitude that REU attributes to the added risk function is already encoded in the utility function, and the risk function is redundant—whatever it does could be absorbed into a suitably transformed utility function. (4) If, to protect REU, we say that the utility function is meant only to measure the goodness of outcomes and not risk attitudes, then we owe an account of what these numbers mean and how they could be elicited that does not itself proceed through risky choice, and none has been given. (5) So REU either double-counts risk attitudes or rests on a notion of utility without content; either way it is not a genuine departure from expected utility theory. Is this argument sound? If not, identify exactly which step fails and state the corrected version of that step under which REU escapes the objection.
