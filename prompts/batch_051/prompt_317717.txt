I keep noticing a pattern I don't have a name for, and it recently bit me in a community farmers market I help run. Each vendor individually started arriving earlier in the morning to get a better spot, which was totally reasonable given what everyone else was doing, but now everyone shows up before dawn, the spots are basically the same as they were relative to eachother, and everyone is exhausted and mad about it. They'd all vote tomorrow to go back to the old opening time, but no single vendor can unilaterally sleep in without losing their usual spot, and any agreement we tried fell apart because someone realized they could grab the best spot by showing up early just once. I've seen the same shape in a group of friends who all tried to be the most responsive friend until the whole group chat burned out, and in a forum where each moderator approved slightly worse posts to avoid looking strict until the place was just spam. Is there one established concept that covers all three, or are these different phenomena? Specifically, how is the farmers market case different from a tragedy of the commons, a prisoner's dilemma, and a plain race to the bottom? I care more about the boundaries between these things than about getting a single word.
