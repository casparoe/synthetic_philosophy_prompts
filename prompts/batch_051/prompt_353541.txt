I'm a philosophy student currently taking a course on early modern moral philosophy, and we've spent several weeks on Hume's account of justice as an artificial virtue. He makes a claim in A Treatise of Human Nature, Book III, Part II, Section II that I think bears on an issue I care about outside class. Hume writes:

"So far from thinking, that men have no affection for any thing beyond themselves, I am of opinion, that tho' it be rare to meet with one, who loves any single person better than himself; yet 'tis as rare to meet with one, in whom all the kind affections, taken together, do not over-balance all the selfish."

That claim is supposed to explain why humans are generous enough to sustain social conventions yet partial enough to need them. I'd like a working definition built on, or at least compatible with, this picture. Specifically, define what makes an everyday convention morally binding on someone, especially on a visitor who did not grow up under it. Please test the definition against these eight cases: (1) tipping 20 percent at a sit-down restaurant in the United States; (2) deliberately not tipping in Tokyo, where tipping is not practiced; (3) joining the back of the queue at a London bus stop; (4) jaywalking across an empty street in a city where everyone locally does so; (5) removing your shoes at the door of a home in a culture where that is expected, though you come from a shoes-on culture; (6) giving a thumbs-up in a country where it is obscene; (7) standing on the left side of an escalator in a city where the convention is stand right, walk left; (8) leaving a cash tip on the table in a country where service is included and tipping is neither expected nor noticed. I think case (3) is clearly obligatory and case (6) is clearly not, and I'm genuinely unsure about the rest. Propose the definition, run it through all eight cases, say where it gives a wrong or surprising answer, and revise or defend it in light of the Hume passage. Finally, mark which of your verdicts are settled among philosophers working on this material and which are genuinely contested, and avoid presenting a contested view as the consensus.
