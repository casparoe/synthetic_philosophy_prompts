Gongsun Long, of the ancient Chinese School of Names, is remembered for defending the claim that a white horse is not a horse. On one common reconstruction, his point is not that a white horse fails to be an animal of the equine kind, but that the compound term white horse and the bare term horse sort things differently: if you are looking for a horse, a bay or black horse will serve, whereas if you are looking for a white horse, it will not, so the two names cannot be treated as equivalent.
