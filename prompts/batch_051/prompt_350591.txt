Everyone is anonymous: no names, no stated occupation, no age, no neighborhood, no reputation score. A moderation bot redacts any appeal to authority or experience — "as a teacher," "I own a business in this district," "I've lived here thirty years" — and replaces it with [appeal to status removed]. The only permitted moves are: assert a claim, give a reason, ask a question, or challenge a reason. And it runs slow: three messages per person per day, and no vote until the floor has been open for 30 days.

Does anonymity plus slowness actually neutralize power or just relocate it? Where does it break first, and can any of those failures be patched while keeping the Habermas core?
