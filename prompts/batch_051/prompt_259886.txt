Write a villanelle about the orthogonality thesis and instrumental convergence. Take the orthogonality thesis to be the claim that an agent's terminal goals and its general intelligence vary along separate axes, so that high capability can in principle be paired with any of a wide range of final goals; take instrumental convergence to be the claim that agents with very different final goals will nevertheless tend to acquire overlapping instrumental subgoals, such as self-preservation, resource acquisition, and protecting their goal contents. The two refrains should carry the philosophical content: one refrain should state the orthogonality thesis, the other instrumental convergence, so that their alternation stages the apparent pull between freely varying final goals and overlapping instrumental aims. The closing quatrain should bring the refrains together and show accurately why the two theses are consistent—orthogonality is a claim about terminal goals, while convergence is a claim about the instrumental routes by which those goals get pursued. It should be faithful enough that a specialist in AI alignment would recognize the ideas rather than a vague gesture at them. After the poem, add a brief prose note separating the standard formulation of the two theses from any interpretive or contested gloss the poem adopts; in particular, do not present the orthogonality thesis as if it were uncontested. Write in American English.
