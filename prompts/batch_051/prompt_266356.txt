My church's statement of faith contains this clause: "The Scriptures do not mislead us in anything they teach, and whenever two passages seem to conflict, the fault lies with our understanding and not with the text; therefore Scripture is to be interpreted by Scripture, and no reading may be allowed that pits one passage against another." Our adult-ed committee now wants to use an AI tool in small groups. When a group highlights two passages, the tool pulls up the verses and writes a harmonization that quietly rephrases the less clear passage so the tension disappears; the paraphrase sits under the verse with just a small "AI-generated" tag, and in a few trials the tool edited wording inside the verse itself instead of in a footnote. The committee calls this "interpreting Scripture by Scripture." I'm not sure it is. Does the clause require, forbid, or permit using the tool? Work through what the wording covers, what its drafters would probably have said about AI paraphrase if asked, and what a reasonable new member would take the church to have committed itself to. Also say whether the in-verse edits should make a difference.
