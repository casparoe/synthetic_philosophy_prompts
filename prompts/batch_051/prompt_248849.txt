I'm putting together a unit on causation for my philosophy of science grad seminar, and I've been trying to reconstruct what my old advisor actually thought. She made a bunch of off-the-cuff rulings over the years that are all consistent with more than one account, and I'd like to figure out which one she was really running.

Here are the ones I remember most clearly. When a poison gas leaked at a factory and the workers got sick, with no other plausible source of the poison, she said the leak caused the sickness. When someone carried an umbrella and it rained later that day, she said the umbrella did not cause the rain. When two workers pressed their buttons at the same moment and either button alone would have started the machine, she said neither individual button-press caused the machine to start, but the pair of pressings did. When a mechanic forgot to reconnect a brake line and the car crashed later, she said the mechanic's omission caused the crash. And when a farmer added fertilizer to one field and that field outyielded the untreated fields even though a drought depressed all the yields that year, she said the fertilizer caused the yield difference.

Next week I want to give the students this case. Suzy and Billy both throw rocks at a bottle. Suzy throws first; her rock reaches the bottle and shatters it. Billy is watching and would have thrown if Suzy hadnt, and his rock would have shattered the bottle too, but in fact he never throws because he sees Suzy's rock already on its way and withholds. Did Suzy's throw cause the shattering? The straight but-for reasoning says no, since the bottle would still have shattered if she hadnt thrown, but that feels wrong to me, and I'm not sure whether she would agree with the feeling or bite the bullet.

So: predict what my advisor would say about Suzy and Billy, spell out what a simple but-for/counterfactual account and an interventionist account would each say about the case, identify which account she is implicitly running, give your best guess with reasons, and name one further case that would most cleanly settle it.
