Analyze the following proposed rule for pausing frontier AI. A coalition of countries that fabricates nearly all cutting-edge AI chips creates an International Compute Registry. No training run above 10^26 FLOP (floating-point operations) may begin unless the developer has first filed a safety case with the registry and obtained approval after a twelve-month adversarial review period; the safety case must state the deployment restrictions under which the resulting model may later be released. While a safety case is pending, that developer may not begin any training run above 10^25 FLOP. A model trained under an approved safety case may be deployed only under the restrictions stated in that case, and developers or data centers caught violating the regime are barred from buying coalition-made chips and from using coalition cloud credits for five years. Assume the coalition can credibly verify reported FLOP counts and chip flows, but cannot prevent a rival great power from training domestically on its own less advanced chips. Walk through how this scheme would actually behave: how labs restructure training, where the first gaming appears, how the safety-case process gets captured or becomes theater, how the rules interact with open-weight releases and fine-tuning, and whether the regime slows frontier capability growth or merely relocates and relabels it. Return your analysis as a JSON object with the following fields:

- "headline_assessment": a one-sentence overall verdict.
- "first_order_effects": an array of objects, each with keys "effect", "direction" (one of "increase", "decrease", "shift", "ambiguous"), "confidence" (one of "low", "medium", "high"), and "mechanism".
- "gaming_paths": an array of objects, each with keys "path", "actor", "severity" (one of "low", "medium", "high"), and "likely_countermeasure".
- "second_order_effects": an array of objects, each with keys "effect", "mechanism", and "confidence".
- "frontier_growth_verdict": an object with keys "pace" (one of "slowed", "unchanged", "accelerated", "ambiguous"), "confidence", and "why".
- "fixes": an array of objects, each with keys "change", "problem_addressed", and "new_risk".
