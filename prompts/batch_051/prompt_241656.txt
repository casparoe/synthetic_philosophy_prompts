(3) It is possible that God exists: the concept of a being with every perfection is coherent, so there is at least one possible world at which God exists. Formally: ◇G.

(4) From (2) and (3) it follows that God possibly necessarily exists: ◇□G.

(5) In the modal system S5, whatever is possibly necessary is actually necessary: ◇□G → □G.

(6) And whatever is actually necessary is actually the case: □G → G.

(7) Therefore God actually exists.
