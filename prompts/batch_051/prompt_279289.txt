Suppose that a consortium of cognitive science funding bodies, worried that representational vocabulary has become too cheap to invoke, introduces the following rule as a five-year trial. Any grant application in cognitive science, cognitive neuroscience, or artificial intelligence that uses the terms representation, internal model, mental content, symbol, code, or information processing must append a one-page explanatory statement. For each such term, the statement must specify (i) the vehicle that carries the state; (ii) the content carried and what fixes that content; (iii) the process by which the content is produced and updated; (iv) the consumer inside the system that reads or reacts to the state; and (v) one observation that would be unlikely if the claimed representational state were absent. Proposals that cannot meet these conditions must instead recast the relevant explanation in non-representational language, for example in terms of coupled dynamical systems, ecological information, or sensorimotor invariants. The rule is intended to operationalise the enactivist and embodied-cognition complaint that representational explanations often postulate inner states whose content, vehicle, and consumer are never made explicit, while leaving genuinely representational work intact.

Treat this as a mechanism to be analysed, not a moral position to be endorsed or condemned. What does it incentivise among researchers and reviewers? How would a well-run laboratory satisfy the letter of the rule while leaving its representational commitments unchanged, and what does that reveal about the rule? Which research programmes would be systematically favoured or burdened, and what would happen to the framing of findings that sit uncomfortably between representational and non-representational description? Would the rule tend to select for substantive, testable uses of representational vocabulary, or merely for rhetorical fluency in a different idiom? Finally, identify the single most likely failure mode and say whether it could be repaired without abandoning the rule.
