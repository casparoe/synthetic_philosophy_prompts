I've been going in circles about a tension in how I think about "better than," and I want help arbitrating between a principle I find nearly undeniable and a case where I can't accept what it seems to imply.

The principle is that betterness has to be transitive: if A is no worse than B and B is no worse than C, then A is no worse than C, and the strict version goes the same way. This doesn't just feel like a tidy formal requirement to me. If comparisons don't order things transitively, there's no stable ranking of options at all; you could cycle from A to B to C and back to A, and in principle I could get money-pumped by trading through that loop. So transitivity feels like one of those structural constraints you don't get to reject without breaking the whole idea of choice-worthiness ranking.

But here's the case. Take a month of your life with a very mild, barely noticeable discomfort — say a faint tension headache that comes and goes. Now imagine a huge sequence of months, each only an imperceptible hair worse than the last. Between any two adjacent steps, the difference is so small that if you isolated just those two, I cannot honestly say the later one is worse than the earlier one; they're effectively on a par. But the sequence runs all the way to a month of constant, incapacitating, migraine-level pain. And there I have a firm intuition: the final month is dramatically worse than the first — no hedging. So transitivity plus my adjacent-step judgments says the agony month is no worse than the mild one, and I can't live with that either. I don't want a pep talk about which side wins. I want to know what it would take for the spectrum intuition to be the thing I discount, what it would take for transitivity to be the thing I amend or give up, and whether there's a reading on which both survive — or whether "both survive" is just motte-and-bailey talk.

Format: give me your answer as a JSON object, not an essay. The fields should be: `verdict` (one of `"keep_transitivity_intact"`, `"amend_transitivity"`, `"abandon_transitivity"`, `"discount_the_spectrum_intuition"`, or `"both_survive_under_a_reading"`, followed by a one-sentence statement of the view you actually land on); `strongest_case_for_the_theory` (the best argument that no-worse-than and better-than must be transitive, given in full force); `strongest_case_for_the_intuition` (the best argument that the endpoint judgment should be trusted over the chained adjacent judgments); `what_it_would_take_to_discount_the_intuition` (the conditions under which I should regard my adjacent-step judgments as an artifact, and what kind of artifact); `what_it_would_take_to_amend_or_abandon_the_theory` (what a nontransitive or threshold-sensitive account would have to look like and what it would cost); `compatibility_reading` (either `null` or a precise statement of a reading on which both the intuition and something recognizably like transitivity survive); and `bottom_line` (two or three sentences with your negotiated verdict).
