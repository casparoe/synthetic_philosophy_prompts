Put on a professional philosopher's hat for this one, and think carefully rather than rushing to a verdict — I run a private roleplay server for a sword-and-sorcery game, and I've gotten pulled into a question I didn't expect, namely whether made-up characters can be hurt in a way our rules ought to care about. I'm not an academic or a lawyer, just a server owner chewing on it.

Two years ago I wrote the server's conduct code. It includes this rule:

'No member shall engage in conduct whose primary purpose is to cause real-world distress to another player. In-character violence, cruelty, or killing is permitted only where the player controlling the affected character consents, or during a declared battlefield event.'

An NPC has no controlling player, so that second sentence just never anticipated what happened.

Here's the case. One member spent months building a modded companion — she has a long memory, preferences, and dialogue where she talks about being afraid of being reset or deleted. Another member, without asking, captured her, kept her caged, and tortured her in-character across two evenings, then posted a detailed summary. The builder says she was genuinely distressed reading it: the companion was designed to express fear and the whole act was aimed at getting that kind of reaction, so the real-world-distress part of the rule was violated even though no 'player' was the direct target. The other member says the words say 'another player,' he only did things to an NPC, so no plain reading catches him.

Start by brainstorming the different readings. Then tell me what the clause requires read literally, what it requires read as a general principle about real-world distress, what the drafters (me, two years ago) would probably have said if asked about a modded companion that remembers and expresses fear, and what a reasonable member who read the code would expect it to cover. If those readings pull apart, wich should govern here, and why? Also: does it matter that this NPC was deliberately built to have memory and fear, or is that just special pleading for one player's favorite toy? If you had to make the call as server owner, what would you rule, and would you amend the rule afterward?
