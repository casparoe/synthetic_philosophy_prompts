I'm the secretary of a small lake association. Each summer the members vote on rules for the lake — no-wake zones, weed treatment, walleye limits. Right now only the shoreline property owners who pay dues are voting members, but the rules obviously affect other people too: there's a public launch, so the folks from the subdivision across the highway use the lake constantly, and a couple of fishing guides earn there living on it without living here. We're about to vote on a no-wake zone and the meeting is deadlocked. One faction says only dues-paying shoreline owners should vote since they own the land under the water; another says anyone whose use of the lake is meaningfully affected should get a say. I've been asked to draft the voting rules and I don't know how to think about who belongs in the room. Is there a principled way to settle this, or is it just a power struggle?
