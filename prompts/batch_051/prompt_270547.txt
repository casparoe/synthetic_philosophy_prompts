I am drafting a paper on the foundations of risk and would like a consistency audit of the following commitments before I show it to others. I am not sure which of these are doing the real work, which are in tension, and which can be kept together cheaply. Please go through them as a set.

1. For one-off monetary gambles, expected utility theory is the correct account: rational preferences can be represented as maximizing expected utility for some concave utility function over wealth, and risk aversion just is diminishing marginal utility of wealth.

2. For a favorable bet that will be offered many times, a rational agent should bet the full Kelly fraction — the fraction of wealth that maximizes expected log wealth, E[log W] (for a favorable even-money bet with win probability p, this fraction is 2p − 1) — rather than maximize expected wealth, which stakes too much and sacrifices long-run growth.

3. I regard Kelly betting as consistent with expected utility theory, since maximizing E[log W] is equivalent to maximizing expected utility with logarithmic utility. I therefore do not think geometric rationality names a separate norm competing with expected utility theory.

4. But I also see no reason to privilege logarithmic utility among concave utility functions. Different degrees of diminishing marginal utility are all permissible, and someone whose utility over wealth is more or less concave than log is not making a mistake.

5. For a single favorable bet that I know will not be repeated, I think the full Kelly fraction is often too timid: what to stake depends on one's utility function over wealth, and an agent with sufficiently low relative risk aversion — whose marginal utility of wealth diminishes slowly — can rationally stake more than Kelly.

6. I also think the framing of a series of bets should not matter: whether it is described as a sequence of independent gambles or as a single compound gamble over final wealth is mere bookkeeping, not a change in the decision problem.

Which of these commitments are genuinely in tension with which, and which pairs only appear to be? Please order the genuine tensions by severity rather than treating every pair as equally problematic, and for each say what revision would resolve it and what that revision costs. Finally, tell me which single revision would do the most to make the whole set hang together.
