Consider the claim that many competitions described as races to the bottom—jurisdictions cutting corporate taxes, platforms loosening content moderation, firms eroding safety standards—are multipolar traps: outcomes each participant would prefer to avoid, but which no participant can unilaterally prevent. The skeptic replies that this is a disguised value judgment; if actors keep choosing the lower standard, that reveals what they actually prefer, and "trap" merely records the observer's displeasure. Choose one concrete case and design the strongest empirical test that could separate these readings.
