I'm taking a course on the philosophy of science, and we recently read Goodman's new riddle of induction. I'm a bit uneasy about how our textbook summarizes it, and I want to know whether my unease is justified before I write my response paper. The textbook says:

"Goodman asks us to imagine the predicate 'grue,' which applies to an object just in case it is first observed before the year 2050 and is green, or is not first observed before 2050 and is blue. Since every emerald examined so far has been green and has been examined before 2050, all our evidence supports both the hypothesis that all emeralds are green and the hypothesis that all emeralds are grue. From the grue hypothesis it follows that emeralds first examined after 2050 will be blue. Goodman's point is that no amount of evidence or logic can tell us which hypothesis to prefer; induction is rational only relative to a set of predicates we happen to have used in the past, with 'green' entrenched and 'grue' not. So induction rests on linguistic habit, not on the way the world is."

I was discussing this with a friend who is a physics student, and he said the whole problem is overblown: grue is a gerrymandered, time-indexed predicate, whereas green is a purely qualitative property. He thinks induction is only ever meant to range over qualitative, non-indexical predicates, so excluding grue at the outset is not a question-begging move, just a proper specification of the domain of induction. He added that Goodman's entrenchment story gives exactly the wrong diagnosis, because what matters is not a word's history of use but whether a predicate picks out a genuine similarity in nature.

I'm not sure who to trust. I'd like you to go through the textbook's summary sentence by sentence: what is fair to Goodman, what is subtly wrong or overstated, and whether the conclusions drawn from it survive correction. Also, please evaluate my friend's reply — is restricting induction to qualitative, non-indexical predicates a real solution to the grue problem, or does it beg the question against Goodman? I need to know how much of this I can safely use in my paper.
