My friend's a lawyer, and he says laws that are technically still on the books but never enforced are in 'desuetude'—a word I had to look up. He insists they still do real work: people act as if they might get invoked, agencies route decisions around them, and the rule of law isn't just about what actually gets prosecuted. To me that's a dead code path: nothing ever calls it, so if you deleted it the system would behave the same. I want to know whether his claim is testable, and what the test would and wouldn't settle. Design the strongest test you can of whether an unenforced law is doing any actual work rather than just sitting their. Say what result would count for his view and what for mine, and be explicit about what a clean result would leave unresolved—like whether that influence is a good thing, or whether the law is 'really law' in the sense the rule-of-law people care about.
