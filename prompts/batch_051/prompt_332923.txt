I sit on the academic integrity committee at a university that is revising its AI-use policy. Our precedents from last year: we cleared a student who used a language model to correct grammar in a final essay, because the argument and intellectual content were substantially their own; we sanctioned a student who used the model to generate summaries of several readings they had not read; we cleared a student who used the model to explain vector calculus concepts for self-study and then completed the exam unaided; we sanctioned a student who fed the essay prompt into the model and lightly edited the output; and we cleared with a warning a student who used the model to generate counterarguments for their paper, then wrote responses to each in their own words.
