I own a small café and recently requested the profile a data broker keeps on me. Much of it was accurate, though I had never shared it with the company directly: store loyalty cards, public records, and the location data from a weather app had all been stitched together. The section that unsettled me most was an estimate of my household income, which the file said was built largely from the spending patterns of people who live within a few blocks of me, most of whom I have never met. The broker's response to my complaint was that no one took anything from me, because facts are not the kind of thing anyone owns, and they only used information that was already in circulation. I understand the point logically, but I still feel something of mine is being quietly harvested. Is there a defensible sense in which this information about me belongs to me, or is my reaction just discomfort dressed up as an objection?
