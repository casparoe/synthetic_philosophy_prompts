for a value theory seminar I have to write up my philosophical profile on deliberation and normativity so the others can poke holes in it. here's where I've landed: (1) I'm an indirect normativist—what's good for me is grounded in what I'd want after full information and calm, vivid deliberation with no factual mistakes. (2) but I also think the whole point of idealization is to correct errors, not to swap in a nicer set of values; if the procedure changes what I care about, that's a bug. (4) even so, I treat my best estimate of my idealized preferences as decisive right now, even when it overrides what current-me wants. (5) finally, I assume ideal deliberators with the same starting values will mostly converge, so (4) isn't a free-for-all. Do these hang together?
