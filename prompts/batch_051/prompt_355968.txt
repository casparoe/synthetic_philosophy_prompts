I work on model evaluations at an AI company, and I just had a long argument with a friend about whether the chain-of-thought text our models produce is real reasoning or theater. The practice we kept coming back to is this: our safety team trains models to emit a step-by-step chain of thought before answering, and then treats that chain of thought as evidence about what is actually going on inside the model — whether it is reasoning soundly, deceiving us, or pandering to reviewers; monitors and classifiers are trained on those traces. At the same time, the same models are optimized with reinforcement learning on the quality of the final answer, which rewards the answer and not the truthfulness of the displayed reasoning. I came away unsure what this practice is actually committed to.
