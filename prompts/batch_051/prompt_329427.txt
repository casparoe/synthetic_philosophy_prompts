I keep seeing the word "complex" used like it picks out a real feature of the object itself, and I want a working definition of a complpex object I can actually defend. I've come across this way of measuring it: the complexity of a thing is the minimum number of assembly steps you'd need to build it from some basic parts, where you're allowed to reuse chunks you've already put together. A single rock comes out near zero, a watch comes out fairly high. But I can already tell it'll do something weird with randomness, and maybe that's fine or maybe it shows the whole measure is tracking the wrong thing. I also know info theory would just say a random string maximises information, but information and complexity don't feel like the same thing to me.

Give me a definition of a complex object that runs through all of these and say where it surprises you:

- a smooth quartz pebble (not complex, I'd have thought)
- a snowflake (looks intricate, but it's just water crystalizing; complex or not?)
- a shuffled deck of 52 cards (feels complex, but probably not in the way a watch is)
- a 10,000-character string from a true random generator (maximal disorder; is that as complex as it gets, or the opposite?)
- a printed novel (complex)
- the same novel stored as an epub file on an otherwise blank drive (is the novel complex, or just the text?)
- a bacterial genome (complex)
- that same genome with the order of all its genes randomly shuffled (more complex, less, or a different kind of thing? my gut says it matters but I can't say why)
- one folded protein
- a working wristwatch vs. the same watch after being crushed into a box of loose parts (same pieces, but something seems to have been lost)
- a sand dune (feels structured but not complex)
- a single random 10,000-character string vs. a billion identical copies of it (does copying change how complex the thing is? I've seen people bolt on a "number of copies" condition for detecting life, but I don't see why copy count should change how complex an individual object is)

I want the definition to actually do the work in each case, not just sort them by vibes. If some cases force you to split "complex" into two or three different senses, say so and tell me which cases move between them.
