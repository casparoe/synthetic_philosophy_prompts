There's another group on the other side of the world pushing for the same pretty obscure cause I've been working on. We can't really communicate — anything I send them gets read or goes missing, and theres no way to make an enforcable deal. A traveler who passed through their city got a message to me: they're about to do a costly thing that would help my side a lot, and they're hoping I'll do a costly thing that would help their side a lot. Neither of us can ever check whether the other actually came through.

Now here's what I keep getting stuck on. I'm fairly sure they're basically the same kind of person as me, under the same pressures, and would think about this the same way I do. But as far as I can see, nothing I decide can reach them or change what they do. So the obvious move is to do nothing and just hope they do the costly thing anyway. Except if that's the obvious move for me, it's the obvious move for them too — and then nobody does anything.

Is there a rational reason to do my part anyway? Or should I accept that doing nothing is just the smart play? I'd love an answer that takes the weirdness seriously and doesn't just tell me to follow my gut. Please write in American English.
