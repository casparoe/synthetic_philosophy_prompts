So I've got this standing bet: a guy I know offers me even money on a coin flip every week, and I'm allowed to stake any amount I want each time. The coin is biased in my favor—it comes up heads 60% of the time, and I get to call heads. I've been staking a flat $20 because I'm scared of a bad streak. A stats friend of mine says that's leaving money on the table: each dollar I stake has positive expected value, so by the law of large numbers I'll clean up in the long run, and to maximize expected value I should bet as much as possible. But if I go all in every week, a single tails wipes me to zero, and I'm pretty sure a long series of all-in bets makes ruin nearly certain no matter how good my edge is. He responds that maximizing expected value is just what rationality means. Is he wrong, and is there a principled answer to how much of my bankroll I should stake each week?
