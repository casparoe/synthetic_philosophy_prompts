Consider two views about left and right. On the second, the labels are inherited sorting devices: they bundle whatever issues, interests, and identities happen to travel together in a given country and era, and there is no single thing that all left positions or all right positions share across contexts. Your task is to design the best feasible empirical test that could distinguish these views.
