so i just got roped into teaching the animal ethics unit of our intro course next semester because the person who usualy teaches it is on sabbatical, and my sister chose this exact month to go vegan and start sending me videos of pigs in farrowing crates. so now i actually have to say out loud what i think, and it's less tidy than i'd like. here are the committments i keep going back and forth on:

(a) i'm broadly kantian. the fundamental moral requirement is something like: treat rational nature as an end in itself, never merely as a means. only beings who can set ends for themselves and be held responsible can be the direct objects of moral duties.
(b) nonhuman animals, even the smart ones, aren't rational agents in that sense. so we have no direct duties to them.
(c) but the kantian indirect-duty line seems right to me as far as it goes: cruelty to animals is wrong because it damages the cruel person's capacity for sympathy and makes them more likely to mistreat humans. the duty is really a duty to humanity in oneself, merely with regard to the animal.
(d) i also think factory farming is one of the great moral evils of our time, and what makes it evil is the suffering of the animals themselves. the effects on human workers and consumers are real but they're not the main thing.
(e) if someone tortured a stray dog in a way no human would ever find out about, and it somehow had no spillover effects on how they treat people, i'd still say it was wrong.
(f) i don't think animals are mere things, and i don't think anything is permitted in how we treat them. but i'm suspicious of saying they have rights, because rights in the full sense seem to belong to beings who can claim them or waive them.

i'd like an audit rather than a list of every possible quibble. go through these and tell me which pairs are actually in tension and which only look like they are, rank the real tensions by how severe they are, and for each say what the cheapest fix would be and what it costs. then tell me which single revision would make the whole set hang together with the least damage.
