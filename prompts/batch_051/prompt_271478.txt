- A manager calls a Black employee "articulate" as a genuine compliment and seems confused about why it landed badly. I'm torn: my gut says it's racist in effect, but punishing him for intent feels wrong. Maybe it's still racist, just the lowest grade.
- A Black comedian uses a slur about himself on stage. Not racist.
- A guy at a party repeats the punchline of a racist joke to mock how dumb the joke is, clearly not endorsing it. Not racist.

What's the difference — is it that one is making the claim himself while the other is mentioning someone else's?
