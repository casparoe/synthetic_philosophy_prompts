My working view is that LLM hallucinations are best understood not as lies and not as ordinary factual errors of the sort a careful speaker occasionally makes, but as something close to what Harry Frankfurt characterized as bullshit: the model emits assertion-shaped text while the truth-value of the output is not the thing governing its production, and the attitude of care for how things really are is absent. What would count against this classification? Say what a defender of this position should themselves concede as counting against it, what the classification predicts about which hallucinations occur and when that rival classifications—lying, ordinary error—do not, and whether the view has been or easily could be insulated against counterexamples, and whether that insulation is a defect in a classification of this kind.
