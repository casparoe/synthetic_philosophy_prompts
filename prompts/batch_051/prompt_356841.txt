I have been trying to write about the difference between the Nordic model, legalization in Germany and the Netherlands, and decriminalization in New Zealand, and I keep hitting the same obstacle: every side of the debate leans on the word exploitation, but they seem to apply it to different things depending on which policy they already favor. I do not want a definition that simply means bad, unfair, or underpaid, because that would settle the debate before it starts. I need a working definition of exploitation that can be applied the same way across policy regimes and is not just a label for whichever outcome I dislike. If you think the word does more than one job, for example one descriptive and one condemnatory, separate those jobs and say which one is doing the work in each case.

Please test your definition against each of the following cases and say what it classifies as exploitation, what it classifies as not exploitation, and where it gives no clear verdict: (1) a person with a secure income and several other job options who sells sex because they prefer it to other work, works independently, and keeps all their earnings; (2) a person who is physically controlled by a pimp who takes everything they earn and punishes any resistance; (3) a person who would otherwise be evicted and who agrees to a low price from a client who knows this; (4) a performer on an online platform that takes a 20 percent cut of their income while offering little protection against stalking or blackmail; (5) a worker in a legal German brothel where the owner takes a large share and the worker's residency permit depends on staying in that job; (6) a person selling sex under the Nordic model, where buying is criminalized but selling is not, who says they chose the work but resent being treated as a victim by the law; (7) a worker who reports genuinely enjoying the work but receives only a small fraction of what the client pays an agency.

Then step back and tell me which of your verdicts you are least confident about, and whether your definition actually helps state what is at stake between the Nordic model and full decriminalization, or whether it merely relabels the underlying disagreement.
