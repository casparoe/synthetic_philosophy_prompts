In my art class we kept arguing about whether a store-bought urinal signed with a fake name and displayed in a museum is art, and I realized we were arguing without any shared definition of the word. I would like a working definition of art that gets these cases right: a symphony written for a concert hall; a sunset; a child's crayon drawing taped to a refrigerator; that same signed urinal in the museum; a hand-thrown ceramic mug made for everyday use; an image generated by an AI from a written prompt; a banana duct-taped to a gallery wall; a video game with elaborately designed landscapes and music; and an unmade bed exhibited in a gallery. I am confident the symphony is art and the sunset is not, and I have no settled view on the others. Propose a definition, run it through all nine cases, and then tell me which of your verdicts is least secure and whether the definition should be revised or the surprising verdict defended.
