A friend and I have been going back and forth about how to slot large language models into Dennett's kinds of minds. She says a GPT-style model is basically a Skinnerian creature scaled up: nobody ever gave it an explicit inner model of the world to test hypotheses against, and the whole training story is reinforcement — backprop plus RLHF is just a fancy operant conditioning chamber, so the model emits whatever got rewarded. I think that undersells what happens during pretraining. The trained transformer seems to contain something like an internal world model, and when it generates a continuation it can "try out" candidates in representational space before committing to a token, which sounds a lot like Popperian hypothesis-testing (with the added Gregorian twist that it's soaked in public language). My credence that a trained LLM is primarily a Popperian creature rather than a Skinnerian one is about 0.65; hers is about 0.25.

Before you give your own number, let me pin down what I mean by "primarily": most of the interesting, novel behavior is best explained by internal selection among candidate representations, rather than blind reinforcement of emitted outputs. Give me your credence for that proposition, but more importantly decompose it. In particular: how much weight do you put on (a) whether the Darwinian–Skinnerian–Popperian–Gregorian taxonomy even applies to artifacts as opposed to evolved organisms; (b) whether autoregressive next-token prediction counts as hypothesis-testing or just as conditioned emission; (c) whether the model's apparent world model is doing real work or is mostly memorized associations; (d) whether RLHF makes the overall system more Skinnerian; and (e) whether chain-of-thought reasoning moves a system into Gregorian territory, and whether that helps or hurts the Popperian classification. Assign a rough probability to each, say how they combine into your overall number, and identify which one most likely explains why someone at 0.8 and someone at 0.2 disagree. Also tell me what one empirical finding would move your number most, and in which direction.
