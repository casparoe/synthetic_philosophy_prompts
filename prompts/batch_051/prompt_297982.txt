I'm running a debate night on sex-work policy and want to hand people a sorting quiz called something like "which legal model are you actually committed to." It should sort by answers to cases and underlying claims, not by whether someone already likes the sound of "Nordic model" or "decrim." The leaves I need are: full criminalization (selling and buying both illegal), the Nordic/equality model (selling decriminalized, buying criminalized), legalization/regulation in the German or Dutch style (licensed brothels, mandatory health checks, zoning, etc.), and full decriminalization New Zealand-style (sex work treated like ordinary work under standard employment and health law). Please do 9 or 10 questions, each built around a short concrete scenario — a street-based worker, a licensed brothel, a migrant worker without papers, someone who left the industry and describes it as coercion, an indoor worker who insists it's freely chosen, a trafficking victim — with two or three answer options each, phrased so no one needs the labels to answer. Then give a scoring key mapping answer patterns onto those four positions. Build the questions out of a few splitting issues: whether the wrong (if there is one) is intrinsic to selling sex or comes from how the trade is regulated; whether the main harm is the work itself or its criminal/stigmatized status; whether the state's job is to shrink demand or to make working conditions safe. Flag any two positions the quiz can't cleanly seperate, and for unoccupied combinations say whether they're empty because the view is incoherent or just unpopular. And none of the questions should be a straight "is sex work work?" style label-check. Keep the whole thing under 900 words.
