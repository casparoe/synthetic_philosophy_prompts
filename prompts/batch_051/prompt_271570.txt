I'm on the board of a small housing co-op, and last month at the end of a brutal three-hour Zoom meeting I got fed up and said something like "fine, I quit the board," half laughing, more like the way you'd say "I'm done with this." My camera was off and I honestly thought I was muted, so I was basically venting to an empty room. Turns out I wasn't muted. The secretary wrote it into the minutes as a resignation, and the chair emailed the next day to say my resignation had been accepted and my seat was vacant.

I told them I didn't intend to resign and that I was just blowing off steam; they say that doesn't matter becuase the words themselves did the resigning, and the bylaws don't require a written notice or a second confirmation. My friend thinks I can't have resigned by accident, since resigning is something you do on purpose. The board thinks the act was complete once it was heard and recorded. Who's right? Does it matter that nobody could see my face or that I thought I was talking to myself?
