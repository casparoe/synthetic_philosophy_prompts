The standard story is that Occam's razor gets a principled footing from Kolmogorov complexity and Solomonoff induction: simpler theories are just the ones with shorter programs, and Solomonoff's universal prior — a mixture over all programs weighted by 2^{-length} — is supposed to be the optimal way to make that precise. The usual defense when someone worries that this all depends on the choice of universal Turing machine is that the choice only changes the the prior by a constant factor, and the celebrated convergence result says the mixture dominates every computable environment, so in the limit the machine-choice washes out and induction "reduces" to the razor.
