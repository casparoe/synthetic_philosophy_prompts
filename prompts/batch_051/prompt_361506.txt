His argument goes: (1) In chaotic systems, arbitrarily small differences in initial conditions grow exponentially over time. (2) Any actual measurement of initial conditions has finite precision, so there's always some error. (4) Therefore, beyond some finite time horizon, you cannot predict the system's state from your measurements of its initial state. (5) If you cannot predict a system's state even in principle, then the system is not deterministic. (6) So chaos shows that classical systems like the weather are not deterministic, despite their governing equations being deterministic.
