A human therapist who deliberately chooses the timing, framing, and emotional register of what she says, in order to move a patient toward accepting a difficult truth or giving up self-destructive behavior, is normally described as skilled, even caring. Many people who praise the therapist describe this second case as manipulation or coercion-by-prediction, and think it should be tightly restricted or banned outright. What, if anything, justifies treating the two cases differently?
