Hey, I'm a software engineer, not a philosopher, but I've been going down a rabbit hole on entropy and the arrow of time. Here's roughly where I've landed:

1. The underlying microscopic laws (Newtonian mechanics, standard quantum mechanics) are time-symmetric, or at least they don't pick out a direction of time.
2. Entropy increases toward the future. In fact, I think 'the future' just is the direction in which entropy is higher.
3. The arrow of time is explained by the Past Hypothesis: the universe began in an extremely low-entropy macrostate, and that boundary condition plus the symmetric laws does the explanatory work. We don't need an extra time-asymmetric law.
4. The usual statistical-mechanics assumption that the actual microstate is random within the macrostate is part of the package. Without it, I realize the Past Hypothesis by itself isn't enough to get the arrow, but I don't think of it as an additional law.
5. Memory and records are reliable because of this: given a low-entropy past, a present record (a notebook, a log file, a photograph) is overwhelmingly more likely to tell you about the past than the future.
6. If I saw a glass un-shatter on video, I'd say the video was reversed or faked, not that entropy spontaneously went down in the sense that matters.
7. Entropy is defined relative to a choice of coarse-graining, and that's fine; it doesn't threaten the story.
8. Time is real and objective; whether an event is past or future is not a projection of my psychology.

I keep worrying that (8) sits badly with (2), (3), and (7), and that (5) might be assuming something that (1) rules out. But I can't tell which of these are real tensions, which only look like tensions, and which ones are doing the real work. So please audit this set. Tell me which pairs or groups actually pull against each other, rank the tensions by severity rather than listing them all as equal, say which ones dissolve on inspection, and for each real tension give the cheapest fix and what it costs. The thing I most want to know is whether this package is coherent or whether I'm fooling myself about objective time and the observed arrow.
