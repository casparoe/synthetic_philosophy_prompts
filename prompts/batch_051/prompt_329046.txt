I am a doctoral student working in the social epistemology of online reputation systems, and I have been unable to find a precise term for a pattern that recurs in my fieldwork. It is not the familiar worry that people manipulate a metric once it becomes a target; the shift is subtler.

Two instances may make it clear. On a discussion platform I studied, an upvote was introduced as a report of quality: users said they pressed it when something was worth another person's time. Once the platform attached public totals, sorting privileges, and badges to votes, the same users began describing the same button differently. Upvoting became encouragement, a thank-you, or a way to keep a professional relationship warm, and downvoting was reserved for outright misconduct because it was read as a personal slight. The interface still called the score a measure of quality, but the practice no longer answered that question.

The same pattern appeared on a peer-to-peer service platform with mutual public ratings. Within eighteen months, scores converged toward the maximum. The raters I interviewed did not describe themselves as lying. One explained that leaving five stars meant making sure the transaction could end well, not that the service had been flawless. Buyers, for their part, had learned to read a seller's score as a signal of agreeableness rather than of the quality of the work. The designers continued to treat the scores as evaluations.

The general phenomenon seems to be this: a rating introduced as a report of a property is quietly renegotiated by its users into a move in a social exchange—a courtesy, a repayment, a gesture of solidarity—while the interface continues to present it as an evaluation. The score comes unstuck from what it was built to measure, but not because anyone is gaming it.

Does this transformation have an established name in the philosophy of measurement, social epistemology, or the study of quantification? I am not content with a bare label. If it is a special case of some broader idea about metrics, or of evaluation inflation, I need to know exactly how the present phenomenon is narrower, and where its boundaries fall. If nothing established fits, I would welcome a proposed term with a short definition and a boundary case. Please keep your answer to no more than 350 words.
