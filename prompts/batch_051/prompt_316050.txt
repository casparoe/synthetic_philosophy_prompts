But the very same office sends out extremely blunt, plain emails about tuition deadlines, late fees, and parking fines. Orwell would read the first habit as a political use of language meant to make something harsh look neutral or even good, and he'd say that kind of language corrupts thought, not just prose. My question is whether the university's practice actually presupposes that view or something weaker. Reconstruct the implicit theory of how language relates to political thought that the practice commits the administration to, distinguish what the practice strictly presupposes from what it merely fits with, say whether that theory is closer to Orwell's or in tension with it, and tell me what the administration would have to change if Orwell's view were applied consistently. Give me exactly five numbered points, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion.
