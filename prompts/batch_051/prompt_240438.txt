I've spent the last few months reading around the defintion of terrorism, and I keep hitting the same wall: the debate has been going in something like its current form since the 1970s, there are dozens of definitions in circulation, and the field doesn't seem to have converged on anything. What's strange is that the basic cases aren't the problem. Everyone agrees that the Oklahoma City bombing and the 2005 London bombings are terrorism, that a mugging is not, and that an ordinary battlefield firefight between uniformed forces is not. But the moment you move to the edges, the consensus evaporates. Some theorists say states can't commit terrorism by definition, so a government that bombs a neighborhood to make the population turn against rebels is committing a war crime or a crime against humanity, but not terrorism. Others say that's exactly terrorism, and that writing states out of the definition rigs the whole project. Some make the target load-bearing: violence against noncombatants. Others make the purpose load-bearing: violence meant to coerce an audience through fear. Others make the perpetrator load-bearing: only non-state actors. And a few say the word is so thoroughly evaluative that a neutral definition was never on the table. The facts about any particular attack aren't usually in dispute; what's in dispute is which features of the act should determine whether the label applies and why it matters so much. My working theory is that this isn't a factual disagreement at all — it's a disagreement about which features carry the normative weight of the word, plus a turf war over who gets to use it against whom. Is that right? I want a diagnosis, not a survey: what kind of disagreement is this really — verbal, evaluative, practical, or something like two groups answering different questions under one word — and which side would each diagnosis favor? And given your diagnosis, what would actually count as progress: a stipulation, a distinction, an agreed set of paradigm cases, or giving up on a single neutral definition?
