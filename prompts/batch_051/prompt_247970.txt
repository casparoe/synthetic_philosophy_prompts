- One being at (3, 7) vs one being at (7, 3): exactly equal.
- Two beings each at (5, 5) vs one being at (7, 7): the first is better.
- Two beings each at (2, 8) vs one being at (5, 5): the first is better.

Next she's deciding about one being at (4, 4) vs one being at (8, 1). What do you think she'll say? I care less about the verdict itself then about the rule she's running. The obvious candidates are additive (add consciousness + happiness within each being, then sum across beings), multiplicative (multiply them within each being, then sum), or lexical (consciousness always wins, happiness only breaks ties). I can't tell which of those fits her track record, and I suspect more than one does. So: which candidate rules fit all four verdicts, what does each one predict for the (4, 4) vs (8, 1) case, which would you bet on and why, and what further comparison would cleanly settle it?
