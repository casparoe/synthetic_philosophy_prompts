High-energy physics has developed a stable institutional pattern: leading journals, hiring committees, and funding panels continue to treat string theory and some multiverse research programs as central, even though neither has produced a confirmed novel empirical prediction after decades of work. The standard defense is not usually that a decisive test is imminent. It is that the theory is mathematically highly constrained, that it unifies existing physics, that it may be the only known consistent ultraviolet completion of gravity, or that a landscape of vacua explains the apparent fine-tuning of our universe.

I am not asking you to adjudicate whether this is good science or whether string theory is true. I want to know what this practice presupposes about scientific evidence.

Reconstruct the implicit epistemology of the practice as precisely as you can. In particular: what gives a theory credence on this picture; what role, if any, empirical test plays; which rival views of theory confirmation the practice quietly rules out; where the practice is underdetermined and is compatible with several rival epistemologies; and what would have to change in the practice if one replaced the implicit view with a straightforwardly falsificationist view.

After the main answer, give me a JSON object with the following fields:

- "practice": a one-sentence description of the institutional pattern being analyzed.
- "implicit_theory": the reconstructed epistemology in no more than three sentences.
- "strict_presuppositions": an array of claims the practice cannot easily survive without.
- "compatible_rival_views": an array of views consistent with the practice but not required by it.
- "empirical_test_role": the role empirical testing plays under the reconstructed view.
- "underdetermined_points": an array of points where the practice does not decide between rival epistemologies.
- "changes_under_falsificationism": an array of concrete changes to funding, publication, and hiring that would follow from replacing the implicit epistemology with a strict falsificationist one.
