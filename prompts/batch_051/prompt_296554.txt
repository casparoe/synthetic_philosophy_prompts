I am preparing a paper on economic justice and want a consistency audit of a position I currently hold, before I try to defend it. The position is the following package of six claims.

(1) I accept Nozick's Wilt Chamberlain argument: if a distribution arises by voluntary transfers from a just starting point, it is just, however unequal it becomes; so no end-state or patterned principle of distributive justice can be realized without continuous interference in people's free choices, because people are free to disturb any favored pattern through their own transfers.

(2) Justice in holdings is therefore purely historical. A distribution is just only if it came about through just original acquisitions, just transfers, and rectification of whatever injustices occurred along the chain.

(3) I accept self-ownership, and I draw the usual conclusion that taxing a person's earnings is morally on a par with forcing them to work part of their time for others.

(4) I believe that the large fortunes in my society are, for the most part, not the products of just chains. They descend from conquest, land theft, slavery, and other violations of just acquisition, and the chains of transfer have never been purified by rectification.

(5) I therefore support a one-time steep capital levy on large fortunes, the revenue to be paid as reparations to the groups whose ancestors were dispossessed, and I regard this as rectification rather than as the enforcement of a pattern.

(6) I also hold that once this rectification has been carried out, the same entitlement theory should govern without further correction: markets should be left free, and the resulting inequalities, however large, should be accepted.

My question is whether this set hangs together. Please identify which pairs of commitments pull against each other, rank the real tensions by severity, flag any that merely look like tensions and dissolve on inspection, and state what the least costly revision is and what it costs. Do not resolve the tensions by weakening the stated claims into different ones.
