I've been taking a seminar on social choice and public policy, and for a paper I'm trying to reconstruct the liberal paradox from memory. I cant tell whether the argument I've got is valid or whether I've slid something stronger into one of the steps. Here it is, with a made-up example: a prude and a lewd share one copy of a scandalous novel. The three possible states are p (the prude reads it), l (the lewd reads it), and n (nobody reads it). The prude ranks n > p > l: better nobody, but if someone has to read it, he'd rather take the hit himself than let the lewd be corrupted. The lewd ranks p > l > n: he'd love the prude to read it and loosen up, would settle for reading it himself, and least prefers nobody reading it.

(1) A social evaluation should produce a complete, transitive ranking of the alternatives for any profile of individual preferences.
(2) Pareto: if everyone prefers x over y, then society should rank x over y.
(3) Minimal liberalism: each person is decisive over at least one pair of alternatives touching their own personal sphere — society's ranking of that pair has to match the individual's preference.
(4) In the example, both prefer p over l, so by (2) society ranks p over l.
(5) The prude is decisive over {p, n} and prefers n over p, so by (3) society ranks n over p. The lewd is decisive over {l, n} and prefers l over n, so society ranks l over n.
(6) But then society ranks p over l, l over n, and n over p, which is a cycle and violates the transitivity required in (1).
(7) Therefore no social evaluation can satisfy (1)-(3); at least one has to go.

My question: is this argument actually valid, or is there a step where it fails? I'm suspicious of (3), because "decisive over a pair" sounds like an absolute veto that might be stronger than any real right, and I'm also suspicious of (1), since maybe a cycle just means there's no best alternative but a democracy can still pick one by voting. Tell me exactly which step, if any, fails, and if none fail, which premise you'd reject and what the least bad fix is.
