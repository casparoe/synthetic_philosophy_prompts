Design an in-class game that stages this — no real money — where the last-move defection is obvious, the other side anticipates it and refuses the exchange, and then the whole dynamic flips once hitchhikers get to pre-commit in a way that seems irrational on its face. Give me rules, materials, a payoff scheme, a timeline, and a debrief that maps what happened onto the step from backward induction to rational irrationality. Don't have me lecture it first; the students should run into the problem themselves and only hear the name afterwards. Also flag where the game will probably go sideways the first time I run it and which feature of the idea the game can't reproduce, so I don't oversell it.
