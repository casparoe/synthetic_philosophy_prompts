A friend and I have been going back and forth about buying from bad actors—cheap shirts, phones, stuff like that—and he finally made me write out my whole view so he could see whether it hangs together. I typed it up and now I'm a little nervous to send it, so I figured I'd get an audit here first. These are the committments I find myself torn between:

(i) It's wrong to buy a shirt produced with forced labor, even though my individual purchase almost certainly won't change any worker's situation; and I don't think this is just about keeping my own hands clean.
(ii) Offsetting usually works: buying the shirt and then donating enough to a labor-rights group to make the workers at least as well off can be at least as good as not buying it and donating anyway—sometimes better.
(iii) In general, when I evaluate participation in a pre-existing harmful practice, I only trust expected-outcome comparisons: if my participating is no worse for the affected people than my not participating, there's no further wrongdoing in it; talk of an inherent taint of complicity doesn't move me.
(iv) But some things can't be made right by after-the-fact compensation. Paying someone to break a neighbor's arm and then covering the hospital bill doesn't make the whole package acceptable.
(v) I lean individual-causal-skeptic: supply chains are so diffuse that there is no detectable difference between my buying the shirt and my not buying it, so it's hard to see how a single purchase could violate a duty not to harm.
(vi) Yet I can't accept that if everyone reasoned like (v), the practice carries on unchanged and we've each acted permissibly—there's something off about a rule whose universal adoption keeps child labor in place.
(vii) And I'm suspicious of boycotts and voting-with-your-wallet logic; it mostly looks like self-expression and signaling, not like something that helps workers.

Run through these and tell me which pairs genuinely pull against each other and which are only apparently in tension. For the real tensions, rank them by severity, say what would have to give to fix each one and what that fix would cost me, and finish with the single revision that would restore the most coherence for the least damage.
