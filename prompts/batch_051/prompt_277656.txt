Angier's Tesla machine does to him, at each performance, something close to what Parfit's Mars teleporter does to the traveler: it produces a successor with his memories, character, and subjective conviction of being the original, while another such successor remains behind to face a fate the audience is invited to find horrifying precisely because he is a copy. Borden's secret turns on a related doubling.
