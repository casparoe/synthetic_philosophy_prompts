In a seminar on the philosophy of psychiatry we got stuck on whether gambling disorder and doomscrolling should count as "addiction." One guy kept closing the discussion down with "that's just semantics," as if arguing about a word meant there was nothing at stake. But there did seem to be something at stake: what the word covers determines who gets diagnosed, blamed, and funded for treatment. I know some disputes are purely verbal, but not automatically this one. Can you lay out the distinct precise claims someone could be making when they say "that's just semantics" — from the harmless to the actually false — and say for each whether it treats the choice of concept as something we can do better or worse at, i.e. whether it leaves room for fixing the concept rather than just analysing it? Then tell me which readings genuinely support his move of shutting the argument down, and which readings would allow the debate to remain substantive.
