I've been reading about global workspace theory and I keep seeing it described as claiming that consciousness just is information getting globally broadcast to the rest of the cognitive system. Here's what I don't get: if that's all consciousness is, then why do people still talk about the hard problem as if there's something left to explain? A state's being conscious is just its contents being made available to memory, report, and planning systems; that seems like a full explanation of both what consciousness does and what it is. Doesn't that make phenomenal consciousness just a redundant label for access consciousness? And wouldn't it follow that any system with the right kind of global broadcast architechture—say, a well-designed LLM with a central workspace that other modules read from—is literally having experiences, not merely acting like it is?
