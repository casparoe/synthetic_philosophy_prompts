I've been trying to get clear on what gratitude actually tracks, and my gut keeps pointing in different directions depending on the case. Here are my verdicts, such as they are. (1) A stranger dives into a river and pulls me out: I'm grateful without qualification, and I feel I owe him something real even if I never see him again. (2) A colleague covers my shift because he wants to look good to our boss, and it happens to save me from missing my kid's recital: I'm glad and I say thanks, but the inside of the feeling is missing — I don't feel I owe him anything, or at most a tiny bit. (3) A driver swerves to avoid a dog and, in doing so, accidentally blocks a car that would have hit me; I never learn his name. I feel lucky, and maybe grateful to no one in particular, but I don't feel grateful to the driver, because he wasn't trying to help me. (4) A friend, without asking, sells her car to pay off a debt I'd mentioned I was stressed about. This actually helps me a lot, but instead of gratitude I mostly feel cornered and a bit angry, and I wish she hadn't done it. (5) A firefighter carries me out of a burning building. I'm grateful, but it feels different from the river case: it's his job, he gets payed for it, he didn't single me out — I thank him, but I wouldn't say I'm in anyone's debt. (6) Years after my grandfather died, I learned he'd refused a promotion so the money could go to a coworker's widow raising kids alone. It doesn't benefit me now, but I feel something like pride mixed with something like owing — though owing what, and to a dead man, I can't say.

So intention seems to matter in (2) and (3), but not in (1)? Cost to the benefactor matters in (1) and (4) but cuts in opposite directions? And (6) makes me wonder whether gratitude can be owed to someone who never intended to benefit me, and whether it can outlive the person it's owed to. I'd like to know what principle, if any, gets most or all of these, which of my verdicts it has to throw out, and what it says about the case I keep flip-flopping on: a great-aunt I barely knew leaves me her house, but only to spite her children, who wanted it. I can't tell whether I'm grateful to her or not.
