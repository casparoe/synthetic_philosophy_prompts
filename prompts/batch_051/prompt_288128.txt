I keep going back and forth on whether "features" in a trained network are real, and it occurred to me that the debate maps suspiciously well onto scientific realism vs. instrumentalism. The feature skeptic says a circuit is just a compressed redescription of activations, and that the multiplicity of equally predictive circuits is a version of underdetermination; the feature realist says the circuit better explain behavior and transfers under intervention, which is starting to sound like the no-miracles argument for electrons. I can't tell whether this is a deep structural analogy or just me pattern matching. If it is deep, what has to be true of a feature for it to be the analogue of an unobservable that supports novel predictions, and what is the thing the analogy predicts about interpretability that is false?
