Give me a working definition of bullshit, understood broadly in Frankfurt's sense as speech that is indifferent to the truth, that correctly classifies the following cases. (1) A liar who first checks the facts, then states the opposite. (2) A commentator who invents a statistic on air because it sounds plausible and does not care whether it is accurate. (3) A student who pads an essay with true but irrelevant sentences to impress the reader. (4) A person with amnesia who sincerely confabulates a false account of what he did that morning. (5) A large language model that emits a fluent but false answer to a factual question. (6) A novelist writing realistic-sounding dialogue. (7) A scientist who speculates about an unverified mechanism but says explicitly that she is guessing. I am confident that (2) is bullshit and (6) is not; I regard (1) as lying rather than bullshit in this sense; I am unsure about (4) and (5); and I suspect (7) is borderline in a way I cannot yet state. Propose the definition, run it through all seven cases, say in particular whether (5) comes out as lying, bullshit, or mere error under your definition, and tell me which of your verdicts is least secure, then either revise the definition or defend the surprising verdict.
