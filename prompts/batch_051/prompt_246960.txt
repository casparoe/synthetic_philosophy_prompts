But I'm not sure what the one-liner is actually claiming. I'd like you to lay out the different precise claims that slogan could be making, from the trivial to the radical, and say which are trivial, which are defensible, which are false or question-begging, and which of them would actually do the work of making the Allais pattern count as a mistake rather than just a quirk. Also tell me which reading the expected-utility people are really leaning on when they invoke the independence axiom.
