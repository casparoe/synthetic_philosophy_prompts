In the process I've been reading about the distinction people draw in epistemology between epistemic bubbles and echo chambers. As I understand it: in an epistemic bubble you simply don't encounter the outside view, the relevant information is missing from your environment. In an echo chamber the outside view is present but has been preemptively discredited, so exposure to it tends to reinforce the inside view; getting out is less about receiving the missing information and more about repairing trust in the sources you've been trained to distrust.
