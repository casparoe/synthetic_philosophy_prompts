I've been applying a rule in AI safety discussions: if someone claims you can define an impact measure or build an AI that 'avoids side effects' without first writing down a complete account of what we actually value, I treat the claim as confused and stop engaging. To me a side effect is just a change to something I care about that I didn't ask for, so without the full list of cared-about things there's no fact of the matter about what counts as an impact, let alone a bad one. A friend of mine says I'm overcorrecting: you could catch most of what matters by penalizing large, irreversible changes to the world even with a very incomplete value model. Is my rule defensible, or is there a middle position where 'low impact' is genuinely meaningful without a full value specification?
