Make the humor come from the actual Hart–Devlin debate: the court insists it is enforcing no morality of its own, then decides the conduct is 'public' because it happened on a bus, then treats the other passengers' nausea as a harm, then patches the hole by inventing an 'ordinary passerby' whose stomach just happens to be as delicate as the judges'. Dont let the judges catch themselves. The satire should stay deadpan and legible, so someone who knows the debate sees Devlin's argument being smuggled back in one distinction at a time, while someone who doesn't still feels the harm principle quietly stop doing any work.
