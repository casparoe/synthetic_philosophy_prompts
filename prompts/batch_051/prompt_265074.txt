For a novel I'm working on, I need to think through how a particular conservation scheme would actually behave, because the plot turns on it. The setting is a rural county where a small land trust has to decide which of about forty properties to protect before a development wave makes them unaffordable. Up to now they've scored candidate parcels by an expert mix: rare species occurrences, wetland function, water quality, flood buffering, scenic views—mostly intrinsic and instrumental value, as one of my characters put it. Frustrated that the ranking keeps favoring empty, out-of-the-way places over the ones people actually care about, the county board adopts a Relational Value Index. A parcel gets RVI points for documented practices tied to it: fishing access, berry picking, hunting leases, hiking club routes, church picnics, a pagan group that holds seasonal rituals in a certain clearing, school field trip sites, a Thanksgiving trail run, oral traditions that name a particular grove or creek bend. It also gets points for how many living residents can draw or describe the site from memory, for multi-generational use, and for ongoing care relationships—brush clearing, prescribed burns, a family that has been pulling invasive garlic mustard off the same slope every spring. Acquisitions are then weighted two-thirds by RVI and only one-third by the old ecological score.

I don't want a verdict on whether this is good environmental philosophy, since the book can hold that question open. I want to know how the policy would play out. What would it incentive among landowners, developers, recreators, and the staff who verify claims? How would people game it, and which groups would game it first? What would happen to parcels with high intrinsic or ecological value but no human relationships attached? What second-order effects should I expect—on which stories about the land get recorded, on stewardship, on conflict between user groups? And are the failure modes reparable by tweaking the formula, or would any version of it drift toward something unrecognizable?
