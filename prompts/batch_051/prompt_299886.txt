Write me a deadpan parody of a property deed — the kind of document you'd get when you buy a house — for a person's personal data. Say a company has quietly started treating your behavioral data as real estate it owns, and it's issuing a "Warranty Deed (Non-Exclusive, Click-Agreement)" to your own search history. The jokes should come from taking "data is property" literally and getting the property concepts right: boundary disputes over who owns the overlap between your location history and your purchase history, an easement for targeted advertising, a fixtures clause covering forgotten passwords, and one quiet line near the end about how the person the data is about never held title in the first place. Around 500 words, formatted as the clauses of a deed. It should land the real "does information about you belong to you?" debate without ever stating it, and it should be readable to someone who hasn't read the literature — dont make it a sermon.
