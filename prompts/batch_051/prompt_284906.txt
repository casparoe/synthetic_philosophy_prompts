In a discussion group I attend, someone summed up the Christian idea of the atonement with the line that "Jesus took the punishment we deserved," and said this is why God can forgive sins. I keep hitting the same objection: if God is the one doing the forgiving, why does anyone need to be punished at all? When I forgive a debt, I absorb the loss myself rather than collecting the money from a third party first. I want to know what that slogan could actually be claiming, from the weaker readings (Jesus' death was a symbolic stand-in for sin, or a demonstration of how serious sin is) to the stronger ones (a specific penalty was literally transferred from us to him because God's justice had to be satisfied). For each reading, say whether it actually supports the conclusion that God could not simply forgive without punishment. Then commit: which reading is the most defensible, and which reading does the slogan's argument actually need? Don't just hand me a menu and leave me to pick.
