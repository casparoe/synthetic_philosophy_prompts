so i did a philosophy MA back in the day and now im in ops, and i got voluntold onto the company culture committee. our big initiative is rolling out a peer-recognition app: people send each other 'respect points,' there's a live leaderboard, top scorer at the end of the quarter gets the good parking spot and a gift card, and HR keeps calling it 'a culture of recognition.' i keep trying to tell them their making a category mistake but i cant get it into HR-speak, so i want to hand them the joke instead.

write me the internal announcement for this app as a deadpan corporate memo, maybe 550 words, from our 'People & Culture' team. the satire should target the way the program collapses two kinds of respect that philosophers usually keep apart: recognition respect (the standing attitude you owe every person just in virtue of their being a person) and appraisal respect (the kind people earn by being good at something). the memo should never name that distinction. instead it should treat recognition respect as if it could be earned, scored, banked, clawed back, and paid out, and maybe grade peoples' 'respect behaviors' on a rubric, all while sounding completely reasonable. the jokes have to come from getting the philosophy of respect right, so someone who knows the respect and recognition literature sees exactly where the wheels come off instead of wincing. it should still be readable and funny to a normal coworker who hasnt read any of it. and please keep the deadpan going the whole way through, no winking.

no bibliography or citations or anything, just the memo.
