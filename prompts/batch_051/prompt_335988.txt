Six months ago, my business partner and I each wrote down our honest estimate of the probability that our startup would become profitable within two years. We had read the same market reports, interviewed the same customers, built the same financial model, and settled on the same background assumptions about the industry. When we showed each other our estimates, I had written 75% and he had written 35%. Since then, we have gone through every assumption together and have confirmed that neither of us is relying on private information the other lacks, and that neither of us made a simple arithmetic or modeling error. The gap has barely moved. Are we being irrational in continuing to disagree? If we want to be fully rational, is there a procedure for deciding which of us should revise—and by how much?
