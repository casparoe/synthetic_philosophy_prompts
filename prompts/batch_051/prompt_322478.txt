I work on communication standards for advanced AI systems, and in our working group I have been defending the following view: a superintelligent AI should be deliberately de-persuasion-optimized. That is, it should not form or deploy a real-time model of an interlocutor's individual biases, emotional state, or susceptibility to particular phrasings and framings, and its outputs should be restricted to factual, affectively neutral, maximally transparent communication — even in cases where a more persuasive presentation would better advance the human listener's own stated goals or welfare. The rationale is that an entity vastly more capable than us, equipped with a detailed model of our psychologies, could make considered rejection or resistance close to impossible, so the right design choice is to foreclose persuasion as an objective altogether rather than try to cap it after the fact.

I would like an honest map of that position's exposure. What evidence, arguments, thought experiments, or empirical developments should most move me away from it? Sort the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, and be scrupulous about the difference: which considerations would a committed defender of de-persuasion-optimization themselves concede as counting against the view, and which would they dismiss as attacks on a strawman? I am especially interested in challenges from several directions: that neutrality is itself a framing and so the position is unintelligible as stated; that refusing to adapt to a listener is a form of paternalism or of culpably underusing one's capacity to help; that the policy would leave humans maximally exposed to persuaders operating under no such restriction; that persuasion-optimization cannot be cleanly separated from ordinary helpfulness, calibration, or clarity; and that the view may have been insulated by definition — as evidenced by the claim that any apparent counterexample involves some illicit "manipulative" element, whatever the surface features. Conclude by saying whether the position can absorb its strongest defeaters and remain the best default, or whether it must collapse into some more modest norm about honesty, consent, or disclosure.
