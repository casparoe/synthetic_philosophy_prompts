I am an undergraduate in a seminar on the philosophy of machine learning, and a recurring disagreement concerns what a chain of thought actually shows us. The claim under dispute is this: when a language model produces a chain-of-thought explanation and says, in effect, that it selected answer B because of feature X, the stated reason is sometimes a faithful, if compressed, report of the features that causally determined the answer, rather than a post hoc rationalization generated after the answer was fixed. I do not want this settled by intuition or by appeal to an author's authority; I want to know how one would test it.

You are an expert in the philosophy of cognitive science and in machine-learning interpretability. Please reason carefully and begin by brainstorming several candidate operationalizations of what it would mean for a chain of thought to be a faithful report of a model's own reasoning. Then commit to the strongest concrete experimental design you can, drawing where useful on tools such as activation patching, attention ablation, counterfactual token substitution, or behavioral transfer probes. Specify the class of models, the task or family of tasks, the experimental conditions, and the measurements you would record. Explain what pattern of results would count in favor of the claim, what pattern would count against it, and how strong a conclusion each pattern would support.

Finally, be explicit about what the test could not settle even if it came out positive. Several issues from our discussion seem relevant: a chain of thought is itself extra computation, so comparing answers with and without it may only measure the effect of additional tokens rather than the truthfulness of the report; a model might state a rule that predicts its behavior on nearby inputs without describing the internal computation; and a report can be faithful at one level of description while misleading at another. Address whichever of these you can, and say what residual part of the concept of a model reporting its own reasoning remains beyond any operational test. I am not asking for references or a reading list, only for the design and its limits.
