So the experience was there and it did no causal work. Nothing else about the world changes — it's a discovery, not a change in physics. I dont want plot ideas. I want your best reasoned prediction of how the next twenty years actually run, with the mechanisms made explicit. In particular, work through the paradox of phenomenal judgment properly in this world: if epiphenomenalism is true, then the scientists' judgement that it's true is itself a physical event with physical causes, so why would it track the truth about experience at all — and does that paradox undermine the original calibration of the signature against self-reports, or only the philosophical commentary around it? Say which links in your chain are robust and which are guesses, and what would have to be different for the guesses to go the other way. Also, please write the answer in British English — recognise, judgement, anaesthetic, that sort of thing. My editor is fussy.
