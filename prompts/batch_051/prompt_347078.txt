So there's this guy in my group chat who is constantly calling people out for flaking on plans, but he's the worst flake of all of us, and when I said something he just went "so? I can still be right about it." And I've been thinking about that. Online its the same move: someone makes an actually good point about someone else's behaviour and the whole discussion shuts down with "you did the same thing, you don't get to talk." Somewhere along the way I read that philosophers have this idea called "standing" — like whether a criticism is true is one thing, but whether you're in a position to make it is another. I'm not an academic or anything so I might be getting it wrong. But the more I thought about it, the more it seemed like if you really took the standing idea to its logical end you'd either end up with nobody allowed to critisize anybody (who has perfectly clean hands?), or you'd need some offical system for deciding who's clean enough to do the blaming. Which would be obviously terrible but also weirdly plausible.
