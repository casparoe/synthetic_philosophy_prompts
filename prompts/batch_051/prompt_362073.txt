i've been using a term I made up and I want to know if it earns its keep: moral endemism. In ecology an endemic species is one that is native to a particular region and basically nowhere else, and it tends to be exquisitely adapted to that one place. I think a lot of the moral intuitions doing heavy lifting in contemporary metaethics are endemic in exactly that sense: they show up very reliably in WEIRD populations (Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich, Democratic) but are rare or absent across the broader human record. Three examples: the intuition that a grossly unfair split in an ultimatum game should be rejected even at a cost to yourself; the intuition that an accidental well-meaning bad outcome is much less blameworthy than the same bad outcome done deliberately; and the idea that the most defensible moral rule is the one an impartial stranger could accept. I am not claiming these never occur elsewhere. The claim is that they are being treated as the global type specimen when they are really a local adaptation.

The concept is supposed to do real work. When an intuition is endemic, that doesn't refute it, but it should move our prior: it is evidence that the intuition is doing local ecological work (keeping a particular kind of social world stable) rather than tracking something mind-independent. So moral endemism picks out a case that isnt just ordinary cross-cultural disagreement and isnt convergence either — it is local consensus plus global rarity. I think a lot of realism-vs-antirealism arguments are actually about that third case but get argued as if it were one of the first two.

What I want checked: is this a real distinction, or am I just relabeling the WEIRD problem and parochialism in philosophy with an ecology metaphor? Does something like it already exist in metaethics under another name — it feels too obvious not to? And where does it break? I can already see one worry: endemism seems to assume local-adaptedness counts against truth, but dentistry and blacksmithing are local bodies of expertise and they track real parts of the world, so why should a WEIRD-shaped moral intuition get downgraded just for being WEIRD-shaped. Give me the strongest objections, and tell me which of them are fatal rather than merely limiting.
