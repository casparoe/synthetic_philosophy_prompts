Past decisions:

- We approved a "redirection" behavior where, instead of telling a resident they can't leave, the robot says something like "hey, let's make some tea" or "there's a card game in the lounge." The minutes explicitly say the robot should not lie there, just shift attention.
- We rejected a proposal to have the robot tell an agitated resident "your daughter just texted and she's on her way" when no text had come in, even though it was the most reliable way to keep her away from the door.
- We rejected a "sundowning comfort" feature where the robot could pretend to be the resident's late husband and read a letter to her, even though it was the only thing that calmed one resident down.
- We approved a version where, if a resident directly asks "when is my daughter coming," the robot reads the shared calendar out loud, including the delays, even though we knew this sometimes caused hours of distress.

The daughter signed off and says proactively announcing the delay causes hours of panic and a fall risk.
