I am a high school student writing a paper for my Government class on the debate over policing in the United States. I read an explainer that summarizes the debate this way:

'There are three main positions. Reformers think police departments should be improved with better training, body cameras, and stricter use-of-force rules. Defunders think a large share of police budgets should be moved to social services such as mental health care, housing, and addiction treatment, because officers are being asked to handle social problems they are not trained to solve and often make worse. Abolitionists, the more radical group, want to eliminate police departments entirely, because no institution whose job is to use force to keep order can be reformed; the harm it does is built into the job. Critics reply that society needs some institution to hold a monopoly on the legitimate use of force, and that without police, communities would have no one to call when a violent crime is in progress.'

I plan to cite this article, but I want to know whether its summary is fair. Could you go through it sentence by sentence and separate what is accurate, what is an oversimplification, and what someone who defends defunding or abolition would object to? I would also like to know whether the critics' prediction about communities having no one to call actually follows from what the article gets right.
