I have been going back and forth with a relative who insists that everything real is physical, and I have realized that our whole disagreement turns on a word neither of us has defined. I am not trained in any of this, but I want to get it straight before returning to the argument. We agree about many concrete cases: a chair is physical, a photon is physical, a magnetic field is physical enough. But we part company over pain, over the fact that seven plus five equals twelve, over the thought that a promise ought to be kept, and even over awkward middle cases like the wavefunction or the value of money. When I press for what physical covers, the answer keeps sliding: if I raise consciousness, I am told future science will deal with it; if I raise numbers, I am told they are just a manner of speaking.

So I would like a specification of what a definition or criterion of the physical would have to get right in order to be adequate for the claim that everything real is physical. Give me the requirements list: the cases it must classify correctly, the distinctions it cannot collapse (I suspect that being physical is not the same as being currently posited by physicists, and not the same as being explainable by physics even in principle), and the results it must not contradict. I have also noticed two traps any candidate must avoid. If physical means whatever current physics countenances or can explain, then the view becomes false every time physics is incomplete, and a person in 1850 would have been wrong for boring historical reasons rather than principled ones. If instead physical means whatever some completed, ideal future physics countenances, then the view seems to stop making any definite claim, because nobody can say now what that future physics will contain. I need to know how a good definition threads that needle, and whether those two traps exhaust the options or hide further assumptions.

For each requirement, please say why it is a requirement and which ones are negotiable rather than mandatory; then tell me which single requirement eliminates the most common ways people try to define physical, since I want to know where the real battleground is. Finally, I would appreciate it if you separated what is settled among people who work on this topic from what is genuinely contested, and did not present a contested view as though it were the consensus.
