I've been trying to think through the proposal to use genetic engineering and neurotechnology to abolish suffering in all sentient life. The most careful version of it seems to be: keep the "this is worse, move away" signal but strip out the part that hurts. I don't see how this is anything more than relabeling. If a state registers as worse than the alternative and impels an organism to leave it, then it is aversive, and being aversive is exactly what suffering is. So the project looks as though it collapses into a dilemma: either it keeps suffering under a new name, or it removes every felt difference between better and worse and leaves an organism with nothing that could direct its behavior. Which horn is the proposal actually on — or is the dilemma itself the mistake? Please say how confident you are in your answer and single out the point you are least sure of.
