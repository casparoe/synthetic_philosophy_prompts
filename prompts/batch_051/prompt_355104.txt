Over the past year I have come to think that functionalism about the mind is more or less right, and the reason is multiple realizability. Pain, understanding a joke, remembering a route — it seems artificial to identify these with any particular biology, and functionalism lets the same mental state be realized in human neurons, an octopus's distributed nervous system, or, in principle, silicon. The theory has a genuine systematic power: it explains how a replacement part can take over a cognitive function, because what matters is the causal role, not the materials.

This stopped being abstract for me when my sister, after a stroke damaged part of her motor cortex, received an implanted device that reads signals from the surviving tissue and drives a speech synthesizer. I find myself thinking that her speech is still her speech because the device occupies the same causal role the damaged region once did. That felt like evidence for functionalism making good on its promises.

But I have a firm intuition that I cannot reconcile with this. If someone realized the entire functional organization of a human brain in a different substrate — say, millions of people exchanging signals so as to reproduce exactly the same pattern of causes and effects among states — I cannot bring myself to believe there would be anything it is like to be that system. Even if it winced and protested, I would think it was not hurting. And yet, under functionalism, it ought to have exactly the same pains, fears, and intentions as the brain it reproduced.

How should I negotiate this? Should I debunk the intuition as a failure of imagination, or as the residue of a picture of feeling that functionalism should discard? Should I amend the theory, perhaps by holding that only certain realizers count — and if so, on what principle? Or is the conflict only apparent, because the intuition is not really about the functional states themselves? Give me the strongest version of both the theory and the intuition, then say which you would yield and what would justify that move over the others.
