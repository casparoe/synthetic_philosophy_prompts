Last night a friend and I stayed up far too late arguing about original sin, and I came away realizing that I had never actually written down my commitments on the topic to see whether they fit together. For what it is worth, I am a Christian, and the positions below are ones I genuinely hold rather than ones I was defending only for the sake of the argument. (1) Original sin is real: every human being is born with a corrupted nature and a built-in bent away from God, inherited from Adam's first sin, and this is a fact about each person's constitution rather than merely a matter of the bad influence of a fallen society. (2) Guilt in the strict sense attaches only to a person's own free acts; the idea that I could be guilty of something Adam did before I existed strikes me as close to incoherent. (3) I therefore reject the Augustinian and Calvinist claim that we inherit the guilt of Adam's sin itself, as opposed to inheriting a nature that issues from it. (4) Even so, I take the doctrine of original sin to be non-negotiable, and I take the fall to be a real event that really changed the human condition. (5) I believe infant baptism is legitimate and does something real; it is not merely a dedication ceremony or an expression of the parents' faith. (6) I believe God is perfectly just, and that no one is ever condemned for something they could not have done anything about. (7) I cannot bring myself to believe that an infant who dies unbaptized is condemned; that seems to me to contradict God's justice outright.

My friend kept insisting that I was quietly using original sin in two different senses and that some of these cannot all be true at once, but he was better at pointing than at explaining, and now I am not sure whether the problem is in my views or in his reading of them. Go through these commitments and tell me which pairs are genuinely in tension and which only look as though they are. Rank the genuine tensions by severity, say for each what the cheapest fix would be and what it would cost, and then tell me which single revision of the seven would do the most to make the set cohere.

Please write your answer as continuous prose, the way you would write an email or a letter: no headings, no bullet points, no bold text, and no tables.
