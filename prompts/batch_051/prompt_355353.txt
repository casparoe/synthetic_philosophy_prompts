Write a short story set in a near-future society where sustained attention is a legally owned asset, traded in secondary markets. The central concept is an "attention lien": when a platform's persuasive design has demonstrably captured a person's attention over time, the platform accrues a limited property claim over that person's future attention, which can be bought, sold, and enforced. The protagonist should be either a designer or an auditor who discovers someone whose attention cannot be reliably captured, steered, or valued by standard persuasive design models. Explore how this exception strains the legal and moral logic of the system.
