Write me a short dialogue between two Nomic players (Nomic is the game where changing the rules is itself a legal move) arguing over wether it's legitimate to use the current amendment rule to repeal that very amendment rule and replace it with one that's deliberately harder to change. One player holds that a move's validity is fixed by the rules as they stand when the move is made, so a repeal that targets the authorising rule itself is just good play if the current rules allow it. The other holds that some rules are constitutional: their job is to say how they themselves may be changed, and using a rule to destroy the very rule that licensed the change is a kind of bootstrapping, because the change undercuts its own source of validity. Neither side should be a strawman; have them respond to each other's actual points, and make them push on the temporal question of when the old rule's authority lapses. Keep it under 400 words.
