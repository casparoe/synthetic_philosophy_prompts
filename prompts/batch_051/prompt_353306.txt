I want the mapping to be disciplined: who counts as the decision-maker, what the choice variable really was (position size, leverage, diversification across supposedly independent spreads), what the payoff structure looked like as an ensemble bet involving many parallel histories, and what it looked like along one actual path with compounding losses and possible ruin.
