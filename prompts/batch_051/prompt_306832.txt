Priya thinks that's treating the past like it's already written when it isn't; she thinks the more natural picture is that going back and changing something branches off a new history, so you can kill your grandfather and you don't disappear, because the you doing the killing came from the old branch. They've mostly been talking past each other, and they don't even mean the same thing by "changing the past" half the time.
