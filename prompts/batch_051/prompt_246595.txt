Plan how I should teach this: the order in which the ideas must be introduced and what depends on what, the one Bongard-style example that should carry the whole talk and the specific properties it must have and must lack, the misconceptions I should expect (for instance, that any rule that cleanly separates the twelve drawings is therefore 'the' answer, or that these are visual classification exercises rather than a window onto concept formation) and the point in the sequence where each should be headed off, a closing question or small exercise that would tell me whether the audience has grasped the distinction between consistency with the examples and possession of a concept, and what I should explicitly decline to claim at this level.
