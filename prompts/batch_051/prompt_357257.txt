I am a philosophy PhD student working on well-being, and I have been interviewing a postdoc in a neighboring moral-psychology lab about a series of short cases. Her verdicts have been consistent enough that I suspect she is running a definite theory, but I cannot yet say which one, and I want to predict her judgment on a new case before I administer it. The cases and her verdicts so far:

A woman enjoys deep, stable happiness in what she takes to be a warm and loving family life, though her spouse and children secretly despise her and only perform affection. The postdoc rates her well-being low: the happiness rests on falsehood.

A painter spends decades producing original and eventually influential work. The process is mostly miserable, recognition gives her almost no pleasure, and she does not regard her life as a happy one. Yet she would choose the same life again without hesitation, because her central, fully informed ambition was to make those paintings, and it was fulfilled. The postdoc rates her well-being high, though she allows that the misery detracts from it.

A man is placed in a familiar experience machine. His apparent life of intimacy, achievement, and pleasure is entirely simulated; nothing in it is real. He did not choose to enter, would have refused if asked, and does not know he is in the machine. The postdoc rates his well-being low.

Next week I am running a new case. A fully informed and fully autonomous adult has one stable, freely chosen desire: to ride the same municipal bus loop every day and keep a meticulous private record of its timetable. He has no false beliefs, no close relationships, no accomplishments beyond the notebooks, and a mild but genuine contentment. He wants nothing more, and the desire is not the product of ignorance, compulsion, or impairment.

How should I expect the postdoc to rate this new case? Which theory of well-being — informed-desire satisfaction, objective-list theory, authentic-happiness theory, hedonism, or some hybrid — best captures her pattern, and which rival theory fits her verdicts equally well? If the leading theories diverge on the bus-loop case, give your best prediction about which way she will go and why; if her existing pattern still underdetermines the answer, say what further vignette would settle between them.
