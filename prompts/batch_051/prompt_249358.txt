Draft the joint statement that a committed restitutionist and a committed defender of the universal museum could both sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration over the Benin Bronzes and the Parthenon Marbles. It should open with a numbered section of substantive agreed claims about the historical record of how the objects were acquired, the present effects of their locations, the value of context and of dispersed collections, and what present institutions are capable of doing; these must not be vacuous compromises that merely restate the conflict. Then provide a disagreements table in which each residual disagreement is formulated so that its own side would accept the wording. For each crux, specify a concrete case, test, or piece of evidence that both sides agree would bear on it, and record each side's prediction in advance. Where part of the dispute appears to be verbal, or turns on different senses of terms such as title, access, or repair, say which part and why. Write in British English.
