I've been trying to work out what I actually think about temperance and self-control, and I keep runing into the problem that my gut verdicts on concrete cases don't obviously add up to one stance. Here are the cases with my verdicts. Limiting myself to one drink before a date so I stay sharp: fine, just temperate. Cutting out sugar after a diabetes scare: fine, obviously prudent. Cutting out sugar at a normal weight because I want to 'master my appetites': I respect it and also find it a little off, maybe vain. Counting calories to the point where I stop accepting dinner invitations: not fine, that's too much, I'd call that repression or at least disordered. Taking cold showers every morning because it supposedly builds discipline: I have no idea; it seems harmless, but the people I know who do it talk about it in a way that makes me roll my eyes. Not checking my phone until after breakfast so I don't start the day scattered: fine, prudence. The same no-phone rule, but I keep breaking it and then beating myself up about it: the rule is fine, the beating myself up isn't. Biting back a nasty comment when I'm angry at my partner: fine, that's self-control in the virtue sense. Biting back my irritation for years and never saying what bothers me: not fine, that's suppression, though I can't say exactly where the first turns into the second. Pushing through tiredness to meet a work deadline: fine, prudent. Training myself on a strict sleep schedule I dislike because I want to prove I can out-will my body: not fine, that feels like repression of a healthy signal. Deleting social media because I've noticed it drains me: fine. Deleting social media for thirty days as a willpower challenge even though I don't think it drains me: I keep relapsing, and the whole thing feels pointless, though maybe that says something bad about me.

Is there a principle that captures all or nearly all of these, and that would also settle the case I keep oscillating on: keeping a year-long spreadsheet where I log every time I give in to a craving, with notes on my mood. Is that prudence, virtue, or the first step of repression? If some of my verdicts have to go, which ones would you overturn and why?
