I want to test the underlying conceptual claim that a reasonable audience receives these ratings and forecasts as non-verifiable opinions rather than as fact-like assertions. Design the strongest test, say which results favor the opinion reading and which favor the fact reading, and be honest about what even a clean result leaves unresolved for the legal question.
