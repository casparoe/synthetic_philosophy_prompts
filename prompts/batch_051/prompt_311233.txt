Compare these two. In particular, work through where they'd give different advise: a single one-shot bet, a known finite sequence of n bets, and an agent with risk-neutral or S-shaped utility. Where the two stories split (or one goes silent), which one is doing the real work, and should we reserve the phrase 'geometric rationality' for one of them only?
