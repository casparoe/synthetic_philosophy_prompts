When they went to pick up the van, they negotiated the price as a group, and the farmer said "so who am I dealing with, you three or the co-op?" One of them said "the co-op's good for it," and the other two nodded and shook hands with him. Each of them would plainly have broken the clause if they'd signed alone. But they acted together, each assuming the others' involvement made it a co-op matter rather than three personal promises.

Does the clause catch what they did?
