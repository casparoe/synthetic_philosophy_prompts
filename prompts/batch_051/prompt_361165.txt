Ok so I'm a software engineer with basically no formal philosophy background, and I keep getting stuck on the difference between a preference and a bias. At work somebody will call a ranking model "biased" when it basically just optimized the engagement metric we told it to optimize, and then somebody else says "well humans have preferences too, so what's the difference." In moral arguments its often the same move in reverse: "that's just your preference" is supposed to be a shrug, but "that's a bias" is supposed to be a criticism. I can't pin down what the actual distinction is.

Give me a working definition of "bias" that separates it from a mere preference, at least in moral contexts. Then run the definition through these cases and tell me which ones are biases, which are just preferences, and which ones the definition can't settle:

- a hiring manager who consistently likes candidates who went to his alma mater
- someone who is only romantically attracted to one gender, and not as a considered choice
- a parent who trusts their kid's version of an incident over a stranger's version, without checking first
- a recommender system that optimizes for watch time and ends up pushing increasingly extreme political content
- a person who treats animal suffering as less important than human suffering just because the animals arent human
- a diner who tips noticeably better when the server has the same accent as them
- a doctor who gives less pain medication to patients whose self-reported pain they "just don't find credible" and can't say why

I think the second one is a preference and the last one is a bias, and I genuinely don't know about the recommender system. My hunch is that "preference" tracks what someone wants or likes, while "bias" tracks some kind of distortion or wrongness, and that some cases will move depending on which word you reach for.

For the answer, give me a JSON object, not prose, with these fields:

- "working_definition": your definition of bias in one or two sentences
- "distinction": how you define preference, and how it differs from bias
- "case_verdicts": an array of objects, one per case above, each with "case" (a short label), "verdict" (one of "bias", "preference", "unresolved", or "depends"), and "reasoning" (one or two sentences)
- "least_secure": which of your verdicts is least secure and why
- "revised_definition": optional; include it only if the cases forced you to revise your original definition
