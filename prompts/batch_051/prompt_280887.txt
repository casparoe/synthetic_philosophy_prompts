A friend and I have been arguing for months about petitionary prayer. I defend it as a practice in which a creature can genuinely ask God for things and thereby make a real difference to what God does; she holds that once you grant that God is omniscient and immutable, petition cannot coherently be supposed to affect God at all, and the practice must be justified, if at all, only by what it does to the one who prays.
