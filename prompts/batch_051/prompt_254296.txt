He'd put is credence at around 0.7 that a broadly Gricean theory of speaker meaning is correct. I'm down around 0.35. My worry isn't so much the basic reflexivity idea; it's the implicature side. The Cooperative Principle and the maxims feel to me like a rationalization-after-the-fact device — you can use them to derive almost any implicature you already believe is there, but they don't really predict which ones arise, and the said/implicated distinction has never been drawn in a way that survives contact with embedded cases, scalar terms, irony, or loose talk.
