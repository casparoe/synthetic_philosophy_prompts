I work on a social platform's trust-and-safety team, and we keep running into the same problem: when our classifiers flag a piece of propaganda and down-rank it, ordinary users repost it anyway to mock or debunk it, and the mockery often gives the original more attention than it would otherwise have gotten. We're considering a 'critical reproduction' feature under which flagged content can be reshared only inside a critique wrapper: the original is collapsed behind a warning overlay, the reader sees the poster's objection before the original, the objection must be at least as long as the original's embedded text, and each such wrapper slightly reduces the poster's own future reach for a period scaled by how many times the original had already been reproduced. Walk through how this would change sharing behavior, what incentives it creates for the people producing the propaganda, and what it does to the overall information environment. Where would it be gamed first, and does it actually solve the amplification problem or just move it around?
