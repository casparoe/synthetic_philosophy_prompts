The academic integrity policy says: 'It is an offence for a student to use any cognitive-enhancing drug or device to gain an advantage in an examination, regardless of whether the substance is prescribed for a diagnosed condition.' That seems to be both 'prescribed medication that offsets an impairment' and 'a prescribed drug used to gain an advantage in an assessment.' Are the two clauses actually in conflict? Give me the readings on which they aren't, and tell me whether any of them is a reading the university could honestly have intended—or whether the therapy/enhancement distinction just collapses into 'whatever diagnosis we're willing to call real.'
