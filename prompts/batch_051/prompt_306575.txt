I'm taking a course on weakness of will. We've been reading Aristotle's Nicomachean Ethics, Book VII, chapter 3, where he explains akrasia through what is often called the practical syllogism. Doesn't the hyperbolic discounting account make Aristotle's practical syllogism obsolete? If weakness of will just is time-inconsistent preference reversal, why would we still describe it as a failure of reasoning?
