I'm a PhD student in moral philosophy, and I keep coming back to the inefficacy objection—the thought that your individual contribution to some big collective thing (one short-haul flight, one more cheeseburger, one uncast ballot) makes no difference to the outcome, so there's nothing wrong with it (or, for voting, nothing wrong with staying home). I've been collecting replies, and they all fall into six families for me. I want one that isn't on this list.

Here's what I've got:

(1) The expected-value reply: even if the chance I'm causally decisive is tiny, it's multiplied by a very large good or bad, so the act is sensible or wrong ex ante anyway.

(2) The pivotal-agent/threshold reply: when a threshold gets crossed, some particular act did cross it; since I can't know in advance mine won't be that one, the objection is overconfident.

(3) The integrity/complicity reply: independant of whether I make a difference, participating in (or failing to distance myself from) a bad practice is wrong because of what it does to me—the person I make myself into.

(4) The universalization/generalization reply: what if everybody did that? Plus its rule-consequentialist cousin, where the test is whether a rule permitting the act, if generally accepted, would have bad consequences.

(5) The joint-participation/collective responsibility reply: the harm is produced by us acting together, so each participant can bear a share of responsibility for the shared output even when no single one of us is pivotal.

(6) The aggregate-contribution reply: not all collective harms work through thresholds; some get worse continuously with each contribution, so each extra unit makes things worse by its expected marginal amount rather than being lost in the noise.

I'm after a seventh family that's genuinely different from these six—not a rewording, not a special case, not a combination of two of them. If you can give me one, lay it out as a proper argument and then tell me honestly whether it's any good, and whether it does better in one of the climate/meat/voting cases than in the others.
