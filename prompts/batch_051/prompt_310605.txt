'Every writing system embodies an implicit theory of language. English looks like a counterexample because its spelling is so irregular, but the standard reply is that English is just a system that never got around to reforming its alphabet to keep up with sound change; it's a historical accident, not a real problem for the view, which is about properly maintained alphabets like Spanish or Finnish.'
