I'm taking a philosophy of the social sciences course this term, and we've spent the last two weeks on Malthus and on the question of whether a theory can be right but early. The thing thats bugging me is that after reading a bunch of defenses of Malthus, I can't tell whether people are actually responding to new evidence or just rotating through a handful of standard rescue moves, and it bugs me alot that I can't tell which. So I'm trying to compile a list of the distinct rescue moves I keep seeing, and I want to know if I'm missing one. Here's what I have so far, with Malthus as the running example throughout:

1. The conditional reading: Malthus's claim was about what happens when population is unchecked, so the fact that we haven't had a global Malthusian catastrophe just means the checks are doing their job in less dramatic ways.
2. The tendency move: the geometric-versus-arithmetic claim was never meant as a dated prediction, just a standing pressure that other forces can temporarily outweigh, so no particular event could falsify it.
3. The demographic transition as a disguised check: falling birth rates in industrializing countries are the preventive check operating through contraception and changed norms rather than famine and disease.
4. The wrong-century move: the underlying gap between exponential growth and finite resources hasn't gone away; technology and fossil fuels just pushed the reckoning into the future, and climate change or soil depletion is going to bring it back eventually.
5. The local-confirmation move: even if the global catastrophe hasn't happened, there are smaller cases, like pre-industrial Europe or Easter Island, where population pressed against carrying capacity in roughly the way the theory says.

I want one more distinct rescue move that isn't on that list and isn't a restatement, special case, or combination of these five. It should be the kind of thing a determined defender of Malthus could actually offer in good faith, not a straw man. Then give me your honest take: taken together, do these moves make the theory unfalsifiable, or is there still some observation that should count against it?
