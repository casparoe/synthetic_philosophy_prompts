I need a thought experiment built to a spec.

I want a case where a Schefflerian who accepts that conjecture and a standard person-affecting consequentialist disagree about whether a loss is bad, even though no individual death or suffering is prevented or caused. Specifically: one option preserves a fragile collective practice into the next generation, the other lets it die out after all current practitioners are gone, and nothing else differs. The person-affecting consequentialist should be indifferent between the two; the Schefflerian should not be. Constraints: realistic, community or workplace setting (no meteors, no science fiction, no perfect information), one paragraph for the scenario. After the scenario, walk through what each view says and why, then say which feature of the case is doing the work, what happens if you remove it, and how a defender of person-affecting consequentialism would attack the case.
