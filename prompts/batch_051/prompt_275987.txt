In the color phi phenomenon, a red spot is flashed, and shortly afterward a green spot is flashed a short distance away; subjects report seeing a single moving spot that turns from red to green roughly halfway across the gap—before the green spot actually appeared. I have been turning over the following argument:

(1) Taken at face value, the subject's report attributes a bit of perceptual content (the spot's being green while moving) to a moment before the stimulus that could have produced it.
(2) So any account of the underlying processing must be either Orwellian or Stalinesque: either the subject was first conscious of a red moving spot and the later green information retroactively revised the memory of the episode, or the early processing never reached consciousness and the whole red-to-green sequence was first consciously experienced only after the second flash, with the color change inserted at the proper place in the represented timeline.
(3) These two accounts differ only over when, if ever, the early content became conscious before being revised.
(4) No possible evidence can settle that question: all reports, memories, and behavior are identical under the two accounts, and any neural timing datum could be absorbed by either account by calling it the neural correlate of the pre-revision experience or the neural correlate of still-nonconscious buffering.
(5) If no possible evidence could distinguish two accounts, then there is no fact of the matter about which is correct.
(6) Therefore, in color phi there is no fact of the matter about whether the revision is Orwellian or Stalinesque, and the distinction is empty.

Is this argument valid? If not, exactly which step fails, and why? I am especially unsure about (4) and (5), but I cannot tell whether the real problem is a false dichotomy earlier in the argument or a verificationist assumption later. Is there a strong non-verificationist version of the argument that still gets the conclusion, or must the conclusion be given up? And if the conclusion holds here, should it be restricted to this experimental setup, or does it extend to the timing of conscious experience generally?
