Consider the following simplified version of the Judy Benjamin problem. A soldier, Judy, is dropped into a region divided into four equal-area sectors: Red Headquarters (RH), Red Second Company (RS), Blue Headquarters (BH), and Blue Second Company (BS). Judy begins with a uniform prior over the four sectors, assigning each probability 1/4. Her radio then delivers a message that she regards as certainly reliable: if she is in Red territory, then the probability that she is in the Red Headquarters sector is 3/4. She learns nothing else.

Here is an argument that her posterior after this message should be P(RH)=3/8, P(RS)=1/8, P(BH)=1/4, and P(BS)=1/4.

(1) The message imposes exactly one constraint on Judy's posterior: P(RH | RH ∨ RS) = 3/4.
(2) A rational update should make the smallest revision to the prior that still satisfies every constraint the evidence imposes.
(3) But the evidence is a conditional claim: it says only how Judy should distribute her probability within Red territory. It is silent about whether she is in Red or Blue territory, and silence cannot justify changing a probability; so rational updating must leave P(RH ∨ RS) at its prior value of 1/2.
(4) From (1) and (3), P(RH) = P(RH | RH ∨ RS) · P(RH ∨ RS) = (3/4)(1/2) = 3/8, and therefore P(RS) = 1/8.
(5) The evidence is likewise silent about how the Blue territory is divided between BH and BS, so those two probabilities should keep their prior 1:1 ratio; together with (3) this gives P(BH) = P(BS) = 1/4.
(6) Hence the uniquely rational posterior is (3/8, 1/8, 1/4, 1/4).

Assess this argument. Is it valid? If not, identify the exact step at which it fails and explain why. In doing so, distinguish the readings of "smallest revision" under which (3) does and does not follow from (2). Then compare with the minimum relative-entropy rule: it selects the posterior Q that minimizes D(Q‖P) = Σᵢ Q(i) log(Q(i)/P(i)) from the uniform prior P, subject only to the constraint in (1). Does that rule yield the posterior in (6)? If not, give the posterior it yields to three decimal places and state which step it rejects.
