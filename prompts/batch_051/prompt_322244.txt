The argument goes like this. (1) Say a theory is complete just in case it correctly accounts for every physical phenomenon whatsoever, so that no future observation would force a revision. (3) To know that no possible experiment would refute it is to know a universal generalization over all possible experiments, including the infinitely many experiments no one has performed. (4) At any given time, we have performed only finitely many experiments. (5) No finite body of experimental results can justify a universal generalization over an infinite space of untried experiments. (6) Therefore, we could never know that a physical theory is complete. (7) So a final theory would be unknowable by definition, and seeking one is aiming at something incoherent.
