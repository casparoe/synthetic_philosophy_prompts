One lab assistant says its obviously 1/2, since the coin was fair and I knew that on Sunday. Another says that's naive, and from inside the experiment it should be 2/3, because on tails I'd be awake-and-asked twice as often. I need one number to write down each time. What number should I give, and why? Give me a committed answer—if you think it's 1/2 or 2/3, defend that, and if you think the question is confused, say what exactly I should ask them to clarify.
