The calibration rests on an extended battery of cross-matching and matching-to-sample tasks, and independent laboratories reproduce the results. Assume for the sake of argument that the device is genuine: its measurements are as repeatable and interpersonally stable as thermometry. Nothing else changes. There is still no agreed theory of why these measured states exist or how they arise from the brain, and the device works only on awake, responsive human adults — not on animals, infants, or AI systems.
