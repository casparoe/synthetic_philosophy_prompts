Henri Poincaré, in the chapter on mathematical creation in Science and Method, says in essence that the unconscious mind generates vast numbers of combinations and that only the harmonious and elegant ones pass into consciousness; he then claims the useful combinations are precisely the most beautiful. I keep going back and forth on what this does: sometimes it sounds like beauty is a filter for truth, sometimes like it is just the mental condition under which discoveries happen, and sometimes like the claim is circular because usefulness and beauty are being defined in terms of each other. Please explain the conceptual issue as you see it and say which of those readings is closest to Poincaré's actual position. Keep the response under 200 words.
