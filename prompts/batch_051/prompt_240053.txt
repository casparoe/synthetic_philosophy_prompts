- a woman gets blackmailed into driving a getaway car — some guy says he'll hurt her family if she doesn't. she drives. she technically could've refused but who would? i don't blame her. maybe i blame her like 5%, but honestly no.

- a guy with a serious addiction problem steals from his job to pay for drugs. people say he couldn't help it, and i do think his choice was squeezed, but i still blame him some. why does "couldn't help it" work for the seizure guy and the sleepwalker but not fully for him?
