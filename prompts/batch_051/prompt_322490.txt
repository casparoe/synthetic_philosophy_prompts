The article says, roughly — I'm writing this from memory, so treat it as a paraphrase:

'Dehaene's global workspace theory explains consciousness as a broadcasting system. Information becomes conscious exactly when it enters a neural workspace and gets broadcast to many systems in the brain — memory, language, decision-making, planning — so you can report it and act on it.
