His ability to predict who will actually pay is extraordinarily good. He believes her and drives her home. As they pull up to her house, the ride is already complete. Nothing she does at this point will cause the rescue to have happened or not to have happened. Should she hand over the two hundred dollars?

Give me a committed answer: what would she say, and what rule is she actually running? I can see at least two different principles that fit her first three verdicts and that might give opposite answers on the hitchhiker case. Tell me which rule you think she is using, what that rule predicts for the new case, which alternative rules fit the earlier verdicts just as well, and which possible answer to the hitchhiker case would most sharply distinguish them.
