The position I want translated is this. In The Social Contract, Rousseau treats the general will as the will of a legitimate political association insofar as that will aims at the common good, and he distinguishes it from the will of all, which is only the sum of private interests. On his account, legitimate association requires each citizen to be governed by the general will rather than by private interest, and when a citizen's private interest would lead him to refuse to obey the general will, the community may compel him to comply. Rousseau argues that this compulsion does not simply constrain the citizen but makes him free, and that without the capacity for such compulsion the association could not solve the problem of legitimacy. This is the claim that has come down to readers as the idea of being forced to be free. Translate this position, claim by claim, into the vocabulary I actually use.
