I recently got into an argument with a coworker who insists that our minds will one day be uploadable — run on silicon after our bodies die. His whole case came down to the slogan that the brain is a computer, and that since a program can run on any suitable hardware, whatever the brain does could be made to run somewhere else. I suspect that slogan is being stretched, but I can't say exactly how. Could you lay out the distinct precise claims 'the brain is a computer' might be making — what kind of computer is being claimed, what it could mean for two physical systems to be running the same program, and what exactly would have to be true of the brain for the upload conclusion to follow — then go through the readings one by one and say which are trivially true, which are substantive and defensible, which are probably false, and which readings his argument actually needs?
