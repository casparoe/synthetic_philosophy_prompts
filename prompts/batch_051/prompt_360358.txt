I can measure them, know they're equal, hold that knowledge in my head, and they still look like different lengths. If a belief that explicit can't touch the look, I don't see how anything else could.

She says the chest x-rays she looked at in her first year were a grey fog with ribs in them, and now the same scans have anatomy and possible pathology just sitting there in the image.
