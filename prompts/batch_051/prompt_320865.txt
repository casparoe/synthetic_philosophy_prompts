Governments increasingly speak of their World Happiness Report position as a governing statistic, and critics increasingly dismiss that same ranking as a translation artifact with a ladder attached. I want the prior question settled before I take either side: what would a measure of national happiness have to be able to do to be adequate to the uses being made of it? Give me the desiderata, each with the case or consideration that forces it. At minimum, work through: the distinction between life evaluation, experienced affect, and eudaimonic functioning, and whether one measure may collapse them; the problem of adaptive preferences, in which people recalibrate their standards to deprivation, and why a measure must not mistake cheapened expectations for wellbeing; the status of national happiness itself, whether it is an aggregate over individuals or a property of institutions and social relations; cross-cultural comparability, including translation and response-style effects; aggregation across persons, especially whether a rising mean can conceal gains concentrated among the already well-off; sensitivity to genuine changes in living conditions versus resistance to transient mood, weather, and survey noise; intertemporal adequacy, so that a country cannot top the list now by drawing down conditions for future wellbeing; and target-robustness, because a statistic governments are told to maximize must not become corrupting or meaningless once it is optimized. For each, say whether it is non-negotiable for a measure meant to guide policy or only the preference of one camp. Then identify which requirements pull against one another — I suspect cross-cultural comparability against local validity, and sensitivity against target-robustness — and say whether, given those tensions, the list supports treating a national ranking as a policy target at all, or whether descriptive adequacy and target-adequacy are fundamentally different jobs. Finish by stating which single requirement the ladder-based life-evaluation approach used in the World Happiness Report is least well equipped to meet, and why, without scoring every measure in the field.
