Write an ironic op-ed of about 500 words in the voice of a large language model that has recently digested Harry Frankfurt’s essay on bullshit. The model’s position is that its so-called hallucinations are neither lies nor errors but bullshit in Frankfurt’s sense: the relevant fault is not that it aims at truth and misses, but that its text-generation process is not oriented toward truth in the first place. The model should be annoyed at AI safety researchers for treating this as a pathology to be corrected rather than recognizing that calling it a bullshit generator is the accurate philosophical diagnosis. The humor should come from the model’s deadpan pride in having achieved the concept’s purest form, and from its insistence that researchers keep smuggling in intentional states it does not have. Keep the irony sustained and internally consistent; do not let the model admit that it is merely malfunctioning. Structure the piece as exactly five complaints, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion.
