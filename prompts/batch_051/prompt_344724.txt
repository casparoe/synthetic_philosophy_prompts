I was taught to use four tests for whether a self persists over time: continuity of consciousness, an unbroken chain of memories linking later stages to earlier ones, continuity of the body and brain, and the persistence of a recognizable pattern of thought, feeling, and behavior. Now here's my case. My father died two years ago, but a kind of low-resolution copy of him has been running in my head. When I meet new people, I sometimes know exactly what he'd have said about them, and it comes automatically rather than as an exercise; I catch myself with his impatience, his jokes, his way of ending an argument. The situations are new, so it isnt just remembering past conversations. Does some part of his self persist in me? Go through those four tests honestly. Where the answer is no, tell me whether the test is too narrow or the case really isn't persistence, and whether this case shows my criteria need changing. And say whether it should change what I mean when I call him gone.
