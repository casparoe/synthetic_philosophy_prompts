On the common reading, a strictly true but misleading statement is not a lie, though it may still mislead. Construct a case in which a person deceives someone through a strictly true but carefully chosen statement, no false assertion, and in which the Kantian verdict and a harm-based verdict diverge sharply: the misleading act must be clearly worse in consequences than the lie that was available, or clearly better, your choice.
