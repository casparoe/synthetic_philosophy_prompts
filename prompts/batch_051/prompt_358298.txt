I keep reading about people who obviously feel pain—they can tell you exactly where it is and how strong it is—but they just don't seem to *mind* it. No wincing, no pulling back, sometimes they'll even smile or laugh while describing it. It makes me wonder wheter the unpleasantness of pain isn't actually baked into the pain itself, but is more like an extra layer that usually comes along and could in principle go missing. Does this exact thing have a name, and what does it actually show about whether pain is bad in itself or only ordinarily bad? I'm not after just a label—I want to know how to carve the difference between the sensation, the unpleasantness, and the suffering, and where the nearby cases are that people tend to run together with this one.
