A state law is proposed under which every non-disclosure provision in a contract, settlement, or severance agreement entered into by a state agency lapses after five years unless the agency obtains renewal from a seven-member Public Disclosure Board. The Board must vote in public, must state in writing which specific harm disclosure would now cause, and its members may not communicate with agency staff about a pending renewal outside the hearing; a person who discloses information covered by a lapsed NDA is immune from civil suit over that disclosure. Analyze this as a mechanism: what it incentivizes for the agencies drafting the agreements, the employees and contractors asked to sign them, and the Board members; where it would first be gamed or routed around; and whether those failure modes can be fixed without undercutting the law's purpose.
