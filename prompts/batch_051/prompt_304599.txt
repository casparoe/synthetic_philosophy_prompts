(3) war as the moment in which the ideality of finite goods such as property, life, and individual autonomy is made actual and recognized;
(4) war as a vehicle by which universal history moves from one national spirit to the next.

Please rank these four rationales by how defensible they remain once detached from Hegel's speculative metaphysics, from the one that can most plausibly be restated as an ordinary social-psychological or political observation to the one most dependent on the dialectical system. Give each a score from 0 to 1 for how much it survives that detachment, with 0 meaning it collapses entirely and 1 meaning it stands fully on its own, and say in a sentence or two why each lands where it does.
