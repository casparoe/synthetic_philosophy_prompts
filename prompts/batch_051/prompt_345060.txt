So this year the school switched to a different textbook that's mostly the Declaration, the Constitution, and the Founders, and it treats slavery like a tragic compromise the founders were already on their way to fixing. Do you actually have to hold some deeper theory of history to prefer one book over the other, or could the same person reasonably assign both in differnt classes?
