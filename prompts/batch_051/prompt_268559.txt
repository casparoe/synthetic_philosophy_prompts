In fact, since the brain would produce exactly the same words and judgments even if there were no consciousness at all, we have no reason to think our introspective judgments track anything real. On this view we could never know that we are conscious — we could not even be conscious of being conscious — which is absurd. That is why most philosophers regard epiphenomenalism as self-refuting."
