The theory is that rationality can be trained like a martial art. The real world does not arrive labeled now do reference-class forecasting; the genuinely difficult skill is recognizing that the present case is of the kind where the drill applies. So the kata does not bypass the transfer problem; transfer is the whole problem, and practicing in a stripped-down context does not train the thing that fails in the wild.
