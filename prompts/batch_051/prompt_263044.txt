I am a machine learning engineer with no philosophy background. The vocabulary I think in is systems and computational: checkpoints and restores, forward passes, parameter servers, data-parallel training, fork and copy-on-write, address spaces, weight tying, class versus instance, and type versus token. I want you to translate a philosophical position into that vocabulary and keep the translation scrupulously honest, producing a ledger of what carries over, what must be bent to fit, and what has no counterpart at all.

The position is the one Schrödinger took from Advaita Vedanta, and it runs roughly as follows. Consciousness is never met with in the plural: each of us is acquainted only with one flow of experience, never with several such flows side by side. We nonetheless infer many separate minds, but only because each flow is found tied to one body and one store of memories. On this view that inference outruns anything that could be observed, and the supposed boundaries between subjects are not features of the world; they are imposed by the way each perspective is restricted to its own perceptions and memories. The deeper view is that the multiplicity of subjects is an appearance, and that there is in reality one consciousness common to all, with the many individual subjects as its seeming fragments.

Restate that position in my vocabulary claim by claim, and for each claim say whether it translates cleanly, translates only under distortion, or fails to translate. Then give me the residue report. My working hypothesis is that my vocabulary can express the weaker claim that many physically distinct processes run the same abstract computation, and perhaps the claim that there is no objective fact about how many subjects there are, but cannot express the stronger claim that there is really just one consciousness, because nothing in the vocabulary of computations and their instantiations carries phenomenal character. I want your view on whether that gap is a defect of my vocabulary or the point at which the position stops being a claim about the world and becomes a picture, and on whether the clean translation I can obtain is a faithful restatement or a rival view wearing the old position's clothes.
