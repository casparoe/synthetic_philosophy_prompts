Read Black Panther through that lens. Where does the film seem to endorse or embody the view that art should function as persuasion? Where does it resist or complicate that view? I am less interested in whether Du Bois would have liked the film than in what his argument helps us see in it, and what the film exposes as too simple in the argument's terms. Be specific: point to scenes or character choices a discussion group should examine, flag where the fit is imperfect, and tell me what a moderator should press on.
