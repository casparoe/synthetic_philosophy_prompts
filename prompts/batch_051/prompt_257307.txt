Lately I keep running into writers who talk about a cold spell or an epidemic or an eruption as if the climate or the germ was a character in the story — it 'pushed' a revolution over the edge, a disease 'decided' a war. I honestly cant tell whether that's just vivid shorthand for 'background condition people reacted to' or whether they actually mean the non-human thing did something in history. How would you test that? I keep thinking you'd have to compare histories that are identical except for the non-human event, but I dont see how that separates 'changing the options people had' from 'choosing for them.' Design the best test or observation you can for whether a volcano, a climate shift, or a pathogen is a real historical actor rather than scenery. Say what result would count for each reading, and then be straight about what even a good test couldn't settle — is some part of 'agency' just not the kind of thing evidence can reach, and does that make the question empty or only partly untestable?
