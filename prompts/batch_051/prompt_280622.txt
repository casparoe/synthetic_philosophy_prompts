Im on the management committee of our local community centre and I've been landed with drafting a decision memo for next weeks meeting. A group calling itself Unseen Histories has applied to hire the main hall for a Saturday series of three talks: one on Atlantis as a real place, one on ancient aliens building the pyramids, and one on the idea that the standard timeline of ancient history is out by centuries. They pay the normal hire fee, bring their own speakers and have their own insurance, and they apparently run similar talks in other towns without any incident.

Half the committee says we should just take the booking like any other. Its not the centres job to police what's true, the events are private, the audience are adults who can make up their own minds, and refusing would amount to censoring ideas we happen not to like. The other half says this is exactly the sort of bunk that pulls people into full-on conspiracy thinking, and by hosting it we'd be putting the centres name to it and making it look respectable; we'd also get complaints from parents of kids who use the centre and probably lose other bookings.

Please draft the memo I can put to the committee. It needs a clear recommendation (approve, reject, or approve with conditions) plus the reasoning, and it has to deal fairly with the strongest objection to whatever you recommend. Keep it to 400 words max because our chair won't read past a page.
