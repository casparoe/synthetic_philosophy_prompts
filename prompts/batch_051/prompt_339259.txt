I am a student in an intellectual history course on classical Islamic theology, and we have been studying the dispute between the Mu'tazila and the Ash'arites over the source of moral normativity. Our professor frames it as an Islamic analogue of the Euthyphro dilemma: whether an act is right because God commands it, or God commands it because it is right. As I currently understand it, the Mu'tazila hold that acts have moral qualities such as good, bad, permissible, and obligatory in virtue of features that human reason can at least partly discern, and that God commands an act because it is good; the command is not the whole explanation of its being right. The Ash'arites, by contrast, hold that moral status just is God's command and prohibition: an act is obligatory because God commands it, and had God commanded the opposite, the opposite would have been obligatory. The two sides also appear to use terms such as reason and good in different ways, which is part of why the dispute has been hard to pin down.

I would like you to draft the document that a good-faith adversarial collaboration between a Mu'tazili and an Ash'ari should have produced after a year of sustained discussion. Please format it as follows. First, a numbered list of substantive claims both sides could sign; these should go beyond truisms and should identify the real common ground about God, moral language, and the structure of the debate itself. Second, a disagreements table in which each side's position is stated in wording that proponents of that side would accept. Third, the cruxes: the sub-questions on which the disagreement genuinely turns. I suspect one crux is whether a human judgment that an act is good is a genuine deliverance of reason or only a non-cognitive attitude dressed up in moral language; there may be others, and I would like them stated precisely. Fourth, for each crux, give a case or argument that both sides would agree bears on it, and record each side's prediction about how it resolves. Finally, identify which parts of the dispute are likely verbal rather than substantive, for instance whether they are using reason or good in different senses.

Please also say how confident you are in the substantive claims you make, and single out the point you are least sure of.
