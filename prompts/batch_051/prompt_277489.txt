I am a third-year undergraduate taking a course on Reformation theology and ecclesiology, and I am writing a term paper on the authority of Scripture. In class we have covered Luther’s distinction between the external clarity of Scripture and the internal clarity worked by the Spirit, Calvin’s account of the internal testimony of the Holy Spirit, and the recurring Catholic objection that Scripture cannot act as judge in its own case because every appeal to Scripture already presupposes an interpreter. I want the paper to argue that sola scriptura does not dissolve the problem of interpretive authority but relocates it, and I need to focus it around the two or three most important unresolved questions in this debate. Which open questions about who decides what the text means matter most, and why? I am less interested in a list than in a defended ordering: for each question, explain why it is upstream of other theological and ecclesiological disputes, how tractable it is for someone working with primary Reformation sources rather than modern hermeneutics alone, and what consideration would most change its place in the ranking.
