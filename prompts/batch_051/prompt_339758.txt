Since I began keeping a dream diary after a period of unusually vivid dreams, I have been trying to settle which episodes in and around sleep deserve to be called experiences. I have been working with three criteria for a genuine experience: an experience must be determinately timed and have a determinate duration, be in principle reportable as a sequence of events, and be something about which one could be more or less accurate. Now consider a subject woken from REM sleep who first says she was aware of nothing; after gentle prompting she offers a vivid account of chasing a dog through a fairground, and ten minutes later she offers a comparably detailed but largely different account, insisting that she is recounting the same dream. Does the dream, considered as an occurrence during sleep rather than as the telling afterward, qualify as an experience by my criteria? Work through each criterion, note where the criteria underdetermine the verdict, and say whether the case shows that any criterion needs revising.
