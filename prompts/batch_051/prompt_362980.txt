Stay in character, use only the concepts in A System of Logic plus what I've just told you, and dont dodge by saying the finding is impossible; you can question my description of it, but then answer. Here's the passage you wrote in the preface: "Whether moral and social phenomena are really exceptions to the general certainty and uniformity of the course of nature; and how far the methods by which so many of the laws of the physical world have been numbered among truths irrevocably acquired and universally assented to, can be made instrumental to the formation of a similar body of received doctrine in moral and political science."
