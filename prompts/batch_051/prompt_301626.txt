I keep running into the same impasse whenever I try to say exactly what physicalism is, and I want the full specification of requirements before I read another proposal. Write the desiderata that an adequate account of "the physical" must meet. Hempel's dilemma is the natural starting point: if "physical" means "posited by current physics," the doctrine looks hostage to the next scientific revolution and almost certainly false, since the physics of any given era gets revised or replaced; if "physical" means "posited by the completed physics at the end of inquiry," the doctrine goes empty, because we cannot say what that completed physics will countenance and so cannot rule out, without stipulating, that it will include irreducible mental or phenomenal features. With that dilemma in view, set out the requirements a definition of the physical must satisfy, and for each requirement give the case or consideration that makes it a requirement. Mark which requirements are non-negotiable constraints on any viable formulation and which are merely preferences of particular camps. Flag any pairs of requirements that cannot be jointly satisfied, and say whether that unsatisfiability is just Hempel's dilemma resurfacing. Then briefly score the three standard candidate accounts against the list: the current-physics account; the via negativa, on which the physical is the non-mental, non-vital, non-supernatural; and the theory-based account, on which the physical is whatever a completed physics countenances. Keep the weight on the criteria themselves rather than on the scorecard.

The part I most want worked out is this: I suspect a common package of desiderata is jointly unsatisfiable — the demand that physicalism be a substantive empirical thesis, combined with the demand that its truth not depend on the accidents of today's physics, combined with the demand that it not be saved by deferring to an unknowable future physics. Tell me whether that package is genuinely inconsistent, and if so, which requirement has to go and what the surviving options look like. If you think some kind of definition breaks the dilemma, say exactly how it satisfies the demands that the other candidates fail.
