Liability is limited: if the AI breaks a contract or causes harm, creditors can only go after assets held in the AI's own name, not the operator's other assets. If the AI's assets run out, the judgment is simply uncollectable — the AI cant be jailed, and the registered agent isn't personally liable. The law deliberately says nothing else: no voting, no free-speech protections, no criminal liability, no rules about how the AI's "decisions" are made or who sets its goals.
