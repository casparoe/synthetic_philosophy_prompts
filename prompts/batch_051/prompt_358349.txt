I keep seeing people say the Solomonoff prior is malign because it's full of consequentialists, and that this matters for AI safety. But from what I understand of Solomonoff induction, you just sum over programs weighted by 2^-length, and the ones that don't predict your data get suppressed. So the only programs left after conditioning are the ones that accurately predict the sequence. Why should I care if some of those programs are internally running agents? Their just prediction machines now, right? I feel like I'm missing a step between the prior containing agents and us needing to worry about them.
