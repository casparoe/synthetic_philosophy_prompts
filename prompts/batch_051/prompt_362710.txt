Physicists say every theory we know is just an effective field theory, valid only below some cutoff, and that renormalization makes scales autonomous: chemistry works fine without knowing about quarks, and condensed matter works fine without the standard model. But if each scale really is autonomous and insensitive to whatever sits above it, then what makes the high-energy theory more fundamental? Isnt reductionism just dead, and are particle physicists just studying one more parochial energy range rather than the deep truth?
