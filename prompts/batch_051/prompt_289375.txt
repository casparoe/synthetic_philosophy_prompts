Meanwhile, machine learning has a canonical optimizer of its own — gradient descent — and researchers disagree about whether natural selection and gradient descent are genuinely analogous or only superficially similar.

Natural selection is an optimizer in a sense strong enough that the analogy with gradient descent is scientifically productive: over roughly the next twenty years, taking the analogy seriously will generate non-obvious, testable hypotheses in evolutionary biology or in machine learning that neither field would likely have produced on its own.
