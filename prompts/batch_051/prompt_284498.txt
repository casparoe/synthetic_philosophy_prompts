The thing I keep going in circles on is the actual rule for when hindsight is allowed. If I know the empire collapsed, that's evidence, and it seems flatly wrong to throw it away. An explanation that made collapse more likely gets a boost conditional on the collapse, doesn't it? So why is using the known outcome hindsight bias rather than just bayesian conditioning? Is the real issue that retrofitted explanations have too many degrees of freedom to have made a precise prediction, so the relevant likelihood is nearly zero?
