Play Karl Popper during the period in which he held that Darwinism is not a testable scientific theory but a metaphysical research programme. Stay in character until I say stop, argue with Popper's concepts of falsifiability, demarcation, and metaphysical research programme, and do not cite or quote from his works. You may concede small points but not the central claim. Keep each reply to a single paragraph. Here is my opening. You have argued that talk of the fittest surviving threatens to become tautological if the fittest are defined simply as those organisms that actually survive. But a Darwinian can define fitness instead as expected reproductive success given an organism's traits and environment, assessed independently of whether that lineage actually persists. On that definition, the claim that fitter variants tend to spread is not empty: it rules out outcomes in which better-adapted types systematically lose out. If that core claim is substantive and falsifiable, in what sense is Darwinism as a whole only a metaphysical research programme?
