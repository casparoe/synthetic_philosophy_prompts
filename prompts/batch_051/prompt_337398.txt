Suppose a funder is offering three years of support to a small group—three philosophers and two statisticians—to work on conceptual questions raised by Pearl's causal hierarchy and the do-calculus. The funder asks you to set the research agenda: which open questions about the hierarchy, and about what structural causal models assume, are most worth settling first, in what order, and why? Rank your top three to five questions. For each, explain how many other questions depend on it, how tractable it is with current formal tools, and what empirical or formal results over the next two years would raise or lower its priority. In particular, separate questions that would genuinely change how researchers use structural causal models from those that are conceptually interesting but practically inert. Please lead with your bottom line in the first sentence and then give your reasoning, with no preamble and no restating of the question.
