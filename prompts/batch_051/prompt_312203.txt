My friends and I have been arguing on and off about pre-AGI bargains: deals made now, before anyone knows whether AGI will arrive or what it will be like, that are meant to bind people (or their uploads, or their companies, or whole countries) once it does. We keep inventing cases, and I find I have strong verdicts on all of them but no general principal, and at least two of my verdicts look as if they can't both be right.

Here are the ones I've settled on, more or less. (1) If two people sign a proper contract in 2025 saying that whoever builds AGI first will hand the other 20% of the resulting profits, and in 2035 one of them builds AGI and tries to get out of it, she's clearly in the wrong: the contract binds. (2) If someone writes an advance commitment addressed to her own radically enhanced future self — for instance, a promise to put half of any post-AGI foundation's money into the town she grew up in — I think the enhanced self is bound too, even if she no longer cares about the town. (3) But if someone signs a document now saying her future upload has to finish her novel, and the upload ends up with most of her memories but a quite different temperament, I don't think the upload is truly bound. That feels like making a promise on behalf of a stranger. (4) I think a government that imposed a windfall tax on AGI just before it arrived would still be entitled to collect it after, despite all the social change in between. (5) I don't think a drunken verbal pact between friends to split whatever AI riches one of them later gets binds anyone. (6) And I keep feeling that pre-AGI people are simply too ignorant about the post-AGI world to bind it — but that seems to undercut (1) and (4), which I also don't want to give up.

Is there a single principle that captures most or all of these verdicts, and if so, which ones would it force me to drop? Please state the principle precisely, say what it predicts about a case I haven't settled — two nations signing a treaty now limiting how either may use AGI, where the next generation of one country later claims the treaty was made by a different generation and doesn't bind them — and be honest about where my verdicts can't be saved.
