An AI lab is thinking about adding a weird "legibility" objective to its training runs. For every response, the model has to give an answer plus a short explanation of how it got there. A separate auditor model is shown the user's question and the explanation, but not the answer, and it has to reconstruct the assistant's actual answer from that alone. The assistant gets rewarded for being correct on the original task and for how closely the auditors reconstruction matches its answer, and penalized for explanations that basically just restate the answer or are so vague the auditor can't do better than chance. Walk through how this actually plays out: what behavior it selects for, how the assistant games the metric, what happens to answers that depend on long chains of reasoning or subtle tradeoffs, and whether the lab ends up with honest legibility or just readable-sounding confabulation.
