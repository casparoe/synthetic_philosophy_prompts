Suppose the leading AI-capable states and firms establish a Breakthrough Escrow. The terms are these. A neutral technical body fixes a ladder of capability thresholds defined by a combination of training compute, benchmark performance, and the number of distinct economic tasks a system can perform without human intervention. When a signatory first crosses a threshold, it must immediately deposit in an international escrow account enough material to reproduce the advance: model weights, training code, dataset provenance, evaluation results, and a complete account of the scaling decisions that produced it. Deposit opens a thirty-six-month window of exclusive commercial deployment for the discoverer, after which the escrow is released to all signatories; but if any other signatory independently crosses the same threshold before the window closes, the escrow is released immediately. Signatories agree to continuous monitoring of all training runs above a declared scale, with unannounced physical inspections of compute sites, and concealment triggers penalties that escalate with the size of the concealed run rather than being fixed.

Analyze this as a mechanism through the rationalist explanations for war, applied to an AI race: private information about progress plus incentives to misrepresent it, commitment problems generated by shifting or potentially decisive advantages, and the perceived indivisibility of the prize of being first. What does the institution do to the incentives of a front-runner, a close second, a laggard, and the technical body itself? Where is it likely to be gamed first—in how thresholds are specified, in how capabilities are measured, in the timing of disclosure, or in the release-on-catch-up trigger—and with what effects on the real distribution of capabilities? Does the exclusivity window make a leader's private information more or less destabilizing, and does the immediate-release clause solve the prevention problem or merely relocate it? What second-order effects should we expect for research tempo, for the location of sensitive work, for the credibility of sanctions, and for the institution's survival, and can those failure modes be repaired without recreating the race the escrow was meant to suppress?
