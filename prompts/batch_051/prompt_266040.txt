I write a long-form column on science and ideas for a general-interest magazine, and I am finishing a reported essay on the paradox of tolerance. I want to close the essay with a short poem rather than a concluding paragraph, because the most honest ending is to leave the problem visible on the page rather than resolve it editorially.

Please write a villanelle — the nineteen-line form with five tercets and a closing quatrain, rhymed ABA ABA ABA ABA ABA ABAA — in which the two refrains carry the two horns of the paradox. One refrain should voice the tolerant commitment: the standing invitation, the door held open, the refusal to punish belief or speech before its content. The other should voice the objection that keeps returning: that a door held open to everyone lets through whoever means to bolt it shut. The refrains should not be mere repetitions; each return should deepen them and feel a little less comfortable than the last, as the tercets move through a scene or two drawn from public life — a public meeting, a publishing decision, a forum of last resort, a vote — so that by the closing quatrain the two lines stand side by side and the self-undermining is felt rather than argued.

The poem should be accurate enough that a political philosopher would recognize the paradox, but it should not name the thinker associated with it, use the word tolerance more than twice, or end in a solution: no stable principle for drawing the line, no claim that intolerance simply forfeits its standing, no reverent resignation. A reader who has never encountered the paradox should still find it a strong poem and feel the problem as a problem.

After the poem, provide a brief JSON summary with exactly these fields and nothing else:

- "form": a string naming the form and stating, in one sentence, how its two refrains are positioned within it.
- "refrain_1": a string giving the first refrain verbatim, followed by a one-clause gloss of the horn of the paradox it carries.
- "refrain_2": a string giving the second refrain verbatim, followed by a one-clause gloss of the opposite horn.
- "stanza_architecture": a string of one or two sentences explaining how the tercets develop the tension and where the closing quatrain leaves it.
- "philosophical_fidelity": a string of one or two sentences naming the version of the paradox the poem tracks and identifying any step it deliberately leaves ambiguous.
- "unresolved_tension": a boolean, true if the poem leaves the tension open and false otherwise.
