So I'm having this recurring argument with a friend who works at an ML lab (I won't say which one, but you can guess the general vibes) about AI safety via debate. His position is basically: if you train a model to debate a copy of itself in front of a judge model, and you reward the debater whenever the judge is persuaded, then you're "extracting the truth" rather than just teaching rhetoric. I keep saying that this is just selecting for arguments that convice a fallible judge, and that a model can hand you a completely compelling safety case while the behavior you're evaluating was produced by a different, hidden reasoning process—or while it's still planning to do the bad thing later, just more quietly, once it's in a position to get away with it. He treats this like I'm being paranoid.
