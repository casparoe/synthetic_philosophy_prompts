I am serving on a working group that has been asked to comment on a proposal to introduce a parental licensing scheme, and before our first meeting I have written out the commitments I would bring to the discussion. I want someone who does not share my priors to audit them, because I can tell the set is not entirely stable but I do not trust my own sense of where the weak joint is.

1. Parenting is among the most consequential things most adults ever do, and we already license driving, teaching, and adoption, so I think a modest parental licensing scheme is legitimate in principle. Prospective parents could be screened for clear, current indicators of serious neglect, abuse, or incapacity, and the assessment should be explicitly neutral about values, religion, and lifestyle.

2. At the same time, I think that once a family exists the state should be much more reluctant to remove a child than to screen someone beforehand. Removal is traumatic; foster care often does net harm; and I think the child welfare apparatus has a documented record of penalizing poor and minority families for parenting that is merely unfamiliar to middle-class caseworkers.

3. Children have a right to an open future: parents may not make decisions that foreclose major life options or leave the child unable, as an adult, to revise their commitments.

4. But parents are also entitled to raise their children within a particular comprehensive tradition—religious or otherwise—and to choose an education that deliberately inclines the child toward that tradition. An open future cannot require a neutral upbringing, because every upbringing tilts somewhere.

5. I hold a concrete verdict in line with that: parents should be free to send a child to an intensive, full-time religious school that offers almost no secular curriculum beyond basic literacy and arithmetic, discourages unsupervised contact with outsiders, and sends almost no graduates to university, provided there is no abuse and the child is free to leave at legal adulthood.

I would like an audit of this set. Which pairs are genuinely in tension, and which pairs only appear incompatible? For the genuine tensions, rank them by severity, say what would have to give in each case, and estimate what each fix would cost. Then tell me whether there is a single revision that would do the most to make the whole set cohere, and what I would lose by making it.
