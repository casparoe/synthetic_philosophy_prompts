Please argue that Weber's Protestant ethic thesis is best read as a causal claim about cultural values acting as stabilizers or selectors, not triggers. On this reading, ascetic Protestant values didn't create new economic ambitions out of nothing; they made one of several already available ways of organizing economic life feel morally legitimate and thereby locked it in. As part of the argument, address the objection that values can't genuinely be causes because they're usually inferred from the very behavior they're meant to explain.
