So here's the question. Suppose you were advising me — a retired natural scientist, no lab, no need to publish — on where to put roughly three years of reading and thinking within this area. Which two or three unresolved conceptual questions should I prioritize, and in what order? I care less about the list than the reasoning. I'm currently torn between three: (1) whether the "cooperation" that shows up in iterated games is really the same concept as the cooperation we care about in ethics — that is, whether the models explain normativity at all or just redescribe it; (2) how much of large-scale human cooperation among strangers is actually carried by reputation and indirect reciprocity rather than by the shadow of the future between two seperate partners; and (3) the noise/forgiveness problem, since real interactions are shot through with misperception and error, and the strategies that win change a lot once you add noise.
