I keep trying to use that frame on the debate about AI companions (Replika and the romantic chatbot services) and whether it's deceptive or wrong for them to tell users they love them and let users form real attachments. One camp says: it's deception, there's no one home to love you back. Another says: users know it's a bot, it's like fiction, nobody's actually deceived. A third says the real harm isn't deception but that you're shaping your capacity for love on a person-shaped object that never pushes back, whether or not you're fooled.
