Does the film, read this way, dramatize something uji gets right about inhabiting the present rather than treating it as a means to an end, or does its ending fall back into an ordinary sequential story of moral progress? Work through the distinct phases of Phil's arc—despair, hedonism, growing competence, and the turn toward care—and rely on paraphrase and close reading rather than quotations or secondary literature.
