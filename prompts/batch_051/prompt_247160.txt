The question must not be answerable by summarizing Adams, Stalnaker, and Lewis; it should require students to say whether either conditional has truth conditions, what governs its assertability, and which account of each conditional best explains the pair. Provide a model answer of roughly 1,200 words and a marking scheme built around five must-hit points, with the marks each is worth (total 20) and a one-line note on how candidates typically get each wrong.
