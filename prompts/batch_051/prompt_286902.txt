A witness says the cab was blue, and the witness is right 80% of the time either way (so P(says blue | cab is blue) = P(says green | cab is green) = 0.8). The standard Bayesian answer is P(cab blue | witness says blue) ≈ 41%, because the base rate does a lot of work.

So the evidence is soft: you haven't learned any proposition for sure.
