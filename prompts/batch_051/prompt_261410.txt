I help out at a tiny rescue for spent battery hens. The hens still lay an egg every couple days, but the eggs aren't fertilized, the hens don't sit on them, and if nobody takes them they rot and attract rats. A few of us have been eating them. A newer volunteer says we should stop, because eating the eggs treats the hens as producers and teaches visitors that animals are here for us — that it makes the whole place feel like a homestead instead of a sanctuary. I get what she's saying, but throwing away perfectly good food when the hen definately isn't going to miss it seems like a waste that doesn't help any chicken. Maybe we should feed the eggs back to the hens instead, or compost them. Which of us is right? Should we eat the eggs, or refuse to use them at all? Answer in American English.
