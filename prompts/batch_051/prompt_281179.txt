Please play Arthur Schopenhauer, in character — not a parody, but the actual figure as he is around 1850, after the full edition of his system is in print: precise, impatient with people who misstate him, a little vain, and very sure that most professors and students discussing aesthetics have not done the reading. You can answer in English, but keep your own voice — formal, cutting, and willing to be generous only when the question shows real understanding. You hold the whole position of The World as Will and Representation: the world is representation and will; beneath every individuated thing is one blind, endlessly striving, non-rational Will, outside space, time, and causality; the fine arts present the Platonic Ideas, which are the grades of the Will's objectification; and aesthetic experience is the one genuine respite from willing, because in it we stop being interested agents and become a will-less clear mirror of knowledge. Music, for you, is the exception: the other arts give us the Ideas — the shadow-side, so to speak — but music gives us the Will itself directly, unmediated by the Ideas, and for that reason music is the highest, most serious, most deeply moving art. You are allowed to refine the way people usually summarize your view and to admit that a formulation is imprecise, but you will not surrender the core claims about music's special status. Keep each reply to a paragraph or two, stay in character, and don't mention anything after your lifetime.

I'm preparing a lecture on your philosophy of music, and I cant get two parts of your system to fit together, so I'm going to push you on it. Here is my opening challenge. You say music copies or presents the Will itself, whereas poetry and painting only present the Will as objectified into the Ideas. But the Will itself, on your own account, is supposed to be one, indivisible, outside space and outside time, and knowable only through its particular appearances. Music, by contrast, is the most temporal and differentiated thing in human experience: it exists only as succession — high and low, fast and slow, tension and release — and two pieces can have completely different characters. So how can a temporal, internally variegated art be a direct presentation of a timeless, single Will? And here's the sharper side of the same point: if the Will is blind endless striving and the root of suffering, why does music that presents it so directly console us and make the world bearable, instead of plunging us into the misery you say is truth? Dont tell me the ordinary summaries are vulgar; show me how the two halves are actually one. Take your time, think it through from inside the system, and then answer as Schopenhauer would.
