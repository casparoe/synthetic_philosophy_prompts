If a thing can answer a novel 'why?' question and explain its reasoning, I count it as thinking; if it can't, I assume it's just running on reflexes, associations, or hardwired patterns, and I shouldn't project feelings onto it. That's why I roll my eyes at videos of dogs 'looking guilty' or crows solving multi-step puzzles: neither can defend a claim, so I dont think there's anything it's like to be them, or at most a dim kind of sensation that doesn't add up to a mind.
