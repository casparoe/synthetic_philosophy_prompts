What would count against it? Please map the position's exposure: which results, cases, and theoretical developments should move me most; which would a committed defender concede as counting against the view (for example, imagistic or map-like cognition, nonlinguistic animals, the regress objection, semantic holism, or the possibility that thought merely inherits its apparent structure from natural language); and which objections only seem threatening. Rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, and close with an honest assessment of whether the view has been qualified into unfalsifiability and whether that would be a serious flaw for a claim about cognitive architecture.
