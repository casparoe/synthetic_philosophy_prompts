I teach a small undergrad decision theory seminar, about fourteen students, 75-minute sessions, and I want to do this as a game before I ever say the words "evidential" or "causal" in class. What I want them to come away feeling is the bind itself: once the predictor has a near-perfect read on what you'll choose, the ordinary distinction between what my choice causes and what my choice reveals stops doing its job, and every way out feels like a trick I'm playing on myself.

Could you design the runnable activity? I need rules, materials I can acutally put together, a payoff scheme that doesn't require real money, and a debrief.
