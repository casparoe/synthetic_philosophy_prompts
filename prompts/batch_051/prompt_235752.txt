I'm drafting a paper on levels of explanation and want a consistency audit of the position I've worked myself into. The centerpiece is a thought experiment I've been using: a careenium, a sealed box of tiny metal balls — call them simmballs — on a motorized vibrating plate. The balls carom chaotically, but the contraption is so designed that large-scale patterns of ball motion function as a control system: it moves toward some stimuli and away from others, and an observer who attributes states such as detected-a-threat or intends-to-flee predicts it far more reliably than an observer who tracks individual balls. Here is the set of commitments I have landed on.

(1) The careenium is microphysically closed and deterministic: each simmball's path is fixed by contact forces and the plate's vibration, and the whole episode is in principle derivable from the initial microstate plus mechanics. No force acts at the level of the large-scale patterns over and above these microphysical interactions.

(2) The large-scale description is nevertheless true, not merely useful: that the machine was in a threat-detection state and moved away is a real feature of the world, and a complete listing of simmball trajectories, exhaustive as it is, leaves that organizing pattern implicit.

(3) I reject downward causation: no pattern-level event ever deflects an individual simmball from the path mechanics assigns it. The pattern's being present just consists in the right mass of simmballs moving as they do, so the micro-level and pattern-level stories are two descriptions of one causal history, not rival mechanisms.

(4) But I also hold that pattern-level states are genuine difference-makers. A particular simmball's trajectory matters to the explanation only as part of a larger pattern, and the best explanation of the machine's overall motion cites the pattern rather than the micro-detail.

(5) These patterns are multiply realizable: the same large-scale transition — threat detected, then away-motion — could have been realized by any of an enormous number of microhistories, and for explaining the behavior those micro-differences are irrelevant, even though each microhistory is by itself sufficient for the resulting behavior.

(6) Finally, I think a structure is real if it earns its keep in compression, prediction, and explanation, yet I do not take this to commit me to downward causation.

Please audit this package. Which pairs are in genuine tension, which only appear to be, and how severe is each real tension? For each, say what would have to give, and then identify the cheapest single revision that would restore joint tenability, and what it costs.
