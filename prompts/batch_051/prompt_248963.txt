So no general principle, like 'don't lie,' can do any real normative work. The particularist therefore thinks we should stop trying to justify what we do by citing rules, and instead judge each case on its own, using a trained intuition or a kind of 'seeing' what the situation calls for. Generalists object that without principles we could never teach moral judgment or criticize anyone's decision, because there would be no shared standard across cases; particularism would make morality arbitrary.
