Im in a political philosophy course on equality and dynastic wealth, and in section someone proposed this scheme and nobody could figure out whether it would work or blow up. The proposal: abolish the estate tax. Instead, treat everything a person ever receives by gift or inheritance as ordinary income, but give everyone a lifetime exemption that depends on their own lifetime earnings from work. If your earned income over your life is below the median, your exemption is large, and it phases down to zero as your earnings rise past the median. Money received above the exemption is taxed at your ordinary income rate. Treat it as a mechanism and walk me through how it would actually play out: what it does to career choices and early retirement, to how wealthy families time and route transfers, to trusts, marriage, and emigration; where it gets gamed first; who pays more than intended and who slips through; and whether the failure modes can be fixed without gutting the equal-opportunity point.
