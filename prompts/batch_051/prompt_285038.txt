Write the joint statement a thoughtful cessationist and a thoughtful continuationist could both sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration about miracles and spiritual gifts. The problem is they've talked past each other forever: one keeps reasoning from the closing of the canon and the foundational role of apostles and prophets, the other keeps going back to 1 Corinthians 13 and to stories of healing and prophecy after the apostolic age, and neither has ever stated the other's view in words the other would accept. Write the document they'd co-sign. Begin with a numbered list of claims both accept — about what the sign gifts were for, how the Spirit ordinarily works, and what should count as good evidence for a post-apostolic miracle. Then write a disagreements table. Each row should have the question in neutral wording, each side's one-sentence answer phrased so its own side would sign it, and the crux under that question that would actually move one of them. For each crux, also name a case or piece of evidence both sides agree bears on it — the Didache's rules about testing prophets, say, or Augustine's reports of healings in The City of God, or a well-documented modern healing claim — and record in advance what each side predicts about it. Don't resolve anything; just make the remaining disagreement exact.
