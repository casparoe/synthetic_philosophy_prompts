I have been trying to think through a picture that keeps coming up whenever I read about Wittgenstein's On Certainty: the idea that our most basic commitments — that the world is more than five minutes old, that there are other minds, that my hands are still there when I am not looking — work like the hinges on a door. Just as a door can swing open or shut only because something keeps it attached to the frame, ordinary doubting and checking can move only because some things are kept fixed and left outside the round of questioning. The analogy is usually brought in to answer a particular challenge. The skeptic claims that since each of my beliefs could in principle be doubted, I ought to be able to doubt all of them together, and if I cannot give a general proof of my starting points, then my whole structure of belief is ungrounded. The hinge reply is that this misdescribes what doubt is. Doubt needs something to turn on; it is not a purer form of ordinary doubt but a use of the word outside the conditions that give it sense. So the conclusion people draw is that the demand to doubt everything is not more rigorous than ordinary doubt — it is a confused one, and the skeptical worry about our most basic commitments dissolves rather than gets answered.

I have not been able to shake the sense that the hinge picture is carrying more weight than it is usually made to admit, and I would like it audited. What corresponds to what in the image — the hinge, the door, the frame, the swinging, the doorway itself? Which correspondences do the anti-skeptical work, and which are only decorative? Where does the mapping begin to break? A real hinge can be replaced one at a time while the door keeps working; it also does not determine what room the door opens into; and it is fixed in place by screws, in a way that looks very different from the way a community keeps a certainty in place. Does the analogy quietly import the idea that our basic certainties are fixed the way hardware is fixed — from outside, and once and for all — rather than something people hold in place through what they do and go on doing? Finally, does the anti-skeptical conclusion survive once these disanalogies are on the table, or was the hinge picture doing the work all along? If it does not hold up, what would be a better image for the role those basic certainties play?
