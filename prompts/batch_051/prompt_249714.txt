Because of that, the movement has a measurability bias: it only trusts interventions that produce a clean number — bednets, deworming pills, cash transfers — and it rules out anything structural from the start, like changing laws, building social movements, or organizing unions. Its 'earning to give' advice tells idealistic young people that the best way to help is to take a high-paying job at a hedge fund or an oil company and donate the salary, which effectively makes EA an endorsement of the very institutions causing the harm.
