Something's been bugging me about mathematical structuralism. People say that with the von Neumann ordinals, 2 ∈ 4 is literally true in set theory, since 2 = {0,1} and 4 = {0,1,2,3}. But structuralism tells us numbers arent sets, just positions in a structure. So would a structuralist have to say that whether 2 is an element of 4 has no determinate answer — that it just depends on an arbitrary coding choice? And if they admit that, doesnt it wreck arithmetic? I mean, when we do arithmetic we're talking about objects with identity conditions, right, so it seems like structuralism makes even basic facts like 2 < 4 weirdly contentless. Whats the structuralist move here? Answer in American English.
