Please provide an intuitive, step-by-step explanation of a conceptual issue in Thomas Metzinger's self-model theory of subjectivity. The issue is this: Metzinger holds that the phenomenal self is a transparent self-model, where transparency means that the representational vehicle is not itself experienced, so the subject seems to be in direct contact with a self rather than with a process of self-modeling. Explain why transparency produces this appearance of direct givenness, and why it would systematically conceal the constructed, representational character of the self-model from the experiencing subject. Along the way, contrast transparent phenomenal content with opaque phenomenal content, and discuss what this implies about the limits of introspection as evidence about the self. Aim for a conceptual reconstruction of the underlying mechanism rather than a survey of claims in the literature.
