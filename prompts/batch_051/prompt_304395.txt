I've started using this in arguments too: if someone says a child would be better off existing because of all the good stuff, I treat that as no evidence at all; if they say the child is better off never existing because they'd avoid a painful disease or abuse, I treat it as strong evidence. Is this actually defensible, or am I just rationalizing that I don't want kids? Where would a tilt like that even come from, and can I restate it so it doesn't just amount to 'pain counts, pleasure doesn't'?
