I am writing up a position for a seminar paper on hinge epistemology and would like an audit of the following set of commitments before I present it. Here is the view as I have come to hold it.

First, I accept the central Wittgensteinian claim from On Certainty: certainties such as that there is an external world, or that I have two hands, do not function like ordinary empirical beliefs. In normal circumstances doubt about them is not a live possibility for me, and their standing fast is part of what makes ordinary empirical inquiry possible.

Second, I take this to be fully compatible with fallibilism about empirical knowledge. No empirical claim, however deeply entrenched, is in principle immune to revision.

Third, I hold that hinge certainties are not beliefs. They are non-propositional certainties: ways of acting, or rules that fix what counts as evidence and what calls for doubt, rather than items that can be asserted, assessed for evidence, or known.

Fourth, it follows from this that hinges cannot serve as premises in inferences. Moore's attempt to prove the existence of the external world from the presence of his hands is therefore epistemically idle as a proof, although it is perfectly intelligible as a reminder or display of what stands fast for us.

Fifth, hinges nonetheless change over time. Particular certainties loosen and others harden as a world-picture changes, and I want to claim that this change can be rational — driven by something like better fit with experience and with the rest of the practice — rather than being merely a brute historical shift that happens to us.

Sixth, the fact that hinge commitments vary across communities does not make them arbitrary. Relative to a practice, there are better and worse ways of fixing what stands fast.

Seventh, some hinges are universal — for example, that there are physical objects — while others are local, such as our trust in a particular kind of scientific instrument.

I would like to know which of these commitments are genuinely in tension, which only appear to be, and how the genuine tensions should be ranked by severity. For each, say what would have to give and at what cost, and identify the single revision that would do the most to make the whole set coherent.
