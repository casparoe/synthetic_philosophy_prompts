Nothing in the rules as they stood forbade doing that. Half the table says the move is plainly valid — it was adopted by the procedure that was in force, and every legal system lets amendments change amendment rules. The other half says its a bootstrap: a bare majority can't legitimately strip every future majority of its power to change the rules, since the authority to make the change flowed from the very procedure the change dismantled. I keep flip-flopping.

Give me your actual credence that the entrenching rule is genuinely valid — not just that the players will go along with it, but that the game really has succeeded in binding its own future self by the ordinary amendment procedure. Assign a rough probability to each with a line of justification, and show how they combine to your overall number.
