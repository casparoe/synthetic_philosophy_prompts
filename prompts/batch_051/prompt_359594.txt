(2) The only faithful explanation of a computation is the computation itself or a compression of it; so any shorter explanation is faithful only insofar as the actual computation is compressible into that shorter story.

(5) Therefore "train models to be legible rather than merely correct" can only mean "use simpler models even when they're wrong more often." Legibility isn't an extra virtue; it's just preferring a clean miss to a messy hit.
