The proposition is this: the prosaic alignment program, as defined above, will be sufficient to align the first AI systems powerful enough to pose an existential risk, assuming the program receives adequate funding and talent and is pursued with ordinary institutional competence.

Give me your credence in that proposition as a number between 0 and 1. Do not give a bare number. Decompose the question into the main sub-questions your credence depends on, assign a rough probability to each, and show how they combine. In particular, address at least the following:

3. How much of the core difficulty is specifying human interests correctly under distribution shift, rather than merely controlling a system to optimize a target that has already been specified?
