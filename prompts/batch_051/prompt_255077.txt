I am looking for a further objection to, or refinement of, Morgan's canon that is not already on my list. By Morgan's canon I mean the methodological rule, standardly glossed, that animal behavior should not be interpreted as the outcome of a higher psychical faculty if it can be interpreted as the outcome of one standing lower in the psychological scale.

The objections and refinements I already know are these:

1. The scale objection: the canon assumes a single, well-ordered hierarchy of psychical faculties, but evolutionary biology gives no reason to think cognition is arranged along one unilinear ladder from lower to higher, so the very contrast on which the rule depends is not a natural kind.

2. The parsimony objection: the canon appeals to parsimony, but parsimony in evolutionary reasoning does not mean preferring the psychologically simpler mechanism; simpler cognitive mechanisms are not automatically more probable or more respectable explanations.

3. The asymmetry objection: the canon imposes an unequal burden of proof, treating cognitive explanations as guilty until proven innocent while treating deflationary explanations as innocent until proven guilty, without an empirical justification for the asymmetry.

4. The underdetermination objection: for most behaviors, some lower-level account can always be constructed if one is willing to complicate it enough, so the canon gives no decisive procedure and risks becoming an unfalsifiable bias dressed up as methodology.

5. The anthropocentrism objection: the distinction between higher and lower faculties is drawn from human introspective psychology, so the canon bakes in a human-centered standard and may rule out animal-specific cognitive processes that do not map neatly onto the human scale.

6. The historical reticence objection: by privileging lean explanations, the canon has discouraged researchers from looking for, or taking seriously, evidence of complex animal cognition, thereby slowing the field rather than disciplining it.

I want one more objection, refinement, or reinterpretation that is not on that list and not a variant of anything on it, where variant includes synonyms, special cases, and combinations. Then tell me honestly whether the new item is any good.
