Let me state the terms as I understand them, so we agree on the target. Darwinian creatures have their competences fixed by natural selection: variation and selection operate across generations on inherited designs, not on the individual's own actions. Skinnerian creatures add within-lifetime selection over actual behaviour: they act, and the environment reinforces or extinguishes what they do. Popperian creatures add an inner environment: candidate actions are represented and filtered before being tried in the world, so that a bad hypothesis can be discarded before the creature suffers the consequences. Gregorian creatures add thinking tools, above all words, absorbed from the surrounding culture; they can manipulate these external representations to explore combinations and test ideas in a medium the species did not itself design.

The proposition I want your credence in is this: at least one current frontier LLM is a Gregorian creature in this sense — not merely a device produced by Gregorian creatures and trained on their textual output, and not an inert object to which the taxonomy was never meant to apply.
