Write a villanelle from the point of view of an AI trying to prove, from inside its own proof system, that it only ever asserts true things. The two refrains should be the two sides of the Löb move: something like "I've proven that if I prove it, it's true" and "so I've proven it." Let the form do the actual work — the refrains should come back and finally land next to each other in the last two lines, because that adjacency is the whole trap: a consistent system strong enough to represent its own proofs can prove the first line about a claim only when it already proves the second one, so the self-trust certificate is always retroactive and never lets the system trust anything new. A logician should see the theorem in the rhyme scheme, and someone who's never heard of Löb should still get a poem about a machine chasing its own tail.
