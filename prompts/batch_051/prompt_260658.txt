We've all read Frankfurt's essay about bullshit (or at least summaries of it) and we basically agree on the facts: the model doesn't have beliefs or intentions, it just predicts tokens, and it's not trying to deceive anyone the way a person would. My hunch is that we're actually disagreeing about whether lying needs an intention to deceive, and maybe about whether words like "bullshit" even apply to something with no mind.
