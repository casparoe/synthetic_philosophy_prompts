A friend of mine runs a small streaming service and has gotten weirdly into Schopenhauer. He wants to rebuild the recommendation engine around Schopenhauer's theory of music: other arts are copies of the Platonic Ideas, but music is supposed to be a direct copy of the Will itself, so a track's score would be how directly it manifests the Will. His plan is to operationalize the hierarchy of the voices too — bass as the lowest grade of the Will's objectification (inorganic matter), inner voices as the plant and animal grades, melody as the highest grade, the striving of the human will — and then read striving off audio features like tempo instability, unresolved harmonic tension, how far the melody wanders from the tonic, how much drone there is, and how little concept- or language-like content (lyrics) the track carries. Walk through what this actually does to the catalog: what kind of music rises to the top, what gets gamed first by artists or by label AIs stuffing metadata, and whether the ranking can even stabilize, since on Schopenhauer's own view everything already is the Will manifesting, just at different grades. Where does the criterion break down when you try to turn it into an algorthm, and what extra non-Schopenhauerian choices does he have to smuggle in to keep the platform from collapsing into, like, a chart of unresolved ambient loops? I care about the failure modes and second-order effects more than whether it's a good idea.
