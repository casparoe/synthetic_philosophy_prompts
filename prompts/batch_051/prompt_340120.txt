I have been trying to get a clearer picture of inverse reinforcement learning — the problem of recovering an agent's objective or "reward function" from observations of its behavior — and I was struck by how badly the inference is underdetermined. Once you observe a policy, or a set of state-action trajectories, there is a whole family of reward functions for which that policy is optimal. A constant reward function makes every policy optimal, and adding certain reward-shaping terms can preserve the ordering of policies in the standard setting; even when the dynamics are known, behavior alone does not pick out a unique objective. The usual response is to break the tie with a further criterion, such as preferring the maximum-entropy or maximally parsimonious reward function consistent with the observed behavior.

The structure of this problem reminds me of Quinean underdetermination in the philosophy of science: any finite body of evidence is compatible with indefinitely many theories, so theory choice is governed not only by consistency with the data but also by pragmatic virtues such as simplicity, conservatism, and scope. The mapping I have in mind is this: observed behavior corresponds to evidence; the reward function corresponds to the theory; optimality under the reward corresponds to consistency with the data; reward ambiguity corresponds to underdetermination; and entropy regularization or simplicity of the inferred reward corresponds to the role of simplicity in theory choice.

I would like to know whether this parallel is deep or superficial. Is the sense in which observed behavior fails to identify a unique reward genuinely the same kind of failure as the sense in which evidence fails to identify a unique theory, or do they merely share the word "underdetermined"? What would need to be true on each side for results to transfer from one domain to the other, and what would the parallel predict that turns out false? I am especially unsure whether the trivial or degenerate reward functions that complicate inverse reinforcement learning have any counterpart in scientific theory choice, or whether they are artifacts of the formalism with no epistemological analogue.
