But Lewis's triviality results seem to show these can't both be right, at least if the conditional expresses a proposition and probabilities update by conditionalising. I'm not asking you to pick a side for me. Give me the strongest case for dropping the equation, the strongest case for dropping the truth-conditional picture, and a clear-eyed view of what each move costs; then say which you'd give up and why — or whether there's a non-fudged reading on which they both survive. If the honest answer is 'a bit of both', tell me where the boundary is.
