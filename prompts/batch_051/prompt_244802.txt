My pet theory is that the real disagreement is about what a probability like 10^-12 even is at that point — one side treating it as a real chance and the other as a rough guess that shouldnt have infinite leverage — and that the two sides are quietly doing different projects (normative idealization vs. advice for bounded agents). Is that the actual crux, or is it a substantive normative disagreement about decision theory, a disagreement about idealization, or something else?
