Had a long argument with a friend last night about whether two rational people can look at the same evidence and still, in good faith, disagree. We kept circling Aumann's agreement theorem, and by the end I wrote down what I think I actually believe. I want a consistency check before I go back to him. Here's the list:

(1) Aumann's theorem is fine as math: if two people share a prior, and their posterior probabilities for some claim are common knowledge between them, those posteriors have to match. I'm not disputing that.

(2) But in the real world, two rational people can see the same evidence and end up with different degrees of belief, with neither of them making a mistake. The difference just traces back to their having started with different priors.

(3) So I think of the common prior assumption as a modeling idealization, not a norm of rationality. There's no principled way to demand that everyone start from the same prior, and as long as a prior is coherent I don't see how to call it more or less rational than another one.

(4) And yet I also want to say that rational disagreement can't rest on a prior that no possible evidence could move. If someone assigns literal zero probability to some contingent claim, so that no amount of shared data could ever budge them off that zero, I don't think I can count that as rational disagreement on both sides. That looks like a defect in the prior, not just a different starting point.

(5) In practice, when I run a forecasting exercise or aggregate a group's credences, I'm perfectly happy to act as if there's a common prior: combine everyone's numbers, compare them to base rates, all that. I don't think that commits me to claiming the participants actually shared one.

Which of these are actually in tension and which only look like it? Go pairwise through the list, rank the tensions by severity, and for each real tension say what would have to give. Then tell me the single revision that cleans up the most. I genuinly can't tell how much of this is real tension and how much is just sloppy wording on my end.
