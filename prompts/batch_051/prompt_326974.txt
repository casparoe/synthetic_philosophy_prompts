Been chewing on Pascal's idea that justice without force is powerless and force without justice is tyranny, so the two have to be fused together. Suppose a small polity tries to constitutionalize it with a two-key rule: no law is valid unless it's certified by both (a) a Justice Council of randomly drafted citizens serving one-year terms, who are told to ask only whether the law is just, and (b) a Force Council made up of the senior commanders of the police and military, who are told to ask only whether they can and will enforce it. Both certifications have to come within ninety days or the proposal dies, and neither body can amend the text. Walk me through how this actually plays out: what each council starts optimizing for, how legislators draft laws differently, where it gets gamed first, and wether the failure modes can be fixed without just giving one council a veto over the other.
