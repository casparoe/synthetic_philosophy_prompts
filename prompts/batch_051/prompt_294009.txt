While it stays, it can guide you just as well as knowledge can, but it doesn't stay by itself, so until it's tied down it isn't worth much. The account of the reason why is the tether: once you have it, the belief becomes knowledge and stays put, so the added value is stability — knowledge doesn't run away, and it can be called up later or passed on to someone else. I also think the account makes the belief more likely to be true than a lucky guess, and that's part of the value too, since a belief that's more likely to be true is worth more than one that merely happens to be right.
