5. A sequence of slow descending minor arpeggios generated by a computer from a random seed, with no human composer selecting the notes: I hear it as sad-sounding, but I'm inclined to say it isn't really sad. This is the case I keep going back and forth on.
6. A loud, aggressive, staccato piece in a minor key: I hear anger, not sadness, though the key is minor.
7. A bird's call that happens to trace a falling minor third: not sad, even though the contour matches a classic sad gesture.
8. A novice composer who fully intends to write something sad but produces bouncy major chords: not sad, despite the intention.
9. A wordless piece in an unfamiliar scale that some culture I don't know uses as a lament: I don't feel it as sad, but I hesitate to deny that it is sad.
