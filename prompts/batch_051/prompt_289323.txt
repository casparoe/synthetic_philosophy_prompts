In the tech world I keep hearing the opposite-sounding idea that artifacts have politics — that an app can be racist or a design can be authoritarian, not just the people who made or use it. Before you write, do some brainstorming silently: list a few candidate readings and then seperate out which ones matter. Then walk me through the distinct precise claims the slogan could be making.
