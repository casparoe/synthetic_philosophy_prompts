She's around 0.85 on this; I'm stuck around 0.4, and we can't even agree on which premise we're actually fighting about.

So give me your own credence, but more importantly decompose it. Walk me through the sub-questions you think do the real work—for instance, whether these categories were ever meant to be natural kinds in a strict sense, how often patients take up a label and reshape their self-understanding around it, how much that actually alters symptoms, course, or prevalence, and whether a category could still be a robustly real or useful kind even if looping happens. Assign a rough probability to each, show how they combine, and tell me which sub-question your final number is most sensitive to. Then say where the disagreement between 0.85 and 0.4 most likely lives: which sub-question would have to move for one of us to end up near the other. Finally, tell me what would change your mind—what evidence or argument would shift your number the most, and where do you think your reasoning is most likely to fail. Dont give me a fence-straddling answer; I want a number with its weak points marked.
