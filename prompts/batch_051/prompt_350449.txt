I'm putting together a three-year research programme on epistemic dependence on AI — six or so postdocs, conceptual work only, so no surveys of how much radiologists or coders actually defer to models, and no "here's how to fix the curriculum" stuff. The obvious questions on the table are the usual ones: whether relying on an opaque model for answers can ever amount to understanding; whether deferring to a language model is different in kind from deferring to an expert colleague, or just in degree; what exactly gets lost when someone never learns to derive things by hand or write a first draft; whether extended-mind style views survive once the tool is one that changes under you and has no stable dispositions. I dont trust that list, partly because it's what everyone already argues about, and partly because I suspect some of those questions are downstream of others or wouldn't change much if answered.

Please include at least one question that isn't on the standard "extended cognition, trust in AI, deskilling" menu — something underappreciated — and make the case for it being near the top.
