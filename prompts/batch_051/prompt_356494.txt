I recently spent a long evening arguing with a friend about whether Newton’s use of vis insita in the Principia can be translated, without distortion, as inertial mass, or whether that rendering is an anachronism that imports a later concept into an early modern text. He kept appealing to the passage in Definition III, in Andrew Motte’s English translation, where Newton characterizes the vis insita as an innate force of matter by which a body continues in its state, says that this force is always proportional to the body and differs from the inactivity of the mass only in our manner of conceiving it, and adds that a body exerts it only when another impressed force tries to change its condition. My friend thinks this is plainly the modern notion of inertial mass. I think it is a corporeal force or power of resisting, belonging to matter, and that identifying it with mass misses what the passage is doing. We could not locate the disagreement.

I would like your credence in the following proposition: the best modern interpretation of Newton’s vis insita in Definition III of the Principia is that it denotes inertial mass, rather than a corporeal force or power of resisting, or the property we now call inertia. Please give me a number, but more importantly, decompose it into the sub-questions you take the claim to depend on. At a minimum, I ask you to assign rough probabilities to something like the following and combine them explicitly: (a) that Newton had a stable concept of quantity of matter distinct from weight and from motive force; (b) that his proportionality claim in Definition III picks out a measure of body rather than a power exerted only under impressed force; (c) that the phrase vis inertiae should be read as naming the same magnitude as vis insita rather than naming a distinct explanatory power; (d) that the English word mass in its modern sense is a reasonable target for this concept; and (e) that the charge of anachronism is itself the right diagnostic tool here. Then tell me where the disagreement between my friend and me most likely lives, and which of these sub-questions would move your credence most if you learned it was false.
