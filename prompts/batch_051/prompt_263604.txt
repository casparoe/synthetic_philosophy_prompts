I don't need the explanation itself; I need a plan for teaching it in about 45 minutes to reporters who don't know any stats. What has to land first, and what has to wait for what, so the outside view doesn't just sound like "look at the averages"? What single example should carry the whole thing — an empire, a firm, a marriage — and what does it need to have and lack for this audience? What should I out loud refuse to explain at this level?
