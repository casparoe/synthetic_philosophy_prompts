I am a software engineer with no formal training in philosophy. I was recently asked to defend a view about games as art, and in doing so I wrote down the claims I seem to hold. I would like a consistency audit of the set before I defend it in public. I am less interested in whether each claim is independently true than in whether the set hangs together: which pairs are in genuine tension, which pairs are only apparently in tension, how severe each genuine tension is, and what the least costly revision would be. After that, please tell me which single revision would do the most to make the set consistent.

The claims:

1. Games are an art form, and their distinctive medium as an art form is agency: the structure of goals, abilities, constraints, and choices that a designer creates for a player.
2. A game's aesthetic value is primarily a function of how interesting its decision space is. A game with crude graphics, no story, and no characters can be aesthetically better than a technically polished one, provided its decisions are more absorbing.
3. Games with almost no meaningful decisions, such as walking simulators or some visual novels, can be excellent art. The near-total absence of meaningful choice does not by itself disqualify a game from high aesthetic value.
4. Player freedom in games is in an important sense illusory. The player only ever selects from a possibility space the designer has authored, whether explicitly through branching or through rules that generate the space; the player never originates the game's possibilities.
5. In sufficiently complex systems, players routinely produce emergent play that the designer never anticipated or intended, such as novel strategies or unintended uses of mechanics, and this emergent play has its own aesthetic value.
6. The special aesthetic value of game agency lies less in the causal outcomes of a player's choices than in the experience of deliberating, committing, and taking responsibility for a choice made under constraints. What happens after the choice matters less aesthetically than the fact and quality of the choice itself.

Please go through these six claims and tell me which pairs are in genuine tension and how badly. For each genuine tension, say what has to give and what the cheapest revision would be. For each apparent tension, say exactly why it dissolves. Then tell me which single revision would clean up the most.
