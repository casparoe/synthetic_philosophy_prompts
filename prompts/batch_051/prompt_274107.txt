I'm not asking you to explain the three positions to me. I want the plan for how to teach it, because last year I just presented the views one after another and the students came away thinking it's a terminological squabble about how strict we should be.

I need the order in which the components have to go and why each depends on the previous one — for instance I keep flip-flopping on wether they need a clean distinction between judging the speaker and judging the speech act before they meet any of the three norms. Then the misconceptions I should expect — my money is on "the norm is just about not lying," "the knowledge account reduces to the belief account plus truth," and "the norm describes what speakers actually do rather than what assertion permits." Tell me exactly where in the sequence I should head each one off, what to leave out and explicitly flag as left out, and one question I can ask at the end to tell whether the distinction actually landed.
