In work on transformative experience — choices whose outcomes change what it is like to be you, so their value cannot be known from the inside in advance — several open questions compete for attention. Some are decision-theoretic: whether the genuinely hard case is ignorance of a future phenomenal state or instability in one's future preferences; whether making the transformation reversible removes the problem or merely moves it; whether testimony from people who have already changed can restore ordinary expected-value reasoning; and, when it cannot, whether the person should satisfice, defer, or switch to a non-consequentialist criterion. These questions are unlikely to be equally important or equally workable, and some cannot be fruitfully addressed before others are. Suppose you are advising a small research fund with roughly three person-years to allocate in this area, using only conceptual, formal, and armchair-experimental methods. Which two or three questions should be funded, and in what order? Give a dependency-aware agenda rather than a survey: for each candidate, say what progress elsewhere it would make possible, how workable it is under those methods, and which widely discussed question you would decline to fund because answering it would change little downstream. Defend the ordering against at least one plausible rival, and name the single result that would most change it.

Structure your response as follows:

- 'Candidates and stakes' (~120 words): the main open questions under consideration and what turns on each.
- 'Dependency and workability' (~150 words): which questions are prerequisites for which others, and how workable each is under the stated methods.
- 'The funded agenda' (~150 words): your two or three funded questions in order, with the criteria you used and how you weighted importance against workability.
- 'A plausible rival' (~100 words): the strongest alternative agenda you rejected and why yours is stronger.
- 'What would change the ranking' (~80 words): the single conceptual or empirical result that would most reorder your agenda.
