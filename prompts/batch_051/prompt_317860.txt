The idea is that if you can't write it cold, you don't understand it well enough to use it. Treat it as a mechanism, not a newsroom-culture sermon. Who actually complies, who games it, and how? What does it do to the early-career reporters who lean on AI the most? Do the archived cold paragraphs become a status signal, a new source of error, or both? Where does the skill loss actually show up if this becomes normal? Then tell me whether there is a tweak that keeps the pressure to read the source but kills the gaming — or whether the whole thing is chasing a distinction between reading it yourself and actually understanding it yourself that was never doing the work.
