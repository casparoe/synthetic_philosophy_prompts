What's a side effect? I want a definition someone whose trying to build a low-impact AI could actually use. By low-impact I mean a system that's told to do a job while keeping its side effects small. That project seems to need a notion of side effect that doesn't smuggle in a full specification of everything that matters; if you already had that, you wouldn't need the word at all, you'd just rank possible outcomes by total value (or whatever). So give me a working definition that takes the intended task or function as input and tells you which further effects count as side effects. It should get these cases right:

1. You take paracetamol for a headache and it irritates your stomach. Obviously a side effect.
2. A Roomba picks up dirt from the carpet. The dirt being gone is the point, not a side effect.
3. A chatbot, asked to summarise a paper, briefly spins up its servers' cooling fans. Side effect?
4. A self-driving car takes you through the roadworks, so you're ten minutes late. Is the lateness a side effect, or just the task done badly?
5. An AI told to "make the flat tidy" throws away a half-finished letter because it looks like clutter. Everyone wants to say that's a side effect and a bad one. Is that because it was irreversible, or because it touched something the user cares about, or both?
6. An AI told to "avoid side effects" does literally nothing all day. Does inaction count as a side effect, or is it a dodge of the whole concept?
7. Minoxidil was a blood-pressure drug whose side effect was hair growth; now people take it for the hair growth. That makes "side effect" look relative to a purpose. Do we just accept that relativity, or can the definition do better?

Propose the definition, run it through all seven cases, and tell me where it gives the wrong answer or no answer. Then say whether, for the low-impact AI use, we'd be better off dropping "side effect" entirely and instructing the system to avoid changes to things the person who gave the instruction would probably prefer left alone — and what we'd lose if we did that.
