I've got ninety minutes and a whiteboard, and I want them to leave with the actual shape of the argument, not just that time is a mystery. Don't explain it to me; plan how I should teach it. What should I openly leave out at this level (divine eternity, Plotinus, what God was doing before creation) so the session doesn't drown? And give me one question to end on that only someone who got the specifically Augustinian point — and not just "time is in the mind" — could answer.
