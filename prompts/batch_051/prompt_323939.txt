But then someone else in the room pointed out that the US gives churches tax exemptions and pays for military chaplains, England has an established church with bishops in the legislature, and France bans conspicuous religious symbols in public schools — and all four countries claim to be some kind of secular, or at least church-state-separated. So the slogan is doing a lot of work and I don't think everyone means the same thing by it.

I want to know what precise claims that sentence could be making, not some cannonical definition. Are those all actually different, and which are platitudes versus genuinely contested norms versus just false as descriptions of any real country?
