Been rereading the bits of A Theory of Justice on envy, especially the idea that envy can be excusable when the inequalities it answers to are unjust. I keep going back and forth on whether that means my envy is actually picking up on something — a real injustice, in the way resentment is supposed to — or whether it's just that I cant be blamed for feeling it, even though it's still a vice. If it's the first, why does Rawls bother keeping envy and resentment separate in the first place? But if it's the second, then whats really gained by calling it excusable: isn't that just saying wrong but understandable? And in that case, should I ever treat my own envy as evidence that some setup is unjust?
