In chapter 25 of Niccolò Machiavelli's *The Prince*, he writes:

> I compare her to one of those raging rivers, which when in flood overflows the plains, sweeping away trees and buildings, bearing away the soil from place to place; everything flies before it, all yield to its violence, without being able in any way to withstand it; and yet, though its nature be such, it does not follow therefore that men, when the weather becomes fair, shall not make provision, both with defences and barriers, in such a manner that, rising again, the waters may pass away by canal, and their force be neither so unrestrained nor so dangerous. So it happens with fortune, who shows her power where valour has not prepared to resist her, and thither she turns her forces where she knows that barriers and defences have not been raised to constrain her.

Using this passage as the fixed text, construct a thought experiment to specification. Build a single invented case where one prince's character—his virtù, his mode of directing his actions—remains strictly unchanged, while fortune shifts from favoring him to ruining him. The case should be invented, not historical; roughly a paragraph long; no demons, no perfect predictors, no magic. Its causal structure must follow the river-and-banks image: the same method that was adequate while conditions were calm becomes inadequate when the flood arrives, not because the prince weakens or changes, but because the terrain around him changes. Then do the check. State the prince's method, the change in the times, the two outcomes, and why Machiavelli's principles in this passage generate those outcomes. What precise feature of the case does the work? If that feature were removed, what would collapse? Finish by saying what a defender of Machiavelli would say to someone who concludes that this case shows virtù is not a stable property of the prince but a relation between his method and the times. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence and then give the reasoning, with no preamble and no restating of the question.
