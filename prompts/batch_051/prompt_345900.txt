(1) the Kripke-style necessary a posteriori cases: I can seemingly conceive of water not being H2O, or heat not being mean molecular motion, but those are supposed to be impossible, so conceivability can't just be possibility.

(4) van Inwagen-style modal skepticism: our modal judgments are only trustworthy close to home, and conceivability arguments for zombies or disembodied minds ask them to reach way beyond that.
