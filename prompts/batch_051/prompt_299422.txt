I have spent the past year working within a broadly Woodwardian manipulationist account of causation. On the position as I hold it, causal claims are best understood in terms of what would happen to an outcome variable under possible interventions on a putative cause variable, and explanatory depth is a matter of the range of interventions over which a generalisation remains invariant. I also accept Woodward's treatment of the familiar circularity worry: the characterisation of an intervention is itself a causal notion, but because the account is not offered as a reductive definition of cause, this circularity is not by itself damaging.

I would like a serious map of what would count against this view. If I am wrong, what would have to be true? I am asking for the evidence, arguments, formal results, and cases that should move a committed interventionist, ranked from decisive through significant to merely awkward. For each, please distinguish whether it is something a defender of the position should themselves concede as counting against it, or something they may reasonably dismiss as demanding what the view never promised. In particular, I want to know whether the circularity worry returns in a more vicious form once the account is asked to do epistemological or semantic work, for instance in fixing which variables are appropriate or in guiding causal discovery; whether the liberality about choice of variables is a genuine liability rather than a feature; what the view predicts about unfamiliar or non-modular systems that rival accounts do not, such that failures of those predictions would count against it; and whether the position has been, or can be, insulated by qualifications to the point of unfalsifiability, and whether that would be a problem for a view of this kind. I would personally be most moved by cases in which a generalisation is highly invariant under interventions yet intuitively does not explain, or in which a clear causal relation resists any natural interventionist representation.

I am not asking for a defence of the view, and I am not asking for a survey of rival theories. I want an honest assessment of how exposed the position actually is, and where its genuine weaknesses lie.
