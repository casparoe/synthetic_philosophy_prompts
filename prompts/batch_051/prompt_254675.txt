In the Sleeping Beauty problem, imagine the usual setup except for one twist. The coin is fair. On heads she's woken once, on Monday. On tails she's woken Monday and Tuesday, with the usual amnesia between wakings. But the amnesia drug is imperfect in a known way: on a tails Tuesday, with probability p the drug fails and she wakes up with a clear memory of Monday's interview; with probability 1-p it works as usual and she remembers nothing. On Monday she never has a memory of an earlier waking, since there wasn't one. Beauty knows all this before she goes under Sunday night.

Heres the thing I want you to work through. When she wakes up and has no memory of an earlier interview, what should her credence be that the coin landed heads? Give me the thirder-style line and the halfer-style line as a function of p, not just for p = .5. Then tell me whether this partial-recall twist makes the two camps converge, move apart, or something less tidy, and where each answer goes at the p = 0 and p = 1 endpoints. If the result turns on a choice about how self-locating evidence should be formalized, spell that out instead of pretending there's a clean consensus.

Also: say how confident you are in the overall analysis and single out the specific point you're least sure of.
