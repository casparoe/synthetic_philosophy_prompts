I want to know what, exactly, the person saying it is claiming. Is it the weak claim that probability axioms extend deductive logic to degrees of belief, the strong Cox-theorem claim that any plausibility measure obeying a few consistency desiderata must be a probability measure, the methodological claim that scientific inference is just Bayesian updating, or the rhetorical claim that non-Bayesian procedures are incoherent? Lay out the distinct precise theses this slogan could express, flag which are trivial, which are substantive and defensible, which are false or overreach, and say which reading is doing the work when the slogan is used to dismiss a p-value-based analysis. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence and then give your reasoning, with no preamble and no restating of the question.
