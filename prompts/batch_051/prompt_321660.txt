Just got out of a long argument with a friend about whether it makes sense to call an AI dishonest when it never says anything false. He kept insisting that deception needs an intention to mislead, and since a language model doesnt have intentions, a model that only outputs literally true sentences is in the clear. I think thats the wrong level of analysis. Please argue for the view that an AI system can be deceptive even if every individual claim it produces is literally true, because what matters is how the whole exchange predictably moves the user's credences away from the world, not the truth-value of each sentence in isolation. Make the strongest version of the argument you can.
