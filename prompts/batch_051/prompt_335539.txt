Lena, one of my roommates, is really into Greek myth. A month ago she promised to co-sign our friend Marcus's lease, and to show him how serious she was she swore on the river Styx — the one oath even the gods can't break without punishment. Now it turns out Marcus lied to her about his income and quit his job, and co-signing could tank her credit. Lena says she can't back out: a Styx oath isn't a normal promise you can revisit, it's more like a self-curse. Our other roommate says that's ridiculous, an oath is just a promise with window dressing, so of course she can back out. Lena asked me what I think. Is a Styx oath actually different from a ordinary promise, and does it bind her here, given that he lied and the situation changed? Keep your answer under 400 words.
