I have been asked to circulate a statement of my views on well-being ahead of a discussion, and I should like an audit of its coherence before I send it. This is the position I have set down.

At bottom I accept an informed-desire account of well-being: what makes a life go better or worse is, in the end, only the satisfaction or frustration of the desires a person would have if they vividly and accurately appreciated the nature of the alternatives.

I hold that variety — regular exposure to new activities, places, and people — is a constituent of a good life in its own right, not merely a means to pleasure or to the achievement of other ends.

I hold that monotony can make a life worse even when no desire, actual or idealised, is frustrated: a person whose days are serenely identical and who would not, even after full acquaintance with the alternatives, prefer change may nonetheless be leading a worse life than they could have led, simply in virtue of the repetition.

I hold that a person who enters an experience machine — a device that delivers the felt experiences of a flourishing life while none of the corresponding achievements, relationships, or discoveries really obtain — has a worse life than they would have in the corresponding real scenario, even if they never discover the difference.

I hold that there is no general prudential requirement to seek adventure: some people are better off in settled, familiar, largely repetitive lives, and a cautious person who repeatedly declines opportunities for variety need not have made their life worse by doing so.

I hold that the fear of living a boring life is ordinarily a reliable signal of a real prudential deficiency, even when the person cannot identify any lost pleasure or frustrated preference.

Please audit this set. I should like to know which pairs of commitments are in genuine tension and which only appear to be, how severe each genuine tension is, which commitment or sub-claim would have to give in each case and what the revision would cost, and which single revision would do most to restore coherence.
