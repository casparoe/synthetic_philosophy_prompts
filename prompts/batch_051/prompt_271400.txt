My friend Dana and I have a running game where I make up a person and she tells me whether the person has a reason to do the thing. She's maddeningly consistent, but she won't just tell me the rule. Here's what she's said so far:

- Leo wants to quit smoking and already uses the patch, but he hates the morning routine and the gum's aftertaste. Dana says he has a reason to keep using it.
- Priya has no urge to visit her dad in the hospital right now and no guilt yet. She isn't even thinking about future regret. But she's the sort of person who reliably gets flooded with regret after skipping visits like this, and if she thought it through from that, she'd want to have gone. Dana says Priya has a reason to go.
- Sam is dead set on running a marathon and would be genuinely happier with himself if he stuck to the training plan; the long runs themselves bore him out of his mind. Dana says he has a reason to do the next one.
- Mara has zero interest in taking up piano: no secret curiosity, no one she wants to impress, and it wouldn't make her happier or better in any way she'd notice. Dana says she has no reason to take piano lessons, and that people who tell her otherwise are mostly projecting thier own tastes.

There's nothing in his head right now that points toward keeping the relationship up.
