So obviously I want the minimum wage raised, and I also want rent control to stay.

My friend's dad is an econ professor, and my friend came back from dinner with him insisting that minimum wage and rent control are secretly the same kind of policy — the government stepping in and fixing a price instead of letting the market set it. His whole argument is that I can't support one and not the other without being inconsistent, since the logic that makes rent control bad also makes minimum wage bad, or the logic that makes minimum wage good also makes rent control good.
