Lately I keep seeing the phrase 'Western values' used like everyone knows what it names. Give me a working definition of Western values that correctly classifies these cases: freedom of speech; rule of law; gender equality; capital punishment; Christianity; the nuclear family; the welfare state; arranged marriage; theocracy; and the idea that the individual is the basic unit of society rather than the family or clan. I'm pretty confident freedom of speech and the individual-over-clan one are in, and arranged marriage and theocracy are out. But I honestly dont know what to do with gender equality or the welfare state: people call them Western values all the time, but they're both pretty recent, and in other arguments they get described as Western impositions. After you propose a definition, run it through all ten and tell me which verdicts you're least secure about, and whether you think the phrase is mostly describing something real or mostly just marking a side in an argument.
