From what I've seen, she's never faced a case where a lower stance was adequate but a higher stance was better.

I need you to say (1) which stance Okafor would most likely put on the list and why, (2) which earlier case is the closest analog, (3) what principle she'd be committing to under each possible verdict, and (4) one further case that would cleanly separate whatever live candidate rules are left. Then tell me where your reconstruction of her rule is most fragile, and what information or argument would most likely change your mind.
