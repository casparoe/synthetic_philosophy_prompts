I spent a long afternoon arguing with a friend about Zhuangzi on death, and the conversation left me holding a package of views that I have not seen laid out together. I would like someone to audit it rather than give me a general introduction to the text. Here is where I have landed. First, death is not the annihilation of a person but one phase in a single process of transformation: what dies is a temporary congealing of qi, not a fixed self, and the process continues in another form. Second, the ordinary fear of one's own death is therefore a kind of cognitive confusion, since it treats the self as the sort of enduring item that could be present at its own dissolution. Third, the ideal in the text is not merely the removal of an error but a change in one's affective orientation: the sage would no more resent dying than a piece of metal would protest being cast into one vessel rather than another. Fourth, this acceptance is compatible with grief at another person's death; Zhuangzi himself begins the episode about his wife by crying before he stops, and I take that sequence to show that the first movement of loss is natural and unargued, even if it cannot justify itself once the transformation is seen clearly. Fifth, not every death is equally in order: the text repeatedly praises living out the span one has been given and criticizes deaths brought on by being useful to rulers, by strain, or by overtaxing the body, as though there is a real difference between dying in one's own time and dying prematurely.

I want the audit to be unsentimental. Tell me which of these five commitments pull against which, ordered by severity; which pairs are only apparently in tension and why; and what the cheapest revision would be. If one of the commitments quietly borrows a notion the others cannot underwrite, I would rather hear that than have the package flattened into consistency. And if I have posed the task badly, or the commitments themselves are too underdescribed to audit, say so plainly instead of pretending the set hangs together.
