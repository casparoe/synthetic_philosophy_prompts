After a fair amount of reading around the units of selection debate, I've settled into a set of commitments that I want checked for coherence rather than for correctness. I'd rather learn which of them cannot be held together as stated, and what the least costly repair is, than be told which individual claim is false.

Here is where I've landed.

(1) For adaptation, I default to the gene's-eye view. Selection requires replicators with differential success, and the stable replicator is the gene lineage. Organisms matter, but they are vehicles or interactors, not units of selection in the same sense.

(2) Most appeals to group selection in the animal-behavior literature look to me like kin selection or reciprocity redescribed at the group level, and the redescription adds no predictive power. I wouldn't call them false exactly; I'd call them unilluminating.

(3) Yet I accept species selection in macroevolution as a distinct phenomenon. If species vary heritably in properties such as geographic range or dispersal mode, and those properties bias speciation or extinction rates, then selection is operating above the organism, and I take this to be more than gene selection viewed from higher up.

(4) I'm also drawn to the thought that much of this fight is bookkeeping. Under a multilevel, Price-style decomposition, the same evolutionary change can be rewritten with selection terms at different levels while the total stays the same, so the choice of level often looks pragmatic rather than factual.

(5) And I make an exception for human cultural evolution, where I take group-level selection to be not just permissible but often the best description. Norms, institutions, and reputational systems seem to spread partly because the groups carrying them outcompete or outlast groups that don't, and I doubt this reduces cleanly to kin selection among genes.

Do these five hang together? I can tell that some of them are doing real explanatory work and some are doing bookkeeping, but I can't reliably sort them myself, and I'd like the sorting done for me rather than have my own guess confirmed. Please audit the set instead of arguing for or against any one item. I'd like the tensions ordered by severity, each with a statement of what would have to give, and I'd like any pair that only appears to be in tension flagged with an explanation of why the tension dissolves. If the cheapest repair is not to drop one of the five but to refine how one is stated, say which refinement and what it costs.
