In the Mūlamadhyamakakārikā, Nāgārjuna says roughly that the buddhas taught emptiness as the relinquishing of all views, and that anyone who takes emptiness itself to be a view is incurable (usually cited as 13.8). But in the chapter on the noble truths he says roughly that what is dependently co-arisen is what we call emptiness; it is a dependent designation, and that is itself the middle path (usually cited as 24.18). These tug in opposite directions as far as I can tell: the first says emptiness isn't a position you can assert, the second sure sounds like asserting emptiness as a claim about dependent origination.
