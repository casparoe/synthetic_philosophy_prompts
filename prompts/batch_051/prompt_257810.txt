I am trying to write up my views on food choices and animal suffering for a seminar, and I would like an audit of the package before I present it. My commitments, as I would currently state them, are these:

(1) The basic currency for comparing diets is expected net suffering across all sentient beings; an option is better just insofar as it has lower expected total suffering.
(2) I accept a doing/allowing asymmetry: harms I cause count more heavily than equivalent harms I merely fail to prevent, which is why I treat animal agriculture as more objectionable than leaving wild ecosystems alone.
(3) But I reject the idea that wild animal suffering can be ignored as merely natural: when a food system clears land, kills or displaces wild animals, or changes the climate, those effects belong in the same ledger.
(4) On beef versus chicken, I currently judge chicken to be better: chickens convert feed to meat far more efficiently, and cattle's methane emissions contribute to climate change that, on my own view, will cause enormous wild animal suffering.
(5) I also accept that crop cultivation kills very large numbers of small animals, such as rodents, insects and ground-nesting birds, so I do not treat plant-based eating as zero-harm; I simply do not know how to weigh a field mouse's suffering against a chicken's.
(6) Finally, I am drawn to the thought that if wild animal lives contain more suffering than happiness on balance, then reducing wild animal populations could itself be good; yet I find that conclusion repugnant and would resist any view that committed me to it.

I cannot tell whether the strain I feel in this set is real or just the ordinary discomfort of trade-offs. Please audit it: identify which pairs are genuinely in tension and which only appear to be, rank the genuine tensions by severity, say for each what would have to give and what the cheapest fix would be, and then tell me which single revision would remove the most tension at the lowest cost. End with a final line of the form \answer{...} naming that single revision and the direction of the change.
