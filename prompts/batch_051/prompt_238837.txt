I'm a PhD student in early modern, and I've been trying to make my chapter on Spinoza's moral psychology do something more than rehash the standard story. I'm pretty conviced by the necessitarian reading of the Ethics: everything follows from God/nature with the same necessity, so the ordinary experience of choosing otherwise is just the effect of ignorance of causes. And I also want to keep Part V's picture of the intellectual love of God as the highest possible state—understanding myself and my affects as modes of the one substance, and thereby passing from bondage to blessedness. But then I keep coming back to the opening of Part IV, where Spinoza says:

"Human infirmity in moderating and checking the emotions I name bondage: for, when a man is a prey to his emotions, he is not his own master, but lies at the mercy of fortune: so much so, that he is often compelled, while seeing that which is better for him, to follow that which is worse."

That doesn't sound like a datum to be explained away; it sounds like a firm description of something real. The person in that sentence sees the better, so in some sense he has an adequate idea, or at least something more than mere ignorance, and he is still dragged by fortune. My problem is that the more I take the opening seriously, the less I understand what the intellectual love of God is supposed to do. If everything is necessary and bondage is just a way of describing how affects follow from inadequate ideas, then the amor Dei intellectualis is itself a mode caused by prior modes; it's not an exit from bondage, just a different point inside the same causal order. But if I give up the intellectual love as the culmination, I lose what makes the system compelling to me. I need a negotiated answer: what should yield? Is the firm intuition that seeing the better and doing worse is real and ineliminable something the theory should accommodate, or should that intuition be debunked as an inadequate idea? Or is there a reading of "seeing" and "following" on which both the bondage and the intellectual love are compatible? Give me the strongest case for each move and tell me which one you would give up.
