I spent forty years in a physics department and now that I'm retired I've been reading philosophy of mind on my own for the first time. I keep getting stuck at the first step: the whole debate is about whether the mind—or everything, depending on the author—is 'physical,' but 'physical' is almost never pinned down. When it is, it collapses one of two ways. If it means 'what current physics describes,' then physicalism would have been false in 1800, since Newtonian physics had no electromagnetic fields, no spacetime curvature, no entanglement, and it'll presumably be false again after the next big theory. If it means 'whatever an ideal, completed physics would describe,' then the claim that everything is physical is empty, because nobody knows what completed physics will include and the term could quietly expand to cover whatever turns out to exist. I gather this is Hempel's dilemma.

Before I read any more of this literature, I want the desiderata. What should an adequate account of 'the physical' actually deliver if physicalism is going to be a claim with content? Spell out: the paradigm cases it must classify correctly (rocks, fields, processes; numbers, perhaps norms or consciousness, if those aren't physical); the distinctions it must respect or refuse (subject matter of physics vs. methods of physics, current vs. completed physics, the physical vs. the merely not-yet-explained); and the failure modes it has to avoid—being hostage to current physics, going vacuous via future physics, going circular via 'not mental,' or making 'physical' just mean 'whatever physicists end up studying.' For each desideratum give the case or reason behind it, say which ones are negotiable and which aren't, and tell me which single criterion is doing the most work to rule out the lazy answers. Then tell me which demands pull against each other. I suspect 'don't be hostage to current physics' and 'have enough content that physicalism could be false' can't both be fully met, and if Hempel's dilemma is really a no-win, I'd like it shown as a conflict among the desiderata rather than just asserted. Finish with a quick score of the usual moves—define the physical via ideal physics, via the negative, or via structure or method—against your list, and say where each one looses.
