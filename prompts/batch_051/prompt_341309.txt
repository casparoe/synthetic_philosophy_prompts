I am drafting a manuscript chapter on the units of biological classification, and I need a working definition of the term "species" that can be stated briefly, applied consistently, and then defended against the cases below. I am less interested in a survey of published species concepts than in a definition you are willing to commit to. Please propose a definition, run it through each case, and say where it gives the wrong answer or no answer; then either revise the definition or defend the surprising verdicts it produces.

The cases:

(a) humans and chimpanzees, which any adequate account should separate into different species;
(b) the domestic dog breeds, from Chihuahuas to Great Danes, which any adequate account should keep within a single species despite their conspicuous differences;
(c) a ring of populations around a mountain range, in which each adjacent pair interbreeds freely but the two end populations, where their ranges meet, do not;
(d) a lineage that has reproduced exclusively asexually for many generations, each individual descending from a single parent;
(e) two long-separated populations that are easy to tell apart and never meet in the wild, but that can still produce fertile offspring in the laboratory;
(f) a fossil sequence in one deposit recording gradual change, which paleontologists nonetheless split into named species at boundaries that a dissenting colleague calls arbitrary;
(g) two populations in the same forest that look nearly identical to a trained taxonomist, breed in different months, and never interbreed;
(h) bacterial strains that reproduce by fission but routinely take up and incorporate short stretches of DNA from only distantly related strains.

My suspicion is that the term is doing two jobs at once — one about what keeps present-day lineages reproductively or genetically separate, and one about how we divide the history of life into units — and that these jobs diverge on several of these cases. If you think that is right, give me the two definitions and say which cases move between them. If you think it is wrong, give me the best single definition and show why it need not fail on the hardest cases.
