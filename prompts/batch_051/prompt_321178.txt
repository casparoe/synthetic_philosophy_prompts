Been reading a bit about this concept called mutually beneficial exploitation — the idea that a deal can be voluntary and both sides gain from it, but its still exploitation because one side is taking unfair advantage of the other's desperation. I want to use that lens on the Rana Plaza collapse in Bangladesh in 2013. As I understand the facts: garment factories in the building made clothes for Western brands, workers kept showing up even after serious cracks appeared the day before because the alternative was losing their income, and over 1100 people died when the building collapsed. Walk me through the case using that framework: who is exploiting who, does the fact the job was genuinely better than the alternative (going back to a village with no income?) get the brands and factory owners off the hook, where does the theory explain the case well and where does it fall short, and what does Rana Plaza say back about the theory — does the case hold up under it, or does it break it?
