Proposal for the forum I help run: get rid of downvotes entirely. Upvotes are free and unlimited, but the weight of every upvote you've cast is 1/n, where n is the number of upvotes you've cast so far this week, recalculated continuously. So one upvote a week counts as 1, two count as 1/2 each, ten count as 1/10 each, and casting a new one shrinks all your earlier ones. Votes are public, karma is just the sum of the weighted upvotes your posts recieve, and karma decays 2% a day. Moderators only remove spam and abuse, never low-quality-but-earnest posts. Treat it as a mechanism, not a moral position: what does this do to who votes and how much, to what gets written, to who accumulates karma, and where does it get gamed first? Can the worst failure mode be fixed without gutting the scheme?
