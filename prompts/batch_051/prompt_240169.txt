I would like a considered probability from you rather than a survey of existing views. The claim I want you to assess is this:

H: There is a moral obligation to pursue, and as the means become safe and available, to carry out the biotechnological abolition of the capacity for phenomenal suffering — the felt badness of pain, fear, grief, despair, and malaise — in human beings and, where feasible, in other sentient beings under human influence, rather than merely treating suffering after it arises.

Please give me your credence in H as a single number, but treat that number mainly as a summary: the real work should be in the decomposition. I would like you to display the reasoning with the following components, assigning each a rough probability and a sentence of justification:

(a) Suffering has no intrinsic value, and nothing we care about — authenticity, motivation, moral understanding, emotional depth, whatever contrast is needed to appreciate goods — essentially depends on the capacity to suffer itself, as opposed to desires, preferences, or forms of dissatisfaction that can exist without felt agony.

(b) Conditional on (a), it is feasible, in principle or within a realistic timeframe, to eliminate the capacity for suffering without producing comparably bad side effects: apathy, blunting, loss of empathy, loss of identity, or a replacement harm that is just as bad.

(c) Conditional on (a) and (b), the lives of beings whose capacity for suffering is removed would, in expectation, go better than they otherwise would, and the intervention would wrong no one — including future beings whose consent cannot be obtained and whose affective constitution would be chosen for them.

(d) Conditional on (a) through (c), the obligation is not defeated by autonomy or pluralism (a world in which some choose to keep the capacity), by reasonable scepticism about our ability to foresee the effects, or by the view that a life without suffering cannot be fully meaningful or properly human.

Then show how you combine these — what conditional or multiplicative structure you are using — into your final credence. Tell me which single sub-question your final number is most sensitive to, and which estimate you are least confident in, and what evidence or argument would move that estimate most.
