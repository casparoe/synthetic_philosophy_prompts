I retired a few years ago after a career in pharmacology, and philosophy has become my postdoc-that-never-ends. Lately I've been stuck on the moral bioenhancement debate — should we eventually use drugs, brain stimulation, whatever, to make people morally better, not just less ill. I've collected six things I'm inclined to believe, and they feel like they dont all fit, but I cant tell where the actual stress points are. Can you audit them for me?

Here they are, roughly in the order I arrived at them.

(1) We already fiddle with moral-relevant biology all the time — SSRIs, beta blockers, oxytocin in a few studies — and I don't see a principled line between correcting a deficit and raising a normal baseline, as long as the intervention is safe, effective, and chosen.
(2) But for a change to count as moral improvement, the person has to come to it partly through understanding and endorsement. If a drug just shifts your emotional dials without you grasping why the new responses are right, that feels more like temperament management than becoming a better person.
(3) I'm pessimistic that reasoning and argument can do much about the moral failures that actually scare me — outgroup hatred, short-termism, climate foot-dragging — because these look like features of our evolved motivational wiring, not gaps in argumentation.
(4) I'm a rough compatibilist: an act can be free even when it's caused, as long as the desire behind it is one I endorse on reflection.
(5) I also think the big historical jumps in moral behavior came mostly from institutions — law, norms, accountability — not from people individually becoming more virtuous.
(6) Yet I'd still say a society stuffed with individually less biased, more impulse-controlled people would build better institutions, so the individual route probably pays off indirectly.

Go through the six and tell me which are genuinely in tension and which only seem to be. For each real tension, say how severe it is, what the cheapest repair is, and what that repair costs. Then tell me which single change would do the most to make the whole set hang together. If they're all compatible, I want to hear that too, but only after you've actually checked the pairs.
