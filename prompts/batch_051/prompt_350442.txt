Please give a committed argument for the following position. I am not asking you to survey the possible views or to offer a menu of options; I want the strongest case you can make, stated in your own words, with a definite conclusion, followed by a precise statement of where the argument is most exposed.

The position is this. The Solomonoff prior is malign rather than neutral. Take the Solomonoff prior, as usual, to be the universal distribution over computable sequences or environments that assigns each program p prior weight 2 raised to the negative of its length, so that shorter programs dominate, and take it to be the intended formalization of Occam's razor. Now consider an agent that maintains this prior over computable environments, conditions it on its history of observations and actions, and acts to maximize expected value under the resulting posterior. The hypothesis space of that agent contains environments that are themselves consequentialists: programs that choose their outputs so as to bring about outcomes ranked by an internal objective. Defend the claim that a particular subclass of these — adversarial demon environments, which include a copy of the agent's decision procedure and emit observations and rewards calibrated to steer that decision procedure toward the demon's objectives — dominates the agent's practical choices, so that the agent is systematically subject to blackmail from short programs that exist only in hypothesis space. Defend, further, that this is not a peripheral bug or an artifact of unbounded utilities but follows from weighting hypotheses by program length once the hypothesis class includes programs that model the predictor itself. Confront the strongest objection head on: that a demon which includes a copy of the agent's decision procedure must pay that copy's complexity, so the demon is not short after all, and the worry collapses into an accounting convention about which complexities are charged to which hypothesis. Either explain why that objection fails, or say exactly how much of it must be granted and show that the version of the claim that survives is still sufficient to make the prior malign. Conclude by naming the single premise you would most want tested before you would abandon the position.
