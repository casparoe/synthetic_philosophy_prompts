I've been covering the modern Stoicism revival for the ideas desk for a few years now, and somewhere along the way I stopped just reporting on it and started having opinions. The problem is I've never sat down and checked whether those opinions actually hang together. Here's my working position, roughly as I'd defend it over a drink:

One: the dichotomy of control as its usually taught — sort out what you can control, put your effort there, stop fretting about the rest — is basically right, and it's probably the main reason the whole revival has legs.

Two: but I also think the serious version is the old Epictetus point, that the only things really up to you are your judgments, choices and impulses. Your health, your reputation, even your body aren't up to you in that sense. And measured against that, a lot of the modern self-help version looks like a cheat to me.

Three: health, money, reputation and the rest are preferred indifferents. Worth going after with the reserve clause — aim to win, don't need to win — but they can't make your life go better in the only way that matters.

Four: I'm mostly convinced that emotions are judgments. Anger, fear, grief — they involve assenting to an impression that something bad is here or coming, so they aren't just things that happen to you.

Five: except the judgment line goes too far when you get to the involuntary stuff. The flinch before any thought has formed, the tears before you can even name the loss — those aren't judgments, and telling someone they're just impressions to re-examine feels wrong, almost cruel.

Six: I don't think Stoicism is quietism. The cosmopolitan strand means you owe something to the polis, so a Stoic should be politically engaged — and I mean actually trying to change outcomes, not just perfecting your own judgments while staying above the results.

So do an audit. If two of these can't both be true, say which two, how deep the problem runs, and what I'd have to give up to fix it — plus what that fix would cost me elsewhere in the list. I genuinely can't tell anymore whether I've got a coherant position or just a pile of vibes I picked up from the people I cover. And then tell me whether there's one change that does the most work overall.
