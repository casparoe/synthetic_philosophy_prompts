Does the story get something right that the philosophical debate tends to miss — maybe about what a whole city's complicity does to the arithmetic? Give me sections in this order: 'What the story is doing' (about 120 words), 'Where the lens fits' (about 250), 'Where it strains' (about 250), and 'What I'd tell the class' (about 150).
