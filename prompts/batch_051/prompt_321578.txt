I keep noticing this thing and I don't have a word for it. When I look at a red cup, I get the cup, not the looking. If I deliberately try to catch the process of seeing, the best I can do is produce another experience: the cup again, a thought about the cup, maybe the faint pressure of my eyes focusing. The part doing the presenting never shows up as part of what's presented. It's not just hard to see; it's as if the medium is invisible by design, and I end up taking the picture for the world. Same thing happens with the feeling of being me: I can't find the one haveing the experience; I just am the experience, usually located somewhere behind the eyes, and the machinery never shows up as machinery. Is there an established name for this invisibility of the process or medium, as opposed to the contents? And is it the same claim as the self being an illusion, or a different idea? If there isn't a name, what would you call it, and where would you draw the boundaries?
