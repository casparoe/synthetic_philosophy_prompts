Her track record so far:

- Odysseus having himself tied to the mast until the ship is safely past the Sirens: she approves.
- A problem gambler putting himself on a casino self-exclusion list for a year, where the ban can only be lifted after he asks and a 30-day cooling-off period has passed: she approves.
- A person with bipolar disorder signing an advance directive while well and with capacity, refusing a particular medication during future manic episodes, operative only while they lack capacity and revocable whenever they're well: she approves.

What should I predict Marta will say about the constitutional case? I'm less interested in the verdict itself than in the rule she seems to be running. What rule or rules fit the four personal cases, and which of them would travel across to the constitutional one? I can see at least two rules that both fit her personal record and seem to point in opposite directions here, and there's a live question about whether you can treat future governments, or future citizens, like your own future self. Give me your best guess at the rule she's actually running and why, and name the further case that would seperate the candidates cleanest if this one doesnt settle it.
