I've been going down a rabbit hole on the philosophy of consciousness lately, mostly because a friend keeps telling me the hard problem is overblown. I read a longform essay about illusionism — the view usually associated with Keith Frankish — and I've reread its summary paragraph enough times that I can nearly reproduce it. It runs something like this:

"Illusionism is the view that consciousness is an illusion. Philosophers like Keith Frankish argue that the subjective, felt side of experience — the 'what-it's-likeness' of seeing red or feeling pain — doesn't exist. You're not wrong that you see red; what you're wrong about is that there's a private, non-physical show playing in your head. Introspection misrepresents the brain's information processing as having special qualitative properties, the way a dashboard icon misrepresents what's happening in the engine. The hard problem of consciousness is therefore a kind of cognitive illusion too — we only think there's an explanatory gap because introspection keeps telling us there's something extra to explain. But here's the problem: an illusion is something that appears to someone, so if consciousness is an illusion, somebody has to be conscious in order to be taken in by it. Illusionists try to dodge this by saying it's the brain, not a self, that gets fooled, but that just relabels the mystery. And if there are no experiences, illusionists are stuck saying we're all zombies, which is exactly the absurd conclusion their critics accuse them of."

I keep feeling this summary is a little to neat, but I can't tell which parts a careful illusionist would actually sign off on and which are a hatchet job. Could you give me an accuracy audit? Specifically: is there a difference between denying consciousness and denying phenomenal consciousness, and does the article respect it? Do illusionists really think I'm wrong that I see red? What would they say to the "who is being fooled?" objection? And does the zombie conclusion follow from the accurate parts of the summary, or only from the distorted ones?
