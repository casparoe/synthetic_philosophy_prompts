I'm a novelist and I'm partway through a book whose central character, a retired physics teacher, keeps a private notebook in which he tries to work out whether he is the same object he was at twenty. He has been reading around the endurantism versus perdurantism debate, and I want his notebook to be more thoughtful than the usual fictional gesture at metaphysics. My own unreflective credence is somewhere around 0.7 in favour of perdurantism, mostly because four-dimensionalism gives me better prose: people as worms through time. But im aware that isn't an argument.

Here is the precise claim I want your credence on. For an ordinary material object, say a particular oak desk, persisting through time consists in its being a four-dimensional entity that has different temporal parts at different times, rather than a three-dimensional object that is wholly present at each moment of its existence. Give me a number between 0 and 1, but the number matters less than the decomposition. I want you to separate out the sub-questions your credence rests on, for instance: whether the endurantist/perdurantist dispute is substantive or merely verbal; whether temporal parts exist in anything like the way spatial parts do; whether endurantism can handle temporary intrinsics (a wire being bent, then straight) without gerrymandering; how much weight to give to special relativity and the idea that simultaneity is frame-relative; and whether our ordinary ways of talking and thinking about persistence should count heavily as evidence. For each sub-question, give a rough probability and a sentence or two of justification, then show how they combine into your final credence. Tell me which sub-question your number is most sensitive to, and what evidence or argument would move it the most.

Finally, since some of this will end up in my character's mouth, tell me which single consideration a retired physics teacher with no professional training in metaphysics would likely find the most decisive, and how he might phrase it in a notebook. Please write your answer in British English.
