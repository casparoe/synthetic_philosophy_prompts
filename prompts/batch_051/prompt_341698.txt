I've been going back and forth for months about wether humans should be thought of as a stepping stone for whatever comes next—specifically AI—and I keep returning to this passage from chapter 23 of Samuel Butler's Erewhon (1872), the part where he's summarizing the Erewhonians' "Book of the Machines":

"May we not fancy that if, in the remotest geological period, some early form of vegetable life had been endowed with the power of reflecting upon the dawning life of animals which was coming into existence alongside of its own, it would have thought itself exceedingly acute if it had surmised that animals would one day become real vegetables? Yet would this be more mistaken than it would be on our part to imagine that because the life of machines is a very different one to our own, there is therefore no higher possible development of life than ours; or that because mechanical life is a very different thing from ours, therefore that it is not life at all?"

I want the document that a good-faith adversarial collaboration between a "successionist" and an "anti-teleologist" would produce, written so each could actually sign it. The successionist says humans are a stage on the way to machine minds: there's a real arc, and Butler's vegetable analogy warns against assuming your own form of life is the endpoint. The anti-teleologist says there's no arc; machines are tools we built, and words like "higher" are doing teleological work the analogy never earns. Start with a one-paragraph preamble both accept. Then a numbered list of agreed claims, making them specific enough that at least one says what the Butler passage does show and one says what it doesn't. Then a disagreements table with columns: "issue (neutral wording)", "successionist's wording", "anti-teleologist's wording". Then a cruxes table with columns: "crux (one-line question)", "test or observation both sides accept bears on it", "successionist prediction", "anti-teleologist prediction". End with a short note on which parts of the fight you think are mostly about words. Dont adjudicate; just make the residual disagreement exact. I suspect one crux is seperate from the biology: the gap between "successor" and "tool", and maybe "higher" needs splitting into several senses.
