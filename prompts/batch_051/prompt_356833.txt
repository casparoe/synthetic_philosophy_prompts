could you write a villanelle about the paradox of blackmail? the two refrains should each be one of the individually-legal ingredients—telling something true about someone, and asking them for money—so that on their own they scan as completely harmless, but as the poem winds the refrains around each other the reader slowly feels the crime forming just out of the relation between them. dont name the paradox, and maybe never even use the word blackmail; let the villanelle's repetitions and final couplet do the explaining. someone who knows the blackmail literature should be able to point to the exact turn where a threat rises out of two things that were each fine alone.
