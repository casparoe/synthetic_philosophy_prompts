I have been trying to pin down the view of animals in Nozick's Anarchy, State, and Utopia well enough to predict how he would come out on a case the book never explicitly resolves. As I read him, his settled points are these. A person may not be used for the benefit of others without consent: he is clear that one may not cut up a healthy nonconsenting person to save five, no matter how much good would result, because persons are protected by side constraints that are not to be traded off against aggregate good. He connects this protection to the capacity to regard oneself as a continuing subject with a life to lead, and to guide one's conduct by an overall plan for that life; he treats the ordinary animals that appear in his examples as falling below this line, while allowing that some unusual animal might in principle come nearer to it. Below the line, however, animals are not morally nothing for him: causing an animal pain where no significant good is achieved is wrong, and when animal cost is to be imposed for a genuine benefit he treats the question as one of weighing and balancing the animal's interests against the end, in a way that he would refuse to do for persons.

Now consider a nonhuman species with intermediate capacities. Its members pass mirror self-recognition tests, show grief, make short-term plans, and use simple symbols to communicate, but they lack anything like a conception of themselves as continuing over a lifetime or the capacity to organize a whole life around an overall plan. Researchers propose to kill one healthy member painlessly and use the organs to save five other members of the same species. Would Nozick, on the reading above, permit this? In answering, set out the candidate rules that fit his earlier commitments—especially a sharp-threshold reading and a sliding-scale reading—say what each predicts for this new case, identify the closest analog among the earlier cases for whichever verdict you recommend, spell out the principle he would be adopting under each option, and name the further case that would most cleanly settle which rule he is actually using.
