Karl Marx, in the Preface to A Contribution to the Critique of Political Economy (1859), summarized his approach to history as follows:

"In the social production of their existence, men inevitably enter into definite relations, which are independent of their will, namely relations of production appropriate to a given stage in the development of their material forces of production. The totality of these relations of production constitutes the economic structure of society, the real foundation, on which arises a legal and political superstructure and to which correspond definite forms of social consciousness. The mode of production of material life conditions the general process of social, political and intellectual life."

Now consider a form of work that postdates him by well over a century: platform-mediated gig labor. A company owns a digital platform, sets prices and assigns tasks algorithmically, and coordinates a dispersed pool of workers who are classified as independent contractors. The workers supply their own tools — a car, a bicycle, a phone, a computer — and are paid by the completed task rather than by the hour. The platform records every interaction and uses the resulting data to refine its algorithms, which in turn dictate how much discretion any given worker retains.

Suppose Marx were alive and regarded this passage as an accurate statement of his own method. Reconstruct, as faithfully as you can, how he would analyze this kind of work. In particular: where in this arrangement would he locate the material productive forces, the relations of production, and the legal and political superstructure? Would he treat the platform worker as a new relation of production, or as an older one — wage labor, petty commodity production, piecework — wearing a new legal costume? What does the passage imply will happen when the legal classification "independent contractor" diverges from the underlying relations of production, and what would count as evidence for or against that prediction? Finally, identify the point where the passage sits least comfortably with the contemporary platform economy — where the base/superstructure vocabulary strains, or where the economic structure seems to explain relatively little — and say what that reveals about the limits of the thesis that the material base conditions everything else.
