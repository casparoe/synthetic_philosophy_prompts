(2) A factory produces cubes, and you know only that each side length lies somewhere between 0 and 1. Indifference over side length therefore assigns probability 1/2 that the side length is at most 0.5.

(3) That same physical event can be redescribed in terms of face area, which also lies between 0 and 1: the event of a side length at most 0.5 is identical to the event of a face area at most 0.25. (6) But rejecting it looks like it proves too much: if complete ignorance fixes no probabilities, then a unique prior is never rationally mandatory in the absence of evidence, and objective Bayesianism — and any formal method that claims to extract a unique prior from ignorance alone — loses its claim to a single rational starting point.
