I keep feeling like the vaccine picture is doing a lot of work there. Audit it for me: what plays the role of the pathogen, the weakened dose, the immune memory, the booster shots; where does the analogy break; and does the "it teaches healthy skepticism" conclusion actually survie the disanalogies, or was the analogy carrying the whole argument? If there's a better analogy for what the Santa lie actually does to a kid's trust, I'd want that too.
