I run a small remote team, and a while back we set up a points system so everyone could see how they're doing: each resolved support ticket earns points, and a couple modest monthly bonuses are tied to the totals. One of my guys noticed that lots of tiny, quick tickets count the same as the gnarly multi-day ones, so he quietly started scooping up the easy ones. The dashboard now has him way ahead of everyone else, and honestly he's started to believe his own number — he calls himself the top performer and seems genuinely happier and more confident. When I pointed out the score might not reflect the value of the work, he said the points are the system we all agreed to, and if I don't like what they show maybe I'm just uncomfortable with the truth. I can't quite articulate why that's wrong, and part of me thinks he has a point: hes following the rules and winning. How do I think straight about this, and should I change the system or just let him have his score?
