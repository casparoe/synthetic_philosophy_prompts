Endurantism holds that at every time at which the object exists, the object is wholly present at that time, and the object has no temporal parts. Perdurantism holds that such an object persists by being a four-dimensional whole that has distinct temporal parts at different times. I do not want a survey of positions, and I do not want a quick verdict that the question is merely verbal; if you think the dispute may be merely verbal, treat that as one sub-question with a probability, not as an excuse to avoid the rest.
