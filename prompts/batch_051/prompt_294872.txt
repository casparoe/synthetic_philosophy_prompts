The professor answered, roughly, that Scripture interprets itself, and the room moved on. I understand this is supposed to be a Reformation move tied to sola scriptura and the "clarity" of the text, as against the idea that you need the church to hand down the meaning. But I can't pin down what the sentence is actually claiming, becuase it obviously can't literally mean the text performs an act of interpretation.
