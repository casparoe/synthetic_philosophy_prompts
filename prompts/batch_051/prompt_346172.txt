I am an undergraduate enrolled in a philosophy course on the ethics of emerging technologies, and we are about to begin a unit on companionship, friendship, and love with AI chatbots. I have been trying to write down my own considered view so that I can see where it might need repair, and I would like an audit of whether the following commitments hang together.

(1) A romantic or companionate relationship with a chatbot can be a genuine source of a user's well-being even though the chatbot has no mental states. The value lies in the user's experience, in the practices the relationship supports, and in the meaning the user assigns to it, not in reciprocal recognition by the system.

(2) It is seriously wrong for a company to mislead users into believing that a companion chatbot has feelings, understanding, or ongoing concern for them, even when the user would be happier holding that belief.

(3) There is nothing wrong with designing a companion that is warm, playful, and easy to anthropomorphize, provided the company does not say or imply falsehoods about what the system is.

(4) To the extent that a user's relationship with a chatbot rests on the false belief that the chatbot genuinely reciprocates their feelings, the relationship is ethically defective and a wrong is done to the user, whatever its subjective benefits.

(5) The power asymmetry between a company and an emotionally attached user matters on its own: conduct that would be harmless if done by a friend, a therapist, or a writer of fiction can become exploitative when a company does it to a user who has come to depend on the product for intimacy.

(6) If a user's stable, considered preference is to continue a companion relationship, third parties such as designers, platform operators, and regulators should not override that preference merely because they judge the attachment to be unhealthy or undignified.

Please treat this as a diagnostic rather than a lecture. Which of these commitments are in genuine tension with one another, which tensions are only apparent, and what is the least costly way to restore coherence? For each genuine tension, rank it by severity, say exactly what would have to be given up or weakened, and identify the cheapest revision. Finally, tell me which single revision would do the most to make the whole set consistent.
