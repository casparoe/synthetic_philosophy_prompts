I am a high school student, and I have been asked to give the opening remarks at a student-run forum called 'Friends We Program: Chatbots and the Future of Friendship.' The audience will be split between students who regularly use AI companions and parents and teachers who find the practice alarming. Please write a five-minute speech that does not collapse into the easy conclusion that both sides have a point. Instead, build the speech around one distinction that has been on my mind: being made to feel understood is not the same as being known, and being soothed is not the same as being accompanied by someone who could have said no. The speech should use that distinction to explain both why an AI companion can feel genuinely close and why treating one as a friend may quietly teach us to prefer comfort over the risk of being known.
