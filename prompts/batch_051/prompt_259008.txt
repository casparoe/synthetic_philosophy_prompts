Consider the Judy Benjamin problem: an agent is dropped into a region partitioned by two binary features, Red/Blue and Second Company/Headquarters. In the familiar version of the puzzle, she begins with a uniform distribution over the four possible quadrant combinations. A radio message then gives her conditional information rather than an outright location: if she is in Red territory, the odds are three to one for Headquarters over Second Company. The puzzle is that this message is not an event in her original algebra; it is a constraint on a conditional probability. One widely discussed response is to choose, among all posterior distributions satisfying the conditional constraint, the one that minimizes relative entropy from the prior.

Now vary the prior. Suppose Judy's initial probabilities are not uniform but are as follows: P(Red ∧ Second) = 0.42, P(Blue ∧ Second) = 0.28, P(Red ∧ Headquarters) = 0.12, and P(Blue ∧ Headquarters) = 0.18. In this prior, P(Red) = 0.54 and P(Headquarters | Red) ≈ 0.222. The same message arrives: it constrains the posterior so that P(Headquarters | Red) = 0.75, with no direct information about Blue territory or about the marginal probability of Red. Work out the minimum relative entropy posterior from this nonuniform prior, giving the four quadrant probabilities exactly or to three decimal places. Then compare the posterior with the prior for each of P(Red), P(Second Company), P(Red | Headquarters), and P(Blue | Second Company), saying which are raised, lowered, or unchanged.

Finally, assess whether this variation changes the philosophical moral of the Judy Benjamin problem. Does the nonuniform prior reveal that the minimum relative entropy rule is doing more than updating on the message, and if so, what exactly is being imported from the prior? If one instead treated the message as a Jeffrey-style constraint on the conditional distribution over Headquarters given Red, would that rule give the same posterior here, and if not, what explains the divergence? Be explicit about the formal update rule you are applying and about which quantities, if any, the update preserves.
