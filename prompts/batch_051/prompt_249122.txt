I'm a philosophy PhD student working on meaning in life, and I've started keeping a list of my own case judgments because they don't obviously cohere. I want you to help me find the principle behind them, if there is one.

Here are the cases and my verdicts:

(1) A nurse in a war zone. She's exhausted, not especially happy, her personal relationships are a mess, but she's plainly doing something worth doing. I want to say her life is meaningful, full stop.

(2) A man spends forty years building an enormous, intricate model city in his garage. It absorbs him, gives him deep satisfaction and a strong sense of purpose, and he knows nobody else will ever value it. I can't bring myself to call his life meaningful — or at least nowhere near the nurse's.

(3) A scientist whose theory turns out to be false, though her work was careful and she trained students who went on to do good things. I want to say her life was meaningful despite the error.

(4) A person plugged into a machine that gives her convincing but wholly simulated experiences of achievement, love, and contribution; she'll never find out. I don't want to say her life is meaningless, but I do want to say it's significantly less meaningful than a matching real life. I flip-flop on this one.

(5) A grass-counter whose community has taught him that counting grass is cosmically important; he feels profound purpose and is genuinely fulfilled. I want to say the activity is worthless from the outside, but I also don't want to say his felt purpose counts for nothing. Stuck.

(6) A miserable artist who produces genuinely great work but is alienated from it and gets no satisfaction from it. I want to say the life is meaningful anyway — the meaning's in the work, not in his feelings about it. But then case (2) bothers me.

So: is there one account — subjective, objective, hybrid, or something weirder — that delivers all six? If not, which of my verdicts have to go? State the account you'd attribute to me precisely, say which clause each case is pulling on, run it on a case I didn't list, and be honest about where it won't accommodate me. I'm after the boundary lines, not a label. And please say what would change your mind about the account: which consideration would make you switch, and where your reconstruction of my pattern is most likely to fail.
