I help run a small composition prize, and for some time I have been turning over a private thought about what composition is: it looks to me like knob-twiddling. A composer takes an inherited template—a form, a tune, a progression—and turns a limited set of knobs (tempo, mode, rhythm, voicing, inversion) until something new-looking falls out. The thought has just become practical, because our rules contain a sentence we drafted with borrowed repertoire in mind and never expected to pinch:

'A work entered must be an original composition by the entrant; a work consisting wholly or chiefly of an arrangement, transcription, or variation on the work of another composer will not be considered.'

An entrant has submitted a twenty-minute piece built entirely as a set of variations on a deliberately plain eight-bar tune of his own. He states the tune and then takes it through a long chain of transformations—sped up, slowed down, set in the minor, inverted, retrograded, reharmonized, put in a different metre, reassigned from instrument to instrument. Each section changes essentially one parameter at a time. There is no second theme and no development beyond these twists, and the tune itself is, by his own admission, nearly a placeholder. In his letter he says the chain of twists is the composition, and that this is all creativity ever is: turning knobs on an inherited template and noticing which settings are interesting.

Does our sentence reach this piece? Work through the words of the rule, the purpose we likely had, what a reasonable entrant would have taken the sentence to permit, and what we as drafters would most plausibly have said had this case been put to us when we wrote it. Then say which of those considerations should control here and why. If you conclude the sentence does not reach the submission, sketch what a redrafted clause would have to say to catch it without also excluding ordinary sets of variations on a composer's own theme. Finally, say what the case shows about whether an originality clause like ours can track originality at all, rather than merely non-borrowing.
