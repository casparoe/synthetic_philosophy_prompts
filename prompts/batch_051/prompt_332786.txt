I am applying for a one-year dissertation fellowship in philosophy of language and need a two-page research proposal built around a half-formed idea. The idea is this: the question whether a language could lack pronouns or indexicals is usually discussed from inside languages that have both in abundance, and the two are often run together even though they may come apart—pronouns are a syntactic category, indexicals a semantic one, and a language might lose one while keeping the other. The topic stopped being abstract for me recently. After a stroke, my father now speaks almost entirely without pronouns or familiar indexical expressions; he combines proper names, definite descriptions, and pointing, and he communicates far more successfully than the literature had led me to expect. I cannot tell whether his case shows that indexicals are optional for language, or whether pointing and shared context are simply doing the indexical work under another name.

Please turn this into a proposal that includes:
(1) a precise statement of the central question, separating a possibility question (could a stable, learnable, naturally acquired language lack pronouns, lack indexicals, or lack both?) from a significance question (would such a language show that indexicality is not essential to language or thought, or only that actual languages grammaticalize it in one convenient form?);
(2) an account of why the familiar essential-indexical arguments do not by themselves settle the possibility question;
(3) a method that is philosophical rather than empirical: draw distinctions, construct cases, and ask what a speaker of such a language could and could not report or infer, and explain why this approach is better here than a typological survey of existing languages;
(4) a statement of what a successful result would look like and what would count as failure; and
(5) a realistic scope for a one-year project.
I will add citations later, so please do not include a bibliography or a literature review by citation; refer to positions and arguments by description. Please write the proposal in American English.
