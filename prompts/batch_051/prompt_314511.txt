Suppose you are advising a working group that has been invited to feed into negotiations over an international instrument on autonomous weapons. The instrument is expected to be organized around a requirement of meaningful human control, but the phrase currently functions more as a political placeholder than as an operational criterion. The working group has about six months and can commission three papers. Identify the open conceptual questions that most need to be resolved before meaningful human control can be written into workable treaty or procurement language; select the three you would prioritize; and defend the ordering. For each question, explain what would change in the drafting depending on how it is answered—for instance, whether it concerns the timing of the human judgment, the distribution of control across operators, commanders, and designers, the difference between control over an individual engagement and control over a campaign, or the sense in which control must be meaningful rather than merely nominal. Also indicate how tractable each question is for a short-term policy-facing working group rather than a longer academic research program, and what evidence or developments over the next year would most shift your ranking. I want the reasoning behind the prioritization more than the list itself.
