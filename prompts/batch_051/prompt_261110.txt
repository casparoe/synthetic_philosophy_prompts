I have been trying to turn a long-running dispute about metaphor into something that could be settled by observation, or at least into a precise statement of where observation gives out.

The first position, associated with Davidson, says that when a speaker tells me that my proposal collapsed under the weight of the objections, the sentence has only its literal meaning, on which it is roughly false, and the image of a structure giving way is not something the sentence says. The image is a routine the speaker has induced: a causal effect on the hearer, not an additional semantic content. The second position, the conceptual metaphor view of Lakoff and Johnson and much later work, says that this gets something important wrong. On this view the mapping between physical collapse and intellectual failure is a stable, structured correspondence that people systematically recruit for reasoning; the metaphor does not merely decorate an already literal thought but supplies a way of thinking about the target domain.

The obvious experiment is to expose people to one metaphorical framing rather than another and measure downstream reasoning — for example, giving one group a disease framing for a social problem and another group a different framing, then comparing their preferred interventions. But I cannot see why a Davidsonian should be moved by that result, because Davidson's claim was never that metaphors lack psychological effects, only that those effects are not part of meaning. If every behavioral difference of this kind can be absorbed as a causal effect, then the natural experiments do not distinguish the two views. What I want is the strongest version of a test that does not beg that question, or a demonstration that no such test exists and the dispute bottoms out in a verbal choice. Please give me (a) a concrete experimental design, (b) the result that would count in each direction, and (c) an explicit account of what even a clean positive result would leave unsettled — in particular, whether the residual disagreement is only about the word meaning or about anything deeper.
