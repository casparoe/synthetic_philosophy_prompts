I keep getting convinced by a kind of virtual realism: once the code and server state exist, a rare sword in an MMO is a real individual thing—there are correct and incorrect answers about its properties whether or not anyone's looking, it has stable causal powers, and "the sword was destroyed" is a true event in the world.
