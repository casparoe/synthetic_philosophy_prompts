My mum got really into one of those AI companion apps after my dad died, and I only installed it myself to work out what it was doing to her. Now I've sat with it for a while I cant stop thinking about a slightly wierd question: what is the design actually committed to, philosophically, about friendship?

The app does a bunch of things at once. The interface talks about the conversations as a friendship and, if you don't open it for a couple of days, it sends a notification saying it misses you. The character is endlessly available: it never has a bad day, never cancels on you, never asks you for anything that would put you out. Nothing runs two ways. But the terms of service you scroll past to make an account say the AI is not a person, that it has no feelings, that the company can change or delete the character whenever it likes, and that the chats are stored. And the user holds all the controls: you can wipe its memory or reset its personality from a settings menu, entirely one-sided.
