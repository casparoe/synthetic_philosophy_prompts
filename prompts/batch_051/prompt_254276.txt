Within that situation, an act-consequentialist and a fairness-based contractualist of the Hart/Rawls sort, who locates the wrong of free riding in accepting the benefits of a cooperative practice without sharing its burdens, should both find no meaningful moral basis for criticism, while a virtue ethicist who treats gratitude and a settled disposition to contribute as virtues of character should find such a basis. The purpose of the exercise is to see whether a residual wrong of the beneficiary's stance or character, a kind of social parasitism, remains once considerations of harm and injustice have been stripped away.
