What is violence? I want a working definition that counts a bar fight and a drone strike as violence, counts a surgeon amputating a limb and a parent forcibly restraining a child about to run into traffic as not violence in the relevant sense, and gives a definite answer either way for the following: a consented-to punch in a professional boxing match; a state's economic sanctions that foreseeably lead to widespread childhood malnutrition; a landlord evicting a tenant through legal channels; a coordinated social media harassment campaign with no physical contact; the deliberate destruction of a rainforest that displaces an indigenous community; a police officer using a Taser on an unresisting suspect; a person cutting their own arm; a corporation knowingly selling a product that causes long-term health damage; and a bull goring a matador during a bullfight. Then state which of your verdicts is least secure, and explain how definitional choices about these cases would affect the claim that the world is becoming less violent, especially if the composition of harms shifts among wars, interpersonal assaults, structural harms, and self-directed injury. Finally, say whether your definition settles the dispute between per capita and absolute measures of violence, or whether that dispute is independent of the definition.
