I run a little film club and we're doing Rashomon next month, the Kurosawa one. I got interested in a Jain idea called anekantavada, the thought that truth is many-sided and no single perspective captures the whole of a thing, like that story of the blind men feeling different parts of an elephant. I want to bring that lens to the film. But the more I think about it the less sure I am it fits: are the different versions in Rashomon really each getting at a partial truth, or are they just self-serving lies? Wich scenes would you point to, and where do you think the movie actually pushes back on the anekantavada reading instead of supporting it?
