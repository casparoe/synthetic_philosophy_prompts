Give me a menu of the precise claims that slogan could be making, from the ones everyone would accept to the ones that flatly conflict with the fossil record, and say for each whether it's plausible, trivial, or false—and which reading (if any) would actually get him his conclusion that animal suffering before the Fall is impossible.
