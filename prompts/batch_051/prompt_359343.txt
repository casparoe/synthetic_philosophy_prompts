I'm giving the toast at a dinner honoring my oldest friend, Mara. Two years ago she leaked internal emails showing her company had buried safety data about a drug. Some of us stayed close to her, but a lot of her old collegues think she's a traitor—and a few of those people will be in the room tonight, along with her parents, who are proud of her but still shaken by what it cost her. I'm a novelist, so I keep trying to write this as a tidy redemption story, but I don't think that's true. I think she really did betray people—her team, her boss, people who trusted her. And I think she had to, and that it was right. The toast has to say both at once and still land as a toast, not a closing argument. Can you write me a five-minute version? Plain language, no jargon, and it shouldn't try to convert anyone in the room.
