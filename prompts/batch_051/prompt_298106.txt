My older sister and I have been circling the same argument ever since: she talks as though the sacrifices our parents made when we were kids created a debt we're now on the hook for, and I keep insisting that what I do for mum should flow from the relationship we actually have now, not from some ledger of past benefits. We both make decent points, and we both end up attacking weaker versions of the other's view than the one they actually hold, so I'd like to see the strongest forms of each position set against each other.

Could you write a dialogue between two characters who each defend a sophisticated version of one of these two views about what adult children owe their parents? I want genuine back-and-forth where they respond to each other's actual points, not two adjacent speeches. Please don't let them reach a tidy compromise; I want to see where the disagreement survives contact with the other side.
