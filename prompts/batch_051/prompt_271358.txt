Here is where I have landed on the significance of cross-cultural variation in philosophical intuition, and I would like an audit of whether the package hangs together.

(1) I take the cross-cultural experimental literature to have established that many of the case verdicts on which philosophers rely — about semantic reference, knowledge ascription, moral responsibility, and adjacent notions — vary systematically across cultures and, in some cases, across socioeconomic groups within a culture.

(2) I regard this variation as methodologically serious. It shows that the spontaneous verdicts of a culturally situated agent are contaminated by local background, and that it is no longer acceptable to present one's own verdict about a contested case as if it were direct evidence about the target phenomenon.

(3) Nonetheless, I remain a realist about the underlying subject matters. I do not think the variation data establish relativism about reference, knowledge, or moral truth; they show only that considered judgment about cases tracks both the target and the judger's cultural training, and so must be used selectively rather than discarded wholesale.

(4) The selectivity I favor is this: trust a case verdict only if it is stable across the documented cross-cultural replications, or if it belongs to a domain sufficiently removed from plausible mechanisms of cultural shaping that variation is not to be expected.

(5) Consistently with (4), I hold a causal-externalist picture of reference, an invariantist account of 'knows', and a non-relativist cognitivism about moral claims, since I take the decisive arguments for these positions to rest on the kind of surviving verdicts that (4) licenses.

(6) I consider the methodological principle behind (4) — that the intuitive support for a philosophical claim should survive cross-cultural scrutiny — to be non-negotiable, and I do not think the principle itself must first pass the test it imposes, since avoiding parochialism is not one more parochial preference.

Is this a consistent set of commitments? Please audit it rather than restating the positions. I want the genuine tensions ordered by severity, an account of what would have to give in each case, and a note on which apparent tensions dissolve on inspection.
