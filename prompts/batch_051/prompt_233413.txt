Write a joke about the Kelly criterion in which the humor turns on the actual mathematics rather than on a generic gambling setup with "Kelly" pasted on. The ideal reader knows that Kelly maximizes expected logarithmic growth, not expected wealth; that the optimal stake is zero when the bet has no edge; and that underbetting merely sacrifices growth while overbetting far enough turns a favorable bet into almost-sure ruin. The punchline should hinge on at least one of these features and should not work as an expected-value or Martingale joke.
