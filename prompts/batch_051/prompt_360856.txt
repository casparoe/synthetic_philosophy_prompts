How much rests on the sincerity, how much on the deliberate omission, how much on the age and power gap, how much on whether the move would actually be good for him, plus whatever else you'd add.

Give it as a numbered list of exactly five items, a sentence or two each, no introduction or conclusion. Item one: your overall credence. Items two through four: the three sub-questions doing the most work, each with a rough probability and one line of justification. Item five: which of those you're least confident in, and what evidence would most shift your number.
