I am trying to work out what I actually believe about human well-being, and my verdicts on cases keep pulling in different directions. Here they are.

1. I would not plug into a machine that directly produces a lifetime of the experiences I most want, even though I would forget having chosen it once inside and could never detect the difference. And I think I would be worse off for plugging in, not merely making a mistake.

2. My aunt spends her last years happily convinced that her children adore her, when in fact they despise her and visit only from a sense of duty. I think her life is worse than the experientially identical life of a woman whose children really do adore her.

3. A novelist endures a miserable decade writing a book she believes matters. She finishes it, is told by someone she trusts that it is excellent, and dies a week later. I think the miserable decade was worth it, and that her life went better because of the book, even though the happiness never caught up.

4. A man wants nothing but to count blades of grass, and he spends a satisfied life doing exactly that. I do not think his life is going very well, even though he is getting and enjoying exactly what he wants.

5. Yet I think the absorption and emotion someone feels in reading a great novel really does make their life better, even though the characters are not real. So I apparently cannot say that what matters is simply being in touch with reality.

6. And if the grass-counter counted from compulsion, hating every hour of it, I would think his life was going worse than the satisfied grass-counter's. So the inner quality of the activity is not irrelevant either.

I would like to know whether any one principle of well-being captures all of this, or nearly all. State the principle precisely; run it through the six cases; say which of my verdicts it cannot accommodate; and apply it to a seventh case I have not settled: someone who spends a year in a machine that makes them believe they are in a happy, loving marriage, while in reality they have no spouse at all. Is that year good for them while they are in it?
