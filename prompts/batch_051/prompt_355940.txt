i've gotten a little obsessed with intuition pumps—those loaded thought experiments people use to drag a principle out of you—and i keep going back and forth on something i can't settle. on one side, i'm really drawn to a strict structural view: a thought experiment is only doing honest work when it's really a disguised argument. the concrete dressing—who the person is, how the room is described, the order you meet the cases in—shouldn't matter except for the bits that are load-bearing. if two cases have the same structure and i treat them differently, that's a flaw in me or in the setup, not a discovery. i like this because it feels like it gives me a clean decision procedure: strip the story down to its moving parts, and if my judgment wobbles when the coloring is removed, i should distrust the judgment, not the bare version. same structure, same verdict, no exceptions.

but my actual experience won't cooperate. when i take a case and make it concrete—an actual named person, a specific relationship, a detail about how it would feel to be the one doing the thing—my reaction often changes, and not just in an "eek, i'm being manipulated" way. sometimes i think the details are pointing at something the bare diagram just doesn't contain. the same structure can be lived out as something merciful versus something cowardly or callous, and i trust that reaction even though i can't state a clean principal for it. so maybe the strict view is wrong and the vividness is doing real epistemic work, not just selling me a conclusion.

but if i open that door, i don't know how to tell apart a thought experiment that's showing me something from one that's just bullying me with imagery. any bad argument can be made cinematic, and any good one can be made dry. so here's what i actually want to know: should i bite the bullet, debunk my feeling, and say the details are noise—full stop? or should i amend the structural view so that concrete details count as carrying genuine moral information, and if so, where's the line between information and manipulation? or are the two actually compatible on some reading of "same structure" that i'm missing? i'm not asking you to just pick a side; i want to know what would justify each move.
