What I'd like you to do is rank the following nine situations from most clearly harmful to others, in the sense Mill would need for his principle to justify interference if he'd even agree, to least, and give a sentence or two for each saying why you placed it there. If you think the harm principle kind of falls apart in some of these becuase the word harm is doing too many different jobs, say where you'd draw the line and what principle you'd use instead for the murky middle cases.
