I keep coming back to a parallel and I cant tell if it's deep or just the word "transitive."

Locke's memory criterion says a person extends as far back as their consciousness does. Reid's brave officer objection is that episodic memory isn't transitive: the old general remembers the young officer, and the officer remembers the boy who got flogged for stealing apples, but the general no longer remembers the flogging. If identity just is direct memory, then general = officer and officer = boy, but general ≠ boy, which is incoherent for a relation that is supposed to be identity. The standard Lockean fix is that identity runs along a chain of overlapping consciousnesses: general and boy are the same person because there is a path of memory-links between them.

Stripped down, that's the same move as closing a non-transitive relation under paths. In graph theory, adjacency is not transitive, but reachability — being joined by some finite path of edges — is an equivalence relation, and its classes are connected components. In topology the relation "joined by a continuous path" gives path-components. So is the Lockean repair just path-connectedness applied to persons?

I want to know whether this is a genuine identity of structure or me pattern-matching on "transitive closure." If it's real, then a lot of the old fuss about the memory criterion's formal defects was just a request to take a transitive closure, and the weirdness of chained identity is the same kind of weirdness as path-connectedness. But the analogy might break exactly where memory is unlike a graph edge: the graph relation is structural, cheap, and symmetric (in the undirected case), while memory-links are directed, fallible, contentful, and patchy. What would have to be true of memory for the graph repair to work, and what does the analogy predict about persons that we would actually reject — for instance, does it imply that a long gappy chain of quasi-memories glues two people into one the way a zigzag of edges connects two vertices? Give me a verdict on whether the parallel is doing real philosophical work or is just redescription in fancier notation.
