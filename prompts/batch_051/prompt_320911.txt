I am fluent in skopos theory, Gideon Toury's notion of translation norms, and the equivalence debates in descriptive translation studies. Take the strongest version of that theological claim you can reconstruct and restate it as precisely as possible in that vocabulary—source text, target text, function, norms, equivalence. Then give me the residue report: which parts translate cleanly, which translate only with distortion, which have no counterpart in the descriptive framework, and whether the untranslatable residue is a defect of the descriptive framework or a sign that the theological claim is less clear than its defenders take it to be. If the task rests on a confusion about what the doctrine says or about what translation studies can express, tell me that plainly instead of forcing the translation. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence, then give the reasoning; do not restate the question.
