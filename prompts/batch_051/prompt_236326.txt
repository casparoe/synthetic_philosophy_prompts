I'm a high school student, and together with two friends I run a small online forum for fans of a video game series. The site is hosted on a server in our own country, and most of our members live here as well, though we have a handful of active members from other places. A while ago, a member posted a long satirical essay making fun of a foreign government official. It was harsh, but clearly intended as parody. The post attracted little attention until a user from that official's country wrote to us saying the essay almost certainly violates his country's laws against insulting public figures, and that because our forum can be opened from his country, we are effectively publishing the material there. He asked us to take the post down or at least block visitors from his country.

My co-moderators are now split. One says we should only concern ourselves with the laws of the place where we and the server are located, since nobody can reasonably be expected to follow every country's rules at once. The other says that if we knowingly keep content available in a place where it is illegal, we cannot simply pretend we are not reaching that place. I am not really asking for legal advice; I want to understand the underlying question. Whose rules should a small internet community answer to, and on what principle should we decide what to leave up?
