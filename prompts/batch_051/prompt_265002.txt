I find the pragmatic encroachment line genuinely attractive as a theory: it explains the whole pattern of cases with one move, and it ties knowledge to action in a principled way — knowing p is the state you're entitled to rely on in practical reasoning, and if relying on p could cost you everything, maybe your basis just isn't good enough to count as knowledge. But I also have a stubborn intuition that knowledge is a relation between a person and the world — evidence, truth, reliability, anti-luck conditions, and that's it.
