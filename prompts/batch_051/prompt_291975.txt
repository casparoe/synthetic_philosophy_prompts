None of us have real training in this, we're just students, so we need something we can use at a meeting, not a lecture.

Can you write us a decision procedure or checklist for deciding when an op-ed crosses from legitimate persuasion into manipulation? Also we ran a satire piece once and some readers said it was manipulating them, so I'd want to know if the procedure handles that or not.

Make at least one of them come out as reject even though we'd probably like the piece, and say clearly where the procedure stops being mechanical and a person has to decide.
