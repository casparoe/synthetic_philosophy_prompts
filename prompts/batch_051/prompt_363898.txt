Treat that doctrine, which he developed within the Islamic tradition, as a general model of the relation between a revealed tradition and demonstrative philosophy. On this view, demonstrated conclusions and revealed truth cannot genuinely conflict; where the apparent sense of a revealed text conflicts with a demonstration, the text must be read allegorically, and only the demonstratively trained are competent to do that.
