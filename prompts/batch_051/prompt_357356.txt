But she sometimes overrides the suggestions because they read as not-her, she doesn't know how the app decides what to surface, and the company could tweak or terminate the service without her input. Walk through each criterion and say where it's genuinely ambiguous, then take a real side: is the app part of Anika's mind in the way the standard extended-memory cases are, or is it just a very good assistant?
