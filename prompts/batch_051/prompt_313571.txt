I have been trying to make the concept of democratic backsliding do real analytical work, and I want my working criteria tested against a case that sits at the edge of the concept. My criteria are these: a regime is undergoing democratic backsliding when (1) competitive elections continue, but the rules of contestation are changed to favor the incumbent; (2) horizontal accountability weakens as courts, election administration, and oversight bodies are captured or sidelined; (3) the media and civil liberties degrade in a lopsided way that mainly narrows the space available to the opposition; and (4) the rule of law becomes selective, constraining opponents far more than the governing camp. Now consider a country I will call N. N has held regular multiparty elections for decades. In the past six years the governing party has redrawn districts to its advantage, replaced the election commission leadership with loyalists, and packed the constitutional court; it has also bought or pressured most broadcasters into loyalist coverage. On election day, however, the vote itself is administered cleanly: no ballot stuffing, no credible fraud, reliable counts, and opposition monitors are present. The opposition survives, wins some municipalities and one regional governorship, and polls near 45 percent. No opposition leaders are imprisoned, though several face tax audits and strategic defamation suits. International observers call the elections free but not fair. The incumbent keeps winning narrowly. Is N a case of democratic backsliding on my criteria, or has it become something else, such as an illiberal but competitive democracy or a one-party-dominant system? Work criterion by criterion and say where the criteria underdetermine the verdict. Then tell me whether the case reveals that one of my criteria is not doing the work I thought it was, or that the concept itself is smuggling in a separate judgment about liberal values. If you think the exercise is misframed, for instance because democratic backsliding is too partisan or too fuzzy a label to be used this way, or because my criteria already decide the answer, say so plainly instead of complying with the checklist. Please write in continuous prose only: no headings, bullet points, bold, or tables.
