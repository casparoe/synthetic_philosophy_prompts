In an ordinary negotiated transaction, by contrast, they would ask what was actually said and understood. Reconstruct the implicit theory, distinguishing what the practice strictly presupposes from what it merely fits well with; say where it is underdetermined between competing theories; and explain what would have to change if the law instead held that a person is bound only by terms to which they gave term-specific, subjective assent.
