My friend Maya has been a vegan for years, but her case-by-case verdicts do not fit the familiar labels. I want to know what her track record commits her to on a new case.

Here are the earlier verdicts I have recorded:

- She will eat packaged deli meat salvaged from a supermarket dumpster. Her stated reason is that it is waste, that taking it creates no demand, and that the animal's death is independent of anything she did.
- She will eat fresh roadkill; she once took home a deer that had been struck by a car on a country road, pointing out that it would otherwise rot or be hauled away.
- She eats the unfertilized eggs from the three rescue hens she keeps in her yard. The hens never brood and nobody else wants the eggs, so they would simply be composted.
- She will wear a secondhand leather jacket from a thrift store. In her view the harm is already done, and declining the jacket changes nothing.
- She will not buy a carton of eggs from a neighbor's well-cared-for backyard hens, even though the neighbor says the eggs would otherwise go to waste. Maya says that paying for them is different from taking eggs that nobody wants.

The new case is this. Maya now volunteers at a sanctuary for cows rescued from dairies. One recently rescued cow is still lactating but has no calf with her. If she is not milked she becomes engorged and risks mastitis, so the volunteers milk her for her comfort. Her lactation is expected to taper off within a few months, and the sanctuary will milk her regardless of whether anyone uses the milk. For now the milk is poured down the drain, and Maya is offered a gallon to take home.

What would Maya say, and what should she say if she wants to remain faithful to the principle behind her earlier verdicts? I want more than a bare answer: state the rule she seems to be running, articulate at least one other rule that fits her record just as well, say what each candidate rule predicts about the offered milk, and give your best guess with the reasons for preferring it. Then propose a further case, not already on the list, that would most cleanly separate the leading candidate rules.
