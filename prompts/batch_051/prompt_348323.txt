The setup as I understand it: suppose every emerald observed so far has been green, so normal induction says the next emerald will be green. Now define a color predicate "grue": a thing is grue iff it's green when first observed before next Tuesday and blue when first observed after that. Then every emerald seen so far is grue as well, and inducing on grue predicts the next emerald will be blue. Same evidence, two generalizations, opposite predictions. And the claim is that this matters for induction itself, not just for people being sloppy about predicates.

Restate what's going on here in my vocabulary.
