so i'm a backend engineer and I've been going down a decision theory rabbit hole mostly through the AI alignment / agent design side of things, not through any formal philosophy. i keep running into the same cluster of questions about whether an agent can be rational *over time* while also being rational *at each moment*, and i've ended up with a bunch of commitments that I *think* hang together but I honestly can't tell where the load-bearing crack is. here's roughly were i've landed:

(1) if your preferences cycle — you'd trade A for B, B for C, and C for A — that's irrational, and the cleanest story about why is that someone can money-pump you by charging a small fee on each swap until you're back where you started but poorer.
(2) at the same time, i think a competent agent who sees the whole sequence coming can just refuse the last trade (or refuse to play at all), so actually getting pumped proves you weren't paying attention, not necessarily that the cycle itself is the problem.
(3) dynamic consistency feels non-negotiable to me: if you make a plan and nothing relevant changes, you should carry it out, and abandoning it for no new reason is a failure of rationality.
(4) but i also think that at any given moment you should just do whatever your current preferences rank highest; the fact that an earlier version of you made a plan shouldn't be an independent reason to follow it.
(5) preferences obviously can change when you learn something new — like if i learn a food gives me migraines, i stop wanting it, and that's not a violation of anything, that's just updating.
(6) and i lean toward thinking hyperbolic discounting — caring wildly more about the next hour than about next month — is a bias that makes your near-term preferences less trustworthy, not just a taste.

i'd like an audit rather than a lecture: which of these are actually in tension, which pairs just look like they conflict but don't, how severe each real tension is, and what would have to give for each. if there are multiple fixes, tell me which single revision to the package would buy the most coherence.
