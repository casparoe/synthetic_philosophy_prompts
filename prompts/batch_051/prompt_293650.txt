(4) Lexical priority of the awful: there's a class of extreme suffering such that no amount of mild positive well-being spread across other lives can justify inflicting one instance of it. I don't take this as a nice-sounding slogan; I really do mean no finite amount.

(5) The repugnant conclusion is false: a vast population of lives barely worth living is not better than a smaller population of genuinely flourishing lives, however vast we make the first population.
