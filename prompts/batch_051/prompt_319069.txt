In Jowett’s translation of Plato’s *Euthyphro*, there’s a moment where Euthyphro accepts a tightened-up definition: "what all the gods love is pious, and what they all hate is impious." Then Socrates starts in on whether the pious is loved by the gods because it’s pious, or pious because it’s loved.

I want you to write an essay treating that exchange as a challenge to what people today sometimes call divine command theory. But I also want you to push on whether the dilemma is really exhaustive—maybe there’s a third option that avoids both arbitrariness and making God subordinate to an outside standard.
