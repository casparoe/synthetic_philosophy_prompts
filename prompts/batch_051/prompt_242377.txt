Some biologists and philosophers have suggested that an ant colony is best understood as a single cognitive system, with individual ants playing roles closer to neurons in a brain than to citizens in a society. Others regard this as an evocative but misleading way of speaking: on their view, everything the colony does is fully explained by individual ants following local reaction rules, so the colony has no mind, no memory, and no point of view of its own. Your task is to say how one could test the first claim, and not merely by producing simulations that already assume it.

Start by choosing one specific mental property that might be at issue, such as integrated perception, contentful memory, decision-making under uncertainty, or another core component of having a mind, and state exactly what a colony would have to do to count as having that property. Then take at least one concrete colony-level behavior as your target. A useful example, though you may substitute another, is nest choice in Temnothorax rock ants: when the nest is destroyed, individual scouts inspect candidate sites and recruit peers at rates and durations that reflect the quality of the site each scout alone has visited, and the colony eventually moves, usually to the better site, even though no ant has compared both sites and no central controller has aggregated the information. Describe a manipulation or measurement that could distinguish the hypothesis that the colony as a whole computes the comparison, retains the alternatives, and decides, from the deflationary hypothesis that colony choice is entirely the product of local ant rules, response thresholds, and quorum dynamics. Say what outcome would favor each hypothesis, and what outcome would show that the two descriptions are only verbally different.

Finally, assume the outcome favors the colony-level reading. What would remain unsettled about whether the colony has a mind? Distinguish what a successful experiment would license us to say about the colony, for example that it has a decision procedure or an integrated representation, from what it would not license, such as claims about phenomenal experience, unified selfhood, or a single point of view. Say whether you take this residue to be a permanent limit on empirical methods or a sign that those further aspects of mind are not well enough defined to be testable in any system, not just colonies.
