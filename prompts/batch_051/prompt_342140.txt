I've been thinking about a particular version of mind uploading: a person's neurons are replaced one at a time by artificial units, each doing exactly what the replaced neuron did, while the person stays awake and responsive throughout; at the end no biological brain remains. I cannot settle whether the person at the end is the same person or a copy that has taken over the original's life, and it matters to me because I would consider undergoing the procedure. The difficulty is that every observation I can think of — memory reports, personality tests, friends' judgments, the person's own insistence — looks the same whether the original survived or was quietly replaced by a copy that sincerely believes it is the original. Is there any concrete test or battery of tests that could tell these apart? Give the strongest design you can, say exactly which result would count for 'same person' and which for 'copy,' and be explicit about what the best possible result would still fail to settle.
