Write a sestina about the stochastic-parrots debate. The six end-words are predict, token, meaning, trained, understand, and parrot. I want the form to do the arguing. A sestina just cycles the same six words through new contexts, and the worry behind the stochastic-parrots charge is exactly that fluent text can be produced by reusing well-practiced tokens without anything standing behind them. So let the parrot in the poem keep insisting it understands, and let the poem's own recurrence of understand keep undercutting the insistence: each new stanza it shows up grammatically well-placed but maybe never accumulating the thing it names. By the envoi, leave it genuinely undecided whether the poem has managed to mean something or has just been a very good stochastic parrot. Someone who knows the debate should recognize both the deflationary move (its all just context-sensitive prediction) and the countermove (then a human poet is also just cycling overused words) in how the end-words do or don't accumulate sense across the returns. Someone who doesnt know the debate should still get a real sestina, not an essay with line breaks.
