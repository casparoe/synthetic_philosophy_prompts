The core mechanic should force each small group to pick one claim to tear up or rephrase, then defend the replacement to the room. Give the exact wording of the cards, the room setup, a minute-by-minute timeline, and a debrief that maps each main choice onto a rough family of views (young-earth creationism, a two-books view on which animal death is just nature's economy, a view on which animal pain isn't really suffering in the morally serious sense, an angelic/prehuman fall view, a nonhistorical/figural reading, and an evolutionary theodicy on which a self-making creation costs God something). Don't show them that map until after they've commited. Before writing the rules, brainstorm which pairs of commitments can be held together comfortably and which pairs need clever footwork. Then tell me what will predictably go wrong the first time I run it and which view the game still can't represent.
