The room will be sympathetic to expected value but allergic to hand-wavy patches. Give me a prep sheet for the Q&A, not a full defense: the objections in roughly the order they'll come, phrased the way this specific crowd would phrase them, which ones actually have bite, what I should concede up front and where I should hold my ground, and the one objection I'm hoping nobody asks, together with my least-bad reply and where that reply is weakest.
