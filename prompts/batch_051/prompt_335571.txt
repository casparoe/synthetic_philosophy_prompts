Write me a parody of a founder's letter (or the FAQ of a wellness startup) for a product called something like Effortless: The Wu-Wei System, which promises spontaneity through a rigorously scheduled eight-week protocol. 650-ish words, deadpan startup-speak the whole way, in the voice of a venture-backed founder who has clearly never had an unforced moment in their life. The humour should come from the actual paradox of trying not to try: every selling point (daily drills, habit tracking, progress metrics, accountability coaches, a completion certificate) is another layer of effort bolted onto effortlessness, and the copy never once notices. By the end, a reader who knows the Daoist sources should feel you actually understand wu-wei, and a reader who doesnt should still get the joke. British English, please. As a novelist I'd trade meanness for tightness of voice.
