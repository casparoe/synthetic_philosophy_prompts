Consider the claim that a turtle's shell is for protection, read not as a report of any designer's intentions and not as a colorful metaphor, but as a literal explanatory claim about why turtles have shells. The view I want your credence about holds that such claims can be true even though no turtle, no predator, and no designer ever represented that fact: the reason is real, but it is not anyone's reason. Call a rationale of this kind free-floating: a consideration that genuinely counts in favor of, or genuinely explains by appeal to what something is for, without any actual or idealized reasoner who entertains it.

Please give me your credence that at least some free-floating rationales of this sort are true of the actual world, as a single number between 0 and 1. Decompose that credence into at least the following terms, giving a number and one or two sentences of justification for each:

1. The probability that teleological or functional statements about undesigned systems (for example, that a heart is for pumping blood, or that a polar bear's white coat is for camouflage) are capable of being literally true or false, rather than being merely metaphorical or false-but-useful.
2. The probability, conditional on such statements being truth-apt, that their truth does not require any mind — divine, human, evolutionary, or an ideal observer — to constitute or endorse the teleology.
3. The probability, conditional on the first two terms, that at least some such reasons are not reducible to ordinary efficient-cause or selection-history explanations without explanatory loss.
4. Any residual term you think the overall credence meaningfully depends on, such as whether it is coherent to say a consideration counts in favor of something when no one values it.

Then show how these terms combine into your overall credence, and flag any dependencies or double-counting you would adjust for. Finally, state which term your final number is most sensitive to, and say what concrete evidence or argument would most shift that term for you.
