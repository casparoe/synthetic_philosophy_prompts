My idea, in a nutshell, is this. The natural kinds debate is usually framed as whether scientific classification discovers real divisions in nature or invents them. So the essay would argue that the “joints” metaphor is doing less metaphysical work than people think. The demand for a natural classification is better understood as a demand for a stable, transitive, projectible grouping under constraints that are partly theoretical and partly practical.
