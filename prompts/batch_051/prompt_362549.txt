What should actually move me off this? Give me the strongest counterarguments and evidence, sorted by how decisive they are rather than just a list of criticisms. I'm most likely to be moved by concrete facts about actual animal lives (survivorship curves, what kills them, whether adults have decent lives) than by abstract population-ethics moves.
