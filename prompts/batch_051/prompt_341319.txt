I've noticed a pattern in words that I can't name. Some changes to a word just feel like grammar bolted onto the same word: walk, walked, walking; cat, cats. Other changes feel like minting a new word: walk becomes walker, kind becomes kindness. But the line blurs on me. Broken in 'a broken window' — is that still a form of break, or has it become its own adjective? And singing in 'the singing went on for hours' — is that still the verb doing a noun's job, or is it a noun now? What makes it trickier is that go and went share no parts at all, yet I'd still want to say went is a form of go. So whatever makes something the 'same word' cant just be about how it looks or sounds. Is there a name for the boundary I'm circling? And for the in-between cases, how do people who think about this decide which side they're on — or is the boundary just a habit in the end?
