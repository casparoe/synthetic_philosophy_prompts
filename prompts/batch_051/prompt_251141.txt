I'm auditing a seminar on collective wrongdoing, and the instructor has spent the term issuing verdicts on complicity cases without ever stating her principle. I've been keeping a list, and it refuses to reduce to any single rule. (1) A city councillor votes for an eviction policy she knows is unjust; the motion passes 7–2, so her vote changed nothing: complicit. (2) A getaway driver waits in the car as agreed for a robbery, but the robbers leave on foot and the car is never used: complicit. (3) A landlord rents ground-floor retail space at ordinary market terms to a payday lender he knows will strip poor tenants of their assets; the lender could have rented the identical shop next door: not complicit. (4) An engineer falsifies safety-test data, and her report is the sole thing that gets a dangerous vehicle past the regulator: complicit. (5) A bystander shouts encouragement from behind the closed window of a parked car while a group beats someone; no one hears him and the beating proceeds exactly as it would have: not complicit. (6) A famous scientist signs an open letter praising a government known to run torture prisons, knowing her name will be used by state media; the letter changes nothing, and she has no contact with the government beyond signing: complicit.

Next week's case is this. A well-known artist sits silently on a stage beside a warlord during a televised press conference, as a personal favor to a mutual friend. She says nothing, applauds nothing, privately disapproves, and was invited solely because her silent presence will be reported as implicit support for the warlord. She knows this and sits there anyway. Would the instructor treat her as complicit in the warlord's ongoing atrocities against civilians?

Lead with your predicted verdict, then give the reasoning. Identify the rule—or the competing rules—that best fit her six prior rulings, say what each predicts about the artist, explain why the record underdetermines the answer between them, and name one further case that would cleanly settle which rule she is actually running. Keep the whole answer under 700 words.
