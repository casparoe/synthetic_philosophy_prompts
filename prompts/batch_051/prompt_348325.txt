My colleague in the philosophy department works on shared agency and keeps a file of vignettes on which she records a bare judgement: whether the people are acting together. Over the term she has shown me the following cases and verdicts:

- Two pedestrians walking abreast towards the same bus stop, each unaware of the other and each intending only to catch the bus: not acting together.
- Two workers on a production line, each wanting only to finish her own quota, with no intention referring to the pair, though each knows the other has a quota as well: not acting together.
- Two friends carrying a sofa up a stairwell, each intending that the two of them get it into the flat, each adjusting grip and pace to the other, and each confident the other is doing likewise; each would be put out if the other walked off without warning: acting together.
- Two strangers in a boat whose engine has died, who exchange a nod, take the oars, each intending that the two of them reach shore, matching strokes and adjusting when one tires; each would take the other to be in the wrong if she stopped rowing mid-crossing without a word: acting together.
- Two housemates who agree on Sunday to clean the kitchen together at six; at six they work in the same kitchen on different jobs, asking each other what remains when one finishes early; each would regard the other as having let her down by not turning up: acting together.
- Two improvisers on stage who perform a duet, each choosing phrases that answer the other's, each intending that they perform together rather than merely giving two solos; each would think the other owed her a signal before quitting mid-piece: acting together.

Next week's case is this. Ana and Bo meet at a trailhead and discover that each had already planned an afternoon walk to the same hut. Bo says, 'Might as well set off at the same time.' Ana agrees. After a few minutes each privately thinks, 'We are walking to the hut together,' and each would later say that they walked together. But they stay silent, never adjust pace or route to the other, stop for rests independently, and each regards the arrangement as entirely cancellable; if the other dropped away at a bend, neither would feel let down.

What should I predict she will record: are Ana and Bo acting together?
