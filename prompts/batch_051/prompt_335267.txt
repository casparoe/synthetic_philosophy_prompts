Lets do a counterfactual about Glaucon's ring of Gyges from the Republic. The idea there, roughly, is that people are just mostly because theyre afraid of being seen and punished, and that a just person with a ring of invisibility would end up acting like an unjust one. Now universalize it. Suppose that starting at midnight tonight, every living human can turn invisible at will, up to one hour total per 24 hours, free, harmless, and spendable in one stretch or in short bursts; once youve used the hour youre visible again until the next day. The invisibility covers your body and whatever you are wearing or carrying at the moment you turn invisible (stuff you pick up afterward stays visible unless it's fully hidden by something invisible), and it fools ordinary eyes and ordinary cameras equally well. It is only visual though: you still have weight and warmth, you still make noise and leave smells, dogs and thermal cameras and microphones and pressure plates still register you. Nothing else about the world changes.
