"Strawson argues that moral responsibility is really about the emotions we can't help but feel toward each other. If someone wrongs you, you feel resentment; if they help you, gratitude; those feelings are what holding someone responsible consists in. Strawson's bottom line is that determinism would only threaten responsibility if we could actually give up resentment and gratitude, and he concedes that if science ever taught us to reprogram those emotions, we would then have to abandon blame altogether."
