I've been selling plasma twice a week for grocery money since my hours got cut, and it turned into a whole fight with my roommate about what should and shouldn't be for sale. My friend, who actually thinks about this stuff, is pretty firm that selling plasma is fine and paid surrogacy is fine, but paying someone for a kidney should stay illegal, buying votes should be illegal, and a store charging forty dollars for water after a hurricane should be prosecuted. She was also sort of dismissive when I asked whether her reasons for the kidney ban and the vote ban where even the same.

Before I bring anything up I'm trying to work out what she'd say about three newer cases: paying a living donor for a piece of their liver, since it grows back; selling eggs the way clinics already pay donors; and paying someone at the passport office to hold a place in line and sell you their spot. Honestly I don't just want a yes or no on each; I want to know what rule she seems to be running, what other rules would fit her calls just as well, and which of those three cases would best force the rules apart — and if they don't fully settle it, what extra case you'd put to her next.
