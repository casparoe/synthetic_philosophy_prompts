In the Discourse on the Method (the Veitch translation on Project Gutenberg), he writes: "for as to the reason or sense, inasmuch as it is that alone which constitutes us men, and distinguishes us from the brutes, I am disposed to believe that it is to be found complete in each individual." Now that LLMs do use language pretty flexibly, does that specific argument survive? I'm not asking whether ChatGPT has a mind. I'm asking whether the marker Descartes relied on (reason/language) has become a much worse discriminator than he assumed.
