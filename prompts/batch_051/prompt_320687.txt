I am a student in a philosophy of emotion course, and our unit on valence is currently considering arguments that a life without painful emotions might be worse, not better. The course uses the phrase "negative emotion" constantly, but I have come to suspect that the term runs together at least two questions: how an emotion feels, and whether having it is on the whole good or bad for the person. I would like a working definition of "negative emotion" that keeps those questions distinct rather than assuming they have the same answer.

Please define "negative emotion" so that it gives a defensible classification of each of the following:

- Acute grief in the weeks after a death.
- The faintly pleasurable sadness of listening to a song in a minor key that one finds beautiful.
- Anger at a clear and serious injustice.
- A diagnosed episode of clinical depression.
- Physical pain from a burn.
- Envy of a friend's success that the envious person would never act on.
- Fear while watching a horror film.
- Boredom during a long lecture.
- Schadenfreude at the downfall of a despised public figure.
- Nostalgia for a childhood home that no longer exists.
- Mild anxiety before a performance that noticeably improves one's focus.

I am fairly confident that acute grief is a negative emotion, and fairly confident that physical pain is not, not because it is pleasant but because it is not an emotion. I also suspect, though less strongly, that schadenfreude should not count as a negative emotion even though its object is bad. The cases I genuinely cannot settle under one principle are the sad-song case and the performance-anxiety case, since each seems to involve a feeling that is unpleasant in one respect yet partly pleasant or plainly beneficial in another.

Propose the definition, apply it to each case, and say where it commits you to a surprising verdict. If you think "negative emotion" needs two definitions, one for felt quality and one for overall value, give both and indicate which cases change their classification when you switch between them.
