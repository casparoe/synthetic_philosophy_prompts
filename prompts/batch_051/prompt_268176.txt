People I know who keep rescued hens eat their eggs, and some also use wool from rescued sheep or the remains of animals who died of natural causes while in their care. They defend these practices by analogy with freegan dumpster-diving: just as a freegan consumes food that would otherwise be thrown away, they consume animal products that would otherwise be discarded, and in neither case, they argue, is any additional demand created or any additional harm done. The analogy is made to do real argumentative work — it is deployed against vegans who object that consuming any animal product treats an animal as a resource and helps keep that way of seeing animals alive. How far does this analogy actually hold?
