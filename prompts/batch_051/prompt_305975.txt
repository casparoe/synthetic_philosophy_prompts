Give me the tests in order, a worked example for each kind (bosons, fermions, and the classical gas where the particles aren't 'really' indistinguishable but the counting still matters), mark the steps where I'm making a physical judgement rather than following the rule, say which cases the procedure can't settle, and tell me where the rule will make my existing code look wrong even though the rule is right.
