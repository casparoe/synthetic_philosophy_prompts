Write me a satirical welcome-to-your-term FAQ page, around 600 words, from the fictional Civic Rotation Authority. Setup: the country has abolished elections, seats its national legislature by random lottery for a single non-renewable 18-month term, and imposes a lifetime ban on anyone who has ever held office. The page is for a citizen whose number just came up, and it should answer the questions such a person would actually google: what the job is, whether they need to know anything, who really does the work, why wanting the job is itself disqualifying, and what happens when the term ends. Deadpan officialese the whole way through, no winking, but a reader should still be able to tell it's satire.

The jokes have to come from getting the actual proposal right, not from inventing silly details. A lottery is a random sample only if everyone serves and nobody wriggles out; term limits guarantee an assembley of novices and whatever staff and lobbyists outlast them; no one is accountable to voters because no one can be thrown out or re-elected; and career politician gets treated as obviously damning without anyone explaining why career surgeon or career pilot is fine. Exaggerate those mechanics.

After the piece, give me a table with one row per satirical move, five or six rows, with these exact columns: Detail (the line or device from the FAQ), Real mechanism it exaggerates (the actual feature of lotteries or term limits being stretched), and Objection it points to (the standard argument the satire lands without stating).
