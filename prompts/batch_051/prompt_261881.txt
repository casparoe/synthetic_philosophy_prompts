Give me your own number and decompose it. How much rides on whether people really do have a huge unacknowledged pool of possible coincidences, how much on whether “felt magnitude” can even be specified well enough for probabilities to apply, how much on whether these anecdotes function more as social speech than probability reports? Which sub-question would move your number most, and where do you think my friend and I actually disagree?
