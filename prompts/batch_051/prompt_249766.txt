I am a novelist, and across the series I am currently writing I repeatedly have to construct the religions of fictional cultures—sometimes an entire pantheon, sometimes a single deified ancestor—and I keep falling into one of two ruts: either the gods are arbitrary personifications with no tether to the culture's remembered past, or they are historical characters so thinly disguised that the worldbuilding reads as mechanical. A classicist friend has pointed me toward euhemerism, the view that many gods began as human beings—often founders, lawgivers, or benefactors—whose biographies were exaggerated, moralized, and merged over generations until the person became a deity. I would like to turn that idea into a working instrument rather than a piece of erudition. Please write me a reusable, step-by-step procedure I can run whenever I need to derive a god from the raw historical memory of one of my fictional cultures. The procedure should take me from a historical figure or event to a plausible deity, in order, and should specify the typical transformations (exaggeration of deeds, drift from name to title, absorption of rival local figures, moral whitening, genealogical inflation, and the like), the tests I should apply at each stage, and the points where the procedure must stop and hand the decision back to me as a matter of authorial judgment. As you translate the theory into steps, tell me which distinctions in the underlying idea must survive the simplification for my purposes and which can be dropped. I have no training in classics or anthropology, and I need something I can run in a single afternoon before a drafting session; my continuity editor is the person who will eventually sign off on the result for internal consistency.

Then please run the procedure on three cases drawn from one fictional culture: a lawgiver whose historical reforms were modest but well attested within the fiction; a recently dead queen whom I had already planned to deify but whose cruelties are still remembered by her subjects; and a storm deity that has no surviving human name attached to it. For each case, show what the procedure does at each step and what it finally produces. At least one of the results should be one I will not like, and the procedure should be honest enough to say so rather than forcing a god into existence; it should also say plainly where euhemerism is the wrong lens and the figure is better explained some other way.
