i sit on the board of a small community hospital and we're voting next tuesday on whether to build our own cardiac cath lab. the other hospital in the county just broke ground on one even though the regional health authority's own planning document said the county has enough volume for one lab at best, not two. our ceo says if we dont answer we'll lose our cardiologists and the ambulance traffic will follow the lab. a friend and i argued about this for hours last night and he kept pushing the moloch thing, the multipolar trap idea where every hospital is responding sensibly to what the other did and the whole system ends up worse, with two half-empty labs and everyone in more debt. i kept saying that sounds right in a seminar but the other hospital already made its move, so refusing on our end is just an abstract gesture that costs us our staff. i need to send a short memo to the other board members before the vote. it should recommend a concrete decision with a time frame, and it should honestly engage my friend's point rather than just saying we have to stay competitive. and please write it as continuous prose like a normal letter or email, no headings, no bullet points, no bold, no tables.
