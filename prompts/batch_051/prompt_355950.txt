Take the standard Sleeping Beauty setup—fair coin, amnesia, one waking on heads and two on tails—but add one twist. On every waking, just before she's asked for her credence in heads, the experimenter truthfully tells her either 'this is not your last waking' or 'this is your last waking,' depending on whether the schedule includes another waking for her. Beauty knows the whole protocol in advance; the wakings are otherwise indistinguishable. So on heads Monday she hears 'last'; on tails Monday she hears 'not last,' and on tails Tuesday she hears 'last.' Run the halfer and thirder calcuations through this version. Do the two camps still disagree after each possible announcement, and if so where? Does the twist just shift the fight, or does it give one side an advantage?
