I got diagnosed with ADHD in my early thirties last year, and it's made every opinion I have about "enhancement" feel like it was formed by someone who never actually had to take a pill just to function normally. I'm trying to reconstruct what principle I actually hold, so here are my honest gut calls on a stack of cases. I'd like you to tell me what principle, if any, gets most of them, which of my calls it forces me to abandon, and what it says about the case at the end.

Glasses to give 20/20: fine, and obviously not enhancement. My ADHD meds so I can do my actual job: fine. The same meds, same dose, after the job is done, to push through a hobby coding project: also fine, but I feel slightly less clean about it. A friend without ADHD using the same meds to cram for the bar exam: not fine, though I can't say why without it sounding like I'm policing who gets to call themselves disabled. A beta-blocker before a wedding speech: fine. Modafinil for a trucker hauling overnight: maybe fine, maybe even desirable. Coffee before a shift: obviously fine, and nobody calls it enhancement. Prozac for someone who is functioning but just emotionally flat: I lean fine, but I worry it's medicating ordinary sadness. Laser surgery corrected to 20/10, better than normal: fine; I had it and it just felt like a very good pair of glasses. Growth hormone for a short-but-normal kid: not fine; for a kid with a deficiency: fine. Steroids in baseball and blood doping in endurance sport: not fine. A prosthetic running blade: fine, even admirable. An ADHD med or nootropic for a fighter pilot on a long mission: fine; I'd probably prefer they take it. Gene editing an embryo for stronger working memory: not fine today, but that might just be yuck.

Is there one principle under all that, or close? Which of my calls does it have to overturn? And what does it say about the case I keep flip-flopping on: an antidepressant that doesn't treat a diagnosable illness but make someone more resilient and better at their job — is there actually a stable therapy/enhancement line doing the work here, or am I just laundering my intuitions through that word?
