Just got done with a long argument with a friend about spectrum cases and whether 'better than' is transitive, and now I'm worried my views don't hang together. Here's roughly where I've landed:

(i) 'better than' is transitive, full stop; if A's better than B and B's better than C, A's better than C.
(ii) In the headache spectrum — going from no headache to a full day of mild headache one added second at a time — no particular added second makes the longer headache worse than the one before it. Each step is basicaly a tie.
(iii) But a full day of mild headache is obviously worse than no headache.
(iv) I can't accept that there's some hidden cutoff, a first second where it becomes worse, because that just seems like the sorites mistake about heaps.
(v) I also treat 'not worse than' as just 'at least as good', so I figure if 'worse than' is transitive then 'not worse than' has to chain too.
(vi) And I think whatever dissolves the heap paradox should dissolve the headache spectrum too.

Go through these and tell me which pairs are actually in tension and which only look like they are. Rank the tensions by severity, then tell me what the cheapest single revision is, what it costs, and what it leaves me stuck with.
