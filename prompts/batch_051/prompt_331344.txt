I'm on the grievance committee of my union local, and I have to draft the committee's decision memo in a case that has split us down the middle. A member was fired after a short cell-phone video appeared to show him making a violent threat toward a coworker in the break room. He swears the video is a deepfake—or at least that the audio was spliced in—and says he was somewhere else for most of the clip. The original phone is gone, we don't have the budget for forensic authentication, and management is pushing us to rule by Friday. Half the committee wants to treat the video as real on the grounds that video evidence has always been the backstop that keeps workers from being steamrolled when it's their word against the company's; the other half says that if we start relying on a video nobody can vouch for, then no video will ever really settle a dispute again. I need a memo that recommends one concrete course of action—reinstate, deny the grievance, or postpone for a set period while we try to get the file checked—and that actually engages the strongest argument on the losing side, not just a list of pros and cons. Please write it in American English.
