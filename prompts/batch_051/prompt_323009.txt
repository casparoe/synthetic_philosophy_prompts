here's one for my existentialism module. I want something to hand out after the bad faith lecture — write me a deadpan parody of a corporate training module (the click-through compliance kind, with learning objectives and a warm-up question) for a café chain whose management has read just enough Sartre to be dangerous. The module is titled something like 'Radical Freedom at Work' and its official aim is to help waitstaff avoid bad faith on the job. The humor has to come from getting the theory right rather than from easy beret-and-Gauloises gags. So: it keeps insisting that employees are not essentially waiters — they are free beings who choose, each shift, to pick up the tray — and then immediately prescribes in numbing detail exactly how a free being must stand, greet, and smile, without ever noticing the contradiction. Include a learning objective that confuses facticity and transcendence, a quiz where every option is bad faith, and a highlighted paragraph on sincerity that accidentally makes the point that sincerity is just another way of turning yourself into a thing. Around 650 words, formatted as a proper training module. Keep it deadpan — it shouldnt ever wink or mention philosophy — but a reader who hasn't read Sartre should still find it funny, and someone who has read the waiter passage should feel the theory is being used fairly.
