He cites the gender-equality paradox, the reported pattern that more gender-equal countries have more sex-typical career choices rather than fewer, as evidence that the water has reached its natural level. His conclusion is that the remaining differences mostly reflect innate preferences, so pushing for parity in fields like nursing or software engineering means fighting a current that injustice did not create.
