Been reading al-Ghazali for a seminar this term and now I can't look at my own lab work the same way. As I understand him, he claims fire doesn't burn cotton; God does, and what we call the causal connection is just God's settled habit, which he could change at any moment. My labmates say its unfalsifiable and I should drop it, but that feels too quick. If you had to bring evidence to bear on the claim that the cause is fire's power rather than God's habit (or vice versa), what would the best test actually be? I want something concrete—an experiment, a novel intervention, a pattern in a dataset—not a hand-wave about miracles. Say what outcome would count for occasionalism and what would count against it, and be honest about what the test could never settle even if it came out the way the occasionalist expects. Also take a definite stand on whether the two views are genuinely empirically separable or whether they're the same picture in different words.
