Write the document that would come out of an adversarial collaboration between a Mu'tazili and an Ash'ari on the Islamic Euthyphro—one both camps could put their names to, with the disagreement left exact rather than papered over. Is an act good because God commands it, or does God command it because it is good, and does unaided reason get to any obligations before revelation arrives? Give me exactly seven numbered entries, each one or two sentences, no intro or conclusion: (1) and (2): two substantive claims both sides could sign about what they share and what the actual fight is about; (3): a neutral statement of the core residual disagreement they'd both accept, and if some of the fight looks verbal, say so here; (4): the Mu'tazili side's own wording of their view on divine justice; (5): the Ash'ari side's own wording of their view on divine power; (6): one genuine crux—maybe whether God could impose obligations beyond a person's capacity (taklīf mā lā yuṭāq); (7): one test or argument both sides would agree bears on that crux, with each side's predicted result written down ahead of time.
