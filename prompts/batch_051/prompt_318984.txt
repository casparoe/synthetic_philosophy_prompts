Draft the joint statement of an adversarial collaboration between a committed frequentist and a committed Bayesian about what the replication crisis in psychology and biomedicine actually shows and what should change in routine empirical practice. They are not allowed to settle for saying both frameworks are useful; every residual disagreement should be expressed as a difference about a concrete practice, quantity, or prediction.

The document should have three parts, in this order.

1. Numbered agreed claims. These should cover at least the following, without named studies or citations: what both sides accept about the basic facts of the replication crisis; what p-values, confidence intervals, posterior probabilities, and Bayes factors do and do not license; how a p < 0.05 publication filter behaves under optional stopping and under selection for positive findings; why pre-registration helps and what it leaves unfixed; and one substantive concession from each side that undercuts its own usual rhetoric. Each claim should be specific enough to be falsifiable, not a platitude.

2. A disagreements table. Each row should give the disagreement stated neutrally, the exact wording each side would accept for its own position, a judgment of whether the disagreement is substantive or partly verbal, and one operationally described condition under which each side would revise its view.

3. A cruxes section. For each crux, state the sub-question and what sort of evidence or argument would settle it, then record each side's prediction in advance. Include at least these two cruxes: first, if beginning in 2000 journals had required effect sizes with interval estimates and routinely published Bayes factors alongside p-values, would the replication rate observed in 2020 have been meaningfully higher, and how would each framework separately diagnose the replication failures that would remain? Second, was the deeper cause of the crisis the machinery of null-hypothesis significance testing, or the incentive to cross a bright-line threshold under any inferential framework?

Write it so that a serious practitioner from either camp could sign the whole document without wincing. Begin with the document itself, with no preface or commentary.
