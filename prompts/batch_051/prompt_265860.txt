Take the butterfly dream passage attributed to Zhuangzi. In the familiar telling, a man dreams he is a butterfly, wakes, and wonders whether he is a man who dreamt he was a butterfly or a butterfly now dreaming it is a man. The standard readings divide between an epistemic/skeptical interpretation, on which the passage belongs with dream skepticism, and a non-skeptical interpretation on which the point concerns transformation rather than knowledge. Now alter one feature: make the imagined symmetry actual. Let the dream be a stable, coherent, memory-rich state rather than a fleeting episode, and in that state let the butterfly, with equal vividness and no less right, be simultaneously wondering whether it is a butterfly dreaming it is a man. How does the standard analysis change? In particular, does this version strengthen the skeptical reading, weaken it, or leave it unchanged, and can the transformation reading still do its work when neither side can be assigned the role of the waking base state? Be precise about which feature of the original passage was doing the argumentative work for whichever reading changes.
