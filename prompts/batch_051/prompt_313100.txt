- A nosy coworker asks why you were out on Tuesday; you say "food poisoning," but you actually went to a job interview. Dana: totally fine; a nosy coworker isn't owed that.
- A friend about to go on stage asks if you like their new song; you say "it's very you," which is sort of true, but you think the song is terrible. Dana: fine, that's just not being a jerk.

What do you think Dana would say about that one, specifically relative to the flat "nothing happened"? Which of her earlier calls is the closest analog?
