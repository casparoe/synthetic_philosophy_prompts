Please describe the most important implications of this for how I should behave in anonymous one-shot online interactions where there is no reputation system, no enforcment, and no chance of meeting again—e.g., a random partner in an online game where we each pick cooperate or defect once and then part ways, or a peer-to-peer sale with no escrow. I want the practical upshot, but I also want you to say clearly where superrationality stops being a good guide, since the other person is not literally a twin of me and might not be running the same reasoning in any meaningful sense.
