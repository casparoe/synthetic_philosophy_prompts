Compare epistemic contextualism and pragmatic encroachment as explanations of the apparent stakes-sensitivity of knowledge ascriptions. Focus on a case in which a speaker in a low-stakes situation says of an agent who is in a high-stakes situation, "She knows that p." Explain how each view would treat this ascription, and in particular whether the two views agree about whether it is true. Then say what this shows about where each view locates the source of stakes-sensitivity: in the content of the word "knows," or in whether the agent stands in the knowledge relation to p. Where the two views come apart, identify which theoretical commitment is responsible for the divergence.
