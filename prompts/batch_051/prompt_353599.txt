Restate each in terms of modality-specific encoders, their latent representational spaces, alignment between those spaces, shared versus modality-specific factors of variation, zero-shot cross-modal transfer, paired versus unpaired multimodal training, invariance, and world-model or architectural priors.

Don't just give me analogies; I want a translation that's as honest as possible about what it breaks.
