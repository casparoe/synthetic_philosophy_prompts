Write a deadpan parody of a corporate HR memo announcing a company-wide "Total Legibility" initiative. The piece should be about 700 words, formatted as numbered clauses with a short preamble. The voice is calm, upbeat management-speak; it should never wink, explain its own joke, or state the moral outright.

The target is the view that there is a general duty to be maximally transparent and legible to others — not the narrower, much more defensible duty not to deceive. The humor should come from exaggerating real features of that ideal rather than from inventing a strawman, so that someone who knows the philosophical debates about sincerity, transparency, and legibility will recognize that the satire is fair. Over the course of the memo, have the policy quietly:

- treat sincerity and transparency as equivalent, so that any thought, feeling, or intention that hasn't been disclosed is treated as a form of deception;
- recast privacy, reticence, and an unexpressed inner life as "information hoarding," "opacity debt," or abuse of an informational asymmetry;
- require trust to be earned through continuous access, as if the only alternative to total legibility were suspicion;
- require employees to render their inner states in a standardized, audit-ready format, as though legibility were a neutral process of disclosure rather than a demand to conform to someone else's categories of relevance;
- treat the refusal to make oneself legible as a harm to others, while the company and its managers remain conveniently exempt from the standards the memo imposes.

Concrete mechanisms might include public decision logs, required reporting of "unvoiced reservations," real-time mood tags, or penalties for failing to disclose context others might later want; choose whatever mechanisms best advance the satire. By the final clause, the reductio should be clear on its own: followed to the end, the memo's logic would abolish the private mind it affects merely to open. Avoid named authors, real quotations, and citations; the piece should work without any outside reading.
