Setup as i understand it: ZFC is a first-order theory, so by the downward Lowenheim-Skolem theorem, if it's consistent it has a countable model. But ZFC also proves Cantor's theorem, so it proves there are uncountable sets — for example the powerset of the naturals. Inside the countable model there's an object that satisfies the definition of being the powerset of the naturals, and the model thinks that object is uncountable, but since the whole model is countable from the outside, that object has only countably many elements viewed externally. The catch is that the external bijection enumerating it isn't an element of the model, so the model can't use it.

The two camps:

- The Skolemite relativist: thinks the paradox shows countable, uncountable, powerset, and maybe even finite are relative to a model or a background framework, and that there's no uniquely intended set-theoretic universe that first-order ZFC pins down.
- The absolutist, or Platonist-ish camp: thinks there's a determinate cumulative hierarchy V that ZFC is really about, and that the countable model is just a first-order artifact that's missing most subsets of the naturals (or whose membership relation isn't the genuine one), so it doesn't tell us anything deep about what's true in V.

Write the joint statement they could both put their names to after the collaboration, not a debate transcript. Structure it as:

3. A cruxes section: the sub-questions such that settling one of them would actually move at least one camp. I suspect the big ones are (a) whether all subsets of the naturals has a determinate, model-independent meaning; (b) whether the fact that the countable model's membership relation might not be the genuine ∈ is a real escape hatch for the absolutist or just special pleading, given that the relativist can say the same kind of thing about any attempted characterization; and (c) whether the relativist is willing to say the same thing about arithmetic and nonstandard models of PA, since Skolem's style of argument seems to apply there too. Add or replace these only if you have a better one.

The point is to remove all the talking-past and slanted phrasing and isolate the exact residual disagreement. If part of the dispute turns out to be about words, say which part and why.
