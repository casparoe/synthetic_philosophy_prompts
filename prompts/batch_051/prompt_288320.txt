I'm also not sure what the ladder even is: is trial and error lower than insight, or just a different kind of process? Is associative learning lower than planning, or are we smuggling in a pre-made ranking of minds? And does the canon apply to me as much as to the crow, or only to animals?

What I want is not a single reading but a menu.
