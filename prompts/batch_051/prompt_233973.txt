Write a short story about a defense official in a small nuclear state who convinces her government to deploy a retaliatory system with a deliberately uncontrollable feature: once the system has been armed during a crisis, there is a small, openly advertised chance that it fires even if every human in the chain of command trys to stand it down. She genuinely hates war, and her public argument is that this is the only way to make the threat credible, because a rational adversary can no longer count on the defender's restraint. The story should live inside the paradox that the exact feature meant to guarantee the peace is the one most likely to cause the war nobody wants. Show her wrestling with that rather than resolving it, and don't end with the system being safely dismantled or the warheads turning out to be duds.
