I am drawn, almost against my own temperament, to the view that argumentation would be improved by being run more like a competitive sport: structured matches with clarified burdens of proof, trained judges, transparent scoring criteria, rankings, and professional competitors. The theory has genuine systematic virtues. Competition is a proven mechanism for raising performance in nearby practices — competitive chess produced a class of players who see further than any amateur, and the expectation of cross-examination forces arguers to prepare the weakest parts of their case. A scoring regime would also give argument the one thing it conspicuously lacks: a decision procedure that does not reduce to the charisma of the speaker or the prior beliefs of the room.

But I have an equally firm intuition that argument is constitutively a cooperative, truth-oriented practice, and that the sport model gets the incentives backwards. If the payoff is winning under a rubric, then the rational arguer optimizes for whatever earns points from judges, and what earns points need not be what tracks truth. A practice that selects for the best contestant under pressure is selecting for something like rhetorical adaptability, not for the disposition to revise one's beliefs. The worry is not that competition would fail to raise performance; it is that it would raise performance at a different activity.

I want the strongest version of both sides, and then a negotiated verdict. In particular: (1) What would justify my dismissing the intuition as a romantic, status-quo picture of inquiry that competition would in fact serve better — and what would that dismissal cost? (2) What would justify amending or abandoning the theory — for instance, by restricting competition to training and excluding it from the goal of inquiry — and what would that cost? (3) Is there a coherent reading on which argument is simultaneously competitive in its form and cooperative in its aim, and would any actual institutional design make that more than a piece of verbal reconciliation?
