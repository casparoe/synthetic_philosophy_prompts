ive been tinkering with my view on free will and punishment after reading the Dennett/Caruso exchange, and for a reading group I have to actually write down my position and defend it. The problem is that different friends keep telling me different pieces of it dont fit together, and I genuinely can't tell which complaints are real. Here is where I've landed:

(1) Im a compatibilist about free will. What matters is reasons-responsiveness --- the capacity to reflect on and revise ones intentions in light of reasons --- and determinism doesnt threaten that, so we have all the free will that matters even if we couldn't have done otherwise in the categorical sense.

(2) But I dont think anyone is morally responsible in the basic desert sense. Retributivism needs the idea that an offender ultimately deserves to suffer, and I cant see how any causal history, deterministic or not, supplies that kind of ultimate deserve-it.

(3) That said, I think ordinary reactive attitudes --- resentment, indignation, gratitude --- are mostly justified and fully compatible with determinism. I dont think we should slide into treating each other in the detached objective way we treat broken machines just because determinism is true.

(4) Legal punishment should be justified entirely by its forward-looking effects: deterrence, incapacitation, rehabilitation. Long sentences for violent offenders are fine when public safety really requires them, but not because the offender has it coming.

(5) Yet I am also confident that two equally reckless drunk drivers should be treated very differently when one happens to kill a child and the other makes it home without incident. The one who caused the death should get a substantially harsher sentence, and that difference is a matter of justice, not just a pragmatic concession.

(6) Finally, I think some people --- say, a psychopath who cannot respond to moral reasons at all --- are not apt targets of resentment, but it is still fine to detain them purely as a public safety measure. That feels more like quarantine than punishment to me.

So: does this package hang together? Please audit it rather than lecture me. Go through the pairs, identify which tensions are real and which only look like tensions, rank the real ones by severity, and say for each what would have to give and what the cheapest repair is. If the whole thing is consistent after one small clarification, say so --- but I have a hunch something has to go.
