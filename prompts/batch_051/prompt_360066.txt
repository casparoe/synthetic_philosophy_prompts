But a commercial developer is about to jump in ahead of us: our planning lead was tipped off, off the record, that the developer files on Monday. The only way to get our bid in first is to stall. Half the committee thinks this is the dirty hands problem — a bit of official bad faith in service of a clearly good outcome, so we should do it and own the wrong.
