One level up it's farming and early cities; then printing presses and steam engines; then the ramp opens into a top floor full of smartphones, gene-sequencing machines, and a mirrored ceiling with a sign about "humanity's climb." So the same building says the past was a hard upward scramble and also a storehouse of lost wisdom; it says the present is the pinnacle, and then immediately says it might be a ledge.
