The instructor wants to build the first unit around Maimonides's claim—or the standard reading of it—that the sacrificial system was a concession to the Israelites' idolatrous surroundings, not something God commanded for its own sake. This has split the committee. Some of us think its exactly the serious framing the course needs, because it forces students to ask whether a commandment remains binding once the historical situation it was a concession to has disappeared. Others think it's corrosive: it makes the Torah look like an instrument for managing bad habits, and students will start treating other mitzvot as similarly disposable. Draft the short decision memo I'll circulate before our vote. I need it to actually land—recommend that we include the unit, drop it, or include it with specific caveats—and give reasons we can act on. Also make sure you engage the strongest consideration on the side you recommend against, not a caricature of it.
