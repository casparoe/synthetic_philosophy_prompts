I've been trying to sort out whether loyalty to one's groups is a virtue or just a bias with good publicity, and I find I keep coming down on the side of partiality. I am explicitly not defending its degenerate forms: nepotism that violates the legitimate claims of strangers, loyalty to a corrupt organization, or the refusal to notice when one's group does wrong.
