What would Nietzsche make of modern rationalist-adjacent health culture if he were around today? I don't want "he'd admire discipline" or "he'd write a takedown." I want you to actually run his physiological/psychological commitments forward:

1. Intermittent fasting / time-restricted eating. He was clearly pro-small-meals and pro-not-overeating. Would he endorse 16:8 as a sign of strength, or would he diagnose it as just another ascetic self-denial with the usual hidden ressentiment structure?
