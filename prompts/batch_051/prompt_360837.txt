(7) A parent tells their kid before a minor operation "you're going to be fine." I think that's fine, even though the parent doesn't know it; it seems to be doing a different kind of job than reporting.

(8) A climate scientist says "on current emissions we're on track for around three degrees of warming by 2100," with the uncertainty made explicit. I think that's fine, even though "around" and the error bars mean it isn't exactly known.
