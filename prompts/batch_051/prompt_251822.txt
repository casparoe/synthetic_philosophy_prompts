The price was way below market, basically a fire sale, because the family had about two weeks to get rid of everything. My grandfather farmed that land for forty years, my dad worked it too, and now it's mine; my kids are growing up on it.

I didn't do the wrong and neither did my dad. My brother says the government already settled the claim and private families aren't responsible for what the state did; my wife thinks selling the farm to send them money would be guilt, not principle.
