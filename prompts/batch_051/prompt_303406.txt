I have been trying to sort out a disagreement with a friend about whether frugality is best understood as a way of conducting yourself or as a way of bringing about a lower-consuming world. I was taught to assess whether someone is frugal by roughly the following criteria: (i) personal consumption is low relative to means; (ii) the person treats waste in her own conduct as something objectionable in itself, not merely as one regrettable contribution to a global total; (iii) restraint is a settled disposition, exercised even when no one is watching and when the particular act will have no measurable effect; and (iv) the person would not regard her own lavish spending as made acceptable by paying someone else to be thrifty.

Now consider a case that sits uneasily against those criteria. A financier lives conspicuously: she flies privately, maintains two large houses, replaces functioning possessions for aesthetic reasons, and shows little day-to-day self-discipline in her own spending. She is also the principal and unusually effective funder of an organization that reduces aggregate consumption at scale—it finances energy and material retrofits across thousands of buildings, buys and retires high-consumption equipment, and runs interventions that demonstrably move far more households toward durable restraint than any number of personally frugal lives could. Her own excess, by her foundation's internal accounting, is a negligible fraction of the consumption her funding eliminates.

Does she qualify as frugal? Work through each criterion honestly; where the criteria return a "no," say whether the criterion is doing real work or whether the case exposes a flaw in treating frugality as purely a feature of personal conduct. Then take a stand on the underlying issue: should frugality be read deontologically, as a virtue realized in one's own conduct, or consequentially, as a value to be promoted by whatever conduct produces the greatest overall reduction? Finally, identify one small change to the case that would flip or stabilize your verdict, and say what that reveals about which feature is doing the judging.
