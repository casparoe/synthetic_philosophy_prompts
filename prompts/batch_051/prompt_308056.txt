I'm working on a piece about whether minds could run on hardware other than brains, and I keep tripping over one word: realize. Philosophers like to say mental states are multiply realizable, but what exactly does it take for a given hunk of stuff to realize, say, a state of panic? Give me a working definition that gets these cases right: a human brain mid-panic-attack; an octopus showing what looks like the same panic response with totally different wiring; a silicon chip running a program whose input-output profile matches the brain's; a giant lookup table that returns all the same outputs with no intermediate processing; a billion people passing paper slips that collectively mimic the program; and a weather simulation that produces a downpour. I'm confident the first two should count and the weather simulation shouldn't, and I'm genuinly torn on the middle three. Propose the definition, run it through all six, and tell me where it surprises you or goes silent.
