Begin by fixing the purpose relative to which you will judge adequacy, namely an account intended to underwrite both the formal mathematics of probability and ordinary judgements about what could happen. Then write the list of requirements that such an account of the relationship between possibility and probability must meet, with the case or consideration behind each. Treat 'possibility' as covering at least logical, metaphysical, physical, and epistemic possibility, and 'probability' as covering both credence and objective chance; say where a requirement applies only to one sense. For each requirement, mark it as non-negotiable or as a preference of a particular camp, and justify that marking. Ensure the list engages at least the following pressure points: the infinite lottery, in which each of countably many tickets is possible although no uniform countably additive probability can assign any ticket a positive real value; uncountable spaces such as the dartboard, where a specific point is possible but has probability zero; the gap between 'almost surely' and 'necessarily'; the regularity requirement that every non-empty possibility receive positive, perhaps infinitesimal, probability, and its tension with countable additivity; the question whether possibility is prior to probability, probability is prior to possibility, or neither is prior; the question whether positive probability is sufficient, necessary, both, or neither for possibility; the existence of rival priors over one and the same space of possibilities; and the deterministic world, in which objective chance takes only the values zero and one while some 'could have been otherwise' talk remains intelligible. Then identify any subset of these requirements that cannot be jointly satisfied, say which member of the inconsistent subset should give way, and explain the grounds; that is the part I most want to see. Finally, state which single requirement does the most to exclude candidate accounts and which is most likely a prejudice of a particular camp rather than a genuine constraint. Skip any scorecard of existing views. Please write the response in British English.
