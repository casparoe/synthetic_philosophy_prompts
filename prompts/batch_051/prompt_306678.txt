I'm a backend engineer who builds payment and workflow systems. My working vocabulary is the one I actually use: replicated state machines, message queues, transactional preconditions and invariants, commit and rollback, idempotency and exactly-once delivery, acknowledgment and consensus, event logs, and role-based permissions. My philosophical vocabulary is nearly nil.

I want a specific job done. The speech act tradition from Austin through Searle holds that some utterances are actions rather than reports: a judge's verdict, a marriage pronouncement, a bet, an apology. Austin initially contrasts constatives, which say how things stand and are true or false, with performatives, which do something and are felicitous or infelicitous. He then generalizes into the locutionary act, the illocutionary act, and the perlocutionary effect. The framework also includes felicity conditions: a conventional procedure must exist, the persons and circumstances must be appropriate, and the procedure must be executed correctly and completely. Infelicities divide into misfires, where the act does not come off, and abuses, where the act comes off but is hollow. And there is uptake: for many acts, the hearer must recognize what was done.

Restate that entire position in my engineering vocabulary as faithfully as you can. I already half-believe a performative is just a signed message that, if it passes preconditions and authorization, commits a state transition to a shared ledger, while a constative is a read query. But I want you to do the translation properly and then give me the audit: which elements map cleanly, which needed distortion, and which have no counterpart at all. For each mismatch, say whether it reflects a limitation of my vocabulary, a looseness in the philosophical account, or something real that my framework cannot express — for example, obligations and permissions that persist because people hold themselves to them, not because a state machine enforces them. End with a verdict on my half-belief: is a performative just a state change caused by a message, or is that intuition missing something my tools cannot say?
