Here are the cases I want it to handle:

A. A man is convinced his wife has been replaced by an identical-looking impostor. He's otherwise pretty ordinary, and no amount of evidence, photos, or friends vouching for her moves him.
B. A woman with severe anorexia is medically underweight but is completely convinced she is fat, and she eats almost nothing because of it.
C. A man believes his partner is cheating on him. He has no real evidence and a fair amount of contrary evidence, but he checks her phone, follows her, and believes she's just very good at hiding it.

F. A seven-year-old firmly believes Santa Claus comes down the chimney on Christmas Eve.

H. A man is convinced he is dead and that his organs rotted months ago, although he still chats and eats; when you ask how that works, he says the body is running on automatic.
