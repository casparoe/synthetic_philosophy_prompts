I'm pulling together a one-pager on group rationality for a reading group, and I want a consistency audit before I send it around. Here's where I've landed:

(a) I'm an individualist about group attitudes: a committee's position is built out of its members' positions, and saying "the committee believes p" is just bookkeeping, not a claim about a collective mind.

(b) For any proposition on the agenda, the group should go with the majority of the members on that proposition. That seems like the only rule that treats each member's judgment as counting equally.

(c) The group's public positions should be logically consistent. If a body accepts p, accepts if p then q, and also rejects q, something has gone wrong at the group level, even if each member's own view is perfectly consistant.

(d) In the discursive dilemma setup—three judges, majority for valid contract, majority for breach, but only one judge for liability, so a majority rejects liability—I think the right way to aggregate is premise-based: take majority votes on the premises and let logic set the conclusion. So I'm okay with the court finding liability because two judges say valid contract and two say breach, even though two judges would vote against the liability proposition itself.

(e) But I also think no proposition is intrinsically a premise. Whether a proposition is premise or conclusion is a seperate modeling choice, not a feature of the proposition, so I don't see why "valid contract" and "breach" should get privileged treatment over "liability."

(f) I don't think (c) forces me to posit a group mind. Consistency is just a normative constraint on a body's output, like the constraint that a database shouldn't store contradictory entries.

Does this package hang together? Please audit it rather than lecture me on it: identify which commitments are genuinely in tension, rank those tensions by severity, say for each what would have to give under the least costly revision, and flag any apparent tensions that dissolve on inspection. And tell me which single change would clean up the most. Please write your answer in American English.
