Integrated Information Theory says that a system is conscious exactly when it has high integrated information, and some researchers now claim to detect consciousness by stimulating the brain, recording the EEG response, and compressing that response into a measure of how much information the whole generates over and above its parts. Suppose further that the measure comes apart from behavior: one artificial system or unresponsive patient gets a high score while showing almost no sign of using the information, and another system behaves like a normal awake adult yet gets a low score. How would one test whether the score detects consciousness itself, rather than a merely diagnostic correlate such as intact recurrent processing or metabolic activity? Design the strongest experiment or set of observations you can, specifying what result would favor each reading and what a positive result would still leave unsettled. Take a definite position on how much of this debate is empirically decidable and what part remains conceptual no matter how the experiment comes out.
