I'm on the writting-system committee for our community's language revitalization project, and I'm supposed to circulate a short written opinion before next month's meeting. I'm not any kind of linguist — I mostly joined because my grandmother speaks the language and I want my kids to be able to read it. So I need help getting this into something the committee can actually vote on.

Here's the situation. Our language has a thing where a sound at the end of a word changes when you add an ending. The word for "hand," said by itself, sounds like "dop," with a p sound at the end. But in "my hand," it comes out "dobi," with a b. There's two camps on the committee. One side says spell it like it sounds: "dop" in one place and "dobi" in the other. They say that's easiest for new readers and for the kids in the immersion school. The other side says underneath, it's the same word, so the root should look the same everywhere: "dob" and "dobi." They say it shows learners that those are forms of one word and it matches how the elders feel the word is, even though the last sound changes. The first side fires back that "dob" by itself would make a beginner say it wrong, and that spelling is for sounds, not for hidden history.

I've been turning this over on my own, and I get that no writing system marks everything — nobody writes every little breath or lip movement. So the real fight seems to be about what the alphabet is trying to capture. Even the "write it like it sounds" side is making a choice: if you listen close, the p in "dop" and the p in "dobi" aren't quite identical either, but everyone's fine treating them as the same sound. So it's not one side being exact and the other being not — it's two different ideas of what the exact thing should be.

Please draft the short written opinion I'll circulate. It should recommend one of the two approaches (or a limited compromise, but it has to be something we can actually vote on), with a concrete decision for our next set of begginner readers and a timeline for making the change. And please take the strongest point from the side you're not recommending seriously, not just knock down a strawman.
