Here is a passage from Zhuangzi, *The Great and Most Honoured Master*, in James Legge’s translation:

> The True men of old knew nothing of the love of life or of the hatred of death. Entrance into life occasioned them no joy; the exit from it awakened no resistance. Composedly they went and came. ... They accepted (their life) and rejoiced in it; they forgot (all fear of death), and returned (to their state before life).
