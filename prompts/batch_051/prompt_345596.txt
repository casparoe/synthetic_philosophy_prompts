I keep seeing charter cities and seasteading pitched like this: if everyone who lives there has signed the master contract or moved there knowing the rules, then political authority is finally based on actual consent rather than the tacit consent of being born somewhere. I don't get why critics still treat that as just a privatized state. If the residents genuinly opted in, isn't the city just the voluntary equivalent of a state, and therefore free of the usual problems of coercion and legitimicy? What exactly would there be left to object to?
