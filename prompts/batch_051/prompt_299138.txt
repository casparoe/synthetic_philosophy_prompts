He wrote up the following argument and insisted it shows they're equivalent. I can't shake the feeling that something goes wrong, but I can't locate it. Can you check it?

(1) The minimax regret rule says: pick the act whose largest possible regret is smallest. (2) The regret of an act in a state is how far it falls below the best act you could have chosen in that same state. (3) So "make your largest regret as small as possible" just means "make your largest shortfall from the best you could have done as small as possible." (4) But your largest shortfall from the best you could have done is just another way of stating how bad your worst-case payoff is. (5) So minimizing your largest regret is the same thing as maximizing your worst-case payoff. That means minimax regret and maximin always pick the same act.

(2) Step-by-step analysis, about four or five sentences: run through each step and say whether it's fine and exactly where any problem creeps in.
