so I've been trying to get clear on what actually makes something funny, and I've noticed my gut reactions to cases don't obviously fit any single theory of humour I've read about. I'd like you to analyse my verdicts and tell me what principle, if any, captures them. Here they are:

(1) A video of a man slipping on ice with his arms windmilling, then landing in a bush. I laugh, but only because I'm fairly sure he was not hurt. If I later find out he broke his wrist, the clip stops being funny in retrospect and I feel a bit grim for having laughed.

(2) A clever pun, the sort that turns on re-parsing one phrase two ways. I find it funny even though no one is being put down and no tension is being released. The laugh seems to come just from the snap of the double take.

(3) A joke whose whole point is that a politician I despise is made to look stupid. I do laugh, and I can't honestly deny that part of why I laugh is that I despise him, because the same joke about a politician I admire seems mean rather than funny. But I also have a standing sense that this is a cheaper, slightly grubby kind of amusement.

(4) At my grandfather's funeral, after a long horrid silence, my brother whispered an old family joke that is not, in itself, funny. I nearly cried from lauging. Whatever that was, it was not about the joke's content.

(5) A stand-up bit about a plane crash in which the comedian works in the names of actual victims. I don't find it funny, even whilst I'm in the mood for dark comedy, and even though structurally it's much like jokes I do laugh at.

(6) Being tickled as a child: hilarious, no joke involved, but only when it's someone I trust. A stranger doing the exact same thing would be horrifying, not funny.

Do these add up to one thing, or is 'funny' just covering four or five different phenomena? If there is a principle, or a small set of candidate principles, that best captures these verdicts, state it precisely and say which of my verdicts it forces me to drop or revise. Then tell me what your account predicts about a case I keep going back and forth on: a sarcastic insult between two close friends, the sort that would be openly hostile if a stranger said it, but they both laugh.

oh and please write the answer in American English.
