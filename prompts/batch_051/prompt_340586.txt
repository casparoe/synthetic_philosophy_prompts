I'm suspicious, because when I read a text the author meant to communicate something, whereas a midden was never addressed to anyone. Audit the analogy properly: give me the correspondence table, say what features of text-reading it quietly imports that the archaeological record lacks, separate the disanalogies that matter for her conclusion from the ones that dont, and tell me whether the conclusion survives.
