In the King James Version, James 2:18–19 (the Epistle of James) reads: "Yea, a man may say, Thou hast faith, and I have works: shew me thy faith without thy works, and I will shew thee my faith by my works. Thou believest that there is one God; thou doest well: the devils also believe, and tremble." Paul, by contrast, insists that a person is justified by faith apart from works of the law (as in Romans 3:28 and Galatians 2:16). Compare these two positions on faith and works. Answer in exactly five numbered points, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion. Your five points should: (1) reconstruct the position James is attacking and explain what the remark about the devils is meant to show; (2) state what Paul means by justification and what "works of the law" does in his argument; (3) assess whether James's "works" and Paul's "works of the law" pick out the same thing; (4) compare what each author means by "faith"; and (5) give a reasoned verdict on whether James and Paul are in real conflict or are answering different questions, including what each side should concede.
