I am a PhD student in philosophy of biology, working through Popper's scattered remarks on Darwinism and natural selection, and I keep tripping over a conceptual knot that I would like someone to untangle for me. Popper's early judgment was that the principle of natural selection, at least as Darwin left it, comes close to being tautological: if fitness is cashed out in terms of actual survival, then the claim that the fittest survive tells us nothing beyond the fact that the survivors survived. From this he seems to have drawn the conclusion that evolutionary theory lacks the empirical riskiness that his criterion of demarcation demands of a genuine scientific hypothesis. Later he took that back and granted that natural selection is testable, pointing in part toward the logical structure of population-level explanations and in part toward what he called situational logic.

What I want to understand is not the biographical sequence but the underlying conceptual structure. Which contentful proposition is doing the work when a Darwinian explanation is genuinely more than a restatement of survival? Is the late Popper's concession best read as the claim that the core of Darwinism, though not by itself falsifiable, operates as a metaphysical research program that makes specific ecological and genetic hypotheses testable, or as the stronger claim that the core itself becomes falsifiable once it is properly formulated? I would like an explanation that works through the relevant distinctions rather than skirting them: the difference between a formulation that is vacuous and one that is merely unfalsifiable, the difference between a metaphysical research program and a scientific theory in Popper's sense, and the difference between a statement that is logically irrefutable and one that is simply hard to refute. Please write in continuous prose only, with no headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables, the way one would write a letter, and end by saying which reading of Popper's recantation you find more defensible and why.
