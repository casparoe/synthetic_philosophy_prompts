Take a committed computationalist who thinks this argues past the mechanist: roughly, that the argument works only against particular systems that are actually specified and known to be consistent, and the mechanist is not committed to any such specification being available (the 'you don't know which program you are' style reply), plus a suspicion that the anti-mechanist's ability to recognise consistency or iterate reflection principles may itself be algorithmic after all. What's the joint statement the two of them could both sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration?
