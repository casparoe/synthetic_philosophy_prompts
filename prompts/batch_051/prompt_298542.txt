I'm an undergraduate in a philosophy of cognitive science course, and I've been asked to lead one 50-minute discussion section of about 16 students on the idea of competence without comprehension. My instructor has framed it through Dennett as a parallel between Darwin and Turing: Darwin reversed the usual order of explanation by showing that design-like outcomes can be produced by blind variation and selection without any designer, and Turing reversed it by showing that competent performance can be produced by a mechanism that does not understand what it is doing. Most of the students are philosophy majors with no programming background and little biology, and the room is a standard seminar room with movable chairs and a whiteboard. The section should be an activity rather than a mini-lecture: I want the students to feel the force of the inversion from the inside before it is named. Please design a runnable classroom activity for that session. Specify the rules or roles, the materials (only what a standard seminar room offers, such as whiteboard, paper, index cards, perhaps a deck of cards), and a minute-by-minute timeline for the 50 minutes. The design should make the competence of the group's process visible while making it obvious that no individual participant comprehends the outcome or the target, and it should let participants experience the shared logical structure of the Darwinian and Turingian cases rather than merely illustrating an analogy. Then provide a structured debrief that maps specific moments in the activity onto specific parts of the idea, naming the idea only after the activity has made the pattern difficult to dismiss. Also anticipate what will predictably go wrong when I run it, for instance participants quietly treating the activity as just a metaphor and re-importing a designer or an understander during discussion, or one person stepping in as a de facto homunculus, and say how to preempt or handle each failure. Be honest about which feature of the idea a classroom simulation cannot reproduce and what I will have to supply explicitly afterwards. The takeaway students should leave with is that the appearance of design or intelligence in a process does not by itself license positing a comprehending agent anywhere in the system.
