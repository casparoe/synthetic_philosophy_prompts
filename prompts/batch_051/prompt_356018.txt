so my friends and i have been playing Nomic, the game where the whole point is that players vote to change the rules themselves. our ruleset has a standing rule, Rule 1, saying any new rule or any repeal passes by a simple majority. a while ago Rob proposed Rule 17, a rule that Rule 17 itself can be amended or repealed only by a unanimous vote, and it passed 6–3 under Rule 1. the other night i moved to repeal Rule 17, and my motion got 7–2. Rob says the repeal is invalid: Rule 17 is still in force until its validly repealed, and it says that takes unanimity, which we didnt have. i say that cant be right: Rule 17 only exists because a majority passed it, so a majority should be able to undo it—otherwise a bare majority got to lock itself in forever, using exactly the lower bar that Rule 17 itself refuses to accept for its own repeal. our ruleset has no rule for what to do when two rules conflict, so we cant punt to that. who's right? and if we're both a little right, what should we agree to do next session?
