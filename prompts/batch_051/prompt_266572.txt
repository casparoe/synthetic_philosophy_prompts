I keep returning to a proposal for low-impact AI that I call the pruned-agent baseline, and I want it pressure-tested.

Consider an agent with a learned policy and a generative world-model that assigns a distribution over future trajectories to each available action. For any action a in a state s, construct a comparison policy by retraining, or re-solving, the same agent in an environment identical to the original except that a—or, more carefully, the class of interventions of which a is an instance—is removed from the action space. Call the result the pruned agent, and call its behavior in s the baseline. The impact of actually taking a is then defined as the divergence between the trajectory distribution predicted under the original agent choosing a and the trajectory distribution predicted under the pruned agent in the same state. I am unsure whether the right divergence is KL, total variation, Wasserstein, or something else.

The motivating claim is that this gives you low-impact behavior without a value function. The measure never asks whether an outcome is good or bad; it asks how far the intervention moves the world from what the same system would most competently have done had it never possessed this particular lever. A robot that can either sweep the floor or activate an industrial press would score sweeping as near-zero impact, because a copy of it retrained without press-related actions would most likely still sweep; pressing the button would score as high impact, because the press-free counterpart would be off doing something ordinary. Low impact thus becomes side effects relative to the agent's own counterfactual competence, rather than relative to an imagined idle state or a human-supplied reward.

I can anticipate several objections: the individuation of action classes is doing hidden normative work; retraining is not a canonical operation; the baseline inherits whatever destructive goals the agent already has; and no divergence between trajectories is itself value-free. Some of those feel fatal, but I want to know whether the core idea is salvageable, whether it is already in the literature under a better name, and where precisely it fails. Please give me the strongest reconstruction of the proposal, then the three or four strongest objections, and say which, if any, is fatal rather than merely uncomfortable.
