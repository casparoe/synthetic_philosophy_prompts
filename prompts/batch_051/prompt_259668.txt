I suspect the slogan is doing real argumentative work, but nobody ever says what exactly it asserts. Could you lay out the menu of precise claims it might be making — about the structure of the race, who should control the technology, what kind of harm we're facing, why normal scientific openness won't help, what policy levers follow — and for each say whether it's a platitude, a substantive and defensible claim, or just false? I especially want to know which readings would actually license my student's inference from "it's a Manhattan Project" to "open-weight models are like published bomb secrets," and which readings leave that inference unsupported.
