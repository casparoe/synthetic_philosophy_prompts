I do scientific simulation for a living — numerical integration, state vectors, update rules, terabytes of trajectory logs. Lately I've been reading about the Humeanism vs. anti-Humeanism debate about laws of nature, and I keep bouncing off the philosophcal vocabulary: Humean mosaic, best system, nomic necessitation, laws as summaries versus laws as governing. From what I can tell, the debate seems to be about whether the laws are just a very good compression of everything that happens, or something over and above the events that somehow constrains or produces them. That maps suspiciously well onto a distinction I use every day: the difference between a model you fit to a trace after the fact, and an update rule that generates the trace in the first place. A fitted model is descriptive, it can overfit, and two very different fitted models can agree perfectly on the data you've seen while disagreeing wildly about what happens next. An update rule is causal — the trace is what it is because the rule said to step that way. So my working hypothesis is that the whole debate is just: does the universe have source code, or is physics a giant compressed logfile? And that the interesting disagreement is over whether there's any fact of the matter about which compression is the real law.

Restate the main positions in the debate — Humean and anti-Humean, including whatever distinctions inside each camp matter (I keep hearing names attached to positions but don't care who said what) — in my vocabulary: traces, state, update rules, compression, overfitting, counterfactual queries against a model vs. against a simulation, determinism. Be as faithful as you can, and tell me where the translation forces you to twist something. Then give me the residue report: what translates cleanly, what translates only with distortion, and what has no counterpart in my vocabulary at all. For the stuff that doesn't survive, tell me whether that's my vocabulary missing something real or the philosophers' disagreement being less substantive than it looks. And crucially: is my update-rule-vs-fitted-model gloss smuggling in a side? Or is it a neutral translation that shows the two sides are talking past each other?
