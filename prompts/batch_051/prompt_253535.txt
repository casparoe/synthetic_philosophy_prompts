Write me a deadpan parody FAQ/citizen's charter, about 600 words, from a government depatment in a state that has fully implemented luck egalitarianism. The department is called the Office of Brute Luck Compensation. The questions and answers should all turn on the actual machinery of the view: the brute luck/option luck distinction, the hypothetical insurance test (if you could have insured against it, you're treated as having chosen it), the expensive-tastes rule, and the problem of deciding whether someone's risky life was really bad luck or just a gamble they endorsed. Don't make up strawman positions — the jokes have to come from the theory itself, not from inventing things the view doesn't say. By the end it should be legible why critics say this set-up is harsh and intrusive, but the satire should stay straight-faced and never state that outright. A reader who knows Dworkin and Elizabeth Anderson should find it fair, and someone who doesn't should still get the joke. Write the answer in British English, and no citations or footnotes.
