But he also admits some reasons that might make future stuff matter less, like you might not exist or your preferences could change. I have to write an essay on whether pure time preference is rational. Like is it defensible to just care less about your own future wellbeing, same certainty, same person, only because it comes later? Weigh the strongest arguments for saying yes and for saying no, and then defend a real conclusion, not a wishy-washy one.
