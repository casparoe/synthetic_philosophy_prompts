I retired a few years back and have been reading epistemology for fun. There's an argument I keep having with an old colleague, and it's starting to bug me. We both read the same big new meta-analysis on whether vitamin D supplements actually reduce fractures in older adults. I went in pretty skeptical, he went in fairly convinced they help. The results were mixed; we both shifted a bit, but we still disagree. He says that by itself shows one of us is being irrational. His line is: if we're both honestly updating on the same evidence and neither of us is dogmatic, then our intial leanings should get washed out as more and more data comes in, so eventually we'd have to end up in the same place. My gut says he's overstating it, but I can't put my finger on where. Who's right? I'd like to know exactly what conditions would have to hold for his claim to be true, and which of those are likely to fail in a real case like ours.
