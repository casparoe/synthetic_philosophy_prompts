Lately I keep seeing "antimeme" used in two different ways, and I can't tell whether the difference is substantive. One usage just means an idea that doesn't spread well because it's boring, fiddly, counterintuitive, or has no hook: a low-fitness meme. The other usage means something stronger: an idea that somehow keeps itself from being remembered or discussed, like a secret that covers its own tracks. Please explain the conceptual difference between these two. In particular: is the strong version a distinct phenomenon or just the weak version plus selection effects and hindsight? And what would it even mean for an idea to suppress its own transmission, given that an idea isnt an agent and can't literally do anything to the people who carry it? Lay out what separates the two notions, and say whether you think the strong sense is coherent or basically a vivid metaphor.
