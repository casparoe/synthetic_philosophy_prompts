I want the idea to show up in what they do, not in what I say afterwards. Could you design it out for me: rules, materials, a timeline, group roles, a debrief that maps what happened onto the idea, what will predictably go wrong the first time I run it, and an honest note on which feature of the idea the activity can't reproduce so I'll have to supply it.
