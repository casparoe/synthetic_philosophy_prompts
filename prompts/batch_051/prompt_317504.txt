The document should open with a numbered agreed-claims section, then a disagreements table. Every agreed claim should be one both sides would actually sign, not diplomatic noise. Keep the distinction between falsifying what a forecast says and falsifying the claim that a stated method was followed in view. If part of the dispute is verbal rather than substantive, say which part and why. Please write the response in American English.
