In Japan I would follow the local no-tip custom without hesitation, and I think a visitor who insists on tipping there is doing something wrong, even if well intentioned. Jaywalking: crossing against the light on an empty street in a German city at three in the morning strikes me as fine; doing the same in Singapore when a child is watching and there is a crossing guard strikes me as wrong, and the tourist's ignorance of local practice doesn't make it fine.
