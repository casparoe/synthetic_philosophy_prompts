Egalitarians are stuck saying it can be better in at least one respect to make the rich poorer even if it helps nobody, because it increases equality; that's the leveling-down objection. He also said prioritarianism sees no value in reducing inequality unless it makes someone better off, which is why they can reject leveling down as pointless.
