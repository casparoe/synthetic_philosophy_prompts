So binding can't be the acoustics or the words as marks; it has to be something about meaning, intention, and what the hearer understood. That's the phenomenon I want the definition to capture.

So here is my request: give me a working definition of a binding promise—the conditions under which a promise actually obligates the speaker, as opposed to being a bare prediction, a plan, or social theater. Don't give me a dictionary gloss of the word "promise"; give me the normative test.
