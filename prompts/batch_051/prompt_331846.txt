Claim B: The irreducibility of the causal hierarchy. Pearl also organizes causal knowledge into three rungs. Rung one concerns seeing, or association, and queries such as P(y | x). Rung three concerns imagining, or counterfactuals, such as what Y would have been for this very unit had X been set to some other value. Pearl repeatedly argues that the rungs are strictly ordered in expressive power: rung two cannot in general be reduced to rung one, and rung three cannot in general be reduced to rung two or rung one.
