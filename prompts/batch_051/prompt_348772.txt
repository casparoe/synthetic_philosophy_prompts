In my methodology class we each have to write a position statement on the theory-vs-intuition question — basically when a compelling theory should overrule a strong intuition and when it should be the other way around — and then defend it while the rest of the seminar pokes holes in it. I'd like to audit mine before I go in. Here is roughly the package I want to defend:

(1) I basically sign on to reflective equilibrium: you start from particular case judgments, build general principles around them, then adjust whichever side needs it until the whole thing hangs together.

(2) I don't think any intuition is in principle unrevisable. But in practice there are some I just wouldn't budge on, like — no theory, however elegant, is going to convince me that torturing an infant for fun is permissible.

(3) The experimental philosophy results still look to me like they show something serious. People's verdicts flip with wording and order, and in the side-effect studies subjects are way more likely to call a bad side effect "intentional" than a good one, which makes it look like a moral reaction is doing the work in what's supposed to be a conceptual judgment. So intuitions as a class seem pretty unreliable.

(4) At the same time I can't see how you could do philosophy — or even how you could run and interpret an x-phi study — without leaning on intuitions somewhere, like intuitions about wether a study was well designed or whether a conclusion actually follows.

(5) When a well-confirmed theory conflicts with a strong intuition — like special relativity saying simultaneity is frame-relative even though it feels like there is one shared "now" — I'm inclined to say the theory wins. But I can't state a general rule for when the intuition should win instead; I want to take conflicts case by case.

(6) I also think "intuition" is too coarse a category. We should at least split it into immediate perceptual seemings, trained expert judgment, and culturally inculcated verdicts, and I'd only give the first two real evidential weight.

So: does this hang together? I have a feeling some of these are pulling against each other but I can't localize it. Please go through them (pairwise, mostly) and tell me which pairs are actually in tension and which only look like they are, rank the real ones by severity, and say what the cheapest repair is and what it would cost me. If one single revision would clean up the most, tell me which.
