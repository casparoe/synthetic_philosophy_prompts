A friend of mine works in behavioral ecology, and over the years she has given me a fairly stable set of verdicts about which displays count as honest signals and what keeps them honest. She says the peacock's train is an honest signal of condition, and that what makes it reliable is that it is costly to grow and drag around. She says a Thomson's gazelle stotting in front of a predator is an honest signal of vigor, and the wastefulness is exactly the point. She says the same thing about a hedge fund manager buying a superyacht: an honest signal of wealth, kept honest by how expensive it is.

But she is not a simple cost enthusiast. She says a red deer stag's antlers are not an honest signal at all; they are weapons, and whatever they reveal about the stag's strength is incidental. She also says a large dog's low-pitched growl is not a signal at all; it is just a byproduct of having a large body, however informative it may be.

I have not raised the following three cases with her. First, a male bullfrog's advertisement call in which the pitch is fixed by body size, so small males anatomically cannot produce the low calls that large males produce, and there is no evidence that a lower-pitched call is more energetically expensive than a higher-pitched one of the same length. Second, the vivid warning coloration of a toxic frog, where the bright pigment is metabolically tied to the toxin, so a nontoxic frog could not produce it. Third, a soft contact call that mated birds give each other while foraging, which is cheap and seems honest only because signaler and receiver currently want the same thing.

For each of the three, predict her verdict, with a rough sense of confidence, and explain the rule or rules she seems to be running. Then give at least one other rule that fits all of her earlier verdicts just as well but would answer some of these new cases differently, say which rule you think she most likely holds and why, and identify which of the three new cases would best discriminate among the candidate rules. Finally, suggest an additional case of your own devising that would settle whatever remains unresolved. I am less interested in whether the rules are right than in whether her track record can be made to hang together, and if it cannot, say exactly where the strain shows up.
