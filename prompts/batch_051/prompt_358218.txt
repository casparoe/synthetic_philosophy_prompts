A colleague recently introduced me to a pattern that Daniel Dennett associates with Darwin and Turing. As I understand it: before Darwin, it seemed obvious that complex functional design required an intelligent designer, and before Turing, it seemed obvious that intelligent-seeming behavior required an intelligent understander. Darwin's and Turing's strange inversions of reasoning showed otherwise — blind variation plus selection can produce the appearance of design, and a mechanical rule-follower can produce the appearance of thought, all without the system comprehending what it is doing. I gather the broader label for this is competence without comprehension.

Please treat the trained LLM and the training algorithm as separate illustrations.
