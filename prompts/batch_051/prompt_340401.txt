Push the deadpan until the reader feels the standard objections to particulate Darwinian memetics: what exactly is the unit, is a remix a variant or a new infection, and does the fitness metaphor just come down to 'whatever spreads'? Around 650 words, with at least one numbered section or table. It should be funny to someone who knows the memetics/cultural-evolution literature and still land with someone who only knows internet culture. American English please. Before drafting, brainstorm which failures of the literal-pathogen picture are worth skewering, then write the whole thing out.
