Suppose a group faces a possible irreversible action that would bind them all, and any member may initiate it unilaterally. Each member will initiate it if their own somewhat noisy assessment says the action is worth taking. Even when the group's pooled judgment is that the action would be a serious mistake, a sufficiently large group with noisy independent judgments makes it almost inevitable that some member with an outlying favorable assessment will set the action in motion—a pattern sometimes called the unilateralist's curse. Please work through the most important implications of this pattern for a consortium of AI labs in which any individual researcher has the technical capacity to publicly release a large model's weights. Organize the implications around decision procedures, thresholds for unilateral release, and how far the labs can rely on the individual restraint of their researchers. Since ranking implications is itself a contestable judgment, also say which counterargument or altered assumption would most likely change your ranking, and where your reasoning is most liable to fail.
