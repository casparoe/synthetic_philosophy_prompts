Write an Onion-style news article, headline plus around 500 words, about a univeristy's new VP for communications who is a deflationist about truth and has banned the word "true" from all official communications on the grounds that it contributes no property over and above the sentence it attaches to. The humor should come from the actual deflationary machinery, not vague freshman-seminar jokes: the T-schema, disquotation, the fact that the predicate is exactly what you need when you want to generalize (the campus-wide email promising "every claim in this report is true" now has to unwind into a nine-hundred-line conjunction), and the spectacle of a communications office that, when it wants to deny the dean's claim, will no longer say "that's false" and just has to flatly assert the negation. Include at least one quote from an invented, aggrieved correspondence-theorist faculty member who keeps say that truth is a real relation to the world. It should be deadpan, legible to someone who has never heard of deflationism, and fair enough that an actual deflationist recognizes the view and laughs rather than winces.
