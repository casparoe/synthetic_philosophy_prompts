The topic is Buridan's ass and the problem of choice without preference: whether an agent confronted with two exactly symmetrical options, with no more reason to choose one than the other, can nevertheless choose, and what such a choice reveals about will, rationality, and the difference between acting arbitrarily and acting without reason.
