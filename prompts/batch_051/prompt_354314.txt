I want to pressure-test a view I've come to hold about deepfakes and what is sometimes called the epistemic backstop. By "epistemic backstop" I mean the surrounding conditions that let us treat a photograph or video as evidence even though individual images have been manipulable since photography began: chains of custody, institutional archives, multiple independent recordings of the same event, corroborating physical traces, metadata and sensor forensics, and the practical difficulty of fabricating an entire surrounding context rather than a single file.

The view is this: cheap, high-quality deepfakes do not eliminate the epistemic backstop; they redistribute it. Institutions, legal processes, and communities with layered verification practices will retain largely workable ways of deciding whether audiovisual evidence is genuine, because a convincing deepfake still has to enter a documentary and social context, and competent institutions can check that context. Ordinary individual receivers, journalists on deadline, and low-resource settings lose much more of the backstop at once, because they rely on the surface features of a recording and on a few sources they cannot independently verify. So the distinctive damage is not that no one can tell real from fake, but that reliable access to audiovisual evidence becomes sharply unequal.

I want you to say what would count against this view, not merely what complicates it. What would the world have to look like for the corrosion to be roughly uniform rather than asymmetric, or for the institutional backstop itself to fail, or for "the backstop" to be the wrong frame altogether — for example, if the main harm turns out to be not false recordings being believed but genuine recordings being discredited by the mere possibility of deepfakes? Sort the considerations from those that should make me abandon the view, through those that should force a serious modification, down to those that are only awkward. Distinguish what critics would say from what a defender of the view would themselves have to concede, and give an honest verdict on whether the position, as I have stated it, has been insulated into unfalsifiability, and whether that is a defect here. I am asking for a committed response: take a definite stand on where the view is genuinely exposed and argue for it, rather than laying out options and leaving me to choose.
