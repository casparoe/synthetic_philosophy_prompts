Write the joint statement that a young-earth creationist who holds that no animal died before Adam's fall and an evolutionary creationist who accepts that predation and animal suffering long predated human beings could both sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration. They are not trying to convert each other; the deliverable is a document that states, in wording each side would accept for its own view, what they jointly hold and where exactly they split. Use a numbered agreed-claims section first (be concrete: what "good creation" must include, what the fossil record does and doesn't show, which questions scripture can settle), then a disagreements table with each camp's position stated in its own preferred terms. Then give the three cruxes on which settling the matter would actually move at least one side, and for each crux write down the evidence or argument both agree would bear on it, together with each side's prediction in advance. Dont make them sign on to more than each could honestly affirm, and say whether any part of their disagreement looks merely verbal.
