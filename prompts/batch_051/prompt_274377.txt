Please describe the most important implications of dialetheism and paraconsistent logic for how I should handle contradictions that show up inside my own web of beliefs. Assume that I already know the essentials: classical logic validates the principle of explosion, so a single inconsistency would let me derive any proposition at all and trivialize my reasoning; paraconsistent logics are built to block that; and dialetheists such as Graham Priest go further and hold that some contradictions are not merely tolerable but true, with the liar sentence as the standard candidate. My situation is this: I have noticed that two of my strongly held, high-prior beliefs entail each other's negation, and I cannot yet identify which one should go. How should I respond? Please give me the most important implications of each option for my downstream decisions and for how I communicate my beliefs to others, ordered by importance, and end with a concrete recommended policy.
