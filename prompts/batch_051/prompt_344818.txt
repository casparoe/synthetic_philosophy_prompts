"Lyotard's whole thing is that the postmodern condition gets defined by skepticism toward metanarratives, and a metanarrative is just any grand story a society tells about itself — science is progressing, history is moving toward freedom, that sort of thing. Science especially is just one narrative among others, with no better claim to truth than myth, because its authority depended on the very grand narratives it now pretends to have escaped.
