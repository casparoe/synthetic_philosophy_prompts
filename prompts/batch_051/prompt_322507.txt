1. A live orchestra playing a symphony in a concert hall.
2. The printed score the conductor used for that performance.
3. A studio recording of the same symphony, assembled from dozens of takes.
4. A cheap MIDI file playing back the first movement on a phone speaker.
5. A jazz trio improvising on the chord changes of a standard.
6. A cover band playing a pop song with the same lyrics and melody but a different tempo, genre, and arrangement.
7. A DJ playing the original recording in a club.
8. A remix built from the separated stems of an existing recording.
9. A song that exists only as a studio recording and has never been played live.
10. A folk tune sung from memory by someone who learned it orally.

I suspect the word is doing two jobs at once: one picks out a live human act in real time, the other picks out any audible instance of a repeatable work, and a recording falls between the two.
