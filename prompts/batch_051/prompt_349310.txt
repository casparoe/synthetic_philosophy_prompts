I've seen Sen's liberal paradox invoked as a formal demonstration that a society cannot consistently respect both individual liberty and the Pareto principle. If that is what the theorem really shows, then designing any social choice mechanism would seem to require a principled decision about which of the two ideals to abandon. Which one should it be, and is there a defensible, non-question-begging way to justify sacrificing it — or is the priority of liberty over efficiency, or efficiency over liberty, ultimately just an arational starting point?
