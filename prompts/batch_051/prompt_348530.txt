- A student asked why the derivative of x^2 is 2x. Maya derived it from the limit definition, expanding (x+h)^2, subtracting, dividing by h, cancelling, the whole thing. She said the power rule gets used so often that seeing where it comes from is worth the pain.
- A student asked why the quadratic formula works. Maya made them sit through completing the square on the general ax^2 + bx + c = 0, step by step, even though the kid just wanted the formula.
- A student asked why you can't divide by zero. Maya did the tight contradiction: if 1/0 equaled some number x, then x times 0 would have to be 1, but anything times 0 is 0, so contradiction. She refused to say it just is.
