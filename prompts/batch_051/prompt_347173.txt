I run an upper-level undergraduate seminar on early modern philosophy, with fourteen students and a ninety-minute session. Design a classroom activity that makes the students feel the force of Montaigne's use of the outsider's gaze in Book I, Chapter 31 of the Essays, 'Of Cannibals'. The activity should be built around a particular passage, which I paraphrase here so that the design does not depend on a single translation: Montaigne reports that three people from Brazil, brought to Rouen, were asked what they found most admirable or astonishing, and among other things they noted two features of French society. They found it strange that so many grown, armed, bearded men should obey a child king, and they found it strange that there were men gorged with every kind of good thing while their destitute 'halves' begged at their gates, wasted with hunger and poverty; they supposedly found it astonishing that the wretched did not seize the rich by the throat or set fire to their houses. The activity should force participants first to occupy the position of the visitors, and then to justify the institutions seen through their eyes, without announcing moral relativism in advance.

Include in your design:

(1) roles or groups and the materials each group needs;  
(2) a precise timeline for the ninety minutes;  
(3) a short briefing for the visitors that gives them enough of an alternative evaluative standpoint to produce their own judgements, rather than simply repeating Montaigne's two examples;  
(4) a structured debrief that maps what happened to the passage and to the broader idea that 'barbarism' names customs different from our own;  
(5) an honest note on which feature of Montaigne's position the activity cannot reproduce and must be supplied by the facilitator; and  
(6) a note on what will predictably go wrong the first time it is run, and how to handle it.

Use British English. End with a paragraph stating what would change your mind about the design or about your interpretation of the passage, and where your reasoning is most likely to fail.
