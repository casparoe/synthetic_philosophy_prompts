Then any evidence against p is evidence for something that is false, given your knowledge, so in an objective sense it's misleading. But it seems like a recipe for bullheadedness to say you can just ignore countervidence because you know p. Write an essay on whether the paradox shows that knowing something can make it rational to stop being open-minded about it. Weigh the main arguments on both sides, but don't end on a non-committal note—defend a verdict, maybe something like knowledge permits ignoring some counterevidence but not all.
