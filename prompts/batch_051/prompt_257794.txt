My philosophy of religion seminar got derailed this week by the slogan "prayer changes things." A student used it to shut down the standard worry that petitionary prayer makes no sense if God is omniscient and immutable—if He already knows what you need and has already decided what He'll do, what is the asking for? I have a vague sense the slogan covers a bunch of very different claims, some defensible and some not. Could you lay out the distinct precise theses it might amount to—say, that God changes His mind in response to requests, that prayer is a means God has eternally chosen to accomplish His purposes, that prayer changes the pray-er rather than the situation—and say for each whether it's compatible with an immutable, omniscient God and whether it actually dissolves the worry. Then tell me which of those readings the student needs for their move to work.
