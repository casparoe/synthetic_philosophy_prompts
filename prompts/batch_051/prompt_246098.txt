The whole setup treats behavior as evidence about what someone actually wants, sometimes better evidence than what they say out loud.

Sometimes it sounds stronger, like your behavior IS your preference and the words are basically noise. Sometimes it sounds almost behaviorist, like your private mental states don't even matter. And sometimes people in AI spaces use it to mean verbal reports are cheap talk, and demonstrated behavior is the real training data.

What I want is the menu of distinct precise claims hiding in "actions speak louder than words." For each one, tell me wether it's trivially true, defensible but controversial, or basically false. I also find it weird that the same field uses reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), where you literally take people's words and ratings as the reward signal, so the field itself seems to flip between "just watch what they do" and "actually just ask them."
