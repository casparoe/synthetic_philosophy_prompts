Ever since my grandfather's memorial I can't get this out of my head. A family friend gave a short speech and said something like "he lived to see history vindicate him," and everyone nodded. Later somebody told my grandmother that his being driven out of his home was "part of God's plan," and she was furious. But I keep thinking: aren't those the same picture at different distances? We love the idea that the arc of history bends toward justice, that things will work out, that present evils will turn out to have played a role in something better. Yet we also think it's obscene to tell a particular victim that their suffering was necessary or for the best. If the grand claim is true, isn't it true of somebody's actual suffering somewhere? And if the local version is revolting, what's left of the grand one?
