Harsanyi's aggregation theorem, as it is usually summarized, says that if each individual's preferences and the social preferences all satisfy the expected-utility axioms, and society is indifferent between two options whenever every individual is indifferent between them, then the social utility function must be a weighted sum of individual utility functions; add an impartiality condition and the weights become equal.
