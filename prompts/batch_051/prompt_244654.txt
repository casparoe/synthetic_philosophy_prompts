So my friend and I were having this long argument about forecasting platforms, and he came up with a scheme I cant stop turning over. Imagine a site that works like this. Every forecaster starts the year with 1000 points. For each binary question, they have to submit a probability p between 1% and 99%, rounded to the nearest whole percent. When the question resolves, their entire bankroll is multiplied by 2p if the event happened and by 2(1-p) if it didn't. So a 90% forecast that hits multiplies your stack by 1.8, and if it misses it multiplies it by 0.2; a 50% forecast leaves your stack unchanged either way. There is no stake-size decision and no cashing out. Each forecaster must submit a forecast on every question the platform opens within 48 hours, and forecasts of exactly 50% don't count toward that requirement. If your bankroll ever drops below 1 point, you're out for the rest of the year. At the end, a fixed prize pool is split in proportion to final bankrolls.
