I'm running a small philosophy reading group on dispositions, specifically the fink/mask problem for the simple conditional analysis. Before anyone has done the reading I want to hand out a "which theory of dispositions do you actually hold?" quiz, so the whole point is to sort people by what they say about cases, not by which label already sounds good to them. That means the questions should never use the words fink, mask, ceteris paribus, dispositional essentialism, powers, or categoricalism; those can show up in the scoring key but not in the questions themselves. Use at least these cases somewhere: a glass that would normally break when struck, but a device makes it non-fragile an instant before the blow (the classic fink); a fragile glass packed in foam so it wouldn't break even if struck (a mask); a poison that always comes bundled with an antidote; and a particle whose usual manifestation is blocked in every actual circumstance, forever. I keep going back and forth on the poison/antidote case myself, so I'm genuinely curious how it sorts people. Eight to ten questions, each with two or three answer options, scored onto at least these positions: the simple conditional analysis, a normal-conditions/ceteris paribus view, a fink-friendly view that analyzes dispositions via an intrinsic causal basis plus a counterfactual, a real-powers/dispositional essentialism view, and a regularity-only view. Include categoricalism or Humean supervenience only if the questions actually separate it from the others; if they don't, say so.

Then give me the questions, the answer options, and an explicit scoring key mapping answer patterns to positions. Flag any pair of positions your questions can't really seperate, and flag empty cells (combinations of answers that almost nobody would produce) with a sentence on whether the cell looks incoherent or just unattractive. At the end, tell me how confident you are that the scoring key sorts people into the view they'd actually defend on reflection, and which single question you're least sure is doing real diagnostic work.
