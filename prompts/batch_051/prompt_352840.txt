I finally read a popular explainer, and the upshot it gives is this: "Gibbard and Satterthwaite proved that any method that turns ranked ballots into a single winner, with three or more candidates, must either be manipulable—meaning that in some election some voter does better by submitting a ranking other than her true one—or be a dictatorship—meaning there is one fixed voter whose top-ranked candidate wins no matter how everyone else votes. So strategic voting is not a bug of particular systems like plurality or Borda: every conceivable ranked system is equally vulnerable to it. The only clean way out is to restrict elections to two candidates, since two-candidate majority rule cannot be gamed."
