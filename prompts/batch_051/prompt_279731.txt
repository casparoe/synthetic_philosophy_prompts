Also identify where the distinction collapses or misleads — cases in which the two kinds come apart only on paper, or in which insisting on the distinction obscures more than it reveals.

Please answer first in formal prose of about 700 words. Then append a structured summary as a single JSON object with this schema: an implications array, where each element has an implication field giving a one-sentence statement; a domain field taking one of assessment, cultivation, institutional_design, or prediction; a divergence field explaining what Resistant versus Quiet Incorruptibility would each imply in that domain; and a caveat field naming the strongest limit on that implication. Add a where_the_distinction_collapses array of strings, and a bottom_line field with a two-sentence gloss of the overall upshot.
