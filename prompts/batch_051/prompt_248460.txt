I want an in-class activity that makes the students actually feel hindsight bias and the narrative fallacy, not just hear about them. No lecture up front—don't name the phenomena until the debrief.

Design the activity as a runnable game. Then they compare with a group that got the opposite outcome and discover both stories feel plausible. The case should be fictional or genuinely obscure so nobody has priors—don't use a famous event with the outcome secretly flipped.
