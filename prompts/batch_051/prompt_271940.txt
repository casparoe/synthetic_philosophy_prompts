I'm a novelist, still in the research phase of a book where a minor charachter is a Quaternary geologist, and I keep coming back to the Anthropocene because its whole status seems strange in a way that feels useful for fiction. Here's what I think I know: the International Commission on Stratigraphy has a formal procedure for adding an epoch to the geological time scale. It doesn't vote on a concept, exactly; it votes on a boundary in a particular body of rock — a GSSP, the golden spike mechanism — like the ones already used to mark off other stages and periods. The Anthropocene Working Group spent over a decade on this and eventually proposed a mid-20th-century boundary, with Crawford Lake as the candidate spike, because the sediments there record plutonium fallout from mid-century nuclear tests, among other markers like fly ash and plastics. Then the parent bodies voted the proposal down, so formally the Holocene just keeps going. Meanwhile ecologists, climate modelers, journalists, and the people who make Great Acceleration graphs talk about the Anthropocene as though it simply is the period we are living in, no committee needed.

What I'd like to understand, for the novel more than anything, is what picture of historical periods the formalization machinery takes for granted. Concretely: what does the practice of requiring a golden spike in actual rock presuppose about what it is for an epoch to exist and to have a start? Does it presuppose that epochs are out there in the strata waiting to be discovered, or that they are conventions enacted by the right body, or some mix of the two? What would it even mean for the Anthropocene to be real independently of the committee while still needing a committee to count? And where is the practice philosophically underdetermined — that is, where would more than one theory of historical periods license exactly the same golden-spike ritual? If someone claimed that geological epochs are just usefull fictions, would the whole procedure be a mistake, an empty formality, or perfectly coherent for different reasons? I'm after a reconstruction of the implicit theory, the places the practice stays silent, and what would have to change about the practice under a rival theory.
