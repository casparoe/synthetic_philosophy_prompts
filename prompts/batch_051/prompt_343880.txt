The jokes shouldn't be random; they should exaggerate real internal tensions from the tradition—renunciation becoming a status game and a form of costly signaling, counted abstinences turning into a weird new kind of property, and paying for simplicity gear quietly reproducing the consumer logic it claims to escape. A reader who knows the frugality/ascetic literature should grin, but it also has to work for someone who's never read a page of it. As an expert on anti-consumerist philosophy and a friendly satirist, first brainstorm the five tensions you most want to land, then render each as a help-center entry. Format the whole thing as exactly five numbered FAQ entries; each entry covers a question and it's answer, and each entry should be no more than two sentences total, with no introduction or conclusion, and no quotations from anyone.
