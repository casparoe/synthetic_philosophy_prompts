A community of 120 people is organized as follows. Each month, every member is paired with a randomly chosen other member for a single prisoner's-dilemma interaction: each chooses to cooperate or to defect, with payoffs of 3 to each for mutual cooperation, 1 to each for mutual defection, 5 to a lone defector, and 0 to a lone cooperator. Repeat encounters between the same two members are possible but rare and unpredictable; the community itself persists indefinitely. All interaction outcomes are publicly observed and recorded without error.

The community institutes a public ledger that displays two numbers for each member: a demerit count and a clemency balance, both starting at zero. The update rule is this. If exactly one member of a pair defects, the defector gains one demerit and the cooperator gains one clemency point. If both defect, each gains one demerit and no clemency is awarded. If both cooperate, nothing changes. A member may spend one clemency point at any time to erase one demerit from their own record. Clemency points expire after one year, and they cannot be transferred or sold. Members may condition their choices on anything in the ledger.

Treat this as a proposed mechanism and analyze how it would actually behave. Work through the incentives it creates in a single month's pairing, over the course of a year, and over many years as members adapt and learn from results. Who accumulates clemency, who spends it, and which strategies become viable or profitable—for example, engineering one's own exploitation to bank a point, defecting only while holding surplus clemency, refusing to cooperate with high-demerit partners, and any others you can identify. Pay attention to whether the mechanism effectively gives one-off encounters something like the shadow of the future, and how it interacts with the usual logic of direct and indirect reciprocity. Say where the scheme is first gamed or breaks down, and explain whether the community could patch that specific failure without reintroducing the problem the mechanism was meant to solve.
