Argue that extending the rationalist explanation of war to an AI development race is most plausible if the race is treated primarily as a commitment problem rather than as a problem of private information or issue indivisibility. Specifically, defend the claim that the familiar mechanisms—private information with incentives to misrepresent, commitment problems, and issue indivisibilities—do not transfer equally: the first and third are largely idle, because progress is only partially concealable and the prize is not a fixed object to be divided, while the second does the real explanatory work. Your argument should also address the natural reply that genuine uncertainty about a rival's progress is precisely what keeps firms racing, so private information cannot be dispensable.
