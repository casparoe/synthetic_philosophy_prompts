The setup: lamp on at t=0, off at t=1/2, on at t=3/4, off at t=7/8, and so on; the question is what state it's in at t=1. Thomson says it can't be on, because every time it was switched on it was switched off at some later time before t=1, and it can't be off, because every time it was switched off it was switched on at some later time before t=1 — so the infinite task is contradictory and impossible. Benacerraf's comeback, as I understand it, is that this is too fast: the story just doesn't determine the state at t=1, and an incomplete description isn't a contradiction.
