Take the standard grandfather paradox—an assassin travels into the past and kills their own grandfather before the relevant parent is conceived—and place it not in a generic wormhole time-travel scenario but inside the rotating universe that Gödel introduced as an exact solution to Einstein's equations. Assume the universe is exactly that solution: a homogeneous rotating dust with a negative cosmological constant tuned to the dust density. In this spacetime there are closed timelike curves, but there are no closed timelike geodesics: a particle in free fall cannot return to its own past. Any closed timelike trajectory must be accelerated throughout. A would-be traveler therefore needs a rocket, and the rocket's fuel expenditure must be integrated along the entire loop. The spacetime is also not globally hyperbolic: there is no global time function, and the dust has nonzero vorticity.

Work through how the standard analysis of the grandfather paradox changes under these conditions. In particular:

- Does the fact that time travel requires non-geodesic acceleration affect the usual consistency constraints, or the usual resolutions such as Novikov self-consistency or branching timelines? If the traveler is not freely falling, does that alter the physical possibility claim?
- Gödel himself took the existence of such closed timelike curves to undermine the objectivity of time. If no freely falling observer can actually traverse them, does that argument survive, or does it now rest on a special status for accelerated observers? Is the relevant notion of physically possible one that includes any rocket trajectory, or only geodesic motion?
- Compare the case with the standard wormhole-based grandfather paradox. What, if anything, does the Gödel setting change about the structure of the paradox itself—for example, the lack of a global time, the need for acceleration, or the role of rotation in fixing a preferred frame?

I want the exact structure, not a general survey: show the consistency reasoning step by step, and say which standard resolution of the grandfather paradox survives in this setting and which breaks.
