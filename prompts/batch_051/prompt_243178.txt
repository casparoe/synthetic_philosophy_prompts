Here's a proposal I've seen going around for video platforms, and I want to understand how it would actually behave, not whether it's a good idea. The rule: any upload that reproduces a group's propaganda in order to critique it must keep a persistent, uncropped banner over the original footage stating who produced it, when, and that it's shown for criticism; if the banner is missing or obscured, or if the propaganda is intercut with commentary so a viewer can't tell which parts are the original, the video gets age-restricted and demonitized, and repeat offenders get deprioritized in recommendations. Treat this as a mechanism, not a moral position. What does it incentivize among creators, among the propagandists themselves, and among platform moderators? How would people game it, what second-order effects show up, and which failure modes are fixable without just scrapping the rule?
