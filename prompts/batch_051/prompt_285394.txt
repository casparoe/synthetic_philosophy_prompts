My friend's argument, as I wrote it down right after:

(1) Two perfectly rational players in exactly symmetric situations with the same information will choose the same action.
(2) So if you cooperate, your counterpart will cooperate too; if you defect, your counterpart will defect too.
(3) Cooperate-cooperate is better for you than defect-defect.
(4) You should choose the action whose outcome is better for you, other things equal.
(5) Therefore you should cooperate.

My hunch is that (2) is the culprit because it treats your choice as if it made the other player's choice happen, or at least as evidence about it, but I couldn't make that stick under questioning.
