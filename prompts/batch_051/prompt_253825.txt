I'm in a phil of science seminar this term, and my professor has a running thing where she'll quickly rule on whether some category carves nature at the joints or is just a label that's convenient for us. I've been keeping a list of her calls from my lecture notes because she seems weirdly consistant, and I'm trying to reverse-engineer the actual rule. Going from memory: jade is not a natural kind, since it lumps jadeite and nephrite, two different minerals that just look similar. Trees aren't a natural kind either—oak, pine and palm look like one category but it's a growth form, not a single evolutionary unit. Electrons are the clearest natural kind she'll admit: every electron has the same charge, mass and spin, end of story. On species she gets careful: grey wolves aren't a natural kind in the old essentialist sense, because there's no hidden microstructure all and only wolves share, but they're a real lineage with a fairly stable cluster of traits that hang together for a causal reason, so she'll call them a real biological unit anyway. On human races she's blunt: not natural kinds, the variation is gradual and the folk categories don't track a single causal story. And on planet she said the label is partly a matter of convention—think Pluto—but the clearing-the-neighborhood criterion picks out something dynamically real, so it's conventional but not arbitrary.

Now a friend from the psych department keeps telling me extraversion is a natural kind. His case: it shows up across cultures, it's partly heritable, it predicts a bunch of life outcomes, and there are neurobiological correlates. The complication is that there's no agreed microstructural essence, the underlying scores form a continuum, and where you draw the line between extraverted and introverted looks like a measuring choice. The professor has never touched personality stuff. If she's being consistent with her track record, what would she most likely say about extraversion? Which of her earlier calls is the closest analog (species? planet? tree? race? jade? electron?), and what rule does each possible verdict commit her to? Give me your best-guess prediction of her call, and then say what would change your mind: which extra fact about extraversion, or about her past calls, would flip you to the opposite verdict, and where is the reasoning most likely to break?
