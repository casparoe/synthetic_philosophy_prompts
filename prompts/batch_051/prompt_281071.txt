I'm not sure what claim is doing the work there. But I suspect there are several readings in between: an empirical regularity about how eponyms get assigned, a methodological warning not to infer history from a name, a sociological claim that "discovery" gets retroactively attributed rather than found, maybe a deflationary claim that "the real discoverer" is not even a well-defined notion.
