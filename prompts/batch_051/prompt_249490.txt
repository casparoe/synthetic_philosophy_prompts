One side says the Wilsonian RG picture basically settles it: decoupling and universality mean the low-energy physics is robust against details of the UV — irrelvant operators get suppressed by powers of energy over the cutoff, different microscopic completions land in the same universality class, and you can do enormously successful low-energy physics (the Fermi theory of weak interactions, low-energy QCD) without solving or even knowing the UV completion. They call that emergence, or at least real autonomy for the higher-level theory. The other side says this is exactly backwards: the EFT setup is top-down all the way. On their view the higher-level theory is a convenient summary of the lower-level theory plus our ignorance, not an autonomous level of nature. The annoying thing is that both sides agree on every equation, every theorem, all of the RG calculations, and they still talk past each other.
