Ground the procedure in what the philosophy of other minds, solipsism, and the main-character intuition actually says about what it is to treat another person as a full subject rather than as scenery. Give me an ordered set of tests or a checklist I can run on any scene or character, and show it working on two or three cases from a hypothetical novel, including one where the procedure tells me a character I'm fond of is still an NPC. Be explicit about where the procedure can't settle the question and where a mechanical fix would be worse than leaving the character thin. I have no formal philosophy training and need something I can actually use during a week-long revision pass, not an essay on solipsism.
