I want the paper to engage the debate over whether epistemic dependence on AI systems—for instance, using them to interpret evidence, summarize research, diagnose patients, or generate code—erodes human understanding rather than merely redistributing cognitive labor. My half-formed thought is that this debate conflates two different losses. The first is the loss of an individual's ability to reconstruct or explain why a conclusion is correct. I suspect the second loss is the more consequential one, and I also suspect it does not reduce to the first: a group could lack any member who understands a result yet still maintain effective safeguards, and an individual could understand a result thoroughly while working inside an institution that has stopped checking whether the systems on which it depends are trustworthy. Please turn this into a proposal.
