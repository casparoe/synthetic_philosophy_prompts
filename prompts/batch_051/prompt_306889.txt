I've got the argument itself down; what I need is the Q&A prep. Give me the objections in roughly the order I'll hear them, phrased the way each camp would actually phrase them, with a note on which ones do real damage, what I should concede up front, where my own argument is softest, and the one question I should be praying nobody asks.
