I have no academic training, but I have spent a long time thinking on my own about natural selection and the work the concept of fitness is meant to do. The difficulty that keeps returning is this: if fitness is simply a record of which organisms did leave more offspring, then natural selection looks circular. We would be explaining differences in reproductive success by citing differences in reproductive success, and the claim that the fitter types prevail would say nothing more than that the types that prevail prevail. As I understand it, the propensity interpretation is designed to break this circle by holding that fitness is a disposition or probabilistic tendency to survive and reproduce, not the realised tally of offspring.

What I would like is a clear specification of the list of requirements an adequate interpretation of fitness must meet if it is to do the explanatory work evolutionary biology asks of it. For each requirement, give the case or consideration that forces it, and mark whether it is non-negotiable or merely the preference of a particular camp. Give particular attention to three points. First, whether the propensity interpretation genuinely satisfies the requirement that fitness be independent of actual reproductive success, or whether it only postpones the difficulty, since a propensity is standardly assessed from the very outcomes it is supposed to explain. Second, whether fitness belongs primarily to individual organisms or to types and populations, given that the models of population genetics assign fitness as a parameter to genotypes within populations; if those two levels are in tension, say plainly whether any interpretation can serve both. Third, whether fitness can legitimately be compressed into a single number at all, or whether it is really a bundle of distinct components — viability, fecundity, timing of reproduction, mating success — that pull apart and cannot be merged without distortion. If any subset of the requirements turns out to be jointly unsatisfiable, that is the part I most want stated.
