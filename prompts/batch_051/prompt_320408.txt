(2) In special relativity, for any two spacelike-separated events, there is an inertial frame in which the two events are simultaneous.
(3) Let A be the event of my sitting here now, and let B be some arbitrary event in my future light cone—say, my sitting down to dinner tomorrow. By (2), since C and B are also spacelike separated, there is a frame in which C and B are simultaneous; by (1), since C exists now, B exists now as well.
(4) Nothing in this construction depended on which future event B was, so the same reasoning applies to every event in the future. Therefore every future event exists now; the distinction between past, present, and future is not fundamental.
