I'm sympathetic but I honestly can't tell what the sentence is supposed to amount to once you press on it. Is it the weak point that a critique needs it's object to be visible, the stronger empirical claim that exposure plus counterspeech reliably neutralizes the manipulation, or something even stronger like "more circulation is always net better because the audience will sort it out"? I want to go back to that thread with a careful menu of the distinct principles the slogan could be expressing, graded from trivial through plausible-but-contestable to likely false, and for each principle a note on whether it actually supports letting the re-upload stay up. I don't need a single "correct" reading; I need the different readings separated so I can see which one the commenters are silently relying on and which ones are just handwaving.
