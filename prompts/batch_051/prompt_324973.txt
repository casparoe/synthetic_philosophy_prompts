In a course I'm TAing, we've been going through this ladder of kinds of minds, and a student and I have been going back and forth about it ever since. The ladder, as it was presented to us, runs like this. Darwinian creatures are the bottom rung: their competences are mostly fixed by natural selection, with little or no useful individual learning. Skinnerian creatures add trial-and-error learning, trying moves out in the world and keeping whatever gets reinforced. Popperian creatures add an inner environment, so a candidate move can get rejected in the head without the creature having to actually perform it and eat the consequences. Gregorian creatures add one more trick: they import tools from the surrounding culture, mostly language and other representational devices, so they can borrow other people's hard-won discoveries wholesale instead of relearning everything themselves.

Now consider a current large language model wired up in the usual way: pretrained on a huge corpus, fine-tuned with reinforcement learning from human feedback, and told at inference time to think step by step before answering. The student wants to call it a Gregorian creature. Gradient descent shaping the weights has a selectionist flavor, which covers the Darwinian rung. RLHF is literally a kind of reinforcement, which covers the Skinnerian rung. Chain-of-thought is an inner environment where candidate continuations are accepted or rejected in the head before anything is output, which covers the Popperian rung. And the training corpus is basically the whole culture's toolkit of language, which covers the Gregorian rung. I want to say it is not a creature at all: there is no self that stands to lose anything when one of its hypotheses dies, and nothing about agency, stakes, or having a point of view appears anywhere on the ladder.

Where would you place the model on the ladder? Work through each rung, flag the places where the criteria underdetermine the answer, and tell me whether the ladder needs an extra criterion, something like agency or selfhood or having skin in the game, or whether the student's reading is exactly right and my objection is just smuggling in requirements the ladder never had.
