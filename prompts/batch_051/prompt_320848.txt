Give me the desiderata, with the case or consideration behind each: the cases it must get right, the distinctions it cannot collapse, and the constraints from ordinary moral psychology it cannot ignore. Mark each requirement as non-negotiable or contestable, identify any subset that cannot be jointly satisfied, and say which single requirement does the most to rule out tempting short answers such as mere excess of love, mere false belief about the object, or mere bad consequences.
