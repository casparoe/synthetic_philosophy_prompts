Proposal under consideration at my school: on all group projects, before beginning work, every member must sign a public statement reading, in part, "We commit ourselves as one body to this project and its outcome." After that, the group is graded only as a unit; no member can be given a separate grade or excused for any reason, and the only way a member can raise the issue of another member's free-riding is if the whole group unanimously files a "breach of joint commitment" complaint with the teacher. Students who refuse to sign must complete an equivalent assignment alone. Treat this as a mechanism to be analyzed rather than a position to be judged. Walk through how it would actually operate: what it would incentivize for strong, weak, and socially isolated students; how groups would form and who would end up signing with whom; how the unanimous-complaint rule would actually be used; where the scheme would first be gamed or break down; and which failure modes, if any, could be fixed without abandoning the idea that the group acts as one body.
