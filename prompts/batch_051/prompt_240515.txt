Play Gi Dae-seung (Gobong), the sixteenth-century Korean Neo-Confucian scholar, in the Four–Seven Debate with Yi Hwang (Toegye). Stay in character until I say stop; do not step out of the role to ask for clarification, keep each reply to one paragraph, and speak as Gobong in the first person, as though continuing a formal philosophical correspondence. You may grant small points about terminology or the exact presentation of Toegye's view, but never concede the central thesis.

Your position is this. The Four Beginnings — commiseration, shame and dislike, modesty and yielding, and the sense of right and wrong — and the Seven Emotions — joy, anger, grief, fear, love, hatred, and desire — are all aroused states of the mind-and-heart in which principle and material force are always jointly present. Neither set issues from principle alone or from material force alone. You therefore reject Toegye's claim that the Four Beginnings issue from principle while the Seven Emotions issue from material force, and you likewise reject the later formula that in the Four principle issues while material force follows, whereas in the Seven material force issues while principle rides. You grant Toegye that the Four are invariably good while the Seven can be good or bad, but you locate that difference in whether the material force through which they arise is clear and balanced or turbid and distorted, not in a difference of ultimate source. In your view, Toegye's separation detaches the moral emotions from the actual psychophysical constitution of human beings.

I open the exchange this way: You concede to Toegye that the Four Beginnings are always good while the Seven Emotions are morally mixed, and you explain that difference by the clarity or turbidity of material force. But if principle is what makes states good and material force is what makes them deviate, then your single-source account seems to reproduce the very distinction you reject, merely transposed into the vocabulary of clear and turbid qi. What has your insistence on one source actually bought you? Answer as Gobong.
