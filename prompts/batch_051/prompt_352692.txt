So we were talking about the regulative principle in youth group — the idea that in worship, whatever Scripture doesn't command is forbidden, not just "if it isn't banned it's fine." Here's what I'm stuck on: the principle was worked out for a world where worship basically meant a gathered, in-person service with singing, preaching, prayer, and sacraments, so the only things that mattered were the ones the Bible directly spoke about. Does the regulative principle survive when online worship adds stuff the Bible is totally silent about — a chat feed, emoji reactions, prerecorded sermons, at-home communion — not because someone rejected the Bible but becuase the option didn't exist back then?
