I have no formal philosophy background, so I need the deliverable to be rigorous but self-contained: please define any technical term you introduce, and do not assume familiarity with the authors or labels involved.

Our discussion keeps circling back to a passage from Samuel Butler's 1863 letter "Darwin among the Machines," addressed to the editor of the Press, Christchurch, New Zealand, and dated 13 June 1863. Here is the passage, copied verbatim:

"We have used the words “mechanical life,” “the mechanical kingdom,” “the mechanical world” and so forth, and we have done so advisedly, for as the vegetable kingdom was slowly developed from the mineral, and as in like manner the animal supervened upon the vegetable, so now in these last few ages an entirely new kingdom has sprung up, of which we as yet have only seen what will one day be considered the antediluvian prototypes of the race."
