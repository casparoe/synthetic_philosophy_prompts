Take the Gricean reference letter — the one where a professor writes a recommendation that says only that the student has excellent handwriting and is always punctual, and the hiring committee treats this as an implicated claim that the student is no good at philosophy. Now change one thing: the job the student is applying for is a university post as calligrapher and keeper of the attendance register, where exactly those two qualities are the central advertised qualifications. The letter is word for word the same, and the professor writes it in full knowlege of what the job is.
