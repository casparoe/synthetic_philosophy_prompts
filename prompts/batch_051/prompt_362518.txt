I'd like you to read the film through the philosophy of whole-brain emulation and personal identity for me. I want a reading that is faithful in both directions: it shouldn't reduce the film to a dressed-up thought experiment, and it shouldn't bend the personal-identity debate to fit the movie; it should also end by saying what the film adds to the debate that a bare teletransporter-style case doesn't.
