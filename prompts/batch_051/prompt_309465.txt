I tried and failed to explain why the interesting problem lies elsewhere, and the experience made me realize I do not have a stable sense of what has to be in place before the sorites paradox actually bites. I have now agreed to lead a one-session discussion for a reading group of non-specialist adults (mostly scientists and lawyers, plus a retired English teacher), and I want it to be about the sorites paradox itself, not a survey of theories of vagueness. Please design the teaching plan rather than the explanation itself.
