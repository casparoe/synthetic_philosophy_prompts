A grad student of mine is convinced that what I mean by '+' is just a dispositional fact about me—how I'd go on if you kept quizzing me. The old skeptical worry wasn't that people answer inconsistently; it's that any finite body of answers, past, present, or future, fits both addition and a bent function that matches addition on every case anyone has actually computed but diverges on some case nobody has hit yet (the classic quus idea: same outputs until the inputs cross a line you've never crossed, then it returns 5). She says a smart enough test battery—novel edge cases, priming, reaction times, what people self-correct toward—could test whether the content really is plus rather than quus. Build the strongest version of that test. Be explicit about what a clean result would show, and what it would leave unsettled no matter how it comes out.
