2. I also think people do what they most want at the moment of action. It's close to definitional for me: action reveals the desire that won, whatever the person says afterward.

3. The best model of my own choices is hyperbolic discounting. I'll pick the larger later reward when both are far off, then systematically flip to the smaller sooner one as it gets close, without learning anything new. I can watch myself do it.

4. If 3 is right, then Monday-me and Sunday-night-me are importantly two different agents with two different preference orderings, and neither one is objectively the "real" me.
