Our philosophy club has two seniors who have been arguing all semester about testimony: a reductionist who says believing what someone tells you is only justified if you have independent evidence that they and people generally are reliable, and an anti-reductionist who says testimony gives you default justification like perception does, unless you have a specific defeater. They’ve agreed to write an adversarial-collaboration statement and I got assigned to draft it. Please write the document for them. It should have: a numbered list of claims both can sign about what testimonial belief is and what they are not claiming; then a disagreements table where each row is a statement of the difference in wording each side would accept for their own view; then the cruxes. Include at least one case I made up: a new student tells me something weird but harmless about the school (like the basement has a plunge pool), I have no prior reason to trust or distrust them, and soon after three older kids say it’s false without giving evidence. Before I check myself, what should my level of justification be, and should I believe it? For that crux and any others you add, record in advance what each camp would predict and what concrete information or argument both sides would agree bears on it. Also flag if part of there fight is just verbal. Keep it specific enough that this doesn’t turn into “it’s a spectrum” unless that’s really forced.
