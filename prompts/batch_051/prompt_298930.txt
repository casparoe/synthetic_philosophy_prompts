I call the familiar kind of Dutch book a frustrating book. My proposal is that this shows only something weak: that the agent is locally exploitable — there exists a menu of trades the agent can be made to accept, and that menu is dominated by rejecting it.

Then the agent is not merely losing a fixed amount in every world; the loss schedule itself encodes a kind of accuracy score, and the agent is charged for every unit of distance from the truth.
