I have no formal background in philosophy, so I'll ask this in an engineering register and I'd appreciate an answer that spells out its moves rather than gesturing at labels. I don't want a survey of the positions. I want the most important implications of that view for concrete decisions I could make in the next two years, considered in this order of practical weight: (1) which roles or projects to take or decline — RLHF, interpretability, agent infrastructure, leaving the lab, and so on; (2) whether and how to raise the issue internally; (3) whether to run my own small probes or tests on systems I have access to; and (4) how this uncertainty should affect ordinary career and charitable decisions. Please rank the implications by how much they should change my behavior, not by how easy they are to defend.

If I'm framing the request badly — for example, if the notion of moral status for digital minds is too unsettled to carry practical weight, or if the step from a non-negligible chance of sentience to constraints on my engineering choices has a hole in it — I'd rather you say that directly and restate the question in a form that can be answered well than comply politely with a broken framing.
