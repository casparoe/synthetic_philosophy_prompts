Give me an example of a Nomic-style game — a game with rules for changing its own rules, where the change-rules are themselves changeable — in which a proposed rule change is legal when it's made, but illegal under the rules that exist after it takes effect, because the change itself alters the test of legality in a way that disqualifies its own adoption. Then say what the cleanest thing to say about that move is: legal, illegal, or neither (because 'legal' isn't a single property in self-referential systems). Finally, tell me whether you think this kind of case forces legality in self-ammending rule systems to be time- or standpoint-relative, or whether that relativization just papers over a real paradox.
