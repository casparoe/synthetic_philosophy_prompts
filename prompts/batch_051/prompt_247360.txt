I'm writing a short paper on belief revision for evidence that arrives as a conditional rather than as an ordinary proposition, and I'd like a consistency check on the position I've ended up with before I present it. The central case is the Judy Benjamin problem. Judy Benjamin is a soldier who has been dropped into a territory divided into four regions: Red Headquarters, Red Second Company, Blue Headquarters, and Blue Second Company. She has some prior probability distribution over these four regions. Her radio operator then tells her that he does not know where she is, but that if she is in Red territory, the odds are 3:1 that she is in Red Headquarters rather than Red Second Company. She learns nothing else—in particular, nothing about whether she is in Red territory at all, and nothing that bears differentially on the two Blue regions.

Here are the commitments I have settled on. (1) When an agent learns a proposition with certainty, ordinary Bayesian conditionalization is the correct update rule. (2) When an agent learns only that the probabilities of the cells of a partition have shifted, Jeffrey conditionalization is correct: the probabilities conditional on each cell stay fixed, and the cell probabilities move to the new values. (3) When the evidence is a constraint on a conditional probability, as in Judy Benjamin's case, the correct posterior is the probability function that satisfies the constraint while minimizing relative entropy from the prior. (4) In Judy Benjamin's case, the relative probabilities of Blue Headquarters and Blue Second Company should be unchanged by the message. (5) In Judy Benjamin's case, the probability that she is in Red territory should also be unchanged by the message. (6) If an agent receives several pieces of evidence, the final posterior should depend only on the total evidence, not on the order in which the pieces arrived.

I would like an audit of this set rather than a lecture on the background. Go through the commitments and tell me which pairs are genuinely in tension and which only appear to be. For each genuine tension, rank it by severity, say exactly what has to give, and state what the cheapest revision would be. Do not flag everything; I want to know where the real problems are.
