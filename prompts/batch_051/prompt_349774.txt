I've started self-censoring alot. I don't push back in meetings, I laugh at jokes that aren't funny, and I stopped applying to other jobs because leaving would need his reference. Who's right? I don't care about the label; I want to know what each position commits you to about what freedom is, what exact feature of my situation does the work separating them, and whether the self-censoring itself should count as evidence of unfreedom or just as a personal quirk.
