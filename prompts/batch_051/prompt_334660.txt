A company that secretly bought people's private diaries and sold what was inside would be treated as a monster. A company that sells a headset which records your brain signals and infers your mood, focus, even the word you're silently rehearsing is treated as a wellness brand. Is there a principled difference between these two things, or is the second just a privacy violation our intuitions haven't caught up with? If you think there is a real difference, what does the work—consent, accuracy, interpretation, ownership of the signal—and does it survive as the decoding gets good enough to reconstruct a sentence you only imagined saying?
