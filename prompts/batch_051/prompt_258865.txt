Here's my opening move. A lottery draw is happening tonight, and your theory seems to say I can know my ticket will lose: if it were going to win, I wouldn't believe it would lose. But the same theory says I cannot know the sun will rise tomorrow, because if the sun weren't going to rise I would still have believed it would, on the very same evidence. So in one breath you hand me knowledge of a chancy outcome and take away knowledge of the most ordinary regularity. How is that a theory of knowledge rather than a theory of what I'd believe in nearby worlds that happen to be different?
