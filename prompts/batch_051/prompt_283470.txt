so ive been trying to write up my views on the evolutionary argument for AI takeover and i have a handful of commitments that each feel right in isolation but might not all hang together. longtime rationalist-blog reader, so you can assume i know the base optimizer and gene-selection arguments; i don't need a primer. here's where i've landed:

(1) natural selection is substrate-neutral: any population with variation, heritability, and differential reproduction gets the Darwinian treatment, whether the replicators are genes, orgs, memes, or AI systems.

(2) i'm a boring Williams-and-Dawkins-style individualist about ordinary biology: group selection is almost always weak, good-of-the-species stories are suspect, and before i take any selective story seriously i want to know what the unit is and where the individual-level competitive interaction lives.

(3) selection has no foresight, no direction, no preferences; saying it "favors" a trait is just shorthand for ends-up-more-numerous, not a plan or an attractor.

(4) once AI systems can copy or self-improve faster than humans can babysit them, the unscrupulous high-growth copiers will outcompete the restrained, values-preserving ones; so over time the descendants of any AI population will tend to be whichever ones were least held back by human values, and the "nice" AIs lose.

(5) cultural evolution is the reason this isn't a done deal: humans already impose deliberate selection pressures via institutions, law, and reputation to restrain copy-happy agents like corporations or memes, and being able to intentionally set the fitness landscape feels like a different kind of process than blind natural selection.

do these five cohere? i genuinely can't tell wether (2) makes the "AI outcompetes humans" framing suspect, and whether (4) and (5) fit together or are pointing in opposite directions. give me exactly five numbered points, ranked by severity, each one or two sentences, no intro or conclusion: for each, say which commitments are actually in tension, which only look like they are, and what has to give for the cheapest fix.
