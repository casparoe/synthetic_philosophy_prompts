- A dentist takes a scan that delivers slightly more radiation then the standard x-ray and doesn't mention the difference. Not fine.

- Someone walks past a bus stop playing music off a speaker for about ten seconds. Fine.
- A city installs speed bumps without consulting residents, even though it slightly slows fire trucks on that street. Fine.
