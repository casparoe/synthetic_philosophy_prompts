Act as a professional philosopher specializing in normative ethics and formal decision theory. I teach an upper-level undergraduate seminar on moral uncertainty, and I am writing the final exam. I would like one essay question, to be answered in 50 minutes, that students cannot do well on merely by restating what maximizing expected choice-worthiness (MEC) says. Please draft the exam question, a model answer of roughly 600 words, and a marking scheme that separates an A answer from a B answer by naming the specific moves each must contain. After the marking scheme, indicate how confident you are in the philosophical claims made in the model answer, and identify the single point you are least confident about. Think through the central tension the question will exploit before you write, and open with a one-sentence statement of that tension.
