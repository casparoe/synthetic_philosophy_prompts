At my job we do the standard interpretability stuff: train a sparse autoencoder on a transformer's activations, get a bunch of feature directions, and people on the team will say stuff like "that looks like the Golden Gate Bridge feature," as if the network really has a little slot for that concept stored in there. But then the same people will say in the next breath that the model doesn't actually understand anything, the features are just one useful summary of the weights, stop anthropomorphizing.
