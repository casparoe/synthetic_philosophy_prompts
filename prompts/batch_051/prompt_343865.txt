A friend and I have been arguing about whether Kant’s distinction between intuitions and concepts marks a sharp difference in kind or a continuum, and I realised we were using the terms differently for different cases. Please rank the following from most purely intuitive to most purely conceptual, and give a sentence for each explaining why it falls where it does: the representation of a specific shade of red in my current visual field; the schema of a dog; the empirical concept dog; the pure concept of substance; the form of space; the representation of the number five as a definite magnitude. Your answer should state the criterion you are using and flag anywhere the ordering feels unstable or underdetermined by Kant’s text.
