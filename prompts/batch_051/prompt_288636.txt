ive been trying to figure out if effort is worth anything on its own, and i keep splitting it into two things. load-bearing effort is when the struggling does real work: remove it and either the result or you are different (working through a proof yourself instead of reading a solution, training rather than watching a highlight reel). decorative effort is struggle glued onto something that would be basically the same without it (doing the arithmetic by hand when a calculator is right there, or taking the stairs instead of the elevator when youll end up in the same hallway either way). my suspicion is that most arguments that effort is intrinsically valuable get their force from load-bearing cases and then quietly extend themselves to all effort. so my actual claim: decorative effort has no intrinsic value, at best instrumental value (signaling, health, boredom). is this distinction already a thing under some name? and give me the strongest steelman of the claim that decorative effort can be intrinsically valuable—the version of it i should actually worry about—then say whether my view survives that version or where it gives.
