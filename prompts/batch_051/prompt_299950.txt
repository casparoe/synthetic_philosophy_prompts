So: what would an adequate account of the epistemic backstop have to do? List the desiderata for any story about how we could still be justified in trusting a recording — or in refusing to trust one — once audio and video can be forged undetectably and at scale. For each requirement give the case or pressure that motivates it, mark which are non-negotiable and which are just a particular camp's preference, and flag any pair that can't be jointly met (privacy versus verification is one I suspect). Then say which one criterion rules out the most popular proposals. Don't score the contenders; the list and the tensions are what I'm after.
