The account of creativity I find myself defending in conversation goes like this. Originality — in music, mathematics, fiction, and even scientific theory choice — is sophisticated knob-twiddling: a domain implicitly defines a high-dimensional space of parameters (register, density, formal constraint, degree of schematicity, distance from a genre norm, and so on), and a creator's originality consists in finding an unexpected but still reachable setting of those knobs. On this view, the difference between the formulaic and the brilliant is not that the brilliant work comes from outside the space or from a special faculty; it is that the brilliant work sits at an unusual point in the same space, one a competent audience can recognize as a variation on a theme they already knew. The picture is deflationary in a specific way: creativity is search, not production ex nihilo.

I want a serious map of what would count against this picture, without a literature review — reason from cases, arguments, and conceptual pressure. Which possible cases, intuitions, and formal considerations about high-dimensional spaces would a committed defender have to concede as counting against the view, and which would they dismiss as misdescriptions or as already handled by adding a knob? Is the invention of a genuinely new genre or notation system a counterexample, or just an extreme setting of an existing knob? Does the view collapse into triviality if the parameter space can always be expanded after the fact to fit whatever gets made — and if it does, is that a defect or just a feature of any explanatory framework? Does it have anything to say about why some novel configurations are good and others worthless, or does it purchase an account of novelty at the price of explaining quality? Does it stick its neck out anywhere — does it predict, for instance, that fine-grained analysis will keep locating new works in a pre-existing space, or that skilled practitioners will describe breakthroughs as finding rather than making — or does it only retrodict? Please rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, identify which ones a defender would themselves concede as counting against the position, and give an honest verdict on whether the view has been insulated into unfalsifiability — and, if it has, whether that is a defect for a view of this kind. For what it is worth, I suspect I would be most moved by cases where the new work seems to require changing which knobs exist rather than merely turning them; tell me whether that suspicion is well founded. Please answer in American English.
