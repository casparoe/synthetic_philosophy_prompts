A linguist friend of mine has spent the semester laying out her views on the boundary between languages and other communication systems, and I have been keeping a record of her verdicts on cases. She holds that American Sign Language is a language, and that British Sign Language is also a language but a different one from ASL despite the English that surrounds both. She treats Esperanto as a language even though it was deliberately constructed, and she treats Nicaraguan Sign Language — which emerged among deaf children brought together with no shared language — as a language. She denies that the honeybee waggle dance is a language, and she says that a single deaf child's home sign system, however systematic, is not a full language. She also denies that the whistled register of Spanish used in the Canary Islands is a separate language; in her view it is Spanish whistled, not a new language. She denies that a trade pidgin used only as a second language by adults is a language. And she denies that Signing Exact English, a system that renders English morphemes and English word order manually for classroom use, is a language, calling it a code for English.

The case I now want to put to her is this. Imagine a small, somewhat isolated community in which something like Signing Exact English has become the community's primary face-to-face language across two generations. Children acquire it from birth; adults use it for work, domestic life, teasing, and negotiation; and it has largely displaced other languages for everything except written English. Its grammar remains, by all accounts, essentially English grammar performed with the hands. Would she, for consistency, call this a language? I care less about the bare yes or no than about the rule she seems to be applying, the alternative rules that fit her earlier verdicts equally well, and what each rule predicts for the new case. If her earlier verdicts genuinely underdetermine her answer, I want to know what principle she would be adopting with each possible choice, and what further case would most cleanly distinguish those principles. Please also say how confident you are in your diagnosis of her position, and single out the point on which you are least certain.
