I host a small philosophy podcast, and next week's guest is a formal epistemologist who's spent a few years defending the Dutch book argument as more than a cute illustration: his line is that vulnerability to a guaranteed-loss book of bets is genuinely what's wrong with incoherent credences, or at least our best diagnostic. I don't want the standard explainer he's given a hundred times. Could you draft eight to ten interview questions that go past restating the view? For each of those three, include a follow-up that assumes he gives the standard reply.
