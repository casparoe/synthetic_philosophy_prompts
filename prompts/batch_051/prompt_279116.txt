ok so here's roughly where I've landed after a semester of reading the mental imagery debate, and I'd like an audit of whether it actually hangs together. I keep going back and forth about wether these are compatible or whether I've just read both sides long enough to paper over a crack.

(1) I'm basically on Kosslyn's side: mental images are depictive—spatially organized, map-like—rather than propositional descriptions. The old image-scanning results, where people take longer to scan across a greater distance on an imagined map, still look to me like our best behavioral evidence for that.

(2) But I also find Pylyshyn's tacit-knowledge objection pretty convincing. Subjects don't need a spatially formatted image to produce those reaction times; they just need to know from real vision that moving across more space takes more time, and then simulate what they'd do. So no single chronometric result pins down the representational format.

(3) The reason I don't collapse into agnosticism is the neuroscience. If imagery activates early visual cortex in a retinotopic pattern that preserves spatial layout, that looks like exactly the physiological signature a depictive code would have and a propositional code wouldn't need. A subject can't fake that the way they can fake a response time.

(4) That said, I don't think depictivism commits me to a literal little picture in the head that some inner homunculus inspects. The format claim is about how information is encoded, not about pictorial physics.

(5) And I do think the debate is substantive rather than just two vocabularies for the same thing. If depictivism and propositionalism were equivalent redescriptions, there shouldn't be downstream differences about which abilities dissociate after brain damage or which tasks interfere with each other.

Does that set hang together? I honestly can't tell which of these is doing the real work, or whether I've got a coherent position with some loose ends rather than a real conflict. I'd like the tensions ordered by severity, with what exactly I'd have to give up or weaken for each; and please flag which pairs only look tense but dissolve on inspection.
