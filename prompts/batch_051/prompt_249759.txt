I'm on the working group that maintains my country's list of recognized sleep disorders, and insurance coverage follows whatever we list. We're deciding whether to add something the proposal calls idiopathic biphasic sleep disorder: a stable pattern where someone reliably wakes for an hour or two in the middle of the night and sleeps in two roughly equal blocks, with no daytime impairment, no trouble falling back asleep, and no other complaints. The proponents say it's a genuine disorder because it deviates from the normal consolidated eight-hour pattern and it causes real distress and friction with jobs and family schedules. The skeptics on the group say we'd be medicalizing a natural variant that was probably common before electric lighting, and that the only problem is a society built around consolidated sleep. I need a short decision memo for our chair by friday: should we list it, decline it, or list it with conditions, and what criteria should we actually be using. Give a clear recommendation and engage the strongest argument on the losing side rather than just restating the obvious.
