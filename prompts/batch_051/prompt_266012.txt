I keep noticing a configuration I do not have a word for, and I only have examples, not a definition. A child has a small, fixed set of wooden blocks — say forty pieces — and over years she builds structures she has never built before; each completed structure suggests several more, and the finite pile is never used up. A competent chess player, working with the same sixty-four squares, the same pieces, and the same rules, keeps meeting positions he has never seen and keeps finding new questions inside each one, although nothing has been added to the game. A reader who has lived with a single novel for decades reports that every rereading turns up something genuinely new, not merely something forgotten, and that the book seems to become more articulate rather than more exhausted with time. A mathematician can spend a career working on one equation or one space and keep finding questions there that she did not have at the start.

I am fairly sure this is not just the familiar observation that a finite set permits an astronomically large number of combinations, most of which no one has happened to call to mind. The striking thing is the character of an unspent source: each new item opens the way to the next, and the sense of inexhaustibility survives repeated returns. The novelty feels generated by the activity itself rather than selected from a fixed heap of possibilities.

I am looking for the established concept, if one exists, that names this exact thing: a finite system, together with rules or constraints, yielding an open-ended and apparently unspent series of experiences or questions. I want it carved off from the nearby ideas — combinatorial explosion, recursion, the capacity of a finite vocabulary and finite rules to generate an unbounded stream of sentences, mere novelty, and loosely 'emergent' complexity. If no established concept fits, coin a term for it, define it, and give me its edges. Does the phenomenon require a mind with a history, or can an unliving finite procedure exhibit it? Is there a real difference between question-generating inexhaustibility and a merely long output stream, and where does that line fall? A bare name will not satisfy me; I want the carving. Finally, tell me how confident you are in the identification or the coinage you propose, and single out the point you are least sure of.
