Draft the joint statement of an adversarial collaboration between a real-patterns theorist and a microphysical mechanist about the careenium. Consider a careenium, stipulated as follows: a sealed, energy-supplied chamber contains a vast number of self-propelled elements, simmballs, whose local collision rules fully generate the chamber's microdynamics; under a coarser description, stable configurations of simmballs function as symbols — some mark boundaries, some store records, some issue instructions — and these symbol configurations appear to redirect the trajectories of the very simmballs that compose them. The real-patterns camp holds that the symbols are real, implemented patterns and that the symbolic level does genuine causal work, without violating the microphysics. The mechanist camp holds that the only causal story is simmball-to-simmball and that symbol-level talk is a compact redescription with no causal content of its own. Produce the document these two could jointly sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration, containing: (a) a numbered agreed-claims section, covering the setup, the uncontested micro-level facts, and what each side explicitly does not claim; (b) a disagreements table stating each residual disagreement in wording each side would accept for its own view; (c) the two or three cruxes — e.g., whether simmball-level information screens off symbol-level generalizations, and whether multiple realizability undermines higher-level causal relevance — such that settling them would move at least one side; and (d) for each crux, a concrete test, intervention, or observation both sides would accept as bearing on it, with each side's prediction recorded in advance. If any part of the dispute strikes you as verbal, say which part and why.
