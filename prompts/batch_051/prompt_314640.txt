The government sets a target of moving from roughly 20th place into the top three within a decade, and ministers must publish a projected score impact for each significant initiative. Predict, with reasoning, how its policy mix changes over that decade: which of the six levers get pulled hardest, which components can be moved most cheaply without improving how people actually feel, and which resist gaming. Then say what this experiment would show about whether the ranking measures happiness and whether trying to top it is a sensible national goal. Make clear which links in your prediction are robust, which are guesses, and what would have to differ for the prediction to fail.
