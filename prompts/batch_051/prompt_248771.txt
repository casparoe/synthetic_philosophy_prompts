Before I read another defense or critique of Kelly betting, expected utility theory, or the geometric-rationality/ergodicity program, I want the specification: what does an adequate theory of rational risk-taking under uncertainty have to get right, where its job is to say how much of a bankroll to stake on a favorable but uncertain opportunity, and how the answer should change when the same decision is repeated many times?

For each desideratum, give the case or consideration that earns it a place on the list, and then mark it as non-negotiable or as a commitment of one camp (the expected-utility axiomatizer, the long-run growth-rate maximizer, the time-average/ergodicity person, the descriptive behavioral-finance view). I want that marking done honestly rather than smoothed over as neutral.

Then do the part I most care about: say whether any subset of the desiderata is jointly unsatisfiable, which of those conflicts is real and which is apparent, and what has to be given up under each resolution. Finally, tell me which single requirement does the most work in narrowing the field or exposing the standard candidates.

Things I suspect belong on the list, but do not let my guesses structure your answer unless they survive scrutiny: a policy that maximizes arithmetic expected value on each of many favorable bets can still drive wealth to zero almost surely; the same bet can be attractive made once and unattractive as part of a long-run policy, or the reverse; the true edge is almost never known exactly, so any criterion that needs precise probabilities must say something about estimation error; concave utility over wealth already represents risk aversion, so log-wealth reasoning has to avoid double-counting it or quietly collapsing into it; and unbounded cases like the St. Petersburg game or Pascal's-mugging-style offers break naive expected-value comparisons. Add any requirements I missed, and say explicitly if any of my items should be dropped as not genuine desiderata.

Throughout, separate what is actually settled among people who work on this from what is genuinely contested, and do not present a contested camp position as the consensus.
