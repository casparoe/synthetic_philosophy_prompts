A science writer I follow summarized Popper on Darwinism roughly like this. Popper held that natural selection is a tautology: the claim that the fittest survive amounts to saying that whatever survives survives, so evolutionary theory makes no risky predictions and, by Popper's own falsifiability criterion, is not science. Later, under pressure from biologists, he admitted the mistake and came to regard evolutionary biology as fully scientific. I have also seen it claimed that Popper originally classified Darwinism as a metaphysical research programme and that this was not meant as a flat dismissal, which makes me suspect the recantation story is too tidy. Is the writer's summary fair? Go through its claims one by one, separate what Popper actually held from what the summary slides over or conflates, and say whether the later shift really amounts to Popper conceding that evolutionary biology is scientific.
