I'm writing a novel where the protagonist has a whole running monologue about whether she has "self-control" — she quits drinking, then gets into ultramarathons, then it starts to look like maybe the ultramarathon thing is the same disease wearing a different hat. I keep drafting scenes where she (or the narrator) makes claims about self-control, and I've realized I dont actually have a working definition of it that I'd sign my name to. So I'd like you to propose one.

Give me a definition of self-control — bring in temperance, continence, enkrateia, akrasia, whatever distinctions you need, but land in plain language — that gets the following cases right, or says clearly why a case shouldn't count.

- A woman on a strict diet who wants the cake and doesn't eat it. (Clearly self-control, under any definition, right?)
- The same woman a year later, when she genuinely doesn't want the cake anymore. Is that greater self-control, or has self-control become unnecessary?
- A teenager with anorexia who restricts her eating through enormous effort. My gut says this is not self-control, but I cant say why without already assuming what self-control is.
- A workaholic who can't stop checking email even though he knows it's wrecking his marriage. Too little self-control, or self-control aimed at the wrong thing?
- A smoker who quits by using patches and never keeping cigarettes in the house, so the craving barely has a chance to arise. Is that self-control, or off-loading self-control onto the environment?
- Someone who represses anger until they feel completely calm, then blows up at a stranger over something trivial hours later.
- A parent who stays perfectly patient with a screaming toddler through pure gritted teeth while silently wanting to scream back.

Run your definition through all seven cases; if it gives a verdict that feels wrong or just goes quiet, say so, and then either revise the definition or defend the surprising verdict. Then tell me which verdict you're least sure about. If the definition forces you to say the dieter and the anorexic are exercising the same capacity, say that too. What I'm really trying to figure out is whether self-control is one thing, or a family of things that share a name because we like praising them.
