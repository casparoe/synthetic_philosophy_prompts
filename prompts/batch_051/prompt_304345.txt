I keep saying "that's true in the story" but I've never had a definition that survives contact with the hard cases. Define it for me, and run your definition through each of these before you commit to it:

1. In the Holmes stories, Holmes lives at 221B Baker Street. Clear yes.
2. In the stories, Holmes has a liver. I'd say yes even though it's never stated, but a strict textualist wouldn't.
3. In the stories, Holmes has a mobile phone. Clear no, even though the text never says he doesn't.
4. In the stories, Holmes was secretly a reptilian alien the whole time. I want to say no, but it's logically consistent with everything written, so what actually rules it out?
5. In the stories, Holmes has some determinate blood type. The text never says which, so is there a fact of the matter at all?
6. In the stories, Holmes is a fictional character. That's false inside the story and true outside it, presumably. How does one definition cover that boundary?
7. In a fan's unauthorized story, Holmes loves cats. Is that true "in the Holmes story," true only in the fan story, or neither? What if the fan story just fills a gap the original never touched?

Propose the definition, test it against all seven, and flag every place it gives a verdict you'd rather not defend.
