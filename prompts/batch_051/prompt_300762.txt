I'm writing up my view on biological functions for a philosophy of biology reading group and I want a consistency check before I circulate it. Here's what I currently believe:

1. The function of a trait is, at bottom, what it was selected for. I take this to be the right analysis of "proper" function, and I think it's what gives functions their normative force: it's what a trait is supposed to do, and that's why we can talk about malfunction.

2. It's completely fine for a physiologist to say the function of the heart is to pump blood, and to say a heart that can't pump blood is malfunctioning, even if the physiologist has never looked at the selection history of hearts and would flunk a pop quiz on vertebrate evolution. The function claim doesn't stand or fall on weather the individual scientist can produce the history.

3. A brand-new mutation that happens to help an organism survive in its first generation doesn't yet have a function. It might acquire one if it spreads and is maintained by selection, but until there's a history of selection for it, calling it a function is premature.

4. In practice, biology is full of functional ascriptions that just track what a component does in the current organism and how that contributes to survival or reproduction here and now. I think these are legitimate function claims too, not sloppy shorthand for something else.

5. What makes the difference between a function and a mere side effect can't be captured by saying "well, it's a causal role that contributes to some capacity we happen to care about." That's why I got off the causal-role boat in the first place. There has to be some normativity.

6. Biologists routinely assign functions to traits in extinct lineages whose selection history we will almost certainly never know. If I'm honest, I think they're doing real biology when they do that, and I don't want my view to say they're just guessing or speaking metaphorically.

So: which of these are actually in tension, which only look like it, and what's the least costly revision? Rank the tensions by severity, and for each say what would have to give and how much it would cost me.
