For each side's central claims, tell me what translates cleanly, what translates only with distortion and what the distortion conceals, and what has no counterpart in that vocabulary at all. Keep the translation honest by not smuggling the database framework's assumptions in and then declaring the historians confused, but also do not excuse vague claims by insisting they are untranslatable without argument.
