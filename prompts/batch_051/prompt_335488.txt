Ok so I've been going back and forth on Schopenhauer on music, mostly section 52 of The World as Will and Idea, and I'd like to know whether the set of things I'm inclined to believe actually hangs together. The passage I keep coming back to is where he says music is unlike the other arts because it doesn't copy the Platonic Ideas but copies the will itself, and then he connects the bass, tenor, alto, and soprano voices to the grades of objectification of the will from inorganic nature up to human consciousness. I'm not going to pretend I fully understand what "copy of the will" amounts to, but here's roughly where I've landed:

1. Music is non-representational in a way the other arts aren't; painting and poetry present Ideas through particular images or concepts, but music bypasses that and gives the inner movement of willing directly.
2. That's why music feels more intimate than the other arts—it doesn't hold a represented object at a distance, it makes the listener feel the striving itself.
3. Schopenhauer's view seems to entail that pure instrumental music is closer to what music essentially is than music with words, because adding a text pulls it back toward representation and makes it copy something other than will.
4. But I'm not willing to accept the full purism: I think some texted or programmatic music can be just as genuinely musical as pure instrumental music, without being degraded by the text or story.
5. And I'm skeptical of the specific harmony mapping—bass as inorganic nature, melody as the highest grades of will, etc. It feels like he's reading an arbitrary analogy back into the structure of music and then treating it as an explanation.

The question is whether (1)–(5) are jointly tenable, or whether something has to give. I'm especially unsure whether my skepticism in (5) undercuts (1) and (2) more than I'd like, and whether (4) is really consistent with the way I'm using (1). If you had to order the tensions by severity, which ones are real and which just look like tensions because of sloppy wording on my part?
