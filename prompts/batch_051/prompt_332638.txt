I teach Chinese philosophy and moral psychology, and over the years I've quietly ended up defending Wang Yangming's unity of knowledge and action as a live view rather then just a historical one. My version goes like this: when someone says "I knew I shouldn't have done that" and then reliably does it again, the thing they're calling knowledge isn't the real thing. It's verbal acknowledgement, dim recognition, knowing the words apply, maybe a rehearsal of what they were taught. What Yangming means by knowledge is already action-shaped; if the action isn't there, the knowledge wasn't there either. So I've started treating akrasia less as clear-eyed weakness of will, and more as a failure of seeing: the person hasn't fully taken in the normative shape of the situation, hasn't seen it as calling for just that response.

I want you to act as a professional philosopher who knows both the Confucian tradition and contemporary moral psychology and philosophy of action, and who is not already committed to my view. Think carefully; maybe start by brainstorming the strongest cases and arguments against the unity thesis before you organise them. Then give me a map of what counts against this position. Which ordinary cases get real traction — the addict, the person with executive dysfunction, the depressive who says perfectly well "I know I should get up" — and which ones can a defender handle? Which empirical findings or phenomena from psychology would be genuinely awkward rather than just grist for the view? What would the world have to look like for the identity of knowledge and action to be false, rather than merely restated under a more careful definition of knowledge?

I'd also like the defeater map sorted from fatal to merely awkward, including what a committed Yangmingean would honestly concede counts against them, not only what critics say. And since I want the answer itself to be self-critical: tell me what would change your mind about your own assessment, or where your reasoning is most likely to fail. Has the view been qualified into unfalsifiability, and if so, is that a problem for a view of this kind?
