I'm trying to work out what I actually think we owe the real dead when we dramatize them, going by my gut on cases.

A novel gives a fully invented inner life to a real fourteenth-century peasant known only through a single tax record, and no one could mistake it for biography. Fine.

A prestige biopic about a recently dead, much-admired public figure invents private conversations no source could support, and markets itself as the true story of her life. Not fine.

A revenge fantasy in which the descendants of a dictator's victims assassinate him years early is plainly counterfactual and makes no claim to accuracy. Fine.

A centuries-old play presents a real king as a scheming, deformed murderer of his child-nephews; parts of the portrait are historically contested, and the play has fixed popular memory of him ever since. Fine, though it does bother me a little.

A stylized musical about a venerated statesman uses anachronistic music and color-conscious casting, openly artificial, but quietly omits his slaveholding so the audience can admire him without reservation. Not fine.

A serious novel reimagines the final hours of a real woman murdered in a still-memorialized injustice, inventing intimate details, and her descendants and community have asked that she not be fictionalized this way. Not fine.

A novel presents a real, recently dead, childless recluse as having committed a gruesome crime she almost certainly did not commit; it is clearly fiction and she has no surviving relatives. Not fine, though I cannot say exactly whom the lie harms.

Can one principle capture all of these, or nearly all of them? If not, which verdicts should I drop? The pair I cannot settle is the play that turns a king into a monster and the musical that turns a statesman into a saint: both knowingly falsify the moral character of a long-dead person, but they feel different. And what should the principle say about a clearly fictional novel that invents plausible, unrecorded private scenes for a long-dead figure, scenes that flatter her while ignoring her documented cruelties?
