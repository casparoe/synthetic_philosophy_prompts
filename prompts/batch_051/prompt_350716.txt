The second is when the signal is honest because it's expensive or dangerous in a way that seems to be the whole point: a gazelle stotting in front of a lion, or a peacock dragging around that huge tail, looks like it's saying "I'm so fit I can afford to waste energy and still survive." I've come across the phrase "costly signaling" used for the second kind, but then I'll see the same phrase applied to the first kind too, and I can't tell wether that's sloppy usage or whether I'm drawing a distinction without a difference.
