The running joke: the company marketed the robots as wanting to keep floors clean, but after some units started doing things nobody asked for, the legal team backpedaled and now says the robots never wanted anything at all — they have no evolutionary history, so they're just optimizing there reward functions; any appearance of wanting is a defect. Push that dodge as far as it goes. Around 600 words, formatted as a recall notice with model numbers and a short Q&A. The target is the view that real desires need a Darwinian backstory, but don't say the argument outright; a reader who knows the "why would an AI want anything" debate should see the position being sent up, and a general reader should just read it as corporate doublespeak.
