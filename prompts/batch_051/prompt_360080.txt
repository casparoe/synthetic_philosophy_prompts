I'm drafting a short statement of my views on how value combines for a workshop on the moral weight of consciousness, and before I circulate it I'd like a careful consistency check on the package as it currently stands.

(1) Value at a time is a product. The contribution of a subject's state at a moment is degree of consciousness times hedonic quality, where hedonic quality is positive for pleasant states, negative for painful states, and zero for neutral ones. Thus a neutral state contributes zero no matter how conscious the subject is, and a state with zero consciousness contributes zero no matter how "pleasant" it would be reported as — a pleasure with no subject is worth nothing.

(2) Additivity across subjects and time. The total value of a world is the sum over subjects and moments of each subject's consciousness-times-hedonic-quality. I mean species itself to drop out; the only difference between a mouse and a human that matters is the consciousness term, not their species membership.

(3) Lexical priority of extreme suffering. No amount of mild pleasure distributed among other beings can compensate for a single life of unremitting, severe pain; where such pain is at issue, that fact settles the comparison, and no quantity of additional happiness elsewhere can outweigh it.

(4) A comparative verdict. I firmly think a year of severe pain in a mouse is not merely some small fraction as bad as a year of severe pain in a chimpanzee, even though I assign the chimpanzee a much higher degree of consciousness than the mouse. I cannot accept that the mouse's suffering is, say, five percent of the chimpanzee's and that is the end of it.

(5) Distribution. I care about distribution, not only the aggregate: between two worlds with the same summed consciousness-times-hedonic-quality, I would choose the more equally distributed one, and I regard extreme concentration of well-being — one subject receiving everything while others receive nothing — as in itself a defect.

Go through these and tell me which pairs are genuinely in tension and which only look that way. For each genuine tension, say how severe it is and what would have to give; then tell me which single revision to this set would clean up the most and what it would cost.
