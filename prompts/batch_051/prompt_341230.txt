The other camp says the language is becoming an implementation detail in the sense assembly is now: the interface that matters will be natural language, and the interesting design work will migrate upward into requirements, verification, and judging whether the system behaves correctly, while the choice of Rust versus Go becomes about as consequential as the choice of instruction set.
