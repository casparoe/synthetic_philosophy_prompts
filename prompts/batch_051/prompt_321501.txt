I keep seeing people argue about when the Anthropocene really started — early farming, the 1610 Orbis exchange, the Industrial Revolution, or the Great Acceleration in the mid-20th century with its plutonium fallout spike in lake mud. But the body that maintains the official geological time scale has declined to add the Anthropocene as a formal epoch. Doesn't that kind of dissolve the question? Like, once geologists refuse to ratify it, there's no fact of the matter about a start date anymore — it's just people picking whichever date fits the story they want to tell about human responsibility. Is that the right way to read the situation?
