- the carbon atoms in a diamond: yes, they compose the diamond
- the cells in a living cat: yes, they compose the cat
- the Hawaiian Islands: no, they don't compose one object
- a swarm of bees flying together: no, not one thing, even though it moves around as a unit
- a table with the legs screwed on: yes, one table
- a plate that was broken and then carefully superglued back together: yes, a plate again

Which rule is she actually running — the contact view (touching things compose), the fastening view (things have to be glued or screwed or otherwise stuck together), organicism (only living things compose), universalism (any two things whatsoever compose a sum), or something else?
