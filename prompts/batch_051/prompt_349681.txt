They clench and unclench the intact hand while watching the mirror, and the phantom pain lets go; sometimes they even feel the phantom hand move. Stay in character — speak as Merleau-Ponty, in the first person, a couple paragraphs per turn, and don't mention anything later than your own life time except what I just described.
