I've been trying to sort out what I actually think about qualia, going by my gut on cases. Here's the set:

1. Chase and Sanborn are two coffee tasters. Both start out liking the coffee, both later dislike it. Chase says the tasting experience itself changed: it used to taste good, now it tastes bad. Sanborn says the experience is exactly the same but his attitude toward it changed: it still tastes the same, he just no longer likes that taste. I can't see any way, even in principle, to settle who's right. But I also don't think the question is meaningless.

2. Inverted spectrum: two people drive a nail into a green post and stop at the red traffic light. One person's whole color spectrum is inverted relative to the other, but they've always used the same words and made the same discriminations, so no behavior or brain scan could ever reveal it. I don't think it makes sense to say there's a hidden fact about which one has the inverted qualia.

3. Mary the color scientist, who knows every physical fact about color vision but has spent her whole life in a black-and-white room. When she first sees red, I think she learns something new. But I also think that something new is just a new ability to recognize and imagine red, not a new fact about redness.

4. A philosophical zombie, physically identical to me in every way but with no phenomenology at all. I can't rule it out, but I also can't find any real difference between no qualia and qualia that are causally inert and undetectable. It feels like the concept is doing no work.

5. A wine taster's palate gets better over a year. She says the same bottle from the same vintage tastes different to her. I think her discrimination has sharpened but the basic taste-feel didn't swap or change; she just notices more. But when I try to say exactly what stayed the same, I can't point to it.

Is there one coherent position on qualia that delivers all five verdicts, or close? If not, which verdicts do I have to give up? And what would the surviving position say about a case I haven't listed: someone who takes a drug that makes sugar taste bitter for an hour, then reports afterward that it tasted exactly like quinine?
