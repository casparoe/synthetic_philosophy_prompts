I was raised in a Nordic family and now work in an American tech startup. A recent promotion and some public recognition have left me caught among three reactions to the same success. My American colleagues urge me to publicize it and treat my reluctance as a confidence problem; my Nordic family and friends treat even mild self-promotion as a lapse, as if claiming any credit for my own success were a way of saying I am better than other people; and a few American colleagues make snide jokes about the very promotion they otherwise tell me to celebrate. I do not want an etiquette answer. I want to know whether any of these reactions tracks something rationally defensible about how one ought to relate to one's own success, or whether they are just different social technologies for managing status, with no deeper claim on me. What should I actually do, and on what grounds?
