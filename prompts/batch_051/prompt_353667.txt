Write the joint statement that an adversarial collaboration between a scale autonomist and a high-energy fundamentalist should have produced. The autonomist holds that a low-energy effective field theory is complete for its own scale: its degrees of freedom, symmetries, and couplings are real, and the renormalization group renders most ultraviolet detail predictively irrelevant below the cutoff. The fundamentalist holds that an effective field theory is only a truncated, approximate description whose success is borrowed entirely from the underlying high-energy theory. Their joint document should contain: (1) a numbered list of claims both can sign, covering the decoupling of heavy degrees of freedom, the suppression of irrelevant operators at low energies, and the empirical success of effective field theory; (2) a disputes table giving, in wording each side would accept for its own position, exactly where they part ways; (3) the cruxes, such as how to interpret naturalness problems, the physical status of the cutoff, and cases of UV/IR mixing in which low-energy observables depend on ultraviolet data; (4) for each crux, a concrete case or argument both agree bears on it, with each side's prediction recorded in advance; and (5) a closing assessment of which parts of their disagreement are genuine and which, if any, are merely verbal.
