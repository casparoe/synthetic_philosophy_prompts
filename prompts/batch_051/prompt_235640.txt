Please give me your own number, but more importantly the decomposed reasoning behind it: your probability that such a coordination failure occurs at all; your probability, conditional on failure, that rationalist mechanisms (private information, commitment problems, issue indivisibilities) rather than non-rationalist ones (organizational dysfunction, accident, ideology) are the best explanation; and, within the rationalist mechanisms, what share of the explanatory weight commitment problems carry. Then show how those terms combine to produce your credence, state which sub-question your number is most sensitive to, indicate where the disagreement between my friend and me most likely lives, and say what concrete technical, political, or institutional development would move your credence most.
