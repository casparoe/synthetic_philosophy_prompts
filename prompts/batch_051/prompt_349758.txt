I recently spent an evening arguing with a friend about causation, and we kept circling one claim: all we ever observe is constant conjunction—a struck match followed by a flame—and never any necessary connection in the objects themselves, so to say that the striking caused the lighting is to say only that such strikings are regularly followed by lightings, plus our habit of expectation. My friend defended the strong version of this: there is nothing further in the world for the words necessary connection to answer to, and no experiment could turn up a residue that mere regularity leaves out. I wanted to resist, but I could not specify an observation that would tell in my favour without already assuming that causes are more than regularities.

I would like you to design the strongest available empirical test of the claim that causes involve something beyond regular succession. Try to operationalize at least part of the dispute rather than declaring it wholly untestable. Suppose you can construct or compare two systems—engineered, simulated, or observed—that are matched on their overt successions but differ in whatever hidden structure you think might underlie genuine necessitation, such as a mechanism, a disposition, a conserved quantity, or some other counterfactual-supporting invariance. Specify the comparison, the manipulation or measurement that would discriminate the two readings, and the results that would favour the regularity reading versus the power-or-necessity reading. Then be explicit about what a positive result would and would not settle: could a regularity theorist always redescribe the detected structure as a higher-order regularity, a law, or a feature of our models rather than of the things themselves? Is any residue a defect in the tests, or a defect in the notion of necessary connection itself? One further wrinkle from our argument: if someone allows that there might be real necessary connections that are in principle unobservable, does that position differ in any testable way from the view that there are none?
