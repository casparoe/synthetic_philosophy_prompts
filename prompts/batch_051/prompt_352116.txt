On my picture these are the same underlying dynamic — optimization pressure flowing upstream from the objective into the machinery that fixes the objective — just landing at different depths. Is this actually a useful unification, or am I just relabeling reward hacking / Goodhart's law? Does the layering metaphor hold up?
