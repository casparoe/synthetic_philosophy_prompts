A government has become convinced by the capabilities approach to distributive justice but needs an operational rule. It institutes a Capability Equalization Scheme. Every citizen is assessed at ages 18, 30, 45, and 60 by a week-long battery that measures achieved functionings across ten dimensions: mobility, bodily health, cognitive performance, social participation, political participation, and the like. For each person, an actuarial model estimates the resources needed to bring that person's measured functioning vector up to their age cohort's median on the dimensions most responsive to resources, and the person receives that amount as an unrestricted cash supplement, recalculated after each assessment. Later gains in functioning are not clawed back, but they lower future supplements. Analyze this scheme as a mechanism: what does it incentivize, where does gaming enter first, what is actually being equalized, and which failure modes can be fixed without abandoning the capabilities framework?
