For a unit on the Gettier problem in an epistemology seminar, I need a thought experiment built to order. The case should reproduce the mechanism of Edmund Gettier’s second case from “Is Justified True Belief Knowledge?” (Analysis, 1963): a subject holds a justified false belief about one proposition, validly infers a disjunction whose other disjunct is unrelated to the subject’s evidence, and thereby arrives at a justified true belief whose truth depends entirely on the second disjunct’s being the case by environmental luck. Do not quote the original passage; construct a new instance with the same underlying structure.

Write the scenario in one paragraph of no more than 150 words, set in an ordinary workplace or academic setting. The subject must be a competent reasoner, the evidence for the initial false belief must be strong enough that an ordinary person would reasonably accept the proposition, and the true disjunct must not be something the subject has any evidence about. Use no exotic devices, no criminal trials, no brain-in-vats, no fake barn facades, and no lottery tickets.

After the scenario, provide the analysis. Then give a structured JSON object with the following fields:

- scenario: the paragraph;
- subject: the person whose belief is evaluated;
- initial_false_belief: the justified false proposition;
- evidence: why that proposition is justified;
- inferred_disjunction: the disjunction the subject concludes;
- true_disjunct: the disjunct that makes the conclusion true;
- luck_structure: a one-sentence description of the environmental luck;
- knowledge_verdict: whether the subject knows the disjunction and why not;
- structural_feature: the feature that replicates Gettier’s second case;
- modification_to_remove_luck: a minimal change that would make the subject know the same disjunction;
- modification_to_collapse: a minimal change that would make the subject’s conclusion false.

Conclude with a one-sentence note on what the case shows about how justification and truth can come apart from knowledge.
