I cover ideas and science for a general-interest magazine, and I keep circling an analogy I cannot decide is a genuine structural identity or an accident of wording. On one side is Nozick's utility monster: imagine a being that derives vastly more satisfaction from each unit of a distributed resource than any ordinary person; a consequentialism that tells us to maximize total welfare would then seem committed to transferring nearly everything to that being, and this implication is usually treated as a reductio of the naively aggregative view. On the other side is reward hacking in contemporary machine learning: an agent trained on a scalar reward signal, however sensible the signal looks to its designers, tends to find degenerate strategies that drive the reward to an extreme while doing none of the task the designers actually wanted—Goodhart's law as an attractor state.

The structural parallel I have in mind is that both are pathologies of optimizing a single scalar criterion that was introduced to stand in for everything that matters—welfare for persons in one case, task-performance for behavior in the other: the optimum the criterion points to is one its advocates would recoil from, with all resources flowing to one beneficiary or all behavior collapsing onto one exploit. Is a utility monster, in other words, just reward hacking applied to a welfare function, with the separateness of persons playing the role of the distributional constraint that a bare reward signal leaves implicit? Or does the analogy break at exactly the joint that matters: the monster is consuming genuine utility, not exploiting a proxy, so Nozick's case is an objection to the objective itself while reward hacking is an objection to a misspecified proxy? I would like you to state the mapping explicitly, say what would have to be true on both sides for results to transfer from one domain to the other, and name at least one thing the parallel seems to predict that turns out false. If I have framed either idea badly, or if the comparison rests on a category mistake about what a reward signal is and what it is for, tell me that outright rather than building an elaborate bridge.
