Can you write me a piece of deadpan satire, around 650 words, in the form of a community-norms / trust-and-safety FAQ for an online forum that has permanently solved the paradox of tolerance? The founding line is "all viewpoints are welcome except viewpoints that would make other viewpoints unwelcome," and the rest of the page is the mods discovering, one clause at a time, that they now have to define "make unwelcome," then define whether contesting that defintion makes the definers unwelcome, then whether tolerating the intolerant's intolerance of the intolerant is itself intolerant, and so on. I want the jokes to come from the actual structure of the paradox rather than from generic mod-cop absurdity: the performative contradiction in banning intolerant speech in the name of tolerance, the attempt to patch it by distinguishing tolerating beliefs from tolerating actions and only making the recursion worse, the way "intolerance" keeps getting operationalized by whichever faction currently holds the moderator console. Stay deadpan the whole time and don't state the objections as objections; it should be legible, so a reader who knows the problem can see exactly which move is being ironized, but it shouldn't wink. Maybe include an FAQ entry where a user is banned for asking whether the FAQ itself is intolerant of intolerant users, and a section about the annual policy review where the guidelines are amended to tolerate the previous version's intolerance. Target the naive solution to the paradox and the practice of writing moderation policy as if the problem were just under-specification.
