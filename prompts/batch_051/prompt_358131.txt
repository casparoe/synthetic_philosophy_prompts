My working hypothesis is that the disagreement is not really about the interpretation of Locke, nor about which distributive criterion is correct, but about what kind of fact the proviso is meant to be: a forensic rule about how a title arises, a moral side-constraint on any property system, or a piece of history that has already played its role and can now be set aside. Is that diagnosis right, or is there a genuine factual or normative question underneath that I am missing? I want a diagnosis of the structure of the disagreement rather than a survey of positions.
