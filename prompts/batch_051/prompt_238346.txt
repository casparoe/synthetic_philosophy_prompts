Other days I think no, a good life is a good life, so adding one just is an improvement if the new life is worth living, even if she's a bit below our current average. I can't tell whether I'm flip-flopping or whether the scenarios I'm picturing differ in some hidden way.

Here's the variable I want isolated: in Case 1 the new person makes the people who already exist somewhat worse off, and a normal person's intuition should be to choose (A), don't add her. As far as possible, everything else should be the same: same initial group, same rough level of wellbeing, no villains, no sci-fi, no repugnant-conclusion style huge populations.
