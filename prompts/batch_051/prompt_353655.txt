ok so ive been thinking about the self on my own for a while and got two friends of mine into it, and theyve been arguing for like a year about whether the self is a real thing or more like the center of gravity of a story -- a useful point you can point to and predict with, but not an actual object inside you. one of them, call them the story camp, is pretty convinced the self is made by narrative: the brain keeps telling a story with a main character and that main character just is the self, and theres nothing extra behind it. the other, call them the witness camp, thinks that leaves out the part doing the experiencing: the subject thats already there before any story starts, the part that would maybe still be there if the story stopped.

i want the document they would produce if they actually did an adversarial collaboration and tried to write something both could sign. not a compromise, more like a clean statement of what they actually agree on, where exactly they part ways, and what would move at least one of them. id also like it to seperate the disagreements that are real from the ones that were just two people using words differently. theyve gone round and round on a couple of cases especially: someone on a long silent retreat who says the inner talk stops but a bare awareness is still there, and someone with a big memory problem who cant put their life into a story but still seems to have a self, and also a made-up diary keeping ai that looks narratively like a self but (both agree, for the sake of argument) has no inner experience. use those as your test cases where they help, and add one or two more if you think theyd both sign them.

i need the whole thing as a json object because im putting it into a spreadsheet after. use these fields:

title: a short neutral name for the document
agreed_claims: list of strings, each a claim both sides can sign
disagreements: list of objects, each with:
  issue: neutral one line version of whats at stake
  story_camp_wording: how the story camp would state their own view in words theyd sign
  witness_camp_wording: same for the witness camp
  verbal_or_real: either verbal, real, or mixed, plus a sentence saying what they mean differently if anything
  crux: the subquestion such that if they found out the answer tomorrow at least one of them would change or seriously weaken their view
  test_or_case: a concrete situation, existing case, or thought experiment both sides accept as bearing on that crux, and a sentence on why it bears
  story_camp_prediction: what the story camp says that test or case would show if theyre right
  witness_camp_prediction: same for the witness camp
verbal_only_notes: list of strings for places where they realised they were just meaning different things by a word

don't give a verdict on who's right and don't cite anyone, just write the document they could both put their names to.
