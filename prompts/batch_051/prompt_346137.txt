Over the past nine months I have been forced to get clear about something I had previously managed to avoid, and I would like the specification written out before I make any further decisions on a system I maintain. The system ranks passages by semantic closeness using the now-standard vector representations of words and sentences. In practice it repeatedly mistakes surface co-occurrence for meaning: it treats 'the dog bit the man' and 'the man bit the dog' as near-synonymous because the words match, and it can push apart two sentences one of which plainly entails the other because their surface forms differ. I have therefore stopped trusting the assumption that closeness in embedding space is itself a theory of meaning, but I do not yet have a crisp statement of what such a theory would have to achieve before it deserved that name.

What I am asking for is the desiderata. What must an embedding-based account of English word and sentence meaning satisfy before it may count as a semantic theory rather than a text-similarity heuristic? Fix the purpose at the level I actually need: retrieval and simple inference over ordinary prose, not a complete formal semantics of a natural language. For each requirement, give the case or consideration that makes it a requirement, and mark whether it is non-negotiable or only the preference of one camp. I am especially interested in places where the list itself becomes impossible: flag any subsets of desiderata that cannot be jointly met by a particular family of models, whether static type-level vectors, contextual token-level embeddings, or pooled sentence embeddings, and say what would have to be traded away. If, for example, a single symmetric similarity relation cannot represent entailment because entailment is directed, or if compositionality and lexical ambiguity pull against each other, those are the tensions I most need stated precisely.

Then, as a short appendix rather than a survey, audit the three families against your list and say which single requirement does the most to exclude the easy identification of embedding similarity with sameness of meaning. Do not make the audit depend on named releases, benchmark scores, or anything I would need to look up. And please write the response in British English.
