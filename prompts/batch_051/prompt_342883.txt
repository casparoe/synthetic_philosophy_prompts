Say the demon from Nietzsche's eternal recurrence thought experiment shows up, but the deal is different: you'll live this exact life again 10^100 times, in the same order, with no memory of the other runs, and after the last one you're annihilated. Do the usual readings of the original still go through? In particular, does the affirmation test—the test of whether you would welcome the news or curse the demon—still do its work when the repetitions are finite, or does it secretly rest on literal infinity? And what about the cosmological reading, where the doctrine is treated as a claim that the universe actually cycles through its states: does swapping the infinite number of cycles for a huge finite one change that reading in kind, or only in scale? Finally, try to say whether there is a cutoff number of repetitions below which the thought experiment looses its bite, and if so, what that cutoff tells us about what the test was really testing.
