Lately I've noticed I've adopted a policy with chatbots and other opaque AI systems: I never use mental-state words about them, not even as shorthand. The other day I spent half an hour rewriting some notes so they didn't say the model 'thought' or 'was trying to' do anything, and I honestly felt like I was seeing the situation more clearly. A friend says I'm just refusing to take the intentional stance, and that describing a system as believing or wanting things is predictively useful whether or not anything is really inside. Is my policy defensible? Under what conditions is the intentional stance a better epistemic shortcut than my flat refusal—and when is my refusal just a bias dressed up as rigor?
