I sit on the appeals panel of a municipal work-conditionality board. Our recent decisions: we upheld a sanction for a claimant who had underreported two weeks of casual earnings and later repaid the overpayment, holding that a breach of honesty is a breach regardless of amount or repayment; we upheld a sanction for a fit claimant with no dependents who refused a warehouse job over a long commute and lower pay, on the ground that the job was suitable and such a preference is not good cause; we overturned a sanction for a claimant with a chronic back condition who refused a cleaning job after submitting a specialist's letter in advance saying the work was medically unsuitable; we overturned a sanction for a lone parent who missed a work-focused interview because her child was unwell, having phoned the office that morning and produced a doctor's note the next day; we overturned a sanction for a claimant with no fixed address who missed several appointments, because a shelter caseworker had already written to the agency explaining the instability and asking for adjustments; and we upheld a sanction for a fit claimant with no dependents who missed two appointments and said he had simply forgotten them.

The new appeal: a claimant with no prior compliance problems missed two mandatory appointments in one week. He says he had just begun a new medication and was suffering severe dizziness and nausea that left him unable to leave home; he was too unwell to telephone on either day and, being new to the medication, did not think to contact the agency afterwards. Three weeks later he submitted a GP's letter confirming the prescription and calling the reported side effects plausible, though the GP had not seen him during the week in question.

If we want to decide consistently with these precedents, what should we do? Identify the closest earlier case, set out the competing principles our decisions could be taken to embody, say what each principle requires in the new appeal, and offer your best recommendation plus the further case that would best settle which principle we are actually applying.
