One of my friends is doing a PhD in early modern history and we have a running fight about whether period labels pick out real things in the world or are just retrospective filing systems. She's creepily consistent, so I've started keeping score of her calls. Her track record so far: the Middle Ages, not real (she calls it the leftover gap between two periods people actually cared about, and nobody in 1100 thought of themselves as medieval); the Renaissance, real (people at the time literally saw themselves as staging a rebirth of antiquity); the Scientific Revolution, real (between Copernicus and Newton something in what counted as knowledge really did break, and the participants kept announcing they were doing something new); the Industrial Revolution, real (people watched their world reorganize around machines and said so at the time); the Enlightenment, real (the people in it were self-conscious about living in an age of criticism and light); the Axial Age, not real (she calls it a twentieth-century projection that glued together unconnected ancient developments); the Cold War, real (people lived inside its structure and knew it). I think I've got the verdicts right even if I'm compressing her reasons.

The case I want to spring on her is the Neolithic Revolution. She's never ruled on it. It's possibly the largest structural rupture in human history, but no one at the time had the concept of living through a "Neolithic Revolution" — that label arrived in the twentieth century. So if her rule is about whether people then had a self-conscious sense of their own epoch, she should say not real; if her rule is about whether there was a genuine structural break, she should say unambiguously real. But those aren't the only options, and I want to know if there's a better, maybe messier, rule that fits her whole record.

What do you predict she'll say about the Neolithic Revolution, and what rule is she running? Lay out the candidate rules that fit her track record, say what each predicts for the Neolithic case, give your best guess with reasons, and tell me which further case would do the most to tell the plausible rules apart.
