Then write the dialogue. Both characters should be sharp and equal in ability, not strawmen; they should respond to what the other person actually says. End without forcing agreement; its ok if they stay divided. If it's useful, one of them can bring up that this particular theorem also has an explicit witness (for example a = sqrt(2) and b = 2*log_2(3) gives a^b = 3, and both are irrational), but if that happens the conversation should turn to whether that fact weakens the constructivist's complaint about the original proof.
