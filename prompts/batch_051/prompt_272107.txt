My friend got caught sharing the answer key for our AP Bio final and now the school is deciding what to do with him. Its a first offense and he confessed right away, and normally that gets you like a week of detention. But the principal wants to suspend him for the rest of the semester and basically admitted that its partly to scare the rest of the grade out of cheating. My friend's parents are saying you can't punish someone extra just to send a message — punishment has to match what the person actually did, and anything beyond that is just using him as an example. But I keep thinking, if the harsher punishment actually stops a bunch of other kids from cheating, isnt that a good thing? Like isnt stopping cheating the whole point of punishing people for it? My gut says using him as a warning is gross, but my brain says the outcomes matter most. Is it wrong to punish one person more than he 'deserves' if it deters a lot of other people? And how do you even figure out what someone 'deserves' when the main reason to punish might be preventing future cheating?
