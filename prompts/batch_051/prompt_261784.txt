so: write me the desiderata that any account of the therapy/enhancement distinction has to meet to count as adequate for those gatekeeping jobs. for each requirement give the case or consideration that forces it, and then flag which ones are non-negotiable (a view that fails this isnt a view of *this* distinction at all) and which are really just camp preferences (a normal-species-function person would insist, a welfarist would say it begs the question). include at least one desideratum about whether the distinction is supposed to be describing a biological fact or doing normative gatekeeping, and say whether a purely descriptive version of it could even satisfy the rest of your list. the part i most want: tell me if some subset of the desiderata is jointly unsatisfiable -- or can only be met by gerrymandering the line so hard that the distinction does no actual work. dont bother scoring the existing views, ill do that in class.
