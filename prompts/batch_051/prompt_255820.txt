I got called for jury duty and the case is eating at me. A corner-store clerk sold beer to a 19-year-old with a fake ID that scanned fine and looked real; he even asked for a second piece of ID. The judge told us that under our state's law it doesn't matter whether the clerk was careful—selling to a minor is a violation, period, and all the state has to prove is that the sale happened. The other jurors are ready to convict cause "the law's the law," but the guy seems to have done everything a reasonable person would do. I'm the one who has to vote guilty or not. Is it actually fair to punish someone for something he tried that hard to prevent? What should I be weighing here?
