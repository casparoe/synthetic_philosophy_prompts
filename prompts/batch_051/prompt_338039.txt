Write a short story where the whole plot turns on the difference between a fink and a mask. Quick setup in case you dont know the jargon: imagine a wine glass that is genuinely fragile. A fink is something that intervenes at the exact moment of impact and removes the fragility—like a tiny forcefield in the glass that switches on only when struck. A mask is different: the glass stays fragile, but something outside keeps the fragility from ever showing up, like a soft robot hand holding it so nothing can ever touch it. Put a character in a situation where they have to bet on which one theyre dealing with—could be a con artist, an insurance fraud investigator, a cursed object, whatever. The story should make the distinction do actual work in the plot, not just namedrop it.
