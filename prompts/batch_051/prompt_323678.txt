"Prioritarianism says that when we're distributing benefits, we should give extra weight to people who are worse off. In practice that makes it a kind of egalitarianism, since favoring the badly off tends to shrink inequality. But like every egalitarian view it faces the levelling-down objection: if inequality itself is bad, then making the rich poorer can improve a situation in one respect even when nobody gains.
