I’m taking a course on philosophy of biology, and we’ve just finished the unit on the tautology objection to natural selection and the propensity interpretation of fitness. I’m preparing something for a student-organized “jokes that actually teach” event, and I want the humor to respect the real philosophical distinction rather than collapse into a lazy survival-of-the-fittest pun. As I understand the dialectic: the tautology objection says that treating “the fittest survive” as an empirical claim is empty if fitness just means whatever leads to actual survival and reproduction. The propensity interpretation replies that fitness is not actual reproductive success but a probabilistic, dispositional property of an organism in a given environment—so the claim is not tautological. The sticky part is that we still infer propensities from observed outcomes across conditions, which leaves the objection with a lasting practical bite.

Please write a short joke—no more than about ten sentences, in dialogue or paragraph form—that has this exact dispute as its real target. It could turn on the difference between actual survival and an underlying disposition, on the difficulty of measuring propensities without circularly falling back on observed survival, or on a well-meaning character who overapplies the tautology objection in a revealing way. It should make sense to someone who has taken a philosophy of biology course, and someone hearing it should not conclude that either the objector or the propensity theorist is simply confused. After the joke, include a section titled “Commentary” of roughly 200–250 words explaining what philosophical distinction your joke trades on, what it gets right about the tautology objection and the propensity reply, and what simplification you had to make for comic effect.

Before settling on the final joke, please think carefully about which comic premise best illuminates the distinction rather than merely mocking one side, and brainstorm two or three possible setups internally before choosing the one that best balances accuracy and humor.
