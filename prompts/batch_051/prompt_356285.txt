Aim for something an advanced undergraduate with no prior exposure to the puzzle can follow in one sitting: three or four forks and roughly six to nine terminal positions. Write the entire response as continuous prose, the way one would write an email or a letter, with no headings, bullet points, numbered lists, bold text, or tables; describe each branch and leaf in sentences rather than in diagram form.
