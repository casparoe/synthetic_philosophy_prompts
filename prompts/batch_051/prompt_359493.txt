I am a novelist writing a book set in a small invented Nordic country, and I want its political culture to read as a deliberate composite of its two real neighbors, Sweden and Norway. The problem is that my background reading gives me no stable polarity: some sources treat Sweden as the more liberal society, others treat Norway that way, and they seem to be measuring different things. Before I build characters and institutions around this contrast, I want to fix the polarity at the level of principle. Which of the two countries is, in fact, the more liberal one, and what single criterion should settle the question?
