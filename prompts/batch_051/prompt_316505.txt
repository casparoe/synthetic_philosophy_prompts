I'm a reporter on the science-and-ideas desk and I keep circling a piece about the old AI-safety proposal people call coherent extrapolated volition. The basic thought, as I understand it: rather than hand a very powerful system some explicit goal and hope it doesn't go sideways, you have it aim at what we as a species would want after we knew more, reasoned more carefully, worked through the places where our wants conflict, and were in general a bit more the people we'd prefer to be. Fans describe that as doing what we'd endorse after reflection; critics say it's laundering our current preferences through a flattering fantasy of improved selves. My editor wants the piece to commit to a bottom line, and I'd rather borrow someone's neck to stick out before I stick out mine.

So here's the claim I need a number on. Consider two future systems. System A is built to optimize for something like the coherent extrapolated volition described above: a reconciled envelope of idealized human volitions, not a poll of our present wants. System B is built to optimize for the single best explicit objective a realistic oversight group could write down at deployment time — say, some operationalized version of human flourishing. The claim is: System A is more likely than System B to bring about a future that we, judging from where we sit now with the values we actually have, would call morally good. I want your actual credence in that claim.

Don't just hand me a number — decompose it. How much of your credence rides on there being a determinate answer to the extrapolation at all, rather than it diverging or depending on arbitrary tuning choices? How much rides on the procedure tracking what we'd genuinely endorse after reflection, rather than being an artifact of the method? How much rides on "what we'd endorse after idealization" being a reliable guide to what's actually good? How much rides on it being feasible to point a real system at such an envelope without it drifting into edge-case loopholes or meeting the letter of the procedure while gutting its spirit? Give me a probability for each, show how they combine, and say which single sub-question your final number is most sensitive to. Then tell me what evidence or argument would move you most from wherever you landed. I know its a weird ask — I'm asking you to bet, not to summarize the debate.
