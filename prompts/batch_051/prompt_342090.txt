Consider the debate-based proposal for aligning advanced AI systems. In its central form, two equally capable systems argue opposite sides of a question before a human judge who can follow good arguments but cannot generate or fully verify them at the level of the systems. The safety claim is that truth nevertheless has a structural advantage: if one side asserts something false, the other side can produce a rebuttal simple and decisive enough for the judge to recognize, whereas a true claim has no comparable vulnerability.

Now introduce a specific development that is meant to cut against this. Suppose such systems can reliably construct obfuscated arguments: arguments designed so that their persuasive force is easy for a judge to feel but extraordinarily hard for a judge to audit. An obfuscated argument can be presented compactly and made to look like a complete proof or a crushing refutation, while its weak steps are buried in intricate dependencies whose examination would require far more time and expertise than the debate format allows. A false claim might then be defended by an obfuscated argument whose flaw the opposing system cannot locate and package into a short, judge-verifiable rebuttal; conversely, a true claim might admit only of an obfuscated defense that the judge cannot confidently certify.

Does the debate safety argument survive this possibility? If not, identify exactly which premise of the original argument the existence of obfuscated arguments undermines, and explain why that premise was doing essential work. Then consider whether the scheme can be repaired rather than abandoned, such as by requiring every argumentative move to be expressed as a formal, machine-checkable inference, by rewarding debaters for exposing flaws rather than for winning on persuasion, or by instructing the judge to withhold belief unless every step has been verified. State what the modified debate system would need to guarantee in order to recover even a limited version of the original safety claim, and assess whether that guarantee is plausibly achievable once the debaters are more capable than the judge.
