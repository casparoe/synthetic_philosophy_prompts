I treat their independent conclusions like votes. My reasoning is that if each person is more likely to be right than wrong, then the majority of several independent judgments should be more reliable than any single judgment, including the single sharpest person in the group. I have even sided with a bare majority of five thoughtful laypeople against one expert, on the principle that the power of the majority comes from the number of independent votes rather than the brilliance of any one voter.
