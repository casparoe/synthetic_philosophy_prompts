She thinks the residual weirdness is pragmatic — the sentence doesn't say something false, it just invites an unhelpful domain restriction if you're not careful. The other, Jonah, holds a kind-based view: generics attach a property to the kind itself (the species duck, the type mosquito), and truth depends on whether that property is characteristic or explanatory of the kind — laying eggs as a species, being a transmission vector for a pathogen — not on how many individual ducks or mosquitoes have it. For him, counting members and carving up domains is exactly the wrong way to explain why 'ducks lay eggs' is true but 'ducks are female' is false.
