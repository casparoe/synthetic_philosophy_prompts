As soon as you get sentences whose truth depends on relations between multiple quantified terms, it breaks down. Syllogistic has no way to keep those readings apart, because it can only treat each sentence as one subject term and one predicate term. Frege's fix was to give up the subject-predicate analysis and read sentences in terms of function and argument.
