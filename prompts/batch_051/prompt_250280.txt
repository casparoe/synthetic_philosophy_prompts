I keep going back and forth on where the line between treatment and enhancement is actualy supposed to fall. Here are five criteria people have used to draw it: (1) the intervention restores species-typical functioning; (2) it restores the person's own previous baseline; (3) it targets a recognized disease or deficit; (4) it levels the playing field rather than conferring a positional advantage; (5) it works through the same biological pathway the body normally uses. Now think about these cases: a student without ADHD taking Ritalin before an exam, a violinist taking propranolol for performance anxiety, a short-but-healthy kid given growth hormone, LASIK for mild nearsightedness, and a cochlear implant in a child born deaf. Rank the five criteria by how well they sort these cases into treatment vs enhancement, and say where even your top criterion breaks down. Don't give me a survey of the literature; I want your own ranking and reasoning.
