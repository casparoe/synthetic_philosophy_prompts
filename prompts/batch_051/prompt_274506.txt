I'm taking a grad seminar on rational choice and I've been going back and forth on the superrationality move. The setup I care about is the clean one: two agents face a one-shot symmetric Prisoner's Dilemma, identical preferences, and each knows the other is running exactly the same decision procedure on exactly the same information—same code, same priors, no private signals, no communication once the game starts. Causal decision theory says defect, because your act doesn't cause the other's act. Superrationality says cooperate, because your act is perfect evidence for what the twin will do, so you should choose as if you were choosing for both copies at once. I used to find that obviously right, but someone in class pointed out that symmetry is not a causal channel, and now its starting to feel like a sleight of hand. My own credence has settled around 0.35 that cooperate is the uniquely rational choice; a friend who loves symmetry arguments is at 0.8.

Give me your actual credence, not a survey of decision theories. By uniquely rational I mean the following: a fully informed ideal reasoner with no prior commitment to causal or evidential decision theory, after fully specified deliberation, would be making a mistake in the same sense that choosing a strictly dominated option is a mistake, if they did not cooperate. Now decompose it: how much rides on whether rationality is about producing good outcomes through your causal influence versus selecting the action you would want all symmetric copies of you to select; how much on whether the perfect symmetry is legitimate evidence in the absence of any causal link; how much on whether there's even a determinate fact of the matter about which move is uniquely rational in this setup; and any other component you think belongs. Give each term a rough probability and a sentence or two of justification, then show how you combined them. Then tell me which term your final number is most sensitive to, what argument or toy case would move that term most, and where my 0.35 and my friend's 0.8 are most likely diverging.
