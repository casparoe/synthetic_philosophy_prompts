The idea is this. Once a legendary figure or event gets admitted into serious historical discussion, the record starts bending around it. Absence of evidence becomes "we haven't dug the right place yet." Contradictory sources get harmonized into one biography instead of being left as competing stories. The more institutional mass a legend accumulates (scholarship, school curricula, national identity, tourism, a grave site you can visit), the stronger the gravitational pull, and at some point the burden of proof quietly flips: the legend no longer has to earn its way into the history books; the skeptic has to explain away the accumulated mass.
