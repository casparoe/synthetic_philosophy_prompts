I said if someone chooses A over B, B over C, but then C over A when they compare them two at a time, that person is just incoherent and ought to fix their rankings. She said real people end up like this all the time with messy multi-attribute choices—apartments, grad schools, jobs—and that the principle is what should give, not the case-by-case judgments. Neither of us could make the other feel the problem instead of just stating it.
