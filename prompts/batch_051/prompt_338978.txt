My criteria for new private net wealth: (1) it's an asset that can be spent or posted as collateral in the future, (2) it raises the present value of someone's future consumption possibilities, and (3) it isn't just a revaluation due to a change in the unit of account. Now take this case: the Federal Reserve creates $1 trillion in reserves and buys long-term Treasury bonds from a pension fund. Assume for now the price level doesn't move. Does the pension fund become wealthier? Does the private sector as a whole? Go criterion by criterion, say where the criteria underdetermine the answer, and tell me whether the case shows one of my three criteria is wrong or redundant. Answer in American English.
