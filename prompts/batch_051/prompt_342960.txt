Earlier this week I got into a long argument with a friend about whether introspection gives us access to our own minds that is privileged, direct, and therefore especially trustworthy. She ran through the familiar skeptical stock: confabulation experiments, choice blindness, split-brain rationalization, the way asking someone why they acted can produce an answer disconnected from what actually produced the act. I spent the evening defending a residual claim — that there is still a difference in kind between my access to my own mental states and yours — without being able to say exactly what that difference buys, epistemically.

The argument kept reminding me of something from software, and I cannot decide whether it is a deep structural point or an attractive false analogy. Debugging forces me to distinguish between what a program actually is doing — its machine state, control flow, memory — and what the program says it is doing in logs, error messages, and status codes. The program does have access to its own variables that no outside observer has, but the self-report is just another computed output, and the code that produces it may be causally distant from the code it describes. Log lines can misattribute a fault, go stale after a refactor, make a failure disappear by changing timing, or report a plausible story with no causal connection to the underlying path. The parallel I want to test is this: treat mental states as analogous to machine state, introspective reports as analogous to logging and error messages, privileged access as analogous to a program's ability to read its own variables, and confabulation as analogous to misleading diagnostics. Is the mapping exact enough to transfer results from one side to the other? In particular, the software case suggests that self-reports are reliable only when the reporting path is causally close to the target state and does not perturb it — is that the right condition for introspection too? Or does the analogy break in a way that reveals something important, perhaps because introspection is not a separate monitor reading a state but partly constitutes the state it reports? I want the parallel either made rigorous, or dismantled.
