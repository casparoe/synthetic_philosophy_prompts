I'm a PhD student in philosophy of physics, and I've increasingly come to hold a view I'll call scale pluralism. On this view, the empirical success of effective field theories and the renormalization group should be taken at face value: the world is genuinely organized into relatively autonomous scales, and the decoupling of high-energy details from low-energy phenomena is a real feature of nature rather than merely a convenient feature of our approximations. Consequently, I'm inclined to say there is no privileged fundamental description. The Standard Model is, on this picture, just the effective field theory adequate to the highest energy regimes we can currently probe, and its continuum fields are no more literal than the continuous stresses of elasticity theory.

I've defended this against reductionist friends, but I'm now trying to figure out what would actually move me off it. Tell me what would count against scale pluralism. I want the evidence, arguments, and formal results that should most disturb someone in this position, sorted by how decisive they would be. In particular: what could experiments plausibly show, or have arguably already shown, that would embarrass the pluralist and favor the reductionist? Are there formal results in quantum field theory or statistical mechanics that my view is committed to, or that would be surprising on my view but natural on reductionism? For my part, I suspect the strongest case against me is the possibility that decoupling is a contingent, almost lucky feature of our current models rather than a deep structural fact about the world, so I'd like that line of objection developed in its strongest form rather than merely waved at. Please also say which defeaters a committed scale pluralist would themselves concede as counting against them, which ones they should dismiss, and whether the view has been qualified into unfalsifiability—and, if so, whether that insulation is a vice or simply what one should expect of a metaphysical reading of a scientific framework.

Please answer as a numbered list of exactly five items, each a sentence or two, with no introduction or conclusion. Be committed: I'd rather you rank a definite five and defend the ranking than survey the space of possible objections.
