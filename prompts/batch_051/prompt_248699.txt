Every so often I encounter a solution to the two-envelope problem that works for the original setup and then falls apart as soon as the two amounts are drawn from anything other than an open-ended, completely unspecified range. Before I read another one, I want the specification it would have to meet. Write the requirements any complete account of the two-envelope problem must satisfy, with the case or consideration behind each. At minimum it should have to say exactly where the familiar switching argument goes wrong; how it handles the apparent symmetry between the two envelopes before either is opened; what is being assumed when someone says the other envelope is equally likely to contain double or half; and how the account behaves in variants where the amounts come from a known or capped set rather than an unknown one. Mark each requirement as non-negotiable or as a preference particular to one approach, and then say which single requirement rules out the most proposed solutions and why.
