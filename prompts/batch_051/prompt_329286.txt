I work as a software engineer and have been reading about chaos theory outside work, mostly popular treatments rather than philosophy. I’ve ended up with a cluster of views I’d like audited as a set. Here is where I’ve landed:

(a) The universe can be fully deterministic at the physical level — a complete state plus the laws fixes every later state — while many real systems are still “unpredictable” in practice because outcomes depend exponentially on small differences in initial conditions that no finite measurement could resolve.

(b) I take Lorenz’s 1963 work on deterministic nonperiodic flow to show that this unpredictability is a fact about the world itself, not merely about our measuring instruments or our ignorance: it reveals something real about the geometry of the state space.

(c) For practical purposes — weather forecasting, turbulence, market behavior — I want to define “unpredictable” operationally: a system is unpredictable for an agent when no physically realizable computation by that agent could beat some specified baseline before the event occurs.

(d) I’m comfortable accepting determinism while denying it has any bearing on free will, because I take free will to be about what agents would do under relevant counterfactual conditions, not about whether the actual course of events was settled in advance.

(e) When I simulate chaotic systems at work, even with exactly specified initial conditions, floating-point roundoff makes the simulation diverge from the model’s true trajectory — and I take this as strong evidence that in the real world unpredictability is inescapable.

Which of these are actually in tension, which only look like they are, and what is the least costly revision? Please rank the tensions by severity and be specific about where the pressure is. I especially want to know whether (b) and (c) are in real conflict or just use two different senses of “unpredictable,” and whether (e) genuinely supports (a) or (b), or is just an artifact of how I implemented the simulation. Commit to a definite view about what, if anything, chaos theory shows about the difference between determinism and predictability.
