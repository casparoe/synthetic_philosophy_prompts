Counterfactual: Mars had its own independent abiogenesis at roughly the same time as Earth, and by 2050 we find the fossils. The Martian organisms use mirror-image amino acids (the opposite handedness from Earth life), so contamination is ruled out. Meanwhile, life on Earth appeared almost as soon as the surface was habitable, and everywhere else we can look — today's Mars, icy moons, nearby exoplanet atmospheres — we see nothing beyond simple microbial-grade biology and no technosignatures.
