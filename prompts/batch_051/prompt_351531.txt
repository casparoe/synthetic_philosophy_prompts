I help run a smallish online forum that uses upvotes and a karma score, and ive watched something odd happen over a couple of years. When we started, the score roughly tracked who actually knew what they were talking about. Now the number itself seems to do the work instead of the knowledge. New members defer to old accounts purely because of the score next to the name, even when those accounts are talking way outside what they know, and the high-score people start believing their own number and expect to be agreed with. Meanwhile the regulars have worked out what gets upvoted fastest: short confident one-liners, posting early in a thread, telling the majority what it already wants to hear. Some of them now farm the score in ways that have nothing to do with being right or helpful. We even had people delete a post the moment it went negative and repost the same thing the next day so it wouldnt sit on their record. It feels like the measure has swallowed the thing it was measuring. Is there a name for this? And if not, can you name and define it so its clearly different from just people gaming a system, and from the score simply becoming a status symbol? I care less about the label than about the edges: where does using the number as a rough shortcut end and the number actually running the place begin?
