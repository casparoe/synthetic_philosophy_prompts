A friend in my AI safety reading group keeps pushing a line like this: RL already gives us the ingredients of suffering, because reward just is the valence signal — positive reward plays the role of pleasure, negative reward the role of pain — so a sophisticated agent taking a large negative reward is, at least in principle, in a morally relevant state. Say which disanalogies matter for the moral conclusion and which dont. Finally, say whether the argument survives once those disanalogies are on the table, and if not, what a better analogy would be, if any.
