(1) envy is a vice, full stop: it's a hostile attitude toward someone else's good as such, not toward a wrong they did me, and it would be a defect of character even when the inequality that occasions it is unjust.

(3) still, I think it's a mistake to moralize at people in badly unjust circumstances who feel what sure looks like envy; the grosser the background inequality, the more excusable and less blameworthy the feeling is.

(5) but I'm also committed to the idea that complaints of injustice should be assessable on their merits without reference to the complainant's emotional state, so you can't just infer a complaint is well-founded from the fact that a lot of people are envious about the same thing.
