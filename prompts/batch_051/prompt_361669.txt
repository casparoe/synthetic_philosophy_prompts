- Someone with no interest in exercise, no worries about health, and no concern for his future self: no reason to exercise.
- Someone who genuinely wants to become a good violinist but has no desire to practice: a reason to practice.
- Someone who doesn't care about social harmony, other people's good opinion, or courtesy for its own sake: no reason to be polite to a stranger.
- A mother who loves her child and wants him to flourish, but who hasn't yet realized he's in danger in the next room: a reason to go check on him.
