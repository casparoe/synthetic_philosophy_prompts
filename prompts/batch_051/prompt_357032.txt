So I'm halfway sold on the idea that good moral thinking is just looking carefully at each case instead of applying a code.

Is the intuition just a leftover from how I was raised, the kind of thing particularism can explain away or debunk, or is it tracking something real that particularism (at least the strong version) gets wrong?
