An adversarial collaboration is being organized between two positions on cultural evolution. The replicationist holds that cultural variants—melodies, phrases, skills, rituals—are particulate replicators: they spread because they are copied with enough fidelity for selection to act on variant lineages, so the term "meme" is a literal causal-explanatory term rather than a metaphor. The reconstructionist holds that most cultural transmission is low-fidelity and inferential: what is transmitted is not the item itself but cues from which receivers reconstruct a version, so a cultural form's observed stability is usually convergence on a psychological or ecological attractor rather than faithful copying. On this view, "replication" and the gene–meme analogy systematically mislead.

Write the joint statement these two camps should be able to sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration. The document should contain:

(1) a numbered section of claims both accept, covering the stylized facts of cultural transmission and stability that any account must explain, as well as the data both sides agree are relevant;

(2) a disagreements section in which each residual disagreement is first stated in neutral wording and then restated in wording each side would accept for its own position;

(3) a list of cruxes—sub-questions whose resolution would move at least one side. Examine my two candidate cruxes and refine or replace them where needed: (a) whether high-fidelity copying occurs often enough outside writing and recording to bear explanatory weight, and (b) whether reconstruction from shared human psychology can drive cumulative cultural adaptation rather than mere convergence;

(4) for each crux, a concrete case, diffusion-chain design, or field comparison that both sides would accept as bearing on it, with each side's prediction recorded in advance.

Conclude by separating any part of the dispute that is only verbal—for example, different uses of "copy" or "fidelity"—from the parts that would remain alive once that is set aside.
