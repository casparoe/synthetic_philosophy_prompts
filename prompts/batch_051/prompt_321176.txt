In a tangled hierarchy—think of Escher's Drawing Hands, where each level draws the level that draws it—an "inviolate level" is whatever level you hold fixed so the loop doesn't collapse into paradox. Please argue that the inviolate level is always a pragmatic modeling choice: it marks where the analyst has stopped asking for an explanation, not where the system really bottoms out, and theres no fact of the matter about which level is "really" inviolate. Part of the job is to defuse the rival intuition that a system with no genuinely inviolate level would be incoherent.
