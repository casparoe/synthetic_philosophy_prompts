3. A four-sided triangle.
4. A philosophical zombie: molecule-for-molecule identical to me, acts exactly like me, but has no conscious experience at all.
5. Traveling back in time and killing your own grandfather before your parent is conceived.
6. A world with nothing at all in it — no objects, no space, no time.

Give me the definition, run it through all six, and tell me which verdict you're least confident about. Then say whether the definition ends up strict enough to block the trick cases but still permissive enough for conceivability to actually be a guide to possibility.
