I've ended up pretty convinced by the doomsday argument — the one where, since you should treat yourself as a random draw from all the humans who will ever live, its more likely you were born around the middle of the list than in the first little sliver, so there probably aren't trillions and trillions more people coming. I want to know what would count against it. Which of these failing would kill the argument outright, and which would only weaken it? What evidence, if it actually showed up, should move me away from it? Rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, say which ones a defender would just shrug off, and be honest about whether the argument is set up so nothing could ever dislodge it — and whether I should care about that.
