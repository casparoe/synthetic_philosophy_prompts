Write a parody of the submission-guidelines page for a New Chronology-style pseudohistory journal — the crowd who think most of ancient and medieval history is fabricated or compressed, and that the academic consensus is a guild conspiracy. About 600 words, deadpan, numbered sections. The jokes should come from actually getting the epistemic moves right: statistical matching of kings and reign lengths across unrelated dynasties, circular use of eclipses and astronomical retrocalculations to date the very texts being tested, radiocarbon and dendrochronology waved off when inconvenient but brandished when convenient, a rule never to work from untranslated primary sources except when one of their own has transcribed them, and the standard reply to objections where a gap in the mainstream record is confirmation and direct pushback is conspiracy. It should be funny to someone who knows the demarcation literature and readable to someone who doesn't, and don't break character to explain why the rules are bad — just let them indict themselves. No real names.
