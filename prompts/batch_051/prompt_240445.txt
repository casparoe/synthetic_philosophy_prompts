(a) There are natural kinds in physics and chemistry (electron, gold), but probably not in biology or psychiatry.
(b) What makes something a natural kind is that it supports reliable induction and explanation better than arbitrary groupings do.

(f) Asking whether 'jade' is a natural kind is a bad question, because the word covers jadeite and nephrite, two different minerals that just look similar, and that superficial interest-relative grouping shouldn't be doing metaphysical work.
