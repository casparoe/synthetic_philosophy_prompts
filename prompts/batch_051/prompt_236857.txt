Van Fraassen's Reflection principle says my current credence in a proposition should equal my expected future credence in it. Since future-me will usually have quite a bit more evidence than I do, this sounds like a rule of deference to a better-informed future self. So suppose I know that after the talk I'll end up with credence .8 in the speaker's thesis, and I also know I'll have misremembered part of the Q&A and be running on four hours of sleep. Reflection seems to tell me to just set my current credence to .8 anyway, since future-me has the extra evidence. But actual deference to a person who's sleep-deprived and misremembering would be crazy — so what exactly is Reflection supposed to be a requirement of?
