(2) It proves that I am essentially a thinking substance, and that my body is not part of that essence.
(3) It doesn't prove that external objects or other minds exist.
(4) The I that is proved is identical with my conscious states, or anyway my conscious states are the only things about me that the proof actually reaches.
(5) The cogito is an inference from 'there is thinking going on' to 'I exist', not a direct non-inferential intuition.
(6) The proof only holds while I'm actually thinking; when I'm not thinking, the certainty lapses.

I genuinely don't see which of these are doing the work together and which are leaning on each other in ways they shouldn't. Could you go through them and tell me which pairs are in tension, which just look like they're in tension but dissolve on closer inspection, and rank the tensions by severity?
