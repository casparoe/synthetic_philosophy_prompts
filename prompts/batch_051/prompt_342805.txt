I keep flip-flopping on what "language" actually amounts to. I'm not after a dictionary gloss; I want a working definition that gets the following cases right and gives a definate yes/no for the messy ones:

- a fluent back-and-forth in ASL
- a toddler pointing at a dog and grunting
- a parrot trained to say "Polly wants a cracker" when it sees a cracker
- written English, including this sentence
- a home sign system a deaf kid invents on their own with hearing parents who never learn sign
- the sign language that emerged among deaf kids in Nicaragua over a few cohorts
- the honeybee waggle dance
- Python code
- whistled Turkish
- a string of emojis like 🔥🔥🐶

I'm confident ASL and written English are language, and I'm confident the parrot and Python aren't — or at least that if you call Python a language, it's a different sense. The toddler, home sign, the bee dance, and emojis I genuinely don't know how to sort. Propose the definition, run it through all ten, say which verdicts are least secure, and tell me where the definition forces you to say something that sounds wrong.
