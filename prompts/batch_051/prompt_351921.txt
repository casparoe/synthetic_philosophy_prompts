For a short position paper I am drafting on the direction of time, I would like my commitments checked before I circulate it. Here is the view I have ended up with:

1. The fundamental dynamical laws are time-symmetric; they do not distinguish past from future. The observed arrow of time—entropy increasing toward the future—is not in the laws but derives from a boundary condition: the universe began in an extraordinarily low-entropy state.

2. That same entropy gradient explains the asymmetry of records: why we have memories, fossils, and other traces of the past but not of the future. Forming or retaining a record is an entropy-increasing process, and such processes are overwhelmingly likely to run away from the low-entropy past.

3. Causation, by contrast, is a strongly objective, mind-independent asymmetry: causes precede their effects, and no event can cause an earlier event. This is a fact about the world itself, not a projection of our knowledge or our limitations.

4. The right account of causal asymmetry is interventionist: X causes Y just in case an unconfounded manipulation of X by an agent would change Y, while no corresponding manipulation of Y would change X.

5. In a universe whose entropy decreased toward the future, there would still be observers with a coherent epistemic arrow: they would form records in whichever direction their entropy gradient permits, and would rightly count those records as traces of their past. Their epistemic and causal arrows would point in what we would call the opposite direction.

6. The psychological arrow—the felt passage of time—is derivative: it is a byproduct of the thermodynamic and epistemic asymmetries and gives no independent reason to posit an objective flow.

I do not have a worked-out view of where the strain is. I would like an audit: which pairs of these commitments are in genuine tension, how severe each tension is, which pairs are only apparently in conflict and why the appearance dissolves, and what the least costly revision would be and what it would force me to give up. Please rank the tensions rather than treating them all as equal, and write the answer in American English.
