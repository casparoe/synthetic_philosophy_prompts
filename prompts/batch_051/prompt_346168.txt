(d) Standard Newcomb's problem but the boxes are transparent and one contains an IOU for the other.

I want you to rank these four variants from most to least instructive for someone trying to understand why "I should pay only because my current decision might cause the predictor to have predicted I'd pay" fails as a causal mode. Also, give each variant a score from 0 to 1 for how much it actually makes the problem cleaner rather than just adding clutter.
