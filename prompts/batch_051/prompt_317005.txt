I want a reusable decision procedure, not advice about one campaign. Then run the checklist on three candidate practices: (1) a campaign to get people to stop idling their engines at school pickup; (2) a campaign to get people to replace part of their lawn with native plants; and (3) a campaign to get neighbors to check in on elderly residents during heat waves.
