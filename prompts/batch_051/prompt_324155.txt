My friend has spent the last year trying to figure out which of her values she treats as worth having for their own sake and which are only tools, and I've been keeping score. Her track record so far:

- Truth-seeking about practical stuff (say, whether a job offer is legit): she says it matters only because it guides decisions, and if a false belief would make her act better and hurt nobody, she'd take the false belief.
- Truth about whether her oldest friend is quietly turning people against her: she wants to know even if nothing about what she could do would change, and she says the truth matters for itself there.
- Her family's friday-night dinner ritual: she'd keep it even if all the togetherness, continuity, and comfort disappeared; she says the ritual itself just feels weighty.
- Her private sandwich habit of always saving the center bite for last, since childhood: she calls it a harmless routine and says it has no value beyond the mild comfort.
- Purity rules from her upbringing around food: she follows them only for her mom, and she says if her mom died she'd drop them tomorrow because the disgust left years ago.
- A dead stranger's sweater, fully cleaned and safe: she won't wear it, and insists the feeling of contamination is itself a reason, not any actual risk.

Our current fight: she's the last person who knows the slightly off-key birthday song her grandparents sang. She says it does nothing for her emotionally and doesn't make her feel close to them, but it's 'just hers' and she hates the idea of it vanishing. I genuinly can't tell if she'll say preserving it is intrinsically valuable or just sentimental hoarding.

Mostly I want a prediction: will she file the song under intrinsic or instrumental? But I'm also trying to reverse-engineer the rule she's running, the other rule that fits her track record just as well, how each rule comes down on the birthday song, and what further case would cleanly sort out which rule it is. And does her whole 'for its own sake' distinction track something real, or is she just post-hoc rationalizing gut feelings?
