Then a "Where we part ways" section that states each view in wording that side would accept — no strawmen. Then two or three cruxes: cases, arguments, or facts that would actually move one side or the other, with each side's prediction recorded in advance. Finish by saying whether any part of the dispute looks merely verbal, and if so which. Begin with the document itself, no preface.
