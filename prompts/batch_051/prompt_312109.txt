In my formal epistemology seminar we have been working through a variant of the Judy Benjamin problem, and I would like to see how well a prediction can be made before the fact. The target has four exhaustive, mutually exclusive states: Red-hills, Red-valley, Blue-hills, and Blue-valley. A paratrooper—let us keep calling her Judy—begins with a prior distribution over these four cells and then receives a single radio message from a source she regards as fully reliable.

One of my colleagues has a precise methodology for such cases. She first asks which constraints the incoming message imposes on her posterior, and then selects the posterior distribution that minimizes Kullback-Leibler divergence from the prior—the standard information-theoretic measure of how far one distribution revises another—subject to exactly those constraints. Her track record on small cases is this. (1) Faced with the plain message "You are not in Red-valley" and prior (0.2, 0.2, 0.3, 0.3), she set Red-valley to zero and renormalized the other three cells, giving (0.25, 0, 0.375, 0.375). (2) Faced with a conditional message when her prior already had P(Red-valley)=0—prior (0.4, 0, 0.2, 0.4)—she said the message imposed no new constraints and left the distribution unchanged. (3) Faced with the statistical report "There is a 60 percent chance you are in Red territory" and a uniform prior, she imposed the single constraint P(Red)=0.6 and preserved the ratios within Red and within Blue, yielding (0.3, 0.3, 0.2, 0.2).

Now for the new case. Judy's prior is uniform—(0.25, 0.25, 0.25, 0.25)—and the reliable message says, "If you are in Red territory, then you are in the hills." My colleague has not yet told us how she extracts constraints from a substantive conditional message of this kind, and we need to predict her answer. I am less interested in the bare numbers than in the reasoning: what constraint sets does her methodology leave open for this conditional message, what posterior distribution does each candidate constraint set produce, which is the best reading of her method in light of the three earlier cases, and what further message or prior would most cleanly separate the candidates that remain.
