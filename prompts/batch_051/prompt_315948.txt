Can you give me a workable definition of a "quota" in the affirmative-action sense? I want one that counts Norway's board rule -- at least 40% of each sex, enforced with real sanctions -- as a quota, and counts a US-style holistic admissions policy where race is considered as one factor among several with no numerical target as not one. Then I want definate answers for: India's reservations of roughly half of public-sector seats for SC/ST/OBC groups (presumably yes?), a university with a soft internal target of 8% Black enrollment that quietly moves the admit line to hit it (I keep flip-flopping on this one), a rule that every hiring committee must interview at least one underrepresented candidate per opening, a tie-breaker that prefers an underrepresented candidate only when candidates are otherwise equally qualified, and a government set-aside of 20% of contracts for minority-owned firms. It feels like the real line is supposed to be fixed number versus flexible goal, but the soft-target case makes me doubt that distinction is doing any work. Give me the definition, run it against each case, and say whether you think the word is mostly doing descriptive work or just pejorative work at this point.
