A proposal is circulating for constitutional reform: a permanent second chamber of two hundred citizens chosen by lot each year from the adult population, with service compulsory and paid at the median national income. This chamber may not introduce or amend legislation. Its only power is that, within thirty days of a bill passing the elected chamber, any quarter of its members may force a vote in the chamber on whether to refer the bill to the electorate; if a majority votes to do so, the bill goes to a binding national referendum within three months. The chamber's votes are public, but its deliberations are closed to everyone but its members. Walk through how this changes the behavior of the elected chamber, of parties and interest groups, and of the members selected by lot. Where does the design get gamed first, and are those failure modes fixable without abandoning the underlying idea?
