The same bet somehow looks great on average and terrible for almost everyone who actually takes it.

(2) Expected value tells you the average outcome across a large group of people each taking the bet once.
(3) Therefore, if each repetition of a bet has positive expected value, a person who takes the bet many times should, on average, end up richer over time.
(4) On each repetition of this coin-flip bet, the expected value is positive.
(5) Therefore, a person who takes this coin-flip bet many times should end up richer over time.
