When I look at a red apple, I seem to be aware of exactly one unified visual experience. But Dennett's multiple drafts model says this apparent singleness is not what it seems: the brain is always producing many parallel drafts of what is happening, and no single draft is privileged as the one I am conscious of. If none of the drafts is privileged, which draft is the experience I am actually having? And if the model says that question is ill-posed, doesn't that amount to denying that there is any fact about what my experience is right now?
