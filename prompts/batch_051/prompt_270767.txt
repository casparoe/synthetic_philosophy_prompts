Does that structure vindicate reductionism, dramatize anti-reductionism's defeater structure, or resist both labels? Give me a reading that is faithful to both the film and the philosophical debate: point to specific moments or choices by the jurors, say where the film complicates an easy epistemological moral, and conclude with what the film adds to the philosophical exchange that argument alone does not capture.
