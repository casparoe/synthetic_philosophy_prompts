I keep seeing the view that when someone criticizes me for something they do themselves, the criticism is somehow defective — that the hypocrite "has no standing." But I don't get why the speaker's record matters to whether the moral claim itself is true. If lying is wrong, my friend's saying "you shouldn't have lied" after he lied all last week doesn't make the proposition false. So isn't the whole hypocrisy discussion just a sophisticated version of an ad hominem?
