I teach a 200-level class on decision theory and free will, and I'm planning a session on the weirdness of trying to assign probabilities to your own choices while you're still deciding. The idea I want them to bump into before I name it is something like: when I'm deliberating between A and B, any probability I put on "I'll do A" doesn't stay a neutral forecast. If I write 80%, then either it starts functioning as a resolution or commitment (and I make myself hit the number), or I use a die to keep myself honest, which means I've stopped deliberating and started randomizing. There doesn't seem to be a stable spot where I both genuinely decide and genuinely predict.
