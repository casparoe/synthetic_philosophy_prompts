I got into a long argument at work about whether generational categories — Boomers, Millennials, Gen Z — are real or just branding. Whenever I push back, someone says the boundaries are fuzzy but the kinds are real, "like biological species." And I've ended up more or less arguing that side myself, more by accident than by conviction: that generations are real social kinds, not merely arbitrary birth-year ranges some marketer picked, even though the exact cutoffs are obviously drawn somewhere for convenience. I want to stress-test this before I keep repeating it.
