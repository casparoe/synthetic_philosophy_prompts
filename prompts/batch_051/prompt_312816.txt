I am a retired natural scientist who came to philosophy late, and I find I trust arguments most when they make their mechanisms explicit and tell me how much confidence to place in each step. Here is a counterfactual I want you to work through carefully.

Consider a counterfactual history. Beginning in 1920, in a large Western industrial country—call it State A—a widely adopted method of infant care eliminates the psychological condition Freud held responsible for the earliest psychological stratum of religion: the infant's experience of helplessness in its own dependency, and the resulting longing for a protective father. The method is introduced in 1920 and is effectively universal by 1930; whether it is medical, administrative, or a change in family practice is irrelevant to the scenario. Children in State A are no less physically dependent than before and still form normal attachments; what they lack is the specific feeling of being at the mercy of a world that will not respond to them, and they do not magnify the father into a protector. Nothing else about human nature changes, and the change is not exported to neighbouring states.

On Freud's account of religion as an illusion that repeats and transforms this childhood predicament, predict how religious belief, religious practice, and the social role of religion in State A diverge from its neighbours over the following three generations. I want reasoning, not narrative. Distinguish the strands of Freud's account that your prediction relies on: the developmental claim about infantile helplessness; the functional claim about religion managing the renunciations that civilization demands; the claim that beliefs produced by wish-fulfilment are unreliable irrespective of their truth; and the social-transmission claim that institutions carry religious ideas long after their psychological origin has faded. Show which effects would follow even if the functional and social-transmission strands operated exactly as they do today, and where the prediction depends on Freud's more specific developmental hypothesis. Say also how the religious institutions of State A and their professional cadres respond, given their incentives and constraints, and identify the outcomes that would show the prediction had gone wrong if they obtained. For each main step, state how confident you are and why; end by singling out the point you are least sure of.
