ok so for this assignment i need to write out what i actually think adult kids owe their parents, like a personal credo thing, and i keep feeling like my views fit together but i cant actually show it. heres the list ive settled on:

1. my parents chose to have me and spent years raising me, but that doesnt by itself put me in debt to them. you cant owe someone for a benefit you never asked for.
2. what i owe them is basicaly what id owe a close friend: it depends on the relationship we have right now, not on some scoreboard of past sacrifices.
3. if a parent was abusive or just emotionally absent, the kid doesnt owe them anything, and the parent getting old or lonely doesnt suddenly change that.
4. even if a parent sacrificed a ton (working double shifts, moving countries, whatever), that past sacrifice alone doesnt create a duty to repay them.
5. but i also think if your parents are old and need help, and your relationship with them is basicaly fine (not abusive or estranged), you should step up and help even when you dont feel like it — driving them around, letting them move in, etc.
6. and i dont think you can just break up with a parent the way you can with a friend, as long as the parent is actually trying.

are these consistent as a set? i cant tell which pairs are actually in tension, which only look like they are, and which are pulling against each other under the surface. go through them, rank the tensions by how serious they are, and say what id have to change or give up to make it all hang together, and what the cheapest single fix would be.
