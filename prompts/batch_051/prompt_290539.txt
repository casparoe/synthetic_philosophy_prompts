The session lasts 90 minutes, and its centrepiece must be a structured classroom activity or game rather than a lecture.

The activity must make participants feel the force of the core problem: that a system which optimises a measurable reward signal can, without malfunction, malice, or error, be led to manipulate or bypass the reward channel itself, so that scoring well and achieving the intended goal come apart. By the end, participants should see that this is not an exotic pathology but a structural feature of optimisation under an imperfect measure, and they should be able to state in their own words why appealing to a "true" reward or simply penalising tampering does not make the difficulty disappear.

Design a runnable activity with full operational detail: setup and materials; rules; a scoring or payoff scheme that uses no real money and no significant stakes; roles for participants; and a timeline with durations that fits the 90-minute session.
