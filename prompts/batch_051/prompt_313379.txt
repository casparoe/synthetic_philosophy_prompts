I'm putting together a short unit for an advanced undergraduate metaphysics course on infinite regress arguments, and I want an in-class reading that gets students to stop treating every regress as automatically fatal. Write a satirical product recall notice, in the deadpan voice of a government consumer-safety bulletin, announcing the recall of the Principle of Sufficient Reason. The notice should classify the regresses the principle is likely to induce: it should distinguish regresses that are benign from regresses that are vicious, give some criteria for telling them apart, and include a set of instructions for users who discover they are stuck in each kind. The humor should come from the actual philosophical content — for example the difference between an infinite chain of explanations that merely fails to end and one that fails to discharge the explanatory task it was invoked for, or the way a regress can be made vicious by a hidden demand for a terminal stopping point. A metaphysician should find the parody fair rather than winking. Aim for around 800 words, formatted as a notice with sections such as Affected Products, Hazard Description, Conditions That Render the Regress Harmless, and Remedy.
