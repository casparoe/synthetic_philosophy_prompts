I went down a rabbit hole arguing with a biologist friend about whether the gene's-eye view of evolution is a literal causal story or just a bookeeping trick that happens to be convenient. I lean toward the critic side: multilevel selection, developmental systems theory, organisms as genuine units, and the worry that genes are not "for" traits in any clean sense.

Write the joint statement these two camps could both sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration. You are an expert in evolutionary biology and philosophy of biology; before you write, think carefully and maybe brainstorm a few phrasings, because the entire skill is finding statements that are concrete enough to matter but not slanted toward either side. Do not include citations or a bibliography, and don't lean on specific papers or authors beyond what I named.
