A reward-seeking RL agent that emits distress-like signals when I block its goal: that feels like a design feature, not suffering. A whole-brain emulation of a person: that basically counts, close to the person. A simple optimizer, like a thermostat-like system that gets an error signal when the room deviates from 20 degrees: nothing there worth protecting. My verdicts seem to track something like fidelity to a real nervous system, not behavior or goal-directedness, but the first case keeps bugging me.
