so I stayed up way too late arguing with a friend about which seafood it's actually okay to eat, and I realized my gut is all over the place. I'm trying to see if there's a principle underneath my verdicts or if I'm just rationalizing after the fact. here are the cases.

- Boiling a live lobster at home because a recipe says to: not okay, I won't do it, it feels indecent.
- Ordering lobster at a restaurant where it's sat in a tank with its claws banded and then gets boiled: also not okay, maybe worse.
- Eating farmed Atlantic salmon: fine. I know the pens get crowded and sea lice are a whole thing, but I eat it without much guilt.
- Eating raw oysters: completely fine. I can't take seriously that an oyster has a life worth worrying about.
- Eating farmed shrimp: not okay. I read about broodstock getting an eyestalk cut off to make them spawn, and that one detail bothers me more than the entire salmon setup, which doesn't seem rational.
- Eating mussels: fine, same as oysters.
- Eating farmed trout that was packed on ice and suffocated without any stunning: I'm queasy about it, but I'd probably still eat it. That's basically the same death as the farmed salmon, so I don't know why it sits worse, but it does.
- Eating salmon that was stunned with a quick blow to the head before bleeding out: fine, better than fine. I'd pay extra and choose it every time.

The pair I keep going back and forth on: I won't boil one lobster in my own kitchen, but I've stood at a crawfish boil where someone dumped a sack of live farmed crawfish into the pot and I ate them and didn't flinch much. That look like the same act at different scale, so one of those verdicts has to be wrong.

My friend's theory: it's about whether the pain is quick and whether the killing happens up close. But that doesn't get the shrimp, or the farmed salmon. My other thought: maybe I only care when the suffering is vivid to me, and that's just a bias, not a principle. If I set that bias aside, what principle captures the rest of the cases? And what does it say about farmed octopus, which I genuinely cant settle? If the principle has to push back on some of my verdicts, which ones, and should I let it?
