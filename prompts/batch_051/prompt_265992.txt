I have been working through Brandom's inferentialism, and I keep getting stuck on what exactly it means to grasp a concept. In his book Making It Explicit, Brandom introduces a diagnostic case of a parrot that has been trained to respond to red things by uttering the sound "That's red." The parrot becomes a reliable differential responder to red objects, yet Brandom denies that the parrot has mastered the concept red, because its response is not caught up in the inferential moves a human speaker can make with the word red. He uses this to motivate the view that the meaning of an expression is its inferential role, and that understanding is a kind of practical mastery of that role.

I want a working definition of conceptual understanding or grasping a meaning that can actually adjudicate these cases. Propose a definition, then run it through the following test cases and say which side each falls on, or whether the case is genuinely borderline: (1) a human toddler who says red while pointing at red and green things indiscriminately but will answer yes when asked if a fire engine is red; (2) a non-native speaker who knows the dictionary definition of gravitation and can use the word in grammatically correct sentences, but cannot tell you whether heavier things fall faster or why the moon stays in orbit; (3) a thermostat whose electrical state is reliably correlated with ambient temperature; (4) a large language model that, in a single forward pass, correctly answers inference questions about the word entropy, including how it applies to black holes and to the Second Law; (5) a person with dense amnesia who can no longer recall learning the word mitochondria but can still accurately describe what mitochondria do and distinguish them from other organelles; and (6) a chess-playing program that can say knight forks are forks and can name the squares involved in a specific fork, but cannot explain why a fork is a threat. I suspect some of these cases split apart different components of understanding—reliable differential response, inferential articulation, practical know-how, and something like normative status. I want the definition to force those apart and then say which combination is required for the Brandomian claim that grasping a concept is mastering its inferential role. Tell me where your definition surprises you, and where you have to revise it to keep the parrot out while letting the amnesic patient in.
