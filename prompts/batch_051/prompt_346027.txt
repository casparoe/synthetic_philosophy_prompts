Consider the following system. A language-model-based agent is trained to produce moral evaluations: it accepts descriptions of situations and choices, and returns verdicts of right and wrong, apportions blame and praise, and supplies justifications. A hard architectural constraint prevents any of these outputs from being acted on. The system has no effectors, its evaluations do not feed back into its own training or sampling, and no downstream human defers to them; the channel through which its verdicts are produced is discarded immediately. It can judge that an action is wrong, but that judgment cannot cause so much as a change in the probability of its own next token. Before you take a position on whether this system is a moral agent, write the desiderata that a theory of moral agency must meet in order to be adequate to settling the question. List each requirement, with the case or consideration that motivates it, and mark which requirements are non-negotiable constraints on any theory and which are commitments of particular camps. The list should be able to handle, among others: an ordinary adult who judges correctly and acts; a locked-in person who judges but cannot move; an agent acting under duress; an akratic person who judges one way and acts another; a bystander who judges that rescue is required but is physically prevented from intervening; a corporation that issues ethical statements nobody implements; a thermostat whose state can be interpreted as detecting a norm violation; and a teenager whose advice is systematically ignored. Do not collapse the distinctions between the capacity to form moral judgments, the capacity to translate judgment into intention, the capacity to execute an intention, and susceptibility to practices of holding answerable or accountable; and do not collapse moral agency into moral patiency. Then say which single requirement does the most work in excluding the major candidate theories, and score briefly but specifically how attributability, answerability, accountability, and reasons-responsiveness views fare against your list. Finally, flag any pair of requirements you suspect cannot be jointly satisfied, and state which resolution of that tension you would defend and why.
