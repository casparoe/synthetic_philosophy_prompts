I keep seeing Galen Strawson's Basic Argument glossed as: to be morally responsible for what you do, you'd have to be responsible for who you are, and to be responsible for who you are you'd have to have made yourself from scratch, which is impossible, so nobody can be truly responsible. What I don't get is why this is treated as having any force against libertarians. If your action isn't fully determined by your character and prior causes, then at the moment of decision you can sort of step outside the causal stream and be the genuine author of the act, and that seems to break the regress. So why do people present the Basic Argument as working whether or not determinism is true? It seems to me it only bites if you already think you couldn't of done otherwise.
