Section 4(c) of the covenants says: "No owner shall use the lot for any activity creating a risk of fire, explosion, or falling objects greater than that incidental to normal single-family residential use, except with the prior written approval of the owners of all lots that could foreseeably be affected." A resident is a high-power rocketry hobbyist. On Memorial Day weekend he's launched a couple of Class 2 rockets from his backyard, which backs onto four other yards. One misfired last year and landed in a neighbor's pool. The engines can ignite on the pad and throw burning propellant, and even an ordinary recovery failure could put a several-pound rocket through a roof, though the actual odds are very low. He says its no worse than the guy two streets over grilling on a wooden deck. Is he violating 4(c)? Walk through what the words, the purpose, what the drafters would have said if asked, and what a reasonable reader in the neighborhood would understand, each give. If they diverge, which reading should govern?
