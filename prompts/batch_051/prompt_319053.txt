I looked up rough figures: a micromort is a one-in-a-million chance of death; driving ~370 miles by car costs about one, cycling ~44 miles and walking ~30 miles each cost about one, the train is ~6,000 miles, one skydive costs about 8, one scuba dive about 5, and one BASE jump about 430. Treat those as placeholders where they matter—I'm not sure they're current or right for our city.
