Ok so for philosophy club we read Searle's Chinese Room and Dennett's two-bitser reply (the one where the rulebook is replaced with a little black box that answers for two bits), and I've come out pretty convinced: the person in the room doesn't understand Chinese, and a digital computer can't either no matter how good the program is. What it has is syntax, and syntax by itself is never enough for semantics. Symbols get their meaning because we humans already have original intentionality and we lend it to the machine, so any intentionality a computer has is just derived from ours.
