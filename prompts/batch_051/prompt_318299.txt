I'm a novelist, and in the book I'm writing there's a mid-level bureaucrat whose whole job is to decide which organizations count as religions, and I want him to be consistent instead of just ruling however the plot needs. So I've been collecting my own gut calls on cases. Buddhism: religion, obviously. Confucianism: I want to say yes, but then I can't say why it isn't just a code of conduct. A monastic order that keeps silence and never mentions gods: religion. A neighborhood festival where people dress as ancestors and burn food at a shrine: religion, I think. A national holiday with flags, salutes, and the founding story sung: religion? I'd say yes but I'm uneasy. A company with a mission statement, ritual team meetings, and a charismatic founder: not religion, absolutly not. A family that does the same small ritual at every meal and can't explain it: religion? I keep flip-flopping. Is there a principle that gets all or most of these? What would it say about a football club with chants and relics, or a therapy where people confess and are absolved? And which of my gut calls does the principle force me to give up?
