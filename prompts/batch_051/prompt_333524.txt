I teach an upper-level political theory seminar on statehood and recognition. For the final, I want one essay question that makes students think through whether a state can exist without being recognized by other states. The question should be answerable in 75 minutes. I don't want it answerable by just define constitutive versus declaratory theory; I want a real crux. So build it around this case: a breakaway territory has governed its claimed territory for 14 years, holds regular elections, collects taxes, runs courts and a police force, and has stable borders, but only 11 UN members recognize it, the parent state still claims it, and the parent has used its Security Council veto to keep it out of the UN. The essay question should ask whether this entity is a state, what the best argument is for saying it is not, and whether mass non-recognition is enough to defeat its statehood or merely leaves it isolated. Then give me a model answer of about 650 words and a marking scheme worth 25 marks. For the marking scheme, name five must-hit moves, the marks each is worth, and a one-line note on a typical mistake students make with each. Please write in American English. The response should begin directly with the essay question, then the model answer, then the marking scheme. No preface or commentary.
