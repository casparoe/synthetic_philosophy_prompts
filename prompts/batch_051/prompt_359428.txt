(2) Evidence is ultimately a matter of observations, expressed in some observation language. (3) In a revolution, rival paradigms impose their own observation language on their practitioners: the same experimental output gets described and weighted differently, and terms like mass or planet shift their meaning, so there is no fully neutral observation language shared by both sides. (4) Therefore rival paradigms in a revolution cannot be compared against a shared body of evidence. (5) So the choice between them isn't rational, at least not in the way Popper wants it to be.

Which step, if any, fails?
