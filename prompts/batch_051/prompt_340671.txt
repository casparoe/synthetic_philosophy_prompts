I'm a PhD student in cognitive science, and over the past few years I've moved from treating eliminative materialism about propositional attitudes as a provocative seminar position to actually holding it. My working view is that the folk-psychological categories of belief, desire, intention, and the rest are the theoretical posits of a rough-and-ready tacit theory, and that a maturing cognitive neuroscience will mostly replace them rather than reduce them to or smoothly map them onto the categories of the successful science — roughly as phlogiston and caloric were abandoned rather than vindicated. I'm not looking for the standard first-year objections; I've heard the self-refutation charge often enough and don't think it lands. What I want is a serious map of what would count against the position. Which experimental findings, modeling successes, or conceptual results would a committed eliminativist have to treat as genuine problems — and which would they legitimately dismiss as demanding something the view never promised? I'd like the defeaters ranked from fatal to merely awkward, and I'd especially like to hear which considerations defenders of eliminativism would themselves concede count against it, rather than only what critics say.

A few of my own anxieties can serve as starting points, though I'd rather you not limit yourself to them: the remarkable everyday predictive success of belief–desire explanation; the possibility that the relevant categories are not natural kinds yet still earn their keep as causal-explanatory kinds at a higher level, in something like the way the gene concept survived fragmentation; and the worry that the view has been quietly insulated by being restated as the weak thesis that folk psychology will merely be revised, or as the indefinite claim that elimination is only a long-run promissory note, in which case nothing could refute it. Please be honest about whether the position as sophisticated defenders actually hold it risks anything at all — and, if it has become unfalsifiable in practice, whether that is a genuine problem for a thesis of this kind or an acceptable feature of a long-run scientific bet. End with a single line beginning "Summary:" that states in one sentence your overall assessment of how exposed eliminative materialism about beliefs and desires currently is.
