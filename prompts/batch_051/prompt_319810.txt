It summarized the coincidence/birthday-paradox stuff like this: "The birthday paradox shows that our intuitions about coincidence are systematically wrong. In a room of 23 people it's more likely than not that two share a birthday, because there are 253 possible pairs. So any 'one in a million' coincidence is actually not surprising at all: with eight billion people dreaming every night, million-to-one events happen thousands of times a day, so a dream matching a real event is basically guaranteed. If you calculate the odds after the fact you're rigging the numbers, so a story like someone dreaming about a plane crash the night before it happened is no evidence for anything psychic—and in fact anecdotal evidence should never shift your beliefs at all."
