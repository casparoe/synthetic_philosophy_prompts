Draft the short recommendation memo for the commission: approve, reject, or approve with conditions, with a bottom line we can actualy vote on. After the memo, give me a brief structured summary as a JSON object with these fields: decision (approve, reject, or approve_with_conditions), one_sentence_rationale, strongest_consideration_on_losing_side, conditions_or_phase_in (or null), and revisit_trigger (what would make you reconsider).
