Write a deadpan parody of a patient FAQ for a new germline therapy that makes severe suffering biologically impossible. The company selling it has repackaged the hedonistic imperative: the idea that we should use gene editing and designer drugs to phase out the raw capacity to suffer in all sentient life, replacing it with graded wellbeing that still tracks how things are going, rather than a flat blissful high. The jokes should come from the actual position, not from strawmanning it. So the FAQ has to field the real questions: is this just wireheading; how do I know something is bad for me if I can't feel bad; did my future kid consent to this edit; won't it cheapen achievement; what if I want to keep my low-grade anxiety because its mine. Keep it around 600 words, in the voice of the startup's communications team, and make them sound earnestly, relentlessly reasonable. The thing being sent up is the view's confidence that every objection has a technical fix.
