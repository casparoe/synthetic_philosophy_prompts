I've been arguing with a friend about updateless decision theory, and I keep feeling the whole thing quietly assumes the agent is a clean piece of code. The standard line, as I understand it, is this: when you're deciding what to do, you shouldn't condition on all the observations you've made, because some of those observations are correlated with your own decisions through your policy rather than through the world. So the rational move is to pick the policy that had the highest expected value from the perspective of your prior, before you got the evidence that's downstream of your policy. The usual supporting intuition comes from things like Counterfactual Mugging, where you pay in the branch you observe so that your policy gets rewarded in a counterfactual branch you never actually see.

But the agents people actually build now — say, an LLM with a context window, weights from SGD, maybe some RLHF on top — don't look much like the agent that argument is about. The thing that decides didn't exist as a unified algorithm with introspective access to itself before it saw the prompt. Its prior is a learned distribution over text, not a set of hypotheses about possible worlds. Its past self was never in a position to precommit to anything; the only precommitment that happened was the training run, if that even counts. And it can't inspect the alternative policies it might have output the way a UDT agent is supposed to inspect its own source code, because the search over policies happened implicitly, before deployment, mostly through gradient descent on data written by humans.

So here's my question: does the classic argument for updatelessness survive this change, or does it break at a specific step? Please answer in British English. I'd like you to focus on which premise of the classic argument the LLM setting actually bears on, whether that premise can be repaired or replaced — for example by treating the training run, the context window, or the inference-time chain-of-thought as the unit that is or isn't updateless — and whether the repaired view is still really UDT, rather than a different theory wearing its clothes.
