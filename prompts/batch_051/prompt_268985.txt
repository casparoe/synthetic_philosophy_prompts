Now the line going around is that deepfakes are basically Photoshop for video. We didnt stop trusting photographs after Photoshop, we just got savvier and settled into a more contextual, still-functional trust; so the same is supposed to happen here. The backstop gets downgraded, society absorbs it, and whoever is panicking about the collapse of shared reality has just fallen for a bad analogy.
