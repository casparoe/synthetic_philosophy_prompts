Background: I'm a retired chemist who's recently gotten into philosphy, and I've been working through Hume's essay on miracles. I ended up with an argument I dont like at all, because it seems to prove too much. Maybe you can find the loose step.

1. A reasonable person proportions belief to the evidence.
2. A miracle is by definition a violation of a law of nature, and a law of nature is just a generalization backed by uniform past experience. So the prior probability of any particular miracle is about as low as anything can get.
3. For any alleged miracle, the evidence we actually have is human testimony.
4. Hume's rule: testimony for a miracle should be believed only if it would be more miraculous for the testimony to be false than for the event to have happened. But liars, the credulous, and wishful reporters are common, while a genuine violation of a law of nature is unprecedented by definition, so that condition is never met. So testimony can never establish a miracle.
5. Therefore a reasonable person can never believe a miracle on testimony.
6. But a scientist considering a well-run lab report that contradicts a long-established law is in the same position: the law has all of uniform past experience behind it, and the only thing on the other side is the report of other people.
7. So by Hume's rule, the scientist should never accept the anomalous result, because it will always be more likely that the report is flawed than that the law fails.
8. But that's absurd. Science has to be able to revise laws on the basis of reported observations. I've lived through textbook revisions myself, so I can't give that up.
9. So either Hume's rule is wrong, or the miracle argument depends on a double standard. Either way the argument against miracles refutes itself.

If I have to choose, I'd rather keep 8 than keep Hume's rule, but I suspect the slip is somewhere around 6 or 7 and I can't name it. Which step do you reject, and what does rejecting it cost?

"Why it looked right" (about a quarter) — explain why the bad step is so easy to accept.
