"Moore's paradox is that a sentence like 'It's raining, but I don't believe that it's raining' cannot be true. This shows that belief is a necessary condition on assertion: you can only properly assert what you actually believe. The same points also show that knowledge is the norm of assertion, not mere belief, because the sentence 'It's raining, but I don't know that it's raining' is Moore-paradoxical in exactly the same way."
