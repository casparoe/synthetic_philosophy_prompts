Two people who've been arguing about moral luck for a semester decide to write up an adversarial collaboration instead of just relitigating the drunk-driver case again. One of them holds that agents are only responsible, and deserve blame or punishment, for what was under their control: the unlucky drunk driver who kills a child deserves no more than the equally reckless one who doesnt. The other holds that responsibility and desert genuinely track outcomes, so the one who kills deserves more. Draft the joint statement they could both sign. Include: (1) a numbered list of claims both accept about the two-driver case, negligence, and the difference between attempted and completed harms; (2) a disagreements table stating each side in wording that side would accept; (3) the cruxes; and (4) for each crux, a concrete variation of or addition to the two-driver case that both sides agree bears on it, with each side's prediction recorded in advance. If any part of their dispute is only verbal, say which part and why.
