There's a part of me that finds the empiricist story about the self genuinely compelling. Everything I know about myself as a particular person — my personality, my body, my history — seems to come in through the senses, and when I try to introspect I never catch a bare self standing behind the thoughts; I just catch particular thoughts, feelings, and bodily sensations. That pushes me toward something like a bundle view, or at least the view that the self is a construction from experience and there's no further fact beyond the stream.

But then there's Avicenna's flying man, and it keeps nagging at me. The setup: a person created all at once as a fully formed adult, suspended in empty air, eyes covered, limbs spread so no limb touches another, no memories, no sound, no scent, no pressure on the skin. Avicenna says this person would still affirm that he himself exists — not as a body and not as a memory, just as something that is. And honestly, when I run the thought experiment, I share the intuition. It doesn't feel like I'm smuggling something in; it feels like I'm noticing that self-awareness is not the same kind of thing as knowing an object through the senses.

So here's the tension. The empiricist/bundle theory has real systematic virtues: it's simple, it doesn't posit a hidden substance, and it accounts for a lot of what selves are like. The flying man intuition seems to show a case of self-awareness with no sensory input at all, which the theory shouldn't be able to explain if it's right. I don't want a ruling on who wins. I want the strongest version of both sides, and then a negotiated answer: should the intuition be debunked and dropped — for example, because the imagined scenario secretly includes proprioception or a body-schema, or because the intuition is just an artifact of how immagination works and I'm tacitly picturing a subject already there? Or should the theory be amended or abandoned, because there's a form of non-sensory self-awareness it left out? And is there a reading on which both survive? I want to know what would justify each of those moves, not just which one you'd pick.
