Write a deadpan satirical public-service FAQ, about 650 words, from a fictional Department of Aggregate Welfare announcing that a Utility Monster has been certified and that all dispensable resources across the population are being redirected to it because this transfer maximizes total welfare. Format it as roughly six to eight questions and answers. The document should work through the transition in bureaucratic detail without ever naming the standard objections as objections: the separateness of persons (each citizen's welfare remains on the books but contributes no decisive weight), the problem of measuring and comparing utility (the Monster's reported marginal utility is accepted on its own submission, and independent verification is said to threaten the very utility being measured), the appeals process for citizens who believe their projected welfare was underweighted, the disposition of assets and dependents, and the recurring question of whether the arrangement is fair. The humor should come from taking the maximization logic entirely at its word and following its administrative consequences; stay in character throughout, do not wink, and let the irony be legible through the content itself.
