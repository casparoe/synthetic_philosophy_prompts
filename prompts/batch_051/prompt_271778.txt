One of my main characters is a nineteen-year-old woman who receives her call-up papers and is quietly thrilled, and her father, who did his service in the late eighties, cannot understand why anyone would want to go back to that. I want the arguments in the book to be real arguments, not straw men.

So heres what id love from you. Take the standard economist's critique of conscription, the idea that the draft is a hidden tax on conscripts, that it conceals the true cost of military manpower from voters, and that a volunteer force selects for people who actually belong in uniform rather than dragging in whichever civilians happen to have the highest outside earnings, and use it as a lens on Sweden's reversal. The 2010 suspension looks like exactly what that theory would recommend, so the interesting part is 2017. Did Sweden simply abandon the theory under political pressure, or was it rationally buying something the theory does not price: credible armed neutrality, a large total-defence reserve, social buy-in, gender equality, a signal that the whole society rather than a thin professional cadre would be involved in defending the country?
