(2) No single thing can have two properties that clash like that full stop; one thing can't be both all-yellow and all-brown. (3) So the only way to avoid the contradiction is that Monday's banana and Friday's banana aren't strictly identical — they're different things, or different slices in a big banana-shaped thing stretched through time, the way a movie is made of separate frames. (4) But if that's right, then the slice I'm looking at now was never yellow. The yellow slice is a different thing entirely, and the thing that rotted is not the thing I bought.
