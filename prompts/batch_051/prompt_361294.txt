Give me exactly five numbered points, each one or two sentences, no introduction or conclusion: (1) your prediction — suspect or ordinary — for the height policy, and which past case is its closest analog; (2) the principle that best explains the tribunal's record so far; (3) the most plausible rival principle that also fits the record, and what it predicts instead for height; (4) which of those two principles you'd bet on and why; (5) one further case the tribunal could take that would settle which principle it's actually running, and what the two principles would each require there.
