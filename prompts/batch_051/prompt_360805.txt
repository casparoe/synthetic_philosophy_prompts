He uses this to argue that a huge amount of negative history—claims that a practice didn't exist, that some group didn't do Y, that no one had a concept of Z—is overconfident, and that we should barely update on documentary silence at all. I think he's right that the archive is not a random sample, but I also suspect the analogy is quietly importing a lot of ML assumptions.
