Where a position maps neatly onto a modelling choice — e.g. treating the cosmos as the only individual in the A-box, or making parthood primitive in the schema, or insisting that every class have multiple instances — say so. Finally, tell me whether the stuff that doesn't survive is evidence that the philosophers are on about something genuinely foreign to how I think, or whether translating the debate reveals that some of it is less substantive than it looks — just different choices about how to carve up a model. Don't smuggle the metaphysics back in by quietly redefining schema-design terms to do more work than they do for me.
