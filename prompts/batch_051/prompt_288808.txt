The theory as I understand it: the no-trade results (Aumann's agreeing-to-disagree, Milgrom–Stokey and the literature after them) say that rational traders with common priors and common knowledge of rationality shouldnt be able to profit from trading purely on differences in private information. The moment someone offers to trade at a price, the offer itself leaks the information that would have made the trade profitable, so the price adjusts and the gain evaporates. The usual escape hatches are different priors, hedging or liquidity motives, information that isn't common knowledge, or good old overconfidence.
