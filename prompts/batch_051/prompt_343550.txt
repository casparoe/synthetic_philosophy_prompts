In the original Yablo setup, there's an infinite sequence of sentences, each one saying all the later sentences are false. Curry, by contrast, usually gets presented as a single self-referential sentence: if this sentence is true then 0=1, and somehow you can derive 0=1 from it. Sometimes people say Curry is nastier than the liar because it doesn't even need negation to blow up.

More formally, S_n is the sentence "If for all m>n, S_m is true, then 0=1." Each one only refers to later sentences, so there's no direct self-reference. Does the contradiction still follow?
