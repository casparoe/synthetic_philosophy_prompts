Next month's guest is a philosopher who defends the thesis that deliberation crowds out prediction: her claim, roughly, is that while you are genuinely deciding whether to do something, you can't coherently assign a probability to the proposition that you will do it, because deciding requires treating the option as open in a way that predicting doesn't. I know the view well enough to introduce it clearly, but I want the episode to be a stress test, not a restating-the-abstract interview.

Could you draft eight to ten interview questions that really get into the mechanics of the position. No "what drew you to this" questions — I'd rather follow four or five deep threads then get ten disconnected ones. Maybe include a question on the murky middle ground of advising your future self or predicting from long-run base rates about your own behavior.
