I'm not any kind of scholar, I just went through a phase where I read a bunch of Tolstoy's novels and then, out of curiosity, worked through his big essay about art (What Is Art?, I think from the 1890s). The part that stuck with me is that he basically says real art happens when a person has actually lived through a feeling and then arranges stuff — words, paint, sounds, gestures — so that other people catch that same feeling. And if the feeling is faked, or if the whole point is just to be pretty or clever or to flatter rich people, then it's not really art, it's something counterfeit. He's also really harsh on imitation: art that just copies other art instead of coming out of something the artist genuinely felt. This is wierd territory for me because I work as a graphic designer and lately half my job is cleaning up Midjourney outputs for clients.

Anyway, I've been arguing with a friend about AI art, and I kept coming back to Tolstoy. The friend says AI images can be beautiful and can make people feel real things, so they're art. My problem is that, on Tolstoy's account, the machine never had a feeling to pass along — it just learned statistical patterns from a huge pile of existing pictures — and the person typing the prompt often didn't feel much either, they just wanted an image of a certain thing. But then again, a person might have a real feeling they're trying to get across and the AI is just their way of doing it, like a brush or a camera, so maybe that's different. So here's what I want to know: if Tolstoy somehow saw all this today, what would his actual position be? Don't just say he'd hate it — work it out. Would he call AI-generated images not-art at all, or bad art, or a new kind of forgery, or a legitimate tool that depends on the sincerity of the person using it? Would he draw the line between the person and the machine, or between images that look handmade and ones that look obviously generated? I'd rather you reason it out of his views about feeling, sincerity, and imitation than just tell me your own opinion about AI art.
