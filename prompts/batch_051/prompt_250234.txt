I'm a journalist who covers philosophy and the sciences, and I'm working on an essay about why so many public intellectual debates seem to run forever without either side moving. The idea I need to get straight is that some of these disputes are merely verbal. Before I read any more theories of what makes a dispute verbal rather than substantive, I want a specification of what an adequate criterion of verbalness has to deliver. Fix the purpose this way: the criterion should let an outside observer who has a full transcript of the exchange but no access to the speakers' private mental states decide whether a dispute between two sophisticated, charitable parties is merely verbal.

Give me the desiderata as structured data: a JSON object with a field called desiderata, whose value is an array of objects. Each object has: id, a short label; requirement, a sentence of the form an adequate criterion must...; motivating_case, the concrete case or consideration that shows this requirement is not parochial; why_failure_would_be_unacceptable; negotiable, true or false, where false means it is a genuine constraint on any adequate criterion and true means it is a demand only one camp would insist on; and conflicts_with, an array of ids of other desiderata that may be hard to meet jointly. Also include jointly_unsatisfiable_pairs, an array of objects each with fields pair and reason, for pairs you suspect no single criterion can satisfy at once; and hardest_requirement, an object with fields id and justification, saying which requirement does the most to exclude candidate criteria and why. Keep the weight on the criteria and their motivating cases, not on the scoring.

Then, under a field called contender_scores, briefly assess three existing approaches against the list: the test that a dispute is verbal just in case the parties would agree once all non-linguistic facts were settled; the view that a dispute is verbal when the two sides are using different but equally legitimate meanings; and the idea that some apparent verbal disputes are really metalinguistic negotiations over which concept to adopt. For each, give fields contender, meets, fails, and bottom_line (one sentence). Do not include a bibliography or citations; I want the reasoning to stand on the cases themselves.
