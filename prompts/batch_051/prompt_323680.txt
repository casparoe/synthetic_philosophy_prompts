(1) I think greed is a genuine vice, by which I mean an insatiable appetite for more — not just having money or wanting a comfortable life, but a kind of bottomless wanting where no amount ever feels like enough.

(2) I think the profit motive in competitive markets has lifted more people out of extreme poverty than charity or government aid ever has.

(3) I think it's fine, maybe even admirable, to start a company with the explicit goal of getting very rich, provided you're honest and you treat your employees and customers decently.

(4) I think trying to stamp out acquisitiveness through law, schooling, or heavy social pressure would be unjust and would make basically everyone poorer.

(5) I find something genuinely obscene about someone sitting on tens of billions while people starve, even when the fortune was legally earned and the company made the world better.

(6) I think the difference between greed and ordinary self-interest isn't about how much you want — it's about what you're willing to do to get it. Greed is self-interest without the usual restraints.
