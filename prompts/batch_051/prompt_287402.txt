And a single sense can undergo copycat slippage: later users copy earlier applications of the word, but each copier abstracts a slightly different set of salient features, so the features treated as central for that one sense drift over a chain of uses. "I'll use the microdomain of 'sport.' Vague: darts, maybe bowling, are borderline, but football and a 100m sprint are plainly central, and those central cases aren't moving. Stably polysemous: 'sport' as an athletic contest, 'sport' as a good-natured person ('he's a good sport'), and 'sport' as game animals are distinct senses; switching between them doesn't push the athletic-contest sense around. A few copying steps later, people call chess and speedrunning sports without any particular act of redefinition; the center of the sense has moved while everyone thought they were using the word normally.
