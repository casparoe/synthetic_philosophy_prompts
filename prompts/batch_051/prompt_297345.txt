A platform proposes that any post alleging a hidden coordinated scheme may be published only after its author and a reviewer drawn at random from an opt-in pool jointly agree on five discrete, publicly observable events over the following six months whose occurrence would count publicly against the scheme. The author stakes a deposit: if any listed event occurs, the deposit is forfeited to a fact-checking fund and the post is automatically labeled contradicted; if none occurs, the deposit is refunded and the post earns a survived-adversarial-preregistration badge. Treat this as a mechanism, not as a policy position. What does it incentivize authors, reviewers, and readers to do; how would bargaining over the falsification conditions actually proceed; where does the system break; and which failure modes could be fixed without abandoning the core idea?
