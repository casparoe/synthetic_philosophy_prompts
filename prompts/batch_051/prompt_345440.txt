i'm in a reading group that's been working through dennett's cranes-vs-skyhooks stuff and his complaints about greedy reductionism, and for next session everyone has to bring a one-page statement of where they've landed on reduction and emergence so the rest of us can poke at it. I've been trying to write mine and I keep going in circles about whether the whole set hangs together; every time I think I've located the problem it sort of dissolves, so I want an outside audit before I present. Here are the commitments, roughly as I'd defend them:

(1) physicalism with no skyhooks: the world is just physical stuff doing what physical stuff does, bottom-up; no designer, no mind-first causes, no point where extra causal oomph gets inserted from outside the system.
(2) anti-greedy-reductionism: psychology, biology, economics and so on are not just temporary placeholders for particle physics; the higher-level patterns are real and do explanatory work that you'd lose if you tried to translate them all the way down.
(3) emergence with teeth: some macro things, like a market crash or a traffic jam, are real and have causal power in a nontrivial sense — the crash is nothing but a bunch of trades, but it still does something that no individual trade does on its own.
(4) no downward causation: all the causal pushing and pulling is done by the parts at the bottom; a macro cause can't contribute any force over and above what its micro realizers already provide.
(5) compatibilism for bottom-up reasons: my capacity to deliberate and control myself is an evolved and culturally installed competence, not a miracle, so free will doesn't need a skyhook.
(6) culture and language are cranes, but they also push back: they get built bottom-up with no designer, yet once they exist they really shape individual behavior — the norms of a market can make me do things I wouldn't otherwise do, even though the market is just people trading.

Go through these and tell me which pairs are genuinely in tension, which only look that way, and rank the genuine tensions by severity. For each tension, say what would have to give and what the cheapest fix is, and then tell me which single revision would clean up the most.
