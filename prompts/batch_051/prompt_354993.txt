Here's a set of criteria I've been using for whether a trait is heritable in a population: (1) the trait varies between individuals; (2) individuals who are more genetically similar resemble each other more on the trait; (3) the resemblance sticks around after you control for shared environment; and (4) the resemblance can be summarized as a nonzero heritability h² for that trait in that population. But divorce isn't a property of an organism — it's a legal event that depends on another person, a court, and a legal system; there's no gene for filing for divorce the way there is for hair texture.
