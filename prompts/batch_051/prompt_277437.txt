(1) If you know that p, then you may rationally treat p as settled in practical deliberation.

Plausibly, you may treat the proposition that the bank is open today as settled in the low-stakes version, but not in the high-stakes version.

(4) In the low-stakes version, you do know it.
(5) Therefore, whether you know can change while your evidence, confidence, and the facts stay the same, purely because the stakes changed.
