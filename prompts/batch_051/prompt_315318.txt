I keep catching myself in an asymmetry at work, and I can't tell whether it's justified. When I get a strong gut-level objection to something we're building—a feature that feels manipulative even if it's good for engagement—I call that "my values" and I mostly trust it. When a coworker gets the same kind of strong gut-level objection to my design, I privately file it under "their bias": they got burned at a previous job, they're over-anxious about edge cases, whatever. Same mental event, different label depending on which head it's in. Is there a real distinction between a moral preference and a bias that would let me sort these into different buckets without rigging the game in my own favor? I want the strongest principle you can find, tested against the case where I'm the one with the messy track record and they're the one with the clean read, and an honest verdict on whether it survives. Separate what's actually settled from what's genuinely contested here—don't give me one confident story if the field doesn't have one.
