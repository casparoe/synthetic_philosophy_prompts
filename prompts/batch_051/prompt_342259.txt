I've been going around in circles on the "hinge of history" idea — the thought that we might be living through one of the most important stretches of human history because of things like AI, nuclear weapons, and how quickly technology compounds over generations. I don't have a background in any of this, just reading on my own. I've got maybe six months of evenings to spend on it, partly to write a longform piece and partly to figure out how much it should actually changes how I live. If you had to pick the open questions in this area that are most worth figuring out first, what would the list be and in what order? I care less about a survey than the reasoning: for each one, why it's upstream of the others, how far a non-academic can realistically get on it without research-level modelling, and what evidence over the next six months would bump it up or down the list. Also which standard talking points in this debate you'd deprioritize because they don't actually change the practical question of whether we're at a hinge.
