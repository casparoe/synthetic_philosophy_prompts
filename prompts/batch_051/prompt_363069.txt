Most people would say without hesitation that the throw of a stone caused the window to shatter, but would regard the claim that the window's shattering caused the stone to be thrown a moment earlier as nonsense rather than merely false. Yet the microscopic dynamics relevant to the collision—electromagnetism and gravity—are time-reversal symmetric: the interaction run backward obeys the same laws. Ordinary causal and explanatory practice is therefore deeply asymmetric in a way the underlying physics is not. Does anything justify this asymmetry, or is it only a projection of creatures who remember the past and act toward the future? Could the causal order come apart from the thermodynamic arrow in a region where entropy decreased toward what we call the future? Present the strongest case for each side, then argue for the one you think is right.
