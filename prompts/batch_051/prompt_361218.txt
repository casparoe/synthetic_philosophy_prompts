I am a PhD student in philosophy of biology, and I keep coming across the claim that the formal equivalence results descended from the Price equation show kin selection and group selection to be just two mathematically interchangeable ways of partitioning the same total selection. If that is right, why is the levels-of-selection debate still treated as a live, substantive dispute rather than a verbal one? Once a translation between the two formalisms is on the table, shouldn't the question of whether selection acts on genes, individuals, or groups simply dissolve? What, if anything, is left to argue about?
