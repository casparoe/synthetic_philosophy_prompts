People invoke Morgan's canon to mean we shouldn't explain animal behavior with higher mental faculties when some lower mechanism could do the job, so how do researchers justify all the recent claims about animal grief, forward planning, and concepts? I'm a retired immunologist, used to parsimony arguments, but I dont see why the associative alternative automatically counts as the parsimonious one: by the time it's a Rube-Goldberg machine of chained associations and counterconditioning, it looks more baroque than the 'higher' story. And if the canon is just about avoiding unnecessary assumptions, shouldn't it apply equally to the dog and my sister? I'm not trying to settle the animal-mind debate; I just want to know what the canon actually licenses and which uses of it are misreadings.
