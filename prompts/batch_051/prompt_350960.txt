It is fully legal, and using it while working or caring for others is socially accepted. Nothing else changes.

Give me a reasoned prediction, not a story, of how society looks fifty years on, concentrating on economic production, close relationships, art and intellectual life, and political institutions. Show the mechanisms: what individuals, firms, and governments would be incentivised to do, and what constraints would still bind. Pay particular attention to the point where wireheading shades into reward tampering, as people and institutions begin gaming the measures meant to keep effort and achievement real. For each major prediction, say whether it is robust or a guess, and name the hidden assumption it depends on. I am less interested in whether the conclusion is optimistic or pessimistic than in the chain of reasoning.
