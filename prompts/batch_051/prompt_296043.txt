Write an essay on whether the problem of aligning advanced AI systems with human values is solvable in principle. Because the phrase "in principle" does much of the work in this debate, begin by distinguishing at least three senses in which alignment might be solvable in principle: first, that there exists a well-defined target—a goal, utility function, or specification—whose optimization would count as alignment; second, that given such a specification, there is a computable procedure that would converge to an adequately aligned policy; third, that the whole task remains feasible for a bounded, real-world agent even after proxy objectives, misspecification, and Goodhart effects are taken into account. Weigh the strongest considerations for and against solvability under each sense you distinguish, and then defend a definite overall conclusion rather than ending in an even-handed standoff. Argue from first principles and do not rely on citations, named papers, or attributed quotations. Write in American English.
