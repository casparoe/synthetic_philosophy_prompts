Here's a rule for a small co-op I'm thinking through. It has around 30 members and makes most decisions by pairwise majority vote. At the start, each member gets to file one "private domain": two possible options such that if the group ever votes on those two, the filer's own ranking automatically becomes the group's ranking, no matter how everyone else feels. The filings are made before the agenda of options is known, they can't be revoked, and they can be bought, sold, or traded, so one person can end up holding several. Everything not covered by a private domain is decided by majority rule, with ties broken by the founder. Walk me through what gets filed, how people would game it, what it does to agenda-setting and to majority outcomes, where the first failure or cycle appears, and whether fixes like capping holdings, requiring periodic re-filing, or letting the majority buy back domains would actually solve it or just relocate the problem.
