OK so I'm putting together a unit on blame for my moral psychology seminar, and I keep hitting the same wall: students can identify a hypocrite instantly, but they cant articulate what exactly the hypocrite lacks. We call it standing to blame. I want a working definition of standing to blame — not a definition of blame itself, and not an account of when blame is true or deserved on the merits. Assume in each case that the criticism is true and the target deserves the blame; the only question is whether this particular blamer is in a position to deliver it.

I'll give you ten cases. For each, your definition should say has standing, lacks standing, or cant decide without more facts (and if so, say which facts). Then run the definition through the cases, flag where it feels forced or gives a weird verdict, and revise it or defend the surprising call. I'm more interested in how you handle the stress-test then in a polished final line.

Cases:

1. A pedestrian who has never driven drunk blames a drunk driver for nearly hitting a child.
2. A serial philanderer publicly condemns a colleague for a single affair.
3. A former drunk driver who has been sober for fifteen years and now campaigns against drunk driving blames a current repeat offender.
4. A person who routinely lies to their spouse about money condemns a friend for tax evasion.
5. Two friends cheated together on a licensing exam. One now openly blames the other, says what they both did was wrong, and blames himself too. The other denies anything happened.
6. The planner of a robbery, who stayed in the car, blames the getaway driver for using a weapon.
7. A stranger with no connection to either the victim or the wrongdoer condemns a mugger.
8. A parent blames an adult who defrauded their adult child.
9. A judge with a decades-old conviction for the same offense sentences a defendant for that offense.
10. A politician who voted for a surveillance program under one administration denounces the same program under the next after a sincere change of heart.

Since I'm collecting these with a script, end with a single line beginning 'Definition:' that states your final definition in one sentence.
