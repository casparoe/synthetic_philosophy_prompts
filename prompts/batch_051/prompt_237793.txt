The trolley is already moving; I merely alter where the existing threat goes. Most people I have asked treat this as acceptable, or at least as something a decent person could do. Second: the same trolley is barreling toward five workers, and the only way to stop it is to push a very large person off a footbridge into its path, killing him in the collision. Most of the same people treat this as clearly wrong.
