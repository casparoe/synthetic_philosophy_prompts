As I understand it, the central idea is that a translation is never simply optimal, because the properties one might want to preserve — meter, rhyme, line count, register, literal content, connotation, even the look of the words on the page — cannot all be maximized at once. Choosing what to save is always choosing what to betray, and the interesting question is which losses the translator is willing to own. I would like the group to feel that problem in their hands rather than receive it as a claim from me.
