Something I've never had a name for. I follow the British and Scandinavian monarchies a bit, and I keep noticing the same arrangement. The monarch is said to have a stack of real powers—sacking a prime minister, refusing to sign off laws, dissolving parliament—and people are careful to insist these powers still exist. But if they were ever actually used, the institution would probably be finished: the public would turn on it, and politicians would pass laws to take the powers away. So the powers survive only because nobody tests them, and a lot of it's value seems to come from nobody knowing whether they could really be used. Is there a name for this kind of arrangement—a power that can't be exercised without destroying itself? And is it fair to call it a power at all, or is it just theatre?
