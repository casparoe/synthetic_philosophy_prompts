ok so in my philosophy-ish class we read this essay by Einstein called "Religion and Science" (it was in the New York Times Magazine, 1930), and one part keeps bothering me. he says religious feeling has stages, and the third one he calls cosmic religious feeling. here's the passage:

"But there is a third stage of religious experience which belongs to all of them, even though it is rarely found in a pure form: I shall call it cosmic religious feeling. It is very difficult to elucidate this feeling to anyone who is entirely without it, especially as there is no anthropomorphic conception of God corresponding to it. The individual feels the futility of human desires and aims and the sublimity and marvelous order which reveal themselves both in nature and in the world of thought. Individual existence impresses him as a sort of prison and he wants to experience the universe as a single significant whole."

what i can't decide is whether this "cosmic religious feeling" should count as religion at all, or if its just some neighboring thing that borrows the word. so i want a working definition of religion that gets these cases right (at least by my gut):

- my grandma, who goes to church every Sunday and says she prays for us — obviously religion
- my friend's dad, a physicist type, who's an atheist but gets kind of choked up about how the universe is ordered, almost exactly like Einstein is describing — my gut says that's not religion, but Einstein seems like he'd say it is
- my cousin who fasts for Ramadan every year but says he's not religious and only does it because the family does it
- a guy who fully belives God exists and will judge him, but basically never thinks about it, never prays, and just lives his life like it doesn't matter
- someone who's super into astrology and says the universe has a specific plan for her
- a buddhist monk who doesn't believe in any creator god at all

propose the definition, run it through all six, and tell me where it surprises you or where you'd have to change it. then run it through the Einstein passage specifically: on your definition, is Einstein's cosmic religious feeling a religion, or just a feeling? and at the end say how confident you are in all this and which point you're least sure about.
