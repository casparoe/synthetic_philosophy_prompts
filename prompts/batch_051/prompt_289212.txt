I'm putting together a seminar on the epistemology of testimony and I've paired Henry James's The Turn of the Screw with some late-nineteenth-century psychical research, and my students keep asking what the two have to do with each other. The connection I want to draw runs through the James family itself: Henry's brother William was a central figure in the Society for Psychical Research, and Henry Sidgwick, who helped found the society, took its methods dead seriously — not as a parlour-game interest but because he saw it as maybe the only empirical route to evidence about whether the universe is morally ordered, the sort of evidence he thought was needed to close the gap between self-interest and duty that his own ethical system seemed to leave open. So I want the novella read through the lense of Sidgwick's psychical-research project.

Read The Turn of the Screw as if it were a case file handed to a Sidgwickian investigator. It is built almost exactly like a piece of SPR evidence: a first-person report of apparitions (Peter Quint, Miss Jessel) gets written down, preserved, and read aloud years later, while the narrator's own reliability is one of the main things in question. What would the SPR's procedural standards — corroboration, separating veridical perception from hallucination, scrutinizing the witness's motives and frame of mind — lead a reader to conclude? Are those standards enough to adjudicate between "the ghosts are real" and "the governess is seeing things," or is the novella engineered so that the evidence systematically underdetermines both readings? And where does the story resist or quietly mock the investigator's stance — for instance in the frame narrative that puts the testimony at several removes, or in the governess's insistence that the children do see the ghosts but won't admit it? I'd like a reading that is serious about both the SPR's methodological debates and James's craft, and that says what the novella dramatizes about the problem of testimony for the paranormal that a straight psychical-research paper cannot get at.
