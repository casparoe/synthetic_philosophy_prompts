I recently became a parent through IVF with preimplantation genetic testing, and the experience has left me preoccupied with a question about identity. I have been persuaded by what I understand of origin essentialism: the thesis that a person could not have originated from a different sperm and egg than the ones from which they actually originated. From this, it seems to follow that if the clinic had transferred a different embryo, my daughter would never have existed, and another child would have existed in her place. But I am unsure whether a further conclusion follows. Does origin essentialism imply that my daughter's exact genome is essential to her identity? If, for instance, a gene-editing procedure had altered a single base pair of the zygote's DNA shortly after fertilization, would the resulting child have been a different person rather than my daughter with a slightly altered genome? Conversely, if a different embryo had by rare chance possessed exactly the same genome, would that embryo have been the same person in a different body?
