Second, grounding chains are well-founded: there are no infinite descending chains of ground, and no circular grounding. Third, fundamentality is absolute rather than merely relative to a level of description — a fact is fundamental or non-fundamental simpliciter, and talk of one fact's being "more fundamental" than another is just a measure of distance from the bottom level.
