As I understand it, the Cartesian theatre (Dennett's label) is the view that somewhere in the brain there is a place where processing 'comes together' and is presented to consciousness: one screen, one audience, one authoritative order of events. Dennett's multiple drafts model denies there is any such place. Instead the brain continuously produces many parallel, partial, revisable interpretations of what is going on, and which of them counts as 'the' conscious experience only gets settled later, by whatever the person is asked, does, or reports; there is no master copy that gets read out. If I've mangled that, correct it briefly and move on.
