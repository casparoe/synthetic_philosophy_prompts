I'm a novelist — I mostly write literary fiction, but a studio brought me on to write the story and dialogue for a single-player tactical shooter, and now I'm stuck on the game's content advisory panel. We're making a game set during a civil war in a fictional country that's pretty transparently based on the 1990s Balkan wars. The campaign has a mission where your squad carries out a reprisal raid on a village, and the problem is that this mission uses the same scoring system as every other mission: headshot bonuses, a combo meter, an end-of-mission grade (including a grade called Clean Sweep for a high-combo run), and achievements. Half the panel thinks you can't put a score multiplier on that mission without turning an atrocity into sport. The lead designer says its just the grammar of the game, like slow motion or a close-up in a film — it doesn't mean you approve of what's on screen, and gating it of for one mission would be moralizing and break the design language. The studio asked us for a short memo with a concrete recommendation they can act on: keep the scoring, strip it for that mission only, cut the mission, or some other option. Can you draft that memo for me? It should have a clear bottom line and the reasoning behind it, and it should give an honest run to the strongest consideration on whichever side we end up losing.
