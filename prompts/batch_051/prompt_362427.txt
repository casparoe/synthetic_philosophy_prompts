Write an essay on whether a studio recording assembled from many takes is a performance of a musical work, a distinct recorded work, or a token of that work. In defending your answer, weigh type–token ontologies of music against causal accounts on which a performance is an unrepeatable sound-event with a specific history, and apply your taxonomy to the limiting case of a synthetic realization of a string quartet generated directly from the score with no human performer. Do not end in mere pluralism; choose and justify one classification.
