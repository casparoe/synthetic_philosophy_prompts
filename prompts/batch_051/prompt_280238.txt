I need a second opinion on a concept I've been using in arguments because I might have just convinced myself. I call it 'standing as credit.' The idea is that standing to blame isn't binary—something you either have or lack—it's a balance. You build it up by being the sort of person whose criticism is more likely to be true than self-serving, and you spend it when you blame. Hypocrisy is blaming beyond your balance: the criticism can be true but it lands as false because there's no standing behind it, like a check that bounces. So far so obvious. The bit my friends call perverse is this: I think standing can be aquired at a discount the longer ago your own wrongdoing was. A reformed cheater might have more standing to condemn cheating than someone who never cheated, because their blame is paid for by demonstrated knowledge of the temptation and the cost of changing. Is this already a position someone's worked out under a better name? And give me the strongest objection to the discount idea specifically—does it wrongly let past wrongdoers out-blame the clean, or is that actually a feature?
