In a lot of places (ours included) a dying patient can be put into a deep, continuous sedation — basically unconscious until they die — and have food and water withheld, even when they're being fed through a tube. The doctors will do this openly, write it in the chart, and call it palliative sedation, or just letting the disease take its course. But if the same patient asked for a lethal injection so they could die sooner and less dragged-out, that's illegal, and the same doctors would call that killing. My friend insists these are completely different acts. I can't quite articulate why I find that suspicious, wich is why I'm here.
