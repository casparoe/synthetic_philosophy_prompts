I read something about Ian Hacking's idea of looping effects, where giving people a psychiatric label doesn't just describe them but actually changes them—they start seeing themselves through the label and acting more like it, so the category kind of makes itself true over time. I want to know how you'd actually test that. Like, take ADHD or depression. Design a study (or a few studies) that could seperate "the label just picks out people who were already like that" from "the label changes people so they fit it better over time." What results would count for each reading? And after the best possible study, what would still be left unsettled—is there part of the looping claim that no experiment can get at?
