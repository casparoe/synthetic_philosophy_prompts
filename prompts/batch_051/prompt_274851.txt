I am a university student in a philosophy of mind course, and we have just worked through Dennett's quining-qualia strategy: the claim that disputes about qualia may not track any real difference, so that the notion cannot carry theoretical weight. Dennett's challenge is to say what, if anything, could make it true that one of them is right rather than the other.

Please write a decision tree that classifies the positions on the question 'Are qualia an incoherent posit?' using that example as the pivot. Please give exactly five numbered items, one per leaf, with no introduction or conclusion; each item should be at most two sentences, the first giving the chain of answers that reaches the leaf plus the leaf's name, and the second stating its most awkward commitment.
