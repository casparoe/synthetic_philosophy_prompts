Suppose you are advising a three-year, five-person philosophy research project on populism and democratic legitimacy. The project's mandate is conceptual rather than empirical: it aims to clarify whether, and under what conditions, appeals to "the will of the people" can legitimately justify overriding counter-majoritarian institutions such as courts, central banks, or entrenched constitutional provisions. Which unresolved conceptual questions should the project tackle first, and in what order? I am less interested in a survey than in a defended agenda. For each candidate question, state why it is upstream of other questions, how tractable it is with philosophical tools rather than empirical social science, and what argumentative developments over the next two years would move it up or down the priority list. In your answer, indicate how confident you are in the claimed dependency structure and single out the point you are least sure of.
