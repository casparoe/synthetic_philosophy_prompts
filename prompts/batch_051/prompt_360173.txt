The idea is basically that the universe is like a dark forest at night: civilisations stay silent because any signal you hear probably comes from a hunter, and if you answer you get wiped out before you can become a threat. Can you write one where a generation ship picks up a weak but definitely artificial radio signal, and the vote on whether to reply is deadlocked, so both sides have to make there case. I want it to actually grapple with the logic, not just turn into an aliens-attack story.
