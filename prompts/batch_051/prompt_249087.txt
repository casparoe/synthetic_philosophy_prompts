I have recently been assigned to a working group reviewing a proposal to build a large black soldier fly larvae facility for animal feed. One member of the group places her credence at roughly 0.8 that the operation faces a serious moral problem around insect welfare; another places his at about 0.15. I find myself at perhaps 0.4, but I distrust that number because I have not actually separated the questions feeding into it. I would like you to give me your own credence in the following proposition, with the reasoning made explicit as a decomposition.

The proposition: for at least one major farmed insect species of this kind—black soldier fly larvae, crickets, or mealworms—the scale argument succeeds, meaning that even a low probability of sentience, multiplied across the very large number of individuals such a facility processes, gives a responsible agent sufficient reason to treat insect welfare as a real constraint now rather than setting it aside until sentience is demonstrated.

Please give each of the following as a probability with one or two sentences of justification, and then show exactly how you combine them into your final number:

1. The probability that at least one of these species has valenced experience—states that feel good or bad to the insect—rather than only nociception, reflexes, or non-valenced information processing.
2. Conditional on sentience, the probability that an individual insect's valenced states have enough intensity or intrinsic weight to register non-negligibly in a moral calculus, rather than being so faint or discounted that even astronomically many instances would not generate a serious claim.
3. Conditional on the first two, the probability that expected-value aggregation is the appropriate way to reason across vast numbers of low-probability welfare subjects, rather than treating a low probability of sentience as below a threshold and acting as if it were zero.
4. Conditional on the first three, the probability that at least one feasible change in husbandry, stocking density, or slaughter method would be robustly net positive on an insect-welfare-inclusive evaluation.

Then state your overall credence in the proposition, explain the combination rule you used and any additional terms you think belong, and say which single component your final number is most sensitive to and what evidence—empirical or philosophical—would shift that component most. Finally, say where the disagreement between the two people at 0.8 and 0.15 is most likely located: which component, if any, accounts for most of that gap.
