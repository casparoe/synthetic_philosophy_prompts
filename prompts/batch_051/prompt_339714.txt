I want to know how far that analogy actually holds. Can you give me the correspondence table — what plays the role of the origin, the axis, the unit, the direction — and then the places where it breaks? I especially suspect the analogy quietly conflates choosing an epoch (where zero goes) with deciding whether to have a year zero at all (the BC/AD vs astronomical numbering thing). And I want to know whether the "it's all just convention, so stop arguing" conclusion survives once the disanalogies are on the table, or whether the number-line picture was quietly importing features — like the axis being homogeneous and unbounded — that civil calendars don't have.
