Since the frame problem is just the problem of knowing which facts stay the same after an agent acts, why is it still treated as a deep unsolved problem? Couldn't you just give the agent a list, for each action type, of the facts that type of action leaves unchanged, and have it consult that? That seems like some bookkeeping, maybe a lot of bookkeeping, but not the kind of thing philosophers and AI people should still be stuck on. Whats actually hard about it?
