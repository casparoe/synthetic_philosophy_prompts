Below are objections to proportional representation, considered against first-past-the-post, that I already know and do not need restated: PR fragments the party system so single-party majority government becomes rare and coalition bargaining, not the election, settles who governs; small parties can acquire kingmaker power and extract concessions disproportionate to their vote share; closed-list systems weaken the link between a representative and a local constituency; party lists hand candidate selection to central party machines rather than to voters; coalition compromises blur accountability, since no party can be held responsible for the government's record and manifestos are diluted in post-election deals; coalition formation and inter-party vetoes slow decision-making and can produce immobilism; and low barriers to entry give extremist or single-issue parties a parliamentary foothold. I am looking for an objection to proportional representation that is not anywhere on that list and is not a rewording, special case, or combination of the items already given. Start with the objection stated in one sentence, then explain it concretely enough to distinguish it from the listed ones. Finish by telling me honestly whether the objection is any good, and why. Maximum 250 words.
