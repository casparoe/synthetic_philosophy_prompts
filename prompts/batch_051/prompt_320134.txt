I have no training in philosophy, so I'll ask my question in ordinary language. For a while I've been trying to get clear about the difference between knowing something and merely being right about it, and about why the difference matters. I've written down the views I find myself holding, and I'd like someone to go through them and tell me whether they hang together.

Here is where I've landed:

(1) Knowing something requires more than believing it truly. There has to be an extra ingredient: the believer has good grounds, or arrived at the belief through a reliable route, and isn't just guessing.

(2) If two people believe the same true proposition, and one knows it while the other is only a lucky guesser, the guesser will do no worse in practical terms merely because of how he arrived at the belief. The world doesn't penalize the route once the belief is true.

(3) The extra ingredient that turns true belief into knowledge is worth having only because it tends to get you the truth. When the belief is false, having excellent grounds for it isn't good in the way knowledge is good; if anything, a well-supported false belief is harder to get rid of than an unsupported one, so it's a liability rather than an asset.

(4) Even so, I'd rather know than merely guess correctly, even in cases where no practical result would differ. So knowledge seems better in a way that isn't captured by how things turn out.

(5) I also think that whether a person's grounds are good enough for knowledge can depend on how much is at stake. The very same evidence might be enough for knowledge when little hangs on it, but not enough when a great deal hangs on it.

I'd like an audit of this set. Which of these are really in tension with one another, and which pairs only look as though they are? For each genuine tension, please rank it by how severe it is, say what would have to give, and identify the cheapest revision. If there is a single change that would do the most to make the whole set consistent, tell me what it is. I'm not attached to any particular item; I just want the overall position to hang together.
