been chewing on this for a while and want it checked. i have a term for a thing i keep noticing: 'loose reasons'. a loose reason is a reason that isnt held by anybody. not just a reason nobody has found *yet*, but a reason that is doing real work in the world without ever needing to live in a head. when a species ends up with camouflage, there is a reason for it — the ones without it got eaten — but no animal has that reason. when a relationship is dying there are reasons to leave that are sitting in the room long before either person can say them. when a market crashes, the crash has reasons, and they were reasons before the economists showed up to argue about them.

my bigger claim is that this is the normal case. reasons are the basic furniture, and minds are just little eddies where a few of them happen to become visible to themselves. so a reason without a reasoner isnt a missing case, its the default. i know 'reasons are furniture' sounds daft. so does my test: the reasons to leave were in the room before anyone thought them — but not in the way the chairs were. what is that second 'in' doing?

i want to know: is this already a thing under some name i wouldn't know, or is it just a metaphor with a spooky glow around it? give me the strongest objection to the actual claim and one case where my way of talking gives the wrong verdict, not just a weird one.
