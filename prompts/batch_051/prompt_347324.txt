I am currently taking a course in the philosophy of cosmology, and a parallel has been troubling me that I cannot settle on my own. The Boltzmann brain worry, as I understand it, is that in a universe old or large enough for every local configuration to fluctuate into existence, a typical conscious observer would more likely be a brief, self-contained fluctuation — one whose apparent memories of a lawful past are themselves part of the fluctuation — than an observer embedded in a genuine thermodynamically ordered history. The inference from my apparent past and the apparent regularities of the world around me to a real, history-bearing universe is therefore threatened by the size of the ensemble of alternative chances that could have produced me by accident.

I have also been reading, for a statistics course, about the multiple comparisons problem and what physicists call the look-elsewhere effect. There, too, a naive inference is undermined by the number of chances in play: run enough tests and some will cross a significance threshold even where there is no underlying effect, so an apparently impressive pattern is not evidence for the hypothesis once one conditions on the large space of possible unreported tests. The data analyst who fails to correct for this strikes me as a kind of Boltzmann brain: someone taking a selected, apparently structured outcome at face value without conditioning on its having been selected from a vast space of random opportunities.

I want to know whether this is a deep identity of structure, a loose analogy, or a confusion. Please give a committed answer rather than laying out the options and leaving me to choose: state the mapping between the two cases precisely, say what would have to be true on both sides for results to transfer, and identify at least one consequence the parallel seems to predict that fails in practice. In particular, is the statistical remedy — controlling the error rate across the whole family of tests or declaring the analysis plan in advance — genuinely the same kind of move as the cosmological remedy of choosing a measure over observers, or does the analogy break down exactly there?
