Write a joke that hinges on the difference between incongruity theory and benign violation theory of humour. Incongruity theory says laughter is basically the perception of violated expectation. Benign violation theory says no — that alone isnt enough; the violation also has to be appraised as benign, i.e. safe, distant, or acceptable in some way, otherwise you get horror or distress instead of comedy. The joke should ride on exactly that gap: the same event being horrifying under one reading and funny under the other. The punchline should only land for someone who's tracking the benign part, not just the surprise. And dont just namedrop the two theories — actually make it funny.
