The reasons she gives are short: evolution has models that make testable predictions and survived; psychoanalysis can absorb any observation; SETI is a systematic empirical search; string theory has yet to make a novel testable prediction; climate models get tested against data they were not built to fit; mathematics has no empirical content; astrology's claims fail when tested and its practitioners don't revise. More than one defensible rule fits those verdicts, and if I ask her she'll just repeat one of her catchphrases, so I want to predict her before asking.

I can imagine her saying it is science because the cellular automata side is a systematic exploration with genuine computational experiments that keep surprising the people doing it, or saying it is not science yet because the big claims about the universe, including the principle of computational equivalence, are still too vague to rule anything out. What verdict would be most consistent with her track record, which earlier case is the closest analog, and if the precedents underdetermine it, what principle would she be adopting with each answer?
