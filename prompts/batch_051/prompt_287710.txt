I have the usual vocabulary — aliasing, side effects, referential transparency, object identity — but I've realized I never wrote down what a satisfactory account of mutable state would actually have to accomplish, and I want that specification before I compare semantic frameworks. Please write me the desiderata: what must a theory of state and object identity in an imperative or object-oriented language deliver in order to count as adequate? For each requirement, give the case or consideration that makes it a requirement. For each requirement, mark whether it is non-negotiable or a preference of a particular camp (functional programmers, verification people, language designers), and flag any pair of requirements you suspect cannot be jointly satisfied without weakening one side.
