Around the same time we did a unit in AP Psych on depressive realism, the idea that mildly depressed people are sometimes more accurate about how much control they actually have, and that mentally healthy people run on a sort of built-in optimistic bias. Ever since, I keep going back and forth about whether my aunt's hope is a virtue or a comfortable mistake.

It gets sharper when I compare cases. If a city official downplays an evacuation warning because he's optimistic the hurricane will miss us, we call it negligent, maybe criminally so.
