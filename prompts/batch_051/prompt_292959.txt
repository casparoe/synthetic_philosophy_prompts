Someone in my town proposed this: any resident can file an envy or resentment complaint against a neighbor whose house, car, or lifestyle seems way beyond what they could of earned by working. If the city investigates and finds the extra spending traces to inheritance, capital gains, or some other unearned luck, the neighbor pays a surtax on it and the person who complained gets a 10 percent cut. The pitch is that envy and resentment aren't just personal vices—they're a kind of sensor for inequality nobody earned, so the city should pay people to use them. Walk me through how this actually plays out. Who files and who gets targeted? What do people do differently once it's known? Where does it get gamed first, and does it end up exposing real injustice or just punishing anyone who visibly succeeds? Is there a fix that wouldn't kill the whole point?
