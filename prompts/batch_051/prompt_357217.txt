The top-level split should be the fundamental vs reducible vs eliminable question — Russell thought causation was a bygone relic physics had outgrown, so make sure a Russell-style eliminativism is reachable. Leaves I need at minimum: a primitivist view, a Humean regularity view, a Lewis-style counterfactual view, a Woodward-style interventionist view, a Russell-style eliminativism, and if it drops out naturally, a projectivist view where causal talk is useful but not fact-stating. Then do a pass through the logical space rather than the list of names: which combinations of node answers are unoccupied, and say for each whether its empty because the combo is incoherent, because nobody would want it, or because nobody's explored it yet. Also flag pairs of leaves your tree can't actually seperate — I suspect the counterfactual and interventionist leaves need a carefully chosen question about whether the causal facts are model-relative, so handle that head-on. Keep it under about eight leaves and phrase every node so a first-year who's never heard the terms could still answer it.
