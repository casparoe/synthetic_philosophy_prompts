Write me a deadpan satirical letter from the CEO of a fictional frontier AI lab — around 600 words, in the voice of a quarterly shareholder update — in which the lab proudly discloses that it has absorbed a 6% alignment tax this year and will keep doing so. The joke has to be carried by the accounting rather than by the CEO winking or being a cartoon villain. The target is the way the competitiveness constraint and the alignment tax actually show up in lab communications: everyone says they'd slow down if only the race would let them, and safety budgets keep purchasing optionality rather than restraint. Exagerate that real feature rather than inventing a strawman, and a specialist should recognize the satire as fair. Stay deadpan throughout, but still make clear to the reader what's being ironized. Start with the letter itself — no preface, no commentary, no restating the prompt.
