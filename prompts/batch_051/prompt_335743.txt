Yesterday a friend and I spent the evening arguing about whether Hobbes's account in Leviathan of the passage from the state of nature to a commonwealth is internally coherent, and the disagreement kept returning to two of his claims that look, on their face, hard to hold together. Early in the work, in his account of the natural condition of mankind, Hobbes maintains that where there is no common power to hold everyone in fear, there is no law and therefore no justice or injustice; nothing a person does can be called unjust, because the very notions of right and wrong have no foothold in such a condition. In the chapter that follows, when he defines the right of nature and the laws of nature, he seems to say two things that point in opposite directions. On the one hand, he defines the right of nature as each person's complete liberty to use their own power in whatever way their own judgment and reason tell them is the means best suited to preserving their own life; from this it follows, he says, that so long as this war of each against all lasts, every person has a right to everything whatsoever, including the use of another person's body. On the other hand, he immediately argues that reason discovers certain laws of nature: the first commands each person to seek peace only to the extent that peace can realistically be hoped for, and to use all the helps and advantages of war where it cannot; the second requires each person to lay down this unlimited right to all things, once others are willing to do the same, and to rest content with no more liberty against others than they would allow others against themselves.

The apparent contradiction is therefore twofold. There are said to be no laws at all in the state of nature, and yet there are said to be natural laws that direct conduct in exactly that condition; and a right to do absolutely anything for self-preservation is said to coexist with a law that forbids some of those very same acts. Can these claims be reconciled, and at what cost? I am not looking for a bare label but for the actual cutting of the joints.
