ok so heres a slightly cursed assignment: I'm organizing a workshop where a Christian exclusivist (salvation requires explicit faith in Christ) and a religious pluralist (the major world religions are all valid paths to the same ultimate reality) have agreed to do a genuine adversarial collaboration instead of the usual debate, and I need the joint statement thier handlers would actually let them put both names on.

Write it as the document itself. Start with a numbered list of agreed claims — substantive ones, not just 'we both care about truth' — where you'd be willing to defend that each side really would sign each sentence. Then do a disagreements table. Each row: the sub-question; the exclusivist's position in wording an exclusivist would own; the pluralist's position in wording a pluralist would own; and a verdict on whether the two rows actually contradict, or are partly a verbal dispute. Then list the cruxes — the sub-questions that, if settled, would actually move at least one side — and for each crux say what sort of argument, case, or evidence both parties would accept as bearing on it, and record each side's prediction in advance.

Be genuinely tough on the verbal-disagreement question. I want you to pin down where they mean different things by 'salvation', 'truth', 'exclusive', or 'valid path', and say which disagreements would survive even after the vocabulary is fixed. Don't soften the exclusivist into a pluralist by another name, and don't turn the pluralist into a relativist just to make the table come out clean. Keep it around 900 words.
