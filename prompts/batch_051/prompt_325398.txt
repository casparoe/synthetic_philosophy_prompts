I am writing a short statement of my views on cultural value for a colloquium, and I would like an audit of whether the following commitments fit together before I defend them. I find that I am committed to all of the following, but I am not sure the set is coherent.

(a) Natural languages, musical traditions, and craft practices can have final value, not merely instrumental value. Their disappearance is a loss in what the world contains that is not reducible to anyone's welfare, pleasures, or preferences.

(b) The value of such a form is anchored in the form of life it makes available to those who live it. A practice that no longer shapes anyone's life, and survives only in recordings, museums, or performances staged for outsiders, has already ceased to be valuable in the relevant sense.

(c) Each culture supplies its own internal standard of value. There is no neutral standpoint from which to rank whole cultures, languages, or ways of life against one another, and attempts at such global comparison are not merely difficult but presumptuous.

(d) Every natural language embodies a unique and irreplaceable way of inhabiting the world, and no language is more valuable, as a language, than any other.

(e) When preservation resources are scarce, they should go first to languages and practices still being transmitted to children, because only these can be kept alive as living practices rather than as documentation or salvage.

(f) The value of a cultural form never justifies forcing or pressuring people to continue it. Individuals have an absolute right to exit any tradition, and particular practices can be criticized from outside on ordinary moral grounds even when the culture they belong to is valuable.

Please work through these commitments. Which pairs are genuinely in tension, and which only appear to be? For each genuine tension, rank it by severity and say what the least costly revision would be and what it costs—which commitment would have to give, and what would be lost by giving it up. For each merely apparent tension, briefly explain why it dissolves. Finally, identify the single revision that would do the most to make the whole set coherent, and say what it would cost.
