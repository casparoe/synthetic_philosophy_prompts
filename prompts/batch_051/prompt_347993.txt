Okay so in my philosophy club we read about this wasp (I forget the exact species, some kind of digger wasp) that does a super elaborate routine every time it brings a cricket back to its burrow: it leaves the cricket at the entrance, goes inside to check the tunnel, comes back out, and then drags the cricket in. If you move the cricket a few inches while the wasp is inside checking, it comes out, sees the cricket isn't exactly where it left it, drags it back to the entrance, and goes inside to check again. You can do this a bunch of times and it never catches on. It looks like it's being careful, but it's really just stuck in a loop. Some people in my club have started calling behavior like that sphexish.

I've been using three criteria to decide whether something is sphexish (rigid/mechanical) or actually flexible:

(1) it follows a fixed sequence of steps;
(2) when the situation changes in an obvious way, the agent doesn't adjust the plan;
(3) the agent keeps going even when the whole point of the action is already defeated.

Now here's the borderline case. My friend Leo runs basically his whole life through a weighted-decision app. For both big stuff (whether to break up with his girlfriend, which colleges to apply to) and small stuff (what to eat, whether to go to a party), he types in his goals, gives them weights, and goes with whatever scores highest. He definitly updates the goals and weights every few months after journaling, and maybe one time in twenty he'll override the app if he feels like it's really off. But normally he treats the score as his final decision. The app doesn't hand him a fixed set of actions; it's more like a general scoring rule he feeds different decisions into. One thing that makes it feel wasp-like though: if something important changes right after he runs the app, he usually won't re-run it until his next monthly reset.

So is Leo sphexish under my three criteria? Is he free/flexible in a way the wasp isn't, or is he just a wasp with more parameters? Go criterion by criterion, and also say whether his case shows that one or more of my criteria are missing something. Please don't just say humans are more complicated than wasps, I want an actual verdict on the criteria.
