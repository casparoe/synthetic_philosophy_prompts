I am trying to work out whether two positions I find persuasive can be held together, and I want the tension forced into numbers rather than left as a mood. The first position is broadly longtermist: if there is a non-negligible chance that decisions made in the coming centuries influence how many lives, and of what quality, come to exist across a very long future, then expected-value reasoning makes those far-future consequences dominate my priorities, since the potential numbers are astronomically large. The second position is that the simulation argument is difficult to escape: if conscious minds can in principle be implemented computationally and technologically mature civilizations could run enormous numbers of ancestor simulations, then one seems forced to assign high probability to at least one of the following — that civilizations at roughly our stage almost never reach that capacity, that they almost universally decline to run such simulations, or that we are ourselves in a simulation.

My question is whether the second position should substantially deflate the first, and I want your answer as a decomposition, not a bare conclusion. Give me your probability for the proposition that, for someone who accepts both positions, the simulation argument substantially dampens far-future fanaticism; take substantial dampening to mean that it at least halves the expected-value weight assigned to consequences more than a few centuries out, relative to ignoring the simulation argument. Then assign probabilities to at least these sub-claims: that we are in a simulation; that conditional on being in a simulation, our present choices still influence far-future welfare in roughly the way they would in a base-level world; that a typical non-simulated civilization genuinely produces far-future value on the scale longtermism needs; and that simulated future beings, if our choices do affect them, count morally in the same way as base-level future beings. Show how these combine into your overall number, identify the sub-claim to which the number is most sensitive, and state what evidence or argument about that sub-claim would move you most.
