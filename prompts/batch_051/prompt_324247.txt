I cant stop turning over an asymmetry in what we can get away with saying, and I'd like you to help me figure out whether it has any rational basis or whether it's just a weird convention of conversation. Take the sentence, It's raining, but I don't believe it is. The situation it describes is completely possible: the rain is falling, and I happen to have a missing or mistaken belief about it. Yet if I actually say that sentence out loud, I sound incoherent. Compare: It's raining, but she doesn't believe it is. Perfectly fine. It was raining, but I didn't believe it was. Also fine, just a report of a past mistake. The same pattern seems to hold for knowledge: It's raining, but I don't know it is sounds absurd, while It was raining, but I didn't know it was does not.

So the oddness is specific to reports of my own present-tense belief or knowledge. What I want to know is whether anything justifies treating those first-person present-tense reports as special in this way, or whether the asymmetry is just a surface rule about assertion with no deeper significance. Don't just tell me the sentence is self-contradictory, because it isn't: the state of affairs is possible. A friend said this thing has a name, Moore's paradox, but the name is all I've got.

If you think the asymmetry is justified, run your account through these nearby cases and say where it draws the line: It's raining, but I'm not sure it is; It's raining, but I can't prove it is; It's raining, but I doubt it is; It's raining, but I don't know it is. And then there's the case that got me into this: after a breakup I kept saying I know the relationship is over, but I don't believe it is. That sentence has the same shape as the rain sentence, but it sounds like something a person in grief or heartbreak could mean, not like a logic glitch. Is that difference real, or are we just being charitable in one case and pedantic in the other?

Finally, please keep clearly separated what is actually settled about this from what is genuinely contested among people who work on it. I dont want a contested account presented to me as if it were the consensus.
