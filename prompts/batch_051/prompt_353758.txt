I'm trying to figure out what I actually think makes a statement racist, going by my gut on a bunch of cases before I commit to any theory. Here are my verdicts:

- A white guy in a bar rant calls a Black bouncer the n-word. Obviously racist.
- A white teacher reads the n-word out loud from Huckleberry Finn in class while discussing the novel. I don't think that's racist.
- A national politician in a stump speech denounces "the welfare queens" and "the inner-city thugs"—no slurs, nothing explicitly about race. I want to say that's racist, or at least a dog whistle.
- The same politician, asked about immigration, starts going on about "globalists" running the media. I honestly go back and forth: it sets off my dog-whistle alarm but I'm not sure I'd call it racist or just adjacent.
- A white colleague tells an Asian-American coworker "you're so articulate." I think it's racist, or at least racialized in a way that counts, even though it's a compliment on the surface.
- A different colleague says "Asians are good at math." That also feels racist to me, even though it's positive.
- In a policy argument about criminal justice, someone says "well, Black men commit a disproportionate share of violent crime" as a pure statistic. I actually don't think the statement itself is racist, though I'd be suspicious about why it was brought up.
- A comedian who's a member of the group uses a slur for their own group in a joke. I don't think that's racist, or if it is it's a very different kind of thing.
- Someone tells a Black person "where are you really from?" after they already said Chicago. I think that's racist, even if the asker is genuinely curious and means nothing by it.
- Someone says "I don't see color" while aruging affirmative action should end. I don't think the sentence is racist, but I have this itch that in that setting it's doing something racist.

Is there a single principle that gets all or most of these, and what does it make me say about the case I keep flipping on, the "globalists" one? And if no clean principle gets them all, which of my verdicts should I drop first?
