I've been trying to write down what I actually believe about grammar, and I keep feeling like there's an inconsistency somewhere but I can't locate it. Here's the list:

- No dialect a child picks up natively is structurally sloppier or less logical than standard English.
- "Whom did you see?" isn't more grammatical than "Who did you see?" — it's just more formal.
- "Between you and I" is just wrong, not merely informal.
- Dictionaries should describe how people actually speak and write, not prescribe how they should.
- If a student writes "I ain't got no time" in an essay, I mark it wrong, even though I'd never say the sentence itself is ungrammatical.
