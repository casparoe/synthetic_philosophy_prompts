I've been reading Hume on causation for a grad seminar and then fell into the secondary literature, and I can't get a grip on why the standoff between the regularist reading and the New Hume reading has lasted as long as it has. On the regularist side, Hume's whole move is to cash causation out as regular succession plus the mind's habit of inference; the necessity we attribute to the cause–effect relation isn't in the objects, it's a projection from the felt compulsion in us, and there's nothing more to causation than the patterns. On the New Hume side, people like Strawson say Hume is only committed to the claim that we can't discover or form a contentful idea of any real bond in nature, not that no such bond exists; he can consistently leave room for hidden powers or unknown necessities in the objects that ground the regularities, and he may even think there must be something like that, since the regularities themselves cry out for explanation.

What's odd to me is that both sides are working from mostly the same passages in the Treatise and the first Enquiry, and the debate has been running for decades with no visible convergence. My working theory is that they're answering different questions under one heading: the regularists are doing metaphysics (what does Hume's finished picture leave in the world?) and the New Humeans are doing exegesis (what is Hume's empiricism actually strong enough to rule out?). But I'm not confident — maybe it's a real substantive dispute about whether you can be a causal realist while remaining an empiricist, or a mostly verbal fight about what 'necessary connection' is even supposed to mean. I'd like a diagnosis of the stalemate, not a verdict on the fight: what kind of disagreement is this, and what would actually count as progress given the diagnosis? A clearer distinction between exegesis and metaphysics, new textual evidence, a formal clarification, or should we just expect the standoff to persist because the two sides start from different intuitions about what Hume would have found acceptable?
