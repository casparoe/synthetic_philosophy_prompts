My friend's summary was roughly this: Hegel is defending war as a kind of moral therapy. Hegel's point, my friend insisted, is not merely that war has this effect but that the effect is ethically necessary and valuable; that prolonged or perpetual peace would leave nations corrupt, the way a prolonged calm leaves the sea foul (he paraphrased the very next sentence of the remark); and that we should therefore regard war not as an evil to be overcome but as a rationally necessary, even welcome, feature of the ethical world.
