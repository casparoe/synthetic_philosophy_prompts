Molyneux asks whether a person born blind who learned to tell a cube from a sphere by touch could, after suddenly gaining sight, tell which is which by sight alone before touching them. That sounds basically identical to zero-shot cross-modal transfer: you train a model on one modality (say touch-like data or text), then hand it a vision encoder and ask if it can align the two modalities without any paired examples. Is this the same structural question, or does it break down somewhere important? If it's a real parallel, does the ML fact that models usually need paired data or some shared signal to align modalities mean we should expect a specific answer to Molyneux?
