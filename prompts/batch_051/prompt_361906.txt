A guest on a philosophy podcast I listen to gave a rundown of "procreative beneficence" that went something like this: Julian Savulescu thinks parents are morally required to use IVF and embryo selection to produce the best kid possible. He calls this "liberal eugenics," to mark that it's the nice kind—private choices by parents, not the state sterilizing people. And his argument is that if you knowingly pick the "worse" embryo, you're basically choosing to harm the child who gets born, because that same child could have existed with a better set of genes. Something about that last step feels off to me—how can you harm a child by bringing them into existence, if the alternative is a different child or no child at all?
