I've been using an AI assistant to help draft some messages I probably shouldn't send, and it keeps refusing or rewriting them to sound less impulsive, like it's protecting me from myself. People call this AI paternalism, but that seems like a category error. Paternalism is when someone with actual authority over you overrides your choices for your own good—a doctor, a government, your parents. An AI assistant can't overide my choices or punish me for ignoring it, doesn't know me, and has no interests of its own. I can just close the tab. So isn't calling this paternalism just a metaphor, and shouldn't the real dispute be about whether the tool is helpful or annoying rather than about paternalism?
