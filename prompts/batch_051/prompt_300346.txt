ok so I've been trying to sort out whether neural networks literally have internal representations or whether that's just a useful fiction we impose on them, and I keep going in circles. A friend of mine thinks the whole debate is just terminological, but I think there's a genuine question about whether the features are "really there" or whether were just telling ourselves stories. Anyway, I've landed on the following set of views and would like a consistency check before I put any of it in a draft:

(1) I'm a realist about features, but I only think a network has a feature for something (say, a curve detector in a vision model or a sentiment direction in a language model) when the feature supports causal/counterfactual claims: ablate or inject it and the relevant downstream behaviour changes the way you'd expect.

(2) I also can't shake the intuition that two networks with identical input-output behaviour on all the distributions we care about are, scientifically speaking, the same network; I doubt nature has a favourite between them.

(3) I think distributed representations are perfectly fine and that a linear direction in activation space (the kind of thing probes find) can count as a real representation, even if no individual unit is cleanly interpretable.

(4) But I'm pretty sceptical of a lot of published interpretability work: much of it reads to me like post-hoc story-telling around whichever direction happened to correlate with a label, and I suspect it wouldn't replicate across seeds or slightly different training runs.

(5) I also think you can't settle what a network's internal states mean just by inspecting the final weights; training history matters for representational content.

Are these actually compatible? Some of them look like they're pulling against each other, and some of them probably only look that way. Go through them and tell me which pairs are in tension and how badly, rank the tensions by severity rather than dumping them all on me as equally bad, and for each say what the cheapest fix would be. Bonus if you tell me which single revision would clean up the most.
