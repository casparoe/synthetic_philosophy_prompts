I am a novelist writing a book whose central character is a machine — call it the Lexographer — that has acquired a complete human language from texts, dictionaries, and conversation alone: it has never seen, touched, caused, or been affected by any of the things its words denote. The plot turns on whether its linguistic behaviour amounts to understanding or only to fluent mimicry, and on whether its utterances can be true or false in the way a person's are. I do not want to characterise this machine from the usual caricatures, so I need the philosophical specification before I settle its inner life.
