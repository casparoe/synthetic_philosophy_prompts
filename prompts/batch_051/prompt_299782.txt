Imagine a careenium: a vast, frictionless surface tiled with two-state dominoes. A falling domino knocks over its neighbors; after each wave of toppling passes, the fallen dominoes are reset; and the tiles have been wired so that the whole cascade implements some large deterministic computation. Read at the lowest level, the system contains no causes except local domino-to-domino pushes. Read at a coarser level, the cascades contain stable, self-preserving patterns — call them simmballs — whose interactions are usefully described in semantic terms: they detect one another, form intentions, and act so as to satisfy them. The puzzle the careenium is meant to sharpen is this: when we explain a simmball’s movement by its beliefs or desires, are those semantic features genuinely pushing individual dominoes over, or is the semantic story merely an economical summary of a process whose only real causes are local collisions? In other words, do symbols push the substrate around?

Now change one thing. Suppose each domino has a small, fixed, independent probability epsilon that, after a wave passes, it takes the state opposite to the one its local rule prescribes: occasionally the wrong domino falls, or the right domino fails to fall. This error rate is common knowledge among observers, and the simmball patterns remain identifiable, though now only probabilistically.

Work through the noiseless and noisy careeniums side by side. First give the strongest domino-only, epiphenomenalist argument for the original setup, and the strongest semantic-causal, real-patterns reply. Then determine what the epsilon-noise does to that exchange: which position survives the modification, and does the noise change what the debate is really about? Is there a threshold — in epsilon, or in the system’s capacity to detect and correct errors — beyond which the semantic level stops predicting, stops existing, or stops being entitled to causal explanations? And does the point at which the semantic level breaks down illuminate whether it was doing genuine causal work before the noise was added?
