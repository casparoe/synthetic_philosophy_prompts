What theory of competition — and what picture of how character gets formed — does a policy like that presuppose? Reconstruct the implicit view as carefully as you can, say whether the pieces actually need the same theory or are philosophically underdetermined, and tell me what the school would have to change if it held the opposite view, that competition builds character. Act like a professional philosopher of education who has thought a lot about competition, rivalry, and moral development; start by brainstorming the possible presuppositions before you settle on the reconstruction.
