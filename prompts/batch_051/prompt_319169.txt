(1) For actions a and states s, define regret R(a,s) as the gap between what a pays in s and the best any available action pays in s, i.e. max_b u(b,s) - u(a,s). (2) Minimax regret ranks actions by their maximum regret, so it picks the action with the largest minimum of v(a,s) = u(a,s) - max_b u(b,s). (3) Picking the action with the largest minimum score just is the maximin rule, applied to v rather than u. (4) Since v is just u with a correction term subtracted, maximin and minimax regret are the same rule written in different payoff units; any ranking one gives, the other can give by re-labeling the payoffs. (6) So either maximin and minimax regret are secretly one rule that can contradict itself, or step 4's re-labeling isn't the innocent move it looks like.
