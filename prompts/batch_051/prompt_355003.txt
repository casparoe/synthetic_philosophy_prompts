For the science-and-ideas section I edit, I'd like a piece of sustained satire. Write a mock page from a biotechnology company's website promoting a new cognitive-enhancement transdermal patch that is sold only as a compulsory bundle with a moral-enhancement patch. The company's copy should present, in earnest biotech marketing idiom, the Persson–Savulescu line that human moral motivation has not kept pace with technological power, so that cognitive enhancement without moral enhancement is too hazardous to leave to consumer choice. Include indications, a list of contraindications, a short FAQ, and a statement from the chief ethics officer. Keep it around 600 words and keep it deadpan. The humour should come from the philosophy itself: who programmes the moral parameters, whether the executives are themselves enhanced, what becomes of autonomy and reactive attitudes such as righteous indignation, and the fact that the firm is presenting compulsion as a benefit. It should remain legible to a reader who has never heard of Persson and Savulescu while reading as fair and informed to someone who knows the moral bioenhancement debate. Please write in British English.
