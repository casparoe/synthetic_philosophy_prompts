I am drafting a policy on when students, clinicians, and researchers in our program may let AI systems do work they used to have to do themselves, and I have noticed in myself and our trainees that conclusions now arrive faster than the understanding that used to accompany them. Then say which desiderata are non-negotiable and which may be one camp's prejudices, flag any pair you suspect cannot be jointly satisfied, name the single requirement that most excludes candidate theories, and address the baseline problem: erosion relative to what — the person's earlier ability, their counterfactual ability without the tool, or the ability of the wider human-tool system?
