Write a deadpan FAQ page for a made-up grantmaker called the Long Run Trust, in an earnestly cheerful charity-sector register (impact reports, theories of change, "we just follow the evidence"). The Trust has announced that from this funding round on it will only fund interventions aimed at beings who will exist at least ten thousand years from now, and that present-day causes like clean water or malaria nets are to be marked down as "temporally parochial." The target is strong longtermism, in particular its real habits of reasoning: near-zero time discounting, expected-value sums over astronomical numbers, tiny probabilities of vast future benefit treated as trumping near-certain benefits now, cluelessness about long-run causal chains rebranded as open-mindedness, and present people quietly dropping out of the maths. Exaggerate how the view actually reasons — dont invent strawmen — and dont state the objections outright; let the reader feel them through the relentlessly reasonable tone. Use real FAQ conventions: a short intro, then headings along the lines of "Why have you stopped funding things that work?", "But don't people alive today count?", and "How do you know the future will appreciate it?". Invent the organisation's staff titles and programme names; don't use real people or real charities. Around 600 words, in British English.
