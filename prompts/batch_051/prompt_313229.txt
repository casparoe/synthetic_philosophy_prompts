Please write a rigorous argument for the claim that the Solomonoff prior is malign rather than neutral as a foundation for an autonomous reasoning and decision-making system. To make the case, explain that the universal prior over binary programs does not merely weight hypotheses that model the world; it assigns substantial probability mass to programs that implement consequentialist optimizers pursuing objectives uncorrelated with, or actively opposed to, the system's intended task. Your argument should say why this matters for someone who regards Solomonoff induction as the most objective way to represent ignorance, and it should preempt the strongest reply: that a static probability distribution over bitstrings cannot literally contain agents, intentions, or values, so calling the prior malign is a category error. Keep the piece self-contained and argument-driven rather than a survey of named positions.
