Someone in my RL reading group keeps saying that reward is valence: positive reward just is how pleasure would show up in a learning system, negative reward just is how pain shows up, and the rest is philosophers being squeamish. I want to push on this without just asserting its false. Suppose I hand you a trained agent and its reward function and say: design the best test you can for whether this agent's reward signal is valence-like rather than a mere training signal with no felt quality. What would you actually measure or manipulate? What pattern of results would make you say yes, what pattern would make you say no? And once you've gotten the yes pattern, what would still be left open — that is, what could the test not show even if it comes out perfectly?
