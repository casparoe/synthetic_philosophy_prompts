I work on welfare certification for farmed seafood, and I keep running into a pattern I do not know how to make sense of. In the same countries that require detailed pre-slaughter stunning for cattle, pigs, and poultry, farmed fish are in much of the industry killed by asphyxiation in air or immersion in an ice slurry, with no dedicated stunning step; electrical stunning of fish remains the exception. Shrimp are commonly chilled and then have their heads removed while many are still alive, and in shrimp hatcheries the females used for breeding routinely have one eyestalk severed or crushed off, without anesthesia, to force spawning. Meanwhile, the sector itself carefully manages oxygen, ammonia, temperature, and handling stress during grow-out, because stressed fish grow poorly and die. And several regulators now classify decapod crustaceans as sentient for animal welfare purposes, yet hatchery and slaughter practice has changed little.

I want to know what philosophical view of sentience and moral status this industry pattern presupposes. Reconstruct the implicit theory as precisely as you can. Specifically: Does the practice strictly presuppose that fish and decapod crustaceans do not feel pain, or does it merely presuppose that whatever they feel counts less, or that it may be discounted because it is harder for us to read? Does the combination of careful stress management during grow-out with indifference at slaughter presuppose inconsistent commitments, or can a single view explain both? Where is the practice philosophically underdetermined, such that several rival theories of moral status would license exactly the same behavior? And if the sector adopted a precautionary principle — treat an animal as sentient for welfare purposes whenever the evidence for a capacity for felt pain is at least as strong as the evidence for a behavioral stress response, and err toward protection under uncertainty — what would it have to change first, and which current practices would be hardest to justify under that principle?

Please write your answer in American English. I am looking for the reconstructed theory and the argument for it, not a survey of the literature or a reading list.
