- On the Irish famine he considered the potato blight against the British government's relief failures and continued food exports, and ruled for the blight.

What should I predict he'll rule? If I want the prediction that coheres best with his track record, which of his earlier rulings is the closest analog, what principle would each possible ruling be attributing to him, and what further case would settle which principle he's actually using?
