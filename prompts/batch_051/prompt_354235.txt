Someone removes the condom without saying anything: invalid. Someone lies about having been tested and being clean: invalid. Someone lies about being married so a date will sleep with them: I also want to say invalid, though I can't say exactly why. But if someone lies about their job, says they're a surgeon when they wait tables, I think the consent is still valid even if the other person wouldn't have gone to bed with a waiter. On coercion: a boss who says 'sleep with me or you're fired' gets invalid consent; a boyfriend who says 'sleep with me or I'm breaking up' gets... I guess valid, though it leaves a bad taste. On competence: blackout drunk, no; tipsy and clearheaded, yes; mild intellectual disability with real understanding, yes; advanced dementia, no. Is there a single principle that delivers all of these, or close? If not, which of my verdicts should I overturn?
