One thing I've never been able to get consistent about is what we owe to insects and other small pests. Here are my verdicts, going by gut. I'll slap a mosquito that's biting me on the arm without a second thought, and in someone else's kitchen I'd swat one against the wall and not feel bad. When ants show up in the kitchen every spring I put out bait traps, and im not exactly sorry it wipes out the colony over a few days, even though that's a slow way to go. But I won't use glue traps for a cockroach: killing the roach feels fine, but leaving it stuck there to starve feels like something I don't want to be doing. There's a spider in the corner of my bedroom right now and I leave it alone, and when one gets in the bathtub I carry it outside in a cup. Yet I killed the aphids on my pothos with soap spray, which meant killing a lot of them, and I'd do it again tomorrow. I pick Japanese beetles off the roses and drop them in soapy water, but I have to steel myself a little; I'd rather flick a caterpillar off a tomato plant than squish it, even though that probably just puts it back on the soil to climb up again. Ticks on the dog, fleas, and lice I kill without thinking of it as anything at all. A wasp nest by the front door I'd spray, and bedbugs I'd pay to have the whole place fumigated and never look back. But a bumblebee that gets into the house, I'll spend half an hour trying to shoo out a window.

The rough rule I keep reaching for is something like: it's fine to kill them when they intrude on my space or my family's bodies, but less fine when I'd be reaching into their world to kill them, and the method matters because slow is worse. But that rule would make the bedroom spider fair game, and I don't act that way. It also doesn't explain why aphids on a houseplant I don't much care about are fair game, while one caterpillar on the same plant would get a stay of execution if I could find somewhere to put it.

So: is there a principle that captures all of these, or nearly all of them, and what does it commit me to? In particular, what should I say about the thing I keep going back and forth on — puting up a bug zapper in the backyard that will kill mostly harmless moths and beetles for the sake of a few fewer mosquitoes at dusk. And which of my verdicts, if any, does the principle force me to give up?
