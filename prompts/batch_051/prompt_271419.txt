Take that translation as far as it can honestly go. Then give me the residue report: what maps cleanly, what had to be fudged, what has no counterpart in physics at all. One thing to face directly: I can't see how something with no internal states can know one contingent future rather than another—that looks like a channel-capacity problem to me.
