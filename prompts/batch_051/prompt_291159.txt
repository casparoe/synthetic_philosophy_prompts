Treat the decision to launch the Space Shuttle Challenger on January 28, 1986, as a case study for antimemetics: the idea that some information has properties that make it resist being remembered, propagated, or acted upon. The salient facts, since the model answering may not be able to look them up: on the night before the launch, engineers from the solid-rocket-booster contractor Morton Thiokol argued that the O-ring seals had shown erosion and blow-by on earlier flights and would be more likely to fail in the unusually cold temperatures forecast for the launch, and they recommended delaying; after a private caucus and pressure from NASA managers to show why the no-launch position should prevail, Thiokol management reversed the recommendation and signed off on the launch; the O-rings failed shortly after ignition and the crew was lost. You need not retell any more of the story than that.

What I want is a disciplined mapping that runs in both directions. First, identify the strongest candidate antimemes in the episode: exactly which claim or warning was supposed to resist propagation, who held it, through which channels it moved or failed to move, and at what specific point the transmission broke down. Second, make the antimeme concept do genuine work by distinguishing an idea that fails to spread because of something about the idea itself from an idea that fails to spread because an institution is structured against it, because an audience is motivated not to know it, or because it is genuinely uncertain and under-evidenced at the decision time. Third, run the case the other way: does Challenger support an antimemetic analysis, or does it show that the framework mostly relabels older, better-understood phenomena such as normalization of deviance, groupthink, production pressure, and ordinary information loss across organizational boundaries? Where does an antimemetic account make predictions that those standard accounts do not, and where is it silent? Finally, use the episode to state what would count as a real antimeme rather than merely a difficult-to-transmit engineering judgment, and say whether that distinction survives contact with what actually happened.

Throughout, separate what is settled about the episode and about antimemetics from what is genuinely contested among people who know the material, and do not present a contested reading as the consensus.
