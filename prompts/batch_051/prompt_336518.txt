A friend of mine is an epiphenomenalist about consciousness: he thinks the brain causes phenomenal experience, but that the experience itself never causes anything, including his own judgments and reports about it. At the same time he says his introspective judgments (like the judgment that he is seeing red right now) are generally reliable. That combination seems a bit self-undermining to me, but I can't tell whether it's the kind of claim evidence could even touch. Is there an observation or experiment that would genuinely bear on whether first-person reports track the experiences themselves, as opposed to just tracking the underlying neural activity that (on his view) causes both? Design the strongest test you can, say exactly what result would count for or against, and be clear about what even a positive result would leave untouched.
