It feels like randomness can't be a property of an individual finite string, yet I keep having to judge individual finite strings: seeds for a generator, hashes, a lottery draw, a shuffled deck. Is there a name for this problem, or for the concept people use to say one finite string is more random than another? I'm not after just a label — I want the carving.
