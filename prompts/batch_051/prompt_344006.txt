Draft the joint closing statement of a good-faith adversarial collaboration between a committed defender of van Fraassen's Reflection principle and a committed opponent of it, the two positions that have each been accused by the other of either vacuity or neglecting obvious counterexamples. The statement should consist of a numbered agreed-claims section followed by a disagreements table. In the agreed section, include: a formulation of the principle both sides can sign — for an agent with current credence P_t and anticipated future credence P_{t+1}, P_t(A | P_{t+1}(A)=x) = x wherever the conditional is defined — together with its initial rationale, the agreed stress-test cases (anticipated memory loss, belief-inducing drugs, foretold future irrationality), and the qualification the defender now accepts in response to those cases. In the disagreements table, use columns for the crux, the case or argument both sides accept as bearing on it, the defender's prediction, and the opponent's prediction. Cover at least these cruxes: whether an agent can rationally be certain of later holding a credence she now regards as unsupported; whether Sleeping Beauty-style centered evidence undermines every non-vacuous version of the principle; and whether the qualified principle remains a substantive rational constraint or collapses into a theorem about credences that will arise by conditionalization. Phrase each side's commitments in wording that side would endorse.
