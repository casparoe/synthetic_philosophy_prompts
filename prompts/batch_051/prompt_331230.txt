I'm taking a course on stereotypes and prejudice this semester, and it's made me realize I have no consistent view about when it's okay to say something about a whole national group. I've been keeping a list, and my own reactions don't add up. When my sister was considering a job in Copenhagen, someone told her Danes are reserved with strangers and don't do small talk, and I thought that was a perfectly useful thing to know. A friend once said German engineering culture is thorough, which is why their cars feel overbuilt, and I just accepted it. But when a colleague looked at a resume and said "he's Nigerian, so he'll have that entrepreneurial drive," I flinched — even though, on paper, it's the same shape as the Denmark remark. Last month I read a piece saying Filipino nurses bring warmth and patience to the ward; it was positive, but I found it kind of invidious. My demographer friend said South Koreans sleep less on average because of work and study culture, and I thought that's just a statistical claim and totally fine. My uncle said Jews are naturally good with money, and I had no problem saying that one's just antisemitic, and not only because it's false. A teacher I know said Vietnamese immigrant kids do well at school because the parents push education, and I half-bought it and half-hated it. And when a Greek host told me "we Greeks are loud and argumentative, but that's how we show love," I thought it was fine — maybe because the group was talking about itself.

I've tried a few rules: maybe it's fine when it's about behavior or custom and not fine when it's about innate character; maybe it's fine when it's about a group you're entering and not fine when it's used to judge a particular person. But the teacher case and the Nigerian case each blow a hole in one of those. I can't tell wether there's a single principle underneath these verdicts or just my gut liking some groups and not others. Is there a coherent account that gets most of them right? If so, state it precisely, tell me which of my verdicts it forces me to drop, and say what it predicts for the cases I keep going back and forth on: a travel guide calling the Swiss punctual, and a charity appeal describing Haitians as resilient. I'd rather have a simple principle that overturns a few of my calls than an elaborate one that just re-describes them. Also, please write your answer in American English.
