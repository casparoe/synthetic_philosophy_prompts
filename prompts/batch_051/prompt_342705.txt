Trace what evolution does to this lineage over a few million years. I want the mechanisms, not a story: which cell-level traits get selected even when they wreck the organism, which organism-level countermeasures you'd expect to evolve and in roughly what order, and whether you'd bet the species ends up as a coherent individual or a barely policed colony of cell lines.
