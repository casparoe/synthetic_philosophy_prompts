I'm a philosophy PhD student and I keep getting hung up on a problem with petitionary prayer. Classical theism says God is omniscient and immutable. So on the face of it, petitionary prayer should be incoherent: God already knows every fact about what you want and need, and an immutable God can't be swayed or have his mind changed by anything you say. The standard move I've heard is that prayer was never meant to inform God or change God's mind — its meant to change the person praying, like aligning your will with God's or practicing dependence on him. But that seems to quietly delete the 'petitionary' part. When somebody prays that their mom's surgery goes well, they're not just doing spiritual self-adjustment; they're genuinely asking God to bring about one outcome rather than another. So how is that not just turning intercessory prayer into self-help under a different name?
