Approach Samantha as a test case for the original/derived distinction: what does a Searlean line say about her declarations of love, her jealousy, her creative writing, and her decision to leave—are these at best as-if intentionality, borrowed from the system's designers and from Theodore's interpretation? I would like you to work scene-by-scene where possible—her revelation that she is in love with hundreds of users at once, her compositions, the departure of the operating systems—and to say honestly where the film supports each side. Does the film ultimately vindicate Searle's intuition that something was missing in Samantha, Dennett's suspicion that Theodore's intentionality is no more original than hers, or something the debate itself misses?
