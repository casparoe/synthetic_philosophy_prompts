im a novelist, and for as long as ive been writing I've held something like the following: art is a commons and all art is theft. influence, homage, borrowing, pastiche — there's no principled line between a writer absorbing the culture around them and "appropriation." once you start saying who is allowed to write which material you've built a tribunal that has to rule on who is really "inside" a culture, and that seems like a dead end: cultures are porous, identity is complicated, and judging a book by the author's lineage instead of the sentences on the page is a category error with better PR. the view is tidy — evaluate the work, not the identity; nothing is off-limits, only badly done.

but there's a case that keeps bothering me. an outsider writes a novel built around a closed funerary practice from a small indigenous community — gets it half wrong, softens the parts the community considers unutterable, and uses the whole thing as a metaphor for the narrator's divorce. the book is praised for its "brave" ventriloquism, wins prizes, and the community's own writers — who've been trying to tell that story for years — can't get a publisher. i find myself thinking: something about that is wrong, and not just "sloppy research" or "bad art" wrong. morally wrong, in a way my tidy view seems unable to say.

you're a professional philosopher who works on aesthetics and the ethics of art. think carefully — maybe start by steelmanning both positions before you try to reconcile them. give me the strongest version of the theory i'm sitting on and the strongest version of the intuition that's challenging it. then walk me through what it would cost to give up each one, and whether there's a reading on which both survive — for instance, maybe the intuition isn't about the borrowing itself but about something else the borrowing does. is this a case where the intuition is a product of squeamishness or in-group protectiveness and should be discounted, or is it tracking something the theory leaves out? i don't just want a verdict — i want to know what would justify each move, wich parts of the theory would have to be amended for the intuition to be honored, and what would have to be true for the intuition to be the thing that goes. please write in American English.
