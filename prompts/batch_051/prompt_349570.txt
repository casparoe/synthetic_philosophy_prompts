I keep noticing a structural resemblance between Dennett's distinction between cranes and skyhooks and the distinction between constructive and nonconstructive existence proofs in mathematics, and I want to know whether the parallel is deep or superficial. On the Dennett side, a crane is a mindless, local, step-by-step process that builds complex design out of simpler parts already present, while a skyhook is a design-imposing force or event invoked without any mechanical account of how it itself came to be. On the mathematical side, a constructive proof of an existence claim supplies a procedure that actually builds the object, while a nonconstructive proof, say by contradiction or by appeal to the law of excluded middle or choice, guarantees that the object exists while supplying no recipe for producing it. The mapping seems natural: demanding cranes rather than skyhooks looks like demanding constructive rather than merely nonconstructive existence. But I am not sure the analogy carries weight. For one thing, nonconstructive existence is still accepted in classical mathematics as legitimate proof, whereas Dennett's claim is that skyhook explanations are not merely unauditable but false of the actual world. For another, cranes operate in time and matter, while mathematical constructions concern effective procedures under no empirical constraints. Are these real disanalogies, or merely differences in subject matter? Is the underlying norm no design without an account of its assembly the same norm as no existence claim without a witness? As a concrete test: proof mining is the program of extracting constructive content, such as explicit bounds or algorithms, out of classical nonconstructive proofs. If the parallel is deep, something analogous to proof mining ought to be possible for skyhook explanations, namely a method for turning an explanation that posits a design-imposing event into a crane-style mechanical account wherever such an account exists. Does that transfer work, and if not, where exactly does the analogy break? Please also name at least one prediction the parallel makes that turns out false. Answer in American English.
