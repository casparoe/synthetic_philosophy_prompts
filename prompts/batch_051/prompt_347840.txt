(g) A platform bans your account after a false report and you lose permanent access to a few thousand dollars' worth of games you'd bought.

My rough priors: (a) and (g) are clearly harms, (f) clearly isn't a harm (maybe wrong for other reasons, but not a harm), and I'm genuinely torn on (b) through (e). Propose the definition, run it through all seven, say where it gives the wrong answer or no answer, and either revise it or defend the surprising verdicts.
