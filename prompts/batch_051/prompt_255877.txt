As I understand it, the problem is this: a person blind from birth can distinguish a cube from a sphere by touch alone. If the person's sight were suddenly restored and they were shown the two shapes before being allowed to touch them, could they say which was the cube and which the sphere? The philosophical debate around it divides, roughly, between those who think the answer is yes because shape is represented in a way that is shared across the senses—something like an amodal representation—and those who think the answer is no because the connection between touch and sight has to be built up through correlated experience.
