I teach a final-year undergraduate seminar in the philosophy of logic and computation and am writing the take-home final. Draft one essay question on the epistemic status of the Church-Turing thesis, to be answered in 75 minutes, that cannot be answered by reciting the formula that the thesis is neither a mathematical theorem nor an ordinary empirical hypothesis. The question should make students earn that formula. Then provide a model answer of roughly 800 words and a marking scheme built around five must-hit points, the marks each is worth out of 20, and a one-line note on how candidates typically get each point wrong.
