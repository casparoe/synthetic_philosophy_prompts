Start from three concrete cases — the sorites heap, 'there will be a sea battle tomorrow', and 'the present king of France is bald' — and make each branch node a claim they can answer off those cases, like wether 'either it's a heap or it isn't' is still true mid-heap, or whether the sea-battle sentence is false now rather than just not yet true. Leaves should include at least hardline bivalence, epistemicism, supervaluationism, a Kleene-style three-valued logic, fuzzy degrees, and an intuitionist/constructivist response, each with a sentence on what makes it distinct. Flag any pairs of leaves the tree can't really separate, especially where bivalence and excluded middle get run together, and for combinations of answers nobody lands in, say briefly whether the view is incoherent or just unattractive.
