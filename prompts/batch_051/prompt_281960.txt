I work as a backend engineer, and most of my job involves systems that are observed through dashboards. To an operator, the dashboard looks like one coherent machine: memory gauge, request graph, a clean network diagram. Underneath, it is a swarm of shards, retries, replications, and stale reads, and the dashboard is a simplified projection rebuilt every few seconds. That gap between the neat interface and the messy substrate has started to bother me in a different direction: what if human consciousness is the same kind of thing? I keep seeing people claim that consciousness is a user illusion, and I tried to spell out the underlying argument as a numbered chain so I could debug it the way I would debug a data pipeline.

Here is what I get. (1) From the inside, experience presents itself as unified, continuous, and richly detailed, all happening on a single stage with a single point of view. (2) But nothing in the brain looks like that stage; neural processing is massively parallel and distributed, with no central place where everything is assembled, and with gaps the system seems to paper over in hindsight. (3) So the unity and detail I find in introspection are not a transparent readout of brain activity; they are actively constructed, the way a dashboard constructs a clean system state out of scattered distributed events. (4) That sort of construct is a user interface: it trades accuracy for tractability and represents the system through simplified, partly arbitrary objects. (5) What a user interface shows the operator is, in an important sense, an illusion; the folders, pointers, and gauges on screen do not correspond to real folders, pointers, and gauges in the hardware. (6) Therefore, the conscious scene I seem to inhabit is itself a user illusion, not a real feature of what my nervous system is doing.

The conclusion feels right to me, but I also have a nagging suspicion that step (5) is doing more work than it is licensed to do. Is the argument valid as written? If it is, what exactly does the conclusion rule in and rule out?
