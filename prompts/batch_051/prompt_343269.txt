For a third-year philosophy of technology course I'm building the take-home final. I need one essay question on AI paternalism: when an assistant may refuse or override a user request. I don't want a question students can answer by reciting helpful-harmless-honest boilerplate. Write the question (90 minutes, about 1,000 words expected, 40 marks), a model answer of roughly 900 words, and a marking scheme that names the specific moves that seperate a first from a 2:1 — for example, whether the student distinguishes requests based on false information from requests based on different weightings of risk, or distinguishes paternalism from deception.
