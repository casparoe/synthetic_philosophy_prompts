I've been trying to get straight what I think about material constitution — a thing and the stuff it's made of occupying the same place at the same time — and my gut verdicts are a bit of a mess. I'd like you to see whether there's a single principle behind them, and if not, which of my verdicts have to go.

Here are my verdicts on some cases:

1. Goliath and Lump. On Monday a sculptor shapes a lump of clay (call it Lump) into a statue of a horse (call it Goliath). I think there are two objects in that region. If you flatten the clay at noon, Goliath is destroyed but Lump survives, so they can't be the same thing.

2. A tree and the aggregate of its living cells. Here I think there's only one object. The tree doesn't seem like a second thing riding on top of the cell-aggregate. But I admit this sits badly with 1.

3. A person and their body. I think there's only one thing there. No souls in this question — I just mean the living human body. The person is the body, not something extra housed in it.

4. A plain lump of clay and the portion of clay that constitutes it. I also think only one object. But then why does molding the clay into a statue make a second object appear? That seems wierd to me, and it's the one I keep flip-flopping on.

5. A river and the water in it right now. I'm inclined to say the river isn't identical to the water in it, because the water is constantly being replaced while the river stays. But I also wouldn't say they are two objects sitting in the same place, because they don't actually coincide — the river and any particular water overlap only partially and briefly.

6. A flag and the piece of cloth it's made of. I want to say there are two things, like the statue and the clay: burn the flag and the flag is destroyed while the charred cloth still exists. But I'm less confident here than in 1.

The pattern I seem to like is: artifact-plus-matter gives two objects, while living-thing-plus-cells or matter-plus-matter gives one. But I worry that's just prejudice, not a principle. And person-plus-body is the case that makes me most nervous about it.
