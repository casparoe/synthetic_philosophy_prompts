"Nozick shows that any patterned principle of distributive justice — equality, need, desert, whatever — cannot survive the free choices of individuals. Then a million basketball fans each voluntarily pay Wilt Chamberlain twenty-five cents to watch him play, and he ends up with $250,000 more than before. Since every step was voluntary and nobody violated anybody's rights, D2 must be just too. If D2 does not fit your pattern, that just shows your principle was wrong to require that pattern. Liberty and patterns are therefore incompatible, so any state that uses progressive taxation to maintain a pattern is acting unjustly, because it forcibly takes from people what they have a right to keep."
