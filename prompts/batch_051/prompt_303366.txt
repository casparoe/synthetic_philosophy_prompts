So murderers and torturers walked free, provided their testimony was convincing enough. Defenders said the truth-telling bought the country a mostly peaceful transition, a public historical record, and maybe some general deterrence or at least a norm against future abuses; critics said it made a mockery of desert and let the guilty go unpunished.
