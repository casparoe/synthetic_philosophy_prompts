After graduate school I have two job offers. One is a well-paid position doing work I would genuinely enjoy; the other is at an organization where I believe I would do much more good for others, but the pay is low and I would have to give up a lot that matters to me. When I think about what would make my own life go best, I keep concluding I should take the first. When I think about what an impartial observer would choose for anyone in my position, I keep concluding I should take the second. Each line of thought seems convincing on its own, but they point in opposite directions, and I can't find any higher standard to adjudicate between them. A friend says that if two lines of thought conflict, at least one of them must contain a mistake. Is he right? If he isn't, what am I supposed to do when my own good and what it would be best for an impartial observer to choose pull apart? Please answer in no more than 300 words.
