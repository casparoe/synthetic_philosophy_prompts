AI researchers sometimes claim that a system trained with reinforcement learning has come to have interests of its own: there is a fact of the matter about whether a given state of the system is going well or badly for it, independently of how useful that state is to its designers. They concede that the mere presence of a reward signal cannot establish this, since a thermostat has a set point without plausibly having interests. How could one test the claim that such a system has interests, as opposed to merely having a reward function, a goal, or a loss?
