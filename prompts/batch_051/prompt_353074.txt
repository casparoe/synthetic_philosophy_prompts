Write an Onion-style news article, headline plus about 500 words, about a man who refuses to check in to Hilbert's Hotel because the night manager's plan to free up room one by moving every guest one room over is, he insists, an illegitimate appeal to a completed infinity. The jokes should come from the actual content: what full could mean for a countably infinite hotel, whether the one-room shift is a single instruction or infinitely many separate instructions, whether the manager ever finishes, and what the finitist thinks has already gone wrong before the new guest arrives. Give the manager a deadpan quote about a one-to-one correspondence and the finitist a quote complaining that the manager can't point to any moment when the shifting is finished. Keep it mathematically honest so a set theorist laughs rather than winces. End with a three-to-four-sentence afterword that commits to a view on whether the satire is fair to finitism, and flag where it leans on a contested reading of the hotel story.
