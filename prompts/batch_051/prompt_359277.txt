"Dennett's whole point is that minds aren't the foundation of design; they're a late product of mindless processes. Turing's inversion completed the picture by showing that even understanding itself can in principle be manufactured by a machine running through totally mechanical, non-understanding steps, so there's no need to posit a little 'you' sitting in a mental theater watching a screen.
