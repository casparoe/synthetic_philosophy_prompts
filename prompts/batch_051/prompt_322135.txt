There's one paragraph I keep coming back to, toward the end, where he says:

"The answer is that you have gone from a situation in which you count your own temporal location as irrelevant to the truth of H, to one in which you count your own temporal location as relevant to the truth of H."

(H is his label for the proposition that the coin landed Heads.) My brother says this paragraph is just smuggling in the thirder conclusion without earning it: Elga never explains why waking up should flip your whole way of thinking so that where you are in the day matters, when no new evidence arrived. I think the paragraph is basically right, but whenever I try to defend it I end up hand-waving about "perspective" and I can feel myself losing.
