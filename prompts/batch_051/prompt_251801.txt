The end-of-unit exam has to ask students which account is more likely accurate and why, instead of asking what happened. The stated goal is to stop the winners from writing the whole story. What happens when an event has overwhelming evidence on one side, or there isn't a real second side? And does the rule actually solve the winners-write-history problem, or does it just move the bias somewhere else? Are the failure modes fixable without gutting the whole idea?
