My angle isn't the usual post-hoc explainability or feature-attribution stuff; I want to argue that legibility should be built into the training objective itself, e.g. scoring reasoning traces on how coherent they look to a human auditor rather than only on whether the final answer matches the label. My rough argument is: evals and specs are always incomplete, so bare correctness on a benchmark hides a lot of failures; the gap between the benchmark and the real task is exactly where legibility buys you something; and legible models are cheaper to debug down the line, so the cost mostly evens out.
