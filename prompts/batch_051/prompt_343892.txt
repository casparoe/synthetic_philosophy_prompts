Among the people I argue with on rationalist-adjacent forums, the question why there is something rather than nothing functions as a Rorschach test: one camp hears a request for a cause of the universe, another hears a request for a selection principle over possibilities, and a third hears a pseudo-question to be dissolved. The result is that everyone scores points against positions nobody was defending. Before I engage with any further candidate answer, I want to fix the standard against which the candidates are being measured.

Write the desiderata that an adequate response to the question must meet, with the case or consideration behind each; treat the requirements themselves as the deliverable rather than as a preamble to defending one view. In particular: (i) list the cases or phenomena the response must get right or explicitly explain away — the apparent contrast between something and nothing, the explanatory remainder left after local explanations of why this rather than that, the modal datum that things seem able to have been otherwise, and the issue of whether nothing is even a coherent alternative for an explanation to range over; (ii) identify distinctions it cannot collapse, such as explaining the existence of contingent things versus explaining that there is a totality at all, or giving a cause versus a reason versus a ground; (iii) separate genuine requirements from camp-specific prejudices, and say for each whether it is negotiable — for example, must an adequate response be causal, must it leave no brute facts, must it not presuppose the existence of anything; (iv) flag any subset of the desiderata you suspect cannot be jointly satisfied — the triple of no brute facts, no self-explaining entity, and no infinite regress looks to me inconsistent, but I would like that checked carefully; and (v) say which single requirement does the most work in excluding the familiar candidate families: brute-fact naturalism, a necessary being, multiverse or anthropic selection, and dissolution of the question.

A brief scorecard against the list is welcome if it is useful, but keep the weight on the criteria and on why each is genuinely a criterion. Write in British English.
