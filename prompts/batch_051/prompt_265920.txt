Mechanistic anomaly detection rests on a distinction that is often left implicit: an unexpected output can reflect either the same mechanism operating in a novel regime, or a different mechanism that reproduces enough of the old behavior to look continuous. How would one test which of these states of affairs obtains in a concrete system—a regulatory network, a feedback controller, a metabolic pathway, or a trained model that has gone off-distribution? Give an experimental design, specify the observations that would favor each reading, and then say what the test would and would not settle about the identity of the mechanism. In particular, address whether the residue is just a practical limit of finite data or indicates that the notion of the same mechanism is not fully operationalizable.
