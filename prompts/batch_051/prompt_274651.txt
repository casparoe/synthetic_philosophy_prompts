Later Bella tells me that Alex is a pathological liar and I shouldn't believe anything he says. On the face of it, the two reports are exactly on a par. Now the argument:

(1) If anti-reductionism is true, then when Alex speaks I have a prima facie entitlement to believe Bella is a pathological liar, since at that point I have no defeater for that belief.
(2) Once I justifiably believe Bella is a pathological liar, I have a defeater for anything Bella tells me subsequently.
(3) So Bella's assertion that Alex is the liar gives me no justification to believe Alex is the liar; I should retain my belief that Bella is the liar.
(4) By parity, had Bella spoken first and Alex second, I'd be entitled to believe Bella and disregard Alex, and I should retain that too.
(5) So which report I end up believing depends entirely on who spoke first, even though the reports are symmetrically self-undermining.
(6) But justification cannot depend on something as arbitrary as the order in which I heard them.
(7) Therefore anti-reductionism is false.
