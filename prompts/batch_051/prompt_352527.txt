A law student friend sent me the provision she thinks is relevant. What I need to know is whether what we've been doing counts as arranging a surrogacy agreement, or promising to pay, "within the national territory." Walk me through the main ways of reading that phrase: the plain wording, the purpose of the provision, what the original 1992 legislators would most likely have said if they were asked about a US-based agency used from someone's couch abroad, and how a reasonable reader today would read it.
