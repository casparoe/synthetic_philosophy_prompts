I keep catching myself applying a double standard I'm not sure I can defend. If a senior dev goes easy on a messy PR from a junior teammate because the guy just went through a rough patch and needs a win, I call that compassion. If the same dev goes easy on the same messy PR from a contractor who went through the exact same rough patch, I catch myself calling it bias, like personal sympathy is contaminating the review. Same reviewer, same leniency, same excuse; the only clean difference is that the first person is inside the team and the second is not. Is there a real principal that makes partiality toward insiders different from partiality toward outsiders, or am I just dressing up in-group loyalty as a virtue? Give me the strongest version of the distinction you can and tell me where it breaks.
