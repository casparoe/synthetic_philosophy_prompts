Put strong reductionism or eliminativism, non-reductive physicalism (supervenience, multiple realizability), and strong emergence (novel powers, downward causation) into terms I actually use. Does that mean the whole dispute between reductionists and emergentists dissolves, or does it just mean my world has no construct for what emergentists are trying to say?
