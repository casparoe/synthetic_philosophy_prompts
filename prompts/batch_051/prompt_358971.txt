There is a familiar argument for treating a model's chain of thought as an at least approximately faithful report of its own reasoning: the model conditions on the intermediate tokens it has generated, so those tokens are literally part of the causal path to the final answer, and the explicit reasoning is therefore not merely post-hoc story. The development that troubles me is the field's shift from next-token prediction on human text to outcome-based reinforcement learning, where intermediate tokens are selected for contributing to a rewarded answer rather than for truthfully reporting the computation. Does the faithfulness argument survive this shift?
