I'm a novelist, and my next book is a barely fictionalized version of something awful that happened to my sister in her twenties. She's read the draft and says she's fine with it—if anything, she loves being the subject. But my mother says my sister doesn't understand what publication will actually do, and that even with permission I'd be turning her worst years into raw material for my career. I'm stuck between two rules I believe in to different degrees. Rule A: a competent adult's consent settles it—publish. Rule B: don't make art out of a loved one's trauma unless you can be certain you're not harming them. I lean maybe 60/40 toward Rule A. But if B is right and I publish, I'll have done something terrible to someone who trusts me; if A is right and I don't publish, I've just lost a book. Does that assymetry matter when I don't know which rule is the right one? Do I just follow the rule I think is more likely correct, or should my uncertainty itself change what I do? And is that even a coherent question, or am I confusing not knowing the facts with not being able to make up my mind?
