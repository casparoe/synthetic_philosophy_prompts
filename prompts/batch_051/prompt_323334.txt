Draft the joint statement of an adversarial collaboration between two camps about how to size repeated favorable bets. Camp A (the expected-utility camp) says the agent should just maximize expected utility over final wealth with whatever utility function they actually have, so Kelly sizing is at most one risk preference among many, not a rationality norm. Camp B (the geometric-rationality camp) says that for a sequence of multiplicative bets the non-arbitrary criterion is expected log growth, so Kelly or fractional-Kelly sizing has a special status other utility functions lack. Use this shared testbed throughout: an agent with $10,000 faces 200 independent flips of a biased coin that pays +50% on heads and loses 40% on tails (heads probability .55), may stake any fraction of current wealth before each flip, no leverage, no deposits or withdrawals. Produce a numbered agreed-claims section followed by a disagreements table. Nail down, in wording each side would accept: (1) the agreed mathematical facts, including what happens to arithmetic mean, geometric mean, and median terminal wealth as the number of flips grows; (2) the precise residual disagreement over whether the normative criterion is expected utility over terminal wealth, expected log growth, or something else; (3) the cruxes — e.g. whether the time-average argument still applies to the one-shot version of the same bet, and whether a log-utility EU maximizer is just a special case of Camp A or an undercover Camp B; and (4) for each crux, a case or argument both camps agree bears on it, with each side's prediction recorded. If part of the dispute is verbal, say which part and why.
