ok, here's the ask: I want a spec for what a theory of intention has to get right, not a survey of who said what. The anchor case is Kavka's toxin puzzle. In case you havent run into it: a billionaire offers you a million dollars to be paid tomorrow at noon iff at midnight tonight you intend to drink a toxin tomorrow afternoon. The toxin makes you sick for a day but does no lasting damage. You never have to actually drink it to get paid; you just have to intend it at midnight. But you also know that tomorrow, once the money is already yours no matter what, drinking is pure cost, so you know you won't drink it. The question is whether you can form the intention at midnight anyway and collect.

Whatever account of intention you give has to say something non-dumb about that case, but it also has to work for the boring cases. So give me the full requirements list. I want: the cases it has to handle — toxin, plus ordinary future-directed intentions, resolutions you make and later break, changing your mind for good reasons, akratic action, intending while you believe you'll probably fail. The distinctions it can't collapse — especially whether intending reduces to expecting you'll do it, or to wanting plus a commitment, or to having adopted a plan. And the facts about self-knowledge, weakness of will, or whatever else it can't flatly contradict. For each requirement say why it's a requirement and whether a theory could drop it or weaken it and still be worth having. Then tell me which single requirement does the most to knock out the usual candidates — intention as belief, intention as desire, intention as a sui generis planning state, whatever else is live. You can score the candidates against the list, but keep the weight on the criteria.

One more specific thing: I keep seeing the toxin puzzle treated as only showing something about when it's rational to intend, but it looks to me like it also constrains what intending is in the first place. If you think that's right or wrong, build the verdict into the desiderata instead of bolting it on at the end.
