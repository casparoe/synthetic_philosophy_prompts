My friend has pretty settled views about sunk costs. She thinks it's irrational to finish a novel you hate just because you bought it, to sit through a bad movie because the ticket was pricey and you drove half an hour, and to keep a subscription you don't use because you already paid for the year. But she thinks it's rational to finish the last semester of a degree you've soured on because the diploma will open doors — and she insists the three previous years play no role in that verdict. She also thinks it's rational to keep restoring a motorcycle she's sunk eight grand and a hundred weekends into, even though it'll never sell for what she's put in, because walking away would make all that money and time feel wasted. Next case: a four-thousand-dollar nonrefundable cruise she's now dreading and wouldn't enjoy at all, where skipping it would leave her feeling sick about the wasted money. What does she say about the cruise? I want a definite call, with reasons, not a menu of options. I'm less interested in the yes/no itself than in the rule (or rules) she's running, which earlier verdict each rule has to strech to fit, your best guess about the cruise and why, and the further case that would cleanly separate the remaining rules.
