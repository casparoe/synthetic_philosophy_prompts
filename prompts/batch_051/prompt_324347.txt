I'm a political theory PhD student working on value aggregation for aligned AI, and I've been tinkering with a variation on Rawls's original position that I can't fully settle.

In the standard version, parties behind the veil don't know their talents, race, sex, or place in society, and they choose principles to govern the basic structure for a whole life. Rawls says they'd reject average utilitarianism and pick his two principles, with the difference principle for inequalities, partly because maximin is the rational response to radical uncertainty and partly because the chosen principles have to be bearable under the strains of commitment.

Now the variation. Instead of choosing constitutional principles once, the parties choose the objective function for a single aligned AI that will act as a perpetual allocator—that is, they choose whose values count, with what weight, and how to trade them off against each other. After the veil lifts, this AI can observe each person's actual income, utility, preferences, even their entire conception of the good, and it redoes distributive decisions continuously rather than by general rule. The objective function is chosen once, under the veil, and can't be renegotiated; after that the machine just applies it with full information about everyone's position. So the informational scarcity that drove maximin is gone after the moment of choice, and the AI has finer-grained information than any real institution Rawls imagined.

Does the difference principle argument survive this change, or does something like continuous prioritarianism or utilitarianism look more defensible once the allocator can see everyone's position in real time? I keep going back and forth about whether the basic structure formulation is doing the real work here: Rawls might just say an AI that makes allocation decisions is exactly the kind of thing principles should regulate, so the variation collapses. But then why doesn't the full information afterward undermine the maximin reasoning? And does liberal neutrality get built into the chosen objective function automatically, or is it a seperate constraint the AI needs on top?
