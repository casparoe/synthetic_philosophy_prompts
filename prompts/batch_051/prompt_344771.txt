- Shelter volunteer: an office worker spends every Saturday morning for a decade serving meals at a homeless shelter. Verdict: supererogatory, though she calls it clearly praiseworthy.

The new case: a firefighter is off duty and out of her district, with no gear, and is walking her kid to school when she comes across a house fire. From her training she can tell the room is near flashover — the whole space could ignite at once. No one else is around who can help, and the department has no policy either way on off-duty rescues; she wouldnt have been disciplined for staying back. She goes in and pulls out a child, but suffers severe burns and a long recovery.

I want you to do the actual work: state the two or three candidate rules that would make her earlier verdicts hang together, say what each one predicts for this case, identify the closest earlier analog and tell me whether it points toward required or supererogatory, then give your best guess at her verdict with a confidence score and the reason you prefer that rule.
