Okay I'm taking a philosophy of music course and I've gotten weirdly hooked on the ontology-of-musical-works stuff. The basic puzzle: Beethoven's Fifth gets performed hundreds of times, and there's also the score, the first printed edition, a famous recording, my memory of seeing it live — and none of these is "the" work, exactly, yet we talk as if there's one thing that all the performances are performances of. So I started collecting the main answers to what kind of thing that one thing is. Here's my list:

(1) Plain Platonism: the work is a timeless abstract sound structure; Beethoven didn't create it, he discovered it, like a theorem.

(2) The indicated-structure view: the work is a structure of sounds and performance means as indicated by a particular person at a particular time. It's abstract, but created, so two composers who independently arrived at an identical sound structure would have composed different works.

(3) Nominalism: there's no abstract object at all — the work just is the scattered sum of its concrete performances (and maybe the score copies), grouped together by us for convenience.

(4) The action view: the work is what the composer did, the unrepeatable act of composing; performances aren't instances of an object but later doings that trace back to that original doing.

(5) The abstract-artifact view: works are like laws or corporations — abstract entities brought into existence by human activity, with a beginning, a history, and possibly changes over time, even though they don't occupy space.

(6) Eliminativism: there isn't really any thing that "Beethoven's Fifth" refers to; work-talk is useful shorthand for a loose family resemblance among performances and scores, not a reference to something in the world.

I feel like this covers every standard option I've run across, which is making me paranoid. Is there a seventh answer that's genuinely different from these — not just a restatement, a special case, or a blend of two of them? And if you do propose one, tell me how it relates to the ones above and whether you think it's any good. Also happy to hear it if you think two of my entries are secretly the same position.
