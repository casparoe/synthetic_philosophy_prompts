I want to separate two features of Nozick's experience machine that usually get fused: the machine is a simulation run by deception, and the machine offers a life free of suffering. Take the original case first. You are offered a lifetime in a machine that gives you any experiences you choose while you float in a tank. The experiences are indistinguishable from the real thing, you will not know you are plugged in, and no one else is harmed. Nozick's view is that most of us would still refuse, and the standard grounds are: we want to actually do things, not merely have the experience of doing them; we want to be a certain kind of person, not merely have the experience of being one; and we want to remain in contact with reality.

Now vary the case. Replace the tank with a safe, one-time, irreversible biotechnical intervention of the sort the hedonistic-imperative project contemplates: an edit to your hedonic range. In the weak version, the edit removes only the most extreme negative states — agony, terror, despair — and leaves mild pain, sadness, and frustration intact. In the strong version, it abolishes felt suffering altogether: you keep your beliefs, preferences, memories, and plans, and you can still recognize that an event is bad for you, but that recognition no longer comes with qualitative hurt. There is no tank, no simulation, and no deception; you live your actual life, knowing what was changed.

I want a committed verdict, not a survey. Does Nozick's argument against the experience machine survive the substitution of edited suffering for simulated pleasure? Go through the three standard reasons — doing rather than merely experiencing, being rather than merely seeming to be, and contact with reality — and say for each whether it has force against the weak edit, the strong edit, both, or neither. Then take a definite position: would it be reasonable to refuse the weak edit? Would it be reasonable to refuse the strong edit? If Nozick's reasons mostly fail to transfer but you still think refusal is justified, give the strongest residual objection you can and say whether it is a genuine reason or status quo bias.
