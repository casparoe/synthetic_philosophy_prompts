- There's a ring of populations around a mountain range where each population can breed with the next one over, but the two ends overlap and won't mate.
- There are lizards where the females just clone themselves—no males, no mating—and they still get called a species.
- Bacteria are constantly swapping bits of DNA with completely unrelated bacteria, but an E. coli is still an E. coli.
- A fossil sequence where a skull at the top and a skull at the bottom would clearly be called different species if you only saw those two, but each layer grades into the next, so somebody has to draw a line somewhere.
