Majority vote on each proposition separately yields: P accepted, Q accepted, R rejected. The group's aggregated answers, taken as a set, violate R ↔ (P ∧ Q), even though no individual judge's answers do.

A reconciliation routine that takes a per-field majority produces (true, true, false): a state that matches no replica and violates the integrity constraint.

The mapping seems exact: individual judgment set maps to replica state; proposition maps to field; logical constraint maps to integrity constraint; proposition-by-proposition majority maps to field-by-field majority merge; group inconsistency maps to a globally invalid state.
