I've been reading the chapter called "Of Prerogative" in John Locke's Second Treatise of Government, trying to make sense of all the pandemic-era emergency powers stuff. If I've got him right, Locke says something like: since lawmakers can't foresee every accident, the executive has to be able to act for the public good in ways the law doesn't prescribe, and sometimes even go against a strict reading of the law. His example is pulling down an innocent man's house to stop a fire from spreading to the whole block. I'm trying to figure out whether my own gut reactions actually hang together.

Here are my verdicts. Pulling down the house: fine. The mayor closing beaches and shutting indoor dining in the first weeks of a pandemic without waiting for the legislature to meet: fine. The president closing the border to non-citizens after the legislature had specifically voted to keep borders open for seasonal workers: not fine, even though I think the public health case was real. Police entering private property without a warrant to rescue people from a flash flood: fine. The state using emergency powers to seize a private hospital to use as a quarantine site: fine. The same state using those same emergency powers to suspend gun sales while the emergency lasts: not fine. Quarantining a whole apartment building where drug-resistant TB was found: fine, even though it detains people who aren't sick yet and may never be. Using an emergency decree after a terror attack to set a curfew and ban large gatherings: fine, but using the same decree to postpone an election: not fine.

I can't tell what principle I'm tracking. I'd guess it has something to do with whether the action stops an actual ongoing harm versus just grabbing extra power, but the quarantine case and the border case don't split that cleanly for me. Is there a single principle that gets all of these, or close, and if not which verdicts do I have to throw out? And would Locke himself have drawn the same lines I'm drawing, or am I smuggling in something he wouldn't have accepted?
