I have no academic background in any of this, just a lot of evenings on youtube and wikipedia, so I keep getting whiplash. Every time someone says the constants are fine-tuned and something has to explain that, someone else says you wouldn't be amazed that you exist on dry land instead of at the bottom of the ocean, and a third person says the multiverse just pushes the problem up a level because you still have to choose the distribution of universes. I can't tell which of these objections are real constraints any answer has to meet and which are just talking points.

So before I read another book or watch another debate, I want the standard fixed in advance. write me the desiderata for an adequate explanation of apparent fine-tuning: the requirements a story has to meet to count as actually doing the job, not just retelling the data in a cosmic accent. Then mark which requirements are non-negotiable for anyone in the debate and which are only insisted on by one camp (design people, multiverse people, brute-fact people, anthropic-rule people).
