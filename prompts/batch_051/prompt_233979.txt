Here is an argument that has been going around my seminar, and it seems to prove too much, but I cannot find the exact point where it fails.

(1) At any time t, a rational agent's credence in a proposition H, conditional on her credence in H at some later time t′ being r, should be r. This is the Reflection principle: your later self is a better-informed epistemic successor, and you should defer to it.
(2) Reflection is not a rule of thumb for occasions when your future self happens to have gathered more evidence. It is a coherence condition on one agent's beliefs over time, and there is no non-circular way to exempt future credences merely because their causal history looks unpleasant from the present standpoint. So it applies to all predicted future credences, including ones you know will be caused by a drug.
(3) Take Ana, who at 9 a.m. rationally has credence 0.2 in H, where H is some ordinary empirical proposition not connected to the process described below. At 10 a.m., a fair coin will be flipped. If it lands heads, Ana will be given a drug that raises her credence in H to 0.9 without supplying any evidence about H; if it lands tails, she receives nothing and stays at 0.2. Ana knows all of this at 9 a.m.
(4) By Reflection, conditional on the later credence being 0.9, Ana's present credence in H should be 0.9; conditional on the later credence being 0.2, her present credence should be 0.2.
(5) The coin outcomes each have probability 0.5, so the probability calculus requires Ana's 9 a.m. credence in H to be (0.5)(0.9) + (0.5)(0.2) = 0.55.
(6) But Ana's 0.2 was rational independently of the coin and the drug, and finding out that her future credence may be artificially shifted is not evidence for H. A principle that forces her to 0.55 merely because a coin may later inflate her confidence seems to prove too much.

Where exactly does this chain go wrong? If the correct response is to restrict Reflection to some subclass of future selves, state the restriction precisely and say what it costs; if Reflection fails outright here, explain exactly what that shows about the principle and whether any salvageable version remains. Please answer in American English.
