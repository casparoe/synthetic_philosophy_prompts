Take the standard game-theoretic reconstruction of Hobbes's state of nature as a one-shot prisoner's dilemma among a large population of roughly equal, self-interested agents. When two agents meet, each can either keep a covenant (cooperate) or renege on it (defect). The payoffs are symmetric and ordered as follows: unilateral reneging while the other keeps is best; mutual keeping is second-best; mutual reneging is third-best; keeping while the other reneges is worst. Because reneging is strictly dominant, the prediction is that no one will keep covenants in the absence of enforcement, and this is taken to motivate Hobbes's conclusion that covenants presuppose a coercive common power that can punish reneging.

Now vary the setup in one respect: make the state of nature an indefinite sequence of encounters rather than a one-shot meeting. In each round, pairs are formed at random from a population of some fixed size; after each round the next round occurs with probability 0.9, and otherwise all interaction ceases. Crucially, an agent's action in a round is observed only by that round's partner. There is no public record, no gossip, and no third-party enforcement. Each agent can privately remember how each person they have personally met has behaved toward them in the past, and before any subsequent round with that same person, either party may decline to interact, at no cost.

Work through how this changes the Hobbesian analysis. Does covenant-keeping become individually rational? If so, under what conditions on population size and memory does it hold, and what strategy would sustain it? If not, precisely which feature of the one-shot reconstruction remains decisive in the modified setting? Then address the philosophical upshot: does the possibility of cooperation in this repeated, privately monitored state of nature remove the need for a sovereign, or can a Hobbesian defensibly argue that the setup either collapses back into the state of war or tacitly presupposes the very common power it claims to dispense with? Make clear which premise of the standard argument is doing the work, what the variation does and does not establish, and whether it refutes the Hobbesian conclusion or merely relocates the problem.
