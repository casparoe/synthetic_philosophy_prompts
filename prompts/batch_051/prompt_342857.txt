One of those old forum threads about specks versus torture got necro'd recently, and it made me notice I've spent years holding the puzzle at arm's length without ever actually committing to a resolution. The setup is the standard one: one person tortured, or 3^^^3 people each getting a single dust speck in the eye. The moves I can reconstruct from memory are: accept that a large enough pile of specks outweighs one torture, because there's no non-arbitrary number where adding one more speck flips the comparison; insist that torture is lexically worse, so no finite number of specks ever catches up; deny that harms can be summed across people in the first place, so the question is ill-posed rather than answerable; or treat the whole thing as a warning that our moral arithmetic falls apart at 3^^^3 and the honest answer is to dissolve the comparison, not make it.

I'd like you to do the thing where you actually rank these, rather than just survey them. Give each move a number between 0 and 1 for how likely a well-informed person should think it is to be the correct resolution, make the numbers sum to about 1 (so include a residual for none-of-the-above or any move I've forgotten), and then tell me which gap in your ranking you're least confident about. I care more about seeing how the alternatives trade off against each other than about getting a single winner.
