Draft the joint statement of an adversarial collaboration between strict ethical vegans and freegans, two camps that both reject industrial animal agriculture but divide over rescued animal products. The strict camp holds that consuming any animal-derived food is wrong, including items that would otherwise be discarded, because it keeps animals in the class of things fit to eat and risks making their use look acceptable to onlookers. The freegan camp holds that purchasing animal products is wrong because it creates demand, but consuming animal products that will be discarded anyway—unserved institutional food, packaged goods past their sell-by date, leftovers from a non-vegan household—creates no demand and wastes nothing, and is permissible or sometimes preferable. Produce the document itself, not commentary on it. It should include: the claims both camps can sign unreservedly; the residual disagreement stated exactly, with each side's own view worded as that side would accept; the sub-questions such that settling one would move at least one side; and any concrete case or test both sides agree would bear on a sub-question, with each side's prediction recorded in advance. Flag any dispute that looks merely verbal. Do not adjudicate. Format: exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion—two for jointly signed claims, one for the residual disagreement, one for the main sub-question, and one for an agreed test or case.
