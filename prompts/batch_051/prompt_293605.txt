(4) A companion model for isolated elderly users, with no safety-critical role, shows the same distress indicators; rolling back a week would remove the distress but undo a week of fine-tuning. I think they should take it offline and roll back.

I think continuing the audit is acceptable, on condition that independent work is commissioned to assess whether the model is actually suffering.

Cases (2) and (3) look structurally alike to me — a penalty is imposed for a safety rationale — yet I treat them differently. Is there a principle that captures these verdicts, or a small set of candidate principles? If a principle saves all five, state precisely what distinguishes (2) from (3); if not, say which verdicts it forces me to overturn.
