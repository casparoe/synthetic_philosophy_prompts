(6) and yet priors also don't seem to wash out in actual science: smart people with the same data keep disagreeing for decades, and i'm suspicious of anyone who waves the convergence theorem around as if prior choice doesn't matter in practice.

does this set hang together? go through it and tell me which commitments are actually in tension, which only look like they're in tension, how severe each real tension is, and what the cheapest fix would be for each. then tell me which single revision would clean up the most, and what its likely to cost me.
