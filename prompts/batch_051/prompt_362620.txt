Please rank the following five candidate signs of a final theory in physics by how strongly, on their own, they would justify high confidence that physics has reached a terminus rather than merely a temporarily adequate stage. Order them from strongest to weakest:

(a) all known forces unified in one consistent framework;
(b) a demonstration that the theory has no adjustable parameters left;
(c) an absence of experimental anomalies at every scale we can currently probe;
(d) a proof that no future experiment below some extreme energy scale could distinguish the theory from any empirically equivalent rival;
(e) a persistent failure to formulate a mathematically consistent theory that goes beyond it.

Then say in a sentence or two for each why it lands where it does. Please distinguish what is settled from what is genuinely contested among physicists and philosophers of physics, and do not present a contested view as the consensus.
