I'd rather get the desiderata right before I trust any method. The bar I'm aiming for is being able to predict and reasonably steer the system, but I also want to know which requirements would survive even for a much more general theory of goal inference.

Act as an expert on optimization and the philosophy of goal-directed behavior. Write me the specification: what must a theory of inferring an optimizer's objective and its search space from behavior actually deliver to count as adequate rather than merely convenient? I want the list of requirements with the case or consideration behind each one. Tell me which single requirement does the most to rule out naive theories. Don't bother with a method-by-method scorecard; the criteria and their conflicts are what I care about. Start by brainstorming several candidate requirements before committing to the list, and think carefully about which "requirements" are really just habits of one method.
