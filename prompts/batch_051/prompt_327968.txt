People in my circles keep saying that a language model's chain-of-thought is basically a person thinking out loud. The idea is that when a model spells out its steps before it answers, it's not just generating more text — it's reporting the reasoning that actually produces the answer, the way I might talk through a hard decision so you can check my work. And they use that to draw a strong conclusion: that we can read a CoT transcript as evidence about why the model did what it did, audit whether it was acting for the reasons it says it was, maybe even catch a model that's lying or sandbagging.
