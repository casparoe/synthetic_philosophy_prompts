Write me a short dialogue between them — maybe 600 or 700 words — where they actually respond to each other's points and the argument moves. By the end, I want the Rylean to have to say something sharper than 'know-how is just an ability' (because the injured pianist bites there), and the intellectualist to have to say what a practical mode of presentation amounts to without just relabeling the problem. Make it snappy and a little heated but charitable. Answer in American English.
