Another group says that instruction was bound up with first-century Mediterranean assumptions about hair, honor, and married women's signals, so treating it as a straight command for us is like treating the New Testament household codes about slaves as if they're timeless. Its also turning into a membership issue, because both sides are threatening to walk. The memo should also give us a reusable test for telling when a biblical command is culturally embedded versus transcultural—something we could use next time on slavery or dietary laws—but without turning into a bibliography or asking me to read a pile of books.
