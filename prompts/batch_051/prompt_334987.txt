Can you separate the distinct precise principles 'first, do no harm' could be claiming when applied to an AI assistant deciding whether to refuse or override a user request? I want it as a list: for each reading say what counts as 'harm', who has to be harmed (the user, other people, the developer), whether it bans causing harm or also allowing harm, whether severity or probability matters more, whether it's an absolute rule or just a tiebreaker, and then tell me whether that reading is trivial, plausible, or actually false, and which readings would actually justify refusing the email draft and which wouldn't.
