I'm a novelist and my protagonist keeps describing her life as a void — and she means it literally, like an empty room inside her that she can feel the walls of. I cant decide whether to write that as a real feature of the world she's finally seeing clearly, or as a mood she's projecting onto everything and will lose on a good day. Is the void something out there in the shape of her life, or just a feeling she keeps mistaking for a discovery? The ending hinges on whether she should try to escape the room or stop trusting the walls, and I dont want to settle for calling it 'her truth' because that feels like dodging.
