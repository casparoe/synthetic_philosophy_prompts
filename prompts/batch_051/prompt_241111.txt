I've come around to mathematical structuralism: numbers are just positions in a structure, and "is 2 an element of 4?" is a pseudo-question created by treating a set-theoretic encoding as what numbers really are. On Zermelo's way of doing it, 2 = {1} and 4 = {3}, so 2 isn't an element of 4; on von Neumann's, 2 = {0,1} and 4 = {0,1,2,3}, so it is. Since both can't be right and there's no principled reason to pick one over the other, I say there's no fact of the matter. My worry is that this position is to comfortable, because I honestly couldn't say what would move me off it.
