I've been asked to write a philosophical profile of my Berkeleyan position for a reading group, and I'm worried I've just accumulated views that sound individually plausible but might not cohere. I wanted an audit before I commit to it. We've been reading the Principles, and this passage from the opening of George Berkeley's A Treatise Concerning the Principles of Human Knowledge keeps bothering me:

"Yet so it is, we see the illiterate bulk of mankind that walk the high-road of plain common sense, and are governed by the dictates of nature, for the most part easy and undisturbed. To them nothing THAT IS FAMILIAR appears unaccountable or difficult to comprehend. They complain not of any want of evidence in their senses, and are out of all danger of becoming SCEPTICS."

My committments, roughly as I'd defend them over coffee:
1. Ordinary objects like tables and trees are collections of ideas, and they have no mind-independent existence.
2. The master argument is basically sound: I cannot conceive of an object existing unconceived, because in trying to conceive it I am conceiving it; so mind-independent existence is not genuinely thinkable.
3. Berkeley is broadly right that his view is a defence of common sense, and the passage above captures something true about why ordinary people are untroubled by scepticism.
4. Esse is percipi holds for sensible objects, but not for finite minds or God.
5. Modern physical science can get on fine without material substance; all it needs are regularities among ideas.
6. Hallucinations and dreams are just less orderly, less vivid, or less regular sequences of ideas, not evidence of a mind-independent world.
7. Locke's primary/secondary quality distinction is unstable: once colour and warmth are in the mind, extension and solidity have to go there too.

I'd like you to go through this list and tell me which pairs of commitments are in tension and how badly. For any pair that only looks like a tension, say why it dissolves. Rank the genuine tensions by severity, and for each one say what the cheapest fix would be and what it costs me. Then tell me which single revision would clean up the most. Please write the audit in British English.
