I'm trying to pin down what the Lockean proviso actually is. You know the setup: in Locke's story about property, you come to own an unowned thing by working on it — mixing your labour with it — but only under some condition about not wronging other people. Roughly, your taking has to leave others with as good a shot at getting the same kind of thing for themselves, or at least not make them worse off than if you'd left it alone. That condition is doing a lot of work, but its usually left hazy.

I want a working definition of the Lockean proviso: a test I can apply to a case and get a yes/no answer, or at least a clear "here's the extra fact I'd need to know." Then run your test on these:

- A family clears and farms a few acres on an enormous frontier, where equally fertile, unowned land is available in every direction.
- The first settler in a dry valley encloses the only freshwater spring and starts charging everyone else for access.
- A biotech firm patents a gene sequence found in every human being. They didn't create the sequence, just identified and described it.
- A radio company takes the last open broadcast frequency in a city; until they got the license, any local with a ham radio could use it.
- A country extracts and burns oil at a rate that makes the present generation richer but the atmosphere worse for people who aren't born yet.
- A musician writes a new song using the standard scales and chords everyone shares. The song is genuinely new; the materials feel like common stock, but nothing seems to get used up.
- A squatter works common pastureland very intensively and triples its output, but the village that used it collectively would rather have their pasture back.
- A logging company clears a forest, then pays the displaced communities enough money that they say they're better off than before.

For each case, say whether your proviso lets the taking go through, blocks it, or requires more information (and what information). Then tell me which verdict you're least sure about, and whether that exposes a flaw in your definition or is just a weird case.
