Give a working definition of proof beyond a reasonable doubt that classifies each of the following cases as proved or not proved under that standard, and then say which of your verdicts is least secure. Do not simply restate the standard as a probability threshold; say what kind of evidence or inference it requires. Assume all listed evidence is admissible. Keep your answer under 300 words.

1. A bus strikes a pedestrian and flees. The only evidence on the bus's identity is that 80% of buses on that route at that hour are Blue Bus Company buses.
2. A fenced arena admits 1,000 spectators through its only gate, but only 499 tickets were sold. A randomly selected attendee is charged with entering without paying.
3. A DNA sample from a burglary matches the defendant's profile, and the lab reports the profile would be expected in 1 in 100 million people. The defendant was tested only because a database search flagged his profile from a pool of prior arrestees.
4. A clerk identifies the defendant as the robber, and the defendant's fingerprint is found on the counter near the register. The defendant says he had shopped there earlier that day.
5. A bystander who observed the shooter for about two seconds from across a dim street identifies the defendant as the shooter, and no physical evidence connects him to the crime.
6. Two bystanders, interviewed separately, each identify the defendant as the shooter from a lineup, and the defendant's phone records place him in the vicinity at the time of the shooting.
