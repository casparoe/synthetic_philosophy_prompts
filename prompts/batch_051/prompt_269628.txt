I've been going around in circles on this and cant settle it. The position I keep wanting to defend is something like the epistemic defense of democracy: under the right conditions, lots of independent voters who are each only a little better than chance on a question will be extremely accurate in aggregate, and public argument seems to surface dispersed local information that no panel of experts has. On this view democratic procedures earn their authority not just because they're fair but because they're truth-tracking in the long run.

But then I hit Athens, and the theory doesnt feel true anymore. The same assembly that debated Mytilene voted one day to kill all the adult men and enslave everyone else, then reversed itself the next day — that isn't the machinery correcting itself, it's the same machinery generating both outcomes. Sicily is worse: the assembly talked itself into a massive expedition on the strength of promises and threats that were mostly fantasy, and lost the expedition and most of the empire. And then the democracy executed Socrates, by an ordinary vote of a large citizen jury, for doing exactly the kind of disruptive questioning that the epistemic story says a healthy democracy should be able to metabolize rather than suppress.

I can't tell whether these are just bad inputs to a basically sound procedure — demagogues, a population ground down by plague and war, an unlucky run — or whether they show that the procedure fails exactly when it matters, because deliberation under fear doesn't aggregate information so much as amplify status and panic. I have a stubborn intuition that a system that produced these three outcomes isn't innocent: something about the procedure itself is doing real work, not just uninformed people voting badly. But I also don't want to give up the epistemic defense just because ancient executions make me upset.

What should give way: the theory or the intuition? If the intuition should be debunked, what would the debunking actually have to show? If the theory should be amended, what version of it survives these cases without becoming vacuous?
