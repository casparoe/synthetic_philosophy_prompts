I'm giving a talk at our church's theology-and-philosophy reading group defending the claim that the usual inerrantist way of handling number discrepancies in the gospels — say, one angel at the tomb in Matthew and two in John — is ad hoc, because once you allow that the text just doesn't say "only," you can reconcile any two accounts of anything, and the doctrine stops making real claims about what happened. The group is mostly lay inerrantists, but two of them have thought hard about this and will say incomplete reports are perfectly compatible and that I'm confusing what a text asserts with what it leaves open. There's also a couple of seminary-trained types who think harmonization is just normal testimony-processing, like two witnesses to a car crash who remember different details. Give me the objections in roughly the order they'll come, phrased the way they'd phrase them, with what I should concede, what I should hold, and the weakest point in my position I should prepare to defend rather than hope nobody notices. Also tell me the question I should hope nobody asks.
