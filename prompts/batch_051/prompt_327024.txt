(2) I can't accept penal substitution — the idea that God's justice demands that sin be punished and so the punishment gets transferred to Christ; that makes forgiveness sound like a transaction and makes the Trinity look internally split. (4) Moral influence views are right as far as they go — the cross does change us by displaying God's love — but they're not an account of how atonement is achieved, just of an effect it has. (5) I also want to say God could not simply have forgiven sin without the cross. Do these hang together? Go through the pairs and tell me which are genuinely in tension, which only look like they're in tension, rank the real tensions by severity, and then tell me what the cheapest revision is or which single change would clean up the most.
