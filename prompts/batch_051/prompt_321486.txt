I've been thinking about the 'latent knowledge' problem with AI, where a model seems to know something but won't come out and say it. I don't get why it's treated as a hard problem. If I can find any prompt that makes the model give the right answer—like telling it to roleplay a brutally honest expert, or to imagine it's writing in a private diary no one will ever read—then the knowledge was never realy hidden. It was just a wording issue. Doesn't that mean the whole problem is just prompt engineering, and there's nothing deeper about what the model 'actually knows'?
