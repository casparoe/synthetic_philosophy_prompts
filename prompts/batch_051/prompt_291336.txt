I'm teaching a graduate seminar on population ethics this term and I keep tripping over the question of how welfare components combine. The 'consciousness times happiness' formula crops up everywhere in animal welfare and consequentialist work, but I'm not convinced it's the right basic operation — maybe the two should add, or maybe one should dominate the other lexically. Before I read another paper defending one of these, I want the desiderata: what any adequate account of this combination has to deliver. Which cases must it get right, which puzzles must it dissolve, which distinctions must it respect — and, for each, the case behind it. Flag any pair of desiderata you think cant be jointly satisfied. Then, briefly, how a purely additive view, a multiplicative view, and a lexical view do against the list. Keep the weight on the criteria, not on scoring the candidates.
