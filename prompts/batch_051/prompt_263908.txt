I've got fifty minutes and a whiteboard, no slides.

I'm not asking you to write the lecture. I want you to think through the teaching plan for it. Treat yourself as an expert on rational-herding models who has actually taught this to non-economists before, and think carefully about what depends on what.

Start by brainstorming two possible running examples and tell me which one you'd keep and why.
