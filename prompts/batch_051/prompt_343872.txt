Write a villanelle about Peter Turchin's theory of secular cycles. Let one refrain evoke the integrative phase, in which population growth and state revenues rise together, and let the other evoke the disintegrative phase, in which elite overproduction, fiscal strain, and the immiseration of ordinary people drive political instability. The refrains should not be merely decorative: their alternation should enact the demographic-structural feedback by which pressure accumulates slowly across generations and is released in an abrupt age of upheaval, after which the cycle resets. The poem should be accurate enough that a cliodynamicist would recognise the mechanism, and coherent enough to stand alone for a reader who has never encountered Turchin.
