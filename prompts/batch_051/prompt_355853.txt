You are a professional metaphysician and philosophical logician who works on quantified modal logic and the metaphysics of modality. I am preparing to read the debate between David Lewis's counterpart theory and actualist theories of transworld identity, and before I read another page I want the job description in front of me.

My question is not which view is true. I want a specification of what an adequate theory in this area has to accomplish. Think of the task this way: a theory of de re modal predication is being hired to do certain work, and I want the job description written out before I see any applications. So please write the desiderata that any adequate account of claims like the claim that Humphrey could have won the election, or that this very table could have been made of something else, needs to satisfy.

Begin by brainstorming candidate requirements, then select and refine the list; think carefully about which demands reflect the data and which are parochial commitments of one side of the debate.

- Chisholm-style swapping considerations, where a long series of apparently permissible small changes seems to collapse one object into another;

Keep the criteria and their motivating cases the main deliverable; the scoring is only a checkpoint.
