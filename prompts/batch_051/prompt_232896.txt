I design authorization and access-control systems for a living, and the concepts have become my default vocabulary for thinking about almost everything: principals and objects, permissions and ACLs, capabilities, roles, revocation and delegation, ambient authority, root, sandboxing, the confused deputy problem. I have been trying to get a grip on the debate between positive, negative, and republican liberty, and I want to run a translation experiment rather than just read more overviews. Take the main positions and restate each in that authorization vocabulary. By negative liberty, I mean the view that freedom is the absence of interference by other people: a protected region of action within which nobody stops me. By positive liberty, I mean the view that freedom is self-mastery: being directed by my own rational or higher self rather than by appetite, impulse, or another person, together with its collective form in which a self-governing people imposes its own law on itself, and its darker application in which people are coerced in the name of their real selves or of a general will. By republican liberty, I mean the view that freedom is non-domination: what matters is not whether someone actually interferes with me, but whether someone stands in a position of arbitrary power over me, so that a slave with a lenient master is unfree even while left alone. If it is useful, also work in the triadic idea that every freedom claim specifies who is free, from what, and to do or be what.

Run the translation position by position. For each, give me the correspondence: what plays the role of the principal, the permission, the capability, the ACL, the granting authority, the revocation, the confused deputy, and so on. Mark which claims translate cleanly, which claims only fit by stretching the model, and which claims have no expression in this vocabulary at all. Then give me a residue report: do the pieces that fail to translate point to genuine confusion in the liberty debate, to a blind spot in how authorization models think about power and freedom, or to some of each? I would rather have the translation be technically precise than watered down.
