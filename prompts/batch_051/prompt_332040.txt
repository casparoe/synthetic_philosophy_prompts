- A medical device company's complaint system auto-closes any complaint filed without the right reference number. For four years, battery-defect reports come in misfiled and get closed before a human reads them. Batteries later injure several people. Dana: blame the company.
- A pipeline company ties bonuses to 'no reportable spills,' and its regional directors start logging leaks as 'transfers' under a head-office guideline. Leaks add up and a corroded line bursts. Dana: blame the company.

I can see more than one rule that fits her track record, and I suspect they'd disagree about the wind farm. So: what's your best prediction for how Dana would rule on the wind farm, what are the rules that fit her record, which one would you bet she's running and why, and what single further case would settle which one she's really using?
