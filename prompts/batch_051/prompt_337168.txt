Draft the opinion I can send around. It should recommend a concrete decision on the head-covering rule with a time frame, lay out a test with criteria we can actually apply, and engage the strongest argument on the losing side honestly — I don't want us just proof-texting the creation-order language, because the same passage also leans on custom, and its closing line about the practice of the churches gets read in both directions. If our existing categories cant do the job, say so and tell us what to replace them with.
