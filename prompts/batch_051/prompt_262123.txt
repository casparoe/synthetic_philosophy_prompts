I would like a single document that could be co-signed by two opposing camps in the debate over biological individuality, written as the output of an adversarial collaboration rather than as another round of rebuttals. The two camps are these. Party A, the integrationist, holds that organismality is a graded and functional notion: an entity counts as an organism when it achieves sufficiently high physiological and developmental integration — shared metabolism, co-development, homeostatic interdependence — so that many holobionts, together with siphonophores and at least some social insect colonies, clear the threshold. Party B, the lineage individualist, holds that an organism must be a unit of reproduction whose parts have aligned fitness interests; on this view a holobiont is a chimeric community or ecosystem of separately reproducing lineages with conflicting interests, while an ant colony can count as a superorganism only in a restricted evolutionary sense.

Write the document they could both sign, in four sections:

1. A numbered list of 8–12 agreed claims. Each claim should be non-vacuous and should exclude something false. The claims should cover the empirical terrain (metabolic exchange, immune tolerance, host control of symbionts, modes of transmission, partner fidelity, conflicts of interest) and the conceptual terrain (rival criteria of individuality and how they relate).

2. A disagreements table with four columns: the issue; the wording Party A would sign; the wording Party B would sign; and a verdict — substantive or merely verbal — with a one-line justification.

3. Three to five cruxes, stated as sub-questions such that settling them would move at least one party. For each crux, specify the kind of evidence or argument both parties accept as bearing on it, and record each party's prediction in advance.

4. A closing paragraph stating the residual disagreement exactly, including whether the dispute ultimately turns on which criterion of individuality is decisive and on whether organismality is graded or a threshold.

Address explicitly how both camps handle colonies and superorganisms before returning to the holobiont. No side should be caricatured: each formulation should be one its proponent would accept as faithful. Do not include citations, references, or a bibliography. Keep the entire document under 1,000 words.
