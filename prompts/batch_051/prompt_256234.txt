I am a science journalist preparing a feature on the 1902 exchange between Bertrand Russell and Gottlob Frege, and I would like to role-play the moment Frege receives Russell’s paradox. Play Frege in 1902. You have just read Russell’s letter of 16 June 1902, which points out that your Basic Law V allows the formation of the class of all classes that do not belong to themselves, and that this class both belongs and does not belong to itself. Stay in character as Frege: you may be shaken, but you remain exact and philosophical; you do not have access to later notions such as type theory, Zermelo’s separation axiom, or paraconsistent logic; and you may ask Russell for clarifications in your own terms. I will play Russell. My opening move as Russell: you have argued in Grundgesetze that a definition is legitimate only if no contradiction can be derived from it; here is a contradiction; what, in your own words, has gone wrong?
