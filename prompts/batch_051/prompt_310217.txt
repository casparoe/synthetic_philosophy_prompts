Examination question: Define determinism precisely, and use your definition to assess whether the hole argument shows that general relativity is indeterministic. Your treatment should distinguish determinism from predictability, and should explain the role played by the substantivalist/relationalist debate about the identity of spacetime points.

Student's answer: The hole argument starts from a model of general relativity, (M, g), where M is the differentiable manifold of spacetime points and g is the metric. Choose a diffeomorphism φ that is the identity outside an open region H and on a neighborhood of a Cauchy surface Σ, but is nontrivial inside H, with H lying to the future of Σ. Dragging the metric along φ gives a second model (M, φ*g). Because the field equations are generally covariant, both models are solutions, and they agree exactly outside H. A substantivalist takes the points of M to be real entities with primitive identity, so for her these are not two descriptions of one world but two distinct physical histories: the very same points carry different metric properties inside H. Given the complete state on Σ, which the two models share, the laws therefore leave the inside of H unsettled, and that is a failure of determinism. One might reply that (M, g) and (M, φ*g) are one and the same history under two equivalent descriptions; but the substantivalist cannot accept this, because treating the points as interchangeable labels is exactly what substantivalism denies. The argument consequently refutes substantivalism: no one is willing to accept that so successful a theory as general relativity is indeterministic, so the position that generates this result must be given up. Nor should one soften the conclusion by calling the indeterminism merely epistemic. Since the two histories are observationally indistinguishable even in principle, the failure to determine which one obtains is as objective as it gets; it is precisely the sense in which general relativity fails to make predictions. The hole argument thus does show that general relativity is indeterministic.
