So I'm on my city council, and we have to vote Thursday on whether to approve a settlement where confidentiality is part of the deal. Short version: a contractor botched a big stormwater drainage project, and the city is negotiating to recover damages. The contractor will only settle if the city signs an NDA covering the independent failure report and the final settlement amount, plus a clause saying city officials won't publicly criticize the contractor's work. Here's the ugly part: the report also contains emails from our own city engineers apparently signing off on shoddy inspections, so the NDA would hide the city's own mistakes along with the contractor's. The council is split. One side says the public paid for the project, paid for the report, and will pay for the repairs, so they have a right to know exactly what went wrong and what we got for it. The other side says refusing the NDA means years of litigation, higher legal fees, and probably a smaller recovery, which also harms the public — and they point out the total dollar figure will show up in the budget anyway, so it's not completely hidden. We're genuinely torn about weather the payout justifies the secrecy. I need to draft the two-page memo I'll circulate before the vote, with a clear recommendation: sign, refuse, or sign with conditions (and which conditions). Make it something we could actually act on, and give an honest treatment of the strongest point on the losing side.

After the memo, give me a structured summary as a JSON object with this exact schema:

- recommendation: a string, one of "sign", "refuse", or "sign_with_conditions"
- conditions: a list of strings; if recommendation is "sign_with_conditions", the conditions to impose (each one something a layperson could verify later); otherwise an empty list
- strongest_argument_for_nda: a string
- strongest_argument_against_nda: a string
- risks_if_we_sign: a list of strings
- risks_if_we_refuse: a list of strings
- bottom_line: a string, one sentence the council could actually vote on
