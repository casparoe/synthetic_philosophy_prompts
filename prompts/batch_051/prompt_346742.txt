He wrote it as a retired British officer looking at history, and read now its tempting to turn it into a base-rate tool: find the reference class of empires, compute the median age, compare the US or China to it, and adjust your confidence in impending collapse accordingly.

I want an audit of the organism analogy itself, not just a list of historical examples. I also want to know whether the base-rate version of the argument can survive once the organism framing is subtracted, or wether it collapses into something mushier. One specific worry: Glubb's stages often look less like an observable clock and more like a story told backward after the fall, and I'd like to know whether that changes what "base rate" could even mean here. Is there a principled reference class for "empires like the current US" that doesn't just get selected after the fact?
