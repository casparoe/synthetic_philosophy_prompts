I am a novelist, and for the book I am writing I need the text of a joint statement produced by two philosophers of logic who, after years of arguing past one another, are forced into an adversarial collaboration. One holds a truth-value-gap view: the Liar sentence is neither true nor false, and a consistent theory can describe truth as a partial notion. The other is a dialetheist: the Liar is both true and false, and the right response is a paraconsistent logic on which some contradictions are true without triviality. Draft the statement they could both sign. It should open with a numbered list of claims both accept about the Liar, the T-schema, the transparency of truth and classical logic; then state, in wording each side would accept for its own position, exactly where they part company, including their treatments of the strengthened Liar and the question whether natural language is inconsistent or merely partial; then identify the cruxes on which the dispute actually turns, and for each crux give a concrete sentence or case both agree bears on it and each side's prediction about it. Where part of the dispute is verbal, say which and why. Throughout, separate what is settled from what is genuinely contested among informed people, and do not present either camp's position as the field's consensus. Begin with the statement itself, with no preface or commentary.
