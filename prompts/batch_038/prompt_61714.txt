The thing I have to get across is our working definition of a deceptive output, which is deliberately not about lying. Roughly: an output is deceptive when it tracks what the system is trying to get rather than what's the case, in the sense that if the facts had been different in some way that mattered to the system's goal, you'd have gotten the same output anyway. An output that's false but would have come out different had the facts been different is just an error, not a deception. That's the whole load-bearing distinction and I need it working in their hands by the end, not just nodded at.
