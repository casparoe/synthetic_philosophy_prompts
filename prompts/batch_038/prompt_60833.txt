I'm in a philosophy of probability seminar and we just hit the reference class problem, and separately I've gotten way too into a forecasting site, and my actual practice is a total mess. Here are my verdicts, mostly from cases I've really had to put numbers on:

- A friend is opening a Thai place downtown. I'd use "independent restaurants in this city still open at year five," not "all new businesses." Narrower wins.
- But when the narrowest class I can even name has three members in it (she'd be one of three Thai restaurants opened by someone with no industry experience within four blocks of campus), I bail and climb back up to the bigger class. Small-and-narrow loses to big-and-broad.
- Coin: I use "tosses of this particular coin," and I refuse to narrow to "tosses of this coin where I called tails beforehand," even though I'm 2 for 9 in that class. Narrowing there feels like pure gerrymandering.
- Medical: the doctor quotes a 1% population figure and I instantly narrow to "women my age with my family history who already have the symptom," and I don't feel like I'm cheating at all. So what makes this seperate from the coin?
- Genuinely single cases: first AI granted legal personhood, first landing on Europa. The rule we got in class is basically to use the narrowest class for which you can compile reliable statistics — but here the narrowest class is a class of one, so there are no statistics. I still want to say there's a right answer and not just whatever mood I'm in.
- Barometer: "storms preceded by a falling barometer" has the same rate as "all storms" in my data, so the barometer is irrelevant and gets dropped — I agree with that. But "storms where the sky went green first" also shifts nothing in my data, and I still feel like it deserves some weight.
- And: a property that moves my frequencies a lot in a small sample but has no causal story behind it doesn't license narrowing, while one with a great causal story that moves nothing does make me distrust the number. Yeah, I know how that sounds.

My tentative principle is: go as narrow as you can subject to (a) at least ~20 members in the class and (b) some causal story for the narrowing property. Does anything like that survive contact with these cases? Tell me what the best principle actually is, what each clause is doing and which of my verdicts it's there to save, what it says about a couple of cases I haven't thought of, and which of my verdicts it forces me to give up.
