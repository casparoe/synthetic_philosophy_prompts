I am circulating a paper defending a deliberately austere reading of Leibnizian optimism, and I want it attacked. The reading runs as follows. The thesis that this is the best of all possible worlds is not a claim about the balance of happiness over suffering, nor one that could be confirmed or disconfirmed by inspecting any region of the world; it is a claim about the architecture of the possibles. Each possible world is a maximal collection of compossible substances, worlds are ordered not by quantities of value but by the ratio of richness of phenomena to economy of laws, and the actual series is the unique maximum of that ordering. On this reading the evidential problem of evil simply does not reach the thesis, and I say so openly: apparent evils are local data whose weight cannot be assessed from inside the series.

Tell me what would count against it. I want the defeaters ranked, from those that would destroy the reading to those that would merely embarrass it, and I want to know which of them a committed Leibnizian would concede as telling against him and which he would dismiss as missing the point. Please address at least the following. Whether an ordering of this kind can be shown to have a maximum at all, given that the possibles may form an unbounded ascending series, and whether the familiar Leibnizian move of inferring that there must be a best from the fact that something exists is anything other than question-begging. Whether a criterion that settles no concrete comparison between two candidate worlds is still a criterion, or only a way of declining to compare. Whether the reading collapses into a rival — necessitarianism about creation, say, or the view that God may permissibly do less than the best without ceasing to be perfectly good. And what, if anything, the thesis risks that its rivals do not.

Close with your honest verdict on the immunity I have conceded: is a claim engineered to survive every possible distribution of evil worth believing, and if not, what would have to be added to it to make it vulnerable without making it false?
