Here's my situation. I'm chairing the commitee that gives out our department's single dissertation fellowship — one year of stipend, one award, and it can't be split (we checked, the donor language is explicit about that). What's on the table so far: (i) one colleague wants to flip a coin, and argues the flip is the only thing that treats the two students as equals, since anything else would just be an arbitrary preference dressed up as a judgement; (ii) another colleague thinks the flip is a disgrace — these two spent a year writing those proposals and we owe them an actual reason, and "the coin came up heads" is not one; (iii) a third wants to adopt a tiebreaker criterion right now, something like which project is likelier to lead to a job, which everyone else pointed out we'd be inventing after seeing the files; (iv) I floated holding the money over and reopening the competition, and got shot down because its not our call and both of them will have graduated by then.
