ok so I'm three quarters through a novel set in the near future, where a national panel licenses body- and mind-modifications, and my problem is that I need their rulings to feel inevitable to a reader rather than arbitrary. Here's the track record I've already wrote into the book — all of it in-world, but I want to know whether it actually hangs together:

- they licensed a stimulant for a 15 year old with a diagnosed attention disorder, and refused the same drug for her twin brother, who has no diagnosis and just wanted it for exam week
- they licensed a gene edit adding four inches of expected height for a child with achondroplasia, and refused the identical edit for that child's short-but-perfectly-healthy cousin
- they licensed a drug that blunts traumatic memory for a soldier with diagnosed PTSD, and refused it to a homicide detective who says the case photos are costing him sleep but who has no diagnosis
- they licensed a powered back brace for a warehouse picker with a documented prior injury, and refused to license it as standard kit for every picker on the floor
- they declined to rule on caffeine at all, calling it ordinary life

The scene I'm stuck on: a 61 year old amateur marathoner, approved two years earlier for a drug that corrected a diagnosed rhythm disorder. His heart is normal now. He's petitioning to stay on the same dose because it holds his resting pulse below what's typical for a man his age, and he swears it's worth a minute a mile. His argument is that normal-for-61 is already a decline, so he's only asking to be judged against the same standard as the thirty year olds he races.

Predict the panel's ruling. Then tell me which rule they're actually running — and I want every candidate rule that fits all five precedents above just as well, because that's exactly what I can't see from inside the book. Say what each candidate predicts for the marathoner and why, give me your best guess at the real one and why you'd bet on it, and then name the one further petition I could invent that would force the candidates apart, so I can tell whether my panel has a determinate character or I'm just fooling myself.
