Does that survive the film? Two things I care about most: what the film does with Delilah, since she's the actual victim and doesn't seem to want any of it, and whether Munny's relapse is honour culture vindicated or refuted. Please don't turn the film into a flowchart — I want the honest spots where the typology just doesn't grip, and a closing section on what the picture shows about honour that the sociology misses.
