I have followed the consciousness literature on and off for about fifteen years, largely through rationalist venues, and the qualia debate strikes me as a fixed point. On one side are the deflationists: qualia as standardly characterized — intrinsic, ineffable, private, directly and incorrigibly apprehended — pick out nothing, and the argument proceeds by showing that the canonical thought experiments are underdescribed. The cleanest illustration is Dennett's pair of coffee tasters, Chase and Sanborn, who after years on the job both report that the brew no longer tastes as good as it used to but give incompatible explanations, one blaming his palate and the other his standards; neither can settle the matter from the inside, and each report is consistent with indefinitely many mixtures of perceptual and evaluative change. Chase and Sanborn's own later work generalizes the move: once the functional details of an inverted-spectrum or zombie case are filled in, the stipulation turns out to describe several distinct scenarios, and only some of them are any threat to functionalism. On the other side, the realists reply that all of this misses the datum — however the description is completed, there is something it is like to taste the coffee, and an exhaustive account of discrimination, memory, and report still leaves that out.

Decades on, nobody has moved, and each camp accuses the other of changing the subject. I want a diagnosis rather than a verdict. Is this a verbal dispute about what 'qualia' is supposed to name? Are two different questions traveling under one label — one about what introspection seems to reveal, one about what a scientific explanation of behavior and report must cover? Is the disagreement about which cases count as data, stipulated scenarios against psychophysical findings? Or is there a hidden empirical question, such as how accurate and how fine-grained memory for one's own experience really is, that both sides are gesturing at obliquely? Ground the diagnosis in what each side actually argues rather than in a survey of positions, and say what would count as progress on your account: a distinction, an experimental result, a reformulation of the question, or a reason to expect none and why. Please write in American English.
