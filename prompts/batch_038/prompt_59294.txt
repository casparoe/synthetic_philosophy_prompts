I would like to know whether the configuration described below has an established name, and, if it does not, I would like help naming and delimiting it. The label matters less to me than the carving: what exactly the phenomenon is, what it is not, and where its edges fall.

The configuration. You acquire a reason to think that a judgement you have just made was formed under defective conditions. You were exhausted, or unusually cheerful, or you have been told by someone you trust that people who reason the way you just did turn out to be wrong at a startling rate. The natural response is to discount your own conclusion. But discounting requires a quantity, and the quantity cannot be had: you must estimate how much of your present conclusion is owed to the defect and how much to the subject matter, and you must estimate what you would have concluded had conditions been sound. The only instrument available for producing those estimates is the judgement whose reliability you have just impugned. The correction is computed by the thing being corrected. Trying to repair this by discounting the discount does not terminate the difficulty, since the second discount is issued by the same instrument, and nothing independent appears anywhere in the resulting series.

Two instances I keep returning to. First, a physician at the end of a long shift suspects that her impression that nothing serious is happening has been shaped by fatigue, and she knows roughly which way fatigue pushes; but the question of how far she has been pushed is being answered by the tired reader. Second, a person in an unusually good mood estimates that a venture will succeed; a friend observes that good mood inflates such estimates; the person concedes the point and shades the estimate downward, where the size of the shading is selected by the very mood that produced the inflation and which is therefore what makes a modest shading look sufficient. In the second case the severity of the defect seems to govern how mild the defect appears, so the correction moves inversely to the need for it. That inverse relation strikes me as the heart of the thing, and I do not know whether it is a recognized feature of anything.

What I am after in a reply:

- Whether this configuration, or that inverse relation in particular, has a name and an established home, or whether the nearest concepts are only approximations. I have in mind, described rather than named: the practice of using a source to confirm the source; the standing difficulty of detecting a bias in oneself that one detects readily in others; and holding a belief while also holding that one ought not hold it. For each candidate you judge nearest, state explicitly how the configuration above differs from it, and whether the difference is one of degree or of kind.
- Whether the difficulty is normative, in that there is no determinate answer to how far one should discount, or instead a limit on the agent's access to an answer that exists. My own diagnosis is the second, and I would like to know whether that diagnosis can be defended or whether it collapses into the first.
- What changes when an independent instrument is at hand: a protocol calibrated on rested clinicians, a colleague who is not in the mood in question. Does its availability dissolve the problem or relocate it, given that the decision to trust the instrument is itself made by the suspect judgement?
- Whether the direction of the defect matters: is the configuration worse, better, or merely different when the reason for doubt says only that one is unreliable here, without indicating in which direction?
- Whether iterating the situation, so that one also has reason to doubt one's assessment of the reason for doubt, adds anything substantive or simply repeats.

If no established term covers it, coin one, define it with conditions that rule out the neighbours, and supply two cases that look like instances but are not and one that does not look like an instance but is. Where you are uncertain whether a term is genuinely established or is instead something you are reconstructing, please say so plainly rather than supplying an attribution.
