I am in the final year of a philosophy degree and currently enrolled in a seminar on the frame problem and the limits of classical AI. Our department's student-run podcast has booked a guest for next month: a philosopher of cognitive science who defends a position she calls depth-indexed sphexishness. Her starting point is the familiar one — Hofstadter's coinage, applied to the digger wasp that, when an experimenter drags its paralyzed cricket a few inches away while the wasp is inside inspecting the burrow, hauls the prey back to the threshold and begins the entire inspection routine again, apparently without end. Where she parts company with the standard reading is in denying that sphexishness is a binary property that some systems possess and others lack. On her account, an agent is sphex-like relative to a perturbation P if applying P causes it to re-enter a subroutine it has just completed; rigidity is then a measure of how many perturbations, at what depth of the monitoring hierarchy, trigger such re-entry. Since any finite agent has some P for which this holds — she is fond of observing that even the most resourceful reasoner can be made to loop by a sufficiently clever intervention — she argues that wasps and people differ only quantitatively, and that what we experience as flexible intelligence is a stack of slower, higher-order rigid routines rather than an escape from mechanism at all.

I want the conversation to test that apparatus rather than let her restate it, and I know her habits from her published work. When challenged to say where flexibility begins, she retreats to the claim that there was never a categorical distinction to be had, and that the burden of proof falls on anyone who wants one; when shown a case of an animal escaping a loop, she absorbs it as further confirmation that her measure is continuous. Please draft eight to ten questions for a forty-minute recording, each phrased so that it can be read aloud as a single spoken question, with a one-sentence setup only where the listener genuinely needs it. For every question, add a follow-up written out in full and keyed to the reply she is most likely to give, so that the sequence makes the no-principled-threshold deflection progressively harder to deploy; the last item should ask what discovery would count against her view.

At a minimum, cover the following, in whatever order makes for the best arc:

1. Whether a measure with no principled threshold can still do the rhetorical work the wasp example is asked to do, and what her account predicts that a binary account gets wrong.
2. The masking problem. Two agents might behave identically under every perturbation anyone actually applies, yet one of them would have adapted under interventions that never occur. If her measure is purely behavioral, does it collapse the difference, and can she appeal to unmanifested dispositions without abandoning the deflationary program?
3. The ethological record. Some work argues that the loops terminate once enough iterations have elapsed, that repeated inspection of a disturbed nest may simply be adaptive, and that the version of the story circulating in philosophy descends from a mid-century secondary account of the brain rather than from primary observation; Fabre's related finding, that the wasp drags its prey by the antennae and does not switch to a leg when the antennae are removed, may be better evidence than the loop. Ask what happens to a theory of rigidity when its exemplar turns out to be flexible, and whether she can answer that without conceding the point in either direction.
4. The top of the hierarchy. If the highest-level routine is monitored by nothing above it, is it itself sphex-like, and does she require something like an unmoved monitor?
5. Human analogues — the Einstellung effect, the water-jar tasks, the candle problem. Are these genuine sphexishness or ordinary habit, and what on her measure separates them?
6. Free will. Does the thesis support compatibilism, by making freedom identical to hierarchical depth, or does it undercut the very distinction compatibilists need? Press her on whether depth smuggles in a normative ranking she claims to have naturalized away.
7. Whether large language models are sphex-like in her technical sense, and what perturbation would discriminate them from a merely stochastic parrot of one.
