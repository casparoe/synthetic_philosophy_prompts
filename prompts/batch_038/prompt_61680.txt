1. non-resistant nonbelief doesn't really exist — anyone who genuinely doesn't believe is either resisting or has been culpably shaped into not looking
2. skeptical theism — we're in no position at all to say what reasons a God would have for staying quiet
3. soul-making: real moral and spiritual growth needs an epistemically ambiguous world, so God keeps it that way
4. the seeking reply — a relationship that starts from unforced seeking is worth more, so God leaves enough darkness to make the seeking possible and meaningful
5. proper-function / cognitive-faculty replies — the capacity for belief in God is real but damaged or misfiring, so the fault is on our side even when we can't feel anything there
