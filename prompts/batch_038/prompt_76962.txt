Our tech lead waved it off: "it's a road map, not a driver. It channels where traffic goes but it doesnt decide anyone's destination. Saying the algorithm has politics is like saying the streets have politics." His conclusion was that if you want to close the service gap you fix the courier pay structure upstream, not the model.

Somthing about that bugs me and I can't locate it. Can you audit the analogy?
