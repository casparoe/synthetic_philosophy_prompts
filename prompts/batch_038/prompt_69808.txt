The catch is that the failure has to survive three constraints at once. First, the proxy must be noiseless: an exact record of something, not an estimate of the target with measurement error attached, so nobody can diagnose the case as a winner's-curse / regression-to-the-mean problem. Third, nobody is deliberately gaming the metric: no cheating, no misreporting, no adversarial agent anywhere in the story. Given all that, the damage has to come purely from the causal structure: the proxy sits downsteam of the target, or shares a common cause with it, and the organization finds a route to the proxy that bypasses whatever was generating the correlation in the first place.
