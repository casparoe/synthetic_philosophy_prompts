And there's a fight about whether that idealized person is still "you" in any way that matters, and about whether you can even write down the idealizing conditions without either smuggling the answer in or making the output arbitrary. Apparently some AI people have floated doing the same trick at civilizational scale, extrapolating everybody's reflective wishes into one coherent thing.

The incident: the product returned a verdict from a user's idealized self and the user absolutely refused to accept it, and on-call spent a week escalating.

Specifically, work these in as engineering failure modes rather than as objections — never name them as objections, never wink at the reader:

- the fact that the product has no enforcement mechanism at all, since nothing makes the real user obligated to listen to their ideal self
