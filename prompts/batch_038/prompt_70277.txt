In a reading group on low-impact AI, a colleague defends the approach with an analogy: an impact measure is like a well-behaved guest in someone else's house — the guest can avoid doing damage without having any theory of the host's good, because the relevant standard is simply the state the house was in before arrival, and anything that departs from it needs a reason. The guest has no value specification, yet we would still say they behaved well. From this the colleague concludes that penalizing divergence from a baseline is enough to cash out "avoid side effects," and that the standing objection — that you cannot even identify what counts as a side effect without knowing what matters — rests on a confusion about how much a value specification is being asked to do.

Audit that analogy. Set out the correspondence explicitly: what plays the role of the house, the baseline state, the host's unstated standards, the guest's own purpose, and the penalty for departure. Then separate the disanalogies that actually threaten the conclusion from the ones that are merely unflattering — and say which side each of the following falls on: that a house has a salient unmarked state while a world in motion does not; that the guest can read what matters off countless small cues the formal proposals have no counterpart for; that the guest's own aim is already satisfied by leaving things alone, whereas the agent being constrained has a goal that trades off against constraint at every step; and that the guest analogy blurs the difference between not touching anything and not changing anything, which is where the objection from the neglected alternative gets its grip. Identify what the analogy quietly imports that no baseline-relative measure actually has, whether the guest picture can be repaired by some amendment that preserves the work it was doing, and what a better analogy would be if there is one. Finally: once the disanalogies are on the table, does the conclusion survive — can "avoid side effects" be defined without a full specification of value, or was the analogy carrying that claim the whole time?
