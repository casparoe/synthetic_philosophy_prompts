Can you restate both positions in the vocabulary I actually think in — operational definitions, verbal report as the only measurement channel, confabulation treated as an artifact, retroactive reconstruction, what counts as evidence for an event you never observed, criterian problems in scoring? Do it faithfully. Don't quietly convert the whole thing into an empirical question just because that's more comfortable for me, and don't smuggle in the lab's assumptions and call it translation.

Then the residue report: which of their claims translate cleanly into bench language, which survive only if you bend them, and which have no counterpart in it at all. My hunch is that Malcolm is just being an operationalist — no report, no event — and that the disagreement mostly evaporates once you say it that way.
