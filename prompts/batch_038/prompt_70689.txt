Veterans groups, chiefly the Air Force Association and the American Legion, and then a lot of Congress called it revisionist and unpatriotic; the script went thru repeated cuts, the anniversary exhibit was scrapped in favor of a much smaller display of just the fuselage plus a short video, and the director resigned a few months later.
