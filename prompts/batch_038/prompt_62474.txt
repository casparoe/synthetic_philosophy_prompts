the idea: say some action would be either good or bad to take, and every person who *could* take it has pretty good but not perfect judgement about which. if only one person can act, you mostly get the right outcome. but if like twenty people could each act on their own, it only takes one of them to be the unlucky wrong one, so the odds that the bad thing happens go UP as the group gets bigger — even though nobody in it is stupid or evil or trying to sabotage anything. and the supposed fix is some kind of veto/consensus rule where everybody has to sign off before it happens, which is awkward bc now one crank can freeze everything.

can you write the story for me? setting: a six-person team at a lab that's just found something, and any one of them could release it on their own — no committee, no chain of command.
