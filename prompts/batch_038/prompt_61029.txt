But I think there are at least three separate axes getting lumped together:

1. intensity — how loud or bright a given experience is;
2. breadth and unity — how many experiences at once, and whether they're bound into a single subject, which matters if you care about the number of welfare subjects rather than the total amount of experience;
3. temporal thickness — whether the experience has anticipatory and retrospective reach: dread, relief, remembering being hurt, caring about what happens next week.

My hunch is that most of the practical disagreements — bugs vs fish vs mammals, whether to weight by neuron counts, whether fission or splitting-brain cases break the moral math — are really disagreements about axis 2 or 3 masquerading as disagreements about axis 1.
