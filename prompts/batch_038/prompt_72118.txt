I want each side's P(heads), each side's fair odds on a bet that pays out if heads, and — the part I actually care about — a real treatment of the wrinkle that the 1000 are qualitatively identical copies of one person: does that collapse the count to one, or leave it at 1000, and does the answer differ by camp? Then the presumptuous-philosopher worry, since in this case indexical information is doing all the work on a claim about the world outside the simulation.

Draft the joint statement the two of them could both sign: a numbered section of claims they agree on, a disagreements table where each position is worded so its own side would accept the wording, the cruxes, and for each crux a test or argument both would grant bears on it, with each side's prediction written down in advance. And if you think a chunk of this is just a verbal fight about what counts as an observer, say which chunk and tell me what's left over that isn't verbal.
