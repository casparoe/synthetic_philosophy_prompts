I am a third-year PhD student in chemical physics, working on metastability: nucleation, Kramers-type escape from shallow basins, Arrhenius temperature dependences, hysteresis in driven systems, and the many ways in which a small perturbation, an impurity or a surface treatment can suppress a transition that a material is otherwise apt to undergo. Reading the metaphysics of dispositions for a side project, I was struck by how familiar the conditional analysis of dispositional properties and its standard counterexamples — finks, masks, antidotes, and the reformed analysis that tries to disarm them by requiring the intrinsic basis to be retained through the stimulus — feel from where I sit.

Translate that package into the vocabulary I actually use, item by item: the disposition, its basis, the stimulus, the manifestation, the fink, the mask, the antidote, and the ceteris paribus clause that keeps being invoked to rescue the analysis. For each, say which physical notion is doing the work — metastable basin, barrier height, escape rate relative to the timescale of the perturbation, passivation or inhibition, annealing that removes the defect altogether — and mark the places where you have to stretch the mapping to make it hold.

Then give me the residue report, and be blunt about it. Which claims in the debate have no counterpart at all in a rate-theoretic description? For each, say whether my vocabulary is missing something real, or whether the absence indicates that the dispute was less substantive than it looked — in particular, whether a framework in which every generalisation already carries implicit background conditions makes the conditional analysis unfalsifiable rather than false, and whether the fink/mask distinction survives as a genuine distinction once everything is expressed in terms of what changes and on what timescale. Do not let my assumptions perform the translation for you: wherever a step works only because I have already taken dispositions to be grounded in structural properties, and modal claims to be claims about rates and timescales, flag it as such rather than presenting it as a neutral mapping.
