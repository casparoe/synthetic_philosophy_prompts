"Samuelson's revealed preference theory rescued economics from the nonsense of unobservable utility. The theory is also unfalsifiable, which is its great virtue — you can never catch it out, bc whatever someone chooses is by definition what they preferred, and that's exactly what makes it a clean behavioral science instead of psychology. And it explains why the anomalies don't threaten anything either: those experiments where people choose inconsistently, or buy more of a good when its price rises, are just noise around the same core fact, that choices reveal preferences and preferences are nothing over and above choices."
