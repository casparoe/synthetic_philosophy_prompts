I train models that learn latent representations of physical systems from sensor data, and a large share of my working life is spent on identifiability: proving that the latent space a model recovers is pinned down only up to some class of invertible transformations, never up to identity, and then deciding which of those equivalences I actually care about. I mention this because it frames how I came to the argument that has cost me most of a weekend. A close friend of mine is an academic philosopher of mathematics. She made me read Benacerraf's 1973 paper on mathematical truth, and my immediate reaction was that what looked to her like a devastating dilemma looked to me like a familiar and quite manageable modeling situation. Her semantic horn — that singular terms in mathematics ought to refer, and that the only candidates are abstract objects — struck me as a demand that a representation be fixed to an object rather than to an equivalence class, which is a demand nobody else in science makes. Her epistemic horn — that abstract objects are outside space, time, and causal interaction, so how could we know about them — struck me as a worry about whether a learning process can be reliable without the right kind of contact with its target, which is again something I have opinions about. She listened to all this and said I was changing the subject, and that reliability is not the issue: contact is. Three hours later we had gotten nowhere, and I have been mulling it since.

What I want is a serious translation exercise. Take the dilemma as Benacerraf frames it and restate it, as faithfully as you can manage, in the vocabulary I actually live in: latent representations, identifiability up to isomorphism or other equivalences, supervision and proxy signals, causal intervention versus mere correlation, distribution shift, the gap between a model being accurate and its being about anything. Both horns, not just the one I found congenial, and also the earlier progression argument if you think it belongs — the point that any structure satisfying the right relations will do, so there is no fact of the matter which objects the numbers are. Where you need Benacerraf's own claims, paraphrase them; I want the argument, not the prose, and I do not want a reading list, since I will get one from her if I ask. Where you need an illustration, use artifacts of my kind rather than toy philosophical examples.

Then give me the residue report, and treat it as the main event rather than the afterthought. Go claim by claim: what translates cleanly; what translates only at the cost of a distortion you should name explicitly; and what has no expression at all in my vocabulary. Two things in particular I want you to be honest about. First, whether the translation begs the question — my vocabulary may already presuppose a causal-informational account of what it is for a representation to be about something, in which case rendering the epistemic horn in my terms might appear to dissolve it only because I have built the answer into the tools, and if so I want that said rather than papered over. Second, whether her objection that I am changing the subject is right: is there a distinction she is drawing that my framework genuinely lacks the resources to state, or is her insistence on contact a residue of a picture of knowledge that does not survive scrutiny? For each piece of residue, tell me whether it is a defect in my vocabulary, a sign that the original claim was less clear than it looked, or a real and permanent limit. And locate the main positions in the neighborhood — Platonism, fictionalism, structuralism, the naturalized indispensability line, whatever else you think belongs — in the translated space, and say which of them have no home there and why.

Be committed rather than diplomatic. I do not want a survey that closes by noting that reasonable people differ; I want your judgment about which horn survives the translation and what that tells me about who was right on Saturday night, stated with whatever confidence you actually have and with the weakest point in your own case identified. Finally, if you think I have set this up wrongly — if there is no translation to be had, or if my framing of the request already decides the outcome and would make the whole exercise a piece of theater — say so plainly and explain why, instead of producing a careful answer that quietly confirms what I was hoping to hear.
