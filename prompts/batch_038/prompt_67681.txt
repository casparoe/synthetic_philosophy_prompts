- Maya is 0.6 that the staff meeting runs long and 0.6 that it doesn't. She'll pay 60c for a bet returning $100 if it runs long, and 60c for one returning $100 if it doesn't. Out $120, back $100, no matter what. Label: irrational, obviously.
- Theo is 0.8 that the biopsy shows cancer. He knows he's a denier, so when the report comes back positive he expects to talk himself down to 0.5. A bookie sells him "$100 if cancer" for $80 today; tomorrow, report in hand, Theo sells the same bet back for $50. Out $30 guaranteed. Label: irrational, this is the textbook one.

Told the result at 9am she goes to 0.99 and buys "$100 if cancer" for $99; by noon she doesn't retain the report, is back at 0.8, and sells it for $80. Out $19 for sure — and the bookie could have arranged the whole thing yesterday, since the sedation was already on the schedule. I wrote "irrational," crossed it out, and wrote "??? she did the right thing at every single step." This is the one that bugs me, because it looks structurally the same as Theo and I want to give the opposite verdict.
