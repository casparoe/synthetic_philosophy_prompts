ok so this is for the humor page of my school's lit mag, and also partly my bio teacher's fault bc we did a unit on animal communication and I went way too deep on the handicap principle afterwards. I want you to write a fake webpage for a bureaucracy that certifies whether someone's spending counts as an honest signal — like an official standards body for costly signaling. Deadpan and officious, no winking at the camera, think a real certification org's "standards, tiers and fees" page with headings, a short FAQ, and a fee schedule. Around 650 words.

The jokes have to come from getting the actual theory right, not from peacock-tail clichés. Please build in all of these:

- tiers that track the real distinction between index signals (you cant fake them bc they're physically tied to the signaller's quality, so they're honest but basically free — the org should be openly snooty about them), conventional signals (cheap and arbitrary, kept honest by receivers beating up cheaters — the org should consider punishment-based honesty low-effort and unprestigious), and true handicaps (deliberately wasteful, the only thing they'll fully certify)
- something should hinge on the fact that what matters in the formal models is the *marginal* cost to the signaller given their quality, not the absolute price tag. like auditors approving a cheap item bc it was condition-dependent enough, or stripping certification from something expensive that turned out to cost that particular buyer nothing
- a rule that you can't recoup the cost afterwards — no tax writeoff, no reselling it, no "but the post did numbers" — bc a signal you earn back isnt a handicap
- the countersignalling problem: their wealthiest applicants stopped signaling entirely, so now the org certifies the absence of signaling, which is obviously unfalsifiable, and they should be smug about this rather than bothered
- one gag about how any behavior whatsoever can be reclassified as a costly signal after the fact, and the appeals board basically runs on that principle

The target is the pop-sci habit of explaining every wasteful human institution as honest signalling (Zahavi's handicap idea plus the game-theoretic versions where cost is what makes it credible), not any real person — invent the org, its name, and its director. It should make someone who knows the signalling literature laugh rather than wince, and still be readable for my friends who've never heard of any of this. Also, if you can work in a line where they brag about the total monetary value destroyed this fiscal year, that'd be perfect, bc that's the deadweight-loss bit my teacher keeps bringing up.
