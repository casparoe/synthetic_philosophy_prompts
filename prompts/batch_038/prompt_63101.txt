Our consciousness reading group blew a fuse last night over color phi. We'd just gone over the setup — blue dot near the top of the screen, red dot further down about 150ms later, and subjects report one dot traveling between them that switches color at the midpoint, which is before there was any second dot to learn the color from.

Marcus said that's a clean win for the Stalinesque side of Dennett's distinction: the brain sits on the input until the second frame lands, composes a motion story with the switch in the middle, and hands that to the subject as the experience. So the temporal shape of the experience is written after the fact, and there's no determinate moment at which the subject was having a blue-dot-starts-moving experience.

Dana said he was overclaiming. The reason Orwellian and Stalinesque get posed as a pair, she said, is that they can't be told apart: on the Orwellian story the subject really does have the veridical experience and the later input just overwrites the memory before it gets reported. Same report either way. So the paradigm can't select between them, and Marcus is reading a conclusion out of data that doesn't contain it.

Marcus said indistinguishable in this paradigm, sure, but not in principle, and offered a test: put the channels in competition. Have the subject saccade to the midpoint, or run a speeded forced-choice instead of describing what they saw — if the overwritten veridical experience is still sitting anywhere, some non-report channel should leak it. Dana said that's re-asserting the rival rather than testing it: if a leak shows up, you've shown there's a second trace, not that there was a second experience, and if none shows up you've proven nothing, since the Orwellian was never committed to a leak.

Then Dana went somewhere else: the question Marcus wants answered isn't which mechanism, it's whether an experience that never surfaces in any report at any time is an experience at all. Marcus's last move was that she needs that question to have an answer too, because if it doesn't, the midway color switch isn't an experience of anything and there's nothing left to explain.

Nobody budged and its 2am for me. Can you take this move by move — who's right at each step, does Marcus's leak test actually discriminate, does Dana's "no experiment could tell them apart so they're notational variants" charge stick or is it just incredulity with a better name — and then say what both of them are treating as settled that shouldn't be?
