My band is putting an English version of an old French song into a wedding set, and the melody locks everything down — every line has to land the same number of syllables on the same beats, or it sounds like mush. So I've been rewriting the lyrics for weeks, swapping images, ditching alot of the literal meaning, just to keep the rhyme scheme and make the stresses fall right. Then our guitarist tells me I'm wasting my time: a translation is good if it says the same thing, so just sing what the French words actually mean and if it doesn't rhyme, who cares, nobodys checking. He ran the original through one of those translator apps, got something perfectly clear and perfectly flat, and says that's the real translation and mine is basically a different song. I feel like he's missing something big but I can't get it into words. Is he right that as long as the meaning comes across, the form is just decoration? And practically — one line turns on a pun that has no English equivalent at all. Do I keep the joke and change what the line is about, or keep what it's about and lose the joke?
