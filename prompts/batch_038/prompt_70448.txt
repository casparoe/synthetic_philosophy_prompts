Compose a short joke whose punchline depends on the color phi phenomenon and, more specifically, on the difference between Orwellian and Stalinesque accounts of what happens when a subject reports that a light changed color midway through an apparent motion that, given the timing, could not have been witnessed as it is described. For the joke to work, the listener should have to understand why the two accounts are supposed to be indistinguishable in their observable consequences, and why that indistinguishability is itself a problem for any story about when a conscious state occurs; a joke that could be retold about zombies, Mary the color scientist, or the trolley problem with only the names changed does not count. Please keep it to a compact setup and payoff rather than a shaggy-dog story, make the timing of the lights part of the machinery rather than window dressing, and avoid jokes that rest on a philosopher's name, on puns involving the word qualia, or on the assumption that the subject in the experiment is simply lying. If the funniest version requires the setup to specify which of the two accounts the joke is siding with, say so and explain why.
