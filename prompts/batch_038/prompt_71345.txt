I lead a small interpretability group inside a company that ships vision models for aerial wildlife surveys, and our written reports routinely contain sentences of two kinds: this internal feature tracks the presence of a juvenile animal rather than an adult one, and this misclassification happened because the system represented a shadow as a body of water. A colleague who works two desks over from me insists both sentence types are instrumentalist shorthand with no fact of the matter behind them — useful fictions, no different in kind from saying a thermostat believes the room is cold. Before I draft a standing methodology document that commits us to one side of that argument, I want the specification, not the arguments for particular views. Tell me what a theory of representational content would have to deliver for our usage to be more than shorthand, given that the systems in question are artifacts whose only relevant history is a training run: no evolutionary lineage, and — this is a stipulation I want respected throughout — no appeal to what any human intended. That stipulation isn't arbitrary; our models are fine-tuned by dozens of engineers and thousands of annotators with inconsistent aims, so there is no single designer whose purposes could plausibly fix what an internal state is about. Whatever fixes content has to be located in the artifact's own causal-functional organization plus whatever historical facts about the training process a naturalist is entitled to invoke.

Here is what I need in the list, and I want the motivating case attached to every requirement rather than the requirement in isolation. Start from cases like these. A feature fires reliably for wolves, for huskies, and for a particular overcast sky at a certain altitude, because in the training distribution those three co-occurred; what would have to be true for there to be a determinate answer to whether it means wolf, canine, or wolf-or-that-sky? A model classifies an image as a gibbon after an imperceptible perturbation is added to a picture of a panda; I want a theory that licenses saying the system has misrepresented rather than the flat-footed alternative that it is faithfully representing a gibbon-like stimulus. Some states are never in a position to be wrong at all, because the property they respond to and some confusable property were perfectly correlated throughout training — say what a theory owes us in that case, and whether the content is simply indeterminate or whether it is determinate but unknowable from inside. Then the structural ones. Does content have to be carried by anything like a unit, or must the theory handle distributed high-dimensional states whose "aboutness" has no ordinary name because it corresponds only to a statistical regularity in a dataset? Must the theory explain why the same internal state can be recruited into quite different downstream behaviors, and whether that is a reason to deny it has content or a reason to demand a richer account of what content does? Must content be causally efficacious, in the sense that a theory on which content never makes a difference to behavior is thereby disqualified? Is naturalism a requirement that content be reducible to non-semantic facts, or only that it be determined by them — and does it matter for us which of those the correct theory offers? Finally, add the requirement I suspect most theories will resist: whatever the account says, content attributions must be capable of being supported or undermined by evidence available to us, since a view on which our reports are systematically verification-transcendent would leave the methodology document with nothing to say.

Then do the sorting, which is the part I actually want. Mark which of these requirements are non-negotiable for any theory of content whatsoever, which are generated by my applied purpose and would drop out for a theory of frog vision or human belief, and which are really the prejudices of one camp wearing the costume of neutral criteria — I have a suspicion about the demand that content be determinate all the way down to a single property, and I want it tested. Flag any pair or triple you believe cannot be jointly satisfied; in particular, tell me whether the conjunction of no-appeal-to-designer-intention, genuine misrepresentation, and determinacy in the presence of perfect co-extension throughout training is satisfiable at all, and if it is not, which conjunct has to be surrendered and what my reports would then have to stop saying. Identify the single requirement that eliminates the most candidates. Close with one paragraph — argued, not surveyed, and no literature map, since I can read that myself — on which structural family fails first against your list: accounts that locate content-making in a selection history of some kind, accounts that locate it in present lawful covariance between state and world, accounts that locate it in the role a state plays in the dispositions of the system as a whole, and accounts that locate it in what an ideal interpreter would attribute. And end by telling me straight whether my colleague is right, on the best surviving account, that our sentences are fictions.
