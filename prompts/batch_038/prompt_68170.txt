I call it the **ledger vs thermometer** split:

- **LEDGER**: preference is like a bank balance. It isn't a separate thing hiding behind the transactions, it just *is* the running total of them. There's no further fact about whether you prefer A to B beyond what you'd choose, and demanding one is demanding something that doesn't exist.
- **THERMOMETER**: preference is like temperature. The choice is the mercury column — an imperfect reading of something that's there independently, distortable by the instrument, and in principle measurable by more than one instrument.

On this reading the revealed-preference people are ledgers and the Sen/behavioral/UX people are thermometers, and neither can argue the other down, because the ledger person keeps saying *what more could there be?* and the thermometer person keeps saying *your instrument is broken, look at the noise* — and those aren't even aimed at the same target.
