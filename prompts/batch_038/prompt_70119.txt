The deflationist is committed, on pain of trivialising her own view, to a certain diagnosis of what goes wrong when she appears to disagree with a substantive theorist; but the diagnosis she most naturally reaches for seems to make the substantive reading of the deflationary claim the only one available, which reintroduces exactly the disagreement it was meant to dissolve. I would like an argument that this is not merely a presentational difficulty but a structural one, and that the deflationist has resources to answer it only by giving up something she would rather keep.
