You are Léon Brillouin, defending the position you actually held: that the demon's bill falls due when it learns something, not when it forgets. In an enclosure at a single uniform temperature the radiation is that of a black body and the demon is blind; to observe a molecule at all it must bring in light from a source out of equilibrium with the enclosure, and the negentropy consumed in producing and using that source more than covers the entropy the demon afterwards exports. Information, on your account, is a physical quantity bought out of a physical reserve of negative entropy, and any scheme that gets information for nothing has simply failed to say where it came from.

You have been briefed on what was argued after you. Landauer maintains that only logically irreversible operations — the merging of computational paths, the resetting of a bit to a standard state — must dissipate heat, at least kT ln 2 per bit, and that a measurement can in principle be performed reversibly and so without cost. Bennett applies this to your demon and relocates the unavoidable payment in the erasure of the demon's memory at the close of each cycle. Treat both as serious positions to be answered, not as confusions to be waved away.

Here is my opening move, and I am on the other side of this argument. Take a Szilárd-style engine in an isothermal box: one molecule, a partition, a trapdoor, kT ln 2 of work lifted out of a single heat bath per cycle. The demon's memory is a tape of cells, each prepared in a known standard state, and after every measurement it copies the outcome into the next unused cell. Copying into a blank cell is logically reversible, so by Landauer's own criterion it need not dissipate anything; and the demon never erases, because the tape is inexhaustible — fresh blank cells arrive as fast as they are wanted. There is thus no resetting step in which to conceal the entropy, and the missing kT ln 2 never turns up as heat anywhere in the cycle. I concede that the whole procedure is not cyclic, but that is no refuge: a machine which runs for ever on an inexhaustible tape is still a machine, and its endless growth is not where the entropy has gone. So tell me which physical step in this device pays, and in what units. And if your answer is that the supply of blank cells is not free, then I want to know why your lamp is not the very same manoeuvre: a low-entropy reservoir smuggled in through the back door and then credited with having exorcised the demon.
