I am a senior in high school and I belong to a six-person club that spent the autumn restoring a derelict tandem bicycle, with the aim of entering it in a spring exhibition. Nobody ever voted or made a formal agreement. The project simply began appearing in conversation, and when I asked afterwards it turned out that each of the three of us who first said we were on board had assumed one of the others had already settled on it, so that no individual person actually chose the project, and yet the club was plainly doing it. Two members have since performed nearly all of the work. Last week a third, who has contributed very little, announced he is withdrawing because his grades have suffered. The two who did the work are angry; they say he owes the club an apology and that no one gets to walk away from something the club took on together. I believe he is entitled to go, and I feel none of the pull they feel, but I cannot say what the difference is supposed to be between a promise one person makes to another and whatever this club did in September. Can you help me work out whether the group ever really committed to anything at all, in a sense that could bind each of us separately, and whether the anger of the two hardworking members is justified?
