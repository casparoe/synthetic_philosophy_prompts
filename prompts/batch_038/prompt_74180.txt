(2) Religious belief arises from the infant's helplessness and the resulting wish for a protective father, so it's an illusion.

(5) But psychoanalysis itself has a pedigree of exactly that sort — it grows out of Freud's own ambivalence toward his father and toward authority figures generally, out of his need to be the discoverer, out of a wish for mastery over the mind.
(6) So psychoanalysis is itself an illusion by (1).

(8) So this argument, if it works, works against itself: it says that no wish-shaped belief deserves assent, including the belief expressed by the argument.
