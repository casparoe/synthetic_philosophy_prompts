He says the argument applies no matter whats in your hand, and it still applies after you've traded, so you should just keep trading back and forth forever and it's free money. When I said that cant be right he said that's not a flaw in the argument, it's because the amounts could in principle be anything, so the average really is infinite, like the St Petersburg lottery thing, and there's no paradox at all once you accept that.
