Consider a hypothetical office of scientific planning of the kind envisaged by Carnap and Neurath: an institution that aspires to convert political controversy into empirical and logical questions which experts can, in principle, settle. Audit that ambition using only resources drawn from Carnap's own philosophical toolkit, on the following terms.

I stipulate a measure. For a doctrine D, the depoliticization yield y(D) is the fraction of one concrete political disagreement that, among disputants who fully accept D, ceases to be a matter of contested values or ultimate commitments and instead becomes answerable by evidence, computation, or the application of agreed rules.

First, choose a single running example of a mid-level political disagreement of your own devising — a housing levy, a compulsory curriculum, a public-health mandate — specific enough that its factual, instrumental, and evaluative components can be pulled apart. Hold that example fixed throughout. Carnap was a committed left-wing internationalist who nonetheless denied that value sentences are truth-apt; treat that combination as the phenomenon to be measured rather than as an embarrassment to be explained away.

Now score each of these six doctrines:

1. The dissolution of metaphysics. Statements purporting to describe the Absolute, Being as such, or the spirit of a people are pseudo-statements: they express an attitude to life without asserting anything.
2. Non-cognitivism about values. Normative sentences are neither true nor false; science can adjudicate the causal and instrumental claims embedded in a program, but not the preferences that program serves.
3. The principle of tolerance. Any linguistic framework may legitimately be adopted, and the choice among frameworks is assessed by pragmatic fruitfulness rather than by correctness.
4. The internal/external distinction. Existence questions raised inside a framework have determinate answers; raised about the framework as a whole, they collapse into practical proposals about which language to employ.
5. Physicalism and the unity of science. Every statement of every special science is translatable into a spatio-temporal language about observable things, so all disciplines can deliberate in a single shared idiom.
6. Inductive logic. Degrees of confirmation are fixed by the logical relations between hypotheses and evidence, and can be computed so as to discipline both belief and choice under uncertainty.

For each doctrine, report y as a decimal, assign a rank, and give two or three sentences identifying precisely which component of your running example the doctrine absorbs and which it leaves standing.

Then answer three further questions.

(a) Give a single composite yield for the conjunction of all six, and explain why it falls where it does relative to the individual scores. If one doctrine interferes with another — if, say, a doctrine reclassifies as mere practical preference something another had promised to render decidable — say so explicitly and quantify the loss.
(b) Name the residual political question that survives even the highest-yield doctrine, and state in one or two sentences whether it is a question Carnap's program is able to formulate at all.
(c) End with a verdict stated flatly: on your numbers, does the Carnapian toolkit support the claim that a scientific world-conception can substitute administration for politics, or does it secure the opposite? Do not hedge.
