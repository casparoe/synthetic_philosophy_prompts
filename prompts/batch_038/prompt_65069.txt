Saturday I give a toast at my dad's retirement party, and on paper the same week the business gets handed over to me. He founded a commercial bakery in 1987 and has told one story about it at every dinner for thirty years: bootstrapped, alone, six days a week, and then he gave his daughter a start. What actually happened is that my mother set the credit terms and did the route sales for two decades, and I've been running the place since 2016 while he comes in on Tuesdays. Everybody in the family knows this; we just dont say it out loud in front of him, and on Saturday two hundred of his friends and old employees will be in the room.

Here's the bind. I'm in the fifth year of a philosophy PhD and self-deception is my thing — specifically the deflationary line: there's no hidden inner knower keeping score, the whole 'deep down he knows' picture is a bad model of what's going on, and a case like my dad's is just motivated belief, built out of which evidence he bothers to go looking for and how heavily he weighs what comes back. No contradiction, no secret self, no buried truth waiting to surface. So I can't reach for any of the obvious consolations or barbs — nothing about the truth he wouldn't face, nothing about him knowing really, nothing about guilt or conscience. If I use that vocabulary I've gotten his mind wrong and insulted him in front of everyone who loves him.

Write the toast. It has to be warm, and it has to land as a joke the room laughs with him at rather than at. The blind spot should come out as part of why we love him, not as a charge. But it also has to quietly do the philosophy: plain English, zero technical terms, and nobody should be able to point at a sentence and say she's announcing a thesis. The two or three academics at the back table should hear the argument, my mother should hear her daughter being kind, and my dad should hear something true enough that he can laugh.

Hard cap of 500 words — I've got four minutes and my uncle will hog the mic. And before the toast, in one sentence: tell me the line you most wanted to use and why I've banned it.
