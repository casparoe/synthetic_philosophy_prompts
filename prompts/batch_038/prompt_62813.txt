Suppose an engineering team proposes a safety monitor for an autonomous vehicle that runs on a paraconsistent logic. The sensor-fusion module routinely yields contradictory readings; the classical monitor deadlocks on detecting them; so the new design tolerates contradiction locally while blocking arbitrary inference from it, leaving the rest of the stack classical. Two philosophers disagree about what this amounts to. A Quinean holist says it shows that even non-contradiction is revisable under empirical pressure: the web of belief has no fixed center, and the team followed the evidence into it. A hinge epistemologist says nothing was revised, because non-contradiction was never a belief but a hinge — exempt from doubt, and displayed in what the team still does everywhere else, since they continue to treat a contradiction elsewhere in the stack as a defect to be hunted down. What changed is a technique, and techniques are adopted, not shown to be true.

Write a dialogue between these two, each fully competent and neither a strawman, arguing through this case rather than reciting their general positions. Have them press on: whether a hinge is anything more than a central belief with the truth-predicate withheld; whether the holist can account for the team's reason to switch without turning that reason into evidence bearing on the hinge itself; and what the hinge theorist gains from Wittgenstein's remark in On Certainty (§204) that "it is our acting which lies at the bottom of the language-game." Then complicate the case: a year on, the engineers have begun reasoning paraconsistently in unrelated subsystems, which appears to withdraw the hinge theorist's "they still treat it as a bug elsewhere" reply — yet the same engineers are unmoved by a bank statement showing an account both credited and overdrawn. Let each side exploit what it can of this. Close with each stating in one sentence the claim the other must deny, and with an assessment of whether that is a substantive disagreement or a quarrel over how "belief" is to be used.
