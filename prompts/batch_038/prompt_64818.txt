Meanwhile I gather the philosophy of language has a whole literature on exactly when saying something counts as doing something — felicity conditions, whether the speaker has the standing to say it, whether the hearer took it the right way, whether it was obviously a joke, the difference between what the words literally mean and what was done in saying them. I've never read any of it and I'm not going to.

What I need is for you to convert it into a triage procedure I can actually run. Fifteen minutes, on my phone, on my lunch break, with a member sitting across from me who is already upset.
