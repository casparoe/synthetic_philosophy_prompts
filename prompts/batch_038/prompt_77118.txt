> "You compare each of your strategies against the others. Then you look at what's left and do the same again, and again, until nothing more goes. Order doesn't matter here: a strictly dominated strategy is dominated whatever else you've already deleted, so everyone who does it carefully ends up in the same place. Same goes for the weakly dominated ones, the strategies that are never better and are sometimes strictly worse — you might as well clear those out too, and you'll still land on the same answer whichever order you clear them in. You only ever need to compare strategy against strategy, by the way. Mixing them doesn't buy you anything, because if a strategy deserves deleting then one of the others will beat it outright.
