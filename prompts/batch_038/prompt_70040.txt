Here's the half-formed thought. The paradox only gets its grip if you grant what I want to call the single-subject assumption: that morality is one decision procedure, addressed to one agent, delivered at one moment. Different addressee, different time of issuance — so maybe the two claims aren't on the same scale and were never available to be weighed against each other, which would make the paradox a scope error instead of a refutation.
