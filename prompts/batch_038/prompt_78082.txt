"Kahneman's insight is that there are two ways to make a prediction. The inside view is when you look at the details of your own case: your team, your plan, why your situation is special. The outside view is when you set all that aside and just ask what happens on average in cases like yours. The planning fallacy happens because the inside view feels so much more informative that people keep using it, even though on its own it tells you nothing. The cure is discipline: find the average, report the average, and only move off it if you have solid evidence that your case is genuinely different. Epistemic modesty just means accepting that you are a typical case until you've proven otherwise."
