ok so this has been going on for like two months and I want a third party who isn't in my family.

I draw. Alot. Most nights until 1 or 2am, sketchbook after sketchbook, I've filled maybe nine this year. My mom has decided this means I want to apply to the arts magnet next year, even tho I've told her maybe six separate times that I don't. Her whole argument is that what people say they want is worthless, because everyone just says whatever sounds reasonable, and the only real evidence is what they actually spend their time doing when nobody's making them. And the annoying part is she's genuinely good at this — last year I said "I don't care where we go for my birthday," she picked this tiny place that had a printmaking studio in the back, and I had the best weekend of my life. I would never have chosen it myself. So she has receipts.

But here's the thing I can't get her to answer. This spring I actually got the packet for the magnet. I had nine days to send it in. I didnt. She says that doesn't count, because that was fear, and fear isn't a preference. And I'm like... okay, but then what would count? If drawing means I want it, and not applying means I was scared so ignore that — is there anything at all I could do that would show her I don't want it? When I say that out loud she tells me I'm being defensive, which she says is more evidence.

I also have my own theory and I don't know if it's any better than hers: maybe I draw at 1am because it's the one thing where I'm allowed to be bad and nobody grades it, and the second it turns into an application it stops being that thing. Which would mean her rule picked up on something completely real about me and then drew exactly the wrong conclusion from it.

So is she doing something legitimate here, or is she just steamrolling me with a nice-sounding theory? And if it's steamrolling, how do I actually show her that, without it turning into a fight about my bedtime? Because the part that really bothers me isn't the magnet. It's that if she wins this one, then from now on any time I tell anyone I don't want something, they get to say they know better than I do about what I want. Is that just me being dramatic?
