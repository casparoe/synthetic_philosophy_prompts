A friend and I argued for the better part of two hours last night about standing to blame, and I came away unsure whether the position I defended is even coherent. Here it is, stated as fairly as I can:

1. Standing to blame derives from one's own endorsement of the standard being invoked. It is not a function of any special relationship to the wrongdoer or to the victim: the well-disposed stranger occupies exactly the position the wrongdoer's brother occupies.
2. What hypocrisy defeats, and all that it defeats, is standing; and what does the defeating is not the bare fact of past wrongdoing but the unfairness of exempting oneself from a demand one presses on others.
3. It follows that the man who did precisely the same thing, who now accepts that the standard applied to him all along, who blames himself and has made amends, retains his standing to blame others — and his blame may be the most apt in the room.
4. Yet the man with an identical record who is inwardly convinced that things were different in his case, and who has never breathed a word of this conviction, forfeits standing even when the blame he issues publicly is word-for-word indistinguishable from the repentant man's.
5. Unexpressed resentment is genuine blame, but I want to maintain that it does not require standing in the way that blame addressed to someone does.
6. Finally, I want to say that the spotless moraliser next door — guilty of nothing resembling the wrong, holding himself to every standard he invokes, connected to the wrongdoer by nothing but curiosity — can nonetheless lack standing, on the ground that it is no business of his.

Where we got stuck: my friend insisted that (2) does all the work I need and that the rest is decoration, and I could not say why that felt wrong. So I would like an audit of the set rather than a lecture on the topic. Tell me which pairs genuinely pull against one another and which only appear to, ordered by severity rather than listed as equally bad. For each genuine tension, say which commitment has to go or be restated, and what the restatement costs me elsewhere. And do not hand me a menu of possible reconciliations to choose between: commit to one, and tell me which single revision buys the most consistency for the least damage.
