His claim: there IS a criterion, something roughly like "an explanation invokes a skyhook iff the design work it needs done can only be done by something that already has the feature being explained," i.e. it presupposes its own explanandum, and that's a real checkable property — and on his account plenty of defended explanations fail it (non-reductive physicalism about consciousness, appeals to top-down causation, maybe even "meaning is constituted by use" if nobody says how use does the constituting). I think his criterion either collapses into "explanation I dislike" or it's so strict that Dennett's own favorite cranes fail it too — natural selection, learning, cultural evolution, and especially the story about how words get meaning — because they all seem to be borrowing design-work from somewhere they haven't accounted for.
