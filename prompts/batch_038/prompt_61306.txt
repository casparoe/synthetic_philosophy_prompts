So here's my situation and I feel kind of dumb even writing it out, but I've been going around in circles on it for a month. I run a coffee cart on a downtown plaza — eleven years as a line cook, saved up, went out on my own last spring. There's one other cart on the plaza, run by a guy about my age who I've never really spoken to beyond "morning." Every day around 6am we each write our prices on a little chalk board, and neither of us can see the other's until we're both set up. About the numbers: if we both charge $4 for a medium, we split the office crowd roughly even and we both clear a good day. If one of us writes $3.50, that guy takes basically the whole rush and the other one stands around for four hours and goes home. Not exaggerating, I watched it happen twice in the first month when we were still feeling each other out.

Somewhere around week five I started doing a thing in my head that I've never heard anyone else describe, and I want to know whether it's legitimate or I've just talked myself into a superstition. I noticed this guy and I are pretty similar — same age, same size cart, same suppliers, both of us running the math constantly, both of us watching the other's board. And it hit me: whatever I decide to write at 6am, he's deciding at 6am, in the same kind of head, with the same information. Not that he copies me or that I can influence him — we genuinely can't, the boards go up at the same time and neither of us sees the other's choice. But that if I'm the kind of person who'd write $4, he's the kind of person who'd write $4, and if I'm the kind who'd write $3.50, he is too. So the $3.50 option isn't really "I get the whole rush," it's "we both get half the whole rush at a worse price," and the $4 option isn't "I lose the race," it's "we both win it." So I write $4, every day, and I don't even agonize over it anymore. Its worked — six-plus weeks of both of us at $4 and I'm making more than I did at the restaurant.

My wife thinks I've lost my mind. Her argument is that there's no wire between our heads, he could wake up tomorrow and write $3.50 and I'd never see it coming, and I'm betting real money on what is basically a horoscope. Then last week two things landed on top of that. A woman who runs the pretzel stand two blocks over told my wife that this guy said to her — joking, maybe not joking — that he thinks I'm "the soft type." And separately, plaza management is adding a third cart license next spring, with a rumor that they'll only renew two of the three permits at the end of the year based on sales. So what I want to know: is my 6am reasoning actually sound, or is my wife right that it's magic? Does hearing he thinks I'm soft change anything, given he still hasn't undercut me once and I still don't know how he reasons? And does the whole way of thinking survive a third cart, where I'd be assuming two strangers think like me instead of one? For what it's worth I know the obvious move is to walk over and just agree on $4 with him, but a guy at a small-business workshop I went to said two competitors verbally settling on prices is flatly illegal, so that's off the table — and I'm not asking for advice about permits, suppliers, or marketing either. Please answer with exactly five numbered points, each one or two sentences, and skip any introduction or wrap-up at the end.
