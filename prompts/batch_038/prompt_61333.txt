write the joint statement the two of them could both put their names to after a real adversarial collaboration. I want the claims about the texts and the underlying argument that both can sign; the residual disagreement phrased so that each camp would accept the wording of its own side; the cruxes — the sub-questions where settling one would actually move somebody — and for each crux the consideration both agree bears on it plus each side's prediction written down in advance; and a flag on whatever part of the dispute you think is merely verbal, saying which part and what the neutral vocabulary would be.
