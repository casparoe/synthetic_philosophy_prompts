Observation gives sequence, never the tie that supposedly binds it. The tie is God's habit — He customarily does it this way, and He could do otherwise without any contradiction, which is what makes miracles possible. I get that this is often called occasionalism, and that he thinks we still have plenty of knowledge of nature because God's custom doesn't change on us.
