The case. A lab I work with runs a warehouse robot I've started calling the Tenant. Details matter here, so use them. Its low-level motor skills were hand-written by engineers; there is no reward signal anywhere inside the machine, nothing has ever been reinforced or extinguished in there, not once. Its goals are typed in by a human at the start of each shift. It plans by writing candidate action sequences into a text scratchpad and criticizing them there, and it will not move an arm until the scratchpad settles. Every skill it has came from reading other people's artifacts — manuals, wiring diagrams, shift handover notes. It has never invented a tool or a symbol of its own; the entire symbolic environment it lives in was built by other minds, for other minds' purposes, and it moved in after the fact. And the scratchpad is wiped at end of shift. Ontogenetically its a mayfly with a library.

Here's my opening move. Your tower is nested, and you say the nesting is the whole point — a Gregorian creature is also Popperian, Skinnerian and Darwinian underneath, because otherwise there's no machinery for the scaffolding to scaffold. The Tenant has the top two floors and nothing under them. No Darwinian base: nothing about it was selected, it was specified. No Skinnerian floor: the consequences of its failures fell on the lab, which kept or lost its funding, not on the robot, which felt nothing tighten or loosen.
