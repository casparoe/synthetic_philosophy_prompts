my brother and I keep having the same argument and I can't settle it. he wants to copy Australia — voting becomes compulsory, you get fined if you dont turn up, and while we're at it drop the voting age to 16, since they're the ones who'll have to live with the results longest. his big argument is that the people forced to vote are exactly the ones who don't pay attention, so they'll basically pick at random, and all that random noise washes out the partisans and drags every election toward the sensible middle. compulsory voting = automatic moderating effect, and the 16 year olds are even less informed so even more of the same noise.

something about this bugs me but I cant quite say what. are apathetic voters actually like a coin flip, or do they lean somewhere specific? and does adding a pile of uninformed votes move an election toward the center, or just make the result less predictable? also — if a big chunk of the electorate only voted because of a fine, does the winner still get a mandate, or is the whole result just an artifact of the rule? tell me where he's wrong, or tell me I'm the one who's wrong.
