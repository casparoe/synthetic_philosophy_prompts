My opening: your view collapses into something boring. If the foil has to be supplied before there's an answer, the question isn't ill-posed, just incomplete -- like 'why did the temperature drop?' with no place or time attached. We handle those by plugging in the obvious contextual fill-in and flagging that we did. So: why not France, why not fifty years later, why nowhere else in Eurasia -- each gets its own answer, and the Needham question turns out to be a family of questions wearing a trenchcoat.
