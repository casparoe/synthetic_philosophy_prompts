Assume the role of a philosopher of decision theory who also understands how regulatory agencies actually operate. Think the problem through privately first — enumerate the candidate reconstructions and what would discriminate between them — but submit only the finished diagnosis, not your deliberation.

I am drafting the background chapter of a dissertation on bounded rationality and I keep returning to a practice that seems to describe itself one way and behave another. In radiological protection the operative requirement is ALARA: exposures below the dose limit must be reduced as far as is reasonably achievable, with economic and social factors taken into account. In application, the reasonably is pinned down by an explicit exchange rate — committees assign a monetary value to a unit of collective dose and stop investing once the marginal cost of averting another unit exceeds it — and that figure varies considerably between countries and has drifted over time without any derivation experts agree on. Meanwhile, under the same federal scheme, a color additive shown to induce cancer in animals or humans is barred outright, with no weighing of cost against benefit at any dose level.

What theory of practical rationality does ALARA presuppose? Specifically: is it a maximizing rule with a shadow price attached to risk, so that the language of sufficiency is a misnomer for constrained optimization; or a genuine aspiration-level rule, on which search terminates at what is administratively good enough and no complete ordering over outcomes is ever consulted; or something structured and plural instead — a lexicographic limit, a prior gate of justification, and optimization only inside that box? Distinguish what the practice strictly requires from what it merely fits comfortably with, and say whether its self-description counts as evidence about the underlying commitments or is only vocabulary. Then tell me what the coexistence with the absolute bar on carcinogenic dyes implies: a contradiction within one legal order about whether lives and money are commensurable, or a defensible division of labor. Finally, state what would have to change in the practice itself — documentation, thresholds, decision records — were the presupposed view replaced by its main rival.

Answer as exactly five numbered points, one or two sentences each, with no preamble and no closing summary. Close by saying where your reconstruction is most vulnerable: which of the three readings you would abandon first under pressure, and what evidence or argument would change your verdict.
