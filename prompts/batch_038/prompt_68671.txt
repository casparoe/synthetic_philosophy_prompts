ok, I've been going in circles on the zombie argument for years (longtime LW/rationalist-blog reader, you know the type) and I finally wrote my actual commitments down so someone can tell me whether they're a coherent package. Here they are:

1. Conceivability is a real guide to metaphysical possibility, but only *ideal* conceivability counts — the verdict of a reasoner with all the relevant a priori connections in view. Ordinary human gut-modalizing has zero evidential weight, positive or negative.
2. Between the two flavors, I trust positive conceivability (actually being able to run the scenario through in detail, from the inside) way more than negative conceivability (just not spotting a contradiction).
3. I'm a type-B materialist: a posteriori necessities are real, water is necessarily H2O, and the phenomenal concepts strategy is enough to explain why the explanatory gap feels unbridgeable. I don't buy the two-dimensionalist machinery with primary and secondary intensions.
4. Zombies are conceivable. I've spent a lot of hours on it and I can't find a contradiction anywhere in the description — same physics, same functional organization, lights off inside.
5. So (4) doesn't get you dualism, given (1). But it also means physicalism isn't established. Stalemate, and I'm fine living with a stalemate.
6. Evolutionary debunking does real damage to moral realism, since our moral intuitions were shaped for coordination rather than truth. Modal intuitions get a free pass tho, because they're just general-purpose reasoning applied to a new topic, not a dedicated evolved module.
7. Whatever theory of consciousness I land on has to make the truth knowable in principle by creatures like us. No permanent mystery.

Audit it: which of these actually pull against each other, worst first, and for each say what has to give and what it costs. At least one of these has to look like a problem but isnt, so tell me which one that is too. Exactly five numbered items, a sentence or two each, no intro and no wrap-up. American English please.
