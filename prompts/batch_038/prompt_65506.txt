Before I read another defence of a particular start date for the Anthropocene, I want the specification rather than the candidates. Suppose the purpose is a single boundary that could do both jobs at once: acceptable to the International Commission on Stratigraphy as the formal base of an epoch, and usable by a historian as the periodising claim of a book about humanity becoming a geological force. Note that the Anthropocene Working Group's proposal — the radionuclide horizon at a Canadian lake — was rejected by the Subcommission on Quaternary Stratigraphy in March 2024; part of what I want is to know which desideratum did that.

Write the list of requirements such a proposal must meet, each with the consideration that makes it a requirement rather than a taste. Include the cases it has to handle: a marker that is global but not synchronous; one that is synchronous but recorded in a single type locality; a boundary that is a process rather than an event; a marker that records the *absence* of human activity rather than its arrival. Include the distinctions it must not collapse: a chronostratigraphic unit versus a cultural concept; the onset of influence versus the onset of detectability; fixing a boundary by an effect versus explaining it by a cause. Include what it must be consistent with: ice-core and radionuclide archives, the post-1492 demographic collapse in the Americas, and the rules the stratigraphic bodies actually apply.

Then separate the non-negotiables from the parochial commitments of particular camps — the geologists' insistence on global synchronicity, the historians' demand that the date carry an attribution of responsibility, the movement's need for something that can be pointed at. I want to contribute one requirement of my own and have you stress-test it: the boundary must have been observable from the inside, by people living through it, rather than being a signal legible only to instruments that did not yet exist. Does it survive, and what does it exclude?

My suspicion is that attribution and the discipline's own method are jointly unsatisfiable — that a body with authority over rock has no authority over meaning — and I would like that demonstrated rather than asserted, together with what a more modest purpose would let through. Finish by identifying the single requirement that eliminates the most candidates, and score the mid-Holocene, 1610, around 1800, and the 1950s against the list in a short table. Please write the whole answer in British English.
