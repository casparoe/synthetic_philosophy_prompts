Here is my opening. Last year we paid a tutoring outfit on the strength of the number of students who passed their end-of-year math test. The number was excellent, and by spring they were enrolling only students already close to passing and spending their hours on the narrow band of item types the test favored. We learned this only because a tutor quit and told us. So here is my worry: we never chose a metric that we could see being gamed at the time. Every one looked sound until the gaming had already ruined it.
