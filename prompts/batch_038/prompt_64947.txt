I am taking a seminar on Victorian liberalism and empire this term, and for our next session each of us is expected to state and then defend a position on Mill's writings about India. Before I present, I would like an audit of whether the package I have settled on is jointly tenable. My commitments are these:

1. Coercion over an individual is justified only to prevent harm to others, and liberty matters substantially because it is self-chosen plans of life that develop character and judgment.
2. Mill's non-intervention thesis: free institutions conferred on a people from outside, or a liberty won for them by a foreign power, do not take root; genuine improvement has to be generated by a people's own efforts and struggles.
3. His treatment of dependencies populated by unlike peoples in the chapter of *Considerations on Representative Government* on the government of dependencies by a free state: such a dependency is best ruled through a semi-autonomous body of professional administrators with continuity and local knowledge, deliberately insulated both from the governed population and from the metropolitan electorate.
4. The educative argument for representative government: participation is not merely instrumental to good outcomes but is what forms active, public-spirited citizens, and an assembly's proper function is to watch and control the executive rather than administer.
5. Mill's own account of China: a society in which an elite corps monopolizes learning and keeps the population passive and leveled-down becomes stationary, however able that elite is.
6. The verdict that Mill was right in 1858 to resist transferring the government of India from the Company to the Crown, since a Secretary of State answerable to Parliament would govern India by whim, patronage, and metropolitan opinion.

Please tell me which pairs genuinely conflict and which merely look as though they do, ranking the real conflicts by severity rather than treating them as equally bad. For each, identify what would have to be abandoned or narrowed and what that revision would cost me elsewhere in the list, and then tell me which single change would clean up the most. I have no idea in advance where the weak link is. Please write your answer in American English.
