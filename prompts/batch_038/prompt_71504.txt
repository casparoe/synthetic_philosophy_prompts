- the regularities of chemistry coming out of quantum mechanics: no, chemistry reduces clean
- a protein folding up from its sequence: no — brute-force computable in principle, only hard in practice
- water freezing: no
- a flock of starlings wheeling around in unison: no — three alignment rules on a laptop and you've got it
- superconductivity: yes, genuinely
- division of labour in an ant colony: yes
- a bank run: yes
- pain, consciousness generally: yes, and "the only strong case there is"
