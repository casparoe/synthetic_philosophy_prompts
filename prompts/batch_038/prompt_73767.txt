Working through Berkeley on my own with a study guide app, and the entry on the master argument reads about like this:

"Berkeley's master argument, in the Principles (§§22–23), goes like this. He challenges you: try to picture a tree standing in a forest with nobody around to see it. And what can't be conceived is impossible, therefore nothing can exist unconceived, i.e. matter is a contradiction in terms. Berkeley thought this was decisive and named it himself, which is why every textbook calls it the master argument. He then needs God as a backup perceiver: without God the forest would blink out of existence whenever nobody was looking."
