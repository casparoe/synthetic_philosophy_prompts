So my building got bought last year by one of those big property management companies and starting on the 1st they're rolling out what they call the Verified Resident program. Here's the deal: you connect your bank feed, let them run a continuing background and credit check, carry a fob that logs every time you come and go, and there's new lobby cameras that apparently match faces. In exchange your rent drops about $120 a month, no security deposit, package room access, your guests come in without having to get buzzed through by staff. If you sit it out you pay full rent plus a bigger deposit and your visitors have to be signed in at the desk every single time.

Two things make it more than a landlord thing. The company says they'll turn over entry logs to police if asked, no notice to you, and the city gives them a break for being in some "safer buildings" program. And the woman in 4B, who has a restraining order against her ex, stood up at the meeting and said she's the reason it should exist — the logs are the first thing that's made her feel safe in years, and she asked people who were planning to opt out to think about that.

I want to say no on principle. But my neighbor who does compliance for a living told me im being an idiot, that the info is already out there anyway, that the discount is just me getting paid for it, and that if I opt out the building will treat me as a risk regardless, so what exactly have I protected. And I noticed something in myself: I already caught myself thinking that if I say no, people will assume I've got something to hide.

Meanwhile my brother's in Munich and he went ballistic, said something like this gets shut down there before it launches, that the whole idea over there is that no one should ever be able to assemble a full picture of a person even if every single piece of it is accurate. And my old college roommate is back in Hangzhou and thinks I'm being dramatic — he's in three of these systems, he says strangers trust each other more than they used to, and the version everyone in the west imagines isnt the version that exists.

Three people I like, three totally different answers, and I have to submit the form by the 15th.

Help me think it through. Is there a real cost to me signing up beyond the creeped-out feeling, or am I just squeamish about a decent deal? And the part I cant get past: if the thing only works when basically everyone's in, and my neighbor in 4B is genuinely safer because of it, am I just letting everybody else carry the cost so I can feel pure? Or is that precisely the pressure im supposed to be suspicious of? Also — does the German no-complete-picture idea actually hold together, or does it fall apart the second you admit everything being collected is true and I'm not a criminal? Tell me what to do and why, and tell me if im asking the wrong question.
