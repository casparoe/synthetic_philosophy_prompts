I'm circulating a short statement of my views for a workshop on gradations of moral status, and I want a consistency audit before I send it out. Here is the package as I currently hold it:

1. Consciousness comes in degrees. The moral weight of a creature's experiences scales with how sentient it is, and there is no sharp line separating the sentient from the insentient.
2. I am not a panpsychist. There is nothing it is like to be a thermostat or a photodiode, and consciousness is not a fundamental property of matter.
3. Moral status attaches to the capacity for welfare — to whether things can go better or worse for the creature — rather than to self-awareness, language, or any other cognitively sophisticated capacity.
4. I am a hedonist about animal welfare: what makes a creature's life go better or worse is the balance of positive against negative experience.
5. Painlessly killing an insect does not harm it. What matters morally is the suffering caused to creatures, not the futures they are deprived of.
6. Moral weight aggregates across individuals: the weight of a group is roughly the number of individuals times the weight of each, so a sufficiently large population of barely sentient creatures can outweigh a much smaller population of highly sentient ones.
7. My credence that insects are sentient at all is perhaps twenty to forty percent, but I hold that the weight of a creature's experiences is fixed by what it is actually like to be that creature, not by what we believe about it.

Tell me which of these genuinely pull against each other and which only look like they do. For each genuine tension, say how severe it is, which commitment has to go or be disambiguated, what the cheapest repair costs, and whether that repair leaves me holding conclusions I have not noticed I am committed to. Then identify the single revision that would clean up the most. I would rather have a decisive verdict I can argue with than a balanced survey: commit to a ranking of the tensions and defend it, rather than listing everything as equally problematic. No bibliography and no tour of who holds what — I will supply the citations myself.
