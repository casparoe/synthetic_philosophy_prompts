Just finished a three hour argument with a friend about multiple realizability and I genuinely can't tell what were disagreeing about anymore. His move: pain can't be identical to any brain state, because an octopus, a bird, and some future silicon thing could all be in pain while sharing no neural machinery. My move: fine, but watch what you have to do to keep that alive. Describe the octopus realizer and the human realizer precisely enough that they actually explain the wincing and the damage-avoidance, and they stop overlapping at all — you're left sharing nothing but the word. So he retreats upward: the realizer is just "whatever internal state registers damage and organizes avoidance." But now a thermostat qualifies, an immune response qualifies, and the property stops picking anything out. Every turn in the argument just slid the description up or down a notch, and both ends fail — too fine and the kind shatters into unrelated pieces, too coarse and it applies to everything in the universe. Is there a name for that particular squeeze, the fact that whether something counts as multiply realizable depends entirely on how you type the realizers? I know its neighbors: the autonomy-of-psychology debate, the charge that pain is just a giant disjunction of unrelated states, objections about whether the differences between realizers are the relevant ones. But none of those seem to be about the choice of typing grain itself. So beyond the label — what exactly is this phenomenon, what isn't it, and what would NOT count as an instance of it?
