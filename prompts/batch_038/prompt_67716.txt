Setup: Skinner is given one afternoon to get up to speed on mechanistic interpretability as it actually stands now — sparse autoencoders pulling out directions that light up for specific concepts, features packed into superposition, and crucially, activating one of those directions changing what the model then says. He died in 1990 so none of it existed for him, but assume he understands the technical detail; he was clever enought for that.

The question i want isn't the boring "would he have liked AI" one. It's narrower: would Skinner have been a realist or an anti-realist about the representations these methods claim to uncover? My own hunch — which i want you to attack or defend, not politely balance — is that he lands with the anti-realists, but for a reason neither camp in that debate ever states: on his view a feature is a redescription of the model's reinforcement history rather than a cause of its behaviour, so the real explanation sits in the training data and the objective function, not in the wieghts.

Here's where the hunch looks in trouble, and it's the bit i most want pressed on. The interpretability people don't only correlate, they intervene — push the feature up and the output moves. Skinner spent his career saying that appeals to inner states are explanatory fictions precisely because they buy you no causal leverage beyond what the environment and the history of reinforcement already give you. So does the intervention evidence force him across to the realists? Or does he have a live reply, something along the lines of: of course you can change behaviour by changing the organism, that was never in dispute, the question is whether the inner description explains anything, and it still only summarises the contingencies that built it? I want you to say which of those two you actually think wins, and why.

One extra wrinkle to fold in: what does he do with the cases the realists like best, where a feature responds to something the model was never explicitly reinforced for and looks for all the world like exactly the kind of inner state he refused to count?
