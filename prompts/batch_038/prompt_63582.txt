so i'm TA-ing a grad seminar on machine ethics and next week is the responsibility-gap session — the one where everyone has read the classics and comes in already having picked a side. my professor asked me to open the meeting with something short instead of another summary, and i'd like it to be a sestina, because i think the form does the argument better than i can in prose: the six end-words cycling through the same slots, never settling anywhere, each one handing off to the next.

the constraint i want: the six teleutons should be the six candidate bearers — the programmer, the manufacturer, the commander, the operator, the machine, and *nobody*. full 39 lines, six sestets plus the three-line envoi, and please actually keep the standard permutation pattern rather than just repeating the words in whatever order sounds good. the whole point is that "nobody" keeps landing in the position one of the real people just vacated, so the rotation itself is what shows the handoff never terminates. if you can't hold the exact scheme, do the closest you can and tell me in the note where it breaks.

content-wise, here's what i'd want a person who works on this to recognize rather than just hear buzzwords:

- at least one sestet should make the gap specifically about culpability — backward-looking blame — and make it turn on the fact that the system's behavior wasn't foreseeable or controllable at deployment because it learned afterward. the programmer didn't write the decision that killed anyone; the commander didn't order it. not "they were negligent," but "there's no act description that fits."
- another sestet should register that the absence of a blameworthy agent isn't the absence of a debt. somebody still owes the family an answer and a check. the poem should let blame and accountability come apart without pretending the second one fills the hole the first one left.
- one sestet on the machine as bearer, and i want this to feel like a category failure, not a technology shortfall — you can't punish something that can't care about being punished, so no amount of extra sophistication closes it. please no terminator imagery and no ghost-in-the-machine stuff.
- one on the many-hands problem: fifty small contributions, each of them below whatever threshold would make it culpable, and none of them adding up to a person who did it.

don't resolve it. i don't want the envoi to land on "therefore ban autonomous weapons" and i equally don't want it to shrug into "therefore nobody's fault, moving on." if the last line feels like it picks a side, that's a bug — the seminar is split and the poem shouldn't outvote either half of it.

then after the poem, give me like 6–10 lines of notes, and this part matters as much as the verse: flag where the form forced you into a claim that isn't true, and separate out what's actually settled in this literature from what's genuinely contested. specifically i want to know whether the "the gap is narrower than it looks once you stop conflating blame with liability/accountability" move that two of my sections keep making is close to consensus or is one contested line among several, and where the real disagreement sits. don't hand me a contested position as if everyone agrees on it, and don't be shy about saying "i don't know, this one's live."
