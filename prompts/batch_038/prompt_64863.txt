I'm on the governance committee of a county board (11 seats, 9 of them elected) and next month we vote on a pilot that would fill four seats by sortition for two years — names drawn at random from the voter roll, stratified by age, renter-vs-owner, and school attendance zone, with a $5,000 stipend and a rule letting the elected members send any measure back to the four once before it passes.

Snag: the invite letter got a 2.6% response rate, so the people who actually said yes are three retired homeowners and a school administrator — nothing like the strata we told the public we'd hit. One colleague wants to seat them anyway, since a re-draw pushes the pilot past the budget cycle and it'll quietly die. Another thinks the whole thing is illegitimate regardless, because nobody elected them and nobody can vote them out.

Draft the short memo I'll circulate before thursday. Concrete recommendation plus a timeline, and engage both objections on their merits rather than just making the case for sortition.
