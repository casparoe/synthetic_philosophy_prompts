Write the remarks so the real disagreement gets named sharply — including the fact that the two camps mean different things when they say "the truth wins" — without anyone being able to tell afterwards which side I'm on, even though I'm honestly with the pessimists and two people in the room have read my papers. I want one concrete toy case in there that both sides would accept as a fair instance of the problem, and please don't make it the usual kind where a debater has to defend a false claim about molecules or a date; invent something else. No closing uplift about how we'll get through this together, and no telling people to be generous with each other's arguments.
