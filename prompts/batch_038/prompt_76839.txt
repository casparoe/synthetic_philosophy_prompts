Two things I want you to actually wrestle with, because that's where the interest is.

(a) Does the appearance of rebellion, or other crime in the constitutional text break his own method? His whole 1860 move was that the 1787 document never uses the word slave, that the fugitive clause speaks of a person held to service or labor (which on his reading is language about labor obligations and doesn't establish property in a man), that the three-fifths and slave-trade clauses are worded so as to withhold any sanction of slavery, that the preamble's declared objects are the master key, and that where a clause admits of two readings the one favoring liberty governs.
