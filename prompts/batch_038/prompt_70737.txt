My draft uses Abu Ghraib as the running case and my prof basically told me I'm leaning on it way too casually, so I want to do it properly before I hand in a revision.

Facts I'll assume we both know, so you don't have to recap the scandal: late 2003 into early 2004, a tier of the prison where a handful of Army Reserve MPs were holding detainees, the photos that went public in spring 2004, the internal investigation, and the framing that came down from the top that it was a few bad apples rather than a broken system. What I want is a case analysis that runs both directions, not a summary of the debate.
