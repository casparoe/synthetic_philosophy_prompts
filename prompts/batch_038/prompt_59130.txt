ok so this is a slightly weird ask. my phil of mind seminar ends this week on epiphenomenalism and we have a stupid-but-beloved tradition where somebody reads a poem at the last session, and I drew the short straw. I want to do a ghazal, in the actual strict sense, not just a pile of couplets. So: five to seven couplets (odd number please), each one syntactically complete and able to stand alone, one rhyme — the qafia — sitting right before a repeated word or phrase at the end of every couplet (the radif), that rhyme showing up twice in the opening couplet, the same meter and roughly the same line length all the way through like the Persian original does, and my first name tucked into the last couplet the way a takhallus goes.

The content should be the paradox of phenomenal judgment, and I want the form doing the arguing rather than decorating it. Here's the bit I care about: if the experience doesn't cause the report, then the report comes entirely out of the brain and the feeling is along for the ride. So when I say this red looks like this, that exact sentence would have come out of my mouth in that exact wording even if there had been nothing it was like to be me. I want the radif to be the thing that keeps saying that, something in the register of and the words came anyway or it was always going to get said — pick your own or improve on mine, but it has to land the same way every single time, like a door closing.

Couplets I'd want in there: Huxley's steam whistle on the locomotive, doing none of the work; a twin of mine with nothing it is like to be him saying the same words in the same tone while we both watch the same sunset; and the coincidence itself — that my reports track my experience better than any instrument could, and yet nothing ever made them track. James's shadow that doesn't press on the walker's steps too, if you can fit it without crowding.

The closing couplet, with my name in it, is where it should get worse: the name is in the causal story and I am not. Don't resolve it, don't console anybody, no wry but maybe interactionism after all at the end. And please no commentary, no notes on the form, no headings or bolded labels or bullet points or tables afterwards — just the ghazal, couplet by couplet, the way you'd paste it into an email. im aware thats a lot of constraints but a loose poem would sound like a fortune cookie in that room, and I can tell when the meter slips.
