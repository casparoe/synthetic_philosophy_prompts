So I've been down a rabbit hole again about that lamp puzzle — the one where a lamp gets switched on for half a minute, off for a quarter, on for an eighth, and so on, so that after exactly one minute it's done infinitely many switchings and nobody can tell you whether it's on or off. I first ran into it years ago and I keep coming back to it, partly because I actually mess around with eletronics as a hobby, and to me half the fun is that the "this is impossible" part is also just a hardware problem — the contacts, the power draw, the heat.

I don't want an explainer though, I want a laugh. Write me a crowdfunding campaign page, kickstarter-style, in deadpan hardware-startup voice, for a company that is genuinely selling this lamp as a finished consumer product. Around 600-700 words. Include the usual furniture of one of these pages: a hype-y opening pitch, a spec sheet, a couple of reward tiers, an FAQ, and at least two backer updates or comments at the bottom.

The jokes have to come from getting the puzzle right, not from generic startup satire, so here's the bit that matters most: the spec sheet has to say something about the lamp's state at exactly the one-minute mark, and the whole page should hinge on that. I want the two answers people actually give to the puzzle in there — the "there's a genuine contradiction, so the product is impossible" side, and the "no contradiction, the recipe just never fixes the one-minute state, it's like asking for the value of a function at a point where it has no limit" side — but rendered entirely as corporate copy. Nobody in the piece says the word philosophy and nobody names a dead guy. The company should be firmly committed to the second answer and use it to weasel out of specifying a deliverable, because that's my real target: I want the satire aimed at how a startup can turn "your product has no defined final state" into a premium feature and then blame the customer for a category error when they ask what they're getting.

One other backer should point out that if you squint the whole thing is just pulse-width modulation at a 50% duty cycle, so the lamp has secretly been a dimmer this whole time; you decide whether that observation is the page's undoing or its best defense.

Two things I want you to commit to instead of hedging.
