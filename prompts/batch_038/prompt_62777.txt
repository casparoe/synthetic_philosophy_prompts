I've been chewing on something and I want it turned into a short story, maybe 1500-2000 words. The idea: two groups that don't disagree about what's good, only about how much weight each good gets. Because the weightings differ, they can trade — you get your way on one thing, I get mine on another, and we both end up better off by our own lights. Sounds fine, except the trading only works while we still disagree. If a hundred small deals slowly pull our weightings toward each other, the reason to deal dries up, and somebody ends up with an interest in keeping the gap open — making sure the other side stays really, sincerely different from us. I'd like the main character to be whoever has that job, in whatever setting you want (a treaty office, two AIs, a family firm, a monastery and the town beside it — its your call). Let them honestly believe they're holding the peace, and let the reader work out whether they are. And I want the story to get at what it feels like to have made a lot of trades you're individually glad about, then look up and not recognize the world they added up to. Keep it in scenes, not explained in dialogue.
