Since my mom died in the spring I've been going to her old Tuesday sitting group, mostly to keep her friends company, and I've noticed the guy who leads it — call him R, he's not a monk, just thirty years of leading that group — keeps making very specific calls about who deserves what. A few of them: the landlord who squeezed his tenants for decades and then died rich, healthy and adored by his kids — R says the account just isn't settled yet, it comes due later or lands on somebody downstream of him. The woman who ran a free food kitchen for forty years and got dementia at 58 — R said that wasn't punishment, that was an old debt finally clearing while she was still sturdy enough to carry it. A teenager who hit a cyclist at a blind junction with genuinely no negligence — R said he picks something up from it anyway, just far less than if he'd meant it. A businessman who paid for a school building purely to get his name on it — R said that counts, but the fruit is thin, like food cooked without salt. And when one street turned on a family and the whole street flooded the next year, R said that's no coincidence, it can pool.

Here's why I'm asking. For about six years in my twenties I was deliberately cruel to one person, and I enjoyed it at the time. She cut me off and I never got to apologise. For the last ten years I've done a lot of quiet repair — anonymous, toward strangers, none of it reaches her — and nothing bad has happened to me at all. I'm healthy, happy, and by every outward measure I got away with it.

What does R say if this comes up next Tuesday? I want the candidate rules that fit all his past calls equally well — I'm pretty sure there's at least three, and I don't think they collapse into one — what each predicts about my case, your best guess at which he's actually running and why, and one new case I could put to him that would force the rules apart so I'd know which it is.

Bottom line first: give me your predicted verdict on me in one sentence, then the reasoning. No preamble, and don't restate the question back to me.
