Is that a version of the Landauer point, with the cost of erased information exported to the environment, or a category error dressed as poignancy? Please tell me which scenes to put in front of the class, where the analogy genuinely illuminates the physics and its philosophy, where it turns on an equivocation between physical and psychological irreversibility, and what the film might show about who bears the cost of erasure that the demon-exorcism debate has not addressed.
