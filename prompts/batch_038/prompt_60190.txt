So I've been reading the anthropics stuff on and off for like twelve years — lesswrong, the ancient SSC comment threads, one sleeping beauty argument that went on so long I started questioning my life choices — and I finally tried to write down where I actually land instead of just having vibes. Someone at my meetup is going to try to break this next week and I'd rather get broken by you first.

Here's the list:

1. I'm an SSA person. When I think about my birth rank I treat myself as a random sample from all humans who will ever live, and I accept that this pushes my credence toward doom coming sooner rather than later. The plain "about 60 billion humans have lived, so there's a decent chance we're past the halfway mark" reasoning feels basically right to me, not like a shell game.

2. SIA, no. Mainly the presumptuous philosopher thing: I can't accept that a hypothesis gets a massive credence boost purely for positing a trillion times more observers.

3. Thirder in sleeping beauty, always have been, and I've never found the halfer position remotely compelling.

4. I think we're probably in a simulation — or at least, if you take the ancestor-simulation argument seriously your credence in "base reality" should be pretty low.

5. But when I actually do the doomsday calculation my reference class is "all humans who will ever live," full stop. I specifically don't want to inflate it with humans inside other simulations, or aliens, or posthuman uploads, because if the class can balloon like that the whole calculation turns to mush.

6. The doomsday argument doesn't tell you anything about the mechanism, so I think it sits perfectly happily next to my view that the risk that matters is misaligned AI rather than something intrinsic to being early.

7. Separately I'm pretty suspicious of anyone who claims the doomsday argument delivers a huge shift, because you can't have a proper uniform prior over N and the size of the update is totally prior-dependant. Which is part of why a star-faring future with trillions of people still feels very live to me.

So: which of these genuinely conflict, which pairs only look like they conflict, how bad is each real one, and where do I have to give something up? For the real conflicts I want you to say which item has to go or get weakened and what the reasoning costs me once it's gone. Flag it too if one of my *reasons* is doing illegitimate work even when the conclusion itself is fine — I suspect at least one of them is and I can't tell which. And rank them by severity instead of listing them all as equally bad. I honestly don't know where the body is buried, and I'm not going to tell you which one I'm most attached to, so don't try to guess my favorite and steer around it.
