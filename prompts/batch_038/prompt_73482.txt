You are an expert in both the philosophy of well-being and the conceptual foundations of AI alignment. Before answering, sketch the space of candidate cases briefly and rule out the obvious ones, explaining why each fails.

For the purposes of this exercise, adopt the following stipulative definitions. An agent *wireheads* just in case it intervenes on the process that generates its reward signal so that the signal ceases to be a reliable indicator of the state of affairs its designers wanted it to indicate. An agent engages in *reward tampering* just in case it modifies either its reward function or the inputs that function receives, in a way that makes the signal a worse indicator of that state. An agent's reward signal is *honest* at a time just in case, at that time, it is a faithful read-out of the designers' target state.

Now give me one fully worked example of a case in which the agent's reward signal is honest at every moment — nothing is intercepted, no function is rewritten, no sensor is fooled, no human evaluator is deceived or coerced — and yet anyone describing the case would call it wireheading. Specify the designers' target state, the reward function, the mechanism by which honesty is preserved throughout, and the precise step at which the agent's relation to its own reward changes. Then identify which of the three definitions is doing the wrong work in classifying the case, and propose the minimal repair: a fourth condition, a revised clause, or an argument that the case should be redescribed rather than reclassified.

Two constraints on the example. It must not turn on the agent's deceiving or manipulating an evaluator, and it must not be a case of the agent discovering an easier route to the same genuine achievement. Finally, close by stating what the case implies about why wireheading is supposed to be bad in the first place — whether the harm lies in what happens to the signal or in what happens to the agent — and note one respect in which your answer would differ if the designers' target state were itself a proxy rather than the thing they ultimately cared about.
