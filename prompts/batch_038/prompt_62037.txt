I rebuilt the Third Man regress for a reading group and then pushed it a step further than the text of the Parmenides does, and now it seems to prove far too much. Here is the chain.

(1) Whenever many things are F, there is one item — the F-ness — in virtue of which each of them is F.
(2) F-ness is itself F; if it were not, it could not serve as the standard against which the others are measured.
(3) F-ness is therefore one of the many things that are F, so by (1) there is a single item in virtue of which it and the original F things are all F.
(4) Nothing explains its own character, so that item must be a second F-ness rather than the first; (2) and (3) then apply to it in turn, and a third, a fourth, and so on follow without end.
(5) An account on which every explanation of a thing's being F demands a further unexplained explainer never terminates in anything that answers the question, and so explains nothing.
(6) Hence the theory of Forms does not explain why anything is F.
(7) Yet (1), (2), and the no-self-explanation clause in (4) are not peculiar to Plato: a rule that fixes which tokens fall under a term is itself a token requiring a rule for its own application, a criterion of identity for properties is itself a property, and a concept that explains why things are red is itself correctly described as a concept. Nominalist and psychologistic accounts meet these conditions just as realism does.
(8) So no account of any kind explains why many things fall under one predicate — and since some account must, the entire line of reasoning collapses.

I would rather abandon (2) than give up the principle that explaining sameness requires something shared, but I cannot tell whether (7) genuinely transplants the regress or merely borrows its plausibility. Diagnose where the argument goes wrong. Give me exactly five numbered items and nothing else — no introduction, no closing summary. Each item should be one or two sentences naming a distinct step that can be rejected, saying why that step looked unobjectionable where it appeared, and pricing what is lost by rejecting it; make the fifth item the exit you would actually take, and say whether on your view the regress is blocked or merely relocated. Write in American English.
