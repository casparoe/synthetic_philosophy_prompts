I'm putting together a handful of conversational scenarios for a little workshop on honesty, and I want ratings, not an essay. For each of the six below, give two numbers on a 0–10 scale: (a) how wrong the speaker acts, where 0 is completely fine and 10 is a serious wrong; and (b) how recoverable the truth is — could the other person find out later and recalibrate, or is the distortion basically permanent? Then rank the six by (a).

1. A colleague gives a rehearsal talk that's competent but flat. Five minutes before the real thing they ask how it was. You say "it landed really well" and steer into logistics.
2. A friend asks if you remembered their birthday. You didn't. You say you did and that the card must have got lost in the post.
3. An elderly patient's son catches the doctor in the corridor and asks, off the record, whether his mother is likely to die this year. The doctor says "probably not," because the mother has explicitly asked that the family not be tipped into crisis by a timeline.
4. At a dinner where you'll see everyone again next week, a neighbor asks whether you and your partner are trying for children. You invent a medical reason rather than say it's none of their business.
5. Your manager asks the whole team, in a meeting, whether anyone has concerns about the reorg. You have real ones. You stay silent.
6. A tourist asks the way to the museum. You're maybe 60% sure it's left at the next corner. You say "left at the next corner" instead of "I think left, but check with someone."

After the numbers: which of these would shift most if the exact same exchange happened in Osaka rather than Amsterdam? Please dont answer "high-context versus low-context culture" and stop there — say which variable actually changes, out of who absorbs the cost, whether the listener can plausibly pretend they werent told, whether the lie is discoverable afterwards, and whether there's a third party the speaker is shielding. Give me roughly how many points each affected scenario moves.

Last thing: pick the pair that a Kantian and a rule-utilitarian would rank most differently, and say which one you'd side with.
