I'm in 11th grade and writing a paper about who was actually responsible for WWI starting, and I've run into something I can't find a word for and its genuinely driving me nuts.

So the Versailles treaty has that clause where Germany basically has to accept responsibility for the war, and my teacher said a German historian in the early 60s argued Germany had been angling for a big war for years before that, and there was an enormous fight about it that never fully ended. Cool, fine. But when I read people arguing about it now, I keep noticing they aren't disagreeing about the same question, and what decides who "wins" is which level of description the argument happens to be sitting at.

Like, someone says Germany's to blame because it pushed Austria into going after Serbia and then didn't pull it back, and they point to that unconditional-support telegram Berlin sent Vienna in early July 1914. And the reply usually isn't "no it didn't," the reply is "but by then the alliance system and the arms race meant a war was coming anyway, so July was just the fuse." Then if you say okay, but somebody still lit the fuse, they go up another level: imperialism, the whole 19th century, everyone's hands are dirty, nobody in particular. And it slides the other way too — someone blames the system, and the answer drops straight back down to one afternoon in Sarajevo.

What I can't name is this: the level where the cause gets located isn't pinned down by the evidence, it moves, and wherever it lands decides whether anybody is blameworthy or whether the war sort of just "happened." Same event gets called the cause by one person and merely a background condition or a trigger by another, and honestly both sound reasonable.

Is there a real term for this? I don't just mean moving the goalposts — the goalposts here are an entire scale of description, and sometimes zooming out is the right move, I just can't tell when. I want the closest named ideas, how what I'm describing differs from each one, and if nothing actually fits, what you'd call it and where you'd draw the line between the legitimate version and the dodge. Please don't just hand me a word — I need the edges of the thing, cause my whole argument depends on it.
