I want the bones of the argument visible in the dialogue: both of them have an equally good claim about which distant events are already real, and all the force comes from sliding between "real for me now" and "real, full stop." And please get the direction right: whether a given far-off event lands in your now or in your past depends on whether it lies ahead of or behind your direction of motion.
