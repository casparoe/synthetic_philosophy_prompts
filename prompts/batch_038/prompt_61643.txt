I am drafting a novel about three generations of a family of clockmakers, and one of its set pieces — a dinner-table argument — currently fails. The father has spent forty years restoring eighteenth-century longcase movements and remains a churchgoing man. His elder daughter is an evolutionary developmental biologist; his son trains neural networks. At the height of the exchange the daughter says that if there is a watchmaker in nature, it is a blind one, and the father replies, in my draft unconvincingly, that nothing blind could ever produce the sort of thing she is pointing at. A reader whose judgment I trust has told me the father is attacking a claim his daughter never made, and that the scene collapses because I have let her phrase do more work than it can bear.

Before I rewrite, I want to know what that phrase can actually be made to assert. I know the ancestry — Paley's watch discovered on the heath, and Dawkins's inversion of it — but I care about the menu, not the history. Please lay out the distinct theses the slogan might carry, distinguishing among them along the axes that seem to matter: whether what is said to be blind is the selecting process, the explanation we offer of it, or the entire causal ancestry of a trait; whether the claim is that no foresight was involved anywhere in that ancestry, or only that the mechanism itself holds no representation of the outcomes it produces; whether the phrase is meant to deny that an organ can genuinely be for something, or to keep such talk as literally true while grounding it in selection history rather than in anyone's intention. I would also like you to note where the blindness runs out inside otherwise standard examples — for instance, whether the choosing done by an animal with preferences and an eye for appearance still counts as blind.

For each reading, tell me whether it is a platitude, a substantive and defensible claim, a live open question, or simply false; and tell me which of them a theist, and which a practicing engineer who designs things on purpose, could accept without embarrassment. Then say which readings my father could attack with a straight face, and what, if anything, survives of his objection when the same language is turned on his son's models, where competence without comprehension describes an artifact we built deliberately and to a specification.
