1. Ubiquity — charge is wherever there's matter, so experience is wherever there's matter, so basically everything physical is a moral patient.
2. Sign — charge comes in positive and negative, experience comes in pleasure and pain, so the valence distribution of the universe is as objective a fact as its charge distribution, and in principle as measurable.
3. Conservation — charge is conserved and additive in a closed system; so, she says, is suffering. Total suffering is a fixed budget you can shuffle around but never reduce, and anything that adds structured matter (space colonization, giant compute clusters, whatever) adds to it. Her conclusion is a kind of cosmological fatalism: the ethical move is to want less stuff, and "reducing suffering" is as confused as trying to lower the net charge of a sealed box.
