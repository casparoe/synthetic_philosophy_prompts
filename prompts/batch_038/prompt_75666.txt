They've finally agreed to sit down for an adversarial collaboration and, as the only person who reads both of their emails, I got volunteered to write the document. Can you draft it for me?

What I need: a numbered list of claims both sides could actually sign — not bland stuff like both want less suffering, but substantive claims about what each regime legally does, what the harms consist of, and what the evidence can and can't currently carry. Then the cruxes — the sub-questions where an answer would actually move someone — and for each one, the observation or study design both would accept as bearing on it, with each side's prediction written down beforehand like a preregistration, not after the results. And flag anywhere you think they're just fighting over words; I suspect trafficking and choice are doing double duty but tell me where else.
