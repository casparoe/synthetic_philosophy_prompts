I've spent a year of evenings trying to turn Hofstadter's account of creativity — the one in *Metamagical Themas*, where variation amounts to twiddling the knobs of an underlying structure while something more central stays fixed — into something with predictive content, and I now have twelve months of self-funded runway, no lab and no students, before I go back to contracting. My own contribution so far is what I call knob depth: a knob is deep to the extent that its setting constrains the admissible settings of the other knobs, so deep variation means twiddling near the root of that constraint graph, and most of what gets praised as creativity, in human art and in generative models alike, looks like twiddling at the leaves.

Which open questions in this neighborhood most deserve that year? I want a ranked agenda of no more than four, defended on upstream-ness (how many further questions turn on the answer), tractability for one person without empirical infrastructure, and whether a negative result would still be worth having — and for each, what artifact I could finish that would be legible outside my own head: a taxonomy, a formal sketch, a worked analysis of a corpus. Say also which adjacent questions you'd deprioritize as unresolvable or as leaving everything downstream unchanged, and what I might turn up in the first three months that should reorder the list.
