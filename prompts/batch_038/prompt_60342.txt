In the persistence seminar I audit, the instructor repeatedly reaches for what he calls the highway analogy. A persisting object stands to time exactly as a road stands to the counties it crosses. No single county contains the whole road, yet nothing is missing from the road in any county that it lacks elsewhere; each county holds a spatial stretch, and the road is nothing over and above those stretches plus the continuity of paving between them. Likewise, on his telling, no single moment contains the whole of you; each moment holds a temporal stretch, and you are nothing over and above those stretches plus whatever connects them.

He uses the analogy to license three conclusions. First, that the puzzles of coincidence — the statue and the clay, the ship whose planks are replaced one by one — are merely verbal, because where a road's boundary falls within a county is fixed by the highway authority's conventions rather than by the paving itself, and the same goes for an object's boundaries at a time. Second, that talk of endurance is at best a useful fiction: he claims nothing in the analogy corresponds to an object's being wholly present anywhere, so the endurantist is demanding a feature the target simply lacks. Third, that we should be equally relaxed about personal identity: what matters is the connectedness of the stretches, and identity is merely the limiting case of it.

I raised one worry, which he dismissed. The stretches of a road are all there together; you can drive the whole route or photograph it at once, and any part of it can act on any other. Nothing seems to permit the analogous thing with your temporal stretches. His reply was that this is precisely what a block universe supplies, and that my discomfort is a prejudice in favor of the present.

Could you audit the analogy? I would like an explicit table of correspondences — what plays the road, the counties, the paving, the map, and the highway authority — followed by the places where the mapping fails. Please distinguish the failures that genuinely undercut each of the three conclusions from those that are merely picturesque and cost him nothing, identify what features of the source the analogy quietly imports into the target, and state which of the three conclusions, if any, survives the audit intact. If one does not, say whether a different analogy would carry it, and what that analogy would have to supply that this one fails to.
