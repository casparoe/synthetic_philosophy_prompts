The conclusion being licensed is that algorithmic feeds aren't a new problem, just the old one with better engineering, so there's nothing special to regulate and the only real fix is inward -- the single individual taking responsibility for what they read -- rather than structural.

Can you audit that analogy? I want the correspondence table first: what in the feed plays the newspaper, the editor, the subscriber, the anonymous crowd, the leveler. Does the nothing-new-to-regulate conclusion survive once the disanalogies are on the table, or was the analogy carrying the whole weight?
