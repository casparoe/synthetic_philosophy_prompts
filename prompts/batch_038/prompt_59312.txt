Constraints: realistic setting (a hospital, a shipwreck, a collapsed building — no trolleys, no exotic technology); the scenario itself a single paragraph; every party a stranger to the agent; and no desert, no responsibility for one's own plight, no special relationships, and no appeal to the intensity of anyone's desire to live, so the divergence cannot be smuggled in through a side consideration.
