I don't want to just tell her that's a platitude doing no work — I want to say precisely why. Then seperate out the ones that would really license a move from a fact about the archive to a conclusion about there being no true account of the past, and tell me whether any of those readings is the scepticism you find in Carr or in White, or something weaker entirely.
