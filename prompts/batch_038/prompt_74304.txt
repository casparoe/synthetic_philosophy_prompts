Write me a parody of one of those independent third-party assurance reports — the deadpan Big Four register, "reasonable assurance," "we do not express an opinion on..." — issued for a smart-home company whose entire product line has been certified as having small but nonzero moral status. Maybe 700 words, broken into the usual sections: basis of opinion, methodology and materiality thresholds, scope exclusions, and a long aggregation footnote.

The jokes should come from getting gradualism right rather than from thermostats being funny. Things I want in there: the sorites problem showing up as a "boundary issue" between firmware releases, the auditor applying a precautionary default and refusing to rule out that the doorbell camera feels something, and an aggregation footnote where 900 million devices at 0.0002 moral-status units get compared against one golden retriever and the arithmetic goes horribly. Someone should also raise the combination/exclusion worry — if only the single most integrated subset counts as a subject, the audit cant say whether the patient is the thermostat, the hub, or the mesh, and that unresolved item is buried in the "matters not adjusted" list. Maybe a dissenting paragraph from a stakeholder who thinks insect welfare dwarfs all of it.

Stay deadpan, no winking, no citations or named philosophers, and keep it legible to someone whos never heard of phi.
