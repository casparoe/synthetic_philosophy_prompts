Deadpan throughout: it should read like a real bookmaker's T&Cs, with a version number, an effective date, numbered clauses, a complaints procedure, an ombudsman sidebar, all the genre furniture. Nobody in the document knows they're funny.

Someone who knows the literature should be able to put a finger on the exact clause where the house view gives out.
