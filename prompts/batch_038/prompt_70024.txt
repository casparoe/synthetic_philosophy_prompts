the reason i want a fake one is that for the last few months i've been crashing my partner's online reading group — a bunch of grad students, very patient with me — and last week we did al-Ghazālī on causation, the discussion in the Incoherence of the Philosophers where he argues the link between what we call a cause and what we call an effect isn't necessary at all. observation only ever shows you the second thing happening *at* the first, never that it happens *by* it; the regularity is just God's custom, which is why it can be interrupted, which is what leaves room for miracles and also for fire that declines to burn. i sat there and realized my whole job is a monument to exactly the inference he's attacking, and i haven't been able to unsee it since.

so: write me a parody blameless postmortem, around 800 words, with the real section headers, written by a platform team that has sincerely and internally accepted the occasionalist position. the humor has to come from getting both the genre and the metaphysics right, not from the theology being silly — al-Ghazālī should read as someone who noticed something genuinely hard about causation, the whole thing should stay deadpan, and it should still be funny to an SRE who's never read any kalām. things i'd love to see handled: the five whys derailing at step two because there's no "because" left to give; "contributing factors" getting renamed to cover events that were merely created alongside each other; the blamelessness turning metaphysically literal, since if no created agent does anything and human action is only acquired, what could an action item with an owner even mean; the monitoring stack reframed as tracking the custom rather than our code, so the SLO is really a standing bet that the regularity continues; and a "where we got lucky" section that has to be rewritten because luck isn't a category anyone can use anymore.
