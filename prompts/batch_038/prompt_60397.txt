He reaches for it about anything: the funeral she couldn't see, what the coast looks like, the color of her own eyes in a mirror. I want that line to be load-bearing instead of mush, so I need to know exactly what it could be claiming.

Act like a philosopher of mind who actually works on the knowledge argument and Mary-the-color-scientist stuff, and teach me the distinctions rather than the history.
