Been reading the old value-theory stuff on the rationalist blogs for like fifteen years now — wireheading, experience machines, fun theory, all of it — and lately I've been poking at the actual philosophy of pleasure, specifically the heterogeneity problem for hedonism. Which is: hedonism about well-being says pleasure is the only thing that's good for you in itself, but pleasures seem to have nothing much in common. The pleasure of a hot bath after being cold, the pleasure of finally getting a proof to go through, of sex, of a good meal, of a joke landing, of being praised, of relief — these feel nothing alike. Sidgwick-type hedonists insist there's still one thing there, a common pleasant feel that makes them all pleasures and all good, and that the differences are differences in what carries it. Mill's higher/lower pleasures business looks like an admission that quality is doing extra non-hedonic work. Then there's the family-resemblance camp, which basically says pleasure is a grab bag and the theory is picking out a disjunction, which is why it feels unfalsifiable. And then the attitudinal move (Feldman and people after him) which relocates pleasure from a sensation to a pro-attitude toward a state of affairs — arguably a rescue, arguably changing the subject.

I work on reward models for a living, so my native vocabulary is: scalar reward signals, a reward head trained on top of a high-dimensional learned representation, preference labels, reward shaping, reward hacking, the fact that in practice "reward" is just whatever the policy is optimized against and nobody claims it's a natural kind. Translate the whole heterogeneity debate into that vocabulary as faithfully as you can. My hunch — treat it as a hunch and attack it if it's wrong — is that the debate is secretly about whether reward is a scalar or a vector, that "hedonic tone" is playing the role of the scalar readout head, and that the heterogeneity objection is just the observation that the representation underneath is enormously high-dimensional and non-uniform. If that's right, does the objection collapse, or does it show the readout head is doing no real work and the hedonist is worshipping a bookkeeping device?
