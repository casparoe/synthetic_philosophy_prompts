ok so I keep watching a move get made in the preference-learning world and I can't tell whether it's a brilliant shortcut or a smuggled premise, and I don't fully trust my own read on it.

The move: you want to learn what someone values from watching them act. So you assume they were roughly optimizing — maximizing the true reward, just imperfectly at it — and you model each action as a softmax over how good the available options look, with a temperature knob, and you fit that knob alongside the reward by likelihood on held-out demos. Everyone then talks about the fitted temperature as a measure of how noisy the human was, where a low value means the demos are informative and a high value means they're mostly garbage.

Things I suspect this presupposes but can't articulate cleanly: that there's a single consistent utility function underneath at all; that every departure from optimal is the same species of thing, so one scalar has to absorb carelessness, the demonstrator having different incentives than the task assumes, them rushing the parts that don't matter, and them performing for an audience rather than just doing the job; and that fitting the knob to maximize prediction accuracy isn't quietly defining rationality as predictability.

And the inconsistency that actually bugs me: same building, on Monday the demonstrator is an approximately optimal planner whose errors are noise, and on Tuesday the demonstrator knows the reward and is deliberately teaching the learner, so you specifically shouldn't read their actions as a plan. Do those two survive in the same head?

Can you reconstruct the implicit theory of preference and agency the softmax apparatus needs — seperate what it strictly requires from what it merely sits comfortably with, mark the places the practice is underdetermined so several rival theories would license it, and say what would actually have to change in the pipeline if you stopped treating deviation as noise and started treating it as signal.

Also give me your confidence on the individual claims rather than one overall vibe, and point at the single one you'd bet least on.
