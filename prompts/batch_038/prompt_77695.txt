Design an in-class game that gets them to feel when that's true and when it quietly isn't. Points only, no real money, but make the scoring something they'll actually care about. They shouldn't hear the label until the debrief; I want them leaving with the sense that this is a contingent claim about a class of plans, not a law of nature.
