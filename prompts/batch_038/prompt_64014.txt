Headline: kids on travel/select teams had roughly twice the injury rate of rec-league kids, something like 11 per 100 players vs 5. So here's my argument. The scary headline is just an artifact of travel kids playing way more hours. If we let her play travel but hold her to about 5 hours a week and skip the indoor winter league, she's sitting in a bracket where travel has the *better* rate, so she'd actually be safer than if she stayed in rec.
