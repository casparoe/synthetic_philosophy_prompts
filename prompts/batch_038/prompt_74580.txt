Then tell me which readings would genuinely let him skip the yes/no question and go right to a ranking, which ones don't get him that at all, and which readings put the thermostat on the bottom rung of the ladder rather than off the ladder entirely — because he slips between those two and I want to be able to name it in the meeting.
