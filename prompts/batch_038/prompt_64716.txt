Track B I threw together in one afternoon for my little brother's 4th grade class. Three chords, kind of dumb. The kids went wild, two of the teachers cried, they still sing it in the hallway. So A is real and lands on nobody, and B lands on everybody but was fake coming out of me. And if it's B, does A even still count as art at all?
