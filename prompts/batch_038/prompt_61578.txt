Next month I present at a workshop on predictive processing and computational psychiatry. The room is mostly computational neuroscientists and cognitive modelers, plus two clinical psychologists who work on alexithymia and impaired insight, and one philosopher of self-knowledge who will be friendly. Nobody there thinks philosophy gets to constrain their models.

My thesis: the intuition of an explanatory gap between functional facts and phenomenal facts is not evidence about consciousness but a byproduct of the format of introspective access. A system whose access to its own processing is low-bandwidth, post-hoc, and unmarked as inference will characterize its own states as intrinsically given; the meta-problem is what arises when such a system compares the richness it attributes to experience with the poverty of the access it can actually inspect. My distinctive claim is about stability. The gap-intuition survives argument neither because it is true nor because of philosophical training, but because it is regenerated at the level of the introspective format on every deployment — so no amount of third-person information should remove it. That yields a prediction: people with measurably degraded introspective access should not report weaker gap-intuitions, and gap-intuitions should be insensitive to manipulations that reliably shift ordinary introspective confidence.

Prepare my Q&A sheet: the five or six objections this audience will actually press, ordered by how much damage they would do, each phrased the way a computational modeler or a clinical psychologist would phrase it rather than the way a philosopher of mind would, with a two-minute reply I can deliver from notes and one sentence flagging where that reply is softest. At least one objection should concern falsifiability — whether my stability claim can absorb any counterevidence — and at least one should concern whether I have merely relabeled the confabulation literature without adding a mechanism. End with the single question I am hoping nobody asks, and why it is dangerous. Keep the whole thing under 900 words.
