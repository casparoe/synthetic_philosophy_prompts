write me a parody of one of those live flip-panel departures boards at a train station — except this one is run by a city's Department of the Present, and it displays events in the Andromeda galaxy, about 2.5 million light years out. Around 600 words, and please format it like the actual page: a header, the board itself as a set of rows with statuses, a "service alerts" section, then a transcript of one call to the complaints line.

The joke mechanism should be the board's refresh logic. It updates whenever a pedestrian crosses the street outside city hall, and which way they're walking changes what the board says. Someone strolling toward Andromeda's side of the sky flips a row to DEPARTED — ON TIME; someone strolling the other way flips the same row to DECISION STILL PENDING. Get the scale right: the shift goes like vL/c², so a person moving a few miles an hour drags the far end of the present around by *days*, not seconds, and the board's staff should find a several-day discrepancy across a single crosswalk completely routine. Somewhere in the small print, note that the board only tracks destinations with a component along the direction of pedestrian travel — a specialist should catch why that caveat has to be there.

Target: the move from "different observers have different planes of simultaneity" straight to "so the future is already fixed, here's your departure time." The staff aren't arguing for the block universe, they're operations people, understaffed, with a backlog. Every standard objection should surface only as an infrastructure complaint, never as an argument: the present being a local notion that can't be stretched into a global slice across the galaxy should read as a coverage/roaming problem; the fact that nobody can ever check, because the light takes 2.5 million years to get here, should read as a data-latency issue and a very tired engineer explaining that the board has only ever displayed the past; the convention-ladenness of distant simultaneity should show up as a rival board across the street posting different statuses for the same rows.

Please include: a presentist advocacy group that's put up its own competing board, which refuses to list anything more than a light-second away and just says PENDING for all of it; one caller who wants to send a warning to somebody in Andromeda and gets told that isn't a service they offer and never will be; a fare-and-refunds note establishing that the Present is free but nonrefundable; and one line of copy that an eternalist would read as a straight endorsement and a presentist would read as the whole reductio, with no wink in either direction.

Keep it deadpan the whole way — no narrator stepping out to explain the thought experiment, no citations, no references to real books or papers, and don't put words in any real person's mouth; everything in-world gets invented. It should land for someone who knows the Rietdijk–Putnam / Andromeda-paradox literature and still be followable for someone who doesn't. Oh, and the infrastructre complaints are the load-bearing part, don't skip them.
