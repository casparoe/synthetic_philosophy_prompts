ok so I want a satirical product page, roughly 600 words, for a startup that scores and sells moral standing to blame. Think credit bureau, but for whether your criticism of someone actually lands. It should have the usual furniture: the score itself (0–850, tiered), what dings it (getting caught doing the very thing you blamed, being complicit in the practice, blaming someone whose conduct profits you), the paid add-ons (standing repair after a public hypocrisy, borrowing a friend's standing for one use, transferring standing up to a manager so she can denounce a policy she wrote), the family plan, and an FAQ.

The comedy has to come from the philosophy being right rather than vague. So: the score measures standing and never truth, and the page has to deal with that honestly — a 200-score customer with a correct accusation is still a 200-score customer. Self-blame should be the one product they can't legally sell and are visibly bitter about. And there should be a clause in the terms where the company tries to blame it's own users and runs headlong into the exact problem it sells its way around.

Deadpan corporate-copy voice the whole way, no winking at the reader, no explaining the jokes. Invent everything: no real companies, no named philosophers, no bibliography. I want someone who works on hypocrisy and standing to laugh and to think it's fair.
