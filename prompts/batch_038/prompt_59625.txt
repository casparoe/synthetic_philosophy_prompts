So I'm a backend engineer, zero philosophy training, and I recently fell into Strawson's Freedom and Resentment — the reactive attitudes essay. The bit that stuck is the objective attitude: when you stop resenting someone and start treating them as a thing to be managed, therapized, or avoided, plus Strawson's claim that you can't actually live in that mode toward people you're in real relationships with.

Now point that at the South African Truth and Reconciliation Commission. The facts im working from: amnesty wasn't blanket, it was individual and conditional — the applicant had to disclose all the relevant facts, and the act had to be tied to a political objective — and the whole scheme survived a constitutional challenge brought by victims' families, including Biko's widow. So a statute effectively took the ordinary reactive responses off the table for a whole class of perpetrators, in exchange for truth.

Can you analyze the amnesty process as a case of legally mandated suspension of the reactive attitudes? I want the mapping done properly: who's being asked to adopt the objective attitude toward whom (is it the state instructing victims, or is it the perpetrators being converted into objects of social policy?), whether a full-disclosure hearing with apparent remorse counts as the perpetrator re-entering the participant stance rather than being objectified at all, and how Strawson's exemption conditions — coercion, abnormality, being a soldier under orders — line up against the political objective test. Then run it the other direction: does the aftermath (families who felt used, hearings that produced reconciliation for some and just left raw anger with nowhere to go for others) confirm Strawson's warning that suspension is unstable, or is a whole society doing it by statute something his framework never contemplated, since he was thinking one-on-one?

dont give me a survey of both readings. Take a position — either Strawson explains the amnesty bargain or the TRC breaks him — defend it, and flag the spots where you're stretching the theory to make it fit.
