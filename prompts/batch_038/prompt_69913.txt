The dispositional side says the culturally supplied belief about the victims was already sufficient, and the scaffolding mostly just let people act on what they already held. I want a thought experiment that makes those two claims issue different, checkable predictions.

Here's the build. Contemporary and realistic: a contracted security outfit running a holding facility for a group the surrounding society already devalues. No lab, no lab coat, no uniforms-and-anonymous-mob business, no sci-fi tech, no mind control, no historical Nazis. Scenario itself capped at two paragraphs. Then hold one variable nailed down and vary the other.
