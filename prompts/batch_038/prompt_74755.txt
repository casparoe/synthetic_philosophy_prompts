Both things seem true-ish and I can't locate where the actual disagreement lives. I tried to settle it with a friend who does evals at an AI lab and he just said "take the theorem at face value," which is not an answer. So instead: write me a dialogue between the two positions. Neither one gets to be a strawman, and neither one gets to win on a technicality the other fails to notice.
