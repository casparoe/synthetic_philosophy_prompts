Ok so — three weeks from now I'm giving a talk at a consciousness center's Friday seminar. Interdisciplinary room, roughly half philosophy half cog sci, and I know the composition decently well: two people who defend the phenomenal concepts strategy and are perfectly happy being physicalists while insisting there is genuinely something it's like to be them; one experimentalist who runs no-report and binocular rivalry paradigms and is openly contemptuous of armchair theorizing; one metaphysician who works on determinates and determinables (exclusion, proportionality, Yablo-ish stuff); and a scatter of grad students, some illusionist-curious, some deep into integrated information theory.

My thesis is a step past Dennett's move in Quining Qualia. He says the four supposed marks of qualia — ineffable, intrinsic, private, directly apprehended — can't be jointly instantiated, so the posit is incoherent. I want to say something stronger and more specific: there's a cognitive mechanism that manufactures the appearance of such properties, and once you name the mechanism the realist loses basically all their evidence rather than just the definitional high ground. I've been calling it determinate masking:

1. Discrimination is a difference-making relation: for a system to tell A from B, some state of it has to vary between A and B.
2. So anything a system can discriminate is picked out relationally, and nothing picked out relationally is intrinsic.
3. Subjects plainly can discriminate very fine-grained phenomenal differences (paint chips, two cups of coffee, two voices) while being unable to say what the difference consists in, and while being wrong about it under pressure — preference shifts get attributed to the world rather than to the taster, etc.
4. Best explanation: the discriminating machinery tracks determinate functional states, while the concepts and vocabulary available to the reporting system only carve things at the determinable level. The felt "ineffable intrinsic quality" is the artifact of that mismatch, not an extra ingredient in the state.
5. So the appearance of qualia is fully explained without positing anything bearing the four marks — and that appearance is the only evidence anybody ever had for the marks.
