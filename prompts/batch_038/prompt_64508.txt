Write me a parody of a corporate employee-handbook section — heading something like "7.4 Supervised Inebriation and Character Assessment" — for a company that has genuinely adopted the Platonic program as HR policy and is completely sincere about it.

About 700 words, numbered clauses with a couple of subheadings and one small sidebar, in deadpan compliance-manual voice.

Stuff I want in there because its the actual substance, not just set dressing: wine handled as a pharmakon that temporarily loosens self-control and therefore works as a cheap assay of character (the test-the-gold-in-the-fire idea); the age tiers from the Laws — nothing fermented at all for the under-18s, i.e. the interns, moderation up to 30 with an explicit no-intoxication rule, and free convivial drinking after 40 framed as Dionysus' medicine against the crabbedness of age (with the obvious staffing implications for the exec layer); and a job description for the appointed symposiarch, including the awkward fact that he's the one responsible for everyone else's restraint while being the person in the room most exposed to losing his own.
