I'm helping to run a third-year political philosophy seminar, and every single year the same thing happens: I put Berlin's two concepts up against Pettit's non-domination, and half the room concludes that republican freedom is just negative liberty with the word arbitrary bolted on, while the other half concludes it's just positive liberty for people squeamish about positive liberty. Neither side budges, and arguing about it more hasn't helped, so I want to try breaking the deadlock with a case instead. What I need from you is a thought experiment built to a spec — essentially a stress test I can hand out — plus a demonstration that it actually meets the spec, because the whole point is that the verdicts have to fall out of the theories rather than being stipulated by the storytelling.

Here are the specs. One scenario, contemporary and realistic, no science fiction, no dictators, no blackmail, and the scenario itself no more than a paragraph. The three verdicts I need: negative liberty high (no interference, actual or conditional, and nobody narrowing her option set), positive liberty high, republican freedom low. The power asymmetry has to come from a standing written arrangement — a register, a licence, a discretionary clause in some institution's rules — not from anybody's temperament, and nobody may exercise or threaten to exercise that power anywhere in the story. The protagonist knows about the arrangement, has thought hard about it, and genuinely passes what Pettit frames as the eyeball test: she can look the people holding the power in the eye as an equal, with no fear and no deference — and that has to be true, not a case of self-deception or adaptive preferences, because I do not want the kindly master or the benevolent patriarch wearing a new outfit. For the positive-liberty leg, use an authenticity or hierarchical view in the neighbourhood of Frankfurt's second-order volitions that deliberately leaves out any independence condition, and say explicitly that you're doing that. And here's the wrinkle I actually care about: build the arrangement so that it was designed to track the interests of the people subject to it as a group — professional standards, minimum rates, a proper complaints procedure that everyone else can use — so that the non-arbitrariness condition is genuinely under strain rather than trivially satisfied. The case should force the question of whose interests need tracking, hers or the group's, and of what control she personally has. I had a rough setting in mind, a freelance court interpreter on a national register whose registrar can strike her off at his discretion with no cause given and no appeal under the current terms, but feel free to swap it for something better if it fits the specs more cleanly.

After the scenario, walk me through each of the three conceptions in its own terms — verdict plus the reasoning, not just a label — then tell me honestly which single feature is doing the work for the republican leg. Run two ablations on it: what happens if the rules are amended so she gets a vote on them and a real right of appeal, and what happens if you swap the Frankfurt-style account of positive liberty for one that does include an independence condition on autonomy, which is where Raz is the obvious candidate. Does the three-way split survive each change, or does it collapse back into a two-way one, and if it collapses, which pair merges? Finish with the two best attacks on the case. One from a negative libertarian insisting that what I'm calling domination is nothing more than a probabilistic claim about the likelihood of future interference and so not a distinct kind of unfreedom at all. And one from a republican arguing the specs are jointly unsatisfiable — that if she really has that much control and those exit options she isn't dominated at all, and if she is dominated then she can't honestly be passing the eyeball test. I want your view on whether that second attack lands, because if it does I'd rather run that seminar than the one I planned. British English throughout, please, and no bibliography or reading list — I know where to look things up, I just want the case and the analysis.
