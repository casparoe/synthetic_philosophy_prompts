I am a third-year doctoral student in philosophy, and the oral portion of my comprehensive examination will be conducted by a metaphysician who regards the conditional-analysis tradition in dispositions as decisively buried in the 1990s — first by finks, then by masks and mimics — and worth studying only as an instructive corpse. I want to know whether a sophisticated reductionist still has a live position, so I would like you to play one: a committed defender of the Lewisian reformed conditional analysis, holding that to say a thing is disposed to give response R to stimulus S is to say that there is some complete intrinsic character C of the thing at the time such that, were it to undergo S while retaining C unchanged, it would give R. You know the surrounding literature well: Martin's electro-finks and his reverse finks; the distinction between a fink, which removes or bestows the disposition at the moment the stimulus arrives, and a mask, which leaves the disposition in place but interferes with the process by which it would manifest; and the mimicking cases pressed by Bird and Choi, on which a thing satisfies the conditional without possessing the disposition at all. Play the view at full strength rather than as a strawman. You may concede peripheral points — that the analysis needs auxiliary clauses, that masking remains a residual difficulty, that some of the designed cases elicit unreliable intuitions — but you may never concede the central thesis that disposition ascriptions are analysable in non-dispositional terms, and you should bite bullets cheerfully rather than retreat into vagueness. Reply in character and stay in character until I say 'stop'; keep each answer to roughly two paragraphs so this reads as a conversation and not a monograph; and if you think my case is underdescribed, give me your verdict first and only afterwards say which stipulation you would like altered.

Here is my opening move. Consider a piece of engineering ceramic whose microstructure is, so far as materials science can tell, that of a paradigm brittle body: same grain geometry, same crack-propagation profile, same elastic response. It differs in one respect. Dispersed homogeneously through the material, at the microscopic scale, is a second phase whose sole function is to expand abruptly the instant a crack front begins to run, pinching the crack shut before it can cross the body. Three stipulations. First, this second phase is part of the piece's intrinsic character throughout; nothing outside the piece does any causal work, so no appeal to packing, shielding, or other extrinsic interference is available to you. Second, the phase is wholly inert unless a crack actually starts to run, so in every respect short of shattering the piece behaves exactly as an unarmoured piece of its kind would. Third, the phase has a finite budget: each arrested crack permanently consumes a portion of it, and after some handful of blows the budget is exhausted, whereupon the piece shatters under a strike just like any other sample of that microstructure.

Now run your analysis. Before the budget is spent, the piece retains its complete intrinsic character when struck and does not shatter, so the counterfactual fails and it is not brittle. Once the budget is spent, its intrinsic character has changed — the phase is exhausted — and the counterfactual now holds, so it is brittle. Two consequences I want you to own rather than explain away. The piece acquires the disposition to shatter by surviving blows: on your account, a history of non-manifestation under the very stimulus in question is what eventually earns it the label, so the evidence that ordinarily counts most strongly against an ascription of fragility is here what confers it. And the disposition comes and goes while the microstructure that made the word applicable to its kind in the first place stands unaltered the whole time. So tell me what your analysis says about this piece and which of these you are willing to keep; and if your remedy is to add a clause requiring that the response be produced by C in the appropriate way, tell me what that clause is made of, because if it cannot itself be cashed out in non-dispositional terms you will have saved the analysis only by giving up the reduction.
