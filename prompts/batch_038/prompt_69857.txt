Here's my opening move and I want you to take it head-on rather than dodge into etymology. Your argument, at its very best, is only about names and requests. Everyone already grants that the phrase white horse and the phrase horse don't have the same extension, and that a request for one can be filled by things that won't fill a request for the other — that's just how modifying a noun works, it isn't a discovery anybody needs a dialogue for. But no white horse stops being a horse because somebody tacked a color word onto it. So you're either making an obvious point about language, in which case there's no paradox and no reason anyone was ever scandalized, or you're making a point about animals that is straightforwardly false.
