I'm TA-ing an upper-level seminar on distributive justice, and we've just worked through the Wilt Chamberlain example from *Anarchy, State, and Utopia*. A sizable chunk of the class has come away believing that Nozick's objection to patterned principles is essentially a dynamical one — that free exchanges will inevitably erode any favored profile of holdings, so a pattern can only be maintained by continual interference. I want to dislodge that reading by handing them a case in which the historical and end-state approaches diverge just as sharply, but where liberty, exchange, and interference play no role whatsoever, so that the students have to say what work the Chamberlain argument is really doing if it isn't the engine of the disagreement. Please construct that thought experiment for me to specifications.

The specifications: there is a final snapshot of holdings in a small, ordinary community, and a patterned end-state principle that you state explicitly — including the variable in which the pattern is specified, whether income, resources, welfare, or something else — pronounces that snapshot fully just, not merely tolerable but exactly on pattern; Nozick's entitlement theory pronounces the very same snapshot unjust; and the entire source of the injustice is a single act of initial acquisition, with nothing downstream contributing to it. There are no transfers of any kind after the initial appropriations: no trades, no gifts, no wages, no bequests, no sales. People simply hold what they acquired, and time passes. There is likewise no force, no fraud, no theft, no enslavement, no conquest, and no expropriation anywhere in the history, because I want to keep the students away from the familiar rectification cases they all reach for, and no government, no taxation, and no redistributive agency appears in the story at all, so that the divergence is generated by the theories themselves rather than by political conflict. The setting should be realistic and contemporary at human scale — a tract of land, a water right, a fishing ground, a firm, a patent, a stretch of coastline, whatever you prefer — with no science fiction and no exotic scarcity setups of the lone-well-in-the-desert variety; indeed the acquisition should look, on its face, like something a court would treat as a paradigm of legitimate original appropriation, with labor mixed in, productive use made, nothing allowed to spoil, and nobody deprived of anything they were actually using. The scenario itself should be one or two paragraphs; you don't need to cite or recommend any literature, since what I want is your construction, not the scholarship around it.

After the scenario, show me that it works. Walk through the history step by step and the resulting snapshot; give the patterned principle's verdict and explain precisely what its metric registers and what it is structurally blind to; give the entitlement theory's verdict, name which of its three principles generates it, and demonstrate that the judgment is not being smuggled in by the rectification principle through the back door. Then identify the single load-bearing feature of the acquisition, and run two removal tests: first, what happens to the case if you insert one purely voluntary transfer at the end — does it turn into a Chamberlain case, or is it the same case with an irrelevant addition, and what does that tell us about whether the liberty premise is doing any work in Nozick's argument; second, what happens if you strip out the load-bearing feature and leave an acquisition that everyone would call clean. Finally, give me the patterned theorist's best reply that does not simply re-assert the pattern — an argument about what makes an acquisition legitimate on her own terms, or about whether historical facts bear on justice at all — and the best reply available from inside Nozick's own framework, via the proviso on appropriation, that would rescue the patterned verdict; say whether conceding that reply costs the entitlement theorist anything substantive. The verdicts have to fall out of the theories as they actually stand rather than being stipulated, and the patterned principle must not be a strawman; if you find that you cannot meet all the constraints simultaneously without bending one of them, say so plainly and tell me the minimal change to the specifications that makes the case go through.
