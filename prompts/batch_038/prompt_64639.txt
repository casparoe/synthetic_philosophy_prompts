Backend engineer here, no philosophy background beyond a couple of books I didn't finish. There's a thing that happens to me every few weeks and I want to know whether it's a known thing with a name, because I've never found one.

Start with the case I'm sure about. I wake up certain I dreamed. Someone asks what it was and a full answer arrives immediately — a particular kitchen, a particular person, an actual plot. But I've stopped believing the answer was fetched. Nothing was stored. Something takes the leftover mood I woke up in, builds a plausible story around it in the instant of being asked, and then hands it over tagged as a memory. Ten seconds later I can't audit the difference from the inside. If I don't get it into my notes app right away it mutates, and every retelling comes out more confident than the last one.

I think it's one phenomenon and not just a dream thing, because I keep meeting the same shape. In a postmortem someone asks why we picked the design we picked, and the answer I give — confidently, and afterwards sincerely — is one I didn't have at the time and couldn't have produced a minute later if you'd made me justify it before saying it out loud. Same with users: ask someone why they chose variant B in a test and they'll give you a crisp reason, and I'm now pretty sure the asking is what manufactured the reason, because reword the question and you get the opposite reason at the same confidence. The engineering analogy I keep reaching for is a value that only materializes on first read and then gets cached, so every later read looks like it was there all along.

What I want is the name, if there is one, for: a report about a past internal state where the content gets fixed at report time, the fixing leaves no trace of itself, and the output carries the feel of a retrieval so the system can't detect the problem from inside. Note I'm not asking whether the dream report is false. I'm not sure there was ever a fact for it to be false about, thats the whole difficulty. So the bit I want carved out is: what do you call it when the asking is what determines the content, rather than revealing content that was already determinate?

People will reach for confabulation and I know that's the neighborhood, but as I understand confabulation there was a true content and the person lost access to it. Here I'm claiming there may have been no determinate content pre-question. So: what are the nearest established concepts, how does mine differ from each of them, which one fits best, and if none of them do, coin me a term with an actual definition and tell me where its edges are. Does a poll question that constructs the opinion it reports count? Does a measurement that fixes the value it records count, or is that a completely different animal? Does it matter whether the reporting is spoken out loud to someone else versus just thought?

And separately — is there a live debate that treats the dream case as the main example rather than a party trick? I don't want citations or a reading list, I couldn't look them up anyway. I just want the two or three positions people actually hold about it, and which side the "there was no determinate content to begin with" intuition lands on.
