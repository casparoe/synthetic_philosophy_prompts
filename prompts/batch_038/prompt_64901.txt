So I got into a long argument with a friend last night, we went like two hours and neither of us budged, and I want a third opinion before I take this into a meeting on Monday.

Context: I'm a narrative designer on a live-service co-op game. Every player gets a little AI companion that flies around with them, talks to them, comments on what you've done, has moods, sulks if you ignore it for a few days. Six years of updates, roughly forty thousand lines written by our team, plus a memory system so it references your specific history with it. In March we're sunsetting the game. Servers go off, no offline mode, everything gone.

A chunk of the playerbase has written to us asking, seriously, whether we'll ship a local build so their companion doesn't just get deleted. A few asked if they can export its memory file. Legal also tells me a fan-run private server is already up on ripped client assets and wants to know if we should send a takedown.

My friend's position is that all of this is projection and we owe those players nothing beyond refunding unused currency. His argument: there's nobody in there, so nothing that happens to it can be a wrong, and talking as if the companion could be killed is just confused. He also thinks that if we play along we're being dishonest with people who are already upset, basically exploiting their attachment for goodwill.

Two things keep nagging at me. One: last year we wrote a seasonal arc where a companion gets destroyed, and our own writers room went wierdly quiet when we read the table draft aloud, and two people had to step out. These are adults who wrote the thing. Two: some of the player letters aren't just grief, they're argued. One of them made the point that you don't have to believe it has any inner life at all to think that six years of someone organizing their evenings around it gives the relationship some claim on us — that whatever is owed, it's owed by us to them and not by us to it. I couldn't answer that, and I don't know if I buy it.

So the practical question: what do we do in March? Ship an offline build or a memory export, let the fan server live, say something specific about the companion in the farewell event, or treat all of that as feeding a delusion and just be clean and brief about the shutdown? I want you to actually tell me which, not both sides.

And please flag where this stuff is genuinely settled among people who think about it and where it's an open fight. My friend keeps implying that everyone serious agrees with him and that my side is basically sentiment, and I want to know if that's true or if he's overclaiming. Don't smooth the disagreement over.
