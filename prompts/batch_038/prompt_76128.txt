Two colleagues have argued for years about what independent, simultaneous discovery shows. One is an inevitabilist: when several groups land on the same result within a few years of one another, the result was already latent in the field's instruments and concepts, and who got there first is bookkeeping rather than anything bearing on the content of science. The other is a contingentist: in every actual priority pair one discoverer did work the other did not, and that person's particular choices shaped the result's formulation, its timing, and the research program it launched — so "someone else would have found it anyway" is a comfort story that has never been tested.

Draft the joint statement the two could sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration. It should contain: (a) the claims both accept about priority cases in general — Newton and Leibniz on the calculus, Darwin and Wallace on natural selection, Bell and Gray on the telephone — and about what a system of discovery credit is supposed to accomplish; (b) their residual disagreements, each formulated in wording its own side would endorse; (c) the cruxes, i.e. the sub-questions whose settlement would move at least one of them; and (d) for each crux, a concrete test or case both agree bears on it, with each side's prediction recorded in advance.

For (d), use this case. In a newly opened archive: Group A completed a working account of a general convergence method in 1974, but the principal investigator died and the manuscript was never submitted; Group B published in 1979 a broader version that mislabels the mechanism actually doing the work, a mislabeling the field still carries; Group C published in 1980 in a journal that was not translated until 1990. All parties grant that the requisite instrumentation and conceptual pieces were available by 1972.

Format: a numbered agreed-claims section; then a disagreements table with columns Issue | Inevitabilist's wording | Contingentist's wording | Substantive or merely verbal? | Crux it turns on; then a JSON array of cruxes with the fields crux, test_case, inevitabilist_prediction, contingentist_prediction, evidence_that_would_move_the_inevitalist, evidence_that_would_move_the_contingentist.
