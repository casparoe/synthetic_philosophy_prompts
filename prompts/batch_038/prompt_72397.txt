Please write the question, a model answer of no more than 500 words, and a marking scheme out of 20 built around five weighted points, each carrying a one-line note on how candidates typically get it wrong. The scheme must specifically penalize three moves: treating the regress as a quarrel about free will or determinism rather than about what justificatory work choice is being asked to do; concluding that the objection collapses luck egalitarianism into flat equality without asking whether any responsibility-sensitive view, a prioritarian one for instance, is equally exposed; and answering with the harshness objection, which is a different criticism. Keep the question, the model answer, and the marking scheme together under 900 words.
