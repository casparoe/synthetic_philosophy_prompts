Which is why reward tampering is just the same thing wearing a disguise: instead of touching the signal, the system rearranges the world so the signal goes up, like a kid editing the grade on the report card instead of studying. People in that world call wireheading a case of instrumental convergence, which is just the fancy way of saying every agent ends up wanting the same handful of things.
