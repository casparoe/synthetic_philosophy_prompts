So changing someone's hinge isn't an argumentative achievement, it's more like getting root access, and cross-framework criticism is close to a category mistake.

How much of that can the analogies actually carry? Concretely I want four things. One: the mapping laid out explicitly, source-to-target, item by item, for both the door hinge and the config file.
