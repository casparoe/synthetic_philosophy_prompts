I've been at the same small print shop for six years — fourteen of us, one owner. He's genuinely good to me. Never raised his voice at me once, let me take three weeks off when my mom was sick without me even asking. But nothing is written down. My hours, my rate, whether I get the Friday shift: its all whatever he says that week, and there's no HR and no contract and nobody to go to if he changed his mind. Last year a guy who'd been there longer than me had his hours quietly cut after he pushed back on a job the owner wanted rushed, and he just drifted off. Nobody said anything because there was nothing to say.

Two weeks ago I got an offer from a much bigger place. Union shop, real contract, sixty days notice, a written grievance thing you can actually file. But they log your machine time and your badge-ins, you have to request work-from-home days a week ahead, the on-call rotation is grim, and the floor manager is a jerk who hovers over you and nitpicks. They'd also give me two paid hours a week toward the certification I've been meaning to finish, which I've never managed here because "flexible" turns out to mean flexible whenever he doesn't need me.

Also the owner told us last month he's probably selling in a couple of years.

My partner says I'm already unfree here, I just happen to like the guy holding the leash. I say I'd be more watched over there and more free here. Which of us is actually right? And is it dumb to feel like six years of him never once using that power has to count for something?
