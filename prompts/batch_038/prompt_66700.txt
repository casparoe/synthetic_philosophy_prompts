We also lifted a trick from some game a member recommended: immutable clauses have to be "transmuted" to mutable status by their own separate two-thirds vote before anyone's allowed to touch their text.

Someone has already queued up the obvious two-step: transmute Clause 12, then amend away the immutability sentence, then next month delete Clause 12 wholesale and substitute a brand new amendment rule with a simple majority threshold. And here's the part that actually keeps me up — that final deleting amendment counts as valid only because Clause 12 authorized it. So the last thing the old rule ever does is terminate its own force, and once it's gone there's nothing anywhere in the document that ever said the replacement rule was allowed to exist.

So the "but who authorizes the authorizer" objection is fake depth, and we should stop fussing and just vote.
