So what is the statue made out of — some non physical layer sitting on top of the matter, like a ghost of the shape? And when you squash it, where does that extra bit go, since the clay obviously didn't go anywhere?

Which is it? Nobody I've read even brings up the weighing thing and I feel like it settles the whole argument in one line.
