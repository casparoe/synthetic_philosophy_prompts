Second was in my group, where a guy used it to argue that Gödel's theorem proves minds aren't machines — his version was that a machine can't step outside its own rules to see its limits but we can, and when I pushed back he told me I was trying to lift the system by its own bootstraps. Third was somebody pointing at the print of Drawing Hands on my wall and saying that's a nice picture but it can't really be a closed loop, something has to be outside it.

Can you lay out the distinct precise claims that one sentence could be standing in for?
