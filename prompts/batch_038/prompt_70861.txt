Setup so you know what I mean by things. In the color-phi style cases you flash two spots of different colours in different places, close together in time, and the subject reports one spot moving across and changing colour partway — a colour it never instantaneously had at any point in the display. Dennett's two options: Orwellian, the experience really happened and then got rewritten before it could be reported; Stalinesque, the rewriting happens while the content is still being assembled, so the un-rewritten experience never occured at all. Sam's move, which I found uncomfortably good: he said a Stalinesque view is just an Orwellian view where you've decided the tape recorder was never plugged in, and that what licenses you to say that is the further claim that two stories agreeing on every possible report and every possible probe response are not two stories. What I hold, written down properly for the first time ever:

(a) there is a fact of the matter about what it's like for a subject at a time. I'm not a multiple-drafts guy. Consciousness happens once, at a time, in a subject.
(b) in the color-phi cases the subject's verbal report is a decent readout of what they experienced, not a post-hoc reconstruction. People confabulate sometimes, sure, but I don't think that's what's going on here.
(c) I'm a Stalinesque about postdiction generally: when a later stimulus changes how an earlier one looked, nothing that was already conscious got edited — the earlier content was still under construction. What's conscious is what the brain has globally settled on and made available.
(d) experience is smeared rather than knife-edged. A moment of consciousness covers a short stretch of a few hundred ms, more like a long exposure than a photograph.
(e) if two theories of consciousness make all the same predictions about every possible report, probe response and behavioural discrimination, they are not really two theories. I'm pretty verificationist about phenomenal claims.
(f) there is something it's like for a subject at times when they have no report and no disposition to report — peripheral stuff you'd deny under questioning, the tail end of a dream you never told anyone about, and so on.
