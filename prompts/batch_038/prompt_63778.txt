Here's the thing that won't let go of me. The novel's divergence point is a pure great-man counterfactual: one bullet kills Roosevelt in 1933 and the whole century tips over. Then Abendsen's counter-history, the one where the Allies win, turns out to be just another empire, so even flipping the outcome leaves the structure standing.

So which book is this really — a Carlylean one wearing structuralist clothes, or Spencer's reply to Carlyle (great men are what their society makes) dressed up as a 1933 assassination plot?
