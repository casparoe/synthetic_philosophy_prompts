Forty years doing animal behaviour in the field, retired last spring, and now I read philosphy for fun — currently Dennett's Darwinian / Skinnerian / Popperian / Gregorian creatures. Write me a joke about those four kinds of minds. The catch is that the punchline has to turn on what actually separates them, i.e. how each one copes with a brand-new problem: the genetic lottery, habits stamped in by reward, letting your guesses die in your head instead of in the world, and borrowing design work that other creatures already did and left lying around in books and tools. I want it to land for someone who's read the book, not a "four X walk into a bar" gag where the labels could be swapped for anything. Start with the joke itself — no preamble, and no explaining why it's funny afterwards.
