I am preparing the final session of a graduate seminar on Nietzsche's philosophy of health, which will be co-taught with a colleague from the medical school's history and philosophy of medicine group. Rather than another round of exposition, I want to hand the students a specimen of philosophical satire and have them reverse-engineer it. I would like you to write that specimen.

The form is a pre-registration record for a clinical trial, submitted to a public trials registry in the house style of such registries. Please use the conventional structured fields, in the conventional order and under their conventional headings: official title and acronym; condition; study design; intervention arms with dosing, timing, and duration; primary and secondary outcome measures, each with its instrument and timepoints; eligibility criteria, including exclusions; enrolment and the justification for the sample size; masking; allocation and randomization; stopping rules; definitions of reportable adverse events; conflicts of interest; a limitations paragraph; and finally the plain-language lay summary addressed to prospective participants. Target length is 800 to 950 words. Append one brief amendment note near the end, in the clipped register registries use, which quietly records that the study's conduct did not go as planned.

The trial's hypotheses must be a faithful rendering of the position Nietzsche takes in the passages of his late autobiography concerned with nutrition, climate, and place of residence: that the question of what and when one eats, and of where one lives, bears more decisively on what a person is capable of thinking than any question traditionally classed as moral or religious; that the refinements are matters of quantity, timing, and digestibility rather than of quantity alone; that tea and coffee are each assessed by their effects on a particular constitution at a particular hour; that alcohol belongs, in his account, among the agents of human deterioration and is grouped there with Christianity; and that the heavy fare of his native country is held responsible for something in the condition of its philosophy. Render each of these as a pre-specified, ostensibly testable hypothesis. Do not modernize his categories: no supplements, no ketogenic or microbiome vocabulary, no biohacking, no fasting protocols. Do not cast him as a vegetarian or as an ascetic; his regimen is one of refinement and selectivity, not renunciation.

The humour must come from the collision, and from nothing else. Blinding is unobtainable, because the intervention consists in a trained capacity for self-observation, so the protocol must say so and then carry on regardless. The primary endpoint is something for which no validated instrument exists, and the form nevertheless requires one; invent the instrument, give it a plausible name, and let its subscales betray the difficulty. The eligibility criteria should end up excluding nearly everyone. Adverse events should be reportable in the registry's own vocabulary, including at least one event that a reader will recognize as an outcome Nietzsche would have welcomed. Keep the register impeccably bureaucratic and entirely deadpan from the first field to the last: no asides, no irony signalled in the prose, no authorial wink.

The piece must also carry a genuine philosophical payload. If thought is a symptom of the body, then the analysis plan, the interpretation of the endpoint, and the limitations paragraph are symptoms too, and the record should be forced to acknowledge this in its own administrative language — most obviously by requiring that the investigators' own dietary logs be published alongside the findings so that readers may assess what produced the assessment. A reader should feel the regress without the document ever naming it or apologizing for it.

Two fairness constraints, which I will enforce with the students. The target of the satire is not a sick man's relationship to food, nor his suffering; it is the methodological claim, together with the evidential discourse that would convert a first-person physiology into a trial. A specialist in Nietzsche should find every attributed position genuinely his, and a specialist in trial methodology should find the protocol formally correct in its conventions; if either is fudged, the exercise fails. Please supply no citations and no reading list. After the document, add a separate paragraph of three or four sentences, addressed to me rather than to the students, identifying the single methodological objection the piece is built to enact and saying where in the record it is enacted.
