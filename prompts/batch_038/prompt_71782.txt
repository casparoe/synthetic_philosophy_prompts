Consider the career of quantum logic. Birkhoff and von Neumann proposed in 1936 that the experimental propositions of quantum mechanics do not form a Boolean algebra but a weaker, non-distributive structure. Hilary Putnam revived the idea in his 1968 paper "Is Logic Empirical?", arguing on explicitly Quinean grounds that the anomalies surrounding quantum measurement license abandoning classical propositional logic, just as anomalies surrounding space had licensed abandoning Euclidean geometry. No community of physicists or logicians ever adopted quantum logic as the logic it actually reasons with; Putnam himself later withdrew the proposal; subsequent foundations work located the trouble in locality and in the assignment of joint values rather than in distributivity; and what survives is orthomodular lattice theory as a branch of mathematics, latterly as an instrument in quantum information. Analyze that episode through the positive doctrine of "Two Dogmas of Empiricism" — not the critique of analyticity but the holism installed in its place: the web of belief, statements facing the tribunal of experience only as a corporate body, nothing immune to revision (Quine's own illustration of that last point being the proposal to give up the law of excluded middle for the sake of simplifying quantum mechanics), the maxim of minimal mutilation, and entrenchment.

Begin with a disciplined mapping. Identify the recalcitrant experience, if there was any: the theory's predictions were confirmed to extraordinary precision, and what pressed on it looks internal — non-commuting observables, the absence of a classical joint-value assignment, the measurement problem — rather than a failed prediction at the boundary of the web. Say whether Quine's spatial metaphor of periphery and interior can accommodate a revision pressure that originates inside the system, and what his account makes of a revision proposed not to save the phenomena but to make them statable.

Then draw a distinction I want held to throughout: adoption-revision, where a community actually ceases to treat an inference pattern as valid, versus articulation-revision, where a new calculus is framed alongside an inferential practice that does not change. Which of the two occurred, at which points in the story, and does holism have the vocabulary to tell them apart — or does it treat the framing of a rival calculus as already a revision of belief? Attend to a wrinkle here: Quine names excluded middle, whereas the lattice logic keeps excluded middle — a closed subspace together with its orthogonal complement spans the whole space — and gives up distributivity instead. Does that mismatch mean his example is simply the wrong one, or is he gesturing at a many-valued proposal for quantum mechanics that would have fit better?

Next, the honest accounting. What does holism genuinely explain: why the proposal arrived when and where it did, why the community localized the difficulty in measurement and nonlocality instead of in logic, why the formalism thrived while the philosophy died? Where is it silent: on what it takes for a revision to be taken up rather than merely proposed, on degrees of entrenchment beyond metaphor, on the worry that justifying a change of logic seems to require the very logic being changed, on the difference between a community's inferential norms and a mathematical structure that describes a domain?

Finally, the verdict in both directions. Does the episode confirm holism, expose it as unfalsifiable — licensing the revision of anything and sitting equally well with nothing being revised — or bypass it altogether, the quarrel having been about the individuation of a logic rather than about analyticity? Compare the conventionalist resources Quine discarded: Carnap's tolerance, Reichenbach's coordinating definitions, Poincaré's claim that a geometry is neither true nor false but convenient. Do any of them predict what actually happened better than holism does? Close by saying what the episode would have had to look like to count as a genuine empirical revision of logic, and if you think holism deserves a better example, nominate one and say whether it is empirical revision or mathematical elaboration in disguise.
