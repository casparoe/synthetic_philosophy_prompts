My friend and I argued late into the night about whether famines belong in the history of weather or the history of crime. His view is that the language of political crime is moralizing after the fact: what actually happens, he says, is that a harvest fails, prices move, and administrative capacity proves too thin to respond — nobody starves anybody, a system simply fails to hold. Mine is that he's describing the failure mechanism while dodging the question of who disabled the safeguards. I'm a control systems engineer; I design process control for chemical plants, so feedback, observability, saturation, and redundancy are my native vocabulary, and I want to see whether my intuition survives being stated in it.

So please restate the Sen-style case that famines are political rather than natural — the entitlement-failure analysis, the claim that a free press and electoral accountability reliably prevent mass starvation even in poor countries, and the pattern across Bengal in 1943, Ireland in the 1840s, and China during the Great Leap Forward — as faithfully as you can in the terms I actually work in: sensors and measurement error, negative and positive feedback, open-loop versus closed-loop control, state estimation and unobservable states, actuator saturation, deadbands and hysteresis, common-cause failure, redundancy, and interlock design. Treat the three cases as worked instances of specific control failures rather than as illustration-after-the-fact.

Then give me the residue report, and be strict about it. Which claims translate cleanly and actually get sharper in control language? Which translate only by distortion — where does my vocabulary force me to assert something Sen isn't asserting? And which have no expression at all; I suspect the difference between a system that failed and an agent who wronged someone is one of them. Finally, flag anywhere the translation smuggles in my own conclusion: control engineering presumes a designer who chose the architecture, and I want to know whether that presumption is doing covert work, and whether the residue is a defect in my vocabulary or evidence that my friend is right.
