I run a graduate reading group in historical explanation, and it has become apparent that when members call an outcome "structural" they mean at least four incompatible things. Please write a decision tree that takes a researcher who already accepts that some historical outcomes are better explained by slow-moving conditions than by decisions and events, and sorts them onto a named position.

Every branch node must be a question about a concrete explanatory case or commitment, never about labels or allegiances: does the silting-up that ends a port's prosperity belong with the coastline or with the war that closed its market; is the explanandum the origin of a divergence or its persistence once begun; does the slow layer merely rule possibilities out, or does it also select among the possibilities it leaves open; can processes on different timescales cause one another, or must explanation run in one direction only; must an adequate account terminate in the reasons of particular people?

The leaves must include Braudel's own layered scheme, hard environmental determinism, possibilism, the world-systems account, the California School's contingent comparison of coal and New World windfalls, the social-property-relations account, an institutional account treating the rules of a polity as the fundamental cause, and at least one leaf for anyone who denies that timescales are layered at all. Add further leaves if the logical space demands them; where a school has internal variants, either separate them or say why they cannot be separated.

Then report any pair of leaves your tree cannot actually distinguish, and at least three combinations of answers that no identifiable position occupies, each with a judgement about whether the cell is empty because the view is incoherent, unfashionable, or merely unexplored.

Set out the tree itself as indented plain text, followed by a JSON object with exactly these fields: "leaves", an array of objects with "label" (the position's name), "path" (the sequence of yes/no answers that reaches it), "paradigm_figures" (empty if none), and "awkward_commitment" (one sentence on what the position must accept that its own defenders find least comfortable); "indistinguishable", an array of objects with "pair" and "reason"; and "empty_cells", an array of objects with "answer_pattern", "status" (one of "incoherent", "unfashionable", "unexplored"), and "diagnosis".
