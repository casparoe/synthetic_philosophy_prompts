A common gloss on Strawson's Freedom and Resentment is that it dissolves the free will problem rather than settling it: resentment, indignation, and guilt are natural, non-optional responses that we cannot revise in light of metaphysics, so the truth or falsity of determinism simply drops out of the justification for holding people responsible. On that reading, what substantive question is left for the compatibility debate — what could Frankfurt-style cases and the consequence argument still be disagreeing about, if not the intelligibility of responsibility itself?
