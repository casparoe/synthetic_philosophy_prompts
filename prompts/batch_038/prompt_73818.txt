Then he dies, three years before the decision, and in the meantime she's come into her own money and has no ties left to anybody who could make trouble for her. She knows exactly what he did. She's spent years going over her own wants trying to work out which ones are hers, and she can't get a clean answer.

Constraints: max 150 words, one paragraph, no brainwashing or sci-fi, no plantation imagery, and the shaping has to be done entirely through control of her environment — I want Berlin's own test (obstacles deliberately placed in my way by other people) to be satisfied at the time and to come up empty afterward.
