When I proposed treating the deletion as more than technical housekeeping — a scripted farewell, an export of each companion's history, an acknowledgment in public comms — a senior colleague dismissed the whole proposal with "it's just a game." I'd rather answer him precisely than emotionally, but I suspect that sentence is doing several different jobs at once and I can't yet tell them apart.

Could you lay out the distinct claims the phrase might be making in this context, from ones I'd happily concede to ones that would be genuinely radical? Please also flag any reading that would prove too much, ruling out things my colleague would certainly not accept. Organize it as a menu for a design review rather than a single verdict, and close by saying which one or two readings he was most likely reaching for.
