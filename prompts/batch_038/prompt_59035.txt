Third-year philosophy of science PhD here, trying to nail down the backbone of a paper on psychiatric kinds before I send it off to a workshop. My argument runs: Hacking's looping effects mean a diagnostic category can't be a real kind, because once it's published and starts getting applied, the people who get labled change in ways that shift who counts as falling under it — the target moves, so there's nothing stable for the category to be true of. That feels like it settles the realism debate about DSM categories in the anti-realists favour, and my paper is basically just defending that inference against objections. Am I missing a step, or is the inference solid? And if it is solid, does the same argument do the same work for race, or is something different going on there?
