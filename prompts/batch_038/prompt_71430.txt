He argues that this makes the feature a pure measurement problem requiring no value judgments: you fix the spec, you measure deviation, done. On his view, anyone who says health is value-laden is just confusing engineering with politics.

Please audit the analogy. Three specific worries I'd like addressed: where the spec comes from if there is no designer and no reference implementation; what happens if the baseline is defined statistically from the same population you then measure individuals against, including the fact that a "spec" for a seventy-year-old differs from one for a twenty-year-old; and whether a value judgment re-enters at threshold-setting even if everything upstream is purely descriptive.
