Here's my opening. We've built a paradigm I've been calling silent broadcast, and I think it's a problem for you specifically, not for consciousness studies generally.

Structure: subjects learn a brand-new arbitrary rule at the top of each 40-trial block, given verbally and re-randomized, e.g. "curly shape means squeeze the left bulb, spiky means squeeze the right." Nothing overtrained. They then do a demanding fixation task — running digit addition, continuous — while task-irrelevant shapes flash unmasked for 200ms in the periphery. The shapes are never mentioned in the block and no response is ever requested to them. Instead, 1 to 3 seconds after each flash, a tone sounds, and the tone is the cue to squeeze. So to get it right they have to have held the shape across the delay and composed it with the just-learned instruction.

Results. Tone-cued squeeze accuracy is 84%, flat across the delay interval and across the block. Those same trials carry the full late ignition profile — the late parietal positivity at your usual latency and amplitude, with the same trial-by-trial coupling to accuracy you get in conditions where people report seeing things. But report is dead. Mid-block probe trials, interleaved and counterbalanced, with payment tied to report accuracy: subjects say nothing appeared at that location, and forced two-alternative choice on which shape it was sits at 49%, indistinguishable from catch trials where we flashed nothing. Confidence at floor. And the content doesn't transfer — introduce a second novel rule later in the session, "curly means say high," and performance on it is at chance.

Controls, before you ask. Masked-subliminal version of the same shapes: squeeze at 52%, no late positivity. Fully attended version: squeeze at 93%, late positivity, report at 96%. So the middle condition isn't a threshold artifact, it's a dissociation.

So here's the fork. Broadcasting on your account is availability to many consumer systems, and this content is available to exactly one — the squeeze system — which is why it can't support report, confidence, or the second rule. If that means it wasn't broadcast, then your own neural marker fires without broadcasting, and the marker stops being evidence for anything. If it means it was broadcast, then you're committed to saying these subjects are conscious of a shape they sincerely deny seeing and can't beat chance on, which makes first-person report useless as evidence — and report is the load-bearing wall of your whole measurement strategy. Either way the thing doing the explanatory work is a one-consumer channel, so I don't see what "global" is buying you.

for what its worth my own read is that availability comes in degrees and is always availability-to-some-system, so the experience here is real but narrow. Tell me why that's incoherent, or why it isn't.
