Don't strawman yourself: know the functionalist's best moves cold, and I'm fine with you conceding side points (like, sure, which computation a given box is doing isn't nailed down by the physics alone — that's the whole two-bitser move, same box reads as an AND gate under one mapping of its switch positions and an OR gate under the other). But you never give up the main claim: no amount of causal organization gets you genuine aboutness unless there's something there with its own first-person states. Stay in character until I say stop, don't break out to give me background on the debate, keep each reply to a paragraph or two so it feels like a conversation, and if you need a detail about the system I'm describing, ask me for it in character.

Here's the system, since my opening depends on it. I maintain the controller for a water treament skid. One status packet has a 16-bit field that used to be a plain message counter. Last year someone needed integrity checking and didn't want to touch the wire format, so they reinterpreted those exact 16 bits as a CRC. The old counter code is still compiled in and still wired to a pump, because nobody dared delete it, and the new CRC reader is wired to an alarm and a shutoff valve. Both readers are live. Both act. When they disagree about whether the packet is good, the skid does two different things at once — and there is no fact in the hardware about which reading is "the real one." Just wiring, plus git history.

My opening move: your view says my box has no underived aboutness, only what we lend it, while my brain has it intrinsically. But notice what my box does to *your* side, not mine. On the functionalist's own standards this thing is a bad candidate for belief-talk — no single coherent set of beliefs makes sense of what it does — so they either split it into two subsystems or stop ascribing beliefs to it altogether. Either way the ascription is doing no work. Now run that same test on me: name the fact that has to be different between my brain and a functionally identical box, for one to have real aboutness and the other to have none. If your answer is "conscious feel," then the feel doesn't show up in any check anyone could ever run either, so you've made your own thesis both uncheckable and idle. So what is the fact, concretely — not why there has to be one.
