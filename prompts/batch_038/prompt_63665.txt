I'm presenting a chapter of my dissertation at our department's metaphysics work-in-progress seminar next Thursday, and I would rather have a Q&A prep sheet than another defense of the view. The chapter argues that grounding's anti-symmetry is not a structural datum of the relation but an artifact of individuating facts finely: once facts are individuated coarsely, by their worldly constituents rather than by the conceptual roles that pick them out, some genuine grounding cycles become admissible — a fact can stand in a finite grounding chain back to itself. My repair is not to allow cycles across the board but to restrict anti-symmetry to a distinguished subclass of grounding claims, and to redefine fundamentality accordingly: the fundamental is not the ungrounded but what is a fixed point of that restriction. I claim this preserves the two things grounding was bought to deliver, asymmetry of explanation and a workable notion of the fundamental.

The room: two senior metaphysicians who are deflationists about grounding — one treats "ground" as a placeholder for a heterogeneous family of specific relations and resents being handed axioms for a single relation, and the other thinks the whole project should be run in explicitly hyperintensional terms and will demand to know what "ground" is doing that "because" is not. One visitor works on formal semantics for grounding, will want to know whether my restricted subclass is recursively specifiable, and will suspect it collapses into triviality. Four or five graduate students who accept grounding but split on whether it is worldly or conceptual.

Give me the eight or so objections I will actually get, ordered by how much damage each would do, phrased the way each of those people would phrase it — the two deflationists and the formalist should not sound alike. For each: a reply I can deliver in two minutes, what I should concede before I answer, and a note on where the reply is soft. Separately flag the objection that is fatal if handled badly, and end with the question I am hoping nobody asks — whether coarse individuation makes every cycle admissible and so destroys the explanatory asymmetry I claim to have saved.
