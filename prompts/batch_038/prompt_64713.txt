my historiography seminar just spent two weeks on why the revolution turned into the Terror and the prof keeps reaching for the same image: the skid. he says the 1789-91 liberal phase was the revolution on its road, then the war and the irruption of the Paris crowds into politics knocked it sideways — dérapage, loss of traction — and it ended up in 1793-94 somewhere it was never headed. the point he uses it for is a big one: if it skidded, the Terror was contingent, an accident of circumstances, not something already sitting in the DNA of 1789 or in the class story.

another person in the group says thats the wrong picture entirely and prefers the old one, the revolution as Saturn eating its own children — not a vehicle leaving the road but an appetite that starts with its own and never gets full. on her view the Terror is what the logic was always going to produce and the skid story is just a way of not looking at it.

can you audit both as analogies rather than as slogans? i want the correspondence tables spelled out. for the skid: what's the road, what's the vehicle, who's driving, what is traction, what plays the guardrail and what plays oncoming traffic. for the devouring image: who's the parent, who are the children, what is the appetite exactly, and where does that leave the agency of the specific people who signed the arrest warrants. then seperate the disanalogies that actually matter for the use each image is being put to — settling whether the Terror was latent or contingent — from the ones that are just decoration. i'm suspicious that the skid is doing the work his argument claims to do, since a skid presupposes a road somebody laid and a destination fixed before departure. does his conclusion survive once you subtract that? does hers, given that a parent who eats its children has no interest in stopping and no way to be full? and if you had to build a better image for a process that seems to have both an internal logic and real contingency in it, what would it be and what would it cost you. no reading list, just the analysis.
