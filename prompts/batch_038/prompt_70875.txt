ok so i run a little rule-changing game with friends, nomic style — the rules include rules about changing the rules. here's the setup, roughly as it stands in our rulebook (ignore the gaps in the numbering, we've repealed things). there were six of us playing.

R1. Immutable. No immutable rule may be repealed, amended, or transmuted.
R2. Any player may propose to repeal, amend, or transmute a mutable rule, or to create a new rule. A proposal passes on a two-thirds majority of the players voting.
R3. A rule is immutable if and only if it says so; every rule not marked immutable is mutable.
R4. If two rules conflict, neither takes precedence over the other: the conflict is to be resolved by amending one of them.
R5. Immutable. This rule may not be repealed.

last session someone proposed: "Repeal R1." It got four votes to two. Half the table said the repeal is void, because R1 flatly forbids repealing an immutable rule and R1 is immutable.
