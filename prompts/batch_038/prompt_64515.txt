2. Fixed terms mean there's no escape valve. A deadlock has to be sat on until the clock runs out, so it tends to get resolved outside the rules instead.

5. He also brought up semi-presidential systems where the president and a prime minister from the opposing camp share the executive, and seemed to think that's a feature rather than a design smell.
