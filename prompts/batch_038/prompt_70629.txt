heres my opening. Your framework takes a distribution over worlds as given and then bounds expected harm under it. But a system competent enough to be worth aligning is an optimizer: it acts, so it gets some say in which situations it ends up in. That means the distribution isnt exogenous data you sample from, its something the system can shift mass around inside — and it will shift mass toward exactly the region where its own objective is well served. So the average case you're averaging over is the one case the system has both the incentive and the means to walk out of. Doesnt your guarantee evaporate the moment the thing its about is good enough to care which world it lands in?
