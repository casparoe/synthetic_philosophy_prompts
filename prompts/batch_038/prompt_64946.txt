2. **Rejected** sortition for Student Government president. Singleton office, enormous time commitment, a contested three-way race every year, and the winner is supposed to speak for the whole student body.
3. **Approved** a draw for the 7 student seats on the study spaces and library hours advisory group. Two meetings a semester, no expertise required, and the last four "elections" for it were unopposed — nobody on the panel could name a single incumbent.

6. **Approved** a draw for the 2 student seats on the dining vendor selection committee. Three meetings total, unopposed, names not published, and the output is basically a ranked list of caterers.
7. **Rejected** it for the class representatives to the faculty curriculum committee. Low stakes and low time cost, but those reps are formally charged with canvassing their class and reporting back, and two of the four class elections were contested.
