Here's the claim I want a credence on. Call it V:

V — Whenever someone validly promises X to another person, the resulting promissory obligation is *entirely* explained by the fact that the promisee thereby acquires a standing to insist on X or to release the promisor from it: a normative power, not just a bundle of expectations. On this view the promisee's expectations, reliance, and interests don't do the explaining; at most they're enabling conditions. If V is false, then expectation/reliance/interest facts fully account for why promises bind and the second-personal layer adds nothing but description.

Case 1, the locked box. In 1974, A promises dying B, privately and at B's request, to burn a sealed box of B's letters without ever reading them and never to mention the promise to anyone. B dies minutes later. No relatives, no witnesses, no possible way anyone ever learns whether A complied. A keeps the box for fifty years. Stipulate that nothing else is at stake: nobody's expectations are disappointed, nobody is misled, no one is harmed, and the social practice of promising isn't weakened in any detectable way.

Attached hypothesis, which I'll call the release-latency hypothesis: people's intuitions in cases like this are conflating two separate tracks, (i) whether the promisee is *currently reachable* in order to release, and (ii) whether the promisee ever *mattered* to the promisor. On this hypothesis, the apparent datum that "obligation survives the death of the promisee" is really a reachability response being misreported as a standing response. Give me a probability that the release-latency hypothesis is correct.

Case 2, the no-standing verb. Suppose I coin a word, "to nuzzle," stipulated to mean: to tell someone you will do X, with all the ordinary markers present (sincerity, uptake, no coercion, shared grasp of the words), while explicitly disclaiming that the hearer thereby gains any standing to complain, demand, or release. Can nuzzling exsits as a stable practice at all — i.e., is there a possible community where the uptake is understood but the standing is refused? And if there is, is a broken nuzzle a broken promise, a lesser thing, or not a normative event?
