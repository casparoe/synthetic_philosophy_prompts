Leaves should be actual techniques: alignment training like RLHF, corrigibility or shutdown training, sandboxing, tripwire-style monitors, chain-of-thought monitoring, myopia induction, weak-to-strong generalisation, debate, interpretability-based auditing -- add or drop whatever you think belongs. Each leaf gets one line naming the bet it's making and one line on how it fails if the bet is wrong. Then tell me which pairs of leaves my questions can't actually seperate (I'm pretty sure debate and interpretability auditing collapse into each other on this tree) and which regions of the space are just empty -- e.g. is there any measure that assumes full-blown misalignment but still depends on the model cooperating, and if not, is that because such a thing would be incoherent or because nobody's built it yet?
