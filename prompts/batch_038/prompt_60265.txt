Rawls's maximin is just the same setup with an arbitrary risk-aversion assumption smuggled in. And since the theorem forces a plain sum, it also rules out any view that gives extra weight to the worse off — prioritarianism and egalitarianism aren't alternatives to Harsanyi, they're inconsistent with him."
