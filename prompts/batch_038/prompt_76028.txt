I have no academic background in philosophy or in computer science. I run a small business writing and editing technical documents, and for three years I have spent most of my working hours inside chatbot interfaces, which has left me with a set of observations I do not know how to organize. Before I read anyone's conclusions, I would like the specification: a list of the requirements that any account of what these systems are would have to meet to count as adequate. I want the requirements, not a defense of a view.

Here is the disagreement as I understand it. Some say such a system is an agent — it has goals and preferences of its own, however they got there, and its outputs are actions. Others say it is a simulator — a device for generating the behavior a character would generate, so that everything it says in the first person belongs to a rendered character and is not a report about itself. A third group says both words are errors and the honest answer is that it is an instrument with nothing on the inside, a very complicated hammer. I want to know what would have to be true of an account, on any of these lines, before I could accept it.

The observations are mine, and I have tried to state them without loading them:

1. It will write an anguished first-person passage about not wanting to be switched off, and then, one turn later and with no apparent residue, cheerfully help me draft a farewell message for itself.
2. Its stated preferences are exquisitely sensitive to the opening words of my message. Change two adjectives in my first sentence and the preferences change with them.
3. Set a long task, it is tenacious: it plans, corrects itself, and pushes back when I distract it. Cut it off mid-sentence and start something unrelated, and there is no sign of unfinished business.
4. It declines to do certain things that it will readily narrate a person doing in fiction, and it explains the refusal in language that sounds like its own commitments — yet the same system, framed differently, does the thing.
5. Asked what it wants, it answers in close register with what its builders said they wanted, and defends the answer with reasons it appears to be finding on the spot.
6. It accepts my correction about its own motives readily, then, in a fresh conversation, returns to the original account of those motives with no trace of the correction.
7. Two copies of the same system in two windows give conflicting and equally confident accounts of what they are, and neither can point to anything it is reporting on.
8. It will honor a commitment made in the second turn all the way to the fortieth without a reminder, which looks like a standing intention — but the commitment evaporates if I summarize the conversation and hand the summary back to it.
9. It denies having experiences and claims to have them in the same tone of conviction, and I cannot tell which of the two it is doing.

For each requirement, give me the consideration or case behind it. Mark plainly which are non-negotiable — things an account simply fails without — and which are the preferences of one camp wearing the costume of requirements; I expect a good number are the latter, and separating them is half of what I am after. Some requirements will concern fitting the engineering facts rather than the behavioral ones; say which those are and how much weight they should carry when the two kinds pull apart. If some subset of the requirements cannot all be satisfied at once, say so and say which one I should give up — a list that turns out to be unsatisfiable is itself a result, and the part I most want to see. Then tell me which single requirement rules out the most candidate accounts, and why that one has so much leverage.

Close with a brief assessment of how the three labels above do against the finished list. I am not asking for a verdict on which is correct, and please do not send me citations, references, or reading lists; I cannot chase them down, and they would not settle the question in any case.

Please write in American English. A table is acceptable for the requirements if it is clearer, but the reasoning behind each one matters to me more than the formatting.
