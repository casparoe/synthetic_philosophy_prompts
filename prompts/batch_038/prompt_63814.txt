Then, totally separately, I was on a long flight and watched the 1990 Tom Stoppard film of Rosencrantz and Guildenstern Are Dead (Oldman and Roth), and I've since realised the opening coin-flip scene is the best thing I've ever seen about that whole topic and nobody has ever pointed it out to me. Please tell me if I'm wrong about that, because I'd like to not be wrong.

The details as I have them from the script: Guildenstern flips, Rosencrantz announces the result, and the running count climbs through the scene — 76, then 85, then 89 — with Guildenstern at one point talking about a single coin being spun ninety times.
