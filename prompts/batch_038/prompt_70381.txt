I also think he notes that with a machine you could in principle crack the case open and read the internal state, which you obviously can't do with a child — not totally sure that's exactly his framing, so correct me if I'm mangling it.

Here's the setup that's making this itch for me. My team runs a nightly loop where a small model is the pupil and a much larger model grades its outputs and produces the fine-tuning signal. So the pupil has partial control of the teacher's evidence. Separately, we *could* dump the small model's weights and activations every night, but we've agreed not to, on the theory that peeking would bias our grading.
