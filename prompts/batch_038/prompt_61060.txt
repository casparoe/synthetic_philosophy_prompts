Hold that as a momentary state, then let the ordinary dynamics resume. The student's questions: does the entropy of the box change at that instant, and could an observer sealed inside the box, limited to local measurements, detect that the doubling happened? The conclusion the student wants: temperature is not a property of a momentary microstate, so any theory that treats it that way is wrong — and we get that result without performing a single experiment.
