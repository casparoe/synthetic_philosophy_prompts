I run the grants committee for a small community foundation — we hand out about 40 checks a year, $2k to $15k each, to local projects. Five years ago it was 5 of us. Now it's 23, mostly because I kept recruiting people, on the theory that more votes means better decisions. Lately I've been doubting that.

I keep a spreadsheet. For every vote we've ever taken I've recorded whether the project actually panned out two years later — finished what it said it would, still running, no scandal. Crude, I know, but it's something. Sorting by member, the picture is: 9 people are right about 70% of the time, 11 are right about 55%, and 3 are right about 40%. Those last three aren't dumb, they just flatly refuse one whole category — anything that looks like harm reduction, needle exchange, supervised use, that kind of thing — and vote no on it regardless of what's actually in the application.

Two other things I can't work out.

First, we all vote off the same packet. One part-time staffer writes a two-page summary of each application and that is literally all most members ever see. Only a handful of us have set foot at a project site in five years. So when her summary gets a project wrong, we all get it wrong in the same direction, together.

Second, one of my better members admitted to me he sometimes votes against his own read on a project, because "my vote only counts when it's close, and if it's close, the others probably saw something I missed." I want to tell him that's backwards — vote what you actually think — but I couldnt come up with a reason that would convince him, and honestly part of me wonders if he's onto something.

So: is the 23-person committee genuinely better than the old five, or have I been making it worse for five years? And what would you actually change — who's on it, how many, what they're shown, how they vote? I'm also tempted to stop doing yes/no votes and have everyone rank all the applications instead, if that helps. Don't give me generic committee hygiene stuff, I want to know whether the arithmetic of how I'm aggregating these votes is sound.
