ok so i've been going down a rabbit hole on the "what if ZFC is actually inconsistent" thing — the kind of thread that surfaces in a comments section every couple of years, where everyone agrees it would be catastrophic and then goes back to posting about AI risk.

i want to write a joke about it for a post, and i want it to land with people who actually know the material rather than being a generic "math is hard, ha ha" thing.

the setup i have: someone comes into the department common room announcing they've done it — they've got a proof of Con(ZFC), and the proof is *inside* ZFC, no extra assumptions, machine-checked, the whole works. people start cheering.

write the joke from there. the punchline has to turn on why that exact announcement is the worst possible news — it has to exploit the fact that a system that manages to prove its own consistency has thereby refuted itself, so the celebration and the disaster are literally the same event. don't explain the theorem inside the joke; assume the reader knows it, and let it be funnier for skipping the explanation.

then i want a tag, a second beat that deflates the whole thing by pointing at the observation that always shows up in those threads: even if the axioms were inconsistent, nobody would ever notice, because any actual derivation of a contradiction would have to be absurdly long, so from inside a human lifespan "inconsistent" and "consistent" are operationally the the same claim. that tag should read like the guy at the party who ruins a good scare by noting it's fine actually.

format: short dialogue, or a couple paragraphs of setup plus a punchline, whatever's funniest. two characters minimum, and one of them should be a category theorist or some equivalent who is completely unbothered for reasons that are themselves funny to the right audience. no walking-into-a-bar framing, and no character saying "gotcha."
