Example two. A utility company in my state flipped everyone onto a green tariff by default. So the pattern I want a name for is this. A change to the choice situation alters what people want, or at least what they report wanting, and then the fact that people want it gets cited as the reason the change was legitimate. The warrant is produced by the thing it's supposed to warrant. And it's unusually hard to attack, because you can't observe what people would have wanted otherwise — there's no clean baseline, and every survey you could run gets run on people who've already been through the machine.
