There's really only one way to dodge it, which is to say that the extra people don't count unless they're better off than the average — and Parfit demolished that too, in what's known as the non-identity problem, showing it's false that you can't harm somebody by bringing them into existence. So the pessimists about population growth have the ethics upside down, and the practical upshot is that we should all be having a lot more children."
