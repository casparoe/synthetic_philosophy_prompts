Our little probability-and-philosophy meetup group (five or six of us, meets in someone's living room) spent last night on the doomsday argument, and it ended with everyone saying "you're not special" at each other for twenty minutes without it meaning the same thing twice. Here's roughly what happened.

Dave set up Gott's version: take the number of humans born so far, call it n, assume your birth rank is a random draw from all N humans who will ever live, and you get with 95% confidence that N is less than about 20n — so no future with trillions upon trillions of people, no galaxy-filling civilization, no uploads. He kept saying the argument isn't really an argument at all, it's just "you're not special" plus arithmetic, and that anyone who resists the conclusion is resisting the slogan.

Then two pushbacks he waved off with the same slogan. (1) Mira said: but I *do* know things that locate me — I know it's the 2020s, that we have the internet and no Mars colony and no uploaded minds, and that's just ordinary evidence about where in history I'm sitting, not special pleading. Dave said using it *is* special pleading, because it's only special relative to the assumption you're not allowed to have. (2) Tom said the whole thing collapses on reference class: I can always cook up a class where I'm in the first percentile — everyone who has ever flown on a plane, everyone who has ever had their genome sequenced, everyone who's ever scrolled TikTok — so if "not special" means uniform over my reference class, the conclusion depends entirely on which class I picked, and the arithmetic isn't doing any work. Dave said you're not allowed to gerrymander the class.

And there was a third moment: someone mentioned that under the self-indication style assumption you ARE special in a precise sense — if there are going to be trillions of observers, it's more likely that you're one of them, and that effect apparently offsets the doomsday reasoning more or less exactly — and Dave's response was again just "well, that's denying you're not special." Which felt like it conceded the point while pretending to win it.
