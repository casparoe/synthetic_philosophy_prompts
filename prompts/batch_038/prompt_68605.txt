The regulars can recite the opposition's positions better than the opposition can. Nobody gatekeeps information. If you suggested they were shutting themselves off from other views they'd be genuinely insulted, and they'd have receipts.

There's also an informal pecking order based on who finds the best specimens, and a newcomer earns standing by bringing one in.

And the group punishes exactly two things. If you stop reading the other side, people notice and get weird about it, you're assumed to be losing your nerve. But if you read constantly and get moved by any of it, you're done, you're out, treated worse than somebody who never showed up at all. So ignorance is suspect and conversion is fatal, and the endless digestion of the enemy's arguments is the one activity guaranteed to be safe.
