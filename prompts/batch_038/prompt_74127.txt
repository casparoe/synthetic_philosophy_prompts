I am drafting a section of a paper on Parfit's priority view and need a purpose-built case rather than a schematic one. Please construct a thought experiment to the following specifications and then demonstrate that it meets each of them.

**Purpose of the case.** To show that the view Parfit offers as the escape from the leveling-down objection buys that escape by giving up something the egalitarian was trying to protect: a complaint made on behalf of the worst off that does not shrink as others gain.

**Specifications.**

1. One realistic institutional choice — a regional health authority, a municipal budget, a disaster-relief fund — not a trolley, not a lifeboat, not organ harvesting. Scene-setting should fit in two or three sentences.
2. A fixed population, described as two or more groups with stated sizes, and two outcomes with stated cardinal wellbeing levels for each group, on some explicit metric (welfare points, QALYs, and so on). The same people exist in both outcomes.
3. The move from outcome 1 to outcome 2 must raise total wellbeing, so that it is not a leveling down in any sense.
4. That move must also leave the worst-off group strictly worse off.
5. Prioritarianism — the sum of nᵢ f(wᵢ), for a strictly increasing, strictly concave f that you choose and state explicitly — must rank outcome 2 better.
6. Telic egalitarianism, and a maximin reading of Rawls's difference principle, must both rank outcome 1 better.
7. Neither verdict may be an artifact of a knife-edge parameter. Report the range of concavity of f over which (5) holds and the range of inequality aversion over which (6) holds, and say plainly whether the case works at moderate values of both or only at extreme ones.

**Then the analysis.** Say which structural feature of the case generates the divergence, and test your answer by removing that feature and reporting what happens. Give the prioritarian's best counterattack, and in particular address the charge that the case only works because the specification smuggles in a privileged cardinal scale for wellbeing: show whether the prioritarian verdict in (5) survives an affine rescaling of the numbers — a shifted zero point, say — and draw the implication for whether this is a genuine objection or a numbering artifact. Finish with a committed position, not a survey: does the case show that prioritarianism escapes the leveling-down objection only by collapsing toward what the objection was aimed at, or is it unfair to the view? Take a side, give reasons, and say whether the case in fact distinguishes telic egalitarianism from maximin or merely re-stages the familiar maximin-versus-priority dispute.

**Format.** Use these sections, in this order: *The scenario* (one paragraph, ~150 words); *Verdicts and arithmetic* (~300 words, numbers shown); *Robustness* (~200 words); *What does the work* (~150 words); *The prioritarian's counterattack* (~250 words); *My verdict* (~150 words).
