I'm putting together an ethics unit on character for my intro class and I keep coming back to The Dark Knight, because the Joker's whole bit is basically the situationist critique — one bad day and the most upright person drops their principles, morality is just a bad joke away from collapsing, and Harvey Dent going from white knight to two-face is the exhibit. The thing is the movie doesn't just assert that, it runs the experiment: two ferries, the civilians and the prisoners each holding the other's detonator, a deadline, and nobody presses the button. So my worry is that the film sets up one big test of situationism and quietly fails it, while passing every small-scale version of the same test — the bank crew turning on each other for a bigger share, the cops, the mob, the mayor's office, Dent himself. Is that a fair reading or am I forcing it?
