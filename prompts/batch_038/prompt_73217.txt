Analyze Alex Jones's deplatforming as a case of punishment. Not as a free speech case — I want the punishment-theory mapping. Facts so you don't have to guess at them: for years he ran Infowars and pushed the claim that Sandy Hook was staged with crisis actors; in August 2018 Apple, Facebook, YouTube and Spotify removed his accounts and content, with Twitter following a few weeks later; four years after that, juries in Texas and Connecticut awarded the families something like a billion and a half dollars in defamation damages; he filed for bankruptcy; and one of the platforms reinstated him later under new ownership.

So run the definition first. Lay out Hart's conditions for what counts as punishment (paraphrase, don't quote) and take them one at a time against the 2018 removal: who is the punishing authority here, what is the offense, what is the hard treatment, where is the procedure, and does the private-property defense ("they were just enforcing their own terms of service") defeat the classification or dodge it? Then the justification side — does the deplatforming look like retribution, general deterrence, incapacitation, or Duff's communicative penance? My hunch is that incapacitation is the one that maps cleanly and the communicative story collapses, since exclusion is the opposite of addressing the offender as someone you want back; tell me where that hunch is wrong. Also handle the notice problem — no published tariff, no advance warning of what speech costs what — and the standing-to-blame problem, given that those same platforms profited from his reach for years before cutting him off.

Then the harder question. Compare the two sanctions: the 2018 removal and the 2022 verdicts both hurt him enormously, but only one is obviously punishment. Say what makes the difference, and whether the fact that the informal one could be undone by a single owner's decision years later shows it was never punishment at all, or just punishment without a sovereign. And mark clearly where the theories run out — I'd rather you say a theory is silent than make it speak. No reading list at the end, and its fine to end without a verdict on whether the sanction was justified.
