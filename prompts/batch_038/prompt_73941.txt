- Two equally careful friends get different totals splitting a bill: she says you move about halfway toward the other one.
- The altitude case, where the guy has checked his numbers three times and nothing feels off from the inside, but his climbing partner points out that at that elevation people reliably get sloppy with figures: she says he should stop trusting the calculation anyway.
- Same setup except the person warning you about your impairment is a known prankster and you have solid independent reason to think he's lying: she says no change at all, dismiss it.
- Anyone who says "p, but it'd be irrational of me to believe p": she calls that combination flatly irrational, and gets annoyed at people who say it's only a problem for assertion.

What will she say he should do? And which principle is he actually — she is running?
