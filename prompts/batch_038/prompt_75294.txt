Nobody there reads fiction for a living. My thesis is that violence isn't a natural kind you count so much as a concept that gets stretched or shrunk to fit whatever is countable, and I want to propose a criterion: an act is violence when it forecloses the victim's imagined futures — options they can no longer picture themselves as having — so chronic fear, coerced "consent," and displacement count even with zero tissue damage. I'll also say the decline in violence is real in the body-count sense, and that this is exactly why the concept question matters rather than a dodge of it.
