Doing a philosophy of biology module this term and we've spent the last four weeks on the units of selection thing and honestly my brain is fried. Our tutor said we could put something silly in the revision pack we're compiling for the first-years, so I want to write a parody of a corporate executive-search document pack — but the whole joke depends on the technical content being exactly right, so please don't hand-wave the biology or the philosophy.

The premise: Darwin & Sons (Executive Search) have been retained to fill the vacancy of **Unit of Selection** — full time, permanent, reporting directly to Natural Selection. Write it as the actual internal document pack HR would circulate, in about 750 words, deadpan throughout, no winking, no jokes signposted as jokes. The comedy has to come entirely from the panel applying genuine HR-speak (competency frameworks, "essential/desirable", culture fit, references, salary band, right to work) to real technical criteria — and from the panel never noticing that it has been begged the question.

It should have four parts:

1. **The Person Specification.** The essential criteria should literally be the conditions something must satisfy to be a unit of selection — heritable variation, differential fitness, and whatever you think the faithful-replication requirement adds on top of those. Include the moment where HR has quietly drafted the criteria so one particular applicant cannot lose, plus a footnote conceding the specification was written by an applicant.

2. **Shortlisting notes** on the candidates, one tight paragraph each. I want: the Gene, the Individual Organism, the Group (or deme), the Clonal Lineage, the Species, and the Developmental System, plus a late application from the Meme. The rejections have to be the real objections, not strawmen. So the Individual Organism's fatal flaw is that it doesn't reproduce faithfully — meiosis and segregation shred it every generation and the panel should say so in the tone of someone explaining a gap in a CV. The Group's rejection should turn on the ambiguity about whether the group-level model is one where groups are merely a context for individual-level advantage, or one where groups genuinely reproduce and beget daughter groups — the panel should be annoyed that the candidate answered a different question to the one asked, and should note that no mechanism of group heredity was supplied. The Species should be rejected for having no contactable referee. The Meme should be turned down because nobody can locate its physical vehicle and its copying fidelity is embarrassing. The Developmental System technically satisfies every criterion and is therefore the panel's problem child: unemployably large, and it wants paying in a currency nobody in the department has.

3. **A withdrawal notice.** Two applicants — the Replicator and the Interactor — withdraw jointly, on the grounds that they've been conflated throughout and are not, in fact, rival candidates for the same post. HR then re-advertises the role as two part-time positions plus an outsourced "fitness bookkeeping" consultancy, since it emerges that most of the dispute was about which level fitness gets assigned to in the first place.

4. **A closing paragraph from the chair of the panel**, conceding — in the blandest possible corporate register — that every candidate arrived with its own job description, and that the panel has been adjudicating with a definition of selection chosen after seeing the applications.

Also put in a short equal-opportunities statement welcoming applications from asexual lineages, holobionts, and organisms with rampant horizontal gene transfer, with the panel noting these applicants would first need to say what they are.

Two constraints: someone who's never heard of the debate should still read it as a coherent, faintly tedious recruitment pack — the technical content should be carried by the HR framing rather than explained to the reader — and please keep it in British English throughout, including the HR register (CVs not résumés, "person specification", "the successful candidate will be appointed on", that kind of thing).
