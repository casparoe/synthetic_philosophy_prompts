A friend sent me an article about Samuel Scheffler's book Death and the Afterlife (the 2013 Oxford one, out of the Tanner Lectures) and I read it on a flight and it did something to me. My understanding, which may well be wrong: his afterlife isn't heaven, it's just humanity going on after you die. The afterlife conjecture being something like: we're far more invested in humanity continuing than we are in our own continuing. Where I'm fuzzy is whether the point is that your projects would become literally pointless, or that you'd lose your confidence that they were ever valuable — those feel like different claims and I dont know which one he actually makes. Please sort that out for me as part of the answer, because the speech depends on it and I'm not going to build five minutes on something I've half-remembered from a magazine article.
