I'm trying to figure out where I actually stand and I've realised I can't, because I don't have a number, just a mood. So give me one.

Here's the proposition I want a credence on. I've tried to make it operational enough to be true or false rather than vibe-y: **P**: if an ordinary, non-nihilistic, reasonably reflective adult became firmly convinced — not vaguely worried, actually convinced past serious doubt — that all of humanity will be wiped out about twenty-five years after their own death, in a way that is instant and painless for everyone involved and kills nobody they will ever have met, then one year later that person would report, sincerely and on reflection, that at least three of their five most important ongoing projects now seem substantially less worth carrying on with than they did before. I want your number, but mostly I want it taken apart: a rough probability for each piece P depends on, one line of justification each, and then how they combine.
