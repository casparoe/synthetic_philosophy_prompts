I understand that proposal to rest on roughly three moves: one that leans on the striking difficulty of producing viable alternatives to the theory, one that generalizes from the track record of the research field itself (theories that once looked uniquely promising and then delivered), and one that points to unexpected explanatory connections between parts of the framework, where a piece built for one purpose turns out to account for something it was never aimed at — a microscopic accounting of black hole entropy is the example I keep seeing. I also understand that the multiverse version of this runs into a measure problem: nobody can say how to weight the different universes.
