Give me a scene where a trapped caver has maybe an hour of air while the drone works it's way down the ladder one rung at a time, with the current budget number ticking down in its log lines, and I want the ending to turn on whether the bottom rung turns out to be too dumb to ask and just acts on a threshold instead of a proof. Don't explain any of the math to the reader — it should show up only as behavior, radio chatter, and log output. 600 words max, and if you go over I'll be annoyed.
