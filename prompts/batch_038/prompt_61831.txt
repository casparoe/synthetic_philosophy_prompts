Hofstadter's careenium: a vat full of little magnetic balls (sims) that careen around and clump together into bigger structures (simmballs), where the bounciness of the walls varies with whats going on outside the vat, so that the clumps end up tracking things and setting each other off in patterns that look for all the world like symbols standing for stuff and entering into meaningful relations. His answer to who shoves whom around inside the careenium is that the question is bogus -- the sims and the simmballs are one and the same causal process seen at two zoom levels, so there's no competition between them and nothing to adjudicate.

Argue against that. Argue that there really is a fact of the matter about which level does the shoving, and that in the careenium it comes out for the simmballs -- the symbol level is causally privileged, not just a coarse-grained redescription of the ball level. Constraints on the argument: you can't wave at downward causation, you can't say the two levels overdetermine the effect, and you can't make it depend on consciousness or on the simmballs having some special kind of intentionality the sims lack. I want to know what, in the setup as I've described it, actually earns the higher level the right to be called the cause rather than a way of talking. End with where you think your own argument is softest.
