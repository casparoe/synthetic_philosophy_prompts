So Adam Smith thinks conscience works by installing an impartial spectator in your chest — an internalized audiance built up out of being watched by people who can watch you back. He's also adamant that wanting praise and wanting to be praise-worthy are two different desires. Given both of those, what do you reckon he'd make of Reddit karma or an Instagram follower count: an audience that's huge, mostly anonymous, and that hands down its verdict as a single number you can refresh? Would Smith say the spectator gets stronger when a million people are looking at you, or does the mechanism break down because none of them actually know who you are? He also never had to handle the fact that you can pick your spectators and hide from the ones who'd disapprove. Pick a side and argue for it rather then splitting the difference.
