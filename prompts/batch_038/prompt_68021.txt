I run a small council-funded hardship fund — discretionary, one-off grants, maybe £40k a quarter: rent arrears, a funeral, a cooker, travel to interviews. Three of us on the panel, two are volunteers, we get about 25 cases a week with maybe 15 mintues each and no investigative powers beyond reading the form and occasionally ringing the claimant back.

The funder's guidance says awards have to be needs-based and not character-based. But our form asks why someone lost their job and whether they're actively looking, so in practice we make exactly the deserving/undeserving call the guidance forbids — just invisibly, as a hunch about who sounds genuine.
