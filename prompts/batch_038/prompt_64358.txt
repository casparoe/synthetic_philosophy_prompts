I know that section reasonably well: the child program as something with not much in it that gets taught by a teacher using something like praise and blame, the aside about building in a random element, the insistence that you'd fit it out with good sensors and then teach it to understand and speak English, and the related claim later in the paper that a machine can surprise us because our own calculations about its behaviour are too fallible to finish.
