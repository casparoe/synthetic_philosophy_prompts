On the other side you've got the patchers: Edgington indexing the principle to actuality so the possibility operator doesn't slide, Kvanvig restricting it to propositions that aren't already cluttered up with epistemic notions, and then Brogaard and Salerno arguing that any restriction like that just begs the question.
