Everybody who sat down consented to a world where deception is a legal move, so calling it immoral is a category error, and moderating it would be like a casino banning someone for a successful bluff. Half the council bought it and the do-nothing side is winning.

Then the main question: does "don't moderate" survive once the disanalogies are on the table, or was the analogy carrying the whole argument the entire way?
