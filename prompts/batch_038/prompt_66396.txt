Here is a pattern I keep running into in arguments for randomly selected citizen bodies, and I do not know whether it has a name.

The body is praised as a statistical portrait of the population: because it is drawn by lot and stratified, it is said to contain in miniature the same distribution of ages, incomes, regions, and starting opinions as the country at large, so that whatever it concludes carries the weight of what the public itself would conclude if the public had the time and the information. But when the same body's conclusions diverge sharply from what the public actually says — an assembly backs a carbon tax that a popular vote would reject, or endorses an electoral reform that polls show losing — the divergence is not counted against the mirroring claim. It is presented as the point: months of testimony, cross-examination, and facilitated small-group work have corrected the misinformation and short horizons that an opinion poll merely records, so the assembly shows what the public's judgment looks like once it is informed.

The upshot is that agreement with the polls counts as evidence the design works (this is us), disagreement with the polls also counts as evidence the design works (this is a better us), and nothing appears to determine which reading is in force on any given occasion.

So: is there an established concept for this? I know part of the terrain — I have come across the distinction between a body's representing people by resembling them and its representing them by being authorized by them, and I know that deliberative democrats distinguish what people happen to think from what they think after discussion — but neither of those seems to capture what I am describing, which is the switching between the two standards together with the absence of any rule for when each one applies. Tell me what the nearest established concepts are, how each differs from what I have described, and which fits best. If nothing fits, say so plainly and coin a term: give a definition, specify its boundary cases, and say what feature of a design would render it immune to the charge. I am not looking for a bare label or a reading list; I want the phenomenon carved correctly, including where it stops being an unobjectionable feature of a two-stage justification and starts being a way of insulating a proposal from any evidence at all.
