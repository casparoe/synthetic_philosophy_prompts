I am writing on whether cultural facts can figure as causes in social explanation, with Weber's Protestant ethic thesis as my test case. Here is the proposition I want a number on. In the best defensible version of that thesis, ascetic Protestant doctrine — the anxiety generated by predestination, the conception of a calling, the treatment of methodical work and accumulation as signs of election — plays a causal role in producing the modern orientation to systematic, rationally calculated profit-seeking which is *not eliminable* in favor of (a) the material interests of the actors and (b) structural variables that merely correlate with confessional membership, such as literacy and schooling, inheritance and land-tenure rules, refugee and trade networks, prior levels of urbanization and commercial development, or the incentive structure facing a religious minority. "Not eliminable" means: if you delete the doctrinal variable from the best available explanation of the observed confessional differentials in economic conduct, something in the explanandum is left unaccounted for.

Give me your credence in that proposition, decomposed rather than asserted. I want at least the following terms, each with a number and one line of justification: the probability that reasons or belief-contents can be causes at all in a non-deviant, non-eliminable sense; the probability that Weber's own mechanism is irreducible given that it runs through psychological sanctions — dread of damnation managed by pastoral counsel — and so looks like interests wearing doctrinal clothing; the probability that the historical record can discriminate doctrine-first from interest-first, given that there is no counterfactual early modern Europe without the Reformation; and the probability that "the spirit of capitalism" is determinate enough as an explanandum that the thesis could genuinely have come out false. Add any further term you think belongs, including any that cut in the thesis's favor.

Then show how the terms combine into your bottom line — a product, a weighted mixture, or whatever structure you think is honest — and tell me which term that bottom line is most sensitive to, and what sort of consideration would move *that* term the most. Lead with the final number, and keep the whole response under 500 words.
