Been on my college's traditions committee for six years now and there's a thing I keep running into that I don't have a word for. Almost everything we call a tradition here is openly recent. The Founder's Mug business started in 1994 — there's a memo about it, and the dean who came up with it is still alive and tells the origin story at every alumni weekend. Our town's harvest fair dates to 2003 and nobody has ever claimed otherwise; the historical society has the flyer from year one in a drawer. And yet these behave exactly like the genuinely ancient ones: first-years go through them and suddenly feel like they belong, people are weirdly protective of the details, alumni who skipped the actual events will fight you over the wording of the toast, and any suggestion of retiring one get's shot down as an attack on "tradition."

The part I can't place is that nobody's confused and nobody's faking it. Everyone knows the mug was invented in the nineties, and knowing that costs the practice nothing — if anything people bring it up fondly, like it's part of the charm. The made-up-ness is out in the open and the thing still holds people together.

So: is this a recognized pattern with a name of its own? I know Hobsbawm's invented tradition is the nearby idea, but as I understand it that one turns on people claiming continuity with a past that isn't actually there, and here there's no such claim, so I'm not sure it reaches the case. How does what I'm describing sit relative to ordinary custom or convention, and relative to situations where everyone is knowingly going along with a shared make-believe? If nothing established fits, coin a term and define it. I care less about the label than the edges — what counts as this, what doesn't, and what feature would break it if you removed it.

Approach this as someone who actually works in social philosophy and heritage/memory studies: run through the candidate concepts first and knock out the ones that don't fit before you settle on anything. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence — does a term exist or not, and which one — then give the reasoning. No preamble and no restating my question back at me.
