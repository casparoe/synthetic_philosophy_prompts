I have been thinking about Brave New World alongside the wireheading and reward-tampering literature, and I have worked myself into a distinction I cannot evaluate. The technical discussions seem to run together two very different things: an agent that tampers with the reward signal it receives, and an agent whose preferences have been rewritten so thoroughly that the signal is never tampered with at all, because it is honestly reporting on wants that were manufactured for it. I have been calling the second one preference laundering, and my worry is that the World State is entirely a case of it. Nobody there is stimulating a pleasure centre; they swallow a drug, they were whispered at in their sleep as children, and by the end they want exactly what they are handed.

Mustapha Mond looks to me like the case that decides it. He keeps the Bible and Shakespeare locked in a safe, tells the Savage that he was once nearly sent to an island for pursuing his own research and took the Controller's post instead, and administers to everyone else a comfort he has declined for himself. Is he an un-wireheaded optimiser who knows precisely what he has traded away, or is his position a more refined version of the same trick — a man whose reward now comes from being the only one who understands the system?

I would like you to say where my distinction holds and where the book breaks it. Bernard and Helmholtz are miserable despite everything done to them, and the sleep-teaching experiments early on show Huxley knew that conditioning sticks to feeling and association rather than to knowledge: does that mean he thinks preferences cannot be laundered all the way down, so that Mond's stability is a holding action rather than a solution? And how should John's flogging of himself at the lighthouse read on this account — penance, or an attempt to keep one honest channel of reward and punishment open?

Take the novel and the reward-tampering idea equally seriously, tell me plainly whether the distinction survives contact with the book or collapses into a difference without a distinction, and end with what Huxley contributes to the wireheading debate that the technical framing misses. Please discuss scenes and characters rather than reproducing lines; I do not have the text to hand.
