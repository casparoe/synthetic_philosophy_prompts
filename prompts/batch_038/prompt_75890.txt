Our reading group is doing personal identity next month and I've been told to write my actual views down so everyone can attack them. Heres where I've landed:

1. Reductionism. A person is just a brain and body and a series of interrelated mental and physical events. There aren't any further facts about identity over and above that.
2. What matters in survival isn't identity, it's Relation R — psychological connectedness and continuity, with whatever reliable cause happens to produce them, holding to any degree.
3. So plain teletransportation isn't death. My body here gets destroyed, a replica wakes up on Mars with all my memories and plans and intentions, and I'd step into the machine without hesitation, because that's survival or as good as it.
4. In the fission case, both successors have everything that matters, neither is identical to me (identity is one-one, R isn't), and the outcome still isn't worse than ordinary survival. I'm at peace with that.
5. Death is bad for the person who dies, and the whole of the badness is that it deprives them of the good life they'd otherwise have had.
6. How much reason I have to care about some future stage should track how strongly R holds between me now and that stage. Full concern for tomorrow's me, considerably less for a me forty years out that I'll have almost nothing in common with.

And a case I made up that I can't shake. Call it the slow fade: a machine that over ten years replaces 99% of my psychology — memories, character, values — with those of a completely different person, while my body stays alive and physically continuous the whole time. No killing, no gap, no branching, nobody else involved. I think that's worse than being teletransported and a lot closer to death, and I'd refuse it.

Go through all this and tell me which pairs genuinely pull against each other, ranked by how bad the problem is, and what I'd have to give up to fix each — including whether my verdict on the slow fade actually follows from 1–6 or is a gut reaction I've bolted on afterwards. Also flag the ones that only look like problems: I keep worrying that 2 and 6 commit me to vague identity, and I know there's a standing argument that identity can't be vague. Then tell me which single revision would clean up the most.
