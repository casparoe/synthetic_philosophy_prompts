I have been at my company for four years and have never taken more than a week of vacation at a time, even though our handbook states the policy is unlimited. Last month I requested three weeks, and approval came through within the hour with no pushback from anyone. Two remarks stuck with me. My manager, signing off, said I had clearly earned it after the launch. A colleague covering my projects said she hoped I would come back recharged and make it worth her while.

Since then I have felt a persistent guilt I cannot account for, and I am trying to work out whether it is picking up on something real. One part of me holds that the time is mine and needs no justification — but if that were so, why did being told I had earned it feel like a relief rather than a slight, and why did the old fixed allotment of fifteen days reliably get used up while the unlimited version leaves me taking eight? Are the two things my coworkers said doing the same justificatory work, or different work? And is the guilt properly about my colleague, who does absorb extra hours when I am gone, or about something else entirely?
