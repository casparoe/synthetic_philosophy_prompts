In the comments on the preprint, a senior researcher from the original lab wrote that our study is uninformative because absence of evidence is not evidence of absence, and that the original effect stands until someone produces a positive result against it. Then tell me which of those readings, if any, would license the conclusion that our null result carries no evidential weight against the original hypothesis, and which reading you suspect the people repeating the slogan to me have in mind — and whether it survives being made explicit.
