I cover science and ideas for a magazine and I've spent the last six months reporting a piece on the reductionism fight — the one where Dennett's crane/skyhook distinction is supposed to have dissolved the problem and, as far as I can tell, hasn't moved anybody. My two camps: the people who say physics exhausts the causal story and everything above it is bookkeeping, and the people who say the special sciences have autonomous explanations and that "reduction" in actual practice means something far weaker than the first group admits. Both sides happily call themselves crane-users and accuse the other of smuggling in a skyhook, which I think is a sign the vocabulary isn't doing the work it was built for.

The stall is sharpest around universality near phase transitions. The condensed-matter people I interviewed love it — critical exponents don't care what your molecules are, the microdetails literally wash out. The emergentists love it for exactly the same reason. One datum, opposite readings, no movement in decades.

So: diagnose the disagreement, don't adjudicate it. Is it verbal (about "reduction" or "causation"), are the two sides answering different questions under one headline, is there a hidden formal or empirical question doing all the work, or are these bedrock intuitions about what counts as a non-question-begging explanation that neither side can argue for? Ground it in what each side actually says, and be honest about whether anything — a theorem about causal emergence, a result from effective field theory, a new distinction, a change of question — could break it, or whether we should just expect it to run forever.

Answer as a single JSON object with these fields:
- bottom_line: one or two sentences, the diagnosis up front
- diagnosis_type: one of verbal | different_questions | hidden_formal_question | hidden_empirical_question | bedrock_intuitions | mixed
- why_that_diagnosis: your reasoning
- sides: array of {label, what_they_say, what_is_actually_doing_the_work_for_them, strongest_move, weakest_move}
- universality_case: {physics_side_reading, emergence_side_reading, is_the_split_genuine_or_an_artifact, explanation}
- hidden_questions: array of {question, type, would_answering_it_help}
- progress_candidates: array of {candidate, kind, verdict, rough_chance_it_resolves_anything}
- safe_claim: one sentence I could put in the piece without overclaiming
