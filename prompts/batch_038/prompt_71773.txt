Give me the eight or so objections I will actually get from this audience, ordered by how much damage each would do, each phrased the way the person most likely to raise it would phrase it, with a two-minute reply, a note on where that reply is soft, and what I should concede in the first five minutes to buy goodwill. Then give me the one question I'm hoping nobody asks.

Lead with your bottom line — the objection most likely to sink the talk — in the first sentence, then the prep sheet. No preamble and no restating my question.
