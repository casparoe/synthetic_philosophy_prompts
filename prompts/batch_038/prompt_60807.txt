I am a fourth-year PhD student in philosophy of mind, and my dissertation concerns whether a two-dimensionalist framework can recover a defensible notion of narrow content. Last week, in a departmental reading group on two-dimensional semantics, I presented the proposal that a term's primary intension — the function from centered worlds to truth-values that a subject can in principle determine a priori — is fixed entirely by her intrinsic cognitive state, so that in this limited sense some meaning does survive the classical externalist arguments. A senior colleague closed the discussion by observing that meaning isn't in the head, that this was settled fifty years ago, and that any attempt to reintroduce narrow content is simply an attempt to relitigate Twin Earth. I am fairly confident the objection trades on an ambiguity, but I have not been able to say where.

Could you lay out, as an ordered menu running from the weakest to the strongest, the distinct theses this slogan might be taken to express? For each entry I would like: a precise formulation; a verdict on whether it is a platitude, a substantive but defensible claim, or false; a note on whether Twin Earth-style cases actually establish it, and whether the division-of-labor considerations establish it independently or not at all; and whether it is compatible with the two-dimensionalist position described above, which happily concedes that secondary intensions are wide. Please make the axes of variation explicit as you go — which notion of meaning is in play (semantic value, extension-determining content, cognitive significance, or psychological state type as individuated for explanation); what being in the head amounts to (determined by intrinsic physical state, accessible to the subject, or sufficient to fix reference); whether the claim concerns an individual's states or the linguistic community's; and whether it ranges over all expressions or only over natural kind terms and indexicals.

Then, as a separate section: identify the weakest reading that would actually license my colleague's dismissal, say whether he is entitled to that reading given what Twin Earth shows, and state what a two-dimensionalist can concede without abandoning the project — together with the question the burden then shifts to. I am after the argumentative geography rather than the scholarly apparatus, so please do not supply references, citations, or a reading list.
