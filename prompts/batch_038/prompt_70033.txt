I drive long-haul freight, eleven-hour runs, and something keeps happening that I can't get straight in my head.

I'll "come to" somewhere around a state line and the last forty minutes are just gone. Not fuzzy — gone, nothing there to remember. But I did them. I held my lane, downshifted for the grade outside Rock Springs, answered the dispatcher twice, and my replies are in the log word for word.

My wife says I was awake the whole time and just didn't take any of it in, like reading a page of a book and not remembering it afterwards. My brother says I was genuinely out, lights off, nobody home, and the truck was just my body doing what it's done ten thousand times. They've gotten into it over this and it doesn't feel silly to me at all, because one of them is wrong and I want to know which.

I've tried to settle it. Last month my buddy rode along and afterwards he quizzed me on things that had gone past the windshield — a billboard, the color of a car, whether it had rained. Nine out of eleven. I couldn't tell you a single one of them going in. I said "green sedan" and when he asked how I knew, I had nothing, it just came out.

Then I set up a random beeper on my phone, the kind that goes off at odd times and asks you whats going through your head right then. It went off maybe thirty times in two weeks and I answered every single one. My wife says that proves nothing, because the beep is what brings me back, so I'm only ever catching the moment I arrive and never the stretch before it.

And here's the thing that broke it for me. There's one stretch I remember clear as day — the sun going down over the salt flats, orange, some song on the radio. My wife pulled the log and I'd been parked at a rest area that whole hour, asleep in the bunk.

I've been to a sleep clinic. Mild apnea, I use the machine, they ran me in the simulator and I'm fine, and the neurologist flat out told me she has no test for whether I was experiencing anything during those stretches and it isn't really her department. So I'm left with the argument.

Which of them is right? Is there even a difference between not paying attention and not being there, and if there is, how would anybody ever tell from the inside? What do I do with a memory of a sunset that never happened? And practically — if my brother is right I should quit, and I dont have another way to make this kind of money.
