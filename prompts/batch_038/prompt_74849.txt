I'm writing a dissertation chapter arguing that reasons-responsiveness accounts of moral responsibility owe us something about the architecture of an agent's responsiveness, not merely an extensional profile of her counterfactual reactions, and I need a case built to order rather than a stock illustration.

Design a scenario in which an agent straightforwardly satisfies moderate reasons-responsiveness — regular receptivity across a wide range of scenarios, weak reactivity, and no mechanism that is pathological or externally installed — while her responsiveness is sphexish in the sense the digger-wasp story gives the term: cue-triggered, executed as a fixed routine that restarts from the top whenever it is interrupted. The verdicts I want are that a Fischer-Ravizza-style theorist must grant her guidance control and therefore responsibility, while the case should read, on the intuitive level, as something much closer to an automaton than to an agent. You don't need the entomology; the structural analogy is what matters.

Constraints: one paragraph, realistic setting such as a workplace or a household, no neurotechnology, no manipulation cases or interveners, no addiction, compulsion, or neurological diagnosis. The rigidity must arise from ordinary habituation in someone we would otherwise admire, and she must be responsive to genuine reasons rather than mere stimuli, so the case cannot be dismissed as conditioning.

Then demonstrate that the design works. Identify the operative mechanism and the reasons she recognizes and reacts to, and say why control is granted. Isolate the single feature doing the work — the restart-from-the-top structure, the fact that reasons are registered only through the routine's fixed cue hierarchy, or something else — and tell me whether removing it collapses the case back into an unremarkable creature of habit. Close with the strongest reply available to the theorist, whether via the ownership condition, the guidance-control/regulative-control distinction, or a demand that receptivity be patterned rather than merely regular, and state plainly whether that reply saves the account or concedes my point.
