So the difference between the two approaches is just whether you fill the gap in or leave it open, and Priest's revenge problem is Kripke's strengthened-liar problem with the extra wrinkle that the revenge sentence is neither true nor false and also both, which feels like it should collapse straight back into triviality unless I'm missing a step.
