I am applying for a one-year dissertation-completion fellowship and need a two-page research proposal built out of an idea that is, at present, only half formed.

The idea: debates between endurantists and perdurantists are almost always fought on the terrain of change and coincidence — temporary intrinsics, the statue and the clay, counting roads and statues through time. Objections drawn from special relativity, on the grounds that being entirely located at a moment is not well defined once simultaneity is frame-relative, so that endurantism can be salvaged only by positing a privileged foliation or by indexing multi-location to a chosen hypersurface, tend to appear late and as a technical coda rather than as the center of the dispute. My suspicion is that this ordering gets the dialectic backwards. The relativistic difficulty does not merely raise the cost of endurantism; it makes the persistence debate derivative on an independent question in the philosophy of spacetime. On that reading, endurantism is refutable or defensible exactly insofar as a privileged temporal structure is available, and what presents itself as a dispute about objects is partly a dispute about spacetime dressed in ontological language.

Please turn this into a proposal that does the following:

1. States the research question precisely, and distinguishes it from the familiar question of whether special relativity refutes endurantism. Say what the dependence thesis asserts that the familiar question does not, and why this framing makes the project more tractable rather than merely broader.

2. Explains why the resources already deployed by each side leave the question open: what the endurantist's move to relativized multi-location, and the perdurantist's appeal to frame-invariant four-dimensional structure, each fail to settle.

3. Sets out the method. Which cases would I work through — Minkowski spacetime, a foliation-privileged or neo-Lorentzian variant, and whether to go to general relativity at all; what would count as an adequate endurantist account of entire location at a moment; how the verdicts from the different frameworks would be compared to establish dependence rather than coincidence. State explicitly which alternative approaches the project should reject and why: taking multi-location as primitive, dissolving the persistence dispute as verbal, or bracketing the physics and arguing only from intuitions about change.

4. Gives a month-by-month plan for twelve months, stating the technical prerequisites honestly. I have a working command of special relativity but no serious training in spacetime geometry.

5. Describes what a successful result would look like, and what would count as failure — including the real possibility that the honest finding is that the relativistic objection is entirely absorbed by index relativization and the debate returns to where it began. Say how I would know early on that this is happening.

6. Offers a candid assessment of scope: what must be cut for this to be completable in a year, and what would remain for a subsequent project.

No bibliography or literature review by citation; I will supply the references myself. Refer to positions and arguments rather than to sources.
