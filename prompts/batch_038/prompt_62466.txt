I sit on our AI company's Model Welfare Review panel, which rules on internal petitions about how trained systems are run, modified, or retired. Our four precedents to date:

- Petition to add a large terminal penalty for episode failure in a game-playing agent: concern upheld, shaping withdrawn.
- Petition to remove the same terminal penalty from a variant of that agent whose reward is otherwise identical: concern not upheld; the panel held there was nothing left to remove.
- Petition to lengthen training for a navigation agent that paid a small per-step cost and had learned to take a slower route avoiding a region where resets were frequent: concern not upheld on the cost, but the panel noted the detour before dismissing the petition.
- Petition to add mild noise to an otherwise purely bonus-based agent's reward: concern not upheld.

The petition now before us asks us to clear, for a large-scale training run, an agent whose reward is a small symmetric per-step penalty with no terminal term and no penalty asymmetry, but which — in pilot runs — learns to take elaborate detours to avoid checkpoints where it can be interrupted mid-episode. The petitioner argues that interruption costs the agent nothing it is scored for, so the detours are instrumental; two members reply that the detours are exactly what we noted in the navigation case, and that consistency commits us.

Draft the panel's decision memo: uphold or dismiss the concern, or clear the run subject to conditions, and say which of our precedents is the closest analog. Identify the principle or principles jointly consistent with all four earlier rulings, state what each predicts here, explain which one our record best supports, and say plainly what principle we would be adopting if we ruled the other way. Name one further pilot we should require — I expect an asymmetric-termination variant will separate the candidates — and say what each outcome would show. Close with a one-sentence statement of the bottom line, then a final line in exactly the form Verdict: [Uphold | Dismiss | Conditions | Defer pending variant].
