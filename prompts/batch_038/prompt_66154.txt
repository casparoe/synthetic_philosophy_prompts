On the side I've been reading the repugnant-markets / commodification debate (Roth's point that repugnance is a constraint you have to design around rather than a datum to respect, Sandel's corruption argument, the crowding-out worry, the voluntariness-under-desperation objection, the stuff about noxious markets and unequal background conditions) and I keep getting the feeling that half of it is an argument my team has every week in a different language.

So translate that debate into the vocabulary I actually use.
