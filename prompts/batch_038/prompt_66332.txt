(4) If C waits and D turns out hostile, C loses everything. If C shoots and D was benign, C loses almost nothing.

(6) Notice nothing in (1)–(5) is about space. Any two agents that can't verify each other's intentions and can undergo fast capability jumps fit the template: two states, two labs, two people in a stairwell.
