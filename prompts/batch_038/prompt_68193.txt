ok so I'm on the committee for my school's philosophy society zine and I've been lumbered with the comedy page, deadline friday, which is a nightmare. this week's theme is moral testimony — like, why it seems wierd to just take someone's word that something is wrong when you'd happily take their word about the weather or about some medicine. my teacher gave the classic case: someone does the right thing, but when you ask them why all they can say is "because she told me it was wrong", and everyone finds that creepy even though they got the answer right.

could you write me a parody crowdfunding page for a startup that sells moral verdicts by subscription? I want the full format: the blurb for the pitch video (describing what the video shows), the reward tiers, the stretch goals, a couple of backer testimonials, an FAQ, and a "risks and challenges" bit at the bottom. about 700 words. you name the company, something plausible and slightly smug.

the important thing is the jokes have to come out of the actual problem, not out of making the founders dumb — they should be clever, sincere and totally unbothered. the reward tiers should escalate from "a verdict, no explanation" up to something like "understanding", which they obviously can't actually supply, and they should cheerfully admit that in the tier description instead of hiding it. the FAQ needs to face the real objections head on: who counts as a moral expert at all, what happens when the experts disagree with each other, and why isn't doing the right thing without knowing why basically the same as getting it right by luck — and it should give the best answers the defenders of deferring really do give, written in bland marketing voice, so it reads as fair rather than a strawman. then in risks and challenges they more or less concede the whole enterprise might be built on sand, but in the tone people use when a delivery date might slip by a month.

also please put in at least one stretch goal that's just "we will work out who the experts are", and one testimonial from a backer whose life has gone noticeably flat in a way they havent picked up on.

british english throughout — my head of department goes mental about it. invent everything, don't name any real philosophers, and no bibliography or further reading, it's a zine not an essay.
