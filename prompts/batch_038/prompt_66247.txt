I am a junior, and I am writing because I need help evaluating an argument rather than making a decision.

For three years I have taken Mandarin, and I have reached a level where I can hold a basic conversation and read short passages. I have decided I want to drop it. The reason is not that I dislike the language; it is that dropping it is the only way to open a period in my schedule for a two-year computer science sequence, which I cannot begin next year if I do not begin it now. So I have a clear sense of what I would gain.

My Mandarin teacher objected with something I have not been able to answer. She said that language ability decays quickly without regular use, and that a student who stops after three years typically retains almost nothing functional within a couple of years. Her conclusion was that dropping the class would not merely forgo whatever I might gain later; it would actively destroy something I already own. On her account, walking away now converts three years of work into something that ends up amounting to nothing, whereas continuing at least preserves it. She was emphatic that this is a real cost of quitting, located in the future, and not just the usual complaint about wasted effort.

My instinct is to reply that decay cannot count as a loss to me, because I have no intention of ever using the language again, and something can only spoil into a loss if there was some future in which I would have benefited from it. But I am not confident in that reply, and I suspect she may have identified something I am missing.

Please tell me whether her argument points to a genuine forward-looking cost, or whether it is the wasted-effort objection wearing a disguise. If it is genuinely different, I would like a test I could apply to other cases to tell the two apart, and I would like you to say directly whether my own reply works or fails. Begin with your bottom line in your first sentence and then give the reasoning; please include no preamble, no restatement of my question, and no advice about how I should arrange my schedule.
