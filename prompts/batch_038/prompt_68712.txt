In my school's required humanities seminar we spent two weeks on Thucydides, and the session on Mytilene set off an argument between two classmates that I was asked to settle and could not. We were working from these facts: after the revolt, the Athenian assembly decreed that every adult male Mytilenean be put to death and the women and children enslaved; the next day it reconvened and rescinded the decree; and a second ship was sent to overtake the first, reaching Lesbos only moments before the sentence was carried out.

Marcus said the episode is the best advertisement for democracy there is: the same body that made the decision undid it within a day, openly and by vote, and because it answered to everyone it had to listen when people started having second thoughts overnight. A regime that answers to no electorate would simply have gone through with it.

Nadia said it proves the opposite. The vote flipped by a handful of hands, and the city survived only because one crew rowed through the night. A system that lands on massacre or mercy by margins that thin is not self-correcting, it is lucky.

Marcus answered that she is grading the decision by how it came out: the assembly chose to stop, and that is what reflects on it. Had the second ship been a day slow, she would be condemning the very same vote for the very same reasons. He added that you cannot write off a government because its good decisions sometimes arrive by inches.

Nadia said she is not grading the decision but the design, and that for an institution the question is how it performs across many cases rather than once. A bridge that happened to hold yesterday's truck is still a bad bridge if the next one might take it down.

Marcus's closing move was that her standard proves too much: you can imagine a near miss for any government in history, so the only meaningful comparison is whether a system has a correction mechanism at all, which is what Athens had.

Please go through their exchange move by move, say which of them is right at each step and why, and then tell me who has the better of it overall. If there is something the two of them are both treating as settled that should not be, I would like to know that as well.
