So. Here's a thing I keep running into and I don't have a word for it.

Case one: the recycling in my building. Every week the bins end up correctly sorted, in the right spot, out before the truck comes. Nobody organised this. There's no group chat, no rota, no committee, and half the people doing it I've never met and couldnt pick out of a lineup. And yet what each of us does only makes sense given what the others do — I rinse the jars because somebody else is going to stack paper in the same room, and paper gets ruined; I drag the empty bins back in on Thursday because whoever took them out may not bother. If two of us happen to be down there at the same moment we wordlessly split the work, one lifts, one steadies.

Case two: the shared path between my place and my neighbour's. One winter we each cleared it without ever discussing it, and we drifted into each doing the half we were better placed for (they had a blower, I had an early shift). When one of us was away the other just did the whole thing, and nothing was said about it afterwards. No scorekeeping, no thanks, no sense of a deal being kept.

What bugs me is that these look like textbook cases of people doing something together — the contributions interlock, they're continuously adjusted to each other, and the outcome isn't anything any one person did. But there's also nothing that looks like an agreement, no shared plan anyone could articulate if you asked them ("we sort the recycling" would sound bizarre coming from any of us), and, crucially, nobody feels entitled to complain. If a new tenant tosses a glass jar into the paper bin, I don't feel they've let me down. I might be irritated, but I wouldn't feel rebuke-worthy on either side. There's also no common knowledge in sight: I don't know that they know that I know that we're doing this.
