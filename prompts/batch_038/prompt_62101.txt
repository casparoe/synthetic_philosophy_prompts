I am a fourth-year doctoral student in philosophy, and I am preparing a session for our department's formal epistemology workshop on indexical evidence. The stock illustrations — Doomsday, the incubator, the Presumptuous Philosopher, Sleeping Beauty — are all cases in which the self-sampling assumption and the self-indication assumption come apart, and my colleagues have encountered them so often that they now argue from remembered verdicts rather than from structure. I want to hand them a case with the opposite shape: one in which SSA (reason as if you were a random sample from the observers in your reference class) and SIA (weight a hypothesis by the number of observers who have your evidence) agree exactly, so that the real fault line is exposed elsewhere. Please construct the case to the following specifications, in the sense that Bostrom's pair of assumptions is usually discussed.

Setting and constraints.

1. An ordinary institutional setting — a workplace inspection, a clinic, a school, a field station. No cosmology, no extinction risk, no simulations, no duplicates or copies, no memory-altering drugs, and no one being woken or tested more than once: the case must not be a Sleeping Beauty variant in disguise.
2. Two hypotheses, H and L, selected by a fair chance device whose outcome the protagonist does not observe. The protagonist is told the entire protocol, including the odds.
3. The same individuals must exist, be awake, and be fully informed under both hypotheses. What varies between them is only how many of those individuals end up in the protagonist's epistemic situation — being called in for an individual interview, say — and those numbers must differ by a ratio of at least ten to one. State the numbers you have chosen.
4. Apart from the fact of being in that situation, the protagonist has no observation, indexical or otherwise, that bears on H versus L: nothing in the room, the schedule, or the instructions leaks the answer.
5. Include a control agent who knows the whole protocol, has not learned which hypothesis obtained, and is not in the protagonist's situation. Every position below must assign this agent exactly the prior.

Required computations. Let the four positions be: (i) SSA with the most inclusive reference class you can defend; (ii) SIA; (iii) SSA with the narrowest reference class you can defend; (iv) conditionalization on the purely non-indexical content of the protagonist's evidence. Your case must come out so that (i) and (ii) agree exactly, (iii) and (iv) agree exactly, and the two pairs differ from each other. Report each credence as an exact fraction, derived in odds form with the arithmetic shown, and state for each position the reference class or evidence proposition you are relying on.

Then:

6. Add a wager on H, at stakes a graduate student would actually accept, that divides the four positions two and two on whether to take it, and give each position's reservation price.
7. Explain the two coincidences structurally rather than numerically: which feature of the case forces (i) to equal (ii), and which forces (iii) to equal (iv)? What is the smallest change to the case that breaks each coincidence, and what do the four numbers become when you make it?
8. Check robustness: show that your answers are unchanged if the protagonist's evidence arrives in two stages — first that they exist and are at work today, then that they are in the situation in question — rather than all at once.
9. Say what the protagonist knows that the control agent does not, on positions (i) and (ii), and whether that extra item is evidence about H or only about the protagonist.
10. My colleagues will insist that (iii) and (iv) are the only defensible readings and that the case trades on treating a trivial fact as information. Give that objection in its strongest form and say whether the case survives it.

What would count as cheating: the verdicts must be generated by the positions' own machinery, not stipulated. If the protagonist's situation is described in terms that already encode the answer, or if a reference class is chosen ad hoc to manufacture the required agreement, the exercise fails — the classes you use must be ones you would defend independently of the result.
