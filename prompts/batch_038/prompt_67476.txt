ok so I'm in an aesthetics seminar and our lecturer spent two weeks on Schopenhauer on music, and basically the whole thing was built on one big analogy I want to pressure-test before I lean on it in my paper. Here's how he laid it out. Music isn't like the other arts, which give us copies of the Ideas — the fixed grades at which the Will shows up in the world. Music skips the Ideas entirely and is a copy of the Will itself. Then he gave the voice-by-voice map: the bass is the lowest grade of objectification, inorganic mass, gravity, the sheer bulk of the planet; the middle filling voices are the plant and animal kingdoms; the melody up top, the free singing line, is the intellect and striving of a human being. He backed it with acoustics — the upper tones are already physically present in the vibration of the fundamental, so the way harmony is generated mirrors the way everything else is generated out of the lower grades — and he added that the bass can only lumber along slowly in heavy steps while the melody is free to run about wherever it likes, and that dissonance and its resolution are pain and its appeasement. The conclusion he used all this for: music is the highest art, it delivers a kind of knowledge nothing else can, and that's why it lands harder than any painting or poem ever could.

My roommate, who composes, says the whole map is just 18th century thoroughbass theory with a metaphysical hat on. His argument: Schopenhauer happened to pick the one style where the lowest line really does generate the harmony and the bass really does lumber, and then read the structure of reality off it. Give him a texture with no functional bass at all — renaissance polyphony where the bottom part is just a tenor, a gamelan piece, a techno track where the bass is the hook, a string quartet where the cello is only doubling — and the map either collapses or can be re-fitted to whatever you like, which is the same as saying it can't be wrong.

Can you audit it for me? I want the correspondence table first, then the disanalogies sorted into the ones that actually threaten the conclusion and the ones that are harmless, and then what features of the source domain — generation from a fundamental, high versus low, slow versus fast, consonance versus dissonance — get quietly imported onto the Will when the Will has no business having them. After that tell me whether the priority-of-music claim survives. Specifically: could Schopenhauer retreat to the weaker version, music as a dynamic analogue of striving in general with no voice-by-voice map, and if he retreats does he still get what he wanted — an account of why music isn't representational, why sad music is pleasurable, and why its the top art rather than merely the most stirring one? And if the analogy is doing more damage than work, what would be a better one to put in its place, if any.
