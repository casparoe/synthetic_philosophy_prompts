On top of that there's the recurring claim that all of it earns its keep only insofar as it serves life, some image about a living thing needing a bounded horizon around it, a region of the unhistorical it can stand in without being crushed, and a capacity for growing out of and reshaping one's own past that I think he calls a plastic power.
