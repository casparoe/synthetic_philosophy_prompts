Give it to me as exactly five numbered items, one or two sentences each except the scenario, with no intro and no conclusion: (1) the scenario; (2) the James check, naming each of the three conditions and why it's met here; (3) the Clifford check, saying precisely what makes the belief wrong despite it working; (4) which feature of the case is doing the work, and what verdict changes if you delete it; (5) the best reply a Jamesian could make to your case and whether it actually rescues it.
