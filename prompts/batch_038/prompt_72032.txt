I am a novelist, and the middle movement of the book I am working on is documentary: it consists of the papers its characters leave behind. The book concerns the last seventy years of the Roman Republic, but I have committed myself to a structural decision I now have to honour. These papers are not written by Cicero, or by any senator with a literary voice. They are produced by a body I have invented — the Standing Commission on Institutional Continuity — which performs for the *res publica* something very like a modern internal-controls and governance assurance review: a statement of scope and basis, numbered findings with severity ratings, a risk register, management's remediation plan, a glossary of defined terms, and a going-concern conclusion. The comedy I am after is entirely one of register. The Commission is technically excellent and morally blind, and it has no way to describe a norm except as a control weakness.

Please draft one such report, approximately 900 to 1,200 words. Assume it is issued in the consulship of Pompey and Crassus and covers the preceding sixty-odd years. Give it a heading with the issuing body, a reference number, and a restricted distribution list. My reader knows the period reasonably well but is not a specialist; a historian should find the history fair and an auditor should find the audit fair.

The report should contain:

1. **Basis of assessment.** The entity's constitution is unwritten; precedent and the deeds of ancestors function as interpretive guidance; there is no codified by-law set, no single accountable owner of the framework, and no defined escalation path. Management has historically treated all of this as a strength. The Commission disagrees, in language that never rises above neutral.

2. **Control environment.** The actual machinery, described as controls: the graded sequence of offices and the statutory minimum ages fixed in 180 BC as access restrictions over senior appointment; the single-officeholder veto of the tribunate as a maker-checker control with no override and no expiry; the declaration of unfavourable omens as a change freeze that halts a measure without adjudicating it; the assemblies' inability to originate anything, voting only on what a magistrate puts to them; and the absence of any standing internal security function, so that every enforcement action depends on ad hoc mobilisation of stakeholders.

3. **Findings.** Numbered, each rated on a three-tier severity scale you define. At minimum, cover: the removal of a sitting tribune from office by popular vote in 133 BC, recorded as the override of a checker control by the requesting function without documented authorisation; the emergency decree first passed in 121 BC, which suspends no law and authorises nothing, and whose entire function is to transfer legal liability onto the magistrate who acts while supplying reputational cover to the body that advises — get this exactly right, and have the Commission praise the design; the armed entry into the city by a serving consul in 88 BC and the removal of a magistrate by force; the reassignment of a military command, by a tribune's bill, from the general holding it to a private citizen; the published register of de-authorised persons after the civil war, with asset forfeiture and a published payment schedule for those who acted on it; the enlargement of the senate from about three hundred to six hundred, with membership made automatic on the quaestorship, assessed for its effect on per-member oversight capacity; the repeated transfer of the standing courts' jury panels between the senatorial and equestrian rolls, each of which is also a party to matters before those courts; and the curtailment of the tribunate's legislative initiative under the dictatorship, together with the reversal of that and other remediation items by successor management within a decade.

4. **Risk register.** A table of four to six rows: risk, likelihood, impact, existing mitigation, residual rating. Every mitigation listed must be an unwritten custom, and every residual rating must be worse than the rating management asserts.

5. **Remediation plan.** Management's response in project language, with named owners and target dates, in which the measures adopted are the real historical ones and a reader who knows the period understands each of them to have made matters worse. Do not say so.

6. **Going concern and glossary.** A closing paragraph stating, without drama, the conditions giving rise to substantial doubt about the entity's ability to continue for the next twelve months, and the mitigating factors management has offered. Then a short glossary — the way of the ancestors, *auctoritas*, *dignitas*, *intercessio*, *provocatio* — each defined accurately, each definition morally blank.

Constraints on execution. Every event must be real and every institution described as it actually functioned rather than as legend has it; where you are unsure of a year, describe the sequence instead of asserting a date. Quote no ancient author, no modern historian, and no real assurance standard verbatim — the Commission has not retained primary documentation, and that omission should itself appear as a records-management finding. The report may not use *decline*, *fall*, *collapse*, *crisis*, *tyranny*, *democracy*, *republic* in the modern sense, *populism*, *oligarchy*, *legitimacy*, *virtue*, or *betrayal*; it has no moral vocabulary and does not notice the absence. There must be no winking — no asides, no irony markers, no sentence in which the Commission appears to grasp what it has just written. The best sentence in the document should be one in which a perfectly accurate description of a Roman custom is simultaneously a perfectly accurate description of a control failure, and the Commission registers only the second.

What I want the piece to leave open, rather than settle, is the real question: whether an unwritten constitution is a defect or a design, and whether a body that can describe the breakdown of norms has thereby explained them. Please give me the document alone — no preface, no notes on your choices, no commentary afterwards.
