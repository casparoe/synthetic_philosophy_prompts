Task: design an in-class game that makes them *feel* the difference between the self-sampling and the self-indication assumption, without me saying either name until the very end. What I want them walking out with is this: that "I'm one of the observers, so how should I bet?" has two defensible answers, because you can either treat yourself as a random draw from the observers who actually exist in some reference class — in which case your answer shifts when you change what the reference class is — or you can treat the bare fact of your own existence as evidence that boosts any hypothesis which says there are more of you around, in which case the reference class stops mattering and you end up able to settle an empirical question about how many things exist without gathering any evidence at all. That last bit is the presumptuous philosopher, and I want at least one student to *become* him, on purpose, in front of everyone.
