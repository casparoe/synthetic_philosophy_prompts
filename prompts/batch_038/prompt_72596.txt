Setup, stipulated by me so you don't have to go looking anything up: humanity's future is bimodal. Either (A) things collapse or level off soon-ish and roughly 150 billion biological humans ever live, total; or (B) we go interstellar and digital — the biological-human count still comes out to about 150 billion, but they're followed by something like 10^16 uploaded minds. Give me your credence in A (assume you start at 50/50 before any of the indexical stuff comes in), decomposed into the parts it actually turns on: the probability that self-sampling over the most inclusive reference class is the right rule, the probability that some restricted class is right instead, the probability that self-indication-ish reasoning is the correct correction, and whatever other term you think belongs in there — a number and one line of justification for each, then how they combine into the final figure.
