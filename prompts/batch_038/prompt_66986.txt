The worksheet. Use these numbers exactly, theyre made up on purpose:

Write the dialogue between two people: Nadia, who thinks the conversion is the better outcome for animals and can defend that without flinching, and Owen, who thinks that kind of math leads somewhere horrific. No strawmen — Owen shouldn't just say nature is sacred and numbers are cold, and Nadia shouldn't be a cartoon who loves bulldozers.
