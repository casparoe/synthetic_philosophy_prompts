Longtime lurker of the rationalist-blog sphere (going back to when some of it was still on livejournal, basically), and there's a pattern I've been chewing on for years that I can't find a word for. Not really a philosophy question — more a "does this exist as a named thing" question.

The pattern: a category starts as somebody's description of a group of people who were already there. The group learns the description and starts using it on themselves. That's the boring half. The half I keep coming back to is that the category then gets *rebuilt out of the people it changed*, and afterwards the older records get quietly re-scored against the rebuilt version — so from outside it all looks like one continuous natural kind, and the loop you're standing in is invisible.

Instances I have in mind:

- "Masking" in autism. Circulated as community vocabulary first — autistic adults describing what they actually do in social situations. Then somebody builds a self-report scale out of those self-descriptions (the CAT-Q, I think), and the scale gets used in studies that feed back into how the criteria and the interview questions are worded. The instrument is downstream of a thing that was reshaped by the instrument's ancestors.
- When Asperger's got folded into the spectrum, existing case files were re-coded under the new wording, so the prevalence curves everyone argues about aren't measuring one constant thing across the break — and the re-coding is exactly what hides the break.
- Small one from my own world: a forum coins a term for a failure mode (akrasia-flavored stuff, or whatever the current equivalent is), people start using it in their own introspective reports, and years later a questionnaire about that failure mode gets validated partly on people who learned the concept from the forum.

I half-remember some philosopher of science having a phrase for categories that change the people they're applied to, but I can't recall it and it doesn't cover the re-scoring part anyway. Also on my list of near-misses: self-fulfilling prophecy, Goodhart, the observer effect, performativity, diagnostic creep/inflation, medicalization. None of them seem to capture the loop closing back onto the criteria plus the retroactive re-scoring that erases the trace.

So: (1) is there an established concept for this exact configuration? (2) If the neighbors only partly fit, tell me which slice of my description each one covers and which slice it misses — I care about the carving way more than the label. (3) If nothing fits, coin something, define it, and give me boundary cases: what counts, what doesn't, and one case that looks like it but isn't.

Please lead with your bottom line in the first sentence and then give the reasoning — no preamble, and don't restate my question back at me.
