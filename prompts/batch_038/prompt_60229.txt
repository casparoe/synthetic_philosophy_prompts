I just started a job doing model evals — writing test batteries and then arguing all day about what a passing score actually entitles us to say about a system. Half my team talks nonstop about dispositions: the model is disposed to flatter the user, disposed to refuse, disposed to grab the shortcut. A teammate with a metaphysics background then pointed out that our vocabulary is a total mess, because an eval suite is exactly the setup philosophers say breaks disposition talk. A jailbreak prompt that leaves the tendency in place but stops it showing up looks like a mask. A fine-tune that deletes a behavior without ever eliciting it looks like a fink. And a model that aces the battery for reasons that have nothing to do with the disposition looks like a mimic. Before I write the conceptual appendix for our protocol I want the metaphysics straight, and I want it as a spec, not a survey.

So: write the desiderata — what any adequate theory of dispositions has to deliver.

I want the requirements themselves to carry the weight. For each one, give the case or consideration behind it, and be explicit about how demanding it is. Specifically I'm after:

- which phenomena the theory has to explain or explain away. At minimum: why the naive conditional analysis (to be soluble just is to be such that it would dissolve if placed in water) goes wrong; finks; masks; mimics, where something manifests without having the property at all; the reverse case where the disposition gets *acquired* precisely when the triggering conditions show up; dispositions that never get a single chance to manifest; degrees and comparison, i.e. one thing being more disposed than another; and dispositions with many different manifestation types rather than one stimulus and one response.
- which distinctions it must not collapse. Having versus manifesting. Being fragile versus merely being breakable. Intrinsic versus extrinsic. The disposition versus whatever underlying property does the work. Ability versus performance — that last one is the distinction my team actually needs and we've been using the words interchangeably.
- what it has to be consistent with, and, honestly, which of those consistency demands are load-bearing versus which are one camp's taste wearing a requirement costume. Is "dispositions must be intrinsic" a real constraint or a physicalist's preference? Is the complaint that dispositional explanations are trivially circular — that saying opium causes sleep because it has a sleep-causing power explains nothing — a genuine desideratum or a hangover from positivism? Does the theory have to square with fundamental physics, where the basic properties look structural rather than powerful? Mark which of these I'd be free to drop.
- any subset of the desiderata you think cannot be jointly satisfied. My guess is that "dispositions are intrinsic" + "there's a true conditional analysis of them" + "finks are genuinely possible, not just an artifact of sloppy description" is a bad triangle, but I may have mis-stated it, and if the real unsatisfiable cluster is a different one I want that instead. If the list turns out to be a result in itself, say so and show the proof sketch.

Then, briefly — a paragraph each, no more — how the main contenders do against the list: the simple conditional analysis, the Lewis-style reformed analysis built on ideal conditions, the approach that takes potentialities and their degrees as primitive, and the view that all properties are dispositional through and through. For each, name the requirement it flunks and what it gives up to satisfy the others.

Last thing, and it's the reason I'm asking: tell me which desideratum, if any, decides whether we're allowed to say "this model has disposition D" on the strength of a test battery. If the metaphysics can't settle that, just say so plainly and then tell me what a passing eval is actually evidence of instead.

No bibliography, no citations, no reading list — I can't chase references and a list of names would be useless to me. Give me the arguments and the cases. If you need a technical term, gloss it in a clause the first time. A table is fine for the requirements if its cleaner, but keep the motivating case visible next to each row rather than banishing it to a footnote.
