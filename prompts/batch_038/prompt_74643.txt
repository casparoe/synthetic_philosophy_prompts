The itch: the importance/tractability/neglectedness framework that EA-type orgs use to rank cause areas isnt scale-invariant. You score each dimension, multiply, and rank — but re-anchor the scales in an equally defensible way (logs instead of raw scores, or a 0–1 range with different endpoints) and the ranking of causes comes out different. So a chunk of the output is an artifact of measurement choices nobody ever defends.

My half-formed hunch is the fix isnt to ditch ITN but to stop treating it as a score and start treating it as a claim about the shape of an impact-vs-resources curve: neglectedness is where on the curve you currently sit, tractability is the slope, importance is the asymptote. Then the real question is what assumptions about that curve you need before the product counts as even a rough first-order approximation of marginal value.
