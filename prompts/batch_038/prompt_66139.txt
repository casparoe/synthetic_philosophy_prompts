I'm helping put together the entertainment for our department's post-exams pub quiz — there's a round where someone has to open with a joke, and since about half the room are logicians and the other half are only there for the free crisps, we thought a logic joke would land reasonably well with both. Can you write one for me? Not the usual "a mathematician walks into a bar and orders the limit of a sequence" business, and not one of those jokes where the punchline is just "ha ha, mathematicians are pedantic". I want the funny bit to actually turn on the difference between classical and intuitionistic logic, so that someone who's read the constructive stuff laughs for the right reason and nobody concludes I have no idea what I'm talking about.

Some material you could draw on (use what you like, ignore the rest): that a constructivist will happily concede that assuming the negation of a claim leads to absurdity — so ¬¬P is in hand — while still flatly refusing to give you P; that the double negation of excluded middle goes through even though excluded middle itself doesn't; the disjunction property, i.e. a genuine proof of A-or-B has to arrive with an actual selection of which disjunct; and the drinker's paradox, the theorem that in any pub there's someone such that if they're drinking then everybody is, whose standard two-case proof splits on whether everyone is drinking or somebody isn't — precisely the case split an intuitionist won't accept unless they're told the drinking relation is decidable. A short exchange between a barman and a very difficult customer would work nicely, or between a classicist and a constructivist arguing over whose round it is. Keep it to a couple of hundred words at most and make sure it ends on a line rather than trailing off into an explanation.

Oh, and please write the whole thing in British English (colour, not color; "maths", never "math"), give it a one-line title if you want, and don't follow it up with a footnote unpacking what it means — if it needs unpacking, it hasn't worked. No need to cite anything or name any papers or authors, I'm not putting this in a bibliography, I just want the joke.
