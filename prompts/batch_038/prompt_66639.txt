Comms wants something a reporter can restate in one sentence. Nobody has yet told me what the rule has to be able to *do* before we start arguing over which one to buy.

So: acting as a decision theorist who has spent real time in this literature and has refereed papers in it, write me the desiderata -- the spec -- that a theory of choice under risk has to satisfy to count as an adequate response to the Allais problem, for my purposes specifically (a rule a public body can apply repeatedly, defend in writing, and audit after the fact).
