Can you write me a joke about Molinism? I don't want a generic "a philosopher walks into a bar" gag — the punchline should turn on the actual machinery of the view, so that someone who knows Molina's account laughs for the right reasons and someone who doesn't at least picks up the shape of it.

The setup I have in mind, improve it if you can: something like a job interview, or a first session with a therapist, where one party knows exactly what the other would freely choose in every hypothetical situation that could ever come up. The joke should hinge on middle knowledge being prevolitional — the counterfactuals of creaturely freedom are true before God decides anything, so he can't edit them, he can only go world-shopping for one whose counterfactual profile is workable. Ideally it exploits why it's called *middle* knowledge at all, i.e. that it's wedged between natural knowledge of everything possible and free knowledge of what will actualy happen once the decree is in place, and it doesn't belong to either side. And the funniest version I can picture involves someone being told that God already knows precisely what they'd freely do in circumstances that will never arise, and that nobody — not even God — can do anything about that particular fact.

Then maybe a second, much shorter one from the other direction: the grounding objection, the complaint that nothing at all makes those counterfactuals true before the decree. That way it lands for the Thomists and the open theists in the room too, not just Molinism's fans.

Two requests about how you hand it over. Keep everything as plain continuous prose, like you'd write an email — no headings, no bullet points, no bold, no tables, the jokes just read out as bits of text with the setup and punchline in the same paragraph. And afterwards add a couple of sentences telling me how confident you are that you've got the doctrine right rather than mangling it, plus which single point you're least sure of — where you think you might be leaning on a contested reading of Molina himself, since I know his actual position gets flattened a lot in the secondary stuff.
