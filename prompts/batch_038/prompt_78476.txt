so: give me the mapping properly -- what plays the atmosphere, the physics, the satellites, the instrumental record, the held-out data -- then sort the disanalogies into the ones that actually bite for each of the two uses the deck makes and the ones that are just noise. say whether a better analogy exists for what cliodynamics is doing with secular cycles and elite overproduction. and take a side: would you fund the lab? definite answer with reasons, not a summary of the debate -- and flag which of your own premises you'd least want to defend.
