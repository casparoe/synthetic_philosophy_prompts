our reading group is stuck on an argument one of us keeps pressing. he says a belief is a thermometer: a physical state that covaries with some condition out in the world. when the column sits at 22 and its really 25 we don't say the thermometer has a false belief, we say it's badly made, or badly used, or we recalibrate it. so "the frog believes there's a fly" is just a dressed-up way of saying "this retinal state goes off when small dark moving things cross the visual field," and all the content/function/misrepresentation machinery is anthropomorphic decoration laid over plain causation. nothing is ever wrong about a thermometer, so nothing is ever wrong about the frog either.
