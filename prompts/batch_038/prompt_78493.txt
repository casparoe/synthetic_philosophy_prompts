So a few things have collided in my head and I can't sort them out on my own.

I found out at 30 that I was donor-conceived — mom was 41, IVF, donor sperm, because the man who raised me turned out to be infertile. Nobody hid it maliciously, it just never came up, and honestly were fine about it now.

What I can't shake is a thought I keep having about origins. Something in me is deeply convinced that I couldn't have come from any other sperm and egg than the ones I did — that if a different donor vial had been warmed up that afternoon, some other kid would be living my life and I simply wouldn't exist. It doesn't feel like a statistical claim about my parents' choices, it feels necessary, like I can't even picture what it would be for me to have had a different start.

And then there's my brother. Identical twin — same embryo, it split a few days in — so we came from literally the same egg and the same sperm, the exact same beginning, down to the last detail. And we are two unmistakably different people, different jobs, different wives, one of us anxious and one of us maddeningly calm. So whatever this thing is that's supposedly essential to me, he has the very same one, and it obviously isn't what's making us two instead of one.

That leaves me stuck. If the origin intuition is that shaky under pressure, is the itch to find the donor — to see his face, to know where I got my hands and my temper — just sentiment I should grow out of, or is it tracking something real that the "you couldn't have come from different gametes" line never actually explains? And Dad being a little wounded that I want to go looking at all, which makes me feel like a jerk on top of everything else.

Tell me what you actually think rather than the careful both-sides version. Exactly five numbered points, each one a sentence or two, no introduction and no conclusion.
