Forty-two years I spent shooting X-rays at protein crystals, so my default setting has always been that anything which sounds like purpose at the level of the whole organism is really just shorthand for atoms getting on with it. Since I packed in the lab I've been reading philosophy for pleasure, and one thing has thoroughly got under my skin: that old Hofstadter dialogue where Achilles and the Tortoise argue about who shoves whom around inside the careenium — the billiard-table brain full of little magnetic simms, some of which clump together into simmballs that stand for things, and the question of whether a simmball's meaning can genuinely shove the substrate about, or whether that's just a picturesque way of describing collisions.

My reading was that he's saying the symbol-level story is only a convenient blur, like saying the gas warmed up when all that's happening is molecules going faster — nothing extra gets pushed. I'd have signed up to that in a heartbeat. Except I keep playing about with a variant of my own and it wont behave.

Suppose one simmball in the careenium — call it Nana, it stands for the Tortoises grandmother — gets frozen solid to the table. Physically welded down. No simm can ever budge it again. But we leave all its magnetic hookups live, so it goes on tugging at every simm that passes within range, and it goes on being part of every clump that forms near it. So it still shoves, in the sense that it deflects things, but it can never be shoved back. Its meaning, apparently, still has consequences — only nothing can ever revise that meaning, because nothing can get at it.

Can you give me an example of a real conceptual problem that this frozen Nana throws up for the whole business of symbols pushing the substrate around? I'd want it spelled out concretely — particular simms, particular shoves, particular clumps forming and coming apart — rather than a general sermon about levels of description. And once you've got the example in front of you, tell me how much trouble it actually is: a genuine crack in the picture, or have I simply built a careenium that's broken for reasons that have nothing whatsoever to do with meaning?
