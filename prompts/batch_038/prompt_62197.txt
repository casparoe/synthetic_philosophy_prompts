I keep going back and forth on whether the Alaska Permanent Fund confirms or embarrasses the difference principle and I'd like you to sort it out. Facts so you don't have to guess: once North Slope oil started flowing, Alaskans amended the state constitution in 1976 to send at least a quarter of mineral royalties into a fund whose principal can't be spent, and in 1980 the legislature created the dividend. The first version paid $50 per year of residency up to twenty; the Supreme Court killed that in Zobel v. Williams as a burden on people moving between states, so from 1982 on it's been a flat check to every resident whos been there a full calendar year — kids included, no means test, first ones around a thousand dollars, recent ones in the one-to-two-thousand range.

Now run it through Rawls properly. What does the difference principle tell the 1976 and 1980 legislators to do with that money, and what does it need before it can say anything at all — a least-advantaged group, a single index of primary goods? Does Alaska even have a determinate answer to that, given that a lot of the lowest-cash-income households are in villages with substantial subsistence economies that an income index scores as destitute? Then the twist I can't resolve: those voters were behind no veil, they knew exactly where they sat, and they picked equal division anyway. Is that a counterexample to maximin as a prediction of what people choose, or is that just not the kind of thing the original position is in the business of predicting? And can a flat check that an oil executive also cashes be difference-principle-justified — my hunch is the stability and publicity material does the work here, since universality is arguably why the thing has survived four decades while targeted programs get means-tested to death, but tell me if I'm reading that into Rawls rather than out of him. Finish with what the theory is simply silent on, especially that the source is a depletable asset, which sounds like an intergenerational problem, and I dont think the just savings principle reaches it.
