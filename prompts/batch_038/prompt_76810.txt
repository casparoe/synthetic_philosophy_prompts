I am preparing a position paper for a workshop on democratic reform, and before circulating it I would like a consistency check on the commitments it rests on. They are these:

1. Elections are not a neutral aggregation of preferences but a filter that selects for people who are good at winning elections — fundraising, media performance, appetite for prolonged conflict — so an elected legislature is systematically unrepresentative in a way no package of campaign-finance reform can correct.
2. Selection by lot is therefore not a second-best substitute for election but the more democratic procedure, being the only selection rule under which every citizen is equally eligible for office.
3. Political judgment is a genuine skill, acquired only through sustained practice, and a legislature's capacity to check a permanent administration depends on members who have accumulated enough of it to recognize when they are being misled.
4. Rotation is the principal protection against capture: members of a sortitioned chamber serve short, non-renewable terms and cannot make a career of the office.
5. Such a chamber should also be denied a permanent professional staff and secretariat of its own, since supplying one would recreate, in unelected form, the very class of political specialists I am objecting to.
6. I nevertheless support strict term limits for existing elected bodies, even though I take those bodies to be defective in the way described in (1).
7. Legitimacy requires that the governed be able to remove those who rule over them; the possibility of removal is not an optional extra.
8. Concretely, a sortitioned second chamber should hold only a suspensive veto, not an absolute one, over finance bills, because on such matters the elected chamber's mandate carries greater weight.

Please give me an audit rather than a defence. Which of these genuinely pull against one another, and which only appear to? Rank the tensions by severity instead of reporting them as equally damaging, state for each what would have to be surrendered or qualified to dissolve it, and identify the single revision that would repair the most. Flag explicitly any pair that looks unstable but is not. I have no prior about which item is the weak link, so do not assume I am protecting any of them.
