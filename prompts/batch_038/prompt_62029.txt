I don't buy that it's a category error, but I also can't say what's wrong, and I've noticed "the stopping rule is irrelevant" seems to mean different things depending on who's saying it. Which readings are true, which are false, which are true but do no work against the reviewer, and which one does my postdoc actually need in order to answer the complaint?
