been going in circles on the great-man-vs-structural-forces thing — would Germany have gone where it went without Hitler, would the revolution have happened without the particular hotheads, etc — and I've concluded the problem is that nobody ever says what would count as a good answer. So skip the history for a sec and give me the spec: what does an account of how much a single individual mattered to a historical outcome have to deliver before it's allowed to call itself adequate?

list the requirements. for each one, the case or consideration motivating it, whether its non-negotiable or just a preference of one camp, and what a theory that fails it sounds like when it tries to answer a real question. cover: which episodes it has to get right (name your test cases — I'd have put in the 1939 Munich beer hall bomb that went off after Hitler cut his speech short and left early, and the wrong turn in Sarajevo, but tell me if those are the wrong tests); which distinctions it can't collapse (necessary vs sufficient, the guy who lights the fuse vs the standing conditions that made the fuse matter, "wouldn't have happened at all" vs "would have happened differently"); what it owes us on whether the counterfactual even has a determinate truth value given that the outcome has to be described at some level of granularity; and what happens when the substitute gets drawn from the same pool the original came from.

then take my pet idea apart. I was going to just define pivotality operationally: swap the person for the most plausible available substitute from the same structural pool, hold everything else fixed, see if the outcome moves past a threshold. which of your desiderata does that satisfy, and which does it wreck?

and the bit I actually care about most: is there a subset of the requirements that can't be jointly met? I suspect the demand that the account discriminate — that it can say this person mattered and that one didn't — is at war with the demand that it stay faithful to the causal structure, where in any dense conjunctural situation practically everything is a difference maker. if you agree, say which one gets dropped and what dropping it costs. if you think I'm wrong, show me where.

finish with a short scorecard of the actual contenders against the list. short. the list is the deliverable.
