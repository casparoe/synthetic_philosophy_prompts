I want to build a game around safe Pareto improvements, and specifically the gap I don't think people feel until they run into it: the difference between "there's an outcome here that would leave both of us better off than where we'd otherwise end up" and "there's a move I can make on my own, right now, that makes me better off and cannot make you worse off no matter what you turn out to do." Everyone in that room will nod along that win-win deals exist. Almost none of them have noticed how thin the set of moves is that survives the second, much stronger test, or how much of what feels like obvious mutual benefit is secretly resting on the other side reciprocating, or on both sides being able to commit.
