Writing a joke for my department's end-of-year thing and I want it to actually land with people who know the material, not just be a generic "philosophers are weird" bit. Here's the cast I have in mind: a negative-liberty type who defines being free purely as nobody getting in his way, a positive-liberty type who thinks real freedom means ruling your own lower appetites, and a republican in Pettit's sense, for whom what matters is that no one holds arbitary power over you even if they never once exercise it.

The punchline needs to turn on the thing the first two structurally can't see: the kindly master who never lays a finger on his slave still leaves that slave unfree, so non-domination isn't a compromise between the other two views but a reclassification of cases where there's no interference at all. Bonus points for a callback that reads one way if you only know the negative/positive distinction and another way if you know the republican one. Keep it under about 150 words, and please don't pun on "negative" and "positive" as attitudes — way too easy. Also write the whole thing in British English, even though I typed all this in American.
