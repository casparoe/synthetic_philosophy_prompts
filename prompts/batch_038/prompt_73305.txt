Here's the thing I keep turning over. A modern drone strike gets cleared like this: a system estimates, from video and weeks of watching a building, roughly how many non-combatents would probably die. Nobody says "I want those people dead," and sometimes there isn't even a specific human being whose death anyone is aiming at, just a probability spread over whoever happens to be in the room.
