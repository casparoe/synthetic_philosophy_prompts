I'm helping draft testimony for a county board hearing on whether to fund a jail expansion or a diversion program instead, and two people on our little team keep reaching for the same line about the Norwegian model — "the punishment is the loss of liberty, nothing more" — and they're using it to argue for opposite conclusions, and I can't quite say what's going on.

The other uses it against spending anything on conditions or programming: the sentence takes away liberty and only liberty, so decent food, healthcare, college classes, actual treatment are perks we don't owe anybody, and complaining about conditions just confuses the punishment with the amenities.

Both insist they're saying the same thing the Norwegians say, and both got annoyed when I suggested they weren't.
