I direct the patient-experience and safety office of a mid-sized public hospital. Roughly fifteen times a month someone — a patient, a relative, a night-shift nurse — alleges that their account was set aside not because it was weak but because of who they are, or that our intake forms and incident categories simply contain no slot for what happened to them. We have no framework for adjudicating these claims: our three-person panel, none of whom has studied philosophy, either treats every such allegation as a confirmed case of epistemic injustice or dismisses the vocabulary as untestable, and our written determinations have started to be appealed on the grounds that the panel applied no consistent standard.

Drawing on Fricker's distinction between testimonial and hermeneutical injustice, write the decision procedure this panel can run on a single allegation in about twenty minutes. I need the questions to ask, in order; what would count as a sufficient answer to each; the point at which the procedure may conclude that a credibility deficit was legitimate rather than prejudicial, and what evidence distinguishes the two; and an explicit marking of every step where the panel is exercising judgment that no rule can supply, since the determination must be defensible to a reviewing body.

The procedure must cope with at least these: an allegation resting on a witness who was over-believed rather than under-believed; a complainant who is discounted in one setting and readily believed in another by the same people; and the second-order risk that working through a credibility checklist leaves panelists more confident in their ratings, not less.

Then apply it to three cases of your own construction, one of which must return a finding of no injustice against a complainant the reader is inclined to sympathize with — and say plainly what the panel is to do with that result, given that the complainant will experience it as a further dismissal. Close by stating what the procedure cannot deliver: which parts of the underlying idea are lost or distorted in the translation into steps, and what patterns in its outputs should make me distrust its verdicts.
