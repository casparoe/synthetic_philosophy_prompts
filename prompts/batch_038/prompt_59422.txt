Second, the point where everyone knows that everyone knows, and matching stops being a gamble. Third, and this is the part I care most about: years later the original problem has simply evaporated, maybe because of some new bit of kit that makes either option costless, and yet the regularity is still there, and now people punish anyone who breaks it, and a character has come to think it's correct rather than merely convenient. A sentence or two per field, max.
