I'm on a philosophy of language module and we did Lewis on convention this week. In seminar our tutor went round the room ruling on whether various regularities count as conventions, and her verdicts were: driving on the left in the UK — yes; adjectives going before nouns in English — yes; everyone standing on the right on Tube escalators — yes; the whole department reply-all-ing to a mailing list that literally everyone moans about — yes, she was firm that the fact we'd all prefer some other arrangement doesn't disqualify it; the residents-only parking bay on my street, where you get a ticket — no, that's just law; tipping 20% in American restaurants — no, on her view that one's held up by guilt and dread of being thought tight rather than by anybody's interest in coordinating.

The case for next week is mine. In the house I live in, everyone takes their shoes off in the hall. Will she call that a convention?
