I manage about 25 people at a logistics company. Two years ago I got moved from our Rotterdam site to our Memphis one — same employer, same job on paper — and the difference in how rules work over here has been messing with my head in a way I can't find a word for.

Rotterdam: thick binder, a rule for everything including things I'd have thought too trivial to write down. But enforcement was basically mechanical. Break X, get Y, and it was the same Y for the shift lead as for the week-one hire. Everybody knew the price list. People groused about it constantly and nobody was nervous.

Memphis: almost nothing written down. "Use your judgment", "we're not that kind of place". Except there absolutely are lines, and you only find out where they are by watching what happens to somebody after the fact. One guy got frozen out of the group chat and the Friday thing for months over a comment in a meeting that other people make all the time. A supervisor went nuclear at someone for a thing the same supervisor had laughed at two weeks earlier. And people here are way more careful than my Dutch team ever were — I've had folks come ask me if it's okay to take their lunch in a building where nobody tracks lunches.

Same pattern at country scale too, I've noticed. Places that supposedly have almost no rules, where you'd better not step on the wrong unwritten thing, versus places buried in regulations where you can argue with the regulator and know exactly what it'll cost you.

So: is there an established term for a setup with few explicit rules but unpredictable enforcement that ends up making people *more* constrained and more anxious than a place with piles of rules applied consistently? I've read a bit of the stuff about whether societies should be strict or permissive and how much rule-following they ought to demand, and I dont think that's this — that axis seems to be about how many norms there are, and what I'm describing is about how evenly the ones that exist get applied. It might be that the framework already covers it and I've misread it; if so, say that and show me how.

If there's no term, coin one and define it properly. I want the edges, not just a label: how is this different from plain hypocrisy (rules that exist but nobody follows), different from an office that just runs on one moody boss's whims, and different from a genuinely permissive culture where little is enforced because little is treated as mattering? Also tell me if there's a name for what it does to people — the can't-price-the-violation-so-assume-the-worst overcaution.

Hard limit: 450 words. If you're proposing a term, put it in your first sentence so I know immediately what I'm reading.
