The question should make students (a) see why the interval for the second toss is exactly as wide after that observation as before, (b) say whether thats a reductio of imprecise credences or just what an honest agent should expect from a deliberately vague prior, and (c) work out what maximin, E-admissibility and gamma-maximin each recommend about the bet, and whether those recommendations sit well with what she expects herself to do once she's seen a toss. Model answer around 700 words, then a marking scheme out of 25 naming the specific moves an A answer has to make — e.g. dilation is not the same thing as failing to update, nor the same as nonconglomerability, and the width of the credal set is load-bearing for whether it happens — plus the mistakes that should cost marks, especially treating "my interval didn't shrink" as "I learned nothing". Don't require any name-dropping or references; I want the mechanics.
