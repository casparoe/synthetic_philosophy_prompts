I manage two small-animal vet clinics that merged in January, and half my job now is arguing with staff about which old habits survive. I did a philosophy degree years back and Lewis on convention is the only thing I've found that actually describes what's going on, but I'm pretty sure some of the pushback doesn't fit his picture at all and I can't tell which. Here's the five things on my list. Rate each 0–10 for how much it's genuinely a convention in Lewis's sense — a regularity that persists because everyone expects everyone else to keep it up, where some other regularity would have done just as well — and then say separately, yes / no / mostly, whether writing down one clear rule and making sure everyone knows everyone else has seen it would get us converged within a month.

1. Appointment slots: the north clinic ran 20 minutes, the south ran 30. Receptionists on both sides say a mixed book wrecks the day because nobody knows what the person after them is expecting. Both lengths worked fine back when everyone was using one of them.

2. Chart notes: south wrote everything in SOAP format, north used its own freeform order. The vets who came over from north say SOAP is just better clinical reasoning and this isn't a matter of taste. About half the south vets agree with them.

3. Drug bin labels: two different but internally consistent naming systems. Nobody can find anything in the other clinic's cupboard, and everyone says they'd have been happy with either one if we'd all been doing the same thing from day one.

4. Surgery day: north blocked Tuesdays off for surgery, south just fitted it in around the appointments. North staff want Tuesday back and describe it as how things are done here. When I asked if they'd be fine with Thursday instead, most said no, Thursday's no good, and then couldn't say what was wrong with Thursday apart from Tuesday having always been Tuesday.

5. Phone answer: everyone's annoyed we dont have one script. But when I floated a script at the all-hands, three people insisted theirs was better, and I'm fairly sure the front desk at south mostly just likes answering it her way.

Rank them at the end, most convention-like to least. And for whichever lands last, tell me what kind of problem it actually is if it isn't a convention problem — because I keep trying to solve all five the same way, and two of them aren't budging.
