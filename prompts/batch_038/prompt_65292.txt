Zygmunt Bauman argued in Modernity and the Holocaust (1989) that the Holocaust was not a barbaric interruption of modern civilization but one of its realized possibilities: bureaucratic rationality, the technical division of labor, and the gardening state's drive to classify and weed out together suspended ordinary moral restraint, chiefly by inserting social distance between perpetrator and victim and by substituting technical for moral responsibility. Take the 1994 genocide against the Tutsi in Rwanda as a test case, and work from these facts rather than retrieving others: roughly a hundred days of killing in which perhaps half a million to eight hundred thousand people died; killing done at arm's length with machetes, clubs, and agricultural tools, much of it by neighbors, colleagues, and in-laws who knew their victims by name; yet organized from above by a state apparatus that used colonial-era Belgian identity cards to classify the population, a hate radio station to direct operations, roadblocks and lists to gather and screen people, and bulk machete imports arranged by businessmen close to the regime.

Go through Bauman's mechanisms one at a time. Which of them were load-bearing in Rwanda, which were simply absent, and which appear to run in reverse, with intimacy doing work that his account assigns to distance? Identify precisely where the framework goes silent rather than straining it to speak. Then say what the case does to the thesis: a genuine counterexample, an anomaly that forces revision, or an instance that was always outside its scope — and what the thesis would have to become if narrowed to industrially administered killing, including whether the narrowing destroys the point Bauman was making. Finally, turn the analysis on itself: does using Rwanda to test a theory built for the Holocaust already concede the comparability that the uniqueness debate denies, and does Bauman's own generalization about modernity license that comparison? Close with an honest statement of what this single case cannot settle about either the theory or the comparison.
