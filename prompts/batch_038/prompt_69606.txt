Hofstadter's careenium thought experiment asks you to picture a substance whose particles — simmballs — jostle, collide, and bind into clumps, where the clumps correspond to symbols and their attractions, fusions, and breakups correspond to thinking. The device is meant to show how a physical system can be driven by meaning without any non-physical ingredient: symbols really do push the substrate around, and nothing but simmball physics is doing the pushing.

Argue that the thought experiment fails at exactly the point it was designed to settle. If the simmball-level dynamics is causally closed, then the claim that a symbol structure caused the subsequent rearrangement of the clump adds nothing to collisions already fully accounted for, and is a redescription rather than a further fact; if it is not closed, the picture has reinstated the gap between levels it was meant to close. Distinguish this from the more familiar worry that higher-level causes are screened off by their physical realizers, since here the higher level is not supposed to be anything over and above a pattern in the realizers.

Then press the strongest reply available to Hofstadter — that a description in terms of colliding simmballs is no less an imposition of our interests than a description in terms of activated symbols, so no level is privileged as the locus of causation — and explain either why that reply fails or why accepting it drains the careenium of the reductionist ambition that made it worth telling.
