But organicism seems to walk right into that same trap, since it's often genuinely unclear whether some particles' activity counts as "a life." Does van Inwagen have a real way out of this, or does biting the bullet on vague existence end up costing him more than universalism's bloated ontology of gerrymandered fusions costs Lewis?
