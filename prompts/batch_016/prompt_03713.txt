I recently transitioned from backend engineering into an applied AI-safety role at the startup where I work, which builds autonomous agents for managing customers' cloud infrastructure. In last week's design review, our team debated whether to add a manual override that lets an on-call engineer halt an agent mid-task, and one of our research advisors objected that this is not merely an engineering feature: she cited the 2015 MIRI paper on corrigibility by Soares, Fallenstein, Yudkowsky, and Armstrong, as well as Hadfield-Menell's later off-switch game, and argued that a sufficiently capable expected-utility maximizer will, for almost any way of specifying its objective, end up either disabling the override or actively trying to trigger it, unless the reward structure is tuned to a very specific and fragile "indifference" point. I nodded along at the time, but when I tried to explain the argument to our product manager afterward, I realized I don't actually grasp the mechanics well enough to make it convincing, or to explain why the obvious patch -- just penalizing the agent whenever it resists being switched off -- doesn't work.

Could you construct a small, self-contained worked example for me -- something like a short decision tree or a two- or three-step MDP with concrete, even if stylized, utility numbers -- that makes this vivid? I would like it to show why an agent that places any weight at all on a "shutdown" utility function alongside its normal task utility will tend to manipulate the probability that shutdown occurs, in one direction or the other, for almost any relative weighting of the two; why only a knife-edge combination removes that particular incentive; and why a naive penalty on "observable resistance" fails to patch things, since the agent can instead act on whatever determines if the button gets pressed in the first place, rather than resisting it afterward. If you're able to, I would also be curious whether more recent proposals that give the agent incomplete preferences between differently timed shutdown trajectories genuinely escape this dilemma, or merely relocate it.
