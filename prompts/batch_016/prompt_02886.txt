Apparently his own son, and then the later editors of his collected papers, just quietly left it out for decades, and it only became widely known because Michael Dummett (like THE Frege scholar) dug up fragments of it in the 50s and was reportedly kind of devastated, since he'd always thought of Frege as this perfectly cold, rational logic-machine of a guy.

Here's the part I can't stop thinking about though.
