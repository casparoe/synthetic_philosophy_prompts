I teach a graduate seminar on philosophy of psychiatry, and a situation involving a close friend has been on my mind for weeks in a way that the usual nature-versus-nurture framing doesn't seem to resolve. My friend, a linguist in her early forties, spent much of last year absorbed in social media videos about adult ADHD before deciding, entirely on her own initiative, to seek a formal evaluation. She was duly diagnosed, joined several online communities organized around the condition, and has since taken to re-describing large stretches of her adult life through that lens, recasting old bouts of forgetfulness or restlessness as symptoms she simply lacked the vocabulary for at the time. A couple of students in my seminar mentioned noticing something similar with other diagnoses that have become fashionable online, so I don't think this is merely an idiosyncrasy of my friend's case.

What strikes me as genuinely puzzling is that, by her own account, she has grown noticeably more distractible and overwhelmed in ordinary situations, such as staff meetings, over the past year than she was before her diagnosis. When I gently suggested that the label itself might be shaping what she notices, what she expects of herself, and perhaps even how she behaves, she pushed back: if ADHD is a genuine underlying condition, she argued, then finally being properly diagnosed is exactly what would allow her to stop unconsciously compensating for symptoms she had spent two decades masking, so naturally they would become more visible now rather than less. I do not think she is wrong to resist my suggestion, but I do not think my original worry was confused either.

I cannot decide whether her diagnosis being real and her diagnosis being partly self-fulfilling are genuinely in tension, or whether we are both right about two different things that a clearer theory would keep apart. Could you help me work out what is actually going on here, conceptually, and whether there is a principled, rather than merely diplomatic, way of holding on to both halves of the story at once?
