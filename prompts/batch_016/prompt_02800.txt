What I did read was genuinely great though, better than most debuts I've finished cover to cover, and everyone in publishing knows blurbing off partial reads is basically an open secret anyway. But it still feels weird to assert something that confidently before I actually know, even if it turns out accurate later. Does that only become okay retroactively once its confirmed, or was it fine all along as long as it checks out in the end?
