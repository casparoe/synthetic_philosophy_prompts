My friend and I spent much of dinner last night arguing about Borges after she read that "The Garden of Forking Paths" anticipates the many-worlds interpretation of quantum mechanics. She pushed the claim further than I was willing to go, arguing that this was no mere coincidence of imagery: Borges's essays on time, especially "A New Refutation of Time," show that he had already worked out, philosophically, the same branching picture of reality that physicists only formalized decades later. I suspected this was a stretch but couldn't quite say why, especially since the many-worlds comparison itself is repeated so widely. Was Borges effectively arguing for a real, branching multiverse, or is my friend collapsing two quite different things into one?
