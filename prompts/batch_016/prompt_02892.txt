Random thing that happened to me a couple months ago and I still haven't fully resolved it. I was solo hiking, rolled my ankle bad, phone dead, loosing light fast. Eventually a guy on an ATV finds me and offers to drive me back to the trailhead, but says upfront he's pulled enough people out of these situations over the years that he can basically tell when someone's bullshitting him, so he's only doing it if he actually believes I'll venmo him $150 for the gas and hassle once we're back — no card reader, cash or venmo only, pure handshake deal. He studies my face for a few seconds, decides he buys it, and off we go.

We get back to the trailhead, my car's right there, he's got zero recourse if I just don't pay, I'm from three states away and never touching this trail system again, no witnesses, nothing. Standing in that parking lot I genuinely tried to construct an argument for paying him and couldn't find one, except that not paying felt monstrous. So I paid anyway.

I spend way too much time reading rationalist-adjacent blogs so part of me feels like this should be a Known Thing with a name, but I cant place it. Was that feeling just me being a sap about a decision that, by that point, literally couldn't change anything anymore? Or is "it can't change anything anymore" actually the wrong frame here? Genuinely can't tell which side is confused.
