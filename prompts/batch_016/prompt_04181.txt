Otto Neurath maintained that Isotype—his "International System of Typographic Picture Education"—could serve as a genuinely universal and neutral picture language, conveying statistical and scientific facts across linguistic and cultural boundaries without the ambiguities he associated with ordinary verbal language. Please argue that this ambition cannot be squared with Neurath's own coherentist epistemology, in particular his denial of any theory-neutral "given" in his account of protocol sentences and his image of the scientist as a sailor who must rebuild his ship plank by plank while remaining at sea.
