The Kleinberg/Mullainathan/Raghavan result is an honest-to-god proven theorem: you can't have calibration and equal false positive/negative rates across groups at the same time, unless the base rates happen to match or you've got a perfect predictor. So doesn't that mean the entire "algorithmic fairness" field, plus all the ProPublica-vs-Northpointe arguing over COMPAS, is basically just people refusing to accept a result that's already been proven? Feels like we should just tell everyone "sorry, mathematically impossible, pick your poison" and stop funding research into a question that's apparently been closed since 2016. Am I missing somthing here, or is nobody willing to admit the field's been mathematically dissolved?
