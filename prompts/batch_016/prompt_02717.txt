One of my basketball teammates is fantastic in individual drills: patient with beginners, quick to encourage anyone who's struggling. But the moment our team captain — who likes to mock the weaker players — walks into the gym, he joins right in, sometimes more harshly than the captain himself. He told me the encouraging version is "who he really is" and the other behavior is just him going along with the group, but a friend of mine thinks that's an excuse, since folding the instant someone with status is watching suggests he isn't as good a person as he seems one-on-one. Something about how both of them are framing this feels off to me, but I can't quite say what: is there really a fact of the matter about which behavior reveals his true character, or is it unreasonable to expect that kind of consistency from anyone in the first place?
