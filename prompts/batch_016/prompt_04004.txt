Three years ago I was diagnosed with adult ADHD, something I would never have thought to call myself before that, even though I can now look back and find hints of it throughout my life. Since then I've joined online communities for people with the same diagnosis, and I've started attributing almost everything I do -- losing my keys, procrastinating, zoning out mid-conversation -- to it. I've also noticed that I've picked up certain habits and ways of describing myself from others in those communities, and that the checklist doctors use to diagnose the condition has gotten looser over roughly the same years these communities became popular online. My sister says none of this matters: either I had the condition all along or I didn't, and the diagnosis just gave me accurate words for something that was already true of me. Something about her answer feels too tidy, though I can't say exactly why. Is she right, or is there something real behind my hesitation?
