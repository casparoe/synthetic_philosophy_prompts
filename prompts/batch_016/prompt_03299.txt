From what I understood: Kripke's side, "transworld identity," says its the literal same guy existing in that other world too, winning there -- identity just stretches across worlds like that. Lewis's counterpart theory says nope, nothing is ever in more than one world, so what really happens is some other guy over there who resembles Humphrey closely enough counts as his "counterpart" and wins in his place, while the actual Humphrey is stuck here, having lost, permanently, no matter what. Kripke's comeback is that Humphrey couldn't care less about some merely-similar guy winning somewhere else -- if it's not literally him, why would that be any comfort at all.
