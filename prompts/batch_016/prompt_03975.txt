(2) Dana objected that this throws away all the information about what's at stake. (6) Dana conceded this is a genuine cost of weighting by stakes — something like Pascal's mugging for ethics — but said the right response is to be more careful about which credences one is entitled to hold in the first place, not to abandon stakes-weighting and default to whichever theory happens to hold a bare majority of one's credence.
