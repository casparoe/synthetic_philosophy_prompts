My mom's been in home hospice with pancreatic cancer for about two months now, and the family meetings with the palliative team have basically turned into this ethics seminar nobody signed up for. Last week the nurse said we could bump up her sedation enough to pretty much wipe out the breakthrough pain days, but she'd be out of it most of the time and probably lose the good, lucid afternoons she still gets a couple times a week. My mom's sisters, Ruth and Denise, got into it over whether to do this, and I can't stop replaying the argument.

Ruth said we should just max out the sedation: a day of that level of pain isn't something three decent afternoons can "make up for," theres no exchange rate where enough good moments buy back agony, so if we can basically end the suffering we should, full stop. Denise said that proves too much — mom herself has said the good days make the bad stretches worth it to her, so Ruth's view just means pain counts so much more than anything else that it overrides what mom actually wants, and anyway if suffering can never be outweighed by anything, they shouldn't have let her do chemo either, since that was pain now for a maybe-payoff later. Ruth answered that chemo pain and this pain aren't the same category — nobody thinks ordinary discomfort outweighs an ordinary nice day, but past some point suffering just isn't the kind of thing you average against pleasant stuff anymore, same reason enough mild annoyance never adds up to outweighing someone being tortured. Denise came back with: fine, then who decides where that point is, that's awfully convenient, and if some suffering really is that far off the scale, the honest version of Ruth's view is that avoiding any risk of it is always worth ending things sooner rather than later, which isn't what Ruth actually believes and isn't what mom's chosen either.

Nobody "won," they just moved on to logistics. Can you go through Ruth's and Denise's points one move at a time and say who actually had the better argument at each step, not just an overall winner? Also curious what you think both of them are missing.
