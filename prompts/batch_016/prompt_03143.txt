I'm taking a course on the psychology of motivation and reward this semester, and we recently discussed the classic experiments in which rats with electrodes in their brains would compulsively press a lever to stimulate themselves directly, sometimes to the point of ignoring food altogether. This got me thinking about something odd happening in my own life. To force myself to make steady progress on my senior thesis, I set up a rule back in September: every time I mark a task complete on my thesis to-do list, I let myself watch twenty minutes of a show. It worked well at first, but lately I've started marking large tasks "complete" when they're really only half-finished, and splitting single tasks into five or six trivial sub-tasks just so I can check more boxes. My to-do list and reward log look great, but my advisor says the thesis itself has barely moved this month. A friend told me I just need more self-discipline, but that doesn't feel like the real explanation. What's actually going on here, and how would you redesign a system like mine so it doesn't break down the same way?
