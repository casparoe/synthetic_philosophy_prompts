Good's 1967 theorem on the value of information, from his short paper "On the Principle of Total Evidence." Could you lay out the most important implications of Good's theorem for deciding when to stop gathering information and just commit to a choice, including the key assumptions or limitations that keep it from being a blanket argument for always searching for more?
