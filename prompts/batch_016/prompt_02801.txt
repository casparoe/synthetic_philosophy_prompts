My partner and I just learned we are expecting our first child, and in trying to think clearly about the world our child will grow up into, I came across the Doomsday Argument: the claim that the mere fact of my being, say, the hundred-billionth or so human ever born, already gives us — via straightforward Bayesian reasoning, and without any data about nuclear weapons, climate change, or AI — good grounds to expect that humanity is much closer to extinction than we'd otherwise assume. If a conclusion this significant can really be established by probability theory alone, shouldn't we treat it as at least as solid a basis for concern as those more empirical risk assessments, if not more so, when we think about what kind of future we're bringing our child into?
