so i've been reading leviathan and it's kind of wild how serious hobbes was about wanting political philosophy to work exactly like euclidean geometry -- start from a couple of self evident defintions about human nature and just deduce the whole commonwealth like it's a theorem. very proto-lesswrong energy imo, "lets just be way more rigorous about this." and it wasn't just talk either, the guy actually cracked the quadrature of the circle, which is presumably part of why he trusted the method enough to found a whole political system on it. bugs me nobody's pulled off the same trick for ethics or political theory since though -- did people just lose their nerve at some point, or is there some deeper reason axiomatic deduction only really works in math?
