Compare newton's bucket argument for absolute space with the hole argument against spacetime substantivalism (Earman & Norton's argument that leans on GR's diffeomorphism invariance). Its kind of funny that one gets used to argue FOR treating space(time) as a real substantival thing and the other gets used to argue against it, just applied to different theories of motion. Do these two actually conflict with each other, or could someone feel the full force of the bucket experiment while also taking the hole argument seriously? And if you had to pick an escape route -- relationism, "sophisticated" substantivalism, or just biting the bullet on indeterminism -- which seems like the smallest price to pay, and why?
