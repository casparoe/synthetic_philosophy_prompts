In my philosophy of mind seminar, we just read David Chalmers's 2018 paper introducing the "meta-problem of consciousness"—his term for the puzzle of why physical creatures like us insist that consciousness is so hard to explain. Since answering that question still seems to require explaining why we have the very experiences that make consciousness seem mysterious, I don't see how the meta-problem is any less intractable than the original hard problem; it looks like a mere relabeling rather than genuine progress, and one that mainly serves illusionists who already deny that phenomenal experience is what it seems to be. Am I missing something about why Chalmers and others treat it as a distinct, tractable problem worth taking seriously in its own right?
