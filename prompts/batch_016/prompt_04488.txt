I'm a PhD student in moral psychology. At a joint psychology-philosophy reading group, two people had a genuine disagreement over how to interpret the literature that followed Knobe's chairman study (82% of respondents judged that a chairman who knowingly harmed the environment as a side effect of a profit-driven policy did so "intentionally," versus only 23% who judged likewise when an identical policy happened to help the environment).

(1) Sonia argued that Machery's trade-off hypothesis settles it: his "extra-dollar" vignette reproduces the same asymmetry from a side effect that is a mere financial cost, no moral valence involved, so the effect just tracks whether an agent knowingly incurs a cost for a benefit. Knobe's larger claim, that our concept of intentional action is inherently moralized, is therefore overreach.

(2) Dev objected that trade-off can't be the whole story, since Uttich and Lombrozo (and, independently, Holton) get the same asymmetry from side effects that merely violate a conventional or statistical norm and cost the agent nothing at all. Something broader than "a cost knowingly incurred" must be doing the work.

(3) Sonia replied that "norm violation, moral or otherwise" is barely a theory -- it just relabels the finding that bad or abnormal outcomes get called intentional, whereas trade-off at least names an independently motivated bit of means-end reasoning.

(4) Dev answered that the norm account isn't mere relabeling, since it predicts the asymmetry should strengthen as the violated norm is more firmly held, regardless of cost -- and that has been tested directly. He then conceded that Sripada's "deep self" studies reproduce a similar asymmetry using cases engineered to remove both norm violation and cost, where only concordance with an agent's stipulated values varies -- which may mean neither account has isolated what's truly fundamental.

Both know this literature well, and neither is strawmanning the other. Could you go through moves (1)-(4) and say who has the better of it at each step, not just who wins overall, and flag anything important that both Sonia and Dev seem to be missing?
