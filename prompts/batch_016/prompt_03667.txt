I want her to lose this exchange decisively, so I need the colleague's invocation of Rawls to be philosophically airtight rather than a caricature of the theory. Could you help me sharpen his rebuke so that it faithfully represents the full force of Rawls's actual argument against bringing scripture into a debate of this kind, and suggest the most devastating line on which he might close?
