This semester I'm taking a bioethics seminar, and a situation in my own family has been on my mind. My older sister has bipolar disorder, and she has asked me to promise her something specific: the next time she starts entering a manic episode and insists she is fine, wants to stop her medication, or wants to leave the hospital against advice, I should disregard whatever she says in that moment, override her wishes, and call her psychiatrist regardless of how furious she becomes or how much she claims she will never forgive me. She wants my agreement now, while she is stable and thinking clearly, precisely because she does not trust her own judgment once an episode begins. Part of me feels I should simply honor what she is asking, but another part worries that overriding the stated wishes of someone who seems conscious and verbally competent crosses a line, especially since in the moment I will have no reliable way of telling whether she is in the grip of an episode or has genuinely changed her mind. Should I agree to this, and if I do, am I bound to carry it out no matter what she says when the moment actually arrives?
