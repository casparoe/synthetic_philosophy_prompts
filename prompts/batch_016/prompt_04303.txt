I'm taking a seminar on personal identity this term, and we've just finished the chapters on teletransportation and the Branch-Line Case in Part III of Reasons and Persons. Since the Branch-Line Case shows that the Replica on Mars and the original who is left dying on Earth cannot both be identical to the person who was scanned — identity being a one-to-one relation — this seems to establish that even ordinary, non-branching teletransportation must fail to preserve identity, and so is really just a painless method of killing the original and manufacturing a duplicate elsewhere. Given this, I don't understand why psychological continuity (Relation R) is still treated as a serious candidate for what matters in survival, rather than being abandoned in favor of a straightforwardly physical criterion of identity.
