So Galen Strawson's "Basic Argument" against moral responsibility only really works if determinism is true — the whole thing hinges on your actions being the output of a chain of causes you never picked. But we know from quantum mechanics that the universe isnt fully deterministic, theres actual randomness under the hood, not just a deterministic process we're too ignorant to predict (kinda like a PRNG vs an actual hardware random number generator). So why do free-will skeptics still treat Strawson's argument as a knockdown case against moral responsibility, when indeterminism should leave some room for real responsibility after all?
