Most of section 6, where he runs through a list of objections to the imitation game, is roughly what you'd expect -- the theological objection, Lady Lovelace's objection, the "machines can't really be creative" stuff. Honestly this kind of broke my brain, ngl. Turing is supposed to be like, THE guy for rigorous thinking about computation, and here he is granting ESP almost the same evidentiary status as long division.
