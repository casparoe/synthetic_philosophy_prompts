Give me their argument as a real back-and-forth, each one pressing on the weak spot in the other's case rather than just restating their own -- and somewhere in there have the philosopher notice that the economist's story sounds a lot like Socrates' old claim that nobody ever really acts against their better judgement, and see how she answers that.
