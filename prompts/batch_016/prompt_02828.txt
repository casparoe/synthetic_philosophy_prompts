Been down a rabbit hole on no-trade theorems lately (partly cause I'm noodling on a little bot for Manifold/Polymarket-style markets), and I think I've found a real contradiction but can't tell if its just me not getting it. The theorem says two rational Bayesians who share a prior can't agree on a bet once each one knows the other is willing to take it, since the willingness to trade itself leaks the info and the whole deal unravels. But isn't the whole premise behind prediction markets (and EMH-type arguments generally) that prices are informative precisely because people trade on private info? If rational trading on private information is provably impossible, where does the info in the price even come from? Feels like one of these two stories has to be wrong, so which is it — or is there some assumption doing all the load-bearing work here that I'm missing?
