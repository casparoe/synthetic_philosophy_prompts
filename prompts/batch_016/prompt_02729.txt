I am drawing on my own family for a novel, and I have run into a problem that has nothing to do with craft. My older sister remembers our late father as cold and domineering; I remember an anxious man who was mostly gentle; and an aunt who saw him only at holidays insists he was the warmest, most easygoing man she knew. None of us is lying, and none of us is demonstrably misremembering. My sister maintains that if her picture of him is correct, the other two must be comforting fictions, and, privately, I sometimes wonder whether the same could be said of hers. Is one of these three accounts simply correct and the others mistaken, or could each be capturing something real about a man too complicated for any single account to hold? I would like to work this out properly before deciding how to render him on the page.
