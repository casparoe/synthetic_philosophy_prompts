So in my phil of science seminar we've been covering Solomonoff induction as basically *the* rigorous version of Occam's razor: you weigh rival hypotheses by 2^-K(x), where K(x) (Kolmogorov complexity) is supposed to be this fully objective, language-independant measure of how simple something really is. If thats true, why do scientists even bother arguing about which theory is simpler (like string theory vs loop quantum gravity) instead of just calculating the Kolmogorov complexity of each one's shortest description and letting that settle it? Feels like it could end a lot of dumb debates tbh.
