R. Lucas and Roger Penrose. Could you compare the two arguments precisely — what does the move from consistency to soundness actually buy Penrose, and does it dodge the standard objection to Lucas (that, by Gödel's own second theorem, nobody can prove their own system's consistency from inside it), or does it just relocate the same problem one level up?
