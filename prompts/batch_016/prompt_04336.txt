My own field sidestepped much of the trouble by conventionally demanding five-sigma significance for any discovery claim, but I have come to suspect that this was always a pragmatic convention rather than a genuine resolution of the underlying interpretive dispute between frequentist and Bayesian conceptions of evidence.
