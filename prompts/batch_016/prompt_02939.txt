So I've been down a Davidson rabbit hole for like two weeks now, started from an old SEP tab I never closed on "radical interpretation" and it's kind of eaten my brain since. Quick recap mostly for my own sake: the radical interpreter starts with zero prior grip on the speaker's language or beliefs, just behavior (assent/dissent to sentences given observed circumstances), and has to bootstrap a Tarski-style truth theory and a theory of belief at the same time, held together by the Principle of Charity -- you can't fix what someone means without simultaneously assuming they're mostly rational and mostly right by your lights. Fine, fairly standard stuff, comes up on LW every so often re: the intentional stance. But the part that actually seems load bearing for the question I want to ask you is the later triangulation stuff: the claim that thought and meaning (and even the very idea of "getting it right or wrong about the world") only show up once you've got two creatures reacting to a shared external cause AND to each other's reactions. No triangulation, no objectivity, no content, on his view.

Here's what's been bugging me and I definitly can't be the first person to think about this: say you could somehow sit Davidson down (RIP 2003, so he missed literally all of this) and walk him through what an LLM actually is -- transformer architecture, training setup, next-token prediction over a scrape of basically all human writing -- in terms he'd actually understand rather than hype-speak. What do you think his real verdict would be on whether such a system is even a *candidate* for radical interpretation at all, i.e. whether "what does the model mean by this sentence" is a question with a real answer, or whether its just charity-satisfying noise with nobody home? I'm especially stuck on the triangulation angle: pretraining data is basically the fossilized record of millions of humans who already did the triangulating, on a world they shared with each other -- so would he say the model inherits that grounding secondhand, or would he call that cheating, like you can't get content just by ingesting the output of triangulation without ever doing the triangulating yourself? Bonus points if you bring anomalous monism into it re: whether he'd even grant the model "mental events" in the first place, token-identical to physical states or not. Don't just hand-wave toward "well Davidson was a naturalist so he'd probably be sympathetic to machines" -- actually run the machinery on this and commit to an actual answer.
