Random thing that's been stuck in my head from a piece I'm reporting on classical musicians and stage fright: apparently its a total open secret that a lot of them take a low dose of a beta blocker (propranolol, same stuff thats prescribed for blood pressure and irregular heartbeats) before auditions and big performances. One violinist I talked to said it's no different from a diabetic taking insulin, since some people are just born with a twitchier nervous system and the pill evens that out. But a friend of hers who also auditions for orchestra jobs says that's nonsense, that it's basically the same as doping in sports since they're both competing for the same handful of chairs. Now I can't stop chewing on it: is there an actual meaningful difference between fixing a problem someone has and just giving them an edge over everyone else, or is that just a line we draw so the first one feels ok and the second doesn't?
