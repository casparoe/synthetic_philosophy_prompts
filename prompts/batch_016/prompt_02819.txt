So this happened over drinks after a few of us watched one of those space documentaries. (1) Dave said the fact that the physical constants are so precisely set for life to be possible -- gravity, the cosmological constant, all of it -- is basically evidence of a designer, since the odds of it happening randomly are absurdly small. (2) Mara said thats a selection effect: we could only ever exist to notice a universe whose constants happen to permit life, so it's not actually surprising and isn't evidence of anything. (3) Dave said that's a cop-out, and compared it to a guy facing a firing squad of 50 expert marksmen who all somehow miss -- he doesn't just shrug and think 'well of course they missed, or I wouldnt be here to wonder about it,' he assumes something's up. So 'you could only ever observe a world compatible with your own existence' doesn't actually kill the need for an explanation. (4) Mara switched to the multiverse: if there's billions of universes out there with random constants, it's no surprise some turn out life-friendly, and we obviously ended up in one of those, no designer needed. (5) Dave said that's actually a classic mistake -- like assuming other people must be gambling elsewhere in the casino tonight just because you personally rolled double sixes. All those unseen universes existing doesn't make it any less surprising that THIS universe, the one we're actually in, got the winning numbers, unless you've got some reason to expect you specifically would end up in this one. Mara got kind of annoyed and didn't really have a comeback, and we moved on to something else.
