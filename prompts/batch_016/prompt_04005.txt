My older sister has an uncanny knack for telling when I truly mean something versus when I am simply saying it to get what I want, and she has never once been wrong. She recently proposed a deal: if, by tonight, she becomes genuinely convinced that I intend to eat a whole raw onion tomorrow afternoon, she will hand me fifty dollars immediately. She has made clear that she does not care whether I actually eat the onion afterward — there is no penalty if I quietly change my mind once the money is mine, and she will never bring it up again either way; all that matters to her is whether I truly intend to do it tonight. The trouble is, I already know that once the money is in my hand, I will have no reason at all to put myself through something as unpleasant and pointless as eating a raw onion. Since I already know that about myself, I cannot talk myself into actually intending to do it — every attempt just feels like I am performing the intention rather than forming a real one. Is it actually possible to honestly meet her condition, or does knowing myself this well make the deal impossible to win?
