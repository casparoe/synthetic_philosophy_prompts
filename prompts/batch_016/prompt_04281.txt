So I've been down a rabbit hole on the replication crisis in psychology and all the p-hacking scandals, and I keep running into two totally different takes on Fisher. So if you could plop Fisher down at a psych conference today and show him a paper that barely scraped past p=.049 after some 'exploratory' tweaking, plus preregistration, registered reports, giant multi-lab replication projects, and the ASA basically telling everyone to stop treating .05 like a magic line -- what do you honestly think he'd say? Would preregistration strike him as fixing the actual thing he cared about, or missing it?
