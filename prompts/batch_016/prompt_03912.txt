So the Boltzmann brain thing in cosmology has been bugging me. If a theory predicts that random fluctuations create way more 'brains' than real evolved observers, can't I just rule out being one of those fluke brains myself -- my memories are consistent and the world around me keeps obeying physical laws moment to moment instead of acting random or dreamlike? Seems like that's already good evidence I'm a real observer and not some brain that fluctuated into existence a second ago, so why do physicists treat this like such a deep unsolved problem insted of just checking?
