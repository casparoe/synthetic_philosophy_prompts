I have no formal background in philosophy, but I've spent a fair amount of my free time this past year reading about Thomas Hobbes, and one thing keeps striking me as funny: Hobbes treated geometry as the one genuinely certain science, apparently because we construct its figures ourselves rather than merely guess at causes we can't inspect. Yet this is the same man who spent almost twenty-five years insisting, against every mathematician who corrected him, that he had squared the circle, and who never once conceded the point even as Wallis repeatedly refuted his constructions. Could you write me a joke built around that irony? I'd like the humor to actually depend on knowing the specifics of Hobbes's epistemology of geometry and the Wallis dispute, rather than landing as a generic joke about a stubborn crank who won't admit he's wrong.
