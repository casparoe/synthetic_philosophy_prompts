Feels like this should update us pretty hard toward taking Kurzweil/Yudkowsky-style singularity predictions more seriously, since even von Neumann apparently converged on the same conclusion totally independently. Am I missing something here, or is this actually a pretty underrated data point in AI risk discussions?
