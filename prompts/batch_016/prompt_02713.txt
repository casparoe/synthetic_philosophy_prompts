So for my seminar on Frege and the origins of analytic philosophy we spent the last two weeks on sense/reference and the Begriffsschrift, and right at the end our professor mentioned, almost in passing, that Frege kept a diary in 1924 (the year before he died) that's apparently full of anti-parliamentary, antisemitic ranting, and that it didn't get properly published until decades later because the Nachlass editors reportedly didn't think it counted as "scientific" enough to belong with the rest of his papers. There's an optional short response paper due next week where we get to stake out a position on whether stuff like this should matter to how we read a philosopher's technical work, and I don't want to just write the obvious "seperate the art from the artist, judge the arguments not the man" paper — I mentioned the assignment to my roommate (not a phil person) and that's literally the first thing she said, so I'm guessing half the class will turn in basically that.

Here's the angle I'm considering instead, though I'm honestly not sure it holds up. Frege is famous for insisting, especially in the Grundgesetze, that a genuine concept has to have totally sharp boundaries — no borderline cases allowed, or else its not a real concept at all in his sense. From what I've picked up secondhand (a blog post and a couple of abstracts — haven't read the German original or even the Mendelsohn translation properly), in the diary he also frets about the practical problem that you can't write an enforceable antisemitic law unless you can specify a crisp, non-vague criterion for who counts as a Jew. My instinct is that these two things aren't just sitting next to each other coincidentally — that his logical hang-up about vagueness is doing actual work in how he thinks through the political problem — which would make the diary at least a little bit philosophically relevant rather than just an embarrassing footnote to his biography. But I could easily be pattern-matching where there's nothing there, and I saw in a comments thread somewhere that people accused another writer of doing exactly that with this same example.

Can you give me some honest comments before I commit to this for the paper?
