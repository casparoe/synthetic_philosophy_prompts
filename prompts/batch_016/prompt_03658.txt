That seems nuts to me, Shakespeare and Wagner have obviously "infected" enormous numbers of people across generations and cultures, arguably way more effectively than most things he approves of. I said that sounds like a totally separate, moralizing criterion that has nothing to do with "does it infect people" and he's just smuggling it in wherever convenient. Priya thinks it's a natural extension of the same idea, not a separate one. We argued about it for like an hour and got nowhere.
