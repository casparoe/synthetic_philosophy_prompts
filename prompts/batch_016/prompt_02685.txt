So I fell down a rabbit hole last night and found out David Lewis -- yeah, the modal realism/possible-worlds guy -- actually wrote a short paper called "Do We Believe in Penal Substitution?" I'm not a philosophy person at all, but his key example seemed pretty airtight: we're totally fine with your friend paying a hefty fine for you, but nobody would accept your friend serving your prison sentence in your place. Since a rigorous analytic philosopher basically caught that logical inconsistency, doesnt that mean the whole "Jesus was punished in our place" idea is just resting on a mistake that the rest of us don't make? Kinda surprised this doesn't come up more whenever people debate the atonement.
