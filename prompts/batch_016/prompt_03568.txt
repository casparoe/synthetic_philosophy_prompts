Rhoda is a "sophisticated" REU-maximizer with Allais-type preferences, so overall she prefers gamble L1 to L2. Briggs's objection: there's a strategy available to her - take the deal, then pick L1 anyway at the end - that nets sweetened-L1, which strictly dominates what she actually gets. Buchak says this strategy was never really "available" to sophisticated Rhoda at any single time.

that feels like question-begging to me but i can't quite pin down why. can you argue for the strongest version of Briggs's side here - that a strategy counts as available as long as each step is individually choosable, even if no time-slice of Rhoda ever picks the whole package at once - so sophisticated REU-maximizers really can rationally end up choosing a dominated strategy?
