For over a year we've run a "streak bonus" leaderboard on a rule Nora made up early on and has applied by feel since -- never written down, the rest of us picked it up from her weekly calls. Every past streak had topped out around 18 days, so when Theo hit 40 last month, Nora's bonus felt way too small given the pattern from shorter streaks. He asked her to just state the rule; when she tried, he showed it contradicts three of her own past scores. Fine, she said, then you say what it is -- his version contradicted a couple of old scores too, just different ones. Then it got weirdly philosophical: Theo said there has to be some fact about what rule she meant, even if she can't state it, and his number is closer to it than hers. Nora said no, she genuinely doesn't think there's any such fact -- every past score fits tons of different rules, and the only thing ever fixed was the old totals, never a rule behind them. Theo said that's insane, it'd mean nobody's ever really followed a rule correctly, here or anywhere. Nora shrugged and said maybe that's just true. i got dragged in to referee this at midnight and have no idea who's got the better of it. Can you go through the exchange move by move, say who's more right at each step rather than just an overall winner, and flag anything you think both of them are missing?
