Write a dialogue between two metaphysicians arguing over how to stop Bradley's regress and thereby secure the unity of the proposition. One defends Frege's idea that concepts are inherently "unsaturated," so that they combine with objects without needing any further relation to glue them together; the other defends Armstrong's view that states of affairs are ontologically fundamental, non-mereological unities, with particulars and universals merely abstracted from them. Have each press the other on whether their preferred move genuinely blocks the regress or simply relocates the same brute, unexplained unity onto a new primitive.
