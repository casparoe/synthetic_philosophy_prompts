I spent my career in ecology and only got into philosophy in the last couple of years, so forgive me if I'm muddling a bit of the terminology. I keep comming back to Schopenhauer's claim that music is the highest of the arts because, unlike every other art, it doesn't copy the Ideas but is a direct copy of the Will itself. I want to defend this, because it looks self-contradictory: if aesthetic contemplation is supposed to free us from the Will, how can the art that most directly mirrors the Will be the one that frees us most? Please argue for the position that this is not a bug but a feature — that precisely because music presents the Will without any particular object of desire, it is the only art that lets us contemplate the essence of existence without being pulled back into wanting, whereas every other art, by tying itself to particular Ideas, re-awakens desire. Make the case as tighly as you can.
