Student answer: The Humean base is the totality of local qualitative facts about the particular things and events in spacetime, what Lewis calls the mosaic, with no appeal to anything nomological or modal. The best system account ranges over all true deductive systems and picks the one that is strongest, i.e. the one that deduces the most truths about the mosaic, since simplicity is just a way of measuring strength. The laws of nature are then the salient theorems of this best system. This is a Humean view because everything, including the laws, supervenes on the mosaic, so there are no extra fundamental necessities. Since Humeanism denies necessary connections, the BSA is really just a sophisticated version of the regularity theory: the laws just describe the regularities that are in the mosaic. And the laws are contingent, since a different mosaic would have selected a different best system.
