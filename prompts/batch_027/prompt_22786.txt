Second, whether the non-haecceitist, by denying that the spheres differ in any qualitative property, is thereby committed to holding that Black's universe is impossible, or whether she can accept the scenario and draw a different conclusion. On balance, which of the two responses do you find handles the triviality problem more cleanly, and why?
