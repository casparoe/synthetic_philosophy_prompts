Write an essay on whether Bradley's regress argument, applied to the unity of the proposition, is a genuine threat to the coherence of relational facts, or whether it dissolves once the distinction between vicious and benign regresses is drawn. The essay should weigh the main responses — including the claim that relations are primitive, the internal-relation reply, and adverbial or recombinationist accounts of fact-construction — and should reach and justify a conclusion rather than ending on a balanced note.
