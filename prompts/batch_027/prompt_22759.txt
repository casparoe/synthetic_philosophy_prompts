For a creative writing assignment, I'd like you to write a short story that turns on a specific idea from the philosophy of language: the gap between what a word literally means and the social force it actually carries in use. Set it in a fictional community and invent one of its slurs — a word whose dictionary definition is completely harmless, just a plain descriptor, but which has, through its history, come to carry a charge of contempt that its surface meaning never mentions. I want the story's real tension to live in that gap: a character who uses the word "merely descriptively," trusting the dictionary, only to find that the force travels with the word no matter what the speaker intends. Please make the distinction between the word's content and its force do real structural work in the plot rather than just being mentioned, and keep it under about 1,500 words.
