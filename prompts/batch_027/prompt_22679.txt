4. variational preferences, expected utility minus a general nonnegative ambiguity index (Maccheroni–Marinacci–Rustichini),
5. Choquet expected utility over a non-additive capacity (Schmeidler).

Please give your answer as a single JSON block with two keys: "ranking", an array listing the five models in order from best to worst on that dimension, and "reasons", an object mapping each model name to a one-sentence justification of its placement.
