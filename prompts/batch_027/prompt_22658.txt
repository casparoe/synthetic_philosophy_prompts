One applicant argued that if we fund their $400 experiment there's a 1-in-20,000 chance it produces a result worth roughly $100M, so the expected value is about $5,000 and any rational committee should just fund it. Half the panel is sold on the expected-value argument and half thinks its a straight-up Pascal's mugging. Can you draft the panel's decision memo -- fund or not -- with reasoning the applicant could actually respond to? I want you to really grapple with why the expected value might be misleading here (the probability is the applicant's own estimate, for one) rather than just concluding muggings are bad and rejecting.
