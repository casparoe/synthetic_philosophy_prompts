I maintain windowing systems and browser rendering pipelines for a living, with nine years of professional experience in operating-systems infrastructure, and I think naturally in terms of front ends and back ends, event loops and schedulers, buffers and caches, APIs and abstractions that deliberately hide implementation detail, and logs and debuggers that reveal it.

I would like a systematic restatement in that working vocabulary of the user-illusion account of consciousness sometimes associated with Daniel Dennett, followed by an honest report on what does not survive the move. For present purposes, take the account to hold roughly the following three commitments: first, that what introspection presents as a seamless, detailed, present-tense phenomenal world is a simplified control interface produced by massively parallel and distributed neural activity with no central viewer; second, that sincere reports of ineffable qualitative properties are best understood as the system usefully misdescribing its own discriminative capacities rather than detecting intrinsic properties of experience; and third, that natural selection would favour such a simplifying interface because it enables fast prediction, action selection, and post hoc justification.

Please proceed in three stages. First, translate each of those three commitments into my engineering vocabulary as faithfully as you can, showing for each which engineering counterpart you are invoking and why. Second, apply the translation to two fresh cases of my devising: a digital audio workstation that displays a single continuous waveform and a single playhead position while in fact stitching together buffered chunks, applying latency compensation, and retrospectively correcting timing; and an on-device assistant that, when asked why it declined a calendar invitation, produces a fluent and confident reason that bears only a loose relation to the constraint-satisfaction process that actually produced the decision. Explain in each case what the story looks like from inside the interface and what it looks like from the back-end engineering perspective. Third, provide a residue report organised under three headings: claims that map cleanly, claims that map only with distortion with an account of what had to be stretched, and claims or distinctions in the original picture that have no expression in interface engineering at all. Conclude by giving a considered view on whether each item of residue reveals a limitation of the engineering vocabulary or suggests that the original philosophical claim was less clear than it appeared.

Please write your answer in British English, using British spelling and usage throughout, in a formal and carefully structured style suitable for an experienced but philosophically non-specialist engineer.
