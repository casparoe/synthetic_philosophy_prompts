ok so I volunteered to mediate this dumb endless fight in my town and I am regretting it already. On one side is Maya, who runs a small gallery and is hardcore into the institutional view — like, stuff counts as art when the artworld confers that status through galleries, curators, that whole system. On the other side is Dev, who runs our community garden and is all about the aesthetic function view — like, stuff counts as art when it is made with the core aim of giving people a focused aesthetic experience, wherever it happens.

The flashpoint is this thing the garden built last spring called the Scent Seed Library. Its basically a bike pedal powered shed that puffs out recreated smells of three apple varieties that used to grow here but died out, plus shelves where people leave and take seeds for free, plus volunteers tell stories about the orchards while you pedal and sniff. It was never meant for a gallery, its free and super useful for gardeners, but Maya started bringing her biennial tour group there and calling it the strongest new piece in town, and now the town council has one grant left for art projects and both Maya and Dev want it.

Can you draft what their adversarial collaboration joint statement would look like if they actually cooperated for once. I want something both Maya and Dev could sign. So like first lay out the claims they actually agree on about the Library and about art in general, then a table or list that states their leftover disagreements super precisely but in wording each of them would accept for their own side, then spell out the cruxes where settling something would actually move one of them, and for each crux give a concrete test case or what-if both would agree is relevant, with what each side predicts would happen. And if some of it is just them using the word art differently, call that out seperately.

oh and to make it harder, also run the same lens over this second thing: a perfume company popped up downtown using basically the same pedal-puff tech but just to make people go wow and buy expensive perfume. Is that art by either lights, and why the answer is or is not the same as for the Library.
