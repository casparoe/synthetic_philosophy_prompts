Having recently been co-opted onto my parish council, I have become preoccupied by a pattern of compliance I observe in myself and my neighbours. We pay council tax willingly, attend consultations, and expect the police and bins to function, yet we routinely and quietly ignore particular rules we regard as pointless, for example leaving bins out a day early despite the fixed collection timetable and using a closed footpath across a field because the official diversion adds twenty minutes, without any wish to protest or to invite others to do the same. Is there an established name for this quiet, selective, non-protest noncompliance by people who otherwise accept the system, and if not, what should we call it. I do not want a bare label; please explain what the phenomenon is, what it is not, and where its edges lie by comparing it to the two or three closest established ideas and saying which fits best and why the others fail. Please answer in British English as a numbered list of exactly five items, each of one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion, and use one item to state which argument or consideration would most likely change your mind about the classification.
