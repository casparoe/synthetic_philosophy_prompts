Compose a 14-line poem in blank verse suitable for opening an undergraduate lecture on decision theory. The first twelve lines should form two six-line stanzas in strict parallel, the first attending only to the pile on the left and the second matching it line for line for the pile on the right, the two piles being identical in every hunger-relevant respect before a perfectly hungry ass. The closing couplet should break the symmetry by having the ass move to one side though given no better reason for that side, so that the form itself enacts the point that rationality can permit picking without preferring. Follow the poem with a brief prose note stating where you simplified the scholastic background or relied on a contested understanding of indifference.
