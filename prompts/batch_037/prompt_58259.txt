you have to include these three stories somewhere in the 7, you can reword them but keep the point: Maya knows everything about why running would be good for her, doctor explained it, stats and all, she is calm and clearheaded and still feels absolutely zero pull to run, does she still have a reason to run; Dev scammed his little brother out of savings, he gets that everyone calls it wrong, but even after a month of careful honest reflection he would still feel zero pull to pay it back, does the fact it was morally wrong by itself give him a reason to pay it back; Lena really wants to drink some cleaning fluid because she mistakenly believes it will boost her immunity, is what she has reason to do fixed by her actual mistaken want, or by what she would want if she knew the truth, or by something else entirely.

keep it fun and a bit snarky, not textbooky, so the result tells each of us what we actually believe from our case picks rather than what label we thought we liked.
