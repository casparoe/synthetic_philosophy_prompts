Please write a short story of approximately 1,400 to 1,800 words suitable for use as a discussion opener in an undergraduate seminar on authority and legal self-reference.

Set the story in a small residential housing cooperative governed by a self-amending charter in which changing the rules is itself a move governed by the rules. For purposes of the story, stipulate that the charter initially contains only the following four provisions: 1. All decisions, including amendments to this charter, are made by simple majority vote of residents present, except where the charter provides otherwise. 2. Each provision is designated as either mutable, which may be amended by majority vote, or immutable, which may be amended only by unanimous vote. 3. A proposal to change the mutable or immutable designation of any provision must pass by whatever threshold is currently required for amending that provision. 4. No amendment shall remove the right of any resident to propose further amendments.

Let the central plot turn on the following event at the annual meeting. A resident proposes an omnibus amendment, carried 7 to 5, that would both designate itself as immutable and revise Provision 3 so that any future change to any immutability designation requires unanimity. Some residents argue the motion has clearly passed under the majority rule in effect when it was voted on. Others argue it has clearly failed, because under its own terms a change to immutability rules required unanimity, and the charter provides no neutral rule for deciding which version of the amendment procedure governs the vote on its own adoption.

Your story should dramatize this paradox of self-amendment rather than explain it in the abstract. Develop three or four residents with distinct, plausible stakes in the outcome beyond love of logic, show at least two internally coherent interpretations of what the rules now require, and show why the charter cannot settle the disagreement from within. Do not resolve the impasse by appeal to an outside court, by force or trickery, or by authorial clarification of the correct reading. End instead with the community having to act in practical life while remaining divided about what its own rules are. Keep the tone realistic and character-driven for nonspecialist readers while preserving the procedural precision of the dispute.
