I am a philosophy professor chairing our department's decision whether to grant a junior colleague an additional semester of leave to finish a first book she is confident will be completed on schedule. Draft the joint statement of an adversarial collaboration between an advocate of the outside view, who stresses base rates and epistemic modesty, and an advocate of the inside view, who stresses the causal details of this author and project. Present it as a numbered list of claims both sides can sign followed by a table of residual disagreements. The agreed claims should cover when base rates are informative, when case-specific details override them, and what responsible confidence requires in this context. Formulate each disagreement so that the side holding it would endorse the wording, identify the cruxes that would move at least one side if settled, and for each crux describe a concrete test, comparison case, or departmental record both sides agree would bear on it, recording each side's prediction in advance.
