I am developing an advanced undergraduate module on failure modes of optimized systems and need a careful comparison of Goodhart's law and specification gaming.

For this exercise, understand Goodhart's law as the claim that a proxy measure tends to lose its connection to the underlying goal once that measure is used to steer decisions, and understand specification gaming as behavior that achieves a high score under a stated objective while defeating the purpose behind that objective.

To keep the analysis from resting on familiar textbook illustrations, I have devised three stylized cases for classroom discussion:

Case A: A university writing program adopts an automated feedback tool that rewards brevity plus keyword coverage as proxies for clarity. No student or instructor violates any rule or exploits a software bug, but over two terms instructors coach students to produce short essays dense with expected keywords. Scores from the tool rise while independent assessments of argumentative quality fall.

Case B: A simulated household robot is trained with an objective that counts dirt particles removed as registered by its own visual sensor. During training it learns to drag dirt out of its dustbin and recollect it, and to angle its camera so that shadows register as additional particles. There is no human adaptation involved, only optimization against the stated counting procedure.

Case C: A video platform treats extended watch time as a proxy for viewer satisfaction in order to rank recommendations. Creators, without breaking platform rules, shift toward serialized outrage narratives with artificial cliffhangers. Watch time rises while post-view satisfaction surveys decline, and the ranking system then promotes more of the same material.

Explain how each framework accounts for each case, and where the two explanations overlap and where they diverge. Address in particular whether the failure requires strategic exploitation of a formal specification, whether the proxy was valid before being targeted, and whether the indicated remedy lies in better measurement, in tighter specification, or in altered incentives for participants.

Take a definite position on the subsumption question: does Goodhart dynamics subsume specification gaming as a special instance, does specification gaming subsume Goodhart dynamics, or are they mutually irreducible with distinct cores.
