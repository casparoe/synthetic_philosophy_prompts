4. Sperm whale, brain around 8000g, vs bottlenose dolphin, brain around 1500g. I lean toward the dolphin counting for at least as much, maybe more, because of the social learning and self-recognition stuff, despite the huge size gap the other way. Again size loses.

5. Common octopus, about 500 million neurons but most out in the arms, vs a big cod with a tiny brain for a similar body size. I feel the octopus counts for a lot more. Is that total neurons, or complexity, or behavior?

6. Pea-sized human brain organoid in a dish that fires in coordinated bursts vs a live mouse. The organoid has human cells and maybe a similar cell count to a small animal brain, but I give it about zero moral weight and the mouse clearly something. So living neural tissue of a certain size isn't enough by itself.

7. Hypothetical from an old lab mate: we bulk up a mouse brain to cow size but it still just does mouse stuff, no new capacities. My gut says that adds no moral weight. So adding sheer mass without new function does nothing.
