Many commercial voice assistants and customer-service chatbots routinely treat indirect utterances phrased as questions about knowledge or ability as requests for information or action, yet revert to literal yes-or-no answers when placed in a diagnostic or pedantic mode. What view of speaker meaning and implicature does this design practice presuppose? Reconstruct the implicit theory, distinguishing what the practice strictly presupposes from what it merely fits well with, assess whether its two modes presuppose consistent views of meaning, and explain what would have to change in the system if a strictly literalist view of utterance meaning were adopted instead.
