Produce the collaborative statement they could jointly endorse. For every crux, sketch a concrete test case both camps would accept as relevant and state each camp's predicted verdict in advance, working in a noisy-predictor version in which the driver is correct four times out of five and an advance-commitment version in which the traveler can incur a modest cost beforehand to remove the option of later refusal.
