I am a junior serving as the student representative on our high school's student activities funding board. We have $1,800 in unspent funds that must be allocated by next Friday, with two final proposals: use it to discount spring field day tickets by about $6 for roughly 300 students, or use it to cover accessible transportation and a sign-language interpreter so three deaf students on our science team can attend the regional finals they qualified for. Some members argue we should choose whichever option produces the greatest total benefit across all students, while others argue it would be wrong to trade a major opportunity for a few students for a small added enjoyment for many. Please draft a short decision memo we can circulate and vote on that recommends one option with a clear rationale, engages seriously the strongest argument for the other option, and proposes how to explain the decision to the students who will be disappointed.
