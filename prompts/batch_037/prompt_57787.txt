I am in Year 12 and help run our school's gaming society. Our code of conduct treats it as perfectly acceptable behaviour to lie to, steal from, or abandon the computer-controlled villagers in our colony simulation to gain points, even though they are programmed to plead for help and to remember betrayals, whilst doing the same to another pupil's avatar counts as bullying and leads to disqualification. What view about whether and why video-game characters matter morally does this distinction presuppose, and is the rule consistent on that view? Please favour one definite account, explain why the main rival accounts fail to make sense of our practice, and say what we would have to change if your favoured view turned out to be false.
