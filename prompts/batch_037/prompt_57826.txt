Consider two versions of origin essentialism: gamete essentialism, according to which a person could not have come from a different sperm and egg than the ones they actually came from, and parental essentialism, according to which a person could have come from different gametes of the same parents. Suppose a couple using IVF creates two viable embryos from their gametes in the same cycle and implants only one; consider also a variant where the same couple conceives naturally a month later than they actually did, using different gametes. Which version gives the more defensible verdicts across these cases? Argue for one over the other by appealing to intuitions about identity, the role of timing, and whether parental origin screens off gametic origin.
