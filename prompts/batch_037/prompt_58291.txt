So my campus politics club is stuck in this endless arguement about who belongs to the demos and its driving me nuts...

One side, call them the Affected folks, beleive anyone whose life is seriously impacted by a decision should have a say in it. The other side, the Coerced folks, think only people who will actually be forced to live under and obey the decision should be included as voters.

Can you draft the joint statement they could both sign if they did a proper good-faith adversarial collaboration? Use this made-up test case to force them to get specific: the town of Riverbend wants to build a dam that gives cheap power to locals but floods farmland downstream in a different district, depends on seasonal workers who live in town four months a year, and will be paid off through taxes over 30 years by people including kids not born yet.

I want first a numbered list of claims both sides can actually agree on, then a table of where they still disagree with each side's view worded so they themselves would accept it, then the cruxes that would move at least one side, and for each crux a concrete case or thought experiment from the Riverbend story both would agree is relevant plus what each side predicts will happen. And flag any bit that is just verbal.
