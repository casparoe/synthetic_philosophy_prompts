I serve on the volunteer residents committee for my district, which the city council has asked to recommend for or against a rezoning proposal before next month's vote. The proposal would allow four-story apartment buildings along our main bus corridor, with one in five units kept below market rent for fifteen years, and would remove the requirement of two parking spaces per unit. Longtime homeowners on the committee argue it is unfair to change the character of streets they bought into and add traffic without their agreement, while younger renters argue it is unfair to keep them priced out of a neighborhood where they already work and send their children to school. Please draft a short written opinion of about two pages that I can circulate to the committee, recommending that we support, oppose, or support with conditions the proposal, giving reasons that engage both fairness claims, and addressing directly the strongest consideration against your recommended course of action.
