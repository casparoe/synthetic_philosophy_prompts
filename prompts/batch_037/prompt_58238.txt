A mathematics major insisted that intuitionistic reservations about excluded middle must collapse once we grant that classical arithmetic is consistent, while a computer science minor claimed that writing a brute-force search program already counts as a constructive witness for existential claims about natural numbers. I want to turn that confusion into an asset.

Could you draft a single take-home essay prompt on intuitionistic logic and mathematical constructivism for students who have already seen natural deduction, the informal proof-interpretation of the connectives, and the classical double-negation translation, to be answered in roughly 800 words over two days and marked out of 25? Please also supply a model answer of about 700 words and a marking scheme that distinguishes A-range from B-range work by listing four or five required moves and assigning marks to each, with a brief note for each on the characteristic error that should lose those marks.
