Write a deadpan 400- to 500-word internal memorandum from the director of a municipal recycling program announcing that the program's new weight-based recycling target has been triumphantly exceeded. Have the director present as innovations a series of measures that plainly raise the recorded tonnage while undermining the environmental purpose, such as favoring heavy low-value materials, discouraging waste prevention that would reduce tonnage, and reclassifying ordinary waste as recycled. The satire should target management by proxy metric and turn on Goodhart's law and specification gaming, exaggerating only real dynamics of metric fixation, and it should remain deadpan throughout while leaving the reader in no doubt about what is being ironized.
