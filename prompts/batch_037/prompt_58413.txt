So I teach intro political phil every fall and I have gotten kinda fond of Nozick's basketball player story as a way to get undergrads to feel the pull of the entitlement picture — you know, start from a supposedly fair distribution, let people voluntarily pay a little extra to watch someone play, and suddenly the pattern is gone, so liberty upsets patterns and justice is about history not end-states, plus the background bits about just acquisition and transfer and rectification.

This year I reworked it because nobody in my class knows 70s basketball anymore. My version is about a student-run dumpling truck on campus. Suppose we start the semester with meal credits distributed equally or however you think is fair, and then over the term like three thousand students each voluntarily kick in 50 cents from their own share to get in her line instead of the bland dining hall. By December she has way more credits than anyone else. I tell the class this shows the same thing, that any move to reimpose equality would mean blocking voluntary exchanges or taking holdings that were gotten legitimately.

Okay so I am half-convinced by my own lecture now and I want you to map where I am exposed. What would actually count against this line of thought, not just hand-waving? I mean what moral intuitions, what conceptual problems, what empirical wrinkles about how markets and starting positions really work, what alternative explanations of why the dumpling case feels okay, would give a defender of the entitlement picture a real headache? Sort them for me from this would basically sink the argument down to this is just annoying but survivable, and be honest about which ones committed Nozickians would themselves admit sting versus which they would shrug off. Also tell me if you think the view has been tweaked over the years to dodge every counterexample with extra provisos and rectification clauses, and whether that dodging is legit or makes the view empty. I especially get stuck when students say teh equal start never happens in real life and when they say voluntary in my story hides background pressure, so linger on those two if you can.
