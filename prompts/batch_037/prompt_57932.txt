For each case, please reconstruct how Sartre would distinguish, if at all, between an authentic acknowledgement of freedom and a manoeuvre of bad faith, attending to the difference between constraint by facticity and evasion of responsibility, and consider whether he would regard these technologies as merely new occasions for an old structure or as altering the structure itself. Please write your answer in continuous prose only, as one might in a thoughtful letter, without headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables, and close by reflecting on where your reconstruction is most vulnerable, which alternative reading of Sartre or which contemporary consideration would most plausibly lead you to revise it.
