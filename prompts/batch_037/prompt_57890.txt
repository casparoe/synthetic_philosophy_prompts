Give each rule a score out of 10 for its handling of the classic game, with a sentence of justification for each score. Then state briefly whether your ranking would shift for either of these two variants I keep returning to: (1) the prize is paid in years of healthy life rather than pounds, and (2) you cannot buy entry directly but may sell a transferable ticket that commits whoever holds it to play at the stated price. Close with a one-sentence verdict on which rule a rationalist should actually internalise.
