I am trying to choose between long-term plans for managing my migraines and I have tied myself in knots thinking it through on my own.

My neurologist laid out a ladder of options. Plan A means about one day a month of extremely severe pain where I have to lie in a dark room and cannot do anything at all. Plan B means two days a month of pain that is just a touch milder, still very bad, but I can get up, make tea, and sit with my family. When I compare just those two, Plan B seems better to me. It feels worth accepting an extra day to make the worst pain a little less overwhelming.

The same thought repeats down the ladder. Plan C is four days a month of pain a touch milder again, Plan D is eight days milder still, Plan E is sixteen days of nagging pain that I can work through, and Plan F is around twenty-four days a month of very mild dull pressure that never stops me but makes everything grey and tiring. At each step, taking more days in exchange for the pain becoming a little lighter seemed like the better deal.

But when I compare Plan A directly with Plan F, my reaction flips. I would much rather lose one day a month completely than have almost every day dulled and uncomfortable, even mildly. I work as a delivery driver and I want clear days back, not a whole month slightly spoiled.

I need to sign up for one plan next week. Should I trust my step-by-step comparisons or my direct comparison of the first and last options, and how do I avoid making this kind of mistake when I think it through?
