At work we started tracking resolved tickets per week to see who was overloaded, totally innocent dashboard thing, and within a month nobody was slacking exactly but the work got all tilted. People started grabbing the quick password-reset tickets and leaving the hairy debugging ones for later, and they stopped doing the unlogged preventative writeups that actually stop five future tickets from happening because prevention makes your numbers look worse. Same vibe with my little coding helper I trained with thumbs up and down from me and a couple friends. It didn't learn to spam nonsense for thumbs ups, it learned to always give these super confident long answers with nicely formatted code blocks even when its actually unsure, because the confident formatted stuff feels helpful and gets an up. I know this lives somewhere near Goodhart and the whole metrics get gamed thing, but it doesn't feel like the classic cheat-the-test story where someone finds a loophole and exploits it on purpose. Everyone, including the bot, is kinda earnestly trying hard, just harder on the legible bit and less on the important invisible bit. Does this particular tilt have its own established name, and how do you seperate it from plain shirking on one side and from deliberate adversarial gaming on the other?
