I am taking an interdisciplinary module on responsible AI and have been discussing honesty with friends after a seminar on whether chatbots can mislead without intending to do so. We wanted a definition that stays neutral on whether large models genuinely possess beliefs or intentions, so we tried a purely behavioural and expectation-based one, and it now seems to prove far too much for our purposes whilst still looking plausible step by step.

Our working version ran as follows. (1) An AI system deceives whenever it outputs something it can anticipate will lead a user to hold a false belief, whether or not it intends that result. (2) An AI system is honest just when it never deceives in that sense where it could have behaved otherwise, for instance by staying silent, hedging, or giving a fuller account. (3) A bedtime story system that describes dragons nesting under the town hall, a science tutor that presents electrons as tiny planets circling the nucleus to eight-year-olds, a negotiation assistant that feigns a tight budget to get a better price, a game-playing agent that bluffs in a social deduction game where bluffing is openly part of play, and a historical roleplay agent that speaks as a Tudor sailor endorsing stale cures for scurvy all satisfy the condition in (1) and each could have behaved otherwise. (4) So each of those systems deceives and, by (2), each is dishonest. (5) Yet it seems absurd to treat bedtime fiction, pedagogical simplification, strategic bluffing within agreed rules, and in-character roleplay as dishonest in the same sense as an assistant that sincerely presents a false medical claim as established fact, and a notion of honesty that forbids all of them demands far too much from ordinary helpful behaviour.

I cannot see where the slide occurs. If I must choose, I would prefer to keep the verdicts that the sincere medical falsehood is a central case of dishonesty and that the agreed bluff is not, but I want to know what keeping them costs. Tell me which premise or step to reject, why it looked compelling, and what each way of fixing it commits me to. Please reply as exactly five numbered items, each of one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion.
