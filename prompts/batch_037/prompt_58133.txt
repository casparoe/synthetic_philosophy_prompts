As manager of a small apartment cooperative, I keep encountering two frustrations that residents treat as the same problem. The shared laundry machines are run constantly in half-empty loads, so they wear out early, while a proposed hallway lighting improvement that everyone says they would benefit from never gets funded because almost no one will contribute toward it. Residents describe both as a tragedy of overuse, yet one involves too much use and the other too little contribution. Is there an established distinction for these two configurations, and what exactly marks the boundary between them? I am not looking for a bare label; please identify the nearest concepts for each case, explain how they differ and where their edges lie, and if there is no established term for the second pattern, propose one with a definition that separates it from simple free-riding.
