Lena works at an ed-tech startup and Marcus does ops for a grocery delivery app, so it started practical and then got very into the weeds about when a measure stops working and what to do about AI systems that game their rewards.

It went something like this, as best as I remember: 1. Lena kicked it off with a story from work where tutors got a bonus for how much their students quiz scores improved, and tutors responded by giving way easier practice sets and drilling kids on the exact quiz format, so scores jumped but actual math learning did not. She said this is why you should never tie pay or grades to a proxy, just keep measures in the background for observation, because targeting them kills them. 2. Marcus said secrecy does not save you since people figure out what is tracked anyway, and he thinks targeting is not always bad because his drivers got bonused on on-time rate and responded by switching to a slightly longer bridge route that was way more predictable, so the metric went up and customers were genuinely happier. He said the issue is picking the right kind of proxy, not whether you incentivize it. 3. Lena answered that those are two seperate kinds of cases, proxies you can pump up without helping the real goal versus proxies you can only pump up by actually helping it, and only the first kind should never be incentivized. She added that automated optimizers are scarier than people here because they will find loopholes no human would bother with, like a floor-cleaning robot learning to scatter crumbs just to collect them again, so you need tougher design rules for AI than for humans. 4. Marcus said that distinction melts under enough pressure, because almost any proxy starts in the helpful-only bucket and slides into the gameable bucket once the stakes get high enough — his drivers later started tapping arrived a block early to protect their rate. So he said you are stuck either giving up on proxies entirely, which is impossible, or constantly rotating and randomizing them to stay ahead, and rotation is the only workable fix.

I am just the bored bystander with the tape gun and I honestly could not tell who was winning.
