For an undergraduate lecture on social learning, please compose a pantoum of four quatrains that follows a line of diners choosing between two crowded food stalls. Let the interlocking repeated lines enact how each diner rationally discounts a weak private hint after seeing a longer line, so the same choice echoes down the row, and let the closing quatrain turn on how a single fresh signal could unravel the pattern. The poem should convey that the herding comes from reasonable inference under uncertainty rather than imitation for its own sake, but without using technical vocabulary.
