I coordinate maintenance for a shared laboratory kitchen and help moderate a volunteer-run discussion forum, and the same obstacle recurs in both settings. In the kitchen, disorder grows not because anyone overuses the facilities but because restoring order would require confronting those who leave messes, a task each member hopes another will undertake. In the forum, inaccurate posts stand uncorrected not for lack of knowledgeable readers but because correction invites protracted dispute that each reader prefers someone else absorb. The trouble is plainly related to failures around shared resources, yet nothing is depleted by use; what is missing is willingness to enforce a norm. Has this arrangement received an established designation, how should it be distinguished from straightforward free riding on a shared benefit and from depletion through overuse, and if no designation exists, what term and definition would you propose together with the boundary cases that separate it from those neighboring phenomena?
