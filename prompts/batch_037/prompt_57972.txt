Consider a self-contained test case I constructed for our debate involving two housemates, Priya and Quinn, choosing among three house-wide outcomes: A where Priya puts a provocative poster on her own bedroom door and Quinn puts up nothing, B where neither puts up anything, and C where Quinn puts a provocative poster on his own bedroom door and Priya puts up nothing. Please argue that in this case no acyclic social ranking can satisfy both the two personal decisiveness requirements and the unanimous preference, so the case is a genuine instance of the paradox and not avoidable by dismissing the preferences as nonstandard.
