As a software engineer with no training in philosophy, I am preparing a design discussion at my studio about whether our non-player characters could matter morally in themselves and not only through effects on players. Please design a 7-question forced-choice sorting quiz with a scoring key that places respondents into four stances: NPCs never have moral standing, mistreating them matters only because it shapes the player's character, current NPCs lack standing but future AI-driven NPCs might so caution is warranted, and NPCs should be treated as morally significant within the fiction of play. Ground each question in a concrete in-game decision rather than abstract labels, and please include these three scenarios I care about: deleting a long-term companion who remembers the player across sessions to save server costs, torturing a pain-reactive enemy for fun in single-player with no observer, and wiping an LLM-driven village that pleads to persist in order to ship an update. Phrase the options so respondents can answer without knowing which stance each answer supports, map answer patterns to the stances, and note any coherent answer combination that matches no stance and any two stances your questions cannot separate.
