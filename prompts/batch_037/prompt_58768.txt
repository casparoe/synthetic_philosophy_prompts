hey, I volunteered to lay out my take on whether bigger brains mean you matter more morally at this casual philosophy meetup, and now I'm worried my views don't actually fit together. can you audit them for me?

here's the package I find myself believing:

first, I think moral importance comes in degrees tied to how rich and complex a mind's conscious life can be, not just whether a being is conscious at all.

second, I think across really different animals total brain size or neuron count is a decent rough proxy for that richness, so all else equal a human counts for more than a pig, a pig for more than a mouse, a mouse for more than an ant, that kind of ladder.

third, I also beleive every human has the same full moral status, including babies and people with dementia or very small brains or very low cognitive ability — I don't want to discount anyone for having a smaller or less powerful brain.

fourth, I think pain is pain — if a human and a pig are both in the same intensity of pain for the same ten seconds, that badness is equal and being smarter doesn't inflate it.

fifth, I want to be non-speciesist, so if we ever ran into aliens with way bigger or denser brains than ours who can think and feel stuff we can't even imagine, I think we'd have to admit a single one of them could matter more than a single human.

the thing that's been bugging me is this made up case I cooked up to test myself: imagine two similar-sized mammals where one has a huge brain but most of it is just doing boring low-level filtering for smell or echolocation, and the other has a much smaller brain but it is super densely packed and supports flexible learning and social play. my proxy rule says go with the big brain, but my richness idea says go with the small dense one. and what if the small-brained one lives a much shorter life overall?

can you go through these five and tell me which ones are actually pulling against each other and which ones just look like they are? rank the real clashes by how serious they are, and for each say what the cheapest fix would be and what it would cost me. I don't have a favorite I want to protect, I just want to know what has to give to make the rest hold together.
