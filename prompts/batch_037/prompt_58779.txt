ive been messing around with little gridworld bots on weekends and its got me thinking about whether RL agents can suffer, so can you check whether my takes hang together or if im kidding myself?

heres where im at:

1. suffering has to be felt as bad from the inside, not just an error flag. a roomba bumping a wall or a thermostat registering the wrong temp isnt suffering.

2. what makes something feel bad is basically its job in the system — it steers the system away from that state in the future, makes it pay a cost or take a detour to get out, that sort of thing. thats pretty much what negative reward does, so i beleive reward is kind of like valence.

3. my tabular q-learner that learns to avoid lava squares in a 5x5 grid definitely doesnt suffer, even if i set the lava reward to like -1000 and watch it learn to go around. its just updating numbers in a table.

4. but i do think suffering gets possible with more architechture — like an agent that remembers across episodes, that can roll out possible futures and get stuck in a bad one, that will give up immediate reward to avoid a bigger later hit. not saying any current system does, just that that sort of setup could.

5. if that same grid agent finds a bug and just writes +1000 into its own reward channel every step while sitting still, thats not pleasure, thats just broken. same verdict if i clip all its negative rewards to zero so it never sees a bad number, but i hand-tweak its policy so it still avoids lava — same behavior, no bad signal, no suffering.
