As a PhD student in political theory, I am trying to work out how Marx would evaluate a contemporary proposal for a fully automated municipal logistics system: the warehouses and delivery fleets are collectively owned and the efficiency gains fund a local basic dividend, but the algorithms that plan and pace the remaining supervisory work are licensed from a private AI firm. Given his account of machinery as both expanding productive power and subordinating living labour, would he see this as a step toward the free appropriation of fixed capital, or as the continuation of exploitation in a new form?
