my high school history teacher explained WWI with the powder keg: Europe before 1914 was a keg of powder (alliances, arms race, nationalism, imperial rivalry), the assassination in Sarajevo was the spark, the war was the bang. He used it to shut down the blame game -- if the keg is already full it doesnt matter which spark lands, so asking who started it misses the point, the whole continent was going to blow one way or another.

Now I'm taking a philosophy of causation class and I cant stop picking at it. A friend in the class says the keg isnt a neutral picture at all, its a whole theory of causation smuggled in: one scalar state that fills up, a threshold, a passive system, and a trigger thats interchangeable with any other trigger. She thinks most of the no-one-is-to-blame and it-was-bound-to-happen conclusion is being carried by the metaphor, not by the history. She also insists causes always have to be stated contrastively -- not why did war break out, but why this war rather than a smaller one, why 1914 rather than 1911 or 1916 -- and says once you ask it that way the keg is the wrong shape for the question.

Can you work through it? Give me the mapping onto the actual history (whats the powder, the keg, the spark, the fuse, the guy holding the match, the other kegs sitting around), then where it breaks down. Separate the breaks that actually matter for what he wanted to conclude from the ones that are just decorative. What does the picture quietly import that the history doesnt have -- a scalar that fills, a passive system, all sparks being equivalent, a bang that leaves nothing behind? Is the spark even a cause on a plain difference-making view, or does the keg make the spark causally idle -- and if so is that a problem for the metaphor or for the view? Then the main thing: does the blame-diffusion and inevitability conclusion definately survive once the disanalogies are on the table, or was the keg carrying it the whole time? And what does the contrastive version do to the blame question -- rescue it, dissolve it, or just relocate it? If the keg is the wrong picture, is avalanche or perfect storm actually better, or do those import their own problems?
