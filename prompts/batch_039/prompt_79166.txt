The strong version: your inside view is evidence about your psychology, not about the world.

What would count against that? I'm curious whether the reference class is itself choosen by inside-view judgment — if so, is that a fatal circularity or just a harmless recursion? And is there a case where taking the outside view is the worse epistemic move, where the inside view has actually earned its weight? What does my view predict that a boring old calibration story doesn't? Rank the objections by how much they should really move me, say which ones a defender of the view would just concede, and tell me straight if I've defined modesty so loosely that nothing could count against it.
