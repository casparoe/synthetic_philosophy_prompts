Story A: a bad deploy changed a retry default, and the logs line up. Story B: a latent network partition caused it, which explains why the same deploy ran fine for three weeks, why three unrelated services got slow at the same time, and why the retry default only mattered that day. The chair says go with the most probable cause given base rates -- deploys cause most outages, and A is simpler. I say go with the explanation that actually accounts for all the evidence, even if it's less probable as a single event. Draft the decision memo I can circulate before Monday: recommend a root cause, say what we do next (rollback? monitoring? postmortem follow-ups?) with owners and rough dates, and give the strongest case for the chair's view. Also be explicit about which criteria you're using to call one explanation 'better,' where those criteria conflict, and what's a judgment call rather than a fact we can test. Keep it readable for engineers who don't know the academic stuff.
