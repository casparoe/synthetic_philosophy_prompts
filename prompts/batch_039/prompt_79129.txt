I've been reading Bentham's stuff on sex — the essay on pederasty he wrote in the 1780s and never published, and then the later manuscripts where he works up the same material into a general case for sexual liberty — and honestly I've ended up mostly convinced by the line he runs. Crudely put: sexual gratification is a pleasure, pleasure is good, and the only thing that can make an act bad is the pain it causes somebody. Other people's revulsion doesn't count, the fact that it's non-procreative doesn't count, the fact that it's been condemned for centuries doesn't count. He even treats non-procreative sex as positively good in one respect, since it doesn't add mouths to feed, and he treats the whole apparatus of disgust and persecution as an irregularity of the moral sense, the same machinery that churns out religious asceticism and self-denial for its own sake.

I want you to play this straight and tell me what would count against it. Not the boring stuff about him being a man of his time — I mean the actual defeators, the ones a symapthetic reader would feel the force of. A few things I'm already worried about and want you to take seriously rather than brush past: if displeasure at other people's conduct is a pain under his own account of the moral sanction, what stops his calculus from licensing suppression of the very acts he wants to decriminalize, and does he need to deny that mere disgust is a legitimate ground of sanction — if so, can he do that without cutting against the rest of his system? Where does the consent boundary come from inside a purely hedonic framework — can he generate a firm line at children, animals, and incapacity, or does he have to smuggle in something non-utilitarian? Is the claim that sexual pleasure is a pure pleasure, with no intrinsic downside, actually defensible given the jealousy, disappointed desire, disease, and unwanted pregnancy he knows about? And does the population argument support sexual liberty, or only non-procreative sex, and if the latter, does it also justify things nobody wants to justify?

Rank these from fatal to merely awkward, say which ones a committed Benthamite would themselves concede and which they'd dismiss as not really on point, and tell me honestly whether the position has been qualified into unfalsifiability — say by retreating to private, consensual, adult — and whether that's actually a problem for a view of this kind or just what any normative view ends up looking like.
