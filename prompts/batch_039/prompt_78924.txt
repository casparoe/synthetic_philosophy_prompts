Right, this is going to be a bit rambly, but I've got a bee in my bonnet about the whole tight-cultures-versus-loose-cultures thing and I want someone to actually explain the concept to me rather than give me the slogan version.

The way it's always put to me is that some places are 'tight' — strong norms, not much tolerance for people who go off and do their own thing — and other places are 'loose', where you can more or less please yourself. And then there's always a follow-up about whether a society *should* be tighter or looser, which is the bit I actually care about, because people argue about it constantly and I can never work out what they're proposing.

Here's my problem. I think the word is doing at least three different jobs and nobody separates them. First, how many rules there are — some places seem to have a norm for everything down to how you hang out your washing, others have almost none. Second, how much the rules leave to your own judgement — you can have a rule like 'be respectful at funerals' that tells you almost nothing in particular, versus one that tells you exactly which shoes to wear. Third, how hard people come down on you when you break one — some places have hardly any rules but the one taboo they do have will get you frozen out of your family for life, and other places have hundreds of rules that nobody enforces and everyone quietly ignores.

I don't think those three things move together at all. If that's right, then 'tight' is either a rag-bag that basically means 'the sort of place I don't like', or it secretly means one of my three and smuggles the other two in. Which is it? And if someone says we should be a tighter society, what would definately have to change — more rules, vaguer rules, harsher punishments? Those look like three completely different demands to me, and I'd probably agree with one of them and not the others.

There's a fourth thing nagging at me too. Suppose everyone behaves the same way, but only because it's never occurred to anyone to do otherwise — no rules, no enforcement, nothing, just uniformity. Is that place tight? Because the word seems to be about how much pressure the society puts on you, yet the thing people actually point at when they say it is usually just how similar everyone's behaviour looks.

So what I want is the conceptual side of this explained properly: what is the notion supposed to be picking out, is it one thing or several, can you define it in a way that doesn't collapse into either my three-way split or a sneaky value judgement, and is the descriptive claim even coherent enough to be the thing the should-be-tighter argument is arguing about? I'm not after a reading list, just the actual mechanics of the concept and where it breaks down.
