Compare Frank Ramsey's take on the meaning of life with what you might call the "magnitude view" — the idea that your life means more the larger the whole you're part of (a nation, a species, a cosmos). Ramsey says the size of the universe is irrelevant to meaning, and that a view from nowhere is a fiction. Does his position actually refute the magnitude view, or does it just change the subject? And if meaning isn't about scale, what is it about for Ramsey — and is that enough? Keep it to the arguments, not the biography.
