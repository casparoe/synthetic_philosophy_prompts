I've been pushing a distinction in lectures that I want them to actually use. Structural akrasia: her preferences are time-inconsistent, so there's no single ranking she's failing to act on — she prefers A at nine and B at five, and five wins. Executive akrasia: at the moment of action she has a stable ranking and still doesn't act on it — she judges, now, that she shouldn't, and does it anyway. Push them to commit, rather than survey.
