Give the poem a single refrain line, in the same position in every stanza: the speaker should spend four stanzas treating its return as a coincidence that wants explaining, and the fifth should show that, once the shared cause is in view, the return carries no independent information at all. It should be recognizable to someone who works on this as a poem about the presumption and about the specific way it fails, and it should stand up for a reader who has never heard of it. Give me the poem alone, with no title, preface, or afterword.
