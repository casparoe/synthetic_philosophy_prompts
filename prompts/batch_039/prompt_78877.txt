I'm a linguistics MA student who wandered into a cognitive science reading group, so I have just enough vocabulary to get myself into trouble. We spent a session on animal communication — the familiar stuff about monkeys that give different alarm calls for leopards and eagles and snakes, and whether a call means the predator, or means climb-the-tree, or means something in between like a big thing coming at you from above — and I came away with a puzzle I can't shake, and I'm fairly sure it has a name that I simply don't know. The puzzle is that you can always redraw the line. Suppose what the animal is actually responding to, at the level of its sensory system, is something like a dark shape moving overhead. Then the state it's in is arguably about the dark shape, or about the eagle (since dark shapes overhead are usually eagles), or about the disjunction of the two, and I can't see anything in the behavioral data that settles which. And the mistakes don't help: say a leaf flutters down and the monkey dives for cover. Is that state about the leaf? If it is, then it looks like the state was never about eagles at all, which seems plainly wrong. If it isn't, I want to know what privileges the eagle case over the leaf case.

I dont think this is just the usual underdetermination of theory by evidence, which I do know about; it feels narrower, like it's specific to states that are supposed to be tracking something out there in the world. And I keep half-remembering that there's a standard problem here and a standard family of replies, something to do with what a state is there for rather than what it typically does, but I can't place the name, and I've been writing "the widening problem" in my notes while knowing perfectly well that isn't it.

So what I'd like is the established name, if there is one, and then the actual carving: what exactly the problem is, how it differs from the Quine thing about rabbits and undetached rabbit-parts (those seem related and I can't say how), and what the most popular way out is and why it isn't just moving the bump in the carpet. If someone tells me that what the state is there for fixes what it's about, I want to know why that isn't circular, and whether I could always describe a broader job for the state and reopen the same hole.
