My business partner and I are winding down our jointly owned online shop and need to divide the $80,000 a competitor is offering for our combined inventory and customer list. If we can't agree, we each just keep our own separate inventory, which is worth about $10,000 to me and $4,000 to her since she has higher storage costs. But if either of us walked away and sold everything on our own instead, I could get up to $50,000 for my share, while she could only get up to $20,000 because she has fewer buyer contacts. She thinks we should simply split the surplus above our fallback amounts equally. I think it makes more sense for each of us to get a share that reflects how close we can get to our own best-case number. Who has the more reasonable approach here, or is there a better way to think about this?
