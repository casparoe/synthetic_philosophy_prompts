Write a short story that dramatizes a no-trade theorem: construct a scene where two agents, both known to be rational and to trust each other's competence, try to make a bet or trade in a market, and have the sudden shared realization that the very fact the other is willing to trade is evidence that they shouldn't. Let the story explore the emotional and social texture of that realization—the awkwardness, the loss of an opportunity, maybe the paranoia it breeds—rather than just stating the theorem.
