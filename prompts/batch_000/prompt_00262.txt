Last night a friend and I argued for hours about a hiring committee that approved a candidate even though a majority of members privately doubted each of the individual qualifications the job required. My friend insists this is just the discursive dilemma at work, but I think it can only arise when someone on the committee is being strategic or dishonest about their judgments. If every member is voting sincerely on each premise, doesn't the inconsistency have to disappear once you tally the premises against the conclusion?
