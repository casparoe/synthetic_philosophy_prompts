I'm working on a long-form piece about existential risk forecasting, and I keep running into the Doomsday Argument (the Carter-Leslie line of reasoning about our birth rank among all humans who will ever live) as something forecasters cite alongside more concrete risks like engineered pandemics, misaligned AI, or nuclear escalation. Could you help me sort out how the anthropic reasoning in the Doomsday Argument actually relates (or fails to relate) to the empirical risk factors that effective altruists and existential-risk researchers usually have in mind, so I can characterize this accurately for a general readership?
