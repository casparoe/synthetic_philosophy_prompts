Write an essay on whether the Best System Analysis (BSA) of laws of nature can adequately ground the objective modal force we ordinarily attribute to laws—their capacity to explain, support counterfactuals, and underwrite causation—given that on Humean terms laws are nothing but compact summaries of the total, unconnected pattern of particular facts (the Humean mosaic). Engage seriously with the anti-Humean charge that BSA laws are explanatorily idle "book-keeping" devices that get the direction of dependence backwards (laws should explain the mosaic, not vice versa), as well as the Humean rejoinder that appeals to primitive necessitation relations or dispositional essences purchase explanatory power only at the cost of intelligibility. Reach and defend a definite verdict on whether Humeanism can accommodate lawhood's explanatory/modal role, or whether some form of anti-Humeanism is required.
