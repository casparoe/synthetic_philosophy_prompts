For my seminar on decision theory and acausal reasoning, I'm trying to get a firmer grip on the surrogate goals proposal as a defense against acausal blackmail. Please write a dialogue between two agents: one who has pre-committed to a surrogate goal to absorb any threats, and one who argues that this strategy doesn't actually neutralize the threat but merely relocates it to a target that a sufficiently sophisticated threatener will still exploit. Have them directly engage each other's objections, especially the worry that credibly adopting a surrogate goal requires making it just as vulnerable as the original goal, which may re-import the very risk the strategy was meant to avoid.
