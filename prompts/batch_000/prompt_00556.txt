A friend and I spent most of last night arguing about Molinism and never quite settled the matter. As I understand it, God's middle knowledge lets him survey all the counterfactuals of creaturely freedom before deciding which world to actualize. But if God is the one selecting which of these counterfactuals turn out true, doesn't that mean he's ultimately fixing how each of us would freely choose in any circumstance he might place us in? It seems like this just reintroduces determinism dressed up in possible-worlds language. Is there something about the logical structure of middle knowledge that I'm missing which is supposed to preserve libertarian freedom here?
