In infinite ethics, philosophers worry about how to compare worlds that each contain infinitely many happy people and infinitely many suffering people. But doesn't Cantor's work on set theory already solve this? If the cardinality of the "happy" set is larger than the cardinality of the "suffering" set, can't we just say that world is better, and vice versa? I'm curious why this is still treated as a live problem in the literature rather than a straightforward application of comparing infinite set sizes.
