That way the AI literally can't stop you from pulling the plug no matter how smart it is. But everytime I read papers by MIRI people or Stuart Russell's group, they treat this like some huge unsolved theoretical puzzle requiring fancy game theory and utility function tweaks, rather than just a wiring problem you could solve with an electrician. Am I missing something here, or are these researchers overcomplicating something that's basically already solved by just unplugging the thing?
