A friend and I got into a long debate after a charity raffle where prize envelopes are handed out in pairs: one envelope contains a certain dollar amount, and its paired envelope contains either half or double that amount, with equal likelihood. I opened mine and found $80. My friend insisted that since the other envelope must hold either $40 or $160, its expected value is $100, so I should always swap no matter what number I see first. Something about this struck me as absurd, since the same logic would apply no matter what amount I'd found, and would justify swapping back and forth forever. Was my friend's reasoning wrong, and if so, exactly where does it break down?
