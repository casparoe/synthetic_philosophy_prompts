I'm trying to get a handle on pure time preference, and a blog post i read summarized the debate like this: 'Pure time preference is the idea that, all else equal, an outcome is less valuable just because it is later. Once you commit to a constant discount factor, the only remaining question is what the right discount rate is, since any other choice just smuggles in a self-control bias.'
