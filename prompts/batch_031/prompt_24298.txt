I'm an engineer, not a philosopher, but I've been following the debate about whether language models and other AI systems "have" mental states, and I keep running into the same argumentative loop, so I wanted to see whether it has a name. Here is the pattern, with two examples. In the first, one person argues that a chatbot "believes" the user is asking about the weather, on the grounds that its responses are organized exactly as a weather-belief would organize them; the other concedes that the belief-description works — that the bot behaves as if it had that belief — but then treats the concession as a defeat for the first person, because "behaves as if" is not "really has." The first person clarifies that they never meant the belief to be a physical object sitting in the machine; they meant that the belief-description is the most accurate and useful way to predict the bot's next output. The second person responds that this is a change of subject — from "does it have beliefs" to "is the belief-description useful" — and that the original question is still unanswered; and the debate loops back to the start. The same loop comes up in a different guise when an engineer and a philosopher argue about whether a robot "believes" the floor ahead of it is solid: the engineer concedes the controller treats the floor as solid (the robot drives toward it as if it were), the philosopher says "treats as solid" isn't "believes it's solid," the engineer says they only meant the solid-floor description is the one that predicts the robot's motion, and the philosopher says that is a change of subject.

What strikes me is that in neither case is any particular fact about what the system does actually in dispute; the whole stalemate seems to be about which question is even being asked, and each side treats the other's reframing as a dodge rather than as a legitimate move. I can't find a word for this specific configuration. Is there an established concept or term of art for it? If so, what exactly is it, and how does it differ from plain equivocation on the word "belief," and from the general point that a useful description of a system is not the same thing as the system itself? I'm not looking for a bare label — I want the carving: what the phenomenon precisely is, what it is not, and where its edges are, so I can tell when the pattern is genuinely present and when it's just two people talking past each other for no deeper reason. And if there is no established term, I'd like you to coin one and give it a definition with clear boundary cases.
