Write a short story about the reference class problem. A person who has just been diagnosed with a disease, and the doctor gives them survival stats for a bunch of different groups they belong to — the group with their symptom, the group with their treatment, the group their age. The stats point in very different directions, and they cant just pick the group that sounds best. The point is to make the reader feel the tension of not knowing which reference class is the "right" one to use.
