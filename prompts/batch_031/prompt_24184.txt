Draft the joint statement that a defender of the principle of alternative possibilities who answers Frankfurt-style cases with the flicker of freedom strategy and a semicompatibilist, who holds that moral responsibility tracks guidance control on the actual sequence rather than the availability of alternatives, could both sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration. I want the claims both would accept about what Frankfurt cases are designed to show and about the status of the principle; the precise statements of where they still differ, each phrased so its own side would accept the wording; the cruxes — I suspect one is whether an alternative possibility the agent never exercises can bear on responsibility at all, and another is how much weight to give the actual sequence over the counterfactual ones; and, for each crux, a case or argument both would accept as bearing on it, with what each side would predict written down in advance. Write it as continuous prose, the way one would write a letter to a colleague: no headings, no bullet points, no bold, and no tables.
