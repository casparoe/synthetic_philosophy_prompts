I'm not an econ person, just a novelist, but this is bugging me. If her ticket purchases really do define her preferences, then can a person ever be irrational, or make a mistake, or want something they never express? What does that whole practice -- pricing things off what people actually pay or what they'd pay -- presuppose about what utility even is? Is it a real thing inside the head, or just a label we paste on behavior? And if its just a label, how does it make sense to say a cigarette tax or a nudge makes someone better off -- better off according to whose preferences, the one who bought the ticket or the one who said she wanted to quit? I mostly need to know if my character can be incoherent, or if the whole framework quietly forbids that.
