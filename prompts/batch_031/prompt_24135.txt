Diagnose what kind of disagreement this actually is — a verbal one, a hidden empirical question about effect size and compounding, a difference in what counts as evidence of harm, or two groups answering different questions under a single heading — using what each side actually argues, and tell me what, if anything, would count as progress: a definition, an experiment, or a change of question.
