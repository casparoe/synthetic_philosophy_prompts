I work in software (backend, boring stuff) and I keep noticing a pattern in myself around emotions and I can't tell if it has a name. I came across the argument somewhere that an emotion is either a judgment, or a perception of something in the situation, or just a raw feeling, and I've been using that as a rough lens. The thing below doesn't sit cleanly with any of the three, so I might be making it up.

The pattern: how strongly I feel seems to track how settled my judgment about the situation is, in an inverse way — the feeling peaks while the judgment is still forming and drops off once it's nailed down. One instance: a coworker kept merging code without review and I was seething for the first hour, but most of that hour was me not being able to say what exactly was wrong, and the moment I landed on the judgment ('he's doing it to pad his numbers for the perf review') the anger dropped to a cold quiet certainty, like a progress bar hitting 100% and the spinner stopping. Another: a friend went distant after I said something, and the anxious feeling was worst exactly while I couldn't settle wether she was mad at me or just depressed; once I'd worked out it was the latter, it mostly went away.

The weird part: if emotions are just judgments, I don't get why the feeling fades when the judgment gets stronger — the judgment is present in both phases, so where did the emotion go? And if emotions are perceptions of a property in the situation, I'd expect the feeling to be strongest when the property is most clearly there, not while I'm still uncertain about it. So it looks like the feeling is its own process that runs during the formation of the judgment and then idles out, and I can't tell from the inside if the residual cold certainty is the same emotion at low volume or a seperate mental state that just gets the same label.

Is there an established concept for this? I feel like it's near the thing about putting feelings into words reducing them, but that's about naming the feeling itself, and what I'm describing is about forming a judgment about the situation. I half-remember 'appraisal' from a psych article but can't place it. I don't want a bare name — tell me what exactly this is, what it isn't, and where the edges are. Does it still count if the feeling stays high because the judgment keeps changing, like when the other person is genuinely ambiguous? And does the fading actually count against the judgment theory, or is it compatible with it in a way I'm not seeing?
