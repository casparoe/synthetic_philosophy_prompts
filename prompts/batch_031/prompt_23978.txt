Here's the shape I have in my head, and I'd like you to turn it into something I can actually run: split the room into two "nations," each with a stock of gold tokens and a small basket of goods they produce, and have them trade across the border. The thing I want them to land on is the moment where the "deficit" nation realizes they're not actually bleeding wealth, and that the real richness is in the goods and the labor, not the hoard of gold.

And tell me which parts of Hume's argument the simulation flatly can't reproduce and I'll have to supply in the debrief — I'm guessing the time lag and the assumption that specie moves freely are the big ones, but I'd like you to actually think it through rather than just agree with me.
