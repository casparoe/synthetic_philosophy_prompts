Okay so I'm a novelist and I'm in a bit of a bind with my co-writer and I want to think it through before I do something I regret. We're finishing a book together and we can't agree on the ending. The book is about a pretty decent person who does something genuinely awful and gets away with it, and the whole point of it, the thing I care most, is that there's no comeuppance — no legal consequence that sticks, no cosmic justice, no lesson. The character just has to live with what he did, and I think that's what makes it honest. My co-writer, who's also the one getting the film option, keeps pushing for an ending where the character actually *changes* somehow, pays an internal price, the reader gets a sense he was altered by it. She says an ending where nothing happens to him, where he just keeps going, feels like a cop-out, like I'm being edgy. And look, I get why she'd want that for a film, and I dont want to just bulldoze her. I've already tried a version where he gets away with it legally but the ending shows him finally admitting to himself what he did, a quiet internal reckoning with no external punishment, and it still felt wrong to me, like I was quietly giving up the thing that actually mattered.

Here's what I actually want to figure out, and please don't just tell me to split the difference or that compromise is always a bit of a loss: is there a version of this ending where we're *both* better off than the realistic alternative, which is us not finishing it together — I publish a version she doesn't back, the film dies, and we're both left annoyed at each other? Or are we actually stuck with incompatible visions where any middle ground is just a watered-down version of both and we're both left with something we'd rather not have? I keep going back and forth on whether what she wants (internal change) and what I want (no external justice) are really two separate things I could trade on, or whether they're the same thing all along and one of us just has to lose. I'd rather sort that out before I burn the friendship.
