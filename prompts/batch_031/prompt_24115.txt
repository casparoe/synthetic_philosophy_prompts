I'm working through Nozick's tracking theory in *Philosophical Explanations* and the subsequent closure literature, and I keep hitting the same wall: the debate over whether knowledge is closed under known entailment has been running for decades and, as far as I can tell, the two sides keep talking past each other. On one side, Nozick uses the tracking conditions — a belief that p counts as knowledge only if, in the nearby possible worlds where p is true, the subject would believe p by the relevant method, and in the nearby worlds where p is false, she would not — to argue that you can know you have hands by looking, and know that having hands entails not being a handless BIV, yet fail to know you're not a handless BIV by the same method, because in the BIV case looking would still deliver the belief that you have hands. On the other side, the closure defenders insist that any adequate account must validate closure under known entailment (or at least under conjunction elimination), and they read Nozick's cases as showing something about how to individuate methods or about which counterfactuals to hold fixed, rather than about the logic of knowledge itself.

What I'd like is a diagnosis of the impasse rather than a verdict on who's right: is this a substantive disagreement about the metaphysics of knowledge, a verbal disagreement about what "the same method" or "would believe" picks out, or two parties answering different questions under one heading — one asking what makes a belief knowledge in a given case, the other asking what structural properties any knowledge operator must satisfy? I want you to engage with what each side actually says — the role of the method condition, the choice of counterfactuals, the handless-BIV case and the standard counterexamples built on it — and to say whether there's a hidden formal question doing the work (say, about which counterfactuals the tracking conditions range over, or about how to individuate methods) that could be settled independently of the philosophical dispute. And given your diagnosis, tell me honestly what, if anything, would count as progress here — a refinement of the tracking conditions, a formal result about the logic of the operator, a change of question — or whether we should expect the impasse to persist and why.
