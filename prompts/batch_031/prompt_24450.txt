For my final independent project I have to commit to one research question, at the scale of a long paper written by one person over a couple of terms, with no access to original data or clinical subjects. I don't want a survey of the looping effect, and I don't want a reading list. What I want is a defended prioritization of the genuinely open conceptual questions in this area, so I can pick the one most worth spending the project on.

Concretely: give me a shortlist of the live questions — I'd guess somewhere between four and six — that you think actually matter, and then order them and defend the ordering.
