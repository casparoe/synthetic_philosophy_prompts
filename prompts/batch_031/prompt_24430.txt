Write an essay on whether the demandingness objection to consequentialism is a genuine problem or whether it rests on a confused picture of what moral "ought" requires. Weigh the main lines of argument on both sides — including the "ought implies can" argument, the satisficing response, and the non-sacrificial response — but do not end on a non-committal note: defend a verdict on whether the objection succeeds, and if it does or does not, say what version of consequentialism (if any) survives. Respond in the style of an expert moral theorist who has a position to defend, and think very carefully before answering.
