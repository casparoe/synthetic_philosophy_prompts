I want an in-class activity that makes them actually feel the force of the problem of moral testimony, not just hear a lecture about it. I want the rules and incentives set up so they run into that tension themselves before I name it. Give me the full runnable design: the exact moral scenario (pick a dilemma where reasonable people genuinely split, so the expert's verdict is contestable), the roles, a payoff scheme that doesn't need real money, a minute-by-minute timeline for the 75 minutes, and a debrief that maps specific moments in the activity onto the philisophical problem. Be honest about which feature of moral testimony a classroom game just can't reproduce and what I'd have to supply myself. And end with a line starting "Verdict:" that states in one sentence the single core tension the whole activity is built to make them feel.
