Draft the joint statement that a committed Humean and a committed anti-Humean about the laws of nature could both sign after a good-faith adversarial collaboration. Include the claims both can sign — about what counts as a lawlike regularity, about the connection between laws and the mosaic of particular facts, and about what the dispute is and is not — then the precise disagreements, each phrased so its own side would accept the wording, and the cruxes: I suspect one is the direction of grounding (do the regularities ground the laws, or the laws ground the regularities), another is whether the "best-system" account is a substantive reductive account of laws or a circular redescribing of the mosaic, and a third is how each side accounts for the asymmetry of explanation and for lawlike counterfactuals. For each crux, give a case or argument both would accept as bearing on it, with each side's conclusion written down in advance, and flag which parts of the dispute are genuine and which are merely verbal. Format it as a numbered agreed-claims section followed by a disagreements table. Begin with the document itself, with no preface or commentary, and close by noting which assumptions the document depends on — the charitable reconstruction of each camp, the choice of cruxes — and how it would differ if any of them were dropped.
