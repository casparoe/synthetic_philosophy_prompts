I think he's wrong, but I don't want to just wave him off, and I'm worried that if I'm not carefull I'll end up restating the same "your action adds up" platitude that everyone's tired of hearing.

I want an honest treatment of the strongest version of his point, and I want you to tell me what would actually change my mind. No citations or reading lists, please, I'll handle the sourcing.
