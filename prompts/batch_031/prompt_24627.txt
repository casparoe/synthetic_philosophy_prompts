For a workshop on AI safety, write the desiderata that any adequate account of latent knowledge in language models and its elicitation must satisfy. It must specify what counts as latent knowledge, what counts as elicited rather than prompted, and what evidence distinguishes the model’s possession of a fact, rule, or disposition from lucky guessing, context copying, or evaluation-specific artifacts. For each desideratum, give the motivating failure mode, mark which requirements are non-negotiable for safety-critical elicitation and which are methodological preferences, and flag any pair that may be jointly unsatisfiable, such as broad coverage and controlled inference. Do not survey particular elicitation methods; the point is the standard they would have to meet.
