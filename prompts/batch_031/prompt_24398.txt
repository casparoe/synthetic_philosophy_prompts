So could you write out the desiderata: what any adequate theory of the comensurability (or incommensurability) of pleasure and pain has to deliver for this view to work? Then, briefly, how does a Bentham-style quantitative hedonism, straight negative utilitarianism, and a Parfit-style lexical / threshold view do against the list?
