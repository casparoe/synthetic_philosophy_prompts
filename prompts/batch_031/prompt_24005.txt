Our town cooperative is weighing a labor-hour credit system: one hour of any member's work is redeemable for one hour of any other member's work, and members may also lend hours at interest to finance long projects. Please explain the most important implications of Aristotle's analysis of money, exchange, and usury for this scheme — where it might succeed, where it might run into trouble that he anticipated, and what he would say about the interest component in particular. As you reconstruct his positions, state how confident you are in each claim, and single out the point you are least sure of.
