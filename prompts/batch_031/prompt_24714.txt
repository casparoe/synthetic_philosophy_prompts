I am drafting a position paper on cooperative bargaining and want a consistency audit of the desiderata I would like my preferred solution concept to satisfy before I commit to one. The set of commitments I am working with is as follows.

(1) Symmetry: the solution should be insensitive to the labels of the players; permuting the players' utility functions should permute the recommended agreement in the corresponding way.

(2) Invariance to positive affine transformations: since utility functions are ordinal up to positive affine rescaling, the solution should be unchanged if I replace each player's utility by a positive affine function of it.

(3) Independence of irrelevant alternatives: if the feasible set of agreements is enlarged to include additional options, but the agreement the solution would have selected in the smaller problem remains available, the solution should still select it; the mere availability of options that are not in fact chosen should not move the outcome.

(4) Resource monotonicity: if the feasible set is enlarged in a way that weakly improves one player's attainable payoff at every level of the other player's payoff, then that player's payoff in the selected agreement should not decrease.

(5) Sensitivity to aspiration levels: the division of the surplus above the disagreement point should be proportional to each player's maximum attainable utility above that same point, so that a player who stands to gain more from reaching an agreement than from the status quo receives a correspondingly larger share.

I have not yet settled on which standard solution concept, if any, is the right home for this profile. Please work through the set and tell me: which pairs of commitments are genuinely in tension and which only appear to be; for each genuine tension, how severe it is and what the cheapest revision would be; and which single change would restore overall consistency at the least cost. Finally, say which, if any, of the standard bargaining solutions (Nash, Kalai-Smorodinsky, or another) survives the audit intact, and which of my commitments it would force me to relinquish.
