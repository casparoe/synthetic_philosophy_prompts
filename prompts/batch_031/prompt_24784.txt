I've been stuck on a thing about beliefs for days and cant shake it. So I was reading about this principle, I think it's called Reflection (a philosopher named Van Fraassen is attached to it I think, but I could be misremembering) that says your belief right now about "H, if E" should match what you expect your plain belief in H to be once E actually happens. Like, if I'm betting on H conditional on E, my odds should match what I think my odds will be after E lands. That sounds reasonable to me.

Then I built this little argument with a memory pill and now I think it proves Reflection is just wrong, or I'm confused.

Say I know a fact H for sure right now. Maybe the answer to a question I looked up, whatever. And I know I'm going to take a pill tonight that wipes my memory of exactly that fact. So after the pill, call that state E, I'll have no idea what H was and I'll be down to a 50/50 guess. I also know for sure I'm taking the pill, so E is definitely going to happen.

(1) By Reflection, my belief in H given E should match what I expect my belief in H to be once I'm in E. And I expect it to be 1/2. So my belief in H given E is 1/2.
(2) I'm certain E is going to happen.
(3) If I'm certain E is going to happen, then my belief in H, full stop, should just be my belief in H given E. If E is certain, then "H" and "H if E" are the same bet.
(4) So my belief in H, full stop, is 1/2.
(5) But I know H right now, so my belief in H, full stop, is 1.
(6) So I should believe H with certainty and with 1/2 at the same time, which is nonsense.

I can't live with (6). And I really want to keep (3) — if I'm certain something will happen, then conditioning on it shouldnt change my belief, that feels like just how probabilities work. But I also don't want to say my belief should be 1/2 when I actually know the answer. So where does this go wrong? If I have to give something up, I'd rather give up Reflection than (3), but I want to know what I lose if I do. Like, is there some argument that's supposed to force Reflection that I'm just throwing away?
