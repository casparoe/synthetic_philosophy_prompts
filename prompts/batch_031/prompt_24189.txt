Analyze the 1994 *Bell Curve* controversy (Herrnstein and Murray) as a case study in what the heritability statistic does and does not support. Reconstruct the strongest, most careful version of the authors' inference — they reported a roughly one-standard-deviation Black–White IQ gap and argued it was at least partly genetic, but I don't want the genetic-determinist straw man; I want the role they assigned to the stability of the gap and to the implausibility of purely environmental explanations, and the hedging they did. Then reconstruct the strongest conceptual objection from the critics (not the point that they were racist): explain precisely why a high within-population heritability estimate does not, by itself, license the inference that a stable between-population mean difference has a genetic basis, and identify the additional assumptions that inference requires — about the comparability of reaction norms across populations, about the relevant environmental differences being ruled out, and about the heritability estimate generalizing across populations — and say which of those the authors did or did not establish. Bring in the standard counterexamples and say exactly what each shows and what it does not: the height case (highly heritable, yet Dutch and US mean heights differ for environmental reasons, and Dutch mean height has risen sharply in recent generations), and the fact that heritability depends on the environmental variance in the population studied, so that a genetically fixed trait can have low heritability in a variable environment while an environmentally sensitive trait can have high heritability in a homogeneous one. Finally, say what the episode reveals about the heritability concept itself: is it a well-defined, useful population parameter that gets systematically misread, or is it so entangled with the intuition of genetic determination that it invites the very fallacy it is supposed to avoid? Be precise about which parameter — within-population heritability versus the genetic contribution to a between-population difference — is doing what work at each step, and be honest about where the authors' argument is stronger than the critics' caricature and where the critics' objection is genuinely correct.
