Is there a name for this? I'm a radiologist, and at some point in training you stop being able to explain your reads. A resident shows me a scan, I say "that one's fine" before I've consciously looked for anything, and when they ask why it's fine there is geniunely nothing to tell them — not a forgotten rule, not a rule I'm too busy to state, I don't think there was ever a rule in my head to begin with. Sometimes I can construct a rule afterwards, but I don't trust it's what I was actually using, because the constructed version misclassifies cases I still call correctly. And it's not muscle memory — there's no motor component in telling whether a sentence in my second language is slightly off, and I get the same wordless certainty there. What convinces me this deserves a name, rather than just being "experience," is that it behaves like knowledge rather than a habit or a reflex: it can be wrong, it can be corrected, it generalizes to scans I've never seen, and two of us can disagree where one of us is actually right, not just reacting differently. So — is there an established concept for being reliably right about things without anything statable existing anywhere in you? If there are several near-miss concepts out there I want to know which one fits and where the edges are, e.g. whether a pianist's fingerings are the same kind of thing or something else entirely. A bare term won't do much for me.
