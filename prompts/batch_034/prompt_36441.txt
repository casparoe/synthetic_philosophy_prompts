so my ethics class got onto whether you can have a reason to want something that's bad, and my teacher brought up this demon case and then basically refused to solve it, and i think i've found a way to make it prove WAY too much and i want to know where it breaks.

the case: a demon will torture you forever unless you desire that your mother suffer. everyone in class agreed (a) the threat is a reason to try to get yourself to want her to suffer, you'd be insane not to try, and also (b) the threat gives you zero reason to think her suffering would be good or worth wanting. my teacher said this is called the "wrong kind of reasons" problem and then literally changed the subject. here's my argument:

(1) A reason to X is just a fact that counts in favor of X-ing, where X can be an action or an attitude like desiring or believing.
(2) The demon's threat counts in favor of you desiring that your mother suffer — if you don't, you get tortured, and facts about getting tortured clearly count in favor of avoiding them.
(3) So the threat is a reason for you to desire that she suffer.
(4) If there is a reason to desire that P, then P is desirable. i mean that's basically what "desirable" means, that there's reason to desire it.
(5) So if the demon shows up, your mother's suffering becomes desirable.
(6) But no threat could make her suffering desirable — that was the ONE thing everyone agreed on.
(7) So (2) is false: the threat isn't a reason for the desire at all, at most it's a reason to try to acquire the desire.

now the part i think proves too much. run the same thing on belief:

(8) A billionaire will pay you a million dollars if and only if you sincerely beleive the moon is made of cheese.
(9) By the same reasoning as (1)-(3), the offer is a reason to believe the moon is made of cheese.
(10) If there's a reason to believe P, then P is supported by evidence — reasons for belief just ARE evidence, that's the whole deal with belief.
(11) So the offer is evidence that the moon is made of cheese. so the moon's composition depends on bank accounts??
