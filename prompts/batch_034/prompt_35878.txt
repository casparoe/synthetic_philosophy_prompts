- Rescue: you can pull five strangers from a burning building or one stranger — same risk, same effort, no difference except the count. She'd pull the five without hesitation.
- One person will suffer a year of severe pain unless we trigger a one-second mild itch in a million people. She said yes, itch the million, though she made a face about it.
