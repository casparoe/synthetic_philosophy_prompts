Here is the proposition, made as precise as I can manage: the Enlightenment, understood as the intellectual and public-sphere movement running roughly from 1680 to 1800, was on net a greater cause of political emancipation — constitutional constraint on rulers, religious toleration, the abolitionist turn, the vocabulary of the rights of man — than of rationalized domination: the centralizing administrative state, the scientific management of populations, colonial "civilizing" projects, and the ideological templates that later, nastier regimes found convenient to inherit. My credence in that proposition, as stated, is about 0.7. Hers is about 0.25. I want your number too, but the number is the least of it; the deliverable is the derivation.

Please decompose along the following lines, and add any terms you think I've missed.
