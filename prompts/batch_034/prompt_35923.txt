so I'm a phd student in philosophy of physics and our reading group wants everyone to write up their metaphysical profile so the rest can poke holes in it before our session. here's mine. go through it and tell me whether it hangs together — and don't be gentle, I'd rather find out now.

(1) I'm an ontic structural realist: at the fundamental level what exists is relational structure, and "objects" like electrons are just node-positions in that structure. I take the quantum statistics seriously on this: fundamental particles aren't individuals, there's no fact of the matter about which one *this* electron is.

(2) I'm a physicalist about the mind, no dualism, but I think experiences are individuals in a robust sense — the fact that this pain is *mine*, this very one, seems to outrun its position in any causal/functional strcture.

(3) I think Newman's problem is a real worry: structure on its own is trivially satisfiable, any old collection of relata can carry any structure. so there has to be a further fact about which structure is physically instantiated, over and above any mathematical fact about isomorphism. but I refuse to ground that in intrinsic natures of the relata — no quiddities at the fundamental level, that way lies Dipert-style stuff I can't buy.

(4) I do NOT think the universe is a mathematical object. our formalism is a representation, and there's a real distinction between the world's structure and any piece of mathematics that captures it.

(5) tables and other middle-sized things are straightforwardly individuals — there's a fact about whether the table in my office is the same one I bought last year — and that's not just a manner of speaking. so individuality emerges somewhere between electrons and tables, and I think that's fine.

what I want from you: rank the tensions by severity instead of listing them all as equally bad, say for each one what the cheapest fix would be and what it costs, and flag which pairs only look like they're in tension but actually dissolve on inspection. then tell me this: if I had to give up exactly one of the five commitments, which single revision would clean up the most? I honestly don't know where the weak link is, so don't decide for my sake. also sorry if this is a mess, I wrote it fast.
