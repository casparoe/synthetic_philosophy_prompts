By the end he was applying it to everything — the smartphone, the internet, even the abolition of slavery — "someone would have done it eventually, the particular person is just who happened to get there first." Something about that bugs me but I couldn't pin down what, and I think it's because the one sentence is secretly doing several different jobs at once.
