I have no academic background in philosophy — these are views I've worked out for myself over several years of reading popular science and arguing with friends, and I would like someone to check the joins. A friend who reads a lot called my second view something like superrational thinking; I don't know whether that's the standard term, and it doesn't matter for what I'm asking.

Here is where I've landed:

(1) In Newcomb's problem — where a very good predictor has already put a million in one box if it predicted I would take only that box, and nothing in it if it predicted I would take both boxes, with a thousand sitting openly in the other box — I take only the sealed box. Something in the past tracked my deliberation, so my present choice is welded to that past thing, and I may as well choose for the pair.

(2) In a one-shot prisoner's dilemma against a person I know to be, in effect, my double — same upbringing, same reading, same habits of thought — I cooperate. I cannot make it the case that I defect while the double cooperates; whatever reasoning moves me moves the double too, so I treat the choice as ours and pick the outcome we would both prefer.

(3) In the same dilemma against a random stranger, I defect. His choice is a fact about the world that my choice does not touch, and the pair of moves is not mine to choose.

(4) I vote in large elections and I press my friends to vote, and I defend it in a way that sounds suspiciously like my second view: my single ballot does not shift the outcome, but the many people who share my values and habits of reasoning will tend to decide as I decide, so my decision rides along with thousands of others. Most of those people, I should admit, are strangers to me.

(5) I have no patience for making yourself worse off here and now in order to be the kind of person who does better somewhere else or later. If a rule costs me in this very situation, following it anyway is a gesture, not rationality.

What I want is an audit, not a lecture. Go through these and tell me which pairs are genuinely in tension and how badly, which pairs only look to be in tension and why the appearance dissolves, and for each real tension what the cheapest repair would be and what that repair would cost me elsewhere in the list. Then tell me whether there is one single revision that would tidy up the most. Please order the tensions by severity rather than presenting them as equally bad, and be specific about which of my five commitments has to give where — I honestly cannot see it myself.
