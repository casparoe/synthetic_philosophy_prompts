So, no such God. That's the shape of it.

What I want from you is your own credence for the soundness claim, but way more importantly the decomposition. Break it into the sub-questions the final number actually rests on — something like: how likely it is that a loving God would in fact prevent nonresistant nonbelief (this is where the anti-coercion replies live, the idea that overwhelming obvious presence would foreclose genuinely free response, plus the ones saying relationship isn't actually blocked by nonbelief); how likely it is that real nonresistant nonbelievers exist, including the tricky cases like lifelong sincere seekers; and whether hiddenness is ultimately just a slice of the problem of evil or a genuinely independent problem (does that change how the premises should be scored?). Give me a rough probability and one line of justification for each, then show how they combine to your bottom number.
