so, background: im a backend engineer, no philosophy training, cs degree with the standard discrete math and that's it. I recently fell down a rabbit hole after a coworker mentioned that "every vector space has a basis" isnt provable in ZF alone — you need the axiom of choice on top. that led me through a bunch of wikipedia pages and mathoverflow threads, and now I have Opinions, but I genuinely can't tell if they form a consistent package or if im contradicting myself somewhere. here's roughly where I've landed:

(1) Im basically a platonist about infinite sets — completed infinities are fine, Cantor's paradise, all the reals "exist" in whatever sense anything mathematical exists, no finitism for me. (2) The axiom of choice itself seems obviously true: given any family of nonempty sets, of course there's a function that picks one element out of each set — just go through the sets one at a time and pick something. (3) On the other hand I have a strong instinct that every object I accept in math has to be definable in principle — if you tell me a set exists but there is literally no possible description of it, at any level of principle, then I feel like you havent actually told me anything. (4) Banach-Tarski, to me, is a straight reductio: a theorem saying you can cut a ball into finitely many pieces and reassemble them into two balls the same size as the original doesnt show that math is surprising, it shows somethings rotten in ZFC. (5) Countable choice im fine with — I can picture a machine marching through a countable list of sets, grabbing one element each — but full choice over arbitrary families of sets of reals feels like hand-waving, and honestly I would deny it if you pushed me. (6) And I'm pretty sure the reals cannot be well-ordered, like there is no way to line up the reals so that every nonempty subset has a least element. that just seems flat out impossible. though I admit the choice principle itself still sounds fine to me, so those feel like seperate claims to me.

So: which of these six are actually in tension, which only look like they are, and what's the cheapest fix for each? please rank the tensions by severity instead of listing them all as equally bad, and if there's a single revision that would clean up the most mess, tell me which one and what it costs. I don't know enough about this stuff to know where the bodies are buried, so dont go easy on me.
