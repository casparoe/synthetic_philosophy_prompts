I'm a science writer working on a piece about cultural evolution, and I keep bumping into the same wall and i want to check my framing before pitching my editor. My working idea is that Darwinian evolution only works when you have discrete units that copy themselves faithfully, like genes, and that culture obviously doesn't work that way — habits and ideas are picked up imperfectly, blended together, and deliberately modified as they pass along, which is basically Lamarckian inheritance of acquired characteristics, the exact thing Darwinism was supposed to replace. So the whole "memes" talk from Dawkins onward seems to me like a catchy metaphor that can't be a real scientific theory, and the honest headline would be something like "culture evolves, but not by natural selection." Is that right? And if so, how should I explain in the piece why so many serious researchers still model culture with selection-style mathematics anyway instead of just saying it's Lamarck all the way down?
