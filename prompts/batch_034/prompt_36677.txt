2. Preferences changing over time isn't a rationality failure in itself — predictable change (getting older, developing new tastes) can be perfectly rational.
3. Money pumps do show something real: an agent with a fixed preference cycle who gets pumped is irrational, so acyclicity is a genuine requirement of rationality.

6. Resolute choice is a mistake: if at a node you'd prefer to deviate from an earlier plan, deviating is what rationality requires — past-you's plan can't bind you.
