I'd like your credence that the reference class problem has a unique correct solution: that for any statistical inference there is a fact of the matter about which reference class is the right one to condition on, rather than the choice being a pragmatic one that different reasonable agents could legitimately make differently. Say which term of your decomposition their disagreement most likely lives in, and whether your framework implies anything about which engineer to trust.
