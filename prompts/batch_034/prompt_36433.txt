For each requirement, give the motivating case or argument, and flag which you take to be genuine constraints versus shibboleths of particular camps — e.g., whether Dutch book or betting-quotient arguments should be allowed to settle Beauty's credence, and whether " Beauty learns nothing new" is a datum or a halfer's talking point. Keep the weight on the criteria and their motivations rather than on scoring existing views against them; I'll do the scorecard in my dissertation. Please keep it under 700 words.
