Fine as far as it goes, but she now grades everything at dinner and I want to be able to predict her before she opens her mouth.

A mouse that learns a maze by running it over and over is Skinnerian, full stop. A scrub-jay that re-caches its food when it notices another jay watching is Popperian, because it's reasoning about what the watcher will remember. A chimp that picks up a few sticks, rejects them, and then uses one to fish termites is Popperian. A tourist consulting a map is Gregorian, obviously. And — the one that started our arguement — she says a rat that watches another rat press a lever and then does it itself on the first try is still only Skinnerian, because copying isn't a mind-tool, it's just trial and error with someone else running the trials.
