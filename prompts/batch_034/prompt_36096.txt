so ive been on the panpsychism train for a couple years now -- specifically the russellian monist version, where fundamental particles have tiny proto-experiential properties that serve as the categorical grounds of physical dispositions, and our consciousness is somehow constituted out of theirs. whenever someone raises the combination problem I reach for what I take to be a deflationary move: composition is mysterious everywhere. nothing in particle physics tells you why a bunch of quarks compose one proton rather than three overlapping things, or why my neurons compose one organism instead of seven slightly different clumps of matter. so the panpsychist's question of how micro-subjects combine into me is, I claim, just the general problem of the one and the many that everybody already has, and it's unfair to treat it as a special embarrassment for my view.

I want a serious stress test of that move. what would count against it? I'm thinking along these lines but feel free to add or correct: is there a real disanalogy between physical composition and phenomenal combination -- e.g. that subjects have a point of view, a "for-me-ness," and it's not clear what a mereological fusion of viewpoints would even be, whereas fusions of matter don't need to be "for" anything? and which versions of the combination problem does my move actually touch -- I gather people distinguish subject combination from quality combination from structure combination -- and which ones does it leave completely untouched even if it works? also: are there panpsychists who used to rely on exactly this "physics has it too" analogy and then abandoned it, and if so what changed their mind? I'd rather hear what defenders themselves concede than what emergentists gloat about.

two more things I want you to be honest about. first, if the analogy between physical and phenomenal composition really holds, does it hold so well that it collapses the view into emergentism -- because if consciousness relates to micro-experience the way macro-objects relate to particles, and macro-objects are just... emergent, haven't I traded the combination problem for the very emergence I took panpsychism to avoid? second, is my position in danger of being qualified into unfalsifiability -- is there anything, at the level of physics or neuroscience or philosophy, that could happen that would make me give it up, or is it tuned so it can absorb anything? rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, and tell me which ones a committed defender would bite the bullet on versus which would genuinely force a rethink. I care more about the disanalogy question than anything else, so lead with that if you can.
