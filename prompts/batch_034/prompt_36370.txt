Can someone check whether my views on weakness of will actually hang together? I've been stewing over this since new year (resolution: earlier nights, going great as you can tell). Here's the set:

(1) Akrasia is a real, irrational failure. When at midnight I scroll for two hours even though hours earlier I judged, all things considered, that I shouldn't, that's weakness of will — a defect in me, not merely a change of mind about what's worth doing.

(2) The best explanation of what's going on is hyperbolic discounting: my valuations reverse as rewards get near. From a distance the early night wins; at midnight the feed looms much larger, and it's the same mechanism behind the classic reversal where you'd take £110 in 31 days over £100 in 30 days, but take £100 now over £110 tomorrow. I find that picture basically right for humans, and I dont think it's a bug in the description — it's just how valuation works, same as for pigeons.

(3) There's no fact of the matter about which time's preferences are the correct ones. Whether the 10pm self or the midnight self should get its way is, I think, like asking which of two people should win a dispute — there's no objective discount rate, and earlier isn't automatically more authoritative. Comparisons across time within a person are on a par with comparisons between people.

(4) Precommitment is rational and I use it shamelessly: my partner holds the screen-time passcode, I buy the small tub of ice cream rather than the large one, and I think this is not just permitted but genuinely sensible, the more of it the better.

(5) Despite all that, when I cave I think self-blame is appropriate. The regret isnt just pain, it tracks something — a real failure — and I'd think someone was fooling themselves if they shrugged and said their midnight self just had different preferences and nothing went wrong.

Something feels off about the package but I honestly can't locate it. A few hunches I'm not confident in: maybe (2) quietly swaps out the thing (1) is about, since a preference reversal isnt yet a judgement betrayed? Or does (3) turn (4) into one self grabbing power over another with no more right to it than the reverse — would I then have to say the midnight me is equally entitled to sabotage the 10pm me's arrangements, which sounds mad but might follow? And does (5) need a standard that (3) insists doesn't exist? Or are some of these fine together and only look incompatible — I suspect at least one pair does. Please go through it pair by pair, tell me which tensions are real and how bad, which ones dissolve on inspection, and what the cheapest fix for each would be. Rank them by severity rather than listing them as all equally damning, and don't rebuild the whole thing unless you really have to.
