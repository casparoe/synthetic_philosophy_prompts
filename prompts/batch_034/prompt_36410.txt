I want to push back but I realize I can't say what the one-liner is actually claiming. I know disjunctivists deny the common element, but I don't want a survey that just shrugs at the end: commit to which reading the argument genuinely needs, and tell me whether that reading is true. And is rejecting it (disjunctivism) really the only escape, or can you accept some weak reading and still refuse the conclusion?
