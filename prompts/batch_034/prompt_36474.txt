I've been keeping a running list of my gut reactions to hypocritical blamers, and I want to know if there's an actual principle behind them, because right now it feels like vibes. My uncle cheated on his taxes for years, stopped only when he got audited, and now rails about tax cheats at every family dinner — his blame feels off to me, like he's forfeited the right. But a college friend who cheated on one exam twenty years ago, admits it freely, and now grades exams and calls out cheating — her blame seems totally fine. Same act, both reformed, different verdict? That can't just come down to the audit. Then my sister has never plagiarized anything, though honestly only because she's never been desparate enough, and when she condemned a plagiarist I'd say go right ahead — but it bugs me that she seems to have MORE standing than my reformed friend, even though my friend actually knows the temptation and my sister doesn't. So standing can't just be "never did it yourself," and it also can't be "knows what it's like," because those point in opposite directions and I hold both.

It gets worse. My coworker who drives an SUV blaming people who fly for climate damage — that blame feels kinda fine to me? Whereas a different coworker who takes one long-haul flight a year blaming our colleague's monthly flying feels off, even though the flight-blamer's own emissions are way lower than the SUV driver's. The person with LESS hypocrisy has LESS standing on my gut, which seems backwards no matter how I slice it. And separately I'm pretty sure a bystander who would obviously have done the same thing if tempted, but never was, has standing — the "there but for the grace of God" person is allowed to blame, right?

Is there a principle that captures all of this, or as close as possible? Please state it precisely, tell me which case motivates each part, and say what it predicts for the case I keep circling: my neighbor who eats meat but says he's morally transitioning off it, and blames me for eating meat without even trying. And be honest about which of my verdicts you'd overturn to get a cleaner principle — I suspect something has to go, probably either the SUV one or the sister one, but I genuinely don't know which.
