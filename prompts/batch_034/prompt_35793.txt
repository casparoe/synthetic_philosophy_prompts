His line: there's just cheese, arranged in a certain shape. The hole is literally no-thing — there's no extra object sitting in the fridge next to the cheese, just cheese and air.

I think this is nuts. Holes have locations (the hole is in the top left corner of the slice), they have sizes, you can count them, and last summer I definately fell into a hole while hiking and broke my ankle. You can't fall into nothing.

So either my word "exist" is so cheap that it's meaningless, or I have to start telling him which of these REALLY exist and which are just ways of talking — and if I claim I can do that, I owe him some account of what the difference is.

I say if it's all verbal then nobody has to concede anything, which annoys him even more.
