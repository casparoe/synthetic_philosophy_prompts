so, full disclosure before I ask this: I've taught philosophy of mind for a long time and every year we hit the panpsychism unit and I conveniently never say whether I believe any of it. This semester a student flat-out asked me where I stood, and I opened my mouth and made it worse by promising I'd write up my actual position and circulate it. So now I want to know if it even hangs together before forty undergrads pick it apart. Here's the package:

(1) The hard problem is real — no amount of structural, functional, or dynamical description will ever explain why there's experience at all. (2) I'm a structuralist about physics: physics characterizes everything relationally, dispositions all the way down, and it's just silent on the intrinsic nature of whatever bears those dispositions. (3) I fill the silence with experience — the intrinsic nature of the fundamental physical properties is phenomenal, and I mean the full-blooded panpsychist version, not the retreat where you say it's some neutral quiddity we can't name: electrons have tiny experiences. (4) The unity of consciousness is a genuine datum. Binding is real; there is one subject here having this thought together with the taste of my coffee, not a committee of micro-subjects that I'm somehow mistaking for a single me. (5) I'm flatly against strong emergence — nothing with genuinely novel causal powers or novel ontology just pops into existence because parts got arranged the right way. (6) At the same time, macro-level functionalism seems basically right to me: a perfect functional duplicate of my brain, built out of whatever substrate, has the same experiences, at least in all structural respects. I know the received line is that panpsychists can't help themselves to functionalism like that, but honestly I've never felt the force of it.

Go through these and tell me which pairs are genuinely in tension, which ones only look like it, and how bad the real ones are. Please rank the tensions by severity rather than just listing them, tell me what would have to give in each case and what it costs, and then tell me which single revision would clean up the most. I genuinely can't tell whether my problem is with (4) — maybe the unity of consciousness is less of a datum than I've been assuming — or whether I'm a closet strong emergentist wearing panpsychist clothing somewhere between (3) and (4), which would be awkward given some of the things I've said in print about emergentists. Be specific and be brutal; this is going out to students who know how to smell blood.
