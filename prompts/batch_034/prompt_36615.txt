I'm a senior in high school taking an elective called Philosophy of Mind (it's basically 45 minutes of arguing, it's great) and I've come out of it pretty convinced of something like epiphenomenalism. It's like the smoke coming out of a train. The train moves because of the engine, not the smoke.

But my friend Maya has been on my case about it and I honestly don't have a good answer for her. Her big point is: if consiousness literally does nothing, then how am I sitting here telling her about my conscious experience? Which seems super weird — the view can't even explain why I'd ever TALK about it, or write songs about it, or that there's a whole field of people who argue about it. Is that a real problem or just vibes?
