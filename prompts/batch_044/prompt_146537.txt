ok so i've been trying to figure out if being sick is just your body being biologial broken or if it's also about what people think is wrong with you. i came up with this argument while walking my dog but it feels like it eats itself.

1. if being sick is purely biological - like your heart is actually broken - then anything that works different but not broken isn't sick. so being intersex or being really old isn't a disease, it's just variation.

2. but doctors do treat intersex bodies and old age stuff, so they must think it's broken somehow. but if it's not actually broken biologically, then they must be using some idea of "this is bad for you" to call it sick. that's normativisim i guess.

3. now here's where i get stuck. i made up this idea i call functional dissent - when your body does its job fine but in a way that dissents from the average. like being left handed used to be corrected even though it works fine. so if normativism is true, then functional dissent becomes a disease whenever society decides it's bad.

4. but then if you personally think your left handedness or intersex body is totally fine, and you reject the idea it's bad, then by normativism it's not a disease for you. so two people with the exact same body, one thinks it's ok and one doesn't, and only one is sick? that seems nuts.

5. so normativism must be wrong. but then we're back to naturalism, which says old age isn't broken. but we still treat osteoporosis. so naturalism can't explain why medicine helps with aging either.

6. therefore neither idea works, which can't be right. where's the mistake? i want to keep the idea that doctors can help old people without saying aging is literally broken, and also that being different isn't automatically sick.
