Please write the essay question itself (suitable for a 75-minute response of approximately 800–900 words), followed by a model answer of about 600 words, and a marking scheme totaling 20 points. Every part should be rigorous enough that a student who has only memorized slogans about erasing history or cancel culture cannot score highly.
