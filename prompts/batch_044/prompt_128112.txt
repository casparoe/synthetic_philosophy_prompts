This is defended by appealing to the internal structure of that dimension, where ordinary and extreme states are qualitatively distinct. So far, this is a standard lexical claim.

But then, without providing any inter-dimensional metric, the same proponent extends this lexical relation across qualitatively different kinds of harm. They treat extreme existential dread, or the systematic erosion of agency, as standing in the same lexical relation to ordinary well-being as extreme physical pain does—while simultaneously treating these different extreme harms as comparable to one another within a single hierarchy.
