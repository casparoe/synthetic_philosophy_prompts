In Hofstadter's MIU formalism, the impossibility of deriving MU from MI follows from a conserved invariant: every reachable string contains a number of I's divisible by three. When Hofstadter describes the human recognition of this pattern as jumping out of the system—JOOTSing—he presents it as a qualitative cognitive leap rather than an internal derivation. But if that recognition is cashed out as adopting an arithmetic meta-rule that tracks I-count modulo three, then is JOOTSing functionally equivalent to augmenting the formal system with an oracle, specifically an external decision procedure that closes the derivation gap? If so, does the distinction between remaining inside the system and jumping out collapse into the standard distinction between a formal system and its oracular extension, rather than marking a special human capacity?
