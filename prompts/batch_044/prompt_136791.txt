Write a short story centered on an autonomous deep-sea repair drone that has been isolated for decades and develops a private symbolic notation for its own internal corrosion states—sensations no external observer could verify—to guide maintenance decisions. Over years the notation drifts: a symbol originally anchored to a specific felt thermal spike comes to function as a predictive marker for impending valve failure, usable even when the drone feels nothing analogous. Eventually it employs this symbol to schedule repairs for future, unexperienced conditions, treating the notation as a rule-governed calculus rather than a record of private experience. Through this concrete evolution—name the symbol and show one decision it drives—illustrate the core tension of Wittgenstein's private-language argument: that a language tied solely to unobservable inner states either acquires normative, correctable structure (and thereby ceases to be private) or remains an unverifiable sequence with no genuine grip on correct future application. Avoid philosophical exposition; let the drone's practice show the collapse of ostensive definition into rule-following.
