Maintain complete deadpan scientific seriousness; do not wink, use scare quotes, or step out of the journal voice. The piece should be legible to a reader unfamiliar with Dennett's wasp but conceptually rigorous enough that someone versed in the free-will literature recognizes the satire as fair.
