Compare these two positions with respect to: (i) whether an infinite regression with diminishing epistemic weight can escape the charge of arbitrariness that foundationalists typically press against non-terminating chains; and (ii) whether foundationalism's basic beliefs suffer from a problem structurally analogous to initiating an experimental protocol from an unverified calibration origin. In your answer, address whether either model can genuinely accommodate ordinary scientific reasoning, in which we routinely act on beliefs supported only by further beliefs while still claiming rational entitlement.
