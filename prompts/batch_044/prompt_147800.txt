Suppose you are advising a three-year interdisciplinary research program—philosophers of mind, conceptual historians of science, and cognitive theorists working without empirical lab resources—that wants to assess whether Erwin Schrödinger’s Vedanta-inspired monism about consciousness can survive as a live philosophical position rather than a historical curiosity. Their premise is that individual minds are not genuinely plural, and that the appearance of separate consciousnesses is in some sense illusory, analogous to the way continuous physical processes can present discrete boundaries. They do not want a scholarly commentary on Schrödinger’s texts; they want a defended agenda for conceptual work.

Please identify and rank the three to five open questions, unresolved tensions, or theoretical directions that matter most for this assessment. For each entry, defend its place in the ordering: why resolving it is upstream of the others, how tractable it is for purely conceptual rather than empirical research, and what kind of argument or development over the next few years would move it up or down the list. Be explicit about the criteria you are using—importance for the viability of monism, dependence relations among problems, tractability for philosophers, and risk of dead ends.

In particular, address where the conceptual pressure is genuinely live: whether a monist can accommodate indexical self-reference and first-person disagreement without collapsing into solipsism; whether split-brain and dissociative-identity phenomena pose an in-principle obstacle or merely an empirical complication; and whether the distinction between original and derived intentionality offers resources for the monist or instead undermines the idea that apparent mental pluralities carry any real ontological weight. Conclude by noting which of these directions should be deprioritized despite historical interest, and whether the overall program risks being insulated from falsification in ways that are problematic for a conceptual research agenda of this kind.
