Consider a synthetic bacterium engineered with a metabolic disposition to process a specific substrate. The moment the substrate appears, a kill-switch activates and destroys the metabolic machinery—functioning as a fink that removes the disposition precisely at its triggering condition—while an independent quarantine pathway simultaneously masks any residual activity without altering the machinery. Given that both an actual fink and an actual mask are present, does the bacterium still possess a "pure" intrinsic disposition, or does this pairing show that dispositions are not properties of the organism itself but merely relational artifacts defined by the absence of interfering structures?
