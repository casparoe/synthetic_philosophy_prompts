Construct a thought experiment in which qualitative hedonism is forced to classify two ordinary-life experiences as pleasures despite their lacking both a shared phenomenal quality and a shared functional role, leaving only a relational feature that hedonists traditionally reject as sufficient. In one paragraph, present two subjects: Subject A, a researcher who feels sustained, intense pleasure upon recognizing that two competing theoretical frameworks in her field are actually describing an identical structure at different levels of abstraction—an experience characterized by closure, unification, and the reinforcement of cognitive commitment; and Subject B, a reader who feels an equally sustained, intense pleasure upon discovering that a literary text sustains two incompatible but equally rich interpretations without resolution—an experience characterized by openness, resistance to closure, and the reinforcement of suspended judgment. Specify that neither case involves any common sensory quality (no bodily relaxation, no warmth, no taste), that the functional roles are opposite (belief-consolidation versus ambiguity-tolerance), and that the only common feature is a second-order relational property: each subject takes pleasure in her own cognitive stance toward a representational structure, regardless of whether that stance is one of mapping or of not-mapping. After the scenario, state exactly what qualitative hedonism predicts—whether both count as pleasures and, if so, what intrinsic phenomenal feel they share—versus what the heterogeneity objection predicts. Identify the structural feature that makes the relational property unavoidable and that therefore forces the hedonist either to deny that one case is genuine pleasure or to identify a shared quality that is merely relational. Then explain how a qualitative hedonist could attack the case, likely by arguing that one experience is not pleasure but rather satisfaction, aesthetic appreciation, or intellectual virtue, and assess whether that move succeeds without rendering the hedonist's category arbitrary. Keep the scenario realistic, without artificial brain stimulation, exotic technology, or deceptive verbal reports.
