But our board's nurse practicioner argues it's just a normative call about how people ought to work in 2025, and tagging it as a disease lets employers medicalize bad scheduling instead of fixing it. Since our taxonomy feeds directly into insurance eligibility algorithms and grant applications, the call isn't theoretical.

I need a written opinion I can circulate before Thursday's meeting that recommends a concrete course of action, either admit it under a restricted category, reject it outright, or approve it with conditions, and it should include a timeline for implementation or review. The reasoning has to seriously engage the practicioner's point that health isn't just statistical normality but involves values about good functioning, though please keep the language accessible since I don't have a philosophy background and don't want a wall of jargon. Write the whole thing as continuous prose with no headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables, just like a long email, and please don't cite any books or papers since I need to understand the argument without going to a library. If you can, also explain briefly where the line between describing a dysfunction and making a value judgment actually sits, because I'm not sure our board knows how to tell the difference in practice.
