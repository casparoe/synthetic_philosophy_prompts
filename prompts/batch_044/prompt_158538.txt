This reminds me of Otto Neurath's ISOTYPE project and his argument that a universal picture language should avoid metaphysical commitments by depicting only observable clusters, or Ballungen, rather than abstract theoretical terms. Given that tension, what philosophical commitments about representation, observation, and cross-cultural universality does the modern signage practice actually presuppose? Does it require a belief that images are inherently transparent, or can it be made consistent with a conventionalist, anti-metaphysical view of meaning?
