i'm borrowing the "hinge" language from wittgenstein's on certainty, but i'm not sure the analogy holds. in epistemology hinges are propositions we take for granted; in my case the silence is more like a negative condition of possibility. am i just smuggling in metaphysics? also, does this differ in any real way from standard "epistemic injustice" or "archival violence" talk, or is it just rebranding with a fancier vocabulary?

1. whether the hinge analogy is stretched past breaking
