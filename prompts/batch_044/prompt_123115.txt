To push the idea further, I have imagined a variation I call the segmented life: a person spends ten years inside the machine, is then returned to reality for one day every decade with all memory of the virtual period erased, and is then reconnected. I want to know what any adequate theory of human well-being would actually have to deliver in order to account for both situations.
