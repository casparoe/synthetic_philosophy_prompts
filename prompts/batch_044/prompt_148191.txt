After a long argument with a friend about whether conceivability gives us any real guide to physical possibility—she held that an absence of contradiction in a clear description is a defeasible reason to treat a mechanism as possible, while I insisted that many descriptions hide contradictions that emerge only when you recursively derive second-order implications against our best background theories, and that proposals often survive only because critical parameters are left undefined—I need to settle the matter for practical use. I serve on an institutional review board that meets monthly to decide whether speculative engineering proposals merit feasibility-study funding; our members have advanced scientific training but no philosophy background, and every decision must be documented with a named sign-off. Please construct a reusable decision procedure, in ordered steps with explicit questions, that distinguishes surface coherence (first-pass imaginative consistency) from depth coherence (recursive derivation without contradiction or undefined parameters), explicitly flags what I will call “modal leakage” (survival of a proposal only through undefined or protected parameters), and marks the exact points where judgment cannot be eliminated and must be handed to a person. For each step, state what counts as a sufficient answer, where the procedure stops with rejection, where it stops with approval for feasibility study, and where it stops with hand-off to expert or value-based judgment rather than modal status. Apply the procedure to three proposals: (1) a macro-scale quantum coherence engine that harvests zero-point energy via resonant cavity arrays; (2) a neuroprosthetic interface that integrates with glial networks to permanently augment long-term potentiation; and (3) a stratospheric aerosol climate-intervention system guided by real-time atmospheric modeling. Include one case—specifically proposal (2)—where the procedure yields an answer the board will resist: that the proposal survives depth testing and should advance, despite the board’s inclination to dismiss it as fantasy. After presenting the procedure and the three applications, provide a brief structured summary in JSON with exactly these fields: “procedure_steps” (ordered array of objects with keys “step”, “question”, “sufficient_answer”, “outcome”), “modal_leakage_checks” (array of strings describing the checks), “case_applications” (array of objects with keys “proposal”, “surface_result”, “depth_result”, “procedure_output”, “expected_board_reaction”), and “irreducible_judgment_points” (array of strings explaining where and why the procedure must hand off).
