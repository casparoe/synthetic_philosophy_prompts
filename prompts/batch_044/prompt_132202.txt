everyone explains al-Ghazali's occasionalism with the movie-projector thing: God projects discrete frames, causation is just rapid succession like motion from still images, so denying necessary connection isn't denying science. they use it to defend him against the charge that he makes the world irrational. but i think the analogy might be hiding more than it shows. can you map it out precisely — what's God, what's the frames, what's the projector, what's the audience's inference — then say where it cracks? especially with his actual example of fire and cotton, where he says the fire doesn't burn at all, God creates the burn separately. does the projector image smuggle in a mechanistic substrate he rejects? and does the defense — occasionalism is science-friendly — survive once you see the disanalogy, or was the analogy doing all the work?
