The prisoner's ascent is therefore not a journey into a mystical otherworld, but the acquisition of access to the abstract layer: reading the interface definition, understanding the type signature, or inspecting the protocol that governs correct behavior across all instances. The sun corresponds to the principle of coherence or consistency that validates a specification across every possible correct implementation.
