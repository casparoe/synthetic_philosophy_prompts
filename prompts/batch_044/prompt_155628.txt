The director says rationing alreadys works like that and philosophy shouldn't block practical care. I need a memo recommending approval or rejection with clear criteria we could actually vote on, plus an honest treatment of the strongest counter-argument. Specifically, I want to know whether the pooling mechanism actually violates the separateness worry or just looks like it does, and wether a modified version — maybe one with individual floor guarantees that keep anyone below a baseline — could solve it without abandoning the utilitarian logic entirely. Don't ask for reading lists or citations, I just need reasoning we can use in the room, and please don't invoke obscure philosophers — stick to the core idea and how it applies to this specific allocation model.
