I'm defending this idea I call "scaffolded cranes" — cultural evolution builds bottom-up like Dennett's cranes, but it relies on temporary environmental scaffolding (institutional norms, writing systems, that kind of thing) that gets internalized over generations and then can fall away. I need them to see the scaffold is genuinely temporary and environmental, not hidden emergence and not hidden purpose.
