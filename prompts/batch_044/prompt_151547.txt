In the philosophy of supererogation—morally meritorious acts that exceed what duty requires—which unresolved conceptual questions should take priority for someone trying to advance the field over the next five to ten years? I am not asking for a literature review; I want a defended agenda. Focus especially on two underdeveloped issues: (1) whether collective supererogation is coherent—cases in which a group performs an act that is praiseworthy yet not required of the group or of any individual member, without reducing to aggregated individual supererogation; and (2) whether epistemic supererogation—exceeding one's evidential obligations in belief formation—marks a genuine normative category or collapses into prudential or moral duty. Rank the top three or four open problems (including these if warranted), explain the dependency relations among them (which are upstream), assess tractability for conceptual versus empirical work, and specify what evidence or argument would cause you to reorder them. State clearly what you would deprioritize and on what grounds—importance, intractability, or limited downstream effect. Write the response in American English.
