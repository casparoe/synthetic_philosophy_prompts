In standard experimental protocols, subjects are asked to rate the visibility or presence of a stimulus—often on a graded scale—while objective discrimination performance is measured simultaneously. When the subjective report and the behavioral measure align, the report is treated as direct, transparent evidence that conscious perception occurred; the subject’s narrative is granted evidential privilege, and the trial is included in the analysis of phenomenal consciousness.
