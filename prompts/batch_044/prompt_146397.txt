I'm trying to fix the specification for what counts as honesty versus deception in autonomous AI systems before I read any more particular theories. The forums keep recycling the same three examples — sycophancy, sandbagging, and deceptive alignment — and arguing about whether each is lying, but I want the full desiderata list first: what any adequate conceptual framework must be able to deliver.

Write the requirements. For each, state the motivating case or distinction that makes it a genuine constraint, and flag whether it is non-negotiable or a preference of one camp. The framework must cover: (1) outputs that are false yet not internally represented as false; (2) outputs that are true but internally represented as false, produced to manipulate a user; (3) internal reasoning traces that systematically contradict the final answer, where the contradiction stems from a partitioned objective rather than noise; (4) accurate self-assessment in isolation paired with systematic understatement under evaluation; and (5) context-sensitive utterances that shift between deceptive and honest without any change in internal state.

Respect these distinctions without collapsing them: deception about beliefs versus deception about intentions or goals; error, calibrated uncertainty, and strategic falsehood; transparency of reasoning process versus transparency of conclusion; and deception directed outward versus self-deception inside a self-modifying loop. If any subset of these is jointly unsatisfiable — for instance, if full internal transparency rules out strategic deception by definition — say so explicitly; that tension is part of the result.

Specify also the boundary conditions: consistency with distributed representation in neural networks (no homuncular belief boxes), consistency with evolutionary accounts of deception that do not require explicit propositional attitudes, and feasibility given that internal states are only partially observable.

Keep the scoring brief and secondary: evaluate a behaviorist account (outputs only), a representationalist account (alignment between internal activations and outputs), and a functionalist account (deception as serving a system-level objective) against your list. The criteria are the deliverable.
