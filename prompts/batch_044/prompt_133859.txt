For each reading, indicate whether it is trivial, defensible, or false, and specify which ones actually license the conclusion that contradictory religious statements can be jointly true without violating the law of non-contradiction. Reach and justify a verdict about which interpretation, if any, makes the slogan do the argumentative work it was invoked to perform. Do not supply bibliographies, citations, or reading lists.
