I hold the view that hyperbolic discounting captures the mechanics of intertemporal preference reversal but cannot explain akrasia proper, because genuine weakness of will requires what I call diachronic normative binding: the rational authority retained by an agent's unrepudiated past evaluative judgment over present choice, even when present motivational profiles have shifted in ways that are not themselves irrational (for example, through fatigue, affective framing, or situational salience). On this account, akrasia is not a failure of hierarchical preference integration—there need be no conflict between first-order and second-order desires—but rather a specific rupture in normative binding caused by a steep present-biased valuation gradient that overwhelms the prior commitment without supplying any competing rational justification. What would genuinely count against this position? I want the arguments, empirical findings, conceptual distinctions, and possible cases that should move me, sorted from decisive to merely awkward. In particular: do empirical studies of intertemporal choice show that agents never treat prior commitments as rationally binding in the manner I describe, reducing all apparent binding to hidden higher-order preferences? Are there clear, well-described cases of akrasia in which no live prior evaluative judgment existed, undermining the claim that binding is necessary? Does my notion of diachronic normative binding collapse into a disguised hierarchical account, and if so, does that dissolve the distinction I draw? Could the view be qualified—say, by allowing that binding is always defeasible by any sufficiently strong present preference—into unfalsifiability, and if it has been so insulated, is that insulation a theoretical virtue or a vice? I also want an honest assessment of which defeaters a committed defender of this position should concede as genuinely damaging, which they could legitimately dismiss as question-begging or misdescribed, and whether the view ultimately risks collapsing into either a pure hyperbolic-discounting model or a standard hierarchical compatibilism. Please include what predictions or descriptions this position makes that its rivals do not, and identify the precise point at which further qualification would render the view empty.
