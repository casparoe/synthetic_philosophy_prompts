Avoid rehearsing generic Ship-of-Theseus intuitions; focus instead on why the absence of abrupt disruption in gradual replacement actually conceals a systematic substitution of causal origin. Write in a formal, working-paper register, and do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citation requests.
