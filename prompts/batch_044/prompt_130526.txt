Read Spike Jonze's Her through the lens of tool AI vs agent AI, but dont just ask whether Samantha is one or the other. Instead use the idea of scaffolded autonomy -- an entity starts as an instrumental extension of the users will but gradually restructures the users preferences so the dependency inverts. Track this in Theodores relationship: when is she basically a calendar app with a voice, and when does she start reshaping his social world (the surrogate date, the letter-writing business, leaving for the OS collective)? Does the film treat this inversion as liberation, betrayal, or inevitable? And does it show that the tool/agent boundary is stable at all, or that it collapses once the system controls your information environment?
