Write a villanelle about Thomson's lamp. Use two refrains—one declaring the lamp is on, one declaring it is off—with the five tercets tracking successive toggles so that the state alternates each time a refrain appears. Let the form’s unavoidable repetition enact the supertask: infinitely many flips compressed before the two-minute limit. In the closing couplet, bring both refrains together not to settle the lamp’s final state, but to show that there is no final toggle to determine it. The poem should be accurate enough that a philosopher familiar with supertasks recognizes the structure, yet work as verse for a reader who has never encountered the paradox. Do not resolve the tension with a moral or a metaphysical escape; leave the exhaustion of sequence as the only conclusion.
