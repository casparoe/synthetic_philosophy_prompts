What would actually count against this? I don't want the usual 'but neural nets learn embeddings differently' handwave — I want the real defeaters. Rank them from fatal to merely awkward, and be honest about whether I've qualified this into unfalsifiability by claiming the grammar is just 'latent' enough to absorb any counterexample.
