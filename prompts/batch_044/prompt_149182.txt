The board must determine whether the post-therapy individual retains the pre-therapy person's contractual obligations, property rights, criminal liabilities, and medical advance directives. Biological continuity is explicitly not required. Instead, the board scores the case across four axes: autobiographical coherence, predictive behavioral threading, relational recognition by close others, and normative commitment stability. Predictive behavioral threading receives double weight.
