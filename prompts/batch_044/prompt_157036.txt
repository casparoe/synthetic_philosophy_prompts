Please argue that the meta-problem of consciousness is best dissolved, rather than solved, by reconceiving phenomenal reports as modal indexicals anchored to a subject's cognitive architecture rather than as descriptions of intrinsic qualitative properties. Defend the claim that a report such as there being something it is like to experience red functions analogously to being here now: it indexes the speaker to a particular point in a space of possible experiential configurations without attributing a non-physical property to the experience. Address the objection that this move eliminates qualitative character rather than explaining it, and explain why treating phenomenal concepts as indexical does not entail eliminativism about first-person experience. Focus on conceptual and modal structure; do not appeal to empirical neuroscience, specific historical texts, or citation-based authority.
