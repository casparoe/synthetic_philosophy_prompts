(3) But "self-justifying" just means the belief justifies itself, which is circular reasoning with a loop of one. (4) So foundationalism collapses into a degenerate form of coherantism. (6) But then a perfectly consistent fairy-tale system is as justified as science, since coherence doesn't care about the world. (8) But a finite mind can never complete an infinite chain, so infinitism means nobody ever actually finishes justifying anything. (9) So on every view, either justification is circular, world-insensitive, or never achieved. (10) But I clearly know basic things, like that I'm tired right now. (11) Therefore the regress problem has no solution and skepticism is unavoidable, which can't be right.
