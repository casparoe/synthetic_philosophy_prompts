Question: In recent work on metaphysical grounding, theorists distinguish vertical grounding—where one fact obtains in virtue of another at a lower ontological level—from horizontal grounding, in which several facts at the same level jointly determine a further fact without hierarchical dependence. A further proposal, call it pure fundamentality, holds that a fact is fundamental only if it lacks both vertical and horizontal ground. Analyze this proposal with respect to quantum entanglement correlations: if the correlation between two spatially separated particles is horizontally grounded in the individual quantum states of those particles, does it follow that the individual states fail to be purely fundamental? Would adopting pure fundamentality push us toward monism about the entangled system? And should horizontal grounding be treated as a genuine threat to fundamentality at all, or as a distinct kind of determination?

Student answer: Vertical grounding is hierarchical: mental facts, for instance, are vertically grounded in neural facts. Horizontal grounding is non-hierarchical: the correlation between entangled particles is horizontally grounded in the states of particle A and particle B together, since neither state alone explains the correlation. The thesis of pure fundamentality requires that a fundamental fact have neither kind of ground. Applying this to entanglement, because the individual particle states serve as horizontal grounds for the correlation, they are not purely fundamental—each is implicated in grounding something else. This clearly supports monism about the entangled system, because if neither particle state is fundamental, then only the total entangled state can be. Schaffer rejects horizontal grounding altogether, arguing that all genuine grounding is vertical; therefore, on Schaffer’s view, the particle states remain fundamental, since no real horizontal determination is occurring. Finally, horizontal grounding avoids the problem of grounding without a grounding base, because the absence of hierarchy means there is no infinite downward chain that requires termination.
