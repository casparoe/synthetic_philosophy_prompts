Judy then applies the law of total probablity with her updated conditional credences, which reproduces the exact same unconditional distribution she would have gotten from standard conditioning. So adams hasn't solved anything; he's just hidden the update inside the conditional probabilities. We still need a non-bayesian revision rule.
