The scenario must satisfy the following design constraints. Finally, tell me how a defender of the substrate-lock principle would most likely attack the scenario, and whether that attack succeeds without begging the question. I ask for conceptual reasoning only; please do not provide bibliographies, reading lists, or citations, as I am working without access to external sources and wish to evaluate the argument on its own terms.
