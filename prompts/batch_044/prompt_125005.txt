Include at least one direct address to the listener emphasizing their privileged endpoint perspective, and one expert sidebar explaining why the Plague of Justinian should be read as stress-testing the resilience of emerging democratic infrastructures. Do not cite, quote, or reference specific modern scholars; paraphrase any allusion to ancient chroniclers without quotation marks. The humor should come from getting the historiographical distortion precisely right: a specialist should recognize the logic of progressivist history being pushed to its absurd conclusion, while a general reader should still understand the joke.
