which rule do you think he's actually running, what should he say about this hybrid for consistency, and what further case — say, an agent where the embedding drives actions but doesn't touch the world-model — would break the tie? also if his precedents genuinely underdetermine it, be honest about that and say what principle he'd be adopting with each answer.
