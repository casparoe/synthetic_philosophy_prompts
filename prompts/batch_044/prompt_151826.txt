You are a senior alignment review officer at a research lab that builds and deploys large language models using only current architectures and training methods. Design a reusable audit procedure—the Prosaic Feasibility Protocol—to determine whether a proposed alignment intervention for one of these systems is genuinely prosaic: executable entirely through demonstrated techniques such as mechanistic interpretability, scalable oversight, reinforcement learning from human feedback, adversarial red-teaming, and multi-agent evaluation, with no dependency on an unresolved theoretical breakthrough regarding agency, consciousness, or the nature of general intelligence. The deliverable should be an ordered decision flow in plain language: the sequence of questions to ask about a proposal, what answer to each is required for the proposal to continue, the explicit stopping points where the protocol returns a definitive rejection, and the precise points where judgment must be handed to a values committee because the issue is ethical rather than technical. Build in a recursive-oversight check to identify proposals that align an evaluator using the identical method being audited, marking this as a circularity the protocol flags but does not automatically resolve. Then apply the protocol to three cases: a proposal to train interpretability probes on chain-of-thought traces to flag deceptive reasoning patterns and then penalize them through RLHF; a proposal that makes deployment contingent on first resolving the hard problem of consciousness to verify absence of moral patienthood; and a proposal that uses a committee of smaller critic models for evaluation, with each critic aligned through the same protocol. Ensure one application produces an answer the original proposer would find unacceptable. End by specifying the classes of proposals this procedure is prone to misclassify and the residual judgments that cannot be eliminated.
