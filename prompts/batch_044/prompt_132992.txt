3. A domain-split rule: partition beliefs into empirical and structural domains, apply the Brier score to the former and a flat-prior logarithmic score to the latter, deliberately tolerating global incoherence if it improves local calibration.

Please rank these three norms by their adequacy for resolving the precision-asymmetry dilemma, assigning each a tier (S, A, B, C, or D) and providing a single sentence of justification for each placement.
