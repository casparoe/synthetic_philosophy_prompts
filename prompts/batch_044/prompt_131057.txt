Before I commit to any framework for analyzing how sustained reliance on generative AI reshapes human cognition, I want the specification first. Write the desiderata that any adequate philosophical account of epistemic dependence on AI — specifically where it produces an erosion of understanding rather than mere knowledge acquisition — must satisfy. For each requirement, state the motivating case or distinction: e.g., the transition from using a model as an external reference tool to treating it as a default reasoning partner; the gap between retaining a true belief and retaining the capacity to generate counterfactual explanations for it; the difference between instrumental delegation and structural cognitive outsourcing in hybrid human-AI belief formation; the asymmetry between understanding an output and being able to reconstruct the inferential path that produced it; and the boundary conditions for assigning epistemic responsibility when an opaque statistical process mediates testimony. Identify which desiderata are non-negotiable (for instance, consistency with established results in social epistemology on testimony and cognitive division of labor, or with empirical findings on the fragility of explanatory access under automation) and which reflect camp-specific preferences (such as demanding full preservation of pre-digital explanatory capacities, or insisting that any delegation to an opaque system necessarily vitiates understanding regardless of the user's meta-cognitive monitoring). Flag any pair or subset of requirements that appear jointly unsatisfiable — particularly if preserving robust individual understanding conflicts with accepting the efficiency gains of delegating complex inference to systems whose internal structure the user cannot represent. Keep the primary weight on the criteria and their motivating cases rather than on the evaluation. Then, briefly, indicate how an extended-mind approach, a corruption-of-testimony model, and a hybrid-cognitive-division view fare against the list, noting where each fails a non-negotiable desideratum or where it resolves an apparent tension.
