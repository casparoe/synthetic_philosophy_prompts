The essay question should require students to do three things: (1) diagnose whether the dispute is genuinely verbal, ontologically substantive, or pragmatically encroached; (2) evaluate whether conceptual engineering—adopting a precise institutional definition—resolves the conflict or merely relocates it into a normative debate about acquisition criteria; and (3) assess whether a deflationary metaontology (e.g., quantifier variance or easy ontology about art-kinds) can consistently support the claim that the engineered definition carries authoritative weight for museum policy.

Please provide: the essay question itself; a model answer of approximately 600 words; and a marking scheme out of 20 points that separates an A from a B answer by naming the specific moves a top answer must contain (e.g., distinguishing speaker-meaning indecision from semantic vagueness, recognizing that conceptual engineering can be ontologically neutral yet normatively loaded, identifying the tension between deflationism and institutional authority) and the specific mistakes that should cost marks. In your response, please also say how confident you are in the philosophical judgments of the model answer and single out the one point about which you are least sure.
