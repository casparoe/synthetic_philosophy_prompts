In an essay I read, evidential cooperation in large worlds is summarized this way: if the universe is large enough to contain innumerable copies of you, then every decision you face is simultaneously faced by those copies, and your choice reveals information about theirs. The author extends this to climate change: with astronomically many Earth-like planets, cutting my emissions is not merely symbolic; it is evidential cooperation with all those planetary analogues, so I should act as though I am in direct causal negotiation with them.
