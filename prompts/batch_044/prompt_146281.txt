Proponents argue that this captures how non-human forces actively restructure sovereignty, labor systems, and public health institutions; opponents warn that attributing agential status to germs and magma flows collapses into environmental determinism and erodes the moral accountability of human decision-makers, especially in episodes like epidemic governance or agricultural collapse.

Then compose the formal memo I will circulate before Thursday’s meeting. The memo should present a clear recommendation—approve, approve with conditions, or reject—together with a concrete implementation or revision time frame of no more than one academic term. It must treat the strongest opposing argument with intellectual honesty: specifically, the concern that granting “actor” status to volcanoes and microbes dissolves human moral responsibility into an undifferentiated causal web. Write in polished, institutional prose appropriate for an academic council, and do not include bibliographies, citations, or reading lists; the reasoning should stand entirely on its own conceptual and argumentative merits.
