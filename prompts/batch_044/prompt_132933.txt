draft the memo recommending either approval, rejection, or conditional approval with conditions and a concrete timeline — say a 6-month pilot with specific safeguards, or a 90-day moratorium. engage the looping/pre-looping worry seriously, don't just handwave it. and treat the strongest counter-argument fairly: if you land on approval, explain why the risk of misrecognition and patient exclusion outweighs the looping danger; if you reject, explain why the reflexive institutional risk is too high even if it means leaving some experiences unnamed. write it like an actual board document — clear recommendation up front, reasoning after, no bibliography needed, this isn't a literature review. just make it something we could circulate to clinicians and advocates without embarassment.
