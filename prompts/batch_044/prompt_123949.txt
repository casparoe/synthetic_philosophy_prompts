If the standard objection to ranking historical evils is that suffering is non-fungible across contexts—making any comparative metric morally obscene—doesn't that entail that we must treat every atrocity as exactly equal in moral gravity, since we have denied the very possibility of comparative judgment? And if philosophers who reject ranking still insist that genocide involves a distinctive moral wrong not present in lethal neglect or forced displacement, aren't they smuggling in an ordinal comparison of overall severity that their own anti-ranking position should forbid?
