I'm working through a seminar problem on gradual substrate replacement and have built an argument that seems to undermine personal identity altogether, but I can't locate the precise misstep. Here is the chain: (1) If a system's token-level causal dynamics are preserved across a change, then the subject of those dynamics survives numerically. (2) Gradual neuron-by-neuron replacement with silicon equivalents preserves those dynamics at every intermediate stage. (3) So the fully uploaded digital mind is identical to the original biological person. (4) Now consider a branching protocol: at each step the replaced neuron is duplicated, with one copy retained in the biological stream B and one integrated into the digital stream D, so that both B and D trace continuous functional histories back to the original. (5) Applying (1)-(3) to B yields B is identical to the original; applying them to D yields D is identical to the original. (6) By transitivity, B and D are identical to each other. (7) But B and D can immediately diverge—B might believe "I am embodied" while D believes "I run on a server"—and numerically identical entities cannot hold contradictory beliefs. (8) So one of (1)-(6) fails. (9) The branching in (4) is just (2) executed twice in parallel; denying D's survival requires saying duplication breaks continuity, yet (1) defines continuity in purely functional terms that both streams satisfy. (10) Hence (1) is false: functional preservation is insufficient for identity. (11) But biological aging involves the same gradual functional turnover—neurons are naturally replaced—so if (1) fails for silicon it fails for biology too. (12) Therefore no one persists through ordinary life, which is absurd. I would strongly prefer to preserve (1) and (11), but I feel a slide between (4) and (6) that I can't name—perhaps an equivocation between token-continuity and type-matching, or a hidden scope shift in the induction. Which step should be rejected, and what does giving it up cost for anyone who wants to allow non-destructive uploading?
