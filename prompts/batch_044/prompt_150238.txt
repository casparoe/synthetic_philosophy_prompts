As a novelist attempting to construct a narrative composed entirely of standardized pictograms, I have been examining Otto Neurath's ISOTYPE principles. I understand that Neurath maintained that visual symbols could function as direct, language-independent representations of empirical states—essentially picturing reality in the manner described in the Tractatus—thereby eliminating interpretive ambiguity. Would it follow that a novel built solely from ISOTYPE-style icons could transmit precise scientific or moral truths without any verbal mediation, or does Neurath's framework implicitly require linguistic context to stabilize meaning?
