Build your case conceptually rather than empirically; you need not cite statutes, court cases, or named scholars. Address the standard Lockean labor-mixing and utilitarian incentive defenses, but show that both presuppose a model of creation as discrete labor on separable artifacts—a model that breaks down when the “object” is a grammar that only realizes its utility as shared infrastructure. Keep the tone rigorous yet accessible to an educated general reader. Finish with a single line beginning exactly "Verdict:" followed by your distilled thesis in one sentence.
