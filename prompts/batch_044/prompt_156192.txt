I'm designing the final examination for a graduate seminar on hierarchical explanation, self-reference, and the architecture of formal systems. I want one 90-minute essay question that deploys Hofstadter's distinction between tangled hierarchies and an inviolate level but requires students to move far beyond summarizing his standard account. The question should present a hypothetical formal system, call it S, that contains a complete syntactic model of itself at every meta-level with no terminating base case, so that each level functions simultaneously as object-language and meta-language for the level immediately beneath it. Students should be asked to determine, first, whether S exemplifies a tangled hierarchy or instead violates an inviolate level; second, whether the notion of an inviolate level retains conceptual coherence once any proposed base can be internalized as an object-level representation within S; and third, whether the normative claim that explanation must be hierarchically ordered to avoid vicious circularity can be sustained independently of the descriptive claim that actual cognitive or formal architectures happen to be stratified. The question must require students to assess whether Hofstadter's use of Escher's Drawing Hands serves as a genuine argument for the necessity of a base level or merely as an illustration of descriptive looping, and to conclude whether circularity in self-modeling is necessarily vicious or can be benign. Please provide the exam question itself, a model answer of approximately 700 words, and a marking scheme totaling 20 marks. The scheme should identify five specific conceptual moves that distinguish an A-range answer (18-20) from a B-range answer (14-16): precise differentiation between descriptive and normative hierarchy; analysis of whether complete self-modeling eliminates a base case or merely relocates it; evaluation of the coherence of an inviolate level under total internalization; critical use of the Drawing Hands analogy to expose rather than resolve the tension; and a reasoned judgment on whether the circularity in S is vicious or benign, with explicit criteria. For each move, note the typical failure mode—such as conflating loop with contradiction, treating the base level as empirically given rather than conceptually required, or assuming that self-reference automatically undermines explanation—that should result in lost marks.
