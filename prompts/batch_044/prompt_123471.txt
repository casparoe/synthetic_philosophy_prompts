My thesis is not a standard Ranke-versus-Carr lecture. This addresses Carr's charge that empiricism ignores the historian's framing, because selection is disciplined by the archive's own patterns of silence, and it addresses White's charge that emplotment is unconstrained, because these omissions are structurally caused by colonial administrative priorities rather than by rhetorical choice.
