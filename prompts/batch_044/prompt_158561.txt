I've been running this small community repair cafe — you know, where people bring broken toasters and bikes and volunteers fix them — and there's this one guy, Devon, who's been the unofficial leader since we started back in 2019. He books the space, trains new people, decides which tools we buy. Lately he's pushing this idea that we should stop tracking repairs in our shared log and only recieve cash donations, which a lot of us think is going to ruin the cooperative vibe because transparency was kind of our whole deal.

Some members say we should just vote Devon out and everything goes back to normal, but others think the problem is deeper — our founding rules never actually said who gets to make those calls, and there's even this weird tie-breaker clause that gives the longest-serving member final say if people split, which is exactly how Devon pushed the cash thing through. I was arguing with my cousin over text and she said it sounds like that history question about whether you need one specific bad leader to mess up a country, or if the country was already organized in a way that lets someone like that take over.

I don't know the fancy terms, but I'm stuck: if Devon gets replaced tomorrow, are we definately saving the repair cafe, or are we just waiting for the next person to slide into the same hole? And how do you tell whether the mess is really about Devon's specific choices — like his weird theory that people value repairs more when they pay cash — or about the way our group is structured so that anyone in that spot would eventually do something similar?
