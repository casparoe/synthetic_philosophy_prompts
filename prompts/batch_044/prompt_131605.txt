According to her, von Neumann's argument is not straightforward technological optimism but a conditional claim—technology survives only if social institutions develop a second-order capacity to revise their own decision rules at roughly the same rate as first-order technological change—and she argues that this condition is implicit in his work on self-replicating automata and on game-theoretic rationality. I want a sequence of eight to ten interview questions that go well beyond asking her to restate the view. Another question should address what I will call the margin of institutional ignorance—if institutions must revise faster than they can accumulate reliable evidence about the technologies they are regulating, is she describing a genuinely rational form of decision-making under radical uncertainty, or is she merely relabeling reactive crisis management?
