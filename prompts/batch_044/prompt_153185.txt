The reason is that surprise is just a mental state — they didn't actually feel like they knew it was Wednesday, so the condition for surprise is satisfied regardless of logic. The common knowledge solution is a red herring because even if every student knows that every other student knows the schedule, they can still be surprised if they don't actively think about the deduction. This is basically the same structure as the lottery paradox, except instead of lottery tickets it's days of the week: you know one will happen but you can't rationally believe it's any particular one. So the teacher's announcement is actually a performative contradiction, like saying 'this statement is false,' because she claims they won't be able to deduce the day but then creates a situation where deduction should be possible.
