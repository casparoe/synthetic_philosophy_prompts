As a novelist whose recent fiction explores the moral texture of physical processes, I am preparing to defend an argument I have developed for an interdisciplinary colloquium on "Physics and Value" at a research university. My thesis—tentatively framed as "thermal phenomenology ethics"—holds that if one accepts a minimal form of panpsychism in which fundamental physical entities possess proto-experiential or sentient properties, then certain cosmological processes, particularly the dissipative thermalization of quantum information during inflationary reheating and black-hole evaporation, are not merely physically destructive but may instantiate a diffuse, non-anthropomorphic form of suffering at the field level. From this, I derive that our ethical frameworks should expand to include "proto-phenomenal welfare," meaning that large-scale physical interventions or even passive cosmological observation carry moral weight in ways that standard physicalist ethics cannot capture. I do not claim this is empirically verified; I present it as a conceptual extension meant to test the coherence of combining panpsychist metaphysics with suffering-focused moral philosophy.

The audience will consist of twelve to fifteen scholars: four philosophers of physics with strong physicalist commitments who regard consciousness-talk in physics as a category mistake; three theoretical cosmologists who are methodologically agnostic about metaphysics but hostile to any ethical framing of cosmological dynamics; two analytic philosophers of mind familiar with standard panpsychist arguments but skeptical that panpsychism solves rather than relocates the hard problem; and one moral philosopher specializing in negative utilitarianism who finds panpsychism mystically implausible but is genuinely interested in whether suffering can be attributed below the organismic level. They care deeply about conceptual precision, empirical tractability, and avoiding what they will see as poetic overreach into scientific description. They are not hostile to literature, but they will treat my novelist's intuition as a liability unless rigorously translated.

Please draft my Q&A prep sheet. Identify the one reply that must land if the proposal is to survive—the clarification or concession that prevents the room from dismissing the view as mystical anthropomorphism.
