The satire should land on the real problem that radical non-absolutism can slide into bureaucratic paralysis, but never say that outright — just show it through the guidebook cheerfulness. Keep it readable to someone who hasn't studied Jain logic but fair to someone who has. Write it in British English, please — and yes, there's a typo or two in this prompt, don't correct them.
