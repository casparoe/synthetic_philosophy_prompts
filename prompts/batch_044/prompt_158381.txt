Write a short story about a being—call it the Archivist—who has perfect middle knowledge but zero free knowledge. It knows every counterfactual: if Maria had been born in Lisbon instead of Porto, she’d still have become a poet; if the rain hadn’t stopped on Tuesday, the treaty would have failed. But it has no idea which of these worlds is the actual one, and it cant look ahead. The Archivist lives in a library where each book is a complete counterfactual life, shelved alphabeticly by circumstance. One day it finds a book with it's own name in the index—something that shouldnt exist, since its own actions arent free, theyre just... indexed. The story should explore what it feels like to understand every 'would' without knowing any 'will,' and whether that kind of knowledge is closer to wisdom or to a very beautifull prison. Don't just rehash Molina—make the Archivist's crisis specific: maybe it starts writing new counterfactuals that include itself observing them, and the library starts looping.
