Each leaf should carry the name of an actual position—Schopenhauer’s orthodox view, a formalist reduction, a psychoanalytic intermediary, a social-constructivist denial—or be labeled unnamed, with one sentence on its most awkward commitment. Identify at least one unoccupied combination in the logical space and diagnose why it remains empty, and flag any pair of leaves that your tree cannot genuinely separate. Keep the structure diagnostic rather than expository: each node must be a question answerable without knowing the interpretation it leads to.
