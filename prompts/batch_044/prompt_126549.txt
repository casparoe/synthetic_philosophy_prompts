I work in neuromorphic engineering and have been thinking about gradual mind-uploading scenarios. Suppose a person's biological neurons are replaced one at a time with silicon equivalents that replicate every synaptic weight and firing pattern, so that global workspace dynamics and reported first-person experience remain continuous across the entire transition. Given this uninterrupted functional and phenomenal continuity, hasn't the scenario already shown empirically that consciousness is purely organizational, which means the hard problem of why any substrate has subjective experience is dissolved rather than solved? And if the resulting system preserves all memory and behavioral dispositions, what non-arbitrary reason remains for saying the original person was destroyed rather than incrementally upgraded—other than an implicit commitment to a non-physical soul? Please take a committed position with clear reasons: is the anxiety about dying during gradual upload a coherent metaphysical worry, or is it a category error that mistakes biological continuity for personal identity?
