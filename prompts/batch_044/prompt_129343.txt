Do these hang together? Rank the real tensions by severity, flag any pair that only looks incompatible but dissolves on closer inspection, and for the worst conflict state the least costly revision. Then tell me which single commitment I should abandon to clean up the package with the least damage. Keep your response under 300 words, ordered from worst tension downward, and audit my specific set rather than giving a general lecture.
