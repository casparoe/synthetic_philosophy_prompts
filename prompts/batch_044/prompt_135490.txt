For decisions that require explicit value-ranking, she claims the gap collapses or reverses: prefrontal deliberative signatures and the motor readiness potential emerge concurrently rather than sequentially. She uses this to block the standard epiphenomenalist reading of Libet without endorsing libertarian free will, arguing instead that conscious deliberation is temporally coextensive with neural initiation but remains causally downstream. Please draft eight to ten interview questions that press the actual mechanics of this position, not generic biography or overview. For each question, include a sharp follow-up keyed to the reply she is most likely to give—for instance, that value-ranking activates dorsolateral prefrontal regions earlier than primary motor cortex, or that habitual choices recruit basal ganglia loops that bypass the prefrontal concurrency she describes. Begin with the questions themselves, with no preface or commentary.
