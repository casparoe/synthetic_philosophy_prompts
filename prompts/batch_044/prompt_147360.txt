I want a question on expected-value fanaticism that cannot be answered by summarizing standard St. Petersburg-style objections or familiar existential-risk arguments. Please provide: (1) the exam question itself; (2) a model answer of about 700 words; and (3) a marking scheme listing five specific analytical moves that a top answer must contain, with point allocations (summing to 20) and a brief note on the characteristic error that costs marks for each.
