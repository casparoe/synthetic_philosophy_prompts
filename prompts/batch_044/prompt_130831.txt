I'm working through the historiography of 1789 for an advanced seminar, and I've become fixated on what seems like a logical tension between the major interpretive camps. The classical Marxist reading treats the Revolution as the necessary expression of ascending bourgeois class interests dismantling feudal property relations; in that framework, the subsequent radicalization and the Terror appear as structurally functional episodes—phases in which the bourgeoisie had to liquidate both aristocratic resistance and popular excess in order to consolidate a capitalist order. By contrast, the revisionist account—associated with historians who emphasize political culture and discursive dynamics—treats the Revolution as a contingent ideological escalation in which revolutionary rhetoric itself generated the machinery of terror, with class position serving more as post-hoc justification than as causal engine. Now, the familiar claim that revolutions devour their children seems to sit awkwardly between these two explanations. If the Marxist structural story is correct, then such self-destruction should be law-like: any revolution that successfully overturns feudal relations must, by structural necessity, purge its radical wing. But if the revisionist story is correct, then the purging was essentially accidental—a byproduct of specific discursive choices that could have unfolded otherwise. So my core question is this: shouldn't resolving the historiographical dispute between class-struggle and ideological-contingency explanations also settle whether revolutionary self-destruction describes a genuine historical regularity or merely a contingent correlation? Or does the status of that claim depend on a different kind of reasoning altogether—perhaps one that neither framework actually addresses? I want to understand whether my assumption, that identifying the correct causal mechanism automatically determines the modal status of revolutionary self-destruction, is itself a category mistake. Please correct me plainly if the premise is confused, and don't try to salvage it if it can't be done well.
