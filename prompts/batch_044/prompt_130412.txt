I'm preparing a philosophical profile on mental illness and social ontology for a small reading group, and I want an internal consistency audit before I present. Here are the commitments I actually hold:

(1) Hacking's looping effects are real and robust: applying psychiatric classifications to people often changes those people in response, so the classified kind is interactive rather than indifferent.

(2) Major DSM categories—say, autism spectrum disorder and borderline personality disorder—are best understood as interactive kinds whose boundaries shift with classification practices, not as natural kinds waiting to be discovered.

(3) Even so, there is a mind-independent fact about whether a given individual is undergoing severe psychological suffering that merits clinical intervention; the reality of that suffering is not constituted by the classification itself.

(4) The recent expansion of certain diagnostic categories (for instance, the broadening of autism criteria across DSM revisions) is driven primarily by socio-political negotiation, institutional incentives, and looping dynamics, rather than by improved empirical tracking of a stable biological substrate.

(5) At the same time, evolutionary psychiatry is correct in holding that some symptom clusters—e.g., major depression and anxiety responses—recur cross-culturally because they are anchored in evolved psychological mechanisms, which gives those clusters a kind of natural-kind stability that looping dynamics alone cannot explain.

Do these hang together, or am I helping myself to incompatible motivations? Specifically: does (3) survive (1) and (2) without collapsing into a hidden essentialism about suffering? Does (4) conflict with (5), or can looping and evolutionary anchoring be reconciled for the same disorder? And does my acceptance of (5) undermine the claim in (2) that DSM categories are not natural kinds?

Please audit these commitments in American English. Rank the tensions by severity, identify any pair that looks tense but actually dissolves on closer inspection, and for each genuine conflict say which single commitment is the least costly to revise and what that revision costs me conceptually. Deliver the result as an ordered audit rather than a lecture on any one view.
