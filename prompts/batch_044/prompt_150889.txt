2. same robbery, but they have plenty of cash and just want the thrill. determinism still holds. verdict: blame is totally appropriate.

6. an addict pushed into addiction by trauma then deterministically steals for drugs. they still have some reflective capacity, just weak. verdict: blame is mostly out, but not exactly zero.
