The first replied that this is only a difference in proof strength, not ontology: classical mathematics is willing to prove existence non-constructively, but the objects referred to are identical, merely accessed through different epistemic routes. The first countered that by this standard theoretical physics is filled with unobservable entities, yet we do not reject quantum mechanics for referring to particles that evade direct observation. The second responded that physical theoretical entities are embedded in predictive frameworks that eventually cash out observationally, whereas a non-constructive existence proof offers no analogous path to identification, making the comparison misleading.
