The teleological suspension of the ethical means that for Abraham, the universal moral law—like don't kill innocent people—is put on hold because he has a direct, private command from God. The ethical is the universal stage where you can explain yourself to others and be understood, but Abraham is in the religious stage where the individual stands in an absolute relation to the absolute.
