Leo replied that Maya confuses access consciousness with phenomenal consciousness. illusionism only denies raw feels (qualia), not self-monitoring or reportability. what's real is the introspective machinery that builds a false model of mental states as having intrinsic "what-it's-likeness." that machinery gets misrepresented, so its genuinely an illusion, not absence—like seeing a bent stick.
