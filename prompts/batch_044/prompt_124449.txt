They have asked me whether the upcoming public exhibition and funding priorities should center on replaceable leaders and recent policy errors, or on deep geographic and climatic rhythms that no individual administrator can easily reverse. Please respond with exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences long, with no introduction or conclusion, advising which framing is more intellectually defensible and what practical difference the choice makes for the institution.
