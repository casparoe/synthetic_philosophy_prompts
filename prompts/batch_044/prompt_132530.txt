The target of the satire is extreme presentism in historiography: the position that historians can and should escape the standards of their own time not by understanding past societies on their own terms, but by imposing a single, unified set of contemporary ethical norms upon every period studied, and treating any deviation from this practice as a failure of objectivity.

To prevent the piece from merely rehearsing familiar objections, you must incorporate two invented methodological instruments into the rubric's language. First, the "Temporal Bias Coefficient" (TBC), a calculated score that penalises applicants for "contextual contamination" — that is, for attempting to reconstruct the normative frameworks of historical actors rather than judging those actors by present-day criteria. Second, the "Epochal Conformity Score" (ECS), which rewards projects that apply identical moral and legal terminology across all historical periods, regardless of anachronism. The humour should emerge from the rubric's relentless internal logic: for example, a study of medieval guild regulations should lose marks if it explains guild members' own justifications for their rules, but gain marks if it condemns those rules using modern employment-law vocabulary; similarly, an applicant proposing to examine Victorian attitudes towards childhood should be praised for "normative consistency" only if the analysis never acknowledges that Victorian conceptions of childhood differed from current ones.

In the FAQ appendix, include answers attributed to an invented senior assessor, Dr. E. P. Chronos. The answers should expose the contradictions inherent in the extreme presentist stance without breaking the deadpan tone. For instance, when asked whether the Protocol itself — drafted in 2024 and reflecting contemporary values — is guilty of presentism, Dr. Chronos should reply that it is exempt because its norms are "timeless", even while insisting that all other historical documents must be evaluated solely by those same 2024 norms. Do not explicitly tell the reader that presentism is problematic; let the absurdity arise entirely from the document's provisions.

Write in British English, maintain a consistently formal register, and avoid any references to real bibliographies, citation lists, or specific scholarly works that an offline model could not reliably recall. Do not include quotation marks around any real textual sources; if you wish to include a sample applicant response, invent it and present it in italics or as a block quotation without attributing it to a real author.
