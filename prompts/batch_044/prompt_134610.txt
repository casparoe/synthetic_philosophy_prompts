I was recently diagnosed with a persistent post-viral condition and am now weighing whether to enroll in a conventional clinical trial or to try an alternative therapeutic protocol whose advocates explain away every adverse outcome as an individual healing crisis. In defending their framework, they invoke Karl Popper’s well-known image of science as a building erected on piles driven into a swamp rather than anchored to bedrock: they claim that because science lacks absolute foundations, demanding risky, falsifiable predictions is merely one arbitrary choice of piling, and that their own approach therefore stands on equal epistemic footing with mainstream medicine—just different piles in the same swamp. Audit this analogy with care. First, provide an explicit correspondence table mapping the building, the piles, the swamp, the absence of bedrock, and the act of replacing piles onto elements of scientific practice, theory testing, and the specific protocol I am considering. Next, identify precisely where the mapping fractures: which features of Popper’s original image—especially the asymmetry between corroboration and falsification, the role of critical testing as a selection mechanism, and the idea that piles must reach a workable depth—are lost or inverted when the analogy is used to license epistemic relativism. Note any properties the analogy quietly imports about the stability of structures built on soft ground, the costlessness of rebuilding, and the interchangeability of foundations, and examine whether those imports actually apply to medical decision-making under conditions of personal risk and uncertainty. Then assess whether the advocates’ conclusion—that the demarcation between science and pseudoscience collapses once we accept there is no bedrock—survives these disanalogies, or whether the swamp image was doing all the argumentative work. If the analogy fails to support their relativist conclusion, suggest what a more accurate analogy for the epistemic difference between falsifiable clinical trials and unfalsifiable therapeutic frameworks would be, and explain what work that replacement analogy does that the swamp image cannot.
