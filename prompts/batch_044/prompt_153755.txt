Please order the objections by how damaging they are and how likely they are to come from this specific mix, phrase each in the vocabulary its subgroup would actually use, and for each provide a concise two-minute reply, a clear note on where that reply is weakest or soft, and an indication of what I should concede upfront rather than defend. Throughout, please separate what is settled in the specialist literature from what remains genuinely contested among people who work on this topic, and do not present any contested claim—especially about the compatibility of presentism with relativity—as though it were the consensus view.
