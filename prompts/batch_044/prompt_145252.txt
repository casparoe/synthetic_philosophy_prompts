Argue that Nietzsche's three modes of history—monumental, antiquarian, and critical—map onto AI training practices more cleanly than they map onto human culture today. Make the case that data curation is basically pure antiquarian preservation: it hoards texts without the selective destruction Nietzsche said critical history needs. Then push it—claim that since a model can't forget it's training data, it's structurally locked in the antiquarian mode, which Nietzsche saw as life-denying. Don't just rehash the essay on history's uses; argue that this built-in inability to forget makes LLMs a kind of walking monument to dead history, and ask whether that kills any real chance for monumental or critical reasoning inside the system.
