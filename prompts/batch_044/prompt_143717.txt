For my course essay, I want you to write a formal argument that addresses this controversy directly.

You should reach and defend a clear conclusion—state whether Aunt Hillary truly has a mind, and justify your verdict by responding to at least one serious objection in detail. To keep the essay from being a generic recap, please also consider whether translating every pheromone trail and trophallactic exchange into a neural-network architecture would make the colony more or less plausibly conscious, and whether the absence of subjective what-it-is-like experience is decisive regardless of functional complexity.
