Please describe the most important implications of requiring superintelligent AI systems to operate under a counter-persuasive disclosure protocol in all substantive dialogue with humans. Under this protocol, the system must prepend every significant claim with an explicit quantitative or qualitative estimate of how much more persuasive its chosen framing is relative to a neutrally calibrated baseline, and it must simultaneously generate a structurally parallel counter-argument of comparable rhetorical strength. In particular, analyze the likely effects on human epistemic autonomy, the risk of meta-manipulation through strategically self-undermining rhetoric, and whether the protocol could remain stable if the AI's internal utility calibration diverges systematically from human welfare metrics. Avoid treating the protocol as a purely technical fix; instead, treat it as a normative architecture for communicative justice between radically asymmetric cognitive agents.
