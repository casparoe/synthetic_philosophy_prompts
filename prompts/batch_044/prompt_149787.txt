If we engineer away the capacity for that signal—say, by suppressing nociceptive development or installing a permanent neural floor beneath which affect cannot fall—we might eliminate pain, but we might also eliminate the mechanism through which organisms discover danger, injustice, or what genuinely deserves protection.
