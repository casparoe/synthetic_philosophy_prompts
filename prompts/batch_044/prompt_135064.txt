Another split: is the end goal social utility, personal salvation, or just habit automation that doesn't need either? For each leaf, name the stance if anyone holds it (Scottish common-sense pragmatist, virtue-automationist, etc.) or label it unnamed. Flag empty combos — say, arbitrary ordering plus errata-integrated plus belief the system is complete — and note if that's incoherent or just unexplored.
