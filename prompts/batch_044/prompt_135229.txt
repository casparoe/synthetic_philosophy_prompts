I'm writing an essay for a seminar on Schopenhauer's aesthetics, and I've built an argument that seems to undermine his own distinction between music and the other arts. Here are the steps.

(1) For Schopenhauer, painting, sculpture, and poetry copy Platonic Ideas—intermediate grades in which the unitary will objectifies itself—whereas music copies the will directly, without mediation through any Idea.

(2) The will in itself is one, blind, and internally undifferentiated; all plurality and grading arise only through its objectification in space and time.

(3) So if music expresses the will immediately, bypassing the Ideas that supply differentiation, then every musical work expresses precisely the same thing: the identical, undifferentiated totality of the will.

(4) Yet Schopenhauer describes bass lines as analogues to inorganic nature, melody to human consciousness, and rhythm to the form of time, and he ranks some compositions as deeper revelations of the will's essence than others.

(5) If every work expresses the same metaphysical content, these internal distinctions are merely acoustic differences in volume or tempo, and no piece can be aesthetically deeper or more profound than another.

(6) That collapses the normative heart of his aesthetics, which treats music as the highest art precisely because it reveals the will with varying degrees of depth and clarity.

(7) Therefore either music is not a direct copy of the will, or Schopenhauer's aesthetic judgments have no metaphysical basis.

I want to keep (1) and (6), since they seem central to his view. Where does the slip occur—perhaps in (3), through an equivocation between expressing "the will" and expressing "the will at a particular grade of objectification"? Or is there a scope shift in moving from the will's metaphysical unity to the claim that its direct expression must be uniform? Please identify the weakest step, explain why it looked plausible, and price the cost of rejecting it. In your answer, separate what is settled in Schopenhauer scholarship from what is genuinely contested, and do not present a disputed interpretive reading as if it were the consensus.
