Here is the specific twist I want you to build in, since I think it makes the problem visceral: divide the group into triads and give each triad a cooperative physical task—say, constructing a stable arch from blocks—under the constraint that no block may be moved until the group plays a physical "authorization token" that names the rule permitting that move. Include clear rules for turn order, a collective success/failure payoff scheme using only points and group recognition rather than real money, assigned roles within each triad (mover, token-keeper, auditor), and a timeline that fits our window.
