Every node must be phrased as a concrete claim or case that a student can answer without already knowing which leaf it leads to; do not ask them to identify positions by label alone. The tree should separate at least four distinct positions, and you should explicitly flag any pair of leaves that the branching questions cannot genuinely distinguish.

Please present the taxonomy with branch nodes clearly labeled as questions, sibling branches showing the answer options, and leaves carrying the position description and its awkward commitment. The audience is graduate students familiar with basic philosophy of science but not specialists in medieval Islamic philosophy, so keep technical references to occasionalism and interventionism at an accessible level. At the very end, provide a one-line machine-readable summary of the form \answer{<number of leaves>, <number of unoccupied nodes flagged>, <any pair that cannot be separated>}.
