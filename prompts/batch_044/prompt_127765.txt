The distinctions it cant collapse -- authority versus welfare, binding versus advising, expressing values versus predicting preferences. The legal and clinical findings it cant flat-out contradict. Then briefly score three standard views -- the autonomy-first view, substituted judgment, and the 'no authority across conceptual rupture' position -- against your list. Keep the weight on the criteria and why each is a genuine requirement, not on the scorecard. No bibliography, just the reasoning.
