Mara asked me what philosophy they're smuggling in, because it feels like they either believe there's a hidden sharp cutoff they just can't see — like that Williamson idea about vagueness being ignorance? — or they're faking a line that doesn't exist and calling it administration. Does that inconsistency damage one philosophical view more than the other, or does it just show they're corrupt?
