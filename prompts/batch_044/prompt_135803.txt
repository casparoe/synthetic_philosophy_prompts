It made me think about that moment in 1995 when Mandela walked out onto the pitch at Ellis Park wearing that Springbok jersey, right before Francois Pienaar lifted the Webb Ellis Cup. I want you to analyse that specific episode through the lens of relational conceptions of personhood, but properly — not just saying it was about reconciliation. Be precise: who are the 'persons' in this relational frame — Mandela, Pienaar, the Afrikaner crowd, the black spectators — and how does their standing or identity actually shift through the interaction? What does the theory explain about why the moment carried such emotional weight, and where does it fall silent or get stretched? Does that break the Ubuntu reading, or does it show that strategic relation is still relation?
