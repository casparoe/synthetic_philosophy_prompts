I hold that Smith's impartial spectator is best reconstructed as an anticipatory affective model—closer to a predictive-processing mechanism than to an internalised moral authority. On this reading, sympathy is not primarily other-directed feeling but a calibration of one's emotional forecasts against an imagined audience, while self-command collapses into error-minimisation within that loop. What would genuinely count against this interpretation? Would committed defenders concede any of these as biting, and does the view risk being qualified into unfalsifiability—if every counter-case can be redescribed as miscalibration—such that this insulation is itself a problem?
