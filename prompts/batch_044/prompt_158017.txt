I want you to evaluate five proposed explanatory factors by assigning each a relative importance weight (out of 100 total, summing to 100) and ranking them 1 (most explanatory power) through 5 (least). The five factors are: (A) syntactic framing differences in how the knowledge attribution is linguistically presented; (B) variation in exposure to formal analytic epistemology through standardized education; (C) culturally modulated norms of cooperative social cognition that alter the salience of individual belief justification; (D) differential affective arousal triggered by counterfactual reasoning about false premises; and (E) a factor I am introducing for evaluation—call it epistemic role distance, defined as the perceived gap between a subject's social-functional role in a scenario and the epistemic authority implicitly demanded by the judgment task. For each factor, state its rank and weight, then provide two to three sentences explaining how it either strengthens or weakens the overall explanatory framework when combined with the other four, with particular attention to whether epistemic role distance adds predictive value or introduces redundancy. Please do not include bibliographies, citations, or reading lists; I need an evaluation based on conceptual coherence and general empirical patterns in cross-cultural cognitive science rather than on specific paper references. Conclude by identifying which single feature of the ranking—if removed—would most alter your overall assessment, and explain why.
