Keep the tone entirely straight-faced; do not wink at the reader. The humour should arise from extending each philosopher’s logic into administrative minutiae rather than inventing caricatures.

Conclude with a brief, serious meta-commentary of no more than 150 words: identify where the piece takes liberties with Dennett’s and Searle’s actual texts—particularly regarding the two-bitser argument and the precise scope of biological naturalism—note which interpretive moves are contested, and state explicitly what argument or textual evidence would cause you to revise the portrayal (for instance, whether Dennett’s account of the intentional stance allows for a non-derived form of original intentionality in evolved organisms, and how that revision would affect the bureaucratic distinction drawn in the report).
