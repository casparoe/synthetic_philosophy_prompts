I am preparing an interview for a philosophy podcast with an unnamed philosopher who has spent the last decade defending a specific reply to consequentialist cluelessness. She has recently extended this framework to collective action, arguing that groups possess collective horizons that need not align with individual ones, and she has addressed structural ignorance about the ontology of future welfare—whether future beneficiaries will be biological descendants, artificial agents, or hybrid forms—claiming that her framework can guide present action even when we cannot identify the ontology of those affected, provided we do not mistake ontological uncertainty for a reason to discount them.
