I'm trying to determine what I actually think about rational self-trust in reasoning agents, working from my judgments on specific cases. A pocket calculator that repeats its own arithmetic and accepts agreement: rational self-trust. A chess engine that audits its evaluation weights against externally computed endgame tablebases and revises them: rational, because the check has an external anchor. A formal system powerful enough for arithmetic that adopts the axiom "all theorems I derive are true": irrational—this is the classic Löbian obstacle, and consistency collapses. A human mathematician who trusts her informal intuition about primes and proofs, knowing Gödel's results apply only to fully formalized systems: rational, since her cognition is not the formal object in question. A large model with full access to its own weights that runs every inference through an internal consistency loop with no external database query: irrational, not by contradiction but by circularity—like pulling oneself up by one's own bootstraps. A distributed scientific community that verifies claims across independent laboratories: rational, and structurally different from solitary self-trust. The pair I cannot reconcile easily: a resource-bounded agent powerful enough for arithmetic applies rigorous local verification to each reasoning step, treats its verification module as just another step subject to the same local check, and deliberately refuses any global consistency claim—this avoids both Löbian collapse and bootstrapping circularity, yet I'm torn whether this is genuine self-trust or an infinite regress that dissolves the self. Meanwhile, I judge that same agent irrational if it once makes a single global statement like "my local checks guarantee my overall reliability." Is there a precise principle—distinguishing scope of verification, presence of an external anchor, and procedural versus global self-reference—that captures all my verdicts, or must I drop either the judgment on the local-step agent or the judgment on the global-claim agent?
