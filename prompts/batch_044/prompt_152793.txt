Still, I keep running into confusion about what that directness actually requires in practice. This struck me as an odd test case for Schopenhauer’s view. If he is right that music expresses the Will immediately, might this infrasonic piece be an especially pure form of music because it bypasses conceptual hearing entirely and acts on bodily will without mediation? Or does it fail to count as music at all, because without audible organization there is nothing that mirrors the inner structure and movement of willing?
