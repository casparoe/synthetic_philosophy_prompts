Write an essay addressing the following controversy: when assessing distributive justice in the allocation of AI training resources across linguistic and cultural communities, should equality be measured in terms of representational resources (proportional data inclusion), welfare (uniform predictive performance across groups), or capabilities (functionally equivalent user agency in diverse contexts)? Weigh the principal arguments for each metric, but do not conclude with a non-committal balance; defend a specific verdict. In your analysis, address what might be called the capability translation problem: the structural mismatch that occurs when equal data resourcing fails to produce equal welfare outcomes because different communities must expend unequal interpretive labor to adapt standardized model outputs to their local epistemic practices. Your conclusion should justify which equality metric—resources, welfare, or capabilities—best accommodates or resolves this translation gap, and whether resolving it requires altering the training allocation itself or the downstream interface through which outputs are delivered.
