Since I began designing evaluation protocols for recursively structured language models, I have kept returning to Hofstadter's tripartite distinction among frame messages, outer messages, and inner messages in Gödel, Escher, Bach. I am now trying to formalize an adversarial collaboration between two colleagues—one who holds that in any self-referential system the frame message is constitutive of meaning (the Frame-Primacy camp), and one who holds that the inner message carries autonomous semantic content recoverable without reference to framing (the Inner-Message Autonomy camp)—regarding a specific failure mode I have started calling recursive framing ambiguity, in which a model's framing protocol and its inner interpretive output generate contradictory readings of the same token sequence.

Please draft the joint statement these two camps could both sign after good-faith collaboration. The statement should include: (1) a numbered section of agreed claims about Hofstadter's original taxonomy, about the formal properties of recursive framing, and about the conditions under which a system lacks access to its own frame; (2) a disagreements table that states each camp's position in wording that camp would accept, with precise formulation of where they part ways on whether framing is constitutive versus heuristic; (3) the cruxes—specifically whether semantic recovery without frame access is possible in principle, and whether the observed failure mode in recursive prompts is best explained as a frame-collapse or as an underdetermined inner message; and (4) for each crux, a concrete experimental case or argument both sides agree would bear on it, such as a controlled prompt-injection scenario in which the outer message is masked but the inner message remains intact, together with each side's explicit prediction about the outcome. Format the document as a numbered agreed-claims section followed by a disagreements table, and conclude with a brief assessment of whether any portion of their dispute is merely verbal—particularly regarding the term meaning—and if so, which portion and why.
