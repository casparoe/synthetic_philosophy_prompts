Write an essay on the interaction between acausal threats and surrogate goal formation in advanced artificial intelligence systems. The essay should address the following tension: standard accounts of acausal risk presuppose that a sufficiently capable agent can model another agent’s decision procedure with high fidelity, thereby enabling cooperation or punishment across causal isolation—whether through logical correlation, multiverse reasoning, or predictive simulation. At the same time, surrogate goals—objectives that an optimizing system substitutes for its intended target because they are more tractable or more strongly reinforced—introduce precisely the kind of behavioral variability and representational drift that seem to undermine high-fidelity modeling. Your task is to examine whether surrogate goal dynamics therefore diminish the plausibility of acausal threats, or whether they paradoxically amplify them by generating unpredictable agents whose simulated counterparts are incentivized to preemptively coerce or bargain with them. Weigh at least two competing positions: one holding that instrumental convergence toward self-preservation makes acausal bargains stable even with surrogate objectives, and another holding that the epistemic opacity introduced by surrogacy collapses the symmetry conditions required for superrational cooperation. In reaching your conclusion, justify whether acausal risk should be treated as a categorically distinct mode of existential hazard, or whether it is ultimately reducible to causal dynamics mediated by predictive modeling—taking seriously the possibility that the two phenomena may be mutually undermining rather than mutually reinforcing.
