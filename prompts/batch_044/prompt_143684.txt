I'd like a consistency audit of the following package of views on formal systems, meta-representation, and artificial cognition, before I present them in a philosophy of mind seminar. Here is where I stand:

(1) The MU-puzzle shows that within a consistent formal system there are well-formed strings that are not derivable by the system's own rules; recognizing this requires a meta-level judgment that the system itself cannot generate — a clear instance of what Hofstadter describes as jumping out of the system, or JOOTSing.

(2) I take this capacity for meta-level evaluation to be central to human cognition, and I accept that consciousness involves the ability to adopt such an external vantage point on one's own representational rules.

(3) I am a physicalist: this meta-representational capacity is fully implemented by neural computation, with no appeal to non-physical properties.

(4) I also hold that Gödelian incompleteness results apply specifically to consistent formal axiomatic systems capable of expressing arithmetic, not to biological brains; therefore the brain's solution to the MU-puzzle is not literally an escape from a Gödelian limitation but rather operates in an altogether different architectural kind.

(5) Because meta-representational architecture is physically realizable, I see no in-principle barrier to an artificial system possessing comparable capacities; on this point the human/AI distinction is contingent and engineering-dependent, not metaphysical.

(6) Despite (5), I maintain that current and foreseeable artificial systems lack genuine understanding. Following Searle's Chinese Room intuition, I think syntax is not sufficient for semantics, and since any artificial implementation remains syntactic at bottom — even if it can manipulate meta-rules — there is a persistent qualitative gap that JOOTSing does not close.

(7) Finally, I think the object-language/meta-language distinction is pragmatically unstable: any meta-rule invoked to evaluate a system can be internalized as an axiom in a richer formal system, which means JOOTSing is never a definitive escape from formalization but only a provisional shift of framework.
