Please argue that the conventional opposition between the self-sampling assumption and the self-indication assumption in anthropic reasoning collapses once reference-class membership is treated not as binary but as graded by what can be termed indexical proximity—the degree of causal-epistemic continuity between an observer's situation and one's own. Specifically, defend the view that Bostrom's presumptuous philosopher objection applies with full force only to an ungraded self-indication applied across cosmological scales, while a self-sampling restricted to a proximity-graded reference class avoids both that presumptuousness charge and the multiplicative update anomalies seen in the Sleeping Beauty problem, all without treating one's existence as evidentially neutral. In your argument, explain why the canonical Sleeping Beauty formulation obscures this resolution by treating awakening events as symmetric evidential updates rather than as differentially indexed by causal continuity, and show how grading the reference class repairs the asymmetry without collapsing into self-indication.
