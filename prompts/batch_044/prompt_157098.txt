She maintains that when an institution preserves formal property deeds while allowing informal land-transfer agreements to decay, the resulting absence functions as a positive sanction, making the remaining archive appear exhaustive and neutral rather than partial and interested. I want eight to ten interview questions that treat this as a live philosophical claim rather than a mere historiographical observation.
