For each desideratum, state the motivating consideration behind it. Flag which pairs you suspect are jointly unsatisfiable—especially any tension between epistemic humility and defensive action. Then, briefly, score Popper’s own defensive account, a Millian harm-based boundary, and a structural-institutional view against your list. Keep the weight on the criteria and their conflicts, not on the scorecard.
