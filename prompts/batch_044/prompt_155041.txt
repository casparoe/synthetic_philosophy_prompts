it needs to be accurate enough that someone who knows the difference between syntactic derivation and semantic interpretation wont wince, but it shouldnt read like a textbook. no bibliography or citations needed, just the poem. also this is just a rough idea so a typo or two is fine — i always write recieve wrong and i probably spelt pantoum wrong somewhere above.
