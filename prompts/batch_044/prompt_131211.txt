I have just come out of a long argument with a friend about Dennett’s proposal that the self is not a Cartesian substance but a center of narrative gravity—a theoretical point that pulls together beliefs, intentions, and actions much as a center of mass pulls together physical trajectories. I left defending a particular variant that I think improves on the standard account. On my view, the gravitational binding is not primarily a matter of autobiographical coherence or consistency; rather, it is generated by what I would call counterfactual inertia. A self maintains its center not merely by integrating actual past episodes, but by continuously generating and suppressing counterfactual branches—narratives of what it would have done in discontinuous scenarios that never actualized—and these suppressed branches exert a structural tension that holds the center in place. My friend objected that this smuggles a substantial self back in through the machinery of suppression, and that without an independent criterion for which counterfactuals count as belonging to the center, the view either collapses into an overpopulated multiverse of proto-selves or hides an essentialist core. I want an honest map of what would genuinely count against this specific position—not just the familiar anti-Dennett objections that selves are useful fictions, but defeaters targeted at the counterfactual-inertia variant. Which observations about disrupted narrative in amnesia, split-brain cases, or radical personal transformation would undermine it? Which conceptual or formal results—perhaps about diachronic identity, the ontology of possible worlds, or the logic of counterfactual attribution—would be decisive? I want the defeaters ranked: fatal if true, merely awkward, and those that a committed defender of this view could concede without the theory collapsing. I also want a direct assessment of whether I have qualified the view so heavily—allowing the center to shift, fragment, or be reconstructed across discontinuities—that it risks becoming unfalsifiable, and whether that insulation is a virtue or a vice for a theory of this kind. If the position survives only by redefining gravitational pull every time a counterexample appears, say so plainly.
