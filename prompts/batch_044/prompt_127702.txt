hey, quick consistency check for a piece I'm writing on memory and forgetting — I cover science and ideas, not academic philosophy, so bear with me if the framing is a little rough. I've landed on these commitments and I want to know if they actually hang together or if I'm helping myself to contradictions:

(1) Margalit's ethics of memory is basically right: there's a duty to remember injustices done to others, not just direct victims, because moral solidarity depends on shared remembrance.
(2) Rieff was basically right too — modern culture treats collective forgetting as a mental-health intervention and that lets societies avoid real moral reckoning.
(3) At the same time, individuals have a robust right to be forgotten, and I don't mean just GDPR-style data deletion — I mean a social right to have past mistakes or old identities not permanently archived.
(4) I think democratic institutions should actively curate collective memory (curricula, museums, archives) instead of leaving it to recommendation algorithims or private memorial markets.
(5) Genuine forgiveness requires an active, deliberate kind of forgetting — releasing the moral debt — and that's a moral achievement, not a betrayal.
(6) The labor of remembering shouldn't fall entirely on affected communities; there's a kind of mnemonic injustice when survivors are expected to carry the archive for everyone else.

My worry is that (1) pulls hard against (3) and (5). If I owe it to others to remember wrongs, doesn't that block the wrongdoer's right to be forgotten and the victim's right to forgive? And is there a hidden tension between (2) and (4) — Rieff seems suspicious of institutional management of culture, but I'm proposing democratic institutional curation. There's also something that feels off about holding (6) alongside (1), though maybe that's just a practical point about distribution.

Can you audit this? Tell me which pairs are actually in tension, which only look like it, rank them by severity, and for each real tension say what the cheapest revision is and what it costs. Don't lecture me on the whole literature — just trace what my own commitments commit me to.
