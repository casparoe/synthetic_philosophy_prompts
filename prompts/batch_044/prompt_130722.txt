My thesis is that Finland's post-2022 dual-layer manpower model—retaining universal conscription for territorial defense while operating a separate, voluntary, high-skill cyber and electronic-warfare reserve—demonstrates that twenty-first-century armed neutrality can no longer be sustained by a single civic-military structure like the traditional Swiss universal militia.
