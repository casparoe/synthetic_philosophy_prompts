The opposition, mostly senior faculty, says this confuses epistemic inquiry with shared goverance and that treating curriculum like a lab experiment undermines disciplinary authority.

Please act as a senior academic administrator with a background in philosophy of education. The memo needs to take the faculty opposition seriously: don't just restate that inquiry is good, but actually address whether pilot studies can legitimately replace or supplement faculty judgment in curriculum design. Make the reasoning concrete enough that the faculty senate could respond to it directly rather than dismissing it as abstract theory.
