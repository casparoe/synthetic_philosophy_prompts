(3) I think this deprivation can be attributed to a counterfactual subject — the person who would have lived — without requiring that I actually exist after death.
(4) I am an animalist about personal identity: I am strictly identical to this living human organism, not to a psychological-continuity relation.

(7) I maintain that phenomenal consciousness is necessary for being a subject of harm, but I deny that this forces me into a psychological-continuity theory of personal identity.

Please audit whether these hang together.
