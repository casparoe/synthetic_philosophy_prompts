Hey, I'm a retired marine ecologist (spent 30 years on tide pools) and lately I've been reading philiosophy of history for fun. I came up with an argument about lost-cause myths — the Confederacy, Masada, Kosovo Field, Dunkirk — that seems to show I either have to defend all of them or reject the ones I actually like. I can't find the leak, so I'm writing it out step by step to see if someone can spot it.

1. If a nation commemorates a defeat, the myth is justified only by its present function, not by the actual cause, since the cause was defeated.
2. The present function is always the same: it creates what I'll call counterfactual solidarity — people bond over a shared "we almost won" or "we went down fighting" narrative.
3. The Lost Cause, Masada, Kosovo Field, and the Dunkirk evacuation myth all do exactly this.
4. So if counterfactual solidarity is enough to justify a myth, then all four are justified.
5. But the Lost Cause is not justified, because its solidarity is built on erasing slavery and romanticizing secession.
6. If I reject the Lost Cause because of the moral content of its solidarity, I have to apply the same test to Masada (solidarity built on mass suicide and rejecting life outside the community) and Kosovo (solidarity built on ethnic martyrdom that fuels present conflict).
7. I don't want to reject Masada or Kosovo, and Dunkirk seems harmless enough.
8. So I need a principle that lets me reject the Lost Cause for its moral content without applying that principle to the others.
9. The only available principle is that some values are objectively worse than others — but that sounds like moral realism I don't know how to defend, and it still might condemn Kosovo if I'm honest.
10. Therefore, either counterfactual solidarity justifies everything (including the Lost Cause), or I must reject Masada and Kosovo too, or I need an objective moral hierarchy I can't ground.

Which step should I give up? I'd really like to keep step 7 — that Masada and Dunkirk stay legitimate — and I'd also like to keep step 5, that the Lost Cause is out. What's the cheapest way out? What does it cost me?
