I was reading about pain asymbolia—those patients with insular damage who can tell you exactly where the burning or stabbing is, rate the intensity, but say it just doesn't bother them, like they're reporting the temperature outside. That made me think about evolutionary decoupling: in squid and other cephalopods you've got nociceptive pathways running without any obvious limbic analog, so maybe suffering is just an evolutionary add-on that mammals bolted onto an older warning system?
