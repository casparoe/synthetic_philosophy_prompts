Don't just defend one interpretation; treat it like a menu and say which ones politicians probably mean when they cite it to justify either missionary intervention or isolationist purity. Also, could the slogan ever support a critique that the US fails its own standards, or is that always a misreading? I'd like this without a bibliography -- I just want the conceptual map, and maybe point out where the slogan does work that people don't realize.
