Please draft eight focused interview questions that go beyond asking her to restate the view. For each question, provide a follow-up keyed to her most likely reply—especially her tendency to distinguish welfare cancellation from welfare absence. At the end of your response, please state how confident you are in the framing of these questions and identify the single point about which you are least certain that the philosophical distinction holds up.
