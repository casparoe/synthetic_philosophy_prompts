Write a villanelle about Zhuangzi's butterfly dream, but skip the usual 'am I dreaming or awake' cliche. I want the poem to turn on wu hua — the transformation of things — not on Cartesian doubt. Use the two refrains to alternate between 'I am the butterfly transforming' and 'the butterfly is my transformation,' and in the middle stanzas show that if both waking and dreaming are just transformations, theres no stable self to do the observing. Make it accurate enough that someone who actually read the Zhuangzi passage wouldnt wince. Classic 19-line form with A1 and B1 refrains, and make sure the rhyme scheme dont fall apart. Keep the language plain.
