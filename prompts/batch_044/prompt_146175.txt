I want eight to ten interview questions that go well beyond having her restate this position. As you draft these, please say where your reasoning is most likely to fail: for instance, if the boundary between inertial framing and standard successor-state representations is blurrier than I've suggested, if the notion of a "stability disposition" risks circularity in defining causation, or if one of the follow-ups presupposes a technical result about causal modeling that may not hold. Do not include citations, bibliographies, or reading lists; I want questions that remain intelligible to a listener without library access, but I want them firmly in the weeds of the actual conceptual debate.
