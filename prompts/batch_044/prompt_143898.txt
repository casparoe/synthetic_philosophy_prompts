I am trying to understand what precise claims could be hiding inside that slogan and which of them actually do the work the speaker needs.

For each reading, indicate whether it is essentially a platitude, whether it is defensible from Arendt's actual argument in Eichmann in Jerusalem, and whether it genuinely supports the conclusion that ideological conviction is unnecessary to explain the atrocity.
