I am writing a paper for my philosophy of medicine seminar on the normative authority of advance directives in progressive neurological decline. Rather than surveying particular authors, I want a rigorous conceptual specification first: what must any adequate theory of the earlier self’s authority over the later self actually deliver?

Please set out the full list of desiderata for such a theory. For each requirement, give the motivating case or conceptual pressure—distinguishing genuine adequacy conditions from camp-specific preferences—and flag whether it is non-negotiable. Specifically include: (1) handling of binary versus gradual, intermittent loss of decision-making capacity; (2) preservation of the distinction between honoring precedent autonomy and promoting current welfare, without collapsing the two; (3) treatment of an “intermediate lucidity” state in which a patient expresses stable but systematically reversed values relative to the directive; (4) consistency with clinical duties to avoid gratuitous suffering; (5) respect for the specificity of directive content (e.g., refusal of artificial nutrition) without requiring impossible psychological continuity across personality change; and (6) a mechanism for weighing the authority of the pre-illness self against that of the intermediate self when they conflict.

Then identify any pair of these desiderata that appear jointly unsatisfiable—especially whether accommodating intermediate-state preferences can be reconciled with strict fidelity to the original directive. State which single desideratum you think does the most to exclude the standard binary positions (strict precedent-autonomy versus pure best-interests). Finally, briefly score three generic approaches against your list: (a) a strict precedent-autonomy view binding clinicians to the pre-illness directive regardless of intermediate expressions; (b) a current-welfare view that discounts the directive in favor of present interests; and (c) a diachronic-negotiation view that attempts to integrate authority across temporal stages. Keep the weight on the criteria, their tensions, and the exclusionary power of each requirement rather than on exhaustive literature mapping. I am not asking for a bibliography, reading list, or citations—only the conceptual architecture.
