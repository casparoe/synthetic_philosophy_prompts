I'm a journalist who covers science and ideas, and lately I've been reporting on the boom in Stoic-inspired mental-health apps. I started using one about eight months ago — every night it asks me to log my frustrations, then rewrites them using this two-part framework: here's what you can actually steer, and here's what you just have to accept. Last week I was in a real argument with my partner and caught myself automatically sorting my feelings with the app's exact phrases. It worked, I calmed down, but then I got this weird vertigo: if the part of me that decides what's worth caring about is being trained by an alogrithm I barely understand, is the calm actually mine?

My partner says I'm overthinking and that tools are tools. But I'm writing the magazine piece and I need to know if there's a real philosophical knot here, not just tech anxiety. How should I think about it?
