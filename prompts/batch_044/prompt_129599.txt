my hunch — but i want you to commit to an actual diagnosis, not just list options — is that this isn't even about consciousness. it's about whether introspection is direct observation or theory-laden interpretation. illusionists treat first-person reports as falsifiable hypotheses; anti-illusionists treat them as data that constrain theory. so are they answering different questions under one name? is it verbal? or is there a hidden empirical disagreement about how the brain builds self-models?

don't survey every interpretation. take a definite position: what kind of disagreement is this really, where is each side actually wrong or right, and could anything — a distinction, a neuroscience finding, whatever — actually end it? or is it just bedrock intuitions? and be straight with me if the question is ill-posed.
