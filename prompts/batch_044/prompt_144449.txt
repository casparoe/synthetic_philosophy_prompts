Provide a one-sentence justification for each score. Then rank the five criteria from most to least defensible as conceptual tools rather than rhetorical instruments. In your analysis, treat "the West" as a contested construct rather than a natural kind, avoid citing specific political speeches delivered after 2010, and explain which of the five is most vulnerable to being emptied by a "no true Scotsman" maneuver.
