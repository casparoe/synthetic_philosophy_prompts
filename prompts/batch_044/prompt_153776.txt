I'm preparing a closing exercise for a graduate seminar on rationality and self-reference, and I'd like a poem that frames the session's central tension rather than resolving it. The concept is embedded agency—specifically, the situation of an optimizing system that must model a world containing its own reasoning substrate, so that every update to its model can rewrite the conditions under which the update was made. Rather than rehearse the standard Newcomb framing, I want the poem to work with the less-discussed "leaky boundary" variant: the agent draws the line between self and environment not as a given Cartesian fact but as a practical decision, yet the instruments it uses to draw that line—its predictive architecture, its value representation, even its memory storage—are themselves part of the environment it is trying to delimit.

Please write a sestina. Use these six end-words, in this order for the first stanza: substrate, model, boundary, predict, loop, instrument. The form's recursive rotation should do the philosophical work: by the envoi, the reader should feel that "boundary" and "loop" have become conceptually inseparable, and that "instrument" and "predict" have collapsed into a single function. The tone should be serious and exploratory—someone actually trying to think through the recursion, not a whimsical mnemonic. Make the envoi register the specific tension that defining the self requires using tools that belong to both sides of the definition.

Please begin with the poem itself, with no preface, no commentary, and no explanatory text before or after the verse. After the poem, include a brief note—no more than a paragraph—indicating where the poem takes liberties with the embedded-agency literature or leans on a contested reading of the "leaky boundary" framing, and say specifically what kind of result or finding (for example, a formal limitation on self-modeling in computability theory, or an empirical result from cognitive science about boundary-drawing in embodied cognition) would lead you to abandon or significantly revise that framing.
