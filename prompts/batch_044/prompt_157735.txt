Please draft a one-page proposal for a six-month philosophical research grant. The core idea: Nick Bostrom's singleton hypothesis treats world-level coordination as primarily a control problem, but it tacitly presumes that such a structure embodies one unified value system. I want to investigate whether a pluralistic singleton is conceptually coherent — a single, non-fragmented decision architecture that internally preserves deep, incommensurable value pluralism, rather than reducing it to instrumental diversity. The precise research question: does singletonhood necessarily entail axiological homogeneity, or can internal pluralism be structured so that it does not generate competing decision centers? Existing literature on singletons focuses on stability and dynamics, not on whether the singleton must be a value monolith; pluralism literature in political philosophy examines value conflict but not within a unitary agent. The method is conceptual analysis: formalize Bostrom's conditions for singletonhood and extract implicit axiological commitments; map canonical forms of value pluralism onto institutional architectures; test each architecture against the singleton conditions; determine whether deep pluralism collapses into hidden fragmentation or into reducibility to a single ordering principle. Month 1: formalization. Months 2–3: mapping pluralism types. Months 4–5: testing architectures. Month 6: writing, with explicit failure conditions — for instance, if every coherent internal pluralism either requires competing authorities (hence not a singleton) or is reducible to one metric (hence not pluralism). The deliverable should be a taxonomy of pluralism-compatible architectures and an argument locating the boundary between a singleton containing values and a coordinated plurality. This avoids empirical forecasting; the scope risk is an unwieldy taxonomy, which I will manage by restricting analysis to three canonical pluralism types. Do not include a bibliography, citation list, or reading list; I will add references. Please include the precise question, why current answers leave it open, the month-by-month approach, what a result and a failure would look like, and an honest assessment of scope.
