I'd like a committed reading that takes both the film and the hard problem of consciousness seriously, specifically through the tension between first-order representationalism and higher-order thought theories. Does this architecture mean the film treats consciousness as merely the right functional integration of first-order emotional representations? Use specific scenes—the collapse of the personality islands, Riley's numbness when the console goes dark, the moment Sadness touches the blue core memory—to defend your stance.
