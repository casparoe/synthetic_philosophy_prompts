So I'm working on this novel about a society that actually votes on which moral emotion to instutionalize—like, they deliberatly design schools and courts to cultivate guilt over shame, or vice versa, because they think one makes people better citizens. And it got me thinking about that old philosophy and psychology debate over whether guilt is genuinely the "better" moral emotion compared to shame, and whether a culture can just choose one. I was arguing about it with my writing group last night and we got completely stuck.

On one side you've got people saying guilt is superior because it's action-oriented—you're guilty for what you did, not for who you are—so it leaves room for repair and doesn't crush the self. The other side says that's just Western individualism talking; shame is what actually binds people to community norms, and guilt is just internalized policing that lets you off the hook once you confess. Meanwhile everyone seems to agree that the old anthropological split—guilt cultures versus shame cultures—is way too neat, but nobody knows what to do with that. My hunch is that the disagreement isn't really about psychology at all, but about what moral improvement is supposed to look like: internal conscience versus social harmony. Or maybe it's verbal—both sides use "better" to mean different things. But I also wonder if there's a hidden empirical claim doing the work, like whether emotional ecosystems are actually malleable enough for a culture to engineer them through institutions.

What I want isn't a survey of every position since the seventies. I want a diagnosis: is this a substantive disagreement about values, a verbal mix-up about "better" or "culture," or are the two sides answering different questions—one normative, one descriptive—under one heading? And be honest about whether anything—maybe a sharper conceptual distinction, maybe an actual study on emotional socialization—could break the stalemate, or if the question is just ill-posed because you can't rank moral emotions outside a specific social practice. If it's stalled because people are smuggling in incompatible intuitions about the self, say so. I need this for the novel's world-building logic, so keep it concrete and don't just list philosophers.
