In the Stalinesque mode, the original discrete impressions are suppressed and replaced by a fabricated motion path, but the unedited sensory traces remain intact—merely airbrushed from conscious access rather than destroyed. She added that Dennett lifted this distinction unchanged from political analyses of propaganda and photographic revisionism, applying it to perception without philosophical modification.
