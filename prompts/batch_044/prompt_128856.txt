Jordan replied rationality symmetry is enough—both being expected-utility maximizers creates correlation regardless of goals. Riley said rationality symmetry still needs common knowledge of decision theory, and with orthogonal values the mediator just collapses into a regular prisoner's dilemma, not an acausal one.
