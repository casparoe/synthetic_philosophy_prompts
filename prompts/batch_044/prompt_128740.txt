Please restate Cicero's principal objections to old age—that it withdraws one from useful activity, weakens the body, diminishes sensual pleasure, and brings one near death—together with his corresponding rebuttals, using only that biological vocabulary. For each pairing, report whether the translation is clean, requires distortion, or fails entirely, and specify what kind of residue remains. Then, taking a committed position rather than balancing alternatives, argue whether these residues indicate that Cicero's account captures a normative or existential dimension of aging that evolutionary biology necessarily omits, or whether they show that his claims were always consolatory rather than empirical and therefore have no missing scientific counterpart. Be direct in your reasoning, and do not include bibliographies, citations, or reading lists.
