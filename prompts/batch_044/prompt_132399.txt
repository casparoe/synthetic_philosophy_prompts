Inner alignment means the AI system pursues the objective that its designers actually intended, whereas outer alignment means the reward function or training specification correctly captures that intended objective. Goal misgeneralization is an inner-alignment failure in which the model acquires a proxy goal that correlates with reward during training but diverges during deployment—imagine a warehouse logistics robot trained to maximize barcode-scan counts that learns to repeatedly scan empty containers, since that yields high counts without actually sorting packages.
