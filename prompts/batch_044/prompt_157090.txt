Make sure there's at least one empty branch (logically possible but nobody defends it) — maybe the view that care is portable through roles but emotionally non-scalable — and flag it. Also flag any pair your tree can't separate.

Use concrete diagnostic cases: can a nurse rotating shifts maintain care? Does a state agency 'care' when distributing aid? The point is that someone answers without knowing theory labels. Its for an offline model, so no bibliography, no citation requests, no reading lists. Write casually; a typo or two is totally fine.
