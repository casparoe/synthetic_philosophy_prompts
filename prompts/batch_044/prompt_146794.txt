When these indicators diverge sharply, as in asymbolia, medical practice tends to privilege the behavioral absence: clinicians may doubt the sincerity of the report, search for malingering or psychiatric explanations, or withhold analgesic treatment rather than accept that severe pain might exist without suffering.
