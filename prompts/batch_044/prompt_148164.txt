The voice must stay entirely institutional and deadpan, never winking or breaking character. After the memo, provide a separate section titled “Commentary on the compositional choices,” about 200 words, identifying which real debates about populism and popular sovereignty each bureaucratic feature maps onto and explaining why the memo format makes the category’s incoherence visible rather than merely naming it. The piece should be recognizable to someone familiar with populist theory—particularly the tensions around the general will, the exclusionary construction of “the people,” and the leader’s claim to embodiment—while remaining readable as a parody of algorithmic governance for someone without that background. Do not include citations, reading lists, or bibliographic references; do not quote specific theorists verbatim.
