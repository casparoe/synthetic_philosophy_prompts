Now consider a narrative about the origins of information-theoretic error correction in the 1940s and 1950s. Predict her verdict. I am less interested in the answer itself than in the competing rules that could underlie her first three rejections and her acceptance—rules perhaps about anachronistic evaluation, teleological inevitability, or the erasure of structurally meaningful alternatives—and in seeing which of those rules diverges on this information-theory case. Give me the candidate rules, your best guess at her actual ruling with reasoning, the rule you think she is really running, and the single further historical case that would best adjudicate between them.
