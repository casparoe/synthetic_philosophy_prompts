Write a parody internal memorandum—approximately 500 words, deadpan bureaucratic tone, with numbered sections and an embedded sample form—from a fictional “Office of Mnemonic Equity” to a municipal heritage board. The form’s fields should include items such as “Degree of Historical Context Available,” “Present-Moral Risk Rating,” and “Remediation Option C: Contextual Plaque with Apologetic Footnote.” Maintain total deadpan seriousness throughout; the irony should be sustained and legible without any authorial wink. The piece should be readable without specialist knowledge, but should reward familiarity with the real tensions between presentism, moral progress, and collective memory. Do not include citations, reading lists, or references to living individuals.
