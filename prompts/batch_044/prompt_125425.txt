The guest contends that Camus's essay Reflections on the Guillotine should be read as an extension of this logic: the guillotine is not merely cruel but bureaucratically rationalized violence, an attempt to impose definitive meaning on life that mirrors the totalitarian pretensions Camus elsewhere condemns.
