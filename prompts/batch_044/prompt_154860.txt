Write an essay on whether genuine joint intentionality can exist between a conscious human and a non-conscious artificial system. Weigh the reductionist position—that collective action is fully analyzable into interlocking individual intentions plus mutual belief—against the we-mode position, which requires participants to represent themselves as parts of a plural subject with shared goals. Focus your analysis on a concrete scenario: a human surgeon and an adaptive AI assistant jointly revising an incision strategy in real time during an operation, each modifying its behavior in response to the other in ways neither pre-programmed. Reach and defend a clear verdict: either this coupling constitutes true joint action despite the AI's lack of phenomenal consciousness and first-person plural self-conception, or the absence of a shared we-perspective necessarily reduces it to sophisticated but merely coordinated individual action, regardless of behavioral integration.
