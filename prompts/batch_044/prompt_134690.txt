(4) But NFL shows that any restriction to S is just adopting a non-uniform prior, and without independent justification for that prior we are back in Hume's circle.
(5) So NFL doesn't solve induction; it formalizes Hume's worry — any successful learner succeeds only by smuggling in an inductive bias that itself needs justification.
