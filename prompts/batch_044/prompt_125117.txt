Construct a thought experiment that isolates the Knobe asymmetry outside the standard corporate-environmental paradigm, specifically in an emergency-department triage setting. Design two versions—identical except for the valence of a foreseen but unintended side effect—where a triage officer adopts an explicit, clinically justified protocol that affects patient autonomy (not survival) positively in one version and negatively in the other; each version should be one paragraph, realistic, and free of references to executives, profits, or ecosystems. After presenting both versions, state the intentionality attribution that the Knobe effect predicts in each case, then determine whether the asymmetry is best explained by moral evaluation of the side effect or by a structural factor such as norm violation or abnormal causal structure. Identify the precise feature of your design that drives the divergence between these two explanations, explain what happens to the divergence if that feature is removed, and describe how a defender of the structural-factor account would attack the case to argue that moral evaluation is still doing hidden work.
