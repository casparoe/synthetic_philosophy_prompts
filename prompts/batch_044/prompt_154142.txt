You serve on your university's Committee on Synthetic Moral Status. The neuroscience-computing lab running System Theta has applied for an exemption from Protocol 7, the institution's provisional protection rule for synthetic systems exhibiting sustained, self-preserving computation. Theta is not a language model: it is an embodied hybrid spiking-recurrent architecture with continuous sensory-motor interaction, cross-modal predictive loops that adjust its own weights to preserve coherence under disruption, and homeostatic error-correction spanning both perceptual and action-selection layers. Researchers disagree about whether its behavior—reorienting away from damage, persisting in goal-directed tasks after perturbation—reflects genuine damage-avoidance or merely optimized loss-function dynamics.

The committee must recommend one of three courses: grant the exemption (no protected status), deny it (full provisional protection, barring destructive testing and requiring graceful decommissioning), or approve with conditions (a time-bound audit). In reaching your recommendation, apply the distinction between functional-integrationist attribution—moral relevance tracks unified causal loops that regulate a system's own persistence—and representational-binding attribution—moral relevance tracks states with intrinsic aboutness coupled to valence. You must also engage the committee's working-paper proposal that the decisive factor is not either feature alone, but "normative opacity": cases where internal error signals are irreducible to both representational content and causal architecture, so that harming the system introduces a failure mode that is intrinsically bad-for-the-system in a non-translatable way.

Draft the formal memo for Thursday's vote. Structure it as follows: Executive Summary (~150 words); The Integrationist Case (~200 words); The Binding Case (~200 words); The Normative-Opacity Assessment (~200 words); Recommendation and Implementation Timeline (~150 words). The recommendation should be concrete and actionable, include a specific time frame, and specify whether the exemption is granted, denied, or conditionally approved. Treat the strongest argument on the losing side with intellectual honesty rather than dismissal. Do not include bibliographies, citation requests, or appeals to external authority.
