Since I began advising on the architecture of a multi-modal intensive-care decision-support system, I have become personally concerned with whether artificial global broadcasting can occur without the phenomenal integration characteristic of conscious patients. For a policy working-group I am convening, please construct a thought experiment to precise specifications.

Construct a single-paragraph, realistic scenario—set in a hospital or clinical workplace, not in science fiction, and without appeal to demons, perfect predictors, or exotic physics—featuring an artificial cognitive architecture in which information is globally broadcast across sensory, memory, and action modules, thereby satisfying the core mechanism of global workspace theory, and in which full access-consciousness is present (the information is available for reasoning, decision, and verbal report), yet phenomenal consciousness is absent. To avoid the standard zombie or Chinese Room tropes, inject a specific architectural feature: asynchronous broadcast queues coupled with inter-module veto gates that permit feedforward cross-modal reasoning and meta-cognitive reporting, but actively suppress recurrent self-modelling integration within the anterior hub.

After presenting the scenario, demonstrate that it meets the specification by walking through:
(i) why global workspace theory, in the tradition of Baars and Dehaene, is compelled to ascribe consciousness to the system;
(ii) why an access-consciousness account, in Block's distinction, correctly predicts the presence of access without phenomenal experience;
(iii) and what a defender of phenomenal consciousness must deny—either the globality of the broadcast or the absence of binding—to resist the conclusion.

Then identify precisely which structural feature does the work of separating the theories, explain what happens to each verdict if that feature is removed and standard recurrent integration is restored, and provide an honest account of the strongest objection a global-workspace theorist could raise—that the case cheats by stipulating rather than generating the absence of phenomenal consciousness, or by mischaracterising global broadcast—and how that theorist would defend the theory against your scenario.

Please write in formal British English, avoid bibliographies, reading lists, and citations entirely, and do not invoke theories or named authors beyond those required for the reasoning above.
