I've been getting into the modern stoic revival and keep seeing the dichotomy of control presented like a mental filing cabinet — you chuck things into my business or not my business. But then I read that Epictetus wasn't really sorting events at all; he was describing prohairesis as this kind of moral immune system that stays intact regardless of externals, and that modern apps like Stoic Journal are basically treating him as an ancient CBT manual. If the dichotomy is actually about the structure of agency and not stress-management categories, doesnt that mean the whole revival is resting on a category mistake? Also, is the trichotomy that Irvine proposes just fixing this, or is it adding a new layer that Epictetus wouldnt recognise?
