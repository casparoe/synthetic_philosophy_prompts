(7) Evolutionary continuity arguments are epistemically inert in this domain: they neither justify granting minds to colonies nor justify denying them, so they should not shift our credences.

(8) The colony's representational content, if present, is restricted to its own pheromonal topology—internal chemical-gradient states—rather than being world-directed at predators, food sources, or spatial locations.

(9) The integration mechanism is not neural synchrony but slow, diffuse pheromonal-wave modulation across subgroups, serving as a distributed broadcast without any central processor.
