Now half of us think the musician is clearly in bad faith too, and half think that's absurd, and we're more stuck than before. We all agree he says he's playing at being a waiter, but we can't agree on whether that proves denial of freedom or just skilled role-playing. My working theory is that this isn't about Sartre at all — it's a hidden disagreement about whether radical freedom has to feel like angush to be real, or whether you can be fully free and still feel at home in a social part, and the musician case just exposes it. But maybe I'm wrong and it's actually that we're mixing up ontolgy and phenomenolgy without noticing.
