The piece should be approximately 700 words, readable to someone unfamiliar with sortition debates but immediately legible to a specialist as a fair, biting reductio of the calibration problem. Do not include citations, footnotes, bibliographic references, or quotations from actual works; build the satire entirely from the internal logic of the fictional bureaucracy. I want the humor to come from the memo’s earnest attempt to square the circle of “random but qualified,” so that the contradictions emerge structurally rather than through explicit commentary.
