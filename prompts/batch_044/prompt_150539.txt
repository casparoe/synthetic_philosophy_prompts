I want you to act like an expert in philosophy of mind and clinical epistemology—please think carefully, maybe start with a quick brainstorm of how Dennett-style heterophenomenology survives contact with actual patients, then build me the deliverable.

the procedure should keep intact the core heterophenomenological distinction—treating reports as data about a subject's theory of experience rather than as direct windows onto phenomenal reality—while dropping distinctions that dont help in a 20-minute clinical window. it must also include a triangulation step: compare the verbal report against a reconstructed pre-morbid linguistic baseline (from family interviews or prior records) and against non-verbal behavioral markers like eye-tracking or autonomic response, without letting those markers override the text entirely. mark every point where judgement cant be eliminated.
