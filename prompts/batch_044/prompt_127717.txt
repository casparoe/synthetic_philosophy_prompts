If inverse reinforcement learning treats such behaviour as direct evidence for an intrinsic reward term, then the inferred reward function appears to be culture-specific in a way that undermines its portability. I would therefore like you to set out the most important implications of this tension. Specifically, how should we understand the relationship between inferred preference structures and the social contexts in which behaviour is observed? And what are the consequences for AI alignment if we construct reward models from behavioural traces that systematically conflate genuine moral commitment with culturally embedded signalling norms?
