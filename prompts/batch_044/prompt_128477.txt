ok so i got this cochlear implant last year and lately ive been obsessing over whether im literally the same person as before. not in a weird way, just metaphysically. i cooked up this argument and now it feels like it eats itself.

1) Endurantism says im wholly present at every time i exist, not spread out like a worm.
2) But now part of my ear is silicon and my memory is partly on this wearable i use. So at t1 i have an organic ear, at t2 i have silicon.
3) If im wholly present at both times, then i have both an organic and silicon ear right now, or im missing a part. Both are false.
4) So endurantism cant handle gradual replacement, at least for people like me.
5) Perdurantism fixes it: im a 4d worm with different temporal parts having different ears.
6) But my thinking extends into the wearable too — its part of my cognitive loop, not just a tool.
7) So the 4d worm that is me includes parts of that device and its server updates.
8) That means either im partly identical to a server rack, or the worm isnt me.
9) Both suck. So (5) must be wrong?
10) But if (5) is wrong, then (3) still kills endurantism, and were back to square one with no account.

i feel like something slipped between the parts-talk and the identity-talk but i cant name it. which step do you reject, and what does giving it up cost? id like to keep that my prosthetics dont erase my identity, but im open to paying.
