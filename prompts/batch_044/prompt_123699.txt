I am designing the conceptual framework for a graduate seminar on representation in artificial neural networks. Before we assess particular philosophical accounts, I want a rigorous specification of what an adequate theory of representation in this domain must deliver.

Write the desiderata for a theory of representational realism about neural networks. For each requirement, state the motivating case or conceptual consideration, indicate whether you regard it as non-negotiable or as a camp-specific preference, and, where relevant, note which existing approaches it tends to exclude.

Your list must specifically include and address the following:

- The tracking-versus-representation distinction, motivated by cases of adversarially induced spurious correlations that are mechanistically disconnected from the functional role of the feature they appear to encode.
- Distributed polysemy: the fact that individual units or subspaces can participate in multiple, context-dependent representations that do not decompose into separable channels. A theory must either accommodate this or provide a principled explanation of why it is illusory.
- Interventional stability (a condition I am introducing for this exercise): a representation should be identifiable in some form through targeted mechanistic disruptions—such as ablation of redundant pathways or perturbation of non-representational weights—provided that overall task performance remains intact. State the case that motivates this condition and argue whether it is a genuine desideratum or an imported prejudice from classical philosophy of mind.
- Empirical accessibility without methodological collapse: representation claims must be supportable by interpretability techniques, yet a theory must not identify representation with whatever is readable by any particular visualization or probing method.
- Consistency with results from learning theory—specifically overparameterization, the existence of multiple global minima with functionally equivalent but mechanistically divergent solutions, and implicit regularization—which suggest that representations may not be unique across training runs.

In addition, flag any pair of desiderata that appear jointly unsatisfiable in light of current understanding of mechanistic redundancy and adversarial robustness. Then, briefly, score the three broad contenders—causal-informational realism, structural-mapping realism, and instrumentalist deflationism—against the list, keeping the weight on the criteria rather than on the scorecard.

Please write your answer in American English, in a formal and polished register. Do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations; I want the conceptual specification itself, not pointers to the literature.
