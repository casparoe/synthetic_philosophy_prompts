If we terraform Mars and thereby create the conditions for an entirely different set of people to come into existence, in what sense can those future people be said to have been wronged by our decision, and does that dissolve the moral urgency of preservation?

What conceptual dependency has to be firmly in place—perhaps a distinction between intrinsic and instrumental value applied to non-terrestrial entities, or a clear framing of intergenerational justice—before the preservation-versus-expansion tension registers as genuinely philosophical rather than merely technological?
