In philosophical defenses of the four-dimensional block universe — especially the line of reasoning that runs from Rietdijk through Putnam on Lorentz-transformed simultaneity — a standard analogy compares "now" to "here." The claim is that "now" functions as a purely indexical locator in an already-complete spatiotemporal manifold, just as "here" locates a speaker in space without granting that point any metaphysical privilege. Proponents use this to dissolve presentist intuitions: the feeling that the present is uniquely real is said to be no more justified than the feeling that one's own spatial position is cosmically central.
