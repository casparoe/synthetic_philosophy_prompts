She asks me whether it would be irrational to refuse the promotion and remain in the orchard. I feel her dilemma raises a deeper question about whether we labour to satisfy genuine needs or merely to secure relative standing, yet I cannot say precisely where the fault in her reasoning lies, if any. Please advise me, using British English, in no more than 250 words. Lead with your bottom line in one sentence, then explain. Do not include a bibliography, reading list, or citations.
