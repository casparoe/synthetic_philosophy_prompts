Check whether this package hangs together. Specifically: does (3) treat phenomenal properties as substantial enough to need explaining, which conflicts with (2)'s claim that they do not exist? Does (5) reduce consciousness to a functional pattern in a way that undermines the seriousness of the illusion described in (2)? Rank any real tensions by severity, distinguish them from tensions that only appear on the surface, and for each genuine conflict state the least costly revision and what it costs.
