You are advising a three-year conceptual philosophy project with a small team and no empirical research arm, focused on reconstructing G. E. Moore's account of intrinsic value—specifically the relation between aesthetic value and the value of personal friendship or affection. Rather than pursuing standard exegesis, the group is interested in structural questions about how Moore's principle of organic unities operates across these domains.

To frame the inquiry precisely, consider a distinction not usually drawn in the literature: synchronic organic unities, in which a whole experienced at a single time (such as the appreciation of a complex aesthetic form) possesses value exceeding the sum of its parts, versus diachronic reciprocal unities, in which friendship unfolds through mutual recognition across time and may generate a different, second-order non-additive value—call it a reciprocity effect. The central suspicion is that determining whether Moore's framework can accommodate this diachronic structure, without collapsing friendship into aesthetic appreciation or reducing it to instrumental benefit, is upstream of several other interpretive and normative issues. The group needs to decide whether this framing is genuinely productive or a conceptual red herring.

Your task is to identify the principal open conceptual questions in this reconstructed Moorean territory and to defend a ranked agenda for which two or three should command the bulk of the team's effort over the three years. For each selection, specify the criteria governing its rank—such as upstream dependency on other problems, current neglect within philosophical scholarship, tractability for a purely conceptual group, and potential to restructure Moore's broader ethics. Explain the trade-offs: why a highly tractable but isolated question might be deprioritized, and why a harder but upstream question might take precedence. Additionally, indicate what specific conceptual result—say, a clarified internal distinction, a resolved tension in Moore's account of consciousness and value, or a demonstrated failure of the diachronic reciprocity framing—would cause you to revise the ordering. The deliverable should be a defended prioritization with explicit reasoning, not a survey or a reading list.
