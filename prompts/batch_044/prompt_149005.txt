Write an essay on whether Walzer's moral equality of combatants still makes sense once you leave conventional state wars behind. The usual story is that soldiers on both sides have equal rights because they face symetrical risks and don't choose the justice of their cause. But apply that to insurgents in an asymetric conflict, or to drone crews who face no physical danger at all—does equality collapse? Weigh the reciprocity-based defence against the rival view that only unjust fighters forfeit rights. Reach an actual verdict: maybe equality is conditional on mutual vulnerability, maybe it's dead altogether, but don't end on a non-committal note.
