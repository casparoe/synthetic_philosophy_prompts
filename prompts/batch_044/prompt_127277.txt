The Renaissance itself was largely constructed by Jacob Burckhardt in his 1860 study as an age of secular individualism and artistic rebirth, yet recent medievalists have shown that universities, Aristotelian philosophy, and artistic patronage all flowed continuously from the medieval period without a sharp rupture.
