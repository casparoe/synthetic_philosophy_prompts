The dissolution of the cluster must arise specifically from the classified individuals’ self-interpretation: once labeled, their understanding of their own conduct changes in a way that fragments the original shared pattern, not through external social stigma or institutional abolition of the label. After presenting the scenario, do the following: (a) identify the belief or behavior pattern being classified, the method of classification, and the relevant nearby worlds for assessing whether the loop is genuinely self-undermining; (b) explain the precise structural feature that causes dissolution rather than reinforcement, contrasting it explicitly with Hacking’s standard examples such as multiple personality disorder or the classification of homosexuality; (c) state how a defender of looping-effect stability would attack your case—specifically, what they would claim is unrealistic about the self-interpretation mechanism; and (d) note whether removing that allegedly unrealistic feature collapses the case back into a standard reinforcing loop. Please do not include bibliographies, reading lists, or citations to texts I should look up; the reasoning should stand on its own, with the structural dynamics of the invented classification made fully transparent.
