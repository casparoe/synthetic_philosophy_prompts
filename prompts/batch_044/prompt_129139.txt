I have been puzzling over something on my own: there is this old question, the Meno problem, about why knowing something is better than just being right about it. It strikes me that if I guess the right answer and you guess the right answer, we both end up in the same place, so I want my group to discover for themselves why that intuition misses something. Design a classroom activity or structured game—complete with rules, roles, materials, and a payoff scheme using only points or social rewards, not money—where participants begin by acting on beliefs that happen to be correct but collapse under a small change in conditions, then shift to acting on knowledge that can be explained and transferred.
