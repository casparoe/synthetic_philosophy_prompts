Write the desiderata an account of moral objectivity must meet if it is to take cross-cultural folk-moral variation seriously — in particular the WEIRD/non-WEIRD divergence in moral foundations, where harm and care judgments appear near-universal but variably weighted, while loyalty and purity judgments vary not only in salience but in conceptual structure (for instance, whether they are framed as hierarchical reciprocity or as communal-sharing obligations). For each requirement, state the motivating case or distinction it protects — such as the difference between a norm being absent versus present but low-salience, or between intersubjective convergence across cultures and mind-independent moral truth, or between weighting differences and genuine incommensurability. Mark which desiderata are non-negotiable for any adequate metaethics and which belong to particular camps (realist, relativist, pluralist). Then indicate whether any subset of these requirements is jointly unsatisfiable given the empirical pattern, and identify which single requirement does the most to exclude popular theories. Keep the focus on the criteria and their motivating cases rather than on scoring existing views.
