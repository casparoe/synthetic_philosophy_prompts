There's this loop I keep seeing in arguments about whether money is socially constructed. One side says paper cash is just ink and collective belief. The other side says gold has actual physical scarcity, so its not the same. Then the first side says scarcity itself depends on tech and human needs, so gold's value is socially mediated too. Then the second side retreats to saying well gold at least has intrinsik physical properties paper lacks. Every time the social layer gets conceded, they push the real foundation one level deeper into materiality. Is there a name for this specific move? I don't mean plain infinite regress — the retreat is always toward a more material boundary, and each step feels individually reasonable. If theres no term, how would you distinguish it from a motte-and-bailey or just shifting the goalposts?
