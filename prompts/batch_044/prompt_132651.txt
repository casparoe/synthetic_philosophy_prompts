Most AI labs run safety evals by testing the bare model on chat benchmarks, but then they deploy agentic versions with tool access and long-context loops and treat those as categorically different for risk assesment. What view of what an LLM fundementally is does that split presuppose? Does it assume the base model is just a simulator and the scaffolding supplies agency, or does it treat agency as emergent and continuous? And is the practice consistent with itself if the same weights generate both, or does it quietly commit to a kind of functionalist dualism where the container matters more than the weights? Also, if we took seriously that these are just simulators all the way down, what would have to change in how we regulate them?
