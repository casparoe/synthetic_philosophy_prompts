Write a step-by-step checklist—ordered questions, what counts as a sufficient answer at each stage, explicit stop points where the verdict is negative, and clearly marked judgment calls—grounded in the actual mechanics of hierarchical generative models, precision-weighted prediction errors, and active inference rather than loose metaphor. Apply it to two hypothetical cases: first, a clinical study linking hallucination severity to independently measured precision-weighting deficits in auditory cortex; second, a behavioral study describing consumer choice as "minimizing surprise" without specifying generative models, action policies, or precision dynamics. Include at least one worked case where the procedure returns a negative verdict that conflicts with the authors' own framing, and be explicit about what the checklist cannot settle.
