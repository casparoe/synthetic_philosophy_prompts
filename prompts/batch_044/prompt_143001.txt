Do not import Confucian metaphysics—no tian, no cosmological harmony, no ritual as cosmic ordering—unless it can be expressed as a claim about organizational structure or social expectation without distortion.

After the translation, provide a residue report: which elements of the Confucian view survive intact (for instance, the anti-individualist, relational structure of the self), which must be stretched or distorted to fit (perhaps the idea that proper role-performance expresses an objective moral order), and which have no expression whatsoever in organizational vocabulary (for example, the claim that cultivating one's role can affect the harmony of the cosmos). Conclude by assessing whether the untranslatable residue reveals a genuine normative gap in organizational role theory—something it should but cannot say—or whether it merely exposes metaphysical baggage in Confucianism that a secular framework is right to discard.
