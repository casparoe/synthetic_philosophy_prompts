They recently invoked this to reject a proposal to replace a mandatory formal-logic requirement with an applied data-science module, insisting that they do not yet understand what institutional dysfunction the logic course was meant to prevent, while at the same time they approve novel interdisciplinary electives with almost no historical scrutiny.

What philosophical commitments does this asymmetry presuppose? Reconstruct the implicit theory of institutional rationality, distinguish what the practice strictly requires from what it merely accommodates, and indicate what would have to change in the committee’s procedures if the underlying view were replaced by a straightforwardly consequentialist or optimisation-based framework.

If the premise that this asymmetry is philosophically loaded rather than administratively inevitable is confused, please say so plainly rather than accommodating the framing politely. Present your answer as exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion.
