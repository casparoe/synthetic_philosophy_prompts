Quine rejects the analytic-synthetic distinction, so he cant wall moral judgments off from factual ones by calling them analytic. That means moral beliefs enter the web of belief like scientific ones, though closer to the edge where theyre easier to revise. But unlike Ayer, Quine lets moral beliefs be revised by experience—if a cultures moral framework stops predicting behavior well, they adjust it. So he ends up with a moral pragmatism where values are useful fictions kept near the periphery.
