I need a reusable decision procedre my team can run in order: specific checks, clear stop points for obvious misuse, and clear hand-off points where the question is genuinely open and we need to call in an expert. Please include a small set of test phrases or patterns we should scan for, then apply the procedre to three cases: (1) a pitch claiming Gödel proves no computer can understand ethics because self-reference is unavoidable; (2) a draft arguing climate models are Gödel-incomplete and therefore long-range predictions are unreliable; and (3) a formal voting-theory paper using recursive axioms, where I personally think it's overreaching but I want the tool to correct me if I'm wrong. For each case, show exactly where the checks stop, where they flag misuse, and where they say "call a philosopher." Be honest about which steps are just judgment calls dressed up as rules, flag where the procedure will misfire or let obvious nonsense slip through, and don't ask me to read Penrose or Lucas -- just build it from the theorems' actual preconditions. Also, no reading lists or citations; we just need the checklist.
