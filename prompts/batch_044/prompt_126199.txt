After the joke, include a brief note that separates what is settled in the setup—the basic closure principle and the propositional/doxastic distinction—from what is genuinely contested: whether the paradox forces voluntarism, whether modal beliefs are the right test case, and whether fallibilism alone dissolves the problem.
