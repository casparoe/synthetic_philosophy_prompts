Quick question that's been bugging me since I dug into Solovay's model and the Banach-Tarski thing. If AC is the thing that lets you construct Vitali sets and break Lebesgue measurability, and Bayesian updating needs a coherent prior over continuous paramter spaces, doesn't AC actually undermine rational epistemics? In Solovay's model you drop full choice but keep dependent choice, and suddenly every subset of R is measurable, which feels way cleaner for probability. So why do working mathematicians still treat AC as default? Are they trading epistemic hygiene for ontological richness, or am I conflaiting the existence of weird subsets with the sigma-algebras we actually use when conditioning?
