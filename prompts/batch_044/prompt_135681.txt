I have been puzzling over why anyone bothers to vote when, in any large election, the chance that one person's ballot actually changes the result is practically zero, while the effort and time cost are completely real. Rather than dismissing the whole thing as foolish, I want a joke that treats this paradox with real care. Imagine a small town where, by local ordinance, no one may cast a ballot until they first write out by hand a calculation showing exactly how likely their single vote is to break a tie, using the town's population and past election margins. The punchline should grow directly out of the logic of the paradox itself: the more precisely people apply the reasoning that voting is individually pointless, the more they collectively destroy the election's validity, even though the law forces them to do this reasoning precisely in order to vote. Please avoid generic jokes about corrupt politicians or lazy voters; the humor should come from the contradiction inside the rational-choice argument, so that someone who genuinely understands the voting paradox would recognize the logic immediately.
