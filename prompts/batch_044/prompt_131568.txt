The second replied that recursive improvement is being overstated as a discontinuity, and that the Industrial Revolution is actually the closer match because it was a general-purpose technology that raised aggregate living standards while producing diffuse institutional failures like climate change and urban squalor, which suggests AI will generate widespread coordination strain rather than a catastrophic singularity. They asked me to adjudicate and I could not determine which moves were strongest.
