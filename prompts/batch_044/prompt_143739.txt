The piece should be fully intelligible to a non-specialist—explain any necessary concept within the manual's own terms—without ever citing authors, providing bibliographies, or quoting real texts verbatim. Maintain sustained deadpan irony throughout: the manual's author should never acknowledge the absurdity, so that the philosophical critique is delivered entirely through the literalization of abstract epistemic conditions into mechanical specifications.
