The debate over Newton’s bucket—whether rotating water’s concavity proves absolute space or only exposes relational dynamics among bodies—has recycled the same arguments for centuries: substantivalists point to absolute acceleration, relationists invoke Mach’s principle or distant matter, and both sides claim general relativity vindicates them.
