For a long-form piece I'm writing on the neuroscience of temporal experience, I have been working out my own stance on revisionist models of consciousness. Here is my profile on the color phi phenomenon, Stalinesque versus Orwellian revision, and the timing of phenomenal content; I need an audit of whether these commitments hang together.

My positions are these. First, I take the color phi illusion to demonstrate Stalinesque revision rather than Orwellian memory distortion: the brain constructs the intermediate trajectory and color shift before that content reaches awareness, editing the perceptual record prior to consciousness rather than altering recall afterward. Second, I reject any Cartesian theater model; phenomenal experience is distributed across temporal integration windows rather than occurring at discrete, localized neural points. Third, I treat introspective reports about the qualitative character and temporal ordering of experience as generally reliable primary data, not as confabulations to be dismissed by third-person neuroscience. Fourth, I hold that because Stalinesque editing requires both physical flashes to be processed before the intermediate percept is assembled, awareness necessarily lags behind physical stimulation by at least the duration of the integration window. Fifth, I maintain that phenomenal consciousness requires a continuous narrative thread linking successive constructed moments; without such continuity there is no single subject experiencing the trajectory across the window. Sixth, I also hold that phenomenal consciousness is always of a present moment, never of genuinely past or future stimuli. Seventh, I take the binding window for color-motion integration to be modality-fixed at roughly 80 to 120 milliseconds.

Please go through these and identify which pairs are in genuine tension, which pairs only appear to conflict, and for each genuine tension how severe it is. For every real conflict, say what the cheapest revision would be and what that repair costs the overall picture. Then tell me which single commitment, if dropped, would clean up the most conflicts without collapsing the framework. I am not asking for a defense of any particular view; I want a disciplined audit of my own set, ordered by severity, with the apparent tensions dissolved on inspection rather than flagged.
