3. I also think counterfactuals in big-history are only kosher when they hold the causal architecture constant right up to the divergence point — otherwise you're just writing fiction.
4. But I also think the divergence wasn't a single critical juncture; it was a slow, path-dependent lock-in where energy, labour, and capital regimes co-evolved, so there's no clean "point" to hold constant.

6. Finally, I think the best explanations are multi-causal and overdetermined, which means no single factor should do the explanatory work in the contrast.
