2. suffering needs integration over time — a flash of pain with no subject persisting through it isn't really suffering, it's just a physical event with a qualitative tone.
3. moral considerability tracks the capacity for integrated suffering, not just raw proto-experience. so rocks and electrons don't count.

5. and i think killing a human is gravely wrong, but if macro-mind is just micro-minds arranged, then destroying a brain just redistributes those experiences rather than wiping them out, which makes the harm seem weird.
