Write an essay on a tension in Schopenhaur's view of music. He ranks it highest because it expresses the Will directy, bypassing Ideas altogether. But he also describes rhythm, harmony and melody in almost geometric terms, and anyone who writes music knows how rule-bound composition is. So there's a puzzle: if music is pure Will-expression, why does its power depend so heavily on formal structure that looks like representation? Push this further: if Schopenhaur is right, what should we say about music generated entirely by algorithms with no conscious will behind them — is it metaphysically hollow, or does his framework actually allow for non-human processes to express Will? Weigh the arguments on both sides, reach a clear conclusion, and defend it properly. Don't just summarise his position; actually take a side on whether the formal, mathematical aspect undermines his metaphysical ranking of music.
