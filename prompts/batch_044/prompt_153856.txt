Construct a one-paragraph thought experiment set in an ordinary institution such as a community land trust or cooperative housing association, engineered so that Nozick's entitlement theory and a pattern-based distributive principle deliver opposite verdicts about whether a present holder should retain a resource, with the conflict arising not from the voluntary-transfer chain itself but from an asymmetric information problem about historical injustice. The case should test whether the rectification principle can be operationalized without undermining the stability of just transfers, so do not stipulate the outcomes—derive them from the theories.
