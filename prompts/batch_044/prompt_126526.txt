She further argues that this blocks the linear aggregation that would otherwise yield a repugnant conclusion for invertebrate populations, and she distinguishes intentional systemic application—such as a farmer deploying neonicotinoids—from incidental crushing.

Please draft eight to ten formal interview questions that move beyond mere restatement of the view. Finally, include a question on how her view treats intentional versus accidental harm when ecological impact is held constant—contrasting, for instance, systemic pesticide use with a pedestrian inadvertently crushing beetles—with a follow-up anticipating her distinction between systemic agency and incidental interaction. The tone should be that of a journalist covering philosophy of science and environmental ethics, precise and polished.
