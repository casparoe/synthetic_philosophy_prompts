Write a deadpan travel-guide entry in the style of a Lonely Planet page, approximately 600 words, describing travel to the Republic of Isotypia, a fictional nation that has adopted Otto Neurath's ISOTYPE as its sole official language, legal code, and administrative protocol. Include a brief description of an invented bureaucratic visa form rendered entirely in pure pictogram logic with no words. Maintain the cheerful, practical tone throughout, never breaking character to explain the joke.
