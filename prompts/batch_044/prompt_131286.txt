hey so i've been thinking about vagueness and that sorites paradox thing, not the tired sand-heap examples but my own weird cases. here are my gut verdicts and they don't line up neat.

1. group chat: 3 people messaging back and forth is a conversation. one person stops replying and just reads, still a conversation with 2. one person sending messages into the void is not a conversation, it's posting. but i feel like there's actually a sharp cutoff: if nobody responds within 24 hours it's dead, but at 25 hours suddenly it's alive again if they reply. that feels wrong but i still believe it.

2. playlist as album: 10 songs flowing together is an album experience. shuffle in 5 other songs and it's broken. insert one song every other track... at some point it's not an album, it's just a playlist. but i don't feel any hidden line, it's just blurry all the way.

3. coffee shop regular: every day for a year, you're a regular. every other day, still regular. once a month? maybe. once a year? definately not. but there's that middle zone where the barista knows your name but you don't come in enough. i want to call that 'semi-regular' which is basically admitting there's a middle state that isn't either.

4. digital painting: starts with 1000 brush strokes, remove one and it's still the painting. keep going to one stroke, that's just a line, not a painting. but now imagine i don't remove strokes, i shift every stroke's color 0.1% toward red each step. after enough steps it's all red and not the original painting at all. every step looks basically like the last. is there a flip point? i say no, it's all gradual.

5. language: english speakers slowly pronounce 'cat' closer to 'cut' over 200 years. i think it's still english, just a dialect, never a sharp break. but if they all suddenly switch to only emojis? then it's not english anymore. that jump feels totally different from the slow sound shift.

so my verdicts: chat has a hidden sharp cutoff (the 24 hour thing), playlist is pure blur with no line, regular-status has an admitted middle category, color-shift painting is pure blur, language is gradual except the emoji case which is a sudden jump.

these feel contradictory. sometimes i want sharp lines, sometimes blur, sometimes a middle zone. is there one principle about vagueness that captures all five? if not, which verdict do i have to drop? especially the chat one where i insist on a cutoff even though logically there shouldn't be one. and what would the principle say about a law that slowly changes word by word over centuries - is it still the same law?
