I want to understand exactly what is being claimed in each case.

Please avoid treating this as merely an exegesis of one canonical philosopher; I'm interested in the full spectrum from platitude to radical thesis, and in whether someone comparing wrong neural-network loss landscapes is invoking the same concept at all.
