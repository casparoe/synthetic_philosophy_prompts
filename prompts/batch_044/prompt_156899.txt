National character fails mainly because of internal heterogeneity: any nation contains different regions, classes, and subcultures, so attributing a single trait like punctuality to all members is a statistical error—specifically the ecological fallacy, where group-level correlations are wrongly projected onto every individual. Once we distinguish distributive properties (held by each person) from systemic properties (held by the interaction network), the coherence objection collapses. So national character is coherent only in this systemic, modal form, and generalizations are legitimate provided they target emergent patterns rather than individual psyches.
