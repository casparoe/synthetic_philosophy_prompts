I have been working through Hobbes's geometric writings, especially his claim that all figures must be generated by continuous bodily motion and his corresponding rejection of Wallis's non-constructive Euclidean proofs. This seems to entail that, for Hobbes, any legitimate concept must ultimately reduce to a mechanical process of physical bodies. Applying that to his political philosophy, I take the social contract to be not an abstract normative agreement but a literal kinematic aggregation of human bodies in space. Where does this reading go wrong? Please answer in at most four sentences, putting the identification of the false presupposition in your opening line.
