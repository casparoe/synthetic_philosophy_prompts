I am a doctoral student in philosophy of mind with a focus on early modern epistemology, and I am trying to refine the pedagogical framing of Descartes’s cogito for a dissertation chapter. The standard brain-in-a-vat and dreaming-demon variations tend to flatten the interpretive landscape into a simple binary—either the subject knows she exists or she does not—whereas the textual and scholarly debate actually turns on three distinguishable claims: (i) the minimal occurrence reading, according to which the cogito establishes only that a particular thinking event is occurring, with no licensed inference to a persisting subject; (ii) the moderate existence reading, according to which it establishes that I exist as a thinking thing at that precise moment, but remains silent on whether I am a substance with an enduring essence; and (iii) the substantialist reading, according to which it establishes that I am a res cogitans, a substance whose entire nature consists in thinking.

Please construct a thought experiment engineered to separate these three verdicts. The scenario should be realistic—set in a clinical neuropsychology or cognitive-rehabilitation context rather than in science fiction—and should be presentable in a single paragraph. Design it around a patient or cognitive system that can genuinely report, in the first person, an occurrent awareness of thinking, yet in which surrounding conditions—perhaps involving disrupted autobiographical consolidation, distributed or transient neural implementation, or the absence of diachronic narrative integration—make it structurally ambiguous whether there is a mere mental occurrence, a momentary existing thinker, or a simple substance whose essence is thought. The divergence among the three readings must fall out of their internal criteria rather than being stipulated by an explicit premise such as “there is no self.” After the scenario, walk through the verdict each interpretation delivers and why; identify the precise structural feature of the case that does the isolating work; explain what happens to the argument if that feature is removed; and provide an honest note on how a defender of the substantialist reading would likely attack the case—probably by denying that the reported state qualifies as genuine cogitatio, or by appealing to the simplicity and indivisibility of the soul to reject distributed or transient implementation. Please avoid bibliographies, citation lists, or requests for further reading; I need the reasoning to stand on its own conceptual structure.
