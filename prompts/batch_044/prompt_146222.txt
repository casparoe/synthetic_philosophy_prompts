This strikes me as structurally analogous to debates about hindsight bias and the narrative fallacy in historical explanation, but I lack the conceptual vocabulary to map where my intuitions sit relative to the philosophical positions. I want a decision-tree taxonomy—built from logical splits rather than from a list of author names—that I could use with colleagues to sort out what we actually believe about retrospective explanation.

Please construct a taxonomy tree for the debate over hindsight bias and narrative fallacy in causal and historical explanation.
