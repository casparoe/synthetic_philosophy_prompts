Write a one-semester independent-study proposal (approximately 1,800 words, philosophy of biology) that takes a definite, argued position on whether the DNA–Gödel isomorphism is structurally sound or breaks down once the physical conditions of replication are made explicit. Frame the central question precisely: does the self-referential structure of Gödel numbering genuinely map onto genetic self-replication, or does the analogy depend on conflating two distinct kinds of “typogenetic closure”?

To keep the question tractable, inject and defend the following distinction, which standard treatments tend to blur. Define strong typogenetic closure as a system in which the replicator directly encodes the machinery that interprets it—analogous to a Gödel sentence that literally contains, within its own formal representation, the rules of the proof system that generates it. Define weak typogenetic closure as a system in which the decoder is physically and informationally decoupled from the replicator but co-evolved with it—analogous to DNA, which does not encode the ribosome or polymerase within its own sequence, but depends on them for interpretation and replication.

State the proposal’s committed thesis: weak closure is the only physically realizable form, and therefore the DNA–Gödel comparison fails at exactly the point where Gödel’s result requires direct self-embedding rather than distributed co-dependence. Explain why treating both as instances of the same isomorphism obscures whether biological replication achieves semantic closure in the formal sense.

In the proposal, do the following: (1) articulate the question so that it has a determinate answer rather than a survey of opinions; (2) explain why existing philosophical and biological discussions leave it open—specifically, that they slide between formal self-reference and causal self-maintenance without examining the architectural difference; (3) describe the method: a conceptual analysis comparing the causal architecture of DNA replication (template, decoder, energy source, error correction) with the formal architecture of Gödel numbering (numeral, formula, proof predicate, diagonalization), identifying the precise step at which decoupling breaks the analogy; (4) specify what the deliverable looks like (a conceptual map locating where the analogy holds, where it collapses, and what this implies for whether life is essentially informational or essentially causal); (5) state honestly what would count as failure—e.g., if the analysis reveals that decoupling can in principle be eliminated in a physical system, or that Gödel numbering itself permits distributed interpretation that mirrors the DNA-ribosome separation; and (6) assess realistic scope for one semester, including which parts of the analysis are conceptual and which, if any, require minimal engagement with molecular biology that can be handled without primary scientific literature.

Do not include a bibliography, reading list, or citation requests; refer to positions and arguments by name or description, and note that citations will be filled in separately. Keep the tone formal and suitable for a committee evaluating feasibility and originality, and take the definite position described above rather than laying out options neutrally.
