I have been thinking about scalable oversight through debate, and I am puzzled by what seems like a built-in failure mode. The proposal is that a weak overseer can supervise a stronger system by judging a debate between two strong instances, yet if the overseer is genuinely weaker, it seems they must be vulnerable to arguments that exploit their cognitive limits—sophisticated but misleading reasoning that they lack the capacity to dissect. If we then train the debaters via recursive reward modelling, where each level optimises for the approval of the level below, would the stronger debater not inevitably learn to target the overseer’s specific biases rather than to reveal truth? Given this, is debate not just a more elaborate form of reward hacking, and ought we not to abandon it in favour of direct mechanistic interpretability?
