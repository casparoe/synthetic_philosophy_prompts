The policy context is a workfare system imposing job-search requirements and fraud-detection protocols, justified partly by the claim that unconditional benefits reward the 'undeserving poor.'

Rank these (or substitute your own upstream questions) by which resolution would do the most work for institutional design: which is upstream of the others, which is tractable through conceptual analysis alone, and which, if settled, would actually alter policy architecture rather than just rhetoric. Defend your ordering with explicit criteria—dependency, tractability, practical leverage—and explain what argument or conceptual clarification over the next few years would change your ranking. I am not looking for a literature survey or citations; I want a defended agenda for limited conceptual effort.
