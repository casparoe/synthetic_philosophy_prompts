In applying Dennett's four-level taxonomy of minds, my professor has delivered these verdicts: C. elegans is Darwinian despite limited plasticity; Skinner's pigeons are Skinnerian; a chess program that searches internal move trees but possesses no cultural tools is Popperian; a human mathematician using symbolic notation and peer collaboration is Gregorian; and a pre-linguistic human infant who solves physical puzzles by mental trial-and-error is Popperian, not Gregorian, because language and cultural scaffolding are absent. Next is Nexus-7, a bioengineered slime-mold colony whose individual nodes operate on hardwired tropisms and local reinforcement, but which collectively inherits—via horizontal gene transfer and chemical tags—a modifiable symbolic map of maze layouts that persists across generations and improves with collective success, even though no single node internally represents the environment. Predict her classification of Nexus-7 as Darwinian, Skinnerian, Popperian, or Gregorian. Identify the two competing principles her track record supports: one that requires internal representational modeling for Popperian status and treats Gregorian cognition as culture-augmented Popperian; and one that permits heritable, collective symbolic storage to qualify as Gregorian even without individual internal models. State which principle she is implicitly running, justify your choice, and name the single further case that would settle the matter between them.
