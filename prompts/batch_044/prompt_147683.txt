Be honest about where the reading breaks down—especially regarding Crow's self-engineered death, Anderton's drug addiction as a competing first-order drive, and the system's reliance on Agatha's dissenting visions elsewhere—and say whether the movie ultimately adds something to the debate about moral responsibility that a standard armchair Frankfurt case cannot capture.
