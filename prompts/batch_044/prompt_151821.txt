Second, the only available remedy—the so-called skeptical solution—is to abandon the idea that meaning is an objective property and to treat it instead as a social contract maintained by collective linguistic approval, which provides justification for attributing rules without appealing to any independent reality. Third, and here is where the summary takes a turn toward my own beat, this framework allegedly dissolves the distinction between human language use and large-scale statistical language modeling: if understanding is nothing more than conformity to communal norms, then a neural network trained solely on distributional patterns in a text corpus possesses understanding in precisely the same sense that a human speaker does, and the absence of internal representations becomes philosophically irrelevant rather than a deficiency.
