State your credence that “Gen Z” denotes a real kind with modal properties—there being something it is essentially like to belong—rather than a purely extensional demographic slice fixed by birth years. Decompose the reasoning into four parts: (i) the prior that socially constituted human groups can have essential properties at all; (ii) the likelihood that a shared formative technological environment produces stable, cross-culturally invariant psychological dispositions; (iii) the likelihood that birth-year boundaries track those dispositions rather than cutting across continuous variation; and (iv) whether essentialism about human groups is conceptually coherent given developmental plasticity. Assign each part a rough probability, show how they combine into an overall number, and identify which component your final credence is most sensitive to and what conceptual or empirical finding would shift it most.
