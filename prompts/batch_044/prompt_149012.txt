I want the answer structured as a clear menu of interpretations with verdicts on each, not a single favored reading. Please put the bottom line up front in one sentence summarizing the most defensible normative core the proverb could carry. Write in American English, keep it under 350 words, and do not include citations, reading lists, or references to specific scholars I should look up.
