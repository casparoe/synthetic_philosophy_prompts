Assume Otto Neurath survived into the present and encountered synthetic pictographic reasoning: large language models that generate structured visual arguments through standardized, culture-neutral icon sets meant to enable literacy-independent scientific and civic deliberation. Drawing on his ISOTYPE project and his broader epistemological commitments, predict how he would assess this technology. Give your response as exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences, with no introduction or conclusion. Cover: (1) whether machine-generated pictograms fulfill or betray ISOTYPE’s democratic, anti-elitist purpose; (2) how his rejection of metaphysical language might apply to claims that these images convey meaning without any linguistic framing; (3) whether cross-cultural icon standardization is feasible or merely reproduces the linguistic fragmentation he sought to overcome; (4) his view on integrating such images into scientific protocol sentences within a unified science; and (5) whether removing the human visual designer strips picture languages of the pedagogical and political agency he assigned them.
