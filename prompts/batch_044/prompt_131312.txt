I've been arguing with my flatmate about whether famines are ever genuinely "natural" or always political crimes, and I've ended up with a chain that seems to make the concept of famine do something backwards. Amartya Sen's entitlement work on the 1943 Bengal famine showed food availability per head was actually higher than in previous non-famine years — people died because wartime inflation and colonial policy destroyed their access rights, not because there was no rice. The Irish case looks like nature because of the blight, but the mass mortality came from continued grain exports and the Poor Law's design; and the Great Leap Forward was explicit requisition despite adequate harvests. So here's the argument, which feels suspicious because the conclusion is weird:

(1) Sen's entitlement theory shows famine is never caused by Food Availability Decline (FAD) but always by entitlement collapse.
(2) Entitlement collapse is always mediated by instutions — markets, laws, state policy — so it is always a political failure.
(3) Therefore every famine is a political crime, not a natural disaster.
(4) To be consistent we should define famine precisely as mass starvation occuring despite adequate aggregate food, caused by entitlement failure.
(5) By that definiton, if a drought genuinely destroys every crop in a stateless region and everyone starves equally, that is not a famine — it's just a natural catastrophe with no institutional mediator.
(6) So the worse the natural destruction (no food left anywhere), the less it counts as famine; the concept becomes inversely tied to natural causation.
(7) Therefore either we accept that "famine" excludes the worst natural food crises (absurd), or we admit natural causes can be sufficient, which undermines Sen's claim that entitlement collapse is the defining feature.

I really want to keep (3) — famines as political failures — and avoid the backwards result in (5)-(6). Which step should I reject, and what does giving it up cost? Is there a hidden slide between describing entitlement collapse and defining famine?
