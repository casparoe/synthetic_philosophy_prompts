Because Benatar's reasoning is typically anchored to specific procreative pairs and identifiable individuals, applying the asymmetry to these structural, synthetic, or contingent-population scenarios raises unresolved questions about how to weigh the neutral absence of pleasure against the positive absence of suffering when no particular being is being deprived of either.
