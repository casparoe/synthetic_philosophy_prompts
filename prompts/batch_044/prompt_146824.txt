im trying to apply nietzsche's three history modes—monumental, antiquarian, critical—to a project about cultural memory. it seems like critical history is the only one thats actually life-affirming, since it destroys the dead weight of the past, whereas monumental just glorifies old heroes and antiquarian is just hoarding artifacts. if the point is to serve life, shouldnt a serious historian just reject the other two? mixing them feels like it just dilutes the critical force.
