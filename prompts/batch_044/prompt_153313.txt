Basically moral standing tracks this kind of recoverable narrative continuty rather than metabolic persistence. Play it straight: what counts against it? I want the evidence and arguments that should move me, sorted by decisive vs merely awkward, including analogies to sleep or anesthesia in biological minds and whether this view collapses into regular psychological continuity once you strip away the tech jargon. Also, has it been qualified into unfalsifiability—if every counterexample gets answered with "well the state wasn't properly preserved," is that a problem for a thesis of this kind?
