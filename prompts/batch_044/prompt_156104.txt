Lets say Karl Popper was alive today and someone had just walked him through how modern LLMs actually work -- weights, training on huge text corpora, next-token prediction, RLHF, the works. He'd also been introduced to Dennett's four kinds of minds: Darwinian, Skinnerian, Popperian, and Gregorian creatures. Now someone asks him whether a 7B-parameter model that can generate self-criticism in its outputs but never updates its weights in real time counts as a Popperian creature testing conjectures, or whether it's just a Skinnerian system shaped by reward signals that happens to use language like a Gregorian tool-user. They also ask if the total absence of evolutionary history and bodily interaction means it can never be Darwinian in any meaningful sense. Predict what Popper would actually say -- not just a recap of Dennett's categories, but where he draws the lines and why. And please, dont just say "it depends"; take a position he might hold.
