I'm trying to figure out what I actually believe about consciousness being one thing or many things, and I've been using Schrödinger's old Vedanta-flavored idea as a kind of mental foil—y'know, the view that there's basically one universal awareness and all these seperate minds are kind of an illusion, like waves thinking they're separate from the ocean. Anyway here are my gut calls on cases and I want to know if there's one principle behind them or if I'm just being messy.

When I'm talking to my friend at a cafe, it really feels like there are two genuinely different streams of experience, not one consciousness arguing with itself. So multiplicity seems real at the everyday level. But then if I imagine dying and my brain stopping, it doesn't feel like a unique private light gets permanently blown out; more like a local pattern dissolving back into something that was never actually divided up. So personal death feels less like annihilation of a seperate soul and more like a shape ending. Now take split-brain patients—when the hemispheres start disagreeing about what they want, I still want to say it's one person being pulled apart, not two new people being born out of nowhere. But cloning gives me the opposite reaction: if you made a perfect atom-for-atom copy of me, I'd say that's definitely a second person, not me existing in two bodies at once. And if I lost every single memory and personality trait but my body kept breathing with a new mind inside, I'd say I'm gone, even though there's still consciousness happening in that skull. So what principle, if any, gets all these? Does it have to do with functional integration versus physical duplication, or with whether there's a continuous 'thread' that has to stay biological? And please tell me what it predicts for a case I haven't listed—like if two people gradually merged into one shared stream of awareness through some tech, or if my consciousness could genuinely split into two that both feel like 'me'. I also need to know which of my verdicts I'd have to drop if I want a simple principle, because right now they feel all over the place.
