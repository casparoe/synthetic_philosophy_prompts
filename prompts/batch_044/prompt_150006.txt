Before I assess the competing responses—Scanlon’s own appeal to weighted reasons, the Taurek-style denial that numbers count, and more recent “threshold contractualist” proposals that try to make the force of a reason sensitive to how many others share an analogous claim—I want to establish the standard of evaluation itself.

Please write the full specification of desiderata that any adequate contractualist resolution of the aggregation problem must satisfy. Then identify any subset of these desiderata that appears jointly unsatisfiable in light of Scanlon’s commitment to pairwise justifiability, and briefly explain the tension. Finally, provide a concise scorecard—no more than a short paragraph for each—of how Scanlon’s own view, Taurek’s position, and a threshold-sensitive contractualist approach fare against your list, but keep the conceptual weight on the criteria rather than on the scoring. Do not provide a bibliography, reading list, or citations; I need this to be usable without external access to the literature.
