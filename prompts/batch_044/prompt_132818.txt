Read Duncan Jones's 2009 film Moon—where Sam Bell, an isolated lunar worker, gradually discovers that he is not the original employee but one in a long sequence of activated clones, each implanted with the original's memories—through the lens of the self-sampling and self-indication assumptions in anthropic reasoning, especially as they figure in the presumptuous philosopher argument. Treat Sam's dawning realization that he belongs to a large reference class of physically and psychologically identical observers as a dramatization of self-locating belief: does he implicitly reason like a self-sampler, distributing credence uniformly over the clones, or like a self-indicator, taking the very existence of many copies as evidence that he is likely one of them? But push the reading further into a niche conceptual twist the film introduces: because each clone is awakened with the same first-person memory traces and initially believes itself to be the original, the reference class is not composed of independent observers with distinct evidence, but of psychologically continuous replicas sharing identical indexical data. Ask whether the presumptuous philosopher's reasoning—normally applied to cosmological populations—breaks down or requires reformulation when the multiplicity is manufactured, sequential rather than simultaneous, and when the observers are not epistemically isolated but linked by a shared false biography. Where does the film illuminate the difference between epistemic uncertainty about which clone one is and ontological claims about whether there is a "real" Sam Bell? Where does it risk conflating the ethics of awakening a disposable worker with the logic of anthropic selection? And finally, what does Moon add to the philosophical debate that a purely abstract treatment of reference classes misses—perhaps something about the affective weight of discovering one's own replaceability, or about how institutional deception structures the very evidence available for self-location? I want a reading that takes both the film's narrative mechanics and the technical distinction between sampling assumptions seriously, and that is honest about where the analogy between clone duplication and cosmological observer selection holds and where it collapses.
