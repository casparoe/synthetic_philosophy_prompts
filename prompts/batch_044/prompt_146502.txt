Specifically: if such a formalization has a countable model, does that undermine the claim that the ontology genuinely contains uncountably many worlds, and does this collapse into the standard set-theoretic version or reveal a distinct kind of referential indeterminacy?

Write the proposal (about one semester, roughly 6000 words). Please do not request or include a bibliography; I will supply references myself. The proposal should defend why this question is open and worth a semester's work.
