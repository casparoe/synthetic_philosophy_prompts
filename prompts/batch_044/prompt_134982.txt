I need you to disentangle what this slogan could actually be claiming.

Please lay out the distinct precise theses it might express, arranged as a menu from the trivial or definitional to the metaphysically radical. For each reading, specify exactly what it asserts about ontology (whether a self exists and in what sense), about psychology (what capacities—memory, agency, temporal integration—are required), and about artificial systems (whether the thesis allows, forbids, or is neutral on an AI possessing a self). Indicate which readings are platitudes, which are substantive but defensible, and which are false or self-undermining.

Then, do not leave me with a neutral list: take a committed position. Tell me which one or two readings are genuinely supported by the philosophical work behind the phrase, which are distortions imported to win arguments about AI, and specifically which reading is being invoked when the slogan is used either to dismiss human exceptionalism or to deny AI potential. Give explicit reasons for each verdict, and state clearly whether the slogan—on its best, most defensible interpretation—actually supports the conclusion it was being made to reach in those technology debates.
