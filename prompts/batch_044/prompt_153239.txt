the tree must have these leaves: (a) hard incompatibilist—PAP is true and frankfurt cases fail; (b) flicker defender—there is always a prior mental sign that counts as an alternative; (c) hierarchical compatibilist—what matters is jones second-order endorsement, blocker is irrelevant; (d) actual-sequence source view—responsibility tracks being the actual causal source, not available alternatives; (e) one empty combination you should flag.

diagnostic nodes (phrased as yes/no about the case, not about theories): 1) is there a detectable mental flicker before the choice that could have triggered the chip? 2) does the chip monitor internal deliberation or only external behavior? 3) does jones have a second-order desire that endorses voting democrat?

map every combination of answers onto the logical space first, then place the four real positions in it. identify the empty slot—probably the combo where there is no flicker, the chip reads internal states, jones lacks second-order endorsement, yet he is still responsible via pure sourcehood. diagnose whether thats incoherent or just unexplored.

also flag any pair of leaves your tree genuinely cant seperate—like maybe c and d collapse on some branch? be honest about it.
