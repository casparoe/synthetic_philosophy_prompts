I have been thinking about Ryle's distinction between knowing how and knowing that in light of inverse reinforcement learning. If an artificial agent can acquire a procedural skill—say, navigating an environment—solely by inferring a reward function from observed behaviour, does this not show that the agent's competence is really just a form of knowing that certain actions maximise that function? And if so, does this not undermine Ryle's anti-intellectualist claim, at least for any system (human or artificial) whose behaviour can be modelled this way? I would appreciate an explanation of where this reasoning goes wrong, written in British English.
