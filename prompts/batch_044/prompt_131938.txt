Then say which of these readings actually dissolve the Meno problem (that knowledge is more valuable than true belief) and which merely change the subject, and indicate whether the protein-folding AI case genuinely illustrates the reading or smuggles in a hidden assumption about what “guidance” requires. Present the results as a menu I can use in discussion, flag any pair of readings that are jointly unsatisfiable, and avoid treating the slogan as if it named one single claim.
