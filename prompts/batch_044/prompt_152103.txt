Jordan said that was a strawman: Szasz never denied that brain diseases exist, he just said the category "mental illness" is conceptually incoherent because it applies medical language to problems in living, like heartbreak or existantial dread. Alex came back that Jordan was smuggling in hidden norms about what counts as a "real" disease — modern neuroscience finds hippocampal shrinkage in chronic PTSD and clear dopaminergic dysfunction in psychosis, so if Szasz's test is "no visible lesion" then he's been empirically defeated for loads of conditions.
