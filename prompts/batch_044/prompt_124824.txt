I am a doctoral student in philosophy of language, and in preparation for a guest discussion section I am leading for an advanced undergraduate seminar on metaphysics and social ontology, I have been trying to work out—after a long conversation with my advisor—how to present the sorites paradox as a structural threat to gradable institutional properties rather than as the familiar puzzle about sand. Specifically, I want the session to center on the concept of binding legal authority and to use as its carrying case the gradual judicial erosion of a landmark precedent: successive lower-court distinctions chip away every substantive feature of the original holding until the ruling remains technically intact but materially hollow, without any formal moment of overturning. The students will arrive assuming that vagueness is merely linguistic imprecision correctable by sharper drafting, and I anticipate they will propose that fuzzy-logic frameworks dissolve the difficulty without metaphysical cost; I therefore need the pedagogical architecture that specifies the exact dependency order—what must be secured about the distinction between semantic indeterminacy and ontological gradation before I can even introduce the legal example—together with the precise calibration the carrying case requires so that it preserves the logical structure of the paradox while lacking the intuitive heap imagery that lets students dismiss it as an artifact of ordinary language.

In addition, I need the misconception map with the point in the sequence where each predictable error should be intercepted, a single diagnostic question I can pose at the end to determine whether they have grasped the structural threat to institutional identity rather than merely followed the legal narrative, and an explicit declaration of what formal machinery—such as degree-theoretic semantics or supervaluationist models—I should openly refuse to explain and set aside as beyond this level. I am not asking for an explanation of the paradox itself, nor for a bibliography; I am asking for the teaching plan alone: the ordered introduction of conceptual components, the properties the carrying example must have and must lack, the interception points for misconceptions, the check for genuine understanding, and the clearly marked omissions.
