The examination is 60 minutes long and the question carries 25 marks. Provide a model answer that executes the required philosophical moves with precision, and supply a marking scheme built around five must-hit points (with marks assigned, totalling 25) plus a one-line note on how candidates typically fail each point. Please present the question, model answer, and marking scheme in British English, and do not include bibliographies, reading lists, citations, or appeals to specific named texts.
