Write a dialogue between a spacetime substantivalist and a relationist about Newton's bucket, modified as follows: in an otherwise empty universe, two identical buckets of water rotate with the same angular velocity relative to each other, but one is situated in a region with periodic boundary conditions while the other lies in an open region, with no distant matter anywhere. Each speaker should address whether the water's surface concavity could differ between them, and whether that result rescues or damages the relationist claim that rotation is defined only relative to other bodies. Deliver exactly five numbered exchanges, each one or two sentences long, with no introduction or conclusion; both interlocutors must be equally sharp and reply directly to the specific point just raised.
