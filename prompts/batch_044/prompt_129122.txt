I’ve been working through decision-theoretic rationality after a seminar on repeated gambles, and I’ve come to defend a stronger version of the Kelly position than is usually articulated: not merely that geometric growth maximization is prudent for investors, but that it reveals the underlying normative structure of rational agency whenever capital—whether monetary, cognitive, or reputational—can be reinvested. I hold that arithmetic expected-utility theory applies only to terminal, non-compounding choices, whereas geometric rationality, which maximizes expected logarithmic utility, is binding for any genuinely sequential agent. I have begun extending this into epistemic practice, treating belief revision as the allocation of fractions of one’s “credibility capital” across competing hypotheses, with updates that should multiply rather than add; on this view, dogmatic linear confidence adjustments are not just imprudent but structurally irrational, analogous to overbetting and facing eventual ruin.

What would genuinely count against this view? I want more than the standard objections about risk aversion or bankruptcy avoidance. Which formal results—say, in non-ergodic environments or with incommensurable value dimensions—would have to hold for geometric rationality to fail as the normative standard? Which empirical patterns in actual human or institutional decision-making, and which conceptual distinctions between instrumental and constitutive rationality, would need to be established? If defenders can always retreat to “that context was one-shot” or “that agent lacked reinvestable capital,” has the position been qualified into unfalsifiability, and is that insulation a virtue or a vice for a theory claiming structural authority over sequential choice? Please rank the potential defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, indicate which ones proponents of geometric rationality would themselves concede as damaging rather than dismissible, and assess whether there remains any live escape route that leaves arithmetic rationality genuinely competitive rather than relegated to a limiting special case.
