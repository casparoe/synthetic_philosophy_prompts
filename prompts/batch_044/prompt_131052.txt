By Gödel’s second incompleteness theorem, a consistent formal system strong enough for arithmetic cannot prove its own consistency; and by Löb’s theorem, if such a system can establish the reflection principle that provability implies truth—the very principle your view of self-trust seems to require—then it can derive its own consistency, which a consistent system cannot do.
