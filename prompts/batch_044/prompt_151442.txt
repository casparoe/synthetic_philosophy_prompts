Keep the weight on the criteria and their motivating cases; then, only briefly, indicate how the main families of approach—functionalist decision-theoretic treatments, causal-decision-theoretic accounts, and frameworks that treat embedding as a logical rather than physical relation—fare against the list. Do not provide bibliographies, reading lists, or citations; I require a specification that stands independent of any particular source.
