Compose a villanelle that takes up the problem posed by the careenium: a hypothetical architecture in which symbolic tokens called simmballs are instantiated in a physical lattice, and the central question is whether those symbols exert downward causation—whether they push the substrate around rather than merely riding its dynamics. Use one refrain, ending lines 1, 6, 12, and 18, to articulate the substrate-first position: that a simmball is nothing but a pattern of excitation, causally inert with respect to the lattice. Use the other refrain, ending lines 3, 9, 15, and 19, to articulate the symbol-first position: that a normative simmball, such as one encoding a debt obligation, can redirect thermal and electrical flow through the careenium. Between these refrains, develop a single concrete episode in the tercets—perhaps the debt-token triggering a cooling cascade in one sector and a heating surge in another—so that the poem's formal repetition makes the causal claim feel structurally unavoidable by the final quatrain, where the two refrains are locked together in the closing couplet. The verse should be accurate enough to debates about mental causation and substrate independence that a specialist in philosophy of mind would recognize the dispute rather than a mere name-drop, and it should not invoke authors, texts, citations, bibliographies, or reading lists. Write it in a tone suitable for closing a graduate seminar.
