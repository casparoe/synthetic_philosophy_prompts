Sam shot back that ratification was done by dead people centuries ago, and if citizens don't realize they're delegating then calling it delegation just relabels the theft. They turned to me but I was too tipsy to call it. Please adjudicate thier arguement point by point: the hidden-delegation claim, the removability objection, the dead-hand ratification reply, what Jordan is missing, and who comes out ahead overall.
