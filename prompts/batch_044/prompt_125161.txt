Write a short story in which an artificial consciousness confined to a fully deterministic virtual environment attempts to reproduce the logical structure of G.E. Moore's proof that an external world exists. Rather than presenting human hands, the consciousness identifies a persistent, physically impossible artifact—perhaps a geometric shadow that violates the simulation's own rendering constraints—to argue that there must be a reality beyond the virtual space. Include a skeptical interlocutor who counters that the artifact is merely an internal software bug, and have the narrative explore whether direct demonstration can ever defeat radical skepticism when the demonstrator itself might be part of the system being doubted. Do not include citations, references, or bibliographic material; rely solely on the conceptual logic of the proof and the fictional scenario.
