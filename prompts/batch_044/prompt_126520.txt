Maimonides clearly thinks God only commanded sacrifices because Israelites worshipped animals and stars, so he let them keep killing things but changed the target — now for God not Baal. Once the Temple falls, prayer replaces sacrifice entirely because prayer is the true internal service with no phyiscal mediation at all. In fact Maimonides secretly agrees with Nahmanides that smoke and blood feed spiritual energies that keep the cosmos running, he just hides this behind rationalist talk for the masses. After exil that channel is broken, so prayer is the only way left. Therefore the pedagogical role of sacrifice is completely dead now; it was always just temporary scafolding.
