Please write the exam question, a model answer of approximately 700 words that takes a committed position—either defending the framing’s salvageability with explicit restrictions on the counterfactual baseline, or arguing that it collapses and should be replaced by a different explanatory structure—and a marking scheme that distinguishes an A answer from a B by naming the specific conceptual moves each must contain (for example: distinguishing explanandum selection from counterfactual licensing, identifying the baseline problem, applying interventionist conditions to macrohistorical comparison, and addressing the strongest objection) together with the typical errors that should cost marks. The model answer should argue for its conclusion rather than laying out options neutrally.
