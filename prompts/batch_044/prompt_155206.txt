The story should not resolve this with a simple moral, but should make clear that the "man of his time" claim is not merely about temporal location but about the agent's relationship to dissent that was already inside their world. Write entirely in continuous prose: avoid headings, bullet points, bold text, tables, and any typographical breaks that would fragment the narrative flow. Begin with a brief conceptual framing that establishes the philosophical stakes, then move into the narrative, and maintain a tone appropriate for serious philosophical fiction rather than popular drama.
