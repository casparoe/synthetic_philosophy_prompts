I run a discussion session for a rationalist reading group—mostly software engineers and researchers with no formal philosophy background—on the idea that human extinction is bad partly because it destroys the collective future that gives our present projects meaning, not merely because of the deaths involved. Only after that is secured should you introduce the conjecture that we implicitly depend on a kind of collective afterlife—future generations carrying forward what matters to us now. Explain exactly what this example must have and what it must lack—specifically, why it should not involve any direct harm to her, and why it should involve a project she regards as valuable independently of her own survival.
