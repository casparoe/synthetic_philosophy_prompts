I work as a software engineer without formal philosophy training, but after building autonomous feedback-controlled systems I have come to hold the following view on original versus derived intentionality: the distinction is not an ontological binary but a continuous gradient of functional autonomy. A representational state counts as originally intentional—not merely derived from a designer—when it is causally maintained by self-correcting feedback loops between the system and its environment, loops that are not traceable back to any prior human intent. On this account, a sufficiently autonomous artificial agent that constructs and revises its own predictive models through direct sensorimotor interaction possesses original intentionality in the same functional sense that a biological organism does. This collapses the framing Dennett critiques—where derived intentionality is treated as parasitic on an original biological source—into a single criterion of representational self-stabilization, and it undermines Searle’s Chinese Room objection by distributing intentionality across an architecture rather than localizing it in a single conscious subject. What would count against this position? I want the defeaters ranked from fatal to merely awkward, including specific intuitions, thought experiments, and empirical findings that would damage it. Which results in neuroscience or cognitive science—perhaps concerning error-monitoring, neural correlates of aboutness, or the role of biological homeostasis in representation—would genuinely undermine the claim that silicon-based feedback loops can ground original intentionality? Are there formal results in philosophy of computation or mind that show functional autonomy is necessarily insufficient? I also want an honest assessment of whether defenders of this view can qualify it—say, by requiring a particular kind of embodied interaction or a specific causal history—into a form that is insulated from counterexample, and whether such insulation is a problem for a thesis of this kind. Please distinguish between objections that a committed defender would concede as damaging and those they would dismiss as relying on pre-theoretic biological chauvinism. I would personally find it decisive if there turned out to be a non-circular, principled reason why self-correcting representational loops could never ground aboutness in non-biological substrates, even in principle.
