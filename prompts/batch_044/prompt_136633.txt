I have noticed a recurring pattern when people use AI systems for sustained intellectual work—working through a philosophical argument, say, or a lengthy proof. At each individual step, the user can still assess whether a claim is sound or an inference valid; they have not suspended their critical faculties. Yet over time they seem to lose the ability to hold the entire structure in mind unaided. The connections between steps—the overall architecture—remain available only through continued interaction with the system. This is not mere outsourcing of thought, since local evaluation is retained, nor is it a straightforward collaboration, because the user has not internalised the global framework well enough to reproduce it independently. Is there an established term for this precise configuration, where local understanding stays intact but integrative, global understanding becomes distributed and dependent on the tool? If existing discussions only address it under wider headings, how exactly should it be distinguished from ordinary cognitive division of labour, and what would a suitable name need to capture about its boundaries?
