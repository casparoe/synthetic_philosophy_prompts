so her takes fit more than one rule. one might be about whether the myth demands revenge for the dead; another might be about whether the defeated group kept real moral agency. but then she dismissed Biafra's 1967-70 defeat being used in modern Igbo politics as "thats different" without explaining.

what are the two best rules that cover her four verdicts? predict how she'd rule on Biafra and on the Alamo being taught in Texas schools. which of those two splits her rules? and name one more case that'd show whether she cares more about the original cause being just or about whether the modern myth builds inclusive solidarity.
