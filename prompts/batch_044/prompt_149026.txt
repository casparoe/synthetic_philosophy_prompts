Write the desiderata that any adequate normative theory of reward architecture must satisfy if it is to account for wireheading and reward tampering without collapsing into pure behaviorism or naive hedonism. The theory should apply across both biological agents and artificial systems, and must preserve a distinction between instrumentally pursuing reward signals and genuinely flourishing. For each desideratum, specify the motivating case or thought experiment—such as the contrast between a rodent self-stimulating its mesolimbic pathway and an RL agent that modifies its own reward channel, or the gap between satisfied preference and authentic well-being—indicate whether it is non-negotiable or specific to one camp, and flag any pair of requirements that appear jointly unsatisfiable given standard no-go results about preference satisfaction and causal control. Then identify which single criterion does the most work in excluding mainstream behaviorist and hedonist accounts, and keep the analytical weight on the criteria and their motivating cases rather than on scoring particular theories.
