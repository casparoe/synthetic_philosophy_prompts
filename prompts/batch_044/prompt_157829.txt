I want to know which stance yields the more coherent pattern of verdicts across these cases, whether there is a deeper principle—perhaps about explanatory power versus ontological commitment—that explains why they diverge, and how each stance would treat a hypothetical system that is perfectly predictable from the outside but whose internal dynamics are provably uninterpretable even in principle.
