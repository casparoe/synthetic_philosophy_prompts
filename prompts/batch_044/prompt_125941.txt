My hunch is that this isn't really about politics — it's about whether moral emotions track actual wrongness, or whether wrong just means violates a rule regardless of context. Or maybe one side is asking what should the agent do and the other is asking what should the institutional role permit. But I want an actual diagnosis, not just my guess.
