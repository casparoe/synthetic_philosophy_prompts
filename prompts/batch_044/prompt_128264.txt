Deliver this as a numbered list of exactly five items, each one or two sentences long, with no introduction or conclusion, covering in order: the deficit measurement and weighted allocation rule; the levelling-down veto step; Case A comparing imminent eviction with lack of textbooks, where the procedure denies the textbook applicant despite residual funds; Case B producing an equal-score tie the protocol cannot resolve; and the boundary at which the officer must defer to a values-based committee decision.
