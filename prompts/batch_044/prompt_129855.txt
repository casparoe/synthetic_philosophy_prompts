In our graduate epistemology seminar we have been comparing what we call “regress architectures”—linear chains, holistic webs, and lattice structures in which justification extends in multiple directions without a unique root. During our session on infinitism, a fellow student kept invoking the line: “If nothing is self-justifying, then nothing is justified.” She deployed it specifically against the lattice model, claiming that without a self-justifying base the entire mesh collapses into skepticism and that infinitism therefore fails.
