first, pushing one person off a bridge to stop a trolley killing five: definitely wrong. killing one healthy patient to harvest organs for five dying ones: also wrong, maybe even worse. but taxing rich people at like 40% to fund healthcare that saves thousands of poor people? that seems totally fine. same with mandatory vaccines that carry a tiny statistical risk for one in a hundred thousand but prevent mass death: okay in my book.
