I am preparing a seminar on David Lewis’s analysis of convention—particularly the claim that conventions are regularities in behavior sustained by common knowledge of mutual expectations, that they solve coordination problems by selecting among arbitrary alternatives, and that they carry a distinctive normative “ought”—but I want to move past standard exegesis. I am fluent in the vocabulary of predictive processing and active inference, including generative models, precision weighting, prediction-error minimization, and Markov blankets, and I would like you to restate Lewis’s core position systematically in those terms rather than in the usual game-theoretic or philosophical idiom.

Specifically, please work within a framing I am developing: treat conventions not as shared priors among homogeneous agents, but as inter-agent behavioral regularities that stabilize through coupled precision-weighting schemes across agents who maintain distinct generative models—what I am calling a heterogeneous consensus within a nested collective Markov blanket. Translate each pillar of Lewis’s view—coordination, mutual expectation, arbitrariness, and conventional normativity—into this vocabulary. Then deliver the residue report: which elements translate cleanly into coupled dynamics and free-energy minimization, which must be distorted to fit (for example, does the arbitrariness of conventional solutions survive once agent-specific priors have already been shaped by evolutionary and learning histories?), and which have no expression at all within this framework. Pay particular attention to whether Lewis’s normative dimension can be reconstructed as a precision-weighted prediction error about deviation without importing propositional attitudes, or whether it requires representational content that predictive processing cannot accommodate. Finally, assess whether the residue reveals a genuine limitation of the active-inference approach—perhaps its tendency to collapse conventionality into model homogeneity—or whether it shows that Lewis’s account was implicitly relying on coupled dynamical structures all along. Do not provide citations, bibliographies, or page references; I want to see how far the conceptual vocabulary can carry the argument on its own, and I need the translation kept honest, without letting the framework’s assumptions do work that Lewis assigns to common knowledge.
