There's this debate about Wittgenstein's hinges in On Certainty -- like whether saying "the earth existed long before me" is literally knowledge or just the frame that makes knowing possible. Feels like it's been stuck since the 80's with the same moves replayed. My hunch is one side treats hinges like firmware (you can't patch it while the OS runs) and the other side treats them like default setting's you could toggle with a reboot. So is this actually about epistemic status, or are we just smuggling in different system-level metaphors? And if it's the second, is there anything that could actually break the stall -- some formal model of belief revision, a distinction we missed -- or is it just bedrock vocabulary clash?
