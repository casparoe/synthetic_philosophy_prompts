Specifically, I'm weighing three lines of open work. Third, whether the framework can ground practical commitment devices for AI alignment—specifically, whether a system can commit to a verifiable program that conditions on human oversight inputs without being able to secretly deviate through hidden state—without requiring the overseer to solve the full verification problem for arbitrary code.
