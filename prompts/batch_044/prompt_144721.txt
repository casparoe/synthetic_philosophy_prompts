I'm part of this local group in a town that was literally built in 1978 around a factory. We started doing this autumn festival about ten years ago — it's a mix of old farming songs and a "blessing of the soil" thing our organizer pulled from a 1923 ethnography that later turnes out to be half fabricated. Now there's a fight: some people say only familes who lived here before 1990 can lead the ceremony, because they're the "real" community, and newcomers are just cosplaying heritage. I feel like that's wrong but I can't say why — the whole thing is obviously recent, yet it feels genuinely ours. Is there a principled way to decide who belongs without pretending we have ancient roots? Or is authenticity basically just shared belief?
