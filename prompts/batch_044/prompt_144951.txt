After presenting the scenario, provide a step-by-step demonstration that it meets the specifications: state the precise verdict each of the three views delivers and why; identify the specific structural feature of the case that forces psychological continuity and bodily continuity to converge on survival while allowing the narrative-integration criterion to split away; explain what happens to all three verdicts if that feature is removed; and then present the narrative-integration theorist’s best attack on the case—specifically, the charge that the scenario begs the question by stipulating a fractured first-person narrative rather than deriving it from independent conditions—together with a concise reply from a defender of one of the continuity views that does not merely reassert the continuity conditions but addresses the charge of question-begging directly. Keep the entire response focused on the engineered case and its analysis; do not provide a bibliography or ask for one.
