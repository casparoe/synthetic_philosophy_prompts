Please construct a realistic thought experiment, set in an ordinary domestic or professional environment and confined to a single paragraph, in which care ethics obliges a carer to sustain an intimate, supportive bond with a dependent person whose behaviour is causing tangible but non-catastrophic harm to others, whereas a Kantian deontological framework demands the immediate dissolution of that bond to protect the rights of those affected, and in which a straightforward consequentialist weighing of outcomes does not clearly settle the matter. After presenting the scenario, please demonstrate how each of the three approaches reaches its verdict, identify the precise structural feature that causes care ethics to diverge, explain whether removing that feature collapses the case into a familiar utilitarian dilemma, and outline how a proponent of the rights-based view might criticise the care-ethical conclusion.
