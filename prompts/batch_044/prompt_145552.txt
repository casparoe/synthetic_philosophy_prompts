Before constructing your answer, please spend a moment brainstorming the full landscape of open problems in origin essentialism — the view that a particular person essentially originates from the specific sperm and egg from which they actually developed, and that they could not have come from different gametes.

Your task is to advise a small interdisciplinary research group — comprising one metaphysician, one bioethicist, and a shared three-year funding window with no laboratory component — on which open questions or lines of work in this domain deserve priority. Consider the following lines of inquiry, though you should treat them as illustrative rather than exhaustive:

3. 4. Whether origin essentialism can ground a distinctive conception of genetic privacy or property rights in one’s own origin story, separate from standard biomedical ethics.
