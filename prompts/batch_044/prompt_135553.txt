The second split should ask whether moral weight, once present, scales continuously with the complexity of that integration or whether there is a sharp discontinuity — perhaps near the boundary between insects and mammals — below which obligations diminish drastically. If two established positions end up giving identical answers across all three branches so that the tree genuinely cannot separate them, flag that limitation explicitly rather than forcing an artificial distinction. Please present all of this in continuous prose only, with no headings, bullet points, bold text, or tables, written as though in a thoughtful email or letter. Finally, state how confident you are in the taxonomy you propose, and point out the specific branch or leaf about which you feel least sure.
