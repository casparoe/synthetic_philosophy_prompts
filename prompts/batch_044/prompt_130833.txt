On the "revisionist" account, marriage is fundamentally a stable, publicly recognized framework for intimate companionship and mutual care; gender is irrelevant to the capacity for care and commitment, so restricting marriage to opposite-sex pairs is arbitrary, and the state's only legitimate interest is in supporting durable caregiving bonds, not in enforcing biological complementarity.
