ok so I just got appointed to the Riverside Landmarks and Naming Commission (yeah sounds boring but bear with me) and its suddenly personal — we bought our place on Vorn Street and my kid started at Vorn Elementary this fall. Anyway theres a bronze bust of Dr. Silas Vorn in the square, and both the street and school are named after him. He founded the towns first hospital in 1887, which is why people grew up treating him like a local saint. But a historian dug up mill company records showing he was medical director for Riverside Mill during the 1892 strike, and he ran "wellness checks" on workers that were basically non-consensual experiments — documented in his own ledgers, not rumor.

So the commission has to decide about the bust and the naming. The usual split: some want full removal and rename everything after Miriam Vorn, his sister, who was a nurse and labor organizer. Others want to keep Silas but add a contextual plaque explaining the harm. Then there's this third idea I floated — "referential redirection" — where we keep "Vorn Street" and "Vorn Elementary" but pass a resolution that the name now officially honors Miriam, not Silas, since they share the surname. No statue change, just updated plaque text. I think it avoids both false hero worship and total erasure, but I'm worried its just bureaucratic sleight-of-hand that lets us avoid a hard choice.
