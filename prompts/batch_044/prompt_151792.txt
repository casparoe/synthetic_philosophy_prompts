Write a taxonomy in tree form that classifies interpretive positions on Schopenhauer’s philosophy of music in The World as Will and Representation. Begin with the root split: does music, unlike the other arts, represent Platonic Ideas (mediated expression), or does it bypass representation entirely to be the direct copy of the Will? From there, branch by answers to three diagnostic claims: (1) whether Schopenhauer’s analogy between melody and human striving, harmony and animal life, and bass with inorganic nature makes tonal hierarchy structurally essential to music’s metaphysical role or only an illustrative comparison; (2) whether his well-known preference for Italian vocal melody over German instrumental harmony is a contingent cultural bias or required by his system; and (3) whether music that abandons tonal melody—atonal composition, electronic drone, or algorithmically generated sound—can still express the Will, or whether it falls outside music proper. Each leaf should carry a position label or “unnamed,” one sentence stating its core interpretive claim, and one sentence on its most awkward commitment (for example, having to dismiss Bach’s fugues or to grant metaphysical transparency to noise). Note any pair of leaves that collapse into one another on Schopenhauer’s own terms. The audience is a graduate aesthetics seminar; include at least six leaves. Respond in at most 200 words, giving only the branching logic and leaf contents—no bibliography, no citations.
