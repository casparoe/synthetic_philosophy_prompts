i'm trying to map my actual gut reactions about transformative commitments — the ones where you choose to become someone you can't currently understand. here are my verdicts, and they're kind of all over the plae.

choosing to become a parent when you've never wanted kids and genuinely can't imagine valuing it: i think that's rational, even brave, as long as you're not pretending you can predic the outcome.

choosing to stay child-free forever because right now you value autonmy and can't imagine ever not valuing it: that feels like a mistake to me, like locking a door from the inside without knowing what's in the other room.

choosing to take an experimental treatment that rewires your aesthetic preferences — say, it makes you love atonal music and hate the pop you grew up with — just to experience something alien: i think that's perfectly fine, maybe even a good thing.

choosing the same treatment when you're a professional musician whose entire career and self-image depend on the tastes it would erase: suddenly that feels reckles, not brave.

choosing to enter a lifelong silent retreat knowing you have no idea what sustained silence does to cognition: i think that's only okay if you've already done months of partial silence and still want it; otherwise it's just reckless gambling with your mind.

choosing to remain Deaf and reject a cochlear implant because your community values Deaf culture, even though you personally feel isolated and want to hear: i think you should take the implant. loyalty to a culture shouldn't block your own potential transformation if you're suffering.

choosing to suppress a formative traumatic memory with a new therapy that might make you kinder but erase the vigilance that made you who you are: i think you shouldn't do it, but i'm genuinely unsure whether that's because transformation should be preserved or because suffering is somehow valuable.

so what's the principle here? is there one that gets all seven, or close? and what does it predict for a case i haven't listed — say, deciding whether to migrate to a country with values so different that you'd probably become unrecognizable to your current self? also, which of my verdicts do i have to drop if i want the principle to stay simple? i keep going back and forth on the music case vs. the musician case — they look structurally identical but i rate them totally differently.
