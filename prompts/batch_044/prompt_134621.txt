Here are my verdicts about which artificial or hybrid systems genuinely understand language, arranged from clear to unsettled, and I want the principle—if there is one—that best organizes them, even at the cost of overturning a verdict.

Searle in the classic Chinese Room, applying explicit symbol-manipulation rules to Chinese input: no understanding whatsoever. The same room, but with the rules compiled into an opaque neural weight matrix that Searle consults by pure pattern recognition, with no conscious grasp of the rules: still no understanding. A disembodied large language model, trained only on text with no sensory-motor coupling: I think it lacks understanding, though I am less certain here than in the first two cases. A distributed system of thousands of agents, each executing one micro-inference, linked by message-passing architecture that closely mirrors neural connectivity, but with no single conscious subject inside: I lean slightly toward thinking the collective system does understand, despite the absence of any individual mind. A human whose Broca's area has been replaced by an algorithmic prosthesis that computes linguistic outputs, yet whose phenomenology remains seamless and transparent: I think she understands fully. A robot that learns a language through embodied interaction, feedback loops, and goal-directed behavior in a physical environment, but whose internal architecture is entirely silicon: I think it understands too.

Cases three and four feel especially hard to reconcile—why deny understanding to a unified neural network while granting it to a distributed, brain-mimicking network with no conscious center? Is there a coherent principle here—perhaps about causal coupling to the world, about subjective transparency, about functional architecture, or about biological implementation—that delivers all six verdicts? If not, which of my verdicts should be dropped? Please also apply your best principle to an unlisted case: a quantum-computing Chinese Room in which correct Chinese responses emerge directly from superposition states, with no classical intermediate steps observable even in principle.
