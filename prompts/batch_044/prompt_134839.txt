If belief about fiction is graded rather than binary, then the paradox of fiction collapses—there's no sharp contradiction between knowing a story is made up and still feeling real grief for a character, since my doxastic state can just be partly activated at a sub-personal level. So why do people still present this like an unsolved puzzle in philosphy of mind? It feels like they're still assuming an old all-or-nothing model of belief that cognitive science moved past decades ago. Am I missing something, or is the paradox basically a dead problem that just hasn't been burried yet?
