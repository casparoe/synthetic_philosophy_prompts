Write an essay examining whether epistocratic mechanisms—such as competence-weighted voting—can be incorporated into democratic systems without collapsing the distinction between technical expertise and normative judgement. Focus on the objection that any metric of political competence necessarily embeds contested value commitments, and evaluate whether a domain-specific pluralist framework, in which distinct weights apply to separate spheres like economic forecasting, environmental risk assessment, and constitutional interpretation, can adequately answer this concern. Weigh the principal arguments on both sides, then reach and defend a clear conclusion about whether democracy ought to include any form of epistocratic filtering.
