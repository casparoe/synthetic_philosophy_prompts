I also think this works in reverse — that frictionless, standardized communication (like automated policy briefs) erodes the specific linguistic resistance that makes political accountability possible. So my claim is that language degradation and political degradation are structurally coupled, and fixing one means changing there institutional incentives, not just individual virtue.
