Write an essay about whether Raphael Lemkins original framing of genocide should cover what id call mnemonic erasure — destroying not the people themselves but the archives, databases, and even language-model corpora that let a group keep its collective memory intact. Some argue this is just cultural genocide with extra steps, others claim its a distinct harm because it breaks the temporal chain of group identity so survivors become strangers to their own history. Weigh both sides but dont just sit on the fence: should international law treat mnemonic erasure as genocide, or does that dilute the term past recognition? And does it matter whether the erasure comes from deliberate state policy or is just an accidental side-effect of tech rollout? Reach a conclusion and defend it.
