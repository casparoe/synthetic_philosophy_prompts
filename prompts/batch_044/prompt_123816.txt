I'm a doctoral student in philosophy of mind writing on hybrid theories of well-being. For an interdisciplinary colloquium on virtual environments and value, I'd like a framing poem: a villanelle of nineteen lines with two refrains, one voicing the experiential condition that felt satisfaction is sufficient regardless of causal origin, the other voicing the objective condition that achievement requires actual connection to the world outside the simulation. The cyclical returns should dramatize the hybrid theorist's problem — that simulated achievements satisfy the experiential refrain without resolving the objective deficit — and the final couplet, where the two lines meet, should leave the tension structurally open. It should be precise enough that a specialist recognizes the hybrid problem in the rhyme scheme, readable as poetry without prior knowledge of the experience machine, and should not resolve the argument: let the form carry the conflict.
