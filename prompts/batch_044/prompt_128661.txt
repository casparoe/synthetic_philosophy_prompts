What view of personal identity or freedom does that split actually presuppose? Does constitutional precommitment only make sense if "the people" are one continuous self across time, while psychiatric contracts assume the real me is the stable one? Or can a control-based view of autonomy explain both without contradiction? And if we replaced the underlying view—say, if future citizens or manic-me have fully equal moral standing—what parts of either practice would have to collapse?
