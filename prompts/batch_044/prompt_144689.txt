Ive been thinking about chaos and determinism and I think Ive tied myself in knots. Heres what I actually hold:

a) The universe runs deterministic — rewind every atom to yesterday and I still order the same lunch.
b) Chaos theory shows some deterministic systems are literally unpredictable, not just hard: weather past two weeks, heartbeats, that kind of thing.
c) I think human choice is in that class. Same atoms, same me, but the causal shadow gets so twisted that no scanner could ever read my choice back out — not because information is lost, but because its spread across too many branching paths.
d) That shadow is what I call free will. Im not outside causality; Im just hidden inside it.
e) Quantum randomness doesnt save anything — if my choice came from a random decay, thats noise, not me.
f) Still, I think if determinism is true then moral blame is an illusion, even if chaos makes it feel real.

Do these hang together? I feel like c and d fit, but f seems to pull the rug out from d — if determinism kills blame, does chaos actually change anything? And a and c: if determinism means perfect knowledge of atoms gives perfect prediction, then chaos shouldnt block a perfect scanner, right? Or is there a real limit?
