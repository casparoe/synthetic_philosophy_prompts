B. Every adequate explanatory hierarchy contains an inviolate level: a bedrock (for example, fundamental physical law, or the axioms of a formal system) that explains the levels above it but is not itself explained, derived, or causally altered by them.

C. In human minds, mental-level self-modification—say, updating one's reasoning strategy after recognizing a bias—is a real strange loop: the mental level causally alters the neural patterns that implement it, without violating physical closure because all causation is mediated by physical processes.
