I've been drafting my working position for a graduate seminar on the meta-problem of consciousness, and I want a consistency audit before I present it. Here is the profile I intend to defend:

(a) I am a physicalist: conscious states are fully realized by physical processes, with no non-physical ontology. (b) I regard Chalmers's hard problem as a legitimate, substantive explanatory challenge—genuinely distinct from explaining cognitive function—not a pseudo-problem that can be dissolved by conceptual analysis alone. (c) I endorse the phenomenal concept strategy: phenomenal concepts operate with a distinctive cognitive architecture (direct, demonstrative, non-descriptive) that explains why physical-to-phenomenal derivations feel impossible. (d) I think this strategy solves the meta-problem: it provides a physicalistically respectable account of why people form anti-physicalist intuitions. (e) I treat zombie conceivability as a real datum that needs explaining; I deny that conceivability entails metaphysical possibility, but I also hold that conceivability is a fallible yet genuine guide to possibility, so the physicalist bears a non-trivial explanatory burden. (f) Even after the meta-problem is solved via (c) and (d), I maintain there is an irreducible "residue" of puzzlement: it remains genuinely mysterious why any physical process should be accompanied by subjective experience, and knowing about phenomenal concepts does not fully eliminate that mystery. (g) I insist that the specialness of phenomenal concepts is purely cognitive and representational, not ontological: it concerns how we think, not an extra feature of reality.

Please audit this package. Go through the pairs and tell me which are genuinely in tension, which only appear tense but dissolve on closer inspection, and rank the real tensions by severity. For each tension, specify what the cheapest revision would be and what it costs—whether weakening (b), qualifying (e), or dropping (f). Also tell me whether there is a single revision that would clean up the most conflict with minimal damage to my core physicalism, or whether the set is salvageable as is with only clarificatory adjustments. Be specific about which commitments pull against each other and why.
