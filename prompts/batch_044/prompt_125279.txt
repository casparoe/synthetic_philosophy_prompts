(2) Define R as "The price assigned to R at timestep 500 is strictly below 0.5." (3) If the price at 500 is indeed below 0.5, then R is true, so coherence forces that price toward 1 — but 1 is not below 0.5, contradiction. (4) So the price at 500 must be ≥ 0.5. (5) But then R is false, so coherence forces the price toward 0, which is below 0.5, contradiction again. (6) Therefore reflective logical induction can't consistently assign any price to R. (7) Since R is a well-formed logical sentence in the language, the framework fails on its own terms. (8) So even the reflective fix doesn't save logical induction from self-reference paradox.
