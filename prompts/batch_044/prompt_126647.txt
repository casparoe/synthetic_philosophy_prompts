Think carefully about how this tension can be made to do structural work in verse rather than being merely illustrated.

Write a villanelle—nineteen lines, ABA rhyme scheme, with two refrains (A1 and A2)—spoken in the voice of an artist defending Du Bois's position against an interlocutor who insists that propaganda corrupts aesthetic purity.
