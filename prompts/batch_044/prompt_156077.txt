Consider a modified Sleeping Beauty problem. A fair coin is flipped. If heads, Beauty is awakened once on Monday in the original laboratory and interviewed. If tails, she is awakened twice: once in the original laboratory and once in a perfect digital simulation that is destroyed immediately after the interview, leaving no trace, no record, and no possibility of later verification. Beauty knows the full protocol, knows she is being interviewed now, but does not know whether she is the original or the simulated awakening; she also knows that if she is simulated, her answer will never enter the real-world record.

What is your credence—state a precise number between 0 and 1—that Beauty's rational credence in heads, upon being interviewed, ought to be 1/3 rather than 1/2?

Decompose your reasoning into at least three independent terms:

(i) The weight that should be given to self-locating, indexical evidence in updating beliefs about objective chances, specifically whether a principle analogous to the Principal Principle applies to centered worlds.

(ii) The evidential significance of the simulation's immediate termination: does the fact that one tails awakening is causally isolated and unrecorded provide Beauty with a reason to treat the two tails awakenings as epistemically asymmetric?

(iii) The metaphysical or logical status of "being this particular awakening"—whether it functions as a non-indexical property that doubles the evidential sample on tails, or merely as an indexical frame that should not affect the objective probability.

For each term, assign a rough probability and provide one line of justification. Then combine them—qualitatively or with approximate arithmetic—to reach your overall number. Identify the term that is the weakest link, meaning the one whose revision would most alter your conclusion. Finally, describe in one or two sentences what specific intuition pump or conceptual distinction—without appealing to named authors or texts—would be most likely to shift that weakest term.
