Weigh the finitist claim that such contradictions show actual infinities are not genuinely instantiated, against the Cantorian response that the ledger commits a category error by treating possible assignments as actual ones. Reach and defend a specific verdict: either that the modified hotel can be formalized so that actual infinities remain coherent, or that the formalization reveals cardinal equality to be too weak a relation to capture real magnitude in infinite cases, leaving finitism as the more coherent metaphysical stance.
