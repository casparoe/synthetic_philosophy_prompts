hey, i'm a phd student in legal philosophy and i've been stuck on this weird loop in how liberal democracies do constitutional review. like, the court strikes down statutes using the constitution, but the court's own authority and membership rules are in that same document, and amendments usually need supermajorities that can involve the court or bodies it appoints. it's like escher's drawing hands — the hand that draws the rules is itself being drawn by them. there's no obvious outside.
