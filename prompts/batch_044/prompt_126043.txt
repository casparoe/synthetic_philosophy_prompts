Consequently, any internal representational architecture must treat the agent's own sensors, metabolic maintenance processes, and boundary conditions as elements of the external world it models, even though those elements are constitutive of the modeling activity itself. This seems to generate a reflexive loop in which the system represents the conditions of its own representation as if they were independent objects.
