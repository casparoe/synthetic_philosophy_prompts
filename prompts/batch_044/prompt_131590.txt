Please compare Zygmunt Bauman's thesis that the Holocaust was not a historical aberration but rather an extreme expression of modern bureaucratic rationality and the production of moral distance with the uniqueness position advanced during the West German Historikerstreit, which treats the genocide as incomparable and singular. Focus specifically on which of these two frameworks more coherently explains the scalability of administrative violence in the absence of exceptionalist psychological dispositions, and articulate one precise conceptual tension that arises when the historiographical category of singular evil is mapped onto the organizational mechanism of moral distancing using the same archival records of camp bureaucracy.
