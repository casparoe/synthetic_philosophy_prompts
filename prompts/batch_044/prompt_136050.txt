I've been taking a course on the historical Jesus methods and my study group cooked up this argument that seems to collapse the whole quest into circular reasoning, but I can't find the exit. Here goes:

1. The criterion of dissimilarity says a tradition is probably authentic only if it doesn't match first-century Judaism or the interests of the early church.
2. The criterion of coherence says that once we have an authentic core, other material that fits its themes gains credibility too.
3. The criterion of embarrassment adds that details awkward for later Christians—like Jesus submitting to John's baptism—count in favor.
4. So we start with Set A, the tiny cluster that survives all three filters.
5. By coherence we expand Set A to include thematically related sayings, say ones about hidden growth that look neither Jewish nor churchy.
6. But dissimilarity has already stripped away almost all the Jewish and Christian context we'd need to check whether that coherence is real or projected.
7. Meanwhile embarrassment depends on knowing what the church actually found awkward, which presupposes we already know the Jesus we're trying to recover.
8. So the three criteria don't filter evidence; they just let us build any portrait we want and then call it the 'historical Jesus' because it coheres with itself.
9. Therefore the quest can never actually settle who Jesus was—every reconstruction is just a mirror of whatever we smuggled into Set A.
10. Yet scholars keep claiming they've reconstructed specific figures with real confidence, not just arbitrary projections.
11. So either they're fooling themselves, or there's a hidden slip in our chain.

My gut says step 8 or 9 jumps too far, maybe there's a modal slide from 'no certainty' to 'no knowledge at all,' but I can't pin it down. Which premise should I actually reject, why did it look fine at first, and what do I pay if I drop it rather than dropping, say, step 6? I'd also like to know whether the plausibility-based defenses that replace pure dissimilarity avoid the circularity or just hide it better. If I'm keeping the circularity worry, what case would best show whether coherence is doing real work or just rubber-stamping?
