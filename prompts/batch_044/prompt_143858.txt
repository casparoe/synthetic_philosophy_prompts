I am a doctoral student in metaphysics with a concentration in the philosophy of biology, and I am preparing a session for my graduate seminar on essentialism and modal metaphysics—ten students, seventy-five minutes, all comfortable with possible-worlds semantics but with uneven backgrounds in reproductive biology.
