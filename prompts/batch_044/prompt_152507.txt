Consider a research program spanning predictive processing and the free energy principle that deliberately avoids both the well-trodden dark-room objection literature and unfalsifiable cosmological extensions of active inference to all living and even non-living systems. The program is staffed by philosophers of science and theoretical modelers with no wet-lab access but strong formal backgrounds, with a three-year horizon. Its aim is not to accumulate another variant of hierarchical predictive coding, but to settle questions whose answers would restructure downstream work in psychiatric nosology, embodied robotics, or the philosophy of interoception.

Given that constraint, which two or three open lines of inquiry should command the bulk of resources, and in what dependency order? I want an explicit defense: for each, whether it is upstream of the others (so that getting it wrong makes subsequent work moot), how tractable it is without direct empirical access, and whether resolving it would change practice rather than merely adding theoretical ornament.

Specifically, weigh these candidates: (i) whether prediction-error minimization across interoceptive and exteroceptive domains requires a representational architecture or can be reconstructed in purely sensorimotor-coupling terms without loss of explanatory power; (ii) whether interoceptive inference fragments psychiatric classification further by multiplying domain-specific generative models, or instead unifies it under a single variational-bound account of allostatic failure; and (iii) whether the free-energy formulation provides a genuinely independent constraint on model selection, or is instead a redescription of dynamics already captured by standard Bayesian inference with different notation.

Then address the meta-question: is the most upstream dispute conceptual—about what minimization denotes when scaled from neurons to organisms—or normative—about what distinguishes an explanatory model from a post-hoc variational gloss? And does settling one render the other dispensable?
