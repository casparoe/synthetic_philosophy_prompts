Evidential cooperation in large worlds asks agents to act cooperatively toward distant counterparts they can never causally influence, on the grounds that their decisions are correlated. But if my choice cannot causally affect what a causally disconnected agent does, how can cooperating on my end possibly raise the actual probability that they cooperate too? Doesn't ECL implicitly require something like backward-in-time causation to be coherent, rather than just an interesting decision-theoretic heuristic?
