I'm inclined to side with Priya — after all, a label is just a label, and relabeling the seats shouldn't change the physical setup. Can you help me understand the structure of this dispute? In particular: (1) is Tom's position actually incoherent, or is there a principled reason to think the shuffling could be "real"? (2) what would it take for Priya to be right — i.e., what is the positive case for the view that the two charts represent the same physical setup? (3) and most importantly for my seminar, is there a way to decide between the two positions, or is the dispute purely verbal? I'd like a response that takes the theater analogy seriously rather than just telling me "labels are conventions and the physical setup is what matters," because I suspect that's exactly the move the dispute is turning on.
