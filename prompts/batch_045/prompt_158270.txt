I'm writing a paper on whether the world has become less violent, and I want the standard of evaluation settled before I look at a single trend line. Give me the desiderata that an account of the level of violence at a time would have to meet to be any use, with the case behind each, and mark each as either non-negotiable or merely a preference of one camp.

Fix the purpose as practical: the account is to be used to decide whether whatever produced the decline, if there is one, is something we could deliberately build on, so it has to support comparisons between policies, not merely describe the past.

I want at least one desideratum covering each of: the denominator, and why it matters that a larger population can be safer per head whilst bloodier in total; the boundary of the category, meaning capital punishment, imprisonment, killings in colonies and on plantations, domestic violence, deaths attributable to remediable deprivation, and animal slaughter; comparability across kinds of evidence, such as medieval coroners' rolls or court records set against modern cause-of-death registration; and displacement, where violence falls inside the unit being measured whilst rising just outside it or being deferred to a later date.

I also have a candidate of my own, clustering-neutrality: a measure should not change its verdict merely because the same number of violent deaths arrive bunched into one event rather than spread across many small ones. Tell me whether that is a genuine desideratum or a prejudice of mine, and what accepting or rejecting it costs.

Give me exactly seven numbered items, each a sentence or two, with no introduction and no conclusion. Items one to five are the desiderata, each with its motivating case. Item six is whether there is any fact of the matter at all: if the level of violence is a weighted aggregate whose trend can flip with the weights, say what the thesis that the world has become less violent could even be asserting. Item seven names the pair of desiderata you think cannot be jointly satisfied, and the single desideratum that does the most work in excluding the accounts currently on offer, meaning the civilising-process account, the state-monopoly account, and the one that takes archaeological evidence about prehistoric lethal violence as its baseline.
