I am applying for a one-year dissertation fellowship and need a two-page research proposal on inverse reinforcement learning and the inference of human preferences from behavior. My half-formed idea: IRL treats observed behavior as approximately optimal for an unknown reward, with deviations handled as noise, and I think that noise term is doing hidden normative work. Whenever behavior is shaped by what an agent cannot do — missing skills, scarce options, fatigue, planning under time pressure — those limits get absorbed into the reward and reported back to us as things she wants. Call this the absorption problem. My suspicion is that it is not a technical artifact to be engineered away with more data or a more flexible noise model, but a non-identifiability that is settled, if at all, only by a substantive choice about which capacities to hold fixed and which to count as the agent's own; and that this choice is normative, not statistical.

Please turn this into a proposal that: states the question in a form that has a determinate answer within a year; explains why the obvious moves — more trajectories, richer models of bounded rationality — cannot settle it; sets out the method I would actually follow (roughly: a clean statement of the absorption result, a distinction between capacity-relative and capacity-free readings of an inferred reward, then the argument about what deference to each reading commits us to); identifies the possible conclusions and the considerations that would decide between them; and gives an honest account of what would count as failure — in particular, what would show that the project merely restages the familiar debate about adaptive preferences without adding anything to it.

Please don't include citations or a literature review; I will add those, and the proposal may refer to positions and arguments by description. Assume the reader knows IRL at the level of a good survey article.

Begin with a single sentence that states the question in one clause and the proposal's central claim in the other, then give the proposal itself, with no preamble and no restatement of what I asked for.
