On the standard Molinist picture, God's knowledge is divided into three logical moments: knowledge of all necessary truths and of all possible worlds (natural knowledge); knowledge of what every possible free creature would freely do in every situation in which it could be placed (middle knowledge); and knowledge of what God actually decrees to create (free knowledge). Middle knowledge is what allows God to select among merely feasible worlds, that is, worlds whose total moral content He can secure without overriding creaturely freedom, and it is the load-bearing element of the Molinist reply to the problem of evil.

I would like an explanation of a distinction that this threefold taxonomy does not explicitly mark. Call a counterfactual of situation one whose antecedent varies the agent's circumstances while holding fixed who and what the agent is. Call a counterfactual of constitution one whose antecedent varies something about the agent herself: her temperament, her upbringing, her native desires, or the standing dispositions she brings to the moment of choice, while the situation is held fixed, and asks what she would then freely do. For example: whether a particular official, had she been created with a markedly more anxious temperament and a harsher childhood, would still freely have refused the bribe.

Please explain, for someone who already has the basics of Molinism in hand, why the difference between these two kinds of counterfactual matters for the doctrine. In particular: must Molinism restrict middle knowledge to counterfactuals of situation, and if so can that restriction be drawn in a principled way rather than a merely stipulative one, given that how we individuate an agent across possible worlds seems to depend on just the sorts of traits the restriction would exclude? How does the notion of a feasible world, and the theodicy that rests on it, change if counterfactuals of constitution are admitted into middle knowledge? And does the well-known grounding worry, that counterfactuals of freedom have no truth-maker, press differently on the two kinds?
