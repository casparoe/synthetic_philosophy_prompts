Next month I want to do Dennett's Orwellian vs Stalinesque business about the timing of experience, built around the color phi case: red dot at A, then a beat later a green dot at B, and subjects report a single dot travelling from A to B that turns green somewhere in the middle — so they report a color change at a time when the only light that has hit the retina is red.

Don't explain the distinction to me, I've got it. I need a plan for getting it across in that slot. Specifically:

- What has to be on the board before the two hypotheses are even intelligible? Give me the dependency order and why each piece has to come where it does.

But the more I look at it the more the analogy seems to smuggle in exactly what's at issue: there really was an earlier rendered frame, and everyone agrees when it was shown. Is that a trap, or does it still work as the thing that carries the idea?
