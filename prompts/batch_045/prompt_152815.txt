I'm a PhD student in formal epistemology, and I'm writing the take-home final for an upper-level seminar on anthropic reasoning. I need one question on the self-sampling assumption versus the self-indication assumption that a student cannot pass by reciting the presumptuous-philosopher slogan that SIA favours hypotheses with more observers.

The case I want to build it around comes in two variants. Variant A: a fair coin is flipped; if heads, exactly ten observers are created, all wearing red shirts; if tails, a thousand observers are created, ten in red and nine hundred and ninety in blue. You wake up and find yourself wearing red. Variant B: same coin, but if heads ten observers are created with one in red and nine in blue, and if tails a thousand observers are created with a hundred in red and nine hundred in blue; again you find yourself in red. My thought is that these numbers make the two principles trade places: in one variant the big-world-friendly principle is the one that treats your red shirt as uninformative, and in the other it is the one that treats it as decisive, so that neither principle can simply be identified with the presumptuous philosopher's inference. Please check that arithmetic yourself rather than taking my word for it, and tell me if I have misdesigned the case.

What I need is: (1) the exam question itself, framing both variants for students, with a stated limit of forty minutes and a nine-hundred-word cap; (2) a model answer of about six hundred words; (3) a marking scheme out of twenty built on four or five must-hit points, with the marks each is worth and one line on the error that should cost a student marks there. The points I have in mind are: the explicit SIA calculation, showing why the prior boost cancels in one variant but not the other; the explicit SSA calculation; a diagnosis of what the residual disagreement is really about, given that the SSA verdict is hostage to whether the blue-shirted observers belong to the reference class; what happens to each verdict if the reference class is narrowed to red-shirted observers; and whether the pair of variants settles, or merely relocates, the presumptuous-philosopher worry.
