Here's my opening. Discounting cash is fine — a pound now can be invested and come back bigger — and I'll even grant you that plenty of projects never happen. But a saved life doesnt earn interest. Strip all that away and the only thing left doing any work is that it's later, which is pure time preference in a suit, and that's the bit I thinks indefensible. What have you got?
