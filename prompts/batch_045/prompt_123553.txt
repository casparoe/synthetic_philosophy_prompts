I've recently started a job that puts me on a plane most months, and after the fourth or fifth flight I found I couldn't give my partner a straight answer about whether I actually owe anything here. So I've written down the five things I find myself believing, and I'd like them checked for coherence before I let them keep guiding what I do.

1. The inefficacy argument works for greenhouse gases. My individual emissions make no difference to any climate outcome, and the expected-difference move over the tiny probability of sitting at a tipping point doesn't rescue it, so I have no individual duty to fly less or to buy offsets.

2. I do think I have a real moral reason not to buy factory-farmed meat, and I think the standard argument for it is sound: my purchase has a small but non-negligible chance of pushing a retailer's restocking decision past a threshold, so the expected difference here, unlike in the emissions case, isn't vanishing.

3. I have a duty to vote in a large national election, and the minuteness of my chance of being decisive isn't what grounds it — I'd still have it in a safe seat — so it must be grounded in something about taking part in a collective practice rather than in what my act brings about.

4. I don't think integrity-based reasons, or reasons about keeping my own hands clean, are genuine moral reasons at all. When the harm is real and the cost to me is small, being worried about my own moral record strikes me as self-indulgent.

5. I can't have a duty whose content depends on facts about what other people happen to be doing, when those are facts I could never observe. This is why I reject complicity and membership-based accounts of what I owe in collective-action cases, on which how much I owe shifts with how many others are also contributing.

What I want is an audit rather than a lecture. Which of these pairs are genuinely in tension, and which only look like they are until you say precisely what each commitment commits me to? Rank the real tensions by severity, tell me for the worst one what would have to give, and tell me what the cheapest single revision to the whole set would be and what it would cost me.
