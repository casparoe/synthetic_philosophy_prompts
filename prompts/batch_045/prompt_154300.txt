my opening is two moves at once.

First, the thing I'm defending, so you know what you're hitting: stakes never change how good your epistemic position has to be. What they change is the contrast. Knowledge claims are elliptical for knows that p rather than q, and raising the stakes just swaps in a more demanding q — open tomorrow rather than open-tomorrow-except-for-the-holiday-schedule, that kind of thing. So all the bankish data gets explained while the underlying relation stays completely stakes-invariant, and you never have to say somebody lost knowledge they already had.
