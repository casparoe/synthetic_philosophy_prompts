The comedy should come out of the structure of the problem itself rather than out of slapstick or exploding gadgets, so please get these right without ever explaining them: that the bulk of what makes an action intelligible is the enormous, unmentioned set of things it does *not* disturb, and that there is no principled place to stop listing them; that a rule about what may be ignored is itself one more thing that can change; that "we closed the world by fiat, we only track what's in the register" just relocates the whole difficulty to whoever maintains the register; and that the categories the form can see are not the categories that matter here.
