ok so our district just announced that next semester every school issued laptop is getting monitoring software on it. From what the tech guy said at the assembly: it takes a screenshot every few minutes, logs every site and every search, and scans your docs and chats for certain words. If it flags something a counselor looks at it (and if it's bad enough the police liaison guy). Everything it collects is kept untill you graduate and parents can ask for a copy of their kid's file.

 Parents and a bunch of kids pushed back, so they came out with a "compromise" version: nothing gets looked at by a human unless the software flags it, unflagged stuff auto deletes after 30 days, and if your parent signs a form you can switch the whole thing off for one 45 minute block a day. Everyone acted like that settled it.

I still hate it and I can't explain why, which is the problem because my mom basically says if you're not doing anything wrong you don't need to hide it, and a friend of mine said the compromise is already pretty good and that if I'm still unhappy then no version of this would ever be good enough for me, I just don't want to be watched at all. That one stung a bit because idk if it's true?

The reason I know it's not just "I want to hide bad stuff" is that I genuinely don't care about most of this. My mom reads my texts sometimes and I don't even mind, my teachers obviously know my grades and attendance, I'm in the school play, I post dumb videos. My english teacher reads all my horrible rough drafts and I'm fine with that too. So it's not drafts being seen and it's not me wanting to be invisible.

What actually makes me sick about it is specific: the poem I started and never showed anyone, a search I did about a health thing I don't want to talk about, and a message to my friend where I said something really awful and then apologized an hour later. All of that sitting in a file for four years that some adult who has no idea who I am can pull up.
