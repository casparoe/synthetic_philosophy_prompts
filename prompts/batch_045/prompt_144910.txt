Take a six-node centipede game: the pot roughly doubles every time someone passes, and whoever grabs it keeps a bit more than half, so grabbing always beats what you'd get by passing straight to the other player but is dwarfed by what both of you get if nobody ever grabs. Now the twist I care about: both players are told, and it's common knowledge, that neither can actually chain more than about three steps of the form if I pass, she'll think that if she passes, then ... — the reasoning fizzles out long before it reaches the last node.

My credence that grabbing at the first node is the right play here is maybe 0.35, and its lower than in the unlimited-reasoning version, which I find weird.
