(2) For basically any real action the far-future part of the causal chain is where almost all the value is, and our credences over it are hopeless: not just imprecise but unstable, in that small changes in modelling assumptions flip the sign of the long-run net effect. Call that cluelessness.
(3) Because of (2) I use what I call the washout rule: treat the inscrutable long-run remainder as mean-zero and independent of whatever I can measure short-run, so the measured near-term difference is an unbiased estimate of the total difference.
