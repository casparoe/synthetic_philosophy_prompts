My wife and I are signed up with a company that does "remote relocation": they scan your body down to the last cell, grow a new one at the destination, and wake it up with your memories. The original is quietly disposed of right after the scan, and honestly that never bothered me much. Now they've sent us two options and I've been going in circles for a week.

Option A: scan, original gone right away, copy wakes up on the settlement.

Option B: same scan, same copy, but the original body is kept under for a year, then wakes up on Earth for a paid year in a nice place, and is only disposed of after that. Two catches. First, the scan they build the copy from is "tidied up" — they strip out my fear of heights and a few memories from a rough patch in my childhood, since those dont go over well in the settlement. Second, in option B the paperwork names the copy as the legal me: it gets the house, the savings, and it's the one who'll be around for our daughter. The body that wakes up on Earth is registered as somebody new, with nothing.

My wife says option B is murder with extra steps, and that the Earth one is obviously me — same body, same world. I feel that pull too, I'd rather be the Earth one even for a year, but I can't say why the body should count for more than the memories, and if the body is what counts, why do I care what happens to the copy at all? Also, that extra year makes option B feel way easier to say yes to, and I can't tell whether that's me getting something real or just me not wanting to think about the end.

Which option should we take? And is there even a fact of the matter about which one is me, or is that just a question with no answer?
