He never gives a formula, just a number. I've got five cases where he gave me one, and now I've got my own situation and I want to know what he'll say, and separately what I should actually do.

His old verdicts. Important detail: in every one of them the account he was betting out of was basically all the money he had, he was between gigs with no salary, so I cant tell from these whether he's thinking about the account or about everything he's got.

1. Even money, win rate 55% measured over about 1,000 logged trials, repeatable whenever, no stake limit. Account was $20k. He said 5% of whatever's in the account, and don't change it when the account goes up or down.
2. Even money, 62% over about 150 trials. He said 12%.
3. Pays 2-to-1, 60% over about 40 trials. He said 20%, and that it already felt aggressive.
4. Even money, 53% over about 3,000 trials. He said 3%.
5. Even money, 52% over about 200 trials. He said don't bet at all, you don't know anything from 200 trials. This one still surprises me, the edge looked fine to me.

Once when he was explaining himself somebody said it's just the log, man, and Dev didn't deny it. I have no idea what that means.

My situation. $60k in a brokerage account, stable job at $160k, I put $2,500 a month into that same account and expect to for another 20 years (if you capitalize that it's what, $350k?), plus $90k in a 401(k) I won't touch. The bet: even money, once a week, win rate 55%, but this time from roughly 40,000 logged instances of the identical setup, so I'm about as sure of the 55% as anyone gets. It only runs 26 weeks and then the offer disappears, and the other side won't take more than $10k a week. The outcomes have nothing to do with my job or my industry.

So: what number does Dev give me, and more usefully what rule is he running? I care less about the number than about the rule, about which rules fit his five old numbers about as well as each other, and about where those rules come apart on mine. Also: do the 26 weeks and the $2,500 a month change anything, and which way?

Give me:

Table 1, one row per candidate rule you're considering, columns:
- rule — one sentence, stated so I could apply it to a new bet myself
- old_cases — which of the five old numbers it reproduces and where it strains, a few words per case
