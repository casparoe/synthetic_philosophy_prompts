ok so I'm on student council this year (long story, I basically got drafted) and part of what I do is helping run the volunteer hours thing, and I keep bumping into the same pattern and I don't know if it has a name. Our school decided service was important, so they made it into something you log: you need a certain number of hours to get the cord at graduation or whatever. And now people do stuff that everyone agrees is volunteering-shaped but isn't really — a kid I know sat in a thrift store sorting the same bin of tshirts for two hours and logged it, while my friend has been tutoring her little brother's whole class for free all year and it counts for zero because there's no form for it. That part I sort of get: people aim at the number instead of the thing. My econ teacher mentioned some law about this, Goodhart I think, and from what I remember it's about a measure being bad once you start targeting it.

But the part I can't find a word for is what happens after, and it happens even when nobody is cheating and everyone involved genuinely cares about the actual goal. After a year or two of tracking, the tracked version quietly becomes what people mean by the word. Teachers now describe the thrift store kid as "really committed to service" and the tutoring girl as "not very involved," and when I tried to say that seemed backwards I got treated like I was saying volunteering doesn't matter, which I definately wasn't. My friend's mom is a hospital administrator and she says the same thing about their wait time stats — nobody is lying, the numbers are honestly collected, but "running an efficient clinic" now just means "the number looks good," and if you ask what the number was standing in for people look at you like you're being difficult. So it's not exactly that the measure stopped being a good measure. It's more like the thing being measured shrank until it fit the measure perfectly and kept the old name.

Is there a real term for that second half, the part where the proxy doesn't just break but gets reabsorbed into the meaning of the word?
