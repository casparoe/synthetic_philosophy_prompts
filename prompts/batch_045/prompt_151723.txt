I work on the foundations of statistical mechanics, and I have been asked to co-referee a submission that is a new variant on the demonic theme. My supervisor has refereed a long run of these over the years and has an unusually consistent nose for them; I would like my report to cohere with the verdicts she has already given rather than merely be defensible in isolation. I have reconstructed her track record from her reports, with the one-line reason she attached to each:

1. The one-molecule engine: partition inserted at the midpoint of a box at temperature T, demon records which half the molecule is in, the molecule expands isothermally against a load, partition withdrawn, one-bit record reset each cycle; kT ln 2 of work per cycle from the single bath. Verdict: no violation — the bit is paid for.
2. A ratchet and pawl in a single bath, with no recording device anywhere in the setup. Verdict: violation — nothing is consumed and there is no gradient.
3. As in (1), except that the demon must scatter a photon off the molecule to find out which side it is on. Verdict: no violation — the measurement is not free, and the record has to be cleared in any case.
4. As in (1), except that the demon clears its record by dumping it into a second reservoir at T_c < T rather than into the working bath. Verdict: no violation, but reclassify: it is a heat engine between two reservoirs, capped by 1 − T_c/T, not a demon.
5. As in (1), except that the demon never resets: it is supplied with fresh blank bits and simply archives the used ones. Verdict: no violation — the blank bits are the fuel; this is a battery with a sorting mechanism bolted on.
6. A stream of molecules each released at the left wall and allowed to relax, with work drawn from the relaxation. Verdict: no violation, and trivially so — a stream of prepared non-equilibrium states is fuel.
7. A proposal that drew net work from a single bath and whose author argued that the books balance because the apparatus had been assembled by a builder who thereby incurred an entropy debt on its behalf. Verdict: reject — that story can be told about any device whatsoever, so it does no work in the argument.

The new submission runs as follows. Take the one-molecule box of (1) at temperature T, single bath. Each cycle a register bit is delivered to the demon, perfectly correlated with which half of the box the molecule occupies. Marginally, on its own, that register is maximally mixed: a lab assaying the registers in isolation would find unbiased noise, indistinguishable from thermal garbage. In fact they are a waste product of the group next door — they cost nothing, they look like noise, and they would otherwise be binned. The demon performs no measurement and no erasure. It consults the register, lets the molecule expand against a load, and returns it to the full box, extracting kT ln 2 from the one bath. At the end of the cycle the molecule is back at equilibrium in the full box, the register is still maximally mixed and is now independent of the molecule, and the register goes back next door (or into the bin). Net result: kT ln 2 per cycle from a single bath, with no erasure, no blank memory consumed, and no change in the marginal state of anything inside the apparatus. The only thing that has changed is that register and molecule have stopped being correlated. One observation, which you should correct if it is wrong: on the joint state, molecule-plus-register gains k ln 2 of entropy, which is exactly what the bath loses — so the books balance if and only if one is willing to keep the ledger on joint states rather than on the separate ones.

So: what rule, or what set of rules, is she running given (1) through (7)? Which of the rules jointly consistent with those verdicts diverge on this case, and how — I care about the classification as much as the bare verdict, i.e. whether this comes out as an outright violation, as no-violation-but-an-engine-consuming-correlation, as no-violation-only-because-the-preparation-must-be-counted, or as no violation with nothing consumed at all. Which verdict do you expect her to give, and why that one over its rivals? Which of (1) through (7) is the closest analog, and does that set genuinely underdetermine the case? If it does, say what principle I would be committing myself to with each of the options, since my name goes on the report. Finally, name the single further case that would best separate the surviving rules and say what it would show either way. Lead with your bottom line in the first sentence and then give the reasoning, with no preamble and no restatement of what I have asked.
