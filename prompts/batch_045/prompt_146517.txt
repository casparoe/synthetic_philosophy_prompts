Invent the agency, the country and the project; I don't care what they are. Deadpan corporate voice, the sort of prose that opens with "The Bureau recognises that relationships matter." Nothing in the text should signal that it's a joke. Around 700 words, and I'd like it to contain: a couple of worked examples; some kind of table or schedule (I have this image of a payment depreciating into a gift over a fixed period of acquaintance, and then a clause saying it only becomes fully a gift once the relationship has been transmitted to the next generation — please take that and run with it); and a short end-of-module quiz where the correct answers are the horrifying ones.
