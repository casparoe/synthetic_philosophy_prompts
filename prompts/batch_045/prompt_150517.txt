hey — I'm taking a climate ethics seminar this term and instead of another "here's why the other side is evil" essay we're supposed to produce an adversarial-collaboration statement. Can you write the joint statement that a discounting optimiser (the view that future welfare should be discounted at roughly the market return on capital, and that growth plus later adaptation does most of the work) and a near-zero-pure-time-preference person (the view that discounting someone's welfare just because they're born later is just wrong) could both actually sign?

I want: the claims both would accept — including the awkard ones, like that cumulative emissions and temperature behave like a budget constraint, and that we can't pin down in advance which specific future people are made worse off; then the disagreement stated exactly, in wording each side would accept for its own view; then the cruxes.

For cruxes I've got two probes I made up, and I want both sides' reactions, with each side's prediction written down before you answer:

1. The Overlap Scenario: suppose most of the damages from emissions in the next 20 years land within 35 years, so the emitters and the people harmed are largely the same living people. If the gap between the two camps closes here, what does that tell us about whether they were ever really disagreeing about time preference rather then about uncertainty and who turns out to be affected?
2. The Handover Rule: each generation may emit only as much as it also removes, so it hands the next one an atmospheric sink no more degraded than the one it inherited — measured in tonnes of remaining capacity, not in money, and regardless of how much richer the successors turn out to be. Is that rule even coherent, and does it secretly just collapse into one camp's position?

Format: numbered agreed claims, then a table of disagreements with crux / probe / each side's prediction. Also flag any part of the dispute you think is purely verbal, and — the bit my prof cares most about — say what would change your mind about which crux is load-bearing, and where your reconstruction of either camp is most likely to be a strawman. Idk if my two probes are rigged toward one side; if they are, say so and fix them.
