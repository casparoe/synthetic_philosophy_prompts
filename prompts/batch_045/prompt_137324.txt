1. **Borrowed voice.** Sometimes an influence route works by routing the message through a relationship it didn't earn — the credibility, warmth or obligation that makes you listen belongs to someone else, and the party who designed the message is a stranger to that relationship. 2. **Mechanism-transparency vs. uptake-transparency.** It's one thing that I know someone is trying to get me to do something; it's another that I know *by what mechanism*, chosen by whom, with what evidence behind it, and for whose benefit. You can have the first without the second very easily.
3. **Reason-less efficacy.** This is the one that gets under my skin about A/B testing. When I know variant B lifted turnout by 0.4 points but I have no idea *why* it worked — no story about what consideration it gave people — then I'm not, as far as I can tell, offering anyone a reason.
