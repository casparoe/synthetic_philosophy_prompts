Plus his God was more or less Spinoza's — the order of the universe, not somebody keeping score. That part I can sort of see. What I can't square is that this is the same guy who spent his whole life telling people what they ought to do: denouncing militarism, signing petitions, writing letters, all of it. I know there's some move where "responsible" just ends up meaning something like responsive to reasons, with no spooky contra-causal freedom needed, but that reads like a word trick to me: if my brother's craving is caused and his drinking is caused, then praise and blame are just noise sprayed on top of a causal chain. I actually want help figuring out how to think about him here, not just about Einstein — so what am I missing?
