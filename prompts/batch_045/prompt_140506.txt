I am writing up a position paper on updating on conditional-probability constraints, and before I commit any of this to print I would like an audit of my own commitments. Here is the case I keep returning to, in my own numbers.

A soldier is dropped by parachute into a region cut by two lines: a north–south line dividing Friendly (F) from Enemy (E) territory, and an east–west line dividing Blue Company's area (B) from Red Company's area (R). Her prior over the four quadrants is P(F∧B) = 0.2, P(F∧R) = 0.2, P(E∧B) = 0.3, P(E∧R) = 0.3, so that P(F) = 0.4, P(B) = 0.5, and F and B are independent. Headquarters radios one of three possible messages: M1, that the odds are 3:1 on her being in Blue Company's area if she is in Friendly territory (a constraint that the posterior satisfy P⁺(B|F) = 3/4); M2, that the odds are 1:3 on Blue if she is in Enemy territory (P⁺(B|E) = 1/4); M3, that the outright probability of her being in Blue Company's area is 0.6.

Where I have landed:

(1) On M1 alone, P⁺(F) should stay at 0.4, and the odds within Enemy territory should stay 1:1. The message tells her only how the Friendly region is divided; which side of the north–south line she is on is exactly what it is silent about, and being told how one region is subdivided should not move you about which region you are in. I would defend this in print.

(2) As a general method: for any message of this kind, the update should be the distribution P⁺ that minimizes Σ_x P⁺(x) log(P⁺(x)/P(x)) subject to the constraint the message imposes. I mean that direction of the divergence and not the reverse, and I take the rule to apply uniformly whatever the constraint happens to be.

(3) M1 should raise her confidence in B: P⁺(B) > 0.5. Correspondingly M2 should lower it. Both the reasoning behind (1) and the rule in (2) seem to deliver this, which is part of why the package feels stable to me.

(4) I am inclined to model the message semantically as the conditional itself: what headquarters imparts is, roughly, that if she is in Friendly territory she is in Blue Company's area. I also accept that the probability of such a conditional is the corresponding conditional probability. So I want to say that Judy learns a proposition, becomes certain of it, and updates by ordinary conditionalization.

(5) I hold order-independence and cumulativity as desiderata on any rule: if she receives two messages in sequence, the final state should satisfy both constraints, and it should not matter which she received first. I have been told that the rule in (2) is path-dependent, which is my main reason for doubting it.

(6) I have also been leaning, perhaps too hard, on the claim that constraint-based updating is not conditionalization in any sense: that no event, in her four-cell space or in any refinement of it, has the likelihoods that would reproduce the update by ordinary conditionalization, and that this is what makes cases like this one a real problem for conditionalization rather than a disguised instance of it.

(7) Methodologically, I hold that norms of updating must be justified by accuracy considerations, expected-accuracy or accuracy-dominance arguments, and I do not think that minimal change, or minimal informational distance, is an independent normative notion that can do justificatory work on its own.

Please audit the set. I do not know where the trouble is, and I do not have a preferred item to sacrifice. Go through the pairs and tell me which genuinely conflict and which only look as though they do, and rank the real conflicts by severity. Where numbers settle something, do the computation and give me the posterior: I would much rather be told that the rule in (2) yields, say, a particular value for P⁺(F) that (1) forbids than be given a general discussion of conservativity. For each real conflict, say what would have to give, what giving it up would cost me, and whether the cost is a verdict, a method, or only a rationale; then tell me which single revision cleans up the most. If some conflict turns out to depend on a further choice — which constraint is at issue, whether it straddles the partition, which divergence — say which choice and why it matters rather than leaving it as a hedge. I am not asking for the literature; I want the argument.
