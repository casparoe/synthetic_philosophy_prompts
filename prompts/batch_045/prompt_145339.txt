So: translate acausal trade into that vocabulary as faithfully as you can — two agents that will never exchange a message and share no common cause in their past, who nonetheless end up 'trading' because each one's decision procedure is supposedly correlated with the other's just by being the right kind of algorithm. Then give me the residue report: what maps cleanly, what you had to distort to make it fit, and what has no expression in my vocabulary at all.

Two bits I especially want you to chew on. Is 'logical correlation' anything more than noticing the copies came out of the same build? Second: the part I really can't express is the exchange rate. In a protocol I'd just say flip a shared coin, but there is no shared coin here, and if the answer is that the decision theory picks the symmetric point, I want to know how, mechanically.
