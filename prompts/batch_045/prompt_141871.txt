So I've been trying to work out when it's actually ok to take someone else's word on a moral question, and I keep going in cirles. Here are my gut verdicts on some cases — I want the principle they add up to, if there is one.

- My colleague (who's Black) tells me a joke I made was racist. I believe her and apologise. Not just fine — I think I'd be wrong not to defer.
- I ask a stranger at a bus stop whether it's ok to skip my sister's wedding for a trip I've already booked. She says go on the trip. Her saying it changes nothing; I feel a bit weird for even having asked.
- I ask a friend who does moral philosophy whether I should be giving more to charity. She says yes, and gives reasons I basically already had. Defering here feels like laziness, and I also feel that if I gave more *because she said so*, my giving would be worth slightly less somehow.
- At 1am, drunk, I text my sober friend: should I text my ex? She says no. I don't. That deference feels totally fine — even though she knows no facts I don't and isn't any kind of moral expert. She's just not drunk.
- My uncle, who says casually racist stuff all the time, tells me the joke was fine. I don't defer, and I don't think I should.
- I ask an AI assistant whether I'm being unfair to a coworker whose work I keep redoing. It says yes, with what look like good reasons. I half-believe it, but I feel like I'm cheating in a way I wouldn't if a friend had said the same thing.
- I ask my therapist whether I'd be justified in cutting off my dad. She says yes. I defer, and it feels fine — though again, no facts I don't have, and she's not really a moral expert either.

My tentative rule: deference is in order exactly when the other person has access to something I don't — a fact about the situation, or what it's like to be on the recieving end of what I did — and out of order when they're only re-weighing considerations I already have. That handles the joke, the bus stop, the charity case and my uncle, but it falls apart on the 1am case and maybe the therapist, and I don't know what to do with the AI one.
