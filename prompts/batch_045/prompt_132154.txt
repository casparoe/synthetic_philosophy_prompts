I'm taking an undergraduate seminar on Marx's political economy, and our final assignment is unusual: instead of an essay we're asked to write a short story that makes a thesis from the reading happen rather than asserting it. I want to try it with the material on machinery — Volume I's chapter on machinery and modern industry, together with the section of the Grundrisse usually called the Fragment on Machines, where production comes to rest on social knowledge, the general intellect, and the contradiction appears: the same drive that pushes necessary labour time towards a minimum also keeps labour time as the sole measure and source of wealth.

The scenario I've sketched: a components plant, the last large employer in a company town, has been automated to the point that eleven people remain on the payroll. They are retained as exception handlers on rotating twelve-hour shifts, paid partly by the number of interruptions they prevent. Write the story itself, not a summary of it — around a thousand words, with scene, a specific protagonist, and no robots-take-our-jobs moralising.

Three constraints. First, someone in the story should make the obvious machine-breaking objection, and the story has to answer it the way Marx does, by distinguishing machinery in itself from its employment by capital — answered through what the characters can and cannot actually do about their situation, not through a speech. Second, the ending should be neither a dystopian catastrophe nor a reconciliation; I want the harder Marxian difficulty, that the workers' acquiring or seizing the automated plant does not by itself dissolve the social form the machinery embodies, and that the fragmentation of the worker into an attendant of the machine partly survives the change of owner. Third, the plant's own accountants should register, without anyone theorising it, that something has gone wrong with profit even though material output has never been higher.

Then add one short paragraph outside the story on which part of the thesis the fiction carried and which part it quietly skipped.
