My claim is specifically that typogenetic self-replication is quine- or von Neumann-type rather than mirror-type, and that this is what makes the isomorphism substantive rather than decorative.

So: what would count against it? Rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, and give me the ones a defender of the isomorphism would themselves concede, not just the critics' favourites.
