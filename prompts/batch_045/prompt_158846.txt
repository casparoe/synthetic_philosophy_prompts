(2) I don't like the accuracy / scoring-rule arguments (Brier and friends) — the choice of scoring rule looks arbitrary to me, and I'd rather have an argument that doesn't smuggle in a measure of accuracy;

(4) when I'm actually forced to act — set a price, take a bet, choose — it's fine to sharpen to any point in that range, and rationality doesn't pick out which one;
(5) conditionalization is the right update rule, and the diachronic 'Dutch strategy' argument is a decent argument for it;
