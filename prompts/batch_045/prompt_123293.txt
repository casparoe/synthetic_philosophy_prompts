Case 1. Amara, asked for the first time to compute 68 + 57, unhesitatingly answers 125. My verdict: she meant addition, 125 is the correct answer for her, and she got it right. There is a fact of the matter here, and her answer tracks it.

Case 2. Ben was drilled exactly as Amara was. We later find his old exercise book in which, entirely on his own initiative, he wrote 68 + 57 = 5 and explained to his sister that this is how the big ones work; asked now, he answers 5 and sincerely insists that this is what he always meant. Case 4. Case 5.
