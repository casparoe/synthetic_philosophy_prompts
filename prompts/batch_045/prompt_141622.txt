So, the paradox: if that's right, then my saying "I'm conscious" isn't caused by my being conscious, which means my belief that I'm conscious merely happens to line up with the facts — a lucky true belief, basically a Gettier case, not knowledge. And the host said the standard reply doesn't help either: sure, the brain state causes both the experience and the belief, so the two are correlated — but a physically identical zombie twin with no experiences at all would say exactly the same thing, so the belief still isn't really tracking the experience.
