I'm doing a piece on inverse reinforcement learning and I keep coming back to one researcher I've been interviewing — she's unusually strict about when you're allowed to read a preference off a behaviour, and I want to know what she'll say about my last senario before I put it to her, so I can ask a decent follow-up instead of just nodding. Her verdicts so far, on cases I raised: a thermostat cycling a furnace — no preference there, just a setpoint; a rat that presses a food lever until it's sated and then stops — yes, and she's happy to let the reward change over time; crowdworkers paid per label turning in sloppy work — no, that's the payment scheme, it says nothing about their standards; a driver in a simulator who brakes late and hard — yes, but only up to an equivalence class, since wanting to arrive fast and liking the risk fit the trace equally well; shoppers who always take the second-cheapest olive oil — yes, but only relative to that shelf, don't go on to conclude they're thrifty people; someone who takes the long way home because they wrongly think the short road is closed — no, that's a belief and not a desire; everyone clicking accept-all on a cookie banner — no, that's a dark pattern; a guy who rates a show two stars and then rewatches it twice — yes, he likes it, and the rating is the thing that needs explaining away. The case I actually want: logs from a video app where a chunk of users thumbs-down nearly every item in some category, watch those same items to the end anyway, and tell interviewers they do it to clean up their feed — except the ranker barely uses explicit thumbs and mostly runs on watch time, so the users' theory of the machine is false. Will she say a preference has been recovered here, and if so a preference for what — less of that category in the feed, or something else entirely? What's the rule she's implicitly running that fits all eight of those earlier calls, what's a rival rule that fits them just as well but comes out differently on mine, and what single change to the app would tease the two apart?
