To make it concrete, here's a variant I invented. Call it fragile rationality. Each player is independently "working" with probability 1−ε and "broken" with probability ε. If you're working you maximize expected utility given your beliefs. If you're broken your action is drawn uniformly at random. Everyone always knows their own status and never knows anyone else's. All of this — ε, the independence, what broken players do — is common knowledge.
