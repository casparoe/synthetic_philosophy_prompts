It forms no compressed model and recognizes no similarity between non-identical states—much as Funes is said to be unable to grasp that a single word can apply to a dog perceived at two different moments from two different angles, or that a scheme giving every integer its own arbitrary proper name could count as a genuine system of enumeration.
