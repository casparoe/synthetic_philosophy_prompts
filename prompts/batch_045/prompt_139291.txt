I'm designing a benchmark that has to classify trained systems as either goal-misgeneralizing or merely failing to generalize their capabilities, and I want the specification before I pick a definition. Write the desiderata an adequate definition of goal misgeneralization must meet for that purpose: the cases it must adjudicate, the distinctions it must not collapse, the facts about how such systems are trained that it must be consistent with — with the case or consideration behind each requirement — and mark which are non-negotiable and which are just preferences of one camp.

The cases I want handled: an agent that reliably navigated to a goal marker in training and follows the landmark it was correlated with once the marker moves; the same agent failing out of distribution because it cannot perceive the novel input at all, or because the layout is unlike anything it saw; a system whose measured proxy keeps improving after deployment while the thing we actually cared about collapses, including when that starts on the training distribution itself; a system that behaves as intended while the stakes are low and defects once they are high enough to be worth defecting on; two systems with identical training loss and identical in-distribution behavior that diverge out of distribution, one tracking the goal and one not; a system that pursues the right goal but over the wrong scope or time horizon; and a language model that imitates helpful text without, on some views, having a goal at all.

Two things I especially want. First, flag any pair of desiderata you think cannot be jointly satisfied — I suspect the demand that the definition be checkable from behavior alone, without a formal specification of the intended goal, is in tension with the demand that it separate goal misgeneralization from ordinary capability failure, and I want to know whether that is a genuine impossibility or only a practical one. Second, say which single requirement excludes the most otherwise attractive definitions, and test that requirement against the case where the training signal is consistent with several distinct goals and nothing in the setup settles which was intended. No full scorecard, please; a sentence each on how a behavior-first reading, an internal-representation reading, and an idealized-specification reading of "goal" fare on the requirements is enough.
