Me and my next-door neighbour have been fighting for over two years about a strip of land between our two houses. It's maybe two metres wide, basically a side passage. Whoever gets it can put a gate in and reach their back garden without walking through the house. Neither of us really needs it badly — if we lost we'd both just be mildly inconvenicted.

Here's the bit that makes it wierd. The council is doing a drainage job on our street and as part of it they're going to formally settle the boundary. The officer handling it told both of us, very off the record, that she'll take into account "who has been maintaining and defending the land." So the money we spend on surveyors and solicitors isn't purely burnt. Every few hundred euros I spend also nudges my odds of getting the passage up a little. Same for him. And we both know the other one knows that. If we both just shut up and did nothing she'd probably split it down the middle, which I'd honestly be fine with.

We're each in for something like nine thousand euros now. The land is worth maybe four. My wife says we're throwing good money after bad and should offer to split and stop. But when I say "if I stop now he just carries on and gets it," that doesn't feel like stubbornness to me, it feels like a fact about him. Also — and I hate to admit this — part of me is now enjoying it. I've started keeping a spreadsheet of what he's spent, which I know is not normal. And I think another part of it is that if I cave here I become the guy who caves, and we've got a shared fence, a shared drain and a massive tree to argue about for the next twenty years. He's a builder. He's got more cash than me and I'm pretty sure he knows it.

A friend said this is a known thing and sent me something about an experiment where people bid on a dollar bill and end up paying five dollars for it. I dont really see it — in our case the money isn't wasted, it shifts the odds. Is he right that it's the same trap, or is ours actually a different animal?

And whatever the answer is: what do I do? There's a mediation session in three weeks. Should I turn up having already decided I'm done? Does saying "I'm done" out loud just hand it to him? Is there any way to stop that doesn't involve loosing?
