The setup: the wasp stings a cricket so it can't move, hauls it back to her burrow, leaves it on the doorstep, goes down inside on her own to check the burrow is clear, comes back up, and then drags the cricket in. Our teacher said the observations are ancient and possibly exaggerated so let's just pretend they're exactly true.
