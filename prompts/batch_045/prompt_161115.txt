I am a professor of philosophy. My first career was in quantitative psychology, so the vocabulary I think in without effort is measurement theory: latent variables and their indicators, reliability, content validity versus criterion validity, nomological networks, measurement invariance across populations and instruments, criterion contamination, and the difference between a noisy indicator and a misrepresentation. That frame is second nature to me; the recent philosophy of mind literature is not, and I do not want it explained to me at an introductory level.

What I want is a translation. Restate, as faithfully as you can, the current dispute about whether large language models should be ascribed beliefs and desires — the instrumentalist line that predictive success from the intentional stance licenses attribution, the realist reply that prediction is not enough and that attribution must be answerable to the system's internal structure and to what the system can get wrong, and the narrower skeptical worry that a system trained to imitate human text will display the marks of belief whatever its internal organization — in my measurement vocabulary. Then give me the residue report I actually care about, in three parts: what survives the translation with no loss, what survives only if you bend it, with the bending made explicit, and what has no expression in measurement terms at all.

Two things I want tested rather than assumed. First, I keep wanting to render the skeptical worry as a case of criterion contamination: every behavioral indicator of belief is impure because the training target was the expression of belief. Tell me whether that is a genuine analogue or whether I am smuggling in a measurement assumption and calling it a translation. Second, consider a variant system whose internal states are fully interpretable, so that the best predictions come from reading them off directly rather than from adopting any stance. Does measurement theory have room for that contrast between direct measurement of a construct and inference from indicators, and does it change any verdict you have given?
