hi — i'm in 11th grade and i help run game afternoon for my school's math club, and there's this thing i keep running into that i dont have a word for.

The game: everybody picks a number from 0 to 100, and whoever's number is closest to two-thirds of the average of all the numbers wins a pizza gift card. I know the game itself probably has a name, that's not what i'm asking about. We've ran it three times now.

Run 1, 24 people. The kid who won put down 22. He told me after that he'd done "two steps": figure most people say 50, so two-thirds of that is 33, so most people will say 33, so i say 22. Another kid sat there for ten minutes, said out loud "but if everyone thinks that then everyone says 22, so it's 15, so it's 10, so it's 0," and wrote down 1. He came second to last.

Run 2, 25 people, all of whom had played before. The winners went lower, like 12 to 15. The kid who went all the way down to 0 again did awful.

Run 3, we split into two rooms of 12 and told each room out loud that the other room was full of people who only think one step. That was a lie — both rooms got told the same thing, and both rooms mostly think two steps. Both rooms guessed higher than in run 2, and the two rooms' averages came out about the same as each other, so being wrong in the same direction seemed to just cancel out and nobody got anything out of it.

So thinking it through past a certain point makes you lose, and it doesn't feel smooth — it feels like there's a right depth and going one step past it costs you about as much as not thinking at all.

The other half of it: when the rules get said out loud to everyone in the room, the game settles down fast. When the exact same sentences get sent to everyone privately in our group chat, people immediately start asking "did everyone see that" and it doesn't settle, even though everyone got the message and everyone could see everyone else was in the chat. Nobody has more information in one case than in the other.

I think those two things are the same thing but I'm not sure. Is there a name for it? I don't just want a word — I want to know what exactly is going on, what it is NOT (people keep telling me it's "just game theory" and that tells me nothing), whether the depth thing has a shape you could actually predict ahead of time, whether it depends on how many people are playing, and whether the room-vs-chat thing is really the same phenomenon as the numbers thing or a different one that just looks similar. And if there's no name, make one up and tell me exactly what it covers and where it stops. Please don't just point me at papers or books — I can't get them from here and they'd be over my head anyway, I want it explained in your own words.
