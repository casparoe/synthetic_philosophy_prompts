I'm trying to get a short paper going on teleosemantics, and I want a case built to order: one and the same token representational state where the producer-side, etiological version of the theory (Millikan/Neander-ish: content is what the state's producing mechanism has the historical proper function of indicating) and the consumer-side version (Papineau-ish: content is the condition that has to hold for the consuming system's response to the state to perform its own proper function) assign different contents. Both have to say the state has content, and I want it to come out true on both readings.

Constraints, since I know how these usually go:

1. No swampmen or their descendants, no brains in vats, no surgically or digitally rewired nervous systems, no Cartesian kit. It should be something that could actually turn up in evolutionary history (ordinary domestication or breeding is fine too).
2. Nothing malfunctioning. The producer produces the state as it was selected to; every consumer does what it was selected to do. No misfirings, no mimicry or exploitation by another organism, no recent environmental shift that breaks a correlation. The disagreement has to arise inside normal functioning, not out of a breakdown.
3. The wedge has to be structural and historical, not "the world changed" or "the mechanism erred." Concretely: I want two downstream consumers, both working fine, whose success-conditions are distinct properties F and P — and don't let F and P be related as genus to species, or determinable to determinate, or one a special case of the other. No quietly collapsing the disjunction back into a natural kind.
4. Give the consumer-side theory its standard repairs before you declare a win: privilege the most demanding consumer; demand the condition all consumers jointly need; restrict to Normal conditions in Millikan's sense. Tell me which, if any, the case survives.

Scenario in one paragraph, then the check: what each theory says and why, the single structural feature doing the work, what happens if you remove it, and the strongest reply the worse-off theory has — is this an objection, or just a friendly amendment? Under 600 words if you can. No citations needed; I want the machinery, not the literature.
