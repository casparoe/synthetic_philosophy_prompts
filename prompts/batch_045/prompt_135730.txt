So I'm a novelist and I'm trying to hang a whole book on this thing I keep circling: the typogenetics bit in Hofstadter's Gödel, Escher, Bach, where he builds a fake DNA out of letter-strands and enzymes that chew on them like tiny typographical programs, and then argues that somewhere in there a strand has to bring about its own replication, the way a Gödel sentence manages to be about itself. I sort of get it. I can't tell it.

In two weeks I've got to do it in about twenty minutes, out loud, for a room of other novelists at a residency — sharp people, zero math, zero biology, and they will check out the instant anything looks like a formula. So don't explain it to me; hand me the telling. What has to land in what order (I'm assuming the quine has to be in place before Gödel shows up, but if that's backwards say so, and say why), and what's the one example that carries the whole thing — my best idea is a book that contains the instructions for printing itself, so tell me what that image needs to have and what it fatally lacks. Then the misconceptions, in the order they'll actually hit me: that this is a claim about real DNA rather than a toy that borrows DNA's look, that "self-replicating" means the strand copies itself unaided with no machinery, and that a strand which merely happens to get copied by an enzyme already floating in the soup is the same thing as one that brings about its own copier — I think that last one is the whole ballgame and I don't know when to spring it.

Also the wobble I keep tripping over: a self-rep *makes* a copy of itself, Gödel's sentence only *says* something about itself. If I sell the isomorphism as an identity it'll come apart on them later, so where in the twenty minutes do I own that, and what do I put in its place? They're also going to ask to be shown the strand, and I genuinely can't recall whether Hofstadter ever exhibits one or just argues it's out there — tell me how to handle that without stalling. One question at the end that shows me they got the encoding trick and not just the vibe. And tell me what to flat-out refuse to explain at this level.
