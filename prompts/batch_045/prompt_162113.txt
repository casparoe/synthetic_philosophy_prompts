I work in condensed matter and statistical field theory, and the vocabulary I actually think in is that of effective field theory: microscopic action, coarse-graining and RG flow, fixed points, relevant/irrelevant/marginal operators, universality classes and their basins, matching conditions, singular limits, and the gap between a continuum limit's being well defined and its being computable. I know the reduction-and-emergence literature only from the outside.

Take as your test case the dispute over whether a higher-level special-science description — thermodynamics, say — is autonomous from, or reducible to, the microphysical one. Restate the main positions in my vocabulary, one by one, as faithfully as you can: Nagel-style derivational reduction with bridge identifications, the multiple-realization argument against it, weak or merely epistemic emergence versus strong or brute emergence, and the claim of downward causation. Then give me the residue report: what translates cleanly onto features of RG flow and EFT, what translates only if you quietly import an assumption my framework does not itself contain, and what has no counterpart in my vocabulary at all.

Three cases I want adjudicated explicitly. First, I want to separate what I'll call fixed-point autonomy — the macro law is a property of the fixed point, insensitive to any change in the microscopic action that stays in the basin — from fragile autonomy — it holds only because certain irrelevant couplings happen to be numerically small at the scales we probe, and would dissolve under a modest retuning of the microphysics. Which philosophical claims are supported by the first but not the second, and does any of them assert the first while its actual argument delivers only the second? Second, is there a home in the debate for the position derivable in principle from the microphysics but not computable by us: a genuine third option, or a refugee both camps disown? Third, can downward causation honestly be rendered as the constraint that the effective theory admit a sensible UV completion, or does that rendering assume what was at issue?

For each piece of residue, say whether it shows my vocabulary is missing something the philosophers need, or that their claim was less clear than it looked.
