I've been sorting my gut verdicts about what rung of Dennett's ladder (Darwinian, Skinnerian, Popperian, Gregorian creatures) various things actually sit on, and I want the test I'm implicitly using, if there is one.

- A Sphex-type wasp that reruns its whole prey-dragging routine when you move the cricket: Darwinian, and moving the cricket doesn't make it even a little bit Skinnerian in my book.
- Pigeon pecking a key on a variable-ratio schedule: Skinnerian, no contest.
- Sultan the chimp fitting two sticks together to rake in a banana, first attempt, after a long stretch of just sitting and looking: Popperian. The bad options seem to have died in his stead.
- A chess engine expanding and pruning millions of lines before it moves: not Popperian, just Darwinian with a bigger tree — even though, like Sultan, it kills candidates off before acting in the world.
- Betty the crow bending a piece of wire into a hook on her first encounter with the task: Popperian, but not Gregorian, since she didn't inherit the hook from anybody.
- A parrot using learned English-ish labels to ask for things: Gregorian (words are cultural tools), but not Popperian — which I realize breaks the idea that the four kinds nest inside each other.
- A language model that's been RLHF'd and writes out chain-of-thought before answering: Gregorian-ish by the same reasoning as the parrot, not Popperian for the same reason. But I'm way less comfortable saying that than I am about the parrot, and I cant put my finger on why.

The case I keep flip-flopping on: a scrub jay that re-caches its food when another jay was watching. If it's modelling what the other bird can see, that's Popperian; if it just learned a rule of the form "re-cache when seen," it's Skinnerian; and the behaviour alone doesn't settle it for me.

Is there one criterion that gets all of these, or nearly all, and what does it have to throw out? Please also say what it predicts for two cases I haven't listed: a human expert making a split-second intuitive call, and an ant colony converging on a shortest path.
