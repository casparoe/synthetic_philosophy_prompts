Take a cave-dwelling population of one of my moths, isolated long enough that there are no bats in the cave at all. Now:

1. The receptor itself has not changed. A1 is still tuned roughly where it always was, 20–50 kHz, same thresholds.
2. The old consumer — the last-ditch dive/steering-away circuit that A1 used to drive — is still anatomically wired up, but the motor side has degenerated, so firing in that pathway no longer produces any behaviour at all. It's vestigial.
3. A new consumer has evolved. Males court females with broadband ultrasonic clicks with plenty of energy up at 30–40 kHz, and females have a selected phonotaxis circuit: A1 firing now drives oriented flight upwind toward the clicking male. Females that orient to clicks mate more; clean selection story.
4. There is a waterfall near the cave mouth that produces broadband ultrasound. Females near it orient to the falls and drown. This happens reliably enough to be a real selective cost, but not enough to eliminate the pathway.

What I want you to argue for is this: that the honest teleosemantic thing to say about the A1 firing state in these moths is that it has no single determinate content — it has at least two, one indexed to each consuming system (a now-vestigial *bat-ish* content and a live *male-ish* one) — and that the obvious ways of restoring uniqueness all fail. Specifically I want you to knock down, one at a time:

- the historical move: that the receptor was selected for bat detection, that's its proper function, end of story, and the new pathway is just a lucky free-rider on an old device;
- the producer-proliferation move: that the right consumer is whichever one explains why the receptor itself got reproduced and spread;
- the specificity move: that you take the most demanding, most specific set of Normal conditions on offer and let that be *the* content.

And then — this is the part the reading group keeps beating me up about — argue that this pluralism is a stable position and not just a slide into content being cheap, i.e. say what still constrains ascription of content to a signal once you've gone pluralist. In particular I want you to deal with the objection that pluralism wrecks misrepresentation: if content is indexed to consumers, why isn't the waterfall case simply another content rather than an outright error? I'd also like you to say what, on your view, the *right* thing to say about the waterfall females is, and why.
