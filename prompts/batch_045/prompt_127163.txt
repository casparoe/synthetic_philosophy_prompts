So me and my flatmate Priya have been going round in circles about prostitution policy for about two years now and we want to try the adversarial collaboration thing, where you produce the document we'd both actually sign at the end. She's firmly in the camp that sex work is work and should be fully decriminalised (New Zealand style, no special licensing regime, normal labour and safety law applies, third parties allowed like in any other job), and I'm the one who thinks the Nordic model is basically right: decriminalise the person selling, keep criminalising the buying, put real money into exits. We both keep using Germany and the Netherlands as evidence against each other and I suspect we're not even agreeing on what those setups are, so the document has to sort that out too.

Rules: please don't give us any citations, studies, reports, or percentages. No "a 2010 evaluation found that X went down by Y". We've both read enough to have priors and we want the structure of the argument, not a bibliography — pretend we're in a room with no internet and just our own reasoning. And plain language please, we're not academics, but don't smooth the disagreements into mush either.

What I want:

A numbered section of agreed claims. I mean substantial ones, not "we both want people to be safe" — things like what we think the actual goal of policy is, what we think the current situation does to people, what we think the state is for, what we believe about how many people sell sex and why, what we think the buyers are like, and what we believe about the German/Dutch systems versus the New Zealand one versus the Nordic one. If there's something surprising buried in there that we agree on, say so explicitly.

Then a table of the disagreements, one per row, with columns: (1) the disagreement in wording neither of us would object to, (2) how I would put my own view, in words I'd sign, (3) how Priya would put hers, in words she'd sign, (4) whether you think this row is a real disagreement or a fight about words, (5) what piece of evidence or what concrete case both of us would accept as bearing on it, (6) what I'd predict we'd find, and (7) what Priya would predict — written down in advance, before either of us gets to see the answer.

Some things I want covered, add more if you spot them:

- Whether the German/Dutch licensed-brothel model tells us anything about New Zealand style decriminalisation. Priya says they're different animals because registration, brothel licensing and zoning create a two-tier market and push anyone who's not a citizen or resident outside the legal bit. I say the direction of travel is the same and that decrim ends up looking like that in practice anyway.
- Whether the goal is a smaller sex industry or better conditions and rights for the people already in it, and whether "shrinking the market" is even something you could in principle measure. I think this is where we're really split.
- Third parties. Test case: four women share a flat, work from it, and split the cost of hiring one person to do door security and another to do their books and taxes; nobody takes a percentage of anybody else's earnings. Is that a pimp? What about the landlord who knows, the taxi driver who drives them, the person who builds their website, the friend who babysits?
- The boring stuff that I think might break her view: if you can't declare the income without losing disability benefits or housing benefit, or if you have no immigration status, decriminalisation doesn't obviously help you at all. What does each position owe people in that situation?
- Remote stuff — camming, subscription content, no physical contact, no client in the room. Does my logic even reach that? Does hers?
- What we each think a buyer is: perpetrator, customer, or just some guy. And whether that matters for what the law should do.

Finally, for each crux, say honestly which one of us would actually move if it came out the other way, and which one of us you suspect would quietly move the goalposts. And if you think part of our two-year argument was never about anything real, tell us which part and why.
