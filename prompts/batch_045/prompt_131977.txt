I'm writing a paper on the varieties-worth-wanting question in the free will debate — whether showing that an agent has the capacities compatibilists cite settles the further question of whether that is the freedom anyone actually wanted. I need one case that isolates a residual demand, built so that neither side can wave it away.

Construct a case in which an agent has, at the moment of acting, everything a reasons-responsive actual-sequence compatibilist counts as free and responsible agency: she responds to reasons, would have acted otherwise had she had sufficient reason to, is not coerced, reflectively identifies with the motives she acts on, and her deliberation makes a causal difference to what she does. Yet the case must raise a genuine question whether what she has is a freedom worth wanting — raised by the structure of the case, not stipulated (no she feels unfree, no narrator's aside telling us to be uneasy).

Four constraints, each doing real work:

1. It must not turn on determinism. The unease should survive filling in the causal details either way, so nobody can say this is the standard incompatibilist intuition in costume.
2. The residual demand must be neither alternative possibilities at the moment of action nor ultimate self-origination, so that both standing dismissals miss: that the worry is really a hankering for randomness, and that it amounts to wanting to be the cause of oneself. What I have in mind is diachronic — something about whether the standards from which she now deliberates are hers in a way that isn't merely inheritance.
3. No designer, manipulator, or coercive history, so a history-sensitive compatibilist cannot file it as a manipulation case and return the usual verdict.
4. Realistic: one paragraph, a decision a person could actually face (a family or workplace decision, not a laboratory), no implants, predictors, or exotic physics.

Then: what a reasons-responsive compatibilist, a history-sensitive compatibilist, and a leeway incompatibilist each say about the case and why; which feature of the case is doing the work and what happens if that feature is removed; which position the case most pressures and how a defender of that position would attack it. Separate what is settled here from what is genuinely contested among people who work on these questions, and treat the coherence of my residual demand as an open question rather than a premise. If the four constraints cannot be met jointly, say which one has to give and why instead of quietly dropping it.
