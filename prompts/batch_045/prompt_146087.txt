Population pressure, harvest failures, war, fiscal stress, all of it works the way it normally does.

The proposition: if we had five hundred years of decent quantitative data from that society and ran the same kind of analysis on it, we would still recover a long cycle with the same sort of period and comparable amplitude to the real historical cases, driven by the population-immiseration and state-fiscal channels on their own.
