Taking a moral responsibility module this term and we just did Strawson: the reactive attitudes, the participant stance versus the objective attitude, and the thought that holding someone responsible isn't really a theory we apply to a person but something we're doing inside a relationship. Can you read the film through that lens? Where does it actually illuminate what's going on between them, and where does the film resist the reading?
