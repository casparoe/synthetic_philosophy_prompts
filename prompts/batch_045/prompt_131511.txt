Every belief, every inclination and every conscious experience of the two is the same; the only difference is the stuff around them. Yet when Oscar says 'water' he refers to H2O, and when Twin Oscar says 'water' he refers to XYZ. So the reference of a natural-kind word is not settled by anything inside the speaker's head.
