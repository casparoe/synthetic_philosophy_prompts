Here is where I've landed, and I want to know what would knock me off it. I no longer think dialetheism stands or falls on the paradoxes — the liar and its relatives motivate it fine. I think it stands or falls on the logic: whether a paraconsistent logic can do the work ordinary reasoning needs.

Three steps.
(1) To be usable, the weaker logic has to get classical reasoning back somehow. The only live strategy I can see is a "well-behavedness" operator — write it ○A, read "A is not both true and false" — where once you've established ○A you recover modus ponens and disjunctive syllogism inside that domain. Call this recapture.
(2) But ○ is itself governed by the weaker logic, so ○A can itself come out true-and-false, and then recapture doesn't fire: you cannot climb out of the hole using the logic that put you in it.
(3) And the dialetheist's own treatment of Curry's paradox (roughly: the Curry conditional is itself both true and false, so detachment fails, and no explosion is needed to get triviality anyway) pulls the other way from recapture, because recapture just is a license to detach in the good cases.

So my position: dialetheism faces a squeeze. Keep the failure of detachment that dissolves Curry and you lose ordinary reasoning; recover detachment through recapture and you re-open the door to Curry-style revenge. It apparently needs both and can't have both.

What would count against this? I want the arguments and distinctions that should move me, ranked from fatal to merely awkward, including: which of my three steps a dialetheist would concede as genuine exposure and which they'd dismiss as resting on a confusion (about whether recapture has to be a detachable conditional, or about what it even means to "get modus ponens back"); whether there's a version of recapture that survives ○A itself being a dialetheia; what would have to be true for the squeeze to be a false dilemma; and an honest verdict on whether I have stated things so that nothing could count against me — and if I have, whether that is a problem for a criticism of this kind.
