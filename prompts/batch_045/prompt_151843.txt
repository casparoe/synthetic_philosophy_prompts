Across several areas of physics the same inferential move is standard practice: one posits a distribution over the degrees of freedom one has not measured, treats those degrees of freedom as statistically independent unless some dynamics forces a dependence, and regards any appeal to a residual correlation as a "conspiracy" that costs a theory its credibility. An assumption of molecular chaos is what makes the approach to equilibrium come out right; measurement independence in Bell-type experiments is treated as close to compulsory, with its denial filed under superdeterminism rather than treated as a hypothesis; an early phase of inflation is credited with erasing primordial correlations; and a dimensionless parameter far from order unity is called fine-tuned and taken to cry out for explanation.

But the practice is uneven. The independence assumption that is mandatory in one setting is disreputable in the next; the low-entropy initial condition is granted special status even though taking typicality at face value elsewhere seems to predict isolated fluctuation observers rather than data like ours; and the time-symmetry of the underlying dynamics is handled by importing precisely the sort of special-initial-condition postulate that the no-conspiracy norm elsewhere rules out of court.

A colleague has proposed to formalize the norm as a simplicity ordering on measures: among distributions compatible with everything observed, prefer the one minimizing total correlation between subsystems, with any proposed correlation charged its own description length. My question is what the practice presupposes. Does it require that such an ordering tracks something in the world — an objective, measure-relative notion of typicality — or can a thoroughgoing subjectivist reconstruct the whole pattern as bookkeeping about our ignorance, with "generic" doing no explanatory work at all? Which parts of the practice strictly presuppose an objectivist view, which merely sit well with it, and where is the practice underdetermined, licensed equally by incompatible philosophies of probability? And if the norm were dropped and every independence assumption had to be dynamically derived, what would be left to predict?
