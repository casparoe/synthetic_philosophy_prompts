I'm an ML engineer — mostly variational inference and probabilistic graphical models — so I'm fluent in factors, latent variables, Pearl-style soft/virtual evidence (likelihood ratios rather than a specified observation), I-projections and M-projections, exponential-family tilts, IPF, EM, and hierarchical Bayes. Not a philosopher, though I go to a reading group with some philosophy grad students and last week we got completely stuck on the Judy Benjamin case, and I've been turning it over on the commute since. Here's the setup as I understood it: a soldier is dropped into terrain her map divides four ways — which company's area she's in, and which battalion's area. Call A = she's in the Second Company's area, B = she's in the Second Battalion's area. Her prior over the four quadrants is P(A,B)=0.2, P(A,not-B)=0.3, P(not-A,B)=0.1, P(not-A,not-B)=0.4. Her radio crackles, and what she learns is apparently not a fact about where she is but a conditional: if she's in the Second Company's area, then she's in the Second Battalion's area. So the datum rules out the (A, not-B) quadrant and, supposedly, says nothing else. The room then split into two camps that produced two different posteriors and neither would budge. One camp just conditions on not-(A and not-B) and gets roughly (0.286, 0.143, 0.571) — which drags P(A) down from 0.5 to about 0.286, i.e. the message ends up being evidence against her being in the Second Company's area at all, which felt wrong to half the people there. The other camp pins P(A) at 0.5 and dumps all the freed mass into (A,B), getting (0.5, 0.1, 0.4). What I noticed afterwards, and want checked, is that both of these look to me like I-projections of the same prior onto linear constraint sets that differ only by whether you also impose the rigidity constraint Q(A) = P(A) — so the fight isn't about the update rule at all, it's about which constraints the datum imposes. I also suspect that the choice of partition in Jeffrey's probability kinematics is doing the same work as choosing which node the evidence enters my model at, but I'm not confident that's the right way to put it.
