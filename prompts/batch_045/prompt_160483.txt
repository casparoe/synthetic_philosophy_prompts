Please explain the thing I cannot get straight about this: why the Gregorian grade is a distinct grade at all, rather than the Popperian grade scaled up by cultural accumulation. My question is whether such a creature counts as Gregorian on the taxonomy's own terms, or whether being Gregorian secretly requires being Popperian first.
