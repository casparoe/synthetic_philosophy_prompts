Every joke i've found about this is either just an algorithm-is-racist bit or generic nerd filler, and neither of those turns on anything.

So: give me a joke whose punchline actually depends on *why* those criteria conflict, not just on the fact that they do. It should be funny to someone who knows the result, and at least half-funny to someone who doesn't.

Angle i had in mind, use it if it works: a vendor sells compliance software to cities. Some options if they help: they redraw the group boundaries until the base rates match; they satisfy each criterion on a different subpopulation and nobody ever checks the overlap; they ship a model that only scores applicants it's confident about and abstains on everyone else, which technically passes everything; they sell the three criteria as separately priced add-ons and the city can only afford one. Take whichever is funniest, or come up with a better workaround than any of those.

Then in three or four sentences, tell me what the joke is leaning on — the exact bit of the result someone has to know for it to land — and be honest about whether it still works on a person who's only vaguely heard that you can't satisfy all the fairness criteria at once without knowing why. If it doesn't, tweak it until it does.

Bonus if you can also give me a short second version for the guy in the third row who thinks the whole impossibility literature is a distraction because the criteria were the wrong thing to aim at in the first place.

Constraints: no punching down, no algorithm wordplay, and don't let the joke turn into a mini-lecture — whatever the theorem does in there, it should be smuggled in, not explained.
