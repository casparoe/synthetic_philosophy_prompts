hi — I'm in a consciousness seminar this term and my final paper is on heterophenomenology. My thesis is that the method can't be both as neutral and as complete as it advertises: its objects are supposed to be just what the subject's text and third-personal behaviour add up to — no believing or disbelieving the reports — and yet it's also supposed to leave nothing out about what it's like for the subject. I want one case that pries those apart, and I dont want the usual inverted-spectrum or blindsight setup, my TA is sick of both.

Spec: give me two conditions, C1 and C2, in an ordinary perception or psychophysics lab (a clinic is fine too) — realistic, no sci-fi, no lesions, nothing exotic. The subject is as good a reporter as you can get: cooperative, practiced at describing their own experience, not confused, not confabulating, and they don't back down when pressed. Every datum heterophenomenology admits — spontaneous remarks, answers under questioning, forced-choice discrimination, matching, confidence, reaction times, anything else you can observe from outside — comes out identical in C1 and C2, so the method has to record no difference. But the case still has to make us want to say the experience changed, and that pull has to come from inside the setup rather than from you stipulating it: either from a mechanism we independently accept changes experience elsewhere, or from the subject's own stable comparative judgement that something about how it looks has shifted even though nothing looks different. I've been calling that a silent shift, but build it how you like.

Then do the check: one paragraph for the scenario; what heterophenomenology says about C1 vs C2 and why it has to say that; what the rival method says — mechanism-first, or the one that treats a subject's own sense of a contrast as data about experience, your pick, but say which one you're using — and why; which single feature of the case is doing the work; what happens to the case if you remove that feature; and how a Dennett-style defender would attack it, and whether that attack is a real argument or just the method's commitments in a different hat. If the spec honestly can't be met without cheating, tell me which constraint has to go and why.
