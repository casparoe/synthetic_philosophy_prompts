The insurance client wants us to put a single number in the report: the millisecond at which she consciously registered the hazard. Their argument is that surely either she was aware of the car at 500 ms or she wasn't, and the whole negligence question hangs on which, so we just need to measure it properly.

Our system can also fire a small vibration through the steering wheel at whatever delay we set, and then ask the driver right at that moment whether she'd seen the car. In a handful of runs she reports she hadn't seen the car yet and then brakes correctly about 200 ms later.
