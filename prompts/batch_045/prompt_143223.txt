I am helping draft the terms of reference for a public body — call it the Currency Board — that must publish, for every citizen, a single comparable figure for how well off that person is, and then set transfers so as to reduce inequality. Before we hear from consultants who each favor a different currency — welfare, resources, capabilities — I want the specification any candidate currency has to meet. Please write the desiderata for an equalisandum: the thing a theory of egalitarian justice says should be equalized, or for which opportunity should be equalized. Include the cases it must get right (someone who has deliberately trained expensive tastes; someone who, after the same loss, deliberately trained her tastes downward instead; two patients with the same condition and the same income, one of whom needs twice the dose for the same relief; someone whose only deficit is that her life goes badly late rather than early; someone whose disadvantage shows up across a whole life but not in any single year of it), the distinctions it must not collapse (between circumstance and choice, between having a claim and the size of the claim, between equalizing shares and improving the worst off, between a currency for assessing one person's situation and a currency for aggregating across persons), and the results it cannot contradict — the levelling-down objection, reasonable pluralism about what makes a life go well, and the administrative fact that any published figure will be audited, appealed, and defended without intrusive inquiry into people's inner lives. For each requirement, give the case or consideration behind it, and mark it either non-negotiable or the preference of one camp. I would add one requirement of my own, which I want you to test rather than simply adopt: that the currency not be reactive under publication, i.e. that it should not reverse its own verdicts merely because people learn the figure and adapt to it. Then tell me which subset of the desiderata you think cannot be jointly satisfied — that is the part I most want — and which single requirement excludes the most candidate currencies. A short table scoring welfarist, resourcist, and capability views against your list is enough; the criteria matter more than the scoring. End with a one-sentence statement of your bottom line as the final line, beginning Verdict:.
