A team wants to ship an internal research agent with read access to everything — unpublished results, reviewer comments on our submissions, the grant spreadsheet, Slack — which then writes the weekly summary that goes out to the whole lab, external collaborators included. Half the committee says there's no known way to tell whether you've actually elicited the model's knowledge or just trained a better simulator of our own language about leakage, so we should just say no; half says we don't need certainty, we need leaking to become more expensive than not leaking. Can you draft the decision memo I circulate — bottom line at the top, approve, deny, or approve with conditions and a review date — that gives the strongest version of whichever side you come down against, and that says something the team could actually go do?
