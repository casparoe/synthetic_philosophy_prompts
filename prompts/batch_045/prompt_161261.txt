Nothing has positive value: pleasure, enjoyment, satisfied desire, flow, meaning — none of it adds anything over and above the fact that, while it lasts, you aren't craving. A good life isn't a life full of happiness, it's a quiet one. The limit case (nirvana, ataraxia, whatever you want to call it) is a state with zero craving that is not thereby a state of maximum pleasure.
