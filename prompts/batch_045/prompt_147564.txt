I spent a career in experimental low-temperature physics and have only in retirement begun to read philosophy of physics properly. On Maxwell's demon I have settled into the Bennett–Landauer view: measurement, and the copying of a result into memory, can in principle be performed reversibly and without dissipation, so the demon is not defeated by the cost of looking but by the cost of forgetting. The whole burden of saving the second law falls on the kT ln 2 that must be paid when the demon's memory is returned to a standard state; Brillouin's appeal to the irreducible cost of illuminating the molecule, and any residue of talk about the demon's intelligence, strike me as special cases or red herrings.

I would like that position mapped for its exposure rather than defended. What would have to be true for it to be false?

First, the bookkeeping worry. Landauer's principle is often derived from the second law; if so, using it to exorcise a threat to the second law looks circular. Is it circular, or is it a legitimate consistency argument of the kind that also ties information to thermodynamics in the fluctuation relations?

Second, two variants I have been unable to put down. (a) A demon that erases against a cold bath at T_c while the gas is at T_h, paying only kT_c ln 2 per bit to dump the entropy. (b) A demon that never erases: it is supplied with fresh memory, or its used records are pushed into a sink whose entropy accounting is not obviously thermal — spins already at infinite temperature, an unbounded state space, an expanding vacuum. For (a) my own arithmetic yields exactly the Carnot efficiency, which reassures me rather than the reverse; say whether I have smuggled the conclusion in. For (b) I want to know where the cost has actually gone.

Rank the defeaters from fatal to merely awkward, and tell me which a committed defender would concede as counting against the view and which they would dismiss as a misdescription of the setup. I am unmoved by anything that turns on the demon's mind; only an accounting error, or the demonstration that the view has been qualified into one that can re-describe any putative violation as an uncounted reservoir, would move me — and in the latter case say plainly whether that is a defect here or simply what a bookkeeping identity looks like.
