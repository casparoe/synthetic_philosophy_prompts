The thing I want people to walk away having felt, not been told, is a distinction I've been messing with in my head, and I want the activity to produce all three cases on its own. My three levels, and tell me if this is a dumb way to cut it: level 1 is where the path matters but only in a boring way — where you end up depends on where you started, like which city you start building a railroad out from, but any start would of been fine and nobody has regrets. Level 2 is where some tiny arbitrary thing early on decides it — a coin flip, a five minute head start, one loud person — but the thing we got stuck with is just as good as the alternative, so there's nothing to be mad about. Level 3 is the one I actually care about: a tiny arbitrary thing early on decides it and we end up stuck with something worse than an option that was right there and available at the time and everyone could see. My read is that all the famous examples get argued about at exactly this point, people just assert level 2 or assert level 3, and nobody ever feels the difference.

My rough mechanic, which I'm pretty sure is broken: everyone secretly picks one of two options each round, you get more points the more other people picked the same one, so there's a coordination or network payoff, but the two options aren't equal — one gives everyone a slightly higher baseline payoff, so it is the better option and people can tell. Then I rig round one with something small and random (a coin flip that gives one side a tiny bonus, or I hand out a few locked early adopter cards to people picked at random), and there's a switching cost that goes up each round so late switchers get punished and the lock-in is real instead of just a suggestion. Then we replay the whole thing with a different random draw and see whether the better option wins this time. I also want a piece where a few people who either didn't play or only get shown the final tally have to write down why the winner won, because I'm betting they'll write a story about it being better — that's the moment I want if you can make it happen.
