Hume says no inference from the observed to the unobserved can be justified without circularity. Both seem to run the same move: assume nothing, notice the symmetry, conclude nothing follows. But NFL looks like it's assuming something after all, namely a flat distribution over targets, which is itself a strong empirical claim. Is that the crack where the analogy breaks, or does Hume's argument quietly need the same kind of assumption?
