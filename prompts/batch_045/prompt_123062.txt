Can you design a runnable activity for that?

Constraints: no lecture up front and don't name the view until afterwards; nothing anyone has to look up, so describe each candidate system in a sentence and don't lean on any empirical claim we'd need to check; and real but tiny stakes that they actually pay in the room, no money and no phones.

Then give me a debrief that maps what actually happened in the room onto the moving parts of the idea, what will predictably go wrong the first time I run it and how to handle it, and a frank note on what the exercise can't reproduce. My worry is that everything they do is compatible both with there being a real continuum out there and with us just being ignorant about a sharp line somewhere, so the activity might only regiment their third-personal guesses and never touch the part that actually matters.
