Call it a purely vector-compositional architecture: it never tokens a discrete symbol anywhere, every representation is a real-valued vector, and composition is one learned bilinear tensor, so the sentence-level representation of "John loves Mary" is just f(vec John, vec loves, vec Mary) with no parse tree, no slots, no symbol tokens, nothing you could point to as a constituent. Suppose it behaves like this: it generalizes correctly to role reversals ("Mary loves John") and to novel fillers it has never seen, but its generalization degrades sharply past embedding depth three and it more or less falls apart on nested relative clauses.
