I have spent a good many years sitting in on workplace grievance meetings, and I keep running into a distinction I have no word for.

Sometimes a person says the relevant words, everyone present hears and understands them perfectly, the person has every standing to say them, and still nothing has been done until the other side says something back. "I bet you twenty pounds the delivery is late" is not yet a bet. An offer for a car is not yet a deal. But other cases of the same shape seem finished the moment they leave the speaker's mouth: "I am sorry for what I did" is an apology whether or not it is accepted, and "I won't mention it to anyone" is a promise even if the other person says they would rather I made no such promise.

I am not after the general point that saying something can be a way of doing something; I have read that and I accept it. I am after the split inside that class, and two things make it messier than a simple division. First, some verbs seem to move between the two groups: a promise given for nothing looks complete on its own, but the same sentence inside a bargain appears to hang in the air until the other side does their part. Second, in the cases that do need something back, once it arrives people speak as though the thing had been there from the first remark — "we have had a bet since Tuesday" — although on the Tuesday itself they would have insisted there was no bet. Then there is "I forgive you", which some people treat as working on its own and others as empty, even offensive, unless it is received.

Does this difference have a name? If there are established notions close to it, I would like to know which they are and exactly where each fails to cover what I have described. If there is nothing established, please name and define the two kinds yourself, say which of my examples sits in which, explain why some verbs seem to shift, and tell me what to say about the way the completed ones get described as having existed earlier. A single label, on its own, will not settle anything for me.
