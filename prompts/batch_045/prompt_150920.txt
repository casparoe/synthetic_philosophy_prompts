2. privacy as control — the German-ish idea of informational self-determination, where nothing is sacred in itself and what matters is that you steer the flow: you consent, you're told the purpose, you can get it deleted, and the very same fact about you is harmless in one context and creepy in another.

Where I want you to dig in is the case where they come apart: data nobody ever collected from you directly. I mean profiles inferred about you from your behavior by other people's systems — stuff you never disclosed, never consented to, and couldn't really have anticipated. Once the default flips like that, which of the two framings still actually bites?
