Mine was: fewer animals isnt the same as less suffering, and farms aren't empty either. But neither of us could make it precise.

Run his argument on that case properly — and I mean properly: give me the actual accounting (net welfare per hectare-year, animals per hectare, how bad a typical life is and how typical deaths are, the r-selected boom-bust breeder versus the big slow K-selected one), tell me what replaces what out there (voles and field mice, deer, insects, and whether the cattle even belong in the ledger), and don't skip the deaths during the clearance itself or the fact that the prairie animals who supposedly benefit by never existing never exist to benefit.
