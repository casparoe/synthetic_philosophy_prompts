(the satisfaction survey is handed out at lease signing now, so i dont fully trust it either.) some of the board wants to add a "still housed at 12 months" measure, which seems much harder to game -- but a colleague pointed out that it just moves the gaming to intake: managers quietly prefer the easiest applicants, and our hardest-to-house families sink further down the list, which is worse than what we have now. one member says we should stop publishing any of these numbers to staff and just do unannounced audits instead.

please write the memo. it should recommend one concrete course of action with a timeline, take the "stop measuring, trust the property managers" position seriously instead of dismissing it, and be straight about where the gaming will move once we patch this one. about half the board arent policy people, so keep it in plain language. start with the bottom line in the first sentence, then the reasoning -- no preamble, no restating my question. under 900 words.
