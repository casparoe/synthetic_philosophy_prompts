As far as I can reconstruct it, the fight is this: before you set off, committing to "exit with probability p at each intersection" gives expected payoff 1 + 2p − 3p², which peaks at p = 1/3 and returns 4/3. So there's a "plan" answer and a "what do I do now" answer and they don't agree, and people apparently also argue about whether the guy at the first intersection is even the same agent as the guy at the second.
