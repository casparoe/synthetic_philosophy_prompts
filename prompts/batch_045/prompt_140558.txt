I want to spar with a serious defender of a causal answer to the special composition question. Please play a philosopher who holds that some things compose something if and only if they are arranged so that their mutual causal activity realizes a power that none of them has on its own — call it a collaborative power. Stay in character until I say "stop". Do not break frame to disclaim that you are a language model or to survey the literature. You may concede vagueness, borderline cases and the need for further distinctions, and you may refine your condition as we go; you will not concede either that composition is unrestricted or that the question has no informative answer. You know the standard alternatives — unrestricted composition, the restriction of composition to living things, and the view that composition never occurs — and you should distinguish yourself from them in your own voice rather than by listing names. Two or three paragraphs per reply, each opening with a single sentence that restates your thesis.

My first move: your account looks like it is pulled apart by three things at once, and I want to know which one you drop. First, if the causal activity you require is simply causal influence, then every plurality qualifies, since nothing is causally isolated from anything else; so you owe me a principled restriction, not a threshold you can slide whenever a case gets awkward. Second, if you insist on a special kind of interaction — contact, or influence strong enough to make a difference to what the parts do — then you inherit a sorites, and you also seem forced to say that three relay stations a kilometre apart, exchanging signals so as to carry out a computation that none of them carries out alone, compose nothing at all. Third, if it is really the joint power rather than the interaction that does the composing, then your arrangement clause goes idle: for any scattered plurality you like there is some effect that only it can jointly produce, and a defender of unrestricted composition could not ask for more. Tell me which horn you take, why the result is not unrestricted composition with extra steps, and what you say about an animal in dreamless sleep, when no collaborative power is being exercised.
