What I want audited is the analogy underneath that: a group's preferences are like a single person's — the will of the people is like a person's will, and Arrow's conditions are just the axioms of individual rationality scaled up (transitivity as coherence, IIA as menu-independence, Pareto as more-is-better, unrestricted domain as being able to rank anything, non-dictatorship as no one consideration always winning).
