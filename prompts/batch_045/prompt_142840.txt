Does this belong to revisionism, denialism, or a third category? In your answer, distinguish denying that an event occurred from denying its moral or legal characterization, and explain what methodological markers — engagement with the archive, falsifiability, responsiveness to evidence, consensus-seeking, and the allocation of the burden of proof — separate legitimate revision from denial. Please write your answer in American English.
