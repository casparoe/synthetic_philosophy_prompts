Worth Without Witness

1. The target. Nearly everyone agrees that moral worth requires acting for the right reasons; the fights are about what for amounts to. The demanding reading is what I will call the Citation Requirement: moral worth requires that the consideration which makes the action right figure in the explanation of the agent's action in a way that is available to her, such that she could, without being coached, offer that consideration as her reason. Kant's contrast between the shopkeeper who gives correct change because honesty is good for trade and the action done from duty is the canonical staging of it, and the requirement has obvious appeal: it explains why the right action done by accident, or done for a fee, seems not to merit the kind of credit we care about. The loose reading is the family of reasons-responsive accounts, on which worth consists in the agent's being appropriately sensitive to the right-making features of her situation, whether or not she conceptualizes them correctly; the stock case is Huck Finn, who does the right thing while sincerely believing himself to be doing something disgraceful. I want to argue that both readings are after the same thing and that the Citation Requirement is only one way of getting it.

2. Tracking and citing. Distinguish tracking from citing. Tracking is a counterfactual structure: the agent's production of the right action is modulated by the right-making features of the situation, so that had those features been different in specified ways, the action would have differed accordingly. Citing is one way tracking gets realized, namely online, by deliberation that the agent can avow. But tracking can also be realized cached: by a disposition formed during an earlier episode of genuine deliberation and held in place by what I will call the Reopening Condition, which says that if the agent registered some feature of the situation that her settled policy did not anticipate, she would reopen the question rather than plow on. Case: a senior nurse who tells patients the truth about a grim prognosis, not because she re-derives the reason each time, but because years ago she worked out that concealment is a way of stealing from a patient the chance to arrange anything. She does not deliberate now. She would deliberate again if a family asked her to wait until after a wedding, or if the patient's capacity seemed to be fluctuating. I claim her action has moral worth, and not merely reliability.

3. The extension. If cached worth is real, then worth can be acquired by the indirect route of arranging oneself, or one's situation, so that one no longer needs to act from duty at all. A rookie officer who requests a partner she respects and a precinct with functioning body cameras precisely because she distrusts her own impulses in a fight; a person who joins a sponsorship program because she knows her own resolve fails at night. Call this Designed Worth. It is uninviting to a Kantian, because it makes worth a product of management rather than of the will's relation to the law, and attractive to anyone who thinks worth should be attainable by people who are not saints. I want to claim it is not a paradox but a discovery about what worth is for.

4. Three objections. First, the episodic-credit objection: worth is a property of an action on an occasion, so a cached disposition confers only derivative credit, and worse, the nurse's action today would have been the same had her original deliberation been self-serving but landed on the same policy, which suggests my counterfactual profile does not track what I say it tracks. Second, the mesh objection: the Reopening Condition is just a Frankfurt-style identification condition, so I have bought worth at the price of inheriting hierarchical theories and their manipulation problems. Third, the dilution objection: if Designed Worth is worth, then worth can be bought, slept into, and institutionally procured, at which point it collapses into reliability; and reliability is what we want from a nurse anyway, so the category of worth has been evacuated rather than defended.

5. Where I am least sure. I want to concede to Stocker that worth and the value a motive has inside a life can come apart: the visit to a sick friend made from duty can have worth and still be a defective way to visit a friend, and I mean to use that concession as a model for all three objections, saying that worth was never the only metric. My suspicion, though, is that I have merely redescribed the reasons-responsive view with a mechanism story bolted onto it, and that the mechanism story is doing no work, or is secretly doing the work by smuggling back in exactly the deliberation the loose reading was meant to replace.
