I'm writing a short statement of my position in epistemology to circulate before a job talk, and I would like an audit of whether the following commitments are jointly tenable before I commit to them in public.

1. The anti-luck condition on knowledge is safety: S knows that p only if, in the nearby cases in which S believes p in the same way as in the actual case, p is true. I prefer safety to sensitivity partly because sensitivity mishandles contraposition and skeptical scenarios.

2. Knowledge is closed under known entailment, at least for single-premise competent deduction. I do not want to give this up, since most of what I want to say about testimony and memory depends on it.

3. No amount of purely statistical evidence yields knowledge of a lottery-style proposition: you cannot know, on the odds alone, that your ticket will lose, however long the odds are.

4. I reject the KK principle: knowing that p does not require knowing that one knows that p.

5. I accept the Williamson-style margin-for-error argument against luminosity, and I take it to be fully general: no non-trivial condition, including a condition concerning one's own current mental states, is such that whenever it obtains the subject is in a position to know that it obtains. This is my explanation of why (4) holds.

6. I hold the transparency view of self-knowledge in the Evans–Moran–Byrne tradition: if I believe that p, then I am in a position to know that I believe that p, and I settle whether I believe that p by settling whether p.

7. Knowledge is the norm of assertion: one may assert that p only if one knows that p.

8. I treat knowledge as primitive and unanalyzable, the most general factive stative attitude, so the safety condition in (1) is offered only as a necessary condition and not as part of an analysis.

Which of these pull against one another, and how badly? Order the genuine tensions by severity; for each, state the mechanism by which the two commitments conflict, what exactly I would have to give up to restore consistency, and what that would cost me elsewhere in the set. Also separate out any pairs that only look inconsistent and say briefly why the appearance dissolves. Please do not assume I already know which commitment is the weak link — I do not — and stay specific about the mechanics rather than describing the general dialectic of the literature.
