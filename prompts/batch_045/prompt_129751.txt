Now my argument, in two parts, with a distinction that my friend Priya came up with and that I've been leaning on: she says we should separate *remedial* residue (compensation, reparations, forward-looking repair) from *attributive* residue (guilt, apology, the sense that you must answer for what you did), and her point is that remedial residue is perfectly comfortable for you — you can pay for a broken window you were justified in breaking — but attributive residue is not, because apologizing and feeling guilt essentially represent you as having done something wrong, and if the representation is fitting then you did something wrong, in which case there were two things you ought to have done and you violated one of them, and we have a dilemma back under a politer name.
