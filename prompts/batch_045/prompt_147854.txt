Hi — I need a decision tree for my department's reading group, and honestly it's mostly because I got into a stupidly long argument with a friend last night.

Context: she's a systems neuroscientist. I said something like "I raised my hand because I wanted another coffee," and she said no — all the causal work was already done by the neural events, and by the time you add the wanting you've added a second cause of something that already had one, so either the wanting just is the neural event or it does nothing. I kept pushing back with "but if you'd swapped my neurons for completely different hardware that realized the same decision, I'd still have reached for the cup," and she said that only shows the neural description was at the wrong grain, not that the mental is an extra cause. We went in circles for about two hours and neither of us could say where exactly we disagreed, which is embarassing for both of us.

So: I want a one-page decision tree / flow chart that takes someone from "my wanting coffee caused my hand to go up" down to a named position, where every branch node is a question you can answer without already knowing which position it leads to, and every question is a concrete case or claim rather than "do you think the mind is physical?"

What I want specifically:
