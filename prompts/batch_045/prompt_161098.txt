Doing a PhD in philosophy of physics, and theres a distinction I keep needing that I dont have a word for. Does the following have a name?

Take conservation of energy. As far as I can tell nobody ever uses it to work out what a system does next — you use the dynamical law for that, and then use conservation to check the answer, or to rule out candidate models before you even start. It constrains the space of allowed histories rather than pushing any particular one along. Same flavour: Pauli exclusion, the uncertainty principle. They say what can't happen, not what does.

Two things make me think it's a real category and not just a mood. First, these are the durable ones: the dynamics got replaced wholesale (mechanics → QM → QFT) while energy and momentum conservation sailed through, quantity redefined, constraint intact — even though the standard symmetry derivation puts them downstream of the dynamics. Downstream, yet more robust. (A friend in cosmology says GR doesn't even have global energy conservation, which seems to fit: the symmetry goes, the constraint goes with it.) Second, sometimes they're all we have: in parts of particle physics you know the constraints but can't solve the dynamics, so the constraints do real predictive work by elimination.

I care because of the Humeanism vs anti-Humeanism debate about laws. On a best-system picture these look like theorems rather than axioms — so maybe they aren't laws at all? And what would "governing" amount to for a law that never pushes anything along? It's not the derived-vs-fundamental distinction (derived claims aren't usually more robust than what they're derived from), it's not the grue/accidental-generalisation thing, and it's not quite the structural realist point about mathematical structure surviving theory change — or is it?

Dont just hand me a label. I want the nearest established concepts, how what I described differs from each, and where the edges are: is the second law of thermodynamics in this box or not? If nothing fits, coin something and define it with borderline cases.
