The angle I actually care about isn't "how do we build a safe AI." It's that Ive started reading the whole setup as a picture of what a *good deliberation* is even trying to be: the thing you fall short of when you answer in thirty seconds something that deserved a week, or when a committee talks for three hours and ends up dumber than any of its members. But I cant tell whether that's a fair reading or just my own hobby-horse, and I don't want to smuggle it in.

So please don't explain HCH to me — plan how I'd get the room to it.

- What has to be on the table before the recursion itself makes sense, and why that order. I assume something about why a hard question can be broken into easier ones, and something about why that isn't plain old divide-and-conquer, but I dont know which depends on which.
