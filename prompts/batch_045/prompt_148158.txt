I want to know which readings of the slogan would count this little program as 'thinking by analogy', which would say it's merely catching surface similarities and missing the essence, and which would say it's not even doing analogy at all. Tell me which of these (or other readings you think are better) are the real candidates, which are strawmen, which are plausible, and which the meetup guy probably meant when he said LLM remixing is cognition.
