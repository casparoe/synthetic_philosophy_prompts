Posting my Zeno position on my blog next week and I'd like someone to poke at it first, because I think one of these is doing work the others don't allow and I can't see which one it is.

Roughly where I've landed:

1. The dichotomy and the Achilles are basically settled: infinitely many positive quantities can have a finite sum, so there's nothing problematic about getting through infinitely many sub-distances in a finite time. When people say the calculus dissolved these, they're right.

2. Except it's always felt like a bit of a dodge to me. Telling me the series converges shows me the description is consistent; it doesn't tell me how the runner actually gets through all those points. I've never felt like I was ever given that part.

3. Thomson's lamp isn't a possible setup, and not merely in the physically-can't-build-it sense either: there's no last switch, so there's no final state for the lamp to be in, and a story with no determinate final state isn't describing anything at all.

4. The arrow is the one that's actually deep, and the at-at theory answers it: being in motion is just being here then there, and there's no further fact of "moving" that's had at an instant.

5. I've also got this residual infinite-set-atheism thing rattling around from years of reading this corner of the internet: I doubt there are actual infinities in physical reality, I suspect the continuum is a useful idealization, and it wouldn't shock me if spacetime turns out to be discrete down at the Planck scale.

6. But I also don't think making things discrete would rescue Zeno. Even with only finitely many steps you still haven't said how anything moves, so the puzzle was never really about infinity.

Go through these and tell me which pairs are genuinely in tension, how badly, and what the cheapest fix would be for each — ranked by severity rather than all flagged as equally bad — and then tell me which single revision cleans up the most. Also say explicitly if any of the apparent conflicts are actually fine as stated, because I'd rather not revise something consistent just to look like I put in work. I don't have a favourite here and I'll drop whichever one is really the problem; I just genuinely can't tell which that is, and the 1/3/5 cluster is where my suspicious are but that's basically a guess.
