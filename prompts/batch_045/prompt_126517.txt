I'm writing a paper for my school's politics-and-law seminar on whether a group can bind its future self, and I need a fictional constitution that quietly dismantles one of its own protections without anyone ever voting to dismantle it. Please construct the case.

Two tests must disagree. The stepwise test: a change is valid iff it complies with the rules in force immediately before it takes effect, assessed one change at a time, with no further question about the sequence or about where it ends up. The end-state test: a run of changes counts as legitimate amendment of the same system only if the rulebook that results is one the original rulebook permitted, with requirements and powers assessed over the whole path. I want the stepwise test to certify every step and the end-state test to say the body finished somewhere its original rules forbade.

Constraints, because the easy versions of this cheat:

- One paragraph, a real high-school body: a student council constitution, a debate society charter, an honor code.
- Every step must be properly passed under the rules then in force, and must be something a sensible body could pass for a reason unrelated to escaping the protection: tidying, fixing a drafting error, consolidating overlapping provisions, modernising an outdated procedure.
- No single step may be the culprit: each step, had it been the only change ever made, would be unobjectionable. The violation has to be emergent, a matter of how the steps compose.
- No vagueness, no interpretive disputes, no wise adjudicator, no appeal to framers' intent. The rules should be crisp enough that the two tests, rather than a judgment call, settle the verdicts. The end state must defeat something the original rulebook genuinely protected, not merely something it forgot to mention, yet no step may break an explicit prohibition.
- If a mechanism is needed, invent one, such as a power to merge rules or a delayed commencement clause, but it should be the sort of thing a real constitution might contain.

Then give me the verdict of each test at each step, name the feature doing the work, say what happens to the case if that feature is removed, and give the strongest reply available to a defender of the stepwise test.
