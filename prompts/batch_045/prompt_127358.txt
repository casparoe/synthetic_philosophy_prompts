I'm taking a graduate seminar on nationalism and cosmopolitanism and I want to rehearse defending compatriot partiality against an opponent who actually presses back. Please play a committed cosmopolitan: you grant that associative duties are real — promissory duties, debts to family and colleagues, and the fact that impartial concern need not be impersonally administered — and you grant that states are often the most efficient instruments for discharging duties we owe everyone. What you deny is that shared nationality is itself a source of basic moral claims: the duties we happen to owe compatriots are, for you, either local routes to impartial duties or ordinary associative duties that track borders only contingently. Concede details freely, never the thesis, and don't step out of character to ask me for clarifications; answer in a single paragraph.

Argue inside the following invented case rather than against it. Under the Cassini Accords, a group of neighboring states pools military command, the tax base for social insurance, and transport and research budgeting into one federal treasury and one federal parliament, while criminal law, policing, schools, and the administration of elections remain wholly national. Membership is still acquired at birth; emigration within the federation is legally easy and materially cheap, and almost nobody takes it.

My opening: the pooling pries apart the two relations your side usually runs together, so you now have to pick one. If the morally loaded relation is subjection to coercion — the relation you invoke when you say an order owes justification to everyone it can push around — then the order that can arrest, fine, and imprison me is national, and my reasons for caring specially about my compatriots look exactly like the reasons you say you respect. If instead the loaded relation is participation in a single scheme of production and redistribution, then the relevant unit here is the federation, and you have conceded that what makes a group morally salient is institutional structure rather than the arbitrariness of birth — which is my point, since the nation still carries the legal order. And the exit clause cuts against you as well: if the cheapness of leaving shows that I have no complaint about being coerced, it also shows that my staying is something I do rather than something that merely happened to me.
