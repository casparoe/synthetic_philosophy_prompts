my roommate is building these weird little blobs in the garage and we've been arguing for like two weeks about what's actually making them move, and I can't tell anymore if he's onto something or just being stubborn in a sophisticated way.

the setup: there's this gel he calls careenium (he says the name is from some old book he read in college, and he calls the little etched beads he drops into it simmballs, which I'm pretty sure is just him showing off). The beads are tiny metal spheres with patterns etched on them, and the gel slowly reshapes itself depending on which beads are in it. The rule for how it reshapes is written in terms of what the beads mean: he keeps a codebook on his laptop where pattern #7 means door, #3 means warm, #11 means damaged, and so on. There's also one bead whose pattern is a little etched picture of the whole blob, which he says means the creature itself. When that one goes in, everything changes — the blob starts keeping itself intact, maneuvering so the picture-bead stays near the top, pulling the damaged-beads toward its middle, that kind of thing. Without it, it just flops around slowly.

My claim: come on, the gel is a dumb material. It reacts to the shape and charge of whatever bead is next to it. The meanings live in a spreadsheet. Delete the spreadsheet and the gel does exactly the same stuff. So the meanings aren't pushing anything around, the patterns are.

His claim: swap two rows of the spreadsheet — make #7 mean warm and #3 mean door — and the thing quits working entirely. Its behavior tracks what the beads are about, not what they look like. So the symbols really are shoving the material around.

Then he did something that honestly muddied it for me. He printed a bead for the north door and put it in a tank that has no north door in it. The blob still went and pressed itself against the wall where the north door would have been. I said: see? there's no door there, so nothing is about anything, it's just the pattern. He said that's exactly the point — the symbol moved the gel even though the thing it's about isn't there.

So who's right and why? Is the picture-bead doing something different in kind from the others, or am I just being sentimental about the one with the drawing of the blob on it? And this is the part I actually care about: if I'm right that the meanings do no work, what do I do with the fact that he can't build or repair these things without the codebook, and neither can I? He says that's like saying your DNA does no work because it's just chemistry. I don't have an answer to that one.
