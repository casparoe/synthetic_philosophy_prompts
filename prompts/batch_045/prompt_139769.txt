I'm a retired physical chemist who only wandered into philosophy a couple of years ago, and I need help figuring out why a fight in my reading group won't die. It's five of us — mostly retired lab types plus one working condensed matter guy — and we've spent the better part of a year on Maxwell's demon: Szilard's one-molecule engine, the erasure story everyone tells now (the demon fills up its memory, resetting costs roughly kT ln 2 per bit, second law survives), and the camp that says that whole story is circular, because you only get the erasure bound out of statistical mechanics by smuggling the second law in somewhere upstream, so the claim that information is physical is really just entropy-talk with better PR. A year in, nobody has moved an inch and I want to know what kind of stalemate this actually is.

My own hunch, offered so you can knock it down: it's a bookkeeping-versus-mechanism fight, with one side doing accounting over state spaces and the other wanting an actual dynamical story, and the two answering different questions under one headline. But it might instead be a genuine hidden empirical question about whether the cost shows up in the memory or in the measurement, or it might be bedrock disagreement about what an explanation in physics is even supposed to look like.

Two gadgets we invented that I think are useful stress tests. First, the trash-tape demon: instead of erasing, the demon seals its full memory in a box, dumps the box, and pulls a fresh blank tape out of a drawer. If the cost is real it still has to land somewhere — so where, and does locating it in the making of blank tape amount to anything more than relabelling? Second, the sloppy demon: it writes down only a coarse, noisy record, one bit that's 90% correlated with which half of the box the molecule is in. Now the information in the record and the thermodynamic entropy come apart — and whatever the two sides are really committed to should give different answers about that case.

Please don't give me a tour of the literature or point me at papers; I have no library access and no patience for it. I want a diagnosis of the disagreement, my two demons used as actual tests rather than decorations, and a straight answer about what would count as progress — a cleaner derivation, a experiment, a conceptual distinction, or nothing at all. Give it to me as exactly five numbered points, a sentence or two each, no intro and no summing up at the end.
