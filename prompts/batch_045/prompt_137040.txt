Bit of an odd one, but I've been going round in circles about this and I want someone to tell me whether my views on it fit together at all. I'm Swiss, did recruit school and a few refresher courses, voted against scrapping conscription in that referendum (it lost heavily anyway), and here's roughly where I've landed:

(1) Peacetime conscription is forced labour. Taking a year of a young persons life and jailing them if they refuse is coercion, and "but the state needs it" has been the excuse for every other forced labour scheme in history.

(2) Armed neutrality is legitimately worth keeping, and not only as free-riding off everyone else: it seems genuinely good that there's a bit of Europe nobody can just march through, and that it's defended by ordinary people rather than by professionals who picked soldiering as a career.

(3) I don't trust all-volunteer armies politically. I half-buy something I once read called the barracks-as-ballot argument: a conscript army makes every war personal for a big slice of the voters, and that brakes adventures abroad. Sweden scrapped conscription and then quietly brought it back a few years later, which I read as the volunteer model not actually delivering.

(4) I'm fine with the state making me do other unpaid things — jury duty, being called out to sandbag a river, the old village fire brigade. So I'm not a taxation-is-theft person.

(5) I think it should be opened to women as well, mostly because men-only ends up being a statement about men; and I think the civilian service alternative is the right answer for anyone who won't carry a gun, kept a bit longer than the army stint so it isn't just a loophole.

Now the arguing: my flatmate says (1) and (4) can't both be true. My dad says (3) is an argument for a stricter parliament, not for conscription. My girlfriend says (5) is the weird one — if the objection in (1) is really coercion, making the alternative longer is just more coercion, and if the real objection is being made to kill, then (1) isn't my actual objection at all. I can't tell which of them is right and none of them will tell me which belief to drop. Can you rank the real tensions by how bad they are, say which ones are only apparent, and tell me which single commitment I'd have to give up and what that would cost me?
