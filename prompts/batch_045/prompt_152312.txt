I'm giving the last, deliberately unserious session of a semester-long graduate reading group on Hofstadter and Sander's Surfaces and Essences: Analogy as the Fuel and Fire of Thinking (2013), and I want to close with a joke that demonstrates the book's thesis rather than gesturing at it. The reading group ended up endorsing three commitments that the joke should presuppose, not argue for: that categorizing and analogizing are the same mental act, so that calling something a member of a kind already is a small analogy; that a concept is not a definition but a slowly accreted bundle of nested analogies, which is why the essence we feel we perceive in a thing is really the after-the-fact obviousness of a reminding; and that the mappings that carry any weight transport relational structure rather than surface features. Assume my audience knows all of this and is a little tired of it.

So: not a joke about analogy, not a joke about an AI system failing at analogy, not a pun whose mechanism is phonetic, and not a generic nerd joke with the word analogy swapped in. I want a joke whose laugh is produced by the listener completing a mapping. The setup should be flat, mundane, and literal, with nothing in it to get, and the punchline should force a re-categorization of something the setup already contained. Pick exactly one of the following as the mechanism and tell me which you used: (a) a concept quietly stretched to cover a case that satisfies none of the features one would have listed for it in advance; (b) two live mappings of the same material, where the surface-respecting one fails and the relational one lands; (c) an apparent blunder that is diagnostic of the right mapping, so that the speaker's mistake is the evidence of understanding; (d) the false sense of obviousness that arrives after a mapping is made, where the joke should make the listener feel they had it all along.

One further constraint, which I have been calling the double-mapping test: after the laugh, a second, shallower mapping should be available for the same material, and it should be the one a literate listener reaches for first; the joke should then be built so that this alternative is not merely different but demonstrably worse once noticed, because it leaves something in the setup unaccounted for or depends on features the setup never had. If you cannot make both mappings genuinely live, say so and explain the obstruction rather than faking it. Please deliver the joke in a form I can say out loud in under a minute, followed by five or six lines of notes: which mechanism is doing the work, the exact point at which the mapping is completed, what the discarded mapping is and why it is worse, and, honestly, whether the joke would land at all on the half of the room that has not read the book — I would rather have one good joke with candid notes than three weak ones.
