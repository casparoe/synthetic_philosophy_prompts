I'm a mathematician by training who reads philosophy of mathematics on the side, and there is a pattern in that literature I keep noticing without having a word for it.

The platonist is asked how anyone could have knowledge of objects that are mind-independent and causally inert. What comes back is never a change in what she says about mathematical practice; it is a clause bolted onto the position — a special faculty, or a non-causal relation, or a story about how our mathematical beliefs were selected for reliability. The nominalist is asked how a fiction can be genuinely indispensable to empirical science, and he too adds a clause: the fictional discourse is conservative, hence useful without being true; asked why conservativeness itself needs no explanation, he adds another. In both cases the clause answers exactly one question, exists only because that question was asked, changes nothing about which mathematical claims are accepted or how they are defended, and has no failure conditions — no finding could show it false. The base view and the clauses strike me as different kinds of thing, with only the second kind keeping the dispute going indefinitely.

I don't think this is an ad hoc hypothesis in the usual sense, since nothing has gone wrong with a prediction that needs saving, and it isn't straightforwardly a Lakatosian degenerating research programme either: no anomalies are being accommodated, because there is no data in the neighbourhood, and the additions answer objections rather than observations. It isn't quietism, since neither party thinks the ontological question is empty.

Is there an established notion for an add-on of this kind — one whose whole function is to dispose of an objection and whose failure conditions are empty — and for the package you get when you take a view together with its clauses? If not, what would you call it, and what is the exact mark separating such a clause from one with genuine content? The line has to fall somewhere, because not every defensive addition is idle: a programme that tries to rewrite empirical science so that it never quantifies over mathematical entities, while preserving everything statable in purely nominalistic terms, could fail, and people really disagree about whether it does. I'm after more than a label, though: what the phenomenon is, what it is not, and where its edges lie.
