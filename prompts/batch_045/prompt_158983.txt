the problem isn't that a dishonest debater can slip in one step that's wrong. it's that they can make every step look fine. ten steps that are each 95% likely to be right only get you to about 60%, and the honest debater can't fix that by adding more steps, because the whole reason the judge needed the debate is that they can't follow long chains. so "break everything into small checkable pieces" doesn't rescue you — decomposition is what multiplies the error. and the honest debater cant just point at the bad move, because there isn't one: the argument fails as a whole while every piece of it is individually defensible. why is that a detail and not a fatal problem for debate?
