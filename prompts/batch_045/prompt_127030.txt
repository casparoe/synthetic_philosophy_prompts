My team builds and retrains the risk-stratification models that a large psychiatric intake service uses to route patients, so I live in the vocabulary of supervised learning under deployment: labels as targets, ground truth and annotation protocols, training-versus-serving distributions, concept drift and label shift, performative feedback from deploy-and-retrain cycles, calibration, inter-annotator agreement, holdout evaluation.

Alongside each model card we have started keeping what I privately call a kind card for every output label: a record of how the population carrying the label, the behaviors clinicians code under it, and the way patients describe themselves once they have been assigned it have each moved since the label first shipped. A philosopher friend says this is an instance of Ian Hacking's looping effects. I have read only a short summary of the idea: classifications of people feed back onto the people classified, the kind is a moving target, some kinds are interactive rather than indifferent, and whole diagnostic categories can appear and then disappear. I want more than an analogy.

Please restate Hacking's looping-effects argument as faithfully as you can in the vocabulary above — targets, ground truth, drift, performativity, calibration — and then give me the residue report: what translated cleanly, what had to be distorted to fit, and what has no expression in my vocabulary at all. Three items I want adjudicated specifically. (1) Is looping just performative prediction plus concept drift, or does it matter to the argument that patients' own self-ascriptions and self-narratives are part of the causal path? (2) Can my vocabulary say that the target is not merely drifting but partly constituted by the classificatory practice, so that even in the limit there is no fixed truth of the matter to be well or badly calibrated against? (3) Hacking treats looping as something that can go better or worse for the people classified, not merely as a threat to accuracy; can my vocabulary express a loop going well, or is that an extra-technical intrusion? Do not smuggle my framework's assumptions in and call them translation, and for each piece of residue tell me whether it is a defect of my vocabulary or a sign that Hacking's claim is less distinctive than it sounds.
