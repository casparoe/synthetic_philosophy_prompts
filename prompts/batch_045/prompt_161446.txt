Can you write me a short story that chews on whether his trade is really as clean as it looks? Exactly five scenes, each with a one-line header, each a paragraph or two, no introduction and no wrap-up, and don't let it land on a tidy verdict. The ground I want hit:

4. a donor circle where your say over the whole pot scales with how much you put in, so his salary is buying agenda-setting and not just pills.

Keep the traders boring and decent, and make his job abstract and victimless-looking.
