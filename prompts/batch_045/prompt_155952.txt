From what I can gather, the positions include at least these: that attributing beliefs and desires to the colony is legitimate because such attributions predict and explain its behaviour; that such attributions are merely useful shorthand and there is no fact of the matter about whether the colony really believes anything; that the real sticking point is not belief but conscious experience, whether there is something it is like to be the colony; that much of what looks like the colony's memory actually lives outside any ant, in pheromone trails and nest structure, so the colony is partly made of its environment; and that the colony is built out of parts that are themselves goal-directed agents with their own ends, which may not line up with the colony's.

The deliverable I want is a translation manual. Second, tracing.
