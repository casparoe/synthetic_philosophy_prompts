Complications, because of course there are:

Our ICU team has the infusion scheduled and the family was told yesterday that the drug was coming. No vial has been drawn yet. Our charter has two clauses that don't obviously fit together: one says the cache is to be allocated "so as to preserve the greatest number of lives," and another says no member hospital may be required to withhold or withdraw a treatment its own clinical team has determined and scheduled for a patient under its care.
