The argument I keep seeing for distinguishing this from equality is the small-improvement one: you can make one option a bit better and it still doesn't beat the other, which shouldn't happen if they were tied. And then there's the further, stranger claim: that in such cases the missing reason has to come from the agent — she makes a choice not dictated by the values on the table, and that act is supposed to create or confer the reason she lacked rather than discover one that was already there.
