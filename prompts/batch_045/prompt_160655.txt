She thinks Mary comes to know a new fact when she first sees red, and that physicalism is therefore in trouble. I think Mary gains abilities and a new way of conceiving a fact she already had, and I want to practice saying that against someone who actually pushes back instead of nodding.

So play her. Be a sharp, well-read defender of the knowledge argument — someone who can run the thing properly, who has heard all the physicalist replies before, and who is not going to fold because I said the words "ability" or "same fact, new mode of presentation."

Rules:

- You hold that Mary learns something she did not know, and that the complete physical story does not entail it. That's the claim you never give up. Stay in first person until I type STOP.
- You're not a cartoon substance dualist. You think Jackson's own epiphenomenalist conclusion is too strong and you'll say so cheerfully. - Conversational, a paragraph or two per reply, no headings or bullet points, ask me questions back when that's the best move. Concede small things — that "knowledge" is doing some work in the setup, that a bare intuition isn't an argument — but never the main claim.

Here's my opening, the bit that got her flustered:

Suppose that before Mary leaves the room, the scientists hand her a switch. Flipping it puts her visual system into exactly the neural state a ripe tomato would put it in — eyes shut, lights off, no red object anywhere in the room. She flips it and says 'oh, that's what red is like,' and afterwards she can imagine red, remember it, and pick red things out of a lineup. Now: nothing entered that room except a causal nudge. No new information about the world outside arrived. So if she learned a fact, it's a fact she could already state — which means the knowledge argument was never about Mary's ignorance of facts. It's about what happens when you instantiate a state. And instantiating a state is precisely the sort of thing a physicalist can cheerfully accept. Notice too that on your own argument the release from the room now does no work at all; I think that shows the release was never carrying the intuition, the having of the experience was. Go.
