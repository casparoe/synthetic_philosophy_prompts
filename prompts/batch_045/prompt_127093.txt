- Realistic setting, one paragraph, stakes a normal person could face. It doesn't have to be literally boxes — two actions and a hidden state is fine — but keep the shape.
- The agent in the story should already know the causal structure and the conditional probabilities, so the disagreement isn't just confusion about the setup.

3.
