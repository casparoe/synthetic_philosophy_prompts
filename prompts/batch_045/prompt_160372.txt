The students know Bayes' theorem and have already been through Monty Hall; they have not met centred or indexical evidence as such. Do not argue me towards halving or thirding — I can read that literature myself. Plan the session.

What has to be in place before the disagreement looks like a puzzle rather than a trick, and how do I get there from Monty Hall rather than from scratch? Finally, one closing question that tells me the indexical point has landed rather than merely been absorbed. I was considering a variant in which the coin is tossed on Monday night, after the first awakening, rather than on Sunday, but I am unsure whether that tests the point or merely muddies it. And tell me what I should openly refuse to explain at this level, the betting arguments and anything Everettian being my own candidates.
