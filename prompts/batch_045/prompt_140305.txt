I am finishing a novel whose narrator, two thirds of the way in, finds on her desk a manuscript in her own handwriting, dated after her death, that narrates the scene she is presently living — including her finding the manuscript. A reader whose judgment I trust says the architecture of the book "is just Escher's Drawing Hands: each level authors the other, so there is no author outside the fiction, no privileged level of reality, no ghost." I want to know whether the lithograph can bear the weight he is putting on it.

Give me the correspondence first, as a table: in my novel, what plays the role of the sheet of paper, of the two hands, of the cuffs rising out of it, of the pencil, of the act of drawing, of the frame, and of Escher's own hand outside the picture — and mark wherever the mapping is forced or a term on one side has no partner on the other. Then the audit proper: which features of the picture my book simply does not have (the two hands existing at once, symmetry of authority between them, a single shared medium, a loop already closed and takeable in at a glance), which features of my book the picture cannot represent (a reader who traverses the levels in an order, levels that make different claims about what is really the case, the cost to a character of discovering she is written), and — the part I care about — which of those disanalogies genuinely matter for the conclusion he is drawing and which are decoration.

Two things I would like settled. First, whether the lithograph is even on his side: it seems to me that the mutual drawing inside the picture is possible only because there is a flat surface the hands cannot draw and a drawer the loop cannot reach, so that the picture is evidence for an inviolate level rather than against one — though I may be reading my own hopes into it. Second, if two hands are the wrong image for a loop that is crossed in time rather than seen whole, what is the better one: two mirrors facing each other, the Bach canon that climbs by whole tones and arrives back at its starting pitch, a camera trained on its own monitor, something else — and would the replacement still license "no privileged level," or does it quietly restore one?

Assume I know the standard vocabulary of strange loops and tangled hierarchies; there is no need to define it. And please do not settle any of this by telling me what Escher said in his letters about what he was up to.
