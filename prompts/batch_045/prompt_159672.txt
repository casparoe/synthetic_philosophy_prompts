- Coin flip, heads gets me 3x my stake back (counting the stake), tails I lose it, and I get to run it a hundred times in a row. I put up about a quarter of my net worth each time, which I'm told is exactly what the growth-rate rule says.
- The identical bet, offered exactly once. I'd put up maybe 10%, definately not a quarter.
- A 1% shot at 1000x my stake, 99% chance I lose what I put in. Rule says about 1% of net worth. I'd throw in 3% and I feel fine about it.
- Home insurance at a premium maybe 60% above the expected loss. I buy it without thinking, even though it's negative expected value and it drags my growth rate down.
