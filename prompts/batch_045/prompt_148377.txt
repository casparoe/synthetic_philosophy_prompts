The palliative attending who runs our pain module has asked me to give a 35-minute session to first-years (whiteboard, one handout, no philosophy background, they've had exactly one lecture on nociception) on what the badness of pain consists in, and specifically on what the pain asymbolia cases do and don't show: whether they support the idea that what makes pain bad is its unpleasantness rather than its intensity or its sensory character.
