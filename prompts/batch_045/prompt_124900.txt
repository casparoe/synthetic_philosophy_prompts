We always end up going in circles because everyone uses the same words to mean different things, so this time I want to sort people *before* we start arguing.

Cases I definitely want in there (add your own, or swap if you've got a better one):

The cube/sphere case, but split into two separate questions: (a) can he tell them apart, i.e. reach for the one he was just handed or pick it out from the other one, and (b) can he name it or say "that's the cube" without touching. Something non-human or artificial, because I've got an ML person coming: say a model (or a robot) trained separately on camera images and on a completely different sensor — depth/lidar, or a tactile glove — with no pairing between the two streams, and then given both at once. Leaves I want the thing to be able to land on: the plain "no"; the plain "yes"; the "yes to matching, no to naming" middle; the "this is a purely empirical question and the real evidence is mixed" position; the "the question is confused, there's no single thing being asked" position; and the "transfer happens but only because the subject quickly learns new sensorimotor contingencies" position.
