I'm in a seminar on collective action and environmental ethics, and we've just worked through Hardin's 1968 essay on the commons. My professor extended the pasture example to AI training data: the stock of human-written public text is the pasture, each lab's scrape plus the synthetic text it then releases back onto the web is another animal, and the degradation is the one reported in recent work on model collapse, where training on model-generated text degrades the models trained on it afterwards. The conclusion drawn was the Hardin-style one, that unilateral restraint is pointless and that only mutual coercion, mutually agreed upon — here a licensing or levy regime over scraped text — can save the corpus.

The mapping sounded clean in the lecture, but I think there are three structural differences. First, the herders are also the ones seeding the pasture: public text grows with use, and the largest herders are also the source of most of the growth, so the resource is produced rather than merely consumed. Second, the damage is contamination rather than depletion, and it may be threshold-like rather than proportional to the number of animals. Third, the herds are wildly unequal — a handful of labs add almost all of the animals — so the payoff symmetry in which each herder takes the full gain and shares the loss does not obviously hold. There is also a prior worry, that text is non-rival, which makes this look more like a public good than a commons proper.

Could you audit the analogy for me: give me the correspondence table (pasture, animal, herder, carrying capacity, the payoff structure), then say which of my three differences actually matter for the coercion conclusion and which are decorative, what the pasture picture imports that the corpus lacks — a naturally fixed limit, visible and legible degradation, a small closed set of herders who can monitor one another — and whether the remedy survives once those imports are removed. If the pasture is the wrong source domain, what would you swap in: an absorptive sink for pollution, a seed bank, an archive being padded with forgeries? And does the non-rivalry worry mean the tragedy framing has to be abandoned, or only re-described?
