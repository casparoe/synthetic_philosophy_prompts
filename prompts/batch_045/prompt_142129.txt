Definitions, so we're working from the same ones. A commitment race: two agents are in a conflict where each one's best outcome requires the other to back down; binding yourself changes the other side's best response; so both sides have an incentive to bind first and hardest, and the interaction tends toward outcomes both would have rejected ex ante. Call a commitment *deep* to the extent that reversing it is costly for the committer (money already spent, legal exposure, reputation on the line, third-party-enforced penalties) and *shallow* to the extent it is not.
