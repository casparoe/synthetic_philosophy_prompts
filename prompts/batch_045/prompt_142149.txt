Here's the part I actually care about, and it's my own dumb coinage rather than anything in the literature as far as I know. I call it the reverse-Groucho trade. Groucho's old line is that he wouldn't want to belong to any club that would have him as a member; the standard trader's version is that you should be suspicious of any counterparty who's eager to trade with you. A reverse-Groucho trader inverts it: she'll only take the other side from someone who refuses to trade with her, since a refusal is the only signal she trusts that the other side isn't dumping adverse selection on her. So she posts quotes forever and never gets filled; worse, if anyone does refuse her, that raises her estimate of how informed he is, which makes her want to trade with him more, which makes him refuse again — an order book full of quotes, zero volume.
