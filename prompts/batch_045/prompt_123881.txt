My girlfriend and I keep having the same argument and I want a word for one specific bit of it, because I think if I had the word the argument would basically end.

Here's the shape of it. On Sunday I decide, quite firmly, that I'm going to start running on Monday morning. Monday comes and I don't run — and I decide I'm going to start on Tuesday. Tuesday, Wednesday. The thing I keep noticing is that at no point does it feel like I've changed my mind. Sunday me and Monday me both say, sincerely, that they're starting tomorrow, and both would insist they still want the same thing and still think it's worth doing. I'm not choosing the sofa over the run, I'm not revising my judgement that running is good, and there isn't even some other thing I'm doing instead — I just keep re-dating the same plan, and the reason I give each time is the same vague one (once I've got a proper block of time, once this week is out of the way), a reason I would totally have accepted on the Sunday as well.

It happens at work too, which is what makes me think it's not about laziness or about my body: our team keeps moving the same decision to next sprint, everyone is still in favour of making the decision, nobody thinks the decision has become less urgent, and yet it slides, and each slide comes with its own reasonable-sounding excuse that would have sounded just as reasonable two sprints ago.

A friend who did a philosophy minor muttered something about akrasia and about hyperbolic discounting, but neither seems to fit what I'm after. Akrasia sounds like acting against your own better judgement, which isn't quite it — I'm not doing the opposite of what I judge best, I'm just not doing it yet. And hyperbolic discounting sounds like my preferences change as the thing gets closer, whereas the weird thing here is that my preference for running over not running feels completely stable; the plan keeps its shape while its start date keeps receding. Also, I know this is going to happen — I've watched me do it dozens of times — and I've actually paid for a year of classes up front to stop myself, which does work. So part of me thinks whatever this is must come apart into two things: the sliding, and the fact that I can predict the sliding perfectly and it doesn't stop the sliding.

So: does this pattern have a name of its own, or is it just ordinary procrastination with a story attached? If there are established concepts nearby, I want to know which one fits best and exactly how my case differs from each of the others — especially from the ones my friend named, and from the ordinary-language one — and whether the predicting-and-precommitting part is a separate phenomenon or the same one. And if there genuinely isn't a term, coin one: give me a definition sharp enough that I could tell, for some borderline case, whether it applied or not. A bare label won't settle the argument, so I need the carving, not the name.
