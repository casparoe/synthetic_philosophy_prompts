At my EA-adjacent reading group someone broke a stalemate about whether to trust our current moral views with "the arc of the moral universe is long but it bends toward justice," and the way they used it was: moral concern has steadily widened, from the tribe to the nation to all humans and now to animals, so in a hundred years people will probably look back at us the way we look at slaveholders, and therefore we shouldn't put much weight on our own current intuitions about how much things like factory farming matter.

I feel like that sentence is doing several different jobs at once and I can't tell which ones. Also: does any reading commit you to saying the trend continues into territory people currently find weird, like digital minds, or does the slogan quietly let you pick the endpoint you already liked?
