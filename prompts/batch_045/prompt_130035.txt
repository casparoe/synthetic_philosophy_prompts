Camp A: the state should fund long, well-paid, job-protected parental leave with a non-transferable use-it-or-lose-it chunk for each parent; roughly the first year of a kid's life isn't interchangeable with non-parental care, and pushing infants into group care mostly shifts care work onto badly paid, mostly female childcare workers rather than sharing it.
