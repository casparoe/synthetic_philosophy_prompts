Write an essay on the paradox of tragedy: why do we enjoy sad art? The usual fight is between people who say the sadness gets outweighed or transformed by the beauty of the work, and people who say the painful feeling itself is part of what we want, maybe because it is safe or because it connects us to others. Now throw in a new case: a immersive simulator that produces the exact felt experience of devastating grief over a loss that never happened, while you know the whole time it is just a simulation. Fans say this is the purest tragic art there is. Write an essay that weighs the arguements on both sides about whether this simulator actually solves the paradox or just changes the subject. Does it show that we enjoy sadness itself, that we really only enjoy the framing or the formal pleasure, or something else? Don't sit on the fence: reach a conclusion and justify it.
