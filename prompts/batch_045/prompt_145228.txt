Hey — no academic background here, I fix printers for a living, but I've fallen down a rabbit hole about the colour phi thing on my own. You flash a red dot, then a green dot a bit to the side, and people swear they saw one dot travel across and turn green *partway along*, before the green was ever actually shown. From what I can gather there are two stories you can tell: either the brain waits for both flashes, builds the finished "moving dot that changes colour" version, and that finished version is what you consciously see (I think this is the one called Stalinesque, Dennett's label?), or the experience really happens in real time and then the memory of it gets edited afterwards (Orwellian). Nobody seems to have settled which one it is, which seems mad to me, because I think I've designed a cheap experiment that would.

Rig: an arduino flashes red then green, and also fires a tiny click in the right ear at a delay I can dial anywhere from 0 to 300 ms after the second flash. The subject just says: did the dot change colour before or after the click. My reasoning: if the Stalinesque story is right, consciousness of the whole moving dot only kicks in after the second flash, so the click should get experienced as coming *before* the colour change no matter where I put it — the ordering judgement should be scrambled, showing the experience was assembled late. If the Orwellian story is right, the click was conciousnessed at its actual time, so the ordering judgement should stay accurate. Second version of the same rig: they hit a button the instant they see the colour change, and I compare that to the real flash times to get the length of the delay in milliseconds.

Three things I'd like from you. What result would actually confirm the Stalinesque story, and would my click test really show it? If the delay comes out at, say, 80 ms, does that mean all of my experience is running 80 ms behind the world and I'm permanently experiencing the recent past? And my mate says Orwellian is just Stalinesque with a pointless extra "memory editing" step bolted on, so Occam's razor settles it before I even build the thing — is he right, or should I build it anyway?
