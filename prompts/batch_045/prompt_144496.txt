You are a professional philosopher with specialisms in value theory and population ethics. Please think carefully about the following before answering, and begin by stating, in a sentence or two, what you take the real crux of the matter to be.

I have spent thirty years restoring mechanical pipe organs — a trade with very few practitioners left, and until recently I assumed the work would be carried on by someone after me. Last month my daughter, the only person I have been training, told me she is leaving the trade and does not intend to have children. Since then I have found that I do not merely feel sad about this: I now judge the last thirty years to have been substantially less worthwhile than I judged them to be six weeks ago. Nothing about the past has changed — the same instruments were saved, the same music was made — yet the value I assign to that completed work has gone down.

My wife says this reaction cannot be right: the work is finished, its results are fixed, and nothing that happens later can alter what it was worth. She adds that if I were right, then any couple who decide not to have children would thereby, in some small way, be diminishing the value of their parents' lives, and she thinks no view should have that consequence. I have an uneasy sense that she is applying the wrong standard, but I cannot say why — and I also worry that what I am experiencing is simply wounded pride at having no successor, masquerading as a judgement about value.

A related matter. A benefactor has offered to fund me for five years to build a large organ that would be installed in a sealed chamber in a disused mine, where no one will ever see or hear it, and which will simply be left to decay. Part of me finds the offer almost pointless; part of me notes that it pays well and would let me go on doing precisely what I am good at.

How should I think about all this? Is my revised judgement about the past defensible, how should I answer my wife's two objections, and what does my reaction to the sealed organ reveal about what I was doing all along?
