A bookie can then sell you the bets you'll accept and buy the bets you'll accept, and however the world turns out you hand over more than you get back: a guaranteed loss, a 'Dutch book'. The Dutch book theorem proves the converse as well: anyone whose degrees of belief do satisfy the axioms can never be Dutch booked.
