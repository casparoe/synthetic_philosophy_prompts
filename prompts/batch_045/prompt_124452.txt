She used it to argue that we basically don't need to sweat what happens to AI debate once the models are smarter than the judges: pit two of them against each other, let a weaker judge pick the winner, and the honest side wins, because making up a story is just more work than reporting one.

Make it concrete by testing each reading against some cases I made up (not from anything I've read, so don't go hunting for the source):

- Case D: same trial again, except each debater has to answer ten follow-ups in a row and stay consistent with what they said earlier.
