I'm writing up my position for a metaphysics reading group on de re modality and would like an audit of my commitments before I circulate it. Roughly where I've landed: (1) I accept the necessity of origin — I could not have been this very person had I originated from a different sperm and egg, and I take that to be a metaphysical thesis about me rather than a fact about how I use the word "I". (2) I accept a broadly Lewisian counterpart-theoretic account of de re modality, on the grounds that transworld identity is unintelligible: "I could have been F" holds in virtue of there being a counterpart sufficiently similar to me in the respects that matter who is F. (3) I accept the necessity of identity, and I want to keep the standard route from it to (1), on which individuals with distinct origins are necessarily distinct. (4) Some case verdicts: a child conceived from a different one of my father's sperm on the same occasion would have been a sibling and not me, and I am prepared to accept that; the very sperm and egg a few hours earlier or later, or joined in vitro rather than in utero, would still have produced me; in monozygotic twinning the twins share a sperm and an egg, so a shared origin is not sufficient for identity; in tetragametic chimerism, where one organism descends from two fused zygotes, I want to say that a single person has four gametes in its origin; and had the zygote split, I take there to be a determinate answer to the question of whether I would then have been one of the twins and which one. (5) I am Parfitian about what matters in survival, so none of this should disturb me prudentially. (6) I am an epistemicist about vagueness, and I rely on that to settle how finely gametes are individuated — whether, say, a sperm differing by a single base pair from the one that in fact fertilized the ovum is the same sperm for the purposes of (1).

Which of these genuinely conflict, and which only seem to? Rank the real tensions by severity, give the cheapest repair for each together with what it would cost me, and say which single revision would do the most work. Please answer in continuous prose, without headings, bullet points, bold text or tables.
