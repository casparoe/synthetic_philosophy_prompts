hey — i need a piece of satire for a small newsletter i send to people who work at the edge of philosophy and biology. Format: an internal memo / consulting deliverable, ~600 words, from a firm ("Attribution Partners," or make up a worse name) that a species has hired to finally settle wether selection is acting on genes, on organisms, or on groups. Voice: deadpan management-consultant — deliverables, stakeholders, spans of control, synergies — and it should never break voice to explain the biology. Readable for my non-specialist subscribers, but someone who knows the multi-level selection literature should feel it was written by a person who's read it.

The target is the level-of-selection dispute itself, especially the way everybody agrees on the population-level facts and then fights over the bookkeeping. The jokes have to come out of the actual content:

- the two ways of counting a group's fitness — count the daughter groups a deme throws off vs. count the average fitness of the individuals in it — and the fact that the two can come apart in sign. Please actually construct a case where they disagree, so one ledger shows the deme thriving and the other shows it failing, and let the consultants exploit that.
- the point that the standard decomposition of change into a within-group term and a between-group covariance term is a statistical partition, not by itself a causal verdict, so the firm can bill the same covariance twice: once as kin selection (neighbour-modulated fitness) and once as group selection.
- downward: a rogue genetic element — a segregation distorter or a transposon — filing its own claim for credit, with the germline acting as a compliance firewall.
- upward: species- or clade-level selection claiming trickle-down credit, and the objection that a level only counts if it has heritable variation in the right currency — births and deaths of the units at that level, not just averages over the ones below.

Be fair to both the gene's-eye and the group-selection camps; the joke is that they're the same numbers. Close with a straight-faced recommendation, still in the firm's voice, that quietly dissolves the organism as an accounting unit — the reductio being that on this way of doing things "which level" was never a fact waiting to be discovered.
