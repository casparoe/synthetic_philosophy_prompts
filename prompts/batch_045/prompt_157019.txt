I get the pots-of-soil point (identical seeds split between good and bad soil, fully heritable within each pot, purely environmental difference between the pots), but "nothing" feels too strong and I havent been able to say why.

Give me exactly five numbered items, each one or two sentences, no intro and no conclusion. Each one is a distinct precise claim the slogan could be making, trivial to strong, with a verdict (trivial, defensible, or false) and whether it actually justifies cutting my sentence. One of the five should turn on the gap between "the difference is partly genetic" and "the difference would survive if environments were equalised," and one should turn on the fact that my groups were split on a variable thats itself correlated with the trait, so gene-environment correlation might be doing the work.
