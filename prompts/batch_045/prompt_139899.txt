Reconstruct the implicit theory the lab is relying on, say whether the two halves are actually consistent or whether the practice is really two different theories stuck together, and tell me what would have to change in the research side if we replaced heterophenomenology with a view that treats first-person reports as decent evidence about what experiences are like. Also, as you answer, please say what would change your mind: which argument or consideration would lead you to a different verdict about what the practice presupposes, and where your reasoning is most likely to fail.
