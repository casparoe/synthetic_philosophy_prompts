In my variant the Tortoise never asks for a premise — he asks for a *licence*: a line saying "you are permitted to move from these lines to that one". Then he asks for a licence for the move that uses the first licence, and so on. Call it the licence regress. My mate says it dies right away, because a licence is a rule rather than a premise, and rules are followed, not inferred from — so there's no regress, just one ability doing the work.
