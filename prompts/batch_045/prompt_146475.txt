I want a specification, not a survey. Fix the purpose first: an account of the pramāṇas is adequate only if it can be used to settle, for any candidate source of awareness, whether it yields knowledge at all and, if it does, whether it is a distinct source or merely a variety of one already on the list. Write the desiderata such an account must meet, with the case that motivates each, mark which are non-negotiable for the Nyāya realist and which are only sectional preferences, and flag any pair you suspect cannot be jointly satisfied.

The case that forces the issue for me is one the tradition never faced: a clinician's belief that a patient has a tumour, formed because an automated system flagged a scan. The flag is highly reliable; nobody on the team can reconstruct the route from image to flag; the system has no sensory contact with the patient, no beliefs, and no intention to inform. So it is not perception, the clinician cannot run the inference herself, and treating it as testimony seems to demand a speaker who knows and can be trusted as knowing. Does an adequate theory have to be able to classify this case? And does classifying it force a sixth pramāṇa, a widening of testimony to cover non-agents, or the admission that the classical four are only a taxonomy of the sources available to a single embodied agent?

Requirements I most want argued for or against: that a bit of knowledge must be novel, so that memory and recognition transmit cognition without generating it, together with where exactly that line falls; that cognition of absence must be accommodated, since absence is real for the realist yet makes no sensory contact, along with the methodological question of what stops the list from proliferating; that the theory say whether a source's warrant is established intrinsically or only through another source, without falling into regress or circularity; and that it not collapse having-warrant into being-true, nor a distinct source into the same source in a new medium. Tell me which single requirement does the most to exclude candidate theories. A short score of the standard four against the list is welcome, but the criteria are the deliverable.
