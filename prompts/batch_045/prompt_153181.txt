On held-out data from the same training period, its outputs were calibrated: events to which it assigned about 0.7 occurred roughly 70 percent of the time. The fund sizes its positions by a rule that maximizes the expected logarithm of final wealth, using these outputs as if they were known chances. The fund now faces a new regime: a rapid energy transition, reconfigured trade blocs, and AI-driven productivity changes that are not represented in the training data. This is a structural break.
