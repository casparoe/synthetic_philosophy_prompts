I've got 90 minutes and a whiteboard, and I volunteered to do next week's session on what people online call the Löbian obstacle: the bind a proof-based agent is in when it wants to hand off to a successor system it can't prove is sound, because Löb's theorem — in PA or anything containing it, if it's provable that "if P is provable then P," then P is provable — knocks out the reflection step such an agent would need.
