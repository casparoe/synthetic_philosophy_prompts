I've been working through Dennett's heterophenomenology again and I keep losing my grip on what the method is actually supposed to buy you, so I'd like a careful conceptual map rather than a verdict on whether qualia exist.

Here is the procedure as I understand it. You treat a subject's first-personal utterances strictly as text — as data about what the subject is disposed to say — you withhold judgment on whether those utterances are true, and you assemble them into a heterophenomenological world: a description of the world as the subject represents it. The selling point is that this gives a science of consciousness all the data it could legitimately want, without smuggling in the subject's authority about precisely the thing in dispute.

What I can't get clear on is which of two things the bracketing step is doing.

On a thin reading, suspending judgment is a methodological checkpoint. You suspend now, you cash the reports out later into third-personal facts about the subject's report-producing machinery, and whatever first-personal residue is left over simply isn't part of the theory.

On a thick reading, the bracketing is a genuine ontological move. The subject's claims are about posits internal to their heterophenomenological world, that world is the real object of study, and the question of whether it is really like that for them is not deferred but dissolved.

My sense is that Dennett needs the thick reading for the method to say anything distinctive, but that the thick reading is doing all the controversial work and amounts to the conclusion wearing methodological costume; whereas the thin reading collapses back into something like methodological behaviorism with an extra bookkeeping layer. So: is there a stable middle position, and if so what is it? Is there any way the suspension can be genuinely answerable-to-later rather than a placeholder for never?

Two cases where I think the distinction gets teeth, and which I'd like your map to handle.

First, report instability. First-personal reports are notoriously labile: rephrase the question, change the response scale, cue a different comparison class, or train the subjects — long-term meditators reporting on the fine-grained structure of their own attention, say — and what people say shifts systematically. Under the thin reading this is just more data about report-dispositions and it's fine. Under the thick reading I don't understand what the heterophenomenological world *is* when a single subject's texts contradict each other across elicitation contexts. Is it a fiction with holes in it, and if so can it still underwrite any claim about consciousness?

Second, the boundary of the reporter class. Reports come in degrees and from unlikely places: infants and non-human animals on one side, and on the other, systems optimized to produce fluent first-personal-sounding text, where the optimization pressure is aimed at something else entirely — plausibility to a human audience, for instance. Does heterophenomenology have a principled criterion for which report-generating systems get a heterophenomenological world constructed for them, or is producing verbal reports just a chauvinism that the method inherits from the accident of who can talk? And does the answer differ under the thin and thick readings?

I'd also like you to say what kind of disagreement this is. If thin and thick are methodological stances rather than empirical hypotheses, is there any finding that could discriminate between them, or is the absence of such a finding itself the point — and if it is the point, does that make the method unfalsifiable in a way that should worry someone who wanted a science here?

I don't need a bibliography or a history of the debate, and I've read enough of the usual move in my corner of the internet — the reports are data, the rest is a confusion, next question — to know it doesn't settle this for me. What I want is the argumentative structure: what each reading commits you to, where each one breaks, and which of the standard test cases (Mary, the inverted spectrum, the possibility of a being behaviorally identical to me with no inner life) actually discriminate between them rather than just getting asserted at.
