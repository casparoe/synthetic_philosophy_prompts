My opening. I fine-tuned two variants off the same base model. Across the whole eval suite and to blinded human raters they are behaviorally indistinguishable: prompt either one with a user who seems to assume the deploy is Tuesday and both produce the same correction behavior. In variant A there is a direction in activation space that decodes to something like "the user takes p to be true; p is false", and patching that state into new contexts shifts downstream behavior exactly as that ascription predicts. In variant B there is no such state: ablations and patches find nothing systematic, its behavior rides shallow lexical cues, and it degrades under distribution shift in a way A does not.

Say, if you like, that A believes and B only behaves as if. But then your "belief" is doing no predictive work I don't already get from eval numbers and the design stance — it just redescribes A's internals in folk-psychological vocabulary. Why isn't that a variable name in my debugger?
