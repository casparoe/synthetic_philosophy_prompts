people keep arguing between (a) keep elections but patch them — ranked choice, reserved seats, campaign rules — and (b) drop elections and pick the council by random lottery, like 15 names drawn, one semester each. Ive been reading about sortition stuff online and i honestly think both of those are bad as stated, so I want to pitch something in the middle but I cant tell if it holds together: instead of one standing council, draw a fresh random panel of 12 for each big decision (funding, the dress code thing, spring fest), keep a tiny elected group of 3 whose only power is deciding what goes to a panel and when, draw from the entire grade instead of from volunteers, and exclude from the draw anyone who already holds a leadership role (team captain, club president, yearbook editor, etc.). Plus a challenge rule: 30 signatures forces a re-hearing of any panel decision in front of a *different* randomly drawn group of 8.

can you draft the memo Ill circulate to the committee?
