My opening. Take an RLHF'd assistant deployed with no system prompt at all, just a user message. It still behaves as one thing: helpful, refusing the same requests it refused yesterday, volounteering corrections, and breaking out of whatever role you put it in to add a disclaimer. That looks like a policy in the weights, not a role cued by a prompt — and a simulator has nothing to simulate when the prompt is empty. Also don't give me "it does nothing between turns": a waiter does nothing between tables either.

So: what exactly is being simulated in the empty-prompt case, and why isn't "what it defaults to" just another name for its goal?
