Compose the question so that candidates must derive FDT's prescription in the charity case and say what this reveals about the relevant difference between counterfactual mugging and XOR blackmail, including where causal and evidential approaches would go wrong. Give a model answer of roughly 700 words. Then give a marking scheme built around five must-hit points, with the marks each is worth (total 20) and a one-line note on the mistake that should cost marks for each point. Finally, after the exam materials, tell me how confident you are in your claims and single out the point you are least sure of.
