I'll grant you the easy case. A skinnerian creature can't be conditioned on imperceptible properties — ownership, debt, next tuesday — because nothing in its reinforcement history picks those out, so it can't even be in the business of getting them wrong. But your argument overgeneralises. Take a species i'll call the cairn-builders: no language whatsoever, but a popperian simulation so good that they routinely run off-line trials on absent objects, on the likely actions of specific other individuals, and on arrangements they have never actually encountered — including something functionally very like "this stone is hers, so if i move it she'll move mine." Gregorian equals popperian plus a bigger, shareable symbol set.
