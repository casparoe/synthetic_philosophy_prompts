The errand must have two side effects such that two impact measures cross. Define the measures as: (a) baseline deviation — penalize how far the resulting world is from the world in which the agent does nothing; (b) attainable-utility preservation — penalize reductions in what the affected people can still bring about, and state whose attainable utility you are tracking. The first side effect must score high on (a) and low on (b), and (b) must be the verdict we want. The second must score low on (a) and high on (b), and (a) must be the verdict we want. Say what happens to the scenario if that feature is removed. Finally, give the strongest reply available to a defender of low-impact AI — for instance that the impact penalty was never meant to carry deontic or intrinsic-value content, and that a proxy failing is not the research program failing — and close with one sentence on whether the moral is that impact measures need a value specification, or that they need a prior account of which kinds of change to count.
