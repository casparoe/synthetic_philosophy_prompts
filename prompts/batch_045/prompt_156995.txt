But two properties that are welded together in every creature he ever actually met come apart in the cases I care about: a being can be sentient but not rational (a pig) or rational but not sentient (a sufficiently capable AI system), and Kant only ever had one dial. Taboo "personhood" for a sec and tell me which property is really doing the work.
