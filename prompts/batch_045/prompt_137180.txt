I belong to a small modern Stoicism practice group that meets monthly, and one line does an enormous amount of work in it: *some things are up to us and some are not*, usually in its contemporary form, *you can't control what happens to you, only how you respond*. I want to know what precise claims this could be making, because I think it is being used with three different extensions and I can't tell whether any of them is true.

Three episodes from the last two meetings. First, when I said I was frightened about whether my father's surgery would go well, I was told that this is not up to me, and that caring about it in the way I do is therefore a mistake in judgment rather than a misfortune. Second, the same person argued that my volunteering on a climate-policy campaign is, at the level that matters, no different from worrying about the surgery: the outcome is not up to me either, since my contribution is one of thousands, so the campaign cannot be where my real agency lies. Third, she said of someone who snapped at her brother after a night without sleep that his response was up to him and he is answerable for it — even though, as I pointed out, the temper and the exhaustion were not something he selected at the moment, or ever.

I suspect the slogan is being stretched, so I want it precisified rather than explained. Here is my own provisional apparatus, which I would like used as a test rather than corrected in passing. I would replace the binary with a three-way partition: things my will now determines; things my actions influence but do not determine; things nothing I do affects. And I can think of at least five notions of *up to me* that the slogan might mean — counterfactual dependence on what I choose now; sufficiency, in the sense that my choosing now guarantees the outcome; diachronic control, where I control it now only because of earlier training or choices; unpreventability, in the sense that no external force can compel my assent; and answerability, where *up to me* just marks the item as one for which I am the proper target of blame, regret, or credit. My worry is that the two most natural readings collapse in opposite directions: on the counterfactual reading almost everything is partly up to me, since my canvassing shifts the election by some epsilon, while on the sufficiency reading almost nothing is, since I cannot guarantee that I will keep my own resolve. That leaves either a continuum with an arbitrary threshold or a partition that is empty on one side. There is also a regress I can't close: whatever I control at a given moment I control because of an earlier choice and a character I did not choose, so it is unclear whether the slogan is meant to be evaluated at a time slice or over a life.

What I want is a menu. Set out the distinct precise theses the slogan could be expressing, from the platitudinous to the radical — including, if it belongs, a reading on which *up to us* is a modal claim about the invulnerability of assent rather than a causal claim about outcomes — and for each one say: how it would be stated so that it has a truth value; what, on that reading, lands on the *up to us* side; whether it is trivial, substantive and defensible, or false; and which of my three episodes it would actually settle. Please be explicit about the gap between a fact's not being up to me, its not being influenced by me, and its not being worth acting on or caring about, and say for each reading whether the inference the group needs goes through. If some readings are true but useless for the therapeutic purpose the group wants, and others support that purpose but are false, say which is which. Finally, tell me whether the contemporary *only how you respond* formulation has a different extension from the older *some things are up to us* formulation, since the former seems to me to inherit the third episode's difficulty about responses that issue from an unchosen character. I would like the answer as a table I can bring to the next meeting, and I would like your recommendation on which single precisification I should propose if I want to keep something of the practice while giving up the quietism about the campaign.
