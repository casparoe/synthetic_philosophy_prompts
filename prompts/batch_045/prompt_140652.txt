My friend works in model safety and insists on sorting every proposed safeguard into "genuine control" or "alignment with better branding" — her rough test being whether the measure still does any work if the system it guards is misaligned and would prefer it to fail. We argued late into last night about which rule she is actually applying. Her verdicts, which I wrote down afterward:

- airgapped deployment, no network egress, no actuators, a human executes anything consequential — control
- safety fine-tuning so that the model is honest and refuses bad requests — not control
- the model critiquing and revising its own outputs before they are released — not control
- a much weaker model flagging the deployed model's outputs for human review — control
- honeypot tasks planted in the deployment environment, with humans reviewing the resulting traces — control
- a second instance of the very same model, under different instructions, reviewing the first — not control
- monitoring the deployed model's written reasoning for stated bad intentions — not control
- asking the model before each consequential action whether it is about to cause harm — not control

The proposal we fought over: the deployed frontier system's actions are reviewed by a small critic model that is weak only in size. Its judgment was distilled from that same deployed system's own verdicts and written reasoning across a large corpus of comparable tasks, so its sense of what counts as suspicious just is the deployed system's sense. The critic gets no tools, no memory across episodes, and can only escalate to a human; the deployed model knows it is being reviewed but not where the escalation threshold sits.

I said that is control — the checker is small, and nothing in the scheme depends on the big model telling the truth about itself. She was unconvinced, and I couldn't articulate why. Give me the two or three rules that fit her track record equally well, what each of them predicts about this proposal, your best guess with the reasons for preferring it, and the variant of the case — a differently sized critic, a critic from a different lineage, a different way of training it — that would cleanly separate them.
