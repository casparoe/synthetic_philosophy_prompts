I want a number, and I want to see how you arrived at it.

**The setup.** I have been trying to prise apart three things people say they want from "free will", because I suspect they can come apart:

(i) the *desert-licensing* function — that blame, praise, resentment and punishment can be *deserved* rather than merely useful, expressive or forward-looking;
(ii) the *achievement* function — that a difficult thing one brought off is genuinely one's own, rather than something that merely happened through one;
(iii) the *prospective self-governance* function — that one can settle now what one will later do, by resolving, training oneself, arranging one's circumstances and so on, in a way that amounts to more than prediction.

Against those three I want to test one structural property. Call it **dense counterfactual reasons-sensitivity** (DCRS). An agent has DCRS to a high degree when this holds: across a suitably dense spread of perturbations to her deliberative situation — a consideration made slightly more vivid, a competing one surfaced earlier or later, a shift in mood, a reframing of the options, a small change in the order in which she attends to things — her eventual choice would vary, and would vary in a way that tracks the comparative weight, by her own lights, of the considerations she is responding to. "Dense" is doing real work: the requirement is not merely that she would have chosen otherwise given some drastically different reason, but that her choice-pattern is finely keyed to the landscape of reasons across a thick neighbourhood of the actual case. Assume further that the perturbations are ones compatible with the laws of nature and with her distal history, so that a deterministic world is not excluded by the definition, and neither, on the face of it, is one in which she could not have done otherwise.

**The proposition.** *P*: DCRS at a high degree is sufficient for all three of (i), (ii) and (iii). That is, an agent who has it — in a deterministic world, lacking alternative possibilities, and not being the ultimate source of her reasons-weighting machinery — is to exactly the same extent an apt target of desert claims, as fully the author of what she achieves, and as much a self-governor, as an agent in the best case that indeterminists imagine. Take the truth of *P* to consist in a claim about *us*: that the concepts we actually employ — desert, ownership of achievement, governing oneself — have, when made fully explicit, application conditions that DCRS alone already satisfies.
