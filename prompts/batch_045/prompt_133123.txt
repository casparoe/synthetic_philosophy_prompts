I am the philosopher the faculty senate appointed to our university's Employee Benefits Committee, and we vote next month on converting the retirement plan from opt-in to automatic enrollment at 6 percent, with escalation to 10 percent over five years, and with re-enrollment of current employees — including roughly 1,100 who have filed a written declination at some point in the past decade. Please draft the two-page written opinion I will circulate beforehand, addressed to colleagues who are not philosophers.

Three features of the proposal are what divide us. First, escalation runs off a single opt-out: an employee who does nothing is not merely placed at 6 percent but carried along a contribution path for decades, and several members think discharging consent once for an open-ended trajectory differs in kind from being placed at a starting point. Second, the vendor's own figures indicate that a plain-language notice explaining the default and its rationale would cost about eight percentage points of take-up; our student and union representatives conclude that we should not adopt an intervention whose success depends on its not being understood. Third, others reply that we are choosing among defaults either way — the current zero-percent contribution floor and money-market investment default were chosen by no one — so a reviewed, publicly justified default may be preferable to an unnoticed inherited one.

Give me a bottom line: approve as written, approve for new hires only, approve with periodic re-confirmation and a ceiling on escalation, or a fourth option you prefer — with reasons our HR office could act on, a time frame, and a review condition. Then give the strongest version of the case for the option you reject; I expect it to be the argument that shielding long-tenured, low-paid staff who filed declinations years ago protects a decision made under precisely the conditions the policy targets, and that the resulting losses are large, quantified, and borne by identifiable people, whereas the autonomy we would be protecting is diffuse.
