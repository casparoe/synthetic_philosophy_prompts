Hey — I need a case built to order for something I'm writing on Davidson's radical interpretation, and I'd rather have a designed case than one of the literature's chestnuts.

Setup as I understand it (say if I've got it wrong): the interpreter starts knowing nothing about the speaker's meanings; her evidence is just which sentences they hold true in which publicly observable circumstances; she fits a truth-theory to that using charity, i.e. read them so that most of what they hold true comes out true; and on top of that Davidson adds a causal or publicity constraint, that the things the sentences are about have to be objects and properties the interpreter and the speaker can jointly triangulate on, things that cause responses in both of them.

Spec: build me a case where charity and that causal constraint come apart. There should be one ordinary sentence the speakers use constantly, and two candidate readings of it that both fit the entire evidence base and both leave the speakers mostly right about the world, but that disagree about it's truth conditions — and where the reading charity prefers is not the reading the triangulation constraint prefers. The disagreement has to fall out of the setup, not be stipulated.

Constraints:

- ordinary working adults in a real skilled job. I was thinking hatchery chick-sexers — people who can sort day-old chicks by sex at a glance and can't say how they do it — but use that or anything equally real. No jungle field site, no armchair philosophers.
- no rabbit / rabbit-stage / undetached-rabbit-part permutation, no private objects, no inverted spectra, and no importing teleology or reference magnets from outside Davidson's own toolkit.
- the gap between the two readings must trace to one feature of the situation that you name and that I can switch off; with it switched off, the two readings have to collapse into one.
- bonus if the property at issue is one the speakers pick out instantly but the interpreter can only identify by a slower, indirect route (waiting six weeks for the comb to grow, a lab test, whatever).
- one paragraph of scenario, nothing science-fictional.

Then: give me each reading's clause for that sentence, run the charity check and the triangulation check on both, name the feature doing the work and show the collapse, and tell me honestly whether this is indeterminacy in Davidson's sense or just underdetermination that more evidence could in principle settle — and if more evidence could settle it, whether that evidence is the kind a radical interpreter is allowed to have. Finally: how would a committed Davidsonian say you cheated?
