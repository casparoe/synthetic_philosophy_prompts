My understanding from reading around is that this is the clean dividing line between the two camps: the Humean says my world is impossible or incoherent, because the laws supervene on the mosaic and same mosaic means same laws; the anti-Humean (the Armstrong/Dretske/Tooley sort) says fine, no problem, because laws are relations among universals that *govern* the events instead of merely summarising them.

Governing is what a government does, after all, and you can defy a government.

Where I need you: is that a fair picture of what the anti-Humean is committed to?
