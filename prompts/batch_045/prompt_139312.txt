She said sure, but then whatever does the unifying is itself just another item, so you need a further relation to tie it to the original ones, and now you need another, and another, ad infinitum—and by the end we were both just drawing arrows on a napkin and I definately think she was mixing up the regress with the problem of instantiation, but she insisted the regress is exactly why a proposition can never be a genuine unity unless relations aren't real or the regress isn't vicious. We're meeting again for coffee and I want to bring something that settles it (or at least makes the stalemate feel deliberate rather than just us being tired).

So what I'd love is for you to write me a villanelle—19 lines, with the two usual refrains alternating as the closing lines of the tercets and then showing up together in the final two lines of the last quatrain.
