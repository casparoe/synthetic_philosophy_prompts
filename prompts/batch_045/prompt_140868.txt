What I want translated is the cluster of claims and counter-claims that underwrite the idea that moral status attaches to computations rather than to the stuff that runs them, plus the worry that there is no determinate answer to which computation a given physical system is running. 1. Substrate independence: the claim that what matters, morally and mentally, is the abstract organisation of causal or functional relations rather than the physical material occupying the roles, so that two systems with the same organisation matter equally however differently they are built.

2. The indeterminacy objection: the claim that for a wide range of physical systems there are arbitrarily many computations the system can be said to implement, including computations with quite different psychological profiles, so that the phrase the computation this system implements has no determinate referent — and the standard repair, which is to demand that the mapping preserve causal structure or be non-gerrymandered, is itself a graded and contestable demand rather than a sharp criterion.

3. The grain problem: the observation that sameness of computation admits of degree. Small numerical perturbations, coarser or finer time steps, shrunken or expanded state spaces, and approximate rather than exact functional equivalence all vary continuously, so any verdict of same computation or different computation is a threshold imposed on a continuum.

4. The familiar style of argument which holds that if what mattered were the material rather than the organisation, then a gradual, part-by-part replacement of a system's components with functionally equivalent substitutes would make experience either fade slowly away or flicker on and off, and that neither outcome is credible — from which we are invited to conclude that organisation, not material, is what carries the weight.

Please work the translation through four concrete cases, because this is where I expect the vocabulary to be stretched:

(a) A batched inference cluster. A thousand user sessions are served by a single fused matrix multiplication over a batched tensor on one GPU. (b) A distilled surrogate. (c) Quantisation.
