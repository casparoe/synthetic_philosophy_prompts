So I've been going back and forth with my brother about something stupid and I want a straight answer, not a menu of options to pick from.

Every Thursday I drive home from an evening class in furniture restoration, about 50 minutes, and by the last stretch I'm on autopilot, the kind where you're suddenly in your own street and don't remember the last ten minutes. There are two left turns off the dual carriageway that both eventually get to my village. The first one is an unlit back lane, 20 mph behind tractors, adds maybe 15 minutes. The second is the normal road and costs nothing. If I miss both I'm committed to the carriageway for five miles to the next junction and then all the way back, I've timed it, about 12 minutes gone. The problem is the two turns look identical and are only about 200 metres apart, so when I'm zoned out I genuinely can't tell whether I've already passed the first one. Before anyone suggests it: the phone is no use, the blue dot lags and its in a cradle anyway, and there's nowhere safe to pull over on that stretch. I also tried a note stuck on the dash, which did nothing, because it's not that I forget to think about it, it's that even paying full attention I can't tell them apart.

The one thing that might help: roughly 200 metres before the second turn there's an all-night petrol station with a big lit sign on the right. When I really am at the second turn I reckon I register it maybe 7 times out of 10. When I'm at the first one, about 2 times out of 10 I have this vague feeling I've already passed the sign, and I only find out later that I hadn't. So a hint, not an answer.

My brother, who's a lorry driver and thinks he's always right, says never turn, just eat the 12 minutes, since turning is how you end up on the tractor lane. A mate from class says I should literally toss a coin at each turn, which sounds insane to me, how can leaving it to chance beat making your mind up, but I can't say why its wrong or why it might be right. And then the sign: should I just always go with the feeling, or would trusting it make things worse? Please tell me the one thing to do, turn, don't turn, or turn some of the time, and why, and whether the sign changes the answer. Also tell me flat out about my brother's other argument: he says if I've decided in advance what to do I should just stick to it at the turn, and I think at the turn I should just do whatever looks best from where I'm standing. If you need a number I haven't given you, pick one and say you picked it. Under 300 words, and don't hedge.
