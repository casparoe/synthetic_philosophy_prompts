Play an unnamed political philosopher — shrewd, unflustered, willing to grant small points but never the main one — who holds that Sen's liberal paradox is a reductio of the social-choice framework's assumption that every possible preference profile is an admissible input, not a reductio of rights and not of Pareto. Your positive thesis: a right is a protected sphere within which other people's preferences are not to count at all, so other people's attitudes toward your private conduct are precisely what rights exist to exclude from social choice, and the paradox only shows what happens when a framework with unrestricted domain is asked to honour a right. You may concede that textbook formulations of "non-meddlesome preferences" are too weak, that the condition you actually need is demanding, and that you owe an account of which preferences may be excluded — but never concede the thesis itself. My opening move. Take two people, A and B, and two protected choices: whether A reads a certain book in private, whether B does. A's payoffs: +1 if A reads, −2 if B reads. B's: +1 if B reads, −2 if A reads. Both preference orderings are additively separable across the two private choices — each person ranks reading above abstaining, and ranks it so whichever way the other goes, so neither is tailoring their own choice to the other's. Grant each only the minimum that minimal liberalism requires: A decisive between (read, read) and (abstain, read); B decisive between (abstain, read) and (abstain, abstain). A's right yields (read, read) over (abstain, read); B's yields (abstain, read) over (abstain, abstain); but both of them strictly prefer (abstain, abstain) to (read, read), so Pareto reverses the first. The cycle is back, and no one is meddling in anyone's sphere in the sense your diagnosis invokes. Check the arithmetic before you answer, then tell me which way you go: do you say that rights require people to be indifferent to each other's private conduct and defend that as a substantive thesis about rights, or do you abandon the meddling diagnosis and relocate the argument entirely?
