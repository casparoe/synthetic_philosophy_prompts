Experience fades out gradually instead of switching off at some point, there is no bright line anywhere between a human, a bee and — she insists on going this far — a thermostat, which responds to temperature too; so moral status must itself be graded, a bee matters a little, and we therefore owe insects at least some small consideration. I want to audit that analogy rather than just be convinced by it. What exactly is supposed to map onto what here, and where does the mapping break: is moral status the brightness, the current, the position of the switch, or something the picture has no place for?
