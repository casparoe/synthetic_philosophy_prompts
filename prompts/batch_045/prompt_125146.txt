Two things I want taken seriously rather than dodged:

1. A requirement of my own, which I've been calling intra-agential discriminability — a source counts as a genuine pramāṇa only if the agent can, from the inside and without already relying on that very source, tell that a given verdict came from it rather than from one of the others. Real desideratum or internalist baggage? What does it do to non-cognition, and to testimony from someone you can't check up on?

And it should handle nested sources: an inference one of whose premises came from a mostly unreliable informant, where the other premise somehow compensates.
