had a long argument with my friend last night about inside view vs outside view and I think I lost more of it then I'd like, so I want to know how exposed my position actually is. Here's the version I'd defend: whenever a case has a usable reference class, the outside view gets default authority, and the inside view earns weight *only* as evidence about which reference class you're in -- and even there it gets a small weight, because my sense that my case is special is exactly the sort of judgment I'm supposed to be modest about. I've been calling the cost of slicing the class finer and finer the "gerrymander tax": every time you narrow, you pay in sample size and in how much the surviving cases really resemble yours, and I claim the tax is real but bounded.

What would count against it? I want defeaters ranked from fatal to merely awkward, and I want you to separate the ones a defender of my view should honestly concede from the ones only a critic would throw. The three that got to me: (1) my friend said reference classes aren't found, they're built -- you can widen or narrow until you like the base rate -- and if modesty applies to my inside view it should apply to my sense of which class is the right one too, which either puts me in a regress or smuggles the inside view back in at the level of class choice; is there a non-arbitrary stopping point, and should the choice of class itself be governed by an outside view? (2) does consistent modesty eat itself -- if I discount my own reasoning about the details across the board, do I lose the ability to ever act on the details, and is that a reductio or a feature? (3) what empirical findings would actually move me, and are any of them arguably already in? I'd also like you to say whether my "usable reference class" escape hatch has qualified the whole thing into unfalsifiability, and if so whether that's damning for a view of this kind. The one that stuck with me is the thought that the outside view can't get off the ground without some inside-view judgment doing load-bearing work -- start there.
