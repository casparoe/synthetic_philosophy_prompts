I chair the quarterly coherence review of my agency's risk register: about forty operational teams each submit five to fifteen probabilities for events that overlap and are logically related (a flood at site X; a flood at X or Y; a river gauge exceeding a threshold; conditional judgements such as the probability of a service outage given the gauge threshold is breached). The numbers are signed off by the chief risk officer and feed planning and capital decisions. My reviewers are engineers and planners, not mathematicians; they have one afternoon, a spreadsheet and no solver; submissions arrive rounded to the nearest five per cent or as ranges; some teams decline to take the other side of their own numbers; and some events may never resolve.

Please turn the Dutch book argument for probabilism — degrees of belief that are not probabilities expose you to a combination of bets at your own odds that loses in every possible state — into a written procedure my reviewers can actually run. It should specify the checks in order, what counts as passing each one, where it stops and rejects a submission outright, and where it must hand the decision to a named person because the issue is a modelling or values choice rather than a matter of coherence. It has to cope with: rounding and range artefacts that mimic incoherence; sets from different teams that are each coherent but jointly exploitable; mid-quarter revisions, so that planning-incoherence across time is caught as well as static incoherence; events that may never be resolved; and a materiality threshold, since a guaranteed loss of well under a per cent of stake is not worth chasing. State plainly what the procedure cannot do — it tests coherence, not accuracy or calibration, and it cannot tell a team what its numbers ought to be — and where it will misfire.

Close with three worked applications: a pair of submissions that are individually coherent but jointly admit a sure loss; a submission that looks incoherent only because of rounding; and one that is fully coherent while everyone regards its numbers as absurd, where honest application of the procedure returns a pass and I must override it on other grounds. Please write in British English.
