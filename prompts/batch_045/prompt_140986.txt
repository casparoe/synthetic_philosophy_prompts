In section last week I came up with a pair of sentences that I would like the question built around, because it seems to me to separate people who understand the machinery from people who have memorised that Kripke assigns a third value to the Liar. Assume a language with its own truth predicate, a name for each of its sentences, and a factual sentence that is determinately true. The pair is:

(B) Either the sentence (A) is not true, or snow is white.

My own calculation is that a Strong Kleene minimal fixed point makes (B) true and (A) false, while a Weak Kleene version makes both ungrounded, and that a Tarskian hierarchical theory cannot even formulate the pair.
