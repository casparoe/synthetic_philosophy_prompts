hey, I've been trying to get my own view on the statue-and-history fights straight and I keep feeling like two bits of it dont fit together. Roughly where I've landed:

1. the "man of his time" line seems basically right to me as a defence against blame — it's unfair to condemn someone for failing to get past a moral horizon that basically nobody in their world had got past.
2. but I also think it stops working the moment the criticism was actually available: if people in their own society were making the objection, "that's just how it was" shouldn't get you off. This is why Columbus looks easy to me, and why I don't really accept the defence for Jefferson either.
3. judging the person and deciding what to do with statues, names and holidays strike me as different questions — the second is about what we now want to publicly affirm, not a verdict on somebody dead.
4. I think our own period is going to look pretty monstrous in hindsight for stuff most of us are entirely comfortable with now (I'd put how we treat animals and the global poor in that bucket), and that the future people saying so will be right.
5. I still want to keep honouring Churchill for 1940 while condemning the imperial stuff — being worth honouring for what you gave people doesn't require being morally clean.

I've also been using a rule of thumb I half invented, which I call the anticipation test: don't condemn someone from the past in a way you wouldn't accept from a future generation that's got your number.

A friend says (3) makes (1) and (2) pointless — if the statue question isn't a verdict on the person, why does it matter whether he's excused? I think she's wrong and I can't say why. What I'm actually stuck on is that (4) and (5) push me toward being way more forgiving in general, while (2) keeps making almost everybody condemnable, and I think Churchill differs from Columbus in a way I can't quite state — what we honour Churchill for feels detachable from what we condemn him for, and with Columbus it doesn't.

Can you audit this for me: which of these really pull against each other, which only look like they do, ordered by how bad the clash is, and for each one what I'd have to give up and what that would cost me? Don't tell me which one you'd drop, just tell me what each option commits me to.
