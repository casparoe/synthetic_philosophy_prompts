On the second run I do all of it, and I feel a bit grim afterwards. Meanwhile I have cheerfully murdered many thousands of anonymous guards, and nobody — me included — thinks that's anything at all. But none of these characters can suffer — they're scripts and animations, nothing is hurt, the whole thing resets if I turn the console off. And yet the guilt is real guilt, and it tracks really specific things: whether the character has a name and a voice, whether it pleads or just grunts, whether the game lets it react afterwards, whether it stays dead or respawns in thirty seconds.

What I can't work out is what any of this presupposes.
