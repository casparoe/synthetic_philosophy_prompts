Three cases where my intuition and theirs come apart, and I want them handled in those terms:

One, we have this thing in the lab we call aversive replay drag: prioritized replay oversamples transitions with large TD error magnitude, and in an environment where most rewards are negative, the agent ends up preferentially revisiting exactly the states where it gets hurt worst and is most surprised about it. The value function says those states are terrible; the behavior says it keeps going back. Under the suffering-is-what-you're-motivated-to-avoid reading, is this agent not suffering, or is the going-back part of the suffering?
