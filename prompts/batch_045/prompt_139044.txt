She uses this to say epiphenomenalism is just sober science with no mystery left in it, and that the paradox of phenomenal judgment is a pseudo-problem: if qualia do no causal work, then my judgment that im in pain is caused by the neural basis of the pain and not by the pain, so it looks like my introspective reports could float free of what they report, and if that generalizes every belief I have is in the same boat — and she thinks the whistle picture shows that worry is just taking the first-person view too seriously.
