Write the full text of a Call for Proposals issued by a fictional funding body, the Institute for Non-Empirical Theory Assurance, announcing its 2027 grant round under the Post-Empirical Theory Confirmation Framework. Target length 750–900 words, laid out as a funding-agency notice with numbered sections: Eligibility; Review Criteria and Weighting; Admissible and Inadmissible Evidence; Reporting Requirements; Worked Example of Two Ranked Proposals; External Reviewer Comment, with the Committee's Response.

Setup. The Institute funds work in the region of fundamental physics — string theory and landscape/multiverse cosmology — where no direct empirical test is available on any foreseeable timescale. Rather than treat this as a problem, it has formalized the informal practice by which such research programmes are assessed without data, and it now scores them. The target of the satire is that practice and the bureaucratic form it has been given: a discipline's honest attempt to say what it could possibly mean to confirm a theory that makes no checkable claims, taken at its word and turned into an administrative procedure with rating scales.

Requirements for the piece.

The three recognized confirmation arguments must each appear as a scored criterion, with a weight and a rating scale: the no-alternatives argument (no comparably successful competitor having been found despite sustained search); unexpected explanatory coherence (the theory's delivering connections it was not constructed to deliver); and the meta-inductive argument (programmes with those two features having historically turned out to be on the right track). The jokes should come from the fact that each is a fact about the history and sociology of a research community being recorded in a column labelled Evidence, and from the Committee's inability to see why that is odd.

Give the composite score an explicit formula, invented for the piece — something like the product of the no-alternatives and unexpected-coherence ratings divided by the number of testable implications the proposal retains — and have the Committee note, in a footnote, that for most applicants this denominator is zero and that the standing convention is then to treat the score as unbounded.

The Inadmissible Evidence section must reject, in the vocabulary of premature empirical closure: proposed experiments, observations, phenomenological work, and any derived prediction about the observable world, on the ground that committing to testability now would lock the programme into the accidental features of present instrumentation and foreclose its deeper insights. Include a clause under which a proposal that does yield a testable prediction is referred for Premature Empirical Closure review and, if the finding is upheld, defunded — a prediction being a liability, a hostage the theory can lose.

Include a straight-faced requirement that applicants state what fraction of the landscape of vacua their proposal renders anthropically disfavored, even though there is no agreed way to count vacua and hence no well-defined measure over which that fraction could be taken; the notice should instruct applicants to normalize in the manner standard in their subfield and to report a sensitivity band across at least three measures, and it should treat this as a solved problem of reporting hygiene.

Include a criterion penalizing credit claimed for accommodating data the theory was built to fit: any such datum must be declared retrofitted and discounted to zero confirmatory value. The notice may observe, without apparent discomfort, that nearly all of the programme's contact with observation is of this kind.

Include a mandatory Cognitive Stability Attestation, in which applicants certify that their proposal does not imply that the reader's own observations are almost certainly random fluctuations in a thermal bath — an attestation the Committee explains was introduced because several previous awards did imply exactly this and were nonetheless confirmed.

Worked example. Rank first, and fund, a proposal that retires a large region of the landscape using anthropic selection and reports the disfavored fraction as a three-measure sensitivity band. Rank last, and decline, a proposal that computes a sharp falsifiable prediction about the cosmic microwave background, docking it for premature empirical closure and for being in principle disconfirmable and therefore speculative.

External reviewer comment. One paragraph in the voice of a falsificationist objecting on principle: a framework that insulates a theory from every possible observation cannot be confirming it; calling the structure of a research programme evidence does not make it evidence; and the framework, scored by its own criteria, would do very well. Then the Committee's response, written so that it has genuinely failed to grasp the objection and answers it using the framework's own resources — noting, for instance, that this is the only objection of its kind and that no alternative framework has been proposed, and treating that as a point in the framework's favour.

Constraints. Deadpan and sustained: no character may suggest that the framework is absurd, and the notice must never announce that it is a joke, while remaining legible to a reader who has not been told. The piece should be funny to a philosopher of science and readable to someone who is not one. The target is the practice of non-empirical theory assessment and the genre of the funding notice — not any person, living or dead, and not physicists as a class. No citations, references, or footnotes to real literature, apart from the invented Committee footnote above.
