Before I start comparing cyclical pictures of history with directional ones, I want the specification rather than the lineup. Please write the desiderata that an account of history's large-scale shape would have to meet to count as adequate — for a theory meant to do two jobs: say what kind of claim "history cycles" or "history has a direction" even is, and tell me whether five centuries of cumulative science and technology are evidence against recurrence views. Give the case or consideration behind each requirement, mark each as non-negotiable or as the preference of a particular camp, and flag any subset you suspect cannot be jointly satisfied; that tension is the part I most want.

Three things I want handled explicitly:

The ratchet asymmetry. Some variables ratchet — a technique, an energy source, or a body of mathematics, once achieved, is very rarely lost — while others plainly do not: regime type, elite cohesion, artistic style, the balance between center and frontier. Call this the ratchet asymmetry. Is a cycle claim about a ratcheting variable coherent at all, or must an honest recurrence view restrict itself to non-ratcheting variables and say why?

Units and their interactions. Whose history has the shape — a polity, a civilization, a species-wide system — and if several, what does the theory owe us about coupling between units whose cycles might combine into an aggregate pattern that is neither cyclic nor linear?

Phase identity and projectivity. What makes two periods instances of the same phase rather than merely similar, and can the criterion be applied forward, to a period not yet periodized, or does it work only retrospectively?

Also say whether the theory must locate us — must it place the present in a phase, and must it explain its own emergence as a stage-relative symptom, in the way a writer who takes himself to be in a late season would have to — or whether that demand is a reductio of the enterprise rather than a requirement on it. Do not collapse type-recurrence with the return of a token, or oscillation with directional accumulation, and treat "no large-scale shape" as a live candidate the desiderata must be able to fail. Name which single requirement excludes the most candidates and which demands are really tribal prejudice. A one-line scorecard for the cyclical tradition from Polybius through Ibn Khaldun's account of group solidarity to the civilizational morphologies of Spengler and Toynbee, and for cumulative or big-history pictures, is welcome at the end, but the criteria are the deliverable.
