I want a case where that move is genuinely open to you and would in a sense work — the helper really is fooled, you really can acquire the disposition — and yet taking it leaves you worse off than not taking it, for a reason that lives inside this one transaction.

- The reason self-binding backfires can't be that the disposition is expensive in the rest of your life (that's the move everyone reaches for and its boring). It has to be something about this deal.
- State the payoffs in the case, in numbers, so the arithmetic is checkable. One paragraph.
