The claim at issue, as I've seen it stated, is the equation P(if A then C) = P(C|A); and the triviality result is that no proposition plays the role of if A then C: demand of any candidate column X that its mean equal P(C|A) under every reweighting of the rows, and every pair of columns comes out probabilistically independent, which for a finite table leaves no non-degenerate weighting that satisfies it.
