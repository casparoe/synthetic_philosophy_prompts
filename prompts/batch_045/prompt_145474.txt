what I want: an excerpt from an official white paper, 700–800 words, from a fictional democracy that has decided the cure for democratic decay is to abolish its directly elected presidency and install a hereditary constitutional monarchy instead. Numbered sections, at least one annex, at least one costed budget line, and one quotation from an unnamed "senior constitutional adviser." Deadpan civil-service voice the whole way through.
