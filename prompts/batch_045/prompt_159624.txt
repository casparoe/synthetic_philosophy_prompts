So: three or four questions to prioritise, in order, with the ordering defended — tell me what you're using as criteria (how much else hangs on the answer, wether a philosopher rather than a modeller can move it in three years, whether the area is already crowded) and where those criteria pull against each other. For each question, say what result or development during the funding period would move it up or down the list. And I'd really like your view on one worry I can't shake: whether the Monday test, applied honestly, just collapses into 'fund better bridge laws', or into some point about explanation that the scientists will ignore anyway — and if it does, what the panel ought to fund instead.
