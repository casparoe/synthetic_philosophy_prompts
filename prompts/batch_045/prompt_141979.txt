I am a distributed systems engineer, and I have just spent a long and unsatisfying evening arguing about presentism and eternalism with a colleague who is a physicist. His claim, in effect, was that the dispute is verbal: separate what is in the current state of the system from what is in the durable log, and there is nothing left to argue about. I think he is smuggling something, but I could not say what. So I want the translation done properly rather than dissolved.

My working vocabulary is events and the happened-before relation, logical and vector clocks, consistent cuts and their frontiers, linearizability, total-order broadcast, quorums, append-only logs, and branching runs. Assume I am fluent in all of it and use the terms without defining them.

Please restate the three main positions — presentism, eternalism, and the growing-block or possibilist view — in that vocabulary, as faithfully as you can. Do not give me a block-universe essay; I want terms like event, state, commit, cut, frontier, log, branch, and invariant used wherever they apply. Break the positions apart into at least four separable claims: what exists simpliciter; what makes tensed statements true, including statements about the future; whether reality grows or whether the passage of time is real; and whether the future is open in a way the past is not. For each claim under each position, say what translates cleanly, what translates only with distortion, and what has no expression in my vocabulary at all.

Then test a candidate translation of my own. My hunch was that the present is the frontier of a consistent cut, a maximal antichain in the happened-before order, and that the passage of time is the advance of that frontier as events commit. Does that land on presentism, on a moving-spotlight view, on the growing block, or on something my vocabulary has quietly substituted for all three? Tell me where it distorts and why.

Finally, the residue report. I expect at least three trouble spots, and I want them worked through rather than gestured at. First, my framework has no global now, only per-node views and a partial order, so the relativity-of-simultaneity argument for eternalism may look to me like something I conceded years ago; and if a presentist can simply relativize the present to a frame or to a single spacetime point, say what that relativization corresponds to on my side — a per-node view, a quorum, or something else. Second, I have no primitive for real or for exists, only for present-in-state and for durable or replicated, which are already representational notions. Third, the growth of the log has itself to be indexed by events inside the log, which I suspect is the old complaint that a growing block needs a second dimension of time in which to grow.

Given that residue, tell me whether it is a defect of my vocabulary or a sign that the philosophical question was less clear than it looks. Where my colleague is right that the dispute dissolves, say so; where he is cheating, identify exactly which claim he has made unstatable rather than answered. I have not read the literature, so keep the answer self-contained and argue for your diagnosis rather than reporting what has been said.
