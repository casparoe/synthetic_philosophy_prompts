posting this here because I keep going in circles and I want someone to tell me which of these has to go. Here's roughly where I've ended up on anthopics, and I can't tell whether it's a package or a pile:

1. I'm a thirder in Sleeping Beauty. On waking up I say 1/3 on heads.

2. I accept something like SIA. If one hypothesis says a billion observers and the other says a trillion, I think the fact that I exist at all is real evidence for the second one, roughly in proportion. I've never understood treating the presumptuous philosopher as a reductio — it reads to me like a bullet you bite, not a problem.

3. In the incubator case (coin flip; heads, one person gets created in a room; tails, two people in two rooms; you wake up not knowing which) I say 1/3 on heads. And I also say 1/3 in a variant I made up, where on tails the second person is created a thousand years later instead of at the same time. That second one I'm less sure about, but the timing feels irrelevant to me — either way all I've got is "I exist and I can't tell which branch I'm in."

4. I still want self-sampling for the within-world stuff. If a hypothesis says there are ten observers, exactly one in a red jacket, and I find myself in a red jacket, I treat myself as a random draw from those ten. I don't know what else "I don't know which one I am" is supposed to mean.

5. I reject the doomsday argument. Being early isn't evidence of doom.

6. Two meta commitments. First: I want one principle covering all of the above, not a reference class I re-tune per case — whether Beauty-on-Monday and Beauty-on-Tuesday count as one observer or two should be settled by how the world is, not by which verdict I'm after. Second: any version of this that ends up telling me I'm probably a Boltzmann brain, or that my ordinary evidence about the world gets swamped by anthropic considerations, has gone off the rails.

So: audit it. Which pairs are genuinely in tension and which only look like it? Rank the real ones by how bad they are, and for each say what would have to give and what giving it up costs me — I'd much rather drop one thing than rebuild the whole view. If a single revision cleans up most of the mess, say which. My guess is the trouble is somewhere in the Sleeping Beauty corner, but I've definately stared at this too long to trust that.
