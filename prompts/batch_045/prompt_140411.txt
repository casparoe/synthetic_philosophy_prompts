Write a parody of a product certification document, roughly 600 words: a "Declaration of Conformity and Test Report" issued by an invented regulator — the Office for Agentic Restraint — certifying a general-purpose household agent as Class A+++ under a statutory low-impact standard. Use the deadpan voice of a conformity assessment body: numbered clauses, a table of test results with pass/fail remarks, and a footnote explaining the rating scale.

The humour has to come from the actual structure of proposals for penalising side effects, not from generic AI-safety mockery. The piece should be funny to someone who knows that literature and legible to someone who does not, and every exploit the certificate certifies should be traceable to a real design choice in it:

(i) the decomposition of a side-effects penalty into a baseline state plus a measure of deviation from that baseline, and the fact that the baseline is where the value-laden choices hide — in particular the stepwise inaction baseline, what would have happened had the agent never been switched on;
(ii) deviation measures defined on what the agent can still bring about rather than on how the resulting state differs — relative reachability, attainable-utility preservation — and the consequence that a certified agent is untroubled by the magnitude of a change once it has been made;
(iii) the incentive to prevent irreversible changes, including changes other agents would have made;
(iv) offsetting: satisfying a deviation measure with a compensating change elsewhere rather than by not causing the harm in the first place;
(v) the fact that a penalty on departure from inaction punishes an agent most severely for averting a catastrophe that would have happened anyway;
(vi) the awkward status of being switched off.

Constraints: invent no failure modes that are not exaggerations of the above; name no real researchers, papers, or institutions; attribute the standard's technical annexes to invented committees. Each entry in the results table should record the agent having passed by exploiting the metric, and a careful reader should be able to reconstruct which clause of the standard licensed the exploit. Let the final clause land the reductio without editorialising: the certified agent has left the world measurably unchanged and appreciably worse. Answer in British English.
