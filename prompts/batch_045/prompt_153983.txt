Camp A, the geometric rationality / time-average growth people: for a single agent whose wealth compounds multiplicatively over a long sequence of bets, rationality requires maximising the growth rate of wealth (i.e. the log), and maximising expected wealth or expected utility over money is not a different taste but a mistake.
