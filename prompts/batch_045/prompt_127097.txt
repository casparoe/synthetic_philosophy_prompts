Could you draft me eight interview questions, each with (i) the answer she's most likely to give, in one sentence, and (ii) a follow-up that takes that answer away from her? I want at least one each on:

- the graft case: two colonies' networks fused into one continuous map (I've been playing with a version were you swap foragers between the colonies over a week) — one mind, two minds, or a new one, and does she have any criterion of identity for colonies over time to settle it?
