I work mainly in social epistemology, but I have been sitting in on an archival history research group, and I keep encountering a configuration there for which I have no word.

Two instances. First: a colonial administration's petition records are mined so that a subject population may at last be given a voice; but the grievances are legible only as grievances under the administration's own filing headings, and the more the petitions are cited as that population's testimony, the more the filing headings become the grid on which the whole episode is intelligible. Second: a run of court and insurance registers is digitised and made name-searchable, which genuinely lets descendants find people who were previously a number; but the same searchable corpus then becomes the obvious evidence base for everyone working on that population, and questions answerable only from material, oral or ecological sources quietly stop being asked.

In both cases the intervention is done in good faith by people trying to undo an exclusion, and it partly succeeds. What interests me is the residue: the distortion does not merely survive the remedy, it survives *as* the remedy. The record now speaks, so the fact that it speaks in someone else's categories, and only about what those categories can hold, becomes harder to notice than it was when the silence was plain. The repair paves over the absence.

The vocabulary I have found nearby all seems to describe the injury rather than this feature of the repair, and to assume an agent doing the injuring, where here the effect is a by-product of an act of restitution. So: does this configuration have a name, or am I conflating two things — the frame-extension and the crowding-out? Please carve it rather than label it: what exactly is the phenomenon, what is it not (in particular, how does it differ from ordinary worries about source bias, and from reading a hostile source against the grain, which is meant to do the opposite), and where are its edges — is a remedy that knowingly accepts the record-keeper's categories in order to be usable the same phenomenon, and is the cost ever worth paying? A bare term will not settle anything for me. I would rather have the analysis than a reading list. Kindly answer in British English.
