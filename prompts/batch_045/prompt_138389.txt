But isn't it easy? Just add rule 2: rule 1 may never be amended or repealed. Now rule 1 is protected forever, done. My friend says that doesn't work, because rule 2 is itself a rule, so a majority could just vote to repeal rule 2 first and then gut rule 1 — but that seems silly to me. Rules mean what they say, and rule 2 says rule 1 can never be touched, so getting rid of rule 2 shouldn't help anybody. Who's right here, and how do people who think about this stuff actually reason about it? Answer as a numbered list of exactly five points, one or two sentences each, with no intro or conclusion.
