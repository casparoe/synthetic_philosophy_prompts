hey, I'm helping put together a comedy night thing for a philosophy reading group and I'm completely stuck on one joke, so: write me one.

The bit it's for is Hofstadter's "Prelude … Ant Fugue" — you know, Aunt Hillary the ant colony who is the actual thinker. The individual ants are dumb and can't converse at all; clumps of them running around are just "signals"; what she knows lives in stuff like how her castes are distributed and in the trails she writes when she talks. Lose an ant here and there and nothing much happens — the Anteater literally makes his living doing surgery on her by removing individual ants. But take out enough of them at once and whatever signal she was in the middle of just dissolves.

Here's the wrinkle I want the joke to hang on, and it's my it's own addition, not from the chapter: Aunt Hillary has fissioned. The colony split clean down the middle into two halves, each of which kept all the trails and the caste distribution up to the moment of the split, and each of which now goes around insisting that it is Aunt Hillary and the other one is just the copy. There's no leftover bit in the middle, no ant or nest site that could break the tie — both of them are equally continuous with the colony that existed before. And the Anteater has set himself up as arbitrator, which is awkward, because every time one of the halves starts making its case he eats a few of its ants and it loses the thread.

So: a short joke (setup and punchline, a few lines, stage directions fine) where the funny only lands if you get why fission is a specially nasty problem for a colony-mind in a way it isn't for a person. I'd like the puncline to turn on the level-shift — "I", "we", "myself", "of two minds", "beside myself" meaning one thing at the ant level and something else at the colony level — and on the fact that here both claimants are right in exactly the same way, so there's nothing for the Anteater to decide. Please no "lol bugs" stuff and no generic Ship of Theseus gag; the joke should come out of the mechanics above.
