Hey — I'm writing up a short position piece on markets and the limits of markets for a law-and-policy reading group, and before I let other people read it I want to know whether my views actually hang together. Here's roughly where I've landed:

1. The money shouldn't be doing the moral work on its own. If its fine for someone to do X for free, the state needs some further reason — coercion, exploitation, harm to third parties — to stop them doing X for pay. That its for money is not by itself a reason.
2. I'm against price gouging laws. After a hurricane, letting the price of ice, plywood and generators jump is doing real work: it rations demand toward whoever needs the stuff most urgently and pulls in supply from out of state. Banning it feels good and makes the shortage worse.
3. I want to legalize payment for kidneys, but through a single state purchaser paying one fixed, generous price rather than an open market. I like that design partly because it stops rich recipients from bidding against each other over poor people's organs.
4. I want commercial surrogacy banned while altruistic surrogacy stays legal.
5. Vote buying should stay a crime even where nobody is threatened, nobody is misled, and the outcome of the election wouldnt change.
6. Bare symbolic objections — it degrades the thing, it corrupts the meaning of having children or of citizenship or of the body, people just find it repulsive — carry no weight for me on their own. Name the coercion, the exploitation or the bad outcome, or I dont hear it.
7. I'm fairly relaxed about the fact that any new market gives richer people better access. The fix is redistribution and transfers, not banning the market.

So: which of these actually pull against each other, and which only look like they do? I cant tell where the problem is, and I dont have a favorite I'd rather give up — I'd rather find the least costly revision. Please rank the tensions by how severe they are rather than flagging all of them as equally bad, say for each what would have to give and roughly what giving it up would cost me elsewhere in the package, and then tell me which single revision would clean up the most. Please answer in American English — US spellings, and phrase things the way a US-trained philosopher or lawyer would.
