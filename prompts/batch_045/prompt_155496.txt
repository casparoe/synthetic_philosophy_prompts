I want a plan for the lecture, not the lecture.

I want one case to carry the session. The two assumptions then pull in opposite directions rather than merely disagreeing about how strongly to prefer the more crowded world. Is that the case to build on, or does it import too much, and what must a carrying example here contain and omit?
