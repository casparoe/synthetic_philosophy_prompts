My guest thinks Judy should adopt the new credence function that stays as close as possible, in the relative-entropy or Kullback-Leibler sense, to her old uniform assignment while making the conditional probability of Red given Second Company equal to 3/4; the result is supposed to nudge her probability that she is in Red above 1/2 and, according to the usual complaints, drag her probability that she is in Second Company down below 1/2. I need eight or nine interview questions for a 30-minute Q&A, not softballs: I want at least one question each on (a) why Bayes’s rule and Jeffrey’s rule are supposed to be unable to handle a bit of evidence that just constrains a conditional probability rather than telling Judy a plain proposition or handing her new probabilities for a partition, (b) why the stay-as-close-as-possible-to-your-old-credences instinct is the right one here at all, (c) the fact that your distance-minimizing update moves Judy’s marginal for Second Company even though the message was only about what happens conditional on Second Company — and a follow-up for the reply that the constraint plus maximum entropy fills in the gap, and (d) the embarassment that other distance measures, like minimizing squared Euclidean distance instead of relative entropy, respect the same 3-to-1 constraint but give Judy a slightly different final probability for Red, so why should we pick your measure over those.
