Second, the skeptical line I keep running into, which says this whole style of exorcism is either circular or irrelevant: that the demon can be defused by ordinary statistical mechanics without invoking information at all, that the second law needs no defence from information theory, and that talk of bits bought and paid for is doing rhetorical rather than physical work.
