Verdict: I want to say knowledge but much less confidently, which seems odd given how similar the two are.

Verdict: knowledge.

6. I check the board and believe the 10:15 leaves from platform 2. It does. Separately I also falsely believe the 10:15 is the last train of the night, and my platform belief would be untouched if I found out it isn't. Verdict: knowledge.
