We then said that for an FFR to become a reason *for me*, some further thing has to hold; we called it anchoring. My friend then built this, and I couldn't touch it:

(2) So something extra has to bridge from an FFR to a reason for me. Call it anchoring. Either anchoring is automatic — every FFR is a reason for every agent — or it's substantive: it requires something of me.

(3) Anchoring can't be automatic.
