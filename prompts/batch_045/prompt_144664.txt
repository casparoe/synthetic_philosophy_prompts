So I'm in a seminar on personal identity this term and we spent last week on Parfit's teletransporter — the scan-and-rebuild-on-Mars machine that destroys the original back on Earth. We got completely stuck on the branching variant, where the scanner misfires and doesnt destroy you, so you and the Mars replica both go on to live separate lives from then on. Parfit's move there, as I understand it, is that this is in no way worse than ordinary survival, because what matters in survival is the holding of psychological continuity with any cause, not identity, and identity just isn't the thing we were ever really caring about. My friend Priya thinks that's nuts and I half think she's right, but I can't tell which part of the package I'm actually resisting — the reductionism, or the bit where two branches at full strength each get the whole of what matters, or something else.
