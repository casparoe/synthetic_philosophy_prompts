Next week I am giving a paper at a joint session of the formal epistemology reading group and the statistics department's Bayesian modelling seminar. The room will be about twenty people: mostly philosophers working on Bayesian epistemology, several of them accuracy-first (update rules justified by scoring-rule or inaccuracy minimisation), four applied Bayesians from statistics and machine learning who work daily with soft evidence, measurement error and virtual-evidence methods, and two people whose home turf is the logic and probability of conditionals (Adams's thesis, the Lewis-style triviality results). Nobody there works on testimony or speech acts, so the vocabulary of report selection and message menus will be new to them.

My thesis is that the Judy Benjamin case is not a puzzle about a distinctive kind of evidence. Judy starts with a uniform prior over four quadrants (her own versus enemy territory crossed with headquarters versus second company) and receives a radio message that constrains only a conditional probability — say, that conditional on being in enemy territory she is three times as likely to be in the second-company quadrant. The standard treatments read the message as a constraint on her credence and then argue over the rule: minimise relative information (KL divergence) subject to the constraint, which spreads the shift across all four cells and moves the unconditional probability of being in enemy territory off one-half, or update rigidly, holding that probability fixed. I claim both answers address an under-described question. What fixes the posterior is the reporter's selection policy: which conditional she would have sent, from which menu of available messages, in each of the four situations. Specify that policy and ordinary conditioning on the message event delivers the answer; KL-minimisation and the rigid update each correspond to a different, unstated policy. So there is no canonical Judy Benjamin posterior, and the apparent non-commutativity of conditional-evidence updating is an artefact of leaving the report process out of the model, since conditioning on a message with a fully specified likelihood commutes.
