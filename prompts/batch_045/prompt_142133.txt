I've been arguing about weakness of will in rationalist-adjacent comment sections for years and have drifted into a position nobody there will poke at properly, so I want a straight consistency audit before I write it up as something I actually endorse. The package is:

(1) Akrasia in the strict sense is real and common: the agent acts intentionally, at the moment of action still judges that the alternative is better, and is not merely forgetful, ignorant, or covertly re-evaluating the option now that it is vivid.

(2) I am Humean about action explanation: motivation is supplied by desire, not by evaluative judgement, and the action that occurs is the one the agent was most strongly moved to perform.

(3) The akratic action is nonetheless, in some important sense, not fully the agent's own — she is alienated from it — and the standpoint she is genuinely identified with is the deliberative one that issued the better judgement. I want to keep some hierarchical, identification-based story here rather than treat the agent as a mere battleground of desires.

(4) Most of what gets called akrasia is well modelled by hyperbolic discounting and present bias, and present bias is a species of irrationality: the temporally neutral, exponentially discounting agent is the normative benchmark, and naivety about one's own future present bias is a further failing on top of the bias itself.

(5) But the addict who judges that she should stop and then uses anyway is not akratic — that is compulsion, and compulsion and weakness are different phenomena, not points on a continuum.

Go through the pairs and tell me which of these genuinely pull against each other and which only look as though they do. Rank the real tensions by severity rather than flagging all of them equally, and for each say what would have to give and what that revision would cost me elsewhere in the set. Where an apparent tension dissolves on a closer look, say why it dissolves — I would rather have one pair correctly cleared than five pairs vaguely suspected. Finally, if there is a single revision that would clean up the most, name it. Please answer in British English.
