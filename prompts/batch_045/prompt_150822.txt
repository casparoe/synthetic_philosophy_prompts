Im a retired soil scientist who's gotten into philosophy late, and there's a practice I keep running into that I want the implicit theory of.

I volunteer with a habitat restoration crew. Their monitoring sheet scores whether each ecosystem "function" has come back at a site, measured against a reference meadow nearby, and two things in it dont sit together. For nutrient cycling and decomposition they plainly dont care who does the work: the lab-grown strain of nitrogen-fixing soil crust they inoculate with was cultured from a site 300 miles away, and if the nitrogen and decomposition numbers match the reference site they tick the box and call the function restored. But for pollination they refuse to tick the box unless the actual historical bumblebee species shows up in the counts, even in the years when a domesticated honeybee has taken over every flower on the site and seed set is identical to the reference. Same sheet, same word function, two opposite rules about whether the identity of the thing doing the job matters.

Third thing, which may or may not be the same problem: there's a native shrub on the site that still makes the same bitter compounds it has always made, which used to defend it against a leaf beetle that's been locally extinct for about eighty years, and now those compounds poison the caterpillars of a butterfly the crew is trying to reintroduce. The trait is doing exactly what it was shaped to do and everybody treats it as a malfunction.

So what I want: what theory of biological function is this sheet running on? There seem to be two candidate stories knocking around the literature, one where a trait's function is what it was historically selected to do and one where it's just what the trait currently contributes to the system it sits in. Can either one generate all three of those calls, or is the protocol quietly switching between them, and if so is that switching principled or sloppy? Tell me which bits it strictly presupposes and which bits it merely goes well with, and what the sheet would have to look like if you made it consistent, under each story taken seriously.
