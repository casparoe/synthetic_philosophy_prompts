I spent most of my career in experimental physics and have only recently begun auditing philosophy seminars, so I'd like you to act as a philosopher of personal identity who is also fluent in the Buddhist no-self (anatta) literature, and to work the reading out before you write: sketch two or three candidate ways of mapping the doctrine onto the film, discard the ones that don't survive contact with the actual scenes, and then give me the strongest one, stating plainly where the film resists it.

The film is Everything Everywhere All at Once. I'm interested in what it does with the self: Evelyn meets versions of herself whose lives diverged decades earlier, and the resolution turns on her coming to see something rather than on her becoming a different person, though the scene in which she and Joy are both rocks seems to gesture at the claim that there was never a self to begin with.

Two things I want pinned down. First, the relation between Evelyn and her other versions is not the branching or fission case familiar from the personal identity literature, where psychological continuity splits and identity is said to become indeterminate or empty. Her counterparts share ancestry and little else; they read more like the same person under different conditions than like continuations of one person. Does that difference matter for whether the film illustrates anatta, or does the no-self reading require the continuity relation of a single causal stream rather than a counterpart relation across worlds? Second, if what is seen is that the self is only a conventional designation, what is supposed to be doing the seeing, and does the film's turn toward kindness quietly reinstate a robust self?

Please keep separate what specialists treat as settled — what the no-self doctrine denies, what reductionism says about fission — from what is genuinely contested: whether anatta is metaphysics or therapy, reductionist or eliminativist, and how far the resemblance to Parfit actually extends. Mark where you are going beyond what the film can carry.
