My second novel is about a woman named Ines who's a reconstruction — a scanned mind running on hardware that has nothing biological in it — and about the engineer who maintains her and the neuroscientist who thinks the engineer is kidding himself. The setup I keep coming back to is that old Hofstadter thing, a careenium full of little magnetic symms careening around and clumping into symmballs that seem to stand for stuff outside, and the question of who shoves whom around in there; but my cases are my own and my writing group says the two characters just talk past each other for forty pages, which may be realistic and is definitly not dramatic, so before I rewrite chapter 11 I want the actual spec.

So: give me the desiderata — what a theory of whether and how the symbol level pushes the substrate around would have to deliver to count as having answered the question, with the case or consideration behind each requirement, and a note on which are non-negotiable and which are just what one camp happens to want. The situations from the book it has to handle: the scene where the engineers apply a relabelling cipher to the mapping between physical patterns and symbols so that every symmball now means something different while her behaviour doesn't change at all (does she still believe what she believed, and is the symbol level still doing work); the scene where two reconstructions are timesharing one substrate, with individual symms doing double duty, so that a single physical event is one symbol-event in Ines and a different symbol-event in the other person; the intervention asymmetry the engineer relies on, where editing her "risk-aversion" at the code level predicts her behaviour, a micro-level tweak that leaves the symbol pattern intact changes nothing downstream, and one that breaks the pattern changes everything; the prosthesis subplot, where chunks of substrate are swapped for physically different but functionally equivalent material until none of the original is left; the counterfactual the lawyer asks about at the inquest, that if she had believed the bridge was safe she would have driven onto it, said about a moment whose microphysics was already fixed, so what on earth makes it true; the blinking wall in the lab that an enthusiast insists is a mind, since apparently anything implements anything under some mapping and the theory needs somewhere to say no; the stretch where Ines's symbol for herself degrades while she keeps functioning for a while, which is a level whose content is about the very system that contains it; and the line she says about keeping the promise because she promised, which the neuroscientist answers with "the symms shoved the symms." It also has to stay consistent with the micro-level causal story being complete — no new forces, nothing that would embarass a physicist — and it has to respect some distinctions rather than collapse them: being causally efficacious versus merely being the best description available, versus being real, versus being indispensable for explanation and intervention, and it shouldn't quietly turn "the symbol level pushes" into "the symbol level predicts."

The part I want most is where the list fights itself: if some subset of the requirements can't be jointly satisfied — non-triviality about which mappings count, plus closure of the micro story, plus the symbol level doing something over and above the micro level — say so plainly, because if the desiderata themselves force a tradeoff then that's the real shape of the argument my characters are having and I can let one of them be wrong in an interesting way instead of both being foggy. Tell me which single requirement knocks out the most candidate answers, and then do a short scorecard, a line or two each, on the obvious contenders: the symbol level is epiphenomenal, a token-identity or reductionist story, an interventionist or autonomy-based account, a real-patterns or stance-based one, a mechanistic account of computation, and straight emergent downward causation. No reading lists or citations please — I draft on the train with no signal and I can't chase anything down anyway.
