I want to be clear about how exposed my own position is. I hold a restricted answer to the special composition question, which I'll call the self-maintenance view: some things compose something just in case their joint activity realises a single self-sustaining causal regime — an organisation that keeps itself going by dissipating energy. Organisms are the paradigm case; I knowingly accept that candle flames, whirlpools and hurricanes also qualify; and I deny that artefacts, mere lumps, and arbitrary fusions such as my left shoe together with the Eiffel Tower compose anything. I favour this over the two clean answers — nihilism and universalism — because it is meant to escape the arbitrariness argument: self-maintenance looks like a genuinely unity-making, naturally joint-carving relation, so the cut it makes is not merely stipulated, and I hold that composition itself is determinate, the vagueness lying in our terms rather than in the world.

What would count against it? I would like the defeaters ranked from fatal to merely awkward, and split into those a defender should concede and those they could reasonably dismiss. Is the criterion's threshold — how much self-maintenance, sustained over how long a stretch of time — genuinely less arbitrary than the thresholds it was meant to replace, or does it merely relocate the arbitrariness one level down? Does the temporally extended character of self-maintenance force composition to hold by degrees, or only over intervals, and if so does the view collapse into a form of four-dimensionalism, or into universalism in disguise once we notice that any arbitrary arrangement instantiates some pattern or other? How much damage is done by the standard pressure cases: the instant of an organism's death, a borderline dissipative system, the many near-candidates surrounding an obvious organism? And is the appeal to non-equilibrium thermodynamics doing real work, or is "self-maintaining organisation" an honourific we attach to whichever cases we antecedently wanted to count? Finally, has the view been qualified into something unfalsifiable — is there any case I would accept as refuting it — and is that a defect in a view of this kind? Please give a committed assessment rather than a menu: name the single defeater you find most serious, say what answering it would cost me, and say whether any cheaper revision preserves what motivated the view in the first place.
