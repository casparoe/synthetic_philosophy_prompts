ok so I think I finally get Bertrand's paradox and I want to sanity check my reading of it. The way it was explained to me: the three answers — 1/3, 1/2, 1/4 — each come from their own physical setup (spin a pointer, toss a straw, pick two points on the rim), so "random chord" was just underspecified, and once you say which mechanism is actually generating the chord there's a unique correct answer. So on my reading the principle of indifference is fine, you just have to apply it to the variable your mechanism really makes uniform, which is an empirical question rather than a philosophical one.

No chords, no circle, no mechanism, and the same split shows up anyway.
