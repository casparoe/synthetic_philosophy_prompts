My claim is that by 2125 at least a third of them will no longer be live research questions, where I'm counting a question as retired if either (a) some single answer has stable acceptance above about 70 percent among people working in the area, or (b) the question has mostly been dissolved — set aside as ill-posed, or split into sub-questions none of which is the original one — rather than answered. Give me your own number, but mostly I want the decomposition. Give a rough probability to each, say how you're combining them into the headline number, and tell me which single term the number is most sensitive to and what evidence or argument would move it most.

I suspect it's the secession-credit thing, since he works in an area that used to be philosophy, but I'd rather you tell me where the wedge is than confirm my guess.
