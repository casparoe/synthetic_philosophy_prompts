"Religion is a parasite of the mind, not a discovery. Add our talent for imagining what other minds are thinking and you get gods, ancestors, spirits — ideas nobody designed and nobody really needs. Once such ideas are loose, cultural evolution takes over. Concepts compete in a marketplace the way genes do, and what wins is what is memorable: the more counterintuitive a concept is, the stickier it is, which is why a god who is a bush that talks beats a god who is simply a very large man, and a god born of a virgin beats both.
