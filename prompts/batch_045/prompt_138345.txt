I've got 20 minutes next month at the consciousness group in the neuro department to argue that global workspace theory is a theory of access consciousness and doesn't have the resources to be a theory of phenomenal consciousness, and that the overflow argument survives the sparseness reply once you seperate phenomenal richness from phenomenal determinacy. My main move is the bottleneck argument: if the workspace is a limited-capacity broadcaster, what gets broadcast is a lossy compressed summary, so GWT either has to say experience is only as rich as the broadcast or admit that most of the phenomenal field is never broadcast at all.
