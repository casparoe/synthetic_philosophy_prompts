so I fell down a rabbit hole about falling birthrates a few months ago and now I have a pile of half-formed views about population and I genuinely cant tell which ones are fighting each other. I have no philosophy background at all, I mostly think about this at the gym, so please don't assume I know the jargon and don't just hand me a reading list.

Roughly where I've landed:

1. Whether to have a kid is nobody elses buisness. I don't think there's a duty to bring happy people into existence, and I don't think a couple who stays childless has thereby made the world worse in any way.

2. But it obviously IS wrong to have a child if you know that kid is going to have a miserable life.

3. What matters is the total: add up everyone's wellbeing and go with the bigger number. The average version seems clearly wrong to me, because it would say a world with one extremely happy person is the best possible world.

4. I think the long run future is a way bigger deal than anything happening today. The number of people who could exist over the next few thousand years is so much larger than the number alive now that keeping humanity going, and especially not going extinct, completely dwarfs ordinary charity or politics. This one feels almost obvious to me.

5. I also think that if a theory says a trillion people each with a life barely worth living is better than a billion people with amazing lives, that theory is broken. I've seen this called the repugnant conclusion and I think it's a real reductio, not something to bite the bullet on.

6. I don't like the state nudging people to reproduce. Baby bonuses and that whole thing feel insulting and vaguely creepy. But I do think it would be genuinely bad if basically nobody had kids anymore and humanity just petered out.

7. I have this instinct that something can't be better unless it's better for somebody. Better but better for no one sounds like a category mistake to me.

8. Case verdict, in case it helps: I'd rather spend money curing an existing kid's chronic pain than spend it on something that results in one extra healthy happy kid being born.

So: which of these pairs are actually in tension and which only look like it? Please rank the real ones by how bad they are, tell me for each what specifically would have to give, and tell me what the cheapest single revision is and what it costs me. I'm not attached to any particular one and I'd rather know the ugly answer than be told it all fits together nicely.
