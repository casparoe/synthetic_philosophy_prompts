Not a philosopher, I just write infrastructure code and read this stuff on the bus. I keep running into the same shape of argument and I dont have a word for it.

On my last team we ran a shard router. The sentence "the router thinks shard 3 is healthy" was easily the best predictor of what that thing would do next — way better than any memory dump I could produce. But there was no flag anywhere in the machine storing it; it fell out of a gossip timeout table and a couple of thresholds. And in the postmortems, that sentence never shows up: the story of why it did what it did is all dropped packets, retries and stale timestamps.

Now when I read about whether people (or models) really have beliefs, I see the same two moves. Side A: the belief-talk earns its keep, it predicts things nothing else predicts, so the state is real. Side B: nothing in the mechanism plays the role, so once we have the real story it'll go the way of phlogiston or witches. Both sides agree on every fact about the machine and still come out opposite. Same thing happens with "it wants to hold the lease".

The bit I can't place is that the belief level was also the only level I could act on. I could hand an operator "it thinks shard 3 is healthy" and they'd fix the thing; nobody ever fixed anything by reading timeout tables at them. So it's not just predictive for me, its where my interventions land. That feels like more than a handy way of talking, but less than "there's a real state in there", and I can't tell which way it cuts, or whether the intervention bit even changes the argument.

Is there a name for this configuration — a vocabulary that predicts and that you can intervene through, but that gets no slot in the causal story, with the disagreement being about what follows from that? I'm not asking "do beliefs exist", I want the specific shape. If something established covers only part of it, tell me what's nearby, how each of those differs from what I described, and which fits best; if there's nothing, coin a name with boundary cases so I can tell whether I found something or just got into a habit.
