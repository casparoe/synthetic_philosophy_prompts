I'm working through the poverty of the stimulus literature and I've lost track of what the argument is even supposed to establish, because innateness seems to change meaning whenever it comes under pressure. So before I defend or attack any position, I want the specification.

Write the desiderata a notion of innateness must satisfy to be adequate for one job only: making a poverty-of-the-stimulus argument sound. I do not want a general theory of innateness — I want the minimal notion that has to hold if the claim that the input underdetermines the output, so part of the structure must come from the organism, is to amount to more than a shrug.

For each requirement, give the case or consideration behind it, and mark it as non-negotiable or as a preference of one camp. Please also separate the distinctions people routinely collapse: I can find at least four things meant by innate (species-typical, present at birth, not learned, genetically channeled), and I want whatever else belongs on that list, including whatever keeps innate-but-experience-dependent traits from being misfiled.

Three things I especially want your judgment on.

First: must the innate specification be stated independently of the outcome it is meant to explain? Poverty arguments always seem to be reconstructed once we already know the target grammar, so the gap is whatever is left over; but if I demand the constraint be fixed in advance, I seem to demand that linguists guess right the first time. Genuine desideratum, or a prejudice of the anti-nativist camp?

Second: is poverty a property of the input at all, or only of the input relative to a specified class of learners? As I see it, the gap claim (input underdetermines output) and the source claim (the missing information comes from the organism rather than from the statistics of the signal, from non-linguistic cognition, or from the learner's own inductive biases) are separable, and relativizing poverty to a learner class is what stops the argument from being vacuous — but then the conclusion is hostage to whatever learner we happened to specify, and every advance in statistical learning reopens it. Are non-vacuity and architectural neutrality jointly unsatisfiable? If they are, that is the result I most want.

Third: which single requirement excludes the most live views? Keep any scoring of the usual positions — nativist, statistical or emergentist, developmental-systems — to a few lines; the criteria are the deliverable.
