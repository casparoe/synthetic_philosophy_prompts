What I want from you: the order the pieces have to go in and what depends on what (do I name a view before or after I've got them voicing the intuition it's built to capture; does framing the whole thing as a disagreement about what sort of thing freedom is a property of — of your option set, of your own will, of your relation to other people — come first or last, and can I say it in words that don't sound like jargon), the one case to carry all three, and the misconceptions with the exact point in the sequence to defuse each.
