ok I have to write up a short position paper for a grad seminar on Schopenhauer and music and id rather find the holes myself than have someone else find them in front of the room. here's roughly my profile:

1. the music claim in the main work should be read straight: unlike the other arts, music isn't a copy of the Platonic Ideas at all, it bypasses the Ideas and copies the will itself.
2. that only makes sense if you've already swallowed the body bit -- that the will is what each of us knows from the inside as our own willing -- so the aesthetics can't be detached from the metaphysics without music collapsing into a bare formalism.
3. music therefore doesn't represent emotions. it presents the movements of will directly, and all the emotion-talk listeners produce is analogy bolted on afterwards.
4. music is nonetheless the highest art, precisely because it's the one art that can't be translated into or replaced by anything conceptual.
5. i think the thing that looks like a counterexample to (3) is actually support for it: listeners from one tradition picking out the same basic affects (grief, joy, fear) in tunes from a tradition they've never heard shows the recognition isn't mediated by convention or learning, which is what a direct-copy story predicts.
6. i also want the account to cover music nobody made: a piece generated by a program, or a sound structure no one ever hears. these are still copies of the will on my reading, because the will isn't the composer's psychology and doesn't need an audience.
7. and i hold the general aesthetic claim too: aesthetic experience is a temporary suspension of willing, the subject goes will-less for a while, which is why art consoles at all.

someone in the seminar said (7) and (3) can't both be true, and also said (1) has a problem because music is irreducibly rhythmic and temporal while the will is supposed to be outside time entirely. i cant tell which of those to actually worry about. go through the list and tell me which pairs really clash and how badly, which ones only look like they clash, what the cheapest fix is for each genuine one, and which single revision would clean up the most. please dont just tell me to drop the metaphysics, i want to keep as much of it as possible.
