So the question i want them to have to answer is what happens when that chain doesn't have a bottom. The founding engineers' civilization, language and records are long gone. The machines now in existence reliably track features of their environment, correct each other, and pass signals to one another — and some of their internal states covary with things nobody ever designed them to covary with. Variant A is just that: a lineage with a lost origin. Variant B is a closed loop of such machines, each one's signals getting whatever content they have from being interpreted by the next machine around the cycle, with no interpreter standing outside the loop at all.
