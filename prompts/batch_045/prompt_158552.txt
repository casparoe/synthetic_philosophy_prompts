"Moral sentences don't describe anything, they just vent attitudes — 'stealing is wrong' is basically 'boo to stealing!', which is why moral claims can't be true or false and why two people can disagree about one forever without either of them being mistaken. The view was dead by the 1990s: everyone who thought hard about it became a realist, and the only people still working on it are a few specialists who like hard problems."
