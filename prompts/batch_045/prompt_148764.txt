I've got a 45-minute works-in-progress slot next week defending a thesis chapter, and I know exactly who's going to be in the room. The chapter argues that Dennett's four kinds of minds get misread as a ladder of increasing individual smarts going up from Darwinian to Skinnerian to Popperian to Gregorian creatures, when really the rungs are about *where the search for good moves gets done*: on my reading the Gregorian tier isn't an ascent but an outsourcing, because the design work migrates out of the animal and into the cultural surround, so an individual Gregorian creature is running less generate-and-test than a Popperian one, not more. The corollary I'm actually committed to is that current LLMs are Gregorian creatures that never passed through the Skinnerian or Popperian tier at all -- they inherit the products of everyone else's searching without ever doing any themselves, I've been calling this hypothesis-free Gregorian -- and that's meant to explain why their competence is real but brittle in a way the tower doesn't predict.
