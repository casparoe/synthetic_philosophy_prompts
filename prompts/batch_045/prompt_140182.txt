hey — I'm in a reading group on consciousness and the timing of experience, and one of the grad students keeps ending every discussion with the same move. When the colour phi thing comes up (red dot at one location, green dot at another, the right interval apart, and you see a single dot travel between them and change colour about halfway along its path — i.e. at a time before the green flash has even been presented) he says: it's just like the cinema. the world hands you discrete stills, the brain runs them past at twenty-four frames a second, and the motion and the colour change are manufactured by the projector. So the change you seem to see at the midpoint was never seen at the midpoint. Hence there's no fact of the matter about when you experienced it, the Stalinesque story is the true one, and asking whether the revision was Orwellian or Stalinesque is asking an empty question. He says this is basically just Dennett.

I think its wrong but I cant say where, and one thing nags at me: film is shot before it's edited, which sounds like the Orwellian picture if anything, so I don't see how he gets from "like a movie" to "Stalinesque."
