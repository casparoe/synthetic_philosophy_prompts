My brother had brain surgery about eight months ago for a pain condition he'd had for years — they cut a tiny bit of tissue somewhere in the cingulate I think, the whole point being to make the pain stop hurting. And it sort of worked, but in a really strange way.

He says the pain is still completely there. He can tell you exactly where it is, how strong it is, whether it's stabbing or burning or aching, and he still yanks his hand off a hot pan before he's thought about it. But he says it doesn't bother him at all any more. When the surgeon presses on the spot that used to make him scream, he describes the sensation cheerfully and then laughs. He's off all his opioids and he says he's never been happier in his life.

Three problems, and I can't tell which of them is the real one.

His physio wants to start him on an aggressive rehab program and is worried that if the pain doesn't "mean" anything to him, he'll push through warning signs and do permanent damage before anyone notices. My brother says that's confused: he gets all the information the pain was ever giving him, he just doesn't mind it.

My mum says the whole thing is unnatural, that he isn't really himself, and that even if he's happier something has been taken away from him that matters more than how he happens to feel. I half think she's just squeamish, but I can't shake the sense that she's pointing at something.

And then there's the bit I keep going round in circles on: if he is in exactly as much pain as he was before, for the rest of his life, and the only thing that changed is that it doesn't hurt him — did the surgery actually help him, or did it just change him into someone who's fine with being in pain? And is there even a difference between "the pain no longer hurts" and "the pain hurts just as much, he just doesn't mind it"? He can't explain the difference and neither can I, and I don't know if that means there's no difference or if it means I'm asking a bad question.

What should I actually say to him, and is my mum onto something or is she just being sentimental?
