- realistic and one-shot — no perfect predictor, just a shrewd person reading a stranger in conversation; and no contract, no deposit, and no reputation that can follow the hitchhiker (they're leaving the country the same day), so the only channel from disposition to benefit is that face-to-face read.

It's got to be something else: a standing policy, a rule, a habit of practical reasoning, whatever you think actually survives their own judgement that paying is now pointless.
