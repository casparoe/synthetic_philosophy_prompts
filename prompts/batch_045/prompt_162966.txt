My friend Dana and I got into a massive argument last night and I need someone to settle it. I run a tiny design studio, four of us, and over the last two years every other studio in town has started answering client texts at 11pm and promising first drafts inside 24 hours. Nobody wanted this. Clients didn't even ask for it at first. It just spread, and now its expected, we all do it, the workday is longer, and as far as I can tell nobody has actually won a single extra client — the only real change is that drafts show up faster and nobody seems to read them any faster.

Dana says we should just stop: put our hours on the website, tell clients we reply next business day, and eat whatever it costs us. I said that's just volunteering to lose — we'd be the only studio doing it, we'd go under, everyone else carries on, and nothing changes except which of us pays. She said that exact logic is what keeps the whole thing running and that someone has to be first.

Two things made it messier and we never resolved either. First, most of the late-night work is done by our two junior designers, who don't own any of the studio, so the people paying the price aren't the people keeping the client. Second, Dana wants to get three studios to agree to it at the same time, on the theory that clients mostly just compare whoever's fastest and if a few of us drop out together the expectation resets. I think the studios that don't sign just clean up.

So who's right, and what should we actually do? I definately don't want generic 'set boundaries' advice. What I want is: is Dana's 'someone has to be first' genuinely confused or just uncomfortable, and is this more like a thing where everyone has to move at once or more like a thing where moving alone is just a gift to everybody else? And is it fair to say no one is really at fault here, or is that a cop-out?

Give me your real advice in prose, then a table with these columns: Option (one row each for: we do nothing, we opt out alone, the three-studio pact, we compete on something other than speed), Short-run cost to us, Effect on the wider pattern everyone else is stuck in, Does one studio moving alone change anything (yes or no, plus why in a few words), and Verdict in one sentence. American English please.
