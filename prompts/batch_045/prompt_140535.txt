I had a long argument with a philosopher friend last night about reduction and emergence, and I left unsure whether we were using the same words for different things, or whether she was pressing a question my own field has already answered in a form I don't recognize as an answer.

I am a condensed-matter theorist with no formal philosophy training. The vocabulary I actually work in is: coarse-graining and integrating out degrees of freedom, effective field theories and their ranges of validity, renormalization-group flow and the relevance or irrelevance of operators, universality classes, quasiparticles, singular limits such as the thermodynamic limit, and consistency conditions that run from the low-energy theory back up to the micro-theory (anomaly cancellation, and more speculatively the thought that only some low-energy theories admit a UV completion at all).

Three claims kept recurring in her side of the argument, and I want each of them restated in that vocabulary as faithfully as you can manage: (1) reduction as the derivation of the special science's laws from the fundamental ones with the help of bridge identities; (2) the multiple-realizability argument that this generally cannot be done, because a single macro-property corresponds to a heterogeneous disjunction of micro-realizers; (3) the distinction between emergence that is merely hard or impossible for us to derive and emergence that is genuinely novel, carrying causal powers of its own. Where you have to choose an interpretation of what she meant, say which you chose.

Then give me the residue report. Which of the three translates cleanly into how I already think; which translates only after being bent, and where exactly the bending happens; and which has no counterpart in my vocabulary at all. I would also like you to place one further thing that never appeared in her taxonomy: the top-down consistency constraints I listed. Are they a species of downward causation, a fourth position the philosophers have no slot for, or an artifact of how physicists misuse the word emergence?

Close with your verdict on the residue: is what fails to translate a blind spot in my framework, or a sign that the philosophical claims were less clear than they sounded when she said them?
