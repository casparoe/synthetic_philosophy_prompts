so I'm writing this op-ed for our school paper and I need help thinking it through before I put my foot in my mouth. Our district started this program (I'll call it ThriveCheck bc I dont want to name the real one) where every morning on our school laptops we do a check-in: pick a mood face, write a sentence or two about how things are going, and some software scans it and flags kids whose language looks worrying over a couple of weeks. If you get flagged you get pulled out of study hall to talk to a counselor, and if you miss three of those a note goes in your file and your parents get an email. They also trained a bunch of students as "wellness ambassadors" to spot warning signs in their friends and quietly report them to the counselor, and because there aren't enuf counselors there's now a chatbot you're supposed to be able to talk to at night. And the messaging around it is very much your brain is sick not you, chemical imbalance, it's just like diabetes. My mom is a counselor at a different school and when I complained about it at dinner she got really defensive and said this stuff catches kids who would otherwise never tell anyone, and honestly she might be right, which is why I'm stuck.
