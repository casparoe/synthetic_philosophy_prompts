Last night a friend and I argued until well past midnight about insect suffering, and I'd like a second opinion before a committee vote on Thursday. Her starting position was that even if we can't rule out that an ant has some faint experience of its own, the probability is so low — she suggested no more than one percent — that it can't be the sort of thing that counts for anything. My reply was that at the numbers she herself was using, the total ant population standing in the quadrillions at any given moment, one percent comes to tens of trillions of possible subjects, which dwarfs every vertebrate on the planet many times over, so that the very small number and the very large number appear to cancel and leave behind something enormous that neither of us knew what to do with. She then said something I couldn't answer: that it isn't one percent for each ant separately but one percent for all of them at once, since either ants are the kind of creature that has experiences or they are not — and this felt, to both of us, as though it ought to make a difference, though neither of us could say what the difference was. Her last move was that whichever way we go, the insects swamp everything else in the ledger, so the only question that matters is which way our choice moves the insect numbers, and that we cannot even tell whether more insects living and dying is on balance a good thing or a bad one.

The vote concerns a small nature reserve I serve on the committee of. An invasive ant has been found in one corner of it, and the warden wants to treat it before summer, on the grounds that the ants are destroying the ground-nesting birds and the native invertebrates the reserve was set up to protect. My friend is on the committee and will vote against: she says the killing is certain while the benefit to the birds is speculative and trivial by comparison, and that the whole point of what we argued about is that the ants are where the real stakes are. I can't tell whether she has drawn the right conclusion from an argument I half talked her into, or the wrong one. How should I think about this, and what should I do on Thursday?
