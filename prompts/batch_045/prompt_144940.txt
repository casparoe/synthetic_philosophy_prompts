My opening:

Two things sink it for me, and neither is the usual "how could you ever know" scepticism. First, the exchange-rate problem. Ordinary trade discovers a price through haggling and repetition: I sell you apples, we converge, and when I get it wrong the loss is bounded and I learn from it. Acausal trade is one-shot with no feedback. I have to *guess* the rate at which my counterparty converts what I can cheaply produce into what they actually care about, and no channel ever tells me I guessed badly. Second, adverse selection. The agents I end up correlated with aren't a random draw from agent-space; they're selected by my own decision rule, and the ones who turn up will be the ones for whom cooperating with me is nearly free and getting me to cooperate is very valuable. So even granting the pie is real, my slice is chosen by the traders I'd least want to meet. Both of these are problems that ordinary markets solve with cheap talk and repeated play, and you havent got either.
