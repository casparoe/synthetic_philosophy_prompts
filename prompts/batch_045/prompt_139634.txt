The core, so you have it: worth doesn't require that the agent cite the right-making feature, only that her actual motivating reason be answerable to it — roughly, that she'd stop if she found out the feature wasn't there. I use two things as my main weapons. First, what I call the scarcity result: most decent action runs on inherited rules, trained habits and reasons borrowed from people we trust, so if worth required citing the feature, almost nothing would have worth, which I take as a reductio of the strict view. Second, the flip side: there are people who do the right thing from a genuinely warm motive but who'd keep doing it even if the good were entirely absent, and those look to me like cases where worth leaks out.
