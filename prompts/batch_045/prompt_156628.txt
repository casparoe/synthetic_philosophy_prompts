Hey — I want to practice attacking lottocracy, so you be the lottocrat. Committed, not a strawman: you think the main legislative chamber of a big country should be chosen by lot instead of elected, you've actually thought through the boring plumbing, you give up small points fast but you never give up the core. Stay in character until I type 'stop'. Two short paragraphs a turn, argue like a person in a disagreement rather than like a survey article, and argue from how the machinery would actually work — don't tell me what books or papers to go read, I want the arguments and not the names.

The version on the table is specific: one chamber, 300 members, drawn by stratified lot from the whole adult population, staggered three-year terms, paid enough that a warehouse worker can serve, permanent professional staff, real power to pass ordinary statutes, elected executive and courts left alone. Anyone drawn can decline and gets replaced by another draw. And here's the rule I most want to fight about: floor time is rationed — every member gets the same small allotment of minutes per month, and when more people want to speak than there's room for, the slots go by lottery, so nobody can turn into the chamber's star orator.

My opening: that speaking rule is your whole theory in miniature, and its a mess. You've built a legislature that deliberately handicaps its most capable members in order to stop confident people from running the room, and that only makes sense if you think the chamber's job is to resemble the country rather than to get things right. Same problem with the stratification. You sell the lottery as neutral — nobody is picked for who they are — and then you pick people definately because of their race, income and zip code, so the only reason anyone is in the room is that they instantiate a demographic category. Elections at least give a member a reason to be there, and give the rest of us a reason to throw them out. So: what exactly does all that amateurism buy you, and why should I expect a body selected as a bundle of tokens and then flattened into equal silence to govern better than the elected mess we already have?
