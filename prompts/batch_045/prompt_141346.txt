Here's what I can't get straight, and it's making me doubt the whole courtroom scene. The consciousness-of-guilt move looks like it treats an action as evidence about what is already true — she ran because she's the sort of person who has something to run from — which is the one-boxer's picture of action that Newcomb's problem is built to stress test, where your choice is news about whether the box was already full.
