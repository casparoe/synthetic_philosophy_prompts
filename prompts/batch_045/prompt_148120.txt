I am a final-year PhD student in philosophy, and next week I am defending a paper at a joint philosophy–psychology seminar. The thesis is that the familiar "just-so story" objection to evolutionary psychology is misdiagnosed: it is not, or not primarily, a complaint that ev psych hypotheses are unfalsifiable, but a complaint about the unconstrainedness of the hypothesis space. For any observed trait one can always tell an adaptive-function story, a by-product/spandrel story, an exaptation story, a maladaptive-lag story, or a cultural-transmission story, and the standard practice of testing a hypothesis against the very behavioural data that prompted it does not discriminate among these. My proposed remedy is a requirement of "task specification": before data are collected, a hypothesis must state (i) the ancestral task structure, (ii) the computational problem the mechanism is claimed to solve, and (iii) a quantitative performance criterion against which candidate mechanisms can be scored — the hypothesis then being evaluated against that criterion rather than against whether the predicted behaviour merely shows up. I concede up front that this rescues only a proper subset of the field, roughly the reverse-engineering wing, and leaves the rest as conjecture.

The room: two philosophers of science who cut their teeth on the Gould–Lewontin debate and treat adaptationism as partly ideological; one formal epistemologist who will want the hypothesis space written down as a probabilistic model; two practising evolutionary psychologists who defend their work by pointing to novel predictions generated from task analysis; and three mainstream social psychologists who regard the field as unfalsifiable and politically damaging, and who will press the naturalistic-fallacy and media-misuse worries.

Please draft my Q&A prep sheet: the eight or so objections I will actually get, ordered by how likely and how damaging, phrased as each group would phrase them, each with a two-minute reply, a note on where that reply is weakest, and a clear indication of what I should concede and what I must hold. Include the question I am hoping nobody asks. Please write the sheet in British English.
