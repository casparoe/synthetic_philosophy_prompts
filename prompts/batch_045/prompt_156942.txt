I recently joined the ethics advisory board of a privately funded project to establish a permanent settlement on Mars, and the question has stopped being academic for me: my partner and I are in the candidate pool for the second crew, and we are trying to decide whether we would take a child with us. I have therefore had to write down my own commitments rather than other people's, and I would like to know whether they hold together.

One piece of background: the project's engineers are clear that the settlement counts as insurance against extinction only if it could survive the permanent loss of resupply from Earth, and that this requires a population far larger than any rotation of adult volunteers could supply.

Here is where I have landed:

(1) Adults may volunteer for risks that are large but honestly disclosed; a state or employer that refuses to let them go is being paternalistic.

(2) It is impermissible to bring children into, or to permit births in, a habitat that is sealed and cannot be expanded for decades, and where the long-term effects of partial gravity and closed-loop life support on human development are not merely risky but unquantified. Children cannot consent, and a childhood there is not something they can later opt out of.

(3) We have a duty to make humanity multiplanetary, as insurance against extinction.

(4) If there is indigenous Martian microbial life, destroying or irreversibly contaminating it is not something the settlement may weigh against its own interests; it is a constraint, not a preference.

(5) The settlers govern themselves. Once they are there, Earth has no authority to legislate for them.

(6) The compromise on (2) that the board keeps returning to — permit births, but guarantee every child born off Earth full citizenship and a funded passage to Earth at adulthood — does meet the objection, because what matters is that the child has a real way out.

Which of these are genuinely in tension, and how badly? Which only look as though they are? I have deliberately not decided which one I would surrender, because I cannot tell which is doing the damage. For each real tension, tell me what would have to give and what it would cost me, and then tell me which single revision would clean up the most.
