Here are the three framings I came up with, because I genuinely want to know which of them are confused and how you'd repair or kill them. (1) The competence-versus-compliance confusion: every scalable-oversight scheme I can think of — debate, amplification-style recursive setups, reward modeling off human or model judgments — treats the overseer's verdict as signal, but a model can fail to produce the right answer either because it doesn't know it or because it won't show it, and those get confounded in the update; so the question is what kind of evidence could ever separate them, and whether the honest answer is that you can't separate them and can only bound the risk.
