Our interpretability reading group keeps a running ledger of calls on whether a modified model still implements the same mechanism as the original, and one senior member has made all of them so far. Her verdicts: a model retrained with a different seed, where the circuit reappears at the same depth with entirely different weights, is the same mechanism; a model of identical architecture trained under a different objective, which matches the original's input-output behavior on distribution but realizes it through a different set of components with no counterpart to the original heads, is a different mechanism; a lightly fine-tuned model in which the original heads are intact but partly overridden by a downstream path, such that ablating the new path restores the original behavior exactly, is the same mechanism merely masked; a distilled model half the size, in which behavior matches and the key intermediate quantity is recoverable by a linear probe but no component plays the role the old head played, is not the same mechanism; and in a grokking run, the circuit that appears suddenly and replaces a memorization-based route to the same outputs counts as a new mechanism rather than the old one revealed.

The case on her desk now: after pruning and continued training, each of the original circuit's two heads has been replaced by a pair of heads at a different depth, where ablating either member alone has no effect but ablating both reproduces the original ablation signature exactly, and the model has additionally grown a backup path, absent before, that switches on only when the primary route is ablated and recovers most of the behavior. On-distribution outputs are unchanged, but off distribution the model now extends the pattern to a token class it previously ignored, and its failure modes under shift differ in kind.

Predict her verdict, and say which rule she is implicitly running: sameness of components, sameness of causal role in the network's machinery, sameness of the full counterfactual profile under interventions, or sameness at the level of the algorithm rather than its realization. Identify which of her precedents actually discriminates between the rules that fit her track record, and specify the one further case that would best separate the leading two.
