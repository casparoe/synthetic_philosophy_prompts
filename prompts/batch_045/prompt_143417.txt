I have been asked to contribute a short position statement to a workshop volume on absences in causal explanation, and before I commit myself in print I would like the set of commitments I intend to defend audited for joint tenability. I hold, first, that some omissions are causes in exactly the sense in which actions are: the lifeguard's failure to throw the ring caused the child's death in the same sense of the verb in which her throwing the brick caused the window to break. Second, I favour a broadly contrastive, difference-making account, on which a causal claim says roughly that had c-star obtained instead of c, e-star would have obtained instead of e, or some near variant of that. Third, I want to insist that the contrast lies in the pragmatics and not in the world: what there is, is a two-place, mind-independent and wholly non-normative relation between happenings, and contrastive talk merely selects which among the many causal facts matters to us. Fourth, I hold that an absence becomes a cause only against a normative background: the lifeguard's failure is a cause and the sunbather's is not, although the counterfactual structure of the two cases is identical, because she was supposed to throw and he was not. Fifth, I take causation to be transitive. Sixth, I deny that there are negative facts or absences in the ontology, since everything that exists is positive and spatiotemporal, and I take this to sit comfortably with the first commitment.

My question is whether this package hangs together. Please go through the pairs that genuinely pull against one another and rank them by severity rather than presenting them as equally serious, and for each say what I would have to give up and what that concession would cost me elsewhere in the set. I have a vague sense that something has to give but not a clear sense of what, and I would rather not be told that everything conflicts with everything: if one or two of the apparent conflicts dissolve on inspection, say which ones and why. Finally, tell me whether there is a single revision that would stabilise the whole package, and if there is not, which two commitments I should expect to lose. Please write in continuous prose, without headings, bullet points, bold text or tables, in the manner of an email to a colleague.
