i dont have any academic background, I just spend way too much time in the same political arguement in a group chat. There's one pattern I keep hitting and I want to know whether there's a proper name for it.

It goes like this. There's a vote — a referendum usually, but sometimes just an election that gets reinterpreted afterwards. Beforehand, both sides are certain the country already made up its mind, but the vote still has to happen because nobody can actually say what the answer is. The day after, the winners talk as though the will was always there: this is what the people have wanted for decades and were never allowed to say. And then anybody who tries to slow it down — courts, the civil service, the upper chamber, protesters, or just people asking whether we could vote again in five years now that we know more — stops being described as someone who disagrees and starts being described as somebody obstructing a decision that has already been made. So: the will exists before the vote, gets revealed by the vote, and is then protected from every later vote.

The part that bugs me is that it isn't symmetrical. When the same crowd loses, the vote is suddenly only a snapshot, or the question was confusing, or the wrong people were on the register. The will gets confirmed but never corrected.

A friend of a friend said "constituent power," which as far as I can tell is about who gets to write a constitution, and someone else said "counter-majoritarian difficulty," which I think is about judges. Both feel nearby but not it.

Is there an established concept for this exact thing — a will that gets dated backwards to before the vote and then frozen against later ones? If there isn't, I'd like a name and a definition. And I need the edges: what would count as a real instance as opposed to just sore winners, and is it always bogus? Because when a country writes a constitution after a revolution, the same "the people have spoken, it's settled" move sounds a lot more respectable, and I can't tell whether that's a difference in kind or just that we like the outcome better. A bare label won't help me, I want the carving.
