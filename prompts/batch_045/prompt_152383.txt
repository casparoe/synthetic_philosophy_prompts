Side N is a naturalist in the biostatistical vein: disease is statistically subnormal functioning of a part or process relative to a reference class, health is species-typical functional ability, and value judgments enter only when we decide what to do about it. Side V is a normativist: "bad for the bearer" is inside the concept, not a extra criterion bolted onto a value-free core.

Assume they've agreed to be honest about which cases are uncomfortable for them. What I want back:

1. A numbered section of claims both can sign — about what the concept is supposed to do, about the easy paradigm cases, about how "function" is to be fixed (and whether selected-effect, causal-role, and "current contribution to survival and reproduction" ever come apart in a way that matters here), and about how the reference class is specified once you let environment in.

2. A disagreements table. Columns: a neutral one-line statement of what's at issue / the formulation Side N would sign / the formulation Side V would sign / real or merely verbal, and why. If a formulation comes out so thin that neither side could deny it, throw it out and write a sharper one.

3. A cruxes table. For each crux: the crux itself, the case or argument both agree would bear on it, Side N's prediction about that case, Side V's prediction, and what each side says would actually change its mind. Please also flag any crux that's a fake crux — one where settling it wouldn't move either party anyway — and say what that reveals about the debate.

Run everything through these four cases (I made them up, sharpen them if they're mushy):

Statistically way out on the tail, no detectable cost, apparently a benefit. I suspect this one separates them less than either expects — say if I'm right and why.
- the anachronistic allergy: an immune system operating exactly to spec, mounting a textbook response to a synthetic protein that's now in most packaged food. Real harm, zero deviation from normal function, and the harm exists only because of what's in the environment now.
