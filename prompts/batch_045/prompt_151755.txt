I'm writing a short position paper on providence and freedom and have ended up calling myself a Molinist. Before I commit to the label I want someone to check whether the package I've assembled is actually stable: it feels coherent to me, and I can't locate the weak point. My commitments are:

(a) Free choice is contra-causal. An agent who freely does X could have done otherwise with the entire prior history and the total circumstances held fixed, and nothing outside the agent causally settles which way it goes.

(b) God has middle knowledge. For every possible creature and every complete circumstance in which it might act, there is a determinate fact about what it would freely do — I read the would at face value and don't think it needs any special semantics — and God knows all of these logically prior to, and not by way of, any decree to create.

(c) Providence is meticulous: every event falls under the divine plan rather than being merely permitted after the fact. But God can only weakly actualize; he can strongly actualize circumstances, never a creature's free response to them.

(d) Necessarily, a perfect being actualizes the best feasible world. Satisficing is not open to God.

(e) The counterfactuals in (b) are not brute. I want them grounded in the possible creature's own nature or character: S would do X in C because of what S is. My reason is aseity — a truth God must accommodate but did not author looks to me like something outside God constraining him.

(f) God is timeless, so nothing in God is literally earlier or later than anything else in God.

Please give me an audit rather than a survey. Which pairs are genuinely in tension and which only appear to be? Rank the real tensions by severity, and for each say exactly what I would have to give up and what that costs me elsewhere in the set — I'd rather you rank by cost than assume that the commitments most definitional of Molinism are the ones I'll keep. For the merely apparent tensions, say briefly why they dissolve. Then name the single revision that buys the most coherence most cheaply, and flag any commitment that is doing work I have not noticed.
