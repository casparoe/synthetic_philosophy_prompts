I'm writing a piece on whether a spatially infinite universe really implies that there are, somewhere, further copies of each of us — and on whether the finitist's refusal to treat infinity as a completed totality is anything more than fastidiousness. The cosmologist I interviewed reached for the infinite hotel: an endless hotel is, he said, just like an ordinary hotel, except that it can be full and still take more guests, because everyone can be shifted up one room. From this he drew two conclusions: that a completed infinite collection can be manipulated much as a finite one is, so that Cantor's arithmetic of the infinite settles the finitist's scruples; and that an infinite universe therefore has room for infinitely many copies of every local arrangement, this one included.

A philosopher I showed the argument to said the hotel is carrying more than it can bear: that the reshuffle needs the rooms to arrive already indexed in a way that gives every guest a next room, and not merely to be infinitely many; that she would want to re-run it with the rooms numbered by the rationals, which are dense, so that no guest has a next room to move into; and that cardinality is in any case the wrong instrument for how-many-copies questions, since an infinite whole can be placed in one-to-one correspondence with a proper part of itself and an infinite hotel can be decorated entirely in one colour.

Please audit the analogy for me. Give the table of correspondences — rooms, guests, full, the reshuffle, the manager — and say which of them each of the two conclusions actually depends on; where the mapping breaks; and what the picture quietly imports about completed totalities and about our being able to survey or act on one as a whole. Tell me whether the rationals-numbered variant genuinely blocks the manoeuvre or whether some other reassignment recovers it, and whether either conclusion survives once the disanalogies are on the table. If a better analogy is available — for actual infinities in general, or for the copies question in particular — say what it is and why it does the work better. I would be grateful for the answer in British English.
