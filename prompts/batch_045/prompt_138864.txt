in my reading group someone shuts down every discussion of infinite ethics with Hilbert's Hotel: the universe already contains infinitely many happy people, so making one more person happy, or sparing one more person from misery, is like turning up at a hotel with infinitely many rooms that's already full — the manager moves everyone up one room, the hotel is exactly as full as it was, nothing has changed. They use this to argue that no infinite outcome is better or worse than any other, so we should stop trying to aggregate and just rank outcomes by some dominance rule over finite patches.

I want to audit that rather then just nod along. Give me the correspondence table first: what plays the part of the rooms, the guests, the manager, the no-vacancy sign, the guest turned away, the actual moving. Then the disanalogies that matter for the work they're making it do. My own candidates: (a) the shift is something an agent performs, and there's no one who can 'move' persons into each other's lives, so the bijection looks like a redescription rather than an event; (b) the hotel tracks how many while the ethical question is how much, so cardinality may be the wrong yardstick rather than a proof that nothing improved; (c) nothing in the hotel corresponds to a welfare level, so it can't even represent the case where the extra person is badly off. Tell me which of these actually bite and what I'm missing. Then: does the give-up-on-aggregation conclusion survive, and does the argument overreach — would it also say that adding infinitely many blissful people, or torturing everyone, changes nothing?

Last bit, the part I care most about: the replacement picture I've been kicking around is the night sky — infinitely many stars of finite brightness, where the naive infinite total is useless but the fix isn't 'adding a star changes nothing', it's switching to brightness per patch of sky, a density or long-run average instead of a sum. Does that picutre do the job the hotel was doing, or does it just relocate the problem into the choice of expansion and ordering?
