- Substrate-first (C): a system cares only if its activity is the activity of a stuff with intrinsic concern. Content is a bonus, not a requirement. What the symbols do matters just insofar as it changes what the substrate goes on to care about.

What I need is a case where S and C come apart in the direction I can never get to work.
