my ethics reading group has a neo-Aristotelian virtue ethicist and a situationist - the Harman/Doris style one who thinks the obedience and helping experiments show there just arent robust global character traits - and after five sessions theyre only repeating themselves. Draft the adversarial-collaboration paper they should have written in week two: the claims both can sign, the disagreements worded so each side would accept the phrasing of its own position, the cruxes, and for each crux a case or study both agree bears on it, with each side's prediction written down in advance. Numbered agreed-claims section first, then a disagreements table.

The bit I actually care about is that you dont skip the following:

- the grain problem. How specific is a trait allowed to be? If the virtue ethicist retreats from honest to something like honest-about-her-own-mistakes-with-colleagues-when-nobody-is-checking, does that make her view unfalsifiable, or does it just relocate character into a profile indexed to situation-types - and if the latter, is that still virtue ethics or is it a concession?
- whether the lab results are better explained by the absence of traits or by the fact that those experiments deliberately strip away scaffolding: roles, scripts, being addressed by name, having a reputation on the line. Both sides should commit up front to what a field study that puts the scaffolding back would show, and to what the stability coefficients in a dense experience-sampling study across a few dozen situation-types over six months would have to look like for them to concede.
- whether a trait is a disposition to behave or a disposition to respond to reasons, and whether the second reading escapes the data without escaping the situationists practical point.
- whether any of it cashes out downstream: habituation and character education versus redesigning situations (roles, checklists, choice architecture). If they end up recommending the same interventions for different reasons, say so, and say plainly which part of the dispute was verbal.

Keep the agreed claims non-vacuous - if the only thing they can both sign is that behavior varies with situation, the collaboration has failed.
