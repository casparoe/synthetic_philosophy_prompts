I'm writing the third chapter of my dissertation, and the whole framing leans on reality having *levels*: a base of things that don't metaphysically depend on anything, then everything else built up from those by non-causal "in virtue of" relations (the set exists in virtue of its members, the ball is coloured in virtue of being crimson, the activity is wrong in virtue of causing pain, and so on). I don't want to win by "fundamental just means whatever our best theory quantifies over". I'm at about 0.4, she's at about 0.8.
