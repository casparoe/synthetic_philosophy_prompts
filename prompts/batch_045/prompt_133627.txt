hey, I've been arguing about this with friends for a while and I want to know whether what I believe is actually a position or just a pile of vibes. Context: I'm a backend dev, no philosophy background, and I got diagnosed with ADHD about four years ago and started taking stimulants. Here's roughly where I've landed:

1. The therapy/enhancement distinction is real and does moral work. Restoring something that's broken is a need; making something better than it was is a want. That difference should show up in who pays for it and what we owe each other.
2. But "normal for the species" or "statistically typical" is a bad way to draw the line. Glasses and vaccines and literacy would all count as enhancement on that account, and it just freezes whatever baseline happens to be common right now.
3. Relatedly, I think a lot of what gets called disability is really disadvantage created by how we arranged the world. The school day is built for kids who can sit still for six hours; that's a design decision, not a fact about my kid's brain.
4. On the meds: when I take them so I can get through a normal workday I think of it as treatment. I also take them to get through a crunch-weekend debugging session, and I don't think that's cheating or that the code I write is any less mine. Effort isn't what makes an output worth anything, the artifact is.
5. I still think it's wrong for parents to push cognitive enhancers on their kids, in a way that isn't just about side effects. And I think it should be illegal for an employer to make enhancement a condition of the job even when the employee nominally says yes.
6. I'm against "moral enhancement" — engineering people to be more patient, kinder, more cooperative, me included. Something in me says the value is in having managed it yourself.
7. "It's unnatural" counts for zero with me. If it's safe and it works, the ick factor is not an argument.

I have a feeling some of these can't all be true at once, but I genuinely can't tell which one is doing the damage. Can you audit the set for me: which pairs are actually in tension, ordered by how bad the tension is, and for each one what the cheapest fix would be and what it would cost me. Also please flag anything that only looks inconsistent but isn't — I'd rather not throw away a belief I don't have to. And don't just tell me everything clashes with everything.
