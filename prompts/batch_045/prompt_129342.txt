Write me a joke about approval-directed agents vs goal-directed agents. Set it somewhere concrete — a lab, a company, a government department, whatever — not in abstract alignment-land. And it should be funny to people who actually know the distinction, i.e. the punchline turns on the content of the two ideas rather than being a generic nerd joke.
