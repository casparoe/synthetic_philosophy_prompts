And I want them to actually fight about whether the quasar buisness even matters: the superdeterminist will say that pushing the common cause further back in time doesn't touch his position at all, because he's not positing a causal influence running from the hidden variables to the experimenter's choice, he's positing a global constraint on the space of allowed worlds — and I want the Bohmian to respond to that move specifically instead of just restating Bell's theorem at him.

Keep the tone casual, like two people arguing over coffee, and end it without either of them winning.
