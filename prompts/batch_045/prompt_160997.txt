Write a joke about inverse reinforcement learning that turns on the field’s core assumption: an agent’s observed behavior is treated as approximately optimal with respect to a reward function the learner is trying to infer. Build the joke around this invented case: the human being observed is a preference-inversion troll, someone whose true reward is to make the learner’s inferred reward as wrong as possible, and who therefore keeps acting in ways that are suboptimal under the learner’s latest hypothesis. The punchline should depend on the regress created by the optimality assumption, not merely on the cliché that people are irrational or hard to predict. Keep it a joke with a setup and a punchline, and make sure it would land for someone who actually knows how inverse reinforcement learning works.
