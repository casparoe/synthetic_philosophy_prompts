One of my friends in the seminar said that the answer was obvious — anyone who is affected by a decision is entitled to a say in making it — and our instructor nodded, said this was the all-affected principle and that it is what most people really mean by democratic inclusion, and then used it to conclude that the residents of Duncairn should get a vote in Ardmuir's referendum.
