I'm a software engineer, no philosophy background, reading about evolutionary debunking arguments in metaethics in my spare time, and the literature looks stuck to me. I want a diagnosis of the disagreement, not a verdict on who's right.

My version of the argument: our moral intuitions came out of an optimizer whose objective function was fitness, not moral truth. So even if there are mind-independent moral facts, our believing what we believe is fully explained without them, and we've got no reason to think the beliefs track the facts. The realist replies, as I read them: (a) evolution did track the facts — true moral beliefs were themselves fitness-conferring, or some third factor explains both the facts and the beliefs; (b) the burden of proof is backwards, a belief-forming faculty is innocent until someone produces an actual defeater, we don't owe an independent explanation of its reliability before trusting it; (c) the misaligned-optimizer analogy is bogus, because moral judgment isn't an instrument pointed at a subject matter from outside, it's constitutive of practical reasoning and there's no place to stand and audit it.

Reading this stuff, nobody moves: debunkers say the realists are just helping themselves to a default, realists say the debunkers are smuggling in an unreasonable demand for explanations. My hypothesis is that the stalled part isn't about evolution at all — it's two prior disagreements wearing a lab coat: one about default epistemology, explanation-required versus innocent-until-defeated; and one about which counterfactual is the relevant one for "tracking", since the debunker seems to hold the moral facts fixed and vary the selective pressures while the realist holds the pressures fixed and varies the facts. Is that the right shape of it, or is something else doing the work — a hidden empirical question about how much selection pressure actually shaped particular moral judgments, an ambiguity in "explanation", or bedrock disagreement about whether moral claims could even turn out false?

Give me the diagnosis with reasons, not a survey of positions.
