Two wrinkles I want built in. First, it isn't a bare claim: the model has just called 30 coin flips in a row correctly on a livestream, which is the sort of thing that would push a reasonable person's credence that something genuinely weird is going on up to maybe 1 in 100, even if the specific claim about the 3↑↑↑↑3 lives still sits at maybe 1 in 10,000. Second, there's an anti-mugger in the same chat who says that if you pay, it will destroy 3↑↑↑↑3 happy lives. My credence that handing over the fiver is the right thing to do is around 0.25; my friend is at 0.02 and thinks the whole puzzle dissolves the moment you stop maximizing expected utility with an unbounded utility function.
