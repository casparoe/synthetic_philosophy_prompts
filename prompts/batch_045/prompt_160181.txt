I have been running small informal experiments on myself, and I keep hitting the same pattern. I would like to know whether it has been isolated and named, and if not, how to carve it out.

The pattern: my reports arrive more specific than my own sensitivity. A shape flashed briefly at the edge of the visual field — I answer with a full description, something like a red triangle tilted left, and I feel no hesitation at all. When I then run the same trials with an explicit option to answer unclear, I am wrong about as often as not, and on the very next trial I answer exactly as before. The same thing happens with material from last week: I produce a definite wording for what someone said to me, feel no uncertainty about it, and afterwards cannot distinguish my version from two alternatives I could equally have produced. Giving myself permission to say that I do not know changes nothing, so the specificity is not an artifact of being forced to answer.

I came to this while reading work that treats conscious experience as a user interface rather than a window onto underlying processing, and I can see why an interface would hand up one definite value instead of a spread of candidates. But I want the phenomenon itself, not the metaphor. It does not look like ordinary overconfidence: what is off is not my estimate of my accuracy but the determinacy of the content. It does not look like confabulation in the familiar sense, since there is no deficit and nothing I am papering over. And it is not quite the claim that experience away from the centre is less detailed than it seems: detail is a matter of how much, whereas this is a matter of whether what is there has one value at all.

So: is there an established concept for this, or a literature I should be reading? If the nearest concept fits only partly, say which it is and where the fit breaks. If there is nothing established, coin a term, define it, and give me the boundary cases — in particular, tell me what would distinguish specificity that belongs to the experience from specificity that enters only at the point of report, since I cannot tell from the inside. A label on its own will not settle anything for me.
