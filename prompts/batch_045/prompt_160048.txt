He added that the later self is closer to the reward and so better informed, which makes the reversal the more rational one if anything. When I pointed out that you can know perfectly well you'll reverse and still have no way to commit, he said then it's not weakness of will at all, it's bad luck about which commitment devices are available. It feels too clean but I cant locate the mistake. And even granting hyperbolic discounting, doesn't the agent's own earlier preference to bind itself still condemn the reversal, so that "nothing irrational is left" is just wrong?
