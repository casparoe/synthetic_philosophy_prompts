Give me the desiderata with the case or consideration behind each: the phenomena it has to cover, the distinctions it can't collapse, the findings it can't contradict, the practical measurement requirements. Some specific things I want adjudicated rather than assumed:

- The cerebellum thing (more neurons than cortex, apparently contributes nothing to experience) and the seizure thing (massive synchronized activity, consciousness gone). Are these genuine desiderata or just anecdotes people repeat because they're rhetorically handy?

- Whether "the system must matter to itself" style requirements (intrinsicality, or whatever the current postulate wording is) are doing real work, or are just a way of defining the competitors out of the running.
