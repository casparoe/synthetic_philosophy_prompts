Our TA handed round a page splitting the slogan into three grades: (i) the motives needed were ordinary — no sadism, no ideology worth the name, just career, diligence, not sticking out; (ii) from inside the role the wrongness wasn't visible to the agent, the work arrived pre-sliced so that nothing looked like a decision; (iii) the agents were typical, swap in near enough anyone from the same pool and it goes the same way. She also muttered something about banality being a claim about what kind of explanation we need rather than a claim about the person, which I did not follow.
