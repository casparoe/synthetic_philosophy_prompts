I'm in a philosophy of science seminar and we just worked through Hacking on interactive kinds and the looping effects of human kinds. My final creative assignment is a piece of satire, and I want the humor to come from getting the mechanics right rather than from generic jokes about psychiatry.

Write a modest-proposal memorandum of 700-800 words, formatted as an internal white paper of a fictional committee revising a diagnostic manual (name it whatever you like). Its proposal: for the next edition, the committee should publish the draft criteria for a newly proposed disorder several years in advance and push them into wide circulation, on the grounds that the feedback loop between a classification and the people classified is not a source of error to be controlled for but the most efficient recruitment instrument the committee has — prospective patients learn what the disorder looks like, come to present with textbook symptoms, found advocacy organizations, and deliver the prevalence figures the field trials need, which then justify the criteria that produced them. Have the committee treat the loop as an engineering specification and invent the institutions that would manage it: I was imagining provisional, docketed diagnoses issued to people who do not yet meet the criteria, and something like a forward market in projected prevalence, but use your own judgment.

The voice should be deadpan and the reasoning internally consistent: these are responsible professionals who think they are improving validity, and every step should be a real feature of interactive kinds pushed one notch further — that classifying people changes them, that the people so changed then force changes in the classification, and that no stable pre-classificatory population remains to sample. The target is the practice of diagnostic classification and the way prevalence research, patient advocacy, and criteria-setting feed into one another; no named real persons and no citations. A reader who has not read Hacking should still be able to tell what is being ironized without being told.
