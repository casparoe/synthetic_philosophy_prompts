I hold a realist view about neural network representations, and of a fairly specific kind: I think there is a fact of the matter about what a direction or feature in a model's activation space is about; I think that fact is fixed primarily by the feature's causal role in the network's later computation rather than by what the feature reliably tracks in the world; and I think successful interpretability work — isolating a direction, building a sparse dictionary of features, using it to predict or steer behavior — is discovery rather than convenient redescription. I am not committed to the model having beliefs, or to its features being concepts in anything like the human sense.

I want a map of my exposure. Suppose each of the following were reported tomorrow. For each, tell me whether it should move me and how far:

- Grain pluralism. For any candidate feature, one can construct an alternative basis in which it decomposes into two components, each of which is more causally efficacious and more predictable from the training distribution, and in which nothing downstream ever uses the sum. The refinement is always available, at every level, with no natural stopping point.
- Two Rosettas. Two labs produce complete-looking feature dictionaries for the same model. Each is equally good at predicting and controlling behavior; neither is a refinement of the other; no mapping between them preserves downstream causal role; and each licenses intervention predictions the other misses.
- Career instability. A direction that is cleanly interpretable in the base model is, after narrow fine-tuning, reused for unrelated inputs with no loss of task performance, and nothing in the model's computation favors saying its meaning changed over saying we were wrong the first time.

Rank these from fatal to merely awkward. Say which a realist of my sort should concede as counting against the view rather than dismissing as an artifact of the measurement method; say what my view predicts that instrumentalist or eliminativist rivals do not; and say whether I have qualified the position into unfalsifiability and whether, for a thesis of this kind, that is a defect or simply its normal condition. I would be most moved by something showing that my view collapses into a rival I do not want. Note which of my three scenarios are merely hypothetical and which are arguably close to what has already been observed, and separate what people working on this actually agree about from what is live disagreement — do not present a contested reading as the consensus.
