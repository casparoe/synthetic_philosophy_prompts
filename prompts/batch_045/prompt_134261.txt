I'd also like a first part where they show that restricting self-trust to any *definable* class of sentences that's closed under diagonalization still collapses, so the budget move looks like the only option left. And the question should make them say what extra strength in a stronger system could still buy you even if it can't legitimize any new action — I'm thinking shorter proofs, but don't put it in their mouths.
