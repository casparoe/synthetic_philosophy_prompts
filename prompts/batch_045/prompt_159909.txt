My coauthor says none of this tracks mechanism identity, only goodness of fit, and that there's no way to separate "the mechanism changed" from "the pump got an input it's never seen and is responding to it exactly as it always would". I think he's overcomplicating it: if the old equations still predict what the machine does, then whatever is going on inside is the same mechanism, and if they stop predicting, something in the mechanism broke.
