Schopenhauer's metaphysics of music turns on a single contrast: the other arts copy the Platonic Ideas and therefore copy the world of appearance at one remove, whereas music is held to be an immediate copy of the will itself, requiring no concept or image as an intermediary — which is why he took its effect on us to be far more direct and overwhelming than that of painting or poetry. Suppose he were confronted with three things he never encountered, and asked to rank them:

1. a supermarket ceiling speaker playing a stream generated in real time by a program tuned to measurable outcomes, its tempo and harmonic tension adjusted minute by minute to make shoppers move faster — no composer, no score, no performance, and no one listening in any ordinary sense;
2. a recording assembled from thousands of separately recorded notes, each corrected for pitch and timing, of a piece that no human being ever played, or could play, as heard;
3. a single sustained low drone, selected for its measurable physiological effect on listeners' bodies, with no melody, no key, no development and no ending.

For each, say whether his account counts it as music at all, where it would sit in his ordering of the arts, and exactly which of his premises the verdict turns on — the immediacy of music's relation to the will, the centrality of melody, the role of the composer's intuition, the analogy between the parts of a composition and the grades of the will's objectification, or something else.

Then two general questions. First, distinguish what follows from his metaphysics from what follows merely from his nineteenth-century musical tastes, above all his preference for melody and his assumption that music originates in a composer's expressive intention, and say where the two pull apart. Second, consider his claim that music's universality consists in its independence from the world of appearance: does a music that works by producing measurable effects on bodies confirm that claim or undermine it? Where his own account genuinely leaves the matter undetermined, say so rather than improvising a verdict. Please answer in British English.
