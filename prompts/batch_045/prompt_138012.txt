Draft the joint statement that a Bradleyan and a structured-proposition theorist could both sign after an honest adversarial collaboration about Bradley's regress and the unity of the proposition. The Bradleyan holds that the regress is sound: no item that is itself a constituent of a proposition can secure that proposition's unity, and positing a further relating relation only relocates the problem. The structured-proposition theorist holds that unity is secured by logical form — by the unsaturatedness of concepts, by a combinatorial operation, or by a regress that turns out to be benign.

I want two deliverables.

First, a numbered section of claims both sides can sign. It should include claims about what the regress argument actually assumes (in particular, whether it presupposes that whatever unifies must be an entity of the same logical type as what it unifies); about whether the regress targets propositions, facts or states of affairs, or both indifferently; and about whether it is in fact the same argument as the familiar regresses of instantiation and of truthmaking, or a distinct one.

Second, a disagreements table. For each row: the crux; each side's own formulation of its position on it, worded so that side would accept the wording; a case, argument, or formal test both sides agree bears on the crux; and each side's prediction about that case, recorded in advance.

Three cruxes must appear. (i) Whether the regress survives the move to function–argument structure — that is, whether saying that a is an argument of the function f differs at all from saying that a bears the application-relation to f. For this crux the agreed test is concrete: a toy language with names, monadic and dyadic predicates, negation, conjunction and a single quantifier, and the question whether a standard recursive compositional semantics for it assigns a value to an atomic formula without implicitly helping itself to an already-unified complex. (ii) Whether an infinite hierarchy of successively unified complexes is vicious or benign and, if benign, whether the item at the bottom still has the truth conditions it was supposed to have. (iii) The whole-first variant, on which the proposition is prior to its constituents and the constituents are abstractions from it: whether this escapes the regress or merely relocates it into the abstraction operation.

If you judge that some of the dispute is verbal, say which part and why; my own suspicion is that unity is being used to mean both whatever makes the constituents combined rather than merely listed and whatever makes the item a truth-bearer. Where a crux admits no test both sides would accept, write that down rather thanManufacture one. Argue the positions directly; do not cite, quote, or reference any author or text.
