Write me a joke about Kelly betting / geometric rationality -- the ole-peters-ish "maximize your time-average growth rate, not your expected wealth" version of it. So something like a guy insisting he's doing great "on average across all possible versions of me" while his actual balance keeps shrinking, or a guy who is technically correct that he's maximizing something. It should land for someone who knows what a geometric mean is doing here, not just be a generic gambling joke, and please skip puns on the word Kelly.
