They have argued for years and now want one document they can both sign.

Structure it as a numbered agreed-claims section, then a disagreements table, then a cruxes section. For each case state the precise disagreement in wording each side accepts for its own view, and record in advance what each predicts about it. Identify at least one crux — for instance whether practical payoff could come apart from ideal convergence, or whether permanent divergence counts as a failure of method — with an argument or test both accept as bearing on it. Say plainly which parts of their dispute you take to be verbal, and why.
