My brother's getting married in the spring and he and his fiancée are blowing something like $40k on one evening. Her brother says the point of the expense is that it's burned and never comes back, so calling it "wasted" misses what it's for: it's a costly signal, and a costly signal is supposed to hurt. I said that sounded wrong to me — if all honesty needs is that faking be more expensive for the low-quality type than for the high-quality one, then a signal can pay for itself and still be honest. He came back with an argument I can't find the hole in, which is annoying:

(1) A signal is honest exactly when producing it is less costly, or more rewarding, for a high-quality signaler than for a low-quality one. Differential cost at the margin is all the theory really needs.
(2) Differential cost at the margin is perfectly compatible with the signal having zero net cost, or even paying for itself — a display that pays back more to a high-quality individual is cheaper for them, whatever its total cost adds up to.
(3) So the theory of costly signaling does not actually require signals to be costly.
(4) But if signals don't have to be costly, the theory can't explain what it was built to explain: peacock tails, a gazelle bouncing up and down in front of a cheetah instead of running, my brother's $40k — the extravagance, the part that looks pointlessly thrown away.
(5) Peacocks really are dragging around a ridiculous and expensive tail.
(6) So either the theory is empty — it's compatible with any old signal, so it predicts nothing — or it can't do the job it was invented for.

I have a feeling "costly" is doing two different jobs somewhere between (1) and (4), but I can't put my finger on it, and I don't want to just dig in on (1) because it's my own point. Which step do you reject, why did that step look fine, and what does giving it up cost me? If I keep (1) and (2) and drop (4), where is the extravagance actually supposed to come from — and do I owe her brother an apology? Please also flag which parts of this are settled among people who actually work on signaling and which are live fights, and don't hand me a contested claim dressed up as the consenus view.
