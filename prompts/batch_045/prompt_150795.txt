The other thing I'm playing with, and this is the bit I made up rather than found anywhere, is a lottery version of the scale argument. On the usual version each insect contributes a small expected disvalue and the numbers do the work. On my version the force comes from the chance that some unknown fraction of insects are not marginal subjects at all but full ones, hidden ones — each insect is a ticket, and across billions of tickets the expected number of hidden Gregors may be large even if the per-insect credence is low. So the horror isn't that every insect hurts a bit, it's that some of them are someone.
