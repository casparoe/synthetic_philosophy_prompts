The topic is that cluster of views about whether whether "S knows that p" can shift with practical stakes and with who's doing the ascribing: strict/classical invariantism, attributor contextualism, subject-sensitive invariantism and pragmatic-encroachment style interest-relative views, assessment-relativism, that neighbourhood.

What I want from you is not prose about the views, it's the tree itself:

Give me the branch questions first, each one a concrete case somebody can answer before they know what any position is called, and only afterwards show which combinations of answers land on which view. - The node I most need right is the one that splits attributor contextualism from subject-sensitive / pragmatic encroachment. - Flag any pair of leaves your tree can't actually pull apart, and name the extra case that would be needed to do it.

Keep the cases boring and everyday (banks, trains, whether the milk's gone off) and keep the whole thing under 700 words, it has to fit on one side of A4 next to the cases themselves.
