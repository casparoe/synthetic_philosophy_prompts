Its headline output is a sharp threshold: below roughly 40% connected cells a fire dies on its own, above that it runs away. (1) Marcus opened with: unrealistic assumptions don't matter, you judge a model by its predictions, PATCHWORK got final burn size within about 20% on a few hundred historical fires, and demanding realistic fuel would just make it intractable — he cited Friedman's essay on positive economics, the one that says wildly false assumptions can be a feature rather than a bug.
