My study group has been leaning on the standard shoes-and-socks illustration to defuse the axiom of choice: from infinitely many pairs of shoes you can select one shoe from each pair by a rule, always take the left, while from infinitely many pairs of indistinguishable socks no such rule is forthcoming, so a genuine choice principle is doing the work. One member extends this into an argument about Zorn's lemma: the places where Zorn's lemma is actually applied, he says — extending a linearly independent set to a basis of an arbitrary vector space, producing a maximal ideal, proving Hahn–Banach — are the shoes case rather than the socks case, because in each of them there is a definite way to go on; and therefore these applications carry none of the worries that attach to the contrived cases, the difficulty showing up only in paradoxes of the Banach–Tarski sort.

Audit that argument. Give the correspondence explicitly: what plays the role of the family of sets, of the choice function, of the left shoe, of the rule. Then press on the disanalogies that matter for the use being made here: can the illustration represent what Zorn's lemma is really about, namely maximality rather than a single simultaneous selection — the hypothesis that every chain has an upper bound, the extension of a chain through infinitely many, indeed uncountably many, stages, and the difference between there being no maximal element and merely never being forced to stop? Which features of shoes and socks — uniform distinguishability, a finitely statable rule, a one-off choice rather than a transfinite process — does the illustration import into the applications it is used to certify, and do those applications actually have them? Say also whether the shoes case amounts to full choice or only to some weaker selection principle, and what follows for the conclusion. Finally: does the claim that workaday applications are innocent survive; and if socks are the wrong picture, what would be a better one — for instance an exploration that can always be carried a further step and that must remain well defined at limit stages — and what does that picture make vivid that the socks do not?
