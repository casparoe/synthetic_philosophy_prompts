Two things about her: she also flirts with the old Lucas–Penrose style thought that self-reference shows something about minds that no mere mechanism captures, and she has a stock deflection — "that's the hard problem in a cheap disguise" — that she uses on any objection that's really just the familiar gap between mechanism and experience.

Draft me eight to ten interview questions that go well past "so what's a strange loop." Please include at least:

- a short closing sequence of three questions that make "that's the hard problem in a cheap disguise" progressively harder to reach for, ending with one about what evidence could count *against* her view.
