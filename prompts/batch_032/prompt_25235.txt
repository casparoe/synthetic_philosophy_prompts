So I've been rereading Tolstoy's *What Is Art?* and it's been messing with my head. You know his argument — that real art has to "infect" the audience with a feeling the artist genuinly experienced, and that if it only works for a small educated elite it's counterfeit. He even disowned his own novels on these grounds. I keep thinking about what it would actually feel like to be an artist who buys into this completely, and then one day realizes your own work doesn't pass the test. Could you write a short story about that? Like, a painter or writer or composer who's a total Tolstoyan about art, and they have this slow or sudden reckoning where they realize the thing they've been pouring their life into is, by their own standards, fake. I want the story to really sit in that tension — not just have them abandon their standards but really wrestle with it.
