I'm organizing a workshop on the foundations of quantum mechanics and I want the centerpiece to be an adversarial-collaboration document between a psi-ontic camp and a psi-epistemic camp. Can you draft the joint statement they'd produce after a year of good-faith work together?

I want a numbered list of claims both sides can sign—about the formalism, about what the no-go theorems (PBR, Frauchiger-Renner, etc.) actually rule out, about what a satisfactory account needs to explain. Then a disagreements table where each side's view is stated in wording its own proponents would accept. Then the cruxes: the sub-questions where settling them would actually move someone off their position. For each crux, what kind of evidence or argument both sides agree would bear on it, and what each predicts.

I suspect part of their dispute might be verbal—if so, say which part and why. And if there are things each side admits would count against their own view but that haven't been shown yet, include those. I'm less interested in a diplomatic compromise than in a document that makes the remaining disagreement genuinely precise.
