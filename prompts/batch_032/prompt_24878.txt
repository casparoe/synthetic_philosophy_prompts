And if it *is* just a metaphor, is there a risk that it's quietly shaping how biologists think — maybe making them miss something? Reconstruct the implicit theory of information at work, say whether the practice actually needs it or could get by with a purely causal description, and tell me what would have to change in how molecular biology is written up if we took the metaphor seriously and decided to drop it.
