Here is my opening move. I want to press a version of the "old fact, new guise" objection. When Mary leaves the room and sees red for the first time, she certainly acquires something she lacked before. But must we describe what she acquires as knowledge of a new fact? After leaving, she comes to know that same physical fact under a phenomenal mode of presentation — she now knows what it is like for that neural activity to occur.
