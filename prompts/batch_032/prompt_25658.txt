I've been reading about the Judy Benjamin problem in formal epistemology, and I'm having trouble seeing what all the fuss is about. The setup, as I understand it, is this: Judy Benjamin is a soldier dropped into a warzone divided into Red and Blue territories, each of which contains two sectors. She has a uniform prior over which of the four sectors she finds herself in. She then receives a radio message saying, "If you are in Red territory, you are in the Headquarters sector." The puzzle is supposed to be how she should revise her credences in light of this message. But this looks like a routine exercise in Bayesian conditionalization: she has learned the material conditional "Either I am not in Red territory, or I am in Headquarters," so she should simply conditionalize on that proposition. Why isn't this the end of the story?
