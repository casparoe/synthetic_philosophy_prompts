Write a dialogue between a mereological nihilist and a mereological universalist. The nihilist holds that composition never occurs — there are no composite objects, only simples arranged in various ways. The universalist holds that composition is unrestricted — for any plurality of objects, there is a fusion of those objects. The dialogue should explore whether either position can avoid an unacceptable degree of arbitrariness without collapsing into the other. Both characters should be philosophically sophisticated and should engage directly with each other's arguments rather than talking past one another. The dialogue should bring out the deepest motivations for each view while probing their respective weaknesses.
