ok so i've been trying to get my head around the two-envelope paradox and i thought it'd be fun to have a poem that captures the structure of the thing. write a villanelle where the two refrains are something like "the other envelope is worth more in expectation" and "but by that same reasoning i'd switch right back." the form should do the work of showing why the paradox wont resolve — the refrains keep taking turns and then collide in the final couplet, which is exactly what happens when you try to think through the problem. it should be accurate enough that someone who knows the paradox would nod along, not wince at a sloppy rendering.
