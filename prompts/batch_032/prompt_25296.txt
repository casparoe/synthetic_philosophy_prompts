Okay so I've been obsessing over this thought experiment for like weeks now and my friends are probably sick of hearing about it. It's the one where you compare torturing one person for 50 years against giving a dust speck in the eye to some absurdly huge number of people — like, billions and billions, or even more. The first time I heard it I was like "obviously the torture is worse, what kind of monster would pick the dust specks?" But then the more I think about it the less sure I get. Because if each dust speck is a tiny tiny harm, and you multiply it by enough people, shouldn't it eventually add up to more total suffering than one person's torture? But also that feels wrong somehow, like there's a category difference between a speck and torture that you can't just arithmetic your way across.

I've been reading whatever I can find about this (mostly forum threads and blog posts, nothing fancy) and I've noticed people seem to split into different camps but they don't always realize what camp they're actually in — like they'll say one thing about the dust specks but then contradict themselves when you ask them about a slightly different case. So I want to make a kind of sorting tool, like a decision tree or a flowchart, that walks someone through the deeper questions and tells them what position they actually hold based on their answers. Not based on what label sounds good to them.

The tree should start from the basic dust specks vs. torture question but then branch based on the real disagreements underneath: stuff like whether tiny harms aggregate at all, whether there's some kind of threshold or lexical priority where severe suffering always trumps trivial suffering no matter the numbers, whether the thought experiment is even coherent given that you can't actually have an infinite or near-infinite number of people experiencing something, whether the distribution of harms across different people matters differently than harms concentrated in one person, etc. Each branching question should be something a normal person could answer without knowing the philosophy jargon. And if there are positions in the logical space that nobody seems to hold, I want those flagged — like is the position just dumb, or contradictory, or has nobody explored it yet? The leaves I definately want included are: the "torture is worse period" view, the "enough specks eventually outweigh torture" view, the "the whole comparison is meaningless" view, and the "it depends on how you define harm" view. But if there are other natural landing spots I'm missing, add those too. I'm not a philosophy person, just someone who fell into a weird internet rabbit hole and can't climb out.
