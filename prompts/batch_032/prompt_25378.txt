I recently had a long and frustrating argument with a friend who works in behavioral economics, and I've been turning it over ever since. His claim was that the philosophical problem of akrasia — weakness of will, acting against one's own better judgment — has essentially been dissolved by the discovery of hyperbolic discounting. His reasoning went like this: philosophers since Plato have puzzled over how someone could knowingly choose the worse course of action, but what's really going on is just that people discount future rewards hyperbolically rather than exponentially. When you prefer the salad today but the cake tomorrow, that's not a failure of will; it's just your discount function doing what discount functions do. The apparent irrationality of preference reversal vanishes once you realize that each temporal self is maximizing locally — there is no single self whose "better judgment" is being overruled, just a sequence of selves with different preferences. He cited Laibson on quasi-hyperbolic discounting and said the whole thing reduces to a parameter-fitting exercise once you give up the fiction of a unified agent.

I pushed back by saying that this seems to redescribe the phenomenon rather than explain it away. After all, the person still judges the salad to be the better choice overall and still takes the cake, and calling that "time-inconsistent preferences" doesn't tell us why the all-things-considered judgment loses out to the momentary impulse. But he countered that "all-things-considered judgment" is precisely the confused notion — once you accept that the self is a succession of temporally located agents with no privileged perspective, there is no fact of the matter about which preference is the "real" one. I found this unsettling but couldn't quite locate the flaw in it, and the argument ended in a stalemate.

I'd like a careful analysis of whether hyperbolic discounting genuinely dissolves the philosophical problem of akrasia, or whether it merely offers a formal model of the behavior while leaving the core puzzle intact.
