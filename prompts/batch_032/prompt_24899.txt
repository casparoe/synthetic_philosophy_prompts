Write a villanelle about the surprise exam paradox. The two refrains should function as the opposing poles of the paradox: one refrain (call it A1) should voice the teacher's announcement that there will be a surprise exam next week; the other (A2) should voice the students' conclusion, reached by backward induction, that no surprise exam is possible. The villanelle's form should track the reasoning: as the refrains alternate through the five tercets, the elimination of Friday, Thursday, Wednesday, and Tuesday should be discernible in the movement of the poem, and when the two refrains finally meet in the closing couplet, their juxtaposition should capture the paradox's irresolvable tension—the fact that the students' reasoning seems unassailable and yet the exam arrives as a surprise all the same. Someone who knows the paradox should recognize its structure in the poem; someone who doesn't should still find it a coherent villanelle. Begin with the poem itself, with no preface or commentary.
