My friend who reads a lot of philosophy of mind told me this basically proves that pain isn't intrinsically bad. He also mentioned that pain asymbolia is like the mirror image of people born without the ability to feel pain at all, who he said still have the normal emotional distress from injuries but just lack the sensation part, which he thinks is more evidence for the same point.
