"The problem of old evidence shows that Bayesian confirmation theory cannot account for how a known fact can confirm a new theory. This is fatal for Bayesianism because the history of science is full of cases where old evidence — like the precession of Mercury's perihelion for general relativity — strongly supported a new theory. Bayesians have tried to escape by saying we should imagine we don't know the evidence, but this just replaces objective confirmation with a psychological fiction."
