I was at a contemporary art show last weekend with a friend, and there was this installation — just a room with a pile of sand and a slowly dripping faucet. I stood there for five minutes feeling absolutley nothing. My friend said I "wasn't engaging with it properly." But I've been reading Tolstoy's What Is Art? lately, and he basically says that if art doesn't infect you with the feeling the artist had, it's failed art. Part of me thinks Tolstoy would have hated this sand installation and would say my blank reaction is actually the correct one. But another part of me thinks that's way too simplistic — maybe some art is supposed to provoke thought rather than transmit feeling, or maybe the "feeling" being transmitted is just really subtle. How would you think about this? Is my friend right that I'm just not trying hard enough, or is Tolstoy onto something that would vindicate my reaction?
