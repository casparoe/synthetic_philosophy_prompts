Format it as a numbered agreed-claims section followed by a disagreements table with columns for the issue, the compatibilist view, the skeptical view, and the crux. Throughout, separate what is settled among philosophers working on free will and moral responsibility from what remains genuinely contested, and do not present either side's position as the consensus.
