7. Doctor with one organ, two patients identical on every medical and personal factor. Verdict: can't just pick. Need a tiebreaker — lottery, age, something.

8. Choosing which of two identical packs of gum to buy at the checkout. Verdict: fine, grab one.

9. Choosing which of two identical twins to save from a burning building, where you only have time to save one. Verdict: you just save whoever's closer or whatever, but the idea that there's "no reason" to pick one over the other feels deeply wrong even though it seems true. I'm torn on this one.

Maybe it's about whether the choice is supposed to reflect your values? Or whether other people are counting on you to have reasons?
