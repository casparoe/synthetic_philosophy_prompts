Here is my opening. I have been thinking about this on my own for some time, and I keep arriving at the same difficulty. When I attend carefully to my own experience — say, the feeling of cold water running over my hands — I find I can break it down into pieces: the temperature, the pressure, the way my hands look underwater, the slight startle, a memory the cold calls up. Each of those pieces looks like something a physical system could do: detect a temperature gradient, register pressure, process visual information, trigger an association. If you gave me a complete explanation of every function, every response, every bit of information processing going on, what exactly would remain unexplained?
