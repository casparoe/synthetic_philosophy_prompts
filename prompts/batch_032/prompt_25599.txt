I'm taking a seminar on nineteenth-century aesthetics, and we've spent the last three weeks on Schopenhauer's theory of music from The World as Will and Representation. The core idea, as I understand it, is that music occupies a unique place among the arts: whereas painting, sculpture, and poetry represent the Platonic Ideas — the eternal forms that stand between the Will and particular things — music bypasses the Ideas entirely and is a direct copy or objectification of the Will itself. That's why, for Schopenhauer, music doesn't depict emotions or events but rather expresses the inner nature of reality directly: the bass notes are the lowest grades of the Will's objectification (inorganic nature), the melody is the highest (the striving of conscious life), and harmony reflects the rational order of the world. He even says that if the whole world were to vanish, music would remain, because it is not about the world of representation at all.

What I can't stop thinking about is how this theory would fare if Schopenhauer were confronted with something he could never have anticipated: generative AI that composes music — not just algorithmic composition in the sense of following rules, but large models trained on vast corpora of human music that produce novel pieces listeners find genuinely beautiful and emotionally affecting. There's no Will behind these compositions; there's no striving, no inner nature being expressed, no composer whose genius unconsciously channels the metaphysical structure of reality. It's just pattern extraction and recombination. And yet the output can be indistinguishable from human-composed music, and people report being moved by it. Would Schopenhauer have to say that AI-generated music isn't really music in his sense — that it's a kind of sonic illusion, formally correct but metaphysically empty, and that listeners who are moved by it are in some way deceived? Or could he accommodate it by arguing that the training data, being human-composed, carries the trace of the Will into the model's outputs, so that the AI is a derivative but still genuine conduit? Would he perhaps double down and claim that this technology actually vindicates his view, because it shows that the surface features of music can be reproduced without capturing what makes music metaphysically significant — and that attentive listeners can tell the difference? I'm trying to write a final paper that works through this, and I'd like you to reason through what Schopenhauer's position would most likely be, given the internal logic of his system, and to flag any places where his system might force him into a claim that looks empirically false or just implausible today.
