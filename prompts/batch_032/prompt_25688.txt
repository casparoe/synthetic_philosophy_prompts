I serve on the Student Services Advisory Board at my university. The administration proposes deploying an AI chatbot to field initial inquiries from prospective students — questions about admissions, financial aid, campus culture, and the like. The chatbot would be designed to adopt a warm, personable tone: it would use first-person pronouns, express enthusiasm about the university, and sign off with a human name. At no point in the interaction would it disclose that it is an AI. The administration argues that this makes exchanges feel natural and welcoming, and that a disclosure would only distract from the information being conveyed. Several board members regard this as straightforwardly deceptive; others see it as no different in principle from a well-crafted FAQ page written in a friendly voice. I need to draft a short written opinion for the board recommending whether to approve the proposal, approve it with conditions, or reject it. The opinion should engage seriously with the strongest arguments on both sides and reach a concrete, actionable recommendation.
