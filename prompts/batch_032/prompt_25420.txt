When I pointed out that the trait could be a byproduct of something else, or could have evolved for a different reason and later been put to its current use, he'd acknowledge the possibility but treat it as irrelevant, because "it's still an adaptation either way."

What I noticed is that the word "adaptation" was doing two different jobs in his argument, and the slippage between them made his position unfalsifiable.
