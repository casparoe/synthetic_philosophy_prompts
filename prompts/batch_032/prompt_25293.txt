ok so i've been reading about pain asymbolia and i want a poem that captures the philosophical puzzle. write a sonnet where the octave gives us ordinary pain — the way the awfulness feels baked right into the sensation, like the hurt and the badness are the same thing. then the volta hits with the asymbolic patient: someone who says "yeah it hurts, i can tell you exactly where and how much, but it doesn't bother me." the sestet should work through what this means — that the badness of pain isn't a property of the sensation itself, it's something about our relation to it, maybe something we bring. the form should do the work: the volta is the moment the reader realizes these two dimensions can come apart, and the sestet's resolution should feel earned, not just stated. it should be accurate enough that someone who knows the pain science literature wouldn't wince, but readable as a poem by someone who's never heard of asymbolia.
