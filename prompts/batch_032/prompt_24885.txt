I keep coming back to Dennett's distinction between Orwellian and Stalinesque revisions of consciousness — the idea from *Consciousness Explained* that when the brain edits experience (like in the color-phi phenomenon, where you see a dot change color mid-trajectory before the second dot even appears), there are two ways to tell the story: either you had one experience and your memory got revised after the fact (Orwellian), or your consciousness was delayed and constructed after all the info was in (Stalinesque). Dennett's point is that there's no fact of the matter — the two accounts are just different narratives about the same underlying processes.
