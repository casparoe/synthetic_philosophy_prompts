I've been noticing something and I wonder if there's a word for it — or maybe two related things that need two different words.

Here's the first thing. I work in an office and I've watched this happen more times than I can count: a woman suggests something in a meeting, nobody reacts, five minutes later a guy says basically the same thing and everyone nods like it's insightful. I started noticing it everywhere after that — not just gender, but also with younger people, or people with the wrong accent, or whatever the local low-status category happens to be. It's not that anyone says "I don't believe you." It's that some people have to work twice as hard to get their testimony taken seriously, and the discount isn't based on whether they've been reliable in the past — it's based on what kind of person they are. I'm sure there's a name for prejudice, but this feels like a specific subtype — it's about the credibility deficit that attaches to a person, about being treated as less of a knower. Does this have its own term?

The second thing is different but seems connected. I read a memoir a while back by someone who'd gone through an experience that, at the time it happened, didn't really have a name in the wider culture — the author kept having to describe it in roundabout ways, using concepts that were adjacent but not quite right, and you could tell that the lack of a shared name for the thing had actually made the experience worse. It wasn't just that she couldn't explain it to others — she had trouble understanding it herself, because the conceptual tools weren't there. And once the term eventually entered the culture (I won't name it because I don't want to make this about that specific case), suddenly all these scattered experiences snapped into focus as instances of one thing. That gap between having the experience and having the concept — that gap itself seems like a kind of harm, not just an inconvenience. Does that have a name?

I'm especially curious whether these two things are treated as separate phenomena or as two sides of one coin, and where the edges are — like, if you're not believed because you're bad at explaining yourself (rather than because of prejudice), is that the first thing or something else? And if a concept exists but your community just doesn't have access to it, is that the second thing or a different problem? I'm not just after labels — I want to understand how to carve these apart properly, or whether they should be carved apart at all.
