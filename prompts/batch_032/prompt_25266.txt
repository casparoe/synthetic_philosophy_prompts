I want to run an in-class game or structured role-play—something that takes about 45 minutes—where the dynamics of the dialectic actually play out between people in the room, so they run into the paradox themselves before anyone names it. The activity should make them experience the way the pursuit of pure recognition can curdle into domination, and the way the dominated position might unexpectedly open onto a kind of independence the dominator can't access. No lecture up front, no Hegel quotes until the debrief.
