The leaves I need: classical type-type identity theory, role functionalism, realizer functionalism, and eliminativism. (If they reject physicalism they just get off the tree at the top — we're bracketing dualism and idealism.)

Each node should be a concrete question they can answer without knowing the jargon. Like: 'Do you think an alien made of silicon goo could feel pain if its internal causal wiring was the same as a human's?' or 'If we found out human pain is C-fibers firing and octopus pain is something totally different, would you say pain is still one thing they both have, or two different things?' Stuff at that level.

Also: flag any pairs of leaves the tree can't cleanly separate (I know the role vs realizer functionalism distinction is slippery), and identify any logical combinations of answers that nobody defends — like, if someone says mental states are physical and refer to real kinds but denies multiple realizability even for closely related species, does anyone actually hold that, or is it a dead branch?

Keep it to six or seven nodes max, I don't want something that takes all section.
