Write an essay on whether Humean supervenience can survive the challenge posed by quantum entanglement. Lewis's best-system account requires laws to supervene on a "mosaic" of local, intrinsic matters of particular fact, but entangled states seem to involve irreducibly non-separable relations that no arrangement of local qualities can capture. Assess whether moves like enlarging the base to include fundamental relations (or retreating to a wave-function realism) actually save the Humean project, or whether they amount to conceding anti-Humeanism. Defend a definite verdict on whether Humean supervenience, suitably modified or not, remains tenable in light of quantum non-separability.
