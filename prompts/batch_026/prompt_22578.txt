I am co-authoring a paper with a colleague who has just been offered a position that will take him out of academia for several years. Before he leaves, he has asked whether we should adopt a standing rule under which, whenever either of us identifies a change that would make the paper better for both of us, we implement it immediately rather than negotiating it case by case. Is his proposed rule unreasonable, or is there some structural reason that an arrangement like this tends to benefit even the party who is more reluctant to change things?
