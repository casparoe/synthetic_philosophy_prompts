I'm preparing a piece on the philosophy of language and I keep getting stuck on slurs. I take it that the "semantic" position holds that a slur's offensiveness is part of its meaning, rather than a matter of the speaker's intent or the surrounding context. But that seems to run into an obvious problem: a member of the targeted group can use a slur, and a reporter can quote one, without it landing as offensive in the same way. If the offense were really built into the word's meaning, wouldn't it have to be offensive in those cases too — and doesn't that show the offense is pragmatic after all?
