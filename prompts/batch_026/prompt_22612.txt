Could you write a dialogue between two people who each defend one of these accounts and are sincerely trying to change the other's mind? On the other, a defender of the self-locating, time-symmetric account, who holds that there is no fundamental arrow and that the apparent one is an artifact of typicality and of the observer's location within the block. I want them to actually grapple with the specific points of contention: whether the past hypothesis is an explanation or a restatement, how each side answers the Loschmidt reversibility objection, what work "typicality" is doing in each account, and why either party is not merely a Boltzmann brain in a thermal fluctuation.
