I'm a PhD student in logic preparing a graduate seminar on set theory, and I'd like a short dialogue between a structuralist and a Platonist discussing Skolem's paradox. Both speakers should be equally competent, and the exchange should contain real back-and-forth rather than talk past each other. They should argue over whether the existence of a countable model of ZFC shows that “uncountability” is not an absolute property of sets, or whether it only reveals a limitation on an external observer’s ability to enumerate the model’s sets. The dialogue should engage the distinction between internal and external notions of countability, and it should not settle for the slogan that the model is “countable from the outside” without explaining what is doing the work.
