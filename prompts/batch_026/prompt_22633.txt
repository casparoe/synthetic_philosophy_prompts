I cover science and ideas for a magazine, and I want an essay on whether F. H. Bradley's regress against the analysis of propositional unity is a genuine objection to the view that propositions are structured complexes of concepts and relations. The essay should explain the target view and Bradley's argument with enough precision to be useful to a philosophically literate reader, weigh the main objections and replies—including whether the regress is vicious, inert, or based on a confusion between different kinds of unity—and conclude by defending a clear verdict.
