I want to push him on that without turning it into "tell us about Galileo again." Could you draft eight to ten interview questions, each with a likely answer and a sharper follow-up? Make the questions sound like something I'd actually ask on air, not like a journal article. I was talking with a grad student who kept saying this whole episode is basically "Galileo vs. the Pope," and I want the questions to make that story visibly too tidy. If the guest is likely to retreat to the standard line that Galileo was just being careful about his sources, build that into the follow-up rather than pretending it won't come up.
