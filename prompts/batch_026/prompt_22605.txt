As a journalist covering science and ideas, I am preparing a feature on algorithmic management and want a dialogue between a Marxian political economist and a liberal political philosopher discussing a freelance data annotator who owns her laptop, sets her own hours, and is paid a market rate, but must accept tasks from a platform that monitors her work, assigns performance scores, and can deactivate her account. They should argue over whether she is exploited in Marx’s technical sense—as a producer whose surplus labor is appropriated under private ownership of the conditions of production—or merely alienated, in the sense of lacking control over her activity, its products, and her relations to others. Both speakers should be equally competent, and the dialogue should contain real back-and-forth rather than parallel monologues.
