So I write about science and ideas for the local paper, and I also sit on the city's new advisory board on algorithmic tools in the criminal justice system. The police department wants to start feeding a repeat-offense risk score into bail recommendations, and the board votes next month. The vendors tool is well calibrated within each racial group, but given the base rates in the data its false-positive rate runs about 15 points higher for Black defendants than for white ones. Could you draft the memo I'll circulate before the vote? It should pick approve, reject, or approve with conditions, actually grapple with the impossability result (you can't have both calibration and equal error rates across groups when base rates differ), and take the police's argument — calibration is what you need for resource allocation — seriously before explaining why it doesn't decide individual bail cases.
