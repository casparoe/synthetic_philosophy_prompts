In a game I've been playing with a friend, a single urn holds 30 red balls and 60 other balls that are either black or yellow in some unknown proportion. In the first round I may bet on red, winning if a red ball is drawn, or on black, winning if a black ball is drawn; I chose red. In a separate second round I may bet on red-or-yellow or on black-or-yellow; I chose black-or-yellow. My friend insists these two choices cannot both be rational, that they reveal a logical inconsistency in my beliefs, and that I am therefore being irrational. I can see why red felt like the better first-round bet and why black-or-yellow felt like the better second-round bet, but I cannot see why those two hunches are supposed to be a contradiction. Is my friend right that my choices are incoherent, or is there a consistent way to make sense of them?
