So my two friends got into a real argument last week at this street food spot, and I'm still not sure who was right. There were two food trucks parked next to each other, one with a huge line wrapping around the block and the other basically empty. My friend Dana said the line was totally rational. The first few people in it must have had some actual reason — they'd been their before, or they could watch the food being made — and she'd read that a line like this can lock in after just a couple of people, because the first few choices are already more telling than any one person's own hunch. So once a couple of people with real info are in, it makes sense for everyone behind them to just follow, even if they have no clue of their own. The other friend, Marcus, said that's exactly the dumb part. Once the line gets long, everyone at the back is just looking at the line, not the food, so the fiftieth person has basicly zero new information and is just copying the forty-ninth. He called it a feedback loop, a classic herd, and said the empty truck is probably just as good or better.

Dana pushed back and said that's not dumb, that's the whole point — each person is correctly weighing the fact that the people ahead of them had a reason to be there, so following is the smart move even without your own info. Marcus said but then the line can be wrong and just stay wrong: if the first two people happened to be regulars who love it but it's actually terrible for a first-timer, the whole line is a mistake, and everyone's "rational" but everyone's wasting their time. He said "rational" here just means nobody's individually stupid, not that the crowd is right. Dana said fine, individual rationality doesn't guarantee the crowd wins, but that's not a bug — it means the line is a real signal that can be wrong, not a mob of copycats.

I was just standing there eating a pretzel and couldn't tell who was making sense. Can you go through their back-and-forth and tell me who's right about each specific point, and is there anything both of them are missing or getting subtly wrong? I don't just want a "Dana wins" or "Marcus wins" verdict, I want the actual reasoning on where each of them landed or missed.
