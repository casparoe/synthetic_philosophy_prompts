I finally read the actual excerpts from Wittgenstein's Lectures on Religious Belief (the bit about the Last Judgment) after seeing him namedropped in old LW threads, and it kinda seems like he scooped Yudkowsky's "belief in belief" by something like 70 years. Isn't that basically the same debunking move as the dragon parable: the "belief" doesn't pay rent in anticipated experience, so its not really a belief, just a verbal habit dressed up as one?
