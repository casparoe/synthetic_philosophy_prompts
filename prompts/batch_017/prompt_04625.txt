I've been mulling over something from my summer job at a small ice cream shop. Two coworkers and I have spent all summer covering each other's shifts whenever someone can't make it, trusting that the favor will be returned before the shop closes for the season—a date we've all known since June, as a couple of us are leaving for college in the fall. Yesterday, one coworker announced she's no longer willing to cover anyone's shifts, given that there's no way to be repaid once that date passes. Another coworker replied that, by that logic, none of us should have covered a single shift all summer, because we could all see this end point coming from day one. That conclusion feels wrong to me, but I can't quite pin down where the reasoning goes astray—is there really a flaw in it, or does it actually hold up?
