Write a joke that plays on the no-trade theorem from economics and game theory — the result that two fully rational agents with common priors should not expect to profit from a purely speculative bet against each other. It should be funny to people who actually understand the theorem, i.e., the punchline should turn on its actual epistemic logic (what a counterparty's willingness to trade reveals, and how a rational agent ought to update on it), rather than being a generic joke about greedy traders or gullible investors.
